# Chilliwack Mountain, Chilliwack — Lots and Land: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No listing addresses or asking prices beyond named developments themselves. No sold prices.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Chilliwack Mountain is a residential hillside neighbourhood in the City of Chilliwack, on the named mountain on the south side of the Fraser River, just west of Chilliwack Creek and west of the downtown core. "Chilliwack Mountain" is the MLS board sub-area name as used in Paragon; the City treats Chilliwack Mountain as a neighbourhood in its own reporting but does not publish a street-by-street boundary for the sub-area name. Chilliwack is CADREB ground. https://apps.gov.bc.ca/pub/bcgnws/names/5861.html and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3237
- The community sits above the Industrial Way / Progress Way industrial area on the valley floor; the City's Schweyey Road forcemain page places the mountain community above that industrial area, with sewage service along Schweyey Road for Chilliwack Mountain and portions of Industrial Way and Progress Way. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3237
- Nearby areas: Chilliwack Downtown and Chilliwack Proper West to the east, Little Mountain to the southeast, Fairfield Island across the river to the north, and the Lickman Road / Industrial Way corridor below the mountain to the west. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- Residential streets are the mountain roads themselves — Chilliwack Mountain Road, Grandview Drive and Hillkeep Place at the top, and Sunrise Drive on the slopes. Highway 1 access is via the Lickman Road interchange south of the mountain, where BC Transit's Lickman Park & Ride sits. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/hillkeep.html and https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=59
- Transit: no BC Transit route in the Chilliwack route list runs onto Chilliwack Mountain residential streets; the closest listed service is route 59 Industrial – Downtown Chilliwack to Cottonwood Mall via the Lickman Park & Ride, with peak-period trips. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- Schools: no SD33 public school is located on Chilliwack Mountain itself; the schools physically in Chilliwack listed in the provincial school directory are all on the valley floor or in Sardis. Catchments are address-based in SD33 and are checked in the district's School Locator; no sample address was run for a mountain address in the research, so catchment schools for mountain addresses are UNVERIFIED — all mountain catchments are UNVERIFIED in this file. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/chilliwack and https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Chilliwack Mountain land trades as fee-simple hillside lots — that is the documented market. No named bare-land subdivision, no verified bare-land strata, and no presale lot offering was verified in the research. What a buyer can actually buy as land here is a fee-simple hillside lot (vacant or with a house on it) whose buildable/subdividable value is set by its hillside zone and its measured slope.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default and confirmed form

- **Confirmed housing/land form:** hillside residential lots carrying single-detached houses, with the housing form governed by the City's hillside development permit areas D-2 and D-5. The research states it directly: the confirmed housing form on the mountain is freehold hillside lots; strata on the mountain, where it exists, is built townhouse strata (siblings below), not bare land. Fee-simple is the default form: own title, no strata, no Form B, no strata fees. https://chilliwack.com/ocp and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Lot character from the hillside zones, as the bylaw states it:** HR (Hillside Residential) Zone, minimum lot area 0.2 ha where served by a community water system and 0.4 ha for all other uses, minimum lot width 25 m; RH (Rural Hillside) Zone, minimum lot area 8 ha and minimum lot width 25 m; SHR (Suburban Hillside Residential) Zone, minimum lot area set by the zone's slope table, starting at 360 m² for the lowest slope band and rising to 990 m² for the highest; SR (Suburban Residential) Zone, minimum lot area 650 m² where served by community water and community sanitary sewer, minimum lot width 17.5 m on the same services — all under City of Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw No. 5000. These are zone minimums, not a claim that every mountain lot is that size. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Slope is the lot character:** in the SHR zone the minimum lot area rises with slope band (360 m² → 990 m²), so a lot's subdivision or rebuild potential depends on its measured slope, not just its area. Price buildable, permit-able area — not gross area and not view alone. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

- **Zoning:** City of Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw No. 5000 — HR / RH / SHR / SR minimums in Section 2. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Hillside permit rule — the governing lot rule on the mountain:** Development Permit Area D-2 applies to Chilliwack Mountain, and a permit with a geotechnical hazard and slope assessment is required before subdivision, construction, land clearing or earthworks. Budget the report and permit timing into any build, subdivision or renovation plan. DPA D-5 (Hillside Development) also covers the mountain. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- **SSMUH as the City states it:** Chilliwack's small-scale multi-unit housing Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024. Its practical reach on the mountain is limited by the hillside zones and DPA D-2 — SSMUH eligibility for a specific mountain lot is UNCONFIRMED in this file and is a City check, not an assumption. (Sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/chilliwack-mountain-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision on the mountain requires the DPA D-2 permit with geotechnical hazard and slope assessment (above) plus the zone minimum for the parcel's zone and slope band (Section 2); servicing is confirmed parcel by parcel (Schweyey Road forcemain position, Section 2). No separate Chilliwack servicing-bylaw number is asserted in this file's research — none is filled in. Confirm with the City for the specific parcel. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- **Flood position:** no mountain-specific floodplain statement was found in City material; the City's flood pages address the Fraser River dyke system on the valley floor below the mountain. A specific lot's flood status is UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked — slope diligence (D-2), not floodplain diligence, is the mountain's documented regime. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- **ALR rules (where a parcel is confirmed ALR — none confirmed in the research):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); BC Assessment farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel with the ALC first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata rules:** no bare-land strata is verified on the mountain (Section 2). Where a BC bare-land strata lot is bought, the full block applies to the lot: Form B diligence (within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1%/$200K, 2% to $2M, 3% above, +2% on residential portion above $3M; FTHB Program (first $500,000 exempt at ≤$835,000, max $8,000, partial to $860,000) and Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, Mar 20 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land — a mountain lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36%/$6,300/$350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000/$8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What land actually trades on Chilliwack Mountain?** Fee-simple hillside lots carrying (or able to carry) single-detached houses. No bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How big can a mountain lot be — and how small can I split it?** By zone and slope: HR 0.2 ha on community water / 0.4 ha otherwise (width 25 m); RH 8 ha; SHR 360 m²–990 m² by slope band; SR 650 m² on full services (width 17.5 m). The SHR slope table decides — get the slope measured. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: What permit do I need before I subdivide or build?** A DPA D-2 development permit with a geotechnical hazard and slope assessment — required before subdivision, construction, land clearing or earthworks anywhere in the permit area, which covers the mountain. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- **Q: Is the lot on City sewer and water?** Partly confirmed at area level only: the Schweyey Road forcemain provides sewage services for Chilliwack Mountain and portions of Industrial Way and Progress Way, but service for a specific parcel is not confirmed — verify connection status for the exact lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3237
- **Q: Is Westpointe a bare-land strata I can buy a lot in?** UNCONFIRMED — no source checked states Westpointe's tenure expressly (strata vs fee-simple vs bare-land strata). Confirm on title and the Form B (if strata) before any tenure claim. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put four units on a mountain lot?** Bylaw 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024) is Chilliwack's SSMUH bylaw, but hillside zones and DPA D-2 limit its reach on the mountain — eligibility for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED and is a City check. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Is any mountain land in the ALR?** Parcel-specific and not confirmed for mountain parcels in the research — check the ALC mapping for the individual lot. If it is ALR, subdivision needs ALC approval and the caps in Section 3 apply. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Q: Will GST apply to a newly created mountain lot?** Generally yes where the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided — generally taxable under CRA GI-003; an individual's personal-use resale is generally exempt. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a mountain lot?** UNVERIFIED — no SD33 locator run for a mountain address exists in the research, and no school sits on the mountain. Run the exact address in the SD33 School Locator. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q: What are holding costs on a vacant mountain lot?** Chilliwack taxes on the City cycle (due July 2, 2026; 5% after July 2, 2026; additional 5% after August 4, 2026), plus drainage and dyking levies on top of the municipal rate. No strata fee — mountain lots here are fee-simple. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
