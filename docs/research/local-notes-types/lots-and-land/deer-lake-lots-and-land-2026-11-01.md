## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026.** Never cite the former 1965 Bylaw No. 4742 for a current Burnaby lot. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 SSMUH District in June 2024. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025,** replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; Burnaby's community plans are policy guidelines for rezoning and development applications. Read any large-lot density assumption against the 2050 OCP map first. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. The research adds the local caution in its own words: heritage-precinct and park-edge lots carry extra review, so eligibility must be confirmed lot by lot — do not price SSMUH potential into a Sperling Avenue heritage lot on the citywide rule alone. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision parcel area, shape and dimensions must conform to the zoning bylaw for the parcel's zone, under the Burnaby Subdivision Control and Development Servicing Bylaw (renamed from the Burnaby Subdivision Control Bylaw 1971 by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024; the City's servicing updates took effect September 30, 2024). Required works and services include highways and lanes, water distribution connected to the City system, sewage collection and drainage collection, built to the City's Engineering Design Criteria Manual standards; the City's Improvements Guide addresses servicing for developments with four or more primary dwelling units on a residential lot. The base bylaw's own number is UNCONFIRMED on a primary source read for this file, so no number is asserted for it — the process above is what is documented. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=77052 and https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-08/Servicing-Improvements-Guide.pdf
- **Heritage and lakeshore diligence:** Sperling Avenue fronting the park lies within the Deer Lake Park Heritage Precinct, and heritage-register houses such as the 1912 Woodward House carry heritage review — check the register and the precinct before planning a rebuild or subdivision on a park-fronting lot. A floodplain designation for Deer Lake's residential streets was not confirmed in the sources read; the lakeshore is the area's wet edge (the City is replacing the boat-launch boardwalk outright), and parcel-level hazard status is a property-specific check: UNVERIFIED lot by lot. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?objectType_facet=Landmark%20Description%20&objectType_facet=History%20Links&ps=50&q=&sort=title_sort%20asc&creator_facet=johnreid000000%7CJohn%20Reid&creator_facet=bernardrhill000000%7CBernard%20R.%20Hill&place_facet=sperlingavenue000000%7CSperling%20Avenue&p=1 and https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no position for this sub-area is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Deer Lake, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Burnaby property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding current taxes (and on an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan, about $61 for the average home. The 2026 detached single-family water charge is $652.33 (Section 2). Area-specific levy notes for Deer Lake: UNVERIFIED. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot at Deer Lake?** No verified one — there is no standing bare-land market here. Land trades as fee-simple detached house lots around the lake and on the Buckingham Heights slope; no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata plan, new residential project or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Are the "Deer Lake townhouses" online actually in Deer Lake?** On this record, no — the listings that surface under Deer Lake wording carry neighbouring sub-area labels (Burnaby Lake, Sperling-Duthie, Greentree Village and others) in their own MLS area fields. Follow the MLS sub-area field, not the listing prose. Greentree Village in particular contributes 0 to Deer Lake. (Sibling townhomes file, §2.)
- **Q: Can a big Buckingham Heights lot be split or multiplexed?** The citywide R1 rule allows 3–4 homes on a standard lot (6 only near frequent transit) and multi-unit lots may be stratified — but the Burnaby 2050 OCP now governs over older maps, and heritage-precinct and park-edge lots carry extra review. Confirm zone, OCP designation and precinct status for the parcel before pricing any split. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Q: What does the heritage precinct do to a Sperling Avenue lot?** Houses on the City's heritage register inside the Deer Lake Park Heritage Precinct — the 1912 Woodward House at 5195 Sperling Avenue is the documented example — carry heritage review for renovations and rebuilds. Check the register before assuming teardown value. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?objectType_facet=Landmark%20Description%20&objectType_facet=History%20Links&ps=50&q=&sort=title_sort%20asc&creator_facet=johnreid000000%7CJohn%20Reid&creator_facet=bernardrhill000000%7CBernard%20R.%20Hill&place_facet=sperlingavenue000000%7CSperling%20Avenue&p=1
- **Q: Is Deer Lake the same market as Deer Lake Place or Upper Deer Lake?** No — SnapStats reports them as three separate Burnaby sub-areas with separate inventory and solds. Deer Lake itself recorded 13 detached listings, 1 sold and no attached listings in September 2026. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report (research file).
- **Q: Is a Deer Lake lot serviced?** The established streets sit inside Burnaby's municipal system on the City's utility wording (2026 detached water charge $652.33; sewer as a parcel tax for single-family homes) — confirm the specific parcel with the City. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). No such developer lot offering is verified in Deer Lake itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does a lot purchase qualify for the first-time buyer GST rebate?** No — the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) applies to new housing, not bare land itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Deer Lake lot?** By exact address only — the one settled locator run (6066 Buckingham Ave → Buckingham Elementary → Burnaby Central Secondary) does not transfer to other streets. Run the address in the SD41 locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q: How thin is this market for pricing a lot?** One detached sale on 13 listings in September 2026 (8% ratio) and no attached product at all — price off wider central-Burnaby and Buckingham Heights comparables over a longer window, not the current month. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report (research file).
