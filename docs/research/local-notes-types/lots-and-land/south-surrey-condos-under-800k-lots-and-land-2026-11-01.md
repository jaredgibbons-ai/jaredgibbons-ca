- **Secondary suites (Surrey side):** one per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit under Bylaw 12000, habitable floor area at most **40% of the principal dwelling unit**, never inside a coach house or garden suite, and not a separate strata lot; no current 90 m² cap exists in the bylaw and none is stated. Coach / garden suites: 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites (read 2026-11-01, via the Surrey city research)

- **Short-term rentals (Surrey side only):** owner-only — only an owner who lives at the property as their primary residence may host, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence is required first. **Licence figure, conflict disclosed:** the City's program page states **$358 per year** (carried here); the City's 2024 corporate report proposing the amendments put the licence at $350/yr plus fees — check the City licence page before quoting either figure. White Rock STR rules: UNVERIFIED in this research (above). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals (read 2026-11-01)
- **Strata block (the form a band purchase actually takes):** a condo owner owns their strata lot and, with the other owners, the common property through the strata corporation. Form B (SPA s. 59) — fees, amounts owed, approved levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page. Depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots (the live page's own framing, consistent with the provincial rule as cited there). CRF minimum 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (long-term; short-term may still be restricted, and Surrey's owner-only municipal rule applies on the Surrey side). Only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, incl. May 1, 2023 exemptions). Form F, bylaws and rules are the companion documents the live page's checklist names. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-condos-under-800k.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Bare-land strata contrast:** where a South Surrey product is a bare-land strata lot (documented in the sub-area land homes in §2, not in this band feed), the same Form B / depreciation / CRF rules apply to the lot itself, and the buyer owns the strata lot (essentially the building lot) plus the house on it — it is NOT fee-simple. No bare-land strata product is counted in this band file.
- **ALR:** no ALR parcel is documented in this band's feed; where a South Surrey parcel outside the band is in the reserve, the full ALR block governs (subdivision requires ALC permission; residences cannot be stratified — ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6, SPA s. 239, ALCA s. 21(1); principal residence ≤500 m² without an application, suite in principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier: $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha, $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha, $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by October 31). Check any edge parcel on the ALC maps. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **PTT (worked examples at the band cap, inputs + tier math stated):** general rates 1% to $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. **$800,000 (the cap), no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = $14,000** — and **$6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer** on that same $800,000 input (the program's worked anchor). $500,000, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. $3,500,000 purely residential (context, outside the band) = $2,000 + $36,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000** (the $93,000 figure governs; a $78,000 figure for this input appearing in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program is an OPEN reconciliation item — it was not met in this slug's siblings and is never carried here). First-time buyers generally: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — new HOME, not bare land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST — CRA GI-003 level only, applied carefully:** a resale condo is not a vacant-land sale; for vacant land itself, most sales by individuals are exempt, with taxable cases including land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and parcels created by subdividing into more than two parts. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% as the published range only; **the CMHC premium-range error is NOT documented for this slug and is NOT asserted here** (mortgage-calculator file only). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
