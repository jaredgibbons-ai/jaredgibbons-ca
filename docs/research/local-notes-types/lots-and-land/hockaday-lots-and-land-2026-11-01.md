## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996:** small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3, with the subdivision minimums in Section 2 (650 m² / 18.5 m in R-1 and R-3; 465 m² / 12.5 m in R-2). Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and s.523 covers riparian areas. Confirm the parcel's zone before pricing a split or a unit count. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId= and https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **OCP — Interim Official Community Plan, Bylaws 5511/5512, 2026:** adopted April 27, 2026, superseding the Citywide OCP No. 3479, 2001 — any Hockaday document still citing OCP No. 3479 as current is stale. The city-wide OCP and the Transit-Oriented Areas work near Pinetree Way apply around the sub-area; a Hockaday neighbourhood plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review
- **SSMUH, as the City states it:** Coquitlam's adopted SSMUH rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — VERIFIED instrument:** servicing runs under the **City of Coquitlam Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 3558, 2003** — stated as verified on the program's direction for Coquitlam files (consolidated bylaw, originally adopted July 24, 2003, with amendments through July 2026; the owner grants or acquires statutory rights-of-way for the works and services needed to serve a subdivision or development, in a form acceptable to the City's solicitor, and pays for traffic-control and street-name signage installed by the City at the owner's expense before subdivision approval or building permit issue). Parcel-level servicing requirements are confirmed with the City for the specific lot. https://publicdocs.coquitlam.ca/coquitlamdoc/getdocIF.asp?doc=6328713
- **Watercourse diligence:** a Watercourse Protection Development Permit can be required within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam, with Zoning Bylaw s.519 flood protection setbacks and s.523 riparian rules behind it — check creek-edge lots early. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Hockaday ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Hockaday lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Hockaday-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Hockaday, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Coquitlam property taxes and utilities:** City of Coquitlam property tax payment was due July 2, 2026, covering January 1 through December 31, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance of current taxes after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants).
