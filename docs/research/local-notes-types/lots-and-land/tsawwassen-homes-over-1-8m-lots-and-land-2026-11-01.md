## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288 adoption is held UNCONFIRMED in this file — a January 24, 2024 City council report proposed readings for "Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024" to repeal and replace Bylaw No. 7162, 2015, and sibling lots files assert it as adopted in 2024; the conflict is flagged and no adoption date is asserted here. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Hazard DPAs / flood:** SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment) and SD3 Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands (2.9 m GSC datum floor level, 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation, 7.5 m tidal setback); the City of Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure — flood construction and insurance questions are address-specific for any low-lying listing in this band. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Delta taxes:** due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **ALR edges:** farm land on the Tsawwassen edges is designated Agriculture (A) in the OCP (Bylaw No. 8400, 2024, s. 1.5.1) and ALR rules apply where land is in the ALR; ALR status is parcel-specific on the ALC maps. ALR subdivision needs ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, by Oct 31) — flat "$2,500+" is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **PTT:** 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above; further 2% on residential value over $3,000,000 (mixed-class: residential portion only; farm-class land with a dwelling: up to 0.5 ha treated as residential). PTT is on fair market value at registration, not annual taxes. Worked math in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Exemptions do not reach this band:** FTHB PTT exemption on the first $500,000, full at ≤$835,000 (max $8,000, partial to $860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Home owner grant at this band:** 2026 threshold $2,075,000 — full regular grant at/below, reduced $5 per $1,000 over, $0 over $2,189,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; Mar 20, 2025–2031) — and it phases out below this band's floor in any case. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
