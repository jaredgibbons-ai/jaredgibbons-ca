## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Verified position, stated plainly at the top: East Newton has NO meaningful bare-land market documented in its research file. No named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata, or lot-only development is verified on East Newton ground. What trades as "land" here is built-out single-family subdivision ground — a house lot bought for its SSMUH / rebuild potential — plus the Business Park ground at the north end. No lot counts, lot prices or lot-only inventory are asserted.**

- **What the ground is.** The City planned East Newton as single-family subdivisions under the East Newton NCPs, and the research file records the confirmed stock as fee-simple (freehold) houses in subdivisions built out under those plans. A "land" purchase here is therefore normally a fee-simple house lot with an existing house on it, valued for teardown, rebuild or added units — not a vacant serviced lot in a new bare-land subdivision. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/RPT_2016-R228.pdf
- **Fee-simple is the default and only verified lot form.** No bare-land strata is verified on East Newton ground in the research file or any sibling file checked. Where a two-unit building exists, Surrey stock can be one fee-simple property or a two-lot strata under the Strata Property Act — tenure is read lot by lot on title, never assumed from the street. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Lot character — zone minimums AS THE RESEARCH STATES THEM.** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs; Newton zones include RA (Acreage Residential) and the R1–R6 residential series, with bylaw minimum lot areas including R1 at the 1,858 m² band, R2 at 929 m², R3 at 464 m² for a single-family home and 557 m² for a duplex, R4 at 250 m², and R5 at 200 m² for a single-family home and 225 m² for a duplex. Older larger lots sit on the acreage-residential edges. These are bylaw minimums, not a survey of lots for sale — no typical for-sale lot size is published in the research. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **ALR vs non-ALR.** No ALR designation inside East Newton was confirmed on a City page in the research; the area sits in the Serpentine River watershed uplands east of the downstream lowlands, and ALR land lies south and east of Newton generally. A specific property's ALR status is confirmed in the Agricultural Land Commission's maps — check the parcel, do not rely on the sub-area generalisation. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Serviced vs unserviced.** The research documents no well/septic pocket inside East Newton; the City's SSMUH page treats properties not connected to community water and sewer as an exemption category, which is the check to run on any acreage-edge (RA) parcel — servicing for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until confirmed with the City. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Not land stock — Silva (presale condos, counted separately).** Silva, by Wanson Group, at 13760 75A Ave — three four-storey buildings with 261 condo homes — is the sub-area's one large new development in the research. It is a presale CONDO project on assembled/developed ground, documented in the condos sibling; it is not a lot offering, is not counted as land stock here, and presale units are never mixed with existing lots. https://rennie.com/developments/silva and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7921-0011-00.pdf
- **Land assemblies / development sites.** No land assembly or development site is named as such in the East Newton research file beyond the Business Park plan area and the Silva site above. Nothing else is presented here as development inventory: UNCONFIRMED beyond those two.
- **Market context, labelled as such.** SnapStats covers Surrey, but it has NO land category — it splits Detached versus "Condos & Townhomes" combined only. For context only (NOT a land figure): East Newton detached inventory was 120 with 7 solds (6% sales ratio) and attached inventory 132 with 11 solds (8%) in September 2026; Surrey community medians were $1,215,000 detached and $512,944 attached. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — current plan only.** The governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026. Bylaw No. 18020 is the old OCP and is never current. The City states the East Newton North NCP was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 update and East Newton South's designations were superseded by it, with that NCP still guiding public amenities and infrastructure; the Business Park designations were superseded the same way. A density claim sourced to an old NCP is sourced to a dead document. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- **Zoning.** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 (zone minimums in Section 2). Confirm the lot's zone and OCP designation in City mapping before valuing any added-unit potential. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it (Jul 8, 2024 framework).** Maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. The City also states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey).** One secondary suite per house under the BC Building Code framework as the City states it; Surrey's suite cap is 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area, one per unit, and a suite is not a separate strata lot. An unpermitted existing suite still needs the permit process. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements.** Subdivision in Surrey proceeds through the City's land development process: after Preliminary Layout Approval / third reading, the developer's engineer submits detailing drawings and the City issues a Servicing Agreement that contractually defines the off-site works and services and the financial securities required for final approval; final approval follows once the signed agreement, fees and securities are in. Lot-specific servicing costs, DCC amounts and timelines for an East Newton parcel: UNCONFIRMED — no figure is stated here because no City source in the research states one for this area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey).** STRs are permitted, owner-only, under a City licence. Licence figure CONFLICT, carried as instructed: the program figure is $358/yr; the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350/yr plus fees. Carry $358 with $350 disclosed, and check the City licence page before quoting either figure to a buyer.
- **Bare-land strata — the form, stated plainly (no East Newton instance verified).** A bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure: the buyer owns a strata lot, and Form B, strata fees and registered bylaws apply to the lot itself. Surrey's documented precedent is Sea Ridge Bays (15875 20 Ave, South Surrey), a bare-land strata documented in the 55-plus program — cross-referenced here for the form only; it is not East Newton stock and is documented in its home area's files, not re-documented here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **BC strata block (applies wherever a bare-land strata lot is the purchase).** Form B Information Certificate: discloses strata fees, amounts owed, approved special levies still to be paid, the CRF balance, parking/storage and an insurance summary; the strata must provide it within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF (strata lots).** Since July 1, 2024, every BC strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years; since November 1, 2023, annual budgets must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022; the only valid age bylaws are 55+ bylaws under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **ALR rules (edge parcels only — no in-area ALR confirmed).** Where a parcel IS in the ALR: residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; the principal residence is capped at 500 m², a secondary suite is permitted within the principal residence only, and one additional residence is permitted without an ALC application up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, subject to City bylaws. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC Assessment farm class (only if an edge parcel claims farm class) — full three-tier form.** Gross annual value of at least $2,500 where the farm area is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha where the area exceeds 4 ha; $10,000 where the area is under 0.8 ha — with the application delivered to the assessor on or before October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an error and is not used here. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land.** PTT is payable on a registered transfer of land unless an exemption applies. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — eligibility turns on occupying a qualifying home, so a bare-lot purchase standing alone must be checked against the BC program rules before any relief is assumed. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief on a new principal-residence home up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000; the Province's exemption expressly includes a manufactured home placed and affixed on vacant land (see the topic file), and a refund route exists where PTT was paid on a vacant lot and a qualifying newly built home is then constructed — confirm the current thresholds and deadlines on the BC source before writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — stated ONLY at the level the CRA source supports.** The CRA states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt, with examples including land kept for personal use; taxable examples include land that is capital property used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts. Sales by developers/businesses and newly subdivided lots beyond that two-part line are therefore generally taxable. Finer points for a specific East Newton lot: UNCONFIRMED — confirm with a lawyer/accountant before writing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax.** The City of Surrey IS a designated taxable area for BC's speculation and vacancy tax — owners of residential property there must complete the annual declaration. Whether a specific vacant lot is taxed, exempt or excluded turns on its property class and the exemption rules: confirm the parcel on the Province's taxable-areas page and declaration guidance. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is a City of Vancouver tax only and does NOT apply in Surrey. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition — bare land.** Secondary sources opened for this run disagree on whether vacant land is inside or outside the prohibition after the March 27, 2023 amendments, and no CMHC/Justice Laws primary text was opened in this pass: application of the prohibition to a specific bare-land purchase in East Newton is UNCONFIRMED — check the CMHC address tool and obtain legal advice before acting. No position is asserted here.
- **First-home-on-land programs — stated precisely.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — but it applies to new HOUSING, not to bare land itself; a land-only purchase does not attract it. The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 from an RRSP. First Home Savings Account: $8,000/year, $40,000 lifetime. Whether a combined land-plus-build arrangement qualifies for housing rebates is transaction-specific: UNCONFIRMED per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsps-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Holding costs / tax dates.** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 (city-wide figures, not area-specific). No area-specific levy was found for East Newton: UNVERIFIED as an area item. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Foreclosure note.** Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry — any foreclosure/court-sale mapping for East Newton is UNVERIFIED and none is asserted here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I actually buy a vacant lot in East Newton?** Rarely as a new-subdivision lot — the research verifies no bare-land subdivision here. The realistic purchase is a fee-simple house lot (usually with a house on it) bought for rebuild or added units, or Business Park ground at the north end. (Section 2 above.)
- **Q: How many units can my lot hold?** Under Surrey's SSMUH framework: 3 units on lots under 280 m², 4 on lots over 280 m², and up to 6 on eligible lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — confirm the lot's size band and bus-stop eligibility with the City before valuing it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Is the lot serviced — water and sewer, or well and septic?** Most subdivision ground is on community services; acreage-edge (RA) parcels need a specific check, because properties not connected to community water and sewer are an SSMUH exemption category. Servicing for a specific lot: UNCONFIRMED until the City confirms it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: What does it cost to subdivide or develop?** The City's process runs through detailing and a Servicing Agreement that sets the off-site works, services and financial securities for final approval. No City source in the research states a dollar figure for this area — any servicing/DCC number quoted without a City estimate for the specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Q: Is my lot in the ALR or a floodplain?** No in-area ALR or floodplain designation was confirmed in the research; the uplands sit east of the Serpentine lowlands. Check the parcel on the ALC maps and the City's flood mapping before subjects. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/ and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **Q: Will I pay GST on the lot?** It depends on the seller and the land's history: the CRA treats most individuals' personal-use vacant-land sales as exempt, and sales in the course of a business, of business capital property, or of a parcel subdivided into more than two parts as taxable. Get the seller's GST position in writing before writing the offer. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Will I pay PTT, and is there first-buyer relief on land?** PTT applies to the land transfer unless an exemption applies; first-buyer and newly-built relief turn on building and occupying a qualifying home (thresholds in Section 3) — do not assume a lot-only purchase is exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Q: Can I hold the lot empty?** Budget the holding costs: Surrey taxes (due July 2, 5% + 5% penalties), plus the speculation and vacancy tax declaration — Surrey is a taxable area, and a vacant residential parcel's position must be declared and confirmed. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Q: Can I add a suite or run a short-term rental while I plan a build?** One suite per house, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area, not a separate strata lot; unpermitted suites are not legalized by SSMUH. STRs are permitted owner-only under licence ($358/yr program figure; $350/yr proposed in the City's 2024 report — verify on the City licence page). (Section 3 above.)
- **Q: Which schools would a future home on this lot feed into?** SD36 schools in the area are Chimney Hill Elementary (14755 74 Ave), M.B. Sanford Elementary (7318 143 St), Tamanawis Secondary (12600 66 Ave) and Panorama Ridge Secondary (13220 64 Ave) — Ministry-listed names only. "East Newton Elementary" DOES NOT EXIST and is never used. Catchment for a specific address is by the SD36 School Locator; a locator result for an East Newton address is UNVERIFIED in the research (the earlier sample address was invalid), so run the exact address before relying on a school. https://www.surreyschools.ca/schools/chimneyhill/
