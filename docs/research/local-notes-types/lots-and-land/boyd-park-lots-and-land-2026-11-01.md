## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000,** covers the area, with Schedule 2 area and sub-area plans addressing neighbourhood issues; no Boyd Park plan exists in that structure — the surrounding ground is planned as Seafair / West Richmond. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **Zoning — Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500:** houses around the park sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1; subzone minimums in Section 2), with RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones in the bylaw as amended. Confirm the parcel's zone and subzone before pricing any split. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states it:** a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in the RS1/RS2 zones — Section 5.4 states a suite minimum of 33.0 m² and a maximum of the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m² — and the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision follows the RS1/RS2 subzone minimums in Section 2. The City's own alphabetical bylaw list carries "Subdivision and Development — Bylaw 8751 (2011)" and "Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 (2011)" (freshly confirmed on the City's bylaw list in the L3 batch) — Richmond's subdivision/servicing instrument is Bylaw 8751, with cost recovery under Bylaw 8752; parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **Flood / dike diligence — Bylaw 8204:** flood construction levels and dike setbacks under the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 apply to new construction; the area sits in the Fraser River floodplain behind the 1:500-design system with dike raising under way. Get the parcel-level position before pricing a rebuild. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Aircraft noise:** aircraft noise policy for Richmond is set out in City Bulletin DEVAPPS-05 and the Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy Area; a street-by-street noise contour finding for the Boyd Park area: UNVERIFIED. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/devapps0534378.pdf
- **Strata block — applies to Apple Greene (built apartment ground), NOT to bare land here:** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (Apple Greene, NWS518, is well over the threshold on either published unit figure — in a 1973–1975 complex its depreciation report and capital-project record are the central diligence items); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (Apple Greene's directory "rentals allowed with restrictions" line is flagged VOID for long-term rentals on that basis; its "pets not allowed" line is a pet bylaw and remains enforceable — confirm both on the Form B); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions — no age restriction for Apple Greene is verified in any source checked, and it is never stated as 55+ in this program; a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Boyd Park ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Boyd Park-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Boyd Park, and the position is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Boyd Park an official neighbourhood I can search by boundary?** No — the City's own area list uses Seafair and West Richmond here; "Boyd Park" means the neighbourhood around Hugh Boyd Community Park at No. 1 Road and Francis Road, and no City boundary for it is published. Search Seafair as well or you will miss stock. https://www.letstalkrichmond.ca/OCP2050-phase2
- **Q: Is there a vacant lot subdivision near the park?** No verified one — the ground is established RS1/RS2 house lots, the park/school/centre site itself, and one built apartment complex. No presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Which schools serve an address near Boyd Park?** UNVERIFIED — for every address, including the park address 9200 No. 1 Road. The locator was never run for this file (an asserted result in the research was downgraded to UNVERIFIED for exactly that reason), and the Seafair file's run for the same address is a single-address result that does not transfer here. Hugh Boyd Secondary School itself is at 9200 No. 1 Road and R.M. Grauer Elementary is at 4440 Blundell Road, but physical location is not a catchment — run the exact address in the SD38 locator before relying on one. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create here?** It depends on the parcel's RS subzone: the Bylaw 8500 Section 8.1 minimums include RS1/K at 315.0 m², RS1/B at 360.0 m², RS1/E at 550.0 m² and RS1/F at 828.0 m², with a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Q: Can a house lot here add a suite or more units?** A secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in RS1/RS2 (33.0 m² minimum; maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling or 110 m²), and the City's Bill 44 framework sets minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots — parcel-specific. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: Is Apple Greene a land or townhouse opportunity?** Neither — Apple Greene (8720–8880 No. 1 Road, NWS518) is a built low-rise APARTMENT condo complex, counted in the condos program. A buyer there buys an apartment-form strata unit; the diligence that matters is the depreciation report and levy history of a phased 1973–1975 complex. Basis: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What flood checks apply to a lot here?** The area averages about 1 metre above sea level in the Fraser River floodplain behind a 1:500-design dike system, and Bylaw 8204 flood construction levels and dike setbacks apply to new construction — confirm the parcel-level position early. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **Q: Is a lot here serviced?** Homes in the area are on City water and sewer services on the research record; confirm servicing for the specific parcel with the City, and note no 2026 utility dollar figure is confirmed. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boyd-park.md)
- **Q: Does facing the park add or subtract value?** Both effects are documented in the research's buying tips: park-facing homes get the fields, courts and pitch and putt — and the sports lighting, weekend tournaments and community-centre / school traffic that come with a City community park and secondary school site. Price the specific frontage, not the label. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=8
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
