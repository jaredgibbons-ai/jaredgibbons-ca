## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current.** Bylaw No. 18020 is never cited as current. The parcel's designation is read in COSMOS, alongside its Fleetwood Plan / Anniedale-Tynehead plan position. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/fleetwood-land-use-plans/fleetwood-town-centre-plan
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000** — parcel zoning confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. No sub-area lot-size table is documented in this research — UNVERIFIED as a table here. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **Fleetwood Plan status:** the plan will guide growth over the next 30+ years as SkyTrain extends along Fraser Highway; Stage 1 was endorsed Mar 7, 2022, an updated Stage 1 was endorsed Oct 16, 2023, further changes have been made to align with the Province's Transit Oriented Areas legislation, and Council will consider the updated plan in 2026 — confirm current zoning and plan status with the City before quoting density for a lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/fleetwood-land-use-plans/fleetwood-town-centre-plan
- **SSMUH / added units (Surrey framework, from Jul 8, 2024):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, on eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary. The 2024 changes renamed RF / RF-SS to R3, RF-12 / RF-12C / RF-13 to R4, and RF-SD to R6 — feed cards here quoting R3 are using a current name. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 12000):** one secondary suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit; a suite is not a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Coach houses and garden suites:** all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access; one extra parking space is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Subdivision / servicing:** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. Verified on the City's own Land Development pages in an earlier program batch: the **Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw, worked through the Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside / riparian:** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and vary by stream class, with the Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit process alongside — read the parcel's class in COSMOS and Part 7A before design work. No single setback figure is stated for this sub-area. East-end acreages additionally carry the drainage context a feed card attributes to a 2024 City drainage report for Anniedale-Tynehead (listing text only, UNVERIFIED in the research). https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only, primary residence, under 90 consecutive days per patron, business licence **$358 per year**. **Conflict disclosed:** the City's 2024 corporate report put the licence at $350/yr plus fees — direct readers to the City licence page before quoting. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **ALR block (applies at the east end where ALR ground exists):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for this sub-area. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot, wherever one surfaces):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% up to and including $200,000, 2% over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example (anchor):** $1,200,000 fully residential parcel, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $1,000,000 = **$22,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Fleetwood Tynehead one neighbourhood or two?** One board sub-area; the City plans the ground under the Fleetwood Plan (Green Timbers in the west towards the ALR in the east), with the Tynehead / Anniedale-Tynehead name at its east end under its own plan process. (§1 above)
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant building lot here?** Yes as individual listings, not as a named subdivision — the feed carries R3-zoned land cards marketed for duplex and single-family builds. Each is checked parcel by parcel in COSMOS for zone, plan status and servicing; no named lot development is verified. (§2 above)
- **Q: What does R3 on a land card actually allow?** R3 is the current (post-Jul 8, 2024) name for the former RF / RF-SS zones. Unit counts still run through the SSMUH bands (3–4 by lot size, up to 6 near a frequent bus stop) and the zone text in Bylaw 12000 — the card's "duplex potential" wording is listing text until the City confirms it for that parcel. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide, or build multiple units instead?** Different permissions: SSMUH does not create new lots; subdivision must meet the zone minimum in Bylaw No. 12000 and servicing under Bylaw No. 8830, confirmed with the City per parcel. (§3 above)
- **Q: How settled is the Fleetwood Plan for a lot near Fraser Highway?** Not fully — Stage 1 was endorsed in 2022 and updated in 2023, Transit Oriented Areas changes followed, and Council was to consider the updated plan in 2026. Price current zoning, and treat future designation as unconfirmed until the updated plan is considered. (§3 above)
- **Q: When does SkyTrain reach Fleetwood?** Fleetwood Station (160 St) and Bakerview-166 Street Station are named project stations on the 16-km extension; the in-service date was not stated on the stations page read in the research and is UNVERIFIED here — price the plan, do not describe SkyTrain as operating. (§1 above)
- **Q: What extra checks does an east-end acreage need?** ALR status (ALC finder), which City plan area it sits in, and drainage — a feed card cites a 2024 City drainage report for Anniedale-Tynehead, but that citation is listing text only. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: Is a lot here fee-simple or strata?** Urban lots are fee-simple by default and no bare-land strata plan here was verified — tenure is still read off title per parcel; a bare-land strata lot, if one surfaces, is strata tenure with Form B, fees and bylaws. A manufactured-home pad in a pad-rental park is not a lot purchase at all. (§2 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a lot purchase here?** PTT on the general tiers — on the §3 anchor example ($1,200,000) the bill is $22,000 with no exemption. GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: How did Fleetwood Tynehead sell in September 2026?** Detached 137 listings, 14 solds, 10%; attached 136 listings, 15 solds, 11% — the third-highest attached ratio in Surrey. All-property figures, community context only; SnapStats publishes no land category and no sub-area medians. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Which school will a home on my lot feed into?** UNVERIFIED for any address here — no locator run is on record, and a listing card naming a school is not a locator result. Run the exact address in the SD36 locator. (§1 above)
