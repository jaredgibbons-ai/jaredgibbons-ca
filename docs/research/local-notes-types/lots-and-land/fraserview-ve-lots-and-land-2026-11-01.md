Fraserview is not one of the City's 22 distinct local areas — it is the southern portion of Victoria-Fraserview, above the Fraser River and around the Fraserview Golf Course lands. ## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market in Fraserview VE — stated plainly at the top, because it is honest. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering was verified on an official or developer source. The district's land trades as fee-simple house lots on the established south-slope streets, and the land-form fact that matters is the R1-1 multiplex ladder that decides what an existing lot can become.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **Established-street house lots:** detached houses on the south-slope streets dominate, around the Fraserview Golf Course lands, with multi-family and strata product toward the Victoria Drive and Fraser Street frontages; the City describes the containing local area as a large area of residential and commercial development. Predominantly freehold on the interior slope streets; the strata/freehold split by street: UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/victoria-fraserview.aspx
- **Lot character from the zone, as stated:** the house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m), 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size; parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Serviced position:** Fraserview homes are on City water and sewer services; a 2026 utility dollar figure confirmed on a City source: UNVERIFIED. Servicing for any single vacant or assembly parcel is confirmed with the City, UNCONFIRMED parcel-by-parcel here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/fraserview-ve.md, §4)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, R1-1 District Schedule:** minimum site areas 306 m² / 464 m² / 557 m² by form as stated in Section 2; a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. Confirm the parcel's district schedule before pricing any multiplex or split. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plan — Vancouver Official Development Plan + Victoria-Fraserview / Killarney community visions:** the citywide Vancouver Official Development Plan replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026, and the Victoria-Fraserview / Killarney community visions work applies to the local area; a Fraserview-specific adopted plan: UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states it:** the R1-1 schedule itself is Vancouver's multiplex frame — up to 6 units, or up to 8 rental units, on a qualifying lot, plus a secondary suite and a laneway house with a single detached house, subject to the minimum site area and frontage for the form. Eligibility is parcel-specific. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — Subdivision By-law No. 5208, fees by No. 14538 (VERIFIED):** Vancouver subdivision is governed by the Subdivision By-law (No. 5208), whose Schedule A sets minimum parcel width and area standards by district and sub-area category, with the Approving Officer administering applications; its fee schedule was most recently amended for 2026 by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026). A fee-simple split must clear both the R1-1 schedule and the Subdivision By-law category for its block — neither is assumed from a neighbouring lot's history. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **Floodplain diligence:** the designated floodplain covers the Fraser River frontage in South Vancouver; parcels in it carry Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements — confirm the specific parcel in the City dataset before pricing the lowest streets. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Fraserview VE ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Fraserview lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — APPLIES here (Vancouver only):** owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the tax is 3% of the property's assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year, and the 2025 payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. The provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax may also apply — stated only as the siblings document it, with no rate imported. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Speculation tax + purchase prohibition beyond the above:** no Fraserview VE-specific position beyond the City tax is documented in this file's research — none imported. The federal non-Canadian purchase-prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Fraserview VE, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Vancouver property taxes:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot subdivision I can buy into in Fraserview VE?** No verified one — the district is established south-slope housing around the golf course; land trades as built house lots and strata units. No presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is this Fraserview the same as Fraserview in New Westminster?** No — this file is Vancouver East, inside Victoria-Fraserview between the Knight Street Bridge corridor and Killarney. New Westminster's Fraserview is a different municipality's sub-area, and its SnapStats rows must never be quoted here. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/victoria-fraserview.aspx
- **Q: How many homes can a Fraserview house lot hold?** Where the R1-1 schedule is met, a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units, and a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house — minimum site areas run 306 m² to 557 m² by form. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Can I split a Fraserview lot into two fee-simple lots?** Only if both resulting parcels clear the R1-1 schedule and the Subdivision By-law No. 5208 Schedule A category for the block, through the Approving Officer — a neighbour's earlier split does not grandfather yours. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm
- **Q: Is Avalon Mews in Fraserview or Killarney?** Both bases exist: the City's 2014 rezoning record treats the Wales Street site as Victoria-Fraserview, while MLS files the built complex under Killarney VE. Program-wide it is QUEUED and counted nowhere — search both sub-area names or you will miss it. (Sibling townhomes file, §2.)
- **Q: Does the Fraser River floodplain affect a lot here?** At the lowest streets, yes — the designated floodplain includes the Fraser River coastal floodplain in South Vancouver, with by-law setback and flood construction level requirements for parcels in it; check the specific parcel in the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: Will I pay the Empty Homes Tax on a vacant Fraserview lot or house?** Vancouver's tax is 3% of assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant, with an annual declaration required — run it into holding costs on any vacant purchase and confirm the seller's declaration status. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Is a Fraserview lot serviced?** District homes are on City water and sewer; confirm servicing for the specific parcel with the City before writing — no parcel-level servicing position is asserted here. (Research file, §4.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Fraserview lot?** By exact address only — no completed locator run is documented in this file's research and the VSB list returned no Victoria-Fraserview rows in this pass, so every catchment is UNVERIFIED until the address is run in the VSB locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
