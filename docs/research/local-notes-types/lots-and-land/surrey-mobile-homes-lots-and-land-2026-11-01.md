## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **MHPTA pad tenancy — the governing Act for the core product:** manufactured home site tenancies are covered by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act; landlords and tenants must have a written tenancy agreement for the site. Assignment on a sale in a park permanently transfers the pad tenancy to the buyer when the home is sold and remains in the park; under MHPTA s. 28 a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an arbitrator's order, or where the tenancy agreement authorises it; before asking, the seller must give the proposed buyer written copies of the park rules and the relevant parts of the tenancy agreement; on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase, and the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Pad rent increases — Act percentages ONLY:** landlords can only increase rent with 3 full months' notice, can increase rent every 12 months, and the increase cannot exceed the yearly rent increase limit; the landlord may also add a proportional amount — the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park — and may apply to the Residential Tenancy Branch for an additional increase for expenses or capital expenditures. **The limits carried in this program are 2.3% plus the proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027.** No dollar pad rent is stated anywhere in this file as fact — a dollar figure for any specific pad comes only from that pad's tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Registration, CSA label and moving:** in BC the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if the transaction is registered; the buyer and seller file the original signed and witnessed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership forms through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the Province directs buyers to make sure the CSA certification label is attached at the point of sale. A home may not be sold unless it has the original legible CSA decal attached; if the label is removed or lost, the home must be re-certified (Technical Safety BC licensed electrical contractor inspection; the electrical approval label can be used in place of a CSA label to list the home). A permit to move a manufactured home is issued by the Manufactured Home Registry, a tax certificate for transport purposes is required (with exceptions including a move to a different pad within the same park), and the transport permit expires 30 days after issue. Manufactured homes include modular and mobile homes, typically built in a factory off-site — modular under CSA A-277, mobile under CSA Z-240. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Taxes on a home in a park:** a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Surrey plan and zoning:** Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 governs Surrey planning; every Surrey property carries a zone under Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000, and a park's zone is confirmed parcel by parcel in the City's COSMOS mapping and the consolidated bylaw text. - **Park age rules vs strata age bylaws — do not conflate:** a park's 55+ restriction is a park rule under the MHPTA set in the written park rules (with assignment consent as its enforcement point) — the Strata Property Act exemption list does not govern it. In a BC strata (the bare-land strata form in §2), by contrast, only a 55-or-older residency bylaw is valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions), rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022, and a "45+" line is NOT a valid age bylaw. That distinction is exactly why Breakaway Bays' directory-vs-MLS conflict is resolved on the MLS basis in §2 rather than by importing a strata rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

Anything finer — including the GST treatment of a manufactured home itself — is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

The GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. Whether any program treats a specific park-home purchase as qualifying housing is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Do I own the land under a Surrey park home?** No: the Province describes park tenancies as owning the manufactured home and renting the site it sits on, under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement governed by the MHPTA; the home's assessed value for tax purposes does not include the land value in a park. That is why pad product is not lot stock in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: Do all Surrey "mobile homes" sit on rented pads?** No. The Surrey feed holds both tenures: park homes where the buyer owns the home and rents the site, and South Surrey strata communities where the buyer owns the land and pays a strata or community fee instead of pad rent — plus manufactured/modular homes on owned lots. Establish the tenure before anything else: the paperwork, financing and monthly costs are different on each form. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-mobile-homes.html
- **Q: Can the park raise my pad rent when I buy?** No — on assignment of the site tenancy the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, and the scheduled date for a subsequent rent increase does not change. Increases otherwise need 3 full months' notice, at most once every 12 months, within the yearly limit plus the proportional levy/utility amount (2026: 2.3% + proportional; 2027: 2.2% + proportional). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What pad rent will I actually pay, in dollars?** UNCONFIRMED in this file — no park's pad rent was confirmed from a park-operator source, and no dollar figure is published here as fact, ever. Get the current rent, in writing, from the tenancy agreement the seller must hand over in the assignment process. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: What is the difference between a mobile home and a modular home in BC?** Both are manufactured homes built off-site, distinguished by their CSA standard: mobile homes under Z-240, modular homes under A-277 — the CSA label on the home states which applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: What if the home has lost its CSA label?** It may not be sold until re-certified: a home may not be sold unless it has the original legible CSA decal attached; if the label is removed or lost, Technical Safety BC arranges a licensed electrical contractor's inspection, and the electrical approval label prepared can be used in place of a CSA label to list the home for sale. Check the label before anything else. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Is any Surrey manufactured-home product on land I would own?** As a form, yes — bare-land strata communities (own the lot as a strata lot, pay strata fees, no pad tenancy) and manufactured/modular homes on fee-simple owned lots are both land-plus-home purchases. No specific community or parcel is verified in this slug's research, so verify per community/parcel from the strata or title documents — and note Breakaway Bays is NOT verified 55+ on the MLS basis this file follows (§2). (§2 above)
- **Q: How does closing work in a park?** Through registration, not just the contract: the purchase is only effective once registered, via a lawyer or notary filing the Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership with the Manufactured Home Registry — plus the landlord's prior written consent to assign the site tenancy under MHPTA s. 28, with no consent fee, and the seller's written park rules and tenancy terms handed over before asking. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Are park 55+ rules the same as strata 55+ bylaws?** No. A park's age rule is a written park rule under the MHPTA, enforced through assignment consent; a strata's 55+ rule is a registered bylaw under SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 with provincial exemptions. Verify park rules from the park, in writing, before removing subjects — no Surrey park's rules were confirmed from an operator source in this research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
