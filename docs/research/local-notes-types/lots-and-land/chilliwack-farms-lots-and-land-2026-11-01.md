## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: Chilliwack farm land is real, substantial land — 67% of the city's land base on the City's own statement — but it trades as whole fee-simple farm parcels under six distinct agricultural zones, NOT as lot product. There is no bare-land subdivision market, no bare-land strata farm form, and no presale LOT offering in this sector; a feed card is a listing, never a confirmed dairy, poultry, berry or forage operation, and no operation of any kind is documented in this file. Subdivision of farm land is the narrow exception (ALC-approved severances and boundary adjustments at agricultural scale), not a lot-creation route.**

- **Fee-simple farm parcels are the only land form.** The research file records farm tenure as overwhelmingly freehold, and that strata farm product is not a Chilliwack pattern (no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source) — on ALR land the strata form is barred in any event (§3). A buyer buys the whole parcel: land, farm buildings, residence position under the Farm Home Plate rules, water position and classification history together. (Research file §5)
- **Farm types by zone, from the official material:** valley-floor farms under the AL (Agricultural Lowland) and AS (Agricultural Small Lot) zones, upland farms under AU (Agricultural Upland), farm-residential parcels under AR (Agricultural Residential), and agricultural commercial and food-processing operations under AC (Agricultural Commercial) and AFP (Agricultural Food Processing) — Zoning Bylaw No. 5000, Section 6 (ss. 6.01–6.06); Section 7 carries the rural zones including R (Rural) and RH (Rural Hillside). A small-lot (AS) purchase and a lowland (AL) purchase are different products with different subdivision and use rules. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Lot character — the bylaw's minimum LOT AREA for a newly created lot, zone by zone (never a typical size):** AL and AU — 7.5 ha where land is excluded or exempted from the ALR or a subdivision is approved within the ALR, 1 ha if created by boundary adjustment to allow the more efficient use of agricultural land, and 0.2 ha if created by homesite severance or a boundary adjustment approved by the ALC (ss. 6.01(2), 6.02(2)); AS — 2 ha where a subdivision is approved in the ALR, 1 ha by boundary adjustment, and 0.2 ha by an ALC-approved boundary adjustment or homesite severance, or by subdivision of ALCA-exempt land served by a community water system (s. 6.03(2)); AR — 0.2 ha where served by a community water system, 1 ha for all other uses (s. 6.04(2)); AC — 2 ha (excluded/exempted or approved subdivision), 1 ha by boundary adjustment served by a community water system, and 0.2 ha by ALC-approved homesite severance (s. 6.05(2)); AFP — 0.4 ha for all uses (s. 6.06(2)). Minimum lot width is 25 m in all six zones. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Bare-land strata: none — not a Chilliwack farm pattern.** No strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source, and on ALR land a strata plan cannot be deposited without ALC permission (§3). If a parcel were ever offered as "bare-land strata" in this sector, that claim would need a strata plan produced — a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure (Form B, strata fees, bylaws) — and none is documented here: UNCONFIRMED / none found. (Research file §5)
- **Presale LOT offerings: none — counted separately, and the count is 0.** No presale or lot-release product of any kind is documented for Chilliwack farm land in the research; the sector's offerings are resale parcels appearing in a generic feed. (Research file §§1–2)
- **ALR vs non-ALR split:** the City states properties in the ALR are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALR regulations, ALC approval may be required before the City can approve related development applications, and an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, for placing fill or removing soil, for non-farm uses, and for subdivision or a boundary adjustment, with ALC applications referred to the City for review. No city-wide ALR percentage is stated in the research (the 67% figure is agriculture's share of the land base, NOT an ALR share) — no split figure is invented; status is parcel-by-parcel. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Water — a diligence item built into the land:** under the Water Sustainability Act, groundwater used for non-domestic purposes such as irrigation requires a water licence and payment of water fees and rentals before the water can lawfully be used; domestic groundwater use does not require authorization. A farm parcel's irrigation position is part of what is being bought, and it is verified from the licence record, not assumed from the presence of a well. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- **Septic / onsite sewage:** Fraser Health states a home or building with indoor plumbing that is not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, and only an Authorized Person (a Professional Engineer or a Registered Onsite Wastewater Practitioner) can design, install, repair, maintain or inspect a sewerage system. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems

(Research file §4)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Chilliwack 2050, Bylaw No. 5500**, carrying the Agricultural Area Plan (created as part of the OCP in 2012 with the Chilliwack Agricultural Commission; ARAC monitors implementation). The OCP also includes **Development Permit Area 4 – Farm Home Plate** (below). https://www.chilliwack.com/ocp and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, Section 6 farm zones** — AL, AU, AS, AR, AC and AFP, with the minimum lot areas and 25 m minimum width in §2. Read the exact agricultural zone, not just "farm": the six zones carry different purposes, subdivision routes and use rules. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Farm Home Plate (Zoning Bylaw + OCP DPA 4):** in the AL, AR and AC zones the Farm Home Plate is capped, as maximum lot coverage, at 4,000 m² for the principal residential use and 1,000 m² for an additional residential use (ss. 6.01(5), 6.04(5), 6.05(5)); the dwelling must be sited no farther than 70 m from the front or exterior side lot line with no part of the plate more than 80 m from that line, or no farther than 50 m from the rear lot line with the plate within 60 m (ss. 6.01(8), 6.04(8), 6.05(8)); and the Farm Home Plate requirements do not apply where a lot is under 0.8 ha (ss. 6.01(10), 6.04(10), 6.05(10)). The AU, AS and AFP sections state no Farm Home Plate figure. A Development Permit is required when the Farm Home Plate regulations in the Zoning Bylaw are not met (OCP Development Permit Area 4 – Farm Home Plate, to reduce the long-term impact of residential uses on agricultural land). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **SSMUH as the City states it:** Chilliwack's SSMUH amendments were adopted as **Bylaw 5395 on June 18, 2024** (program standing frame for Chilliwack, Nov 1, 2026). SSMUH is an urban residential-lot framework; it does not create subdivision or added-residence rights on Section 6 farm land, where the ALR and Farm Home Plate rules in this section govern instead. The Province granted Chilliwack an extension for part of the Proper neighbourhood and surrounding Hazel Park (new compliance date December 31, 2030), which is not farm-sector ground. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **Subdivision:** all subdivisions are subject to the City's Approving Officer and the Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055 — and on farm land the operative gates are the Section 6 minimums in §2 and, in the ALR, ALC approval for subdivision or boundary adjustment. There is no urban-style lot-split route on a Chilliwack farm parcel. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=653 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **ALR block — full form:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified — depositing a strata plan causes a subdivision (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)). Residence caps: land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted **only inside** the principal residence; one additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and City of Chilliwack bylaws must also permit it. Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form:** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income requirements at farm gate prices — $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha; farm applications must be submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. A parcel that keeps farm classification must keep meeting the threshold for its size — a new owner who plans to continue farming must apply by October 31. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Quota / marketing-board items: UNVERIFIED — stated plainly.** No marketing-board source was opened in this research, so nothing in this file states or implies anything about supply-managed quota (dairy, poultry, egg or otherwise), quota transferability, board licensing or board approval on any Chilliwack farm purchase. A buyer whose plan depends on quota needs the relevant board's own material and advice — a feed card's barn or flock description is not quota evidence, and no card is treated here as a confirmed operation in any named sector. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/chilliwack-farms.md — method note, §1)
- **Strata block — background only, governing no farm stock:** if a strata lot were ever lawfully produced on non-ALR ground in this sector, the BC block would apply to the LOT — Form B (SPA s. 59) within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. No strata farm product is documented (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-time buyers' exemption: no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — both are home exemptions, and their application to a bare farm parcel purchase is facts-specific: UNCONFIRMED for bare land in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. Working-farm sales sit on the business side of that analysis as a general matter, and farmland has its own CRA treatment — the GST position on any specific Chilliwack farm parcel is UNCONFIRMED until the vendor's status and the parcel's history are established. Get it in the contract, not from the listing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — new housing, NOT bare land or a farm parcel. The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Chilliwack property taxes (city ground):** a 5% late-payment penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026, per the City's property tax FAQ — a mid-year farm buyer inherits whichever step has landed on an unpaid balance, and farm classification (§3) is the parcel-level tax treatment to protect through closing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How much of Chilliwack is farmland?** The City states agriculture accounts for 67% of the City's total land base and 29% of its economic activity, and its OCP carries an Agricultural Area Plan for that land — that is agriculture's share of the land base, not an ALR percentage. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Q: What zones cover Chilliwack farms?** Section 6 of Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 sets six agricultural zones: AL (Agricultural Lowland), AU (Agricultural Upland), AS (Agricultural Small Lot), AR (Agricultural Residential), AC (Agricultural Commercial) and AFP (Agricultural Food Processing) — with different minimum lot areas (§2) and different use rules per zone. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: Can I split a farm parcel into building lots?** No urban-style split exists here. A newly created lot must meet its zone's Section 6 minimum — 7.5 ha at the AL/AU subdivision gate, down to 0.2 ha only through an ALC-approved homesite severance or boundary adjustment — and in the ALR, subdivision or a boundary adjustment may require an ALC application first, referred to the City for review. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Chilliwack?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; Chilliwack also applies its Farm Home Plate rules (OCP Development Permit Area 4; plate caps of 4,000 m² principal / 1,000 m² additional in the AL, AR and AC zones) to residential building on agricultural land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Q: Can a Chilliwack farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and City bylaws must also permit it; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. The City lists a new house, coach house or suite among the items that may need an ALC application. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Does a farm listing come with quota?** UNVERIFIED — and never assume it. No marketing-board source was opened in this research, so this file states nothing about quota, quota transfer or board approval for any operation; a listing card is not a confirmed operation in the named sector and is not quota evidence. Verify any quota position with the relevant marketing board before writing. (FILE STATUS block; research file method note)
- **Q: What keeps the farm tax treatment in place?** BC Assessment farm classification, which requires the operation to keep meeting its size-tier gross income threshold ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha) at farm gate prices, with the application submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year. Sellers should document the farm income history that supports the classification — it is one of the first things a farm buyer's lender and lawyer ask for. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Is the water for irrigation included?** Only if the licence position supports it: non-domestic groundwater use such as irrigation requires a water licence (with fees and rentals) under the Water Sustainability Act, while domestic use is exempt. Confirm the licence record for the parcel — wells alone prove nothing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- **Q: Does GST apply when I buy a farm?** It turns on the seller and the parcel's history, per CRA GI-003: an individual's personal-use land sale is generally exempt, while a sale in the course of a business or of a parcel subdivided into more than two parts can be taxable. A working farm is a business-side purchase as a general matter — the specific position is UNCONFIRMED until established, and belongs in the contract. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Why are there no market numbers in this file?** Chilliwack is a CADREB market. The SnapStats reports held for this program cover the Fraser Valley and Greater Vancouver boards only, publish no farm category, and contain no Chilliwack figures — so any Chilliwack or farm-specific statistic is UNVERIFIED here rather than borrowed from a neighbouring board. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The Farm Home Plate has an under-0.8 ha escape hatch — and three zones with no plate figure at all.** The plate caps and siting rules in §3 do not apply where a lot is under 0.8 ha, and the AU, AS and AFP zone sections state no Farm Home Plate figure — a buyer who applies the AL-zone plate numbers across all farm land is misreading the bylaw. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **The 0.2 ha lot is real — but only through the ALC door.** Across the AL, AU, AS and AC zones, the smallest newly created lot (0.2 ha) arises only by ALC-approved homesite severance or boundary adjustment (or, in AR, simply by being on a community water system). Anyone marketing a farm parcel on its "severance potential" without an ALC position is selling the exception as the rule. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Fill is an ALC item, not a landscaping decision.** The City lists placing fill or removing soil among the items that may require an ALC application on ALR land, and the ALC caps fill for a principal residence built without an application at 1,000 m² — groundworks planned before closing can need approval first. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
