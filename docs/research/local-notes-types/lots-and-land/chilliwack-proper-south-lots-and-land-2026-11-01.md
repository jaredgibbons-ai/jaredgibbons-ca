## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — frame carried with its research caveat:** the program's standing position is that Chilliwack's current plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500. This slug's own research records that the live page attributes the area's policies to a "Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan" and Downtown/Proper infill policies, and that the plan name and its application to this area were not confirmed on a City page in that pass: the bylaw frame is carried here from the program's city-wide verification (see the Proper East file), and the per-area application caveat from this slug's research is stated, not smoothed. A parcel's designation is a per-parcel City check. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Zoning: Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** — the parcel's actual zone is confirmed with the City per parcel; no zone label is adopted for any specific Proper South lot from listing text (the Nowell Street card's R1-C claim included — see §2). Farm zones sit in §6 of Bylaw 5000 where ALR/farm ground arises. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH (Bylaw 5395, adopted Jun 18, 2024):** the City's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment to Bylaw 5000 allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide and does not create new lots. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Secondary suites and accessory units (Bylaw 5000):** a secondary suite is an accessory dwelling unit of not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building — the lesser of 90 m² and 40% (s.2); only 1 ADU may be located on a lot, and the permitted types include secondary suite, coach house and garden suite (s.4.06(1)); garden suites cap at a maximum gross floor area of 100 m²; ADU parking is addressed in s.5.04. The City's Secondary Suites Policy permits rental of suites within any single detached dwelling regardless of zone; a suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level under the Floodplain Regulation Bylaw. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **Subdivision / servicing — process documented, bylaw number held UNCONFIRMED:** the Chilliwack servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in this program (held — never borrowed from another municipality), so the process is what is documented: subdivision creates or changes lot boundaries; all subdivision plans require approval by the City's Approving Officer under the Local Government Act and Land Title Act before Land Title registration, ensuring adequate lot size and shape, parkland dedication as necessary, essential services in place, adequate access, protection of natural features and preservation of future subdivision opportunities; a Preliminary Layout Approval is the first major milestone (typically 8–12 weeks from application, valid 12 months) and sets out the servicing, engineering and legal steps to final approval. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8208
- **Flood diligence:** flood hazard zoning exists in the bylaw structure (RS-FHA reserve zone, §12 of Bylaw 5000) and the City applies flood protection requirements and a Floodplain Regulation Bylaw; new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. Parcel-level flood construction levels for Proper South lots: UNVERIFIED in the research — confirm with the City before pricing suite or infill potential. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Short-term rentals (Chilliwack):** STR operations are regulated under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141, which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968 — confirm a property can be licensed under it before valuing any rental income from a lot purchase. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **ALR block (provincial frame — no sub-area ALR parcel is documented in the research, stated so it is not misapplied):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for this sub-area. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot, wherever one surfaces):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw, and no Chilliwack park or strata is stated as verified 55+ on listing text alone in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** a $700,000 fully residential parcel, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Chilliwack property taxes (2026):** notices mailed at the end of May, due the first working day after July 1; a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
