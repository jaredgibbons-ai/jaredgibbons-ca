## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current.** Council adopted Surrey 2050 on June 15, 2026; Bylaw No. 18020 is the replaced predecessor and is never cited as current. A finer City planning breakdown of this sub-area's ground under the OCP and its secondary plans: UNVERIFIED in the research pass (429) — the parcel's designation is read in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000** — parcel zoning, designations and servicing are confirmed per parcel in the City's COSMOS mapping. Zone minimum subdivision lot areas come from the consolidated bylaw text; no sub-area lot-size table is documented in this research — UNVERIFIED as a table here. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units (Surrey framework, adopted Jul 8, 2024):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, on eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide and does not create new lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 12000, as verified in the program's rules-gaps pass):** one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit; no 90 m² cap was found in the current consolidated bylaw text; and a suite is not a separate strata lot. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Coach houses and garden suites (rules-gaps verified):** all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; the unit must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house; one extra parking space is required. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Subdivision / servicing:** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, confirmed per parcel in COSMOS (which also shows water/sanitary/storm mains, road dedications and environmental or riparian permit areas). The research file states no servicing-bylaw number; verified on the City's own Land Development pages in this pass: the **Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw, worked through the City's Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. No other city's number is used for Surrey. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside / riparian — the creek diligence item for this sub-area:** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, with different setbacks applied to various types of streams, complemented by the Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit (SEDP) process — a parcel near Bear Creek or another watercourse is checked against its stream class in COSMOS and Part 7A, and no single setback figure is stated for the sub-area because the bylaw sets them by class. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** the program's rules-gaps verification confirms Surrey STRs are permitted, owner-only — only the property owner may host from their primary residence, tenants cannot operate one, for periods of less than 90 consecutive days per patron, with a business licence at **$358 per year**. **Conflict disclosed:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposing the STR amendments put the licence at $350/yr plus fees — $358 is carried with $350 disclosed. Note the research file for THIS slug could not open the City's STR page in its own pass (429) and stated no STR rule; the rule carried here is the program's verified Surrey frame, sourced to the rules-gaps pass, not to this slug's research. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R094.pdf and (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **ALR block (provincial frame — no sub-area ALR parcel is documented in the research, stated so it is not misapplied):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for this sub-area. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot, wherever one surfaces):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** $1,215,000 parcel (the Surrey community detached median for Sep 2026, arithmetic illustration only — NOT a sub-area or land price), fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $1,015,000 ($20,300) = **$22,300**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Bear Creek Green Timbers one area or two?** One board sub-area with a compound name — a single sub-area of the Surrey community in the SnapStats data, joining the Bear Creek Park ground (which the City places in Whalley) with the Green Timbers Urban Forest ground. - **Q: Can I buy a vacant building lot here?** No named bare-land subdivision or lot offering was verified in this sub-area. What trades as land is the existing fabric — detached house lots, including suite-heavy houses and the occasional lot marketed to builders in listing text — each checked parcel by parcel in COSMOS. (§2 above)
- **Q: What creek or park diligence applies to a lot near Bear Creek Park?** The streamside check: Surrey's setbacks sit in Part 7A of Bylaw No. 12000 and vary by stream class, with the Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit process alongside — read the parcel's class and permit-area position in COSMOS before design work. No single setback figure is stated for the sub-area, because the bylaw does not set one. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can a house lot here add a suite or a coach house?** One secondary suite per dwelling unit, capped at 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area and never inside a coach house or garden suite; a coach house or garden suite of 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR, with lane access required on smaller lots and one extra parking space). Verify a suite's permit status rather than relying on listing text. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a lot here, or build multiple units instead?** Different permissions: SSMUH allows 3–4 units on most lots by size band (up to 6 near a frequent bus stop), but that does not create new lots; subdivision must meet the zone minimum in Bylaw No. 12000 and the servicing requirements under the Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830, confirmed with the City per parcel. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is a lot here fee-simple or strata?** The detached fabric is fee-simple by default, and no bare-land strata plan in this sub-area was verified — but tenure is read off title per parcel, and a bare-land strata lot, if one surfaces, is strata tenure with Form B, fees and bylaws. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: Does the coming SkyTrain change what a north-edge lot is worth today?** Green Timbers Station (140 Street and Fraser Highway, on the westernmost edge of Green Timbers Urban Forest) is a named project station with an anticipated in-service date of 2029 — price the plan, and do not describe SkyTrain as operating. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/ and https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/surrey-langley-skytrain
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a lot purchase here?** PTT on the general tiers — on the §3 worked example ($1,215,000, illustration only) the bill is $22,300 with no exemption. GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: How did this sub-area sell in September 2026?** Slowly, in both splits: detached 93 listings, 5 solds, 5% ratio — near the bottom of the Surrey set — and "Condos & Townhomes" 37 listings, 3 solds, 8%. Those are all-property figures, community context only; SnapStats publishes no land category and no sub-area medians. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
