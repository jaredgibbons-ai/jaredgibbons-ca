- Queensborough is the City of New Westminster's island neighbourhood on the eastern tip of Lulu Island, comprising District Lots 757 and 758 in the Zoning Bylaw wording, bordered by the Fraser River (main arm) to the south and east and the North Arm to the north, and sharing Lulu Island with the City of Richmond. ## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Queensborough has no standing vacant-subdivision market — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community and no presale LOT offering was verified. What trades as land here is (1) fee-simple house lots on the island's older streets, governed by the Zoning Bylaw's Queensborough sewer-connection subdivision rule (s.190.36) and by flood construction levels on every parcel; (2) the built strata fabric of Port Royal at the north end and the Ewen Avenue corridor — built lot fabric, documented once in the townhomes sibling, not lot offerings; and (3) Port Royal as a City-named development area — a planning designation, not a lot program. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — Our City 2041, Bylaw No. 7925 (2017), with the Queensborough Community Plan as a schedule:** the Queensborough Community Plan governs the area together with the Queensborough Eastern Neighbourhood Node Master Plan; the (RLR) Residential – Low Rise designation in the OCP amendment materials (Bylaw No. 8574, 2026) provides compact-lot single detached form, low-rise buildings up to six storeys, and stacked and side-by-side townhouses. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Draft_Queensborough_Land_Use_Designations_and_Map___March_9_2026.pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 6680 (2001):** the Queensborough-specific rule is s.190.36 — no subdivision in District Lots 757 and 758 may produce a lot under one-half acre (0.20 ha) or with frontage under 66 feet (20.12 m) unless the land can connect to the City sanitary sewer system. City-wide, the townhouse districts are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B ONLY (RT-3 is the district recorded for the Jardine Street townhouse site above) — RT-A/RT-B DO NOT EXIST in this bylaw. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/100_Introduction(2).pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — VERIFIED:** New Westminster **Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw No. 7142, 2007** regulates the subdivision of lands and establishes the standard of works and services to be provided in respect of subdivisions and developments (effective March 12, 2007; consolidated September 25, 2023). All subdivisions require approval by the City's Approving Officer, and new lots must be adequately serviced with water, sewers, underground electrical, communications, street lighting, roads, sidewalks, street trees and other City requirements, meet the Zoning Bylaw's minimum lot size and frontage requirements, and be consistent with the OCP. On Queensborough ground the s.190.36 sewer-connection test sits on top of that frame: an unsewered parcel cannot be subdivided below the half-acre / 66-foot floor at all. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/7142_Subdivision2023.pdf and https://staging.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/General%20Guide%20to%20Subdivision%20Process%202012-Final.pdf
- **Floodplain diligence — THE defining item for Queensborough ground (Zone 1, per the sources):** Queensborough is **Floodplain Zone 1** in the City's floodplain study: a low-lying area with high groundwater, poor compressible soils and poor drainage, protected by a shared dyking and pumped drainage system with Richmond; the BC dike inventory lists Map 5, New Westminster Queensborough, with the City of New Westminster as diking authority. In the 1960s a perimeter dyke was built protecting approximately 350 hectares; Queensborough has 350 hectares of designated floodplain and is the highest priority for flood protection improvements in the city, and both Queensborough and sections of the mainland are included within the provincial floodplain. (The "Zone 3" wording in this batch's brief is NOT followed — see the FILE STATUS block: Zone 3 belongs to the Quay and Downtown NW files.) https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf and http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- **Flood construction levels — the numbers every Queensborough lot is read against:** the City's Flood Hazard Development Permit Area sets minimum construction levels of **3.53 metres above Geodetic Survey of Canada datum for Area A** (generally downstream from Derwent Way on the south side and Wood Street on the north side) and **4.20 metres above GSC datum for Area B** (upstream of Derwent Way and Wood Street) — new buildings in Queensborough should be constructed at an elevation sufficient to minimize loss in the event of dyke failure or an extreme flood topping the perimeter dykes. Confirm the specific lot's area (A or B), its compliance and an insurance quote before subjects come off. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Flood_Hazard_DPA.pdf
- **Dike work on the record:** the City received $800,000 from the provincial Community Emergency Preparedness Fund for the Queensborough Dike Shoreline Protection Rehabilitation project, restabilising eroding dike slopes at two locations on the northeast side of Lulu Island, behind 345 Gifford Street and behind 805 Boyd Street — for lots near those reaches, confirm the dyke-side position early. https://www.newwestcity.ca/2023/02/14/city-of-new-westminster-receives-provincial-funding-for-queensborough-dike-rehabilitation.php
- **Strata diligence (island strata stock):** for strata purchases, review depreciation and contingency under the provincial strata framework early; BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. The full BC strata block applies to any strata lot: Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — Queensborough ALR status is UNVERIFIED in the sources read):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Queensborough lot purchase alone earns no rebate.
