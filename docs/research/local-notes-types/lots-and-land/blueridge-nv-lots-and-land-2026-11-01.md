## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No meaningful bare-land market exists in Blueridge — stated plainly at the top. The pocket trades as established fee-simple single-family lots on the Berkley Road / Hyannis Drive grid, protected as predominantly single-family by the Seymour Local Plan, with no named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering verified on any official or developer source. The land diligence that matters here is the zone minimum ladder, the SSMUH classification, and the Berkley escarpment landslide check.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default (and only) form

- **Established detached lots:** detached houses on District detached residential zones across the Blueridge and Seymour Heights streets — predominantly freehold detached in the sources read; any specific strata product inside Blueridge is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed on an official source). Attached forms concentrate near Parkgate Village and along Mount Seymour Parkway, outside this pocket. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/SLP_2003_6.0.pdf
- **Lot character from the zone ladder, as stated:** District Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525 (Bylaw 8799) — R1 minimum lot 4,000 m² with 30 m width and 34 m depth; R2 minimum lot 660 m² with 18 m width and 34 m depth; modifiers R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m, R2(S2) at 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m. These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size; the zone on a single lot is confirmed through the District's GeoWeb. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Serviced position, as the District states it:** District single-family utility fees in 2026 include water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60, with combined single-family utilities of $2,571.20 in 2026 — the serviced position for District lots as the District states it. OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets Development Permit Areas including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard, and the District publishes property-level GeoWeb reports including modelled flood construction levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-1-district-landslide.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — District Zoning Bylaw No. 3210, Section 525 (Bylaw 8799):** the R1/R2 minimum-lot ladder in Section 2; an R2 lot allows a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house. Since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **OCP — District Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 7900:** the District plan covering the area, including Schedule B Development Permit Areas (Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard, Slope Hazard); the Seymour Local Plan sits under it for this community and protects Blueridge's single-family character. The City of North Vancouver's OCP 8400 is a different municipality's plan and is never imported here. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **SSMUH as the District states it:** the District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop — check the lot's classification before marketing density, because the Seymour Local Plan's limited-growth position still shapes what is realistic here. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — District servicing-bylaw number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** no District of North Vancouver subdivision/servicing bylaw number is confirmed on a primary source in this file's research, so none is quoted and none is invented; the City of North Vancouver's Bylaw 8014 is a City bylaw and is expressly NOT the District's — it is named here only as that exclusion. The process is documented instead: subdivision potential runs off the parcel's R1/R2 zone minimum (Section 525), servicing is District water and sewer/drainage (2026 single-family utilities $2,571.20 combined), and hazard development permits (below) gate escarpment, creek-side and slope work. Confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the District for the specific parcel. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **Hazard Development Permits (District frame — the live Blueridge checks):** OCP Schedule B permit areas — slope, creek, streamside, wildfire and natural-environment protection — with the Berkley escarpment landslide record (January 19, 2005 slide; proactive management since; Landslide Risk Assessment updated 2026) as the first check on Berkley-side lots; pull the GeoWeb hazard layers and budget for a geotechnical report before pricing a renovation or rebuild. http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-1-district-landslide.pdf
- **Strata block (for completeness — no Blueridge bare-land strata stock is verified):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Blueridge ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Blueridge?** No verified one — the pocket is established fee-simple houses under a plan that protects single-family character, and no bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How small can a lot be subdivided?** By zone: R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m width is the smallest minimum, standard R2 is 660 m² / 18 m / 34 m, and R1 and R2(S3) sit at 4,000 m² / 30 m. The parcel's zone is confirmed through District GeoWeb. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Q: What hazard check matters most on the Berkley side?** Landslide. District work names the Berkley escarpment for landslide risk, managed proactively since the January 19, 2005 slide and updated in the 2026 Landslide Risk Assessment — pull the GeoWeb hazard layers and expect a geotechnical report. http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-1-district-landslide.pdf
- **Q: Can a Blueridge lot hold a suite and a coach house?** Yes — since June 2024 a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite plus a coach house (three units total) is permitted on single-family zoned lots within the Urban Containment Boundary, and the SSMUH classification can allow more by lot size and bus-stop distance. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve my address?** UNVERIFIED at address level — Blueridge Elementary (2650 Bronte Drive) and Seymour Heights Elementary (2640 Carnation Street) both sit in the pocket and Windsor Secondary (931 Broadview Drive) is the Seymour secondary beside it, but the boards reviewed establish no catchment for a Blueridge address; check the exact address with SD44 and never name one elementary from the street alone. https://blueridge.sd44.ca/
- **Q: Is Dorothy Lynas the neighbourhood school?** No — Dorothy Lynas Elementary, 4000 Inlet Crescent, is the Seymour-area French Immersion choice program, offered district-wide by registration regardless of catchment. https://www.sd44.ca:443/registration/french-immersion/?month=09&year=2026&search=month
- **Q: What does servicing cost on a District lot?** The District's 2026 single-family utilities total $2,571.20 (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60, plus the balance of the combined figure as the District states it). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What transit serves the pocket?** Route 214 through Berkley Road and Carnation Street to Phibbs Exchange, some trips through to Burrard Station, and the R2 RapidBus one-seat to Metrotown since September 7, 2026. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt214.pdf
