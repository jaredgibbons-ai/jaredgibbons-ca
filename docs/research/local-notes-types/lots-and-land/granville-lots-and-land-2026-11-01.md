## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**South Granville has no meaningful bare-land market — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community and no presale LOT offering was verified. Land here is fully built corridor ground: fee-simple side-street house lots priced partly on R1-1 multiplex math, commercial / mixed-use frontage parcels priced partly on Broadway Plan station-area policy, and a large rental-apartment landholding stock that trades as whole assets, never by lot. Everything named below is built product, heritage fabric or plan policy — never a lot for sale.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, R1-1 (side streets, via By-law No. 13817) + commercial / mixed-use (frontage):** R1-1 allows up to 6 strata units, or up to 8 secured rental units, on former single-family lots, subject to the district schedule (306 m² / 464 m² / 557 m² minimum site areas by form; secondary suite and laneway house with a house). The Granville Street frontage sits under commercial / mixed-use districts; a corridor strata example, The Avedon, is recorded in its listing as zoned RM-3. Exact schedule per frontage parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plan — the Broadway Plan + Vancouver Official Development Plan:** the Broadway Plan (Clark Drive to Vine Street, 1st to 16th Avenues; approved June 22, 2022; updated December 2024) sets the station-area heights in Section 2 — policy, not zoning. Citywide direction otherwise comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. Distinguish plan policy from zoning before pricing any corridor lot on tower density. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-broadway.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Subdivision — Subdivision By-law No. 5208, fees by No. 14538:** Vancouver subdivision is governed by the Subdivision By-law (No. 5208), whose Schedule A sets minimum parcel width and area standards by district and sub-area category, administered by the Approving Officer; its fee schedule was most recently amended for 2026 by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026). A fee-simple split of a side-street lot must clear both its district schedule and its Subdivision By-law category — neither is assumed from a neighbouring lot's history. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states them:** the R1-1 regime above IS Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit framework for these streets — suite and laneway permissions and the 6-unit / 8-rental-unit multiplex ceilings are what price the side-street lots around the corridor's strata stock. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Heritage / character diligence (documented for this corridor):** the three Sixteenth & Burrard houses are designated protected heritage property, and Douglas Lodge and Crescent Court sit on the City's Heritage Register — heritage rules ride on top of whichever tenure title shows, and character-home retention rules must be confirmed before marketing "development potential" on a side-street lot. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-vancouver-heritage-register.pdf and http://council.vancouver.ca/20171114/documents/phea20171114min.pdf
- **Strata diligence (the corridor's strata stock — and any bare-land strata lot, if one is ever met here):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page) — in this corridor the Form B is also the fastest way to prove a building is a strata at all, since the rental towers have no Form B to give; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. No corridor building counted in the siblings is published as 55+ (Terraces on 7th's 55+ position belongs to the Fairview files). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no corridor ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%, Vancouver only:** every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration; homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year are taxed at 3% of assessed taxable value, and the 2025 payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — directly relevant to this corridor's second-home and investor stock. Confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. The City tax is separate from the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, which may also apply. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Speculation tax + purchase prohibition:** no South Granville-specific speculation-tax position is documented in this file's research beyond the City tax and the siblings' "may also apply" note — none imported. The federal non-Canadian purchase-prohibition position for corridor land is UNCONFIRMED in this file: the research cites no prohibition source for this ground, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Vancouver property taxes:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot or a new lot subdivision in South Granville?** No verified one — the corridor is fully built. Land trades as built house lots, frontage parcels and whole rental buildings, and no presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Does the Broadway Plan mean my lot is zoned for 30–40 storeys?** No — the Plan contemplates buildings up to 30–40 storeys near the future South Granville Station (40-storey maximum in the Granville/Burrard Slopes – Area A policy), but the Plan is policy, not zoning, and most higher-density forms still require rezoning. Do not price a lot as if tower density were already zoned. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-broadway.pdf
- **Q: Can a side-street house lot be redeveloped as a multiplex?** Generally yes where the R1-1 schedule is met — By-law No. 13817 allows up to 6 strata units (or 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots, subject to the district schedule; heritage-designated properties carry additional rules. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **Q: Why do so many South Granville "apartment buildings" never have units for sale?** Because they are purpose-built rentals held as whole assets: The Leeward is a 48-suite rental tower (1968) and Santa Monica a 13-suite rental building (1929), and the West 11th–16th / Fir–Birch district is documented as an intact pre-WWII rental apartment neighbourhood. A building name on the Heritage Register is not evidence of strata tenure. https://goodmanreport.com/sold-properties/1184-the-leeward-1686-w-12th-avenue-vancouver/ and http://heritagevancouver.org/top10-watch-list/2007/3-south-granville-apartment-district/
- **Q: Can I split a side-street lot into two fee-simple lots?** Only if both resulting parcels clear the district schedule and the Subdivision By-law No. 5208 Schedule A category for the block, through the Approving Officer — a neighbour's earlier split does not grandfather yours. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm
- **Q: Is any corridor land bare-land strata?** None verified — the corridor's strata stock is building-form strata. If a bare-land strata lot were offered, it would still be strata tenure: Form B, strata fees and bylaws would apply to the lot itself. (Section 2.)
- **Q: When does the SkyTrain reach South Granville?** The Broadway Subway, including South Granville Station, is scheduled to enter service in fall 2027 on the Province's published date — underwrite to that date rather than to "someday." https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025TT0110-001048
- **Q: A listing far south on Granville Street is filed "South Granville" — is it this corridor?** Not necessarily: MLS files addresses as far south as 6160 Granville Street and 7128 Adera Street (Kerrisdale) under "South Granville." This file's ground ends at West 16th Avenue; verify the civic address, not the sub-area label. (Sibling file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/south-granville-condos-2026-11-01.md.)
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003); the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not the bare lot. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a corridor lot?** By exact address only — L'École Bilingue Elementary is located in Fairview at 1166 West 14th Avenue, but located-in is not catchment and no completed locator run is documented for this ground. Run the address in the SD39 locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
