## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Seafair has no meaningful bare-land market — stated plainly at the top. There is no standing vacant-subdivision market, no verified bare-land strata plan, and no presale lot offering here. Land trades as fee-simple house lots on the established streets; the land-form facts that matter are the RS1/RS2 subdivision ladder in Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 and the flood/dike framework in Bylaw 8204. The area's two strata townhouse complexes and its one rental apartment building are built ground, documented below as pointers only — none is a lot offering.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **Established-street house lots:** overwhelmingly freehold detached houses in Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with secondary suites under Section 5.4 and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones (RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L, RSM/XL) in the bylaw as amended. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Lot character from the zone ladder, as stated:** minimum lot areas by subzone include RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m², with a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones. These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size; the zone and dimensions of a specific lot are confirmed parcel by parcel. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Serviced position, as the City states it:** Seafair homes are on City water and sewer services. A 2026 utility dollar figure for a Seafair house confirmed on a City source: UNVERIFIED. Septic or well use in Seafair: UNVERIFIED. Servicing for any single vacant or assembly parcel is confirmed with the City. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seafair.md)
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** no ALR parcel inside the Seafair planning area is confirmed in the research; ALR status is a property-specific check through the Agricultural Land Commission. No ALR percentage split is stated. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/

The Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements — a core diligence item on every Seafair lot. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000,** covers Seafair (planning area 6), with Schedule 2 area and sub-area plans addressing neighbourhood issues; a Seafair-specific area plan name or approved major project: UNVERIFIED. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **Zoning — Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500:** houses sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1; subzone minimums in Section 2), with RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones in the bylaw as amended. Confirm the parcel's zone and subzone on the City record before pricing any split. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states it:** a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in the RS1/RS2 zones — Section 5.4 states a suite minimum of 33.0 m² and a maximum of the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m² — and the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision follows the RS1/RS2 subzone minimums in Section 2. The City's own alphabetical bylaw list carries "Subdivision and Development — Bylaw 8751 (2011)" and "Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 (2011)" (freshly confirmed on the City's bylaw list in the L3 batch) — Richmond's subdivision/servicing instrument is Bylaw 8751, with cost recovery under Bylaw 8752; parcel-level standards are not quoted here and are confirmed with the City for the specific lot. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **Flood / dike diligence — Bylaw 8204:** the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements; the area sits behind a 1:500-design dike system with an active raising program (3.5 m to 4.7 m). Get the parcel-level flood construction level and any dike right-of-way position before pricing a rebuild or a split. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **Aircraft noise:** aircraft noise policy for Richmond is set out in City Bulletin DEVAPPS-05 and the Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy Area; a street-by-street noise contour finding for Seafair: UNVERIFIED. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/devapps0534378.pdf
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Seafair ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Seafair lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Seafair-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Seafair, and the position is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot subdivision I can buy into in Seafair?** No verified one — Seafair is an established residential planning area; land trades as built house lots and strata units. No presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create here?** It depends entirely on the parcel's RS subzone: the minimums stated in Bylaw 8500 Section 8.1 include RS1/K at 315.0 m², RS1/B at 360.0 m², RS1/E at 550.0 m² and RS1/F at 828.0 m², with a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones. Confirm the subzone before pricing any split. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Q: Can a Seafair house lot add a suite or more units?** A secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in RS1/RS2 (33.0 m² minimum; maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling or 110 m²), and the City's Bill 44 framework sets small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots — eligibility is parcel-specific. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: What does subdivision servicing run through in Richmond?** The City's bylaw list carries the Subdivision and Development Bylaw 8751 (2011) and the Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 (2011) — servicing works are required through the subdivision process under that framework, with costs recovered as those bylaws provide. Parcel-level standards: confirm with the City. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **Q: Is a Seafair lot serviced?** Seafair homes are on City water and sewer services on the City's record; confirm servicing for the specific parcel with the City before writing, and note no 2026 utility dollar figure is confirmed on a City source in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seafair.md)
- **Q: What is the flood position on a west-side lot?** The lot sits in the Fraser River floodplain behind a 1:500-design dike system (dikes being raised from 3.5 m to 4.7 m), and Bylaw 8204 flood construction levels and dike setbacks apply to new construction — get the parcel-level position early. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **Q: Is any of Seafair in the ALR?** No ALR parcel inside the planning area is confirmed in the research. ALR status is a per-parcel check on ALC mapping; if a parcel were in the ALR, subdivision would require ALC approval and the residence caps in Section 3 would apply. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Are Edgewater Park or Seafair West lots I can buy?** No — both are built strata townhouse complexes (Edgewater Park, 3031 Williams Road, NWS438; Seafair West, 3088 Francis Road). A buyer there buys a strata lot in a built complex, with Form B diligence — not a bare-land lot. Home file: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/seafair-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **Q: Which schools serve a Seafair lot?** By exact address only — the one settled locator run in this file (9200 No. 1 Road → Diefenbaker Elementary → Boyd Secondary) does not transfer to other streets. Run the address in the SD38 locator. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
