## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001:** slope houses sit in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series, s.310) — RS-1: minimum site 557.40 sq. m. (6,000 sq. ft.), FSR not over 0.5. **Townhouse districts in the bylaw are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B ONLY — RT-A/RT-B do NOT exist** (standing citywide correction; this file's own research carries no RT-A/RT-B wording to correct, and the 836.10 m² figure that travels with that error elsewhere is an RM apartment-district density threshold, not an RT figure). https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **OCP — "Our City 2041," Bylaw No. 7925, 2017:** the area is governed by Our City 2041; the OCP Green Zone includes the Esplanade (North Arm) and the Annacis Channel shoreline. No North Arm area plan was confirmed on a City source. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the city states it:** no North Arm-specific SSMUH or multiplex position is documented in this file's research — none is imported from other New Westminster files. What the research does state is the RS frame in Section 2 (RS-1 minimum site and FSR); any added-unit or split potential for a slope parcel is a parcel-level City confirmation, UNCONFIRMED here.
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — Bylaw No. 7142, 2007 (VERIFIED):** subdivision is regulated by the Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw No. 7142, 2007, which requires Approving Officer approval and ensures new lots are adequately serviced and meet the Zoning Bylaw's minimum lot size and frontage requirements, through the City's pre-application / application / Works & Services Agreement process. Parcel-level standards are confirmed through that process; none is quoted here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/7142_Subdivision.pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/General%20Guide%20to%20Subdivision%20Process%202012-Final.pdf
- **Foreshore + flood diligence:** the City floodplain study covers west-end Fraser River land in Floodplain Zone 2 and the North Arm is Fraser River foreshore — confirm the parcel's floodplain zone, any foreshore / water-lot tenure, and the insurance position before subject removal. The City publishes flood management information for its low-lying zones. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf and http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no North Arm ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a North Arm lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is **Vancouver ONLY — it does NOT apply here** (North Arm is in New Westminster). No other North Arm-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in the research — none imported. The federal non-Canadian purchase-prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for North Arm, and the position is not conformed from other files.

New Westminster operates its own electrical utility. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php
