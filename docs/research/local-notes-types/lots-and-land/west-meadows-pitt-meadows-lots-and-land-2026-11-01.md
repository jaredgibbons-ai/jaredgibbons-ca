## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- West Meadows is the part of the City of Pitt Meadows, BC, in Metro Vancouver, west of Harris Road: the Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK) lands, large farm parcels along Ford Road, Fraser Way and Airport Way, and the Pitt River and Fraser River shorelines. "West Meadows" is the Paragon sub-area name as given (code UNVERIFIED); it is not an official City neighbourhood name, and no official City planning-area name for the west side was found in City sources. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport
- Boundaries: no official boundary is published. Working limits only, from the research file: Harris Road to the east, the Pitt River to the west and north-west, the Fraser River to the south, and Lougheed Highway to the north, taking in Airport Way, Ford Road and the west end of Fraser Way, including the airport lands and large farm parcels; exact limits of the MLS sub-area are not confirmed. Nearby: Central Meadows to the east across Harris Road, Mid Meadows to the south-east, North Meadows across Lougheed Highway to the north, and Port Coquitlam across the Pitt River to the west. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- Main roads and transit: Airport Way, Ford Road, Fraser Way and Harris Road serve the area; Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) runs along the north edge, and Golden Ears Way / Golden Ears Bridge connects north toward Maple Ridge and south to Langley and Surrey. Route 722 runs via Harris Road and Ford Road on its west-side segment, continuing as route 719; routes 701, 791, the R3 RapidBus and the West Coast Express connect at Pitt Meadows Station and the Harris Road stop on Lougheed Highway, east of the area. Commute times in minutes: UNVERIFIED. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt722.pdf
- Civic anchors: Harris Landing, at Harris Road and Fraser Way, is the start of the Pitt River Greenway (about 10.2 km west along the Fraser and Pitt River dikes); Fraser Way Roundabout Park is at 19501 Fraser Way. No recreation centre, library branch, shopping centre or grocery store inside the sub-area was verified — the Family Recreation Centre (12027 Harris Road) and the city's only library branch (200-12099 Harris Road) sit just east of Harris Road, and the nearest confirmed shopping is Meadowtown (19800 Lougheed Highway, Real Canadian Superstore) and Meadowvale (Harris Road, Save-On-Foods). Nearest hospital: Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/harris-landing-pitt-river-greenway and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/dmlive-155673-v1-gpra_report_to_pitt_meadows_20190405_v2_pdf.pdf
- Schools: no public school building is located inside this sub-area on the district list. SD42 catchments are address-based and SD42 has no middle-school tier — elementary K–7, secondary 8–12. The research file documents an SD42 School Locator result for the sample address 19452 Fraser Way only: Davie Jones Elementary (K–7) and Pitt Meadows Secondary (8–12) — Pitt Meadows Secondary, 19438 116B Avenue, is the city's only public secondary school. ## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**West Meadows is the one Pitt Meadows sub-area in this batch with a genuine bare-land market — and it is a FARM market, not a subdivision market. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan, and no presale LOT offering was verified. What trades as land here is the large fee-simple farm parcel under A-1 / A-2 zoning on ALR ground — 78% of the city's land base is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, and this sub-area is where that percentage lives — plus, at the east edge, fee-simple house lots in the Fraser Way / Wildwood Crescent pocket. The sub-area recorded 1 detached listing and 0 sales, and 0 condos & townhomes listings and 0 sales, in September 2026: a thin, lumpy land market by the data.**

### Fee-simple farm parcels — the sub-area's real land stock

- **Farm parcels along Ford Road, Airport Way and Fraser Way:** farm houses on large agricultural parcels under A-1 General Agriculture and A-2 Large Lot Agricultural zoning, the main zones in the sub-area. A-2 protects large-lot farming areas under regulations consistent with the Agricultural Land Commission Act. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Lot character from the zones, as the bylaw states it:** A-1 minimum lot area **for subdivision** 8 ha; A-2 minimum lot area for subdivision 16 ha (per the city-assembly file's reading of the same bylaw). A-1 allows one farm house per lot (maximum gross floor area 500 m²), one additional farm house for farm operations over 8 ha and under 40 ha, and two if 40 ha or more. These are zone rules, not a claim about any existing parcel's size. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf and Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **ALR vs non-ALR, as documented:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states 78% of Pitt Meadows land falls within the ALR; the large farm parcels in this sub-area are zoned A-1 / A-2 under rules consistent with the Agricultural Land Commission Act, and a specific property's ALR status is confirmed on ALC maps. No finer West Meadows-specific ALR percentage is stated in the research. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Servicing — the farm-parcel check:** septic and well servicing details for individual farm parcels are UNVERIFIED in the research; wells are checkable in the provincial Groundwater Wells and Aquifers (GWELLS) application, and a home not connected to a community sewer must have an onsite system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation. Do not assume urban servicing on A-1 / A-2 ground. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Siting:** A-1 zoning requires residential buildings sited 15.0 m from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches — a buildable-envelope constraint on ditch-crossed farm parcels. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Flood position:** over 95% of the city land base is in the Fraser / Pitt floodplain behind 60 km of dikes, plus ditches, pump stations and flood boxes (City 2020 Business Plan — governing figure; live-page "over 85%" conflict flagged at the top and in Section 5); the Kennedy drainage area (Area 3) in this part of the city discharges to the Alouette, Pitt and Fraser Rivers. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Airport ground is not lot inventory:** Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK), at 1028-18799 Airport Way inside this sub-area — three paved runways and a water runway, owned and operated by the Pitt Meadows Airport Society — is operating airport land, documented here as the sub-area's dominant land use neighbour, never as for-sale land. No airport noise-zone map covering the sub-area was verified (research UNVERIFIED item). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 (A-1 / A-2 agricultural zones as the main zones here; RS / R-3 / R-4 / R-6 in the east-edge pocket), under the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," OCP Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022 (repealing OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007), under which agriculture is the dominant land use in the city. Small-scale multi-unit housing under OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3034, 2025 covers secondary suites, garden suites, duplexes, triplexes, townhomes and small apartment buildings (2–6 units) where it applies. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/media/8989 and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **ALR rules — the core land rules in this sub-area:** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, on top of the A-1 (8 ha) / A-2 (16 ha) bylaw minimums; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha) — the research file states the ALC position directly: one additional detached residence without an application where there is one existing residence when construction begins, to a maximum of 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less, and local government may further restrict it. Note the zoning layer differs in form (A-1 additional **farm** houses for farm operations over 8 ha — Section 2): a farm-residence thesis must satisfy BOTH the ALC caps and the A-1 rules. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — three-tier, never flat:** BC Assessment farm-class thresholds in full three-tier form, as carried in the city-assembly file ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Farm-class status drives the tax picture below, so confirm the parcel's class and production position before quoting carrying costs. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §3.
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision follows the zone minimums in Section 2 (A-1 8 ha; A-2 16 ha; RS 0.2 ha; R-3/R-4/R-6 1,215 m² in the east-edge pocket), plus ALC approval for any ALR split. A servicing-bylaw number could not be confirmed on a primary source in the research — held UNCONFIRMED (a fresh search for this batch found the City's Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw named in City minutes, with an Amendment Bylaw No. 2833, 2019, but no base-bylaw number on a primary source, so no number is quoted); servicing detail beyond the minimums, the 15 m siting rule and the septic/well position in Section 2 is UNCONFIRMED and is confirmed with the City parcel by parcel. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Floodplain — the defining lot rule:** **over 95%** of the land base lies within the Fraser River and (to a smaller degree) Pitt River floodplain (City 2020 Business Plan — the figure these notes retire to), protected by a 60 km dike system, under Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 (per the city-assembly file). LIVE CONFLICT: all five Pitt Meadows guide pages state "over 85%" (City Financial Plan 2026–2030 attribution) — flagged at the top, never silently resolved. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Short-term rentals: PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows** — "Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Zoning Bylaw" (City staff report), and Pitt Meadows is in the Province's principal-residence-requirement community list. No West Meadows lot or farm stay thesis may be priced on STR income. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Airport diligence:** YPK is inside the sub-area (three paved runways and a water runway), owned and operated by the Pitt Meadows Airport Society, with a City page for safety and noise concerns and a Fly YPK Neighbourly Program (per the city-assembly file) — disclose aircraft activity early and check flight paths against the specific address; no public source quantifies noise for a specific address, so any specific noise claim is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport
- **Bare-land strata rules (no such stock verified here; stated so the form is never mis-sold):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 with May 1, 2023 exemptions. A listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a subdivision where I can just buy a lot in West Meadows?** No — no named bare-land subdivision and no presale lot offering was verified. What trades is the large A-1/A-2 farm parcel and the east-edge house lot. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How much of this area is farmland, and what does that mean for a purchase?** The city figure is 78% ALR (City 2020 Business Plan), and this sub-area is the farm side of the city: on ALR ground, subdivision needs ALC approval on top of the A-1 (8 ha) / A-2 (16 ha) minimums, and an ALR residence cannot be stratified. Confirm the specific parcel on ALC maps first. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Q: Can I split a big farm parcel?** Only through both gates: the zone minimum (A-1 8 ha, A-2 16 ha) AND ALC approval for any ALR split. Neither gate can be promised from this file — get zone and ALR status confirmed for the parcel. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Q: How many homes can a farm parcel carry?** Two layers, both capped: zoning A-1 allows one farm house (max 500 m²), one additional farm house for farm operations over 8 ha and under 40 ha (two if 40 ha or more); the ALC caps a principal residence at ≤500 m² with a suite within the principal only, and an additional residence at 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). A second-home thesis must satisfy both. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Are the Fraser Way townhomes West Meadows stock?** On this file's geography reading, the research file places them here; on the BOARD classification, which this file follows, Shoreline (19452 Fraser Way) and Coho II (19572 Fraser Way) are South Meadows, counted in the mid-meadows townhomes file. They contribute nothing to West Meadows land totals either way — they are built strata, not lots. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/west-meadows-pitt-meadows-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **Q: Is my parcel in the floodplain?** Almost certainly — **over 95%** of the city land base is floodplain behind 60 km of dikes. Check the flood construction level under Bylaw No. 2384, 2008, the 15.0 m ditch/watercourse siting rule in A-1, any covenant on title, and insurance early; budget the drainage levy at the farm-class rate if the parcel is Class 9. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Q: Is a farm parcel serviced?** UNCONFIRMED parcel by parcel — septic/well detail for individual farm parcels is not in the research. Check wells in GWELLS, confirm any community-sewer connection, and require the onsite system to comply with the Sewerage System Regulation where there is no connection. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Q: Does the airport affect a Fraser Way or Airport Way purchase?** YPK is a working regional airport inside the sub-area — three paved runways and a water runway (floatplane traffic is part of the soundscape). Disclose it early, use the City's noise-concern process page, and visit at a busy hour; no source quantifies noise for a specific address. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy farmland?** At CRA GI-003 level: developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable; an individual's personal-use land sale is generally exempt. Farm transactions have finer points that are UNCONFIRMED here — confirm per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a West Meadows parcel?** By exact address only — no school building sits inside the sub-area, and the one settled locator sample (19452 Fraser Way → Davie Jones Elementary → Pitt Meadows Secondary) does not transfer to Ford Road or Airport Way addresses. Run the address in the SD42 locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The floodplain number is a live conflict, not a rounding difference (flagged in every Pitt Meadows file in this batch).** The City's 2020 Business Plan states **over 95%** floodplain; the superseded OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007 era put it at about 86%, and all five Pitt Meadows guide pages now state "over 85%" sourced to the City Financial Plan 2026–2030. These notes retire to 95% and flag the conflict as a Jerry item. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Ford Road is two sub-areas at once:** MLS sources class Ford Road addresses near 122 Avenue as Central Meadows, while the road's south-west reach reads as West Meadows farm country — and the Ford Road co-op's own board sub-area is UNCONFIRMED from public sources.
