## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Barnston Island is a Fraser River island between Surrey (Port Kells) on the south bank and Pitt Meadows across the main Fraser channel; there is no bridge — the only access is the free Barnston Island Ferry across Parson's Channel from 104 Ave in Port Kells, a barge-and-tug provincial inland ferry, capacity 5 vehicles and 50 passengers, running on demand roughly 6:15 a.m.–11:55 p.m. (to 12:55 a.m. weekends), with crossings that can be interrupted by river traffic. There is no bus service on the island itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- The island's main road is its perimeter dike road — the dike rings the island, protects it as low Fraser River floodplain, and doubles as the transportation route; Dyke Road addresses run around it (the SD36 locator test range was 2–328 Dyke Rd). https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- Legal geography, restated because everything else runs on it: most of the island is unincorporated Metro Vancouver Electoral Area A; the southeast portion is Barnston Island Indian Reserve No. 3 (Katzie First Nation), which is federal reserve land and not fee-simple market housing. Nearby areas: Port Kells (Surrey) at the ferry landing; Pitt Meadows across the main channel (no direct connection); Katzie's main reserve in Pitt Meadows. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Barnston Island part of Surrey?** No — for no purpose. It uses Surrey mailing addresses, but the island is unincorporated Metro Vancouver Electoral Area A plus Katzie IR 3; services, taxes, building permits and planning run through Metro Vancouver and the Province, not Surrey City Hall. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **Q: Can I buy a small lot and build a house on Barnston Island?** Not on a small-lot pattern: most of the island is agricultural land under an OCP vision of a farming community, building is tied to farm parcels and ALR rules (principal ≤500 m², suite inside it only, one additional residence 90 m² at ≤40 ha / 186 m² above 40 ha), and subdivision in the ALR needs ALC approval — a parcel-by-parcel check with Metro Vancouver and the ALC is essential. (§§2–3)
- **Q: Are there any bare-land strata lots or subdivision lots for sale as a program?** None verified — no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata offered as lots, or presale lot offering is documented in this slug's research; land is large fee-simple farm parcels traded individually. (§2)
- **Q: How do residents get on and off the island?** Only by the free Barnston Island Ferry from 104 Ave in Port Kells — about 5 minutes across Parson's Channel, on demand within operating hours (roughly 6:15 a.m.–11:55 p.m., to 12:55 a.m. weekends), capacity 5 vehicles and 50 passengers. It does not run all night, and crossings can be interrupted by river traffic. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- **Q: Who collects my property tax, and what happens if I pay late?** The Province, under the rural system — notice from the Surveyor of Taxes, deadline July 2, a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance July 3 and a further 5% on Nov 1; Metro Vancouver and school levies ride on top. This is NOT any City of Surrey schedule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/important-dates
- **Q: Is a parcel on Katzie IR 3 the same kind of purchase as a fee-simple lot?** No — IR 3 is federal reserve land with different tenure, financing and resale rules than fee-simple lots; it is not fee-simple market housing and is not counted as land stock here. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **Q: Is the island in a floodplain?** Yes — it is a low Fraser River island protected by a perimeter dike that also serves as its main road, and Metro Vancouver maintains a flood construction level study for the island; confirm dike condition, flood construction level and insurance availability for the specific parcel early. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **Q: Where do island kids go to school?** School District 36 serves the island; children ride the ferry to their assigned schools — the settled locator chain for the island test address 52 Dyke Road is **Bothwell Elementary → École Fraser Heights** (SD36 School Locator, run 2026-11-01). Port Kells Elementary serves landing-side addresses, not island addresses. School-bus boarding priority: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- **Q: Are there any market statistics for the island?** No. No board sub-area named "Barnston Island" was found and SnapStats FVR has no Barnston row; Surrey community figures describe a different ground and are not used as island figures here — any conflict between a Surrey row and the island's legal geography is stated in §1, reconciliation UNVERIFIED, no market claim made.
