## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- There are no streets or boundaries for this file: the research file's scope is a buyer's guide to purchasing a manufactured home (the term BC legislation uses; "mobile home" is the everyday term) anywhere in British Columbia — "there is no MLS sub-area for this guide and no single municipality — the rules below are the provincial framework, with municipal zoning varying by city." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes (Research file §1)
- The guide is built around two ownership forms, which this land file expands to the three ground-holding forms in Section 2: (1) you own the home and rent the pad (site) it sits on in a manufactured home park — the land is the park owner's; (2) you own both the home and the land it sits on — as a fee-simple owner or as a bare-land strata lot owner. https://jaredgibbons.ca/how-to-buy-a-mobile-home-in-bc-guide.html (Research file §1)
- Which law applies follows the ground, not the home: the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land you own is dealt with through the land title system instead, and a bare-land strata lot is governed by the Strata Property Act. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- Terminology: a manufactured home is factory-built to the CSA standard and registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the registry/CSA-label rules in Section 3 are what separate a registrable manufactured home from an unregistered unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry (Research file §1)
- Market numbers for manufactured homes: UNVERIFIED, by design — the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (this program's only stats source) reports detached and attached (condos & townhomes) housing and does not report manufactured homes as a property type, and it has NO land category in any case. No figure is substituted. (Research file §2)
- Zoning for the ground is municipal, not provincial: there is no province-wide park zoning — for example, Surrey's Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 has a Manufactured Home Residential Zone (RM-M) permitting manufactured home parks, and Maple Ridge's Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019 has an RMH Manufactured Home Park Residential zone. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7915-0417-00.pdf (Research file §4)

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**This topic file carries NO land stock of its own — named land developments counted here: 0; bare-land strata communities counted here: 0; fee-simple parcels counted here: 0.** A province-wide "list of lots" would be incomplete by definition. What follows is the ground-holding map the topic genuinely needs — the three forms, each documented as its own form, plus the one named bare-land strata precedent carried from the sibling program with its basis stated and NOT counted here.

### (a) Pad rental in a manufactured home park — you own the home, you do NOT own land

- The buyer owns the manufactured home and rents the site under a pad tenancy agreement; the land is the park owner's, and the MHPTA is the governing statute. There is no land title, no strata lot and no Form B in this form. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- On a sale, the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is ASSIGNED to the purchaser, with the park owner's prior written consent (RTB Form 10; response by the 10th day or consent is deemed given; no fee may be charged for consent). Before consent is even requested, the seller must give the proposed purchaser the written tenancy agreement and the written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- Pad rent rises by formula, once every 12 months on at least 3 full months' notice: the inflation rate (BC all-items CPI) plus a proportional amount (the park's change in local government levies plus regulated park utilities, divided by the number of sites). The verified formula figures for this program are 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027, per the RTB framework in the research. **NO pad-rent DOLLAR figure is published in this file** — no official or operator source states one, and live-page dollar ranges are UNVERIFIED site copy, flagged as such only where a file's own research documents them; this topic's research documents none, so none is flagged and none is quoted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- The ground risk in this form is the park's future: conversion of all or a significant part of a park requires all permits/approvals in place first, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation payable on or before the notice's effective date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- Tax follows the split: a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential owners, but the assessed value does NOT include the land's value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home

### (b) Bare-land strata lot — strata tenure; the lot IS the purchase

- In a bare-land strata, the buyer owns a strata lot and the manufactured home sits on land the buyer owns as that lot. Strata law — not the MHPTA — is the frame: Form B, monthly strata fees and registered bylaws apply TO THE LOT, and there is no pad rent. Stated plainly wherever this form appears in this program: a bare-land strata lot is strata tenure, full diligence applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Named precedent — Sea Ridge Bays, 15875 20 Ave, Surrey (South Surrey).** A 55+ manufactured-home community that is a bare-land strata, carried from the sibling 55-plus topic file with its basis stated: MLS property data records "Age Restricted 55+" in its bylaw restrictions, and listings state "NO PAD FEES" and that as a bare-land strata "YOU OWN THE LOT," with a monthly strata fee instead of pad rent. It is documented ONCE as a community in its home program files (55-plus) and is cross-referenced here for the FORM only — counted as land stock nowhere in this file. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/how-to-buy-a-mobile-home-in-bc-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md, §2. https://realtylink.org/en/print/property/263120958
- Age rule note: the only valid strata age bylaws are 55+ bylaws (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions) — which is why a 55+ bare-land strata like Sea Ridge Bays is a lawful form, while a pad-rental park's 55+ rule is a park designation rule instead (at least one tenant on the site must be 55 or older, per the Province). Two different statutes produce a similar-looking "55+" label: do not merge them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes

### (c) Fee-simple land with a manufactured home on it — full land ownership

- The buyer owns both the home and the land on a fee-simple title, dealt with through the land title system. There is no pad rent, no park owner and no strata — municipal zoning, building/siting rules and title charges govern the land, and the home itself must still satisfy the registry/CSA requirements in Section 3 to be a registrable manufactured home. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- Siting/CSA position, stated at the level the sources support: the home must carry its CSA certification label at the point of sale and the transfer is only effective when registered in the Manufactured Home Registry (filing through a lawyer or notary: original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership). Municipal siting, foundation and permit requirements for placing a manufactured home on fee-simple land vary by municipality and are UNCONFIRMED at topic level — confirm with the municipality for the specific parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- Tax in this form: the home is taxed as an improvement on land the owner holds (the park exclusion in (a) does not apply), and a manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for annually. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant

- Co-operative holding (e.g., Pioneer Park, Aldergrove, in the 55-plus program): the land is held through the co-op and the buyer holds a co-op interest, not a pad tenancy, strata lot or fee-simple title. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND (TOPIC FORM)

- **Registry first, all three forms.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. A missing label is a red flag for financing and resale in every form. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Pad-form rules (form (a) only).** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change them only with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies fairly to all tenants, is clear, is properly noticed and does not change a material term. On assignment the purchaser steps into the agreement on the same terms from the assignment date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Strata-form rules (form (b) only) — the BC strata block.** Form B Information Certificate (fees, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). A bare-land strata lot with a clubhouse/serviced common property carries exactly the assets a depreciation report prices — ask for the current one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Fee-simple-form rules (form (c) only).** No strata documents and no pad tenancy: zoning (municipal), siting/permit rules and title govern. Where the land is in the ALR, the ALR block also applies: residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; principal residence ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). BC Assessment farm class, full three-tier form: $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 (<0.8 ha); applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" statement is an error. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Property transfer tax — stated ONLY at the level the BC source supports.** The Province's newly built home exemption from PTT expressly includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) as a newly built home — relevant to form (c) buyers placing a NEW manufactured home on their own land (full relief to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 under that exemption). For a RESALE home on a rented pad (form (a)), the purchase is of the home and an assigned tenancy, not a registered transfer of land: at exactly that level, it is generally not a PTT transaction on land. Finer points — bare-land strata lot transfers (form (b), which DO transfer a registered strata lot), partial interests, and any specific transaction's treatment — are UNCONFIRMED here and must be confirmed against the BC PTT guidance / a lawyer before writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST — stated ONLY at the level CRA sources support.** For the land forms: the CRA treats most individuals' sales of vacant land kept for personal use as exempt, with taxable examples including land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and parcels created by subdividing into more than two parts — directly relevant to a form (c) lot bought from a subdivider vs an individual. GST treatment of the manufactured HOME itself (new vs resale, builder status) is a separate analysis: UNCONFIRMED at topic level in this file — confirm per transaction. The FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself; whether a new manufactured home placed on owned land qualifies is transaction-specific: UNCONFIRMED per transaction. New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year — applicability to a pad-form purchase (no land) must be confirmed per program rules before it is promised to a buyer. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy taxes + federal prohibition — topic level.** Both turn on the specific municipality, property class and buyer status, and this topic has no single area: no area position is imported here. A fee-simple or bare-land strata home in a designated BC speculation and vacancy tax area (e.g., Surrey, Township of Langley) carries the annual declaration; a pad-form home's position differs again. Confirm for the specific property/municipality — UNCONFIRMED at topic level beyond that statement. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Financing.** Lender and mortgage-insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home treatment, age and foundation requirements) were not confirmed on an official source in the research pass: UNCONFIRMED — confirm financing for the specific home AND its ground form before removing subjects; the same home can finance differently in forms (a), (b) and (c). (Research file §4.)
- **Zoning/siting for placing a home.** Municipal only: parks sit in park zones (e.g., Surrey RM-M; Maple Ridge RMH); placing a manufactured home on fee-simple land is governed by that municipality's zoning and siting rules — no province-wide designation exists and none is asserted. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7915-0417-00.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: If I buy a mobile home in a park, do I own the land?** No. You own the home and rent the site under a pad tenancy agreement; the MHPTA applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on. The exceptions prove the rule: in a bare-land strata such as Sea Ridge Bays you own the lot as a strata lot, and on fee-simple land you own the parcel outright. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes (Research file Q1)
- **Q: What is a bare-land strata lot, in one sentence?** A strata lot that is land rather than a built unit: you own the lot, pay strata fees instead of pad rent, and get the strata document set (Form B, bylaws, depreciation report) for the lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Q: Can the park owner refuse my purchase?** The assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent — but the owner must respond by the 10th day, silence is deemed consent, no fee may be charged, and unreasonably withheld consent can go to dispute resolution. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf (Research file Q2)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up — and what is pad rent, in dollars?** By formula only: once every 12 months, 3 full months' notice, inflation (2.3% for 2026; 2.2% for 2027, per this program's verified RTB figures) plus the park's proportional levy/utility amount. No dollar figure is published in this file because no official/operator source states one — ask the park for the current pad rent and its increase history in writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes (Research file Q3)
- **Q: What happens if the park is redeveloped?** Permits first, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may leave early on at least 10 days' written notice. Check the municipality's development applications for the park's address before buying. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes (Research file Q4)
- **Q: Do I pay property transfer tax?** On a pad purchase: generally not a PTT transaction on land, at the level stated in Section 3. On owned land: the newly built home exemption expressly covers a new manufactured home placed and affixed on vacant land. Bare-land strata transfers and finer points: UNCONFIRMED — confirm before writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Q: How is the purchase registered?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered; a lawyer or notary files the Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA label must be attached at the point of sale. Land forms (b) and (c) ALSO involve the land title system for the lot/parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry (Research file Q5)
- **Q: What are my holding costs in each form?** (a) Pad rent (formula-capped increases) + property tax on the home only (land value excluded from the assessment in a park). (b) Strata fees + CRF/levies + tax on the lot and home. (c) Municipal tax on land + home, plus servicing costs. No dollar amounts are stated — none is published on an official source at topic level. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Q: Can I move my home to land I buy later?** The topic sources confirm the registry/CSA and municipal-zoning framework, but moving, siting and permitting a specific home onto a specific parcel is municipality- and home-specific: UNCONFIRMED at topic level — confirm CSA status, age/condition acceptance and siting permits before buying either the home or the land for that plan. (Research file §§4–6.)
- **Q: Does the park's 55+ rule work the same as a strata's 55+ bylaw?** No — a park designation requires at least one tenant on the site to be 55 or older under the tenancy framework; a strata 55+ rule is a registered bylaw under SPA ss. 123.1/123.2. Similar label, different statute, different enforcement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The consent clock is the deal clock in a park purchase.** Ten days, in writing, on RTB Form 10 — and silence works FOR the buyer (deemed consent). Subject dates written without that window are written wrong. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Pad rent rises by formula with two moving parts, so two parks in the same city can lawfully post different increases in the same year** — the inflation component is province-wide (2.3% for 2026; 2.2% for 2027 in this program's verified figures), but the proportional amount depends on the individual park's levy/utility changes divided by its site count. And neither park's increase can be checked against a published dollar rent, because none exists on an official source. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **"You own the lot" is the single most valuable sentence in this topic — and it only appears in one form.** Sea Ridge Bays' listing language ("NO PAD FEES… YOU OWN THE LOT") exists precisely because pad buyers do not own their ground. When a listing for any manufactured home does not say which of the three forms it is, that omission is the first question, before price. https://realtylink.org/en/print/property/263120958
