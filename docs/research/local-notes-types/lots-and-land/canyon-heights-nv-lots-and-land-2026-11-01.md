## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: Canyon Heights has no meaningful bare-land market. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community, no named strata or townhome complex, and no presale LOT offering was verified inside Canyon Heights on any source checked — the research file and all three siblings record the same position. What trades as land here is the individual fee-simple house lot in the District's R1 and R2 detached zones, almost always with a detached house on it, bought for the lot, the plateau setting and suite/coach-house potential. No lot count or lot price is stated.**

### Fee-simple lots — the only land form

- **R1 / R2 lot fabric:** detached houses under Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525 (Bylaw 8799), on the Highland Boulevard plateau between the Capilano River and the Mosquito/Mackay creek ravines. Section 525 states the R1 zone minimum lot is 4,000 m² with 30 m width and 34 m depth, and standard R2 is 660 m² with 18 m width and 34 m depth, with modifiers R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m, R2(S2) at 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m. These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size — the zone for a specific lot is confirmed through the District's GeoWeb. A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Suites as lot value:** an R2 principal building may include a secondary suite, and a coach house is a dwelling unit accessory to and detached from the principal dwelling unit; since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. A suite or coach house is part of the house lot's ownership, not a separately saleable lot. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Bare-land strata: none verified.** No bare-land strata plan in Canyon Heights was confirmed in the research or the siblings — the research records strata product in Canyon Heights as none confirmed on an official source. Stated plainly for any strata lot form, if one ever surfaced: a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — the buyer owns a strata lot, pays strata fees, is bound by the strata bylaws, and diligence runs through the Form B (Section 3) — but no such stock is documented here. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP + zoning (District only):** District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900 (including its Schedule B Development Permit Areas), and Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525 (R1/R2 minimums in Sections 1–2). A Canyon Heights-specific plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). The City of North Vancouver's OCP 8400 / Zoning 6700 belong to the CITY ONLY and are never imported here. https://dnv-docs.simplicitycms.ca/documents/ocp-schedule-b-dpa.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units, as the District and Province state them:** three units total (principal, secondary suite, coach house) are permitted on single-family zoned lots within the Urban Containment Boundary since June 2024. The District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop; provincial rules set 3 units on lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on larger lots within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop. Verify the parcel's classification before pricing in unit potential. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there vacant land for sale in Canyon Heights?** No meaningful bare-land market is documented — no vacant-lot subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified. "Land" here is an existing fee-simple R1/R2 house lot, usually with a house on it. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How small a lot can I create here?** It depends entirely on the parcel's zone and modifier: standard R2 allows a 660 m² minimum lot, R2(S1) goes to 335 m², while R1 and R2(S3) sit at 4,000 m². Confirm the zone and modifier through the District's GeoWeb before pricing any split. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Q: Can a Canyon Heights lot hold a suite and a coach house?** Yes — since June 2024, three units total (principal dwelling, secondary suite, coach house) are permitted on single-family zoned lots within the Urban Containment Boundary, and the District's SSMUH classification can potentially allow up to 6 units on a non-exempt lot above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. Eligibility is parcel-specific. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Q: Is this the City or the District — and why does it matter?** Canyon Heights is District ground: District OCP 7900, District Zoning 3210, District taxes. The City (OCP 8400 / Zoning 6700, and its Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw 8014) is a separate municipality to the south-east — City servicing or zoning wording in a Canyon Heights listing is a red flag to verify. https://dnv-docs.simplicitycms.ca/documents/ocp-schedule-b-dpa.pdf
- **Q: What hazard checks apply to a river-side or steep lot?** Schedule B Development Permit Areas — Streamside Protection, Creek Hazard, Slope Hazard and Wildfire Hazard among the seven — are checked lot by lot on the District's GeoWeb, and the District's flood construction level data is modelled to a 2100 horizon plus 0.6 m. Check before pricing a renovation or rebuild. https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **Q: Is the lot serviced?** District single-family utilities (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60; $2,571 combined in 2026) cover the established streets on the District's wording; septic/well status by street is UNVERIFIED — confirm the specific lot's services with the District. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does buying the lot get me the first-time-buyer GST rebate?** No — the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4; Royal Assent March 13, 2026) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. The rebate question arises on the new home, not the lot purchase alone. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Canyon Heights lot?** By exact address only. The one settled locator run in this file (4501 Highland Boulevard → Canyon Heights Elementary → Handsworth Secondary) does not transfer to other streets — Canyon Heights Elementary and Highlands Elementary split the plateau by address. Run the address with SD44. https://www.sd44.ca/school/canyonheights/Pages/default.aspx
- **Q: Can I buy a 55+ strata lot or unit in Canyon Heights?** No verified 55+ stock exists inside Canyon Heights in any sibling file; North Vancouver's verified 55+ strata roster (9 complexes program-wide) sits in Lonsdale, Edgemont Village, Lynn Valley, Delbrook and Cedar Village — none here.
