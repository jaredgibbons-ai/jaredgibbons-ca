The settled ALR frame those posts operate under: ALR subdivision needs ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no such flat line is documented in this file's research as a blog statement. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

- **PTT (general frame the farm posts rely on):** 1% on first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on residential portion above $3M; FTHB first $500,000 exempt at ≤$835,000 (max $8,000, partial to $860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000 / partial to $1,150,000 (new-HOME, bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). The 20% additional PTT figure above is the farmland post's own statement for its subject. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Anything finer UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
