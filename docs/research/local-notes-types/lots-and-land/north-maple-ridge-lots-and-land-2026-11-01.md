Subdivision of an RS-1 lot must still meet the s. 605 minimums — SSMUH unit counts do not create new fee-simple lots by themselves. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new

- **Floodplain:** the North and South Alouette River and Fraser River floodplain maps cover this ground; the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), no habitable floor space may sit below that level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. Which lots are affected is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **ALR block — full form (Yennadon farm ground):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; one additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Farm class (BC Assessment) in full three-tier form: $2,500 of gross farm income at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **BC strata block — applies in full to any strata lot here (including the 3 cross-referenced townhome complexes' resales, and to any bare-land strata lot if one is ever verified):** Form B (SPA s. 59), provided within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. None of the 3 counted complexes is verified 55+ — Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (before any exemption): $700K → $12,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.1M → $20,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. First-time buyers' exemption (full on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000) and newly built home exemption (full to $1,100,000, phasing out by $1,150,000) are HOME exemptions — not bare-lot exemptions; a bare lot's eligibility is UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — new housing, NOT bare land. The GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
