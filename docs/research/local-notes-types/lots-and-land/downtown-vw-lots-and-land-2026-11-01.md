## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No bare-land market exists in Downtown VW at all — stated plainly at the top, on the same honest-zero as the peninsula's land home file. There are no house lots: detached houses are not a VW-core housing form, the housing is almost entirely strata apartment form in towers above and beside the business district, and no named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community, no development site offered as land and no presale lot offering was verified on any City or developer source. Downtown "land" trades only as strata units, as airspace and development rights inside DD / CD-1 / C-3A parcels, and as whole-building or assembly transactions documented as such — never as lots. The shared peninsula land record (district minimums, the airspace fee line, lease positions never imported) is homed in the downtown-ve lots file and is cross-referenced, not repeated; below is only what this slug's own research adds.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, Downtown District (DD) schedule:** the DD position in Section 2, with CD-1 and C-3A districts also applying in parts of the area. A plan named only for the Downtown local area or the VW core is UNVERIFIED; the citywide Vancouver Plan applies, and the Downtown Eastside Plan covers the east-side planning area that belongs to the downtown-ve file's ground. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/dtes-local-area-plan.aspx
- **Subdivision — Vancouver Subdivision By-law No. 5208 (fees under By-law No. 14538):** the City's subdivision framework for this ground; its downtown-relevant form is airspace (Class V), not lot-splitting — the fee line and the major-subdivision process are treated in the land home file (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/downtown-ve-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §3), cross-referenced here rather than restated. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **SSMUH / house-lot density:** not a Downtown form — there are no house lots to densify; added residential capacity in the core runs through DD / CD-1 development permits, not small-scale multi-unit rules. (Research file above.)
- **Floodplain standards:** land in the City's designated floodplain is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for shoreline setbacks and flood construction levels; whether a specific VW-core parcel is in the floodplain is UNVERIFIED — check the parcel against the City's designated-floodplain dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Strata block (the operative tenure block for this ground):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. For strata purchases, review the depreciation report, contingency reserve and any special levies before pricing an offer; BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Downtown VW ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY, applies here:** Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax is 3% of assessed value for vacant properties; every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration (due February 3, 2026), and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — a standing check on any VW-core purchase, where investor-held units are common. The provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax is a separate filing; no rate or area-specific position beyond what this file's own sources support is stated here. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Purchase prohibition:** the federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this ground is stated ONLY as this file's own cited sources support it: the research cites no prohibition source for this area, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and is not conformed from other files.
- **City of Vancouver property taxes + additional school tax:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. At VW-core price points the additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Downtown VW a different neighbourhood from Downtown VE?** No — the City has one Downtown local area among its 22 distinct areas; VW and VE are the board/site split of that one area, with this page covering the west-side core. The exact board dividing street is UNVERIFIED — do not state Main Street or Ontario Street as the line without a board source. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot or a house lot downtown?** No — detached houses are not a VW-core housing form, no bare-land or bare-land-strata stock was verified, and downtown land trades as strata units, airspace and development rights inside DD / CD-1 / C-3A parcels. (Section 2; land home file.)
- **Q: Is Coal Harbour or Yaletown part of Downtown VW?** They sit inside or beside the Downtown local area but are their own Paragon sub-areas with their own pages, so their buildings and plans are not part of this page. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **Q: What zoning governs most of the core?** The Downtown District (DD) schedule in By-law 3575, which permits the uses set out in the Downtown District Official Development Plan — commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses — with CD-1 and C-3A districts also applying in parts of the area. There are no house-lot minimums; C-3A shows no minimum site area for its listed uses. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf
- **Q: Which hospital is inside the area?** St. Paul's Hospital, 1081 Burrard Street, on the Burrard corridor in the VW core — a Providence Health Care hospital, so do not cite Vancouver Coastal Health pages for it. Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, is in Fairview, south across False Creek. https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/clinics/rapid-access-stroke-prevention-clinic
- **Q: Which schools serve my address?** UNVERIFIED at address level — the two Downtown elementaries (Elsie Roy, 150 Drake Street; Crosstown šxʷwəq̓ʷəθət, 55 Expo Boulevard) sit at the area's south and east sides, no secondary school inside the area was confirmed, and the sample address (350 West Georgia Street) was not run in the VSB locator. Run every address in the locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
- **Q: What extra taxes should a downtown buyer budget for?** The annual Empty Homes Tax declaration on every purchase (3% of assessed value if the property is vacant), and — where the residential assessment exceeds $3 million — the additional school tax: 0.2% between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Q: How is a downtown parcel subdivided, if not into lots?** Through Vancouver's Subdivision By-law No. 5208 framework, whose downtown-relevant form is airspace (Class V under the fee by-law, No. 14538) — treated in the land home file. There is no lot-split fee a downtown buyer will ever pay, because there are no lots to split. (Land home file, §3.)
- **Q: What transit terminates in the VW core?** The R5 Hastings St RapidBus terminates at Burrard Station, route 22 also runs from Burrard Station, and route 50 starts at Waterfront Station — the core is the hub where the Expo, Millennium and Canada Lines meet. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/ttR5.pdf
- **Q: Will GST apply on a developer sale here?** Developer sales are generally taxable under CRA's rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003 states the vacant-land position; strata-unit GST follows the new-housing rules — finer points UNCONFIRMED here). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
