## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020,** covering the whole city including this sub-area, plus the **North Lougheed Area Plan, adopted by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026,** covering approximately 50 ha north of Lougheed Highway between Meadows Gardens Golf Course and Harris Road, with development to proceed gradually and further approvals required. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/north-lougheed-area-plan
- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011:** A-1 General Agricultural minimum lot area for subdivision 8 ha (one farm house per lot to a maximum gross floor area of 500 sq m); A-2 Large Lot Agricultural minimum 16 ha; RR-2 minimum 4 ha; RR-3 minimum 2 ha; RR-4 minimum 1 ha (RR zones are for land outside the ALR); RS Large Lot Residential minimum 0.2 ha. A garden suite is permitted in the A-1, A-2, RR-1 to RR-5 and RS zones, one per lot, 33 sq m to 90 sq m, and a property may have a garden suite or a secondary suite, not both. Any specific parcel's zone and ALR status: per-parcel, UNVERIFIED. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **SSMUH as the City states it:** the City's SSMUH framework (SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986, adopted June 25, 2024; lots 280 sq m or less up to 3 units, lots over 280 sq m up to 4, and lots over 280 sq m within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6) is an urban-lot framework; whether SSMUH unit counts apply to a given rural Pitt Polder parcel is per-parcel: UNCONFIRMED — do not price a North Meadows acreage on urban SSMUH unit counts. - **Subdivision and servicing — Pitt Meadows:** subdivision follows the zone minimums above and, where ALR applies, ALC approval (ALR block below). The Pitt Meadows servicing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED — no number is quoted and no other city's bylaw is borrowed; the documented process is zone minimums + dike/drainage position, confirmed with the City for the parcel.
