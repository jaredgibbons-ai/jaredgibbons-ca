# Annacis Island, Delta — Lots and Land: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No listing addresses or asking prices beyond named developments themselves. No sold prices.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Annacis Island is an island in the Fraser River, in the City of Delta, in the Highway 91 / Alex Fraser Bridge corridor; the island is bounded by the Fraser River channels around it (Annacis Channel / Annieville Channel), and street access is by Derwent Way, Cliveden Avenue, Eaton Way and Audley Boulevard. https://www.delta.ca/
- "Annacis Island" is the Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name as given; it is not one of the neighbourhoods named in the former North Delta Area Plan (Schedule C.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950), and the current Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), treats it as an industrial area — there is no separate Annacis Island area plan. Paragon sub-area code: UNVERIFIED. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Nearby areas: Nordel and the rest of North Delta to the south across the Alex Fraser Bridge, New Westminster to the north, Richmond to the west, Tilbury industrial lands to the south-west. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf
- Roads and commute: Highway 91 via the Cliveden Interchange and the Alex Fraser Bridge; access into Annacis Business Park is via Chester Road / Audley Boulevard and Cliveden Avenue; Highway 91 connects to the Highway 99 and Highway 17 routes. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf
- Transit: route 340 runs Scottsdale Exchange to 22nd Street Station via Scott Road, 64 Avenue, Kittson Parkway, Highway 91, the Alex Fraser Bridge and the Cliveden Interchange, serving the Annacis Island industrial area edge; no bus exchange on the island itself was confirmed. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf
- Schools: no public school on Annacis Island was confirmed in School District 37 material; SD37 catchments are address-based through the SD37 School Locator, and the island has no residential population to catchment. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No meaningful residential bare-land market exists on Annacis Island — stated plainly at the top. The island is industrial land with essentially no housing, and what trades as land here is industrial land: freehold industrial lots and strata industrial lots in the Annacis Business Park, plus port-terminal land. There is no residential fee-simple lot subdivision, no residential bare-land strata, and no presale lot offering verified in any source checked.**

- **What trades instead — industrial lots:** freehold industrial lots and strata industrial units in the Annacis Business Park, plus port-terminal land at the Annacis Auto Terminal. Where a strata plan is filed, BC strata guidance applies — owners own their strata lots and together own common property as a strata corporation. Stated plainly for the strata form that does exist here: a strata industrial lot IS strata tenure — Form B / strata fees / bylaws apply to the lot — but it is warehouse/industrial tenure, not a residential bare-land strata lot, and it is not counted as residential land stock in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Fee-simple as the default land form:** outside the strata industrial complexes, island land is fee-simple industrial land — the default form for land generally — held for logistics, warehousing, automotive-sector and service-industrial use. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision was verified by an official or developer source in the research; no subdivision is named here rather than inventing one. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any residential land to buy on Annacis Island?** No. No residential housing mix was confirmed on the island; the Delta OCP treats the island as industrial land, home to the Annacis Business Park, the Port of Vancouver auto terminal and Metro Vancouver's wastewater treatment plant. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: What land actually trades here, then?** Freehold industrial lots and strata industrial units in the Annacis Business Park, plus port-terminal land — industrial land, not house lots. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Q: I saw a strata lot for sale in the business park — is that a bare-land strata house lot?** No. It is a strata industrial lot: strata tenure applies (Form B, fees, bylaws), but the use is warehouse/industrial, not a residential lot you can live on. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Q: How big are the lots, and what does the zoning allow?** UNCONFIRMED in this file — the industrial zone names and minimum lot standards for Annacis parcels were not confirmed from a City zone sheet, and the zoning-bylaw number itself is contested (7600, 2017 vs an internal "8400, 2025" title). Get the parcel's zone sheet from the City. (Section 3.)
- **Q: City land or port land — why does it matter?** Because the approval path differs: City-zoned industrial land follows the City path; port-terminal land (e.g. 820 Dock Road, designated "Port Terminal") is under Vancouver Fraser Port Authority permitting. Establish the regime first. https://www.portvancouver.com/sites/default/files/wp-permits/wpid-48523/2022-09-26-WEB-COPY_PER-21-019-Issued-Project-Permit-and-Report-VFPA-obo-WWL-Annacis-Auto-Terminal-Optimization.pdf
- **Q: What flood homework comes first?** Flood Construction Level, dike setbacks and any flood covenants on title — the island is surrounded by Fraser River channels inside the City's dike system (over 63 km citywide). Parcel detail: UNVERIFIED until checked. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Will GST apply to an industrial lot purchase?** Generally, yes where the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided — generally taxable under CRA GI-003; an individual's personal-use sale is generally exempt. Confirm the seller's status per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Can SSMUH or a rezoning turn island land residential?** No source supports that: SSMUH Bylaws 8394+8395 (adopted June 24, 2024) apply to residential properties, and the island has none; the OCP designates the island industrial. Any future change is UNCONFIRMED speculation — do not price it. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **Q: Who are the permanent neighbours?** The Annacis Auto Terminal (expanded to 480,000 vehicles/year capacity, works complete early 2025) and Metro Vancouver's secondary wastewater treatment plant serving the Fraser Sewerage Area — both permanent, high-activity industrial neighbours affecting truck traffic and odour expectations. https://www.portvancouver.com/project/annacis-auto-terminal-optimization-project and https://metrovancouver.org/services/liquid-waste/wastewater-treatment-plants-and-processes
- **Q: Where do people who work on the island live?** Across the bridge — Nordel and the rest of North Delta to the south, New Westminster to the north, Richmond to the west; route 340 is the transit link serving the island's edge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf
