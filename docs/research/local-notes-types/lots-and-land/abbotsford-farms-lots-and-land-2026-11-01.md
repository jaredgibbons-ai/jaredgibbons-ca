- **ALR block (carried in full — this topic IS ALR ground):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, and depositing a strata plan is itself a subdivision needing ALC permission (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6); ALR residences cannot be stratified (SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); residence caps: principal ≤500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); local bylaws may be more restrictive, not more permissive. - **Q: What residence can an ALR farm have?** One principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² of fill), a suite only inside it, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha); local rules may be stricter. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify farm land or create bare-land strata lots on it?** No — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision in the ALR needing ALC permission (Bulletin 09, s. 6), and ALR residences cannot be stratified (SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). (§§2–3.)
