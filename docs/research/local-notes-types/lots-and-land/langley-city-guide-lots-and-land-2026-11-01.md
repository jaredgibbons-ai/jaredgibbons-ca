## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Planning frame — City:** the City's Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 3200 was adopted November 22, 2021, and its current zoning bylaw is the City of Langley Zoning Bylaw, 2025, No. 3300, which repeals Zoning Bylaw 1996 No. 2100. **Bylaw 3300 is cited by NUMBER only — its adoption date is conflicted in sources and is UNVERIFIED in this file; no adoption date is carried here.** Full land-plan detail is in the langleycity research (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/langleycity.md) and is not duplicated here. Township Bylaw 2500 does NOT apply here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/langley-city-guide.md, §4, citing ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/langley-city.md) and https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw
- **Zoning minimums — City (Bylaw 3300, as printed, via the land home):** R1 Suburban Residential — 557 m² / 16 m frontage; R2 Townhouse Residential — 1,000 m², except lots created by subdivision with a registered party wall agreement (maximum 280 m², minimum frontage 4.5 m); R3 Low Rise Residential — 1,000 m² / 26.0 m. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw
- **SSMUH — City:** the City amended its Zoning Bylaw to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on RS1 and RS2 lots by Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 — RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. This is the CITY's own SSMUH frame; the Township's Bylaw 6020 frame is different and is never imported here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/langley-city-guide.md, §4.)
- **Suites — City (Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300):** a secondary suite must not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the gross floor area of the building; the registered owner must reside on the lot; at least one extra off-street parking space is required; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/langley-city-guide.md, §4.)
- **Subdivision — City:** Bylaw 3300 states "No person shall subdivide land except in accordance with this bylaw"; subdivisions are subject to the City's Subdivision Control Bylaw and Development Cost Charge Bylaw; a lot created by subdivision must meet its zone's minimum lot area; and meeting the minimum standards does not bind the Approving Officer to approve. The Subdivision Control Bylaw's number: UNVERIFIED. City DCC Bylaw 2024 No. 3256 (via the land home). https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw
- **Short-term rentals — City:** UNVERIFIED — no City of Langley STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page in this program; confirm with the City before any STR claim. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/langley-city-guide.md, §4.)
- **Property taxes — City:** City of Langley payments received after the July 2 due date incur a penalty; the City's payment page lists City Hall at 20399 Douglas Crescent and a 2.2% processing fee on credit-card payments. The penalty percentage for 2026: UNVERIFIED — the City page read states a penalty applies but does not state the rate. https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, if met):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $700,000 City lot purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$700,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. (Anchor at $3,500,000 for reference: $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — a townhomes sibling in this program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met, and $93,000 is followed here.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FTHB PTT exemption / Newly Built Home Exemption:** first $500,000 exempt, full at value ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000), partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; a person who paid PTT on vacant land and then has a newly built home on it may be eligible for a refund of the tax paid. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
