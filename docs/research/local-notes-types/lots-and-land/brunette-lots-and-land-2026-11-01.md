## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001:** single-detached homes sit under the RS series (s.310) — RS-1 minimum site 557.40 sq. m. (6,000 sq. ft.), FSR 0.5; secondary suite and laneway / carriage house rules are set in the same bylaw. Townhouse form is regulated under **RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B only** — the research file's "RT-A and RT-B" is an error, corrected at the top of this file; RT-A/RT-B do not exist, and 836.10 m² is an RM apartment-district density threshold, not an RT figure. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **OCP — Our City 2041, Bylaw No. 7925, 2017:** the Official Community Plan governing the area; the Sapperton and Braid stations area is one of the City's Frequent Transit Development Areas, the growth framework for this edge — confirm the designation for the specific parcel at the City before promising redevelopment. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states it:** the RS framework above carries the bylaw's secondary suite and laneway / carriage house rules — eligibility and form are parcel-specific under Bylaw No. 6680; confirm with the City before pricing in added-unit potential. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — Bylaw No. 7142, 2007 (VERIFIED):** the Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw No. 7142, 2007 requires Approving Officer approval and ensures new lots are adequately serviced and meet the Zoning Bylaw's minimum lot size and frontage requirements, through the City's pre-application / application / Works & Services Agreement process. Parcel-level standards are confirmed through that process; none is quoted here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/7142_Subdivision.pdf
- **Flood diligence — Floodplain Zone 4 (Brunette's defining item):** the City floodplain study places the industrial land along the Brunette River in Floodplain Zone 4, below the Flood Construction Level, with the Brunette River running through it — any lot on the creek / river corridor needs its flood construction level confirmed before building or insuring, and lot-level status is property-specific: UNVERIFIED per lot. Zone 4 belongs to Brunette only in this ledger — it is not Queensborough's Zone 1, the Quay/Downtown's Zone 3, or North Arm's Zone 2. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Strata block (for completeness — no Brunette bare-land strata is verified):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Brunette in New Westminster or Coquitlam?** New Westminster — SnapStats carries it as a New Westminster sub-area with no Coquitlam row, and City sources place it on the east side of mainland New Westminster along the Brunette Creek corridor by Hume Park; the creek and Brunette Avenue continue into Coquitlam, but that side is a different municipality, different sub-areas and a different school district (SD40 vs SD43). https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/2_4 Parks and Open Space.pdf
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Brunette?** No verified one — September 2026 recorded 0 listings and 0 solds in both segments, and no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2; SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.)
- **Q: What is Floodplain Zone 4 and why does it lead this file?** The City's floodplain study places the industrial land along the Brunette River in Floodplain Zone 4, below the Flood Construction Level — pull the property's flood construction level and an insurance quote before subjects come off on any creek-adjacent lot. Lot-level status is UNVERIFIED per lot. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Q: How small can a lot be, and can I split one?** RS-1 sets a minimum site of 557.40 sq. m. (6,000 sq. ft.) with FSR 0.5. Subdivision itself runs under Bylaw No. 7142, 2007, with Approving Officer approval and adequate servicing. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Q: What do RT-A and RT-B allow here?** Nothing — those districts do not exist in New Westminster. The townhouse districts are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B only; the research file's RT-A/RT-B line is an error, corrected at the top of this file. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Q: Does station proximity change what a lot can become?** It can frame expectations: the Sapperton / Braid station area is a designated Frequent Transit Development Area in Our City 2041, so station-proximate lots may carry different expectations than interior RS-1 streets — confirm the designation for the specific parcel at the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **Q: Which schools serve my address?** For the one settled run — 331 Richmond Street → Skwo:wech Elementary, École Glenbrook Middle and New Westminster Secondary (SD40 locator, run 2026-10-21) — that result is single-address only. Verify every other address in the locator; Richard McBride Elementary sits in adjoining Sapperton, so neighbouring streets can feed different elementaries. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- **Q: Is a Brunette lot serviced?** The City operates its own electrical utility plus water / sewer services, and new lots must be adequately serviced under Bylaw No. 7142, 2007 — the servicing position of a specific parcel is confirmed through that process. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/7142_Subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
