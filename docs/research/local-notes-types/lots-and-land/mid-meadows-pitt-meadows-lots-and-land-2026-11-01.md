## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Mid Meadows covers the Bonson Road area south of the CPKC rail line in the City of Pitt Meadows, BC, in Metro Vancouver — including the North Bonson, South Bonson and Osprey Village areas named in City park and facility pages. "Mid Meadows" is the Paragon sub-area name as given (code UNVERIFIED); the City publishes no official neighbourhood boundary or name for it — the City's own labels for this part of the city are North Bonson, South Bonson and Osprey Village. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/pitt-meadows-athletic-park and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- Working geography from official facility addresses: along Bonson Road south of the Canadian Pacific rail line and Hammond Road and north of the Fraser River dike, roughly between Harris Road in the west and the Maple Ridge boundary in the east. Nearby: Central Meadows (Town Centre) to the north-west, West Meadows to the west, South Meadows lands toward the Fraser River, and Maple Ridge to the east. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/harris-landing-pitt-river-greenway
- Main roads and transit: Bonson Road is the main road, with Hammond Road and the CPKC rail line at the north edge, Harris Road at the west edge, and Fraser Way and Barnston View Road in Osprey Village; Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and Golden Ears Way north of the area serve Maple Ridge, Coquitlam and, via the Golden Ears Bridge, Langley. Routes 719/722 run the Meadowtown–Bonson/Fraser Way circular service via Pitt Meadows Station; route 701 runs along Hammond Road at the north edge. No West Coast Express station is in this sub-area. Commute times in minutes: UNVERIFIED. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt722.pdf
- Civic anchors: South Bonson Community Centre, 10932 Barnston View Road; Pitt Meadows Athletic Park, 11431 Bonson Road, beside Pitt Meadows Arena; Shoreline Park, 51 Bonson Road, on the Fraser River dike in Osprey Village; Somerset Park at the Edith McDermott school site, 12778 Bonson Road; North Bonson Park at the Davie Jones school site on Blakely Road. Shopping: Osprey Village shops at Fraser Way and Barnston View Road, including Osprey Village Market (City Commercial & Industrial Lands Analysis); larger centres (Meadowtown, Meadowvale) are north of the area. The city's only library branch is in Central Meadows. Nearest hospital: Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/harris-landing-pitt-river-greenway and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/283
- Schools: SD42 catchments are address-based and SD42 has no middle-school tier — elementary K–7, secondary 8–12. The research file documents an SD42 School Locator result for the sample address 10932 Barnston View Road only: Davie Jones Elementary (K–7) and Pitt Meadows Secondary (8–12). Schools physically in the area: Davie Jones Elementary, 12030 Blakely Road, and Edith McDermott Elementary, 12178 Bonson Road; Pitt Meadows Secondary, 19438 116B Avenue, is the city's only public secondary school. That sample result does not transfer to other addresses. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/ and https://elementary.sd42.ca/daviejones/

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Mid Meadows has no meaningful bare-land subdivision market — stated plainly at the top, on the city-assembly position. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan, and no presale LOT offering was verified on an official or developer source in the research file or the siblings. What trades as land here is (a) the fee-simple urban lot on the Bonson Road corridor streets, priced with R-3 / R-4 / R-6 multi-unit potential, and (b) the built Osprey Village / South Bonson strata ground — which is townhome stock counted in the townhomes sibling, documented here by pointer only, never as land inventory.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default (and only verified) form

- **Bonson corridor house lots:** detached houses on the Bonson Road corridor streets under RS Large Lot Residential, R-3, R-4 and R-6 Urban Residential zones in Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011, with townhomes concentrated on Bonson Road, Fraser Way and Barnston View Road in North Bonson, South Bonson and Osprey Village. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Lot character from the zones, as the bylaw states it:** RS minimum lot area 0.2 ha; R-3 / R-4 / R-6 minimum lot area **for subdivision** 1,215 m² (minimum lot width for subdivision 12 m per the city-assembly file); RM-1 (townhouse) minimum lot area 0.4 ha. These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **The 1,215 m² clarification:** that figure is a subdivision minimum ONLY and does not apply to development on existing lots, per the City's SSMUH implementation update — an existing smaller Bonson corridor lot can still be developed under R-3 / R-4 / R-6. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states 78% of Pitt Meadows land falls within the ALR citywide; ALR parcels inside the urban Bonson Road area were **not confirmed** in the research — ALR status is a per-address check on ALC maps, and no Mid Meadows ALR split is stated. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Servicing:** the research documents the diked urban context — this sub-area is in drainage Area 3 (Kennedy, including McKechnie) of the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District, the urban development area, with Shoreline Park and the greenway on the Fraser River dike — and a separate drainage levy (Section 3). A parcel-level water/sewer vs well/septic statement: UNCONFIRMED in the research — confirm servicing for the specific parcel with the City. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Siting note for RS lots:** zoning siting in RS requires buildings 15 m from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches, per the research file's flood/dikes record — a lot-depth constraint on ditch-edge Bonson parcels. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Flood position:** over 95% of the city land base is in the Fraser / Pitt floodplain behind 60 km of dikes (City 2020 Business Plan — governing figure; live-page "over 85%" conflict flagged at the top and in Section 5). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011, under the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," OCP Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022 (repealing OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007); no neighbourhood or area plan specific to the Bonson Road area was found in official sources in the research. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/media/8989 and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** R-3 up to 3 units per lot, R-4 up to 4, R-6 up to five, or six where at least one unit meets the adaptable housing requirements in the BC Building Code on lots close to frequent transit; provincially, lots 280 m² or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 m² up to 4, and lots over 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6; SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986 adopted June 25, 2024. The 1,215 m² figure applies to subdivision, not development on existing lots. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **Suites:** one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit, 33 to 90 m², inside the same building, where the zone permits it; garden suites one per lot at 33 to 90 m² in the zones listed in the City garden suite guide, including RS — a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision follows the zone minimums in Section 2. A servicing-bylaw number could not be confirmed on a primary source in the research — held UNCONFIRMED (a fresh search for this batch found the City's Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw named in City minutes, with an Amendment Bylaw No. 2833, 2019, but no base-bylaw number on a primary source, so no number is quoted); the process documented instead is zone minimums + the 15 m ditch/watercourse siting rule + drainage/dike position below, confirmed with the City for the parcel. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Floodplain — the defining lot rule:** **over 95%** of the land base lies within the Fraser River and (to a smaller degree) Pitt River floodplain (City 2020 Business Plan — the figure these notes retire to), protected by a 60 km dike system, under Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 (per the city-assembly file); this sub-area is in drainage Area 3 and sits directly behind the Fraser River dike at Osprey Village. LIVE CONFLICT: all five Pitt Meadows guide pages state "over 85%" (City Financial Plan 2026–2030 attribution) — flagged at the top, never silently resolved. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **ALR rules (citywide ground; Bonson urban parcels not confirmed ALR):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); BC Assessment farm-class thresholds in full three-tier form, as carried in the city-assembly file ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Apply only after a per-address ALC check confirms the parcel is ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §3.
- **Short-term rentals: PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows** — "Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Zoning Bylaw" (City staff report), and Pitt Meadows is in the Province's principal-residence-requirement community list. A Bonson lot priced on STR income is priced on a use the City does not permit. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Bare-land strata rules (no such stock verified here; stated so the form is never mis-sold):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 with May 1, 2023 exemptions. A listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. (The Coho cluster's built-form strata are governed by the same block in their townhomes home file.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1%/$200K, 2% to $2M, 3% above, +2% on residential portion above $3M; FTHB Program (first $500,000 exempt at ≤$835,000, max $8,000, partial to $860,000) and Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §3.
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Mid Meadows lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36%/$6,300/$350K–$450K. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000/$8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy taxes:** the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is VANCOUVER ONLY — it does NOT apply to Pitt Meadows. No Pitt Meadows position beyond that is documented in the research file; no rate is asserted here. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §3.

A 2026 flat utility fee amount: UNVERIFIED in the research. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a subdivision where I can just buy a lot in Mid Meadows?** On this file's evidence, no — no named bare-land subdivision and no presale lot offering was verified. Land trades as individual fee-simple house lots on the Bonson corridor and as built townhome strata in Osprey Village. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is the Osprey Village / Bonson ground a land play?** No — that ground trades as built townhouse strata (Coho, Coho II, Shoreline, Nature's Walk), counted in the mid-meadows townhomes file. Classification note: MLS files the cluster under "South Meadows"; the sibling programs count it under Mid Meadows. Bonson by Mosaic is counted in NO sub-area file because its sub-area is unsettled. Home file: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **Q: Can I split a big Bonson corridor lot?** Zone first — R-3/R-4/R-6 subdivision minimum 1,215 m² / 12 m width, RS 0.2 ha, RM-1 0.4 ha for townhouse sites — and ALR status per-address, since ALR parcels inside the urban Bonson area were not confirmed either way. No subdivision promise can be made from this file. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Q: My lot is smaller than 1,215 m². Can I still build the R-6 units?** Yes on the City's own clarification — the 1,215 m² figure applies to subdivision, not to development on existing lots. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **Q: Is my lot in the floodplain?** Almost certainly — **over 95%** of the city land base is floodplain behind 60 km of dikes, and Osprey Village sits directly on the Fraser River dike. Check the flood construction level under Bylaw No. 2384, 2008, any covenant on title, and insurance early; budget the drainage levy. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Q: Does the 15 m ditch setback affect a Bonson lot?** In RS zoning, yes as the research records it: buildings must be sited 15 m from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches — measure the buildable envelope, not just the lot area, on ditch-edge parcels. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Q: Can I add a garden suite to a lot?** In the zones that list it (RS, RR, R-4, R-6 accessory uses in the city framework), the City guide allows one garden suite per lot at 33–90 m² — and never alongside a secondary suite on the same lot. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Q: Can I buy a lot and run short-term rentals in a garden suite?** No — short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Pitt Meadows at all. Price the lot on long-term residential use only. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Mid Meadows lot?** By exact address only — two elementaries sit inside the area (Davie Jones, Edith McDermott) and the one settled locator sample (10932 Barnston View Road → Davie Jones Elementary → Pitt Meadows Secondary) does not settle any other street. Run the address in the SD42 locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
