## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: Neilsen Grove has no meaningful vacant bare-land market. No named bare-land subdivision, presale lot offering, or serviced vacant-lot development for sale was verified in this sub-area on an official or developer source. What trades here is (a) individual fee-simple detached lots under the RS zones, and (b) existing homes on bare-land strata lots at South Pointe — built stock on strata lots, not vacant development land. Any lot count, typical lot size beyond the zoning minimums in Section 3, or lot price would be invented, so none is stated.**

### What land actually trades here

- **Fee-simple lots — the default form.** The detached stock sits on fee-simple lots under the Delta single-detached residential zones; zoning is assigned parcel by parcel on the zoning maps. A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants on title govern instead. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future" — consolidated, with NO lettered schedules.** The former area plans were schedules of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; farm land under the current OCP is designated "Agriculture (A)". Any source attributing a lettered schedule (e.g. "Schedule D.1") to Bylaw 8400 is wrong — that attribution belongs to the old bylaw. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted.** City zone sheets cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, while an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates in program sources. Neither is asserted as settled here; the ruling is Jared's. What governs in practice is the parcel's own zone sheet and, for Hampton Cove phases, the site-specific CD zone (Westhampton's is recorded as CD430; South Pointe's as CD98A). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Zoning / subdivision minimums (zone sheets, as the City states them):** RS5 550 m² (460 m² outside the SFR designation; width 16 m or 15 m; depth 30 m), RS6 390 m² (width 13 m, depth 30 m), RS7 335 m² (width 11 m), RD1 duplex 836 m² (width 22 m, depth 30 m), RD4 duplex 504 m² (width 18 m, depth 28 m); RS1, RS2, RS3, RD2 and RD3 at 4,000 m² in lower-density areas; there is no RD5 zone. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024.** Delta's SSMUH amendments allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots per the City's notice to the Province. Added-unit permission is NOT subdivision permission: it does not create new lots and does not waive a permit area on the parcel. The RS1 zone also lists a secondary suite as an accessory use to a single detached dwelling. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision in Delta runs under the City's Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 (which repealed and replaced Bylaw No. 7162, 2015), made under Local Government Act s. 506 to regulate the provision of highways, water, sanitary sewers, drainage and other services on subdivision. Parcel-specific works, frontage and cash-in-lieu requirements beyond that bylaw's existence: UNCONFIRMED in the research — confirm with the City for the specific parcel, never filled in from another municipality. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Bare-land strata block (applies to South Pointe lots):** Form B first — under Strata Property Act s. 59 the Form B Information Certificate discloses the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owed, approved special levies still to be paid, the CRF balance, parking/storage and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report attached; the strata must provide it within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Since July 1, 2024 every strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, non-deferrable by vote. Since November 1, 2023 the annual budget must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Rental-restriction bylaws have been VOID since November 24, 2022 for long-term rentals (short-term rentals may still be restricted by bylaw). Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and children in defined care cases) — the rule that makes South Pointe's verified 55+ position lawful. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **ALR rules (edge parcels only — no ALR parcel is verified inside this sub-area):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, as the ALC states it. ALC residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², a suite within the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR and appears nowhere in this file as a rule. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief on a new principal-residence HOME up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a home exemption, not a bare-land exemption; do not promise it on vacant land alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — stated only at the level the CRA source supports:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use), while sales can be taxable where the land was capital property used primarily in a business, sold in the course of a business, or created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts. Sales by developers/businesses and newly subdivided lots are therefore generally TAXABLE and an individual's personal-use sale generally exempt, at that level only. Finer points for a specific Neilsen Grove parcel: UNCONFIRMED — get tax advice. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / HBP / FHSA — land angle stated precisely:** the First-Time Home Buyer GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not to bare land itself. The older New Housing Rebate is 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000, likewise a housing rebate. Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 per buyer from an RRSP, repayable over 15 years. First Home Savings Account: $8,000 per year, $40,000 lifetime, combinable with the HBP. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy taxes + foreign-buyer prohibition — Delta position only, as an official source states it:** the Province's taxable-areas list for the Speculation and Vacancy Tax includes the City of Delta (Metro Vancouver Regional District), and states reserve lands, treaty lands and lands of self-governing Indigenous Nations are NOT part of the taxable areas — relevant at Delta's TFN edge, not inside Neilsen Grove itself. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does NOT apply here. Whether SVT tax (as opposed to a declaration/exemption) is payable on a specific vacant parcel, and any federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for a specific buyer: UNCONFIRMED in this area's research — confirm per buyer/parcel, never imported. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Dyke / flood / streamside diligence:** Ladner is low land protected by the City's flood protection system — over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure. All properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — a live check on harbour-side and marsh-edge lots, including around South Pointe. The City directs owners to DeltaMap for parcel-level flood information; search title for flood covenants before subjects. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf and https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **CMHC premium range:** 0.60% of the loan at 65% loan-to-value or less, to 4.00% at 90.01%–95%, and 4.50% in that band with a non-traditional down payment. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot I can buy and build on in Neilsen Grove?** No verified bare-land subdivision or lot offering exists in this sub-area — what trades is resale detached homes on fee-simple lots and resale homes on South Pointe's bare-land strata lots. A "lot" listing here needs a title check first: fee-simple or bare-land strata changes the whole diligence package. (Section 2 above)
- **Q: Is a South Pointe lot fee-simple?** No. It is bare-land STRATA (plan NWS2528): you own a strata lot and the detached house on it, pay strata fees for gates, roads and common property, and get a Form B, bylaws and a depreciation report. "Bare land" describes what the lot looks like, not the tenure. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3075824-5371-regatta-way-delta-bc-v4k-4r9
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Neilsen Grove detached lot?** Only if the parcel's zone minimum is met — RS7 creates lots at 335 m², RS5 at 550/460 m², RD1 duplex at 836 m², and the low-density zones at 4,000 m² — plus City subdivision/servicing requirements under Bylaw No. 8288, 2024. Confirm the zone on the City zoning maps before pricing any split. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put four homes on one lot instead?** Delta's SSMUH bylaws (8394/8395, June 24, 2024) allow combinations of a single detached dwelling, duplex, secondary suite, coach house and garden suite where the zoning applies — but that is added units on one lot, not four new lots, and inside South Pointe the strata bylaws sit on top of the zoning. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q: Is the lot serviced — city water and sewer, or well and septic?** UNCONFIRMED per parcel in the research. Confirm the connections with the City; for an unserviced parcel, check the GWELLS well record and whether an onsite system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation (designed/installed by an Authorized Person, Fraser Health) is required. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: Will I pay GST on the land?** Depends on the seller and history: CRA states most individual personal-use vacant-land sales are exempt, but business-use, in-the-course-of-business and more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable. A developer's newly subdivided lot is generally taxable. Confirm in the contract and get tax advice. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Is any of this ALR or farm-class land?** No ALR parcel inside Neilsen Grove is verified — UNVERIFIED in the research. If an edge parcel is in the ALR, stratification is barred, subdivision needs ALC approval, and farm class runs on the three-tier test ($2,500 / 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; apply by Oct 31). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Q: What flood questions should I ask on a harbour-side lot?** Whether the parcel is within 30 m of a stream/slough/ditch (Streamside DPA), what DeltaMap shows for parcel-level flood information, and whether a flood covenant sits on title — Ladner sits behind the City's dike and drainage system, and the paperwork follows the geography. https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection
- **Q: What are the holding costs on a lot here?** Delta property taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalties after July 2 and again after September 2, 2026), plus — on a South Pointe strata lot only — monthly strata fees (dated listing figures of $113/mo and $173/mo appear in sources from different years; neither is current — the Form B is the only current source). A fee-simple lot has no strata fee. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and (~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/neilsen-grove-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Which school catchment is a lot in?** Only the sample is documented: 5500 Admiral Boulevard maps to Neilson Grove Elementary (note the spelling — "Neilson" for the school, "Neilsen" for the area) and Delta Secondary. Run the lot's exact address in the SD37 locator before marketing school access. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/ and https://ng.deltasd.bc.ca/
