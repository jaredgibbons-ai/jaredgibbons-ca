## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Grandview-Woodland has no meaningful bare-land market — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community and no presale LOT offering was verified. This is fully built east-side ground: what trades as "land" is fee-simple house lots on the older interior streets, priced on the house plus the lot's R1-1 / RT schedule potential and read through the Grandview-Woodland Community Plan — plus arterial development sites, which are zoning and plan positions, never for-sale lot inventory. The one large new project on the area's southern edge, The Cut, is queued and counted nowhere (see below).**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **Interior house lots (R1-1 District Schedule):** house lots sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a multiple dwelling with 3 or 4 units, a duplex or a single detached house, 464 m² for 5 units, and 557 m² for 6, 7 or 8 units; single detached houses may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and duplexes may include secondary suites and lock-off units. These are schedule minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 (City of Vancouver):** the districts that govern Grandview-Woodland land are R1-1 (minimum site areas 306 m² / 464 m² / 557 m² by unit count; multiplex to 6 units or 8 rental units per the schedule's unit table; secondary suite and laneway house with a single detached house; secondary suites and lock-off units with a duplex) and the RT districts on parts of the older house stock. Confirm the district for the specific lot on the City's zoning map before pricing any potential — the R1-1/RT line decides what the lot can become. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Community plan / SSMUH position as the City states it:** the Grandview-Woodland Community Plan is the area-specific plan over the base zoning, and the Vancouver Plan is the citywide plan that sits over it. Vancouver's added-units framework operates through the district schedules above (R1-1 multiplex permissions) rather than through a separate SSMUH bylaw citation in this file's research — price both the house and the lot's schedule potential, and read the Community Plan before pricing land value into a listing. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/grandview-woodland-open-house-boards-180129.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing:** subdivision follows the district schedule minimums above and the City's subdivision and servicing requirements for the specific application; a parcel-level servicing standard for a Grandview-Woodland lot is UNCONFIRMED on a source in this file's research — confirm requirements with the City for the parcel. Established lots sit inside the City's serviced system on the research's wording (City water and sewer services). https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Floodplain / hazard diligence:** the City's designated floodplain mapping is a citywide dataset covering coastal and Still Creek floodplains, and a parcel-level floodplain check for this area: UNVERIFIED in the research — run the parcel check rather than assuming an interior-street lot is clear. No ALR land is identified in City sources for this area; ALR status remains a parcel-level check. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Older-strata diligence on the arterials:** for older strata on the arterials, review the strata documents under the provincial strata framework early; BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. Where a strata lot is involved, the full BC strata block applies (Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 with the May 1, 2023 exemptions; a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Grandview-Woodland ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Grandview-Woodland lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%, VANCOUVER ONLY, applies here:** properties declared, determined or deemed vacant are subject to a 3% tax of the property's assessed value, levied on empty and under-utilized Class 1 residential properties in the City of Vancouver; most residential properties are not subject to the tax, including principal residences and homes rented out for at least six months of the year. Vancouver owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. A house lot held empty here carries this exposure; the EHT does not apply in New Westminster or Burnaby. https://council.vancouver.ca/20251029/documents/pspc6.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Grandview-Woodland-specific speculation-tax position is documented in the research beyond the EHT above — none imported, and no False Creek South leasehold position is imported into this file (that position belongs to False Creek South ground in Fairview only). The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Grandview-Woodland, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Vancouver property taxes:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
