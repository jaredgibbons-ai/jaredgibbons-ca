## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **ALR block (province-wide frame, ALC):** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may further regulate or restrict residences, and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, and strata plans in the ALR cause subdivision per ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm classification (BC Assessment, province-wide):** farm classification is a voluntary program; the income thresholds are three-tiered — $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha; applications must be submitted to BC Assessment by October 31 for consideration for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. **Flat "$2,500+" position for THIS slug: this file's own research documents the correct three-tier form above and does NOT document a flat "$2,500+" line for this slug, so no such error is asserted for this slug** — the three-tier form is stated, the error is not attributed here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is this a listings page for chicken farms?** No — as read it is a buying guide with no listing feed, no cards and no result count; it links onward to the site's chicken-farms and poultry-farms listing pages, whose generic feed cards are never confirmed operations in the named sector. https://jaredgibbons.ca/buying-a-chicken-farm-bc.html
- **Q: What residence can a BC chicken-farm parcel in the ALR have?** Under the ALC frame: one principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, with local government rules possibly more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I subdivide or stratify a farm parcel for a second homesite?** Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, strata plans in the ALR cause subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6), and ALR residences cannot be stratified (SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)) — do not assume a parcel can be split. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What income must a farm operation show to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's thresholds are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; applications are due by October 31 for the next tax year, and a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: How does a BCCMB quota transfer work, and is quota included in a farm purchase?** UNVERIFIED as a general rule in this file — no marketing-board source was opened in this program. The guide states quota never transfers automatically, that both parties apply and the Board reviews eligibility and premises before allotting quota to the buyer, and that no quota is included unless the contract expressly says so; treat that account as site copy, read the specific contract, and verify the transfer position with the BC Chicken Marketing Board before subjects are removed. https://jaredgibbons.ca/buying-a-chicken-farm-bc.html
- **Q: Can I raise chickens without quota, and is the new entrant program open?** UNVERIFIED in this file on both points: the guide states up to 200 birds a year for personal use without a permit, a Small Lot Permit between 200 and 2,000 birds, up to 99 hens on the egg side, and a new entrant program that is not currently accepting applications — none was confirmed on a board or Province source opened in this program, so check both with the Board before relying on either. https://jaredgibbons.ca/buying-a-chicken-farm-bc.html
- **Q: What does a BC chicken farm cost?** UNVERIFIED in this file — there is no published BC broiler price, price range or quota price in this program, SnapStats has no farm category, and the guide's "multi-million dollar range" framing is site copy only (§5). A valuation stands on the specific land, barns, residence and any quota included, checked part by part. https://jaredgibbons.ca/buying-a-chicken-farm-bc.html
- **Q: How much PTT on a $2M farm purchase?** $38,000 at general tiers — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) plus 2% on the next $1,800,000 ($36,000) — before any farm-specific apportionment, which is UNVERIFIED here; have an agricultural lawyer and accountant model PTT/GST apportionment before an offer is written. (§3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Will GST apply on the land part of a farm purchase?** At CRA GI-003 level only: developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable and an individual's personal-use land sale is generally exempt — farm-purchase apportionment between land, buildings, residence and quota is UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
