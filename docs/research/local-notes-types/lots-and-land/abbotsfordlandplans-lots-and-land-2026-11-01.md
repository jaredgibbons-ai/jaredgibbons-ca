## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is the difference between the OCP and the Zoning Bylaw?** Zoning Bylaw 2014 No. 2400 regulates what can be built on a parcel today without rezoning; the OCP (Abbotsforward 2050, current) sets the long-term future land use designation and rezoning policy. Check both — current zoning and future land use, plus permit areas — through the City's property information search or WebMap. Mind the bylaw-number caution in §3: do not copy the live page's "2500-2016" into an offer. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/development-planning
- **Q: Which Abbotsford plans are actually adopted neighbourhood plans?** UDistrict, City Centre, Historic Downtown and McKee — with McKee verified as Bylaw 3400-2023, Schedule E of the OCP. The page's fifth topic, a Marshall Road Corridor Plan, is UNVERIFIED as an adopted plan on any City source opened in the research. (§2.)
- **Q: How do SSMUH rules apply to a plan-area lot?** Accessory units count toward up to 4 units on one lot in SSMUH areas (3 on lots 280 m² or less, 4 on larger), with suites under 50% of the principal unit and coach/garden suites to 15% of the lot / 100 m² max — and the SSMUH bylaw number itself is UNCONFIRMED (§3). https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites
- **Q: Can ALR land be planned or rezoned into an urban subdivision?** Not through the City alone: exclusion from the ALR needs ALC approval, and inside the ALR the residence frame and the A1/A2 minimums (8.0 ha / 16.0 ha) govern regardless of what a nearby corridor plan shows. The page's related "72% in the ALR" claim is UNVERIFIED (§2). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How did the plan areas sell last month?** No plan-area figures exist in the permitted source. At sub-area level (context only, never plan-area data): Central Abbotsford — containing City Centre, Historic Downtown and UDistrict — recorded 256 combined attached listings with 35 sold (14%) in September 2026, and Abbotsford East — containing McKee — held the city's largest detached inventory (165). (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report.)
- **Q: What should I check before pricing rezoning potential?** Both plan layers for the exact parcel (WebMap), its DPA overlays, any registered geotechnical or heritage constraints (McKee slope land; Clayburn Village Heritage Conservation Area), and the current DCC rates from the City — DCC figures are UNVERIFIED in this file, so budget them from the City, not from this page. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Does a plan designation change whether my lot is strata or fee-simple?** No — designation governs use, not tenure. A bare-land strata lot stays strata (Form B diligence, §3 block) whatever the plan says, and ALR ground cannot be stratified at all. (§2.)
- **Q: What taxes apply if I buy assembly land?** General PTT tiers and anchors are in §3 (including the $93,000 anchor at $3,500,000 and the open $78,000 conflict); GST at the CRA GI-003 level for land; Abbotsford property taxes on the July 2, 2026 calendar. The home-based exemptions (first-time; newly built) attach to qualifying homes, not to bare land held as assembly land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
