## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), NO lettered schedules:** farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)"; the former area plans (including the North Delta Area Plan, Schedule C.1, and the Tsawwassen Area Plan, Schedule D.1) belonged to the repealed Bylaw No. 3950 framework. A "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw No. 8400" claim is an ERROR wherever it is met. Development Permit Areas are in Appendix B (ND12, ND3, ND1 for North Delta, §1) — a lot inside one carries different expectations and review than an interior lot outside them. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Zoning bylaw number — CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted:** City pages cite Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017, while an internal document title reads "8400, 2025" — 8400 is the OCP's number. - **SSMUH / added units:** Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing Bylaws 8394 and 8395 were adopted June 24, 2024 — small-scale multi-unit housing is permitted on single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies, and Delta permits secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites in combination where the zoning applies. Confirm the parcel's zone and eligibility before pricing in added-unit potential; SSMUH is added-unit potential on an existing lot, not a subdivision right. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/
- **Subdivision and servicing — adoption HELD UNCONFIRMED:** a City council report (January 24, 2024) presents the Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 to repeal and replace Bylaw No. 7162, 2015, made under s. 506 of the Local Government Act to regulate works and services on subdivision. Final adoption status and parcel-level standards of Bylaw No. 8288: UNCONFIRMED in this program — document the process (City subdivision application, servicing to the standard the City confirms for the file) and confirm the governing bylaw with the City before pricing a subdivision. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **ALR block (applies parcel by parcel, only where the parcel is in the reserve):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for any North Delta parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for a Sunbury Terrace-type lot, wherever one trades):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** an $800,000 parcel, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000**; a fully-qualifying first-time buyer on the same inputs pays **$6,000** (2% × the $300,000 above the $500,000 exempt portion). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Delta property taxes and utilities:** City of Delta 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on balances outstanding after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 2, 2026; the approved 2026 Financial Plan included a 2.9% property tax increase for City operational services, and the 2026 flat-rate utility fee is $1,658 for a single-family home.
