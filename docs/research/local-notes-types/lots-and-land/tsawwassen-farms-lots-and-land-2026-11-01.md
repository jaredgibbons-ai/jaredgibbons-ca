## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Topic scope: farm land, working farms and agricultural land in and around Tsawwassen — principally the farm land on the Tsawwassen edges (the Southlands side and the farm belt between Tsawwassen and Ladner), which Delta's Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 8400, 2024) designates Agriculture (A), the designation whose stated purpose is to maintain agricultural lands (OCP s. 1.5.1, Map 2). The urban Tsawwassen neighbourhoods themselves are residential; the farm topic is the agricultural fringe, not the town centre. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Tsawwassen is one of Delta's three communities (Ladner, Tsawwassen and North Delta), planned under OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — the current OCP consolidated the former area plans (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950) into a single Land Use Plan, and its Agriculture policies (OCP s. 2.6, informed by the City's 2023 Agricultural Plan) apply to Delta's agricultural land. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Treaty-land boundary: Tsawwassen First Nation Lands sit on Tsawwassen's western edge. Tsawwassen First Nation is a self-governing First Nation with jurisdiction over land management on Tsawwassen Lands — over 724 hectares transferred under the Tsawwassen First Nation Final Agreement — so City of Delta zoning and Delta farm parcels do not extend onto Tsawwassen Lands. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- There is no single MLS sub-area for this topic; Tsawwassen farm parcels sit at the edges of the board's Tsawwassen sub-areas and in the Ladner-side farm belt (East Delta / Ladner Rural ground), and a specific parcel's sub-area label is a listing question, not a planning fact. ## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**The farm ground here is a fringe, not a belt of its own — stated plainly at the top. It is the southern end of Delta's agricultural land, designated Agriculture (A) in the OCP and governed by the OCP's Agriculture policies (s. 2.6), with ALC housing and use rules layered over Delta's A-zone zoning where a parcel is in the ALR. This file presents NO listing card as a confirmed farm operation in any named sector: the feeds behind this topic are generic, no specific operation, crop, livestock type or quota holding is confirmed for any parcel here, and farm-specific market figures are UNVERIFIED.**

- Land base: working farms and bare agricultural land on the Agriculture (A)-designated fringe, farm parcels in the Southlands area, and rural acreages with farm potential toward the Ladner side of the belt. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Tenure: farm parcels are overwhelmingly freehold; strata farm product is not a Delta pattern — no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source, and ALR residences cannot be stratified in the ordinary way (see §3). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/tsawwassen-farms.md)
- Treaty-land exclusion: Tsawwassen Lands parcels are under Tsawwassen First Nation jurisdiction, not City of Delta zoning or the Delta farm feed, and are excluded from this file's scope; any leasehold, corporation-share, undivided-interest, co-op, non-market rental or park pad-rent holding on the fringe would likewise not be freehold farm product and is not counted — none was verified in this pass: UNCONFIRMED, none counted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- Soil: no agricultural capability class for the Tsawwassen-edge farm land was confirmed from a text source in this pass; capability is mapped per parcel in the BC Soil Information Finder, and the ALC soils page defines the classes from Class 1 (no or very slight limitations) through the lower classes — any specific parcel's class is UNVERIFIED until that map is read. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/resources/soils-and-agricultural-capability/
- Water on the ground: the provincial GWELLS application is the registry for recorded wells and aquifers; older wells may not appear in the registry; under the Water Sustainability Act, domestic groundwater use does not need a licence, and non-domestic use, including irrigation, requires a water licence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- Flood/dike reality: the City of Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure; the City is raising sections of the Boundary Bay dike between 72 Street and 88 Street with provincial grant funding. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Farm classification matters to value: a parcel that keeps BC Assessment farm classification must keep meeting the minimum gross farm income thresholds, which scale with the size of the operation (three tiers in §3). https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Delta plan:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, no lettered schedules; farm land designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1 purpose: maintain agricultural lands and promote a thriving agricultural sector; s. 2.6.1 recognizes farming as the primary use of agricultural land; Agriculture policies at s. 2.6 protect the ALR and soil-based agriculture). A parcel's reserve status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **ALR block:** subdivision of ALR land needs ALC approval; residences cannot be stratified — ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6 treats a strata plan as a plan that causes subdivision in the ALR (SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Housing caps: principal residence ≤500 m² total floor area (with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction), secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence, and one additional detached residence at 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller / 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha, where its conditions are met and Delta bylaws also permit it; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class — three tiers (BC Assessment, by Oct 31):** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices of **$2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha** — a flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Delta farm zoning — number flagged, not asserted:** the research cites the A1 Agriculture zone of Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600; the program's standing correction records the Delta zoning-bylaw number as CONTESTED (7600, 2017 vs "8400, 2025"), so the number is flagged here, never asserted as settled, and zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. In the A1 zone as cited: one farm house per lot, farm house maximum 500 m², farm home plate maximum 3,600 m², one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit of maximum 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **Subdivision of farm land:** in the A1 zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is permitted only where it complies with the Official Community Plan Bylaw and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy; A2 (s. 10.20.6) also sets an 8 ha minimum, and A3 (s. 10.30.6) does not permit subdivision. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **SSMUH / DSSS (urban Delta, for contrast):** SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395 (adopted June 24, 2024) govern single-detached and duplex lots, not ALR farm parcels. Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288 adoption is held UNCONFIRMED in this file (see FILE STATUS). Parcel-specific subdivision/servicing requirements beyond the zone rules above: UNCONFIRMED — confirm with the City.
- **Hazard DPA SD3 + streamside:** on the Boundary Bay foreshore and the Southlands, OCP hazard DPA SD3 provides a 2.9 m Geodetic Survey of Canada datum floor level, a 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation and a 7.5 m tidal setback; all properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — farm ditches count. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Septic:** a home not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite sewage disposal system that complies with the Sewerage System Regulation; only an Authorized Person can design, install, repair, maintain or inspect a sewerage system, and an Authorized Person must file a record of sewerage system before construction and a letter of certification after. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Delta taxes:** due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **PTT:** 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above; further 2% on residential portion over $3,000,000 (mixed-class: residential portion only; farm-class land with a dwelling: up to 0.5 ha treated as residential, per the general PTT frame). FTHB PTT exemption on the first $500,000, full at ≤$835,000 (max $8,000, partial to $860,000) and the Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000) are residential programs whose fit to a farm purchase is parcel- and use-specific. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant/farm land:** at CRA GI-003 level only for vacant land — developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE, individual's personal-use land generally exempt, finer points UNCONFIRMED. GST treatment of farm land itself (farm-use and going-concern questions) is transaction-specific: UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel, and no general farm GST rate is stated here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a new-housing rebate, not a farm-land rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there real farmland in Tsawwassen, or is it all residential?** Both — the OCP designates farm land on the Tsawwassen edges Agriculture (A) (Bylaw No. 8400, 2024, s. 1.5.1), ALR rules apply where land is in the ALR, and the urban neighbourhoods are residential. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Does a listing card on this topic confirm a working farm operation?** No — the feeds are generic and no card is presented here as a confirmed operation in any named sector; what a specific parcel actually produces is a per-parcel diligence fact, UNCONFIRMED from the feed alone. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/tsawwassen-farms.md)
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land near Tsawwassen?** A principal residence built without an ALC application: 500 m² or less total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; Delta's A1 zone as cited sets the same 500 m² farm house maximum and a 3,600 m² farm home plate maximum. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a farm have a second dwelling?** One additional residence without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and Delta bylaws must also permit it; the A1 zone as cited allows one accessory farm dwelling unit within those same limits, plus one secondary suite inside the farm house only. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Delta farm parcel?** Only within tight limits: a new A1 lot needs a minimum of 8 ha, OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy; A3 zoning does not permit subdivision. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: What income does a farm need to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income (farm gate) thresholds: $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: How do I check whether a parcel is in the ALR?** On the Agricultural Land Commission's maps, by PID or civic address — reserve status is parcel-specific. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Does quota come with a farm purchase here?** UNVERIFIED — no marketing-board source was opened in this research, so no quota position is stated for any sector; verify quota directly with the relevant board before pricing it into an offer. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/tsawwassen-farms.md)
- **Q: Does Delta zoning apply on Tsawwassen Lands?** No — Tsawwassen First Nation has jurisdiction over land management on Tsawwassen Lands (over 724 hectares under the Final Agreement); establish which side of the treaty-land boundary a parcel sits on before applying Delta or ALR farm rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **Q: What should I check on a Southlands-side parcel?** Hazard DPA SD3 (2.9 m floor level, 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation, 7.5 m tidal setback), the Streamside DPA if any ditch or slough is within 30 m, the well record in GWELLS, and any water licence position if the operation irrigates. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
