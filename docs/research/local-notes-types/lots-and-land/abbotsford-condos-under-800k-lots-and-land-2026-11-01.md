## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning and OCP: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 applies city-wide, under Abbotsforward 2050, the CURRENT Official Community Plan.** Stale "2600-2016" / "2500-2016" OCP citations met in program material are Jerry sweep items and are not used as the current citation here. The City Centre (Bylaw 2913-2019) and Historic Downtown (Bylaw 2920-2019) neighbourhood plans are the adopted plans for the core. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- **SSMUH / suites / coach houses (Abbotsford specifics):** the zoning bylaw's s. 140 small-scale multi-unit provision allows 3 units on lots 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots greater than 280 m² in the stated conditions, and s. 140.3.8 states one secondary suite is permitted in all single detached dwellings. The secondary-suite size cap is **under 50%** of the size of the principal unit, and a coach/garden suite is capped at **15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m²** (Abbotsford only figures). **The SSMUH amending bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED** — the research file's "3560-2024" assertion is not asserted here (FILE STATUS block). https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140 and https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites
- **Subdivision and development process:** subdivision and development run through the City under the Zoning Bylaw and the OCP designations for the parcel; **the Development Bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED** — the process is documented here, no number is asserted, and requirements are confirmed with the City for the parcel. (Abbotsford frame, carried in the FILE STATUS block.)
- **BC strata block — applies in full to any strata lot in the city, including any bare-land strata lot:** Form B (SPA s. 59), provided within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. Abbotsford's verified 55+ strata total in this program is 26 complexes (55-plus sibling) — band status UNCONFIRMED for every one of them. BC strata disputes can be resolved using the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **ALR block — full form (Abbotsford's rural sub-areas):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; one additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Farm class (BC Assessment) in full three-tier form: $2,500 of gross farm income at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Property transfer tax:** tiers and worked examples are in §2 (inputs + tier math, as band discipline requires). First-time buyers' exemption and newly built home exemption (full to $1,100,000, phasing out by $1,150,000) are HOME exemptions — not bare-lot exemptions; a bare lot's eligibility is UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary, with the date discrepancy stated in-line:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA — this date governs**) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — new housing, NOT bare land. **Discrepancy noted, not overwritten:** five C1 Abbotsford condo files in this program carry March 12, 2026 for the assent with the discrepancy noted; any March 12 reading met in a sibling is superseded by CRA's March 13. The GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
