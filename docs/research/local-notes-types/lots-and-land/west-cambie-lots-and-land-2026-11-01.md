## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**West Cambie has no standing bare-land subdivision market — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan, and no presale LOT offering was verified on an official or developer source in the research file or the siblings. What trades as land here is (a) fee-simple house lots in The Oaks, Odlinwood and the older Alexandra pattern, and (b) assembly / rezoning ground in and around the Alexandra pocket, where the completed product is strata apartments and townhomes documented in the siblings — never lot inventory.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **The Oaks and Odlinwood house lots:** freehold detached houses in The Oaks (described in the Area Plan as a low-scale residential neighbourhood) and Odlinwood (an established residential neighbourhood), and on the older Alexandra lots. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **The older Alexandra pattern — the area's defining lot character:** single-family homes on 0.4 ha lots in the older pattern, under Single Detached (RS1/F) in the older Alexandra pattern shown in the Area Plan and City applications, all under Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500. Current parcel sizes vary by zone; the specific zone and minimums for a lot are confirmed with the City. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **Serviced position — split, as the Area Plan states it:** the Alexandra part of West Cambie was described in the Area Plan as remaining on septic sewage disposal in its older lot pattern — a material servicing check for older Alexandra lots; wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS registry where they exist. No equivalent septic position is documented for The Oaks / Odlinwood streets in the research — servicing for any single parcel is confirmed with the City, UNCONFIRMED parcel-by-parcel here. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** West Cambie is not described as Agricultural Land Reserve land in the Area Plan, and no ALR parcel or ALR percentage split for the area is stated in the research. ALR status remains a per-address check on ALC maps for any parcel where it matters. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **Flood position — applies to every lot:** Richmond is islands in the Fraser River floodplain at an average of 1 metre above sea level, protected by 49 km of dikes designed for a 1:500 event, with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres under way; all lands are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, with flood construction levels (FCLs) habitable floors must meet unless exempt. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Noise / environment position on the Alexandra ground:** the Area Plan states the area is affected by aircraft noise from Vancouver International Airport, that **no new single-family development is permitted in Alexandra** due to the Official Community Plan Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy, and that an Environmentally Sensitive Area is in the south-east portion of Alexandra. That policy line is the single biggest constraint on what an older Alexandra lot can become. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **Market context:** none quoted. Richmond is outside SnapStats coverage in this program — no inventory, median or sales-ratio figure exists for West Cambie in the sources held, and no other stats source is substituted. Land has no SnapStats category in any case. (Research file above, §2.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP:** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 (zones met here include RS1/F in the older Alexandra pattern, site-specific Town Housing ZT67 and ZT71 on Alexandra Road, and RSM on the 9720 Cambie Road site) under Richmond's 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000; the West Cambie Area Plan is Schedule 2.11A to the OCP (plan adoption July 24, 2006), with area plans held in Schedule 2 under Bylaws 7100 and 9000. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm and https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states it:** Richmond allows at least one secondary suite and/or an accessory dwelling unit per lot on covered lots, with provincial small-scale multi-unit housing minimums of three units on lots 280 m² or less and four units on lots greater than 280 m². In Alexandra, that general position is cut across by the aircraft-noise policy below — never price SSMUH potential on an Alexandra lot without checking both. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Aircraft-noise constraint (Alexandra):** no new single-family development is permitted in Alexandra under the OCP Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy, per the Area Plan — a lot-level development constraint, not a marketing footnote. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision follows the parcel's zone minimums under Bylaw 8500 (confirmed with the City lot by lot; no West Cambie-specific minimum is quoted beyond the older Alexandra 0.4 ha pattern the Area Plan describes). Fresh check for this file (Nov 2, 2026): the City's own alphabetical bylaw list carries "Subdivision and Development — Bylaw 8751 (2011)" and "Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 (2011)" — Richmond's subdivision/servicing instrument is Bylaw 8751, with cost recovery under Bylaw 8752; parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City. Separately, the older Alexandra septic position in Section 2 means a servicing agreement / sewer-connection check belongs at the front of any older-lot file. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **Flood:** all lands are designated flood plain under Bylaw No. 8204 and habitable floors must meet the FCL for the area unless exempt; confirm the FCL and any flood covenant on title before planning a build or major renovation. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **ADEU carrying cost (Alexandra ground only):** buildings on the City's ADEU list (Alexandra Road, Odlin Road, Tomicki Avenue) carry a district-energy utility charge on top of strata fees and standard utilities — pull the actual charge from the strata documents for any Alexandra purchase. This position belongs to West Cambie / Alexandra only; it is not carried into any other Richmond file in this batch. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_2_-_2021_Annual_General_Meeting_Resolution_of_the_Lulu_Island_Energy_Company_Sp58691.pdf
- **Covenant vs bylaw — the Omega LEMR point:** strata rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022, but Omega's Low End Market Rental units are restricted by a registered housing **covenant**, not a bylaw — covenants were not voided and those units remain rental-restricted today. Check whether a specific Omega unit is a market strata lot or an LEMR covenant unit from title before pricing it. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/west-cambie-condos-2026-11-01.md, §3.
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no West Cambie ALR parcel is documented here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm-class thresholds are **not** asserted in this file — the file's own research documents no farm-class position for West Cambie, and the Area Plan does not describe the area as ALR land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata rules (no such stock verified here; stated so the form is never mis-sold):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). That distinction is live on an Alexandra assembly sale by a developer versus an individual owner's long-held lot sale. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a West Cambie lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no West Cambie-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for West Cambie, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Richmond property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026. Flat-rate utilities were due March 31, 2026 with the 10% early-payment discount lost if paid late; the typical single-family metered total for 2026 is $2,251 per year (water $631, sewer $835, garbage/recycling/environmental programs $443, flood protection $342) — Alexandra strata additionally carry the ADEU charge above, which appears in neither figure. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm and https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/finance/utility-fees/utilityrates.htm

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant-lot subdivision I can buy into in West Cambie?** No verified one — no named bare-land subdivision and no presale lot offering was verified. Land trades as house lots and as Alexandra assembly ground whose completed product is strata. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Why do older Alexandra lots price so differently from The Oaks streets?** Different land logic entirely: the older Alexandra pattern is 0.4 ha lots with a septic history in the Area Plan and an aircraft-noise policy barring new single-family development in Alexandra, while The Oaks is a low-scale house neighbourhood and Odlinwood an established one. Do not comp across that line without adjusting. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **Q: Is an older Alexandra lot on sewer?** The Area Plan describes the older Alexandra pattern as remaining on septic sewage disposal — confirm sewer connection status and cost for the specific lot before subject removal; wells, where they exist, are in the provincial GWELLS registry. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **Q: Can I build a new single-family house on an Alexandra lot?** The Area Plan states no new single-family development is permitted in Alexandra under the OCP Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy. Any redevelopment thesis for Alexandra ground has to start from that policy, the parcel's zone, and the City — not from the lot size alone. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **Q: What extra carrying cost comes with Alexandra strata ground?** The Alexandra District Energy Utility: buildings on Alexandra Road, Odlin Road and Tomicki Avenue connect to it, and its charges sit on top of the strata fee. Pull the charge from the strata documents. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_2_-_2021_Annual_General_Meeting_Resolution_of_the_Lulu_Island_Energy_Company_Sp58691.pdf
- **Q: Is every Omega unit a normal market condo?** No — some Omega units are Low End Market Rental homes secured by a registered covenant restricting who they can be rented to and at what rent, and that covenant survives the 2022 voiding of rental bylaws. Confirm from title whether the unit is market or LEMR before pricing. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/west-cambie-condos-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **Q: What is happening at 9720 Cambie Road — can I buy a lot or home there?** Nothing is for sale as land on this evidence: RZ 23-015980 is a rezoning application for an apartment on an RSM/XL-zoned site, documented as an application only — not approved, not a presale. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **Q: Is my lot in a floodplain?** Yes — all Richmond lands are designated flood plain under Bylaw No. 8204, behind a dike system designed for a 1:500 event; habitable floors must meet the area FCL unless exempt. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Q: Can a West Cambie house lot add a suite or accessory unit?** Richmond allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot on covered lots, with provincial minimums of three units on lots 280 m² or less and four on larger lots — but in Alexandra the aircraft-noise policy governs what can actually be built, so check both layers. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: Which schools serve a West Cambie lot?** By exact address only — the one locator result documented in the research (9671 Odlin Road → Tomsett Elementary → MacNeill Secondary) does not transfer to other streets. Run the address in the SD38 locator. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
