## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market in Cape Horn. The slope trades as fee-simple house lots in an established detached-house area — the City's own 2022 permit record for planning areas 15/16 is entirely single-family / secondary-suite / carriage-house form. The one large development site filed under this sub-area, Fraser Mills, sits below the slope on the Fraser River waterfront and sells presale strata units, not lots. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale LOT offering was verified.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **Established-slope house lots:** a detached-led sub-area (18 detached listings against 13 attached listings in September 2026), and the City's 2022 development statistics for Cape Horn record 18 net residential units created that year (36 created, 18 demolished), all in single-family / secondary-suite / carriage-house form, with no townhouse or apartment units. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/291/Cape-Horn-C15-C16-PDF?bidId=
- **Lot character from the zone minimums, as stated:** small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3 under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 — minimum lot size for subdivision is 650 m² with 18.5 m width for R-1 and R-3, and 465 m² with 12.5 m width for R-2. These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size; the zone and dimensions of a specific lot are confirmed parcel by parcel. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Serviced position:** Coquitlam's SSMUH rules as adopted exclude lots not connected to municipal water or sewer — so a lot's connection status decides its added-unit potential, and property-level septic or well status is UNVERIFIED in the research. Coquitlam utility charges cover water, sewer and drainage and solid waste. Confirm servicing for the specific parcel with the City. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** ALR status for the sub-area is UNVERIFIED in the research; ALR status for a specific property is checked in the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder. No ALR percentage split is stated. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Slope and watercourse diligence:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam; Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and s.523 covers riparian areas. Slope / geotechnical status for a specific lot: UNVERIFIED. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — City of Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996:** the small-scale residential zones on the slope are R-1, R-2 and R-3, with the subdivision minimums in Section 2. Confirm the parcel's zone on the City's zoning maps. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **OCP:** the City's frequently-requested bylaws list names the Citywide Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 3479, 2001 and also lists an Official Community Plan No. 5511, 2026 — which instrument is current at the time of a specific application is UNCONFIRMED in this file's research; confirm with the City. A Cape Horn-specific neighbourhood plan: UNVERIFIED in the research; Southwest Coquitlam planning (including the Transit-Oriented Areas Update and the Southwest Shoulders and Corridors amendments) covers the south-west part of the city. https://www.coquitlam.ca/556/Frequently-Requested-Bylaws and http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1968
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states it:** up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Subdivision + servicing:** subdivision follows the zone minimums in Section 2, and the City's frequently-requested bylaws list names the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 3558, 2003 as Coquitlam's servicing instrument — confirm its current standards and the parcel's requirements with the City; no standard is quoted from it here beyond its existence on the City's own list. https://www.coquitlam.ca/556/Frequently-Requested-Bylaws
- **Watercourse / flood diligence:** a Watercourse Protection Development Permit can be required within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse; Zoning Bylaw s.519 flood protection setbacks and s.523 riparian areas apply as the bylaw states them. The Fraser Mills site's floodplain position (preloaded 20 feet above median sea level, per the developer) is site-specific and does not transfer to slope lots. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Infrastructure diligence — Coquitlam Water Main, Cape Horn Section:** Metro Vancouver is constructing the Coquitlam Water Main (approximately 12 km in four sections); the Cape Horn Section is approximately 4 km long and 2.2 m in diameter, running from Westwood Street and Dewdney Trunk Road to Hickey Drive and Mariner Way, including a trenchless tunnel crossing under Lougheed Highway — construction on the Mariner Way corridor is a live diligence item for slope addresses. https://metrovancouver.org:443/services/water/coquitlam-water-main-cape-horn-section
- **Bare-land strata rules (applies to any bare-land strata lot, if one is ever verified here):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Cape Horn ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Cape Horn lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Cape Horn-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Cape Horn, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Coquitlam property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property tax payment was due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants); the City's 2026 budget materials record an average-household total property-charges increase of $137 (taxation, water, sewer/drainage and solid waste combined), and metered properties under the Voluntary Residential Water Metering Program are billed every four months instead of the annual flat rate. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes and https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17426/2026-Budget-Highlights

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot subdivision on the Cape Horn slope?** No verified one — the slope is an established detached-house area whose 2022 City permit record is entirely single-family / suite / carriage-house form. Land trades as built house lots. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create here?** It depends on the parcel's zone: 650 m² with 18.5 m width in R-1 and R-3, and 465 m² with 12.5 m width in R-2, under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996. Confirm the zone before pricing any split. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Q: Can a Cape Horn house lot add units?** Yes under the City's SSMUH rules — up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes — but lots not connected to municipal water or sewer fall outside the rules, so confirm services and the exact zone first. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Q: Is Fraser Mills a land purchase?** No — it is a presale strata development below the slope (Debut, Encore, Chapter by Beedie Living) filed under Cape Horn by listing sources. You buy a presale unit under REDMA, documented in the condos sibling, not a serviced lot. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Can I buy a home at Crescentview on Cape Horn Avenue?** Tenure first: the only sources checked present it as a rental community and its strata/rental status is UNCONFIRMED — establish tenure on a title or strata source before treating any home there as purchasable. https://www.apartments.com/crescentview-townhomes-coquitlam-bc/3dp9wwd/
- **Q: What does the water main mean for a Mariner Way lot?** Metro Vancouver's Cape Horn Section runs to Hickey Drive and Mariner Way with a tunnel crossing under Lougheed Highway — confirm the construction window and any shaft locations against the specific address before closing. https://metrovancouver.org:443/services/water/coquitlam-water-main-cape-horn-section
- **Q: Does the interchange help or hurt a lot's value?** Both conversations are the same lot: the ramps are the access premium and the noise/air-quality question. The research gives no sub-area discount figure — price view-and-noise lots street by street, and walk the lot at rush hour. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cape-horn.md, §6)
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Cape Horn lot?** By exact address only — no Cape Horn catchment is verified in this file; the one locator run on file was for a Poirier Street library address outside the sub-area and does not transfer. Run the address in the SD43 locator. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q: What setbacks apply near a ravine or creek on the slope?** A Watercourse Protection Development Permit can be required within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank, and the zoning bylaw's ss.519/523 flood and riparian setbacks apply — check the specific lot early. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
