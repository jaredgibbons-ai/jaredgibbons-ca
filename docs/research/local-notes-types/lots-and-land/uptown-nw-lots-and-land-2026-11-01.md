## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No named bare-land subdivision and no bare-land strata community is verified for Uptown — stated plainly at the top. Uptown is a built-out plateau: what trades as "land" here is the individual fee-simple house lot on the RS side streets, priced against its suite/laneway potential and its distance from the Sixth Street core, plus strata apartment ground along the core itself, which is built stock counted in the condos program — not lot inventory. No lot count, lot-size table or offering is verified for this sub-area and none is stated.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- The residential side streets off the Sixth Street core are single-detached homes under the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series) of Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 — a mix of the city's older housing stock and later infill. RS-1 (s.310) sets a minimum site area of **557.40 sq. m. (6,000 sq. ft.)** and a floor space ratio of **0.5**. Freehold dominates the side streets: a fee-simple Uptown lot carries no strata, no Form B and no strata fees — title, City bylaws and any registered covenants govern. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf (Research file as above)
- Added-homes potential is part of the lot's value: the RS districts allow secondary suites, and laneway / carriage houses are regulated under the same bylaw — a standard check on any Uptown house purchase, and documented, permitted suite space is a marketable feature on resale. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Proposed_Laneway_and_Carraige_House_Zoning_Bylaw_Amendment_Bylaw_28_August_2017.pdf (Research file as above)

A $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in one townhomes sibling; it omits the further 2% tier and is an OPEN summary-pass reconciliation item — this file follows **$93,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
