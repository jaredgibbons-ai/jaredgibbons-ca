## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP:** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 (Single Detached RS1/RS2, Section 8.1, for this pocket) under Richmond's 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000, with area and sub-area plans held in Schedule 2 under Bylaws 7100 and 9000; the Laurelwood Sub-Area Plan is the plan context for the Granville Avenue / Railway Avenue bend. Quilchena itself has no OCP area or sub-area plan name. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm and https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/laurelwood562.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states it:** the City states at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit such as a coach house is allowed per lot on covered lots, with Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (RSM) zoning applied under Bill 44 and provincial minimums of three units on lots 280 m² or less and four units on larger lots. Confirm the parcel's subzone and coverage before pricing in added-unit value. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision follows the RS1/RS2 subzone minimums in Section 2. Fresh check for this file (Nov 2, 2026): the City's own alphabetical bylaw list carries "Subdivision and Development — Bylaw 8751 (2011)" and "Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 (2011)" — so Richmond's subdivision/servicing instrument is Bylaw 8751, with cost recovery under Bylaw 8752; its parcel-level standards are not quoted here and are confirmed with the City for the specific lot. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **Flood — the defining lot rule in Richmond:** all lands are designated flood plain under Bylaw No. 8204 and habitable floors must meet the FCL for the area unless exempt; confirm the lot's FCL, and any flood covenant on title, before planning a build or major renovation. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **No ADEU here:** the Alexandra District Energy Utility is a West Cambie / Alexandra utility documented in the west-cambie files only. No ADEU charge position is documented for any Quilchena lot in the research, and none is imported. Basis: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-cambie.md, §4 (Alexandra pocket only).
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no pocket ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm-class thresholds are **not** asserted in this file — the file's own research documents no farm-class position for this pocket. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata rules (no such stock verified here; stated so the form is never mis-sold):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Quilchena lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Quilchena-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Quilchena, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Richmond property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid 2026 taxes after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% penalty on any outstanding balance after September 1, 2026. Annual flat-rate utility payments were due March 31, 2026, with a 10% discount only for payments received on or before the due date; the City's discounted cost for a typical single-family metered residence for 2026 totals $2,251 per year (water $631, sewer $835, garbage/recycling/environmental programs $443, flood protection $342) — the $342 flood-protection line is a holding cost on every Richmond lot. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm and https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/finance/utility-fees/utilityrates.htm

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant-lot subdivision I can buy into in Quilchena?** No verified one — the pocket is established house streets. No named bare-land subdivision and no presale lot offering was verified; land trades as built house lots. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How small a lot can be created here?** It depends entirely on the parcel's RS1/RS2 subzone: Section 8.1 minimums run from 360 m² (RS1/B, RS2/B) through 550 m² (RS1/E) and 828 m² (RS1/F) to 2,000 m² (RS1/G). Confirm the subzone with the City before pricing any split. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=24187
- **Q: Can a Quilchena house lot add a suite or a coach house?** The Zoning Bylaw lists a secondary suite as a secondary use in RS1/RS2, and the City states at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit such as a coach house is allowed per lot on covered lots, with provincial SSMUH minimums of three units on lots 280 m² or less and four on larger lots — eligibility is parcel-specific. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: Is Quilchena Green a Quilchena lot or home I can buy here?** No — Quilchena Green (91 units, LMS423) is a verified 55+ strata in Terra Nova, north-west of this pocket. Same first word, different sub-area; it is counted in no total in this file. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/quilchena-ri-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **Q: A listing calls 7580 No. 1 Road "Quilchena RI" — is that in this pocket?** On MLS geography, yes; on the research file's pocket geography, which this file follows, no — it sits on the No. 1 Road corridor south of the pocket. Search both geographies or you will miss / mis-file stock. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/quilchena-ri-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **Q: Is my lot in a floodplain, and what does that cost me to build?** Yes — all Richmond lands are designated flood plain under Bylaw No. 8204 and habitable floors must meet the area FCL unless exempt. Confirm the FCL and any flood covenant before planning a build. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Q: Is a Quilchena lot serviced?** UNCONFIRMED at pocket level — no City source read confirms septic/well detail for this pocket. Confirm the specific parcel's water, sewer and drainage connections with the City before writing. (Research file, §4.)
- **Q: What does subdivision servicing run through in Richmond?** The City's bylaw list carries the Subdivision and Development Bylaw 8751 (2011) and the Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 (2011) — servicing works are required through the subdivision process under that framework, with costs recovered as those bylaws provide. Parcel-level standards: confirm with the City. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Quilchena lot?** By exact address only — the one locator result documented in the research (3760 Moresby Drive → Quilchena Elementary → Boyd Secondary) does not transfer to other streets. Run the address in the SD38 locator. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
