## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: Caulfeild has no meaningful bare-land market. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community, and no presale LOT offering was verified inside Caulfeild on any source checked — the research file and the siblings record the same position for their own forms. What trades as land here is the individual fee-simple lot — inside the former Land Use Contract lands under the CD86 (Caulfeild Zone) rules, or on RS-zone streets nearby — almost always with a detached house on it. The zoning itself explains the shape of the market: in CD86 Residential Area A, stratification of buildings or land is prohibited. No lot count or lot price is stated.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **CD86 lots (the former Land Use Contract area):** the former Caulfeild Land Use Contract area is now zoned CD86 (Caulfeild Zone) in Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010, set by Amendment Bylaw No. 5321, 2024. CD86 has Residential Area A, Residential Area B, a Commercial Area and a Parks and Greenspaces Area; the minimum permitted site area for a residential parcel is the site area lawfully registered on the parcel (no new minimum is imposed), and Residential Area A density is a maximum of three dwelling units per lot. A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Appendix%20B%20-%205321%20Zoning%20Bylaw%20No%204662%202010%20Amendment%20Bylaw%20No%205321%202024.pdf
- **RS lots nearby:** Section 200 of Bylaw No. 4662 states minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5. These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size — the zone for a specific lot is confirmed on the District's Zoning Map. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Heritage-shaped lots (Lower Caulfeild):** Lower Caulfeild is a designated Heritage Conservation Area; the District states Francis Caulfeild planned and designed the subdivision from 1899 to control the character of later development, and owners within the area need a Heritage Alteration Permit for any work. The 1899 plan is the subdivision fabric of Lower Caulfeild — its lots are heritage-regulated fee-simple lots, not a current lot offering. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/heritage-conservation/lower-caulfeild-heritage-conservation-area
- **Bare-land strata: none verified.** No bare-land strata community in Caulfeild was confirmed in the research or the siblings, and CD86 Residential Area A prohibits stratification of buildings or land outright. Stated plainly for any strata lot form, if one ever surfaced outside CD86: a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — Form B / strata fees / bylaws apply to the lot (Section 3) — but no such stock is documented here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **Serviced vs unserviced:** West Vancouver water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly by the District — the serviced position for District lots as the District states it. Septic or well servicing by street is UNVERIFIED in the research (UNVERIFIED item 8) — no street-level claim is made, and a specific lot's services are confirmed with the District. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** parcel-level ALR status is UNVERIFIED in the research; no ALR split is stated for Caulfeild and none is invented. (Research file above.)
- **Hazard-shaped land:** all three District permit areas can apply in Caulfeild — the Foreshore Development Permit Area on Lower Caulfeild waterfront lots, the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area (within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface), and the Environmental Development Permit Area (within 15 metres of the top of a watercourse bank). Gross lot area is not buildable area here (Section 3). https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP (District):** Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 — including CD86 (Caulfeild Zone), set by Amendment Bylaw No. 5321, 2024, for the former Land Use Contract lands, and Section 200 for nearby RS zones — and Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, including Policy 2.3.4 for the Caulfeild Village Centre. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Appendix%20B%20-%205321%20Zoning%20Bylaw%20No%204662%202010%20Amendment%20Bylaw%20No%205321%202024.pdf
- **The stratification bar (CD86 Residential Area A):** stratification of buildings or land is prohibited under Amendment Bylaw No. 5321, 2024. Added units in Residential Area A (maximum three dwelling units per lot) therefore cannot be sold as separate strata lots — suite and coach-house value there is rental/family value on one fee-simple title, never strata-lot value. District building-permit guidance addresses secondary suite and detached secondary suite (coach house) applications in the single-family areas around CD86. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Appendix%20B%20-%205321%20Zoning%20Bylaw%20No%204662%202010%20Amendment%20Bylaw%20No%205321%202024.pdf
- **Ground-oriented density outside CD86, as the bylaw states it:** 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop (Bylaw 4662, ss.620–630). Inside CD86, the CD86 rules above govern — do not price a CD86 lot off the District-wide figure. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** inside CD86, subdivision runs against the registered-parcel rule (the minimum permitted site area is the site area lawfully registered on the parcel); on RS streets it runs against the Section 200 minimum site area for the lot's zone, confirmed on the District Zoning Map. A District subdivision/servicing bylaw NUMBER could not be confirmed on a primary source — held UNCONFIRMED, consistent with the District-wide assembly file; servicing for District lots is District water/sewer, metered and billed quarterly. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Heritage Alteration Permit (Lower Caulfeild):** any work inside the designated Heritage Conservation Area needs a Heritage Alteration Permit, on top of ordinary permits — flag it up front rather than letting it surface at subject removal. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/heritage-conservation/lower-caulfeild-heritage-conservation-area
- **Hazard development permits:** an **Environmental Development Permit** is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank; a **Wildfire Hazard Development Permit** is required for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface; a **Foreshore Development Permit** is required for work within the Foreshore Development Permit Area (waterfront property or property at risk of coastal flooding), which applies on Lower Caulfeild waterfront lots. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Strata block (the only strata form in the area is Caulfeild Cove, counted in the townhomes sibling; stated for any strata lot):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (a directory "rentals not allowed" line for Caulfeild Cove is void for long-term rentals on this rule); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions. A listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. No Caulfeild Cove age restriction is verified — it is NOT 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (no Caulfeild ALR parcel is confirmed here; standing rules for completeness only):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; residence caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **PTT on land + the additional school tax that actually bites here:** PTT general tiers 1% / 2% / 3% + further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000; first-time buyer and newly built home exemptions are home-purchase exemptions as BC states them — bare-land eligibility for the newly built home exemption: UNCONFIRMED here. **Additional school tax (documented in this file's research):** 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Caulfeild lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is VANCOUVER ONLY — it does NOT apply in West Vancouver. No Caulfeild-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in this file's research. Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition: no position is documented in this file's own cited sources — UNCONFIRMED, and none is conformed from other files.
- **District property taxes (documented in this file's research):** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026; approximately 50% of the tax bill is collected by the District and remitted to other taxing authorities. Water/sewer utilities are metered, billed quarterly. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there vacant land for sale in Caulfeild?** No meaningful bare-land market is documented — no vacant-lot subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified. "Land" here is an existing fee-simple CD86 or RS lot, usually with a house on it. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Can I stratify a Caulfeild lot or its buildings and sell the pieces?** Not in CD86 Residential Area A — stratification of buildings or land is prohibited under Amendment Bylaw No. 5321, 2024, even though a lot there may hold a maximum of three dwelling units. The units stay on one title. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Appendix%20B%20-%205321%20Zoning%20Bylaw%20No%204662%202010%20Amendment%20Bylaw%20No%205321%202024.pdf
- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create?** Inside CD86 there is no new minimum to meet: the minimum permitted site area for a residential parcel is the site area lawfully registered on the parcel. On nearby RS streets, Section 200 minimums run from 488 m² (RS5) to 8,094 m² (RS1). Confirm the parcel's zone on the District Zoning Map. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Q: What does the Lower Caulfeild heritage designation do to a rebuild?** Lower Caulfeild is a designated Heritage Conservation Area whose subdivision was planned by Francis Caulfeild from 1899; any work inside it needs a Heritage Alteration Permit on top of ordinary permits. Price the process in, up front. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/heritage-conservation/lower-caulfeild-heritage-conservation-area
- **Q: What permits does a waterfront or forest-edge lot trigger?** Possibly three: Foreshore DP (waterfront / coastal-flooding risk), Wildfire Hazard DP (within 100 m of an undisturbed forest interface), and Environmental DP (within 15 m of a watercourse bank top). Check all three before subjects. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Q: Is Caulfeild Cove a bare-land strata I can buy a lot in?** No — Caulfeild Cove (4957 Marine Drive) is a built strata townhouse complex (Plan VAS117), counted in the townhomes sibling; you buy a townhouse strata lot in an existing complex, with Form B diligence — not a bare lot. Its directory "rentals not allowed" line is void for long-term rentals since November 24, 2022, and no age restriction is verified for it. (Sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/caulfeild-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §2.)
- **Q: Is the lot serviced, or on a well/septic?** District water and sewer are metered and billed quarterly by the District; septic/well use by street is UNVERIFIED in the research — confirm the specific lot's services with the District. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Q: What extra tax applies at Caulfeild prices?** The Province's additional school tax where the residential assessment exceeds $3M: 0.2% on $3M–$4M and 0.4% above $4M for 2026, rising to 0.3% / 0.6% on January 1, 2027 — on top of District taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% + 5% penalties). https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Q: Will GST apply to my lot purchase?** At CRA GI-003 level: a developer/business sale or a newly subdivided lot is generally taxable; an individual's personal-use resale is generally exempt. Confirm per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Caulfeild lot?** By exact address only. The one settled locator run in this file (4685 Keith Road → Caulfeild Elementary → Rockridge Secondary) covers that address; both schools are physically in the area, but proximity is not catchment — run the listing address in the SD45 locator. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The subdivision is from 1899, and it still governs:** Francis Caulfeild planned and designed the Lower Caulfeild subdivision from 1899 specifically to control the character of later development — the Heritage Conservation Area and its Alteration Permit are that plan still operating, which is why Lower Caulfeild lots carry a permit layer no other West Vancouver lot carries in the same way. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/heritage-conservation/lower-caulfeild-heritage-conservation-area
- **The Land Use Contract is gone — old listing language is stale:** any Caulfeild document still describing the area as governed by a Land Use Contract predates Amendment Bylaw No. 5321, 2024, which replaced the contract with CD86 zoning (Residential Areas A and B, a Commercial Area and a Parks and Greenspaces Area). Underwrite suite and density questions off CD86, not the contract. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/development-regulations/contracts
- **The spelling is a search trap:** the District and SD45 use "Caulfeild" (second "u"); a comp search run only under "Caulfield" misses District documents and school material, and a search run only under the correct spelling can miss misspelled listings. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/heritage-conservation/lower-caulfeild-heritage-conservation-area
- **Caulfeild Cove and the municipal dock are history, not stock:** the District's Caulfeild heritage walk notes Caulfeild Cove and the municipal dock where pilot boats were moored, and St. Francis-in-the-Wood church — the cove name a buyer meets in heritage material is the waterfront place; the strata complex of the similar name is the townhouse stock counted once, in the townhomes file. https://timewalk.westvancouver.ca/routes/caulfeild/caulfeildDirectionsPrint.html
- **Three permit areas stack at the west end of the District:** foreshore at the water, wildfire at the forest interface toward Lighthouse Park, and watercourse setbacks inland — Caulfeild is the one sub-area in this batch where all three District permit regimes are documented as able to apply on ordinary house lots. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
