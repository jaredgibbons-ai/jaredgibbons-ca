## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- West Newton is in the City of Surrey, in the Newton community, covering the Newton lands west of King George Boulevard toward the Delta border (Scott Road / 120 Street side), including the Highway 99 industrial area at the south; exact boundary streets are as drawn in the City plan documents. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- The board/MLS sub-area name is "West Newton," the same wording as the City's West Newton land use plan area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- Nearby areas: East Newton (east, across King George Boulevard), Scottsdale / North Delta (west, across Scott Road), Bear Creek Green Timbers (north), and the Serpentine & Nicomekl lowlands (south). (Research file §1.)
- The Newton Town Centre at King George Blvd / 72 Ave, at the area's east edge, is the City-designated town centre serving the area, under the Newton Town Centre Plan. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/newton-town-centre-plan
- Transit relevant to land value: two RapidBus corridors bracket the area — the R1 on King George Boulevard (Newton Exchange to Guildford Exchange) and the R6 Scott Road RapidBus on the west edge (Scott Road Station to Newton Exchange); routes 323 (via 124 St) and 364 (via 64 Ave) run through the area. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/R6/direction/0/round-trip

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Verified position, stated plainly at the top: West Newton has NO meaningful bare-land market documented in its research file. No named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata, or lot-only development is verified on West Newton ground. The land that trades is (a) built-out fee-simple house lots west of King George Boulevard, bought for SSMUH / rebuild potential, and (b) industrial ground in the Highway 99 industrial area at the south of the plan area — a separate, non-residential land class that must never be presented as residential lot inventory. No lot counts, lot prices or lot-only inventory are asserted.**

- **Residential ground — fee-simple house lots.** The research records single-family houses on the residential streets west of King George Boulevard as the typical form, with the confirmed house stock fee-simple; strata in the area is mainly the town-centre apartment form. A "land" purchase on the residential streets is therefore normally an existing house lot, not a vacant new-subdivision lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Industrial ground — Highway 99 area.** The southern part of the West Newton plan area is the Highway 99 industrial area, zoned in the industrial zones under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 per the City plan. It is documented here as a land class named in the research — NOT as for-sale inventory, and no parcel, price or availability is asserted. A buyer near that edge must verify neighbouring uses and truck routes. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- **No bare-land strata verified.** No bare-land strata is verified on West Newton ground in the research file or any sibling file checked. Fee-simple is the default lot form; any strata lot is read on title lot by lot. (Research file §5.)
- **Lot character — zone minimums AS THE RESEARCH STATES THEM.** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 — residential zones R1–R6 in the neighbourhood portion (the research carries the East Newton zone table: R3 at 464 m² for a single-family home, R4 at 250 m², R2 at 929 m²) and industrial zones in the Highway 99 area. These are bylaw minimums, not a survey of lots for sale. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **ALR vs non-ALR / flood split.** The residential upland portion is outside the Serpentine & Nicomekl lowland floodplain; the south end falls toward those dyke-protected lowlands, and ALR land lies south and west toward the lowlands. A specific property's ALR status is checked in the Agricultural Land Commission's maps, and a south-end lot needs the City's flood mapping checked before subjects. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **Serviced vs unserviced.** The research documents no well/septic pocket on the residential streets; properties not connected to community water and sewer are an SSMUH exemption category, which is the servicing check for any edge parcel — servicing for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until confirmed with the City. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Not land stock — Harth at Tamanawis Park (presale condos, counted separately).** Harth, by Almas Group, at 128 St & 64 Ave beside Tamanawis Park — a mid-rise presale condo project — is documented in the condos sibling. - **Market context, labelled as such.** SnapStats covers Surrey but has NO land category (Detached vs "Condos & Townhomes" combined only). ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — current plan only.** The governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026; Bylaw No. 18020 is never current. Within the West Newton NCP set, the City states West Newton South was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 update and is no longer in effect. Confirm any density or use claim against the current OCP and Zoning Bylaw 12000, not the old plans. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps
- **Zoning.** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 — R1–R6 residential in the neighbourhood portion, industrial zones in the Highway 99 area. The zone line between residential and industrial ground is the first diligence item for any south-end parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it (Jul 8, 2024 framework).** Maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the SSMUH changes do not retroactively legalize suites built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey).** One secondary suite per house; Surrey's suite cap is 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area, one per unit, and a suite is not a separate strata lot. Verify a suite's permit status before marketing its income. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements.** Surrey subdivision proceeds through detailing and a Servicing Agreement that contractually defines the off-site works and services and financial securities required for final approval. Lot-specific servicing costs, DCC amounts and timelines for a West Newton parcel: UNCONFIRMED — no City source in the research states a figure for this area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey).** STRs are permitted, owner-only, under a City licence. Licence figure CONFLICT: program figure $358/yr vs the City's 2024 corporate report proposing $350/yr plus fees — carry $358 with $350 disclosed and check the City licence page before quoting.
- **Flood diligence (south end).** South-end streets fall toward the Serpentine & Nicomekl lowlands — confirm the specific lot against the City's flood mapping and ask about flood insurance before subjects come off. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **Bare-land strata — the form (no West Newton instance verified).** A bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — Form B, strata fees and bylaws apply to the lot. Surrey's documented precedent is Sea Ridge Bays (15875 20 Ave, South Surrey), cross-referenced for the form only, documented in its home area's files. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **BC strata block (wherever a bare-land strata lot is the purchase).** Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, disclosing fees, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance and insurance summary; depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **ALR rules (south/west edge parcels only).** Where a parcel IS in the ALR: residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; principal residence capped at 500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC Assessment farm class — full three-tier form.** $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 (<0.8 ha); application to the assessor on or before October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an error. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land.** PTT applies to a registered land transfer unless an exemption applies. First-Time Home Buyers': no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — relief turns on a qualifying home, so a lot-only purchase must be checked against the BC rules before relief is assumed. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000, for a new principal-residence home. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only.** Most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g., land kept for personal use); taxable examples include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts. Developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots beyond that line are generally taxable. Finer points for a specific lot: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax.** Surrey IS a designated taxable area — residential owners must file the annual declaration; a vacant parcel's position turns on its class and the exemptions. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition — bare land.** Secondary sources disagree on the vacant-land position after March 27, 2023 and no primary CMHC/Justice Laws text was opened in this pass: UNCONFIRMED for a specific bare-land purchase — check the CMHC tool and obtain legal advice. No position asserted.
- **First-home-on-land programs — stated precisely.** The FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. Land-plus-build qualification is transaction-specific: UNCONFIRMED per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Holding costs / tax dates.** Surrey taxes due July 2, 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 (city-wide). No West Newton-specific levy found: UNVERIFIED as an area item. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Foreclosure note.** Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry — foreclosure mapping for West Newton is UNVERIFIED and none is asserted.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there vacant residential land to buy in West Newton?** No bare-land subdivision is verified here — the realistic residential purchase is a fee-simple house lot bought for rebuild/added units; the only other land class in the plan area is Highway 99 industrial ground. (Section 2 above.)
- **Q: How many units can my lot hold?** 3 units under 280 m², 4 over 280 m², up to 6 on eligible lots of 281 m²+ within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — confirm band and eligibility with the City. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Should I worry about flood on a south-end lot?** Yes — check it, don't assume: the south end falls toward the dyke-protected Serpentine & Nicomekl lowlands while the residential upland is outside that floodplain. Confirm the lot on City flood mapping and ask about flood insurance. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **Q: Is the industrial edge a problem for a nearby lot?** It is a diligence item, not a verdict: verify neighbouring uses, truck routes and the residential/industrial zone line for any parcel near the Highway 99 area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- **Q: Is the lot serviced?** Subdivision streets are on community services as the default; edge parcels need confirmation, because unconnected properties are an SSMUH exemption category. Specific lot: UNCONFIRMED until the City confirms. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: What will subdivision/servicing cost?** The City sets off-site works, services and securities in a Servicing Agreement after detailing — no area figure exists in the research, so any number without a City estimate for the parcel is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Q: Will GST apply?** Depends on the seller and subdivision history: individuals' personal-use sales are generally exempt per the CRA; business-course sales and parcels subdivided into more than two parts are taxable examples. Get the GST position in writing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What are the holding costs on an empty lot?** Surrey property taxes (due July 2; 5% + 5% penalties) plus the speculation and vacancy tax declaration — Surrey is a taxable area. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Q: Can I rely on the old West Newton plan densities?** No — West Newton South was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update; read the current OCP (Bylaw 21900) and Zoning Bylaw 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps
- **Q: Which schools serve a lot here?** T.E. Scott Elementary (7079 148 St) is inside the plan area and Bear Creek Elementary (13780 80 Ave) at its north edge; Frank Hurt Secondary (13940 77 Ave) is the area secondary. A settled SD36 locator chain IS documented for 7079 148 St: T.E. Scott Elementary → Frank Hurt Secondary (locator run 2026-11-01, research file §4). Any other address: run the SD36 School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
