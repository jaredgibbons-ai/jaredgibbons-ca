## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026).** Bylaw 18020 is REPLACED and is never cited as current. The City records that some older NCPs' land use designations have been superseded or rescinded through the Surrey 2050 update — East Newton North was rescinded and East Newton South's and the East Newton Business Park's designations superseded, with the OCP's designations now guiding land use while the NCPs continue to guide amenities and infrastructure implementation; West Cloverdale is recorded the same way. A tile therefore does not by itself prove a plan's designations are still operative — check the current OCP designation for the parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/west-cloverdale-ncps
- **Zoning — Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000** (zone minimums read parcel by parcel in COSMOS). **Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated for Surrey.** https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH — Surrey:** the City adopted its provincial small-scale multi-unit housing zoning change on **July 8, 2024**, when Zoning By-law No. 12000's residential zones R1 to R7 replaced the RF, RH and RQ zones (RF-12/RF-12C became R4; RF-SD became R6) — the zoning now does the small-lot permitting that older NCP designations predate, so a plan tile and the current zone can point in different directions on the same lot. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html
- **Secondary suites — Surrey:** one suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, habitable floor area at most **40% of the principal dwelling unit**, never within a coach house or garden suite, and **never a separate strata lot**. A "90 m² cap" is NOT current and is never stated as current. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Short-term rentals — Surrey:** permitted, owner-only from the principal residence. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is this page a Surrey listings page?** No: at reading it carried no MLS® listings, no result count and no market figures — it is an index of 36 City of Surrey secondary-plan tiles linking onward to the City's plan material and to the site's Surrey search pages. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey.html
- **Q: What is the difference between a GLUP, an NCP and an LAP in Surrey?** On the City's own organization: a General Land Use Plan sets the overall framework for an area, a Neighbourhood Concept Plan provides the detailed neighbourhood plan under it, and a Local Area Plan does the same guiding work for areas planned outside the NCP process; approved plans are developed through community consultation and endorsed by Council, and they guide designations, density, roads, amenities and servicing. https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans
- **Q: Is "Surrey City Centre" a real estate board sub-area?** No — Surrey City Centre is NOT a board name; the land is the Whalley sub-area. "City Centre" is the City of Surrey's planning name for its downtown, which is why the City Centre Plan tile legitimately uses it. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey.html
- **Q: Where does Serpentine file?** Both ways, stated openly: Serpentine IS a board sub-area (owner ruling); the live feed files its ground in South Surrey and board research files it in Cloverdale — dual filing, no market claim built on either. Its SnapStats row (0/0/NA both splits) conflicts with its live feed's 16 listings; reconciliation UNVERIFIED. (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/surrey-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: How big can a secondary suite be in Surrey?** One suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite, and never a separate strata lot. A "90 m² cap" is not the current rule. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Q: If my lot is inside an NCP, is that plan still the operative one?** Not necessarily: the City records that the Surrey 2050 OCP update rescinded East Newton North and superseded the land use designations of East Newton South, the East Newton Business Park and West Cloverdale, with the OCP (Bylaw 21900) now guiding land use in those areas. Check the parcel's current OCP designation with the City before relying on any plan tile. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- **Q: In what order do I read a Surrey parcel?** Layers, in this order: the current OCP designation first, then the GLUP/NCP/LAP for the neighbourhood, then the current zoning under By-law No. 12000 — the plan shows where the City wants growth; the zoning is what can be built as-of-right today. Several tiles predate both the Surrey 2050 update and the July 8, 2024 SSMUH rezoning. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html
- **Q: Can I run a short-term rental from a Surrey lot's home?** Short-term rentals are permitted in Surrey, owner-only from the principal residence, with a business licence — carried here at $358/yr with the conflict disclosed (the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350/yr plus fees); check the City's licence page before quoting. (§3)
- **Q: How much PTT on a $1.2M Surrey lot?** $22,000 — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) plus 2% on the next $1,000,000 ($20,000), no exemption. (§3) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: What setback applies to a stream on my Surrey lot?** There is no single Surrey riparian figure: streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 and vary by stream class — read the parcel's stream class in the bylaw/COSMOS before pricing a building envelope. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
