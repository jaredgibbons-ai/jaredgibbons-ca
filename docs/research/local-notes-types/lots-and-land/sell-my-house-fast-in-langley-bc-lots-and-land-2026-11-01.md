## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **The regulator's framework (the service behind this page):** in British Columbia, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); a licensed professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. Disclosure must happen at the outset of dealings; dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. When agency disclosure occurs in this fast-sale process: UNVERIFIED — the page does not state it. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Land-frame pointers (kept separate, never blended — full treatment in the land topic file):** Township land sits under Zoning Bylaw 2500 with SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024; 3–4 units by servicing eligibility; NO 6-plex tier exists in it; suite cap 120 m²; coach house = fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata; R-CL s.403 = 220 m² min LOT / 464.5 m² max LOT area — a "355 m²" figure is nowhere in the bylaw); City land sits under Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 (number only; suite = lesser of 100 m² and 40%). A deadline price for a parcel is set inside the correct municipality's frame only. ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/langley-lots-land-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md
- **ALR pointer (Township acreage sellers):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Full block: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/langley-lots-land-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §3.
- **Strata block (a bare-land strata parcel on a deadline):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). Ordering the Form B early is the one document step a strata-lot seller controls. A fee-simple parcel carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math; a buyer's tax, shown because sellers are asked about it):** on a $1,000,000 Langley parcel — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,000,000 ($16,000) = **$18,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. Whether a fast-sale route changes a parcel's GST position: not stated on the page — UNVERIFIED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
