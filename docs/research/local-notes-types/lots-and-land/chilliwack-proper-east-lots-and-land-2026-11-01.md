## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — current.** The City's city-wide plan, including protection of the Agricultural Land Reserve; this ground sits in its Schedule B2 "Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan," with the Schedule D development permit areas (D-1 Drinking Water Supply, D-2 Hillside Environment, D-3 Riparian Area, D-4 Farm Home Plate, D-5 Hillside Development, D-6 Downtown, D-7 Mixed-Use Areas, D-8 Residential, D-9 Commercial, D-10 Industrial) applying per parcel — which areas apply to a given parcel is a per-parcel check, UNVERIFIED for any specific lot here. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Zoning: Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** — the parcel's actual zone is confirmed with the City per parcel. This file states NO zone label for any specific Proper East lot: the live page's editorial shorthand and one feed card conflict on zone labels for this ground (see §5), and neither is adopted. Farm zones sit in §6 of Bylaw 5000 where ALR/farm ground arises. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH (Bylaw 5395, adopted Jun 18, 2024):** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, amending Bylaw 5000, allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide and does not create new lots. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Secondary suites and accessory units (Bylaw 5000):** a secondary suite is an accessory dwelling unit of not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building — the lesser of 90 m² and 40% (s.2); only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot, and the permitted ADU types include secondary suite, coach house and garden suite (s.4.06(1)); garden suites are capped at a maximum gross floor area of 100 m² (s.4.06(1)(g)); ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite (s.5.04). No secondary suite is legal unless built with a building permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Subdivision / servicing — process documented, bylaw number held UNCONFIRMED:** the Chilliwack servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in this program (held — never borrowed from another municipality), so the process is what is documented: subdivision is the process of changing or creating new lot boundaries; under the Local Government Act and Land Title Act all subdivision plans must be approved by the City's Approving Officer before registration with the Land Title and Survey Authority, to ensure adequate lot size and shape, parkland dedication as necessary, sewer, water and other essential services in place, adequate road, lane and emergency access, protection of natural features, and preservation of future subdivision opportunities; a Preliminary Layout Approval is the first major milestone, typically issued 8–12 weeks from application, outlining all required servicing, engineering and legal steps, and is valid for 12 months. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8208
- **Flood diligence:** flood hazard zoning exists in the bylaw structure (RS-FHA reserve zone, §12 of Bylaw 5000) and the City applies flood protection requirements and a Floodplain Regulation Bylaw; the suite rule above ties legality directly to the flood construction level. Parcel-level flood construction levels for Proper East lots: UNVERIFIED in the research — confirm with the City before pricing suite or infill potential. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **ALR block (provincial frame, for any ALR parcel at the sub-area's edge):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for any parcel here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot, wherever one surfaces):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** a $500,000 fully residential parcel, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Chilliwack property taxes (2026):** notices mailed at the end of May, due the first working day after July 1; a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant building lot in Chilliwack Proper East?** Only as individual parcels surface — the research feed showed two land listings among a house-led first page, and no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata plan or presale lot offering was verified here. Each lot is a parcel-by-parcel purchase: zone, permit areas, flood level and servicing read off the City file for that PID. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is there an official median price or sales pace for this sub-area?** No. Chilliwack is a CADREB market outside the SnapStats extraction, and CADREB publishes board-wide statistics only — no median, inventory or sales-ratio figure for Chilliwack Proper East exists in the permitted sources, and none is borrowed from a neighbouring board. (§1 above)
- **Q: The page says lots here have duplex or small-lot subdivision potential — can I rely on that?** No. The page's editorial zone shorthand conflicts with one of its own feed cards' rezoning claim for this ground, so zone claims for any specific parcel are UNVERIFIED until the parcel's actual zone and any subdivision approval are confirmed with the City. Do not copy either label into an offer. (§§2, 5 above)
- **Q: Can a house lot here add a suite or a coach house?** One accessory dwelling unit per lot where the zone permits — a secondary suite capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space, a coach house, or a garden suite capped at 100 m² — with ADU parking at 1–2 spaces, and a suite is legal only if built with a permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and flood requirements; new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a lot here, or build multiple units instead?** Different permissions: SSMUH (Bylaw 5395) allows 3–4 units on urban-growth-boundary parcels zoned single-detached/duplex, but that does not create new lots; subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer process — Preliminary Layout Approval first (typically 8–12 weeks, valid 12 months), then the servicing, engineering and legal steps it sets out, before Land Title registration. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is a lot here fee-simple or strata?** The house-lot fabric is fee-simple by default, and no bare-land strata plan inside this sub-area was verified — but tenure is read off title per parcel, and a bare-land strata lot, if one surfaces, is strata tenure with Form B, fees and bylaws. The gated bare-land strata buyers sometimes find in Quarry Road searches (Braeside Estates) is Little Mountain ground documented in that area's land file, not this sub-area's stock. (§2 above)
- **Q: What flood diligence applies to a Proper East lot?** Confirm the parcel's flood construction level and Floodplain Regulation Bylaw position with the City, and ask for the permits on any existing suite — a suite below the flood construction level is not legal, whatever the listing says. Parcel-level levels are UNVERIFIED in the research. (§3 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a lot purchase here?** PTT on the general tiers — on the §3 worked example ($500,000, illustration only) the bill is $8,000 with no exemption. GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — a parcel created by subdividing into more than two parts is the classic taxable case; confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which school will a home built on my lot feed into?** UNVERIFIED for any address here — SD33 catchments are address-based, the §1 schools are Ministry-listed names by address only, and no locator run for this sub-area is on record. Run the exact address in the SD33 locator before relying on a school. (§1 above)
- **Q: How do Chilliwack tax dates affect a summer closing?** Notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1, with 5% penalties after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 4, 2026 — a summer completion straddles both penalty dates, so the adjustment statement needs the parcel's paid/unpaid position, not an assumption. (§3 above)
