## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- South Cambie is the City of Vancouver's Cambie Street corridor between roughly West 16th Avenue and the Fraser River (Marine Drive), including the Cambie Village stretch and the area around the King Edward and Oakridge–41st stations' northern edges; the board sub-area name is "South Cambie," with nearby areas Oakridge, Riley Park, Shaughnessy, Douglas Park, Marpole and Langara. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md)
- The corridor is planned under the Cambie Corridor Plan: Council approved it on May 9, 2011 to guide transit-oriented growth along Cambie Street from 16th Avenue to the Fraser River, centred on the Canada Line stations at Marine Drive, Langara–49th Avenue, Oakridge–41st Avenue and King Edward; Phase 3 (approved 2018) extended land-use policy to the neighbourhood streets within walking distance of the stations, adding townhouse and low-rise apartment forms off the arterial. https://www.cip-icu.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Cambie-Corridor-Plan-FINAL-Aug-2011_WEB.pdf and https://council.vancouver.ca/20180417/documents/rr2_presentation.pdf
- Landmarks and anchors: Queen Elizabeth Park (52 hectares at 125 metres above sea level — the highest point in the City of Vancouver) anchors the east side; Douglas Park Community Centre is at 801 West 22nd Avenue; BC Children's Hospital and BC Women's Hospital & Health Centre share the campus at 4500 Oak Street on the west side; VanDusen Botanical Garden (5251 Oak Street, 22 hectares) sits at the northwest edge; Langara College sits at the corridor's south end at West 49th Avenue. https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/queen-elizabeth-park.aspx and http://www.bcchildrens.ca/ and https://vandusengarden.org/
- Schools: public schools are run by the Vancouver School Board (SD39). The corridor's named public elementary school is Edith Cavell Elementary (500 West 20th Avenue); Sir Winston Churchill Secondary (7055 Heather Street) is the public secondary school located inside the corridor. Location is not catchment: the SD39 locator run documented in the research — for the single sample address 500 West 20th Avenue only — returns Edith Cavell Elementary / Eric Hamber Secondary; that single-address result does not transfer to other corridor addresses, and all other catchments are UNVERIFIED. https://www.vsb.bc.ca/edith-cavell and https://www.vsb.bc.ca/schools/sir-winston-churchill
- Market figures: Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage — the September 2026 GVR report contains no City of Vancouver community sections at all, so no inventory, solds, median or days-on-market figure exists for South Cambie in this program and none is substituted. All market figures: UNVERIFIED. (Research file above.)

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**South Cambie has no meaningful bare-land market — stated plainly at the top. No named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan, and no presale lot offering was verified. This is built-out Vancouver corridor ground: what trades as "land" is the redevelopment value of built fee-simple house lots on the side streets (priced partly on R1-1 multiplex math) and of Cambie Corridor Plan station-area parcels (priced on plan policy, most of which still requires a rezoning). Everything named below is built product or a documented development site — never lot inventory.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form, on the side streets

- **Side-street house lots:** the documented stock is older detached houses on the side streets — mostly mid-century and earlier character homes on standard lots, freehold — with townhouses and 4–6 storey strata and rental buildings concentrated on Cambie Street and around the King Edward and Oakridge–41st stations under the Cambie Corridor Plan. A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. The research states no lot-size range for the corridor, and none is invented here; the zone and dimensions of a specific lot: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md)
- **Multiplex character of the house lots:** for detached-house lots away from the corridor arterials, By-law No. 13817 replaced the RS districts with the R1-1 district, allowing up to 6 strata units (or 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots — that potential, not bare land, is what a South Cambie house-lot sale actually carries. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **Serviced position:** corridor homes are on City water and sewer services; a 2026 utility dollar figure confirmed on a City source: UNVERIFIED. Septic or well use in the corridor: UNVERIFIED in the research. (Research file above.)
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** no ALR parcel in South Cambie is confirmed in the research and no split is stated — ALR status is a per-parcel check on ALC mapping. (Research file above.)
- **Flood position at the south end:** the streets running down to Marine Drive sit at the edge of the Fraser River floodplain environment, and the Vancouver Building By-law's Flood Plain Standards and Requirements set a minimum flood construction level of 4.6 m (GVRD datum) for new construction in designated flood plains; lot-by-lot designation must be confirmed with the City. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/guidelines-flood-plain-standards-requirements.pdf

### Development sites and assemblies — documented as such, never as inventory

- **Heather Lands — a documented rezoned development site, not lots for sale:** 8.5 hectares (21.1 acres) between West 33rd and West 37th Avenues, owned by the MST Nations (Musqueam, Squamish, Tsleil-Waututh) with Canada Lands Company. Council approved the original rezoning on May 24, 2022 under the 2018 Heather Lands Policy Statement (about 2,600 homes, 4.4 acres of park); that scheme was superseded on March 31, 2026, when Council approved a replacement rezoning under the provincial Attainable Housing Initiative — 16 buildings of 4 to 46 storeys with roughly 4,200 homes, including about 2,940 discounted leasehold strata (AHI) units, about 700 market leasehold strata units and about 610 social housing units, plus a 125-space childcare, an MST Cultural Centre, a one-acre school site and over four acres of park. The future product is predominantly LEASEHOLD strata on MST/CLC land — a development site in build-out, documented here as such; no lot in it is bare-land inventory, and construction phasing / first occupancy dates are UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/heather-lands-to-advance-city-s-largest-indigenous-led-redevelopment.aspx and https://www.shapeyourcity.ca/4911-5255-heather-637-657-w-37-ave-and-620-689-w-35-ave
- **Oakridge Municipal Town Centre:** the corridor's growth framework targets the Oakridge Municipal Town Centre at 41st and Cambie as its southern anchor, with new housing in townhouse and medium- to higher-density forms along the corridor — plan policy for assembled station-area parcels, not a lot offering. http://vancouver.ca/files/cov/oakridge-park-open-house-boards-december-2017.pdf
- **Assemblies generally:** the research's documented position is that on or near Cambie Street the Cambie Corridor Plan — not just base zoning — drives value, and plan designation must be distinguished from approved zoning because most corridor densities still require a rezoning application; price land on policy support, not as-built density. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Plan framework — Cambie Corridor Plan (2011; Phase 3, 2018):** the corridor's growth framework, centred on the Canada Line stations; most corridor densities still require a rezoning application, so a plan designation is policy support, not zoning. https://www.cip-icu.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Cambie-Corridor-Plan-FINAL-Aug-2011_WEB.pdf
- **Citywide direction — Vancouver Official Development Plan:** the Vancouver Plan was replaced by the Vancouver Official Development Plan on March 31, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf
- **Zoning / SSMUH — R1-1 multiplex district (By-law No. 13817):** the RS districts were replaced with R1-1, allowing up to 6 strata units (or 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots, subject to the district schedule. Corridor arterial and station-area parcels sit under corridor designations and site-specific districts rather than R1-1 — confirm the parcel's district with the City. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing:** subdivision in Vancouver is governed by the City's Subdivision By-law No. 5208, with subdivision fees under By-law No. 14538 (verified ledger position for Vancouver). Servicing standards are confirmed parcel by parcel with the City; no corridor-specific servicing figure is stated in the research. (Standing verified ledger; research file above.)
- **Flood construction level:** minimum 4.6 m (GVRD datum) for new construction in designated flood plains under the Vancouver Building By-law's Flood Plain Standards and Requirements, based on a coastal flood risk assessment that considered 1 m of sea level rise; confirm whether the specific lot is designated. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/guidelines-flood-plain-standards-requirements.pdf
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no South Cambie ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no flat line is asserted for this area. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500,000 → $8,000; $900,000 → $16,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000; at the $800,000 cap the fully qualifying FTHB figure is $6,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Empty Homes Tax — Vancouver only:** the City charges 3% of assessed taxable value on homes declared or deemed empty; the annual declaration was due February 3, 2026 and payment April 16, 2026. A vacant house-lot held empty is the land-buyer exposure here; the tax is a City of Vancouver tax and applies in South Cambie. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Vancouver property taxes:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Purchase prohibition / speculation taxes beyond the above:** the federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for a South Cambie lot is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for this area, and the position is not conformed from other files. No other speculation/vacancy position is documented in the research.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in South Cambie?** No verified bare-land offering exists — the corridor is built out. Land here trades as a built house on its lot, a corridor parcel with plan policy, or (at Heather Lands) future leasehold strata product — never as a marketed lot program. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What does a side-street house lot actually carry — a house, or development potential?** Both: off-corridor lots sit in the R1-1 district, allowing up to 6 strata units (or 8 secured rental units) under By-law No. 13817, so the multiplex math is part of the lot's value. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **Q: Does a Cambie Corridor Plan designation mean I can build that density now?** No — the research is explicit: distinguish plan designation from approved zoning; most corridor densities still require a rezoning application, so price on policy support, not as-built density. https://council.vancouver.ca/20180417/documents/rr2_presentation.pdf
- **Q: Is the Heather Lands product freehold?** No — the March 31, 2026 rezoning's roughly 4,200 homes are predominantly discounted and market LEASEHOLD strata (about 2,940 AHI + about 700 market) plus about 610 social housing units, on MST Nations / Canada Lands Company land. Price and finance it as leasehold. https://www.shapeyourcity.ca/4911-5255-heather-637-657-w-37-ave-and-620-689-w-35-ave
- **Q: Which bylaw governs subdividing a Vancouver lot?** The City's Subdivision By-law No. 5208 (fees under By-law No. 14538); servicing and approval conditions are confirmed with the City for the parcel. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Do flood rules touch the south end of the corridor?** They can — streets down to Marine Drive sit at the edge of the Fraser River floodplain environment, and designated flood-plain construction carries a minimum flood construction level of 4.6 m (GVRD datum). Confirm the lot's designation with the City. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/guidelines-flood-plain-standards-requirements.pdf
- **Q: If I hold a house-lot empty, what extra tax applies?** The City of Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3% of assessed taxable value on homes declared or deemed empty, with an annual declaration. It is Vancouver-only and it does apply here. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Q: Will GST apply if a developer sells me a newly subdivided lot?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). No such lot offering is verified in South Cambie today. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a South Cambie address?** By exact address only — the one settled locator run in the research (500 West 20th Avenue → Edith Cavell Elementary / Eric Hamber Secondary) does not transfer to other streets, and Churchill Secondary's location inside the corridor is not its catchment for that address. https://www.vsb.bc.ca/schools/sir-winston-churchill
- **Q: What is OPAL, and is it land I can buy into as lots?** No — OPAL by Element (438 W King Edward Ave) is a built freehold-strata retirement community (44 condos / 56 rental / 30 care; "one resident must be 55+") counted in the condos program. It is documented here only as an exclusion; see the FILE STATUS block for the open double-count housekeeping item between the cambie and south-cambie condos siblings.
