Depreciation reports every 5 years for strata corporations with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, non-deferrable. CRF minimum contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Delta Manor?** No verified bare-land market exists here — this is a built-out grid. A land play is an existing fee-simple lot (usually with a house on it) bought for its lot value, suite/coach-house potential, or a future subdivision against its zone minimum. (§2 above)
- **Q: What is a Laburnum Park Place lot, exactly?** A bare-land strata lot: you own the strata lot and the detached rancher on it; the strata corporation owns/maintains the common property; Form B, strata fees and registered bylaws apply, including the MLS-recorded 55+ age restriction. It is not a fee-simple lot, and it is not South Pointe (NWS2528, Neilsen Grove). (§2 above)
- **Q: The Delta Manor 55+ file doesn't list Laburnum — which is right?** The files conflict, openly: the sub-area 55-plus file omits it and records no verified 55+ ownership here; the Ladner-wide and Hawthorne files place it here as a 55+ bare-land strata. This file follows the Ladner-wide basis. For a purchase, neither file decides it — the registered bylaws on the Form B and the strata plan do. (§2 above)
- **Q: How small a lot can I create here?** Against the zone ladder: RS7 335 m² is the smallest urban minimum, then RS6 390 m², RS5 550 m² (460 m² outside the SFR designation); duplex zones RD4 504 m² and RD1 836 m². The parcel's own zone on the City maps governs, plus the subdivision process in Section 3. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me split a lot into four lots?** No — Bylaws 8394/8395 allow up to four UNITS on single-detached and duplex lots (suites, coach houses, garden suites, duplex forms). Units are not lots; subdivision is a separate process. (§3 above)
- **Q: What is the 30-metre rule I keep hearing about?** The Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area: any Delta property within 30 m of a stream — sloughs and ditches included — is in it, and it can limit additions, coach houses and rebuilds. Central Ladner has sloughs and ditches throughout. (§3 above)
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a lot purchase here?** At the CRA GI-003 level: an individual's personal-use vacant-land sale is generally exempt; a developer's or business sale, or a more-than-two-part subdivision sale, can be taxable. Per-parcel answer: UNCONFIRMED beyond GI-003 — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What PTT on a $1M lot?** $18,000 with no exemption ($2,000 at 1% + $16,000 at 2%). The FTHB GST rebate does not apply to bare land — new housing only. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I rent out a Laburnum rancher?** Long-term, the strata cannot ban it — rental-restriction bylaws are void since November 24, 2022 — but a tenant must still satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Delta Manor lot?** Address-based. The sample (4600 53 Street) maps to Ladner Elementary and Delta Secondary — single-address only. Run the parcel address in the SD37 locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
