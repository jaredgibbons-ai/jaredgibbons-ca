## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**East Cambie land is fee-simple detached house lots — that is the documented base form and the only standing land form verified here. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision offered as lots, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering was verified. The land story that matters is threefold: (1) the RS1/E lot frame on Cambie Road; (2) the ALR edge on No. 6 Road and Westminster Highway, where the planning area simply stops at farmland; and (3) one documented rezoning application (11671/11691 Cambie Road, RS1/E to RTL4 townhouses) — an application record, never for-sale inventory. The built strata in the area is townhouse form, counted in the townhomes sibling, not lots.**

- **Established-street house lots:** single detached housing is the documented base form — a City data sheet for 11671 and 11691 Cambie Road gives the existing zoning as Single Detached (RS1/E) under an OCP designation of Neighbourhood Residential — and the Area Plan states residential projects were anticipated along some major roads at townhouse or apartment densities. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning:** Zoning Bylaw 8500 governs — Single Detached (RS1/RS2) under Section 8.1 is the base form; the RS1/E subzone documented on Cambie Road sets a minimum lot area of 550.0 m², minimum width 18.0 m and minimum depth 24.0 m, one principal dwelling per lot, secondary suite as a permitted secondary use. Townhouse sites proceed under low-density townhouse zones (the RTL4 application in Section 2 is the worked example), and multiple-family sites in East Cambie are Development Permit Areas under the Area Plan. https://Richmond.Ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=558
- **Plan:** the East Cambie Area Plan is Schedule 2.11B to the Official Community Plan (plan adoption October 21, 2002; Land Use Map updated by Bylaw 9874), under the city-wide 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000 (Schedule 1); the City began a further OCP update process in fall 2024 toward a 2050 horizon. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot on covered lots, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — servicing-bylaw number held UNCONFIRMED, process documented instead:** no single Richmond subdivision/servicing bylaw number was confirmed on a primary source in this file's research or in a fresh check for this file, so none is asserted. The documented process is: subdivision proceeds through the City's Development Applications Department under the Zoning Bylaw lot frame above, with a Servicing Agreement for design and construction of road works, engineering infrastructure and frontage improvements where the City requires one (the standard rezoning/subdivision consideration in City reports), flood covenant registration under Bylaw 8204 where applicable, and fees under the Consolidated Fees Bylaw 8636 as the City's subdivision checklist states. Confirm the servicing requirements for the specific lot with the City. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=75006
- **Flood rules — the Richmond-specific diligence item:** all lands within Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels (FCLs) that habitable floors must meet unless an exemption applies; the Area Plan requires new development in East Cambie to provide adequate flood protection. Confirm the lot's FCL before planning any build or major renovation. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **ALR rules (the area genuinely abuts ALR ground, so these bite at the edges):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm-class thresholds are not documented in this area's own research, so no farm-class figure is asserted here — a flat "$2,500+" line would in any case be an error under the three-tier structure, and no tier figure is imported without this file's own research documenting it. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=558
- **Heritage note for lot diligence near the node:** the Area Plan asks the School Board to conserve the Mitchell School buildings as heritage structures, and Mitchell Elementary is the longest continuously operating school in the Richmond public system — relevant context for redevelopment expectations near the Cambie Road / No. 5 Road node, not a lot rule. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/archives/exhibits/schools/early/mitchell.htm
- **Bare-land strata rules:** no bare-land strata plan is verified in this area (Section 2), so no strata block is triggered here. The BC frame that applies to East Cambie's conventional townhouse stratas — and would apply to any bare-land strata lot — is: Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 with the May 1, 2023 exemptions (no East Cambie complex is verified 55+ in the siblings); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — an East Cambie lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for East Cambie, and the position is not conformed from other files. **ADEU note (negative, stated so it is not imported by mistake):** Alexandra District Energy Utility charges are a West Cambie carrying cost; East Cambie's research documents no ADEU charge and none applies in this file.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot subdivision I can buy into in East Cambie?** No verified one — the area's base form is fee-simple detached houses on established streets, with strata townhouses on the corridors. No bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata and no presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What lot frame does the Cambie Road zoning set?** For the documented RS1/E subzone: minimum lot area 550.0 m², minimum width 18.0 m, minimum depth 24.0 m, one principal dwelling per lot plus a secondary suite as a permitted secondary use, under Zoning Bylaw 8500, Section 8.1. Other subzones apply elsewhere — confirm the specific lot's subzone with the City. https://Richmond.Ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Q: Does the ALR edge affect an east-side lot?** Yes, as an interface and a hard planning boundary: the planning area abuts the ALR along No. 6 Road and Westminster Highway, the plan's goal is a distinct urban/agricultural boundary, and any parcel actually inside the ALR needs ALC approval to subdivide and cannot be stratified. Confirm ALR status per address on ALC maps before pricing. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=558
- **Q: Can a house lot here add a suite or more units?** The RS1/RS2 zone lists a secondary suite as a secondary use, and the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: Do Cambie Road corridor lots carry townhouse-redevelopment value?** The plan direction supports the question — the Area Plan anticipates townhouse or apartment densities along some major roads, multiple-family sites are Development Permit Areas, and application RZ 14-670471 already documents an RS1/E-to-RTL4 townhouse rezoning at 11671/11691 Cambie Road — but that application is a record, not an approval a neighbouring lot can borrow; confirm the specific lot's designation with the City. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_1_App_11671_11691_CambieRd46833.pdf
- **Q: Is an East Cambie lot in a floodplain?** All of Richmond is designated flood plain under Bylaw No. 8204, behind 49 km of dikes designed for a 1:500 event with raising to 4.7 m under way; habitable floors must meet the lot's flood construction level unless exempt. Check the FCL lot by lot before planning a build. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Q: Is a lot here serviced?** UNVERIFIED parcel-by-parcel in the research — the Area Plan itself refers to sanitary and storm sewer upgrades in older subdivisions, so servicing history differs by pocket. Confirm water, sewer and storm servicing for the specific lot with the City before writing. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=558
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
