## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Lindell Beach part of Chilliwack?** Not municipally. It is an unincorporated community in FVRD Electoral Area H with a Chilliwack mailing address; the FVRD is its local government, and its plan and zoning are FVRD documents (OCP Bylaw 1115; Zoning Bylaw 1638), not City of Chilliwack bylaws. For board purposes it files under CADREB, under the Cultus Lake MLS area. (§1 and §3)
- **Q: What am I actually buying in Creekside Mills or The Cottages — a house or a lot?** Both, in bare-land strata form: a detached cottage or house plus a strata LOT in a bare-land strata plan (Creekside Mills is EPS3777; The Cottages is BCS3022) — strata fees, bylaws and a Form B on purchase apply to the lot itself, but there are no shared walls. That is the community's strata-land form, documented in §2 and counted in the sibling homes, never as fee-simple land. (§2 and §3)
- **Q: What am I buying at Lindell Beach Holiday Resort?** A pre-fabricated holiday trailer on a pad the resort leases to you — not land, not a strata lot (BC Court of Appeal, 2009 BCCA 5). Pad tenure is never lot stock; current lease terms are UNVERIFIED, and the MHPTA pad-rent caps (2026: 2.3% plus proportional; 2027: 2.2% plus proportional) are the frame governing pad increases. (§2 and §3)
- **Q: Is Cultus Lake Village a 45+ or a 55+ park — and is it here?** Neither question settles: the park is in Cultus Lake East ground, not Lindell Beach, and its age rule is UNCONFIRMED — carried as a conflict in this program, never settled (the same home listed "45+" in spring 2026, MLS R3107057, and the park listed "55+" later, MLS R3127457; no operator source found). It is pad-rental stock either way. (§2)
- **Q: Can I rent out a Lindell Beach lot or cottage short-term?** Only if the lot's zone expressly permits Temporary Tourist Accommodation under FVRD Bylaw 1638 — Residential use excludes it otherwise — and you hold a provincial STR registration number (required since May 1, 2025). The FVRD has specifically enabled Area H enforcement on STRs and RV accommodation; a seller's Airbnb history is not proof a buyer can continue it. (§3)
- **Q: Is Lindell Beach leasehold like Cultus Lake Park?** Mostly no — Lindell Beach is predominantly freehold (including 100% freehold bareland strata at Creekside Mills), while north-shore Park homes are leasehold — but the Holiday Resort is the documented exception (owned trailer on a leased pad), so tenure is a per-property title check, never an assumption.
