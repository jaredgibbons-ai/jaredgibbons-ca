## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I split a Mt Lehman acreage and build townhouses or a small subdivision?** Start with a planner, not a pro forma: Abbotsforward 2050 protects the agricultural land base and directs growth to urban centres, most working land is A1-zoned (8.0 ha and larger intent), ALR parcels need ALC approval for subdivision, and the City's servicing bylaw number itself is UNCONFIRMED in this program — rural lot-splitting assumptions need a City/ALC answer first. (§3)
- **Q: Can a Mt Lehman farm's second home be sold to me separately?** No. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); a second residence on ALR land is an accessory farm/employee residence or a suite inside the principal residence, and it conveys only with the parcel. (§3)
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a coach house on a Mt Lehman acreage?** That is the realistic multi-household route here, under two regimes: Abbotsford caps a secondary suite at under 50% of the principal dwelling and a coach/garden suite at 15% of the lot to a maximum of 100 m² (Abbotsford's rules only), A1 zoning lists a Secondary Suite as an accessory use — and on ALR land the ALC allows a suite only inside the principal residence, with anything beyond the permitted number/size needing ALC approval. Check the parcel's zone and ALR status first. (§3)
- **Q: Is Mt Lehman in the floodplain?** The community sits on raised ground above Matsqui Prairie — the origin of the "Mount." The prairie at its foot is diked floodplain behind the 11.3 km Matsqui Dike, and the November 2021 disaster was a Sumas Prairie (Nooksack overflow) event, not a Mt Lehman event — parcel-level checks still matter for plateau-edge and prairie-floor properties.
