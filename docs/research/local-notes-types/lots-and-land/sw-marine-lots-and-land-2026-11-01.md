## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**S.W. Marine has no bare-land subdivision or lot program — stated plainly at the top — but it is not an honest-zero in the same way as a downtown core: this is estate land. What actually trades here is large fee-simple house lots, above all the oversized RA-1 Southlands lots (some over an acre) held large by policy since 1955, plus conventional detached lots on the Kerrisdale/Marpole-edge streets and the Deering Island waterfront enclave. No named bare-land development, no verified bare-land strata plan, and no presale lot offering was verified. A buyer does not buy a vacant lot here on this evidence; they buy a house on a very large lot, or a teardown/rebuild proposition priced against the RA-1 and flood rules in §3.**

### Fee-simple lots — the land form that trades

- **Southlands RA-1 lots — estate lots, policy-protected:** the most estate-like housing in Vancouver's southwest — large detached homes on oversized lots in Southlands (some over an acre), many with stables, barns or nursery-greenhouse structures. The lot pattern is protected by policy, not just by ownership: the RA-1 (Limited Agriculture) District — created in 1955 specifically for Southlands, intended "to maintain and encourage the semi-rural, equestrian and limited agricultural nature of this District" — permits one-family dwellings and, in specific circumstances, infill one-family dwellings, which keeps parcel sizes large and density near zero compared with the rest of the city. No typical lot size range beyond "some over an acre" is stated in the research, and none is invented here. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-southlands.pdf
- **Conventional streets at the Kerrisdale/Marpole edges:** more conventional detached homes on the streets climbing toward Kerrisdale, where the sub-area's limited attached and rental stock is also confined. Tenure is predominantly freehold within the City of Vancouver. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sw-marine.md)
- **Deering Island — waterfront enclave lots:** a small enclave of waterfront homes on a man-made island at the foot of the area on the Fraser River. How often Deering Island product trades: UNVERIFIED in the research (posed as a question for Jared, unanswered). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sw-marine.md)
- **Serviced position:** no unserviced-lot position is documented for City-side lots in the research; servicing for a specific parcel is confirmed with the City through the subdivision process in §3. Septic/well positions: UNVERIFIED.
- **ALR vs non-ALR — the defining split, per-parcel:** the provincial Agricultural Land Reserve designation was implemented over the Southlands area in 1973, and the City's Southlands Plan (1988) remains the area plan, now read within the Vancouver Official Development Plan (which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026). Whether an individual lot sits inside the ALR boundary must still be confirmed parcel by parcel — no ALR percentage split is stated. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-southlands.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — RA-1 (Southlands) vs R1-1 (elsewhere), never blended:** Southlands lots are governed by the RA-1 (Limited Agriculture) District schedule (1955; one-family dwellings, limited infill in specific circumstances), read with the Southlands Plan (1988) within the Vancouver Official Development Plan. Elsewhere in the sub-area, for standard detached-house lots, Vancouver's multiplex rules apply: By-law No. 13817 replaced the RS districts with the R1-1 district, allowing up to 6 strata units (or 8 secured rental units) — but RA-1 lots are NOT R1-1 lots, and By-law 13817's permissions must never be quoted for a Southlands lot without a zoning confirmation. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-southlands.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — Vancouver Subdivision By-law 5208 (fees 14538), verified ledger:** Vancouver's subdivision instrument is Subdivision By-law No. 5208, with its fee schedule carried as Bylaw 14538 in this program's verified ledger. Parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City. Whether any RA-1 lot can in practice be subdivided is a per-parcel zoning + ALR question (below), UNCONFIRMED as a general position.
- **ALR rules — live on this ground:** the ALR designation has applied over the Southlands area since 1973 (per-parcel boundary check with the ALC required). Where a parcel IS in the ALR: subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR and is not asserted for any S.W. Marine parcel (the tier structure is stated; whether any given Southlands parcel holds farm class is a per-parcel check). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-southlands.pdf
- **Flood — Flood Plain Standards and Requirements, with a dedicated RA-1 section:** the City's Flood Plain Standards and Requirements (adopted September 16, 2014, effective January 1, 2015, amended March 7, 2023) subject lands in the designated floodplain — which includes the Fraser River coastal floodplain — to flood construction level (FCL) and setback requirements under the Vancouver Building By-law, and the Standards contain a dedicated section (Section 6.0) for the RA-1 District. The City publishes the designated floodplain as an open dataset covering the coastal and Still Creek floodplains; confirm the individual lot against that dataset and look for registered flood covenants on title before subject removal. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/guidelines-flood-plain-standards-requirements.pdf and https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Jurisdiction trap — three tenures within a few blocks:** City of Vancouver freehold (RA-1/R1-1), UEL ground to the west, and Musqueam reserve leasehold sit within a few blocks of each other and finance, insure and resell differently — confirm jurisdiction on any listing near the Musqueam boundary and state the tenure in the first line of any listing copy. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sw-marine.md)
- **Property transfer tax:** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked examples (inputs + tier math): $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M ($38,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I subdivide a big Southlands lot or build multiplex units on it?** Do not assume so — Southlands lots are RA-1, not R1-1: RA-1 permits one dwelling per lot with only limited infill in specific circumstances, and By-law 13817's R1-1 multiplex permissions (up to 6 strata / 8 secured rental) apply to standard lots elsewhere in the sub-area, not to RA-1 lots. Get a zoning confirmation for the parcel. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **Q: Can I keep horses here?** Southlands is the one part of Vancouver planned for it: the RA-1 district was created in 1955 to maintain the area's "semi-rural, equestrian and limited agricultural nature," and stables operate on its large lots. Permitted use is still property-specific, and the ALR designation (1973) may govern the parcel — confirm zoning and ALR status before relying on equestrian use. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-southlands.pdf
- **Q: Is my lot in the ALR?** Per-parcel only — the designation was implemented over the area in 1973, but the research confirms no individual lot's boundary position. Check with the ALC before marketing farm or stable use. (§3.)
- **Q: Is flooding regulated on the riverfront streets?** Yes — the Flood Plain Standards and Requirements apply FCLs and setbacks in the designated floodplain (including the Fraser River coastal floodplain) and carry a dedicated RA-1 section (Section 6.0). Check the lot against the City's floodplain dataset and title for flood covenants before subject removal. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/guidelines-flood-plain-standards-requirements.pdf
- **Q: Is there any strata or bare-land strata stock in Southlands?** Effectively none inside Southlands itself — RA-1's one-dwelling framework leaves the sub-area's attached stock at its Kerrisdale/Marpole edges, and no bare-land strata plan is verified. Riverview Court on the corridor is QUEUED/UNCONFIRMED, counted nowhere. (§2.)
- **Q: What is Deering Island?** A small enclave of waterfront homes on a man-made island on the Fraser River at the foot of the sub-area, with Deering Island Park (3530 Deering Island Place) as its park. Trade frequency: UNVERIFIED. http://covapp.vancouver.ca/parkfinder/parkdetail.aspx?inparkid=38
- **Q: Is a listing near Musqueam the same kind of purchase as a City lot?** No — City freehold, UEL ground and Musqueam reserve leasehold are three different purchase structures within a few blocks; reserve homes are leasehold under federal/First Nation jurisdiction. Confirm jurisdiction before comparing prices. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve an S.W. Marine address?** By exact address — the settled SD39 run (5351 Camosun Street → Southlands Elementary / Point Grey Secondary) is a single-address result; secondary catchments elsewhere are served by Magee Secondary and Point Grey Secondary depending on the address, otherwise UNVERIFIED. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax apply here?** Yes, as City of Vancouver ground: 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed empty (declaration due Feb 3, 2026; payment due Apr 16, 2026). It does NOT apply on UEL ground west of here. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Q: How much PTT on a $1.8M estate-lot purchase?** $34,000 on the general tiers ($2,000 + $32,000 at 2% on $1.6M), before any exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
