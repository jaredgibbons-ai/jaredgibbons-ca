## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market in Downtown New Westminster — stated plainly at the top, because it is honest. Downtown recorded 0 detached listings in September 2026; no named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering was verified. Downtown land is comprehensively-zoned, strata and mixed-use parcel land on and around Columbia Street, Carnarvon Street and the station lands, and the two large sites buyers ask about — 811 Carnarvon and Columbia Square — are proposals only, documented below as proposals and never as for-sale inventory.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form, near-absent downtown

- **Parcel form:** Downtown parcels sit in commercial, multiple-unit and comprehensive development districts under Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001, with zone-specific minimums in the bylaw text; a Downtown-specific minimum lot size: UNVERIFIED. The Downtown Community Plan plans the core for higher-density, mixed-use forms, and there is effectively no detached stock (0 detached listings in September 2026). https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Strata vs freehold:** strata dominates almost entirely — 102 condos & townhomes listed against 0 detached listings in September 2026, the deepest attached pool in the city's core. A buyer "buying land" downtown is in practice buying a strata unit or underwriting a comprehensive-development parcel through the City process, not purchasing a vacant fee-simple lot. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.)
- **Serviced position:** New Westminster operates its own electrical utility and City water / sewer services; a 2026 utility dollar figure for a Downtown property confirmed on a City source: UNVERIFIED. Servicing for any single parcel is confirmed through the City's subdivision/development process under Bylaw No. 7142, 2007 (Section 3). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/downtown-nw.md, §4)
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** no Agricultural Land Reserve land is identified in Downtown in the sources read — no ALR split is stated, and ALR status for a specific property is checked in the ALC's ALR Property and Map Finder. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 (CORRECTED):** Downtown zoning includes Columbia Street historic and high-rise commercial districts; minimum lot sizes are zone-specific in the bylaw text, and a Downtown-specific minimum is UNVERIFIED. **Townhouse districts in the bylaw are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B ONLY — RT-A/RT-B do NOT exist** (see the FILE STATUS correction; the research file's RT-A/RT-B line is an error and is not carried forward). The 836.10 m² figure sometimes attached to that error is an RM apartment-district density threshold, not an RT figure. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **OCP — "Our City 2041," Bylaw No. 7925, 2017, with the Downtown Community Plan as Schedule C:** Downtown is designated a Regional City Centre, and the OCP is under a 2025–2026 update process (OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 8574, 2026). https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **Transit-oriented density as the Province/City state it:** the Province's Transit Oriented Development Areas, introduced in 2024, enable density within 800 metres of the city's five SkyTrain stations — two of the five (New Westminster and Columbia) are in Downtown. What that means for a specific parcel is a City / bylaw check, not a listing-line claim. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/2026_02_17_APC___Regional_Planning_and_Transit_Oriented_Development_Area_Extensions___2026_78.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — Bylaw No. 7142, 2007 (VERIFIED):** subdivision is regulated by the Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw No. 7142, 2007, which requires Approving Officer approval and ensures new lots are adequately serviced and meet the Zoning Bylaw's minimum lot size and frontage requirements, through the City's pre-application / application / Works & Services Agreement process. Parcel-level standards are confirmed through that process; none is quoted here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/7142_Subdivision.pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/General%20Guide%20to%20Subdivision%20Process%202012-Final.pdf
- **Flood diligence — Floodplain Zone 3:** confirm the specific building's or parcel's flood construction level compliance, insurance and deductible position before subject removal; the City publishes flood management information for its low-lying zones, including the Downtown waterfront. http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Downtown ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Downtown lot or parcel purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is **Vancouver ONLY — it does NOT apply here** (Downtown is in New Westminster). No other Downtown-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in the research — none imported. The federal non-Canadian purchase-prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Downtown NW, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **New Westminster property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining unpaid on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. New Westminster operates its own electrical utility. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot I can buy in Downtown New Westminster?** No verified one — Downtown recorded 0 detached listings in September 2026 and its land sits in commercial, multiple-unit and comprehensive development districts. Land exposure downtown is strata product or a development parcel through the City process. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is Downtown the same as the Quay?** No — on the board taxonomy they are separate sub-areas: Downtown is the Columbia Street core above the waterfront, and the Quay is the Quayside Drive waterfront pocket. The City's OCP map, by contrast, names one area "Downtown (including Quayside)." https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/OUR_CITY_2041_Official_Community_Plan_New_Westminsters_Residential_Neighbourhoods_Map.pdf
- **Q: Can I buy a lot or a townhouse at 811 Carnarvon or Columbia Square today?** No — both are proposals only (811 Carnarvon at pre-application stage; Columbia Square at rezoning stage), QUEUED in the townhomes program and counted nowhere. Nothing in either proposal is for sale as land on this evidence. (Section 2.)
- **Q: I read about RT-A and RT-B townhouse districts — can I build under them?** No — RT-A/RT-B do not exist in New Westminster; that wording in the research file is corrected at the top of this file. The bylaw's townhouse districts are RT-2–RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B, and a townhouse form still needs the parcel to actually sit in one of those districts. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Q: Is Downtown in the Fraser River floodplain?** The waterfront is — Floodplain Zone 3, where many newer buildings have floor space above the Flood Construction Level but egress and the waterfront path sit below it. Confirm the specific parcel's compliance and insurance before subject removal. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Q: What does subdividing or re-servicing a Downtown parcel require?** Approving Officer approval under the Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw No. 7142, 2007, with new lots adequately serviced and meeting the Zoning Bylaw's minimum lot size and frontage, through the City's pre-application / application / Works & Services Agreement process. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/7142_Subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Does SkyTrain proximity give my parcel automatic extra density?** No — provincial Transit Oriented Development Areas enable density within 800 m of the stations (two are in Downtown), but what that means for a specific parcel is a City / bylaw check, not an entitlement to quote. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/2026_02_17_APC___Regional_Planning_and_Transit_Oriented_Development_Area_Extensions___2026_78.pdf
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax apply to a vacant Downtown property?** No — that 3% tax is Vancouver only. Downtown is in New Westminster. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Downtown address?** By exact address — the one settled locator run in this file (85 Merivale Street → École Qayqayt Elementary → Fraser River Middle → New Westminster Secondary) does not transfer to other streets. Run the address in the SD40 locator. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Downtown's supply is the story, not its land (September 2026):** Downtown's 102 condos & townhomes listings were the second-largest attached inventory of any New Westminster sub-area that month, behind Uptown's 108, and its 15 solds were second only to the Quay's 16 — with zero detached listings, any "land value" conversation downtown is really a building-by-building strata conversation. - **Above the Flood Construction Level is not the same as out of the flood file:** in Floodplain Zone 3 many newer buildings have floor space above the Flood Construction Level while egress and the waterfront path sit below it — the insurance and access questions survive even in a new tower. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
