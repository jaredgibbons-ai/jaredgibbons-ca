## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: this is a built-out village pocket with no meaningful bare-land market. No named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata, or presale LOT offering inside the sub-area is verified on any official or developer source in this program. What trades as land here is (a) improved fee-simple house lots in and around the village core, (b) the fee-simple lot fabric of one new row-house group (48 Landing, cross-referenced below), and (c) assembled development land that has already been absorbed into the Bridge & Elliott mixed-use project. The newest land story in the sub-area is a tenure story, not a vacant-lot story.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future", plus the village permit areas.** A single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules (the former Ladner Area Plan was a schedule of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)"). On top of the plan, the Ladner Village (LV1) and Ladner Village Core (LVC) development permit areas apply in the core — the LVC guidelines address the Village's historic buildings and shape exterior work and redevelopment on village lots, for a fee-simple owner (48 Landing fabric) and a strata alike. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged not settled.** City zone sheets cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, parcel by parcel; an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates. Neither is asserted as settled here — the ruling is Jared's. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it.** Delta adopted its SSMUH zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024, allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies; the RS1 zone also lists a secondary suite as an accessory use. SSMUH is added-unit potential on an existing lot, not a subdivision right — the zone minimums in §2 still govern any actual subdivision, and in the village core the LV1/LVC permit areas sit on top. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing requirements.** A City of Delta council report (January 24, 2024) presents the Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 to repeal and replace Subdivision and Development Standards Bylaw No. 7162, 2015, made under s. 506 of the Local Government Act to regulate and require works and services on subdivision — highways, water, sanitary sewers, drainage and other services and utilities. Final adoption status of Bylaw 8288 and the servicing standard for a specific village parcel: UNCONFIRMED in this pass — confirm with the City before pricing a subdivision. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Streamside rule.** All properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream — including Chilukthan Slough and other sloughs and ditches — fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, which can limit building and subdivision work on the lot. West-of-slough village lots are exactly where this check bites. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Dyke / flood diligence.** Ladner is low land protected by the City's dike, seawall and foreshore system (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure maintained by the City, plus its drainage pump stations). Confirm flood construction requirements for the specific lot with the City before assuming a rebuild or infill design works. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **ALR rules (village-surrounding farm ground; in-sub-area ALR UNVERIFIED, §2).** ALR residences cannot be stratified — strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)) — and subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, as the ALC states it. ALC residence caps: principal residence 500 m² or less total floor area; a secondary suite only within the principal residence; one additional residence of 90 m² or less (parcels 40 ha or less) or 186 m² or less (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — three tiers, never a flat figure.** Gross annual farm income required: $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on an operation under 0.8 ha. Applications are due by October 31 for the next taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an error. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **GST on vacant land vs GST on new homes — the distinction this sub-area turns on.** CRA's position, stated only at the level its publication supports: most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (for example, land kept for personal use), but a sale is taxable where the land was subdivided into more than two parts, and sales by developers/businesses are generally taxable supplies. Separately, the new HOMES at 48 Landing and Bridge & Elliott carry GST because they are new housing sold by a developer — that is a housing tax point, not a land rule, and the two must not be merged on a page. Finer points for a specific sale: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home money — stated precisely.** The first-time home buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — it applies to new housing (a 48 Landing home), NOT to bare land itself. The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs and the First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit, for a qualifying home. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-time buyers' exemption: no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, phasing to $1,150,000 — the exemption that matters for a new village home; its application to a bare-land purchase is facts-specific: UNCONFIRMED for bare land in this file. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA) — the Bridge & Elliott frame.** A presale buyer must receive a disclosure statement; s. 21 gives 7 days from the later of signing or acknowledging that statement to rescind, and deposits are held in trust. https://meridianlawgroup.ca/pre-sale-properties-and-a-developers-requirements-to-disclose-building-permits-financing-commitments-and-buyers-rights-to-terminate-agreements/
- **Speculation and vacancy taxes — Delta's actual position.** The City of Delta IS listed by the Province as a designated taxable area for the BC Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so owners of residential-class property in Delta must complete the annual declaration. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is a City of Vancouver tax ONLY and does not apply in Delta. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition — the land point.** CMHC states the prohibition (to January 1, 2027, in census metropolitan areas including Metro Vancouver) covers buildings of three or fewer dwelling units, and that effective March 27, 2023 it does NOT apply to vacant land — vacant land zoned for residential and mixed use can be purchased by non-Canadians and used for any purpose, including residential development. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/media-newsroom/news-releases/2023/amendments-prohibition-purchase-residential-property-non-canadians-regulations
- **Strata block — for the sub-area's strata lots, not a land form here.** Form B (SPA s. 59) within 7 days of request, disclosing fees, levies, CRF balance and insurance summary; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — the framework behind Delta Green's verified 55+ apartment building at 4686 51 Street in this sub-area. A 48 Landing fee-simple lot carries NONE of this: no Form B, no fees, no bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Delta property taxes and holding cost.** Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. No area-specific levy for this sub-area was confirmed on a City source: UNVERIFIED. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any bare land for sale in the village core?** No named bare-land development is verified here — the core is built out, so "buying land" means an improved fee-simple lot, a teardown, or a new home on a newly created fee-simple lot at a project like 48 Landing. (§2 above)
- **Q: What exactly is a 48 Landing lot?** A fee-simple lot: your own title, your own small lot, a row house on it, and no strata at all — no fees, no Form B, no bylaws. The checks are title, covenants and municipal rules, including the village permit areas. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/4778-48b-street
- **Q: Can I subdivide a village lot?** Only if the parcel's zone allows it — minimums run from RS7 at 335 m² and RS6 at 390 m² up to RS5 at 550 m² (460 m² outside the SFR designation), read parcel by parcel — and then the City's subdivision and servicing standards, the LV1/LVC permit areas and, within 30 m of the slough, the Streamside DPA all apply. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Q: Is a village lot on municipal water and sewer, or well and septic?** The village core is within the City's water, sanitary sewer and drainage systems; no source in this program documents well/septic inside this sub-area. Confirm the specific parcel's services and connection charges with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Does the heritage/village-core status limit what I can build?** The Ladner Village Core development permit area in the OCP addresses the Village's historic buildings and can shape exterior renovations and redevelopment in the core — it binds a fee-simple owner just as it binds a strata. Interior work is a different matter; exterior form is the permit area's business. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf
- **Q: Does GST apply when I buy here?** Two different answers for two different purchases: a resale lot from an individual is generally GST-exempt per CRA (personal-use land), while a new 48 Landing home carries GST as new housing — partly recoverable for eligible first-time buyers under the Bill C-4 rebate, which applies to the housing, not to bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What are the holding costs on a village lot?** Delta property taxes on the City's calendar (2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026), plus insurance and drainage/utility charges as the City bills them — and on a fee-simple lot, every exterior and drainage cost is the owner's, with no strata to share it. Parcel-specific figures: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Q: What about the slough and flooding?** Any property within 30 m of a stream, including Chilukthan Slough and drainage ditches, is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement DPA, and the whole village sits behind the City's dike and drainage system — confirm flood construction requirements for the lot with the City before designing a build. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Q: Could a lot here be ALR?** Inside this sub-area, ALR is UNVERIFIED and none is confirmed — the village core is urban. The ALR is the farm belt around the village; if a rural-fringe parcel is in play, the §3 ALR block (no stratification, ALC subdivision approval, residence caps) governs it. (§2–§3 above)
- **Q: How long does a subdivision or village-core build take?** UNCONFIRMED — no official source in this program publishes Delta approval or build timelines for a village lot; the City's development department is the only authoritative source.
