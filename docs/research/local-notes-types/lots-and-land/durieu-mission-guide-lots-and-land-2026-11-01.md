- **Property taxes — RURAL regime (stated expressly):** a property not located in a city, town, district or village is in a rural area; its owner receives the tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province, not the FVRD or the City of Mission. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural taxes are due July 2, a 5% penalty is added to any portion unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% to any portion unpaid on October 31. **The City of Mission flat 10% penalty does NOT apply to this ground.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Secondary suites in Area F:** the FVRD records significant limits on development options in Electoral Area F with the majority of lands in the ALR, and that secondary suites are freely permitted with no restrictions related to farm use, but must be located within the principal dwelling and could not be a separate detached structure, such as a garden suite, or a multi-unit structure for farm workers. Feed cards describing a property as "zoned for a SECONDARY DWELLING or a SUITE" are listing text only — confirm the FVRD zoning and, on ALR land, the ALC frame for the specific PID. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf
- **ALR block (the majority of inhabited valley land is ALR per the FVRD; status per-parcel, UNVERIFIED here):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR; no flat farm-class figure is asserted for this slug because this file's own research does not document one. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Flood:** the valley floor has the documented 1948 dyke-break history (§2); a specific Durieu property's current flood status, dyke protection and flood-construction requirements are UNVERIFIED at parcel level — check the PID against current floodplain mapping. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, where met — none verified on this ground):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,500,000 lot purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,500,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. (Reference anchors: $500,000 → $8,000; $800,000 → $14,000, with a $6,000 first-time-buyer exemption value at that cap; $1.8M → $34,000; $3.5M → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — a townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met, never silently carried.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

- **Q: Is Durieu part of Mission?** No. Listings carry Mission mailing addresses, but Durieu is an unincorporated FVRD Electoral Area F community in the Hatzic Valley — the FVRD's housing-needs report places Durieu among the Hatzic Valley settlement areas of Electoral Area F — and the City of Mission does not govern it. Tax follows the jurisdiction, not the mailing address. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf
- **Q: Who do I pay property tax to, and what is the penalty?** The Province (Surveyor of Taxes). Rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31 (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11). Mission's flat 10% penalty does NOT apply here. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a second home on a Durieu acreage?** Two gates apply. In Area F a secondary suite must be located within the principal dwelling — a separate detached structure such as a garden suite is not the suite route — and if the parcel is in the ALR, an additional detached residence is limited to 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha) on top of one principal residence of at most 500 m². FVRD bylaw numbers and zone names for Durieu lots are UNVERIFIED in this file — confirm both gates for the specific PID. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I subdivide an ALR parcel?** Only with Agricultural Land Commission approval — ALR subdivision is an ALC application under the Agricultural Land Commission Act, not a local approval alone. The parcel's ALR status itself is UNVERIFIED until checked on the ALC maps by PID. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Is this valley floodplain?** The valley floor has a documented 1948 dyke-break flood (Hatzic Lake grew five times in size and the Hatzic Prairie was largely under water, per the FVRD's Area F history). A specific property's current flood status is UNVERIFIED at parcel level — check the PID before valuing building potential. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf

The confirmed SD75 schools on the valley/Mission side are Dewdney Elementary (37151 Hawkins-Pickle Rd B107), Hatzic Elementary (8465 Draper St), Hatzic Middle School (34875 Moffat Avenue) and Mission Senior Secondary (32939 7th Ave) — but catchment assignment for a specific Durieu address is UNVERIFIED, so confirm with School District 75.
