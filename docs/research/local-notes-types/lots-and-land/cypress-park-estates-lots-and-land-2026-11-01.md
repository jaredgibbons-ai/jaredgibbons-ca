## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no open bare-land market in Cypress Park Estates — stated plainly at the top. The existing ground trades as fee-simple RS lots and Rodgers Creek CD3 parcels, and the only large land story is a documented development program, not lot inventory: the Cypress Village / Eagleridge Area Development Plan west of Cypress Bowl Road (approved, with its own CV zone) and the Rodgers Creek development permits. No bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering was verified. The land diligence that matters here is the hazard permit frame — wildfire, watercourse and foreshore — and the CD3 lot-size-band density.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form on the existing streets

- **Established Upper Lands lots:** single-family homes on Chippendale Road, Uplands Way, Woodgreen Drive and the Rodgers Creek streets, in RS2, RS3 and RS5 single-family zones (Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010, Sections 202, 203 and 205), including British Pacific Properties subdivisions of single-family parcels on Uplands Way and at the end of Chippendale Road under the Cypress Village (CV) Zone as the District's development-applications record states them — built lot fabric, documented as such, not a lot offering. https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/development-applications/cypress-village-area

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010:** RS2, RS3 and RS5 single-family zones (Sections 202, 203, 205) with the Section 200 minimum-site-area ladder in Section 2; CD3 for Rodgers Creek, with density set by lot-size band (Sections 601–603); and the CV (Cypress Village) Zone (Section 750) for Cypress Village. Secondary suites and a detached secondary suite are permitted uses in the single-family zones under the bylaw's ground-oriented rules, and secondary suites must be registered with the District. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zoning%20Bylaw%204662%20-%20Section%20601%20-%20603%20Comprehensive%20Developmental%20Zone%20%28CD1%20to%20CD3%29.pdf

- **SSMUH / added units as the District states it:** RS-zone lots carry the suite positions above (secondary suite + detached secondary suite, registration required); the area-specific SSMUH unit counts as the District states them are UNVERIFIED in the research — do not price density here from another area's table; confirm with the District for the parcel. https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/development-regulations/single-family-lots/secondary-suites
- **Subdivision + servicing — District servicing-bylaw number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** no District of West Vancouver subdivision/servicing bylaw number is confirmed on a primary source in this file's research, so none is quoted and none is invented; the process is documented instead: subdivision potential runs off the parcel's RS zone minimum site area (or its CD3 lot-size band in Rodgers Creek), servicing is District metered water and sewer billed quarterly, a subdivision can trigger a difficult-terrain development permit on steep terrain, and the hazard permits below gate forest-interface and creek-side work. Confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the District for the specific parcel. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Hazard Development Permits (District frame — the live Cypress checks):** a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface — the defining check here, on consistently sloped terrain per the District's Community Wildfire Resiliency Plan; an Environmental Development Permit for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank (Cypress Creek and Rodgers Creek both run through the area); and the Foreshore Development Permit Area frame for waterfront property at risk of coastal flooding, as the District states it. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Community%20Wildfire%20Resiliency%20Plan%20-%20Out%202025.pdf and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Cypress Park Estates the same place as Cypress Mountain?** No — the residential area is on and below Cypress Bowl Road, while BC Parks states Cypress Mountain Resort is entirely within Cypress Park (Cypress Provincial Park), reached by the same road from Highway 1. Never blur the three Cypress names in a listing. https://bcparks.ca/cypress-park/
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot here?** No verified open-market one — the existing ground is built RS and CD3 parcels, and the large land program (Cypress Village) is an approved development plan with permits for initial works, documented as a program, not as lots for sale. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What lot sizes govern the existing streets?** RS minimums named for this area are RS2 at 1,625 m² and RS3 at 975 m² (RS5 at 488 m² also appears in the area's zone set); in Rodgers Creek, CD3 sets density by lot-size band instead. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Q: What permit does a forest-edge lot need?** A Wildfire Hazard Development Permit — the permit area runs within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface, and the District wildfire plan calls this consistently sloped terrain; confirm FireSmart/insurance requirements before subjects come off. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Community%20Wildfire%20Resiliency%20Plan%20-%20Out%202025.pdf
- **Q: What about creek-side lots?** An Environmental Development Permit for work within 15 metres of a watercourse bank top — Cypress Creek and Rodgers Creek both cross the area — and steep-lot subdivisions can also trigger a difficult-terrain permit. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Q: Which school serves my address?** UNVERIFIED — the SD45 locator returned NO RESULT for the sample address 3900 Cypress Bowl Road (outside its listed ranges for that road), and Cypress Park Primary is a K–3 school at 4355 Marine Drive, not a confirmed catchment for an Upper Lands home. Verify every address directly with SD45. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/
- **Q: What is Cypress Village, and is it built?** A planned new community west of Cypress Bowl Road — Area Development Plan approved, Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 5206, 2024, initial works under Development Permit 24-115 to British Pacific Properties Limited, Eagleridge lands dedicated as park. Its shopping, school and community centre are future, not present. https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/development-applications/cypress-village-area
- **Q: Where does Hawksley belong in the page set?** It is documented in this slug's condos and townhomes siblings and counted in the british-properties townhomes file; page ownership is an OPEN item for Jared. This land file takes nothing from it. (FILE STATUS; Section 2.)
- **Q: Does the additional school tax apply here?** It can: 0.2% on the residential assessed portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
