## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**This topic file carries NO land roster of its own — stated plainly at the top. No named subdivision, bare-land strata plan or presale lot offering is counted as lot inventory here. What a Surrey-wide land search surfaces is individual parcels under two different rule systems, and this section documents those systems, the tenure forms, and the queue items — never a roster.**

### System 1 — serviced urban infill lots (fee-simple by default)

- Inside the residential neighbourhoods, lots sit under the zones of Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000; the consolidated bylaw text is the source for any zone's minimum subdivision lot area, confirmed parcel by parcel in COSMOS. A complete Surrey zone-minimum table was not re-extracted in the research pass — UNVERIFIED as a table here; the program's master research records individual zone minimums (for example, R3 at a 660 m² minimum subdivision lot area in the South Newton plan area) from the same bylaw text. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- Stock character as the research states it: serviced single-family building lots; permit-ready and SSMUH-positioned lots (the feed's Fleetwood cards include a permit-ready duplex lot and a fourplex-positioned vacant lot); older houses held at land value, including assembly parcels near planned SkyTrain and NCP areas. Fee-simple is the default tenure: no strata corporation, no Form B, no strata fees. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-lots-land.html
- Servicing sorts this stock: SSMUH exemptions include properties not connected to community water and sewer, and lowland parcels carry the Serpentine–Nicomekl floodplain layer, before any design work starts. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands

### System 2 — agricultural parcels on the edges (ALR where applicable)

- Farm and acreage parcels sit under the Agricultural Land Reserve rules where a parcel is in the reserve: subdivision generally requires Agricultural Land Commission permission, and the housing envelope is the ALC frame in §3 — a principal residence of 500 m² or less with the suite inside it, not a future strata site. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **ALR vs non-ALR split:** no Surrey-wide split is published on an official source — none is stated. A specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address; that single check controls the housing caps and subdivision permission in §3. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- Riparian constraints reach into both systems: Surrey plan areas record fish-bearing creeks whose riparian rules can affect parcels — for example, the South Newton plan area includes Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton

### Bare-land strata — its own form

- A bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure: the buyer owns the strata lot (essentially the building lot itself) and the house on it; Form B, strata fees for common property and registered bylaws apply to the lot. It is NOT fee-simple, and bare-land strata parcels can surface in a land feed — tenure is a title question per parcel. Bare-land strata lots exist in newer Surrey communities, but **no named bare-land strata development is counted as lot inventory in this topic file**, and the Surrey-wide freehold versus bare-land strata split for land is UNVERIFIED — no official source publishes it. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current, in full.** Council adopted Surrey 2050 on June 15, 2026; it replaces the Surrey Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2013, No. 18020, which is never cited as current. Secondary plans (NCPs and town centre plans) sit under it, and parcel designations are read in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000** divides the city into zones and regulates use, density, siting and subdivision; the SSMUH amendments (adopted Jul 8, 2024) consolidated 20 existing single-family and duplex zones into 9 zones (RA, R1, R2, R2-O, R3, R4, R5, R5-S, R6). https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH / added units:** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, applying to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide, does not create new lots, and does not waive floodplain, riparian or servicing requirements. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites:** only one suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite, and never a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision / servicing:** a new lot must meet its zone's minimum subdivision lot area in Bylaw No. 12000. The research file states no servicing-bylaw number; verified on the City's own Land Development pages in this pass: the **Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** is the City's subdivision and development (servicing) bylaw, and the City's process runs through its Engineering Land Development process to a Servicing Agreement that defines the off-site works and securities required for final approval. No other city's servicing number is used for Surrey. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Development Cost Charges:** the live page describes DCCs as per-lot or per-square-foot fees collected by the City of Surrey and Metro Vancouver at subdivision or building permit issuance to fund roads, water, sewer, drainage and parkland infrastructure. Current DCC rates from the City's DCC bylaw: UNVERIFIED in the research — direct opening of the City's DCC pages was stopped by a 429 response on 2026-11-01 and was not retried. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-lots-land.html
- **Tree protection:** the live page states that protected trees on private lots require a City Tree Cutting Permit before removal, backed by an arborist report and a retention or replacement plan. The Tree Protection Bylaw's own thresholds (protected-tree definitions, permit triggers, replacement ratios, Tree Fund amounts): UNVERIFIED in the research — direct opening was stopped by the same 429 response. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-lots-land.html
- **Floodplain:** Surrey's coastal floodplain runs along the Nicomekl and Serpentine Rivers and the Serpentine–Nicomekl lowlands, so low-lying rural and edge parcels carry flood-construction and diking questions upland lots do not. Parcel-level flood elevation: UNVERIFIED — confirmed with the City per parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **Streamside / riparian:** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, with different setbacks by stream class, complemented by the Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit process; COSMOS shows environmental or riparian development permit areas per parcel. No single setback figure is stated for Surrey land generally — the parcel's class and Part 7A govern. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only — only the property owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, business licence **$358 per year** on the City's program page. **Conflict disclosed:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposing the STR bylaw amendments put the licence at $350/yr plus fees — this file carries $358 with $350 disclosed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R094.pdf
- **ALR block, in full:** subdivision of ALR land requires ALC permission, apart from the limited cases in the ALR General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). A principal residence built without an ALC application must be **500 m² or less** total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at **90 m² or less** (parcels of 40 ha or smaller) or **186 m² or less** (larger parcels), where Surrey bylaws also permit it. **Farm class — three-tier, in full:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot):** Form B (SPA s.59) — fees, amounts owed, approved levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page. Depreciation reports every 5 years for 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024. CRF minimum 10% since Nov 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted). Only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. **Fee-simple contrast:** a fee-simple urban lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** $1,215,000 parcel (the Surrey community detached median for Sep 2026, used as an arithmetic illustration only — it is NOT a land price), fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $1,015,000 ($20,300) = **$22,300**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. The live page's own blanket GST position was not verified against a CRA source in the research and is not carried here; anything finer: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Water and septic on unserviced parcels:** the provincial GWELLS registry holds recorded wells (older wells may not appear); domestic groundwater use needs no licence, while non-domestic use, including irrigation, requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act; Fraser Health requires a home not connected to a community sewer to use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. **Foreclosure:** Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry — the registry mapping for a Surrey foreclosure is UNVERIFIED here and no foreclosure angle is documented in this slug's research. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where do buildable lots in Surrey come from?** Subdivision against the parcel's zone minimum in Zoning By-law No. 12000, and added-unit development on existing lots under SSMUH (3 units under 280 m², 4 units above it, up to 6 near a frequent bus stop). On the agricultural edges, reserve land generally needs ALC permission to subdivide. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: What is the difference between an urban lot and an ALR parcel in the same search?** Everything that matters: the urban lot runs on Bylaw No. 12000 and SSMUH; the reserve parcel also runs on ALC housing caps (principal ≤500 m², suite inside only, additional residence 90 m² / 186 m²) and ALC subdivision permission. Pricing one system off the other is the standard Surrey land error. (§2 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a farm parcel in Surrey?** Only within tight limits: ALR land generally needs ALC permission to subdivide, apart from limited approving-officer cases in the ALR General Regulation, and any new lot must also meet its Bylaw No. 12000 zone minimum. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Q: How do I check whether a lot is in the ALR?** On the ALC maps, by PID or civic address — reserve status controls the 500 m² cap, the additional-residence limits, and whether subdivision needs Commission permission. Run it before subjects come off. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same as a freehold lot?** No. A bare-land strata lot is a strata lot (Form B, fees, bylaws) whose lot is essentially the building lot itself; a fee-simple lot has no strata at all. The Surrey-wide split is UNVERIFIED — read tenure off title per parcel. (§2 above)
- **Q: What extra rules apply to a low-lying lot near the Nicomekl or Serpentine?** Surrey's coastal floodplain runs along those rivers and the Serpentine–Nicomekl lowlands, so a lowland parcel can carry flood-construction levels and diking-related requirements set by the City — confirm the parcel's position and levels with the City before writing. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **Q: What will servicing and subdivision cost on top of the price?** The servicing works themselves run under the Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 through the City's Servicing Agreement process, parcel by parcel. Current DCC rates: UNVERIFIED in this research (the City's DCC pages could not be opened in the pass) — get the parcel's DCC and servicing figures from the City in writing, never from the listing. (§3 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a Surrey lot?** PTT on the general tiers — on the worked example in §3 ($1,215,000, illustration only) the bill is $22,300 with no exemption. GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level; the live page's GST summary is UNVERIFIED in the research, so confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is an NCP assembly parcel a lot I can build one house on?** It is a development site priced on a plan designation, documented as such in §2 — not a serviced building lot. Its value question is the designation and the assembly, confirmed in COSMOS and with the City, not the zone minimum alone. (§2 above)
- **Q: Are there market statistics for Surrey land?** No land figure is stated in this file: SnapStats publishes no vacant-land category, so land sale counts, price per acre and land days on market are UNVERIFIED. The §1 figures are Surrey all-residential context only. (§1 above)
