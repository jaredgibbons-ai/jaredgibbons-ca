## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, in effect July 1, 2026:** adopted June 9, 2026, it replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) outright — the 1965 bylaw is repealed, NOT current, and must never be cited as current for this area. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025:** replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. The Lochdale Urban Village Community Plan (adopted July 25, 2022) covers the urban village north of the area across Lougheed Highway — it is not a Government Road plan. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **SSMUH, as the City states it:** Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing District in June 2024 — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. Corridor parcels in other zones (including the industrial and office parcels) follow their own zone. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — base number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** Burnaby's servicing instrument is the Subdivision Control and Development Servicing Bylaw — the former Subdivision Control Bylaw as renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024. The BASE bylaw number is held UNCONFIRMED in this file on the program's direction: no base number is asserted. The process documented here runs off the zone (new parcels must conform to the Zoning Bylaw for the zone) and the City's servicing-improvements regime for larger residential projects; parcel-level works-and-services requirements are confirmed with the City's Engineering Department for the specific lot before a split is priced. (Program direction for batch L5; research file above.)
- **Watercourse diligence:** Stoney Creek and its trail system are dedicated park lands and the setback rules travel with the creek — check watercourse setbacks early on creek-edge lots. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Government Road ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Government Road lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Government Road-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Government Road, and the position is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant-lot subdivision or a lot presale I can buy into on Government Road?** No verified one — the corridor is established houses, strata pockets and industrial/office land; no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata plan or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Can a Government Road house lot add units?** If the lot is R1, yes — under Burnaby's R1 SSMUH District a house lot may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and multi-unit lots may be stratified. Corridor parcels in other zones follow their own zone, so confirm the parcel's zone first. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create here?** No zone minimum is stated in this file beyond the R1 unit-count thresholds (280 m² / 281 m²), because typical lot sizes are UNVERIFIED and subdivision runs off the parcel's confirmed zone plus the City's servicing requirements — get the Engineering pre-application answer for the specific lot before pricing a split. (Sections 2–3.)
- **Q: Is the lot I am looking at residential or industrial land?** Both exist on this corridor: houses and strata sit south of Lougheed Highway, and the Lake City industrial and office lands run along the Lougheed corridor as the area's employment side. A corridor parcel's zone is confirmed parcel by parcel — never assume R1. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- **Q: Does the Stoney Creek edge affect a lot?** Stoney Creek Park and Trail System and the conservation lands are dedicated park lands running through the area's east side toward Burnaby Lake, and the setback rules travel with the creek — check watercourse setbacks early, and note no floodplain designation for the residential streets was confirmed. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- **Q: Is a Government Road lot serviced?** The City's utility wording puts established homes on annual water and sewer billing (detached water charge $652.33 for 2026, a 5% discount if paid by the due date, sewer as a parcel tax for single-family homes) — confirm the specific parcel's connections and any servicing upgrade a subdivision will require. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Q: Which schools serve a Government Road lot?** By exact address only — in-area schools include École Seaforth Elementary (7881 Government Rd) and Stoney Creek Community Elementary (2740 Beaverbrook Cres), with Cameron Elementary and Burnaby Mountain Secondary on the east side, but the locator result for the sample address is UNVERIFIED in this file. Run the address in the SD41 locator, and do not assume a French immersion seat from the Seaforth name. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
