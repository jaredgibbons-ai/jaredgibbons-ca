## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is 48 Landing a strata?** No — the sibling files record it as Row House (Non-Strata) by Asanti Homes: fee-simple lots, no strata corporation, no Form B, no strata fees. That is exactly why it is this area's fee-simple exemplar. It is documented here as land form only, with no building count taken. (§2 above)
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot near Ladner Elementary?** Occasionally one trades — the feed surfaced a building-lot listing — but no bare-land development or subdivision selling lots was verified here. Expect to buy an existing lot, usually with a house on it. (§2 above)
- **Q: Does living in this sub-area guarantee Ladner Elementary?** No. Catchments are address-based; the published catchment boundary is a document-based fact from SD37's Ladner Area maps (Oct 2017), and the locator sample exists only for the school's own address. Check the exact address. (§1 above)
- **Q: How small a new lot can be created here?** By zone: RS7 335 m² at the small end, RS6 390 m², RS5 550/460 m², duplex RD4 504 m² / RD1 836 m², and 4,000 m² in the RS1–RS3 / RD2–RD3 lower-density zones. The parcel's zone governs. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I add a coach house instead of subdividing?** That is the SSMUH route: up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots, with coach house and garden suite options where the zoning applies — units, not new lots. Confirm the zone first. (§3 above)
- **Q: What does the village-core permit area do to a rebuild?** The Ladner Village Core DPA addresses the Village's historic buildings — exterior renovation and redevelopment in the core are read against its guidelines, on top of zoning. (§3 above)
- **Q: What is the 30-metre streamside rule here?** Any property within 30 m of a stream — Chilukthan Slough and ditches included — is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement DPA, which can limit additions and rebuilds. (§3 above)
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a 48 Landing home?** It is new construction, so GST applies to the home purchase — with the FTHB GST rebate for eligible first-time buyers (100% to $1M, max $50,000) and the New Housing Rebate underneath. On bare land, the CRA GI-003 frame governs by seller/history instead. (§3 above)
- **Q: What PTT on a $1.5M lot?** $28,000 with no exemption ($2,000 at 1% + $26,000 at 2%). (§3 above)
- **Q: Is Bridge & Elliott a land opportunity?** No — it is a six-storey mixed-use presale of built homes by Headwater Projects, documented as a development site. A purchase there is a REDMA presale, not a lot purchase. https://headwaterprojects.com/development/bridge-and-elliott
