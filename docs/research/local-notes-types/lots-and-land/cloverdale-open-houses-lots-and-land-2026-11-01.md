## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), carried verbatim as the sibling files sourced it:** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, states that a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Representation at an open house:** a licensed real estate professional in BC must work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party. The regulator is BCFSA, and the Province's Buying and selling a home page directs prospective purchasers to BCFSA. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/real-estate-bc/buying-selling/buying-and-selling-home

- **OCP / zoning (Surrey frame):** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026), and Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **SSMUH as the City states it (Jul 8, 2024).** Maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Secondary suites: one per dwelling unit, not in a coach house or garden suite, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area — not a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision + servicing process.** Surrey subdivision proceeds through detailing and a Servicing Agreement defining off-site works, services and financial securities for final approval. **The Surrey servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in this file** — no number is stated in this slug's research or a cited City source opened for it, and no other city's number is borrowed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey).** Permitted, owner-only, under 90 consecutive days per patron, owner must live at the property as primary residence. Licence figure CONFLICT: $358/yr program figure carried, with the City's 2024 corporate report proposing $350/yr + fees disclosed — check the City licence page before quoting. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Strata block — for any strata lot (including a bare-land strata lot) viewed on this feed:** Form B (SPA s. 59) within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR block (farm-edge parcels at the community's edges):** subdivision requires ALC approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class in FULL three-tier form: $2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; apply by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors: $500,000 → $8,000; $700,000 → $12,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
