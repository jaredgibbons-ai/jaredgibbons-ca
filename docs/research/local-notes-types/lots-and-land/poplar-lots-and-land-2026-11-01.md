## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: Poplar has no named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata, and no presale LOT offering verified on an official or developer source — and the land position for this ground is documented in its LAND HOME, the poplar-guide lots-and-land file, which this file cross-references and does not re-document. What trades here is individual fee-simple parcels in two distinct fabrics: urban RS lots in the north (King Road / Windsor Street / Marshall Road) and larger rural lots toward Huntingdon Road and the south edge, where A1 zoning and the ALR take over. Verified named land developments counted in THIS file: 0 new.**

- **Fee-simple lots are the default — and effectively the only — land form.** The research file records the established stock as freehold detached housing, on urban lots in the north and on larger rural lots toward the south edge; strata in the sub-area is limited to the townhome complexes along King Road and Windsor Street / Marshall Road. A bare lot sold in either fabric is a fee-simple parcel: the buyer's own title, no strata corporation, no strata fee and no Form B. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- **Lot character, as the research states it:** urban minimums are set by the RS zones — RS3 minimum interior lot 540 m² and RS5 minimum interior lot 420 m² in City rezoning reports applying Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014; rural A1 land subdivides at a minimum of 8.0 ha. These are subdivision minimums, not measured sizes and not a promise any specific lot can be split — a lot already at or near its zone minimum has no subdivision room. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/56399/ and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/content/coa/coabylaws/z/zoning/?xsl=/templates/browse.xsl
- **Bare-land strata: none verified in Poplar.** No bare-land strata plan with a Poplar address was confirmed in this slug's research or the land home file. The form is strata tenure where it exists — a bare-land strata lot IS a strata lot, and Form B, strata fees and strata bylaws would apply to the lot exactly as to a built strata unit (§3) — but no Poplar lot is described as bare-land strata here. - **Serviced vs unserviced ground — the north/south split is the point:** urban north lots sit in the City's serviced subdivision fabric (parcel-level connection status still confirmed with the City). Lots at the rural south edge may be on wells and onsite sewage rather than community systems — wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database and onsite systems must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation through Fraser Health. Never assume south-edge servicing matches King Road servicing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs** — RS3 (minimum interior lot 540 m²), RS5 (420 m²) in the urban north; A1 agricultural in the rural south (minimum subdivision lot area 8.0 ha where it applies). The zone section for the specific lot — not the sub-area name — decides subdivision, suite and added-unit questions. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014 and https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/56399/
- **OCP: Abbotsforward 2050 is CURRENT (adopted December 2025).** This slug's research file's "Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated" citation is STALE — the standing correction governs and is followed here; flag the stale number as a Jerry sweep item wherever a page carries it. No Poplar-specific neighbourhood plan is listed in the OCP. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **SSMUH / added units on a lot:** in June 2024 Council adopted Zoning Bylaw amendments implementing provincial small-scale multi-unit housing — June 2024 adoption is City-confirmed; the SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED ("3560-2024" is asserted in some research material and must not be asserted — mark it UNCONFIRMED wherever met). Zoning Bylaw s.140 carries the small-scale rules and s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings, with City-report criteria including a lot of at least 400 m², at least 12.0 m frontage, a Collector or Local road and not on a cul-de-sac bulb. **Abbotsford's suite cap is under 50% of the principal dwelling, and a coach/garden suite is capped at 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m² — Abbotsford's rules ONLY.** https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Subdivision and servicing requirements:** subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer against the zone's minimum lot size/width/depth and the City's works-and-services (Development Bylaw) requirements. The Development Bylaw's current number/year is UNCONFIRMED in this program — none is quoted; the detailed servicing requirements for a specific Poplar subdivision are UNCONFIRMED until the City's current Development Bylaw text and the file's Works and Services Report are read. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58305/
- **ALR rules, where ALR ground exists (south edge):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal residence 500 m² or less; a secondary suite only within the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² (parcels 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — three tiers, never a flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on an operation under 0.8 ha, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Strata block — background for any strata lot a Poplar search spills into:** Form B (SPA s. 59) within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. No bare-land strata is verified in Poplar (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-time buyers' exemption: no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — both are home exemptions, and their application to a bare-land purchase is facts-specific: UNCONFIRMED for bare land in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. Finer points for any specific Poplar parcel: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I actually buy a bare building lot in Poplar?** Yes — but as an individual fee-simple parcel, not from a named bare-land subdivision: no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified for Poplar. The full land treatment is in the land home file (§2 cross-references). (Section 2 above)
- **Q: How big are the lots, and can I split one?** Zoning minimums are RS3 540 m² and RS5 420 m² for interior lots in the urban north, and 8.0 ha under A1 in the rural south — those are the floors a NEW lot must meet, so a purchase only has subdivision room if the parcel is comfortably larger than its zone minimum and meets frontage, road and servicing requirements through the Approving Officer. Most built Poplar lots sit at or near their minimum. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/56399/
- **Q: Is a south-edge lot serviced, or will I need a well and septic?** South-edge rural lots may be on wells and onsite sewage rather than community systems — pull the GWELLS well record and the Fraser Health sewerage filings before offering, and confirm City connection status parcel by parcel: UNCONFIRMED for any single lot until those records are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners
- **Q: What extra units can go on a detached lot?** One secondary suite is permitted in all single detached dwellings (s.140.3.8), subject to criteria including a 400 m² minimum lot and 12.0 m frontage; Abbotsford caps a suite at under 50% of the principal dwelling and a coach/garden suite at 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m². The SSMUH bylaw number sometimes cited ("3560-2024") is UNCONFIRMED — do not rely on it. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Q: Is this lot in the ALR — and why does it matter?** South-edge lots can be in or beside the ALR; check the parcel on the ALC map. If it is ALR, subdivision needs ALC approval, residences cannot be stratified, the A1 minimum is 8.0 ha, and the residence caps (principal 500 m²; suite inside the principal only; additional residence 90 m² / 186 m² by the 40 ha line) replace the urban rules. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Is Poplar in a floodplain?** Poplar drains toward the Sumas Prairie system the City describes as low-lying and subject to Nooksack overflow — but a lot-level floodplain finding requires the Zoning Bylaw Schedule E map in the City WebMap and is UNVERIFIED for any single lot in this file. Check before removing subjects. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- **Q: Does GST apply when I buy a lot?** Per CRA GI-003: an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt; a sale in the course of a business or of a parcel subdivided into more than two parts can be taxable, and developer sales of newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Have the contract state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What will it cost to hold the land while I plan a build?** Property taxes (due July 2; 5% penalty July 3 and a second 5% September 16, 2026), plus insurance and any servicing or covenant obligations on title. There is no strata fee on a fee-simple lot; if a lot were bare-land strata there would be — one way to test a "bare-land strata" claim: no Form B and no strata fee means it is not strata. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Q: Which schools would a home on this lot feed into?** Catchments are address-based in SD34 and this slug's research runs no locator result — it documents feeder patterns only: the Traditional pathway (South Poplar → Abbotsford Traditional School, 6–12, a choice program) and Jackson Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary as a published feeder pattern. Neither is a locator-confirmed catchment for a specific lot — run the exact address. Note the basis difference with the poplar-guide lots home file, which carries settled locator chains for two sample addresses (FILE STATUS). https://www.abbyschools.ca/policy-18-school-catchment-areas
- **Q: Does the airport limit what I can build on a Poplar lot?** Possibly, at the west side: lots inside the Airport Zoning Regulation area on City drawing M-500 face AZR height surfaces and NAV CANADA / Transport Canada approvals ahead of a City permit. Whether a specific lot is inside that area is a City-map check: UNCONFIRMED parcel by parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
