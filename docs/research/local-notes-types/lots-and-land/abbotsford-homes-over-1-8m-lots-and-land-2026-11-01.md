## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a list of vacant lots over $1.8M in this file?** No — this is a topic/band file with no land roster of its own. The ground behind the band is hillside fee-simple lots and rural acreage, documented in the Abbotsford area files; land status is UNCONFIRMED per parcel. (§2 above)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at the band floor?** At $1,800,000, fully residential, no exemption: $2,000 + 2% × $1,600,000 = **$34,000**. At $2,000,000 it is $38,000. (§2 above)
- **Q: What happens over $3,000,000?** Two tiers stack: 3% on the portion over $2,000,000, **plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000** — at $3,500,000 the full bill is **$93,000 including that further 2% tier**, with the math shown step by step in §2. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a rural estate in this band?** Not at urban scales: A1 minimum subdivision lot area is 8.0 ha and A2 is 16.0 ha in the City's parcel-size work, and subdivision **within the ALR** requires ALC approval on top of the zone minimum. (§3 above)
- **Q: What are the house-size rules on ALR land in this band?** Principal residence ≤500 m², a suite only within the principal residence, and an additional residence at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha) — use the ALC figures, not the live page's FAQ summary, which omits the 186 m² tier. (§3 above)
- **Q: What hillside diligence applies to a McKee / Eagle Mountain lot?** The Steep Slope Development Permit Area position, read against the OCP schedules and the McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023), plus a title check for any registered geotechnical covenant — UNVERIFIED for any individual property until its title is read. (§3 above)
- **Q: Will GST apply to an acreage or lot purchase in this band?** At the CRA GI-003 level only: an individual's personal-use sale is generally exempt; business-course sales and parcels subdivided into more than two parts are taxable examples — and a farm or business parcel needs its own CPA answer per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Does any rebate or exemption soften the tax bill at this band?** Not on price: first-time and newly-built PTT exemption thresholds sit far below $1.8M, and the FTHB GST rebate (max $50,000, phasing out by $1.5M, for new **housing**, not bare land) does not reach a band purchase either.
