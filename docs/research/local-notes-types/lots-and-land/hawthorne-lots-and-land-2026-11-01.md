## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** A single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules; the Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950. Any page attributing a lettered Ladner schedule to Bylaw 8400 is wrong. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged not settled.** City zone sheets cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, parcel by parcel; an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates. Neither is asserted as settled here — the ruling is Jared's. The parcel's own zone sheet is always the read. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **SSMUH.** Delta adopted its SSMUH zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024, allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies; the RS1 zone also lists a secondary suite as an accessory use. SSMUH is added-unit potential on an existing lot, not a subdivision right — the zone minimums in the land home still govern any actual subdivision. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — process, adoption HELD UNCONFIRMED.** A City council report (January 24, 2024) presents the Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 to repeal and replace Bylaw No. 7162, 2015, made under s. 506 of the Local Government Act to regulate works and services on subdivision. Final adoption status of Bylaw 8288: HELD UNCONFIRMED — confirm the governing standard with the City before pricing a subdivision. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Streamside rule.** All Delta properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, which can limit building and subdivision work on the lot. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Dyke / flood diligence.** Ladner is low land protected by the City's dike, seawall and drainage system (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations, approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure). Confirm flood construction requirements for the specific lot with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **ALR rules (in-sub-area ALR UNVERIFIED, §2 — stated for any parcel a buyer is told is ALR).** Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); caps: principal 500 m² or less, suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² or less (40 ha or less) / 186 m² or less (over 40 ha). Farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by October 31) — flat "$2,500+" is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **PTT:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **PTT exemptions.** First-time buyers: no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000), full exemption at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility beyond the documented vacant-land refund route is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122; 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing out to $1,500,000; agreements March 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Strata block (background — no bare-land strata verified in Hawthorne, §2):** Form B within 7 days of request, maximum $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Speculation and vacancy taxes — Delta's actual position.** Delta IS a designated taxable area for the BC Speculation and Vacancy Tax (annual declaration required for residential-class property). The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply in Delta. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition:** no position beyond this file's own cited sources is stated — this slug's research cites no prohibition source, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and not conformed (the guide research for the same ground is a separate file's record and is not imported).
- **Delta property taxes.** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

- **Q: What can I add to a lot without rezoning?** Delta's SSMUH bylaws (8394 and 8395, June 24, 2024) allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — confirm the parcel's zone first, and check the 30 m streamside rule before designing a coach house. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q: Does GST apply when I buy a lot?** At CRA GI-003 level: an individual's personal-use land is generally exempt; land subdivided into more than two parts and developer/business sales are generally taxable. A newly created lot is exactly the case to check before writing the offer. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Is a Hawthorne lot on municipal water and sewer, or well and septic?** Urban Ladner is within the City's water, sanitary sewer and drainage systems; no source in this program documents well/septic inside Hawthorne. Confirm the specific parcel's services and connection charges with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Could a Hawthorne lot be ALR?** Inside Hawthorne, ALR is UNVERIFIED and none is confirmed. If a parcel were ALR, the §3 block would apply: no stratification, ALC approval for subdivision, and the residence caps (500 m² principal; 90 m² / 186 m² additional). (§2–§3.)
- **Q: Which school serves a Hawthorne address?** SD37 School Locator by exact address — the one documented sample (5160 Central Avenue) is Hawthorne Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12); other addresses are UNVERIFIED until run. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
