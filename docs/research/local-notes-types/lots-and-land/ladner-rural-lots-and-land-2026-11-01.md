- **Subdivision of farm land (A1 s. 10.10.8):** in the A1 zone, subdivision is not permitted except where it complies with the Official Community Plan Bylaw and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf

- **ALR block — the governing frame for most of this ground:** residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Delta's A1 text states the same structure in zone terms: one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot (90 m² maximum on lots of 40 ha or less, 186 m² on lots over 40 ha) on the same farm home plate as the farm house, ALC approval required for more than one; an additional farm house (180 m² maximum on lots under 8 ha, 233 m² on lots of 8 ha or more) requires a Professional Agrologist's report supporting its necessity and written ALC approval. Migrant farm worker housing is permitted only where, among other criteria, the lot is in the ALR with farming as a permitted use, the lot is classified as a farm under the Assessment Act, written ALC approval has been obtained, the farm employing the workers is at least 8 ha and the lot is at least 4 ha. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

- **Q: Is Ladner Rural the same search as Ladner farms or East Delta?** No — on this page, Ladner Rural is its own Paragon sub-area and its whole 11-listing feed is labelled Ladner Rural only, with no East Delta or Westham Island cards; the separate ladner-farms page is the one whose feed spans sub-areas. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-rural.html

Establish what is actually owned — the float, the slip, any lease or strata interest — and what the moorage costs and rules are, before subjects. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-rural.html
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Ladner Rural?** Delta's A1 zone permits one farm house per lot at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², on a farm home plate of at most 3,600 m² (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate) — matching the ALC's principal-residence cap of ≤500 m². https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can a Ladner Rural farm have a second dwelling?** Yes, within limits: the A1 zone permits one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot — 90 m² or less on lots of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger lots — on the same farm home plate as the farm house, with a secondary suite only inside the farm house; more than one accessory unit needs ALC approval, and an additional farm house needs an agrologist's necessity report plus written ALC approval. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Ladner Rural farm parcel?** Only within tight limits: in the A1 zone a new lot created by subdivision needs a minimum of 8 ha, Official Community Plan compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy; and any parcel's ALR status itself is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can I stratify the residences on a farm parcel and sell them separately?** No — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); depositing a strata plan is a subdivision and requires ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What does farm class require, in dollars?** The full three-tier test: $2,500 of qualifying farm sales on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the land's value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" figure understates the test and is an error. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Q: Are water and drainage the City's business out here, or mine?** Both, in part: Delta's agricultural ditches run in drainage mode in winter and irrigation mode in summer under City control gates, across 31 pump stations and about 538 km of drainage infrastructure — get the parcel's ditch, drainage and irrigation position from the City, not from a listing's wording, and establish the parcel's flood construction level before designing a farm house (UNVERIFIED for any parcel in this file). http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Is any card on this feed a confirmed working farm I can price as an operation?** No card is presented that way in this file: the feed is generic, sector/quota/board position for any parcel is UNVERIFIED (no board source was opened), and a card's presence confirms nothing about the operation on the ground. Sellers should document any ALC approvals, farm leases and dwelling approvals in place before listing — those are the items a farm buyer's lender and lawyer ask for first. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-rural.html
