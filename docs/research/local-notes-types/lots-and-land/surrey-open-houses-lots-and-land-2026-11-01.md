## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

For a topic file, this section carries first the open-house rule that governs a land viewing, then the Surrey land rules a buyer needs if the open house is for a lot or a house lot — because the open house is where the viewing happens, and title, zoning and tax are where a land purchase is actually diligenced.

### The open-house frame

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), verbatim as sourced in the townhomes sibling for this slug:** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, states that the disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download and ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/surrey-open-houses-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §3
- **Representation at an open house:** a licensed real estate professional in BC must work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party to the transaction. The regulator is the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), and the Province's Buying and selling a home page directs prospective purchasers to BCFSA for information on purchasing property in British Columbia. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/real-estate-bc/buying-selling/buying-and-selling-home
- **What that means in practice:** chatting factually with the host at an open house does not by itself make you their client — but once you start sharing your motivation, budget or needs with a licensee who represents the seller, the disclosure rules engage. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Identification or sign-in for open-house visitors:** NOT stated in any BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source found — UNVERIFIED as a rule; a host may still ask, as a matter of the seller's instructions, but that practice is not in an official source. ### The Surrey land frame (after the open house)

- **OCP:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026 — the old OCP Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and is never cited as current in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Zoning + SSMUH:** Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs, including its SSMUH zones RA to R6 — up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; Surrey's SSMUH zoning was adopted July 8, 2024 in the program record. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites:** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area, one suite per unit; a suite is NOT a separate strata lot. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/surrey-open-houses-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §3.
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision follows the zone for the parcel under Bylaw No. 12000 plus City servicing — a Surrey servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED on a primary source in this topic's research and is not asserted here; confirm with the City for the parcel.
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** STRs are permitted, owner-only, with a City business licence the City's own page prices at $358 per year (plus any applicable application fees) — carried here at $358, with the disclosed conflict that the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350 per year plus fees; direct readers to the City licence page before quoting. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **ALR rules — framework only, no parcel in this file:** where a Surrey parcel is in the ALR, subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); BC Assessment farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Bare-land strata block (applies if the lot viewed is bare-land strata):** a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — Form B diligence (Strata Property Act s. 59; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page), depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023, rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022, and only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above, plus further tax on the residential portion above $3 million as BC sources state it; First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no topic-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file: the topic research documents no primary text on it. Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (foreclosure mapping UNVERIFIED) — carried as a Surrey position, with no foreclosure property documented in this topic file.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I see a vacant lot at a Surrey open house?** If a land listing carries an OPEN HOUSE label in a given week, it appears on this feed like any other listing — detached, townhomes and condos are the documented feed types, and an open house is an event on a listing, not a land program. This file documents no lot of its own to visit. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-open-houses.html
- **Q: Do I need my own agent to attend an open house for a lot?** No official source requires it. Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure of representation where a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or answering general factual questions — but the moment you share your motivation, financial qualifications or needs, that changes, and BCFSA requires unrepresented consumers to be told the risks of dealing with a licensee who represents the other party. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download and https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: Is the host at the open house the seller's agent?** The host is a licensee connected to the listing side; BCFSA requires a licensee to work for either the buyer or the seller and to inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients — assume anything non-factual you tell the host can reach the seller. Use the open house for facts; save your story for your own agent. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: How do I know the open house time is current — and is there a time error on this page?** Each listing carries an OPEN HOUSE label with a date and start and end times, and the site's FAQ states the page updates automatically throughout the day as new MLS open houses go live — check the page the day you plan to go. On accuracy: this page documents NO card time block (times appear only in description text), so the 1-hour card offset cannot occur on this page, and offsets documented on other feeds are not importable here. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-open-houses.html and (Section 2)
- **Q: Do I have to sign in or show ID at an open house?** No BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states an identification or sign-in requirement for open house visitors — UNVERIFIED as a rule; an individual host may still ask, on the seller's instructions. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: How many Surrey lots / homes hold open houses this weekend?** UNVERIFIED — no official source publishes open-house counts, and SnapStats publishes no open-house category. The feed itself is the count for the week in question. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-open-houses.html
- **Q: If I like a lot, what land rules decide what I can build?** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and the Surrey 2050 OCP (Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026), including the SSMUH tiers (3 / 4 / 6 units by lot size and bus-stop distance in Section 3) — confirm the parcel's zone on City mapping, not from the open-house card. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy that lot?** Generally yes if the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided; generally no for an individual's personal-use resale, per CRA GI-003 — and the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which Surrey areas can I cover in one trip?** Group by the City's own communities — City Centre, Cloverdale, Fleetwood, Guildford, Newton, South Surrey and Whalley — which is also how the site splits its open-house pages. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- **Q: Where do I start homework before offer day?** At the regulator: the Province's Buying and selling a home page directs prospective purchasers to BCFSA for purchasing information — read its consumer pages before offer day, not after. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/real-estate-bc/buying-selling/buying-and-selling-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The disclosure rule has an open-house carve-out with a tripwire.** Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure of representation only while the host is hosting an advertised open house or giving factual answers to general questions — the moment the host solicits or receives your motivation, financial qualifications or needs, the exception ends. That is the legal line between small talk and sharing with the seller's side, at a land viewing as at any other. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **The feed is the schedule — there is no separate official open-house calendar.** The page's listings each carry the OPEN HOUSE label with the date and start/end times, and the publisher's FAQ states the page updates automatically throughout the day as new MLS open houses go live; how those dates and times are entered on the brokerage side (board systems, ShowingTime, TouchBase) is not documented on an official public source and is UNVERIFIED. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-open-houses.html
- **A topic page cannot tell you a lot's catchment, ALR status or servicing.** Those are parcel answers in the area file for the ground the lot sits on — SD36 catchments are address-based, ALR is checked parcel by parcel on ALC mapping, and servicing is confirmed with the City. This file asserts none of them for any address. https://www.surreyschools.ca/grandviewheights and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/permitted-uses-in-the-alr/living-in-the-alr-information/
- **Surrey has a new Official Community Plan.** The Surrey 2050 OCP (Bylaw No. 21900) received final adoption on June 15, 2026 — older material citing the PlanSurrey 2013 OCP (Bylaw No. 18020) as current is out of date, which matters when an open-house feature sheet quotes plan designations for a lot's neighbourhood. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
