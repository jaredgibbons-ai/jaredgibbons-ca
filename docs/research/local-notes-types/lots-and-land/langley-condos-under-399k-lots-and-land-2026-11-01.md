## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Township frame (Township ground only):** Zoning Bylaw 2500. SSMUH Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — an eligible lot (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units if 280 m² or smaller, or 4 units if between 280 m² and 4,050 m²; lots larger than 4,050 m² are not eligible; **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020.** Secondary suite cap 120 m² (s.102). A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102 — in the Township definition, an accessory unit only within the second storey of a detached garage — never strata. **Live conflict, flagged not resolved:** s.102 as amended July 2025 lists suite locations including townhouse/rowhouse, while the Township's January 2025 Secondary Suites guide position differs on townhouse suites — both documents stand by date in the research program. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
