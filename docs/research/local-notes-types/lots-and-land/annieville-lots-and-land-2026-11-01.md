## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Annieville land trades as fee-simple house lots — there is no bare-land strata and no named bare-land subdivision verified here. The land story is the large river-side lot and its subdivision path: big RD3/RS1 parcels at 4,000 m² minimums splitting, by rezoning, into small RS6 lots at 390 m². That infill pattern, documented in a City file below, is what "land" means in Annieville.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default and only verified land form

- **Established fee-simple stock:** an established residential area of predominantly single detached houses, mostly freehold detached and duplex lots. The former North Delta Area Plan identified residential infill east of 116 Street between 90 Avenue and 96 Avenue. Fee-simple is the default form here: the owner owns the lot outright, with no strata, no Form B and no strata fees. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf and (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/annieville.md)
- **Lot character from the zone ladder, as the City states it:** RD3 at a 4,000 m² minimum lot, RS6 at 390 m² (width 13 m, depth 30 m) and RS1 at 4,000 m² (width 30 m, average depth 30 m, maximum one single detached dwelling per lot); the full bylaw ladder runs RS1/RS2/RS3/RD2/RD3 at 4,000 m², RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² or 460 m², RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m², RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m², and there is no RD5 zone. These are zone minimums, not a claim that every Annieville lot is that size — confirm the parcel's zone on the City maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS6.pdf and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD3.pdf

- **OCP:** Delta Official Community Plan 2024, Bylaw No. 8400 — the consolidated OCP, with NO lettered schedules (the former area plans sat under the repealed Bylaw No. 3950; the North Delta Area Plan was Schedule C.1 under that bylaw). - **Zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted:** City pages / zone sheets cite Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 (zone sheets and the 10604 River Road notice proceed under No. 7600), while an internal title elsewhere reads "8400, 2025" — CONTESTED in the program's sources; zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel from the zone sheet (RD3 4,000 m² / RS6 390 m² / RS1 4,000 m² ladder in Section 2). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS6.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024; Delta's SSMUH rules allow a combination of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the bylaw applies. Confirm the parcel's zone sheet before promising a configuration. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision in Annieville proceeds by rezoning where the target lot size needs a smaller-lot zone (the documented RD3-to-RS6 two-lot example, Section 2) plus City servicing — detail beyond that documented process is held as follows: Delta's Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 is the program's cited reference, but its adoption detail and parcel-level standards for an Annieville parcel are UNCONFIRMED on a primary source in this file's research. Confirm servicing requirements with the City for the specific parcel; no servicing standard is invented here. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/224579/
- **Escarpment / hazard rule — first-order for river-side lots:** the Fraser River frontage is in the ND1 Fraser River Escarpment Development Permit Area; the OCP states that area is subject to mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence, and that river bank and ravine slopes are subject to erosion. Geotechnical review belongs in the subject list for a frontage lot. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **ALR rules (where an ALR parcel is confirmed — none confirmed in Annieville in the research):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); BC Assessment farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel with the ALC first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata rules:** no bare-land strata is verified in Annieville (Section 2), so no strata block is applied to an Annieville lot in this file. For reference only, where a BC bare-land strata lot is bought elsewhere, the full block applies to the lot: Form B diligence (within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1%/$200K, 2% to $2M, 3% above, +2% on residential portion above $3M; FTHB Program (first $500,000 exempt at ≤$835,000, max $8,000, partial to $860,000) and Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). This is the live distinction for a newly created RS6 lot sold by a builder vs a resale house lot from an individual. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, Mar 20 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land — a lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36%/$6,300/$350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000/$8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What land actually trades in Annieville?** Fee-simple house lots — established detached/duplex lots, and large river-side parcels whose value turns on splitting them. No bare-land strata and no named bare-land subdivision was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How big are the big river-side lots, really?** The zones that carry them — RD3 and RS1 — both set a 4,000 m² minimum lot; RS6, the small-lot zone they split into, sets 390 m² (width 13 m, depth 30 m). Confirm the individual parcel's zone on the City maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS6.pdf
- **Q: How does a big lot become two lots here?** By rezoning plus subdivision — the City's own documented example is 10604 River Road, proposed from RD3 to RS6 for a two-lot subdivision under Bylaw No. 7600. Price the rezoning and servicing answer, not just the house. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/224579/
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house instead of subdividing?** Delta's SSMUH rules allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the bylaw applies (Bylaws 8394+8395, adopted June 24, 2024) — confirm the parcel's zone sheet before promising a configuration. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q: Is the river frontage a hazard area?** Yes — the ND1 Fraser River Escarpment Development Permit Area covers the frontage, and the OCP lists mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence for that area. Geotechnical review belongs in the subjects. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Is any Annieville land in the ALR?** Not confirmed in the sources read — ALR status is a property-specific check through the ALC. If a parcel is in the ALR, subdivision needs ALC approval and the residence caps in Section 3 apply. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What is Annieville Heights — can I buy a lot there?** It is a duplex-home project by Fika Developments and Satori Homes (developer source), not a bare-lot offering; its civic address is UNVERIFIED. It is not counted as presale lots in this file. https://evernest.ca/project/annieville-heights/
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a builder?** Generally yes — newly subdivided lots and developer/business sales are generally taxable under CRA GI-003, unlike an individual's personal-use resale. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a River Road lot?** Catchments are address-based: the documented sample (9240 112 Street) returns Annieville Elementary (K–7) and Delview Secondary (8–12) in the SD37 locator — run the exact lot address. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: What are holding costs on a vacant Annieville lot?** Delta taxes on the City cycle (due July 2, 2026; 5% after, further 5% after September 2, 2026) plus the flat-rate utility fee where it applies ($1,658 for a single-family home in 2026 — vacant-lot application UNCONFIRMED). No strata fees: Annieville lots here are fee-simple. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
