## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

### Surrey frame (Surrey-side parcels)

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — current.** Older neighbourhood concept plans (Morgan Heights, Rosemary Heights, Douglas) are rescinded or superseded — Douglas's designations "have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 – Official Community Plan Update corporate report." Bylaw 18020 is never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/douglas-ncp
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000** governs Surrey-side lots (zones, suite and coach-house rules below; RA dimensions carried as site copy only, §2).
- **Subdivision / servicing: Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830**, through the City's Servicing Agreement process — a new lot must meet its zone's minimum subdivision lot area in Bylaw No. 12000, with servicing confirmed per parcel with the City.
- **SSMUH / added units (Surrey, as the City states it):** up to 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, on eligible lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including properties not connected to community water and sewer — the live page's guide copy likewise notes lots without community water and sewer stay limited to two units. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m.html
- **Secondary suites (Surrey):** only one suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most **40%** of the principal unit's habitable floor area under Bylaw No. 12000, never inside a coach house or garden suite, and never a separate strata lot. The old 90 m² Surrey suite cap is NOT a current rule — never state it. **Coach houses / garden suites:** 35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA/R2/R2-O/R3 lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only — only the property owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, business licence **$358 per year**. **Conflict disclosed:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposing the STR bylaw amendments put the licence at $350/yr plus fees — this file carries $358 with the $350 proposal disclosed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals

### ALR block, in full (eastern South Surrey acreage in scope)

- Subdivision of ALR land requires ALC permission, apart from the limited approving-officer cases in the ALR General Regulation. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). A principal residence built without an ALC application must be **500 m² or less** total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at **90 m² or less** (parcels of 40 ha or smaller) or **186 m² or less** (larger parcels), where Surrey bylaws also permit it. **Farm class — three-tier, in full:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR wherever met; no page stating such a figure is documented in this slug's research, so no such error is asserted for this page. ALR status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps

### Bare-land strata block (the Sea Ridge Bays form, §2)

- Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; CRF minimum 10% since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. **Fee-simple contrast:** the band's detached acreage carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

### Taxes and rebates — with the band's worked PTT examples (inputs + tier math shown)

- **Property transfer tax structure:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Neither exemption reaches this band in practice: First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, maximum saving $8,000) and Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land) both cap out below the band floor. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Worked example — $1,800,000 (the band floor), fully residential, no exemption:** $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $32,000 (2% × $1,600,000) = **$34,000**.
- **Worked example — $2,000,000, fully residential, no exemption:** $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $36,000 (2% × $1,800,000) = **$38,000**.
- **Worked example — $3,500,000, fully residential, no exemption:** $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $36,000 (2% × $1,800,000) + $45,000 (3% × $1,500,000) + $10,000 (further 2% × $500,000 residential portion above $3,000,000) = **$93,000**, including the further 2% tier. (Standing anchors; the townhomes sibling's $78,000 figure for this input is not carried — see FILE STATUS.)
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). For a built band home, 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land, and phase out below this band:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — fully phased out at band prices. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
