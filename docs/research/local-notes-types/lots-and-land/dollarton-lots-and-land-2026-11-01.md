## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No meaningful bare-land market exists in Dollarton — stated plainly at the top. The shore trades as established fee-simple R1/R2 lots along and above Dollarton Highway, with one built strata complex beside Cates Park (cross-referenced, counted elsewhere), no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering verified. The land diligence that matters here is the shoreline itself: the 7.6 m Ocean Natural Boundary setback, the eroding Cates Park shoreline, the District flood-level model, and the federal port-authority foreshore layer.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default (and only) form

- **Established shore lots:** detached houses on R1 and R2 lots along and above Dollarton Highway; the port authority's shoreline report notes the shore is backed by residential properties. Predominantly freehold detached in the sources read. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://www.portvancouver.com/sites/default/files/2025-04/02%20A4%20Overview%20Effects%20Assessment.pdf
- **Lot character from the zone ladder, as stated:** District Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525 (Bylaw 8799) — R1 minimum lot 4,000 m² with 30 m width and 34 m depth; R2 minimum lot 660 m² with 18 m width and 34 m depth; modifiers R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m, R2(S2) at 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m. The R1/R2 setback table sets a minimum setback to the Ocean Natural Boundary Line of 7.6 m for a principal building — directly relevant on Dollarton's waterfront lots. These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size; the zone on a single lot is confirmed through the District's GeoWeb. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Serviced position, as the District states it:** District single-family utility fees in 2026 include water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60, with combined single-family utilities of $2,571.20 in 2026. Parcel-level septic or well status: UNVERIFIED. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** parcel-level ALR status: UNVERIFIED in the research. No ALR percentage split is stated; check any parcel on ALC mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Shoreline / hazard position:** the port authority states the Whey-ah-Wichen (Cates Park) shoreline is eroding and likely to worsen with sea level rise and climate change, threatening archaeological resources, shoreline habitat, park infrastructure and cultural sites; a shoreline adaptation and restoration project at the park is proposed by the District of North Vancouver and the Tsleil-Waututh Nation and is under review by the port authority. District OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets Development Permit Areas including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard, and the District publishes property-level GeoWeb reports including modelled flood construction levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. The waters of Burrard Inlet, including the intertidal area along this shore, fall within federal lands and waters managed by the Vancouver Fraser Port Authority — foreshore work is not a District-only approval. https://www.portvancouver.com/permit/shoreline-adaptation-and-restoration-whey-ah-wichen-cates-park and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/ and https://www.portvancouver.com/about

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — District Zoning Bylaw No. 3210, Section 525 (Bylaw 8799):** the R1/R2 minimum-lot ladder and the 7.6 m Ocean Natural Boundary setback in Section 2; an R2 lot allows a maximum of one principal dwelling unit, one secondary suite and one coach house. Since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **OCP — District Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 7900:** the District plan covering this area, including the Maplewood Village Centre plans for the west end of Dollarton and its Development Permit Areas in Schedule B. The City of North Vancouver's OCP 8400 is a different municipality's plan and is never imported here. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **SSMUH as the District states it:** the District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop — check the lot's classification before marketing density. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — District servicing-bylaw number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** no District of North Vancouver subdivision/servicing bylaw number is confirmed on a primary source in this file's research, so none is quoted and none is invented; the City of North Vancouver's Bylaw 8014 is a City bylaw and is expressly NOT the District's — it is named here only as that exclusion. The process is documented instead: subdivision potential runs off the parcel's R1/R2 zone minimum (Section 525), servicing is District water and sewer/drainage (2026 single-family utilities $2,571.20 combined), hazard development permits (below) and the port-authority foreshore check gate waterfront work. Confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the District for the specific parcel. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **Hazard Development Permits (District frame — the live Dollarton checks):** OCP Schedule B permit areas — natural-environment protection, streamside, wildfire, creek and slope — plus the shoreline record: Cates Park shoreline erosion worsening with sea level rise (port authority), District flood levels modelled to a 2100 horizon plus 0.6 m, and the 7.6 m Ocean Natural Boundary setback for waterfront principal buildings. Pull the GeoWeb flood construction level and get an insurance quote before subjects come off on a waterfront lot. https://www.portvancouver.com/permit/shoreline-adaptation-and-restoration-whey-ah-wichen-cates-park
- **Foreshore layer (federal):** Burrard Inlet waters and the intertidal area are port-authority-managed federal lands and waters — dock, seawall or foreshore work needs that check, not just a District permit. https://www.portvancouver.com/about
- **Strata block (for completeness — no Dollarton bare-land strata stock is verified):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Dollarton ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Dollarton?** No verified one — the shore is established fee-simple houses, and no bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How close to the water can a house sit?** The R1/R2 setback table sets a minimum setback to the Ocean Natural Boundary Line of 7.6 m for a principal building — the first measurement on any waterfront lot file. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Q: What shoreline risks affect a waterfront lot?** The port authority states the Cates Park shoreline is eroding and likely to worsen with sea level rise and climate change; District creek, slope and wildfire permit areas can also apply, and District flood levels are modelled to a 2100 horizon plus 0.6 m. https://www.portvancouver.com/permit/shoreline-adaptation-and-restoration-whey-ah-wichen-cates-park
- **Q: Who approves dock or seawall work?** Not the District alone — Burrard Inlet waters and the intertidal area are port-authority-managed federal lands and waters, so foreshore work needs the port-authority check as well. https://www.portvancouver.com/about
- **Q: How small can a lot be subdivided?** By zone: R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m width is the smallest minimum, standard R2 is 660 m² / 18 m / 34 m, and R1 and R2(S3) sit at 4,000 m² / 30 m. The parcel's zone is confirmed through District GeoWeb. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Q: Can a Dollarton lot hold a suite and a coach house?** Yes — since June 2024 three units total (principal, suite, coach house) are permitted on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary, and non-exempt lots over 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop can potentially allow up to 6 units under the SSMUH classification. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve my address?** No public school inside Dollarton is confirmed — the named schools beside the area are Cove Cliff Elementary and Seycove Secondary in Deep Cove and Seymour Heights Elementary to the north-west, and the catchment for any address must be run with SD44 before it is marketed. Do not market Dorothy Lynas French Immersion as the neighbourhood school; it is a choice program at 4000 Inlet Crescent. https://covecliff.sd44.ca/
- **Q: Where is shopping?** The District OCP's Maplewood Village Centre plans retail with apartments and live/work units above along Old Dollarton Road at the west end; a grocery store by name on Dollarton Highway is not confirmed on an official source. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What transit runs the shore?** Routes 211, 212 and 215 along Dollarton Highway to Phibbs Exchange, the R2 RapidBus one-seat to Metrotown since September 7, 2026, and some 211 trips through to Burrard Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt211.pdf
