## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Municipality: the City of Mission, a municipality in the Fraser Valley Regional District, on the north side of the Fraser River, bounded by the Fraser River to the south, the District of Maple Ridge to the west, and Fraser Valley Regional District electoral areas to the north and east. The City renamed itself from District of Mission to City of Mission, and its own site, bylaws and Official Community Plan all use "City of Mission." https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan and https://careers.fraserhealth.ca/pages/mission-community-profile
- Board sub-areas (Paragon/FVREB) that make up the city page: "Mission" (Paragon F83) and Mission West (Paragon F88) are the urban umbrella sub-areas, and the rural reporting sub-areas are Dewdney Deroche, Durieu, Hatzic, Hemlock, Lake Errock, Stave Falls and Steelhead. The City's Rural Mission Strategy covers the rural communities of the city — the same communities carried as rural sub-areas. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy
- Main roads and commute: Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) runs east–west through the city and crosses the Fraser River to Abbotsford on the Mission Bridge (Highway 11); Highway 11 also runs south from the bridge toward the Sumas border crossing, and Dewdney Trunk Road runs along the north side of the city toward Maple Ridge; published drive times were not confirmed. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/3/D/Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre---Mission
- Transit: Mission City Station, 33200 North Railway Ave, is the eastern terminus of the West Coast Express, with trains to Downtown Vancouver on weekday rush hours; BC Transit's Central Fraser Valley system serves Mission with local routes including Route 31 Mission to Abbotsford. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express and https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/
- Schools: SD75 Mission Public Schools, catchments address-based through the district's School Locator Tool. For the downtown sample address 7466 Welton St (the City Hall area), the locator gives École Mission Central Elementary (K–6), 7466 Welton St; Heritage Park Middle School (7–9), 33700 Bell Ave; and **Mission Senior Secondary** (10–12), 32939 7th Ave — the Ministry-listed name is Mission Senior Secondary, never "École Mission Senior Secondary." SD75 runs a middle tier (elementary K–6, middle 7–9, secondary 10–12), unlike SD42 next door; catchments for other addresses are not confirmed — all other catchments UNVERIFIED in this file. https://media.mpsd.ca/media/Default/medialib/catchment-areas-cross-boundary-schools-of-choice-and-programs-of-choice-policy.cfe7b51178.pdf and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075
- Civic anchors: Mission Leisure Centre, 7650 Grand Street; Mission Library, 33247 Second Avenue (Fraser Valley Regional Library); Mission Memorial Hospital, 7324 Hurd St; Mission Urgent and Primary Care Centre, 304–32555 London Ave, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m., at the main retail node on London Avenue at the Highway 7 / Highway 11 junction. http://www.mission.ca/leisure and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Mission/mission-memorial-hospital

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**This assembly contributes no land roster of its own — and, on this page's research, Mission has no named bare-land subdivision, no verified bare-land strata development, and no presale lot offering to roster. Stated plainly: the city's land trades as built fee-simple lots — urban house lots in the "Mission" core and large rural acreage parcels in the northern and eastern sub-areas — plus redevelopment parcels under the OCP designations. The one named presale in the research (Montgomery Condos) sells strata condo units, not lots. Any named land development belonging to a Mission sub-area will be documented once in that sub-area's own lots-and-land file when produced, and cross-referenced here — never double-documented as this assembly's stock.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form, in two characters

- **Urban lots (the "Mission" core / Mission West):** single-detached houses across the city's residential neighbourhoods under the OCP's Urban Residential and Suburban Residential designations, plus an Urban Residential Townhouse designation. Zoning is under City of Mission Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020; parcel-level minimum lot sizes are set out in the bylaw itself — no urban minimum lot size is stated in the research beyond the rural RU16 figure below, so none is asserted here (UNCONFIRMED lot-by-lot). https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan and https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning
- **Rural acreage parcels (Hatzic, Dewdney Deroche, Durieu, Hemlock, Lake Errock, Stave Falls, Steelhead):** rural acreage stock in the northern and eastern sub-areas under rural zones such as RU16, which carries a 1.6 ha minimum in City notices. The rural sub-areas are effectively all detached freehold — Hatzic, Dewdney Deroche, Durieu, Stave Falls and Steelhead recorded zero active attached listings in September 2026. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning and Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file above)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026:** the current Official Community Plan replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025 and keeps the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction, with a Regional Context Statement added, per the City's public hearing notice. Any "June 1, 2026" adoption date for this bylaw is WRONG — that date is flagged as a Jerry sweep item wherever a page states it; this file's own research does NOT document a page stating June 1, 2026, so no instance is asserted from this file (the flag is carried generally in the sibling townhomes assembly). https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 (adoption date April 21, 2026: program standing corrections, 2026-11-01)
- **Zoning + SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 governs; parcel-level minimum lot sizes are set out in the bylaw itself, and rural zones such as RU16 carry a 1.6 ha minimum in City notices. The City's SSMUH page states that zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted, and the City's planning pages set out how secondary suites and detached accessory units are handled by zone — what is allowed on a specific lot depends on its zone. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning and https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** no Mission subdivision/servicing bylaw number is confirmed on a primary source in this file's research — any such number is held UNCONFIRMED here and is not asserted; subdivision follows the Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 zone minimums plus City requirements confirmed with the City for the parcel. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/mission-homes-for-sale.md)
- **ALR rules (rural-edge ground where ALR applies):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Whether any single Mission parcel is in the ALR is checked on ALC mapping first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Bare-land strata rules (where a bare-land strata lot is ever bought — the BC strata block applies in full to the lot):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **City tax penalty — FLAT 10%, one regime inside the city:** City of Mission 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026. This is the flat 10% penalty on unpaid taxes after July 2 under the Community Charter ss.233–234 and the Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s. 3; Mission has NO alternative-scheme bylaw under Charter s. 235, so there is no second staged penalty date inside the city. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes (penalty structure: program standing corrections, 2026-11-01)
- **Do NOT import the rural regime — and do not export the city one:** the FVRD electoral areas around Mission are a DIFFERENT regime — under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11, rural taxes are due July 2 with 5% added to any portion unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on any portion unpaid October 31. Those 5% + 5% figures belong to FVRD electoral-area parcels only; Stave Falls and Steelhead parcels inside the City of Mission pay under the City's flat 10% regime above. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Foreclosure geography:** Mission has NO Supreme Court registry — the foreclosure mapping for Mission files is UNVERIFIED (proceedings are commenced at the registry in the municipality where the land is located if there is one there, otherwise at a registry in the judicial district, under the Law and Equity Act s. 21(2)). Court-ordered LAND sales in Mission follow the settled BC treatment in ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/langley-foreclosures-court-ordered-sales-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §3. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol2/consol2/96253_01
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Mission lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Mission-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Mission, and the position is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there vacant subdivision land to buy in Mission?** Not as a verified standing market on this research — no named bare-land subdivision and no presale lot offering was verified city-wide. What trades is built house lots, rural acreage parcels, and the occasional redevelopment parcel. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How small can a rural Mission lot be?** By zone — rural zones such as RU16 carry a 1.6 ha minimum in City notices, and parcel-level minimums are set out in Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 itself. Urban minimums are not stated in this file's research — read the bylaw zone for the parcel. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning
- **Q: Is Stave Falls taxed as rural FVRD land?** No — Stave Falls (and Steelhead) are inside the City of Mission, so City taxes apply: due July 2, 2026 with a flat 10% penalty on balances outstanding July 3, 2026. The rural 5% + 5% regime is for FVRD electoral-area parcels outside the city. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Q: Can I add a suite or extra units to a Mission house lot?** The City's SSMUH page states that zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial SSMUH legislation the City has adopted; secondary suites and detached accessory units are handled by zone — confirm the lot's zone under Bylaw 5949-2020. https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **Q: Is my rural parcel in the ALR?** ALR land sits mainly on the city's rural edges and status is parcel-specific on ALC mapping — check the parcel; if it is in the ALR, subdivision needs ALC approval and the residence caps in Section 3 bind. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca
- **Q: Does the floodplain or the dyke affect a Hatzic or south-edge lot?** The Fraser River floodplain runs along the south edge and Hatzic Prairie sits behind the Dewdney Dyke system; taxes also carry a diking levy. Confirm floodplain status, flood construction requirements and the levy for the specific parcel. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- **Q: Is the lot serviced, or on well and septic?** UNCONFIRMED at city level in this file — the research states no general servicing position. Confirm with the City for an urban lot, and by well/septic records for a rural parcel, before pricing servicing into an offer. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided Mission lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: When was Mission's current OCP adopted?** April 21, 2026 (Bylaw 6405-2026, replacing Bylaw 6350-2025). Any source stating June 1, 2026 is wrong. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026
- **Q: Which schools serve a rural Mission lot vs a downtown lot?** Catchments are address-based in SD75 and split across the city — only the downtown sample chain (7466 Welton St → École Mission Central Elementary → Heritage Park Middle School → Mission Senior Secondary) is settled in this file; every other address is UNVERIFIED until run in the SD75 locator. https://www.mpsd.ca
