No named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata and no presale LOT offering inside the narrow pocket is verified on any official or developer source. What trades is improved fee-simple residential lots — detached houses and, where the RD zones apply, duplex product — bought for the house or for a rebuild inside the zone and hazard rules in §3. - **Strata block — stated for completeness, governing no verified Cliff Drive land stock:** if a bare-land strata lot were ever the purchase (none is verified here), the BC block would apply to the LOT — Form B (Strata Property Act s. 59) within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page, with fees, CRF balance, levies, insurance summary, rules, budget and depreciation report; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; anything finer for a specific Cliff Drive parcel is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a bare building lot in Cliff Drive?** There is no lot product to buy — no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering is verified in the pocket. What trades is improved fee-simple houses and duplex-zone parcels under the RS/RD zones. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is Cliff Drive an official neighbourhood?** No. It is a Paragon sub-area name; the City plans this ground as part of Tsawwassen under OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) and publishes no boundary for the name — and whether the board's use of the name reaches Garden Place is an OPEN question flagged at the top of this file, resolved neither way. Describe a property by street, zone and address-based catchment. (Research file §1)
- **Q: A listing labels a Garden Place unit "Cliff Drive" — should I treat it as in this pocket?** Treat the label as UNCONFIRMED for this pocket: the condos sibling documents 1440 / 1441 Garden Place (NWS1841, built 1982) carrying the label in secondary sources while sitting outside the narrow 12 Avenue pocket. Locate the property by its street address, zone and catchment instead. (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/cliff-drive-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: How small can a new lot be?** By zone, as the research states it: RS7 at 335 m² is the smallest in-town standard, RS6 390 m², RS5 550 m² (460 m² outside the Single Family Residential designation), RS4 610 m², RD4 504 m², RD1 836 m², and RS1/RS2/RS3/RD2/RD3 at 4,000 m² — confirm the parcel's own zone on the City maps before assuming any split is possible. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **Q: Can I add units under SSMUH?** Delta's updates allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies (SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) — but on the pocket's western edge the SD2 hazard DPA conditions sit on top, and servicing and setback rules still apply parcel by parcel. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Is my lot in the SD2 hazard area?** UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel in this file — SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment) lies immediately west of the pocket, and DPA status is read off the OCP DPA maps for the exact parcel before subjects come off. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Cliff Drive lot?** SD37 catchments are address-based. The one recorded sample (1299 56 St, eastern edge) gives Cliff Drive Elementary (K–7), 5025 12 Ave, and South Delta Secondary (8–12), 750 53 St; any other address needs its own locator run — the sample does not bind the whole pocket. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Does GST apply to a vacant-lot purchase here?** At CRA GI-003 level only: an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt, while a business sale or a parcel subdivided into more than two parts can be taxable; the position for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and parcel history are established — have the contract state it. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What will PTT cost on a land purchase?** At the standard tiers (1% / 2% / 3%, plus the further 2% on the residential portion above $3M): $700K → $12,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000. Home exemptions are home exemptions — their application to a bare parcel is facts-specific: UNCONFIRMED for bare land in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: How do I read Cliff Drive's market?** There are no published sub-area statistics to read — SnapStats does not cover South Delta and publishes no land category. Price from the parcel's own comparables, its zone and its DPA status. (Research file §2)
