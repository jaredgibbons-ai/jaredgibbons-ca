## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, in effect July 1, 2026:** adopted June 9, 2026, it replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) outright — the 1965 bylaw is repealed, NOT current, and must never be cited as current for this area. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025:** replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; Burnaby is organized into 20 Community Plan Areas under the 2050 OCP. The Lochdale Urban Village Community Plan (adopted July 25, 2022) covers the urban village east of Parkcrest along Hastings / Lougheed. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **SSMUH, as the City states it:** Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing District in June 2024 — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. The 6-unit tier matters most on the Sperling and Kensington side, nearest the frequent-transit corridor and Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station — eligibility is still parcel-specific. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — base number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** Burnaby's servicing instrument is the Subdivision Control and Development Servicing Bylaw — the former Subdivision Control Bylaw as renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024. The BASE bylaw number is held UNCONFIRMED in this file on the program's direction: no base number is asserted. The process documented here runs off the zone (new parcels must conform to the Zoning Bylaw for the zone) and the City's servicing-improvements regime for larger residential projects; parcel-level works-and-services requirements are confirmed with the City's Engineering Department for the specific lot before a split is priced. (Program direction for batch L5; research file above.)
- **Undivided-interest diligence (Kensington Gardens, cross-reference only):** because Kensington Gardens is undivided interest ownership, there is no Form B, no strata depreciation report and no CRF to review — diligence runs on the ownership agreement and title, not the strata block. It is documented once in the Capitol Hill BN condos file and counted nowhere in this file. ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/capitol-hill-bn-condos-2026-11-01.md
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Parkcrest ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Parkcrest lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
