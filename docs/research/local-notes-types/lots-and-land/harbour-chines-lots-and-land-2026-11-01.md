## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996:** small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3, with the subdivision minimums in Section 2; s.519 flood protection setbacks and s.523 riparian areas also sit in this bylaw. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **OCP — Interim Official Community Plan, Bylaws 5511/5512, 2026, adopted April 27, 2026, superseding OCP No. 3479:** the City-wide plan applies; a Harbour Chines neighbourhood plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). Any Harbour Chines document still citing OCP No. 3479 as current is stale. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. Confirm zone and services for the parcel before pricing in added units. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Subdivision + servicing — Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 3558, 2003 (VERIFIED):** subdivision minimums run off the zone (Section 2), and servicing runs under Bylaw No. 3558, 2003 — the consolidated bylaw requires the owner to grant or acquire statutory rights-of-way for the works and services needed to serve a subdivision or development, in a form acceptable to the City's solicitor, and to pay for traffic-control and street-name signage installed by the City at the owner's expense before subdivision approval or building permit issue. Parcel-level requirements are confirmed with the City. https://publicdocs.coquitlam.ca/coquitlamdoc/getdocIF.asp?doc=6328713
- **Watercourse Development Permit:** within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam a Watercourse Protection Development Permit can be required. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Bare-land strata block (for the form — no Harbour Chines bare-land strata plan is verified):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Harbour Chines ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31). No page stating a flat farm-class figure is documented in this slug's research, so no such error is asserted here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
