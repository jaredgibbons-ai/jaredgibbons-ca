## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan:** adopted December 9, 2025; it replaces the 1998 OCP Bylaw, and its land use map takes precedence over older neighbourhood community plan maps where they conflict. Review the 2050 OCP land use map before assuming density or subdivision potential on a large lot. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815:** adopted June 9, 2026 and in effect July 1, 2026 (never the former 1965 No. 4742). Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024 — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing — HELD UNCONFIRMED:** Burnaby's servicing bylaw base number is UNCONFIRMED (an Amendment 14667, 2024 is on record, but the base number is not adopted here). The subdivision/servicing process is therefore documented instead of numbered: subdivision and servicing requirements are confirmed with the City for the parcel, and no bylaw number for Burnaby servicing is asserted in this file. (Standing ledger position.)
- **Heritage precinct:** properties on Sperling Avenue fronting Deer Lake Park sit in the Deer Lake Park Heritage Precinct with heritage-register listings — check the register and precinct requirements before planning renovations or a rebuild on that frontage. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?objectType_facet=Landmark%20Description%20&objectType_facet=History%20Links&ps=50&q=&sort=title_sort%20asc&creator_facet=johnreid000000%7CJohn%20Reid&creator_facet=bernardrhill000000%7CBernard%20R.%20Hill&place_facet=sperlingavenue000000%7CSperling%20Avenue&p=1
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Sperling-Duthie ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no flat line is asserted for this area. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500,000 → $8,000; $800,000 → $14,000 ($6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer at that cap); $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Burnaby property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does NOT apply in Burnaby.
