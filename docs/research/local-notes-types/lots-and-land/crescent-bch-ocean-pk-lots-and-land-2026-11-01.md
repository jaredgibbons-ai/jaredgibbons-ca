## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP / Zoning — Surrey:** Surrey Official Community Plan Bylaw 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) is current — never cite 18020 as current. Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000 governs. The Crescent Beach Local Area Plan guides Crescent Beach parcels within that frame. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **SSMUH — Surrey (City position as stated):** maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the City adopted the SSMUH zoning changes on July 8, 2024. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Suites — Surrey:** a secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area under Zoning Bylaw 12000. A "90 m²" figure is NOT the current Surrey cap and is not used here. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Short-term rentals — Surrey:** licence $358/yr, with the City's $350 proposal conflict disclosed — both figures are carried, neither is silently chosen. (Standing correction for Surrey in this program.)
- **Subdivision and servicing — Surrey:** servicing is determined under Surrey Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986), verified on City pages. Subdivision itself requires Approving Officer approval under the Land Title Act. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans
- **Property taxes — Surrey:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **ALR block (where ALR ground exists — parcel status here UNCONFIRMED):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, if a parcel is one):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,500,000 lot purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,500,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FTHB PTT exemption:** first $500,000 exempt, full at value ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000), partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption is a new-HOME exemption (full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Foreclosure / court sales (where met):** a settled court process exists (BC Supreme Court Civil Rules 21-7 / 13-5, Practice Direction PD-66, Form 60); whether Form B and the PTT/GST treatment on a court-ordered sale of a lot here differ is UNCONFIRMED. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there actually vacant land for sale in Crescent Beach / Ocean Park?** Yes, in the feed: two of the five cards read in the research were Land listings. No subdivision name or lot count verifies on an official source, so treat the cards as feed evidence of a land thread, not a verified subdivision roster. https://jaredgibbons.ca/crescent-bch-ocean-pk.html
- **Q: Which plan governs a Crescent Beach parcel?** The Crescent Beach Local Area Plan, inside Surrey OCP Bylaw 21900 and Zoning Bylaw 12000 — the parcel's plan designation drives what can be built. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put six homes on a lot here?** Only on an eligible lot of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; otherwise the Surrey frame is 3 units under 280 m² and 4 units over 280 m². https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: How big can a secondary suite be?** Up to 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area under Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000 — not 90 m². https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Q: Is flooding a lot-buying issue in Crescent Beach?** Check the specific parcel: the page, citing the City, calls Crescent Beach low-lying at the mouth of the Nicomekl River and reports about 70% of properties now have a storm sewer fronting or flanking them — which leaves a remainder. Parcel flood status is UNVERIFIED here. https://jaredgibbons.ca/crescent-bch-ocean-pk.html
- **Q: Are Ocean Park Village, Ocean Park Grove, The Courtyards, Bentley Wynd or the 13585 16 Ave development land I can buy?** No — all five are townhouse-form candidates documented in the QUEUE in §2, counted nowhere as land; they are built-form complexes, not bare-land offerings.
- **Q: How much PTT on a $1.5M lot here?** $28,000 — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) plus 2% on the next $1,300,000 ($26,000). (§3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a vacant lot?** Generally yes if the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided; generally no for an individual's personal-use resale, per CRA GI-003 — and the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
