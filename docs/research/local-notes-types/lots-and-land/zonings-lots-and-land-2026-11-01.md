## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does this page list lots I can buy?** No — it is a zoning directory with an address-check tool, an ALR section and a buyer checklist. It carries no land roster of its own (§2); lots are listed in area files and on the MLS, and this page tells you what a lot's zone allows. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html
- **Q: My Surrey listing says RF-12 — what zone is the lot in now?** R4 Small Lot Residential: Surrey's R1–R7 zones replaced RF/RH/RQ on July 8, 2024 (RF-12/RF-12C → R4; RF-SD → R6), and the directory cards show the former code so old text can be translated. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Q: How big can a secondary suite be on a lot?** It depends on the regime, and the regimes must not be mixed: Surrey 40% of the principal unit's habitable area; ALR — a suite only inside the principal residence; Abbotsford's A1 card states up to 100 m² as the page summarizes that city's bylaw. (§3)
- **Q: What homes does the ALR allow on a farm parcel?** One principal residence up to 500 m² (fill ≤1,000 m²), a suite only inside it, and one additional detached residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha) without an ALC application — municipal zoning can limit further, and ALR land cannot be stratified (Bulletin 09 s.6). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I subdivide a rural lot because its zone shows a minimum lot size?** Never on the minimum alone: confirm subdivision approval and servicing with the municipality (ledger in §3), and on ALR land subdivision needs ALC approval, including parcels under 2 acres. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html
- **Q: What do I check before buying acreage, in order?** The page's own six checks: ALR status (ALC map, then confirm with the municipality); the zone and its minimum lot size; how many homes are allowed (zoning and ALC rules both bite); septic records and inspection; water source (well yield/quality/licence vs. municipal); and farm class with BC Assessment, plus Premises ID if livestock or poultry are planned. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html
- **Q: Does farm class really save that much tax, and what are the thresholds?** Farm class is a BC Assessment classification with three tiers: $2,500 gross annual farm income at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by Oct 31. A flat threshold figure is an error. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Can I rely on a zone card instead of checking with the municipality?** No — the page was checked Oct 2, 2026 per its header, bylaws amend constantly (the Township's SR-3B card records a July 27, 2026 addition), card-level detail beyond the research's consistency checks is UNVERIFIED, and one mid-page segment could not even be re-read for the research (429, stopped). Confirm zone, uses and ALR status per parcel. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a vacant lot, and does the first-time-buyer GST rebate cover land?** At the GI-003 level: developer/business and newly subdivided lots generally taxable, an individual's personal-use land generally exempt. The FTHB GST rebate applies to new HOUSING, not bare land. (§3)
- **Q: How much PTT on a $700K lot in any directory city?** $12,000 at the general tiers with no exemption (§3) — the tiers are provincial, so the math is the same in Abbotsford, Langley, Surrey, Delta and Mission; the zoning answer for the same lot is not. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
