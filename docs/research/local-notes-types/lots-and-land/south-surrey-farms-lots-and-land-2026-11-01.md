## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **City plans:** Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current; Bylaw 18020 is REPLACED, never cite as current. Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Agricultural zoning — UNVERIFIED as verified rules in this slug's research:** the live page states as site copy only that South Surrey farm land falls mainly under A-1 General Agriculture and A-2 Intensive Agriculture, with a 2,000 m² farm residential footprint, an additional 1,000 m² for an additional dwelling on a farm lot of 4 ha or more, a footprint up to 60 m deep, new lots of at least 4 ha inside the ALR and 2 ha outside it in A-1, and an additional farm house in A-1/A-2 where the operation is at least 8 ha on contiguous lots, in the ALR and classed as farm. No official City bylaw source was opened in the research pass to confirm any of those statements (search results were non-official mirrors, not used) — verify with the City for the specific lot before relying on them. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-farms.html
- **SSMUH (Surrey, Jul 8, 2024):** 3 units (≤280 m²) / 4 units (larger) / up to 6 near frequent bus stops, on lots under 4,050 m² inside the urban containment boundary — an urban-lot frame; ALR farm parcels answer first to the ALR block below. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Suites (Surrey):** one suite per dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never a separate strata lot. A "90 m²" cap is NOT the current Surrey suite rule. On ALR land the ALC suite rule (suite only within the principal residence) also applies — both frames must be met. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision + servicing (Surrey):** Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830, verified on City pages. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement In the ALR, subdivision additionally needs ALC approval (below).
- **Streamside / flood:** streamside setbacks in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000, varying by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated. Flood position / Flood Construction Level for lowland (Serpentine, Nicomekl, Mud Bay) parcels is set lot by lot per the live page — check City mapping per parcel: UNVERIFIED at parcel level. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf and https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-farms.html
- **STRs (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only, owner must live at the property as primary residence; licence $358/yr carried with the City's $350/yr proposal disclosed as a CONFLICT — check the City licence page before quoting. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **ALR block, in full:** one principal residence without an ALC application at ≤500 m² total floor area (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite only within the principal residence; at most one additional residence of ≤90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or ≤186 m² (>40 ha); local bylaws may be more restrictive, never more permissive. Subdivision in the ALR needs ALC approval, and depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6); ALR residences cannot be stratified (Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class — three-tier, in full (documented in this file's own research):** $2,500 where the operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha where the operation is over 4 ha; $10,000 where the operation is under 0.8 ha; applications to BC Assessment by Oct 31 for the next assessment/tax year, and a buyer continuing to farm must file the General Application for Farm Classification by Oct 31 after purchase. Farm classification is a voluntary program. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **BC strata block (any bare-land strata lot):** Form B (within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; min 10% CRF since Nov 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT:** general tiers 1% / 2% to $2M / 3% above, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M.
