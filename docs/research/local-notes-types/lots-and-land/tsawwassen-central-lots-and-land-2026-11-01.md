What will be built and when: UNCONFIRMED — get the current file status. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/
- **Q: How small a lot can I create in the centre?** By zone: RS7 335 m² smallest, RS6 390 m², RS5 550/460 m², RS4 610 m²; duplex RD4 504 m² / RD1 836 m² — plus the Urban Centre DPA design layer and the City subdivision process. The parcel's zone governs. (§3 above)

- **Q: Do I pay GST on a centre lot?** CRA GI-003 level: an individual's personal-use vacant-land sale is generally exempt; business/developer and more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable. - **Q: Are the six condo buildings a cheaper way into Central land value?** They are apartment strata — you buy a strata lot in a building, counted in the condos file only. Windsor Woods alone is 270 units on plan LMS1318. Land exposure there is the strata corporation's, shared — not a lot purchase. (§2 above)
