## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — District of West Vancouver Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010:** houses sit in the RS single-family dwelling zones (Section 200 minimums in Section 2 above). The White Rock pair (Zoning 2506 / OCP 2220) belongs to White Rock ONLY and never applies here. Confirm the parcel's zone on the District's Zoning Map. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **OCP — District of West Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018:** the District-wide plan covering Chartwell; a Chartwell-specific local area plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/major-projects/official-community-plan-review
- **SSMUH / added units, as the Province states it:** provincial SSMUH legislation requires zoning to allow a minimum of 4 dwelling units on single-family and duplex parcels greater than 280 m², and a minimum of 6 units on such parcels greater than 280 m² near frequent bus service (a route stopping at least every 15 minutes on average). West Vancouver's local implementing-bylaw status: UNVERIFIED — no District source confirming the adoption state was read. Do not price SSMUH density into a Chartwell lot on this file's evidence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/local-governments-and-housing/housing-initiatives/smale-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Subdivision + servicing — District servicing-bylaw number held UNCONFIRMED:** no District of West Vancouver subdivision/servicing bylaw number is confirmed on a primary source in this file's research, so none is quoted; the process is documented instead: subdivision potential runs off the parcel's RS zone minimum site area (Section 2), servicing is District metered water and sewer billed quarterly, and hazard development permits (below) gate work on hillside and creek lots. Confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the District for the specific parcel. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Hazard development permits (District frame):** an Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank; a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit is required for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface; a Foreshore Development Permit applies within the Foreshore Development Permit Area (waterfront / coastal-flood-risk property), which does not ordinarily reach this upper hillside. Both the wildfire and environmental frames can apply on Chartwell's hillside and creek lots. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Building scheme / covenants — title diligence:** whether a Chartwell lot carries British Properties building-scheme or covenant restrictions beyond District zoning is UNVERIFIED in this pass — no covenant term is stated here; pull the individual title before marketing a rebuild or pricing subdivision potential. (Research file above.)
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Chartwell ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the land's value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Additional school tax (live at British Properties price points):** 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements March 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Chartwell lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Chartwell-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this area's land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Chartwell, and the position is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot or new subdivision I can buy into in Chartwell?** No verified one — Chartwell is an established British Properties enclave; land trades as built fee-simple house lots, and no bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata plan or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Chartwell lot?** Only if the parcel's RS zone minimum allows it: minimum site areas run from 488 m² (RS5) to 8,094 m² (RS1), with RS6 on the site of record — and title covenants, which this file does not confirm, can restrict further. Confirm the zone on the District Zoning Map and pull title first. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Q: Is "Chartwell" an official neighbourhood?** Not as a District planning name — it is NOT CONFIRMED in the District's OCP. The name's confirmed official anchor is Chartwell Elementary at 1300 Chartwell Drive; District and legal work runs on "British Properties" and the street address. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/chartwell/
- **Q: Are both schools actually in the area, and will my address feed them?** Both schools sit on Chartwell Drive inside the area — Chartwell Elementary (1300) and Sentinel Secondary (1250) — and SD45 links Chartwell's Grade 6/7 Pre-AP Capstone to Sentinel's Advanced Placement opportunities; catchments are still address-based and UNVERIFIED per address here, so run the exact address in the SD45 locator before promising either school. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/
- **Q: Can the lot hold a suite or a coach house?** Secondary suites and a detached secondary suite are among the permitted uses in the District's single-family zones; provincial SSMUH minima (4 units over 280 m²; 6 near frequent bus service) are stated by the Province, but West Vancouver's implementing-bylaw status is UNVERIFIED — confirm parcel eligibility with the District. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/local-governments-and-housing/housing-initiatives/smale-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: What permits does a hillside rebuild need beyond a building permit?** Potentially a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit (work within 100 m of an undisturbed forest interface) and an Environmental Development Permit (work within 15 m of a watercourse bank) — check both before writing. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Q: Is the lot serviced?** District water and sewer are metered and billed quarterly, and Chartwell's established streets sit inside that system on the District's wording; street-by-street septic/well status is UNVERIFIED — confirm the parcel with the District. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a lot from a developer or a newly subdivided lot?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What extra tax applies at British Properties values?** The Province's additional school tax: 0.2% on the residential portion between $3M and $4M and 0.4% above $4M for 2026, rising January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
