## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP — Surrey:** Surrey Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) is the current OCP — Bylaw 18020 is never the current OCP. Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs zoning. The parcel's own zone and designation govern any subdivision or added-unit question for the complex ground; neither is stated for 15988 32 Avenue in the research: UNCONFIRMED per parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans
- **SSMUH — Surrey:** the City adopted its SSMUH zoning changes on July 8, 2024 — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Added SSMUH units are not subdivision and do not create new lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites — Surrey:** a secondary suite's habitable floor area must not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex building, not a separate strata lot; only one suite per dwelling unit. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision and servicing — Surrey:** servicing is determined under the City of Surrey subdivision/servicing bylaw No. 8830 (1986), verified on City pages; subdivision itself runs through the Approving Officer under the Land Title Act. No subdivision of the Blu Living strata ground is documented or proposed in the research. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development
- **Strata block (the complex's strata lots):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (the live page's FAQ states the same provincial frame for long-term rentals, with short-term rentals still bylaw- and licence-dependent); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://jaredgibbons.ca/blu-living-townhomes-for-sale.html
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on an $800,000 purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$800,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000** (a first-time buyer fully within the FTHB exemption pays $6,000 on the same price under the program anchor). Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. At $3,500,000 the full tier math including the further 2% is $93,000; a townhomes sibling in this program carries $78,000 for $3,500,000 — that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item, flagged here and never silently carried. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. No vacant-land sale is documented for this built complex. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
