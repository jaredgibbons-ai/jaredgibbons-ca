## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **No parcel rules live on the homepage.** Zoning, OCP, SSMUH, subdivision/servicing, ALR and strata rules follow the parcel's municipality and sub-area, and are carried in full in the sub-area land files — they are not restated per-city here, and no city rule is imported into this topic file. - **Regulator frame behind the practice the page advertises (BCFSA, as the research cites it):** in BC a licensed real estate professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party, and only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Listing-data notice the page carries (page content):** the data comes in part from the MLS® Reciprocity program of GVR, FVREB or CADREB; listings held by participating firms are marked with the MLS® logo and include the listing agent's name; the representation is based on board-generated data the boards do not warrant for accuracy; materials may not be reproduced without the boards' express written consent. https://jaredgibbons.ca/
- **PTT — worked example (program anchor, for a buyer pricing a lot from the homepage; inputs + tier math):** on a $1,000,000 purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,000,000 ($16,000) = **$18,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. The homepage itself states no PTT figure; this anchor is program reference, not a page statement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only (program reference, not a page statement):** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate (program reference, not a page statement):** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
