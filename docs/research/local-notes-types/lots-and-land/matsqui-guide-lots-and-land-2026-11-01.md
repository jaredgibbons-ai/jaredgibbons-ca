## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**The land in Matsqui Prairie is farm land: fee-simple agricultural parcels on a dyked river prairie, in the A1 Agricultural Zone (subdivision minimum 8.0 ha) and the RR Rural Residential Zone (minimum lot size 4.0 ha). Strata is a structural zero here — no strata development is a documented housing form in this sub-area, there is no bare-land strata, and ALR residences cannot be stratified in any event. No named bare-land subdivision and no presale lot offering exists in the research record. Verified named land developments counted here: 0.**

- **Fee-simple farm parcels are the land form — and nearly the whole market.** The research file's housing profile is the land profile: "the housing stock is freehold farm and rural-residential land; strata development is not a documented housing form in this rural sub-area," with single-detached houses on agricultural and rural parcels as the dominant dwelling type. A Matsqui purchase is a fee-simple title purchase: no strata corporation, no Form B, no strata fee — Zoning Bylaw 2400-2014, ALR rules where the parcel is in the reserve, the dyking levy and any registered covenants govern. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2014%20Matsqui%20Prairie%20Demographic%20Profile_spreads.pdf (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/matsqui-guide.md, §5)
- **Lot character, as the research states it — set by the rural zones, not by a subdivision pattern:** in the A1 Agricultural Zone (Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, s.210), parcels created through subdivision must be a minimum of 8.0 ha; in the RR Rural Residential Zone (s.310), intended for single detached dwellings and agricultural uses on large lots in a rural setting, the minimum lot size is 4.0 ha. Those two numbers are the prairie's lot character: most prairie parcels cannot be split further at all (the research file's own buying guidance). No smaller "typical lot" figure is documented, and none is invented here. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400310
- **Bare-land strata: structural zero, stated plainly.** No bare-land strata — and no strata of any kind — was verified in Matsqui Prairie in the research file or in the condos, 55-plus or townhomes siblings (all three record the same zero for their product types). The bar is structural, not incidental: ALR residences cannot be stratified (Section 3), and the A1/RR minimums leave no lot pattern for a bare-land strata to form on. A bare-land strata lot, where the form lawfully exists elsewhere in Abbotsford (Downes Road Estates, filed under Abbotsford West), is strata tenure — Form B, strata fees and bylaws apply to the lot. Any strata claim for a Matsqui address is UNCONFIRMED until a strata plan is produced. (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/matsqui-guide-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §2; ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/abbotsford-west-guide-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **The dyked prairie — the land's defining physical fact.** The City states that the Matsqui Dykes are designed to prevent water in the Fraser River from entering into Matsqui Prairie, and Matsqui Trail Regional Park runs along the Fraser River dyke. Properties in the Matsqui Prairie Dyking District pay a separate dyking district levy on top of general municipal rates (Section 3). Design protection is not a parcel-level guarantee: the flood status of a specific parcel is a City confirmation, not an assumption. http://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/2022-spring-freshet-update-2
- **Servicing — wells and onsite sewage:** rural properties on the prairie rely on wells and onsite sewage systems; wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database, and Fraser Health requires an authorized person to file a record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair of an onsite system and a letter of certification after construction. Servicing for any individual property: UNVERIFIED — read the GWELLS record and the sewerage filings for the parcel before pricing it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons
- **ALR frame:** the prairie's agricultural policy frame is the Agricultural Land Reserve as administered through the City's AgRefresh review, which led to OCP Amendment Bylaw 3247-2022 and Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 3246-2022. The research file documents the A1/RR zoning and the ALC housing rules as the prairie's governing set; a parcel-by-parcel ALR determination for a specific Matsqui property is UNCONFIRMED in the research file until the ALC map and title are read for that parcel — but a buyer should underwrite every prairie parcel as ALR-framed until that check says otherwise, because the A1 zone and the ALC caps already assume the farm frame. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city_hall/plan_for_200K/plan_for_200K_-_agrefresh.htm and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Named subdivisions / bare-land developments: none verified.** No presale or new-construction project in Matsqui Prairie was verified on a City development page or a developer site (research file §4: UNVERIFIED). Matsqui Village itself is a historic rural centre recognized in the OCP's rural-centres policy — a village of existing parcels, not a lot development. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/abbotsforward
- **Presale / pre-construction LOT offerings: none — counted separately, and the count is 0.** (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/matsqui-guide.md, §4)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, rural sections.** A1 Agricultural Zone (s.210): Agricultural Use and Single Detached Dwelling are principal uses; parcels created through subdivision must be a minimum of 8.0 ha; one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit is permitted per lot only in accordance with the ALR Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the Agricultural Land Commission, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, with a maximum floor area of 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha; one secondary suite per lot is permitted, to a maximum floor area of 100 m². RR Rural Residential Zone (s.310): minimum lot size 4.0 ha; a coach house is permitted only on a lot greater than 0.4 ha, and a coach house or secondary suite is limited to one per lot and a maximum floor area of 90 m² or 40% of the net floor area of the single detached dwelling, whichever is less. None of these added dwellings creates a separate title for sale. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400310
- **OCP — with the Matsqui divergence flagged, not resolved:** Abbotsford's CURRENT Official Community Plan is Abbotsforward 2050, adopted December 2025, whose rural-centres policy recognizes Matsqui Village among Abbotsford's seven historical rural centres; that standing correction is applied in this file. The research file's citation of the plan as "Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated" is the stale/conflicted form under that correction, and any live-page "2500-2016" citation is likewise stale — both flagged for Jerry. Noted without resolution, as the sibling programs leave it: this area's files carry a documented divergence on the 2600-2016 citation (the guide/townhomes line treats it as stale; other program research states it as updated) — this file flags the divergence and resolves it nowhere. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/abbotsforward and https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **ALR rules — the prairie's governing frame:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, stated as the ALC states it. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — the statutory reason the strata count in Section 2 is structural, not a market gap. ALC housing rules as the research file states them: generally no more than one residence per parcel unless the owner qualifies for an additional residence; the principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less; a secondary suite is permitted in the ALR only within the principal residence; an additional detached residence may be up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR; no such line is asserted in this file, and none is documented in this area's research file. On the prairie, farm class is a core holding-cost term: it turns on produced and reported farm income, not on the A1 zone label alone. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **The dyking levy — Matsqui's local tax term:** Abbotsford property tax is the BC Assessment value multiplied by a rate per $1,000 of net assessed value set under the Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026, and the City's 2026 Tax Rates Summary lists a separate Matsqui Prairie Dyking District levy charged on properties in the district, on top of the general municipal rates. Check the parcel's tax notice for the line item — two otherwise identical prairie parcels can carry different bills if only one sits in the district. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Summary%20May%2022.pdf
- **SSMUH — not a prairie land lever:** June 2024 Council adoption of Abbotsford's SSMUH amendments is City-confirmed; the SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED ("3560-2024" must not be asserted). SSMUH is a ground-oriented urban form: it does not create lot zoning on A1/RR prairie land, does not override the 8.0 ha / 4.0 ha minimums, and does not touch the ALR stratification bar. Abbotsford's general suite cap (under 50% of the principal dwelling) and coach/garden cap (15% of lot area to 100 m² maximum — Abbotsford's rules ONLY) sit behind the zone-specific A1 suite (100 m²) and RR coach-house/suite (lesser of 90 m² or 40%) rules stated above, and behind the ALC rules on ALR parcels. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/matsqui-guide-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §3)
- **Subdivision and servicing requirements:** subdivision means the Approving Officer route against the A1 (8.0 ha) / RR (4.0 ha) minimums — which most prairie parcels fail on size alone — plus ALC approval for ALR parcels, plus the City's works-and-services (Development Bylaw) requirements. The Development Bylaw's current number/year and detailed servicing schedule were NOT confirmed on a City page read for this file: UNCONFIRMED. Servicing reality on the prairie is wells and onsite sewage (Section 2), not municipal connections. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Flood diligence:** the Matsqui Dykes are designed to keep Fraser River water out of the prairie — a design statement by the City, not a guarantee for a specific parcel. Ask about flood history directly, confirm the parcel's flood status with the City, and read the dyking levy as the ongoing price of the protection system. http://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/2022-spring-freshet-update-2
- **If a strata purchase is made elsewhere in Abbotsford (redirect case):** the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). None of it applies to a Matsqui fee-simple parcel purchase itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. The home-based exemptions (First-Time Home Buyers' Program — including its vacant-lot build route at $835,000 / $860,000 with build-and-occupy by the first anniversary — and the Newly Built Home Exemption refund route at $1,100,000 / $1,150,000) are home routes, not farm-parcel relief: at prairie parcel values and under ALC housing caps they will rarely drive a Matsqui price, and land held as farmland gets neither. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/ and https://wowa.ca/calculators/bc-property-transfer-tax
- **GST on farm and vacant land — stated only at the level the CRA sources support:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland is the caution that governs here: the CRA treats sales of farmland by individuals in a separate info sheet as usually taxable, with limited exemptions (including certain transfers to a related person acquiring for personal use). A Matsqui farm purchase must never be assumed GST-exempt on the vacant-land rule alone — the GST treatment of any specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status, farm use and the applicable CRA sheet are applied, and the contract must state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax:** the Province's taxable-areas list names the City of Abbotsford — owners of residential-class property in Abbotsford must complete the annual declaration. Prairie farm parcels sit in the agricultural assessment world, and the tax's application to any specific Matsqui parcel (its class, its exemptions) is UNCONFIRMED until the parcel's assessment class and the current exemption list are read. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal prohibition on purchase by non-Canadians:** the March 27, 2023 regulatory amendments repealed the provision that had made vacant land zoned residential or mixed-use inside a census metropolitan area or census agglomeration "residential property" — as amended, vacant land itself can be purchased by a non-Canadian, including for residential development, per the law-firm summaries; dwellings remain governed by the prohibition and specific application is UNCONFIRMED at the official-text level in this file. https://www.mross.com/what-we-think/article/update-canadian-government-prohibits-non-canadians-from-purchasing-residential-real-estate
- **First-home programs — the land boundary, stated precisely:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; the GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. On the prairie either rebate matters only through a qualifying new home — which on ALR land must first fit the ALC caps. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property taxes / holding cost:** due July 2, 2026 by midnight, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026, each including any unclaimed Home Owner Grant — plus the Matsqui Prairie Dyking District levy where the parcel is in the district (above), and farm class as the earned reducer. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Market context, labelled as community-level only:** Abbotsford IS a SnapStats-covered community; Matsqui's September 2026 row (6 detached listings, 1 sale, 17% ratio; attached 0/0) and the Abbotsford-wide detached median ($949,000) are housing figures. SnapStats has NO land category and no farm-parcel measure — a prairie parcel is priced from its soil, dyke position, ALR status, farm-class position and housing caps. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file above).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What land can I actually buy on Matsqui Prairie?** A whole fee-simple farm parcel (A1 zone) or a large rural-residential parcel (RR zone). There is no lot subdivision, no bare-land strata, no strata of any kind and no presale lot product — the strata zero is structural (ALR residences cannot be stratified, and the subdivision minimums are 8.0 ha and 4.0 ha). (Section 2 above.)
- **Q: Can I split a prairie parcel for my kids or to sell a lot?** Only if the math survives: A1 parcels created by subdivision must be at least 8.0 ha and RR lots at least 4.0 ha, and subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval on top. Most prairie parcels cannot be split further — that is the research file's own buying guidance, and it should be run before any family-transfer plan is priced. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400310
- **Q: What second dwelling can go on an A1 lot?** One Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot, only via the ALR Use Regulation (B.C. Reg. 30/2019) or ALC approval, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, capped at 90 m² (lots of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha) — plus one secondary suite per lot to 100 m² under the zone, subject on ALR land to the ALC rule that a suite sits only within the principal residence. In the RR zone: one coach house or suite on a lot over 0.4 ha, at the lesser of 90 m² or 40% of the main house. None of these creates a separate saleable title. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Q: What are the ALC residence caps, in one place?** Principal residence: 500 m² or less, generally one per parcel. Suite: only within the principal residence. Additional detached residence: 90 m² maximum at 40 ha or less, 186 m² over 40 ha, and only where the owner qualifies for an additional residence. Residences cannot be stratified. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is the parcel serviced?** Prairie servicing is wells and onsite sewage. Wells are recorded in GWELLS; Fraser Health requires an authorized person's record of sewerage system before work on an onsite system and a letter of certification after. Pull both for the specific parcel — servicing for any individual property is UNVERIFIED in the research record until those filings are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners
- **Q: What does the dyking levy mean for my tax bill?** Properties in the Matsqui Prairie Dyking District pay a separate levy on top of general municipal rates (Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026; 2026 Tax Rates Summary). It is a permanent holding-cost line for district parcels — confirm whether the parcel is in the district and read the line on its tax notice before comparing two prairie parcels on price alone. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Summary%20May%2022.pdf
- **Q: How serious is flood risk behind the dyke?** The City states the Matsqui Dykes are designed to prevent Fraser River water entering the prairie — design protection, maintained and levied for, but not a parcel-level guarantee. Ask about the parcel's flood history directly and confirm its flood status with the City; do not let "behind the dyke" end the inquiry. http://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/2022-spring-freshet-update-2
- **Q: What farm income keeps farm class on a prairie parcel?** The three tiers: $2,500 on a 0.8–4 ha operation; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31 (B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5). A flat "$2,500+" figure is an error for both small and large operations. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Q: Do I pay GST when I buy a Matsqui farm?** Do not assume the vacant-land exemption carries a farm: the CRA's vacant-land sheet (GI-003) exempts most individual personal-use sales, but its farmland guidance treats farmland sales as usually taxable with limited exemptions. The answer for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and farm use are applied — and the contract must allocate GST expressly. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a prairie parcel?** Mathxwí Elementary is in the village (33661 Elizabeth Avenue, formerly Matsqui Elementary). The settled locator chain for that address is Mathxwí Elementary → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary (SD34 School Locator, mybaragar.com, run 2026-11-01). Catchments are address-based — any other prairie address needs its own locator run. https://mathxwi.abbyschools.ca/ and https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments
