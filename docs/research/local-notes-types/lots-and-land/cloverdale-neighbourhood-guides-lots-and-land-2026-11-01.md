## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Surrey 2050, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current. NEVER cite the replaced Bylaw No. 18020 as current.** The Cloverdale Town Centre Plan update in §1 sits under it; confirm a parcel's designation in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs** across all three guide areas. Streamside/riparian setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is stated in this file, and the Serpentine guide's floodplain subject matter makes that per-stream-class check a parcel-level step, not a hub-level number. (Brief frame for this workstream; research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/cloverdale-neighbourhood-guides.md)
- **SSMUH / suites (Surrey-wide, applies in all three areas):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Secondary suites: one per dwelling unit, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area (the Surrey suite rule carried in the Cloverdale research set). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision + servicing: Subdivision and Development Bylaw 1986 No. 8830** — the servicing bylaw for Surrey subdivision, verified on the City's own pages in an earlier batch of this program (sourcing stated here as the brief directs; no rate or parcel figure is taken from it). Lot-specific servicing costs, DCC amounts and timelines behind any guide area: UNCONFIRMED — confirm per parcel with the City. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **ALR block (the Serpentine guide's ground, parcel by parcel, only where the parcel is in the reserve):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for any Serpentine parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (applies wherever a bare-land strata lot trades — none documented behind this hub, §2):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** a $900,000 parcel, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $700,000 ($14,000) = **$16,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Short-term rentals (house-on-lot holding strategies only):** owner-only hosting, owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, under 90 consecutive days per patron, business licence $358 per year — **DISCLOSED CONFLICT:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350 per year plus fees; the $358 figure on the City's STR page is the one carried here. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/cloverdale-bc-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **City property taxes:** Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
