- **SSMUH / added units as the City states it:** the City amended its Zoning Bylaw to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on RS1 and RS2 lots by Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 — RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Added-unit permission is not permission to subdivide. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/langleycity.md)
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 3300):** a suite must not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the gross floor area of the building (s.1.3 / s.2.4(c)); the registered owner must reside on the lot; at least one off-street parking space is required in addition to the two normally required for a single detached use; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. Garden suites and carriage homes (s.2.4(d)) are permitted in the R1 zone, with a maximum gross ground floor area of 100 m², a maximum total floor area of accessory buildings on an R1 lot of 60 m², and the registered owner residing on the lot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/langleycity.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Langley City the same as the Township of Langley for land rules?** No — the City is a separate municipality with its own OCP (adopted November 22, 2021) and its own Zoning Bylaw, 2025, No. 3300; the Township has its own bylaws, and the two must never be mixed. A Township rule applied to a City lot is an error. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Langley City from this page?** This page has no listings at all — it is an OCP/zoning page that links out for inventory. And no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata plan or presale lot offering was verified for City ground in this research. (§1, §2 above)
- **Q: How many units can a Langley City single-family lot hold?** Under Bylaw No. 3284 (adopted June 17, 2024), RS1 and RS2 lots permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Those are added units on an existing lot, not a subdivision into new lots. (§3 above)
- **Q: What are the City's suite rules?** Under Bylaw No. 3300: the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, owner-occupancy (the registered owner must reside on the lot), one extra off-street parking space, and permit/inspection/registry recording. A listing's "suite" claim is a claim to verify against those rules. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is Longlea Estates a land opportunity?** No — it is a built 1976 freehold strata townhouse complex (Plan NW 877, 58 units) that sits in this program's QUEUE, counted nowhere. It is documented and flagged in §2, never absorbed as land stock. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is Morningside Estates the same as Glenmont Park?** No — and they must never be merged. Glenmont Park is 5375 205 St, verified, in the exactly-3 apartment roster. Morningside Estates is the neighbouring 5365 205 St address, held UNCONFIRMED in every sibling. (§2 above)
- **Q: How did Langley City sell in September 2026?** The City sub-area recorded 64 detached listings with 8 sold (13%) and 195 "Condos & Townhomes" listings with 34 sold (17%). No City-only median is published, and land has no SnapStats category — do not quote the combined Langley medians as City prices. (§1 above)
- **Q: When are property taxes due in Langley City?** Payments received after the July 2 due date incur a penalty; the penalty percentage itself is UNVERIFIED on the City page read. Township properties follow the Township's own notice — do not carry a City date or rate across the boundary. https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes
- **Q: Which schools serve my City lot?** The Ministry-listed schools physically in the City are in §1 — but catchments are address-based and no SD35 locator result for a City address is on record, so confirm the exact address with the district before relying on a school claim. (§1 above)
- **Q: Will GST apply to a newly subdivided City lot bought from a developer?** Generally yes at the CRA GI-003 level — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable, unlike an individual's personal-use resale. The FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
