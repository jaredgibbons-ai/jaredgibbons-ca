## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026 and in effect July 1, 2026 (never No. 4742, which is superseded):** the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing District (consolidated from the former single- and two-family districts in June 2024) allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and minimum lot area of 281 m²; permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025:** replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. The Edmonds Community Plan covers the town centre plan area west of The Crest. Check both the 2050 OCP land use map and the Edmonds plan before assuming redevelopment value on 10th Avenue-fronting lots. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** the R1 counts above (3 / 4 / 6 by lot size and the 400-metre frequent-transit test) — lot area and transit proximity can separate two otherwise similar Crest houses by a wide margin, so price the multiplex value before listing a lot as a teardown. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing — Burnaby servicing-bylaw BASE number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** the servicing bylaw was renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024, and no base number is asserted here and none is invented; the documented process instead: new homes and SSMUH projects require Engineering pre-application approval through My Permits Portal before a building permit application, and where offsite servicing requirements are identified in the Pre-Approval Report, a cash-in-lieu contribution to City infrastructure is required, calculated on Engineering unit rates. Confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the City for the parcel. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/development-permits-construction/new-home-construction
- **Bare-land strata block (for the form — no Crest bare-land strata plan is verified; it applies to any R1 multiplex stratification):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Crest ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31). No page stating a flat farm-class figure is documented in this slug's research, so no such error is asserted here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
