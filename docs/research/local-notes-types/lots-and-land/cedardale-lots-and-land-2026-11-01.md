## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Cedardale is a small residential neighbourhood in the east part of the District of West Vancouver, below the British Properties and west of the Capilano River corridor, around Burley Drive, Kings Avenue and Mathers Avenue, between Sentinel Hill / Ambleside below and the Taylor Way corridor at its east side. The District uses the name on its neighbourhood context map and municipal map, but publishes no street-by-street boundaries and no District planning-area status for it — it is a mapped neighbourhood name, not a named OCP plan area; boundaries are UNVERIFIED. https://www.westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/DWVMunicipalMap202306.pdf
- Nearby areas: Park Royal (east/south-east, at the foot of Taylor Way), Ambleside (south, along Marine Drive), Sentinel Hill and Queens (west), the British Properties (north, uphill), and — across the Capilano River — North Vancouver's Capilano / Norgate side. https://www.westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/DWVMunicipalMap202306.pdf
- Main roads and transit: Burley Drive, Kings Avenue and Mathers Avenue are the internal streets; Taylor Way reaches Highway 1 directly, and Marine Drive / Taylor Way reach the Lions Gate Bridge. TransLink route 251 runs through the area via Kings Avenue, Burley Drive and Mathers Avenue to Park Royal Exchange; route 254 select trips also route via Burley Drive and Kings Avenue; Park Royal Exchange is the western terminus of the R2 RapidBus, which since September 7, 2026 runs one-seat to Metrotown. Commute times in minutes: UNVERIFIED. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt251.pdf and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- Civic anchors: Cedardale Park and Burley Drive Park, both listed in the District's Parks Master Plan; the Capilano Pacific Trail along the Capilano River corridor at the east edge; West Vancouver Community Centre and Aquatic Centre, 2121 Marine Drive in Ambleside; West Vancouver Memorial Library, 1950 Marine Drive; Park Royal, 2002 Park Royal South, the District's regional shopping centre named in OCP Policy 2.3.2. Nearest hospital: Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/PARKS_MASTER_PLAN_FINAL.pdf and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital
- Schools: SD45 (West Vancouver Schools) catchments are address-based with no middle-school tier (elementary K–7, secondary 8–12). The school physically inside Cedardale is École Cedardale (K–7), 595 Burley Drive — a single-track French Immersion program and authorized IB World School, entered by lottery, NOT the English catchment school. The English catchment for any Cedardale address is UNVERIFIED in this file (the SD45 locator was not run for this research); neighbouring schools are Westcot Elementary (K–7), 760 Westcot Road, and West Vancouver Secondary (8–12), 1750 Mathers Avenue — which of them a given address feeds is UNVERIFIED without the locator. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/ecole-cedardale-french-immersionib-candidate/ and https://westvancouverschools.ca/french-immersion-faq/

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No meaningful bare-land market exists in Cedardale — stated plainly at the top. This is a small, built-out pocket of fee-simple detached-house lots on the Burley Drive / Kings Avenue / Mathers Avenue grid. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan, no presale lot offering and no approved major project inside Cedardale was verified on an official or developer source. Land trades here as an existing house lot — usually with a house on it — and its value turns on the lot's confirmed RS zone, not on any lot program.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default and only verified form

- **Established-street house lots:** predominantly freehold detached houses on RS single-family dwelling zone lots; strata condos and townhomes inside Cedardale are UNVERIFIED (none confirmed on an official source). A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf

These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size — the zone and minimum for a specific lot are confirmed on the District's Zoning Map. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/PARKS_MASTER_PLAN_FINAL.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP (District of West Vancouver):** Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 (Section 200 RS minimums in Sections 1–2; the lot's zone is on the District Zoning Map) and Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, applying District-wide. A Cedardale-specific local area plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- **Ground-oriented / SSMUH position, as this file's own research states it:** the bylaw's ground-oriented dwelling rules state 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop; Section 200 also lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses in the single-family zones. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **Provincial density directive — verify per property:** on July 21, 2025 the Province directed West Vancouver to amend its OCP to provide increased density in the single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave (and at Park Royal–Taylor Way) by December 31, 2025. Cedardale sits between Ambleside and Park Royal–Taylor Way, so whether its streets fall inside those "adjacent" areas is UNVERIFIED and must be verified per property. The District missed the December 31, 2025 deadline on all three directives; a provincial order that took effect April 7, 2026 amended the District's bylaw to implement the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan, and the District must file progress reports every three months through 2026 and 2027 on the remaining directives. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698 and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0038-000377
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision runs against the Section 200 minimum site area for the lot's RS zone, confirmed on the District Zoning Map. A District subdivision/servicing bylaw number could not be confirmed on a primary source in this file's research — held UNCONFIRMED, no number asserted; the process is documented here from the zoning minimums, the hazard permit areas and the District's metered water/sewer position above. Confirm requirements with the District for the parcel. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Hazard diligence:** check the Environmental Development Permit Area (watercourses/riparian) on east-edge lots near the Capilano River corridor, and the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, before pricing any build or split. https://westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/natural-environment-and-hazard-development-permit-areas
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Cedardale ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Additional school tax (live at Cedardale assessments):** 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Cedardale lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Cedardale-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Cedardale, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **District property taxes and utilities:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026; approximately 50% of the tax bill is collected by the District and remitted to other taxing authorities. Water and sewer are metered and billed quarterly. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant-lot subdivision or a lot presale I can buy into in Cedardale?** No verified one — Cedardale is a built-out detached-house pocket; no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata plan or presale lot offering was verified. Land trades as existing house lots. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How big is a Cedardale lot, and what is the smallest lot I can create?** It depends on the parcel's RS zone: Section 200 minimums run from 488 m² (RS5) to 8,094 m² (RS1), with RS3 at 975 m² and RS5 at 488 m² the zones evidenced in and beside the area. Confirm the zone on the District Zoning Map before pricing any split. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a detached suite?** Section 200 lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses in the single-family zones, subject to the bylaw's conditions — confirm the specific lot's zone and conditions with the District. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Q: Can I put 4–6 dwellings on a Cedardale lot?** The bylaw's ground-oriented rules state 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop — and the Province's "adjacent to Ambleside / Park Royal–Taylor Way" density areas may or may not include a given Cedardale street; that inclusion is UNVERIFIED per property. Do not price multiplex potential without a District check. (Section 3.) https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **Q: Is École Cedardale my catchment school if I buy the lot across the street?** No — École Cedardale is a single-track French Immersion IB school entered by lottery (Kindergarten, or Grade 1 to end of Term One, space permitting). The English catchment for a Cedardale address is UNVERIFIED in this file; run the exact address in the SD45 locator. https://westvancouverschools.ca/french-immersion-faq/
- **Q: Does the Capilano River edge affect an east-side lot?** The District's Environmental Development Permit Area covers watercourses and riparian habitat, and the river corridor forms Cedardale's east edge — check permit status early on east-edge lots. https://westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/natural-environment-and-hazard-development-permit-areas
- **Q: Is a Cedardale lot serviced, and how is it billed?** District water and sewer are metered and billed quarterly — that is the District's stated serviced position; parcel-level servicing beyond that wording is UNCONFIRMED, so confirm the specific lot with the District. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does the additional school tax apply here?** It can — 0.2% on the residential portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising January 1, 2027. At Cedardale assessments this is a real carrying-cost line, not a footnote. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Q: Is Cedardale part of the British Properties?** No — it sits below (south of) the British Properties, between the Properties and Park Royal / Ambleside. Whether any Cedardale lot carries a British Pacific Properties covenant is a title-by-title question, UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/DWVFiveYearFinancialPlan2022%20.pdf
