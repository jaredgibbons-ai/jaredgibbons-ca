## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Riverdale's land trades as fee-simple house lots on the established pocket streets — there is no standing vacant-subdivision market here and no meaningful bare-land market as a separate category. What matters is the RS1/RS2 subzone ladder (minimums from 360 m² to 2,000 m² in the bylaw ranges documented), the suite and Bill 44 multi-unit potential the City states for those lots, and the flood/airport diligence that sits over the whole Thompson-side pocket. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering was verified.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw 8500 (RS1/RS2, Section 8.1):** houses in the pocket sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2, with minimum lot areas by subzone in Section 8.1.8 and a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones; Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL also apply in the bylaw as amended. Confirm the parcel's subzone before pricing any split. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **OCP — Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000:** the city-wide plan covering the pocket, with Schedule 2 area and sub-area plans listed by the City for the Thompson side of Richmond; no Riverdale-specific plan exists on the City record read. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states it:** a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in the RS1/RS2 zones — Section 5.4 states a suite minimum of 33.0 m² and a maximum of the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m² — and the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit such as a coach house per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — VERIFIED bylaws:** the City's own alphabetical bylaw list carries "Subdivision and Development — Bylaw 8751 (2011)" and "Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 (2011)" — Richmond's subdivision/servicing instrument is Bylaw 8751, with cost recovery under Bylaw 8752 (verified, banked in the L3 Richmond batch). Subdivision follows the parcel's RS subzone minimums in Section 2; parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **Flood Control — Bylaw 8204:** new construction is subject to flood construction levels and dike setbacks under the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Aircraft-noise diligence:** the pocket sits on the Sea Island / YVR side of Richmond; aircraft noise policy is set out in City Bulletin DEVAPPS-05 and the Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy Area. A street-by-street noise contour finding for Riverdale: UNVERIFIED — check the property's position under that policy material before a buyer commits. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/devapps0534378.pdf
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no pocket ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Riverdale a real Richmond neighbourhood?** The ground is real; the name is board-style. No City OCP area or sub-area plan by that name was confirmed — the City plans and serves this ground as part of Thompson, and no official boundary is published. (FILE STATUS; Section 1.)
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in the pocket?** No verified one — Riverdale trades as built fee-simple house lots. No named bare-land subdivision and no presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create here?** It depends entirely on the parcel's subzone: documented RS1 minimums run from 315.0 m² (RS1/K) and 360.0 m² (RS1/B) up to 2,000 m² (RS1/G), with a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones. Confirm the subzone in Section 8.1.8 first. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Q: Can a Riverdale lot add a suite or coach house?** The City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit such as a coach house per lot; in RS1/RS2 a suite is a permitted secondary use (33.0 m² minimum; maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling or 110 m²), and Bill 44 minimums run 3 units at 280 m² or less and 4 units above — eligibility is parcel-specific. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: Is there a Riverdale Elementary for my kids?** No — not in Richmond. Riverdale Elementary is a Surrey (SD36) school. SD38 catchments here are address-based and this file's sample-address result is UNVERIFIED; run the exact address in the SD38 locator. https://www.surreyschools.ca/Riverdale
- **Q: Does the flood system actually protect the pocket?** The City system is 49 km of dikes and 39 pump stations designed for a 1:500 flood event with an active raising program (3.5 m to 4.7 m) — and Bylaw 8204 flood construction levels still apply to new construction. Both facts belong in the disclosure conversation. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **Q: Should I worry about aircraft noise?** Raise it early: this is the Sea Island side of Richmond and the City's Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development policy material applies — but a street-by-street contour finding for this pocket is UNVERIFIED, so check the specific property's position. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/devapps0534378.pdf
- **Q: Are the No. 2 Road townhouses part of the pocket's land stock?** They are built strata product on the pocket's edges, counted in the townhomes sibling — not lots. The interior streets are fee-simple houses. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What recreation actually serves the pocket?** McCallan Neighbourhood Park (3.514 acres) inside the pocket, the McCallan Road Trail link to the West Dyke and Middle Arm Dyke Trails, and Thompson Community Centre at 5151 Granville Avenue — City-documented, sellable by name. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=79
