## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Hemlock" is a Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area name, reported by SnapStats under the community Mission — it is not a municipality, and Hemlock Valley is not inside the City of Mission. Hemlock Valley is unincorporated land in FVRD Electoral Area C; the FVRD's Hemlock Valley Official Community Plan (November 2019) governs the plan area, and the FVRD administers that plan. (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report) https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Plan-area basis: the OCP plan area is based on the Controlled Recreation Area (CRA) established by the Province in 2016 as part of approval of the Hemlock Valley All Seasons Resort Master Plan, for what is now Sasquatch Mountain Resort; the CRA carries the licence of occupation that lets the resort developer use Crown land for the purposes in the Resort Master Plan. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- The place: Hemlock Valley is the residential village at Sasquatch Mountain Resort (formerly the Hemlock ski area); the live page is titled "Hemlock Valley Homes for Sale | Sasquatch Mountain Resort" and its listings sit on streets including Sakwi Creek Road, Edelweiss Drive, Mt Downing Road, Enzian Way and Snowflake Crescent. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hemlock.html
- Mailing reality vs governance: live-page listings carry both "Mission, BC V0M 1A1" and "Agassiz, BC V0M 1A1" mailing addresses — neither mailing town is the governing authority; governance is FVRD Electoral Area C. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hemlock.html
- Neighbourhood planning areas inside the OCP area: Hemlock West (the historic resort community), Hemlock East and Hemlock North, each to be detailed through neighbourhood plans as the resort expands. Distances per the OCP: Agassiz (District of Kent) is approximately 38 km away, Mission approximately 55 km west, and the Village of Harrison Hot Springs sits at the southern end of Harrison Lake, south of the plan area. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Paragon sub-area code for "Hemlock": UNVERIFIED. Exact board boundary: UNVERIFIED.
- Community scale: the FVRD's Hemlock Valley Evacuation Guide describes Hemlock Valley, a designated place in FVRD Area C, as having approximately 200 dwellings — mainly vacation homes and short-term rentals — with Sasquatch Mountain Resort at the village's upper end. http://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Hemlock%20Valley.pdf
- Schools — no school in the valley: the OCP states the small permanent population will limit the viability of locating schools and medical services within the CRA. Nearest schools confirmed against the BC Ministry K-12 file: in SD78 Fraser-Cascade — Kent Elementary, 7285 Mccullough Rd, Agassiz; Agassiz Elem-Secondary, Agassiz; Harrison Hot Springs Elementary, Harrison Hot Springs; in SD75 Mission — Deroche Elementary, 10340 N Deroche Rd, Deroche, and Dewdney Elementary, 37151 Hawkins-Pickle Rd, Dewdney. ## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No meaningful bare-land market is verified for Hemlock Valley — stated plainly at the top. No named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata, and no presale lot offering was verified on an official or developer source. The valley's land story is a small built resort village (~200 dwellings) inside a provincially approved resort plan area — the expansion in the plan is a long-range vision, documented in §2 as a plan, never as lot inventory.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form, as far as the sources go

- **Forms on the live page:** detached cabins and chalets on Sakwi Creek Road, Edelweiss Drive and Mt Downing Road, half-duplex product on Snowflake Crescent, and slope-side condo units on Enzian Way. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hemlock.html
- **Tenure split today: UNVERIFIED.** The OCP's build-out distribution proposes 40% public (nightly-rental) bed units, 45% private bed units and 15% resident/employee deed-restricted housing at full build-out — a plan target for the future resort, NOT a description of today's tenure split. No freehold/strata split for existing lots is stated. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Lot character: UNVERIFIED.** No official source publishes a valley-wide lot-size or build-era profile; the OCP is a forward plan, not a stock survey. No lot size, zone minimum or typical parcel is invented in this file — an FVRD zoning bylaw number and its minimum lot sizes are UNVERIFIED in this slug's research (see the status block) and are confirmed parcel-by-parcel with the FVRD.
- **Existing accommodation base, per the OCP:** 226 resort residential units at Hemlock equating to 1,072 bed units, about 60% of them ski-in/ski-out or within comfortable walking distance of the base area. That is built resort stock, not vacant land. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Crown-land tenure nuance a land buyer must understand:** inside the CRA, the resort developer uses Crown land under a licence of occupation for Resort Master Plan purposes. That licence is the developer's tenure for resort purposes — it is not a statement that any individual residential lot is Crown land or fee simple; the tenure of any single parcel is a title question, UNCONFIRMED per parcel in this file. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Governing plan — FVRD Hemlock Valley Official Community Plan (November 2019).** The FVRD is responsible for administering the plan. Its chapters cover land use for Hemlock West / East / North, Infrastructure & Services (water supply, sanitary sewer, utilities and communications, stormwater, snow clearing and storage, solid waste, emergency/protective services), and Hazard & Risk Management including a Wildfire Interface Hazard section; Development Permit Areas in Schedule I include protection of development from hazardous conditions. **The OCP bylaw number and any FVRD zoning bylaw number: UNVERIFIED in this slug's research — none is asserted, and no Chilliwack or Mission bylaw number is borrowed in its place.** https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Servicing process:** servicing is a resort-community system administered in the FVRD plan framework, not City of Mission services. Parcel-level servicing (which system a given lot is on, and its fees): UNVERIFIED — confirm the lot's water/sewer system and fees with the FVRD before subjects; where any servicing or subdivision bylaw number is unconfirmed, this file documents the process (FVRD confirmation for the parcel) and states the number as UNCONFIRMED rather than supplying one. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential:** no SSMUH position for Hemlock Valley parcels is documented in this slug's research: UNCONFIRMED. The provincial small-scale multi-unit framework as applied to unincorporated FVRD Area C resort land is a parcel question for the FVRD — do not import a city's SSMUH unit counts (Chilliwack's, Mission's or any other) onto a valley lot. Subdivision of a valley parcel likewise runs through the applicable provincial / regional approving process for unincorporated land: the governing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED here, the process confirmation route is the FVRD / provincial approving officer for the parcel, and no subdivision outcome is promised.
- **Hazard / DPA check before valuing a slope lot:** the OCP's Development Permit Areas include hazardous-conditions protection and its risk chapter covers the wildfire interface — confirm the lot's DPA status with the FVRD. Parcel-level hazard status: UNVERIFIED. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Property taxes — RURAL regime (the only regime that applies here):** a property not located in a city, town, district or village is in a rural area; its owner receives the tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province, not the FVRD. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural taxes are due July 2, a 5% penalty is added to any portion unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% on any portion unpaid on October 31. Do not quote any city's tax dates for a Hemlock Valley lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Property transfer tax:** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption netted): $700,000 → $12,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **PTT exemptions:** the First-Time Home Buyers' Program and the Newly Built Home Exemption are home-buyer exemptions as the Province describes them; bare-land eligibility for either: UNCONFIRMED in this file — do not budget an exemption on a vacant-lot purchase without a parcel-specific ruling. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most individual personal-use sales exempt; business-use / in-the-course-of-business / more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally taxable — directly relevant if any future resort-phase lot is sold by a developer. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a valley lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Federal purchase prohibition:** no primary prohibition text was opened for this slug in the research — the position for a Hemlock Valley lot buyer is UNCONFIRMED here and nothing is conformed from another file.
- **BC strata block (applies in full to any strata lot in the valley — including the Sakwi Creek Road townhouse strata cross-referenced in §2, and to any bare-land strata lot if one is ever verified):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (the Sakwi Creek strata's lot count is UNCONFIRMED, so its 5+ status is confirmed on the Form B / strata plan); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children). - **ALR block (conditional — no ALR parcel in the valley is documented in this slug's research; applies only where an ALC map check shows the parcel is ALR):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Hemlock Valley part of Mission?** No — SnapStats groups "Hemlock" under community Mission for reporting and many listings carry a Mission mailing address, but the valley is unincorporated FVRD Electoral Area C land governed by the FVRD's Hemlock Valley OCP. Title, permits and taxes run through the FVRD and the Province. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot and build in Hemlock Valley?** No bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering is verified in this file, and lot sizes / zoning minimums are UNVERIFIED in the research — any lot opportunity is a parcel-specific FVRD confirmation (tenure on title, servicing system, DPA status) before it is priced. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Will the resort expansion make my lot more valuable?** The expansion (toward 19,969 bed units at full build-out) is a phased long-range plan vision in the OCP / Resort Master Plan, not built product and not a sales program — do not price a cabin or lot as if the expanded resort already exists. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Q: Who provides water and sewer to a valley lot?** A resort-community system administered in the FVRD plan framework — not City of Mission services. Which system a given lot is on, and its fees: UNVERIFIED — confirm with the FVRD for the parcel. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Q: Who do I pay property tax to, and when?** The Province (Surveyor of Taxes), under the rural regime: due July 2, 5% on amounts unpaid July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid October 31. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $700,000 / $1,500,000?** $12,000 at $700,000 and $28,000 at $1,500,000 (fully residential anchors, §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Can I run a short-term rental on a valley lot/cabin?** The valley's pattern is vacation homes and short-term rentals per the FVRD, but a specific property's rental permission, strata rules and licensing need parcel-level confirmation — and strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022 while short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw. (§3.)
- **Q: Where do children go to school if we live here full-time?** There is no school in the valley — the OCP says the permanent population is too small to make one viable inside the CRA. The nearest Ministry-confirmed schools are in Agassiz (SD78) and at Deroche/Dewdney (SD75), but the catchment for a valley address is UNVERIFIED. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Q: Is there wildfire or slope risk on a valley lot?** The OCP carries a Wildfire Interface Hazard section and Development Permit Areas that include protection from hazardous conditions — confirm the lot's DPA status with the FVRD before subjects; parcel-level status is UNVERIFIED here. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Q: How did Hemlock land/homes sell in September 2026?** The sub-area recorded 0 sales in both splits (13 detached and 9 attached listings, 0 sold) — there is no Hemlock median to quote, and the Mission community medians in §1 are a city market ~55 km away, not Hemlock pricing evidence. (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report)
