## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022** (it repealed OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007), under which agriculture is the dominant land use in the city. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/media/8989
- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011:** the main zones are A-1 General Agriculture — one farm house per lot (maximum gross floor area 500 sq m), one additional farm house allowed for farm operations over 8 ha and under 40 ha (two if 40 ha or more), minimum lot area for subdivision 8 ha — and A-2 Large Lot Agricultural (minimum 16 ha), which protects large-lot farming areas under regulations consistent with the Agricultural Land Commission Act; RS Large Lot Residential (minimum lot area 0.2 ha) and R-3 / R-4 / R-6 Urban Residential apply in the Fraser Way / Wildwood Crescent pocket at the east edge (R-3 / R-4 / R-6 subdivision minimum 1,215 sq m). Any specific parcel's zone: per-parcel, UNVERIFIED. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **SSMUH as the City states it:** the City's SSMUH framework (SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986, adopted June 25, 2024; lots 280 sq m or less up to 3 units, lots over 280 sq m up to 4, and lots over 280 sq m within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6) is an urban-lot framework; whether SSMUH unit counts apply to a given A-1 / A-2 farm parcel is per-parcel: UNCONFIRMED — do not price a West Meadows farm on urban SSMUH unit counts. Suites on this ground: secondary suites and garden suites are listed accessory uses in the RS zone; the City garden suite guide sets one suite per lot at 33 to 90 sq m, and a lot cannot have both a garden suite and a secondary suite. Basis: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/central-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md, §4 (citywide SSMUH statement) and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf

The Pitt Meadows servicing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED — no number is quoted and no other city's bylaw is borrowed; the documented process is zone minimums + dike/drainage position, confirmed with the City for the parcel. Septic and well servicing details for individual farm parcels: UNVERIFIED; wells are checkable in the provincial GWELLS application, and a home not connected to a community sewer must have an onsite system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
