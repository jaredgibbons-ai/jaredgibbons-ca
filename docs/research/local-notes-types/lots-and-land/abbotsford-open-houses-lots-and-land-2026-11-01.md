## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), carried VERBATIM:** *"(3) Unless a licensee solicits or receives information from a party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate, a disclosure to the party is not required under subsection (1) when the licensee is only (a) hosting an advertised open house, or (b) providing factual responses to general questions from the party."* (Real Estate Services Rules, B.C. Reg. 209/2021, now carried as s.54(3) in the renumbered Rules consolidation: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/209_2021; the rule as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report: http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download). The hosting agent at an Abbotsford open house represents the seller: BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers

- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** an $800,000 parcel, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000**; a fully-qualifying first-time buyer on the same parcel pays **$6,000** (the $8,000 tax on the exempt first $500,000 removed). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Abbotsford property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026; penalties are legislated and not waivable. **Court note:** Abbotsford HAS a Supreme Court registry — the exception to the no-registry pattern in the program's smaller-city frames. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice and (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/abbotsford-open-houses-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
