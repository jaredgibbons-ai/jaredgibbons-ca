- **Zoning — City of Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 (§6 farm zones where farmland is met).** A specific parcel's zone is a per-parcel City check: UNVERIFIED here for any parcel. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH — Bylaw 5395 (adopted Jun 18, 2024), amending Zoning Bylaw 5000:** allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Suites and accessory units — Bylaw 5000:** a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the habitable floor space of the building (s.2); only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot, with permitted types including secondary suite, coach house and garden suite, garden suites capped at 100 m² (s.4.06(1)); ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite (s.5.04); a suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. Do not price a lot as if it can carry a suite plus a coach house. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
