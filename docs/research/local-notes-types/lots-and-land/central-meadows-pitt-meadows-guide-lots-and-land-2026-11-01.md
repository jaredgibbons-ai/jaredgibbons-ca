## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022** (it repealed OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007), designating the Town Centre for high and medium density multifamily and mixed-use commercial/residential development. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/media/8989
- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011:** houses sit under RS Large Lot Residential, R-3, R-4 and R-6 Urban Residential zones — RS minimum lot area 0.2 ha; R-3 up to 3 units per lot, R-4 up to 4 units and R-6 up to five units, or six where at least one unit meets the adaptable housing requirements in the BC Building Code on lots close to frequent transit, each with a minimum lot area for subdivision of 1,215 sq m (a subdivision minimum only, not a development minimum on existing lots, per the City's SSMUH implementation update); RM-1 Multi-Family Residential 1 (townhouse) minimum lot area 0.4 ha. Any specific parcel's zone: per-parcel, UNVERIFIED. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **SSMUH as the City states it:** under provincial SSMUH rules applied citywide, lots 280 sq m or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 sq m up to 4 units, and lots over 280 sq m within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units; the City's SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986 was adopted June 25, 2024. Suites: one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit, 33 to 90 sq m, inside the same building, where the zone permits it; garden suites one per lot at 33 to 90 sq m in the zones listed in the City garden suite guide, including RS — a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — Pitt Meadows:** subdivision follows the zone minimums above. The Pitt Meadows servicing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED — no number is quoted and no other city's bylaw is borrowed; the documented process is zone minimums + the SSMUH update + the drainage/dike position, confirmed with the City for the parcel. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/central-meadows-pitt-meadows-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §3.
- **Floodplain:** over 95% of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the floodplain for the Fraser River and, to a smaller degree, the Pitt River (City 2020 Business Plan — the figure these notes retire to); the City is almost entirely protected by 60 km of dikes, plus ditches, pump stations and flood boxes; Central Meadows is in drainage Area 3 (Kennedy, including McKechnie). LIVE CONFLICT: this guide page quotes "over 85%" (City Financial Plan 2026–2030) — flagged at the top, never silently resolved. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Short-term rentals: PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows** — short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in the City's Zoning Bylaw, per the City's zoning bylaw amendments staff report. Do not price any Central Meadows lot on STR income. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **ALR block (citywide 78% ALR; Town Centre parcel status per-address, UNVERIFIED here):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR; no flat farm-class figure is asserted for this slug because this file's own research does not document one. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, where met — none verified on this ground):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,500,000 lot purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,500,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K cap→$14,000, $6,000 FTHB; $1.8M→$34,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000). No $78,000 figure for $3.5M was met in this slug's research or siblings checked; where that figure appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program it is an OPEN reconciliation item — follow $93,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

The servicing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED; confirm the process with the City for the parcel. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Q: Can a Central Meadows lot have a suite or a garden suite?** One secondary suite per principal dwelling unit (33 to 90 sq m, inside the same building) where the zone permits it, or one garden suite per lot in the zones listed in the City guide — never both on one lot. SSMUH counts of 3, 4 or 6 turn on the 280 sq m threshold and the 400 m frequent-transit test. STRs are PROHIBITED. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Q: Is Central Meadows in a floodplain?** Yes, on the figure these notes retire to: the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of Pitt Meadows lies within the Fraser River and Pitt River floodplain, the city is almost entirely protected by 60 km of dikes, and this area is in drainage Area 3. The guide page's "over 85%" Financial Plan figure is the other side of a LIVE CONFLICT between two City sources, flagged at the top. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve Central Meadows?** Pitt Meadows Elementary (11941 Harris Rd) is physically in the area and Pitt Meadows Secondary (19438 116b Ave) is the city's only public secondary school, both in SD42 (elementary K–7, secondary 8–12) — but address-level catchments are UNVERIFIED in this file, so confirm any address with SD42. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/education/work/bc_k12_schools.csv
- **Q: How much PTT on a $1.5M Central Meadows lot?** $28,000 — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) plus 2% on the next $1,300,000 ($26,000). (§3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a vacant lot?** Generally yes if the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided; generally no for an individual's personal-use resale, per CRA GI-003 — and the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
