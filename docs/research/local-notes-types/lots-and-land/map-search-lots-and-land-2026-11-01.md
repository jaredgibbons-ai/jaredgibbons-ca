## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning / OCP, per municipality:** a site-wide map crosses many municipalities, each with its own zoning bylaw and OCP — there is no single bylaw to cite for this page, and the governing bylaw for any result is that parcel's own municipality's, verified parcel by parcel. The page's own GIS section points the same way: turn on the parcel, zoning, OCP, ALR, flood plain and environmentally sensitive area layers on the municipality's own GIS. What each municipality's GIS shows layer by layer, and the exact setback or easement figures for any lot: UNVERIFIED at municipal level in this research. https://jaredgibbons.ca/map-search.html
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential:** likewise municipal — the added-unit and subdivision position for a parcel is its own municipality's, as that city states it, stated in that area's files in this program. No site-wide SSMUH frame is stated for a region-wide tool, and added-unit permission is never permission to subdivide.
- **ALR block, in full (the ALR layer is a legal gate, not a map decoration):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); a principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at 90 m² or less (parcels of 40 ha or smaller) or 186 m² or less (larger parcels), where local bylaws also permit it. Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" figure is an ERROR wherever met; no page stating such a figure is documented in this slug's research, so no such error is asserted for this page. A parcel's ALR status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot a search surfaces):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; CRF minimum 10% since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. **Fee-simple contrast:** a fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax — the figure a map-search user is usually budgeting around:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000, based on fair market value at registration unless an exemption applies. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** $800,000 fair market value, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $12,000 (2% × $600,000) = **$14,000**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, maximum saving $8,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. A map's price filter does not compute this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). A price filter does not separate new-build from resale, so two pins at the same price can carry different tax. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Who a listing's agent works for, for a user who finds a parcel and contacts its brokerage:** in British Columbia, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), and BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Speculation / vacancy taxes and purchase prohibition:** no position for this site-wide page is documented in this slug's research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and a region-wide map does not change that. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position is **UNCONFIRMED** here — no primary text was opened for this slug, so no position is stated or conformed from other files.
