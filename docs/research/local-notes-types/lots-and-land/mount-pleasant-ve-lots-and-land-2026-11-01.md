## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **City frame:** Vancouver's Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 governs, as amended by site-specific CD-1 bylaws along the corridors; the **Broadway Plan** (approved June 22, 2022; updated December 12, 2024) is the area plan — policy, not zoning, with most higher-density forms still requiring rezoning; citywide direction now comes from the **Vancouver Official Development Plan** (replaced the Vancouver Plan March 31, 2026). https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/mount-pleasant-ve.md)
- **Multiplex on a house lot (the SSMUH-equivalent Vancouver frame, as the City states it):** By-law No. 13817 replaced the RS districts with the **R1-1 district**, allowing up to 6 strata units (or 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots, subject to the district schedule. Check heritage register status before suggesting demolition of a character house. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/mount-pleasant-ve.md)
- **Subdivision and servicing — VERIFIED ledger entry:** City of Vancouver **Subdivision By-law 5208** governs subdivision of land in Vancouver, with fees under **By-law 14538** — carried exactly from the standing verified ledger. Parcel-level subdivision approval, servicing and floodplain-standard compliance are confirmed with the City for the specific application. (Standing verified ledger.)
- **Floodplain standards:** parcels in the City's designated floodplain (the Great Northern Way / False Creek lowland at this sub-area's north edge) are subject to the Building By-law's Floodplain Standards and Requirements, including flood construction levels — a per-parcel check, never assumed from the street. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/mount-pleasant-ve.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax = 3% — VANCOUVER ONLY:** the City charges 3% of assessed taxable value on homes declared or deemed empty, with the annual declaration due February 3, 2026 and payment due April 16, 2026 — a live holding cost for anyone holding a vacant house or unit on a VE lot, and a tax that exists only inside the City of Vancouver. City 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/mount-pleasant-ve.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Mount Pleasant VE?** There is no lot shelf: no bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering is verified for this ground (§2). What trades is fee-simple character houses on their lots — the lot's value story is the R1-1 multiplex option and, on corridors, the Broadway Plan — priced parcel by parcel. (§2)
- **Q: Can I build a multiplex on a Mount Pleasant house lot?** Generally yes: By-law No. 13817 rezoned Vancouver's RS districts to R1-1, allowing up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units per lot subject to the district schedule; Broadway Plan designations may allow more on corridor sites through rezoning. Check heritage register status before suggesting demolition. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **Q: The Broadway Plan promises 20–30 storeys near the station — is my lot a teardown site?** Not automatically: the Plan supports that height in the station area, but it is policy, not zoning, and most sites still need rezoning; older rental buildings also carry the Plan's renter protections and tenant relocation requirements. Price station-adjacent land against the Plan with a planner's answer, not a headline. (§3)
- **Q: What does the Empty Homes Tax mean for holding a vacant house on a VE lot?** Budget 3% of assessed taxable value per year, on top of ordinary property tax, with an annual declaration due each February (declaration due February 3, 2026; payment due April 16, 2026). It is Vancouver-only — the same vacant property in Burnaby or North Vancouver owes no EHT. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Q: When does the SkyTrain actually reach Mount Pleasant?** On the Province's schedule, fall 2027: Mount Pleasant Station is at Main Street and Broadway, and Broadway's four lanes at the station block reopened in July 2026 — price the opening as a dated event, not a rumour. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026TT0046-000500.pdf
- **Q: Is 9 On The Park a lot opportunity?** No — it is a built 9-unit freehold-strata townhouse complex (2337 Brunswick St, EPS3128, built 2015) counted in the townhomes home. Built stock, never land. (§2)
- **Q: Are the heritage apartment buildings on Broadway buyable strata or land plays?** Not on any source verified here: the Lee Building and Wenonah Apartments are carried as heritage apartment buildings in the City's context statement; the Lee Building is documented in §2 as a tenure record only, with its strata status UNCONFIRMED on the sibling evidence — read title and disclosure documents before treating any heritage apartment address as strata or development land. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/mount-pleasant-historic-context-statement.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Mount Pleasant VE lot?** SD39 catchments are address-based and this sub-area is split between several elementary catchments; the only locator run on record (2600 East Broadway) returned Chief Maquinna Elementary / Vancouver Technical Secondary. Any other address is UNVERIFIED until run. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/039
- **Q: Is the north edge in a floodplain?** The Great Northern Way lowland is former False Creek shoreline inside the City's designated floodplain, with flood construction levels under the Building By-law; the grid south of 2nd Avenue sits on the slope. Check the specific parcel. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
