## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — City ONLY:** City of Langley Zoning Bylaw, 2025, No. 3300 governs the City (cited by NUMBER only — its adoption date is conflicted in sources and is UNVERIFIED in this file, exactly as both earlier City land files carry it). No Township of Langley bylaw is used for any City rule in this file. Full land-plan detail is cross-referenced to the langleycity research, not duplicated here. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw-2025-no-3300pdf-2
- **OCP:** City of Langley Official Community Plan, adopted November 22, 2021. (Research file §4.)
- **SSMUH — City:** the City amended its Zoning Bylaw to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on RS1 and RS2 lots by Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 — RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Added-unit permission is not permission to subdivide. This is the City's own frame; the Township's Bylaw 6020 frame is different and is never imported here. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3284
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 3300):** a suite must not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the gross floor area of the building; the registered owner must reside on the lot (s.2.4); at least one extra off-street parking space is required; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry; a carriage/garden ground-floor unit is capped at 100 m². A listing's "legal suite" claim is a claim to verify against this frame. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw-2025-no-3300pdf-2
- **Subdivision / servicing — City:** Bylaw 3300 states that no person shall subdivide land except in accordance with the bylaw, and subdivisions are subject to the City's Subdivision Control Bylaw and Development Cost Charge Bylaw — the Subdivision Control Bylaw's number is UNVERIFIED in this program (exactly as the earlier City land files carry it), so the process is documented and the number is not asserted; parcel-level requirements are confirmed with the City. (Earlier City land files, §3.)
- **Short-term rentals in Langley City: UNVERIFIED** — no City STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page in this program. Do not market a City lot or home as an STR on an uncited rule. (Research file §4.)
- **ALR block (for completeness — no City ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, asserted only where a file's own research documents it, and no such line is documented for this slug. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata diligence (the BC strata block — applies in full to any bare-land strata lot, if one is met, and to the City's built strata stock):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. Longlea Estates, if purchased as strata, would sit under this block — it is QUEUED here, so the block is stated for the form only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Langley City property taxes:** payments received after the July 2, 2026 due date incur a penalty; the City's payment page lists City Hall at 20399 Douglas Crescent and a 2.2% processing fee on credit-card payments. The penalty percentage for 2026: UNVERIFIED — the City page read states a penalty applies but does not state the rate. https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not bare land; a person who paid PTT on vacant land and then has a newly built home on it may be eligible for a refund of the tax paid). **Worked examples, inputs + tier math stated (fully residential, no exemption unless stated):** $500,000 → $2,000 + $6,000 = **$8,000**; $700,000 → $2,000 + $10,000 = **$12,000**; $800,000 → $2,000 + $12,000 = **$14,000** (fully-qualifying first-time buyer: **$6,000**); $900,000 → **$16,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → $2,000 + $26,000 = **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → $2,000 + $32,000 = **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 (3% tier) + $10,000 (further 2% on the $500,000 over $3M) = **$93,000**. Where a $78,000 figure for $3.5M is met in program siblings, it omits the further 2% tier and is an OPEN reconciliation item — this file follows **$93,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
