## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP / zoning:** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026), and Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **SSMUH as the City states it (Jul 8, 2024).** Maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; former RF-12 / RF-12C → R4 and RF-SD → R6. Secondary suites: one per dwelling unit, not in a coach house or garden suite, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area — not a separate strata lot. Coach / garden suites: 35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Subdivision + servicing process.** Surrey subdivision proceeds through detailing and a Servicing Agreement defining off-site works, services and financial securities for final approval. **The Surrey servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in this file** — no number is stated in this slug's research or a cited City source opened for it, and no other city's number is borrowed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey).** Permitted, owner-only, under 90 consecutive days per patron, owner must live at the property as primary residence, tenants cannot run one. Licence figure CONFLICT: $358/yr program figure carried, with the City's 2024 corporate report proposing $350/yr + fees disclosed — check the City licence page before quoting. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **BC strata block (any strata lot in this band, including any bare-land strata lot).** Form B (SPA s. 59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation every 5 years for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. Garage configuration and driveway-apron parking for a townhouse are set by the complex's own strata plan and bylaws — verify them in that package, not from listing copy. BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **ALR block.** Residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class in FULL three-tier form: $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **PTT in this band.** Every purchase in this band is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers (worked examples in §2). First-time home buyers': full exemption requires fair market value of $835,000 or less, partial above $835,000 and below $860,000 — so only the lower slice of this band can use the program at all, and a purchase at the band's upper end cannot; all other program conditions also apply. Newly built home exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000) is a HOME exemption — a bare lot's eligibility is UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only.** Most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example); a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing into more than two parts. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs.** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031) is for new HOUSING, not bare land. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA).** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any presale named in the sibling files. Presales are never counted as resale band stock, in this file or any sibling.
