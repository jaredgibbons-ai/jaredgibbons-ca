## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current.** Bylaw No. 18020 is the replaced predecessor and is never cited as current. The parcel's designation is read in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000** — parcel zoning confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **RA (Acreage Residential) figures — HELD UNVERIFIED:** the live page states a 4,050 m² minimum lot size for a new RA subdivision with 50 m minimum width and 60 m minimum depth under Part 12 of Bylaw 12000, up to two dwelling units on an RA lot, and agriculture/horticulture uses only on lots of 2 hectares or larger. The Part 12 text itself was not re-opened in the research pass (429, stopped, not retried), so **those figures are UNVERIFIED against the bylaw text in this file — confirm in the current consolidated bylaw / COSMOS before quoting them for a lot.** The coach-house figures that do apply to RA lots (35–75 m², up to 120 m² below maximum FAR) are confirmed separately below. https://jaredgibbons.ca/elgin-chantrell.html
- **SSMUH / added units (Surrey framework, from Jul 8, 2024):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, on eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary — acreage ground at or over that size, or off community services, sits outside the standard framing and needs a parcel check. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 12000):** one secondary suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit; no 90 m² cap was found in the current consolidated text; a suite is not a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Coach houses and garden suites:** all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access; one extra parking space is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Subdivision / servicing:** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, confirmed per parcel in COSMOS (which also shows water/sanitary/storm mains). Verified on the City's own Land Development pages in an earlier program batch: the **Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw, worked through the Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. On acreage ground, servicing is the threshold question — a lot on well/septic is not a serviced lot, and SSMUH eligibility itself turns in part on community water/sewer connection. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside / riparian:** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and vary by stream class, with the Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit process alongside — the Nicomekl River bounds this pocket on the north and Chantrell Creek gives it half its name, so river/creek-edge parcels are checked against their stream class in COSMOS and Part 7A. No single setback figure is stated. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Heritage:** Elgin Heritage Park and the Stewart Farmhouse at 13723 Crescent Road are City-designated heritage entries (Bylaws 12922 and 7823) — a property in the Crescent Road heritage cluster needs its heritage status checked before renovation or subdivision plans are priced. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/heritage-bylaws
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only, primary residence, under 90 consecutive days per patron, business licence **$358 per year**. **Conflict disclosed:** the City's 2024 corporate report put the licence at $350/yr plus fees — direct readers to the City licence page before quoting. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **ALR block (applies parcel-by-parcel where ALR ground exists here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); local zoning may further restrict. Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for any parcel here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot, wherever one surfaces):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% up to and including $200,000, 2% over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example (anchor):** $1,500,000 fully residential parcel, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $1,300,000 = **$28,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Elgin Chantrell an official City neighbourhood?** No — it is a Paragon board name combining Elgin and Chantrell Creek; the City publishes no single boundary for it and uses the two names separately. Compare a lot against its actual street and parcel, not the label. (§1 above)
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant acreage lot here?** No named bare-land subdivision or lot offering was verified. The feed snapshot carried one land listing card, but land here generally trades as the existing estate/acreage fabric, parcel by parcel. (§2 above)
- **Q: What size lot can I subdivide an RA acreage into?** The live page states a 4,050 m² RA minimum (50 m width / 60 m depth) under Part 12 of Bylaw 12000 — but that text was not re-read in the research pass, so the figures are UNVERIFIED here; confirm in the consolidated bylaw and COSMOS before relying on them. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is my lot on sewer and water, or well and septic?** A parcel question — check COSMOS and title, and for an existing well/septic use the GWELLS record and Fraser Health's Authorized Person framework the live page points to. Never assume servicing from the acreage label. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: Is the parcel in the ALR, and what changes if it is?** Check the ALC finder by PID/address. If it is in the ALR: subdivision needs ALC approval, residences cannot be stratified, the principal is capped at 500 m² with a suite inside it only, and one additional residence is capped at 90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can an acreage home here add a suite or a coach house?** One suite per unit at ≤40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house; a coach house/garden suite of 35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA lots below maximum FAR, one extra parking space — and on ALR parcels the ALC frame applies first. (§3 above)
- **Q: Does heritage status affect a Crescent Road lot?** It can — the Stewart Farmhouse and Elgin Heritage Park are City-designated entries on that corridor; check the parcel's own heritage status before pricing renovation or subdivision plans. (§3 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a lot purchase here?** PTT on the general tiers — on the §3 anchor example ($1,500,000) the bill is $28,000 with no exemption. GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: How did Elgin Chantrell sell in September 2026?** Detached 74 listings, 10 solds, 14% ratio (joint with Grandview); attached 13 listings, 0 solds, ratio NA. All-property figures, community context only — SnapStats publishes no land category and no sub-area medians. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
