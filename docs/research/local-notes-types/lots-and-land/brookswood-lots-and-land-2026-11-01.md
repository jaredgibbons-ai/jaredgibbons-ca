## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Township:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs; minimum lot sizes (s.110.1, as reproduced in a Township council report): SR-1 — 3,716 m² / 40 m frontage; SR-2 — 8,094 m² / 45 m; SR-3 — 1,765 m² / 27.45 m; RU-1 — 1.7 ha; RU-2 / RU-3 — 8.0 ha; urban R zones from 464.5 m². City of Langley bylaws do NOT apply here. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024):** 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible, eligibility requires municipal water and sewer, and **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020**. The Township's June 10, 2024 council report estimates only ~15% of Brookswood's single-family/duplex lots eligible for 3–4 units, varying by servicing. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Suites and coach houses — Township:** secondary suite cap 120 m². A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata. **LIVE CONFLICT — flagged, not resolved:** s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted; neither position is adopted here. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **R-CL compact lots — Township s.403:** minimum lot area 220 m² to maximum LOT area 464.5 m². A "355 m²" figure is nowhere in the bylaw and is not used in this file. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — Township:** all subdivisions must be approved by the Approving Officer under the Land Title Act, through application, review, decision letter, conditions, final approval and registration. Servicing is determined under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED. A permit is required to cut or remove a protected tree under Tree Protection Bylaw 2019 No. 5478. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **ALR block (where ALR ground exists — parcel status in Brookswood is parcel-specific and UNVERIFIED here):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check a parcel in GeoSource / on the ALC maps. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Water and septic:** the provincial GWELLS registry holds recorded wells (older wells may not appear); a home not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person (Fraser Health). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,200,000 lot purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,200,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FTHB PTT exemption / Newly Built Home Exemption:** first $500,000 exempt, full at value ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000), partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; a person who paid PTT on vacant land and then has a newly built home on it may be eligible for a refund of the tax paid. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Purchase prohibition:** stated ONLY as this file's own sources support it — the research encodes no prohibition position for Brookswood land, so none is asserted: UNCONFIRMED here. (Research file above.)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there actually vacant land for sale in Brookswood?** Yes, in the feed: the live page's first page carries a run of vacant-land cards at 19642 32 Avenue, described in their own listing text as lots in a planned subdivision for duplex or single-family development. No subdivision name or lot count verifies on an official source, so treat the cards as feed evidence of a land thread, not a verified subdivision roster. https://jaredgibbons.ca/brookswood.html
- **Q: Which plan governs what I can build on a Brookswood parcel?** The Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan, with Booth, Fernridge and Rinn neighbourhood plans under it; the Township states the plan allows previously undeveloped areas to grow while protecting trees and watercourses — the parcel's plan designation drives what can be built. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/brookswoodfernridge-community-plan-adopted.aspx
- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create here?** It depends on the zone under Bylaw 2500 s.110.1: SR-3 needs 1,765 m², SR-1 needs 3,716 m² and SR-2 needs 8,094 m² — and ALR land also needs Agricultural Land Commission permission to subdivide. RU-2 minimums are UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put four homes — or six — on a Brookswood lot?** Bylaw 6020 permits 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², with eligibility turning on municipal water and sewer — and the Township estimates only ~15% of Brookswood lots eligible. **There is no 6-plex tier in Bylaw 6020.** https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: What is a coach house — is it a strata unit?** No. A coach house is a fee-simple second unit on a detached lot under Township Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata. The suite cap is 120 m². https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Q: Is Bromley a land offering?** No. Bromley (4558 208 St, Leone Homes) is a strata townhouse presale — 26 customizable townhomes, Phase 1 completion estimated Summer 2027 — counted separately in the townhomes sibling, never as land stock. ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/brookswood-townhomes-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: Are Oxford Mews or Brookswood Mills buyable land developments?** Neither is counted anywhere: Oxford Mews is a fee-simple townhouse listing record and Brookswood Mills is guide-FAQ site copy only — both are QUEUED for a future verification pass, not inventory. ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/brookswood-townhomes-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: How much PTT on a $1.2M Brookswood lot?** $22,000 — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) plus 2% on the next $1,000,000 ($20,000). (§3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a vacant lot?** Generally yes if the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided; generally no for an individual's personal-use resale, per CRA GI-003 — and the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **SSMUH eligibility in Brookswood is a servicing map, not a slogan (Township council report, June 10, 2024).** The Township's own estimate puts Brookswood at ~15% of single-family/duplex lots eligible for 3–4 units, against ~99% in Walnut Grove — because eligibility follows existing municipal water and sewer. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **A strata plan is a subdivision in the ALR — even with no building (ALC Information Bulletin 09).** Filing a bare-land strata plan on reserve land triggers the same ALC permission requirement as a conventional subdivision. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **The R-CL maximum is a LOT-area cap, not a floor-area cap (Bylaw 2500 s.403).** R-CL runs 220 m² minimum lot area to 464.5 m² maximum lot area; a "355 m²" figure appears nowhere in the bylaw. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
