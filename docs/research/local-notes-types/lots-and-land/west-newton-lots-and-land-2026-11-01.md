## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current.** Bylaw No. 18020 is never cited as current. Within the West Newton NCP set, the City states **West Newton South was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 update and is no longer in effect** — do not read old plan densities as current law. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000** — parcel zoning read in COSMOS. The research carries **no West Newton lot-size table of its own** for this slug (the zone table in the guide research is East Newton material and is **not imported here**); zone minimums for a specific lot are read from the consolidated bylaw text for that lot's zone — UNVERIFIED as a table in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units (Surrey framework, adopted Jul 8, 2024):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. SSMUH changes do **not** retroactively legalize suites built without permits. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 12000, rules-gaps verified):** one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit**; no 90 m² cap was found in the current consolidated text; a suite is not a separate strata lot. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Coach houses and garden suites (rules-gaps verified):** all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house; one extra parking space is required. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Subdivision / servicing:** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. Verified on the City's own Land Development / Servicing Agreement pages: the **Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw, worked through the Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process, which contractually defines off-site works, services and financial securities for final approval. No area servicing figure is stated — none exists in the research. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside / flood diligence:** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in **Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and vary by stream class**, with the Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit process alongside — no single riparian figure is stated for any lot here. Separately, south-end lots fall toward the Serpentine & Nicomekl lowlands: confirm the specific lot against the City's flood mapping and ask about flood insurance before subjects. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only, primary residence, under 90 consecutive days per patron, business licence at **$358 per year**. **Conflict disclosed:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350/yr plus fees — $358 is carried with $350 disclosed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R094.pdf
- **ALR block (south/west edge parcels only, where a parcel IS in the ALR):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for West Newton. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot, wherever one surfaces):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw, and older "45+" descriptions of the built Glenwood Village in the 55-plus sibling predate the current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% up to and including $200,000, 2% over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** $1,215,000 parcel (the Surrey community detached median for Sep 2026, arithmetic illustration only — NOT a West Newton or land price), fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $1,015,000 ($20,300) = **$22,300**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition: UNCONFIRMED in this file** — no primary text was opened for this slug, so no position is stated or conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there vacant residential land to buy in West Newton?** No bare-land subdivision is verified here, in this file or in the land-home guide file — the realistic residential purchase is a fee-simple house lot bought for rebuild / added units; the only other land class in the plan area is Highway 99 industrial ground. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is the 122a Street assembly a lot I can buy?** It is documented AS an assembly in listing text only (RA zoning and a transit-oriented-area designation are the listing's own claims) — not verified zoning, not a City-confirmed proposal in this research, and not inventory in this file. Verify any assembly claim against the current OCP (Bylaw 21900) and Zoning Bylaw 12000 with the City. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: How many units can my lot hold?** 3 units under 280 m², 4 over 280 m², up to 6 on eligible lots of 281 m²+ within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — and SSMUH does not retroactively legalize unpermitted suites. Confirm band and eligibility with the City. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Should I worry about flood or ALR on a south-end lot?** Check both, don't assume: the south end falls toward the dyke-protected Serpentine & Nicomekl lowlands while the residential upland is outside that floodplain, and ALR land lies south and west toward the lowlands. Confirm the lot on City flood mapping and in the ALC maps. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is the industrial edge a problem for a nearby lot?** It is a diligence item, not a verdict: verify neighbouring uses, truck routes and the residential/industrial zone line for any parcel near the Highway 99 area. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is my lot fee-simple or strata?** The house fabric is fee-simple by default and no bare-land strata plan in West Newton was verified — but tenure is read off title per parcel, and a bare-land strata lot, if one surfaces, is strata tenure with Form B, fees and bylaws. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: What will subdivision / servicing cost?** The City sets off-site works, services and securities in a Servicing Agreement under Bylaw No. 8830 after detailing — no area figure exists in the research, so any number without a City estimate for the parcel is UNCONFIRMED. (§3 above)
- **Q: Will GST apply to a lot purchase?** Depends on the seller and subdivision history at the CRA GI-003 level: individuals' personal-use sales are generally exempt; business-course sales and parcels subdivided into more than two parts are taxable examples. Get the GST position in writing. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I rely on the old West Newton plan densities?** No — West Newton South was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update; read the current OCP (Bylaw 21900) and Zoning Bylaw 12000. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which school will a home built on my lot feed into?** Only one chain is settled: 7079 148 St → T.E. Scott Elementary / Frank Hurt Secondary (single-address, non-transferable). Every other address is UNVERIFIED until its own SD36 locator run — and T.E. Scott's correct address is **7079 148 St**, not 7075 124 St. (§1 above)
