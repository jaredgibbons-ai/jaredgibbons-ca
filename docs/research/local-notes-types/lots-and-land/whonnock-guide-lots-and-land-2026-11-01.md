- **Q: Does the Lougheed Transit Corridor change what I can build in Whonnock?** No — the corridor was ADOPTED Jan 27, 2026 (Bylaw 7781-2021) and ends at 221 St, far west of Whonnock; it does not designate Whonnock parcels. (§3)
