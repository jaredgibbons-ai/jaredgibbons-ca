## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No named bare-land subdivision and no bare-land strata community is verified for Vancouver Heights — stated plainly at the top. The land story here is the fee-simple hillside house lot itself: nearly every residential lot now sits in Burnaby's citywide R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district, so an ordinary Heights lot carries stratifiable multiplex potential of 3–6 units depending on lot size and the frequent-transit test (§3) — that potential, not a lot offering, is what trades. No lot count or lot offering is verified for this sub-area and none is stated.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default and dominant form

- The sub-area's stock is detached houses on fee-simple lots — typical Burnaby residential lots, with larger and steeper view lots on the hill; Capitol Hill's upper streets are known for views over Burrard Inlet, downtown Vancouver and the North Shore mountains. A fee-simple Heights lot carries no strata, no Form B and no strata fees — title, Burnaby bylaws and any registered covenants govern. The strata supply is thin and Hastings-focused (22 attached listings against 13 detached in September 2026) and is apartment-form, counted in the condos program — never lot stock. (Research file as above)
- Multiplex potential is part of the lot fabric, stated as a rule rather than an offering: under the R1 SSMUH District (§3), lots with two or more primary units may be stratified, so future ground-oriented product on a Heights house lot can be strata product sold unit by unit — documented fresh, by name and title, if and when it is built. (Research file as above)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **City plans and zoning (Burnaby's own bylaws):** the **Burnaby 2050 OCP** (adopted December 9, 2025) governs and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict; zoning is the **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026)** — never the former 1965 Bylaw No. 4742. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one **R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District** in June 2024: **R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified.** https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite (Research file as above)
- **Subdivision + servicing:** Burnaby's subdivision-servicing bylaw base number is **UNCONFIRMED** in this program — the bylaw was renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024, and no verified base number is asserted. The process is documented instead: subdivision and servicing requirements for a Heights lot run through the City of Burnaby under its servicing bylaw as renamed; confirm the current bylaw citation and the servicing requirements for the specific application with the City before pricing a subdivision. (Program servicing ledger)
- **Pricing the multiplex test:** under R1, two otherwise similar Heights houses can be worth very different amounts to builders depending on lot area and the 400 m transit test (the R5/R2 Hastings corridor makes that test live on many lots — 6 units only near frequent transit). For Hastings-fronting and near-Hastings properties, review the Burnaby 2050 OCP land use map and the Heights Urban Village direction before assuming redevelopment or assembly value, because the new OCP takes precedence over older community plan maps. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite (Research file as above)
- **Older-stock diligence:** older homes here predate modern drainage and oil-tank rules — the research file's tip to budget for an oil tank scan and perimeter drain inspection on pre-1970s houses is carried as UNVERIFIED general guidance, not a sourced area fact, exactly as the research file flags it. - **Strata diligence (the BC strata block — applies to any strata lot, including a bare-land strata lot if one is ever met here):** Form B diligence under Strata Property Act s.59 (fees, amounts owed, approved special levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (a sub-5-lot strata — relevant to a future small R1 multiplex strata — sits UNDER that threshold; check the lot count before assuming a report exists); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (the building-directory lines for The Sienna, Heritage Green, Evergreen Place and The Heights each carry a "rentals allowed w/restrictions" element — VOID in-entry to the extent it purports to restrict long-term rentals, flagged in the siblings wherever met; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws are valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemption expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

The provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax is its own regime and is not the EHT. (§1; townhomes sibling)
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; any page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. A $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in one townhomes sibling; it omits the further 2% tier and is an OPEN summary-pass reconciliation item — this file follows **$93,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Vancouver Heights in Vancouver?** No — Burnaby. Despite the name, Vancouver Heights is a City of Burnaby neighbourhood in North Burnaby, east of Boundary Road; its market statistics are reported under Burnaby in the SnapStats GVR report, and Burnaby's taxes, bylaws and school district (SD41) apply — including **no Vancouver Empty Homes Tax**. (§1, §3.)
- **Q: Is there a bare-land subdivision or a strata lot I can buy in the Heights?** No named bare-land subdivision and no bare-land strata community is verified here. What trades is the fee-simple hillside house lot — and its R1 multiplex potential. If a listing suggests strata tenure on a detached-looking lot, tenure is a title check, not a listing-field check. (§2.)
- **Q: What is my Hastings house lot worth as a multiplex site?** Under the R1 SSMUH District, most Heights lots allow 3–6 homes — 6 only within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop on a lot of at least 281 m² — and multi-unit product may be stratified, so lot area and the transit test can make two similar houses worth very different amounts to builders. Price the multiplex value before listing as a teardown, and check the Burnaby 2050 OCP / Heights Urban Village direction for Hastings-fronting lots. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a multiplex built on a Heights lot be sold unit by unit?** Yes, where the district's conditions are met — Burnaby's R1 district provides that lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. The resulting units are strata lots: Form B diligence, fees and bylaws apply to each. (§3.)
- **Q: Which secondary school serves the Heights — Alpha or Burnaby North?** **Alpha Secondary**, on the settled SD41 locator chain: the run for 4375 Pandora St returned Rosser Elementary / Alpha Secondary, and the Capitol Hill sample on the same street (4715 Pandora St) also returned Alpha Secondary. Burnaby North Secondary is located in North Burnaby but is not the catchment secondary for those Pandora addresses. Other addresses: UNVERIFIED — run the specific address. (§1.)
- **Q: Why do two Pandora Street elementaries split one street?** Because catchment ranges, not school proximity, govern: Confederation Park Elementary (4715 Pandora St) and Rosser Elementary (4375 Pandora St) both sit on Pandora inside the sub-area, and the locator assigns the street by address range (the 4303–4375 range to Rosser; the 4715 address to Confederation Park). Confirm by address before writing school claims into an offer or listing. (§1.)
- **Q: Siena or The Sienna — what's the difference?** One letter, five blocks, 30 years: **The Sienna** (3939 Hastings St) is a 1998 resale building of 22 units, counted in the condos file; **Siena** (4451 Hastings St) is a 38-residence presale by Landa Global Properties with a spring 2028 completion. Neither is land stock; a search or offer that drops one letter lands on the wrong building. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I buy a suite at Seton Villa?** No. Seton Villa (3755 McGill St) is a non-profit retirement centre — suites you rent into or are placed into, including publicly subsidized assisted-living suites placed through the public system. There is no strata ownership to purchase, and there is no verified 55+ ownership stock in this sub-area. (§2.)
- **Q: How did Vancouver Heights sell in September 2026?** Briskly for detached, slowly for attached: detached 13 listings / 5 solds (38% sales ratio); condos & townhomes 22 active / 1 sold (5%). Community medians (Burnaby, the only level published): detached $1,825,000; condos & townhomes $686,000 — combined-category community figures, not sub-area or land figures. (§1.)
- **Q: What does PTT cost on a Heights land purchase?** On the general tiers (fully residential, no exemption): $500,000 → $8,000; $800,000 → $14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier over $3M. (§3.)
