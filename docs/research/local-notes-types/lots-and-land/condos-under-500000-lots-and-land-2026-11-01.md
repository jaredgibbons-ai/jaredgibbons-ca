## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **No single governing city frame:** this band is region-wide across many municipalities, so there is no one OCP, zoning bylaw or subdivision bylaw to cite — zoning, subdivision and servicing for any specific parcel are per-municipality, per-parcel checks, UNCONFIRMED at band level. The provincial and federal rules below are the rules that actually travel with the band.
- **The $500,000 line is a federal down-payment tier boundary (CMHC):** homes $500,000 or less require a minimum 5% down payment; homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999 require 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% on any amount over $500,000; homes $1,500,000 or more require minimum 20% down and mortgage loan insurance is not available. Mortgage loan insurance is required with less than 20% down, protects the lender not the buyer, and CMHC puts premiums at 0.6%–4.5% of the total mortgage amount. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **PTT:** 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above; further 2% on residential value over $3,000,000 (never reached in this band). Worked math in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **BC first-time home buyers' program:** where all conditions are met, exempt on the first $500,000 of purchase price (maximum saving $8,000); full exemption requires fair market value $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024), with a partial exemption between $835,000 and $860,000 — so a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere on this page is inside the full-exemption value range on price alone, though the program's other conditions still apply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **BC newly built home exemption:** full threshold $1,100,000 (effective April 1, 2024), eliminated at $1,150,000 — a qualifying newly built purchase anywhere in this band is inside that threshold on price alone; a person who paid PTT on vacant land and then has a newly built home on it may be eligible for a refund of the tax paid. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; window Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Strata block (the tenure this band's documented stock actually carries, including any bare-land strata lot):** Form B diligence (within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 incl. May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. The Province's strata buying guidance directs buyers to the Form B, Form F, bylaws and rules, fees and special levies — age, pet and rental restrictions live in those documents, not in the listing price. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners
- **Purchase prohibition:** the federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition is stated ONLY as this file's own cited sources support it — the research cites no prohibition source for this band, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and not conformed.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Why does this page stop at $500,000 — is that just a budget line?** It is a price filter, but the line also matches a federal financing tier: at $500,000 or less the minimum down payment is a flat 5%; one dollar over, the portion above $500,000 needs 10% down. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained

Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Which areas does the feed actually cover?** Confirmed in the visible feed: Langley City and White Rock only. The editorial's wider scope (Surrey, Mission, Vancouver's outer neighbourhoods and beyond) is UNVERIFIED as feed scope. https://jaredgibbons.ca/condos-under-500000.html
- **Q: Is any of this band vacant land or a building lot?** None is documented — the research documents apartment-style condo units only, and band status for any land parcel is UNCONFIRMED per parcel (§2). A lot purchase is diligenced per parcel (title, zone, ALR, servicing), not from this page.
- **Q: How much PTT at the top of the band?** Worked in §2: $8,000 at $500,000 on the general tiers, before any exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer avoid PTT on a condo on this page?** On price alone, yes — the program exempts a qualifying purchaser on the first $500,000 and its full-exemption value ceiling is $835,000; the program's other conditions still have to be met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Are these Jared Gibbons' own listings?** No — the visible cards are other brokerages' listings (Royal LePage - Wolstencroft, Stonehaus Realty Corp.), with Jared's contact block shown as site owner, not listing agent. https://jaredgibbons.ca/condos-under-500000.html
- **Q: Is GST due if I buy a lot under $500K to build on?** At CRA GI-003 level only: developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's personal-use land sale is generally exempt; finer points UNCONFIRMED. The FTHB GST rebate then reaches the new HOUSING built, not the bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What decides what a sub-$500K condo actually costs each month?** The strata documents, not the list price: Form B, Form F, bylaws, fees and special levies — at least one visible card includes electricity, gas and hot water in its maintenance fee and others do not state that, so two condos at the same price can carry very different monthly totals. https://jaredgibbons.ca/condos-under-500000.html
- **Q: Can a 55+ building sit inside this band?** Yes, as feed evidence: one visible Langley City card describes a secure 55+ community — but only 55+ age bylaws are valid in BC (SPA ss.123.1/123.2), and the restriction for that building is confirmed only from its strata documents. https://jaredgibbons.ca/condos-under-500000.html
