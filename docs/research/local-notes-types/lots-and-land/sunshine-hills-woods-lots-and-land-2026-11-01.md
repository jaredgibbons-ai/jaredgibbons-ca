## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP:** the Delta Official Community Plan 2024, consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), applies — a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules; "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw No. 8400" is an error wherever met. The former North Delta Area Plan was Schedule C.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950. OCP Map 2 shows a Small Scale Residential designation for this part of North Delta. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted:** zoning is parcel-by-parcel under the Delta zoning bylaw cited in the research and siblings as No. 7600 (2017), with the program standing correction recording a competing internal title "8400, 2025" — this file flags the contest and asserts neither as settled; confirm the bylaw and the parcel's zone sheet with the City. The RS/RD minimum-lot ladder is in Section 2. No City source names a predominant zone for Sunshine Hills Woods. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/
- **SSMUH:** Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaws (Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) permit small-scale multi-unit housing on all single-detached and duplex lots citywide, and Delta's rules allow secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites in combination with detached and duplex dwellings where the zoning applies. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/ and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — servicing bylaw UNCONFIRMED:** Delta's Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 is carried as the Delta precedent in the program record, with its adoption and parcel-level standards UNCONFIRMED on a primary source in this file's research — no parcel-level servicing standard is asserted from it. Subdivision follows the zone minimums in Section 2; confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the City for the parcel, from council / City process documents, before pricing subdivision potential.
- **Streamside / SPEA:** any property within 30 m of a stream falls in Delta's Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, which can limit additions, coach houses and rebuilds — directly relevant on Cougar Creek lots when pricing suite or garden-suite potential. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **ALR rules — framework only, no Agricultural designation documented for this ground:** where a parcel is in the ALR, subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); BC Assessment farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above, plus further tax on the residential portion above $3 million as BC sources state it; First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Bare-land strata / strata block:** no bare-land strata is verified here (Section 2), so no strata block is applied to a named lot in this file. The BC position, stated plainly for any bare-land strata lot: a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — Form B diligence (within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page), depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023, rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022, and only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there bare land for sale in Sunshine Hills Woods?** No named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified on this slug's own research. What exists is fee-simple detached lots (and strata townhouses at the edges) — price a specific lot by its zone sheet, not a land program. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How small can a lot be, and can I subdivide?** The bylaw ladder runs RS7 at 335 m² up to RS1 at 4,000 m² for single-detached, and RD4 at 504 m² up to RD1 at 836 m² / RD2–RD3 at 4,000 m² for duplex — the parcel's zone decides. Subdivision and servicing requirements are confirmed with the City; the servicing-bylaw position is UNCONFIRMED in Section 3. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Q: Can I add a suite, coach house or garden suite?** Delta's SSMUH regulations allow garden suites and coach houses in combination with single detached and duplex dwellings where the zoning applies, and SSMUH is permitted on all single-detached and duplex lots citywide under Bylaws 8394 and 8395 (adopted June 24, 2024) — confirm the parcel's zone first. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q: Does Cougar Creek cut into my buildable area?** Possibly — the creek is salmon-bearing, streamside setback detail is lot-specific, and any property within 30 m of a stream is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. Check the setback before valuing a lot's buildable area. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Q: Is Sunridge Estates land / townhouse stock in this file?** No. Sunridge Estates (6712 Baker Rd, NWS1316) counts in the North Delta assembly townhomes file, with a move-the-count annotation there — this file cross-references it only and never counts it as its own stock. (FILE STATUS, top.)
- **Q: Which OCP and zoning bylaw apply?** OCP: consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), with no lettered schedules — "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" is an error. Zoning: the number is CONTESTED (7600, 2017 vs "8400, 2025") — confirm with the City, do not quote either as settled. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Will GST apply on a vacant lot?** Generally yes if the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided; generally no for an individual's personal-use resale, per CRA GI-003. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Sunshine Hills address?** The documented locator result for 11285 Bond Blvd is Sunshine Hills Elementary / Seaquam Secondary; Nicholson Road addresses can split to Cougar Canyon Elementary — run the exact address in the SD37 locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: What are holding costs on a house lot here?** Delta taxes due July 2, 2026 (5% after, further 5% after September 2, 2026) plus the $1,658 flat-rate utility fee for a single-family home in 2026. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
