## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future" — adopted July 8, 2024, a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules.** Its land-use designations sit in its Land Use Plan (s. 1.5), and farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". **"Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" — and likewise the batch-1 "East Ladner Area Plan, Schedule B.1" framing carried into Bylaw 8400 — is an ERROR wherever met:** those schedules belonged to the superseded Bylaw 3950 structure. Neither is asserted as settled in this file; the ruling is Jared's, and a parcel's zone is read off the City's maps. (Standing Delta frame for this program.)
- **SSMUH:** Delta adopted its SSMUH zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024. The City states its provincially required updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots, with Housing Accelerator help for accessory dwelling units such as coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites. Added-unit permission is not subdivision permission, and what a specific Holly lot can build is a parcel-level City check. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Subdivision and servicing standards — status stated precisely:** the Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 is cited from civicweb / a January 2024 council report presenting it to repeal and replace Bylaw No. 7162, 2015. **Final adoption of Bylaw 8288 and the parcel-level servicing standards under it are UNCONFIRMED in this file** — confirm with the City before pricing a subdivision. Conforming-pass note: the land home file states Bylaw 8288 in governing terms; this file follows the standing correction and flags the difference, without editing the sibling.
- **Bare-land strata block (Hollyview Estates and Ashley Green, §2):** Form B (Strata Property Act s. 59) discloses the lot's strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, CRF balance and insurance summary, and must be provided within 7 days of request (max $35 plus up to 25¢/page) — in a bare-land strata it is also how a buyer learns what the strata does and does not maintain, because the house itself is the owner's responsibility. Since July 1, 2024 every strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years — Hollyview's 13 lots are inside this rule; since November 1, 2023 the CRF must receive at least 10% of the operating fund annually. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022; the only valid age bylaws are 55+ (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions), a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw, and no Holly complex is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR block (edge parcels where ALR status is confirmed for the parcel):** ALR residences cannot be stratified — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)) — and subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² total floor area; a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; one additional residence of 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or less, 186 m² or less on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 of gross farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31 for the next taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR and appears nowhere in this file as a rule. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Property transfer tax on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $700,000 → $12,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. First-time buyers' exemption: no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — both are home exemptions, and their application to a bare-land purchase is facts-specific: UNCONFIRMED for bare land in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. Anything finer for a specific Holly parcel: UNCONFIRMED. A new Skye townhome carries GST as new housing; a resale lot or home does not. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — new housing, NOT bare land. The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there bare land for sale in Holly?** No verified bare-land subdivision and no presale lot offering. The growth edge sells finished townhomes (Skye at Holly Park, counted once in its Holly home files), and the rest of the land market is improved fee-simple lots plus two bare-land strata. (§2 above)
- **Q: What is a bare-land strata lot, in plain terms?** You own your lot and your house, and the strata owns only the common property — but it is still strata tenure: strata fees, bylaws, a Form B and a depreciation report all apply to the lot. Hollyview Estates (townhouse form) and Ashley Green (half-duplex form) are Holly's two, counted in the townhomes guide file. (§2–§3 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Holly lot?** Only if the parcel's zone minimum allows it and the City's subdivision and servicing requirements are met — and the servicing bylaw's final adoption position is UNCONFIRMED in this file (Bylaw No. 8288, 2024, cited from a January 2024 council report), so confirm the current standard with the City before pricing a split. East-edge parcels also need the ALR check below. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can a Holly lot add units instead of subdividing?** The framework allows it: Delta's SSMUH updates allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots, with City support for coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites (Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024). What a specific lot can build is a parcel-level City check. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Could a lot at Holly's east edge be in the ALR?** Inside Holly, ALR is UNVERIFIED and none is confirmed in this slug's research; the documented farm belt starts east of the urban edge, designated "Agriculture (A)" in the OCP. If a parcel is in the ALR, the rule book changes entirely: no stratification, ALC approval for subdivision, and the ALC residence caps. Check the ALC mapping before subjects. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Does GST apply when I buy land here?** It turns on the seller and the parcel's history, per CRA GI-003: an individual's personal-use sale is generally exempt, while a business sale or a parcel subdivided into more than two parts can be taxable, and a developer's newly subdivided lot is generally taxable. Have the contract state the position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What will PTT cost on a Holly purchase?** At the general tiers — 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million: $700,000 → $12,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000. The first-time and newly built exemptions are home exemptions; their application to a bare lot is UNCONFIRMED here. (§3 above)
- **Q: What does it cost to hold a Holly lot?** Delta property taxes on the City's calendar (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026), plus insurance and maintenance; a bare-land strata lot adds strata fees, confirmed on the Form B, not from a listing. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Q: Which schools serve a Holly lot?** Catchments are address-based in SD37 and UNVERIFIED for any Holly address in this slug's research — Holly Elementary (4625 62 St) is physically in the sub-area, but no address's catchment is asserted here, including where listing copy asserts one.
