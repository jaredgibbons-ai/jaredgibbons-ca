## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — consolidated, NO lettered schedules.** The Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950. Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (City sheets cite No. 7600, 2017; an internal title reads "8400, 2025"). Zoning is parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. Monterra's data sheet records zoning CD201 for its parcel. https://www.cotala.com/tours/72783/FullPublic.pdf?i=1871186173
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024** — up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots in Delta (the six-unit tier is Scott Road / North Delta). Added units are NOT subdivision and do not waive any permit area. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **Subdivision / servicing — process documented, number not asserted:** a Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 (repealing No. 7162, 2015) is cited in this program from a City of Delta council report dated January 24, 2024 (File 12100-05/8288), which recommended first, second and third readings; **final adoption and parcel-level standards are UNCONFIRMED** in the sources read, so this file documents the PROCESS — subdivision runs through the City's subdivision/servicing standards under Local Government Act s. 506, confirmed with the City for the specific parcel — instead of asserting the number as governing. Sibling lots-and-land files that assert "8288, 2024" in governing terms are noted as conflicts, not followed. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands) — the core land diligence on this shore:** no building with the underside of a wooden floor system or the top of a concrete slab for habitable areas lower than **2.9 metres GSC datum**; minimum land surface elevation around construction **2.3 metres GSC datum**; no building within **7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area**. Whether a given lot falls inside SD3 needs a parcel-level (DeltaMap) check: UNVERIFIED for any individual lot here. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Dyke / flood frame:** the shore sits behind the Boundary Bay dike; Delta is the diking authority (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations, ~538 km of drainage infrastructure). **Foreshore tenure:** the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; tenure is by lease or licence only via FrontCounter BC, and private moorage is held under General Permission or Specific Permission — a "private dock" is not land the seller owns. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **ALR block (farm-edge parcels, where ALR ground is met):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval. Residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). **Farm class — three-tier, in full:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Bare-land strata / strata block (applies to any strata lot here, Monterra included):** Form B (SPA s. 59) discloses monthly fees, amounts owed, approved levies still payable, CRF balance and insurance summary, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page. Depreciation reports every 5 years for strata corporations with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, non-deferrable. CRF minimum contribution 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (long-term; short-term may still be restricted) — Monterra's data-sheet "Rentals Not Allowed" line is VOID for long-term rentals. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, incl. the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw, and Monterra's "Adult Oriented" site-influence line is not an age bylaw — no 55+ bylaw is verified for Monterra. **Fee-simple contrast:** a fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — it applies to a never-occupied new HOME, not to bare land alone. **Worked example, inputs stated:** a $1,200,000 fee-simple lot with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,000,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer / newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE. Anything finer than GI-003 for a specific Boundary Beach parcel: UNCONFIRMED — confirm with a CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself; the New Housing Rebate (36%, max $6,300, $350,000–$450,000) sits underneath it. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% — stated as the published range only; **the CMHC premium-range error documented on the mortgage-calculator page is NOT documented for this slug and is NOT asserted here.** https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** where feed product is a developer sale of a built home, the buyer must receive the disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (s. 21). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Speculation / vacancy + prohibition:** SVT taxable areas include the City of Delta; reserve/treaty/self-governing lands are excluded; the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. SVT payable on a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED. **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition: UNCONFIRMED in this file** — no primary text was opened for this slug, so no position is stated or conformed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Delta property taxes:** due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. **Foreclosure:** Delta has NO Supreme Court registry — foreclosure-registry mapping for a Delta parcel is UNVERIFIED. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Boundary Beach and build?** No verified bare-land market exists here — no subdivision selling serviced vacant lots was verified on any source read. What surfaces is built homes on fee-simple lots, strata homes at Monterra, and Southlands built product at the feed's edge. A "lot" opportunity here is usually a teardown or a rebuild on an existing fee-simple parcel, priced per parcel. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is a Monterra lot a freehold lot?** No. The MLS data sheet records Title to Land: Freehold Strata, plan LMS469 — "freehold strata" is still strata: a strata lot, Form B, fees and registered bylaws. It is counted in the townhomes file, not as land stock here. https://www.cotala.com/tours/72783/FullPublic.pdf?i=1871186173
- **Q: What flood rules touch a shore lot?** Hazard DPA SD3: 2.9 m GSC datum habitable floor level, 2.3 m minimum land elevation around construction, 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of a tidal area — behind the Boundary Bay dike Delta maintains. Confirm the lot's SD3 application with the City before design work. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is the beach / a dock part of the land?** No — the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; moorage is tenure by lease/licence via FrontCounter BC under General or Specific Permission. Price the house lot, not the water. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **Q: Can I subdivide a big waterfront lot?** Only against the parcel's own zone minimum on the City zoning maps, plus the SD3 overlay and a parcel-level DeltaMap check — zoning is parcel by parcel and no area-wide lot-size profile is published in the research. The subdivision/servicing process runs through the City under LGA s. 506 (Section 3). (§3 above)
- **Q: Does the four-unit SSMUH permission mean I can create four lots?** No — Bylaws 8394/8395 allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots; added units are not subdivision and do not create new lots. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a lot here?** At the CRA GI-003 level: an individual's personal-use vacant-land sale is generally exempt; business-use, in-the-course-of-business and more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable, and developer lots are generally taxable. The specific parcel's answer is a CPA question — UNCONFIRMED beyond GI-003. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What PTT do I pay on a $1.2M lot?** $22,000 with no exemption — $2,000 at 1% on the first $200,000 plus $20,000 at 2% on the next $1,000,000. The FTHB GST rebate does not apply to bare land — it attaches to new housing. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I rent out a Monterra home?** Long-term, yes in law: a strata rental-restriction bylaw is void province-wide since November 24, 2022, whatever a data sheet records. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Boundary Beach lot?** Address-based in SD37. The program's sample for 570 Boundary Bay Rd is South Park Elementary and South Delta Secondary — a single-address result only. Run the exact parcel address in the locator before writing a school into an offer. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
