## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**The Knight corridor has no meaningful bare-land market — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community and no presale LOT offering was verified. Land trades as fee-simple side-street house lots (frontage lots price differently from lots one block in), one completed comprehensively-zoned mixed-use site at the Kingsway / King Edward node, and two boutique townhouse assemblies at the north end. The corridor's two other named applications are proposals only — one with a staff recommendation to refuse — never inventory.**

- **Plans — local-area visions (one repealed) + Vancouver Official Development Plan:** the Kensington-Cedar Cottage, Sunset and Victoria-Fraserview / Killarney community visions apply to the local areas the corridor runs through, alongside the citywide Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026; a Knight-corridor-specific adopted plan: UNVERIFIED. - **Subdivision — Subdivision By-law No. 5208, fees by No. 14538:** Vancouver subdivision is governed by the Subdivision By-law (No. 5208), whose Schedule A sets minimum parcel width and area standards by district and sub-area category, administered by the Approving Officer; its fee schedule was most recently amended for 2026 by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026). A fee-simple split of a side-street lot must clear both R1-1 and its Subdivision By-law category — neither is assumed from a neighbouring lot's history. CD-1 ground follows its own by-law, not the R1-1 split path. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states them:** the R1-1 regime above IS Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit framework for these streets — suite and laneway permissions with a house, and the 6-unit / 8-rental-unit multiplex ceilings — and it is what prices the side-street lots around both townhouse collections. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Floodplain:** the corridor's south end descends to the Fraser River's north arm; the City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain along the Fraser River in South Vancouver, with Vancouver Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements for parcels in it. The counted stock sits at the corridor's north end, away from the river slope — a specific parcel's status: UNVERIFIED, check the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Strata diligence (King Edward Village, Knight 18 — and any bare-land strata lot, if one is ever met here):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page) — at King Edward Village the depreciation report is also where podium, parkade and retail-interface obligations surface; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw — King Edward Village's "nothing less than 30 days" listing line is exactly that enforceable short-term form); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. No corridor complex is published as 55+. For The Charles, tenure comes first: if it proves fee-simple, there is NO Form B at all — title and City rules only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no corridor ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED — the exemption to test first on a developer sale at Knight 18). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. Separately, 5% GST applies to new housing (a developer sale at Knight 18), not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%, Vancouver only:** every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration; homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year are taxed at 3% of assessed taxable value, and the 2025 payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. Confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. The City tax is separate from the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, which may also apply. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot or a new lot subdivision on the Knight corridor?** No verified one — the corridor is built arterial and side-street fabric. Land trades as built house lots and assembled development sites, and no presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Are 4028 Knight, 4078 Knight, 1432 Kingsway and 1483 King Edward different developments?** No — they are addresses within the one King Edward Village development by the Aquilini Group (398 residences over a retail podium, CD-1 (420), Freehold Strata). Price and compare them as one development's phases/faces, not as four competing sites. https://faithwilson.com/listings/516-4078-knight-street/
- **Q: Can a side-street lot hold more than one home?** Yes, where the R1-1 schedule is met — a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units, with minimum site areas from 306 m² to 557 m² by form. Knight Street frontage parcels sit under their own districts (UNVERIFIED schedule). https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Can I split a side-street lot into two fee-simple lots?** Only if both resulting parcels clear R1-1 and the Subdivision By-law No. 5208 Schedule A category for the block, through the Approving Officer — a neighbour's earlier split does not grandfather yours. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm
- **Q: Is the 23-storey tower across from King Edward Village coming?** The Conwest application for 4001–4009 Knight Street (233 strata units) was referred to public hearing with a staff recommendation to refuse it (June 3, 2025 report). Treat it as a refused-recommendation proposal, not as incoming supply or as land for sale. https://council.vancouver.ca/20250603/documents/rr10.pdf
- **Q: What is the building proposed at 1406 East King Edward?** BC Housing supportive housing — approximately 90 studio homes with supports in the fact sheet (109 modular units in the later application report), operated by non-profit societies. It is non-market housing, not homes or lots for sale. https://www.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Vancouver-East-King-Edward-Fact-Sheet.pdf
- **Q: Is The Charles strata or fee-simple — does it have a Form B?** UNCONFIRMED from citable public sources — the only strata-plan detail found rests on a directory this program does not cite, so the tenure is held open. It matters: strata means a Form B, depreciation report and CRF; fee-simple means none of those, title and City rules only. Get the disclosure statement or a title search before writing. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/the-charles-cedar-cottage-presale-vancouver-townhomes-by-medallion-hawthorne/
- **Q: How noisy / busy is a frontage lot?** Knight Street is one of Vancouver's heaviest truck and commuter arterials and part of the Highway 99 corridor via the Knight Street Bridge — frontage exposure is the corridor's defining trade-off, and a Knight-frontage home must never be comped directly against a side-street home without a frontage adjustment.
