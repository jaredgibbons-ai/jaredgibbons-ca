## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), carried verbatim as the sibling open-houses files sourced it** (this slug's own research could not re-open the source document after the 2026-11-01 fetch-service 429 and does not restate it from memory; the wording below is the sibling files' sourced wording): Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, states that a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Representation at an open house:** BCFSA states a licensee must work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party, and must disclose at the outset whether they will be able to represent the consumer as a client. Dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances — the host at an open house cannot simply "also represent" a buyer who walks in. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
