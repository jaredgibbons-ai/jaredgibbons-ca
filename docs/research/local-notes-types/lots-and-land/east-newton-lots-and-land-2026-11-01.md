## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current.** East Newton North was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 update and is no longer in effect, and East Newton South's and the Business Park's designations were superseded by that update — confirm a lot's current OCP designation in COSMOS rather than relying on an NCP label alone. Bylaw No. 18020 is never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000** — parcel zoning, designations and servicing are confirmed per parcel in the City's COSMOS mapping. No sub-area lot-size table is documented in this research — UNVERIFIED as a table here. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units (Surrey framework, from Jul 8, 2024):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, on eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary. The Jul 8, 2024 changes renamed RF / RF-SS to R3, RF-12 / RF-12C / RF-13 to R4, and RF-SD to R6 — a listing quoting an old zone name is using pre-2024 wording. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide. The changes do not retroactively legalize a suite or coach house built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 12000):** one secondary suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit; no 90 m² cap was found in the current consolidated text; a suite is not a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Coach houses and garden suites:** all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; the unit must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house; one extra parking space is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Subdivision / servicing:** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. Verified on the City's own Land Development / Servicing Agreement pages in an earlier program batch: the **Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw, worked through the City's Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside / riparian:** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, with different setbacks for various stream types, complemented by the Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit process — Hyland Creek and Bear Creek bound this area, so a parcel near either is checked against its stream class in COSMOS and Part 7A. No single setback figure is stated, because the bylaw sets them by class. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only — only the property owner may host from their primary residence, tenants cannot operate one, for periods of less than 90 consecutive days per patron, with a business licence at **$358 per year**. **Conflict disclosed:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposing the amendments put the licence at $350/yr plus fees — direct readers to the City licence page before quoting either figure. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **ALR block (provincial frame — no sub-area ALR parcel is documented, stated so it is not misapplied):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for this sub-area. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot, wherever one surfaces):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example (anchor):** $1,200,000 fully residential parcel, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $1,000,000 = **$22,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition: UNCONFIRMED in this file** — no primary text was opened for this slug, so no position is stated or conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant building lot in East Newton?** No named bare-land subdivision or lot offering was verified here. What trades as land is the existing fabric — detached house lots in a largely built-out NCP area, checked parcel by parcel in COSMOS. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is the old East Newton North / South plan still what governs my lot?** No — East Newton North was rescinded and the South and Business Park designations were superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update; read the current OCP designation and zone in COSMOS. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can a house lot here add a suite or a coach house?** One secondary suite per dwelling unit, capped at 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area and never inside a coach house or garden suite; a coach house or garden suite of 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR, lane access required on smaller lots, one extra parking space). (§3 above)
- **Q: My listing says R3 / RF-12 — which zone name is current?** R3 — the Jul 8, 2024 SSMUH changes renamed RF / RF-SS to R3, RF-12 group to R4 and RF-SD to R6. An old name in listing text is pre-2024 wording. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide, or build multiple units instead?** Different permissions: SSMUH allows 3–4 units on most lots by size band (up to 6 near a frequent bus stop), but that does not create new lots; subdivision must meet the zone minimum in Bylaw No. 12000 and the servicing requirements under Bylaw No. 8830, confirmed with the City per parcel. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is a lot here fee-simple or strata?** The detached fabric is fee-simple by default and no bare-land strata plan in this sub-area was verified — but tenure is read off title per parcel, and a bare-land strata lot, if one surfaces, is strata tenure with Form B, fees and bylaws. **Sullivan Mews is neither** — it is membership tenure and is excluded everywhere. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: What creek diligence applies near Hyland Creek or Bear Creek?** The streamside check: setbacks sit in Part 7A of Bylaw No. 12000 and vary by stream class, with the Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit process alongside — read the parcel's class in COSMOS before design work. (§3 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a lot purchase here?** PTT on the general tiers — on the §3 anchor example ($1,200,000) the bill is $22,000 with no exemption. GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: How did East Newton sell in September 2026?** Detached 120 listings, 7 solds, 6% ratio; "Condos & Townhomes" 132 listings, 11 solds, 8% — both in Surrey's slower half that month. Those are all-property figures, community context only; SnapStats publishes no land category and no sub-area medians. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Which school will a home on my lot feed into — is it East Newton Elementary?** There is no such school — **"East Newton Elementary" does not exist** in the Ministry file or SD36's directory. SD36 catchments are address-based and East Newton's locator run returned no result, so no catchment is claimed; run the exact address in the SD36 locator. (§1 above)
