## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **The $1.5 million line is also the federal insured-mortgage line (CMHC):** mortgage loan insurance is required when a homebuyer makes a down payment of less than 20% of the purchase price; the insurance protects the lender, not the buyer, and lets a buyer qualify with a down payment as low as 5%. Minimum down payment: homes $500,000 or less — 5%; homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999 — 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% on any amount over $500,000; homes $1,500,000 or more — minimum 20% down, and mortgage loan insurance is not available. CMHC's published premium range is 0.6%–4.5% of the total mortgage amount, payable in full at purchase or added to the mortgage. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Property transfer tax — the base calculation:** PTT is payable when a person purchases or gains an interest in property registered at the Land Title Office, unless an exemption applies; it is based on the fair market value of the property (land and improvements) on the day of registration, and it is not annual property tax. General rates: 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000; 2% of the portion over $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000; 3% of the portion over $2,000,000; plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000 where that applies. (The "Over $3M — 5%" shorthand sometimes shown for that top band is a compression of the 3% tier plus the further 2% — flagged here as a compression, not a separate tier.) Additional PTT for foreign nationals, foreign corporations and taxable trustees applies in specified areas; its rate was not re-read for the research file and is UNVERIFIED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Worked PTT example — input $1,500,000 (the band ceiling, general tiers only, no exemption):** 1% × $200,000 = $2,000; 2% × ($1,500,000 − $200,000 = $1,300,000) = $26,000; total PTT = $28,000. No 3% tier is reached (it starts above $2,000,000) and the further 2% over $3,000,000 does not apply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Worked PTT example — input $1,200,000 (general tiers only, no exemption):** 1% × $200,000 = $2,000; 2% × ($1,200,000 − $200,000 = $1,000,000) = $20,000; total PTT = $22,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Worked PTT example — input $1,100,000 (general tiers only, no exemption):** 1% × $200,000 = $2,000; 2% × ($1,100,000 − $200,000 = $900,000) = $18,000; total PTT = $20,000. (At exactly this price a qualifying newly built purchase can instead attract the full newly built home exemption below — the two figures are alternatives, not cumulative.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **BC first-time home buyers' program (PTT exemption):** where all conditions are met, the purchaser is exempt from PTT on the first $500,000 of the purchase price (maximum saving $8,000); full exemption requires fair market value $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024), 0.5 hectares or smaller, only residential improvements, principal-residence use only, with a partial exemption between $835,000 and $860,000; the buyer must move in within 92 days of registration and continuously occupy to the first anniversary. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **BC newly built home exemption (PTT):** full-exemption threshold $1,100,000 (effective April 1, 2024), phasing out over the $50,000 above it and completely eliminated at $1,150,000 for qualifying purchasers; a person who paid PTT on vacant land and then has a newly built home on it may be eligible for a refund of the tax paid — the documented vacant-land refund route. Band consequence: a qualifying buyer under $835,000 (resale) or under $1,100,000 (newly built) pays materially less PTT than the general tiers alone suggest, and a buyer above $1,150,000 gets neither exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Anything finer than GI-003 level is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST/HST rebate — new HOUSING, not bare land:** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent March 13, 2026 (CRA News No. 122). The rebate is 100% of the GST (or federal part of the HST) to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, for agreements entered March 20, 2025–2031; buyers apply via Form GST190 (builder purchases) or GST191 (owner-built). It applies to newly constructed or substantially renovated HOUSING — a bare-land purchase itself attracts no FTHB rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **GST/HST new housing rebate:** 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, on homes $350,000–$450,000 (phase-out range), for a buyer (or related person) who uses the housing as a primary place of residence and is the first to live in it, where all requirements are met. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/campaigns/cra-guide-taxes-housing-benefits.html
- **FHSA:** $40,000 lifetime limit, $8,000 annual participation room; a qualifying FHSA withdrawal and a Home Buyers' Plan withdrawal can both be made for the same qualifying home if all conditions are met at each withdrawal. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Home Buyers' Plan:** withdraw up to $60,000 from an RRSP to buy or build a qualifying home, repaid within the published repayment period — with the 2026–2028 repayment-relief deferral as the program states it for the relief window. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/campaigns/cra-guide-taxes-housing-benefits.html
- **Home owner grant (annual, not closing money):** it reduces annual property tax on a principal residence and is not purchase money in this band; the 2026 grant threshold is $2,075,000 for a full grant, and $0 over $2,189,000 — every purchase in this band sits under that threshold on price alone, but the grant is claimed annually by an occupying owner, not at completion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Strata block (for a purchase in this band that is a strata lot, including a bare-land strata lot):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for a parcel in this band on ALR land):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Purchase prohibition:** the federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition is stated in this file ONLY as its own research supports it: the research encodes no prohibition position for this band, so none is asserted.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Why does this page stop at $1.5 million — is that just an asking-price filter?** It is a price filter, but the line also matches a federal financing rule: CMHC states homes $1,500,000 or more require a minimum 20% down payment and mortgage loan insurance is not available, while homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999 need only 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% on the rest. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Q: Which areas does the under-$1.5M feed cover?** On the first page read for the research, Surrey (Sullivan Station, West Newton, King George Corridor, Queen Mary Park Surrey, Bear Creek Green Timbers) and Langley (Willoughby Heights, Langley City, Aldergrove Langley); whether the full feed reaches further is UNVERIFIED, because only the first page was read. https://jaredgibbons.ca/homes-under-1500000.html

Jared's own listings are on the site's our-listings page. https://jaredgibbons.ca/homes-under-1500000.html
- **Q: How much PTT would I pay at the top of this band?** Worked example, input $1,500,000, general tiers only: 1% × $200,000 = $2,000 plus 2% × $1,300,000 = $26,000, total $28,000 — nothing in this band reaches the 3% tier or the further 2% over $3,000,000, and a BC exemption could reduce the bill further if you qualify. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: How much PTT on a $1.2 million or $1.1 million purchase in this band?** Worked examples, general tiers only: at $1,200,000 — $2,000 + (2% × $1,000,000 = $20,000) = $22,000; at $1,100,000 — $2,000 + (2% × $900,000 = $18,000) = $20,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: If I buy land under $1.5M, is its band status settled?** No — band status is UNCONFIRMED per parcel in this file: the page is a homes feed, no land roster is documented, and whether a specific parcel is priced inside the band, and whether an insured purchase is available for it, is confirmed per parcel with the lender, not from the band page. https://jaredgibbons.ca/homes-under-1500000.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Q: Do the BC exemptions help inside this band?** Yes, at its lower end: a qualifying first-time buyer at $835,000 or less is exempt on the first $500,000 (partial exemption to $860,000), and a qualifying newly built purchase gets a full exemption to $1,100,000 (eliminated at $1,150,000) — a buyer above $1,150,000 gets neither. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Q: Is GST payable if I buy a lot in this band to build on?** At CRA GI-003 level only: developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's personal-use land sale is generally exempt; finer points are UNCONFIRMED here. The FTHB GST rebate then reaches the new HOUSING built on the lot, not the bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: As a seller, does pricing just under $1.5M matter?** Yes — a list price just under $1,500,000 keeps the home inside the insured-mortgage universe for buyers with less than 20% down; a price at or over it removes that buyer group entirely, because insurance is not available at $1,500,000 or more. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
