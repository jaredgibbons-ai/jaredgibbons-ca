### ALR farm land — its own land form, beside the pocket

- **AG1 framework, as the bylaw states it:** for farm land beside the pocket, Agriculture (AG1) in Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 Section 14.1 provides for farming and compatible uses consistent with the Agricultural Land Reserve, one principal dwelling unit per lot, and a secondary suite as a secondary use. Subdivision in the ALR is not permitted unless approved by the Agricultural Land Commission, and where that approval is not required the minimum lot area is 2.0 ha; single detached housing is not to be built on a lot under 828.0 m² in that zone. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=53061
- **ALR vs non-ALR split:** the pocket's house lots are non-ALR residential ground; the farm land to the west and south is ALR ground under AG1. No parcel count or percentage split for Terra Nova is stated in the research — none is invented; ALR status for a specific parcel is confirmed on ALC mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000,** with Schedule 2.2B Thompson (Terra Nova) as the sub-area plan listed by the City for this ground. https://Richmond.Ca/city-hall/strategies/sale.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Zoning — Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500:** residential pocket lots under their parcel zones (confirmed lot by lot); farm land under Agriculture AG1, Section 14.1 (one principal dwelling per lot, secondary suite as a secondary use, 2.0 ha subdivision minimum where ALC approval is not required, no single detached housing on a lot under 828.0 m² in that zone). https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=53061
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states it (non-ALR lots only):** at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. AG1 farm lots follow the ALR framework below instead — do not price SSMUH potential into an ALR parcel. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision follows the parcel's zone; in the ALR it is not permitted unless approved by the ALC (below). The City's own alphabetical bylaw list carries "Subdivision and Development — Bylaw 8751 (2011)" and "Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 (2011)" (freshly confirmed on the City's bylaw list in the L3 batch) — Richmond's subdivision/servicing instrument is Bylaw 8751, with cost recovery under Bylaw 8752; parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **ALR rules (the farm-land framework):** subdivision in the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC guidance allows one principal residence and, if the regulation conditions are met, one additional residence per parcel — caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Separate the residential pocket from the farm file before pricing: the ALC rules do not apply the same way to the non-ALR house lots. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Flood / dike diligence — Bylaw 8204:** dike setbacks and the flood plain construction level (Schedule B) apply; the pocket sits behind the 1:500-design perimeter system with dike raising under way. Get the parcel-level position early on west- and north-edge purchases. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **Heritage diligence:** the Terra Nova Heritage Area is on the City Heritage Inventory — heritage status is a lot-level diligence item for parcels in or beside the inventoried area. https://richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/inventory/Details.aspx?ID=160
- **Strata block — applies to the built townhouse ground (Quilchena Green and peers), NOT to bare land here:** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (covenant-based rental obligations, where registered, are a separate position — see the Omega context in Section 2); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children). **Quilchena Green's age label is FLAGGED and never resolved in this program (45+ in a 2016 MLS sheet vs 55+ in current listings) — and a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw in any event; the registered bylaw on the Form B governs.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — neither a pocket lot nor an ALR parcel purchase alone earns it. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Terra Nova" means "new land" — and the older name is on the school:** Indigenous Peoples used Terra Nova for seasonal fishing camps during salmon and sturgeon runs and the area was named Spul'u'kwuks; Newfoundland fishermen later renamed it Terra Nova, and the Terra Nova Cannery operated there until the middle of the 20th century. The elementary school in the pocket still carries the Spul'u'kwuks name. https://www.richmondmuseum.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Terra-Nova_Self-Guided-Historical-Tour-Brochure.pdf
- **The rural park is a no-dogs park:** Terra Nova Rural Park (63 acres, Sharing Farm, Adventure Play Environment, Pacific Flyway trails) is a NO DOGS ALLOWED area under Animal Control Bylaw 8612 — a real factor for dog-owning buyers pricing dyke-side lots, and one listing copy rarely mentions. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?id=80
- **The foreshore is inside the area description:** the City Heritage Inventory's Terra Nova description includes the foreshore outside the dyke to the north and west, beside the Sturgeon Bank Wildlife Management Area — edge "land" there is foreshore and habitat ground under the dike framework, not buildable lot area. https://richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/inventory/Details.aspx?ID=160
