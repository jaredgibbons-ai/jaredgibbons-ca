## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Sumas Mountain rises between Abbotsford and Chilliwack, above Sumas Prairie to the south and the Fraser River lowland to the north. The built part is inside the City of Abbotsford; the upper and eastern rural part is unincorporated Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) land next to the City of Abbotsford/City of Chilliwack boundary. http://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/sumas-mountain.html
- "Sumas Mountain" is the FVREB sub-area name in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, and the City uses it for the mountainside neighbourhoods on the west and south slopes, including the McKee plan area; the FVRD plan for the rural part is the Sumas Mountain Official Community Plan, Consolidated OCP Bylaw No. 584. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/projects
- Neighbourhoods inside or at the foot: Auguston, McKee and the Eagle Mountain side on the City slopes; Clayburn Village at the northwest foot. Nearby areas: Abbotsford East (west/southwest foot), Sumas Prairie (south), Chilliwack (east side). https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- Main roads are Sumas Mountain Road (becoming Upper Sumas Mountain Road toward the regional park), McKee Road, Dawson Road and Whatcom Road, connecting down to Highway 1 at the Whatcom Road interchange at the south foot. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/new-park-dianne-brook-ave
- Exact Paragon boundary line: UNVERIFIED in the research.
- Schools on the slopes: Auguston Traditional Elementary (36367 Stephen Leacock Drive), Mountain Elementary (2299 Mountain Drive), Irene Kelleher Totí:ltawtxw Elementary (2540 Eagle Mountain Drive); Clayburn Middle (35139 Laburnum Avenue) at the northwest foot; no secondary school is on the mountain. Settled SD34 locator runs (documented in the research) SPLIT: 36367 Stephen Leacock Drive → Auguston Traditional Elementary → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary; 2540 Eagle Mountain Drive → Irene Kelleher Totí:ltawtxw Elementary → William A. Fraser Middle → Yale Secondary. There is no single Sumas Mountain catchment — run the exact address. https://mountain.abbyschools.ca/schoolplan/
- Transit: BC Transit routes 7 (Sumas Mountain), 9 (McKee) and 15 (Auguston) serve the built slopes/foot; no route to the upper mountain trailheads was confirmed. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=7

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Sumas Mountain DOES have a documented bare-land strata — Straiton — and that is the centre of this file. Beyond Straiton, land trades as fee-simple City slope lots and large FVRD rural parcels. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision and no presale lot offering was verified. The McKee proposal below is a rezoning-stage development proposal, documented as such — never presented as for-sale inventory.**

### Bare-land strata — its own form, documented here as land stock

- **Straiton / Straiton Mountain Estates — 4595 Sumas Mountain Road. BARE-LAND STRATA (Strata Plan EPS1299).** A City council report describes the Straiton strata subdivision at this address (owner Straiton Development Corporation) as 26 single-family strata lots on the west side of Sumas Mountain Road, south of Dawson Road — a bare-land-style strata of detached houses, not a townhome or condo building. Stated plainly: a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — the buyer owns a strata lot, pays strata fees, is bound by the strata bylaws, and diligence runs through the Form B, exactly as with any strata; what differs is that the lot itself is bare land / a detached-house lot rather than a unit in a shared building envelope. A listing describing the wider community as a "gated 42 lot" community conflicts with the City report's 26 strata lots — the wider total is UNCONFIRMED; the strata-plan figure relied on here is the City's 26. Fees, bylaws detail, and rental/pet/age restrictions: UNCONFIRMED — read the filed bylaws with the Form B. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/45965/ (Basis also: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/sumas-mountain-condos-2026-11-01.md, §2)

### Fee-simple land — the default form everywhere else

- **City slope lots (Auguston, McKee, Eagle Mountain side):** single detached homes in built City neighbourhoods on the west and southwest slopes — fee-simple lots in the ordinary form. Lot character follows the City rural/suburban zones where they apply: Country Residential (CR) minimum lot 2.0 ha, Suburban Residential (SR) minimum lot 0.4 ha, Rural Residential (RR) minimum lot 4.0 ha under Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014. These are zone minimums for the zones named, not a claim that every slope lot is that size — urban slope lots inside the built neighbourhoods sit under their own zones, UNCONFIRMED lot-by-lot here. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- **FVRD rural parcels (upper/eastern mountain):** rural/country-residential homes on larger lots under the FVRD Sumas Mountain OCP (Bylaw No. 584). The rural part has no public sewer or water at rural density — homes use wells and onsite (septic) services. FVRD zoning minimum lot sizes: UNVERIFIED (the bylaw text is not hosted on a readable FVRD source, per the research) — no FVRD lot-size figure is invented here. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014

Servicing for any single City-side vacant lot: confirm with the City — UNCONFIRMED parcel-by-parcel in this file.

- **Slope/open-space character:** the McKee plan area's open space network is made up of streamside setbacks, critical habitat, geotechnical areas and slopes over 40%, with trail corridors in the McKee Trail Strategy — a material share of mountainside "land" is protected open space, not buildable lot area. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Two governments, two rule sets — confirm which governs the lot first.** City side: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 (CR 2.0 ha / SR 0.4 ha / RR 4.0 ha minimums above) and Abbotsforward 2050 OCP (CURRENT, adopted December 2025; stale "2600-2016"/"2500-2016" citations flagged). Rural side: FVRD Sumas Mountain OCP, Bylaw No. 584. The McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023) is Schedule E of the City OCP and guides the west-slope mountainside neighbourhood. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **SSMUH / suites:** City-side SSMUH adoption is City-confirmed (June 2024) but the bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED — "3560-2024" must not be asserted. Secondary suite / SSMUH rules for the rural FVRD part: UNVERIFIED in the research — do not import Abbotsford's rules (suite cap under 50%; coach/garden 15% of lot to max 100 m² — Abbotsford ONLY) onto FVRD ground.
- **Steep-slope / geotechnical diligence:** the City OCP includes a Steep Slope Development Permit Area for slope land, and the McKee plan protects unstable geotechnical areas and slopes over 40% as open space. Budget for geotechnical review on slope lots before subjects come off. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** City-side subdivision follows the zone minimums above plus City servicing — detail beyond the minimums is UNCONFIRMED in the research and is not filled in. FVRD-side: no public water/sewer at rural density (well/septic); FVRD subdivision minimums UNVERIFIED (Section 2). Confirm with the governing authority for the parcel.
- **Bare-land strata rules (Straiton — the BC strata block applies in full to the lot):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (Straiton at 26 lots is inside the requirement); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children). No Straiton age restriction is verified — do not market it as 55+ or all-ages on this file's evidence; read the bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (east-side/Agriculture-designated ground where ALR applies):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval as the ALC states it; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)) — note the contrast: Straiton is a lawful bare-land strata because its ground is City mountainside strata ground as documented, NOT an ALR-residence stratification. ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Flood position:** the mountain itself is above the floodplain; the floodplain land is at its foot in Sumas Prairie. Slope lots trade flood risk for geotechnical risk — diligence follows the slope rules above, not floodplain setbacks. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1%/$200K, 2% to $2M, 3% above, +2% on residential portion above $3M; FTHB Program (first $500,000 exempt at ≤$835,000, max $8,000, partial to $860,000) and Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** most individual personal-use sales exempt; business-use / in-the-course-of-business / more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally taxable (CRA GI-003). This matters directly for a newly created lot (e.g. any future McKee-plan lot sold by a developer) vs a resale Straiton lot from an individual — confirm per transaction. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Straiton or slope lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36%/$6,300/$350K–$450K. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000/$8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Sumas Mountain–specific position documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here.
- **Taxes — TWO regimes on one mountain:** City part — Abbotsford taxes due July 2, 2026, 5% penalty July 3, 2026, second 5% September 16, 2026. FVRD/rural part — the owner receives the notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes and pays the Province, not the FVRD; under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11, rural taxes are due July 2, with 5% added to any portion unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on any portion unpaid October 31. Do not quote City dates for an FVRD parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Quarry diligence:** five quarries operate on Sumas Mountain and gravel trucks use an access road to Highway 1 through the Lightning Rock site (Semá:th First Nation statement); a City report covers extraction to the 170 m elevation at Jamieson Quarry for Mainland Sand & Gravel Ltd. Truck traffic on mountain roads is a lot-level diligence question. https://sumasfirstnation.com/press-releases/sacred-land-returned-to-semath/ and https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/40562/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What does "bare-land strata" at Straiton actually mean for me?** You own a strata lot (EPS1299, 26 lots per the City report), pay strata fees, follow the bylaws, and get a Form B on resale — but your lot is a detached-house lot, not a unit in a shared building. There is no shared roof/envelope the way a townhouse strata has. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/45965/
- **Q: Why do listings say 42 lots when the City says 26?** The 42 figure is a listing description of the wider community; the City report's strata-plan figure is 26 single-family strata lots. The wider total is UNCONFIRMED — rely on the plan/Form B. (Section 2.)
- **Q: City lot or FVRD lot — how do I tell, and why care?** The governing plan, servicing, and even who you pay taxes to all change at the boundary (City vs FVRD Bylaw 584 / Surveyor of Taxes). Confirm the parcel's jurisdiction before relying on any rule in this file. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Can I build on a steep McKee-area lot?** Possibly — but Steep Slope DPA guidelines apply and slopes over 40% / unstable geotechnical areas are protected open space in the McKee plan. Get geotechnical advice before subjects. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **Q: Is there water and sewer on the upper mountain?** No — the FVRD rural part has no public water/sewer at rural density; homes use wells and septic. Check the GWELLS well record and Fraser Health sewerage filing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Q: Can I subdivide a large rural parcel?** City-side minimums run 0.4 ha (SR) to 4.0 ha (RR) by zone; FVRD minimums are UNVERIFIED here, and any ALR parcel needs ALC approval to subdivide. No subdivision promise can be made from this file — get the zone/jurisdiction confirmed. (Sections 2–3.)
- **Q: When will McKee lots be for sale?** UNCONFIRMED — the McKee Road lands are a July 2025 council-consideration proposal (~108 detached lots proposed), not a sales program. No presale was verified. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2025-07/2025-07-10%20City%20Page.pdf
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a new lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What are holding costs on a mountain lot?** Taxes under the correct regime (City July 2 / 5% + 5% Sept 16, 2026, or rural July 2 / 5% + 5% Oct 31), plus strata fees if the lot is a Straiton bare-land strata lot, plus well/septic costs on rural ground. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve Eagle Mountain vs Auguston?** They split — the two settled locator chains are in Section 1 and they go to different middle/secondary schools. Run your exact address. (Section 1.)
