## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Scope is the whole City of Mission: properties sold under the BC Supreme Court foreclosure process — by court order with conduct of sale given to a party (usually the lender's side), or heading toward a final order of foreclosure. In BC a "court-ordered sale" is a sale inside a foreclosure proceeding, not a separate process; "bank foreclosure" is a misnomer — sales run through the court, not the bank. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- Mission is the smallest market in its batch: 353 total listings in September 2026 across both property types, so a Mission foreclosure feed will normally hold very few listings at a time. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Step 1 — petition:** a foreclosure/redemption proceeding must be started by petition in the Supreme Court — Rule 21-7(1); all persons whose interest is sought to be extinguished must be respondents — Rule 21-7(2); the petitioner must serve the court's foreclosure information notice, Form 125 — Rule 21-7(4.1). Rule 21-7 as read states no demand step before the petition; a prescribed-mortgage-terms demand is UNVERIFIED in this slug's research. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Order / redemption:** the court may order payment of what is due within a redemption period the court fixes — Rule 21-7(5)(b) (no fixed length in the Rules); it may certify the amount due — Rule 21-7(5)(c),(e). In default, a final order of foreclosure may be granted — Rule 21-7(6). A party of record may apply at any time for an order that the property be sold — Rule 21-7(7). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Confirming a sale:** even before redemption expires, the person with conduct of sale may apply for an order confirming the sale, directing proceeds and vesting title — Rule 21-7(9). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Conduct of sale / Form 60 / PD-66 (settled treatment; this slug's research held these UNVERIFIED in its own pass — see FILE STATUS):** conduct of sale and court directions (reserve/minimum price, bid and offer rights, particulars) sit in Rule 13-5 (sale may be by contract conditional on court approval — Rule 13-5(4)(b)); the result of a sale by court order is certified in Form 60 — Rule 13-5(6); Practice Direction PD-66 governs foreclosure proceedings. Bid timing/manner under PD-66 for a specific file: get the conduct-of-sale party's written instructions for that file. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo106/loo106/168_2009_02
- **Mission city frame:** OCP = **Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026**, which replaced and repealed OCP Bylaw 6350-2025 and is substantively the same plan, brought forward to address a procedural matter and to formally incorporate a Regional Context Statement. Mission Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 governs zoning. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/notice-public-hearing-april-7-2026
- **Mission taxes (city ground only):** due July 2, 2026; a **FLAT 10% penalty** is added to current balances outstanding (including unclaimed home owner grants) on July 3, 2026 — there is no second staged penalty on city ground. This is NOT a staged 5% + 5% regime. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Rural contrast (not city ground):** Mission-area land in FVRD electoral areas sits under the separate rural regime — 5% after July 2 and a further 5% on October 31 under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11. Do not apply the city's flat 10% to rural parcels, or the rural staging to city parcels. (Standing program correction, 2026-11-01)
- **ALR (Mission parcels where present, notably the outlying ground):** subdivision needs ALC approval; residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, by Oct 31) — flat "$2,500+" is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Form B availability on a court sale: UNCONFIRMED.** Ordinary-resale strata block (which may or may not be reachable on a court sale): Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation 5-yr cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; 10% CRF since Nov 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). Do not promise a Form B package for a court-ordered bare-land strata lot until the specific file confirms what is produced. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT/GST treatment of court sales: UNCONFIRMED** — neither filled in. Kept distinct from the general frames: (a) general PTT — 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% above, +2% on residential portion over $3M; FTHB PTT exemption on the first $500,000, full at ≤$835,000 (max $8,000, partial to $860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (new-HOME; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED); (b) general GST on vacant land at CRA GI-003 level only — developer/business and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE, individual's personal-use land generally exempt, finer points UNCONFIRMED. Whether either frame applies unchanged to a court-confirmed purchase must be confirmed by the buyer's lawyer for the specific transaction. At Mission's Sep 2026 medians, FTHB PTT relief is a live question on many attached purchases. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How does a BC foreclosure start?** By petition under Rule 21-7(1), with all interest-holders as respondents and Form 125 served with the petition. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: How long is the redemption period?** No fixed length — the period the court fixes under Rule 21-7(5)(b). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: Is my accepted offer final?** No — conduct of sale applies under Rule 21-7(9) for confirmation, proceeds direction and vesting, even before redemption expires; until that order, the sale is not final. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: Can someone outbid me at approval?** Treat it as a real possibility: bid/offer rights sit in the court's Rule 13-5 directions and PD-66 practice — get the specific file's bid instructions in writing before spending on inspections. (§3.)
- **Q: Where do I check a Mission court file — and which registry holds it?** Court Services Online eSearch (class "H - Foreclosure") shows file number, registry, parties and documents. Mission has NO Supreme Court registry and the serving-registry mapping is UNVERIFIED — read the registry off the file itself; do not assume Abbotsford or Chilliwack. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- **Q: Will I get a Form B on a bare-land strata lot in foreclosure?** UNCONFIRMED — no source in the research states a general rule; what is produced is a per-file fact. (§3.)
- **Q: Do I pay PTT/GST the normal way on court-ordered land?** UNCONFIRMED — court-sale tax treatment is not stated in any source in the research; the general PTT and GI-003 frames in §3 are starting frames only, confirmed by your lawyer per transaction.
- **Q: Are foreclosures discounted in Mission?** No foreclosure discount statistic exists in any official source — UNVERIFIED. General-market context only, with the small-sample warning: attached median $430,000 on 10 solds (Sep 2026) says nothing about any individual court sale. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: What tax penalty applies to arrears on a Mission lot?** On city ground, one FLAT 10% penalty after the July 2 due date; on FVRD rural ground, the separate 5% + 5% (Oct 31) rural staging — confirm which jurisdiction the parcel sits in first. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Q: As a seller in difficulty, what is my window?** The court-fixed redemption period is the window to pay what is due or sell before a final order can be granted under Rule 21-7(6) — earlier advice is worth more. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
