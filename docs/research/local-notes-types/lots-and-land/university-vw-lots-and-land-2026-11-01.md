## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Municipality: none — the key identity fact. The UEL is an unincorporated community between the City of Vancouver and UBC on the Point Grey Peninsula; it is the only part of BC where the provincial government provides local services directly to an urban area, it has no elected municipal council, and the Minister of Municipal Affairs governs it under the University Endowment Land Act. UBC's campus lands are governed by the university. "University VW" is the GVR board sub-area name (VW = Vancouver West). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/local-governments/improvement-districts-governance-bodies/university-endowment-lands
- For Metro Vancouver purposes the UEL sits in Electoral Area A; day-to-day services are delivered by the UEL Administration, guided by the UEL Official Community Plan and the Land Use, Building and Community Administration Bylaw. The UEL comprises four areas (A, B, C and D) and has a population of nearly 4,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/local-governments/improvement-districts-governance-bodies/university-endowment-lands and https://www.universityendowmentlands.gov.bc.ca/library/Reports/Realtor%20Information%202026-03-12.pdf

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**University VW has no fee-simple bare-land market on UBC ground at all, and no lot program on UEL ground — stated plainly at the top. The land itself is never sold on the UBC side: residential properties on UBC's endowment lands are never sold outright, but are leased as prepaid 99-year leases, so what trades is a leasehold interest in a strata home, not land. On the UEL side, what trades is a built freehold house on its lot on the older streets. No named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan, and no presale lot offering was verified.**

### UBC ground — leasehold only, never freehold land stock

- **The 99-year prepaid leasehold structure (confirmed from a UBC source):** UBC Campus and Community Planning's Wesbrook Place case study states that residential and commercial properties on UBC's endowment lands are never sold outright, but are leased as prepaid 99-year leases, with the net revenue funding UBC's endowment. The remaining term on any home runs from the project's original lease date, not from the buyer's purchase date — Hampton Place (1990s, UBC's first campus neighbourhood) carries the shortest remaining terms; Wesbrook Place is the largest and newest. https://elementslab.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Wesbrook-Study_reducedsize_secure2.pdf and https://ubcproperties.com/history/
- **What the leasehold stock is:** almost all strata condos and townhouses in the planned neighbourhoods (Chancellor Place, Hawthorn Place, Hampton Place, Wesbrook Village), documented in full in the condos and townhomes siblings — cross-reference only here, never re-documented as land. Named Wesbrook Village buildings in the research include Eton by Polygon and Wesbrook Properties' rental buildings (Georgia Point, Vista Point, Granite Terrace, Mews) — built housing, not lots. https://polyhomes.com/community/eton/ (Siblings: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/university-vw-condos-2026-11-01.md; ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/university-vw-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)

### Bare-land strata — its own form

- **No bare-land strata plan in University VW was verified.** The UBC neighbourhood strata are conventional (building) strata on leasehold land, not bare-land strata. Stated plainly so the form is never mis-sold: a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure (Form B / strata fees / bylaws apply) — but no such plan is documented for this ground. The BC strata block that applies to the leasehold strata stock generally: depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for strata corporations with 5 or more units since July 1, 2024; a minimum 10% contingency reserve fund contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions) — which is why Tapestry's MLS-wording 55+ position above remains UNSETTLED until a Form B is read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Governing frame — UEL (provincial), not a city:** the UEL is governed under the University Endowment Land Act by the Minister of Municipal Affairs, administered by the UEL Administration under the UEL Official Community Plan and the Land Use, Building and Community Administration Bylaw. There is no municipal council, no city zoning bylaw and no city subdivision bylaw for UEL ground. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/local-governments/improvement-districts-governance-bodies/university-endowment-lands
- **Governing frame — UBC ground:** UBC's campus lands are governed by the university and developed through UBC Properties Trust; homes sit on the prepaid 99-year lease structure in §2. For a Wesbrook/Hampton Place purchase, order and read the head lease: confirm the lease start date, prepaid status, remaining term, and any UBC rights (including faculty/staff resale restrictions on some program homes) before subject removal — document-level verification is required per property. https://elementslab.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Wesbrook-Study_reducedsize_secure2.pdf
- **Subdivision — City bylaw NOT imported:** Vancouver's Subdivision By-law 5208 is a City of Vancouver instrument. It is cited in this file ONLY to state the boundary: the research does not apply it to UEL or UBC ground, and no City subdivision route is asserted for this area. A subdivision position for a specific UEL parcel: UNCONFIRMED in the research — confirm with the UEL Administration. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/university-vw.md)
- **UNA services levy (UBC neighbourhoods):** the University Neighbourhoods Association delivers municipal-like services (community centres, parks, recreation) to UBC's residential neighbourhoods under its Neighbours Agreement with UBC — confirm the UNA services levy and any neighbourhood-specific charges in the strata budget; the UNA, not a city, is the service provider. https://www.myuna.ca/about-us/
- **UEL property taxes — the rural regime, stated exactly:** UEL properties do NOT pay City of Vancouver property tax. Because the UEL is taxed as a rural area, annual property taxes are payable to the Province's Surveyor of Taxes and are due and payable on July 2 of the year they are first levied; a penalty of 5% of the unpaid taxes is added to any portion unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% to any portion still unpaid on October 31 of that year (Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s. 11). Rural tax notices are mailed by May 31, and payment must be received by the due date to count as on time. UEL water is billed separately from the tax notice. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 and https://www.universityendowmentlands.gov.bc.ca/library/Reports/Realtor%20Information%202026-03-12.pdf
- **Empty Homes Tax — does NOT apply here:** Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is a City of Vancouver tax on City ground. This file's ground is UEL/UBC, taxed under the rural regime above — the EHT is stated here ONLY as a non-application, and no Vancouver vacancy position is imported. (§1; Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/university-vw.md)
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked examples (inputs + tier math): $900,000 → $16,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land / leasehold-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED. How PTT applies to a prepaid leasehold interest specifically: confirm on title/PTT guidance per transaction — no leasehold-specific PTT position is stated in the research and none is invented here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points — including any leasehold-specific GST position — UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Am I buying land when I buy in Wesbrook Village?** No — you buy a prepaid 99-year leasehold interest in a strata home on UBC land that is never sold outright. The remaining lease term runs from the project's original lease date and matters to lenders and pricing; read the head lease before subject removal. https://elementslab.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Wesbrook-Study_reducedsize_secure2.pdf
- **Q: What about the older streets east of campus — are those freehold?** Yes, predominantly: the UEL's older residential streets are largely freehold lots originally granted by the Crown, carrying built houses (mid-century homes plus high-end rebuilds). Confirm tenure on title for every property — the split is by street, not by postal code. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/university-vw.md)
- **Q: Who do I pay property tax to, and when?** The Province's Surveyor of Taxes, under the rural regime: due July 2, with 5% added to any portion unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on any portion still unpaid on October 31. Not the City of Vancouver — and water is billed separately from the tax notice. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Q: Does Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax apply to a UBC-area home?** No — the 3% EHT is a City of Vancouver tax, and this ground is UEL/UBC, taxed provincially as a rural area. Do not carry a City vacancy position onto this ground. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot and build here?** No verified lot offering exists on either side of the file: UBC land is leased, never sold, and the UEL side trades as built freehold houses. Named presale projects in the UBC neighbourhoods: UNVERIFIED. (§2.)
- **Q: What is Tapestry at Wesbrook Village, in land terms?** A leasehold strata building (134 rental homes + 46 condominium homes in two six-storey mid-rises) on UBC ground — a tenure record, not land stock. Its 55+ position rests on MLS wording only; a Form B is owed before anyone calls it a verified 55+ building. https://www.wesbrookproperties.com/office-retail-leasing/
- **Q: Who provides city-type services in the UBC neighbourhoods?** The University Neighbourhoods Association, under its Neighbours Agreement with UBC — community centres (Wesbrook Community Centre, 3335 Webber Lane; Old Barn Community Centre), parks and recreation for roughly 15,000 residents — funded in part through the UNA services levy confirmed in the strata budget. https://www.myuna.ca/about-us/ and https://www.myuna.ca/facilities/
- **Q: Which schools serve a Wesbrook address?** By exact address — the settled runs (5395 Chancellor Blvd → University Hill Elementary/Secondary; 5488 Ortona Road → Norma Rose Point / University Hill Secondary) are sample-address results; the elementary boundary line for other addresses is UNVERIFIED. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/039
- **Q: Are there bare-land strata lots here?** None verified — the UBC strata stock is building strata on leasehold land, a different form from both fee-simple lots and bare-land strata. (§2.)
- **Q: Will GST or the FTHB rebate help on a land purchase here?** There is no bare-land purchase to apply them to on UBC ground; at the CRA GI-003 level, developer/newly-subdivided lot sales are generally taxable and the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
