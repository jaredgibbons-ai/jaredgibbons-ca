## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Jurisdiction — FVRD Electoral Area D (stated expressly):** Popkum is in FVRD Electoral Area D (Popkum–Bridal Falls); the FVRD is the local government for its electoral areas, providing planning and development approvals, building inspection and bylaw enforcement among its electoral-area services, while member municipalities govern themselves — so City of Chilliwack zoning does NOT apply in Popkum, and City of Mission bylaws are likewise never borrowed. https://jaredgibbons.ca/popkum-guide.html
- **Planning/zoning process — numbers UNVERIFIED, process documented:** the guide page states the Popkum-Bridal Falls (Area D) Official Community Plan, FVRD Bylaw No. 1501, was adopted by the FVRD Board in January 2021, after a public hearing on December 15, 2020 — the bylaw text itself was not opened on an FVRD source in the research pass, so the bylaw number and adoption details are UNVERIFIED against the FVRD record and are carried here as guide-page statements only. The FVRD zoning bylaw and the zone of any Popkum parcel were likewise not confirmed on an FVRD source: UNVERIFIED. Other regional districts' same-letter bylaws are a name collision, NOT citable. The process instead: confirm the parcel's plan designation and zone with the FVRD before relying on any use or building claim. https://jaredgibbons.ca/popkum-guide.html
- **Property taxes — RURAL regime (stated expressly):** Popkum is not in a city, town, district or village, so its properties are rural-area properties: the owner receives the tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural taxes are due July 2, a 5% penalty is added to any portion unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% to any portion unpaid on October 31. **No municipal flat penalty applies to this ground.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **ALR block (applies where a parcel is ALR — status per-parcel, UNVERIFIED here):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR; no flat farm-class figure is asserted for this slug because this file's own research does not document one. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Flood and hazard:** the guide page states published records it read do not list an FVRD or BC document stating a flood or debris-flow hazard for the Popkum area. Parcel-level flood or hazard mapping for any Popkum property: UNVERIFIED — check site-specific hazard mapping with the FVRD before building or buying. https://jaredgibbons.ca/popkum-guide.html
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, where met — none verified on this ground):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on an $800,000 lot purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$800,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000** (the $800,000 first-time-buyer cap anchor carries a $6,000 exemption value where that exemption actually applies). Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. (Reference anchors: $500,000 → $8,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $3.5M → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — a townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met, never silently carried.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Registry / foreclosure geography:** no Supreme Court registry mapping for Popkum (FVRD Area D) court-ordered land sales is verified in this research: UNVERIFIED, stated plainly, and never filled in with a neighbouring city's registry (Mission has NO Supreme Court registry either). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol2/consol2/96253_01

Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
