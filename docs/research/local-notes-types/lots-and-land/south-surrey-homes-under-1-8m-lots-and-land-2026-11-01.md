## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** (standing correction; Bylaw 18020 is never cited as current). https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- **SSMUH / zoning:** the City states lots smaller than 280 m² may hold a maximum of 3 units, lots larger than 280 m² a maximum of 4 units, and eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop with 15-minute service a maximum of 6 units; the framework applies to eligible residential properties under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. Zone renames: former RF and RF-SS replaced by R3 Urban Residential, former RF-SD Semi-Detached replaced by R6 Semi-Detached, RM-D duplex zone removed. The live page's R3/R6 lot-size minima (R3 560 m²; R6 200 m² interior / 226 m² corner) are UNVERIFIED — not confirmed on a City source in this slug's research. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Suites:** Surrey suite cap is 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area (standing correction — parcel eligibility is a City check).
- **Short-term rentals:** permitted owner-only — the host must be the property owner living in the home as their primary residence; tenants cannot operate them. Licence figure carried as $358/yr, with a DISCLOSED CONFLICT: the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350/yr plus fees; confirm on the City licence page before quoting. A strata can still restrict STRs by bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Flood / ALR:** no lot-level floodplain or ALR status for any property in this band was confirmed in this slug's research — both checks are parcel-specific on City and ALC mapping: UNVERIFIED at parcel level. ALR block where a parcel is in the ALR: subdivision needs ALC approval; residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps 500 m² / suite-in-principal / 90 m² ≤40 ha / 186 m² >40 ha; farm class three tiers: $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha, $10,000 (<0.8 ha), by Oct 31. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **PTT:** 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above; further 2% on residential value over $3,000,000 (never reached in this band). Worked math in §2. FTHB full exemption at $835,000 or less; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — both live only at this band's lower end. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
