## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — current plan only.** Land use across Surrey now follows the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026), and Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and is never cited as current — including for any redevelopment claim made about a band building's parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it (Jul 8, 2024 framework) — the house-lot comparison frame.** Maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. SSMUH applies to house lots; it does not add units to an existing condo building, and a strata parcel's redevelopment position is a per-title, per-plan fact: UNCONFIRMED per parcel in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey).** Only one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. Relevant to the house-with-a-helper comparison, not to a condo unit. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision + servicing process.** Surrey subdivision proceeds through detailing and a Servicing Agreement defining off-site works, services and financial securities. No servicing figure is stated for any band building's parcel: UNCONFIRMED per parcel — where a number is unconfirmed, the process is what to follow, not an estimate. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey).** Permitted, owner-only: renting a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, an apartment unit with one lock-off suite (if applicable) is a permitted housing type, and a business licence is required before operating. Licence figure CONFLICT carried openly: $358/yr is the program figure, WITH the City's $350/yr plus fees proposal disclosed — read the City licence page before quoting either. Strata bylaws are a separate, building-level check. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **BC strata block (applies to every band purchase, and to a bare-land strata lot in the same terms).** Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page (fees, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary); depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **ALR block — conditional only (no ALR parcel is band stock).** Where a Surrey parcel IS confirmed ALR: residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALR subdivision requires ALC approval; principal residence ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class, full three-tier form: $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 (<0.8 ha); applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an error pattern. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked examples with inputs + tier math (the band's core land-cost question).** General PTT: 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value and 2% on the portion above $200,000 (the tiers above the band — 3% above $2,000,000 plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — are stated for completeness; no band purchase reaches them). Worked examples, no exemption: **$500,000** → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. **$700,000** → $2,000 + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. **$800,000 (band cap)** → $2,000 + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000**. Program anchors above the band, for a buyer trading up to a lot: $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,100,000 → $20,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier over $3M. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **PTT — First-Time Home Buyers' Program (the exemption that covers the whole band on value).** The full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024) and exempts the tax on the first $500,000 of the purchase price (maximum $8,000), with a partial exemption only above $835,000 and below $860,000, subject to the program's other conditions (principal residence; property 0.5 hectares or smaller). Because the band caps at $800,000, a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in the band sits under the full-exemption value ceiling — on the $800,000 worked example, a fully qualifying first-time buyer pays $14,000 − $8,000 = **$6,000**. Qualification is personal and per-transaction: confirm before relying on it. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only (for the lot cross-shop, not a resale condo).** Most individuals' vacant-land sales are exempt (e.g., personal-use land); taxable examples include business capital property, sales in the course of a business, and parcels created by subdividing into more than two parts. Finer points: UNCONFIRMED per lot. A resale condo is not a vacant-land sale, and the live page's GST treatment statements are UNVERIFIED site copy (§2). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — stated precisely.** The FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a presale condo is new housing; a bare lot is not. New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. Land-plus-build qualification: UNCONFIRMED per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax.** Surrey IS a designated taxable area. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition.** No primary text was opened in this slug's research lineage for the position as it applies to a band purchase or to bare land: UNCONFIRMED — CMHC tool + legal advice. No position asserted.
- **Holding costs / tax dates.** Surrey taxes due July 2, 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 (city-wide) — for a condo, the strata's budget and the unit's tax share are Form B / disclosure facts. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Foreclosure note.** Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry — foreclosure mapping is UNVERIFIED and none is asserted.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many Surrey condos are for sale under $800,000 right now — and how many are on bare land?** The band count cannot be stated from the sources used here: SnapStats publishes no price-band cut and no condo-only cut, and no official source publishes a count or share for the band. - **Q: Does the $800K cap cover the typical Surrey condo?** The cap sits above the middle of the combined attached market: the Surrey Condos & Townhomes median was $512,944 in September 2026 — $287,056 below the cap — though that median mixes townhomes with condos, so treat it as context for where the cap sits, not as the price of a typical condo. (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $700,000?** $12,000 with no exemption ($2,000 on the first $200,000 + $10,000 at 2% on the next $500,000). A fully qualifying first-time buyer exempts the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000), paying $4,000 on that same purchase — confirm qualification first. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at the $800,000 cap?** $14,000 with no exemption; $6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer ($14,000 − $8,000 maximum exemption). Inputs and tier math in §3. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Is a presale condo under $800K counted the same as a resale in this band?** No. Presales are never counted as resale band stock in this file; a presale is a developer contract (new housing — GST and the FTHB GST rebate frame in §3 apply to it in a way they do not apply to a resale or to bare land). Band status for any specific presale: UNCONFIRMED per project in this research — no Surrey building was verified on a developer source in this pass. (§2 above.)
- **Q: Who owns the land under my condo building?** The strata corporation holds the common property (including the land) for the owners in proportion to unit entitlement; your strata lot is the unit. The parcel's tenure, any lease, and any redevelopment or wind-up position are title and strata-document facts for that building — UNCONFIRMED per parcel in this research; read them on title and in the Form B, not from a listing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Q: Can I run an Airbnb in a Surrey condo under $800K?** Only within the City's rules: the host must be the owner, living at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot operate one, an apartment unit with one lock-off suite is a permitted type, and a business licence is required first (figure conflict in §3 — check the City licence page). Strata bylaws are a separate building-level check. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Q: Are the strata fees on the page ($270–$460 / $380–$620) reliable?** No — those ranges are live-page copy that no official source confirmed in this pass: UNVERIFIED. The fee for a specific unit is a Form B fact for that building. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-condos-under-800k.html
- **Q: Would a house lot with a suite beat a condo in this price range?** That comparison is governed by §3's house-lot frame: one suite per dwelling unit at most 40% of the principal unit, SSMUH at 3–4 units by lot size (up to 6 near a frequent bus stop) — but a lot purchase carries the vacant-land GST question (CRA GI-003) and PTT relief turns on building/occupying a qualifying home, so the two purchases are not tax-equivalent. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
