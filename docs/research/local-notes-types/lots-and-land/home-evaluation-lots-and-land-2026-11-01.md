## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **The regulator's framework behind the service:** in British Columbia, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); a licensed real estate professional in BC must give consumers information about commissions and fees, must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party. Disclosure must happen at the outset of dealings; dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. A CMA request through this page is, on the page's own text, a request for an evaluation — the page does not state when agency disclosure occurs in Jared's process: UNVERIFIED. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **The listing-data notice the site carries:** the page's footer states the data relating to real estate on the website comes in part from the MLS® Reciprocity program of either the Greater Vancouver REALTORS® (GVR), the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board (FVREB) or the Chilliwack and District Real Estate Board (CADREB); listings held by participating firms are marked with the MLS® logo and include the listing agent's name; the representation is based on board-generated data the boards do not warrant for accuracy; and the materials may not be reproduced without the boards' express written consent. https://jaredgibbons.ca/home-evaluation.html
- **No municipal land rule attaches to this slug:** zoning, OCP designation, SSMUH, subdivision and servicing rules are properties of a parcel's municipality and zone, not of a service page — they are documented in each area's own lots-and-land file (for Surrey ground: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 adopted Jun 15, 2026, and the Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830). No Surrey (or any other municipality's) rule is conformed onto this topic file, and no parcel rule is stated here as if the evaluation page set it.
- **PTT on land (the frame a land seller/buyer brings to an evaluation conversation):** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Anchors:** $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K cap → $14,000 ($6,000 fully qualifying first-time buyer); $1.0M → $18,000; $1.1M → $20,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier over $3M. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. An evaluation states a market value; it does not settle the parcel's GST status. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** the FTHB GST rebate applies to new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; window Mar 20, 2025–2031); the new-housing rebate underneath is 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 annual. A buyer evaluating land to build on should not price any of these rebates against the land purchase itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Bare-land strata block (relevant only if the property being evaluated is a bare-land strata lot — a tenure the evaluation page does not mention):** a bare-land strata lot is strata tenure on the lot — Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. Whether the CMA process values strata lots differently is not stated on the page: UNVERIFIED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR frame (relevant only if the parcel being evaluated is in the ALR — again, not mentioned on the page):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, asserted only where a file's own research documents it, and no farm-class figure is documented for this slug. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
