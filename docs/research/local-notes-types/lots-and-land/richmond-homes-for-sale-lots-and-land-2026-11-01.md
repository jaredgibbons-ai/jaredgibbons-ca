## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500:** single-detached land sits in RS1/RS2 (Section 8.1) with the subzone minimum lot areas in Section 2. A secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in RS1/RS2 (suite rules in Section 5.4: at least 33.0 m², at most the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m²). https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **OCP — 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000:** the city-wide planning framework; district plans sit inside it as schedules (Steveston Area Plan = Schedule 2.4, Bylaw 7100). https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/steveston566.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. Confirm the parcel's subzone and servicing before pricing in added units. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Subdivision + servicing — Bylaws 8751 / 8752 (VERIFIED frame):** Richmond subdivision and servicing requirements run under Bylaw 8751 and Bylaw 8752. Parcel-level works, frontage and servicing conditions are confirmed with the City for the specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED beyond the bylaw frame stated here.
- **Flood construction — Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204:** sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements in Richmond; new construction on Richmond land must meet its flood construction level, behind a perimeter dike system designed for a 1:500 flood event with dikes being raised from 3.5 m to 4.7 m. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf and https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **ALR block (where ALR ground is met at Richmond's edges):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31). No page stating a flat farm-class figure is documented in this slug's research, so no such error is asserted here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for the form, wherever a Richmond bare-land strata plan is met):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property taxes:** City of Richmond 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026 (the first business day of July), with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. First-Time Home Buyers' exemption: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, maximum saving $8,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, out at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not bare land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50% per the condos sibling. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Richmond-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in this slug's research — none imported. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply in Richmond. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this ground is UNCONFIRMED here — the research cites no prohibition source for this area, and the position is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot anywhere in Richmond from this page?** This city file carries no lot roster — land is documented in the sub-area lots-and-land files cross-referenced in Section 2. Start from the sub-area file for the ground you want, then confirm the parcel's subzone, flood construction level and servicing. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How small can a Richmond house lot be?** By subzone under Zoning Bylaw 8500 Section 8.1: minimum lot areas include RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m², with a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones. Confirm the parcel's subzone first. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Q: How many homes can one Richmond lot hold?** The City states at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. A legal suite must meet Section 5.4 (at least 33.0 m²; at most the lesser of 40% of the dwelling or 110 m²). https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: Is Richmond land flood-constrained?** Yes — the whole city is a low-lying island in the Fraser River floodplain behind a 1:500-design perimeter dike system, and Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction levels for new construction. Confirm both for the parcel before pricing a rebuild. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Q: What do Bylaws 8751/8752 mean for subdividing?** They are Richmond's subdivision and servicing bylaw frame — the works and servicing a subdivision must provide run under them, confirmed parcel by parcel with the City. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Does the Alexandra District Energy Utility charge apply to Richmond land generally?** No — ADEU charges are a West Cambie / Alexandra carrying cost only. They do not generalize to other Richmond districts. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Will GST apply to a newly subdivided lot bought from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA GI-003, unlike an individual's personal-use resale. The FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: How much property transfer tax on a $2.0M Richmond lot?** $38,000 on the general scale (1% / 2% / 3% tiers); $3.5M attracts $93,000 including the further 2% tier. The FTHB exemption maxes at an $8,000 saving; the Newly Built Home Exemption is for a new home, not bare land. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve my Richmond address?** SD38, address-based, no middle tier. Ministry-confirmed schools in the research include McMath Secondary (4251 Garry St), Steveston-London Secondary (6600 Williams Rd), Hugh Boyd Secondary (9200 No. 1 Rd), Lord Byng Elementary (3711 Georgia St), Westwind Elementary (11371 Kingfisher Dr) and Diefenbaker Elementary (4511 Hermitage Dr) — but catchment for a specific address is UNVERIFIED in this city file; run the exact address in the SD38 locator. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Q: Does SnapStats publish a Richmond median I can price land against?** No — SnapStats publishes no Richmond figures at all, and land has no SnapStats category in any community. Pricing support comes from Paragon comparables. (Section 1.)
