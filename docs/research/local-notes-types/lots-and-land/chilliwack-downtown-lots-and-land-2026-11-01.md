## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Chilliwack 2050, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — current.** Schedule B3 is the Downtown Neighbourhood Plan (adopted June 2010, updated 2025, per the plan record read via search result) articulating the vision for the Downtown Plan Area over 30 years, and Schedule D-6 is the Downtown development permit area, whose guidelines exist to facilitate the Downtown Design Guidelines under s.488(1)(d) of the Local Government Act and to support revitalization of the historic downtown core. The live page's "Urban Quarter" designation wording was NOT confirmed on the City OCP page opened in the research pass: UNVERIFIED as stated. https://chilliwack.com/ocp and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3556
- **Zoning / subdivision / servicing:** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs lot by lot (§6 farm zones apply where a parcel is in a farm zone — no downtown parcel is stated to be in one in the research). Subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer and the Preliminary Layout Approval process: confirm the parcel's zone, subdivision requirements, servicing and frontage conditions with the Approving Officer for the PID before valuing a split. **The servicing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED — no number is asserted in this file, and no other city's number is borrowed.** DCC rates for a specific downtown subdivision: UNVERIFIED — no DCC bylaw or rate page was opened for this slug. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/chilliwack-downtown.md)
- **SSMUH / suites:** Chilliwack's SSMUH amendment is Bylaw 5395 (adopted Jun 18, 2024), amending Bylaw 5000. Under Bylaw 5000, a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the habitable floor space of the building (s.2); only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot, with permitted ADU types including secondary suite, coach house and garden suite (s.4.06(1)); garden suites cap at 100 m² (s.4.06(1)(g)); ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite (s.5.04). (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, items 6 and 7f)
- **ALR block (applies parcel by parcel, only where the parcel is in the reserve):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for any downtown parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (applies wherever a bare-land strata lot trades — none verified downtown, §2):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** a $500,000 parcel, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **City property taxes (2026):** notices are mailed annually at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant-lot subdivision I can buy into downtown?** None verified. No named fee-simple subdivision selling serviced vacant lots was confirmed on an official or developer source — downtown "land" trades lot by lot as holding properties and infill parcels (§2 above).
- **Q: Is Chilliwack Downtown a Fraser Valley board market?** No — it is a CADREB market, and CADREB publishes statistics board-wide only. The live page's FVREB-feed FAQ line is wrong as stated (§5 below). https://www.cadreb.com/market/
- **Q: Which plan governs a downtown lot?** The Chilliwack 2050 OCP (Bylaw 5500), Schedule B3 Downtown Neighbourhood Plan, with the D-6 Downtown development permit area in the core — confirm the parcel's designation and permit-area set with the City before valuing redevelopment potential. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Q: A listing says a lot is "designated R5 in the OCP" — can I rely on it?** No — that is the listing's own text. Confirm OCP designation and zoning with the City for the PID before pricing any assembly or holding value. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-downtown.html
- **Q: Can a downtown house add a suite or a coach house?** Under Bylaw 5000, one ADU per lot: a secondary suite at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of habitable floor space, or a coach house / garden suite (garden suites to 100 m²), with ADU parking at 1–2 spaces under s.5.04; Bylaw 5395's SSMUH frame sits on top of that for eligible parcels. (§3 above)
- **Q: How do I subdivide a downtown parcel?** Through the City's Approving Officer and Preliminary Layout Approval — zone minimums, servicing and frontage conditions are confirmed for the specific parcel. The servicing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in this file, and DCC rates for a specific subdivision are UNVERIFIED. (§3 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a lot purchase here?** PTT on the general tiers — on the §3 worked example ($500,000, illustration only) the bill is $8,000 with no exemption. GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is my lot in the ALR?** Check the ALC maps by PID or civic address. If the lot IS in the reserve, subdivision needs ALC approval and residences cannot be stratified. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: When are Chilliwack property taxes due?** The first working day after July 1 — 5% penalty on amounts outstanding after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Q: Which schools serve a downtown lot?** The Ministry-confirmed downtown schools are Chilliwack Central Elementary Community, Bernard Elementary, Chilliwack Middle School and Chilliwack Secondary — but SD33 catchments are address-based and no locator result is on record, so the catchment for a specific address is UNVERIFIED. (§1 above)
