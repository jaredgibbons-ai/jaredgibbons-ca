## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Sea Island is a separate island in the Fraser River in the City of Richmond, BC — Lulu Island (the main part of Richmond) to the south and east, and the City of Vancouver across the North Arm to the north. "Sea Island" is both the board sub-area name (GVR board area) and one of the City's own planning areas — planning area 12 on the City Official Community Plan Key Map — so the City name and the board name match. Paragon sub-area code: UNVERIFIED. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/CNCL_062209_Bylaw_840323090.pdf
- Where the housing is: the island is dominated by Vancouver International Airport (YVR) on federal land; the only residential pocket is Burkeville, in the south-east part of the island near Miller Road and Russ Baker Way. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/CNCL_062209_Bylaw_840323090.pdf and https://www.yvr.ca/en/about-yvr/community/engagement
- Nearby areas: Bridgeport (Richmond, across the Moray Channel / Dinsmore Bridge), Terra Nova (Richmond, across the Middle Arm), and Vancouver (across the North Arm via the Arthur Laing Bridge). Buyers also search "Burkeville," "YVR," "Vancouver International Airport" and "Templeton" (the Canada Line station area). https://www.translink.ca/rider-guide/visiting-vancouver
- Roads and bridges: Grant McConachie Way serves the airport terminals, Russ Baker Way runs south to the Dinsmore Bridge and Richmond, and Miller Road runs through Burkeville; bridges off the island are the Arthur Laing Bridge (to Vancouver), the Moray Channel Bridge, the Dinsmore Bridge and the No. 2 Road Bridge. Commute times in minutes: UNVERIFIED. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/CNCL_062209_Bylaw_840323090.pdf
- Transit: the Canada Line airport branch serves Sea Island at Templeton, Sea Island Centre and YVR-Airport stations, with a change at Bridgeport Station for Richmond–Brighouse branch trains. Local bus route numbers for Burkeville streets: UNVERIFIED. https://www.translink.ca/rider-guide/visiting-vancouver
- Schools: there is no operating public school on Sea Island — Sea Island Elementary School on Wellington Crescent has closed. SD38 catchments are address-based, with no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12); for the sample address 7140 Miller Road (Sea Island Community Centre, Burkeville), the SD38 School Locator gives Brighouse Elementary and Richmond Secondary — a completed locator run for that sample address only. Nearest hospital: Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments and https://www.richmondmuseum.ca/locations/sea-island-school/

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Sea Island has no meaningful bare-land market — stated plainly at the top, and more completely than in most Richmond areas. Most of the island is federal airport land for YVR, which is not market land at all; the only residential land is the built Burkeville pocket of single-detached houses. No named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan, and no presale lot offering was verified. A buyer cannot buy a vacant lot program on Sea Island on this evidence — the land question here is a house-lot question in Burkeville, inside a federal-airport planning frame.**

### Fee-simple lots — the only residential form, in one pocket

- **Burkeville — built house lots, not a lot program:** housing on Sea Island exists only in Burkeville — a pocket of single-detached houses on the south-east part of the island, built for aviation workers in three designs; the City of Richmond Archives states Burkeville is the only remaining population centre on Sea Island. The documented form is freehold-style single-detached houses; the pocket is historic built lot fabric, not an offering. https://www.richmondmuseum.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Sea-Island_Self-Guided-Historical-Tour-Brochure-1.pdf

- **ALR vs non-ALR:** Sea Island was once mostly farm land with small communities including Eburne and Burkeville, per the City Archives — history, not a current ALR finding. ### Federal airport land — documented as such, never as inventory

- **YVR lands:** most of Sea Island is federal airport land; the Vancouver Airport Authority states the airport occupies federal property within the City of Richmond. Land use at the airport is guided by the YVR 2037 Master Plan, approved by the federal Minister of Transport, and federal Airport Zoning Regulations administered through Transport Canada set zoning in areas surrounding the airport, including maximum building heights. Airport facility development follows that federal / YVR process — it is not City subdivision land and no part of it is documented here as for-sale inventory. https://www.yvr.ca/en/about-yvr/community/engagement and https://news.yvr.ca/yvrs-new-master-plan-has-arrived/ and https://news.yvr.ca/statement-regarding-airport-zoning-regulations/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Planning split — read first:** Sea Island is planning area 12 in the City Official Community Plan framework (City of Richmond 2041 OCP, Bylaw 9000) for the Burkeville pocket, while senior governments are not regulated by the City OCP — airport-side changes follow the federal / YVR process under the YVR 2037 Master Plan. Which regime governs a given parcel is the first diligence question on this island. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm and https://news.yvr.ca/yvrs-new-master-plan-has-arrived/
- **Zoning — Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 (Burkeville pocket):** the exact zone and minimum lot size applying to Burkeville lots: UNVERIFIED in the research, so no zone or minimum is quoted for Burkeville here. Most of the island sits under the YVR land use plan and federal airport zoning regulations rather than the City bylaw. Confirm the parcel's position with the City before pricing any rebuild, addition or split. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Federal Airport Zoning Regulations:** administered through Transport Canada, they set zoning in areas surrounding the airport so aircraft can operate safely, including maximum building heights — they can affect additions, rebuilds and tree height on a Burkeville lot, and belong early in due diligence. https://news.yvr.ca/statement-regarding-airport-zoning-regulations/
- **Aircraft noise:** noise is managed through the YVR 2025–2029 Noise Management Plan, which includes work to promote compatible land use planning near the airport; YVR publishes annual noise reports with monitoring data. The City's Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development (ANSD) Policy Area framework (City Bulletin DEVAPPS-05) applies to noise-sensitive development in the airport's vicinity. Price the airport factor with that published evidence, not guesswork. https://www.yvr.ca/en/about-yvr/noise-management/reports and https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/devapps0534378.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential:** no Burkeville-specific SSMUH or subdivision position is stated in the research — UNCONFIRMED for this pocket, and no citywide Richmond figure is imported as a Burkeville finding. Confirm with the City for the parcel.
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision in the Burkeville pocket follows the City process; the City's own alphabetical bylaw list carries "Subdivision and Development — Bylaw 8751 (2011)" and "Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 (2011)" (freshly confirmed on the City's bylaw list in the L3 batch) — Richmond's subdivision/servicing instrument is Bylaw 8751, with cost recovery under Bylaw 8752; parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City. No subdivision route exists through the City for the federal airport lands. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **Flood / dike diligence — Bylaw 8204:** the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets flood construction levels and dike setbacks; the island sits in the Fraser River floodplain behind the City dike system described in Section 2. Confirm the lot's requirements before planning a rebuild. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Sea Island ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Sea Island-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Sea Island, and the position is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot on Sea Island?** No verified offering exists — most of the island is federal YVR land, and the only residential ground is the built Burkeville pocket of detached houses. Land here trades as a house on its lot, not as bare land. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What is Burkeville, exactly?** The only remaining population centre on Sea Island: a pocket of single-detached houses in the south-east of the island near Miller Road and Russ Baker Way, built for aviation workers in three designs. https://www.richmondmuseum.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Sea-Island_Self-Guided-Historical-Tour-Brochure-1.pdf
- **Q: Who plans land use on the island — the City or the airport?** Both, by parcel: the Burkeville pocket sits inside the City (planning area 12 in the OCP framework), while most of the island is federal airport land under the YVR 2037 Master Plan, and senior governments are not regulated by the City OCP. Establish which regime governs the parcel first. https://www.yvr.ca/en/about-yvr/community/engagement
- **Q: Do height limits affect a Burkeville rebuild?** They can — federal Airport Zoning Regulations cap heights in areas surrounding the airport to protect aircraft operations, which can affect additions, rebuilds and tree height. Check early. https://news.yvr.ca/statement-regarding-airport-zoning-regulations/
- **Q: How loud is it, and where is the evidence?** In YVR's own material: the YVR 2025–2029 Noise Management Plan covers land use planning near the airport and YVR publishes annual noise reports with monitoring data — review those before writing, rather than relying on a showing-day impression. https://www.yvr.ca/en/about-yvr/noise-management/reports
- **Q: What zone and lot minimum apply to a Burkeville lot?** UNVERIFIED in this file's research — no zone or minimum is quoted. Confirm the parcel's zoning under Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 with the City before pricing any split or rebuild. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Q: Is a Burkeville lot serviced?** Burkeville homes are on City water and sewer services on the research record; confirm servicing for the specific parcel with the City, and note no 2026 utility dollar figure is confirmed. (Research file above.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Burkeville address?** There is no operating school on the island. By exact address only — the settled locator run here (7140 Miller Road → Brighouse Elementary → Richmond Secondary) does not transfer to other streets. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Q: Will GST apply if a developer sells me a newly subdivided lot?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). No such lot offering is verified on Sea Island today. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
