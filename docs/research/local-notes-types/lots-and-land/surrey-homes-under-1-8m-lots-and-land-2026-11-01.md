## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current.** Bylaw No. 18020 is the replaced predecessor and is never cited as current. A parcel's designation is read in the City's COSMOS mapping per parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000** — parcel zoning confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. Zone-name currency: the July 8, 2024 SSMUH changes renamed the former RF-12 / RF-12C zones to the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone and RF-SD to R6 Semi-Detached — older material using RF-12 / RF-SD names is using pre-2024 zone names. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH / added units (Surrey framework, adopted Jul 8, 2024):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, on eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide and does not create new lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 12000, rules-gaps verified):** one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40%** of the principal dwelling unit; no 90 m² cap was found in the current consolidated bylaw text; a suite is not a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Coach houses and garden suites:** all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; the unit must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house; one extra parking space is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Subdivision / servicing:** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. Verified on the City's own Land Development / Servicing Agreement pages: the **Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw, worked through the City's Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. No other city's number is used for Surrey. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** renting a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at **$358 per year** is required. **Conflict disclosed:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposing the STR amendments put the licence at $350/yr plus fees — $358 is carried with $350 disclosed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R094.pdf
- **ALR block (provincial frame — no band ALR parcel is documented, stated so it is not misapplied):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, asserted only where a file's own research documents it (none does here). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot, wherever one surfaces):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land — worked through the band, inputs + tier math stated (anchors):** general tiers 1% to $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — a purchase under $1.8M meets only the first two tiers. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 = **$8,000**; $1,000,000 = **$18,000**; $1,200,000 = **$22,000**; $1,500,000 = **$28,000**; $1,800,000 (the band cap) = $2,000 + 2% × $1,600,000 = **$34,000**; and, above the band, $2,000,000 = **$38,000**. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — only the lower part of this band can sit inside the full-exemption range on price alone. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. personal-use land); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — Surrey's two-step structure, distinct from Mission's flat 10%. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
