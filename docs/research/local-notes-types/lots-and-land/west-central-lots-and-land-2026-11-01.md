## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a bare building lot in West Central?** There is no lot product to buy — no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering exists here. What trades is built fee-simple house lots and strata units in existing complexes. (§2 above)
- **Q: Are Sherwood Park and Maple Villas land I can buy as lots?** No — they are built strata townhouse complexes (21801 and 21707 Dewdney Trunk Road), counted in the townhomes file. A purchase there is a strata-lot purchase with fees, bylaws and a Form B, not a vacant-land purchase; the title wording "freehold strata" is UNCONFIRMED in-entry and the strata plan governs. (Townhomes sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/west-central-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create by subdividing an RS-1 property?** The RS-1 minimums are 668.0 m² area, 18.0 m width and 27.0 m depth (Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 s. 605) — both the parent-lot remainder and any new lot must meet them. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Q: Does the Lougheed corridor plan change what my lot is worth?** The corridor plan IS adopted (Jan 27, 2026, Bylaw 7781-2021, ending at 221 St), so its designations are settled policy — but a designation is not a rezoning approval for a specific parcel. Check the parcel's current designation in the City's document before pricing corridor upside into it. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP
- **Q: Can a house lot carry a suite or more units?** One secondary suite per lot inside the house (maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the floodplain), and eligible lots may add up to three, four or six SSMUH units — run both paths before listing an older house on a full-size RS-1 lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q: Do floodplain or escarpment rules affect West Central lots?** On affected lots, yes: no habitable floor space below the 6.7 m flood construction level, suites prohibited on floodplain properties, and a geotechnical report on a Section 219 covenant within 300 m of the escarpment crest. Which lots are affected is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED until title and City records are checked. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q: What PTT do I pay on a $1.5M house lot?** $28,000 on the stated tiers before any exemption ($200,000 × 1% + $1,300,000 × 2%). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes at the CRA GI-003 level — developer/business sales and parcels subdivided into more than two parts can be taxable, unlike an individual's personal-use resale, which is generally exempt. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a West Central lot?** UNVERIFIED per address — no locator result for a West Central address was recorded in this program. The live page names Alouette, Glenwood and Laity View elementaries and Maple Ridge and Westview secondaries (Ministry-confirmed names/addresses in §1), but assignment needs the exact address run in the SD42 locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: How do I read West Central's market numbers for land?** As slow built-stock numbers, not land numbers: 66 detached listings / 2 sales (3% ratio, the city's lowest) and 78 attached listings / 10 sales in September 2026, with SnapStats publishing no land category. Deep choice favours buyers; price a lot from its own comparables and designation. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report (research file §2).
