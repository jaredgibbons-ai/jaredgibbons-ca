## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Fairview has no meaningful bare-land market — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community and no presale LOT offering was verified. This is fully built central-city ground: what trades as "land" is (1) fee-simple house lots on the Fairview Slopes and the southern streets, priced partly on their FM-1 / R1-1 schedule potential; (2) arterial and Broadway Plan assembly and development sites, which are proposals and zoning positions, never for-sale lot inventory; and (3) False Creek South, where the ground itself is City-held leasehold — a buyer there never buys the land at all. South Granville corridor land is documented once in the granville lots-and-land file and cross-referenced here, not re-documented.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **Fairview Slopes lots (FM-1 District Schedule):** the FM-1 schedule covers the Fairview Slopes and states its intent is to enhance the small-scale residential character of the neighbourhood by encouraging retention of existing houses and permitting new low-profile residential development; FM-1 permits single detached houses, duplexes, multiple dwellings and multiple conversion dwellings, with a maximum floor space ratio of 0.60 and no minimum site area stated in the schedule overview. These are schedule rules, not a claim about any existing lot's size. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-FM-1.pdf
- **Southern-street lots (R1-1 District Schedule):** residential lots in the southern part of the local area also fall under R1-1 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m) and 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a multiplex of up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units is permitted, and a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house. The zone for a specific lot: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Commercial / mixed-use frontage:** C-3A covers the commercial mixed-use streets (Broadway, Granville and the other arterials) — arterial frontage is development-site ground, not house-lot ground, and is priced on the district schedule and the Broadway Plan, not on lot comparables alone. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Serviced position:** homes here are on City water and sewer services; a 2026 utility dollar figure for a home in this area confirmed on a City source: UNVERIFIED. No well/septic position applies to this serviced urban ground on this file's evidence. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 (City of Vancouver):** the district schedules that govern Fairview land are FM-1 (Fairview Slopes — maximum FSR 0.60, no minimum site area stated in the schedule overview, intent of retaining existing houses and low-profile new development), R1-1 (southern streets — minimum site areas 306 m² / 464 m² / 557 m² by unit count, multiplex to 6 units or 8 rental units, secondary suite and laneway house with a single detached house) and C-3A on the commercial mixed-use streets. Confirm the district for the specific lot on the City's zoning map before pricing any potential. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-FM-1.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Community plan / SSMUH position as the City states it:** the Broadway Plan covers Fairview's northern half (Clark Drive to Vine Street, 1st to 16th Avenues) as the plan for new housing, jobs and amenities around the Broadway Subway, and the Broadway Subway is scheduled to open in fall 2027. Vancouver's multiplex framework operates through the R1-1 schedule figures above rather than through a separate SSMUH bylaw citation in this file's research — price the schedule that actually covers the lot (FM-1 on the Slopes, R1-1 in the south), not a generic multiplex assumption. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/broadway-plan.aspx/ and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0050-000621
- **Subdivision + servicing:** subdivision in Vancouver follows the district schedule minimums above and the City's subdivision and servicing requirements for the specific application; a parcel-level servicing standard for a Fairview lot is UNCONFIRMED on a source in this file's research — confirm requirements with the City for the parcel. Established lots sit inside the City's serviced system on the research's wording (City water and sewer services). https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Floodplain diligence — the north-edge item:** the False Creek shoreline along the north edge is inside the City's designated floodplain dataset, and the City's False Creek Coastal Adaptation Plan states the False Creek floodplain area includes False Creek South passing through Fairview along the south shore; land in the floodplain is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for setbacks and flood construction levels. Whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED — check the parcel in the City dataset before subjects come off. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Leasehold diligence (False Creek South):** homes there sit on City-held land under ground leases, so confirm the lease structure, the remaining term and how the building's lease is administered before pricing — leasehold does not comp against freehold Fairview product, and False Creek South planning for the City-held lands is an active City file. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south-leases-on-city-land.aspx
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Fairview ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Fairview lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%, VANCOUVER ONLY, applies here:** properties declared, determined or deemed vacant are subject to a 3% tax of the property's assessed value, levied on empty and under-utilized Class 1 residential properties in the City of Vancouver; most residential properties are not subject to the tax, including principal residences and homes rented out for at least six months of the year. Vancouver owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. A vacant Fairview lot or a house lot held empty carries this exposure in a way a New Westminster or Burnaby lot does not — the EHT is a City of Vancouver tax only. https://council.vancouver.ca/20251029/documents/pspc6.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot or a lot subdivision I can buy into in Fairview?** No verified one — Fairview is fully built central-city ground. Land trades as built house lots on the Slopes and southern streets, strata units on the arterials, and leasehold homes in False Creek South. No presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What can I build on a Fairview Slopes lot?** Work the FM-1 schedule, not R1-1 assumptions: FM-1 permits single detached houses, duplexes, multiple dwellings and multiple conversion dwellings at a maximum floor space ratio of 0.60, under a schedule written to retain existing houses and keep new development low-profile — a multiplex pitch copied from an R1-1 street will misprice a Slopes lot. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-FM-1.pdf
- **Q: What does an R1-1 lot in south Fairview allow?** Minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house, a duplex or a 3–4 unit multiple dwelling, 464 m² for 5 units and 557 m² for 6 to 8 units; a multiplex of up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units, and a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house. Confirm the lot's district and dimensions parcel by parcel. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: If I buy in False Creek South, do I own the land?** No. False Creek South housing was built on City-held land under ground leases — you buy the home and the leasehold interest, not the fee-simple land. Confirm the lease structure, remaining term and administration from the building's own documents before pricing; lease terms for a specific building are UNVERIFIED in this file. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south-leases-on-city-land.aspx
- **Q: Does the Broadway Plan change what my lot is worth?** It can change expectations, not entitlements by itself: the Plan covers Fairview's northern half (Clark Drive to Vine Street, 1st to 16th Avenues) around the Broadway Subway (opening fall 2027 per the Province), and lots inside the plan area carry expectations the base zoning alone does not show. Price the plan position and the district schedule together. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/broadway-plan.aspx/ and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0050-000621
- **Q: Is a seawall-edge lot in the floodplain?** The False Creek shoreline along the north edge is inside the City's designated floodplain dataset, and the floodplain area includes False Creek South along the south shore — parcels in it carry Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements. Whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain is UNVERIFIED here; check the parcel in the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: Where is South Granville land covered?** In the granville lots-and-land file — the corridor's declared land home for South Granville ground (Granville Street, Granville Bridge to West 16th Avenue, inside this local area). This file cross-references it and does not double-document that ground. (Sibling file: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/granville-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax hit a vacant lot here?** The EHT is levied at 3% of assessed value on empty and under-utilized Class 1 residential properties in the City of Vancouver, and every Vancouver owner must file an annual property status declaration — a lot or house held vacant is exactly the exposure the tax targets. It is a Vancouver-only tax; it does not apply in New Westminster or Burnaby. https://council.vancouver.ca/20251029/documents/pspc6.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Fairview lot?** By exact address only. L'Ecole Bilingue Elementary (1166 West 14th Avenue) and False Creek Elementary (900 School Green) are located in the area, no public secondary school inside the area was confirmed, and no locator run is documented for this file — run the address in the VSB locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
