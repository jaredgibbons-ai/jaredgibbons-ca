## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market in Moody Park — stated plainly at the top because it is the honest position. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering was verified. The sub-area recorded 9 detached listings against only 4 condos & townhomes listings in September 2026. Land here is fee-simple house lots on the park streets, with the only strata land-form being the apartment buildings on the 6th Street / 8th Avenue edges — completed strata counted in the condos sibling, never lot inventory.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **Park-street house lots:** single-detached houses under the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series, s.310) of Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001, on the residential streets around the park, with apartment buildings on the 6th Street / 8th Avenue edges under multiple-unit residential districts. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Lot character from the zones, as the research states it:** RS-1 sets a minimum site area of 557.40 m² (6,000 sq. ft.) and a floor space ratio of 0.5; the edge apartment district RM-1 (s.420) sets maximum site coverage of 40% and density of 35 units per net acre / FSR 1.2. The RS figures are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size; the zone and dimensions of a specific lot are confirmed parcel by parcel (UNVERIFIED for any individual lot). https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Serviced position, as the City states it:** New Westminster operates its own electrical utility and City water / sewer services, and the City's subdivision guide states new lots must be adequately serviced with water, sewers, underground electrical, communications, street lighting, roads, sidewalks and street trees. Servicing for any single parcel is confirmed through that subdivision process — UNCONFIRMED parcel-by-parcel here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/General%20Guide%20to%20Subdivision%20Process%202012-Final.pdf
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** no Agricultural Land Reserve land is identified in Moody Park in the sources read; ALR status for a specific property is a per-address check in the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder. No ALR percentage split is stated. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Flood position:** the City's floodplain study zones only the city's low-lying edges — Zone 1 Queensborough, Zone 2 west-end Fraser River industrial land, Zone 3 the Quay waterfront and Zone 4 the Brunette River industrial area. No floodplain zone for the Moody Park plateau was confirmed; lot-level status is UNVERIFIED and is a City check. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning:** Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 governs. Houses sit in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series, s.310) — RS-1: minimum site area 557.40 m² (6,000 sq. ft.), FSR 0.5, with secondary suites allowed and laneway / carriage houses regulated under the same bylaw; edge apartments sit in multiple-unit districts (RM-1, s.420: 40% maximum site coverage, 35 units per net acre / FSR 1.2). Townhouse districts are RT-2–RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B only (s.410 (RT-2) permits row houses or townhouses) — RT-A/RT-B do not exist, and this file's research carries no such wording to correct. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf and https://newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/400___Multifamily_Residential(3).pdf
- **Plan:** "Our City 2041," the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 7925, 2017, governs the area; Uptown adjoining it is identified in the OCP as a Frequent Transit Development Area. Moody Park itself is named in the OCP as part of the city's Green Zone park list. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports and http://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/community-parks
- **SSMUH / added units as the research states them:** the RS districts allow secondary suites and regulate laneway / carriage houses under Bylaw No. 6680, 2001, within the RS-1 frame above. A Moody Park-specific SSMUH unit-count figure on a City source is UNCONFIRMED in the research — pull City permits before advertising suite or laneway income on any lot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — confirmed bylaw, process stated:** subdivision is regulated by the Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw No. 7142, 2007, which requires all subdivisions be approved by the City's Approving Officer (the Director of Engineering Services) and ensures new lots are adequately serviced and meet the Zoning Bylaw's minimum lot size and frontage requirements. The process runs pre-application, application, and — where required — a Works & Services Agreement with detailed servicing drawings, before final approval and Land Title registration. Parcel-level standards are confirmed through that process; none is quoted lot-by-lot here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/7142_Subdivision.pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/General%20Guide%20to%20Subdivision%20Process%202012-Final.pdf
- **Heritage diligence:** Moody Park is one of the neighbourhoods documented in the City's Historic Neighbourhood Context Statements, and the City protects heritage properties through its heritage program — heritage status must be checked property by property before planning a subdivision, demolition or major work on an older Moody Park lot. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php
- **Flood diligence:** no floodplain zone covers the plateau on the City study's zoning — the contrast with the Quay (Zone 3) down the hill is real — but lot-level status remains UNVERIFIED and is checked with the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Bare-land strata rules:** no bare-land strata plan is verified in this area (Section 2), so no strata block is triggered here. The BC frame that would apply to such a lot is: Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 with the May 1, 2023 exemptions; a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Moody Park ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm-class thresholds are not documented in this area's own research, so no farm-class figure is asserted here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Moody Park lot purchase alone earns no rebate. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Moody Park, and the position is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot subdivision I can buy into in Moody Park?** No verified one — Moody Park is an established park-side house neighbourhood. Land trades as fee-simple house lots; there is no bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata and no presale lot offering on the verified record. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What is the smallest lot the zoning contemplates here?** RS-1 sets a minimum site area of 557.40 m² (6,000 sq. ft.) with an FSR cap of 0.5 — the frame a subdivision application is measured against, subject to the Approving Officer process under Bylaw No. 7142, 2007. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Q: Can a house lot here carry a suite or a laneway house?** The RS districts allow secondary suites and regulate laneway / carriage houses under Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 (RS-1: 557.40 m² minimum, FSR 0.5) — a fee-simple second unit on the lot, never a separate strata lot; confirm permits with the City before pricing the income. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Q: Moody Gardens units are two levels — is that a bare-land or townhouse lot?** Neither. Moody Gardens (777 Eighth Street, NWS2534) is a 4-storey apartment strata counted once in the condos sibling — a buyer there buys an apartment strata lot with full Form B diligence, not land. Basis: the classification note in the FILE STATUS block.
- **Q: Is Moody Park in the floodplain?** No floodplain zone for the plateau was confirmed in the City's floodplain study — its zones are Queensborough, the west-end industrial land, the Quay (Zone 3) and the Brunette River industrial area. Lot-level status is UNVERIFIED; check with the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Q: Does heritage status affect a lot purchase here?** It can — Moody Park has a Historic Neighbourhood Context Statement and the City runs a heritage register and permit process, so work on a listed property can need heritage approvals. Check the specific property before planning a teardown or subdivision. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php
- **Q: Is a Moody Park lot serviced?** The City's subdivision process requires new lots to be adequately serviced with water, sewers, underground electrical, communications, street lighting, roads, sidewalks and street trees, and the City runs its own electrical utility and water / sewer services — confirm the specific parcel's servicing through that process before writing. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/General%20Guide%20to%20Subdivision%20Process%202012-Final.pdf
- **Q: How thin is the pricing evidence for land here?** Very: 1 detached sale and no attached sales in September 2026, against community medians of $1,365,000 detached and $560,000 for "Condos & Townhomes" combined — price a lot off Uptown comparables and the community figures, not a single sub-area sale. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report (research file).
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Moody Park lot?** By exact address only — the one settled locator run in this file (1714 Eighth Avenue → École Lord Tweedsmuir Elementary → Fraser River Middle → New Westminster Secondary) does not transfer to other streets. Run the address in the SD40 locator. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The park is the amenity building:** Moody Park itself holds the outdoor pool, spray park and sports fields, Moody Park Arena is at 701 8th Avenue, and the təməsew̓txʷ Aquatic and Community Centre at 65 East Sixth Avenue fully opened in May 2024 — at 10,644 sq. m. the City's largest capital project to date. Land value on the park streets is amenity value, not density value. https://www.newwestcity.ca/facilities and http://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/community-parks
- **The townhouse zoning exists even though the townhouse stock does not:** Bylaw No. 6680's Townhouse District RT-2 (s.410) expressly permits row houses and townhouses, and the OCP carries a Development Permit Area (1.3) for townhouse form — a buyer waiting for new townhouse product on Moody Park land is waiting on rezoning and assembly, not on a missing rule. https://newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/400___Multifamily_Residential(3).pdf (via the sibling townhomes file)
- **New Westminster runs its own electrical utility** — City power, water and sewer, not BC Hydro for electricity — a carrying-cost detail that surprises buyers arriving from other suburbs; a 2026 utility dollar figure for a Moody Park house is UNVERIFIED. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php
