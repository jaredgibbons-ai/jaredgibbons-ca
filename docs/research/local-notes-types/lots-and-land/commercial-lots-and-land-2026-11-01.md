- **The regulator frame (BCFSA) — the one rule set the research does document for this page:** real estate trading services in BC are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority, for commercial services as for residential; a licensed professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party. Disclosure must happen at the outset of dealings; dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Listing-data notice the page carries:** the footer states the data relating to real estate on the website comes in part from the MLS® Reciprocity program of either the Greater Vancouver REALTORS® (GVR), the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board (FVREB) or the Chilliwack and District Real Estate Board (CADREB); listings held by participating firms are marked with the MLS® logo and include the listing agent's name; the representation is based on board-generated data the boards do not warrant for accuracy; and the materials may not be reproduced without the boards' express written consent. CREA owns the MLS® and REALTOR® trademarks. https://jaredgibbons.ca/commercial.html and https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/how-we-foster-competition/promotion-and-advocacy/regulatory-adviceinterventions-competition-bureau/submission-ftc-and-doj-residential-real-estate-brokerage
- **GST on vacant land — stated at the CRA GI-003 level ONLY, and not extended to commercial transactions:** GI-003 addresses sales of vacant land by individuals — most such sales are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use), with taxable cases including land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts. **The GST treatment of a commercial land purchase beyond that individual frame is UNCONFIRMED in this file's research** — it turns on the parties' registration status and the property's use, and is confirmed with a tax professional per transaction, never assumed from the residential or GI-003 individual frame. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **PTT:** the Province's general PTT tiers (1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000) are a general frame; **how PTT applies to a specific commercial or mixed-use parcel — including any residential/non-residential split — is UNCONFIRMED in this file's research** and is modelled by the buyer's lawyer or notary per transaction. The residential exemptions (First-Time Home Buyers'; Newly Built Home) are home-buyer exemptions and are not commercial land treatment. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land or commercial ground:** the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING — not to bare land and not to commercial property. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year — all housing programs, stated so they are not misapplied to a commercial parcel. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **ALR:** no ALR position is documented in this topic's research and none is imported. Where a commercial feed parcel sits in the Agricultural Land Reserve, the provincial frame is the ALC's: subdivision requires ALC approval; residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, by Oct 31) — stated as the provincial frame only, with parcel status checked on the ALC maps. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Strata block (for any strata-titled commercial or bare-land strata parcel):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
