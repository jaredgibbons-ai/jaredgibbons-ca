## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Municipality: City of Surrey, BC — Sunnyside is in South Surrey, in the Semiahmoo peninsula area between 16 Avenue and 24 Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bakerview-park
- "Sunnyside Park" is the Paragon (FVREB MLS) sub-area name; the City of Surrey uses "Sunnyside" for the park, hall and area facilities in South Surrey. Do not confuse it with "Sunnyside Heights," a separate Grandview Heights neighbourhood plan area near 168 Street, 24 Avenue and Highway 99 — the two names are not the same plan area. https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp
- Boundaries: no City neighbourhood boundary map for the Paragon sub-area was confirmed in the sources read; City material places Bakerview Park, home to Sunnyside Community Hall, at 1845 - 154 Street, and the South Surrey Recreation and Arts Centre at 14601 - 20 Ave in this part of South Surrey. The sub-area is an MLS filing, not a City boundary — MLS files Bishop's Green (2533 152 St) and St. Martin's Lane (15020 27A Ave) under Sunnyside Park Surrey even though their addresses sit at the edges of the City's 16–24 Avenue description. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bakerview-park
- Nearby areas: Ocean Park to the south-west, Crescent Beach / Elgin Chantrell to the west, Semiahmoo Town Centre to the east, Morgan Creek / Rosemary Heights to the north-east. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bakerview-park

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026 — the old OCP Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and is never cited as current in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Zoning:** Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs; its SSMUH zones RA to R6 are in Section 2. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH:** Surrey's SSMUH zoning was adopted July 8, 2024 in the program record — up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, as the City states it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/sunnyside-park-surrey-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §3
- **Secondary suites:** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, one suite per unit; a suite is NOT a separate strata lot. The old 90 m² cap is NOT a current rule. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/sunnyside-park-surrey-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §3.
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision follows the zone for the parcel under Bylaw No. 12000 plus City servicing — a Surrey servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED on a primary source in the research and is not asserted here; confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the City for the parcel.
- **Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan:** the plan was approved by Council and later updated to align with the King George Boulevard Bus Rapid Transit and the Surrey 2050 OCP designations; the plan area, 16 Avenue to 24 Avenue from Martin Drive to Earl Marriott Secondary School, borders this sub-area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/semiahmoo-town-centre-plan
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** STRs are permitted, owner-only — the host must be the property owner living in the home, tenants cannot operate them. Licence figure CONFLICT, carried as instructed: $358 per year on the City's Short-Term Rentals page (the figure carried here), with the City's 2024 corporate report proposing $350 per year plus fees disclosed — direct readers to the City licence page before quoting. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/sunnyside-park-surrey-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §3
- **ALR rules — framework only, no ALR ground documented here:** where a parcel is in the ALR, subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); BC Assessment farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Property transfer tax:** general rates as BC sources state them in the siblings: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above, plus further tax on the residential portion above $3 million as the condos sibling states it; First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Bare-land strata / strata block:** no bare-land strata is verified here (Section 2), so no strata block is applied to a named lot in this file. If a lot elsewhere in Surrey is bare-land strata, the BC position is: a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page), depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023, rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022, and only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Sunnyside-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file: the research documents no primary text on it for this ground.
- **Property taxes:** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — carried from the sibling 55-plus file; no Sunnyside-specific tax or levy note was confirmed in the research (UNVERIFIED list). https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there bare land for sale in Sunnyside Park?** No named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified. Land value here is in fee-simple house lots — price the lot for its SSMUH tier, suite potential and plan position, not as a bare-land program. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How many units can a house lot hold in Sunnyside?** Surrey's SSMUH zoning allows up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Can I add a secondary suite, and is it a separate lot?** One suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area, one suite per unit — a suite is NOT a separate strata lot. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/sunnyside-park-surrey-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §3.
- **Q: Is the 2301 152 Street site a lot I can buy to build a house?** No — as documented, it is a 100-unit, 6-storey mixed-use condo development site with planning history (approved at third reading in listing material), current status UNCONFIRMED. It is not a house lot and not counted as inventory. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/c8075795-2301-152-street-surrey-british-columbia-v4a4p1-8750000/brochure
- **Q: Is Peninsula Village or Elisa land in this file?** No. Peninsula Village counts in the King George Corridor townhomes file, and Elisa is a presale townhome project documented in the sibling programs — both are cross-referenced in Section 2, neither is this file's stock.
- **Q: Which OCP applies to a lot here now?** The Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026. Bylaw No. 18020 is replaced — do not rely on a document citing it as current. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Q: Can I run a short-term rental on a lot / house here?** STRs are permitted, owner-only, with a City licence — $358/yr on the City's page, with $350/yr + fees proposed in the City's 2024 corporate report disclosed; check the City licence page before quoting. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Q: Will GST apply on a vacant lot?** Generally yes if the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided; generally no for an individual's personal-use resale, per CRA GI-003. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Sunnyside address?** The settled sample is 1785 148 St → H.T. Thrift Elementary / Semiahmoo Secondary. Catchments are address-based — run any other address in the SD36 locator; do not assume from the nearest school. (Section 1.)
- **Q: Is Sunnyside Park the same as Sunnyside Heights?** No. Sunnyside Heights is a Grandview Heights plan area by 168 Street, 24 Avenue and Highway 99; Sunnyside Park facilities such as Bakerview Park (1845 - 154 Street) are in South Surrey proper. https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp
