## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Plans and zoning — Surrey:** Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) is current — Bylaw 18020 is never cited as current. Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs; no City land use plan is named for Elgin or Chantrell. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **SSMUH — Surrey (adopted Jul 8, 2024):** maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Suites, coach houses and garden suites — Surrey:** one secondary suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit (one per unit, not a separate strata lot); no 90 m² cap is current. A coach house or garden suite must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR, with one extra parking space required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **RA figures — held UNVERIFIED:** the RA statements on the sibling page (4,050 m² minimum lot size, 50 m width / 60 m depth, two-unit cap, 2-hectare agriculture threshold under Part 12 of Bylaw 12000) were not re-opened in the bylaw text in the research pass (429, stopped) — UNVERIFIED against the bylaw text; the coach-house figures that apply to RA lots above are confirmed on the City's coach houses page. https://jaredgibbons.ca/elgin-chantrell-guide.html
- **Subdivision and servicing — Surrey:** servicing under Surrey Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986, verified on City pages). Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is ever stated. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **ALR block (ALR ground exists in this pocket — parcel status is parcel-specific):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no such line is asserted for this slug because its research does not document one. Check a parcel in the ALC finder. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, where met):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since Nov 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Short-term rentals — Surrey:** renting by the owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host and must live at the property as their primary residence; a business licence at $358 per year is required (the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350 + fees — $358 is carried with the $350 conflict disclosed). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Taxes:** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,200,000 lot purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,200,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. (At $3.5M the total is $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — a townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M; OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Heritage is designated ground here, not just character (City heritage bylaws list).** The Stewart Farmhouse at 13723 Crescent Road (Bylaw 7823) and Elgin Heritage Park itself (Bylaw 12922) are City-designated heritage entries; the municipality purchased 54 acres of the farm site in 1984 and it holds both city and provincial heritage site designation — a property near the cluster needs its heritage status checked before renovation or subdivision plans are priced. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/heritage-bylaws and https://www.surrey.ca/arts-culture/historic-stewart-farm/about-site
- **A 3-km riverfront park is coming along the Nicomekl's south side (City release, Nov 23, 2023).** The Nicomekl Riverfront Park will be a 3-km, 80-acre linear park from Elgin Road to 40 Avenue, developed in three phases — a City park project, not a development site and not land inventory. https://www.Surrey.ca/news-events/news/council-approves-final-concept-plan-phase-one-of-surreys-new-3km-riverfront-park
