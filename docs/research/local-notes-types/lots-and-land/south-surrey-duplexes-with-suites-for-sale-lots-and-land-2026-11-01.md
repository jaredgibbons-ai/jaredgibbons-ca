## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — Surrey:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026 — the former OCP Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and is never cited as current in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Zoning — Surrey:** Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Secondary suites — Surrey (the core rule for this topic):** a secondary suite is a dwelling unit that is an accessory use, with habitable floor area not exceeding 40% of the principal dwelling unit, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex residential building, and not a separate strata lot. Only one secondary suite is permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a secondary suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; one suite may be permitted in dwellings within small-scale, multi-unit housing zones, including Comprehensive Development zones; and each application is reviewed for legal restrictions such as Land Use Contracts and Building Schemes. To become legal, the owner must meet the conditions of zoning, hold the necessary building permits, meet the BC Building Code, and pay the secondary suite utility and service fees. A "90 m²" cap is NOT current and is not stated as a rule anywhere in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **SSMUH — Surrey (adopted July 8, 2024):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (a stop with 15-minute service, identified with TransLink); the new zoning does not retroactively legalize suites built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Subdivision and servicing — Surrey:** the Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 is the City's subdivision and development (servicing) bylaw, verified on the City's own pages in this program; subdivision proceeds through the City's process including a Servicing Agreement defining off-site works, services and financial securities for final approval. Lot-specific servicing costs for a duplex parcel: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Short-term rentals — Surrey:** permitted, owner-only (owner must live at the property as primary residence; tenants cannot run one). Licence figure CONFLICT carried openly: $358/yr is the program figure, WITH the City's $350/yr proposal disclosed — read the City licence page before quoting either. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Property taxes — Surrey:** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3) (carried because this page IS an open-house feed):** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report, states that disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. Use an open house on this feed for facts about the home; save your motivation, finances and needs for your own agent. The 1-hour time offset on this page's three open-house cards is documented in §5; the authoritative time is UNVERIFIED. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **ALR block (parcel status UNCONFIRMED per parcel here):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (where a half duplex IS a strata lot, and for bare-land strata parcels):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. A non-strata half duplex carries none of this — which is why the tenure check in §2 comes first. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,200,000 half-duplex purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,200,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FTHB PTT exemption / Newly Built Home Exemption:** first $500,000 exempt, full at value ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000), partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; a person who paid PTT on vacant land and then has a newly built home on it may be eligible for a refund of the tax paid. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. A resale half duplex is not vacant land — this block is carried for a bare-lot purchase on the same ground, not for the feed's built product. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How big can a secondary suite be in a Surrey duplex?** Its habitable floor area cannot exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit, it must sit within the duplex building, and it cannot be a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Q: Can a Surrey half duplex have two secondary suites?** No — only one secondary suite is permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, and a suite is not permitted inside a coach house or garden suite. A card advertising both a legal suite and a second guest suite needs the second unit's status explained in writing before subjects come off. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Q: Am I buying land or a strata lot when I buy one of these half duplexes?** On this feed's typical product, neither a bare lot nor a strata lot: card after card carries "NO STRATA FEES," i.e. a non-strata half duplex held as an undivided-interest / fee-simple style purchase — stated from card text, not title searches. Confirm the exact title for the specific property; where a half duplex IS a strata lot, the strata's bylaws apply as well. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-duplexes-with-suites-for-sale.html
- **Q: The listing says the suite is "legal" — can I rely on that?** No. The page itself warns that a listing note is not proof of permits; pull the City registration status and the permit history, and note that Surrey's new zoning does not legalize an unpermitted suite retroactively. The permitted status of any individual suite on this page is UNVERIFIED. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Does Surrey's SSMUH zoning legalize an existing unpermitted suite?** No. The City states the legislation does not provide retroactive legalization for suites built without the necessary building permits; owners must still go through the permit process. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Is there a duplex-only sales figure for South Surrey?** No. SnapStats publishes Detached and Condos & Townhomes splits only — September 2026 community medians were $1,628,000 detached and $700,000 for Condos & Townhomes — and no duplex-only figure exists in program sources. Do not price from a "duplex median"; price the property from its own half-duplex comparables. (Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.)
- **Q: How many listings does this page's feed show, and where are they?** The feed displayed "1-12 / 24" — 24 listings — and the first-page cards read in full were in Grandview Surrey, Pacific Douglas and the King George Corridor at reading. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-duplexes-with-suites-for-sale.html
- **Q: How much PTT on a $1.2M half duplex?** $22,000 — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) plus 2% on the next $1,000,000 ($20,000). (§3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
