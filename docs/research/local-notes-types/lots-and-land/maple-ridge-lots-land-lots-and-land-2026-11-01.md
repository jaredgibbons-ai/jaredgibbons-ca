- **SSMUH / suites on non-ALR lots:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- **Floodplain:** the City publishes North and South Alouette River and Fraser River floodplain maps; the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), no habitable floor space may sit below that level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s.402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. Parcel-level floodplain status for any specific lot: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **ALR block (applies parcel by parcel, only where the parcel is in the reserve):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, apart from the limited cases in the ALR General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for any Maple Ridge parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Bare-land strata block (for a bare-land strata lot, wherever one trades):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** an $800,000 parcel, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. The live page's own GST summary is UNVERIFIED in the research and is not restated here. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are the market numbers on this page land prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no vacant-land category; the §1 figures are Maple Ridge residential context only (detached median $1,131,000; attached median $525,000, September 2026), and land-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. Price a lot on its own zone, services and comparable land sales. (§1 above)
- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create in Maple Ridge?** Zone-driven under Bylaw 7600-2019: RS-1 requires 668.0 m² (18.0 m width, 27.0 m depth), RS-2 requires 0.4 ha, RS-3 requires 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha depending on the zone text, and the agricultural zones run from A-1 at 2.0 ha to A-3 at 8.0 ha — and ALR land also needs ALC permission to subdivide outside limited exceptions. (§§2–3 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide land in the Thornhill Urban Reserve now?** The OCP holds Urban Reserve land for long-term future urban services and housing, and development waits for specific triggers and milestones. The live page's specific figures (about 343 hectares, a 2.0 ha minimum, a 100,000-population trigger) are UNVERIFIED — do not price a Thornhill parcel on a subdivision date. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same purchase as a freehold lot?** No. A bare-land strata lot sits inside a strata corporation: Form B, strata fees, bylaws (only a 55+ age bylaw is legal; rental bylaws are void), a CRF and — for stratas with 5+ lots — a five-year depreciation report cycle. A freehold lot has none of that. Whether any current feed parcel is bare-land strata is UNVERIFIED — check title. (§§2–3 above)
- **Q: Can I build a second home on a rural Maple Ridge lot?** On ALR land, one additional detached residence is permitted under the ALC frame where conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — with the principal residence capped at 500 m² and any suite inside it; City bylaws must also permit it, and off-reserve lots follow the City's suite and SSMUH rules instead. (§3 above)
- **Q: How do I check whether a lot is serviced?** Parcel-level, with the City: whether municipal water, sanitary sewer and storm connections are at the lot line, or the lot needs a well and a Sewerage System Regulation onsite sewage system installed by an Authorized Person. No feed parcel's servicing is verified in this file. (§2 above)
- **Q: Where is new strata product most likely to appear on this ground?** Where the adopted plans designate it: the Lougheed Transit Corridor (adopted January 27, 2026, western boundary to 221 Street) and the Albion Area Plan ground (adopted October 28, 2025) — plan frames, not lot inventory. On ALR land, residences cannot be stratified at all. (§3 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a lot purchase here?** PTT on the general tiers — on the §3 worked example ($800,000, illustration only) the bill is $14,000 with no exemption. GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — the live page's GST and lender down-payment positions are UNVERIFIED in the research; confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
