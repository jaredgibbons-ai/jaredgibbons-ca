- **Q: Is a townhouse lot "land" I own fee-simple?** No — a townhouse strata lot is strata tenure: Form B, strata fees and registered bylaws govern it (§3). A bare-land strata lot is also strata tenure on the lot itself; only a non-strata title is fee-simple land. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot under this band instead of a townhome?** No lot offering is verified or counted in this band file — the feed is townhouse product by definition, and no named subdivision or bare-land strata plan was verified for the band. - **Q: Where is most of Maple Ridge's attached stock?** East Central (120 listings) and West Central (78) dominate the combined pool; the newer areas the page leads with are thinner — Cottonwood 34, Albion 18, Silver Valley 15 — and Silver Valley recorded no attached sale in Sep 2026. (§1 above)
- **Q: What does the Lougheed Transit Corridor plan change for a lot near Lougheed Highway?** Policy direction, not zoning: the plan (adopted Jan 27, 2026, Bylaw 7781-2021) directs growth around four future BRT stations to 221 Street and adds development permit guidelines, but the City states it does not change zoning or approve construction. Price the parcel's current zone; treat corridor upside as plan-stage. (§3 above)

GST on a strata townhome resale versus a developer sale is a separate question — for vacant land, the CRA GI-003 level in §3 governs. (§3 above)
- **Q: What strata documents matter most on a townhouse lot?** The page's own list: the Form B, two years of council and AGM minutes, the latest depreciation report (roof, siding, window and deck timelines), pet and parking bylaws, and the strata insurance policy — plus garage layout and backyard tenure (common vs limited common property), which are unit-and-plan questions. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve the Albion and Cottonwood townhome areas?** The Ministry-confirmed names are in §1 — but SD42 catchments are address-based with no locator result on record, and three addresses on the live page differ from the Ministry file: confirm any complex's catchment by exact address, using the Ministry addresses. (§1, §5 above)
- **Q: How do buyers here commute?** West Coast Express from Port Haney Station (22300 River Road) to Waterfront, Maple Meadows Station on the west side, and the R3 RapidBus from Haney Place Exchange to Coquitlam Central; the Langley–Haney Place BRT is in detailed planning (22 km, 10-minute peak service, timelines not yet determined).
