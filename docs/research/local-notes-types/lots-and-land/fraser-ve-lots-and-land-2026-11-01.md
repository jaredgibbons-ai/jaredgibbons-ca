## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Fraser VE has no meaningful bare-land market — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community and no presale LOT offering was verified on any source in the research or the siblings. Land here trades as individual fee-simple house lots on the side streets off the corridor — almost always with a house, suite or laneway house on them — priced substantially on R1-1 multiplex math, plus commercial frontage lots on Fraser Street itself. Everything named below is built product, a plan expectation, or a queued item — never a lot for sale.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **Side-street house lots:** freehold houses on the side streets under the R1-1 District Schedule, many with secondary suites or laneway houses as the schedule permits; strata condos and mixed-use buildings sit on the Fraser Street frontage itself. A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. The split of frontage versus side-street tenure by street is UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Lot character, as the research states it:** R1-1 minimum site areas run from 306 m² (single detached house, duplex, or 3–4-unit multiple dwelling; minimum frontage 10.0 m) through 464 m² (5 units; 13.4 m) to 557 m² (6–8 units; 15.1 m). These are schedule minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size; the zone and dimensions of a specific lot are confirmed parcel by parcel. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Serviced position:** Vancouver homes are on City water and sewer services billed through the City's utility billing; a 2026 utility dollar figure for a Fraser VE house confirmed on a City source is UNVERIFIED. Servicing capacity for a specific multiplex or subdivision proposal is confirmed with the City, parcel by parcel. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** ALR land inside the corridor is UNVERIFIED in the research — no ALR parcel is confirmed and no split is stated. (Research file above.)
- **Flood position:** the corridor is inland from the Fraser River, but the City's designated floodplain dataset covers the Fraser River coastal floodplain at the south end of the city, and land in the floodplain is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for setbacks and flood construction levels. Whether a specific corridor parcel — including the Marine Drive end — is in the floodplain is UNVERIFIED parcel by parcel. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, R1-1 District Schedule:** the residential side streets sit under R1-1 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m), 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. The district schedule for the Fraser Street commercial frontage itself is UNVERIFIED in the research. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plans:** the Kensington-Cedar Cottage Vision and the Sunset and Victoria-Fraserview / Killarney Visions apply to the local areas the corridor runs through, alongside the citywide Vancouver Plan; the Villages Planning Program position for Fraser and 33rd is in Section 2. Because "Fraser" is not a local area, the governing vision for a lot is the one for its official local area — confirm which one before relying on any plan claim. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/share-your-thoughts-on-vancouver-villages-nov-2025.aspx
- **Subdivision — Subdivision By-law No. 5208 (fresh check, Nov 2, 2026):** Vancouver subdivision is governed by the Subdivision By-law (No. 5208), whose Schedule A sets minimum parcel width and area standards by district and sub-area category, with the Approving Officer administering applications; its fee schedule was most recently amended for 2026 by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026). A fee-simple split of a corridor lot must clear both the R1-1 schedule and the Subdivision By-law category for its block — neither is assumed from a neighbouring lot's history. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states them:** Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit framework for these streets IS the R1-1 schedule above — house plus secondary suite and laneway house, duplex, or a multiplex to 6 strata units / 8 rental units by lot size — so the "SSMUH question" on a Fraser lot is a per-lot schedule check (306 / 464 / 557 m² and frontage), not a separate overlay. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Floodplain standards:** land in the City's designated floodplain — which covers the Fraser River coastal floodplain at the city's south end — is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for setbacks and flood construction levels. Check the specific parcel in the City dataset before pricing any south-end lot. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Bare-land strata / strata rules (the BC strata block — applies in full to any strata lot on the corridor, and would apply to a bare-land strata lot if one were offered):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw, and Vancouver runs its own short-term rental licensing layer); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no corridor ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, asserted in this program only where the horse-properties research documents it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Fraser lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%, Vancouver only:** every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration; homes declared or deemed vacant are taxed at 3% of assessed taxable value, the 2025 tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. A vacant Fraser lot or a house lot held empty between purchase and build carries this annual step — confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Speculation tax + purchase prohibition:** no Fraser VE-specific speculation-tax position is documented in this file's research beyond the City tax above — none imported. The federal non-Canadian purchase-prohibition position for corridor land is UNCONFIRMED in this file: the research cites no prohibition source for Fraser VE, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Vancouver property taxes:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot or a lot subdivision for sale on the Fraser corridor?** No verified one. The corridor is fully built out — land trades as house lots (usually with a house, suite or laneway house on them), frontage lots under existing buildings, and strata units. No presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What does the R1-1 math do to a side-street lot's value?** A qualifying lot can hold a house with a secondary suite and a laneway house, or a multiplex of up to 6 units (up to 8 rental units), with minimum site areas of 306 m² / 464 m² / 557 m² and frontages of 10.0 m / 13.4 m / 15.1 m by unit count — so site area and frontage, not just the house, drive the price. Measure before you compare. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Can I split a Fraser lot into two fee-simple lots?** Only if both resulting parcels clear the R1-1 schedule and the Subdivision By-law No. 5208 Schedule A category for the block, through the Approving Officer — a neighbour's earlier split does not grandfather yours. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm
- **Q: Is Avalon Mews a Fraser-area land opportunity I should track?** It is a built strata townhouse complex on the former Avalon Dairy site at Wales Street — and it is QUEUED in this program: the City record places the site in Victoria-Fraserview while MLS files the complex under Killarney VE, so it is counted in neither area's files. There is no lot offering in it in any case; you would buy a strata townhouse, in whichever sub-area's file ultimately holds it. https://council.vancouver.ca/20140610/documents/p3.pdf
- **Q: Does the Fraser and 33rd village designation make a frontage lot worth more?** It changes expectations, not entitlements: the Villages Plan is to be implemented through private development and City-initiated rezonings with retail expansion planned — until a rezoning is enacted for the specific lot, the R1-1 / frontage schedule in force is what you own. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/share-your-thoughts-on-vancouver-villages-nov-2025.aspx
- **Q: Is the Marine Drive end of the corridor in the floodplain?** The City's designated floodplain covers the Fraser River coastal floodplain at the south end of the city, with Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements for parcels in it — check the specific parcel in the City dataset before pricing any south-end lot, including land under existing buildings. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: Will I pay the Empty Homes Tax on a lot I hold while I design a build?** Vancouver's tax is 3% of assessed taxable value on homes declared or deemed vacant, and every owner must file the annual declaration regardless — a property held vacant can be caught, so confirm the declaration position and any exemption with the City before closing, not after. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003); and the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not to the bare lot itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Is there any bare-land strata on the corridor?** None verified — the corridor's small strata product (including the fourplex form at Fraser E49) is building-form strata in the townhomes sibling. If a bare-land strata lot were offered, treat it as strata in full: Form B, fees and bylaws on a lot of land. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Fraser lot?** By exact address only — the one settled locator chain in this file (530 East 41st Avenue → Sir Alexander Mackenzie Elementary → John Oliver Secondary) confirms only that address, even though John Oliver sits on the corridor itself. Run the address in the SD39 locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
