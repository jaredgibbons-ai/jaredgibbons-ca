## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Township:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs; the district's farm and acreage homes sit in the rural zones, including RU-2, and zone assignments for a parcel come from the bylaw Map Schedules through the Township's GeoSource. The RU-2 minimum lot area and frontage were not confirmed from a readable bylaw section: UNVERIFIED. Minimum lot sizes (s.110.1, as reproduced in a Township council report): SR-1 — 3,716 m² / 40 m frontage; SR-2 — 8,094 m² / 45 m; SR-3 — 1,765 m² / 27.45 m; RU-1 — 1.7 ha; RU-2 / RU-3 — 8.0 ha; urban R zones from 464.5 m². City of Langley bylaws do NOT apply here. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024):** 3 units on eligible lots of 280 m² or smaller and 4 units on larger eligible lots up to 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible for the multi-unit tiers, and on lots larger than 4,050 m² the rules allow one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit in addition to the primary unit depending on zoning. **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020.** https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Suites and coach houses — Township:** secondary suite cap 120 m². A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata. **LIVE CONFLICT — flagged, not resolved:** s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **R-CL compact lots — Township s.403:** minimum lot area 220 m² to maximum LOT area 464.5 m². A "355 m²" figure is nowhere in the bylaw and is not used in this file. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — Township:** Approving Officer approval under the Land Title Act; servicing under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED per the verified ledger. A permit is required to cut or remove a protected tree under Tree Protection Bylaw 2019 No. 5478. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **Flood — the land rule that prices this district:** the Salmon River valley is Fraser River floodplain land — the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station was originally built to help mitigate flooding of the surrounding agricultural lands during freshet, provides flood protection when Fraser River levels rise, and has flood box gates that close as river levels rise; Council has authorized staff to proceed with detailed design and tendering for the pump station upgrades. Parcel-level floodplain mapping and watercourse setback distances for a specific parcel: UNVERIFIED — the Salmon River is salmon-bearing, so setbacks and riparian rules can limit building envelopes near the river; check with the Township before planning a build. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **ALR block (farm land in the valley is in the ALR in places; parcel status UNVERIFIED per lot):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR; no flat farm-class figure is asserted for this slug because this file's own research does not document one. Confirm a parcel on the ALC maps. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, where met):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Water and septic:** rural properties may use wells — search GWELLS for a parcel's record, and note well reports are due within 90 days of constructing, altering or decommissioning a well; Fraser Health requires an authorized person to file a record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair of an onsite system, and a letter of certification after construction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons
- **Taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on an $800,000 lot purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$800,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000** ($6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer). Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. (Worked example at $3.5M for reference: $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — a townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met, never silently carried.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: Is the Salmon River district in a floodplain?** The valley is Fraser River floodplain land; the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station provides flood protection when Fraser River levels rise and was built to mitigate flooding of the surrounding agricultural lands during freshet, and upgrades are in detailed design and tendering. Parcel-level mapping: UNVERIFIED. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Q: Is the land in the ALR?** Farm land in the valley is in the Agricultural Land Reserve in places; confirm a specific parcel on the ALC ALR maps. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can an acreage add a second dwelling?** On ALR land the ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its size rules are met (up to 90 m² at 40 ha or less, up to 186 m² over 40 ha), subject to Township bylaws; the Township's small-scale multi-unit rules also allow a suite or accessory unit on lots over 4,050 m² depending on zoning. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put four homes — or six — on a Salmon River lot?** Bylaw 6020 allows 3 units on eligible lots of 280 m² or smaller and 4 units on larger eligible lots up to 4,050 m², with lots over 4,050 m² ineligible for the multi-unit tiers. **There is no 6-plex tier in Bylaw 6020** — most Salmon River acreages sit over that cap and fall to one unit plus a suite or accessory unit depending on zoning. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: How did Salmon River sell in September 2026?** Detached 22 / 5 / 23% — the second-highest published detached ratio in the Langley set, on a small base; Condos & Townhomes 4 / 0 / NA. No sub-area median is published; combined-Langley medians (detached $1,345,000; attached $611,000) are community context only. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/stats/
- **Q: Which schools serve a Salmon River address?** For the sample address 4452 256 Street, the SD35 School Locator returns North Otter Elementary and D.W. Poppy Secondary, with no middle school shown; other addresses may fall in different catchments. Coghlan Fundamental Elementary at 4452 256 Street is a district choice school, and the guide page also names Peterson Road Elementary at 23422 47 Avenue. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- **Q: What bus serves the district?** Route 562 runs on Glover Road at the Fort Langley edge of the district, between Langley Centre and Walnut Grove / Carvolth Exchange; no route inside the valley roads was confirmed. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf
- **Q: How much PTT on an $800K Salmon River parcel?** $14,000 — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) plus 2% on the next $600,000 ($12,000); $6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer. (§3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a vacant parcel?** Generally yes if the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided; generally no for an individual's personal-use resale, per CRA GI-003 — and the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
