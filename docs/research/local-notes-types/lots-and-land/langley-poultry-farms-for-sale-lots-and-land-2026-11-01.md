- **ALR block (the governing frame for this sector's land):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal residence ≤500 m² total floor area (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), suite within the principal residence only, one additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha), where local bylaws also permit it; local governments may regulate further but cannot be more permissive than the ALC.
