## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Township frame (Township ground ONLY):** Zoning Bylaw 2500. SSMUH Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — an eligible lot (Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water AND sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units if 280 m² or smaller, or 4 units if between 280 m² and 4,050 m² (1 acre); lots larger than 4,050 m² are not eligible. **There is NO 6-unit tier in the Township.** Secondary suites: s.102 caps a suite at 120 m², one per principal dwelling unit. Coach house = fee-simple second unit under s.102 (accessory unit only within the second storey of a detached garage), never strata. **Live conflict, flagged not resolved:** s.102 as amended July 21, 2025 (Bylaw 6147) lists suite locations including within a townhouse/rowhouse, while the Township's January 2025 Secondary Suites guide position differs — both documents stand by date. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Township R-CL:** s.403 = 220 m² minimum LOT area / 464.5 m² maximum LOT area. The "355 m²" figure is NOWHERE in the bylaw. (Standing correction.)
- **Township subdivision/servicing:** Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED as the servicing bylaw for Township subdivision. Per-lot servicing costs UNCONFIRMED, none stated.
- **Township / Willoughby plan frame:** Willoughby Community Plan = Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025). Willoughby Plan Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 = first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY, Public Hearing directed June 15, 2026 — never present as repeals/adoptions done. (Standing corrections.)
- **City frame (City ground ONLY):** Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300, cited by number — adoption date UNVERIFIED. SSMUH Bylaw 3284, adopted June 17, 2024: RS1/RS2 lots permit 4 units, or 6 if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Suites: lesser of 100 m² and 40% of gross floor area; registered owner must reside on the lot (s.2.4); carriage/garden ground-floor unit capped at 100 m². City apartment roster exactly 3 (Parkway Estates; Parkway II; Glenmont Park, 5375 205 St); Morningside Estates, 5365 205 St, UNCONFIRMED, never merged. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3284
- **ALR block (where ALR ground is confirmed per parcel):** subdivision in the ALR needs ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — asserted here in short form for ALR parcels; the full documented form lives in the farm-sector file for this batch, whose own research documents it. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **BC strata block (applies in full to any bare-land strata lot):** Form B diligence (within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page) with depreciation report, CRF and levies; depreciation reports 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. **Worked examples (fully residential, no exemption unless stated):** $500,000 → $2,000 + $6,000 = **$8,000**; $700,000 → **$12,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000** (fully-qualifying first-time buyer: **$6,000**); $900,000 → **$16,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → $2,000 + $26,000 = **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 (further 2% over $3M) = **$93,000**. A $78,000 figure for $3.5M is an OPEN reconciliation item where met in siblings — this file follows **$93,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
