## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: Braemar has no meaningful bare-land market. No named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community, no named condo building or townhome complex, and no presale LOT offering was verified inside Braemar on any source checked — the research file and all three siblings record the same position. What trades as land here is the individual fee-simple detached lot in the R1/R2 zones, almost always with a house on it, bought for the lot, the French Immersion draw and the suite/coach-house potential the District now permits. No lot count or lot price is stated.**

### Fee-simple lots — the only land form

- **R1/R2 lot fabric (Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525, as amended by Bylaw 8799):** the R1 Rural and Suburban Detached Residential Zone has minimums of 4,000 m² / 30 m width / 34 m depth; the R2 Urban Detached Residential Zone has minimums of 660 m² / 18 m width / 34 m depth, with modifiers R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m, R2(S2) at 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m. The applicable detached-zone minimums run the full 335–4,000 m² range — the zone on a single lot is confirmed through the District's GeoWeb. A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Secondary forms as lot value:** R2 allows a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite in the principal building and a coach house; since June 2024 District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. December 2023 changes let a secondary suite be up to 49% of the house with no minimum size and raised the maximum coach house height to two storeys. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Bare-land strata: none verified.** No bare-land strata community name with an address inside Braemar was confirmed in the research or siblings: none found; strata product in Braemar generally is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed on an official source). Stated plainly for any strata lot form, if one ever surfaced: a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — Form B / strata fees / bylaws would apply to the lot (Section 3) — but no such stock is documented here. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Serviced vs unserviced:** District single-family utility fees are published for 2026 (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60; combined single-family utilities $2,571.20) — the District-serviced position as the District states it. Parcel-level septic or well servicing is UNVERIFIED in the research (UNVERIFIED item 10) — no lot-level well/septic claim is made. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **ALR / flood:** parcel-level ALR status and floodplain designation for a single lot are both UNVERIFIED in the research — no ALR split is stated and none is invented. The hazard frame that does apply is the District's Development Permit Areas (Section 3), not an ALR frame. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)
- **Creek-shaped land:** the Mosquito Creek and MacKay Creek ravines west of the area are protected through the North Lonsdale–Delbrook plan's development permit areas for the natural environment — lots near the ravines warrant a GeoWeb property-level check before buildable area is priced. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP (District only):** District OCP Bylaw 7900; the local plan is the North Lonsdale–Delbrook Official Community Plan, Bylaw 6750; Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525 (R1/R2 table in Sections 1–2), as amended including by Bylaw 8799. City of North Vancouver bylaws (OCP 8400 / Zoning 6700) are NEVER imported into District ground. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/418109.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **SSMUH, as the District states it:** the District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. Separately, since June 2024 a principal dwelling + secondary suite + coach house (three units) are permitted on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; the suite can be up to 49% of the house with no minimum size, and a coach house can be two storeys (December 2023 changes). Price suite/coach-house potential from the bylaw and the lot's GEOweb classification, not from a listing template. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision runs against the Section 525 zone minimums (area / width / depth) for the lot's R1/R2 zone, confirmed per lot through GeoWeb. A District subdivision/servicing bylaw NUMBER could not be confirmed on a primary District source — held UNCONFIRMED (a fresh search Nov 2, 2026 surfaced the CITY of North Vancouver's Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw 2010 No. 8014 — a City bylaw, expressly NOT a District authority and never cited as one here). - **Hazard Development Permit Areas (OCP Schedule B):** the District OCP establishes DPAs for Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard, and the District publishes property-level reports through GeoWeb. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there vacant land for sale in Braemar?** No meaningful bare-land market is documented — no vacant-lot subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified. "Land" here is an existing fee-simple R1/R2 detached lot, usually with a house on it. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How big are Braemar lots?** By zone minimum: R2 standard 660 m² / 18 m width / 34 m depth; R2(S1) 335 m² / 10 m; R2(S2) 1,100 m² / 24 m; R1 and R2(S3) 4,000 m² / 30 m. Confirm the single lot's zone and modifiers through the District's GeoWeb. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Q: Can a Braemar lot carry three units?** Yes, in form: since June 2024 a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house (three units) are permitted on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary; the suite can be up to 49% of the house with no minimum size, and a coach house can reach two storeys. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Q: What does the SSMUH classification say about my lot?** On the District's GEOweb classification, non-exempt lots potentially allow up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. Pull the lot's own classification report. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Q: Can I subdivide a large R1 lot?** Only against the Section 525 minimums (R1: 4,000 m² / 30 m / 34 m) plus District subdivision processing — and a District servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in this file, so no subdivision promise can be made from it. Confirm with the District. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Q: What hazard checks does a Braemar lot need?** The OCP Schedule B DPAs — natural environment, streamside, wildfire, creek hazard and slope hazard — with property-level reports through GeoWeb; lots near the Mosquito/MacKay Creek ravines are the obvious check. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **Q: Is the lot serviced?** District single-family utilities are published (2026 combined $2,571.20; water $932.80, sewer/drainage $1,255.60). Parcel-level septic/well status is UNVERIFIED in the research — confirm the specific lot with the District. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **Q: Will GST apply to my lot purchase?** At CRA GI-003 level: a developer/business sale or a newly subdivided lot is generally taxable; an individual's personal-use resale is generally exempt. Confirm per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which school serves a Braemar lot?** The one documented locator run is for the school's own address — 3600 Mahon Avenue → École Braemar Elementary → Carson Graham Secondary (SD44 locator, run 2026-11-01). Every other address must be run individually; no other Braemar catchment is verified. https://braemar.sd44.ca/
- **Q: What is happening at the Delbrook Lands, and does it affect Braemar lots?** At 600 West Queens Road, south-west of Braemar: a new neighbourhood park (north portion) plus a four-storey low-to-moderate-income rental and seniors' care building (south portion), under Bylaws 8397/8398/8399 (2019). It is a District project, not lots for sale — disclose its phasing to buyers on the closest streets. https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=4100529
