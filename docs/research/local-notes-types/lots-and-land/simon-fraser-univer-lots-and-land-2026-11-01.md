## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no freehold land to buy inside UniverCity — stated plainly at the top, because it is the whole land story for this ground. The land remains under SFU ownership; a buyer here buys a strata lot and a lease interest, never the land. Outside UniverCity, the sub-area's detached listings sit in pockets at the mountain's edges — no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata plan, or lot offering on those edges was verified either.**

### The UniverCity tenure record — documented as a tenure record, never as land stock

- **How the land was created, as the Trust states it:** the land remains under SFU ownership: SFU Community Trust (SFU's trustee corporation) leases fully serviced, subdivided parcels to developers through 99-year lease agreements, so UniverCity homes are leasehold, not freehold. The servicing and subdivision happened at the Trust-to-developer level — a buyer never buys one of those parcels; the parcels become strata developments. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/
- **What a buyer actually holds:** a leasehold prepaid strata interest — the strata lot plus the remaining term of the 99-year ground lease. A current Origin listing states the structure in terms: "Leasehold expires in 2109" (9150 University High Street). The condos sibling counts 7 developments on this tenure — Crescent Court, The Nest, Origin, Altitude, Centre Block, The Hub, Terraces at the Peak — **all leasehold prepaid strata, zero freehold**; the townhomes sibling counts 0 new on the same basis, its townhouse-form units (Crescent Court's 14 townhomes, Terraces at the Peak and Slopes Mews units) being leasehold units inside those developments. None of that product is documented as land in this file. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/9150-university-high-street/412 (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/simon-fraser-univer-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **No fee-simple lots inside UniverCity:** the research states it directly — there are no conventional fee-simple residential lots inside UniverCity itself: every market home is a strata unit on SFU's 99-year prepaid ground lease, so "lot size" comparisons between buildings come down to unit size, floor and outlook rather than land. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/simon-fraser-univer.md)
- **SFU student residences and campus housing are not market housing** and are not land stock in any form. (Research file above.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **The ground lease is the governing document (this area's first rule):** SFU Community Trust leases parcels to developers on 99-year leases and the land remains SFU's. Before anything else, confirm the lease commencement date, remaining term, whether rent is prepaid for the full term, and any rent-review or end-of-term provisions — the lease, not the building, is what a buyer's lender and lawyer will scrutinize. Lender treatment of leasehold (some lenders cap mortgage terms against the remaining lease term) is general practice guidance in the research file — UNVERIFIED against any specific lender. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/
- **City plan and zoning:** citywide direction is the **Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** (adopted December 9, 2025, replacing the 1998 OCP Bylaw); UniverCity is governed by its own OCP framework from the 1996 approvals (up to 4,536 units on about 65 hectares). Zoning is the **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026)** — the former Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) is superseded and is never cited as current in this file. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing — HELD UNCONFIRMED (the process is documented instead):** Burnaby's servicing base bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in this program (an amendment bylaw, 14667 (2024), is on record, but the base number is not adopted here as fact) — no servicing number is asserted for Burnaby. Inside UniverCity the subdivision question does not arise for a buyer at all: parcels were subdivided and serviced by the Trust before leasing (§2), so there is no buyer-side subdivision route on this ground. An edge-pocket subdivision question is taken to the City for the parcel.
- **SSMUH position:** UniverCity's own parcels are developed under its 1996 OCP/lease framework, not lot-by-lot SSMUH (55-plus sibling, citing the City's zoning rewrite project). No SSMUH unit count for an SFU-ground lot is asserted here. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Strata frame (the form every UniverCity purchase actually takes):** a UniverCity strata is still a strata corporation under the Strata Property Act, so Form B diligence under s. 59 applies (fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page) — read alongside the ground lease, which the Form B does not answer. Depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for strata corporations with 5 or more lots since July 1, 2024; at least 10% of the annual operating budget to the CRF since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (the rule governs strata bylaws, not the SFU ground lease itself); only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw, and the 55-plus sibling verifies no UniverCity building carries a sourced 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no SFU-ground ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and it is not asserted for this ground. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Burnaby property taxes (2026):** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax in Burnaby:** the EHT is a City of Vancouver tax (3%) and does not apply here — stated expressly because this sub-area's university name invites the confusion; it also does not apply on UEL/university-vw ground, which is a different ground in any case. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). PTT treatment of a specific leasehold interest is confirmed on the lease before an offer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED — and the GI-003 frame is about vacant land, which is not what a UniverCity strata purchase is; 5% GST applies to new construction (including an Arbour presale purchase), not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits held in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21) — the live regime for an Arbour purchase; the disclosure statement's ground-lease terms are the document to read inside the 7 days. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Foreclosure / court sales:** no foreclosure or court-sale position is documented in this file's research — none is asserted here. Form B and PTT/GST treatment on court sales: UNCONFIRMED, kept distinct from the CRA GI-003 position above.
- **Purchase prohibition:** the federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this ground is stated only as far as this file's own cited sources support — the research cites no prohibition source for this sub-area, so the position is UNCONFIRMED and is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a lot — freehold land — at SFU / UniverCity?** No. There are no conventional fee-simple residential lots inside UniverCity: SFU Community Trust leases fully serviced, subdivided parcels to developers on 99-year leases, and every market home is a leasehold strata unit on that structure. (§2 above; https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/)
- **Q: So what do I own when I buy at UniverCity?** A strata lot and a leasehold interest for the remaining term of the 99-year prepaid ground lease — one current listing states its building's lease expires in 2109. You do not own the land under the building. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/9150-university-high-street/412
- **Q: What should I read first — the Form B or the ground lease?** Both, in that order of risk: the Form B for the strata's finances and bylaws, and the ground lease for commencement date, remaining term, prepaid status and end-of-term provisions. The lease is what a lender and lawyer will scrutinize; some lenders cap mortgage terms against the remaining lease term (general guidance — UNVERIFIED per lender). (§3 above)
- **Q: Is Arbour a chance to buy a lot in the Slopes?** No — Arbour (9688 University Crescent) is a 178-home leasehold condominium and stacked-townhome presale. Its sales are REDMA presales of strata units, counted in the condos sibling — presale LOT offerings in this sub-area are zero. (§2 above)
- **Q: Are there any detached lots for sale on the mountain?** Detached homes are rare — 5 active detached listings in the sub-area in September 2026 (SnapStats, §1) — and sit mainly in pockets at the mountain's edges. No named bare-land subdivision or vacant-lot offering on those edges was verified. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/simon-fraser-univer.md)
- **Q: Does the remaining lease term show up in pricing between old and new buildings?** UNVERIFIED — the research file records this as a question for first-hand knowledge and no source checked publishes a term-versus-price comparison. Check each building's lease commencement on title instead of assuming. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is The Ambassador an SFU land or condo option in this file?** Neither — it is an apartment building at 7321 Halifax Street, counted in the Montecito condos file only. MLS files it under Simon Fraser Univer.; its research geography is Montecito. (§2 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve a UniverCity address?** University Highlands Elementary sits inside the community and Burnaby Mountain Secondary is the on-mountain secondary — but the only SD41 locator run documented (8800 Eastlake Drive) returned Cameron Elementary / Burnaby Mountain Secondary, so catchment follows the address, not proximity. Other addresses: UNVERIFIED — run the address in the SD41 locator. (§1 above)

Treat it as upside, not current transit or current land value. https://www.translink.ca/news/2023/november/translink%20seeking%20feedback%20on%20burnaby%20mountain%20gondola
- **Q: Is there any 55+ or fee-simple product on the mountain?** Neither: the 55-plus sibling verifies zero 55+ stock of any tenure on Burnaby Mountain, and there are no fee-simple residential lots inside UniverCity. A buyer who needs either has to look off the mountain. (~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/simon-fraser-univer-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
