## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

**Two municipal frames — apply each ONLY to its own city's parcels. Establish which city a parcel is in before anything else.**

- **Surrey frame — OCP and zoning (Surrey parcels only):** the current Official Community Plan is the Surrey OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900 ("Surrey 2050"), adopted June 15, 2026**; the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is NOT current and is never cited as such. Zoning is under Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. (City of Surrey, OCP: https://www.surrey.ca/planning-development/plans-strategies/surrey-official-community-plan; research file §4)
- **Surrey SSMUH and suites (Surrey parcels only):** SSMUH implemented July 8, 2024 — up to 3 units on lots under 280 m², up to 4 units on lots over 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Secondary suites: one per dwelling unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite, not a separate strata lot, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area. https://www.surrey.ca/planning-development/housing/small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/secondary-suites
- **Surrey subdivision and servicing (Surrey parcels only):** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, confirmed per parcel in the City's mapping. The City's subdivision/servicing bylaw is the **Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** — verified on the City's own Land Development pages in an earlier batch of this program (that sourcing is stated here because this slug's own research pass opened no servicing page); servicing runs through the Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Surrey streamside (Surrey parcels only):** streamside setbacks are defined in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and vary by stream class, with the Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit process alongside — NEVER a single riparian figure; a creek- or river-edge parcel's setback is read against its stream class in the City's mapping. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Surrey short-term rentals and taxes (Surrey parcels only):** STRs permitted only in the operator's own principal residence, owner-only, with a City business licence at **$358/year** as stated on the City's licence page — DISCLOSED CONFLICT: a City of Surrey 2024 corporate report proposed $350/year plus fees; this file carries $358 and directs the reader to the licence page before quoting. Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/short-term-rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **White Rock frame — strictly separate (White Rock parcels only):** White Rock addresses follow the City of White Rock's own bylaws — Zoning Bylaw **2024 No. 2506** and OCP Bylaw **2017 No. 2220**, which belong to White Rock ONLY and are never applied to Surrey ground, exactly as Surrey's bylaws are never applied here. White Rock SSMUH caps under Bylaw 2024 No. 2506 (3 units ≤280 m² / 4 units >280 m² / 6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop, adopted June 27, 2024) are recorded in the program's rules-gaps file and carried in the companion catchments file, not re-verified here; White Rock tax dates and zone-by-zone rules for a specific listing are UNVERIFIED in this file. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, via the research file)
- **ALR block (applies parcel by parcel, in either city, only where the parcel is in the reserve):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for a Sea Ridge Bays-type lot, wherever one trades):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. Sea Ridge Bays' 55+ position is listing-stated in §2; the registered bylaw on the Form B is the verification. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land, and both thresholds sit far below this feed's visible prices. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** a $3,500,000 purchase, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $36,000 (2% × $1,800,000) + $45,000 (3% × $1,500,000) + $10,000 (further 2% × the $500,000 residential portion above $3,000,000) = **$93,000, including the further 2% tier**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
