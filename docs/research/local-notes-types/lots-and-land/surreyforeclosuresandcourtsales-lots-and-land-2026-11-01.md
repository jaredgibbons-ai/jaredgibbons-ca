## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Step 1 — petition:** a foreclosure/redemption proceeding must be started by petition in the Supreme Court — Rule 21-7(1); all persons whose interest is sought to be extinguished must be respondents — Rule 21-7(2); the petitioner must serve the court's foreclosure information notice, Form 125 — Rule 21-7(4.1). Rule 21-7 as read states no demand step before the petition; a prescribed-mortgage-terms demand is UNVERIFIED in this slug's research. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Order / redemption:** the court may order payment of what is due within a redemption period the court fixes — Rule 21-7(5)(b) (no fixed length in the Rules); it may certify the amount due — Rule 21-7(5)(c),(e). In default, a final order of foreclosure may be granted — Rule 21-7(6). A party of record may apply at any time for an order that the property be sold — Rule 21-7(7). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Confirming a sale:** even before redemption expires, the person with conduct of sale may apply for an order confirming the sale, directing proceeds and vesting title — Rule 21-7(9). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Conduct of sale / Form 60 / PD-66 (settled treatment; this slug's research held these UNVERIFIED in its own pass — see FILE STATUS):** conduct of sale and court directions (reserve/minimum price, bid and offer rights, particulars) sit in Rule 13-5 (sale may be by contract conditional on court approval — Rule 13-5(4)(b)); the result of a sale by court order is certified in Form 60 — Rule 13-5(6); Practice Direction PD-66 governs foreclosure proceedings. Bid timing/manner under PD-66 for a specific file: get the conduct-of-sale party's written instructions for that file. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo106/loo106/168_2009_02
- **Surrey city frame:** OCP = **Surrey Official Community Plan Bylaw 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** (standing correction; Bylaw 18020 is never cited as current). Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs zoning. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Suites / STRs:** Surrey secondary-suite cap is 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area (standing correction). Short-term rentals are permitted for owners only, with the owner residing at the property as principal residence; licence figure CONFLICT — the City's short-term rental page states $358 per year (plus any applicable application fees), while the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350 per year + fees — $358 is carried here with the $350 figure disclosed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R094.pdf
- **Floodplain:** Surrey's coastal floodplain stretches from Boundary Bay and Mud Bay along the Nicomekl and Serpentine Rivers toward Cloverdale and Newton, includes the Little Campbell River area, and is about 20% of Surrey's land area; most is dyke-protected — flood construction/insurance is address-specific for any low-lying court-sale parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain
- **ALR (Surrey parcels where present):** subdivision needs ALC approval; residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, by Oct 31) — flat "$2,500+" is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Form B availability on a court sale: UNCONFIRMED.** Ordinary-resale strata block (which may or may not be reachable on a court sale): Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation 5-yr cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; 10% CRF since Nov 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). Do not promise a Form B package for a court-ordered bare-land strata lot until the specific file confirms what is produced. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How does a BC foreclosure start?** By petition under Rule 21-7(1), with all interest-holders as respondents and Form 125 served with the petition. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: How long is the redemption period?** No fixed length — the period the court fixes under Rule 21-7(5)(b). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: Is my accepted offer final?** No — conduct of sale applies under Rule 21-7(9) for confirmation, proceeds direction and vesting, even before redemption expires; until that order, the sale is not final. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: Can someone outbid me at approval?** Treat it as a real possibility: bid/offer rights sit in the court's Rule 13-5 directions and PD-66 practice — get the specific file's bid instructions in writing before spending on inspections. In Surrey's deep condo market, assume other buyers are watching the same listing. (§3.)
- **Q: Where do I check a Surrey court file — and which registry holds it?** Court Services Online eSearch (class "H - Foreclosure") shows file number, registry, parties and documents. Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry and the serving-registry mapping is UNVERIFIED — read the registry off the file itself; New Westminster is a working assumption in the research, not a fact. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- **Q: Will I get a Form B on a bare-land strata lot in foreclosure?** UNCONFIRMED — no source in the research states a general rule; what is produced is a per-file fact. (§3.)
- **Q: Do I pay PTT/GST the normal way on court-ordered land?** UNCONFIRMED — court-sale tax treatment is not stated in any source in the research; the general PTT and GI-003 frames in §3 are starting frames only, confirmed by your lawyer per transaction.
- **Q: Are foreclosures discounted in Surrey?** No foreclosure discount statistic exists in any official source — UNVERIFIED. General-market context only: Surrey detached sold at 95% sale-to-list with a 7.5% sales ratio in Sep 2026 — the general market was already negotiable, and that says nothing about any individual court sale. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Can I run a short-term rental in a Surrey court-sale house?** Only as an owner residing there as principal residence, with a business licence — carried at $358/yr from the City page, with the City 2024 corporate report's proposed $350/yr + fees disclosed as a conflicting figure. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Q: As a seller in difficulty, what is my window?** The court-fixed redemption period is the window to pay what is due or sell before a final order can be granted under Rule 21-7(6) — earlier advice is worth more. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
