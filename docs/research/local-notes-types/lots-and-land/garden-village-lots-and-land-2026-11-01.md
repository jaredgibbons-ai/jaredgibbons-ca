## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No meaningful bare-land market exists in Garden Village today. The land that "trades" is the fee-simple lot inside a single historic planned subdivision — the 1953 Garden Village subdivision — bought with a post-war house on it and increasingly priced for R1 SSMUH redevelopment. No bare-land strata plan, no named new development project and no presale lot offering was verified on any source in the research or the siblings, and the current market record is entirely detached (9 detached listings, 0 attached listings, September 2026).**

### The named subdivision — documented ONCE here, as history and lot fabric (not inventory)

- **Garden Village subdivision — Central Park historic neighbourhood, Burnaby (Heritage Burnaby).** Heritage Burnaby records that subdivisions began appearing in the Central Park neighbourhood in the 1920s, that growth slowed in the Depression and accelerated in the post-war boom, and that the Garden Village subdivision was undertaken in 1953 with a plan to build 500 modern homes on the 140-acre site; despite the closure of the interurban line in 1953, the neighbourhood remained an active commercial and residential centre. **That is the whole land record: a completed 1953 fee-simple subdivision whose lots are the stock. Nothing in it is offered as lots today, no original lot count beyond the planned 500 homes is asserted, and no lot size is stated — original lot dimensions are UNVERIFIED on a source in this pass.** https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?objectType_facet=Landmark%20Description%20&objectType_facet=Cartographic%20Material&ps=50&q=&sort=title_sort%20asc&decade_facet=1860&decade_facet=1930&p=1

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026.** Never cite the former 1965 Bylaw No. 4742 for a current Burnaby lot. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 SSMUH District in June 2024. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025,** replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; Burnaby's community plans are policy guidelines for rezoning and development applications. Read any Kingsway-side assembly assumption against the 2050 OCP map first. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. Patterson Station puts the sub-area's north-side lots closest to the 400 m test — the south-side streets are materially farther, so measure the test for the parcel. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision parcel area, shape and dimensions must conform to the zoning bylaw for the parcel's zone, under the Burnaby Subdivision Control and Development Servicing Bylaw (renamed from the Burnaby Subdivision Control Bylaw 1971 by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024; the City's servicing updates took effect September 30, 2024). Required works and services include highways and lanes, water distribution connected to the City system, sewage collection and drainage collection, built to the City's Engineering Design Criteria Manual standards; the City's Improvements Guide addresses servicing for developments with four or more primary dwelling units on a residential lot. The base bylaw's own number is UNCONFIRMED on a primary source read for this file, so no number is asserted for it — the process above is what is documented. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=77052 and https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-08/Servicing-Improvements-Guide.pdf

- **Hazards:** no area-wide hazard designation for Garden Village's residential streets was confirmed in the sources read; parcel-level hazard status is a property-specific check: UNVERIFIED lot by lot. (Research file above, §4.)
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Garden Village?** No verified one — the subdivision was built out from 1953 and land trades today as house lots with post-war houses on them. No bare-land strata plan, new named development or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What was the original Garden Village subdivision?** A 140-acre site undertaken in 1953 with a plan for 500 modern homes, inside what Heritage Burnaby treats as the Central Park historic neighbourhood — the reason the interior streets read as one planned subdivision. Original lot dimensions are UNVERIFIED on a source in this pass. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?objectType_facet=Landmark%20Description%20&objectType_facet=Cartographic%20Material&ps=50&q=&sort=title_sort%20asc&decade_facet=1860&decade_facet=1930&p=1
- **Q: What can a Garden Village lot become under the new zoning?** Under the R1 SSMUH district in Zoning Bylaw 2026 No. 14815: 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; multi-unit lots may be stratified — a stratified multiplex, not a bare-land strata lot. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Does the Patterson Station side price differently from the Rumble Street side?** The mechanism is documented even though no premium figure is: Patterson Station puts north-side lots closest to the 400 m frequent-transit test that unlocks 6 units, and the research warns any "walk to SkyTrain" claim must be measured for the specific address because the south-side streets are materially farther. Any dollar premium: UNVERIFIED. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- **Q: Are the Patterson towers part of Garden Village?** No — Grand Central Park Place and 6188 Patterson Ave are filed under Metrotown, and Sheffield Court II under Central Park BS, with Garden Village named only as a nearby neighbourhood. They are documented in the siblings precisely so they are not mis-counted here. (Sibling condos file, §2.)
- **Q: Is a Garden Village lot serviced?** The established streets sit inside Burnaby's municipal system on the City's utility wording (2026 detached water charge $652.33; sewer as a parcel tax for single-family homes) — confirm the specific parcel with the City. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does a lot purchase qualify for the first-time buyer GST rebate?** No — the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) applies to new housing, not bare land itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Garden Village lot?** By exact address only — Windsor, Clinton and Maywood-area elementaries and Moscrop and Burnaby South secondaries all sit around the edges, and the one settled locator run (5455 Rumble St → Nelson Elementary → Burnaby South Secondary) is for the secondary school's own address. Run the lot's address in the SD41 locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q: How thin is the record for pricing a teardown here?** Nine detached listings and 1 sale in September 2026 (11% ratio), no attached listings at all — build pricing from wider south-west Burnaby comparables and the Burnaby detached median of $1,825,000 over a longer window. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report (research file).
