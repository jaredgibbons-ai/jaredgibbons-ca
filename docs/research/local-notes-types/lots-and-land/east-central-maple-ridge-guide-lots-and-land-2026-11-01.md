## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market in East Central — stated plainly at the top. This is a built-up downtown and established-residential sub-area: land trades as built RS-1 fee-simple house lots, Town Centre strata lots under existing condo/townhome buildings, and redevelopment assemblies under the Town Centre plan — not as vacant subdivision lots. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no named bare-land strata plan, and no presale lot offering was verified on any source in the research. Do not present the Town Centre condo projects (Podium, Brickwater, Highpointe) as lot offerings — they are strata unit projects, documented in the condos sibling.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **RS-1 house lots (residential streets east and north of downtown):** single-detached housing on fee-simple lots under RS-1 (Single Detached Residential), Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², minimum lot width 18.0 m, minimum lot depth 27.0 m, maximum lot coverage 40%. These are the zone's subdivision minimums, not a claim that every existing lot meets them — older lots predate the current bylaw and vary lot by lot, UNCONFIRMED here. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Town Centre parcels:** Town Centre commercial, multi-family and comprehensive development zones under the same Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 govern the downtown core parcels; minimum lot sizes for those multi-family and comprehensive development zones are UNVERIFIED in the research and are not filled in here. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Servicing:** the research documents no parcel-level servicing statement for East Central (municipal water/sewer vs well/septic). The sub-area is the City's downtown and established residential core, but servicing for any single vacant or assembly parcel is UNCONFIRMED in this file — confirm with the City for the parcel. No well/septic position is asserted for this urban sub-area on this file's evidence. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/east-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** no Agricultural Land Reserve land in East Central was identified in the official sources checked; parcel-level ALR status can be checked on the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping. No ALR percentage split is stated. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Market thinness, labelled correctly:** SnapStats recorded East Central detached inventory 82 active / 5 sold (6% sales ratio) and "Condos & Townhomes" 120 active / 11 sold (9% sales ratio) in September 2026 — the largest attached inventory of any Maple Ridge sub-area. SnapStats has NO land category (Detached vs "Condos & Townhomes" combined; Maple Ridge community detached median $1,131,000 and attached median $525,000 that month) — no detached or attached figure is presented as a land figure. Maple Ridge IS a SnapStats-covered community; figures are community/sub-area context only. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report (research file above).

### Bare-land strata — its own form, present only as scattered lots

- **Form, stated plainly:** a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — the buyer owns a strata lot, pays strata fees, is bound by the strata bylaws, and diligence runs through the Form B, exactly as with any strata; what differs is that the lot itself is bare land / a detached-house lot rather than a unit in a shared building envelope. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **What is verified here:** listings in this sub-area include bare-land strata detached homes (for example at 11125 232 Street), documented as their own form in the townhomes sibling and NOT counted as townhouse stock there. No named bare-land strata development, strata plan number, lot count, or fee/bylaw detail for any East Central bare-land strata was verified on an official, developer or strata source in the research — all UNCONFIRMED. Do not quote a plan number, a lot count or a fee for a bare-land strata lot here until the title and Form B are pulled. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP:** Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs (RS-1 s. 605 minimums in Section 2). The Official Community Plan citation is CONTESTED — Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024, with an update adopted November 25, 2025 — flagged here wherever met and resolved nowhere in this file; quote planning policy for a specific lot from a current City document, not from either number alone. The Town Centre Area Plan (OCP s. 10.4) covers the west end. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading and https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- **Lougheed Transit Corridor — ADOPTED Jan 27, 2026:** Official Community Plan Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021 was given third reading and adopted at the Jan 27, 2026 council meeting (public hearing Jan 20, 2026; City news release Feb 4, 2026); the corridor runs from the City's western boundary to the Town Centre at 221 Street along Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road. Cite it as adopted. An earlier contrary reading of this plan's status exists in program material — any page or note still calling it proposed is stale. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP and https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- **Albion Area Plan:** adopted Oct 28, 2025 as part of the City's fall 2025 OCP/area-plan update series (Town Centre, Hammond, Silver Valley and Albion) — Albion is east of this sub-area, but the same update series is the planning context for East Central's Town Centre changes. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-advances-housing-strategy-completion-area-plan-updates
- **SSMUH / suites / subdivision potential as the City states it:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-family and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. Subdivision of an RS-1 lot must still meet the s. 605 minimums (668.0 m² / 18.0 m / 27.0 m) — SSMUH unit counts do not create new fee-simple lots by themselves. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** beyond the zone minimums above, the research confirms no Maple Ridge subdivision/servicing bylaw number on a primary source in this file — any servicing-bylaw number is held UNCONFIRMED here and is not asserted; confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the City for the parcel. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/east-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **Floodplain (south edge):** along River Road / Port Haney the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Escarpment:** the Fraser River Escarpment policy area reaches the sub-area's west edge (its east boundary is 224 Street); lots within 300 m of the escarpment crest require a geotechnical report attached to a Section 219 covenant before a building permit. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- **Bare-land strata rules (where a bare-land strata lot is bought — the BC strata block applies in full to the lot):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. No East Central bare-land strata age restriction is verified in this file — read the filed bylaws with the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no ALR ground was identified here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. None of this is asserted as applying to an East Central parcel on this file's evidence — check the parcel on ALC mapping first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). That distinction is the whole GST question on an East Central assembly or newly created lot vs a resale house lot from an individual — confirm per transaction. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no East Central–specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is stated ONLY as the research file's own sources support it — this file's research cites no prohibition source for this area, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and is not conformed from other files.
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there vacant land to buy in East Central?** Effectively no as a standing market — the research verifies no bare-land subdivision and no presale lot offering; what trades is built house lots, strata units, and occasional redevelopment assemblies. A "lot" listing here is almost always a teardown or hold lot, not a serviced vacant lot. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create by subdividing an RS-1 property?** The RS-1 minimums are 668.0 m² area, 18.0 m width and 27.0 m depth (Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 s. 605) — both the parent lot remainder and any new lot must meet them, and Town Centre zone minimums are UNVERIFIED in this file. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Q: What does "bare-land strata" mean if I see it on a detached-house listing here?** You own a strata lot, pay strata fees, follow the bylaws and get a Form B — but the lot is a detached-house lot, not a unit in a shared building. No named plan or fee is verified for East Central in this file, so pull the title and Form B before quoting anything. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Can an older house lot carry more units without subdividing?** Yes, on the City's wording: one secondary suite per lot (maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the floodplain) plus up to three, four or six units on eligible single-family and duplex lots under the SSMUH bylaws. Eligibility is lot-specific — confirm with the City. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q: Does the floodplain affect a south-edge lot?** Yes — flood construction level 6.7 m geodetic, no habitable floor space below it on affected lots, a covenant on title, and suites prohibited on floodplain properties under s. 402.8(h). Confirm the covenant and level on title before valuing any lower-level space. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q: What extra diligence does an escarpment-edge lot need?** Within 300 m of the escarpment crest (east boundary 224 Street), a geotechnical report on a Section 219 covenant is required before a building permit — budget for it on any rebuild or addition plan. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does buying a lot get me the first-time buyer GST rebate?** No — the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. It can matter when the new home itself is bought/built under a qualifying agreement, not on the lot alone. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Which OCP bylaw number should I trust for a lot — 7060-2014 or 7998-2024?** Neither is settled in this file — the citation is CONTESTED (update adopted November 25, 2025). Quote the current City document for the specific parcel instead of either number. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a lot on the east side vs downtown?** Catchments split by exact address — the downtown sample (11897 227 Street) maps to Eric Langton Elementary and Thomas Haney Secondary, but other East Central addresses are not confirmed. Run the exact address in the SD42 locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
