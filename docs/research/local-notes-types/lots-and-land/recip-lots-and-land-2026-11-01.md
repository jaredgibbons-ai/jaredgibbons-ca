## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Scope note:** the rules below are the frame for the Surrey ground the feed's first page displays, carried because a land buyer arriving via this search lands on Surrey parcels. They are NOT asserted as rules of the recip page itself, whose filter and geography are UNVERIFIED (§1).
- **Surrey plans (for Surrey parcels):** Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current; Bylaw 18020 is REPLACED, never cite as current. Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **SSMUH (Surrey, Jul 8, 2024):** inside the urban containment boundary, on lots under 4,050 m² — 3 units (280 m² or less), 4 units (larger), up to 6 on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Added units are NOT subdivision permission. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Suites (Surrey):** one suite per dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision + servicing (Surrey):** Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830, verified on City pages. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement Per-lot figures: UNCONFIRMED.
- **Streamside (Surrey):** setbacks in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000, varying by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **STRs (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only, owner must live at the property as primary residence; licence $358/yr carried with the City's $350/yr proposal disclosed as a CONFLICT — check the City licence page before quoting. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **ALR block (for any ALR parcel a search surfaces):** subdivision in the ALR needs ALC approval; residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC strata block (bare-land strata lots included):** Form B (within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; min 10% CRF since Nov 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT:** 1% / 2% to $2M / 3% above, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked examples: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → **$93,000** including the further 2% tier (tier math in §3 of the Morgan Crossing file in this batch). If a sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M, flag as OPEN reconciliation and follow $93,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level ONLY:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Programs attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — new HOUSING only. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000/yr. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Reciprocity governance:** CREA owns the MLS® and REALTOR® trademarks and agents must be CREA and local-board members complying with CREA's rules including the REALTOR® Code and local board rules to access the MLS® system (declared reuse of batch-7 verification in the research). https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/how-we-foster-competition/promotion-and-advocacy/regulatory-adviceinterventions-competition-bureau/submission-ftc-and-doj-residential-real-estate-brokerage The boards' reciprocity display rules themselves: UNVERIFIED — FVREB, GVR and CADREB do not publish them on a public page read for this research. https://jaredgibbons.ca/recip.html
