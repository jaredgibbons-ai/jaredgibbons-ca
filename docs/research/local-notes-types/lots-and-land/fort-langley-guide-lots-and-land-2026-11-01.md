## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Fort Langley is a village in the Township of Langley on the south bank of the Fraser River, described by the Township as the birthplace of BC, with its commercial core on Glover Road. https://www.pc.gc.ca/apps/dfhd/page_nhs_eng.aspx?id=66 and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/fort-langley-community-projects.aspx
- The wider village is framed by River Road, 88 Avenue, 96 Avenue and 240 Street, with the Salmon River and Bedford Channel at its edges; exact Paragon sub-area boundary roads are UNVERIFIED. https://www.pc.gc.ca/apps/dfhd/page_nhs_eng.aspx?id=66
- Nearby areas: Walnut Grove (west/southwest), Langley City and the rest of the Township (south), and Maple Ridge across the Fraser River via the Golden Ears Bridge route. (Research file §1.)
- Where the land actually differs: three fabrics — the heritage village grid around Glover Road (fee-simple village lots, many inside the Heritage Conservation Area), Bedford Landing on Billy Brown Road along the Bedford Channel (a planned enclave where fee-simple lots and strata buildings share one community name), and acreage edges mainly south and east toward the Salmon River valley, where ALR ground begins. Sources: Sections 2 and 3 below.
- Glover Road is the village commercial street; Lee's Market, the village grocery, is at 23320 Mavis Avenue, and there is no shopping centre in Fort Langley. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/garbage-cart.aspx
- Transit is a single bus: TransLink route 562 through the village on Glover Road between Langley Centre and Carvolth Exchange, via Walnut Grove. No SkyTrain or West Coast Express station is in Fort Langley — a material fact for any density claim made for village land. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf
- SD35 schools physically in the village: Fort Langley Elementary (8877 Bartlett St) and Langley Fine Arts School (9096 Trattle St), a district K–12 fine arts choice school; a locator run for 8877 Bartlett St returned Fort Langley Elementary and D.W. Poppy Secondary, with no middle school shown. Catchments are address-based. https://fortlangley.sd35.bc.ca/ and https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Verified position: Fort Langley DOES have a real fee-simple lot fabric — village lots and acreage-edge lots are the land that trades here — but NO named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata, or lot-only development is verified in the research, and no lot counts, sizes or prices are asserted. The honest land story is tenure and constraint, not inventory: heritage controls on village lots, floodplain on low ground, ALR on the edges, and a fee-simple/strata split inside Bedford Landing that must be read lot by lot.**

- **Village fee-simple lots (the default form).** The established village stock is freehold houses on fee-simple lots under the Fort Langley Community Plan and Zoning Bylaw No. 2500; downtown-core lots also sit inside the Heritage Conservation Area (Section 3). Village residential zoning includes R-1E, named for the village in a Township public-hearing document; minimum lot areas and frontages for the village zones are UNVERIFIED from a readable Bylaw No. 2500 section — no lot-size figure is invented here. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/fort-langley-community-projects.aspx and https://tol.maps.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/0c6ddccb89034b6787c285311714b8ce/data
- **Bedford Landing — the fee-simple lot fabric (the named land angle for this area).** Bedford Landing, on Billy Brown Road along the Bedford Channel, is a mixed master community built on the former McDonald Cedar Mill lands (mill opened 1921, 88-acre riverfront property): fee-simple row homes on their own lots (recorded as Row House (Non-Strata), built 2007) sit alongside strata buildings (The Village, EPS998; Waterfront, BCS3620; Flatiron live/work strata). AS THE RESEARCH STATES IT: the row-home fabric is fee-simple; the strata buildings are separate — tenure is read address by address, because the community name alone tells a land buyer nothing about whether a parcel is a fee-simple lot or a strata lot. No Bedford Landing lot count or lot size is published in the sources checked: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r2516238-23086-billy-brown-road and https://hexaflexagon-salmon-whfg.squarespace.com/bedford-landing
- **Acreage-edge lots (south and east).** Acreage lots sit on the village edges, mainly south and east toward the Salmon River valley; farm land on the village edges is in the ALR in places — confirm a parcel on the ALC maps before valuing any farm, coach-house or subdivision angle. https://tol.maps.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/0c6ddccb89034b6787c285311714b8ce/data and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **No bare-land strata verified.** No bare-land strata is verified in Fort Langley in the research or siblings. Where a Fort Langley home is strata, it is a building strata (condo/townhouse) under the Strata Property Act — not a bare-land strata lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Serviced vs unserviced — split by fabric.** The village and Bedford Landing are serviced urban ground as the research presents them; on the acreage edges, wells and septic are the documented diligence track: well reports are due within 90 days of constructing, altering or decommissioning a well and wells are searchable in the provincial GWELLS system; onsite sewage follows Fraser Health filing rules through an authorized person (record of sewerage system before construction, letter of certification after). Which specific edge parcels are on well/septic vs municipal services: UNVERIFIED per parcel — pull the GWELLS record and the Fraser Health filings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons
- **Floodplain ground.** Fort Langley is low-lying: the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station provides flood protection for the Fort Langley community and its flood box gates close when Fraser River levels rise. Parcel-level floodplain mapping: UNVERIFIED — get it from the Township for the specific lot. Low ground is a land characteristic here, not a building detail. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Not land stock — The 1912 (presale, counted separately).** The 1912, by Lanstone Homes — three heritage-inspired townhomes plus the restored 1912 George Albion Towle House, pre-sales coming Winter 2027, move-in Fall 2027 (developer-stated) — is a presale project documented in the townhomes sibling. The Township projects documented are civic, not lot inventory: salishan Place by the River (23430 Mavis Ave) and the Salmon River Pump Station upgrades. Nothing is presented here as for-sale development land beyond the lot fabrics above. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/fort-langley-community-projects.aspx

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Village plan: Fort Langley Community Plan, Township of Langley Bylaw No. 2527** (adopted October 26, 1987, as an amendment to the Township Official Community Plan) — the governing plan for village land. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **Heritage Conservation Area (Bylaw 3710, October 27, 1997).** The plan designates a Heritage Conservation Area under the Local Government Act covering the downtown core, to protect heritage character; non-exempt work requires heritage approval, with exemptions including minor additions under 50 m² that do not front a road (other than a lane) and conform to the guidelines. For a land buyer this is a buildability rule: what can be built or altered on a core lot is heritage-gated before it is zoning-gated. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **Design controls on top of zoning.** A Development Permit Area (Bylaw 3204, May 3, 1993) and the Fort Langley Design Guidelines (Bylaw 5063, April 28, 2014) also cover the village; the Building Façade Design Guidelines are non-binding policy. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **Zoning: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500.** R-1E is named for the village; the Bedford Landing strata buildings sit in site-specific CD zones (the Waterfront's is CD-55 — a zone number, not an age designation). Village zone minimum lot areas/frontages: UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section — never filled in here. https://tol.maps.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/0c6ddccb89034b6787c285311714b8ce/data
- **SSMUH in the Township: Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024**, amending Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 for small-scale multi-unit housing in most zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings. There is NO 6-plex tier in the Township's framework — and no Fort Langley page claim of one is documented in the research, so no defect is asserted here. In the village, SSMUH operates inside the heritage and design controls above, not as an exemption from them. A March 9, 2026 Township council report on an SSMUH development permit area names the Fort Langley Community Plan among the plans Bylaw No. 6116 would amend — the form-and-character rules were still being written in 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
- **Secondary suites: capped at 120 m²** of gross floor area under Township rules, with Council's 2025 direction extending suites to ground-oriented housing including fee-simple rowhouses; parking requirements apply. https://udi.org/advocacy/updates/township-of-langley-secondary-suites-and-lock-off-units
- **Coach houses: fee-simple s. 102 units, never strata.** Under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102, a coach house is a second dwelling unit on a fee-simple lot, part of the same title as the principal house, and cannot be sold separately. A live s. 102 conflict is flagged in the program record and is NOT resolved here — confirm the current s. 102 text with the Township before relying on a coach-house angle for a specific lot. (Standing correction for this run; framework: https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx)
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements (Township).** No Township subdivision/servicing standard, DCC figure or timeline for Fort Langley is documented in the research file: UNCONFIRMED — never filled in. A subdivision angle on a village or edge lot must be scoped with the Township (Approving Officer process, heritage/design overlays and flood/ALR status first) before any value is placed on it.
- **ALR rules (edge parcels).** Residences on ALR land cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval. Residence caps as the ALC states them: principal residence ≤500 m²; a secondary suite within the principal residence only; one additional residence without an ALC application up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, subject to Township bylaws. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **BC Assessment farm class — full three-tier form.** Gross annual value of at least $2,500 (farm area 0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 (<0.8 ha); application delivered to the assessor on or before October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an error and is not used here — it matters on Fort Langley's edges because an acreage lot priced on "farm status" must actually meet the tier for its size. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Flood diligence.** Ask the Township for the parcel's flood information before purchase; the Salmon River Pump Station's flood box gates close when Fraser River levels rise, and Council has authorized detailed design and tendering for pump station upgrades. For any strata purchase, also check the Form B insurance summary for flood coverage/deductibles. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Well / septic diligence (acreage edges).** GWELLS search for the well record (reports due within 90 days of well work) and Fraser Health authorized-person filings for onsite sewerage (record before construction, letter of certification after). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons
- **Bare-land strata — the form (no Fort Langley instance verified).** If a lot WERE bare-land strata, it would be strata tenure — Form B, fees and bylaws on the lot. The BC block: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page; depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). For Fort Langley's fee-simple lots there is NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fee — title covenants/building scheme plus municipal rules govern instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Property transfer tax (PTT).** First-Time Home Buyers': no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — relief turns on a qualifying home. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. Township taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties after July 2 and again after September 3, 2026, and utilities are now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only.** Most individuals' vacant-land sales are exempt (e.g., personal-use land); taxable examples include business capital property, sales in the course of a business, and parcels created by subdividing into more than two parts. Developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots beyond that line are generally taxable. Finer points for a specific Fort Langley lot: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax.** The Township of Langley IS a designated taxable area — residential owners file the annual declaration; a vacant lot's position turns on class and exemptions. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition — bare land.** Secondary sources disagree on the vacant-land position after March 27, 2023; no primary text opened in this pass: UNCONFIRMED — CMHC tool + legal advice. No position asserted.
- **First-home-on-land programs — stated precisely.** The FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — relevant here mainly if a buyer builds a first home on a village/edge lot or buys The 1912 presale. New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsps-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What kind of land can I actually buy in Fort Langley?** Fee-simple village lots (heritage-gated in the core), fee-simple Bedford Landing lots (read tenure address by address), and acreage-edge lots (ALR/flood/well-septic checks first). No bare-land subdivision or bare-land strata is verified. (Section 2 above.)
- **Q: Is a Bedford Landing lot fee-simple or strata?** Either exists under the same community name: row homes are recorded fee-simple (Row House (Non-Strata)); The Village, Waterfront and Flatiron are strata. The civic address and title decide — never the "Bedford Landing" label. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r2516238-23086-billy-brown-road
- **Q: Can I subdivide or add units to a village lot?** The Township's SSMUH bylaw (6020, Nov 18, 2024) amended the single-family/duplex zones — with NO 6-plex tier — and in the village the Heritage Conservation Area, Development Permit Area and Design Guidelines sit on top of it. Subdivision/servicing specifics for a parcel: UNCONFIRMED until scoped with the Township. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
- **Q: Can I build a coach house on an edge lot?** A coach house under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s. 102 is a fee-simple second unit on the same title — never a separate strata lot or separate sale. On ALR land, the ALC's additional-residence caps (90 m² ≤40 ha / 186 m² >40 ha) and Township bylaws both apply, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. A flagged live s. 102 conflict means: confirm the current text with the Township first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How big can a suite be?** Maximum 120 m² gross floor area under Township suite rules. https://udi.org/advocacy/updates/township-of-langley-secondary-suites-and-lock-off-units
- **Q: Is the lot in a floodplain?** The village is low-lying and pump-station protected; parcel-level mapping is UNVERIFIED in the research — get the parcel's flood information from the Township before subjects. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Q: Is the edge lot in the ALR, and does it have farm class?** Two different checks: ALR status on the ALC maps; farm class against BC Assessment's three tiers ($2,500 / $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha / $10,000, by size; apply by Oct 31). ALR land does not automatically carry farm class, and neither follows from the other. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Q: Well and septic — what do I pull?** The GWELLS well record and the Fraser Health sewerage filings (record of sewerage system; letter of certification), both through the official systems in Section 3. No filings found is itself a diligence finding. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers
- **Q: What does it cost to hold a lot here?** Township property taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% after July 2 and a further 5% after September 3; utilities billed separately) plus the speculation and vacancy tax declaration — the Township is a taxable area. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Q: Will GST apply to my lot purchase?** Depends on the seller and subdivision history under the CRA rules (Section 3) — personal-use individual sales are generally exempt; business-course and more-than-two-part subdivision sales are taxable examples. Get the position in writing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Bedford Landing" is a tenure trap for land buyers too, not just home buyers.** One community name covers fee-simple lots/row homes and three strata buildings on a former mill site — a lot listing that says only "Bedford Landing" has told you nothing about title, fees or who maintains what. The address and the Land Title record decide. (Section 2 above.)
- **Heritage approval can outrank zoning on a core lot.** A lot can pass every zoning test and still need heritage approval for what is built on it — with only narrow exemptions (e.g., minor additions under 50 m² not fronting a road, conforming to the guidelines). Price core land on the heritage path, not just the zone. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **There is no 6-plex tier in the Township — and no Fort Langley page in the research claims one.** The absence is recorded here deliberately, with the same discipline as the townhomes file: no defect is asserted, and any future marketing claim of a village 6-plex right would contradict Bylaw 6020's framework. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
- **Flood protection is a Township works program, not a lot feature.** The Salmon River Pump Station provides the community's protection, its gates close as Fraser levels rise, and upgrades are in detailed design/tendering — a lot's protection story is municipal infrastructure plus the parcel's own flood information, not a seller's assurance. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
