## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Tilbury IS a land market — but it is INDUSTRIAL land, not residential lots. There is no residential lot stock here to invent: no houses, condos or townhomes as a market, no residential bare-land subdivision, no residential bare-land strata, and no presale residential lot offering was confirmed inside the Tilbury industrial lands in City of Delta sources. What trades is industrial land in two forms, documented below on the research's own industrial evidence.**

### Industrial land — what actually trades here

- **Land use, as the OCP states it:** the Delta Official Community Plan 2024 (Bylaw No. 8400) states Delta's industries are primarily located along River Road East, in Tilbury Industrial Park, Annacis Island and Roberts Bank, with pockets of industry along River Road West and at Boundary Bay Airport; Delta's industrially zoned and designated land totaled 1,527 hectares in Metro Vancouver's 2020 Regional Industrial Lands Inventory, approximately 13% of the region's overall inventory. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Form 1 — freehold industrial lots:** freehold industrial lots trade in Tilbury alongside the strata bays — industrial freehold, the default fee-simple form for this district, not residential fee-simple lot product. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/tilbury.md, §5)
- **Form 2 — industrial strata lots (NOT bare-land strata, and NOT residential):** industrial strata units — small-bay and mid-bay warehouse/office strata complexes on streets like Progress Way and Vantage Way — trade as industrial premises. Stated plainly on tenure: strata industrial ownership follows the Strata Property Act framework — owners own their strata lots and together own common property as a strata corporation — so an industrial strata lot IS strata tenure, with Form B / strata fees / bylaws applying to the lot, exactly as the bare-land strata statement applies in residential land files; what differs is the use, which is warehouse/industrial, not residential and not bare land. No individual industrial strata complex is named in the research — none is named or counted here, and no lot count, size or price for any complex is invented. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Named land areas, by name only as the sources state them:** Tilbury Industrial Park and Tilbury Island — the City's own names for these industrial lands (Section 1). Neither is documented in the research as a for-sale subdivision or a lot program, and neither is presented here as inventory with lots, sizes or prices. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP:** the Delta Official Community Plan 2024, consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), covers Tilbury as industrial land — a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules; "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw No. 8400" is an error wherever met. The former area plans sat under the repealed Bylaw No. 3950, and Tilbury was not among its residential neighbourhoods in any case. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Zoning — codes documented, standards and bylaw number flagged:** City material for Tilbury-area properties references the I2 and I7 industrial zone codes; the full industrial zone names and minimum lot standards were not confirmed from a City zone sheet — UNVERIFIED. Delta's zoning-bylaw number is CONTESTED (cited as No. 7600, 2017 in the research and siblings, and elsewhere as "8400, 2025") — flagged here, never asserted as settled. Permitted-use, outdoor-storage and parking rules differ by zone, so a use that works in one bay can be prohibited two streets over — verify the exact zone and permitted uses on the City zoning maps before anything else. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/
- **SSMUH does not create housing here:** Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaws (Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) apply to single-detached and duplex lots; Tilbury's industrial lands are not single-detached residential lots, so SSMUH is not a route to housing in the industrial park. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** industrial subdivision follows the zone for the parcel — and because the full I2/I7 standards are UNVERIFIED (above), no subdivision minimum is asserted here. Delta's Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 is carried as the Delta precedent in the program record, with its adoption and parcel-level standards UNCONFIRMED on a primary source in this file's research — no parcel-level servicing standard is asserted from it; document the process from City / council documents for the specific parcel instead.
- **Flood / dike diligence — a first subject:** Tilbury Island and the River Road lands are Fraser River riverfront lands inside Delta's dike system, so confirm the parcel's Flood Construction Level, dike setbacks and any Section 219 flood covenants on title before subject removal — for an industrial purchase exactly as for a home. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Industrial strata block (applies to the strata bays in Section 2):** on an industrial strata resale the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate (Strata Property Act s. 59 — fees, amounts owing, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page in the residential-form statement carried in the siblings); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — this reaches industrial stratas too; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 with May 1, 2023 exemptions — none of which creates a residential use on industrial land, and no residential or age-restricted use is stated for any Tilbury industrial strata. Add a Phase I environmental site assessment for the unit's and the complex's prior uses to the usual strata read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Living in an industrial unit — UNCONFIRMED, do not assume:** no source checked states any residential, caretaker-suite or live-work permission in Tilbury's industrial zones. Do not price or market a Tilbury unit as livable without the parcel's zone sheet and a City answer in hand. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/
- **ALR rules (edge parcels where ALR applies):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); BC Assessment farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Property transfer tax + GST — commercial treatment UNCONFIRMED:** Tilbury is commercial/industrial land. Commercial PTT / GST treatment is UNCONFIRMED where the research is silent — it is not filled in here, and the residential PTT bands / FTHB and Newly Built exemptions in the sibling files are NOT carried as Tilbury industrial treatment, because they are residential programs and no source in this file's research applies them to an industrial parcel. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Tilbury a residential neighbourhood where I can buy a lot to build a house?** No. The Delta OCP treats Tilbury as industrial land — Tilbury Industrial Park is named with River Road East, Annacis Island and Roberts Bank as Delta's main industry locations — and no residential housing mix was confirmed inside the industrial lands in City sources. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: What land actually trades in Tilbury?** Industrial land in two forms: freehold industrial lots, and industrial strata lots (small-bay and mid-bay warehouse/office strata complexes on streets like Progress Way and Vantage Way). Typical buyers are owner-user businesses and industrial investors near Highways 17, 91 and 99. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How big are Tilbury industrial lots, and what are the I2 / I7 standards?** UNVERIFIED — the full industrial zone names and minimum lot standards were not confirmed from a City zone sheet in the research. Confirm the zone sheet for the specific parcel on the City zoning maps; no lot-size figure is quoted from this file. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/
- **Q: Are the strata bays a cheap way to buy a home or a residential lot?** No. They are strata industrial units, not homes and not bare land: permitted uses, outdoor storage and parking differ by zone, and a use that works in one bay can be prohibited two streets over. Living in one is UNCONFIRMED as permitted on any source checked. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/
- **Q: A listing says "Tilbury" but shows a house — how?** Postal-area naming is wider than the industrial park: dwellings carrying a Tilbury-area address sit on surrounding Ladner / East Delta farm and riverfront parcels, outside the industrial park lands. Check which side of the industrial boundary the parcel is on, and run that parcel's own ALR, flood and zoning checks as a Ladner/East Delta purchase. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: What flood homework comes first?** Confirm the parcel's Flood Construction Level, dike setbacks and any Section 219 flood covenants on title before subject removal — Tilbury is riverfront industrial land inside Delta's dike system (over 63 km of flood protection, 31 pump stations, approximately 538 km of drainage citywide). http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: What about ALR or a farm lease on an edge parcel?** City material shows farm use leases on some Tilbury-area parcels, and ALR status is parcel-specific through the ALC — a leased or ALR parcel does not price like a clean freehold industrial lot, and subdivision in the ALR requires ALC approval. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/
- **Q: Does the strata document regime apply to an industrial bay?** Yes — Form B on resale, a depreciation report every five years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, and at least 10% of the operating budget to the CRF since November 1, 2023. Add a Phase I environmental site assessment for prior industrial uses. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: What PTT and GST will I pay on an industrial lot?** UNCONFIRMED in this file — commercial PTT/GST treatment is not documented in the research and is not filled in here. At CRA GI-003 general level only, developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable for GST; get transaction-specific tax advice before writing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which highways serve Tilbury, and why does that drive value?** The South Fraser Perimeter Road (Highway 17) alignment runs through the Tilbury Industrial area, and the City's Tilbury Ice posting places the area between Highway 91 / Alex Fraser Bridge and Highway 99 / Massey Tunnel off River Road — value turns on that Highways 17/91/99 triangle and on yard and ceiling specs, not on housing amenities. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-09/Tilbury%20Ice%20-%20For%20Lease%20Brochure.pdf
