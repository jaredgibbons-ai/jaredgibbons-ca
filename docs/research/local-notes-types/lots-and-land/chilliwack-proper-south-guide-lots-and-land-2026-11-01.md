## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — current;** this ground sits in its Schedule B-2 "Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan." The guide's December 16, 2025 adoption-date statement is UNVERIFIED (§1). A parcel's designation is a per-parcel City check. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Zoning: Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** — the parcel's actual zone is confirmed with the City per parcel; no zone label is adopted for any specific lot from listing text (the guide feed's R1-C claim included — see §2). Farm zones sit in §6 of Bylaw 5000 where ALR/farm ground arises. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH (Bylaw 5395, adopted Jun 18, 2024):** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, amending Bylaw 5000, allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide and does not create new lots. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Secondary suites and accessory units (Bylaw 5000):** a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the habitable floor space of the building (s.2); only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits, with types including secondary suite, coach house and garden suite, garden suites capped at 100 m² (s.4.06(1)). A suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Subdivision / servicing — process documented, bylaw number held UNCONFIRMED:** the Chilliwack servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in this program — the candidate "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" is NOT adopted here — so the Approving Officer / Preliminary Layout Approval process is what is documented: all subdivision plans require approval by the City's Approving Officer under the Local Government Act and Land Title Act before Land Title registration; a Preliminary Layout Approval is the first major milestone (typically 8–12 weeks from application, valid 12 months) and sets out the servicing, engineering and legal steps to final approval. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8208
- **Flood diligence:** the guide states that under the City's Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 4519, habitable floor space in areas protected by a standard dike must be at or above the applicable Flood Construction Level plus 0.6 m of freeboard, and that the City has an ongoing $30 million dyke-raising program — the City's floodplain bylaw text and flood pages were not opened in the research pass, so the Bylaw 4519 specifics and the $30 million figure as stated are UNVERIFIED in this file; the parcel-level Flood Construction Level for any specific lot is likewise UNVERIFIED. Confirm with the City before pricing suite or infill potential. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-proper-south-guide.html
- **Short-term rentals (Chilliwack):** STR operations are regulated under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141, adopted November 24, 2021, which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968 — confirm a property can be licensed under it before valuing any rental income from a lot purchase. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **ALR block (provincial frame — no sub-area ALR parcel is documented in the guide research, stated so it is not misapplied):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for this sub-area. City-wide context only: the guide states 67% of Chilliwack's land is in the ALR, matching the City figure in the sibling research; a specific parcel's reserve status is UNVERIFIED here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot, wherever one surfaces):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $700,000 fully residential parcel, no exemption — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$700,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. (Worked example at $3.5M for reference: $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — a townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met, never silently carried.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
