## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current** for every Surrey parcel behind this page. Bylaw No. 18020 is the replaced predecessor and is never cited as current. A parcel's designation is read in the City's COSMOS mapping. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000** — parcel zoning, designations and servicing confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. No band lot-size table exists — none is stated. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units (Surrey framework, adopted Jul 8, 2024):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, on eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide and does not create new lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 12000, rules-gaps verified):** one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit**; no 90 m² cap was found in the current consolidated text; a suite is not a separate strata lot. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Coach houses and garden suites (rules-gaps verified):** all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; the unit must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house; one extra parking space is required. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Subdivision / servicing:** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. Verified on the City's own Land Development / Servicing Agreement pages: the **Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw, worked through the Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside / riparian:** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in **Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and vary by stream class**, complemented by the Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit process — no single riparian figure is stated for any parcel in this file; the setback is read off the parcel's stream class in COSMOS and Part 7A. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only — only the property owner may host from their primary residence, tenants cannot operate one, for periods of less than 90 consecutive days per patron, with a business licence at **$358 per year**. **Conflict disclosed:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposing the STR amendments put the licence at $350/yr plus fees — $358 is carried with $350 disclosed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R094.pdf
- **ALR block (provincial frame, applied only where a parcel IS in the ALR):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for this band. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (built townhome lots in this band, and any bare-land strata lot):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. The research records a gated 55+ description for one feed complex in listing text only; the restriction itself is confirmed only from that complex's strata documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. Worked examples are in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Insured-mortgage context documented in the research (not a land rule):** CMHC states homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999 require 5% down on the first $500,000 plus 10% on the portion over $500,000, insurance is required under 20% down, and premium rates range from **0.6% to 4.5%** of the total mortgage amount. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a list of vacant lots under $1.2M in this file?** No — this is a topic/band file with no land roster of its own. The page is a townhome feed filter; land status is UNCONFIRMED per parcel and is read off title, zone and COSMOS for the specific parcel. (§2 above)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at the top of the band?** At $1,200,000, fully residential, no exemption: $2,000 + 2% × $1,000,000 = **$22,000**. At $700,000 the same math is $12,000. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer get PTT relief in this band?** On price alone, in the lower part: full exemption requires fair market value of $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, with exemption on the first $500,000 where all conditions are met — above $860,000 there is no first-time relief on price. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Does the newly built exemption help on bare land?** No — it is a Newly Built **Home** Exemption (full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000), for a new home, not a vacant lot. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is a townhome lot the same as a bare-land strata lot?** No. A built townhome lot is a strata lot in a built complex; a bare-land strata lot is strata tenure on the lot itself, with no building necessarily on it — both carry Form B, fees and bylaws, and neither is fee-simple. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: Can a Surrey lot add a suite or coach house?** One secondary suite per dwelling unit, capped at 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area and never inside a coach house or garden suite; a coach house or garden suite of 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR, lane access on smaller lots, one extra parking space). (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide instead of adding units?** Different permissions: SSMUH allows 3–4 units on most lots by size band (up to 6 near a frequent bus stop) but does not create new lots; subdivision must meet the zone minimum in Bylaw No. 12000 and the servicing requirements under Bylaw No. 8830, confirmed with the City per parcel. (§3 above)
- **Q: Will GST apply to a vacant lot I find?** At the CRA GI-003 level only: an individual's personal-use sale is generally exempt; business-course sales and parcels created by subdividing into more than two parts are taxable examples. Confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: How did Surrey attached housing sell in September 2026?** "Condos & Townhomes" combined: 1,523 listings, 114 solds, median $512,944 — whole-community attached figures, labelled context only; SnapStats publishes no band cut, no townhome-only split and no land category. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Which school will a home on my lot feed into?** UNVERIFIED for any address behind this page — SD36 catchments are address-based and no locator run for a feed address is on record. Run the exact address in the SD36 locator. (§1 above)
