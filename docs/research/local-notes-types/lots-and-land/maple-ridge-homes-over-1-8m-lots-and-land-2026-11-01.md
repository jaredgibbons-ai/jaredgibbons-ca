## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019 governs.** The standard urban house zone: RS-1 (Single Detached Residential) under s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², minimum lot width 18.0 m, minimum lot depth 27.0 m, maximum lot coverage 40%; accessory uses include a secondary suite inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **OCP citation — CONTESTED (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024):** flagged here and wherever met in this file, resolved nowhere. Growth is planned area by area: the City's OCP updates and area plans for the Town Centre, Hammond, Silver Valley and Albion were adopted in fall 2025 to align with provincial housing legislation — the Silver Valley Area Plan modernizes the 2002 plan and introduces a River Village Mixed-Use designation, and the **Albion Area Plan (adopted October 28, 2025)** creates buffers where urban development meets farmland. The **Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was ADOPTED January 27, 2026** (OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021) per the City record — cited as adopted; no contrary reading is documented in this file's own research file. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan and https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP
- **SSMUH:** eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws — a development-value question on any large urban lot in this band, settled lot by lot, not by the listing price. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- **Floodplain — a must-check for river-edge estates:** properties along the Fraser River frontage sit in a floodplain where the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Escarpment — a must-check on the south-western slopes:** in the Fraser River Escarpment policy area (bounded by 207 Street west, 124 Avenue north, 224 Street east and the escarpment crest along the south), properties within 300 m of the crest require a geotechnical report attached to a Section 219 covenant before a building permit, and properties within 100 m of the crest require a detailed geotechnical report addressing potential seismic slope failure. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- **Watercourse:** Kanaka Creek is a salmon-bearing watercourse running through the Cottonwood / Albion side of the city, protected by Kanaka Creek Regional Park (Metro Vancouver) — creek-adjacent acreages carry watercourse and development-permit questions that are parcel-specific. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- **Property taxes:** Maple Ridge property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **PTT:** 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above; further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000 (mixed-class: residential portion only; farm-class land with a dwelling: up to 0.5 ha treated as residential). PTT is on fair market value at registration, not annual taxes. Worked math in §2. Additional PTT rate for foreign nationals/corporations/taxable trustees: UNVERIFIED in this file (separate provincial page not re-read). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Exemptions do not reach this band:** FTHB full exemption caps at $835,000 (first $500,000 exempt, maximum saving $8,000, partial to $860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. Budget full PTT at this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Home owner grant at this band:** 2026 threshold $2,075,000 — full regular grant at/below, reduced $5 per $1,000 over, $0 over $2,189,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000; agreements March 20, 2025–2031) — and it phases out below this band's floor in any case. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, where one exists in the band):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR:** ALR status is parcel-specific — the ALC directs owners and buyers to its maps and online mapping tools; no area-wide ALR statement is made for any acreage in this band without a parcel check. Where a parcel is in the ALR: subdivision needs ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, by Oct 31) — flat "$2,500+" is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Purchase prohibition:** stated ONLY as this file's own research supports it — the research cites no prohibition source for this band, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and not conformed. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY.
