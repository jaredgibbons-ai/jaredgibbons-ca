## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** A single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules; the East Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, and farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". Any page attributing a lettered schedule to Bylaw 8400 is wrong. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged not settled.** City zone sheets and the Bassi notice cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017 (the Bassi application itself seeks rezoning to a new Townhouse Residential Zone under "Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017"); an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates. Neither is asserted as settled here — the ruling is Jared's; zoning is read parcel by parcel off the City's maps. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/4617/7676/7348/3ff51f67a63aa5b3830e9b533891cc59_LU009770_Resident20Notice20V4.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it.** Delta adopted its SSMUH zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024, allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies; the RS1 zone also lists a secondary suite as an accessory use. SSMUH is added-unit potential on an existing lot, not a subdivision right — the zone minimums in §2 still govern any actual subdivision. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing requirements.** A City of Delta council report (January 24, 2024) presents the Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 to repeal and replace Subdivision and Development Standards Bylaw No. 7162, 2015, made under s. 506 of the Local Government Act to regulate and require works and services on subdivision — highways, water, sanitary sewers, drainage and other services and utilities. Final adoption status of Bylaw 8288 and the servicing standard for a specific Holly parcel (including the 60B Street lands, whose terms sit in their application files): UNCONFIRMED in this pass — confirm with the City before pricing a subdivision. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Streamside rule.** All properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — a live constraint on east-Ladner lots, where ditches are part of the farm-edge fabric. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Dyke / flood diligence.** Ladner is low land protected by the City's dike, seawall and drainage system (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations, ~538 km of drainage infrastructure). Confirm flood construction requirements for the specific lot with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **ALR rules (East Delta farm-belt ground at Holly's edge; in-sub-area ALR UNVERIFIED, §2).** ALR residences cannot be stratified — strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)) — and subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, as the ALC states it. ALC residence caps: principal residence 500 m² or less total floor area; a secondary suite only within the principal residence; one additional residence of 90 m² or less (parcels 40 ha or less) or 186 m² or less (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — three tiers, never a flat figure.** Gross annual farm income required: $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on an operation under 0.8 ha. Applications are due by October 31 for the next taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an error. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Bare-land strata block (Hollyview Estates and Ashley Green, §2).** Form B (SPA s. 59) discloses the lot's strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, and must be provided within 7 days of request — in a bare-land strata it is also how a buyer learns what the strata does and does not maintain (the house itself is the owner's problem). Since July 1, 2024 every strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, non-deferrable by vote — Hollyview (13 lots) is inside this rule; since November 1, 2023 the CRF must receive at least 10% of the operating fund annually. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022; the only valid age bylaws are 55+ (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions), and no Holly complex is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **GST on vacant land and on new product.** CRA's position, stated only at the level its publication supports: most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (for example, land kept for personal use), but a sale is taxable where the land was subdivided into more than two parts, sales of farmland are usually taxable, and sales by developers/businesses are generally taxable supplies. A new Skye townhome carries GST; a resale lot or home does not. The specific treatment of a 60B Street parcel sale is a facts question — finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home money — stated precisely.** The first-time home buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — it applies to new housing (a Skye home, a new build), NOT to bare land itself. The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs and the First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit, for a qualifying home. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-time buyers' exemption: no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, phasing to $1,150,000 — the exemption that matters for a new 60B Street home; its application to a bare-land purchase is facts-specific: UNCONFIRMED for bare land in this file. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA) — the Skye frame.** A presale buyer must receive a disclosure statement; s. 21 gives 7 days from the later of signing or acknowledging that statement to rescind, and deposits are held in trust. Read the Skye disclosure statement, not the marketing, for phasing and completion dates. https://meridianlawgroup.ca/pre-sale-properties-and-a-developers-requirements-to-disclose-building-permits-financing-commitments-and-buyers-rights-to-terminate-agreements/
- **Speculation and vacancy taxes — Delta's actual position.** The City of Delta IS listed by the Province as a designated taxable area for the BC Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so owners of residential-class property in Delta must complete the annual declaration. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is a City of Vancouver tax ONLY and does not apply in Delta. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition — the land point.** CMHC states the prohibition (to January 1, 2027, in census metropolitan areas including Metro Vancouver) covers buildings of three or fewer dwelling units, and that effective March 27, 2023 it does NOT apply to vacant land — vacant land zoned for residential and mixed use can be purchased by non-Canadians and used for any purpose, including residential development. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/media-newsroom/news-releases/2023/amendments-prohibition-purchase-residential-property-non-canadians-regulations
- **Delta property taxes and holding cost.** Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there bare land for sale in Holly?** No verified bare-land subdivision or lot presale — the growth edge sells finished townhomes (Skye), and the rest of the land market is improved fee-simple lots and two bare-land strata. (§2 above)
- **Q: What is a bare-land strata lot, in plain terms?** You own your lot and your house outright, and the strata owns only the common property — at Hollyview the listing says it plainly: you share the common property only and take care of your own home and yard within your fence. But it is still strata tenure: fees, bylaws, a Form B and a depreciation report all apply. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3072787-4767-64-street-13-delta-bc-v4k-3m2/brochure
- **Q: What is the difference between Hollyview Estates and Ashley Green?** Form. Hollyview is townhouse-form bare-land strata (13 units, 1992); Ashley Green is half-duplex bare-land strata (1990). Both are bare-land strata tenure; neither is a conventional townhouse strata like Skye, and neither is fee-simple. (§2 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Holly lot?** Only if the parcel's zone allows it — minimums run from RS7 at 335 m² and RS6 at 390 m² up to RS5 at 550 m² (460 m² outside the SFR designation), read parcel by parcel — and then the City's subdivision and servicing standards apply. East-edge parcels also need the ALR check in the next answer. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Q: Could a lot at Holly's east edge be ALR?** Inside Holly, ALR is UNVERIFIED and none is confirmed; the documented farm belt starts east of the urban edge, designated "Agriculture (A)" in the OCP. If a parcel is ALR, everything changes: no stratification, ALC approval for subdivision, and the ALC residence caps (500 m² principal; suite within the principal only; 90 m² / 186 m² additional). Check the ALC mapping before subjects, not after. (§2–§3 above)
- **Q: Are the 60B Street lands serviced, or is servicing part of the deal?** The townhouse applications on 60B Street run through the City's application process, where servicing terms sit in the application files; Holly's established grid is on the City's municipal water, sewer and drainage systems. Parcel- and project-specific servicing terms: UNCONFIRMED from public sources — read the application file. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219618/
- **Q: Does GST apply on a land purchase here?** Per CRA: an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt; land subdivided into more than two parts, farmland, and developer/business sales are generally taxable. A newly created lot on the 60B Street edge is exactly the case to check. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What are the holding costs on a Holly lot?** Delta property taxes on the City's calendar (2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026); for a bare-land strata lot, strata fees on top — Ashley Green's recorded fee is listing-stated and low, but the Form B figure governs. Parcel-specific figures: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Q: Is flood or ditch drainage a real diligence item here?** Yes — Ladner is low land behind the City's dike and drainage system (over 63 km of flood protection, 31 pump stations, ~538 km of drainage), and any lot within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch is in the Streamside Protection DPA — on Holly's farm-edge lots, ditches are the fabric, so assume the check is needed and confirm with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: How long does a subdivision or a 60B-style application take?** UNCONFIRMED — the Bassi application (File LU009770) was still preliminary in October 2025, which is the only timeline evidence in this file; no official source publishes Delta approval timelines for a Holly parcel.
