## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Arbutus Ridge has no meaningful bare-land market — stated plainly at the top, and this is the strongest honest zero in this batch. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community, no named townhouse complex and no presale LOT offering was verified; the research file confirms no condo building or townhome complex name in Arbutus Ridge on a developer or strata source at all. What trades as land here is the individual fee-simple detached-house lot on the ridge's tree-lined streets, priced on the house, the heritage character, the mountain views and its R1-1 multiplex math. The single exception in form — the Arbutus Village lands under their own CD-1 — is documented below as a landholding and a redevelopment, never as lots for sale.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default and effectively the only land form

- **Ridge house lots:** detached houses on tree-lined streets dominate — the City calls Arbutus Ridge a mature community with restored heritage houses and striking views of the North Shore mountains — with limited strata product compared with the arterial corridors around it. A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. The strata share by street is UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/arbutus-ridge.aspx
- **Lot character, as the research states it:** R1-1 minimum site areas run from 306 m² (single detached house, duplex, or 3–4-unit multiple dwelling; minimum frontage 10.0 m) through 464 m² (5 units; 13.4 m) to 557 m² (6–8 units; 15.1 m); a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. These are schedule minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size; the zone and dimensions of a specific lot are confirmed parcel by parcel. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Serviced position:** Arbutus Ridge homes are on City water and sewer services; a 2026 utility dollar figure for a house here confirmed on a City source is UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, R1-1 District Schedule:** the house streets sit under R1-1 — 306 m² minimum site area for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m), 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. The Arbutus Village lands are the exception: CD-1 (78) for 4255 Arbutus Street. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. Heritage status can change the answer on these streets and must be checked — the City describes the stock as including restored heritage houses. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf and http://cd1-bylaws.vancouver.ca/ByCD-1Number/cd1s/iCD-1(078)_SummaryAndBackupNotes.pdf
- **Plan — Vancouver Official Development Plan:** a separate adopted Arbutus Ridge community plan is UNVERIFIED in the sources read; citywide land-use direction comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which **replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026** — a buyer reading older "Vancouver Plan" material for these streets is reading a superseded document. The Arbutus Centre Policy Statement (2008) is the City policy document named in the CD-1 (78) file for the village lands. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf
- **Subdivision — Subdivision By-law No. 5208 (fresh check, Nov 2, 2026):** Vancouver subdivision is governed by the Subdivision By-law (No. 5208), whose Schedule A sets minimum parcel width and area standards by district and sub-area category, administered by the Approving Officer; its fee schedule was most recently amended for 2026 by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026). A fee-simple split of a ridge lot must clear both R1-1 and its Subdivision By-law category. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** the R1-1 schedule above IS Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit framework for these streets — house plus suite and laneway, duplex, or a multiplex to 6 strata / 8 rental units by lot size — so the "SSMUH question" here is a per-lot schedule check (306 / 464 / 557 m²), not a separate overlay. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Seniors-housing rezoning route (land-relevant policy):** the City of Vancouver Seniors Housing Strategy (approved July 23, 2024) created a Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy to enable more seniors housing across neighbourhoods — in an R1-1 house area with no verified 55+ ownership stock (55-plus sibling), new seniors supply here would arrive by rezoning, not by right. https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/seniors-housing-strategy.aspx
- **Bare-land strata / strata rules (the BC strata block — applies in full to the Arbutus Village strata on its CD-1 land, and would apply to a bare-land strata lot if one were offered):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — the diligence centre for a 1975-era enclave; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. No Arbutus Village age bylaw is verified — do not market it as 55+ on this file's evidence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Arbutus Ridge ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, asserted in this program only where the horse-properties research documents it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — an Arbutus lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%, Vancouver only:** the research states the rate directly for this area — the tax is **3% of the property's assessed taxable value** for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year; every owner must submit an annual property status declaration, the declaration deadline for 2025 was February 3, 2026, and the 2025 payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. A ridge lot or house held vacant carries this annual step — confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Speculation tax + purchase prohibition:** the arbutus townhomes sibling notes the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax can also apply in Vancouver, separate from the City tax — no Arbutus-specific rate or position beyond that is documented in this file's own research, and none is imported. The federal non-Canadian purchase-prohibition position for Arbutus land is UNCONFIRMED in this file: the research cites no prohibition source for Arbutus Ridge, and the position is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot, a lot subdivision or a bare-land strata in Arbutus Ridge?** No verified one of any kind. The land stock is built fee-simple house lots; the one strata enclave sits on its own CD-1 land at the southern edge and is board-filed under Quilchena. No presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What can a ridge house lot actually become?** Where the R1-1 schedule is met: a house with a secondary suite and a laneway house, a duplex, or a multiplex of up to 6 strata units (up to 8 rental units), on minimum site areas of 306 m² / 464 m² / 557 m² by form — subject to heritage status, which must be checked on these streets before any teardown math. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Is Arbutus Village part of Arbutus Ridge or Quilchena — and can I buy its land?** Both readings exist and this file states them: the village lands sit at Arbutus Ridge's southern edge under their own CD-1 (78), but directory and listing sources file the strata under the board sub-area "Quilchena," and this program counts it once, in the condos sibling. You cannot buy its land in any case — you would buy a strata unit in the enclave, or nothing, in the redevelopment's rental and non-market blocks. http://cd1-bylaws.vancouver.ca/ByCD-1Number/cd1s/iCD-1(078)_SummaryAndBackupNotes.pdf
- **Q: What does the "461 units" figure for Arbutus Village mean?** It is the CD-1 (78) zoning maximum for the whole village lands (4255 Arbutus Street, Bylaw No. 4634, enacted August 1, 1972) — a cap on what the land may hold, not the strata's unit count, which is UNCONFIRMED. Do not quote 461 as the size of any building. http://cd1-bylaws.vancouver.ca/ByCD-1Number/cd1s/iCD-1(078)_SummaryAndBackupNotes.pdf
- **Q: What will the Arbutus Village redevelopment add — and is any of it lots?** On the 2017 construction reporting: four blocks with 399 strata units among 490 residential units, two-storey townhouses in the later blocks, and a first block split between market rental (115 units) and City-owned non-market homes (100 units). No lots are in it at any phase, and whether any townhouse or strata block is complete today is UNCONFIRMED — do not price it as existing stock. https://vancouver.skyrisecities.com/news/2017/11/work-started-arbutus-village-development.29725
- **Q: Does the swamp history matter to a lot purchase here?** It is the right question on low ground: the City ties the area's development pattern to its swampy origins, and the low areas were made buildable with imported sand in the 1940s–1950s — order the geotechnical and drainage diligence for a low lot, and check its floodplain status in the City dataset, because the research leaves parcel-level status UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/arbutus-ridge.aspx and https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: Can I split a ridge lot into two fee-simple lots?** Only if both resulting parcels clear R1-1 and the Subdivision By-law No. 5208 Schedule A category for the block, through the Approving Officer — and the village lands cannot be split this way at all, being CD-1. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm
- **Q: What is the Empty Homes Tax risk on a purchase here?** 3% of assessed taxable value if the home is declared or deemed vacant for the reference year — and deemed vacant is what happens if the annual declaration is not filed. Get the seller's declaration status in the contract documents. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003); the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not the bare lot. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve an Arbutus lot?** Nobody can honestly say from this file: Carnarvon Elementary (3400 Balaclava Street) and Trafalgar Elementary (4170 Trafalgar Street) are located in the area, but SD39 catchments are address-based, the locator was not run for this file's sample address, and no secondary was confirmed for the area in the research pass. Run the exact address in the VSB locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The area was once called "Asthma Flats":** a neighbourhood profile (secondary source) records that in the 1920s residents of Point Grey and Kerrisdale referred to the future Arbutus Ridge that way — it was a huge swamp crossable only by a three-plank sidewalk — and sand brought in from False Creek in the 1940s and 1950s allowed the low-lying areas to be developed with houses, schools and shops. A drainage and soil history worth a geotechnical question on low lots. https://www.michaeltudorie.com/blog/52777/neighbourhood-profile-arbutus and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/arbutus-ridge.aspx
- **Valley Drive is older than the grid:** the same profile records that Valley Drive dates from the 1870s, cut through the forest as the right-of-way for BC's first logging railway — one of the few streets in the community not oriented to the regular street grid, which is why lots on it sit and price differently. https://www.michaeltudorie.com/blog/52777/neighbourhood-profile-arbutus
- **The Greenway is a former rail line, not a park that was always there:** the Arbutus Corridor runs about nine kilometres from the Fraser River to False Creek along the area's east border, and its temporary path runs 8.5 km between Fir Street and Milton Street — the corridor that separates the ridge from Shaughnessy is the area's spine for walking and cycling, and its east-edge lots front a transportation corridor, not a street. https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/explore-the-arbutus-greenway.aspx
- **The area's only condo enclave is zoned as its own district, and its redevelopment's first homes were not condos at all:** Arbutus Village has its own CD-1 (78) bylaw capping the village lands at 461 units, and Block A of the Larco rebuild was reported as 115 market rental units plus 100 City-owned non-market units — "new at Arbutus Village" does not automatically mean "for sale," and none of it is land. http://cd1-bylaws.vancouver.ca/ByCD-1Number/cd1s/iCD-1(078)_SummaryAndBackupNotes.pdf and http://denebmanagement.com/2017/12/04/construction-set-to-begin-on-508-unit-arbutus-shopping-centre-condo/
- **The land-use plan changed under the area on March 31, 2026:** the Vancouver Official Development Plan replaced the Vancouver Plan on that date — the only file in this batch whose own research records the change, and the reason older plan citations for these streets must be re-checked before they are relied on in a lot valuation. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf
