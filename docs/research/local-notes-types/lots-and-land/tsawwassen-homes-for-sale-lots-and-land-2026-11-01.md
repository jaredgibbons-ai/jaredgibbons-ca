## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — consolidated, NO lettered schedules.** The Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, 1985 — "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" is an ERROR wherever met. Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1, Map 2; agriculture policies at s. 2.6). Development Permit Areas live in Appendix B, including the Tsawwassen Urban Centre DPA. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (City sheets cite No. 7600, 2017; an internal title reads "8400, 2025"). Zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps; zone minimums are stated in the sub-area land homes in §2. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024** — up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide, does not create new lots, and does not waive a hazard or streamside permit area. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Subdivision / servicing — process documented, number not asserted:** a Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 (repealing No. 7162, 2015) is cited in this program from a City council report dated January 24, 2024 (File 12100-05/8288) recommending first, second and third readings; **final adoption and parcel-level standards are UNCONFIRMED**, so subdivision/servicing is confirmed with the City for the specific parcel under LGA s. 506. Sibling lots files asserting "8288, 2024" in governing terms are noted as conflicts, not followed. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Hazard permit areas — the overlays that sort Tsawwassen land:** **SD2 "English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment)"** (OCP Appendix B) covers the west shore — mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence; hazard-affected development is subject to conditions set by a suitably qualified Professional Engineer; no building within 7.5 m of the natural boundary of the sea. **SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands):** no building with habitable floor level lower than 2.9 m GSC datum, minimum land surface elevation 2.3 m GSC datum, no building within 7.5 m of the natural boundary of a tidal area. Whether a specific lot falls inside SD3: UNVERIFIED — requires a DeltaMap check for that lot. **Streamside:** properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, are in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement DPA in this program's Delta framing, on farmland ditches as well as urban lots. Delta is the diking authority (over 63 km of flood protection, 31 pump stations, ~538 km of drainage). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Suites / duplexes at parcel level: UNVERIFIED in this slug's research.** The live page states Delta zoning permits registered secondary suites, detached garden suites on qualifying lots, and infill duplexes in designated zones — site copy in this pass; permission for a specific lot was not verified against the current bylaws in the research, so no suite permission is asserted for any Tsawwassen lot here. https://jaredgibbons.ca/tsawwassen-homes-for-sale.html
- **ALR block, in full (for edge parcels in the reserve):** subdivision of ALR land requires ALC permission, apart from the limited cases in the ALR General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). A principal residence built without an ALC application must be **500 m² or less** total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at **90 m² or less** (parcels of 40 ha or smaller) or **186 m² or less** (larger parcels), where Delta bylaws also permit it. Delta's A1 zone mirrors this in the program's Delta framing: minimum lot size created by subdivision **8 ha** (A3 does not permit subdivision). **Farm class — three-tier, in full:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Bare-land strata block (where a Tsawwassen lot is a bare-land strata lot):** Form B (SPA s. 59) — fees, amounts owed, approved levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page. Depreciation reports every 5 years for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, non-deferrable. CRF minimum 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (long-term; short-term may still be restricted). Only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, incl. May 1, 2023 exemptions). **Fee-simple contrast:** a fee-simple City-side lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** general rates are 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% of the portion over $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — new HOME, not bare land. **Worked examples, inputs + tier math stated:** $700,000 lot, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**; $1,800,000 lot = $2,000 + 2% × $1,600,000 ($32,000) = **$34,000**; $3,500,000 purely residential parcel = $2,000 + $36,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000** (the $93,000 figure governs; a $78,000 figure for this input appearing in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program is an OPEN reconciliation item — it was not met in this slug's siblings and is never carried here). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME purchase, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% as the published range only; **the CMHC premium-range error is NOT documented for this slug and is NOT asserted here** (mortgage-calculator file only). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where is Tsawwassen land documented, if not here?** In the sub-area land files and the Tsawwassen Lots & Land topic file cross-referenced in §2 — shore ground in the English Bluff / Cliff Drive / Beach Grove / Boundary Beach homes, Southlands in the East guide home, farm ground in the Farms home. - **Q: Is Tsawwassen North land a freehold lot option?** No — that product sits on Tsawwassen First Nation Lands under TFN law, as leasehold in this program's documentation. Excluded tenure for any City-side land comparison. The page's specific 99-year / tax wording is UNVERIFIED site copy in this slug's research. (§2 above)
- **Q: What extra rules apply to a bluff or beach lot?** West shore: SD2 — Professional Engineer conditions for hazard-affected development, 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of the sea. Boundary Bay: SD3 — 2.9 m datum floor level, 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation, 7.5 m tidal setback. Confirm both with the City before writing, not from a listing's wording. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a farm parcel near Tsawwassen?** Only within tight limits: in the A1 framing the minimum lot created is 8 ha, A3 does not permit subdivision, and ALR land generally needs ALC permission to subdivide. (§3 above)
- **Q: How big a house can go on a reserve parcel?** Principal residence without an ALC application: 500 m² or less, ≤1,000 m² of fill; suite only inside the principal; one additional residence at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha) where Delta bylaws also permit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me split a lot into four lots?** No. Bylaws 8394 + 8395 (Jun 24, 2024) allow up to four units on a lot; added-unit permission is not subdivision permission and creates no new lots. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same as a freehold lot?** No. A bare-land strata lot is a strata lot (Form B, fees, bylaws) whose lot is essentially the building lot itself; a fee-simple lot has no strata at all. The Tsawwassen-wide split is UNVERIFIED — read tenure off title per parcel. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a $700,000 lot?** PTT $12,000 with no exemption ($2,000 at 1% + $10,000 at 2%). GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Are there market statistics for Tsawwassen land?** No figure is stated in this file: SnapStats does not cover South Delta and publishes no vacant-land category, so land sale counts, price per acre and land days on market are UNVERIFIED; pricing support requires a Paragon pull. (§1 above)
- **Q: How do I check whether a lot is in the ALR?** On the ALC maps, by PID or civic address — reserve status controls the 500 m² cap, the additional-residence limits, and whether subdivision needs Commission permission. Run it before subjects come off. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
