## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

The land-form facts that matter are three: (1) the RS subdivision ladder that decides what an existing lot can become; (2) one older bare-land strata plan, Sunbury Terrace, documented below as its own form; and (3) the ND12 Social Heart, where Delta Gardens is the one completed comprehensively-zoned development and two further applications are proposals only — never inventory. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot subdivision I can buy into in Nordel?** No verified one — Nordel is an established residential area; land trades as built house lots, strata units, and Social Heart assemblies/applications. No presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create here?** It depends entirely on the parcel's zone: the ladder runs from RS7 at a 335 m² subdivision minimum up to RS1 at 4,000 m², with duplex zones RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m² (no RD5 zone exists). Confirm the zone on the City zoning maps before pricing any split. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Q: What does "bare-land strata" at Sunbury Terrace actually mean?** You own a bare-land strata lot (Plan NWS2806 per a directory record) in a 12-unit, 1989 half-duplex-style community at 8376 Centre Street — you pay strata fees, follow the bylaws and get a Form B, but your lot is land with your home on it, which changes how lot-level responsibilities and insurance sit versus a conventional townhouse strata. Read the strata plan and bylaws, not just the Form B summary. https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3083112-8376-centre-street-delta-bc-v4c-3x4/brochure
- **Q: Is Delta Gardens in Nordel, Scottsdale or Annieville?** Listings use all three names for the one complex. The City's application file LU007852 places the site in the ND12 Nordel Social Heart permit area under CD Zone No. 475 — that primary-source placement governs in this file; search all three sub-area names or you will miss listings. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/164210/
- **Q: Can a Nordel house lot add a suite, coach house or garden suite?** Delta's SSMUH framework (Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) permits small-scale multi-unit housing on all single-detached and duplex lots citywide, and Delta permits secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites in combination with single detached and duplex dwellings where the zoning applies — eligibility is parcel-specific. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/
- **Q: Is more supply coming to the Social Heart, and can I buy a lot in it?** Two applications were active in the research — LU009689 (apartment and townhouse, 112 Street and 82 Avenue) and LU009611 (mixed-use, 112 Street and 84 Avenue), both inside ND12. - **Q: Does the Burns Bog edge affect a west-side lot?** The Bog is protected as an Ecological Conservancy Area and borders Nordel at the west edge; Delta directs owners to confirm flood and development-permit status lot by lot, and no floodplain designation for Nordel's residential streets was confirmed — check the specific lot's permit status early. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/burns-bog-ecological-conservancy-area
