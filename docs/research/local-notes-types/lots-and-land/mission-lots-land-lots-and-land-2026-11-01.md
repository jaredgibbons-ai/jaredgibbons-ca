## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **City plan (City parcels): Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026** (standing correction governing — see FILE STATUS; the research file records the April 7, 2026 public hearing to replace and repeal OCP Bylaw 6350-2025, substantively the same OCP with a Regional Context Statement formally incorporated). - **SSMUH (City parcels):** the City incorporated the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation into Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 by **Bylaw 6299-2024, adopted July 17, 2024**. Mission unit counts per lot size are held **UNCONFIRMED** in this file — Surrey's 3/4/6-unit figures are NEVER imported into Mission. https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh and (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/mission-bc-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **Suites on a future build, City side:** all secondary suites must be registered with the City; a suite is a self-contained living space in a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; a new suite goes through the building permit process. A feed card's UC372 suite claim or RR7s coach/garden-home claim is listing text: UNVERIFIED against the bylaw. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **Subdivision / servicing (City parcels): Development and Subdivision Control Bylaw No. 5650-2017 (DSCB 5650-2017), stated only from a City of Mission source found in an earlier batch of this program (the bylaw title is confirmed on mission.ca) and carried with that sourcing** — its schedules govern site assessment for rezoning, development permit and subdivision applications. Specific servicing standards and DCC rates per parcel are not asserted from it here. (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/mission-bc-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **ALR block (applies parcel by parcel, only where the parcel is in the reserve):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval — and a lease of part of an ALR parcel for more than 3 years is itself a subdivision requiring the Commission's permission; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for any parcel on this feed. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Bare-land strata block (applies only where a lot IS bare-land strata — none is verified under this slug):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** a $700,000 parcel, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property taxes, two systems on one page, never mixed:** City of Mission lots — 2026 taxes due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a **FLAT 10% penalty** on current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 (Community Charter ss.233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s.3; Mission has NO alternative-scheme bylaw under Charter s.235 — the rural 5% + 5% is NEVER the City regime). Rural FVRD lots — the owner receives the notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province; under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a median price for Mission lots and land?** Not in the permitted source: SnapStats publishes no land-only split — its Mission figures cover dwellings only (September 2026 detached median $814,000; Condos & Townhomes median $430,000) — so no land count, share or median is stated here, and a lot must be priced on comparable lot sales, not dwelling medians. (§1 above)
- **Q: Are all the listings on this page inside the City of Mission?** No — the first page alone spans six board sub-areas, including Lake Errock (FVRD Electoral Area C), Dewdney Deroche (Area G) and Durieu (Area F), which are unincorporated FVRD ground with rural taxes paid to the Province, alongside City of Mission lots that pay City taxes. Stave Falls and Steelhead, by contrast, are inside the City. (§1 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Mission-area acreage?** It depends on which government governs the parcel and whether it is in the ALR: City parcels follow the City's process (DSCB 5650-2017, §3) and FVRD parcels the regional district's, and ALR landowners who wish to subdivide must apply under the Agricultural Land Commission Act and secure ALC approval — a lease of part of an ALR parcel for more than 3 years also counts as a subdivision requiring permission. (§3 above)
- **Q: What can I build on ALR land near Mission?** The ALC frame permits one principal residence of at most 500 m², a secondary suite only if it is entirely contained within that principal residence and local bylaws allow suites, and at most one additional detached residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); local governments may further restrict residences through zoning. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is everything on this feed a vacant fee-simple lot?** No — the first page includes a resort use-area at Everglades Resort (not land ownership) and a parcel with a large shop already on it (not vacant). Read the tenure line on every "Land" card and confirm exactly what title or tenure transfers. (§2 above)
- **Q: How do I price the servicing gap between lots?** The feed runs from fully serviced city lots to raw acreage with a drilled well — all listing text for that parcel only. Confirm water, septic approval, connection costs and DCCs for the specific PID in writing before comparing a raw parcel's price to a serviced lot's price; parcel-level servicing is UNVERIFIED in the research. (§2 above)
- **Q: Who do I pay property tax to on a vacant lot?** The government the lot sits in: City of Mission lots pay the City (2026 taxes due July 2, 2026, flat 10% penalty July 3); rural FVRD lots receive their notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pay the Province, with rural 5% + 5% penalties under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act. (§3 above)
- **Q: How many extra units can I put on a City lot under SSMUH?** UNCONFIRMED as a number in this file — Mission incorporated SSMUH into Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 on July 17, 2024 (Bylaw 6299-2024), but per-lot unit counts are not carried here, and Surrey's figures are never used in Mission. Confirm the lot's zone and count with the City. (§3 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a lot purchase here?** PTT on the general tiers — on the §3 worked example ($700,000, illustration only) the bill is $12,000 with no exemption. GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level; the FTHB GST rebate is for new housing, not bare land. Confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is Montgomery Condos a lot offering I can buy into as land?** No — it is a presale apartment project (7375 Horne St, 85 units, completing 2026 per the sibling condos files), documented as built-product presale only. Presale LOT offerings under this slug: 0 verified. (§2 above)
