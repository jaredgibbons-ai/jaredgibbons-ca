## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning: City of Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 — Bradner lands are zoned A1 and A2 Agricultural.** Confirm the parcel's A1 or A2 section before assuming any farm or home-based use is permitted; the urban RS minimums used elsewhere in Abbotsford do not describe Bradner parcels. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/bylaws/zoning-bylaw

- **SSMUH / added units:** in June 2024 Council adopted Zoning Bylaw amendments implementing provincial small-scale multi-unit housing — June 2024 adoption is City-confirmed; the SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED ("3560-2024" must never be asserted). In Bradner the practical added-unit rules are the ALR rules below, not the urban SSMUH frame: Abbotsford's urban suite position (s.140.3.8; suite cap under 50% of the principal dwelling; coach/garden suite at 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m² — Abbotsford ONLY) does not override the ALC caps on an ALR parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer against the zone and the City's works-and-services (Development Bylaw) requirements; the Development Bylaw's current number/year is UNCONFIRMED in this program — none is quoted. On ALR land, subdivision additionally requires ALC approval (§3 ALR block) — for most Bradner parcels the subdivision question ends at the agricultural rules, not at a servicing standard. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58305/
- **ALR block — full form:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified — depositing a strata plan causes a subdivision (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite is permitted **only inside** the principal residence — never in a barn or a detached building; one additional residence for farm use or another permitted use, capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Agri-tourism and temporary farm worker housing are permitted under conditions on ALR land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form:** $2,500 of gross farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — with applications by October 31 for the next taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR; no such flat line is asserted in this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Revitalization tax exemption — not a Bradner item:** the City offers a Revitalization Tax Exemption for eligible properties within designated revitalization areas; no Bradner-specific exemption was confirmed in the material read — do not price one into a Bradner parcel: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/tax-incentives-exemptions
- **Strata block — stated for completeness, governing no Bradner stock:** if a strata lot were ever lawfully produced on non-ALR Bradner ground, the BC block would apply to the LOT — Form B (SPA s. 59) within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. On ALR parcels the strata form is barred outright (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-time buyers' exemption: no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — both are home exemptions, and their application to a bare farm parcel purchase is facts-specific: UNCONFIRMED for bare land in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. Farm-land sales in particular are treated by the CRA in separate material — the GST treatment of any specific Bradner farm parcel is UNCONFIRMED until the vendor's status and the parcel's history are established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a bare building lot in Bradner?** There is no lot product to buy — no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering exists here. What trades is whole fee-simple farm and rural-residential parcels under A1/A2 zoning; the full land treatment is in the land home file. (§2 above)
- **Q: Can I build a second house on a Bradner farm property?** Only within the ALC limits: one principal residence per parcel (maximum 500 m²), a secondary suite only inside that residence, and an additional small residence for farm use or another permitted use — maximum 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, 186 m² only on parcels over 40 ha. Do not price a parcel as if a full second house can be added. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a suite go in a barn or a detached building?** No — under the ALC housing rules a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; a detached second dwelling is the "additional residence," which is size-capped and tied to farm use or another permitted use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I subdivide or stratify a Bradner parcel later?** Subdivision in the ALR requires ALC approval, and a strata plan cannot be deposited without the ALC's permission — a Bradner farm home cannot later be converted into strata lots and sold separately as of right (ALCA s. 21(1); SPA s. 239; ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6). How long an ALR subdivision would take: UNCONFIRMED — no official timeline is published. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Is this parcel in the ALR?** Bradner is only partly in the ALR, and no source in this program confirms any single parcel — check the ALC map and the title before pricing subdivision, a second dwelling or farm-class value into the parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: What does farm class require, exactly?** Three tiers, never a flat figure: $2,500 of gross farm income at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — and the application is due by October 31 for the next taxation year. A parcel that stops meeting its tier loses the classification and its tax treatment. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Q: Does GST apply when I buy a farm parcel?** It turns on the seller and the parcel's history, per CRA GI-003: an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt, while a sale in the course of a business or of a parcel subdivided into more than two parts can be taxable. Farm-land sales have their own CRA treatment — the position for a specific Bradner parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and history are established; have the contract state it. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What will it cost to hold the parcel?** Property taxes (due July 2; 5% penalty July 3 and a second 5% September 16, 2026), plus insurance, well/septic maintenance and any right-of-way or covenant obligations on title — the Bradner logging right-of-way on some titles is the local example. There is no strata fee: the parcel is fee-simple. Farm classification, where it is kept, is the main tax-treatment reducer — and it depends on the income actually being produced and reported. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Q: Which schools serve a Bradner parcel?** Catchments are address-based in SD34. The SETTLED chain for the test address 5396 Bradner Road is Bradner Elementary (K–5) → **Eugene Reimer Middle (6–8), NOT W.A. Fraser** → Rick Hansen Secondary (9–12) (SD34 School Locator, mybaragar.com, run 2026-11-01). Any other Bradner address needs its own locator run. https://eugenereimer.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/ermsfamilyofschools/eugenereimer
