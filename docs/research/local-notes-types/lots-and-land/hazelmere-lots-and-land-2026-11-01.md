## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current.** Bylaw No. 18020 is never cited as current. The parcel's designation is read in COSMOS, alongside the South Campbell Heights plan position for ground at the north edge. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000** — the A-1 (Part 10) and RA figures in §2 are live-page statements held UNVERIFIED on the bylaw text in this file; parcel zoning is confirmed per parcel in COSMOS and in the current consolidated bylaw before any figure is quoted for a listing. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **ALR block (the governing frame for any Hazelmere parcel that is in the ALR):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel — a principal residence up to 500 m² total floor area without ALC application, a secondary suite only inside the principal residence and only where local bylaws allow it, and at most one additional detached residence of up to 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); local governments may further restrict residences through zoning. Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR; whether any specific Hazelmere property carries farm-class taxation is a BC Assessment / parcel question, UNVERIFIED in the research. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Coach house on A-1 / ALR ground — UNVERIFIED parcel-by-parcel:** the live page states a separate coach house on an A-1 or ALR acreage is not confirmed on a City source, and the ALC frame permits an additional residence only within its size limits (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size) — a Hazelmere coach-house claim (including a feed listing that already shows detached coach-house product) is therefore confirmed with the City and, where the parcel is in the ALR, against the ALC frame, before it is marketed as a permitted second dwelling. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere.html and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **SSMUH caution for this ground:** Surrey's SSMUH allowances apply to eligible residential-zoned properties under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including lands not connected to community water and sewer — most Hazelmere acreage ground is outside that shape, so the standard 3–4 unit framing (3 units under 280 m² / 4 units over 280 m² / up to 6 near a frequent bus stop, from Jul 8, 2024) must NOT be applied to a Hazelmere lot without a parcel check of zone, containment boundary and servicing. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites / coach houses (urban Surrey frame, for completeness — not assumed to govern A-1 ground):** one suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex unit, capped at 40% of the principal unit and never inside a coach house or garden suite; coach/garden suites 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR). On A-1 / ALR parcels the ALR block above and the parcel's zone govern first. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision / servicing:** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000 (for A-1 ground, the Part 10 figures in §2, held UNVERIFIED on the bylaw text), confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. Verified on the City's own Land Development pages in an earlier program batch: the **Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw, worked through the Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. Servicing itself is parcel-specific here: the live page points well/septic buyers to the Province's GWELLS well-record system (carried as the page's guidance, UNVERIFIED on a Province source in the research), and one feed parcel is described as municipally serviced — read the services for the parcel, not the sub-area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside / riparian:** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and vary by stream class, with the Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit process alongside — the Little Campbell River and Jacobsen Creek run through this ground, so a parcel near either is checked against its stream class in COSMOS and Part 7A. No single setback figure is stated, and no parcel-level figure was opened in the research. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only, primary residence, under 90 consecutive days per patron, business licence **$358 per year**. **Conflict disclosed:** the City's 2024 corporate report put the licence at $350/yr plus fees — direct readers to the City licence page before quoting. Rural/acreage application to a specific parcel: confirm with the City. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot, wherever one surfaces):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% up to and including $200,000, 2% over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example (anchor):** $1,500,000 fully residential parcel, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $1,300,000 = **$28,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. For farm/ALR parcels, anything finer than GI-003 (including any farm-specific treatment) is UNCONFIRMED in this file — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
