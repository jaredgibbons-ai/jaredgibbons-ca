- **Secondary suites and detached dwellings — Mission:** a Mission secondary suite is a self-contained living space inside a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; a suite is not a coach home or garden cottage (those are detached secondary dwellings); suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; all suites must be registered with the City, new suites go through the building permit process, and an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus utility fees applies.
