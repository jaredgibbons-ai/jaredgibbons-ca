## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), carried verbatim as this file's own research opened the OSRE source:** Rule 5-10(3) provides that a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Representation / dual agency:** BCFSA states a licensee must only work for either buyer or seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of duties to clients and non-clients and inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party; dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest circumstances. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Visitor ID/sign-in:** no BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states an ID or sign-in requirement — UNVERIFIED as a rule; a host may ask on the seller's instructions.
- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 5000:** R1-A (Urban Residential) minimum lot area 500 m² on community water and sewer, minimum lot width 15 m on same services; R1-C (Urban Infill) minimum 300 m² interior / 360 m² corner. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **OCP — Chilliwack 2050, Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 5500:** long-term vision for residential (urban, rural, hillside), commercial, industrial, institutional uses and ALR protection; Schedule D permit areas include D-1 Drinking Water Supply, D-2 Hillside Environment, D-3 Riparian Area, D-4 Farm Home Plate, D-5 Hillside Development. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **SSMUH:** Chilliwack SSMUH Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024 (standing program correction; parcel eligibility is a City check).
- **Subdivision + servicing:** no verified Chilliwack subdivision/servicing bylaw number is stated in this file's research — none quoted. Subdivision runs off Bylaw 5000 zone minimums above; servicing position is per-parcel.
- **ALR:** Chilliwack has ALR land covered by OCP Schedule B1 Agricultural Area Plan; ALR subdivision needs ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31) — flat "$2,500+" is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1_Agricultural_Area_Plan_1.pdf
- **Flood:** the City's Fraser River flood protection is a dyke system keyed to the Mission gauge; areas outside the dyking system flood at higher stages — address-specific. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1_Agricultural_Area_Plan_1.pdf
- **PTT:** general 1% on first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on residential portion above $3M; FTHB Program first $500,000 exempt at ≤$835,000 (max $8,000, partial to $860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's personal-use land sale generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** applies to new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Strata block (where a bare-land strata lot is the parcel):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation 5-yr cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; 10% CRF since Nov 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Chilliwack taxes:** notices mailed end of May, due first working day after July 1; 2026: 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and further 5% after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Chilliwack-specific position is documented in this file's research — none imported; position UNCONFIRMED here. Vancouver EHT 3% is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Do I need my own agent to attend a Chilliwack open house?** No official source requires it. Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure only while hosting/factual answers — sharing motivation, finances or needs engages disclosure. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download

- **Q: Does every listing on the page hold an open house?** NOT CONFIRMED — one displayed card showed no open-house label. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-open-houses.html
- **Q: Do I have to sign in or show ID?** No official consumer source states a requirement — UNVERIFIED as a rule; a host may ask on seller's instructions. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: If a lot I see is in the ALR, can it be subdivided or stratified?** Subdivision needs ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Check the parcel on ALC mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Q: How small can a Chilliwack urban lot be?** By zone in Bylaw 5000: R1-A 500 m² / 15 m width on community services; R1-C 300 m² interior / 360 m² corner. Parcel zone governs. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: What flood check matters for a farm-edge or low-lying lot?** Whether it sits inside the dyke system keyed to the Mission gauge — outside areas flood at higher stages. Address-specific. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1_Agricultural_Area_Plan_1.pdf
- **Q: Will GST apply to a vacant lot bought from a developer?** Generally yes — developer/business and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable (CRA GI-003); an individual's personal-use resale is generally exempt. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which school will a Chilliwack address feed?** Run the exact address in SD33's School Locator — catchments are address-based and under review from fall 2026, with Cheam changes effective September 2027. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q: When are Chilliwack taxes due if I buy mid-year?** Due first working day after July 1; 2026 penalties 5% after July 2 and +5% after August 4; lawyer/notary allocates on the Statement of Adjustments. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
