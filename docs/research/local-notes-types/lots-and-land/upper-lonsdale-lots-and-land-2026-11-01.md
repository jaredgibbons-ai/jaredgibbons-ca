## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Upper Lonsdale has no meaningful bare-land market — stated plainly at the top. This is a built-out, predominantly single-detached slope neighbourhood split between two municipalities. No named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan, and no presale lot offering was verified. Land here trades as a house on its slope lot — with value rising with elevation and view corridor — and the live land questions are the two municipalities' different density rules (§3) and the District's slope-hazard regime, not lot inventory.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **READ FIRST — two rulebooks, each confined to its own side:** a specific address's City-vs-District status determines its OCP, zoning bylaw, permit desk and property tax bill. Nothing from one side's rulebook is imported to the other side anywhere in this section. The split point for a given parcel is a per-parcel check on municipal mapping; 29th Street is the avenue's working line. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/plans-and-projects/area-planning/north-shore/phase1/nsatp_phase1_app7.pdf
- **CITY SIDE — OCP 8400 / Zoning 6700:** the City of North Vancouver's Official Community Plan is the Official Community Plan, 2014, No. 8400 (amended December 8, 2025, Amendment Bylaw 2025 No. 9114, for provincial housing requirements; the City sized its updates to a 20-year housing need of 21,301 units on top of its existing 29,021 units). Zoning is City Zoning Bylaw 6700 (1995). In a City developer information session for 2762 Lonsdale Avenue, staff described the blocks along Lonsdale as within Residential Level 5 with a maximum height of 6 storeys. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Official-Community-Plan-2014-No-8400-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9114-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-t.pdf and https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Major-Development/2762-Lonsdale-Avenue/Developer-Information-Session-Summary.pdf
- **CITY SIDE — suites, coach houses, GO Zone (SSMUH position as the City states it):** the City legalized secondary suites in all single-family dwellings in 1993; one suite per single-family property, the owner must reside at the property, and a suite may be combined with an accessory coach house (permitted where the front lot line is at least 10 m / 32.81 ft, requiring a Development Permit and a Building Permit). On December 8, 2025 the City adopted the Ground Oriented (GO) Zone, increasing permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 residential properties and allowing up to 6 units per lot on approximately 600 properties — check the GO Zone before pricing a City-side lot as single-family only. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Permits-Inspections/building-permits/secondary-suites and https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/HTO-Year-2-Progress-Report-July-1,-2025-June-30,-2026.pdf
- **CITY SIDE — subdivision + servicing, Bylaw 8014 (City files ONLY):** the City's subdivision/servicing instrument is Bylaw 8014 in this program's verified ledger — citable for City-side parcels only. It is never applied to a District-side parcel.
- **DISTRICT SIDE — OCP 7900 / Zoning 3210:** the District's Official Community Plan is Bylaw 7900; it designates Queensdale as a Village Centre and directs growth to its four centres while preserving established low-density neighbourhoods, which covers the District portion of Upper Lonsdale. District zoning is Bylaw 3210. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf
- **DISTRICT SIDE — SSMUH position as the District states it:** District zoning has permitted a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary since June 2024, with suites up to 49% of the house and coach houses up to two storeys. The District's GeoWeb SSMUH classification shows potential for up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 above 280 m², and up to 6 above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop; the Province's Bill 25 requires full SSMUH density by June 30, 2026 and the District's amendments were still in preparation in spring 2026 — so the District-side density position is stated as in-transition, never as settled. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **DISTRICT SIDE — subdivision + servicing number UNCONFIRMED (held):** no District servicing bylaw number is asserted anywhere in this file. The City's 8014 is named here solely as a never-imported exclusion for District parcels — a District-side subdivision follows the District's own process, confirmed with the District.
- **DISTRICT SIDE — hazard DPs:** the District designates Natural Hazard Development Permit Areas and publishes property-level GeoWeb hazard reports; steep lots should be checked before additions or rebuilds, as slope DPAs can add geotechnical cost. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-1-district-landslide.pdf
- **Taxes differ by side of the line:** both municipalities set 2026 property taxes due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026. City 2026 rates: residential municipal-general 1.89202 and total 3.34028 per $1,000 of assessed value, plus a separate residential storm drainage rate of 0.10697 per $1,000 (the City illustrates an average residential property assessed at $1,387,684 paying $4,790 in total 2026 taxes). District 2026 rates: residential municipal 1.83419 and total 3.28195 per $1,000. Same street, different bills — confirm which municipality the parcel is taxed in. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026_TAX_RATE_SUMMARY_CITY_OF_NORTH_VANCOUVER-2.PDF and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked examples (inputs + tier math): $1,200,000 → $22,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Foreclosure / court-ordered sales:** the settled court process applies (BC Supreme Court Civil Rules 21-7/13-5, Practice Direction PD-66, Form 60). Form B and PTT/GST treatment on court sales: UNCONFIRMED, kept distinct from GI-003. No registry statement is made for North Vancouver in this program's per-city ledger — UNCONFIRMED here. https://www.bccourts.ca/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is this lot in the City or the District — and why does it matter?** It decides everything regulatory: OCP (City 8400 vs District 7900), zoning (City 6700 vs District 3210), servicing (City 8014 vs District number UNCONFIRMED), permit desk and tax bill. Roughly 25th–29th St is City; 29th St north to Rockland is District — confirm the specific address on municipal mapping. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/plans-and-projects/area-planning/north-shore/phase1/nsatp_phase1_app7.pdf
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Upper Lonsdale?** No verified bare-land offering exists — this is a built-out detached slope neighbourhood. Land trades as a house on its lot, priced on elevation, view corridor and the density rules for its side of the line. (§2.)
- **Q: How many units can I build on a City-side lot?** Check the December 2025 GO Zone first: the City raised permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 residential properties and allows up to 6 per lot on about 600 properties, on top of the long-standing suite + coach house framework (one suite per property, owner must reside, coach house needs a front lot line of at least 10 m plus DP/BP). Which properties carry which count is a per-parcel check. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/HTO-Year-2-Progress-Report-July-1,-2025-June-30,-2026.pdf
- **Q: How many units on a District-side lot?** Three units total (principal + suite + coach house) on single-family zoned properties in the Urban Containment Boundary since June 2024, with GeoWeb showing potential up to 4 above 280 m² and up to 6 above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop — but full SSMUH density under Bill 25 was still in preparation in spring 2026, so treat the higher counts as in-transition, not settled. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Q: What will slope do to my rebuild budget?** On the District side, Natural Hazard DPs and the GeoWeb hazard report come first — steep lots can add geotechnical cost to additions and rebuilds. Pull the property-level report before pricing the lot as a clean rebuild. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf
- **Q: Are Somerset Green and Vista 29 on the same rulebook?** No — that is the straddle in one pair of buildings: Somerset Green is City-side (City bylaws, City servicing 8014 citable for its side), Vista 29 (BCS2494) is District-side (District bylaws, District servicing number UNCONFIRMED). Both are built strata complexes documented in the siblings, not land. (§2.)
- **Q: Are there bare-land strata lots here?** None verified — and the form itself is strata tenure (Form B, fees, bylaws), not fee-simple land, wherever it exists. (§2.)
- **Q: Which schools serve my address?** SD44 on both sides (the municipal split does not change the district): Westview and Larson elementary (Larson is dual-track French Immersion), École André-Piolat, and Carson Graham Secondary for most addresses — but catchments are address-based, the eastern edge needs an Argyle-boundary check, and no address-level locator result is asserted in this file. https://www.sd44.ca/school/larson/About/
- **Q: Do taxes really differ on the same avenue?** Yes — both due July 2, 2026 with 5% + 5% penalties, but the totals differ (City total 3.34028 + storm drainage 0.10697 per $1,000; District total 3.28195 per $1,000 for 2026), and District single-family utility fees (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60) apply only on the District side. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **Q: How much PTT on a $1.5M slope-lot purchase?** $28,000 on the general tiers ($2,000 + $26,000 at 2% on $1.3M), before any exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
