## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP:** Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, adopted July 8, 2024 — NO lettered schedules; farm land designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1), agriculture policies at s. 2.6. "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" is an error wherever met. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted:** City of Delta pages cite Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017 (residential zones incl. RS1, RS2 and RS3 at a minimum lot size for subdivision of 4,000 m²; RS4 at 610 m²; RS5 at 550 m² or 460 m² depending on designation; RS6 at 390 m²; RS7 at 335 m²; RD1 at 836 m²; RD2 and RD3 at 4,000 m²; RD4 at 504 m²; there is no RD5 zone), while an internal consolidated title in this program says "8400, 2025" — the collision is flagged and neither number is asserted as settled; zoning is parcel-by-parcel on the City zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites:** Delta Bylaws 8394 and 8395 (adopted June 24, 2024) and the City's provincially required updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies; the RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use to a single detached dwelling, and the City supports accessory dwelling units such as coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites. What applies to a specific lot in this band is a parcel-level zone check; no prevalence claim about suites in recent Ladner builds is made here. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Subdivision and servicing — process, adoption HELD UNCONFIRMED:** a City council report (January 24, 2024) presents the Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 to repeal and replace Bylaw No. 7162, 2015 (Local Government Act s. 506). Final adoption status: UNCONFIRMED in this file — confirm the governing standard with the City for any specific subdivision file. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Flood / streamside:** the City of Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure; all properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — flood construction level and insurance questions are address-specific. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Foreshore tenure (riverfront stock):** the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; tenure is by lease or licence only via FrontCounter BC, and private moorage is held under General Permission or Specific Permission — upland title, a dock and provincial foreshore tenure are different things. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **ALR block:** ALR status is parcel-specific via ALC mapping; ALR subdivision needs ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **PTT:** 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above; further 2% on residential value over $3,000,000 (mixed-class: residential portion only). Worked math in §2. **Exemptions do not reach this band:** FTHB full exemption caps at $835,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Home owner grant at this band:** 2026 threshold $2,075,000 — full regular grant at/below, reduced $5 per $1,000 over, $0 over $2,189,000, so many homes in this band receive a reduced grant or none. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Delta property taxes:** Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; Mar 20, 2025–2031) — and it phases out below this band's floor in any case. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many Ladner homes are over $1.8M right now?** Cannot be stated from sources used — SnapStats publishes no price-band cut and does not cover South Delta at all; the live feed is the only place the current count can be seen. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-homes-over-1-8m.html
- **Q: Which Ladner areas appear on this page?** The cards read were labelled Holly, Ladner Elementary, Ladner Rural, Hawthorne and Neilsen Grove — feed labels only, not a price claim about any sub-area. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-homes-over-1-8m.html
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $1.8M / $2M / $3.5M?** Worked in §2: $34,000 just over $1.8M, $38,000 at $2,000,000 and $93,000 at $3,500,000 (fully residential). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Is there extra PTT over $3 million?** Yes — a further 2% on the residential property value greater than $3,000,000, on top of the general tiers; on a mixed-class property it applies only to the residential portion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Does any exemption help at $1.8M+?** No — the first-time program caps at $835,000 and the newly built exemption is gone at $1,150,000; budget full PTT. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Will I still get the home owner grant?** Reduced over $2,075,000 assessed and $0 over $2,189,000 for the regular grant (2026) — a real annual-cost difference between two otherwise similar homes straddling the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Q: What should I check on a riverfront or acreage home in this band?** Flood and streamside position (behind the City's dike system; within 30 m of a slough or ditch = Streamside Protection and Enhancement DPA), foreshore tenure for any dock or moorage (provincial Crown land, by lease or licence), and the parcel's ALR status on ALC maps. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Can I subdivide a large Ladner lot?** Only against its zone minimum (zoning number CONTESTED — §3) plus City subdivision/servicing requirements, with DSSS Bylaw 8288's adoption UNCONFIRMED — and on ALR land, ALC approval is required and ALR residences cannot be stratified at all. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Q: Is GST due on a newly subdivided lot in this band?** Generally yes under CRA GI-003 (developer/newly subdivided = generally taxable); an individual's personal-use land sale is generally exempt. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which school serves an over-$1.8M address?** SD37 locator by exact address — for the one documented sample (4600 53 Street) the result is Ladner Elementary and Delta Secondary; that result does not transfer to any other address. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
