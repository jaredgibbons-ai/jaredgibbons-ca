- **ALR block, in full (for Ladner Rural / East Delta parcels in the reserve):** subdivision of ALR land requires ALC permission, apart from the limited cases in the ALR General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). A principal residence built without an ALC application must be **500 m² or less** total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at **90 m² or less** (parcels of 40 ha or smaller) or **186 m² or less** (larger parcels), where Delta bylaws also permit it. Delta's A1 zone mirrors this in the program's Delta framing: minimum lot size created by subdivision **8 ha**, one farm house per lot, farm house maximum 500 m². **Farm class — three-tier, in full:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Bare-land strata block (where a Ladner lot is a bare-land strata lot):** Form B (SPA s. 59) — fees, amounts owed, approved levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page. Depreciation reports every 5 years for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, non-deferrable. CRF minimum 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (long-term; short-term may still be restricted). Only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, incl. May 1, 2023 exemptions). **Fee-simple contrast:** a fee-simple village lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** general rates are 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% of the portion over $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — new HOME, not bare land. **Worked examples, inputs + tier math stated:** $500,000 lot, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**; $1,200,000 lot = $2,000 + 2% × $1,000,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**; $3,500,000 purely residential parcel = $2,000 + $36,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000** (the $93,000 figure governs; a $78,000 figure for this input appearing in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program is an OPEN reconciliation item — it was not met in this slug's siblings and is never carried here). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME purchase, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% as the published range only; **the CMHC premium-range error is NOT documented for this slug and is NOT asserted here** (mortgage-calculator file only). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where is Ladner land documented, if not here?** In the sub-area land files cross-referenced in §2 — village ground in the Ladner Elementary / Delta Manor / Hawthorne / Holly / Neilsen Grove / Port Guichon homes, farm-belt ground in the Ladner Rural / East Delta / Ladner Farms homes. - **Q: Is Port Guichon part of Ladner, and is East Delta North Delta?** Port Guichon is in Ladner. East Delta, per its research, is the Ladner (South Delta) farm-belt side — not North Delta ground. (§1, §2 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Ladner farm parcel?** Only within tight limits: in the A1 framing the minimum lot created by subdivision is 8 ha, and ALR land generally needs ALC permission to subdivide. Confirm the parcel's zone and reserve status first. (§3 above) https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: How big a house can go on a reserve parcel?** Principal residence without an ALC application: 500 m² or less, ≤1,000 m² of fill; suite only inside the principal; one additional residence at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha) where Delta bylaws also permit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What flood check comes before pricing a Ladner lot build?** The parcel's Flood Construction Level — Ladner is dike-protected floodplain and the FCL is parcel-specific; the research marks it UNVERIFIED for any specific lot until the City confirms it. (§3 above)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me split a village lot into four lots?** No. Bylaws 8394 + 8395 (Jun 24, 2024) allow up to four units on a lot; added-unit permission is not subdivision permission and creates no new lots. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same as a freehold lot?** No. A bare-land strata lot is a strata lot (Form B, fees, bylaws) whose lot is essentially the building lot itself; a fee-simple lot has no strata at all. The Ladner-wide split is UNVERIFIED — read tenure off title per parcel. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a $500,000 lot?** PTT $8,000 with no exemption ($2,000 at 1% + $6,000 at 2%). GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Are there market statistics for Ladner land?** No figure is stated in this file: SnapStats does not cover South Delta and publishes no vacant-land category, so land sale counts, price per acre and land days on market are UNVERIFIED; pricing support requires a Paragon pull. (§1 above)
- **Q: Can I rely on the page's suite and coach-house wording for my lot?** Not at parcel level — the research carries that wording as site copy, UNVERIFIED against the current bylaws for a specific lot. Confirm with the City before writing. (§3 above) https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-homes-for-sale.html
