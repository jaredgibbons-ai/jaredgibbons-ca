## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP — Chilliwack:** Zoning Bylaw No. 5000 governs (§6 carries the farm zones). The Official Community Plan is Bylaw No. 5500, "Chilliwack 2050" — Sardis = Schedule B5; Agricultural Plan = B1; Eastern Hillsides = B4; Chilliwack Proper = B-2; Downtown = B3 + D-6. The parcel's own zone and schedule govern any subdivision or added-unit question: UNCONFIRMED per parcel here. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **SSMUH — Chilliwack Bylaw 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024):** the small-scale multi-unit housing amendment to Zoning Bylaw 5000 (proven from the Bylaw 5000 s.14 amendment record; settled position in this program). Added SSMUH units are not subdivision and do not create new lots. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Subdivision and servicing — HELD UNCONFIRMED, process documented instead:** the Chilliwack servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in this file. The candidate "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" is NOT adopted here and is never asserted as the governing bylaw — subdivision runs through the Approving Officer / Preliminary Layout Approval (PLA) process with the City, confirmed for the specific parcel, with conditions, DCCs, frontage and servicing requirements settled per parcel: UNVERIFIED in the research pass for any specific lot.
- **ALR block (ALR ground exists across the Chilliwack farm belt):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no such line is documented for this slug. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (strata lots, including any bare-land strata parcel):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — the research directs hillside buyers to roof, siding, window and driveway timelines and any retaining-wall maintenance obligations in Promontory or Eastern Hillsides complexes; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-townhomes-under-1-2m.html
- **Leasehold caution (from the research's own buying tips):** on a leasehold townhome, check the lease expiration year, whether the lease is prepaid or collects monthly ground rent, and which lenders will finance that specific complex — the first question on any Vedder Road / Bailey Road / Promontory Road unit. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-townhomes-under-1-2m.html
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and a further 5% after August 4, 2026 (City Tax FAQ, opened directly and verified). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $900,000 purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$900,000 ($14,000) = **$16,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. At $3,500,000 the full tier math including the further 2% is $93,000; a townhomes sibling in this program carries $78,000 for $3,500,000 — that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item, flagged here and never silently carried. Newly Built Home Exemption (Province): full PTT exemption at fair market value $1,100,000 or less for a qualifying newly built home as a principal residence, partial phasing out to $1,150,000, effective April 1, 2024 — the live page's FAQ statement of these thresholds matches the Province's page; it is a new-HOME exemption, not a bare-land exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
