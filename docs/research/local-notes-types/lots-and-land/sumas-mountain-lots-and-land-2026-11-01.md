## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

Neighbourhoods inside or at the foot: Auguston, McKee and the Eagle Mountain side on the City slopes; Clayburn Village at the northwest foot. Nearby areas: Abbotsford East (west/southwest foot), Sumas Prairie (south), Chilliwack (east side). https://jaredgibbons.ca/sumas-mountain.html and https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- Main roads are Sumas Mountain Road (becoming Upper Sumas Mountain Road toward the regional park), McKee Road, Dawson Road and Whatcom Road, connecting down to Highway 1 at the Whatcom Road interchange at the south foot. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/new-park-dianne-brook-ave

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What does "bare-land strata" at Straiton actually mean for me?** You own a strata lot (EPS1299, 26 lots per the City report — documented once in the guide lots file, §2 cross-reference), pay strata fees, follow the bylaws, and get a Form B on resale — but your lot is a detached-house lot, not a unit in a shared building. There is no shared roof/envelope the way a townhouse strata has. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/45965/
- **Q: Why do listings say 42 lots when the City says 26?** The 42 figure is a listing description of the wider community; the City report's strata-plan figure is 26 single-family strata lots. The wider total is UNCONFIRMED — rely on the plan / Form B. (§2.)
- **Q: City lot or FVRD lot — how do I tell, and why care?** The governing plan, servicing, and even who you pay taxes to all change at the boundary (City vs FVRD Bylaw 584 / Surveyor of Taxes). Confirm the parcel's jurisdiction before relying on any rule in this file. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I build on a steep McKee-area lot?** Possibly — but Steep Slope DPA guidelines apply and slopes over 40% / unstable geotechnical areas are protected open space in the McKee plan. Get geotechnical advice before subjects. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **Q: Is there water and sewer on the upper mountain?** No — the FVRD rural part has no public water/sewer at rural density; homes use wells and septic. Check the GWELLS well record and Fraser Health sewerage filing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Q: Can I subdivide a large rural parcel?** City-side minimums run 0.4 ha (SR) to 4.0 ha (RR) by zone; FVRD minimums are UNVERIFIED here, and any ALR parcel needs ALC approval to subdivide. No subdivision promise can be made from this file — get the zone/jurisdiction confirmed. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: When will McKee lots be for sale?** UNCONFIRMED — the McKee Road lands are a July 2025 council-consideration proposal (~108 detached lots proposed), not a sales program. No presale lot offering was verified for this slug. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2025-07/2025-07-10%20City%20Page.pdf
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a new lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What are holding costs on a mountain lot?** Taxes under the correct regime (City July 2 / 5% + 5% Sept 16, 2026, or rural July 2 / 5% + 5% Oct 31), plus strata fees if the lot is a Straiton bare-land strata lot, plus well/septic costs on rural ground. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve Eagle Mountain vs Auguston?** They split — the two settled locator chains are in §1 and they go to different middle/secondary schools, for those two addresses only. Run your exact address. (§1.)
