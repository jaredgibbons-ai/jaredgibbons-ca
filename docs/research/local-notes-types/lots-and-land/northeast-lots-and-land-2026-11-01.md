## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs;** its schedule minimum lot areas include A-1 2.0 ha; A-2 4.0 ha; A-3 8.0 ha; A-4 1.8 ha; A-5 8.0 ha; RS-2 0.4 ha; RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha (refer to zone text); RS-1d 2,000 m²; RS-1c 1,200 m². The live page's summary (RS-2 as a 1-acre minimum, RS-3 as 2-acre with community water / 5-acre without) is broadly consistent with the RS-2 0.4 ha figure but was not reconciled line by line with the bylaw in this pass — use the bylaw figures, and confirm any parcel's zone per property. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **OCP citation — CONTESTED (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024):** flagged here and wherever met in this file, resolved nowhere — quote planning policy from a current City document for the specific parcel. City-wide frame carried for completeness: the Albion Area Plan was adopted October 28, 2025 and the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was ADOPTED January 27, 2026 (Bylaw 7781-2021) per the City record; neither plan area is inside this sub-area as documented. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP
- **Suites:** one secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, contained within the single detached building, to a maximum of 40% of gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area. On ALR parcels, the ALR Use Regulation also governs: one secondary suite in the principal residence (s.31) and an additional residence only if the s.32 conditions are met. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **Flood / hazards:** the City's floodplain framework addresses flood risks from the Fraser River, the Alouette River system, steep creek watercourses and groundwater-related flooding; low land along the Fraser River at the sub-area's south edge is the area to check on City flood maps, property by property. The City describes Thornhill as heavily forested; a City-documented wildfire interface map for specific Northeast streets: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-climate-action-flood-resilience-environmental-protection-and-mckenney-creek
- **ALR block:** ALR status is per-parcel — check the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder; no sub-area-wide ALR share or boundary is stated on City pages. Where a parcel IS in the ALR: subdivision needs ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, by Oct 31) — flat "$2,500+" is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (applies only if a parcel is bare-land strata — none verified here):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **History note for land buyers:** the City states Ruskin has a long history of lumber and sawmills, with shake and sawmills still active along the Stave River, and Ruskin Community Hall remains an important meeting place — working-land context for the sub-area's east side. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- **Tax timing:** a City of Maple Ridge 2026 property-tax due date confirmed on a City source in this pass: UNVERIFIED — no date is asserted here. (Research file above, §4.)
- **PTT:** 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above; further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000 (mixed-class: residential portion only; farm-class land with a dwelling: up to 0.5 ha treated as residential). PTT is on fair market value at registration, not annual taxes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FTHB PTT exemption:** first $500,000 exempt, full at fair market value ≤$835,000 (maximum saving $8,000), partial to $860,000. **Newly Built Home Exemption:** full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000; agreements March 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What and where is "Northeast" Maple Ridge?** "Northeast" is the board/Paragon sub-area name for the rural northeast of the City of Maple Ridge — the live page is titled "Northeast Maple Ridge Homes for Sale" and SnapStats lists "Northeast" as a Maple Ridge sub-area; it is not a City neighbourhood name, and it spans the City neighbourhoods of Ruskin, Thornhill, Webster's Corners, Whonnock and Yennadon. It is not in Coquitlam. https://jaredgibbons.ca/northeast.html and https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- **Q: What sold in Northeast in September 2026?** Nothing — detached: 0 solds from 2 listings; condos & townhomes: 0 listings and 0 solds, so no sales ratio applies. At the Maple Ridge community level the detached median sale price was $1,131,000 and the condos & townhomes median was $525,000 — community context only. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Can I subdivide an acreage here?** Only if the zone minimum, ALR status and floodplain position all allow it — the zoning minimums (A-2 at 4.0 ha, A-3 at 8.0 ha) only matter if the parcel can actually be subdivided, and on ALR land subdivision needs ALC approval. Parcel-level: UNVERIFIED until checked. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can an acreage home here add a suite or a second dwelling?** One secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3 if it sits inside the single detached building, is at most 40% of gross floor area and is not in the Floodplain Area; on ALR land the ALR Use Regulation also applies, and an additional separate residence is allowed only under its s.32 conditions. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **Q: What major plan affects northeast Maple Ridge?** The North 256 Street Industrial Lands Area Plan, approved by Council as OCP amendments, covers both sides of 256 Street north of 128 Avenue (about 500 acres); it sets future designations and does not itself approve construction. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-approves-new-500-acre-256-street-industrial-area-plan-support-jobs-and-economic
- **Q: Which schools are inside Northeast?** Blue Mountain, Webster's Corners and Whonnock elementary schools and Garibaldi and Samuel Robertson Technical secondary schools are physically inside the sub-area; for the sample address 25554 Dewdney Trunk Road the SD42 locator gives Webster's Corners Elementary and Garibaldi Secondary — check any other address in the locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: Is a Northeast lot on city water and sewer?** Rural properties here may be on wells and septic — wells sit in the provincial GWELLS database and sewerage systems must meet the Sewerage System Regulation's setbacks (including 30 m from a well unless professional advice is obtained); both are subject-removal items here, not closing-week surprises. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Q: Is GST due on a newly subdivided lot here?** Generally yes under CRA GI-003 (developer/newly subdivided = generally taxable); an individual's personal-use resale is generally exempt — and the FTHB GST rebate reaches new housing, not bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: How do I price a Northeast acreage with no sub-area sales?** From the Maple Ridge community evidence (detached median $1,131,000, 97% sale-to-list, 29 days on market in September 2026) plus acreage comparables from the surrounding sub-areas — and say plainly that the sub-area sample is effectively empty. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Does the 256 Street plan mean industrial building is coming to my lot?** No — the plan establishes future designations and does not itself approve construction; existing uses in the plan area include gravel extraction, institutional operations and civic services. Disclose it as a designation change, not a development approval. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-approves-new-500-acre-256-street-industrial-area-plan-support-jobs-and-economic
