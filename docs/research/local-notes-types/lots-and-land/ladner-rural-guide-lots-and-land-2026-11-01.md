## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — consolidated, with NO lettered schedules.** Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)"; Development Permit Areas live in the OCP's Appendix B. Any "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" attribution is an ERROR wherever it is met (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the superseded Bylaw No. 3950). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (City pages cite No. 7600, 2017, against an internal title reading "8400, 2025"; a ruling is needed). The A1 zone sheet itself is the source used for the A1 rules in this file. SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024, allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies — the A1/ALR frame below, not SSMUH, governs farm parcels. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **A1 residential limits:** farming is a principal use; accessory uses include a farm house accessory to farming, one secondary suite accessory to a farm house, an accessory farm dwelling unit, migrant farm worker housing, and equestrian facilities to a maximum of 40 permanent stalls accessory to farming. One farm house per lot is permitted, maximum total floor area 500 m²; the farm home plate maximum is 3,600 m² for a farm house with or without an accessory farm dwelling unit (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **A1 second and additional dwellings:** one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot is permitted, at a maximum of 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, located on the same farm home plate as the farm house; ALC approval is required for more than one accessory farm dwelling unit; an additional farm house (180 m² maximum on lots under 8 ha, 233 m² on lots of 8 ha or more) requires a Professional Agrologist's report supporting its necessity and written ALC approval. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Subdivision of farm land (A1 s. 10.10.8):** subdivision is not permitted except where it complies with the Official Community Plan Bylaw and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — DSSS Bylaw No. 8288, 2024, adoption UNCONFIRMED:** cited in this program from a civicweb / January 2024 council report — final adoption and parcel-level standards UNCONFIRMED in this file; confirm with the City for the specific parcel. A specific parcel's ALR status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address; the ALC states its online tools are for general reference only, with official confirmation from its Mapping and GIS team. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Farm class — three-tier, in full:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR.
