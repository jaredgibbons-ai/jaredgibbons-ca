## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — SLRD Electoral Area D Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 1135-2013** (adopted October 28, 2013), with the Howe Sound East Sub-area Plan as Schedule C covering Furry Creek. https://slrd.civicweb.net/document/149848/
- **Zoning — SLRD Electoral Area D Zoning Bylaw No. 1350-2016** — Furry Creek zones include CD3 (Furry Creek Comprehensive Development 3), FCR residential zones, FCC1 (Furry Creek–Commercial 1) and FCC2 (Furry Creek Golf Course Commercial 2, dedicated to the golf course commercial use). FCR/CD3 minimum lot figures: UNVERIFIED from the bylaw text in this pass. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw-electoral-area-d2pdf
- **SSMUH — exempt, as the Province/SLRD state it:** the Province has determined that the SLRD's master-planned communities of Britannia Beach, Furry Creek and Porteau Cove are NOT subject to the additional 3–6 unit SSMUH densities, because they are not within an urban containment boundary for the purposes of the SSMUH legislation; Amendment Bylaw No. 1887-2025 removed the extra densities that had briefly been added to the Furry Creek FCR3 and CD3 zones. Do not assume suite or multiplex rights — secondary rights are whatever the base zoning and the building scheme allow. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/250129-ssmuh-area-d-amendment-bb-extension-update-rfd
- **Building schemes:** the SLRD records that the Furry Creek neighbourhood has existing statutory building schemes and covenants, which constrain what owners can build or alter — read the building scheme before planning changes; exterior changes, additions and even some landscaping may need scheme approval. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/250129-ssmuh-area-d-amendment-bb-extension-update-rfd
- **Hazards and development permits:** expanded Development Permit Areas for Howe Sound East were adopted through OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 1717-2021 (adopted May 25, 2022), covering environmental protection and natural hazards including landslide hazard areas, excavated rock and foundation stability, seismic liquefaction and fan stability, coastal hazards and creek hazards; for the creek-fan lands the guidelines address dike design to IOD standard dike criteria and a flood construction level achieved by landfill, with habitable areas below the FCL requiring justification and variance. https://getinvolved.slrd.bc.ca/sites/default/files/pdfs/planning/staff-reports/Area%20D%20DPA%20Housekeeping%204th%20Reading%20Report-FULL.pdf
