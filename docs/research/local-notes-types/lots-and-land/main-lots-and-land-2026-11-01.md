## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market on the Main corridor — stated plainly at the top, because it is honest. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering was verified on an official or developer source. Corridor land trades as fee-simple house lots on the side streets and as strata / rental / mixed-use parcels on the Main Street, Kingsway and Broadway frontages, and the land-form facts that matter are (1) the R1-1 multiplex ladder on the side streets and (2) the Broadway Plan at the north end — a plan expectation, never entitled density and never for-sale inventory.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form on the side streets

- **Side-street house lots:** heritage and character detached houses on the side streets, much of them converted or suited, sit freehold; older walk-up rental apartments run along Main Street, Kingsway and Broadway, with newer low-rise and mid-rise strata condos and purpose-built rentals near Main Street and Kingsway. The strata/freehold split by street: UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/mount-pleasant.aspx
- **Lot character from the zone, as stated:** the residential side streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m), 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. The district schedule for the Main Street commercial frontage itself: UNVERIFIED in the research. These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, R1-1 District Schedule (side streets):** minimum site areas 306 m² / 464 m² / 557 m² by form as stated in Section 2; a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. Main Street frontage parcels sit under commercial / mixed-use districts instead — the frontage district schedule is UNVERIFIED in this file's research, so confirm it parcel by parcel. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plans — Broadway Plan + Vancouver Official Development Plan:** the Broadway Plan (approved June 22, 2022; updates December 2024) covers the corridor's north end and supports buildings up to 20–30 storeys near Mount Pleasant Station; the Vancouver Plan (approved July 22, 2022) was rescinded and replaced by the Vancouver Official Development Plan on March 31, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states it:** the R1-1 schedule itself is Vancouver's multiplex frame — up to 6 units, or up to 8 rental units, on a qualifying side-street lot, plus a secondary suite and a laneway house with a single detached house, subject to the minimum site area and frontage for the form. Eligibility is parcel-specific. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — Subdivision By-law No. 5208, fees by No. 14538 (VERIFIED):** Vancouver subdivision is governed by the Subdivision By-law (No. 5208), whose Schedule A sets minimum parcel width and area standards by district and sub-area category, with the Approving Officer administering applications; its fee schedule was most recently amended for 2026 by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026). A fee-simple split must clear both the R1-1 schedule and the Subdivision By-law category for its block. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **Heritage / renter diligence at the north end:** for older Main Street rental buildings, the Broadway Plan's renter protections and tenant relocation requirements must be factored in before underwriting a redevelopment purchase, and Mount Pleasant's heritage-retention expectations affect the side-street stock the context statement documents. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/mount-pleasant-historic-context-statement.pdf
- **Floodplain diligence:** the designated floodplain includes the False Creek coastal floodplain at the corridor's north end, with Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements for parcels in it — confirm the specific parcel in the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Main corridor ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a corridor lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — APPLIES here (Vancouver only):** owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the tax is 3% of the property's assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year, and the 2025 payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — a live cost for investors holding the corridor's older rental or vacant stock. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Speculation tax + purchase prohibition beyond the above:** no Main-specific position beyond the City tax is documented in this file's research — none imported. The federal non-Canadian purchase-prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Main, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Vancouver property taxes:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot subdivision I can buy into on Main?** No verified one — the corridor is a built, densifying spine; land trades as side-street house lots and frontage parcels. No presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is "Main" an official Vancouver neighbourhood?** Not one of the 22 distinct local areas — the City lists "Main" as a recognised smaller area; the corridor runs through Mount Pleasant, Riley Park and Sunset, and Main Street splits the board's Mount Pleasant VE and VW sub-areas. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **Q: How many homes can a side-street lot hold?** Where the R1-1 schedule is met, a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units, and a house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house — minimum site areas run 306 m² to 557 m² by form. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Does the Broadway Plan mean my lot can hold a 20-storey building?** No — the plan supports buildings up to 20–30 storeys near Mount Pleasant Station, but most sites still need rezoning. Separate plan expectation from entitled density in every land pitch. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Q: Can I split a corridor house lot into two fee-simple lots?** Only if both resulting parcels clear the R1-1 schedule and the Subdivision By-law No. 5208 Schedule A category for the block, through the Approving Officer. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm
- **Q: Why is the Lee Building cash only?** Because its title is Undivided Interest under a tenants-in-common agreement — listings state it cannot be financed and there is no strata Form B regime. It is excluded from condo, townhouse and land stock in this program. (Condos sibling, §2.)
- **Q: When does the Main Street SkyTrain station open?** Mount Pleasant Station, at Main Street and Broadway, is one of six underground stations on the 5.7 km Broadway Subway, scheduled to enter service in fall 2027. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025TT0110-001048
- **Q: Will I pay the Empty Homes Tax on a vacant corridor purchase?** Vancouver's tax is 3% of assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant, with an annual declaration required — run it into holding costs and confirm the seller's declaration status. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Main corridor lot?** By exact address only — the corridor crosses at least three elementary catchments in two local areas and no completed locator run is documented here, so run every address in the VSB locator before advertising school appeal. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
