## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** A single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules; the Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, and farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". Any page attributing a lettered Ladner schedule to Bylaw 8400 is wrong. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged not settled.** City zone sheets cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, parcel by parcel; an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates. Neither is asserted as settled here — the ruling is Jared's. Site-specific CD zones also exist on the ground (Keira Gardens' listing records CD376), so the parcel's own zone is always the read. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it.** Delta adopted its SSMUH zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024, allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies; the RS1 zone also lists a secondary suite as an accessory use. SSMUH is added-unit potential on an existing lot, not a subdivision right — the zone minimums in §2 still govern any actual subdivision. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing requirements.** A City of Delta council report (January 24, 2024) presents the Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 to repeal and replace Subdivision and Development Standards Bylaw No. 7162, 2015, made under s. 506 of the Local Government Act to regulate and require works and services on subdivision — highways, water, sanitary sewers, drainage and other services and utilities. Final adoption status of Bylaw 8288 and the servicing standard for a specific Hawthorne parcel: UNCONFIRMED in this pass — confirm with the City before pricing a subdivision. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Streamside rule.** All properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, which can limit building and subdivision work on the lot. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Dyke / flood diligence.** Ladner is low land protected by the City's dike, seawall and drainage system (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations, ~538 km of drainage infrastructure). Confirm flood construction requirements for the specific lot with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **ALR rules (farm-belt parcels; in-sub-area ALR UNVERIFIED, §2).** ALR residences cannot be stratified — strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)) — and subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, as the ALC states it. ALC residence caps: principal residence 500 m² or less total floor area; a secondary suite only within the principal residence; one additional residence of 90 m² or less (parcels 40 ha or less) or 186 m² or less (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — three tiers, never a flat figure.** Gross annual farm income required: $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on an operation under 0.8 ha. Applications are due by October 31 for the next taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an error. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **GST on vacant land.** CRA's position, stated only at the level its publication supports: most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (for example, land kept for personal use), but a sale is taxable where the land was subdivided into more than two parts, and sales by developers/businesses are generally taxable supplies. A resale house lot in Hawthorne will usually sit on the exempt side; a newly subdivided lot sold by a developer usually will not — the specific sale's treatment is a facts question, finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **GST on the new housing that replaces land here.** New homes (Wildwood presale homes, a new build on a Hawthorne lot) carry GST; resales do not. The first-time home buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — it applies to new housing, NOT to bare land itself. The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax on land.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-time buyers' exemption: no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, phasing to $1,150,000 — both are home exemptions, and their application to a bare-land purchase is facts-specific: UNCONFIRMED for bare land in this file. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Down-payment programs.** The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs and the First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit, for a qualifying home; a lot-then-build buyer should confirm eligibility timing with CRA before relying on either against the land purchase alone. CMHC homeowner premiums run 0.6%–4.5% of the loan across its loan-to-value bands. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA) — the Wildwood frame.** Where a presale exists, the developer must deliver a disclosure statement; s. 21 gives the buyer 7 days from the later of signing or acknowledging that statement to rescind, and deposits are held in trust. That is the protection set on the Wildwood homes — it does not create any lot offering. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Speculation and vacancy taxes — Delta's actual position.** The City of Delta IS listed by the Province as a designated taxable area for the BC Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so owners of residential-class property in Delta must complete the annual declaration. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is a City of Vancouver tax ONLY and does not apply in Delta. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition — the land point.** CMHC states the prohibition (to January 1, 2027, in census metropolitan areas including Metro Vancouver) covers buildings of three or fewer dwelling units, and that effective March 27, 2023 it does NOT apply to vacant land — vacant land zoned for residential and mixed use can be purchased by non-Canadians and used for any purpose, including residential development. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/media-newsroom/news-releases/2023/amendments-prohibition-purchase-residential-property-non-canadians-regulations
- **Strata block — background for any strata lot a Hawthorne search spills into.** Form B (SPA s. 59) within 7 days of request, disclosing fees, levies, CRF balance and insurance summary; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). No bare-land strata is verified in Hawthorne (§2), so this block governs the sub-area's conventional strata stock, not a land form here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Delta property taxes and holding cost.** Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **No SnapStats figure exists for this area.** The SnapStats FVR September 2026 report has no Ladner section and no Hawthorne row — Delta is NOT a SnapStats community for South Delta, and North Delta figures were not substituted. Never cite a SnapStats figure for this area. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/hawthorne-guide.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any bare land for sale in Hawthorne?** No verified bare-land development, bare-land strata or lot presale — land trades as improved fee-simple house lots on a built-out grid. (§2 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Hawthorne lot?** Only if the parcel's zone allows it — subdivision minimums run from RS7 at 335 m² and RS6 at 390 m² up to RS5 at 550 m² (460 m² outside the SFR designation), read parcel by parcel — and then the City's subdivision and servicing standards apply. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Q: What did the Wildwood site teach about assembled land here?** That a Central Avenue assembly becomes a townhome project, not a lot sale: Wildwood Ladner is 40 presale townhomes by Shato Holdings at 4900 Central Avenue, counted in the townhomes file — a buyer wanting land should expect to compete with that end use, and a buyer of a Wildwood home is under REDMA, not buying a lot. https://wildwoodladner.com/
- **Q: Is a lot here on municipal water and sewer, or well and septic?** Urban Ladner is within the City's water, sanitary sewer and drainage systems; no source in this program documents well/septic inside Hawthorne. Confirm the specific parcel's services and connection charges with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: What can I add to a lot without rezoning?** Delta's SSMUH bylaws (8394 and 8395, June 24, 2024) allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — confirm the parcel's zone first, and check the 30 m streamside rule before designing a coach house. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q: Does GST apply when I buy a lot?** Per CRA: an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt; land subdivided into more than two parts and developer/business sales are generally taxable. A newly created lot is exactly the case to check before writing the offer. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What are the holding costs while I hold a lot for a future build?** Delta property taxes on the City's calendar (2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026), plus insurance and any City utility/drainage charges as billed — parcel-specific figures UNCONFIRMED. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Q: Is flood risk a real diligence item in Hawthorne?** Yes — Ladner is low land behind the City's dike and drainage system (over 63 km of flood protection, 31 pump stations), and any lot within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch is in the Streamside Protection DPA. Confirm flood construction requirements lot by lot with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Could a Hawthorne lot be ALR?** Inside Hawthorne, ALR is UNVERIFIED and none is confirmed. If a parcel were ALR, the consequences would be the §3 block: no stratification, ALC approval for subdivision, and the ALC residence caps (500 m² principal; 90 m² / 186 m² additional). (§2–§3 above)
- **Q: How long would a subdivision or rebuild take?** UNCONFIRMED — no official source in this program publishes Delta approval or build timelines for a Hawthorne lot; the City's development department is the only authoritative source, and any quoted timeline is an estimate.
