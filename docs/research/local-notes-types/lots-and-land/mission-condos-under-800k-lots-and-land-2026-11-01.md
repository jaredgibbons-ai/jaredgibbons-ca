- **Worked PTT examples — inputs stated, tier math shown:** tiers are 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Higher anchors, shown only to mark the tiers: $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning, OCP and added units:** OCP **Bylaw 6405-2026 (adopted April 21, 2026** — any source stating "June 1, 2026" is WRONG; this slug's research does not document that wrong date on this page, so it is carried as the standing correction, not asserted as a defect of this page, §5); zoning under **Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020**, with parcel-level minimum lot sizes in the bylaw itself (rural RU16 at a 1.6 ha minimum in City notices). The City's SSMUH page states that zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted, and the City's planning pages set out how secondary suites and detached accessory units are handled by zone. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning and https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision in Mission runs through the City under the OCP and Zoning Bylaw, with engineering servicing requirements applied under the City's **Development and Subdivision Control Bylaw No. 5650-2017 (DSCB), as amended** — that number is stated here only because a City of Mission source supports it (a City rezoning information package citing the DSCB by name and number, found in this pass); this slug's research file itself carries no servicing number, and no other city's number is borrowed. Requirements for a specific parcel are confirmed with the City. https://www.mission.ca/sites/default/files/2024-03/Information%20Package%20-%20Notice%20of%20Rezoning%20Bylaw%20-%20R18-023.pdf
- **ALR block — full form (Mission's rural-edge parcels):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6; Strata Property Act, s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; one additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Farm class (BC Assessment) in full three-tier form: $2,500 of gross farm income at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR; none is documented on this band's page in its research, so no page defect is asserted on that point. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC strata block — applies to any strata parcel in the city, including a bare-land strata lot:** Form B (SPA s.59), within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** tiers and worked examples are in §2. First-time buyers' and newly built home exemptions are HOME exemptions — a bare lot's eligibility is UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. Anything finer for a specific band parcel is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA) rebates 100% of GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — new housing, **NOT bare land**. The GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
