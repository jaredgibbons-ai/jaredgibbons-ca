## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Eastern Hillsides is a hillside neighbourhood in southeast Chilliwack and a Neighbourhood Plan area, Schedule B4 of the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan (Bylaw 2025, No. 5500). The plan was adopted in 2012, amended in 2017 to clarify development-approval requirements protecting future development from rock fall, landslide or earthflow with unstable conditions, and updated a third time in 2025 as part of the OCP update. https://www.chilliwack.com/ocp and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1962
- Boundaries: west edge the Ryder Lake uplands, south edge Lookout Ridge and Elk Mountain, east edge the Cascade foothills / Fraser Valley Regional District boundary, north edge the Trans-Canada Highway (Highway 1) and valley-floor farmland. Nearby: Ryder Lake west, Promontory and Sardis northwest across the valley floor, Rosedale / Bridal Falls east along Highway 1, Chilliwack Proper East at the northwest foot. Marble Hill is a named slope area inside the plan area. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: Eastern Hillsides has NO verified named bare-land subdivision, NO bare-land strata, and NO presale LOT offering — and no approved major project or presale of any kind was verified inside the plan area on a City or developer source. Land here is rural hillside lot fabric: fee-simple detached lots whose size is set by zone AND slope, running from small-lot cells up to 8-hectare rural hillside parcels, with geotechnical hazard as the defining diligence. Verified named land developments counted here: 0.**

- **Fee-simple lots are the default form.** The plan describes early development as large semi-serviced lots with single-family homes, with small-lot zoning in later cells, organized into development cells with unit allocations; the established stock described in the plan is freehold detached housing on individual lots. An area-wide strata/freehold split: UNVERIFIED. A bare lot here is the buyer's own title — no strata corporation, no strata fee, no Form B — with zoning, the OCP, hazard rules and any geotechnical covenants on title governing instead. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- **Lot character, only as the research states it (Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 minimums — not measured sizes):** SHR (Suburban Hillside Residential) minimum lot area is set by the bylaw's slope table and ranges from 360 m² to 990 m², minimum width 12 m, depth 20 m; R3 (Small Lot Residential) 360 m², width 12 m interior / 13.5 m corner; HR (Hillside Residential) 0.2 ha with a community water system and 0.4 ha in all other cases, width 25 m; RH (Rural Hillside) 8 ha, width 25 m. The zone and slope applying to any specific parcel: UNVERIFIED — confirm per property. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

The HR zone's minimum doubling (0.2 ha with a community water system, 0.4 ha otherwise) is the bylaw's own signal that community-water status changes what a lot can be. Whether a specific lot is on community water/sewer or on well/septic: UNCONFIRMED in the research — confirm with the City and on title, parcel by parcel. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Chilliwack 2050, Bylaw No. 5500; Eastern Hillsides = Schedule B4.** The 2050 update replaced the plan's land-use designations with city-wide OCP designations, while the plan's development cells and unit allocations continue to regulate how many units may be developed in the plan area. Under the previous OCP (Bylaw 2014, No. 4025) the Eastern Hillsides Comprehensive Development Plan formed part of the bylaw and Development Permit Area 2 covered the hillside/upland area — where older and newer documents differ, the 2050 OCP and the 2025-updated Neighbourhood Plan govern. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/common/downloads/publication.pdf
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** — the SHR / R3 / HR / RH minimums in §2. Price lot potential from the zone AND the slope table, not the zone alone: the SHR minimum alone swings from 360 m² to 990 m² depending on slope, and HR doubles from 0.2 ha to 0.4 ha without a community water system. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH as the City states it:** Chilliwack's SSMUH amendments were adopted as **Bylaw 5395 on June 18, 2024**, bringing the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules into the zoning bylaw (program standing frame for Chilliwack, Nov 1, 2026). The Province granted Chilliwack an extension for part of the Proper neighbourhood and surrounding Hazel Park, with a new compliance date of December 31, 2030 — that extension area is not Eastern Hillsides, but it shows the rollout is not uniform city-wide. Unit potential on a specific hillside lot is read off Bylaw 5000 as amended, per property. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision is the process of changing or creating new lot boundaries, must be approved by the City's Approving Officer, and all subdivisions are subject to the Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055; creating a bare land strata development is itself a subdivision application type. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=653
- **Hillside Development Permit Area (Schedule D-5):** a development permit is required for works including subdivision of land with 20% or greater slopes — slope measurement is a closing-condition item on hillside lots, not an afterthought. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- **ALR rules, where ALR ground is met (north-edge parcels; parcel status UNVERIFIED, §2):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified — strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal residence 500 m² or less total floor area; a secondary suite only within the principal residence; one additional residence of 90 m² or less (parcels 40 ha or less) or 186 m² or less (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — three tiers, never a flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on an operation under 0.8 ha, with applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Strata block — background if a strata lot is ever produced here:** Form B (SPA s. 59) within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page, disclosing fees, levies, CRF balance and insurance summary; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions). No bare-land strata is verified in Eastern Hillsides (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-time buyers' exemption: no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — both are home exemptions, and their application to a bare-land purchase is facts-specific: UNCONFIRMED for bare land in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — stated only at the level the CRA source supports:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. The treatment of any specific hillside parcel is UNCONFIRMED until the vendor's status and the parcel's history are established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — it applies to new housing, NOT to bare land itself. The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account: $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit — both applied at the home purchase. CMHC homeowner premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Chilliwack property taxes (city ground):** a 5% late-payment penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026, per the City's property tax FAQ — a mid-year land buyer inherits whichever step has landed on an unpaid balance. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any bare land for sale in Eastern Hillsides?** Not as a product — no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified. Land trades as individual fee-simple hillside lots, from small-lot cells (R3/SHR minimums from 360 m²) up to rural hillside parcels (RH minimum 8 ha). (§2 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a hillside lot?** Only if zone, slope and servicing all allow it: the SHR minimum runs 360–990 m² by the slope table, HR is 0.2 ha with community water and 0.4 ha without, subdivision needs the Approving Officer under the Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055, and subdivision of land with 20% or greater slopes needs a development permit under the Schedule D-5 Hillside DPA. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=653 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- **Q: What is the Marble Hill risk, in plain terms?** The plan identifies geotechnical hazard areas, the 2017 amendments exist specifically because of rock fall, landslide and earthflow risk, the lower and upper Marble Hill slide areas are identified as undevelopable lands, and an expanded Marble Hill development moratorium was directed in 2017 — get the property's geotechnical reports and any covenants on title before pricing or removing subjects. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1962
- **Q: Is a hillside lot serviced, or will I need a well and septic?** It varies — the plan describes early development as "large semi-serviced lots," and the HR zone minimum doubles without a community water system. No source in the research confirms the servicing of any single lot: UNCONFIRMED parcel by parcel — confirm with the City before offering. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- **Q: What extra units can go on a lot?** Chilliwack's SSMUH amendments (Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024) brought the provincial small-scale multi-unit rules into the zoning bylaw; what a specific hillside lot can hold is read off Bylaw 5000 as amended for that parcel's zone and slope — and hazard and DPA rules can limit what zoning alone suggests. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **Q: Could a lot here be ALR?** The plan area is hillside above the valley floor with farmland along its north edge; parcel-level ALR status is UNVERIFIED for any single lot. Check the ALC map — if the parcel is ALR, subdivision needs ALC approval, residences cannot be stratified, and the ALC caps apply (principal 500 m²; suite inside the principal only; additional residence 90 m² / 186 m² by the 40 ha line). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Does GST apply when I buy a lot?** Per CRA GI-003: an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt; a sale in the course of a business or of a parcel subdivided into more than two parts can be taxable, and developer/business sales of newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Have the contract state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What will it cost to hold the land?** City of Chilliwack property taxes on the City's calendar (5% penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, a further 5% after August 4, 2026), plus insurance and any covenant or servicing obligations on title. There is no strata fee on a fee-simple lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Q: Which schools would a home on this lot feed into?** There is no school inside the plan area, so every child travels. The one recorded locator sample (51314 Rowanna Crescent) returns East Chilliwack Elementary → Rosedale Traditional Community School → Chilliwack Secondary; any other address needs its own SD33 locator run — do not carry the sample across the hillsides. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q: How long would a subdivision or build take here?** UNCONFIRMED — no official source in this program publishes Chilliwack approval or build timelines for a hillside lot; the sequence (geotechnical review, DPA where slopes reach 20%, Approving Officer review, servicing) is what to price time on, not a promised date. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=653

- **Slope is a price variable here, not a footnote.** The same SHR zone carries a 360 m² minimum on gentle ground and a 990 m² minimum on steep ground — two lots with the same zone and the same area can have completely different subdivision value once the slope table and the 20%-slope development-permit trigger are applied. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
