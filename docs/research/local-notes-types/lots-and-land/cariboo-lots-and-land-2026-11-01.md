## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815), adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026** — never cite the repealed 1965 Bylaw No. 4742 as current. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 SSMUH District in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025,** replacing the 1998 OCP and taking precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict. The Lochdale Urban Village Community Plan (Lougheed-side urban village north-west of Cariboo) was adopted July 25, 2022 and sits under the 2050 OCP's precedence rule. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/official-community-plan and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- **Subdivision + servicing:** Burnaby's servicing-bylaw BASE number is HELD UNCONFIRMED in this program (renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024) — no base number is asserted here. The documented process: new homes and SSMUH projects require Engineering pre-application approval through My Permits Portal before a building permit application, and where offsite servicing requirements are identified in the Pre-Approval Report, a cash-in-lieu contribution to City infrastructure is required, calculated on Engineering unit rates. Confirm requirements with the City for the parcel. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/development-permits-construction/new-home-construction
- **Watercourse diligence:** the Cariboo and Brunette River Conservation Lands and the Stoney Creek Park and Trail System are dedicated by City park bylaw on the area's north side — for creek-side lots, check watercourse setbacks early; the setback rules travel with the creek, not the lot line. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- **Bare-land strata rules (applies to any bare-land strata lot, including a future stratified R1 multi-unit lot):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Cariboo ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Cariboo lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Cariboo-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Cariboo, and the position is not conformed from other files.

- **Q: Why does Cariboo show no detached sales?** Because there was nothing to sell: the September 2026 row is 0 detached inventory and 0 detached solds (ratio not applicable), while attached stock carried 23 listings and 1 sale (4%). Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report (research file).
- **Q: Can a Cariboo house lot add units — and can they be strata?** Under the R1 SSMUH District: 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop — and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified, which is how new small strata product is created here. Confirm the parcel's district and transit distance with the City. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: What is a bare-land strata lot, if a new Cariboo project offers one?** A strata lot that is land with the home on it: you own the strata lot, pay strata fees, are bound by the bylaws and diligence through the Form B, exactly as with any strata — the lot form differs, the tenure does not. No Cariboo plan is verified in this file; read the specific project's strata plan and Form B. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Are the Manchester Drive buildings a way to buy land here?** No — Barclay Woods, Brookside Court, Brookside Park and Strathmore Towers are completed strata complexes counted in the condos sibling; a purchase there is a strata unit, with Form B diligence, not a lot. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What checks does a creek-side lot need?** Watercourse-setback checks, early — Stoney Creek and the Brunette conservation lands are dedicated park by bylaw on the north side, and no floodplain designation for the residential streets was confirmed, so the position is parcel-specific. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- **Q: Is the lot serviced?** The City's utility wording puts established single-family homes on municipal water (2026 charge $652.33) with sewer as a parcel tax — confirm servicing for the specific parcel with the City before writing. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Cariboo lot?** By exact address only — Armstrong Elementary, Lyndhurst Elementary and Cariboo Hill Secondary sit in or beside the area, but no catchment for any address in this slug is verified in this file; run the address in the SD41 locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q: What is happening at Cameron?** A $267 million replacement Community Centre and Library under construction at the Lougheed side, with the temporary centre and library inside The City of Lougheed — a real amenity story with a construction-period gap; it is a civic project, not lot supply. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2024-07-23/council-approves-award-contract-advance-construction-replacement-cameron
