### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **House lots:** single-detached houses under Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 RS1/RS2 zones (Section 8.1) on the residential streets, with secondary suites permitted under Section 5.4 (a suite must be at least 33.0 m² and no more than the lesser of 40% of the dwelling's floor area or 110 m²) and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL added under provincial Bill 44 legislation. Lot character beyond those zone rules: a sourced area-wide typical lot-size range is UNVERIFIED in the research, and none is stated here. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Village-core strata ground:** strata housing concentrates at the village core in low-rise mixed-use buildings under Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU32) – Steveston Village and Steveston Commercial (CS3) zoning — built strata form on village parcels, not bare-land sales. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Heritage/dike-constrained parcels:** lots inside the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area need a Heritage Alteration Permit for exterior changes, and waterfront/dyke-adjacent lots are governed by Bylaw 8204 dike setbacks and flood construction levels (Section 3) — both constraints are priced into the land, parcel by parcel. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500:** houses sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with secondary suite rules in Section 5.4 (at least 33.0 m²; at most the lesser of 40% of the dwelling's floor area or 110 m²) and SSMUH zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL added under Bill 44; village commercial and mixed-use zones named by the City include Steveston Commercial (CS3) and Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU32) – Steveston Village. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **OCP / Area Plan — Steveston Area Plan, Schedule 2.4 of the 2041 OCP (Bylaw 9000; Area Plan Bylaw 7100):** guides the whole Steveston planning area, including the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area and its Heritage Alteration Permit requirement for properties inside it. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/steveston566.pdf and https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** the City also allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot under its Bill 44 housing rules, on top of the RS1/RS2 suite permission above. Confirm the parcel's zone and servicing before pricing in added units. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Subdivision + servicing — Richmond Bylaws 8751 / 8752 (VERIFIED frame, city frame carried from the Richmond city file):** subdivision and servicing requirements for Steveston land run under the City of Richmond's Bylaw 8751 and Bylaw 8752. Parcel-level works, frontage and servicing conditions are confirmed with the City for the specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED beyond the bylaw frame stated here.
- **Flood construction — Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204:** sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements in Richmond; Steveston fronts the South Arm behind the perimeter dike system (1:500 design; dikes being raised from 3.5 m to 4.7 m), and the Steveston Island Dike project addresses this waterfront specifically. Confirm dike setbacks and the flood construction level before pricing in a rebuild or addition on any waterfront or dyke-adjacent lot. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf and https://www.letstalkrichmond.ca/StevestonIslandDike
- **Bare-land strata block (for the form — no Steveston bare-land strata plan is verified):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property taxes (city-wide Richmond rule):** property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. First-Time Home Buyers' exemption: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, maximum saving $8,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, out at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not bare land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50% per the condos sibling. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Steveston-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply in Richmond. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this ground is UNCONFIRMED here — the research cites no prohibition source for this area, and the position is not conformed from other files. ADEU charges (West Cambie / Alexandra ground, in the Richmond city file) do not apply to Steveston land and are not imported here.
