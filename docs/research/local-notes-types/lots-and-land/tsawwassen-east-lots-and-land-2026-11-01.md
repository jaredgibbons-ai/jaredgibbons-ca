## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — consolidated, with NO lettered schedules and no "Schedule A" land-use plan; Development Permit Areas live in the OCP's Appendix B.** The current OCP consolidated the former area plans into a single land use plan and does not use a "Single Family Residential" designation; that designation belongs to the former Tsawwassen Area Plan (Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, superseded), as City report LU009394 framed it for 1728 Beach Grove Road. Any "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" attribution is an ERROR wherever it is met. Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (City pages cite No. 7600, 2017, against an internal title reading "8400, 2025"; this slug's research cites "Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, section 6.2.3" for the waterfront setback — carried here as the research states it, with the contest flagged). RD2 (per §2): 4,000 m² subdivision minimum, the waterfront-lot zone for Beach Grove. Zoning is parcel by parcel on the City maps. (Adaptation source: batch-1/tsawwassen-east-guide.md, via this slug's research.)
- **Waterfront setback:** Zoning Bylaw s. 6.2.3 sets a waterfront setback for lots fronting on Boundary Bay along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue, under which a seawall may encroach into the minimum front setback; the bylaw states no Beach Grove flood construction level — the flood-level question goes to the permit area and the City (§3 SD3), not the zoning text. (Adaptation source: batch-1/tsawwassen-east-guide.md, via this slug's research.)
- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands):** no building with the underside of a wooden floor system or the top of a concrete slab for habitable areas lower than 2.9 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum; minimum land surface elevation around construction 2.3 metres GSC datum; no building within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. Whether a given lot falls inside SD3 needs a DeltaMap check for that lot: UNVERIFIED for any individual lot in this file. Hazard DPA SD2 (English Bluff escarpment) is a west-side overlay, not an east-side one. (Adaptation source: batch-1/tsawwassen-east-guide.md, via this slug's research.)
- **Flood and foreshore frame:** the east side faces Boundary Bay behind the Boundary Bay dike; Delta operates over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure, and is the diking authority. The Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; tenure is by lease or licence only via FrontCounter BC, and private moorage is held under General Permission or Specific Permission — a "private dock" is not land the seller owns. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024** — up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies. Added units are not subdivision and do not waive SD3 or any permit area. **Subdivision/servicing — DSSS Bylaw No. 8288, 2024, cited with its limit stated:** cited in this program from a civicweb / January 2024 council report — final adoption and parcel-level standards UNCONFIRMED in this file; the guide land home's governing-terms assertion of 8288 is noted as a conflict, never asserted here. Confirm with the City for the specific parcel. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **ALR block (farm-edge parcels, and the 6 Avenue claim's frame):** ALR subdivision requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class runs on the full three-tier test ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; apply by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (applies to Beachcomber Cottages lots in the land home, and any strata lot here):** Form B (SPA s. 59) within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢/page, with rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. Fee-simple contrast: a fee-simple Beach Grove lot carries none of this — no strata, no Form B, no fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Presales (REDMA) — live in Southlands:** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it; deposits are held in trust. Assignment terms per collection: UNCONFIRMED. (Land home: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/tsawwassen-east-guide-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **Property transfer tax on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $700,000 → $12,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. The Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000) can apply to a never-occupied new HOME in Southlands, not to bare land alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST — land and new homes, CRA GI-003 level only:** an individual's personal-use vacant-land sale is generally exempt; a business sale or a parcel subdivided into more than two parts can be taxable; anything finer UNCONFIRMED. On a new Southlands HOME, GST applies with the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031) — the rebate applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — and the New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000) underneath it. HBP $60,000; FHSA $8,000/year to $40,000. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
