### The forms, at city level only

- **Fee-simple lots — the default form:** the city's house districts are fee-simple lot fabric, with infill now framed by the June 2026 OCP and Zoning amendments (Section 3): up to six units of infill housing on single detached and duplex properties across most of the city under zones RGO-A (up to four units) and RGO-B (up to six units), without a site-specific rezoning. Lot sizes and dimensions are stated ONLY as each sub-area research file states them — no city-wide lot size, lot count or lot price is stated in this slug's research, and none is stated here: UNVERIFIED at city level. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- **Townhouse districts — corrected, as the standing correction governs:** New Westminster's townhouse zoning districts in Zoning Bylaw No. 6680 (2001) are RT-2, RT-2A, RT-2B, RT-2C and RT-3, RT-3A, RT-3B ONLY. RT-A and RT-B do not exist in that bylaw; any sub-area document using them is in error (see FILE STATUS). The 836.10 m² figure sometimes attached to those districts is an RM apartment-district density threshold, not an RT or lot figure.
- **Bare-land strata — its own form:** where a bare-land strata plan exists on New Westminster ground, the lot IS strata tenure — Form B / strata fees / bylaws apply to the lot itself (Section 3 block). No bare-land strata plan is named or counted in this city file; any verified plan lives in its sub-area land file.
- **Suites as land value, with the City's limits:** a secondary suite is a separate dwelling unit within a house, accessory to a single detached dwelling or duplex and contained within the same structure; suites cannot be strata-titled, sold or used for short-term rentals, and all properties with suites are subject to annual secondary suite water and sewer charges. Suite size cap and owner-occupancy rule: UNVERIFIED — neither is stated in the City guide read in this pass. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Secondary_Suite_Guide_August_2025.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 6680 (2001):** the city's zoning frame, including the townhouse districts RT-2–RT-2C + RT-3/3A/3B ONLY (RT-A/RT-B do not exist — standing correction, FILE STATUS) and the June 2026 infill zones RGO-A / RGO-B below. The 836.10 m² figure is an RM apartment-district density threshold, not an RT figure. (Townhomes sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/new-westminster-homes-for-sale-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **OCP — "Our City 2041", Bylaw No. 7925 (2017):** the city's long-term land-use frame, as amended — including the June 2026 amendments in the next bullet, adopted while the Interim Development Review Framework governs application review. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** in June 2026 Council adopted amendments to the OCP and Zoning Bylaw to permit up to six units of infill housing (secondary suites, laneway and carriage houses, triplex, quadplex and other multiplex forms) on single detached and duplex properties across most of the city, under zones RGO-A (up to four units) and RGO-B (up to six units), removing the need for a site-specific rezoning. The City's earlier record: most mainland residential properties already permitted a house plus a secondary suite and an accessory dwelling unit, and Bylaw No. 8453, 2024 rezoned the roughly 160 "restricted zone" duplex properties to a new RD-1 district permitting rental secondary suites; Queensborough SSMUH compliance was extended by the Province to a new compliance date of May 4, 2029. Verify the specific property's zone before pricing either frame. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Attachment_C2___Small_Scale_Multi_Unit_Housing_Legislation_and_Implementation_Summary.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — Bylaw 7142, 2007 (VERIFIED frame):** New Westminster subdivision and servicing requirements run under Bylaw 7142, 2007. Parcel-level works and servicing conditions are confirmed with the City for the specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED beyond the bylaw frame stated here.
- **FLOODPLAIN LEDGER — per sub-area, never blended:** Queensborough sits in Zone 1; the Quay and Downtown NW sit in Zone 3; Brunette sits in Zone 4; the North Arm sits in Zone 2; Fraserview NW states its own precise position in its own land file (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/fraserview-nw-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md). A floodplain zone is a sub-area diligence item — no city-wide floodplain statement is made, and no zone is averaged across sub-areas. (Sub-area land files, Section 2.)
- **Secondary suites:** suites must meet the Zoning Bylaw, Building Code and Tree Protection and Regulation Bylaw, cannot be strata-titled, sold or used for short-term rentals, and carry annual suite water and sewer charges. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Secondary_Suite_Guide_August_2025.pdf
- **Bare-land strata block (for the form, wherever a New Westminster bare-land strata plan is met):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property taxes:** City of New Westminster property taxes for 2026 were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current taxes remaining, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants, on Friday July 3, 2026, and a second 5% penalty applies to any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. The City held no 2026 tax sale — all delinquent property taxes had been collected. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php and https://www.newwestcity.ca/2026/09/24/2026-no-property-tax-sale.php
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. First-Time Home Buyers' exemption: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, maximum saving $8,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, out at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not bare land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50% per the condos sibling. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no New Westminster-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in this slug's research — none imported. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply in New Westminster. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this ground is UNCONFIRMED here — the research cites no prohibition source for this area, and the position is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot anywhere in New Westminster from this page?** This city file carries no lot roster — land is documented in the sub-area lots-and-land files cross-referenced in Section 2. Start from the sub-area file for the ground you want, then confirm the parcel's zone, floodplain zone and servicing. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How many homes can one New Westminster lot hold?** Since June 2026, up to six units of infill housing — suites, laneway and carriage houses, triplex, quadplex and other multiplex forms — on single detached and duplex properties across most of the city, under RGO-A (up to four units) and RGO-B (up to six units), without a site-specific rezoning. Queensborough's SSMUH compliance runs on a separate provincial timeline to May 4, 2029. Verify the property's zone first. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- **Q: What are the RT townhouse districts I keep seeing?** RT-2, RT-2A, RT-2B, RT-2C and RT-3, RT-3A, RT-3B in Zoning Bylaw No. 6680 (2001) — there are no RT-A or RT-B districts; documents using them are in error, and 836.10 m² is an RM apartment threshold, not an RT figure. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Which floodplain zone is my lot in?** It depends on the sub-area and they are never blended: Queensborough Zone 1, North Arm Zone 2, Quay + Downtown NW Zone 3, Brunette Zone 4, and Fraserview NW states its own precise position in its own file. Confirm the parcel's zone in its sub-area land file. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Can a New Westminster secondary suite be sold separately or used as a short-term rental?** No — the City's guide states suites cannot be strata-titled, sold or used for short-term rentals, and properties with suites pay annual suite water and sewer charges. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Secondary_Suite_Guide_August_2025.pdf
- **Q: What governs subdividing a New Westminster lot?** Subdivision and servicing run under Bylaw 7142, 2007, on top of the zoning above; parcel-level conditions are confirmed with the City. (Section 3.)
- **Q: How did New Westminster trade in September 2026?** Detached sold 9 of 164 at a $1,365,000 community median, while "Condos & Townhomes" combined sold 67 of 444 at $560,000 — the attached median's lowest month in the 13-month period. Land has no SnapStats category; medians are community-level only. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report.)
- **Q: Will GST apply to a newly subdivided lot bought from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA GI-003, unlike an individual's personal-use resale. The FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: How much property transfer tax on a $1.2M New Westminster lot?** $22,000 on the general scale (1% / 2% / 3% tiers). The FTHB exemption maxes at an $8,000 saving; the Newly Built Home Exemption is for a new home, not bare land. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve my address?** SD40, address-based. Ministry-confirmed schools in the research include Lord Kelvin Elementary (1010 Hamilton St), Connaught Heights Elementary (2201 London St), Queen Elizabeth Elementary (921 Salter St), Queensborough Middle School (833 Salter St) and New Westminster Secondary (820 6th St) — but catchment for a specific address is UNVERIFIED; confirm the exact address with the district. (Section 1.)
