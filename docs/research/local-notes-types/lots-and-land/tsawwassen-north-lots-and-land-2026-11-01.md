## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Tsawwassen North" is a Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name, not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name, and the City publishes no boundary for it; it is the north side of Tsawwassen along Highway 17 and 52 Street toward Ladner, under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — on the City side only (below). Paragon sub-area code: UNVERIFIED. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/215646/ and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **The defining boundary is jurisdictional:** the north side sits beside Tsawwassen First Nation Lands. Tsawwassen First Nation is a self-governing First Nation with jurisdiction over land management on Tsawwassen Lands — over 724 hectares transferred under the Tsawwassen First Nation Final Agreement — so **TFN land follows TFN law, not City of Delta bylaws**, and City zoning does not apply on Tsawwassen Lands. That boundary decides whether a parcel in this file is City fee-simple/strata land or TFN leasehold land, and the two are never mixed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- Main roads: Highway 17 (to the BC Ferries Tsawwassen terminal and to Highway 17A), 52 Street, 56 Street and Canoe Pass Way; Highway 17A leads to Highway 99 and the George Massey Tunnel. Transit: route 609 runs from South Delta Exchange via 56 Street, 12 Avenue, 52 Street and Canoe Pass Way, through Tsawwassen First Nation, to Ladner Exchange; route 601 serves 56 Street and South Delta Exchange toward Bridgeport Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Nearby areas: Tsawwassen Central to the south, Ladner across the farm belt to the north, Tsawwassen First Nation Lands to the west and north. Port Guichon is in LADNER, not Tsawwassen — it is not part of this sub-area. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/tsawwassen-north.md)
- Schools — the north side's trap: for the sample address 5000 Canoe Pass Way, the SD37 School Locator result is **Ladner Elementary (K–7), 5016 44 Ave., and Delta Secondary (8–12), 4615 51 St** — Ladner catchments, not Tsawwassen ones. Every address must be checked in the locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Market stats: the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report contains no South Delta community section — South Delta has NO SnapStats coverage — so no land or housing figure is cited from SnapStats in this file. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/tsawwassen-north.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Jurisdiction first — the rule that decides everything here:** City of Delta zoning and BC fee-simple/freehold-strata law govern the City side. **On Tsawwassen Lands, TFN land follows TFN law, not City of Delta bylaws** — Tsawwassen First Nation is self-governing with jurisdiction over land management on Tsawwassen Lands (724+ hectares under the Final Agreement), and the residential product there is held under 99-year prepaid leases (Boardwalk) or leasehold prepaid-strata plans (Seaside). Establish which side of the boundary a parcel sits on, and which tenure document is actually being bought, before pricing anything. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/boardwalk6-by-aquilini-townhomes/
- **OCP (City side): Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — consolidated, NO lettered schedules.** The Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; **"Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" is an ERROR wherever met.** Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". The OCP does not govern Tsawwassen Lands. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted (City side)** (sheets cite No. 7600, 2017; an internal title reads "8400, 2025"). Residential ladder: RS4 610 m², RS5 550/460 m², RS6 390 m², RS7 335 m², RD1 836 m², RD4 504 m² subdivision minimums, parcel by parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **SSMUH (City side only): Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024** — four units on all residential properties in Delta; six within 400 m of frequent transit along Scott Road (North Delta). Added units are not subdivision, and SSMUH does not apply on Tsawwassen Lands. **Subdivision/servicing (City side):** Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 (repealing No. 7162, 2015), under LGA s. 506; parcel-specific requirements UNCONFIRMED beyond that. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **ALR rules (farm-belt parcels):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, as the ALC states it, including one-residence-per-parcel limits relevant to large lots at the farm edge. ALC caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). **Farm class — three-tier, in full:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Flood (City side):** farm and ALR land lies between Tsawwassen and Ladner, and Delta is the diking authority (over 63 km of flood protection, 31 pump stations, ~538 km of drainage). A flood construction level for any specific Springs-area or farm-edge lot: UNCONFIRMED — check DeltaMap and title. Flood/drainage arrangements on a specific Tsawwassen Lands parcel are a TFN-jurisdiction question for that parcel: UNCONFIRMED here. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Strata block (City-side strata, and any future 4800 Springs strata):** Form B (SPA s. 59) — fees, amounts owed, levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules/budget/depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page. Depreciation reports every 5 years for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024. CRF minimum 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). **Whether and how each provincial strata rule interacts with a specific TFN leasehold project's own documents is a per-project legal question — do not assume the City-side answer carries over: UNCONFIRMED per project.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Presales (REDMA) — live on the TFN side:** Boardwalk phases are sold as presales; the buyer must receive the disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (s. 21), deposits in trust — and for TFN product the disclosure package is also where the lease structure is spelled out. Read both together. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **PTT / GST / rebates — City-side frame, TFN application UNCONFIRMED per tenure:** PTT general tiers 1% / 2% / 3% + further 2% over $3M residential; first-time buyers exempt on first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. GST on vacant land at CRA GI-003 level only: most individual personal-use sales exempt; business-use, in-the-course-of-business and more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable; developer/newly-subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. **How each applies to a specific TFN leasehold purchase is a lawyer/notary question for that tenure — do not assume the freehold answer.** https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy taxes — the boundary in tax form:** the Province's SVT taxable-areas list includes the City of Delta and states expressly that **reserve lands, treaty lands and lands of self-governing Indigenous Nations are NOT part of the taxable areas** — so Tsawwassen Lands are excluded from SVT taxable areas on the Province's own list, while City-side Delta parcels are in a listed area. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. SVT payable on a specific vacant City-side parcel, and any federal non-Canadian prohibition position per buyer/tenure: UNCONFIRMED in this research. No federal non-Canadian purchase-prohibition position for vacant land is documented in this file's own research — none is conformed from other files. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Delta property taxes (City side):** due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. Tax administration on a specific Tsawwassen Lands property is a separate question to confirm for that property. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What am I actually buying at Boardwalk or Tsawwassen Landing — the land?** No. You buy a home under a 99-year prepaid lease structure on Tsawwassen Lands: Boardwalk's presale source states "Leasehold via a 99-year prepaid lease" (landowners lease to the developer, which subleases to homeowners), and Seaside's MLS reports record "Leasehold prepaid-Strata" title (EPS4960/EPS5770). You are not buying City of Delta fee-simple title to the land. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/boardwalk6-by-aquilini-townhomes/
- **Q: Does City of Delta zoning / SSMUH apply to a TFN parcel?** No. TFN land follows TFN law, not City bylaws — Tsawwassen First Nation is self-governing over land management on Tsawwassen Lands (724+ ha, Final Agreement). Delta's SSMUH bylaws (8394/8395) and zoning ladder govern City-side lots only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **Q: Can I get a mortgage on a 99-year leasehold lot, and what happens at resale?** The lease term stated in the sources is 99 years, prepaid. Mortgage availability, lender remaining-term rules and resale handling for a specific TFN project are lender- and project-specific and were not verified in a primary source in this pass: UNCONFIRMED — take the project's lease and disclosure documents to your lender and lawyer before writing. https://ownboardwalk.com/
- **Q: Is there a fee-simple lot I can buy on the City side instead?** The City-side market is individual resale detached lots under RS/RD zoning (minimums 335 m² RS7 to 836 m² RD1) — no named vacant-lot subdivision was verified. The 4800 Springs Boulevard townhouse project is pipeline only (QUEUE, tenure UNCONFIRMED), not lots for sale. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/2026-01-15%20Delta%20Housing%20Snapshot.pdf
- **Q: Can I subdivide or add units to a City-side lot?** Subdivision runs against the zone minimum plus Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 servicing standards; SSMUH allows up to four units on a City-side single-detached/duplex lot without creating new lots. At the farm edge, ALR status can bar stratification entirely and require ALC approval to subdivide — check the parcel on the ALC maps first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Is a north-side lot serviced?** UNCONFIRMED per parcel — and the answer comes from different governments on each side of the boundary. Confirm City connections for a City parcel; for a TFN parcel, confirm servicing under the project's TFN documents. For an unserviced City parcel, GWELLS plus Fraser Health onsite-sewerage rules apply. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: Will I pay speculation tax on land here?** It depends which side of the boundary: City of Delta is a listed SVT taxable area; treaty lands / self-governing Nation lands are expressly NOT part of the taxable areas on the Province's list. Whether tax (vs a declaration/exemption) is payable on a specific vacant parcel: confirm per parcel. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax does not apply anywhere in Delta. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Q: What are holding costs / timeline risks?** City side: Delta property taxes (due July 2, 2026, 5% penalties after July 2 and September 2, 2026) and, if building, the subdivision/building timeline under City standards. TFN side: the holding terms are the lease/project documents' terms — read them for fees, term and renewal/assignment language before subjects. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Q: If I buy a Boardwalk presale, when can I change my mind?** Within REDMA's 7-day rescission window from the later of signing or acknowledging the disclosure statement, deposit in trust. Use that same window to have the lease structure reviewed — it is the cheapest diligence week available. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Q: Which schools serve a north-side lot?** Possibly Ladner's. The locator result for 5000 Canoe Pass Way is Ladner Elementary and Delta Secondary — run the exact address (including TFN addresses) in the SD37 locator before a school is promised. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
