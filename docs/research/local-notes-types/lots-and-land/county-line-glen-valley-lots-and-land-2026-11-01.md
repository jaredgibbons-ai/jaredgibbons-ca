## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2500**, lot by lot. Secondary suites under s.102 (as amended July 2025): capped at 120 m² maximum gross floor area, one per principal dwelling unit, and on ALR lands a suite must comply with ALC regulations. A "coach house" under s.102 is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage — a fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata; no coach-house size cap appears in s.100 as read — a cap is UNVERIFIED. **Live conflict, flagged not resolved:** s.102 (July 2025) permits a suite in a townhouse/rowhouse, while the Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide says suites are NOT permitted in townhouses/rowhouses — cite the bylaw, not the guide, where they differ. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, via the research file)
- **SSMUH: Township Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024** — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², subject to eligibility (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area); lots over 4,050 m² are not eligible, and **NO 6-plex tier exists**. Most parcels in this feed exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by a wide margin, so SSMUH generally does not apply to them. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/county-line-glen-valley.md)
- **Subdivision and servicing: Township Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED** — the servicing standard a new Township lot is built to; confirm the works required for a specific subdivision with the Township. (Program frame for this workstream)
- **ALR block (applies parcel by parcel, only where the parcel is in the reserve):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (stated for completeness — no such lot is verified here):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** a $1,500,000 parcel, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is County Line Glen Valley in Abbotsford?** No — it is entirely Township of Langley ground; County Line is the boundary street with Abbotsford, and Township bylaws, zoning and taxes apply. (§1 above)
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant serviced lot here?** No named subdivision selling serviced vacant lots was verified — land trades as individual fee-simple acreage parcels, checked parcel by parcel for servicing, zoning and ALR status. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: Is my parcel in the ALR?** Check the ALC maps by PID or civic address — Glen Valley is farm ground and no parcel's status was verified in the research pass; a listing's farm language is not verification. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can I add a second home or a suite?** Parcel by parcel: on ALR land the ALC caps apply (principal ≤500 m²; one additional residence 90 m² ≤40 ha / 186 m² >40 ha; suite inside the principal only); under Township s.102 a suite is capped at 120 m², one per principal unit, and a coach house is a fee-simple second-storey garage unit under s.102, never strata. Feed claims about approved plans or a mobile home as a second dwelling are document claims to verify. (§3 above)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put 4–6 units on an acreage?** No — Bylaw 6020 eligibility ends at 4,050 m² and requires Urban Containment Boundary land on municipal water and sewer, and there is NO 6-plex tier; most parcels here exceed the ceiling by a wide margin. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I split or stratify an ALR parcel into lots?** Subdivision in the ALR needs ALC approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). (§3 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a land purchase here?** PTT on the general tiers — on the §3 worked example ($1,500,000, illustration only) the bill is $28,000 with no exemption. GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Glen Valley address?** The Ministry-confirmed schools in or near the sub-area are listed in §1, but catchments are address-based and no rural locator result is on record — run the exact address in the SD35 School Locator before relying on a school. (§1 above)
- **Q: Is there a "355 m²" lot cap I should know about?** No — no "355 m²" figure exists in Township Zoning Bylaw 2500; s.403 R-CL is 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum lot area, and R-CL does not govern these acreage parcels in any event. (§2 above)
