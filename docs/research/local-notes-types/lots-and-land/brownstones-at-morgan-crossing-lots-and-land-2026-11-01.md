## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP — Surrey:** Surrey Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) is the current OCP — Bylaw 18020 is never the current OCP. - **SSMUH — Surrey:** the City adopted its SSMUH zoning changes on July 8, 2024 — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Added SSMUH units are not subdivision and do not create new lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites — Surrey:** a secondary suite's habitable floor area must not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex building, not a separate strata lot; only one suite per dwelling unit. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision and servicing — Surrey:** servicing is determined under the City of Surrey subdivision/servicing bylaw No. 8830 (1986), verified on City pages; subdivision itself runs through the Approving Officer under the Land Title Act. No subdivision of the Brownstones strata ground is documented or proposed in the research. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development
- **Strata block (strata lots, the complex's form as framed):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page — the live page itself directs buyers to the Form B, the strata bylaws and rules, the last two years of minutes and the depreciation report; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000.
