## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no bare-land market in the Quay at all — stated plainly at the top because it is the honest position, and it is structural: this is a built waterfront of high-rise and mid-rise apartment strata on Quayside Drive, with 0 detached listings in September 2026 against 59 condos & townhomes listings. No fee-simple lot, no named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering was verified. The "land" facts that govern a Quay purchase are the waterfront parcels' zoning frame and, above everything else, Floodplain Zone 3.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Floodplain Zone 3 is the defining diligence item for Quay land:** the City's floodplain study puts the Quay in Floodplain Zone 3, the Fraser River waterfront zone, where many newer buildings have floor space above the Flood Construction Level but egress and the waterfront path sit below it. The City publishes flood management information for its low-lying zones including the Quay; confirm the specific building's flood construction level compliance and the strata's insurance and deductible position lot by lot before subject removal. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf and http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- **Zoning:** Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 governs — multiple-unit residential and comprehensive development districts for the Quayside area, with zone-specific minimum lot sizes in the bylaw text and a Quay-specific minimum UNVERIFIED. Townhouse districts in the bylaw generally are RT-2–RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B only — RT-A/RT-B do not exist, and this file's research carries no such wording to correct. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Plan:** the Downtown Community Plan (Schedule C of the OCP) governs the downtown waterfront, under "Our City 2041," the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 7925, 2017. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **SSMUH / suites:** no SSMUH or suite rule specific to the Quay's strata waterfront was confirmed on a City source in the research — the stock is strata apartment form, where suites are not an accessory-use question; a Quay-specific SSMUH figure is UNCONFIRMED and none is published here. Basis: the sibling townhomes file, §3.
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — confirmed bylaw, process stated:** any re-subdivision of a waterfront parcel runs under the Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw No. 7142, 2007, which requires Approving Officer approval and ensures new lots are adequately serviced and meet the Zoning Bylaw's minimum lot size and frontage requirements, through the City's pre-application / application / Works & Services Agreement process. Parcel-level standards are confirmed through that process; none is quoted here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/7142_Subdivision.pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/General%20Guide%20to%20Subdivision%20Process%202012-Final.pdf
- **Bare-land strata rules:** no bare-land strata plan is verified in this area (Section 2), so no strata block is triggered here. The BC frame that applies to the Quay's conventional strata lots — including any townhouse-form unit inside a tower strata — is: Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 with the May 1, 2023 exemptions (the sibling 55-plus file checked every named Quayside strata and found no age restriction anywhere); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Quay ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm-class thresholds are not documented in this area's own research, so no farm-class figure is asserted here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. Stated for completeness — no vacant-land sale is documented in this area. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a lot — or a detached house — in the Quay?** No, on the verified record: the sub-area recorded 0 detached listings in September 2026 and its stock is apartment strata along Quayside Drive. There is no fee-simple lot inventory here to buy. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is the Quay in the Fraser River floodplain?** Yes — Floodplain Zone 3. Many newer buildings have floor space above the Flood Construction Level, but egress and the waterfront path sit below it; confirm the specific building's compliance, strata insurance and deductible position before subject removal. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Q: What does Floodplain Zone 3 mean for a ground-level unit?** It is the sharpest version of the Quay flood file: a ground-oriented unit's doors open at path level, below the Flood Construction Level the upper floors sit above. Confirm the unit's own floor level against the building's FCL compliance, plus the strata insurance position, in the strata documents — not the listing. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Q: If I buy a RiverSky 2 townhouse-form unit, what am I actually buying?** A freehold strata lot in a 33-storey tower strata (EPS5412, built 2019) — full Form B diligence, tower depreciation report, tower CRF and tower insurance, including the flood position — not a small townhouse strata's simpler file and not a land purchase. Basis: the sibling townhomes file, §2.
- **Q: Are Westminster Landing and Riverbend land I can buy into?** No — they are co-operative / non-market complexes (84 and 72 units, mixed tenures) in the City's non-market inventory; membership housing, not strata title or fee-simple land for sale. https://newwestcity.ca/database/rte/Housing%20web%20update%20-%20Non-Market%20details.pdf
- **Q: How active is this market, honestly?** The Quay sold 16 of 59 "Condos & Townhomes" listings in September 2026 (27% sales ratio) — the strongest sub-area ratio among the New Westminster files in its research batch — against a community combined median that eased to $560,000. Building-by-building pricing beats city-wide averages here, and SnapStats publishes no Quay-specific median. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report (research file).
- **Q: Can I rent out a Quay strata home long-term?** Yes under provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws in the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is any Quay building 55+?** No — the sibling 55-plus file checked every named Quayside strata and found no age restriction anywhere; do not buy here expecting a 55+ bylaw. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/quay-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **Q: Which schools serve a Quayside Drive address?** For the sample station address (800 Carnarvon Street): École Qayqayt Elementary, Fraser River Middle and New Westminster Secondary — but SD40 catchments are address-based, so run the exact Quayside Drive address in the locator. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- **Q: Two stations — is that real for every building?** New Westminster Station and Columbia Station (both Expo Line) sit at the top of the waterfront and route 103 runs Quayside Drive — but door-to-station walks lengthen for the east-end buildings (Laguna Court, The Q); measure the specific building's walk rather than pricing a generic "two-station" claim. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf
