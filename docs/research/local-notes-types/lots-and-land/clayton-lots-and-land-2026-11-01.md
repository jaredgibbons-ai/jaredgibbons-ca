## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Clayton is a board sub-area of the Fraser Valley board community "Cloverdale" (Clayton, Cloverdale, Serpentine) and a neighbourhood inside the City of Surrey's Cloverdale community, one of the City's seven communities. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- Municipal geography: the City plans this ground as **Clayton Heights** — approximately 809 hectares (2,000 acres), extending from Fraser Highway in the south to the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) in the north and west, bordered by the Township of Langley in the east. The City's plan neighbourhoods are East Clayton, West Clayton and the not-yet-initiated North Clayton. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/clayton-general-land-use-plan
- The board's street-level boundary is UNVERIFIED — FVREB publishes it only as a member-only Paragon / AUTOPROP map layer, and no municipal boundary is substituted for it. https://news.fvreb.bc.ca/member-updates/tech-tip-18-map-of-the-bc-boards-areas-subareas/
- East Clayton is located north of Fraser Highway along the border with the Township of Langley; homes east of that boundary are in the Township, not Surrey Clayton. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton
- Planning frame: the Clayton General Land Use Plan (approved January 25, 1999) set up the Neighbourhood Concept Plan process; East Clayton's NCP was endorsed in 2003 and West Clayton's final NCP was approved in July 2015, planned for over 12,000 residents with a mixed-use centre at 184 Street and Fraser Highway. The City records that those NCP designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update — confirm a lot's current OCP designation rather than relying on an NCP label alone. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/west-clayton-ncp

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — current plan only.** Land use now follows the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026), and Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and is never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it (Jul 8, 2024 framework).** Maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. The former RF-12 / RF-12C zones were replaced by R4 Small Lot Residential and RF-SD by R6 Semi-Detached in those changes — older Clayton material using RF-12 / RF-SD names uses pre-2024 names. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey).** Only one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit — a suite is not a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Coach houses and garden suites.** All Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; the unit must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house; one extra parking space is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Subdivision + servicing process.** Surrey subdivision proceeds through detailing and a Servicing Agreement defining the off-site works, services and financial securities for final approval. **The Surrey servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in this file** — no number is stated in this slug's research or a cited City source opened for it, no other city's number is borrowed, and lot-specific servicing costs, DCC amounts and timelines for a Clayton parcel are UNCONFIRMED; the process above is what to follow, not an estimate. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey).** Permitted, owner-only: renting of a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one. Licence figure CONFLICT carried openly: $358/yr is the program figure, WITH the City's 2024 corporate report proposing $350/yr + fees disclosed — read the City licence page before quoting either. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **BC strata block (wherever a bare-land strata lot is the purchase — form only; no Clayton instance verified).** Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR block (Clayton's north and west edge parcels).** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; principal residence ≤500 m², a suite within the principal residence only, and one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC Assessment farm class — full three-tier form.** $2,500 (farm operation 0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 (under 0.8 ha); applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an error and is not used here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land.** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Anchors: $500,000 → $8,000; $700,000 → $12,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. First-Time Home Buyers': no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — relief turns on a qualifying home, not the lot alone. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only.** Most individuals' vacant-land sales are exempt (e.g., personal-use land); taxable examples include business capital property, sales in the course of a business, and parcels created by subdividing into more than two parts. Finer points per lot: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs.** The FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, window March 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land. New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax.** Surrey IS a designated taxable area; owners file the annual declaration and a vacant parcel's position turns on class and exemptions. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition:** no primary text was opened in this slug's research, so its application to a Clayton parcel is UNCONFIRMED here and is not conformed from other files. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/clayton.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot I can buy in Clayton?** No bare-land subdivision is verified in the research — the purchase is an existing fee-simple house lot for rebuild / added units, or an ALR-edge parcel. The queued townhouse names in §2 are built strata product for the townhomes home file, not lots. (§2 above.)
- **Q: Is Clayton in Surrey or Langley?** Surrey. Clayton Heights runs from Fraser Highway to the ALR, bordered by the Township of Langley in the east — homes east of that boundary are in the Township, with different bylaws, taxes and school district. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/clayton-general-land-use-plan
- **Q: How many units can a Clayton lot hold?** 3 under 280 m², 4 over 280 m², up to 6 on eligible 281 m²+ lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — subject to zone, servicing and, on ALR parcels, the ALC caps instead. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Can I add a coach house?** In zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes, yes — 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), lane access required for a coach house on smaller R3–R6 lots, one extra parking space. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Q: The listing says "RF-12" — is that the lot's zone?** Not currently: RF-12 / RF-12C became R4 and RF-SD became R6 on July 8, 2024. Check the lot's current zone in Bylaw 12000 before quoting potential. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Is The Grove in Clayton the same as The Grove in Walnut Grove?** No — the queue states expressly that Clayton's The Grove is a DIFFERENT complex from Walnut Grove's condo The Grove. Neither is counted in this land file. (§2 queue note.)
- **Q: Why do two files queue different Clayton names?** Because the brief-lineage queue (11 names) and the townhomes home file on disk (7 verified; queues only Balmoral, The Edge + a 16488 64 Ave complex) use different bases. This file states both, follows the research file + disk, and takes no count — the difference is flagged as housekeeping in FILE STATUS. (§2 above.)
- **Q: Is an edge lot ALR, and what changes if it is?** Check the parcel: ALR parcels are capped at a 500 m² principal residence with any suite inside it and at most one small additional residence (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size), and subdivision needs ALC approval — do not value an edge lot on urban SSMUH density. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: When does SkyTrain reach Clayton?** Hillcrest-184 Street and Clayton (190 Street) stations are named project stations with an anticipated in-service date of 2029 — price the plan, do not describe SkyTrain as operating. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/
