## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Central Coquitlam's land trades as fee-simple house lots on the established Como Lake / Austin streets, plus comprehensively developed strata sites at the City Centre edge. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan, and no presale LOT offering was verified inside Central Coquitlam. The one non-fee-simple land tenure documented in the siblings is a pad-rental manufactured-home park — the buyer owns the home and rents the pad, and never owns the land. Presale in the sub-area is an apartment-condo project, counted separately below and never as lots.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **Established-street house lots:** freehold detached houses across the central streets under the City's small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3 in Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996. Subdivision minimums as the City states them: 650 m² with 18.5 m width for R-1 and R-3, and 465 m² with 12.5 m width for R-2. These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size — the zone and dimensions of a specific lot are confirmed parcel by parcel. A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **City Centre edge sites:** apartment buildings cluster near Pinetree Way and the City Centre stations — the tower cluster (Oasis, MThree, the Grand Central buildings, Altamonte, Parc Laurent) is documented in the condos sibling on its street addresses. Those are completed strata development sites, not lot offerings, and no unit or lot count is taken from them in this file. (Sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/central-coquitlam-condos-2026-11-01.md, §2.)
- **Bare-land strata: none verified.** No bare-land strata plan in Central Coquitlam was confirmed in the research or the siblings. The form is contemplated by the zoning bylaw — Part 6, s.601(2) provides that bare land strata subdivision is permitted only if a roadway providing vehicular access to each strata lot is provided — but a permitted form is not stock. Stated plainly, wherever a bare-land strata lot does exist: a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — the buyer owns a strata lot, pays strata fees, is bound by the strata bylaws, and diligence runs through the Form B (Section 3). https://northeastsector.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1171/Part-6-Subdivision-PDF--Subdivision-permissions-and-regulations
- **Serviced vs unserviced, as the City states it:** Coquitlam utility charges cover water, sewer and drainage and solid waste, and the City now offers a Voluntary Residential Water Metering Program under which metered properties are billed every four months instead of the annual flat rate. Critically for lot value: lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are **outside** the City's SSMUH rules — so property-level septic or well status changes what a lot can do, and that status is UNVERIFIED parcel-by-parcel in the research. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17426/2026-Budget-Highlights and https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** ALR status for the sub-area is UNVERIFIED in the research; no ALR split is stated and none is invented. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any ALR rule. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-coquitlam.md)
- **Watercourse-shaped land:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam; Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and s.523 covers riparian areas. A Fraser River floodplain designation for Central Coquitlam's residential streets: UNVERIFIED. Gross lot area is not buildable area on creek and ravine lots (Section 3). https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP:** Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 (R-1/R-2/R-3 subdivision minimums in Sections 1–2). The plan is now the **Interim Official Community Plan — Bylaws 5511/5512, 2026 — adopted April 27, 2026, superseding the Citywide OCP No. 3479, 2001**; the City states the Interim OCP streamlined the former plan and did not introduce new land-use policy except as provincial legislation required, with a future OCP update project anticipated to begin in 2027. Any Central Coquitlam document still citing OCP No. 3479 as current is stale. City Centre and Town Centre planning and the Transit-Oriented Areas framework apply at the Pinetree Way station area. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review (Siblings: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/central-coquitlam-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §3; ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/central-coquitlam-condos-2026-11-01.md, §3.)
- **SSMUH / added units, as the City states it:** up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes — **but lots not connected to municipal water or sewer fall outside the SSMUH rules**, so services are confirmed before any unit potential is priced in. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit and https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision minimums run off the zone (650 m² / 18.5 m in R-1 and R-3; 465 m² / 12.5 m in R-2), and servicing runs under the **City of Coquitlam Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 3558, 2003** — confirmed on the City's own document portal in a fresh check (Nov 2, 2026): the consolidated bylaw (original adoption July 24, 2003; amendments through July 2026) requires the owner to grant or acquire statutory rights-of-way for the works and services needed to serve a subdivision or development, in a form acceptable to the City's solicitor, and to pay for traffic-control and street-name signage installed by the City at the owner's expense before subdivision approval or building permit issue. Parcel-level servicing requirements are confirmed with the City for the specific lot. https://publicdocs.coquitlam.ca/coquitlamdoc/getdocIF.asp?doc=6328713
- **Watercourse / flood diligence:** a Watercourse Protection Development Permit can be required within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam, and Zoning Bylaw s.519 (flood protection setbacks) and s.523 (riparian areas) apply — check creek and ravine lots early, before pricing a rebuild or a split. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Bare-land strata block (no bare-land strata plan is documented in Central Coquitlam; stated for the form, which the zoning bylaw permits only with roadway access to each strata lot):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children). A listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://northeastsector.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1171/Part-6-Subdivision-PDF--Subdivision-permissions-and-regulations
- **ALR rules (sub-area ALR status UNVERIFIED here; standing rules for completeness only):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; residence caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — asserted here only as the standing three-tier rule; the research documents no farm-class parcel in Central Coquitlam, and a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever it appears. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Central Coquitlam lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Central Coquitlam-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in this file's research or siblings on a cited official source — none is imported or asserted here. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is VANCOUVER ONLY and does not apply in Coquitlam. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — no prohibition source is cited in the research, and the position is not conformed from other files.

- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create here?** It depends on the zone: 465 m² with 12.5 m width in R-2, or 650 m² with 18.5 m width in R-1 and R-3, under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 — and servicing runs under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 3558, 2003. Confirm the parcel's zone before pricing a split. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Q: Can a house lot add units?** Yes under the City's SSMUH rules — up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes — but lots not connected to municipal water or sewer fall outside the rules, so confirm services and the exact zone first. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Q: Why can't I find the City Centre towers under "Central Coquitlam"?** Because listing feeds label the verified tower cluster North Coquitlam / Canyon Springs / Town Centre, not Central Coquitlam — a "Central Coquitlam" board filter misses them.
