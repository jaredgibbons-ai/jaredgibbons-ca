## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Killarney has no bare-lot market in the ordinary sense — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community and no presale LOT offering was verified. Land here exists in three distinct forms, and confusing them is the expensive mistake in this area: (1) fee-simple house lots on the established streets; (2) the East Fraser Lands (River District) — a single comprehensively-planned development landholding on the former sawmill site, which trades as built strata product, never as lots; and (3) Champlain Heights ground, the majority of which is City leasehold, not fee-simple land at all. Each form is documented separately below.**

### Fee-simple lots — the established streets

- **House lots:** the City describes Killarney as primarily single-family residential, with some multi-family homes, and highrises in the recently developed Fraserlands. The established streets and Champlain Heights' freehold pockets are freehold detached houses under the R1-1 District Schedule. A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/killarney.aspx
- **Lot character, as the research states it:** R1-1 minimum site areas run from 306 m² (single detached house or duplex) through 464 m² (multiple dwelling with 5 units) to 557 m² (6 to 8 units); a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. These are schedule minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size; the zone and dimensions of a specific lot are confirmed parcel by parcel. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Serviced position:** Killarney homes are on City water and sewer services; a 2026 utility dollar figure for a Killarney house confirmed on a City source is UNVERIFIED. Servicing for any specific subdivision or multiplex proposal is confirmed with the City. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** ALR land inside Killarney is UNVERIFIED in the research — no ALR parcel is confirmed and no split is stated. (Research file above.)
- **Flood position:** the City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain along the Fraser River in South Vancouver, and land in the floodplain is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for setbacks and flood construction levels — this applies to the Fraserlands / River District foreshore. Whether a specific Killarney parcel is in the floodplain is UNVERIFIED parcel by parcel. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/

### Development land — the East Fraser Lands (River District), documented as a plan, NEVER as lot inventory

- **The landholding:** the East Fraser Lands Official Development Plan (River District) applies at the south edge — a mainly residential community with a variety of housing types and a commercial centre on the **former Canadian White Pine Sawmill site**, with **Wesgroup Properties owning the majority of the land**; Area 1, Area 2 and Kerr Street have been rezoned with Official Development Plan / CD-1 amendments enacted. The unbuilt balance of this land is one developer's phased development land — it is not subdivided into lots for public sale, and no bare-lot offering within it was verified. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/east-fraser-lands-river-district-documents-and-policies.aspx
- **What a buyer actually buys there:** built strata product under site-specific CD-1 districts — the Town Centre buildings, Avalon Park, MODE, Paradigm, Currents at Water's Edge, New Water and Rhythm — all documented and counted in the condos sibling (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/killarney-ve-condos-2026-11-01.md), with townhouse-form components carried in the townhomes sibling. No unit or lot is re-counted here. The district's tenure mix is part of the plan: the **Sawmill Housing Development** (337 homes — 220 non-profit rentals operated by M'akola Housing Society and 117 co-op homes with Community Land Trust) is rental and co-operative housing on district land, not sale stock. https://canadianarchitect.com/gbl-opens-337-unit-rental-and-co-op-building-in-vancouvers-river-district/
- **A specific current presale project in the district verified on a developer site: UNVERIFIED** in the research — future River District phases are documented here as plan build-out only, never as for-sale inventory. (Research file above.)

### Leasehold ground — Champlain Heights (tenure context; counted in its own files)

- **The majority of Champlain Heights is leasehold property — pre-paid 99-year leases from the City of Vancouver expiring in the 2080s** — alongside housing co-operatives, per the condos sibling's sourced entry. A home there sits on City land under a prepaid lease: there is no fee-simple lot to buy, financing and resale run on the remaining lease term, and there is no strata Form B for a co-op or leasehold purchase. Per-complex tenure must be read off title — at least one Champlain Heights complex (Marine Woods) has listing evidence calling a unit a rare **freehold** townhouse, so the area's general leasehold reputation must never be blanket-applied to a specific complex. The River District strata and Park Place (the one verified standalone townhouse complex, in the townhomes sibling) are building-form strata, **not** bare-land strata.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575:** the house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule (Section 2 minimums; suite, laneway, multiplex to 6 units / 8 rental units as the schedule states), while East Fraser Lands parcels are under site-specific CD-1 districts — for example CD-1 (566), East Fraser Lands Town Square Precinct (By-law No. 10941), and CD-1 (567), East Fraser Lands Waterfront Precinct (By-law No. 10943), each an amendment to By-law No. 3575. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf and https://former.vancouver.ca/commsvcs/bylaws/CD-1/CD-1(566).pdf and https://former.vancouver.ca/commsvcs/bylaws/CD-1/CD-1(567).pdf
- **Plan — East Fraser Lands Official Development Plan (River District):** the governing plan for the south-edge land, as documented in Section 2 — a mainly residential community with a commercial centre on the former sawmill site, Wesgroup owning the majority of the land, with Area 1, Area 2 and Kerr Street rezoned by enacted ODP / CD-1 amendments. Land outside the district follows the citywide framework and the R1-1 schedule. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/east-fraser-lands-river-district-documents-and-policies.aspx
- **Subdivision — Subdivision By-law No. 5208 (fresh check, Nov 2, 2026):** Vancouver subdivision is governed by the Subdivision By-law (No. 5208), whose Schedule A sets minimum parcel width and area standards by district and sub-area category, administered by the Approving Officer; its fee schedule was most recently amended for 2026 by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026). A fee-simple split of a Killarney house lot must clear both R1-1 and its Subdivision By-law category. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states them:** Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit framework for the house streets IS the R1-1 schedule — house plus secondary suite and laneway house, duplex, or a multiplex to 6 strata units / 8 rental units by lot size — a per-lot schedule check, not a separate overlay. River District land is CD-1 and follows the ODP instead. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Floodplain standards:** the designated floodplain covers the Fraser River frontage, and parcels in it carry Vancouver Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements — for River District land this is a design condition of the district itself, and for any specific parcel the City dataset governs. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Leasehold diligence (Champlain Heights ground):** a prepaid 99-year City lease expiring in the 2080s is valued on its remaining term, not as fee-simple land; confirm the lease's rent/prepayment status, assignment and financing terms on title before comparing any Champlain Heights price to a freehold Killarney lot, and verify the specific complex's tenure — the freehold exception noted in Section 2 means title, not neighbourhood reputation, decides. (Sibling file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/killarney-ve-condos-2026-11-01.md.)
- **Bare-land strata / strata rules (the BC strata block — applies in full to every River District strata lot, and would apply to a bare-land strata lot if one were offered):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Killarney ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, asserted in this program only where the horse-properties research documents it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Killarney lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%, Vancouver only:** every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration; homes declared or deemed vacant are taxed at 3% of assessed taxable value, the 2025 tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. A house lot held vacant carries this annual step — confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Speculation tax + purchase prohibition:** no Killarney-specific speculation-tax position is documented in this file's research beyond the City tax above — none imported. The federal non-Canadian purchase-prohibition position for Killarney land is UNCONFIRMED in this file: the research cites no prohibition source for Killarney VE, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Vancouver property taxes:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a building lot in the River District?** No lot offering was verified — the East Fraser Lands are a single phased development landholding (Wesgroup owns the majority) sold as completed strata homes under CD-1 districts. You buy a strata home there, documented in the condos sibling, not a lot. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/east-fraser-lands-river-district-documents-and-policies.aspx
- **Q: What land does trade in Killarney, then?** Fee-simple house lots on the established streets — usually with a post-war house on them — priced off the lot plus R1-1 potential: suite and laneway house with a house, or a multiplex to 6 units (8 rental) on a qualifying lot. (Section 2.)
- **Q: A Champlain Heights townhouse looks cheap for Vancouver — what am I missing?** Usually the tenure: the majority of Champlain Heights sits on pre-paid 99-year City of Vancouver leases expiring in the 2080s, or is co-op housing — neither carries fee-simple land value. But read the title on the specific complex: Marine Woods has listing evidence of a rare freehold unit, so the general rule is a warning, not a verdict. (Sibling file: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/killarney-ve-townhomes-2026-11-01.md.)
- **Q: Is Champlain Heights part of Killarney or not?** Both, depending on the map: the City counts it inside its Killarney local area; MLS files it as its own sub-area, and this program follows MLS for counting. The practical difference is tenure and search — search both sub-area names or you will miss stock. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/killarney.aspx
- **Q: Where does Avalon Mews belong — and can I buy a lot in it?** Nowhere, yet, in this program: it is QUEUED — the City rezoning record places the Wales Street site in Victoria-Fraserview while MLS files the built complex under Killarney VE, so it is counted in no file. And there is no lot in it to buy in any case; it is a built strata townhouse complex. https://council.vancouver.ca/20140610/documents/p3.pdf
- **Q: Does the floodplain touch a Killarney lot?** At the river edge, yes — the designated floodplain covers the Fraser River frontage with Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements; the established house streets sit inland. Check the specific parcel in the City dataset before pricing any river-side property. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: Can I split a house lot here?** Only if both resulting parcels clear the R1-1 schedule and the Subdivision By-law No. 5208 Schedule A category for the block, through the Approving Officer — and CD-1 land in the River District does not subdivide this way at all. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003); the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not the bare lot. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Is there any bare-land strata in Killarney?** None verified. Every strata form documented here is building-form (River District towers and low-rises, Park Place townhouses). A bare-land strata lot, if one were ever offered, would still be full strata tenure: Form B, fees and bylaws on a lot of land. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Killarney lot?** By exact address only — four elementaries and Killarney Secondary sit inside the area, and the one settled locator chain in this file (6454 Killarney Street → Dr. George M. Weir Elementary → Killarney Secondary) confirms only that address. Run the address in the SD39 locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The River District sits on a sawmill site, and one owner phases it:** the East Fraser Lands plan area is the former Canadian White Pine Sawmill site and Wesgroup Properties owns the majority of the land — one developer's phasing decisions drive nearly all new supply at Killarney's south edge, and the "land market" there is that phasing, not lot sales. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/east-fraser-lands-river-district-documents-and-policies.aspx
- **Killarney was forest and farms until after WWII:** the City records that the area remained second-growth forest and farms until after the war, and that the first non-native settler, William Rowling (1868), came to own 3.5 kilometres of Fraser River shoreline including all of Killarney's riverfront — which is why the house stock is post-war and the only comprehensively-planned ground is the old sawmill land. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/killarney.aspx
- **Everett Crowley Park was a landfill:** the roughly 40-hectare park at 8200 Kerr Street is Champlain Heights' green core and a former city landfill — relevant context for lots on its edge, and part of why the Champlain Heights ground was planned as one community in the 1970s rather than sold lot by lot. (Sibling file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/killarney-ve-condos-2026-11-01.md.)
- **One Town Centre answers to two addresses:** the developer markets 8550 River District Crossing, while MLS® sale records address One Town Centre units at 3557 Sawmill Crescent (Strata Plan EPS4966) — confirm which address a unit's title and strata records use before ordering documents; the same dual-address habit is what keeps Avalon Mews mis-filed between two sub-areas. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3175717-717-3557-sawmill-crescent-vancouver-bc-v5s-0e2
- **The library serving Killarney carries the other neighbourhood's name:** the Vancouver Public Library branch for the area is the Champlain Heights Branch at 7110 Kerr Street — a small sign of how completely the City's "inside Killarney" geography and the board's separate sub-area interleave on the same ground. https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/killarney-community-centre.aspx
