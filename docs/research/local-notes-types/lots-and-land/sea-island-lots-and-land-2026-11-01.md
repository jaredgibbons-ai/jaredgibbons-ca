## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Sea Island is a separate island in the Fraser River in the City of Richmond, BC — Lulu Island (the main part of Richmond) to the south and east, and the City of Vancouver across the North Arm to the north. "Sea Island" is both the board sub-area name (GVR board area) and one of the City's own planning areas — planning area 12 on the City Official Community Plan Key Map — so the City name and the board name match. Paragon sub-area code: UNVERIFIED. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/CNCL_062209_Bylaw_840323090.pdf
- Where the housing is: the island is dominated by Vancouver International Airport (YVR) on federal land; the only residential pocket is Burkeville, in the south-east part of the island near Miller Road and Russ Baker Way. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/CNCL_062209_Bylaw_840323090.pdf and https://www.yvr.ca/en/about-yvr/community/engagement
- Nearby areas: Bridgeport (Richmond, across the Moray Channel / Dinsmore Bridge), Terra Nova (Richmond, across the Middle Arm), and Vancouver (across the North Arm via the Arthur Laing Bridge). Buyers also search "Burkeville," "YVR," "Vancouver International Airport" and "Templeton" (the Canada Line station area). https://www.translink.ca/rider-guide/visiting-vancouver
- Roads and bridges: Grant McConachie Way serves the airport terminals, Russ Baker Way runs south to the Dinsmore Bridge and Richmond, and Miller Road runs through Burkeville; bridges off the island are the Arthur Laing Bridge (to Vancouver), the Moray Channel Bridge, the Dinsmore Bridge and the No. 2 Road Bridge. Commute times in minutes: UNVERIFIED. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/CNCL_062209_Bylaw_840323090.pdf
- Transit: the Canada Line airport branch serves Sea Island at Templeton, Sea Island Centre and YVR-Airport stations, with a change at Bridgeport Station for Richmond–Brighouse branch trains. Local bus route numbers for Burkeville streets: UNVERIFIED. https://www.translink.ca/rider-guide/visiting-vancouver
- Schools: there is no operating public school on Sea Island — Sea Island Elementary School on Wellington Crescent has closed. SD38 catchments are address-based, with no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12); for the sample address 7140 Miller Road (Sea Island Community Centre, Burkeville), the SD38 School Locator gives Brighouse Elementary and Richmond Secondary — a completed locator run for that sample address only, non-transferable to other addresses. Nearest hospital: Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments and https://www.richmondmuseum.ca/locations/sea-island-school/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Planning split — read first:** Sea Island is planning area 12 in the City Official Community Plan framework (City of Richmond 2041 OCP, Bylaw 9000) for the Burkeville pocket, while senior governments are not regulated by the City OCP — airport-side changes follow the federal / YVR process under the YVR 2037 Master Plan. Which regime governs a given parcel is the first diligence question on this island. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm and https://news.yvr.ca/yvrs-new-master-plan-has-arrived/
- **Zoning — Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 (Burkeville pocket):** the exact zone and minimum lot size applying to Burkeville lots are UNVERIFIED in the research, so no zone or minimum is quoted for Burkeville here. Most of the island sits under the YVR land use plan and federal airport zoning regulations rather than the City bylaw. https://news.yvr.ca/statement-regarding-airport-zoning-regulations/
- **Federal Airport Zoning Regulations:** administered through Transport Canada, they set zoning in areas surrounding the airport so aircraft can operate safely, including maximum building heights — they can affect additions, rebuilds and tree height on a Burkeville lot. https://news.yvr.ca/statement-regarding-airport-zoning-regulations/
- **Aircraft noise:** noise is managed through the YVR 2025–2029 Noise Management Plan, which includes work to promote compatible land use planning near the airport; YVR publishes annual noise reports with monitoring data. The City's Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development (ANSD) Policy Area framework (City Bulletin DEVAPPS-05) applies to noise-sensitive development in the airport's vicinity. https://www.yvr.ca/en/about-yvr/noise-management/reports and https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/devapps0534378.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential:** no Burkeville-specific SSMUH or subdivision position is stated in the research — UNCONFIRMED for this pocket, and no citywide Richmond figure is imported as a Burkeville finding.
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — VERIFIED for Richmond:** the City's own alphabetical bylaw list carries "Subdivision and Development — Bylaw 8751 (2011)" and "Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 (2011)" — Richmond's subdivision/servicing instrument is Bylaw 8751, with cost recovery under Bylaw 8752; parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City. No subdivision route exists through the City for the federal airport lands. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **Flood / dike diligence — Bylaw 8204:** Richmond is made up of low-lying islands in the Fraser River floodplain, protected by a perimeter dike system (49 km of dikes, 39 pump stations, designed for a 1:500 flood event, dikes being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise); the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets flood construction levels and dike setbacks. Confirm the lot's requirements before planning a rebuild. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Sea Island ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and it is not asserted for this ground. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata frame (applies only if a bare-land strata lot ever surfaces here — none is verified):** Form B under SPA s. 59; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for strata corporations with 5 or more lots since July 1, 2024; at least 10% of the annual operating budget to the CRF since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: an $800,000 purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000**; a fully qualifying first-time buyer at that price pays **$6,000** after the exemption on the first $500,000. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
