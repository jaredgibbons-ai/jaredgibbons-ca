## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Ambleside has no standing vacant-lot market: no named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community, and no presale LOT offering was verified inside Ambleside. What trades as land here is (1) the individual fee-simple RS house lot on the streets north of Marine Drive, and (2) town-centre development land whose value is now set by the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan as implemented by provincial order in April 2026 — a plan framework, documented as such below, never as for-sale inventory. Ambleside's most distinctive land fact runs the other way: the District has spent fifty years buying waterfront lots OUT of the market (the 32-lot assembly below). The 8 verified condo buildings are strata stock in the condos sibling — this file takes no lot or unit count from them.**

### Fee-simple lots — the residential form

- **RS lot fabric:** detached houses in the RS single-family dwelling zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010, on the streets north of Marine Drive and below the highway. Section 200 states minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, with RS6 using the area of the site of record, and lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses in the single-family zones. These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size — the zone for a specific lot is shown on the District's Zoning Map. A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Town-centre land:** apartment and mixed-use form concentrates in the town-centre blocks under the OCP and the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan. The completed strata buildings on that land (including Grosvenor Ambleside, 1355 Bellevue Avenue, phases completed 2018 and 2020 per the condos sibling) are documented once, in the condos sibling — cross-referenced here, never re-documented as land stock. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf (Sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/ambleside-condos-2026-11-01.md, §2.)

### The waterfront assembly — public acquisition, NEVER inventory

- **The District's 32-lot waterfront assembly:** the District has acquired waterfront lots since 1975 to complete a continuous public waterfront; on March 31, 2025 the District announced the final home on the Ambleside waterfront, 1444 Argyle Avenue, had been acquired — the last of 32 lots acquired since 1975 — to form part of Brissenden Waterfront Park, connecting Ambleside Park to John Lawson Park. The plan's connected green spaces are John Lawson Park, Millennium Park, Brissenden Waterfront Park and Ambleside Green, under the Ambleside Waterfront Concept Plan endorsed by Council on June 13, 2016; the 2013 Waterfront Guiding Principles (an OCP policy) state that public ownership of the waterfront from 13th to 18th Streets is a community value. These lots were removed from the private market by design — they are the opposite of lot inventory, and no waterfront "land opportunity" should be marketed off this assembly. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/major-projects/ambleside-waterfront-park-implementation-project
- **The seawalk edge:** the Centennial Seawalk runs west from Ambleside Park to Dundarave Park — built in 1967 for Canada's centennial, roughly 1.7 km — so the waterfront amenity anchoring these lots' value is public parkland, not lot frontage. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/dundarave-park

### Plan framework — documented as such, NEVER inventory

- **Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan, implemented by provincial order:** on July 21, 2025 the Province directed West Vancouver to adopt the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan (with increased density at Park Royal–Taylor Way and in single-family/duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave) by December 31, 2025. - **Hazard development permits:** a **Foreshore Development Permit** is required for work within the Foreshore Development Permit Area (waterfront property or property at risk of coastal flooding) — the operative permit on Ambleside's Bellevue Avenue and waterfront-edge lots; an **Environmental Development Permit** is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank; a **Wildfire Hazard Development Permit** is required for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there vacant waterfront land for sale in Ambleside?** No — the waterfront from 13th to 18th Streets is publicly owned by policy, and the District completed a 50-year assembly of 32 waterfront lots in March 2025 by acquiring the last house at 1444 Argyle Avenue for Brissenden Waterfront Park. Waterfront-edge value here is in existing lots and buildings beside public parkland, not in acquirable vacant waterfront lots. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/major-projects/ambleside-waterfront-park-implementation-project

- **Q: How big are Ambleside house lots?** It depends entirely on the RS zone: Section 200 minimum site areas run 488 m² (RS5) to 8,094 m² (RS1), RS6 by site of record. - **Q: Can I add a suite or a coach house?** Section 200 lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses in the single-family zones, subject to the bylaw's conditions — a standard feature of these lots' value. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Q: Can I put 4–6 units on an Ambleside lot under SSMUH?** The Province's legislation requires zoning to allow a minimum of 4 units on single-family/duplex parcels over 280 m², and 6 near frequent bus service — but West Vancouver's local implementing-bylaw status is UNVERIFIED in this file's research, and the Local Area Plan was only implemented by provincial order on April 7, 2026. Verify per property with the District before pricing multiplex potential. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/local-governments-and-housing/housing-initiatives/smale-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: What does the Local Area Plan order change for a town-centre lot?** The plan is now in force by provincial order (April 7, 2026) after the District missed its December 31, 2025 adoption deadline — price the lot off the plan as implemented, not the pre-2026 OCP alone, and note the District must file progress reports with the Province every three months through 2026 and 2027 on the remaining directives. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0038-000377
- **Q: What permit does a Bellevue Avenue lot trigger?** A Foreshore Development Permit — required for waterfront property and other property at risk of coastal flooding, which is exactly the Bellevue/Argyle edge stock. Check it before subjects. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Q: Is the Park Royal land next door the same tenure as Ambleside?** No — Park Royal is a separate sub-area, and part of it sits on Capilano IR No. 5 under the 1974 Park Royal Lease, split from District fee-simple ground at that edge. Ambleside lots west of Taylor Way are District ground; anything at the Park Royal edge needs a parcel-level tenure check. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do
