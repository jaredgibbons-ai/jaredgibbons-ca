## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — Surrey Official Community Plan Bylaw 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** (standing correction; Bylaw 18020 is never cited as current). Community frame: https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- **Zoning / suites:** Surrey suite cap is 40% (standing correction — suite size cap as City states it; parcel eligibility is a City check). Short-term rentals: permitted owner-only — licence figure CONFLICT carried as $358/yr, with the City's 2024 corporate report proposing $350/yr + fees disclosed.
- **Subdivision + servicing:** no verified Surrey subdivision/servicing bylaw number is stated in this file's research — none quoted; subdivision and servicing are per-parcel City checks against the parcel's zone and OCP 21900.
- **Floodplain:** Surrey's coastal floodplain stretches from Boundary Bay and Mud Bay along the Nicomekl and Serpentine Rivers toward Cloverdale and Newton, includes the Little Campbell River area, and is about 20% of Surrey's land area; named residential areas include Crescent Beach, Panorama/Gray Creek, Cloverdale, Inter-River, Colebrook, Mud Bay and Nico-Wynd/Crescent Road; most is dyke-protected with sea dams, ditches, flood boxes, pumps and spillways — flood construction/insurance is address-specific. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain
- **ALR:** the floodplain contains over 60 sq km of agriculture land and 10% of Metro Vancouver's ALR; ALR status is parcel-specific via ALC mapping. ALR subdivision needs ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, by Oct 31) — flat "$2,500+" is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **PTT:** 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above; further 2% on residential value over $3,000,000 (mixed-class: residential portion only; farm-class land with a dwelling: up to 0.5 ha treated as residential). PTT is on fair market value at registration, not annual taxes. Worked math in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Exemptions do not reach this band:** FTHB full exemption caps at $835,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. Additional PTT rate for foreign nationals/corporations/taxable trustees: UNVERIFIED in this file (separate provincial page not re-read). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Home owner grant at this band:** 2026 threshold $2,075,000 — full regular grant at/below, reduced $5 per $1,000 over, $0 over $2,189,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; Mar 20, 2025–2031) — and it phases out below this band's floor in any case. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation 5-yr cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; 10% CRF since Nov 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
