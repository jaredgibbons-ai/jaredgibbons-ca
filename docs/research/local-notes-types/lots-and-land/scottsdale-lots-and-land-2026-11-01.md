## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Scottsdale has no bare-land subdivision market as documented in the research — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata, and no presale lot offering was verified on an official or developer source. What actually trades as land here is fee-simple residential lots on the interior grid — detached-house and duplex lots, many carrying an older house priced with SSMUH infill potential, plus TOD-influenced lots within 400 metres of Scottsdale Exchange — and, at the Scott Road / 80 Avenue node, development sites documented as such, never as lot inventory.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **Interior grid lots (the real land stock):** single detached houses and duplexes on the residential grid, with strata condos and townhomes concentrated toward the Scott Road / 72–84 Avenue node; OCP Map 2 shows Small Scale Residential and Mixed Residential designations in and around the area. A "land" purchase here is almost always a fee-simple lot with an existing house on it. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Lot character from the bylaw ladder, as stated:** single-detached minimum lots run RS1 at 4,000 m² (width 30 m, average depth 30 m), RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² or 460 m², RS6 at 390 m² (width 13 m, depth 30 m) and RS7 at 335 m² (width 11 m); duplex zones run RD1 at 836 m² (width 22 m, average depth 30 m), RD2 and RD3 at 4,000 m², and RD4 at 504 m² (width 18 m, average depth 28 m, infill duplex); there is no RD5 zone. These are subdivision minimums for the zones named (zoning bylaw number contested — Section 3), not a claim that every lot is that size; the zone for a specific lot is confirmed on the City zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD1.pdf https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS7.pdf
- **Duplex title form is a land question here:** a duplex can be strata or non-strata in BC — in a strata the owners own their individual strata lots and together own common property as a strata corporation, while a non-strata duplex has no strata corporation. Confirm the title form for a specific lot through BC Land Title / BC Assessment before pricing or writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Servicing:** Scottsdale is urban North Delta grid — the research documents City flat-rate utilities (2026 flat-rate utility fee $1,658 for a single-family home) rather than well/septic diligence. Parcel-level servicing confirmations (laterals, off-site works on a subdivision): UNCONFIRMED in the research — confirm with the City for the parcel. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- **ALR vs non-ALR split, as stated:** OCP Map 2 shows no Agricultural designation for this part of North Delta, and a floodplain statement specific to Scottsdale was not confirmed in the sources read; property-level ALR status is a check through the Agricultural Land Commission. No ALR split figure is stated for Scottsdale. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a bare lot in a Scottsdale subdivision?** On this file's evidence, no — no named bare-land subdivision and no presale lot offering was verified. Land trades as individual fee-simple house/duplex lots on the grid, usually with a house on them. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How small can a new lot be?** By zone, from the bylaw ladder: RS7 at 335 m² (width 11 m) is the smallest single-detached subdivision minimum; RS6 390 m², RS5 550 m² or 460 m², RS4 610 m², RS1 4,000 m². Duplex minimums run RD4 at 504 m² (infill) to RD2/RD3 at 4,000 m²; there is no RD5 zone. Confirm the parcel's zone on the City maps — and note the zoning bylaw number itself is contested (Section 3). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS7.pdf
- **Q: What can I build on an ordinary house lot under SSMUH?** Delta's SSMUH regulations (Bylaws 8394 + 8395, June 24, 2024) allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites, with reduced parking. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q: Does being near Scottsdale Exchange change my lot's density?** Potentially, materially: Bill 47 minimum densities apply within 400 metres of Scottsdale Exchange per the City, and the 2024 OCP designates a Scottsdale Exchange TOD Area. Confirm which side of the TOD line the lot is on before pricing development potential. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q: Is a Scottsdale duplex always a strata?** No — the Province lists duplexes as a form strata housing can take, which means a duplex may be strata-titled or not; check the title and strata plan for the specific property. If it is strata (including any bare-land form), diligence runs through the Form B; if not, there is no strata document set at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Q: Is my lot in the ALR or a floodplain?** OCP Map 2 shows no Agricultural designation for this part of North Delta, and no Scottsdale-specific floodplain statement was confirmed — both are parcel checks (ALC maps for ALR status), not neighbourhood assumptions. (Research file §4.)
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot?** Generally yes at CRA GI-003 level — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable, unlike an individual's personal-use resale. Confirm per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What are holding costs on a Scottsdale lot?** Delta property taxes (2026: due July 2, 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2) plus the $1,658 single-family flat-rate utility fee, plus strata fees only if the lot is a strata lot (none verified as bare-land strata here). http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Q: Which schools serve a lot near Scott Road and 72nd?** Address-dependent: the locator result for the sample address 7031 120 St is Heath Elementary and Seaquam Secondary (SD37, no middle tier). Run the lot's exact address in the locator before promising a catchment. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Is Scottsdale Exchange actually in Delta?** No — the exchange itself sits on the Surrey side of Scott Road, per the City, even though the TOD Area and the sub-area carry its name. That matters for any "steps to the exchange" claim made for a Delta-side lot. http://www.delta.ca/node/1838
