- **ALR rules (the rural stock's governing frame — ALR ground is documented in this feed):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, and depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission — ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6 ("Strata Plans Cause Subdivision"); ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). Residence frame: one principal residence permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of **500 m²** (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite permitted **only when located within the principal residence**, and at most one additional residence of up to **90 m² on properties 40 ha or less** or up to **186 m² on properties over 40 ha**; local government bylaws may be more restrictive and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class — three tiers, documented in this slug's own research:** farm classification is a voluntary BC Assessment program; the income thresholds are **$2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha**; applications must be submitted to BC Assessment **by October 31** for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. The farm-class position of the feed's rural stock as a group: UNVERIFIED — classification is property-by-property. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not bare land). **Worked examples, inputs + tier math stated (fully residential, no exemption unless stated):** $500,000 → $2,000 + $6,000 = **$8,000**; $700,000 → $2,000 + $10,000 = **$12,000**; $800,000 → $2,000 + $12,000 = **$14,000** (fully-qualifying first-time buyer: **$6,000**); $900,000 → $2,000 + $14,000 = **$16,000**; $1,000,000 → $2,000 + $16,000 = **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → $2,000 + $20,000 = **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → $2,000 + $26,000 = **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → $2,000 + $32,000 = **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 = **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 (3% tier) + $10,000 (further 2% on the $500,000 over $3M) = **$93,000**. Where a $78,000 figure for $3.5M is met in program siblings, it omits the further 2% tier and is an OPEN reconciliation item — this file follows **$93,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
