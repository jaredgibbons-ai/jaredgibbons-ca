## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Pad-tenure law (the governing frame for every park in §2):** MHPTA site tenancies require a written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement. Pad-rent increases: only with 3 full months' written notice, only once every 12 months, and only up to the yearly limit — **2026: 2.3% plus a proportional amount; 2027: 2.2% plus a proportional amount.** The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park. The landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion of a prior year's limit. These percentages are the ONLY pad-rent figures an official source gives — no dollar figure is stated. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale (MHPTA s.28):** assignment permanently transfers the pad tenancy to the buyer; a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an order of the director — s.28(1)(b) wording is "an order of the director," NOT arbitrator — or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it. The landlord cannot charge a fee for consent, and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent increase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s.42):** where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to another use, all necessary government permits and approvals must be in place before notice, with 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation paid to the tenant on or before the notice's effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Registration and certification:** in BC a manufactured home sale/transfer is only effective if registered with the Manufactured Home Registry (lawyer/notary filing the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership); a home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 mobile homes, A-277 modular), and a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Taxes on a park home:** a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential owners, but the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner — the tax treatment itself confirms the pad is not the buyer's land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Township frame (all six directory parks are Township ground):** Zoning Bylaw 2500; SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024; 3 units at ≤280 m² / 4 units at 280–4,050 m² for eligible lots; NO 6-plex tier); suite cap 120 m² (s.102); coach house = fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata — with the live s.102 (July 2025) vs January 2025 guide conflict on townhouse suites flagged, not resolved; R-CL s.403 = 220 m² min / 464.5 m² max LOT area ("355 m²" is NOWHERE in the bylaw); Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED. The manufactured-home-park zone name/rules in Bylaw 2500 are UNVERIFIED in this slug's research — neither bylaw's park-zone section was opened — so no park-zone rule is stated. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **City frame (kept separate; no directory park is City ground):** Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 by number (adoption UNVERIFIED); SSMUH Bylaw 3284 (RS1/RS2: 4 units, 6 within 400 m of a frequent bus stop); suite = lesser of 100 m² and 40%, owner must reside on the lot (s.2.4). Its park-zone rules are likewise UNVERIFIED here. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3284
- **Park rules are NOT strata bylaws:** there is no strata corporation, no Form B and no SPA age-bylaw analysis for a pad-rental purchase — age and pet rules are park rules under the MHPTA frame, confirmed with the park. **BC strata block, for contrast and for any bare-land strata lot met elsewhere:** Form B diligence (within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR block (for any Langley parcel where reserve status is confirmed — no directory park is documented as ALR in this research):** subdivision in the ALR needs ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. **Worked examples (fully residential, no exemption unless stated):** $500,000 → $2,000 + $6,000 = **$8,000**; $700,000 → **$12,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000** (fully-qualifying first-time buyer: **$6,000**); $900,000 → **$16,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → $2,000 + $26,000 = **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 (further 2% over $3M) = **$93,000**. A $78,000 figure for $3.5M is an OPEN reconciliation item where met in siblings — this file follows **$93,000**. Note the park-home angle: PTT treatment follows the actual transaction (home vs land) — per-deal confirmation is UNCONFIRMED in this slug's research beyond the general tiers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level ONLY:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. (No vacant land is sold through this directory.) https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs:** Bill C-4 rebate (Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements March 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: If I buy a home in one of these parks, do I own the land?** No. You own the manufactured home and rent the pad under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement (MHPTA); your property tax assessment in a park excludes the land's value for exactly that reason. This file counts no park home as lot stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Q: How many parks are there in Langley?** No official count exists — there is no official Langley park registry. This directory names six, the listing feed surfaces others it omits (Cedar Creek Estates, Pioneer Park among them), so six is not a complete count. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-mobile-home-communities.html
- **Q: Which of the directory's "55+" parks are actually verified 55+?** Two of its four: Fernridge Park and Pineridge are in the verified roster in the 55-plus siblings. Cedar Lane Park and Deer Creek Estates are directory-named candidates with listing support only — UNCONFIRMED at park-source level, never counted. The third verified park, Cedar Creek Estates, is not on the directory at all. (`~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/langley-mobile-home-communities-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md`)
- **Q: How much is pad rent in these parks?** No dollar figure is published as fact here, and this directory states no pad rent for any park. No official source publishes a Langley pad-rent figure, average or range. The figure that matters is the specific park's current rent in its written site tenancy agreement. (§5 flags the site's conflicting UNVERIFIED ranges.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Only by the Act limit: 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027 — once every 12 months, on 3 full months' notice, no rounding up, no carry-forward. The proportional amount is park-specific. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park raise my pad rent when I buy?** No — on assignment under MHPTA s.28 the rent stays the same for the new tenant and the next increase date does not reset; assignment needs the landlord's prior written consent (or an order of the director) and no consent fee can be charged. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: What happens if the park is converted to another use?** Under MHPTA s.42: all permits first, then 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation per tenant, with defined additional routes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Is Pioneer Park a pad-rental park I missed on this page?** It is not on this directory, and it is not pad-rental: it is a 55+ co-operative in Aldergrove — you buy the home plus co-op shares and must be accepted by the co-op. Its full tenure record is in the 55-plus siblings; the feed treatment is in `langley-mobile-homes`. (`~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/langley-mobile-home-communities-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md`)
- **Q: Can a park home be sold without its CSA label?** No — without the original legible CSA decal (Z-240 / A-277) the home cannot be sold until Technical Safety BC re-certification, and the transfer must be registered with the Manufactured Home Registry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Are there market statistics for these parks?** No — SnapStats has no manufactured-home category and no per-park figures are published in any source opened. The Langley figures in §1 are all-residential context only. (Research file above.)
