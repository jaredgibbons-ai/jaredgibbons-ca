## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Scope is the whole City of Maple Ridge: properties sold under the BC Supreme Court foreclosure process — by court order with conduct of sale given to a party (usually the lender's side), or heading toward a final order of foreclosure. In BC a "court-ordered sale" is a sale inside a foreclosure proceeding, not a separate process; "bank foreclosure" is a misnomer — sales run through the court, not the bank. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- Governing rules are provincial: Supreme Court Civil Rules (Rule 21-7 foreclosure; Rule 13-5 court-ordered sales), the Law and Equity Act, and the Land Title Act's order nisi reference. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol2/consol2/96253_01
- Feed scope as read on the live page: court-ordered detached houses, strata townhomes, apartment condos and rural acreage listings across East Central, West Central, Albion, Silver Valley, Cottonwood, Southwest Maple Ridge, and rural Websters Corners and Whonnock; the visible feed cards included a West Central detached house, a Silver Valley land/development listing, a Websters Corners estate and a Southwest Maple Ridge land listing, each card stating "COURT ORDERED SALE" or "Court-Ordered Sale" in its own text. Card prices and details are feed text at the crawl moment, not market statistics, and no card price is carried here. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mapleridgeforeclosuresandcourtsales.html
- **Registry: UNCONFIRMED for Maple Ridge.** This slug's research opened no filing-registry rule (the Law and Equity Act s.21(2) text was not opened) and no official source in the research maps Maple Ridge files to a named registry. Whether a given file is filed at any particular registry is a per-file check on Court Services Online, which shows registry location per file. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- Court records: Court Services Online eSearch is open to anyone with Internet access; file details include file number, registry, parties and filed documents; file classes include "H - Foreclosure." It publishes no area statistics. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Step 1 — petition:** a foreclosure/redemption proceeding must be started by petition in the Supreme Court — Rule 21-7(1); all persons whose interest is sought to be extinguished must be respondents — Rule 21-7(2); the petitioner must serve the court's foreclosure information notice, Form 125 — Rule 21-7(4.1). Rule 21-7 as read states no demand step before the petition; a prescribed-mortgage-terms demand is UNVERIFIED in this slug's research. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Order / redemption:** the court may order payment of what is due within a redemption period the court fixes — Rule 21-7(5)(b) (no fixed length in the Rules); it may certify the amount due — Rule 21-7(5)(c),(e). In default, a final order of foreclosure may be granted — Rule 21-7(6). A party of record may apply at any time for an order that the property be sold — Rule 21-7(7). (Subrules (6) and (7) are carried on the settled treatment; this slug's research held them UNVERIFIED after a 429 stopped its re-open — FILE STATUS.) https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Confirming a sale:** even before redemption expires, the person with conduct of sale may apply for an order confirming the sale, directing proceeds and vesting title — Rule 21-7(9) (settled treatment; held UNVERIFIED in this slug's own pass). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Conduct of sale / Form 60 / PD-66 (settled treatment; this slug's research held these UNVERIFIED in its own pass — see FILE STATUS):** conduct of sale and court directions (reserve/minimum price, bid and offer rights, particulars) sit in Rule 13-5 (sale may be by contract conditional on court approval — Rule 13-5(4)(b)); the result of a sale by court order is certified in Form 60 — Rule 13-5(6); Practice Direction PD-66 governs foreclosure proceedings. Bid timing/manner under PD-66 for a specific file: get the conduct-of-sale party's written instructions for that file. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo106/loo106/168_2009_02

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How does a BC foreclosure start?** By petition under Rule 21-7(1), with all interest-holders as respondents and Form 125 served with the petition. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: How long is the redemption period?** No fixed length — the period the court fixes under Rule 21-7(5)(b). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: Is my accepted offer final?** No — conduct of sale applies under Rule 21-7(9) for confirmation, proceeds direction and vesting, even before redemption expires; until that order, the sale is not final. (Settled treatment; this slug's research held (9) UNVERIFIED in its own pass.) https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: Can someone outbid me at approval?** Treat it as a real possibility: bid/offer rights sit in the court's Rule 13-5 directions and PD-66 practice — get the specific file's bid instructions in writing before spending on inspections. The live page's sealed, unconditional competing-bid description is the page's own statement, not a verified rule in this slug's research. (§3.)

The registry for a Maple Ridge file is **UNCONFIRMED at topic level** — read the registry shown on the individual file. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
