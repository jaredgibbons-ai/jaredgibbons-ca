## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Scope is Tsawwassen, the South Delta community of the City of Delta: properties sold under the BC Supreme Court foreclosure process — by court order with conduct of sale given to a party (usually the lender's side), or heading toward a final order of foreclosure. In BC a "court-ordered sale" is a sale inside a foreclosure proceeding, not a separate process; "bank foreclosure" is a misnomer — sales run through the court, not the bank. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- Local identity note: Tsawwassen is in SOUTH Delta. SnapStats' Delta coverage is North Delta only, and North Delta figures must never be presented as Tsawwassen figures — this file is number-free on market data by design. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Step 1 — petition:** a foreclosure/redemption proceeding must be started by petition in the Supreme Court — Rule 21-7(1); all persons whose interest is sought to be extinguished must be respondents — Rule 21-7(2); the petitioner must serve the court's foreclosure information notice, Form 125 — Rule 21-7(4.1). Rule 21-7 as read states no demand step before the petition; a prescribed-mortgage-terms demand is UNVERIFIED in this slug's research. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Order / redemption:** the court may order payment of what is due within a redemption period the court fixes — Rule 21-7(5)(b) (no fixed length in the Rules); it may certify the amount due — Rule 21-7(5)(c),(e). In default, a final order of foreclosure may be granted — Rule 21-7(6). A party of record may apply at any time for an order that the property be sold — Rule 21-7(7). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Confirming a sale:** even before redemption expires, the person with conduct of sale may apply for an order confirming the sale, directing proceeds and vesting title — Rule 21-7(9). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Conduct of sale / Form 60 / PD-66 (settled treatment; this slug's research held these UNVERIFIED in its own pass — see FILE STATUS):** conduct of sale and court directions (reserve/minimum price, bid and offer rights, particulars) sit in Rule 13-5 (sale may be by contract conditional on court approval — Rule 13-5(4)(b)); the result of a sale by court order is certified in Form 60 — Rule 13-5(6); Practice Direction PD-66 governs foreclosure proceedings. Bid timing/manner under PD-66 for a specific file: get the conduct-of-sale party's written instructions for that file. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo106/loo106/168_2009_02
- **Delta city frame:** OCP = **consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future," with NO lettered schedules** — the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, and any citation of "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw No. 8400" is a conflation, flagged wherever met. Delta's zoning bylaw number is CONTESTED (City pages cite No. 7600, 2017 vs an internal title "8400, 2025") — flagged, never asserted; zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision:** Delta SSMUH Bylaws 8394 + 8395 were adopted June 24, 2024 and govern single-detached and duplex lots, not court process. Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288: its adoption is held UNCONFIRMED in this file — a January 24, 2024 City council report proposed first, second and third readings for "Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024" to repeal and replace Bylaw No. 7162, 2015, and sibling lots files in this program assert it as adopted in 2024; the conflict is flagged and no adoption date is asserted here. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Delta taxes:** due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Hazard permit areas (Tsawwassen land):** hazard DPA SD2 covers English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment) and SD3 covers the Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands, where SD3 provides a 2.9 m Geodetic Survey of Canada datum floor level, a 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation and a 7.5 m tidal setback — address-specific diligence on bluff/foreshore court-sale parcels. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **ALR (Tsawwassen-edge parcels where present):** subdivision needs ALC approval; residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, by Oct 31) — flat "$2,500+" is an ERROR. ALR status is parcel-specific on the ALC maps. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps

Do not promise a Form B package for a court-ordered bare-land strata lot until the specific file confirms what is produced. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How does a BC foreclosure start?** By petition under Rule 21-7(1), with all interest-holders as respondents and Form 125 served with the petition. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: How long is the redemption period?** No fixed length — the period the court fixes under Rule 21-7(5)(b). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: Is my accepted offer final?** No — conduct of sale applies under Rule 21-7(9) for confirmation, proceeds direction and vesting, even before redemption expires; until that order, the sale is not final. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: Can someone outbid me at approval?** Treat it as a real possibility: bid/offer rights sit in the court's Rule 13-5 directions and PD-66 practice — get the specific file's bid instructions in writing before spending on inspections. (§3.)
- **Q: Where do I check a Tsawwassen court file — and which registry holds it?** Court Services Online eSearch (class "H - Foreclosure") shows file number, registry, parties and documents. Delta has NO Supreme Court registry and the serving-registry mapping is UNVERIFIED — read the registry off the file itself. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- **Q: Will I get a Form B on a bare-land strata lot in foreclosure?** UNCONFIRMED — no source in the research states a general rule; what is produced is a per-file fact. (§3.)
- **Q: Do I pay PTT/GST the normal way on court-ordered land?** UNCONFIRMED — court-sale tax treatment is not stated in any source in the research; the general PTT and GI-003 frames in §3 are starting frames only, confirmed by your lawyer per transaction.

- **Q: As a seller in difficulty, what is my window?** The court-fixed redemption period is the window to pay what is due or sell before a final order can be granted under Rule 21-7(6) — earlier advice is worth more. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
