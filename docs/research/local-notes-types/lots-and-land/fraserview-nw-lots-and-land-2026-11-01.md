### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **Slope-street house lots (RS districts, Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001, s.310):** houses sit in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series) — RS-1 sets a minimum site area of 557.40 m² (6,000 sq. ft.) and a floor space ratio of 0.5; the RS districts allow secondary suites, and laneway / carriage houses are regulated under the same bylaw. The RS-1 minimum is a subdivision floor for RS-1 lots, not a claim about any existing lot's size; the zone and dimensions of a specific lot are confirmed parcel by parcel: UNVERIFIED for any individual lot. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Townhouse-district ground (RT family — stated correctly):** the bylaw's townhouse districts are RT-2, RT-2A, RT-2B and RT-2C (ss.410–413), with RT-3, RT-3A and RT-3B in Queensborough (ss.417–419); the RT districts regulate density, height and coverage rather than setting a minimum site area or frontage (RT-2, s.410: base density of 18 units per net acre or a floor space ratio of 0.6, height capped at 25 feet and site coverage at 40 percent). RT-A and RT-B do not exist in this bylaw. Apartment buildings sit on the Quayside / Jamieson Court waterfront edge under the RM multiple-dwelling districts. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — Our City 2041, Bylaw No. 7925 (2017):** the City's Official Community Plan governs the area, including its Development Permit Areas (Schedule B) for the mainland. Fraserview has no separate City neighbourhood plan in the sources read — its planning treatment is under the OCP mainland schedules. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 6680 (2001):** RS series (s.310) for detached lots (RS-1: minimum site area 557.40 m² / 6,000 sq. ft., FSR 0.5; secondary suites allowed; laneway / carriage houses regulated under the same bylaw); townhouse districts **RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B ONLY — RT-A/RT-B DO NOT EXIST** (this file's research states this correctly and this file affirms it); the RT districts regulate density, height and coverage rather than minimum site area or frontage (RT-2 base density 18 units per net acre or FSR 0.6, height 25 feet, coverage 40 percent); and the **836.10 m² (9,000 sq. ft.) figure in the bylaw is a density threshold in the RM multiple-dwelling districts (e.g., s.462.14), not a townhouse minimum** — any page or pitch that presents 836.10 m² as an RT figure, or names an RT-A/RT-B district, is wrong on the bylaw itself. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — VERIFIED:** New Westminster **Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw No. 7142, 2007** regulates the subdivision of lands and establishes the standard of works and services to be provided in respect of subdivisions and developments (effective March 12, 2007; consolidated September 25, 2023). All subdivisions require approval by the City's Approving Officer, and the subdivision process ensures new lots are adequately serviced with water, sewers, underground electrical, communications, street lighting, roads, sidewalks, street trees and other City requirements, meet the Zoning Bylaw's minimum lot size and frontage requirements, and are consistent with the OCP. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/7142_Subdivision2023.pdf and https://staging.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/General%20Guide%20to%20Subdivision%20Process%202012-Final.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the city states it:** the RS districts allow secondary suites, and laneway / carriage houses are regulated under Zoning Bylaw No. 6680 — added-unit potential on a Fraserview lot is read off the RS district and the bylaw's laneway/carriage provisions for the specific parcel. A separate city SSMUH bylaw citation for this ground: UNCONFIRMED in this file's research — do not price suite or carriage potential the parcel's district does not support. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Floodplain diligence — stated precisely, as the research documents it:** the City states that both Queensborough and sections of the mainland are included within the provincial floodplain, and that the city covers twelve square kilometres of which five are within the flood boundaries on the Ministry of Environment Floodplain Map; the City's floodplain study **Zone 2 covers the historically commercial and industrial Fraser River land at the west end of the city**. No floodplain zone number is assigned to Fraserview's residential slope in the research, and **lot-level flood status for a Fraserview property: UNVERIFIED — check with the City**. Do not import Queensborough's zone (Zone 1, its own file) or any other sub-area's zone into this file. http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- **Strata diligence (waterfront-edge stock):** for strata purchases on the esplanade edge, review depreciation and contingency under the provincial strata framework early; BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. The full BC strata block applies to any strata lot: Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Fraserview ALR land is identified here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot subdivision I can buy into in Fraserview NW?** No verified one — the sub-area is a built slope and waterfront edge. Land trades as RS-district house lots and built strata; no presale lot offering was verified and no development project inside the sub-area was verified in the research. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How small can an RS-1 lot be, and can I split one?** RS-1 sets a minimum site area of 557.40 m² (6,000 sq. ft.) with an FSR cap of 0.5 — a split that would produce a lot under that floor fails the district. Subdivision itself runs under Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw No. 7142, 2007, with Approving Officer approval and full servicing (water, sewers, roads, sidewalks and the rest of the City's works-and-services standard). https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/7142_Subdivision2023.pdf
- **Q: A pitch mentions "RT-A zoning" and an "836.10 m² townhouse minimum" — is that real?** No. New Westminster's townhouse districts are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B only — RT-A/RT-B do not exist — and 836.10 m² is a density threshold in the RM apartment districts, not a townhouse minimum. The RT districts regulate density, height and coverage rather than minimum site area. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Q: Is this the same market as Vancouver's Fraserview?** No — entirely different city, price points and school district (SD40 here). Fraserview NW's September 2026 market was 53 condos & townhomes listings with 10 solds against 2 detached listings with no sales; detached pricing evidence has to come from the wider New Westminster community (detached median $1,365,000), not from Fraserview detached solds, because there were none. Say "Fraserview NW" in every ad and showing note. (Section 1; SnapStats GVR September 2026 report via the research file.)
- **Q: Can a slope-street house lot add a suite or a carriage house?** The RS districts allow secondary suites, and laneway / carriage houses are regulated under Zoning Bylaw No. 6680 — potential is parcel- and district-specific; confirm the lot's RS district and the bylaw provisions before pricing it in. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Q: Is a waterfront-edge lot in the floodplain?** Sections of the mainland sit inside the provincial floodplain and five of the city's twelve square kilometres fall within the Ministry flood boundaries — but lot-level flood status for a Fraserview property is UNVERIFIED in this file. Confirm the specific property's flood position and flood construction level with the City before subjects come off. http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- **Q: How do residents reach SkyTrain from a Fraserview lot?** There is no station inside the sub-area; TransLink route 103 (Quayside / New Westminster Station) serves the waterfront, and the nearest Expo Line stations are New Westminster Station and 22nd Street Station. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax apply here?** No — the 3% Empty Homes Tax is a City of Vancouver tax only. This is New Westminster ground; no equivalent municipal vacancy tax is documented for it in this file. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Fraserview NW lot?** By exact address only — the one settled locator run in this file (1010 Hamilton Street → Lord Kelvin Elementary → Fraser River Middle → New Westminster Secondary, locator run 2026-10-21) is a single-address result and does not transfer to other streets; waterfront-edge addresses can differ.
