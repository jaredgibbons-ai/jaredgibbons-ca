## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996:** small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3, with subdivision minimums of 650 m² / 18.5 m width (R-1, R-3) and 465 m² / 12.5 m width (R-2). https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **OCP — Interim Official Community Plan, Bylaws 5511/5512, 2026, adopted April 27, 2026,** superseding the Citywide OCP No. 3479, 2001; the City states the Interim OCP streamlined the former plan and did not introduce new land-use policy except as provincial legislation required, with a future OCP update project anticipated to begin in 2027. The city-wide OCP and the Transit-Oriented Areas Update apply in this part of Coquitlam; a Canyon Springs neighbourhood plan: UNVERIFIED. Any Canyon Springs document still citing OCP No. 3479 as current is stale. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review and http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1968
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules as adopted in Coquitlam allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. Services decide density here, not just lot size. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — VERIFIED bylaw:** subdivision follows the zone minimums above, and works and services are governed by the **City of Coquitlam Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 3558, 2003** — confirmed on the City's own document portal in this program (consolidated bylaw, original adoption July 24, 2003, amendments through July 2026): the owner must grant or acquire statutory rights-of-way for the works and services needed to serve a subdivision or development, in a form acceptable to the City's solicitor, and pay for traffic-control and street-name signage installed by the City at the owner's expense before subdivision approval or building permit issue. Parcel-level servicing requirements are confirmed with the City for the specific lot. https://publicdocs.coquitlam.ca/coquitlamdoc/getdocIF.asp?doc=6328713
- **Watercourse / flood diligence:** a Watercourse Protection Development Permit can be required within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam, and Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses while s.523 covers riparian areas — check setbacks early on ravine and creek-edge lots, before pricing redevelopment value. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Bare-land strata rules (the BC strata block — stated for the form; no Canyon Springs bare-land plan is verified; the same block governs diligence on the Princess Crescent apartment stratas):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. Princess Gate's 55+ position rests on listing descriptions of a "55+ community" — the registered bylaw is confirmed on the Form B, as with any strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — sub-area ALR status UNVERIFIED here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Canyon Springs lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Canyon Springs-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in this file's research or siblings on a cited official source — none is imported or asserted here. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is VANCOUVER ONLY and does not apply in Coquitlam. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — no prohibition source is cited in the research, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Coquitlam property taxes and utilities (documented in this file's research):** City of Coquitlam property tax payment was due July 2, 2026, covering January 1 through December 31, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance of current taxes after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants). - **Q: Can a Canyon Springs house lot add units?** Yes under the City's SSMUH rules — up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage / laneway homes — but lots not connected to municipal water or sewer fall outside the rules, so confirm services and the exact zone (R-1 / R-2 / R-3) before advertising unit potential. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Q: Is a Canyon Springs lot serviced?** The City's framework assumes municipal water and sewer for SSMUH eligibility and bills water, sewer/drainage and solid waste as City utilities — but parcel-level septic or well status is UNVERIFIED in the research, so confirm servicing for the specific lot with the City before writing. - **Q: What do the watercourse rules mean for a ravine-edge lot?** A Watercourse Protection Development Permit can be required within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam, and Zoning Bylaw s.519 / s.523 set flood and riparian setbacks — run that check before pricing any redevelopment value on a creek-edge lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
