### Reserve side — leasehold tenure record, never land stock

- **Capilano IR No. 5 lands under the Park Royal Lease (indenture dated April 1, 1974):** part of the shopping centre — and, per the research, leasehold residential interests to the south — sit on Squamish Nation land held under that lease. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Tenure check first (the Park Royal diligence order):** confirm whether the property is freehold municipal land or a leasehold interest on Capilano IR No. 5, and if leasehold, get the head-lease terms, remaining term and any prepaid/rent-reset structure before pricing or financing discussions. Only after tenure is settled do the District rules below apply, and they apply only on the District side. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do
- **OCP — District of West Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985 (2018):** the OCP classifies Park Royal as West Vancouver's regional shopping centre, the Park Royal Shopping Centre lands are a named Form & Character Development Permit area in it, and it was amended again December 1, 2025 (OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 5406, 2025, aligning it with the 2024 Interim Housing Needs Report) — cite the current consolidated OCP, not the 2018 original. On Capilano IR No. 5, the Squamish Nation controls zoning under the Park Royal Lease arrangements — the District OCP does not govern there. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/housing/official-community-plan-housing-policy-update
- **Zoning — District Zoning Bylaw No. 4662 (2010), District side only:** secondary suites and coach houses are both permitted in the District and require building permits, Zoning Bylaw s. 130 and BC Building Code compliance, and a business licence to operate as rentals (per the siblings). A specific lot's zone and minimum site area: confirm on the District Zoning Map — no zone minimum for a Park Royal lot is stated in this file's research, and none is imported from another West Vancouver area's file. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/development-regulations/single-family-lots
- **Density in active transition — the Park Royal–Taylor Way directive:** on July 21, 2025, the Province issued ministerial directives requiring West Vancouver to amend its OCP to increase density in the Park Royal–Taylor Way area (with defined minimum densities) and in single-family/duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave, and to adopt the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan — all by December 31, 2025. The District missed that deadline on all three directives and delivered only 58 of its 220-unit year-one housing target; a provincial order that took effect April 7, 2026 amended the District's bylaw to implement the neighbouring Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan, while the Park Royal–Taylor Way density directive and the apartment-area expansion remain outstanding, with the District required to file progress reports with the Province every three months through 2026 and 2027. The area's zoning is in active transition — treat any density-based value claim for a specific parcel as UNCONFIRMED until an adopted bylaw names it, and verify the current designation for the property. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698 and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0038-000377
- **Subdivision + servicing — District number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** no District of West Vancouver subdivision/servicing bylaw number is confirmed on a primary source in this file's research, so none is quoted and none is invented. The documented process instead: subdivision runs against the lot's zone (District Zoning Map), servicing for District lots is District water/sewer metered and billed quarterly, and the development-permit frame below gates centre, foreshore and environmental work. Confirm requirements with the District for the parcel — and, on a reserve parcel, with the Nation's process, not the District's. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Hazard and form-and-character development permits:** the Park Royal Shopping Centre lands are a named Form & Character Development Permit area in the OCP, and foreshore and environmental DP rules apply along the waterfront and watercourses — the District's hazard/foreshore DP frame for this ground. Confirm whether a Foreshore Development Permit area applies to a waterfront-adjacent lot before promising renovation or subdivision scope. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Park Royal ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no flat farm-class figure is asserted for this slug because this file's own research does not document one. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, where met — none verified on this ground; the block governs a District-side freehold strata purchase):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. A fee-simple lot carries none of this, and a leasehold purchase has no equivalent freehold Form B frame — the lease documents govern alongside any strata documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **District property taxes + additional school tax:** municipal-side homes pay District of West Vancouver taxes — the 2026 payment was due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026; roughly half of the tax bill is collected by the District for other taxing authorities, and water/sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. The Province's additional school tax also bites at West Vancouver values: 0.2% on the residential portion assessed between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. Homes on reserve land are under a different tax/tenure regime — UNCONFIRMED per title. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Property transfer tax — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,800,000 District-side freehold purchase, fully residential, no exemption — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,800,000 ($32,000) = **$34,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000). Note: PTT treatment of a reserve-land leasehold differs from a freehold purchase — confirm with the buyer's lawyer/notary where tenure is leasehold. No $78,000 figure for $3.5M was met in this slug's research or siblings checked; where it appears elsewhere in the program it is an OPEN reconciliation item — follow $93,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED — and no vacant-land offering is verified at Park Royal in any event. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
