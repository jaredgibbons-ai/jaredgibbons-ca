## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Surrey OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current.** Bylaw 18020 is the replaced predecessor only, never cited as current. White Rock lots answer to White Rock's own OCP Bylaw No. 2220 (2017) instead — neither municipality's plan substitutes for the other. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000; White Rock Zoning Bylaw No. 2506 (adopted Jun 27, 2024).** Bylaw 2506 incorporates SSMUH (up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 over 280 m², up to 6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop). https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html
- **SSMUH (Surrey, in force Jul 8, 2024):** inside the urban containment boundary, on lots under 4,050 m² — 3 units on a lot of 280 m² or less, 4 units on a larger lot, up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; a triplex/fourplex (houseplex) is allowed only in the R3 zone, on a lot with a lane or two road frontages, with no secondary suite (houseplex detail is page copy in the research pass). Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey):** only one suite per dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite, and never a separate strata lot. A coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA/R2/R2-O/R3 lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision + servicing (Surrey):** the Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 is the City's subdivision and development (servicing) bylaw, verified on the City's own pages in this program. The page's subdivision account — planner review, a Preliminary Layout Approval (PLA) letter, 12 months to meet conditions (extension for a fee), conditions including road dedication, covenants, tree permits and DCCs, and a surveyor's final plan to the Land Title Office — is site copy: UNVERIFIED beyond site copy, because no City subdivision page was opened in this slug's research pass. Servicing: the page states a new lot on a well or septic field needs Fraser Health testing before an application can proceed, and some acreage land is unserviced — parcel-level servicing: UNVERIFIED. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement and https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html
- **Streamside / riparian (Surrey):** streamside setbacks are defined in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, varying by stream class — no single riparian figure is ever stated for Surrey land generally; COSMOS shows environmental or riparian permit areas per parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only — only the property owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, business licence $358 per year. Conflict disclosed: the City's 2024 corporate report proposing the STR amendments put the licence at $350/yr plus fees — $358 is carried, $350 disclosed. White Rock's STR rule: UNVERIFIED in this pass. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **ALR block, in full:** subdivision of ALR land requires ALC permission, apart from limited approving-officer cases in the ALR General Regulation. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). A principal residence is 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted at 90 m² or less (parcels of 40 ha or smaller) or 186 m² or less (larger parcels), where local bylaws also permit it. **Farm class — three-tier, in full:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for Sea Ridge Bays and any bare-land strata lot):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with fees, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, rules, budget and most recent depreciation report. Depreciation reports every 5 years for 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024. CRF minimum 10% since Nov 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted). Only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked anchors: $500K → $8,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer); $1.5M → $28,000. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, max saving $8,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, out at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level ONLY:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt. The research pass opened no CRA material, so anything finer is UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; a March 12 date in a secondary source is noted in-line in the siblings, never silently overwritten — CRA governs; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. **Foreclosure geography:** Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry — no foreclosure angle is documented in this slug's research. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many lots are for sale in South Surrey right now, and what is the median lot price?** UNVERIFIED as statistics — SnapStats publishes no lots/land category for S Surrey White Rock, so no land count or median exists in the source data. Price land off land sales, not house medians. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/south-surrey-lots-land.md)
- **Q: How do I find a lot's zone?** On the Surrey side, on the City's COSMOS map against Zoning By-law No. 12000; White Rock lots sit under Zoning Bylaw No. 2506. The zone on the specific lot decides which unit counts and suite rules even apply. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html
- **Q: How many homes can go on a South Surrey lot?** In Surrey, inside the urban containment boundary, lots under 4,050 m² may hold 3 units (280 m² or less) or 4 units (larger), with up to 6 for some lots within 400 m of frequent bus service; White Rock's Bylaw 2506 carries the same 3 / 4 / 6 tiers. (§3 above)
- **Q: Surrey lot or White Rock lot — does it matter which side of the line?** Yes: a White Rock lot answers to Bylaw No. 2506 and White Rock's OCP Bylaw No. 2220; a Surrey lot to Bylaw No. 12000, COSMOS and the Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 — minimum-lot, unit-count and suite answers differ across the seam. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same as a freehold lot?** No. A bare-land strata lot is a strata lot (Form B, fees, bylaws) whose lot is essentially the building lot itself — Sea Ridge Bays is the South Surrey case. A fee-simple lot has no strata at all. (§2 above)
- **Q: Can I stratify an ALR acreage and sell the homes separately?** Not without ALC permission: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)), and the ALC caps the program at one principal residence (≤500 m², suite inside it only) plus at most one additional residence (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: A card says "serviced lot" and "subdivision potential" — can I rely on that?** No. Both phrases are listing copy, UNVERIFIED at parcel level. The checks are City servicing records, Fraser Health testing where a well/septic is involved, and the PLA condition list. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html
- **Q: What if the lot has no city sewer or water?** The page's copy states a new lot on a well or septic field needs Fraser Health testing before a subdivision can proceed, and SSMUH exemptions include properties not connected to community water and sewer — confirm services with the City and Fraser Health before writing. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a vacant lot?** At the CRA GI-003 level only: developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable; an individual's personal-use sale is generally exempt. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED — confirm with an accountant per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Are Harlowe, Calais or Rockford lot offerings?** No — they are built-form presales, explicitly NOT 55+, counted nowhere as land. A presale buys a future built home under a developer contract, not a vacant lot. (§2 above)
