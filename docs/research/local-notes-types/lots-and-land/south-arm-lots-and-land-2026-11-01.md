## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- South Arm is the City of Richmond's south-central residential district along Williams Road, between the Steveston side of the city and No. 4 Road, fronting the South Arm of the Fraser River along the city's south edge; the name refers to that river channel. "South Arm" is the Greater Vancouver REALTORS® (GVR) board sub-area name, and the City itself uses "South Arm" officially as a recreation area name — the City's park listing for South Arm Community Park gives its recreation area as "South Arm." The board's exact boundary for the sub-area is not published on an official source and is UNVERIFIED. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=50 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
- Where the housing is: single-detached houses in the RS1/RS2 zones around the South Arm Community Park and school cluster — freehold detached housing is the district's documented base form. (Research file above.)
- Landmarks: South Arm Community Park, 8680 Williams Road — a 57.3-acre community park at Williams Road and Garden City Road with 10 sports fields, 9 baseball diamonds, 4 basketball courts, 8 tennis courts, 3 pickleball courts and a large playground (playground officially reopened July 2023) — adjacent to South Arm Community Centre (8880 Williams Road) and to Hugh McRoberts Secondary and James Whiteside Elementary schools. South Arm Outdoor Pool is at 10100 South Arm Place and South Arm Community Hall is at 9020 Williams Road. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=50 and https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/buildings/buildings.htm?PageMode=HTML
- Main roads: Williams Road runs east–west through the district, with Garden City Road, No. 3 Road and No. 4 Road running north to the city centre and Steveston Highway connecting west Richmond to Highway 99. There is no SkyTrain station in the district — Canada Line access is by bus to Richmond–Brighouse Station. Transit route numbers serving Williams Road: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
- Schools (SD38, Ministry-listed names): Hugh McRoberts Secondary and James Whiteside Elementary sit beside South Arm Community Park; at the west end of the Williams Road corridor, Steveston-London Secondary School, 6600 Williams Road, is a Grades 8–12 school of 1,250 students, formed from the merger of Steveston Secondary and Charles E. London Secondary. SD38 catchments are address-based and the district has no middle-school tier; no SD38 locator run for a South Arm address is documented in this file's research, so catchments per address are UNVERIFIED — run the exact address. https://slss.sd38.bc.ca/school-information/about-us and https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- Nearby areas: Steveston and London/Steveston (west), Broadmoor (north), Bridgeport and East Richmond (east/north-east), and Ladner in Delta across the South Arm (via Highway 99 and the George Massey Tunnel). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)

### Fee-simple lots — the district's documented base form

- **Zones and lot minimums, as Bylaw 8500 states them:** houses sit in Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m² and a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones. Which subzone a given Williams Road lot carries is a per-parcel check — the subzone, not the district average (none is published), decides whether a lot can split or take an added unit. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **City plans and zoning:** Richmond Zoning Bylaw **8500** governs; the Official Community Plan is OCP Bylaw **9000** — no separate South Arm area plan is confirmed, so the district sits in the OCP 9000 framework rather than an area plan of its own. Houses sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with the subzone minimum lot areas and suite rules stated in §2. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **SSMUH / added units, as the City states it:** the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots; a secondary suite under Section 5.4 is a minimum of 33.0 m² and a maximum of the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m² (the 40% suite cap is Richmond's stated position here — no other city's suite cap is imported). https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Subdivision + servicing — VERIFIED for Richmond:** the City's own alphabetical bylaw list carries "Subdivision and Development — Bylaw 8751 (2011)" and "Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 (2011)" — Richmond's subdivision/servicing instrument is Bylaw 8751, with cost recovery under Bylaw 8752; parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **Flood rules — Bylaw 8204:** the district is on Lulu Island in the Fraser River floodplain along the South Arm channel, behind the City perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations, designed for a 1:500 flood event with dikes being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres; the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements — confirm both for any Williams Road lot before pricing in a rebuild or addition. No other city's floodplain zone is imported here. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no South Arm ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and it is not asserted for this ground. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata frame (for the district's built strata only — no bare-land strata is verified here):** Form B under SPA s. 59; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for strata corporations with 5 or more lots since July 1, 2024 (Kingz Court's published 22 units in its home file put it over that threshold); at least 10% of the annual operating budget to the CRF since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw, and the 55-plus sibling verifies zero 55+ ownership complexes in this district. Covenant Court (9260 No. 2 Road, listing-stated 55+, registered wording UNCONFIRMED) is counted in NO total in any program and is not South Arm stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Richmond property taxes (city-wide rule):** due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. No ADEU charge applies on this ground — Alexandra District Energy Utility charges are a West Cambie / Alexandra carrying cost only, never a South Arm one. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $900,000 purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $700,000 ($14,000) = **$16,000**. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in South Arm?** No verified bare-land offering exists. What trades is detached houses on fee-simple RS1/RS2 lots around the park and school cluster. (§2 above)
- **Q: What decides whether my lot can split or take more units?** Its subzone. RS1/RS2 minimum lot areas run from RS1/K 315.0 m² to RS1/F 828.0 m², with a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones — and the City's Bill 44 position is 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots, plus at least one suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot. Read the specific lot's subzone in Bylaw 8500, Section 8.1, before pricing any of it. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: How big can a suite be on a South Arm lot?** Under Section 5.4: a minimum of 33.0 m² and a maximum of the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m². Check an existing suite against that before valuing it. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: Is Mandalay a South Arm land or townhouse opportunity?** No — on fresh listing evidence Mandalay by Cressey (9371/9373 Hemlock Drive) is a concrete apartment-condo building in McLennan North. This area's research file carried it as a townhome complex on a property-manager source; the townhomes sibling corrected it, this file follows that correction, and Mandalay is documented as South Arm land or stock nowhere here. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is Kingz Court a land development I can buy a lot in?** No — Kingz Court (8233 Williams Road) is a completed 2026 strata townhouse complex, counted in the townhomes sibling. A purchase there is a strata lot purchase (Form B diligence), not a land purchase. (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/south-arm-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: What flood diligence applies on a Williams Road lot?** The district fronts the South Arm channel in the Fraser River floodplain behind a 1:500-design dike system (dikes being raised 3.5 m to 4.7 m); Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction levels for new building — confirm both for the specific lot before subjects come off, especially for a dike-side lot. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Q: Which schools serve a Williams Road address?** Hugh McRoberts Secondary and James Whiteside Elementary sit beside South Arm Community Park and Steveston-London Secondary is at the corridor's west end — but SD38 catchments are address-based and no locator run for a South Arm address is documented in this file's research, so run the exact address in the SD38 locator before relying on a school. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Q: Is there any bare-land strata in South Arm?** None verified. If a lot were bare-land strata, the lot itself would carry Form B, strata fees and bylaws — a different purchase from a fee-simple RS1/RS2 lot. (§2 above)
- **Q: Will GST apply if a developer sells me a newly subdivided lot?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). No such lot offering is verified in South Arm today. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
