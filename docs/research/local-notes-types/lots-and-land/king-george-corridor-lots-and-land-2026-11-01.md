## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No meaningful bare-land subdivision market is verified in this corridor — stated plainly at the top. No named subdivision selling serviced vacant lots to individual builders was verified on an official or developer source in this research, and no lot count, lot size or lot price is stated. What exists as land here is the corridor's existing lot fabric: fee-simple house lots on the side streets behind the boulevard, boulevard-fronting lots whose value question is added units, manufactured-home community ground, and the floodplain lots at the Nicomekl north end. The corridor's built stock — 10 apartment buildings in the condos program, Peninsula Village and Keystone in the townhomes file — is cross-referenced only and is never land inventory here.**

- **Lot fabric, as the research states it:** detached houses on side streets off the boulevard; low-rise condos and townhomes near the Semiahmoo Town Centre south end; duplex and fourplex-style homes fronting the boulevard; and manufactured homes in the corridor's South Surrey communities (several feed cards are manufactured-home addresses on 160 Street and Cranley Drive that also appear in the surrey-mobile-homes feed). The plan framework concentrates multiple-residential and commercial designations at the town-centre south end and along the boulevard, with lower-density residential behind it. https://jaredgibbons.ca/king-george-corridor.html and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south
- **Fee-simple lots are the default form:** a side-street house lot here is fee-simple — no strata corporation, no Form B, no strata fees; diligence is title, the parcel's zone and designation in COSMOS, and the floodplain check at the north end. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current.** The corridor's own 1995 concept-plan designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update (via Corporate Report R246, endorsed Dec 2, 2024); Bylaw No. 18020 is the replaced predecessor and is never cited as current. The Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan covers the south end (16–24 Avenues) per the live page. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000** — every Surrey property carries a zone under it, shown in the City's COSMOS map; zone minimum subdivision lot areas come from the consolidated bylaw text and are confirmed parcel by parcel in COSMOS. No corridor lot-size table is stated in the research — UNVERIFIED as a table here. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units (Surrey framework, adopted Jul 8, 2024):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, applying to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. Homes fronting the boulevard's frequent bus service may fall inside a Frequent Bus Stop Area — check the parcel in COSMOS. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide and does not create new lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites:** Surrey permits only one suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite, and a suite is not a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision / servicing:** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, confirmed per parcel in COSMOS (which also shows water/sanitary/storm mains, road dedications and environmental or riparian permit areas). The research files for this slug state no servicing-bylaw number; verified on the City's own Land Development pages in this pass: the **Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw, and the City's process runs through its Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process — read the parcel's servicing position off the City's process, never off another city's bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Floodplain at the north end:** the corridor's north edge is the Nicomekl River, and Surrey's coastal floodplain runs along the Nicomekl and Serpentine Rivers and the Serpentine–Nicomekl lowlands. Parcel-level flood levels: UNVERIFIED — confirmed with the City per parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **Streamside / riparian (Surrey-wide frame, for any creek-side corridor parcel):** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, with different setbacks by stream class, complemented by the Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit process — the setback for a specific parcel is read off Part 7A and COSMOS, never assumed from a single figure. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only — only the property owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence is required at **$358 per year** on the City's program page. **Licence-figure conflict, disclosed:** the City's 2024 corporate report on the STR bylaw amendments proposed $350/yr plus fees — this file carries the $358 program figure with the $350 proposal disclosed, not hidden. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R094.pdf
- **ALR block (provincial frame — no corridor ALR parcel is documented in the research, stated so it is not misapplied):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for this corridor. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot, wherever one surfaces):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** $700,000 lot, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. **Foreclosure:** Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry — a Surrey foreclosure runs through a registry outside the city; the registry mapping is UNVERIFIED here and no foreclosure angle is documented in this slug's research. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition: UNCONFIRMED in this file** — no primary text was opened for this slug, so no position is stated or conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is "King George Corridor" land the whole boulevard, including Whalley?** No. On this page, in the City plan and in the board data it is the south segment only — 8 Avenue to the Nicomekl River in South Surrey. A Newton or City Centre parcel on the same road is a different sub-area with different numbers. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant building lot in the corridor?** No named bare-land subdivision selling serviced lots was verified here. What trades as land is the existing fabric — side-street house lots, boulevard-fronting lots priced on added-unit potential, and teardown houses at land value — each checked parcel by parcel in COSMOS. (§2 above)
- **Q: Which plan sets what my lot can become?** The Surrey 2050 OCP (Bylaw No. 21900, adopted Jun 15, 2026). The corridor's 1995 concept-plan designations were superseded by the OCP update carried through Corporate Report R246 (endorsed Dec 2, 2024) — check the parcel's current designation and zone, not the 1995 plan. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can a house lot here add units?** Under SSMUH, most eligible lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary carry 3 units (under 280 m²) or 4 units (280 m² or more), and eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop can carry up to 6 — boulevard-adjacent lots may qualify. That is added-unit permission, not subdivision. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Can I subdivide a corridor lot?** Only if each new lot meets its zone's minimum subdivision lot area in Bylaw No. 12000 and the subdivision/servicing requirements under the Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 — confirmed with the City for the specific parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Q: What flood check applies at the north end?** Parcels near the Nicomekl River sit in Surrey's coastal floodplain system (Nicomekl / Serpentine lowlands); flood-construction levels are parcel-specific and are confirmed with the City before writing. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same as a freehold lot?** No. A bare-land strata lot is strata tenure — Form B, strata fees and bylaws apply to the lot itself. No named bare-land strata plan in this corridor was verified in this research, so read tenure off title per parcel. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a $700,000 lot?** PTT $12,000 with no exemption ($2,000 at 1% + $10,000 at 2%). GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Does the planned BRT make my lot worth more today?** The BRT is in full design — approximately 19 km, 12 stations, City Centre to Semiahmoo Town Centre, peak service every five minutes — but station locations and construction timing for a specific corridor address were not confirmed in this research: price it as a plan, not a service. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/council-approves-next-stage-of-king-george-boulevard-brt
- **Q: Which school will my built home feed into?** UNVERIFIED for any corridor address — SD36 catchments are address-based, the Ministry-listed schools in §1 are the nearby names only, and no locator run for a corridor address is on record. Run the exact address in the SD36 locator before relying on a school. (§1 above)
