## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** District of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw 3210 (as amended by Bylaw 8698, June 2024) governs: a main house, secondary suite and coach house are allowed on most single-family lots, and the December 2025 Ground-Oriented Housing rules permit up to four homes on more than 4,000 eligible District properties. The duplex is inside that multi-unit permission structure; the exact position for a specific Lynnmour lot (zone, hazard development-permit area, servicing) is UNCONFIRMED until read per lot. The District's OCP is Bylaw 7900. City of North Vancouver bylaws never apply east of Lynn Creek. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/new-provincial-housing-legislation-fact-sheet.pdf (Research file above.)
- **SSMUH position.** The District's response to the provincial housing legislation runs through the instruments above (Zoning 3210 as amended by 8698; the December 2025 ground-oriented rules; Transit-Oriented Area designations under Bylaw 8679, 2024 for Phibbs Exchange and the Capilano University area). No separate District SSMUH bylaw number is asserted in this file. (Research file above; District council report on SSMUH approaches: http://docs.dnv.org/documents/3-1-multiunit-housing.pdf)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — District frame.** The District's zoning allows a secondary suite alongside a main house on most single-family lots (§3 above); no District suite-size cap is stated in this slug's research, so none is asserted — other cities' suite caps are never borrowed into a District of North Vancouver file. (Research file above; program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **STRATA block — stated for completeness; applies to a strata duplex wherever one is met.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists: Form B diligence applies in full (Strata Property Act s.59 — the Form B states strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. **None of this block applies to Edgewater Estates** — it is undivided interest, not strata, and has no Form B at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate (Strata frame as carried in ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/lynnmour-condos-2026-11-01.md.)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. These are tier illustrations only — no verified Lynnmour price figure exists to use as an input (no SnapStats for North Vancouver), and none is invented. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer; an undivided-interest purchase (Edgewater) is a transfer of an interest in land and its PTT position follows that interest — confirm with the buyer's lawyer, as no source in this program prices it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any half duplex for sale in Lynnmour?** None verified by name in any source in this program. - **Q: Is Edgewater Estates a cheap strata duplex or condo on Premier Street?** No — it is a 131-unit undivided-interest complex: no strata lot, no strata corporation, no Form B, and its monthly fee bundles property taxes and heat. Price and finance it as co-ownership, never as strata. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Lynnmour in the City or District of North Vancouver?** The District — OCP Bylaw 7900 and Zoning Bylaw 3210 govern; City bylaws never apply east of Lynn Creek. (§1, §3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to a Lynnmour house?** The District's zoning allows a main house, secondary suite and coach house on most single-family lots, and the December 2025 ground-oriented rules permit up to four homes on more than 4,000 eligible District properties — confirm the specific lot's eligibility and any hazard development-permit area first. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Which secondary school serves a Lynnmour address?** Windsor Secondary — settled on the documented locator run (1491 Hunter Street → Lynnmour Elementary / Windsor Secondary), NOT Sutherland.
