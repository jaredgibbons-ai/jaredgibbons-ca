## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. In the Township, a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata, never a half duplex. A home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership at all — the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing; settled Township frame.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan (Township frame):** the Fort Langley Community Plan, Bylaw No. 2527 (adopted October 26, 1987, as an amendment to the Township Official Community Plan), is the governing plan for the village, sitting under the OCP as updated by Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025). The downtown-core Heritage Conservation Area requires new development and alterations to complement and enhance the area's heritage buildings and character; work that is not exempt requires approval under the heritage rules, and the plan's exemptions include minor additions under 50 m² that do not front a road (other than a lane) and conform to the guidelines. The Development Permit Area (Bylaw 3204, 1993) and the Fort Langley Design Guidelines (Bylaw 5063, 2014) sit on top of normal zoning in the village core. (Research file, §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx)
- **Zoning, SSMUH and suites (Township frame — SETTLED):** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs. SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) allows **3 units on eligible lots of 280 m² or smaller and 4 units on larger eligible lots up to 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible, and NO 6-unit tier applies in the Township** — duplexes are the baseline SSMUH form, and in the village SSMUH operates inside the heritage and design controls above, not as an exemption from them. A secondary suite in the Township is capped at **120 m²** — the Township's own cap, never borrowed. A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata. Servicing: **Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 is CONFIRMED** — the position that governs in this file, against the guide lots sibling's UNCONFIRMED servicing stance flagged in FILE STATUS. On ALR land, the ALC frame also applies: one principal residence and, subject to conditions in the ALR Use Regulation, one additional residence limited to 90 m² or less on parcels 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha. (Research file, §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **Wells and septic (acreage edges):** well reports are due within 90 days of constructing, altering or decommissioning a well, and recorded wells can be searched in the Province's GWELLS system; onsite sewage follows Fraser Health filing rules through an authorized person, with a record of sewerage system before construction and a letter of certification after. (Research file, §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers; https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Township ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a Bedford Landing row home a half duplex?** No. The row homes are recorded as Row House (Non-Strata) — complete dwellings on their own fee-simple lots, with no strata corporation, no Form B and no strata fee. A half duplex is one half of a two-unit building sold separately, either as a 2-lot strata or on a subdivided fee-simple lot; the row-home record is neither. (§2.)
- **Q: How many units can I put on a Fort Langley lot?** Under SSMUH Bylaw 6020: 3 units on an eligible lot of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on a larger eligible lot up to 4,050 m²; over 4,050 m² is ineligible, and there is no 6-plex tier in the Township. In the village core the Heritage Conservation Area, Development Permit Area and Design Guidelines still apply on top. A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102, never a separately saleable strata half. (§3.)
- **Q: Do I need a heritage permit to renovate a house in the village?** If the property is in the downtown-core Heritage Conservation Area, alterations that are not exempt must follow the heritage rules — the plan's purpose is to protect the downtown's heritage character, and its exemptions cover items like minor additions under 50 m² that do not front a road and conform to the guidelines. Confirm the property's status with the Township before removing subjects. (§3; research file, §6.)
- **Q: How did Fort Langley sell in September 2026?** Detached: 19 listings, 3 sold, 16% ratio. Condos & Townhomes: 9 listings, 1 sold, 11% ratio. No Fort Langley-only median is published; the combined Langley medians are community context only, and duplexes have no published category. (§1.)
- **Q: What should I ask about flooding before buying a duplex here?** The village is low-lying by the Fraser and Salmon Rivers; the Township says the Salmon River Pump Station provides flood protection and its flood box gates close when Fraser River levels rise. Parcel-level floodplain mapping is UNVERIFIED in this program — get the parcel's flood information from the Township before purchase. (§§2–3.)
