## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. Which of the two legal forms — if either — a given half-duplex card represents is a title question: read the title before assuming strata or fee-simple tenure. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`.)

- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Financing tiers inside this band (CMHC): under $500,000 a flat 5% down; $500,000–$1,499,999, 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% above it; at $1,500,000 or more, 20% down and mortgage loan insurance not available; CMHC premium range 0.6%–4.5%. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax ; https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
