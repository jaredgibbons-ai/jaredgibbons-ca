## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **ALR frame first — it governs the feed's acreage ground.** The visible cards are dominated by farm and acreage parcels (blueberry farms of 9–57 acres, a 48.28-acre parcel, a 21.46-acre farm, in listing text). On ALR parcels the ALC housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), with any secondary suite inside the principal residence, and subdivision of ALR land requires Agricultural Land Commission permission outside limited regulation exceptions. Which feed parcels are actually in the ALR is UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel on ALC mapping; listing text is not an ALR determination. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/serpentine.md; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/)
- **Zoning and SSMUH (Surrey frame, urban lots only — not ALR parcels).** Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 governs. Surrey's zoning allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Duplexes are the baseline form under that framework. Which zone permits a two-unit form on a given parcel is a parcel-by-parcel read of Bylaw 12000 and the parcel's zone in COSMOS — no zone-by-zone table is asserted here. (Research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (Surrey's own rule, never borrowed).** Only one secondary suite is permitted per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. Coach houses and garden suites: all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow one; 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access, and one extra parking space is required. Urban lots only — on ALR parcels any suite sits inside the principal residence under the ALC frame above. (Research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites)
- **City plan and servicing (Surrey frame).** The current plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 appears in this slug's siblings ONLY as the replaced predecessor, never as current. Servicing is under Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw 8830 (1986), VERIFIED from City pages. Streamside setbacks are in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is stated. Servicing claims on feed cards (city water, wells, irrigation/ditch water, services "at lot line") are listing text only — UNVERIFIED, verified parcel by parcel. (Research file above; siblings listed in the FILE STATUS block.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; ask what reports the strata holds anyway. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process (and, on ALR land, ALC permission outside limited exceptions, above); no finer detail is stated. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Taxes and rentals (Surrey frame).** Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. A short-term rental (under 90 consecutive days per patron) may be hosted only by an owner living at the property as their primary residence, with a business licence at $358/yr — carried with the City's 2024 $350/yr proposal disclosed. Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED) — a feed card's court-ordered-sale claim is listing text, confirmed against court records, never against a Surrey registry that does not exist in the ledger. (Research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (for transfers where PTT applies).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Farm-class and other parcel-specific PTT positions are a title-and-assessment check, not a page figure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate, and bare land itself is not new housing. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
