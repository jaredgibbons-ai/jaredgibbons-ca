## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**FORM CLASS ONLY — no named duplex stock is verified on this ground:**

- **The farm belt form — which is NOT duplex stock.** The dominant form is the farm house on agricultural land: the A1 Agriculture zone provides one farm house per lot, a farm house maximum of 500 m², a farm home plate maximum of 3,600 m², one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit (maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less, or 186 m² on lots over 40 ha). Those are ALR farm-residence forms on a single farm parcel under one title — a suite inside the farm house and an accessory farm dwelling are not a duplex, are not sold separately, and are never counted as duplex or half-duplex stock here. ALR residences cannot be stratified, per this slug's acreages sibling, so a strata half duplex is not this ground's farm form. (Research file above; https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf — zoning number contested, see §3; acreages sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/acreages/east-delta-acreages-2026-11-01.md; ALR housing: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** On the farm belt, the A1/A2/A3 agriculture zones govern — A1: subdivision only where it complies with the OCP and has Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, minimum lot size created by subdivision 8 ha (home site severance exception under ALC policy); A2 (s. 10.20.6): 8 ha minimum created by subdivision; A3 (s. 10.30.6): subdivision not permitted. On the urban edge, parcels take the RS and RD zones parcel by parcel. The zone of a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until read off the zoning maps. (Research file above; https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf; https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A2.pdf)
- **ALR housing caps — the farm second-dwelling rule, as the ALC states it.** The Agricultural Land Commission generally allows no more than one residence per ALR parcel; a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence if local bylaws allow it; and one additional detached residence is permitted up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha — anything beyond that needs ALC approval. (Research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **SSMUH position.** Delta's SSMUH zoning amendments (Bylaws 8394 + 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 per this slug's siblings) allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — urban-edge context, not a farm-belt rule; on ALR parcels the ALC caps above govern residences. A duplex is the baseline two-unit form; SSMUH does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Townhomes sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/east-delta-townhomes-2026-11-01.md; https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Delta's own rule as its sources state it.** No Delta percentage or size cap for a suite inside an urban duplex is stated in this slug's research or siblings, so no figure is borrowed from another city and the cap is UNCONFIRMED here. What Delta's own sources state: the RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use to a single detached dwelling, and in A1 the one secondary suite sits inside the farm house (above). (Research file above; hawthorne research on RS1: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/hawthorne.md)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the municipality's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums (on farm land, the A1 8 ha minimum and ALC position above); no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate, and the rebate does not apply to bare farm land itself. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a farm house with a suite and an accessory farm dwelling a duplex I can buy half of?** No. Those are ALR farm-residence forms on one farm parcel under one title (§2/§3) — the parcel sells whole, and ALR residences cannot be stratified per the acreages sibling. (§2.)
- **Q: Is there any named duplex development in East Delta?** None is verified in this slug's research or siblings — §2 is the form class only (individual duplexes on single urban-edge lots under the RD zoning of their day). (§2.)
- **Q: Can a farm parcel add a second detached residence?** Only within the ALC caps: one additional detached residence up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or 186 m² on larger parcels; beyond that needs ALC approval. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me split a duplex into two titles?** No — SSMUH adds unit potential on a lot; a separately titled half needs a strata plan or a subdivision, and on farm land the ALR position governs first. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for East Delta?** No — SnapStats does not cover South Delta at all, and duplexes have no separate published category in this program's sources. No market figure is stated in this file. (§1.)
