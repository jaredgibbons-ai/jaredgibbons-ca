## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

(Research file above; https://jaredgibbons.ca/our-listings.html, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Who owns the system and who may use it.** CREA, a trade association, owns the trademarks Multiple Listing Service® ("MLS®") and REALTOR®, and comprises over 100,000 real estate brokers and agents across 100 local real estate boards and associations across Canada. Agents and brokers must become members of a local real estate board and CREA to access the MLS® system — described by the Competition Bureau as a comprehensive database of current and historical listing information crucial to residential real estate brokerage services — and must adhere to CREA's rules, such as the REALTOR® Code, and their local boards' rules. The October 2010 consent agreement between the Commissioner of Competition and CREA (registered with the Competition Tribunal for a 10-year period) encompassed, among other areas, where a seller's information can be displayed; it is cited as the documented federal milestone on MLS® display governance, not as a current BC display rule — its term as described has run and what followed it is UNVERIFIED. (Research file above; https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/how-we-foster-competition/promotion-and-advocacy/regulatory-adviceinterventions-competition-bureau/submission-ftc-and-doj-residential-real-estate-brokerage, read 2026-11-01.)
- **The BC regulator's rules that apply to every listing on this page.** In BC, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); a licensed professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party. Disclosure must happen at the outset of dealings, and dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. Practical consequence: a buyer who clicks "Schedule viewing / Email" on one of Jared's listings while unrepresented is, in BCFSA's framework, a non-client dealing with the seller's professional until a client relationship is formed. (Research file above; https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Where duplexes are permitted.** This topic has no ground, so no zoning position is carried here: whether a duplex or an additional unit is permitted on a specific lot is read in that property's home area file against its municipality's current zoning bylaw. No city's rule is borrowed into this file. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **STRATA block — applies where a listing on this page is a strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full on an ordinary purchase. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the municipality's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles; a feed card does not answer either question. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (the frame any purchase from this page is priced against).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
