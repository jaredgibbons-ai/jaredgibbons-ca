## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **The farm-residence form, exactly as the ALR sources support it:** a Surrey farm parcel in the ALR carries one principal residence per parcel, permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction. A secondary suite is permitted in the ALR only inside the principal residence — a separate or detached dwelling cannot be used as a secondary suite, and local bylaws must also permit suites. One additional, detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — maximum 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Tenure and form on ALR land: housing is freehold in practice, under that frame. (Research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Additional farm-residence forms — ONLY as the ALC sources support them:** an additional residence beyond the permitted frame is not a general entitlement. The routes the ALR sources document are the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation s.34(3) framework for an additional manufactured-home residence as administered under the ALC's Policy L-25, and, where the use does not adhere, a Non-Adhering Residential Use (NARU) application to the ALC; further residences beyond what is permitted can only be approved by the ALC where the residence is necessary for farm use. **A general farm-worker entitlement to an additional residence is UNCONFIRMED** — each parcel's position is read from its own ALC position, not assumed from another farm's approval. Replacement-house trap, as the ALC states it: living in the existing house while a new principal residence is built needs ALC approval; demolishing and removing the old residence first is the route that does not. (Settled ALR position, the poultry-farms duplex treatment; research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Why there is no half-duplex form on ALR farm ground:** residences in the ALR cannot be stratified — ALR residences cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)), and subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, as this slug's acreages sibling carries it. A strata duplex / strata half duplex is therefore not an available form on ALR parcels in this topic, and no fee-simple half-duplex farm subdivision is documented under this topic. A listing that calls a farm residence a "duplex" is stating a RECORD ONLY — marketing wording for a two-unit or two-residence farm property sold as one parcel — never verification of a separable half, and no such mention is counted in this file. This slug's townhomes sibling states the excluded-tenure position the same way: farm-worker or caretaker accommodation offered on a rental or licence basis is tied to the farm operation, not a purchasable townhome — and not duplex stock. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted — two frames, kept separate.** On ALR parcels the Agricultural Land Commission's housing rules govern the residences themselves and the stratification route is closed by rule (§2). Off the ALR, Surrey's frame governs: duplexes are a permitted two-unit form in Surrey's residential zones that allow a duplex under Zoning By-law No. 12000, and the provincial SSMUH implementation (Surrey, Jul 8, 2024) makes the duplex the baseline multi-unit form on eligible lots — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. **SSMUH is an urban-lot framework (eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer); it is not a route to extra dwellings on ALR farm parcels, where the ALC rules above govern.** The OCP governing is Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is the replaced predecessor, never cited as current. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated for Surrey, which matters on the Colebrook / Sullivan side, where the South Newton plan area includes fish-bearing creeks (Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries) and riparian rules can affect creek-side farm parcels. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (read 2026-11-01); https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton (read 2026-11-01.)
- **Secondary suites — Surrey's own rule, and the stricter ALR rule.** On urban lots, one secondary suite may be permitted in a single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most **40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area**, and never inside a coach house or garden suite — Surrey's rule, never borrowed. On ALR land the ALC rule is the stricter frame: the suite must be inside the principal residence only. (Research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite, read 2026-11-01; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/, read 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — stated for completeness; no strata duplex exists on the ALR ground.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists off the ALR: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** Subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel off the ALR requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; this slug's acreages sibling.)
- **Servicing, STR and farm classification.** Surrey's servicing position is Bylaw 8830 (1986), VERIFIED from the City's own pages in the verified ledger. Water licensing, septic and servicing position for Surrey farm parcels: UNVERIFIED on official sources opened in the research pass — checked parcel by parcel; parcel-level ALR status itself is UNVERIFIED in this pass (the ALC maps re-open returned a 429 rate-limit error and was not retried). Short-term rentals carry two layers here: in the ALR, whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence is not permitted outright and requires ALC approval, and an additional residence may be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism accommodation on a property that is actively farmed and holds farm classification under the Assessment Act; the City layer sits on top — only an owner who lives at the property as their primary residence may host, tenants cannot operate one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required before operating ($358/yr carried, $350 conflict disclosed). BC Assessment farm classification income thresholds: $2,500 if the farm operation's total area is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha; $10,000 if the total area is less than 0.8 ha — applications by the legislated deadline of October 31 for the next assessment and tax year. (Research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/, read 2026-11-01; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals, read 2026-11-01; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification, read 2026-11-01.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; **$3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = $93,000 including the further 2% tier** — $93,000 governs at $3.5M (see §5). Farm / mixed-use parcels can split residential and non-residential value, so a real farm bill turns on that split — confirm it with a lawyer/notary; the worked examples are the fully-residential illustration only. A half duplex, where one exists off the ALR, is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale farm residence is not new housing for this rebate, and whether a new farm residence purchase qualifies turns on its own facts — confirm with a tax professional. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where are Surrey's farms?** There is no single Surrey farm belt in the sources used here: the live feed's three listings at reading sat in Sullivan Station (two, on the Colebrook side) and Fleetwood Tynehead (one), and the page's own related links point to Cloverdale Farms, South Surrey Farms and Port Kells Acreages as separate pockets. (§1.)
- **Q: Can I buy half of a Surrey farm's residence?** No separable half exists to buy on ALR ground: ALR residences cannot be stratified, and no fee-simple half-duplex farm subdivision is documented under this topic. The parcel sells whole, under one title. (§2.)
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Surrey?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. (§2.) https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a Surrey farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and City bylaws must also permit it; beyond that frame the routes are the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / Policy L-25 additional manufactured-home residence route or a Non-Adhering Residential Use application, and anything further requires an ALC application showing the residence is necessary for farm use. A general farm-worker entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put 4 units on a farm parcel?** No. SSMUH is an urban-lot framework for eligible lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary; on ALR land the ALC rules govern how many residences a parcel may have. (§3.)
- **Q: I want to live in the old house while I build the new one — allowed?** That needs ALC approval. Demolishing and removing the old residence first is the route that does not. (§2.) https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I Airbnb a Surrey farm house?** Not as a whole-house rental by right: in the ALR it is not permitted outright and requires ALC approval (an additional residence can be used only as agri-tourism accommodation on an actively farmed, farm-classified property), and the City separately requires the host to be an owner living at the property as their primary residence, with a $358-per-year business licence. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $3.5M?** $93,000 on a fully residential $3,500,000 purchase with no exemption — tier math in §3. A farm parcel's actual bill turns on its residential / non-residential split. (§3; §5.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Are the Surrey market numbers in this file farm prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no farm category; the §1 figures are Surrey residential context only, and farm-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. (§1.)
- **Q: With only 3 farm listings city-wide at reading, how do I price one?** Parcel by parcel: with a thin feed, the competition is not a deep Surrey farm market — document the production history, water position, buildings and any ALC approvals in place, because those parcel facts are what a farm buyer's lender and lawyer will price.
