## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A coach house in the Township is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — it is never a strata half duplex. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan and zoning (Township frame):** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2500 governs; Community Plans sit under OCP Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025). Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 is CONFIRMED per the settled ledger. - **SSMUH (Township's own position):** the Township adopted Bylaw 6020 on November 18, 2024 — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and **NO 6-unit tier**. Most Campbell Valley acreage parcels exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by a wide margin, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them; duplexes are the baseline form where SSMUH does apply. - **Secondary suites and coach houses (Township's own rules, never borrowed):** the Township suite cap is **120 m²** — Surrey's 40%, Langley City's and Abbotsford's figures are never borrowed into a Township file. A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 (the definition source) — never strata. What a specific parcel may build is the parcel-level question: UNVERIFIED. (Research file, §4; program frame, D18 brief.)
- **ALR frame (for parcels in the ALR — status per-parcel, UNVERIFIED here):** one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local bylaws may be more restrictive, never more permissive. **Depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6)** — so a strata half duplex on ALR land needs ALC permission; no such permission is documented for any Campbell Valley parcel here. BC Assessment farm classification thresholds: $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), $2,500 plus 5% of actual value over 4 ha, $10,000 (under 0.8 ha); applications by October 31. (Research file, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Township ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. This block currently applies to nothing named in Campbell Valley (§2) — it is the diligence frame if a strata half duplex is ever verified here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Township's Approving Officer process under Servicing Bylaw 5382, and on ALR land subdivision requires ALC approval. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file, §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. How PTT and GST are apportioned on a specific farm/acreage purchase is UNVERIFIED — no Province farm-transfer source was opened. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
