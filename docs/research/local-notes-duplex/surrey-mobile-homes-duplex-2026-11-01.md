## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A manufactured home is none of these forms: it is a single dwelling unit, factory-built and registered in the provincial Manufactured Home Registry, held either as a chattel on a rented pad or on land the buyer owns. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; manufactured-home forms per the research file above and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry)

- **The tenure rules that govern this topic's product (NOT duplex rules).** Registration is what makes a manufactured-home sale effective: the buyer and seller file the original signed and witnessed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership forms through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the Province directs buyers to make sure the CSA certification label is attached at the point of sale (modular homes are built under the A-277 standards, mobile homes under the Z-240 standards; a home whose label is lost must be re-certified through Technical Safety BC's electrical inspection route before it may be sold). In a park, assignment of the site tenancy needs the landlord's prior written consent under s. 28 of the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (the settled wording is "director," never "arbitrator" — see §5), the seller must give the proposed buyer written copies of the park rules and the relevant parts of the tenancy agreement before asking, and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. Pad rent increases otherwise need 3 full months' notice, at most once every 12 months, within the yearly limit plus the proportional levy/utility amount (Act percentages only in the siblings — 2026 = 2.3% + proportional; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional — and NO pad-rent dollar figure is published as fact in this file). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (for transfers where PTT applies — own-land / strata-lot transfers, including a Sea Ridge Bays lot).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a Surrey mobile home ever a duplex or half duplex?** No. A manufactured home is a single dwelling unit — held as a chattel on a rented pad, on a bare-land strata lot, or on an owned fee-simple lot. None of those tenures is a two-unit building, and none is bought "by the half." This topic file verifies no duplex stock. (§2.)
- **Q: What is Sea Ridge Bays, then — and is it duplex stock?** A bare-land strata manufactured-home community at 15875 20 Ave, South Surrey: you own the home AND the strata lot and pay a strata fee instead of pad rent. It is a land form documented at home in the south-surrey-mobile-homes files — NOT duplex stock, and counted nowhere here. (§2.)
- **Q: Do all Surrey "mobile homes" sit on rented pads?** No. The feed holds pad-rent park units and own-your-land strata units side by side, plus manufactured homes on owned lots. Establish the tenure before anything else: the paperwork, financing and monthly costs are different for each. (§1; §2.)
- **Q: If I buy in a pad-rental park, do I own the land?** No — you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad; your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, which needs the park owner's prior written consent under s. 28. Sea Ridge Bays is the exception in form: a bare-land strata, where you own the strata lot. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Can the park raise my pad rent when I buy?** No — on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same and the next scheduled rent-increase date does not reset; increases otherwise need 3 full months' notice, at most once every 12 months, within the yearly limit plus the proportional amount. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a bare-land strata lot or a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). In a park there is no Form B — diligence runs through the registry, the CSA label, the park rules and the tenancy agreement. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: What if the home has lost its CSA label?** It may not be sold until re-certified: Technical Safety BC arranges a licensed electrical contractor's inspection, and the electrical approval label prepared can be used in place of a CSA label to list the home for sale. (§3.)
- **Q: Which Surrey parks are actually 55+?** On the settled roster carried in the siblings: Westwood Court (Fleetwood) and Westwood Estates (Queen Mary Park) verify as 55+ on listing-stated positions in their home files; Newton MHP is a 55+ TENURE RECORD; Sea Ridge Bays is 55+ in the strata framework, confirmed on its Form B. Breakaway Bays is NOT verified 55+ — MLS governs over the directory claim. (§2.)
