## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A manufactured home on a rented pad is none of these forms: the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the site. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing; pad tenure per the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act frame in the research file above.)

- **Manufactured-home park frame (MHPTA — the settled mobile block).** In a manufactured home park, the tenant owns the manufactured home and rents the site (pad) under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. Pad-rent increases: landlords can only increase rent with 3 full months' notice, once every 12 months, and no more than the yearly limit — **2026 is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; 2027 is 2.2% plus the same proportional amount**; above-limit increases require an RTB order. **No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact anywhere in this file** — the live page's $650–$950/month range and the $782/month figure in one Georgian Park card are UNVERIFIED (see §5). Assignment on a sale (MHPTA s.28): a tenant may assign a site tenancy only with the landlord's prior written consent (or deemed consent, a director's order, or authorization in the agreement), consent may be withheld only in prescribed circumstances, and under s.28(3) the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting; the request runs on RTB-10. Park closure/conversion (MHPTA s.42): all necessary permits in place first, then 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation, with defined additional-compensation routes. In B.C., the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if the transaction is registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; a home may not be sold unless the original legible CSA decal is attached (Z-240 series for mobile homes, A-277 series for modular homes), and a lost label must be re-certified through Technical Safety BC's electrical inspection and approval-label route. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry (research file above, §4, declared reuse from this program's batch-12 verification pass.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), and to a bare-land strata lot such as Downes Road Estates.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. Inside a pad-rent park the reverse regime governs: park rules under the MHPTA, no Form B, no strata fees for the pad tenancy itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail and no servicing bylaw number is stated, the number being UNCONFIRMED above. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Taxes and registry (Abbotsford frame).** City of Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026; a first 5% penalty applies July 3, 2026 and a further 5% penalty September 16, 2026. How a specific park home is assessed and taxed depends on its tenure and registration — a per-property check, not stated generally. Abbotsford HAS a Supreme Court registry (the ledger exception). https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice (research file above, §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (for transfers where PTT applies).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer, and an own-land purchase (including a Downes Road Estates lot) takes the general tiers. **For a home-only purchase on a rented pad, the live page's statement that PTT "does not apply" / that pad buyers are "exempt" is UNVERIFIED** — no passage on the Province PTT page opened in this program states that treatment; a lawyer or notary confirms it per purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate, and how any first-home program applies to a home-only purchase on a rented pad is a per-purchase professional question: UNCONFIRMED generally. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

A home in any of them sits on a rented pad. (§2.)
- **Q: If I buy a home in an Abbotsford park, do I own land?** On a rented pad, no — you own the manufactured home as a chattel and hold a manufactured home site tenancy under the MHPTA, paying monthly site rent to the park. Land ownership exists only in the own-land forms: fee-simple lots, or bare-land strata lots such as Downes Road Estates. (§2.)
- **Q: What is the difference between buying a duplex and buying a half duplex in Abbotsford?** A duplex purchase is the whole two-unit building and lot on one title. A half duplex is one unit sold separately, which is only possible where the pair was strata-divided (a 2-lot strata plan — Form B diligence) or the parcel was subdivided fee-simple (party-wall title). If neither happened, there is no "half" to buy. Abbotsford also expressly allows a coach house accessory to a duplex on the same lot. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Does the park have to approve me as a buyer?** For a pad tenancy, assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent under MHPTA s.28 (RTB-10), consent may be withheld only in prescribed circumstances, and no fee may be charged for consent under s.28(3). At Downes Road Estates (strata) there is no park-consent step at all. (§§2–3; research file §4.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on 3 full months' notice, capped at the yearly limit for manufactured home site tenancies: 2026 is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; 2027 is 2.2% plus the proportional amount; above-limit increases require an RTB order. No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact in this file — the live page's $650–$950 range and the $782 card figure are UNVERIFIED (see §5). (§3; research file §4.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget?** On an own-land transfer, run the tiers on your own price: $800,000 → $14,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000 (fully residential, no exemption).
