## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning (Abbotsford frame).** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs — there is no Bylaw 3100. Residential Zone RS3 minimum interior lot 540 m² and RS5 minimum interior lot 420 m², as stated in City rezoning reports applying the bylaw. Which zone permits a two-unit (duplex) form on a given parcel is a parcel-by-parcel read of Bylaw 2400-2014 — no zone-by-zone duplex permission table is asserted, because no source opened for this slug states one. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-west.md, §4; https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/)
- **Suites and accessory units inside / beside a duplex (Abbotsford frame).** A secondary suite must be **within the principal unit, registered with the City, and less than 50% of the size of the principal unit** (changed from a 100 m² cap). A coach house sits on the second storey of an accessory building and a garden suite is the single-storey at-grade form; both may occupy **up to 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m²** — the 15% / 100 m² figures are Abbotsford ONLY. A coach house is a dwelling unit accessory to a single detached dwelling **or duplex** on the same lot. Accessory units cannot be sold independently from the principal unit, and parking is 1 required stall for each accessory unit type under Zoning Bylaw Section 150. Owner-occupancy as a suite requirement is UNVERIFIED in the Zoning Bylaw text itself (no requirement is stated in the City pages and guides read in this program; the bylaw text was not read in this slug's research pass). (Research file above, §4; https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites)
- **SSMUH position (Abbotsford frame, number UNCONFIRMED).** Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial SSMUH framework. - **ALR frame (the rural west and southwest edges, near Mt. Lehman Road and YXX).** The ALC's housing guidance caps a principal residence on ALR land at a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill; permits a secondary suite in the ALR only when it is located within the principal residence; and limits an additional residence to up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. City agricultural-zone subdivision scale: minimum lot area 8.0 ha in the Agricultural One Zone (A1) and 16.0 ha in the Agricultural Two Zone (A2). Airport height rules also apply inside the Airport Zoning Regulation area. (Research file above, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process (and, on A1/A2 land, against the 8.0 ha / 16.0 ha minimums above); no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Taxes and registry (Abbotsford frame).** City of Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. Abbotsford HAS a Supreme Court registry — the ledger exception. Health context in the research: Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre, 32900 Marshall Rd; Abbotsford Urgent and Primary Care Centre, 100-2692 Clearbrook Rd. (Research file above, §4; https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

- **Q: What is the difference between a duplex, a strata half duplex and a house with a suite?** A duplex is one building with two dwelling units on one title. A strata half duplex is one half of a 2-lot strata plan, bought as its own strata lot with Form B diligence. A house with a suite is one dwelling with a secondary suite inside it — the suite is not a separate title and cannot be bought on its own. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a duplex lot in Abbotsford West also carry a coach house?** On the City guide position in this slug's research, a coach house is a dwelling unit accessory to a single detached dwelling or duplex on the same lot; it may occupy up to 15% of the lot to a maximum of 100 m², needs 1 parking stall, counts toward the lot's total permitted units, and cannot be sold independently from the principal unit. The specific lot's zoning controls. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $800,000? At $1,500,000?** On the worked tiers in §3 — fully residential, no exemption — $14,000 at $800,000 and $28,000 at $1,500,000. A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. (§3.)
