## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (each municipality's own rule, never borrowed).** Township: Zoning Bylaw s.102, as amended (including by Bylaw #6147, July 21, 2025), caps a secondary suite at 120 m² maximum gross floor area, one per principal dwelling unit; a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102 (an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage on a lot), never strata. The program carries a noted conflict between the Township's January 2025 "Residential Construction Guide — Secondary Suites" and the July 2025 s.102 text on suites in townhouses/rowhouses, with both documents reported by date — rely on neither alone. City: under Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300, a suite must not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area; the registered owner must reside on the lot (s.2.4); a carriage/garden ground-floor unit is capped at 100 m². (Research file above, §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-100-Administration.pdf)

- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

- **Q: Are Churchill Park and Spencer Green half duplexes?** No. They are large gated 55+ strata townhouse complexes (147 and 97 units) built in a paired, duplex-style form — townhouse stock counted in their 55-plus coverage, not 2-lot strata duplexes and not fee-simple half duplexes. (§2.)
- **Q: What is the difference between a duplex, a strata half duplex and a house with a suite?** A duplex is one building with two dwelling units on one title. A strata half duplex is one half of a 2-lot strata plan, bought as its own strata lot with Form B diligence. A house with a suite is one dwelling with a secondary suite inside it — the suite is not a separate title and cannot be bought on its own; several cards in this page's feed are exactly that third form. (§2.)
- **Q: How many units can an eligible Langley house lot hold?** Start with the municipality: Township Bylaw 6020 gives 3 units at ≤280 m² and 4 units at 280–4,050 m², with eligibility conditions (Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water AND sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) and no 6-unit tier; City Bylaw 3284 gives RS1/RS2 lots 4 units, or 6 within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $1,200,000? At $1,500,000?** On the worked tiers in §3 — fully residential, no exemption — $22,000 at $1,200,000 and $28,000 at $1,500,000. A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a Township house also carry a coach house?** A coach house under Zoning Bylaw s.102 is a fee-simple second unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage on a lot — never strata, never sold independently. A secondary suite is capped at 120 m² in the Township; in the City the cap is the lesser of 100 m² / 40% with owner-occupancy. The specific lot's zoning and, on rural ground, its ALR status control. (§3.)
