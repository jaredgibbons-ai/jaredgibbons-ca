## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A semi-detached house is the paired built form; it is a half duplex ONLY where one of the two title forms above is documented for it. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Secondary suites inside a duplex.** No District of Kent suite percentage cap was stated in this slug's research — UNCONFIRMED, and no other municipality's cap is borrowed here. On ALR land the ALC rule governs instead: a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. (Research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **Flood / diking — a first-order rule on this ground.** Flood Protection & Erosion is OCP Section 5.5; the District's flat lands are Fraser/Harrison floodplain protected by dikes, the District maintains drainage systems (including 135 km of drainage ditches, per the OCP material) and supports Fraser River gravel removal. Diking is a visible, separate line in the District's own rate structure: the 2026 Tax Rates Summary carries a dedicated "Dyking" column charged as a rate per $1,000 of taxable value — 0.03076 for Class 1 Residential and 0.29356 for Class 9 Farm — an assessment-based rate, not a flat per-parcel charge. Parcel-specific flood construction levels remain UNVERIFIED. (Research file above, §4; https://www.kentbc.ca/media/mxrbvfky/tax-rates-summary-2026.pdf)
- **STRATA block — stated for completeness; no strata duplex exists on this ground.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists elsewhere: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. On ALR parcels the strata form is barred outright without ALC permission (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Approving Officer process; no such split is documented on this ground. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** The District of Kent servicing position is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — no servicing number is quoted, and none is asserted. (D19 frame.)
- **Tax timing:** District of Kent tax notices are mailed in early June and are **due July 15** (or the next business day if the 15th falls on a weekend), after which late-payment penalties apply; rates must be set before May 15 each year; properties also carry FVRD levies. The 2026 summary's total municipal rate is 2.80493 (Residential) and the all-in Residential total is 4.65328 including Hospital, Regional District, MFA, BC Assessment and School Tax columns. (Research file above, §4; https://www.kentbc.ca/living-here/home-and-property/property-taxes/ and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/mxrbvfky/tax-rates-summary-2026.pdf)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. (The kent condos sibling gives no worked example because no verified Kent median exists to use as an input — the anchors here are the program's standard illustrations, not Kent prices.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12; see the sibling check in §5) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: Is "Kent" what I search for?** No — Kent is the legal/municipal name; Agassiz is the market and board name. Searches, schools and daily life say Agassiz; taxes, bylaws, permits and the OCP say District of Kent. (§1.)
- **Q: Is Harrison Hot Springs part of Kent?** No — it is a separate village municipality carved out of Kent at the south end of Harrison Lake, with its own taxes, OCP (Bylaw No. 1184, 2022 — Harrison's bylaw, never imported into this file) and market. (§1.)
- **Q: Can a Kent single-dwelling lot take more units now?** Per the District's SSMUH guide as cited in the research, the rules adopted June 10, 2024 allow up to four dwelling units on single-dwelling lots in the RS1 zone — confirm the lot's zone, flood status and ALR status before pricing on it. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex (off this ground)?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: What does the diking line on a Kent tax notice mean?** Diking is its own assessment-based rate line in the District's rate structure (2026 Residential: 0.03076 per $1,000 of taxable value) — a standing cost of floodplain ground, budgeted for, not a surprise. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for Kent?** No. CADREB publishes no area stats and no SnapStats row exists for Kent or Agassiz — pricing rests on comparable sales. (§1.)
