## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

A duplex on a farm parcel in a given week is an individual listing record, documented if anywhere in its home-area file. (Research file above, §§1, 5; sibling set per the FILE STATUS block — the townhomes sibling likewise counts 0 townhouse complexes for this slug, structurally.)

- **The form class with its basis (program/topic level ONLY):** the farm-residence form class, ONLY as ALR sources support it. On ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may further regulate or restrict residences and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. (Research file above, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/, read 2026-11-01.) That ALC frame — carried in this program under the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / Policy L-25 housing provisions, with a Non-Adhering Residential Use application as the route where a source supports a non-conforming residence — describes a principal residence plus at most one small additional residence on one farm parcel under one title. It does NOT create a separable half duplex: a second small residence under that frame is not a strata lot and not a subdivided fee-simple half, and no source opened for this slug documents a farm parcel whose residence was strata-divided or subdivided into halves. Not every farm parcel is in the ALR — one describable feed card states its property is not — so ALR status is a per-parcel check, never a portal assumption. (Research file above, §§4, 5.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted — NO frame asserted for this topic.** A duplex takes the zoning of the municipality and parcel where it sits; this file asserts no city's zoning, SSMUH, suite, servicing or short-term-rental rule for the topic, because the topic has no single ground. The live page's reference to South Surrey A-1 and A-2 zoned acreage is a link to a separate page's scope, recorded as site copy only. Read the home-area file for the parcel in question. (Research file above, §4.)
- **ALR housing frame (province-wide, ALC).** As stated in §2: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill) without an ALC application, suite only within the principal residence, at most one additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha), local bylaws may be stricter, never more permissive. (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/, read 2026-11-01.)
- **BC Assessment farm classification (province-wide).** A voluntary program; income thresholds under the Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation are $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) to 4 ha (10 acres), $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if less than 0.8 ha; applications are due to BC Assessment by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year (late applications not accepted), and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. (Research file above, §4; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Quota / board items — UNVERIFIED in this file, stated plainly.** This portal links quota-based bird operations out to the poultry pages rather than treating them itself; no marketing-board source was opened in this slug's research, so all quota and supply-management claims are UNVERIFIED here — including BC Egg Marketing Board layer transfer rules, premises-certification wording, turkey transfer rules beyond scheme provisions, and any quota price. (Research file above, §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax (general tiers): 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000, based on fair market value at registration unless an exemption applies. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. How PTT and GST are apportioned between land, buildings and residence on a specific farm purchase: UNVERIFIED in this file — no Province GST/PTT farm-transfer source was opened; have an agricultural lawyer and accountant model it before an offer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) wherever it sits.** Form B diligence applies in full: depreciation report, financial statements, CRF balance, approved special levies still payable and the insurance summary. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through that municipality's Approving Officer process, against its current zoning minimums — and on ALR land the ALC frame in §2 governs what residences the parcel may carry at all; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Per-parcel diligence, UNVERIFIED at portal level:** whether a specific parcel is in the ALR, its zoning, servicing and floodplain position, its farm-class status and its municipal or rural tax schedule — each is a per-parcel check with the applicable local government, BC Assessment and title. - **Q: Does this topic file list farm duplexes or half duplexes?** No. It is a province-wide topic file with no roster; the farm-residence form class (§2) is a principal residence plus at most one small additional residence on one parcel under one title — not a separable half. A duplex on a farm parcel is documented, if anywhere, in its home-area file with its title form verified on title. (§2.)
- **Q: What residence can an ALR farm parcel have?** Under the ALC frame: one principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, with local government rules possibly more restrictive — and only where the parcel is actually in the ALR. (§3; research file §7.)
- **Q: Can I strata-divide or subdivide a farm residence into two halves to sell separately?** No source opened for this slug documents that on a farm parcel, and the ALC frame governs what residences an ALR parcel may carry at all. A fee-simple split anywhere requires subdivision approval through the municipality's Approving Officer process; a strata half requires a 2-lot strata plan and full Form B diligence. Treat any "half duplex on a farm" marketing line as a record to verify on title, not as a form this file verifies. (§2, §3.)
- **Q: What income must a farm show to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's thresholds are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; applications are due by October 31 for the next tax year. (§3; research file §7.)
- **Q: Does buying farm land automatically keep its farm classification?** No — classification is a voluntary, property-by-property BC Assessment program, and a buyer who plans to continue farming must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase or the classification can be removed. (§3; research file §7.)
