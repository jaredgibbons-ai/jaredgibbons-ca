## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Duplexes are a permitted two-unit form in the City's residential zones that allow a duplex under the current zoning bylaw text (Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014), are an enabled form under Historic Downtown's Urban 2 – Ground Oriented designation, and are the baseline form under the provincial SSMUH implementation. The exact position for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until its zone is read. The OCP governing is Abbotsforward 2050, CURRENT. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/bylaws/zoning-bylaw (research file §4)
- **SSMUH position.** The City implemented the provincial SSMUH rules (the research file states the zoning bylaw's s.140 allows 3 units on lots 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots greater than 280 m² in the stated conditions, and one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings); the bylaw number carried in research material as "3560-2024" is held UNCONFIRMED in this program and is never asserted. (Research file §4; standing Abbotsford frame, brief chain.)
- **Secondary suites — Abbotsford's own rule.** Abbotsford's suite rule is a suite under 50% of the principal dwelling — that is this city's rule, never borrowed from another city's. Abbotsford's coach/garden suite figures (15% of lot to max 100 m²) apply in Abbotsford ONLY. (Standing Abbotsford frame, brief chain.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Abbotsford's Development servicing position is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — no servicing bylaw number is asserted in this file. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **Property taxes (context).** City of Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. Abbotsford HAS a Supreme Court registry. (Research file §4; https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000** (the band cap); $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. The First-Time Home Buyers' Program full exemption runs to a fair market value of $835,000 (exempting the first $500,000 of price; partial to $860,000), so a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in this band sits under the full-exemption value ceiling, subject to the program's other conditions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Abbotsford under $800K?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. None is verified by name in this band; a single-lot duplex sells whole. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is there a named duplex development counted in this band?** No. The band's verified rosters are 18 apartment-condo buildings by type (0 counted in band) in the condos sibling and 0 townhouse complexes in band in the townhomes sibling; the duplex evidence is the form class only. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a duplex here?** Abbotsford's own rule governs — a suite under 50% of the principal dwelling, where the zoning applies. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for this band?** No. SnapStats has no duplex category and no price-band cut; its figures are Abbotsford-wide community context only. (§1.)
- **Q: Which assent date governs the first-home GST rebate — March 12 or March 13?** March 13, 2026 per CRA. No sibling of this slug states March 12 as its own date (§5). (§3; §5.)
