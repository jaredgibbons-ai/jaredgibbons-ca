## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**FORM CLASS ONLY — farm ground; no named duplex stock is verified in this topic:**

- **The farm form — which is NOT duplex stock.** The dominant form is the farm house on agricultural land under one title. On ALR land the ALC position governs: generally no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area (with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction); a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence, if local bylaws allow it. Those are farm-residence forms on a single parcel — a suite inside the farm house and an additional farm residence are not a duplex, are not sold separately, and are never counted as duplex or half-duplex stock here. Tenure in this topic is overwhelmingly freehold; strata farm product is not a Maple Ridge pattern (no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source in the research). (Research file above, §§4–5; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **The additional-residence form — stated ONLY as ALC sources support.** The ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, total floor area 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and City of Maple Ridge bylaws must also permit it. Beyond that envelope, the routes are the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 additional manufactured-home residence route and the Non-Adhering Residential Use (NARU) application route, each on its own ALC terms and approvals — **a general entitlement to a farm-worker or second residence beyond those stated routes is UNCONFIRMED and is never asserted in this file** (the settled poultry-farms / bradner treatment). An additional farm residence under any of these routes remains part of the one farm parcel under one title: it does not create a duplex, a strata half, or a separately saleable half. (Research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) and ALC Policy L-25 as cited in the brief chain)
- **The urban-edge form class, for completeness:** off the farm ground, Maple Ridge's duplex form class is the individual duplex building on a single lot built under the zoning of its day (Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019; RS-1 documented in the area files at minimum lot area 668.0 m²). That is a FORM CLASS statement for the city frame only — this topic file verifies no individual duplex by name anywhere, in or out of the farm districts. A specific duplex is verified on its title (one lot, strata plan, or subdivided pair). (Research file above; area files for east-central / west-central / albion in this batch)
- **Directory / listing mentions = RECORDS ONLY.** Any directory entry, listing description or feed card that calls a Maple Ridge farm property a "duplex" or "half duplex" is a record of marketing wording, labelled as such, and is never verification of a separable title. No such mention is counted in this file.

The settled position that governs any strata: only a 55+ age bylaw is valid (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning / OCP.** Zoning is governed by Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019, which applies to all lands in the City; the City publishes an A1, A2, A3 Agricultural Zoning Compliance Summary Checklist, which flags that structures proposed in these zones may require ALC approval and records whether a property is in the ALR and in a flood plain. **UNVERIFIED:** the live page's five-zone table (A-1 through A-5, minimum lots 1.8 ha to 8 ha) is site copy, not an official source, and the consolidated bylaw text could not be opened in the research pass — no agricultural zone minimum is asserted in this file. The Maple Ridge OCP position is CONTESTED in this program (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024) — flagged, resolved nowhere. (Research file above; https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/Zoning%20bylaw%20report.pdf; https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/a1-a2-a3-agr-zoning-checklist202103081624103844)
- **ALR status first.** A specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission maps by PID or civic address; the ALC states its online tools are for general reference and official confirmation comes from the ALC Mapping and GIS team. Reserve status controls residences (the 500 m² principal cap), the one-additional-residence size limits (90 m² / 186 m²), and most use permissions. (Research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **SSMUH position.** Eligible single-family and duplex lots in the urban area may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws — urban context, not a farm-belt rule; on ALR parcels the ALC caps above govern residences first. SSMUH does not by itself split anything into separately titled halves. (Area research for the Maple Ridge slugs in this batch; https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new)
- **Secondary suites — two regimes, never blended.** Inside a farm house on ALR land, a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence if local bylaws allow it (ALC position above). The City's urban suite rule — one suite per lot, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the Floodplain Area — is Maple Ridge's own rule for its urban lots and is never borrowed onto a farm parcel past the ALC position. (Research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) if one is ever the purchase.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, and on ALR land subdivision and use are ALC-governed first; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. No agricultural zone minimum lot size is asserted here (UNVERIFIED, above). Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file above.)
- **Servicing.** Maple Ridge's Subdivision Servicing Bylaw No. 4800-1993, from a City source (verified ledger, brief chain). Farm diligence beyond servicing: groundwater used for non-domestic purposes such as irrigation requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act (domestic use does not); a building with indoor plumbing not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person (Fraser Health). (Research file above; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights; https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems)
- **Farm classification (value-relevant, not a duplex rule).** BC Assessment minimum gross farm income at farm gate prices: $2,500 if the operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation; $10,000 if under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31 for the following taxation year. (Research file above; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer; a farm parcel's residential portion needs transaction-specific tax advice — UNCONFIRMED per parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate, and the rebate does not apply to bare farm land itself. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a farm house with a suite and a second farm residence a duplex I can buy half of?** No. Those are ALR farm-residence forms on one farm parcel under one title (§2/§3) — the parcel sells whole. (§2.)
- **Q: Can any Maple Ridge farm add a second dwelling as of right?** Only within the stated ALC envelope: one additional residence, 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller or 186 m² or less on larger parcels, where City bylaws also permit it — or through the s.34(3) / Policy L-25 manufactured-home route or a NARU application on their own terms. A general entitlement beyond that is UNCONFIRMED. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is there any named duplex development in this farms topic?** None is verified in this topic's research or siblings — §2 is the form class only. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $2,000,000 fully residential with no exemption = $38,000 (worked in §3). A farm purchase's split needs its own tax advice. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me add units on a farm parcel?** On ALR land the ALC housing caps govern residences first; SSMUH is the urban framework and does not override the ALR position stated in §3. (§3.)
- **Q: Are farm or duplex prices published for this topic?** No — SnapStats publishes no farm category and no duplex category; §1 is Maple Ridge residential context only. (§1.)
