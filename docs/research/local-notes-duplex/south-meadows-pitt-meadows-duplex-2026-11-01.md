## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A duplex-FORM home inside a strata townhouse complex is a strata lot in that complex — it is not a 2-lot strata duplex, and its diligence is the complex's Form B, not a duplex file's. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 carries this ground's zones (RS, R-3, R-4, R-6 on the Bonson Road corridor in the research's statement). The City's OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3034, 2025 covers 2–6 units through secondary suites, garden suites, duplexes, triplexes, townhomes and small apartments where it applies — duplexes are an expressly named form in this city, not an inference. The zone of a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until read. (Research file above.)
- **SSMUH position.** Under provincial rules applied citywide: lots 280 m² or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 m² up to 4 units, and lots over 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units; the City's SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986 was adopted June 25, 2024, with OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3034, 2025 above. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Research file above.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the city's own rule.** Garden suite 33–90 m², one suite per lot, and never both a garden suite and a secondary suite on one lot (settled Pitt Meadows position, brief chain) — that is PITT MEADOWS' OWN rule, never borrowed into another city's file. (D3 brief position.)
- **Short-term rentals.** STRs are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows (settled position) — a duplex or half duplex here is not bought on a short-term-rental case. (Brief chain.)
- **Floodplain.** Pitt Meadows' floodplain is ~95% of the city (City 2020 Business Plan) and that figure governs — never the guide page's "over 85%". A South Meadows duplex sits on Fraser River-side ground by definition in this sub-area; the specific lot's flood position is read before subject removal. (Brief chain; research file above.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), and, for the two named complexes, to their own strata diligence as complexes.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. (Nature's Walk and Shoreline are complexes, not 2-lot stratas — their own depreciation position is read on their Form B records.) Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 (the STR prohibition above is a municipal rule, a different instrument). Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. Inside Nature's Walk and Shoreline there is no party-wall-agreement question of this kind: the homes are strata lots and the strata's bylaws and Form B govern. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Pitt Meadows' servicing position is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — no servicing bylaw number is asserted in this file. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A strata lot in Nature's Walk or Shoreline, like a half duplex, is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
