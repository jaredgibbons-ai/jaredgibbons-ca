## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **City frame — OCP and SSMUH.** The City's Official Community Plan was adopted on November 22, 2021, built on the "Nexus of Community" vision adopted July 23, 2018. SSMUH: the City amended its Zoning Bylaw to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on RS1 and RS2 lots by Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 (City-only) — RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Duplexes are the baseline form inside that framework: a two-unit building sits below the 4-unit permission. (Research file above, §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md; https://www.langleycity.ca/city-hall/reports-plans-strategies/strategic-vision)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the City's own rule, never borrowed.** Under Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300: a secondary suite must not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the gross floor area of the building (s.1.3 / s.2.4(c)); the registered owner must reside on the lot (s.2.4(c)(i) owner-occupancy); a suite must provide at least one off-street parking space in addition to the two normally required for a single detached use, and must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. Garden suites and carriage homes (Bylaw No. 3300, s.2.4(d)): permitted in the R1 zone; the maximum gross ground floor area for a carriage home or garden suite in R1 is 100 m², the maximum total floor area of accessory buildings on an R1 lot is 60 m², and the registered owner must reside on the lot. A listing's "suite" claim is a claim to verify against this rule. (Research file above, §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) in the City.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. (The cross-referenced larger City stratas sit the other side of the line: Longlea Estates' 58 units, if purchased as strata, are well over the threshold — townhomes sibling.) Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 — flagged against the City roster's own records: both Parkway buildings carry directory lines saying "rentals not allowed," and those lines are void for long-term rentals and must not be read as current law. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate; 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the verified City apartment roster is 1988–1990 resale stock, so GST does not apply to it. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

The City's named strata stock is apartment and townhouse form (§2 cross-references), not duplex stock. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I buy just half of a City duplex?** Only if that half is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot after subdivision approval. A duplex never strata-divided or subdivided sells whole, building and lot together. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: How many units can a Langley City single-family lot hold?** Under Bylaw No. 3284 (adopted June 17, 2024), RS1 and RS2 lots permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — a duplex sits inside that permission as the baseline two-unit form. (§3; research file §7.)
- **Q: What are Langley City's secondary suite rules for a duplex?** Under Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300, a suite must not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, the registered owner must reside on the lot, at least one extra off-street parking space is required, and the suite must be permitted, inspected and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. (§3; research file §7.)
- **Q: How did Langley City sell in September 2026?** The Langley City sub-area recorded 64 detached listings with 8 sold (13%) and 195 Condos & Townhomes listings with 34 sold (17%, joint with Aldergrove in the Langley set); no City-only median is published — the combined Langley community medians (detached $1,345,000; attached $611,000) blend in the Township and are not City figures. (§1.)
- **Q: The Parkway directory says rentals are banned and it's 55+. Which one binds me?** The rental line does not — rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022. The 55+ line can: a 55-or-older bylaw is the one age bylaw the Strata Property Act still permits (ss. 123.1/123.2), with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (since May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Those buildings are apartment stock cross-referenced in §2 — the same reading governs any strata half duplex's registered bylaws. (§2; §3.)

Morningside Estates is the neighbouring 5365 205 St address, held UNCONFIRMED in this program: no verified fact about Glenmont Park may be carried across to it. (§2.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex in the City need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: When are property taxes due in Langley City?** Payments received by the City after the July 2 due date incur a penalty; the City's payment page lists City Hall at 20399 Douglas Crescent and a 2.2% processing fee on credit-card payments — the penalty percentage itself is UNVERIFIED on the City page read.
