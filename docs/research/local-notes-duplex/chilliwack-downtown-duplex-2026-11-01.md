## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning (Chilliwack frame).** Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs. No zone-by-zone duplex permission table is asserted in this file, because no source opened for this slug states one; which zone permits a two-unit form on a given downtown parcel is a parcel-by-parcel read of Bylaw 5000 and the parcel's zone, inside the Schedule B3 plan and the D-6 Downtown form-and-character permit area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/chilliwack-downtown.md; https://chilliwack.com/ocp)
- **SSMUH position (Chilliwack frame).** Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing framework. Chilliwack's SSMUH amendment is Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, amending Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, adopted June 18, 2024, allowing 3–4 units on urban-growth-boundary parcels zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. (Research file above.)
- **Secondary suites inside / beside a duplex (Chilliwack's own rule, never borrowed).** Under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000: a secondary suite is an accessory dwelling unit of not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building — the lesser of 90 m² and 40% (s.2); only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot, and the permitted ADU types include secondary suite, coach house and garden suite (s.4.06(1)); garden suites are capped at a maximum gross floor area of 100 m² (s.4.06(1)(g)); ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite (s.5.04). (Research file above.)
- **City plan (Chilliwack frame).** The current plan is the 2050 Official Community Plan, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — Schedule B3 is the Downtown Neighbourhood Plan covering this ground and Schedule D-6 is the Downtown development permit area; the other Schedule D areas apply per parcel (per-parcel application UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel). (Research file above; https://chilliwack.com/ocp)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (for transfers where PTT applies).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a named duplex development or half-duplex subdivision I can shop in Chilliwack Downtown?** No named one is verified. This slug's research and all six of its siblings verify no named strata duplex and no named fee-simple half-duplex subdivision on this ground — what exists is the ordinary form class: individual duplex buildings on single lots under the zoning of their day. (§2.)
- **Q: What is the difference between buying a duplex and buying a half duplex here?** A duplex purchase is the whole building and lot on one title. A half duplex is one unit sold separately, which is only possible where the pair was strata-divided (a 2-lot strata plan — Form B diligence) or the parcel was subdivided fee-simple (party-wall title). If neither happened, there is no "half" to buy. (§2.)
- **Q: The feed showed four-plex listings on Bole Avenue — are those duplex stock?** No. They are strata four-plex listings, recorded in the siblings as investment/infill product and counted nowhere as duplex, condo or townhouse stock. A four-unit building is not a two-unit duplex. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Cedar Court a duplex option?** No. Cedar Court (45882 Cheam Ave) is a strata townhouse complex, counted once in this slug's townhomes file. Townhouse form is not duplex form. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a downtown duplex also carry a suite or a garden suite?** Only 1 accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot under Bylaw 5000: a secondary suite capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space, or a coach house, or a garden suite capped at 100 m² — Chilliwack's own figures. Whether a specific duplex parcel qualifies is a parcel read of the bylaw and the lot. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on a duplex purchase?** Run the tiers on your own price: $800,000 → $14,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000 (fully residential, no exemption). A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. (§3.)
