## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **District plans and zoning — District of North Vancouver ONLY.** The Official Community Plan is Bylaw 7900, which directs 75–90% of growth to four centres and preserves established low-density neighbourhoods; the zoning bylaw is Zoning Bylaw 3210 (cited in District material as amended by Bylaw 8698, June 2024, for the suite and coach-house rules). The exact zone for a specific Norgate lot is read off the current Bylaw 3210 text and District GeoWeb for that lot — zone entitlements asserted beyond that read are UNCONFIRMED in this file. **City of North Vancouver bylaws (OCP 8400 / Zoning 6700) do not apply anywhere in this file's ground.** https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Suites and coach houses — the District's own rule, and this ground's documented second-unit form.** Since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — **three units total** — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. December 2023 changes let a secondary suite be **up to 49% of the house** with no minimum size and raised the maximum coach house height to two storeys. That 49% figure is the District of North Vancouver's own suite rule, stated for this District only — no other municipality's suite cap is borrowed or exported. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **SSMUH — the District's classification, with its compliance position stated.** The District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. The Province's Bill 25 (November 2025) requires full SSMUH density by June 30, 2026, but a March 2, 2026 District Council motion asked staff to pursue exempting the Lions Gate area — which includes Norgate — from SSMUH, so the specific lot's classification is read off GEOweb, not assumed; a duplex (two units) sits inside even the lowest tier where the lot is non-exempt. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-4-detached-residential.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **Hazards.** Schedule B of the District OCP establishes Development Permit Areas for Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard; Mackay Creek runs along the neighbourhood's east side, and whether any specific Norgate lot falls inside a mapped creek hazard DPA along it is property-specific — GeoWeb check required, UNVERIFIED here. The District's GEOweb flood construction level data is defined as modelled flood water levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/ (Research file §4.)
- **Servicing: UNCONFIRMED.** The settled ledger holds the District of North Vancouver's servicing bylaw number UNCONFIRMED — no number is asserted in this file. Utility figures that ARE sourced (the 2026 single-family water and sewer/drainage fees in §5) are tax/utility facts, not a servicing-bylaw citation. (Settled ledger; research file §4.)
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / half duplex in Norgate.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the District's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Property taxes.** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value, and single-family utility fees include water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60 (combined single-family utilities $2,571 in 2026). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a half duplex in Norgate?** No named half duplex — strata or fee-simple — is verified anywhere in this program for Norgate. What sells here is a whole freehold house (often with a suite), not a separable half. If a listing uses "half duplex" wording inside the subdivision, read the title first: no such complex is verified in this program. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Norgate in the City of North Vancouver?** No — it is in the District. Norgate Park is a District park and District council documents count Norgate among the District's Lower Capilano neighbourhoods; District bylaws, taxes and permit desks apply. (§1.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite and a coach house to a Norgate rancher?** That is the District's documented permission: since June 2024 a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — are permitted on single-family zoned lots within the Urban Containment Boundary, with the suite up to 49% of the house. Confirm the specific lot on GeoWeb before counting rental income. (§3.)
- **Q: Will SSMUH give my lot four or six units?** Not assumed. The District's classification tiers exist, but Council voted in March 2026 to pursue exempting the Lions Gate area — which includes Norgate — from SSMUH. The lot's own GEOweb classification governs. (§3.)
- **Q: What do I buy in one of the Marine Drive buildings?** A strata apartment-condo lot (X61, The Drive at 1177 Marine, The Ivy — counted in the condos file) — Form B diligence in full; in a strata that size the depreciation-report cycle for 5+ lots applies, unlike a 2-lot strata duplex, which is below the threshold. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: What is the land across Welch Street?** Capilano Indian Reserve No. 5 (X̱wemelch'stn), Squamish Nation land under the Nation's own land-use control. In October 2024 Nation Council approved land uses for the reserve's Marine Drive site — mixed-use market residential, Nation housing and a new Administrative and Community Service Centre — so the southern outlook is an active development front, not a static edge. (§1; research §4.)
- **Q: What should I inspect on an original Norgate Park house?** The heating system: originals are reported (heritage-society account only — UNVERIFIED from official sources) to have radiant hot-water lines embedded in the concrete slab; where that system survives, its condition is a real inspection item. (§5; research §6.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Is there a Norgate market median I can rely on?** No — SnapStats does not cover North Vancouver and no substitute figure is used in this program. (§1.)
