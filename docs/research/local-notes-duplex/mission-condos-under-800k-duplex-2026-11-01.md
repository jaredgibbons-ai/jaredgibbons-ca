## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) seen through any feed.** Form B diligence applies in full. - **Taxes — the two regimes, never blended.** City of Mission 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a **FLAT 10% penalty** added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 — the city regime. Mission has NO Supreme Court registry (the mapping of Mission files to another registry is UNVERIFIED, never filled in). https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes, read 2026-11-01.
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; **$3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = $93,000 including the further 2% tier** — $93,000 governs at $3.5M. Band anchors: only the 1% and 2% tiers arise under $800,000 — at the $430,000 attached-median input, $6,600 gross, and a qualifying first-time buyer at that price pays $0 net because the whole price sits inside the exempt first $500,000; at $799,000, $13,980 gross ($5,980 net for a qualifying first-time buyer). A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Band tax facts from the research:** because this band caps at $800,000, a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in the band sits under the first time home buyers' program full-exemption value ceiling ($835,000, exempting the tax on the first $500,000; partial only above $835,000 and below $860,000), subject to the program's other conditions — confirm the buyer's own position with a legal professional before relying on it. The newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and eliminated at $1,150,000 per the siblings. (Research file above; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers, read 2026-11-01.)
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
