## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **ALR frame governs second units here, stated plainly:** under Agricultural Land Commission housing rules, land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless the owner qualifies for an additional residence; the principal residence must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with a maximum of 1,000 m² of fill for it; a secondary suite is permitted in the ALR only within the principal residence; and an additional detached residence may be up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. A "duplex conversion" story on an ALR parcel runs into this frame first. The zone, ALR status and parcel-level flood status of any specific prairie property are UNVERIFIED — per-parcel checks. (Research file, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **Age statements:** only as verified — no age-restricted duplex stock is documented on this ground. The settled position that governs any strata age bylaw: only a 55+ age bylaw is valid (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan and zoning (Abbotsford frame):** the current plan is Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted Dec 2025). The guide research cites the OCP as "Bylaw 2600-2016" among its unverified quick-fact claims and the live page carries a "2500-2016" form — both are stale/conflicted against the current plan and are flagged in §5, never asserted. (Research file, §§4–5; https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf)
- **SSMUH position:** the SSMUH bylaw number for Abbotsford is UNCONFIRMED in the settled ledger — "3560-2024" is asserted in this slug's siblings (see §5) and is NEVER asserted in this file. (Settled Abbotsford frame, D1/D15 briefs; research file, §4.)
- **Suites inside a duplex / principal dwelling (Abbotsford's own rule, never borrowed):** under Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, a secondary suite must be **less than 50% of the size of the principal unit**, and garden suites and coach houses may occupy **up to 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m²** — the 15%/100 m² coach/garden figure is Abbotsford ONLY. On ALR land the ALC frame in §2 applies over this: a suite only within the principal residence. (Research file, §4, from the batch-12 sibling file.)
- **Servicing:** Abbotsford's Development (servicing) bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED per the verified ledger — no number is asserted. Abbotsford HAS a Supreme Court registry (the exception in the registry ledger) — foreclosure process for an Abbotsford property is not mapped further here. (Settled ledger, D1 brief.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), the only strata form a duplex here could take.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Approving Officer process; on ALR land it additionally requires ALC approval, and the A1/A2 minimum parcel sizes above govern the practical answer on the prairie. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; ALR frame: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales — a resale farmhouse or duplex on the prairie is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I add a second dwelling or suite on a prairie farm parcel?** Only inside the ALC frame: one principal residence at 500 m² or less, a suite only within that residence, and at most one additional detached residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha) — and Abbotsford's own suite cap (under 50%) and coach/garden limit (15% of lot to 100 m²) apply where the City bylaw governs.
