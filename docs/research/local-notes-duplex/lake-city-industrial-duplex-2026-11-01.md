## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted — and why not here.** Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815 (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) governs — NEVER the former Bylaw No. 4742 as current; properties previously zoned under repealed districts were reassigned to the closest comparable new district, and the City states the reassignment approach for employment and industrial lands follows the Burnaby 2050 OCP and Metro Vancouver designations. The OCP governing is Burnaby 2050 (adopted December 9, 2025), whose policy directions aim to protect existing industrial land and intensify employment lands; the 2050 OCP takes precedence over the 2000 Lake City plan map where they conflict. The City defines industrial lands for heavy and light industrial activities including distribution, warehousing, repair, construction yards, infrastructure, outdoor storage, wholesale, manufacturing, trade and e-commerce. (Research file above: https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite; https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan; https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/burnaby-2050-planning-our-city-together/burnaby-2050-policy-guide-share-your-feedback/economic-development-employment-and-industrial-lands)
- **SSMUH position.** Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; permitted forms include duplexes, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. That is the city's RESIDENTIAL framework — it is carried here because it is the frame a buyer will meet elsewhere in Burnaby, and it does not convert Lake City's industrial parcels into duplex ground. (Research file above: https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the city's own rule.** In Burnaby's R1 district, secondary suites count toward the lot's unit total and cannot be stratified (55-plus sibling's record of the City framework). No suite question arises on a Lake City industrial parcel on the sources read, and no Burnaby suite size-cap figure is stated in the sources read for this slug — never borrow another city's cap. (55-plus sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/lake-city-industrial-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), carried for completeness; no such strata is verified in this district.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. For industrial strata purchases in the district, the research's own tip stands: review the strata documents under the provincial strata framework early. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. - **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031.
