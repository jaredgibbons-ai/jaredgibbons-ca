## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. On ALR farm ground the first form is closed by rule (below). (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Why the strata form is closed on the farm ground:** the Agricultural Land Commission's housing rules state that residences in the ALR cannot be stratified. A strata duplex / strata half duplex is therefore not an available form on ALR parcels — the same structural position the D1 Bradner file documents for Abbotsford's ALR ground. A farm property with two units or two residences sells as one parcel under one title. (ALC housing position, carried in the sibling program; D1 bradner-guide duplex file.)

A buyer who needs a separately titled half widens the search off the ALR farm ground.
- **Non-ALR Langley lots:** off the ALR, the ordinary Township form class applies — individual duplexes on single lots under the zoning of their day, with the Township's SSMUH 6020 position in §3. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** On ALR parcels the Agricultural Land Commission's housing rules govern the residences themselves and the stratification route is closed by rule (above). Off the ALR, duplexes are a permitted two-unit form in the Township's residential zones that allow a duplex under the current zoning text, and the Township's SSMUH Bylaw 6020 position applies — **6020 carries NO 6-plex tier** (standing Township lock). The exact position for a specific farm parcel is UNCONFIRMED until its zone and ALR status are read. (Research file above; standing Township frame, brief chain.)
- **SSMUH position.** Township SSMUH Bylaw 6020 — no 6-plex tier exists in the Township's position, and SSMUH does not rewrite ALR housing rules: farm parcels follow the ALC position above. (Standing Township frame, brief chain.)
- **Secondary suites / second residences — the Township's and the ALC's own rules.** The Township's suite cap is 120 m² — that is the Township's rule, never borrowed. On an ALR parcel, the ALC additional-residence rules govern instead of the ordinary suite frame; confirm per parcel before counting any second-unit income. (Standing Township frame, brief chain; ALC position in §2.)
- **STRATA block — stated for completeness; no strata duplex exists on the ALR ground.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists off the ALR: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Township's Approving Officer process, and on ALR land an ALC subdivision position as well; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it, and no such split is documented on the farm ground. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** The Township's servicing position is Bylaw 5382, CONFIRMED in the verified ledger. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. Farm transfers can carry mixed-use apportionment — the worked examples are the fully-residential case; a farm parcel's actual PTT follows its residential/non-residential split, which is parcel-specific and UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any half duplex for sale on Langley farm ground?** None verified, in any source in this program — and the strata form is closed on ALR parcels by rule, because ALR residences cannot be stratified. A farm purchase is a whole parcel under one title. (§2.)
- **Q: A listing calls a Langley farmhouse a duplex — can I buy one side?** No. That wording is a record of marketing language for a two-unit or two-residence farm property; without a strata plan or a subdivided lot there is no separate half to buy, and the strata route is closed in the ALR. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex (off the farm ground)?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a second residence on a Langley farm parcel?** On ALR land the ALC additional-residence rules govern; off ALR, the Township's suite rule (120 m² cap) and the zoning govern. Confirm per parcel before counting rental income. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). Farm parcels can apportion differently; the split is parcel-specific. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for Langley farms?** No. No duplex category is published for farm ground. (§1.)
