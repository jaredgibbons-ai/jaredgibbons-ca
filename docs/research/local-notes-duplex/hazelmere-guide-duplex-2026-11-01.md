## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A secondary suite inside a principal residence is not a duplex, and a detached additional residence under the ALC frame is not a half duplex. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan and zoning (Surrey frame):** Surrey OCP = Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is never cited as current; Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs. The City publishes no land use plan named for Hazelmere, so a parcel's zone under Bylaw 12000 (A-1 / RA per §2, carried UNVERIFIED on the bylaw text where stated) and its ALR status drive what can be built on it. Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) is VERIFIED from City pages. Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED). Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — the Little Campbell River and its tributaries run through this ground, and no single riparian figure is ever stated. (Research file, §§1, 4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000)
- **SSMUH caution for this ground (Surrey frame, stated exactly as the research states it):** Surrey's SSMUH allowances apply to eligible residential-zoned properties under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including lands not connected to community water and sewer — most Hazelmere acreage ground is outside that shape, so the standard 3–4 unit framing (3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; SSMUH zoning changes adopted Jul 8, 2024) must not be applied to a Hazelmere lot without a parcel check of zone, containment boundary and servicing. A secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit** — Surrey's own cap, never borrowed — subject on ALR parcels to the ALC rule that a suite sits only inside the principal residence. (Research file, §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Surrey ground, and to Hazelmere Village as a strata purchase.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds (Hazelmere Village is over the threshold on its complex scale — confirm on its Form B). Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. The fee-simple contrast governs most of this ground: the surrounding acreage homes are fee-simple — no strata, no Form B, no strata fees — but they carry the ALR rulebook instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process under Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986), and the zone minimums in the current consolidated Bylaw 12000 decide whether a lot can split at all (the A-1 / Part 10 figures in §2 are UNVERIFIED on the bylaw text and are not relied on here). Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file, §4.)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey-wide):** renting a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence is required before operating — carried at **$358 per year**, with the City's 2024 **$350 per year** proposal disclosed alongside it. (Settled Surrey frame; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
