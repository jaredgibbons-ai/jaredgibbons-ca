## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815).** Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024 — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified — carried into the current bylaw: the **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815), adopted June 9, 2026 and in effect July 1, 2026**. The former Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) is superseded and is **never cited as current** in this file; any source citing it for this ground is citing a repealed bylaw. Citywide direction is the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (adopted December 9, 2025, replacing the 1998 OCP Bylaw); its land use map takes precedence over older neighbourhood community plan maps where they conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan (Research file §4; townhomes sibling §3.)
- **SSMUH position.** The R1 district above IS Burnaby's SSMUH implementation — the duplex is the baseline form inside it, and the stratification permission for lots with two or more primary units is the legal mechanism by which a Burnaby strata duplex (a 2-lot strata half-duplex pair) comes to exist at all. Review the Burnaby 2050 OCP land use map before assuming density or subdivision potential on a large lot, because the new OCP governs over older community plan maps — this is the pricing question on the area's biggest Buckingham Heights and Canada Way-edge lots. (Townhomes sibling §3; research file §6.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Burnaby's own position.** No Burnaby suite size cap is stated in this slug's research or siblings, so **NO cap figure is carried here** — Surrey's 40% of principal habitable area and every other city's figure are never borrowed. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a 7-unit building like Sperling Heights sits above it. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. - **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
