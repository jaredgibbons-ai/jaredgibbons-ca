## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Hazard layer — the defining rule on this shore.** Development Permit Area SD2, named "SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment)" in the OCP, Category "b" (protection of development from hazardous conditions), covers this shore: the waterfront area is subject to mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence, and the ocean-frontage slopes are subject to erosion and require protection of existing vegetation and tree cover. SD2 building rules include: no building with the underside of a wooden floor system, or the top of a concrete slab for habitation, business or storage of goods damageable by floodwaters, lower than the Flood Construction Level (unless a lesser elevation is set by the provincial ministry); no building within 7.5 m of the natural boundary of the sea or within 7.5 m of the inboard toe of any dike; a restrictive covenant acknowledging the land may be subject to flooding; and development affected by hazardous conditions is subject to conditions set by a suitably qualified Professional Engineer. (Research file above; https://www.delta.ca:443/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations)

Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any duplex stock in English Bluff at all?** No named duplex stock of any form is verified here — §2 is an honest zero. The verified stock is freehold detached homes on estate-scale RS3 lots. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a lot here add units under SSMUH?** Delta allows up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies — but on this shore the SD2 hazard conditions (engineer's conditions, Flood Construction Level, 7.5 m setbacks, flood covenant) are the deciding layer. (§3.)
- **Q: Who owns the beach below a waterfront house?** The foreshore below the natural boundary is Crown land; upland ownership alone does not grant foreshore use. (§3.)
- **Q: Does slope vegetation matter to a duplex or rebuild plan?** Yes — SD2 requires protection of existing vegetation and tree cover on the ocean-frontage slopes; tree removal for a view can need approvals and can affect the slope itself. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Should insurance and financing be checked early here?** Yes — the OCP itself documents mud flow, debris flow, erosion and land-slip hazards for this shore, so insurability and lender comfort should be confirmed before subjects come off. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for English Bluff?** No — SnapStats does not cover South Delta at all, and duplexes have no separate published category in this program's sources. No market figure is stated in this file. (§1.)
