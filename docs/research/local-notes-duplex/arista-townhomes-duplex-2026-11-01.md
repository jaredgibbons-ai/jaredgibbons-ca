## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning and plan (Surrey frame).** Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 governs; the governing OCP is Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is REPLACED and never cited as current. Which zone permits a two-unit (duplex) form on a given parcel is a parcel-by-parcel read of Bylaw 12000. For Arista itself the governing form is its registered strata townhouse form — a buyer reads the strata plan and bylaws, not a duplex permission. (Research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-development/zoning; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan)
- **SSMUH position (Surrey frame).** Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial SSMUH framework; Surrey adopted its SSMUH zoning changes on July 8, 2024 — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. (Research file above, §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Secondary suites — Surrey's own rule, and the townhouse-suite point that matters at Arista.** The City defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit whose habitable floor area does not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit**, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex building, and not a separate strata lot; only one suite is permitted per dwelling unit, and a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite. Separately, Surrey Council approved bylaw changes to permit secondary (lock-off) suites in townhouses in the RM-15, RM-23 and RM-30 zones **for new developments in transit-oriented areas and frequent bus stop areas**, with a suite minimum of 30 sq. m and less than 40% of the habitable floor area of the larger principal unit — those townhouse permissions are NOT a blanket right in a 2011-era complex like Arista; if a lock-off or suite use matters to a purchase there, check the townhouse-suite rules and the strata bylaws first. (Research file above, §§4, 6; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite, read 2026-11-01; https://www.Surrey.ca/news-events/news/surrey-creating-more-housing-options-secondary-suites-townhouses, read via search result 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — applies in full to Arista as a strata complex, and to any strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies: the live page itself directs buyers to the Form B, bylaws, depreciation report and minutes, and to confirm what the monthly fees include. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — Arista's page-stated 65 homes would put it well over that threshold, but the count itself is UNCONFIRMED (§2), so the threshold position is read off the strata plan, not the page; **a 2-lot strata duplex, by contrast, is BELOW the 5-lot threshold on lot count alone**. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts — and Arista is in any case NOT 55+ in any source checked. (Research file above, §6; townhomes project file above, §3; https://jaredgibbons.ca/arista-townhomes.html, read 2026-11-01; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws)
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. Servicing: Surrey Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED from the City's own pages in the ledger. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is ever stated. Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED). STRs in Surrey are permitted, owner-only, at $358/yr carried with the City's 2024 $350/yr proposal disclosed. (Program discipline, D1 brief; standing Surrey frame, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. The newly built home exemption does not apply to a 2011 Arista resale. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. (Townhomes project file above, §3; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax)
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales — an Arista resale (2011) carries no GST, and a resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. (Townhomes project file above, §3; https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html)
