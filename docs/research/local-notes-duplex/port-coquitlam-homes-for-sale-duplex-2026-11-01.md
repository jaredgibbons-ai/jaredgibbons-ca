## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Planning frame.** The City's Official Community Plan (OCP Bylaw No. 3838) was adopted in 2005 and substantially updated in 2013; the City states it will undertake an OCP update to align with Metro 2050 and provincial housing legislation, with 2024–2025 work including SSMUH accommodations and defined Transit Oriented Areas. (Research file above, §4; https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/reports-plans-publications/official-community-plan)
- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Port Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw, 2008, No. 3630 governs (the riverwood position — Riverwood IS Port Coquitlam). The zone of a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until read off the City's current zoning for that lot. (Research file above, §4; brief chain.)
- **SSMUH position.** The City's Zoning Bylaw 3630 as amended for SSMUH renames the RS zones "Residential Small-Scale," permits up to 4 dwelling units on lots 281 m² to 4,050 m² and up to 6 units on lots 281 m² or larger within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop, and replaces "coach house" with "accessory dwelling unit" (ADU floor area limit 90 m², excluded from FAR). Whether that amendment package is the in-force text of Bylaw 3630 today is UNVERIFIED in this slug's research — the summary read is a bylaw-amendment attachment, and the consolidated bylaw read carries the current secondary-suite definition without the full SSMUH schedule; confirm the in-force text for a specific lot with the City. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Research file above, §4; https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11157)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Port Coquitlam's own rule.** The secondary suite cap is 40% of the floor area of the principal dwelling unit, with suites permitted in a house, duplex or triplex where units are vertically separated — never borrowed from another city. The suite owner-occupancy rule is UNVERIFIED: no owner-occupancy clause was read in the City documents opened in this slug's research. (Research file above, §4; https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11157)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the municipality's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Port Coquitlam servicing is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — no servicing bylaw number is asserted in this file. (Brief chain.)
- **SkyTrain — stated precisely, never as a station.** Port Coquitlam has NO SkyTrain station. The proposed SkyTrain extension to Port Coquitlam is an exploratory business case only (Mayors' Council, Feb 19, 2026, per the brief-chain ledger) — never a funded project, never a station. - **Property taxes (Port Coquitlam frame).** City of Port Coquitlam 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026 under Annual Tax Rates Bylaw, 2026, No. 4441; a 5% penalty applies to current taxes not paid before end of day July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty applies to current taxes outstanding after September 15, 2026. (Research file above, §4; https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Port Coquitlam?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. None is verified by name here; the City's own suite definition describes a suite unit as forming a single real estate entity, not separately strata-subdividable. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is Mayfair Terrace duplex stock?** No. It is an apartment-form freehold strata (2266 Atkins Ave, LMS4171, 2000, 46 units) — PoCo's one verified 55+ ownership complex, counted in the condos sibling and carried here as an excluded-tenure record only. (§2.)
- **Q: Is there any 55+ ownership I can buy in Citadel?** No — not on any source checked. Citadel = ZERO verified 55+ ownership; the city's one verified 55+ strata is Mayfair Terrace in Central Port Coquitlam. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a duplex here?** Port Coquitlam's own rule governs — suite cap 40% of the principal unit's floor area, suites permitted in a house, duplex or triplex where units are vertically separated; confirm the in-force bylaw text for the specific lot. (§3.)
- **Q: Does Port Coquitlam have SkyTrain?** No. Its rail link is the West Coast Express; a SkyTrain extension is an exploratory business case only — never a station, never a funded project in this file. (§3; §5.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,000,000 fully residential with no exemption = $18,000; $3,500,000 = $93,000 including the further 2% tier (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
