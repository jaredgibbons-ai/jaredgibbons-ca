## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **ALR housing frame (governs where the parcel is in the ALR).** On ALR parcels the ALC housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); a secondary suite in the ALR is permitted only within the principal residence; landowners who wish to pursue other uses or to subdivide ALR property must apply under the Agricultural Land Commission Act and secure ALC approval. - **STRATA block — applies IF a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) is ever the purchase.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. No strata of any form is verified on this ground (§2), so this block is the standing diligence frame only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** Subdivision of ALR property requires ALC approval (above); a fee-simple split of a duplex parcel otherwise requires subdivision approval through the governing (here, FVRD / provincial Approving Officer) process — no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it for Area F. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file above.)
- **Taxes (RURAL regime — distinct from every city regime, never blended).** A property not located in a municipality is in a rural area: its owner receives the property tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays property taxes to the Province, not to the FVRD or the City of Mission. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural property taxes are due and payable on July 2, a 5% penalty is added to any portion that remains unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% penalty is added to any portion that remains unpaid on October 31. Servicing (water/sewer vs well/septic) for Durieu lots: UNVERIFIED at parcel level — feed references to drilled wells, public water at a lot line, creeks and artesian wells are listing copy only. (Research file above; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (for transfers where PTT applies).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate. (§§1, 3.)
- **Q: What is the difference between buying a duplex and buying a half duplex, if one appears?** A duplex purchase is the whole two-unit building and lot on one title. A half duplex is one unit sold separately, which is only possible where the pair was strata-divided (a 2-lot strata plan — Form B diligence) or the parcel was subdivided fee-simple (party-wall title). If neither happened, there is no "half" to buy. (§2.)
- **Q: A listing says "zoned for a secondary dwelling or a suite" — can I rely on it?** No. That is listing text only. In Area F a secondary suite must sit within the principal dwelling (a detached garden suite is not the suite route), and on ALR land a second residence must also fit the ALC frame — confirm both gates for the specific PID. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Can I add a second home on a Durieu acreage?** Two gates apply: the FVRD zoning for the parcel, and — if the parcel is in the ALR — the ALC frame (one principal residence to 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² or 186 m² by parcel size; ALR subdivision requires ALC approval). Parcel ALR status is UNVERIFIED until checked by PID. (§3.)
- **Q: Who do I pay property tax to?** The Province: rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31, under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on a Durieu purchase?** Run the tiers on your own price: $800,000 → $14,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000 (fully residential, no exemption). (§3.)
