## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**BAND / TOPIC + FORM CLASS ONLY at the top: a vacant lot is not duplex stock. No duplex building of any form is verified on a lot in this feed, and no named fee-simple half-duplex subdivision — the one form that would be created BY a land transaction — is verified anywhere in this slug's research or siblings. What this file can state is the FORM CLASS a serviced urban lot can carry, with its zoning basis.**

- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Chilliwack's own rule, never borrowed.** Under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 (ss. 2, 4.06, 5.04, confirmed in rules-gaps item 7f): a secondary suite is capped at the **lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling**, one accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot, garden suites are capped at 100 m², and parking rules sit at s. 5.04. These figures are the added-unit side of what a serviced lot can carry. (Research file above.)

Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a Chilliwack lot and split it into two half-duplex lots?** Not on anything in this file: zone minimums, the subdivision process, DCCs and servicing requirements are all UNVERIFIED in this slug's research, and any fee-simple split needs subdivision approval confirmed with the City for the specific parcel — plus ALC approval first if the parcel is in the ALR. Get the City's position in writing before subjects come off. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a serviced Chilliwack lot carry a duplex — or more?** Potentially more: Bylaw 5395 allows 3 to 4 units on eligible parcels inside the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. Eligibility is parcel-specific. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same as a half duplex?** No. A bare-land strata lot is a vacant lot inside a strata plan — you buy land, with strata diligence on the plan. A half duplex is half of a built two-unit building on its own strata lot or fee-simple lot. Different purchases, different diligence. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite or garden suite to a duplex I build on a lot?** Under Chilliwack's own figures: a secondary suite to the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling, one accessory dwelling unit per lot, garden suites to 100 m², parking at s. 5.04 — all subject to the parcel's zone and permit areas. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex built on a subdivided lot?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata (bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, age bylaw against the settled SPA position — only 55+ valid), plus the registered party-wall / easement documents where the form is fee-simple. A 2-lot strata sits below the 5-lot depreciation-report threshold on lot count alone. (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a lot, and later on a half duplex?** Each transfer is taxed on its own price on the standard tiers — $500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $8,000; $1,500,000 = $28,000 (worked in §3). GST on the lot itself is UNVERIFIED in this file — have a lawyer cost it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Does the first-time buyers' GST rebate help with a bare lot?** No — Bill C-4's rebate (Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA) is for new HOUSING, not bare land. Whether a lot-plus-build structure qualifies in part is UNVERIFIED here. (§3.)
- **Q: Why are there no land prices, price-per-acre figures or lot-size minimums in this file?** CADREB publishes board-wide statistics only with no land category, SnapStats does not cover Chilliwack, and the Zoning Bylaw minimums could not be opened in this research pass (429) — so every such figure is UNVERIFIED here rather than borrowed or estimated.
