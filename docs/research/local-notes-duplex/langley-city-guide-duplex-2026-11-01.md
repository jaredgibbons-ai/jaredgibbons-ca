## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan (Langley City frame):** the City's OCP Bylaw No. 3200 (adopted November 22, 2021) and Zoning Bylaw, 2025, No. 3300 (NUMBER ONLY — its adoption date is conflicted in sources and UNVERIFIED; a live page's March 9, 2026 adoption date was NOT adopted, and that item is distinct from any March-12 item) govern City parcels. A Township fact applied to a City property is an error, and vice versa. (Research file, §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/langley-city.md, cited in the research file.)
- **SSMUH and suites (Langley City frame — SETTLED in the research):** the City amended its Zoning Bylaw to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on RS1 and RS2 lots by **Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 — RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop** (the City's own tier — the Township has NO 6-unit tier, and the two are never blended). Secondary suites (Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300): a suite must **not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the gross floor area of the building** — the City's own cap, never borrowed — the registered owner must reside on the lot, at least one extra off-street parking space is required, and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. That cap applies to a suite inside a City duplex exactly as it applies to a suite in a single-family house. (Research file, §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/langley-city.md, cited in the research file.)

- **Floodplain:** the guide page itself warns buyers to ask the City about low-lying lots near the Nicomekl floodplain — confirm the exact lot with the City before pricing in a build. - **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on City ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a City duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, and the parcel's RS1/RS2 zone and lot dimensions decide whether a City lot can split at all — no zone minimum figure is stated here because none is carried in this slug's research, and none is invented. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file, §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a duplex development in Langley City I can buy into?** No named duplex development is verified in this program. The City's documented duplex-relevant form is the RS1/RS2 house lot carrying the City's SSMUH permissions — 4 units, or 6 within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — and its verified attached stock is the exactly-3 apartment-condo roster counted in CONDOS (§2). (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Is Langley City the same as the Township of Langley?** No — the City of Langley is its own municipality, separate from the Township, with its own OCP Bylaw No. 3200 (adopted November 22, 2021) and its own Zoning Bylaw, 2025, No. 3300. The two Langleys have different councils, bylaws, tax notices and planning frames; never quote a Township rule for a City property. (§1.)
- **Q: How many units can a Langley City single-family lot hold?** Under Bylaw No. 3284 (adopted June 17, 2024), RS1 and RS2 lots permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — the City's own tier. A coach house is a Township form (fee-simple second unit under Township Bylaw 2500 s.102) and is not a City rule. (§3.)
- **Q: What are Langley City's secondary suite rules — including inside a duplex?** Under Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300, a suite must not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, the registered owner must reside on the lot, at least one extra off-street parking space is required, and the suite must be permitted, inspected and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. A listing's "suite" claim is a claim to verify against that registry position. (§3.)
- **Q: A detached listing says "fourplex potential" — is it a duplex?** No. The 19600 49 Avenue card's wording is listing marketing for the lot's SSMUH position under Bylaw 3284 — a build possibility on a house lot, not a built duplex in either legal form on any primary source here. Price the lot on the City rules in §3. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: How did Langley City sell in September 2026?** The City sub-area recorded 64 detached listings with 8 sold (13%) and 195 Condos & Townhomes listings with 34 sold (17%). No City-only median is published — the combined Langley community medians (detached $1,345,000; attached $611,000) blend in the Township and are not City figures; never quote them as a City price. (§1.)
- **Q: Can I run an Airbnb in a City duplex half?** UNVERIFIED — no City of Langley STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page in this program. Confirm with the City before any short-term-rental claim is written. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $1,200,000? At $2,000,000?** On the worked tiers in §3 — fully residential, no exemption — $22,000 at $1,200,000 and $38,000 at $2,000,000. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. (§3.)
- **Q: Is SkyTrain in the City yet?** No station today. The guide page states the Surrey Langley SkyTrain extension runs 16 km from King George Station to 203 Street in the City, with Langley City Centre Station east of 203 Street and Industrial Avenue and a current anticipated in-service date of late 2029; the City's vision page likewise states SkyTrain is coming by the end of 2029.
