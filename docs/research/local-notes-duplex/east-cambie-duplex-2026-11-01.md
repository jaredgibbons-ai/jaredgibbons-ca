## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A duplex-STYLE townhouse inside a multi-unit strata complex — the Cambie Gardens form below — is a townhouse strata lot whose layout reads as a duplex; it is not a 2-lot strata duplex and not a fee-simple half, and its count lives in the townhomes file. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Richmond's zoning is Zoning Bylaw 8500 — the area's base zone is Single Detached (RS1/RS2) under Section 8.1, with the RS1/E subzone on Cambie Road as above. The governing plan is OCP Bylaw 9000. The exact duplex position for a specific East Cambie lot is UNCONFIRMED until its zone and subzone are read. (Townhomes sibling above.)
- **SSMUH / suites — the City's own position.** The City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot on covered lots, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. That suite/ADU position is Richmond's own rule, carried exactly; no other municipality's cap is borrowed. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development (via the townhomes sibling above.)
- **Flood — the Richmond-specific diligence item.** All lands within Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels (FCLs) that habitable floors must meet unless an exemption applies, behind 49 km of dikes designed for a 1:500 event with raising to 4.7 m under way. For a ground-oriented duplex or duplex-style unit, the FCL position of the specific building is a first-order diligence item. (Townhomes sibling above.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan); Cambie Gardens is a 13-lot strata and carries this block in its townhomes home.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone** (a 13-lot strata such as Cambie Gardens is over the threshold). Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, measured against the RS-zone lot standards above; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Richmond's servicing position is Bylaws 8751/8752, VERIFIED in the verified ledger. Amenity Density / ADEU charges in Richmond apply in the West Cambie / Alexandra area ONLY — no ADEU charge position is asserted for East Cambie. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in East Cambie?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. A Cambie Gardens unit is a strata lot in a 13-unit townhouse strata, which is a different purchase. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is Cambie Gardens a duplex development?** It is a 13-unit freehold-strata townhouse complex of duplex-style, two-level homes (plan BCS1148, built 2005), counted in its townhomes home. "Duplex-style" describes the layout, not a separable half-duplex title. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Cambie Gardens age-restricted?** No age restriction appears in any source checked (55-plus sibling). (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the strata — for Cambie Gardens, the 13-lot strata's full package including its depreciation report (over the 5-lot threshold). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,200,000 fully residential with no exemption = $22,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Does flood risk affect a duplex purchase here?** Yes, structurally: all of Richmond is designated flood plain under Bylaw No. 8204 with flood construction levels for habitable floors. Ask for the specific building's FCL position. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a duplex here?** Address-based in SD38 — the documented locator run for 11671 Cambie Road returned Mitchell Elementary and H.J. Cambie Secondary for that address only. King George Secondary is a Vancouver school and is never an East Cambie answer. (§5.)
