## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A duplex-STYLE home inside a strata townhouse complex is a strata lot in that complex — it is not a 2-lot strata duplex, and its diligence is the complex's Form B, not a duplex file's. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 governs the pocket's houses (Single Detached RS1/RS2 framework, s.8.1, minimum lot sizes by subzone in Section 8.1.8, RSM SSMUH zones in the bylaw as amended); the OCP governing is Bylaw 9000 (Schedule 2.2B Thompson (Terra Nova) for this ground). Farm land beside the pocket sits in the AG1 / ALR framework — an ALR parcel is never read under the residential duplex position. The zone of a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until read. (Research file above.)
- **SSMUH position.** The City allows at least one secondary suite and/or coach house (ADU) per lot on covered lots, and the provincial Bill 44 minimums carried in the ledger are 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots, expressed in Richmond through the RSM zones. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the city's own rule.** Richmond's suite rule is Section 5.4: a secondary suite is 33.0 m² minimum, with a maximum of the lesser of 40% of the dwelling or 110 m² (Richmond position carried in this program's Richmond files). That is RICHMOND'S OWN rule — never borrowed into another city's file. (Brief chain; research-file Q&A record for the Richmond grounds.)
- **Flood position.** All of Richmond is designated flood plain under Flood Bylaw 8204, with Flood Construction Levels and 49 km of dikes in the city frame — Terra Nova includes the foreshore outside the dyke to the north and west in the Heritage Inventory's description, so a duplex purchase here is read against the specific lot's FCL and dyke position. (Verified ledger, brief chain; research file above.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), and, for the two named complexes, to their own strata diligence as complexes.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. (Quilchena Green at 91 homes and Sonoma as a gated community are both far above the threshold on lot count — their own depreciation position is read on their Form B records, not under the 2-lot sentence.) Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the Section 8.1.8 subzone minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. Inside Quilchena Green and Sonoma there is no party-wall-agreement question of this kind: the homes are strata lots and the strata's bylaws and Form B govern. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Richmond's servicing Bylaws 8751/8752 are VERIFIED in the verified ledger and are carried as verified here. ADEU charges are a West Cambie / Alexandra position ONLY — never asserted here. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A strata lot in Quilchena Green or Sonoma, like a half duplex, is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Quilchena Green a duplex complex?** It is a gated townhouse complex of duplex-style / 2-level townhomes (91 homes, strata LMS423, built 1992) counted once in the townhomes file. - **Q: Sonoma — townhouse or duplex?** Both filings exist inside one gated strata community: units are filed as both Townhouse and Duplex/Half Duplex by sub-type (townhomes sibling's carried conflict). Built form governs — the complex counts once as a townhouse complex, and a unit's purchase is a strata-lot purchase either way. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Terra Nova?** Only if that side is legally separate outside a complex — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. None is verified by name here; a single-lot duplex sells whole. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is Quilchena Green 55+?** The townhomes sibling carries the 55+ label as FLAGGED — not asserted as verified in this file. Read any age bylaw on the complex's Form B against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid; 45+ VOID). (§2; §3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). For Quilchena Green / Sonoma units the same Form B diligence applies at complex scale. (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. The two named complexes are far above the threshold — ask for their reports directly. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. Inside the two named complexes, the strata governs instead. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a duplex here?** Spul'u'kwuks Elementary sits physically inside the pocket, and the locator result for its own address is Spul'u'kwuks Elementary / Burnett Secondary — single-address basis only. (§5.)
