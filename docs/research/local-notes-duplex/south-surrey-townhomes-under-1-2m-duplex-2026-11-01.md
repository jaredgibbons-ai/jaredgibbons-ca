## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning and plan (Surrey frame).** Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 governs the Surrey ground; the governing OCP is Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is REPLACED and never cited as current. Which zone permits a two-unit (duplex) form on a given parcel is a parcel-by-parcel read of Bylaw 12000. White Rock units in the band sit under White Rock's own bylaws, never Surrey's — check which municipality the unit is in before applying any rule. (Research file above, §6; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-development/zoning; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan)
- **SSMUH position (Surrey frame).** Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial SSMUH framework; Surrey's Jul 8, 2024 changes allow a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. (Research file above, §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (Surrey's own rule).** A secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit** under Zoning By-law No. 12000 — Surrey's rule, never borrowed (the "90 m²" figure is NOT current, per this slug's research); a suite is not a separate strata lot and cannot be sold independently. (Research file above, §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Strata frame for the townhouse stock this band surfaces.** In BC, townhome condos are governed by the Strata Property Act — the documents that matter are the Form B (Information Certificate), the bylaws and rules, recent minutes, the budget and the depreciation report; the live page states the strata must provide the Form B within one week. Read the strata documents before the unit — an older complex's depreciation report and levy history matter more than its list price. (Research file above, §§4, 6; https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.html, read 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. Servicing: Surrey Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED from the City's own pages in the ledger. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is ever stated. Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED). STRs in Surrey are permitted, owner-only, at $358/yr carried with the City's 2024 $350/yr proposal disclosed. (Program discipline, D1 brief; standing Surrey frame, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000** (the band's ceiling price, on the same tiers); $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. The page's specific exemption thresholds are carried in §5 as site copy only, exactly as its research carries them. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. (Research file above, §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax)
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
