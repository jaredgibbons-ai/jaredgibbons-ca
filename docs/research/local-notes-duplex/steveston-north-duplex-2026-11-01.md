## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Richmond Zoning Bylaw No. 8500).** Houses in this district sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with minimum lot areas by subzone as stated in §2, and SSMUH zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL applied under Bill 44; the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the OCP, Bylaw 9000) guides the district's southern streets. The exact zone for a specific lot is read off the current Bylaw 8500 text for that lot — zone entitlements asserted beyond that read are UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/bylaws.htm (Research file §4; settled Richmond frame.)
- **SSMUH position — Richmond's own, as the City states it.** The City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots; the duplex is the baseline form under that framework. Verify the lot's subzone minimums in Bylaw 8500 Section 8.1.8 before promising redevelopment value. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm (Research file §§4, 6.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Richmond's own position, as this ground's sources state it.** A secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in the RS1/RS2 zones, governed by Section 5.4 of Bylaw 8500. No suite size cap figure is stated in this slug's own research, so NO cap figure is carried in this file — no other city's figure is ever borrowed, and no figure from another slug's research is imported here. The suite position for a specific home is UNCONFIRMED until read in the current zoning text and, for a strata unit, the strata's bylaws. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm (Research file §4.)
- **Flood.** The district is on Lulu Island in the Fraser River floodplain, behind the City perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations; the system is designed for a 1:500 flood event and dikes are being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. The Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements for new construction. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex (and in full to Heron).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — a 28-unit complex like Heron is INSIDE that cycle on lot count; **a 2-lot strata duplex, by contrast, is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone** — the distinction is stated precisely for both forms. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the titles — read them. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Richmond servicing Bylaws 8751/8752 VERIFIED in the verified ledger. ADEU charges are West Cambie / Alexandra ONLY — no ADEU position is carried for Steveston North. (Verified ledger; D20 frame.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex here?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. No such property is verified by name here; a single-lot duplex sells whole. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count — unlike Heron (28 units), which is inside it. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add units on a detached lot here?** The City states at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots — verify the lot's subzone minimums in Section 8.1.8 before pricing redevelopment value. (§3.)

- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
