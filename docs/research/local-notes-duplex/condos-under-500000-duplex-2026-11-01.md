## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted — read per municipality, never from this band file.** A duplex's permitting frame is its own municipality's current zoning bylaw and SSMUH implementation, carried in its home-area file (for Surrey ground: Zoning By-law No. 12000, SSMUH Jul 8, 2024, suite cap 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area, servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED, OCP Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026) — never 18020-as-current). This band file asserts no zoning position of its own for any ground. The zone for a specific property is read off its municipality's current zoning for that lot. (Program discipline, D1 brief chain.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — each city's own rule, never borrowed.** The suite cap is the rule of the municipality the duplex sits in (Surrey 40% of principal habitable area; Township of Langley 120 m²; Langley City lesser of 100 m² / 40%; Abbotsford under 50%) — the home-area file for the property states which rule applies. No suite figure is stated for this band as a whole, because the band has no municipality. (Settled city ledgers, D1 brief.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) wherever one is bought.** Form B diligence applies in full — the Province's strata buying guidance directs buyers to the Form B information certificate, the Form F, strata bylaws and rules, fees and special levies; age restrictions, pet rules and rental restrictions live in those documents, not in the listing price. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners (read 2026-11-01); https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Approving Officer process of the parcel's own municipality, against its current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. A purchase inside this band meets only the first two tiers and never the 3% tier or the further 2%. Fully residential, no exemption: $450,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $250,000 = **$7,000**; $500,000 (the band ceiling) → **$8,000**; and the program anchors above the band for scale: $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; **$3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier** — $93,000 governs at $3.5M (see §5). A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Exemptions that reach this band on price alone: the first time home buyers' program exempts a qualifying purchaser on the first $500,000, with a full exemption requiring a fair market value of $835,000 or less (partial to $860,000) — every home on this page is inside that value range on price alone, though the program's other conditions still have to be met; the newly built home exemption's full threshold is $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (read 2026-11-01); https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers (read 2026-11-01.)
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale condo, duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does this band file list duplexes or half duplexes under $500,000?** No. It is a region-wide condo band file with no roster; its feed as read showed apartment-style condos only, and a duplex's home is its own area file, where its title form is verified on title, not on a card. (§1; §2.)
- **Q: A card somewhere calls a sub-$500K property a half duplex — can I buy just that half?** Only if that half is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot. The card settles nothing; check the title in the property's home-area file. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: How many condos are for sale under $500,000 right now?** No sourced number exists: no source publishes a cross-region under-$500,000 condo count, SnapStats publishes no price-band cut and combines condos with townhomes, and no pagination count appeared in the page text read for the research — so no band count, share or median is stated here. (§1.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at the $500,000 ceiling?** $8,000 on a fully residential $500,000 purchase with no exemption — and a qualifying first-time buyer, exempt on the first $500,000, pays $0 on those inputs. Tier math in §3. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Why is $500,000 a clean ceiling for financing?** At $500,000 or less the minimum down payment is a flat 5%; one dollar over it, the portion above $500,000 needs 10% down. (§1.) https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). The same document set prices a condo in this band — Form B, Form F, fees and special levies live in the strata documents, not the listing price. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a duplex I buy near this price point?** That turns on the suite rule of the municipality the duplex sits in — each city's own cap, stated in the property's home-area file, never borrowed from another city. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
