## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies in an ordinary sale; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together, and a court-ordered sale of it sells the whole title the court has before it. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **The court-ordered sale process (settled treatment).** Foreclosure and court-ordered sales in BC run under the Supreme Court Civil Rules — Rule 21-7 (foreclosure proceedings) and Rule 13-5 (conduct of sale) — with Practice Direction PD-66 governing the sale-approval process and Form 60 the standard offer form in that process. A purchase completes only on court approval of the sale; until approval, a "sold" report on a court-ordered listing is a pending-approval record, not a completed transfer. (Settled foreclosure treatment, brief chain; research file above.)
- **Form B on a court sale — UNCONFIRMED.** In an ordinary strata purchase the seller provides Form B diligence; on a court-ordered sale the seller-side channel is the court process, and **Form B availability for a strata duplex / strata half duplex sold by court order is UNCONFIRMED in this program** — no official source read for this topic confirms it. Do not assume the ordinary document set will be produced; confirm what will actually be provided, from the court file and the listing, before bidding. (Settled treatment, brief chain.)
- **PTT/GST on court-ordered sales specifically — UNCONFIRMED.** This topic's condos sibling states the position in terms: no official source read for the topic states a special Property Transfer Tax or GST treatment for court-ordered sales specifically. The general PTT tiers (worked below) are the program's settled general position; whether and how they apply to a specific court-ordered duplex purchase — and any GST position on it — is UNCONFIRMED here and is confirmed with the buyer's lawyer/notary on the specific file before bidding. (Condos sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/langley-foreclosures-court-ordered-sales-condos-2026-11-01.md.)
- **Where duplexes are permitted (frame only).** Zoning does not change because a sale is court-ordered: a Langley duplex sits under its own municipality's framework (Township: SSMUH 6020, NO 6-plex tier, suite cap 120 m², servicing 5382 CONFIRMED; the property's own area file carries its ground's frame). This topic file asserts no zoning position of its own. (Standing Township frame, brief chain.)
- **STRATA block — the ordinary position, carried so the gap above is visible.** In an ordinary strata duplex / strata half duplex purchase (2-lot strata plan): Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. On a court-ordered sale, which parts of that document set will actually be produced is the UNCONFIRMED point above. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A court-ordered sale does not subdivide anything: the title sold is the title that exists. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles from the court file searches, not from listing copy. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — general worked examples with inputs + tier math (general position only; court-sale application UNCONFIRMED above).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only (general position).** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A court-ordered resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate, and any GST position on a court sale specifically is UNCONFIRMED above. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy one half of a duplex at a court-ordered sale?** Only if the title before the court is that half — a strata lot or a subdivided fee-simple lot. The court sells the title in the proceeding, nothing smaller. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Will I get a Form B for a strata half duplex sold by court order?** UNCONFIRMED — no official source read for this topic confirms Form B availability on court sales. Confirm what will actually be produced before bidding, and price the gap. (§3.)
- **Q: Is PTT payable on a court-ordered duplex purchase, and is GST?** UNCONFIRMED for court-ordered sales specifically in this program — the general tiers are worked in §3, but the application to a specific court file is confirmed with your lawyer/notary before bidding. (§3.)
- **Q: What rules govern the sale itself?** Supreme Court Civil Rules 21-7 and 13-5, Practice Direction PD-66, and Form 60 for the offer — with court approval required before the sale completes. (§3.)
- **Q: The listing says "sold" — is the duplex mine?** Not until the court approves the sale. A sold report pre-approval is a pending record. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count — one more document not to assume on a court file. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals after I buy?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half bought at court sale?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says — read the titles from the searches, not the listing. (§3.)
- **Q: Does this topic file list Langley duplex foreclosures?** No. It is a topic file with no roster; a specific property's form lives in its own area file. (§2.)
- **Q: Are the taxes on a court sale the "3% to $3M, 5% above" shorthand?** That shorthand is wrong as a general rate (full tiers in §3) AND the court-sale application is separately UNCONFIRMED — two different reasons not to rely on it. (§3; §5.)
