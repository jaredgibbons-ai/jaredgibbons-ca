## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**TOPIC — NO ROSTER at the top: this file names no duplex stock as its own and takes no count.** This topic has no housing stock of its own; what it governs is the range of housing the Delta plans cover. The duplex content is the FORM CLASS those plans create:

- **The urban form class.** Single-detached and duplex housing sits under the RS and RD zones of Delta's zoning bylaw, assigned parcel by parcel on the zoning maps; the RD zones are Delta's duplex zones, so the duplex form class in Delta is the individual duplex building on a single lot built under the zoning of its day. That is a FORM CLASS statement: no individual Delta duplex is verified here by name. A specific duplex is verified on its title (one lot, strata plan, or subdivided pair). (Research file above; https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf, read 2026-11-01.)
- **The SSMUH layer on that form class.** Delta's provincially required zoning updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots; the City frames SSMUH as ground-oriented housing between single-detached homes and condominiums, intended to be attainable for middle-income families. SSMUH adds unit potential on a lot — it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Research file above; https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future, read 2026-11-01.)
- **The farm form — which is NOT duplex stock.** On Agriculture (A)-designated land, the A1 Agriculture zone provides the farm-residence forms (one farm house per lot, a suite inside it, an accessory farm dwelling under the ALC caps in §3) on a single farm parcel under one title. Those are ALR farm-residence forms, not a duplex, are not sold separately, and are never counted as duplex or half-duplex stock here. (Research file above; https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Tenure under the plans:** both strata and freehold tenures exist across Delta; no Delta-wide tenure split is stated in this file: UNVERIFIED (no official source read publishes one for this topic). (Research file above, §5.)

- **Directory / listing mentions = RECORDS ONLY.** Any directory entry, listing description or feed card that calls a Delta property a "duplex" or "half duplex" is a record of marketing wording, labelled as such, and is never verification of a separable title. No such mention is counted in this file.

- **ALR housing caps — the farm layer, as the ALC states it.** Agricultural Land Reserve rules apply on top of the OCP designation and zoning where land is in the ALR; the Agricultural Land Commission generally allows no more than one residence per ALR parcel, a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area, and one additional detached residence is permitted up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on larger parcels — anything beyond that needs ALC approval. (Research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Development Permit Areas.** The OCP's Appendix B carries the Development Permit Areas and Guidelines, including the Tsawwassen Urban Centre DPA guidelines and the hazard DPAs SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment) and SD3 Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands. Work in the right order: OCP designation first (s. 1.5.1 / Map 2), then the parcel's zone on the zoning maps, then any Development Permit Area in Appendix B — a use can pass one layer and fail the next. (Research file above; https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf, read 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate, and the rebate does not apply to bare farm land itself. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is Delta's current Official Community Plan?** The Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future", adopted by Delta Council on July 8, 2024 — a single consolidated plan whose Land Use Plan (s. 1.5, Map 2) sets the designations for the whole city. (§3.) https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Is there still a Tsawwassen Area Plan or a Ladner Area Plan in Delta's OCP?** No. Those were schedules of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950 (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1). Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) superseded them with one consolidated plan — there is no Schedule D.1 in the current OCP, and any document citing one is citing a superseded plan. (§3.)
- **Q: How do I find what can be built on a Delta property?** Check the property's OCP designation in the Land Use Plan (s. 1.5.1 designations, applied on Map 2), then its zone on the zoning maps, then whether a Development Permit Area applies — all three layers govern. (§3.)
- **Q: What did SSMUH change on Delta's single-family and duplex lots?** Delta introduced the provincially required zoning updates allowing up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots, as part of the framework the 2024 OCP provides for Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing. (§3.) https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me split a duplex into two titles?** No — SSMUH adds unit potential on a lot; a separately titled half needs a strata plan or a subdivision. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: What does the Agriculture (A) designation mean for a farm parcel with a house and a suite?** In the OCP's s. 1.5.1 designations, Agriculture (A) is the designation applied on Map 2 to maintain agricultural lands; where the land is also in the Agricultural Land Reserve, ALC rules on residences and uses apply on top of the City's A-zone zoning — the suite and accessory dwelling are farm-residence forms on one parcel, not a duplex. (§2; §3.)
