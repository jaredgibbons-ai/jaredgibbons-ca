## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. On this corridor the definition question comes second: the first question is which municipality the lot is in, because the two sides carry different zoning, different OCPs and different servicing positions. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES — TWO RULEBOOKS, EACH ON ITS OWN SIDE

- **CITY side (south of Highway 1) — where duplexes are permitted.** The City of North Vancouver's frame: OCP Bylaw 8400; Zoning Bylaw 6700. A City-side duplex is read against the specific lot's zone in Bylaw 6700, which is UNCONFIRMED until read for the lot. No District rule is applied to a City lot, and no City rule is applied to a District lot. (Brief chain, City NV frame.)
- **DISTRICT side (north of Highway 1) — where duplexes are permitted.** The District of North Vancouver's frame: OCP Bylaw 7900; Zoning Bylaw 3210 (s.525 detached residential zones). Since June 2024 a principal house plus a secondary suite plus a coach house (three units total) has been permitted on single-family zoned lots within the Urban Containment Boundary on the District side; the District's GEOweb SSMUH position runs up to 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units above that, and 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of frequent transit. That framework adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (D3 brief frame for District NV.)

- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), on either side.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Approving Officer process of the municipality the lot is actually in; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it for either side. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing — the sharpest two-rulebook line in this file.** City side: servicing Bylaw 8014 is the City-files position, carried in City files only. District side: the District's servicing position is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — no servicing bylaw number is asserted for a District-side lot, and Bylaw 8014 is NEVER carried into a District position. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. PTT is provincial — the tiers are the same on both sides of the line. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which municipality is a Boulevard duplex in — City or District?** It depends which side of Highway 1 the lot sits on: Grand Boulevard south of the highway is City of North Vancouver ground; Grand Boulevard East / Boulevard Crescent north of it is District ground. As a sub-area boundary the split point is UNVERIFIED — confirm the lot's municipality on its address/title before applying any rule. (§1; §3.)
- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex on the corridor?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. None is verified by name here; a single-lot duplex sells whole. Check the municipality first, then the title. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Can I buy a townhouse on Grand Boulevard itself?** None verified — 0 strata, 0 fee-simple in the townhomes file; no named townhome complex on Grand Boulevard, Grand Boulevard East or Boulevard Crescent was confirmed on any source checked. The corridor's verified form is detached houses on individual lots. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard provincial tiers — the same on both sides of the line: $2,000,000 fully residential with no exemption = $38,000; $3,500,000 = $93,000 including the further 2% tier (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for Boulevard?** No. North Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage and no duplex category exists in any case. (§1.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a duplex here?** Queensbury and Ridgeway elementaries sit beside the corridor and Sutherland Secondary west of it — and SD44 is reviewing catchments for a new Cloverley elementary opening 2026/27, so re-check for that year. No corridor catchment is confirmed. (§5.)
