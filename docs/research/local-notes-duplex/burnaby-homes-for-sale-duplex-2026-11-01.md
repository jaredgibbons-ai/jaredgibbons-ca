## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **SSMUH position (Burnaby frame — the R1 district).** Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial framework, and Burnaby's R1 SSMUH District (June 2024) states the local ladder: **3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, 6 on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop**, with the total unit count **inclusive of any secondary suites**; the program frame records that multi-unit lots under this district **may be stratified** — the rule route by which a new half-duplex (strata-half) form can lawfully be created on this ground. (Research file above; program Burnaby frame, brief chain.)

- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), including a future R1 strata split.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. - **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it (and the servicing base that would govern it is UNCONFIRMED, above). Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is the difference between buying a duplex and buying a half duplex here?** A duplex purchase is the whole building and lot on one title. A half duplex is one unit sold separately, which is only possible where the pair was strata-divided (a 2-lot strata plan — Form B diligence) or the parcel was subdivided fee-simple (party-wall title). If neither happened, there is no "half" to buy. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a new half duplex be created on a Burnaby detached lot?** The rule route exists: Burnaby's R1 SSMUH District allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots and 6 on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop (suites counted inside the total), and multi-unit lots under the district may be stratified. Whether a specific lot qualifies is a parcel read of Zoning Bylaw 2026 No. 14815 — never the repealed 4742. (§3.)
- **Q: Are the 36 townhouse complexes in the assembly duplex options?** No. They are strata townhouse complexes counted once in their sub-area townhomes files and cross-referenced here — townhouse form is not duplex form, and this file takes no count for them. Metrotown, Highgate and SFU have no townhomes file at all, which is a coverage limit, not a zero. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Kensington Gardens a duplex or half-duplex option?** No — it is an undivided-interest holding, excluded program-wide; it is not strata or fee-simple stock of any form. Hall Towers is likewise excluded as BC Housing non-market rental. (§2.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a Burnaby duplex carry a secondary suite?** Suites are a permitted R1 form counted inside the unit total, and a secondary suite must remain on the same title as the primary unit and cannot be stratified. Burnaby's suite size cap and owner-occupancy rule are UNVERIFIED in this slug's sources — no figure is borrowed from another city. (§3.)
