## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**HONEST ZERO — no duplex stock of any form is verified on this ground.**

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Why the zero is honest, on the sources:** the research file's housing profile states the ground's form directly — almost entirely strata apartment form in residential towers along the waterfront and on West Cordova Street, West Hastings Street and the downtown street grid under the Official Development Plan, and **single detached houses are not a Coal Harbour housing form**. Parcels sit under the Downtown District (DD) Schedule of Bylaw No. 3575 with the Coal Harbour Official Development Plan (By-law 6754) — commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses in conformity with an Official Development Plan — not under any house/duplex district, and no minimum lot size figure for DD parcels is stated in the schedule (UNVERIFIED). There is no house-lot fabric on this ground for a duplex form class to sit on. (Research file above; https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/ODP/odp-coal-harbour.pdf; https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf)
- **Documented, never duplex stock — the tower townhouse components.** The developer's own project pages record townhouse homes inside Coal Harbour's tower developments: Cascina, 590 Nicola Street (117 suites + 30 townhouses per the condos sibling's developer record); Denia, 499 Broughton Street (86 suites + 4 townhomes); Escala, 323 Jervis Street (98 suites + 7 townhouses); 1 Harbour Green, 1169 West Cordova Street, and 2 Harbour Green, West Cordova Street (each includes townhouse homes; per-building counts UNCONFIRMED); and Carina, 1233 West Cordova Street (17 adjacent townhomes recorded by a building source alongside its apartments, with a unit-total conflict flagged in the condos sibling). These are townhouse-form strata lots inside tower strata — counted in the condos sibling, documented in the townhomes sibling, and NO duplex count is taken here. A townhouse lot inside a tower strata is not a half duplex: it is not one of two units in a two-unit building under either legal form in the definitions above. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted — not on this ground.** Coal Harbour parcels sit under the Downtown District (DD) Schedule of Zoning and Development Bylaw No. 3575 together with the Coal Harbour Official Development Plan (By-law 6754); the DD schedule permits commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses in conformity with an Official Development Plan, with development permits approved by the Development Permit Board or the Director of Planning. No DD provision for a house-form duplex on a house lot is stated in the sources read, and none is asserted. (Research file above.)
- **SSMUH / R1-1 position — the framework, and why it does not reach this ground.** Vancouver's SSMUH framework is R1-1 under Bylaw No. 3575 **where it applies**: minimum site area 306 m² (house / duplex / 3–4-unit multiple dwelling, 10.0 m frontage), 464 m² (5 units), 557 m² (6–8 units); a house may include a secondary suite + laneway house; multiplex up to 6 strata / 8 rental units. Coal Harbour's DD ground is not R1-1 ground — the R1-1 figures are carried here only so a reader does not import them onto a Coal Harbour parcel; they govern Vancouver's house-lot districts, not the downtown peninsula's DD parcels. (Brief chain; research file above.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the city's own rule.** Vancouver's house-form suite rule (secondary suite + laneway house inside the R1-1 framework above) belongs to its house-lot districts; no suite rule is asserted for a Coal Harbour DD parcel, because no source opened for this slug states one for this ground. (Brief chain.)
- **Floodplain.** The City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain; parcels in the floodplain are subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for shoreline setbacks and flood construction levels. Whether a specific Coal Harbour parcel is in the floodplain is UNVERIFIED — read the building's strata documents and insurance against it. (Research file above.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), stated for completeness; no such strata is verified on this ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. For Coal Harbour's actual stock — tower strata — the same Form B, depreciation and CRF diligence governs at tower scale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the municipality's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Vancouver's Subdivision (servicing) Bylaw 5208 (fees Bylaw 14538) is VERIFIED in the verified ledger. (Brief chain.)
- **Empty Homes Tax — Vancouver only.** Vancouver owners must submit an annual property status declaration (due February 3, 2026 for the cycle in the research file); the Empty Homes Tax rate is 3% — a Vancouver-only tax and a standing check on any Coal Harbour purchase, where investor-held units are common. The Province's additional school tax can also apply at Coal Harbour price points: 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. (Research file above; brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any duplex stock in Coal Harbour?** No — honest zero. The ground is downtown peninsula apartment/commercial parcels under the DD Schedule and the Coal Harbour Official Development Plan; single detached houses are not a Coal Harbour housing form on the sources read. (§2.)
- **Q: Are the townhouses at Cascina, Denia, Escala or the Harbour Green buildings half duplexes?** No. They are townhouse-form strata lots inside tower strata — counted in the condos sibling, documented in the townhomes sibling. A half duplex is one of two units in a two-unit building, split by a 2-lot strata plan or a fee-simple subdivision; a tower townhouse is neither. (§2.)
- **Q: Does Vancouver's R1-1 multiplex framework apply to a Coal Harbour parcel?** No — R1-1 is the SSMUH framework where it applies, on Vancouver's house-lot districts. Coal Harbour parcels are DD ground governed with the Official Development Plan (By-law 6754). (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence matters instead on this ground?** Tower-strata diligence: Form B, the depreciation report, contingency reserve and any special levies, read against the floodplain position — plus the annual Empty Homes Tax declaration. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. No 2-lot strata is verified on this ground in any case. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a Coal Harbour purchase?** On the unit's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000; $3,500,000 = $93,000 including the further 2% tier (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: What is the Empty Homes Tax check?** A Vancouver-only 3% tax with an annual property status declaration on every purchase — budget the declaration into closing, and the tax itself if the unit will sit empty. (§3.)
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says — the general rule, stated here for completeness; no fee-simple half is verified on this ground. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for Coal Harbour?** No — duplexes have no separate published category in this program's sources, there is no duplex stock here to price, and Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage, so no market figure is stated at all. (§1.)
