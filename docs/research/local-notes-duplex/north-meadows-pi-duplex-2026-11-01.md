## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (City of Pitt Meadows Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011).** The zones on this ground, with their subdivision minimums per the research: A-1 General Agricultural — minimum lot area 8 ha, one farm house per lot to a maximum gross floor area of 500 sq m; A-2 Large Lot Agricultural — minimum 16 ha; RR-2 Large Lot Rural Residential — minimum 4 ha; RR-3 — minimum 2 ha; RR-4 — minimum 1 ha (RR zones are for land outside the Agricultural Land Reserve); RS Large Lot Residential — minimum 0.2 ha. The exact zone for a specific lot is read off the current Bylaw 2505 text for that lot. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **SSMUH position.** The City's SSMUH framework (SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986, adopted June 25, 2024 — lots 280 sq m or less up to 3 units, lots over 280 sq m up to 4 units, lots over 280 sq m within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units, per the Pitt Meadows guide research as carried in the lots-and-land sibling) makes the duplex the baseline two-unit form on eligible urban lots; most of this sub-area's lots are large RS/RR/agricultural lots, so the accessory-dwelling rules below are the operative second-unit route on most North Meadows ground — read the specific lot's zone before assuming either route. (Lots-and-land sibling §3.)
- **Second dwellings — Pitt Meadows' own rule only.** A **garden suite is permitted in the A-1, A-2, RR-1 to RR-5 and RS zones, one per lot, 33 sq m to 90 sq m, and a property may have a garden suite OR a secondary suite, not both**. On ALR land, the Agricultural Land Commission allows one additional detached residence without an application where there is one existing residence when construction begins, to a maximum of 90 sq m on properties 40 ha or less. No other city's suite rule is borrowed. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file §4.)
- **Flood / ALR — the position these notes retire to.** **Over 95% of Pitt Meadows is in the floodplain** (City 2020 Business Plan, per the siblings), protected by roughly 60 km of dikes, and this sub-area is the low Pitt Polder land drained by floodboxes and pump stations (Alouette Pump Station, Fenton and Pitt Polder catchments); the Codd Island Wetlands are undiked floodplain wetlands on the Alouette River. **The ~85% figure quoted on live guide pages (attributed to the City Financial Plan 2026–2030) is a page error / live conflict against the governing ~95%+ position and is never used here** — the conflict is flagged for Jerry/Jared in the acreages sibling and is a sweep item, not a figure. Most of the city's land falls within the Agricultural Land Reserve and agriculture is the dominant land use, with property-level ALR status confirmed per address; zoning requires residential and agricultural buildings to be sited 15 m from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches in the A-1 and A-2 zones. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163 and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/media/8909 (Research file §4; siblings' flood position.)
- **Services on rural lots.** Whether a given North Meadows lot is on municipal water/sewer or on a well and septic system is not confirmed per lot on an official source: UNVERIFIED — check well records in the provincial GWELLS application and confirm any onsite sewerage system was designed / installed by an Authorized Person under the Sewerage System Regulation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems (Research file §4.)
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex (and in full to The Fairways).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex, by contrast, is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the large zone minimums above (8 ha in A-1, 16 ha in A-2 — do not price in a split before checking), and subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the titles — read them. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file §4.)
- **Servicing.** The Pitt Meadows servicing bylaw number is **UNCONFIRMED** — no number is quoted and no other city's bylaw is borrowed. City taxes apply (the rural north also sits inside dike/drainage service areas; 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 per the research), and the specific levy set for a given lot is UNVERIFIED. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf (Verified ledger; research file §4.)
- **Plans.** The North Lougheed Area Plan, adopted by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026, covers approximately 50 ha north of Lougheed Highway, with development to proceed gradually and further planning, technical studies and Council approvals required; no residential presale in the sub-area was verified: UNVERIFIED. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/north-lougheed-area-plan
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: Can an acreage lot have a second dwelling?** Within the rules: one garden suite (33–90 sq m) per lot in the A-1/A-2/RR/RS zones — or a secondary suite, not both — plus the ALC's additional-residence path on ALR land to 90 sq m on properties 40 ha or less where the conditions are met. (§3.)

- **Q: Can I split a farm lot into half-duplex lots?** The subdivision minimums are large — 8 ha in A-1 and 16 ha in A-2 — and ALR subdivision needs ALC approval, so do not price in a split before checking the zone and the ALR status of the parcel. (§3.)
- **Q: Is the lot on sewer or septic?** UNVERIFIED per lot in the research — check GWELLS for the well record and confirm the onsite system was built and certified by an Authorized Person. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** Not on lot count: the 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, and a 2-lot strata is below that threshold. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Is the 200% detached sales ratio a hot market?** No — the research states it plainly: it rests on one active listing against two sales and is a small-numbers artefact, not a signal. (§1.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
