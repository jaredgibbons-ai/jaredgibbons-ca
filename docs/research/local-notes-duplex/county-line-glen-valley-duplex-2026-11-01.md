## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning (Township frame).** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs. Which zone permits a two-unit (duplex) form on a given County Line Glen Valley parcel is a parcel-by-parcel read of Bylaw 2500 and the parcel's zone — no zone-by-zone duplex permission table is asserted in this file, because no source opened for this slug states one. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/county-line-glen-valley.md; program ledger in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D12-BRIEF.md`.)
- **ALR frame — the first check on this ground's parcels.** Land in the Agricultural Land Reserve may generally have no more than one residence per parcel; a principal residence may be up to 500 m² total floor area without ALC application; one additional detached residence may be permitted of up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence and only where local bylaws allow it; local governments may further restrict residences through zoning. Whether any specific parcel in this feed is in the ALR is UNVERIFIED — parcel-specific on ALC mapping, not checked parcel by parcel in the research pass. (Research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **SSMUH position (Township frame — stated so it is not misapplied to this ground).** Township Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024) allows 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², subject to eligibility (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area); lots over 4,050 m² are NOT eligible, and NO 6-unit transit tier applies in the Township. Most parcels in this feed exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by a wide margin, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them. Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial framework where the framework does apply. (Research file above; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md.)
- **Secondary suites inside / beside a duplex (Township's own rule, never borrowed).** Under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s.102 (as amended July 2025), a secondary suite may not exceed **120 m²** maximum gross floor area, each principal dwelling unit may have up to one secondary suite, and on ALR lands a suite must comply with Agricultural Land Commission regulations. A coach house under s.102 is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage — a fee-simple second unit, never strata; no coach-house size cap appears in s.100 as read in the research pass, so a cap is UNVERIFIED in this file. LIVE CONFLICT, reported with dates and not resolved: the Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states suites are NOT permitted in townhouses/rowhouses, while the current s.102 definition (July 2025) says a suite may be located in a townhouse, rowhouse or manorhome — the bylaw definition is the later source. Surrey's and Langley City's suite figures are other municipalities' rules and are never carried here. (Research file above; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md.)
- **Short-term rentals (Township position, as far as the sources go).** The Township does not issue business licences for short-term rental accommodations; the permitted-use status of STRs under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 was not readable on a Township page in the research pass — STR status in the Township is UNVERIFIED beyond the licensing point. - **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Township's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (for transfers where PTT applies).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Acreage and mixed-use transfers are taxed on their own residential/non-residential split — the anchors above are the fully residential case. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a named duplex development or half-duplex subdivision I can shop in County Line Glen Valley?** No named one is verified. (§2.)
- **Q: A listing here shows two homes, or a home plus a mobile home — is that a duplex?** No. Second homes, in-law suites and a mobile home carried as a second dwelling are acreage accessory forms under the ALR frame and the Township's 120 m² suite cap, each a parcel-and-document question. A duplex is one building with two units on one lot; a half duplex additionally requires a strata division or a fee-simple subdivision of that building's parcel. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Can I use SSMUH to put 3–4 units on a Glen Valley acreage?** Generally no on this ground: Township SSMUH eligibility ends at 4,050 m² and requires Urban Containment Boundary land on municipal water and sewer, and most parcels here exceed the ceiling by a wide margin. There is no 6-unit tier in the Township. (§3.)
- **Q: What is the difference between buying a duplex and buying a half duplex here?** A duplex purchase is the whole building and lot on one title. A half duplex is one unit sold separately, which is only possible where the pair was strata-divided (a 2-lot strata plan — Form B diligence) or the parcel was subdivided fee-simple (party-wall title). If neither happened, there is no "half" to buy. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a County Line Glen Valley parcel carry a suite or a coach house?** Under Township s.102 a secondary suite caps at 120 m² gross floor area, one per principal unit, and on ALR land must also comply with ALC regulations (a suite only inside the principal residence on ALR land); a coach house sits only within the second storey of a detached garage. Whether a specific parcel qualifies is a parcel read. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on a duplex purchase?** Run the tiers on your own price: $800,000 → $14,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000 (fully residential, no exemption). A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. (§3.)
