## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`.)

- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full (SPA s.59: fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, CRF balance and insurance summary, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; ask what the strata holds anyway. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

(Research file above; https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes; acreages sibling of this slug)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: The Mission feed shows half-duplexes — can I buy just one half?** Only if that building's title was actually divided — a 2-lot strata plan or a fee-simple subdivision. The feed label alone does not answer it: check the title and, if strata, the strata plan and Form B for that unit. This file verifies no named half duplex city-wide. (§2.)
- **Q: Are Cherry Lane, Luther Place, Briskham Hill or Archer Green duplexes?** No — they are verified strata TOWNHOUSE complexes in this program, counted in their townhomes home. Built form governs; a paired townhouse unit is not a half duplex unless a source documents that form, and none does for these four. (§2.)
- **Q: What is a "legal suite" in a Mission half-duplex listing?** Listing text only until verified. In Mission every secondary suite must be registered with the City, a suite forms part of the same real estate entity as the single family home, and suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes — ask for the suite's registration status before valuing suite income. (§3.)
- **Q: Could I build a duplex on a Mission lot under SSMUH?** The City adopted the provincial SSMUH legislation (Bylaw 6299-2024, July 17, 2024) for zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings, subject to eligibility and servicing — but Mission's specific unit counts are UNCONFIRMED in this program, so the answer for a given lot is a City confirmation for that parcel, not a figure from another city. (§3.)
- **Q: Which OCP is in force — I have seen two dates.** Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026, replacing 6350-2025 with substantively the same plan plus a Regional Context Statement. A "June 1, 2026" date is wrong wherever it appears; this page's own documents do not carry it. (§3; §5.)
- **Q: If I buy a strata half duplex, what diligence is different?** Form B diligence on a 2-lot strata: fees, levies, CRF, insurance, bylaws and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). A 2-lot strata is below the 5+ lot depreciation-report threshold on lot count — ask what reports the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: How does Mission's tax penalty compare with its neighbours?** It does not match them: Mission charges a single FLAT 10% penalty on July 3, 2026 on balances outstanding after the July 2 due date. The rural 5% + 5% pattern belongs to a different (electoral-area) regime and Abbotsford/Surrey use their own two-step city patterns — never blend them on a completion adjustment. (§3.)
- **Q: Is there a court registry in Mission for a court-ordered sale?** No — Mission has NO Supreme Court registry, and the registry mapping for a Mission file is UNVERIFIED in this program; do not assume a neighbouring city's registry. (§3.)
- **Q: How do I commute from a Mission duplex to downtown?** West Coast Express from Mission City Station: five weekday morning westbound trains (5:25–7:25 a.m.) reach Waterfront in 75 minutes, with five afternoon returns and no weekend or holiday service, per TransLink's timetable effective January 5, 2026. (§5.)
