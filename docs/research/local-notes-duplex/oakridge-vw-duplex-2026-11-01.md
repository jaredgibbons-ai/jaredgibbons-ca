## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Vancouver Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, R1-1 district).** The R1-1 District Schedule sets a minimum site area of 306 m² for a single detached house or a duplex and for a 3–4-unit multiple dwelling, 464 m² for 5 units and 557 m² for 6 to 8 units; a multiplex may have up to 6 strata units or up to 8 secured rental units, and a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house. Lots inside Cambie Corridor designations may carry different or additional options under the plan — the zone and designation of a specific lot are read off the current bylaw and plan for that lot, and are UNCONFIRMED here beyond that read. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **SSMUH position.** Vancouver's R1-1 multiplex framework (By-law No. 13817, replacing the RS districts) is the city's small-scale multi-unit housing implementation on former single-detached lots, with the duplex as the baseline two-unit form inside it. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Vancouver's own position only.** The R1-1 schedule permits a secondary suite (and, for a house, a laneway house) as the schedule states it; no suite size cap from any other city is borrowed, and no Vancouver cap figure is asserted here beyond the schedule's own text — read the current schedule for the specific lot. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex (and in full to Oakhaus and the corridor towers).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**, stated precisely. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** Vancouver's Subdivision By-law No. 5208 is VERIFIED in the program ledger (fees under By-law No. 14538): a fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval under that bylaw; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the titles — read them. (Program discipline, D1 brief; verified ledger.)
- **Taxes and levies (citywide Vancouver).** 2026 advance property taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances. The City's Empty Homes Tax charges 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed empty — **3%, VANCOUVER ONLY** — with the annual declaration due February 3, 2026 and payment due April 16, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a duplex complex in Oakridge I can buy into?** No — none is verified by name. The duplex form here is individual two-unit and multiplex homes on side-street lots under R1-1, plus corridor townhouse/low-rise product; the one counted townhouse complex is Oakhaus, in the townhomes file. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a side-street house lot become a duplex or multiplex?** That is the R1-1 baseline: minimum site area 306 m² for a house, a duplex or a 3–4-unit multiple dwelling, up to 6 strata or 8 secured rental units at the larger site areas — but lots inside Cambie Corridor designations follow the plan as well, so read both for the specific lot. (§3.)
- **Q: Is a house with a suite the same as a duplex?** No. A detached home with a secondary suite under the R1-1 schedule is a two-unit form class, but it is one title, not a strata half and not a fee-simple half — the halves of a true half duplex each carry their own title. (§2.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need depreciation reports?** Not on lot count: the 5-year cycle captures stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, and a 2-lot strata is below that threshold — which is exactly why a buyer of a strata half needs their own building diligence instead of relying on a report that may not exist. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: What is happening at Oakridge Park and when can I buy there?** Per the developers (QuadReal/Westbank), a 28-acre redevelopment with 3,000+ homes, a 9-acre park and a civic centre; retail's first phase opened in 2026 and the first residential towers were expected ready in early 2027 — developer-sourced, phase by phase, not City-verified. (§1; research §4.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax apply to a vacant duplex half?** Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax is 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed empty, Vancouver only, with the annual declaration due February 3, 2026 — a vacant half is exactly the kind of home the declaration catches. (§3.)
