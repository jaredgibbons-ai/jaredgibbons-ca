## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted — Vancouver's multiplex frame + the Broadway Plan.** By-law No. 13817 amended the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 to replace the old RS districts with the R1-1 district, allowing up to 6 strata units (or up to 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots citywide, subject to the district schedule and site-specific City confirmation — the duplex / two-unit form is the baseline product of that district, and Mount Pleasant VE's older half-duplexes stand under the zoning of their day. Character houses on the side streets: check heritage register status before suggesting demolition. On corridor and station-area sites, Broadway Plan designations may allow more through rezoning (20–30 storeys near Mount Pleasant Station), and older rental buildings carry the Plan's renter protections and tenant relocation requirements — a redevelopment-economics fact, not a duplex permission. Citywide direction now comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf (Research file above.)
- **SSMUH position.** Vancouver implemented the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing direction through its own multiplex rezoning (the R1-1 district above) rather than a separate SSMUH bylaw — the duplex is the baseline form of the new district. No separate Vancouver SSMUH bylaw number is asserted. (Research file above; standing Vancouver frame, brief chain.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex.** Mount Pleasant VE's detached stock is, per the research, "much of it converted or suited" — suites are a lived feature of the form class here. No Vancouver suite-size cap for a suite inside a duplex is stated in this slug's research or siblings, so none is asserted — the position for a specific property is UNCONFIRMED until its district schedule and any registered suite are read. Other cities' suite caps are never borrowed into a Vancouver file. (Research file above; program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **STRATA block — the operative frame for this area's half-duplexes.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists — and Mount Pleasant VE's side streets are full of them: Form B diligence applies in full (Strata Property Act s.59 — provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; the townhomes sibling makes the same point for this sub-area's half-duplexes ("the sub-5-lot consequences that follow — including sitting UNDER the 5-lot depreciation-report threshold"). Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. **No Form B position is taken for the Lee Building** — its tenure is UNCONFIRMED while the §2 conflict stays OPEN. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate (Strata frame as carried in ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/mount-pleasant-ve-townhomes-2026-11-01.md.)
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel in Vancouver requires subdivision approval under the City's Subdivision By-law No. 5208 (VERIFIED in the ledger; fees bylaw 14538). The townhomes sibling verifies NO fee-simple townhouse complex in Mount Pleasant VE and notes the fee-simple ground-oriented product on these streets is the detached character house, not a fee-simple project — if a "fee-simple townhouse" listing surfaces here, read the title first: a strata half-duplex marketed as a townhouse is the usual explanation. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles. (Standing Vancouver frame, brief chain; townhomes sibling above.)
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY.** The City charges an Empty Homes Tax of 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed empty — a live cost for investors holding any of the area's older rental or vacant stock — with the annual declaration due February 3, 2026 and payment due April 16, 2026. City property-tax dates as the research states them: 2026 advance taxes due February 3, 2026, main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances. The EHT is a City of Vancouver tax only and is never carried into another municipality's file. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx (Research file above.)
- **Flood note for the north edge.** Flood exposure concentrates at the sub-area's north edge: the Great Northern Way lowland is former False Creek shoreline inside the City's designated floodplain, so parcels there are subject to the Building By-law's Floodplain Standards and Requirements, including flood construction levels; the residential grid south of 2nd Avenue sits on the Mount Pleasant slope — confirm per parcel. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/ (Research file above.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. These are tier illustrations only — no verified Mount Pleasant VE price figure exists to use as an input (no SnapStats for Vancouver), and none is invented. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate; a NEW multiplex-era half duplex can be. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: A listing calls a Mount Pleasant home a "townhouse" but the form says half duplex — which is it?** The form governs. This sub-area's listing feed is full of strata half-duplexes and converted character houses wearing townhouse language; a half-duplex is a duplex (2-unit strata), with the sub-5-lot consequences that follow — including sitting UNDER the 5-lot depreciation-report threshold. Read the strata plan and lot count, not the headline. (§2, §3.)
- **Q: Can I buy a unit in the Lee Building like a condo?** Not on any source verified here — it is a tenure record only: the standing record says undivided interest / tenants-in-common (cash-only, no Form B), this slug's research and condos sibling carry its tenure as UNCONFIRMED as strata, and the conflict stays OPEN pending settlement from title. Do not treat a Lee Building address as condo or duplex stock without a title check. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). On a character-house strata conversion, the Form B and the strata plan are also where you confirm the conversion was actually registered. (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I build a multiplex on a Mount Pleasant house lot?** Generally yes: By-law No. 13817 rezoned Vancouver's RS districts to R1-1, allowing up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units per lot subject to the district schedule; Broadway Plan designations may allow more on corridor sites through rezoning. Check heritage register status first. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (For the area's older walk-up RENTAL buildings, the Broadway Plan's renter protections run the other way — they protect existing tenants on redevelopment.) (§3.)
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax apply to a vacant Mount Pleasant half duplex?** Yes — the City of Vancouver's EHT is 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed vacant (declaration due February 3, 2026; payment due April 16, 2026). Vancouver only. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: When does the Mount Pleasant Station open?** The Province states the Broadway Subway is on schedule to open in fall 2027, and all four Broadway lanes between Main and Quebec Streets reopened by July 2026 — price the opening as a dated event, not a rumour. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026TT0046-000500.pdf (§1.)
