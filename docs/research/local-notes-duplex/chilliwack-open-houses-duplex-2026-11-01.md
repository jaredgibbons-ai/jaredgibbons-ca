## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (city frame for the topic).** Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw No. 5000 sets the urban residential zones — the research states the R1-A (Urban Residential) Zone with a minimum lot area of 500 m² on community water and sewer and a minimum lot width of 15 m on the same services, and the R1-C (Urban Infill) Zone with a minimum lot area of 300 m² on an interior lot and 360 m² on a corner lot. Chilliwack's SSMUH position is Bylaw 5395 (adopted Jun 18, 2024); a duplex is the baseline two-unit form, and SSMUH adds unit potential — it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. The OCP governing the whole city is Bylaw No. 5500. The zone for a specific property seen at an open house is read off the City's current zoning for that lot. (Research file above; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the city's own rule.** No Chilliwack suite-size figure is stated in this file: no figure is borrowed from another city, and the suite rule for a specific Chilliwack duplex lot is read in Zoning Bylaw No. 5000 for that lot. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Open-house conduct rule — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), verbatim.** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, provides that **a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate**. In practice: chatting factually with the host does not by itself make you their client — but once you share your motivation, budget or needs with a licensee who represents the seller, the disclosure rules engage; BCFSA also states a licensee must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party, and that dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. An identification or sign-in requirement for open house visitors is NOT stated in any BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source found — UNVERIFIED (a host may still ask, on the seller's instructions). (Research file above; http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download, read 2026-11-01; https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers, read 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) seen through any feed.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Chilliwack's servicing position is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger: the "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" named on the City's subdivisions webpage is held in the verified ledger as NOT adopted — no servicing bylaw number is asserted for Chilliwack in this file (see §5). - **Chilliwack 2026 property tax dates (City-published).** Tax notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1 each year; for 2026 the City published a 5% late payment penalty for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% for taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026; on a sale, the buyer's and seller's lawyer or notary allocates the annual bill and the Home Owner Grant claim on the Statement of Adjustments. (Research file above; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522, read 2026-11-01.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: An open-house card shows a duplex — can I buy just the side I walked through?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot. The card settles nothing; check the title. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is the open-house time on the Chilliwack feed reliable?** No offset is asserted for this slug — there is no paired card evidence in its research — and the base research found one displayed card with no open-house label. Treat the feed time as a record and confirm with the listing brokerage before driving out. (§2.)
- **Q: Do I need my own agent to attend, and what can I safely tell the host?** No official source requires your own agent. Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure of representation only while the host is hosting an advertised open house or answering general factual questions — once you discuss your motivation, finances or needs with the seller's licensee, you are sharing with the other side's representative. (§3.) http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Q: Do I have to sign in or show ID?** No BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states an identification or sign-in requirement for open house visitors — UNVERIFIED as a rule; an individual host may still ask, on the seller's instructions. (§3.)
- **Q: Does this topic file list Chilliwack's duplexes?** No. It is a topic file with no roster; duplex stock lives in the home-area files. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: If I buy after an open house in Chilliwack, when are property taxes due?** Notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1 each year — for 2026, a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 4, 2026; your lawyer or notary allocates the bill on the Statement of Adjustments. (§3.) https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
