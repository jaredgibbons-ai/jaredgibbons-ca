## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Duplexes are a two-unit form under Mission's Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 in the zones that allow them; parcel-level minimum lot sizes are set out in the bylaw itself (rural zones such as RU16 carry a 1.6 ha minimum in City notices). The exact position for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until its zone is read. The OCP governing is Bylaw 6405-2026 (adopted April 21, 2026), which replaces and repeals Bylaw 6350-2025. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan (research file §4)
- **SSMUH position.** Mission has adopted the provincial SSMUH legislation under Bylaw 6299-2024 — zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to it, subject to eligibility and servicing; the unit counts are UNCONFIRMED in this program and no count is asserted for any lot. (Mission frame, brief chain; research file §4.)
- **Secondary suites — Mission's own rule.** A Mission secondary suite is a self-contained living space inside a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; a suite is not a coach home or garden cottage (those are detached accessory units, handled by zone); suites must be registered with the City under its secondary-suites program. No other city's suite-size rule is borrowed here, and no Mission suite size cap is stated because no source opened for this slug states one. (Research file §4; https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing / development charges.** Development Cost Charges are under Mission's DSCB 5650-2017 from City sources (frame carried; no rate is stated here). The band's acreage product sits outside the urban service core in the rural sub-areas — water, sewer/septic and road access are verified parcel by parcel rather than assumed. (Mission frame, brief chain; research file §6.)
- **Property taxes (context).** City of Mission 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a **FLAT 10% penalty** added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 — the city regime; the rural 5% + 5% regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11) is a DISTINCT regime for FVRD electoral areas, never blended. Municipal rates are set by the City, with additional taxes levied for diking on top of the municipal, school, regional district and BC Assessment levies. Mission has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED). (Research file §4; https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. At this band's floor, $1,800,000 with no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $1,600,000 = **$34,000**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. No PTT exemption reaches this band (First-Time Home Buyers' Program ends at $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption ends at $1,150,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out completely by $1,500,000 — below this band's floor, so no band purchase qualifies; a band buyer of a new build budgets full GST. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 — likewise far below this band. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Mission over $1.8M?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. None is verified by name in this band — attached product over $1.8M in Mission is UNVERIFIED on the sources used; a single-lot duplex sells whole. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is there a named duplex development counted in this band?** No. The band's confirmed character is freehold detached and acreage estates; the duplex evidence is the form class only, city-wide. (§2.)
- **Q: What PTT do I actually pay at this level?** Run the tiers, not a flat rate: at the $1,800,000 floor with no exemption the tax is $34,000; at $3,500,000 it is $93,000 on the inputs stated in §3, with the slice above $3M taxed at 3% + a further 2%. No PTT exemption reaches this band. (§3.)
- **Q: Does the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate help at this price?** No — it phases out completely by $1.5M, below this band's floor. Band buyers budget full GST on a new build and full PTT. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for this band?** No. SnapStats has no duplex category and no price-band cut; its figures are Mission-wide community context only. (§1.)
- **Q: Does Mission flood at the high end too?** The city's south edge is Fraser River floodplain and low areas such as Hatzic Prairie sit behind the Dewdney Dyke system; some band acreage sits in those rural sub-areas, and taxes carry a diking levy — flood status is property-specific and must be checked for the parcel. (§3; research file §4.)
