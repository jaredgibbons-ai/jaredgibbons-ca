## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A townhouse described as "duplex-style" or "half-duplex-style" in listing copy is townhouse-form strata, not a duplex title. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Delta frame for the band's Delta side).** Delta's current OCP is Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the PREVIOUS Bylaw 3950); its Development Permit Areas live in Appendix B. The RD zones are Delta's duplex zones, assigned parcel by parcel on the zoning maps. **ZONING NUMBER CONTESTED:** the zoning bylaw number ("7600, 2017" vs "8400, 2025") is carried as citations only, flagged here, and asserted NOWHERE. On Tsawwassen Lands, City of Delta zoning does not apply at all — Tsawwassen First Nation jurisdiction governs. (Research file above, §4; Delta frame per the verified ledger.)
- **SSMUH position (Delta side).** Delta's SSMUH zoning amendments (Bylaws 8394 + 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots on the Delta side. A duplex is the baseline two-unit form; SSMUH does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves, and it does not change a strata townhome's own lot. (Condos sibling, §3; https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Delta's own rule as its sources state it.** No Delta percentage or size cap for a suite inside an urban duplex is stated in this slug's research, so no figure is borrowed from another city and the cap is UNCONFIRMED here. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), and to the band's Delta-side strata townhouses as strata diligence generally.** Form B diligence applies in full; BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws for the specific complex). Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. (Research file above, §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal, read 2026-11-01; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws)
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Delta's Subdivision and Development Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024: final adoption is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — no servicing number is asserted for Delta in this file. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)

- **Property taxes.** On the Delta side: due date July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. Tax administration for homes on Tsawwassen Lands runs under Tsawwassen First Nation, not the City — the live page's tax-matching claim is UNVERIFIED. (Research file above, §4; https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes, read 2026-11-01.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier** — $93,000 governs at $3.5M. **Band reading:** every purchase in this band is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers — the 3% tier and the further 2% do not arise under $1.2M; the townhomes sibling's worked examples are $900,000 → $16,000 and $1,150,000 → $21,000, both with no exemption. The first-time buyers' program full exemption requires fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — only the lower slice of this band can use it at all. PTT application to a Tsawwassen Lands leasehold transfer is transaction-specific — confirm on the specific purchase rather than assuming the Delta-side treatment (condos sibling). A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — most of this band is resale stock. GST treatment of a leasehold purchase on Tsawwassen Lands is transaction-specific — UNCONFIRMED here; confirm with a CPA/notary on the specific unit. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
