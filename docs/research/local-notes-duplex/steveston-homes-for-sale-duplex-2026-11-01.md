## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Planning frame.** The Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000) guides the whole Steveston planning area. Properties inside the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area are subject to Heritage Alteration Permit requirements under that plan. (Research file above, §4; https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/steveston566.pdf)
- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 governs: houses sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL added under provincial Bill 44 legislation, and village commercial/mixed-use zones including Steveston Commercial (CS3) and Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU32) – Steveston Village. The zone of a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until read off the City's current zoning for that lot. (Research file above, §4; https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf)
- **SSMUH position.** Richmond's Bill 44 minimums as carried in the brief-chain ledger: 3 units on lots up to 280 m² / 4 units on larger lots, with the RSM SSMUH zones in Zoning Bylaw 8500 as amended, and at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot on covered lots. A duplex is the baseline two-unit form in that framework. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Brief chain; research file above, §4.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Richmond's own rule.** Zoning Bylaw 8500 s.5.4: a suite must be at least 33.0 m² and no more than the lesser of 40% of the dwelling's floor area or 110 m² — never borrowed from another city. (Research file above, §4; https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf)
- **Flood position.** All of Richmond is designated flood plain: Steveston fronts the South Arm of the Fraser River behind the City's perimeter dike system (49 km of dikes in the city frame) with drainage pump stations, designed for a 1:500 flood event with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres; the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements, and the City's Steveston Island Dike project addresses the Steveston waterfront and harbour. Pull the property's flood construction level and insurance position before subjects come off. (Research file above, §4; brief chain; https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML; https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Richmond's servicing Bylaws 8751/8752 are VERIFIED in the verified ledger. ADEU (Alexandra District Energy Utility) charges apply to West Cambie / Alexandra ONLY — not Steveston. (Brief chain.)
- **Property taxes (Richmond frame).** Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. (Research file above, §4; https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Steveston?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. None is verified by name here; a single-lot duplex sells whole. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Are The Pier and Harbour Walk duplex stock?** No. Both are apartment/mixed-form entries counted solely in the Steveston condos file for the London Landing pocket — cross-referenced in §2 with no duplex count taken. Harbour Walk also contains townhouse-form units at the same address; establish which form a specific unit is before comparing anything. (§2.)
- **Q: Is it "Lower Landing" or "London Landing"?** London Landing. "Lower Landing" is not a Richmond name — the official name is written into Zoning Bylaw 8500 itself. A search under "Lower Landing" will miss the record. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a duplex here?** Richmond's own s.5.4 rule governs — minimum 33.0 m², maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling or 110 m². Check the specific suite's permits before valuing the income. (§3.)
- **Q: What flood questions come with a Steveston duplex?** All of Richmond is designated flood plain behind the dike system; Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction levels — confirm both, plus the insurance position, before subjects come off. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,200,000 fully residential with no exemption = $22,000; $3,500,000 = $93,000 including the further 2% tier (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
