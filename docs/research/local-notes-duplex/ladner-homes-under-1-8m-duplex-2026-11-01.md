## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (the band's own arithmetic, per its siblings).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $800,000 = **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $1,300,000 = **$28,000**; at the band ceiling itself, $1,800,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $1,600,000 = **$34,000** — every price in this band is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers and never reaches the 3% tier; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier** (above the band, shown for the program anchors). A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. **Divergence note (from this band's townhomes sibling, carried openly):** the batch-8 research states the general PTT tier rates as UNVERIFIED in its own pass because the Province's general PTT page could not be opened (429); the tiers used here are the program-verified tiers carried from the sibling files where the Province's page was read — budget from the Province's current page at offer time. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-time buyer position in this band (the Province's own numbers).** The first time home buyers' program's full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024), exempting the first $500,000 of the purchase price, with a partial exemption only above $835,000 and below $860,000 (plus the program's personal and property conditions). A detached Ladner home priced between $860,000 and $1,800,000 is therefore outside the program entirely — only the band's lowest tail, if any, could qualify. The newly built home exemption (full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000) is the live exemption question inside this band if a unit is newly built. (Research file above; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers)
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
