## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Canyon Heights' housing sits under the District's R1 and R2 detached residential zones (Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525 — Section 525 (Bylaw 8799) sets the R1 minimum lot at 4,000 m² / 30 m width / 34 m depth, with the standard R2 position in the same section per the townhomes sibling). The governing plan is the District OCP, Bylaw 7900 (including its Schedule B Development Permit Areas). No duplex-specific district is asserted for this ground; the exact position for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until its zone is read. (Townhomes sibling above.)
- **SSMUH / suites — the District's own position, stated in terms.** Since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. A suite or coach house is part of the house lot's ownership — it cannot be bought separately. The District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop. SSMUH adds unit potential on one title; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Townhomes sibling above.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the District's own position.** The District's suite position is the three-unit framework above (principal + suite + coach house on one title). No District suite size cap figure beyond that framework is asserted here — the position for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until read in Zoning Bylaw 3210. (Townhomes sibling above.)
- **Hazard / development-permit frame.** District OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets seven Development Permit Areas including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard — on river-side and steep lots around the Capilano corridor these checks matter to any duplex build or purchase, per the townhomes sibling. (Townhomes sibling above.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. A Canyon Heights detached house purchase itself carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — diligence is title, District municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw 3210, OCP Bylaw 7900) and any registered covenants only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the District's Approving Officer process, measured against the Section 525 lot minimums above; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** The District of North Vancouver's servicing position is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — no servicing bylaw number is asserted in this file. (The City of North Vancouver's Bylaw 8014 is City-files-only and appears NOWHERE in this District file.) (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. At this district's price points the $3.5M anchor is the working one. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Canyon Heights?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. None is verified by name here; the ground's stock is detached houses. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is the "EIGHT" project on Highland Boulevard a Canyon Heights duplex complex?** No. The listing for 3206 Highland Boulevard describes eight duplex-style 3-bedroom homes "in the heart of Edgemont Village" — Edgemont product in duplex form, expressly not counted as Canyon Heights stock in the sibling files, and recorded here as an exclusion only. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I buy a suite or coach house separately in Canyon Heights?** No. Since June 2024 a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house (three units total) are permitted on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary, but they sit on one lot's title — the suite and coach house are not separate purchases. (§3.)
- **Q: How much density could a Canyon Heights lot take under SSMUH?** The District's GEOweb classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 above 280 m², and up to 6 above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop — a classification, not a lot-level permission. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). A detached Canyon Heights house has no strata at all. (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $3,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $93,000 including the further 2% tier (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a duplex here?** Address-based in SD44 — the documented locator run for 4501 Highland Boulevard returned Canyon Heights Elementary and Handsworth Secondary for that address only; Canyon Heights Elementary and Highlands Elementary split the Upper Capilano plateau by address. Run the exact address. (§5.)
