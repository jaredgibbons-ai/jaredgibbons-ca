## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A bare-land strata half duplex — Ashley Green, below — is the strata form in bare-land dress: each home sits on its own strata lot, the buyer owns a strata LOT (land ownership in strata form, not a rented pad), and Form B diligence applies. A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership at all — the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad; no pad-rental park is verified in Holly. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing.)
