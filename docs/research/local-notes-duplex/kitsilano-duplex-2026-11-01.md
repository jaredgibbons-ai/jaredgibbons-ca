## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. Kitsilano's interior-street duplexes are, on the research's own tenure line, freehold product on house lots — the fee-simple side of this definition as standing stock, bought whole unless a title shows a strata plan or a subdivided pair. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Kitsilano's RT areas sit under the RT-7 and RT-8 District Schedules of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — RT-8 states a minimum site area of 306 m² for a duplex, and the RT-7 / RT-8 intent is to encourage retention and renovation of buildings consistent with the historical character of the area. - **SSMUH position.** Vancouver's district schedules ARE the small-scale multi-unit framework for these streets — the RT schedules for the RT areas and the R1-1 schedule (house plus suite and laneway, duplex, or a multiplex to 6 strata / 8 rental units by lot size) elsewhere — so the "SSMUH question" here is a per-lot schedule check, not a separate overlay. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Research file above.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the City's own position.** A single detached house under R1-1 may include a secondary suite and a laneway house. For a duplex, the governing district schedule's unit and site-area rules apply; no Vancouver suite size cap figure beyond the schedules is asserted here — the position for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until its schedule is read. (Research file above.)
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY, 3%.** Vancouver owners must file an annual property status declaration, and a property that meets the tax's vacancy tests is taxed at 3% (Vancouver only — never applied to any other municipality in this program). A duplex or half duplex left empty between tenants is read against this tax. (Vancouver frame, brief chain.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, measured against the governing schedule's minimum site areas; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Vancouver's servicing position is carried in the verified ledger as Bylaw 5208 (fees 14538). (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. At Kitsilano price points the $3.5M anchor is the working one. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Kitsilano?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. The interior-street duplex stock here is freehold product that ordinarily sells whole. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is there a named duplex development in Kitsilano?** None verified. The duplex evidence here is the form class under the RT-7 / RT-8 and R1-1 schedules. (§2.)
- **Q: Does the RT character-retention intent affect a duplex purchase?** The RT-7 / RT-8 schedules state an intent to retain buildings consistent with the area's historical character — that prices the house and duplex stock, and exterior alterations can face both strata approval (where a strata exists) and the character-retention framework. Confirm the exact district for the address. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax hit a Kitsilano duplex left empty?** Yes, in Vancouver: owners must file an annual property status declaration, and a property meeting the vacancy tests is taxed at 3% — Vancouver only. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $3,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $93,000 including the further 2% tier (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a duplex here?** Address-based in SD39 — the documented locator run for 3505 West 7th Avenue returned Bayview Elementary and Kitsilano Secondary for that address only, and four elementaries plus Kitsilano Secondary sit inside the area. Run the exact address. (§5.)
