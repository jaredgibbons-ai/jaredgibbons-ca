## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A farm residence on ALR land is none of these forms: it is a single principal residence (with, at most, a suite inside it and one additional small residence under the ALC frame in §3), depositing a strata plan in the ALR causes subdivision and needs ALC permission, and a fee-simple split of a farm parcel runs into the ALR / A1–A2 subdivision frame, not a duplex programme. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing; ALR strata position: ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6, per this slug's research: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf)

- **ALR housing frame (the residence rules that govern this file, ALR sources only).** On ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may be more restrictive and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. **ALR strata prohibition:** depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6, "Strata Plans Cause Subdivision") — do not stratify ALR land on assumption. - **Farm classification (BC Assessment, opened source named).** Farm classification is a voluntary, property-by-property programme; the income thresholds are $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — a flat "$2,500+" line would be an error and none is carried here. Applications must be submitted by the legislated deadline of October 31 for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. (Research file above, §4; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification)
