## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815 (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) governs — NEVER the former Bylaw No. 4742 as current. The OCP governing is Burnaby 2050 (adopted December 9, 2025); the Edmonds Community Plan covers the town centre plan area west of The Crest underneath it. The zone of a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until read. (Research file above: https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite; https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan)
- **SSMUH position.** Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and minimum lot area of 281 m²; permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites; and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified — so new multi-unit product on an R1 lot, duplex form included, can be strata-divided and sold unit by unit. There is no SkyTrain station inside The Crest, so the 6-unit frequent-transit tier will turn on the lot's own facts. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split an existing duplex into separately titled halves. (Research file above: https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the city's own rule.** In Burnaby's R1 district, secondary suites count toward the lot's unit total and cannot be stratified (55-plus sibling's record of the City framework). No Burnaby suite size-cap figure is stated in the sources read for this slug, so no cap is asserted here — never borrow another city's cap (Surrey's 40%, the Township's 120 m² and Langley City's lesser of 100 m² / 40% are those cities' rules, not Burnaby's). (55-plus sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/the-crest-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the municipality's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums (R1: 281 m² minimum lot area, 10 m minimum lot width); no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; R1 minimums per the research file above.)
- **Servicing.** Burnaby's subdivision-servicing base bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — the servicing bylaw was renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024, and no current base number is asserted anywhere in this file (the amendment number is asserted by siblings program-wide as the amendment on record; the base stays unconfirmed). The documented route per the lots-and-land sibling: new homes and SSMUH projects require Engineering pre-application approval through the City's My Permits Portal before a building permit application, and where offsite servicing requirements are identified in the Pre-Approval Report, a cash-in-lieu contribution to City infrastructure is required, calculated on Engineering unit rates — confirm for the specific parcel. (Brief chain; lots-and-land sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/the-crest-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md: https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/development-permits-construction/new-home-construction)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate; the townhomes sibling notes the newly built home exemption is relevant to any new stratified multiplex product built on Crest R1 lots, not to resales. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a named duplex development in The Crest I can target?** No — no named duplex, strata duplex or fee-simple half-duplex subdivision is verified on this ground. What trades as "duplex" here is the individual duplex building on a single side-street lot (§2 form class); verify any one of them on its title.
- **Q: Are Deerfield, Wildflower or Chancery Lane duplex complexes?** No — they are strata townhouse complexes counted once in the townhomes file, clustered on the Cumberland streets at the sub-area's north side. No duplex element is documented for any of them. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me split a duplex into two titles?** No — SSMUH adds unit potential on a lot; a separately titled half needs a strata plan or a subdivision. Burnaby does allow lots with two or more primary units to be stratified, which is the strata route. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a Crest house lot be redeveloped into stratified multi-unit product?** Potentially: under the R1 SSMUH framework most Crest lots allow 3–4 homes (6 only on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop), and multi-unit product on those lots can be stratified and sold individually — now under Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815). Price the lot's multiplex value before listing it as a teardown. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for The Crest?** No — duplexes have no separate published category in this program's sources, and this sub-area's September 2026 sample was two sales each way; the §1 figures are labelled community context only. (§1.)
