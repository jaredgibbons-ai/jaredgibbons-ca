## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A manufactured home on a rented pad is none of these forms: it is a single dwelling unit held as a chattel under a pad tenancy. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`.)

- **Property taxes (rural regime — DISTINCT from every city regime in this batch).** A property not located in a city, town, district or village is in a rural area: its owner receives the property tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays property taxes to the Province, not to the FVRD or the City of Mission. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural property taxes are due and payable on July 2 of the year they are first levied, a 5% penalty is added to any portion that remains unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% penalty is added to any portion that remains unpaid on October 31. Mission's flat 10% city penalty does NOT apply here, and Chilliwack's city calendar does not apply here — the regimes are never blended. - **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (for transfers where PTT applies — fee-simple / strata-lot transfers on this ground).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. How PTT applies to a home-only purchase on a rented pad is not settled as a flat rule in this program — take conveyancing advice before writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031.
