## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **District plans and zoning — District of North Vancouver ONLY.** The Official Community Plan is Bylaw 7900; the Seymour Local Plan (2003) manages slow, limited growth and maintains the predominantly single-family character of Seymour Heights, Blueridge, Riverside East and Windsor Park, while contemplating townhouse development on assembled sites on the north side of Mount Seymour Parkway (3200–3500 blocks) at 0.6–1.2 FSR. The zoning bylaw is Zoning Bylaw 3210 (cited in District material as amended by Bylaw 8698, June 2024). The exact zone for a specific Seymour lot is read off the current Bylaw 3210 text and District GeoWeb for that lot — zone entitlements asserted beyond that read are UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/SLP_2003_6.0.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/new-provincial-housing-legislation-fact-sheet.pdf
- **Suites and coach houses — the District's own rule, and this ground's documented second-unit form.** Under Zoning Bylaw 3210 as amended by Bylaw 8698 (June 2024), most single-family lots in the District may hold a main house, a secondary suite and a coach house — **three units total**; the District's December 2023 changes let a secondary suite be **up to 49% of the house** with no minimum size and raised the maximum coach house height to two storeys. That 49% figure is the District of North Vancouver's own suite rule, stated for this District only — no other municipality's suite cap is borrowed or exported. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf (Research file §4; settled District frame.)
- **SSMUH / Ground-Oriented Housing — in flux, lot by lot.** December 2025 Ground-Oriented Housing rules permit up to four homes on more than 4,000 eligible District properties, and in April 2026 Council voted against adopting the six-home near-transit tier — so multiplex rules are still in flux and must be checked lot by lot. The District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop — a duplex (two units) sits inside even the lowest tier where the lot is non-exempt, but the specific lot's classification is read off GEOweb, not assumed. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/new-provincial-housing-legislation-fact-sheet.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **Hazards.** The District designates Natural Hazard Development Permit Areas for slope and creek hazards and publishes property-level hazard reports through GeoWeb; after the January 19, 2005 landslide on the Berkley Escarpment, the District began proactive District-wide management of landslide and other natural hazards, with its Landslide Risk Assessment updated in 2026. Sloped and creek-adjacent Seymour lots should be checked before purchase, and winter access on upper Seymour streets is a real showing topic (the Province requires drivers to obey winter tire and chain signs on designated routes from October 1 to April 30). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/bgclandslideriskassessment2026updatereportfinal.pdf and http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/driving-and-cycling/traveller-information/seasonal/winter-driving/enforcement (Research file §§4, 6.)
- **Servicing: UNCONFIRMED.** The settled ledger holds the District of North Vancouver's servicing bylaw number UNCONFIRMED — no number is asserted in this file. District utility fees (water, sewer, drainage, solid waste) are billed separately from the tax notice, per the research — a utility fact, not a servicing-bylaw citation. (Settled ledger; research file §4.)
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / half duplex in Seymour NV (and in full to the Parkgate / Parkway stratas).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the District's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Property taxes.** Property taxes are billed by the District of North Vancouver: Council approved a 3.53% municipal tax increase for 2026 (2.03% for services plus 1.5% for infrastructure renewal); the District's published 2026 Class 1 (residential) total rate is $3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value across all taxing authorities, taxes were due July 2, 2026, and a 5% penalty applied after July 2 with a further 5% after September 1, 2026. District utility fees (water, sewer, drainage, solid waste) are billed separately from the tax notice. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
