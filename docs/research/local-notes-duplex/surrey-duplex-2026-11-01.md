## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. In Surrey's current zoning language the paired form also appears as the semi-detached dwelling unit: Zoning 12000 permits one secondary suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit (§3). (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; research file, §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000)

(Research file, §4; D18 Surrey frame; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/dart-hill-ncp; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/south-westminster)
- **Zones permitting a duplex / two-unit form + SSMUH (Surrey's own position):** Surrey adopted its provincial SSMUH zoning change on **July 8, 2024**, when Zoning 12000's residential zones R1 to R7 replaced the RF, RH and RQ zones — RF-12/RF-12C became R4 and RF-SD (the semi-detached form) became R6 — so the zoning now does the small-lot permitting that older NCP designations predate, and a plan tile and the current zone can point in different directions on the same lot. Duplexes are the baseline form where SSMUH applies. (Research file, §4; https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (Surrey's own rule, never borrowed):** under Zoning 12000, one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area**. A "90 m² cap" is NOT current and is never stated as current. The Township's 120 m², Langley City's and Abbotsford's figures are never borrowed into a Surrey file. (Research file, §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey frame):** STRs are owner-only in this program's Surrey frame, with a licence of **$358/yr** carried — the City's 2024 **$350/yr** proposal figure is disclosed alongside it wherever the frame is stated, and the two are never blended. (D18 Surrey frame.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Surrey ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process under servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986); party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; D18 Surrey frame.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales, and is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
