## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

(Research file above; siblings listed in the FILE STATUS block.)

- **The farm-residence form, exactly as the ALR sources support it:** a Cloverdale farm parcel in the ALR carries one principal residence per parcel, permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction. A secondary suite is permitted in the ALR only inside the principal residence — a separate or detached dwelling cannot be used as a secondary suite, and local bylaws must also permit suites. One additional, detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — maximum 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. (Research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Additional farm-residence forms — ONLY as the ALC sources support them:** an additional residence beyond the permitted frame is not a general entitlement. The routes the ALR sources document are the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation s.34(3) framework for an additional manufactured-home residence as administered under the ALC's Policy L-25, and, where the use does not adhere, a Non-Adhering Residential Use (NARU) application to the ALC; further residences beyond what is permitted can only be approved by the ALC where the residence is necessary for farm use. **A general farm-worker entitlement to an additional residence is UNCONFIRMED** — each parcel's position is read from its own ALC position, not assumed from another farm's approval. (Settled ALR position, the poultry-farms duplex treatment; research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/, read 2026-11-01.)

A strata duplex / strata half duplex is therefore not an available form on ALR parcels in this topic, and no fee-simple half-duplex farm subdivision is documented under this topic. A listing that calls a farm residence a "duplex" is stating a RECORD ONLY — marketing wording for a two-unit or two-residence farm property sold as one parcel — never verification of a separable half, and no such mention is counted in this file. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted — two frames, kept separate.** On ALR parcels the Agricultural Land Commission's housing rules govern the residences themselves and the stratification route is closed by rule (§2). Off the ALR, Surrey's frame governs: duplexes are a permitted two-unit form in Surrey's residential zones that allow a duplex under Zoning By-law No. 12000, and the provincial SSMUH implementation (Surrey, Jul 8, 2024) makes the duplex the baseline multi-unit form on eligible lots — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. SSMUH is an urban-lot framework; it is not a route to extra dwellings on ALR farm parcels, where the ALC rules govern. The OCP governing is Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is the replaced predecessor, never cited as current. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated for Surrey. The live page's statement that Surrey A-1 / A-2 zoning applies to Cloverdale ALR parcels is UNVERIFIED on a City source opened in the research pass — confirm a parcel's zone in the City's mapping / Zoning Bylaw 12000 before relying on it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (read 2026-11-01); research file above.
- **Secondary suites — Surrey's own rule, and the stricter ALR rule.** On urban lots, one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area** under Zoning By-law No. 12000, and never inside a coach house or garden suite — Surrey's rule, never borrowed. On ALR land the ALC rule is the stricter frame: the suite must be inside the principal residence only. Coach houses and garden suites on urban lots run 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), with one extra parking space; whether a coach house can serve as the ALR "additional residence" on a given parcel is a parcel-level ALC + City check: UNVERIFIED as a general rule. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite (read 2026-11-01); https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (read 2026-11-01).
- **STRATA block — stated for completeness; no strata duplex exists on the ALR ground.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists off the ALR: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** Subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel off the ALR requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; this slug's acreages sibling.)
- **Servicing, flood and farm classification.** Surrey's servicing position is Bylaw 8830 (1986), VERIFIED from the City's own pages in the verified ledger. Short-term rental figures for Surrey carry a disclosed $358/$350 conflict in the program record — and on ALR land whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence is not permitted outright and requires ALC approval in any event, so no STR figure is relied on in this file. Flood, water and septic for Serpentine lowland parcels (City floodplain mapping, ditches and pump stations, municipal water vs wells / water licences, private septic) are carried in the research as live-page statements with NO official source opened in the pass — all UNVERIFIED here, checked parcel by parcel; parcel-level ALR status itself is UNVERIFIED in this pass (the ALC maps re-open returned a 429 rate-limit error and was not retried). BC Assessment farm classification (Class 9) income thresholds: $2,500 if the farm operation's total area is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha; $10,000 if the total area is less than 0.8 ha — applications by the legislated deadline of October 31 for the next assessment and tax year. The live page's "$2,500 to $10,000+" summary omits the middle tier and is site copy, not adopted. (Research file above; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification, read 2026-11-01.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; **$3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = $93,000 including the further 2% tier** — $93,000 governs at $3.5M (see §5). Farm / mixed-use parcels can split residential and non-residential value, so a real farm bill turns on that split — confirm it with a lawyer/notary; the worked examples are the fully-residential illustration only. A half duplex, where one exists off the ALR, is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale farm residence is not new housing for this rebate, and whether a new farm residence purchase qualifies turns on its own facts — confirm with a tax professional. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy half of a Cloverdale farm's residence?** No separable half exists to buy on ALR ground: ALR residences cannot be stratified, and no fee-simple half-duplex farm subdivision is documented under this topic. The parcel sells whole, under one title. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I add a second residence on a Cloverdale farm?** One additional detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — maximum 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, 186 m² only on parcels over 40 ha — and City bylaws must also permit it. Beyond that frame, the routes are the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / Policy L-25 additional manufactured-home residence route or a Non-Adhering Residential Use application; a general farm-worker entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. (§2; §3.) https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a suite go in a barn or a detached building on a Cloverdale farm?** No — under the ALC housing rules a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; a detached second dwelling is the "additional residence," size-capped under the 90 m² / 186 m² limits. (§2.) https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR farmland in Cloverdale?** One principal residence per parcel without an ALC application, at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; anything larger requires ALC approval, and the City may regulate further. (§2.) https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Another farm nearby has two houses — can mine?** That farm's position is a record of its own ALC history, not an entitlement for yours. The route is s.34(3) / Policy L-25 or a NARU application, read per parcel. (§2.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $3.5M?** $93,000 on a fully residential $3,500,000 purchase with no exemption — tier math in §3. A farm parcel's actual bill turns on its residential / non-residential split. (§3; §5.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put 4 units on a farm parcel?** No. SSMUH is an urban-lot framework for eligible lots inside the Urban Containment Boundary; on ALR land the ALC residence rules govern. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I rent the farm house short-term?** Not as a whole-house rental by right: in the ALR it is not permitted outright and requires ALC approval, on top of the City's own short-term rental rules. (§3.) https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Are the Cloverdale market numbers in this file farm prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no farm category; the §1 figures are Cloverdale residential context only, Serpentine's residential rows were 0 / 0 in both splits, and farm-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. (§1.)
