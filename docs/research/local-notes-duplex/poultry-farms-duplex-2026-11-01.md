## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. On ALR farm ground the first form is closed by rule (below). (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Additional residences — the ALR route.** ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / Policy L-25 frame the additional-residence position for farm parcels, with the NARU application route where the use does not adhere. No general entitlement is asserted; prior approvals on other farms are records of those parcels' positions, not precedents carried here. (Settled ALR position, D2 brief.)
- **SSMUH position.** SSMUH does not rewrite ALR housing rules — farm parcels follow the ALC position above regardless of the surrounding municipality's SSMUH bylaw. (Program discipline, D1 brief chain.)
- **STRATA block — stated for completeness; no strata duplex exists on the ALR ground.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists off the ALR: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy half of a poultry farm's duplex residence?** No separable half exists to buy: ALR residences cannot be stratified, and no fee-simple half-duplex farm subdivision is documented under this topic. The parcel sells whole, under one title. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I add a second residence on a poultry farm?** Only through the ALR route — ALR Use Reg s.34(3) / Policy L-25, or a NARU application where the use does not adhere. A general entitlement is UNCONFIRMED; do not price a purchase on a second residence until the parcel's ALC position is read. (§3.)
- **Q: Does the chicken board's framework affect the residence?** The board sources govern quota and the operation. The residence position runs through the ALC, not the board. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex (off the ALR)?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a poultry-farm purchase?** The general tiers are worked in §3 for the fully-residential case; a farm parcel's actual tax follows its residential/non-residential split, which is parcel-specific and UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for poultry farms?** No. No duplex category is published for farm ground. (§1.)
- **Q: Another poultry farm nearby has two houses — can mine?** That farm's position is a record of its own ALC history, not an entitlement for yours. The route is s.34(3)/L-25 or NARU, read per parcel. (§3.)
