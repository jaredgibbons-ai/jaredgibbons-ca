## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **District plans and zoning — West Vancouver's own bylaws ONLY.** Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 specifies how land can be used and regulates intensity, height, density, setbacks and lot coverage; the Official Community Plan is OCP Bylaw No. 4985, 2018 (Council adopted Amendment Bylaw No. 5406, 2025 on December 1, 2025, aligning the OCP with the 2024 Interim Housing Needs Report, per the condos sibling). White Rock's bylaw numbers are never attributed to West Vancouver in this file. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/bylaws-licensing/find-bylaw/zoning-bylaw-no-4662-2010
- **Density in transition — the Dundarave-adjacent duplex areas specifically.** On July 21, 2025, the Province directed West Vancouver to amend its OCP to provide increased density in the single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave (and at Park Royal–Taylor Way), by December 31, 2025. The District's final adopted zoning amendments for the Dundarave-adjacent single-family/duplex areas were still outstanding in the research file (its UNVERIFIED list), so the current duplex/SSMUH permission for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED in this file — verify the current zoning per property with the District, and never market multiplex potential off the directive alone. - **Secondary suites (West Vancouver's own rule).** The District permits secondary suites under Zoning Bylaw No. 4662 with its own registration process — one per lot, within the principal building (not detached), with the registered owner occupying either the principal dwelling or the suite (per the townhomes / 55-plus siblings). No suite size-cap figure is stated for West Vancouver in the sources opened: any cap is UNCONFIRMED here, and no other municipality's suite rule is borrowed. (Townhomes sibling above.)
- **Servicing: UNCONFIRMED.** The settled ledger holds West Vancouver's servicing bylaw number UNCONFIRMED — no number is asserted in this file. (D19 brief frame.)
- **Development Permit Areas that bite on duplex work.** The Dundarave Village Neighbourhood Centre is a named Form & Character Development Permit area, so new development, additions and renovations in the village core are reviewed against OCP design guidelines. A Foreshore DP applies to waterfront properties and others at risk of coastal flooding — Bellevue Avenue waterfront homes should assume it is in play; an Environmental DP is required for work within 15 metres of the top of a watercourse bank; a Wildfire Hazard DP applies within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface. (Research file above, §4; https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits)
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / half duplex in Dundarave.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the District's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Property taxes and the additional school tax.** Property taxes are billed by the District of West Vancouver — the 2026 payment was due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026; roughly half the bill is collected for other taxing authorities, and water/sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. At Dundarave price points the Province's additional school tax also applies: 0.2% on the residential portion assessed between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. (Research file above, §4; https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes; https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Dundarave?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. Half duplexes exist here as a form class (the townhomes sibling states them as part of the ground-oriented freehold product), but no named separable half is verified in this file — check the title of the specific property first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is the Pink Palace a condo or duplex I can buy?** No. Villa Maris (2222 Bellevue Avenue) is a 101-unit purpose-built rental building, in continuous use as a rental residential high-rise since 1965 per the District's heritage statement — its suites are leased, not sold. A preliminary concept would one day replace it with a strata building, but no such project has been submitted. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Dundarave densifying around the village?** By provincial push, in transition: a July 2025 provincial directive required more density in the single-family/duplex areas adjacent to Dundarave by the end of 2025, but the District's final adopted amendments for those areas were still outstanding in the research — verify the current zoning per property before marketing multiplex potential. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a secondary suite to a Dundarave house?** The District permits secondary suites under Zoning Bylaw No. 4662 with its registration process — one per lot, within the principal building (not detached), with the registered owner occupying either the principal dwelling or the suite. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: What extra rules hit a waterfront half duplex on Bellevue Avenue?** The Foreshore Development Permit area covers properties at risk of coastal flooding, village-core and near-core properties carry Form & Character guidelines, and work within 15 metres of a watercourse's top of bank triggers an Environmental DP. (§3.)
- **Q: What taxes beyond the District bill apply at Dundarave prices?** The Province's additional school tax: 0.2% on the residential portion assessed between $3M and $4M and 0.4% above $4M for 2026 (rising to 0.3% / 0.6% on January 1, 2027), on top of the District bill due July 2, 2026 with its 5% + 5% penalties. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,200,000 fully residential with no exemption = $22,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve Dundarave?** Irwin Park Elementary is in Dundarave itself and West Vancouver Secondary is the catchment secondary on the research's basis — confirm by exact address with SD45; see §5. (§5.)
