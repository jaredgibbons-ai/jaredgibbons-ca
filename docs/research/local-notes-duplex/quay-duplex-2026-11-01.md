## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A ground-oriented unit at the base of a tower strata is none of these forms: it is one strata lot in a large strata, bought with full tower Form B diligence. A co-operative home is also none of these forms: the buyer holds membership / shares, not title to a unit. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Where duplexes are permitted — and the Quay's own zoning.** City-wide, duplex / two-family form sits in the RT districts of Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 — **RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B ONLY**. The Quay's own waterfront zoning is in the same bylaw's multiple-unit residential and comprehensive development districts for the Quayside area; minimum lot sizes are zone-specific and a Quay-specific minimum is UNVERIFIED in the research. The governing plan is OCP Bylaw 7925 (2017). No duplex permission is asserted for a Quay lot in this file. (Research file above; townhomes sibling above; verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **SSMUH / suites position.** No SSMUH or suite rule specific to the Quay's strata waterfront was confirmed on a City source in the research file — the stock is strata apartment form, where suites are not an accessory-use question; a Quay-specific SSMUH figure is UNCONFIRMED and none is asserted. (Townhomes sibling above.)
- **Floodplain — Zone 3 governs every ground-level purchase here.** A building can sit above the Flood Construction Level while its egress and the waterfront path sit below it. Confirm the specific building's compliance position, insurance treatment and any flood covenants on title before removing subjects — for a ground-oriented unit this is the first diligence item, ahead of the strata package. (Townhomes sibling above.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), and in practice to the tower strata lots a Quay buyer actually meets.** Form B diligence applies in full (for a tower unit: the tower's Form B, depreciation report, CRF and insurance — not a small strata's simpler file). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex would be BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; every Quay strata named in the siblings is well over the threshold. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it, and no such split is documented on Quay ground. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** New Westminster's servicing position is Bylaw 7142, 2007, VERIFIED in the verified ledger — and its March 12, 2007 effective date is CORRECT as stated in the City record. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a half duplex at the Quay?** None is verified on this ground — the honest answer is that the Quay's stock is strata apartments (including ground-oriented tower units) plus co-operative housing, not duplexes. - **Q: A Quay listing calls a ground-floor unit a "duplex" — what is it?** On this ground, almost certainly a ground-oriented strata lot in a tower strata: full tower Form B diligence, tower depreciation report and the Zone 3 flood position — not a 2-lot strata duplex. - **Q: What are Westminster Landing and Riverbend — can I buy in?** No. They are co-operative / non-market complexes in the City's non-market inventory (84 and 72 units, mixed tenures) — membership housing, not strata title for sale, and not duplex stock. (§2.)
- **Q: What does Floodplain Zone 3 mean for my purchase?** The Quay is the City's Fraser River waterfront flood zone: many newer buildings have floor space above the Flood Construction Level, but egress and the waterfront path sit below it. Confirm the specific building's position before removing subjects. (§1; §3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. The Quay's tower stratas are all well over it. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw; read the registered bylaws in the Form B package. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $2,000,000 fully residential with no exemption = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for the Quay?** No. SnapStats has no duplex category. (§1.)
