## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Framing for this topic.** No duplex rule is created by an open house. The rules below are (i) the open-house conduct rules that actually govern a visit to any Mission duplex, stated in the settled treatment, and (ii) the Mission frame that governs the ground, carried so a duplex question arising from this feed is answered in the right frame. (Brief chain Mission frame; research file above.)
- **The regulator and the open-house exception — the settled treatment, verbatim where it applies.** In BC, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report, provides that **a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about their motivation, financial qualifications or needs**. How open house dates and times are set and entered for publication (the listing brokerage side, via board systems) is UNVERIFIED — no BC Gov or BCFSA source states it; identification or sign-in requirements for open-house visitors are likewise UNVERIFIED in any BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source found. (Research file above; https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers; http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download)
- **Mission frame (ground rules).** OCP **Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026**; Zoning **Bylaw 5949-2020**; SSMUH incorporated July 17, 2024 (**Bylaw 6299-2024**) with Mission SSMUH **unit counts UNCONFIRMED**; servicing/subdivision **DSCB 5650-2017** from City sources; property taxes due July 2, 2026 with a **FLAT 10% penalty** on July 3, 2026 on balances outstanding (city regime — the rural 5% + 5% regime is DISTINCT and never blended); Mission has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED). Secondary suites: all suites in Mission must be registered with the City; a suite is a self-contained living space in a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity, suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes, and adding one goes through the building permit process — one first-page card in the research describes a suite "registered w. City" in its listing text, listing text only, not independently verified. (Research file above; https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes; https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) a visitor may view on this feed.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; ask what the strata holds anyway. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City process under DSCB 5650-2017 and the zone minimums in Bylaw 5949-2020; party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. No finer detail is stated. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (for a purchase that follows a visit).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale home viewed at an open house is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

(§5.)
- **Q: A home on this feed has a suite — what should I check?** Its registration status. Mission requires every secondary suite to be registered with the City, and an unregistered suite can trigger a bylaw investigation and a possible decommission permit — a card saying "registered w. City" is a claim to verify, not a fact. (§3.)
