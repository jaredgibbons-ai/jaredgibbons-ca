## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 (mixed-class: residential portion only; farm-class land with a dwelling: up to 0.5 ha treated as residential). Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. First-time home buyers' program: first $500,000 exempt, full at a fair market value of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
