## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC only as **(a) a STRATA duplex** (2-lot strata plan; each half a strata lot; Form B diligence) or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex** (parcel subdivided; each half on its own fee-simple lot, party-wall form where a source documents it). A duplex never divided cannot be bought "by the half." (Program definition, `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning (Maple Ridge frame).** Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs; RS-1 minimums are in §1. Which zone permits a two-unit form on a given parcel is a parcel-by-parcel read of the bylaw and City mapping — no zone-by-zone duplex permission table is asserted, because no source opened for this slug states one. (Research file above; http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading)
- **SSMUH position (Maple Ridge frame).** Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial SSMUH framework: one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. Whether a multi-unit SSMUH project is held as strata, rental or freehold lots is a project-level choice, not stated for any feed parcel. (Research file above; https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (Maple Ridge's own rule, never borrowed).** Maximum 40% of gross floor area, inside the principal building, and not within the Floodplain Area — Zoning Bylaw s.402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties; the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), with no habitable floor space below that level on affected lots. (Research file above; https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0)
- **City plan — CONTESTED, resolved nowhere.** This slug's research asserts OCP Bylaw 7998-2024 (adopted June 25, 2024); the program ledger holds the Maple Ridge OCP CONTESTED (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024). The 7998-2024 line is carried here as that research's citation only. Corridor/plan facts carried as sourced: Lougheed Transit Corridor ADOPTED Jan 27, 2026 (Bylaw 7781-2021, City news release Feb 4, 2026), ending at 221 St; Albion Area Plan adopted Oct 28, 2025. Servicing: Subdivision Servicing Bylaw 4800-1993 per the D12 frame (City source); parcel-level servicing (water/sewer/storm at the lot line, or well and a Sewerage System Regulation onsite system by an Authorized Person) is UNVERIFIED per parcel. (Research file above; townhomes sibling §3; https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP; https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems)
- **STRATA block — strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that threshold, so the cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; ask what reports the strata holds anyway. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since Nov 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer detail is stated. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Taxes and registry (Maple Ridge frame).** Property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% September 2, 2026. Supreme Court registry: UNCONFIRMED (ledger). (Research file above; https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC PTT: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. Whether a specific vacant-land transfer attracts PTT on a residential portion is a closing question — no parcel-specific position is stated. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, max $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. It attaches to a new home, not to a bare lot price. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a named duplex development I can shop on this land topic?** No named one is verified. This is a band/topic file: the stock position is the form class — individual duplexes on single lots, and serviced lots that may lawfully take a duplex/SSMUH form parcel by parcel. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I build a duplex on a Maple Ridge lot from this feed?** Possibly, but it is a parcel read: zone under Bylaw 7600-2019, SSMUH eligibility (three, four or six units on eligible lots), floodplain status and ALR status all gate it. No feed parcel is asserted duplex-approved here. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: What is the difference between a duplex and a half duplex?** A duplex purchase is the whole building and lot on one title. A half duplex is one unit sold separately — only where the pair was strata-divided (2-lot plan, Form B diligence) or the parcel was subdivided fee-simple (party-wall title). (§2.)
- **Q: Can a duplex here also carry a suite?** Maple Ridge's own rule: one secondary suite inside the principal building, max 40% of gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area — suites are prohibited on floodplain properties under s.402.8(h). (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget?** Run the tiers on your own price: $800,000 → $14,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 (fully residential, no exemption). A half duplex is taxed on its own price. (§3.)

- **Q: Can I subdivide in the Thornhill Urban Reserve now?** OCP policy holds Urban Reserve development until specific triggers and milestones are attained; the live page's hectarage, section number and population trigger are UNVERIFIED — do not price a parcel on a subdivision date. (§1.)
