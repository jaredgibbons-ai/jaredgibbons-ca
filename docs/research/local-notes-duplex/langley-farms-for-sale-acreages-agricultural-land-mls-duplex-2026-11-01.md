## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES AND FARM RESIDENCES

- **ALR housing frame (the governing frame on farm ground).** A principal residence permitted without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction. A secondary suite in the ALR is permitted only when it is located entirely within the principal residence — a separate or detached dwelling is not used as a secondary suite — and local government bylaws permit secondary suites. One additional detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met (ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25): a maximum total floor area of 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less, and 186 m² on properties more than 40 ha — and Township bylaws must also permit it. Beyond those conditions, an additional residence is a Non-Adhering Residential Use (NARU) application to the ALC; approval is never assumed. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-farms-for-sale-acreages-agricultural-land-mls.md, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **Zoning — Township frame.** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 applies across the Township; Bylaw No. 2500 s.102 defines a coach house as an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage on a lot — a fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata. A general minimum parcel size for the rural (RU) zones from the direct bylaw zone text, and any farm home plate rule, are UNVERIFIED in this slug's research. Servicing: Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED. In the R-CL zone, s.403 sets a 220 m² minimum lot area and a 464.5 m² maximum lot area — the "355 m²" figure is NOWHERE in the bylaw and is never carried. Which zone permits a two-unit (duplex) form on a given non-ALR parcel is a parcel-by-parcel read of Bylaw 2500; no zone-by-zone table is asserted. (Research file above, §4; settled Township frame, D15 brief.)
- **SSMUH position (Township frame; duplexes are the baseline form off the ALR).** Township SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — an eligible lot (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on both municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units if 280 m² or smaller, or 4 units if between 280 m² and 4,050 m²; lots larger than 4,050 m² are not eligible; there is NO 6-plex tier in the Township. The City frame, carried only because the feed is Langley-wide (City ground is not farm ground in this research): SSMUH Bylaw 3284 (City-only), RS1/RS2 lots 4 units or 6 within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; City suite = lesser of 100 m² / 40% under Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 (number only). (Research file above, §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (Township rule).** Township Zoning Bylaw s.102 caps a secondary suite at 120 m² maximum gross floor area, one per principal dwelling unit. On ALR land the ALC rule above governs first: the suite must sit entirely within the principal residence. (Research file above, §4.)
- **Farm classification (BC Assessment) — the value frame a farm buyer prices.** A producing farm must meet minimum gross income requirements at farm gate prices: $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — with farm classification applications due by October 31. (Research file above, §4; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification)
- **Water and septic (diligence frame).** The provincial GWELLS application is the registry for recorded wells and aquifers; under the Water Sustainability Act, domestic groundwater use does not need a licence, and non-domestic use, including irrigation, requires a water licence. A home not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite sewage disposal system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, and only an Authorized Person can design, install, repair, maintain or inspect a sewerage system (Fraser Health). The Township's documented dyke system is on the Fraser River at the Salmon River mouth; Township-wide floodplain mapping and parcel-level floodplain status are UNVERIFIED. Supply-managed production (quota): no marketing-board or CFIA source was opened in this slug's research — every quota claim is UNVERIFIED; never price quota from this file. (Research file above, §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration; https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems; https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/salmon-river-dike-upgrades.aspx)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), off-ALR ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. On ALR land, the ALC Bulletin 09 s.6 frame above governs strata questions first. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Township's Approving Officer process under Servicing Bylaw 5382 (CONFIRMED) — and on ALR land, ALC subdivision rules apply as well; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer; farm purchases can mix residential and farm-class portions — have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or farm residence is not new housing for this rebate. No GST-on-farmland rule beyond this standard block is stated — this slug's research opens no farmland-GST source, so any farm-specific GST treatment is UNCONFIRMED here. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a named farm duplex or half duplex I can shop on this topic?** No. No named duplex stock of any form is verified for this topic in any source in this program, and none of this slug's six siblings mentions a duplex at all. On farm ground the second-dwelling question runs through the ALR forms in §3, not through duplex tenure. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a Langley farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, only through the ALR routes: a secondary suite entirely within the principal residence, or one additional detached residence where the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / Policy L-25 conditions are met (90 m² maximum on parcels of 40 ha or less; 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha) and Township bylaws also permit it — or a Non-Adhering Residential Use (NARU) approval from the ALC. A general entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. (§§2, 3.)
- **Q: How big can the principal residence be on ALR land?** A principal residence permitted without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction. (§3.)
- **Q: What is the difference between a duplex and a farm's additional residence?** A duplex is one building with two dwelling units on one title (or a 2-lot strata / subdivided pair as half duplexes). A farm's additional residence under the ALR frame is a separate, size-capped dwelling (90 m² / 186 m²) permitted under ALC conditions — a different legal route, not a duplex, and not separately saleable as a half duplex. (§§2, 3.)
- **Q: What income does a farm need to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income (farm gate) thresholds: $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation; $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; applications due by October 31. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $1,500,000? At $2,000,000?** On the worked tiers in §3 — fully residential, no exemption — $28,000 at $1,500,000 and $38,000 at $2,000,000. Farm purchases can mix portions, so have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. On ALR land, check the ALC Bulletin 09 s.6 strata frame first. (§3.)
- **Q: Is there a median price for Langley farms or farm duplexes?** No. SnapStats publishes no farm or agricultural-land category and no duplex category, so no farm median, price-per-acre or farm sales count exists in the sources used; §1's figures are Langley residential community context only. (§1.)
- **Q: Does quota come with a Langley farm purchase?** UNVERIFIED — no marketing-board or CFIA source was opened for this file. If a listing mentions quota, verify it directly with the relevant marketing board before pricing it in. (§3.)
