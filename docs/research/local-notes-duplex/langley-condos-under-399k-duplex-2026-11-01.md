## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **SSMUH position (both frames; duplexes are the baseline form).** Township: SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — an eligible lot (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on both municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units if 280 m² or smaller, or 4 units if between 280 m² and 4,050 m²; lots larger than 4,050 m² are not eligible; there is NO 6-plex tier in the Township. City: SSMUH Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 — RS1/RS2 lots permit 4 units, or 6 if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (City-only bylaw; never applied to Township ground). (Research file above, §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx; https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3284)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (each municipality's own rule, never borrowed).** Township: Zoning Bylaw s.102 caps a secondary suite at 120 m² maximum gross floor area, one per principal dwelling unit; a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102 (an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage on a lot), never strata. City: under Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300, a secondary suite may not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, the registered owner must reside on the lot (s.2.4), and a carriage/garden ground-floor unit is capped at 100 m². (Research file above, §4.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. - **Q: What is the difference between a duplex, a strata half duplex and a duplex-style townhouse?** A duplex is one building with two dwelling units on one title. A strata half duplex is one half of a 2-lot strata plan, bought as its own strata lot with Form B diligence. A "duplex-style townhouse" (Churchill Park, Spencer Green) is a townhouse unit in a larger strata complex built in a paired, two-unit look — it is townhouse stock, not a 2-lot duplex. (§2.)

- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $500,000? At $800,000?** On the worked tiers in §3 — fully residential, no exemption — $8,000 at $500,000 and $14,000 at $800,000. A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: How many units can a Langley lot hold if I buy a duplex lot instead?** It depends on the municipality: Township Bylaw 6020 gives an eligible lot 3 units at 280 m² or smaller or 4 units at 280–4,050 m², with no 6-plex tier; City Bylaw 3284 gives RS1/RS2 lots 4 units, or 6 within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Check the municipality first. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a Township duplex also carry a coach house or suite?** A Township secondary suite is capped at 120 m² under s.102, and a coach house under s.102 is a fee-simple second unit in the second storey of a detached garage — never a strata unit and never sold separately. In the City, a suite is capped at the lesser of 100 m² / 40% with owner-occupancy under Bylaw 3300. The specific lot's zoning controls. (§3.)
