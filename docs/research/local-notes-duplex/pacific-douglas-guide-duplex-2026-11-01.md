## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. **"Surrey Half Duplexes" IS the Paragon Surrey half-duplex grouping (Jared's ruling, Oct 21, 2026)** — stated as the board grouping it is where a Surrey file's feed/research uses it; this slug's guide research does not itself use that grouping label, so no feed claim is taken from it here. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan and zoning (Surrey frame):** the in-force check for any Douglas lot is the Surrey 2050 OCP designation (OCP Bylaw 21900, adopted Jun 15, 2026 — NEVER 18020-as-current) plus the lot's zone under Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 — not the 1999 Douglas NCP designations that listing text still quotes, which the City states have been superseded. Zones permitting a duplex / two-unit form are those allowing duplexes and houseplexes under Bylaw 12000 as the research states them; a parcel's exact zone is a per-lot check. SSMUH position (Surrey, Jul 8, 2024): duplexes are the baseline small-scale multi-unit form. Coach houses and garden suites in Surrey: permitted in residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes; 35 m² minimum and 75 m² maximum, rising to a maximum of 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the principal dwelling has not achieved its maximum floor area. Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated. (Research file, §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/douglas-ncp; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (Surrey's own rule, never borrowed):** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, and its habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area** under Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000; the old 90 m² Surrey suite cap is NOT a current rule — do not state it. A feed card's "licenced revenue suite" wording is a claim to verify against City records, not a fact. (Research file, §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted only where the owner hosts from their principal residence and holds a City business licence at **$358 per year** (Zoning By-law No. 12000, Part 4 s. B.7(d); Business License By-law No. 13680, s. 60.1) — carried with the City's 2024 **$350/yr proposal disclosed** as a proposal, not the licence fee. (Research file, §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **ALR edge:** the plan area is bordered by the ALR to the north; any parcel in the ALR follows the ALC housing frame of one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). Whether a specific parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level. Current parcel-level servicing (City water/sewer vs well/septic) is UNVERIFIED in this pass — the City servicing statement read is from a 2007 corporate report. (Research file, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/RPT_2007-L001.pdf)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Surrey ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED) — relevant to court-ordered sales diligence, stated as the ledger carries it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process under Servicing Bylaw 8830, and the zone minimums decide whether a Douglas lot can split at all. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Presales / REDMA — Harlowe:** REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission period and s.18 requires deposits held in trust. GST applies to Harlowe as new construction, not to resales. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction (Harlowe), not resales. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
