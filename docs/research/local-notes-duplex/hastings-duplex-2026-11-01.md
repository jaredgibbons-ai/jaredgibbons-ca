## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** The house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Vancouver Zoning and Development Bylaw 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m) and 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units, and a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house. Corridor commercial and mixed-use sites on Hastings Street sit under other district schedules, including C-3A on parts of the street. The zone of a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until read. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf (research file above)
- **SSMUH position.** R1-1 IS Vancouver's SSMUH framework for these streets. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Brief chain.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the city's own rule.** Vancouver's R1-1 schedule permits a secondary suite and a laneway house with a single detached house in the forms the schedule states, and secondary suites / lock-off units inside duplexes as the schedule states; the specific lot's schedule governs and no size figure is borrowed from another city. (Research file above.)
- **Plan position.** The western edge of Hastings-Sunrise sits inside the Grandview-Woodland Community Plan area, which layers plan policy on top of base zoning; the citywide Vancouver Plan also applies. A separate community plan named only for Hastings-Sunrise: UNVERIFIED. (Research file above.)
- **Flood position.** The City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain; the Burrard Inlet shoreline at the area's north side, including New Brighton Park, is the part to check against that dataset. Whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/ (research file above)
- **Empty Homes Tax.** Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax is 3% — VANCOUVER ONLY, never carried into another city's file. Owners must submit an annual property status declaration (due February 3, 2026); the 2025 tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx (research file above)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the municipality's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums in §3; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. This is the operative diligence on this ground's documented fee-simple half-duplex product: the "no strata fee" listing signal is verified on title, and the party-wall agreement is read on both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; townhomes sibling above.)
- **Servicing.** Vancouver's servicing (Subdivision) Bylaw 5208 (fees under Bylaw 14538) is VERIFIED per the verified ledger. (Brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is "Hastings" the same as Hastings-Sunrise — and is it in Burnaby?** Neither, exactly: Hastings-Sunrise is the City of Vancouver's official local area anchoring the Paragon "Hastings" name; Burnaby's Hastings area is a separate area in a different municipality, east across Boundary Road. Write "Hastings-Sunrise, Vancouver" in every search and listing. (FILE STATUS.)
- **Q: Are the new half-duplexes here strata?** The documented product is fee-simple house form: current listings on Triumph Street and Eton Street show new half-duplex homes, several expressly "no strata fee." Verify on title — a fee-simple half has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; diligence is title, the R1-1 schedule and any registered party-wall agreement. (§2, §3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me split a duplex into two titles?** No — R1-1 adds unit potential on a lot (a multiplex of up to 6 units, or up to 8 rental units, at the §3 site areas); a separately titled half needs a strata plan or a subdivision. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for Hastings?** No — duplexes have no separate published category, and Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage, so no market figure is stated in this file at all. (§1.)
