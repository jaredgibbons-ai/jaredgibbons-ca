## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Delta's SSMUH amendments permit small-scale multi-unit housing on single-detached and duplex lots — combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies. Delta's zoning bylaw number is CONTESTED in this program (7600 vs "8400, 2025"): "7600" citations in the research and siblings are carried as citations, flagged, and asserted NOWHERE in this file; the zone for a specific East Delta lot is read off the City's current zoning for that lot. The OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) with NO lettered schedules — any source citing a lettered schedule (e.g. "Schedule B.1" for East Ladner) is citing the OLD Bylaw 3950 plan, consolidated away in 2024. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **SSMUH position.** Zoning Amendment Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024, are Delta's SSMUH amendments. On this ground their reach is the urban edge: SSMUH is added-unit potential on an existing lot, not a subdivision right, and it does not displace the ALR / A-zone subdivision rules on farm ground. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Delta's own position.** Delta's SSMUH framework includes secondary suites in the permitted combinations on single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies. A Delta-specific suite size cap is not stated in this ground's research or siblings, so NO cap figure is carried here — asserting Surrey's 40% or any other city's figure for Delta would be borrowing, and the cap for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until read in Delta's current zoning text. (Research file above; acreages sibling §SSMUH.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. On ALR parcels the ALC position also applies: ALR residences cannot be stratified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, and on ALR land an ALC subdivision position as well; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles; a general party-wall rule for this ground is UNCONFIRMED. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** The Delta Subdivision and Development Standards Bylaw 8288 adoption position is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — siblings that assert DSSS 8288 as adopted are flagged in the ledger, and no servicing position is asserted in this file. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer; a farm parcel's PTT follows its own residential / non-residential mix, which is a per-parcel calculation, not a figure stated here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; it does NOT apply to bare land itself. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy half a duplex in East Delta?** No verified half-duplex stock exists here. The duplex form class reaches only the East Ladner urban edge under Delta's SSMUH combinations; the farm belt is whole-parcel ALR ground. A separately titled half would need a strata plan or a subdivided lot, and none is documented. (§2.)
- **Q: A farm listing says "duplex" — what am I buying?** Almost always one parcel under one title: a principal house plus a second residence or a two-unit farm building, sold whole. The wording is a record only; the title is the verification. (§2.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me split a farm lot into two half-duplex lots?** No. The siblings state it expressly: SSMUH is added-unit potential on an existing lot, not a subdivision right, and it does not displace the ALR / A-zone subdivision rules. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex (at the urban edge)?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a duplex here?** Delta's SSMUH combinations include secondary suites on duplex lots where the zoning applies; a Delta suite size cap is UNCONFIRMED in this program — read it in Delta's current zoning text for the lot before counting rental income. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for East Delta?** No. SnapStats does not cover South Delta and CADREB/FVREB publish no duplex category for this ground. (§1.)
