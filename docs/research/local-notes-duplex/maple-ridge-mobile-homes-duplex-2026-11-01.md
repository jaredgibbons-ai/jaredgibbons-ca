## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A manufactured home on a rented pad is none of these forms: the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the site — there is no lot, no strata plan and no subdivided half. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing; park tenure per the research file above)

(Research file above; https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/Zoning%20bylaw%20report.pdf)
- **SSMUH position (city frame).** Eligible single-family and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws — a lot-based framework; it does not convert a park pad or a manufactured home into a duplex or a separately titled half, and it does not apply to a rented pad the buyer does not own. (Area research for the Maple Ridge slugs in this batch; https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new)
- **Secondary suites (city frame).** Maple Ridge's own rule — one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of the gross floor area, not within the Floodplain Area — is a rule for a house on its own lot; no suite position for a manufactured home or a park pad is stated in this topic's research, so none is carried here: UNCONFIRMED for the manufactured form. (Research file above; https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites)
- **The manufactured-home rules that actually govern this topic (for contrast with the strata block).** Registration is what makes a sale effective: the buyer and seller file the original signed and witnessed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership forms through a lawyer or notary with the Manufactured Home Registry; modular homes are built under CSA A-277 and mobile homes under CSA Z-240, and a home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal (a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification). In a park, assignment of the site tenancy needs the landlord's prior written consent under MHPTA s. 28 (or deemed consent / an arbitrator's order / tenancy-agreement authorisation); on assignment the pad rent stays the same and the next rent-increase date does not reset; increases otherwise need 3 full months' notice, at most once every 12 months, within the yearly limit plus the proportional levy / regulated-utility amount. A park home's assessed value for tax purposes does not include the land value. (Research file above; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), stated so the contrast is exact.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. **None of this block governs a pad-rental park home** — the MHPTA and the Registry govern there (above). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. A manufactured home has no party-wall title structure of this kind. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Maple Ridge's Subdivision Servicing Bylaw No. 4800-1993, from a City source (verified ledger, brief chain).
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. How PTT applies to a manufactured home on a rented pad (as opposed to land) is transaction-specific: UNCONFIRMED per transaction — confirm with the lawyer / notary handling the Registry filing (per this slug's townhomes sibling). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a manufactured home on a pad a half duplex if it is paired or semi-detached in form?** No. On a rented pad you own the home as a chattel and rent the site under the MHPTA — there is no lot, no strata plan and no subdivided half to buy, whatever the built form looks like. (§2.)
- **Q: Does this file list Maple Ridge's parks?** No — TOPIC file, no roster. Alouette Retirement MHP is documented at its East Central GUIDE mobile home and Val Maria at its Southwest Maple Ridge mobile home; Baker Trail Village and Sea Ridge Bays live in their designated topic files elsewhere. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Maple Ridge?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. This topic file verifies none by name; the ground files carry the city's form class. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex versus a park home?** Entirely different regimes: a strata half takes Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata; a park home takes the Registry filing, the CSA label check and the MHPTA s. 28 assignment consent, with the pad rent unchanged on assignment. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can the park raise my pad rent when I buy?** No — on assignment the pad rent stays the same and the scheduled increase date does not change; increases otherwise follow the 3-month / 12-month / yearly-limit-plus-proportional-amount rules in §3. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. That is a strata rule; a park's rules are the park's written rules and tenancy agreement. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $8,000 (worked in §3). A park-home transfer's PTT position is per-transaction: UNCONFIRMED — confirm at filing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Are manufactured-home or duplex prices published for Maple Ridge?** No — SnapStats publishes neither category; §1 is Maple Ridge residential context only, and any pad-rent range is UNVERIFIED. (§1.)
