## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. In the Township, a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata, never a half duplex. A home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership at all — the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad (the tenure line, applied to the Forest Green record below). (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing; settled Township frame.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan (Township frame):** the Walnut Grove Community Plan is Bylaw 1978 No. 1836, adopted by Council on November 26, 1979, now Schedule 6 to the OCP as updated by Bylaw No. 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025); the Township's OCP update is revising community plans including this one — check the plan designation for the exact lot before planning changes. (Research file, §§1, 4, 6; https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/ATT-F---Walnut-Grove-CP---Redlined.pdf)
- **Zoning, SSMUH and suites (Township frame — SETTLED in the research):** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs. SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) allows **3 units on eligible lots of 280 m² or smaller and 4 units on larger eligible lots up to 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible, and NO 6-unit tier applies in the Township** — duplexes are the baseline SSMUH form. Eligibility in the research's terms: inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on both municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area. A secondary suite in the Township is capped at **120 m²** — the Township's own cap, never borrowed. A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata. Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 is CONFIRMED. (Research file, §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Township ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. This block does NOT apply to the pad-rental Forest Green record in §2 — it is non-strata leasehold-side under the MHPTA, and its tenure position is UNCONFIRMED in any event. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Township's Approving Officer process under Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file, §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Township taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. (Research file, §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx)
