## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A strata townhouse complex built in duplex style is the multi-lot expression of the strata form: each home is a strata lot in one strata plan, sold separately, with Form B diligence — the form cross-referenced below. Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Port Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw No. 3630).** Single-detached homes sit under RS1, RS2, RS3 and RS4 (minimum lot widths above); apartment zones RA1 and RA2 and townhouse zones RTh1, RTh2 and RTh3 exist in the bylaw (a unit checked at The Uplands is zoned RTH2, per the townhouses sibling's listing basis), but no Citadel apartment buildings or further townhome complex names were confirmed from an official source in the research. The two-unit form sits in the zones Bylaw 3630 permits it in; the zone for a specific lot is read off the current bylaw text for that lot. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2025-08/3630%20-%20Zoning%20Bylaw%202008.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **SSMUH position (City position as the research states it).** The City permits 3 to 6 small-scale multi-unit housing units on RS-zoned lots without rezoning, depending on lot size, location and transit proximity — the City's own published range, stated as the City states it. Duplexes are the baseline two-unit form within it. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — PoCo's own position.** No PoCo suite size cap is stated in this slug's research or siblings, so NO cap figure is carried here — no other city's figure is borrowed. The suite position for a specific Citadel home is UNCONFIRMED until read in Bylaw 3630's current text. (Research file above; central-pt-coquitlam siblings carry the same position.)
- **STRATA block — applies in full to Citadel Pointe and to any strata duplex / strata half duplex.** Form B diligence applies in full (Citadel Pointe's pet position is a Form B confirmation). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**, stated precisely. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts — and no Citadel strata is verified to carry any age bylaw at all (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Approving Officer process; no finer PoCo process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves (or paired strata homes, beyond the strata's own common-property regime) rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the titles — read them. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** PoCo servicing UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — never asserted. (D19 frame.)
- **Tax note (research-stated only):** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes unpaid after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026; utility bills are flat annual water and sewer fees, billed separately and due March 31, 2026. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes (Research file §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex (or a Citadel Pointe strata home) is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. No Citadel presale project was verified on a developer site or a City development page. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
