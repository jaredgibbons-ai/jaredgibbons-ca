## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Nightly-rental rules (material to any duplex bought as a rental).** Nightly rental runs on Village zoning/licensing, not the provincial principal-residence rule — the Province lists the Village among the 14 resort regions **exempt** from that requirement. (Research file above, §4; https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2023hous0060-001598 and https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Bylaw-1128---Business-Licence-and-Regulation-%28Consolidated-to-December-15%2C-2025%29.pdf)
- **STRATA block — stated for completeness; no strata duplex exists on this ground.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists elsewhere: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. **Fractional interests (the Harrison Beach Hotel record) are NOT strata-lot purchases** — no Form B diligence framework is asserted for a 1/3 share; the interest is read on title. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

(Research file above, §4; https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/village-office/public-notices/council-meeting-highlights-june-15-2026)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12; see the sibling check in §5) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any half duplex for sale in Harrison Hot Springs?** None verified by name, in any source in this program. The census counts 45 semi-detached houses and 5 apartments/flats in a duplex (2021, occupied dwellings) but states no tenure for any of them — a semi-detached house here cannot be assumed to be a separately titled half. (§2.)
- **Q: What is the Harrison Beach Hotel offering I saw?** A tenure record, not ordinary stock: the hotel is a strata hotel, and a current listing documents a 1/3 fractional-ownership offering (2 weeks' yearly use per the listing record in the siblings). Read the exact interest on title before treating it as a unit purchase. (§2.)
- **Q: Can any Harrison condo or duplex be run as a nightly rental?** No — "Tourist Accommodation" is a commercial-zone use under Zoning Bylaw No. 1115, 2017, the Village has specific Resort Residential provisions, and since Dec 15, 2025 each dwelling unit so operated needs its own Village business licence. The Village is exempt from the provincial STR principal-residence rule as a resort region, but Village licensing governs. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex (off this ground)?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (Village nightly-rental licensing is a separate layer; §3.) (§3.)
- **Q: Is flood risk real for a lakeshore duplex here?** Yes — dike crest 13.9 m vs the 1894 flood at 14.1 m and a required 15.1 m crest; FCL 14.55 m (Bylaw No. 1179, 2022). Check the home's floor elevation against the FCL before writing. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for Harrison?** No. CADREB publishes no area stats and no SnapStats row exists — pricing rests on comparable sales, and resort demand is seasonal. (§1.)
- **Q: Where do Harrison kids go to school?** Harrison Hot Springs Elementary (K–6) in the village, then Agassiz Elementary-Secondary School (7–12) in Agassiz, both SD78. A replacement elementary school inside a shared "Civic Campus" is being explored with SD78 but is conceptual and unfunded — the June 15, 2026 MOU motion failed and a new exploratory MOU is in development; never present it as a promise. (§5.)
