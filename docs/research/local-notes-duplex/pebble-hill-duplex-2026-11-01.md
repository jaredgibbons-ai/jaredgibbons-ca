## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **SSMUH position.** Delta's SSMUH zoning amendments (Bylaws 8394 + 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 per this slug's siblings) allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies, and the City's Housing Accelerator initiatives include help for owners creating accessory dwelling units such as coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites. A duplex is the baseline two-unit form; SSMUH does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves — a specific lot's buildable form is a parcel-level check with the City. (Research file above; http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Delta's own rule as its sources state it.** No Delta percentage or size cap for a suite inside a duplex is stated in this slug's research or siblings, so no figure is borrowed from another city and the cap is UNCONFIRMED here. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. Note the contrast this ground actually presents: the feed's half duplexes are documented as fee-simple ("no strata fees"), so for those homes there is NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — diligence is title, zoning and municipal requirements. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the municipality's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: Are there any townhomes or condos in Pebble Hill?** None verified — 0 townhouse complexes and 0 apartment-condo buildings in the sibling runs; attached product here is the half duplex form. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a fee-simple half duplex?** No Form B and no strata documents — instead: title, the registered party-wall agreement / easement on both titles, zoning, and municipal flood-construction requirements. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a Pebble Hill lot add a suite or more units?** Delta's framework allows up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies, with City support for coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites — what a specific lot can build is a parcel-level check with the City. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
