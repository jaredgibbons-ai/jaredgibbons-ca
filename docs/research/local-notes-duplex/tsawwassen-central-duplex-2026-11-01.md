## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Delta frame).** Delta's current OCP is Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules. The RD zones are Delta's duplex zones, assigned parcel by parcel on the zoning maps; subdivision minimums in the zones found in central Tsawwassen include RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² (or 460 m² outside the Single Family Residential designation), RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m², RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m². The zone for a specific property is read off the City's current zoning for that lot. **ZONING NUMBER CONTESTED:** the research cites "Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600" flatly (e.g. the zone sheets and the bylaw PDF) — that citation is carried as a citation, flagged here per the verified ledger ("7600, 2017" vs "8400, 2025"), and the number is asserted NOWHERE in this file. (Research file above; https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf, read 2026-11-01.)
- **SSMUH position.** Delta's SSMUH zoning amendments (Bylaws 8394 + 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots. A duplex is the baseline two-unit form; SSMUH does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves, and added-unit permission is not subdivision permission. (Research file above; http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future, read 2026-11-01; https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Delta's own rule as its sources state it.** No Delta percentage or size cap for a suite inside an urban duplex is stated in this slug's research, so no figure is borrowed from another city and the cap is UNCONFIRMED here. Delta does permit 2 to 4 hens for personal use on a lot in any RS or RD zone (coop and run max 10 m², no roosters), with an annual licence under the Animal Control Bylaw — a small but real disclosure point for sellers with coops. (Research file above, §6; program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier** — $93,000 governs at $3.5M. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: A listing calls a central property a half duplex — can I buy just that half?** Only if that half is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot. The listing wording settles nothing; check the title. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is Sundial or South Grove duplex stock?** No. Sundial is a strata townhouse complex counted once in its guide townhomes home; South Grove is a 37-unit freehold-strata townhouse collection QUEUED in both files that touch it and counted nowhere. Both are townhouse-form. (§2.)
- **Q: Are the 56 Street buildings duplexes?** No — the six verified central buildings (Windsor Woods, Oliva, The Lexington, Heron Place, Tsawwassen Greene, Cambridge Place) are apartment condos. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add units to a central duplex lot?** Delta's SSMUH bylaws (8394 + 8395) allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots — as added units on the one lot, not as new titles. In the Urban Centre, the OCP Appendix B permit-area guidelines also apply. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Which schools serve a central duplex?** Address-based in SD37 — for the sample address 1299 56 St the locator result is Cliff Drive Elementary and South Delta Secondary; run the exact address. (§5.)
