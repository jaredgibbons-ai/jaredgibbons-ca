## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). For this topic the form matters only through the tax question: GST is charged on a NEW duplex or half duplex sold by a builder, and is not charged on a resale — the legal form (strata vs fee-simple) does not change that boundary. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; new-vs-resale boundary: research file, §5; https://jaredgibbons.ca/gst-rebate-in-bc.html)

A newly built duplex bought whole from a builder, a newly built strata or fee-simple half duplex bought from a builder, and an owner-built duplex all sit inside that class on the same terms as any other new single-unit residential complex; a resale duplex or half duplex sits outside it entirely. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **FTHB GST/HST Rebate — the core rule (verified on CRA pages opened in this slug's research pass).** Enactment: amendments to Part IX of the Excise Tax Act in Bill C-4 introduced the rebate; **Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 per CRA** (CRA Excise and GST/HST News No. 122) — the date the live page states; March 12 in secondary sources is REJECTED. Amount: together with the GST/HST new housing rebate, the FTHB rebate provides **100% of the GST (or the federal part of the HST) on a newly constructed or substantially renovated single unit residential complex or residential condominium unit valued up to $1 million; between $1 million and $1.5 million the rebate is gradually reduced; and no FTHB rebate is available at $1.5 million or more**; the maximum is **$50,000** (100% of the 5% GST on a $1 million home in BC). First-time buyer definition: at least 18, a Canadian citizen or permanent resident, and not have lived in a home — in or outside Canada — that they or their spouse or common-law partner owned or jointly owned as a primary place of residence at any time in the calendar year of the purchase or in the preceding four calendar years. Timing/property conditions: meet the existing new housing rebate's criteria (or would meet them if that rebate's ceiling were $1.5 million instead of $450,000); a purchase agreement with a builder on or after **March 20, 2025 and before 2031** (for an owner-built home, construction beginning on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031); construction beginning before 2031 and substantially completed before 2036; the buyer the first to occupy the home after substantial completion; and no previous FTHB rebate application. The rebate also generally covers a home bought on land leased from the builder and a share in a co-operative housing corporation for a new unit. Claiming: usually within two years of taking ownership or finishing construction; a builder can credit the rebate at transfer; otherwise the buyer applies directly to CRA (Form GST190 for a builder purchase; Form GST191 for an owner-built home). **The $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism:** the live page's "phases out on a straight-line basis / 50% at $1.25M (up to $25,000)" wording was not stated on the CRA pages opened — they say only "gradually reduced" — so the midpoint figure is carried as page copy only, never as a CRA-stated formula. GST on land / new housing generally is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/news/newsroom/tax-tips/tax-tips-2026/first-time-buyers-can-save-more-on-new-homes-the-first-time-home-buyers-gst-hst-rebate-is-available-now.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html (Research file, §4.)

- **BC PTT — the page's "other programs" section (verified in this pass).** General rates: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000, on fair market value at registration. Worked anchors (fully residential, no exemption): $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier** ($2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). BC first-time home buyers' program: exempt on the first $500,000 where the property is a principal residence with fair market value **$835,000 or less** (effective April 1, 2024), 0.5 hectares or smaller, residential improvements only, with the Province's qualifying conditions; a partial exemption may apply between $835,000 and $860,000; the page's "up to $8,000" saving is the general-rate tax on $500,000. The federal GST rebate and the BC PTT exemption are separate programs and can both apply to the same qualifying purchase. Relied on batch-6 without re-opening, stated plainly: the BC newly built home exemption (full to $1,100,000, phase-out to $1,150,000) was verified on the Province's page in batch-6 and is not restated as a figure beyond this note, because the live gst-rebate page does not raise it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers (Research file, §4.)
- **STRATA block — for a NEW strata duplex / strata half duplex bought from a builder (2-lot strata plan), the diligence that follows completion.** Form B diligence applies once the strata exists (a presale duplex is a REDMA developer contract until completion and registration — the disclosure statement is the presale diligence document). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the applicable Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this topic slug states it, and no single city's process is asserted for a province-wide topic. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. GST treatment does not turn on the split: a new fee-simple half sold by a builder is new housing either way. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How much GST can a first-time buyer in BC get back on a new home?** Up to $50,000: together with the new housing rebate, the FTHB rebate returns 100% of the GST on a new or substantially renovated home valued up to $1 million, is gradually reduced between $1 million and $1.5 million, and is not available at $1.5 million or more. The same thresholds apply to a new duplex or half duplex bought from a builder. (§3.)
- **Q: Was Royal Assent March 12 or March 13, 2026?** March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — that date governs. March 12 appears in some secondary sources and is REJECTED in this program; this file states March 13 exactly as the settled position. (§§1, 3.)
- **Q: Does the rebate apply to a resale duplex or half duplex?** No. The rebate is for new housing only, and resale homes are not subject to GST in the first place. Only a newly built or substantially renovated duplex/half bought from a builder (or owner-built) inside the window can qualify. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Who counts as a first-time buyer for this rebate?** At least 18, a Canadian citizen or permanent resident, and neither you nor your spouse or common-law partner lived in a home either of you owned as a primary residence in the purchase year or the four preceding calendar years. Verify your own four-year history before relying on the rebate. (§3.)
- **Q: What happened to the old GST new housing rebate?** It still exists on its own terms: generally 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, gradually reduced between $350,000 and $450,000, unavailable at $450,000 or more. The FTHB rebate is the separate top-up that takes a qualifying first-time buyer's combined recovery to 100%. (§3.)
- **Q: My new home costs $1.25 million — do I get exactly $25,000 back?** UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. The live page's table assumes a straight-line phase-out (50% at the midpoint), and its result is consistent with the verified thresholds, but the CRA pages opened say only "gradually reduced" between $1M and $1.5M. Have the builder, lawyer or CRA calculation confirm the amount for your agreement before relying on the midpoint figure. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: How and when do I claim?** Usually within two years of taking ownership or finishing construction: a builder can credit the rebate at closing (Form GST190 jointly completed), or you apply directly to CRA through a CRA account or by mailing Form GST190 — Form GST191 for an owner-built home. Settle the crediting arrangement before completion. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I combine the GST rebate with BC's PTT exemption?** Yes — separate federal and provincial programs: the BC first-time program exempts PTT on the first $500,000 at a fair market value of $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, and nothing in either program's conditions as read in this pass bars claiming both on the same purchase. (§3.)
- **Q: Does the signing date or the completion date control eligibility?** The signing date is the gate the research stresses: a builder agreement entered into on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031, with construction begun before 2031 and substantially completed before 2036 — a presale signed before March 20, 2025 is outside the rebate no matter the price or the completion date. (§3.)
