## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **SSMUH positions (each municipality's own, never blended).** Township: Bylaw 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024) — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², subject to eligibility; NO 6-plex tier in the Township. Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial framework. City of Langley: Bylaw 3284 (City-only). Which frame applies to a given parcel is a parcel read of its municipality. (Research file above; program ledger in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D12-BRIEF.md`.)
- **Secondary suites (each city's own rule, never borrowed).** Township: under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s.102, a secondary suite may not exceed **120 m²** maximum gross floor area, one per principal dwelling unit; a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata. City of Langley: a secondary suite may not exceed the **lesser of 100 m² and 40%** of the building's gross floor area, with owner-occupancy required (s.2.4). Surrey's 40%-of-principal-habitable-area rule and Abbotsford's under-50% rule are other cities' rules and are never carried here. (Research file above; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md.)
- **Pad-rent mechanism — the topic's own rules, carried because a pad buyer meets them instead of strata rules.** A park landlord may increase rent only with 3 full months' written notice, only once every 12 months, and only up to the yearly limit: **2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026; 2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027.** The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park. The landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion of a prior year's limit; an increase above the limit requires a Residential Tenancy Branch application (RTB Form 52). On a sale, assignment of the site tenancy under MHPTA s.28 requires the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given), the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent, and **the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same**, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent increase. Park closure/conversion under MHPTA s.42 requires all permits in place first, then 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation. (Research file above; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), NOT to a pad tenancy.** Form B diligence applies in full to a strata purchase. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Approving Officer process of the municipality the parcel sits in; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (for transfers where PTT applies; a pad-home transfer's PTT position turns on the interest transferred — confirm with the closing lawyer/notary).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate, and a manufactured home on a rented pad is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a duplex or half duplex I can shop through this pad-fees page?** No. This page has no listing feed and names no property of any form. It is a guide to pad-rent costs; duplex stock in Langley is the ordinary form class (individual two-unit buildings on single lots), verified by name nowhere in this slug's files. (§§1–2.)
- **Q: How much is pad rent in Langley?** No official source publishes a Langley pad-rent figure, average or range, so no figure is stated in this file. A pad rent is set park by park in the written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement; the figure that matters is the specific park's current rent. (§§1, 5.)
- **Q: If I buy a home on a rented pad, do I own land?** No. You own the home as a chattel and rent the pad. That tenure line is the whole distinction this topic turns on — it is not land ownership, and it is not a half duplex. (§2.)
- **Q: Does the park raise my pad rent when I buy?** No. On assignment under MHPTA s.28 the pad rent stays the same for the new tenant and the scheduled increase date does not change; the landlord's prior written consent is required and no consent fee may be charged. (§3.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up each year?** Up to the yearly limit — 2.3% plus the proportional amount for 2026 (2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027), once every 12 months on 3 full months' notice; anything above the limit requires an RTB order. (§3.)
- **Q: What is the difference between a half duplex and a home on a pad?** A half duplex is one unit of a two-unit building sold separately — possible only where the pair was strata-divided (2-lot strata plan) or the parcel was subdivided fee-simple. A pad home is a manufactured home on rented land under the MHPTA; no part of the land transfers. (§2.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on a duplex purchase?** Run the tiers on your own price: $800,000 → $14,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000 (fully residential, no exemption). A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. Whether PTT applies to a specific park-home transfer turns on the interest transferred — confirm at closing. (§3.)
- **Q: What happens if the park is converted to another use?** Under MHPTA s.42 the landlord must first have all necessary permits, then give 12 months' notice and pay $20,000 compensation, with defined additional-compensation routes where the home cannot be moved. (§3.)
