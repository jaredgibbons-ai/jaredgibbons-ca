## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** The RD2 zone is the research's documented waterfront duplex-house zone for Beach Grove (4,000 m² subdivision minimum, carried as the bylaw states it; Delta's zoning bylaw NUMBER is contested in this program — "7600" vs "8400, 2025" — and is asserted nowhere here). The zone for a specific lot is read off the City's current zoning for that lot. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf

- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands):** SD3 provides that no building be located with the underside of a wooden floor system or the top of a concrete slab for habitable areas lower than 2.9 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum, that the minimum land surface elevation around construction be 2.3 metres GSC datum, and that no building be constructed within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. Development Permit Areas live in Appendix B of Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). Whether a specific Beach Grove waterfront lot falls inside SD3 is UNVERIFIED — it needs a DeltaMap check for that lot. (Research file §4, adaptation source basis.)
- **Foreshore tenure:** the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; tenure is by lease or licence only via FrontCounter BC, and private moorage is held under General Permission or Specific Permission. (Research file §4, adaptation source basis.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan); for Heirloom it applies ONLY if a project source confirms strata tenure, which none read does.** Form B diligence applies in full to a strata purchase. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are Heirloom's duplexes strata or fee-simple?** UNCONFIRMED — the community-profile source records "33 townhouses and duplexes" without a tenure split, and no public source read states it. Southlands pockets vary tenure by pocket, so the answer comes from the specific home's title / Form B, never from the Southlands name. (§2.)

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Tsawwassen East?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. None is verified by name here. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is Beachcomber Cottages a duplex complex?** No — it is a bare-land strata community in Southlands; its land form is documented once in the tsawwassen-east-GUIDE lots-and-land file. Bare-land strata is not duplex stock. (§2.)
- **Q: What does SSMUH change on the east side?** Delta's amendments state four units on all residential properties in the City (the six-unit tier is Scott Road in North Delta, not here) — added-unit potential on the lot, not a right to split it into half-duplex titles. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: What flood rules apply to a waterfront duplex in Beach Grove?** Hazard DP Area SD3 sets a 2.9 m GSC datum minimum floor level, a 2.3 m minimum land elevation around construction, and a 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of a tidal area — and whether a given lot is inside SD3 needs a DeltaMap check for that lot. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
