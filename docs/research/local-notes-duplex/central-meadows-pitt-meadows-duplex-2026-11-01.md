## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A home in a housing co-operative is none of these forms: the buyer holds co-op membership / shares, not title to the home or the land — which is why all three records below are excluded. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Pitt Meadows' zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011, and small-scale multi-unit housing under OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3034, 2025 covers secondary suites, garden suites, duplexes, triplexes, townhomes and small apartment buildings (2–6 units) where it applies. The exact position for a specific Central Meadows lot is UNCONFIRMED until its zone is read. (Research file above.) https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites — the City's own position.** The City's garden-suite rule, as the research documents it: a garden suite runs 33 to 90 sq m, one suite per lot, and a lot cannot have both a garden suite and a secondary suite. That is Pitt Meadows' own rule, carried exactly; no other municipality's cap is borrowed. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Research file above.)
- **Short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows** at the city layer — the provincial voiding of strata rental bylaws does not create a short-term rental right here. Long-term rental is different: strata rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. (Townhomes sibling above.)
- **Floodplain — ~95% governs.** Over 95% of the city's land base lies within the Fraser/Pitt floodplain (City 2020 Business Plan), protected by dikes. For a ground-oriented duplex purchase, the specific lot's floodplain and dike position is a first-order diligence item; any "over 85%" figure met in a sibling or a guide page is superseded here by the 95% Business Plan figure, with the conflict flagged. (Research file and siblings above.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan); Meadowland Village carries this block in its townhomes home.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone** (Meadowland Village is over the threshold on either published lot figure in its townhomes entry). Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term rentals separately PROHIBITED at the city layer, above). Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Pitt Meadows' servicing position is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — no servicing bylaw number is asserted in this file. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Central Meadows?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. None is verified by name here; a single-lot duplex sells whole. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is Meadow Highlands a duplex purchase?** No. It is a manufactured-home housing co-operative — co-op stock documented at home in this slug's mobile-homes file. You hold membership, not a duplex title. (§2.)
- **Q: What about the Advent Road row-homes — Meadowlands and Meadowland Village sound alike?** They are different tenures on the same pocket: Meadowlands (19032 Advent Road) is a 55+ housing CO-OPERATIVE of row-homes; Meadowland Village (18960 Advent Road) is a freehold STRATA townhouse complex counted in the Central townhomes file. Neither is duplex stock. (§2.)
- **Q: Is there a named duplex development in Central Meadows?** None verified. The duplex evidence here is the form class under Bylaw 3034, 2025 and Zoning Bylaw 2505. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I Airbnb a Central Meadows duplex?** No — short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows at the city layer. Long-term rental is different: strata rental-restriction bylaws are void since November 24, 2022. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a duplex here?** Address-based in SD42, which has no middle-school tier — the documented locator run for 12007 Harris Road returned Pitt Meadows Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary for that address only. Run the exact address. (§5.)
