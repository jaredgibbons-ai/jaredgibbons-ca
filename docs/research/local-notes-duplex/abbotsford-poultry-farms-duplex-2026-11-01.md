## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A farm residence on ALR land is none of these forms: it is a single principal residence (with, at most, a suite inside it and one additional small residence under the ALC frame in §3), and ALR residences cannot be stratified — so the strata-half-duplex form cannot arise on this stock, and a fee-simple split of a farm parcel runs into the ALR subdivision frame, not a duplex programme. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing; ALR non-stratification position as carried in this slug's siblings: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/abbotsford-poultry-farms-townhomes-2026-11-01.md.)
