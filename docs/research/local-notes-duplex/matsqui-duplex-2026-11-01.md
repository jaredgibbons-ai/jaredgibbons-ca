## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`.)

**HONEST ZERO at the top: no duplex, strata duplex, fee-simple half duplex or named duplex-form development is verified for Matsqui in this slug's research or in any of its siblings, and none is counted here.** No condo building or townhome complex was confirmed on an official source for this sub-area either, and SnapStats recorded 0 attached listings for Matsqui in September 2026. (Research file above; SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.)

- **The form class, stated as a form class only — NOT stock in this sub-area:** individual duplex buildings on single lots built under the zoning of their day are the ordinary duplex form class in BC generally. Matsqui's own zones do not document that form here: the A1 Agricultural Zone lists Agricultural Use and Single Detached Dwelling as principal uses, and the RR Rural Residential Zone is intended for single detached dwellings and agricultural uses on large lots in a rural setting. No Matsqui parcel is named in any source in this program as carrying a duplex. (Research file above; https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210; https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400310)
- **Second dwellings here are farm/suite forms, NOT duplexes:** in A1, one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot is permitted only in accordance with the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the Agricultural Land Commission, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, capped at 90 m² (lots of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (lots over 40 ha), and one secondary suite per lot to a maximum of 100 m² is permitted. In RR, a coach house is permitted only on a lot greater than 0.4 ha, and a coach house or secondary suite is limited to one per lot and a maximum of 90 m² or 40% of the net floor area of the single detached dwelling, whichever is less. A suite, coach house or farm dwelling unit inside or beside a single-detached house is not a duplex and is never counted as one. (Research file above; A1 and RR bylaw links above.)
- **ALR limit over the whole prairie position:** under Agricultural Land Commission housing rules, ALR land may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless the owner qualifies for an additional residence; the principal residence must be 500 m² or less in total floor area; a secondary suite is permitted in the ALR only within the principal residence; and an additional detached residence may be up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). Which individual Matsqui parcels are in the ALR is a parcel-by-parcel check: UNVERIFIED per parcel in this file. (Research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a half duplex in Matsqui?** Not on any source in this program. No strata duplex, fee-simple half duplex or duplex building is verified for the sub-area — the stock is single-detached farm and rural-residential homes. If a listing uses "half duplex" wording, treat it as listing text and verify the title/strata plan before treating either half as separately buyable. (§2.)
- **Q: Is a Matsqui home with a suite a duplex?** No. A secondary suite inside a principal residence (A1: to 100 m²; RR: 90 m² or 40%, whichever is less) is a suite, and a coach house or Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit is a second-dwelling farm form — none of them is a two-unit duplex building, and none can be bought separately from the lot. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Could SSMUH turn a Matsqui lot into a duplex or multiplex?** SSMUH is ground-oriented and Abbotsford adopted its amendments in June 2024 (bylaw number UNCONFIRMED), but this sub-area's ground is A1/RR with 8.0 ha / 4.0 ha subdivision minimums and, on ALR parcels, the ALC's one-residence frame — no source in this program documents SSMUH creating duplex stock on prairie land. Per-parcel eligibility is UNCONFIRMED. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: What protects the prairie from flooding, and what does it cost?** The City states the Matsqui Dykes are designed to prevent Fraser River water from entering Matsqui Prairie, and properties in the Matsqui Prairie Dyking District pay a separate dyking levy on top of general taxes — check the tax notice for the line item, and confirm a specific parcel's flood status with the City; design is not a guarantee for a parcel. (§3; research file.)
- **Q: Can I add a farm-worker or family dwelling?** In A1, one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot only through the ALR Use Regulation (B.C. Reg. 30/2019) route or ALC approval, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, capped at 90 m² / 186 m² by lot size. Read the A1 rules before valuing any second dwelling. (§2.)
- **Q: What should I check on wells and septic?** Wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database, and Fraser Health requires an authorized person's record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair of an onsite system and a letter of certification after. Servicing for any individual property is UNVERIFIED. (§5; research file.)
- **Q: If a strata duplex ever did exist here, what diligence applies?** Form B diligence in full; a 2-lot strata is below the 5+ lot depreciation-report threshold on lot count, the CRF minimum is 10% since Nov 1, 2023, rental bylaws are void since Nov 24, 2022, and only a 55+ age bylaw is valid. (§3.)
- **Q: When are Abbotsford taxes due and what is the penalty?** Due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a further 5% September 16, 2026, each including any unclaimed Home Owner Grant — plus the Matsqui Prairie Dyking District levy where the parcel is in the district. (§3.)
- **Q: Does the Abbotsford market context tell me duplex prices?** No. SnapStats has no duplex category; §1's figures are Abbotsford community context and a Matsqui sub-area detached/attached split in which attached recorded zero. There is no duplex price figure for this sub-area. (§1.)
