## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A townhome in this band is neither form: it is townhouse-form strata (or, where a count home verifies it, fee-simple townhouse) product, and a townhome listing under the ceiling does not make any duplex "in band." (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Framing for a band.** A band has no bylaws of its own: a duplex bought anywhere in Abbotsford — in this band or any other — takes the Abbotsford frame below, plus the ALR frame on ALR parcels, each checked parcel by parcel. (Brief chain Abbotsford frame; research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-townhomes-under-1-2m.md.)
- **Zoning and added units, city-wide (Abbotsford frame).** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs — there is no Bylaw 3100. A secondary suite must be **within the principal unit, registered with the City, and less than 50% of the size of the principal unit**; garden suites and coach houses may occupy **up to 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m²** — the 15% / 100 m² figures are Abbotsford ONLY. A coach house is a dwelling unit accessory to a single detached dwelling or duplex on the same lot; accessory units cannot be sold independently from the principal unit. Which zone permits a two-unit (duplex) form on a given parcel is a parcel-by-parcel read of Bylaw 2400-2014 — no zone-by-zone duplex permission table is asserted, because no source opened for this slug states one. No suite claim is made for any strata complex on this page — strata bylaws and the specific unit's permits govern in practice. (Research file above, §4; https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites)
- **SSMUH position (Abbotsford frame, number UNCONFIRMED).** Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial SSMUH framework. - **ALR frame (the rural edge of this band).** The ALC's housing guidance caps a principal residence on ALR land at a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill; permits a secondary suite in the ALR only when it is located within the principal residence; and limits an additional residence to up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. (Research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), wherever in the city it sits.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Taxes and registry (Abbotsford frame).** City of Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. Abbotsford HAS a Supreme Court registry — the ledger exception. Health care context in the research: Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre, 32900 Marshall Rd; Abbotsford Urgent and Primary Care Centre, 100-2692 Clearbrook Rd. (Research file above, §4; https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $700,000 → **$12,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 (this band's ceiling) → $2,000 + 2% × $1,000,000 = **$22,000** — a purchase inside this band meets only the 1% and 2% tiers; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031 — inside this band the rebate is available on price only at or under the $1.5M phase-out end, and only for new housing. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
