## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. In the Township, a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata, never a half duplex. A home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership at all — the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad (the tenure line, applied to the records below). (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing; settled Township frame.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning, SSMUH and suites (Township frame — SETTLED in the research):** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs. SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) allows **3 units on eligible lots of 280 m² or smaller and 4 units on larger eligible lots up to 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible for the multi-unit tiers, and NO 6-unit tier applies in the Township** — duplexes are the baseline SSMUH form. On lots larger than 4,050 m² — the frame most Salmon River acreages fall under — the Township states the rules allow one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit in addition to the primary unit depending on zoning. A secondary suite in the Township is capped at **120 m²** — the Township's own cap, never borrowed. A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102, located only within the second storey of a detached garage — never strata. Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 is CONFIRMED. (Research file, §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx)
- **ALR and flood — the governing facts for the valley floor:** farm land in the valley is in the Agricultural Land Reserve in places; confirm a specific parcel on the ALC maps. On ALR land the ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its size rules are met — up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha — subject to Township bylaws; the principal residence maximum is 500 m² and a secondary suite must be entirely inside the principal residence. The Salmon River valley is Fraser River floodplain land — the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station was originally built to help mitigate flooding of the surrounding agricultural lands during freshet, provides flood protection when Fraser River levels rise, and has flood box gates that close as river levels rise; Council has authorized staff to proceed with detailed design and tendering for the Pump Station Upgrades. Parcel-level floodplain mapping and watercourse setback distances for a specific parcel are UNVERIFIED — the Salmon River is salmon-bearing, so setbacks and riparian rules can limit building envelopes near the river; check with the Township before planning a build. (Research file, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps; https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx)
- **Wells and septic (acreage lots):** the Province's GWELLS application is the registry for recorded wells and aquifers; well reports are due within 90 days of constructing, altering or decommissioning a well. Fraser Health states authorized persons must file a record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair and a letter of certification after construction. (Research file, §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration; https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Township ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. This block does NOT apply to the tenure records in §2 — any pad tenancy there is non-strata under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Township's Approving Officer process under Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382, and the zone minimums for the parcel's zone decide whether a Salmon River lot can split at all — parcel zones come from the Bylaw 2500 Map Schedules through GeoSource (RU-2 minimums UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section, §2). Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file, §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
