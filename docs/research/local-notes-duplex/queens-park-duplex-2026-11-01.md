## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** New Westminster Zoning Bylaw 6680 (2001) governs; the OCP is Bylaw 7925 (2017). Houses sit in the RS districts (s.310) — RS-1 minimum site 557.40 m² (6,000 sq ft), FSR 0.5 — overlaid by the HCA. The RT districts are RT-2–RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B ONLY; the 836.10 m² figure is an RM-district threshold, never an RT figure. The zone of a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until read. (Research file above; verified ledger, brief chain; https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf)
- **Heritage Conservation Area — the defining overlay on this ground.** The Queen's Park HCA is currently the only HCA in New Westminster; it places heritage protection over ALL properties in the area regardless of construction age, classed Protected or Non-protected. Building Permit applications for new buildings, demolition, or changes affecting the front, sides or visible roofline of Protected properties, and subdivision applications, require a Heritage Alteration Permit (HAP) reviewed for design-guideline compliance; Protected properties also need an HAP for some exterior changes that require no Building Permit. Protected properties are also under the Heritage Property Maintenance Standards Bylaw; Protected-property incentives include additional density, special BC Building Code provisions, density transfer, relaxed laneway/carriage house guidelines and no heritage permit fees. **For a duplex buyer this means: a fee-simple split, an addition or a rebuild on this ground is an HCA file first and a zoning file second — confirm the property's Protected / Non-protected class with the City before pricing any of it; parcel-level class is UNVERIFIED here.** (Research file above; https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-protection-and-development/sb_expander_articles/582.php)
- **SSMUH position.** New Westminster's SSMUH position is carried in its Zoning Bylaw 6680 framework as amended; a duplex is the baseline two-unit form. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves — and on this ground any split also runs through the HCA / HAP layer above. (Brief chain.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the city's own rule.** The City's suite position is read in its Zoning Bylaw 6680 framework; no size figure is borrowed from another city. (Brief chain.)
- **Flood position.** **UNVERIFIED for this ground — no floodplain zone is stated and none is imported.** The governing overlay here is heritage, not floodplain. Brunette's Zone 4 belongs to Brunette only. (Research file above.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the municipality's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums — and on this ground a subdivision application also requires a Heritage Alteration Permit (§3 heritage line). Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file above.)
- **Servicing.** New Westminster's servicing Bylaw 7142, 2007 is VERIFIED in the verified ledger (its March 12, 2007 effective date is CORRECT history — a different date from Bill C-4's assent, never conflated). (Brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any named duplex development in Queens Park?** No — §2 is form class only. The ground's one verified attached complex, Clinton Place, is townhouse stock counted in the townhomes file, with no duplex element documented.
- **Q: Is "Queen's Park Estate" in Queens Park?** No — despite the name, it is Queensborough ground and is counted nowhere for this slug. (§2.)
- **Q: What does the Heritage Conservation Area mean for a duplex purchase?** Every property is classed Protected or Non-protected; new buildings, demolition, subdivision and specified exterior changes need a Heritage Alteration Permit, and Protected properties carry maintenance-standards obligations — and incentives. Confirm the class before pricing work. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me split a duplex into two titles here?** No — SSMUH adds unit potential on a lot; a separately titled half needs a strata plan or a subdivision, and a subdivision on this ground also needs a Heritage Alteration Permit. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a duplex here?** See §5 — Ministry-listed names only, catchments only on the documented single-address locator basis, else UNVERIFIED. (§5.)
