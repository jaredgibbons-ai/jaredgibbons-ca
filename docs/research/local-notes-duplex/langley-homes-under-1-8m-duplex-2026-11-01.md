## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted — two rulebooks, never blended.** Township ground: duplex / two-unit form under Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, with the provincial SSMUH implementation in SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024; NO 6-plex tier in the Township). City ground: duplex / two-unit form under City of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 (number only carried; its adoption date is conflicted in the program record and is not asserted), with SSMUH Bylaw 3284 applying City-only. The rulebook for a specific band property is read off which municipality the lot sits in — the band page itself does not settle it. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx, read 2026-11-01.
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — each Langley's own rule.** Township: a secondary suite is capped at **120 m²**. City: a secondary suite is capped at the **lesser of 100 m² and 40%** of the dwelling. Neither figure is borrowed from the other municipality, and neither is Surrey's or Abbotsford's rule. (Standing Langley frames, brief chain; research file above.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) seen through any feed.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the applicable municipality's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Township servicing position: Bylaw 2019 No. 5382, CONFIRMED in the verified ledger. No City of Langley servicing figure is asserted in this file. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. - **Q: How big can a suite inside a Langley duplex be?** Township ground: up to 120 m². City ground: the lesser of 100 m² and 40%. Confirm which municipality the lot is in first. (§3.)

- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
