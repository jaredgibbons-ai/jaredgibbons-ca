## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** City of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw No. 6700 governs, including the Ground Oriented (GO) Zone adopted December 8, 2025: a lot of 280 m² or less allows up to 3 units, a lot over 280 m² allows up to 4 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 designation and up to 6 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations; GO Zone subdivision minimum lot width is 7.5 m in Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 (10.0 m for a corner lot) and 15.0 m in other designations. The City adopted the GO Zone increasing permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 residential properties City-wide and allowing up to 6 units per lot on approximately 600 properties. The OCP governing is Bylaw 2014, No. 8400, amended December 8, 2025 (Amendment Bylaw 2025, No. 9114). Older bylaw minimum lot areas include 464.5 m² in RS-2 and 334.4 m² in RS-3; the zone and designation on a single lot are confirmed through the City. (Research file above; https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf)
- **SSMUH position.** The GO Zone IS the City's SSMUH framework for this ground — the unit counts above are its form. A duplex is the baseline two-unit form inside it; SSMUH adds unit potential and does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. Multi-unit lots' stratification position for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until the City confirms it. (Research file above.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the City's own rule.** The City legalized secondary suites in all single-family dwellings in 1993; one suite is permitted per single-family property, the owner must reside at the property, and a suite may be combined with an accessory coach house, which is permitted where the front lot line is at least 10 m and requires a Development Permit and a Building Permit. No suite percentage is borrowed from another city. (Research file above; https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Permits-Inspections/building-permits/secondary-suites)
- **Streamside.** The City's Streamside Protection Development Permit Area covers land within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse or within 10 metres of the top of a ravine bank, and a development permit is required before new development there; the City's stormwater plan notes a City creek channel from the Calverhall – Cloverly neighbourhoods. Check creek-side duplex lots against it. (Research file above.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the municipality's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums (the GO Zone widths in §3 are the City figures a split is read against); no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** The City of North Vancouver's servicing Bylaw 8014 is carried City-files-only in the verified ledger — it belongs to City ground like this file and is NEVER carried in District files. (Brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Calverhall in the City or the District?** The City of North Vancouver — City zoning (Bylaw No. 6700), City OCP (No. 8400) and City tax rates apply. A District-based estimate misprices the file. (§1.)
- **Q: Can I buy half of a Calverhall duplex?** Only if the pair was legally split — a 2-lot strata plan or a subdivided fee-simple pair (the GO Zone's 7.5 m / 15.0 m subdivision widths are the figures a split is read against). An undivided duplex sells whole, on one title. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: How many units can a Calverhall house lot hold?** Under the GO Zone (December 8, 2025): up to 3 units on a lot of 280 m² or less, up to 4 on a larger lot in Low Rise Neighbourhood 1, and up to 6 in Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 — the lot's OCP designation decides. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a Calverhall house have both a suite and a coach house?** The City's rule allows one suite per single-family property with the owner residing at the property, and a suite may combine with an accessory coach house where the front lot line is at least 10 m, with a Development Permit and Building Permit. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for Calverhall?** No — duplexes have no separate published category in this program's sources, and North Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage, so no market figure is stated at all. (§1.)
