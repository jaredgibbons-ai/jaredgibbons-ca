## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A bare-land strata lot is a strata lot with a single-family home/cottage form — it is NOT a half duplex merely because the community is strata. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex, where one legally exists, is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer; a bare-land strata lot purchase is taxed on its own price the same way. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale cottage or home is not new housing for this rebate. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex at the south end?** There is no verified duplex at the south end to buy a side of. What sells here is a whole fee-simple home/cottage, or a whole bare-land strata lot with its cottage in one of the three named communities. (§2.)
- **Q: Is the south end leasehold like the north end?** No — do not import the Park lease assumption. The south end is NOT Park Board leasehold: tenure here is freehold or bare land strata under FVRD bylaws, so title and strata documents, not a Park lease, drive the deal. (§1; §2.)
- **Q: Are the Cottages at Cultus Lake half duplexes?** No. The Cottages at Cultus Lake, Creekside Mills and Aquadel Crossing are bare-land strata single-family/cottage communities — each home sits on its own strata lot as a whole unit. They are filed at their condos/lots homes and cross-referenced here with no duplex count. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Cultus Lake South part of Chilliwack?** No. It is FVRD Electoral Area H, south of the City of Chilliwack and outside the Cultus Lake Park Board boundary. Chilliwack bylaws and tax dates do not apply. (§1; §3.)
- **Q: What diligence applies in a bare-land strata cottage community?** Form B diligence: bylaws, rules, fees, special levies, depreciation/CRF position, and what the strata owns versus what sits on your lot. A 2-lot depreciation threshold does not apply to these multi-lot communities — the 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 does. (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Can I run a south-end cottage as a vacation rental?** Only through three gates: strata short-term rental bylaws are still allowed even though long-term rental bans are void; the FVRD has enabled Area H to regulate short-term vacation rentals and its Zoning Bylaw No. 1638 excludes Temporary Tourist Accommodation from Residential use unless the zone expressly permits it; and a provincial STR registration number has been required since May 1, 2025. (§3.)
- **Q: Sewer or septic?** Confirm for the exact lot: the FVRD sewer service covers a mapped service area, and lots outside it rely on private septic systems regulated by Fraser Health. (§3.)
- **Q: Who do I pay tax to, and what are the penalties?** The Province / FVRD rural regime — rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31 (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11), plus service-area levies where the lot participates. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a bare-land strata cottage?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $1,200,000 fully residential with no exemption = $22,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (§3.)
- **Q: Which school serves a south-end address?** No sample address was run for this sub-area, so the address-level catchment is UNVERIFIED — see §5 for the area-level feeder pattern, which is not a catchment promise. (§5.)
