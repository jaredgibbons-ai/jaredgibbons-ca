## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

That is the whole roster — none.** No named strata duplex development, no named fee-simple half-duplex subdivision, and no individual duplex building is verified by name in the batch-8 research file or in any sibling in any completed set. - **Why the zero is structural, not a gap in searching:** Bradner lands are zoned A1 and A2 Agricultural under the City of Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw, and part of the area is in the ALR. On ALR land, residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (Strata Property Act s.239), and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1); ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6). A strata duplex is therefore not an available form on Bradner's ALR parcels, and no fee-simple half-duplex subdivision is documented anywhere on the ground. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/bylaws/zoning-bylaw and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **The farm-residence form class (the only two-unit form this ground supports):** farm and rural-residential parcels and detached houses on agricultural land, effectively all fee-simple freehold under one title. On ALR land the ALC housing rules permit one principal residence per parcel (maximum 500 m² gross floor area), a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence, and one additional detached residence for farm use or another permitted use — capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. A principal home plus a lawful additional residence is a two-residence FARM PARCEL, sold whole — it is not a duplex and neither residence is a separately titled half. (Research file above, §§4–5; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **Second residences / manufactured homes on farm ground — ONLY as the ALR sources support:** a manufactured home can figure on farm ground only as the parcel's principal or additional residence within those same ALC caps and conditions (the ALR Use Regulation / ALC Policy L-25 additional-residence framework, with the Non-Adhering Residential Use application route where the as-of-right conditions are not met). **A general farm-worker housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED** — temporary farm worker housing is permitted under conditions on ALR land per the ALC housing page, but no source in this slug's research grants a stand-alone entitlement, and none is asserted (the poultry-farms treatment in this program is the model for this restraint). No manufactured-home park inside Bradner was verified in any sibling. (Research file above, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **What a "duplex" word on a Bradner listing would be:** a RECORD ONLY — marketing wording for a farm residence with two units or a principal home plus a second residence, sold as one parcel under one title. It is never verification of a separable half, and no such mention is counted in this file.

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs; Bradner lands are A1/A2 Agricultural — not a duplex zone form on this ground. - **SSMUH position.** Abbotsford's SSMUH implementation makes the duplex the baseline multi-unit form on eligible urban lots; **the SSMUH bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED — "3560-2024" is NEVER asserted in this file.** SSMUH does not create Bradner duplexes and does not override the A1/A2 zoning or the ALR no-stratification rule, which are the controlling framework here. (Brief chain; research file above.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the city's own rule, and the ALR override.** Abbotsford's general rule: one secondary suite in single detached dwellings (s.140.3.8), sized at **under 50%** of the principal unit under the 2025 updates, with a coach/garden suite capped at **15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m² (Abbotsford ONLY)**. On ALR land the ALC rule governs instead: a secondary suite is permitted ONLY inside the principal residence — never in a barn or a detached building. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **STRATA block — stated for completeness; no strata duplex exists on this ground.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists elsewhere: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. On ALR parcels the strata form is barred outright without ALC permission (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, and on ALR land an ALC subdivision position as well (ALCA s.21(1)); no such split is documented on this ground. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Abbotsford's Development (servicing) bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — none is quoted. Parcel-level water/sewer versus well/septic for Bradner parcels: UNCONFIRMED in this slug's research — a parcel-level check, never assumed. (Brief chain; research file above.)
- **Tax timing:** City of Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026 by midnight, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026, each including any unclaimed Home Owner Grant. (Research file above, §4; https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12; see the sibling flag in §5) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any half duplex for sale in Bradner?** None verified, in any source in this program — and the strata form is closed on ALR parcels by rule, because ALR residences cannot be stratified without ALC permission. A Bradner purchase is a whole parcel under one title. (§2.)
- **Q: A listing calls a Bradner farmhouse a duplex — can I buy one side?** No. That wording is a record of marketing language for a two-unit or two-residence farm property; without a strata plan or a subdivided lot there is no separate half to buy. (§2.)
- **Q: What housing CAN be added on a Bradner farm parcel?** One principal residence up to 500 m², a secondary suite inside that residence only, and one additional small residence for farm use or another permitted use — 90 m² maximum on parcels of 40 ha or less, 186 m² on larger parcels. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a suite go in a barn or a detached building?** No — a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; a detached second dwelling is the size-capped "additional residence" tied to farm use or another permitted use. Abbotsford's general suite cap (under 50%) and coach/garden cap (15% / 100 m²) do not override the ALR rule on ALR land. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a Bradner farm parcel as a second home?** Only within the ALR additional-residence framework (caps and conditions in §2, with the Non-Adhering Residential Use route where the as-of-right conditions are not met). A general farm-worker housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED and is not asserted. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex (off this ground)?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for Bradner?** No. SnapStats has no duplex category, Bradner recorded 0 solds in September 2026, and §1's figures are labelled Abbotsford community context only. (§1.)
