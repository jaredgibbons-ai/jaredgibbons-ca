## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 No. 14815 (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) governs — the former bylaw number 4742 is NEVER carried as current. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024: permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and a minimum lot area of 281 m². The zone of a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until read. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite (research file above)
- **SSMUH position.** In the R1 SSMUH District: 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. A duplex is the baseline two-unit form. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split an existing duplex into separately titled halves. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite (research file above)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the city's own rule.** Secondary suites are a permitted form inside the R1 district as the bylaw states; no size figure is borrowed from another city. (Research file above.)
- **Plan position.** The governing plan is the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (adopted December 9, 2025), which replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; the village's own 1972 Community Plan is the historical plan record for the pocket (§1). https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan (research file above)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. The same strata law governs each Greentree phase corporation on its own Form B — a phase's report set is never borrowed from a neighbouring phase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the municipality's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums in §3; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Burnaby's servicing-bylaw base number is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger (a 14667, 2024 number asserted by four siblings program-wide is flagged wherever met and never asserted here). (Brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where is Greentree Village, actually?** In the V5G pocket south of Canada Way behind BCIT, at the west edge of Deer Lake — bounded by Wayburne Drive, Woodsworth Street, Forest Lawn Cemetery and Moscrop Street under the City's 1972 Greentree Village Community Plan. It is NOT at Marlborough/Sussex west of Metrotown; that placement in the research file is wrong. (§1.)
- **Q: Are the Greentree phases duplexes?** No — the 4 counted phases are strata townhouse corporations (1974–1975 stock), counted once in the townhomes file. No duplex element is documented in any phase, and no duplex count is taken here. (§2.)
- **Q: Is a Greentree phase a bare-land strata?** No — the phases are building strata: each phase is its own strata corporation with its own Form B, CRF and depreciation report. No bare-land strata plan is verified in the village. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me split a duplex into two titles?** No — the R1 district adds unit potential on a lot (3–4 units, 6 near frequent transit; multi-unit lots may be stratified going forward); it does not retitle an existing duplex by itself. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a duplex here?** See §5 — and do NOT use École Marlborough or Burnaby South for this pocket; those names belong to the research file's wrong placement. (§5.)
