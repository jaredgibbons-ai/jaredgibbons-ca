## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A manufactured home on a rented pad is none of these forms: the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the site. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing; pad tenure per the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act frame in the research file above.)

- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), and to a bare-land strata lot such as Baker Trail Village.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ cycle does not capture it on lot count alone** (Baker Trail Village, at 157 lots, is above the threshold); a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. Inside a pad-rent park the reverse regime governs: park rules under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, no Form B, no strata fees for the pad tenancy itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail and no servicing bylaw number is stated, the number being UNCONFIRMED above. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Taxes and registry (Chilliwack frame).** City of Chilliwack property tax notices are mailed annually at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% penalty applies after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 4, 2026. - **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (for transfers where PTT applies).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. - **Q: Is there a duplex or half-duplex roster for Chilliwack mobile homes?** No — this is a topic file and it carries no duplex roster. No named strata duplex and no named fee-simple half-duplex subdivision is verified on this topic in the research or any sibling. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Baker Trail Village a duplex or half duplex?** No. It is a 157-lot bare-land strata of manufactured homes at 46511 Chilliwack Lake Road — you own the home AND a strata lot, pay strata fees instead of pad rent, and diligence runs on the Form B. Its park home is this topic's mobile file and its land is counted once in this slug's lots-and-land file; it is cross-referenced here with no count taken, and it is not duplex stock. (§2.)
- **Q: If I buy a home in a Chilliwack park, do I own land?** On a rented pad, no — you own the manufactured home and hold a manufactured home site tenancy under the MHPTA, paying monthly site rent to the park. Land ownership exists only in the own-land forms: fee-simple lots, or bare-land strata lots such as Baker Trail Village. (§2.)
- **Q: What is the difference between buying a duplex and buying a half duplex in Chilliwack?** A duplex purchase is the whole two-unit building and lot on one title. A half duplex is one unit sold separately, which is only possible where the pair was strata-divided (a 2-lot strata plan — Form B diligence) or the parcel was subdivided fee-simple (party-wall title). If neither happened, there is no "half" to buy. (§2.)
- **Q: Does the park have to approve me as a buyer?** For a pad tenancy, assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent under MHPTA s.28 (RTB-10), consent may be withheld only in prescribed circumstances, and no fee may be charged for consent under s.28(3). At Baker Trail Village (strata) there is no park-consent step at all. (§§2–3; research file §4.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on 3 full months' notice, capped at the yearly limit for manufactured home site tenancies: 2026 is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; 2027 is 2.2% plus the proportional amount; above-limit increases require an RTB order. No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact in this file — the live page's $550–$950 range is UNVERIFIED (see §5). (§3; research file §4.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget?** On an own-land transfer, run the tiers on your own price: $800,000 → $14,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000 (fully residential, no exemption). On a home-only pad purchase the PTT position is UNVERIFIED in this program — confirm it with a lawyer or notary for the exact structure. (§3.)
