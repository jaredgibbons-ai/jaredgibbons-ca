## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. An apartment condominium in this band is neither form — it is one strata lot inside a multi-lot apartment strata plan. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (the comparative ground).** Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs, under OCP Bylaw 5500 "Chilliwack 2050." The zone of a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until read. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **SSMUH position.** Chilliwack's SSMUH amendment is Bylaw 5395, amending Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, adopted June 18, 2024 — established from the amendment record in Section 14 of the consolidated Zoning Bylaw 5000. Duplexes are the baseline two-unit form in that framework. (Research file above, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Chilliwack's own rule, never borrowed.** Under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000: a secondary suite is an accessory dwelling unit of **not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building — the lesser of 90 m² and 40%** (s.2); only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot (s.4.06(1)); garden suites are capped at 100 m² gross floor area; ADU parking sits at s.5.04 (1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite; 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite). This is the rule a duplex buyer in this price conversation is actually buying under — a condo in this band carries no suite right at all. (Research file above, §4.)
- **STRATA block — stated for both the band's condos and any strata duplex.** Form B diligence applies in full to any strata purchase. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone** (an apartment building in this band, with 5+ lots, sits inside the cycle — the contrast is the diligence point). Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. Strata vs freehold split, building ages and fee levels for the Chilliwack condo stock are UNVERIFIED — no official source publishes a Chilliwack-wide condo profile, and the page copy's statements about what fees "usually cover" are site copy, not verified fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support, with the 3055 conflict carried OPEN.** A fee-simple half-duplex purchase is only possible where the parcel was subdivided through the City's Approving Officer process. **Chilliwack's servicing (Land Development) bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger: the ledger holds "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" as NOT adopted, while the City's own subdivisions webpage asserts that bylaw name/number and the chilliwack-proper-west + chilliwack-farms LOTS siblings assert it as governing. - **Tax timing:** Chilliwack property tax notices are mailed at the end of May, taxes due the first working day after July 1, with a 5% penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026 — a summer completion in this band straddles those dates. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math, plus this band's exemption position.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. **Band-specific:** the First Time Home Buyers' Program requires a fair market value of **$835,000 or less for the full exemption** (effective April 1, 2024; property 0.5 ha or smaller, principal residence only, residential improvements only; exemption covers the PTT on the first $500,000 of price), with a partial exemption above $835,000 and below $860,000 — **every price in this under-$800K band sits under the full-exemption value ceiling, but the buyer and property conditions still decide per purchase**. The Newly Built Home Exemption's full-exemption threshold is $1,100,000 (effective April 1, 2024), phasing out at $1,150,000. Verify the exempt figure against the buyer's actual qualification before quoting it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale condo, duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there an official median price for Chilliwack condos under $800,000?** No. Chilliwack is not in the SnapStats extraction, SnapStats publishes no price-band or condo-only cut in any case, and CADREB publishes no area statistics in the sources used — so no band count, share or median exists, and none is stated here. - **Q: Does the $800,000 cap matter for the first-time buyers' PTT exemption?** Yes — in the buyer's favour. The full exemption runs to a fair market value of $835,000 (tax exempt on the first $500,000 of price), partial only to $860,000, with all other program conditions applying — so a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in this band sits under the value ceiling. (§3.)
- **Q: Condo under $800K or a half duplex — what is legally different?** Tenure and diligence. A band condo is one strata lot in a multi-lot apartment plan (5+ lots: inside the depreciation-report cycle). A strata half duplex is one of only 2 lots (below that cycle's threshold on lot count alone) with Form B diligence on a much smaller corporation; a fee-simple half sits on its own subdivided parcel with party-wall documents instead of a strata plan. The suite rules in §3 belong to the duplex side only. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Are 55+ age-restricted condos still legal in Chilliwack?** Yes. Since November 24, 2022, 55+ is the only age restriction a BC strata bylaw may impose; rental-restriction bylaws are not permitted at all; exemptions cover caregivers, people already residing when the bylaw passed, children and younger spouses/partners. (§2.)
- **Q: The page says $800K "covers nearly the entire market" — can I rely on that?** No — that claim and the page's editorial ranges are THIS slug's documented defect (see §5): the ranges start at $280,000 while the page's own feed shows $145,000 and $236,900 actives below that floor, and no official source publishes a Chilliwack condo price distribution to support the "nearly the entire market" claim. Both are UNVERIFIED site copy. (§5.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Chilliwack condo?** SD33, address-based — the Ministry-confirmed names are listed in §5, but no locator result is on record for any Chilliwack condo address, so check the building's exact address before relying on a school. (§5.)
- **Q: How does a condo buyer get out of Chilliwack without a car?** BC Transit's Route 66 Fraser Valley Express serves Chilliwack, Abbotsford, Langley and Burnaby with daily service, operated by Chilliwack Transit; BC Transit's January 4, 2026 service changes added later and Sunday-evening service on local routes including Route 51, Route 53 Fairfield and Route 57 Broadway, and expanded weekend trips on Route 66. (§5.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex versus a band condo?** Scale. On a 2-lot strata there may be no depreciation report under the 5+ cycle, thin financials and a self-managed corporation — read everything that exists and price what does not. On a band condo, insist on the full Form B package for a 5+-lot plan, including the depreciation report. (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex at this level?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $8,000; $800,000 = $14,000 (worked in §3), before any first-time-buyer exemption the buyer actually qualifies for. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
