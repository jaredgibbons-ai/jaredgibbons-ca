## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. Surrey's form class also includes the R6 semi-detached / zero-lot-line form carried in the program's Surrey frame. A home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership at all — the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad (the tenure line, applied to Newton MHP below). (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plans (Surrey frame):** the current city-wide plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is never carried as current. The City's West Newton plan covers the lands west of King George Boulevard including the Highway 99 industrial area; within the West Newton NCP set, West Newton South was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update and is no longer in effect — do not read the old plans as current law. The Newton Town Centre Plan covers the King George Blvd / 72 Ave core. (Research file, §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps)
- **Zoning, SSMUH and suites (Surrey frame):** Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs (residential zones R1–R6 in the neighbourhood portion; industrial zones in the Highway 99 industrial area; streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is ever stated). SSMUH (Jul 8, 2024 frame): a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; one secondary suite per house under the BC Building Code; properties not connected to community water and sewer are among the exemptions. Duplexes are the baseline SSMUH form. The Surrey suite rule is its own: a secondary suite is capped at **40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area** — never borrowed for another city. The SSMUH changes do not retroactively legalize suites built without permits — verify a suite's permit status before marketing its income. Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) is VERIFIED. Short-term rentals in Surrey are owner-only, with a licence carried at $358/yr and the City's 2024 $350/yr proposal figure disclosed alongside it wherever the fee is stated. Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry. (Research file, §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing; settled Surrey frame, D21 brief.)
- **Flood and ALR:** the south end of West Newton falls toward the Serpentine & Nicomekl lowlands — the dyke-protected floodplain south of Newton — while the residential upland portion is outside that lowland floodplain; ALR land lies south and west toward the lowlands, and a specific property's ALR and flood status is checked parcel by parcel (ALC maps; City flood mapping) before subjects come off. Homes near the Highway 99 industrial edge also need a neighbouring-uses and truck-route check. (Research file, §§4, 6; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Surrey ground; it does NOT apply to Newton MHP, which is non-strata pad rental under the MHPTA.** Form B diligence applies in full to a strata purchase. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process under Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986), and the R-zone minimums in §2 decide whether a West Newton lot can split at all. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file, §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. PTT treatment of a resale manufactured home on a rented pad is UNCONFIRMED on this ground's sources — no flat claim is made here. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales — relevant here to developer sales such as the Harth presale, not to resale houses or park homes. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
