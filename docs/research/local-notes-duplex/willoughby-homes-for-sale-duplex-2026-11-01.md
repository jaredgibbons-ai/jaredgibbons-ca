## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC only as **(a) a STRATA duplex** (2-lot strata plan; each half a strata lot; Form B diligence) or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex** (parcel subdivided; party-wall / fee-simple row form where a source documents it). In the Township, a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata. (Program definition, `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; research file §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning (Township frame).** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs. **R-CL (Residential Compact Lot Zone), s.403: minimum lot area 220 m², maximum lot area 464.5 m² (s.403.7), minimum frontage 11 m front-loaded / 8.2 m rear-loaded. There is NO 355 m² figure in s.403 and none is stated anywhere in this file.** (Research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf, read 2026-11-01.)
- **SSMUH position.** Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024) amended Bylaw No. 2500 to allow SSMUH in zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings, subject to eligibility (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water/sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area, lot ≤4,050 m²): **3 units on lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger eligible lots, and NO 6-plex tier in the Township.** A duplex is the baseline two-unit form; SSMUH does not by itself create separately titled halves. (Research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Secondary suites + coach houses — the Township's own rules.** Bylaw No. 2500 s.102 caps a secondary suite at a maximum gross floor area of **120 m²**; each principal dwelling unit may have up to one secondary suite; a building permit is required for all suites. A **coach house** is defined in s.102 as an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage on a lot — a fee-simple second unit, never strata. A coach-house size cap and an owner-occupancy rule: UNVERIFIED in the Section 100 passages opened. (Research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-100-Administration.pdf, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Plans.** The Willoughby Community Plan was Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025). The Plan Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 (Willoughby Community Plan replacement; Transit Corridor Plan; Gordon Neighbourhood Plan) stood at **first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY** in the settled ledger — never stated as adopted in this file; the research's "replaced/created" wording is carried as the research's description of the update package, with the ledger's reading-stage position governing status. **Brief-vs-disk note, stated plainly:** where the research text reads as if 6201–6203 are in force, this file follows the D14 settled ledger (readings only) and says so.
