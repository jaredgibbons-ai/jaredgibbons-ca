## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. In the Township, a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata, never a half duplex. A home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership at all — the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad (the tenure line, applied to the park below). (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing; settled Township frame.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning, SSMUH and suites (Township frame — SETTLED in the research):** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs. SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) allows **3 units on eligible lots of 280 m² or smaller and 4 units on larger eligible lots up to 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible, and NO 6-unit tier applies in the Township** — duplexes are the baseline SSMUH form. Eligibility also requires the property to be within the Urban Containment Boundary and connected to both municipal water and sewer; properties that do not meet those criteria — the frame most rural Otter acreages will fall under — are instead eligible for one residential unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit, depending on zoning. A secondary suite in the Township is capped at **120 m²** — the Township's own cap, never borrowed. A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102, located only within the second storey of a detached garage — never strata. **s.102 suite-figure tension, flagged UNRESOLVED where met:** the otter-district townhomes siblings record that s.102 as read in this program permits townhouse suites at 120 m² while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted — the tension is flagged here exactly as the D14 plain file flagged it, resolved nowhere, and no figure beyond the settled 120 m² Township suite cap is asserted from it. No coach-house size cap appears in s.100 as read in the program's rules-gaps pass, so no cap is stated. Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 is CONFIRMED. (Research file, §4; guide townhomes sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/otter-district-guide-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §3; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **ALR frame — the governing fact for much of this district:** ALR land may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless it qualifies for an additional residence, and local governments may further regulate or restrict residences through zoning. On ALR land the principal residence maximum is a total floor area of 500 m²; a secondary suite is permitted only when it is entirely contained within the principal residence (a separate or detached dwelling is not a secondary suite) and local bylaws permit it; and one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application at up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, subject to local bylaws. Parcel-level ALR status for any specific Otter District property: UNVERIFIED — confirmed on the ALC's ALR maps, not from the sub-area name. A property already carrying two dwellings needs its approvals history checked, not assumed. (Research file, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps)
- **Wells and septic (acreage lots):** the Province's GWELLS application is the registry for recorded wells and aquifers; older wells may not appear in it. Fraser Health states authorized persons must file a record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair and a letter of certification after construction — on an acreage duplex or suite build, search GWELLS for the well record and ask for both Fraser Health documents. (Research file, §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration; https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Township ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. This block does NOT apply to the pad-rental park in §2 — it is non-strata leasehold under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Township's Approving Officer process under Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382, and the zone minimums for the parcel's zone decide whether an Otter lot can split at all — parcel zones come from the Bylaw 2500 Map Schedules through GeoSource. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file, §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. PTT treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad is UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule in this program — a lawyer or notary must confirm handling for the exact structure. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
