## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan and zoning frame.** Land use is under City of Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000. The settled program frame carries the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 (Schedule B-2 for Chilliwack Proper ground); stated precisely, this slug's own research attributes the area's policies to a "Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan" on the live page but could not confirm that plan name and its application to this area on a City page in its pass — UNVERIFIED at that research strength, and the frame is carried here on the program ledger, not on this slug's research alone. - **SSMUH position.** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 is the City's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment to Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, adopted June 18, 2024 — allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. Duplexes are the baseline form inside that frame; added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide and does not by itself create a separately saleable half. (Research file above, §4; bylaw PDF above; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3090/Signed%20Staff%20Report%20%2D%20RZ001761%2Epdf, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Chilliwack's own rule.** Under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, s.2, a secondary suite is an accessory dwelling unit with a gross floor area of not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building, located within a building containing only 1 principal dwelling unit — the cap is the lesser of 90 m² and 40%. Section 4.06(1) permits only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits an ADU (secondary suite, coach house or garden suite); garden suites are capped at a maximum gross floor area of 100 m², and off-street parking for accessory units is addressed in s.5.04. The City's Secondary Suites Policy permits the rental of secondary suites within any single detached dwelling in the City regardless of zone; a suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level under the Floodplain Regulation Bylaw. Short-term rentals are regulated operations under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141. (Research file above, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724, read 2026-11-01; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf, read 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full (the strata must provide the Form B within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer / Preliminary Layout Approval process against the parcel's actual zone; the Chilliwack servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in this program (see §5), so no servicing-bylaw number is stated here. Flood hazard zoning exists in the bylaw structure (RS-FHA reserve zone, s.12 of Bylaw No. 5000); parcel-level Flood Construction Levels for Proper South lots are UNVERIFIED. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Research file above, §4; program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Property taxes (city regime).** City of Chilliwack tax notices are mailed annually at the end of May and are due the first working day after July 1 of every calendar year; for 2026, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. (Research file above, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522, read 2026-11-01.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: What is the difference between buying a duplex and buying a half duplex here?** A duplex sale is the whole two-unit building on one lot under one title. A half duplex is one unit sold separately, which in BC requires either a 2-lot strata plan (each half a strata lot, Form B diligence) or a subdivided fee-simple parcel (each half on its own lot, party-wall agreement on title). If neither exists, the half cannot be sold separately. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a house here have a suite AND a coach house?** No. Only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits — a secondary suite (capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space), a coach house, or a garden suite (capped at 100 m²) — under Bylaw 5000. Separately, SSMUH (Bylaw 5395) allows 3–4 units on eligible single-detached/duplex parcels inside the urban growth boundary. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
