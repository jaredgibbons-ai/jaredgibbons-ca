## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **SSMUH position (Langley City frame).** Bylaw No. 3284 (City-only), adopted June 17, 2024, amended the Zoning Bylaw to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on RS1 and RS2 lots: RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. The duplex is the baseline two-unit form inside this framework; SSMUH adds unit potential on a lot and does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. The Township's Bylaw 6020 does NOT govern this slug. (Research file above; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Secondary suites (Langley City's own rule — never borrowed).** Under Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300, a secondary suite must not exceed **the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the gross floor area of the building**; the registered owner must reside on the lot; at least one extra off-street parking space is required; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. A listing's "suite" claim is a claim to verify against that rule. (Research file above; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, read 2026-11-01.)

- **Servicing / processes** are carried per this slug's own research only; no servicing bylaw number is asserted for the City in this file. (Brief chain, Langley City frame.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Approving Officer process; SSMUH unit permission alone never effects that split. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Langley City the same market as the Township of Langley?** No — the City is its own municipality with its own OCP (adopted November 22, 2021) and its own Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300. A Township fact applied to a City property is an error; this file imports no Township bylaw. (§1, §3.)

- **Q: How many apartment-condo buildings does Langley City actually have?** Exactly 3 verified by name, where the city siblings state it: Parkway Estates (5360 205 St, 1988, 54 units), Parkway II (5363 206 St, 1989, 42 units) and Glenmont Park (5375 205 St, 1990, 43 units in strata) — all 55+ communities, counted once in the condos sibling. - **Q: Is 5365 205 Street the same complex as Glenmont Park?** No. Glenmont Park is 5375 205 St, verified. Morningside Estates at 5365 205 St is held UNCONFIRMED beside it — never merged, never counted. (§2.)
- **Q: How many units can a Langley City single-family lot hold?** Under Bylaw No. 3284 (City-only, adopted June 17, 2024), RS1 and RS2 lots permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. That is unit permission, not a subdivision into separately titled halves. (§3.)
- **Q: What are the City's secondary suite rules?** The lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, owner-occupancy, one extra off-street parking space, and permit/inspection/registry recording under Bylaw No. 3300. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no, including the directory "rentals not allowed" lines carried for the Parkway buildings. Short-term rentals are a separate question, and the City's own STR rule is UNVERIFIED. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on a Langley City duplex purchase?** Run the tiers on your own price: $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier (fully residential, no exemption). A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I quote the $1,345,000 Langley detached median for a City home?** No. That is the combined City + Township community median, labelled as such in §1; no City-only median is published, so price a City home off City comparables. The City's own September 2026 tempo is its sub-area row: 13% detached, 17% attached. (§1.)
