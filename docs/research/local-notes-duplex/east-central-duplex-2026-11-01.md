## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Community plan / OCP CONTESTED — flagged, resolved nowhere.** The program position for Maple Ridge is CONTESTED (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024); no OCP number is asserted as current in this file. The downtown part of this ground is covered by the Town Centre Area Plan (OCP s. 10.4), whose update expanded its boundaries to incorporate River Bend and added a Town Centre Core designation; Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024 covers the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station TOAs inside the plan area. (Research file above; https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town)
- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Duplexes are a permitted two-unit form under Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019 in the residential zones that allow them; the standard house zone documented for this ground is RS-1, s. 605 (figures in §2). The zone of a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until read off the City's current zoning for that lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/business-development/zoning-bylaws (research file above)
- **SSMUH position.** Eligible single-family and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws, subject to the City's conditions — the tier for a specific lot is read in the City's current framework, not assumed. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Research file above; https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Maple Ridge's own rule.** One secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of the gross floor area, not within the Floodplain Area, building permit required — never borrowed from another city. Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties; the south edge's flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic. (Research file above; https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites; https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), and to Gold Creek Estates as strata townhouse diligence.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Maple Ridge's Subdivision Servicing Bylaw No. 4800-1993, from a City source (verified ledger, brief chain).
- **Property taxes (Maple Ridge frame).** Maple Ridge property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. (Research file above; https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in East Central?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. None is verified by name here; a single-lot duplex sells whole. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is Gold Creek Estates a half-duplex complex I can count as duplex stock?** No. It is a 10-unit duplex-style STRATA townhouse complex at 12191 228 Street, counted once in the townhomes home and cross-referenced here with no count. A unit there is a strata townhouse purchase with Form B diligence. (§2.)
- **Q: Are the Fraserview Village buildings duplexes?** No. Rosewood Court, Emerald Manor, The Willows and Castlemount (with Riverside named in the siblings' cluster line) are apartment-form 55+ strata documented in the East Central condos files — condo stock, never duplex stock. (§2.)
- **Q: Is this Abbotsford ground?** No — East Central is a Maple Ridge sub-area. The research file governs over any brief line that says otherwise. (FILE STATUS.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a duplex here?** Maple Ridge's own rule governs — maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the Floodplain Area, building permit required; suites are prohibited on floodplain properties. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
