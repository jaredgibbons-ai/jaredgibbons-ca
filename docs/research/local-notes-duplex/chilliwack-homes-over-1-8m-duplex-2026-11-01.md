## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs, under OCP Bylaw 5500 "Chilliwack 2050" (Schedule B plans B1–B7 and Schedule D permit areas D-1 to D-10 as listed on the City's OCP page). The zone of a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until read; the research pass could not open the consolidated bylaw PDF for §6 zone figures (429), so no zone minimum is stated here beyond the sourced positions below. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **SSMUH position.** Chilliwack's SSMUH bylaw is Bylaw 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024), allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing (City staff-report description, rules-gaps item 6). Duplexes are the baseline form in that framework. On ALR land the ALR rules govern instead (below). (Research file above.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Chilliwack's own rule, never borrowed.** Under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 (ss. 2, 4.06, 5.04, confirmed in rules-gaps item 7f): a secondary suite is capped at the **lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling**, one accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot, garden suites are capped at 100 m², and parking rules sit at s. 5.04. On ALR land, a secondary suite is permitted ONLY inside the principal residence (ALC rule, §2/§3 farm position). (Research file above.)
- **ALR / farm-residence position at this band.** ALC housing guidance caps a principal residence at 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), permits a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and limits an additional residence to up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. The OCP includes Development Permit Area 4 – Farm Home Plate; a Development Permit is required when the Zoning Bylaw's Farm Home Plate regulations are not met. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **STRATA block.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support, with the 3055 conflict carried OPEN.** Subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer process and, in the ALR, may require an ALC application first (subdivision or boundary adjustment is on the City's ALC-application list). **Chilliwack's servicing (Land Development) bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger: the ledger holds "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" as NOT adopted, while the City's own subdivisions webpage asserts that bylaw name/number and the chilliwack-proper-west + chilliwack-farms LOTS siblings assert it as governing. The conflict is OPEN, settlement requires a read of the City's actual bylaw text, and 3055 is NEVER asserted in this file.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through that process; party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Tax timing:** Chilliwack property taxes carry a 5% penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031 — above this band's $1.8M floor there is no rebate under that program on price alone. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only.
