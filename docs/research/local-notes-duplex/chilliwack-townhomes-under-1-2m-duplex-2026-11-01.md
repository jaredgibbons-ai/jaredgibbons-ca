## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math:** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. **Newly built home exemption (Province, effective April 1, 2024):** a qualifying newly built home bought as a principal residence is fully exempt at a fair market value of $1,100,000 or less, with a partial exemption phasing out between $1,100,000 and $1,150,000 — a real closing-cost difference inside this band; the live page's FAQ statement of these thresholds matches the Province's page. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. (Research file, §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption)
