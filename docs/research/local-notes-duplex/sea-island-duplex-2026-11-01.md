## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Municipality: City of Richmond, BC. Sea Island is a separate island in the Fraser River, between Lulu Island (the main part of Richmond) to the south and east and the City of Vancouver across the North Arm to the north. "Sea Island" is the GVR listing sub-area name and also one of the City's own planning areas (planning area 12 on the City Official Community Plan Key Map). The island is dominated by Vancouver International Airport (YVR) on federal land; the only residential pocket is Burkeville, in the south-east part of the island near Miller Road and Russ Baker Way. ## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. - **Q: Does aircraft noise affect a Burkeville purchase?** It is a documented due-diligence item: YVR's 2025–2029 Noise Management Plan covers land use planning near the airport, YVR publishes annual noise reports with monitoring data, and the City's ANSD framework applies to noise-sensitive development in the vicinity. - **Q: Is there a school on Sea Island?** No operating public school — Sea Island Elementary School on Wellington Crescent has closed. For the sample address 7140 Miller Road the SD38 locator gives Brighouse Elementary and Richmond Secondary — that address only. (§5.)

- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
