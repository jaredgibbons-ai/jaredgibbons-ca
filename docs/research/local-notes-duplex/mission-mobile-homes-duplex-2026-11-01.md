## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC only as **(a) a STRATA duplex** (2-lot strata plan; each half a strata lot; Form B diligence) or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex** (parcel subdivided; each half on its own fee-simple lot, party-wall form where a source documents it). A manufactured home on a rented pad is none of these forms: the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad — that is not land ownership and not a duplex. (Program definition, `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; research file above; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **SSMUH position (Mission frame — duplexes generally, not pad stock).** Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial SSMUH framework; Mission's SSMUH Bylaw is 6299-2024, verified as Mission's SSMUH bylaw, with its unit counts UNCONFIRMED and none asserted. (Mobile sibling above; D12 brief Mission frame.)
- **Secondary suites (Mission's own rule, never borrowed).** In the City of Mission all secondary suites must be registered with the City; a suite is a self-contained living space in a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; a new suite goes through the building permit process. This rule concerns houses, not pad-rent manufactured homes. (Mission-lots-land research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/mission-lots-land.md; https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program)
- **Manufactured-home tenancy rules (the rules that actually govern this topic's stock).** Pad rent increases: only with 3 full months' notice, only once every 12 months, and not above the yearly limit for manufactured home site tenancies — 2.3% for 2026 and 2.2% for 2027 — plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; anything above requires an RTB order. Assignment on sale: under MHPTA s.28 the site tenancy may be assigned only with the landlord's prior written consent (or deemed consent, or a director's order, or authorization in the agreement itself); the landlord must not charge for consent; the seller must give the proposed buyer the park rules and relevant tenancy terms before requesting consent; on assignment the pad rent and next increase date carry over unchanged. Park closure/conversion (MHPTA s.42): all permits in place first, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation, with defined additional-compensation routes. Park rules, pet and age provisions for any specific park on this feed: UNVERIFIED — listing text only; obtain the park's own rule package. (Research file above; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes)
- **STRATA block — strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan); applies only if a home is in a bare-land strata, none verified for Mission here.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that threshold, so the cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; ask what reports the strata holds anyway. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since Nov 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. (Mobile sibling §3; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws)
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the governing approving authority; no finer detail is stated. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. Pad ground is not subdivided by a pad tenant: a home on a rented pad is not land ownership. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file above.)
- **Taxes — TWO REGIMES, NEVER BLENDED (Mission frame).** Park homes inside the City of Mission sit in a city where 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a **FLAT 10% penalty** on current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026. Parks in the FVRD electoral areas are rural: notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office, paid to the Province, due July 2, with **5% + 5% penalties** under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11 (the further 5% on amounts unpaid October 31) — a DISTINCT regime, never blended with the City's flat 10%. How tax is split between a park owner and individual pad tenants in any specific park: UNVERIFIED — it is set by the park's tenancy agreements. Mission has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED). (Research file above; https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (for transfers where PTT applies; a chattel home on a rented pad is not a land transfer of this kind).** BC PTT: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (max $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, max $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate, and neither is a resale manufactured home on a pad. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

Its 55+ position is listing-stated only, and its parcel-level City/FVRD jurisdiction is UNVERIFIED. (§2.)

- **Q: What is the difference between a duplex and a half duplex — and where do park homes fit?** A duplex purchase is a whole two-unit building and lot on one title; a half duplex is one unit sold separately, only where strata-divided (2-lot plan) or subdivided fee-simple. A park home fits neither: it is a chattel home on a rented pad. (§2.)
- **Q: One card said "mobile on its own lot" — does that make it a half duplex?** No. That Triple Creek Estates card is an own-lot tenure CLAIM in listing text, to be confirmed on title; an owned-lot manufactured home is still not a duplex, which is a two-unit building form. (§§1–2.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up, and can the park refuse my buyer?** Pad rent: once every 12 months, on 3 full months' notice, up to the yearly limit (2.3% for 2026; 2.2% for 2027) plus the proportional levy/utility amount. On sale, the site tenancy is assigned under MHPTA s.28 with the landlord's consent (which cannot be charged for); pad rent carries over unchanged on assignment. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. No bare-land strata is verified for Mission in this topic in any case. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on a duplex purchase in Mission?** Run the tiers on your own price: $800,000 → $14,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 (fully residential, no exemption). A chattel home on a rented pad is not taxed as a land transfer of this kind. (§3.)
- **Q: Which tax penalty applies to a park home — 10% or 5% + 5%?** It depends on the park's jurisdiction, which is UNVERIFIED at parcel level for all three roster parks: City parcels carry the FLAT 10% after July 2; rural FVRD parcels carry the separate 5% + 5% regime. Confirm the specific parcel's jurisdiction before quoting either — the two are never blended. (§3.)
