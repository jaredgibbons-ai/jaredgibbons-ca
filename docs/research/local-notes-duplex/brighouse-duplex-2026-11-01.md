## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Richmond Zoning Bylaw No. 8500).** For the remaining detached pockets, Single Detached zones RS1/RS2 in Bylaw 8500 Section 8.1 apply outside the City Centre plan designations, with secondary suites under Section 5.4; inside the plan designations the City Centre transects (T6/T5/T4) govern form. The exact zone for a specific Brighouse lot is read off the current Bylaw 8500 text for that lot — zone entitlements asserted beyond that read are UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/bylaws.htm (Research file §4; settled Richmond frame.)
- **SSMUH position.** The provincial SSMUH rules as Richmond implemented them govern added units on eligible lots, with the duplex as the baseline form; no Richmond-specific SSMUH figure is carried in this ground's research, so none is asserted here. (Standing Richmond frame, brief chain.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Richmond's own position.** Suites in the RS1/RS2 pockets run under Bylaw 8500 Section 5.4, as the research states it; no Richmond suite size cap is stated in this ground's research or siblings, so NO cap figure is carried here — Surrey's 40% or any other city's figure is never borrowed. The suite position for a specific home is UNCONFIRMED until read in the current zoning text and, for a strata unit, the strata's bylaws. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **Flood.** New construction in Richmond is subject to flood construction levels under the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204, within the city-wide dike system (designed for a 1:500 flood event, dike raising from 3.5 m to 4.7 m) — confirm the FCL position for any ground-oriented purchase. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex (and in full to the towers and Ascott Wynde).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — a 102-unit complex like Ascott Wynde is INSIDE that cycle on lot count; **a 2-lot strata duplex, by contrast, is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone** — the distinction is stated precisely for both forms. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 (the directory line for Ascott Wynde recording rentals "allowed with restrictions" carries a VOID rental element on this rule). Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the titles — read them. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Richmond servicing Bylaws 8751/8752 VERIFIED in the verified ledger. ADEU charges are West Cambie / Alexandra ONLY — no ADEU position is carried for Brighouse. (Verified ledger; D19 frame.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
