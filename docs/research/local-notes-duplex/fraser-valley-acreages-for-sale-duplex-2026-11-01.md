## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **The ALR residence frame — the structural reason acreage duplex stock is not a strata market.** Acreage housing in the ALR is freehold in practice and single-residence-led: land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless it qualifies for an additional residence; the principal residence must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, and an additional residence is capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha, with local governments able to regulate further but not to be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. And residences / land in the ALR **cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission** — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALCA s. 19 / s. 21(1), as explained in ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6). That is a permission rule, not an absolute ban — but it means the strata-duplex half of the duplex form class has no ordinary ALR acreage instance, and the multi-unit strata profile of urban pages does not exist on ALR parcels. ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. - **ALR residence and stratification rules (the one frame that IS valley-wide, because the ALR is provincial).** One principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); local governments may regulate further but not more permissively. A plan that needs a third dwelling, a larger house, or a strata split needs ALC permission, not just a municipal permit: ALR land cannot be stratified **without the ALC's permission** (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; ALCA s. 21(1)) — never written as "can never be stratified," and Bulletin 05 is never cited for stratification. ALR short-term accommodation limits, relevant to acreage income plans: renting out the entire principal residence for short-term stays is not permitted outright in the ALR and requires ALC approval; an additional residence may be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism accommodation on a property that is actively farmed and holds farm classification under the Assessment Act. (Research file above.)
- **Farm classification (BC Assessment Class 9) — the tax frame an acreage duplex buyer inherits.** A producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices of $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha. Farm classification continues only while the thresholds keep being met; BC Assessment will remove it if a property no longer meets the requirements or the owner fails to provide supporting information when asked, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by the legislated deadline of October 31, for consideration for the next assessment and tax year. (Research file above; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), where one lawfully exists off ALR land.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. On ALR land, the strata form itself first needs the ALC permission stated above. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Approving Officer process of the municipality (or, for electoral-area parcels, the provincial approving authority) where the parcel sits — this topic file names no municipality's process as its own, because it has no ground. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. - **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (for transfers where PTT applies).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. (This slug's mobile-homes sibling's own worked example — a $1,500,000 fully residential fee-simple purchase, no exemption — is $2,000 + 2% × $1,300,000 = $28,000, on the same tiers; acreage transfers can also carry mixed-use apportionment, which is a per-transaction professional question, not a figure this file states.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate, and the rebate is for new housing, not bare land itself. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I shop Fraser Valley acreage duplexes from this page?** Not as a roster. This is a regional topic file with no single ground: it states no duplex roster, and no named strata duplex or fee-simple half-duplex subdivision is verified as acreage stock. Duplex questions resolve parcel by parcel, in the municipality where the parcel sits. (§2.)
- **Q: Why is there no acreage duplex market the way there is an urban one?** On the ALR — which covers most Fraser Valley acreage housing — the frame is one principal residence (≤500 m²) plus at most one additional residence (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size), and land cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission. That structure leaves no ordinary strata-duplex form on ALR parcels. (§2, §3.)
- **Q: What about Bishop's Green and St. Martin's Lane — aren't those half-duplex acreages?** No. Both are gated rancher TOWNHOUSE strata entries on South Surrey (Sunnyside Park) ground, listed in this slug's 55-plus sibling's regional assembly with "duplex-style" / "half-duplex" wording. Built form governs — they are townhouse stock at their own home, not duplex stock and not acreage stock, and no count is taken for them here. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide an ALR acreage into two half-duplex lots?** Not without the ALC's permission: depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and ALCA s. 21(1) bars subdividing agricultural land unless the Commission permits it or the ALR General Regulation allows it. Off ALR land, a fee-simple split runs through the subdivision approval of the municipality where the parcel sits. (§3.)
- **Q: How many homes can I have on an ALR acreage?** Generally one principal residence (maximum 500 m² total floor area), plus, where the property qualifies, one additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; more than that, or a larger residence, requires ALC approval, and local bylaws may restrict further. (§3.)
- **Q: What farm income does an acreage need to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's thresholds are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; new applications are due by the legislated October 31 deadline. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on a half-duplex purchase on an acreage?** Run the tiers on its own price as its own transfer: $800,000 → $14,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000 (fully residential, no exemption; mixed-use acreage transfers can apportion — a per-transaction question). (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence — and on ALR land, ask first whether the strata itself had ALC permission. (§2, §3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Fraser Valley acreage duplex?** There is no answer at the regional level: this topic has no single ground, and school catchments are address-based in the district where the parcel sits. The research for the parcel's own area file — not this topic file — is where any catchment position lives, and an exact-address locator run is the only basis on which one is ever stated in this program. (§5.)
- **Q: Are there any Fraser Valley acreage market numbers?** No. SnapStats publishes no acreage segment and no duplex category; acreages sit inside their own communities' ordinary Detached rows. Never quote an "average acreage price" — price an acreage parcel by parcel, on its own community's comparables, its ALR status, water, buildings and farm income. (§1.)
