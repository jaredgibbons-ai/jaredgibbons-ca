## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **District plans and zoning — District of North Vancouver ONLY.** The Official Community Plan is Bylaw 7900, which directs 75–90% of growth to its four centres and preserves established low-density neighbourhoods outside them; the zoning bylaw is Zoning Bylaw 3210 (cited in District material as amended by Bylaw 8698, June 2024, for the suite and coach-house rules). The exact zone for a specific Pemberton Heights lot is read off the current Bylaw 3210 text and District GeoWeb for that lot — a Pemberton-Heights-specific zone name or minimum lot size is not stated in this slug's research and is UNCONFIRMED here. **City of North Vancouver bylaws (OCP 8400 / Zoning 6700) do not apply anywhere in this file's ground.** https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Suites and coach houses — the District's own rule, and this ground's documented second-unit form.** Since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — **three units total** — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. December 2023 changes let a secondary suite be **up to 49% of the house** with no minimum size and raised the maximum coach house height to two storeys. That 49% figure is the District of North Vancouver's own suite rule, stated for this District only — no other municipality's suite cap is borrowed or exported. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **SSMUH — the District's classification, with its compliance position stated.** The District's GEOweb SSMUH classification (updated May 1, 2024) shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. The Province's Bill 25 (November 2025) requires full SSMUH density by June 30, 2026, and the District's compliance amendments were still in preparation in spring 2026 reports, tied to the Zoning Bylaw Rewrite. For this area specifically, a March 2, 2026 Council motion asked staff to pursue exempting the Lions Gate area from SSMUH, on the basis that it is better suited to development supporting rapid transit — so the specific lot's classification is read off GEOweb, not assumed; a duplex (two units) sits inside even the lowest tier where the lot is non-exempt. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-4-detached-residential.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **Hazards.** The District's OCP designates Natural Hazard Development Permit Areas (including slope and creek hazards), and the District publishes property-level hazard information through GeoWeb; Schedule B of the OCP establishes DPAs for Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard. Properties near the Capilano River on the west edge warrant a flood/hazard check before purchase — the District's GEOweb flood construction level data is defined as modelled flood water levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m, and Metro Vancouver warns the river below Cleveland Dam can rise more than 5 metres in a release event. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf and https://metrovancouver.org/river-safety/about-the-capilano-river (Research file §4.)
- **Servicing: UNCONFIRMED.** The settled ledger holds the District of North Vancouver's servicing bylaw number UNCONFIRMED — no number is asserted in this file. Utility figures that ARE sourced (the 2026 single-family water and sewer/drainage fees in §5) are tax/utility facts, not a servicing-bylaw citation. (Settled ledger; research file §4.)
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / half duplex in Pemberton Heights (and in full to the Lions Gate Village stratas).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the District's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Property taxes.** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; Council approved a 3.53% municipal tax increase for 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value, and single-family utility fees include water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60 (combined single-family utilities $2,571 in 2026). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a half duplex in Pemberton Heights?** No named half duplex — strata or fee-simple — is verified anywhere in this program for this ground. What sells on the bench is a whole freehold house (often with suite/coach-house potential); the separable attached product is at Lions Gate Village, in townhouse and apartment form. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Pemberton Heights in the City or the District — and is it the Pemberton near Whistler?** The District of North Vancouver. It is one of the neighbourhoods in the District's Lions Gate community; the City is a separate municipality to the east, and the Town of Pemberton in the Sea-to-Sky corridor is a different place entirely — the board's "Pemberton NV" name exists to keep them apart. (§1.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite and a coach house to a bench house?** That is the District's documented permission: since June 2024 a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — are permitted on single-family zoned lots within the Urban Containment Boundary, with the suite up to 49% of the house. Confirm the specific lot on GeoWeb. (§3.)
- **Q: Will SSMUH give my lot four or six units?** Not assumed. District Council voted in March 2026 to pursue exempting the Lions Gate area from SSMUH, and the Bill 25 compliance amendments were still in preparation in spring 2026 — price the lot as a single-family lot unless a specific application or the lot's GEOweb classification says otherwise. (§3.)
- **Q: What do I buy at Belle Isle or Mason?** A strata townhouse lot in an occupied Lions Gate Village complex (88 townhouses at Belle Isle; 40 at Mason, per the District's development update) — counted in the townhomes file, Form B diligence in full. Townhouse form, not a duplex or a half duplex. (§2.)
- **Q: Does Park West's unit count matter to me?** The condos sibling carries Park West's unit count as UNCONFIRMED between sources — read the Form B and the strata's own records for the lot you are buying rather than any published total. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: What hazard check matters on the west edge?** The Capilano River: pull the GEOweb flood construction level before pricing a renovation on a river-side lot — the District publishes FCL points modelled on a 2100 flood horizon plus 0.6 m, and the river below Cleveland Dam can rise more than 5 metres in a release event. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Is there a Pemberton Heights market median I can rely on?** No — SnapStats does not cover North Vancouver and no substitute figure is used in this program. (§1.)
