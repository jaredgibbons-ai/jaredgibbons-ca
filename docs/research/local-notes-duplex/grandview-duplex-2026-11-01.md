## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Surrey's R6 semi-detached form (§3) is the fee-simple expression of this. A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; R6 form per the City's SSMUH page: https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000) — the Topic M figures for this region.** In the R3 Zone, lots under 280 sq. m allow up to 3 units, lots from 280 sq. m to under 4,050 sq. m allow up to 4 units, and eligible lots in Frequent Bus Stop Areas allow up to 6 units; R3 subdivision minimums are lot area 560 sq. m, width 15 m and depth 28 m. In the R6 Zone, a zero-lot line semi-detached building on a lot under 280 sq. m may accommodate 2 to 3 units and a duplex on a lot over 280 sq. m may accommodate 4 to 6 units; R6 subdivision minimums are interior lot area 200 sq. m, width 7.2 m, depth 28 m and corner lot area 226 sq. m, width 8.7 m, depth 28 m, with a 0.0 m side yard permitted at the common side lot line between paired lots and maximum lot coverage of 60 percent. The rules apply to eligible lots under 4,050 sq. m inside the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including hazard lands and lots not connected to community water or sewer. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH position.** Surrey's SSMUH changes took effect July 8, 2024 (the R3/R6 renaming above is those amendments). The OCP governing is Surrey 2050, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026); Bylaw No. 18020 is the replaced predecessor and is never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Surrey's own rule.** One secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, and never inside a coach house or garden suite; a suite is not a separate strata lot. A coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), with lane access required for a coach house on smaller lots and one extra parking space. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — Surrey's documented form.** The R6 zone IS Surrey's documented fee-simple semi-detached mechanism: paired lots subdivided to the R6 minimums above, with a 0.0 m side yard permitted at the common side lot line between paired lots — the party wall sits on that line. A fee-simple split of an existing duplex parcel still requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall maintenance obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles (the Topic M rollup records party-wall maintenance rules for duplexes as not confirmed — UNCONFIRMED at the general level). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Servicing.** Surrey servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) per the verified ledger. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Grandview?** Yes, where the side is legally separate — Surrey's R6 semi-detached form is exactly that (paired fee-simple lots at a zero-lot-line common side), and a strata duplex half is a strata lot. A single-lot duplex still sells whole. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: What are "Surrey Half Duplexes" and "South Surrey Half Duplexes"?** The first is the Paragon board grouping for half-duplex homes across Surrey; the second is a site search category on jaredgibbons.ca. Neither is a place. A Grandview half duplex can surface under either label, filed by form. (§2.)
- **Q: Is there a named duplex development in Grandview?** None verified. The named stock here is townhouse/project form counted in other files (The Loop, The Boroughs, Berkeley Village and the rest, §2 cross-references); the duplex evidence is the Topic M form basis. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). With two lots, every common expense splits two ways. (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside my half?** Surrey permits one secondary suite in a semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite. A strata half also needs the strata's bylaws checked. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on an R6 semi-detached pair?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says — the provincial rollup records party-wall maintenance rules for duplexes as not confirmed at the general level, so the titles are the only source. (§3.)
- **Q: Is Berkeley Village a Grandview complex?** It is counted in the pacific-douglas townhomes file, with the Pacific Douglas vs Grandview Heights filing conflict carried openly — not moved, not duplicated, and townhouse form in any case, not duplex stock here. (§2.)
