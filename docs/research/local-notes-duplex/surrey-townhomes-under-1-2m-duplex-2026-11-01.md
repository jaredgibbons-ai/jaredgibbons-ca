## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A "duplex-style" townhouse is none of these forms: it is a strata townhouse unit whose plan or marketing resembles one half of a duplex pair, sold as a townhouse strata lot inside a multi-unit complex — built form governs, and it is townhouse stock. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`.)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. A purchase under this band's ceiling meets only the first two tiers. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. First-time buyers' exemption: full at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — the lower part of this band can sit inside it on price alone, the part above $860,000 cannot; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000.
