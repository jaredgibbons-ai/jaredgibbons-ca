## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. **A COACH HOUSE fits none of these forms:** it is a second, smaller dwelling unit on the same fee-simple lot as the principal house — it has no separate title, no strata lot, and no subdivided parcel, so it can never be bought on its own. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Township coach-house and suite rules (SETTLED program positions, conflict reported not resolved):** coach house per s.102 above (second storey of a detached garage only). Township secondary suite cap: Zoning Bylaw s.102, as amended, caps a Township secondary suite at **120 m²** with no percentage cap — the Township's own rule, never borrowed. Documented conflict: the Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states suites are NOT permitted in townhouses/rowhouses, while the current s.102 definition (July 2025) states a suite may be located in a townhouse or rowhouse — both documents are reported with their dates; a buyer relying on either point should confirm with the Township in writing. (Research file, §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md.)
- **Township SSMUH (SETTLED program position):** Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 (NO 6-plex tier) — eligible lots (Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units at ≤280 m² or 4 units at 280–4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible. Properties that do not qualify are instead eligible for one unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit, depending on zoning. Do not stack permissions: an eligible lot's SSMUH route (3–4 units) and its suite/ADU route are alternatives governed by eligibility and zoning — confirm which route the lot is actually on with Township staff. Duplexes are the baseline SSMUH form where the route applies; a coach house belongs to the suite/ADU side, not the SSMUH multi-unit side. (Research file, §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx.)

- **ALR carriage-house exception (relevant to the rural cards in this feed):** for an ALR additional residence, an attached garage is excluded from the total floor area calculation if the additional residence sits on the second storey above a one-storey garage, the garage is for the principal residence's vehicles, its floor area does not exceed the additional residence's maximum (90 m² / 186 m²), there is no internal connection, and the residence is clustered close to the principal residence. On ALR ground, test the second unit against the ALR frame first: one additional residence at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha). (Research file, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) wherever one exists; it does NOT apply to a coach-house lot, which is fee-simple and has no strata.** Form B diligence applies in full to a strata purchase. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Approving Officer process of the municipality the parcel sits in; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. A coach house cannot be subdivided off its lot by calling it one: it is an accessory unit on the principal lot. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A coach house is taxed as part of its lot's single transfer — there is no separate coach-house transfer, because there is no separate title. A half duplex, by contrast, is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is a coach house in the Township of Langley?** Under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s.102, a coach house is a dwelling unit on the second storey of a detached garage only. Section 100's own text states no size cap for one — a full-text search of Section 100 found none — but whether a cap sits in the bylaw's individual zone sections is UNVERIFIED, which is why a ground-level cottage or barn loft marketed as a "coach house" needs its permit checked. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Can I buy just the coach house?** No. A coach house is a fee-simple second unit on the principal house's lot — never strata, no separate title — so it sells only with the lot. That is the core difference from a half duplex, which is a separately titled half of a two-unit building. (§2.)
- **Q: Is a carriage home the same as a coach house?** They describe similar buildings under different bylaws: the Township's Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 uses "coach house," while the City of Langley's Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300 uses "carriage home" — the live page's detailed City limits are UNVERIFIED in this file because the City bylaw was not opened in this pass. Never borrow one municipality's limits for the other's ground. (§§1–3.)
- **Q: How big can a secondary suite be in the Township of Langley?** Zoning Bylaw s.102, as amended, caps a Township secondary suite at 120 m² with no percentage cap; note the documented conflict — the Township's January 2025 guide says suites are not permitted in townhouses/rowhouses, while the July 2025 s.102 definition says they may be. Get the Township's current answer in writing where it matters. (§3.)
- **Q: The listing says "coach house" — how do I verify it?** Ask for the building permit and the final inspection for the coach house itself, and confirm the zone with the right municipality — a building marketed as a coach house may have been approved as a garage, studio or workshop, as the page itself warns. Permit status for every card in this feed is UNVERIFIED. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Can an ALR property's additional residence sit over a garage?** Yes, on the ALC's carriage-house exception: the garage below is excluded from the additional residence's floor area if it is one storey and for the principal residence's vehicles, does not exceed the additional residence's maximum floor area (90 m² or 186 m²), has no internal connection to the residence, and the residence is clustered close to the principal residence. (§3.)
- **Q: What did Langley homes sell for in September 2026?** As board context only — not coach-house-segment figures, for which no figure exists in this source: the Langley community detached median was $1,345,000 on 449 listings and 64 sales, and the Condos & Townhomes median was $611,000 on 699 listings and 128 sales. (§1.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay on a house with a coach house?** One transfer, one bill, on the whole purchase price — on the worked tiers in §3, fully residential with no exemption, $22,000 at $1,200,000 and $28,000 at $1,500,000. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. (§3.)
