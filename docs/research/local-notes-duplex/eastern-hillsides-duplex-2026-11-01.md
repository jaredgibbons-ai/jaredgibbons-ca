## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs; the SSMUH amendment (below) speaks to parcels "zoned for single-detached and duplex housing," which is the bylaw's own confirmation that duplex zoning exists as a class in this city. Whether any Eastern Hillsides parcel carries that zoning is a per-lot read of the current consolidated Bylaw 5000 text and the B4 plan — UNCONFIRMED for this hillside in this program, and the hillside's geotechnical development-approval requirements apply on top of any zoning permission. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1962
- **SSMUH position.** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, adopted June 18, 2024, allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing — the duplex is the baseline form the amendment builds on. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3090/Signed%20Staff%20Report%20%2D%20RZ001761%2Epdf
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Chilliwack's own rule.** A secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space (Bylaw No. 5000, s. 2); only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits (s. 4.06(1) — secondary suite, coach house or garden suite), and garden suites are capped at 100 m². The City's Secondary Suites Policy permits rental of a suite in any single detached dwelling regardless of zone, with a building permit, BC Building and Fire Code compliance and flood-protection compliance; new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. Those are Chilliwack's figures, never borrowed from another city's. https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **STRATA block — stated for completeness; no strata duplex exists on this ground.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists elsewhere: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it, and no such split is documented on this ground. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Chilliwack servicing is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger: the "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" named on the City's subdivisions webpage is held NOT adopted in the ledger, and no servicing bylaw number is asserted in this file. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any duplex or half duplex in Eastern Hillsides?** None verified — the word does not appear in this ground's research file or any sibling. The documented stock is detached homes and strata townhouse/condo development under the B4 plan. Widen the search to the valley floor (Sardis, Chilliwack Proper) for the duplex form. (§2.)
- **Q: Could I build a duplex on a hillside lot under SSMUH?** The city-wide amendment allows 3–4 units on eligible parcels zoned for single-detached and duplex housing inside the urban growth boundary — but the parcel's zoning, the B4 plan and the hillside geotechnical approvals all have to line up, and none of that is verified for any specific lot here. Per-lot confirmation with the City first. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex (off this ground)?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a house here have a suite AND a coach house?** No — only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits (suite at the lesser of 90 m² / 40%, coach house, or garden suite at 100 m² cap), under Bylaw 5000, ss. 2 and 4.06. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for Eastern Hillsides?** No. CADREB publishes no area stats and SnapStats does not cover Chilliwack. (§1.)
