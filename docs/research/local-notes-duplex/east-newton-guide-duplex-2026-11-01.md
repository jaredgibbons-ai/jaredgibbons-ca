## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership at all — the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad. The research states Surrey's own version of the split plainly: a two-unit building in Surrey can be one fee-simple property or a two-lot strata under the Strata Property Act, in which case each unit carries its own title, strata fees and bylaws. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; research file, §5; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**FORM CLASS ONLY — NO DUPLEX ROSTER, stated at the top of this section.** No named duplex, strata half duplex or fee-simple half-duplex development is verified in East Newton in this guide slug's research or its guide siblings. A secondary suite in Surrey is capped at **40% of the principal habitable area** — Surrey's own cap, never borrowed. - **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Surrey ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. This block does NOT apply to the Crispen Bays / Crestway Bays records — on their recorded form they are pad tenancies under the MHPTA, with per-park tenure UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process under Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986, VERIFIED), against the zone minimums in §2. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. The research's own buying guidance states the diligence split plainly: a fee-simple duplex is one title with no strata fees or bylaws, while a strata duplex is two strata lots with monthly fees, bylaws and shared insurance obligations under the Strata Property Act. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file, §6; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales — relevant in East Newton to developer sales such as Silva, not to resale houses or park homes. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
