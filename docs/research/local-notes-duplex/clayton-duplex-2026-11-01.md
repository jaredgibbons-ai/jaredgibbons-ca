## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Surrey's R6 semi-detached form (§3) is the fee-simple expression of this: paired units on their own lots at a zero-lot-line common side. A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; R6 form per the City's SSMUH page: https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000).** Surrey's SSMUH amendments renamed the former RF zone to R3 Urban Residential Zone (Part 15) and the former RF-SD zone to **R6 Semi-Detached Residential Zone (Part 18)**; the former RM-D Duplex Residential Zone was removed and grouped by lot size — the current zones are R3 and R6. Permitted small-scale multi-unit forms are secondary suites, detached accessory dwelling units including garden suites and coach houses, duplexes (side-by-side or up-and-down) and houseplexes (triplexes and fourplexes). In R6, a zero-lot line semi-detached building on a lot under 280 sq. m may accommodate 2 to 3 units and a duplex on a lot over 280 sq. m may accommodate 4 to 6 units; R6 subdivision minimums are interior lot area 200 sq. m, width 7.2 m, depth 28 m and corner lot area 226 sq. m, width 8.7 m, depth 28 m, with a 0.0 m side yard permitted at the common side lot line between paired lots and maximum lot coverage of 60 percent. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH position.** Surrey's SSMUH changes took effect July 8, 2024: a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² (in R3: lots under 280 sq. m allow up to 3 units, lots from 280 sq. m to under 4,050 sq. m allow up to 4 units), and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the rules apply to eligible lots under 4,050 sq. m inside the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including hazard lands and lots not connected to community water or sewer. The OCP governing is Surrey 2050, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026); Bylaw No. 18020 is the replaced predecessor and is never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Surrey's own rule.** One secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, and never inside a coach house or garden suite; a suite is not a separate strata lot. A coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), with lane access required for a coach house on smaller lots and one extra parking space. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — Surrey's documented form.** The R6 zone IS Surrey's documented fee-simple semi-detached mechanism: paired lots subdivided to the R6 minimums above, with a 0.0 m side yard permitted at the common side lot line between paired lots — the party wall sits on that line. A fee-simple split of an existing duplex parcel still requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall maintenance obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Servicing.** Surrey servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) per the verified ledger. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is stated. (Verified ledger, brief chain; mobile-homes sibling §3.)
- **Short-term rentals, where met.** Surrey permits STRs owner-only (the owner must live at the property as their primary residence; tenants cannot run one); the licence figure is carried as **$358 per year** per the City's Short-Term Rentals page, with the conflict disclosed: the City's 2024 corporate report proposed **$350 per year plus fees** — confirm the current figure before quoting it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Clayton?** Yes, where the side is legally separate — Surrey's R6 semi-detached form is exactly that (paired fee-simple lots at a zero-lot-line common side), and a strata duplex half is a strata lot. A single-lot duplex still sells whole. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: What is "Surrey Half Duplexes"?** The Paragon board grouping for half-duplex (semi-detached) homes across Surrey — a feed category, not a Surrey neighbourhood. A Clayton half duplex can surface under it. (§2.)
- **Q: Is there a named duplex development in Clayton?** None verified — the research file confirms no named building on a developer or strata source; named candidates are listing-card text only, carried as records. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). With two lots, every common expense splits two ways. (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside my half?** Surrey permits one secondary suite in a semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite. A strata half also needs the strata's bylaws checked. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. Surrey STRs are a separate, owner-only licence regime (§3). (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on an R6 semi-detached pair?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says — the 0.0 m side yard at the common line is a zoning permission, not a maintenance agreement. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for Clayton?** No. SnapStats has no duplex category; Surrey figures are community-level Detached vs "Condos & Townhomes" combined, labelled context only. (§1.)
