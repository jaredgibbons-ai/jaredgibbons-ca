## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex on the east shore?** There is no verified duplex on the east shore to buy a side of — and inside the Park the land is Park Board lease land, so neither a strata-lot half nor a fee-simple half exists there on any source checked. What sells is the whole home plus its leasehold interest. (§2.)
- **Q: Is an east-shore home freehold?** No — homes inside Cultus Lake Park are on leased lots under the Cultus Lake Park Board. Lease terms, Park bylaws and Board consent rules drive the deal, not freehold title. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is Cultus Lake East part of Chilliwack?** No. It is FVRD Electoral Area H / Cultus Lake Park Board ground south of the City of Chilliwack. Chilliwack bylaws and Chilliwack tax dates do not apply; a Chilliwack mailing address does not change that. (§1; §3.)

(§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite or second unit to an east-shore home?** UNCONFIRMED — no suite or SSMUH rule for the east shore's zones was opened in this slug's research. Read the current Park zoning bylaw and Park bylaws for the specific lot before assuming any second-unit right. (§3.)

Rural-regime taxes outside the Park are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31 (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11). (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated if I do buy a separable half somewhere?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,200,000 fully residential with no exemption = $22,000 (worked in §3).
