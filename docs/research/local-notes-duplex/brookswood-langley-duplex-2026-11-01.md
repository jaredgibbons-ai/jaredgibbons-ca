## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Township Zoning Bylaw 2500).** Zones named in Township notices for the area include Suburban Residential SR-1, SR-2 and SR-3 and Rural Zone RU-2; Bylaw 2500 Section 110.1 as read in Township council reports gives SR-1 3,716 m² / 40 m frontage, SR-2 8,094 m² / 45 m, SR-3 1,765 m² / 27.45 m; RU-2 minimums are UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section. The duplex / two-unit form sits in the zones Bylaw 2500 permits it in; the zone for a specific lot is read off the current bylaw text for that lot. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **SSMUH position (Township Bylaw 6020).** Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible for the SSMUH tiers. **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020.** Duplexes are the baseline form within that frame. (Condos sibling §3; settled Township frame.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the Township's own rule: 120 m² cap** (Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 definition; a building permit is required for all suites). A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102 — never strata. **Live Township conflict flagged, resolved nowhere** (carried from the siblings): s.102 as read in this program permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted. Township zoning for a second dwelling / coach house on non-reserve Brookswood acreage is otherwise UNVERIFIED in this slug's research. (Townhomes sibling §3; research file §4.)
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex (and to the Bromley presale strata on completion).** Form B diligence applies in full (SPA s.59). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — Bromley's 26 homes put it over the threshold once complete; **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**, stated precisely. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. The block does NOT apply to the pad-rental parks in §2, which have no strata corporation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Approving Officer process; R-CL s.403 (220 m² min / 464.5 m² max lot area) governs only where that zone applies. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the titles — read them. (Program discipline, D1 brief; settled Township frame.)
- **Servicing.** Township servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED in the verified ledger. (D19 frame.)
- **ALR / second dwellings:** much of the Township is ALR; the ALC frame permits one principal residence and, subject to conditions, one additional residence (≤90 m² on parcels ≤40 ha; ≤186 m² above) — Township zoning must also permit it, and that zoning for Brookswood acreage is UNVERIFIED (above). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. (The townhomes sibling's $10,220 worked example uses the $611,000 Langley community attached median as an arithmetic illustration only — NOT a Brookswood figure — and is not repeated as a market figure here.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate; developer sales at Bromley are new housing and carry GST / REDMA treatment in the siblings. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a named duplex complex in Brookswood?** No — none is verified by name. The stock is freehold single-family housing on large lots; the duplex picture is the form class of individual two-unit / paired homes. (§2.)
- **Q: Are Cedar Creek and Fernridge bare-land strata I can get a Form B for?** No — both are PAD-RENTAL parks per the disk: you own the home and rent the pad, under the MHPTA. There is no strata corporation and no Form B. (§2.)
- **Q: Are the Brookswood parks really 55+?** Listings describe both parks as 55+ — AS listing-stated only. A park's age rule lives in its park rules and tenancy documents, not a strata bylaw; get the current rule in writing. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I put six units on a Brookswood lot?** No — Bylaw 6020 has NO 6-plex tier (3 units ≤280 m² / 4 units under 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² ineligible). The Brookswood DEVELOPMENT guide page's 6-plex claim is a defect of that page (§5). (§3.)
- **Q: How big can a suite be inside a Township duplex?** The Township cap is 120 m² (s.102). Note the live conflict on townhouse suites (§3) — confirm the form with the Township. (§3.)
- **Q: If I buy half of a strata duplex, what diligence applies?** Form B diligence in full — and a 2-lot strata is below the 5-lot depreciation-report threshold, so ask what reports actually exist. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. Park rules are a different regime (§2). (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
