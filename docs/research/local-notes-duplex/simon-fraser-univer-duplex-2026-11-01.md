## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

A fee-simple half duplex requires a subdivided parcel a buyer can own; no such parcel exists inside the community. No named strata duplex (2-lot strata plan) is verified there either — the strata product is apartment and townhouse-form units inside larger developments. (Research file §5; acreages sibling §2.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815).** Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024 — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified — carried into the current bylaw: the **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815), adopted June 9, 2026 and in effect July 1, 2026**. The former Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) is superseded and is **never cited as current** in this file; any source citing it for this ground is citing a repealed bylaw. Inside UniverCity itself the zoning question is academic for a buyer — the tenure (§2) governs what can be bought. Citywide direction is the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (adopted December 9, 2025, replacing the 1998 OCP Bylaw); UniverCity is governed by its own OCP framework from the 1996 approvals. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan (Research file §4; townhomes sibling §3.)
- **SSMUH position.** The R1 district above IS Burnaby's SSMUH implementation — the duplex is the baseline form inside it, and the stratification permission for lots with two or more primary units is the legal mechanism by which a Burnaby strata duplex (a 2-lot strata half-duplex pair) comes to exist at all. No SSMUH unit count beyond the R1 figures above is asserted. (Townhomes sibling §3.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Burnaby's own position.** No Burnaby suite size cap is stated in this slug's research or siblings, so **NO cap figure is carried here** — Surrey's 40% of principal habitable area and every other city's figure are never borrowed. Suites inside the R1 frame count within the unit framework of the lot; the suite position for a specific home is UNCONFIRMED until read in the current Zoning Bylaw 2026 text and, for a strata unit, the strata's bylaws. (Research file; siblings per the FILE STATUS block.)
- **Leasehold diligence — the governing diligence inside UniverCity.** Read the ground lease before anything else: confirm the lease commencement date, remaining term, whether rent is prepaid for the full term, and any rent-review or end-of-term provisions — the lease, not the building, is what a buyer's lender and lawyer will scrutinize (structure per SFU Community Trust). Expect lender questions on leasehold — some lenders cap mortgage terms against the remaining lease term (general practice guidance in the research, flagged UNVERIFIED there). https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/ (Research file §6.)
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex, and in full to the leasehold strata developments cross-referenced in §2.** Form B diligence applies in full — on leasehold strata, the Form B is read alongside the ground lease. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — the counted UniverCity developments are INSIDE that cycle on lot count; **a 2-lot strata duplex, by contrast, is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone** — the distinction is stated precisely for both forms. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Inside UniverCity the buyer-side subdivision question does not arise at all — parcels were subdivided and serviced by SFU Community Trust before leasing (acreages sibling §2). - **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate; 5% GST applies to new construction (including an Arbour presale purchase), not resales. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy half a duplex at SFU?** Inside UniverCity, no — there is no fee-simple parcel to halve and no 2-lot strata duplex is verified there; every home is a strata unit on a 99-year prepaid SFU ground lease. At the sub-area's detached edge pockets, the duplex form class exists on single lots, but no individual duplex is verified by name in this program. (§2.)
- **Q: Are the UniverCity buildings freehold strata?** No — all 7 developments counted in the condos file (Crescent Court, The Nest, Origin, Altitude, Centre Block, The Hub, Terraces at the Peak) are 99-year prepaid **leasehold** strata. A buyer owns the strata lot and a lease interest, not the land — and none of them is duplex stock. (§2.)
- **Q: What should I read first on a UniverCity purchase?** The ground lease — commencement date, remaining term, prepaid status, rent-review and end-of-term provisions — then the Form B. The lease is what your lender and lawyer will scrutinize. (§3.)
- **Q: Does the gondola change what I should pay?** No — TransLink states the Burnaby Mountain Gondola is not yet approved or funded; treat it as upside, not as current transit, when pricing a unit. (§1.)
- **Q: What is the market doing on the mountain?** In September 2026 the sub-area recorded 91 Condos & Townhomes listings with 6 sales (7% ratio) and 5 detached listings with 0 sales — attached sellers compete against many near-identical units in the same few buildings. There is NO SFU sub-area median; the Burnaby community medians in §1 are community context only. (§1.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. For investors, verify the position building by building anyway; occupancy demand on the mountain is heavily student-driven (research guidance). (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count — unlike the large UniverCity developments, which are inside the cycle. (§3.)
- **Q: Is The Ambassador an SFU building I can compare against?** It is filed under this sub-area by MLS but sits in Montecito on the research geography and is counted in the montecito condos file — do not import it into an SFU comparison set. (§2.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Which Burnaby zoning bylaw is current?** Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815 — adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026. The former 1965 No. 4742 is superseded; any guide citing it for this ground is citing a repealed bylaw. (§3.)
