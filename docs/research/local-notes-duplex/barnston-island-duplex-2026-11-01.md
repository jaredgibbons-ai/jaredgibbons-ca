## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

Most of the island is Metro Vancouver Electoral Area A; the southeast portion is Barnston Island Indian Reserve No. 3 (Katzie First Nation), which is federal reserve land and not fee-simple market housing. ## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Barnston Island in Surrey?** No. It uses Surrey mailing addresses, but the island is unincorporated Metro Vancouver Electoral Area A plus Katzie IR 3. Services, taxes, permits and planning run through Metro Vancouver / the Province, not Surrey City Hall — and never price it off a Surrey market figure. (§1.)
- **Q: Can I buy a half duplex on Barnston Island?** No — none exists in any source. There is no strata product and no half-duplex subdivision on the island; the housing is farmhouses and rural single-detached homes on farm parcels. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a farm parcel hold two homes?** Only through the ALR frame: a principal residence (≤500 m²) with a suite within it, or an additional small residence (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size), via the routes in §3 — and those residences cannot be stratified or sold as halves. (§3.)
- **Q: How do residents get on and off the island?** Only by the free Barnston Island Ferry from 104 Ave in Port Kells — about 5 minutes across Parson's Channel, on demand within operating hours, capacity 5 vehicles. Ride it at a busy time before writing. (§1.)
- **Q: Where do island kids go to school?** SD36 (Surrey) serves the island — for the island test address 52 Dyke Road, Bothwell Elementary → École Fraser Heights (locator run 2026-11-01). Port Kells Elementary serves landing-side addresses, NOT island homes. (§5.)
- **Q: Is the island in a floodplain?** Yes — low Fraser River floodplain behind a perimeter dike that is also its main road. Confirm dike / flood construction and insurance early; parcel-level FCLs are UNVERIFIED. (§1.)
- **Q: Who collects the taxes?** The Province, under the rural Surveyor-of-Taxes system — not a city. Rural deadlines and the 5% + 5% penalties are in §3. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I buy on the reserve portion?** IR 3 lands are Katzie First Nation reserve lands with different tenure, financing and resale rules — outside the fee-simple market treatment in this file entirely. (§2.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on an island farm parcel?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3); mixed farm use is parcel-specific. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
