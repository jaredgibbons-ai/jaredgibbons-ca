## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning (Richmond frame).** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 governs; houses across the residential districts sit in the Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m² and a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones. The OCP governing is Bylaw 9000 (2041). RSM SSMUH zones sit in the bylaw as amended. The zone and subzone of a specific lot are UNCONFIRMED until read — verify the lot's subzone minimums in Bylaw 8500 Section 8.1 before promising redevelopment value. (Research file above; https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf, read 2026-11-01.)
- **SSMUH position (Richmond frame).** The City states it allows **at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot** on covered lots, with **Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots**. A duplex is the baseline two-unit form inside that ladder. SSMUH adds unit potential on a lot; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. Development cost charges for the ADEU form apply in **West Cambie / Alexandra ONLY** (program frame) — never stated city-wide. (Research file above; https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm, read 2026-11-01; brief chain.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the city's own rule (s.5.4).** A secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in the RS1/RS2 zones; the Section 5.4 rules are **at least 33.0 m², and at most the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m²**. No other city's suite figure is borrowed into a Richmond file. (Research file above; https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Flood position (Richmond frame).** **All of Richmond is designated flood plain** under the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204, which sets dike setbacks and flood construction level (FCL) requirements. The city is a low-lying island city in the Fraser River floodplain, protected by a perimeter dike system (49 km of dikes in the program frame) with drainage pump stations, designed for a 1:500 flood event, with dikes being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. Flood construction level and insurance position are first-round diligence on any Richmond duplex. (Research file above; https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML, read 2026-11-01; https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Servicing.** Richmond's servicing bylaws 8751/8752 are VERIFIED in the verified ledger. (Brief chain.)
- **Property taxes (Richmond frame).** City of Richmond 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. (Research file above; https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm, read 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums (§3 RS1/RS2 subzones); no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier — this figure GOVERNS in this file, against the townhomes sibling's $78,000 anchor flagged in §5**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
