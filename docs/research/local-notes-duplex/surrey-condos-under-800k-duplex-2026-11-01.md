## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Duplexes are a permitted two-unit form in Surrey's residential zones that allow a duplex under Zoning By-law No. 12000, and the provincial SSMUH implementation (Surrey, Jul 8, 2024) makes the duplex the baseline multi-unit form on eligible lots: a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. The OCP governing is Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is the replaced predecessor, never cited as current. The zone for a specific property is read off the City's current zoning for that lot. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated for Surrey. The City frames City Centre as a walkable, transit-oriented downtown core serviced by four SkyTrain stations, and home to City Hall, Simon Fraser University and Kwantlen Polytechnic University — the context for the Whalley-core attached pool in §1, not a duplex rule. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (read 2026-11-01); https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities (read 2026-11-01.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Surrey's own rule.** One secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area** under Zoning By-law No. 12000, and a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite — Surrey's rule, never borrowed. (Research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite, read 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) seen through any feed, and to every condo in this band.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing and STR.** Surrey's servicing position is Bylaw 8830 (1986), VERIFIED from the City's own pages in the verified ledger. Short-term rentals in Surrey: a short-term rental is the renting of a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, rentals cannot be run by tenants, an apartment unit with one lock-off suite (if applicable) is a permitted housing type, and the owner must first obtain a business licence at $358 per year plus any application fees — the $358/yr figure is carried with the City's 2024 $350/yr proposal disclosed. Strata bylaws are a separate, building-level check on top of the City rule. (Research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Transit context, as sourced.** TransLink states the Province is delivering a 16-kilometre Expo Line extension on an elevated guideway along Fraser Highway from King George Station to 203 Street in Langley City, with an anticipated in-service date of 2029. The live page's list of Surrey's current Expo Line stations (Scott Road, Gateway, Surrey Central and King George) was not confirmed station-by-station on a TransLink page in the research pass and is carried as live-page copy only: UNVERIFIED on an official source in this file. (Research file above; https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/surrey-langley-skytrain, read 2026-11-01.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. A purchase in this band pays only the 1% and 2% tiers. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $600,000 → **$10,000** (a qualifying first-time buyer on the same $600,000 purchase, exempt on the first $500,000, pays 2% × $100,000 = **$2,000** on those inputs); $800,000 (the band ceiling) → **$14,000**; and the program anchors above the band: $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; **$3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier** — $93,000 governs at $3.5M (see §5). A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Because this band caps at $800,000, a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in the band sits under the first time home buyers' program's full-exemption value ceiling of $835,000 (exempting the first $500,000 of price, partial only above $835,000 and below $860,000, subject to the program's other conditions — principal residence; property 0.5 hectares or smaller); the newly built home exemption's full threshold is $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (read 2026-11-01); https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers (read 2026-11-01.)
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale condo, duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate; in this band GST applies to developer sales and presale contracts, not the resale stock. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: Can I run an Airbnb in a Surrey condo I buy under $800K?** Only within the City's short-term rental rules: the host must be the owner, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot operate one, an apartment unit with one lock-off suite is a permitted type, and a business licence ($358 per year, $350 conflict disclosed) is required first — strata bylaws are a separate, building-level check. (§3.) https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). For a condo in this band the same documents — not the listing or the site's FAQ copy — decide fees, levies and pet / rental rules. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
