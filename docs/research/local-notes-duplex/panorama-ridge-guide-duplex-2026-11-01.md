## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. Surrey's own semi-detached / zero-lot-line forms, where a source documents them for a specific parcel, are read from that parcel's zone — no such parcel is documented for this slug. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan (Surrey frame):** land use now follows the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026), and Zoning Bylaw 12000. The South Newton NCP was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update and is no longer in effect — a seller quoting NCP densities (including the City-endorsed update with density uplift along Colebrook Road) is quoting a dead document. (Research file, §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton)
- **Zoning, SSMUH and suites (Surrey frame — SETTLED):** Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs, R-series zones with RA at the rural edges (R3 minimum subdivision lot area 660 m² in this plan area, §2). SSMUH (adopted July 8, 2024): a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — duplexes are the baseline SSMUH form. **Secondary suite inside a duplex: Surrey permits one secondary suite per single-family/duplex dwelling, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area — Surrey's own cap, never borrowed.** Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) is VERIFIED. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is ever stated for Surrey ground; the plan area includes fish-bearing creeks (Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries), so riparian rules can apply to creek-side lots, and unpermitted existing suites are not legalized by the new rules. (Research file, §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing; guide townhomes sibling, §3.)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey frame):** STRs are owner-only, with a licence of $358/yr carried with the City's 2024 $350/yr proposal disclosed — both figures stated, neither silently chosen. (Settled Surrey frame, D1 brief.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Surrey ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process under Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986), and the zone minimums in §2 decide whether a Panorama Ridge lot can split at all. No fee-simple half-duplex subdivision is verified on this ground. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file, §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
