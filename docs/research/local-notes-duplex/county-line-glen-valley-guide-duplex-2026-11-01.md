## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. In the Township, a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata, never a half duplex. A secondary suite inside a principal residence is not a duplex and not a half duplex. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **ALR frame (governs any parcel in the Agricultural Land Reserve — the first check on this sub-area's acreage stock):** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel; a principal residence may be up to 500 m² total floor area without ALC application; one additional detached residence may be permitted of up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence and only where local bylaws allow it; local governments may further restrict residences through zoning. Whether any specific parcel in this feed is in the ALR is UNVERIFIED — parcel-specific on ALC mapping, not checked parcel by parcel in this program. (Research file, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **Zoning, SSMUH and suites (Township frame — stated so it is not misapplied to this sub-area):** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs. SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) allows **3 units on eligible lots of 280 m² or smaller and 4 units on larger eligible lots up to 4,050 m²**, subject to eligibility (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area); **lots over 4,050 m² are not eligible, and NO 6-unit tier applies in the Township**. Most parcels in this feed exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by a wide margin, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them — do not apply SSMUH duplex math to Glen Valley acreages. Secondary suites: under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s.102 (as amended July 2025), a secondary suite may not exceed **120 m²** maximum gross floor area, each principal dwelling unit may have up to one secondary suite, and on ALR lands a suite must comply with Agricultural Land Commission regulations. **Live conflict, reported with dates and not resolved:** the Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states suites are NOT permitted in townhouses/rowhouses and describes suites as within detached single-family dwellings, while the current s.102 definition (July 2025) says a suite may be located in a townhouse, rowhouse or manorhome — the bylaw definition is the later source. Coach houses: in s.102, a "coach house" is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage — a fee-simple second unit, never strata; no size cap for a coach house appears in s.100 as read in the program's rules-gaps pass, so a coach-house size cap is UNVERIFIED in this file. Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 is CONFIRMED. (Research file, §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx)
- **Short-term rentals (Township):** the Township does not issue business licences for short-term rental accommodations (Township licence-application page, as recorded in the rules-gaps pass); the permitted-use status of STRs under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 was not readable on a Township page in that pass — STR status in the Township is UNVERIFIED beyond the licensing point. - **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Township ground, if one is ever met here.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Township's Approving Officer process under Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. No invented process detail is stated. - **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Farm / ALR parcels can carry mixed-use apportionment questions the anchors do not model — have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
