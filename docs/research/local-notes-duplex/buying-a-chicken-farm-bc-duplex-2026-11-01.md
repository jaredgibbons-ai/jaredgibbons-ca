## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. On ALR land the strata route is further constrained: strata plans in the ALR cause subdivision per ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6, and subdivision of ALR land generally requires ALC approval — which is why this program's condos sibling for this slug counts 0 apartment condos as a structural position. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; research file, §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **ALR housing frame (province-wide, ALC — the frame a farm purchase actually turns on):** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may further regulate or restrict residences, and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. Farm-residence forms are carried ONLY as ALR sources support (ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 + the Non-Adhering Residential Use route); a general entitlement beyond that frame is UNCONFIRMED. Subdivision of ALR land generally requires ALC approval, and strata plans in the ALR cause subdivision per ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6 — relevant to any guide reader assuming a farm parcel can be split for a second homesite or stratified into halves. Whether a specific parcel is in the ALR, its zoning, servicing and floodplain position, and its farm-class status are UNVERIFIED at parcel level — each is a per-parcel check with the applicable local government, the ALC, BC Assessment and title. (Research file, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **BC Assessment farm classification (province-wide):** farm classification is a voluntary program; the income thresholds are $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha; applications must be submitted to BC Assessment by October 31 for consideration for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. The farm-class position of BC chicken farms as a group is UNVERIFIED — classification is property-by-property. (Research file, §4; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification)
- **STRATA block — applies where any two-unit farm or rural building a buyer is looking at is in fact a strata duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. On ALR land, the ALC subdivision position above governs whether a strata form can exist at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the applicable municipality's Approving Officer process, and on ALR land ALC approval as above — no invented process detail. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file, §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax (general tiers, carried from the sibling files): 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. **How PTT and GST are apportioned between land, buildings, residence and quota on a specific chicken-farm purchase is UNVERIFIED in this file — no Province farm-transfer tax source was opened in this program; have an agricultural lawyer and accountant model it before an offer is written.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only; how GST is apportioned across a farm purchase's parts is UNVERIFIED here (above). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
