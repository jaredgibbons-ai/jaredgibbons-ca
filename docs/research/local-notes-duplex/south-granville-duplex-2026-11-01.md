## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Vancouver Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, R1-1 district).** The R1-1 District Schedule sets a minimum site area of 306 m² for a single detached house or a duplex and for a 3–4-unit multiple dwelling, 464 m² for 5 units and 557 m² for 6 to 8 units; a multiplex may have up to 6 strata units or up to 8 secured rental units, and a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house. The Broadway Plan is a policy framework, not zoning: most higher-density forms near the future South Granville Station still require rezoning, so no lot is priced here as if tower density were already zoned. Heritage and character-home retention incentives may also apply to a given lot — the zone, plan policy and heritage status of a specific lot are UNCONFIRMED here beyond that read. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf and https://syc.vancouver.ca/projects/broadway-plan/broadway-plan-summary-highlight-document.pdf
- **SSMUH position.** Vancouver's R1-1 multiplex framework (By-law No. 13817) is the city's small-scale multi-unit housing implementation on former single-detached lots, with the duplex as the baseline two-unit form inside it; station-area density in this corridor runs through the Broadway Plan and rezoning, not through the multiplex framework. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Vancouver's own position only.** The R1-1 schedule permits a secondary suite (and, for a house, a laneway house) as the schedule states it; the research notes many side-street character homes here are converted or suited. No suite size cap from any other city is borrowed, and no Vancouver cap figure is asserted here beyond the schedule's own text. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Renter protections.** Purpose-built rental buildings in the Broadway Plan area carry tenant-relocation implications under the plan's renter protections — verify current tenant status and the plan's protections before an investor purchase. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx (Research file §6.)
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex (and in full to FORM, Pintura and Terraces on 7th at their count homes).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**, stated precisely. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — Terraces on 7th's published 55+ restriction is that one legal form, at its Fairview count home; a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** Vancouver's Subdivision By-law No. 5208 is VERIFIED in the program ledger (fees under By-law No. 14538): a fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval under that bylaw; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the titles — read them; for a heritage conversion like the Bayne Residence, the heritage designation and any heritage revitalization agreement on title govern alongside. (Program discipline, D1 brief; verified ledger; townhomes sibling §2.)
- **Taxes and levies (citywide Vancouver).** Advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance. The City's Empty Homes Tax charges 3% of the property's assessed taxable value on homes declared or deemed vacant — **3%, VANCOUVER ONLY** — with the 2025 reference-year payment due April 16, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
