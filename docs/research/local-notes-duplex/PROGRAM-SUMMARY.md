## The format (settled in the D1 brief, held program-wide)

- **Definitions first in every file.** A DUPLEX = one building, two dwelling units. A HALF DUPLEX = one of the two units sold separately, in BC only as (a) a 2-lot STRATA duplex (Form B diligence) or (b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex (subdivided parcel / party-wall form). A fee-simple subdivision of detached lots is not, by itself, a half-duplex subdivision.
- **Count-once.** Duplex-form components inside townhome-counted complexes are documented in the duplex file and counted in their townhomes home; duplex files import NO count and create no second count. The two-lot strata nuance is stated precisely: the BC depreciation-report rule (5-year cycle) applies to stratas with **5 or more lots** since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that threshold** — while the minimum 10% CRF contribution (since November 1, 2023) and the Form B diligence apply regardless. Strata block held everywhere: Form B (SPA s.59); rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01 + the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+.
- **PTT followed at the tier anchors:** $500,000 → $8,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $2.0M → $38,000; **$3,500,000 → $93,000** (including the further 2% tier over $3M). First-Time Home Buyers' = new **HOUSING** only (the FTHB $6,000 figure is stated with its newly-built framing wherever used). FTHB GST rebate: **Bill C-4 Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA** — every "March 12" formulation met in any sibling was flagged, never followed.
- **City frames settled once and cited per file** (SSMUH suite/coach caps, servicing numbers where verified, school-district catchment positions carried as their own research/siblings state them, SnapStats used only as labelled community context where the named community is covered — duplex has no SnapStats category).
- **Local-notes programs used as sources throughout** — 55-plus, condos, townhomes, lots-and-land, acreages and mobile-homes home files follow the area (cross-reference, never recount); nearby files were checked for the D19–D21 slugs (FOUND for all but `how-to-buy-a-mobile-home-in-bc-guide`).
