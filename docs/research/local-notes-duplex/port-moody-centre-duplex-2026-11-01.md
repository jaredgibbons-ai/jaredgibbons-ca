## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages, which the research itself cites for the point that strata housing includes duplexes: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Port Moody Zoning Bylaw, 2018, No. 2937).** Single-detached homes sit in RS zones, multi-residential homes in RT and RM zones, and mixed-use development in TC and CRM zones; apartment and mixed-use buildings are concentrated in the Moody Centre and Inlet Centre transit-oriented areas. The exact zone for a specific lot is read off the current Bylaw 2937 text for that lot — entitlements asserted beyond that read are UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/ (Research file §4.)
- **SSMUH position — the station-area exemption governs here.** The transit-oriented areas are **exempt** from small-scale multi-unit housing rules — an RS lot inside a TOA does not gain SSMUH units — while RS lots outside the TOAs can add units (Port Moody's implementation permits up to four units on lots from 280 m² to 4,050 m² in RS and RT zones outside the TOAs, and up to six within 400 m of frequent bus stops, per the sibling record of Bylaw Amendment No. 94, 2024, No. 3460). The duplex is the baseline two-unit form inside that outside-TOA permission. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/ (Research file §6; lots-and-land sibling §3.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Port Moody's own rule only.** Secondary suites are allowed in single-detached zones (except manufactured-home parks), and a secondary suite may not be stratified. No Port Moody suite size cap is stated in this ground's research or siblings, so NO cap figure is carried here — no other city's figure is borrowed. The suite position for a specific home is UNCONFIRMED until read in the current zoning text and, for a strata unit, the strata's bylaws. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/building-and-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx (Lots-and-land sibling §3.)
- **Small-lot subdivision.** In Moody Centre, RS1 lots in OCP Single Family–Low Density areas can be **rezoned** to RS1-S for small lots of at least 9 m width and 325 m² area — a rezoning route, not an as-of-right split. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/ (Research file §4.)
- **Heritage — Moody Centre.** Moody Centre is a heritage conservation area (with the Ioco townsite), so a heritage alteration permit can be required for new construction or reconstruction on included properties — check before planning a duplex conversion on an older side-street home. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/ (Research file §4.)
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex (and in full to the centre's strata towers and complexes).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex, by contrast, is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, and in Moody Centre the small-lot route is the RS1-S rezoning above; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the titles — read them. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** The Port Moody servicing bylaw number is **UNCONFIRMED** — no number is quoted and no other city's bylaw is borrowed; servicing for a specific parcel is confirmed with the City. Utilities (water, sewer, waste) are billed separately from property taxes, the 2026 utility bill due February 27, 2026. https://www.portmoody.ca/utilities (Verified ledger; research file §4.)
- **Property taxes (city-wide Port Moody rule, research):** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding amounts after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/property-taxes/
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: Can I split a Moody Centre lot into small lots?** Only by rezoning: RS1 lots in OCP Single Family–Low Density areas can be rezoned to RS1-S (minimum 9 m width, 325 m²) — an application, not an as-of-right split. (§3.)

- **Q: What is the heritage catch on an older home here?** Moody Centre is a heritage conservation area — included properties can need a heritage alteration permit for construction or reconstruction. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** Not on lot count: the 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, and a 2-lot strata is below that threshold. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: What changes when Inlet District completes?** On the developer source in the research, six towers and three low-rise buildings with about 2,500 homes, a supermarket, drug store, daycares and a park, first residents expected around 2029 — new supply beside Inlet Centre Station, not duplex stock.
