## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Duplexes are a permitted two-unit form in Surrey's residential zones that allow a duplex under Zoning By-law No. 12000, and the provincial SSMUH implementation (Surrey, Jul 8, 2024) makes the duplex the baseline multi-unit form on eligible lots: a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, applying to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. Homes fronting the boulevard's frequent bus service may fall inside a Frequent Bus Stop Area — check the parcel in COSMOS. The OCP governing is Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is the replaced predecessor, never cited as current. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated for Surrey; the corridor's north edge is the Nicomekl River and Surrey's coastal floodplain runs along the Nicomekl and Serpentine Rivers, with parcel-level flood levels UNVERIFIED and confirmed with the City per parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Surrey's own rule.** One secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area** under Zoning By-law No. 12000, and never inside a coach house or garden suite — Surrey's rule, never borrowed. A coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Surrey's servicing position is Bylaw 8830 (1986), VERIFIED from the City's own pages in the verified ledger. Short-term rentals in Surrey are owner-only with a licence figure carried as $358/yr, with the City's 2024 $350/yr proposal disclosed — no STR figure is relied on in this file. City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. (Verified ledger, brief chain; research file above.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; **$3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = $93,000 including the further 2% tier** — $93,000 governs at $3.5M. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031 — relevant to remaining developer inventory at 2025 completions in the corridor set, per the condos sibling, and to no resale. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is the King George Corridor the whole of King George Boulevard?** No — not on this page, in the City plan or in the board data. The plan area and sub-area run from 8 Avenue to the Nicomekl River in South Surrey; the boulevard continues north through Newton to City Centre (Whalley), but those stretches sit in other sub-areas with other stock. (§1.)
- **Q: Is there a named duplex development on the corridor I can buy into?** None is verified in this slug's research or siblings — the corridor's duplex product is the individual-lot form class (boulevard-fronting duplex and fourplex-style homes), and its counted named stock is the condos sibling's 10 apartment-condo complexes, which are condo stock, not duplex stock. (§2.)
- **Q: A listing shows a half duplex on the boulevard — can I buy just that half?** Only if that half is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot. The listing settles nothing; check the title. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a house lot in the corridor add units?** Under Surrey's SSMUH framework, most eligible lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary can carry 3 units (under 280 m²) or 4 units (280 m² or more), and eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop can carry up to 6 — boulevard-adjacent lots may qualify, so check the parcel's zone and Frequent Bus Stop Area status in COSMOS. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a Surrey duplex?** Surrey permits one secondary suite in a duplex dwelling capped at 40% of the principal unit's habitable area, where the zoning applies. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay on an $800,000 corridor home?** $14,000 with no exemption — $2,000 (1% on the first $200,000) + $12,000 (2% × $600,000). Tier math in §3. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Is the BRT a current service I can price in?** No — it is in full design with 12 stations planned City Centre to Semiahmoo Town Centre; station locations and construction timing for a specific corridor address were not confirmed in the research. (§1.)
