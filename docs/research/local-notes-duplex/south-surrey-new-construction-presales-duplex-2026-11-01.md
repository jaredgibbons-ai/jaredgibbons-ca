## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A presale contract itself is not a strata lot — it is a developer contract under REDMA (§3) until completion and strata-plan registration. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing; presale-contract position: townhomes sibling, ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/south-surrey-new-construction-presales-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §2.5.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **REDMA block — core to this file (stated from the Act itself, as the townhomes sibling states it):** **REDMA s.21 — 7-day rescission:** a presale buyer may rescind by written notice within 7 days of the later of signing the purchase agreement and acknowledging receipt of the disclosure statement. **REDMA s.18 — deposits in trust:** purchase deposits must be held in trust as the Act requires. The disclosure statement — not the listing card, not the presentation centre — governs completion dates, deposits and what is being bought; have a lawyer read it inside the 7-day window. The live page's own presale summary (disclosure before signing, 7 days to cancel, deposits "usually 10%–20% total, staged over construction and held in trust", a 2-5-10 warranty of 2 years materials and labour / 5 years building envelope / 10 years structure, the Newly Built Home Exemption thresholds and the GST rebate thresholds, attributed there to "BC Financial Services Authority, Presales Information") is carried in the research as site copy, UNVERIFIED on an official source in that pass — the s.21/s.18 frame above is stated from the Act, and every other figure in this section is stated from its own official source, not from the page. (Townhomes sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/south-surrey-new-construction-presales-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §3; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01; research file, §4.)
- **The regulator:** in BC, real estate trading services are regulated by BCFSA, which states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. The live page states the developer pays the buyer's agent's fees and that registration order matters for representation — register with your own agent before a first presentation-centre visit. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers (research file, §§4, 6)
- **Plan, zoning, SSMUH and suites (Surrey frame — Surrey ground only; White Rock addresses answer to White Rock's own bylaws, Zoning Bylaw 2024 No. 2506 / OCP Bylaw 2017 No. 2220 per the 55-plus sibling, and no Surrey rule is asserted for a White Rock parcel):** the current Surrey plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is replaced and is NEVER cited as current. - **STRATA block — applies once a presale project completes and registers, and to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full (a presale has no Form B until the strata exists — the REDMA disclosure statement is the presale equivalent). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. - **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. For a qualifying new principal residence, the page states (site copy) the BC Newly Built Home Exemption — no PTT up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — the exemption most presale buyers here will test against; verify it against the Province for your own purchase. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only — directly relevant to every true presale in §2.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales — a nearly-new resale in this feed carries no GST, and a resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
