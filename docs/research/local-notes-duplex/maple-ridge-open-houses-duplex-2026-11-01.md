## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

For this topic file, this section carries the open-house frame first, then the duplex-purchase frame a buyer needs after the open house — because the open house is where a Maple Ridge duplex viewing starts, and title and the strata documents are where it is actually diligenced.

### The open-house frame

- **The regulator:** in British Columbia, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), and the Province's Buying and selling a home page directs prospective purchasers to BCFSA for information on purchasing property in British Columbia. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/real-estate-bc/buying-selling/buying-and-selling-home (Research file above.)
- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), carried verbatim:** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, states that the disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download (Research file above.)
- **Representation at an open house:** a licensed real estate professional in BC must work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party to the transaction. In practice: chatting factually with the host at an open house does not by itself make you their client — but once you start sharing your motivation, budget or needs with a licensee who represents the seller, the disclosure rules engage. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers (Research file above.)
- **Identification or sign-in for open-house visitors:** NOT stated in any BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source found — UNVERIFIED as a rule; a host may still ask, as a matter of the seller's instructions, but that practice is not in an official source. (Research file above.)

### The duplex-purchase frame (after the open house)

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019 governs; the standard urban house zone in the program's Maple Ridge research is RS-1 (Single Detached Residential), s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², minimum lot width 18.0 m, minimum lot depth 27.0 m, maximum lot coverage 40%. The zone of a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until read. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading (City frame, D7 brief.)
- **City plan.** The OCP citation is **CONTESTED (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024)** — flagged here and wherever met, resolved nowhere. Settled plan facts: the **Albion Area Plan was adopted October 28, 2025**, and the **Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was ADOPTED January 27, 2026** (OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021) per the City record — the corridor ends at 221 Street and does NOT reach Whonnock; any reading that carries the corridor to Whonnock, or treats the plan as unadopted, is flagged wherever met and never carried. No contrary Lougheed reading is documented in this slug's own research file, which does not address the corridor plan. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP (Siblings above; D7 brief frame.)
- **SSMUH position.** Eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws — a duplex is the baseline two-unit form. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new (Siblings above.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the city's own rule.** One secondary suite per lot, contained within the single detached building, to a maximum of 40% of gross floor area, and NOT within the Floodplain Area; Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. No size figure is borrowed from another city. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites (Siblings above.)
- **Floodplain — a Maple Ridge-specific check on any rural-edge open house:** properties along the Fraser River frontage sit in a floodplain where the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, and no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0 (Research file above.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full (Strata Property Act s. 59; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums and the servicing bylaw below; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Maple Ridge's Subdivision Servicing Bylaw 4800-1993 is carried ONLY from a City source in the program ledger — no finer servicing standard is quoted for any parcel from a listing or summary. (D7 brief frame.)
- **Property taxes (Maple Ridge).** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments (Siblings above.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. The first-time home buyers' program exempts PTT on the first $500,000 at a fair market value of $835,000 or less (partial to $860,000), and the Newly Built Home Exemption runs full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
