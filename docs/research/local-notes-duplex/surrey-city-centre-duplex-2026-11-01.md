## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000).** Surrey's SSMUH amendments renamed the former RF zone to R3 Urban Residential Zone (Part 15) and the former RF-SD zone to **R6 Semi-Detached Residential Zone (Part 18)**; the former RM-D Duplex Residential Zone was removed and grouped by lot size — the current zones are R3 and R6. Permitted small-scale multi-unit forms are secondary suites, detached accessory dwelling units including garden suites and coach houses, duplexes (side-by-side or up-and-down) and houseplexes (triplexes and fourplexes). In R6, a zero-lot line semi-detached building on a lot under 280 sq. m may accommodate 2 to 3 units and a duplex on a lot over 280 sq. m may accommodate 4 to 6 units; R6 subdivision minimums are interior lot area 200 sq. m, width 7.2 m, depth 28 m and corner lot area 226 sq. m, width 8.7 m, depth 28 m, with a 0.0 m side yard permitted at the common side lot line between paired lots and maximum lot coverage of 60 percent. Inside the downtown core itself, the City Centre Plan's high-density mixed-use designations govern form — the R3/R6 duplex frame applies to the surrounding Whalley blocks, not to a tower site. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH position.** Surrey's SSMUH changes took effect July 8, 2024: a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — on eligible residential lots. The OCP governing is Surrey 2050, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026); Bylaw No. 18020 is the replaced predecessor and is never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Surrey's own rule.** One secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, one per unit, not a separate strata lot, and never inside a coach house or garden suite. The "90 m² cap" sometimes quoted is NOT current (condos sibling §3). This is Surrey's OWN cap — carried here because this is a Surrey file, and never exported to another city's file. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex, and in full to the core's towers.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — the core's towers are INSIDE that cycle on lot count; **a 2-lot strata duplex, by contrast, is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone** — the distinction is stated precisely for both forms. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. Surrey's documented R6 zero-lot-line mechanism is stated in the zoning line above. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing and charges.** Surrey servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED in the verified ledger. On the development side: City Centre projects seeking bonus density paid Community Amenity Contributions under Surrey's two-tier density-bonus framework — and the regime changed mid-2026: the City states CACs were superseded by its Amenity Cost Charge and bonus density provisions as of June 15, 2026, with the CAC page kept as a historic reference for eligible in-stream applications. Exact per-file CAC/ACC/DCC amounts are application-specific — UNVERIFIED as general figures. Surrey's new (October 2025) rental replacement policy affects redevelopment math on the 1970s rental stock. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/community-amenity-contributions (Research file §4.)
- **Property taxes (City of Surrey):** due July 2 (notices sent end of May; if the due date falls on a weekend or statutory holiday, payment the next business day is without penalty), with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes (Research file §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate — and note the edge case the townhomes sibling flags for this core: a never-occupied unit in a 2025-completed tower can still be a developer sale, so establish per unit whether the sale is a developer sale or a resale (5% GST applies to new construction, not resales). GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is "Surrey City Centre" the same as Whalley?** City Centre is the downtown core inside the larger Whalley community. The City lists it as "City Centre (Whalley)," one of six Surrey communities, while the board's sub-area name for the whole area is Whalley — file your search under "Whalley," because a "City Centre" search term will miss inventory. (§1.)
- **Q: Can I buy half a duplex in downtown Surrey?** None is verified by name in the core. The core's verified stock is strata towers (11 buildings counted in the condos file); the duplex form here is the individual-duplex form class on the older detached Whalley blocks at the core's edges. (§2.)
- **Q: One Central has townhomes — are those duplexes I can buy separately?** They are townhouse-form units inside a 44-storey apartment strata, counted once in the condos file as part of that tower — not duplexes, not a separate townhouse complex, and not counted again anywhere. Same rule for Park George's townhome units. (§2.)
- **Q: What is "Surrey Half Duplexes"?** The Paragon Surrey half-duplex grouping — the board-side grouping under which Surrey half-duplex listings are organized (Jared's ruling). It is a board grouping, not a neighbourhood and not a City designation; this slug's own research and siblings do not use the string, so treat any feed filed under it as board data to verify by street and title, exactly as in §2. (FILE STATUS; §2.)
- **Q: How deep is this market?** Whalley's 635 attached listings in September 2026 are the deepest attached inventory of any Surrey sub-area — sellers compete with new-build launches, so presentation and pricing discipline matter more here than almost anywhere else in the city. There is NO City-Centre-only market cut. (§1.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count — unlike the core's towers, which are inside the cycle. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (Surrey's rental replacement policy is a redevelopment rule, not a strata rental rule — do not conflate them.) (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a duplex on a Whalley block?** Surrey's rule applies: one suite in a semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area, never in a coach/garden suite — the "90 m² cap" sometimes quoted is not current. Confirm per lot. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a City Centre address?** Per exact address — the one documented run (13367 97 Ave) returns A. H. P. Matthew Elementary / Queen Elizabeth Secondary; École Kwantlen Park Secondary is located in-area but is not that address's catchment. (§5.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
