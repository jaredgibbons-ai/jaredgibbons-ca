## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Community plan.** The Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, is the City's current city-wide plan, and its Development Permit Areas are administered by the City's Land Development Department. (Research file above, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881)
- **Zoning.** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000. The hillside residential zones include the HR (Hillside Residential) Zone — minimum lot area 0.2 ha where served by a community water system and 0.4 ha for all other uses, minimum lot width 25 m — and the SHR (Suburban Hillside Residential) Zone, whose minimum lot area is set by the zone's slope table, starting at 360 m² for the lowest slope band and rising to 990 m² for the highest. Which zone applies to a specific Little Mountain lot is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. No zone in this slug's research is stated as a duplex-specific zone list, so no duplex permission table beyond the form class in §2 is carried here. (Research file above, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf)
- **Hillside development rules — the central fact for this area.** Development Permit Area 2 (Hillside and Upland Areas) protects developments from hazardous hillside conditions and environmentally sensitive areas, and a Geotechnical Report from a qualified professional is required with each application; Development Permit Area 5 (Hillside Development) applies to all lands in the City with slopes of 20% or more, and within a designated DPA a Development Permit is required before subdivision, construction, addition or alteration of a building, or alteration of land, except where exemptions apply. A duplex build, addition or fee-simple split on a sloped hill lot runs through this framework first. (Research file above, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881)
- **SSMUH position.** Chilliwack's SSMUH bylaw is Bylaw 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024) — an urban residential-lot framework; duplexes are the baseline two-unit form on eligible lots. SSMUH does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves, and on hillside lots the DPA 2 / DPA 5 framework above governs development regardless. (Brief chain; mobile-homes sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/mobile-homes/little-mountain-mobile-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Chilliwack's own position as its sources state it.** No Chilliwack percentage or size cap for a suite inside a duplex is stated in this slug's research or siblings, so no figure is borrowed from another city and the cap is UNCONFIRMED here. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. (For the cross-referenced Meadows, the lot count is UNCONFIRMED, so whether it sits over the 5-lot threshold is confirmed on its strata plan.) Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support, with the 3055 conflict carried OPEN.** Subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer / Preliminary Layout Approval process per the City's Subdivision Application Guide, with a Development Permit where a DPA applies. **Chilliwack's servicing (Land Development) bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger: the ledger holds "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" as NOT adopted, while the City's own subdivisions webpage asserts that bylaw name/number. (Acreages sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/acreages/little-mountain-acreages-2026-11-01.md; mobile-homes sibling above.)
- **Property taxes (Chilliwack frame).** Property tax notices are due the first working day after July 1 each year, with a 5% late payment penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% on taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026; separate rates are established for each property class, and additional taxes are levied for drainage and dyking on top of the municipal rates. (Research file above, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527)
- **Flood:** the hill itself is upland; the City's Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets minimum building setbacks of 60 m from the Fraser River, 75 m from the Vedder River and 30 m from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal, which matter for base-of-hill and Hope River-side lots rather than the hilltop. Parcel-level flood status: UNVERIFIED. (Research file above, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Little Mountain Meadows a duplex complex I can buy half of?** No — it is a built freehold strata townhouse complex (9855 Quarry Rd, LMS1998) counted once in the little-mountain townhomes file. Its units are townhouse strata lots, not duplex halves, and its build year (1995 vs 1997) is UNCONFIRMED. (§2.)
- **Q: Is there any named duplex development on Little Mountain?** None is verified in this slug's research or siblings — §2 is the form class (individual duplexes on single hillside lots under the zoning of their day) plus the townhouse cross-reference. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Little Mountain the same as Chilliwack Mountain?** No — Chilliwack Mountain is a separate hillside community on the south side of the Fraser River, west of downtown; its listings, schools and hazard rules are not this area's. (§1.)
- **Q: What hillside rules apply to building or subdividing here?** DPA 2 requires a Geotechnical Report from a qualified professional with each application, and DPA 5 applies to all City lands with slopes of 20% or more; a Development Permit is required before subdivision, construction or land alteration in a designated area, except where exemptions apply. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for Little Mountain?** No — Chilliwack is not covered by SnapStats, CADREB publishes no area stats in this program, and duplexes have no separate published category. No market figure is stated in this file. (§1.)
