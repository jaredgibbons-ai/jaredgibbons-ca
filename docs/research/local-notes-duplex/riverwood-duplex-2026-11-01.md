## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Port Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw No. 3630).** Single-detached homes sit under RS1, RS2, RS3 and RS4 zones, and townhouse zones RTh1, RTh2 and RTh3 exist in the bylaw. The exact zone and unit count for a specific lot are read off the current Bylaw 3630 text for that lot — entitlements asserted beyond that read are UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2025-08/3630%20-%20Zoning%20Bylaw%202008.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **SSMUH position.** The City permits 3 to 6 small-scale multi-unit housing units on RS-zoned lots without rezoning, depending on lot size, location and transit proximity; the duplex is the baseline two-unit form inside that permission. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing (Research file §4.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Port Coquitlam's own rule only.** The City permits secondary suites only in the RS-1, RS-2, RS-3 and RS-4 zones, and a building permit is required; the City waives 50% of the extra suite utility levy when the suite is occupied by a parent or grandparent, and waives it entirely when a paid caregiver lives in the suite to care for a permanently disabled owner or family member. No Port Coquitlam suite size cap is stated in this ground's research or siblings, so NO cap figure is carried here — **Surrey's 40%-of-principal-dwelling cap does NOT apply here and is never borrowed** (townhomes sibling, stated expressly). https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing (Townhomes sibling §3.)
- **Flood.** Land toward the Pitt River is low-lying, and a significant portion of the city is within the Fraser and Pitt River floodplain behind dykes; lot-level flood status for Riverwood is UNVERIFIED — confirm the specific lot with the City. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/port-coquitlam-reminds-residents-prepare-possible-flooding (Research file §4.)
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex (and in full to the town-home strata and Fremont Green).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex, by contrast, is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 (the directory line for Mountain View Estates recording rentals "allowed with restrictions" carries a VOID rental element on this rule). Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the RS zone minimum widths above; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the titles — read them. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** The Port Coquitlam servicing bylaw number is **UNCONFIRMED** — no number is quoted and no other city's bylaw is borrowed; servicing for a specific parcel is confirmed with the City. (Verified ledger; mobile-homes sibling FILE STATUS.)
- **Property taxes (city-wide Port Coquitlam rule, research):** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes unpaid after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026; utility bills are flat annual water and sewer fees, billed separately and due March 31, 2026; the City states it has the lowest average property taxes and utility fees in Metro Vancouver and its 2026 increase was 1.95% for the average home. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes (Research file §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Riverwood in Surrey?** No — Riverwood is in Port Coquitlam, under Port Coquitlam's OCP Bylaw 3838 and Zoning Bylaw 3630. No Surrey rule (including Surrey's suite cap) applies to a Riverwood lot. (§1.)
- **Q: Is there a duplex complex in Riverwood I can buy into?** No — none is verified by name. The duplex form here is individual two-unit homes on RS lots under the 3-to-6-unit SSMUH permission; the area's verified attached stock is 3 townhouse complexes plus one condo building. (§2.)
- **Q: Can an older Riverwood house lot hold more homes?** RS-zoned lots can hold 3 to 6 small-scale multi-unit housing units without rezoning, depending on lot size, location and transit proximity, plus a secondary suite in RS-1 to RS-4 (building permit required). (§3.)
- **Q: Is there a SkyTrain line coming to Port Coquitlam?** Not a funded line: TransLink's commitment is only an "exploratory business case" for a Millennium Line extension to PoCo, still "underway" in the Mayors' Council's February 19, 2026 work plan — no route, stations or construction funding approved. Do not price a SkyTrain premium on it. (§1.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** Not on lot count: the 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, and a 2-lot strata is below that threshold. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: What is the flood question on the river side?** Land toward the Pitt River is low-lying and much of the city is floodplain behind dykes — lot-level status for Riverwood is UNVERIFIED, so confirm the specific lot with the City. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a suite in a duplex get a utility-levy break?** The City waives half the extra suite utility levy for a parent or grandparent occupant, and all of it for a paid caregiver caring for a permanently disabled owner or family member. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Where is the closest hospital?** Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody (Fraser Health). (§5.)
