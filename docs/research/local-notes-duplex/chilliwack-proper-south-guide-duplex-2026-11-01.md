## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan (Chilliwack frame):** land use sits under the City's 2050 Official Community Plan (Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500), whose Schedule B2 is the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan; the plan was first added to the 2040 OCP as Schedule D through Bylaw 2018, No. 4533, with the June 5, 2018 public hearing notice stating its purpose as guiding future decision making on residential infill development. Plan designations as the guide states them: Residential 1 (one and two family housing) and Residential 2 (attached housing and narrow lot infill). **Guide-only plan-date statement:** the guide states the plan "is Schedule B2 of Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, which the City adopted on December 16, 2025" — the plan identity and Schedule B2 position match the City OCP page carried in the sibling research, but that December 16, 2025 adoption date was not confirmed on a City page in this program: UNVERIFIED. (Research file, §§1, 4; https://chilliwack.com/ocp; https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-proper-south-guide.html)
- **Zoning, SSMUH and suites (Chilliwack frame — the city's own rules, never borrowed):** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs. The SSMUH amendment, Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024), allows **3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing** — duplexes are the baseline form. A secondary suite is capped at **the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the habitable floor space of the building** (s.2); only **1 accessory dwelling unit per lot** where the zone permits, with types including secondary suite, coach house and garden suite, garden suites capped at **100 m²** (s.4.06(1)); a suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. (Research file, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf; https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724)
- **Short-term rentals:** the City regulates short-term rental operations under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141, adopted November 24, 2021, which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968 — confirm the property can be licensed under it before valuing any rental income from a duplex or suite. (Research file, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf)
