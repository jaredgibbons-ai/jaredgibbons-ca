## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **SSMUH position (Langley City frame — City-only).** The City amended its Zoning Bylaw to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on RS1 and RS2 lots by Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 — RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Bylaw 3284 is the City's own SSMUH bylaw; the Township's Bylaw 6020 does not apply inside the City. Duplexes are the baseline form under that framework. (Research file above, §4; https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3284, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (Langley City's OWN rule, Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300).** A secondary suite must not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the gross floor area of the building; the registered owner must reside on the lot (s.2.4); at least one extra off-street parking space is required; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. A carriage/garden ground-floor unit is capped at 100 m². The lesser-of-100 m²/40% figure is Langley City's own rule — never borrowed for another city, and no other city's cap is borrowed here. A listing's "legal suite" claim is a claim to verify against this rule. (Research file above, §4; https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw-2025-no-3300pdf-2, read 2026-11-01.)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**.
