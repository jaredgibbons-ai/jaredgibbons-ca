## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs; urban Poplar lots are in the RS family (RS3 540 m² / RS5 420 m² minimum interior lots in City reports), and southern rural lots fall under A1 (minimum subdivision lot area 8.0 ha where it applies). The CURRENT plan is Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted December 2025). The zone of a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until read. (Research file above, §4; https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014)
- **SSMUH position.** Abbotsford's SSMUH implementation sits in the Zoning Bylaw s.140 framework; a duplex is the baseline two-unit form. **The SSMUH bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED — "3560-2024" is NEVER asserted in this file** (see the sibling flag in §5). SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Brief chain; research file above.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Abbotsford's own rule.** Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings (City-report criteria include a 400 m² minimum lot and 12.0 m minimum frontage); the City's 2025 updates set the suite maximum (in most zones) at **under 50%** of the principal unit's size, and a coach/garden suite is capped at **15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m² — Abbotsford's rule ONLY**, never borrowed by or from another city. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140 and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Zoning%20Bylaw%20Updates%20Overview%202025.pdf
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums; on Poplar's south edge, ALR status (parcel-by-parcel in the ALC's ALR Property and Map Finder) and the A1 8.0 ha minimum govern first. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file above, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps)
- **Servicing.** Abbotsford's Development (servicing) bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — none is quoted. South-edge rural lots may be on wells and onsite sewage rather than community systems (wells in the provincial GWELLS database; onsite systems under the Sewerage System Regulation through Fraser Health). (Brief chain; research file above, §4.)
- **Tax timing:** City of Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026 by midnight, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026, each including any unclaimed Home Owner Grant. (Research file above, §4; https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy half a duplex in Poplar?** Only if the pair was strata-divided (2-lot strata plan) or the parcel was subdivided fee-simple — no such named property is verified on this ground in any source in this program. Most Poplar duplex-form buildings sell whole, on one title. (§2.)
- **Q: Is the King Road cluster duplex stock?** No — The Argyle, Crown Point, Kingsview Estates and Kings Crossing are townhouse-form stock, counted once in the townhomes files. Kings Crossing's tenure is UNCONFIRMED even as townhouse stock. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Maplewood Estates a duplex or strata complex?** Neither — it is a pad-rental manufactured-home park at 2035 Martens St: you buy the home and rent the pad, with park-owner approval. Its home file is the poplar-guide mobile-homes file. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a Poplar duplex or house?** One secondary suite is permitted in all single detached dwellings under s.140.3.8 (400 m² minimum lot, 12.0 m frontage in City reports), sized at under 50% of the principal unit; a coach/garden suite is capped at 15% of the lot to a maximum 100 m². (§3.)
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for Poplar?** No — duplexes have no separate published category in this program's sources; §1's figures are labelled Abbotsford community context and thin sub-area counts only. (§1.)
