## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** The established house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex; a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units, with minimum site areas of 464 m² (5 units) and 557 m² (6–8 units). River District / East Fraser Lands parcels are under site-specific CD-1 districts and are read on their own CD-1 text — nothing about them is asserted here. The zone and dimensions of a specific lot are UNVERIFIED until read parcel by parcel. (Research file above; condos sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/killarney-ve-condos-2026-11-01.md.)
- **SSMUH position.** The R1-1 schedule above IS Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit framework for these streets — house plus suite and laneway, duplex, or a multiplex to 6 strata / 8 rental units by lot size — so the "SSMUH question" here is a per-lot schedule check (306 / 464 / 557 m²), not a separate overlay. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Research file above.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the City's own position.** A single detached house under R1-1 may include a secondary suite and a laneway house. For a duplex, the schedule's unit and site-area rules govern the lot; no Vancouver suite size cap figure beyond the schedule is asserted here — the position for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until its schedule is read. (Research file above.)
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY, 3%.** Vancouver owners must file an annual property status declaration, and a property that meets the tax's vacancy tests is taxed at 3% (Vancouver only — never applied to any other municipality in this program). A duplex or half duplex left empty between tenants is read against this tax. (Vancouver frame, brief chain.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, measured against the R1-1 minimum site areas above; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Vancouver's servicing position is carried in the verified ledger as Bylaw 5208 (fees 14538). (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Killarney?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. None is verified by name here; a single-lot duplex sells whole. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is there a named duplex development in Killarney?** None verified. The duplex evidence here is the form class under the R1-1 schedule. (§2.)
- **Q: What can the R1-1 lot under a Killarney house actually hold?** Up to 6 units in a multiplex, or up to 8 rental units, with minimum site areas of 306 m² (house/duplex), 464 m² (5 units) and 557 m² (6–8 units) — frontage and site area, not just the house, drive value on these streets. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax hit a Killarney duplex left empty?** Yes, in Vancouver: owners must file an annual property status declaration, and a property meeting the vacancy tests is taxed at 3% — Vancouver only. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $2,000,000 fully residential with no exemption = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a duplex here?** Address-based in SD39 — the documented locator run for 6454 Killarney Street returned Dr. George M. Weir Elementary and Killarney Secondary for that address only, and four elementaries sit inside Killarney. Run the exact address. (§5.)
