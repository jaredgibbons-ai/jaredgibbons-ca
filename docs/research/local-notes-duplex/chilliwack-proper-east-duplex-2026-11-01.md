## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan and zoning frame.** The governing plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — specifically its Schedule B-2 Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan for this ground, with the Schedule D development permit areas (D-1 Drinking Water Supply, D-2 Hillside Environment, D-3 Riparian Area, D-4 Farm Home Plate, D-5 Hillside Development, D-6 Downtown, D-7 Mixed-Use Areas, D-8 Residential, D-9 Commercial, D-10 Industrial) applying per parcel; which areas apply to a given parcel is a per-parcel check: UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel. Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs zones parcel by parcel. (Research file above, §4; https://chilliwack.com/ocp, read 2026-11-01.)
- **SSMUH position.** Chilliwack's SSMUH amendment is Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, amending Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, adopted June 18, 2024 — allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. Duplexes are the baseline form inside that frame; added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide and does not by itself create a separately saleable half. (Research file above, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Chilliwack's own rule.** Under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000: a secondary suite is an accessory dwelling unit of not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building — the lesser of 90 m² and 40% (s.2) — located within a building containing only 1 principal dwelling unit; only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot, and the permitted ADU types include secondary suite, coach house and garden suite (s.4.06(1)); garden suites are capped at a maximum gross floor area of 100 m² (s.4.06(1)(g)); ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite (s.5.04); and no secondary suite is legal unless built with a building permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, with new suites not permitted below the flood construction level. A buyer pricing the James Street record's "basement suite" wording, or any duplex-with-suite claim, measures it against exactly this rule and asks for the permits. (Research file above, §4; bylaw PDF above.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full (the strata must provide the Form B within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer / Preliminary Layout Approval process against the parcel's actual zone; the Chilliwack servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in this program (see §5), so no servicing-bylaw number is stated here. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; siblings in the FILE STATUS block.)
- **Property taxes (city regime).** City of Chilliwack tax notices are mailed annually at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% late payment penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. (Research file above, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522, read 2026-11-01.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: What is the difference between buying a duplex and buying a half duplex here?** A duplex sale is the whole two-unit building on one lot under one title. A half duplex is one unit sold separately, which in BC requires either a 2-lot strata plan (each half a strata lot, Form B diligence) or a subdivided fee-simple parcel (each half on its own lot, party-wall agreement on title). If neither exists, the half cannot be sold separately. (§2.)
- **Q: The page copy mentions "R1-A duplex potential" — can I rely on it?** No. The page's editorial zone shorthand conflicts with one of its own feed cards (an R 1-A to R 1-C rezoning claim), and zone claims for any specific parcel are UNVERIFIED. Confirm the parcel's actual zone and any subdivision approval with the City before pricing potential. (§§1–2.)
- **Q: Can a Proper East duplex carry a suite as well?** Chilliwack allows only 1 accessory dwelling unit per lot where the zone permits — a secondary suite capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space, a coach house, or a garden suite capped at 100 m² — and a suite must be built with a permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and flood protection requirements; new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. Separately, SSMUH (Bylaw 5395) allows 3–4 units on eligible single-detached/duplex parcels inside the urban growth boundary. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: Are there market statistics for duplexes in Chilliwack Proper East?** No. Chilliwack is a CADREB market with no SnapStats coverage and no published sub-area statistics in the sources used — there is no duplex median, and no other community's figure is substituted. - **Q: What PTT do I pay on a half duplex?** PTT on its own purchase price as its own transfer, on the standard tiers — for example $500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $8,000; $800,000 = $14,000; $1,000,000 = $18,000. (§3.)
