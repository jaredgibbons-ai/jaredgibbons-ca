## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where two-unit forms are permitted.** Inside First Shaughnessy, the First Shaughnessy District Schedule to the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 governs: multiple conversion dwellings, infill and secondary suites as the schedule states them, multiple dwellings only on sites without protected heritage property, FSR 0.45 with a conserved protected heritage property, otherwise 0.25 plus 139 m² to a 911 m² maximum floor area. Outside the HCA, the R1-1 district applies: minimum site area 306 m² for a house, a duplex or a 3–4-unit multiple dwelling, 464 m² for 5 units, 557 m² for 6–8 units; up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units. The exact schedule for a specific lot is read off the current bylaw text for that lot. http://former.vancouver.ca/commsvcs/BYLAWS/zoning/fsd.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Heritage protection.** Protected (pre-1940) heritage properties can only be removed from the protected list through a Council process following a heritage character and value assessment; demolition of a protected home is not as of right. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/bulletins/bulletin-first-shaughnessy-heritage-conservation-area-procedure-for-review-of-protected-heritage.pdf
- **SSMUH position.** Vancouver's R1-1 multiplex framework (By-law No. 13817) is the city's small-scale multi-unit housing implementation on former single-detached lots, with the duplex as the baseline form — but inside First Shaughnessy the District Schedule and HCA framework govern first, and their interaction on a given lot is UNCONFIRMED until the City confirms it for that property. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **Secondary suites — Vancouver's own position only.** The First Shaughnessy District Schedule allows secondary suites as part of its stated intent, and the R1-1 schedule permits a secondary suite (and, for a house, a laneway house) as it states; no suite size cap from any other city is borrowed and none is asserted here beyond the schedules' own text. http://former.vancouver.ca/commsvcs/BYLAWS/zoning/fsd.pdf
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex (and in full to Shaughnessy Place and Whitford).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**, stated precisely. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** Vancouver's Subdivision By-law No. 5208 is VERIFIED in the program ledger (fees under By-law No. 14538), and its Schedule A sets First Shaughnessy's minimum parcel width at 100 ft (30.48 m) and minimum parcel area at 13,000 sq ft (1,207.7 m²) — the bylaw basis for the district's estate lots, and the first check before any Shaughnessy lot is priced as splittable. A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval under that bylaw; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the titles — read them. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/scheda.pdf
- **Taxes and levies (citywide Vancouver, plus the provincial line that bites here).** Advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance. The City's Empty Homes Tax charges 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed empty — **3%, VANCOUVER ONLY**. Because most Shaughnessy homes assess above $3 million, BC's additional school tax is also material: 0.2% on the residential portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% effective January 1, 2027. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — at Shaughnessy price points that further 2% tier is a live item on every file. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a duplex in Shaughnessy?** No named duplex stock is verified here at all. What the district actually offers in two-unit form is a protected heritage house with a multiple conversion, infill or coach house under the HCA incentives — a different purchase, with heritage diligence first. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a protected heritage house be demolished to build a duplex pair?** Not as of right: removal from the protected list needs a heritage character and value assessment and a Council process at a public hearing. (§3.)
- **Q: What do the HCA incentives actually add?** Coach house, multiple conversion dwelling and infill permissions for conserving a pre-1940 home, plus the higher 0.45 FSR where a protected heritage property is conserved — real value for the right buyer, priced into protected homes. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Can a Shaughnessy estate lot be split into two fee-simple halves?** Check the Subdivision By-law first: First Shaughnessy's minimums are 100 ft (30.48 m) parcel width and 13,000 sq ft (1,207.7 m²) parcel area, and any split needs subdivision approval under By-law No. 5208 — most single estate lots were never sized to split twice over. (§3.)
- **Q: Does R1-1 multiplex apply north of King Edward?** UNCONFIRMED per lot — the First Shaughnessy District Schedule governs inside the HCA and the interaction must be confirmed property by property; south of King Edward the standard R1-1 framework applies. (§3.)
- **Q: Is Shaughnessy Place a duplex option?** No — it is one mixed strata complex (apartments plus townhouse units) counted once in the condos file; a purchase there is a strata purchase with Form B diligence, not a half-duplex purchase. (§2.)
- **Q: What extra tax lines hit at Shaughnessy prices?** PTT with the further 2% tier above $3,000,000 (worked in §3: $3,500,000 = $93,000), the additional school tax above $3 million of assessed value, and the 3% Vancouver Empty Homes Tax if the home is declared or deemed empty. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Is there a Shaughnessy market median I can rely on?** No — SnapStats does not cover Vancouver and no substitute figure is used in this program. (§1.)
