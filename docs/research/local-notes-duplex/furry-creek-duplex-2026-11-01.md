## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** SLRD Electoral Area D Zoning Bylaw No. 1350-2016 governs; Furry Creek zones shown on the bylaw schedules include CD3 (Furry Creek Comprehensive Development 3), FCR residential zones, FCC1 and FCC2 (golf course commercial). No zone provision naming a duplex form for Furry Creek was read from the bylaw text in the research pass — the duplex position for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until its zone is read. Existing statutory building schemes and covenants in the neighbourhood constrain what owners can build or alter, on top of zoning. (Research file above, §4; https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw-electoral-area-d2pdf and https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/250129-ssmuh-area-d-amendment-bb-extension-update-rfd)
- **SSMUH position — EXEMPT, stated exactly.** The Province has determined that the SLRD's master-planned communities of Britannia Beach, Furry Creek and Porteau Cove are NOT subject to the additional 3–6 unit SSMUH densities, because they are not within an urban containment boundary for the purposes of the SSMUH legislation; Amendment Bylaw No. 1887-2025 removed the extra densities briefly added to the Furry Creek FCR3 and CD3 zones. The provincial SSMUH minimums do NOT apply here — do not underwrite a Furry Creek lot on 3–6 unit rights. (Research file above, §4; https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/250129-ssmuh-area-d-amendment-bb-extension-update-rfd)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex.** Suite/ADU rights are whatever the base zoning (Bylaw No. 1350-2016) and the building scheme allow; no Furry Creek-specific suite rule was stated in the research pass — UNCONFIRMED at lot level. No other jurisdiction's suite cap is borrowed here. (Research file above, §6.)
- **STRATA block — stated for completeness; no strata duplex exists on this ground.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists elsewhere: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel in an SLRD electoral area requires subdivision approval through the provincial Approving Officer process; no such split is documented on this ground. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Services / servicing.** The SLRD provides Furry Creek-specific local services — water, wastewater treatment, street lighting, solid waste disposal, open spaces maintenance, fire protection, and a dyking/drainage service — under local service bylaws (e.g., Furry Creek Dyking Local Service Establishment Bylaw No. 481; Furry Creek Sewer Local Service Bylaw No. 1619); per-lot levy amounts are UNVERIFIED. (Research file above, §4; https://www.slrd.bc.ca/inside-slrd/bylaws?community=All&type=All&page=4)
- **Tax regime — electoral-area regime, stated as such.** As an SLRD electoral area community, properties pay provincial rural property tax plus SLRD service levies; the rural regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11) applies a 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after October 31 — never blended with any municipal tax regime. (Research file above, §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12; see the sibling check in §5) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any half duplex for sale in Furry Creek?** None verified, in any source in this program. A Furry Creek purchase is a whole lot under one title, or a strata lot in one of the two named strata (townhouse form at Oliver's Landing; detached form at Ocean's Crest). (§2.)
- **Q: Can I add 3–6 units to a Furry Creek lot under the provincial SSMUH rules?** No. Furry Creek is expressly EXEMPT — the Province determined the master-planned Howe Sound communities are not subject to the added SSMUH densities, and Bylaw No. 1887-2025 removed them from the FCR3 and CD3 zones. (§3.)
- **Q: Is Furry Creek part of Squamish?** No — it is an unincorporated SLRD Electoral Area D community south of the District of Squamish boundary, governed by OCP Bylaw No. 1135-2013 and Zoning Bylaw No. 1350-2016. (Research file, §7 Q1; https://slrd.civicweb.net/document/149848/)
- **Q: What else constrains a renovation or addition?** The statutory building schemes and covenants recorded for the neighbourhood — the SLRD cites them as a constraint on this ground; exterior changes may need scheme approval on top of any permit. (§1/§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex (off this ground)?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: What hazard rules affect building here?** Expanded Development Permit Areas adopted in 2022 (OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 1717-2021) cover landslide, slope stability, coastal and creek hazards in Howe Sound East, with dike design and flood construction level requirements on creek-fan lands. (Research file, §4; https://getinvolved.slrd.bc.ca/sites/default/files/pdfs/planning/staff-reports/Area%20D%20DPA%20Housekeeping%204th%20Reading%20Report-FULL.pdf)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for Furry Creek?** No. SnapStats covers this corridor in neither report, and no market figure is substituted. (§1.)
