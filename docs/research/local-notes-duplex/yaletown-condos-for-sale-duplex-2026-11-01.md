## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning (Vancouver frame — downtown ground).** The City of Vancouver Zoning and Development Bylaw 3575 governs, but Yaletown ground is **downtown-district zoning with the Yaletown Historic Area District Schedule (HA-3)** for its historic core — NOT the R1-1 residential district. The R1-1 figures carried in the city file (306 m² minimum site area for a house/duplex/3–4-unit multiple dwelling, 464 m² for 5 units, 557 m² for 6–8 units) describe the city's house/duplex streets where R1-1 applies; they are NOT a Yaletown permission table, and no district-by-district duplex permission for a Yaletown parcel is asserted, because no source opened for this slug states one. The HA-3 schedule encourages conversion and renovation of the core's heavy-timber, brick and concrete buildings and compatible new buildings for a mix of commercial, industrial and residential uses — the heritage frame any ground-oriented proposal on this ground is read against. (Research file above; https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx, read 2026-11-01; program Vancouver frame, brief chain.)
- **SSMUH position (Vancouver frame).** R1-1 IS the SSMUH framework where it applies in the city; Yaletown's downtown-district ground is not R1-1 streets, so no SSMUH unit ladder is asserted for a Yaletown parcel in this file. In any case SSMUH adds unit potential on a lot; it does not by itself split a building into separately titled halves. (Brief chain.)
- **Secondary suites (Vancouver position).** No Yaletown-specific suite rule is stated, because no source opened for this slug states one for downtown-district ground, and no other municipality's cap is borrowed into a Vancouver file. - **Servicing.** Vancouver's servicing (Subdivision) Bylaw 5208 is VERIFIED in the verified ledger (fees bylaw 14538). (Brief chain.)
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY.** Every Vancouver residential owner must submit an annual property status declaration on or before the 2nd business day in February under Vacancy Tax By-law No. 11674; a vacancy tax unpaid as of the 10th business day of April bears a 5% penalty. The rate carried in this program's Vancouver frame is **3%** — Vancouver-only, never exported; this slug's research did not itself state the rate (UNVERIFIED in its own pass). (Research file above; https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/11674c.pdf, read via search result 2026-11-01.)
- **Property taxes (Vancouver frame).** Advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances after the main-tax deadline. (Research file above; https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx, read via search result 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), stated for completeness on a ground with no verified strata duplex.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. For Yaletown's actual stock — apartment stratas — the same Form B discipline is the diligence route, read in the condos sibling. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a two-unit parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale unit is not new housing for this rebate — this slug's verified stock is resale stock. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
