## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A barn, arena or stable is a farm building, not a dwelling form — none of these is duplex stock. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning, SSMUH, suites and coach houses (Township frame):** Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs this ground. SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024; NO 6-plex tier): eligible lots (Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water and sewer, 4,050 m² or smaller) may have 3 units at ≤280 m² or 4 units at 280–4,050 m² — most farm and equestrian parcels fail on size and servicing alone, so SSMUH does not create duplex stock on this band. Township secondary suite cap: **120 m²**, with no percentage cap — the Township's own rule, never borrowed. **Coach house = a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102, NEVER strata** — stated exactly. R-CL s.403 = 220 m² min lot / 464.5 m² max lot area ("355 m²" is NOWHERE in the bylaw — never carried). Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 is CONFIRMED. Township property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies at midnight July 2, 2026, and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. (Township frame, D16 task / D15 brief; research file, §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx; https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx.)
- **ALR frame:** the residence ceilings in §2 — anything beyond needs ALC approval and local bylaws may be more restrictive. Farm-class thresholds (the corrected frame for the page's flat line, §5): $2,500 in farm-gate sales for a farm operation area of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for larger operations; $10,000 if the total area is under 0.8 ha; applications must be delivered to BC Assessment by October 31. (Research file, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo89/loo89/411_95_pit.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) wherever one exists.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Township's Approving Officer process (and, on ALR land, ALC permission — depositing a strata plan on ALR land is a subdivision under the program position carried in the acreages file in this batch, citation UNVERIFIED in that slug's pass); no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales — a resale farmhouse, duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
