## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A detached coach house on an acreage is a second dwelling form, not a duplex: a duplex is two units in ONE building. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zones / the governing frame on this ground.** Zoning By-law No. 12000 governs parcel by parcel (confirmed in COSMOS), but Hazelmere's operative frame is agricultural/rural: A-1 General Agriculture and RA Acreage Residential zoning as the live page states them (figures UNVERIFIED on the bylaw text, §2), plus the ALC frame on any parcel in the ALR. Local governments may further restrict ALR residences through zoning — the stricter rule governs. (Research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **SSMUH position — rural limit stated first.** Surrey's SSMUH allowances (framework adopted Jul 8, 2024: 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, up to 6 near a frequent bus stop) apply to eligible residential-zoned properties under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including lands not connected to community water and sewer. **The standard 3–4 unit framing must not be applied to Hazelmere lots without a parcel check** — most Hazelmere acreage ground sits outside that shape. A duplex is the baseline two-unit form inside the SSMUH framework where the framework applies; whether it applies to a given Hazelmere parcel is UNCONFIRMED parcel-by-parcel. (Research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing)
- **Secondary suites / additional residences (the rules that actually govern a second unit here).** Surrey-wide suite rule: one secondary suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most **40% of the principal dwelling unit's** habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite — carried here as the Surrey rule, not assumed to govern A-1 ground (the lots-and-land sibling states that caution expressly). On ALR parcels the ALC frame governs: no more than one residence per parcel generally; a principal residence up to 500 m² total floor area without ALC application; one additional detached residence of up to 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); a secondary suite only inside the principal residence and only where local bylaws allow it. ALR status is parcel-specific on ALC mapping — no Hazelmere parcel result is stated in this file. (Research file §4; lots-and-land sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/hazelmere-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) if one is ever met on this ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the Zoning 12000 minimums — on A-1 ground the live page states new-lot minimums of 4 ha (inside ALR) / 2 ha (outside), UNVERIFIED on the bylaw text (§2), which is why no half-duplex subdivision is a realistic form on this ground without a bylaw read. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. Streamside setbacks on the Little Campbell River and Jacobsen Creek are live parcel checks (Part 7A of Zoning 12000, vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure). (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file §4.)
- **Servicing / frame items.** Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED as the Surrey ledger position; rural homes here may use private wells and onsite septic (parcel-specific — GWELLS / Fraser Health specifics are carried as the live page's pointers only, UNVERIFIED on a Province source in the research); STR licence **$358/yr** carried with the City's 2024 **$350/yr** proposal disclosed; Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED); Surrey property taxes due July 2, 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2; farm-class taxation for any specific property is a BC Assessment / parcel question: UNVERIFIED. (Brief chain Surrey frame; research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. (Farm / mixed-use parcels can change the residential-portion math — take conveyancing advice; no parcel-specific PTT position is stated here.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale acreage home is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
