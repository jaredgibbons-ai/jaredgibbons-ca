## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**FORM CLASS ONLY at the top: the only residential form this ground supports is the farm-residence form on a whole fee-simple parcel. No named duplex, strata duplex or fee-simple half-duplex subdivision is verified on Chilliwack farm ground, and strata farm product is not a Chilliwack pattern — no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source.** (Research file above, §5.)

- **Why the strata form is closed here:** on ALR land a residence cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (Strata Property Act s.239), and agricultural land must not be subdivided unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1); ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6). Farm parcels here are overwhelmingly freehold. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **The farm-residence form class with its basis:** valley-floor farms under the AL and AS agricultural zones, upland farms under AU, farm-residential parcels under AR, and agricultural commercial / food-processing operations under AC and AFP — Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, §6. On ALR land the ALC housing rules permit one principal residence per parcel (500 m² or less built without an ALC application, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction), a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence, and one additional detached residence where conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — with City bylaws also required to permit it. A principal home plus a lawful additional residence is a two-residence FARM PARCEL, sold whole — not a duplex, and neither residence is a separately titled half. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Second residences / manufactured homes on farm ground — ONLY as the ALR sources support:** a manufactured home can figure on farm ground only as the parcel's principal or additional residence within those same ALC caps and conditions (the ALR Use Regulation / ALC Policy L-25 additional-residence framework, with the Non-Adhering Residential Use application route where the as-of-right conditions are not met). **A general farm-worker housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED** — the City lists a new house, coach house or suite among the items that may need an ALC application, and no source in this topic's research grants a stand-alone entitlement (the poultry-farms treatment in this program is the model for this restraint). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm Home Plate (the residential footprint rule on this ground):** in the AL, AR and AC zones the Farm Home Plate is capped, as maximum lot coverage, at 4,000 m² for the principal residential use and 1,000 m² for an additional residential use, with the siting limits the bylaw states; the requirements do not apply where a lot is under 0.8 ha; the AU, AS and AFP sections state no Farm Home Plate figure. The OCP includes Development Permit Area 4 – Farm Home Plate, and a Development Permit is required when the Zoning Bylaw's Farm Home Plate regulations are not met. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs; §6 sets the farm zones (AL, AU, AS, AR, AC, AFP, ss.6.01–6.06) and §7 the rural zones including R (Rural) and RH (Rural Hillside). The governing plan is OCP Bylaw 5500 "Chilliwack 2050" — for this farm ground, Agricultural = Schedule B1. The farm zones are not a duplex-subdivision form: minimum lot areas for newly created lots run from 7.5 ha (AL/AU, ALR-approved or excluded/exempted) down to 0.2 ha only through an ALC-approved homesite severance or boundary adjustment (AR: 0.2 ha with community water, 1 ha otherwise; AFP: 0.4 ha), with a 25 m minimum lot width in all six zones. The zone of a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until read. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH position.** Chilliwack's SSMUH bylaw is Bylaw 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024) — an urban residential-lot framework. It does not create subdivision or added-residence rights on §6 farm land, where the ALR and Farm Home Plate rules govern instead; a duplex is the baseline two-unit form on eligible urban lots, not a farm-parcel form. (Brief chain; townhomes sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/chilliwack-farms-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the city's own position on this ground.** On ALR farm land the ALC rule governs: a secondary suite is permitted ONLY inside the principal residence, and the City lists a new house, coach house or suite among the items that may need an ALC application before the City can approve related development. No suite size figure is borrowed from another city for this ground. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **STRATA block — stated for completeness; no strata duplex exists on this ground.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists elsewhere: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. On ALR parcels the strata form is barred outright without ALC permission (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support, with the 3055 conflict carried OPEN.** Subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer process and, in the ALR, ALC approval for subdivision or a boundary adjustment. **Chilliwack's servicing (Land Development) bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger: the ledger holds "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" as NOT adopted, while the City's own subdivisions webpage asserts that bylaw name/number — and this slug's lots-and-land sibling asserts 3055 as governing (§5). The conflict is OPEN, settlement requires a read of the City's actual bylaw text, and 3055 is NEVER asserted in this file.** There is no urban-style lot-split route on a Chilliwack farm parcel. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles. (Brief chain; lots-and-land sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/chilliwack-farms-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **Tax timing:** Chilliwack property taxes carry a 5% penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after August 4, 2026; drainage and dyking levies sit on top of municipal rates. (Research file above; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Note for farm parcels: PTT applies on the residential portion as the Act provides — the worked examples are the fully-residential program anchors, and a mixed farm parcel's exact split is facts-specific: UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate, and neither is a farm parcel. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only — a working farm is a business-side purchase as a general matter, and the GST position on any specific Chilliwack farm parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and parcel history are established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy half a duplex on a Chilliwack farm?** No verified half duplex of either legal form exists on this ground — and on ALR land the strata form is closed without ALC permission, because a strata plan is a subdivision of agricultural land. A farm purchase here is a whole parcel under one title. (§2.)
- **Q: A farm listing shows two houses — is that a duplex I can split?** No. That is the farm-residence form: a principal residence (500 m² cap) plus, where the conditions are met, one additional residence (90 m² / 186 m² by the 40 ha line) — both on one parcel, sold together. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a suite go in a barn or a detached building on a farm?** No — a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; a detached second dwelling is the size-capped "additional residence," and the City lists a new house, coach house or suite among the items that may need an ALC application. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a farm parcel as a second home?** Only within the ALR additional-residence framework (caps and conditions in §2, with the Non-Adhering Residential Use route where the as-of-right conditions are not met). A general farm-worker housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED and is not asserted. (§2.)
- **Q: What is the Farm Home Plate, in one sentence?** Chilliwack's cap and siting control on the residential footprint of a farm parcel — 4,000 m² maximum coverage for the principal residence in the AL, AR and AC zones (1,000 m² for an additional residential use), with a Development Permit required when the Zoning Bylaw's Farm Home Plate regulations are not met; the rules do not apply where a lot is under 0.8 ha. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a farm parcel into building lots?** Only inside narrow rules: a newly created lot must meet its zone's §6 minimum — 7.5 ha at the AL/AU subdivision gate, down to 0.2 ha only through an ALC-approved homesite severance or boundary adjustment — and in the ALR, subdivision may require an ALC application first. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex (off this ground)?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Why are there no market numbers in this file?** Chilliwack is a CADREB market; CADREB publishes no area stats in this program and the SnapStats reports held cover other boards and publish no farm category — so any Chilliwack or farm-specific statistic is UNVERIFIED here rather than borrowed. (§1.)
- **Q: What farm income do I need to keep farm class?** All three tiers, never a flat figure: $2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha above 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — with the application due by October 31 for the following taxation year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
