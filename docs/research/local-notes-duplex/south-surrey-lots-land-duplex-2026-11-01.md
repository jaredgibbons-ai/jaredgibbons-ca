## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**TOPIC — NO ROSTER at the top: this file names no duplex stock as its own and takes no count.** A vacant lot is not a duplex: duplex stock can only arise later, when a two-unit build is completed on a lot, and it would then count once in its sub-area home file — never here. What this section carries is the form class a land buyer is actually choosing between, and the tenure records that are NOT duplex stock. (Research file above; townhomes sibling FILE STATUS: `~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/south-surrey-lots-land-townhomes-2026-11-01.md`.)

- **The form class with its basis:** on a Surrey lot, the duplex is the baseline two-unit form under the SSMUH frame (§3) — a buyer of a lot who builds two units ends with either (i) one duplex building on one fee-simple lot, sold whole; (ii) a 2-lot STRATA duplex, if the build is strata-titled, making each half a separately saleable strata lot; or (iii) two fee-simple halves, only if the parcel itself is subdivided so each half sits on its own lot. Which of those a given lot can carry is a zone, servicing and subdivision question on that lot (§3) — the topic file asserts none of them for any parcel. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted, and how many homes can go on a lot.** Duplexes are a permitted two-unit form in Surrey's residential zones that allow a duplex under Zoning By-law No. 12000, and the provincial SSMUH implementation (Surrey, Jul 8, 2024) makes the duplex the baseline multi-unit form on eligible lots: inside the urban containment boundary, lots under 4,050 m² may hold 3 units (280 m² or less) or 4 units (larger), up to 6 for some lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, as the City states them; a triplex/fourplex (houseplex) is allowed only in the R3 zone, on a lot with a lane or two road frontages, with no secondary suite (site copy for the houseplex particulars). Lots without community water and sewer stay limited to two units even under Surrey's multi-unit rules. The OCP governing is Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is the replaced predecessor, never cited as current. The zone for a specific lot is read off the City's COSMOS map against the current Bylaw 12000 text. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated for Surrey. White Rock lots answer to White Rock Zoning Bylaw No. 2506 (adopted Jun 27, 2024; SSMUH tiers up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 over 280 m², up to 6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop) — no Surrey rule in this section is borrowed across that seam. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-development/zoning (Research file §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Surrey's own rule.** One secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area** under Zoning By-law No. 12000 — Surrey's rule, never borrowed; the old 90 m² Surrey suite cap is NOT a current rule and is never stated. Coach houses and garden suites: 35 m² minimum and 75 m² maximum, rising to a maximum of 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the principal dwelling has not achieved its maximum floor area. (Research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), and to any bare-land strata lot such as Sea Ridge Bays.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums. The page's subdivision sketch — City planner review, a Preliminary Layout Approval (PLA) letter listing conditions, 12 months to meet them (an extension can be requested for a fee), conditions that can include road dedication, covenants, tree permits and development cost charges, and a registered land surveyor's final plan to the Land Title Office — is site copy: no City subdivision page was opened in the research pass, so the PLA timeline is UNVERIFIED beyond site copy. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Research file §4; program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Surrey's servicing position is Bylaw 8830 (1986), VERIFIED from the City's own pages in the verified ledger. Parcel-level servicing is a separate, live check: the page states that if a new lot would use a well or septic field, Fraser Health testing must be done and the application cannot proceed if standards are not met, and that some acreage land is unserviced — parcel-level servicing for any specific lot is UNVERIFIED. Short-term rental figures for Surrey carry a disclosed $358/$350 conflict in the program record; White Rock's STR rule is UNVERIFIED in this slug's research — no STR figure is relied on in this file. (Research file §4; verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **ALR.** Larger acreage lots sit on the community's east side near Hazelmere and the farm areas; ALR parcels follow the ALC housing frame — one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha) — and ALR residences cannot be stratified, so no strata duplex or strata half duplex can be created on an ALR parcel. Whether a specific parcel is in the ALR is UNVERIFIED at parcel level. (Research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/, read 2026-11-01.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; **$3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = $93,000 including the further 2% tier** — $93,000 governs at $3.5M (see the §5 carrier restatement: this slug's townhomes sibling states $78,000 for this input in its PTT-on-land worked anchors, and that figure is the ERROR). A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer; whether the newly-built exemption can attach to a lot-then-build sequence is a per-transaction question for the buyer's lawyer — UNCONFIRMED at topic level, not promised here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — carried at the CRA GI-003 general level ONLY.** The page's finer statement (most vacant-land sales by individuals who held the land for personal use are GST exempt, while land sold in a business or a parcel split into more than two lots can be taxable) is site copy — no CRA material was opened in the research pass, so that treatment is UNVERIFIED beyond site copy and is not carried as this file's position; confirm the GST position for a specific lot with the buyer's accountant, and the contract must state it. (Research file §4.)
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. The rebate attaches to the home eventually built, not to bare land as such; a resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does this topic file list South Surrey lots where I can build a duplex?** No roster — it is a topic file. Whether a specific lot can carry a duplex is a zone, servicing and subdivision question on that lot, read in §3; a completed duplex counts in its sub-area home file, never here. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: If I build two units on a lot, can I sell one half separately?** Only if it is made legally separate — strata-titled as a 2-lot strata duplex, or the parcel subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot. A duplex built on one lot under one title sells whole. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Can I stratify a duplex on an ALR acreage lot?** No — ALR residences cannot be stratified, and the ALC frame caps the program at one principal residence plus at most one additional residence. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $3.5M on a lot?** $93,000 on a fully residential $3,500,000 purchase with no exemption — tier math in §3 ($2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + the further 2% × $500,000 = $10,000). This slug's townhomes sibling states $78,000 for this input in its PTT-on-land worked anchors — that figure is the ERROR, flagged in §5; follow $93,000. (§3; §5.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Is Sea Ridge Bays a duplex community I can buy a half of?** No. Sea Ridge Bays is a bare-land strata manufactured-home community — you buy a home and its strata lot, not half of a two-unit building — and it is documented at home in the south-surrey-mobile-homes topic file, a different slug; it is cross-referenced here as an excluded-tenure record only. (§2.)
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same as a freehold lot?** No. A bare-land strata lot is a strata lot (Form B, fees, bylaws) whose lot is essentially the building lot itself. A fee-simple lot has no strata at all. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a Surrey duplex built on one of these lots?** Surrey permits one secondary suite in a duplex dwelling capped at 40% of the principal unit's habitable area, where the zoning applies — and note the houseplex particular: in the R3 houseplex form, no secondary suite is permitted (site copy). (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: A card says "serviced building lot" — can I rely on it?** No — it is listing copy, UNVERIFIED at parcel level. Prove servicing against City records and, where a well/septic is involved, Fraser Health testing, before writing. (§1; §3.)
