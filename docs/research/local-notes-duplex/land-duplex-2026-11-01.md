## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

A duplex enters this topic only as a possible FUTURE form on a parcel — and a listing whose text raises a future duplex is a parcel with a claimed development potential, not duplex stock. (Condos sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/land-condos-2026-11-01.md; sibling set per the FILE STATUS block.)

- **The ALR frame — the structural limit on building a duplex on rural land in this feed.** Land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless it qualifies for an additional residence; a principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; and one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, or 186 m² or less on larger parcels, where local bylaws also permit it; local governments may regulate further but cannot be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. Subdivision of ALR land requires permission from the Agricultural Land Commission, apart from the limited cases in the ALR General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted — NO frame asserted for this topic.** A duplex built on a parcel from this feed takes the zoning of the municipality and parcel where it sits; this file asserts no city's zoning, SSMUH, suite, servicing or short-term-rental rule for the topic, because the topic has no ground. Where this slug's own siblings carry a frame, they carry it as a citation for the ground named there — read the home-area file for the parcel in question. Parcel-level zoning, servicing (municipal water/sewer versus well/septic) and development timing or cost for any specific feed parcel: UNVERIFIED — listing text is listing-agent copy, not a municipal confirmation. (Research file above, §4.)
- **ALR checks before pricing any rural parcel.** A parcel's ALR status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps by PID or civic address; reserve status controls the 500 m² principal-residence cap, the 90 m² / 186 m² additional-residence limits and whether subdivision needs Commission permission. (Research file above, §6; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000, based on fair market value (land and improvements) on the day of registration at the Land Title Office unless an exemption applies. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex, where a transfer is a residential transfer, is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 GENERAL LEVEL ONLY (the settled position, governing in this file).** Most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel — at the general level: developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable; an individual's personal-use land is generally exempt. Whether GST applies to a specific land sale depends on the seller and the parcel's history and is UNVERIFIED for any specific feed parcel — confirm with the buyer's legal professional before writing. Anything finer than the GI-003 general level: UNCONFIRMED in this file. (Research file above, §4; lots-and-land sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/land-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md; https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) wherever one is later built and strata-divided.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. The strata rules apply only after a strata plan is deposited and create no rights in a vacant parcel today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through that municipality's Approving Officer process, against its current zoning minimums (and, on ALR land, the ALC permission stated in §2); no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. The rebate applies to new HOUSING, not to bare land itself: 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — so a home later built and first sold on a parcel bought from this feed is a GST purchase, but the vacant parcel itself is not new housing for this rebate. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing either. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Representation framework (from the research).** In BC, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party — relevant when a buyer contacts a land listing's brokerage directly. (Research file above, §4; https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does this topic file list duplexes for sale?** No. It is a province-wide land topic file with no roster and no single ground; the feed carries vacant land, and a duplex that could one day be built on a parcel is documented, if anywhere, in that parcel's home-area file. (§2.)
- **Q: A land card says a duplex could go on the lot — can I rely on that?** No. That wording is listing-agent copy about claimed development potential, recorded in §2 for the one Cloverdale card that carries it. Verify it against the in-force municipal zoning for that PID and, for ALR parcels, the ALC rules before pricing the plan. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I build a duplex on ALR land from this feed?** Generally the ALR frame is one principal residence (500 m² or less) plus at most one additional residence (90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, 186 m² on larger parcels), with a secondary suite permitted only inside the principal residence — more than that, or a strata split, needs ALC permission. Check the parcel's status on the ALC's maps by PID or civic address first. (§2, §3.)
- **Q: Can I buy half a duplex that gets built on a parcel like these?** Only if that half is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot after subdivision approval. A duplex never strata-divided or subdivided sells whole. (§2.)
- **Q: Do I pay GST on vacant land?** At the CRA GI-003 general level only: an individual's personal-use land sale is generally exempt; a developer/business sale or a newly subdivided lot is generally taxable. For any specific parcel it is UNVERIFIED — confirm with your legal professional before writing. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on a land purchase?** The general tiers on fair market value at registration: $500,000 → $8,000; $800,000 → $14,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 fully residential → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Are there land market statistics I can price from?** None is stated anywhere in this program for this topic: SnapStats publishes no land category and no duplex category, and a detached-house median describes houses, not parcels. Price a parcel on its own zoning, ALR status, servicing and comparables. (§1.)
- **Q: Is this feed really only Fraser Valley land?** No — that is the page's title, not its feed: the first cards read include Quesnel, Fort St. John, Gibsons and Squamish alongside Langley, Surrey and Mission parcels. Confirm a parcel's actual municipality before treating it as a Fraser Valley option. (§1; research file §7.)
