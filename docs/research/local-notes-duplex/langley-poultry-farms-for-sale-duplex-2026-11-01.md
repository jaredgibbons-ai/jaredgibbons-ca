## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. On ALR land a strata plan itself causes subdivision under the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation framework (ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6), so a strata half duplex on a farm parcel is an ALC-approval question before it is a form question. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; research file, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
