## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A First Nations leasehold interest is neither form — it is a lease on First Nations land, not a fee-simple or strata title. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan and zoning (Chilliwack frame):** land use sits under the City's 2050 Official Community Plan (Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500). Its Schedule B neighbourhood plans are B1 Agricultural Area, B2 Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island, B3 Downtown, B4 Eastern Hillsides, B5 Sardis, B6 South Vedder and B7 Yarrow; Schedule C is the Mountain View Heritage Conservation Area and Schedule D is the Development Permit Areas schedule (D-1 Drinking Water Supply through D-10 Industrial). Any parcel's actual zone is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. Chilliwack's Supreme Court registry position: UNCONFIRMED. (Research file, §4; https://chilliwack.com/ocp)
- **SSMUH (Chilliwack's own position):** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, amending Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, adopted June 18, 2024, allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing — duplexes are the baseline form where it applies. (Research file, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf)
- **Secondary suites and accessory units (Chilliwack's own rule, never borrowed):** under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the habitable floor space of the building (s.2); only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot, with permitted types including secondary suite, coach house and garden suite, garden suites capped at 100 m² (s.4.06(1)); ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite (s.5.04); a suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. So a Chilliwack duplex carries at most ONE accessory unit on its lot on this rule. (Research file, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf; https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724)
- **Short-term rentals:** the City regulates short-term rental operations under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141, adopted November 24, 2021, which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968. (Research file, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
