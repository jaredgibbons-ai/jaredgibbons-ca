## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Suites and accessory units (Abbotsford frame).** A secondary suite must be **within the principal unit, registered with the City, and less than 50% of the size of the principal unit**; a coach house sits on the second storey of an accessory building and a garden suite is the single-storey at-grade form; both may occupy **up to 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m²** — the 15% / 100 m² figures are Abbotsford ONLY. A coach house is a dwelling unit accessory to a single detached dwelling or duplex on the same lot. Parking is 1 required stall for each accessory unit type under Zoning Bylaw Section 150, and accessory units cannot be sold independently from the principal unit. - **ALR frame.** The ALC's housing guidance caps a principal residence on ALR land at a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill; permits a secondary suite in the ALR only when it is located within the principal residence; and limits an additional residence to up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is the difference between Abbotsford's OCP and its Zoning Bylaw for a duplex lot?** The Zoning Bylaw (2014, No. 2400) regulates what is legally permitted on a parcel today without rezoning; the OCP sets the long-term future land use designation and rezoning policy. The City directs both checks — current zoning, future land uses and development permit areas — to its property information search or WebMap. (§3.)
- **Q: Which Abbotsford plans are actually adopted neighbourhood plans?** The adopted neighbourhood plans listed in the OCP as carried in this program are UDistrict, City Centre, Historic Downtown and McKee, with the McKee Neighbourhood Plan verified as Bylaw 3400-2023, Schedule E of the OCP. - **Q: How do SSMUH rules treat a duplex lot in Abbotsford?** On the frame this slug's research documents, eligible single-family and duplex lots inside the Urban Development Boundary connected to municipal water and sanitary sewer permit typically 3 to 4 units depending on lot size, plus secondary suites and garden suites, with accessory units counting toward the lot total. - **Q: What PTT do I pay at $800,000? At $1,500,000?** On the worked tiers in §3 — fully residential, no exemption — $14,000 at $800,000 and $28,000 at $1,500,000. A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: Can ALR parcels be rezoned for urban subdivisions?** Not through the City alone: removing land from the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and inside the ALR the ALC residence frame and the City's A1/A2 subdivision minimums (8.0 ha / 16.0 ha) govern. (§3.)
