## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Subdivision of farm land.** In the A1 zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is not permitted except where it complies with the Official Community Plan Bylaw and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable; the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel in any case requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **ALR status check.** A specific parcel's Agricultural Land Reserve status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission maps by PID or civic address; the ALC states its online tools are for general reference only, with official confirmation from its Mapping and GIS team. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps

Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a duplex development in Ladner Rural?** None is verified by name in this slug's research or any plain sibling — the ground's documented housing is ALR farm parcels and West River Road float homes. (§2.)
- **Q: Is a farm house plus an accessory farm dwelling a duplex?** No — those are A1 farm-residence forms (one farm house to 500 m², one accessory farm dwelling unit to 90 m² / 186 m² on the same farm home plate), governed by the A1 zone and the ALR regime, not a two-unit duplex title. (§§2–3.) https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a Ladner Rural farm as a second residence?** Only through the ALR routes — ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25, with the Non-Adhering Residential Use application where the use does not adhere. A general entitlement is UNCONFIRMED, parcel by parcel. (§2.)
- **Q: Are the Canoe Pass float homes strata half duplexes?** No — they are float homes in a marina setting per their listing texts, and the community's tenure is UNVERIFIED. They are not duplex stock in any form documented here. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex on this ground?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. None is verified by name here. On A1 land a subdivision needs a minimum 8 ha new lot, OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a farm parcel to create a half-duplex lot?** The A1 rule in §3 governs: 8 ha minimum for a new lot, with the home site severance exception under ALC policy — a half-duplex split of a farm parcel is not a documented form on this ground. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). Farm / mixed-use and float-home PTT treatment is property-specific / UNVERIFIED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
