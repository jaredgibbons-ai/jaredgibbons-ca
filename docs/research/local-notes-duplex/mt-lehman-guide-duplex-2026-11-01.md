## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Duplexes are a permitted two-unit form in the City's residential zones that allow a duplex under the current zoning bylaw text, and the provincial SSMUH implementation makes the duplex the baseline multi-unit form on eligible lots. Mt Lehman is rural ground: on an ALR parcel the Agricultural Land Commission's housing rules govern the residences themselves, and the stratification route is closed by rule. The exact position for a specific Mt Lehman lot is UNCONFIRMED until its zone and ALR status are read. https://www.abbotsford.ca/planning-development (research file above)
- **SSMUH position.** The City implemented the provincial SSMUH rules; the bylaw number carried in some research material as "3560-2024" is held UNCONFIRMED in this program and is never asserted. SSMUH does not rewrite ALR housing rules. The OCP governing is Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted Dec 2025), CURRENT. (Standing Abbotsford frame, `~/workspace/local-notes/mobile-homes-M20-BRIEF.md`.)
- **Secondary suites / second residences — Abbotsford's own rules.** Abbotsford's suite rule is a suite under 50% of the principal dwelling — that is this city's rule, never borrowed from another city's. On an ALR parcel, the ALC additional-residence rules govern instead; confirm per lot. Abbotsford's coach/garden suite figures (15% / 100 m²) apply in Abbotsford ONLY. (Standing Abbotsford frame, brief chain above.)
- **STRATA block — stated for completeness; no strata duplex exists on this ground.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists elsewhere: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, and on ALR land an ALC subdivision position as well; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it, and no such split is documented on this ground. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Abbotsford's Development servicing position is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — no servicing bylaw number is asserted in this file. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer; a farm parcel's PTT follows its own residential / non-residential mix, a per-parcel calculation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
