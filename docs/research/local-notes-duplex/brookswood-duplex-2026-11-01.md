## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC only as **(a) a STRATA duplex** (2-lot strata plan; each half a strata lot; Form B diligence) or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex** (parcel subdivided; party-wall / fee-simple row form where a source documents it). **THE TENURE LINE, which governs the park records below: a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad.** (Program definition, `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**FORM CLASS ONLY for Brookswood, stated at the top — with the feed's own duplex reference carried as a record only.** No named strata duplex development and no named fee-simple half-duplex subdivision is verified in this slug's research or siblings. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning / plans (Township frame).** Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs; zones named in Township notices for this area include Suburban Residential SR-1, SR-2 and SR-3 and Rural Zone RU-2, with the Section 110.1 minimums in §2. The Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan (Booth, Fernridge and Rinn neighbourhood plans under it) allows previously undeveloped areas to grow while protecting trees and watercourses — a parcel's plan designation and neighbourhood plan drive what can be built. R-CL s.403 (220 m² min LOT / 464.5 m² max LOT area) is the Township compact-lot position carried program-wide; **"355 m²" is NOWHERE in the bylaw and is never carried.** (Research file §4; D14 brief Township frame.)
- **SSMUH position.** Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024) — 3 units on eligible lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger eligible lots, **NO 6-plex tier in the Township**. A duplex is the baseline two-unit form; SSMUH does not by itself create separately titled halves. **This slug's DEVELOPMENT-page 6-plex claim is owned by the development-guide files ONLY and is never asserted in this file.** (D14 brief.)
- **Secondary suites + coach houses — the Township's own rules.** Bylaw No. 2500 s.102 caps a secondary suite at **120 m²**, one per principal dwelling unit, building permit required. A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102 (second storey of a detached garage), never strata. Township coach-house/second-dwelling rules for non-reserve Brookswood acreage specifically: UNVERIFIED in this slug's research. On ALR parcels, the ALC housing rules permit one principal residence and, subject to the ALR Use Regulation, one additional residence (≤90 m² on parcels ≤40 ha; ≤186 m² on larger parcels) — Township zoning must also permit it, and a specific parcel's ALR/flood status is UNVERIFIED (check GeoSource). (Research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Servicing / wells & septic / taxes.** Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 is CONFIRMED. On acreage lots, verify the well record in the Province's GWELLS registry (older wells may not appear) and the Fraser Health record of sewerage system + letter of certification for the septic. Township taxes due Thursday, July 2, 2026, 5% at midnight July 2 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026; utility fees billed separately. (D14 brief; research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx, read 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — strata duplex / half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence in full. Depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW the 5-lot threshold on lot count alone**; ask what the strata holds anyway. Minimum CRF 10% since Nov 1, 2023. Rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. Pad-rental park homes (§2) are NOT strata — this block does not apply to them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Township's Approving Officer process against current zoning and plan designations; no finer detail is stated. Party-wall duties rest on the registered easement/agreement on both titles. Before pricing a subdivision, match the lot to the zone minimums in §2. (D1 brief discipline; research file §6.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** 1% on first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. PTT applies to own-land/strata-lot transfers; a pad-rental park home purchase is a chattel purchase, not a land transfer — the mobile sibling carries PTT at the own-land level only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST = new HOUSING only.** Bill C-4 GST/HST Rebate, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** (never March 12): 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (max $50,000), phasing to $1,500,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, max $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST at CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
