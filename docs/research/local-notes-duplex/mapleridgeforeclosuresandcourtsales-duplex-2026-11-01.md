## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**TOPIC — NO ROSTER at the top: this file names no duplex stock as its own and takes no count.** A duplex becomes "court-ordered stock" only for the life of one court file, so any standing roster would be stale on publication. The visible feed cards in this slug's research (a West Central detached house, a Silver Valley land/development listing, a Websters Corners estate, a Southwest Maple Ridge land listing — each card's own text stating "COURT ORDERED SALE" or "Court-Ordered Sale") are feed records at the crawl moment, not a duplex roster, and none is counted here. In process terms, any mortgaged property type can be the subject of a Rule 21-7 proceeding, which by its terms covers "mortgaged property" without a property-type limit — a duplex or half duplex reaches this topic exactly the same way any other mortgaged property does. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **The settled court process, stated at the level the sources support.** Step 1 — the petition: a foreclosure proceeding must be started by petition in the Supreme Court of British Columbia — Rule 21-7 (1); all persons whose interest in or claim to the mortgaged property is sought to be extinguished must be made respondents — Rule 21-7 (2); a petitioner who serves the petition must include the court's informational notice for foreclosure proceedings, Form 125 — Rule 21-7 (4.1). Step 2 — the order and the redemption period: the court may order that a respondent pay what is due under the mortgage and for costs within a redemption period the court may fix, and that in default the respondent is foreclosed of the equity of redemption — Rule 21-7 (5)(b); the court may also make a final order of foreclosure and may determine or certify the amount due — Rule 21-7 (5)(a) and (c). The redemption period has no fixed length in the Rules. Conduct-of-sale, reserve/upset-price, bid-rights and approval mechanics sit in Rule 13-5 and Practice Direction PD-66, with Form 60 certification — carried as the settled program treatment (verified in the Langley foreclosures siblings); this slug's own research pass did not open those texts (see FILE STATUS), so no finer mechanic is asserted from this slug's research alone. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03, read 2026-11-01.
- **Maple Ridge ground rules behind any duplex in a court file.** Duplex / two-unit form under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019; one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, capped at 40% of gross floor area, not within the Floodplain Area; servicing (Subdivision Servicing) Bylaw 4800-1993 (City source); OCP citation CONTESTED (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024), resolved nowhere. Maple Ridge property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026 (5% penalty July 3, 2026; further 5% September 2, 2026) — arrears and adjustments on a court file are a per-file closing question. (Research file above; https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments, read 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), with the court-sale caveat in §2.** In an ordinary resale, Form B diligence applies in full; on a court-ordered sale, Form B availability is UNCONFIRMED (see §2). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — on a court sale, read both titles and the court file. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT / GST on a court sale: UNCONFIRMED — stated plainly, never filled in.** No source in this slug's research states a special property transfer tax or GST treatment for a purchase confirmed through a court-ordered sale, and none is asserted here. The ordinary frame a buyer's lawyer will price against — PTT 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — must be confirmed for the specific court transaction before subjects are removed. Worked anchors on that ordinary frame (fully residential, no exemption): $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; **$3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier** — $93,000 governs at $3.5M. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate, and whether any of it applies unchanged to a court-confirmed purchase is part of the UNCONFIRMED court-sale tax question above. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does this topic file list Maple Ridge's court-ordered duplexes?** No. It is a topic file with no roster; a duplex is court-ordered stock only for the life of one court file, and its form is verified on title and in the court file, not on the card. (§2.)
- **Q: How does a BC foreclosure start?** By petition in the Supreme Court under Rule 21-7 (1); everyone whose interest is sought to be extinguished must be made a respondent, and the petitioner must serve the court's foreclosure information notice, Form 125, with the petition. (§3.)
- **Q: How long is the redemption period?** There is no fixed length in the Rules — the court fixes the period in its order under Rule 21-7 (5)(b). Ask which stage the file is at before spending money. (§3.)
- **Q: Is my accepted offer on a court-ordered duplex final?** Treat it as not final until the court confirms it for that file, and get the conduct-of-sale lawyer's written process for the specific file before booking movers or spending on inspections. (§3; research file.)
- **Q: Can someone outbid my accepted offer?** Bid rights sit in the court's sale directions (Rule 13-5) and PD-66; the live page describes sealed, unconditional competing bids at the hearing, but that is the page's own statement — get the seller's counsel's bid instructions for the specific file in writing. (§2; §3.)
