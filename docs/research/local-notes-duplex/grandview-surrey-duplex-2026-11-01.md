## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan and zoning (Surrey frame):** Surrey OCP = Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is never cited as current; Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs. Grandview Heights development followed the General Land Use Plan and its neighbourhood plans as the live page states them (Morgan Heights, Sunnyside Heights, Orchard Grove, Redwood Heights, Darts Hill, and Area 5) — the page's NCP status table and Area 5 timeline were NOT opened on a City source in the research pass and are UNVERIFIED as stated (§5); the two City-confirmed plan facts are the North Grandview Heights NCP and the Morgan Heights NCP in §1. Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) is VERIFIED from City pages. Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED). Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is ever stated. (Research file, §§1, 4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000; https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans)
- **SSMUH and suites (Surrey frame — the research's settled position):** SSMUH allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the City adopted the SSMUH zoning changes on July 8, 2024 — duplexes are the baseline form. A secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area** under Zoning Bylaw 12000 — Surrey's own cap, never borrowed; a "90 m²" figure is NOT the current Surrey cap and is not used here. (Research file, §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Surrey ground, and to the strata townhouse/condo product in §2 as strata purchases.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process under Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986), and the zone minimums in the current consolidated Bylaw 12000 decide whether a Grandview lot can split at all. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Presales (REDMA) — for the Harlowe presale in §2:** REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s.18. A presale contract is not completed stock and is priced on the disclosure statement, not on resale comparables. (Townhomes sibling, §3; https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ — carried as the sibling's citation.)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey-wide):** renting a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence is required before operating — carried at **$358 per year**, with the City's 2024 **$350 per year** proposal disclosed alongside it. (Settled Surrey frame; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales — relevant on this ground to the Harlowe presale, not to resale houses or townhouses. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Surrey taxes:** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. (Research file, §4; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes)
