## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted — Township.** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs the Township; the Township states "Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 applies to the entirety of the Township." Minimum lot sizes (Bylaw 2500, s. 110.1, as reproduced in a Township council report) include SR-1 — 3,716 m²; SR-2 — 8,094 m²; SR-3 — 1,765 m²; RU-1 — 1.7 ha; RU-2 / RU-3 — 8.0 ha; urban R-1B / R-1C / R-2 — 464.5 m². The zone of a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until read. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf (Research file above.)
- **Where duplexes are permitted — City.** City of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 governs the City; it "applies to the entire area within the boundaries of the City of Langley" and repeals Zoning Bylaw 1996 No. 2100 (the City's media library still serves a consolidated Bylaw 2100 file — Bylaw 3300 is the current source). **The adoption date of Bylaw 3300 is conflicted in sources and is UNVERIFIED in this file; the number (2025 No. 3300) is cited, not a date.** City minimums as printed include R1 Suburban Residential — 557 m² / 16 m frontage; R2 Townhouse Residential — 1,000 m², except lots created by subdivision with a registered party wall agreement (maximum 280 m², minimum frontage 4.5 m); R3 Low Rise Residential — 1,000 m² / 26.0 m. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw (Research file above.)
- **R-CL compact lots — Township s. 403.** The Residential Compact Lot Zone R-CL runs from a minimum lot area of 220 m² to a maximum lot area of 464.5 m². A "355 m²" figure is NOWHERE in the bylaw and is never carried in this file. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf (Lots-and-land sibling above, §3.)
- **SSMUH position — Township.** Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible, and **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020** — a duplex is the baseline two-unit form the bylaw builds on. Added-unit housing eligibility requires a property to be connected to both municipal water and sewer. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Research file above.)
- **SSMUH position — City.** The City's SSMUH bylaw is Bylaw 3284 — City-only, never applied to Township ground, and the Township's Bylaw 6020 is never applied to City ground. (D7 brief frame; lots-and-land sibling above.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — each Langley's own rule, never borrowed.** Township: secondary suite cap 120 m². City: a suite is capped at the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling. The two caps are never interchanged. **LIVE CONFLICT carried from the lots-and-land sibling, flagged, not resolved:** Township Zoning Bylaw s. 102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m² while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted — both positions stand; confirm with the Township for the parcel. (Lots-and-land sibling above, §3; research file above.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), in either Langley.** Form B diligence applies in full (Strata Property Act s. 59; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. The same block governs a bare-land strata lot where one exists. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval: in the Township, all subdivisions must be approved by the Approving Officer under the Land Title Act, through application, review, decision letter, conditions, final approval and registration in the Land Title Office; in the City, Bylaw 3300 states subdivisions are subject to the City's Subdivision Control Bylaw (its bylaw number: UNVERIFIED) and that meeting the minimum lot standards does not bind the Approving Officer to approve. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — the City's R2 exception for lots created by subdivision with a registered party wall agreement (maximum 280 m², minimum frontage 4.5 m) is the one party-wall figure an official source states for this topic. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx and https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw (Research file above.)
- **ALR overlay on subdivision.** Subdivision of Agricultural Land Reserve land requires permission from the Agricultural Land Commission, apart from the limited cases in the ALR General Regulation; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Information Bulletin 09; ALCA s. 21(1); SPA s. 239) — creating a bare-land strata on ALR land is itself a subdivision needing the same permission. ALR housing rules: a principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less with no more than 1,000 m² of fill; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or smaller or 186 m² on larger parcels, where municipal bylaws also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file above.)
- **Servicing.** Township servicing upgrades are determined under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED in the verified ledger; the Township's development servicing forms include Septic Sewer Certification and Well Testing (Forms F2–F5), and all required works and services must be built to bylaw standards before subdivision approval or building permit issuance. Development cost charges: Township DCC Bylaw No. 5897; City DCC Bylaw 2024 No. 3256. Township water and sewer service-area boundaries as a written description: UNVERIFIED. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx (Research file above.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. PTT is payable on a land purchase registered at the Land Title Office unless an exemption applies; a vacant lot's first-time-buyer eligibility is a PTT-return question: UNCONFIRMED here for bare land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate, and bare land itself is not new housing. GST on a vacant-lot purchase is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only: developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt; finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a list of Langley duplexes or half duplexes in this file?** No — this is a lots-and-land topic file with no roster. No named duplex of any form is verified for this topic; the position is the form class (individual duplex buildings on single lots under the zoning of their day), stated in §2.
- **Q: What is the difference between a duplex and a half duplex?** A duplex is the whole two-unit building on one title. A half duplex is one unit sold separately — legally possible only as a 2-lot strata half (Form B diligence) or as a fee-simple half on its own subdivided lot with a party-wall agreement. (§2 definitions.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me split a duplex into two titles?** No — SSMUH adds unit potential on a lot (Township Bylaw 6020: 3–4 units, NO 6-plex tier; City Bylaw 3284); a separately titled half needs a strata plan or a subdivision. (§3.)
- **Q: How big can a suite inside a duplex be?** It depends which Langley: Township cap 120 m²; City cap the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling. Never borrow one municipality's cap for the other. (§3.)
- **Q: Is a coach house a half duplex I can buy separately?** No — in the Township a coach house is a fee-simple second unit on a detached lot under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s. 102, never strata and never a separate title. (§2, §3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). A 2-lot strata sits below the 5-lot depreciation-report threshold on lot count — ask what reports it holds anyway. (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide a duplex on ALR land?** Only with Agricultural Land Commission permission — ALR residences cannot be stratified, and a strata plan on ALR land is itself a subdivision. With 235 km² of the Township in the ALR, run the ALC parcel check first. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a duplex built on a Langley lot?** See §5 — School District 35 catchments follow the parcel address; no catchment is asserted without a documented locator run. (§5.)
