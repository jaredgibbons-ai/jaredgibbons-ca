## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. "Duplex-style" in listing text describes a paired townhouse floor-plan feel; it is not a title form and never makes a townhouse a duplex. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zones permitting a duplex / two-unit form (Surrey).** Zoning By-law No. 12000 governs parcel by parcel (confirmed in COSMOS); the City's suite rule expressly contemplates a suite per "single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit," and its coach-house rule expressly contemplates zones allowing "detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes." The RA acreage figures are carried as UNVERIFIED in §2 and are not restated as permissions here. (Research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000)
- **SSMUH position.** Surrey's zoning allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (SSMUH framework adopted Jul 8, 2024). A duplex is the baseline two-unit form inside that framework — subject, on this ground, to the parcel's zone, ALR status and servicing; most Elgin Chantrell acreage lots need a parcel check before any added-unit claim. (Research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (Surrey rule).** One secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit** (no 90 m² cap was found in the current consolidated bylaw text). Coach houses / garden suites: all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow one; 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house; one extra parking space is required. ALR parcels additionally follow the ALC housing frame: one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); ALR status is a parcel-by-parcel check in the ALC's ALR Property and Map Finder. (Research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), and to strata townhouse diligence at Elgin Pointe.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the Zoning 12000 minimums (on RA ground, against the RA minimums — UNVERIFIED figures, §2); no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. Heritage is a real constraint on the Crescent Road edge: Elgin Heritage Park and the Stewart Farmhouse at 13723 Crescent Road are City-designated heritage entries — a property near that cluster needs its heritage status checked before renovation or subdivision plans are priced. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/heritage-bylaws)
- **Servicing / frame items.** Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED; STR licence **$358/yr** carried with the City's 2024 **$350/yr** proposal disclosed (owner-hosted, primary residence, under 90 consecutive days per patron); streamside setbacks in Part 7A of Zoning 12000, vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure (live checks on the Nicomekl River / Chantrell Creek edge); Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED); Surrey property taxes due July 2, 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2. (Brief chain Surrey frame; research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Elgin Pointe a half-duplex community?** No. It is a gated strata townhouse complex (14655 32 Ave) of duplex-style executive townhouses — counted in its townhomes home, cross-referenced here with no count. - **Q: Can I buy "half" of an Elgin Chantrell duplex?** Only if the pair was legally split — either a 2-lot strata plan (each half a strata lot, Form B diligence) or a fee-simple subdivision (each half on its own lot). An undivided duplex sells whole. No named split pair is verified for Elgin Chantrell in this file. (§2.)
- **Q: What is "Surrey Half Duplexes" in Paragon?** The Paragon Surrey half-duplex grouping (Jared's ruling, Oct 21, 2026) — a search grouping, not a verification that any given card is a legally split half. (§2.)
- **Q: Can an acreage lot here take a duplex or extra units?** Only after a parcel check: zone, ALR status, servicing (well/septic), and streamside setbacks all govern, the RA subdivision figures on the live page are UNVERIFIED against the Part 12 bylaw text, and ALR parcels follow the ALC frame (principal residence to 500 m²; one additional residence to 90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size). Never quote the urban SSMUH framing for an acreage lot without that check. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Can a duplex unit in Surrey have a secondary suite?** One suite per duplex dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price as its own transfer, on the standard tiers — e.g., $1,500,000 → $28,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000, fully residential with no exemption. (§3.)
