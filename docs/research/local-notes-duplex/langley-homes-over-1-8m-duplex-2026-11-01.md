## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it — the Township's coach house is a related fee-simple second-unit form under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102, and is never strata). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A "duplex-style" townhouse is none of these forms: it is a strata townhouse unit whose plan or marketing resembles one half of a duplex pair — built form governs, and it is townhouse stock. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; Township frame above.)
