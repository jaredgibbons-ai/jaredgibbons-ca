## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC only as **(a) a STRATA duplex** (2-lot strata plan; each half a strata lot; Form B diligence) or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex** (parcel subdivided; each half on its own fee-simple lot, party-wall form where a source documents it). A duplex never divided cannot be bought "by the half." (Program definition, `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning (Maple Ridge frame).** Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs; RS-1 subdivision minimums are 668.0 m² lot area, 18.0 m width, 27.0 m depth, max coverage 40%. Which zones apply to specific Silver Valley parcels is UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel; no zone-by-zone duplex permission table is asserted. (Research file above; http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading)
- **SSMUH position (Maple Ridge frame).** Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial SSMUH framework: one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. (Research file above; https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites; https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC PTT: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (max $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, max $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate.
