## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A manufactured home on a RENTED PAD is not a half duplex and not land ownership — the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad. **For clarity on a carried program position: the program's ONE named strata half duplex, at Snowflake Crescent (Hemlock, Plan NWS2041), sits on Hemlock ground at its own form home — it is NOT Stave Falls ground, is not a Stave Falls record, and is imported nowhere in this file.** (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)
