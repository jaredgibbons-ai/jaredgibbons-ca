## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **SSMUH position.** Eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws — a duplex is the baseline two-unit form. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. Settled lot by lot, not by listing price. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new (Research file above.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the city's own rule.** One secondary suite per lot, contained within the single detached building, to a maximum of 40% of gross floor area, and NOT within the Floodplain Area; Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. No size figure is borrowed from another city. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites (Siblings above.)
- **Floodplain at the south edge of the core.** The Port Haney / River Road edge sits along the Fraser River — flood construction level 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, and no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots; for a strata building, flood status is a building-level check on title and in the strata documents. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0 (Research file above.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full (Strata Property Act s. 59; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums and the servicing bylaw below; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Maple Ridge's Subdivision Servicing Bylaw 4800-1993 is carried ONLY from a City source in the program ledger — no finer servicing standard is quoted for any parcel from a listing or summary. (Acreages sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/acreages/maple-ridge-condos-under-800k-acreages-2026-11-01.md; D7 brief frame.)
- **Property taxes (Maple Ridge).** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments (Research file above.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. In this band only the 1% and 2% tiers arise: the townhomes sibling works $525,000 at $8,500 gross ($500 net for a qualifying first-time buyer) and $799,000 at $13,980. The first-time home buyers' program full exemption runs to a fair market value of $835,000 (exempting the first $500,000; partial to $860,000), so a qualifying first-time buyer can in principle use the program anywhere in this band, subject to all the program's other conditions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
