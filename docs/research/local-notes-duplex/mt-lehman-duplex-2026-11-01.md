## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** The workhorse rural zone is the Agricultural One Zone (A1) under Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 — intent: "to accommodate Agricultural and Agri-Tourism uses on lots that are 8.0 ha and larger in size"; principal uses include Agricultural Use, Agri-Tourism, Farm Retail Sales, a Single Detached Dwelling and a Winery, plus accessory family/employee residential uses and a Secondary Suite (Bylaw No. 2400-2014, s.210). That is a single-detached frame, not a duplex frame: on Mt Lehman's rural ground the two-unit question runs through the suite and ALC rules below, not through a duplex permission. The governing OCP is Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted December 2025), which protects the agricultural land base and directs growth to Abbotsford's urban centres. The exact position for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until its zone and ALR status are read. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan (Research file above.)
- **SSMUH position.** The City implemented the provincial SSMUH rules; the bylaw number carried in some research material as "3560-2024" is held UNCONFIRMED in this program and is never asserted. SSMUH does not rewrite ALR housing rules. (Standing Abbotsford frame, brief chain.)
- **Secondary suites / second residences — Abbotsford's own rules + the ALC overlay.** Abbotsford's suite rule is a suite under 50% of the principal dwelling — this city's rule, never borrowed from another city's. Abbotsford's coach/garden suite figures (15% of lot to a maximum 100 m²) apply in Abbotsford ONLY. On an ALR parcel, the ALC's "Housing in the ALR" package governs instead: generally one residence per parcel unless it qualifies for an additional one; a principal residence of 500 m² or less (max 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; anything beyond the permitted number/size needs ALC approval, and the City may further restrict residences. ALC Policy L-26 (amended May 2025): an additional residence beyond the regulation may only be permitted if necessary for a farm use — the farm-worker housing route. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf (Research file above.)
- **STRATA block — stated for completeness; no strata duplex exists on this ground.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists elsewhere: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, and on ALR land an ALC subdivision position as well; Abbotsforward 2050's direction (protect the agricultural base, growth to urban centres) means rural lot-splitting assumptions need a planner's answer first. No finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it, and no such split is documented on this ground. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles. (Research file above; program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Abbotsford's Development servicing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — no servicing bylaw number is asserted in this file. On the ground: fire protection is Abbotsford Fire Rescue Service Fire Hall 5 (Mt. Lehman), 30373 Merryfield Avenue; municipal water vs. well and septic vs. sewer are parcel-specific here — verify per property. https://www.abbotsford.ca/public-safety/fire-rescue-service/fire-safety-events (Research file above.)
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — a value fact on this ground.** Class 9, Farm: land "must produce a prescribed amount of qualifying primary agricultural products for sale, such as crops or livestock"; farm buildings are Class 1, and 50% of Class 9 land's assessed value is exempt from school taxes. Farm class is earned by production, not automatic on ALR land — pull the status per parcel. Current-year City rates are set by Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026; all City of Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026 (penalties legislated, cannot be waived). https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/understanding-property-classes-and-exemptions and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice (Research file above.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer; a farm parcel's PTT follows its own residential / non-residential mix, a per-parcel calculation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate; on ALR land any qualifying new home must first fit the ALC caps above. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any half duplex for sale in Mt Lehman?** None verified, in any source in this program — and the strata form is closed on ALR parcels by rule. A Mt Lehman purchase is a whole parcel under one title: an acreage home, a hobby farm or a working farm. (§2.)

Its 55+ description is listing wording only — it is counted NOWHERE program-wide and sits in the home-file-pass queue for the Abbotsford West home file; never treat it as verified 55+ or as Mt Lehman stock. (§2.)
- **Q: Why is there no Mt Lehman row in board stats?** Because Mt Lehman is a community name, not a board sub-area — its listings are absorbed into the neighbouring rural sub-areas (Bradner/Matsqui side). The Abbotsford community figures in §1 are context only; any "Mt Lehman market" figure from another source is UNVERIFIED. (§1.)
- **Q: Can I add a second residence or suite on a Mt Lehman farm parcel?** On ALR land the ALC package governs: a secondary suite only inside the principal residence as-of-right, and an additional residence only through the farm-use route (Policy L-26) or ALC approval. Off ALR, Abbotsford's suite rule (under 50% of the principal dwelling) and the zoning govern. Confirm per lot. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex (off this ground)?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). On a farm parcel, the residential/non-residential mix drives the calculation per parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Is Mt Lehman in the floodplain?** The community sits on raised ground above Matsqui Prairie — the origin of the "Mount." The prairie at its foot is diked floodplain behind the 11.3 km Matsqui Dike, and the November 2021 disaster was a Sumas Prairie (Nooksack overflow) event, not a Mt Lehman event — parcel-level flood checks still matter for plateau-edge and prairie-floor properties. (§5.)
