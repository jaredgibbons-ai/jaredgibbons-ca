## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**HONEST ZERO — stated at the top of this section.** No duplex or half duplex of any form — whole duplex, strata duplex, or fee-simple half duplex — is verified for this slug on any official, strata, developer, municipal or primary source opened for it. This is the same outcome as the program's downtown-ve and downtown-vw honest-zero files for this same ground class, and it follows this slug's own research and siblings: the downtown peninsula is apartment/commercial form class, and detached houses — let alone duplexes — are not a downtown housing form. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning (Vancouver frame).** The City of Vancouver Zoning and Development Bylaw 3575 governs. Downtown ground itself is downtown-district zoning per this slug's research — the DD schedule governs much of the core (permitted uses per the Downtown District Official Development Plan, including commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses), with CD-1 and C-3A districts in parts of the area; downtown ground is NOT R1-1 streets. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/downtown-vancouver-condos-for-sale.md; https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf, read 2026-11-01.)
- **The R1-1 duplex ladder (Vancouver frame, carried for the duplex form itself).** In the R1-1 district the minimum site area is **306 m² for a house, a duplex, or a 3–4-unit multiple dwelling (10.0 m frontage), 464 m² for 5 units, and 557 m² for 6–8 units**; a house may include a secondary suite plus a laneway house, and the multiplex form runs up to 6 strata units / 8 rental units. (Program Vancouver frame, brief chain.)
- **SSMUH position (Vancouver frame).** **R1-1 IS the SSMUH framework where it applies** — the duplex is the baseline two-unit form inside it. SSMUH adds unit potential on a lot; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves, and it does not turn DD-zoned downtown tower ground into duplex ground. (Brief chain.)
- **Secondary suites / laneway (Vancouver position).** A house in R1-1 may include a secondary suite plus a laneway house, per the frame above; no suite size figure is borrowed from another municipality into a Vancouver file. Whether a specific property's suite is lawful is a parcel read of Bylaw 3575 and the property's permit file. (Brief chain.)
- **Servicing.** Vancouver's servicing (Subdivision) Bylaw 5208 is VERIFIED in the verified ledger (fees bylaw 14538). (Brief chain.)
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY.** Vancouver owners of residential property must submit an annual property status declaration; in this slug's research the declaration was due February 3, 2026 and the 2025 tax payment April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date, and the rate itself was UNVERIFIED in the research pass. The **3%** rate is carried from the program's Vancouver frame — a Vancouver-only item, never exported to another city. (Research file above; https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Property taxes (Vancouver frame).** Advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. The Province's additional school tax can also apply at downtown price points — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. (Research file above; https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx, read 2026-11-01; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate, read 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), wherever one is met.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums (the R1-1 site areas above, where that district applies); no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
