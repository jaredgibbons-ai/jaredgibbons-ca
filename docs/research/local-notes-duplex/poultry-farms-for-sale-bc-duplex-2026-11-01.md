## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**TOPIC — NO ROSTER, stated at the top of this section. No named duplex stock of any form is verified in this topic, and none is invented. A poultry farm sale, in process terms only, can combine ALR land, poultry barns and other farm buildings, a principal residence and possibly one additional residence, plus — separately, and only if the contract states it — production quota, whose transfer position is carried in §3 only on the named basis stated there. None of that is duplex stock: the residences sit on one farm parcel under one title, and on the `poultry-farms` siblings' ALR sources a strata plan causes a subdivision in the ALR, which requires Commission permission after an application or a permission in the ALR General Regulation.**

- **The farm-residence form — the ONLY residence form stated, and only as the ALR sources support it:** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted ONLY when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government or First Nation government bylaws may further regulate or restrict residences, and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. That is a whole-parcel, two-residence form at most — not a duplex, not separable halves. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Manufactured homes on farm ground — ONLY as the ALR sources support:** under the ALR Use Regulation's additional-residence provisions (s. 34(3), as summarised in ALC Policy L-25), an additional residence that is a manufactured home and not a pre-existing structure is permitted only if it is 9 m or less in width and is used only by the owner or specified relatives of the owner; Policy L-25 adds that if it is no longer occupied as permitted it must be demolished, removed, or converted to a non-residential use; an additional residence beyond what the Regulation permits runs through the Non-Adhering Residential Use (NARU) application route. That is a family-occupancy rule, not a park, not a duplex form, and NOT a general farm-worker entitlement — **a general farm-worker housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED in the sources behind this file and is stated nowhere here as fact.** (ALR position as carried in this program's farm-topic siblings: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_-_policy_l-25_-_manufactured_homes_in_the_alr.pdf)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **No single city frame governs this topic.** Municipal zoning, servicing and tax schedules are parcel-by-parcel: the bylaw to read is the one for the municipality the specific farm sits in. For example only, as this slug's own 55-plus sibling carries it: Chilliwack's Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 defines POULTRY as any domesticated fowl including chickens, turkeys, ducks, geese and swans, and lists General Agriculture as a permitted principal use in its AL, AU and AS zones. No frame is blended province-wide in this file. (~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **ALR / farm-residence position (province-wide, the residence rules that actually bind here):** principal residence 500 m² cap (1,000 m² fill limit); suite only within the principal residence; one additional residence to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); manufactured-home additional residences ONLY per ALR Use Regulation s. 34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 as stated in §2, with the NARU route beyond that. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Supply management and quota — carried ONLY on the named basis, stated precisely.** Basis: the `poultry-farms` slug's condos and 55-plus siblings (`~/workspace/local-notes/condos/poultry-farms-condos-2026-11-01.md`; `~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/poultry-farms-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md`), whose research (batch-2 `poultry-farms`, the ONLY research file that opened board sources) opened: **BCCMB General Orders** — for chicken, quota is the amount of chicken, expressed in kilograms of live weight, that the BC Chicken Marketing Board allots to a grower for production and marketing; the General Orders name mainstream quota (regular broilers, roasters, Cornish, certified organic) and specialty quota, and treat transfer of quota with registered premises and acquisition or disposition of quota without registered premises as different cases; the board approves quota transactions, ensuring growers are in good standing and production is allocated appropriately for each growing cycle, and transfers are facilitated privately rather than through a board-run central platform; a BC Chicken Quota Exchange platform was stated by the board to be in development, its operating status NOT CONFIRMED on those sources. **BCCMB annual report** — hatcheries, growers, transporters and processors are licensed by the board on an annual basis. **BC Broiler Hatching Egg Commission annual report** — its regulated product is a broiler hatching egg, a saleable chick or a broiler breeder; mainstream quota there is hen-based (one unit = one placeable broiler breeder hen) and specialty is hatched-chick-based. **BC Turkey Marketing Board scheme material** — turkey quota is the number of pounds of turkey meat a producer is entitled to market in intraprovincial trade through normal channels, or to have marketed on the producer's behalf by the board, during a specified period. **BCFIRB appeals page** — the BC Farm Industry Review Board supervises the regulated marketing system and hears appeals of a board's decision, order or determination, with appeals to be filed within 30 calendar days of the board's final decision. https://bcchicken.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/BCCMB-GENERAL-ORDERS-Aug-26-2011.pdf and https://bcbhec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2022-Annual-Report.pdf and https://bcturkey.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/174_66.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/organizational-structure/ministries-organizations/boards-commissions-tribunals/bc-farm-industry-review-board/npma-appeals
- **What stays UNVERIFIED in this file (unopened everywhere in the sources behind it):** BC Egg Marketing Board layer transfer rules; premises-certification wording; turkey transfer rules beyond the scheme provisions carried above; any quota price or per-unit value in any sector (no official source publishes quota transaction prices on the named basis). - **Farm classification (BC Assessment, province-wide regime as opened in this slug's research):** farm classification is a voluntary program; the income thresholds are $2,500 if the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha; applications must be submitted by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year, late applications are not accepted, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **STRATA block — stated for contrast, because a buyer cross-shopping a strata half duplex meets it there, not on a farm.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists on urban ground in any municipality: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. None of this applies to a freehold poultry operation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** On the `poultry-farms` siblings' ALR sources: a strata plan causes a subdivision in the ALR (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6), and subdivision of agricultural land is prohibited unless permitted by the Commission after an application or permitted under the ALR General Regulation — which is why the farm form cannot be split into halves by strata. Off ALR land, a fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the applicable municipality's Approving Officer process; party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles. No finer process detail is stated because no official source behind this file states it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (province-wide general tiers).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. **How PTT and GST are apportioned between land, buildings, residence and quota on a specific poultry purchase is UNVERIFIED in this file** — no Province GST/PTT farm-transfer source was opened in this slug's research pass; have an agricultural lawyer and accountant model it on the actual asset allocation. A half duplex, where one ever exists, is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A farm operation is not new housing for this rebate, and a resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing either. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
