## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC only as **(a) a STRATA duplex** (2-lot strata plan; each half a strata lot; Form B diligence) or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex** (parcel subdivided; party-wall / fee-simple row form where a source documents it). A duplex never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half." (Program definition, `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning / OCP (Surrey frame).** OCP Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026) governs — never cite 18020 as current. Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs zones. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — **no single riparian figure is ever stated**. (D14 brief Surrey frame; research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000, read 2026-11-01.)
- **SSMUH position.** Surrey allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (SSMUH changes Jul 8, 2024). A duplex is the baseline two-unit form inside that ladder; SSMUH does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Research file above; City SSMUH page above.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Surrey's own rule.** Under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit**. The often-quoted "90 m²" cap is NOT current and is never stated as current. Coach houses/garden suites: 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR, one extra parking space required. (Research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Short-term rentals.** Only an owner living at the property as their primary residence may host; tenants cannot run one; a business licence is required. The licence figure is carried as **$358/yr, with the City's 2024 $350/yr proposal disclosed as a conflict** — neither figure is silently adopted alone. (Research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Servicing / courts / taxes.** Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) is VERIFIED from City pages. Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (registry mapping UNVERIFIED — never filled in). Property taxes due July 2, 5% penalty after midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. (D14 brief; research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes, read 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — strata duplex / half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, so that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; ask what reports the strata holds anyway. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since Nov 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process against current zoning; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement/agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (D1 brief discipline.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC PTT: 1% on first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (max $50,000), phasing to $1,500,000, agreements on or after Mar 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, max $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST carried at CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a named duplex development I can shop city-wide in Surrey?** No named one is verified at city level. Surrey duplex stock here is the form class — individual duplexes on single lots, plus the R6 semi-detached form — with named components documented only in sub-area home files. (§2.)
- **Q: What does "Surrey Half Duplexes" mean?** It is the Paragon board grouping for Surrey half-duplex product (Jared's ruling) — a search grouping, not proof that any given listing carries a separable half title. Verify strata plan or subdivision on title. (§2.)
- **Q: Duplex vs half duplex — what changes?** A duplex purchase is the whole building and lot on one title. A half is buyable only where the pair was strata-divided (2-lot plan, Form B) or the parcel subdivided fee-simple (party-wall title). (§2.)
- **Q: How many units can a Surrey lot hold?** 3 on lots under 280 m², 4 on larger lots, up to 6 on eligible lots ≥281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. (§3.)
- **Q: How big can a suite inside a Surrey duplex be?** One suite per dwelling unit, habitable area at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite. There is no current 90 m² cap. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to 5+ lot stratas since Jul 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold. Ask what it holds and run full Form B diligence regardless. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget?** Anchors: $800,000 → $14,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I run a short-term rental in a Surrey duplex?** Only as an owner living there as your primary residence, with a business licence — carried at $358/yr with the City's 2024 $350/yr proposal disclosed as a conflict. Tenants cannot host. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Surrey duplex address?** SD36 catchments are address-based and UNVERIFIED for any address in this city file — run the exact address in the district locator. Ministry-listed names are in §5. (§5.)
