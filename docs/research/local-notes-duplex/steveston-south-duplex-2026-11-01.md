## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Richmond Zoning Bylaw No. 8500).** Houses sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with secondary suite rules in Section 5.4 and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL added under provincial Bill 44 legislation; the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the OCP, Bylaw 9000) guides the planning area, and the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area at the district's south-west waterfront carries Heritage Alteration Permit requirements for properties inside it. Flood construction is Bylaw No. 8204 — a live rule on this low, dike-protected ground. The exact zone table for a specific lot is read off the current Bylaw 8500 text for that lot — zone names asserted beyond that read are UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML (Research file §4; settled Richmond frame.)
- **SSMUH position — Richmond's own, as the City states it.** The City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots; the duplex is the baseline form under that framework. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm (Research file §§6–7.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Richmond's own position, as its sources state it.** In a single-detached house the suite runs under Section 5.4 of Bylaw 8500: at least 33.0 m², at most the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m². That is Richmond's own figure, carried from this ground's own research — no other city's cap is borrowed. For a strata unit, the strata's bylaws also govern, and the suite position is UNCONFIRMED until read in both. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf (Research file §5.)
- **Flood.** The district fronts the South Arm of the Fraser River behind the City dike system; Richmond is a low-lying island city in the Fraser River floodplain protected by a perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations, designed for a 1:500 flood event with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres, and the City's Steveston Island Dike project addresses the Steveston waterfront and harbour. The Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements — confirm both for waterfront and dike-adjacent lots before pricing in a rebuild or addition. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles; a general party-wall rule for this ground is UNCONFIRMED. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Richmond servicing Bylaws 8751/8752 VERIFIED in the verified ledger. ADEU charges are West Cambie / Alexandra ONLY — no ADEU position is carried for Steveston South. (Verified ledger; D20 frame.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Steveston South?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. No such property is verified by name here; a single-lot duplex sells whole. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is there a named duplex development in Steveston South?** None verified. Steveston's verified strata stock is the London Landing townhouse count (3 complexes, in the lower-landing townhomes file) and the 2 condos-program buildings — none of it duplex-form stock counted here. (§2.)
- **Q: Is it "Lower Landing" or "London Landing"?** London Landing is the official name; "Lower Landing" is not a Richmond name and survives only in the count home's file slug. The place is the same; the official name governs in documents. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). With two lots, every common expense splits two ways. (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside my half?** In a detached house the suite runs under Section 5.4 (minimum 33.0 m²; maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling or 110 m² — Richmond's own figures); a strata half also needs the strata's bylaws checked. Read both before counting rental income. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Are the queued Steveston complexes (Village building, McKinney Walk) duplexes?** They are documented-not-counted townhouse-form names in the townhomes set, with tenures partly UNCONFIRMED — none is duplex-verified, and none is counted here. (§2.)
