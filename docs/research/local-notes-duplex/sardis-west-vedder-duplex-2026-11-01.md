## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A manufactured home described as "duplex-style" is none of these: it is a home FORM on a rented pad, not a legal duplex tenure. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs; the SSMUH amendment (below) speaks to parcels "zoned for single-detached and duplex housing," which is the bylaw's own confirmation that duplex zoning exists as a class in this city. The zone for a specific Sardis lot is read off the current consolidated Bylaw 5000 text — and any "duplex infill" density claim for a specific lot is confirmed with the City, per the §2 flag. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf (research file above)
- **SSMUH position.** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, adopted June 18, 2024, allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing — the duplex is the baseline form the amendment builds on. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies** — it is a lot-zoning framework, not a park-tenancy right (the Cottonwood record above is untouched by it). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3090/Signed%20Staff%20Report%20%2D%20RZ001761%2Epdf
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Chilliwack's own rule.** Only 1 accessory dwelling unit per lot where the zone permits: a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space (Bylaw No. 5000, s. 2; s. 4.06(1)), and garden suites are capped at 100 m². The City's Secondary Suites Policy permits rental of a suite in any single detached dwelling regardless of zone, with a building permit, BC Building and Fire Code compliance and flood-protection compliance; new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. Those are Chilliwack's figures, never borrowed from another city's. https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles; a general party-wall rule for this ground is UNCONFIRMED. - **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. (A home-only purchase on a rented pad at Cottonwood is a different structure — its PTT treatment is UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule in this program and is confirmed per transaction by a lawyer or notary.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Cottonwood Village a duplex development?** No. It is a pad-rental manufactured-home park at 7610 Evans Road; listings describe some homes there as "duplex-style townhouse-form" — copy about the home's shape, not its tenure. You would own a home as a chattel and rent its pad, under the manufactured-home park regime — not buy a duplex or a half duplex. (§2 record.)
- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Sardis West Vedder?** Only if a side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. No such property is verified on this ground; a single-lot duplex sells whole. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is there a named duplex development here?** None verified. The verified attached roster is 5 apartment-condo buildings (condos program); the duplex evidence is the Cottonwood record and an UNVERIFIED plan-framing line, both carried as what they are. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). With two lots, every common expense splits two ways. (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a house here have a suite AND a coach house?** No — only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits (suite at the lesser of 90 m² / 40%, coach house, or garden suite at 100 m² cap), under Bylaw 5000, ss. 2 and 4.06. The SSMUH route (3–4 units on eligible parcels, Bylaw 5395) is a different route with its own eligibility. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for Sardis West Vedder?** No. CADREB publishes no area stats and SnapStats does not cover Chilliwack. (§1.)
