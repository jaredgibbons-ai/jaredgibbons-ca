## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. The research's own strata note supports the strata limb: under BC strata law, strata housing includes condos, townhouses and duplexes. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing, read in the research file §4.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Port Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw No. 3630).** The two-unit / row form runs through the rowhouse zone RRh, the townhouse zones RTh1–RTh3 and the RS1–RS4 single-detached zones (with suites / SSMUH below); apartment zones RA1 (FAR 1.0, increasable to 1.5 with required parking underground / in structure, further to 2.0 on a $50/sq ft amenity contribution) and RA2 (FAR up to 2.5) govern the downtown apartment form, not duplexes. The zone for a specific lot is read off the current Bylaw 3630 text for that lot. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2025-08/3630%20-%20Zoning%20Bylaw%202008.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **SSMUH position (City position as the research states it).** RS-zoned lots across the city can hold 3 to 6 small-scale multi-unit housing units without rezoning, depending on lot size, location and transit proximity. That is the City's own published range, stated as the City states it — duplexes are the baseline two-unit form within it. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — PoCo's own position.** The research states only that "secondary suite rules apply" alongside the SSMUH position; no PoCo suite size cap is stated in this slug's research or siblings, so NO cap figure is carried here — no other city's figure is borrowed. The suite position for a specific home is UNCONFIRMED until read in Bylaw 3630's current text. (Research file §4.)
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex.** Form B diligence applies in full (for Mayfair Terrace, ask for the current service agreement alongside the Form B — the mandatory service plan sits on top of strata fees). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**, stated precisely. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions — the frame for Mayfair Terrace's registered 55+ position, the city's only one); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Approving Officer process; no finer PoCo process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the titles — read them. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** PoCo servicing UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — never asserted. (D19 frame.)
- **Tax note (research-stated only):** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes unpaid after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026; utility bills are flat annual water and sewer fees, billed separately and due March 31, 2026. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes (Research file §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a named duplex complex in downtown PoCo?** No — none is verified by name. The duplex / row picture is the form class: RRh rowhouses, RTh townhouses and RS lots holding suites or 3–6 SSMUH units, house by house. (§2.)
- **Q: What is Mayfair Terrace — is it a duplex option?** No — it is a 46-unit apartment-form strata condo (LMS4171, built 2000), PoCo's one verified 55+ ownership complex, counted in the city-wide condos file. Budget for the mandatory Aspira service plan on top of strata fees (listing-stated $715/mo first resident / $400 second). (§2.)
- **Q: How many units can an RS lot hold without rezoning?** 3 to 6 small-scale multi-unit housing units, depending on lot size, location and transit proximity, per the City's published position. (§3.)
- **Q: Does PoCo have SkyTrain?** No — Port Coquitlam Station is West Coast Express (weekday peak-direction). The nearest SkyTrain is Coquitlam Central; a PoCo SkyTrain extension is an exploratory business case only, never approved or funded in any source. (§1.)
- **Q: If I buy half of a strata duplex, what diligence applies?** Form B diligence in full — and a 2-lot strata is below the 5-lot depreciation-report threshold, so ask what reports actually exist. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. A registered 55+ bylaw (Mayfair Terrace) still limits who may reside. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a PoCo duplex?** Secondary suite rules apply, but no PoCo cap figure is carried in this program — read Bylaw 3630 for the lot before counting rental income. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Is downtown PoCo in a floodplain?** Much of it is — low-lying central land sits in the Fraser / Pitt River floodplain behind dykes. Check the specific lot with the City. (§1.)
- **Q: Is there a manufactured-home park in PoCo?** No — in any source; the siblings agree no park exists inside the city limits. A park buyer widens the search to another municipality. (§2; mobile-homes sibling.)
