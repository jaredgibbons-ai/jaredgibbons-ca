## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zones permitting a duplex / two-unit form (Surrey frame).** Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 governs this ground; its SSMUH framework (in force since **July 8, 2024**) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, and the City states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits — directly relevant here, because the feed's detached listings lean on basement suites. Land-use designations come from the **OCP — Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026), NEVER 18020-as-current**; a finer City planning breakdown of this exact ground is UNVERIFIED in the research (fetch 429, stopped rather than retried), so no planning name is asserted for the sub-area beyond the City community framing in §1. Servicing: **Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED** from City pages. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (Surrey's OWN rule).** One secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area**, never inside a coach house or garden suite, and not a separate strata lot; no 90 m² cap was found in the current consolidated bylaw text (per the program's rules-gaps verification). A coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house, and one extra parking space is required. Short-term rentals are permitted, owner-only (the host must be the owner living at the property as their primary residence); the licence figure is carried as **$358 per year** per the City's Short-Term Rentals page, with the conflict disclosed: the City's 2024 corporate report proposed **$350 per year plus fees** — confirm the current figure on the City's licence page before quoting it (this slug's own research could not open the City STR page — fetch 429). Surrey property taxes: due July 2, 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2, further 5% after September 2. (Research file above, citing ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on this ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. Fee-simple contrast, as the townhomes sibling states it: a fee-simple property carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules (Zoning 12000) plus any registered covenants govern instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the Zoning 12000 minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Exemptions as the siblings state them: First-Time Home Buyers' Program — full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. The condos sibling's GST point for this ground: 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — both Kings Landing buildings are completed stock trading as resales, so GST turns on the specific unit's status, not the building's age alone. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for the FTHB rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: A card shows a half duplex here — can I buy just that half?** Only if that half is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot. The card settles nothing; check the title. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay on a home here?** The general tiers on the property's own price — for example $1,200,000 fully residential with no exemption = $22,000; a half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer (§3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a coach house to a house here?** One secondary suite per dwelling unit (at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite), a coach house or garden suite of 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR, lane access required for a coach house on smaller lots, one extra parking space), and 3–4 SSMUH units on most lots by size band, up to 6 near a frequent bus stop. A suite built without permits is NOT retroactively legalized by the SSMUH changes — verify a suite's permit status rather than relying on listing text. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and a Surrey STR needs the owner-only City licence ($358/yr on the City's page — see §3 for the disclosed $350 conflict). (§3.)
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Is there a 55+ duplex or townhouse here?** None verified inside the sub-area — the 55-plus sibling found no 55+ ownership complex of any form here; the nearest verified 55+ stock is in Fleetwood Tynehead, Queen Mary Park, West Newton and East Newton, per that sibling. (§2.)
