## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000).** Surrey's SSMUH amendments renamed the former RF zone to R3 Urban Residential Zone (Part 15) and the former RF-SD zone to **R6 Semi-Detached Residential Zone (Part 18)**; the former RM-D Duplex Residential Zone was removed and grouped by lot size — the current zones are R3 and R6. Permitted small-scale multi-unit forms are secondary suites, detached accessory dwelling units including garden suites and coach houses, duplexes (side-by-side or up-and-down) and houseplexes (triplexes and fourplexes). In R6, a zero-lot line semi-detached building on a lot under 280 sq. m may accommodate 2 to 3 units and a duplex on a lot over 280 sq. m may accommodate 4 to 6 units; R6 subdivision minimums are interior lot area 200 sq. m, width 7.2 m, depth 28 m and corner lot area 226 sq. m, width 8.7 m, depth 28 m, with a 0.0 m side yard permitted at the common side lot line between paired lots and maximum lot coverage of 60 percent. Those are Surrey-wide rules; Port Kells' own parcels are predominantly industrial and ALR zoning, where the ALC position governs farm residences. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH position.** Surrey's SSMUH changes took effect July 8, 2024: a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — on eligible residential lots; SSMUH does not rewrite ALR or industrial zoning. The OCP governing is Surrey 2050, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026); Bylaw No. 18020 is the replaced predecessor and is never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Surrey's own rule, off-ALR parcels.** One secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, one per unit, not a separate strata lot, and never inside a coach house or garden suite. ALR parcels follow the ALC rules instead. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **STRATA block — stated for completeness; no strata duplex exists on this ground.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists elsewhere in Surrey: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, and on ALR land an ALC subdivision position as well; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. Surrey's documented R6 mechanism is stated in the zoning line above. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Surrey servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) per the verified ledger. REGISTRY LEDGER: Surrey has NO manufactured-home registry in the ledger — nothing registry-related is asserted for this file. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer; an industrial or farm parcel's PTT follows its own residential / non-residential mix, a per-parcel calculation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any half duplex for sale in Port Kells?** None verified, in any source in this program — the sub-area is industrial and ALR ground, and every sibling program verifies zero attached stock here. (§2.)
- **Q: A listing calls a Port Kells property a duplex — can I buy one side?** Almost certainly not: without a strata plan or a subdivided lot there is no separate half, and on ALR parcels residences cannot be stratified at all. The wording is a record only; the title is the verification. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is Barnston Island part of Port Kells for a purchase?** No — the island is Electoral Area A plus Katzie IR 3, not Surrey; it only codes to Port Kells in board data because the ferry lands here. Island purchases follow the island's own files and rules. (§1.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex (elsewhere in Surrey)?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a duplex here?** Off-ALR, Surrey's rule applies (one suite in a semi-detached or duplex dwelling, 40% cap, never in a coach/garden suite); on ALR parcels the ALC rules govern. Confirm per lot. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
