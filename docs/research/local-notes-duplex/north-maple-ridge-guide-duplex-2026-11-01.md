## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Secondary suites (Maple Ridge's own rule, never borrowed):** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area. A suite inside a duplex follows the same City rule as carried in the research — no other city's cap (Surrey's 40% of principal habitable area is a different measure, the Township's 120 m², Langley City's or Abbotsford's figures) is borrowed into this file. - **Floodplain and ALR:** the City publishes North and South Alouette River and Fraser River floodplain maps; the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), with no habitable floor space below that level on affected lots and rental accommodation (suites) prohibited on floodplain properties under Zoning Bylaw s.402.8(h). ALR status is per-parcel — the City describes Yennadon as an active agricultural production area, but check the ALC's mapping for any specific lot; on ALR parcels the provincial ALR rules also govern, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. (Research file, §4; https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Maple Ridge ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. This block currently applies to nothing named on this ground (§2) — it is the diligence frame if a strata half duplex is ever verified here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process under the servicing frame above, and the zone minimums in Bylaw 7600-2019 (RS-1 at 668.0 m²; the rural/agricultural minimums far larger) decide whether a lot on this ground can split at all. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file, §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
