## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**TOPIC — NO ROSTER at the top: this file names no duplex stock as its own and takes no count.** This topic has no housing stock of its own; the "profile" is what the four municipalities' plans collectively govern: housing location and density are set municipality by municipality through each OCP's land use designations and then parcel by parcel through each zoning bylaw — verified in this research for Delta, and at the lookup level for Abbotsford. The duplex content is the FORM CLASS those plans create:

- **The verified Delta form class.** Single-detached and duplex housing under the RS/RD zones of Delta's zoning bylaw (the RD zones are Delta's duplex zones), with Delta's SSMUH updates allowing up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots. The duplex form class is the individual duplex building on a single lot built under the zoning of its day — a FORM CLASS statement; no individual duplex is verified here by name. - **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A plan designation does not change transaction tax — PTT runs on the standard tiers, and a half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate, and the rebate does not apply to bare land itself. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which municipalities does this page cover?** Four, per the live page's title: Surrey, Langley, Abbotsford and Delta — each of which plans through its own Official Community Plan and zoning bylaw; there is no single shared land use plan document for the four. (§1.) https://jaredgibbons.ca/land-use-plans.html
- **Q: What is an Official Community Plan?** In Delta's own description — the verified example here — an OCP describes where and what type of housing can be added across the city and contains long-term objectives and policies that guide decisions about land use, development, services, amenities and infrastructure. (§3.) https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: What is Delta's current land use plan?** The Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), adopted July 8, 2024: a single consolidated plan with the Land Use Plan at s. 1.5, designations at s. 1.5.1 on Map 2, and no Schedule D.1 or community area-plan schedules (those belonged to the superseded Bylaw No. 3950). (§3.)
- **Q: How do I check zoning and future land use for an Abbotsford property?** The City of Abbotsford's Development Planning pages direct property checks to the City's property information search or a property search in WebMap for current zoning, future land uses and development permit areas. (§3.) https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/development-planning
- **Q: Does an OCP designation settle what I can build?** No. In the verified Delta model, the OCP designation is the first of three layers — designation (s. 1.5.1 / Map 2), then the parcel's zone on the zoning maps, then any Development Permit Area — and on ALR land the Agricultural Land Commission's rules apply on top of all three. (§3.)
- **Q: The seller's documents cite an old plan — what do I do?** Treat it as a dating problem, not a fact: Delta's governing OCP is Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) and any Tsawwassen Area Plan / Schedule D.1 reference is the superseded Bylaw No. 3950 — the same dating check has to be run in Surrey, Langley and Abbotsford before a plan claim goes into an offer or a listing. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). A favourable plan designation changes use, not tax. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
