## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC only as **(a) a STRATA duplex** (2-lot strata plan; each half a strata lot; Form B diligence) or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex** (parcel subdivided; each half on its own fee-simple lot, party-wall form where a source documents it). A duplex never divided cannot be bought "by the half." (Program definition, `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **SSMUH position (Mission frame).** Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial SSMUH framework. Mission's SSMUH Bylaw is 6299-2024 (the City's SSMUH page states it as Bylaw 6299-2024-5949(163), adopted July 17, 2024, applying to zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings); its unit counts are UNCONFIRMED per the D12 frame and none is asserted. (55-plus sibling above; D12 brief Mission frame; https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (Mission's own rule, never borrowed).** In the City of Mission all secondary suites must be registered with the City; a suite is a self-contained living space in a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; a new suite goes through the building permit process (the 55-plus sibling adds a $63 annual suite fee plus utility fees, per the City program). A detached coach home or garden cottage is a separate detached accessory dwelling handled by zone. Feed cards' suite/coach zoning claims (UC372, RR7s) are UNVERIFIED against the bylaw. (Research file above; https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program)
- **Servicing (Mission frame).** Development and Subdivision Control Bylaw 5650-2017, from City sources, per the D12 frame. Parcel-level servicing status for any lot on this feed: UNVERIFIED (§2). (D12 brief Mission frame; research file above.)
- **STRATA block — strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that threshold, so the cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; ask what reports the strata holds anyway. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since Nov 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the governing approving authority (City of Mission Approving Officer for City parcels; the FVRD / provincial process for electoral-area parcels) — and on ALR land, ALC approval as well (§2). No finer process detail is stated, because no subdivision document was opened in this slug's research. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Research file above; program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Taxes — TWO REGIMES, NEVER BLENDED (Mission frame).** City of Mission lots: 2026 taxes due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a **FLAT 10% penalty** on current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026. Rural (FVRD) lots: the owner receives the notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province; under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural taxes are due July 2, with a **5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31** — a DISTINCT regime from the City's flat 10%, stated separately and never blended. Mission has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED). (Research file above; https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC PTT: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (max $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, max $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. It attaches to a new home built/bought under its rules, not to a bare lot price. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a named duplex development I can shop on this land topic?** No named one is verified. This is a band/topic file: the stock position is the form class, and the one duplex wording on the feed — a card's "PLA APPROVED FOR DUPLEX" claim — is an UNVERIFIED listing-text record, not an approval this file can stand behind. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I build a duplex on a Mission-area lot from this feed?** It depends on which government governs the parcel (City of Mission vs FVRD electoral area), its zone, SSMUH position and ALR status. Verify the parcel's actual zone and any approvals with the governing authority before marketing density, suite or coach-home potential. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: What is the difference between a duplex and a half duplex?** A duplex purchase is the whole building and lot on one title. A half duplex is one unit sold separately — only where the pair was strata-divided (2-lot plan, Form B diligence) or the parcel was subdivided fee-simple (party-wall title). (§2.)
- **Q: Is the Everglades Resort card a lot I can build a duplex on?** No. That card is a resort use-area (40' x 40') inside Everglades Resort on Hatzic ground — an undivided-interest/use-area record, not a fee-simple lot and not land ownership. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a home I build here carry a suite or a coach house?** City side: a secondary suite must be registered with the City (building permit for a new suite; suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes); a detached coach home or garden cottage is handled by zone; the SSMUH framework (Bylaw 6299-2024) governs small-scale multi-unit forms on former single-family/duplex zones, unit counts UNCONFIRMED. Feed zoning claims for a specific lot are UNVERIFIED until checked against the bylaw. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget?** Run the tiers on your own price: $800,000 → $14,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 (fully residential, no exemption). A half duplex is taxed on its own price. (§3.)
- **Q: Who do I pay property tax to on a vacant lot — and what is the penalty?** The government the lot sits in, and the regimes are distinct: City lots pay the City (due July 2, 2026; FLAT 10% penalty July 3); rural FVRD lots pay the Province (due July 2; 5% + 5% under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, the second 5% on amounts unpaid Oct 31). Never blend the two. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a lot I build on?** SD75 catchments are address-based and catchment schools for any specific lot address are UNVERIFIED — confirm the exact address with the district. Ministry-confirmed Mission-area schools are in §5. (§5.)
- **Q: Are the market numbers in this file land or duplex prices?** Neither. SnapStats publishes no land-only split and no duplex category; §1's figures are Mission community dwelling context only, and land-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. (§1.)
