## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**TOPIC — NO ROSTER, stated at the top of this section. No named duplex stock of any form is verified in this topic, and none is invented. A poultry farm sale, in process terms only, can combine ALR land, poultry barns and other farm buildings, a principal residence and possibly one additional residence, plus — separately, and only if the contract states it — production quota, whose position in this slug's research is UNVERIFIED (§3). None of that is duplex stock: the residences sit on one farm parcel under one title, and a strata plan on ALR land is a subdivision requiring ALC permission on the ALR sources in this program.**

- **The farm-residence form — the ONLY residence form stated, and only as the ALR sources support it:** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted ONLY when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may be more restrictive. That is a whole-parcel, two-residence form at most — not a duplex, not separable halves. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Manufactured homes on farm ground — ONLY as the ALR sources support:** under the ALR Use Regulation's additional-residence provisions (s. 34(3), as summarised in ALC Policy L-25), an additional residence that is a manufactured home and not a pre-existing structure is permitted only if it is 9 m or less in width and is used only by the owner or specified relatives of the owner; Policy L-25 adds that if it is no longer occupied as permitted it must be demolished, removed, or converted to a non-residential use; an additional residence beyond what the Regulation permits runs through the Non-Adhering Residential Use (NARU) application route. That is a family-occupancy rule, not a park, not a duplex form, and NOT a general farm-worker entitlement — **a general farm-worker housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED in this slug's sources and is stated nowhere in this file as fact.** (This slug's 55-plus sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/chilliwack-poultry-farms-for-sale-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_-_policy_l-25_-_manufactured_homes_in_the_alr.pdf)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **The governing frame for this topic is the farm frame, not a duplex zone table.** Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs under OCP Bylaw 5500 "Chilliwack 2050" (Schedule B1 Agricultural Area Plan; Schedule D includes D-4 Farm Home Plate). The zone of a specific farm parcel is UNCONFIRMED until read. Chilliwack's SSMUH bylaw is Bylaw 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024) — an urban-lot framework (3 to 4 units on eligible parcels inside the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing) that does not create strata or duplex product on ALR farm land; on ALR land the ALR rules below govern the residence question. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Secondary suites — Chilliwack's own rule, never borrowed, and the ALR overlay.** Under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 (ss. 2, 4.06, 5.04, confirmed in rules-gaps item 7f): a secondary suite is capped at the **lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling**, one accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot, and garden suites are capped at 100 m². On ALR land, a secondary suite is permitted ONLY inside the principal residence (ALC rule). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/chilliwack-homes-under-1-8m.md research lineage; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, item 7f; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **ALR / farm-residence position (the residence rules that actually bind here):** principal residence 500 m² cap (1,000 m² fill limit); suite only within the principal residence; one additional residence to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); manufactured-home additional residences ONLY per ALR Use Regulation s. 34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 as stated in §2, with the NARU route beyond that. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm classification (BC Assessment, province-wide regime as opened in this slug's research):** farm classification is a voluntary program; the income thresholds are $2,500 if the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha; applications must be submitted by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031.
