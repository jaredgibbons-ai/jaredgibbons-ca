## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan, zoning, SSMUH and suites (Surrey frame):** the current Surrey plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is replaced and is NEVER cited as current. Zoning Bylaw 12000 governs. SSMUH in force Jul 8, 2024: a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — duplexes are the baseline SSMUH form. The City defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit whose habitable floor area does not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area**, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex building, and not a separate strata lot; only one suite is permitted per dwelling unit — Surrey's own cap, never borrowed. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is ever stated. (Research file, §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000)

- **STRATA block — applies to Blu Living purchases (a strata complex) and to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan):** Form B diligence applies in full — the live page itself directs a buyer to the Form B information certificate, the strata bylaws and rules, the last two years of minutes and the depreciation report, and to confirm what the monthly fees cover, before subjects are removed. - **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math:** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer.
