## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A presale contract itself is not a strata lot — it is a developer contract under REDMA (§3) until completion and strata-plan registration. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **REDMA block — core to this file (stated from the Act and the regulator):** **REDMA s.21 — 7-day rescission:** a purchaser may rescind a purchase agreement by serving written notice on the developer within seven days after the later of the date the purchase agreement was made and the date the developer obtained the purchaser's receipt for the Disclosure Statement. **REDMA s.18 — deposits in trust:** purchase deposits must be held in trust as the Act requires. The developer must give each purchaser a copy of the filed Disclosure Statement, and any filed amendment, before entering into the agreement (s.15, per the regulator's guidance). The disclosure statement — not a roundup page, not the presentation centre — governs completion dates, deposits and what is being bought; have a lawyer read it inside the 7-day window. (Research file, §4; https://www.bcfsa.ca/industry-resources/real-estate-developer-resources/real-estate-development-marketing-act-faqs, read via search result 2026-11-01; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01.)
- **Plan, zoning, SSMUH and suites — each side's frame only as the research states it (Township ground vs City ground are never blended):** Township of Langley — Zoning Bylaw 2500; SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024; NO 6-plex tier); servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED; suite cap 120 m²; coach house = fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata. Langley City — a SEPARATE municipality: Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 (number only); SSMUH Bylaw 3284 (City-only); suite = lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the dwelling. Duplexes are the baseline SSMUH form on both sides. No Township rule is asserted for a City parcel and no City rule for a Township parcel in this file. (Settled frames, D15 brief; research file, §4.)
- **STRATA block — applies once a presale project completes and registers, and to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full (a presale has no Form B until the strata exists — the REDMA disclosure statement is the presale equivalent). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the applicable municipality's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math, plus the newly-built exemption verified in this research.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. **Newly Built Home Exemption (verified, matching the live page's thresholds):** effective April 1, 2024 the full-exemption threshold is a fair market value of $1,100,000, with a partial exemption phasing out over the $50,000 range above it and complete elimination at $1,150,000 — and the buyer does not need to be a first-time buyer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption, read via search result 2026-11-01.
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only — directly relevant to every true presale.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales — a resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html, read via search result 2026-11-01, and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

- **Q: Can a first-time buyer get the GST back on a new Langley home?** Yes: under Bill C-4 (Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA) an eligible first-time buyer receives a rebate of 100% of the GST on a newly constructed home valued up to $1 million, gradually reduced between $1 million and $1.5 million, with no rebate at $1.5 million or more.
