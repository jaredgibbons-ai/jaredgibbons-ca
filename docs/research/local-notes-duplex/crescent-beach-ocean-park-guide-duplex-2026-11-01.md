## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it; Surrey also carries the R6 semi-detached / zero-lot-line form in its zoning history, stated as a form class only). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. **"Surrey Half Duplexes" IS the Paragon Surrey half-duplex grouping** (Jared's ruling, Oct 21, 2026) — stated as the board grouping it is where a Surrey feed or research uses it; it is a feed grouping, not a verified roster for this sub-area. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Zoning, SSMUH and suites (Surrey frame — the city's own rules, never borrowed):** Zoning Bylaw 12000 governs. SSMUH allows a maximum of **3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop**; the City adopted the SSMUH zoning changes on **July 8, 2024** — duplexes are the baseline form. A secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area** under Zoning Bylaw 12000 — Surrey's own cap; a "90 m²" figure is NOT the current Surrey cap and is not used here. Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) is VERIFIED. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is ever stated. - **Short-term rentals (Surrey frame):** Surrey's STR frame is owner-occupied with a licence carried at **$358/yr**, with the City's 2024 **$350/yr** proposal disclosed as a conflict in the settled frame — both figures stated, neither suppressed. (Settled Surrey frame, D15 brief.)
- **Flood (per-parcel):** as the guide page states it, Crescent Beach sits 0 to 3 metres above sea level inside a dyke system, a 1982 storm surge heavily damaged frontage properties, and the City's Coastal Flood Adaptation Strategy, approved in November 2019, covers Crescent Beach. Flood status for any specific property is UNVERIFIED — ask for the flood construction level and get storm-surge insurance quotes early, before subjects. - **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Surrey ground, and to the strata complexes cross-referenced in §2 as strata stock.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 — which voids, in entry, the directory "rentals not allowed" lines recorded for Ocean Park Place and Ocean Park Village; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process under Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986). Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; settled Surrey frame.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales — relevant here to a presale such as Rockford, not to resale houses or strata units. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
