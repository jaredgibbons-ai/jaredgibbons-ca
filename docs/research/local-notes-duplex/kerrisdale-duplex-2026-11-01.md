## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted — Vancouver's multiplex frame.** By-law No. 13817 amended the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 to replace the old RS districts with the R1-1 district, allowing up to 6 strata units (or up to 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots, subject to the district schedule and site-specific City confirmation; heritage-listed status should be checked first. The duplex / two-unit form is the baseline product of that district, and older Kerrisdale duplexes stand under the zoning of their day. Kerrisdale has no separate adopted community plan; citywide direction now comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf (Research file above.)
- **SSMUH position.** Vancouver implemented the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing direction through its own multiplex rezoning (the R1-1 district above) rather than a separate SSMUH bylaw — the duplex is the baseline form of the new district. No separate Vancouver SSMUH bylaw number is asserted. (Research file above; standing Vancouver frame, brief chain.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex.** No Vancouver suite-size cap for a suite inside a duplex is stated in this slug's research or siblings, so none is asserted — the position for a specific Kerrisdale duplex is UNCONFIRMED until its district schedule and any registered suite are read. Other cities' suite caps (Surrey 40%, Township 120 m², and so on) are never borrowed into a Vancouver file. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **STRATA block — for strata duplexes / strata half duplexes (e.g. a 2-lot R1-1 strata pair).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; the townhomes sibling makes the same point for Kerrisdale's new small multiplex stratas, each of which still sits under the Form B frame. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws (strata frame as carried in ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/kerrisdale-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel in Vancouver requires subdivision approval under the City's Subdivision By-law No. 5208 (VERIFIED in the ledger; fees bylaw 14538). The fee-simple half-duplex pairs the townhomes sibling records ("no strata fees") are the product of that route or of the multiplex strata route — which route a specific pair took is read off its title, not assumed. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles. (Standing Vancouver frame, brief chain; townhomes sibling above.)
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY.** The City charges an Empty Homes Tax on homes declared or deemed vacant — 3% of the property's assessed taxable value for the 2025 reference year, with the 2025 reference-year payment due April 16, 2026. It is a City of Vancouver tax only and is never carried into another municipality's file. City property-tax dates as the research states them: advance taxes due February 3, 2026, main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied to the unpaid balance after the due date. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx (Research file above.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier** — at Kerrisdale price points the above-$3M tier is a live item, not a footnote. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate; a NEW multiplex-era half duplex can be. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What does "half duplex" mean in Kerrisdale — strata or not?** Both forms trade here. Fresh listings include fee-simple pairs whose copy states "no strata fees" expressly, and new strata product under the R1-1 multiplex district. Read the title: a strata half carries a strata plan and Form B diligence; a fee-simple half has no strata at all — municipal rules plus any registered party-wall agreement only. (§2, §3.)
- **Q: Is The Kerrisdale Collection a townhouse complex?** No — the townhomes sibling documents it as half-duplex product (a listing records Sub-Type Half Duplex, built 2026, with strata fees) and counts it nowhere; in this file it is a RECORD ONLY, because a single listing is the only source opened for the name. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a Kerrisdale house lot be redeveloped as a multiplex?** Generally yes: By-law No. 13817 rezoned Vancouver's RS districts to R1-1, allowing up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units per lot subject to the district schedule; check heritage-listed status first and confirm site-specifically with the City. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the small strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). Small multiplex stratas sit under the same Form B frame even when they are below the depreciation-report lot threshold. (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax apply to a Kerrisdale half duplex I hold vacant?** Yes — the City of Vancouver's EHT is 3% of assessed taxable value on homes declared or deemed vacant (2025 reference year; payment due April 16, 2026). It applies in Vancouver only. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000; at $3,500,000 the bill is $93,000 including the further 2% tier above $3M (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Which secondary school catchment is a Kerrisdale duplex in?** It splits by address between Point Grey Secondary and Magee Secondary — two locator samples in the research confirm the split (5555 Carnarvon Street → Kerrisdale Elementary / Point Grey Secondary; 7455 Maple Street → Dr. R.E. McKechnie Elementary / Magee Secondary). Run the exact address in the VSB locator before relying on a school. (§5.)
