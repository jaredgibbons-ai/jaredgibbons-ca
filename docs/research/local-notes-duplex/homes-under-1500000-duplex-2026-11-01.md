## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Where duplexes are permitted — per ground, never blended.** A band property takes the frame of the community it sits in: Surrey — Zoning By-law No. 12000, OCP Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026), SSMUH Jul 8, 2024, servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED, streamside setbacks in Part 7A varying by stream class (NO single figure); Township of Langley — Zoning Bylaw 2500, SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024; NO 6-plex tier), servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED; Langley City — Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 (number only), SSMUH Bylaw 3284 (City-only). - **Secondary suites inside a duplex — each city's OWN rule, stated per ground.** Surrey: one suite in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area**. Township of Langley: suite cap **120 m²**. Langley City: suite = **lesser of 100 m² and 40%**. A band duplex takes the rule of its own municipality only. (Brief chain city frames.)
- **The $1.5M line is also the federal insured-mortgage line (research).** CMHC states mortgage loan insurance is required with less than 20% down; homes $500,000 or less require a minimum 5% down payment; homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999 require 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% on any amount over $500,000; homes $1,500,000 or more require a minimum 20% down payment and mortgage loan insurance is not available — so every home on this page is on the insurable side of the line, at CMHC premiums of 0.6% to 4.5% of the total mortgage amount. Worked in the townhomes sibling: a $1,400,000 insured purchase needs $25,000 + $90,000 = $115,000 minimum down; the same home at $1,500,000 would need 20% ($300,000) and could not be insured. The CMHC premium range here is the correct 0.6%–4.5% — **no 2.80%–4.00% statement is documented in this slug's own research, and none is asserted for this slug** (that error belongs to the mortgage-calculator slug's research, a different slug, never blended). (Research file above; https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained, read 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) seen through any feed.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through that municipality's Approving Officer process, against its current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. **Band note:** nothing inside this band reaches the 3% tier or the further 2% — a purchase under $1,500,000 meets only the 1% and 2% tiers (for example $1,400,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $1,200,000 = $26,000, the townhomes sibling's worked example). The $2.0M / $3.5M anchors are carried as the program's full anchor set, for a band-edge comparison only. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. The additional-PTT rate for foreign nationals / foreign corporations / taxable trustees is UNVERIFIED in this slug's research (separate provincial page not re-read). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

(Research file above.)
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000 — the phase-out ends exactly at this page's ceiling — agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does this band file list duplexes under $1.5M?** No. It is a region-wide band file with no roster and no single ground; a duplex surfacing in the feed is documented in its home-area file, and its title form is verified on title, not on the card. (§2.)
- **Q: Why does the band stop at $1.5M — is it just a budget line?** It is also a financing cliff: at $1,500,000 or more a 20% minimum down payment is required and mortgage insurance is not available; just under the line the minimum is 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% on the rest (§3, CMHC). (§3.)
- **Q: A band card shows a half duplex — can I buy just that half?** Only if that half is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot. The card settles nothing; check the title. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay inside the band?** Only the 1% and 2% tiers are ever met — for example $1,400,000 fully residential with no exemption = $26,000; a qualifying exemption in the band's lower part can reduce it further (§3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a duplex in this band?** Under that property's own municipal rule only — Surrey 40% of principal habitable area, Township 120 m², Langley City lesser of 100 m² / 40% (§3). Never assume another city's figure travels. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: How many homes are under $1.5M right now, and how many are duplexes?** Neither figure can be stated as a statistic — SnapStats publishes no price-band cut, the feed's 238 was its own momentary count at the crawl moment, and the feed's full type split is UNVERIFIED. (§1; §2.)
