## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**NO ROSTER — FARM-RESIDENCE FORM CLASS ONLY, stated at the top of this section.** No named duplex or half duplex of either legal form is verified on South Surrey farm ground in this slug's research or any sibling, and none is invented. On ALR land the duplex question is governed first by the ALR residence frame, not by ordinary duplex zoning — what follows is that frame, stated only as ALR sources support, plus the strata bar that closes the strata-duplex route on ALR parcels.

- **The ALR residence frame — the only farm-residence forms ALR sources support:** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite is permitted only when it is located within the principal residence; and at most one additional residence is permitted, of up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. Local government bylaws may be more restrictive and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. A two-unit principal residence is therefore a form question answered parcel by parcel against that frame — a general duplex entitlement on ALR land is UNCONFIRMED, and no farm duplex is named as stock in this file. (Research file, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/, read 2026-11-01.)
- **The strata bar on ALR land — why there is no strata-duplex route here:** depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission — ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6 ("Strata Plans Cause Subdivision"). The townhomes sibling states the same bar in full (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)) and counts 0 townhouse complexes on South Surrey farm/ALR land on that basis; the condos sibling counts 0 apartment condos on the same bar. A strata half duplex on an ALR parcel is therefore not a form a buyer can assume — it exists only where ALC permission was actually granted, and no such granted case is verified for this ground in any source in this program. (Research file, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf, read via search result 2026-11-01; townhomes sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/south-surrey-farms-townhomes-2026-11-01.md.)
