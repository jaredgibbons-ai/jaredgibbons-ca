## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Vancouver Heights in Vancouver?** No — Burnaby. Despite the name, it is a City of Burnaby neighbourhood in North Burnaby, east of Boundary Road, and its market statistics are reported under Burnaby. There is no Vancouver Empty Homes Tax here. (§§1, 3.)
- **Q: Is there a duplex development in Vancouver Heights I can buy into?** None is verified in this program — §2 is form class only. What exists is the R1 rule: a Heights lot can hold 3–6 units and product with two or more primary units may be stratified, so a stratified duplex half can exist on an ordinary house lot — verify the title and strata plan on the specific property rather than looking for a named duplex project. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Can a multiplex on a Heights lot be sold unit by unit?** Yes, where the district's conditions are met — Burnaby's R1 district provides that lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. The resulting units are strata lots: Form B diligence, fees and bylaws apply to each. (§3.)
- **Q: Siena or The Sienna — what's the difference?** One letter, five blocks, about 30 years: The Sienna (3939 Hastings St) is a 1998 resale apartment building counted in the condos file; Siena (4451 Hastings St) is a 38-residence presale by Landa Global Properties. Neither is duplex stock; confirm the street address, not just the name, before booking a showing. (§2.)
- **Q: How many units can I put on a Heights lot?** Under the R1 SSMUH District: 3 units on a lot up to 280 m², 4 on a larger lot, and 6 on a lot of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop — the R5/R2 Hastings corridor makes that transit test live on many lots. Price the multiplex value before listing a house as a teardown. (§3.)
- **Q: How did Vancouver Heights sell in September 2026?** Detached: 13 listings, 5 sold, 38% ratio. Condos & Townhomes: 22 listings, 1 sold, 5% ratio. No sub-area median is published; the Burnaby community medians ($1,825,000 detached; $686,000 condos & townhomes) are community context only. (§1.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $1,500,000? At $2,000,000?** On the worked tiers in §3 — fully residential, no exemption — $28,000 at $1,500,000 and $38,000 at $2,000,000. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Heights address?** Address-based in SD41 — the documented locator run for an in-area Pandora Street address returned Rosser Elementary and Alpha Secondary (§5). Burnaby North Secondary is located in North Burnaby, but location is not catchment, and the Montecito chain (Montecito Elementary / Burnaby North Secondary) belongs to Montecito's own address — never promise it for a Heights address.
