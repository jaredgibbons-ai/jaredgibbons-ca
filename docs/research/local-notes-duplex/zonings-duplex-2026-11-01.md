## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A zone card is never evidence that a duplex exists: the directory summarizes what a zone permits, not what was built. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; townhomes sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/zonings-townhomes-2026-11-01.md; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: first $500,000 exempt at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is the zonings page?** A zoning directory and address-check resource: plain-English card summaries of the Abbotsford, Township of Langley, Surrey, Delta (and Mission rural) zoning bylaws, a free address lookup for zoning / ALR status / lot size covering Surrey, Langley and Abbotsford, an ALR basics section and a before-you-buy checklist — checked against each official bylaw on October 2, 2026 per the page, and general information only, not legal advice. (§1.)
- **Q: Does a zone card prove a duplex exists on a lot?** No — a card summarizes what the zone permits, never what was built. This file counts no duplex stock by design (§2); a specific property's form is verified on its own area file and its title. (§2.)
- **Q: My Surrey listing says RF-12 — what zone is that now?** R4: Surrey's R1–R7 zones replaced RF/RH/RQ on July 8, 2024, with RF-12 and RF-12C becoming R4 and RF-SD (the semi-detached / duplex form) becoming R6; the directory's Surrey cards show the former code on each card so old listing text can be translated. (§3.)
- **Q: How big can a secondary suite be in a duplex?** It depends which regime governs the property, and the three on this page must not be mixed: in Surrey the cap is 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area (a "90 m² cap" is not current); on ALR land a suite is permitted only within the principal residence under the ALC frame; and Abbotsford's A1 card states a suite of up to 100 m² under that city's bylaw as the page summarizes it. The Township's own cap (120 m²) is a fourth, separate municipal figure, carried on the Township files. (§3.)
- **Q: What homes does the ALR allow on a farm parcel?** One principal residence of up to 500 m² (fill ≤1,000 m²), a secondary suite only inside that principal residence, and one additional detached residence of up to 90 m² at 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² over 40 ha, without an ALC application — with municipal zoning still able to limit further. A duplex as a market form is not an ALR as-of-right form on this frame. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I split a duplex into two fee-simple halves?** Only through subdivision approval in the host municipality — and on ALR land, ALC approval as well, since strata plans in the ALR cause subdivision and most ALR subdivision needs ALC approval. Read both titles for the party-wall / easement position before pricing either half. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $1,500,000? At $2,000,000?** On the worked tiers in §3 — fully residential, no exemption — $28,000 at $1,500,000 and $38,000 at $2,000,000. A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I rely on a zone card instead of checking with the municipality?** No — and one part of the directory could not even be re-read for this program: a repeat fetch returned a 429 from the fetch service, so the Delta tail / Mission opening segment's detail is UNVERIFIED here. The page itself says the same in general terms — summaries may not reflect recent amendments (the Township's SR-3B card alone records a July 27, 2026 addition) — so confirm the zone, permitted uses and ALR status with the municipality and the ALC for the specific parcel before you buy. (§§1, 3.)
- **Q: Where do I start on an acreage purchase?** With the page's own six checks, in its order: ALR status (ALC map, then confirm with the municipality); the zone and its minimum lot size (never assume subdivision — on ALR land it needs ALC approval); how many homes are allowed (zoning and ALC rules both bite); septic records and inspection; water source (well yield/quality/licence vs. municipal); and farm class with BC Assessment, plus Premises ID if livestock or poultry are planned. (§3; research file, §6.)
