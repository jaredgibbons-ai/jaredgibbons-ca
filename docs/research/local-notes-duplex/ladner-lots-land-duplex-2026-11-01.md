## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a named duplex or half-duplex development on Ladner land I can shop?** No named duplex stock is verified for Ladner in any source in this program, and this band file invents no roster. Duplexes enter Ladner only as the form class on urban building lots and as listing-by-listing product; on ALR parcels the farm-residence frame governs instead. (§2.)
- **Q: What is the difference between a duplex, a strata half duplex and a house with a suite?** A duplex is one building with two dwelling units on one title. A strata half duplex is one half of a 2-lot strata plan, bought as its own strata lot with Form B diligence. A house with a suite is one dwelling with a secondary suite inside it — the suite is not a separate title and cannot be bought on its own. On ALR land a suite is permitted only inside the principal residence in any case. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I split a Ladner duplex lot into two fee-simple halves?** Only through subdivision approval — and on ALR land, depositing a strata plan is itself a subdivision needing ALC permission under Bulletin 09 s.6. Confirm the parcel's ALR status, zoning and Flood Construction Level with the City (and the ALC position where it applies) before pricing any split. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $800,000? At $1,500,000?** On the worked tiers in §3 — fully residential, no exemption — $14,000 at $800,000 and $28,000 at $1,500,000. A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
