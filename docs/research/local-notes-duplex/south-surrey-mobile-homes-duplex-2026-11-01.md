## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A manufactured home on a rented pad is neither form: it is a chattel home on a tenancy site, not a duplex and not land ownership. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing; pad tenure per https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning (Surrey frame, with the topic's zoning claims held UNVERIFIED).** Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 governs on Surrey ground. The live page's RM-M (Manufactured Home Residential) zone claim under Bylaw 12000 and its no-manufactured-home-park-zone claim for White Rock's Zoning Bylaw 2506 were NOT read in the bylaw texts in the research pass — both are UNVERIFIED and neither is asserted here. Which zone permits a two-unit (duplex) form on a given Surrey parcel is a parcel-by-parcel read of Bylaw 12000 and the parcel's zone in COSMOS; no zone-by-zone table is asserted in a topic file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/south-surrey-mobile-homes.md; https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-mobile-homes.html, read 2026-11-01.)
- **SSMUH position (Surrey frame).** Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial framework. Surrey allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (Jul 8, 2024 changes, which replaced RF-SD with R6 Semi-Detached). Manufactured-home park tenure is governed by the MHPTA framework below, not by SSMUH unit counts. (Program Surrey frame, as carried in this slug's siblings; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (Surrey's own rule, never borrowed).** Only one secondary suite is permitted per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. Coach houses and garden suites: all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow one; 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access, and one extra parking space is required. (Program Surrey frame, as carried in this slug's siblings; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites)
- **Manufactured-home framework (the rules that actually govern this topic's stock).** Registration is what makes a sale effective: the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, through a lawyer or notary filing the Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership; a home may not be sold without its original legible CSA label (Z-240 for mobile, A-277 for modular) — a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification. Pad tenancies are under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act: assignment on a sale requires the landlord's prior written consent under s.28 (the seller must first give the buyer the park rules and tenancy terms; the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent; the pad rent and the next increase date carry over unchanged); rent increases only on 3 full months' notice, once every 12 months, up to 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 (2.2% plus proportional for 2027); park closure/conversion under s.42 requires permits first, then 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation, with the defined additional-compensation routes in the research. None of that framework applies to a fee-simple or strata duplex purchase, and no duplex rule above applies to a pad tenancy — the tenure decides which document set exists. (Research file above; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) AND, in its bare-land form, to Sea Ridge Bays.** Form B diligence applies in full (for Sea Ridge Bays, the lots sibling states the Form B position: SPA s.59, within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; ask what reports the strata holds anyway. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. A pad-rental park home has NO Form B, no CRF and no depreciation report. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer detail is stated. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Taxes and servicing (Surrey frame).** Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2; servicing is under Bylaw 8830 (1986), VERIFIED; a short-term rental licence is $358/yr with the 2024 $350/yr proposal disclosed; Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED). On taxes for a home in a park, the Province states the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. (Research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (for transfers where PTT applies).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. How PTT applies to a specific park-home purchase (home only, no land) is a closing-specific question for the buyer's lawyer — the townhomes sibling states exactly that, and no park-home PTT position is asserted here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
