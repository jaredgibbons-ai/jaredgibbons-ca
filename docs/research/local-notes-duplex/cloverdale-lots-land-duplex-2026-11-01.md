## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning (Surrey frame).** Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 governs. Surrey's SSMUH changes of July 8, 2024 replaced RF-12 / RF-12C with R4 Small Lot Residential and RF-SD with R6 Semi-Detached. Which zone permits a two-unit (duplex) form on a given Cloverdale parcel is a parcel-by-parcel read of Bylaw 12000 and the parcel's zone in COSMOS — no zone-by-zone duplex permission table is asserted in this file, because no source opened for this slug states one. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/cloverdale-lots-land.md; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing)
- **SSMUH position (Surrey frame).** Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing framework. Surrey's zoning allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Urban lots only — ALR parcels follow the ALC frame in §2. (Research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (Surrey's own rule, never borrowed).** Only one secondary suite is permitted per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. Coach houses and garden suites: all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; the unit must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house, and one extra parking space is required. (Research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites)
- **City plan and servicing (Surrey frame).** The current plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is NEVER carried as current. Servicing is under Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw 8830 (1986), VERIFIED from City pages. Streamside setbacks are in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is stated. Development Cost Charges: the live page's FAQ names municipal DCCs, Metro Vancouver regional charges, latecomer agreements, frontage works and BC Hydro connections, but the applicable DCC rates and any parcel's servicing status are UNVERIFIED in the research — no DCC figure is stated in this file. (Research file above; https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-lots-land.html, read 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Short-term rentals and taxes (Surrey frame).** A short-term rental is the renting of a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required before operating — carried with the City's 2024 $350/yr proposal disclosed as a conflict in the program ledger. City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED). (Research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (for transfers where PTT applies).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Bare-land PTT treatment for a specific parcel is a closing question — no land-specific PTT position from the live page is carried as fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate, and bare land itself is not new housing. The live page's GST position on developer/builder-sold subdivided lots and its lender down-payment ranges are UNVERIFIED in the research — no CRA or lender source was opened for them — and neither is restated as fact here. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a named duplex development or half-duplex subdivision I can shop through this Cloverdale land page?** No named one is verified, and this page is a land feed, not a duplex feed. What exists on the urban ground is the form class: individual duplexes on single lots under the zoning of their day, plus Surrey's R6 semi-detached form as a class. (§2.)
- **Q: What is the difference between buying a duplex and buying a half duplex here?** A duplex purchase is the whole building and lot on one title. A half duplex is one unit sold separately, which is only possible where the pair was strata-divided (a 2-lot strata plan — Form B diligence) or the parcel was subdivided fee-simple (party-wall title). If neither happened, there is no "half" to buy. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I build a duplex on a Cloverdale lot from this feed?** That is a parcel read, not a feed answer: check the lot's current zone in COSMOS (post-July 2024 names — R4 and R6 replaced RF-12 and RF-SD), its OCP / plan designation, and the SSMUH band for its size (3 units under 280 m², 4 over, up to 6 on eligible lots near a frequent bus stop). ALR parcels follow the ALC frame, not the urban bands. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Can a Surrey duplex also carry a suite or a coach house?** Surrey permits one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex unit, capped at 40% of the principal unit's habitable area, not inside a coach house or garden suite; a coach house / garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² only on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR). (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on a duplex purchase?** Run the tiers on your own price: $800,000 → $14,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000 (fully residential, no exemption). A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. (§3.)

- **Q: What extra costs sit on a Cloverdale land purchase beyond PTT?** The research names the categories — municipal DCCs, Metro Vancouver regional charges, latecomer agreements, frontage works and BC Hydro connections — but the rates are UNVERIFIED in this file; get the DCC figure from the City for the specific build and the GST answer from a CPA or lawyer before removing subjects.
