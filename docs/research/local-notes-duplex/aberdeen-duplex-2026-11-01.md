## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

The housing stock is overwhelmingly single-detached houses on rural and agricultural lots; homes are effectively all freehold farm and rural residential lots. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Duplexes are a permitted two-unit form in the City's residential zones that allow a duplex under the current zoning bylaw text (Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014) and are the baseline form under the provincial SSMUH implementation; in the agricultural zones the subdivision minimums are 8.0 ha (A1) and 16.0 ha (A2), and ALR rules govern added dwellings where the lot is in the ALR. The exact position for a specific Aberdeen lot is UNCONFIRMED until its zone is read in the City's WebMap. The OCP governing is Abbotsforward 2050, CURRENT. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/bylaws/zoning-bylaw (research file §4)
- **SSMUH position.** The City implemented the provincial SSMUH rules; the bylaw number carried in the research file as "3560-2024" is held UNCONFIRMED in this program and is never asserted. (Standing Abbotsford frame, brief chain.)
- **Secondary suites — Abbotsford's own rule.** Abbotsford's suite rule is a suite under 50% of the principal dwelling — that is this city's rule, never borrowed from another city's. Abbotsford's coach/garden suite figures (15% of lot to max 100 m²) apply in Abbotsford ONLY. (Standing Abbotsford frame, brief chain; research file §4.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process (and, in the agricultural zones, the 8.0 ha / 16.0 ha minimums above); no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file §4.)
- **Servicing.** Abbotsford's Development servicing position is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — no servicing bylaw number is asserted in this file. Rural lots outside the urban boundary may use wells and onsite sewage rather than community water and sewer. (Verified ledger, brief chain; research file §4.)
- **Property taxes (context).** City of Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. Abbotsford HAS a Supreme Court registry. (Research file §4; https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Aberdeen?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. None is verified by name here; a single-lot duplex sells whole. On farm ground the A1/A2 subdivision minimums (8.0 ha / 16.0 ha) also govern. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is there a named duplex development in Aberdeen?** None verified. The only attached stock confirmed is Lantern Park, a townhome complex at the western edge, counted in the townhomes siblings — not duplex stock. (§2.)
- **Q: Is an accessory farm dwelling a duplex?** No. It is a permitted additional farm residence under the ALR Use Regulation / A1 zone regime — a different legal form, never counted as a duplex here. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a duplex here?** Abbotsford's own rule governs — a suite under 50% of the principal dwelling, where the zoning applies. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Which assent date governs the first-home GST rebate — March 12 or March 13?** March 13, 2026 per CRA. This slug's condos sibling states March 12 as its own date; that is flagged in §5 and never carried here. (§3; §5.)
