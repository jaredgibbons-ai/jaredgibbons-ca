## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC only as **(a) a STRATA duplex** (2-lot strata plan; each half a strata lot; Form B diligence) or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex** (parcel subdivided; party-wall / fee-simple row form where a source documents it). (Program definition, `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Surrey's own rule.** One suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000; never inside a coach house or garden suite; habitable area at most **40% of the principal dwelling unit**. No "90 m²" cap is current. (Research file, surrey-homes-for-sale §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite, read 2026-11-01.)

- **Servicing / taxes (Surrey side).** Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED. Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED). Surrey taxes due July 2, 5% after midnight July 2, further 5% after September 2. White Rock's tax due date/penalties: UNVERIFIED in this pass. (D14 brief; research file above.)
- **STRATA block — strata duplex / half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence in full. Depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW the 5-lot threshold on lot count alone**; ask what the strata holds anyway. Only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
