## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (New Westminster Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001) — corrected districts.** Houses sit in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series, s.310 — RS-1 minimum site 557.40 sq. m. / 6,000 sq. ft., FSR 0.5), with secondary suites and laneway / carriage houses regulated in the same bylaw; the townhouse districts in this bylaw are **RT-2, RT-2A, RT-2B, RT-2C and RT-3, RT-3A, RT-3B ONLY** — the research file's "RT-A and RT-B" wording is an error (see the FILE STATUS correction) and its 836.1 sq. m. figure is an **RM apartment-district density threshold (836.10 m²), not an RT figure**. The hospital / Brewery District lands sit in institutional and comprehensive development districts. The exact zone for a specific lot is read off the current Bylaw 6680 text for that lot. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf (Research file §4, corrected in-file.)
- **SSMUH position.** No Sapperton-specific SSMUH unit count is stated in this slug's research (lots-and-land sibling, stated expressly) — the documented density frame for lots near the stations is the OCP's Frequent Transit Development Area designation for the Sapperton and Braid station areas; a duplex is the baseline two-unit form, and the unit position for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED here until confirmed with the City under the zoning above. No unit count is imported from another city or another New Westminster slug. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports (Lots-and-land sibling §3.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — New Westminster's own rule only.** Most single detached dwellings **and some duplexes** in New Westminster are zoned to permit a secondary suite, which must meet Zoning Bylaw s.190.29 and the Building Code — **one suite per house; a property may have both a suite and a laneway / carriage house**. No other city's suite rule is borrowed, and no suite size cap beyond that rule is carried here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Secondary_Suite_Guide_August_2025.pdf (55-plus sibling §3, suite line only — its RT-A/RT-B lines are flagged, not followed.)
- **Flood — Sapperton's own position only, as its own research states it.** The City's floodplain study places **the industrial land along the Brunette River in Floodplain Zone 4, below the Flood Construction Level** — the risk pocket in Sapperton is the low Brunette River / Braid-side land, **not the residential slope**, and lot-level status should be confirmed with the City. NO zone figure is imported from any other New Westminster slug (the floodplain ledger is slug-precise, and no other slug's zone is Sapperton's). https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf and http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management (Research file §§4, 6.)
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex (and in full to the Brewery District strata and Sapperton Living).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex, by contrast, is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the RS zone minimums above; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the titles — read them. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** New Westminster Development and Servicing Bylaw **No. 7142, 2007 VERIFIED** in the verified ledger. Sapperton Green is planned to connect to the City's Sapperton District Energy System — a City utility cost future owners there should ask about; it is not a charge on existing Sapperton duplexes and none is asserted. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Council_Feb_24_2020___Sapperton_Green_Update.pdf (Verified ledger; research file §4.)
- **Property taxes (city-wide New Westminster rule, research):** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining on Friday, July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
