## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **City plan — current bylaw, stated as a standing correction.** Port Moody Official Community Plan **Bylaw 2026, No. 3522, adopted April 28, 2026**, which repeals Bylaw No. 2955 — 2955 is NEVER cited as current. Chapter 14 sets policy for the city's neighbourhoods; the Suncor refinery lands beside College Park and Seaview are designated a Special Study Area in the OCP — the main planning item hanging over this end of the city, and a disclosure item on any College Park purchase. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- **Zoning and SSMUH.** Zoning Bylaw 2018, No. 2937 puts single detached homes in RS zones and multi-residential homes in RT and RM zones. Under Bylaw Amendment No. 94, 2024, No. 3460, small-scale multi-unit housing is permitted in RS and RT zones outside the transit-oriented areas (the TOAs are exempt from the SSMUH rules): up to four units on lots from 280 m² to 4,050 m², and up to six units within 400 m of frequent bus stops. A duplex is the baseline two-unit form inside that permission. **Trap specific to this sub-area: the RS1-S small-lot subdivision standard (minimum 9 m width, 325 m²) is available only in Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside OCP Single Family–Low Density areas — College Park is NOT on that list**, so do not price a College Park lot off the small-lot offer; confirm any split idea with the City. (Research file above; townhomes sibling above; https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/; https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/)

- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / half duplex in College Park.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; the RS1-S shortcut does NOT apply in College Park (above). Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file above, §6.)
- **Property taxes.** Port Moody property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding amounts after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026; utilities are billed separately, with the 2026 utility bill due February 27, 2026. (Research file above; https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/property-taxes/)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in College Park?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. No named separable half is verified in College Park (§2); a single-lot duplex sells whole. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is Westhill Place a duplex?** No — Westhill Place is an apartment-form condo building, this slug's 1 counted condo in the condos sibling. It is cross-referenced here with no duplex count. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Mountain View Co-op a cheaper way to buy a half duplex?** No. Mountain View Housing Co-operative (902 Clarke Road) is co-op membership tenure — not ownership at all — carried as an excluded-tenure record only, and never merged with Clarke Townhomes at 909 Clarke Road. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I split my College Park lot into small lots for two halves?** Not under the RS1-S small-lot rule — that standard is limited to Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside, and College Park is not on the list. A fee-simple split needs full subdivision approval; confirm with the City before pricing it in. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a College Park lot hold a duplex under SSMUH?** SSMUH is permitted on qualifying RS and RT lots outside the transit-oriented areas — up to four units on lots from 280 m² to 4,050 m², up to six within 400 m of frequent bus stops, per the 2024 amendment — so a two-unit duplex form sits inside that permission, subject to the specific lot, DPA 4 / DPA 5 and riparian checks. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: What about the Suncor lands next door?** The refinery lands beside College Park and Seaview are an OCP Special Study Area — the land use next door is an open planning question. Disclose it and check the current study status with the City. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,200,000 fully residential with no exemption = $22,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Which schools serve a College Park address?** SD43 catchments are address-based and must be checked in the SD43 School Locator by exact address — see §5. (§5.)
