## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Duplexes are a permitted two-unit form in Surrey's residential zones that allow a duplex under Zoning By-law No. 12000, and the provincial SSMUH implementation (Surrey, Jul 8, 2024) makes the duplex the baseline multi-unit form on eligible lots. The OCP governing is Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is the replaced predecessor, never cited as current, and the 1999 Douglas NCP designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update, so a listing quoting a 1999 NCP designation is quoting history. The zone for a specific Pacific Douglas lot is read off the City's current zoning for that lot. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated for Surrey. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-development/zoning
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Surrey's own rule.** One secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area** under Zoning By-law No. 12000 — Surrey's rule, never borrowed; the old 90 m² Surrey suite cap is NOT a current rule and is never stated. Coach houses and garden suites are permitted in residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes, 35 m² minimum and 75 m² maximum, rising to a maximum of 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the principal dwelling has not achieved its maximum floor area. (Research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites, read 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), and to the strata townhouse components cross-referenced in §2.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Surrey's servicing position is Bylaw 8830 (1986), VERIFIED from the City's own pages in the verified ledger. Parcel-level servicing on this ground is a separate question: a 2007 City corporate report states a City sanitary sewer system services about half the lands within the Douglas neighbourhood, primarily the more developed southwest area, flowing to the Douglas sanitary pump station — that statement is from 2007, and current parcel-level servicing (City water/sewer vs well/septic) is UNVERIFIED. Short-term rental figures for Surrey carry a disclosed $358/$350 conflict in the program record ($358/yr per Zoning By-law No. 12000, Part 4 s. B.7(d) and Business License By-law No. 13680, s. 60.1, against the City's 2024 proposal of $350/yr) — no STR figure is relied on in this file. (Research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/RPT_2007-L001.pdf, read via search result 2026-11-01.)
- **ALR edge.** The plan area is bordered by the Agricultural Land Reserve to the north; any parcel that sits in the ALR follows the ALC housing frame of one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). Whether a specific parcel is in the ALR is UNVERIFIED at parcel level. (Research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/, read 2026-11-01.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; **$3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = $93,000 including the further 2% tier** — $93,000 governs at $3.5M. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate; a presale such as Harlowe is new construction and carries GST as such, under REDMA's 7-day rescission (s. 21) and deposit-in-trust (s. 18) frame. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
