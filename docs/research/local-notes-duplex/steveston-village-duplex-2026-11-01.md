## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Richmond Zoning Bylaw No. 8500).** Houses on village side streets sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2, with secondary suite rules in Section 5.4; village commercial and mixed use zones named by the City include Steveston Commercial (CS3), Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU32) – Steveston Village, and Steveston Office Commercial (ZC49) – Chatham Street (Steveston). The exact zone for a specific lot is read off the current Bylaw 8500 text for that lot — zone entitlements asserted beyond that read are UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/bylaws.htm (Research file §4; settled Richmond frame.)
- **Heritage — the rule that actually governs the core.** Steveston Village is designated as a heritage conservation area in the Steveston Area Plan, and properties inside it are subject to Heritage Alteration Permit requirements under the Steveston Area Plan; protected heritage buildings and other resource types such as docks and landscape features are identified in the Official Community Plan. Any duplex build, split or exterior change on a village lot runs into that permit regime first — before zoning form questions. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML (Research file §4.)
- **SSMUH position — Richmond's own, as the City states it.** The City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots; the duplex is the baseline form under that framework. The implementing particulars for a specific village lot — including the heritage overlay — are confirmed against the City's current SSMUH materials. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm (Research file §5; settled Richmond frame.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Richmond's own position, as its sources state it.** A suite must be at least 33.0 m² and no more than the lesser of 40% of the dwelling's floor area or 110 m² under Section 5.4 of Bylaw 8500. That is Richmond's own figure, carried from this ground's own research — no other city's cap is borrowed. For a strata unit, the strata's bylaws also govern, and the suite position is UNCONFIRMED until read in both. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf (Research file §5.)
- **Flood.** The village sits on the South Arm of the Fraser River behind the City dike system — Richmond is a low-lying city in the Fraser River floodplain protected by a perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations, with dikes being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres — and the City's Steveston Island Dike project, a proposed dike around Steveston Island from London Farm to Garry Point Park with a navigation gate for Steveston Harbour, addresses flood protection for the Steveston waterfront and harbour. The Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements — verify both for a specific lot before planning a rebuild or addition. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf and https://www.letstalkrichmond.ca/StevestonIslandDike
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex (and in full to the village core's mixed-use strata).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the titles — read them. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Richmond servicing Bylaws 8751/8752 VERIFIED in the verified ledger. ADEU charges are West Cambie / Alexandra ONLY — no ADEU position is carried for Steveston Village. (Verified ledger; D20 frame.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: What does heritage status change for a village duplex?** Everything about alterations: properties inside the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area need a Heritage Alteration Permit for exterior changes, which affects renovation plans, timelines and resale positioning. Check heritage status before anything else. (§3.)
- **Q: Is the 12040 2nd Avenue building a duplex?** No — it is a strata building with townhouse units and one retail unit, documented in full in the village townhomes file and counted nowhere. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in the village?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. No such property is verified by name here; a single-lot duplex sells whole. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a village house?** Suites run under Section 5.4 (minimum 33.0 m²; maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling or 110 m² — Richmond's own figures), but a heritage property also needs its alteration permit, and a strata half also needs the strata's bylaws checked. Read all three before counting rental income. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
