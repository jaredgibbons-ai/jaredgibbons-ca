## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A leasehold interest on reserve land is NEITHER of these forms: it is a lease interest under the head lease, not a fee-simple half and not a strata lot. Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Property taxes and the additional school tax (District side).** Municipal-side homes pay District of West Vancouver taxes — the 2026 payment was due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026; roughly half of the tax bill is collected by the District for other taxing authorities, and water/sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. The Province's additional school tax also bites at West Vancouver values: 0.2% on the residential portion assessed between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. Homes on reserve land are under a different tax/tenure regime — tenure must be confirmed per title, and the current tax/tenure terms for reserve-land homes are UNVERIFIED in the research (its UNVERIFIED item 4). https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate (Research file §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. PTT is a provincial tax; a reserve-land leasehold transfer's tax position is a per-title question and is not asserted here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a half duplex at Park Royal?** No named half duplex — strata or fee-simple — is verified anywhere in this program for this ground, on either side of the reserve line. What sells here is a strata apartment condo at the centre, a whole detached house south of Marine Drive, or — on the reserve side — a leasehold interest, which is a different purchase entirely. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Park Royal in West Vancouver or on First Nations land?** Both, depending on the parcel. The shopping centre and surrounding area sit at West Vancouver's eastern edge; part of the centre stands on Capilano Indian Reserve No. 5, Squamish Nation land held under the 1974 Park Royal Lease, where the Nation exercises zoning control. The two can be streets apart — tenure is confirmed on the individual title, never assumed from the sub-area name. (§§1–2.)
- **Q: Are homes near Park Royal freehold?** Homes on the municipal side are freehold; homes on reserve land are leasehold, on the head-lease terms, remaining term and any prepaid/rent-reset structure. Run the tenure check first, before pricing or financing discussions. (§2.)
- **Q: Is a reserve-land leasehold like a strata purchase?** No. There is no strata lot and no Form B on a leasehold interest — the governing diligence is the head lease itself, and lenders treat reserve-land leaseholds individually, so flag the tenure to the mortgage broker at the offer stage. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: What do I buy at The WestRoyal or Evelyn?** A strata apartment-condo lot, counted in the condos file (The WestRoyal, 328/338 Taylor Way, built 1993; Evelyn, 768 Arthur Erickson Place, built 2019) — Form B diligence in full, and the depreciation-report cycle for 5+ lots applies to stratas of that size, unlike a 2-lot strata duplex, which is below the threshold. Apartment form — The WestRoyal is not townhouse stock, whatever shorthand a listing uses. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: What is changing around Park Royal?** Density, on paper. The July 2025 provincial directive required OCP amendments increasing density in the Park Royal–Taylor Way area by the end of 2025; the District missed the deadline, the Ambleside Centre plan was implemented by provincial order in April 2026, and the Park Royal–Taylor Way directive remains outstanding with quarterly progress reports through 2026 and 2027. The current designation on title may have changed recently — check it before assuming density value. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a District-side house?** West Vancouver's own rule: suites are permitted under Zoning Bylaw 4662 with the District's registration process — one per lot, within the principal building (not detached), owner occupying either the principal dwelling or the suite. No West Vancouver suite size cap is carried in this program — read the current bylaw text for the lot. (§3.)
- **Q: What extra tax should a Park Royal buyer budget for?** On the District side, the Province's additional school tax at West Vancouver values — 0.2% on the assessed portion between $3M and $4M and 0.4% above $4M in 2026, rising to 0.3% / 0.6% on January 1, 2027 — on top of District taxes due July 2, 2026. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
