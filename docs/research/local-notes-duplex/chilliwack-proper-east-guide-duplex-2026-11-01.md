## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan (Chilliwack frame):** the City's 2050 Official Community Plan (Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500) is the city-wide plan, and its Schedule B2 is the "Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan" covering this ground; the plan was added to the 2040 OCP as Schedule D through Bylaw 2018, No. 4533 (public hearing June 5, 2018), updated in 2025 as part of the 2050 OCP, and its purpose is to guide future decision making on residential infill development. Plan designations as the guide states them: Residential 1 (one and two family housing) and Residential 2 (attached housing and narrow lot infill). (Research file, §4; https://chilliwack.com/ocp; https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-proper-east-guide.html)
- **Zoning, SSMUH and suites (Chilliwack frame — the city's own rules, never borrowed):** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs. The SSMUH amendment, Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024), allows **3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing** — duplexes are the baseline form. A secondary suite is capped at **the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the habitable floor space of the building** (s.2); only **1 accessory dwelling unit** may be located on a lot, with permitted types including secondary suite, coach house and garden suite, garden suites capped at **100 m²** (s.4.06(1)); ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite (s.5.04); no secondary suite is legal unless built with a building permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. (Research file, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf)
- **Flood (per-parcel):** the Downtown Neighbourhood Plan position carried on the guide — Chilliwack Proper is subject to the flood risk of the Fraser River and the dyke system is built to a 1-in-200-year flood standard, and at higher river stages areas outside the City's dyking system flood. Which flood rules and flood construction level apply to any specific lot is a per-parcel City check: UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel. (Research file, §4; https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-proper-east-guide.html)
- **ALR:** the City states agriculture accounts for 67% of the City's total land base, and ALR parcels follow the ALC housing frame of one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). ALR status for any specific Chilliwack Proper East parcel is a parcel-by-parcel check on the ALC map: UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel. (Research file, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Chilliwack ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer — the James Street record's $649,900 list price would sit in the $500K–$800K band of these anchors, but list price is not a sale price and no PTT figure is stated for that listing. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: The feed shows a half-duplex with a suite on James Street — can I buy just that half?** Only if the legal form allows it, and the listing text alone does not settle that. If it is a 2-lot strata plan or a subdivided fee-simple half, the half sells separately; if the duplex was never divided, the whole building sells together. Search the title and any strata plan before writing, and check the suite's permit position under Bylaw 5000. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Is there a duplex development in Chilliwack Proper East I can buy into?** No named duplex development is verified in this program. The documented form is the individual duplex on a single lot under the zoning of its day, plus SSMUH infill on eligible parcels. (§2.)
- **Q: How many units can I put on a Proper East lot?** Under SSMUH Bylaw 5395: 3 to 4 dwelling units on a parcel within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. Separately, only 1 accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot under Bylaw 5000 — do not price a lot as if it can carry a suite plus a coach house plus a garden suite. (§3.)
- **Q: What are the suite rules here?** Chilliwack's own cap: a secondary suite is limited to the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space; a garden suite caps at 100 m²; the suite must be permitted and built to the BC Building and Fire Code and flood protection requirements, with no new suites below the flood construction level. (§3.)
- **Q: Is there an official median price for Chilliwack Proper East?** No figure is stated in this file: Chilliwack is a CADREB market that the SnapStats extraction used by this program does not cover, and CADREB publishes no area statistics in the sources used, so no median, sales ratio or inventory figure for this sub-area exists in these sources. Price from the property's own CADREB comparables. (§1.)
- **Q: Is Chilliwack Proper East in a floodplain?** Chilliwack Proper is subject to the flood risk of the Fraser River, the dyke system is built to a 1-in-200-year standard, and areas outside the dyking system flood at higher river stages — flood status is set lot by lot, so ask the City about the specific lot before subjects, especially for a basement or ground-floor suite. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $800,000? At $3,500,000?** On the worked tiers in §3 — fully residential, no exemption — $14,000 at $800,000 and $93,000 at $3,500,000 including the further 2% tier. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. (§3.)
