## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**FORM CLASS ONLY — no named duplex stock is verified on this farm ground:**

- **The farm belt form — which is NOT duplex stock.** The dominant form is the farm house on agricultural land: the A1 Agriculture zone permits one farm house per lot (maximum total floor area 500 m²; farm home plate maximum 3,600 m²), one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot (maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less, or 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, on the same farm home plate as the farm house; ALC approval is required for more than one). An additional farm house (180 m² maximum on lots under 8 ha, 233 m² on lots of 8 ha or more) requires a Professional Agrologist's report supporting its necessity and written ALC approval. Migrant farm worker housing is an A1 accessory use only under its own criteria (ALR lot with farming as a permitted use, farm classification under the Assessment Act, written ALC approval, employing farm at least 8 ha and lot at least 4 ha; new buildings must be manufactured homes on a temporary foundation or footings). Those are ALR farm-residence forms on a single farm parcel under one title — a suite inside the farm house and an accessory farm dwelling are not a duplex, are not sold separately, and are never counted as duplex or half-duplex stock here. ALR residences cannot be stratified, per this ground's siblings, so a strata half duplex is not this ground's farm form; and strata farm product is not a Delta pattern — no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source for this topic. A duplex is the baseline two-unit form; SSMUH does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (East-delta duplex file: ~/workspace/local-notes/duplex/east-delta-duplex-2026-11-01.md; https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a farm house with a suite and an accessory farm dwelling a duplex I can buy half of?** No. Those are ALR farm-residence forms on one farm parcel under one title (§2/§3) — the parcel sells whole, and ALR residences cannot be stratified per the siblings. (§2.)
- **Q: Is there any named duplex development on Ladner farm ground?** None is verified in this topic's research or siblings — §2 is the form class only, and on the farm belt itself the governing form is the farm-residence set, not duplex stock. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a farm parcel add a second detached residence?** Only within the ALC caps: one accessory farm dwelling unit up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or 186 m² on larger parcels, on the same farm home plate; an additional farm house needs an agrologist's necessity report plus written ALC approval. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I stratify a farm house into two half-duplex titles?** No — ALR residences cannot be stratified, and depositing a strata plan is a subdivision requiring ALC permission on ALR land. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex (urban edge)?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000 (worked in §3). Farm / mixed-use apportionment on a farm purchase is transaction-specific and UNCONFIRMED at topic level. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me split a duplex into two titles?** No — SSMUH adds unit potential on a lot; a separately titled half needs a strata plan or a subdivision, and on farm land the ALR position governs first. (§3.)
- **Q: Are farm or duplex prices published for Ladner?** No — SnapStats does not cover South Delta and publishes no farm category, and duplexes have no separate published category in this program's sources. No market figure is stated in this file. (§1.)
