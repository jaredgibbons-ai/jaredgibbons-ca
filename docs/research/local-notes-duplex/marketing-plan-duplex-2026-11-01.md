## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **No municipal frame asserted for this topic.** Zoning, SSMUH, secondary-suite, servicing and short-term-rental rules for a duplex are the rules of the municipality where the duplex sits; this utility file asserts none of them for the topic. The page's South Surrey / Langley framing is service framing only. A seller's duplex takes its own home-area file's frame. (Brief chain discipline; research file above.)
- **The BC regulator's framework (from the research).** In British Columbia, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); a licensee must give consumers information about commissions and fees, must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients and of the risks of dealing unrepresented, and dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. Practical consequence for this page: commission and fee information is part of what BCFSA requires a licensee to give a consumer, but the page itself states no fee or commission figure — any fee discussion for a seller reading this page happens off-page, in the listing conversation. (Research file above; https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math (buyer-side frame, as the townhomes sibling states it).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Buyer-side exemptions as the siblings state them: First-Time Home Buyers' Program — full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. These are buyer-side taxes, stated here because, as the townhomes sibling puts it, pricing strategy is set against the buyer's total cost, not the list price alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) a seller lists.** Form B diligence applies in full (the seller's strata produces the Form B package a buyer reviews). Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; what reports the strata holds is still a listing-conversation item. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through that municipality's Approving Officer process, against its current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — a seller of one half should expect a buyer to read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST point the condos and townhomes siblings state for sellers: 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — so a seller's GST position depends on whether the unit is a developer sale or a resale, and listing copy must not blur the two. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for the FTHB rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
