## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. On the land side of this topic, the fee-simple half is the form a duplex lot is bought to create: the parcel is subdivided first, then each half is built and held on its own lot. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zones permitting a duplex / two-unit form (Surrey frame).** Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000 divides the city into zones and regulates use, density, siting and subdivision; the SSMUH consolidation (July 8, 2024) put the former single-family and duplex zones into the 9 zones named in §2 (RA, R1, R2, R2-O, R3, R4, R5, R5-S, R6). The consolidated bylaw text is the source for any zone's minimum subdivision lot area, and zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel in COSMOS. Land-use designations come from the Official Community Plan — **OCP Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026), NEVER 18020-as-current** — and its secondary plans (Neighbourhood Concept Plans and town centre plans). Servicing: **Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED** from City pages. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated. (Research file above; brief chain Surrey frame.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (Surrey's OWN rule).** Surrey permits only one suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most **40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area**, and never inside a coach house or garden suite. Coach houses and garden suites (per the program's rules-gaps verification carried in the siblings): all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite, 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house, and one extra parking space is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Reserve-land boundary of the form class.** On Surrey's agricultural edges, subdivision of Agricultural Land Reserve land requires permission from the Agricultural Land Commission, apart from the limited approving-officer cases in the Agricultural Land Reserve General Regulation; a principal residence built without a Commission application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, and one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller (186 m² or less on larger parcels) where Surrey bylaws also permit it. A specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the Commission's maps by PID or civic address. Low-lying edge parcels also carry the Serpentine–Nicomekl floodplain layer. (Research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), and to a bare-land strata lot.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. Fee-simple land is the contrast: the lots-and-land sibling states the default for this topic's lots — fee-simple, no strata corporation, no Form B, no strata fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel — the route to two fee-simple halves — requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000 (and Commission permission on reserve land, above); no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 (city-wide, not land-specific). (Research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex, and a subdivided half-duplex lot, is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. GST treatment of a specific Surrey lot sale depends on the seller and the lot's history — UNCONFIRMED per transaction in the research; confirm with a CPA, and do not rely on the live page's blanket GST summary. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. The boundary the lots-and-land and acreages siblings state expressly: rebates attach to housing, not bare land — a qualifying new HOME built on the lot, not the lot itself. First-Time Home Buyers' PTT exemption (full on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000) and the Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000) are carried as the siblings state them; a vacant lot's own PTT-exemption eligibility is a PTT-return question — UNCONFIRMED here for bare land, per the townhomes sibling. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does this topic file list duplexes or duplex lots for sale?** No roster. - **Q: A lot is marketed as "permit-ready duplex" — what does that verify?** Only the listing's own wording. It verifies no built duplex, no title form and no right to split the parcel into two fee-simple halves; the split itself needs subdivision approval against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Can I build a duplex and sell the halves separately?** Two routes only: strata-divide the pair into a 2-lot strata plan (each half a strata lot, Form B diligence), or subdivide the parcel so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (Approving Officer approval; Commission permission as well on reserve land). Added SSMUH units alone create neither. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay on a Surrey lot?** The general tiers on the lot's own price — for example $700,000 fully residential with no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $500,000 = $12,000, the worked example the siblings carry; $3,500,000 fully residential = $93,000 including the further 2% tier (§3). Whether a bare lot itself qualifies for an exemption is a PTT-return question, UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a Surrey duplex?** One suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex or a bare-land strata lot?** Form B diligence on the strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). A fee-simple lot has no strata and no Form B. (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Are there market statistics for Surrey land or duplexes?** No figure of either kind is stated here: SnapStats publishes no vacant-land category and no duplex category; §1's figures are Surrey all-residential community context only. (§1.)
