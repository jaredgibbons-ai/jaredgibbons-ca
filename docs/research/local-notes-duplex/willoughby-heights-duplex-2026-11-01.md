## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. The Westbrooke record below is the fee-simple form's local face: freehold row homes, each unit on its own fee-simple title, with no strata at all. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** A standalone Willoughby duplex bylaw zone distinct from R-CL is NOT confirmed (research file) — no duplex zone is asserted. The exact position for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until its zone is read. (Research file above; standing Township frame, brief chain.)
- **SSMUH position.** Township SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (no 6-plex tier). SSMUH adds unit potential on eligible lots; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Standing Township frame, brief chain.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the Township's own rule.** The Township's suite cap is 120 m² — the Township's rule, never borrowed from another municipality. (Standing Township frame, brief chain.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan); Westbrooke itself is fee-simple and carries NONE of this.** Form B diligence applies in full to a strata half. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Township's Approving Officer process; the Westbrooke row homes are the completed local example of the fee-simple end state (each unit on its own title from the development's subdivision). Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles; for a Westbrooke unit, the registered covenants/HOA documents are the set the townhomes sibling names. (Program discipline, D1 brief; townhomes sibling above.)
- **Servicing.** The Township's servicing position is Bylaw 5382, CONFIRMED in the verified ledger. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex in Willoughby Heights?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot. The Westbrooke row homes are the fee-simple case: each unit is its own title. A single-lot duplex elsewhere sells whole. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Are the Westbrooke duplexes counted as duplex stock anywhere?** The collection (56 units across duplexes, triplexes and quadplexes) is counted in its townhomes home; no source splits the 56 by form, so NO duplex count is taken in this file. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence replaces Form B on a Westbrooke unit?** None — there is no strata. Diligence is title, Township rules and any registered covenants or HOA documents, per the townhomes sibling. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Is there a Willoughby duplex zone?** A standalone duplex zone distinct from R-CL is NOT confirmed in the research file — do not rely on one. The documented frame is R-CL s.403 (220 m² min / 464.5 m² max lot area) plus SSMUH 6020. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a duplex here?** The Township's own rule governs — 120 m² cap, where the zoning applies. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (Fee-simple Westbrooke units have no strata at all; check covenants instead.) (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement or covenants on the two titles say. Nothing registered = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for Willoughby Heights?** No. SnapStats has no duplex category. (§1.)
