## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A coach house is none of these: in the Township it is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102, NEVER strata (see §3). (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing.)

**FORM CLASS ONLY — NO DUPLEX ROSTER, stated at the top of this section.** No named duplex or half-duplex development is verified in this slug's research, feed or siblings, and none is invented. What exists on Langley acreage ground is the ordinary form class below, plus the ALR residence forms — neither is a duplex roster.

- **Ordinary form class (Township ground):** individual houses — including, where the zoning of their day permitted, an individual duplex building on a single fee-simple lot — on rural and acreage parcels under Township Zoning Bylaw 2500 (the feed's own cards cite RU-2 and RU-3 zoning; the zone for a specific parcel must be confirmed with the Township). A duplex on an acreage lot sells as the whole building and lot unless it was strata-divided or the parcel subdivided, and no such split on an acreage parcel is verified in this program. (Research file, §4; https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html.)
- **Farm-residence forms — ONLY as ALR sources support:** land in the ALR may generally have one principal residence of 500 m² or less total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill; a secondary suite is permitted only within the principal residence; and one additional residence may be permitted at up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. Building more, or larger, requires ALC approval, and local government bylaws may be more restrictive. In the program's settled framing these additional-residence forms rest on ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25, with the Non-Adhering Residential Use application as the route beyond the as-of-right frame — a general entitlement to a second residence on any acreage is UNCONFIRMED and is never stated. An additional ALR residence is a farm-residence form, not a duplex and not a half duplex: it is not separately strata-titled or subdivided by that permission. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning, SSMUH, suites and coach houses (Township frame):** rural Langley acreages sit under Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500. SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024; NO 6-plex tier): eligible lots (Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water and sewer, 4,050 m² or smaller) may have 3 units at ≤280 m² or 4 units at 280–4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible — duplexes are the baseline SSMUH form where a lot is eligible at all. Township secondary suite cap: **120 m²**, with no percentage cap — the Township's own rule, never borrowed. **Coach house = a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102, NEVER strata** — stated exactly; a coach house is bought and sold with its lot, never as a separate strata half. R-CL s.403 = 220 m² min lot / 464.5 m² max lot area ("355 m²" is NOWHERE in the bylaw — never carried). Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 is CONFIRMED. Township property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies at midnight July 2, 2026, and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. (Township frame, D16 task / D15 brief; research file, §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx; https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx.)
- **ALR frame (governs a large share of Langley acreage):** the residence ceilings in §2 (500 m² principal residence, ≤1,000 m² fill, suite only inside the principal residence, one additional residence at 90 m² / 186 m²) — anything beyond needs ALC approval and local bylaws may be more restrictive. Farm-class thresholds (BC Assessment, Assessment Act farm-class regulation): $2,500 in farm-gate sales for a farm operation area of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for larger operations; $10,000 if the total area is under 0.8 ha; applications must be delivered by October 31. Whether any specific acreage holds farm class, and the ALR status of any specific parcel, are per-parcel checks: UNVERIFIED in this file. (Research file, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo89/loo89/411_95_pit.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) wherever one exists.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. On ALR land, depositing a strata plan is itself a subdivision requiring ALC permission per the program position carried in §2 (citation UNVERIFIED in this slug's pass). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Township's Approving Officer process (and, on ALR land, ALC permission); no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales — a resale acreage house, duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy half a duplex on a Langley acreage?** Only if the pair was legally split — a 2-lot strata plan or a subdivided fee-simple parcel. No such split on an acreage parcel is verified in this program, and on ALR land a strata plan is a subdivision requiring ALC permission (citation carried as UNVERIFIED in this slug's pass). An ordinary acreage duplex sells whole, building and lot together. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Does an ALR acreage come with a right to a second house?** Not as a general entitlement — that is UNCONFIRMED. What ALR sources support is one additional residence at up to 90 m² (properties 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha) alongside a principal residence of 500 m² or less, with a suite only inside the principal residence; more or larger needs ALC approval. (§2.)
- **Q: Is a coach house the same as a half duplex?** No. A Township coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — it is never strata and never sells separately from its lot. A half duplex is a separately titled half of a two-unit building. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put 3–4 units on an acreage?** No for most acreages: Township SSMUH (Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024) requires a lot of 4,050 m² or smaller, inside the Urban Containment Boundary and connected to municipal water and sewer. (§3.)
- **Q: What farm income does an acreage need for farm class?** BC Assessment's thresholds are $2,500 in farm-gate sales for 0.8–4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that size, and $10,000 under 0.8 ha, with applications due by October 31. (§3.)
- **Q: What did Langley homes sell for in September 2026?** As board context only — not acreage-segment figures, for which no figure exists in this source: the Langley community detached median was $1,345,000 on 449 listings and 64 sales, and the Condos & Townhomes median was $611,000 on 699 listings and 128 sales. (§1.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $1,500,000? At $2,000,000?** On the worked tiers in §3 — fully residential, no exemption — $28,000 at $1,500,000 and $38,000 at $2,000,000. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. (§3.)
- **Q: What should I check on a 1–5 acre property before removing subjects?** The page's own advice, adopted in the research as sound: test water flow, water quality and the septic system, confirm permits for outbuildings, suites and shops, and ask the Township what the zone allows for a second home, a shop or a suite. Per-property well/septic status is UNVERIFIED in this file. (§3; research file, §§4, 6.)
- **Q: Why is a 39.5-acre estate on a page that defines an acreage as 1–10 acres?** Because the feed does not hold the page's own line — the research records the tension as a snapshot finding, and the feed's exact filter criteria are UNVERIFIED. Read the feed, not the label. (§1.)
