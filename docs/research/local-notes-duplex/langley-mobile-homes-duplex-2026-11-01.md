## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES AND PARK HOMES

- **Pad rent increases — the regulated mechanism (Province / Residential Tenancy Branch).** A park landlord may increase rent only with 3 full months' written notice, only once every 12 months, and only up to the yearly limit; the 2026 limit for manufactured home site tenancies is **2.3% plus a proportional amount**, and the 2027 limit is **2.2% plus a proportional amount** (verified live from RTB Nov 1, 2026, per the settled position). The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park. The landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion of a prior year's limit. These percentages are the only pad-rent numbers an official source gives: **no pad-rent DOLLAR FIGURE is published as fact anywhere in this file** — no official source publishes a Langley pad-rent figure, average or range (§5 states the individual card figures exactly as what they are: individual listings' copy). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-mobile-homes.md, §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes)
- **Assignment on a sale (MHPTA s.28).** Assignment of the site tenancy permanently transfers the pad tenancy to the buyer; a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an order of the director, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it — s.28(1)(b) wording is "an order of the director" (NOT arbitrator); the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent, and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. Landlords and tenants in a manufactured home park must have a written tenancy agreement for the site. (Research file above, §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes)
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s.42).** Where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to another use, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes where the home cannot be moved or no site is available within a reasonable distance. (Research file above, §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes)
- **Registration and certification.** In BC the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered with the Manufactured Home Registry, through a lawyer or notary filing the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership; a home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular), and a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification. A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. (Research file above, §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home)
- **Zoning and SSMUH (both frames, for the duplex form class only — park homes sit outside this frame).** Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500; SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024) — an eligible lot (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on both municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units if 280 m² or smaller, or 4 units if between 280 m² and 4,050 m²; no 6-plex tier. Township secondary suite cap 120 m² (s.102); a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata. Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED. R-CL s.403 = 220 m² min lot / 464.5 m² max lot ("355 m²" NOWHERE in the bylaw). City: Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 (number only); SSMUH Bylaw 3284 (City-only); suite = lesser of 100 m² / 40%. **The manufactured-home-park zone name and rules in Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 and City Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300 are UNVERIFIED** — neither bylaw's park-zone section was opened in this program. (Research file above, §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), not to park homes.** Form B diligence applies in full to strata purchases. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Township's Approving Officer process under Servicing Bylaw 5382 (CONFIRMED); no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. No subdivision route turns a pad-rental park home into land ownership; the tenure line in §2 governs park purchases. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)

- **PTT — the standard tiers, with the park-purchase caveat stated exactly as the siblings state it.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. **How PTT applies to a specific park purchase (no land transfers) is UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule** — no primary source opened settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad, and no flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the wider site copy are UNVERIFIED); PTT treatment follows the actual transaction (home vs land). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. (Townhomes sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/langley-mobile-homes-townhomes-2026-11-01.md; mobile-homes sibling above; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption)
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. The stock on this page is resale park homes — not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Do I own the land in a Langley park?** In a pad-rental park, no: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad.** Pioneer Park is the co-op exception in form — its Aldergrove guide home file governs that tenure. (§2.)
- **Q: Is a park home a half duplex if it sits beside another home?** No. A half duplex is one half of a two-unit building sold as a 2-lot strata lot or on its own subdivided fee-simple lot. A manufactured home on a rented pad is a chattel on a tenancy — adjacency to another home changes nothing about the tenure. (§2.)
- **Q: Are the parks on this page all 55+?** Most first-page cards describe their parks as a "55+ community" — an age-restriction claim taken from listing copy only, not verified park by park. Langley Grove Estates' settled position is the opposite: "no age restrictions" listing-stated, expressly NOT 55+, at its Otter District home. Confirm each park's written rules. (§2.)
- **Q: How much is pad rent on a Langley mobile home?** No official source publishes a Langley pad-rent figure, average or range, so no figure is stated as fact in this file. Sibling pages of this site carry two conflicting unverified ranges ($600–$900 and $650–$950 per month); neither is adopted (§5). The figure that matters is the specific park's current rent in its written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement. (§§3, 5.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up each year?** Up to the RTB limit for manufactured home site tenancies: 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027. The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of sites in the park. Increases require 3 full months' notice and can occur only once every 12 months. (§3.)
- **Q: Does the park raise my pad rent when I buy the home?** No. On assignment under MHPTA s.28 the pad rent stays the same and the scheduled date of a subsequent increase does not change; assignment requires the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director) and the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent. (§3.)
- **Q: What happens if the park is converted to another use?** Under MHPTA s.42: the landlord must first have all necessary permits and approvals, then give 12 months' notice and pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes where the home cannot be moved or no site is available within a reasonable distance. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay on a duplex at $800,000? And on a park home?** On the worked tiers in §3 — fully residential, no exemption — $14,000 at $800,000, and a half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. For a park home on a rented pad, PTT treatment is UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule — it follows the actual transaction (home vs land), so have a lawyer or notary confirm it for the exact structure before offering. (§3.)
- **Q: Are there market statistics for Langley mobile homes or duplexes?** No manufactured-home statistics and no duplex statistics are published in the SnapStats report — it has neither category. §1's figures are all-residential Langley community context only. (§1.)
- **Q: What should I check before anything else on a park purchase?** The CSA label: without the original legible CSA decal the home cannot be sold until Technical Safety BC re-certification — label status is a price and timeline fact on every park purchase. Then the written site tenancy agreement: current rent, last increase date, and what the rent includes. (§3.)
