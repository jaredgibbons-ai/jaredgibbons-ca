## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning and plan (Surrey frame).** Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 governs the Surrey ground; the governing OCP is Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is REPLACED and never cited as current. Which zone permits a two-unit (duplex) form on a given parcel is a parcel-by-parcel read of Bylaw 12000 and the City's current zoning for that lot. White Rock ground in the same feed sits under White Rock's own bylaws, never Surrey's. (Research file above; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-development/zoning; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan)
- **SSMUH position (Surrey frame).** Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial SSMUH framework; Surrey's Jul 8, 2024 changes permit a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, as the siblings carry the City's SSMUH page. (Condos sibling of the presales slug: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/south-surrey-new-construction-presales-condos-2026-11-01.md, §3; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (Surrey's own rule).** A secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area** under Zoning By-law No. 12000 — Surrey's rule, never borrowed; a suite is not a separate strata lot and cannot be sold independently. Suite legality for any specific listing is UNVERIFIED from listing text alone. (Standing Surrey frame, brief chain; presales condos sibling above, §3; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing)
- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), verbatim where the settled treatment applies.** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, provides that **a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate**. In BC, real estate trading services are regulated by BCFSA, which states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. The live page's own "Before you walk in" copy is consistent with that frame: it warns that the listing agent works for the seller and advises asking for the Form B, depreciation report and minutes at a strata open house. How open-house dates and times are set and entered for publication, and any identification or sign-in requirement for visitors, are UNVERIFIED — no BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states either. (Research file above, §4; http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download, read 2026-11-01; https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers, read 2026-11-01; https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-open-houses.html, read 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) seen through any feed.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. Servicing: Surrey's Subdivision and Development (Servicing) Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 is VERIFIED from the City's own pages in the ledger. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is ever stated. Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (foreclosure mapping UNVERIFIED). - **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
