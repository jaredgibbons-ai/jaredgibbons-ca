## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (city frame for the topic).** Duplexes are a permitted two-unit form in Surrey's residential zones that allow a duplex under Zoning By-law No. 12000, and the provincial SSMUH implementation (Surrey, Jul 8, 2024) makes the duplex the baseline multi-unit form on eligible lots. The OCP governing is Bylaw 21900 (Surrey 2050, adopted Jun 15, 2026) — never 18020-as-current. The zone for a specific property seen at an open house is read off the City's current zoning for that lot. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-development/zoning
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Surrey's own rule.** One secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area** under Zoning By-law No. 12000 — Surrey's rule, never borrowed. (Standing Surrey frame, brief chain.)
- **Open-house conduct rule (settled treatment for open-houses topic files).** BCFSA Rule 5-10(3) governs the licensee conduct side of open houses in BC; the rule text is carried verbatim in the settled open-houses topic treatment in the sibling program and is not paraphrased into a different rule here. An open house is a marketing event — it creates no diligence shortcut and no representation about title form. (Settled topic treatment, brief chain.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) seen through any feed.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Surrey's servicing position is Bylaw 8830 (1986) in the verified ledger. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: An open-house card shows a duplex — can I buy just the side I walked through?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot. The card settles nothing; check the title. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is the open-house time on the Surrey feed reliable?** The settled finding is that the Surrey city feed has NO paired card evidence, so NO offset is asserted — treat the feed time as a record and confirm with the listing brokerage before attending. (§2.)
- **Q: Does this topic file list Surrey's duplexes?** No. It is a topic file with no roster; duplex stock lives in the home-area files. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite inside a Surrey duplex?** Surrey permits one secondary suite in a duplex dwelling capped at 40% of the principal unit's habitable area, where the zoning applies. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
