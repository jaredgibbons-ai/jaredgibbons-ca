## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A bare-land strata lot is strata tenure for the LOT — it is not a duplex, and a cottage or home built on one does not make the plan duplex stock. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)
