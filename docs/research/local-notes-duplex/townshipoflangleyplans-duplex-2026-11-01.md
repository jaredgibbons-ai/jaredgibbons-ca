## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. In the Township, a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — a second unit on the same fee-simple lot, never a strata unit and never a half duplex. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing; Township coach-house position: settled Township frame.)

- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) on Township ground.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is this a listings page?** No — it is an index of twelve Township community and neighbourhood plan tiles linking to tol.ca, with no MLS® listings or result count at reading. Shop duplexes on the area pages for the ground; use this page to understand the plan layer over a parcel. (§1.)
- **Q: How do I read a Township parcel before pricing a duplex build?** In layers, in this order: OCP designation (Bylaw No. 6200 update, December 1, 2025), then the community plan, then any neighbourhood plan, then current zoning under Bylaw No. 2500. The plan shows where the Township wants growth; the zoning is what can be built as-of-right today. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Have the Carvolth, Jericho, Latimer, Routley and Gordon Estate plans been repealed?** UNVERIFIED as stated on the live page — its tiles state repeal on June 15, 2026 and replacement by Bylaw Nos. 6202/6203, but the Township's Willoughby update page records only first and second reading of Bylaw Nos. 6201, 6202 and 6203 on May 25, 2026. Confirm adoption with the Township before relying on either version, and never copy the page's repeal dates into an offer or a listing. (§§3, 5.)
- **Q: How many units can SSMUH give a Township lot?** 3 units at ≤280 m² and 4 units at 280–4,050 m² on eligible lots under Bylaw No. 6020 — there is no 6-plex tier in the Township — and a Development Permit under the April 27, 2026 SSMUH DPA may be required unless an exemption applies. Price the SSMUH layer separately from the plan layer. (§3.)
- **Q: How big can a suite or coach house be?** A secondary suite is capped at 120 m² under the Township rule; a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — it sits on the same lot under the same title, is never strata, and cannot be sold as a "half" of anything. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $1,200,000? At $2,000,000?** On the worked tiers in §3 — fully residential, no exemption — $22,000 at $1,200,000 and $38,000 at $2,000,000. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a neighbourhood plan change what I can build this year?** A plan sets policy; it does not itself permit. In Brookswood-Fernridge, for example, the Rinn plan (Bylaw No. 6010, adopted June 10, 2024; Rinn Update 2026, Bylaw No. 6219) shows the Township still refining that area lot-by-lot — check the neighbourhood plan, not just the community plan, then confirm current zoning. (§3.)
- **Q: Which school district serves the Township?** SD35 Langley — but this topic file names no schools and promises no catchment; catchments are address-based and are covered in the area files for each ground. (Settled Township frame.)
