## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. Neither a pad tenancy nor an undivided-interest resort holding is either form. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Q: What is the difference between buying a duplex and buying a half duplex, if one appears?** A duplex purchase is the whole two-unit building and lot on one title. - **Q: Can I add a suite on a Hatzic property?** It depends which Hatzic: on the Bench, City registration and the City suite rules govern (no suites in townhouses or rowhomes); on the Prairie (Area F), a suite must sit within the principal dwelling — a detached garden suite is not the suite route — and on ALR land the ALC frame also applies. - **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on a Hatzic purchase?** Run the tiers on your own price: $800,000 → $14,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000 (fully residential, no exemption). A half duplex is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. (§3.)
