## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Municipality: **none — and that is the key identity fact.** "University VW" is not in the City of Vancouver. It covers the University Endowment Lands (UEL) and UBC's Point Grey campus lands (UBC's own residential neighbourhoods such as Wesbrook Village and Hampton Place). The UEL is an unincorporated community administered directly by the Province of BC under the University Endowment Land Act, with no elected municipal council; for Metro Vancouver purposes it sits in Electoral Area A, and day-to-day services are delivered by the UEL Administration, guided by the UEL Official Community Plan and the Land Use, Building and Community Administration Bylaw. Postal addresses read "Vancouver, BC," which is the source of most confusion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/local-governments/improvement-districts-governance-bodies/university-endowment-lands (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/university-vw.md)
- Board name: the GVR board's MLS® sub-area name is "University VW" (VW = Vancouver West in the board's naming convention). Nearby areas: West Point Grey, Dunbar-Southlands, Kitsilano (across Pacific Spirit Regional Park), and the Musqueam First Nation lands to the south. (Research file §1.)
- The ground splits in two, and the split governs everything in this file: (1) the UEL's older residential streets east of campus — predominantly **freehold** lots originally granted by the Crown; and (2) UBC's planned neighbourhoods — Wesbrook Village, Hampton Place, Chancellor Place, Hawthorn Place, East Campus — built on **99-year prepaid leasehold** land held by UBC and developed through UBC Properties Trust; UBC Campus and Community Planning confirms residential properties on UBC's endowment lands are never sold outright but are leased as prepaid 99-year leases. The UEL comprises four areas (A, B, C and D) with a population of nearly 4,000, and City of Vancouver water billing is not included on the UEL property tax bill. https://www.universityendowmentlands.gov.bc.ca/library/Reports/Realtor%20Information%202026-03-12.pdf and https://elementslab.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Wesbrook-Study_reducedsize_secure2.pdf (Research file §4.)

## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the rules of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Jurisdiction first — the rule that frames everything here.** Establish which ground a property sits on before applying any rule: the UEL (provincial administration under the University Endowment Land Act, guided by the UEL Official Community Plan and the Land Use, Building and Community Administration Bylaw), UBC campus lands (the university's own framework), or — for streets east of Blanca — actually the City of Vancouver. Permitting, bylaws and tax billing differ in each. **NO City of Vancouver zoning frame is asserted in this file**: no Vancouver zone number, SSMUH district or suite rule is carried for UEL/UBC ground, and any source applying one here is not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/local-governments/improvement-districts-governance-bodies/university-endowment-lands and https://www.universityendowmentlands.gov.bc.ca/library/Reports/Realtor%20Information%202026-03-12.pdf (Research file §§4, 6.)
- **Where a duplex form could sit — stated only as the instruments support.** On the UEL side, land use runs under the UEL OCP and the Land Use, Building and Community Administration Bylaw; the research names no UEL duplex zone and no UEL two-unit entitlement, so **the duplex entitlement for any specific UEL lot is UNCONFIRMED until read in the current UEL instruments for that lot**. On the UBC side, the planned neighbourhoods were built as strata product on 99-year prepaid ground leases — a buyer there never buys a subdivided parcel at all (see the subdivision line below). The provincial SSMUH municipal framework does not apply to this unincorporated / university ground in the form it applies inside a municipality, and no SSMUH unit count is asserted for this slug. (Research file §4; program discipline.)
- **Secondary suites — no borrowed rule.** No UEL or UBC suite rule (and no suite size cap) is stated in this slug's research or siblings, so **NO cap figure is carried here** — Surrey's 40%, the Township's 120 m² and every other jurisdiction's figure are never borrowed. The suite position for a specific home is UNCONFIRMED until read in the governing instrument and, for a strata unit, the strata's bylaws. (Research file; siblings per the FILE STATUS block.)
- **Leasehold diligence — the governing diligence on the UBC side.** For any Wesbrook / Hampton Place / Chancellor Place / Hawthorn Place purchase, order and read the head lease before subject removal: confirm the lease start date, prepaid status, remaining term (which runs from the project's original lease date, not the purchase date), and any UBC rights, including faculty/staff resale restrictions on some program homes. Confirm the University Neighbourhoods Association services levy and any neighbourhood-specific charges in the strata budget — the UNA, not a city, delivers municipal-like services to roughly 15,000 residents of UBC's residential neighbourhoods under its Neighbours Agreement with UBC. https://elementslab.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Wesbrook-Study_reducedsize_secure2.pdf and https://www.myuna.ca/about-us/ (Research file §§4–6.)
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex, and in full to the leasehold strata stock above.** Form B diligence applies in full — on leasehold strata, the Form B is read alongside the head lease, never instead of it. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** On UBC lands there is no buyer-side subdivision at all: SFU-style parcel subdivision happened at the institutional level — here, UBC Properties Trust develops on land UBC retains — and a strata plan, not a fee-simple split, is how homes are divided. On the UEL side, a fee-simple split of a duplex parcel would require subdivision approval under the UEL's provincial administration; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the titles — read them. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file §5.)
- **Taxes — the lines that differ here, stated plainly.** **The City of Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply to this area** — the EHT is a City of Vancouver tax and this area is not in the City; there is no EHT declaration or tax for a University VW property. UEL owners pay taxes under the provincial (Electoral Area A / Surveyor of Taxes) framework rather than to a city: because the UEL is taxed as a rural area, annual property taxes are payable to the Province's Surveyor of Taxes and are due July 2 of the year they are first levied, with a penalty of 5% of the unpaid taxes added to any portion unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% to any portion still unpaid on October 31; rural tax notices are mailed by May 31. UEL water is billed separately from the tax notice. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 and https://www.universityendowmentlands.gov.bc.ca/library/Reports/Realtor%20Information%202026-03-12.pdf (Research file §4; condos sibling §3.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer; a leasehold interest transfer is taxed on its own consideration — confirm the filing position per transaction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is this area part of the City of Vancouver?** No. The sub-area covers the provincially administered UEL plus UBC's Point Grey lands; the postal city reads "Vancouver," but no City of Vancouver bylaws apply here. (§1.)
- **Q: Can I buy half a duplex near UBC?** None is verified by name. No strata duplex or subdivided fee-simple half is named on any primary source for this ground; the attached stock that does verify is leasehold strata condos and townhouse-form product in the UBC neighbourhoods. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Tapestry at Wesbrook Village a duplex option?** No — it is apartment-form condominium stock (46 condominium homes beside 134 rental apartments, completed 2010), leasehold strata, counted at its condos home. It is carried in this file as a tenure record only: a purchase there is a leasehold strata purchase with Form B diligence owed, not a half-duplex purchase. (§2.)
- **Q: A listing calls a Hampton Place home "half-duplex-style" — what am I buying?** On the documented case (Sandringham), a townhouse-form unit in a leasehold strata complex — the wording is a listing description, not a legal form. Diligence is the Form B plus the head lease, not a party-wall agreement. (§2.)
- **Q: Do I file a Vancouver Empty Homes Tax declaration here?** No — the EHT applies only inside the City of Vancouver, and this area is not in the City. (§3.)
- **Q: What is different about taxes on the UEL side?** UEL homes are taxed as a rural area: taxes go to the Province's Surveyor of Taxes, due July 2, with 5% penalties after July 2 and after October 31 — and water is billed separately from the tax notice, which catches out buyers who assume City of Vancouver practice. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a UEL house?** UNCONFIRMED per property — no UEL suite rule is carried in this program, and no other city's cap is borrowed. Read the current UEL instruments for the lot before counting rental income. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. Separately, verify any UBC-area rental rules in the strata bylaws and any UBC/UEL restrictions building by building — the research flags that check as required diligence, not as a sourced rule. (§3; research file §6.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
