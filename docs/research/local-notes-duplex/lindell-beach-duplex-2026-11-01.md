## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any half duplex for sale at Lindell Beach?** None verified, in any source in this program — and the governing OCP's designations permit only single-family residential use. A Lindell Beach purchase is a single-family cottage or home on its own parcel (or a bare-land strata lot in one of the cross-referenced communities). (§2.)
- **Q: Is Lindell Beach part of Chilliwack?** Not municipally. It is an unincorporated community in FVRD Electoral Area H with a Chilliwack mailing address; the FVRD is its local government, and its plan/zoning are FVRD documents (OCP Bylaw 1115; Zoning Bylaw 1638), not City bylaws. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws/planning-land-use.html (§1, §3.)
- **Q: Is this the same tenure as Cultus Lake Park?** No. North-shore Park homes sit on leased park land under the Park Board's leaseholder system; Lindell Beach ground is predominantly freehold. The exception that proves the rule is the Holiday Resort, where the documented model is an owned trailer on a leased pad — check title on every individual property. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I run a short-term rental in a Lindell Beach cottage?** Only if the zone expressly permits Temporary Tourist Accommodation under Bylaw 1638, and a provincial STR registration number has been required since May 1, 2025. The FVRD enabled Area H to proactively address STRs — do not count on a seller's rental history carrying over. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex (off this ground)?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. (§3.)
- **Q: Is Lindell Beach on sewer and water, or septic and well?** FVRD service bylaws cover Cultus Lake water supply and South Cultus Lake sewage treatment, with parcel taxes for participating properties — confirm connection status lot by lot; south-shore services are tax-funded, and the sewer parcel tax is shared equally across the roll. (§3.)
- **Q: When are property taxes due here?** Under the rural regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11): July 2, with 5% penalties after July 2 and a further 5% after October 31 — not a city tax calendar. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a cottage purchase here?** On the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). There is no electoral-area PTT variant. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: What is the flood / debris risk on a fan lot?** The community sits partly on the Frosst Creek alluvial fan; Floodplain Bylaw 1669, 2022 requires fan development to meet the level/setback set by a site-specific geohazard assessment. Ask for the lot's assessment report; property-specific reports are UNVERIFIED in this file. (§3.)
