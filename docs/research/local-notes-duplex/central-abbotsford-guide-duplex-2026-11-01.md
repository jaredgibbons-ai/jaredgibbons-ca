## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. A home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership at all — the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**FORM CLASS ONLY — NO DUPLEX ROSTER, stated at the top of this section.** No named duplex, strata half duplex or fee-simple half-duplex development is verified in Central Abbotsford in this guide slug's research or its guide siblings. None is invented. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. - **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a duplex development in Central Abbotsford I can buy into?** No named duplex development is verified in this program. What is verified is the form class — individual duplexes on single lots, with the Historic Downtown plan's Urban 2 and Urban 3 designations expressly enabling duplex housing on qualifying lots. - **Q: The plan says my street "enables duplex housing" — does that mean a duplex project is coming?** No. Urban 2 – Ground Oriented and Urban 3 – Infill are designations that permit the form; they name no project, no builder and no timeline. Price a lot on its zone and its s.140 position, not on a plan label. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: How many units can I put on a central lot, and can it have a suite?** Under s.140: 3 units on a lot of 280 m² or less, 4 units on a larger lot, in the stated conditions; and one secondary suite is permitted in all single detached dwellings (s.140.3.8), capped at under 50% of the principal dwelling. A coach / garden suite is capped at 15% of the lot to a maximum of 100 m² — Abbotsford's own figures. (§3.)
- **Q: Is Georgian Park a strata or bare-land purchase?** Neither — it is a pad-rental manufactured home park at its guide mobile home: you would own the home as a chattel and rent the pad; you would not own land, and the MHPTA — not the Strata Property Act — governs. Its 55+ position is listing-stated; confirm it in the park's written rules. (§2.)
- **Q: How did Central Abbotsford sell in September 2026?** Detached: 70 listings, 12 sold, 17% ratio — joint-fastest in the city. Condos & Townhomes: 256 listings, 35 sold, 14% — the fastest attached ratio and the deepest attached selection in Abbotsford. No Central-Abbotsford-only median is published; the Abbotsford medians are community context only, and duplexes have no SnapStats category. (§1.)
- **Q: What lot size do I need to subdivide or infill?** Match the lot to the zone first: RS3 needs 540 m², RS5 needs 420 m², and RS7 infill lots go down to 300 m² (10.0 m width, 22.5 m depth) — among the smallest detached lot minimums in the city — but zone and stated conditions decide, so confirm the zone section before paying for potential. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $1,500,000? At $2,000,000?** On the worked tiers in §3 — fully residential, no exemption — $28,000 at $1,500,000 and $38,000 at $2,000,000. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. (§3.)
