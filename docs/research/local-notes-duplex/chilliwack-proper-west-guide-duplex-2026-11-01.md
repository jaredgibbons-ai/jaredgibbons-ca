## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **SSMUH (Chilliwack's own position):** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, amending Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, adopted June 18, 2024, allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing — duplexes are the baseline form where it applies. (Research file, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf)
- **Secondary suites and accessory units (Chilliwack's own rule, never borrowed):** under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the habitable floor space of the building (s.2); only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot, with permitted types including secondary suite, coach house and garden suite, garden suites capped at 100 m² (s.4.06(1)); ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite (s.5.04); a suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. So a Chilliwack duplex carries at most ONE accessory unit on its lot on this rule — a suite inside one half does not create a second ADU for the other half. (Research file, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf; https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724)
- **Short-term rentals:** the City regulates short-term rental operations under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141, adopted November 24, 2021, which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968. (Research file, §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf)
