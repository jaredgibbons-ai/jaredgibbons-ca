## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **CITY side (roughly 25th–29th Street) — City of North Vancouver rulebook ONLY on this side.** The Official Community Plan is the Official Community Plan, 2014, No. 8400, amended December 8, 2025 (Amendment Bylaw 2025, No. 9114) for provincial housing requirements; the zoning bylaw is Zoning Bylaw 6700. City planning staff described the blocks along Lonsdale as within Residential Level 5 with a maximum height of 6 storeys in a developer information session summary for 2762 Lonsdale Avenue. Subdivision on the City side runs under the City's Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw 8014 — a City-side instrument, never carried north of 29th Street. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Official-Community-Plan-2014-No-8400-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9114-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-t.pdf and https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Major-Development/2762-Lonsdale-Avenue/Developer-Information-Session-Summary.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **CITY side suites and units — the City's own rule.** The City legalized secondary suites in all single-family dwellings in 1993; **one suite is permitted per single-family property, the owner must reside at the property**, and a suite may be combined with an accessory coach house, which is permitted where the front lot line is at least 10 m (32.81 ft) and requires a Development Permit and a Building Permit. On December 8, 2025 the City adopted the Ground Oriented (GO) Zone, increasing permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 residential properties and allowing up to 6 units per lot on approximately 600 properties. No suite size-cap figure for the City is stated in this slug's research — none is carried here, and the District's 49% figure is never borrowed across the line. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Permits-Inspections/building-permits/secondary-suites and https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/HTO-Year-2-Progress-Report-July-1,-2025-June-30,-2026.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **DISTRICT side (29th Street to Rockland Avenue) — District of North Vancouver rulebook ONLY on this side.** The Official Community Plan is Bylaw 7900, which directs growth to its four centres and preserves established low-density neighbourhoods — covering the District portion of Upper Lonsdale; the zoning bylaw is Zoning Bylaw 3210. District-side planning is as the District OCP implementation summary states it; the City's GO Zone and Residential Level 5 material are never carried north of 29th Street. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **DISTRICT side suites and units — the District's own rule.** District zoning has permitted a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — **three units total** — on single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary since June 2024, with suites **up to 49% of the house** and coach houses up to two storeys. That 49% figure is the District's own suite rule, stated for the District side only. The District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows potential for up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 above 280 m², and up to 6 above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop; the Province's Bill 25 requires full SSMUH density by June 30, 2026 and the District's amendments were still in preparation in spring 2026. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **SSMUH position, both sides stated separately:** on the City side the operative position is the City's own December 2025 GO Zone adoption above; on the District side it is the District's GEOweb classification plus its still-in-preparation Bill 25 compliance amendments above. A duplex (two units) sits inside even the lowest tier on either side — but which municipality's instrument governs a specific lot is read off the municipal maps first, never assumed from the sub-area name. (Research file §4.)
- **Hazards.** Slope is the defining condition: the District designates Natural Hazard Development Permit Areas and publishes property-level GeoWeb hazard reports — steep upper-Lonsdale lots should be checked before pricing an addition or rebuild. That hazard frame is stated for the District side as the research states it; a City-side lot's permit position is read at the City's desk. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-1-district-landslide.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **Servicing, side by side and never blended:** City side — the City's Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw 8014 governs on the City side only, per the lots-and-land sibling. District side — **the District's servicing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED** in the settled ledger; no number is asserted for the District side. A City-side parcel does not carry District instruments, and vice versa. (Lots-and-land sibling §3; settled ledger.)
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / half duplex on either side (and in full to the 29th Street cluster).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — a 143-unit complex like Somerset Green or the 50-unit Vista 29 strata is INSIDE that cycle on lot count; **a 2-lot strata duplex, by contrast, is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone** — the distinction is stated precisely for both forms. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the governing municipality's Approving Officer process — City process on the City side (under Bylaw 8014's frame), District process on the District side; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Property taxes — same street, different bills.** Homes south of the boundary pay City of North Vancouver taxes; homes north of it pay District taxes. Both municipalities set 2026 property taxes due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026. City 2026 rates: residential municipal-general 1.89202 and total 3.34028 per $1,000 of assessed value, plus a separate residential storm drainage rate of 0.10697 per $1,000; the City illustrates an average residential property assessed at $1,387,684 paying $4,790 in total 2026 property taxes. District 2026 rates: residential municipal 1.83419 and total 3.28195 per $1,000, with single-family utility fees including water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026_TAX_RATE_SUMMARY_CITY_OF_NORTH_VANCOUVER-2.PDF and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer — the same math on either side of 29th Street, as PTT is a provincial tax. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a half duplex in Upper Lonsdale?** No named half duplex — strata or fee-simple — is verified anywhere in this program for this ground, on either side of 29th Street. - **Q: Is Upper Lonsdale in the City or the District?** Both. Roughly 25th to 29th Street is City of North Vancouver; 29th Street north to Rockland Avenue is District of North Vancouver. Check the specific address — taxes and bylaws follow the municipality, not the sub-area name. (§1.)
- **Q: Why do two houses on the same street get different tax bills?** Because they are in different municipalities: City 2026 total residential rate 3.34028 per $1,000 (plus a separate storm drainage rate) versus District total 3.28195 per $1,000, with different utility structures — both due July 2, 2026. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite on a City-side house?** The City's own rule: one suite per single-family property, legalized in 1993, owner must reside at the property; a coach house may be added where the front lot line is at least 10 m, with a Development Permit and Building Permit. The District's 49% suite figure does not apply on the City side. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite and coach house on a District-side house?** The District's own rule since June 2024: principal dwelling + suite + coach house, three units total, suite up to 49% of the house, coach house up to two storeys. The City's owner-occupancy suite rule does not apply on the District side — read your own side's rulebook. (§3.)
- **Q: Does the density math really change at 29th Street?** Yes: the City adopted its GO Zone on December 8, 2025 (3 → 4 units on nearly 4,300 properties; up to 6 per lot on about 600), while the District's Bill 25 compliance amendments were still in preparation in spring 2026 — which side of 29th Street the lot sits on changes the density math entirely, as the research's buying tips put it. (§3.)

It is a 55+ development; exact occupancy-covenant wording is a Form B item. It is not a duplex. (§2.)
- **Q: Which secondary school serves the east edge — Carson Graham or Argyle?** UNVERIFIED without a chosen numbered address: the research states Carson Graham serves most of the sub-area and flags the easternmost addresses for an SD44 check against the Argyle boundary; no locator run for an Upper Lonsdale east-edge address is documented in this program, so the east-edge position is stated as UNVERIFIED, never assumed. (§5.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
