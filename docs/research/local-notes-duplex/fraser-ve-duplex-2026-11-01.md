## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. Fraser E49 below is neither a 2-lot duplex nor a fee-simple half: it is a 4-home strata multiplex whose individual homes MLS splits between Townhouse and Half Duplex sub-types — the sub-type label is a feed classification, and the file counts the complex once, in its townhomes home, as strata product. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** The residential side streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m), 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); the zone and dimensions of a specific lot are confirmed parcel by parcel and are UNVERIFIED for any individual lot until read. (Research file above.) https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **SSMUH position.** The R1-1 schedule above IS Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit framework for these streets — house plus suite and laneway, duplex, or a multiplex to 6 strata / 8 rental units by lot size — so the "SSMUH question" here is a per-lot schedule check (306 / 464 / 557 m²), not a separate overlay. SSMUH adds unit potential; it does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Townhomes sibling above.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the City's own position.** A single detached house under R1-1 may include a secondary suite and a laneway house. For a duplex, the schedule's unit and site-area rules govern the lot; no Vancouver suite size cap figure beyond the schedule is asserted here — the position for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until its schedule is read. (Research file above.)
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY, 3%.** Vancouver owners must file an annual property status declaration, and a property that meets the tax's vacancy tests is taxed at 3% (Vancouver only — never applied to any other municipality in this program). A duplex or half duplex left empty between tenants is read against this tax; a strata purchase does not remove it. (Condos sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/fraser-ve-condos-2026-11-01.md.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), and to Fraser E49's 4-lot strata.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone** (a 4-lot strata such as Fraser E49 is also below it on lot count — ask what the strata holds anyway). Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, measured against the R1-1 minimum site areas above; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Vancouver's servicing position is carried in the verified ledger as Bylaw 5208 (fees 14538). (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex on the Fraser corridor?** Only if that side is legally separate — a strata lot in a 2-lot strata plan, or its own fee-simple lot from a subdivision. A Fraser E49 home is a strata lot in a 4-home multiplex strata, which is a different purchase. Check the title first. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is Fraser E49 a half-duplex development?** No — it is a 4-home strata fourplex by Alabaster Homes, counted once in its townhomes home; MLS splits its homes between Townhouse and Half Duplex sub-types, which is a feed classification, not a title form. (§2.)
- **Q: The listing says "townhouse" but shows no strata fee — what am I buying?** Usually a fee-simple duplex or half-duplex on an R1-1 lot: no strata, no Form B, no strata fees, and diligence runs on title and municipal rules instead. Check the Title to Land field before assuming either way. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count, and a 4-lot strata is too. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax hit a Fraser duplex left empty?** Yes, in Vancouver: owners must file an annual property status declaration, and a property meeting the vacancy tests is taxed at 3% — Vancouver only. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a duplex here?** Address-based in SD39 — the documented locator run for 530 East 41st Avenue returned Sir Alexander Mackenzie Elementary and John Oliver Secondary for that address only. Run the exact address. (§5.)
