## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan and zoning (Surrey frame).** The current plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is replaced and is NEVER cited as current. Zoning Bylaw 12000 governs. Which zone permits a two-unit (duplex) form on a given village-edge or Grandview parcel is a parcel-by-parcel read of Bylaw 12000 — no zone-by-zone duplex permission table is asserted, because no source opened for this slug states one. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is ever stated. (Surrey frame, settled ledger; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000)
- **SSMUH position (Surrey frame).** Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial SSMUH framework; Surrey's SSMUH changes are in force Jul 8, 2024 — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, as verified in this program. (Reused from batch-11/elgin-chantrell research, cited in this program's South Surrey presales research: https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing)
- **Suites inside a duplex (Surrey frame).** A secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area** — Surrey's own cap, never borrowed from another city; a "90 m²" cap is NOT the current Surrey suite rule. (Settled Surrey position in this program; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000)
- **STR (Surrey frame).** Short-term rental licence $358/yr carried, with the City's 2024 corporate-report proposal of $350/yr disclosed alongside it — the conflict is stated, neither figure is silently adopted; check the City licence page before quoting. (Settled Surrey frame, D15 brief.)
- **Servicing and registry (Surrey frame).** Subdivision and Development (Servicing) Bylaw 8830 (1986) is VERIFIED from the City's own pages. Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry — foreclosure registry mapping is UNVERIFIED and is never filled in. (Settled Surrey frame, D15 brief.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. For the village's 2008–2011 condo buildings, the live page's own advice is the same diligence in practice: read the depreciation report and the last two years of strata minutes closely, looking for envelope, roof and elevator work coming up. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws (page advice: https://jaredgibbons.ca/morgan-crossing-homes-for-sale.html)
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. First-time buyers: the page states many Morgan Crossing condos are priced under $835,000, where the BC first-time buyer exemption can exempt the first $500,000 (saving up to $8,000, partial to $860,000) — site copy; verify eligibility before relying on it. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Morgan Crossing's buildings are 2008–2011 resale stock per the page table, so a resale carries no GST and is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
