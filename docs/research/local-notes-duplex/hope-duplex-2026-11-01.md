## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** The District of Hope Zoning Bylaw implements the OCP; the OCP and Zoning Bylaw were updated together to meet current provincial housing requirements, and the current plan is **OCP Bylaw 1617, 2025, adopted February 9, 2026**. - **SSMUH position.** Per the District's Interim Housing Needs Report as cited in the research: the 2024 SSMUH amendments allow 3 units on parcels under 280 m² and 4 on larger parcels in former 1–2-unit zones — duplexes are the baseline form inside that permission. No SSMUH bylaw number is asserted. (Research file above, §5.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex.** Suites are a documented part of Hope's recent build mix (22 suite permits 2021–Oct. 2024 per the Housing Needs Report), but no District suite percentage cap was stated in this slug's research — UNCONFIRMED, and no other city's cap is borrowed here. - **Flood — a first-order rule on this ground.** New provincial floodplain maps covering the Coquihalla and Fraser rivers from Yale to Mission were released in June 2026, and the Fraser's design flood profile is anchored to the Hope gauging station (the 1894 flood is the design event). The District administers Coquihalla River dikes (River Parade Flood Protection, 0.93 km; Glenhalla Subdivision, 1.01 km), and construction in hazard areas needs a **Flood and Erosion Hazards Development Permit** with a qualified-professional report (Council, Jan. 13, 2025). Parcel-level flood construction levels are UNVERIFIED. Start every Hope duplex file with the flood maps. (Research file above, §4; https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026WLRS0029-000715 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/air-land-water/water/integrated-flood-hazard-mgmt/dikes_listed_by_riverwatercoursepdf.pdf)
- **STRATA block — stated for completeness; no strata duplex exists on this ground.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists elsewhere: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the District's Approving Officer process; no such split is documented on this ground. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)

- **Tax notes, stated with their limits:** District of Hope municipal taxes plus FVRD levies (recreation is an FVRD service here); the 2025 Annual Tax Rate Bylaw is No. 1601, 2025, and Council's finance report (April 28, 2025) finalized the 2025 increase at 2.81% for items the District taxes directly. - **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12; see the sibling check in §5) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any half duplex for sale in Hope?** None verified by name, in any source in this program. What trades here is predominantly freehold detached houses; a duplex on a single lot sells whole. (§2.)
- **Q: A listing calls a Hope home a duplex — can I buy one side?** Only if a strata plan or a subdivided lot gives that side its own title. Without one there is no separate half to buy. (§2 definitions.)
- **Q: Can a Hope lot take 3–4 units now?** Per the District's Housing Needs Report as cited in the research, the 2024 SSMUH amendments allow 3 units on parcels under 280 m² and 4 on larger parcels in former 1–2-unit zones — confirm the lot's zone and flood status before pricing on it. (§3.)
- **Q: Is flooding a real issue for a Hope duplex?** Parts of the District are Fraser/Coquihalla floodplain; updated provincial floodplain maps for this reach were released June 2026, the design flood is measured at the Hope gauge, and hazard-area construction needs a District Flood and Erosion Hazards Development Permit with a QP report — a dike nearby does not remove the permit step. (§3.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex (off this ground)?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). (§3 strata block.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Are duplex prices published for Hope?** No. CADREB publishes no area stats and no SnapStats row exists — pricing rests on comparable sales, separated by micro-market (townsite, Silver Creek, Kawkawa Lake, Flood/Laidlaw). (§1.)
- **Q: Is there public transit?** Limited — BC Transit Route 72 links Hope with Agassiz via Highway 7, connecting to Route 71 for Chilliwack and Harrison Hot Springs; the FVRD administers the routes. Hope is a driving community. (Research file, §7; https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html)
