## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. On ALR land there is a further gate: depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; ALR strata rule: ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6 — https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning (Township frame).** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2500 governs. The coach-house definition is in s.102 — cited as the definition source only; a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata. Which zone permits a two-unit (duplex) form on a given Campbell Valley parcel is a parcel-by-parcel Township read — no zone-by-zone duplex permission table is asserted, because no source opened for this slug states one. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/campbell-valley.md, §4.)
- **SSMUH position (Township frame — generally inapplicable to acreage parcels).** The Township adopted Bylaw 6020 on November 18, 2024 — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and NO 6-plex tier. Most Campbell Valley acreage parcels exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by a wide margin, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them; parcel-level eligibility is UNVERIFIED. Duplexes are the baseline form under the provincial SSMUH framework where it does apply. (Research file above, §4.)
- **Suites inside a principal dwelling (Township frame + ALR overlay).** Township secondary suite cap: 120 m². On an ALR parcel the ALC frame is the tighter gate: a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence. The Township cap is the Township's own rule, never borrowed from another city. (Township frame, D15 brief; ALC frame: research file above, §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **Servicing (Township frame).** Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED. Well and septic position for a specific parcel: UNVERIFIED at parcel level — feed cards refer in their own text to drilled wells, filtration, septic fields and mobile-home pads, which are listing copy for those cards only. (Research file above, §4.)
- **Farm classification (BC Assessment).** Farm classification is a voluntary program; the income thresholds are $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha; applications must be submitted to BC Assessment by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. (Research file above, §4; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. On ALR land, creating that strata plan itself needs ALC permission (Bulletin 09, s.6). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Township's Approving Officer process under Bylaw 5382; on an ALR parcel, subdivision and stratification additionally need ALC permission. No finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file above, §4.)
- **Taxes (Township frame).** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a penalty of 5% is applied to current year taxes, including the unclaimed home owner grant, at midnight July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. (Research file above, §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. How PTT and GST are apportioned on a specific farm/acreage purchase is UNVERIFIED. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
