## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan — PlanCultus, never Chilliwack.** PlanCultus, the Cultus Lake Park Plan, Bylaw No. 1080, 2016, is the land use plan for Cultus Lake Park and designates the existing residential neighbourhoods as Residential, with single-family residential use and, subject to Park bylaws, secondary suites or coach houses. Village Centre redevelopment is a Park Board request-for-proposals process with a preferred proponent in negotiations; it is not a verified developer presale project. (Research file above; https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/)
- **Zoning — FVRD Cultus Lake Park Zoning Bylaw No. 1375, 2016.** The bylaw zones the Park lease lands; zones in this area include R-1 Suburban Residential and R-2 Urban Residential. R-1 allows one single-detached dwelling per lot; **R-2 allows one single-detached dwelling plus one secondary suite**. The bylaw tables state no minimum lot area figure for R-1/R-2, so minimum lot area and frontage are UNVERIFIED. That R-2 suite permission is this ground's own documented second-unit rule, stated as the bylaw states it — no other municipality's suite size cap is borrowed, and no suite size cap for these zones is stated in the research (any cap: UNCONFIRMED). No zone permission for a two-unit duplex form on a Park lease lot is stated in the research: a duplex permission here is UNCONFIRMED on the bylaw text as researched. (Research file above; https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Area-H-Zoning-Bylaw-1375-Cultus-Lake-Park-Amended.pdf)
- **SSMUH — NOT ASSERTED for this ground.** No SSMUH tier for Park Board lease land was opened in this slug's research, so none is stated in this file. The provincial SSMUH minimums are not imported onto lease lots here. (Research file above.)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata purchase at the lake (Lakeside's condos home), not to Park lease lots.** Form B diligence applies in full to a strata purchase. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold on lot count alone**. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 (long-term; a strata's short-term/vacation rental bylaws are a separate, parcel-level question — UNCONFIRMED for any specific building until its bylaws are read). Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. On Park lease lots the lease and Park bylaws govern instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split requires fee-simple land and subdivision approval; inside Cultus Lake Park the land is Park Board lease land, so the fee-simple half-duplex route does not arise at the north end on any source checked. No finer process detail is stated. (Program discipline, D1 brief; research file above, §4 tenure.)
- **Property taxes / levies — rural regime, never Chilliwack's.** Park lease lots pay Park Board lease charges rather than a standard municipal tax bill (schedule and per-lot levies UNVERIFIED). Electoral-area properties pay under the provincial rural regime: under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural property taxes are due and payable on **July 2** of the year they are first levied, a **5% penalty** is added to any portion unpaid on July 2, and a **further 5% penalty** to any portion unpaid on **October 31**. Chilliwack's city dates never apply here. (Research file above; https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/new-resident-information/; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. Whether and how PTT applies to a specific Park leasehold transfer is a conveyancer question — confirm the treatment of the specific tenure before budgeting (the lots sibling for this slug flags the same caution). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy just one side of a duplex at the north end?** There is no verified duplex at the north end to buy a side of — and inside the Park the land is Park Board lease land, so neither a strata-lot half nor a fee-simple half exists there on any source checked. What sells is the whole home plus its leasehold interest. (§2.)
- **Q: Start with the house or the lease?** The lease. Homes in Cultus Lake Park are on Park Board leased lots, so a buyer is purchasing the home and the leasehold interest, not freehold land — read the lease terms and Park bylaws before writing. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is Lakeside a duplex or townhouse complex?** Neither — Lakeside at Cultus Lake (3175 Columbia Valley Highway) is a 21-home waterfront condominium building on freehold waterfront land, filed and counted in this slug's condos sibling. It is cross-referenced here with no duplex count. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a north-end Park home?** Possibly, by zone: R-2 allows one single-detached dwelling plus one secondary suite under FVRD Zoning Bylaw No. 1375, 2016, and PlanCultus contemplates secondary suites or coach houses subject to Park bylaws. R-1 allows one single-detached dwelling only. Confirm the lot's zone and the Park bylaws first. (§3.)
- **Q: Is Cultus Lake North part of Chilliwack?** No. It is FVRD Electoral Area H / Cultus Lake Park Board ground south of the City of Chilliwack. Chilliwack bylaws and tax dates do not apply. (§1; §3.)
- **Q: Can I rent out a Park home?** For a Park lease lot, the lease and Park bylaws govern — read them before counting on rental income. (For a strata purchase, long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since November 24, 2022; short-term/vacation rental use is a separate bylaw question, UNCONFIRMED per building.) (§3.)
- **Q: Who do I pay, and what are the penalties?** Park lease lots pay Park Board lease charges rather than a municipal tax bill (schedule UNVERIFIED). Rural-regime taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31 (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11). (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated if I buy a separable half somewhere?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,200,000 fully residential with no exemption = $22,000 (worked in §3). For a Park leasehold transfer, confirm PTT treatment with the conveyancer first. (§3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Is bus service year-round?** No. Route 56 Cultus Lake serves the Park in summer months only, so year-round living here means driving. (§5.)
- **Q: Which school is at the lake, and is it my catchment?** Cultus Lake Swílhcha Community School (K–5) is the only school at the lake; catchment is address-based — see the single sampled locator result in §5. (§5.)
