## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zones permitting a duplex / two-unit form (Surrey).** Zoning By-law No. 12000 governs parcel by parcel; the zone table carries duplex minimum lot areas (R3: 557 m² for a duplex; R5: 225 m² for a duplex, per the siblings' bylaw cites in §2), and the R6 zone is the former RF-SD semi-detached form renamed Jul 8, 2024. Confirm the parcel's current zone and designation in COSMOS / the consolidated bylaw before quoting duplex potential. (Siblings in §2; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000)
- **SSMUH position.** Surrey's zoning allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (SSMUH framework adopted Jul 8, 2024). A duplex is the baseline two-unit form inside that framework. The SSMUH changes do not retroactively legalize a suite or coach house built without permits. (Research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex (Surrey rule).** One secondary suite is permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit**. Coach houses / garden suites: all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow one; 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house; one extra parking space is required. (Research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites)
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the Zoning 12000 minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing / frame items.** Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED; STR licence $358/yr carried with the City's 2024 $350/yr proposal disclosed; streamside setbacks in Part 7A of Zoning 12000, vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure; Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED); Surrey property taxes due July 2, 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2. (Brief chain Surrey frame; research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy "half" of an East Newton duplex?** Only if the pair was legally split — either a 2-lot strata plan (each half a strata lot, Form B diligence) or a fee-simple subdivision (each half on its own lot). An undivided duplex sells whole, building and lot together. No named split pair is verified for East Newton in this file — verify the title structure on the specific property. (§2.)
- **Q: Does East Newton actually have duplex zoning?** Yes, as a form class: the zone table the siblings cite carries duplex minimum lot areas (R3 at 557 m² for a duplex; R5 at 225 m² for a duplex), and the R6 zone is the former semi-detached (RF-SD) form renamed Jul 8, 2024. That is a zoning permission, not a roster — confirm the parcel's zone in COSMOS before relying on it. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: What is "Surrey Half Duplexes" in Paragon?** The Paragon Surrey half-duplex grouping (Jared's ruling, Oct 21, 2026) — the board grouping for half-duplex searches in Surrey. It is a search grouping, not a verification that any given East Newton card is a legally split half. (§2.)
- **Q: Are Crispen Bays or Crestway Bays duplexes?** No. Both are manufactured-home park TENURE RECORDS placed in East Newton by MLS listings in the mobile-homes sibling (7790 and 8220 King George Blvd), sourced only to a park directory and recorded as all-ages — never verified stock, and a park home is a single unit, not a duplex. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a duplex unit in Surrey have a secondary suite?** One suite per duplex dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite. An existing unpermitted suite is not legalized by the SSMUH changes — check permit status. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price as its own transfer, on the standard tiers — e.g., $800,000 → $14,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000, fully residential with no exemption. (§3.)
