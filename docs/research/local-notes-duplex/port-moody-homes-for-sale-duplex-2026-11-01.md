## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning / SSMUH (Port Moody frame).** Zoning Bylaw, 2018, No. 2937 puts single detached homes in RS zones and multi-residential homes in RT and RM zones, with mixed-use development in TC and CRM zones at the centre; small-scale multi-unit housing, including coach houses and accessory dwelling units, is permitted in RS and RT zones outside the transit-oriented areas, and the transit-oriented areas are exempt from the SSMUH rules. In June 2024 Council approved Zoning Bylaw Amendment No. 94, 2024, No. 3460 to comply with Bill 44: up to four dwelling units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², and up to six units on lots in that size band within 400 m of frequent bus stops (15 minutes or less), with minimum parking eliminated in the six-unit case. A Guide to SSMUH was adopted July 22, 2025. Watercourse lots need a riparian assessment and Zoning Bylaw Section 5.4 setbacks, and environmentally sensitive and steep-slope development permit areas can apply on the hillsides. The duplex is the baseline two-unit form inside this framework; SSMUH does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Research file above; https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/, read 2026-11-01; https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/, read 2026-11-01; 55-plus sibling above.)

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

- **Q: What is the difference between buying a duplex and buying a half duplex in Port Moody?** A duplex purchase is the whole building and lot on one title. A half duplex is one unit sold separately, which is only possible where the pair was strata-divided (a 2-lot strata plan — Form B diligence) or the parcel was subdivided fee-simple (party-wall title). - **Q: How many units can a Port Moody lot hold under SSMUH?** Under the 2024 Amendment No. 3460: up to four dwelling units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², and up to six on lots in that band within 400 m of frequent bus stops — with SSMUH (including coach houses and accessory dwelling units) permitted in RS and RT zones outside the transit-oriented areas, which are exempt. Confirm the specific lot against the current consolidated zoning bylaw. (§3.)
