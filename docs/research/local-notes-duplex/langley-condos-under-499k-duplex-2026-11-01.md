## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

- **SSMUH positions (each municipality's own, never blended).** Township: Bylaw 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024) — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m²; NO 6-plex tier in the Township. - **Secondary suites (each city's own rule, never borrowed — carried as investor context, as the research carries it).** City of Langley: under Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300, a secondary suite may not exceed the **lesser of 100 m² and 40%** of the building's gross floor area, the registered owner must reside on the lot (s.2.4), and a carriage/garden ground-floor unit is capped at 100 m². Township: under Bylaw 2500, s.102, a suite caps at **120 m²**, one per principal unit; a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata. - **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), and in its general diligence to every strata purchase in this band.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds.
