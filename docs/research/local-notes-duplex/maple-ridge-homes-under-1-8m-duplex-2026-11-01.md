## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Duplex / two-unit form under City of Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 on the zones that allow it; houses sit under RS-1 (s.605 minimums above). The OCP citation is CONTESTED (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024) — flagged here, resolved nowhere; no OCP number is asserted as current in this file. The Town Centre Area Plan (OCP s.10.4) update and Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024 (Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station TOAs) are carried as the research states them. The Lougheed Transit Corridor was ADOPTED Jan 27, 2026 (OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021) and ends at 221 Street — it does not extend east into East Central's Fraserview Village cluster, and it does not reach Whonnock. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway, read 2026-11-01.
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Maple Ridge's own rule.** One secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, capped at **40% of gross floor area**, and **not within the Floodplain Area** — Maple Ridge's rule, never borrowed. On floodplain properties Zoning Bylaw s.402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites). https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites, read 2026-11-01.
- **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) seen through any feed.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing and hazard checks.** Servicing (Subdivision Servicing) Bylaw 4800-1993, from a City source in the verified ledger. Floodplain: along the Fraser River edge the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies floodplain construction, and no habitable floor space may sit below that level on affected lots; lots within 300 m of the Fraser River Escarpment crest (east boundary 224 Street) require a geotechnical report on a Section 219 covenant before a building permit. ALR status on the city's rural edges is parcel-specific on ALC mapping. Maple Ridge property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026. (Research file above; https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0, read 2026-11-01; https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments, read 2026-11-01.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; **$3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = $93,000 including the further 2% tier** — $93,000 governs at $3.5M. Band anchors: only the 1% and 2% tiers arise under $1.8M — at the $525,000 attached-median input, $8,500 gross ($500 net for a qualifying first-time buyer); at $1,799,000, $33,980. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Band tax facts from the research:** the first time home buyers' program full exemption requires fair market value of $835,000 or less, exempting the tax on the first $500,000, with a partial exemption only above $835,000 and below $860,000 — so it bears only on the lowest-priced end of this band; the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and eliminated at $1,150,000 per the siblings. (Research file above; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers, read 2026-11-01.)
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
