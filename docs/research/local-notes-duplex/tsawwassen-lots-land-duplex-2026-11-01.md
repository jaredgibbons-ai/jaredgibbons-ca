## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**TOPIC — NO ROSTER at the top: this file names no duplex stock as its own and takes no count.** A vacant lot is not duplex stock; it is the ground a duplex could be built on. The duplex-relevant content of this topic is the two routes by which duplex-form housing arises on Tsawwassen land, stated as a FORM CLASS with its basis:

- **Route 1 — build the two-unit form on one lot.** Inside the residential neighbourhoods, lots sit under the RS and RD zones; the RD zones are Delta's duplex zones, and Delta's SSMUH updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots. Added-unit permission is NOT subdivision permission: it creates units on the one lot, not new titles, and it does not waive a hazard or streamside permit area on the parcel. (Research file above; https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf, read 2026-11-01; http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Route 2 — subdivide, then title separately.** New urban lots are created by subdivision against the parcel's zone minimum — RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² (or 460 m² outside the Single Family Residential designation), RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m², RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m² — and a fee-simple half duplex exists only where a parcel was actually subdivided into separately titled halves. No such named subdivision is documented for this topic, and none is invented here. On the bluff shore, the RS3 zone sets a minimum subdivision lot area of 4,000 m², lot width 30 m and average lot depth 30 m — the estate-scale standard fitting English Bluff and Cliff Drive. (Research file above; https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf, read 2026-11-01.)
- **The farm side does not produce duplexes on this record.** In the A1 Agriculture zone, subdivision is permitted only where it complies with the OCP and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, with an 8 ha minimum lot size (home site severance exception under ALC policy); A2 also sets 8 ha and A3 does not permit subdivision. Subdivision of ALR land requires ALC permission apart from the limited General Regulation cases. Housing on reserve land runs on the ALC caps in §3 — a farm-residence regime, not a duplex market. (Research file above; https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf, read 2026-11-01; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Tenure caution carried from the siblings:** urban and farm parcels are overwhelmingly freehold, and bare-land strata lots exist in newer Delta communities, but no Tsawwassen-wide freehold versus bare-land strata split is published on an official source — UNVERIFIED. A bare-land strata lot is a strata lot (Form B, fees, bylaws), not a fee-simple lot; read tenure off title per parcel. Delta's bare-land strata / leasehold records elsewhere in the program (South Pointe; Boardwalk + Tsawwassen Landing TFN/leasehold, where met) are excluded-tenure records, never duplex stock. (Research file above, §5; program positions, D1/D9 briefs.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Delta frame for the topic).** Delta's current OCP is Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules (farm land designated "Agriculture (A)" at s.1.5.1, applied on Map 2, with the Agriculture policies at s.2.6 informed by the City's 2023 Agricultural Plan). The RD zones are Delta's duplex zones, assigned parcel by parcel on the zoning maps. **ZONING NUMBER CONTESTED:** this slug's research cites "Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600" flatly throughout (zone sheets, A1 sheet, bylaw PDF) — those citations are carried as citations, flagged here per the verified ledger ("7600, 2017" vs "8400, 2025"), and the number is asserted NOWHERE in this file. (Research file above; https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf, read 2026-11-01.)
- **SSMUH position.** Delta's SSMUH zoning amendments (Bylaws 8394 + 8395) allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots. A duplex is the baseline two-unit form; SSMUH does not by itself split a duplex into separately titled halves. (Research file above; http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Delta's own rule as its sources state it.** No Delta percentage or size cap for a suite inside an urban duplex is stated in this slug's research, so no figure is borrowed from another city and the cap is UNCONFIRMED here. On ALR land the suite rule is the ALC's: a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. (Research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/, read 2026-11-01; program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Housing on reserve / farm land (the farm-side residence frame).** A principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on larger parcels, where Delta bylaws also permit it. Delta's A1 zone provides one farm house per lot, a 500 m² farm house maximum, a 3,600 m² farm home plate maximum, one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit of maximum 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha). A specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. (Research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/, read 2026-11-01; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Hazard and streamside permit areas — priced before any duplex design.** SD2 "English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment)" (OCP Appendix B, hazardous-conditions category) covers the west shore — mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence; development affected by hazardous conditions is subject to conditions set by a suitably qualified Professional Engineer, and no building may sit within 7.5 m of the natural boundary of the sea. SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands) sets a 2.9 m Geodetic Survey of Canada datum floor level, a 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation and a 7.5 m tidal setback. All properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. Delta is the diking authority, operating over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure. (Research file above.)
- **Water and septic on unserviced parcels.** The provincial GWELLS registry holds recorded wells (older wells may not appear); domestic groundwater use needs no licence, while non-domestic use, including irrigation, requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act; a home not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person (Fraser Health). (Research file above; https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems, read 2026-11-01.)
- **STRATA block — applies where a duplex is created as a 2-lot strata plan on a lot.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process, against the current zoning minimums in §2; no finer process detail is stated. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **Servicing.** Delta's Subdivision and Development Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024: final adoption is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — no servicing number is asserted for Delta in this file. (Verified ledger, brief chain.)
- **Property taxes (Delta frame).** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. (Research file above; https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes, read 2026-11-01.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier** — $93,000 governs at $3.5M. PTT applies to the land purchase itself; at typical Tsawwassen lot prices the purchase is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers. GST treatment of a specific lot sale depends on the seller and the lot's history — the research expressly leaves the live page's GST summary UNVERIFIED, so confirm with a CPA per transaction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. The rebates attach to a qualifying new home built on the lot, not to raw land as such; a resale duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does this topic file list Tsawwassen duplex lots for sale?** No. It is a topic file with no roster; a lot's duplex possibility is read off its zone, its permit areas and its title — not off the feed card. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Tsawwassen lot into two half-duplex lots?** Only against the parcel's zone minimum (urban minimums run 335 m² in RS7 to 836 m² in RD1, and 4,000 m² in RS3) plus subdivision approval — and on farm land, an 8 ha A1 minimum with ALC permission where the reserve applies; A3 land cannot be subdivided under its zone. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Does the four-unit permission mean I can sell the units separately?** No. SSMUH added units sit on the one lot under one title unless the project is strata-divided or the parcel subdivided — permission to build is not permission to title separately. (§2; §3.)
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same as a fee-simple half-duplex lot?** No. A bare-land strata lot is a strata lot with Form B, fees and bylaws; a fee-simple half sits on its own subdivided lot with no strata. Read the tenure off title. (§2.)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a $700,000 lot?** PTT of $12,000 with no exemption ($2,000 at 1% + $10,000 at 2% — the tiers in §3). GST depends on the seller and the lot's history and is UNCONFIRMED for any specific lot from these sources — confirm with a CPA. (§3.)
- **Q: What extra rules apply to a bluff or beach lot?** SD2 on the west shore (Professional Engineer conditions; 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of the sea) and SD3 on Boundary Bay (2.9 m datum floor level, 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation, 7.5 m tidal setback), plus the streamside permit area within 30 m of any stream, slough or ditch. (§3.)
- **Q: How do I check whether a lot is in the ALR?** On the Agricultural Land Commission's maps, by PID or civic address — reserve status controls the 500 m² principal-residence cap, the additional-residence limits, and whether subdivision needs Commission permission. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Are there market statistics for Tsawwassen land or its duplexes?** No figure is stated in this file: SnapStats does not cover South Delta and publishes no vacant-land category and no duplex category. (§1.)
