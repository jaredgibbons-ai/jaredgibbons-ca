## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted (Township Zoning Bylaw 2500).** The research reads Bylaw 2500 Section 110.1 minimums as stated in Township council reports — SR-1 3,716 m² / 40 m frontage; SR-2 8,094 m² / 45 m; SR-3 1,765 m² / 27.45 m; R-1E 930 m² / 22 m; R-1D 650 m² / 18.25 m; R-1B and R-1C 464.5 m² / 15.25 m — and names RU-2, C-2 and CD-192 for Aldergrove parcels with their minimums UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section. The duplex / two-unit form sits in the zones Bylaw 2500 permits it in; the zone for a specific lot is read off the current bylaw text for that lot. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **SSMUH position (Township Bylaw 6020).** Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) amended Bylaw 2500 to enable 3–4 units on most lots previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings (an estimated 55% of such lots Township-wide): 3 units ≤280 m² lot area basis / 4 units to 4,050 m² per the settled Township frame. **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020** — any page claiming a Township 6-plex tier is wrong. Duplexes are the baseline form within that frame. (Townhomes + condos siblings §3; settled Township frame, D19 brief.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — the Township's own rule: 120 m² cap.** The Township suite cap is 120 m² — this municipality's own figure, never Surrey's 40% or any other city's. A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata. (Settled Township frame, D19 brief.)
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**, stated precisely. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the Approving Officer process; the R-CL s.403 figures in the settled frame (220 m² min lot / 464.5 m² max lot area — "355 m²" is NOWHERE in the bylaw and is never carried) govern only where that zone applies. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the titles — read them. (Program discipline, D1 brief; settled Township frame.)
- **Servicing.** Township servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED in the verified ledger. (D19 frame.)
- **ALR note:** much of the Township's land is in the Agricultural Land Reserve; check a parcel's ALR designation and watercourses in GeoSource before assuming a rural lot can be built on or split. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is "Aldergrove Langley" a different place from Aldergrove?** No — it is the missing-areas-list variant for the same Township of Langley community. The official name is Aldergrove; no separate official area was confirmed. (§1.)
- **Q: Is there a named duplex complex in Aldergrove?** No — none is verified by name. The duplex picture is the form class: individual two-unit / paired homes on single lots, plus unnamed fourplexes on 30 Avenue west of 272 Street in the core plan. (§2.)
- **Q: What is Pioneer Park?** A 55+ manufactured-home co-operative at 27111 0 Avenue — you buy co-op shares and pay a monthly co-op fee, not pad rent or strata fees. It is excluded-tenure stock, counted nowhere as duplex, townhouse or strata. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I build 6 units on an Aldergrove lot under SSMUH?** No — Township Bylaw 6020 has NO 6-plex tier; it enables 3–4 units on most former single-family / duplex lots. (§3.)
- **Q: How big can a suite be inside a Township duplex?** The Township cap is 120 m². A coach house is a separate fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata. (§3.)
- **Q: If I buy half of a strata duplex, what diligence applies?** Form B diligence in full — and a 2-lot strata is below the 5-lot depreciation-report threshold, so ask what reports actually exist. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Is the Langley median an Aldergrove price?** No — SnapStats publishes Langley community medians only ($1,345,000 detached / $611,000 attached, September 2026); Aldergrove sub-area medians are not published and are UNVERIFIED. (§1.)
- **Q: Is land south of the core farm land?** Much of it is ALR — check the parcel in GeoSource before pricing in a build or a split. (§3.)
