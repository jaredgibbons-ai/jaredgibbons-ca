## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Plan, zoning, SSMUH and suites (Surrey frame):** the current Surrey plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is replaced and is NEVER cited as current. Zoning Bylaw 12000 governs. SSMUH in force Jul 8, 2024: a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — duplexes are the baseline SSMUH form. A secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area** — Surrey's own cap, never borrowed; the City defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit within a single family, semi-detached or duplex building, not a separate strata lot, one suite per dwelling unit, and never within a coach house or garden suite. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is ever stated. (Research file, §4 frame; townhomes sibling, suite line: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/hyde-park-south-surrey-townhomes-2026-11-01.md; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000.)

- **STRATA block — applies to Hyde Park and Hyde Park Corner as strata townhome complexes, and to any strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan).** Form B diligence applies in full: the research's own buying guidance names the Form B information certificate, the strata bylaws and rules, the last two years of minutes and the depreciation report, reviewed before subjects are removed — and establishing WHICH strata the unit is in comes first, because the two complexes' fees, bylaws, reports and minutes differ. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. (Hyde Park's stated 156 homes and Hyde Park Corner's stated 16 homes each sit over the threshold on the page's own counts.) Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 — the live page states the same position: since the November 24, 2022 Strata Property Act amendment a strata corporation can no longer restrict long-term rentals, while bylaws limiting short-term rentals can still apply. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts — and neither complex here is a 55+ complex in any case. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws (research file, §§4, 6; https://jaredgibbons.ca/hyde-park-south-surrey.html.)
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval through the City's Approving Officer process; no finer process detail is stated because no official source opened for this slug states it. Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Hyde Park and Hyde Park Corner units are completed resales — the newly built exemption does not apply to these resales, as the townhomes sibling states. Have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill from the current Province pages before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. GST applies to new construction, not resales — Hyde Park (per the condos sibling, 2016) and Hyde Park Corner (2021) are completed resale stock, so a resale carries no GST and is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
