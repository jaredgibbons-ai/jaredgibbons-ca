## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). Ordinary individual duplexes on single lots, built under the zoning of their day, are stated as a FORM CLASS — no invented roster, no listing addresses. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted — two different answers on one ground.** Inside Southlands: the RA-1 District Schedule governs, permitting one-family dwellings and, in specific circumstances, infill one-family dwellings — no duplex permission is stated in the district's own framework as the research carries it, and none is asserted here. On the conventional edge streets: the R1-1 district (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575) sets a minimum site area of 306 m² for a single detached house or a duplex and for a 3–4-unit multiple dwelling, 464 m² for 5 units and 557 m² for 6 to 8 units, with up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units. Verify the lot's district schedule before assuming multiplex or subdivision value — the specific lot's schedule is UNCONFIRMED here beyond that read. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-southlands.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **SSMUH position.** Vancouver's R1-1 multiplex framework (By-law No. 13817) is the city's small-scale multi-unit housing implementation on former single-detached lots, with the duplex as the baseline form — **and it does not reach RA-1 Southlands lots**, which are governed by their own district schedule and must be checked individually. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf (Research file §4.)
- **Secondary suites — Vancouver's own position only.** The R1-1 schedule permits a secondary suite (and, for a house, a laneway house) as the schedule states it, on R1-1 ground only; no suite position is asserted for an RA-1 lot beyond its own district schedule, and no suite size cap from any other city is borrowed. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Flood — a live rule on this ground.** The City's Flood Plain Standards and Requirements (adopted September 16, 2014, effective January 1, 2015, amended March 7, 2023) subject lands in the designated floodplain — which includes the Fraser River coastal floodplain — to flood construction level (FCL) and setback requirements under the Vancouver Building By-law, and the Standards contain a dedicated section (Section 6.0) for the RA-1 District. The City publishes the designated floodplain as an open dataset covering the coastal and Still Creek floodplains; confirm the specific lot against that dataset and look for registered flood covenants on title before subject removal. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/guidelines-flood-plain-standards-requirements.pdf and https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **STRATA block — applies to any strata duplex / strata half duplex (and, in their own forms, to Gulf View Court and the Yewbrook Place bare-land strata).** Form B diligence applies in full to a strata purchase. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW the 5-lot threshold, and that cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**, stated precisely. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — the 55-plus sibling verifies no age-restricted stock of any form in S.W. Marine; a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** Vancouver's Subdivision By-law No. 5208 is VERIFIED in the program ledger (fees under By-law No. 14538): a fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval under that bylaw — and on RA-1 ground, the district's one-dwelling framework and any ALR boundary position govern first, so no split is priced here without a zoning and ALR confirmation for the parcel. Party-wall obligations rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the titles — read them. (Program discipline, D1 brief; verified ledger; research file §6.)
- **Taxes and levies (citywide Vancouver).** 2026 advance property taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances. The City's Empty Homes Tax charges 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed empty — **3%, VANCOUVER ONLY** — with the annual declaration due February 3, 2026 and payment due April 16, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — at S.W. Marine estate price points that further 2% tier can be a live item. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a duplex or half duplex in S.W. Marine?** No verified one exists in any file for this ground. Inside Southlands the RA-1 one-dwelling framework prevents the form; on the edge streets a duplex would be an individual R1-1 home, and no instance is verified by name. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Yewbrook Place a half-duplex buy?** No — it is a bare-land strata of individual single lots whose houses are described as semi-detached/duplex-like in a single published profile; it is counted nowhere in the program, its board placement is UNCONFIRMED, and a purchase there is a bare-land strata purchase with Form B diligence, not a half-duplex purchase. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I split a big Southlands lot into two halves?** Do not price it that way: RA-1 permits one dwelling per lot with only limited infill in specific circumstances, the lot may sit inside the ALR boundary (applied over the area in 1973), and any subdivision runs under By-law No. 5208 — zoning and ALR confirmation for the parcel comes first. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I keep horses here?** Southlands is the one part of Vancouver planned for it — the RA-1 district was created in 1955 to maintain the area's "semi-rural, equestrian and limited agricultural nature" — but permitted use is property-specific and the ALR may govern the parcel; confirm both before relying on equestrian use. (§3; research §7.)
- **Q: Is flooding a real diligence item on the riverfront streets?** Yes, and it is regulated, not informal: FCL and setback requirements apply in the designated floodplain, the Standards carry a dedicated RA-1 section, and registered flood covenants may sit on title. Check the lot against the City's floodplain dataset before subject removal. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need depreciation reports?** Not on lot count: the 5-year cycle captures stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, and a 2-lot strata is below that threshold — a strata-half buyer needs their own building diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On its own purchase price, on the standard tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000; at estate prices, $3,500,000 = $93,000 including the further 2% tier (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Is a home near the Musqueam boundary the same kind of purchase?** No — City of Vancouver freehold, UEL ground and Musqueam reserve leasehold are three different purchase structures within a few blocks; they finance, insure and resell differently, and the tenure belongs in the first line of any listing conversation. (§1; research §6.)
- **Q: Is there an S.W. Marine market median I can rely on?** No — SnapStats does not cover Vancouver and no substitute figure is used in this program. (§1.)
