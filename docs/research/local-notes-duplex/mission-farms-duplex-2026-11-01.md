## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

**FARM GROUND — FORM CLASS ONLY at the top: no named duplex stock of any form is verified on this ground:**

- **Why the strata form is closed here:** on ALR land a residence cannot be stratified — ALR residences cannot be stratified per this slug's acreages sibling (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)), so a strata half duplex is not this ground's farm form. Farm parcels here are overwhelmingly freehold, and strata farm product is not a Mission pattern — no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source in the research. (Acreages sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/acreages/mission-farms-acreages-2026-11-01.md; research file above, §5.)
- **The farm-residence form — which is NOT duplex stock.** The Agricultural Land Commission generally allows no more than one residence per ALR parcel; a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; and one additional detached residence is permitted up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha — anything beyond that needs ALC approval, and City of Mission bylaws must also permit it. A principal home plus a lawful additional residence is a two-residence FARM PARCEL, sold whole under one title — not a duplex, and neither residence is a separately titled half. (Research file above, §4/§6; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **Second residences / manufactured homes on farm ground — ONLY as the ALR sources support:** a manufactured home can figure on farm ground only as the parcel's principal or additional residence within those same ALC caps and conditions — the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation s.34(3) framework as administered under ALC Policy L-25 (additional manufactured-home residence), with the Non-Adhering Residential Use (NARU) application route where the as-of-right conditions are not met. **A general farm-worker housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED** — no source in this slug's research grants a stand-alone entitlement, and none is asserted (the poultry-farms treatment in this program is the model for this restraint). (Settled ALR position, D8 brief; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)

It creates no duplex, no strata and no separable half on an ALR farm parcel, where the ALC caps above govern. (Townhomes sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/mission-farms-townhomes-2026-11-01.md; https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh)

- **Farm classification (a value item on this ground, not a duplex rule).** A producing farm must meet BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income at farm gate prices — $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if larger; $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — and farm applications must be submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year. (Research file above, §4; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. Note for farm parcels: the worked examples are the fully-residential program anchors; a mixed farm parcel's exact residential-portion split is facts-specific and UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate, and the rebate does not apply to a farm parcel itself. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy half a duplex on a Mission farm?** No verified half duplex of either legal form exists on this ground — and on ALR land the strata form is closed, because ALR residences cannot be stratified. A farm purchase here is a whole parcel under one title. (§2.)
- **Q: Is a farm house with a suite and a second small house a duplex?** No. Those are ALR farm-residence forms on one parcel under one title (§2/§3) — the parcel sells whole.
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Mission?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. (§2.)
- **Q: Can a Mission farm have a second dwelling?** Only within the ALR additional-residence framework: one additional detached residence up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or 186 m² on larger parcels, where the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 conditions are met (or through a Non-Adhering Residential Use application), and City bylaws must also permit it. A general farm-worker entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. (§2.)
- **Q: What diligence is different on a strata half duplex?** Form B diligence on the 2-lot strata: bylaws, rules, financials, insurance, and any age bylaw read against the settled SPA position (only 55+ valid). No such strata is verified on this farm ground — the block in §3 is stated for completeness. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: How is PTT calculated on a half duplex?** On the half's own price, on the standard tiers — $1,500,000 fully residential with no exemption = $28,000; $2,000,000 = $38,000 (worked in §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Who maintains the shared wall on a fee-simple half?** Whatever the registered party-wall agreement / easement on the two titles says. No agreement on title = UNCONFIRMED obligations — get legal advice before removing subjects. (§3.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me split a farm parcel into two titles?** No — SSMUH is an urban-lot framework; on ALR land the ALC position governs first, subdivision needs ALC approval, and stratification of an ALR residence is barred. (§3.)
- **Q: Are farm prices published for Mission?** No — SnapStats publishes no farm category and no duplex category; the §1 Mission residential rows are community context only, and farm-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. (§1.)
