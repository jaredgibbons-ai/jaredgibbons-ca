## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Where duplexes are permitted.** Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs, under OCP Bylaw 5500 "Chilliwack 2050" with the Schedule B2 Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan for this ground. The zone of a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until read. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **SSMUH position.** Chilliwack's SSMUH bylaw is Bylaw 5395 (adopted Jun 18, 2024) — 3 to 4 units on eligible single-detached/duplex parcels inside the urban growth boundary (staff-report description). Duplexes are the baseline two-unit form in that framework. (Research file above, §4.)
- **Secondary suites inside a duplex — Chilliwack's own rule, never borrowed.** Under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 (ss. 2, 4.06, 5.04, confirmed in rules-gaps item 7f): a secondary suite is capped at the **lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling**, one accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot, garden suites are capped at 100 m², and parking rules sit at s. 5.04. Application to any specific parcel remains a per-parcel check. (Research file above, §4.)
- **STRATA block.** Where a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan) exists: Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**.
