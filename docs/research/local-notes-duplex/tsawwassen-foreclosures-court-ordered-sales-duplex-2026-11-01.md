## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **The settled court process (carried; this slug's research re-verified Rule 21-7 live and held the rest UNVERIFIED — see FILE STATUS).** A foreclosure proceeding is started by petition in the Supreme Court of BC — Rule 21-7(1); all persons whose interest is sought to be extinguished must be respondents — Rule 21-7(2); the petitioner must serve the court's informational notice, Form 125 — Rule 21-7(4.1). The court may order payment within a redemption period the court fixes (no fixed length in the Rules) — Rule 21-7(5)(b); in default, a final order of foreclosure may be granted — Rule 21-7(6); a party of record may apply at any time for an order that the property be sold — Rule 21-7(7); the person with conduct of sale may apply, even before the redemption period expires, for an order confirming the sale, directing the proceeds and vesting title in the purchaser — Rule 21-7(9). Conduct-of-sale mechanics, reserve / upset price, bid rights and Form 60 certification sit in Rule 13-5 and Practice Direction PD-66 on the settled treatment. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03

- **PTT — general worked examples with inputs + tier math (general frame only).** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only, kept distinct from the UNCONFIRMED court-sale GST position above. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a list of Tsawwassen duplexes in foreclosure?** No standing list exists in this file — a duplex is court-ordered stock only for the life of its court file; check the live feed and the court file itself. (§2.)
- **Q: Is my accepted offer final?** No — the person with conduct of sale applies under Rule 21-7(9) for an order confirming the sale, directing the proceeds and vesting title, and that application can be made even before the redemption period expires; until the order is made the sale is not final. (§3.) https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: Can someone outbid my accepted offer?** On the settled treatment, bid / offer rights sit in the court's Rule 13-5 directions and PD-66 for the specific file — treat a competing bid at the approval stage as a real possibility, and get the seller's counsel's bid instructions for the specific file in writing before spending on inspections. This slug's own research held the bid-rights wording UNVERIFIED in its pass. (§3; research file.)
- **Q: How long is the redemption period?** There is no fixed length in the Rules — it is the period the court fixes in its order under Rule 21-7(5)(b). Ask which stage the file is at before spending money. (§3.)
- **Q: Will I get a Form B for a strata half duplex in a court sale?** UNCONFIRMED as a general promise — see the §3 strata block caveat; confirm what the specific file's listing documents actually produce. (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT and GST do I pay on a court-ordered duplex?** UNCONFIRMED for a court sale specifically — have your lawyer cost both on the specific property before setting a ceiling; the §3 worked examples are the general frame only. (§3.)
- **Q: How do I check the court file myself?** Court Services Online eSearch is open to anyone with Internet access; Supreme Court civil file details include the file number, registry, party names and filed documents, and foreclosure files are class "H - Foreclosure." Delta has no registry of its own and the registry mapping is UNVERIFIED — the file's own registry on CSO is the check. (§1; §3.) https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no; a valid 55+ age bylaw can still bind. (§3.)
