## 2. THE DUPLEX STOCK

**Definitions, stated plainly.** A DUPLEX is one building containing two dwelling units, on one lot under one title unless it is strata-divided. A HALF DUPLEX is one of the two units sold separately — in BC that happens in only two legal forms: **(a) a STRATA duplex**, where the pair is a 2-lot strata plan, each half is a strata lot, and Form B diligence applies; or **(b) a FEE-SIMPLE half duplex**, where the parcel itself was subdivided so each half sits on its own fee-simple lot (the party-wall / fee-simple row form, where a source documents it). A duplex that was never strata-divided or subdivided cannot be bought "by the half" — the whole building and lot sell together. (Program definition, settled in `~/workspace/local-notes/duplex-D1-BRIEF.md`; strata form per the Province's strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT DUPLEXES

- **Zoning (Township frame).** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs; rural homes in this district sit under its rural zones (the feed's own listings name RU-2 for acreage parcels), with zone assignments for a specific parcel read from the bylaw Map Schedules through the Township's GeoSource. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/otter-district.md; https://www.tol.ca/at-your-service/bylaw-enforcement/bylaws/, read 2026-11-01.)
- **SSMUH position (Township frame).** Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — **NO 6-plex tier exists in it.** The bylaw amendments permit SSMUH in residential zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex use, but eligibility requires the property to be within the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to both municipal water and sewer, not larger than 4,050 m² and not in a zone with a minimum lot size larger than 4,050 m². Properties that do not meet those criteria — the frame most rural Otter acreages fall under — are instead eligible for one residential unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit, depending on zoning. Urban SSMUH numbers are never imported onto an Otter acreage. (Research file above; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Secondary suites (Township's own rule — never borrowed).** Suite cap **120 m²** under the Township frame. On ALR land, a secondary suite is permitted only when it is entirely contained within the principal residence — a separate or detached dwelling is not a secondary suite (§1). (Brief chain, Township frame; research file above; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Coach house (Township definition, settled in this program).** In Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s.102, a "coach house" is a **fee-simple second unit** — an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage; never strata. A ground-floor detached suite is a different accessory unit with different rules. No coach-house size cap appears in s.100 as read in the program's rules-gaps pass, so no cap is stated; the townhomes sibling flags a s.102 suite-figure conflict (120 m² permitted vs a January 2025 guide) — flagged, resolved nowhere. - **STRATA block — applies to a strata duplex / strata half duplex (2-lot strata plan), wherever one is met.** Form B diligence applies in full. Depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 — **a 2-lot strata duplex is BELOW that 5-lot threshold, so the 5+ depreciation-report cycle does not capture it on lot count alone**; a buyer still asks what reports, if any, the strata holds. Minimum contingency reserve fund (CRF) contribution 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Subdivision / party-wall facts — only as official sources support.** A fee-simple split of a duplex parcel requires subdivision approval (Approving Officer process under the Land Title Act, servicing per Bylaw 5382 above; on ALR land the ALR frame in §1 also applies). Party-wall obligations between two fee-simple halves rest on the registered easement / party-wall agreement on the two titles — read both titles. (Program discipline, D1 brief.)
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs + tier math.** BC property transfer tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $38,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. A half duplex is taxed on its own purchase price as its own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home / GST material = new HOUSING only.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA** — never March 12) applies to new housing: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. A resale duplex or half duplex is not new housing for this rebate. GST on land / new housing is carried at the CRA GI-003 general level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any duplex or half duplex I can buy in Otter District?** None verified. §2 is an honest zero — zero verified condos, zero townhouse complexes, zero attached board listings in September 2026, and no named strata or fee-simple half duplex on any primary source. The district's stock is freehold acreages plus one pad-rental park. (§2.)
- **Q: A listing shows two homes on one Otter acreage — is that a duplex?** Not as a title matter. It is a multi-dwelling acreage record in listing copy. On ALR land the permitted frame is one principal residence (max 500 m²), a suite only inside it, and at most one additional detached residence (90 m² at ≤40 ha; 186 m² above) without an ALC application — a property already carrying two dwellings needs its approvals history checked, not assumed. (§1, §2.)
- **Q: Is Langley Grove Estates a 55+ park or a strata I can buy land in?** Neither. It is a manufactured-home park at 3665 244 Street (also carded at 24330 Fraser Highway): listings state "no age restrictions" — expressly NOT 55+ — and record leasehold not prepaid-non-strata title. You buy the home as a chattel and rent the pad; THE TENURE LINE applies in full. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I put 3–4 SSMUH units on an Otter acreage?** Almost certainly not on the urban frame: Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024, NO 6-plex tier) requires Urban Containment Boundary location, municipal water and sewer, and a lot no larger than 4,050 m². A rural Otter lot that fails those tests falls to one unit plus a suite or accessory unit depending on zoning. (§3.)
- **Q: What is a coach house in the Township?** Under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102, a fee-simple second unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage — never strata. A ground-floor detached suite is a different accessory unit. (§3.)
- **Q: How big can a secondary suite be here?** The Township cap is 120 m² — the Township's own figure, never borrowed from another municipality. On ALR land the suite must also sit entirely inside the principal residence. (§3.)
- **Q: Does a 2-lot strata duplex need a depreciation report?** The 5-year cycle applies to stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; a 2-lot strata is below that threshold on lot count. Ask what the strata holds anyway, and run full Form B diligence. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a strata restrict my rentals?** A strata rental-restriction bylaw is VOID since November 24, 2022 — no. That voiding does not reach a manufactured-home park's rules, which sit under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. (§2, §3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on an Otter District purchase?** Run the tiers on your own price: $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier (fully residential, no exemption). A half duplex, where one exists elsewhere, is taxed on its own price as its own transfer. (§3.)
- **Q: Why does the page show 26 homes when the statistics show only 2 listings?** The sources classify differently: the feed's 26 results include Langley Grove Estates park homes as well as freehold acreages, while the SnapStats rows count 2 detached and 0 attached listings. Neither number prices the other — with zero sales in the month, price parcel by parcel off a longer window and the wider rural Langley geography. (§1.)
