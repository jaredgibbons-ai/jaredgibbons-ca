# Surrey (City-Wide) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Scope note:** this is the city-wide framing file for the City of Surrey — the definitive roster file in this series. It assembles the verified 55+ stock of the whole municipality once, each complex named once and placed by community, from the sibling Surrey files in this series (surrey-condos-under-800k, surrey-homes-under-1-8m, surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m, surrey-lots-land, surrey-mobile-homes, king-george-corridor, queen-mary-park-surrey, cloverdale-bc and the South Surrey files), built on the batch-18 plans research file (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/surrey.md). That research file is a land-use plans index file — it carries the City's plan system, the current OCP and the suite rule, but no housing stock of its own; the roster below is the sibling files' verified work, not that file's.

> **⚠️ ROSTER FLAG — PENDING RE-CONSOLIDATION, READ BEFORE USING ANY SURREY COUNT: the consolidated roster carried in this file is 15 verified 55+ strata complexes + Sullivan Mews (buyable membership, NOT strata) + 3 verified 55+ manufactured-home parks + Sea Ridge Bays (bare-land strata manufactured community, its own category). ## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The City of Surrey names seven communities: City Centre, Cloverdale, Fleetwood, Guildford, Newton, South Surrey and Whalley. A city-wide 55+ search has to be run community by community — there is no single Surrey 55+ district. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- Where the verified 55+ ownership stock sits, by community: **South Surrey** — six strata complexes on the 152 Street / 144 Street corridor (Sunnyside Park, King George Corridor, Elgin Chantrell), plus the Sea Ridge Bays bare-land strata on 20 Avenue; **Newton** — six strata complexes (Chelsea Gardens and Charleston Green in East Newton, Glenwood Village and Lakeview Estates in West Newton, Lakewood Heights and Lakewood Place in Panorama Ridge), plus the Sullivan Mews membership complex in East Newton and two of the three verified parks; **Cloverdale** — two strata complexes (Carriage House downtown, Manor Ridge on 192 Street); **Fleetwood** — one strata condo building (Fleetwood Gardens) and one park (Westwood Court). **Guildford, City Centre and Whalley:** no complex in the consolidated roster — Whalley's one verified complex, Camberley Green, sits in the re-consolidation queue in Section 2, and the SkyTrain-corridor condo stock in Guildford and City Centre is general-market in the listings checked. Sources: Section 2 below.
- Board geography is not municipal geography: for market figures, "Surrey" as a SnapStats board community does NOT include the separate board communities Cloverdale or S Surrey White Rock even though both sit inside the municipal boundary — the building list in this file is municipal (the whole city); board-community figures are not interchangeable with it (surrey-condos-under-800k file, scope note). Two settled board positions carried through this series: Serpentine IS a Fraser Valley board sub-area (owner ruling, Nov 1, 2026), on the South Surrey side; and Surrey City Centre is NOT a board name — the land is the Whalley sub-area, while "City Centre" remains the City's own planning name for its downtown. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/surrey.md
- White Rock is a different city, with its own bylaws (Zoning Bylaw 2024 No. 2506 / OCP Bylaw 2017 No. 2220) — no White Rock complex is in this file's roster. The border cuts the other way too: at least two residences with "White Rock" in their name or marketing — Amica White Rock, 15333 16 Ave, and Whitecliff, 15501 16 Ave — are at Surrey civic addresses and are carried in Section 2 as Surrey care stock. Sources: Section 2 below.
- The plan stack a Surrey parcel can sit under, from the City's structure as read in the batch-18 research: the Official Community Plan (Surrey 2050, Bylaw No. 21900 — Section 3), a General Land Use Plan where one exists, a Neighbourhood Concept Plan or Local Area Plan for the neighbourhood, Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, and any Development Permit Areas — each layer checked separately for the parcel. The City organizes its approved secondary plans by community and describes them as developed through community consultation and endorsed by Council. https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/surrey.md
- School District 36 (Surrey) serves the whole city; catchments are address-based through the SD36 School Locator, and no SD36 locator result for a specific 55+ complex address was recorded in this program, so the catchment for any specific building is UNVERIFIED.
- Health care: Surrey Memorial Hospital, 13750 96 Ave (City Centre), serves most of the city; Peace Arch Hospital, 15521 Russell Avenue, White Rock, serves South Surrey; the Surrey-Newton Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 6830 King George Blvd. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Surrey/surrey-newton-urgent-and-primary-care-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/white-rock/peace-arch-hospital
- Transit spine: SkyTrain (Expo Line) serves City Centre / Whalley only; the Surrey-Langley SkyTrain is a 16-kilometre extension along Fraser Highway from King George Station to 203 Street in Langley City with an anticipated in-service date of 2029; the R1 RapidBus runs on King George Boulevard through Newton to South Surrey. None of the verified 55+ complexes in the roster is on a SkyTrain line today. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/surrey-langley-skytrain

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the "rentals not allowed" lines in older directory records for roster complexes (Semiahmoo Estates, Manor Ridge, Braemore at Carrington) cannot be read at face value. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on a different statute.** Pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) — the law that applies where a tenant rents a pad site but owns the manufactured home. Age rules in a park are park rules/policies, not strata bylaws — get them in writing from the park, with the tenancy agreement. Surrey's own policy brochure states the property owner must give 12 months' notice of a final moving date using a Notice to End Tenancy, which can only be given after the owner receives all their permits, and the City's Manufactured Home Park Policy requires an affordable housing program for residents when a park is redeveloped. Sea Ridge Bays is the exception that proves the split: it is a bare-land strata, so strata law — not the MHPTA — governs it. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- **Membership complexes are neither strata nor freehold purchase in the usual sense.** Sullivan Mews' own listings state it is a membership, not a strata, and that mortgages are not possible — financing, resale and transfer rules come from the membership documents, not the Strata Property Act. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure
- **OCP: "Surrey 2050" Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900** is the current OCP — adopted June 15, 2026, replacing OCP Bylaw No. 18020 (cited here only as the replaced bylaw, never as current). The batch-18 research file records that the Surrey 2050 update rescinded East Newton North's plan and superseded the land use designations of East Newton South, the East Newton Business Park and West Cloverdale, with the OCP's designations now guiding land use in those areas while the older NCPs continue to guide amenities and infrastructure — a seller quoting an old NCP designation may be quoting a document no longer in effect. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/surrey.md
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000**, as amended for small-scale multi-unit housing on July 8, 2024, when residential zones R1 to R7 replaced the RF, RH and RQ zones (RF-12/RF-12C became R4; RF-SD became R6): up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. This affects detached streets around the complexes, not the strata / membership / park lots inside them. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/surrey.md
- **Secondary suites (settled position):** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite, and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area. A "90 m² cap" is NOT current and is never stated as current in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Short-term rentals in Surrey:** under 90 consecutive days per patron counts as short-term; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required before operating — strata bylaws are a separate, building-level check on top. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. This is the City of Surrey regime — it does not apply to unincorporated FVRD ground elsewhere in this batch. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **For strata purchases,** the Form B (Information Certificate), the bylaws and rules, minutes, budget and depreciation report are where a complex's age bylaw is actually proved — for the listing-sourced queue complexes in Section 2 (Ocean Park Place, Ocean Park Grove, The Carrington), that Form B check is the step that converts listing-stated 55+ into a verified bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many 55+ complexes are there in Surrey, in total?** On the consolidated roster carried in this series: 15 verified 55+ strata complexes, plus Sullivan Mews (membership, not strata), plus 3 verified 55+ manufactured-home parks, plus Sea Ridge Bays as a bare-land strata in its own category. Five more verified or partly-verified complexes — Camberley Green, The Greens at Douglas, Ocean Park Place, Ocean Park Grove and the Carrington community — sit outside that roster pending re-consolidation, and Breakaway Bays is UNCONFIRMED on conflicting sources. Any single Surrey total quoted without that flag is ahead of the evidence. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Why do so few 55+ listings show up in a condos-only search?** Form, not price: ten of Surrey's fifteen verified 55+ strata complexes are townhouse complexes, which a condos-only feed excludes by type; the membership complex, the three parks and Sea Ridge Bays are also outside a strata-condo feed. The 55+ condo-form supply is five buildings: Fleetwood Gardens, Carriage House, Semiahmoo Estates, 13965 16 Ave, and Chelsea Gardens' apartment section. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55?** It varies by complex in its own words: Lakewood Heights and Peninsula Village listings state only one occupant needs to meet the age requirement, Manor Ridge listings state 1 occupant must be 55+, and a Greens at Douglas listing (queue) states one of the owners must be 55+. Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is exempt in any case — confirm the building's bylaw in its Form B. https://sothebysrealty.ca/en/property/british-columbia/region-fraser-valley/surrey-real-estate/3076580/6211-w-boundary-drive-no13/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** In a strata, ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside; the Province states under-55s can own but may not be able to live in the unit. In a park, at Sea Ridge Bays' strata, or in the membership complex, the equivalent question is answered by the park rules, the strata bylaws or the membership documents respectively — three different documents, three different answers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit, or run an Airbnb?** Long-term: yes in a strata — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022; the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption. Short-term: Surrey allows it only for an owner living at the property as their primary residence, with a $358-per-year business licence, and the strata can still restrict short-term rentals by bylaw. In a pad-rent park, rental rules are the park's own — do not import the strata position. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Q: Is Sullivan Mews a cheap way into a Surrey "condo"?** It is not a condo and not a strata: its own listings state the purchase is a membership, that mortgages are not possible, with a 55+ age restriction, no smoking, 1 small pet and a maximum of 2 occupants. Read the membership documents before pricing it against strata units. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure
- **Q: How does Sea Ridge Bays differ from the three parks?** You own the lot. Sea Ridge Bays is a bare-land strata — title recorded as Freehold Strata, listings stating "NO PAD FEES" and a monthly strata fee ($210 in the listings checked, including water) instead of pad rent — so strata law, including the 55+ bylaw and its provincial exemptions, governs instead of the MHPTA. Its listings also state "Council Approval Required," a strata-side wording a park buyer will not meet in a pad-rent park, where the equivalent step is the park owner's assignment consent. https://www.jagsidhu.com/listing/r3099331-15875-20-avenue-50-surrey-bc-v4a-2b1/brochure
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Complex by complex across the city: Carriage House listings state no pets permitted; Fleetwood Gardens records pets allowed with restrictions; Manor Ridge allows 1 pet with restrictions; Sullivan Mews allows 1 small pet; Westwood Estates allows large dogs with park approval, no vicious breeds; Ocean Park Grove's listing (queue) states 2 indoor cats and no dogs, while Ocean Park Place's current listing (queue) records no cats and no dogs. There is no Surrey-wide pet rule for 55+ housing. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/311-16068-83-ave-1729077
- **Q: Why aren't Camberley Green and the others in the count of 15?** Because the consolidated roster in this series was built from earlier passes, and those five complexes were verified — or, for the Ocean Park pair, surfaced in listing text — in later sub-area passes. They are carried in a labelled queue in Section 2, pending re-consolidation, rather than silently added to the city total or silently dropped. The same discipline holds Breakaway Bays at UNCONFIRMED, where a directory and a current listing directly disagree. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is a "White Rock" seniors residence actually in Surrey?** Sometimes. Amica White Rock (15333 16 Ave) and Whitecliff (15501 16 Ave) are at Surrey civic addresses despite the name and the border marketing, and Peace Arch Hospital itself is in White Rock. The civic address, not the brand name, decides which city's bylaws and tax regime apply — White Rock is a separate municipality with its own zoning and OCP bylaws. https://www.amica.ca/white-rock and https://www.cogirseniorliving.ca/retirement-home-south-surrey-british-columbia/whitecliff
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** In a strata, mechanically yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. In the parks and the membership complex, the resale pool is set by the park's / membership's own transfer and age rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
