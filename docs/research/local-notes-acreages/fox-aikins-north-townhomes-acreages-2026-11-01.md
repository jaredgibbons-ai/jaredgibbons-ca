## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) is CURRENT** — Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and is never cited as current. Zoning: **Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000** — the complex's own zone is UNCONFIRMED in this slug's research; confirm per parcel in COSMOS before stating one. No Township of Langley bylaw applies to this ground (geography lock, §1). https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **SSMUH (Surrey position):** zoning changes adopted Jul 8, 2024 — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. SSMUH is a fee-simple / small-lot frame; it does not re-subdivide an existing strata complex — a Fox & Aikins North strata lot's density is fixed by its strata plan and zoning, not by SSMUH. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 12000):** the City defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit whose habitable floor area does not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit**, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex building, and not a separate strata lot; only one suite is permitted per dwelling unit. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision / servicing:** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. Verified on the City's own Land Development pages in this program: the **Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw, worked through the Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. A strata lot cannot be "subdivided" by its owner — strata plan amendment is a strata-corporation and Land Title process, not a City subdivision of the lot. The Township of Langley servicing bylaw (No. 5382, 2019) does NOT apply here. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside setbacks:** Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 sets streamside setbacks **by stream class** — there is no single riparian figure for Surrey; whether any watercourse setback touches this site is UNCONFIRMED in this slug's research. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000

- **BC strata block — applies to Fox & Aikins North in full (its lot IS a strata lot):** Form B diligence; the live page itself directs buyers to the bylaws, budget and any warranty information, to confirm what the monthly strata fees cover, and to ask for the Form B, bylaws and rules, the last two years of minutes and the depreciation report before subjects are removed. - **ALR RULES IN FULL (for completeness — no ALR parcel is documented for this site):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it — owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page; no farm-class parcel is documented in this slug's research. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a SEPARATE info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. A Fox & Aikins North resale is not vacant land; whether GST applies to any specific unit turns on its new/resale status, which is a per-unit question — anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
