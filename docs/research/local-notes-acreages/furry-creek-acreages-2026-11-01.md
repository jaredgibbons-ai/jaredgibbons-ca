## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: SLRD Electoral Area D Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 1135-2013** (adopted October 28, 2013), with the Howe Sound East Sub-area Plan as Schedule C covering Furry Creek. There is no municipal OCP here — this regional-district plan is the frame. https://slrd.civicweb.net/document/149848/

- **Building schemes:** the SLRD records that the Furry Creek neighbourhood has existing statutory building schemes and covenants, which constrain what owners can build or alter — exterior changes, additions and some landscaping may need scheme approval, on fee-simple and strata lots alike. Read the scheme registered on the parcel's title before planning any change. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/250129-ssmuh-area-d-amendment-bb-extension-update-rfd
- **SSMUH — EXEMPT, as the Province and SLRD state it:** the Province has determined that the SLRD's master-planned communities of Britannia Beach, Furry Creek and Porteau Cove are NOT subject to the additional 3–6 unit small-scale multi-unit housing densities, because they are not within an urban containment boundary for the purposes of the SSMUH legislation; Amendment Bylaw No. 1887-2025 removed the extra densities that had briefly been added to the Furry Creek FCR3 and CD3 zones. Do not price any Furry Creek lot with multiplex rights — secondary suite/ADU rights are whatever the base zoning and building scheme allow. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/250129-ssmuh-area-d-amendment-bb-extension-update-rfd
