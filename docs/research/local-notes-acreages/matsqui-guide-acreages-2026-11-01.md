## 2. THE ACREAGE ITSELF

**The acreage in Matsqui Prairie is farm land: fee-simple agricultural parcels on a dyked river prairie, in the A1 Agricultural Zone (subdivision minimum 8.0 ha) and the RR Rural Residential Zone (minimum lot size 4.0 ha). This is the one ground in this group where an acreage market genuinely exists — and it trades as whole farm parcels, not as a named acreage subdivision product. No named acreage subdivision/development is verified in the research record, no presale acreage offering exists, and no parcel count, typical parcel size beyond the zone minimums, or acreage price is stated, because none is documented. Strata is a structural zero here — no strata development is a documented housing form in this sub-area, there is no bare-land strata, and ALR residences cannot be stratified in any event (§3). The count home for this ground is the matsqui-guide lots file, cross-referenced in the FILE STATUS block; this file documents the acreage position in the research file's terms and takes no count of its own.**

- **Fee-simple farm parcels are the acreage form — and nearly the whole market.** The research file's housing profile is the land profile: "the housing stock is freehold farm and rural-residential land; strata development is not a documented housing form in this rural sub-area," with single-detached houses on agricultural and rural parcels as the dominant dwelling type. A Matsqui acreage purchase is a fee-simple title purchase: no strata corporation, no Form B, no strata fee — Zoning Bylaw 2400-2014, the ALR rules in §3 where the parcel is in the reserve, the dyking levy and any registered covenants govern. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2014%20Matsqui%20Prairie%20Demographic%20Profile_spreads.pdf (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/matsqui-guide.md, §5)
- **Parcel character, as the research states it — set by the rural zones, not by a subdivision pattern:** in the A1 Agricultural Zone (Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, s.210), Agricultural Use and Single Detached Dwelling are principal uses and parcels created through subdivision must be a minimum of 8.0 ha; in the RR Rural Residential Zone (s.310), intended for single detached dwellings and agricultural uses on large lots in a rural setting, the minimum lot size is 4.0 ha. Those two numbers are the prairie's parcel character: most prairie parcels cannot be split further at all (the research file's own buying guidance). ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, rural sections.** A1 Agricultural Zone (s.210): Agricultural Use and Single Detached Dwelling are principal uses; parcels created through subdivision must be a minimum of 8.0 ha; one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit is permitted per lot only in accordance with the ALR Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the Agricultural Land Commission, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, with a maximum floor area of 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha; one secondary suite per lot is permitted, to a maximum floor area of 100 m². RR Rural Residential Zone (s.310): minimum lot size 4.0 ha; a coach house is permitted only on a lot greater than 0.4 ha, and a coach house or secondary suite is limited to one per lot and a maximum floor area of 90 m² or 40% of the net floor area of the single detached dwelling, whichever is less. None of these added dwellings creates a separate title for sale. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400310

- **ALR rules in full — the prairie's governing frame.** Subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, stated as the ALC states it. ALR residences cannot be stratified — strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)) — the statutory reason the strata count in §2 is structural, not a market gap. ALC housing rules as the research file states them: generally no more than one residence per parcel unless the owner qualifies for an additional residence; the principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less; a secondary suite is permitted in the ALR only within the principal residence; an additional detached residence may be up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form.** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR; no such line is asserted in this file, and none is documented in this area's research file (the flat figure is owned only by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page in this program). On the prairie, farm class is a core holding-cost term: it turns on produced and reported farm income, not on the A1 zone label alone. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What acreage can I actually buy on Matsqui Prairie?** A whole fee-simple farm parcel (A1 zone) or a large rural-residential parcel (RR zone). There is no named acreage subdivision, no bare-land strata, no strata of any kind and no presale acreage product — the strata zero is structural (ALR residences cannot be stratified, and the subdivision minimums are 8.0 ha and 4.0 ha). (§2 above)
- **Q: Can I split a prairie parcel for my kids or to sell a lot?** Only if the math survives: A1 parcels created by subdivision must be at least 8.0 ha and RR lots at least 4.0 ha, and subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval on top. Most prairie parcels cannot be split further — that is the research file's own buying guidance, and it should be run before any family-transfer plan is priced. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400310
- **Q: What second dwelling can go on an A1 lot?** One Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot, only via the ALR Use Regulation (B.C. Reg. 30/2019) or ALC approval, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, capped at 90 m² (lots of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha) — plus one secondary suite per lot to 100 m² under the zone, subject on ALR land to the ALC rule that a suite sits only within the principal residence. In the RR zone: one coach house or suite on a lot over 0.4 ha, at the lesser of 90 m² or 40% of the main house. None of these creates a separate saleable title. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Q: What are the ALC residence caps, in one place?** Principal residence: 500 m² or less, generally one per parcel. Suite: only within the principal residence. Additional detached residence: 90 m² maximum at 40 ha or less, 186 m² over 40 ha, and only where the owner qualifies for an additional residence. Residences cannot be stratified. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is the parcel serviced?** Prairie servicing is wells and onsite sewage — not municipal connections. Wells are recorded in GWELLS; Fraser Health requires an authorized person's record of sewerage system before work on an onsite system and a letter of certification after. Pull both for the specific parcel — servicing for any individual property is UNVERIFIED in the research record until those filings are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners
- **Q: What does the dyking levy mean for my tax bill?** Properties in the Matsqui Prairie Dyking District pay a separate levy on top of general municipal rates (Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026; 2026 Tax Rates Summary). It is a permanent holding-cost line for district parcels — confirm whether the parcel is in the district and read the line on its tax notice before comparing two prairie parcels on price alone. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Summary%20May%2022.pdf
- **Q: How serious is flood risk behind the dyke?** The City states the Matsqui Dykes are designed to prevent Fraser River water entering the prairie — design protection, maintained and levied for, but not a parcel-level guarantee. Ask about the parcel's flood history directly and confirm its flood status with the City; do not let "behind the dyke" end the inquiry. http://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/2022-spring-freshet-update-2
- **Q: What farm income keeps farm class on a prairie parcel?** The three tiers: $2,500 on a 0.8–4 ha operation; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31 (B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5). A flat "$2,500+" figure is an error for both small and large operations. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Q: Do I pay GST when I buy a Matsqui farm?** Do not assume the vacant-land exemption carries a farm: the CRA's vacant-land sheet (GI-003) exempts most individual personal-use sales, but its farmland guidance treats farmland sales as usually taxable with limited exemptions. The answer for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and farm use are applied — and the contract must allocate GST expressly. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What PTT would I pay on a $1.8M prairie parcel?** $34,000 on the program's worked anchors (1% on the first $200K = $2,000; 2% on the next $1.6M = $32,000), before any exemption — and the home exemptions are not farm-parcel relief, so do not net them off. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve a prairie parcel?** Mathxwí Elementary is in the village (33661 Elizabeth Avenue, formerly Matsqui Elementary). The settled locator chain for that address — single-address basis — is Mathxwí Elementary → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary (SD34 School Locator, mybaragar.com, run 2026-11-01). Catchments are address-based — any other prairie address needs its own locator run. https://mathxwi.abbyschools.ca/ and https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments
