## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: New Westminster Zoning Bylaw No. 6680 (2001). OCP: "Our City 2041" Bylaw No. 7925 (2017)** — the City's long-term land-use frame (program standing frame). The City's Development Updates page records the Interim Development Review Framework above. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- **RT CORRECTION (standing, governs over research errors): the townhouse/houseplex districts are RT-2, RT-2A, RT-2B, RT-2C and RT-3, RT-3A, RT-3B ONLY — RT-A and RT-B DO NOT EXIST in Bylaw No. 6680.** The error sits in the downtown-nw, sapperton, uptown-nw, connaught-heights, Brunette and Glenbrooke North research files (and several 55-plus siblings) — **corrected in-file here and wherever those sub-areas are met, flagged, with the siblings noted as conforming-pass items; siblings are never edited by this file.** **836.10 m² is an RM apartment-district density threshold, NOT an RT figure** — it is not used as a townhouse, lot or acreage figure anywhere in this file.
- **Infill / SSMUH position:** in June 2026 Council adopted OCP and Zoning Bylaw amendments permitting up to six units of infill housing (secondary suites, laneway and carriage houses, triplex, quadplex and other multiplex forms) on single detached and duplex properties across most of the city, under zones RGO-A (up to four units) and RGO-B (up to six units), without a site-specific rezoning. Earlier record: Bylaw No. 8453, 2024 rezoned the roughly 160 "restricted zone" duplex properties to a new RD-1 district permitting rental secondary suites; **Queensborough SSMUH compliance was extended by the Province to May 4, 2029.** https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Attachment_C2___Small_Scale_Multi_Unit_Housing_Legislation_and_Implementation_Summary.pdf
- **Secondary suites:** a separate dwelling unit within a house, accessory to a single detached dwelling or duplex and contained within the same structure; most single detached dwellings and some duplexes are zoned to permit them; they must meet the Zoning Bylaw, Building Code and Tree Protection and Regulation Bylaw; **they cannot be strata-titled, sold or used for short-term rentals**; properties with suites pay annual secondary suite water and sewer charges. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How did New Westminster sell in September 2026?** Detached 164 listings / only 9 sold / median $1,365,000; Condos & Townhomes 444 / 67 / median $560,000 — the attached median's lowest month in the September 2025–September 2026 period. Highest ratios: GlenBrooke North and The Heights detached, joint 12%; Quay attached 27% on 16 sales. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report.)
- **Q: Is Queensborough part of New Westminster?** Yes — a board sub-area and city neighbourhood across the Fraser River from the mainland city; it carried the city's largest detached shelf in September 2026 (41 listings, 1 sale, 2%) and its own Ministry-listed schools (Queen Elizabeth Elementary, 921 Salter St; Queensborough Middle School, 833 Salter St). Mainland comparables do not price Queensborough. (§§1, 3.)
- **Q: How many units can a New Westminster house lot hold?** Since June 2026, up to six infill units on single detached and duplex properties across most of the city under RGO-A (up to four) and RGO-B (up to six), without a site-specific rezoning; Queensborough SSMUH compliance runs to May 4, 2029. Verify the specific property's zone before writing either claim. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- **Q: Can a secondary suite be sold separately or used as a short-term rental?** No — the City's suite guide states suites cannot be strata-titled, sold or used for short-term rentals, and properties with suites pay annual suite water and sewer charges. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Secondary_Suite_Guide_August_2025.pdf
- **Q: What flood rules hit a Queensborough lot?** Zone 1 in the corrected ledger, plus the s.190.36 lot floor (½ acre / 66 ft unless sewer-connectable) and flood construction levels of 3.53 m (Area A) / 4.20 m (Area B) GSC. Glenbrooke North and Queen's Park flood positions are UNVERIFIED — no zone is imported for them. (§3.)
- **Q: Are RT-A and RT-B real New Westminster zones?** No — they do not exist. The districts are RT-2–RT-2C + RT-3/3A/3B only, and 836.10 m² is an RM apartment-district density threshold, not an RT figure. Any document using RT-A/RT-B is in error. (§3.)
- **Q: Is Portside a presale?** No — Portside (Queensborough) is a COMPLETED strata, counted in queensborough townhomes; a presale framing for it is superseded. (§2.)
- **Q: When are New Westminster property taxes due?** For 2026: July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties on July 3 and again September 3, 2026 on unpaid balances. (§3.)
- **Q: Is there an acreage market statistic for New Westminster?** No — SnapStats has no acreage/land category and publishes no sub-area medians; this file counts no acreage roster for the city. (§§1–2.)
- **Q: What PTT should I budget?** The §3 anchors — e.g., $900K→$16,000; $1.5M→$28,000. GST on a vacant lot only at GI-003 level. (§3.)
