## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Township zoning:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2500 governs this ground. Feed cards use RU1 and RU-3 labels in their own listing text — card labels are listing copy only, and a specific parcel's zone is confirmed with the Township per parcel. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **SSMUH (Township):** Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and NO 6-plex tier exists in it. Most Campbell Valley acreage parcels exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by a wide margin; parcel-level eligibility is UNVERIFIED.
- **Suites / coach houses (Township):** secondary suite cap 120 m². A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102, never strata. Live Township conflict flagged, not resolved: s.102 as read in this program permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted. R-CL s.403 (220 m² minimum lot / 464.5 m² maximum LOT area — no 355 m² figure exists in that section) is a Willoughby compact-lot rule, carried here only as part of the Township frame, not as a Campbell Valley rule.
- **Subdivision / servicing:** Township of Langley Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED — the servicing frame for any Township subdivision or bare-land servicing question on this ground. Parcel-level requirements are confirmed with the Township. (Standing servicing ledger.)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — this sub-area is farm-adjacent ALR ground, so the full frame governs every ALR parcel in it:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan IS a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — never the shorthand "cannot be stratified," and Bulletin 05 is not cited for stratification. Residence caps as the ALC states them: a principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite is permitted ONLY when located within the principal residence; one additional residence is permitted up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, and building more than the permitted number, or larger than the size limits, requires ALC approval. Township zoning and the ALC frame both apply; neither displaces the other. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices of $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer who plans to continue farming must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by the horse-properties page only); it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Quota, supply management and barn standards — UNVERIFIED in every particular in THIS file:** no marketing-board or CFIA source was opened in this slug's research, so no quota, board-transfer or barn-standard position is stated for any Campbell Valley operation.
- **BC strata block (for any bare-land strata lot, if one is met):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A home on a rented pad is NOT lot stock, NOT land and NOT acreage. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Township of Langley property taxes:** due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a penalty of 5% is applied to current-year taxes, including the unclaimed home owner grant, at midnight July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies to a qualifying home purchase); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. - **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a SEPARATE info sheet and treats farmland sales as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — no Campbell Valley farm parcel is assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
