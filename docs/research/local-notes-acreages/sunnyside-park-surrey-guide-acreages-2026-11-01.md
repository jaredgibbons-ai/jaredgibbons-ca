## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP / plan:** Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) governs — Bylaw No. 18020 is replaced and never cited as current. The Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan was approved January 31, 2022 and later updated to align with King George Boulevard Bus Rapid Transit and the Surrey 2050 designations; the plan area (16 Avenue to 24 Avenue, Martin Drive to Earl Marriott Secondary School) borders this sub-area — check whether an address is inside it before pricing growth upside. Zoning: **Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000**. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/semiahmoo-town-centre-plan
- **SSMUH (Surrey position):** SSMUH zones RA to R6 under Bylaw 12000 (in force Jul 8, 2024) allow up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Secondary suites are capped at **40%** of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area, one per dwelling; the old 90 m² cap is NOT a current rule. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Subdivision / servicing:** **Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 8830 (1986) VERIFIED** for Surrey. Subdivision potential runs against the zone minimums plus Bylaw 8830's servicing standards, confirmed per parcel with the City. (Verified ledger; FILE STATUS.)
- **Streamside / floodplain (corrected ledger):** Surrey streamside setbacks sit in **Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000, by stream class** — there is NO single riparian figure and none is stated here. Lot-level floodplain status: UNVERIFIED — check City flood information by parcel. (Research file above.)
- **Short-term rentals:** Surrey STR licence **$358/yr**, with the **$350 proposal disclosed** as a conflict in the program record — direct to the City licence page before quoting either figure. (Surrey frame; FILE STATUS.)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (carried because the brief requires the full block wherever ALR ground exists or the area is farm-adjacent; parcel ALR status here is UNCONFIRMED, §2):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a SEPARATE info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **BC strata block (for any strata product a Sunnyside buyer prices — no bare-land strata community is documented in §2):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
