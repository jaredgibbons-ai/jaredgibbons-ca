## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500** governs this ground. Secondary suites sit in s.102; coach houses are defined in s.102 (below). The Township frame — not the City of Langley frame and not the Abbotsford frame — applies to every parcel in this file. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **SSMUH — Township frame, kept distinct:** the Township of Langley adopted Bylaw No. 6020 on November 18, 2024, allowing 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², subject to eligibility (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area); lots over 4,050 m² are not eligible, and **no 6-unit transit tier currently applies in the Township — there is NO 6-plex tier.** Most parcels in this feed exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by a wide margin, so SSMUH math generally does not apply to them. This is the Township position only; Langley City's SSMUH (Bylaw 3284) is a different municipality's rule and is not applied here. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Secondary suites (Township):** under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s.102 (as amended July 2025), a secondary suite may not exceed 120 m² maximum gross floor area, each principal dwelling unit may have up to one secondary suite, and on ALR lands a suite must comply with Agricultural Land Commission regulations. **LIVE CONFLICT, reported with dates and not resolved:** the Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states suites are NOT permitted in townhouses/rowhouses and describes suites as within detached single-family dwellings, while the current s.102 definition (July 2025) says a suite may be located in a townhouse, rowhouse or manorhome — the bylaw definition is the later source; cite the bylaw, not the guide, where the two differ. For a detached acreage home the point is academic, but the conflict is carried wherever it is met. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Coach houses (Township):** in Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s.102, a "coach house" is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage — a fee-simple second unit under the zoning bylaw, never a strata unit. No size cap for a coach house appears in s.100 as read in the program's rules-gaps pass — a coach-house size cap is UNVERIFIED in this file. Abbotsford's coach/garden rule (15% / 100 m²) is Abbotsford ONLY and is expressly not applied to this Township ground. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)

- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame wherever a Glen Valley parcel is in the reserve (the first check on this sub-area's stock):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC and this slug's research state them: land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel; a principal residence may be up to 500 m² total floor area without ALC application; one additional detached residence may be permitted of up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; a secondary suite is permitted ONLY inside the principal residence and only where local bylaws allow it; local governments may further restrict residences through zoning. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

- **Q: Can a Glen Valley acreage have a second home or a suite?** Parcel by parcel.
