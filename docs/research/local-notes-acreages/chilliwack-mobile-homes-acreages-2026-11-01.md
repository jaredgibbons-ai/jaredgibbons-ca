## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Pad-rent increases (MHPTA / RTB) — percentages ONLY, never a dollar figure:** landlords in manufactured home parks can only increase rent with 3 full months' notice, can increase rent once every 12 months, and the increase cannot exceed the yearly rent increase limit; the 2026 limit for manufactured home site tenancies is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees, and the 2027 limit is 2.2% plus the same proportional amount; standard increases are notified on RTB Form 11a, and a landlord may apply to the RTB (Form 52) for an additional increase above the limit for qualifying expense or capital reasons. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale (MHPTA s.28):** a tenant may assign a manufactured home site tenancy agreement, or sublet the manufactured home site, only with (a) the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed obtained under the Regulation), (b) an order of the director authorizing it, or (c) authorization in the tenancy agreement itself; consent may be withheld only in prescribed circumstances, and under s.28(3) the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting. The consent request runs on RTB-10, Request for Consent to Assign a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement. (Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, s.28, BC Laws) and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/calculators-and-resources/tenancy-forms/forms-listed-number
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s.42):** where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or a residential use other than a manufactured home park, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice covering a full year, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; the tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, may dispute the notice within 15 days, may in defined circumstances (home cannot be moved, no site within a reasonable distance, no tax owing on the home) apply for additional compensation of the home's assessed value minus the $20,000, and if the landlord does not take steps toward the stated conversion within a reasonable period the landlord must pay the greater of $5,000 or 12 months' rent. The conversion notice is issued on RTB-31. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Manufactured Home Registry and CSA labels:** manufactured homes include modular and mobile homes, typically built in a factory off-site; mobile homes are built under CSA Z-240 standards and modular homes under CSA A-277 standards; in B.C., the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if the transaction is registered, and the Registry maintains the central register of manufactured home ownership details, controls the movement of manufactured homes in B.C., and improves the security of lenders financing manufactured-home purchases. The home may not be sold unless it has the original and legible CSA decal attached; if a CSA label is removed or lost, the home must be re-certified — Technical Safety BC has a licensed electrical contractor inspect the home's wiring and an electrical approval label is prepared, which can be used in lieu of a CSA label to list the home for sale (the official route behind the live page's "Silver Label" shorthand). A permit to move a manufactured home is issued by the Registry, supported by an Application to Transport and a tax certificate; the transport permit expires 30 days after issue. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **PTT on a pad — the live page's claim flagged UNVERIFIED in this file only:** the live page states BC Property Transfer Tax "generally does not apply" when buying only a manufactured home on a rented pad, with the transfer filed through the Manufactured Home Registry. The Registry part is confirmed above; **no passage on the Province PTT page opened in this program states the manufactured-home-on-a-pad treatment, so the PTT claim is UNVERIFIED in this file — have a lawyer or notary confirm it for the specific purchase.** This flag belongs to the chilliwack-mobile-homes file only; it is not asserted for any other slug. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-mobile-homes.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: If I buy a home in a Chilliwack park, do I own land or acreage?** No — on a rented pad you own the manufactured home and hold a manufactured home site tenancy under the MHPTA, paying monthly site rent to the park. Land ownership exists only in the own-land forms: fee-simple lots, or bare-land strata lots such as Baker Trail Village. A pad purchase is never acreage. (§2.)
- **Q: What is Baker Trail Village, and why is it different?** A 157-unit bare-land strata of manufactured homes at 46511 Chilliwack Lake Rd — you own the home AND a strata lot, pay strata fees (not pad rent), and take a Form B. It is documented ONCE in this topic's lots-and-land home file; this file cross-references it only and counts it nowhere. It is strata tenure on the lot, not a pad tenancy and not fee-simple. (§2.)
- **Q: What pad rent should I expect in a Chilliwack park?** No figure is stated in this file: no official source publishes Chilliwack pad rents, and the live page's $550–$950 range is UNVERIFIED site copy, flagged in this file only. The figure that governs is the specific site's tenancy agreement — confirm the current pad rent, what it includes, and its increase history with the park for the exact site before offering. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-mobile-homes.html
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up each year?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, and no more than the yearly limit: 2026 = 2.3% plus the proportional levy/utility amount; 2027 = 2.2% plus the proportional amount. Increases above the limit require an RTB order (Form 52). Model growth from the Act, not from a park brochure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does PTT apply when I buy a home on a rented pad?** Do not rely on the live page's answer: its "generally does not apply" claim is UNVERIFIED in this file — no Province PTT passage stating that treatment was opened in this program. Have a lawyer or notary confirm PTT and Registry handling for the exact structure before subjects come off. (§3.)
- **Q: Why does the CSA label matter?** A manufactured home may not be sold without the original legible CSA decal attached (Z-240 for mobile, A-277 for modular); if the label is lost, the home must be re-certified through Technical Safety BC's electrical inspection and approval-label route before it can be listed for sale. Check the label before anything else. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Can the park refuse my purchase, or charge me for approving it?** Assignment needs the landlord's prior written consent (or deemed consent, a director's order, or agreement authorization) under MHPTA s.28, consent may be withheld only in prescribed circumstances, and under s.28(3) the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting. The request runs on RTB-10. (MHPTA s.28)
- **Q: What happens if the park converts or closes?** Under MHPTA s.42: all permits in place first, then 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation, with defined additional-compensation routes and a further payment (greater of $5,000 or 12 months' rent) if the landlord does not proceed. Price the risk with the park's ownership and any redevelopment signals checked in due diligence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: The two Ashwell Road listings state different age rules (40+ vs 45+) — which is right?** Neither is relied on: both are listing text for the same community (Rainbow Estates, 9055 Ashwell Road), they conflict, and the community's actual age rule is UNVERIFIED in this file. Age rules, pet rules and approval conditions must come from the park's current documents for the specific site. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-mobile-homes.html
