## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: the Downtown District (DD) schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575** governs much of the area — the only permitted uses are those in the Downtown District Official Development Plan, including commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses, with development permits approved by the Development Permit Board or the Director of Planning as the schedule states; CD-1 and C-3A districts also apply in parts of the area. The C-3A schedule provides for a wide range of goods and services and dwelling uses compatible with commercial uses. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-C-3A.pdf
- **Plans:** the Downtown Eastside Plan provides a vision, policies and strategies for the Downtown Eastside, at the east side of the Downtown local area and in Strathcona; a plan named only for the Downtown local area: UNVERIFIED. Heritage controls for Gastown, Chinatown and Yaletown (boundaries in Section 1) preserve and protect historic fabric and affect renovation, alteration and any redevelopment of a heritage-district parcel from day one. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/dtes-local-area-plan.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **Subdivision — VERIFIED City instruments:** subdivision in Vancouver runs under the Subdivision By-law No. 5208, and its 2026 fees are set by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026), which substitutes a new Schedule F: Class I (Major) $174,300, Class II (Intermediate) $87,200, Class III (Minor) $15,020, Class V (Air Space) $125,100 where FSR is greater than 3.0 (the class a downtown tower parcel would meet), and Class VI (Freehold Rowhouses) $15,020 plus $1,950 per freehold lot. The City's major-subdivision process (sites over 10,000 m², or 10,000–40,000 m² likely to require complex legal agreements) requires a preliminary proposal with a current Land Title Office title search and a lawyer's charge summary, submitted to the Approving Officer. There is no house-lot subdivision stream a downtown buyer will use, because there are no house lots to split. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/major-subdivision.aspx
- **Floodplain diligence:** the City's designated floodplain includes the False Creek coastal floodplain, and land in the floodplain is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for shoreline setbacks and flood construction levels; whether a specific downtown parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED — check the parcel in the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **ALR position — stated plainly: no ALR position is documented for this area.** No ALR parcel is documented in the research and no farm adjacency is raised, so no ALR rule is applied to any Downtown parcel in this file. For completeness only, the standing conditional rules IF a parcel were ever confirmed ALR are: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; residence caps are a principal residence to 500 m², a suite within the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); and farm class runs on the full three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met, is documented only on the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, and is never attributed to this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC strata block (the operative regime downtown, since nearly every Downtown purchase is a strata purchase):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. Review the depreciation report, contingency reserve and any special levies before pricing an offer on any downtown tower unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 purchase treated as fully residential with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** (tier math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 further-tier = $93,000) — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Vancouver taxes — property tax, the 3% Empty Homes Tax (Vancouver ONLY), and additional school tax:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date, and the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. Vancouver owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the Empty Homes Tax rate is 3%, the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — a standing check on any Downtown purchase, where investor-held units are common. At Downtown price points the Province's additional school tax can also apply: 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at the CRA GI-003 level:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Anything finer for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and use are applied. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; a source stating March 12 is noted in-line wherever met, never silently overwritten. The GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A $1.25M straight-line phase-out row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism where met. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
