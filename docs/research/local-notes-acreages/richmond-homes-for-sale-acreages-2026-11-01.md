## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning + OCP:** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 governs parcel zoning city-wide; the 2041 Official Community Plan is Bylaw 9000, with district plans as schedules (Steveston Area Plan = Schedule 2.4). Single-detached districts sit in RS1/RS2 (s.8.1), with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m². Farm land sits in AG1 (s.14.1): one principal dwelling per lot, secondary suite as a secondary use; subdivision in the ALR is not permitted unless approved by the Agricultural Land Commission, and where that approval is not required the AG1 minimum lot area is 2.0 ha; single detached housing is not to be built on a lot under 828.0 m² in that zone. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=53061
- **Suites / SSMUH (non-ALR lots):** a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in RS1/RS2 (Bylaw 8500 s.5.4: at least 33.0 m², at most the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m²); the City allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. Do not price SSMUH potential into an ALR parcel — AG1 lots follow the ALR frame below. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — Richmond has ALR ground (East Richmond / Terra Nova), so the full frame applies:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban; ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** minimum gross farm income at farm gate prices of $2,500 if the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — new applications by the legislated deadline of October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by HORSE-PROPERTIES); it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to it. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Servicing:** Richmond Subdivision and Development Bylaws 8751/8752 VERIFIED (SERVICING LEDGER). On rural/ALR lots, check the well record in the provincial GWELLS application and confirm septic installation by an Authorized Person under the Sewerage System Regulation through the health authority. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Floodplain:** Richmond Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction levels — the governing flood bylaw for Richmond ground (the corrected ledger position; Surrey's Part 7A stream-class system does not apply here). The dike system is designed for a 1:500 event and is being raised from 3.5 m to 4.7 m. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **ADEU — a West Cambie / Alexandra carrying cost ONLY, never generalized:** buildings in the Alexandra pocket (Alexandra Road, Odlin Road, Tomicki Avenue) connect to the Alexandra District Energy Utility, and its charges form part of carrying costs on top of a strata fee. ADEU charges are NOT a Richmond-wide or acreage cost and are never stated as one. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_2_-_2021_Annual_General_Meeting_Resolution_of_the_Lulu_Island_Energy_Company_Sp58691.pdf
- **Property taxes (Richmond):** 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — anchors with tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000). Mixed-use/ALR parcels split residential vs non-residential portions on the return. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example); a sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland is addressed by the CRA in a separate info sheet and treated as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — no Richmond farm parcel is assumed GST-exempt on the vacant-land rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — housing, not bare land:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 noted in-line in some sources — March 13 governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land — 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phase-out to $1.5M; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **BC strata block (where bare-land strata exists):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Pad ground:** a manufactured home on a rented pad is NOT lot stock, land or acreage. Manufactured-home pad tenancy percentages only (2026 = 2.3% + proportional share; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional share); NO pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact anywhere in this file.

Any other speculation/vacancy position for a specific Richmond parcel: UNCONFIRMED until its assessment class and the current exemption list are read.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a published Richmond acreage or land statistic?** No. SnapStats publishes no Richmond row at all and no acreage category anywhere, so no Richmond land median, count or $/acre figure exists in the permitted source — any such figure is UNVERIFIED. (§1.)
- **Q: Where is Richmond's acreage ground?** In the ALR farm belt — East Richmond and the AG1 farm land beside Terra Nova — not in a subdivision market. The city page's feed is condos, detached houses and Steveston product. (§§1–2.)
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide an ALR farm parcel in Richmond?** Not without the ALC's permission: depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and ALCA s.21(1) bars subdividing agricultural land unless the Commission permits it or the ALR General Regulation allows it (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6). AG1 also sets a 2.0 ha minimum lot area where ALC approval is not required. (§3.)
- **Q: How many homes can an ALR parcel hold?** One principal residence (maximum 500 m²), a secondary suite ONLY inside it, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha). (§3.)
- **Q: What farm income keeps farm classification?** $2,500 for an operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value over 4 ha for a larger operation; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. (§3.)
- **Q: Does Vancouver's 3% Empty Homes Tax apply to a Richmond acreage?** No — it is a City of Vancouver tax only and does not apply anywhere in Richmond. (§3.)
- **Q: What does flood risk mean for a Richmond farm parcel?** The whole city sits in the Fraser River floodplain behind a 1:500-design dike system; Flood Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction levels for new construction. Confirm the parcel's flood construction level before pricing a build. (§§1, 3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget?** The tier anchors in §3 — e.g., $800K→$14,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 including the over-$3M tier. ALR/mixed parcels split on the return. (§3.)
- **Q: Is GST charged on vacant farm land?** At GI-003 level only: personal-use land sold by an individual is generally exempt; business sales and subdivisions over two parts can be taxable, and farmland carries the CRA's separate caution. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED — get the contract's GST position confirmed. (§3.)
- **Q: Which school will a Richmond acreage's address feed?** UNVERIFIED until the exact address is run in the SD38 locator — catchments are address-based, there is no middle school tier, and sample-address results elsewhere in this program are single-address only. (§1.)
