- **Secondary suites (Mission):** all suites must be registered with the City; suites sit in single family homes only and are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes — a suite described on a band townhouse card is a claim to verify with the City, not a feature to price. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (where ALR ground exists):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — full three-tier form:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. Pad ground is NOT lot stock / land / acreage; MHPTA percentages only (2026 = 2.3% + proportional; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional), no pad-rent dollar figure as fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked through the band:** 1% first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M (band cap)→$22,000 (= $2,000 + 2% × $1,000,000); $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — follow $93,000 wherever a $78,000 figure is met and flag it (open reconciliation item). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there an acreage under $1.2M on this page?** This file verifies none: the page is a townhome band feed, its land-form content is detached-form strata homes inside a complex, and band status is UNCONFIRMED per parcel. An acreage parcel's price is a live-feed question at the time, not a band statistic. (§2 above)
- **Q: What is the median Mission townhome price under $1.2M?** No such figure exists: SnapStats publishes no townhome-only split and no band cut. Its "Condos & Townhomes" community median was $430,000 in September 2026 on 10 solds (and $642,500 the month before) — context, never a band valuation. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report.)
- **Q: A card shows a detached rancher — is that a freehold acreage lot?** Not necessarily: Heritage Park Place cards describe detached ranchers inside a strata subdivision. Check the strata plan — a detached strata lot carries Form B, fees and bylaws that a fee-simple lot does not. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at the top of this band?** At $1,200,000, fully residential with no exemption: $22,000 (tier math in §3). At $800,000 it is $14,000 — or $6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can a townhome in this band have a suite?** No — Mission does not permit secondary suites in townhouses or rowhomes, and every Mission suite must be City-registered in a single family home. A suite claim on a townhouse card is a verification item, not income. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **Q: Can I subdivide a large Mission acreage instead of buying a townhome?** Subdivision is a City process under DSCB 5650-2017 and the zone minimums in Bylaw 5949-2020 — and ALC approval if the parcel is ALR. No parcel's potential is stated without its zone and ALR status. (§3 above)
- **Q: Does GST apply to a newly subdivided lot in Mission?** At the CRA GI-003 level, generally yes — a parcel created by subdividing into more than two parts, or land sold in the course of a business, is a taxable case, while most individual sales of personal-use vacant land are exempt. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. (§3 above)
- **Q: How is the commute from a Mission acreage or townhome?** West Coast Express: five weekday morning trains, 75 minutes Mission City to Waterfront, five afternoon returns, no weekend service — a weekday asset only. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- **Q: Which school catchment applies?** UNVERIFIED for any address here — SD75 is a three-tier, address-based system, names must be Ministry-listed (§1), and no Mission locator run is on record. Confirm the exact address with the district. (§1 above)
- **Q: Why is the $1.2M ceiling rarely the binding number in Mission?** Because the published attached community median ($430,000, September 2026) sits $770,000 below the ceiling — arithmetic against a community median, labelled as such; it does not state how many listings fall inside the band, and no band count exists. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report.)
