## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 3000** — small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3 with the subdivision minimums in Section 2 (650 m² / 18.5 m in R-1 and R-3; 465 m² / 12.5 m in R-2), as the research states them from the City's SSMUH summary guide. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=

- **Subdivision and servicing — VERIFIED LEDGER:** Coquitlam's subdivision/servicing bylaw is **Bylaw No. 3558, 2003** — carried exactly as verified. Subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Section 2 through that bylaw's process; no servicing standard is quoted from a listing or a summary.
- **SSMUH as the City states it:** up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes, where zoning and municipal services allow; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. SSMUH creates house-lot units, not acreage. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Watercourse / flood setbacks:** a Watercourse Protection Development Permit can be required within 30 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam, and Zoning Bylaw s.519 / s.523 set flood and riparian setbacks — check early on ravine and creek-edge lots. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **ALR — conditional block, carried because parcel status is UNVERIFIED:** if a specific parcel were found to be in the ALR, subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence, and an additional residence capped at 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). No parcel in this sub-area is asserted to be in the ALR in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form, carried with the ALR block:** $2,500 (0.8–4 ha operation); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha (over 4 ha); $10,000 (under 0.8 ha) — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met and is not asserted here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Strata purchases elsewhere — the BC strata block, for redirect cases only:** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). - **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,800,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,600,000 ($32,000) = **$34,000**.
