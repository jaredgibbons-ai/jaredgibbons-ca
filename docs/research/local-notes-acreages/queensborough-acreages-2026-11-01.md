## 2. THE ACREAGE ITSELF

**NO ACREAGE MARKET — stated plainly at the top, because it is the honest answer for this file. Queensborough is a built-out island neighbourhood: single-detached houses (including compact-lot form under the (RLR) designation), low-rise apartment buildings, and stacked and side-by-side townhouses — the largest listed stock of any New Westminster sub-area in this batch (41 detached listings and 72 condos & townhomes listings in September 2026). Nothing here trades as farm land, hobby acreage or a bare-land parcel; the island's only "acreage-sized" rule is a subdivision floor that stops lots getting smaller, not a market in large lots. No named fee-simple acreage subdivision, no bare-land strata acreage community and no presale LOT offering was verified on an official or developer source. Verified named acreage developments counted here: 0. The land home for this ground is the lots-and-land sibling (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/queensborough-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md).**

- **The half-acre rule — what it is and what it is not:** Zoning Bylaw s.190.36 provides that no Queensborough (Lulu Island, District Lots 757 and 758) subdivision may produce a lot under one-half acre (0.20 ha) or with frontage under 66 feet (20.12 m) unless the land can connect to the City sanitary sewer system. Most Queensborough lots are sewer-connected, but the connection status of a specific lot governs what can be done with it: UNVERIFIED for any individual lot. This rule matters to an acreage buyer in exactly one way — a lot that cannot connect to the sewer cannot be created under a half acre — and it does not create acreage: the island's detached stock is built urban lots, including compact-lot single-detached form under the (RLR) Residential – Low Rise designation in the OCP amendment materials (Bylaw No. 8574, 2026), which provides compact-lot single detached form, low-rise buildings up to six storeys, and stacked and side-by-side townhouses. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/100_Introduction(2).pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Draft_Queensborough_Land_Use_Designations_and_Map___March_9_2026.pdf
- **The flood position, because it governs every island parcel:** Queensborough is Floodplain Zone 1 in the City's floodplain study (Zone 1, not Zone 3 — FILE STATUS): a low-lying area with high groundwater, poor compressible soils and poor drainage, protected by a shared dyking and pumped drainage system with Richmond. In the 1960s a perimeter dyke was built on Queensborough protecting approximately 350 hectares; Queensborough has 350 hectares of designated floodplain and is the highest priority for flood protection improvements in the city. New construction must meet flood construction levels of 3.53 metres above Geodetic Survey of Canada datum for Area A (generally downstream from Derwent Way on the south side and Wood Street on the north side) and 4.20 metres above GSC datum for Area B (upstream of Derwent Way and Wood Street). https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Flood_Hazard_DPA.pdf
