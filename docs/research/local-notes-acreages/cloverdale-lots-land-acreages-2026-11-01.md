## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP / zoning:** OCP = Surrey 2050 Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — NEVER cite Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning By-law No. 12000 governs parcel by parcel; check the parcel in COSMOS for current zoning (post-July 2024 zone names — R4 and R6 replaced RF-12/RF-12C and RF-SD), OCP designation, secondary plan status, water/sanitary/storm main locations, road-dedication requirements and riparian setbacks. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is stated in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH and suites:** Surrey's zoning allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (SSMUH changes Jul 8, 2024). Only one secondary suite is permitted per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite, and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. Coach houses and garden suites: all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; the unit must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR, smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house, and one extra parking space is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame wherever a feed parcel is in the reserve (Serpentine farm parcels and the Fraser Highway acreage expressly included):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, apart from the limited cases in the Agricultural Land Reserve General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Stated expressly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in Cloverdale Lots & Land's own research file — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Subdivision and servicing:** Surrey's Subdivision and Development Bylaw 1986, No. 8830 is VERIFIED in the standing ledger and governs subdivision servicing in Surrey. The live page's FAQ directs buyers to budget for municipal Development Cost Charges, Metro Vancouver regional charges, latecomer agreements, road/sidewalk frontage works and BC Hydro service connections when subdividing or building — the categories are confirmed as the page's list; the applicable DCC rates and any parcel's servicing status are UNVERIFIED in the research pass — no Surrey DCC bylaw or rate page was opened, and no DCC figure is stated in this file. Tree protection: the live page states Surrey's Tree Protection Bylaw regulates removal of protected trees and sets replacement ratios or cash-in-lieu during demolition, subdivision and building-permit applications; the bylaw's specific thresholds and ratios were not confirmed on a City page opened in the research pass: UNVERIFIED as stated. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-lots-land.html
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; business/developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland is the caution: the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a separate info sheet — a Serpentine farm parcel must never be assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone. The live page's finer GST position and its lender down-payment ranges (35–50% on raw land; 25–35% on serviced lots) are UNVERIFIED — no CRA detail beyond GI-003 or lender source was opened in the research pass, and neither position is restated as fact here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html and https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-lots-land.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 noted in-line in some sources — March 13 governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. On ALR land any qualifying new home must first fit the ALC caps above. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Short-term rentals (holding strategies on house-on-lot purchases):** a short-term rental is the renting of a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence is required before operating. **Licence-figure CONFLICT carried as directed: the program figure is $358/yr; the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350/yr + fees — carry $358 with $350 disclosed, and confirm on the City licence page before operating.** https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **BC strata block — applies wherever a bare-land strata lot trades:** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions).
