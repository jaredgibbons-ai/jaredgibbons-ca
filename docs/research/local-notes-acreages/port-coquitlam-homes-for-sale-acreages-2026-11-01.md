## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Planning frame (per this slug's research, sourcing stated):** Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 3838 (adopted 2005, substantially updated 2013 to align with Metro Vancouver's Regional Growth Strategy; the City states it will undertake an OCP update to align with Metro 2050 and provincial housing legislation, with 2024–2025 work including SSMUH accommodations and defined Transit Oriented Areas). https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/reports-plans-publications/official-community-plan Zoning Bylaw, 2008, No. 3630. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11157
- **Zoning / SSMUH frame (with its verification limit stated):** the City's SSMUH amendment summary for Bylaw No. 3630 renames the RS zones "Residential Small-Scale," permits up to 4 dwelling units on lots 281 m² to 4,050 m² and up to 6 units on lots 281 m² or larger within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop, replaces "coach house" with "accessory dwelling unit" (ADU floor area limit 90 m², excluded from FAR), and caps a secondary suite at 40% of the floor area of the principal dwelling unit, with suites permitted in a house, duplex or triplex where units are vertically separated. Whether that amendment package is the in-force consolidated text of Bylaw No. 3630 today is UNVERIFIED — the consolidated bylaw read carries the current suite definition (a second dwelling unit within a building containing a principal dwelling, forming a single real estate entity, not separately strata-subdividable) without the full SSMUH schedule. Confirm the specific lot with the City. Secondary-suite owner-occupancy and PoCo short-term rental rules: UNVERIFIED — no such clause/bylaw was read on an official City page in this pass.
- **Property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026 under Annual Tax Rates Bylaw, 2026, No. 4441; a 5% penalty applies to current taxes unpaid after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after September 15, 2026; penalties are set by provincial legislation and the City cannot waive or modify them. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Servicing: Port Coquitlam servicing is HELD UNCONFIRMED** (process documented, no bylaw number asserted) — see the ledger below. **Registry:** PoCo's registry position is not separately listed in the registry ledger; no registry is asserted for a PoCo parcel in this file.
- **ALR RULES IN FULL, wherever ALR ground exists or the area is farm-adjacent:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a PERMISSION rule, never an absolute ban, and ALC Bulletin 05 is never cited for it. Residence caps: one principal residence, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; at most one additional residence of 90 m² on parcels ≤40 ha or 186 m² on parcels >40 ha; local bylaws may restrict further but not be more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if less than 0.8 ha. Applications by the legislated October 31 deadline for the next assessment/tax year; late applications are not accepted; a buyer continuing to farm must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by HORSE-PROPERTIES) — it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

Worked anchors (inputs + tier math stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). - **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example); an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland is addressed by CRA in a separate info sheet and is usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — no farm parcel is assumed exempt on the vacant-land rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
