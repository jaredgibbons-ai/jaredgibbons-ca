## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: City of Pitt Meadows Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011** — RS (minimum lot area 0.2 ha), R-3 / R-4 / R-6 (subdivision minimum 1,215 sq m; the figure is a subdivision minimum only, per the City's SSMUH implementation update) and RM-1 (townhouse sites, minimum 0.4 ha), as carried in Section 2. - **SSMUH in Pitt Meadows, as the City states it:** under provincial rules applied citywide, lots 280 sq m or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 sq m up to 4 units, and lots over 280 sq m within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units; the City's SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986 was adopted June 25, 2024. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf

- **Floodplain / dike diligence — LIVE CONFLICT flagged, notes retired to 95%:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the floodplain for the Fraser River and, to a smaller degree, the Pitt River, while all five Pitt Meadows guide pages quote "over 85%" sourced to the City Financial Plan 2026–2030 — a genuine LIVE CONFLICT between two City sources, flagged here and never silently resolved; this file uses the 95% figure.
