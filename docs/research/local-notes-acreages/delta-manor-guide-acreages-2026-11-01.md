## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** The current OCP is a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules; the Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, and farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". Any page attributing a lettered schedule to Bylaw 8400 is wrong — the known "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" conflation (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the OLD Bylaw 3950) is an error wherever met, including in any sibling file that asserts it. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged not settled.** City zone sheets cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, with zoning assigned parcel by parcel on the bylaw's zoning maps; an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates for the zoning bylaw. Neither is asserted as settled here — the ruling is Jared's. A buyer reads the parcel's zone off the City's maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it.** Delta adopted its small-scale multi-unit housing zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024, allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies; the RS1 zone also lists a secondary suite as an accessory use to a single detached dwelling. SSMUH is added-unit potential on an existing lot — it is not a subdivision right, and minimum lot areas in the zone sheets above still govern any actual subdivision. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing requirements.** A City of Delta council report (January 24, 2024) presents the Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 to repeal and replace Subdivision and Development Standards Bylaw No. 7162, 2015, made under s. 506 of the Local Government Act to regulate and require works and services on subdivision — highways, water, sanitary sewers, drainage and other services and utilities. The final adoption status of Bylaw 8288 is HELD UNCONFIRMED in this program: sibling lots files asserting "8288, 2024" in governing terms are noted as conflicts wherever met and the number is never asserted here. The current servicing standard for any specific Delta Manor parcel: UNCONFIRMED — confirm with the City's Engineering/Development department before pricing a subdivision. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Streamside rule.** All properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, which can limit building and subdivision work on the lot. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Dyke / flood diligence.** Ladner is low land protected by the City's dike, seawall and drainage system (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations, ~538 km of drainage infrastructure). Confirm flood construction requirements for the specific lot with the City before assuming a rebuild or infill design works. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **ALR rules in full (farm-belt parcels; in-sub-area ALR UNVERIFIED, §2).** ALR residences cannot be stratified — strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)) — and subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, as the ALC states it. ALC residence caps: a principal residence of 500 m² or less total floor area; a secondary suite only within the principal residence; one additional residence of 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or less, or 186 m² or less on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — three tiers, never a flat figure.** Gross annual farm income required: $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on an operation under 0.8 ha. Applications are due by October 31 for the next taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an error and is not asserted here; no file in this group documents a flat-figure assertion for this area (that error is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, outside this group). https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Bare-land strata block (applies to Laburnum Park Place, §2).** Form B (Strata Property Act, s. 59) discloses the lot's strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, and must be provided within 7 days of request (max $35 plus up to 25¢/page). Since July 1, 2024 every strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, non-deferrable by vote; since November 1, 2023 the CRF must receive at least 10% of the operating fund annually. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022; the only valid age bylaws are 55+ (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions) — which is the framework that makes Laburnum Park Place's recorded 55+ bylaw lawful; a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only.** Most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (for example, land kept for personal use), but a sale is taxable where the land was subdivided into more than two parts, and sales of farmland are usually taxable; sales by developers/businesses are generally taxable supplies. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. Whether GST applies to a specific Delta Manor lot sale turns on the seller's use and subdivision history — get a ruling or tax advice. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Property transfer tax on land.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000; $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M — an OPEN reconciliation item; this file follows $93,000, and no sibling for this area carries the $78,000 figure). The first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) for homes valued at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000; the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and phases out by $1,150,000 — both are home exemptions, and their application to a bare-land purchase is a facts-specific question: UNCONFIRMED for bare land in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-home money and land — stated precisely.** The first-time home buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — it applies to a new home built or bought, NOT to bare land itself. The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000, likewise a housing rebate. The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs and the First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit, for a qualifying home. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation and vacancy taxes — Delta's actual position.** The City of Delta IS listed by the Province as a designated taxable area for the BC Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so owners of residential-class property in Delta must complete the annual declaration; exemptions and rates are as the Province states them on its site. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is a City of Vancouver tax ONLY and does not apply in Delta. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition — stated only as this file's sources support.** CMHC states the prohibition (in force to January 1, 2027, in census metropolitan areas, which include Delta in Metro Vancouver) covers buildings of three or fewer dwelling units, and that effective March 27, 2023 the prohibition does NOT apply to vacant land — vacant land zoned for residential and mixed use can be purchased by non-Canadians and used for any purpose, including residential development. The position is not conformed beyond that cited source. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/media-newsroom/news-releases/2023/amendments-prohibition-purchase-residential-property-non-canadians-regulations
- **Foreclosure / court sales:** Delta has NO Supreme Court registry — any registry mapping for a Delta Manor court sale is UNVERIFIED and none is asserted here. The court-sale process itself is the settled BC process (Supreme Court Civil Rules 21-7 / 13-5, Practice Direction PD-66, Form 60); Form B and PTT/GST treatment on a specific court sale are UNCONFIRMED in this file and are kept distinct from the CRA GI-003 position above. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/168_2009_00
- **Delta property taxes and holding cost.** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026 — a vacant lot carries the same tax calendar, with no home on it to offset the carry. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy an acreage in Delta Manor?** No — no acreage product is verified here. The sub-area is a built-out village grid of city lots (subdivision minimums 335–836 m² in the urban zones). Ladner's acreage ground is the ALR farm belt east of the village, outside this sub-area — a different search, with the ALR rules in §3 applying in full there. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is there any bare land for sale in Delta Manor?** No named bare-land development or subdivision is verified here — "buying land" means buying an improved fee-simple lot, usually with a house on it. (§2 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Delta Manor lot?** Only if the parcel's zone allows it: subdivision minimums run from RS7 at 335 m² and RS6 at 390 m² up to RS5 at 550 m² (460 m² outside the SFR designation), and zoning is read parcel by parcel off the City's maps — then the City's subdivision and servicing standards apply (Bylaw 8288's adoption is UNCONFIRMED, §3). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Q: Is a lot here on municipal water and sewer, or well and septic?** Urban Ladner is within the City's water, sanitary sewer and drainage systems; no source in this program documents well/septic inside Delta Manor. Confirm the specific parcel's services and any connection charges with the City before subjects. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: What can I add to a lot without rezoning?** Delta's SSMUH bylaws (8394 and 8395, June 24, 2024) allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — confirm the parcel's zone first. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q: Is Laburnum Park Place an acreage or a normal detached purchase?** Neither — it is bare-land strata: you own the lot and rancher, but there is a strata corporation, strata fees, registered bylaws (including its recorded 55+ age restriction) and Form B diligence. It is documented once in its Ladner-wide 55+ home file and cross-referenced here; its sub-area placement is also the subject of an open sibling conflict — see §2. https://realtylink.org/en/print/property/263117720
- **Q: Does GST apply when I buy a lot?** It depends on the seller and the land's history, per CRA GI-003: an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt, while land subdivided into more than two parts, farmland, and developer/business sales are generally taxable. Price the answer into the offer — do not assume either way. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What are the holding costs on a vacant or teardown lot?** Delta property taxes on the City's calendar (2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026), plus insurance and drainage/utility charges as the City bills them — parcel-specific figures UNCONFIRMED. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Q: Is flood risk a real diligence item on a flat Ladner lot?** Yes. The whole of Ladner sits behind the City's dike and drainage system — over 63 km of flood protection and 31 pump stations — and lots within 30 m of a slough or ditch are also in the Streamside Protection DPA. Confirm flood construction requirements for the lot with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Could a lot here be ALR, and could it get farm class?** Inside Delta Manor, ALR is UNVERIFIED and none is confirmed — the ALR is the farm belt east of the village. If a listing claims ALR status, the consequences are large: no stratification, ALC approval for subdivision, and the ALC residence caps in §3. Farm class is a separate BC Assessment test — the three tiers in §3, applied for by October 31 — and ALR status alone does not confer it. (§2–§3 above)
- **Q: How long does a subdivision or infill build take?** UNCONFIRMED — no official source in this program publishes Delta approval or build timelines for a Delta Manor lot; anyone quoting a timeline is estimating, and the City's development department is the only authoritative source.
