## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP — current plan only.** Land use now follows the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900, and Zoning Bylaw 12000. The South Newton NCP was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 update and is no longer in effect; Bylaw No. 18020 is **never** cited as the current OCP in this file. A seller quoting NCP densities is quoting a dead document. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Zoning.** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 (R-series, RA at the Colebrook rural edges; R3 minimum subdivision lot area 660 m² as recorded for this plan area — see §2 for the two-figure caution). https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it (Jul 8, 2024 framework).** Maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; one secondary suite per house; unpermitted existing suites are not legalized by the new rules. Servicing is a condition in practice: properties not connected to community water and sewer are among the exemptions — directly relevant at the Colebrook edge. Added units are not subdivision and do not create acreage parcels. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey).** One per house; Surrey's cap is 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area, one per unit, not a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Subdivision + servicing — VERIFIED LEDGER entry, carried exactly:** Surrey subdivision and servicing runs under the Surrey Subdivision and Development By-law No. 8830 (1986), with subdivision proceeding through detailing and a Servicing Agreement defining the off-site works, services and financial securities for final approval. Lot-specific servicing costs, DCC amounts and timelines for a Panorama Ridge parcel: UNCONFIRMED — no City figure for this area is stated in the research. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside / riparian (Surrey).** Streamside protection in Surrey is set in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 and is applied **by stream class — there is no single setback figure** for the city, and none is stated in this file. Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries run through the plan area; check City mapping and the parcel's permit areas before valuing an addition, rebuild or subdivision on a creek-side parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R053.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Slope / south-edge diligence.** The research documents the ridge form, Highway 10 at the south edge and the lowlands beyond it: check highway noise exposure and confirm the parcel is on the upland side of the floodplain picture before subjects. A parcel-level geotechnical report requirement for a specific Panorama Ridge parcel: UNCONFIRMED in the research — obtain the City's requirements for the parcel rather than assuming a slope study is or is not required. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/panorama-ridge-guide.md, §6)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey).** Permitted, owner-only, under licence. Figure CONFLICT, disclosed: $358/yr (program figure) vs $350/yr plus fees proposed in the City's 2024 corporate report — carry $358 with the $350 conflict disclosed; verify on the City licence page before quoting. (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/panorama-ridge-guide-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **ALR rules in full (they govern any parcel later confirmed ALR — none is confirmed in-area):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALC residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², a suite within the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR and appears nowhere in this file as a rule. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — rates and worked anchors (inputs + tier math stated):** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked examples: $500,000 → $8,000; $700,000 → $12,000; $800,000 → $14,000 (net $6,000 after the maximum $8,000 First-Time Home Buyers' relief, where it applies); $900,000 → $16,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 **including** the further 2% tier on the $500,000 over $3,000,000. (No townhomes sibling for this ground carries a $3.5M worked figure in the files checked — the $93,000 anchor is followed here as the program's settled figure; if a sibling carrying $78,000 for the same input is met, that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item, not a second answer.) First-Time Home Buyers': no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief on a new principal-residence HOME up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a home exemption, not a bare-land exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most individuals' vacant-land sales are exempt (e.g. personal-use land); taxable examples include business capital property, sales in the course of a business, and parcels created by subdividing into more than two parts. Developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots beyond that line are generally taxable. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED per parcel. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — land angle stated precisely:** the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026**, CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. Land-plus-build qualification: UNCONFIRMED per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **BC strata block (no bare-land strata is verified on this ground — the block governs if a buyer meets that form elsewhere):** a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — Form B (within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page; fees, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary), depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023, rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022, and only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Speculation and vacancy tax:** Surrey IS a designated taxable area; residential owners file the annual declaration and a vacant parcel's position turns on class and exemptions. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is **Vancouver ONLY**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there acreage I can buy on the ridge?** No acreage subdivision or acreage offering is verified — the purchase available is an existing fee-simple house lot for rebuild/added units. Strata complexes and the Amson Bloc presale are built/presale product, not acreage. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is a Colebrook-edge parcel a farm parcel?** Not on this file's evidence: ALR status inside the sub-area is UNVERIFIED, no farm operation is confirmed, and the RA label at that edge is zoning, not proof of farm use or farm class. Check the parcel on the ALC maps and with BC Assessment before pricing farm value into it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: How much of my creek-side parcel can I actually build on?** Streamside setbacks under Part 7A of Bylaw 12000 are set by stream class — there is no single figure — and Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries can limit the envelope. Get the City's watercourse mapping and permit-area check for the parcel before valuing a rebuild. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R053.pdf
- **Q: Is a Colebrook-edge parcel on sewer and water?** UNVERIFIED per parcel in the research — septic/individual services are possible at that edge, and unconnected properties are an SSMUH exemption category. Confirm with the City before counting added units. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: How many units can a large lot hold under SSMUH?** 3 under 280 m², 4 over 280 m², up to 6 on eligible 281 m²+ lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — subject to zone, servicing and any creek/permit-area limits. That is added units on one lot, not a subdivision into acreage parcels. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: What is the minimum lot I can subdivide to?** The research records an R3 minimum subdivision lot area of 660 m² for this plan area (Bylaw 12000, Part 26 Section F) — confirm the parcel's own zone section with the City, because the general R3 figure elsewhere in Surrey research differs (464 m²). Subdivision and servicing then run under Bylaw 8830 (1986) and a Servicing Agreement. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **Q: Do I need a geotechnical study on the ridge/south edge?** The research does not document a parcel-level requirement — UNCONFIRMED. What is documented: Highway 10 noise/floodplain-edge checks for south-edge lots. Ask the City what studies the parcel's permit areas require before subjects. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/panorama-ridge-guide.md, §6)
- **Q: Will GST or PTT relief help on a large-lot purchase?** GST turns on the seller/subdivision history (CRA GI-003, §3); PTT relief turns on building/occupying a qualifying home; the FTHB GST rebate is for new housing, not bare land. None can be assumed on a lot alone. On the general PTT tiers, a $1,200,000 purchase carries $22,000 before any exemption. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Can I get farm-class taxes on a Colebrook-edge parcel?** Only if the three-tier income test in §3 is actually met ($2,500 / 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; apply by Oct 31) — acreage alone, or an RA zone label alone, does not qualify a parcel. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Q: Which schools serve a ridge parcel?** Panorama Ridge Secondary (13220 64 Ave), Colebrook Elementary (5404 125a St), McLeod Road Traditional (6325 142 St) and W.E. Kinvig Elementary at the west edge are the in-area schools named in the research. The one settled SD36 locator chain documented is for 13220 64 Ave: North Ridge Elementary → École Panorama Ridge (locator run 2026-11-01). Any other address: run the SD36 School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
