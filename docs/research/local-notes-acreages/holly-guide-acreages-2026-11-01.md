- **ALR rules in full (East Delta farm-belt ground at Holly's edge; in-sub-area ALR UNVERIFIED, §2).** ALR residences cannot be stratified — strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)) — and subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, as the ALC states it. ALC residence caps: principal residence 500 m² or less total floor area; a secondary suite only within the principal residence; one additional residence of 90 m² or less (parcels 40 ha or less) or 186 m² or less (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form, never a flat figure.** Gross annual farm income required: $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on an operation under 0.8 ha. Applications are due by October 31 for the next taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an error; no such line is asserted in this file, and the flat figure is owned only by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page in this program — it is not documented in this file's research. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Bare-land strata block (Hollyview Estates and Ashley Green, cross-referenced in §2).** Form B (SPA s. 59) discloses the lot's strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, and must be provided within 7 days of request. Since July 1, 2024 every strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years; since November 1, 2023 the CRF must receive at least 10% of the operating fund annually. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022; the only valid age bylaws are 55+ (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw — and no Holly complex is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax on land — tiers and worked anchors (inputs + tier math).** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 (at the first-time-buyer cap the $8,000 saving on the first $500K leaves $6,000 payable); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M purely residential → **$93,000** ($2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 at the 3% tier + $10,000 further 2% on the $500K over $3M). **OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met:** townhomes-program siblings elsewhere carry $78,000 for the $3.5M anchor; this file follows $93,000 and flags the conflict. First-time buyers' exemption: no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, phasing to $1,150,000 — the exemption that matters for a new 60B Street home; its application to a bare-land purchase is facts-specific: UNCONFIRMED for bare land in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only.** Most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (for example, land kept for personal use), but a sale is taxable where the land was subdivided into more than two parts, sales of farmland are usually taxable, and sales by developers/businesses are generally taxable supplies. A new Skye townhome carries GST as new housing; a resale lot or home does not. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home money — housing, not bare land.** The first-time home buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA — a source stating March 12 is noted in-line, never silently overwritten) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; it does NOT apply to bare land itself. (A live page's $1.25M straight-line phase-out row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism wherever met; none is documented in this file's research.) The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 per buyer. First Home Savings Account: $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

Read the Skye disclosure statement, not the marketing, for phasing and completion dates. (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/holly-guide-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §3)
