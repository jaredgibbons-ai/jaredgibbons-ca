## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500.** Houses sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with secondary suite rules in Section 5.4 (a suite must be at least 33.0 m² and no more than the lesser of 40% of the dwelling's floor area or 110 m²) and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL added under provincial Bill 44 legislation; village commercial and mixed-use zones named by the City include Steveston Commercial (CS3) and Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU32) – Steveston Village. The City also allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot under its Bill 44 housing rules. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **OCP: Richmond Official Community Plan Bylaw 9000** (2041 OCP framework), with the Steveston Area Plan as Schedule 2.4 guiding the whole Steveston planning area, and the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area inside it — properties inside the conservation area need a Heritage Alteration Permit for exterior changes. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/steveston566.pdf and https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Floodplain — Steveston's defining diligence: Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204** sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements in Richmond. Steveston fronts the South Arm of the Fraser River behind the City dike system — Richmond is a low-lying island city in the Fraser River floodplain protected by a perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations, designed for a 1:500 flood event with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres — and the City's Steveston Island Dike project addresses the Steveston waterfront and harbour. For waterfront and dyke-adjacent lots, confirm dike setbacks and the flood construction level under Bylaw 8204 before pricing in a rebuild or addition. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf and https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.letstalkrichmond.ca/StevestonIslandDike
- **Subdivision / servicing: Richmond Subdivision Bylaws 8751/8752 VERIFIED** (standing servicing ledger). No other city's servicing number is used for Richmond ground. (Program standing frame.)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried because Steveston is farm-adjacent (east Richmond ALR ground); the frame governs only a parcel actually in the reserve, and no Steveston-district parcel is documented as ALR in this slug's research:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; local governments may regulate further but not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure (ALR-adjacent ground only):** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices of $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — and new applications for farm classification must be submitted by the legislated deadline of October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it — owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page; it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies to a qualifying home purchase); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
