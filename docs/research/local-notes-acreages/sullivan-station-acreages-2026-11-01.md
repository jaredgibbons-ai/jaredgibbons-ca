## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026)** — current; Bylaw 18020 is never cited as current. The South Newton NCP is rescinded; designations were updated by Corporate Report R246 (endorsed Dec 2, 2024). Use the current plan, not the rescinded one. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000** — R-series zones in South Newton, RA at the rural edges toward Colebrook. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH (Surrey frame, Jul 8, 2024):** maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the BC Building Code permits one secondary suite per house; properties not connected to community water and sewer are among the exemptions. The City states SSMUH does NOT retroactively legalize a suite or coach house built without permits. Surrey's suite cap is 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Streamside setbacks:** Surrey streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000, set BY STREAM CLASS — no single riparian figure is ever stated here. Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries run through South Newton, so check City mapping before valuing an addition or rebuild on a creek-side lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Subdivision and servicing: Surrey Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED** on the City's own Land Development / Servicing Agreement pages — subdivision and servicing agreements run under 8830 against the zone minimums and the current OCP designations.
- **ALR rules — conditional frame for any RA-edge / Colebrook-side parcel actually in the ALR:** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); principal residence ≤500 m², suite inside the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class in the full three-tier form: $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha, $10,000 (<0.8 ha), applications by Oct 31. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy an acreage in Sullivan Station?** No — there is no acreage market in this sub-area: no named acreage subdivision, farm parcel product or bare-land strata acreage community was verified. What you can buy is a detached house lot (fee-simple, R-series zoning) or strata attached product. (§2.)
- **Q: What is a Sullivan lot actually worth building on?** Its SSMUH band: up to 3 units under 280 m², 4 units over 280 m², up to 6 on an eligible lot ≥281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — confirm the band and bus-stop eligibility with the City before valuing a teardown. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Does the old South Newton plan still govern my lot?** No — it was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP (Bylaw 21900, adopted Jun 15, 2026); designations were updated by Corporate Report R246 (Dec 2, 2024). Price the lot on the current OCP and Zoning Bylaw 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Q: Can I add a suite, and how big?** One secondary suite per house under the BC Building Code within Surrey's SSMUH frame; Surrey's suite cap is 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area. An existing unpermitted suite is NOT legalized by SSMUH — it still needs the permit process. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: My lot backs onto a creek — what limits apply?** Streamside setbacks in Part 7A of Bylaw 12000, set by stream class (no single figure) — Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries all run through South Newton. Check City mapping before valuing an addition. (§3.)
- **Q: Is any Sullivan Station ground in the ALR?** No ALR designation inside the sub-area was confirmed in the research; RA zoning sits at the rural edges toward Colebrook and the Serpentine lowlands lie south of Hwy 10. A specific property's ALR status is confirmed on ALC maps. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Q: Are the townhouse complexes here land I can buy into as acreage?** No — the 9 complexes counted in the townhomes file are strata built product; The Uplands counts in the Panorama Ridge guide townhomes file only, and Sullivan Mews is excluded everywhere (membership tenure).
