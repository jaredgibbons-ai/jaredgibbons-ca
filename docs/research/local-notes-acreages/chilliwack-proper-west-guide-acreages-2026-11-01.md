## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Plan frame:** land use sits under the City's 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP Bylaw 2025, No. 5500), whose Schedule B-2 is the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan; the plan was first added to the 2040 OCP as Schedule D through Bylaw 2018, No. 4533. The guide page's statement that Bylaw 5500 was adopted on December 16, 2025 was not confirmed on a City page in this program: UNVERIFIED as a date; the plan identity and Schedule B-2 position are confirmed. The guide states the plan's designations as Residential 1 (one and two family housing) and Residential 2 (attached housing and narrow lot infill). https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Zoning and SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs — s.6 sets the agricultural (farm) zones in the city-wide frame, with rural zones and the RS-FHA (Flood Hazard Area) reserve zone elsewhere in the bylaw; the area page's specific infill-zone claims were NOT confirmed section-by-section: UNVERIFIED, page copy only. SSMUH: Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, amending Bylaw 5000, adopted June 18, 2024, allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Suites and accessory units:** under Bylaw 5000, a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the habitable floor space of the building (s.2); only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot (secondary suite, coach house or garden suite, garden suites capped at 100 m², s.4.06(1)); ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite (s.5.04); a suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **Short-term rentals:** the City regulates short-term rental operations under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141, adopted November 24, 2021, which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968. Confirm a property can be licensed under it before valuing any rental income. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **Subdivision / servicing — number UNCONFIRMED, process documented (the 3055 conflict):** the Chilliwack servicing-bylaw number is HELD UNCONFIRMED in the standing ledger. The candidate "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" is NOT adopted per the ledger and is NEVER presented as verified in this file — even though the City's subdivisions webpage names it, and several Chilliwack lots siblings including the chilliwack-proper-west lots home and the chilliwack-farms lots file assert 3055 as governing; those assertions are documented here as conflicts, the ledger governs, and those siblings are flagged as conforming-pass items. The documented process instead: a Chilliwack subdivision proceeds by application to the City's Approving Officer, with Preliminary Layout Approval setting the conditions (servicing, works and dedications) the subdivision must meet before final approval and registration, tested against provincial statutes, the Zoning Bylaw, environmental sensitivity and the OCP. No servicing-bylaw number is asserted.
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — Chilliwack is farm country citywide and ALR questions arise on larger lots at this ground's edges, so the full frame is carried (applying parcel-by-parcel, only where the parcel is in the ALR):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan IS a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — never the shorthand "cannot be stratified." Residence caps as the ALC states them: a principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite is permitted ONLY when located within the principal residence; one additional residence is permitted up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, and building more than the permitted number, or larger than the size limits, requires ALC approval. City zoning and the ALC frame both apply where the parcel is in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by the horse-properties page only); it is NOT documented in this slug's research. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Quota and board items — UNVERIFIED in every particular in THIS file:** no marketing-board source was opened in this slug's research, so no quota or board position is stated for any Chilliwack operation.
- **BC strata block (for any strata lot met, including the cross-referenced Wolfe Road stock):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **City of Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed annually at the end of May, taxes are due the first working day after July 1, a 5% late payment penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. This is the CITY regime — the rural regime in §§ for FVRD ground does not apply here, and Mission's flat penalty does not apply here either. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's personal-use land is generally exempt, with taxable cases including land used primarily in a business, sold in the course of a business, or subdivided into more than two parts. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — CRA governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
