- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $900,000 fully residential infill-lot purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $700,000 ($14,000) = **$16,000**. The home-based exemptions have land routes — a first-time buyer registering a vacant lot and building can keep the FTHB exemption if lot value plus build cost is $835,000 or less (partial under $860,000), building and occupying by the first anniversary; the Newly Built Home Exemption can refund lot PTT once a qualifying new home is built ($1,100,000 / $1,150,000 thresholds). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any acreage to buy in Central Abbotsford?** No. This is built-out urban ground: no acreage subdivision, no hobby-acreage pocket, no working-farm ground and no bare-land strata acreage community in the core. What trades as land is an infill parcel, a teardown lot or a development site — documented in the land home named in the FILE STATUS block. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How small can a central lot be cut?** By zone: RS3 minimum interior lot 540 m², RS5 420 m², and RS7 infill lots down to 300 m² (10.0 m width, 22.5 m depth) — among the smallest detached lot minimums in the city. Confirm the lot's zone section before paying for potential. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58178/
- **Q: Can a single detached home have a secondary suite?** Yes — Zoning Bylaw 2400-2014 s.140.3.8 states one secondary suite is permitted in all single detached dwellings, subject to bylaw conditions; Abbotsford's suite cap is under 50% of the principal dwelling, and its coach/garden cap is 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m². https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Q: How many units can go on a central detached lot under SSMUH?** The bylaw's s.140 provision allows 3 units on lots 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots greater than 280 m² in the stated conditions. The SSMUH bylaw number itself is UNCONFIRMED in this program — "3560-2024" must not be quoted as settled. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Q: Which plans govern a central parcel?** The City Centre Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2913-2019) and Historic Downtown Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2920-2019), listed as adopted neighbourhood plans under the city-wide Abbotsforward 2050 Official Community Plan — the CURRENT plan; older "2600-2016" / "2500-2016" citations are stale. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **Q: Is Central Abbotsford in the Sumas Prairie floodplain?** No — the City flood page describes Sumas Prairie as low-lying agricultural land in East Abbotsford, a separate area from the central upland; but Willband Creek runs through the central area, so parcel-level floodplain status still requires a City floodplain map check. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- **Q: Is any central land in the ALR?** Not confirmed — the central area is urban in City plan designations, and parcel-level ALR status for central lots is on the research file's UNVERIFIED list. Run the ALC map check for the specific parcel before assuming either way. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- **Q: How are school catchments checked for a central address?** Enter the primary home address into the SD34 School Locator portal; SD34 school pages state the portal determines the neighbourhood catchment school, and registration starts at the catchment school. Two settled runs (single-address basis): 33130 Bevan Ave returns Godson Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary, and 32161 Dormick Avenue returns Dormick Park Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary (SD34 School Locator, mybaragar.com, run 2026-11-01). Any other address needs its own run. https://swift.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/kindergartenregistration/swift
- **Q: Will GST apply to a teardown or infill lot I buy from a builder?** Generally yes where the GI-003 taxable patterns apply — land sold in the course of a business, or a subdivided parcel of more than two parts — while an individual's personal-use resale is generally exempt. Parcel-level position: UNCONFIRMED until vendor status is applied; state it in the contract. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What does it cost to hold a central lot?** Property taxes on the Abbotsford cycle (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3; second 5% September 16, 2026). A fee-simple infill lot carries no strata fee; a strata purchase in the core carries the Form B / CRF diligence in Section 3 instead. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
