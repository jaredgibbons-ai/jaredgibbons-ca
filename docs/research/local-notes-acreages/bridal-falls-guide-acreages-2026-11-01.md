- **ALR RULES IN FULL (farm-adjacent electoral-area ground — this block applies wherever ALR ground exists):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha) — on ALR land in Bridal Falls' frame: the principal residence maximum is a total floor area of 500 m²; a secondary suite is permitted only when it is entirely contained within the principal residence and local bylaws permit it; and one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application at up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, subject to local bylaws. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
