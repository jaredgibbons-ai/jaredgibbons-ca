## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Pad rent increases — the regulated mechanism (Province / Residential Tenancy Branch):** a park landlord may increase rent only with 3 full months' written notice, only once every 12 months, and only up to the yearly limit; the **2026 limit for manufactured home site tenancies is 2.3% plus a proportional amount, and the 2027 limit is 2.2% plus a proportional amount**. The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park. The landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion of a prior year's limit. **These Act percentages are the ONLY pad-rent numbers stated as fact in this file — no pad-rent dollar figure is ever published as fact here.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale (MHPTA s.28):** assignment of the site tenancy permanently transfers the pad tenancy to the buyer; a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an order of the director, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it — s.28(1)(b) wording is "an order of the director" (NOT arbitrator); the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent, and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s.42):** where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to another use, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes where the home cannot be moved or no site is available within a reasonable distance. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Registration and certification:** in BC the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered with the Manufactured Home Registry, through a lawyer or notary filing the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership; a home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular), and a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Am I buying land or acreage when I buy a Langley mobile home under $500K?** On a rented pad, no — you own the manufactured home and rent the site under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement (MHPTA). It is NOT lot stock, NOT land and NOT acreage, which is why this file carries no acreage roster. (§§1–2.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much is pad rent on a Langley mobile home under $500K?** No figure is stated in this file: no official source publishes a Langley pad-rent figure, average or range. This page's "$600–$900 per month" FAQ range is UNVERIFIED site copy (§5), and the figure that matters is the specific park's current rent in its written site tenancy agreement. (§§1, 3.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up each year?** Up to the Residential Tenancy Branch limit for manufactured home site tenancies: **2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027** — the proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of sites in the park. Increases require 3 full months' notice, only once every 12 months, with no rounding up and no carry-forward. (§3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park raise my pad rent when I buy the home?** No. On assignment under MHPTA s.28 the pad rent stays the same for the buyer and the next increase date does not reset; assignment needs the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), and the landlord cannot charge a consent fee. (§3.)
- **Q: What happens if the park is converted to another use?** Under MHPTA s.42: the landlord must first have all necessary permits and approvals, then give 12 months' notice and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes. (§3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What document replaces the Form B on a pad purchase?** There is no Form B — there is no strata. The diligence set is the written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement (current rent, last increase date, inclusions), the park rules, and the Manufactured Home Registry record; the sale is only effective if registered with the Registry, and the home cannot be sold without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 / A-277) or Technical Safety BC re-certification. (§3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Is a card that says "55+ community" or "Lease runs to 2072" reliable?** Both are listing copy only in this research — a 55+ claim is not verified park by park, and a lease-to-2072 tenure does not fit the standard pad frame at all. Confirm either against the tenancy agreement, park rules and title before offering. (§2.) https://jaredgibbons.ca/cheap-mobile-homes-langley-under-500k.html
- **Q: Which municipality's rules govern my park?** Pad tenancy is provincial (MHPTA) in both Langleys. Land-use rules split: Township parks sit under Township Zoning 2500 / SSMUH 6020 / Servicing 5382 (2019) CONFIRMED; City parks sit under City Zoning 2025 No. 3300 (by number only, adoption UNVERIFIED), City suite = lesser of 100 m² and 40%, City SSMUH Bylaw 3284. The park-zone section of either bylaw is UNVERIFIED in this research — confirm the park's municipality first, then its zone with that municipality. (§3.)

- **Q: Is the tax on a park home the same as on a house on its own lot?** The home is assessed and taxed like other residential property, but in a park the assessed value does not include the land's value (park not on reserve land, or land owned by someone else) — so the tax base is the home alone, and the pad rent is a separate, regulated tenancy cost on top.
