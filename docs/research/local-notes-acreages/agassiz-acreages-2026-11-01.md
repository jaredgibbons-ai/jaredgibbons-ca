## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What am I actually buying when I buy an "acreage" in Agassiz?** A whole fee-simple farm-plain parcel or farmhouse outside the townsite boundary, predominantly ALR ground. There is no acreage subdivision, no bare-land strata and no presale acreage product to buy instead — each parcel is judged on its own title, Kent zoning and ALR status. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is Agassiz a town of its own — and is Kent a board name?** No on the first, no on the second: Agassiz is the main community inside the District of Kent (Municipal Hall at 7170 Cheam Avenue), and Kent is NOT a board name — Agassiz is the CADREB MLS® area name (Harrison Mills is a sub-area under it). https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf
- **Q: Is this parcel in the ALR — and what changes if it is?** Confirm the parcel itself on the ALC map; the flat lands around the townsite are predominantly farm/ALR. If it is in: subdivision needs ALC approval, the residence cannot be stratified, and the housing caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside that residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I split the parcel, stratify it later, or put a suite in a barn?** Stratify: no, if it is ALR (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Split: only within the Kent zone requirements and, in the ALR, only with ALC approval. Suite in a barn: no — under the ALC rules a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; a detached second dwelling is the size-capped "additional residence." https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What farm income do I need to keep farm class?** All three tiers, not a flat figure: $2,500 on a 0.8–4 ha farm operation; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: The plan map shows Residential–Future land — can I buy and build there now?** Not on the designation alone: some of those lands are currently in the ALR and stay in farm use until an ALC exclusion is approved, with a Council-approved Neighbourhood Plan required first. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/vpwaiuh5/2026-01-21-dok-2025-ocp-update.pdf
- **Q: Is an Agassiz acreage in a floodplain, and is it serviced?** Floodplain: yes in character — diked Fraser floodplain, with a District Floodplain Bylaw and area-specific flood construction levels; confirm the FCL for the specific property before writing. Servicing: UNCONFIRMED per parcel — the four-unit SSMUH position covers serviced townsite lots, not farm parcels; confirm water/sewer/septic with the District for the exact parcel. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf

At CRA GI-003 level, an individual's personal-use land is generally exempt while developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable; the answer for any specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and farm use are applied, and the contract must state who bears GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
