## 2. THE ACREAGE ITSELF

**What Chilliwack farm acreage actually is: whole fee-simple farm parcels across a city that is 67% agricultural land by the City's own statement — valley-floor farms under the AL and AS agricultural zones, upland farms under AU, farm-residential parcels under AR, and agricultural commercial / food-processing operations under AC and AFP. This TOPIC file carries NO roster of its own: no named farm, no named acreage subdivision, and no presale farm offering is documented here, because the topic's research names none on an official source. Tenure: farm parcels are overwhelmingly freehold; strata farm product is not a Chilliwack pattern (no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source).**

- **Farm types in the topic (from the official material):** valley-floor farms under the AL and AS zones, upland farms under AU, farm-residential parcels under AR, and agricultural commercial and food-processing operations under AC and AFP — a zone typology, not a roster of operations. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Minimum lot areas inside Section 6, zone by zone — AS THE RESEARCH STATES (the bylaw's minimum LOT AREA for a newly created lot):** AL and AU — 7.5 ha where land is excluded or exempted from the ALR or a subdivision is approved within the ALR, 1 ha if created by boundary adjustment to allow the more efficient use of agricultural land, and 0.2 ha if created by homesite severance or a boundary adjustment approved by the ALC (ss. 6.01(2), 6.02(2)); AS — 2 ha where a subdivision is approved in the ALR, 1 ha by boundary adjustment, and 0.2 ha by an ALC-approved boundary adjustment or homesite severance, or by subdivision of ALCA-exempt land served by a community water system (s. 6.03(2)); AR — 0.2 ha where served by a community water system, 1 ha for all other uses (s. 6.04(2)); AC — 2 ha (excluded/exempted or approved subdivision), 1 ha by boundary adjustment served by a community water system, and 0.2 ha by ALC-approved homesite severance (s. 6.05(2)); AFP — 0.4 ha for all uses (s. 6.06(2)). Minimum lot width is 25 m in all six zones. These are creation minimums keyed to the approval route — not surveyed sizes for any parcel, and no typical farm size is claimed beyond them. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Farm Home Plate figures inside Section 6:** in the AL, AR and AC zones the Farm Home Plate is capped, as maximum lot coverage, at 4,000 m² for the principal residential use and 1,000 m² for an additional residential use (ss. 6.01(5), 6.04(5), 6.05(5)); the dwelling must be sited no farther than 70 m from the front or exterior side lot line with no part of the plate more than 80 m from that line, or no farther than 50 m from the rear lot line with the plate within 60 m (ss. 6.01(8), 6.04(8), 6.05(8)); and the Farm Home Plate requirements do not apply where a lot is under 0.8 ha in area (ss. 6.01(10), 6.04(10), 6.05(10)). The AU, AS and AFP sections state no Farm Home Plate figure. The OCP carries Development Permit Area 4 – Farm Home Plate, and a Development Permit is required when the Farm Home Plate regulations in the Zoning Bylaw are not met. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **ALR vs non-ALR split:** properties in the ALR are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALR regulations; ALC approval may be required before the City can approve related development applications, and the City directs owners to check ALR status on the City Webmap. The City states an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, for placing fill or removing soil, for non-farm uses, and for subdivision or a boundary adjustment, with ALC applications referred to the City for review. A specific parcel's reserve status is also checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address (the ALC states its online tools are for general reference and official confirmation comes from the ALC Mapping and GIS team). No city-wide ALR percentage is stated in the research, so none is stated here — the 67% figure above is agriculture's share of the land base, not an ALR share, and the two are never conflated. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, Section 6** establishes the AL, AU, AS, AR, AC and AFP agricultural zones (ss. 6.01–6.06), and Section 7 the rural zones including R (Rural) and RH (Rural Hillside). Read the exact agricultural zone, not just "farm": a small-lot (AS) purchase and a lowland (AL) purchase are different products with different subdivision and use rules (minimums in Section 2). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **OCP frame: Chilliwack 2050 (Bylaw No. 5500)** — Agricultural Plan = Schedule B1; Eastern Hillsides = Schedule B4; Sardis = Schedule B5; Chilliwack Proper = Schedule B-2; Downtown = Schedules B3 + D-6. The Agricultural Area Plan was created as part of the OCP in 2012 in partnership with the Chilliwack Agricultural Commission, and the Agricultural and Rural Advisory Committee (ARAC) monitors its implementation through reviewing agricultural development applications. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **ALR RULES IN FULL:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; residence caps as the ALC / City material states them — land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; one additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and City of Chilliwack bylaws must also permit it. Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, in this topic's own research wording:** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income requirements at farm gate prices — $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha; farm applications must be submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year. This topic's research documents the full form itself — a flat "$2,500+" line would be an ERROR here as everywhere, and no such line is asserted in this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **SSMUH:** Chilliwack's SSMUH amendments were made by Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024. SSMUH is an urban small-lot framework; it creates no Chilliwack farm lots and does not change the Section 6 minimums, the Farm Home Plate rules, or the ALR frame above.
- **Subdivision and servicing process:** subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer / Preliminary Layout Approval process as documented in the City's Subdivision Application Guide — for ALR parcels, ALC approval is required in addition (the City lists subdivision and boundary adjustments among the items that may need an ALC application). The Chilliwack servicing-bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED: a "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" surfaced in search and is NOT adopted here — never present 3055 as verified, and never quote a servicing standard for a farm parcel from a listing or a summary. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on farm acreage:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program and is flagged wherever met, not followed. Mixed farm/residential parcels split the tiers by portion — the residential-portion math for a specific farm is UNCONFIRMED until its assessment split is read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on farm and vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The Farm Home Plate has a geography, not just a size cap.** In the AL, AR and AC zones the dwelling must sit no farther than 70 m from the front or exterior side lot line (plate within 80 m), or no farther than 50 m from the rear lot line (plate within 60 m) — and the plate rules fall away entirely on lots under 0.8 ha. A house plan drawn for the middle of a large lowland parcel can fail the plate before it fails any size test. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **The same 0.2 ha minimum means four different things in Section 6.** A 0.2 ha lot is reachable by homesite severance or ALC-approved boundary adjustment in AL/AU, by ALC-approved severance or community-water subdivision of exempt land in AS, as the standard community-water minimum in AR, and by ALC-approved homesite severance in AC — the approval route, not the number, is what a buyer is actually purchasing. (Section 2, zone by zone.)
- **The Agricultural Commission is built into the approval path.** The Agricultural Area Plan was created in 2012 in partnership with the Chilliwack Agricultural Commission, and the Agricultural and Rural Advisory Committee (ARAC) monitors its implementation by reviewing agricultural development applications — farm applications in Chilliwack are reviewed through an agricultural lens by design. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
