## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning (City side): Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020.** Parcel-level minimum lot sizes are set out in the bylaw itself, and rural zones such as RU16 carry a 1.6 ha minimum in City notices. Confirm the parcel's zone before pricing development potential — the band ceiling says nothing about what the zone allows. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning
- **OCP (City side): OCP Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026,** replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025 and keeps the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction, with a Regional Context Statement added, per the City's public hearing notice. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — for ALR parcels on the city's rural edges, in or out of the band:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in this band file's research — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **SSMUH (City side):** SSMUH incorporation Bylaw 6299-2024 (Jul 17, 2024) verified via City sources; Mission unit counts still held UNCONFIRMED. The City's SSMUH page states that zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted, and the City's planning pages set out how secondary suites and detached accessory units are handled by zone. On ALR land the ALC suite rule (inside the principal residence only) controls. https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **Subdivision and servicing (City side):** the City's servicing bylaw is the Development and Subdivision Control Bylaw (DSCB) No. 5650-2017, stated from a City source (title confirmed on mission.ca). - **Property taxes — the two regimes, never blended:** CITY of Mission ground: 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a FLAT 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 (Community Charter ss.233–234; the City's taxes page) — never the 5%+5% form for City ground. - **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked at the band ceiling:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; **$1.8M — this band's ceiling →$34,000** (worked: 1% × $200,000 = $2,000 + 2% × $1,600,000 = $32,000); $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
