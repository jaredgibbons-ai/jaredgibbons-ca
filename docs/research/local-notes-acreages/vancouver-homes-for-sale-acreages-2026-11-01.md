- **Empty Homes Tax = 3% — VANCOUVER ONLY:** Vancouver owners of residential property must submit an annual property status declaration on or before the 2nd business day in February under the City's Vacancy Tax By-law No. 11674, and a vacancy tax unpaid as of the 10th business day of April bears a 5% penalty. The 3% rate is verified from a City page in the program's Vancouver frame; this slug's research pass read the declaration and penalty frame but did not state the rate on the City pages it read, so the rate rests on the program's verified Vancouver frame, not on this slug's research alone. The tax applies in the City of Vancouver ONLY — never exported to any other municipality, and expressly does NOT apply to UEL / university-vw ground. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/11674c.pdf
- **Vancouver property taxes:** the City collects property tax twice a year — advance taxes due the second business day of February (February 3, 2026) and main taxes due the second business day of July (July 3, 2026) — and the last day to pay the 2026 main tax, claim the homeowner grant and complete a deferment application to avoid a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance was July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **ALR position:** no ALR parcel or acreage ground is documented in this slug's research, and Vancouver's large-lot RA-1 Southlands ground is a City district schedule, not ALR land — no ALR housing cap, subdivision or stratification position is asserted for any Vancouver parcel in this file. If a parcel's reserve status is ever in question, it is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address; the ALC frame in the Fraser Valley files does not travel into the city. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies to a qualifying home purchase); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — CRA governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **BC strata block (the dominant Vancouver tenure — applies to strata purchases city-wide):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). For presales, REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission period and s.18 requires deposits held in trust.
