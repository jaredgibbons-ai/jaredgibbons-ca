- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the frame for any band parcel a parcel check puts in the reserve:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. BC Assessment farm-class thresholds, full three-tier form: $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever stated; it is not documented in this file's research and is not asserted here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **BC strata block — where a band purchase is strata (redirect case):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). - **Property transfer tax (PTT) at this band — the rule that bites hardest:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million (only the residential portion on a mixed-class property; where land is classed as farm only because it is used for an owner's or farmer's dwelling, up to 0.5 hectares is treated as residential property).
