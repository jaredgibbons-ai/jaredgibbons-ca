## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Sumas Mountain rises between Abbotsford and Chilliwack, above Sumas Prairie to the south and the Fraser River lowland to the north. The built part is inside the City of Abbotsford; the upper and eastern rural part is unincorporated Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) land next to the City of Abbotsford/City of Chilliwack boundary. http://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/sumas-mountain.html
- "Sumas Mountain" is the FVREB sub-area name in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, and the City uses it for the mountainside neighbourhoods on the west and south slopes, including the McKee plan area; the FVRD plan for the rural part is the Sumas Mountain Official Community Plan, Consolidated OCP Bylaw No. 584. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/projects
- Neighbourhoods inside or at the foot: Auguston, McKee and the Eagle Mountain side on the City slopes; Clayburn Village at the northwest foot. Nearby areas: Abbotsford East (west/southwest foot), Sumas Prairie (south), Chilliwack (east side). https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- Main roads are Sumas Mountain Road (becoming Upper Sumas Mountain Road toward the regional park), McKee Road, Dawson Road and Whatcom Road, connecting down to Highway 1 at the Whatcom Road interchange at the south foot. Exact Paragon boundary line: UNVERIFIED in the research. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/new-park-dianne-brook-ave

## 2. THE ACREAGE ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: Sumas Mountain DOES have real acreage ground — large fee-simple parcels on the City slopes under the rural/suburban zones, and larger rural parcels on the FVRD upper/eastern mountain on well and septic. What it does not have, on any source checked, is a named fee-simple acreage subdivision or a presale acreage offering: acreage here trades parcel by parcel, not as a development roster. The one named bare-land strata community on the mountain, Straiton Mountain Estates, is cross-referenced to its lots-and-land home file and is not re-documented here. The McKee lands at the foot are a rezoning-stage proposal, documented as a proposal only. No parcel count, parcel size beyond the zone minimums in §3, or acreage price is invented here.**

### What acreage actually trades here

- **City slope acreage — fee-simple, the serviced side.** Single detached homes in the built City neighbourhoods on the west and southwest slopes (Auguston, McKee, Eagle Mountain side) sit on fee-simple lots; where the rural/suburban zones apply, parcel character follows them: Country Residential (CR) minimum lot **2.0 ha**, Suburban Residential (SR) minimum lot **0.4 ha**, Rural Residential (RR) minimum lot **4.0 ha** under Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014. These are zone minimums **as the research states them**, not a claim that every slope parcel is that size — urban slope lots inside the built neighbourhoods sit under their own zones, UNCONFIRMED parcel-by-parcel here. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- **FVRD rural acreage — the unserviced side.** Rural/country-residential homes on larger lots on the upper and eastern mountain sit under the FVRD Sumas Mountain OCP (Bylaw No. 584). The rural part has **no public sewer or water at rural density — homes use wells and onsite (septic) services**. FVRD zoning minimum lot sizes: UNVERIFIED (the bylaw text is not hosted on a readable FVRD source, per the research) — no FVRD lot-size figure is invented here. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014 and (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/sumas-mountain-guide.md, §4)
- **ALR vs non-ALR split:** the City OCP includes an ALR map (Map 16) and shows an **Agriculture designation on the east side of the mountain**; whether any single parcel is in the ALR must be checked parcel by parcel — no ALR percentage split is stated, and no parcel in this file is labelled ALR or non-ALR on area-level evidence. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps

FVRD side: no public water/sewer at rural density — the GWELLS well record and the Fraser Health sewerage filing (system installed by an Authorized Person under the Sewerage System Regulation) are the diligence, before subjects. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration

- **Two governments, two rule sets — confirm which governs the parcel first.** City side: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 (CR 2.0 ha / SR 0.4 ha / RR 4.0 ha minimums above) and Abbotsforward 2050 OCP (**CURRENT**, adopted December 2025; stale "2600-2016"/"2500-2016" citations without that currency are flagged, not repeated). The McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023) is Schedule E of the City OCP and guides the west-slope mountainside neighbourhood. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **SSMUH / suites:** City-side SSMUH adoption is City-confirmed (June 2024) but the bylaw **NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED — "3560-2024" must not be asserted**. Abbotsford suite rules (**Abbotsford ONLY** — suite cap under 50%; coach/garden 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m²) apply on City ground only. Secondary suite / SSMUH rules for the rural FVRD part: UNVERIFIED in the research — do not import Abbotsford's rules onto FVRD ground. (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/sumas-mountain-guide-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** City-side subdivision follows the zone minimums above plus City servicing — and the Abbotsford Development Bylaw **number is HELD UNCONFIRMED** in the program's verified ledger (the process is documented instead of a number; no number is asserted here). FVRD-side: no public water/sewer at rural density (well/septic); FVRD subdivision minimums UNVERIFIED (§2). Detail beyond the minimums: UNCONFIRMED — confirm with the governing authority for the parcel.
- **Steep-slope / geotechnical diligence:** the City OCP includes a Steep Slope Development Permit Area for slope land, and the McKee plan protects unstable geotechnical areas and slopes over 40% as open space. Budget for geotechnical review on slope acreage before subjects come off. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **ALR rules IN FULL (east-side / Agriculture-designated ground, where ALR applies to the parcel):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — note the contrast the lots file draws: Straiton is a lawful bare-land strata because its ground is City mountainside strata ground as documented, NOT an ALR-residence stratification. Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval. ALC residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², a suite within the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on under 0.8 ha; applications by **October 31**. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR and appears nowhere in this file as a rule. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Bare-land strata block (applies in full to a Straiton lot — cross-referenced in §2, not re-documented):** Form B diligence (SPA s.59; fees, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw, and no Straiton age restriction is verified, so it is not marketed as 55+ or all-ages on this file's evidence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Flood position:** the mountain itself is above the floodplain; the floodplain land is at its foot in Sumas Prairie. Slope acreage trades flood risk for geotechnical risk — diligence follows the slope rules above, not floodplain setbacks. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — rates and worked anchors (inputs + tier math stated):** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked examples: $500,000 → $8,000; $700,000 → $12,000; $800,000 → $14,000 (net $6,000 after the maximum $8,000 First-Time Home Buyers' relief, where it applies); $900,000 → $16,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 **including** the further 2% tier on the $500,000 over $3,000,000. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief on a new principal-residence HOME up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a home exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most individual personal-use sales are exempt; business-use / in-the-course-of-business / more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are therefore generally taxable, at that level only. This matters directly for any future McKee-plan lot sold by a developer versus a resale rural parcel from an individual — confirm per transaction. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — an acreage purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy taxes + purchase prohibition:** no Sumas Mountain–specific position is documented in the research — none is imported in this file. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is **Vancouver ONLY**, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for a specific buyer is **not conformed here — UNCONFIRMED** where no primary text was opened.
- **Taxes — TWO regimes on one mountain, do not mix them:** City part — Abbotsford taxes due **July 2, 2026**, 5% penalty **July 3, 2026**, second 5% **September 16, 2026**. FVRD/rural part — the owner receives the notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes and pays the Province, not the FVRD; under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11, rural taxes are due **July 2**, with 5% added to any portion unpaid on July 2 and a **further 5% on any portion unpaid October 31**. Do not quote City dates (or the September 16 date) for an FVRD parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Quarry diligence:** five quarries operate on Sumas Mountain and gravel trucks use an access road to Highway 1 through the Lightning Rock site (Semá:th First Nation statement); a City council report covers a soil removal permit to extract gravel to the 170 m elevation at Jamieson Quarry for Mainland Sand & Gravel Ltd. Form B treatment and PTT/GST treatment on a court-ordered sale: UNCONFIRMED — kept distinct from the CRA GI-003 vacant-land position above. **Registry ledger: Abbotsford HAS a Supreme Court registry** — for the FVRD/rural part of the mountain, which registry serves a specific parcel is not documented in the research and is not asserted here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What kind of acreage actually comes up on Sumas Mountain?** Individual fee-simple parcels: City slope parcels under the CR/SR/RR zones (minimums 2.0 ha / 0.4 ha / 4.0 ha as the research states them) and larger FVRD rural parcels on well and septic. There is no named acreage subdivision selling lots, and no presale acreage program — the McKee lands are a proposal, not a sales program. (§2 above)
- **Q: City parcel or FVRD parcel — how do I tell, and why does it matter?** The governing plan, the servicing, the suite rules and even who you pay taxes to all change at the boundary (City Zoning Bylaw 2400-2014 / Abbotsforward 2050 vs FVRD OCP Bylaw 584 / Surveyor of Taxes). Confirm the parcel's jurisdiction before relying on any rule in this file. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is there water and sewer on the upper mountain?** No — the FVRD rural part has no public water/sewer at rural density; homes use wells and septic. Check the GWELLS well record and the Fraser Health sewerage filing before subjects. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Q: Can I build on a steep McKee-area acreage parcel?** Possibly — but Steep Slope Development Permit Area guidelines apply and slopes over 40% / unstable geotechnical areas are protected open space in the McKee plan, so gross acres overstate buildable acres. Get geotechnical advice before subjects. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **Q: Can I subdivide a large rural parcel?** City-side minimums run 0.4 ha (SR) to 4.0 ha (RR) by zone; FVRD minimums are UNVERIFIED in this file, and any ALR parcel needs ALC approval to subdivide, with ALR residences barred from stratification. No subdivision promise can be made from this file — get the zone and jurisdiction confirmed for the parcel. (§§2–3 above)
- **Q: Is a Straiton lot an acreage lot I can buy fee-simple?** No — it is a bare-land STRATA lot (EPS1299; 26 lots per the City report, documented in the lots-and-land home file): you own a strata lot, pay strata fees, follow the bylaws and get a Form B. A listing's "42 lot" figure for the wider community conflicts with the City's 26 and is UNCONFIRMED. (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/sumas-mountain-guide-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: When will the McKee acreage and lots be for sale?** UNCONFIRMED — the McKee Road lands are a July 2025 council-consideration proposal (Bylaw 3761-2025; ~108 detached lots, ~192 townhouses and four rural residential lots proposed), not a sales program. No presale was verified on the mountain. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2025-07/2025-07-10%20City%20Page.pdf
- **Q: Is an east-side parcel farm land — can I get farm class or build a second farm residence?** The OCP shows an Agriculture designation on the east side and Map 16 is its ALR map, but ALR status is parcel-by-parcel. If the parcel is ALR: farm class still needs the full three-tier income test in §3 (apply by Oct 31), and an additional residence is capped at 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha) under the ALC rules. If it is not ALR, none of those ALC rules apply — the check comes first. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a new lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA GI-003, unlike an individual's personal-use resale of a rural parcel. Confirm per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What are the holding costs on mountain acreage?** Taxes under the correct regime (City: July 2, 2026 / 5% July 3 / 5% September 16, 2026; rural: July 2 / 5% July 2 / further 5% October 31, paid to the Province), plus well/septic maintenance on rural ground, plus strata fees only if the parcel is a Straiton bare-land strata lot. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve Eagle Mountain acreage vs Auguston acreage?** They split — the two settled locator chains are in §1 and they go to different middle/secondary schools, on a single-address basis each. Run your exact address in the SD34 locator. (§1 above)
