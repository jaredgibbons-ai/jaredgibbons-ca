## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500** — Single Detached RS1/RS2 (Section 8.1; minimums in Section 2), suite rules in Section 5.4, and SSMUH zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L, RSM/XL as amended; the City allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. - **Heritage — a lot-level rule in this district:** properties inside the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area need a Heritage Alteration Permit for exterior changes under the Steveston Area Plan, which affects renovation plans and timelines for village-edge lots. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Subdivision and servicing — VERIFIED ledger:** Richmond Subdivision Bylaw 8751 and Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 are VERIFIED (carry exactly). Subdivision runs against the Bylaw 8500 zone minimums and Bylaws 8751/8752; confirm the works required for a specific lot with the City. (Standing servicing ledger.)
- **Flood — Bylaw 8204 governs every lot here:** the district fronts the South Arm of the Fraser River behind the City dike system; Richmond is a low-lying island city in the Fraser River floodplain protected by a perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations, designed for a 1:500 flood event with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres, and the City's Steveston Island Dike project addresses the Steveston waterfront and harbour. Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements — confirm both before pricing in a rebuild or addition on a waterfront or dyke-adjacent lot. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.letstalkrichmond.ca/StevestonIslandDike
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried for completeness; no Steveston South ALR parcel is documented in this file's research:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps: principal residence maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
