## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, R1-1 District Schedule (side streets)** — minimum site areas 306 m² / 464 m² / 557 m² by form as stated in Section 2; a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. - **Plans: Broadway Plan + Vancouver Official Development Plan** — the Broadway Plan (approved June 22, 2022; updates December 2024) covers the corridor's north end and supports buildings up to 20–30 storeys near Mount Pleasant Station; the Vancouver Plan (approved July 22, 2022) was rescinded and replaced by the Vancouver Official Development Plan on March 31, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — VERIFIED:** Vancouver subdivision is governed by the Subdivision By-law No. 5208, whose Schedule A sets minimum parcel width and area standards by district and sub-area category, with the Approving Officer administering applications; its fee schedule was most recently amended for 2026 by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026). A fee-simple split must clear both the R1-1 schedule and the Subdivision By-law category for its block. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **Heritage / renter diligence at the north end:** for older Main Street rental buildings, the Broadway Plan's renter protections and tenant relocation requirements must be factored in before underwriting a redevelopment purchase, and Mount Pleasant's heritage-retention expectations affect the side-street stock the context statement documents. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/mount-pleasant-historic-context-statement.pdf
- **Floodplain diligence — this corridor's own position (no zone imported):** the corridor's north end touches the former False Creek shoreline lowland at Great Northern Way / Terminal, where the City's designated floodplain includes the False Creek coastal floodplain and parcels are subject to the Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements; the residential grid south of 2nd Avenue sits on the Mount Pleasant slope. Whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **ALR — conditional statement only (no ALR ground documented on the Main corridor):** IF a parcel were in the Agricultural Land Reserve, the ALC frame would govern: subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); and farm class would run on the three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met; that line is documented on the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file). None of this is asserted to apply to any corridor lot in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **If a strata purchase is the redirect case (the frontage condos):** the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. None of it applies to a fee-simple lot purchase itself — and none of it applies to an undivided-interest tenure, which has no Form B regime (see the Lee Building flag in Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 fully residential corridor lot purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Property taxes and utilities — as the research documents them:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — APPLIES here (Vancouver ONLY):** owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the tax is 3% of the property's assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year, and the 2025 payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — a live cost for investors holding the corridor's older rental or vacant stock. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there acreage to buy on the Main corridor?** No. There is no acreage subdivision, farm ground or presale lot offering documented — the corridor is a built, densifying spine, and land trades as side-street house lots and frontage parcels. Verified named acreage developments counted here: 0. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is "Main" an official Vancouver neighbourhood?** Not one of the 22 distinct local areas — the City lists "Main" as a recognised smaller area; the corridor runs through Mount Pleasant, Riley Park and Sunset, and Main Street splits the board's Mount Pleasant VE and VW sub-areas. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **Q: How many homes can a side-street lot hold?** Where the R1-1 schedule is met, a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units, and a house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house — minimum site areas run 306 m² to 557 m² by form. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Does the Broadway Plan mean my lot can hold a 20-storey building?** No — the plan supports buildings up to 20–30 storeys near Mount Pleasant Station, but most sites still need rezoning; separate plan expectation from entitled density in every land pitch. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Q: Can I split a corridor house lot into two fee-simple lots?** Only if both resulting parcels clear the R1-1 schedule and the Subdivision By-law No. 5208 Schedule A category for the block, through the Approving Officer. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm
- **Q: Is the Lee Building a strata purchase?** UNCONFIRMED — and flagged OPEN in this file. The standing tenure record is undivided interest / tenants-in-common; the research file names the building only as a heritage landmark and does not settle tenure, and a strata framing of it is UNCONFIRMED-as-strata. Follow the lots-and-land sibling's treatment (tenure record only) and do not price it as strata product until the tenure question is settled. (Section 2; lots-and-land sibling, §2.)
- **Q: When does the Main Street SkyTrain station open?** Mount Pleasant Station, at Main Street and Broadway, is one of six underground stations on the 5.7 km Broadway Subway, scheduled to enter service in fall 2027. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025TT0110-001048
- **Q: Will I pay the Empty Homes Tax on a vacant corridor purchase?** Vancouver's tax is 3% of assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant, with an annual declaration required — run it into holding costs and confirm the seller's declaration status. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a lot purchase?** Only as the CRA GI-003 analysis lands for that vendor and parcel — most individual personal-use vacant-land sales are exempt, business or more-than-two-part subdivided sales can be taxable; UNCONFIRMED per parcel, and the contract must state who bears GST. - **Q: Which schools serve a Main corridor lot?** By exact address only — the corridor crosses at least three elementary catchments in two local areas and no completed locator run is documented here, so run every address in the VSB locator before advertising school appeal. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
