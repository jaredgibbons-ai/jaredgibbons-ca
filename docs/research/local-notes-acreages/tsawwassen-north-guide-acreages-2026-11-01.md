## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Tsawwassen North" is a Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name, not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name, and the City publishes no boundary for it; it is the north side of Tsawwassen along Highway 17 and 52 Street toward Ladner, under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — on the City side only (below). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/215646/ and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **The defining boundary is jurisdictional:** the north side sits beside Tsawwassen First Nation Lands. Tsawwassen First Nation is a self-governing First Nation with jurisdiction over land management on Tsawwassen Lands — over 724 hectares transferred under the Tsawwassen First Nation Final Agreement — so **TFN land follows TFN law, not City of Delta bylaws**, and City zoning does not apply on Tsawwassen Lands. That boundary decides whether a parcel in this file is City fee-simple/strata land or TFN leasehold land, and the two are never mixed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- Main roads: Highway 17 (to the BC Ferries Tsawwassen terminal and to Highway 17A), 52 Street, 56 Street and Canoe Pass Way; Highway 17A leads to Highway 99 and the George Massey Tunnel. Transit: route 609 runs from South Delta Exchange via 56 Street, 12 Avenue, 52 Street and Canoe Pass Way, through Tsawwassen First Nation, to Ladner Exchange; route 601 serves 56 Street and South Delta Exchange toward Bridgeport Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Nearby areas: Tsawwassen Central to the south, Ladner across the farm belt to the north, Tsawwassen First Nation Lands to the west and north. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/tsawwassen-north-guide.md)

Every address must be checked in the locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Jurisdiction first — the rule that decides everything here:** City of Delta zoning and BC fee-simple/freehold-strata law govern the City side. **On Tsawwassen Lands, TFN land follows TFN law, not City of Delta bylaws** — Tsawwassen First Nation is self-governing with jurisdiction over land management on Tsawwassen Lands (724+ hectares under the Final Agreement), and the residential product there is held under 99-year prepaid leases or leasehold prepaid-strata plans. Establish which side of the boundary a parcel sits on, and which tenure document is actually being bought, before pricing anything. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **OCP (City side): Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — consolidated, NO lettered schedules.** The Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; **"Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" is an ERROR wherever met**, swept to zero across the sibling programs. Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". The OCP does not govern Tsawwassen Lands. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted (City side)** (sheets cite No. 7600, 2017; an internal title reads "8400, 2025"). Residential ladder: RS4 610 m², RS5 550/460 m², RS6 390 m², RS7 335 m², RD1 836 m², RD4 504 m² subdivision minimums, parcel by parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **SSMUH (City side only): Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024** — four units on all residential properties in Delta; six within 400 m of frequent transit along Scott Road (North Delta). Added units are not subdivision, and SSMUH does not apply on Tsawwassen Lands. **Subdivision/servicing (City side) — CONFLICT FLAGGED, adoption not asserted:** the sibling lots-and-land files cite the Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 (repealing No. 7162, 2015), under LGA s. 506, in governing terms; the program's verified ledger holds the **adoption of DSSS 8288 (2024) UNCONFIRMED**, so the sibling assertion is noted as a conflict and 8288 is not asserted as governing here. Parcel-specific requirements UNCONFIRMED beyond that. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **ALR rules in full (farm-belt parcels):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239 deposit = subdivision; ALCA s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, including one-residence-per-parcel limits relevant to large lots at the farm edge. ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). **BC Assessment farm class — full three-tier form:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR (the horse-properties page owns that assertion; not this file). No marketing-board source was opened in this research, so quota/board items stay UNVERIFIED site copy. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Flood (City side):** farm and ALR land lies between Tsawwassen and Ladner, and Delta is the diking authority (over 63 km of flood protection, 31 pump stations, ~538 km of drainage). A flood construction level for any specific farm-edge lot: UNCONFIRMED — check DeltaMap and title. - **Property transfer tax (PTT), with worked examples (inputs stated, tier math shown) — City-side frame, TFN application UNCONFIRMED per tenure:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. No exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $700,000 → **$12,000**; $900,000 → **$16,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 fully residential → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 (3% × $1,500,000) + $10,000 (further 2% × $500,000) = **$93,000**. First-time buyers: exempt on first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 (at the $800,000 example: $14,000 gross, $6,000 after the $8,000 FTHB relief). Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. **OPEN reconciliation item:** a townhomes sibling (tsawwassen-homes-over-1-8m) carries $78,000 for the $3,500,000 input; this file follows **$93,000** and flags the conflict. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST / rebates — City-side frame, TFN application UNCONFIRMED per tenure:** GST on vacant land at CRA GI-003 level only: most individual personal-use sales exempt; business-use, in-the-course-of-business and more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable; developer/newly-subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED. - **Strata block (City-side strata only):** Form B (SPA s. 59) — fees, amounts owed, levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules/budget/depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page. - **Speculation/vacancy taxes — the boundary in tax form:** the Province's SVT taxable-areas list includes the City of Delta and states expressly that **reserve lands, treaty lands and lands of self-governing Indigenous Nations are NOT part of the taxable areas** — so Tsawwassen Lands are excluded from SVT taxable areas on the Province's own list, while City-side Delta parcels are in a listed area. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there acreage for sale on the north side?** No verified acreage market — City-side ground is urban detached lots, the farm belt is ALR ground with no parcel roster in the research, and the TFN side is built leasehold housing. A buyer who needs genuine acreage is not shopping in this sub-area. (Section 2 above)
- **Q: What about the big parcels fronting the farm belt — are those acreages?** The research's own wording is the limit: lots fronting the farm belt and Highway 17 corridor **can be larger rural parcels, confirmed parcel by parcel**. - **Q: Can I build a second home or suite on a large farm-edge lot?** On ALR land, Agricultural Land Commission rules — including one-residence-per-parcel limits and the residence caps in Section 3 — can decide the question before the City does; ALR residences cannot be stratified, and subdivision needs ALC approval. On a City-side non-ALR lot, the parcel's zone and SSMUH frame govern instead. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What am I actually buying at Boardwalk or Tsawwassen Landing — the land?** No. You buy a home under a leasehold structure on Tsawwassen Lands (99-year prepaid lease at Boardwalk; leasehold prepaid-strata at Seaside), documented in the land home file. - **Q: Does City of Delta zoning / SSMUH apply to a TFN parcel?** No. TFN land follows TFN law, not City bylaws. Delta's SSMUH bylaws (8394/8395) and zoning ladder govern City-side lots only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
