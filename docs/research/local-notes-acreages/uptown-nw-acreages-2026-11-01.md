## 2. THE ACREAGE ITSELF

**No acreage market exists in Uptown — stated plainly at the top. There is no ALR land, no farm parcel, no named acreage subdivision and no bare-land strata community in this sub-area on any source checked. Uptown is a built-out urban plateau: what trades as "land" here is the individual fee-simple house lot on the RS side streets, priced against its suite/laneway potential and its distance from the Sixth Street core, plus strata apartment ground along the core itself, which is built stock counted in the condos program — not lot or acreage inventory. Verified named acreage subdivisions counted here: 0. The ground's land position is documented once, in its lots-and-land home file (FILE STATUS), and is cross-referenced here, not re-documented.**

- **Fee-simple house lots — the only land form that trades:** the residential side streets off the Sixth Street core are single-detached homes under the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series) of Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 — a mix of the city's older housing stock and later infill. RS-1 (s.310) sets a minimum site area of **557.40 sq. m. (6,000 sq. ft.)** and a floor space ratio of **0.5**. A fee-simple Uptown lot carries no strata, no Form B and no strata fees — title, City bylaws and any registered covenants govern. Added-homes potential is part of the lot's value: the RS districts allow secondary suites, and laneway / carriage houses are regulated under the same bylaw — a standard check on any Uptown house purchase, and documented, permitted suite space is a marketable feature on resale. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf (Research file as above)
