- **Q: Does the first-time buyer exemption help at $1.8M?** No. The first time home buyers' program full exemption caps at a fair market value of $835,000 — far below this band — so a buyer here budgets full property transfer tax: $34,000 at the $1,800,000 floor itself (Section 3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: What parcel checks matter most on a Maple Ridge acreage over $1.8M?** Three, all parcel-specific: ALR status, through the Agricultural Land Commission's maps and online mapping tools; floodplain status on Fraser River frontage (flood construction level 6.7 m geodetic, covenant on title); and escarpment status on the south-western slopes (geotechnical report within 300 m of the crest, detailed report within 100 m). Add the Kanaka Creek watercourse check on creek-adjacent parcels. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/ and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q: Can an acreage home here add a suite or more units?** One secondary suite per lot is permitted inside the single detached building, up to 40% of gross floor area, not in the Floodplain Area — on ALR land the ALC caps in Section 3 govern instead (suite inside the principal residence only; additional residence 90 m² / 186 m² by the 40 ha line). Eligible urban lots may carry up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH rules — eligibility is per-lot, so confirm the lot's zone before pricing the potential. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q: Will I still get the home owner grant on a $2M+ home?** Often reduced or nil: the 2026 grant threshold is $2,075,000, reduced by $5 for each $1,000 of assessed value over it, and $0 for the regular grant over $2,189,000 — a real annual-cost difference between two otherwise similar homes straddling the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Q: How big are lots in this band?** No "typical" lot size for the band is documented on an official source, and none is stated. What is documented is the zone frame: RS-1 minimum 668.0 m² on urban lots, and rural minimums from RS-2 at 0.4 ha to A-3 / A-5 at 8.0 ha (Section 2) — confirm the specific parcel's zone and surveyed area before relying on any size claim. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Q: Which schools serve an acreage address in this band?** Catchments follow the address, not the sub-area name or the price: SD42 has no middle tier (K–7 / 8–12) and its east-zone secondaries are Garibaldi Secondary, Samuel Robertson Technical Secondary and Thomas Haney Secondary — run the exact address in the SD42 locator; no catchment for any specific band address is claimed in this file. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
