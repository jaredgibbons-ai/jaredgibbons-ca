- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for ALR parcels (valley farm land is in the reserve in places):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence (permitted by the ALC without an ALC application where its size rules are met, subject to Township bylaws) capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, with applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, and no such line is asserted in this file or documented in this slug's research file. Farm class is the holding-cost difference between a working valley parcel and an idle one — confirm the parcel's current class and income record before pricing the tax position. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
