## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500** — Single Detached RS1/RS2 (Section 8.1, minimums in Section 2) and SSMUH zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L, RSM/XL as amended. A secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in RS1/RS2 — Section 5.4: minimum 33.0 m², maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m²; the City allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm

Subdivision runs against the Bylaw 8500 zone minimums and Bylaws 8751/8752; confirm the works required for a specific lot with the City. (Standing servicing ledger; a sibling carrying Richmond servicing as UNCONFIRMED is a conforming-pass item.)
- **Flood — Bylaw 8204 governs every lot here:** Richmond is made up of islands within the Fraser River floodplain at an average of 1 metre above sea level, protected by a dike system (49 km of dikes, 39 pump stations) designed to handle a 1:500 flood event, with dikes being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise; new construction is subject to flood construction levels and dike setbacks under the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Aircraft noise:** policy is set out in City Bulletin DEVAPPS-05 and the Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy Area; a street-by-street noise contour finding for the Boyd Park area: UNVERIFIED. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/devapps0534378.pdf
- **ALR — conditional statement only (no ALR ground documented under this label):** IF a parcel were in the Agricultural Land Reserve, the ALC frame would govern: subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); and farm class would run on the three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met; that line is documented only on the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file). None of this is asserted to apply to any Boyd Park lot. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **If a strata purchase is made instead (redirect case — e.g. Apple Greene in the condos sibling):** the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw; disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. None of it applies to a fee-simple lot purchase itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 fully residential Boyd Park-area lot purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**.
