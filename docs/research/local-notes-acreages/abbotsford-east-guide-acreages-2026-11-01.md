## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs.** Urban zones as the research states them: RS3 (minimum interior lot 540 m²), RS5 (420 m²), RS6 compact-lot. Agricultural zones city-wide, for any parcel zoned that way: A1 minimum subdivision lot area 8.0 ha, A2 minimum 16.0 ha, as the research states them. The zone section for the specific parcel — not the neighbourhood name — decides whether a subdivision, a suite or added units are possible at all. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/ and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **OCP: Abbotsforward 2050 is CURRENT** (adopted December 2025). The research file's "Bylaw No. 2600-2016, as updated" citation and any live-page "2500-2016" citation are STALE / conflicted — apply 2050 and flag both numbers as a Jerry sweep item wherever a page carries them. The McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023) sits under it as Schedule E for the mountainside. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **SSMUH / added units:** in June 2024 Council adopted Zoning Bylaw amendments implementing provincial small-scale multi-unit housing — June 2024 adoption is City-confirmed; the SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED ("3560-2024" is asserted in research files and must not be asserted — mark it UNCONFIRMED wherever met; both bases are stated here and the correction governs). Zoning Bylaw s.140 carries the small-scale rules and s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings, with City-report criteria including a lot of at least 400 m², at least 12.0 m frontage, a Collector or Local road and not on a cul-de-sac bulb. Abbotsford's suite cap is under 50% of the principal dwelling, and a coach/garden suite is capped at 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m² — Abbotsford's rules ONLY. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer against the zone's minimum lot size/width/depth and the City's works-and-services requirements, as City council reports apply them file by file (servicing agreement and Works and Services Report are the standard instruments). HELD UNCONFIRMED: the Abbotsford Development Bylaw number — the process is documented instead of a number; a 2021 draft and 2022 references both circulate, so detailed servicing requirements for a specific subdivision are UNCONFIRMED until the City's current Development Bylaw text and the file's report are read. Never quote a servicing standard from a listing. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58305/
- **ALR RULES IN FULL, wherever ALR ground exists:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, stated as the ALC states it. ALR residences cannot be stratified — the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). ALC residence caps: one principal residence of 500 m² or less per parcel; a secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. East-side application: parcel-level ALR status is UNCONFIRMED in the built neighbourhoods (Section 2) — confirm on the ALC map before pricing any parcel as subdividable or stratifiable. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, with applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR; no such flat line is asserted in this file, and none is documented in this area's research file (that flat line is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, not this group). https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Floodplain and slope:** (1) low land at the south/southeast edge toward Sumas Prairie is floodplain land by the Sumas River; City flood notices name properties south of the Sumas River and along Whatcom Road in evacuation order and alert areas, and the OCP includes a Drainage and Flood Control map (Map 12) — check the parcel in the City's WebMap. (2) The OCP applies a Steep Slope Development Permit Area to slope land, and the McKee plan's open-space network includes geotechnical areas and slopes over 40% — budget for a geotechnical report on hillside acreage. Clayburn Village lots are additionally protected through a Heritage Conservation Area in the OCP. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **If a parcel is bare-land strata (redirect case):** the BC strata block applies to the LOT — Form B diligence (strata fee on the lot, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage, insurance summary; the strata must provide it within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. No east-side bare-land strata acreage is verified (Section 2); this block governs the Downes Road Estates redirect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on acreage:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 fully residential acreage purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 with the FTHB exemption applied to the first $500,000); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling in this program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000 and flag the conflict wherever met. Exemptions are home-based with a land route each: under the First-Time Home Buyers' Program a buyer who registers a vacant lot and builds can keep the exemption if lot value at registration plus construction cost is $835,000 or less (partial under $860,000), building and moving in by the first anniversary; under the Newly Built Home Exemption, PTT paid on a vacant lot can be refunded once a qualifying newly built home is constructed ($1,100,000 full / $1,150,000 partial). Land bought to hold gets neither. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at the CRA GI-003 level:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts. Anything finer for a specific parcel — farmland in particular, which the CRA treats in a separate info sheet — is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and parcel history are established. Get the GST position in the contract. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) rebates GST on new HOUSING — 100% to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. It does NOT apply to bare land itself. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account: $8,000 per year, $40,000 lifetime. A live page's $1.25M straight-line phase-out row for the FTHB GST rebate is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism where met — the phase-out runs to $1.5M as CRA states it. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax:** the Province's taxable-areas list names the City of Abbotsford — owners of residential-class property must complete the annual declaration. Application to a specific acreage parcel (assessment class, exemptions) is UNCONFIRMED until its class is read. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is City of Vancouver ONLY and does not apply in Abbotsford. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal prohibition on purchase by non-Canadians:** this file states ONLY what its cited sources support. The March 27, 2023 regulatory amendments repealed the provision that had made vacant land zoned residential or mixed-use inside a census metropolitan area or census agglomeration "residential property" — as amended, vacant land itself can be purchased by a non-Canadian, including for residential development, per law-firm summaries of the amendments. The prohibition still governs dwellings; application to a specific purchase is UNCONFIRMED at the official-text level in this file (no primary text was opened in the research). https://www.mross.com/what-we-think/article/update-canadian-government-prohibits-non-canadians-from-purchasing-residential-real-estate
- **Foreclosure / court-ordered sale — where met:** BC court-ordered sales follow the settled Supreme Court process (Supreme Court Civil Rules 21-7 / 13-5, Practice Direction PD-66, Form 60). Whether Form B diligence and the PTT/GST treatment on a specific court sale apply as they do on a market sale is UNCONFIRMED in this file and is kept distinct from the CRA GI-003 vacant-land position above. REGISTRY LEDGER: Abbotsford HAS a Supreme Court registry. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/168_2009_07
- **Property taxes / holding cost:** City of Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I actually buy an acreage in Abbotsford East?** Yes — as an individual fee-simple parcel toward Whatcom Road / the Sumas Mountain foot, not from a named acreage subdivision: no named acreage subdivision, bare-land strata acreage community or presale acreage offering was verified for this sub-area on an official or developer source. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is an east-side acreage serviced, or on well and septic?** Inside the built subdivisions, parcels are urban — but the research file confirms no servicing statement for any individual parcel and lists septic/well use in the urban east side as unverified. For a Whatcom-side parcel, confirm water/sewer connection status with the City parcel by parcel: UNCONFIRMED for any single parcel until the City record is read. (Research file, UNVERIFIED item 7.)
- **Q: How big is a typical east-side acreage, and can I split one?** No typical acreage size is documented — do not rely on one. The documented floors are the zone minimums (RS3 540 m², RS5 420 m² interior; A1 8.0 ha / A2 16.0 ha where agriculturally zoned), so a purchase has subdivision room only if the parcel is comfortably larger than twice its zone minimum and meets frontage, road and servicing requirements through the Approving Officer — and, in the ALR, only with ALC approval. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/
- **Q: What extra units can go on an acreage parcel?** On urban ground: one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings (s.140.3.8), subject to the 400 m² / 12.0 m / Collector-or-Local / no-cul-de-sac-bulb criteria; suite under 50% of the principal dwelling; coach/garden suite 15% of lot area to 100 m² maximum. On ALR ground the ALC caps replace those: principal 500 m², suite inside the principal only, additional residence 90 m² / 186 m² by the 40 ha line. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is this parcel in the ALR — and why does it matter?** Check the parcel on the ALC map: the research file does not confirm ALR status parcel by parcel inside the built neighbourhoods. If it is in the ALR, subdivision needs ALC approval, residences cannot be stratified, and the ALC residence caps govern. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can I keep farm class / farm taxes on a small acreage?** Only on the full three-tier test — $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha (over 4 ha), $10,000 (under 0.8 ha) — with an application by October 31. Acreage alone does not qualify; a flat "$2,500+" quote understates the test. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Q: What taxes do I pay on the acreage itself?** PTT at the general rates (worked example in Section 3: $1.5M → $28,000, no exemption), with the first-time / newly-built exemptions reachable only through the build-and-occupy routes. GST turns on the seller, not the land: an individual's personal-use sale is generally exempt per CRA GI-003; a business sale or a more-than-two-part subdivision sale can be taxable — have the contract state the GST position. Abbotsford is a speculation and vacancy tax area for residential-class property. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What will it cost to hold the acreage while I plan?** Property taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3, second 5% September 16, 2026), plus insurance and any covenant/servicing obligations on title. No strata fee on a fee-simple parcel; a bare-land strata parcel would carry one plus a Form B — which is one way to test a "bare-land strata" claim. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Q: What are the hillside and flood risks on an east-side acreage?** Slope/geotechnical conditions on McKee/Eagle Mountain ground (Steep Slope Development Permit Area; McKee open-space network includes slopes over 40%) and flood exposure on low parcels toward Sumas Prairie (City notices name properties south of the Sumas River and along Whatcom Road). Price both checks into the offer timeline. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie
- **Q: Which schools would a home on this acreage feed into?** Catchments are address-based in SD34 and only two locator runs are settled for the east side, both on a single-address basis: 36367 Stephen Leacock Drive returns Auguston Traditional Elementary → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary, and 3836 Old Clayburn Road returns Sandy Hill Elementary → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary (SD34 School Locator, mybaragar.com, run 2026-11-01). Any other parcel needs its own run. https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments
