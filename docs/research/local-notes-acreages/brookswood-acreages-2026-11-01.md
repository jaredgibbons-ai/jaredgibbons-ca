## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Plan and zoning — Township ONLY:** the Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan covers Brookswood-Fernridge, with Booth, Fernridge and Rinn Neighbourhood Plans under it; the Township states the plan allows previously undeveloped areas to grow while protecting trees and watercourses. Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs; minimum lot sizes (s.110.1, as reproduced in a Township council report): SR-1 — 3,716 m² / 40 m frontage; SR-2 — 8,094 m² / 45 m; SR-3 — 1,765 m² / 27.45 m. City of Langley bylaws do NOT apply here. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/brookswoodfernridge-community-plan-adopted.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024):** 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible, eligibility requires municipal water and sewer, and **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020**. The Township's June 10, 2024 council report estimates only ~15% of Brookswood's single-family/duplex lots eligible for 3–4 units, varying by servicing. This is SSMUH/subdivision potential as the governing Township states it. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Suites and coach houses — Township:** secondary suite cap 120 m². A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata. **LIVE CONFLICT — flagged, not resolved:** s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted; neither position is adopted here. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **R-CL compact lots — Township s.403:** minimum lot area 220 m² to maximum LOT area 464.5 m². A "355 m²" figure is nowhere in the bylaw and is not used in this file. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — Township:** all subdivisions must be approved by the Approving Officer under the Land Title Act, through application, review, decision letter, conditions, final approval and registration. Servicing is determined under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED (the verified ledger entry for the Township of Langley). No other city's servicing number is used for Brookswood ground. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — parcel status in Brookswood is parcel-specific, and the full frame governs wherever a parcel is in the reserve:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps as the ALC states them: a principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, and no more than 1,000 m² of fill can be placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted ONLY when located within the principal residence, entirely contained within it; one additional residence, detached from the principal residence, is permitted up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. Whether a specific Brookswood parcel is in the reserve or a floodplain is UNVERIFIED — the Township directs applicants to check designations in GeoSource. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — applications by the legislated deadline of October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page only; it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **BC strata block (for any bare-land strata lot cross-referenced in §2):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). - **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies to a qualifying home purchase); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland caution per CRA's separate info sheet applies where a parcel is farmland. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
