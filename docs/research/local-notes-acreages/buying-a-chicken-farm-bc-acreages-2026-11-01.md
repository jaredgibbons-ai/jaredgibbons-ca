## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **ALR RULES IN FULL (the governing frame for this topic — carried in full):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha); local government bylaws may further regulate or restrict residences and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. The live guide's statement that ALR rules generally allow barns, a principal residence and, with restrictions, additional dwellings is consistent with this frame at that general level. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
