## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Chilliwack 2050, Bylaw No. 5500** — the current city-wide plan; this ground sits in the Agricultural Area Plan, Schedule B1. (Other Chilliwack schedules named program-wide — Sardis B5, Eastern Hillsides B4, Chilliwack Proper B-2, Downtown B3 + D-6 — are not this ground and are not imported.) https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf
- **Zoning: AL (Agricultural Lowland) under Zoning Bylaw 2020 No. 5000 (§6 farm zones where met)** — minimums in §2. Confirm the parcel's zone before assuming any farm or residential use. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **ALR RULES IN FULL:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps: one principal residence ≤500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels ≤40 ha and 186 m² on parcels >40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Stated expressly: the flat form is NOT documented in East Chilliwack's own research — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **SSMUH: Chilliwack Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024** — small-scale multi-unit housing is a city frame, not an acreage lever on ALR farm ground, where the ALC housing caps and AL zone govern. No SSMUH lot creation is priced into an ALR parcel here.
- **Subdivision and servicing — number UNCONFIRMED, process documented:** the candidate "Chilliwack Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" is NOT adopted and is never presented as verified. Subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer / Preliminary Layout Approval process as set out in the City's Subdivision Application Guide; for ALR parcels ALC approval is required in addition.
- **Floodplain:** Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets minimum building setbacks of 60 m from the Fraser River, 75 m from the Vedder River and 30 m from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal; the City maintains a diking network for the Fraser and Vedder systems. Which East Chilliwack watercourses carry parcel-level setbacks is a per-property check: UNVERIFIED. Additional taxes are levied for drainage and dyking on top of municipal rates. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M lives in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is an OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant / farm land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland is addressed by CRA in a separate sheet and is usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — never assume a farm parcel is GST-exempt on the vacant-land rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED; the contract must state the GST position for the specific parcel. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What am I actually buying as an "acreage" in East Chilliwack?** A whole fee-simple farm or rural-residential parcel, typically in the AL zone — rural houses on larger lots, hobby farms, equestrian properties and working farm ground as the page and the Agricultural Area Plan describe them. There is no acreage subdivision or bare-land strata product to buy instead; verified named developments: 0. (§2.)
- **Q: Is this parcel in the ALR?** Per-parcel only — check ALC mapping. The research confirms no parcel's status. If it is in: subdivision needs ALC approval, the residence cannot be stratified, and the housing caps in §3 apply. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Q: Can I subdivide it?** Only within the AL minimums — 7.5 ha for ALR-excluded/exempted land or ALC-approved subdivision, 1 ha for a boundary adjustment, 0.2 ha for an ALC-approved homesite severance, 25 m minimum width — and, in the ALR, only with ALC approval, through the City's Approving Officer / Preliminary Layout Approval process. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: Can I stratify it for family later?** No, if it is ALR — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Where can a suite go?** Inside the principal residence only, on ALR land. A detached second dwelling is the "additional residence," capped at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What farm income keeps farm class?** All three tiers: $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A "$2,500+"-only answer understates the test. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Is the parcel serviced?** UNCONFIRMED for any single parcel in the research — verify well and septic records for that parcel before removing subjects. (§2.)
- **Q: What flood setbacks apply?** 60 m Fraser River, 75 m Vedder River, 30 m Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal under Bylaw 4519; which watercourses affect a given parcel is a per-property check, and drainage/dyking levies sit on top of municipal taxes. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a farm acreage?** Do not assume either way — CRA's vacant-land sheet exempts most individual personal-use sales, but farmland is treated in a separate sheet as usually taxable with limited exemptions. UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel until vendor status and farm use are applied; the contract must state it. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve my address?** SD33 catchments are address-based and no locator run was recorded for an East Chilliwack address — UNVERIFIED. The schools named for the area are East Chilliwack Elementary (49190 Chilliwack Central Road) and Cheam Elementary (9895 Banford Road), and a new 500-space east-side elementary at 10125 McGrath Road is estimated for April 2027 occupancy. Run the exact address in the SD33 locator. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
