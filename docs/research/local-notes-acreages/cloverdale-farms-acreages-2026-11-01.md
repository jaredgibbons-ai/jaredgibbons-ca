## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for this whole topic:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence — a separate or detached dwelling cannot be used as a secondary suite, and local bylaws must also permit suites; one additional detached residence permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — maximum 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha — and it does not need to be intended for farm help, provided local bylaws are met. Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR (number, size, siting), and building more than the permitted number, or larger than the size limits, requires ALC approval. One hectare = 2.471 acres. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.nrcan.gc.ca/maps-tools-publications/tools/geodetic-reference-systems/converting-units-measurement/10798
- **ALR short-term accommodation:** whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence is not permitted outright in the ALR and requires ALC approval; bed-and-breakfast use inside a lived-in principal residence is permitted within the ALC's four-bedroom / 30-day limits. The City layer sits on top (§ below) — neither layer alone permits a farm-house Airbnb. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 if the total area of the farm operation is less than 0.8 ha; applications must be submitted by the legislated deadline of October 31, for consideration for the next assessment and tax year. **The live page's "$2,500 to $10,000+" summary is site copy and is NOT adopted here** — and a flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (the HORSE-PROPERTIES page owns it); it is never attributed to this file. Protect the farm-class position: check the parcel's own farm income against the tiers before pricing a parcel on its farm-class tax treatment. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **City frame:** OCP = **Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026)** — never 18020 as current; Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 (a parcel's zone confirmed in the City's mapping — the page's A-1 / A-2 naming is UNVERIFIED on a City source, §2). Servicing: **Subdivision and Development Bylaw No. 8830 (1986), VERIFIED on the City's own pages** — for an ALR parcel, ALC approval is required in addition. Streamside setbacks in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated.
- **Suites / coach houses — urban contrast, ALR rule governs on farm ground:** Surrey's urban rule is one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most **40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area**, never inside a coach house or garden suite and not a separate strata lot; coach houses / garden suites run 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR) with one extra parking space. On ALR land the ALC rule above is the stricter frame (suite inside the principal residence only). **Whether a Surrey coach house can serve as the ALR "additional residence" on a given parcel is a parcel-level ALC + City check: UNVERIFIED as a general rule.** Surrey SSMUH (3 / 4 / up to 6 units by lot band, Jul 8, 2024) is an urban-lot framework — it is not a route to extra dwellings on ALR farm parcels. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Short-term rentals (City layer):** permitted, **owner-only** — only an owner who lives at the property as their primary residence may host; tenants cannot operate one. Licence figure carried as **$358 per year** per the City's Short-Term Rentals page, with the conflict disclosed: the City's 2024 corporate report proposed **$350 per year plus fees** — confirm the current figure before quoting it. On ALR land the ALC approval requirement above applies first. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on farm acreage:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program (south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m) and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on farmland — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; farmland is the caution — the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a SEPARATE info sheet and treats farmland sales as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions, so a Cloverdale farm purchase must never be assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file, and the contract must state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 noted in-line in some sources — March 13 governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. On ALR land any qualifying new home must first fit the ALC caps above. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **BC strata block — where a strata form is met at all:** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). ALR farm parcels themselves are freehold and cannot be stratified (§ above) — the block is carried for completeness where a buyer cross-shops strata product, not as a farm rule. Pad ground is NOT lot stock / land / acreage; pad tenancy percentages only (2026 = 2.3% + proportionate; 2027 = 2.2% + proportionate), NO pad-rent dollar figure as fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
