## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **The process — what this slug's own research read on an official source:** a proceeding for foreclosure of the equitable right to redeem mortgaged property, or for redemption, must be started by petition in the Supreme Court of British Columbia — Rule 21-7 (1). All persons whose interest in or claim to the mortgaged property is sought to be extinguished, and all persons against whom relief is sought, must be made respondents — Rule 21-7 (2); a petitioner may also join a claim arising out of the mortgage or collateral security and a person liable to pay the mortgage debt — Rule 21-7 (3). If the petitioner registers a certificate of pending litigation, a person who subsequently registers an interest need not be served, is bound by an order made in the proceeding, and may file a response to petition — Rule 21-7 (4). A petitioner who serves the petition must include the informational notice for foreclosure proceedings in Form 125 — Rule 21-7 (4.1). The court may make a final order of foreclosure — Rule 21-7 (5)(a); order payment of what is due within a redemption period the court may fix, with foreclosure of the equity of redemption in default — Rule 21-7 (5)(b); and determine summarily, or order an account taken and certified, the amount due — Rule 21-7 (5)(c). The redemption period has no fixed length in the Rule text read: it is the period the court fixes. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How does a BC foreclosure start?** By petition in the Supreme Court of British Columbia under Rule 21-7 (1); everyone whose interest in the property is sought to be extinguished must be made a respondent, and the petitioner must serve the court's foreclosure information notice, Form 125, with the petition. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: How long is the redemption period?** There is no fixed length in the Rule text read in this slug's pass — the redemption period is the period the court fixes in its order under Rule 21-7 (5)(b). Ask which stage the file is at before spending on inspections: the court may also already make a final order of foreclosure under Rule 21-7 (5)(a). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
