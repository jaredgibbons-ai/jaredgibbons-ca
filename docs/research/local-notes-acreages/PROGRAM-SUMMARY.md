## Format adaptations for acreages (settled in A1 and held program-wide)

- **§2 is THE ACREAGE ITSELF, not a building roster.** Named acreage subdivisions and developments are documented by name only where an official/developer source verifies them. **Bare-land strata acreage communities are documented ONCE, in their lots-and-land home files — acreage files cross-reference, never re-document or re-count.** Fee-simple parcels are the default form; industrial ground is treated as industrial parcels in process terms, with no residential acreage market asserted; assemblies, development sites and rezoning proposals are documented **AS SUCH, never as inventory**; presale LOT offerings are counted separately, never mixed with resale stock.
- **The ALR split governs farm files.** ALR vs non-ALR is stated as each file's own research documents it; parcel sizes and zones are carried AS THE RESEARCH STATES; hobby farm is distinguished from working farm only as the research supports; farm operations are described in process terms only — generic feeds are NEVER presented as confirmed operations in a named sector.
- **§3 carries the full ALR block wherever ALR or farm-adjacent ground exists:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision — ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); caps — principal residence ≤500 m², a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence, an additional residence capped at 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); farm classification in the full three-tier form ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the actual value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an error wherever met.
- **Marketing-board discipline (slug-precise).** Board items (quota, transfers, certification) are carried ONLY where that file's own research opened a board source — see the board-source outcome below. Every other farm file carries board items UNVERIFIED on its own research.
- **Rules held to primary sources.** The servicing-bylaw ledger is the lots-and-land program's verified ledger, followed unchanged (Township of Langley 5382 CONFIRMED in the bylaw text; Coquitlam 3558, 2003; New Westminster 7142, 2007; Richmond 8751/8752; Vancouver 5208 (fees 14538); Surrey 8830 (1986); Maple Ridge 4800-1993 (City source); Mission DSCB 5650-2017 (City source); City of North Vancouver 8014 cited in City files ONLY; HELD UNCONFIRMED: Abbotsford Development, Chilliwack (candidate 3055 NOT adopted — the Approving Officer / Preliminary Layout Approval process documented instead), Delta DSSS 8288 adoption, District of North Vancouver + District of West Vancouver servicing, Burnaby base (renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024), Pitt Meadows / Port Moody / Port Coquitlam servicing, FVRD numbers, District of Kent zoning + servicing, and the Anmore tax-rate tie-break (Bylaw No. 740-2026; 0.68552 vs 0.60480 OPEN)). ## Method note

Each acreages file was built on its area's research file plus **all** sibling program files for the same ground (55-plus, condos, townhomes, lots-and-land — read from disk, not from briefs), with official/primary sources first. The program's working discipline is **SOURCE/DISK governs**: where a brief line, a sibling's framing or a research file's shorthand conflicted with a primary source or with the disk, the source/disk governed and the file says so — the named precedents are Brookswood (the R-CL s.403 correction and the Bylaw 6020 6-plex correction), riverwood (Port Coquitlam, not Surrey), boulevard (the City/District straddle at Highway 1), Queensborough (floodplain Zone 1), Cliff Drive (Tsawwassen, not South Surrey), the Lee Building (the disk's tenure record over the sibling's finer claim), the poultry-farms slug settlement (board sources belong to that slug alone), Montecito (its own locator run over the Sperling shorthand), Hockaday (Coquitlam), the Campbell Valley land-home (its lots file exists), the Brownstones sibling placement (the listing-evidence entry lives in the condos sibling; the townhomes sibling carries QUEUE with 0 counted), and the Cultus Lake land-home (the cultus-lake-south lots file; no plain cultus-lake lots file exists). Standing corrections banked by the earlier programs were carried into every acreages file without re-asking: Surrey 2050 OCP 21900 replacing 18020; Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 No. 14815, never 4742; Mission's flat-10% tax penalty (the 5%+5% figures in Mission-area files are the separate rural regime); the New Westminster RT-2–RT-2C + RT-3/3A/3B position; the corrected floodplain ledger (Queensborough Zone 1; Quay + Downtown NW Zone 3; Brunette Zone 4; North Arm Zone 2; Fraserview NW its own position; Glenbrooke North + Queen's Park UNVERIFIED with no zone imported); Pitt Meadows' "over 95%" floodplain figure (the five guide pages' "over 85%" is a live conflict between two City sources, flagged in every Pitt Meadows file; the notes retire to 95%); and the Neilsen Grove spelling lock (AREA "Neilsen Grove," SCHOOL "Neilson Grove Elementary").
