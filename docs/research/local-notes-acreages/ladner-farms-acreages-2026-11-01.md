## 2. THE ACREAGE ITSELF

**What acreage trades on this topic: whole fee-simple farm parcels and bare agricultural land under the OCP Agriculture (A) designation and A1 zoning across the Ladner Rural / East Delta / Westham Island belt, with ALC rules layered over A1 where a parcel is in the ALR. - **Fee-simple parcels are the only farm form.** The research farm profile: working farms and bare agricultural land under Agriculture (A) / A1, farm parcels with greenhouses and on-farm processing as accessory uses, equestrian operations within the A1 stall limit, and operations using migrant farm worker housing under the A1 criteria. Tenure is overwhelmingly freehold; no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **A1 residential limits AS THE RESEARCH STATES:** one farm house per lot, maximum total floor area 500 m²; farm home plate maximum 3,600 m² for a farm house with or without an accessory farm dwelling unit (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate). One accessory farm dwelling unit per lot: maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less, 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, on the same farm home plate as the farm house; ALC approval is required for more than one. An additional farm house (180 m² maximum on lots under 8 ha, 233 m² on lots of 8 ha or more) requires a Professional Agrologist's report supporting its necessity and written ALC approval. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **A1 accessory uses AS THE RESEARCH STATES:** farming is a principal use; accessory uses include a farm house accessory to farming, one secondary suite accessory to a farm house, an accessory farm dwelling unit, migrant farm worker housing, and equestrian facilities to a maximum of 40 permanent stalls accessory to farming. Migrant farm worker housing is permitted only where, among other criteria, the lot is in the ALR with farming as a permitted use, the lot is classified as a farm under the Assessment Act, written ALC approval has been obtained, the farm employing the workers is at least 8 ha and the lot is at least 4 ha; new buildings for it must be manufactured homes on a temporary foundation or footings. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), single consolidated plan, NO lettered schedules.** Farm land is designated Agriculture (A) (s. 1.5.1, Map 2 — purpose: maintain agricultural lands and promote a thriving agricultural sector); the OCP's Agriculture policies (s. 2.6) protect the ALR and soil-based agriculture. Any "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" citation is an error — the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the superseded Bylaw 3950. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Zoning — NUMBER CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted:** City pages cite Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017, while an internal title says "8400, 2025"; the A1 zone content in §2 is stated from the City's A1 zone sheet as cited (Part 10, as amended by Bylaw 8285, 2023), parcel by parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for ALR parcels in the belt:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps as the ALC states them: land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence is capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR — Delta's A1 plate and dwelling rules in §2 are that further regulation. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Subdivision of farm land (A1, s. 10.10.8):** subdivision is not permitted except where it complies with the OCP Bylaw and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable; the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is **8 ha**, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31 for the following taxation year. Leased land can qualify where the owner applies, a lease is in place, the leased land makes a reasonable contribution to the farm operation, and the lessee-farmer meets the income requirements. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR — stated expressly: the flat form is NOT documented in this topic's research (this research states the full three-tier form itself); the flat error is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **SSMUH: Delta Bylaws 8394 + 8395, adopted June 24, 2024** — up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies. SSMUH is not a farm-belt lever: on A1/ALR ground the A1 zone and ALC rules govern, and SSMUH creates no farm lots and no farm strata. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Subdivision servicing:** subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer against the zone minimums and works-and-services conditions, plus ALC approval in the ALR. Farmland is the caution: farmland sales must never be assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Ladner farmland the same market as East Delta?** On this page, yes in practice: the live feed carried both Ladner Rural and East Delta sub-area listings, and both sit under the same rules — the OCP Agriculture (A) designation and A1 zoning where the zone applies. East Delta here is the Ladner-side farm belt, not North Delta. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-farms.html
- **Q: Is this parcel in the ALR — and what changes if it is?** Check the parcel itself on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. If it is in: subdivision needs ALC approval, the residence cannot be stratified, and the housing caps are a 500 m² principal residence (with a 1,000 m² fill limit), a suite inside that residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land near Ladner?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area; Delta's A1 zone sets the same 500 m² farm house maximum and a 3,600 m² farm home plate maximum (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can a Ladner-area farm have a second dwelling?** Yes, within limits: one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot — 90 m² or less on lots of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger lots — on the same farm home plate as the farm house. More than one needs ALC approval; an additional farm house needs a Professional Agrologist's necessity report plus written ALC approval — a heavier test. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Delta farm parcel?** Only within tight limits: a new A1 lot needs a minimum of 8 ha, OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, apart from the home site severance exception under ALC policy. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: What income does a farm need to keep farm classification?** All three tiers: $2,500 for an operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31 for the following tax year. A lease to a local farmer can carry the income test where the BC Assessment conditions are met. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Can I board horses or run a kennel on A1 land?** Equestrian facilities to a maximum of 40 permanent stalls are an accessory use to farming in A1, and the A1 zone is intended for farming, breeding pets, kennels and related uses — parcel-level confirmation with the City is still required, and whether any specific operation holds quota or board status is UNVERIFIED (no board source was opened in this research). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: What about water for irrigation?** Non-domestic groundwater use (including irrigation) requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act, with fees and rentals, before lawful use. The parcel's ditch/drainage irrigation arrangements with the City: UNVERIFIED — get them from the City, not from a listing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a Ladner farm purchase?** Do not assume either way. The CRA's vacant-land sheet (GI-003) makes most individual personal-use sales exempt, but a working farm parcel is the fact pattern where buyers get surprised. UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel until vendor status and farm use are applied; the contract must state who bears GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Farm classification is earned by income, not by zoning.** A parcel can be A1-zoned, Agriculture (A)-designated and in the ALR and still lose farm class if the operation (or its lease) cannot meet BC Assessment's size-scaled gross income thresholds — sellers who document the farm income history, leases, water licences and ALC approvals in place hold the items a farm buyer's lender and lawyer ask for first. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **The farm home plate is the hidden cap.** Beyond the 500 m² house limit, Delta's A1 zone caps the whole farm home plate at 3,600 m² (5,000 m² with an additional farm house on it) — the plate, not the lot, is what a large-house-plus-yard plan actually runs into. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Migrant worker housing has a two-size test most buyers miss.** The employing farm must be at least 8 ha AND the lot at least 4 ha, with ALR status, farm classification and written ALC approval all in place — and new buildings must be manufactured homes on a temporary foundation or footings. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
