## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Read a Surrey parcel in layers, in this order:** the current OCP designation first (the Surrey 2050 update has rescinded or superseded some older NCP designations — East Newton and West Cloverdale are the documented examples), then the GLUP/NCP/LAP for the neighbourhood, then the current zoning under Bylaw No. 12000 — the plan shows where the City wants growth; the zoning is what can be built as-of-right today. Do not price a lot off its plan tile alone: several tiles predate both the Surrey 2050 OCP update and the July 8, 2024 SSMUH rezoning. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps and https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html
- **OCP currency:** the current OCP is Surrey 2050, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026). Bylaw 18020 is the REPLACED OCP and is never cited as current in this file. One tile verified at City-source level in this slug's research: Darts Hill (approved May 10, 2021; designations incorporated into the OCP). South Westminster currency: on March 10, 2025 Council endorsed Corporate Report R048, concluding the plan update for the predominantly industrial and employment portion of that plan area and identifying a Waterfront District Study Area and a Transit Village Study Area at Scott Road Station — the live page's South Westminster tile describes the original NCP area only, so the tile is behind the City's current position. The remaining tiles' detailed summaries (approval dates, population figures, plan-area sizes) were not opened in the plan documents themselves in this pass: UNVERIFIED as stated beyond the City-level facts above. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/south-westminster
- **SSMUH interaction:** Surrey adopted its provincial small-scale multi-unit housing zoning change on July 8, 2024, when Zoning Bylaw No. 12000's residential zones R1 to R7 replaced the RF, RH and RQ zones (RF-12/RF-12C became R4; RF-SD became R6) — the zoning now does the small-lot permitting that older NCP designations predate, so a plan tile and the current zone can point in different directions on the same lot. SSMUH is an urban-lot frame, not a farm route. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html
- **Secondary suites:** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite, and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area. A "90 m² cap" is NOT current and is never stated as current in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Subdivision / servicing:** Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 1986, No. 8830 VERIFIED (City source) — the servicing frame for any Surrey subdivision or bare-land servicing question. Parcel-level requirements are confirmed with the City. (Standing servicing ledger.)
- **Streamside setbacks:** streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated in this file, for any parcel, on any stream. A creek-side farm or acreage parcel's setback is read from Part 7A for its stream class with the City. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Short-term rentals — permitted, owner-only, licence figure in CONFLICT:** short-term rentals are permitted in Surrey only for an owner who lives at the property as their principal residence; tenants cannot run one, and a business licence is required first. Licence figure carried as **$358/yr — CONFLICT DISCLOSED:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350/yr plus fees; this file carries $358 with the $350 proposal disclosed, and the figure is checked direct to the City licence page before quoting either number. (Standing Surrey position; lots-and-land sibling §3.)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — Surrey has extensive ALR ground, so the full frame governs every ALR parcel in the city:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan IS a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — never the shorthand "cannot be stratified," and Bulletin 05 is not cited for stratification. Residence caps as the ALC states them: a principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less; a secondary suite is permitted ONLY when located within the principal residence; one additional residence is permitted up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, and building more than the permitted number, or larger than the size limits, requires ALC approval. City zoning and the ALC frame both apply; neither displaces the other. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices of $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer who plans to continue farming must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by the horse-properties page only); it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Quota, supply management and barn standards — UNVERIFIED in every particular in THIS file:** no marketing-board or CFIA source was opened in this slug's research, so no quota, board-transfer or barn-standard position is stated for any Surrey operation. (BOARD DISCIPLINE in FILE STATUS.)

- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies to a qualifying home purchase); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a SEPARATE info sheet and treats farmland sales as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — no Surrey farm parcel is assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — CRA governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
