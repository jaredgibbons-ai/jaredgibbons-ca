## 2. THE ACREAGE ITSELF

**Honest position, stated plainly: Willoughby Heights has no acreage market. It is a built-out and still-building urban growth area inside the Township's Urban Containment Boundary, whose detached product is the compact R-CL lot (220 m² minimum to 464.5 m² maximum lot area) and whose market weight is attached strata product — 369 attached listings against 100 detached in September 2026. No named acreage subdivision, no bare-land strata acreage community, no farm parcel roster and no presale acreage offering is documented for this ground on any source. Verified named acreage developments counted here: 0.**

- **What "land" actually trades here: compact fee-simple house lots.** The Residential Compact Lot Zone R-CL (Section 403 of Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500) is Willoughby's compact-lot pattern, covering front-loaded lots and rear-loaded lots with rear lane access; under s.403.7, R-CL lots run from a minimum lot area of 220 m² to a maximum lot area of 464.5 m², with minimum lot frontage of 11 m (front-loaded) and 8.2 m (rear-loaded). A 464.5 m² maximum is roughly a ninth of an acre — a buyer searching Willoughby for acreage is searching the wrong sub-area, and no figure in this file dresses a compact lot up as one. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **The "355 m²" figure does not exist.** No 355 m² cap, minimum or threshold appears in R-CL s.403 or anywhere in the bylaw material read — it is never carried in this file, and any Willoughby page or listing stating a 355 m² R-CL figure is stating a number with no bylaw source. The verified pair is 220 m² / 464.5 m². (Standing correction; bylaw PDF above.)
- **ALR position: none confirmed inside Willoughby.** The research file states expressly that ALR land inside Willoughby is NOT confirmed, and that a floodplain designation for specific Willoughby parcels is not confirmed either; the area is an urban growth area inside the Urban Containment Boundary. Because the Township around Willoughby is farm country and buyers routinely import farm assumptions, the full ALR block is carried in Section 3 as the governing frame for any parcel where ALR status IS confirmed — conditionally, never asserted for a Willoughby parcel in this file. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/WILL-documents/WILL-Phase-1-BP-paper-pages---December-2018.pdf
- **Hobby acreage vs working-farm ground: no documented split, because no farm ground is documented inside the plan area.** No farm operation is named on an official source in the research, so none is named here; farm operations are described in process terms only, a generic feed is NEVER presented as a confirmed operation in a named sector, and no marketing-board source was opened in the research file, so quota/board items stay UNVERIFIED site copy. The Township's farm files (the langley-farms ground) are a different file's ground and are not imported here. (Research file above.)
- **Bare-land strata acreage: none.** No bare-land strata community is named for Willoughby in the research or the siblings; the area's strata stock is conventional condo and townhome product — the named projects in the research are attached housing, not land: Latimer Heights towers by Vesta Properties, Union Park by Polygon, and Cascadia at Willoughby Town Centre by StreetSide Developments (Qualico), all developer sources, all counted (where counted) in the built-form siblings, never here. https://vestaproperties.com/project/latimer-heights-towers/ and https://www.polyhomes.com/community/union-park and https://www.qualico.com/news/details/streetside-developments-launches-cascadia-at-willoughby-town-centre-in-langley
- **Coach houses are a housing form here, not acreage.** Rear-lane (coach) houses are planned as an alternate housing form in the neighbourhood plans, on the same compact R-CL lots — a second small dwelling on a 220–464.5 m² lot, under the Township frame in Section 3 (suite cap 120 m²; coach house fee-simple s.102 position flagged as a live conflict). A coach-house lot is a compact urban lot with two dwellings, not a small acreage. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf
- **Serviced vs unserviced ground:** Willoughby is urban serviced growth ground by plan — the research documents no well/septic pattern for the area, and parcel-level servicing for any specific lot is confirmed through the Township's subdivision and servicing process (Bylaw 5382, Section 3), never assumed from a listing. No unserviced-acreage servicing narrative (wells, septic fields, GWELLS) is documented for this ground in the research, and none is invented here. (Research file above.)

- **ALR RULES IN FULL — conditional frame (no ALR land confirmed inside Willoughby; carried because Township buyers meet ALR ground at the area's edges):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, stated as the ALC states it. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel to 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). Farm class (BC Assessment) runs on the full three-tier test — $2,500 on a 0.8–4 ha operation; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha (B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, asserted only where a file's own research documents it (the HORSE-PROPERTIES page owns it) and never attributed to this file's research. None of this block is asserted to apply to any Willoughby parcel in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf

- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $900,000 fully residential Willoughby purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $700,000 ($14,000) = **$16,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — the Willoughby townhomes sibling's PTT treatment is followed here at $93,000, and the $78,000 figure carried in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. First-time buyers: the FTHB exemption has a land route (vacant lot plus build at $835,000 or less, partial under $860,000, build and occupy by the first anniversary) and the Newly Built Home Exemption can refund lot PTT once a qualifying new home is built ($1,100,000 / $1,150,000 thresholds). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at the CRA GI-003 level:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a separate sheet as usually taxable — never assume any parcel is GST-exempt on the vacant-land rule alone; the GST position for a specific Willoughby lot is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and use are applied, and the contract must state it. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy an acreage in Willoughby Heights?** No — not in this sub-area. Its detached ground is compact R-CL lots of 220–464.5 m² and its market is attached product (369 attached listings vs 100 detached, September 2026). A buyer who needs real acreage in the Township is looking at the Township's rural and farm ground, documented in other files — not at Willoughby. (Section 2; SnapStats FVR September 2026.)
- **Q: What are the compact-lot rules, exactly?** R-CL, Section 403 of Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s.403.7: minimum lot area 220 m², maximum lot area 464.5 m², minimum frontage 11 m for front-loaded lots and 8.2 m for rear-loaded lots with lane access. The "355 m²" figure sometimes quoted for this zone is nowhere in the bylaw — do not price on it. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Q: Can I subdivide my Willoughby lot?** Almost certainly not an R-CL lot: subdivision must meet the zone minimums under the Subdivision and Servicing Bylaw 5382 (2019, CONFIRMED), and a lot already at or below the 464.5 m² maximum cannot yield two lots of 220 m² or more except at the very top of the range — a survey and the Township's Approving Officer answer it parcel by parcel. SSMUH units under Bylaw 6020 (no 6-plex tier) are density on the lot, not subdivision of it. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Is a coach house here fee-simple or strata?** That is a LIVE CONFLICT, flagged in Section 3: the bylaw's s.102 fee-simple treatment of the coach house form sits against the tenure treatment carried elsewhere in the program. Both bases are stated, the conflict is OPEN, and the answer for a specific property comes from its title — never from a listing template. (Standing corrections.)
- **Q: How big can a suite be?** The Township's secondary-suite cap is 120 m². (Langley City's different suite rule — the lesser of 100 m² and 40% — is a City-only figure and does not apply in the Township.) On ALR land the ALC rule (suite inside the principal residence only) would govern instead — but no ALR land is confirmed inside Willoughby. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Did the Willoughby plan update already change what can be built beside me?** Not yet, on the verified record: the Community Plan is Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to OCP Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025), and Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 were at first/second reading on May 25, 2026 with a Public Hearing directed for June 15, 2026. Price future form off the adopted documents, not the reading-stage drafts. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf
- **Q: Is any of Willoughby in the ALR or a floodplain?** Neither is confirmed inside the plan area in the research — Willoughby is an urban growth area inside the Urban Containment Boundary. Yorkson Creek runs through the east side and Township materials flag hazards around greenways and creeks, so an east-side lot gets a setback check; a parcel-level floodplain designation is UNCONFIRMED in the research. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/WILL-documents/WILL-Phase-1-BP-paper-pages---December-2018.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Willoughby address?** Catchments are address-based in SD35. For the sample address 7888 200 Street, the research-documented SD35 School Locator result is Donna Gabriel Robins Elementary → Peter Ewart Middle School → R.E. Mountain Secondary — stated on a single-address basis. Schools physically in Willoughby with SD35 addresses include Donna Gabriel Robins Elementary (7844 204B Street) and Lynn Fripps Elementary (21020 83 Avenue); Township statements also name Willoughby Elementary and Yorkson Creek Middle School as Willoughby schools. Run the exact address. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- **Q: Do I pay GST or PTT on a Willoughby lot purchase?** PTT on the standard tiers (worked example in Section 3: $900,000 = $16,000 with no exemption). GST only as the CRA GI-003 analysis lands for that vendor and parcel — most individual personal-use vacant-land sales are exempt, business/subdivided-parcel sales can be taxable; UNCONFIRMED per parcel, and the contract must state the position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
