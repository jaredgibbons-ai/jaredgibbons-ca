## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000.** Section 6 sets the agricultural (farm) zones and Section 7 the rural zones — the sections under which any farm-adjacent parcel at the city's edge is read; Section 12 includes the RS-FHA (Flood Hazard Area) reserve zone. - **OCP: Bylaw 5500 "Chilliwack 2050"** — Chilliwack Proper is Schedule B-2; the Agricultural Plan is Schedule B1, Sardis is Schedule B5, Eastern Hillsides is Schedule B4, and Downtown is Schedules B3 + D-6. A Proper West lot is read under B-2; B1's farm policies do not make an urban lot farm ground. (Standing Chilliwack frame.)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried conditionally (no ALR parcel in the working scope is confirmed; the block governs any parcel a City Webmap check shows to be in the reserve, and the city is farm-adjacent):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Properties in the ALR are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALR regulations, and the City directs owners to check ALR status on the City Webmap. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure (carried for the same conditional reason):** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR — stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in this slug's research; it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Suites, ADUs and SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw 5000, Section 2 defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit of not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building, located within a building containing only 1 principal dwelling unit — the cap is the lesser of 90 m² and 40%. Section 4.06(1) permits only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits an ADU (types: secondary suite, coach house, garden suite); garden suites are capped at 100 m² gross floor area by s.4.06(1)(g). The City's Secondary Suites Policy permits the rental of secondary suites within any single detached dwelling regardless of zone; a suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level under the Floodplain Regulation Bylaw. SSMUH: Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 (adopted Jun 18, 2024, per the amendment record in the consolidated Bylaw 5000) allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. On ALR land the ALC suite rule (inside the principal residence only) controls instead. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
