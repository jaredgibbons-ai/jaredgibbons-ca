- **SSMUH (Township, Bylaw 6020, adopted Nov 18, 2024; NO 6-plex tier exists in it):** eligible lots must be 4,050 m² or smaller, inside the Urban Containment Boundary and on municipal water and sewer — 3 units at ≤280 m² and 4 units at 280–4,050 m². SSMUH does not rescue a large rural lot: most acreages fail on size and servicing alone. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Subdivision / servicing (Township):** subdivisions require Approving Officer approval under the Land Title Act. - **ALR RULES IN FULL (ALR ground exists across this topic — carried in full, no stratification shortcut):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). Local government bylaws may be more restrictive. Note on this slug's own research: the ALC housing page was opened in its pass; Bulletin 09 itself was not opened in that pass, so the bulletin citation is carried here on the program's settled basis above, exactly as the research file flags it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 in farm-gate sales if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications must be delivered by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo89/loo89/411_95_pit

- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a SEPARATE info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What counts as an acreage on this page?** The page's own definition is a home on 1 to 10 acres, with land over 10 acres routed to the farms page — but the live feed at reading included a 39.5-acre estate and a bare-land Land listing, so treat the definition as the page's stated intent rather than a verified filter. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html
- **Q: How many homes can I build on an ALR acreage in Langley?** Generally one principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), with a secondary suite only inside that residence, plus one additional residence of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; more or larger requires ALC approval and local bylaws may be more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide an ALR acreage to sell pieces of it?** Subdivision in the ALR requires ALC approval, and depositing a strata plan IS a subdivision — ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Price the land as one parcel unless that permission exists. (§3.)
- **Q: What farm income does a Langley acreage need for farm class?** $2,500 in farm-gate sales at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that size; and $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications to BC Assessment by October 31, including for a new owner continuing to farm. (§3.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put 3–4 units on a Langley acreage?** No for most acreages: Township SSMUH (Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024; no 6-plex tier) requires a lot of 4,050 m² or smaller, inside the Urban Containment Boundary and connected to municipal water and sewer — 3 units at ≤280 m² and 4 units at 280–4,050 m². https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: What did Langley homes sell for in September 2026?** As board context only — not acreage-segment figures, for which no figure exists in this source: the Langley community detached median was $1,345,000 on 449 listings and 64 sales, and the Condos & Townhomes median was $611,000 on 699 listings and 128 sales. Salmon River detached showed 22 listings / 5 solds / 23%; Campbell Valley 16 / 0. As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: What should I test before removing subjects on a 1–5 acre property?** Follow the page's own checks: test well flow and water quality, test the septic system, confirm permits for outbuildings, suites and shops, and ask the Township what the zone allows for a second home, a shop or a suite. Per-property well/septic status is UNVERIFIED until those tests are run. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html
- **Q: Can I add a coach house to a Langley acreage?** A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102, NEVER strata — but on ALR ground the second unit must first fit the ALR additional-residence caps (90 m² / 186 m², §3), and on non-ALR ground the zone governs. Confirm the parcel's ALR status and zone before pricing any second-unit plan. (§3.)
- **Q: What does PTT cost on an acreage purchase?** On the general tiers (fully residential, no exemption): $1.5M → $28,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier over $3M (tier math in §3). Mixed-use / farm parcels can apportion differently — the anchors are the fully-residential case. (§3.)
- **Q: Is GST payable on a bare Langley acreage?** Only at the CRA GI-003 level stated in §3: most vacant-land sales by individuals are exempt, but a sale in the course of a business, of business capital property, or of land subdivided into more than two parts can be taxable, and farmland is treated in a separate CRA sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED — get the seller's GST position in the contract. (§3.)
