## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: City of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw No. 6700 (1995)** — side streets sit in the Ground Oriented (GO) Zone: a lot of 280 m² or less allows up to 3 units, a lot over 280 m² allows up to 4 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 designation and up to 6 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations. The City adopted the GO Zone on December 8, 2025, increasing permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 residential properties and allowing up to 6 units per lot on approximately 600 properties. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf and https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/HTO-Year-2-Progress-Report-July-1,-2025-June-30,-2026.pdf
- **OCP: City of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, 2014, Bylaw No. 8400, amended December 8, 2025 (Amendment Bylaw 2025, No. 9114)** for provincial housing requirements; the City sized its December 2025 updates to a 20-year housing need of 21,301 units on top of its existing 29,021 units. The District of North Vancouver's OCP 7900 is a different municipality's plan and is never imported here. https://www.cnv.org/Community-Environment/Housing/Provincial-Housing-Legislation-Updates
- **Subdivision and servicing — CITY instrument, citable here:** the City's Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw 8014, Section 3, regulates and requires the provision of works and services for subdivision and development in the City. This bylaw belongs to the City frame only — it is never applied to District ground such as Delbrook. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/city-of-north-vancouver/documents/bylaws/consolidated/8014-c.pdf
- **Suites and coach houses:** the City legalized secondary suites in all single-family dwellings in 1993; one suite is permitted per single-family property, the owner must reside at the property, and a suite may be combined with an accessory coach house, which is permitted where the front lot line is at least 10 m and requires a Development Permit and a Building Permit. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Permits-Inspections/building-permits/secondary-suites
- **Streamside permits:** land within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse or within 10 metres of the top of a ravine bank is in the Streamside Protection Development Permit Area and requires a development permit before new development — the west-side check near Wagg Creek and Mahon Park. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits/Streamside-Development-Permit-Areas
- **ALR position — stated plainly: no ALR position is documented for this area.** No ALR parcel is documented in the research and no farm adjacency is raised, so no ALR rule is applied to any Central Lonsdale parcel in this file. For completeness only, the standing conditional rules IF a parcel were ever confirmed ALR are: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; residence caps are a principal residence to 500 m², a suite within the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); and farm class runs on the full three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met, is documented only on the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, and is never attributed to this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC strata block (applies to the area's strata stock, and in full to any bare-land strata lot if title ever showed that tenure):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. Chesterfield Manor is NOT strata (corporation share), so this block does not govern it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 purchase treated as fully residential with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** (tier math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 further-tier = $93,000) — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **City property taxes:** City of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal-general rate is 1.89202 and the total residential rate is 3.34028 per $1,000 of assessed value, plus a separate residential storm drainage rate of 0.10697 per $1,000; the City illustrates an average residential property assessed at $1,387,684 paying $4,790 in total 2026 property taxes. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026_TAX_RATE_SUMMARY_CITY_OF_NORTH_VANCOUVER-2.PDF
