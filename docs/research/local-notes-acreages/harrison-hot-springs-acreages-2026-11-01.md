- **Q: Is this fringe parcel in the ALR?** Confirm the parcel itself on the ALC map — only a small ALR fringe on the village's east side is documented, and its exact share is UNVERIFIED. If it is in: subdivision needs ALC approval, the residence cannot be stratified, and the housing caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside that residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

- **Q: What farm income keeps farm class on a small farm parcel?** All three tiers, not a flat figure: $2,500 on a 0.8–4 ha farm operation; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Is the parcel serviced?** Assume nothing — this slug's research documents no Village servicing statement for fringe parcels. - **Q: Do I pay GST on a Harrison land purchase?** Do not assume either way.
