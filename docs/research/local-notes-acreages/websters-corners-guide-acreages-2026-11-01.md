- **ALR RULES IN FULL — this ground is farm-adjacent ALR ground, so the full frame governs every ALR parcel in it:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan IS a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — never the shorthand "cannot be stratified," and Bulletin 05 is not cited for stratification. Residence caps as the ALC states them: a principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite is permitted ONLY when located within the principal residence; one additional residence is permitted up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, and building more than the permitted number, or larger than the size limits, requires ALC approval. City zoning and the ALC frame both apply; neither displaces the other. ALR status is per-parcel — check the ALC's ALR Property and Map Finder. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
