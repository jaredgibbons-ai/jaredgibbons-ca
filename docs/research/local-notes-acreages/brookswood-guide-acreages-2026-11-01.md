## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Community plan:** the Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan covers Brookswood-Fernridge, replacing an earlier community plan, with Booth, Fernridge and Rinn Neighbourhood Plans under it; the Township states the plan allows previously undeveloped areas to grow while protecting trees and watercourses. A parcel's plan designation and neighbourhood plan (Booth, Fernridge or Rinn) drive what can be built — price the plan, not just the lot. Zoning: **Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500** (zone minimums in §2). https://www.tol.ca/en/news/brookswoodfernridge-community-plan-adopted.aspx
- **SSMUH (Township position) — SETTLED:** Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) allows **3 units on eligible lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger eligible lots up to 4,050 m²**; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible, and **no 6-unit tier applies in the Township**. Any 6-plex claim for Brookswood ground contradicts Bylaw 6020 — and the one such claim in this program belongs to the separate DEVELOPMENT-guide slug, expressly not to this guide (§5). https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Secondary suites + coach houses (Township):** suite cap **120 m²**; a coach house is a **fee-simple second unit under s.102**. Township zoning for a second dwelling or coach house on non-reserve Brookswood acreage beyond that frame: UNVERIFIED in this slug's research — confirm per parcel. (Township frame, FILE STATUS; research file above.)
- **R-CL zone (s.403):** minimum LOT area **220 m²**, maximum LOT area **464.5 m²** — lot-area figures. A "355 m²" figure is NOWHERE in the bylaw and is never carried in this file. (Township frame, FILE STATUS.)
- **Subdivision / servicing:** **Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED** for the Township. Subdivision potential runs against the zone minimums in §2 plus Bylaw 5382's servicing standards, confirmed per parcel with the Township (GeoSource for designations). (Verified ledger; FILE STATUS.)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (Brookswood is farm-adjacent Township ground — this block applies wherever ALR ground exists):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). The ALC frame as the research states it: one principal residence and, subject to conditions in the ALR Use Regulation, one additional residence at the same caps — Township zoning must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it — owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page; it is never carried here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property taxes (Township of Langley):** property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026; utility fees are now billed separately. This is the TOWNSHIP (municipal) regime — the separate FVRD rural regime does not apply inside the Township and is never blended in. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a SEPARATE info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — CRA governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
