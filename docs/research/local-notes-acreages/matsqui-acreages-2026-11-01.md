## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What am I actually buying when I buy an "acreage" in Matsqui?** A whole fee-simple farm parcel (A1 zone, 8.0 ha subdivision minimum) or a large rural-residential parcel (RR zone, 4.0 ha minimum). There is no acreage subdivision, no bare-land strata and no presale acreage product — and no parcel-size statistic is published for the area (UNVERIFIED), so each parcel is judged on its own title, zoning and ALR status. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is this parcel in the ALR — and what changes if it is?** Confirm the parcel itself on the ALC map; the research does not settle it parcel-by-parcel. If it is in: subdivision needs ALC approval, the residence cannot be stratified, and the caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside that residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I split the parcel, or stratify it later for family?** Stratify: no, if it is ALR (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Split: only if the parcel survives the A1 8.0 ha / RR 4.0 ha minimums and, in the ALR, only with ALC approval — most prairie parcels cannot be split further. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Q: What second dwelling can go on an A1 lot?** One Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot, only via the ALR Use Regulation (B.C. Reg. 30/2019) or ALC approval, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, capped at 90 m² / 186 m² by lot size — plus one secondary suite per lot to 100 m² under the zone, subject on ALR land to the ALC rule that a suite sits only within the principal residence. None of these creates a separate saleable title. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Q: What farm income do I need to keep farm class?** All three tiers, not a flat figure: $2,500 on a 0.8–4 ha farm operation; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Is the acreage serviced?** Assume well and onsite sewage until proven otherwise. Pull the GWELLS well record and the Fraser Health sewerage filings for the parcel before offering; parcel-level status is UNCONFIRMED in every source checked until those records are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners
- **Q: What does the dyking levy mean for my tax bill, and how serious is flood risk?** Properties in the Matsqui Prairie Dyking District pay a separate levy on top of general municipal rates — confirm whether the parcel is in the district and read the line on its tax notice. The City states the Matsqui Dykes are designed to keep Fraser River water out of the prairie — design protection, not a parcel-level guarantee; ask about flood history directly and confirm flood status with the City. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Summary%20May%2022.pdf and http://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/2022-spring-freshet-update-2
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a Matsqui farm acreage?** Do not assume either way. The CRA's vacant-land sheet makes most individual personal-use sales exempt, but its farmland guidance treats farmland sales as usually taxable with limited exemptions. The answer is UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel until vendor status and farm use are applied; the contract must state who bears GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What PTT would I pay on a $1.8M prairie parcel?** $34,000 on the worked anchors (1% × $200,000 = $2,000 + 2% × $1,600,000 = $32,000), before any exemption — and the home exemptions are not farm-parcel relief. (Section 3.)

The settled locator chain for that address — single-address basis — is Mathxwí Elementary → Clayburn Middle (Ministry address 35139 Laburnum Ave) → Robert Bateman Secondary (35045 Exbury Ave) (SD34 School Locator, mybaragar.com, run 2026-11-01).
