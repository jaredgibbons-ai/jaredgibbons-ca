## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP and zoning (Surrey only):** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is **Bylaw No. 21900** (adopted June 15, 2026) — the former OCP Bylaw No. 18020 is replaced and is NEVER cited as current in this file. The downtown's own layer is the City Centre Plan (approved 2017; an update underway adds a defined Central Business District, an Entertainment District, and more transit-oriented development around stations). Zoning is **Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000**, which governs the core's high-density mixed-use designations and the fringe lots alike. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/city-centre-plan and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential, as the municipality states it:** Surrey's SSMUH framework (in force since July 8, 2024) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, on eligible lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary — relevant to the detached fringe blocks around the core, not to the tower parcels. The City states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites — 40% cap:** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area**. Relevant to fringe house lots, not tower parcels. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision and servicing — Bylaw No. 8830 (1986), VERIFIED ledger:** Surrey's subdivision/servicing instrument is the **Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** — verified in this program's ledger. Parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City.
- **Streamside setbacks:** Surrey's streamside setbacks sit in **Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, varying by stream class** — NO single riparian figure is ever stated for Surrey; the applicable setback is read off Part 7A for the watercourse class on the specific lot, in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Density charges — CAC superseded by ACC (June 15, 2026):** City Centre projects seeking bonus density paid Community Amenity Contributions under Surrey's two-tier density-bonus framework; the City states CACs were superseded by its Amenity Cost Charge and bonus density provisions as of June 15, 2026, with the CAC page kept as a historic reference for eligible in-stream applications. Exact per-file CAC/ACC/DCC amounts are application-specific — UNVERIFIED as general figures. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/community-amenity-contributions
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried for completeness; the core is outside the ALR and no ALR parcel is documented in this file's research:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps: principal residence maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, and it is NOT documented in this slug's own research — asserted nowhere in this file, and NOT asserted for any Surrey City Centre ground. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **BC strata block (where a strata purchase is made instead of land):** Form B diligence (SPA s.59, within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; 45+ does NOT convert). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2 (notices sent end of May; if the due date falls on a weekend or statutory holiday, payment the next business day is without penalty), with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply here. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors, tier math shown: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K cap → $14,000 ($6,000 fully qualifying First-Time Home Buyers'); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (1% × $200,000 = $2,000 + 2% × $1,800,000 = $36,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 = $45,000 + further 2% × $500,000 = $10,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. For built product the siblings state the companion position: 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; CRA governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land — 100% to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism).
