- **ALR RULES IN FULL (the island's farm ground is ALR ground — this block applies in full):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. - **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Westham Island in the ALR?** ALR status is set property by property — check the exact address on the ALC map. The one official sample in this file (2194 Westham Island Road, 65.2 ha) is A 1 Agriculture and entirely in the ALR. Where land is in the ALR, a principal residence is capped at 500 m², a suite is allowed only inside that residence, and one additional residence is capped at 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How do you get to Westham Island?** By the Westham Island Bridge, the island's only road access — a TransLink-owned, partial single-lane swing bridge over Canoe Pass, opened March 29, 1910, with a 50-ton load limit. It was closed to vehicles after the January 20, 2026 vessel strike and reopened March 28, 2026 (corroborated basis in §1) — check the City of Delta's updates page for its current status before travelling. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2013/07/roads-bridges-getting-to-know-westham-island/ and https://www.delta.ca/westhamislandupdates
- **Q: What is Westham Island known for?** Farming. The Province states the island produces more than 25% of B.C.'s seed potatoes, plus other fruit and vegetables, honey and livestock, on more than 700 hectares, alongside two federal wildlife areas. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026AF0010-000103.htm
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide an island farm parcel?** Subdivision in the ALR requires ALC approval, and an ALR residence cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Delta's servicing position runs through DSSS 8288, whose 2024 adoption is UNCONFIRMED (§3). (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve Westham Island?** There is no school on the island in the BC Ministry K-12 file; Ladner's schools serve the island side of Delta, and the guide names Delta Secondary (4615 51 St) as the secondary school in Ladner. Catchments are address-based and UNVERIFIED for any specific island address — check the SD37 locator. (§1.)
- **Q: What wildlife areas are on Westham Island?** Alaksen National Wildlife Area (349 ha, established 1976, managed by the Canadian Wildlife Service, open 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. Monday to Friday) and the George C. Reifel Migratory Bird Sanctuary, which overlaps part of Alaksen and is reached through its gate at 5191 Robertson Rd. https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/national-wildlife-areas/locations/alaksen.html
- **Q: How is an island property protected from flooding?** The island sits behind dikes on the Fraser River estuary; the City of Delta is the diking authority. Ask whether the property is in a floodplain, what dike protects it, and get insurance quotes before removing subjects — the guide's own checklist directs exactly that. (§5.)
- **Q: What does farm class cost on an island farm?** In the full three-tier form: $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha (over 4 ha); $10,000 (under 0.8 ha) — applications by October 31. (§3.)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

Reifel Migratory Bird Sanctuary; both areas are reached through their front gates on Robertson Road (NWA gate 5421 Robertson Rd; sanctuary gate 5191 Robertson Rd). https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/national-wildlife-areas/locations/alaksen.html
