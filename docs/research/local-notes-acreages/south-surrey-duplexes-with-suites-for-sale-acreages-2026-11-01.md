## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) is CURRENT** — Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and is never cited as current. Zoning: **Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000** governs. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Secondary suites — Surrey (the core rule for this topic):** a secondary suite is a dwelling unit that is an accessory use, with habitable floor area **not exceeding 40% of the principal dwelling unit**, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex residential building, and **not a separate strata lot**. Only one secondary suite is permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a secondary suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; one suite may be permitted in dwellings within small-scale, multi-unit housing zones, including Comprehensive Development zones; and each application is reviewed for legal restrictions such as Land Use Contracts and Building Schemes. To become legal, the owner must meet the conditions of zoning, hold the necessary building permits, meet the BC Building Code, and pay the secondary suite utility and service fees. A "90 m²" cap is NOT current and is not stated as a rule anywhere in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **SSMUH (Surrey position, adopted Jul 8, 2024):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (a stop with 15-minute service, identified with TransLink); the new zoning does **not** retroactively legalize suites built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Subdivision / servicing:** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. Verified on the City's own Land Development pages in this program: the **Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw, worked through the Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. Lot-specific servicing costs for a duplex parcel: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside setbacks:** Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 sets streamside setbacks **by stream class** — there is no single riparian figure for Surrey; whether any watercourse setback touches a specific duplex parcel is UNCONFIRMED in this slug's research. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000

- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (carried IN FULL for completeness — no ALR parcel is documented for this feed's ground in §2):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it — owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page; no farm-class parcel is documented in this slug's research. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **BC strata block (applies where a half duplex IS a strata lot, and to any bare-land strata lot):** Form B diligence (SPA s.59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How big can a secondary suite be in a Surrey duplex?** Its habitable floor area cannot exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit, it must sit within the duplex building, and it cannot be a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Q: Can a Surrey half duplex have two secondary suites?** No — only one secondary suite is permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, and a suite is not permitted inside a coach house or garden suite. A card advertising both a legal suite and a second guest suite needs the second unit's status explained in writing before subjects come off. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Q: The listing says the suite is "legal" — can I rely on that?** No. Verify the suite, don't trust the listing word: pull the City registration status and the permit history — the page itself says a listing note is not proof of permits, no permit record for any card property was opened in this research (so each suite's status is UNVERIFIED), and Surrey's new zoning does not legalize an unpermitted suite retroactively. (§2.) https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
