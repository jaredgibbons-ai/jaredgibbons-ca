There is no ALR ground documented in this District in the research, no named acreage subdivision is documented in this file, and verified named acreage developments counted here: 0.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: District of West Vancouver Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010.** Section 200 (single family dwelling zones) states the RS minimum site areas carried in Section 2 (RS1 8,094 m² through RS5 488 m²; RS6 by site of record) and lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses in those zones. Apartment areas (Ambleside) are zoned RM1 and RM2, and Form and Character Development Permit guidelines apply in the Ambleside Apartment Area and the Ambleside Village Centre. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018** — the District-wide plan; Ambleside Municipal Town Centre is its emphasized heart and Park Royal its recognized regional shopping centre. (White Rock's OCP 2220 and Zoning 2506 are a different municipality's bylaws and govern nothing here.) https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- **SSMUH / ground-oriented density as the District's bylaw states it:** 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop (Bylaw 4662, Sections 620–630). Stated as the bylaw states it — the West Van figures are the District's own ground-oriented rules and are never restated as provincial maximums or minimums in either direction. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision runs through the District's approval process against the RS zone minimums and the District's works-and-services requirements. HELD UNCONFIRMED: the District of West Vancouver's subdivision-servicing bylaw number — a fresh District-site search in the lots program (L2) found none, so no number is asserted in any District file in this program and the process is documented instead. Never quote a District servicing standard from a listing or a summary. (Lots sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/west-vancouver-homes-for-sale-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md.)
- **Hazard and foreshore development permits — the District's real large-lot regime.** A Foreshore Development Permit is required for work in the Foreshore Development Permit Area (waterfront property or property at risk of coastal flooding), before a building permit; new habitable space must be built to the Flood Construction Level where the property is at risk of coastal flooding; work within 15 metres of the natural boundary (high tide mark) must follow the Foreshore Protection Area guidelines; and an Environmental Development Permit is required within 15 metres of the top of a watercourse bank. The research also documents the District's wildfire hazard permit area in the sibling District files (work within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface). Price the permit regime, not just the view or the lot size. https://www.westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/natural-environment-and-hazard-development-permit-areas/foreshore-development-permits and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **ALR — conditional statement only (no ALR ground documented in this District):** IF a parcel were in the Agricultural Land Reserve, the ALC frame would govern: subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); and farm class would run on the three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met). None of this is asserted to apply to any District parcel in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **If a strata purchase is made in the District (Ambleside condos):** the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw; strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. None of it applies to a fee-simple lot purchase itself: no strata, no Form B, no strata fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $2,000,000 fully residential estate-lot purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,800,000 ($36,000) = **$38,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. At District price points the further 2% tier and the thresholds matter more than the home-based exemptions, which rarely reach these values. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at the CRA GI-003 level:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. The GST treatment of any specific District lot is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and the parcel's use are applied, and the contract must state the GST position. Farmland caution, stated for completeness: the CRA treats farmland sales in a separate sheet as usually taxable — never assume any parcel is GST-exempt on the vacant-land rule alone. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Speculation, vacancy and additional school taxes:** no application of the provincial speculation and vacancy tax to this District is documented in this file's research, so none is asserted — UNCONFIRMED for any specific property until its class and the current lists are read. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply in the District. The Province's additional school tax at high assessed values is documented in the District sibling research for the altamont slug (0.2% on the residential portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising January 1, 2027) — a real holding-cost line at District price points, verified per property. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Federal prohibition on purchase by non-Canadians:** this file states ONLY what its cited sources support — no primary text was opened in this file's research, so the position is UNCONFIRMED and is NOT conformed from any other file. https://www.mross.com/what-we-think/article/update-canadian-government-prohibits-non-canadians-from-purchasing-residential-real-estate
- **Foreclosure / court-ordered sale — where met:** BC court-ordered sales follow the settled Supreme Court process (Rules 21-7 / 13-5, Practice Direction PD-66, Form 60). Form B diligence and PTT/GST treatment on a specific court sale are UNCONFIRMED in this file, kept distinct from the CRA GI-003 position above. REGISTRY LEDGER: the standing ledger does not cover West Vancouver, and no registry position is filled in for a city the ledger does not cover — UNCONFIRMED. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/168_2009_07
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; a source stating March 12 is noted in-line wherever met, never silently overwritten. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A live page's $1.25M straight-line phase-out row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism where met. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
