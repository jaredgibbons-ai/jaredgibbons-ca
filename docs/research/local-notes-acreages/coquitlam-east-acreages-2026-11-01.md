## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: City of Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996** — small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3, with subdivision minimums of 650 m² / 18.5 m width (R-1 and R-3) and 465 m² / 12.5 m width (R-2). https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **OCP: Interim Official Community Plan, Bylaws 5511/5512, adopted April 27, 2026, superseding OCP No. 3479** — CURRENT per the settled ledger. The City states the Interim OCP streamlined the former plan and did not introduce new land-use policy except as provincial legislation required, with a future OCP update project anticipated to begin in 2027; any document citing 3479 as current is stale. A Coquitlam East neighbourhood plan: UNVERIFIED. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review (per the lots-and-land sibling, §3.)
- **Subdivision and servicing — VERIFIED ledger:** Coquitlam Subdivision Servicing Bylaw 3558, 2003 is VERIFIED (full name in the lots-and-land sibling: Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 3558, 2003, original adoption July 24, 2003, amendments through July 2026 — the owner must grant or acquire statutory rights-of-way for the works and services needed to serve a subdivision or development, in a form acceptable to the City's solicitor, and pay for traffic-control and street-name signage installed by the City at the owner's expense before subdivision approval or building permit issue). Subdivision runs against the Zoning Bylaw 3000 minimums and Bylaw 3558's servicing requirements; confirm the works required for a specific lot with the City. https://publicdocs.coquitlam.ca/coquitlamdoc/getdocIF.asp?doc=6328713
- **SSMUH in Coquitlam, as the City states it:** up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. Price unit potential from the bylaw and the lot's own zone and services, not from a listing template. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Watercourse / hazard diligence — the 50-metre Northeast rule:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 50 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse in Northeast Coquitlam, and within 30 metres in the rest of Coquitlam; Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and s.523 covers riparian areas. Which side of that line a Coquitlam East lot sits on is a parcel check. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **ALR — conditional statement only (no ALR ground documented in Coquitlam East):** IF a parcel were in the Agricultural Land Reserve, the ALC frame would govern: subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); and farm class would run on the three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met; that line is documented on the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file). None of this is asserted to apply to any Coquitlam East lot in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Pad ground, where met (Hideaway Park cross-reference only):** a manufactured home on a rented pad is governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not by land law — pad-rent increases run on the MHPTA percentages only (2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount). No pad-rent dollar figure is stated in this file. (Lots-and-land sibling, §2.)
- **If a strata purchase is made in the Atlin Place / Bromley segment (redirect case):** the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (the 455 Bromley Street feature sheet's 2019 "Pet/Rental: Not allowed" line is flagged in the siblings: the rental half is VOID for long-term rentals since that date); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw; strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. None of the strata block applies to a fee-simple lot purchase itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 fully residential Coquitlam East lot purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. - **GST on vacant land — ONLY at the CRA GI-003 level:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts.
