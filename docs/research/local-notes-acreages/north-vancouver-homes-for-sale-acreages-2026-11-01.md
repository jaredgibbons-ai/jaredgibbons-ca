## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning — City:** City of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw, 1995, No. 6700 (as amended). In the Ground Oriented (GO) Zone, a lot of 280 m² or less allows up to 3 units; a lot over 280 m² allows up to 4 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 designation and up to 6 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations, with maximum floor area of 0.85× lot area (LN1) and 1.0× (LN2/RL4); City small-scale multi-unit housing follows the provincial framework as the City states it in the GO Zone. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **Zoning — District:** District of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw 3210, administered under District OCP Bylaw 7900. District rules do not apply in the City and City rules do not apply in the District. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **OCPs:** City OCP Bylaw, 2014, No. 8400 and the Lower Lonsdale Plan guide the City; District OCP Bylaw 7900 (which replaced the former neighbourhood plans, including the former North Lonsdale–Delbrook plan, Bylaw 6750) governs the District. Never blend the two frames: a City parcel is read under 8400/6700, a District parcel under 7900/3210, and the straddle sub-areas (Boulevard at Highway 1, Upper Lonsdale at 29th Street) are decided parcel by parcel. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/418109.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — City ground ONLY:** the City of North Vancouver Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw 8014 is the City's subdivision/servicing instrument, cited ONLY for City ground. **District ground exclusion, stated expressly:** Bylaw 8014 is NEVER imported into District ground — it is a City bylaw and has no application to a District parcel. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits (City approvals route, per the research file)
- **Subdivision and servicing — District ground:** HELD UNCONFIRMED — the District of North Vancouver servicing-bylaw number could not be confirmed on a primary source in the research; the process is documented instead of a number: subdivision and servicing for a District parcel run through the District's Approving Officer and works-and-services requirements, confirmed with the District for the parcel. Never quote a District servicing standard or number from a listing or a summary. (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/north-vancouver-homes-for-sale-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §3)
- **Hazard and permit areas (City, as the research states them):** the City's Shipyards and Harbourside Waterfront precincts are Development Permit Areas where a permit is required before new development, and Streamside Protection Development Permit Area rules apply near watercourses — land within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse or 10 metres of a ravine bank. On hillside District ground, slope, creek and wildfire diligence is parcel-specific: UNCONFIRMED in the research at parcel level — read the parcel's DP-area mapping with the governing municipality before pricing a build envelope. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried because buyers ask them of any large parcel (no ALR acreage trading is documented in North Vancouver, Section 2):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, with applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. No North Vancouver parcel's farm-class position is documented in the research: UNCONFIRMED per parcel. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Strata block — where a strata purchase is made instead (the City/centre case):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. Strata disputes can be taken to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. None of this applies to a fee-simple large-lot purchase itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,800,000 fully residential large-lot purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,600,000 ($32,000) = **$34,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in this program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at the CRA GI-003 level:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland caution: the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a SEPARATE info sheet and treats farmland sales as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — never assume a farm parcel is GST-exempt on the vacant-land rule alone; parcel-level GST: UNCONFIRMED. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. A live page's $1.25M straight-line phase-out row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism where met. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy and Empty Homes Tax:** Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is VANCOUVER ONLY and does not apply in either North Vancouver municipality — stated expressly because buyers import it across the inlet. Any speculation-and-vacancy-tax application to a specific North Vancouver parcel: only as an official source for that parcel supports — UNCONFIRMED at parcel level in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

- **Q: How many units can go on a City lot?** Under the GO Zone: up to 3 units on a lot of 280 m² or less; over 280 m², up to 4 units in Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 and up to 6 units in Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4, with maximum floor area 0.85× lot area (LN1) and 1.0× (LN2/RL4). - **Q: Can I stratify a large lot or a house with a suite?** On ordinary urban ground, strata form is a Strata Property Act question (Form B, depreciation cycle, CRF and rental/age rules in Section 3). On ALR land — if a parcel were in the reserve — stratification is barred outright (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Confirm ALR status and tenure on title before assuming any strata exit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is the parcel serviced, or on well/septic?** North Vancouver is municipal-servicing ground and the research documents no well/septic pattern here — but parcel-level servicing is UNCONFIRMED until the governing municipality confirms connection and off-site works for that parcel. Do not import a rural well/septic assumption, and do not assume connection either. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Do I pay GST and PTT on a large-lot purchase?** PTT: yes, on the standard tiers — the worked example in Section 3 prices a $1,800,000 fully residential purchase at $34,000 with no exemption. GST: only at the CRA GI-003 level — most individual personal-use vacant-land sales are exempt, with business/subdivision exceptions, and farmland is treated separately as usually taxable; parcel-level GST is UNCONFIRMED and the contract must state the position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

Every other address needs its own locator run — never market a North Vancouver home on a school from the neighbourhood name alone. https://queenmary.sd44.ca/
- **Q: What do the waterfront and creek rules do to a build?** In the City, the Shipyards/Harbourside precincts are Development Permit Areas (permit before new development) and streamside rules reach 15 m from a watercourse bank and 10 m from a ravine bank. Read the DP mapping before valuing a building envelope near water. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits
- **Q: Is there a market figure I can price a large lot from?** No SnapStats figure exists for North Vancouver at all, and SnapStats has no acreage category in any event. - **Q: Does Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax apply here?** No — the 3% Empty Homes Tax is Vancouver ONLY. It does not apply in the City or the District of North Vancouver. (Section 3.)
