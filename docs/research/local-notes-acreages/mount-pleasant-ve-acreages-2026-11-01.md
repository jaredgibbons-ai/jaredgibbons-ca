## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning and subdivision (VERIFIED ledger):** City of Vancouver Zoning and Development By-law governs the sub-area, and subdivision runs under the **Subdivision By-law No. 5208 (fees By-law No. 14538) — VERIFIED** in the standing servicing ledger. For detached-house lots, the multiplex rules apply citywide: By-law No. 13817 replaced the RS districts with the R1-1 district, allowing up to 6 strata units (or 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots, subject to the district schedule. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **Plan layer:** the Broadway Plan (approved June 22, 2022; updated December 12, 2024) provides for housing, job space and amenities near Mount Pleasant Station with buildings up to 20–30 storeys in the station area, commits over $1 billion of public benefits across Mount Pleasant, Fairview and Kitsilano in its first 10 years, and carries renter protections and tenant relocation requirements that must be factored into any older-rental-building redevelopment purchase. The Vancouver Official Development Plan replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026 as the citywide direction. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax = 3% — VANCOUVER ONLY, and it applies in this file:** the City's Empty Homes Tax charges 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed empty, with the annual declaration due February 3, 2026 and payment due April 16, 2026 — a live holding cost for investors holding older rental or vacant stock here, and a tax that applies in NO other municipality in this program. Separately, 2026 advance property taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances, citywide. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **BC strata block (where a strata purchase is made instead of a house lot):** Form B diligence (SPA s.59, within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (9 On The Park, at 9 units, sits above that threshold in its townhomes home); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; 45+ does NOT convert). - **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried for completeness; no Mount Pleasant VE ALR parcel is documented:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)).
