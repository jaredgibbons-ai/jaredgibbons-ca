## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series, s.310), Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001** — RS-1 minimum site 557.40 sq. m. (6,000 sq. ft.), FSR 0.5. Standing citywide correction carried where met: the residential districts are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B ONLY — no RT-A/RT-B districts exist and they must never be cited for this city. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf

- **Heritage — the governing overlay, in full:** the Queen's Park Heritage Conservation Area is currently the only HCA in New Westminster; it places a layer of heritage protection over ALL properties in the area regardless of construction age, classed Protected or Non-protected. Building Permit applications for work such as new buildings, demolition, or changes affecting the front, sides or visible roofline of Protected properties, and subdivision applications, require a Heritage Alteration Permit (HAP) reviewed for design-guideline compliance; Protected properties also need an HAP for exterior changes that do not require a Building Permit. Protected properties are also under the City's Heritage Property Maintenance Standards Bylaw; standard in-kind maintenance does not require an HAP; Protected-property incentives include additional density, special BC Building Code provisions, density transfer, relaxed laneway/carriage house guidelines and no heritage permit fees. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-protection-and-development/sb_expander_articles/582.php
