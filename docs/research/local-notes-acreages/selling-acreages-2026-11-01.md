## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **The regulator's framework (BCFSA, opened in the research pass):** in BC a licensed real estate professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party, and only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction; disclosure of whether the professional can represent the consumer, the duties owed, and how to file a complaint with BCFSA must happen at the outset of dealings, and dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Commission — this slug's defect (b), documented precisely:** the page states no commission rate, amount or structure anywhere, and its BCFSA-attributed costs list omits commission entirely, even though commission is the cost BCFSA's consumer framework leads with. What a seller pays Jared's brokerage is therefore UNVERIFIED from this page — under the BCFSA framework above, commission and fee information is given to the consumer in the listing conversation, off-page. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers https://jaredgibbons.ca/selling.html
- **Cooling-off / rescission, verified at the regulator:** if a buyer rescinds an accepted offer under the Home Buyer Rescission Period Regulation within three business days, the brokerage pays the seller the 0.25% rescission fee directly and returns the remaining balance of the deposit to the buyer, with no separate release form signed by either party. The page's own statement matches: a cancelling buyer must tell the seller in writing and pay 0.25% of the offer price. Deposits generally: a buyer's deposit is usually held in the brokerage's trust account with the brokerage as stakeholder; where subject clauses are not removed, the deposit is not automatically returned and both parties must sign a release, except where the buyer exercises the right of rescission. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/selling-home/deposits https://jaredgibbons.ca/selling.html
- **BC home flipping tax, verified on the Province page the selling page links to:** the tax applies to profit from selling a BC property (including presale contracts) owned for less than 730 days, under the Residential Property (Short-Term Holding) Profit Tax Act effective January 1, 2025; the rate is 20% of net taxable income for a property disposed of within 365 days and gradually decreases over the next 365 days, with no tax at 730 days; a return must be filed within 90 days of the sale where the seller is subject to the tax or an exemption applies only after filing. The page's shorter wording — "20% of the profit," "drops to zero by day 730," "a return may be due within 90 days" — is a simplification of "net taxable income" and the filing conditions as the Province states them. The tax applies to a bare lot or acreage parcel sold inside 730 days on the same terms as to a home, on the Province's wording ("a BC property"). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/income-taxes/bc-home-flipping-tax https://jaredgibbons.ca/selling.html
- **Municipal frames for an acreage seller (kept separate — the page spans both):** Surrey ground — OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026; Bylaw 18020 is REPLACED, never cited as current), Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, secondary suite capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area (one per unit, not a separate strata lot), SSMUH adopted Jul 8, 2024 (3 units under 280 m², 4 units over 280 m², up to 6 on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop), streamside setbacks in Part 7A by stream class (NO single riparian figure), Subdivision and Development Bylaw 1986 No. 8830 VERIFIED. Township ground — Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024; NO 6-plex tier), secondary suite cap 120 m², coach house fee-simple s.102 never strata (**LIVE CONFLICT flagged:** s.102 permits townhouse suites at 120 m² while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted — neither position adopted), R-CL s.403 = 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum LOT area (no "355 m²" anywhere), Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED, Willoughby Plan Bylaw 1836 now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (Dec 1, 2025), Plan Update 6201/6202/6203 reading-stage only. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (where ALR ground exists — parcel status UNCONFIRMED per parcel):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a SEPARATE info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
