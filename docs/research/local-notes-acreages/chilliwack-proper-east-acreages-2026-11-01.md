## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Chilliwack 2050, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500** — the long-term vision and growth strategy for the whole city, including protection of the Agricultural Land Reserve; Schedule B2 is the “Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan” covering this ground. Schedule D permit areas as named on the City page: D-1 Drinking Water Supply, D-2 Hillside Environment, D-3 Riparian Area, D-4 Farm Home Plate, D-5 Hillside Development, D-6 Downtown, D-7 Mixed-Use Areas, D-8 Residential, D-9 Commercial, D-10 Industrial; which apply to a given parcel is a per-parcel check: UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, §6 farm zones** where farm/ALR ground arises; the parcel's actual zone is confirmed with the City per parcel — no zone label is adopted from editorial copy or a feed card for any specific lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH: Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, adopted Jun 18, 2024**, amending Bylaw 5000, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. Added-unit permission is not permission to subdivide and does not create acreage lots. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Secondary suites and accessory units (Bylaw 5000):** a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the habitable floor space of the building (s.2); only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot, types include secondary suite, coach house and garden suite (s.4.06(1)); garden suites cap at 100 m² (s.4.06(1)(g)); ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite (s.5.04); no secondary suite is legal unless built with a building permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, with new suites not permitted below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — number UNCONFIRMED, process documented:** the candidate “Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055” is NOT adopted per the program ledger and must NEVER be presented as verified. Documented process instead: all subdivision plans require approval by the City's Approving Officer under the Local Government Act and Land Title Act before Land Title registration; a Preliminary Layout Approval is the first major milestone (typically 8–12 weeks from application, valid 12 months) and sets out servicing, engineering and legal steps to final approval. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8208
- **ALR RULES IN FULL:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31. A flat “$2,500+” statement is an ERROR. Stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in this slug's own research — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M lives only in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: CRA addresses farmland in a separate sheet from GI-003 — never assume a farm parcel is GST-exempt on the vacant-land rule alone. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 is noted in-line in some sibling files and is not silently overwritten — March 13 governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after Mar 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out — housing only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **BC strata block, where a bare-land strata purchase is met elsewhere:** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); REDMA s.21 7-day rescission for presales. None of it applies to a fee-simple acreage purchase itself. Pad ground is NOT lot stock / land / acreage; manufactured-home pad tenancy percentages only (2026 = 2.3% + proportionate; 2027 = 2.2% + proportionate) and NO pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel until vendor status and use are applied; the contract must state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
