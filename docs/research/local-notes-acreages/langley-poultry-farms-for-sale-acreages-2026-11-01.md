- **Township frame in full:** Zoning Bylaw 2500 governs this ground. SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024): 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and NO 6-plex tier exists in it — most farm parcels exceed the ceiling, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them; parcel eligibility UNVERIFIED. - **ALR RULES IN FULL — this topic is ALR farm ground, so the full frame governs every ALR parcel in it:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan IS a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — never the shorthand "cannot be stratified." Residence caps: a principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence; one additional residence up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, with ALC approval needed beyond those limits. Township zoning and the ALC frame both apply. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
