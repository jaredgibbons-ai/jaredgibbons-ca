## 2. THE ACREAGE ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: Poplar's acreage is real ground but not a named-development market. No named acreage subdivision, acreage development, bare-land strata, or presale acreage offering was verified in Poplar on an official or developer source. What acreage actually trades here is individual fee-simple rural parcels — larger lots toward Huntingdon Road and the south edge, in or beside the Agricultural Land Reserve, under rural/agricultural zoning (A1 where it applies, with an 8.0 ha minimum subdivision lot area). Urban RS lots in the north are house lots, not acreage. No parcel count, parcel size beyond the zoning minimums in §3, or acreage price is invented here.**

### What acreage actually trades here

- **Fee-simple rural parcels — the acreage form.** Single-detached houses are the main form in Poplar — on urban lots in the north and on larger rural lots toward Huntingdon Road and the south edge. The established stock is freehold; strata in the sub-area is limited to the townhome complexes along King Road and Windsor Street/Marshall Road. Acreage trades in that same fee-simple form: a house (or a buildable envelope) on a large rural parcel, with no strata, no Form B and no strata fees. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014 and (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/poplar-guide.md, §5)
- **Parcel sizes / zones AS THE RESEARCH STATES THEM:** urban minimums are set by the RS zones — RS3 minimum interior lot 540 m² and RS5 minimum interior lot 420 m² in City rezoning reports; rural A1 land subdivides at a minimum of **8.0 ha**. These are zone minimums for creating a parcel, not surveyed sizes for any individual parcel, and no typical acreage size is claimed beyond them. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/56399/ and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/content/coa/coabylaws/z/zoning/?xsl=/templates/browse.xsl
- **ALR vs non-ALR split:** rural lots at the south edge toward Huntingdon Road are **in or beside the ALR**, as the research documents it; no Poplar-wide ALR percentage is published, so no split figure is stated — status is parcel-by-parcel, checked on the ALC maps and against City zoning before any farm, subdivision or added-dwelling plan is priced. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/content/coa/coabylaws/z/zoning/?xsl=/templates/browse.xsl and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps

On that record this file does **not** classify any Poplar parcel as a working farm, and does not present any generic farm feed as a confirmed operation in a named sector. No marketing-board source was opened in the research, so any quota/board item for this area stays UNVERIFIED site copy. Farm use on a specific parcel is a parcel-level check (zoning, ALR status, BC Assessment class), not an area label. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/poplar-guide.md, §§4, 8)
- **Serviced vs unserviced ground:** lots at the rural south edge **may be on wells and onsite sewage rather than community systems** — wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database and onsite systems must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation through Fraser Health. Urban north lots sit in the City's serviced fabric (parcel-level confirmation still required). Never assume south-edge servicing matches King Road servicing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems

The BC strata block in §3 is included only so a buyer who meets that form elsewhere is not misled — it governs no Poplar parcel documented here. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014.** Urban lots are in the RS family (RS3 540 m² / RS5 420 m² minimum interior lots per City reports); southern lots fall under rural/agricultural zoning, and where A1 applies the minimum subdivision lot area is 8.0 ha. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- **OCP: Abbotsforward 2050 governs — CURRENT form, adopted December 2025 (the updated Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2600-2016, as updated).** No Poplar-specific neighbourhood plan is listed in the OCP. Bare "2600-2016" / "2500-2016" citations without the Abbotsforward 2050 currency are stale — a Jerry sweep item where a live page carries them. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **SSMUH:** City-confirmed June 2024 adoption; the SSMUH bylaw **NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED — "3560-2024" must not be asserted** (the condos sibling asserts it; conflict flagged in the FILE STATUS block, not repeated as fact here). Abbotsford suite rules (**Abbotsford ONLY**): one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings under Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8, with City-stated criteria including a 400 m² minimum lot and 12.0 m minimum frontage; suite cap **under 50%** of the principal dwelling; coach/garden suite 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m². Verify the parcel meets the suite criteria before pricing suite income into acreage value. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements beyond zone minimums:** the Abbotsford Development Bylaw **number is HELD UNCONFIRMED** in the program's verified ledger — the subdivision/servicing **process** is documented in the research instead of a number, and no number is asserted in this file. Detail beyond the zone minimums: UNCONFIRMED in the research — confirm subdivision servicing and frontage requirements with the City for the specific parcel, never filled in from another municipality. (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/poplar-guide-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **ALR rules IN FULL (south-edge / ALR ground):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, as the ALC states it; ALC residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², a suite within the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Subdivision and added dwellings on ALR land also follow the ALR Use Regulation and City zoning — and the City's A1 minimum (8.0 ha) sits on top of the ALC rules, it does not replace them. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on under 0.8 ha; applications by **October 31**. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR and appears nowhere in this file as a rule — and no file in this group carries the flat form as its own documented rule (that form is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, not this group). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Airport diligence:** Poplar is east of Abbotsford International Airport (YXX); where a parcel falls inside the Airport Zoning Regulation area on City drawing M-500, AZR height limits (approach, outer and transitional surfaces) and NAV CANADA / Transport Canada approvals apply before a City permit. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- **Flood diligence:** check the parcel against Zoning Bylaw Schedule E Floodplain Boundaries in WebMap before removing subjects — drainage toward Sumas Prairie makes this a live question on south-edge acreage, and no parcel-level finding is stated here. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — rates and worked anchors (inputs + tier math stated):** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked examples: $500,000 → $8,000; $700,000 → $12,000; $800,000 → $14,000 (net $6,000 after the maximum $8,000 First-Time Home Buyers' relief, where it applies); $900,000 → $16,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 **including** the further 2% tier on the $500,000 over $3,000,000. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief on a new principal-residence HOME up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a home exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most individual personal-use sales are exempt; business-use, in-the-course-of-business, and more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable — developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are therefore generally taxable, at that level only. Anything finer for a specific Poplar parcel: UNCONFIRMED — get tax advice. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy taxes + purchase prohibition:** no Poplar-specific position is documented in the research — none is imported in this file. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is **Vancouver ONLY**, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for a specific buyer is **not conformed here — UNCONFIRMED** where no primary text was opened.
- **Bare-land strata block (none verified in Poplar — form guidance only):** a bare-land strata lot is strata tenure — Form B, bylaws and strata fees apply to the lot; 5-year depreciation-report cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Abbotsford property taxes:** due **July 2, 2026**, with a first 5% penalty on **July 3, 2026** and a second 5% penalty on **September 16, 2026**, each including any unclaimed Home Owner Grant; the current rates bylaw is Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026. About 35¢ of each tax dollar passes to other taxing authorities, per the City. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes
- **Heritage note:** the Old South Poplar School is on the Abbotsford Community Heritage Register and the community was named for poplar trees planted on the hilltop — a title/heritage check is a diligence item for older south-edge parcels near the school site. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2006%20Commission%20-%20PRC%2028%20Abbotsford%20Community%20Heritage%20Register%20Addition....pdf
- **Foreclosure treatment:** BC foreclosure is a settled court process (Supreme Court Civil Rules 21-7 / 13-5, Practice Direction PD-66, Form 60). Form B treatment and PTT/GST treatment on a court-ordered sale: UNCONFIRMED — kept distinct from the CRA GI-003 vacant-land position above. **Registry ledger: Abbotsford HAS a Supreme Court registry** — the registry position for an Abbotsford parcel is therefore not the no-registry position that applies in Surrey/Delta/Mission.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What kind of acreage actually comes up for sale in Poplar?** Individual fee-simple rural parcels on the Huntingdon Road / south edge — a house on a large lot, or a buildable rural parcel — not lots in a named acreage subdivision, because none is verified here. The north (King Road) stock is urban house lots and townhouses, a different purchase. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is a Huntingdon Road parcel in the ALR?** South-edge parcels are in or beside the ALR — check the specific parcel on the ALC maps; in the ALR, subdivision needs ALC approval, residences cannot be stratified, and the residence caps in §3 apply on top of City zoning. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Poplar acreage parcel?** Where the A1 zone applies, the City minimum for a parcel created by subdivision is 8.0 ha — so a parcel has to be very large before any split is even arguable, and an ALR parcel additionally needs ALC approval. Subdivision servicing detail for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED until the City confirms it (no Development Bylaw number is asserted in this file). https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/content/coa/coabylaws/z/zoning/?xsl=/templates/browse.xsl
- **Q: Will a south-edge acreage parcel have city water and sewer?** It may be on well and septic instead. Pull the GWELLS well record and the Fraser Health sewerage filings before subjects — and do not assume it matches a King Road parcel's servicing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners
- **Q: Can I add a second home or a coach house on an acreage parcel?** On City-zoned ground, Abbotsford's suite rules (Abbotsford ONLY) allow one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings if the criteria are met (400 m² minimum lot, 12.0 m frontage, cap under 50%), and a coach/garden suite at 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m². On ALR ground the ALC caps in §3 govern instead — an additional residence is 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha), and a suite must be within the principal residence. Which regime applies is a parcel check first. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is Poplar flood risk real for an acreage parcel?** Drainage runs toward the Sumas Prairie system the City calls low-lying and Nooksack-overflow-prone; your parcel's status is a Schedule E / WebMap check, parcel by parcel — UNVERIFIED until done. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- **Q: Does the airport cap what I can build on acreage?** Only if the parcel is inside the Airport Zoning Regulation area on City drawing M-500 — then AZR height surfaces and NAV CANADA / Transport Canada approvals apply before a City permit. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I get farm-class taxes on a Poplar acreage parcel?** Only if the parcel is farm land meeting the full three-tier test in §3 ($2,500 / 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; apply by Oct 31). Acreage alone does not qualify, and this file does not document any Poplar parcel as a working farm — BC Assessment's class for the specific parcel governs. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Q: Will I pay GST on a vacant Poplar acreage parcel?** Developer/newly subdivided = generally taxable; individual personal-use = generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Confirm the vendor's status in writing and get tax advice — anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What PTT would a $900,000 acreage purchase carry?** On the general tiers in §3: $16,000 (1% on the first $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% on the next $700,000 = $14,000), before any exemption that actually applies — the home exemptions do not apply to bare land alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Which schools serve a Huntingdon Road acreage parcel?** The settled chain for 32746 Huntingdon Road itself is Jackson Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary for the regular stream, with the separate Traditional pathway South Poplar Traditional Elementary → Abbotsford Traditional School (6–12). That chain is documented for that single address — run any other address in the SD34 locator. (§1 above)
