- **Planning frame:** the City's Official Community Plan was adopted November 22, 2021, built on the "Nexus of Community" vision adopted July 23, 2018, guiding growth over 25+ years for housing, streets, parks and neighbourhoods. https://www.langleycity.ca/city-hall/reports-plans-strategies/strategic-vision

- **SSMUH: Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024** — RS1/RS2 lots permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. This is a **City-only figure**; the Township's SSMUH position is a different municipality's rule and is never imported. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 3300):** a suite must not exceed **the lesser of 100 m² and 40%** of the building's gross floor area (s.1.3 / s.2.4(c)); the registered owner must reside on the lot (owner-occupancy); at least one off-street parking space in addition to the two normally required for a single detached use; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Garden suites / carriage homes (Bylaw No. 3300, s.2.4(d)):** permitted in the R1 zone; maximum gross ground floor area for a carriage home or garden suite in R1 is 100 m²; maximum total floor area of accessory buildings on an R1 lot is 60 m²; the registered owner must reside on the lot. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
