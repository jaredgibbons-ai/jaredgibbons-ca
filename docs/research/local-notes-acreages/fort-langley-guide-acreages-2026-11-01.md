## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Fort Langley is a village in the Township of Langley on the south bank of the Fraser River, described by the Township as the birthplace of BC, with its commercial core on Glover Road. https://www.pc.gc.ca/apps/dfhd/page_nhs_eng.aspx?id=66 and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/fort-langley-community-projects.aspx
- The wider village is framed by River Road, 88 Avenue, 96 Avenue and 240 Street, with the Salmon River and Bedford Channel at its edges; exact Paragon sub-area boundary roads are UNVERIFIED. https://www.pc.gc.ca/apps/dfhd/page_nhs_eng.aspx?id=66
- Nearby areas: Walnut Grove (west/southwest), Langley City and the rest of the Township (south), and Maple Ridge across the Fraser River via the Golden Ears Bridge route. (Research file §1.)
- Where the land actually differs: three fabrics — the heritage village grid around Glover Road (fee-simple village lots, many inside the Heritage Conservation Area), Bedford Landing on Billy Brown Road along the Bedford Channel (a planned enclave where fee-simple lots and strata buildings share one community name), and acreage edges mainly south and east toward the Salmon River valley, where ALR ground begins. Sources: Sections 2 and 3 below.
- Glover Road is the village commercial street; Lee's Market, the village grocery, is at 23320 Mavis Avenue, and there is no shopping centre in Fort Langley. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/garbage-cart.aspx
- Transit is a single bus: TransLink route 562 through the village on Glover Road between Langley Centre and Carvolth Exchange, via Walnut Grove. No SkyTrain or West Coast Express station is in Fort Langley — a material fact for any density claim made for village or edge land. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf
- SD35 schools physically in the village: Fort Langley Elementary (8877 Bartlett St) and Langley Fine Arts School (9096 Trattle St), a district K–12 fine arts choice school; a locator run for 8877 Bartlett St returned Fort Langley Elementary and D.W. Poppy Secondary, with no middle school shown (single-address basis). Catchments are address-based. https://fortlangley.sd35.bc.ca/ and https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Village plan: Fort Langley Community Plan, Township of Langley Bylaw No. 2527** (adopted October 26, 1987, as an amendment to the Township Official Community Plan) — the governing plan for village land. Fort Langley has its own plan context per its research; the Willoughby Community Plan (Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200) is a different community's plan and is not imported here. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **Heritage Conservation Area (Bylaw 3710, October 27, 1997).** The plan designates a Heritage Conservation Area under the Local Government Act covering the downtown core, to protect heritage character; non-exempt work requires heritage approval, with exemptions including minor additions under 50 m² that do not front a road (other than a lane) and conform to the guidelines. For a land buyer this is a buildability rule: what can be built or altered on a core lot is heritage-gated before it is zoning-gated. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **Design controls on top of zoning.** A Development Permit Area (Bylaw 3204, May 3, 1993) and the Fort Langley Design Guidelines (Bylaw 5063, April 28, 2014) also cover the village; the Building Façade Design Guidelines are non-binding policy. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **Zoning: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500.** R-1E is named for the village; the Bedford Landing strata buildings sit in site-specific CD zones (the Waterfront's is CD-55 — a zone number, not an age designation). Village zone minimum lot areas/frontages: UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section — never filled in here. Township R-CL context, as the program's ledger states it: R-CL s.403 sets a 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum LOT area for that zone — carried as the Township's R-CL standard, not as a Fort Langley village zone claim. https://tol.maps.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/0c6ddccb89034b6787c285311714b8ce/data
- **SSMUH in the Township: Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024**, amending Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 for small-scale multi-unit housing in most zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings. There is NO 6-plex tier in the Township's framework (Bylaw 6020 has no 6-plex tier) — and no Fort Langley page claim of one is documented in the research, so no defect is asserted here. In the village, SSMUH operates inside the heritage and design controls above, not as an exemption from them. A March 9, 2026 Township council report on an SSMUH development permit area names the Fort Langley Community Plan among the plans Bylaw No. 6116 would amend — the form-and-character rules were still being written in 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
- **Secondary suites: capped at 120 m²** of gross floor area under Township rules, with Council's 2025 direction extending suites to ground-oriented housing including fee-simple rowhouses; parking requirements apply. https://udi.org/advocacy/updates/township-of-langley-secondary-suites-and-lock-off-units
- **Coach houses: fee-simple s. 102 units, never strata.** Under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102, a coach house is a second dwelling unit on a fee-simple lot, part of the same title as the principal house, and cannot be sold separately. A live s. 102 conflict is flagged in the program record (and in the lots sibling) and is NOT resolved here — confirm the current s. 102 text with the Township before relying on a coach-house angle for a specific lot. (Standing correction for this run; framework: https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx)
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements (Township).** Per the verified ledger carried in the FILE STATUS block: the Township's Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw is **No. 5382 (2019) — CONFIRMED**. The lots-and-land sibling's UNCONFIRMED stance on Township servicing is superseded by that ledger and is flagged as a conforming-pass item; the sibling itself is not edited from here. No Township DCC figure or subdivision timeline for Fort Langley is documented in the research file: those figures remain UNCONFIRMED — a subdivision angle on a village or edge lot must still be scoped with the Township (Approving Officer process, heritage/design overlays and flood/ALR status first) before any value is placed on it.
- **ALR rules in full (edge parcels — ALR ground exists on the edges per §2).** Residences on ALR land cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239 — deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval. Residence caps as the ALC states them: principal residence ≤500 m²; a secondary suite within the principal residence only; one additional residence without an ALC application up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, subject to Township bylaws. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **BC Assessment farm class — full three-tier form.** Gross annual value of at least $2,500 (farm area 0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 (<0.8 ha); application delivered to the assessor on or before October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an error and is not used here — it matters on Fort Langley's edges because an acreage priced on "farm status" must actually meet the tier for its size; no file in this group documents a flat-figure assertion for this area (that error is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, outside this group). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Flood diligence.** Ask the Township for the parcel's flood information before purchase; the Salmon River Pump Station's flood box gates close when Fraser River levels rise, and Council has authorized detailed design and tendering for pump station upgrades. For any strata purchase, also check the Form B insurance summary for flood coverage/deductibles. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Well / septic diligence (acreage edges).** GWELLS search for the well record (reports due within 90 days of well work) and Fraser Health authorized-person filings for onsite sewerage (record before construction, letter of certification after). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons
- **Bare-land strata — the form (no Fort Langley instance verified).** If a lot WERE bare-land strata, it would be strata tenure — Form B, fees and bylaws on the lot. The BC block: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page; depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. For Fort Langley's fee-simple lots and acreages there is NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fee — title covenants/building scheme plus municipal rules govern instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Property transfer tax (PTT).** Tiers: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000; $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M — an OPEN reconciliation item; this file follows $93,000, and no sibling for this area carries the $78,000 figure). First-Time Home Buyers': no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — relief turns on a qualifying home. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only.** Most individuals' vacant-land sales are exempt (e.g., personal-use land); taxable examples include business capital property, sales in the course of a business, and parcels created by subdividing into more than two parts. Developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots beyond that line are generally taxable. Anything finer for a specific Fort Langley acreage: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax.** The Township of Langley IS a designated taxable area — residential owners file the annual declaration; a vacant acreage's position turns on class and exemptions. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition — not conformed.** Secondary sources disagree on the vacant-land position after March 27, 2023; no primary text opened in this pass: UNCONFIRMED — CMHC tool + legal advice. No position asserted.
- **First-home programs — stated precisely.** The FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — relevant here mainly if a buyer builds a first home on a village/edge lot or buys The 1912 presale. New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsps-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What kind of acreage can I actually buy in Fort Langley?** Individual fee-simple acreage-edge parcels south and east toward the Salmon River valley — some of them ALR farm land — bought one parcel at a time. There is no acreage subdivision or acreage development product here, and no bare-land strata. Village lots (heritage-gated in the core) and Bedford Landing lots (tenure read address by address) are the other land forms. (Section 2 above.)
- **Q: Is the edge parcel in the ALR, and what does that change?** Check the parcel on the ALC maps first — the research confirms ALR farm land on the edges "in places" only. If it is ALR: no stratification, ALC approval for subdivision, principal residence ≤500 m², suite inside the principal residence only, and one additional residence up to 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha) without an ALC application, subject to Township bylaws. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is a Bedford Landing lot fee-simple or strata?** Either exists under the same community name: row homes are recorded fee-simple (Row House (Non-Strata)); the strata buildings are separate. The civic address and title decide — never the "Bedford Landing" label. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r2516238-23086-billy-brown-road
- **Q: Can I subdivide or add units to a village or edge lot?** The Township's SSMUH bylaw (6020, Nov 18, 2024) amended the single-family/duplex zones — with NO 6-plex tier — and in the village the Heritage Conservation Area, Development Permit Area and Design Guidelines sit on top of it. Subdivision runs under Servicing Bylaw 5382 (2019); parcel-specific servicing costs and timelines: UNCONFIRMED until scoped with the Township. On ALR land, subdivision additionally needs ALC approval. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I build a coach house on an edge lot?** A coach house under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s. 102 is a fee-simple second unit on the same title — never a separate strata lot or separate sale. On ALR land, the ALC's additional-residence caps (90 m² ≤40 ha / 186 m² >40 ha) and Township bylaws both apply, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. A flagged live s. 102 conflict means: confirm the current text with the Township first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How big can a suite be?** Maximum 120 m² gross floor area under Township suite rules. https://udi.org/advocacy/updates/township-of-langley-secondary-suites-and-lock-off-units
- **Q: Is the lot in a floodplain?** The village and its agricultural edges are low-lying and pump-station protected; parcel-level mapping is UNVERIFIED in the research — get the parcel's flood information from the Township before subjects. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Q: Does the edge parcel have farm class?** A separate check from ALR status: farm class is tested against BC Assessment's three tiers ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha; apply by Oct 31). ALR land does not automatically carry farm class, and neither follows from the other. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Q: Well and septic — what do I pull?** The GWELLS well record and the Fraser Health sewerage filings (record of sewerage system; letter of certification), both through the official systems in Section 3. No filings found is itself a diligence finding. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers
- **Q: What does it cost to hold an acreage here?** Township property taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% after July 2 and a further 5% after September 3, 2026; utilities billed separately) plus the speculation and vacancy tax declaration — the Township is a taxable area. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Q: Will GST apply to my acreage purchase?** Depends on the seller and subdivision history under the CRA GI-003 rules (Section 3) — personal-use individual sales are generally exempt; business-course and more-than-two-part subdivision sales are taxable examples. Get the position in writing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Bedford Landing" is a tenure trap for land buyers too, not just home buyers.** One community name covers fee-simple lots/row homes and strata buildings on a former mill site — a lot listing that says only "Bedford Landing" has told you nothing about title, fees or who maintains what. The address and the Land Title record decide. (Section 2 above.)
- **Heritage approval can outrank zoning on a core lot.** A lot can pass every zoning test and still need heritage approval for what is built on it — with only narrow exemptions (e.g., minor additions under 50 m² not fronting a road, conforming to the guidelines). Price core land on the heritage path, not just the zone. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **There is no 6-plex tier in the Township — and no Fort Langley page in the research claims one.** The absence is recorded here deliberately: no defect is asserted, and any future marketing claim of a village 6-plex right would contradict Bylaw 6020's framework. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
- **Flood protection is a Township works program, not a lot feature.** The Salmon River Pump Station provides the community's protection — including, per the Township, mitigation of flooding of Fort Langley's agricultural lands during freshet — its gates close as Fraser levels rise, and upgrades are in detailed design/tendering. An acreage's protection story is municipal infrastructure plus the parcel's own flood information, not a seller's assurance. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **The village's own school locator sample shows no middle-school tier.** The SD35 run for 8877 Bartlett St returned Fort Langley Elementary → D.W.
