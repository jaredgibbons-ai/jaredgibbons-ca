## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), VERBATIM with its sourcing:** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, provides that a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. This file's own research opened that source, and the text above is carried in the form the sibling files that opened the same source record it. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **The regulator:** real estate trading services in BC are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers

- **Farm class — full three-tier form:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. - **BC strata block, where strata is met (including any bare-land strata lot visited):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); REDMA s.21 7-day rescission for presales. - **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — follow $93,000 wherever a $78,000 figure is met and flag it (open reconciliation item; that figure lives only in a townhomes sibling elsewhere). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

City" is a claim to verify, not a fact. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
