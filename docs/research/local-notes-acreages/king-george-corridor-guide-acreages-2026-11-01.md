## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Community plan / OCP:** the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026), now carries the corridor's designations; the corridor's 1995 concept-plan designations were superseded through Corporate Report R246 (endorsed December 2, 2024). Bylaw No. 18020 (PlanSurrey 2013) is replaced and is NEVER cited as current. Zoning: **Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000**, shown parcel by parcel in the City's COSMOS map. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south
- **SSMUH (Surrey position, adopted July 8, 2024):** a maximum of **3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop**, applying to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. Homes fronting the boulevard's frequent bus service may fall inside a Frequent Bus Stop Area — check the parcel in COSMOS. Added-unit permission is not permission to subdivide. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey):** only one suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most **40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area**, and never inside a coach house or garden suite. The old 90 m² Surrey suite cap is NOT a current rule and is not used here. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision / servicing:** the **Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 VERIFIED** for Surrey; the City's process runs through its Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only — only the property owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence is required at **$358 per year** on the City's program page. Licence-figure conflict, disclosed: the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350/yr plus fees — this file carries the $358 program figure with the $350 proposal disclosed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Floodplain at the north end:** the corridor's north edge is the Nicomekl River, and Surrey's coastal floodplain runs along the Nicomekl and Serpentine Rivers and the Serpentine–Nicomekl lowlands. Parcel-level flood levels: UNVERIFIED — confirmed with the City per parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **Streamside / riparian (Surrey frame):** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in **Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and vary by stream class** — the setback for a specific parcel is read off Part 7A and COSMOS; no single riparian figure is ever stated in this file.
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (provincial frame — stated so it is never misapplied to this corridor; no corridor ALR parcel is documented in §2):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. No farm-class claim is asserted for any corridor parcel in this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property taxes (Surrey):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. South-end addresses near the White Rock line need the municipality checked — a White Rock civic address pays White Rock taxes on White Rock's schedule. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
