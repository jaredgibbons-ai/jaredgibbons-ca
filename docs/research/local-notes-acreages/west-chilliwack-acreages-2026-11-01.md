- **OCP: Chilliwack 2050, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — schedule map, stated in full so schedules are not confused:** Agricultural Plan = Schedule B1; Chilliwack Proper = Schedule B-2; Downtown = Schedules B3 + D-6; Eastern Hillsides = Schedule B4; Sardis = Schedule B5. - **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020 No. 5000 — §6 farm zones where met, and the AL zone minimums in full:** AL (Agricultural Lowland) — 7.5 ha minimum lot area for land excluded or exempted from the ALR or approved for subdivision within the ALR; 1 ha for a boundary adjustment; 0.2 ha for an ALC-approved homesite severance or boundary adjustment; 25 m minimum lot width. Per-parcel zone minimums beyond the AL zone: UNVERIFIED in this slug's research. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **ALR RULES IN FULL:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in West Chilliwack's research — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **SSMUH: Chilliwack Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024,** brought provincial small-scale multi-unit housing into Chilliwack zoning. On ALR farm parcels the practical added-unit rules are the ALR rules above, not SSMUH density — SSMUH does not create subdivision or multi-unit rights on AL-zone farm parcels. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **Subdivision and servicing — number HELD UNCONFIRMED, process documented instead:** no verified Chilliwack servicing bylaw number is asserted in this file. The candidate "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" is NOT adopted in the program's verified ledger and is never presented as verified — even though the City's subdivisions webpage states that subdivisions are subject to that bylaw; the conflict is flagged here and in FILE STATUS, and the ledger governs. What is documented is the process: under the Local Government Act and Land Title Act, all subdivision plans must be approved by a legally appointed Approving Officer before registration with the LTSA; a Preliminary Layout Approval (PLA) is the first major milestone, providing conditional approval and outlining all required servicing, engineering and legal steps for final approval (the City's guide states PLA issuance typically takes 8–12 weeks from application and a PLA is valid for 12 months). Confirm subdivision servicing, frontage and approvals with the City (and the ALC, where ALR applies) for the specific parcel. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=653 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8208
- **Flood rules:** the City's Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets minimum building setbacks of 60 m from the Fraser River, 75 m from the Vedder River and 30 m from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal; the City maintains a diking network for the Fraser and Vedder River systems. Measure the setbacks on any lot near a river or canal before valuing its buildable area. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- **Property taxes:** Chilliwack property tax rates are the sum of each taxing authority's levy, with separate rates for each property class and additional taxes levied for drainage and dyking on top of the municipal rates — read the full tax notice, not just the municipal line. 2026: notices are due the first working day after July 1 each year; the City applies a 5% late payment penalty to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, with an additional 5% late payment penalty on taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on acreage:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland is addressed by the CRA in a SEPARATE info sheet as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — a West Chilliwack farm purchase must never be assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file; the contract must state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What am I actually buying when I buy an "acreage" in West Chilliwack?** An individual fee-simple farm or rural parcel — land bought as farm land with a homesite, in a working agricultural landscape under the AL zone and the Agricultural Area Plan (Schedule B1). There is no acreage subdivision, no bare-land strata and no presale acreage product to buy instead. (§2.)
- **Q: Is this page about farms or about the industrial corridor?** Both framings exist in the record and the conflict is unresolved: the live page sells the Lickman / Progress Way / Yale Road West commercial corridor, while the board sub-area name also covers the rural west side. Confirm with the listing's own zoning and ALR status which market a given property is actually in before pricing it. https://jaredgibbons.ca/west-chilliwack.html
- **Q: Can I split the parcel, or stratify it later?** Stratify: no, if it is ALR (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Split: only within the AL zone minimums — 7.5 ha for ALR-excluded/exempted land or ALC-approved subdivision, 1 ha for a boundary adjustment, 0.2 ha for an ALC-approved homesite severance — and, in the ALR, only with ALC approval, through the Approving Officer / PLA process. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: How long does subdivision take?** The City's subdivision guide states a Preliminary Layout Approval typically takes 8–12 weeks from application and is valid for 12 months, outlining all servicing, engineering and legal steps for final approval — but no servicing bylaw number is verified for Chilliwack in this program (the candidate 3055 is held NOT adopted), so budget the process, not a bylaw citation. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8208
- **Q: What farm income keeps farm class?** All three tiers, not a flat figure: $2,500 on a 0.8–4 ha farm operation; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: A listing calls a Cottonwood Village home a townhouse on a lot. Is it land I would own?** No. Cottonwood Village (7610 Evans Road) is a manufactured-home park: you own the home and rent the pad on leased land — no strata, no Form B, no land title. Its placement (West Chilliwack vs Sardis West Vedder) and its lease terms (2076 vs November 30, 2039 vs ~16 years remaining) are both UNRESOLVED conflicts in the sources; decide on the address, the head lease and the pad tenancy agreement, not the listing label. (§2.)
- **Q: What flood setbacks apply to a rural lot?** The City floodplain bylaw requires buildings to sit at least 60 m from the Fraser River, 75 m from the Vedder River and 30 m from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal — measure them before valuing the buildable area of any lot near a river or canal. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- **Q: Is the acreage serviced?** Assume well and onsite sewage until proven otherwise — servicing for a single parcel was not confirmed in this research. Pull the well record and the sewerage filing for the parcel before removing subjects. (Research file §6.)
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a West Chilliwack farm acreage?** Do not assume either way. UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel until vendor status and farm use are applied; the contract must state who bears GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
