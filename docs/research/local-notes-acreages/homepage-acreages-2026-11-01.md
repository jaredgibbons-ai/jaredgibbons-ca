## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

No city frame is imported into this file — the homepage's 11 communities sit under different OCPs, zoning bylaws and servicing bylaws, and each community's own acreages file carries its frame in full. What follows is the province-level block an acreage buyer reaching land through this page meets, carried at program-ledger level; where a rule's application turns on the municipality, that is stated rather than conformed.

- **ALR RULES IN FULL (apply wherever ALR ground exists among the 11 communities — parcel status UNCONFIRMED per parcel from this page):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it — owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page; no farm-class figure is documented in this slug's research. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; the tax is based on fair market value on the day of registration at the Land Title Office. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. First-time buyers' program: a qualifying buyer is exempt on the first $500,000 where the fair market value is $835,000 or less, with a partial exemption between $835,000 and $860,000, on the Province's stated conditions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a SEPARATE info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — CRA governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. The gst-rebate-in-bc topic file in this program carries this frame in full. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
