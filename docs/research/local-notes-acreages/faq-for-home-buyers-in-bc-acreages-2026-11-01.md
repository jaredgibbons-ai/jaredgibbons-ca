## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — generic BC tiers with worked anchors (inputs + tier math):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000, on fair market value on the day of registration at the Land Title Office. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies to a qualifying home purchase); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (tier math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. The live page's PTT answer states the same tier structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://jaredgibbons.ca/faq-for-home-buyers-in-bc.html
- **PTT first-time buyers' program (Province, verified in the sibling passes this research reuses):** a qualifying buyer is exempt on the first $500,000 where the property is used only as a principal residence, has a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024), is 0.5 hectares or smaller and contains only residential improvements; a partial exemption may apply between $835,000 and $860,000. Note for acreage buyers: the 0.5-hectare limit means most acreage parcels fall outside this exemption — a parcel-level position is UNCONFIRMED until the specific purchase is read against the program rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: CRA addresses farmland sales by individuals in a separate info sheet and treats them as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **GST on homes, as the page states it and the rebate frame supports:** GST of 5% applies to newly built and presale homes and not to resale homes. https://jaredgibbons.ca/faq-for-home-buyers-in-bc.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary, carried per THIS slug's research verification status:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — CRA governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), gradually reduced between $1 million and $1.5 million, none at $1.5 million or more, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. **Home Buyers' Plan and FHSA figures in THIS file: the live page states HBP $60,000 and FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year — in this slug's own research the CRA HBP page returned 403 and the CRA FHSA main page verifies only the $8,000 first-year participation room, so the $60,000 and $40,000 lifetime figures are carried flagged UNVERIFIED here, following this slug's research position and not any other sibling's pass.** https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Down payment and mortgage insurance (CMHC, verified in the sibling passes):** insurance is required below 20% down; minimum down payment is 5% to $500,000, 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% on the portion over $500,000 for homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999, and 20% at $1,500,000 or more; CMHC's published premium range is 0.6%–4.5% of the total mortgage amount. The page's $750,000 worked example ($25,000 + $25,000 = $50,000 minimum) matches the verified tiers. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **CMHC premium error — expressly NOT documented for this slug:** this page states only that mortgage insurance "adds a premium" and gives no percentage range, so the 2.80%–4.00% error is NOT asserted here; that error is owned ONLY by the mortgage-calculator and first-time-home-buyer-program slugs.
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (generic province-wide frame, applying wherever ALR ground exists):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan IS a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: a principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence; one additional residence up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, with ALC approval needed beyond those limits; local bylaws may be more restrictive, never more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if less than 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer who plans to continue farming must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (only the horse-properties page owns it) — it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **BC strata block (for any bare-land strata lot met through this FAQ):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there an acreage section or acreage listings on this FAQ page?** No — the page carries no listings, parcels or land product at all; it is a process FAQ. Acreage stock is documented in each ground's own acreages and lots-and-land home files, cross-referenced in §2, never here. (§2.)
- **Q: What is a subject in a BC purchase contract?** A condition that must be met before the deal becomes firm — the page lists financing, inspection, property disclosure statement, title search and strata document review as the common subjects, completed during a negotiable subject-removal period; the page's "typically 7 days" norm is practitioner copy, UNVERIFIED as a stated norm in this file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/faq-for-home-buyers-in-bc.html
- **Q: How much PTT will I pay, and what does a first-time buyer save?** At the general tiers: $500,000 → $8,000; $800,000 → $14,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier over $3M. A qualifying first-time buyer is exempt on the first $500,000 at a fair market value of $835,000 or less (property 0.5 ha or smaller, principal residence only), with a partial exemption between $835,000 and $860,000 — so the $800,000 anchor falls to $6,000 for a fully qualifying buyer. (§3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a purchase?** On a new build or presale, yes — 5% at completion; resale homes are generally exempt. On vacant land, only the CRA GI-003 position in §3 is stated: most individual personal-use land sales are exempt, business/subdivided/developer sales are generally taxable, and anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What GST rebates can a first-time buyer of a new home claim?** The FTHB GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), gradually reduced to $1.5 million, none at $1.5 million or more — new HOUSING only, not bare land. The pre-existing New Housing Rebate returns 36% of GST to a maximum of $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. (§3.)
- **Q: What is the minimum down payment, and when is mortgage insurance required?** 5% to $500,000; 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% on the balance to $1,499,999; 20% at $1,500,000 or more. Below 20% down, mortgage insurance is required and CMHC's published premium range is 0.6%–4.5% of the mortgage amount — and no CMHC premium-range error is documented for THIS page (§3). https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Q: Can I combine the Home Buyers' Plan and the FHSA, and how much can I take out?** CRA states a person can make a qualifying FHSA withdrawal and also withdraw under the HBP for the same qualifying home if all conditions are met at each withdrawal (verified in the sibling passes). The amounts are flagged in THIS file: the page's HBP $60,000 and FHSA $40,000 lifetime figures are UNVERIFIED in this slug's research (CRA HBP page returned 403; the FHSA page read verifies only the $8,000 first-year room) — confirm both limits with CRA or your lender before planning around them. (§3.)
- **Q: If I buy ALR acreage, what residence can it have?** One principal residence of 500 m² or less (fill ≤1,000 m²), a secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); subdivision and any stratification of residences need ALC permission. (§3.) https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Does the rescission period protect me if I write without subjects?** The page states a 3-business-day rescission period with a 0.25% fee — that statement is UNVERIFIED in this file (no BCFSA page was opened in this pass), so confirm the current rescission rules with your REALTOR® or lawyer before writing without subjects. (§3.)
