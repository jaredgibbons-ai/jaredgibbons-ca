## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **The three-layer check (verified Delta model):** designation (OCP s. 1.5.1 / Map 2) → the parcel's zone (Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 zoning maps) → any Development Permit Area (OCP Appendix B) — in that order, in whichever municipality the property sits. Delta's OCP also carries a Regional Context Statement (Appendix A of Bylaw No. 8400) tying the municipal plan to Metro Vancouver's regional planning — the one verified example here of a municipal plan nesting inside a regional one. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Delta SSMUH:** Delta's provincially required zoning updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots (SSMUH Bylaws 8394 + 8395 per the sibling files), under the framework the 2024 OCP provides. SSMUH unit permission is not subdivision permission, and it does not override ALR rules on ALR parcels. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Delta subdivision/servicing — HELD UNCONFIRMED:** DSSS Bylaw 8288's adoption is UNCONFIRMED in this program (sibling governing-terms assertions are noted as conflicts wherever met, never asserted); document the City Approving Officer process instead of a number for Delta parcels.
- **City rules are researched once per city and cited per file — the ledger positions this topic touches:** Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw 8830 (1986) CONFIRMED; Township of Langley Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 5382 (2019) CONFIRMED; Abbotsford Development Bylaw number HELD UNCONFIRMED (process documented instead). No servicing number from one city is ever borrowed for another.
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the overlay on acreage in all four municipalities:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in this topic's research — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on acreage:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program (south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m) and is flagged wherever met as an OPEN reconciliation item, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file, and the GST treatment of any specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until the vendor's status and the parcel's history are applied; the contract must state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 noted in-line in some sources — March 13 governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **BC strata block — where a bare-land strata purchase is made in one of the four municipalities (redirect case):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there one land use plan for Surrey, Langley, Abbotsford and Delta?** No. Each municipality plans through its own Official Community Plan and zoning bylaw — there is no single shared document covering the four together (verified for Delta and Abbotsford in the research; the Surrey and Langley equivalents were UNVERIFIED in that pass). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/land-use-plans.md)
- **Q: What is an Official Community Plan?** In Delta's own description — the verified example here — an OCP describes where and what type of housing can be added across the city and contains long-term objectives and policies that guide decisions about land use, development, services, amenities and infrastructure. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: I'm looking at an acreage — what is the checking order?** Designation → zoning → development permit area, in that order, in whichever municipality the property sits (verified as the Delta structure: OCP s. 1.5.1 / Map 2, then Bylaw No. 7600 zoning maps, then OCP Appendix B DPAs) — and layer the ALC check over the municipal check on any farm or acreage parcel: the municipal designation does not settle what the Agricultural Land Commission allows. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Does an OCP designation settle what I can build on an acreage?** No. The designation is the first of three municipal layers, the zone sets the parcel rules (including minimum parcel size), any DPA adds conditions, and on ALR land the ALC's rules apply on top of all three — including ALC approval for subdivision. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: "Langley" on a plan document — which Langley?** Always ask. The City of Langley and the Township of Langley sit under different OCPs and different zoning bylaws; a property in one is not planned by the other, and the Township holds the farm belt. Confirm which municipality the parcel is in before opening either plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/land-use-plans.md)
- **Q: How do I check zoning and future land use for an Abbotsford acreage?** The City of Abbotsford's Development Planning pages direct property checks to the City's property information search or a property search in WebMap for current zoning, future land uses and development permit areas. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/development-planning
- **Q: Can I stratify an ALR acreage later, or split it for family?** Stratify: no — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Split: only within the zone minimums and, in the ALR, only with ALC approval. The residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside that residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What farm income keeps farm class on an acreage?** All three tiers, not a flat figure: $2,500 on a 0.8–4 ha farm operation; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a vacant acreage parcel?** At CRA GI-003 level: most individual sales of personal-use vacant land are exempt, but a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel subdivided into more than two parts. The answer for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and history are applied; the contract must state who bears GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What does it cost in transfer tax at acreage prices?** PTT is 1% / 2% / 3% by tier plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 including the top tier (§3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
