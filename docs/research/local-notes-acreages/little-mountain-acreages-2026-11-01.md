## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Chilliwack 2050, Bylaw No. 5500** — current city-wide plan; its Development Permit Areas are administered by the City's Land Development Department. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- **Hillside development rules — the governing frame on this hill:** Development Permit Area 2 (Hillside and Upland Areas) requires a Geotechnical Report from a qualified professional with each application; Development Permit Area 5 (Hillside Development) applies to all City lands with slopes of 20% or more, and within a designated DPA a Development Permit is required before subdivision, construction, addition or alteration of a building, or alteration of land, except where exemptions apply. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- **Zoning: HR and SHR hillside zones, Zoning Bylaw 2020 No. 5000** — minimums in §2; slope decides the lot math in SHR. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What does the geotechnical requirement cost me in time?** DPA 2 requires a Geotechnical Report from a qualified professional with each application — budget for the report and the permit before assuming a build, addition or subdivision timeline.
