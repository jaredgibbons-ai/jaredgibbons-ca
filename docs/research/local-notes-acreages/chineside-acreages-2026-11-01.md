## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning — Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996:** small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3; subdivision minimums 650 m² with 18.5 m width (R-1 and R-3) and 465 m² with 12.5 m width (R-2). https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=

- **SSMUH as the City states it:** up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules, where zoning and municipal services allow. Run the SSMUH math before pricing a house lot — confirm the exact zone (R-1 / R-2 / R-3) and services first. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Subdivision and servicing — Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 3558, 2003 (VERIFIED):** subdivision minimums run off the zone (above), and servicing runs under Bylaw No. 3558, 2003 — the consolidated bylaw requires the owner to grant or acquire statutory rights-of-way for the works and services needed to serve a subdivision or development, in a form acceptable to the City's solicitor, and to pay for traffic-control and street-name signage installed by the City at the owner's expense before subdivision approval or building permit issue. - **ALR RULES IN FULL (carried because the ALR check is mandatory diligence here, even though Chineside ALR status is UNVERIFIED in the research):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)).
