## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Burnaby frame in full:** Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026 — NEVER the former Zoning Bylaw 1965, No. 4742, which is repealed. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025 — the City's current OCP, a long-term document integrating land use, transportation, housing, environment, community facilities and economic planning, reviewed and updated every five years. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/official-community-plan
- **Servicing:** Burnaby servicing base number UNCONFIRMED (servicing ledger — the base bylaw was renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024; the process is documented, no number is asserted). Do not borrow another municipality's servicing number for Burnaby ground.
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** on June 10, 2024 Council adopted regulations rezoning all R District lots to the new R1 SSMUH District; under provincial legislation, R1 SSMUH lots are eligible to build up to 3 to 6 dwelling units depending on lot area and proximity to a frequent transit network bus stop, and the total unit count is inclusive of any secondary suites; the R1 zone still allows single-family homes as well as laneway homes, secondary suites, duplexes, triplexes, fourplexes and, on some properties, fiveplexes and sixplexes. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite Secondary-suite size cap and owner-occupancy rule for Burnaby: UNVERIFIED — the City pages read confirm suites as a permitted R1 form counted inside the unit total but state no cap and no owner-occupancy rule; suites classified as secondary suites under the BC Building Code must remain on the same title as the primary unit and cannot be stratified. Short-term rental rules in Burnaby: UNVERIFIED in this pass.
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried wherever ALR ground exists or the area is farm-adjacent (Big Bend flats):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a PERMISSION rule, never an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; local governments may regulate further but not be more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by HORSE-PROPERTIES); it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is expressly not attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property taxes (Burnaby):** Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026, after which a 5% late penalty applies, with an additional 5% penalty applied after the September due date to outstanding current taxes; the City has no authority to waive or modify the penalties. Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land — worked examples with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example at $800,000: $2,000 + $600,000 × 2% = $12,000, total $14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies to a qualifying home purchase). Worked example at $3.5M fully residential: $2,000 + $36,000 (to $2M) = $38,000, plus $1,500,000 × 3% = $45,000, plus $500,000 × 2% = $10,000 (over $3M), total **$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — follow $93,000**; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed (open summary-pass item). Full anchor set: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000; $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland caution per CRA: farmland sales are addressed separately and treated as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
