## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP / plan:** City of Chilliwack **Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 ("Chilliwack 2050")**, whose **Schedule B-2** is the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan (first added to the 2040 OCP as Schedule D through Bylaw 2018, No. 4533; purpose: guiding residential infill). The guide's statement that Bylaw 5500 was adopted December 16, 2025 was NOT confirmed on a City page in this program: that date is UNVERIFIED. Frame schedules: Sardis = B5; Agricultural = B1; Eastern Hillsides = B4; Downtown = B3 + D-6. Zoning: **Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000**, farm zones in §6. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **SSMUH (Chilliwack):** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, **No. 5395, adopted June 18, 2024**, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Suites / ADUs (Bylaw 5000):** secondary suite capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space (s.2); only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits, types including secondary suite, coach house and garden suite, garden suites capped at 100 m² (s.4.06(1)); a suite must be built with a permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and flood protection requirements, and new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Short-term rentals:** regulated under **Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141, adopted November 24, 2021** (repealing the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **Subdivision / servicing — number UNCONFIRMED, 3055 conflict documented, ledger followed:** the Chilliwack servicing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger. The candidate "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" is NOT adopted per the ledger and is NEVER presented as verified here; the City's subdivisions webpage names a bylaw of that number/title, and several Chilliwack lots siblings assert 3055 — documented as conflicts, flagged as conforming-pass items, ledger followed. The documented process is Approving Officer approval under the Local Government Act / Land Title Act, with Preliminary Layout Approval as the first major milestone in the sibling record. (Verified ledger; FILE STATUS)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (farm-adjacent / ALR ground):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by the horse-properties slug); it is NOT documented in this file's research and is never carried here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Floodplain:** as the guide states it, under the City's Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 4519 habitable floor space in areas protected by a standard dike must be at or above the applicable Flood Construction Level plus 0.6 m freeboard, with a $30 million dyke-raising program — the City's floodplain bylaw text and flood pages were NOT opened in this pass, so the Bylaw 4519 specifics and the $30 million figure as stated are UNVERIFIED in this file; parcel-level Flood Construction Level: UNVERIFIED. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-proper-south-guide.html
- **Property taxes (Chilliwack):** notices mailed at end of May, due the first working day after July 1; 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** 1% on first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt, while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: CRA treats farmland in a separate info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land — 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out, housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **BC strata block (where bare-land strata exists — none documented in §2 here):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
