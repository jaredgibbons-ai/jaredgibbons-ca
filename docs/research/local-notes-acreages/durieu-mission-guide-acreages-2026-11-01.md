- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the majority of the inhabited valley is ALR ground, so the full frame governs every ALR parcel in it:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and landowners who wish to pursue other uses or to subdivide ALR property must apply under the Agricultural Land Commission Act and secure ALC approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan IS a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — never the shorthand "cannot be stratified." Residence caps as the ALC states them: a principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite is permitted ONLY when located within the principal residence; one additional residence is permitted up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, and building more than the permitted number, or larger than the size limits, requires ALC approval. FVRD zoning and the ALC frame both apply where the parcel is in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
