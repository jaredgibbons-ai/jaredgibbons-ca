- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 ("Chilliwack 2050")** — the City's long-term vision and growth strategy embracing physical planning (land use / transportation / utility), economic development, environmental protection / enhancement and social / cultural initiatives, with directions for residential (urban, rural and hillside), commercial, industrial and institutional uses and the protection of the Agricultural Land Reserve. - **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs** what can be built as-of-right today — the same OCP-vs-zoning distinction the live page is built on. Farm-zone specifics sit in §6 of Bylaw 5000; no zone-by-zone table was opened in this slug's research, so permitted uses and minimum lots by farm zone are UNVERIFIED here beyond the suite / ADU rules below. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **SSMUH: Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, adopted June 18, 2024**, amending Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000. Within that frame: a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling (s.2); only 1 accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot (s.4.06(1)); garden suites are capped at 100 m² gross floor area; ADU parking is set at s.5.04. On ALR ground the ALC caps below govern instead. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — Chilliwack is farm-adjacent across most of its land base (Schedule B1 Agricultural Area Plan), so the full frame governs:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps as the ALC states them: a principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, and no more than 1,000 m² of fill can be placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted ONLY when located within the principal residence; one additional residence, detached from the principal residence, is permitted up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; building beyond the permitted counts and sizes requires ALC approval. City zoning and the ALC frame both apply; neither displaces the other. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices of $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — and new applications for farm classification must be submitted by the legislated deadline of October 31, and a buyer who plans to continue farming must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it — owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page; it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies to a qualifying home purchase); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

Farmland caution: the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a SEPARATE info sheet and treats farmland sales as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — no Chilliwack farm parcel is assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone.
