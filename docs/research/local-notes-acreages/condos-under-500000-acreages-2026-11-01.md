## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Why acreage rules are carried in a condo-band file at all:** they are not rules of this band. They are carried so a reader who starts at a $500,000 ceiling and moves to land is not misled: the moment the purchase is a parcel rather than a strata unit, the frame below — not the condo frame — governs. For the strata units this band actually surfaces, the BC strata block at the end of this section governs instead.
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (apply to any ALR parcel a buyer reaches from here; no ALR parcel is documented in this band):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR — and stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in this slug's research; it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors (inputs + tier math shown):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. $500K → $8,000 ($2,000 + $6,000); $700K → $12,000; $800K cap → $14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M fully residential → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000. A purchase inside this band meets only the 1% and 2% tiers. First-time buyers: full exemption requires fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective Apr 1, 2024), exempting the first $500,000 — a qualifying buyer anywhere on this page is inside that range on price alone, subject to the program's other conditions (including the 0.5-hectare property limit, which is itself an acreage-relevant condition: a parcel over 0.5 ha does not qualify in full). Newly built home exemption: full threshold $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **The $500,000 line is also a financing tier:** CMHC states homes $500,000 or less require a minimum 5% down payment; homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999 require 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% on the amount over $500,000; mortgage loan insurance is required with less than 20% down, at premium rates of 0.6% to 4.5% of the total mortgage amount; homes at $1,500,000 or more require 20% down and insurance is not available. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland is addressed by the CRA in a separate sheet and is usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — never assume a farm parcel is GST-exempt on the vacant-land rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 noted in-line in some sources — March 13 governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out — housing only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Subdivision and servicing:** no single servicing bylaw governs a region-wide band — servicing is municipal and per-parcel. The verified ledger carried program-wide: Township of Langley 5382 (2019) CONFIRMED; Surrey 8830 (1986) VERIFIED; the remaining municipal numbers are carried only as their own area files carry them, and where a number is held UNCONFIRMED in the ledger (including Abbotsford's development number and Chilliwack's servicing number) the process — Approving Officer / Preliminary Layout Assessment — is documented instead of a number.
