- **ALR position for this ground, stated in full so nothing is imported:** no ALR land was confirmed in Heritage Woods in this slug's research, so the ALR permission rules, residence caps and farm-class tiers that govern farm ground elsewhere in this program are NOT asserted for any Heritage Woods lot in this file. - **SSMUH — as the City states it:** small-scale multi-unit housing, including coach houses and accessory dwelling units, is permitted in RS and RT zones **outside** the transit-oriented areas; under the June 2024 Zoning Bylaw amendment (Amendment Bylaw No. 94, 2024, No. 3460), the City must allow up to four units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², and up to six units on such lots within 400 m of frequent bus stops. Secondary suites are allowed in all single-detached residential zones (except manufactured home parks); a suite may not be stratified. SSMUH is the only density lever on a Heritage Woods detached lot — it creates units, not acreage lots and no strata exit beyond its own terms. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
