- **Flood / streamside frame for the whole band.** The City operates over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure; all Delta properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — for a strata purchase this surfaces in the depreciation report, the insurance certificate and the building's history, not in the listing text. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (farm-adjacent ground; no ALR parcel is band stock).** Subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). Residence caps: principal ≤500 m² total floor area; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; one additional residence capped at 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) / 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form, never a flat figure.** Gross annual farm income required: $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha; $10,000 on an operation under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR asserted ONLY in the horse-properties file; it is not documented in this file's research and is never attributed to any file here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Property transfer tax in this band — tiers and worked anchors (inputs + tier math).** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — every purchase in this band is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers. Worked anchors: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K cap → $14,000 ($6,000 payable for a fully qualifying first-time buyer, who saves $8,000 on the first $500K); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M purely residential → **$93,000** including the further 2% tier over $3M (tier math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). A townhomes sibling elsewhere carries $78,000 for $3.5M — OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. First-time buyers' program: full exemption requires fair market value $835,000 or less (exempting the first $500,000), partial above $835,000 and below $860,000 — so a qualifying first-time buyer can in principle use the program anywhere in this band, subject to all other conditions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST — two distinct frames, never blended.** (a) Resale condos in this band carry no GST.
