## 2. THE ACREAGE ITSELF

**East Delta acreage is ALR farm-belt land — fee-simple farm parcels under the A1 Agriculture zone, bought and sold as farms and rural holdings, not as a bare-land subdivision market and not as hobby-lot inventory. No verified bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale LOT offering is named in the research, and none is asserted here. The one named project on the urban edge, Skye at Holly Park, is a townhome project (homes, not lots, not acreage) counted once in its Holly townhomes home file and cross-referenced below as an urban-edge development only.**

### Fee-simple farm parcels — the default (and effectively only) form

- Most East Delta land is farm or rural freehold rather than strata, mainly under A1 Agriculture zoning (one farm house per lot), with wells and onsite sewage where a property is not connected to a community sewer. Existing farm parcels vary in size and are confirmed parcel by parcel; no "typical" East Delta parcel size is published by any official source: UNCONFIRMED. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- Subdivision math that governs any new lot: in the A1 zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is permitted only where it complies with the OCP and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. A2 (s. 10.20.6) likewise sets an 8 ha minimum lot area created by subdivision; A3 (s. 10.30.6) does not permit subdivision, and s. 10.30.7 sets a maximum farm home plate of 3,600 m². https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A2.pdf
- Residential footprint on a farm parcel, as the research states it: one farm house per lot, farm house maximum 500 m², farm home plate maximum 3,600 m², one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit of maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on lots over 40 ha. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- Urban edge: East Ladner parcels take the RS and RD zones in the Ladner ladder, assigned parcel by parcel on the City's zoning maps — the zone on a specific edge parcel is a City-map read, not a sub-area assumption. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- Servicing: farm properties not connected to a community sewer use onsite sewage systems — Fraser Health requires a building with indoor plumbing that is not connected to a community sewer to use an onsite system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person; a property's well is searched in the provincial GWELLS registry, noting wells built before the Water Sustainability Act came into force may not be in the registry. Per-parcel water/sewer status: UNCONFIRMED until checked. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** A single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules; the East Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, and farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". SSMUH is added-unit potential on an existing lot, not a subdivision right — and it does not displace the ALR / A-zone subdivision rules in §2 on farm ground. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official

- **Streamside / flood diligence.** All Delta properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area; the City requires checks for flood protection requirements and the Riparian Areas Regulation before development near regulated water bodies, and is raising sections of the Boundary Bay dike between 72 Street and 88 Street with provincial grant funding. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-receives-provincial-grant-boundary-bay-dike
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — this file's core rule set.** Subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m² total floor area; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha — anything beyond needs ALC approval. Delta's A1 zone mirrors the caps (500 m² farm house; 3,600 m² home plate; 90 m² / 186 m² accessory farm dwelling unit). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form, never a flat figure.** Gross annual farm income required: $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha; $10,000 on an operation under 0.8 ha — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s. 5, applications by October 31 for the next taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR asserted ONLY in the horse-properties file in this program; it is not documented in this file's research and is never attributed to any file here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Property transfer tax — tiers and worked anchors (inputs + tier math).** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors used across this program: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 (at the first-time-buyer cap the $8,000 saving on the first $500K leaves $6,000 payable for a fully qualifying buyer); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M purely residential → **$93,000** ($2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 at the 3% tier + $10,000 further 2% on the $500K over $3M). A townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for the $3.5M anchor — that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met, never silently carry $78,000. First-time buyers' exemption: no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, phasing to $1,150,000 — both are home exemptions, and their application to a bare-land purchase is facts-specific: UNCONFIRMED for bare land in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only.** Most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (for example, land kept for personal use), while sales by developers/businesses and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE supplies. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED — and a farm parcel is never assumed GST-exempt on the vacant-land rule alone without advice on the specific sale. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

The GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs. First Home Savings Account: $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is East Delta in North Delta or Ladner?** Ladner — South Delta. The research file places East Delta on the Ladner farm-belt side (the rural farm land and East Ladner urban edge east of the Ladner village core); it is a Paragon sub-area name, not an official City neighbourhood, and no North Delta figure applies to it. - **Q: Can I subdivide a farm parcel here?** Only within the A-zone / ALR frame: a new A1 lot created by subdivision needs a minimum of 8 ha, OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable (home site severance excepted under ALC policy); A2 likewise needs 8 ha; A3 does not permit subdivision at all. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can I build a second house on an ALR parcel?** Within the caps only: principal residence 500 m² or less, a suite only inside the principal residence, and one additional residence of 90 m² or less (40 ha or less) or 186 m² or less (over 40 ha) — anything beyond needs ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can an ALR parcel be turned into bare-land strata lots?** No — ALR residences cannot be stratified; strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). (§3 above)
- **Q: Is a farm parcel on city water and sewer?** Often not — unconnected buildings use onsite sewage systems under the Sewerage System Regulation (Authorized Person design/install), and wells are searched in GWELLS, with pre-Water Sustainability Act wells possibly unrecorded. Confirm per parcel. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: What farm income keeps farm class?** The three tiers: $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" figure is wrong. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Q: Is GST due when I buy vacant farm land?** At CRA GI-003 level: a developer/business sale or a newly subdivided lot is generally taxable; an individual's personal-use land is generally exempt. The specific sale is a facts question — finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which school serves a farm address here?** SD37 School Locator by exact address (K–7 / 8–12, no middle tier) — no sample catchment was run for East Delta in the research, so none is stated: UNVERIFIED until the address is run. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Is flood risk a diligence item on the farm belt?** Yes — the City requires flood-protection and streamside checks before development near regulated water bodies, any lot within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch is in the Streamside Protection DPA, and Boundary Bay dike sections between 72 Street and 88 Street are being raised. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-receives-provincial-grant-boundary-bay-dike
