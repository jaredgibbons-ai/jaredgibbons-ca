## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

*The rules that actually govern this page are the RTB rules in the first bullets — stated at the level the page + its cited RTB sources support. The acreage-program blocks that follow (ALR, farm class, PTT, GST, first-home programs, strata, registry) are carried at GENERIC level only, labelled generic, because no parcel or city attaches to this slug; they assert nothing about any specific property.*

- **Rent increases (RTB, as cited on the live page):** the 2026 rent increase limit for residential tenancies is **2.3%**, and the published 2027 limit is **2.2%** (verified live Nov 1, 2026 on the research's RTB basis — the RTB Rent increases page the page cites, last updated August 27, 2026); rent can be increased only once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' written notice on form RTB-7 showing the exact dollar amount and effective date; only the current year's limit can be used (no carry-forward, no rounding up); a larger increase requires an arbitrator application on form RTB-52 or the tenant's voluntary written agreement. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Deposits (RTB, as cited on the live page):** a security deposit can be no more than half of the first month's rent, and a pet damage deposit the same cap, no matter how many pets, with no pet damage deposit for certified guide or service dogs and no other pet fees; a landlord cannot keep a deposit without the tenant's written permission or an RTB order; once the landlord has the tenant's forwarding address in writing (form RTB-47), the deposits must be returned with any interest within 15 days if there are no issues; deposit interest for 2026 is 0% per the page. Deposit interest for years before 2026 at a year-by-year level: UNVERIFIED in the research. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Agreements, inspections, ending a tenancy, disputes, sale (RTB, as cited on the live page):** RTB-1 written agreement, copy to the tenant within 21 days; RTB-27 move-in/move-out inspections with at least 2 opportunities offered (RTB-22 when the tenant was unavailable); landlord's notices RTB-30 (10-day, unpaid rent/utilities), RTB-33 (one-month, cause), RTB-32L/32P (three-month, landlord's/purchaser's use — must be generated through the RTB web portal, a non-generated notice is legally unenforceable; one month's rent compensation; occupant must live in the unit at least 12 months), RTB-29 (four-month, demolition/conversion); tenant's notice and RTB-8 mutual agreement; four dispute processes with monetary claims generally $35,000 or less; and on a sale the buyer becomes the new landlord and the tenancy continues on the same terms — showings/open houses need written notice or the tenant's permission. Whether each form number/version on the page is still current at rebuild time: UNVERIFIED — a per-form check against the RTB forms page. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Manufactured home park tenancies — the regime boundary only:** pad rent sits under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, NOT the rates above as a bare figure — the 2026 limit is **2.3% plus a proportional amount** for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees, and the 2027 limit is **2.2% plus the proportional amount**, on 3 full months' notice, once every 12 months — the same headline figures as the residential caps, plus the proportional amount. Where the MHPTA speaks of the decision-maker, the settled program wording is "an order of the director." MHPTA rules beyond the pad-rent limits and that wording: UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — GENERIC (no ALR ground attaches to this slug; carried in full because an acreage reader needs the complete permission form):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — GENERIC, full three-tier form:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does any acreage market attach to this page?** No. This is a province-wide RTB forms guide — no roster, no ground, no land stock. §2 states that plainly; an acreage question is answered on the relevant area's own file, not here.
- **Q: How much can rent be increased in BC in 2026?** Up to 2.3% for a residential tenancy (the published 2027 limit is 2.2%), only once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' written notice on form RTB-7; anything above the limit needs an RTB-52 arbitrator application or the tenant's voluntary written agreement. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: How much can a landlord charge for deposits?** A security deposit of no more than half the first month's rent, and a pet damage deposit of no more than half the first month's rent regardless of the number of pets; once the landlord has the forwarding address in writing, the deposits (with any interest) must be returned within 15 days if there are no issues, and a landlord cannot keep any of a deposit without the tenant's written permission or an RTB order. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: Can a landlord end a tenancy because the property is being sold?** No — the tenancy continues and the buyer becomes the new landlord on the same terms. Only once all conditions of sale are met can a purchaser who intends to live in the unit (or have a close family member live there) ask the seller in writing to give a Three Month Notice (RTB-32P), which must be generated through the RTB web portal. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: What form starts a tenancy, and when must the tenant get a copy?** Form RTB-1, or an equivalent containing all the standard terms the Residential Tenancy Act requires; the landlord must give the tenant a copy within 21 days of signing. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: Does the 2.3% limit also cap manufactured home pad rent?** Not on its own — a manufactured home site tenancy sits under the MHPTA, whose limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027, on 3 full months' notice once every 12 months. MHPTA rules beyond those limits are UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Why is there no market data in this file?** Deliberately — the page has no SnapStats row and no market figures, and the rent limits and deposit caps are statutory rules, not market data. No SnapStats figure is quoted for this slug. (§1.)
- **Q: What does PTT cost on a tenanted acreage purchase?** PTT follows the general tiers regardless of tenancy — generic anchors: $800K→$14,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.5M→$28,000 (fully residential, no exemption). The tenancy itself continues across the sale on the same terms (§3). (§3.)
- **Q: Is this page legal advice?** No — the page carries an express "general information, not legal advice" disclaimer, and RTB information officers cannot give legal advice either. This file documents the page; it adds no advice of its own. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
