- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — the tax that bites specifically at this band:** general PTT as published by the Province: 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000; 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000; 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000 — every purchase in this band reaches the 3% tier on its top portion. If a property has residential property worth over $3,000,000, a further 2% tax applies to the residential property value greater than $3,000,000; on a mixed-class property the further 2% applies only to the residential portion. Worked anchors with tier math: $1.8M→**$34,000** ($2,000 + $32,000); $2.0M→**$38,000** ($2,000 + $36,000); $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** (tier math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 at 3% on $1.5M + $10,000 further 2% on $500K).
