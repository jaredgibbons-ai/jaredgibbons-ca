## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **City plan:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the **Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current in this file. The City records that the East and West Clayton NCP designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update, so a lot's current OCP designation — not its NCP label — is the designation that prices it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000.** Its SSMUH framework (in force for this area since **July 8, 2024**) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. The former RF-12 / RF-12C zones were replaced by the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone and RF-SD by R6 Semi-Detached in those changes — older Clayton material using RF-12 / RF-SD names is using pre-2024 zone names. On ALR land the ALC rules below govern instead. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for Clayton's north and west edge parcels:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR owned ONLY by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page — it is NOT documented in Clayton's own research file and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Suites, coach houses and STRs (urban ground):** one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area**, never inside a coach house or garden suite, and not a separate strata lot; a coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), with lane access required for a coach house on smaller R3–R6 lots and one extra parking space required. Short-term rentals are permitted, **owner-only** (a short-term rental is the renting of a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one) — the licence figure is carried as **$358 per year** per the City's Short-Term Rentals page, with the conflict disclosed: the City's 2024 corporate report proposed **$350 per year plus fees** — confirm the current figure on the City's licence page before quoting it. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Subdivision and servicing:** Surrey servicing is **Subdivision and Development Bylaw No. 8830 (1986), VERIFIED from the City's own pages** — carried with that sourcing. For an ALR parcel, ALC approval is required in addition to the City process. **Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated in this file; read the setback per parcel in COSMOS.**
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on acreage:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program (south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m) and is flagged wherever met, not followed — it is not met in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant / farm land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; farmland is the caution — the CRA addresses farmland sales in a separate sheet and treats them as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions, so a Clayton edge-parcel purchase must never be assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone.
