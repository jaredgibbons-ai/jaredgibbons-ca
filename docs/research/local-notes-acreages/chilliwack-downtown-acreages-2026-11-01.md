- **Plan frame — the operative frame for this ground:** City of Chilliwack Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, "Chilliwack 2050," is the long-term vision and growth strategy for the whole city, including protection of the Agricultural Land Reserve; **Schedule B3 is the Downtown Neighbourhood Plan covering this ground and Schedule D-6 is the Downtown development permit area** — confirm a parcel's designation, DPA set and design-guideline position with the City before valuing redevelopment potential. Chilliwack's SSMUH amendment is Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, amending Bylaw 5000, adopted June 18, 2024 — ESTABLISHED from the amendment record in the consolidated Bylaw 5000. https://chilliwack.com/ocp (see ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, item 6)
- **Suites and accessory units (Bylaw 5000) — CONFIRMED:** a secondary suite is an accessory dwelling unit of not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building — the lesser of 90 m² and 40% (s.2); only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot, and the permitted ADU types include secondary suite, coach house and garden suite (s.4.06(1)); garden suites are capped at a maximum gross floor area of 100 m² (s.4.06(1)(g)); ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite (s.5.04). (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, item 7f)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried in full because Chilliwack is a farm-adjacent city (agriculture = 67% of the city land base) and because any downtown-edge parcel found to be in the reserve is governed by this frame at once:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Farmland is the caution: the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a separate info sheet — a farm parcel must never be assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone.
