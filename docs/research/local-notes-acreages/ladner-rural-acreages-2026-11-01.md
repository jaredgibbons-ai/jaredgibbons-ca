## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), single consolidated plan, NO lettered schedules.** Farm land is designated Agriculture (A), whose stated purpose is to maintain agricultural lands. Any "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" citation is an ERROR wherever met — the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the superseded Bylaw 3950. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Zoning — NUMBER CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted:** City pages cite Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017, while an internal title says "8400, 2025"; the A1 zone content in §2 is stated from the City's A1 zone sheet as cited, parcel by parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for ALR parcels in Ladner Rural:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR — Delta's A1 plate and dwelling rules in §2 are that further regulation. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Subdivision of farm land (A1, s. 10.10.8):** subdivision is not permitted except where it complies with the Official Community Plan Bylaw and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable; the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR — stated expressly: the flat form is NOT documented in this slug's research (the BC Assessment source could not be re-opened after the 2026-11-01 429, so the tier form here is carried in the program's settled form); the flat error is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **SSMUH: Delta Bylaws 8394 + 8395, adopted June 24, 2024** — the City's small-scale multi-unit housing incorporation. SSMUH is not a farm-belt lever: on A1/ALR ground the A1 zone and ALC rules govern, and SSMUH creates no farm lots and no farm strata. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision runs through the City against the zone minimums and works-and-services conditions, plus ALC approval in the ALR. HELD UNCONFIRMED: Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 — final adoption and parcel-level standards UNCONFIRMED; sibling lots files asserting "8288, 2024" in governing terms (holly-guide, port-guichon-guide, tsawwassen-east-guide land homes) are noted as conflicts wherever met, never asserted here.
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on farm acreage:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on farm and vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland is the caution: the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a separate info sheet — a Ladner Rural farm parcel must therefore never be assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
