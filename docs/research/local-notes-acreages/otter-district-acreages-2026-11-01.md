## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs.** Rural homes in this part of the Township sit under Bylaw 2500, and the feed's own listings name RU-2 zoning for acreage parcels; zone assignments for a specific parcel come from the bylaw Map Schedules through the Township's GeoSource. Subdivision runs through the Approving Officer under the Land Title Act, to the zone minimums carried in the lots sibling (RU-2 and RU-3 need 8.0 ha for a new lot; RU-1 needs 1.7 ha) — and ALR land also needs Agricultural Land Commission approval to subdivide. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **R-CL s.403 — stated exactly:** R-CL s.403 = 220 min / 464.5 max LOT area. A "355 m²" figure is NOWHERE in the bylaw and is never carried in this file. (Township frame, per brief; bylaw: https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx)
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024), NO 6-plex tier:** 3–4 units only where eligibility is met — inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to both municipal water and sewer, lot not larger than 4,050 m², and not in a zone with a minimum lot size larger than 4,050 m². Properties that do not meet those criteria are instead eligible for one residential unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit, depending on zoning — the frame most rural Otter acreages will fall under. Suite cap under the Township frame: 120 m². https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Coach house — s.102 definition, live conflict flagged:** in Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s.102, a "coach house" is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage; no coach-house size cap appears in s.100 as read in the program's rules-gaps pass, and a coach-house size cap is therefore not stated in this file. The fee-simple coach-house treatment under s.102 carries a live conflict in the program's Township files — flagged here, never resolved: a ground-floor detached suite is a different accessory unit with different rules, and the exact s.102 wording governs for the parcel. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — Otter District is farm-adjacent Township ground and its feed expressly carries ALR parcels, so the full frame governs wherever a parcel is in the reserve:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps as the ALC states them: land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless the parcel qualifies for an additional residence, and local governments may further regulate or restrict residences through zoning; a principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, and no more than 1,000 m² of fill (including structural aggregate for foundations and driveways) can be placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted ONLY when located within the principal residence, entirely contained within it; one additional residence, detached from the principal residence, is permitted up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, and building more than the permitted number, or larger than the size limits, requires ALC approval. Township zoning and the ALC frame both apply; neither displaces the other. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices of $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — and new applications for farm classification must be submitted by the legislated deadline of October 31.
