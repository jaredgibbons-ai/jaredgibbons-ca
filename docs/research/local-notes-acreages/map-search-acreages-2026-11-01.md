- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the one frame this slug's research does open, because the page tells readers to turn on the ALR layer:** a parcel's ALR status is checked on ALC maps by PID or civic address. Subdivision of ALR land requires ALC permission (limited approving-officer cases aside). ALR residences cannot be stratified without ALC permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)) — a **permission rule, never an absolute ban**, never cite Bulletin 05. Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite ONLY inside the principal, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); local bylaws may restrict further but not be more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
