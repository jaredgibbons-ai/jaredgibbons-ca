## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **The foreclosure process — settled treatment (see the FILE STATUS narrower-stance flag):** a proceeding for foreclosure of the equitable right to redeem mortgaged property, or for redemption, must be started by petition in the Supreme Court of British Columbia — Rule 21-7(1); all persons whose interest in or claim to the mortgaged property is sought to be extinguished must be made respondents — Rule 21-7(2); a petitioner who serves the petition must include the court's informational notice for foreclosure proceedings, Form 125 — Rule 21-7(4.1). The court may order that a respondent pay what is due under the mortgage and for costs within a redemption period the court may fix, and that in default of payment the respondent is foreclosed of the equity of redemption — Rule 21-7(5)(b); the redemption period has no fixed length in the Rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Court-ordered sale mechanics — settled treatment:** conduct of sale, court directions, reserve or minimum price, bid and offer rights, and sale by contract conditional on court approval sit in Rule 13-5 (including the court's power to define "the rights of a person to bid, make offers or meet bids" — Rule 13-5(4)(d)) and in the Supreme Court's foreclosure practice direction PD-66, with Form 60 certification. **Form B availability on court sales is UNCONFIRMED. PTT/GST treatment of court sales is UNCONFIRMED, distinct from GI-003 below.** https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo106/loo106/168_2009_02
- **Where the proceeding is filed:** unless the court otherwise orders, a foreclosure proceeding is commenced at the Supreme Court registry in the municipality where the land is located, if there is one there, and otherwise at a registry in the judicial district in which the land is located (Law and Equity Act, s.21(2)). **REGISTRY LEDGER: Maple Ridge's registry position is UNCONFIRMED — per-file Court Services Online checks govern.** The live page states its hearings run at the New Westminster or Vancouver registry; that is the page's own statement, unverified site copy, NOT a verified mapping — a live page's courthouse claim is never carried as fact. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol2/consol2/96253_01 and https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019** — a court-ordered acreage parcel keeps its zone (including the rural A/RS set in §2); the sale process changes the seller, not the zoning. Verify a specific parcel's exact zone with the City before pricing any use. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading

- **Suites and added units on a court-ordered acreage:** the ordinary City rules apply to the land after purchase — one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. A court sale confers no suite or unit rights beyond the parcel's own zoning. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame wherever a court-ordered parcel is in the reserve or farm-adjacent:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. A foreclosure changes none of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How does a BC foreclosure start?** By petition in the Supreme Court of British Columbia under Rule 21-7(1); everyone whose interest in the property is sought to be extinguished must be made a respondent, and the petitioner must serve the court's foreclosure information notice, Form 125, with the petition. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: How long is the redemption period?** There is no fixed length in the Rules — the redemption period is the period the court fixes in its order under Rule 21-7(5)(b), within which the respondent must pay what is due and for costs or be foreclosed of the equity of redemption. Ask which stage the file is at before spending money. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: Is my accepted offer on a court-ordered acreage final?** No — treat an accepted offer on a court-ordered sale as not final until the court confirms it under the Rule 13-5 / PD-66 process for that file, and get the conduct-of-sale lawyer's written process for the specific file before booking movers or spending on inspections. (§3.)
- **Q: Can someone outbid my accepted offer?** Plan for the possibility: the court's directions may define the rights of a person to bid, make offers or meet bids (Rule 13-5(4)(d)), and PD-66 governs practice in these proceedings. The live page describes sealed, unconditional competing bids at a hearing — that is the page's own statement, not a verified rule; get the seller's counsel's bid instructions for the specific file in writing. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo106/loo106/168_2009_02
- **Q: Where do I check the court file — and which courthouse?** Court Services Online eSearch is open to anyone with Internet access; Supreme Court civil file details include the file number, registry, party names and filed documents, and foreclosure files are class "H - Foreclosure" — the registry shown on the file itself is the check that settles it, because Maple Ridge's registry mapping is UNCONFIRMED in this program and the live page's New Westminster-or-Vancouver statement is unverified site copy. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- **Q: Is there a standard foreclosure discount on acreage?** No published source supports a standard foreclosure discount percentage. Price against the general market — Maple Ridge detached sold at a 97% sale-to-list ratio and condos & townhomes at 94% in September 2026 — plus the parcel's zone, ALR status and condition; the live page's own FAQ makes the same point in its words (discounts reflect condition, as-is risk or occupancy rather than automatic below-market pricing). (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.) https://jaredgibbons.ca/mapleridgeforeclosuresandcourtsales.html
- **Q: What taxes do I budget on a court-ordered acreage?** Budget with advice, not assumptions: PTT and GST treatment of a court-ordered purchase are UNCONFIRMED in this program (distinct from the general GI-003 frame in §3) — have your lawyer cost both on the specific property before you set your ceiling.
