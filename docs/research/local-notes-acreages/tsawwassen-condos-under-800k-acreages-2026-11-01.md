- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame wherever ALR ground exists or the area is farm-adjacent (Tsawwassen is farm-adjacent Delta ground):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR — stated expressly: the flat form is NOT documented in this slug's research; it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **SSMUH: Delta Bylaws 8394 + 8395, adopted June 24, 2024** — the City's small-scale multi-unit housing incorporation, City lands only. SSMUH does not apply on TFN lands and creates no lots on ALR ground, where the zone and ALC rules govern. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future

- **Property transfer tax (PTT) in this band:** the general tiers are 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000; 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000; 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000 — a purchase under $800,000 is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers, where the general tax applies.
