Route 601 runs Boundary Bay trips via 12 Avenue, Boundary Bay Road, 3 Avenue and 67 Street to 1A Avenue, continuing via South Delta Exchange and Ladner Exchange to Bridgeport Station. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf

## 2. THE ACREAGE ITSELF

**Position, stated at the top: Tsawwassen East has large-lot ground but no acreage market. No named acreage subdivision, no acreage parcel roster, and no presale acreage offering was verified here. What exists is (a) individual large fee-simple lots — notably RD2 waterfront lots in Beach Grove at a 4,000 m² subdivision minimum, just under an acre as a minimum, not multi-acre parcels — and (b) Southlands, a 537-acre master-planned community whose land phases are built homes in named collections, not acreage parcels for sale. No parcel count, parcel size beyond the zoning minimums in Section 3, or price is stated.**

### Beach Grove / Boundary Bay shore — the large-lot ground

- **Beach Grove waterfront lots — fee-simple, large-lot form.** 1728 Beach Grove Road is confirmed zoned RD2 (Duplex/Single Detached Residential 2); RD2 carries a **4,000 m² minimum lot size for subdivision**, a zone the bylaw states is for waterfront lots in Beach Grove. RD2/RD3 and RS1–RS3 sit at 4,000 m² in the ladder, so east-side lots — especially on the waterfront — are much larger than town-centre minimums; urban zones RS4 (610 m²) through RS7 (335 m²) also exist in the ladder and zoning is parcel by parcel. These are subdivision minimums in the zones as the research states them, not surveyed sizes of any parcel offered. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/215646/
- **Character of the ground:** detached and duplex houses on freehold lots facing Boundary Bay behind the Boundary Bay dike — estate-scale house lots, not hobby farms and not working-farm parcels. No farm operation on any named east-side parcel is documented in the research; hobby-acreage vs working-farm distinctions are therefore not asserted for individual parcels here. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — consolidated, NO lettered schedules.** The Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; the current OCP consolidated the former area plans into a single Land Use Plan and does not use the old "Single Family Residential" designation (s. 1.5.1). **"Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" is an ERROR wherever met**, swept to zero across the sibling programs. Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (City sheets cite No. 7600, 2017; an internal title reads "8400, 2025"). Zoning is parcel by parcel on the City maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf
- **Waterfront setback:** Zoning Bylaw s. 6.2.3 sets a waterfront setback for lots fronting Boundary Bay along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue, under which a seawall may encroach into the minimum front setback; the bylaw states no Beach Grove flood construction level — the FCL question goes to the permit area and the City, not the zoning text. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands) — the east side's overlay, and it names Southlands expressly:** no building with the underside of a wooden floor system or the top of a concrete slab for habitable areas lower than **2.9 metres GSC datum**; minimum land surface elevation around construction **2.3 metres GSC datum**; no building within **7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area**. Whether a given lot falls inside SD3 needs a DeltaMap check for that lot. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Dyke / flood frame:** the east side faces Boundary Bay behind the Boundary Bay dike; Delta is the diking authority (over 63 km of flood protection, 31 pump stations, ~538 km of drainage). **Foreshore tenure:** the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; tenure is by lease or licence only via FrontCounter BC, and private moorage is held under General Permission or Specific Permission — a "private dock" is not land the seller owns. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024** — four units on all residential properties in Delta (the six-unit tier is Scott Road / North Delta). Added units are not subdivision and do not waive SD3 or any permit area. **Subdivision/servicing — CONFLICT FLAGGED, adoption not asserted:** the sibling lots-and-land files (this ground's land home among them) cite the Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 (repealing No. 7162, 2015), under LGA s. 506, in governing terms; the program's verified ledger holds the **adoption of DSSS 8288 (2024) UNCONFIRMED**, so the sibling assertion is noted here as a conflict and 8288 is not asserted as governing in this file. Parcel-specific requirements beyond that UNCONFIRMED — confirm with the City. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **ALR rules in full (farm-edge parcels):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239 deposit = subdivision; ALCA s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval. ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). **BC Assessment farm class — full three-tier form:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. - **Bare-land strata block (the form — applies to Beachcomber Cottages lots as documented in the land home file, and to any strata Southlands phase):** Form B (SPA s. 59) discloses monthly fees, amounts owed, approved levies still payable, CRF balance and insurance summary, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page. Depreciation reports every 5 years for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, non-deferrable. CRF minimum 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (long-term; short-term may still be restricted). Only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2; May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax (PTT), with worked examples (inputs stated, tier math shown):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. No exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $700,000 → **$12,000**; $900,000 → **$16,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 fully residential → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 (3% × $1,500,000) + $10,000 (further 2% × $500,000) = **$93,000**. First-time buyers: exempt on first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 (at the $800,000 example: $14,000 gross, $6,000 after the $8,000 FTHB relief). Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — can apply to a never-occupied new HOME in Southlands, not to bare land alone. **OPEN reconciliation item:** a townhomes sibling (tsawwassen-homes-over-1-8m) carries $78,000 for the $3,500,000 input; this file follows **$93,000** and flags the conflict. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST — land and new homes, CRA level only:** CRA GI-003: most individual personal-use vacant-land sales are exempt; business-use, in-the-course-of-business and more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable — developer/newly-subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. A live page's $1.25M straight-line phase-out row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year; CMHC 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Beach Grove an acreage area?** Not in the market sense — it is large-lot waterfront ground: RD2's subdivision minimum is 4,000 m², so parcels are estate-scale house lots just under an acre at the minimum, behind the Boundary Bay dike. No acreage subdivision or farm parcel roster exists here. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf
- **Q: Can I buy an acreage parcel in Southlands and build my own house?** No phase is documented as selling vacant parcels — Southlands phases sell built homes in named collections, each with its own tenure, and the community's bare-land strata form (Beachcomber Cottages) is documented in the East lots-and-land home file, cross-referenced in Section 2. (Section 2 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Beach Grove waterfront lot?** RD2's subdivision minimum is 4,000 m², and the waterfront setback (s. 6.2.3), SD3 hazard rules and a DeltaMap lot check all sit on top of the zone math. Most waterfront parcels are one homesite at that minimum, not a subdivision play. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf
- **Q: What flood rules touch a shore or Southlands-edge lot?** SD3: 2.9 m GSC datum habitable floor level, 2.3 m minimum land elevation around construction, 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of a tidal area — and the shore sits behind the Boundary Bay dike Delta maintains. Confirm the lot's SD3 application with the City before design work. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: Is the foreshore / a dock part of the land?** No — the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; moorage is tenure by lease/licence via FrontCounter BC under General or Specific Permission. Price the house lot, not the water. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **Q: Is the farm land beside Southlands future acreage inventory?** No — the plan's farm acres (275-acre Southlands Farm + 50-acre community farm) are planned agriculture inside the 537-acre community, and ALR ground generally cannot be stratified and needs ALC approval to subdivide. Do not price farm ground as the next phase without a City/ALC source. https://renx.ca/century-group-long-awaited-southlands-project-begin
- **Q: Could a big east-side parcel be in the ALR?** Possibly at the farm edge — no area-wide split is published, so check the parcel on the ALC maps by PID or civic address before pricing in a second dwelling or a split; if it is in the ALR, the Section 3 caps and approval rules govern. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Is an east-side lot serviced?** UNCONFIRMED per parcel — confirm City connections for the specific parcel; for any unserviced parcel, GWELLS plus Fraser Health's onsite-sewerage rules apply. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a large-lot purchase here?** On a resale fee-simple lot, no GST question arises. On a new developer home in Southlands, yes — with the FTHB rebate for eligible first-time buyers and the New Housing Rebate underneath. On bare land trading separately, CRA's GI-003 frame governs by seller/history. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Beach Grove / Southlands address?** Address-based: the sample (570 Boundary Bay Rd) maps to South Park Elementary and South Delta Secondary. Run the exact address in the SD37 locator before writing a school into an offer. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
