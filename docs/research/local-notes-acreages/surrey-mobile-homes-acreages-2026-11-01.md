The only land-owning forms in this topic are the bare-land strata form (own the lot as a strata lot) and the fee-simple owned-lot form, documented below as forms — with no specific community or parcel counted as acreage here. There is no land ownership, no title to the site, and no acreage in any sense — the assessed value of a home in a park does not include the value of the land. SSMUH is an owned-lot framework; it does not convert a rented pad into land. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites:** only one suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, and never inside a coach house or garden suite. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite

- **Streamside / riparian:** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, with different setbacks by stream class — NO single riparian figure is stated for Surrey generally; the parcel's class and Part 7A govern. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only — only the property owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, business licence **$358 per year** on the City's program page. **Conflict disclosed:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposing the STR bylaw amendments put the licence at $350/yr plus fees — this file carries $358 with the $350 proposal disclosed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Manufactured-home framework (the topic's governing rules):** manufactured homes include modular and mobile homes, typically built in a factory off-site and delivered and assembled on-site; modular homes are built under CSA A-277 standards and mobile homes under CSA Z-240 standards. In BC the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if the transaction is registered; buyer and seller file the original signed and witnessed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership forms through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the Province directs buyers to make sure the CSA certification label is attached at the point of sale. A home may not be sold unless it has the original legible CSA decal attached; if removed or lost, the home must be re-certified (Technical Safety BC licensed electrical contractor inspection; the electrical approval label can be used in place of a CSA label to list the home). A permit to move a manufactured home is issued by the Manufactured Home Registry, a tax certificate for transport purposes is required (with exceptions including a move to a different pad within the same park), and the transport permit expires 30 days after issue. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Pad tenancy and assignment (MHPTA):** manufactured home site tenancies are covered by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act; landlords and tenants must have a written tenancy agreement for the site. Assignment permanently transfers the pad tenancy to the buyer when the home is sold and remains in the park; under MHPTA s.28 a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an arbitrator's order, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it; before asking, the seller must give the proposed buyer written copies of the park rules and the relevant parts of the tenancy agreement; and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Pad rent increases — percentages ONLY:** landlords in manufactured home parks can only increase rent with 3 full months' notice, can increase rent every 12 months, and the increase cannot exceed the yearly rent increase limit — **2026 = 2.3% + proportionate; 2027 = 2.2% + proportionate** — where the proportionate amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park; a landlord may also apply to the Residential Tenancy Branch for an additional increase for expenses or capital expenditures. **NO pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact in this file; pad rents quoted inside individual listing cards — and any live-page pad-fee range — are UNVERIFIED site copy.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Taxes on a home in a park:** a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried for any owned-lot parcel in this topic that sits in the reserve:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m² (with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction); a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Do all Surrey "mobile homes" sit on rented pads?** No. The Surrey feed holds both tenures: park homes where the buyer owns the home and rents the site under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, and South Surrey strata communities where the buyer owns the land and pays a strata or community fee instead of pad rent. Only the second form involves land ownership — and it is strata tenure, not acreage. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-mobile-homes.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: Is a mobile home on a pad an acreage or land purchase?** No — stated plainly: you own the home and rent the site. The assessed value does not even include the land. It is not lot stock, not land ownership and not acreage, in this program or in law. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Q: Can the park raise my pad rent when I buy?** No — on assignment of the site tenancy the pad rental for the new tenant stays the same, and the scheduled date for a subsequent rent increase does not change; increases otherwise require 3 full months' notice, at most once every 12 months, within the yearly limit (2026 = 2.3%; 2027 = 2.2%) plus the proportionate levy and utility amount. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What if the home has lost its CSA label?** A manufactured home may not be sold unless it has the original and legible CSA decal attached. If the label is removed or lost, the home must be re-certified: Technical Safety BC arranges a licensed electrical contractor's inspection, and the electrical approval label prepared can be used in place of a CSA label to list the home for sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Is Breakaway Bays a verified 55+ park?** No — NOT verified 55+ in this program. The MLS basis ("NO AGE RESTRICTION" / "Family Park") governs over the directory's 55+ claim, and no park fact about it is asserted from either basis. Verify any age rule from the park's own written rules before writing. (§2.)
- **Q: What is the difference between a mobile home and a modular home in BC?** Both are manufactured homes built off-site, distinguished by their CSA standard: mobile homes are built under the Z-240 standards and modular homes under the A-277 standards, and the CSA label on the home states which applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
