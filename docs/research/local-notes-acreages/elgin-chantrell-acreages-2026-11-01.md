## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) is CURRENT — Bylaw 18020 is NEVER cited as current.** Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000; the South Surrey ground is governed under that bylaw, and no single City land use plan for the "Elgin Chantrell" name exists — confirm a parcel's zone and OCP designation directly. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **RA zone figures — page statements, held UNVERIFIED:** the live page states a 4,050 m² minimum lot size for a new RA subdivision (50 m width / 60 m depth, Part 12 of Bylaw 12000), up to two dwelling units on an RA lot, and agriculture/horticulture uses only on lots of 2 hectares or larger. The Part 12 text itself was not re-opened in the research pass (429, stopped rather than retried) — do not quote these figures for a parcel until they are read in the current consolidated bylaw. https://jaredgibbons.ca/elgin-chantrell.html (research file §4)
- **SSMUH:** Surrey allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (Jul 8, 2024 framework). SSMUH is an urban-lot framework — it creates no acreage lots on RA ground and does not override the ALR frame on an ALR parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites:** one secondary suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit; no 90 m² cap was found in the current consolidated bylaw text; a suite is not a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Coach houses and garden suites:** all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; the unit must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house, and one extra parking space is required. The RA uplift to 120 m² is the confirmed coach-house figure that applies to this pocket's acreage lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — for any parcel that proves to be in the reserve:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in Elgin Chantrell's own research file — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Heritage constraint:** Elgin Heritage Park and the Stewart Farmhouse at 13723 Crescent Road are City-designated heritage entries (Bylaws 12922 and 7823) — a property near the Crescent Road heritage cluster needs its heritage status checked before renovation or subdivision plans are priced. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/heritage-bylaws
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer against the zone minimums and the Subdivision and Development (servicing) Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 — VERIFIED from the City's own Land Development / Servicing Agreement pages. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 and vary by stream class — the Nicomekl River edge makes this a live check on river-side parcels — NO single riparian figure is ever stated; read the setback per parcel in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** an STR is the renting of a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, and tenants cannot run one. LICENCE FIGURE CONFLICT, carried openly: the City's STR page states a business licence at $358/yr, while the City's 2024 corporate report states $350/yr + fees — carry $358/yr with the $350/yr + fees figure disclosed, and direct readers to the City licence page before quoting either. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

Farmland is the caution: the CRA addresses farmland sales in a separate info sheet — never assume an Elgin Chantrell farm parcel is GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year.
