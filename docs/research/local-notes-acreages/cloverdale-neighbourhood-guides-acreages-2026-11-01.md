## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP / zoning:** OCP = Surrey 2050 Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — NEVER cite Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs across all three guide areas. Streamside/riparian setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is stated in this file, and the Serpentine guide's floodplain subject matter makes that per-stream-class check a parcel-level step, not a hub-level number. Confirm a parcel's designation and streamside position in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH and suites:** Surrey's SSMUH frame (Jul 8, 2024): a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Only one secondary suite is permitted per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the Serpentine guide's ground, parcel by parcel, wherever a parcel is in the reserve:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Stated expressly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in Cloverdale Neighbourhood Guides' own research file — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file; no farm-class claim is asserted for any Serpentine parcel. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Subdivision and servicing:** Surrey's Subdivision and Development Bylaw 1986, No. 8830 is VERIFIED in the standing ledger and governs subdivision servicing across all three guide areas. DCC rates for a specific subdivision: UNVERIFIED in this slug's research — no DCC bylaw or rate page was opened, and no DCC figure is stated. On Serpentine rural ground, confirm the well record in the provincial GWELLS application and the septic position through Fraser Health / the Sewerage System Regulation, because the guide's own frame is wells and septic, not municipal services. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; business/developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland is the caution: the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a separate info sheet — a Serpentine farm parcel must never be assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file; the contract must state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 noted in-line in some sources — March 13 governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. On ALR land any qualifying new home must first fit the ALC caps above. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Short-term rentals:** Surrey STRs are permitted owner-only — a short-term rental is the renting of a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence is required before operating. **Licence-figure CONFLICT carried as directed: the program figure is $358/yr; the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350/yr + fees — carry $358 with $350 disclosed, and confirm on the City licence page.** https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
