## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Delta plan:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated — NO lettered area-plan schedules; farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1 purpose: maintaining agricultural lands; designations on Map 2; Agriculture policies at s. 2.6, informed by the City's 2023 Agricultural Plan). - **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for Agriculture (A) / reserve parcels:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval (apart from the limited cases in the ALR General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission). ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum total floor area 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence at 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on larger parcels, where Delta bylaws also permit it. Delta's A1 zone mirrors this: one farm house per lot, 500 m² farm house maximum, 3,600 m² farm home plate maximum, one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit at the same 90 m² / 186 m² limits. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR — and it is asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (the HORSE-PROPERTIES page owns it); no flat form is documented in this slug's research and none is attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **SSMUH (urban ground):** Delta Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots. Added-unit permission is NOT subdivision permission: it does not create new lots and does not waive the zone minimums in §2 or any permit area. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Subdivision and servicing:** for a non-ALR parcel, subdivision runs through the City against the zone minimums; for an ALR parcel, ALC approval is required in addition. **Delta's Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw 8288 (2024) adoption is UNCONFIRMED** — where unconfirmed, the process is documented instead of a number: confirm the servicing standard for the specific parcel with the City before pricing. **CONFLICT noted, never followed:** the tsawwassen-east-guide land home asserts "8288, 2024" in its governing terms; that assertion is a sibling governing-terms conflict, recorded here and in the FILE STATUS block, and is not asserted in this file.
- **Hazard permit areas:** SD2 "English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment)" (OCP Appendix B, hazardous-conditions category) covers the west shore — mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence; development affected by hazardous conditions is subject to conditions set by a suitably qualified Professional Engineer, and no building may sit within 7.5 m of the natural boundary of the sea.
