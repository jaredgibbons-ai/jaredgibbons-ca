## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for this whole topic:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence — a separate or detached dwelling cannot be used as a secondary suite, and local bylaws must also permit suites; one additional detached residence permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — maximum 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha — and further residences beyond what is permitted can only be approved by the ALC where the residence is necessary for farm use. Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. Replacing an existing principal residence has its own ALC conditions, including ALC approval to live in the old residence while a new one is built. One hectare = 2.471 acres. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.nrcan.gc.ca/maps-tools-publications/tools/geodetic-reference-systems/converting-units-measurement/10798
- **ALR short-term accommodation:** whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence is not permitted outright in the ALR and requires ALC approval; an additional residence may be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism accommodation on a property that is actively farmed and holds farm classification under the Assessment Act. The City layer sits on top (§ below). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 if the total area of the farm operation is less than 0.8 ha; applications must be submitted by the legislated deadline of October 31, for consideration for the next assessment and tax year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (the HORSE-PROPERTIES page owns it) — no flat form is documented in this slug's research and none is attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **City frame:** OCP = **Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026)** — never 18020 as current; Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Servicing: **Subdivision and Development Bylaw No. 8830 (1986), VERIFIED on the City's own pages** — for an ALR parcel, ALC approval is required in addition. Streamside setbacks in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated (the South Newton plan area's fish-bearing creeks in §1 are the live example for creek-side farm parcels).
- **Suites / SSMUH — urban rules, expressly NOT farm routes:** Surrey's urban suite rule is one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most **40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area**, never inside a coach house or garden suite and not a separate strata lot. Surrey's SSMUH framework — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — applies, on the City's own statement, to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. SSMUH is an urban-lot framework; it is not a route to extra dwellings on ALR farm parcels, where the ALC rules above govern. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Short-term rentals (City layer):** permitted, **owner-only** — only an owner who lives at the property as their primary residence may host; tenants cannot operate one. Licence figure carried as **$358 per year** per the City's Short-Term Rentals page, with the conflict disclosed: the City's 2024 corporate report proposed **$350 per year plus fees** — confirm the current figure before quoting it. On ALR land the ALC approval / agri-tourism conditions above apply first. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on farm acreage:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program (south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m) and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on farmland — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; farmland is the caution — the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a SEPARATE info sheet and treats farmland sales as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions, so a Surrey farm purchase must never be assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file, and the contract must state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 noted in-line in some sources — March 13 governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. On ALR land any qualifying new home must first fit the ALC caps above. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **BC strata block — where a strata form is met at all:** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). ALR farm parcels themselves are freehold and cannot be stratified (§ above) — the block is carried for completeness where a buyer cross-shops strata product, not as a farm rule. Pad ground is NOT lot stock / land / acreage; pad tenancy percentages only (2026 = 2.3% + proportionate; 2027 = 2.2% + proportionate), NO pad-rent dollar figure as fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Speculation and vacancy tax:** application to any specific farm parcel is UNCONFIRMED until its assessment class and the current exemption list are read. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply here.

- **Q: Where are Surrey's farms?** There is no single Surrey farm belt in the sources used here: the live surrey-farms feed's three listings at reading sat in Sullivan Station (two, on the Colebrook side) and Fleetwood Tynehead (one), and the page's own related links point to Cloverdale Farms, South Surrey Farms and Port Kells Acreages as separate pockets. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-farms.html
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Surrey?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a Surrey farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and City of Surrey bylaws must also permit it; anything beyond that requires an ALC application showing the residence is necessary for farm use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: I want to live in the old house while I build the new one — allowed?** That replacement-house route needs ALC approval; demolishing and removing the old residence first is the route that does not. Price the plan accordingly. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
