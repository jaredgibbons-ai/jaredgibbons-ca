## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Governing plan — FVRD Hemlock Valley Official Community Plan (November 2019).** The FVRD is responsible for administering the plan; its neighbourhood areas are Hemlock West, Hemlock East and Hemlock North. **The OCP bylaw number and any FVRD zoning bylaw number: UNVERIFIED** — no number is asserted in this file, and no neighbouring municipality's bylaw numbers (Mission's DSCB 5650-2017 and Chilliwack's numbers expressly included) are borrowed for FVRD ground. Subdivision and servicing on FVRD ground are therefore documented as a process instead: confirm the parcel's plan designation, DPA status and servicing system with the FVRD, and read any Crown-land / CRA licence position on title, before pricing any division or build. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried conditionally, because no ALR parcel is documented in the valley (§2) and the rules govern any parcel where the per-parcel check finds reserve status:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in Hemlock's own research file — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. No valley parcel's farm-class position is documented in the research, so none is asserted. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program (south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m) and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file, and the GST treatment of any specific valley lot is UNCONFIRMED until the vendor's status and the parcel's use are applied; the contract must state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 noted in-line in some sources — March 13 governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **If a strata purchase is made in the valley (the Sakwi Creek / Snowflake product):** the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). - **Property taxes — the RURAL regime, in full:** a property not located in a city, town, district or village is in a rural area; its owner receives the property tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays property taxes to the Province, not to the FVRD. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural property taxes are due and payable on July 2 of the year they are first levied, a 5% penalty is added to any portion that remains unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% penalty is added to any portion that remains unpaid on October 31. The City of Mission's flat 10% penalty does NOT apply here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there an acreage or farm parcel I can buy in Hemlock Valley?** No acreage market is documented here at all (§2): the valley's stock is resort cabins, chalets and strata product on village lots inside the Controlled Recreation Area. - **Q: The listing says "Mission" — do Mission's bylaws and taxes apply?** No. The mailing address is not the government: title, zoning, permits and taxes run through the FVRD (Electoral Area C) and the Province. - **Q: Will the resort expansion make my lot an acreage play?** Do not price it that way: the OCP / Master Plan build-out (19,969 bed units, 16,641 daily capacity) is a multi-phase vision longer than the life of the plan itself. - **Q: Where do children in the valley go to school?** There is no school in the valley — the OCP states the permanent population is too small to make one viable inside the CRA, so students travel to outside communities.
