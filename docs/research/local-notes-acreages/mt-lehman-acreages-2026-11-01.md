- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for ALR parcels:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: generally one residence per parcel unless it qualifies for an additional one; a principal residence of 500 m² or less (maximum 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. ALC Policy L-26 (amended May 2025): an additional residence beyond the regulation may only be permitted if necessary for a farm use — the farm-worker housing route. The City may further restrict residences. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, and it is NOT documented in this slug's own research — asserted nowhere in this file. The research's own farm-class note stands alongside it: Class 9, Farm land must produce a prescribed amount of qualifying primary agricultural products for sale; farm buildings are Class 1, and 50% of Class 9 land's assessed value is exempt from school taxes — farm class is earned by production, not automatic on ALR land. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/understanding-property-classes-and-exemptions
