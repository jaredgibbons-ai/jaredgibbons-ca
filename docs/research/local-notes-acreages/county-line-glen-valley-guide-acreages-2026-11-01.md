## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning / plan frame (Township only):** **Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500** governs; the Abbotsford frame is expressly excluded (FILE STATUS). A specific parcel's zone and any community-plan designation are per-parcel Township checks in this research. https://jaredgibbons.ca/county-line-glen-valley-guide.html
- **SSMUH — stated so it is not misapplied here:** Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) allows 3 units on eligible lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger eligible lots up to 4,050 m², subject to eligibility (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area); lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible and **no 6-unit tier applies in the Township**. Most parcels in this feed exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by a wide margin, so SSMUH generally does not apply to them. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Secondary suites (Township):** under Bylaw 2500 s.102 (as amended July 2025), a secondary suite may not exceed **120 m²** maximum gross floor area, one per principal dwelling unit, and on ALR lands a suite must comply with ALC regulations. LIVE CONFLICT, reported with dates and not resolved: the Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states suites are NOT permitted in townhouses/rowhouses, while the current s.102 definition (July 2025) says a suite may be located in a townhouse, rowhouse or manorhome — the bylaw definition is the later source. - **Coach houses (Township):** in Bylaw 2500 s.102, a "coach house" is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage; it is carried in this program's Township frame as a fee-simple second unit under s.102. No coach-house size cap appears in s.100 as read — a size cap is UNVERIFIED in this file. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)

- **ALR RULES IN FULL (this is ALR farm ground — the core rule set for this file):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill in the sibling frame), secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha); local governments may further restrict residences through zoning. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by the horse-properties slug); it is NOT documented in this file's research and is never carried here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** 1% on first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt, while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: CRA treats farmland in a separate info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
