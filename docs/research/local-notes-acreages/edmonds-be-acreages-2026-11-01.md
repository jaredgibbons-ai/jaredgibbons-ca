- **ALR — conditional statement only (no ALR ground documented in Edmonds):** IF a parcel were in the Agricultural Land Reserve, the ALC frame would govern: subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); and farm class would run on the three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met). Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,200,000 fully residential Edmonds lot purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,000,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**.
