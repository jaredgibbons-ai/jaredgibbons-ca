- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for South Albion ALR parcels:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. The 2025 Area Plan's farmland buffers sit on the urban side of the line; they do not change any ALC cap on the farm side. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in Albion's own research file — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. For a South Albion parcel, farm class is the holding-cost difference between a working parcel and an idle one — confirm the parcel's current class and income record before pricing the tax position. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Suites and SSMUH (urban ground):** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. On ALR land the ALC suite rule above (inside the principal residence only) controls instead — the two regimes meet at the South Albion line and must not be mixed. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
