## 2. THE ACREAGE ITSELF

**What "acreage" means in Ambleside: nothing that trades. Ambleside has no standing vacant-lot or acreage market — no named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata acreage community, and no presale LOT offering was verified inside Ambleside. What trades as land here is (1) the individual fee-simple RS house lot on the streets north of Marine Drive, and (2) town-centre development land whose value is set by the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan as implemented by provincial order in April 2026 — a plan framework, documented as such below, never as for-sale inventory. Ambleside's most distinctive land fact runs the other way: the District has spent fifty years buying waterfront lots OUT of the market (the 32-lot assembly below). Verified named acreage developments counted here: 0.**

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The town centre's land story is fifty years of the District buying, not selling.** Cottages and houses were removed to create Ambleside Park in the mid-1950s, to accommodate the Hollyburn Sailing Club in the mid-1960s, and to develop John Lawson Park from the mid-1950s to the late-1960s — and the acquisition program only closed on March 31, 2025, with the last of 32 waterfront lots. The continuous public waterfront from 13th to 18th Streets is assembled parkland, not a future development strip. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/major-projects/ambleside-waterfront-park-implementation-project
- **The density now governing Ambleside arrived by provincial order, not by District vote.** The Province directed adoption of the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan by December 31, 2025; the District missed the deadline on all three directives and delivered only 58 of its 220-unit year-one housing target, and a provincial order implemented the plan effective April 7, 2026, with quarterly District progress reports owed through 2026 and 2027. Price the Local Area Plan, not the old zoning alone. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0038-000377
