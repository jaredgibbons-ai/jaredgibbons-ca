## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

### Township of Langley frame (never blended with the City frame)

- **Zoning — Township:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs the Township; the Township states "Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 applies to the entirety of the Township." Minimum lot sizes (Bylaw 2500, s. 110.1, as printed in the bylaw text reproduced in a Township council report; minimum lot area / frontage / depth): SR-1 — 3,716 m² / 40 m / 55 m; SR-2 — 8,094 m² / 45 m / 61 m; SR-3 — 1,765 m² / 27.45 m / 30.5 m; RU-1 — 1.7 ha (no frontage or depth figure printed); RU-2 — 8.0 ha; RU-3 — 8.0 ha. Urban zones (same source): R-1B and R-1C — 464.5 m² / 15.25 m / 25 m; R-1D — 650.0 m² / 18.25 m / 25 m; R-1E — 930.0 m² / 22 m / 25 m; R-2 — 464.5 m² / 15.25 m / 25 m; RM-1 — 3,716 m²; RM-2, RM-3, RM-3A and RM-4 — 1,672 m². A consolidated Bylaw 2500 PDF downloaded directly from the Township site was not obtained in the research — the s. 110.1 figures are quoted from the bylaw text as reproduced in the council report, and that gap stays on the record. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024):** the Township's SSMUH rules were brought in by Bylaw 6020; added-unit housing eligibility requires a property to "be connected to both municipal water and sewer." **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020** — any source claiming a Township 6-plex tier is wrong. Eligibility is a servicing question parcel by parcel, not a zone slogan. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Suites and coach houses — Township:** secondary suite cap 120 m². A coach house is a fee-simple second unit on a detached lot under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata. **LIVE CONFLICT — flagged, not resolved:** s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted; this file carries both positions and adopts neither — confirm with the Township for the parcel.
- **R-CL compact lots — Township s.403:** the Residential Compact Lot Zone R-CL (s.403) runs from a minimum lot area of 220 m² to a maximum LOT area of 464.5 m². A "355 m²" figure is nowhere in the bylaw and is never used in this file.
- **Subdivision and servicing — Township:** "All subdivisions in the Township must be approved by the Approving Officer, as required under the Land Title Act," and the Township process runs Submit Application, Application Review, Decision Letter Issued, Fulfill Conditions, Final Approval (documents returned for registration in the Land Title Office), Servicing Completion, then Building Permit Application. In areas without existing water or sanitary system capacity the required upgrades are determined under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED; the Township's development servicing forms include Septic Sewer Certification and Well Testing (Forms F2–F5), and all required works and services must be built to bylaw standards before subdivision approval or building permit issuance. Development cost charges: Township DCC Bylaw No. 5897. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Willoughby plan status — Township:** the Willoughby Community Plan was Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025). The Willoughby Plan Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 were at first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY — never present them as adopted. Land in Willoughby prices off the adopted documents, not the reading-stage drafts.
- **Environment — Township:** development permit types are Form and Character, Streamside Protection and Enhancement, Energy Conservation and GHG Emission Reduction, and Heritage Alteration Permit; Schedule 3 of the Township Official Community Plan establishes Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Areas for watercourses not in the ALR; a permit is required to cut or remove a protected tree under the Township Tree Protection Bylaw 2019, No. 5478. Township water and sewer service-area boundaries as a written description: UNVERIFIED — Township pages state connection requirements and servicing-bylaw rules, not boundary descriptions. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-activity.aspx

### City of Langley frame (kept strictly separate)

- **Zoning — City:** City of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 governs the City; the bylaw states it "applies to the entire area within the boundaries of the City of Langley" and repeals Zoning Bylaw 1996 No. 2100. The City's media library still serves a consolidated Bylaw 2100 file — Bylaw 3300 is the newer source and is the bylaw quoted here. **Bylaw 3300 is cited by number only; its adoption date is conflicted in sources and is UNVERIFIED.** Minimum lot sizes (Bylaw 3300, as printed): R1 Suburban Residential — 557 m² minimum lot size / 16 m minimum frontage; R2 Townhouse Residential — 1,000 m² / not applicable, except lots created by subdivision with a registered party wall agreement (maximum 280 m², minimum frontage 4.5 m); R3 Low Rise Residential — 1,000 m² / 26.0 m. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw
- **SSMUH and suites — City:** City SSMUH Bylaw 3284. A City secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling — a different cap from the Township's 120 m², and the two are never interchanged in this file. (Program standing correction.)
- **Subdivision — City:** Bylaw 3300 states "No person shall subdivide land except in accordance with this bylaw," that subdivisions are subject to the City of Langley Subdivision Control Bylaw and the Development Cost Charge Bylaw, that a lot created by subdivision must meet the minimum lot area for its zone, and that the bylaw does not bind the Approving Officer to approve a subdivision that meets the minimum standards. The bylaw number of the City's Subdivision Control Bylaw: UNVERIFIED. City DCC Bylaw 2024 No. 3256. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw

### ALR, strata, tax and federal rules (both Langleys)

- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the frame for every Langley acreage parcel a parcel check puts in the reserve:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, apart from the limited cases in the Agricultural Land Reserve General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — creating a bare-land strata on ALR land is itself a subdivision needing the same permission. Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for a principal residence built without an ALC application; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha, where municipal bylaws also permit it. BC Assessment farm-class thresholds, full three-tier form: $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever stated; it is asserted ONLY in the horse-properties file in this program, is not documented in this file's research, and is not asserted here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **BC strata block (bare-land strata parcels in either Langley):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). A fee-simple lot carries none of this — no strata, no Form B, no strata fees — tenure proved from title, not the feed card. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Water and septic on rural lots:** the provincial GWELLS registry holds recorded wells (older wells may not appear); domestic groundwater use needs no licence, while non-domestic use, including irrigation, requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act. Fraser Health requires a home not connected to a community sewer to use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **PTT — worked examples with inputs and tier math shown:** PTT is based on fair market value on the day of registration at the Land Title Office and is not annual property tax. General rates: 1% to $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Anchor checks, standard rates, no exemption unless stated: **$500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 for a fully-qualifying first-time buyer after the $8,000 saving); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.1M→$20,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (tier math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000)** — a townhomes sibling in this program (south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m) carries $78,000 for $3.5M; this file follows $93,000, flags the conflict wherever met, and never silently carries $78,000. The "Over $3M — 5%" page row seen on band pages is a compression of 3% + 2% on the residential portion — flagged wherever met. First-time buyers' exemption: first $500,000 exempt, full at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, on the program's conditions (including 0.5 hectares or smaller — a condition most acreage parcels fail by size). Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; a person who paid PTT on vacant land and then has a newly built home on it may be eligible for a refund of the tax paid, per the Province's exemption page as carried in this program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; a source stating March 12 is noted in-line where met and never silently overwritten; a live page's $1.25M straight-line phase-out row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism (CRA says only "gradually reduced"). The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer (plus the 2026–2028 repayment-relief deferral carried in this program); First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation and vacancy taxes / federal prohibition:** this file states ONLY what its cited sources support — the research file opens no primary text settling either for a Langley land purchase, so both are UNCONFIRMED for a specific purchase in this file and are NOT conformed across files. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY in any event. (Research file above.)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How much of Langley is in the ALR?** The Township of Langley states 235 square kilometres of the Township is within the Agricultural Land Reserve; check a specific parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: What is the smallest rural lot I can create in the Township?** It depends on the zone under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 110.1: RU-1 requires 1.7 ha for a new lot, RU-2 and RU-3 require 8.0 ha, SR-2 requires 8,094 m² and SR-1 requires 3,716 m² — and ALR land also needs Agricultural Land Commission permission to subdivide. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **Q: Who approves a subdivision in Langley?** The Approving Officer, as required under the Land Title Act — in the Township the process runs application, review, decision letter, conditions, final approval and registration in the Land Title Office; in the City, Bylaw 3300 adds that meeting the minimum lot standards does not bind the Approving Officer to approve. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx and https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw
- **Q: Can I build a second home on a rural Langley lot?** On ALR land, one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and municipal bylaws must also permit it; the principal residence is capped at 500 m² and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put four homes — or six — on a Township lot?** Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) is the Township's SSMUH bylaw, and added-unit eligibility turns on connection to both municipal water and sewer. **There is no 6-plex tier in Bylaw 6020.** https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a townhouse in the Township — and what about the City?** The Township position is IN CONFLICT and is flagged, not resolved: s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m²; the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted — confirm with the Township for the parcel. The City position is separate: a suite is capped at the lesser of 100 m² and 40%. (Section 3.)
- **Q: What is a coach house — is it a strata unit?** No. A coach house is a fee-simple second unit on a detached lot under Township Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata. (Section 3, Township frame.)
- **Q: Are the Langley market numbers in this file land prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no land category and no acreage category; the Section 1 figures are Langley residential context only (detached median $1,345,000 and "Condos & Townhomes" combined median $611,000 in September 2026), and land-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a vacant Langley lot?** Generally yes if the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided; generally no for an individual's personal-use resale, per CRA GI-003 — and the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which school serves a buildable lot?** SD35 School Locator by exact parcel address — catchments follow the address, not the district name; no catchment is asserted in this file because no locator run is documented in its research. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
