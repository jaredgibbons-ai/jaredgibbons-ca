## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **City plan:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the **Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current in this file. The Cloverdale Town Centre Plan is being updated (boundary expanded July 22, 2024). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000.** Its SSMUH framework (in force for this area since **July 8, 2024**) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the former RF-12 / RF-12C zones were replaced by R4 and RF-SD by R6 in those changes. On ALR land the ALC rules below govern instead — the two regimes must not be mixed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame wherever a band parcel is in the reserve (the band's ground is farm-adjacent and its own copy invokes ALR parcels):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR owned ONLY by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page — it is NOT documented in this slug's research file and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Suites, coach houses and STRs (urban ground):** one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area**, never inside a coach house or garden suite, and not a separate strata lot — the feed's own cards at this band describe both "legal" and "unauthorized" suites, so suite legality is verified in documents, not listing text; a coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), with lane access required for a coach house on smaller lots and one extra parking space. Short-term rentals are permitted, **owner-only** (the host must be the owner living at the property as their primary residence; tenants cannot operate one) — the licence figure is carried as **$358 per year** per the City's Short-Term Rentals page, with the conflict disclosed: the City's 2024 corporate report proposed **$350 per year plus fees** — confirm the current figure on the City's licence page before quoting it. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Subdivision and servicing:** Surrey servicing is **Subdivision and Development Bylaw No. 8830 (1986), VERIFIED from the City's own pages** — carried with that sourcing. For an ALR parcel, ALC approval is required in addition to the City process. **Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated in this file; read the setback per parcel in COSMOS.**
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — the band's bite, with the anchors:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — every purchase in this band reaches the 3% tier on its top portion, and a residential value over $3,000,000 draws the further 2% on the portion over $3,000,000 (on a mixed-class property the further 2% applies only to the residential portion). Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program (south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m) and is flagged wherever met, not followed — it is not met in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Neither BC purchase exemption reaches this band:** the first time home buyers' program full exemption caps at a fair market value of $835,000 and the newly built home exemption's full threshold is $1,100,000 with elimination at $1,150,000 — both far below this page's floor, so buyers in this band budget full PTT. The home owner grant threshold for 2026 is $2,075,000 — a full regular grant at or below that assessed value, reduced by $5 for each $1,000 over $2,075,000, and $0 for the regular grant over $2,189,000 — so many homes in this band receive a reduced grant or none. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
