Added-unit permission is not permission to subdivide. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-zoning-bylaw-housekeeping-amendments
- **Secondary suites (Mission's own rule):** all secondary suites in Mission must be registered with the City; a suite is a self-contained living space in a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; a new suite goes through the building permit process, and an unregistered suite can trigger a bylaw investigation and a possible decommission permit. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **Subdivision / servicing: Development and Subdivision Control Bylaw No. 5650-2017 (DSCB 5650-2017)** — carried only from a City of Mission source, not as a research-file fact for this slug; its schedules govern site assessment for rezoning, development permit and subdivision applications. Specific servicing standards per parcel: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/site-assessment-development
- **ALR RULES IN FULL:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR — stated expressly: that flat form is NOT documented in this slug's own research; it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **BC strata block, where strata is met:** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission period for presale purchases. None of it applies to a fee-simple acreage purchase itself. Pad ground is NOT lot stock / land / acreage; manufactured-home pad tenancy percentages only (2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount) and NO pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000 wherever a $78,000 figure is met, and flag it (that figure lives only in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m townhomes sibling; open reconciliation item). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. The GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What am I actually buying when I buy an "acreage" in Mission?** A whole fee-simple parcel, checked parcel by parcel for zone under Bylaw 5949-2020, ALR status and servicing. There is no verified acreage subdivision, bare-land strata acreage community or presale acreage product city-wide to buy instead. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is this parcel in the ALR — and what changes if it is?** Confirm the parcel itself on the ALC map; the rural sub-areas only "include" farm land. If it is in: subdivision needs ALC approval, the residence cannot be stratified, and the caps are principal ≤500 m², suite inside that residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify or split the parcel later?** Stratify: no, if it is ALR (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Split: only through the City under DSCB 5650-2017 and the zone minimums, plus ALC approval if ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What farm income do I need to keep farm class?** All three tiers: $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: How much PTT on a $1.5M Mission acreage?** $28,000 fully residential with no exemption — 1% × $200,000 = $2,000 + 2% × $1,300,000 = $26,000. At $800,000 it is $14,000 ($6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Do I pay GST on vacant Mission land?** Do not assume either way — at CRA GI-003 level most individual personal-use sales are exempt, while business-use, business-course and more-than-two-part subdivided parcels are taxable cases. UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Is a suite on a Mission acreage legal?** Only if City-registered, inside a single family home (never a townhouse/rowhome), and — on ALR land — only inside the principal residence under the ALC rule. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: My acreage has a Mission mailing address — do I pay City taxes?** Not necessarily where Hatzic is concerned: Bench = City of Mission, Prairie = FVRD Area F, Island = Area G, and tax follows jurisdiction. Inside the City the penalty is a flat 10% after July 2; the rural 5% + 5% regime applies only on FVRD ground. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Q: How did Mission sell in September 2026?** Detached 231 listings / 28 solds / $814,000 median / 29 days; "Condos & Townhomes" 122 / 10 / $430,000 / 61 days — community context only; SnapStats has no acreage category and the attached median rests on 10 sales. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Mission acreage?** Catchments are address-based in SD75, names must be Ministry-listed (§1), "Mission Elementary School" does not exist, and no Mission locator run is on record in this slug's research — every specific address is UNVERIFIED without its own run. (BC Ministry K-12 schools file, 2025/2026, via the research file)
