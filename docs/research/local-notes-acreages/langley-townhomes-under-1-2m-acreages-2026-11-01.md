## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Township frame:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500. Secondary suites (s.102, as amended July 2025): a secondary suite may be located within a townhouse, rowhouse or manorhome, is capped at 120 m² maximum gross floor area, and each principal dwelling unit may have up to one; a coach house on fee-simple ground is dealt with under s.102 of the same bylaw — **the fee-simple s.102 position is carried with a LIVE CONFLICT flagged in the program (the research chain treats the coach-house/suite wording as contested), so the specific unit's permits govern in practice.** The Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states the opposite for townhouses/rowhouses — suites NOT permitted there. The conflict is reported with dates and not resolved; the bylaw definition is the later source. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, via the research file)
- **Township SSMUH:** Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and **NO 6-plex tier in the Township**. Strata townhome lots are not the SSMUH shape, and no SSMUH claim is made for any complex or parcel under this slug. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, via the research file)
- **Township plans:** the Willoughby Community Plan is Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (Dec 1, 2025). Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 are reading-stage only (first/second reading May 25, 2026; Public Hearing directed June 15, 2026) — they are not adopted law and no rule is taken from them here.
- **Township subdivision / servicing:** subdivisions require Approving Officer approval under the Land Title Act; servicing works are determined under the **Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED**. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
