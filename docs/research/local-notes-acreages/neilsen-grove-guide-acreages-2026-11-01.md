Added-unit permission is NOT subdivision permission: it does not create new lots and does not waive a permit area on the parcel. - **ALR rules in full (they govern any parcel later confirmed ALR — none is verified inside this sub-area):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALC residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², a suite within the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR and appears nowhere in this file as a rule. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land — rates and worked anchors (inputs + tier math stated):** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked examples on those tiers: $500,000 → $8,000; $700,000 → $12,000; $800,000 → $14,000 (net $6,000 after the maximum $8,000 First-Time Home Buyers' relief, where that program applies); $900,000 → $16,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 **including** the further 2% tier on the $500,000 over $3,000,000. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief on a new principal-residence HOME up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a home exemption, not a bare-land exemption; do not promise it on vacant land alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — stated only at the CRA GI-003 level:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use), while sales can be taxable where the land was capital property used primarily in a business, sold in the course of a business, or created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts. Sales by developers/businesses and newly subdivided lots are therefore generally TAXABLE and an individual's personal-use sale generally exempt, at that level only. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — get tax advice. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / HBP / FHSA — land angle stated precisely:** the First-Time Home Buyer GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not to bare land itself. The older New Housing Rebate is 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000, likewise a housing rebate. Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 per buyer from an RRSP, repayable over 15 years. First Home Savings Account: $8,000 per year, $40,000 lifetime, combinable with the HBP. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy taxes + foreign-buyer prohibition — only what this area's sources support:** the Province's taxable-areas list for the Speculation and Vacancy Tax includes the City of Delta (Metro Vancouver Regional District), and states reserve lands, treaty lands and lands of self-governing Indigenous Nations are NOT part of the taxable areas — relevant at Delta's TFN edge, not inside Neilsen Grove itself. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is **Vancouver ONLY** and does NOT apply here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for a specific buyer is **not conformed in this file — UNCONFIRMED** where no primary text was opened; confirm per buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Bare-land strata block (applies where a bare-land strata lot is the purchase — e.g. a South Pointe lot, cross-referenced in §2):** Form B first — under Strata Property Act s.59 the Form B Information Certificate discloses the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owed, approved special levies still to be paid, the CRF balance, parking/storage and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report attached; the strata must provide it within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Since July 1, 2024 every strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, non-deferrable by vote. Since November 1, 2023 the annual budget must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Rental-restriction bylaws have been VOID since November 24, 2022 for long-term rentals (short-term rentals may still be restricted by bylaw). Only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and children in defined care cases) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Dike / flood / streamside diligence:** Ladner is low land protected by the City's flood protection system — over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure. All properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. The City directs owners to DeltaMap for parcel-level flood information; search title for flood covenants before subjects. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf and https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Foreclosure treatment:** BC foreclosure is a settled court process (Supreme Court Civil Rules 21-7 / 13-5, Practice Direction PD-66, Form 60). Form B treatment and PTT/GST treatment on a court-ordered sale: UNCONFIRMED — kept distinct from the CRA GI-003 vacant-land position above, and narrower research stances elsewhere in the program are noted, not imported. **Registry ledger: Delta has NO Supreme Court registry — foreclosure registry mapping for a Delta parcel is UNVERIFIED and no registry is named here.**

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there acreage for sale in Neilsen Grove?** No verified acreage market exists in this sub-area — it is built-out urban Ladner ground. What trades is resale detached houses on fee-simple RS lots and strata housing in the Hampton Cove / Marina Garden Estates pocket. An "acreage" result here is a mislabelled urban lot until title and zoning prove otherwise. (§2 above)
- **Q: I want a hobby farm near Ladner — is this the pocket?** Not on this file's evidence: no ALR parcel, hobby-acreage enclave or working-farm parcel is verified inside Neilsen Grove. ALR status is UNVERIFIED in the research — check any edge parcel on the ALC maps, and look to Delta's A1 Agriculture ground (documented in other Ladner files) rather than assuming it exists here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can I subdivide a large Neilsen Grove lot into acreage parcels?** Only if the parcel's zone minimum is met — RS7 creates lots at 335 m², RS5 at 550/460 m², RD1 duplex at 836 m², and the low-density zones at 4,000 m² — plus City subdivision requirements (whose governing bylaw position is held UNCONFIRMED in §3). None of those minimums creates acreage-scale parcels. Confirm the zone on the City zoning maps before pricing any split. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put four homes on one lot instead of subdividing?** Delta's SSMUH bylaws (8394/8395, June 24, 2024) allow combinations of a single detached dwelling, duplex, secondary suite, coach house and garden suite where the zoning applies — but that is added units on one lot, not new acreage lots. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q: Is a South Pointe lot an acreage lot?** No. It is a bare-land STRATA lot (plan NWS2528) in a gated community of detached houses, documented once in the lots-and-land file — you own a strata lot and the house on it, pay strata fees, and get a Form B. "Bare land" describes the lot form, not acreage and not fee-simple tenure. (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/neilsen-grove-guide-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Will I pay GST on land here?** Depends on the seller and history: CRA GI-003 states most individual personal-use vacant-land sales are exempt, but business-use, in-the-course-of-business and more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable. A developer's newly subdivided lot is generally taxable. Confirm in the contract and get tax advice. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What PTT would I pay on a $1.5M land purchase?** On the general tiers in §3: $28,000 (1% on the first $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% on the next $1,300,000 = $26,000), before any exemption that actually applies to the transaction — and the home exemptions do not apply to bare land alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: What flood questions should I ask on a harbour-side parcel?** Whether the parcel is within 30 m of a stream/slough/ditch (Streamside DPA), what DeltaMap shows for parcel-level flood information, and whether a flood covenant sits on title — Ladner sits behind the City's dike and drainage system, and the paperwork follows the geography. https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection
- **Q: Could a parcel here qualify for farm-class taxes?** Only if it is farm land meeting the three-tier income test in §3 ($2,500 / 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; apply by Oct 31) — no parcel in this sub-area is documented as farm-class in the research, so the answer for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until BC Assessment confirms it. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Q: Which school catchment is a parcel in?** Only the sample is documented: 5500 Admiral Boulevard maps to Neilson Grove Elementary (note the spelling — "Neilson" for the school, "Neilsen" for the area) and Delta Secondary. Run the parcel's exact address in the SD37 locator before marketing school access. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/ and https://ng.deltasd.bc.ca/
