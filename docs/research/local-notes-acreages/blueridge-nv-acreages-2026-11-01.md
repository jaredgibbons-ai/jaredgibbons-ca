## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: District of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw 3210** — Section 525 minimums as stated in Section 2; an R2 lot allows a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **OCP: District of North Vancouver OCP Bylaw 7900**, Schedule B setting Development Permit Areas including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard; the Seymour Local Plan is the local plan layer for this pocket. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **Hazard rules — the governing diligence frame here:** District landslide work names the Berkley escarpment for landslide risk; after the January 19, 2005 landslide on the Berkley Escarpment the District began proactive District-wide hazard management, and its Landslide Risk Assessment was updated in 2026. The District publishes property-level GeoWeb reports, including modelled flood construction levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. No single floodplain zone number applies to Blueridge in the corrected program ledger — do not import another city's zone. http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-1-district-landslide.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **SSMUH, as the District states it:** since June 2024 District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; the District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. SSMUH creates no acreage and no strata exit by itself — check the lot's classification before marketing density. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — District number HELD UNCONFIRMED, process documented instead:** the District of North Vancouver servicing bylaw position is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger — never quote a District servicing bylaw number for a Blueridge lot. The documented route is District subdivision approval against the Section 525 zone minimums and the hazard permit areas above. **Exclusion, named once as required: City of North Vancouver Subdivision Bylaw 8014 is City law for City ground (e.g. Calverhall) and is NEVER imported into this District file.**
- **ALR rules — conditional, stated because the check is parcel-by-parcel:** no ALR parcel is confirmed in Blueridge; IF a specific lot were in the ALR, subdivision would require Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, asserted nowhere here and documented nowhere in Blueridge's research. No Blueridge parcel is documented as farm-classed. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **BC strata block (applies to strata purchases in the wider area, not to a fee-simple Blueridge lot):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 fully residential Blueridge lot purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** (a townhomes sibling in this program carries $78,000 for $3.5M — an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag wherever met). Land routes for the home exemptions: a first-time buyer registering a vacant lot and building can keep the FTHB exemption if lot value plus build cost is $835,000 or less (partial under $860,000), building and occupying by the first anniversary; the Newly Built Home Exemption can refund lot PTT once a qualifying new home is built ($1,100,000 / $1,150,000 thresholds). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at the CRA GI-003 level:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there acreage for sale in Blueridge?** No verified acreage market exists here — no acreage subdivision, bare-land strata, or farm parcel product was confirmed on any source. What trades is detached houses on R1/R2 lots; the largest zone minimum is R1 at 4,000 m². (Section 2.)
- **Q: How big can a Blueridge lot be?** The zoning minimums run from R2(S1) at 335 m² to R1 and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² (30 m width, 34 m depth); standard R2 is 660 m². Confirm the zone on the specific lot through District GeoWeb. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house?** Yes — since June 2024 an R2 lot allows a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite and a coach house (three units total) within the Urban Containment Boundary, and the GEOweb SSMUH classification may allow up to 4–6 units by lot size and bus-stop distance. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Q: What hazard check matters most?** Landslide on the Berkley side — the District names the Berkley escarpment for landslide risk, managed proactively since the January 19, 2005 slide and updated in the 2026 Landslide Risk Assessment; Schedule B also sets slope, creek, streamside and wildfire permit areas. Pull the GeoWeb hazard layers and budget for a geotechnical report. http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-1-district-landslide.pdf
- **Q: Is any Blueridge land in the ALR or farm-classed?** None is documented — parcel-level ALR status is UNVERIFIED in the research. Check the ALC map for the specific lot; if it were in the ALR, the Section 3 caps and ALC subdivision approval would govern, and farm class would turn on the three-tier income test (never a flat "$2,500+"). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Is Baden Park an acreage or land opportunity?** No — Baden Park is a built mixed community (townhomes + garden flats + rental) on Lytton Street, documented in the townhomes sibling and counted nowhere as a standalone complex; MLS splits it between Blueridge NV and Windsor Park NV. It is not land stock. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Blueridge address?** Blueridge Elementary (2650 Bronte Drive) and Seymour Heights Elementary (2640 Carnation Street) are in the pocket and Windsor Secondary (931 Broadview Drive) is the Seymour secondary beside it — but NO catchment is confirmed for any Blueridge address in the research; check the exact address with SD44. Dorothy Lynas French Immersion is a choice program by registration, never the catchment school. https://blueridge.sd44.ca/
- **Q: What bus serves the pocket?** Route 214 from Sechelt Drive at Hyannis Drive via Berkley Road and Carnation Street to Phibbs Exchange, some trips through to Burrard Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt214.pdf
- **Q: What will it cost to hold a Blueridge lot?** District taxes on the 2026 cycle (due July 2, 2026; 5% after July 2; further 5% after September 1, 2026; total residential rate 3.28195 per $1,000) plus combined single-family utilities of $2,571.20 in 2026. A fee-simple lot carries no strata fee. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
