## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP — current plan only.** The governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900; Bylaw No. 18020 is never current. Within the West Newton NCP set, the City states West Newton South was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 update and is no longer in effect. Confirm any density or use claim against the current OCP and Zoning Bylaw 12000, not the old plans. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps
- **Zoning.** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 — R1–R6 residential in the neighbourhood portion (minimums as the research states them: R3 464 m², R4 250 m², R2 929 m²), industrial zones in the Highway 99 area. The zone line between residential and industrial ground is the first diligence item for any south-end parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it (Jul 8, 2024 framework).** Maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; one secondary suite per house; the SSMUH changes do not retroactively legalize suites built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey).** One secondary suite per house; Surrey's suite cap is 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area, one per unit, and a suite is not a separate strata lot. Verify a suite's permit status before marketing its income. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — VERIFIED LEDGER entry carried exactly:** Surrey's subdivision/servicing bylaw is **Bylaw No. 8830 (1986)**. Subdivision proceeds through detailing and a Servicing Agreement that contractually defines the off-site works and services and financial securities required for final approval. Lot-specific servicing costs, DCC amounts and timelines for a West Newton parcel: UNCONFIRMED — no City source in the research states a figure for this area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside protection:** Surrey's streamside rules sit in Zoning Bylaw Part 7A and run **by stream class — there is no single setback figure** to quote for the area; the class of the specific watercourse governs. (Program standing record; parcel-level class UNCONFIRMED in this file's research.)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey).** STRs are permitted, owner-only, under a City licence. Licence figure CONFLICT: program figure $358/yr vs the City's 2024 corporate report proposing $350/yr plus fees — carry $358 with $350 disclosed and check the City licence page before quoting. (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/west-newton-guide-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **Flood diligence (south end).** South-end streets fall toward the Serpentine & Nicomekl lowlands — the dyke-protected floodplain south of Newton — while the residential upland portion is outside that lowland floodplain. Confirm the specific lot against the City's flood mapping and ask about flood insurance before subjects come off. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **ALR rules in full (south/west edge parcels only — no in-area ALR parcel documented):** where a parcel IS in the ALR: residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239 deposit = subdivision; ALCA s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; principal residence capped at 500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). No marketing-board source was opened in this research, so quota/board items stay UNVERIFIED site copy. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC Assessment farm class — full three-tier form:** $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 (<0.8 ha); application to the assessor on or before October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an error (asserted only where a file's own research documents it — the horse-properties page owns it; not this file). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Property transfer tax (PTT), with worked examples (inputs stated, tier math shown):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. No exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $700,000 → **$12,000**; $900,000 → **$16,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 fully residential → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 (3% × $1,500,000) + $10,000 (further 2% × $500,000) = **$93,000**. First-Time Home Buyers': no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 (at the $800,000 example: $14,000 gross, $6,000 after the $8,000 relief) — relief turns on a qualifying home, so a lot-only purchase must be checked against the BC rules before relief is assumed. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000, for a new principal-residence home. **OPEN reconciliation item:** a townhomes sibling (tsawwassen-homes-over-1-8m) carries $78,000 for the $3,500,000 input; this file follows **$93,000** on the tier math and flags the conflict. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only.** Most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g., land kept for personal use); taxable examples include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts. Developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots beyond that line are generally taxable. Finer points for a specific lot: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — stated precisely.** The FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. A live page's $1.25M straight-line phase-out row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. Land-plus-build qualification is transaction-specific: UNCONFIRMED per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax.** Surrey IS a designated taxable area — residential owners must file the annual declaration; a vacant parcel's position turns on its class and the exemptions. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there acreage for sale in West Newton?** No — no acreage market is verified here. The residential purchase is a built-out fee-simple house lot (zone minimums run 250 m² to 929 m² in the R-zone table the research carries); the only other land class in the plan area is Highway 99 industrial ground. A buyer who needs genuine acreage is looking south/west toward the lowlands' ALR edge or outside Newton entirely. (Section 2 above)
- **Q: A listing calls a West Newton lot an "acreage" — should I believe it?** No. No acreage land class is documented in this area's research; treat the word as page copy and verify the parcel's zone, surveyed size and title directly. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **Q: How many units can my lot hold instead?** 3 units under 280 m², 4 over 280 m², up to 6 on eligible lots of 281 m²+ within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — confirm band and eligibility with the City. That is the densification route on urban lots; it creates units, not acreage. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Should I worry about flood on a south-end lot?** Yes — check it, don't assume: the south end falls toward the dyke-protected Serpentine & Nicomekl lowlands while the residential upland is outside that floodplain. Confirm the lot on City flood mapping and ask about flood insurance. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **Q: Could a lot near the lowlands be in the ALR?** ALR land lies south and west toward the lowlands — a specific property's status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission maps, and if it is in the ALR the Section 3 caps, stratification bar and ALC subdivision approval govern before any City permission matters. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Is the industrial edge a problem for a nearby lot?** It is a diligence item, not a verdict: verify neighbouring uses, truck routes and the residential/industrial zone line for any parcel near the Highway 99 area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- **Q: Is the lot serviced?** Subdivision streets are on community services as the default; edge parcels need confirmation, because unconnected properties are an SSMUH exemption category. Specific lot: UNCONFIRMED until the City confirms. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: What will subdivision/servicing cost?** The City sets off-site works, services and securities in a Servicing Agreement after detailing, under the subdivision/servicing frame of Bylaw 8830 (1986) — no area figure exists in the research, so any number without a City estimate for the parcel is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Q: Will GST apply to a land purchase here?** Depends on the seller and subdivision history: individuals' personal-use sales are generally exempt per the CRA; business-course sales and parcels subdivided into more than two parts are taxable examples. Get the GST position in writing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What are the holding costs on an empty lot?** Surrey property taxes (due July 2; 5% + 5% penalties) plus the speculation and vacancy tax declaration — Surrey is a taxable area. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
