## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **No city frame is imported beyond what this slug's research documents — stated first.** The hub spans South Surrey, White Rock, Langley City and the Township of Langley, each with its own OCP, zoning, suite, servicing and tax rules; those frames live in their own area files in this program and are not blended or imported here. The rules below are the province-wide / program frames a buyer using this hub actually budgets under.
- **The regulator behind the professionals:** real estate trading services in BC are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA). BCFSA states a licensed professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Strata frame (Province):** strata owners own their strata lot and together own the common property as a strata corporation; strata disputes can be resolved using the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (farm files carry the full block):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a PERMISSION rule, never an absolute ban, and ALC Bulletin 05 is never cited for it. Residence caps: one principal residence, maximum 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; at most one additional residence of 90 m² on parcels ≤40 ha or 186 m² on parcels >40 ha; local bylaws may restrict further but not be more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if less than 0.8 ha. Applications by the legislated October 31 deadline for the next assessment/tax year; late applications are not accepted; a buyer continuing to farm must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by HORSE-PROPERTIES) — it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Servicing ledger (carried exactly, for reference when a parcel's jurisdiction is known — no hub-wide servicing position exists):** Township of Langley 5382 (2019) CONFIRMED; Coquitlam 3558 (2003) VERIFIED; New Westminster 7142 (2007) VERIFIED; Richmond 8751/8752 VERIFIED; Vancouver 5208 (fees 14538); City of North Vancouver 8014 (City files ONLY); Surrey 8830 (1986) VERIFIED; Maple Ridge 4800-1993 (City source); Mission Development and Subdivision Control Bylaw 5650-2017 (City source). - **Registry ledger:** REGISTRY LEDGER: Abbotsford HAS a Supreme Court registry; Surrey / Delta / Mission have NONE (mapping UNVERIFIED); Chilliwack + Maple Ridge UNCONFIRMED.
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). Follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M lives ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling — flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant/farm land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example); an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland is addressed by CRA in a separate info sheet and is usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 noted in-line in some sources — March 13 governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land — 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5M; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **BC strata block, where bare-land strata exists:** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions). Poultry operations sit on freehold farm land in the pattern documented here, so the strata block normally has nothing to attach to. A manufactured home on a rented pad is NOT lot stock / land / acreage; pad tenancy percentages only (2026 = 2.3% + proportionate; 2027 = 2.2% + proportionate) and NO pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
