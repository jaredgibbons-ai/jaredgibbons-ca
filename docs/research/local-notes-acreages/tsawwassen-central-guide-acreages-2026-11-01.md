## 2. THE ACREAGE ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: Tsawwassen Central has NO acreage market. This file carries no acreage roster of its own. No named acreage subdivision or development, no bare-land strata acreage community, and no farm parcel was verified inside this sub-area on an official or developer source. - **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land, with worked examples (inputs stated, tier math shown):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. No exemption: $500,000 → $2,000 + $6,000 = **$8,000**; $700,000 → **$12,000**; $900,000 → **$16,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 fully residential → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 (3% × $1,500,000) + $10,000 (further 2% × $500,000) = **$93,000**. First-Time Home Buyers': no PTT on the first $500,000 (max $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — at the $800,000 cap example the tax is **$14,000** gross, $6,000 after the $8,000 FTHB relief. Newly Built Home Exemption: full on a new HOME to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — not a bare-land exemption. **OPEN reconciliation item:** a townhomes sibling (tsawwassen-homes-over-1-8m) carries $78,000 for the $3,500,000 input; this file follows **$93,000** on the tier math above and flags the conflict. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land), while sales can be taxable where land was used primarily in a business, sold in the course of a business, or created by subdividing into more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are therefore generally TAXABLE. A live page's $1.25M straight-line phase-out row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. New Housing Rebate: 36%, max $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP: $60,000. FHSA: $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. CMHC premiums: 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any real acreage for sale in Tsawwassen Central?** No verified acreage market exists here — the sub-area is built-out urban-centre ground, and the largest subdivision minimum in its zoning ladder is RD1 at 836 m². A buyer who needs genuine acreage is looking at Delta's farm belt or another community, not the town centre. (Section 2 above)
- **Q: A listing calls a Central lot an "acreage" — should I believe it?** No. No acreage land class, ALR parcel or farm operation is verified in this sub-area's research; treat the word as page copy and verify the parcel's zone, size and title directly. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **Q: Can I subdivide a big Central lot into acreage-style parcels?** No — subdivision runs against the zone minimums (335 m² RS7 to 836 m² RD1), City subdivision/servicing requirements, and, in the Urban Centre, the OCP's DPA guidelines. The output is urban lots, not acreages. (Section 3 above)
- **Q: Does SSMUH mean I can build four units instead of subdividing?** Yes as added units — Delta allows up to four units on single-detached/duplex lots (Bylaws 8394/8395) — but no new lots are created, and the Urban Centre DPA still applies. (Section 3 above)
- **Q: Can I keep animals or run a small farm on a Central lot?** The one verified animal rule is narrow: Delta permits 2 to 4 hens for personal use on a lot in any RS or RD zone (coop and run max 10 m², no roosters), with an annual licence under the Animal Control Bylaw. No farm use is documented for Central lots beyond that. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **Q: Is the lot on city water and sewer?** UNCONFIRMED per parcel — the topic research leaves the live page's servicing claim unverified, so do not rely on it. Confirm connections with the City; for an unserviced parcel, GWELLS plus Fraser Health's onsite-sewerage rules (Authorized Person, Sewerage System Regulation). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: Will I pay GST and PTT on a lot here?** PTT yes, on the general tiers (worked examples in Section 3) unless an exemption's own conditions are met — the exemptions attach to buyers/homes, not to vacant land alone. GST depends on the seller: CRA states individual personal-use sales are generally exempt, business/subdivided-more-than-two-parts sales can be taxable. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does the mall redevelopment create development land I can buy?** No — File LU009754 is a redevelopment process on assembled commercial land (Century Group), with what will be built UNCONFIRMED. It is a question to ask on a nearby purchase, not inventory and not a premium to pay on spec. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/
- **Q: What are holding costs on a Central lot?** Delta property taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalties after July 2 and September 2, 2026); no strata fees on a fee-simple lot. If a first home will be built, the HBP ($60,000) and FHSA ($40,000/$8,000/yr) engage at the home stage, and the FTHB GST rebate attaches to new housing, not the land. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Q: Which school catchment is the lot in?** Only the sample is documented: 1299 56 St maps to Cliff Drive Elementary and South Delta Secondary. Run the lot's exact address in the SD37 locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
