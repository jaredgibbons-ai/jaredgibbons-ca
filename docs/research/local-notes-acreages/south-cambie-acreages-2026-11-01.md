## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Municipality: City of Vancouver — the Cambie Street corridor between roughly West 16th Avenue and the Fraser River (Marine Drive), including the Cambie Village stretch and the area around the King Edward and Oakridge–41st stations' northern edges. MLS sub-area name (GVR board): "South Cambie." Nearby areas: Oakridge, Riley Park, Shaughnessy, Douglas Park, Marpole, Langara. Search names: "Cambie Village," "Cambie Corridor," "King Edward Village," "South Cambie Street." (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md, §1.)
- Planning frame: the Cambie Corridor Plan, approved by Council on May 9, 2011, guides transit-oriented growth along Cambie Street from 16th Avenue to the Fraser River, centred on the Canada Line stations at Marine Drive, Langara–49th Avenue, Oakridge–41st Avenue and King Edward; Phase 3 (approved 2018) extended land-use policy to the neighbourhood streets within walking distance of the stations, adding townhouse and low-rise apartment forms off the arterial. The corridor's growth framework targets the Oakridge Municipal Town Centre at 41st and Cambie as its southern anchor. https://www.cip-icu.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Cambie-Corridor-Plan-FINAL-Aug-2011_WEB.pdf and https://council.vancouver.ca/20180417/documents/rr2_presentation.pdf
- Parks: Queen Elizabeth Park (130.7 acres), Douglas Park (13.2 acres), Tisdall Park (12.4 acres) and Oak Meadows Park (11.7 acres), as inventoried in the City's Cambie Corridor park planning; Queen Elizabeth Park itself is 52 hectares at 125 metres above sea level — the highest point in the City — with the Bloedel Conservatory, quarry gardens and an arboretum. Douglas Park Community Centre (801 West 22nd Avenue) is the corridor's community centre. http://vancouver.ca/files/cov/oakridge-park-open-house-boards-december-2017.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/queen-elizabeth-park.aspx
- Institutions: BC Children's Hospital and BC Women's Hospital & Health Centre share the PHSA campus at 4500 Oak Street (Oak Street at West 28th Avenue), one of the largest health-care employment and patient-trip generators on the west side; VanDusen Botanical Garden (5251 Oak Street, at West 37th Avenue, 22 hectares / 55 acres) sits at the corridor's northwest edge; Langara College sits at the corridor's south end (West 49th Avenue); Hillcrest Centre (4575 Clancy Loranger Way), immediately east of Queen Elizabeth Park in Riley Park, is the converted 2010 Olympic curling venue, now a combined community and aquatic centre. http://www.bcchildrens.ca/ and https://vandusengarden.org/
- Schools — names and locations only, catchments in Section 4: Edith Cavell Elementary (500 West 20th Avenue), a Douglas Park–area school of roughly 330 K–7 students whose seismic upgrade was completed between January 2021 and May 2023; Sir Winston Churchill Secondary (7055 Heather Street) is the public secondary school located inside the corridor, at Heather and West 54th — location is not catchment (Section 4).
