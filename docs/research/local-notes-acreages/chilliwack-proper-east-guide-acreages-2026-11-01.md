## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP / plan:** City of Chilliwack **Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 ("Chilliwack 2050")**, whose **Schedule B-2** is the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan covering this ground (first added to the 2040 OCP as Schedule D through Bylaw 2018, No. 4533; purpose: guiding residential infill). Frame schedules: Sardis = B5; Agricultural = B1; Eastern Hillsides = B4; Downtown = B3 + D-6. Zoning: **Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000**, with farm zones in §6. https://chilliwack.com/ocp and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH (Chilliwack):** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, **No. 5395, adopted June 18, 2024**, amending Bylaw 5000, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Suites / ADUs (Bylaw 5000):** secondary suite capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space (s.2); only 1 accessory dwelling unit per lot, types including secondary suite, coach house and garden suite, garden suites capped at 100 m² (s.4.06(1)); ADU parking 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite, 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite (s.5.04); no suite is legal unless built with a permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and flood protection requirements, and new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Subdivision / servicing — number UNCONFIRMED, 3055 conflict documented, ledger followed:** the Chilliwack servicing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger. The candidate "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" is NOT adopted per the ledger and is NEVER presented as verified here; the City's subdivisions webpage names a bylaw of that number/title, and several Chilliwack lots siblings assert 3055 — those are documented as conflicts and flagged as conforming-pass items, and this file follows the ledger. What IS documented is the process: subdivision requires approval by the City's Approving Officer under the Local Government Act / Land Title Act process (Preliminary Layout Approval as the first major milestone in the sibling record), ensuring lot size/shape, services, access and related requirements before Land Title registration. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (Chilliwack is farm ground city-wide — 67% of the land base — so this block applies wherever ALR ground exists or the area is farm-adjacent):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by the horse-properties slug); it is NOT documented in this file's research and is never carried here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Floodplain:** as the guide states it, citing the Downtown Neighbourhood Plan: Chilliwack Proper is subject to Fraser River flood risk, the dyke system is built to a 1-in-200-year flood standard, and areas outside the dyking system flood at higher river stages. Which flood rules and flood construction level apply to any specific lot is a per-parcel City check: UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-proper-east-guide.html
- **Property taxes (Chilliwack):** notices mailed at end of May, due the first working day after July 1; 5% penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** 1% on first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt, while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: CRA treats farmland in a separate info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land — 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out, housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **BC strata block (where bare-land strata exists):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
