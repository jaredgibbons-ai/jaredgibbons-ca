## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500** governs, with the s.110.1 minimums in Section 2. Get the parcel's zone from the Township/GeoSource first — RU-2, C-2 and CD-192 minimums are UNVERIFIED from a readable section and are not filled in here. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **Plans:** the Aldergrove Community Plan and the Aldergrove Core Area Plan govern the community, within the Township OCP update work. (Willoughby context carried for accuracy only: the Willoughby Community Plan is Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025), and Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 had first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY — none of that is an Aldergrove plan and none is stated as adopted beyond what is written here.) https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/community-planning.aspx
- **SSMUH in the Township: Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024)** amended Bylaw 2500 to enable small-scale multi-unit housing in zones previously restricted to single-family/duplex. NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020 — any page claiming a Township 6-plex tier is wrong. SSMUH does not create acreage subdivision rights on RU-2 or ALR ground. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
- **Suites and coach houses:** secondary-suite cap is 120 m² (Township rule — do NOT import Abbotsford's under-50% / 15%-to-100 m² caps). A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — part of the same fee-simple title, never a separate strata lot, and it cannot be sold separately. LIVE s.102 CONFLICT — flagged, not resolved: the July 2025 bylaw text permits townhouse suites at 120 m² while the January 2025 guide says not permitted. A suite/coach-house plan for a specific acreage needs the current Township position in writing. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **R-CL context (Willoughby, carried only to prevent misapplication):** R-CL s.403 is 220 m² minimum lot area / 464.5 m² maximum LOT area — a Willoughby zone figure, NOT an Aldergrove standard; no 355 m² figure exists in that section, and no R-CL figure is applied to an Aldergrove parcel in this file.
- **Subdivision + servicing: Township of Langley Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 5382 (2019) CONFIRMED** — carried exactly as given in the verified ledger. Detailed servicing requirements (roads, water, sewer/septic, drainage) for a specific Aldergrove acreage subdivision are still application-specific: confirm with the Township for the specific application, and never import another municipality's servicing number. (Verified ledger, commissioning brief, Nov 1, 2026.)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (where ALR ground exists):** check the parcel in GeoSource/ALC mapping first. Subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, as the ALC states it. ALR residences cannot be stratified — the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence; an additional residence of 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) / 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR and is not asserted here. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Flood:** parcel-level floodplain status for Aldergrove acreages is UNVERIFIED in the research — check the specific parcel (Bertrand Creek bounds the core) before building plans are priced.
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on acreage:** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $900,000 fully residential acreage purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $700,000 ($14,000) = **$16,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. Exemptions: First-Time Home Buyers' Program — no PTT on the first $500,000 (max $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, reachable on land only through the register-lot-and-build route; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption that can refund lot PTT once a qualifying home is built; bare-land eligibility without a build is UNCONFIRMED, do not promise it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most individual personal-use vacant-land sales are exempt, while business-use, in-the-course-of-business, and more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are therefore generally taxable. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED per parcel. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate: 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K phase-out. HBP: $60,000 per buyer. FHSA: $8,000/year, $40,000 lifetime, combinable with HBP. A live page's $1.25M straight-line phase-out row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism where met. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Aldergrove-specific speculation-tax position is documented in this area's research, so none is imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY — not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition is stated ONLY as this file's sources support it — vacant land itself can be purchased by a non-Canadian as amended March 27, 2023, per law-firm summaries; dwellings remain governed, specific application UNCONFIRMED at the official-text level (no primary text opened in the research).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a serviced acreage in a named Aldergrove subdivision?** No named acreage subdivision was verified here — the market is individual fee-simple parcels. Treat any "new acreage subdivision" claim as UNCONFIRMED until a developer/municipal source names it. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How small can an Aldergrove acreage be?** It depends entirely on the zone: the s.110.1 ladder runs from 464.5 m² (R-1B/R-1C) through SR-3 1,765 m² and SR-1 3,716 m² to 8,094 m² (SR-2); RU-2 minimums are UNVERIFIED from a readable section. Get the zone before valuing a split — subdivision and servicing then run under Bylaw 5382 (2019). https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **Q: Is the rural acreage in the ALR?** Much Township land is; check the parcel in GeoSource. In the ALR, subdivision needs ALC approval, residences cannot be stratified, and the ALC residence caps govern (principal 500 m², suite inside the principal only, additional residence 90 m² / 186 m²). https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to an acreage I buy?** Suite cap 120 m²; a coach house under s.102 stays on the same fee-simple title and cannot be sold separately or stratified off. Note the live s.102 townhouse-suite conflict in Section 3 — get the Township's current position for your housing form in writing. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put 6 homes on a single-family acreage lot?** No — Bylaw 6020 has NO 6-plex tier. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
- **Q: Is the acreage on city water/sewer or well/septic?** UNCONFIRMED per parcel in the research — confirm with the Township for the exact parcel; do not assume core servicing extends to rural parcels south/east of the core. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Will GST apply to my acreage purchase?** If the seller is a developer/business or the parcel is newly subdivided, generally yes (taxable); an individual's personal-use sale is generally exempt per CRA GI-003. Confirm in the contract. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What does it cost to hold an Aldergrove acreage?** Township taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% + 5% penalties after July 2 / September 3, 2026) plus separately billed utilities where connected, and farm-class compliance costs on farm parcels. No strata fees on a fee-simple acreage. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **Q: Can I get farm class on a small Aldergrove acreage?** Only on the full three-tier test — under 0.8 ha needs $10,000 of farm income, the highest tier; 0.8–4 ha needs $2,500; over 4 ha needs $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha. Acreage alone does not qualify; applications by October 31. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Q: Which school will an acreage near 29 Avenue feed?** Only the 26850 29 Avenue locator chain is documented, on a single-address basis (Parkside Centennial → Betty Gilbert → Aldergrove Community Secondary). Run the exact address in the SD35 locator. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
