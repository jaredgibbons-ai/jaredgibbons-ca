## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

The City is bounded by the Fraser River to the south, the District of Maple Ridge to the west, and Fraser Valley Regional District electoral areas to the north and east, per Fraser Health's community profile of Mission. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan and https://careers.fraserhealth.ca/pages/mission-community-profile (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/mission-homes-for-sale.md)

- **SSMUH: Bylaw 6299-2024 VERIFIED — Mission unit counts still UNCONFIRMED.** The City's SSMUH page states that zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted, and the City's planning pages set out how secondary suites and detached accessory units are handled by zone. - **Subdivision and servicing: Development and Subdivision Control Bylaw (DSCB) 5650-2017 — City source, title confirmed.** Subdivision of a Mission parcel runs through the City against the zone minimums and the DSCB's works-and-services requirements; the parcel-level standards are read from the bylaw and confirmed with the City for the specific lot. For an ALR parcel, ALC approval is required in addition (below). https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for ALR parcels on Mission's rural edges:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, stated as the ALC states it. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR wherever it is stated; no such line is documented in this file's research and none is asserted here. Farm class is the holding-cost difference between a working Mission parcel and an idle one — confirm the parcel's current class and the income record before pricing the tax position. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Floodplain and diking levies:** the Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's south edge and Hatzic Prairie sits behind the Dewdney Dyke system; municipal tax rates are set by the City, and additional taxes are levied for diking on top of the municipal, school, regional district and BC Assessment levies. Confirm floodplain status, flood construction requirements and the levy for the specific parcel before valuing low ground. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- **If a strata purchase is made in the urban assembly (redirect case):** the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ units since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. The assembly's strata stock itself (including the four townhouse strata cross-referenced in Section 2) is documented in the condos and townhomes siblings, not here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: an $800,000 fully residential Mission acreage purchase at the first-time-buyer cap with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000** ($6,000 if the FTHB exemption fully applies). Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** (tier math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000) — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. Land held as farmland gets neither the FTHB exemption nor the Newly Built Home Exemption ($1,100,000 / $1,150,000 thresholds). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on farm and vacant land — ONLY at the level the CRA sources support:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland is the caution: the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a SEPARATE info sheet and treats farmland sales as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. A Mission farm-edge parcel must therefore never be assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone — parcel-level GST is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status, farm use and the applicable CRA sheet are applied, and the contract must state the GST position. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply here. (Research file above.)
