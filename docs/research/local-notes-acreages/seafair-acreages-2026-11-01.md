## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there acreage to buy in Seafair?** Not as a product. The only land form is the fee-simple detached lot — subdivision minimums from RS1/K 315.0 m² to RS1/F 828.0 m² — in an established west Richmond planning area. Verified named acreage developments counted here: 0. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a large Seafair lot?** Only within the Bylaw 8500 minimums for the resulting lots' subzone, through the City's process under Subdivision Bylaw 8751 and Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 (both VERIFIED). Confirm the subzone parcel by parcel before pricing a split. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Q: How many homes can a house lot hold?** A secondary suite is permitted in RS1/RS2 (33.0 m² minimum; maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling or 110 m²), the City allows at least one suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, and Bill 44 minimums are 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: Which schools serve a Seafair address?** On this file's own single-address basis ONLY: for the sample address 9200 No. 1 Road (Hugh Boyd Secondary School), the completed SD38 School Locator run in this file's research gives Diefenbaker Elementary and Boyd Secondary, plus French Immersion options (Early at Dixon Elementary, Late at Diefenbaker Elementary, Secondary at McMath Secondary). That result is cited for that single address only and is NON-TRANSFERABLE — it must not be adopted as any other area's catchment, including Boyd Park's. SD38 has no middle tier; confirm every other address in the locator. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Q: What flood checks does a Seafair lot need?** The area is in the Fraser River floodplain behind a 1:500-design dike system with an active dike-raising program (3.5 m to 4.7 m), and Bylaw 8204 flood construction levels and dike setbacks apply to new construction — get the parcel-level position early. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **Q: Is any Seafair ground in the ALR or farm class?** None is documented — parcel-level ALR status is UNVERIFIED in the research and no farm ground is named on any source. If a specific parcel proved to be ALR, the full ALC frame in Section 3 would govern. (Research file, UNVERIFIED item 10.)
- **Q: What will it cost to hold a lot here?** City property taxes on the 2026 cycle (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after that date; further 5% after September 1, 2026) plus City water and sewer charges (dollar figure UNVERIFIED); no strata fee on a fee-simple lot. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a lot purchase?** Only as the CRA GI-003 analysis lands for that vendor and parcel — most individual personal-use vacant-land sales are exempt, business or more-than-two-part subdivided sales can be taxable; UNCONFIRMED per parcel, and the contract must state who bears GST. PTT is separate and always applies on the tiers in Section 3. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Are the townhouse complexes near Williams Road and Francis Road an acreage or land buy?** No — Edgewater Park and Seafair West are built strata townhouse complexes counted in the townhomes sibling; a purchase there is a strata purchase under the Section 3 strata block, not a land purchase.
