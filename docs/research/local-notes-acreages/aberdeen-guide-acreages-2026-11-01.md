## 2. THE ACREAGE ITSELF

**Aberdeen IS acreage country — but it has no named acreage subdivision market. No named acreage development, bare-land strata acreage community, or presale acreage offering was verified in this area on an official or developer source. What trades here is individual fee-simple farm and rural-residential acreage parcels — land first, house second. Verified named acreage developments counted here: 0. Any parcel count, or parcel size beyond the zoning minimums in Section 3, would be invented, so none is stated.**

- **Fee-simple farm and rural parcels — the default and effectively only acreage form.** The housing stock is overwhelmingly single-detached houses on rural and agricultural lots; the area is rural in character with working farm parcels, and homes are effectively all freehold farm and rural residential lots. An Aberdeen acreage purchase is a fee-simple title purchase: no strata corporation, no Form B, no strata fee. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Parcel character, as the research states it:** agricultural in scale. Subdivision minimums are 8.0 ha in the A1 zone and 16.0 ha in the A2 zone, and an additional farm dwelling is only contemplated on lots of at least 0.4 ha. These are zoning minimums, not a claim about any individual parcel's size — check the parcel's zone in the City WebMap and its area on title. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- **Hobby acreage vs working-farm ground:** the research file characterises the area as "rural in character with working farm parcels" and its typical-buyer note names farm operators and acreage buyers who need land first and a house second — that is the only hobby-vs-working distinction the research supports, stated at area level, not parcel level. No specific farm operation is named on an official source in the research, so none is named here. Farm operations are described in process terms only; a generic feed is NEVER presented as a confirmed operation in a named sector, and no marketing-board source was opened in the research file, so quota/board items stay UNVERIFIED site copy. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/aberdeen-guide.md, §§5–6.)
- **ALR vs non-ALR split:** rural Aberdeen parcels are in or beside the Agricultural Land Reserve; no area-wide ALR percentage is published in the research, so no split figure is stated — ALR status is parcel-by-parcel, checked on the ALC map and title. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Serviced vs unserviced ground:** rural residential lots outside the urban boundary may use wells and onsite sewage rather than community water and sewer — unserviced (well/septic) ground is the rural default here, not the exception. Servicing for any single parcel: confirm the well record in GWELLS and the Fraser Health sewerage filing — never assume municipal services. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Bare-land strata acreage: none verified — and barred on ALR ground.** No bare-land strata is documented for Aberdeen in the research or siblings, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (Section 3), so no ALR acreage here can be converted into a bare-land strata community. Where bare-land strata lawfully exists elsewhere it is strata tenure (Form B/fees/bylaws apply to the lot — Section 3 block). (Research file above.)
- **Market thinness, labelled correctly:** SnapStats recorded Aberdeen detached inventory at 25 with 3 sales (12% ratio) and attached inventory at 7 with 0 sales in September 2026. SnapStats has NO acreage category and NO land category — it splits Detached vs "Condos & Townhomes" combined — so no detached figure is presented here as an acreage figure; the numbers are community context only. Abbotsford-wide detached median was $949,000 in September 2026. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file above).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014**, including Section 200 agricultural zones. In Agricultural One Zone (A1) the minimum subdivision lot area is 8.0 ha; in Agricultural Two Zone (A2) it is 16.0 ha. A single parcel's zone is shown in the City WebMap. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **OCP: Abbotsforward 2050 is CURRENT** (adopted December 2025). There is no Aberdeen neighbourhood plan. STALE-CITATION FLAG: the research file's "Bylaw No. 2600-2016" citation and any "2500-2016" citation are stale forms — flagged as a Jerry sweep item wherever a live page still carries them. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **SSMUH:** June 2024 SSMUH adoption is City-confirmed, but the SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED — "3560-2024" is asserted in some material and must NOT be stated as settled (both bases stated; the correction governs). SSMUH applies to lots previously limited to single-detached/duplex use, which is not Aberdeen's agricultural zoning, so it does not create subdivision rights on A1/A2 acreage. Abbotsford suite rules (Abbotsford ONLY): suite cap under 50% of the principal dwelling; coach/garden suite 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m². https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **Second dwellings on farm acreage:** the City-hosted A1 section permits one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot in accordance with the ALR Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the ALC, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, to a maximum floor area of 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, plus one secondary suite per lot to a maximum of 100 m². Note the tension a buyer must resolve in writing: the City A1 suite line (to 100 m²) vs the ALC rule that a suite in the ALR sits ONLY within the principal residence — on ALR ground the ALC rule governs (next bullet). https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Subdivision + servicing beyond the zone minimums:** HELD UNCONFIRMED in detail — the Abbotsford Development Bylaw number is held unconfirmed (process documented instead), and no Aberdeen-specific servicing standard beyond the A1/A2 minimums and the well/septic position in Section 2 was documented in the research; do not fill the gap from another city's rules. Confirm with the City for the specific parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- **ALR RULES IN FULL:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, as the ALC states it; non-farm use and added dwellings need City zoning compliance plus, where the ALR Use Regulation requires it, ALC approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence; an additional residence of 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) / 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR and appears nowhere in this file as a rule. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on acreage:** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $2,000,000 fully residential acreage purchase with no exemption = $2,000 + $36,000 (2% × $1,800,000) + $0 in the 3% tier = **$38,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. Exemptions: the First-Time Home Buyers' Program removes PTT on the first $500,000 at values ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000, partial to $860,000), reachable on land only through the register-lot-and-build route; the Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000) is a new-HOME exemption that can refund PTT paid on a vacant lot once a qualifying home is built — do not promise it on land held without a build. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at the CRA GI-003 level:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use), while sales can be taxable where the land was used primarily in a business, sold in the course of a business, or created by subdividing into more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are therefore generally taxable. Anything finer for a specific Aberdeen farm parcel — farmland is treated by the CRA in a separate info sheet — is UNCONFIRMED: get the GST position in the contract and take tax advice. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyer GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not to bare land itself — buying an Aberdeen acreage does not itself earn the rebate. MARCH-DATE NOTE: the condos sibling for this area carries a March 12, 2026 assent date in its own text — March 13 governs per CRA, the sibling date is noted here in-line and is NOT silently overwritten, and the sibling stands as a conforming-pass item. The older New Housing Rebate (36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 per buyer. FHSA: $8,000/year, $40,000 lifetime. A live page's $1.25M straight-line phase-out row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism where met. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy taxes + purchase prohibition:** no Aberdeen-specific speculation-tax or foreign-buyer position beyond the Abbotsford frame is documented in this area's research file, so none is imported from another city: Abbotsford IS on the Province's speculation and vacancy tax taxable-areas list for residential-class property (annual declaration; parcel-level application to farm acreage UNCONFIRMED until its assessment class is read). Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does NOT apply here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition is stated ONLY as this file's sources support it — vacant land itself can be purchased by a non-Canadian as amended March 27, 2023, per law-firm summaries; dwellings remain governed, and specific application is UNCONFIRMED at the official-text level (no primary text opened in the research). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Foreclosure / court-ordered sale — where met:** BC court-ordered sales follow the settled Supreme Court process (Rules 21-7 / 13-5, Practice Direction PD-66, Form 60). Form B diligence and PTT/GST treatment on a specific court sale are UNCONFIRMED in this file, kept distinct from the CRA GI-003 position above and from the narrower research stances noted in sibling files. REGISTRY LEDGER: Abbotsford HAS a Supreme Court registry. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/168_2009_07
- **Airport diligence:** land covered by the Abbotsford Airport Zoning Regulations (AZR), SOR/83-253, shown on City drawing M-500, is subject to height limits (approach, outer, transitional surfaces), and a developer on AZR land must obtain NAV CANADA and Transport Canada approvals before the City issues a building permit — check before planning a tall shop or barn on an acreage. https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/sor-83-253/FullText.html?wbdisable=true
- **Floodplain diligence:** Zoning Bylaw Schedule E sets floodplain boundaries; the floodplain status of any single Aberdeen acreage must be checked for that parcel in the City WebMap. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **Property taxes:** City of Abbotsford taxes due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3, 2026, second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Bare-land strata block (for completeness — none verified in Aberdeen):** where a bare-land strata lot does exist in BC, it is strata tenure — Form B diligence applies, stratas with 5+ lots are on a 5-year depreciation-report cycle since July 1, 2024, a minimum 10% CRF contribution has applied since November 1, 2023, rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022, and only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. No Aberdeen bare-land strata acreage is verified, so this block governs no Aberdeen parcel in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I subdivide my Aberdeen acreage into smaller lots?** Almost never at urban scale: A1 subdivides at a minimum of 8.0 ha and A2 at 16.0 ha, and subdivision inside the ALR additionally requires ALC approval. Price "future subdivision" on the zone, not on frontage. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **Q: Is the parcel in the ALR, and why does it matter?** Rural Aberdeen parcels are in or beside the ALR; ALR status controls subdivision, non-farm use and added dwellings, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. Check the specific parcel on the ALC map — status does not follow from the street address alone. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I add a second home for family or farm workers?** In A1, one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot (lot ≥0.4 ha; max 90 m² at ≤40 ha, 186 m² above 40 ha) under the ALR Use Regulation or ALC approval, plus one secondary suite per lot to 100 m² under the City A1 line — but in the ALR the ALC suite rule (suite ONLY within the principal residence) governs. Resolve which frame applies to the parcel in writing before designing. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is there city water and sewer on an Aberdeen acreage?** Outside the urban boundary, parcels may be on wells and onsite sewage instead. Pull the GWELLS well record and confirm the sewerage filing was made by an Authorized Person through Fraser Health before removing subjects. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: Does the airport limit what I can build?** If the parcel is in the AZR area on drawing M-500, height is capped by the approach/outer/transitional surfaces and NAV CANADA + Transport Canada approvals precede a City permit — directly relevant to a tall barn or shop. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- **Q: Will I pay GST on an Aberdeen farm acreage?** It depends on the seller and the land's history: CRA GI-003 states most individual personal-use sales are exempt, but business-use, in-the-course-of-business, and more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable, and farmland has its own CRA sheet. The answer for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and farm use are applied — confirm in the contract. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Can I get farm-class taxes on an Aberdeen acreage?** Only if the full three-tier income test is met ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha) with an application by October 31 — hobby acreage with no qualifying production does not qualify on acreage alone. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Q: Is the acreage in a floodplain?** Check Zoning Bylaw Schedule E in the City WebMap for that parcel; floodplain status is parcel-specific and is UNVERIFIED for any parcel until checked. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- **Q: What are the holding costs while I wait to build or farm?** City taxes (due July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties July 3 and September 16, 2026), plus well/septic maintenance and farm-class compliance costs where applicable; no strata fees exist on a fee-simple acreage. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Q: Which schools serve a Bradner Road acreage?** Only the settled locator chain for 2975 Bradner Road is documented, on a single-address basis (Aberdeen Elementary → Eugene Reimer Middle → Rick Hansen Secondary). Run your exact address in the SD34 locator — do not carry the chain across the sub-area. https://www.abbyschools.ca/policy-18-school-catchment-areas
