## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 (RU-2 named in feed listings for acreage parcels; per-parcel zone via GeoSource). https://www.tol.ca/at-your-service/bylaw-enforcement/bylaws/
- **SSMUH (Township position) — SETTLED:** Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) allows 3 units on eligible lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger eligible lots up to 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible, and no 6-unit tier applies in the Township. Eligibility also requires Urban Containment Boundary location and municipal water and sewer; a rural Otter lot failing those tests falls to one residential unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit, depending on zoning — the frame most rural Otter acreages will fall under. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Secondary suites + coach houses (Township):** suite cap 120 m²; a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102 — in Bylaw 2500 s.102 a "coach house" is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage; no coach-house size cap appears in s.100 as read in the rules-gaps pass, so none is stated. **Live conflict flagged:** s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m² vs the Jan 2025 guide saying not permitted — carried as a conflict, resolved nowhere in this file. (Township frame, FILE STATUS; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **R-CL zone (s.403):** minimum LOT area 220 m², maximum LOT area 464.5 m². A "355 m²" figure is nowhere in the bylaw and is never carried. (Township frame.)
- **Subdivision / servicing:** Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED for the Township (verified ledger). https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- **Willoughby Plan lineage (Township frame, carried for completeness):** Willoughby Plan = Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025); Willoughby Plan Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 = first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY, Public Hearing directed Jun 15, 2026 — reading-stage bylaws are never upgraded in this file. Otter's own plan context in the guide is the Rural Plan material, which is site copy / UNVERIFIED in §1.
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (ALR ground exists across this district — this block applies in full):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is NOT cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property taxes (Township of Langley):** due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026; utility fees are billed separately. This is the Township municipal regime — no rural regime is blended in. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a SEPARATE info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — CRA governs; a March 12 date is noted in-line where met elsewhere, never silently overwritten) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
