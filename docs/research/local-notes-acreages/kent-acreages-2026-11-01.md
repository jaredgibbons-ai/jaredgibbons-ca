## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Municipality: District of Kent, BC — a district municipality in the Fraser Valley Regional District on the north side of the Fraser River, covering 19,374 hectares (47,870 acres) of Fraser Valley floodplain and the slopes around the lower end of Harrison Lake, running from Harrison Mills in the west about 22 km northeast to Ruby Creek.
