## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **The regulator frame that applies from the first message (the rule set behind this page):** in BC, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); a licensee must give consumers information about commissions and fees, must work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. Disclosure happens at the outset of dealings; dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. A first call or text starts that disclosure conversation — until a client relationship is formed, an enquirer is a non-client. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried because acreage enquiries through this page routinely concern ALR ground across the footprint:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR — stated expressly: the flat form is NOT documented in this slug's research; it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — the program anchors an acreage buyer budgeting through this page will use:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **BC strata block — where an enquiry concerns a bare-land strata acreage elsewhere in the footprint:** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). Pad ground is NOT lot stock / land / acreage; manufactured-home pad tenancy percentages only (2026 = 2.3% + proportionate; 2027 = 2.2% + proportionate) and NO pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

No primary federal prohibition text was opened in this slug's research: UNCONFIRMED here.
