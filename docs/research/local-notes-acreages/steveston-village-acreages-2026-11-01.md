## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500** — houses on village side streets in Single Detached RS1/RS2 with suite rules in Section 5.4; village commercial and mixed-use zones named by the City include Steveston Commercial (CS3), Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU32) – Steveston Village, and Steveston Office Commercial (ZC49) – Chatham Street (Steveston). SSMUH / added-unit potential as the City states it generally: at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots — per-lot eligibility in a heritage conservation area is confirmed with the City, never assumed. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm

- **Subdivision and servicing — VERIFIED ledger:** Richmond Subdivision Bylaw 8751 and Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 are VERIFIED (carry exactly). Subdivision runs against the Bylaw 8500 zone minimums and Bylaws 8751/8752; confirm the works required for a specific lot with the City. (Standing servicing ledger.)
- **Flood — Bylaw 8204 governs every lot here:** the village sits on the South Arm of the Fraser River behind the City dike system — Richmond is a low-lying city in the Fraser River floodplain protected by a perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations, with dikes being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres — and the City's Steveston Island Dike project, a proposed dike around Steveston Island from London Farm to Garry Point Park with a navigation gate for Steveston Harbour, addresses flood protection for the Steveston waterfront and harbour. Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements — verify both for the specific lot before planning a rebuild or addition on a waterfront-adjacent street. https://www.letstalkrichmond.ca/StevestonIslandDike and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried for completeness; no Steveston Village ALR parcel is documented in this file's research:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)).
