## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Governing plan: the Eastern Hillsides Neighbourhood Plan, Schedule B4 of the Chilliwack 2050 OCP (Bylaw No. 5500).** For orientation inside the same bylaw: the Agricultural Plan is Schedule B1, Sardis is Schedule B5, Chilliwack Proper is Schedule B-2, and Downtown is Schedules B3 + D-6 — Eastern Hillsides is B4 only, and any document placing it under another schedule is wrong against the research. Under the previous OCP (Bylaw 2014, No. 4025) the Eastern Hillsides Comprehensive Development Plan formed part of the bylaw and Development Permit Area 2 covered the hillside/upland area — where older and newer documents differ, the 2050 OCP and the 2025-updated Neighbourhood Plan are the current sources. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/common/downloads/publication.pdf
- **Zoning: Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** — Section 6 carries the farm zones city-wide; the hillside zones governing this ground are SHR, R3, HR and RH with the minimums stated in Section 2. Price lot potential from the zone AND the slope table, not the zone alone: the SHR minimum alone swings from 360 m² to 990 m² depending on slope, and HR doubles from 0.2 ha to 0.4 ha without a community water system. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Geotechnical rules — the defining rule set for this ground:** steep slopes, main watercourses and ravines cross the plan area and the plan identifies geotechnical hazard areas; the 2017 plan amendments were made specifically to protect future development from rock fall, landslide or earthflow with unstable conditions, and Council directed an expanded Marble Hill development moratorium area in 2017 after continuing ground movement was reported. The City's Greenspace Plan identifies the lower and upper Marble Hill slide areas as undevelopable lands. Get the property's geotechnical reports and any covenants on title before pricing or removing subjects, and check whether the lot sits in the moratorium area before counting on subdivision or a build. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1962 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2650/2021-05-28%20GSP%20MASTER%202020%20-%20Urban%20Format.pdf
- **Hillside Development Permit Area:** hillside lots are subject to the 2050 OCP's Hillside Development Permit Area (Schedule D-5), under which a development permit is required for works including subdivision of land with 20% or greater slopes — slope measurement is a closing-condition item, not an afterthought. Heritage note in the same plan: the historic wagon road (Browlee Road) is identified as a heritage asset to protect; wagon roads in the area include Nixon, Ruddock, Allan and Hinkley. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557

Parcel-level ALR status for Eastern Hillsides ground is UNVERIFIED in the research — check the ALC maps before applying or excluding this block for a specific parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
