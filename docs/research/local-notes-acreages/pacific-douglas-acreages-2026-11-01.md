## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP — Surrey 2050, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026):** the current plan. Bylaw No. 18020 is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current. The Douglas NCP (1999) designations have been superseded — the in-force check for any Douglas lot is the Surrey 2050 designation plus the lot's zone under Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000, not the 1999 NCP designations listing text still quotes. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/douglas-ncp
- **Zoning / suites / SSMUH (Surrey frame):** Zoning By-law No. 12000. One secondary suite may be permitted in a single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling; its habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area; a suite is not a separate strata lot. The old 90 m² Surrey suite cap is NOT a current rule — do not state it. Coach houses and garden suites: permitted in residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes; 35 m² minimum and 75 m² maximum, rising to a maximum of 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the principal dwelling has not achieved its maximum floor area. Surrey's SSMUH update was adopted July 8, 2024. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only — an owner must host from their principal residence and hold a City business licence. **Licence figure CONFLICT, carried openly:** $358/yr (Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000, Part 4 s. - **Streamside setbacks:** Surrey's streamside setbacks run under Part 7A by stream class — there is NO single riparian figure for this ground, and none is stated.
- **Servicing:** Surrey Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED — see the ledger carried in full below. **Registry:** Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED).
- **ALR RULES IN FULL, wherever ALR ground exists or the area is farm-adjacent:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a PERMISSION rule, never an absolute ban, and ALC Bulletin 05 is never cited for it. Residence caps: one principal residence, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; at most one additional residence of 90 m² on parcels ≤40 ha or 186 m² on parcels >40 ha; local bylaws may restrict further but not be more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if less than 0.8 ha. Applications by the legislated October 31 deadline for the next assessment/tax year; late applications are not accepted; a buyer continuing to farm must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. Farmland is addressed by CRA in a separate info sheet and is usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — no farm parcel is assumed exempt on the vacant-land rule alone.
