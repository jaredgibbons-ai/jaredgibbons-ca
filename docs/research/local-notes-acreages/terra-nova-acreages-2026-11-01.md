## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500** — AG1 (Section 14.1) for the farm ground beside the pocket (farming and compatible uses consistent with the ALR, one principal dwelling unit per lot, a secondary suite as a secondary use, 2.0 ha subdivision minimum where ALC approval is not required, no single detached housing on a lot under 828.0 m² in that zone); for non-ALR residential lots, at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot with Bill 44 minimums of 3 units at 280 m² or less and 4 units above. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=53061 and https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **OCP: City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000**, with Schedule 2.2B Thompson (Terra Nova) as the sub-area plan for this ground — the City planning name is Thompson; the board name Terra Nova is followed for the ground itself. https://Richmond.Ca/city-hall/strategies/sale.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Subdivision and servicing — VERIFIED ledger:** Richmond Subdivision Bylaw 8751 and Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 are VERIFIED. In the ALR, subdivision additionally requires ALC approval (below). (Standing servicing ledger; a sibling carrying Richmond servicing as UNCONFIRMED is a conforming-pass item.)
- **Flood — Bylaw 8204:** Richmond is protected by a perimeter dike system (49 km of dikes, 39 pump stations city-wide) designed for a 1:500 flood event, with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise; the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets setbacks from a dike right-of-way and bars building below the flood plain construction level in its Schedule B — a core check on west- and north-edge parcels beside the dyke. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **ALR — full block, GOVERNING the AG1/ALR farm parcels documented in Section 2:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval (in AG1, not permitted unless approved by the ALC; 2.0 ha minimum lot area where that approval is not required); ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (parcels over 40 ha); and farm class runs on the three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of production over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31; a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met — that line is documented only on the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file). This block governs the ALR parcels only; it does not apply to the non-ALR house lots in the pocket. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=53061
- **If a strata purchase is made instead (redirect case — e.g. Quilchena Green in its townhomes home file):** the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — Quilchena Green's conflicting "45+" / "55+" published labels are carried unresolved in its home file, and a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw; disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 fully residential Terra Nova purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Property taxes:** City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there real acreage in Terra Nova?** Yes — but it is ALR farm ground, not an acreage subdivision. The AG1/ALR parcels beside the pocket carry ALC subdivision approval, the residence caps and the farm-class test in Section 3; the residential pocket itself is ordinary fee-simple house lots. Verified named acreage developments counted here: 0. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Can an ALR parcel beside the pocket be subdivided?** Not unless approved by the Agricultural Land Commission; where that approval is not required, the AG1 minimum lot area is 2.0 ha. Price no split before the ALC position is confirmed for the parcel. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=53061
- **Q: Can a farm parcel have a second dwelling?** ALC guidance allows one principal residence (to 500 m², suite inside it only) and, if the regulation conditions are met, one additional residence per parcel — 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, 186 m² over 40 ha. AG1 zoning also lists a secondary suite as a secondary use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Does farm class cut the tax bill on a small farm parcel?** Only on the three-tier test in Section 3 — $10,000 of production under 0.8 ha, $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" quote is an error wherever it appears. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Terra Nova address?** On a single-address basis only: for the sample address 5999 Blanshard Drive, the SD38 School Locator run in this file's research gives Spul'u'kwuks Elementary and Burnett Secondary. Spul'u'kwuks Elementary sits inside the pocket, but catchments are address-based and Richmond sample-address results are non-transferable — check every other address in the locator. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Q: Is the No. 1 Road corner development a buying opportunity?** No — 6011/6031 No. 1 Road is a 2012 City-panel proposal record (QUEUE), documented as a proposal only and counted nowhere; a current market presale on a developer source is UNVERIFIED. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/agendas/archives/dpp/2012/082212_minutes.htm
- **Q: Is Quilchena Green Terra Nova acreage?** No — it is a built 91-unit townhouse complex (LMS423) counted in the terra-nova townhomes file, and it is not the Quilchena sub-area. A purchase there is a strata purchase under the Section 3 strata block, with its 45+/55+ age-label conflict carried unresolved in its home file. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What flood checks does a west-edge parcel need?** The pocket sits behind the City perimeter dike system (1:500 design, dikes being raised from 3.5 m to 4.7 m) and Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and a flood plain construction level — get the parcel-level position early, especially beside the dyke. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **Q: Do I pay GST on an ALR farm parcel?** Do not assume the vacant-land exemption: at the CRA GI-003 level most individual personal-use vacant-land sales are exempt, but the CRA treats farmland sales as usually taxable in its separate farmland sheet — UNCONFIRMED per parcel until vendor status and use are applied, and the contract must state who bears GST. PTT is separate (Section 3). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Can I bring dogs to the rural park beside the farm ground?** No — Terra Nova Rural Park is a NO DOGS ALLOWED area under Animal Control Bylaw 8612, despite its 63 acres of trails, the Adventure Play Environment and the Sharing Farm. That surprises dog-owning buyers pricing the park as an amenity. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?id=80
