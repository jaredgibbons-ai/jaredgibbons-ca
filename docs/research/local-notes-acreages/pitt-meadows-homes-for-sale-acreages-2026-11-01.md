## 2. THE ACREAGE ITSELF

Verified named acreage developments counted here: 0. With 78% of the land base in the ALR, the acreage market is the farm/rural parcel market itself: A-1 and A-2 agricultural parcels and RR rural-residential parcels outside the urban containment boundary, bought as whole titles. There is no acreage subdivision product, no bare-land strata acreage community and no presale acreage offering verified on any source checked.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: City of Pitt Meadows Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011.** RS Large Lot Residential minimum lot area 0.2 ha; R-3, R-4 and R-6 Urban Residential carry a minimum lot area for subdivision of 1,215 sq m and a minimum lot width for subdivision of 12 m, allowing up to three, four and six dwelling units (in R-6 the maximum is five units, or six where at least one unit meets the adaptable housing requirements in the BC Building Code); RM-1 Multi-Family Residential 1 sets a minimum lot area of 0.4 ha for townhouse developments; A-1 General Agricultural minimum subdivision lot area 8 ha; A-2 Large Lot Agricultural 16 ha. The 1,215 sq m figure is a subdivision minimum ONLY: the City's SSMUH implementation update states the housekeeping amendment clarified that the minimum lot area requirement applies to subdivision and not to development on existing lots. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **OCP: "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022;** the North Lougheed Area Plan was adopted by Official Community Plan Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/official-community-plan-0
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for most Pitt Meadows acreage (78% of the land base):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, in addition to the A-1 (8 ha) / A-2 (16 ha) bylaw minimums above. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, with applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR: it understates the test for small and large parcels alike, and no such line is asserted in this file or documented in this area's research file. Farm class is the holding-cost difference between a working parcel and an idle one — confirm the parcel's current class and income record before pricing the tax position. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Suites and garden suites (non-ALR reading; ALR parcels are governed by the ALC caps above):** secondary suites and garden suites are listed accessory uses in the RS, RR, R-4 and R-6 zones, and the City's garden suite guide sets one suite per lot at 33 sq m to 90 sq m. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Short-term rentals: PROHIBITED.** A City staff report states "Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Zoning Bylaw," and Pitt Meadows appears in the Province's principal-residence-requirement community list. Price no Pitt Meadows acreage — house, suite or garden suite — on STR income. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699 (basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-condos-2026-11-01.md, §3)
- **Floodplain — the defining acreage rule here:** **over 95%** of the land base lies within the Fraser River and (to a smaller degree) Pitt River floodplain (City 2020 Business Plan — the figure these notes retire to), protected by a 60 km dike system, under Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008; zoning requires buildings to be sited 15 m from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches in agricultural and rural zones. LIVE CONFLICT (flagged, never silently resolved): live pages stating ~85–86% (Financial Plan 2026–2030 attribution; superseded OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007 at about 86%) conflict with the Business Plan figure. Check the flood construction level, any covenant on title, and insurance early on every parcel. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer against the zone minimums above and, in the ALR, ALC approval in addition. HELD UNCONFIRMED: the Pitt Meadows servicing / subdivision-and-development bylaw number — process documented instead of a number; never quote a servicing standard for a Pitt Meadows parcel from a listing or a summary. - **Property transfer tax (PTT) on acreage:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 fully residential acreage purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. - **GST on farm and vacant land — ONLY at the CRA GI-003 level:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What am I actually buying when I buy an "acreage" in Pitt Meadows?** A whole fee-simple farm or rural-residential parcel — A-1/A-2 agricultural or RR rural-residential ground, in a city where 78% of the land base is ALR and over 95% is floodplain (95% figure: City 2020 Business Plan; live conflict flagged in Section 3). There is no acreage subdivision, no bare-land strata acreage and no presale acreage product to buy instead. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is this parcel in the ALR — and what changes if it is?** Check the parcel on the ALC maps; at city scale the odds are 78%. If it is in: subdivision needs ALC approval on top of the A-1 (8 ha) / A-2 (16 ha) minimums, the residence cannot be stratified, and the housing caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside that residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is the parcel in the floodplain?** Almost certainly — over 95% of the land base is floodplain behind the 60 km dike system (City 2020 Business Plan; the live pages' "over 85%" Financial Plan figure is the flagged conflict, Section 3). Check the flood construction level under Bylaw No. 2384, 2008, any covenant on title, and insurance early; budget the separate drainage levy. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Q: Can I split the parcel, or stratify it later for family?** Stratify: no, if it is ALR — residences in the ALR cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Split: only within the zone minimums (A-1 8 ha / A-2 16 ha; RR-2 4 ha / RR-3 2 ha / RR-4 1 ha; RS 0.2 ha) and, in the ALR, only with ALC approval. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Q: Can I run short-term rentals on the acreage or in a garden suite?** No — short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Pitt Meadows at all. Garden suites are a long-term form here: one per lot at 33 sq m to 90 sq m in the zones that list them (RS, RR, R-4, R-6), subject on ALR land to the ALC suite rule (inside the principal residence only). https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699 and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Q: What farm income do I need to keep farm class?** All three tiers, not a flat figure: $2,500 on a 0.8–4 ha farm operation; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31. A page or listing stating only "$2,500+" understates the test for small and large parcels. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Q: Is the acreage serviced?** UNCONFIRMED at parcel level in every source checked: the City documents its diked, drained urban servicing context and a drainage levy, but no parcel-level water/sewer vs well/septic statement. Confirm with the City for the parcel — do not assume urban servicing on rural/ALR ground. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a Pitt Meadows farm acreage?** Do not assume either way. The CRA's vacant-land sheet makes most individual personal-use sales exempt, but it treats farmland in a separate sheet as usually taxable with limited exemptions. The answer is UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel until vendor status and farm use are applied; the contract must state who bears GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two City documents give two floodplain figures — and both are City sources.** The 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the land base in the floodplain; the live guide pages state "over 85%" sourced to the City Financial Plan 2026–2030, and the superseded OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007 stated about 86%. This program retires to 95% and flags the conflict rather than averaging it — a buyer quoted "85%" on a Pitt Meadows page is reading the other City document, not a typo to ignore. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **The 1,215 sq m minimum does not do what sellers say it does.** It is a subdivision minimum for R-3/R-4/R-6, clarified by the City's own SSMUH update to apply to subdivision and NOT to development on existing lots — an existing smaller urban lot can still be developed. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **Meadow Highlands' zoning had to be invented to match its existing use.** The park's land use was governed by a land use contract that expired June 30, 2024 (as mandated by the Province) over an RM-1 zone that does not permit manufactured homes; Zoning Amendment Bylaw 2991, 2024 created a Residential Mobile Home (RMH) zone for it. It is a co-op, family/all-age, NOT 55+ — the Pitt Meadows property most likely to be mis-assumed as seniors housing.
