- **ALR RULES IN FULL — ALR ground exists inside the municipal boundary (Port Kells / farm-edge ground) and Surrey is farm-adjacent, so the full frame governs wherever a parcel is in the reserve:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps as the ALC states them: a principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, and no more than 1,000 m² of fill (including structural aggregate for foundations and driveways) can be placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted ONLY when located within the principal residence, entirely contained within it; one additional residence, detached from the principal residence, is permitted up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, and building more than the permitted number, or larger than the size limits, requires ALC approval. City zoning and the ALC frame both apply; neither displaces the other. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices of $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — and new applications for farm classification must be submitted by the legislated deadline of October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it — owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page; it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are the market numbers in this file land prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no acreage category; the §1 figures are Surrey residential context only (detached median $1,215,000 and Condos & Townhomes median $512,944 in September 2026, both 13-month lows), and land-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. Price a lot on its own zone, services and comparable land sales. (§1.)
- **Q: How many communities does Surrey have — and why does it matter for land?** Seven, including City Centre: City Centre, Cloverdale, Fleetwood, Guildford, Newton, South Surrey and Whalley, per the City's own Our Communities page. It matters because plan designations, servicing and the board data all split along those lines — and because the board sub-area covering City Centre product is Whalley, not "Surrey City Centre," which is not a board name at all. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- **Q: Can I build a house on vacant ALR land in Surrey?** Under the ALC frame, generally one principal residence of 500 m² or less total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite ONLY inside that principal residence; and one additional detached residence up to 90 m² (properties of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). City zoning must also permit the residence, and anything larger or more numerous needs ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide an ALR parcel into strata lots?** Not without the ALC's permission: depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and ALCA s.21(1) bars subdividing agricultural land unless the Commission permits it or the ALR General Regulation allows it (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6). Do not write "can never be stratified," and do not cite Bulletin 05 for stratification. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a Surrey house lot add a suite or a coach house?** One secondary suite per dwelling unit, with habitable floor area at most 40% of the principal dwelling unit and never inside a coach house or garden suite — there is no current 90 m² suite cap in Bylaw No. 12000, and a suite is not a separate strata lot.
