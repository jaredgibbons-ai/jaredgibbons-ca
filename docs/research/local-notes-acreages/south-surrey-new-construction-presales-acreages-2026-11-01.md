## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **REDMA block (the presale frame):** under BC's Real Estate Development Marketing Act, the developer must give the buyer a disclosure statement before signing (as the live page states it, attributed there to BCFSA presales information); **REDMA s.21 — the buyer may rescind by written notice within 7 days**; **REDMA s.18 — deposits are held in trust**. The page describes deposits as "usually 10%–20% total, staged over construction and held in trust" — that range is page copy, UNVERIFIED as a rule in this slug's research (no BCFSA, BC Laws or Province page stating the legal specifics was opened in that pass); the disclosure statement for the specific project governs its deposit schedule. Verify current REDMA rules on BCFSA's site and have a lawyer read the disclosure statement inside the rescission window. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-new-construction-presales.html and https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Regulator frame:** in British Columbia, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), and BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Warranty (page copy):** the page lists a 2-5-10 warranty (2 years materials and labour, 5 years building envelope, 10 years structure) — carried as the page's statement, UNVERIFIED on an official source in this slug's research; the specific warranty for a project is in its disclosure statement. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-new-construction-presales.html

- **ALR RULES IN FULL — completeness only (no ALR parcel documented here):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan IS a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — never the shorthand "cannot be stratified," never cite Bulletin 05 for stratification. Caps: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (parcels 40 ha or less) / ≤186 m² (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if the operation is under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page only); it is NOT documented in this slug's research. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **BC strata block (the built-form product this page sells is strata; applies in full to any bare-land strata lot):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies to a qualifying home purchase); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. Farmland caution: CRA treats farmland sales in a separate info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. GST on a new built home itself is a different question from GST on vacant land and is governed by the housing rebates below — no project-specific GST position is stated here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
