## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020.** Parcel-level minimum lot sizes are set out in the bylaw itself; rural zones such as RU16 carry a 1.6 ha minimum in City notices. Verify a specific parcel's exact zone with the City before pricing any use. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning
- **OCP: Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026** — it replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025 and keeps the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction, with a Regional Context Statement added, per the City's public hearing notice. The OCP carries Urban Residential and Suburban Residential designations for its housing areas, plus an Urban Residential Townhouse designation. Live guides stating an adoption date of "June 1, 2026" are wrong — that error is a Jerry item asserted ONLY in the three guide files named in the FILE STATUS block, not in this page's research. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan
- **Suites and SSMUH:** zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted (SSMUH incorporation Bylaw 6299-2024, Jul 17, 2024), and the City's planning pages set out how secondary suites and detached accessory units are handled by zone. **Mission's SSMUH unit counts are held UNCONFIRMED in this program — Surrey's figures are never imported.** On ALR land the ALC suite rule below controls instead. https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for the city's rural-edge ALR parcels:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in this band file's own research — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Subdivision and servicing:** for a non-ALR Mission parcel, subdivision runs through the City against the zone minimums and the City's servicing requirements under the **DSCB (Development Servicing and Construction Bylaw) No. 5650-2017 (City source, title confirmed)**; for an ALR parcel, ALC approval is required in addition. Never quote a servicing standard for a rural parcel from a listing or a summary.
- **Floodplain — the south edge and Hatzic:** the Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's south edge and Hatzic Prairie sits behind the Dewdney Dyke system; confirm floodplain status, flood construction requirements and the diking levy for the specific parcel. Which parcels are affected is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED until City/FVRD floodplain mapping is read for that parcel. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on acreage:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M lives in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, not followed — it is met in this band's conversation precisely because this file's floor is $1.8M, and the $1.8M anchor above ($34,000) is the tier math at this band's own floor. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on farm and vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland is the caution: the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a SEPARATE info sheet and treats farmland sales as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — a Mission farm purchase must therefore never be assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file; the contract must state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. At this band's prices the phase-outs are spent — the programs are carried here for completeness of the land boundary, not as band savings. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
