## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Surrey frame:** OCP = Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — never cite Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. SSMUH (Jul 8, 2024): a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Zone-name currency — this slug's documented defect:** Surrey's July 8, 2024 SSMUH changes renamed RF-12 / RF-12C / RF-13 to the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone, RF-9 / RF-9C / RF-10 to R5, RF / RF-SS to R3, and RF-SD to R6. The live page's FAQ still quotes "RF-12c or RF-13" for Clayton coach-home lots — pre-2024 names, out of date against the current bylaw names; use R4 in offers and marketing. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites and coach houses:** one secondary suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. Verifying a suite's legality runs through City building-permit records and registration — an unpermitted suite is not retroactively legalized by the SSMUH changes. A coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² only on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR); all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow one; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house, and one extra parking space is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. Verified on the City's own Land Development pages: the Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw, worked through the City's Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. SSMUH units do not create new lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside / riparian:** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, with different setbacks applied to various types of streams — read per parcel against its stream class in COSMOS; NO single riparian figure is stated in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only — only the property owner may host from their primary residence, tenants cannot operate one, for periods of less than 90 consecutive days per patron, with a business licence at $358 per year. **Licence figure CONFLICT disclosed:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposing the STR amendments put the licence at $350/yr plus fees — $358/yr is carried with the $350/yr + fees figure disclosed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R094.pdf
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — for Cloverdale's ALR-edge parcels:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR — stated expressly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in this slug's own research; it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — a purchase under $1,800,000 meets only the first two tiers. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math, fully residential, no exemption unless stated): $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000 (1% × $200,000 = $2,000 + 2% × $1,300,000 = $26,000); $1.8M → $34,000 ($2,000 + 2% × $1,600,000 = $32,000); $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000. A $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program and is flagged wherever met as the OPEN summary-pass reconciliation item, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file, and the GST position of any specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and use are applied. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 is noted in-line in some sibling files — March 13 governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **BC strata block — redirect case (a strata purchase elsewhere in Surrey):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions); REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission right on a developer sale. None of it applies to a fee-simple detached or acreage purchase itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
