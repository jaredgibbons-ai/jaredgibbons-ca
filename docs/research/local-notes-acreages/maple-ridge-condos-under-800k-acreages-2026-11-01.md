- **First-time buyers' PTT program — live across this whole band:** the full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024), the property 0.5 hectares or smaller with only residential improvements, used only as the buyer's principal residence, and the exemption applies to the first $500,000 of the purchase price; a partial exemption is available above $835,000 and below $860,000 — so a qualifying first-time buyer can in principle use the program anywhere in this band, subject to all the program's other conditions. The home-based exemptions also have land routes — a first-time buyer registering a vacant lot and building can keep the FTHB exemption if lot value plus build cost is $835,000 or less (partial under $860,000), building and occupying by the first anniversary; the Newly Built Home Exemption can refund lot PTT once a qualifying new home is built ($1,100,000 / $1,150,000 thresholds). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
