## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) is CURRENT — Bylaw 18020 is NEVER cited as current.** Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. The neighbouring plan document (South Campbell Heights) is context for the ground to the north, not a Hazelmere designation — confirm a parcel's OCP designation and zone directly. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- **A-1 / Part 10 figures — page statements, held UNVERIFIED:** the live page states A-1 General Agriculture zoning with Part 10 minimum lot areas of 4 hectares inside the ALR and 2 hectares outside it for new A-1 lots, and one single-family dwelling which may contain one secondary suite under A-1. Those figures were not re-read in Bylaw 12000 in the research pass — do not quote them for a parcel until they are read in the current consolidated bylaw. The RA 4,050 m² figure for nearby land IS consistent with the City's SSMUH zone table. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere.html and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH caution for this ground — do not import the urban framing:** Surrey's SSMUH allowances apply to eligible residential-zoned properties under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including lands not connected to community water and sewer. Most Hazelmere acreage ground sits outside that shape, so the standard 3–4 unit framing (Jul 8, 2024 framework) must not be applied to a Hazelmere lot without a parcel check of zone, containment boundary and servicing. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites:** Surrey-wide, one secondary suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit; a suite is not a separate strata lot. On A-1 ground the live page states one dwelling which may contain one suite — carried as a page statement (above); on ALR ground the ALC rule (suite inside the principal residence only, where local bylaws allow it) governs. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Coach houses and garden suites:** Surrey-wide, all residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite of 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 below maximum FAR, with one extra parking space required. For Hazelmere specifically, a separate coach house on an A-1 or ALR acreage is UNVERIFIED parcel-by-parcel (Section 2) — the Surrey-wide permission does not settle an A-1 / ALR parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — for any parcel that proves to be in the reserve:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel; a principal residence may be up to 500 m² total floor area without ALC application; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence and only where local bylaws allow it; one additional detached residence may be permitted of up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; local governments may further restrict residences through zoning. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in Hazelmere's own research file — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. Whether a specific Hazelmere property carries farm class: UNVERIFIED in this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer against the zone minimums and the Subdivision and Development (servicing) Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 — VERIFIED from the City's own Land Development / Servicing Agreement pages. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 and vary by stream class — the Little Campbell River and Jacobsen Creek make this a live check here — NO single riparian figure is ever stated; read the setback per parcel in COSMOS. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere.html
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** an STR is the renting of a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, and tenants cannot run one. LICENCE FIGURE CONFLICT, carried openly: the City's STR page states a business licence at $358/yr, while the City's 2024 corporate report states $350/yr + fees — carry $358/yr with the $350/yr + fees figure disclosed, and direct readers to the City licence page before quoting either. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What am I actually buying when I buy an "acreage" in Hazelmere?** An individual fee-simple rural parcel at South Surrey's south-east corner — the ground the City's neighbouring plan document describes as having kept its rural, agricultural character for a century. There is no acreage subdivision product to buy instead; each parcel is judged on its own title, zone (A-1 vs RA), servicing, setbacks and ALR status. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is Hazelmere in the ALR, and what housing does the ALR allow?** ALR status is parcel-specific and no Hazelmere parcel result is stated in this file — check the parcel on ALC mapping. Where a parcel is in the ALR, the ALC's published frame is: one principal residence up to 500 m², a secondary suite only inside that residence where local bylaws allow it, and at most one additional detached residence of up to 90 m² (parcels 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (parcels over 40 ha), with local zoning able to be more restrictive — and no stratification of the residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I split a Hazelmere acreage?** Only against the zone minimums and, in the ALR, only with ALC approval. The page states Part 10 minimums of 4 hectares inside the ALR and 2 hectares outside it for new A-1 lots — carried as page statements, UNVERIFIED on the Bylaw 12000 text in this file. Confirm the parcel's zone and the current consolidated bylaw before pricing any split. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere.html
- **Q: Can a Hazelmere acreage add the usual Surrey SSMUH units?** Generally not on the standard framing: Surrey's SSMUH allowances apply to eligible residential-zoned properties under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including lands not connected to community water and sewer. Hazelmere's acreage lots need a parcel-level check of zone, containment boundary and servicing before any added-unit claim. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: The listing shows a coach house — is it permitted?** Verify it before marketing it. Feed listings here include coach-house product, but on ALR land an additional residence must fit the ALC size limits, and on A-1 land the live page itself flags a separate coach house as unconfirmed on a City source — the unit's status is UNVERIFIED parcel-by-parcel until the City (and, where applicable, the ALC frame) confirms it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere.html
- **Q: Is the acreage serviced?** Parcel-specific — both fabrics exist inside this one sub-area. The page's general guidance is private wells and onsite septic (with GWELLS as the well-record check), yet one feed listing is described in its own text as on city sewer and water. Whether a specific Hazelmere property carries farm class today is a BC Assessment / parcel question: UNVERIFIED here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
