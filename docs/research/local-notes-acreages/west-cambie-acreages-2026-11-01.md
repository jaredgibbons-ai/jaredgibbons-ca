## 2. THE ACREAGE ITSELF

West Cambie is built-out urban Richmond ground: freehold detached houses in The Oaks, Odlinwood and the older Alexandra lots, plus strata apartments and townhomes concentrated along Alexandra Road and Odlin Road. There is no acreage subdivision, no bare-land strata acreage community, no farm ground and no presale land offering documented for West Cambie on any source. This variant file carries no acreage roster of its own. Verified named acreage developments counted here: 0.**
