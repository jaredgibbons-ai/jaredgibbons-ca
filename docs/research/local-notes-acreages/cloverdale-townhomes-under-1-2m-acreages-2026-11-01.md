## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **City plan:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the **Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current in this file. The Cloverdale Town Centre Plan is being updated (boundary expanded July 22, 2024). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000.** Its SSMUH framework (in force for this area since **July 8, 2024**) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the former RF-12 / RF-12C zones were replaced by R4 and RF-SD by R6 in those changes. On ALR land the ALC rules below govern instead. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried wherever ALR ground exists or the area is farm-adjacent; Cloverdale is farm-adjacent, with ALR parcels on its lowland edge:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR owned ONLY by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page — it is NOT documented in this slug's research file and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Suites, coach houses and STRs (urban ground):** one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area**, never inside a coach house or garden suite, and not a separate strata lot; a coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), with lane access required for a coach house on smaller lots and one extra parking space. Short-term rentals are permitted, **owner-only** (the host must be the owner living at the property as their primary residence; tenants cannot operate one) — the licence figure is carried as **$358 per year** per the City's Short-Term Rentals page, with the conflict disclosed: the City's 2024 corporate report proposed **$350 per year plus fees** — confirm the current figure on the City's licence page before quoting it. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Subdivision and servicing:** Surrey servicing is **Subdivision and Development Bylaw No. 8830 (1986), VERIFIED from the City's own pages** — carried with that sourcing. For an ALR parcel, ALC approval is required in addition to the City process. **Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated in this file; read the setback per parcel in COSMOS.**
- **The strata frame for this band's actual product:** BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal, and the provincial strata-housing pages are the official route for the rules on depreciation reports, contingency reserve funds and strata governance that a townhome buyer meets in the document package — read the depreciation report and Form B before removing conditions. The full BC strata block: Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — the band's tiers, with the anchors:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — every purchase in this band is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers; the 3% tier and the further 2% do not arise under $1.2M.
