Residence caps: principal ≤500 m² total floor area; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; one additional residence capped at 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) / 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form, never a flat figure.** Gross annual farm income required: $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha; $10,000 on an operation under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. - **Property transfer tax in this band — tiers and worked anchors (inputs + tier math).** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — every purchase in this band is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers; the 3% tier and the further 2% do not arise in this band. Worked anchors: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K cap → $14,000 ($6,000 payable for a fully qualifying first-time buyer); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M (band ceiling) → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M purely residential → **$93,000** including the further 2% tier over $3M (tier math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). A townhomes sibling elsewhere carries $78,000 for $3.5M — OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met, never silently carry $78,000. Band-specific worked example from the townhomes sibling (input stated): at $1,100,000, general PTT = 1% × $200,000 + 2% × $900,000 = $2,000 + $18,000 = $20,000, before any exemption. First-time buyers: full exemption at $835,000 or less (first $500,000), partial to $860,000 — only the band's lower slice can use it at all. Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
