## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) governs** — never cite Bylaw 18020 as current. The South Newton plan was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 update; development is led by the OCP, with the Dec 2, 2024 designation update endorsed for this area. **Zoning: Bylaw No. 12000** (R3 minimum subdivision lot area 660 m², Part 26 Section F, as the research records). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Suites, coach houses and SSMUH (Surrey frame):** one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite, habitable floor area at most **40%** of the principal unit; coach houses/garden suites in all residential zones allowing detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes, 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR, one extra parking space required. **SSMUH (in force Jul 8, 2024):** maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Subdivision and servicing: Surrey Bylaw 8830 VERIFIED** — subdivision/servicing runs through the City against the zone minimums and Bylaw 8830's works-and-services requirements. **Streamside: Zoning Part 7A setbacks apply BY STREAM CLASS — there is NO single figure**; South Newton holds fish-bearing creeks including Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries, so a creek-side lot's building envelope is a per-lot Part 7A check. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Short-term rentals:** only an owner who lives at the property as their primary residence may host; tenants cannot operate one; a business licence at **$358 per year** is required — with the City's **$350 proposal disclosed** as a proposal, not the current fee. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED, and the contract must state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
