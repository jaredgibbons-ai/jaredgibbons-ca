## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **The settled court process, step by step (program treatment, carried in full):** (1) Petition — "A proceeding for foreclosure of the equitable right to redeem mortgaged property or for redemption must be started by petition" in the Supreme Court of British Columbia (Rule 21-7 (1)); all persons whose interest in or claim to the mortgaged property is sought to be extinguished and all persons against whom any relief is sought must be made respondents (Rule 21-7 (2)); a petitioner may also join a claim arising out of the mortgage or collateral security and a person liable to pay the mortgage debt (Rule 21-7 (3)). (2) Later interests — if the petitioner registers a certificate of pending litigation, a person who subsequently registers an interest need not be served, is bound by an order made in the proceeding, and may file a response to petition (Rule 21-7 (4)). (3) Information notice — a petitioner who serves the petition must include the informational notice for foreclosure proceedings in Form 125 (Rule 21-7 (4.1)). (4) Powers of the court — the court may make a final order of foreclosure (Rule 21-7 (5)(a)); order payment of what is due within a redemption period the court fixes, with foreclosure of the equity of redemption in default (Rule 21-7 (5)(b)) — the Rule text states no fixed redemption length; and determine summarily, or order an account be taken and certified, the amount due (Rule 21-7 (5)(c)). Subrules (1)–(5)(c) were re-opened on BC Laws and CONFIRMED verbatim in batch 10 of this program. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Conduct of sale and approval (Rule 13-5 / PD-66 / Form 60 — settled treatment, research stance flagged):** under the settled program treatment, a court-ordered sale is conducted under Rule 13-5 — the court gives directions on conduct of sale, may set a reserve (upset) price, a sale proceeds by contract conditional on court approval, and competing offers are dealt with under the court's directions and Practice Direction PD-66, with the registrar's certification in Form 60. **Flagged precisely:** this slug's own research holds Rule 13-5 mechanics, PD-66 (including the live page's sealed-bids-by-9:45 a.m. account) and everything in Rule 21-7 after (5)(c) UNVERIFIED program-wide in its own pass — the live page's accounts of those mechanics are the page's own statements, not adopted from the research. - **ALR RULES IN FULL — for any ALR acreage parcel met through this topic (a court sale does not change them):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban; ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How does a BC foreclosure start?** By petition in the Supreme Court of British Columbia under Rule 21-7 (1); everyone whose interest is sought to be extinguished must be made a respondent, and the petitioner must serve the court's foreclosure information notice, Form 125, with the petition. (§3.)
- **Q: How long is the redemption period?** There is no fixed length in the Rule text — the redemption period is the period the court fixes in its order under Rule 21-7 (5)(b). Ask which stage the file is at before spending on inspections: the first order can already be a final order of foreclosure under Rule 21-7 (5)(a). (§3.)
- **Q: Can someone outbid my accepted offer at the approval hearing?** Under the settled treatment, a sale proceeds by contract conditional on court approval under Rule 13-5 / PD-66, and competing offers are a real possibility to plan for — but this slug's own research holds those mechanics UNVERIFIED in its pass, so get the bid instructions for the specific file from the seller's counsel, confirmed by your own lawyer, rather than relying on any general account (including the live page's 9:45 a.m. sealed-bid account). (§3.)
- **Q: Where is a South Surrey foreclosure filed?** UNVERIFIED as a stated mapping — Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry, and this slug's research opened no registry source. New Westminster is a working assumption only, never a fact to rely on; the court file itself states its registry. (§§1, 3.)
- **Q: Will I get a Form B or a Property Disclosure Statement on a court-ordered acreage or strata lot?** Form B availability on a court sale is UNCONFIRMED, and PDS/Schedule "A" treatment is UNVERIFIED in this slug's research as a stated process rule — expect the conditions in the court-approved form for that file, confirmed with a lawyer, not a standard residential contract. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: What taxes do I pay on a court-ordered purchase?** The general PTT tiers are in §3 (e.g., $1.5M→$28,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 including the over-$3M tier), but the GST treatment and any exemption position on the specific purchase are UNCONFIRMED — the GI-003 vacant-land position does not decide a court sale. Cost both with a lawyer before setting a ceiling. (§3.)
- **Q: Does buying an ALR acreage through foreclosure change its farm rules?** No — ALR status, the ALC caps (principal ≤500 m², suite inside it only, additional 90/186 m², no stratification without ALC permission) and the farm-class income tiers ride through the vesting order unchanged. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Are foreclosures discounted by a standard percentage?** No published source supports one. The September 2026 community figures (102% detached / 99% attached sale-to-list) are all-property context, not foreclosure figures — the court, not the market feed, confirms the sale. (§1.)
- **Q: How many South Surrey properties are in foreclosure right now?** UNVERIFIED — SnapStats publishes no foreclosure category and no official source opened publishes South Surrey foreclosure counts. The feed shows what is listed; it is not a count of proceedings. (§1.)
- **Q: As a seller in difficulty, what is my window?** The redemption period the court fixes under Rule 21-7 (5)(b) is the window to pay what is due before the equity of redemption is foreclosed — earlier advice is worth more than late advice. (§3.)
