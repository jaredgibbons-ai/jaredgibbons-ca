## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning + OCP frame (Township):** equestrian acreages sit under the **Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500**; the zone for a specific parcel must be confirmed with the Township. Willoughby ground additionally sits under the **Willoughby Community Plan = Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025)**; Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 reached first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY (Public Hearing directed for June 15, 2026) — never upgraded. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/community-planning.aspx
- **Coach house / suite frame (Township):** a coach house is a **fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102, NEVER strata**; the Township secondary suite cap is **120 m²**. Live conflict, flagged not resolved: s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's Jan 2025 guide says suites are not permitted in townhouses. - **R-CL s.403:** 220 m² minimum LOT area to 464.5 m² maximum LOT area. A "355 m²" figure is NOWHERE in the bylaw and is never carried in this file. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **SSMUH (Township, Bylaw 6020, adopted Nov 18, 2024; NO 6-plex tier exists in it):** eligible lots must be 4,050 m² or smaller, inside the Urban Containment Boundary and on municipal water and sewer — 3 units at ≤280 m² and 4 units at 280–4,050 m². Rural equestrian lots fail on size and servicing. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Subdivision / servicing (Township):** subdivisions require Approving Officer approval under the Land Title Act. Servicing is determined under the **Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED in the bylaw text**. Ledger discipline elsewhere, stated so no number is imported: Surrey 8830 (1986) VERIFIED; Abbotsford's number UNCONFIRMED; Chilliwack servicing UNCONFIRMED (candidate Bylaw 3055 NOT adopted, never verified). https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (ALR ground exists across this topic — carried in full, no stratification shortcut):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). Local bylaws may be more restrictive. A barn conversion or a second house beyond that frame needs ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, carried in full BECAUSE this slug's page states it wrong:** $2,500 in farm-gate sales if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications must be delivered to BC Assessment by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. The live page's flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR for this page (§1, §5) — the flat figure is never correct as a complete statement, at any size. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo89/loo89/411_95_pit
- **BC strata block (where bare-land strata exists):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). No bare-land strata stock is verified as this topic's own stock (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a SEPARATE info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — directly relevant to equestrian farm sales, and stated only at this level. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
