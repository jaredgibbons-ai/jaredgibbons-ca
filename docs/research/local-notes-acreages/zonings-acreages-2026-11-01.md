- **ALR frame — exactly as the page documents it (no more, no less):** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; ALC rules apply in addition to municipal zoning on ALR land, and local governments can add further restrictions. Strata plans in the ALR cause subdivision per ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6, and most subdivision of ALR land needs ALC approval, including parcels under 2 acres, as the page's ALR section also states. The page's Delta A1 card (farm house to 500 m²; accessory farm dwelling 90 m² / 186 m²) states this frame consistently. No stratification shorthand beyond the page's own documented position is asserted here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html

- **Q: What homes does the ALR allow on a farm parcel, per this page?** Exactly the page's documented frame: one principal residence up to 500 m² (fill ≤1,000 m²), a secondary suite only inside that principal residence, and one additional residence of up to 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha) — with municipal zoning still able to limit further. - **Q: How big can a secondary suite be?** It depends which regime governs — and the three on this page must not be mixed: Surrey caps a suite at 40% of the principal unit's habitable area; on ALR land a suite is permitted only within the principal residence; Abbotsford's A1 card states its own 100 m² figure. (§3.)
- **Q: What should I check before buying acreage, in the page's own order?** The page's six checks: ALR status; the zone and its minimum lot size (never assume subdivision — on ALR land it needs ALC approval); how many homes are allowed; septic records and inspection; water source; and farm class with BC Assessment, plus Premises ID if livestock or poultry are planned. - **Q: Can I rely on a zone card instead of checking with the municipality?** No — the page itself says summaries may not reflect recent amendments, card-level detail beyond the §1 consistency checks is UNVERIFIED in this file, and one mid-page segment could not even be re-read for this research (429, stopped). - **Q: What do PTT and GST cost on a land purchase (generic)?** Generic anchors only (this page has no purchase of its own): PTT $700,000 → $12,000 and $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier over $3M, on the tiers in §3; GST on vacant land is stated ONLY at CRA GI-003 level in §3 — anything finer is UNCONFIRMED.
