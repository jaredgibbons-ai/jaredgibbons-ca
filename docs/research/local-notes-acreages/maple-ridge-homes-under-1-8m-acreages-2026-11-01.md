## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019** — including RS-1 (Single Detached Residential) at s.605 (minimum lot area 668.0 m², minimum lot width 18.0 m, minimum lot depth 27.0 m, maximum lot coverage 40%) and the rural zone set. - **Lougheed Transit Corridor — ADOPTED, per the City record:** cited as **ADOPTED Jan 27, 2026** (Bylaw 7781-2021 third reading + adoption Jan 27, 2026; City news release Feb 4, 2026). **The corridor ENDS at 221 St — it does NOT reach Whonnock.** The contrary initial-readings-only reading sits in the `cityofmapleridge` research, 3 of 5 Maple Ridge guide research files and the whonnock-guide research; wherever met it is corrected in-file and the City record governs.
- **Suites and added units:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-family and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. Suite legality is a permit-level check on the specific lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- **Floodplain and escarpment as rules:** along the Fraser River south edge the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, no habitable floor space may sit below the flood construction level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s.402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties; the Fraser River Escarpment policy area's east boundary is 224 Street, and lots within 300 m of the escarpment crest require a geotechnical report attached to a Section 219 covenant before a building permit. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0 and http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame wherever a Maple Ridge parcel is in the reserve or farm-adjacent:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in this slug's own research — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision runs through the City against the zone minimums and the City's servicing requirements under **Servicing Bylaw 4800-1993 (stated only from a City source — carried with that sourcing)**; for an ALR parcel, ALC approval is required in addition. Never quote a servicing standard for a rural parcel from a listing or a summary.
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on acreage:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program (south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m) and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on farm and vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland is the caution: the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a SEPARATE info sheet and treats farmland sales as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — a Maple Ridge farm parcel must therefore never be assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file; the contract must state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 noted in-line in some sources — March 13 governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. On ALR land any qualifying new home must first fit the ALC caps above. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
