## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Scope note:** this page has no land and no municipality of its own. The blocks below are the program-level land frame a buyer who arrives via this page will meet on an actual parcel, carried for completeness and labelled as general BC / Surrey-frame rules — NOT asserted as rules documented in this slug's research, except the BCFSA consumer frame, which the research does document.
- **Consumer / regulator frame (documented in this slug's research):** BC real estate trading services are regulated by BCFSA; a licensed professional must give information about commissions and fees, inform consumers of duties owed to clients and non-clients, inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party, disclose at the outset whether they can represent the consumer and how to complain to BCFSA, and work for only buyer or seller (dual agency prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest circumstances). https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Testimonial-display rules:** any BCFSA, board or platform rule governing how a REALTOR® may display testimonials on his own website (attribution, consent, or whether a selection may be shown without the full set): UNVERIFIED in this file — no such policy page was opened in the research pass, and none is described from industry habit. https://jaredgibbons.ca/reviews.html
- **Surrey frame (for Surrey parcels generally):** Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026; cite 21900, NEVER 18020 as current); Zoning Bylaw No. 12000; SSMUH Jul 8, 2024 (3 units on lots 280 m² or less / 4 units on larger lots / up to 6 on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, on lots under 4,050 m² inside the urban containment boundary); suites one per unit at most 40% of principal habitable area, never a separate strata lot; Subdivision and Development (Servicing) Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 VERIFIED from the City's own pages; streamside setbacks in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 varying by stream class — NO single riparian figure; short-term rentals permitted, owner-only, licence $358/yr carried with the City's 2024 corporate report proposing $350/yr + fees disclosed as a CONFLICT — direct to the City licence page before quoting. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — for any ALR parcel a reader later considers:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan IS a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — never the shorthand "cannot be stratified," never cite Bulletin 05 for stratification. Caps: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (parcels 40 ha or less) / ≤186 m² (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if the operation is under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page only); it is NOT documented in this slug's research. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **BC strata block (bare-land strata lots included):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. For built presales, REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission period and s.18 requires deposits held in trust. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies to a qualifying home purchase); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while sales by a developer / in the course of a business and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: CRA treats farmland sales in a separate info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
