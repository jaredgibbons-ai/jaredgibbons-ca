## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: City of New Westminster Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 (CORRECTED)** — Downtown zoning includes Columbia Street historic and high-rise commercial districts; minimum lot sizes are zone-specific and a Downtown-specific minimum is UNVERIFIED. **Townhouse districts are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B ONLY — RT-A/RT-B do NOT exist** (see the FILE STATUS correction), and 836.10 m² is an RM apartment-district density threshold, NOT an RT figure. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **OCP: "Our City 2041," Bylaw No. 7925, 2017, with the Downtown Community Plan as Schedule C** — Downtown is designated a Regional City Centre, and the OCP is under a 2025–2026 update process (OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 8574, 2026). https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **Subdivision and servicing — VERIFIED:** Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw No. 7142, 2007 requires Approving Officer approval and ensures new lots are adequately serviced and meet the Zoning Bylaw's minimum lot size and frontage, through the City's pre-application / application / Works & Services Agreement process. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/7142_Subdivision.pdf
- **Transit-oriented density:** the Province's Transit Oriented Development Areas, introduced in 2024, enable density within 800 metres of the city's five SkyTrain stations — two of the five (New Westminster and Columbia) are in Downtown; what that means for a specific parcel is a City / bylaw check, not a listing-line claim. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/2026_02_17_APC___Regional_Planning_and_Transit_Oriented_Development_Area_Extensions___2026_78.pdf
- **Flood diligence — Floodplain Zone 3 (this file's own position):** the City's floodplain study places the Downtown waterfront in Zone 3, where many newer buildings have floor space above the Flood Construction Level but egress and the waterfront path sit below it; confirm the specific parcel's flood construction level compliance and insurance position lot by lot. No other area's zone is imported here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf and http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- **ALR — conditional statement only (no ALR ground documented in Downtown):** IF a parcel were in the Agricultural Land Reserve, the ALC frame would govern: subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); and farm class would run on the three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met; that line is documented on the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file). None of this is asserted to apply to any Downtown lot in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **If a strata purchase is made downtown (the redirect case, and in practice the main case):** the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 fully residential Downtown parcel purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Property taxes and utilities — as the research documents them:** City of New Westminster 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining unpaid on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. New Westminster operates its own electrical utility and City water / sewer services. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at the CRA GI-003 level:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. The GST treatment of a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and use are applied; the contract must state the position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

- **Q: I read about RT-A and RT-B townhouse districts — can I build under them?** No — RT-A/RT-B do not exist in New Westminster; that wording in this area's research file is corrected at the top of this file. The bylaw's townhouse districts are RT-2–RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B, and 836.10 m² is an RM apartment-district density threshold, not an RT figure. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Q: Is Downtown in the Fraser River floodplain?** The waterfront is — Floodplain Zone 3, where many newer buildings have floor space above the Flood Construction Level but egress and the waterfront path sit below it; confirm the specific building's compliance and insurance before subject removal. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Q: Is Downtown the same as the Quay?** No — on the board taxonomy they are separate sub-areas: Downtown is the Columbia Street core above the waterfront and the Quay is the Quayside Drive waterfront pocket; the City's OCP map, by contrast, names one area "Downtown (including Quayside)." https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/OUR_CITY_2041_Official_Community_Plan_New_Westminsters_Residential_Neighbourhoods_Map.pdf
- **Q: What does subdividing or re-servicing a Downtown parcel require?** Approving Officer approval under the Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw No. 7142, 2007, with new lots adequately serviced and meeting the Zoning Bylaw's minimum lot size and frontage, through the City's pre-application / application / Works & Services Agreement process. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/7142_Subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Does SkyTrain proximity give my parcel automatic extra density?** No — provincial Transit Oriented Development Areas enable density within 800 m of the stations (two are in Downtown), but what that means for a specific parcel is a City / bylaw check, not an entitlement to quote. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/2026_02_17_APC___Regional_Planning_and_Transit_Oriented_Development_Area_Extensions___2026_78.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Downtown address?** On the settled single-address basis only: for the sample address 85 Merivale Street, the SD40 School Locator run recorded in the master research (2026-10-21) returned École Qayqayt Elementary (K–5), Fraser River Middle (6–8) and New Westminster Secondary (9–12). - **Q: Do I pay GST on a Downtown parcel purchase?** Only as the CRA GI-003 analysis lands for that vendor and parcel — most individual personal-use vacant-land sales are exempt, business or more-than-two-part subdivided sales can be taxable; UNCONFIRMED per parcel, and the contract must state who bears GST. PTT is separate and applies on the tiers in Section 3. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax apply to a vacant Downtown property?** No — that 3% tax is Vancouver only; Downtown is in New Westminster. (Section 3.)
