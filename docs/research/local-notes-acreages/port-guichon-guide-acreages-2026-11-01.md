- **Flood covenants on title.** In Ladner approvals on Chisholm Street, the City required Section 219 flood-proofing covenants and statutory rights-of-way so the dike can be raised to 5.5 metres — the pattern a buyer on river-side and west-edge parcels here should search title for, on an acreage purchase exactly as on a house purchase. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/104061/
- **Streamside rules.** All properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — a live constraint on farm buildings, suites, coach houses or any ground-oriented unit on a Port Guichon parcel. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — rates and worked anchors (inputs + tier math stated):** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked examples: $500,000 → $8,000; $700,000 → $12,000; $800,000 → $14,000 (net $6,000 after the maximum $8,000 First-Time Home Buyers' relief, where it applies); $900,000 → $16,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 **including** the further 2% tier on the $500,000 over $3,000,000. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief on a new principal-residence HOME up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a home exemption, not a bare-land exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — stated only at the CRA GI-003 level:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use), while sales can be taxable where the land was capital property used primarily in a business, sold in the course of a business, or created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts. Sales by developers/businesses and newly subdivided lots are therefore generally TAXABLE and an individual's personal-use sale generally exempt, at that level only. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED — get tax advice. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / HBP / FHSA — land angle stated precisely:** the First-Time Home Buyer GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not to bare land itself. The older New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 per buyer. First Home Savings Account: $8,000 per year, $40,000 lifetime. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Port Guichon in Tsawwassen?** No — it is in Ladner, in west Ladner around River Road West and 46A Street; Ladner and Tsawwassen are separate Delta communities that together make up South Delta. Any page or listing framing it as Tsawwassen is carrying the known geography error. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: What acreage can I actually buy here?** Individual resale farm parcels under A1 zoning at the west edge — no named acreage subdivision, no bare-land strata acreage community, and no presale acreage offering is verified. The RS house lots in the east are not acreage. (§2 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a west-edge farm parcel into house lots?** No at residential scale: A1 sets an 8-hectare minimum for parcels created by subdivision, and if the parcel is in the ALR, subdivision additionally requires ALC approval and an ALR residence cannot be stratified at all. Confirm ALR status on the ALC maps before pricing any split. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Does A1 zoning mean the parcel is in the ALR?** No — they are separate checks. A1 is the City's zone; ALR status is provincial and parcel-specific, and it is UNVERIFIED for any specific Port Guichon parcel in the research. Both checks have to be run; neither answer substitutes for the other. (§§2–3 above)
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to a Port Guichon parcel instead of subdividing?** Possibly, on the RS ground: a secondary suite is an accessory use in the RS1 zone, and Delta's SSMUH amendments (Bylaws 8394/8395, June 24, 2024) add duplex, coach house and garden suite options where the zoning applies — but on ALR-confirmed ground the ALC residence caps in §3 govern, and any parcel within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch is also in the Streamside DPA. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is the parcel behind a dike, and can a flood covenant sit on title?** Yes to the dike system — low Fraser River-side land protected by Delta's flood protection system — and covenants are a real Ladner pattern: the City has required Section 219 flood-proofing covenants and rights-of-way for future dike raising to 5.5 m in Ladner approvals, so search title and check DeltaMap before subjects come off. https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/104061/
- **Q: Is the parcel serviced, or will I need a well and septic?** UNCONFIRMED per parcel in the research — the file's own questions for Jared flag wells/septic/drainage as a live issue on older west-Ladner properties. Confirm City connections; for an unserviced parcel check GWELLS and the Fraser Health onsite-sewerage requirements (a system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed/installed by an Authorized Person). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: Can I get farm-class taxes on a west-edge parcel?** Only if the parcel is farm land meeting the full three-tier test in §3 ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha) with an application by October 31 — acreage alone, and A1 zoning alone, do not qualify a parcel. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Q: Will I pay GST on a vacant farm parcel here?** Depends on the seller and history: CRA GI-003 states most individual personal-use sales are exempt, but business-use, in-the-course-of-business and more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable; a developer's newly subdivided lot is generally taxable. Farm-business use is exactly the kind of fact that can move a parcel out of the personal-use exemption — get tax advice on the specific parcel. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What PTT would a $2,000,000 acreage purchase carry?** On the general tiers in §3: $38,000 (1% on the first $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% on the next $1,800,000 = $36,000), before any exemption that actually applies — and the home exemptions do not apply to bare land alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Which schools and buses serve a parcel here?** SD37 catchments are address-based; the sample address 4381 46A St maps to Port Guichon Elementary and Delta Secondary. Route 618 (Ladner South loop) calls at 46A Street at River Road, and Ladner Exchange is the hub for the 601 to Bridgeport Station. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/ and http://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/exchange_maps/ladner.pdf
