## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Pad-tenure law (the governing frame for every home in §2):** written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreements are required in a park, and the MHPTA covers most manufactured home tenancies. Pad-rent increases: only with 3 full months' written notice, only once every 12 months, and only up to the yearly limit — **2026: 2.3% plus a proportional amount; 2027: 2.2% plus a proportional amount.** The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park. The landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion of a prior year's limit. These percentages are the ONLY pad-rent figures an official source gives — NO pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale (MHPTA s.28):** assignment permanently transfers the pad tenancy to the buyer; a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an order of the director — s.28(1)(b) wording is "an order of the director" (NOT arbitrator) — or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it. The landlord cannot charge a fee for consent, and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s.42):** where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to another use, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Registration and certification:** in BC the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered with the Manufactured Home Registry, through a lawyer or notary filing the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership; a home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular), and a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Taxes on a home in a park:** a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **TOWNSHIP frame (the directory's ground):** Zoning Bylaw 2500; SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024; 3 units at ≤280 m² / 4 units at 280–4,050 m² eligible lots; NO 6-plex tier); suite cap 120 m² (s.102); coach house = fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata — live s.102 conflict flagged in the siblings, not resolved; R-CL s.403 = 220 m² min / 464.5 m² max LOT area ("355 m²" is NOWHERE in the bylaw); Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED. (FILE STATUS; lots-and-land sibling §3.)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried because the Township is farm-adjacent (no park in §2 is counted as ALR acreage):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: one principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence; one additional residence up to 90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if less than 0.8 ha — applications by October 31; a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page only; it is NOT documented in this slug's research. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies to a qualifying home purchase); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. How PTT applies to a specific manufactured-home-on-pad purchase is a per-transaction check — no park-specific PTT position is stated here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level (carried for completeness; this page sells no land):** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; a developer's/business sale and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: CRA treats farmland sales in a separate info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does buying a home in one of these parks buy me land or acreage?** No. In a park you own (at most) the manufactured home and rent the pad under the MHPTA; at Pioneer Park you own the home plus co-op shares. Neither is a lot or acreage purchase, and this file counts none of it as land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Q: How many mobile home parks are there in Langley?** No official count is stated in this file: no official Langley park registry exists, and this page's six-park list is site copy sourced from MLS® listings — the live listing pages show additional parks with homes for sale that this page does not name, so six is not a complete count. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-mobile-home-communities.html https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-mobile-homes.html
- **Q: What are the age and pet rules in Langley parks?** Park by park, and they can change — this page lists its four Brookswood-Fernridge parks as 55+ and its Aldergrove and Salmon River parks as all-ages, with pet rules ranging from "not allowed" to "allowed with restrictions," all sourced by the page from recent listings. Confirm the current rules and pad fee with the park manager before writing an offer, exactly as the page itself advises. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-mobile-home-communities.html
- **Q: How much is pad rent in these parks?** This page states no pad rent for any park, and no official source publishes a Langley pad-rent figure, average or range — NO pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact in this file. The figure that matters is the specific park's current rent in its written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement. (§§3, 5.)
- **Q: How much can a park raise pad rent each year?** Up to the Residential Tenancy Branch limit for manufactured home site tenancies: 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027, on 3 full months' notice, once every 12 months. The proportional amount is park-specific — ask the specific park for its last calculation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park raise my pad rent when I buy the home?** No. On assignment under MHPTA s.28 the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same and the scheduled date of a subsequent increase does not change; assignment requires the landlord's prior written consent (or an order of the director) and the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent. (§3.)
- **Q: What happens if the park is converted to another use?** Under MHPTA s.42: the landlord must first have all necessary permits and approvals, then give 12 months' notice and pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Are there market statistics for Langley mobile home parks — or for Langley acreage?** Neither. SnapStats has no manufactured-home category and no acreage category. The only SnapStats figures for Langley are all-residential community context, as of September 2026: Detached median $1,345,000 (449 active, 64 sold) and Condos & Townhomes median $611,000 (699 active, 128 sold) — neither describes park homes or acreage. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.)
- **Q: What must be in place before a manufactured home sale is effective?** Registration with the Manufactured Home Registry (lawyer or notary filing the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership), and the home's original legible CSA decal — without it, Technical Safety BC re-certification is required before the home can be sold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Which schools serve a specific park address?** The Ministry-listed schools named in §1 are physical locations, not catchments. Catchments are address-based in SD35 and UNVERIFIED for any specific park address in this file — run the exact address in the SD35 School Locator. (Research file §4.)
