## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, in effect July 1, 2026** — adopted June 9, 2026, it replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) outright; the 1965 bylaw is NOT current and must never be cited for this area. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP: Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025** — it replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; Lougheed is one of Burnaby's town centres, and the town-centre edge is where plan expectations change fastest, so review the 2050 land use map before assuming density beyond the R1 district. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **SSMUH in Burnaby, as the City states it:** Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing District in June 2024 — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and minimum lot area of 281 m², and permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites. Distance to the Lougheed corridor stops is therefore a priced fact, not a slogan. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision and servicing — base number UNCONFIRMED, process documented:** subdivision parcel area, shape and dimensions must conform to the zoning bylaw for the parcel's zone, under the Burnaby Subdivision Control and Development Servicing Bylaw (renamed from the Burnaby Subdivision Control Bylaw 1971 by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024; the City's servicing updates took effect September 30, 2024). Required works and services include highways and lanes, water distribution connected to the City system, sewage collection and drainage collection, built to the City's Engineering Design Criteria Manual standards; the City's Servicing Improvements Guide addresses servicing for developments with four or more primary dwelling units on a residential lot. **The base bylaw's own number is held UNCONFIRMED in this file — never filled in.** https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=77052 and https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-08/Servicing-Improvements-Guide.pdf
- **Flood / hazard diligence:** no floodplain or hazard designation for this sub-area was confirmed in the sources read; parcel-level hazard status is a property-specific check: UNVERIFIED for individual lots. No other area's floodplain zone is imported here. (Research file §4.)
- **ALR — conditional statement only (no ALR ground documented in Oakdale):** IF a parcel were in the Agricultural Land Reserve, the ALC frame would govern: subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); and farm class would run on the three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met; that line is documented on the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file). None of this is asserted to apply to any Oakdale lot in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **If a strata purchase is the redirect case (an R1 stratified multiplex lot, or product outside the sub-area):** the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. None of it applies to a fee-simple lot purchase itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 fully residential Oakdale lot purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
