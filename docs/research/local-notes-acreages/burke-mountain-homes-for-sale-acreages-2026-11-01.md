- **ALR RULES IN FULL — conditional here (no ALR parcel is confirmed on Burke Mountain), carried because the area check is parcel-by-parcel and farm-adjacent buyers ask:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a PERMISSION rule, never an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; local governments may regulate further but not be more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — new applications by the legislated deadline of October 31. - **Property taxes (Coquitlam):** City of Coquitlam property tax payment was due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any unpaid balance of current taxes after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty applies after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land — worked examples with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example at $800,000: $200,000 × 1% = $2,000, plus $600,000 × 2% = $12,000, total $14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies to a qualifying home purchase). Worked example at $3.5M fully residential: $2,000 + $1,800,000 × 2% = $36,000 (to $2M) = $38,000, plus $1,500,000 × 3% = $45,000 (to $3.5M), plus $500,000 × 2% = $10,000 (residential portion over $3M), total **$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — follow $93,000**; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed (open summary-pass item). Full anchor set: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000; $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland caution per CRA: farmland sales are addressed in a separate info sheet and treated as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — no parcel is assumed GST-exempt on the vacant-land rule alone.
