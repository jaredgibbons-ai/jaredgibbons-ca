West-of-slough village lots are exactly where this check bites. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf

- **ALR rules in full (village-surrounding farm ground; in-sub-area ALR UNVERIFIED, §2).** ALR residences cannot be stratified — strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)) — and subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, as the ALC states it. ALC residence caps: principal residence 500 m² or less total floor area; a secondary suite only within the principal residence; one additional residence of 90 m² or less (parcels 40 ha or less) or 186 m² or less (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form, never a flat figure.** Gross annual farm income required: $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on an operation under 0.8 ha. Applications are due by October 31 for the next taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an error; no such line is asserted in this file, and the flat figure is owned only by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page in this program — it is not documented in this file's research. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Property transfer tax on land — tiers and worked anchors (inputs + tier math).** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 (at the first-time-buyer cap the $8,000 saving on the first $500K leaves $6,000 payable); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M purely residential → **$93,000** ($2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 at the 3% tier + $10,000 further 2% on the $500K over $3M). **OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met:** townhomes-program siblings elsewhere carry $78,000 for the $3.5M anchor; this file follows $93,000 and flags the conflict. First-time buyers' exemption: no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, phasing to $1,150,000 — the exemption that matters for a new village home; its application to a bare-land purchase is facts-specific: UNCONFIRMED for bare land in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand
- **GST on vacant land vs GST on new homes — the distinction this sub-area turns on.** CRA's position, stated only at the GI-003 level its publication supports: most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (for example, land kept for personal use), but a sale is taxable where the land was subdivided into more than two parts, and sales by developers/businesses are generally taxable supplies. (A live page's $1.25M straight-line phase-out row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism wherever met; none is documented in this file's research.) - **Q: Is a village lot on municipal water and sewer, or well and septic?** The village core is within the City's water, sanitary sewer and drainage systems; no source in this program documents well/septic inside this sub-area. - **Q: Does the heritage/village-core status limit what I can build?** The Ladner Village Core development permit area in the OCP addresses the Village's historic buildings and can shape exterior renovations and redevelopment in the core — it binds a fee-simple owner just as it binds a strata. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf
- **Q: Does GST apply when I buy here?** Two different answers for two different purchases: a resale lot from an individual is generally GST-exempt per CRA GI-003 (personal-use land), while a new 48 Landing home carries GST as new housing — partly recoverable for eligible first-time buyers under the Bill C-4 rebate, which applies to the housing, not to bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What PTT would I pay on a $900K village lot?** $16,000 on the program's worked anchors (1% on the first $200K = $2,000; 2% on the next $700K = $14,000), before any exemption — and the home exemptions' application to bare land is UNCONFIRMED, so do not net them off without advice. (§3 above)

Parcel-specific figures: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Q: What about the slough and flooding?** Any property within 30 m of a stream, including Chilukthan Slough and drainage ditches, is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement DPA, and the whole village sits behind the City's dike and drainage system — confirm flood construction requirements for the lot with the City before designing a build. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
