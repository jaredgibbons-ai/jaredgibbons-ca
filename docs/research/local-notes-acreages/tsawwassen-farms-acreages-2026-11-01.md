## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024)** — consolidated into a single Land Use Plan with NO lettered schedules. Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)": s. 1.5.1 sets the designation's purpose as maintaining agricultural lands and promoting a thriving agricultural sector, designations are applied on Map 2 (Land Use), and s. 2.6.1 recognizes farming as the primary use of agricultural land. The Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the PREVIOUS OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; any "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw No. 8400" attribution is the standing conflation error, swept wherever met. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Zoning number CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted:** the research file applies Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, including its A1 Agriculture zone; the standing position is that the number is contested (7600, 2017 vs an internal "8400, 2025" title), so this file uses the zone content without asserting a year. In the A1 zone the bylaw provides one farm house per lot, a farm house maximum of 500 m², a farm home plate maximum of 3,600 m², one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit of maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on lots over 40 ha. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — they govern every ALR parcel on this fringe:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; one additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and Delta bylaws must also permit it; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Subdivision of farm land (Delta zones):** in the A1 zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is permitted only where it complies with the Official Community Plan Bylaw and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under Agricultural Land Commission policy; A2 (s. 10.20.6) also sets an 8 ha minimum, and A3 (s. 10.30.6) does not permit subdivision. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income requirements at farm gate prices — $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR asserted ONLY in the horse-properties file; it is never attributed to this file's research and is not asserted here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024** — Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing amendments govern residential lots; they do not create farm lots, do not subdivide ALR land, and do not apply on Tsawwassen Lands. Added SSMUH units are not subdivision. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing standards — DSSS 8288 held UNCONFIRMED:** adoption of the Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288 (2024) is held UNCONFIRMED in the standing ledger. The tsawwassen-east-guide land home asserts it in governing terms; that assertion is noted here as a conflict, never asserted, and the subdivision process (City Approving Officer, zone minimums, ALC approval in the ALR) is documented instead of the number. (Pattern file: ~/workspace/local-notes/acreages/tsawwassen-north-acreages-2026-11-01.md, §3.)
- **Water:** the provincial GWELLS application is the registry for recorded wells and aquifers; well reports must be submitted within 90 days after a well is constructed, altered or decommissioned, and older wells may not appear in the registry; under the Water Sustainability Act, domestic groundwater use does not need a licence, and non-domestic use, including irrigation, requires a water licence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Septic:** Fraser Health states a home not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite sewage disposal system that complies with the Sewerage System Regulation; only an Authorized Person can design, install, repair, maintain or inspect a sewerage system, and an Authorized Person must file a record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair and a letter of certification after construction. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Flood, dykes and foreshore:** the City of Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure; the City is raising sections of the Boundary Bay dike between 72 Street and 88 Street with provincial grant funding. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD3:** on the Boundary Bay foreshore and the Southlands, OCP hazard DPA SD3 provides a 2.9 m Geodetic Survey of Canada datum floor level, a 2.3 m Geodetic Survey of Canada datum minimum land surface elevation and a 7.5 m tidal setback — directly relevant to Southlands-side farm parcels. Parcel-level flood elevation / flood construction level for any specific farm parcel: UNCONFIRMED (the City states those values are property-specific). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Streamside rules:** all properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — farm ditches count, so the setback check applies on working farm land as well as urban lots. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** (tier math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 at 3% on $1.5M + $10,000 further 2% on $500K) — the $78,000 figure lives in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling; OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant and farm land — ONLY at the CRA GI-003 level:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a separate sheet as usually taxable, so a farm parcel is never assumed GST-exempt on the vacant-land rule alone; anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED for a specific parcel until vendor status and farm use are applied. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there real farmland in Tsawwassen, or is it all residential?** Both. Delta's Official Community Plan designates farm land on the Tsawwassen edges Agriculture (A) (Bylaw No. 8400, 2024, s. 1.5.1), and Agricultural Land Reserve rules apply where land is in the ALR; the urban neighbourhoods are residential, and the farm topic is that agricultural fringe plus the shared Delta farm belt toward Ladner. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land near Tsawwassen?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; Delta's A1 zone sets the same 500 m² farm house maximum and a 3,600 m² farm home plate maximum. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a Tsawwassen-area farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and Delta bylaws must also permit it; the A1 zone allows one accessory farm dwelling unit within those same size limits, and a secondary suite is permitted ONLY inside the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Delta farm parcel — or stratify the house and sell it separately?** Subdivide: only within tight limits — in the A1 zone a new lot created by subdivision needs a minimum of 8 ha, OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy; A3 zoning does not permit subdivision. Stratify an ALR residence: no — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: What income does a farm need to keep farm classification?** All three tiers: BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income (farm gate) thresholds are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" answer is an error. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: How do I know if a specific parcel is in the ALR?** Check the parcel on the ALC maps by PID or civic address — reserve status controls residences (the 500 m² principal cap), the one-additional-residence size limits (90 m² / 186 m²), and most use permissions, so confirm ALR status first and price second. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can I irrigate — what water diligence applies?** Water is a diligence item, not an assumption: search GWELLS for the well record (older wells may not appear), and if the operation irrigates, confirm the water licence position — non-domestic groundwater use, including irrigation, requires a licence under the Water Sustainability Act, while domestic groundwater use does not need a licence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights/water-licences-approvals/new-requirements-for-groundwater-users
- **Q: What do the dike and ditch rules mean for a Southlands-side parcel?** Southlands-side parcels can sit in hazard DPA SD3 (2.9 m floor level, 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation, 7.5 m tidal setback), and any lot within 30 m of a ditch or slough is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement DPA — farm ditches count. Confirm both with the City, not from a listing's wording. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: Does City of Delta zoning apply to the farm-looking land on the west side, on Tsawwassen Lands?** No. Tsawwassen First Nation is self-governing with jurisdiction over land management on Tsawwassen Lands (over 724 hectares under the Final Agreement); TFN land follows TFN law, not City bylaws — establish which side of the treaty-land boundary a parcel sits on before pricing anything. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **Q: Is there a market statistic for Tsawwassen farms — price per acre, days on market?** No figure is stated in this file, by design: SnapStats publishes no farm category and does not cover South Delta at all, and no official source publishes Tsawwassen farm sale counts, price per acre or farm days on market — all UNVERIFIED.
