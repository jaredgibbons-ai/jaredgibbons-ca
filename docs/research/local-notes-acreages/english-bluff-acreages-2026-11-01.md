## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: RS zones under Delta's zoning bylaw — number CONTESTED, flagged never asserted.** This slug's research cites "Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600" RS zones and the RS3 sheet (4,000 m² minimum subdivision lot area on this shore); the program's standing position is that the number is contested (No. 7600, 2017 vs an internal title "8400, 2025"). Zoning is assigned parcel by parcel on the bylaw's zoning maps — read the parcel's zone off the map, not off a listing. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf
- **OCP: consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), NO lettered schedules.** Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" in the current plan; the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the superseded Bylaw No. 3950 — any "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw No. 8400" citation is the known conflation error, flagged wherever met. - **SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment) — the defining rule on this shore:** Development Permit Area SD2, Category "b" (protection of development from hazardous conditions), covers this shore; the OCP states the waterfront area is subject to mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence, and the ocean-frontage slopes require protection of existing vegetation and tree cover. Building rules: no building with the underside of a wooden floor system, or the top of a concrete slab for habitation/business/storage of damageable goods, lower than the Flood Construction Level (unless a lesser elevation is set by the provincial ministry); no building within 7.5 m of the natural boundary of the sea or within 7.5 m of the inboard toe of any dike; a restrictive covenant acknowledging the land may be subject to flooding is required, and development affected by hazardous conditions is subject to conditions set by a suitably qualified Professional Engineer. Site-specific Flood Construction Level for an individual lot: UNVERIFIED. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 + 8395, adopted June 24, 2024.** Delta's rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot — on this shore the SD2 hazard conditions sit on top of that permission and are the deciding layer. SSMUH creates no acreage lots and no strata acreage here. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer against the zone minimums (RS3: 4,000 m² on this shore). HELD UNCONFIRMED: adoption of Delta's Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288 (2024) — process documented instead of a number; never quote a servicing standard for an English Bluff parcel from a listing or a summary.
- **ALR RULES — carried because Delta is farm-adjacent, applied only where a parcel proves to be ALR (none is documented in English Bluff):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. - **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there acreage for sale in English Bluff?** No — not as a product. No named acreage subdivision, hobby-acreage pocket or working-farm parcel is verified in the sub-area. What trades is fee-simple estate lots (RS3 minimum subdivision lot area 4,000 m² on this shore) with houses on them. A large lot is not an acreage parcel. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Can I build or rebuild right on the bluff edge?** Only inside SD2: a suitably qualified Professional Engineer's conditions, the Flood Construction Level, 7.5 m setbacks from the natural boundary of the sea and from any dike toe, and a registered restrictive covenant acknowledging flood risk. Budget for the geotechnical file before pricing the lot. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Do I own the beach below my lot?** No. The foreshore below the natural boundary is Crown land administered through FrontCounter BC, and foreshore use is interjurisdictional — do not market private beach ownership, stairs or docks without checking tenure. https://www.delta.ca:443/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
- **Q: Can I clear trees to open up the view?** Carefully, if at all — the SD2 justification requires protection of existing vegetation and tree cover on the ocean-frontage slopes; removal can need approvals and can affect the slope itself. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Can a lot add a suite or coach house?** Delta's SSMUH rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, but on this shore the SD2 hazard conditions sit on top of that permission and are the deciding layer for any specific lot. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Is any of this ALR land?** UNVERIFIED for the sub-area — no ALR parcel is documented in English Bluff's research. Check the specific parcel on the ALC maps; if a parcel proved to be ALR, subdivision would need ALC approval, the residence could not be stratified, and the ALC housing caps in §3 would govern. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Which schools serve an English Bluff address?** The settled SD37 locator chain for the sample address 402 English Bluff Road is English Bluff Elementary (K–7) → South Delta Secondary (8–12), stated on a single-address basis; SD37 has no middle-school tier. Any other address needs its own locator run. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a vacant English Bluff lot?** Do not assume either way. At CRA GI-003 level, most individual personal-use vacant-land sales are exempt, but a sale can be taxable where the land is business capital property, sold in the course of a business, or a parcel subdivided into more than two parts. UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel until vendor status and use are applied; the contract must state who bears GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What will it cost to hold the property?** Delta property taxes on the 2026 cycle (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after; further 5% after September 2, 2026), plus the SD2 covenant obligations on title and any engineering/monitoring the hazard conditions impose. There is no strata fee: the purchase is fee-simple. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Q: Will insurers and lenders accept a bluff property?** Ask early — the OCP itself documents mud flow, debris flow, erosion and land-slip hazards for this shore, so the designation will surface in any serious search; confirm insurability and lender comfort before subjects come off. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
