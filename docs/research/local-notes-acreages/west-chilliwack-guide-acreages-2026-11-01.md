## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, §6 farm zones.** The AL (Agricultural Lowland) zone sets a minimum lot area of 7.5 ha for land excluded/exempted from the ALR or approved for subdivision within the ALR, 1 ha for a boundary adjustment, and 0.2 ha for a homesite severance or boundary adjustment approved by the ALC; minimum lot width 25 m. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **OCP: Chilliwack 2050, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 is CURRENT.** The Agricultural Area Plan is Schedule B1 and covers this sub-area; Sardis is Schedule B5; Eastern Hillsides is Schedule B4; Chilliwack Proper is Schedule B-2; Downtown is Schedule B3 + D-6. STALE-CITATION FLAG: live pages stating "Schedule D / Bylaw 5024" or "Schedule C" for this ground are stale — flagged as Jerry sweep items wherever the research/siblings document them; the correct citations are Bylaw 5500 / B1 / B5 / B4 / B-2 / B3 as applicable. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf
- **SSMUH: Chilliwack Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024,** brought provincial small-scale multi-unit housing into Chilliwack zoning. ALR land in this sub-area is governed by the ALR rules below, not by SSMUH density — SSMUH does not create subdivision or multi-unit rights on AL-zone farm parcels. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** no verified Chilliwack servicing bylaw number is asserted in this file. The candidate "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" is **NOT adopted** and is never presented as verified; subdivision here is documented through the Approving Officer / Preliminary Layout Approval (PLA) process instead. Confirm subdivision servicing, frontage and approvals with the City (and the ALC, where ALR applies) for the specific parcel. (Program verified ledger; ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/west-chilliwack-guide-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **ALR rules in full:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239 deposit = subdivision; ALCA s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, and the AL zone's minimums (7.5 ha, or 0.2 ha for an approved homesite severance) decide what is even possible. ALC residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Check the parcel on ALC mapping before pricing any split or second home. No marketing-board source was opened in this research, so quota/board items stay UNVERIFIED site copy. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC Assessment farm class — full three-tier form:** $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 (under 0.8 ha); applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR (asserted only where a file's own research documents it — the horse-properties page owns it; not this file). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Floodplain diligence — a deciding rule on this ground:** Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets minimum building setbacks of 60 m from the Fraser River, 75 m from the Vedder River and 30 m from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal; the City maintains a diking network for the Fraser and Vedder systems, and levies additional taxes for drainage and dyking on top of municipal rates — read the full tax notice, not just the municipal line. Measure setbacks on any parcel near a river or canal before valuing its buildable area. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **Property taxes (City ground):** due the first working day after July 1; for 2026, a 5% penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% on taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. Rates are the sum of each taxing authority's levy, with separate rates per property class plus drainage/dyking levies. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **Property transfer tax (PTT), with worked examples (inputs stated, tier math shown):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — on a farm-classed parcel the further 2% applies only to the residential portion, so model the actual class mix before offering. No exemption: $500,000 → **$8,000**; $700,000 → **$12,000**; $900,000 → **$16,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 fully residential → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 (3% × $1,500,000) + $10,000 (further 2% × $500,000) = **$93,000**. First-Time Home Buyers': no PTT on the first $500,000 (max $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 (at the $800,000 example: $14,000 gross, $6,000 after the $8,000 relief). Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED. **OPEN reconciliation item:** a townhomes sibling (tsawwassen-homes-over-1-8m) carries $78,000 for the $3,500,000 input; this file follows **$93,000** on the tier math and flags the conflict. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** most individual personal-use sales exempt; business-use, in-the-course-of-business, and more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally taxable (CRA GI-003). The CRA frame also notes farmland sales are usually taxable with limited exempt circumstances. Working-farm sales need parcel-specific tax advice — finer points UNCONFIRMED here, including any farm-specific treatment beyond GI-003. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. A live page's $1.25M straight-line phase-out row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP $60,000 per buyer; FHSA $8,000/year, $40,000 lifetime. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no West Chilliwack–specific position is documented in the research — none is imported from any other city, and the federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for a specific buyer is UNCONFIRMED (no primary text opened in this file's research). Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does NOT apply here.
- **Bare-land strata / strata block (none verified here — form guidance only):** Form B first (s. 59 SPA — fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, parking/storage, insurance summary; within 7 days of request, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What kind of acreage actually comes up for sale here?** Individual fee-simple farm and rural parcels with a homesite — land in a working agricultural landscape (the plan area's sector is dairy and poultry operations, nurseries and greenhouses, in the City plan's generic terms). There is no acreage subdivision with lots to pick from. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf
- **Q: Can I subdivide a West Chilliwack farm?** Only within the AL rules: 7.5 ha minimum for ALC-approved subdivision within the ALR (or ALR-excluded/exempted land), 1 ha for a boundary adjustment, 0.2 ha for an ALC-approved homesite severance — and ALC approval is required inside the ALR. Most farm parcels cannot be split at urban scale. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: How do I know if the parcel is in the ALR?** Per address, on ALC mapping plus the City plan — status was not confirmed for any parcel in the research, so assume nothing until checked. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: How much of the parcel can I actually build on?** Flood setbacks can remove large strips: 60 m from the Fraser River, 75 m from the Vedder River, 30 m from the Chilliwack River/Vedder Canal. Measure the setback envelope before valuing the homesite. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- **Q: Is the parcel serviced?** UNVERIFIED per parcel — verify well/septic (or any municipal connection) for the specific parcel before removing subjects; this is working farm land, not a serviced subdivision. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Can I build a second home or suite for family/workers?** On ALR ground, only within ALC caps (principal ≤500 m²; suite within principal only; additional residence 90 m² ≤40 ha / 186 m² >40 ha) plus City zoning — and ALR residences cannot be stratified or sold separately. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Can the land keep farm-class taxes?** Only if production meets the three-tier test ($2,500 / $2,500 + 5% / $10,000 by size) with an application by October 31. A non-farming acreage buyer should budget without farm class until BC Assessment confirms it. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Q: A listing says the farm comes with quota — is that verified?** No. No marketing-board source was opened in this research, so any quota or board item in listing copy is UNVERIFIED — verify it with the board and the seller's records before pricing it. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Will GST apply to a farm-land purchase?** CRA's vacant-land guidance notes farmland sales are usually taxable with limited exempt circumstances, and developer/subdivided sales are generally taxable while individual personal-use sales are generally exempt — get parcel-specific tax advice before closing; finer points are UNCONFIRMED here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What extra costs come with Chilliwack farm land?** Drainage and dyking levies on top of municipal rates, plus the July/August penalty calendar (5% after July 2, 2026; +5% after August 4, 2026) if closing straddles tax season. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **Q: Which schools serve an Evans Road parcel?** Only the 7600 Evans Road chain is documented (Evans Elementary → Vedder Middle → Sardis Secondary). Run your exact address in the SD33 locator. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
