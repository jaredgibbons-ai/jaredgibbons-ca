- **Q: Is there acreage to buy in Dunbar?** Not as a product. - **Q: Can an ALR lot in Southlands be stratified or subdivided freely?** No — subdivision in the ALR requires ALC approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Residence caps are principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). - **Q: Can a Dunbar house lot hold more than one home?** Yes, where the R1-1 schedule is met — a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units, with minimum site areas from 306 m² to 557 m² by form. Southlands lots sit under different districts. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Can I split a Dunbar lot into two fee-simple lots?** Only if both resulting parcels clear R1-1 and the Subdivision By-law No. 5208 Schedule A category for the block, through the Approving Officer — and, for a Southlands parcel in the ALR, only with ALC approval as well. The Dunbar Street buildings are on the retail frontages, not the river edge — confirm the specific parcel in the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
