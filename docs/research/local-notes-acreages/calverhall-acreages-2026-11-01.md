## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: City of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw No. 6700 (1995), Ground Oriented (GO) Zone** — a lot of 280 m² or less allows up to 3 units; a lot over 280 m² allows up to 4 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 designation and up to 6 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations. GO Zone subdivision minimum lot width: 7.5 m in Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 (10.0 m corner) and 15.0 m in other designations. The City adopted the GO Zone on December 8, 2025, increasing permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 residential properties City-wide and allowing up to 6 units per lot on approximately 600 properties. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf and https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/HTO-Year-2-Progress-Report-July-1,-2025-June-30,-2026.pdf

- **Suites and coach houses, as the City states them:** the City legalized secondary suites in all single-family dwellings in 1993; one suite is permitted per single-family property, the owner must reside at the property, and a suite may be combined with an accessory coach house, which is permitted where the front lot line is at least 10 m and requires a Development Permit and a Building Permit. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Permits-Inspections/building-permits/secondary-suites
