- **Suites / SSMUH:** one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit, 33 to 90 sq m, inside the same building, where the zone permits it; garden suites are one per lot at 33 to 90 sq m in the zones listed in the City garden suite guide, including RS, and a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite; SSMUH unit counts of 3, 4 or 6 turn on the 280 sq m lot-size threshold and the 400 m frequent-transit test; SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986 was adopted June 25, 2024. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf

Residence caps: a principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence; one additional residence up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. Citywide context only: the City's 2020 Business Plan states 78% of Pitt Meadows land falls within the ALR — ALR parcels inside the urban Bonson Road area were not confirmed, and ALR status is a per-address check on ALC maps: UNVERIFIED at parcel level. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
