- **Q: Can I subdivide a farm parcel?** Only at the bylaw minimums (8 ha in A-1, 16 ha in A-2) through the City's approval process — and, on ALR ground, subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval on top of the City process. Pitt Meadows' servicing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED, so no servicing standard can be quoted from a number. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How many homes can a farm parcel hold?** Under A-1: one farm house per lot (maximum 500 sq m), plus one additional farm house for operations over 8 ha and under 40 ha (two if 40 ha or more). On ALR ground the ALC caps apply as well: a principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); ALR residences cannot be stratified. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 - Consolidated Version_0.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I get farm class on a West Meadows parcel?** Only on the BC Assessment farm-class test as it applies to that parcel's actual farm use and income — the three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by October 31). Acreage alone does not earn farm class, and the tax bill differs sharply by class (Farm general $48.6824 per $1,000 vs residential $2.8438 per $1,000 in Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 — assessed-value bases differ by class, so confirm the parcel's class and assessed value before quoting carrying costs). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- **Q: Is a farm parcel serviced, on a well, or on septic?** UNVERIFIED per parcel in the research — check wells in the provincial GWELLS application and confirm any onsite sewerage system complies with the Sewerage System Regulation before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Q: Is West Meadows in a floodplain?** The City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of Pitt Meadows lies within the Fraser/Pitt floodplain, protected by 60 km of dikes, plus ditches, pump stations and flood boxes — while the live guide pages quote "over 85%" from the City Financial Plan 2026–2030: a LIVE CONFLICT between two City sources, flagged in Section 3, with these notes retired to 95%. A-1 homes must also sit 15.0 m from watercourses and ditches. Verify the specific parcel with the City. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Q: Can I run a farm stay or short-term rental on a farm parcel?** No — short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Pitt Meadows' Zoning Bylaw, and the city is in the Province's principal-residence-requirement community list. Do not price any parcel on STR income. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
