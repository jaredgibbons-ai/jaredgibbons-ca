## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP and zoning (District only):** District of North Vancouver OCP Bylaw 7900 directs 75–90% of residential growth into four centres — Lynn Valley Town Centre, Lynn Creek Town Centre, Maplewood Village Centre and Lions Gate Village Centre — and preserves the character of established low-density neighbourhoods outside the centres; Pemberton Heights is outside the centres, with Lions Gate Village Centre the growth node at its southwest corner. Zoning Bylaw 3210 governs. Since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; December 2023 changes let a secondary suite be up to 49% of the house with no minimum size and raised the maximum coach house height to two storeys. City of North Vancouver bylaws are never imported. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **SSMUH — as the District states it, with the Lions Gate caveat:** the District's GEOweb SSMUH classification (updated May 1, 2024) shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop; Bill 25 (November 2025) requires full SSMUH density by June 30, 2026 and the District's compliance amendments were still in preparation in spring 2026, tied to the Zoning Bylaw Rewrite — and a March 2, 2026 Council motion asked staff to pursue exempting the Lions Gate area from SSMUH, on the basis that it is better suited to development supporting rapid transit. Do not price in full SSMUH density for a Lions Gate lot without the lot's own GEOweb classification. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-4-detached-residential.pdf
