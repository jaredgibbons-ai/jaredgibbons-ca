## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Two-municipality frame (the page's own organizing fact):** Langley is two municipalities — the Township of Langley and the City of Langley — with different zoning bylaws, tax notices and planning frames; both updated zoning in 2024 for the provincial SSMUH law, differently. Start every lot question with the municipality. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-houses-for-sale.html
- **Township SSMUH (SETTLED position):** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 Amendment (SSMUH) Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — an eligible lot (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on both municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units if 280 m² or smaller, or 4 units if between 280 m² and 4,050 m² (1 acre); lots larger than 4,050 m² are not eligible. **There is NO 6-unit tier in the Township** — a live Brookswood DEVELOPMENT page claiming a 6-plex tier would be wrong on this frame (asserted only where that slug's own research documents it; no such claim is documented in THIS slug's research). https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Township suites / coach houses:** Zoning Bylaw s.102, as amended (including by Bylaw #6147, July 21, 2025), caps a secondary suite at 120 m² maximum gross floor area, one per principal dwelling unit; a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata. **Live Township conflict flagged, not resolved:** the January 2025 "Residential Construction Guide — Secondary Suites" and the July 2025 s.102 text conflict on suites in townhouses/rowhouses (the guide stating the 120 m² cap alongside a not-permitted position, s.102 listing townhouse/rowhouse suite locations) — both documents are carried by date, resolved nowhere. R-CL s.403 = 220 m² min LOT / 464.5 m² max LOT area (the "355 m²" figure is NOWHERE in the bylaw — never carried). Subdivision/servicing: Township Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED. Willoughby Plan = Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025); Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 = first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY (Public Hearing directed June 15, 2026) — never upgraded, and a research "replaced" reading is corrected wherever met. (Research file §4.)
- **City SSMUH (SETTLED position):** City of Langley Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 — RS1/RS2 lots permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — a different answer from the Township's, which is why the municipality check comes first. **City suites (Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300):** a suite must not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area; the registered owner must reside on the lot (s.2.4); a carriage/garden ground-floor unit is capped at 100 m². City lot-size figures the live page states (RS1 minimum 557 m², average RS1 798 m² / RS2 1,715 m²; Brookswood-Fernridge minimums) are the page's site copy only — the Community Plan and staff memo behind them were not opened in this pass: UNVERIFIED at source. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3284 and https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-houses-for-sale.html
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — for rural Langley parcels where reserve status is confirmed (this region file inventories no ALR parcel, §2):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan IS a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — never the shorthand "cannot be stratified," and Bulletin 05 is not cited for stratification. Residence caps: a principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite is permitted ONLY when located within the principal residence; one additional residence is permitted up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. Municipal zoning (Township or City, per the parcel) and the ALC frame both apply. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer who plans to continue farming must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase.
