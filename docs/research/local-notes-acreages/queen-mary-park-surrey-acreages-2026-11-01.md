## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026)** — current; Bylaw No. 18020 is the replaced predecessor and is never cited as current. There is no City plan area named Queen Mary Park under it (§1); parcel designations are read in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000** — every parcel carries a zone under it, confirmed parcel by parcel in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH (adopted Jul 8, 2024):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, applying to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. With RapidBus corridors on both Scott Road and King George Boulevard, edge lots in this sub-area may carry the 6-unit position — check the parcel in COSMOS. Added-unit permission is not permission to subdivide. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites:** only one suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, and never inside a coach house or garden suite. Suite legality on any specific Queen Mary Park property: UNVERIFIED — the feed includes homes described with unauthorized suites, which are listing admissions, not City records. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision and servicing: Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 — VERIFIED** on the City's own pages; subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000 through the City's Engineering Land Development process to a Servicing Agreement. No other city's servicing number is used for Surrey. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside / riparian:** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, with different setbacks by stream class — NO single riparian figure is stated for Surrey generally; the parcel's class and Part 7A govern. Healy Creek runs through Queen Mary Park itself (§1), so creek-adjacent parcels in this sub-area carry the Part 7A check in practice. https://www.surrey.ca/activities-parks-recreation/parks/queen-mary-park
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only — only the property owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, business licence **$358 per year** on the City's program page. **Conflict disclosed:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposing the STR bylaw amendments put the licence at $350/yr plus fees — this file carries $358 with the $350 proposal disclosed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried because this area is farm-adjacent at the city scale and any edge parcel's status is per-parcel:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m² (with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction); a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. - **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
