## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Two planning frames, kept separate:** the Burkeville residential pocket sits inside the City of Richmond frame — Zoning Bylaw 8500 / OCP Bylaw 9000 (Sea Island is planning area 12 for the Burkeville pocket) — while senior governments are not regulated by the City OCP and airport-side changes follow the federal / YVR process under the YVR 2037 Master Plan. The exact Bylaw 8500 zone for Burkeville lots: UNVERIFIED (Section 2). https://news.yvr.ca/yvrs-new-master-plan-has-arrived/
- **Subdivision and servicing — VERIFIED ledger (City frame):** Richmond Subdivision Bylaw 8751 and Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 are VERIFIED for Richmond. They govern City-frame subdivision in the Burkeville pocket; they do not turn federal airport land into subdivision land. (Standing servicing ledger; a sibling carrying Richmond servicing as UNCONFIRMED is a conforming-pass item.)
- **Federal Airport Zoning Regulations:** administered through Transport Canada, they set zoning in areas surrounding the airport so aircraft can operate safely, including maximum building heights — check them early for any addition, rebuild or tree-height question on a Burkeville lot. https://news.yvr.ca/statement-regarding-airport-zoning-regulations/
- **Aircraft noise:** managed through the YVR 2025–2029 Noise Management Plan, which includes work to promote compatible land use planning near the airport; YVR publishes annual noise reports with monitoring data. The City's Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy Area framework (City Bulletin DEVAPPS-05) applies to noise-sensitive development in the airport's vicinity. https://www.yvr.ca/en/about-yvr/noise-management/reports and https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/devapps0534378.pdf
- **Flood — Bylaw 8204 framework:** Richmond is made up of low-lying islands in the Fraser River floodplain, protected by a perimeter dike system (49 km of dikes, 39 pump stations, designed for a 1:500 flood event, dikes being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise); the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets flood construction level requirements — confirm the lot's position with the City before planning a rebuild. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **ALR — conditional statement only (no current ALR ground documented on Sea Island):** IF a parcel were in the Agricultural Land Reserve, the ALC frame would govern: subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); and farm class would run on the three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met; that line is documented only on the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file). None of this is asserted to apply to any Sea Island parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **If a strata purchase is ever the redirect case:** the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw; disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. Whether any strata property exists on Sea Island is UNVERIFIED, so this block attaches to no documented stock here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 fully residential Burkeville lot purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Property taxes:** City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026; this city-wide rule applies to Burkeville homes. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm

Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a separate sheet as usually taxable — never assume any parcel is GST-exempt on the vacant-land rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED; the contract must state the position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
