- **Ground-oriented density as the bylaw states it:** 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop (Bylaw 4662, Sections 620–630) — stated as the District's own rules, never restated as provincial maximums or minimums in either direction. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf

- **Hazard development permits — the hillside's governing diligence.** An Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit is required for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface — both can apply on Altamont's hillside and creek lots and can add geotechnical and permit cost to a rebuild. A Foreshore Development Permit is required for work within the Foreshore Development Permit Area (waterfront property or other properties at risk of coastal flooding), which can apply to the lowest streets below the hillside. Check the hazard permits on a hillside lot early, before pricing a renovation or rebuild. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **ALR — conditional statement only (no ALR ground documented for Altamont):** IF a parcel were in the Agricultural Land Reserve, the ALC frame would govern: subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); and farm class would run on the three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met). None of this is asserted to apply to any Altamont lot in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **If a strata purchase is made elsewhere in the District (redirect case):** the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. None of it applies to an Altamont fee-simple lot purchase itself: no strata, no Form B, no strata fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $3,500,000 fully residential hillside-lot purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,800,000 ($36,000) + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + a further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000, INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** (tier math shown). Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000. A townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Additional school tax — documented for this ground in its own research:** at Altamont price points the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. Budget for it in the buyer's closing and holding math where the residential assessment exceeds $3 million. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Property taxes:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied to unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026; approximately 50% of the tax bill is collected by the District and remitted to other taxing authorities.
