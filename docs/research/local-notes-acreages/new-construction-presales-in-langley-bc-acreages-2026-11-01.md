## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **REDMA — the presale frame (applies to every Langley presale, either municipality):** under the Real Estate Development Marketing Act, a purchaser may rescind a purchase agreement by serving written notice on the developer within **seven days** after the later of the date the purchase agreement was made and the date the developer obtained the purchaser's receipt for the Disclosure Statement (**s.21**); a developer must give each purchaser a copy of the filed Disclosure Statement, and any filed amendment, before entering into the agreement (s.15); **deposits must be held in trust as s.18 requires**. https://www.bcfsa.ca/industry-resources/real-estate-developer-resources/real-estate-development-marketing-act-faqs https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01
- **Township frame (projects on Township ground ONLY):** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs. SSMUH — Township Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024): eligible lots (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on both municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units if 280 m² or smaller, or 4 units if between 280 m² and 4,050 m²; lots larger than 4,050 m² are not eligible, and **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020**. Secondary suite cap **120 m²**; a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102 — never strata (**LIVE CONFLICT — flagged, not resolved:** s.102, July 2025, permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted). R-CL s.403: **220 m² minimum lot area to 464.5 m² maximum LOT area** — no "355 m²" figure exists. Subdivision and servicing: **Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED**; subdivisions are approved by the Approving Officer under the Land Title Act. Willoughby Plan Bylaw 1836 (1979) became Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025); Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 reached first/second reading ONLY — never upgrade reading-stage bylaws. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **Langley City frame (projects on City ground ONLY — never imported into Township ground):** Langley City Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300, cited by number (adoption date UNVERIFIED); SSMUH Bylaw 3284; secondary suite = the lesser of 100 m² and 40%. City servicing-bylaw number: UNCONFIRMED in this file — no number is asserted for the City. (Program standing corrections, carried from the lots sibling.)
- **Newly Built Home Exemption (PTT, verified — and it matches the live page's thresholds):** the exemption reduces or eliminates property transfer tax on a qualifying principal-residence purchase; effective April 1, 2024 the full-exemption threshold is a fair market value of $1,100,000, with a partial exemption phasing out over the $50,000 range above it and complete elimination at $1,150,000. The buyer does not need to be a first-time buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (where ALR ground exists — parcel status UNCONFIRMED per parcel):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it — owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page; no farm-class parcel is documented in this slug's research. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **BC strata block (a completed strata or bare-land strata lot, where one exists):** Form B diligence (SPA s.59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. None of this exists yet for an unbuilt presale unit at signing — the disclosure statement governs instead (REDMA above). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. For a qualifying newly built principal residence, the Newly Built Home Exemption above displaces these anchors up to its thresholds. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a SEPARATE info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. A presale home is new housing, not vacant land — GST on the home itself is addressed by the rebate below. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — CRA governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Warranty, as the live page states it (UNVERIFIED as stated — no BC Housing or other official source was opened in this pass):** "New homes in BC carry 2-5-10 home warranty coverage: 2 years on materials and labour, 5 years on the building envelope and 10 years on structure." https://jaredgibbons.ca/new-construction-presales-in-langley-bc.html
