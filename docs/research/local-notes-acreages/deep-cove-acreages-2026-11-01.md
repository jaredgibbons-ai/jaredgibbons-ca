## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: District of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw 3210** — Section 525 detached zones R1 and R2 with the minimums in Section 2; an R2 principal building may include a secondary suite, and a coach house is a dwelling unit accessory to and detached from the principal dwelling unit. Confirm the parcel's zone through the District's GeoWeb. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **OCP: District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900** — covers this area and names Deep Cove a Village Centre; the Livable Deep Cove project is the current village-core implementation work under it. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/livable-deep-cove-survey-2022.pdf

- **Three-unit frame and SSMUH in the District, as the sources state them:** since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. The District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop; provincial rules set 3 units on lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on larger lots within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Hazard Development Permit Areas (District frame, as the research states them):** District OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets seven Development Permit Areas, including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard — Deep Cove's forested slope, creek and shoreline lots can fall in several of these at once. The District publishes property-level GeoWeb reports, including modelled flood construction levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. Mount Seymour Provincial Park rises directly behind the Seymour / Deep Cove side. https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/ and https://bcparks.ca/mount-seymour-park/
- **ALR — conditional statement only (no ALR ground documented in Deep Cove):** IF a parcel were in the Agricultural Land Reserve, the ALC frame would govern: subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); and farm class would run on the three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met; that line is documented on the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file).
