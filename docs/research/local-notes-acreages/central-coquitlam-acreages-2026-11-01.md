## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: City of Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996** — small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3, with subdivision minimums of 650 m² / 18.5 m width (R-1 and R-3) and 465 m² / 12.5 m width (R-2). https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **OCP: Interim Official Community Plan, Bylaws 5511/5512, adopted April 27, 2026, superseding OCP No. 3479** — CURRENT per the settled ledger. - **Subdivision and servicing — VERIFIED ledger:** Coquitlam Subdivision Servicing Bylaw 3558, 2003 is VERIFIED. Subdivision runs against the Zoning Bylaw 3000 minimums and Bylaw 3558's servicing requirements; confirm the works required for a specific lot with the City. (Standing servicing ledger.)
- **SSMUH in Coquitlam, as the City states it:** up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. Price unit potential from the bylaw and the lot's own zone and services, not from a listing template. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Watercourse / hazard diligence:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam; Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and s.523 covers riparian areas. No single riparian figure beyond the bylaw's own is stated, and a Fraser River floodplain designation for the residential streets is UNVERIFIED. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **ALR — conditional statement only (no ALR ground documented in Central Coquitlam):** IF a parcel were in the Agricultural Land Reserve, the ALC frame would govern: subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); and farm class would run on the three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met; that line is documented on the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file). None of this is asserted to apply to any Central Coquitlam lot in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **If a strata purchase is made at the City Centre edge (redirect case):** the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw; strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. None of it applies to a fee-simple lot purchase itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 fully residential Central Coquitlam lot purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
