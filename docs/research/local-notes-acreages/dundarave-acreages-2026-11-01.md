- **Q: What extra permits does a waterfront lot need?** A Foreshore Development Permit applies to waterfront properties and others at risk of coastal flooding, and an Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of a watercourse bank; a Wildfire Hazard DP applies within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface. Budget the permit lens from day one. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Q: Can I build a multiplex on a lot near the village now?** The Province directed West Vancouver on July 21, 2025 to increase density in the single-family/duplex areas adjacent to Dundarave by December 31, 2025, but the District's final adopted amendments for those areas were still outstanding as of November 2026 in the research — the position is UNCONFIRMED per property until the current Bylaw 4662 zoning for that lot is read. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698
- **Q: Do village-core rules limit a renovation?** Yes — the Dundarave Village Neighbourhood Centre is a Form & Character Development Permit area, so exterior changes in the core are reviewed against OCP design guidelines and are not a simple building permit. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Q: What will transfer tax cost on a $3.5M lot purchase?** $93,000 if fully residential, with the tier math shown in Section 3 (including the further 2% on the portion over $3M). - **Q: Do I pay GST on a vacant Dundarave lot?** At CRA GI-003 level only: most individual personal-use vacant-land sales are exempt, but a sale can be taxable where the land is business capital property, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED; the contract must state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
