## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Governing frame — UEL (provincial), not a city:** the UEL is governed under the University Endowment Land Act by the Minister of Municipal Affairs, administered by the UEL Administration under the UEL Official Community Plan and the Land Use, Building and Community Administration Bylaw. There is no municipal council, no city zoning bylaw and no city subdivision bylaw for UEL ground. UBC's campus lands are governed by the university and developed through UBC Properties Trust on the prepaid 99-year lease structure in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/local-governments/improvement-districts-governance-bodies/university-endowment-lands

- **Head lease diligence (UBC purchases):** order and read the head lease — confirm lease start date, prepaid status, remaining term, and any UBC rights (including faculty/staff resale restrictions on some program homes) before subject removal; document-level verification is required per property. Confirm the UNA services levy and any neighbourhood-specific charges in the strata budget — the UNA, not a city, delivers municipal-like services under its Neighbours Agreement with UBC. https://elementslab.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Wesbrook-Study_reducedsize_secure2.pdf and https://www.myuna.ca/about-us/
- **UEL property taxes — the rural regime, stated exactly:** UEL properties do NOT pay City of Vancouver property tax. Because the UEL is taxed as a rural area, annual property taxes are payable to the Province's Surveyor of Taxes and are due and payable on July 2 of the year they are first levied; a penalty of 5% of the unpaid taxes is added to any portion unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% to any portion still unpaid on October 31 of that year (Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s. 11). Rural tax notices are mailed by May 31, and payment must be received by the due date to count as on time. UEL water is billed separately from the tax notice. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/important-dates/overdue
- **Empty Homes Tax — does NOT apply here:** Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is a City of Vancouver tax on City ground; this ground is UEL/UBC, taxed under the rural regime above — the EHT is stated here ONLY as a non-application, and no Vancouver vacancy position is imported.
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried for completeness; no University VW ALR parcel is documented in this file's research:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps: principal residence maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, and it is NOT documented in this slug's own research — asserted nowhere in this file. No parcel here is carried as farm-class. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **BC strata block (where a strata purchase is made instead of land):** Form B diligence (SPA s.59, within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; 45+ does NOT convert). - **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million.
