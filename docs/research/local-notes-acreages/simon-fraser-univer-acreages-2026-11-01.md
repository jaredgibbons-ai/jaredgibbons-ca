## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy an acreage — or a freehold lot — at SFU / UniverCity?** No. There are no conventional fee-simple residential lots inside UniverCity: SFU Community Trust leases fully serviced, subdivided parcels to developers on 99-year leases, and every market home is a leasehold strata unit on that structure. (§2; https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/)
- **Q: So what do I own when I buy at UniverCity?** A strata lot and a leasehold interest for the remaining term of the 99-year prepaid ground lease. You do not own the land under the building. (§2.)
- **Q: What should I read first — the Form B or the ground lease?** Both, in that order of risk: the Form B for the strata's finances and bylaws, and the ground lease for commencement date, remaining term, prepaid status and end-of-term provisions. The lease is what a lender and lawyer will scrutinize; lender treatment is UNVERIFIED per lender. (§3.)
