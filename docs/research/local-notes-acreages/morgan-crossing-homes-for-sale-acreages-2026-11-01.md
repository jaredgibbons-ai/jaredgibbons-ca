## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) is CURRENT — cite 21900, NEVER 18020 as current.** Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs. - **SSMUH (Surrey, in force Jul 8, 2024):** inside the urban containment boundary, on lots under 4,050 m² — 3 units on a lot of 280 m² or less, 4 units on a larger lot, up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey):** only one suite per dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never a separate strata lot. A "90 m²" cap is NOT the current Surrey suite rule. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision + servicing (Surrey):** Subdivision and Development (Servicing) Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 VERIFIED from the City's own pages. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan IS a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — never the shorthand "cannot be stratified," never cite Bulletin 05 for stratification.
