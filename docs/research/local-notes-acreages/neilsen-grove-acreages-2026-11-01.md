## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: RS/RD zones under Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 as this slug's research cites it — number CONTESTED program-wide, flagged never asserted as settled.** The urban Ladner ladder as the research states it: RS5 (minimum lot area for subdivision 550 m², or 460 m² outside the SFR designation; width 16 m or 15 m; depth 30 m), RS6 (390 m², width 13 m, depth 30 m), RS7 (335 m², width 11 m), RD1 duplex (836 m², width 22 m, depth 30 m) and RD4 duplex (504 m², width 18 m, depth 28 m), while RS1, RS2, RS3, RD2 and RD3 sit at 4,000 m² in lower-density areas, and there is no RD5 zone. - **OCP: consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future," adopted July 8, 2024, NO lettered schedules.** Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" in the current plan; any lettered-schedule citation under Bylaw 8400 (the old Bylaw No. 3950 structure) is an error wherever met. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 + 8395, adopted June 24, 2024.** Delta's updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies; the RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use to a single detached dwelling. Confirm the parcel's zone on the zoning maps before assuming a lot can add a unit. SSMUH creates no acreage lots. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf

- **Streamside and flood — the harbour-side rules:** all properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. Ladner is low land protected by the City's flood protection system: the City operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure (dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection), 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure. Surrey's streamside Part 7A figures are Surrey's and are never imported into a Delta file. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **ALR RULES — carried because Delta is farm-adjacent, applied only where a parcel proves to be ALR (none is documented in Neilsen Grove):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. - **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million.
