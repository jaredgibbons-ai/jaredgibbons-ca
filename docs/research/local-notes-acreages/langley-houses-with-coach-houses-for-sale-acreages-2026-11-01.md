## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is a coach house in the Township of Langley?** Under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s.102, a coach house is a dwelling unit on the second storey of a detached garage only — a fee-simple second unit on the same lot, NEVER strata. Section 100's own text states no size cap for one, but whether a cap sits in the bylaw's individual zone sections is UNVERIFIED, which is why a ground-level cottage or barn loft marketed as a "coach house" needs its permit checked. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Can I buy or sell the coach house separately from the house?** No. A coach house is an accessory unit on one fee-simple parcel — it is not a strata lot, not a bare-land strata lot and not separately titled, so it transfers only with the whole parcel, and PTT is payable once on that parcel. Subdividing it off would be a subdivision (and, on ALR ground, would need ALC approval — §3). (§§2, 3.)
- **Q: Is a carriage home the same as a coach house?** They describe similar buildings under different bylaws: the Township's Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 uses "coach house," while the City of Langley's Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300 uses "carriage home" — the live page's detailed City limits (6.8 m / two levels, 100 m² ground floor, owner-occupancy) are UNVERIFIED in this file because the City bylaw was not opened in this research pass. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-houses-with-coach-houses-for-sale.html
- **Q: How big can a secondary suite be in the Township of Langley?** Zoning Bylaw s.102, as amended, caps a Township secondary suite at 120 m² with no percentage cap; note the documented conflict — the Township's January 2025 guide says suites are not permitted in townhouses/rowhouses, while the July 2025 s.102 definition says they may be. The conflict is flagged, not resolved — confirm in writing. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: How do I know a marketed "coach house" is actually permitted as one?** Follow the page's own check: ask for the building permit and the final inspection for the coach house itself, and confirm the zone with the right municipality — a building marketed as a coach house may have been approved as a garage, studio or workshop. No card in this feed had its permit record opened in this research. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-houses-with-coach-houses-for-sale.html
- **Q: Can an ALR property's additional residence sit over a garage?** Yes, on the ALC's carriage-house exception: the garage below is excluded from the additional residence's floor area if it is one storey and for the principal residence's vehicles, does not exceed the additional residence's maximum floor area (90 m² or 186 m²), has no internal connection to the residence, and the residence is clustered close to the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Does SSMUH give me more units than the coach-house route?** They are alternatives, not a stack: an eligible Township lot's SSMUH route (3–4 units under Bylaw No. 6020, adopted Nov 18, 2024; no 6-plex tier) and its suite/ADU route are governed by eligibility and zoning — lots over 4,050 m² are SSMUH-ineligible and fall back to one unit plus a suite or ADU depending on zoning. Confirm which route the lot is actually on with Township staff before pricing the extra unit. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
