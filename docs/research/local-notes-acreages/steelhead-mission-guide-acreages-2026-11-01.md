## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Official Community Plan:** City of Mission Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026 (settled position). The guide page's "June 1, 2026" adoption date is WRONG and is the defect owned in §5 — never repeated as fact here. **Zoning: Mission Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020** (rural zone set in §2). https://jaredgibbons.ca/steelhead-mission-guide.html
- **Secondary suites (Mission):** a Mission secondary suite is a self-contained living space inside a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; a suite is not a coach home or garden cottage (those are detached secondary dwellings); suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; all suites must be registered with the City, new suites go through the building permit process, and an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus utility fees applies. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **Detached secondary dwellings (Mission rural):** on rural lots a coach home or garden cottage generally requires the secondary-dwelling ("s") variant of the parcel's zone — City application material for the neighbouring Stave Falls area confirms RU16-to-RU16s rezonings are the route used to permit a secondary detached unit on a rural lot outside the Urban Growth Boundary on land designated Rural Residential. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rsd25-4
- **SSMUH (Mission):** Mission's SSMUH incorporation is Bylaw 6299-2024 (Jul 17, 2024), verified via City sources — zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings as of December 7, 2023 are subject to it, subject to eligibility and servicing. Steelhead's rural lots sit outside the urban serviced area, so whether SSMUH unit counts apply to a given Steelhead lot is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. Unit counts are held UNCONFIRMED in this file — Surrey figures are never imported. https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **Subdivision / servicing:** Mission Development and Subdivision Control Bylaw (DSCB) No. 5650-2017 (City source, title confirmed — verified ledger). (Verified ledger; FILE STATUS.)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (ALR ground exists in Mission's rural area and Steelhead is farm-adjacent — this block applies wherever ALR ground exists):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is NOT cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property taxes (City of Mission):** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a FLAT 10% penalty on unpaid taxes after July 2 — added to any current balances outstanding (including unclaimed home owner grants) on July 3, 2026 (Community Charter ss.233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s.3; Mission has NO alternative-scheme bylaw under Charter s.235). The separate FVRD rural 5% + 5% regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11) does NOT apply inside the City of Mission, including in Steelhead, and is never blended in. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a SEPARATE info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — CRA governs; a March 12 date is noted in-line where met elsewhere, never silently overwritten) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
