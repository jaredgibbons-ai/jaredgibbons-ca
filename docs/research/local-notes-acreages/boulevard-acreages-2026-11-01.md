## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning — City side ONLY: City of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw No. 6700, Ground Oriented Zone** — a lot of 280 m² or less allows up to 3 units; a lot over 280 m² allows up to 4 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 designation and up to 6 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations. The City adopted the GO Zone on December 8, 2025, raising permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 properties. Subdivision minimum widths are in Section 2. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **Zoning — District side ONLY: District of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525** — R1/R2 minimums as in Section 2; an R2 lot may hold a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite and a coach house, and since June 2024 three units total are permitted on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **OCPs — two plans, one corridor:** City side, City of North Vancouver Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400 (2014, amended December 8, 2025); District side, District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900. Each plan governs only its own side. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — split by side:** on the CITY side, the City's Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw 8014 is the City servicing/subdivision instrument and is citable for City-side lots ONLY — it must never be applied to a District-side lot. On the DISTRICT side, the District servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED, so no number is quoted and the process is documented instead: subdivision runs off the Section 525 minimums in Section 2, and hazard permit areas (below) gate District-side work. Confirm requirements with the lot's own municipality in every case. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits/Streamside-Development-Permit-Areas
- **Hazards — City side:** the City's Streamside Protection Development Permit Area covers land within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse or within 10 metres of the top of a ravine bank, and a development permit is required before new development there. Grand Boulevard is also a City heritage character area in the Green Necklace — frontage and character expectations, not just zoning, shape what can be built facing the boulevard on the City side. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits/Streamside-Development-Permit-Areas
- **Hazards — District side:** District OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets Development Permit Areas for Streamside Protection, Creek Hazard, Slope Hazard and Wildfire Hazard, any of which can apply on the District portion toward Lynn Valley. https://dnv-docs.simplicitycms.ca/documents/ocp-schedule-b-dpa.pdf
- **ALR — conditional statement only (no ALR ground documented on the corridor):** IF a parcel were in the Agricultural Land Reserve, the ALC frame would govern: subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); and farm class would run on the three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met; that line is documented on the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file). None of this is asserted to apply to any corridor lot in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **If a strata purchase is made on the corridor instead (redirect case — the 1555 Grand Boulevard single-lot form, IF title confirms its strata tenure):** the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (a single-lot form sits outside that 5+ threshold on its face — confirm the plan's lot count on title); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. None of it applies to a fee-simple lot purchase itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — the same provincial tiers on both sides of the corridor. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 fully residential Boulevard lot purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Property taxes — as the research documents them, by side:** City side, City of North Vancouver 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal-general rate is 1.89202 and the total residential rate is 3.34028 per $1,000 of assessed value, plus a separate residential storm drainage rate of 0.10697 per $1,000. District side, District of North Vancouver 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000, and single-family utility fees total $2,571.20 in 2026. The provincial additional school tax is NOT documented in this file's research, so no rate for it is asserted here. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026_TAX_RATE_SUMMARY_CITY_OF_NORTH_VANCOUVER-2.PDF and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at the CRA GI-003 level:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a separate sheet as usually taxable — never assume any parcel is GST-exempt on the vacant-land rule alone. The GST treatment of a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and use are applied; the contract must state the position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Speculation and vacancy taxes:** no application to this corridor is documented in this file's research, so none is asserted — UNCONFIRMED for a specific property until its class and the current lists are read. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply in North Vancouver. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal prohibition on purchase by non-Canadians:** this file states ONLY what its cited sources support — no primary text was opened in this file's research, so the position is UNCONFIRMED and is NOT conformed from any other file. (Research file above.)
- **Foreclosure / court-ordered sale — where met:** BC court-ordered sales follow the settled Supreme Court process (Rules 21-7 / 13-5, Practice Direction PD-66, Form 60). Form B diligence and PTT/GST treatment on a specific court sale are UNCONFIRMED in this file, kept distinct from the CRA GI-003 position above. - **The GO Zone changed the City side in December 2025.** The Ground Oriented Zone adopted December 8, 2025 raised permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 City properties and allows up to 6 units in the higher designations — a City-side lot's value now turns on its OCP designation, while the District side still runs on principal + suite + coach house. (Research file §4.)

(Research file §4.)
