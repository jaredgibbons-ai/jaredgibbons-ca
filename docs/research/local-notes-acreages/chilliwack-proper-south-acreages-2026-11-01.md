## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP — frame carried with its research caveat:** the program's standing position is Chilliwack 2050, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500. This slug's own research records that the live page attributes the area's policies to a “Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan” and Downtown/Proper infill policies, and that the plan name and its application to this area were not confirmed on a City page in that pass: the bylaw frame is carried from the program's city-wide verification, the per-area application caveat from this slug's research is stated, not smoothed, and a parcel's designation is a per-parcel City check. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, §6 farm zones** where farm/ALR ground arises; the parcel's actual zone is confirmed with the City per parcel — no zone label is adopted for any specific lot from listing text (the Nowell Street R1-C claim included). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH: Bylaw 5395, adopted Jun 18, 2024** — the small-scale multi-unit housing amendment to Bylaw 5000 allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. Added-unit permission is not permission to subdivide and does not create acreage lots. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3090/Signed%20Staff%20Report%20%2D%20RZ001761%2Epdf
- **Secondary suites and accessory units (Bylaw 5000):** a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space (s.2); only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits, types include secondary suite, coach house and garden suite (s.4.06(1)); garden suites cap at 100 m²; ADU parking is addressed in s.5.04. The City's Secondary Suites Policy permits rental of suites within any single detached dwelling regardless of zone; a suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level under the Floodplain Regulation Bylaw. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **Flood diligence:** flood hazard zoning exists in the bylaw structure (RS-FHA reserve zone, §12 of Bylaw 5000) and the City applies flood protection requirements and a Floodplain Regulation Bylaw. Parcel-level flood construction levels for Proper South lots: UNVERIFIED — confirm with the City before pricing suite or infill potential. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Short-term rentals:** STR operations are regulated under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141, which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968 — confirm a property can be licensed under it before valuing rental income. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — number UNCONFIRMED, process documented:** the candidate “Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055” is NOT adopted per the program ledger and must NEVER be presented as verified. Documented process instead: all subdivision plans require approval by the City's Approving Officer under the Local Government Act and Land Title Act before Land Title registration; a Preliminary Layout Approval is the first major milestone (typically 8–12 weeks from application, valid 12 months) and sets out servicing, engineering and legal steps to final approval. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8208
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (provincial frame — no sub-area ALR parcel is documented, stated so it is not misapplied):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — full three-tier form:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value over 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31. A flat “$2,500+” statement is an ERROR. Stated explicitly: the flat form is NOT documented in this slug's own research — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file, and no farm-class claim is asserted for any Proper South parcel. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M lives only in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land), while business sales, capital property used primarily in a business, and subdivided parcels of more than two parts can be taxable; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution per GI-003's separate sheet. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — land boundary:** FTHB GST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 noted in-line in some siblings, not silently overwritten) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land — 100% to $1M (max $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. New Housing Rebate 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
