## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP: Delta's current Official Community Plan is Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future," adopted July 8, 2024)** — a single consolidated plan with NO area-plan schedules; farm land in Bylaw 8400 is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s.1.5.1). Schedules B.1 (East Ladner), B.3 (Ladner), C.1 (North Delta) and D.1 (Tsawwassen) were all schedules of the PREVIOUS OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, 1985. - **Zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted:** City zone sheets cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, while an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates in program sources. Neither is asserted as settled here; zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps and the parcel's own zone sheet. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024** — up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide, does not create new lots, and does not waive a hazard or streamside permit area. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Suites / coach homes:** the live page states Delta zoning permits registered secondary suites and detached garden suites / coach houses on qualifying lots, subject to floodplain, coverage and parking rules — site copy in this pass; the current bylaw wording for a specific lot was not re-opened here: UNVERIFIED at parcel level. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-homes-for-sale.html
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — Ladner is farm-adjacent Delta ground (Ladner Rural / East Delta), so the full frame governs wherever a parcel is in the reserve:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps as the ALC states them: land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless the parcel qualifies for an additional residence, and local governments may further regulate or restrict residences through zoning; a principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, and no more than 1,000 m² of fill (including structural aggregate for foundations and driveways) can be placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted ONLY when located within the principal residence, entirely contained within it; one additional residence, detached from the principal residence, is permitted up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, and building more than the permitted number, or larger than the size limits, requires ALC approval. City zoning and the ALC frame both apply; neither displaces the other. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices of $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — and new applications for farm classification must be submitted by the legislated deadline of October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it — owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page; it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Servicing / subdivision — DSSS 8288 adoption UNCONFIRMED, process documented instead:** a Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 (repealing No. 7162, 2015) is cited in this program from a City council report dated January 24, 2024 recommending first, second and third readings; final adoption and parcel-level standards are UNCONFIRMED, so subdivision is documented as process (Approving Officer, zone minimums, servicing at the lot line confirmed with the City) and no number is asserted as governing. - **Floodplain:** Ladner is dike-protected floodplain (see §1); the applicable Flood Construction Level is parcel-specific — confirm it with the City of Delta for the exact lot before pricing a build or major addition. All properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area per the program's Delta files. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies to a qualifying home purchase); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland is the caution: the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a SEPARATE info sheet and treats farmland sales as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — no Ladner farm parcel is assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — CRA governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
