## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Legal geography: Barnston Island is a Fraser River island between Surrey (Port Kells) on the south bank and Pitt Meadows across the main Fraser channel. Most of the island is unincorporated **Metro Vancouver Electoral Area A**; the southeast portion is **Barnston Island Indian Reserve No. 3 (Katzie First Nation)**, which is federal reserve land and not fee-simple market housing. Mailing addresses use "Surrey," which is why the name is filed that way, but no part of the island is inside the City of Surrey. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- Access: there is no bridge — the only access is the free Barnston Island Ferry across Parson's Channel from 104 Ave in Port Kells, a barge-and-tug provincial inland ferry (capacity 5 vehicles and 50 passengers), running on demand roughly 6:15 a.m.–11:55 p.m. (to 12:55 a.m. weekends), with crossings that can be interrupted by river traffic. There is no bus service on the island itself. Every trip starts with the ferry (5-minute crossing); door-to-door timed commutes: UNVERIFIED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry

Metro Vancouver's Barnston Island Regional Park provides riverfront rest areas and protects riparian habitat on the island, and the dike-top perimeter road is the island's walking/cycling loop. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
