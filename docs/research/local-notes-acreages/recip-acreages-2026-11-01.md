- **Consumer frame (BCFSA, documented in this slug's research):** in British Columbia, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), and BCFSA states a licensed professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party, must disclose at the outset whether they can represent the consumer and how to complain to BCFSA, and can only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction — dual agency was prohibited in B.C. in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. That frame matters on this page precisely because the homes shown are other firms' listings: the agent whose contact block sits beside each card is not the listing agent. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers

Secondary suites: one per dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
