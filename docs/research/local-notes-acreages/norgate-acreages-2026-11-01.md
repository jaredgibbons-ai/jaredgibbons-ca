## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP and zoning (District only):** District of North Vancouver OCP Bylaw 7900 directs 75–90% of growth to four centres and preserves established low-density neighbourhoods; Zoning Bylaw 3210 governs. Since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; December 2023 changes let a secondary suite be up to 49% of the house with no minimum size and raised the maximum coach house height to two storeys.
