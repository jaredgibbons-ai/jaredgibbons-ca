## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **TOWNSHIP — zoning + plan:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs Township ground. The Willoughby Community Plan was Bylaw 1836 and is now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025); the Willoughby Plan Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 had first/second reading only — never describe them as adopted or as having replaced the plan. **CITY — zoning:** City of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300, cited by number only — its adoption is UNVERIFIED, and a live page's Mar 9, 2026 adoption date was NOT adopted. Confirm which municipality a parcel sits in on title before applying either frame. (FILE STATUS; lots-and-land sibling §3; condos sibling §5.)
- **TOWNSHIP — SSMUH + suites:** SSMUH implemented through Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 Amendment Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024) — NO 6-plex tier: 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m². Township suite cap 120 m². Township coach house = fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102, never strata — a live conflict between the s.102 text and a Township guide on townhouse suites is flagged in the siblings, not resolved here. **CITY — SSMUH + suites:** SSMUH Bylaw 3284 (RS1/RS2 lots 4 units, or 6 if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop); City suite = lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, with the registered owner required to reside on the lot. On ALR ground the ALC caps below govern instead. (FILE STATUS; lots-and-land sibling §3.)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the Township is farm-adjacent and carries ALR ground, so the full frame is carried for any parcel where reserve status is confirmed:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite is permitted ONLY when located within the principal residence, entirely contained within it; one additional residence, detached, is permitted up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. Municipal zoning and the ALC frame both apply; neither displaces the other. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer who plans to continue farming must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it — owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page; it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Servicing — CONFIRMED for the Township, City position as the ledger carries it:** Township of Langley Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019, No. 5382 — CONFIRMED in the bylaw text (standing servicing ledger). No per-lot servicing figure is stated in this slug's research and none is asserted. (Standing servicing ledger.)
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies to a qualifying home purchase); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; a developer's/business sale and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: CRA treats farmland sales in a separate info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — CRA governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a published median price for a Langley condo — or for Langley acreage?** Neither. SnapStats reports Langley only as Detached and as Condos & Townhomes combined; the combined median was $611,000 in September 2026, which blends townhomes and is expressly NOT a condo-only figure. Acreage has no SnapStats category at all. Condo-only and acreage figures are UNVERIFIED in this file. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats.)
- **Q: Does buying a Langley condo buy me land?** No — a strata condo is a unit in a building on already-subdivided land. Langley's acreage record lives in the farm/acreage home files cross-referenced in §2, not on this page. (§2.)
- **Q: Are Langley City and the Township of Langley the same for lot rules?** No — separate municipalities, and the frames differ on every line that matters to a lot: Township Zoning 2500 with SSMUH 6020 (no 6-plex tier; suite cap 120 m²) vs City Zoning 3300 by number (adoption UNVERIFIED) with SSMUH 3284 (suite = lesser of 100 m² and 40%). Confirm the municipality on title before comparing anything. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I build a 6-plex on a Township lot under SSMUH?** No — Township Bylaw 6020 has NO 6-plex tier: 3 units on lots ≤280 m², 4 units on lots to 4,050 m². The 6-unit figure belongs to the City's Bylaw 3284, and only within 400 m of a frequent bus stop in RS1/RS2 — a different municipality's rule. (§3.)
- **Q: What residence can a Langley ALR parcel have?** Under the ALC frame: one principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, with municipal rules possibly more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide an ALR parcel in the Township?** Not without the ALC's permission: depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and ALCA s.21(1) bars subdividing agricultural land unless the Commission permits it or the ALR General Regulation allows it (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6). (§3.)
- **Q: What does PTT cost on a Langley purchase?** On the general tiers (fully residential, no exemption): $500,000 → $8,000; $800,000 → $14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $1,500,000 → $28,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier over $3M. (§3.)
- **Q: What should I check before buying a Langley condo?** The strata bylaws, the depreciation report, recent strata minutes, the Form B information certificate and the contingency reserve fund — the checklist the live page itself gives — plus, on newer buildings, the exact home-warranty position confirmed from the building's documents. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-condos-for-sale.html
- **Q: When will SkyTrain reach Langley?** The Surrey Langley SkyTrain extension ends at Langley City Centre station; all eight stations are under construction and the Province expects the extension to enter operation in late 2029 — price today's commute on today's bus network. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0022-000515
- **Q: Which schools serve a specific Langley condo or acreage address?** The Ministry-listed schools named in §1 are names and addresses only. SD35 catchments are address-based; settled locator runs in this program cover sample addresses in Aldergrove, Salmon River and Willoughby Heights only, on a single-address basis — no run for a specific building or parcel on this feed is on record, so confirm the exact address in the SD35 School Locator.
