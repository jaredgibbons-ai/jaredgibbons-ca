## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs, at two scales.** Urban: RS3 minimum interior lot 540 m², RS5 420 m². Rural: A1 minimum subdivision lot area 8.0 ha, A2 16.0 ha — the single most important number set for a west-side acreage buyer, because it decides whether a rural parcel can ever be split. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/ and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **OCP: Abbotsforward 2050 is CURRENT** (adopted December 2025). The research file's "Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated" citation and any live-page "2500-2016" citation are STALE / conflicted — apply 2050 and flag both as a Jerry sweep item. The OCP's transit structure (Secondary Transit Corridors connecting Highstreet and the hospital to the Primary Transit Corridor on South Fraser Way between Clearbrook and McCallum Road) is plan context only. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **SSMUH / added units:** June 2024 Council adoption of the SSMUH Zoning Bylaw amendments is City-confirmed; the SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED ("3560-2024" is asserted in research files and must not be asserted — both bases stated, correction governs). Small Scale Multi-Unit Housing rules are in Zoning Bylaw s.140, and s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings, with City-report criteria including minimum lot size 400 m², minimum frontage 12.0 m, not on a cul-de-sac bulb and location on a Collector or Local road. Abbotsford's suite cap is under 50% of the principal dwelling, and a coach/garden suite is capped at 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m² — Abbotsford's rules ONLY. SSMUH does not override the A1/A2 minimums or the ALR on the rural edge. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer against the zone minimums above and the City's works-and-services requirements, applied file by file through a Works and Services Report and servicing agreement. HELD UNCONFIRMED: the Abbotsford Development Bylaw number — process documented instead; detailed servicing requirements for a specific subdivision are UNCONFIRMED until the current Development Bylaw text and the file's report are read. The one servicing rule the research states outright is the rural one: lots outside the urban boundary in rural residential zones may not connect to the Community Water System or Community Sanitary Sewer System. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58305/
- **ALR RULES IN FULL, on the west/southwest edge:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, stated as the ALC states it. ALR residences cannot be stratified — the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — which is why no bare-land strata can be created out of ALR ground here. ALC residence caps: principal residence 500 m² or less; suite ONLY within the principal residence; additional residence 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) / 186 m² (over 40 ha). The City's A1 zone mirrors the added-dwelling route: one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot in accordance with the ALR Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the ALC, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, at the same 90 m² / 186 m² caps. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR; none is asserted here and none is documented in this area's research file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Bare-land strata block (governs every Downes Road Estates lot, via its home file):** Form B diligence (the lot's strata fee, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage and insurance summary; the strata must provide the Form B within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with legacy-resident, caregiver and the May 1, 2023 spouse/partner and defined-children exemptions) — which is why the "45+" marketing line in the cross-reference cannot stand as a bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Well and septic diligence (rural edge):** check the well record in the provincial GWELLS database and confirm the onsite sewage system was filed under the Sewerage System Regulation by an Authorized Person through Fraser Health. A rural parcel priced as if it had city services is mispriced ground. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on acreage:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,200,000 fully residential acreage purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,000,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. Exemptions: First-Time Home Buyers' Program can be kept by a buyer who registers a vacant lot and builds, if lot value plus construction cost is $835,000 or less (partial under $860,000), building and occupying by the first anniversary; the Newly Built Home Exemption can refund PTT paid on a vacant lot once a qualifying newly built home is constructed ($1,100,000 full / $1,150,000 partial). Land bought to hold gets neither. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at the CRA GI-003 level:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts. Anything finer for a specific west-side parcel — farm parcels in particular, which the CRA addresses in a separate info sheet — is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and parcel history are established. State the GST position in the contract. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. MARCH-DATE NOTE: the condos sibling for this area carries a March 12, 2026 assent date in its own text — March 13 governs per CRA, the sibling date is noted here in-line and is NOT silently overwritten, and the sibling stands as a conforming-pass item. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A live page's $1.25M straight-line phase-out row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism where met. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax:** the Province's taxable-areas list names the City of Abbotsford — owners of residential-class property must complete the annual declaration. Parcel-level application to a rural acreage (assessment class, exemptions) is UNCONFIRMED until its class is read. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal prohibition on purchase by non-Canadians:** this file states ONLY what its cited sources support. The March 27, 2023 regulatory amendments repealed the provision that had made vacant land zoned residential or mixed-use inside a census metropolitan area or census agglomeration "residential property" — as amended, vacant land itself can be purchased by a non-Canadian, including for residential development, per law-firm summaries; dwellings remain governed by the prohibition and specific application is UNCONFIRMED at the official-text level in this file. https://www.mross.com/what-we-think/article/update-canadian-government-prohibits-non-canadians-from-purchasing-residential-real-estate
- **Foreclosure / court-ordered sale — where met:** BC court-ordered sales follow the settled Supreme Court process (Rules 21-7 / 13-5, Practice Direction PD-66, Form 60). Form B diligence and PTT/GST treatment on a specific court sale are UNCONFIRMED in this file, kept distinct from the CRA GI-003 position above. REGISTRY LEDGER: Abbotsford HAS a Supreme Court registry. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/168_2009_07
- **Property taxes / holding cost:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. A bare-land strata lot adds its strata fee on top; a fee-simple acreage carries its own servicing and maintenance instead. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What kinds of acreage can I buy in Abbotsford West?** Two tenures: a fee-simple rural/ALR parcel toward Mt. Lehman Road and YXX (the real acreage market), or a bare-land strata lot at Downes Road Estates (32250 Downes Road, LMS2380 — cross-referenced in Section 2, documented in the lots-and-land home file). No named fee-simple acreage subdivision or presale acreage offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Can I split a rural parcel on the Mt. Lehman side?** Almost never at house-lot scale: A1 subdivision minimum is 8.0 ha and A2 is 16.0 ha, and if the parcel is in the ALR, subdivision also requires ALC approval. Price a west-side acreage as one parcel unless the zone math and the ALR map both say otherwise. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is a rural acreage serviced?** Outside the urban boundary, no city connection is available in rural residential zones — those parcels use wells and onsite sewage. Check the GWELLS well record and the Fraser Health sewerage filing before offering, and confirm which side of the urban boundary the parcel sits on. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Q: Is Downes Road Estates 55+?** UNCONFIRMED. The sibling 55-plus file records listings stating "Age Restricted 55+", while one listing record markets "Age restricted 45+" — a line that cannot be a valid BC age bylaw, since only 55+ bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2). The registered bylaws attached to the Form B govern; do not rely on either marketing line. Its full entry is in the lots-and-land home file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What can I add to an acreage — a suite, a coach house, more units?** On urban ground: one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings (s.140.3.8) subject to the City-report criteria (400 m² lot, 12.0 m frontage, Collector or Local road, no cul-de-sac bulb); suite under 50% of the principal dwelling; coach/garden suite 15% of lot area to 100 m² maximum; SSMUH rules in s.140 (bylaw number UNCONFIRMED). On ALR ground the ALC caps replace the urban ones: principal 500 m², suite inside the principal only, additional residence 90 m² / 186 m². https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Q: Can I keep farm class on a west-side acreage?** Only on the full three-tier test — $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha (over 4 ha), $10,000 (under 0.8 ha) — applications by October 31. Acreage alone does not qualify. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Q: What taxes hit an acreage purchase?** PTT at the general rates (worked example in Section 3: $1.2M → $22,000, no exemption), with the first-time/newly-built exemptions reachable only by building and occupying a qualifying home. GST depends on the seller per CRA GI-003 (personal-use individual sale generally exempt; business or more-than-two-part subdivision sale can be taxable). Abbotsford is a speculation and vacancy tax area for residential-class property. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What does it cost to hold an acreage while I decide?** Property taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% July 3, second 5% September 16, 2026), insurance, and well/septic maintenance on a rural parcel — or, on a bare-land strata lot, the strata fee and any levies on the Form B. A fee-simple acreage has no strata fee. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Q: Which schools serve a west-side acreage?** Three locator chains are settled, each on a single-address basis: 3614 Clearbrook Road returns Clearbrook Elementary → Colleen and Gordie Howe Middle → W.J. Mouat Secondary; 2990 Oriole Crescent returns John MacLure Community School → Colleen and Gordie Howe Middle → W.J. Mouat Secondary; 3433 Firhill Drive returns Blue Jay Elementary → Eugene Reimer Middle → Rick Hansen Secondary (SD34 School Locator, mybaragar.com, run 2026-11-01). Blue Jay Elementary is at 30995 Southern Dr per the Ministry listing. Any other address — a rural Mt. Lehman parcel in particular — needs its own run. https://www.abbyschools.ca/policy-18-school-catchment-areas
- **Q: Does the airport change what I can build on an acreage?** It can: airport height rules apply inside the Airport Zoning Regulation area around YXX, and the research file directs buyers near Mt. Lehman Road and YXX to check both ALR status and the airport area before planning additions. Parcel-level height limits: UNCONFIRMED until the regulation area map is read for the parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
