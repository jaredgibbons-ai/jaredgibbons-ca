## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP and zoning (District only):** District of North Vancouver OCP Bylaw 7900 directs growth to the Lynn Creek Town Centre at Lynnmour's south end, and Zoning Bylaw 3210 (as amended by Bylaw 8698, June 2024) allows a main house, secondary suite and coach house on most single-family lots; December 2025 Ground-Oriented Housing rules permit up to four homes on more than 4,000 eligible District properties, and Phibbs Exchange and the Capilano University area were designated Transit-Oriented Areas under Bylaw 8679 (2024). City of North Vancouver bylaws are never imported. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/new-provincial-housing-legislation-fact-sheet.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — District number UNCONFIRMED (held, never borrowed):** the standing ledger holds District of North Vancouver servicing UNCONFIRMED, so no District servicing number is asserted; the process is documented instead — subdivision approval, servicing and Natural Hazard Development Permit Area compliance are confirmed with the District for the specific application. **For the record, the City of North Vancouver's Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw 8014 belongs to City files ONLY and is named here solely as a never-imported exclusion — it is not a District number.**
- **Hazard layer:** the District designates Natural Hazard Development Permit Areas and publishes property-level hazard reports through its GeoWeb viewer; the low bench between Lynn Creek and the Seymour River is exactly where that mapping applies. - **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried for completeness; no Lynnmour ALR parcel is documented in this file's research:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps: principal residence maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, and it is NOT documented in this slug's own research — asserted nowhere in this file. No Lynnmour parcel is carried as farm-class here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **BC strata block (where a strata purchase is made instead of land):** Form B diligence (SPA s.59, within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; 45+ does NOT convert). **Edgewater Estates has no Form B at all** — undivided interest, not strata (Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The elementary school is at end-of-life — and the District is asking the Province to replace it (November 2025).** Lynnmour Elementary's current building dates to 1973, enrolment reached 256 students in September 2025, and SD44's capital plan seeks a full replacement on seismic and condition grounds — a material long-term fact for any family buying a house lot in Inter River. https://www.sd44.ca/media/nvsd/nvsd/content-assets/documents/board-of-education/Letter-to-Minister-Bowinn-Ma---Lynnmour-Replacement---Nov-2025.pdf
- **A community recreation centre opened inside a condo development (February 19, 2025).** The Lynn Creek Community Recreation Centre at 1491 Hunter Street — the same address as the settled school-locator run — opened February 19, 2025 as one component of Intergulf's Hunter at Lynn Creek development, with licensed childcare operated by Parkgate Society and an NVDPL library kiosk inside. https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/community-recreation-centres/lynn-creek-community-recreation
- **A 5,668-hectare reserve starts at the end of Lillooet Road.** The Lower Seymour Conservation Reserve — managed by Metro Vancouver primarily for water supply, with more than 100 km of trails, Rice Lake, and over 900,000 visitors a year — begins just past Capilano University, minutes from the Purcell Way townhouse pocket. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/Pages/Lower-Seymour-Conservation-Reserve.aspx
- **Two Purcell Way addresses, two opposite tenures.** Lynnmour South (1825 Purcell Way) is a strata townhouse complex whose units sell individually; Lynmour Village Apartments (1923 Purcell Way) is a purpose-built rental building beside Capilano University that cannot be bought into at all. - **The R2 now runs through to Metrotown (September 7, 2026).** The R2 RapidBus extension gives Lynnmour's south end all-day RapidBus service from Park Royal through Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station, every 6–7 minutes at peak — the transit fact that underpins the town-centre density at the neighbourhood's south end. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
