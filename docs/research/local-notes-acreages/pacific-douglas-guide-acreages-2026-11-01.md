## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Official Community Plan:** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is replaced and is never cited as current. The Douglas NCP (process concluded 1999) designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 update. **Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000.** https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/douglas-ncp and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Secondary suites (Surrey):** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling; its habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area under Bylaw 12000. The old 90 m² Surrey suite cap is NOT a current rule — do not state it. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Coach houses and garden suites (Surrey):** permitted in residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes; 35 m² minimum and 75 m² maximum, rising to a maximum of 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the principal dwelling has not achieved its maximum floor area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **SSMUH (Surrey):** incorporated Jul 8, 2024 (Surrey frame). Per-parcel unit counts for a Pacific Douglas lot beyond the City frame: confirm with the City — no Surrey figure is imported from another city and none is invented here. (Surrey frame, FILE STATUS.)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted — an owner must host from their principal residence and hold a City business licence at $358 per year (Zoning By-law No. 12000, Part 4 s. B.7(d); Business License By-law No. 13680, s. 60.1). A $350 figure met elsewhere is a proposal, disclosed as such, never the in-force fee. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Subdivision / servicing:** Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 8830 (1986) VERIFIED for Surrey (verified ledger). Current parcel servicing: UNVERIFIED (§2). (Verified ledger; FILE STATUS.)
- **Streamside protection (Surrey):** Zoning Bylaw Part 7A setbacks apply BY STREAM CLASS — there is NO single riparian figure for Surrey, and none is carried here. Confirm the stream class and setback per parcel with the City. (Corrected ledger; Surrey frame.)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (ALR ground borders this area to the north and the Glades lot is stated in the ALR — this block applies wherever ALR ground exists):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is NOT cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a SEPARATE info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — CRA governs; a March 12 date is noted in-line where met elsewhere, never silently overwritten) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **BC strata block (Berkeley Village / Douglas Point are strata-form product in their home files):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
