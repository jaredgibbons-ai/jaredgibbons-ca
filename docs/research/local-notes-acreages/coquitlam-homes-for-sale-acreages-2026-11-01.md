## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Coquitlam frame in full:** Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996. https://coquitlam.ca/556/Frequently-Requested-Bylaws Interim OCP Bylaws 5511/5512 adopted April 27, 2026 supersede OCP No. 3479 — 3479 is NOT current; the City news release (posted May 1, 2026) confirms the streamlined Interim OCP (former OCP of more than 1,000 pages; 55 land use designations reduced to 18; over 60 maps simplified to 19; provincial housing legislation requirements incorporated), with a comprehensive OCP review anticipated to begin in 2027. http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1932
- **Servicing:** City of Coquitlam Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 3558, 2003 VERIFIED (servicing ledger). https://coquitlam.ca/556/Frequently-Requested-Bylaws
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** up to four dwelling units are allowed in low-density neighbourhoods throughout Coquitlam; the City's guide describes four small-scale residential zones (R-1 through R-4), forms including secondary suites, laneway homes, duplexes, triplexes and fourplexes, with R-1/R-2 caps of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m² and 4 units on lots 280 m² or larger. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId Secondary-suite size cap and owner-occupancy rule for Coquitlam: UNVERIFIED. Short-term rental rules in Coquitlam: UNVERIFIED in this pass.
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried wherever ALR ground exists or the area is farm-adjacent:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a PERMISSION rule, never an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; local governments may regulate further but not be more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by HORSE-PROPERTIES); it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is expressly not attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property taxes (Coquitlam):** City of Coquitlam property tax payment was due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any unpaid balance of current taxes after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty applies after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land — worked examples with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example at $800,000: $2,000 + $600,000 × 2% = $12,000, total $14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies). Worked example at $3.5M fully residential: $2,000 + $36,000 (to $2M) = $38,000, plus $1,500,000 × 3% = $45,000, plus $500,000 × 2% = $10,000 (over $3M), total **$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — follow $93,000**; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed (open summary-pass item). Full anchor set: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000; $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland caution per CRA: farmland sales are addressed separately and treated as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions.
