- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for any Thornhill ALR parcel:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence (ALR Use Regulation s.31 as the research records it); an additional residence only if the s.32 conditions are met, capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (HORSE-PROPERTIES owns it) — it is NOT documented in Thornhill's research and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Suites and SSMUH:** one secondary suite per lot in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, contained within the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the Floodplain Area; on ALR parcels the ALR Use Regulation governs instead (suite inside the principal residence only, s.31; additional residence only under s.32). Eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws — on rural/ALR ground the ALC frame above controls first. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
