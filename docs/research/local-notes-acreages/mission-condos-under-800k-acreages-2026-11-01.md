## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020** — Mission's zoning bylaw, under which both the urban condo stock of this page and the city's rural parcels sit. Verify a specific parcel's exact zone with the City before pricing any use. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning
- **OCP: Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026** — it replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025 and keeps the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction, with a Regional Context Statement added, per the City's public hearing notice. Live guides stating "June 1, 2026" are wrong — that error belongs ONLY to the three guide files named in the FILE STATUS block and is NOT carried as a defect of this page. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/notice-public-hearing-april-7-2026
- **SSMUH — with the narrower stance noted in-file:** Mission has adopted the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation (incorporation Bylaw 6299-2024, Jul 17, 2024) — zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings as of December 7, 2023 are subject to it, subject to eligibility and servicing. **Mission's unit counts are held UNCONFIRMED in this program — this slug's research likewise states the adoption without publishing unit counts, and that narrower stance is noted here rather than followed silently or filled in from another city; Surrey's figures are never imported.** https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **Suites and detached accessory units:** handled by zone under the City's planning pages — a Mission secondary suite is a self-contained living space inside a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; detached accessory units are a separate form governed by the parcel's zone. Confirm the form and the zone on the specific parcel before pricing a second dwelling. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame wherever a Mission parcel is in the reserve or farm-adjacent:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in this slug's own research — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision runs through the City against the zone minimums and the City's servicing requirements under the **DSCB (Development Servicing and Construction Bylaw) No. 5650-2017 (City source, title confirmed)**; for an ALR parcel, ALC approval is required in addition. Never quote a servicing standard for a rural parcel from a listing or a summary.
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on acreage:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program (south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m) and is flagged wherever met, not followed. Band note from this slug's research: because this page's cap is $800,000, a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in the band sits under the Province's $835,000 full-exemption value ceiling (exempting the first $500,000 of price, partial exemption only above $835,000 and below $860,000, subject to the program's other conditions) — qualification is a per-buyer legal check. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on farm and vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland is the caution: the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a SEPARATE info sheet and treats farmland sales as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — a Mission farm parcel must therefore never be assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file; the contract must state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 noted in-line in some sources — March 13 governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. On ALR land any qualifying new home must first fit the ALC caps above. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **If a strata purchase is made in Mission (this page's own stock):** the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). Building-specific bylaws, fees and deductibles sit in each building's own document package — no Mission building's registered bylaws were read for this slug's research. Pad ground is NOT lot stock / land / acreage; manufactured-home pad tenancy percentages only (2026 = 2.3% + proportionate; 2027 = 2.2% + proportionate) and NO pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
