## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any real acreage for sale in Southwest Maple Ridge?** No — not as a market. The sub-area is built-out urban ground under RS-1 zoning (668.0 m² minimum lots); no acreage subdivision, bare-land strata acreage community, presale acreage offering or ALR parcel market was verified on any source checked. What trades is large urban house lots. Buyers who need true acreage need a different sub-area; buyers who want a big Hammond lot with suite and SSMUH potential are shopping the right ground for that different product. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a big Hammond lot into smaller lots?** Only down to the RS-1 floor — 668.0 m² lot area, 18.0 m width, 27.0 m depth — through the City's subdivision process under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 4800-1993 (City source), and lots in the escarpment or floodplain frames carry the added reports and covenants in Section 3. There is no route from an RS-1 lot to "acreage lots." http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Q: What do the escarpment rules do to a lot near the Fraser River crest?** The policy area (207 Street west, 124 Avenue north, 224 Street east, the crest along the south; City Policies 6.23 and 6.24) requires drainage to the City storm system; within 300 m of the crest, a geotechnical report on a Section 219 covenant before a building permit; within 100 m, a detailed report on seismic slope failure. Septic fields are to be avoided and new residences require sanitary sewers. Price the reports and the covenant into the lot, not after. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- **Q: Can a floodplain lot carry a rental suite?** No. Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties, secondary suites are not permitted within the floodplain area, and no habitable floor space may sit below the 6.7 m geodetic flood construction level on affected lots. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q: How many homes can go on one lot here?** One single-detached house with one secondary suite inside it (maximum 40% of gross floor area) or a detached garden suite as an RS-1 accessory use — and eligible single-detached and duplex lots may carry up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. That density, plus the adopted Hammond Area Plan's mixed-use enablement near its commercial nodes, is the redevelopment math for this ground. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q: Did the Lougheed corridor plan rezone my lot?** No. The Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was ADOPTED January 27, 2026 (Bylaw No. 7781-2021), and the corridor ends at 221 Street — but the City states the plan does not immediately change any property's zoning. It frames future development; it is not a rezoning in hand. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP
- **Q: Is Val Maria a land purchase — or The Maples a fee-simple buy?** Neither. Val Maria is a leasehold non-strata manufactured home park: you own the home and rent the pad. The Maples is a housing co-operative: you hold membership/shares in 31 semi-detached co-op homes. Neither is lot stock or acreage stock, and neither is counted anywhere in this file. (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/southwest-maple-ridge-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §2)
- **Q: Is this lot in the ALR?** No ALR ground is documented inside this urban sub-area in the official sources checked — but the check is parcel-level on the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping, and if a parcel ever returned ALR, subdivision would need ALC approval, stratification would be barred, and the ALC residence caps (500 m² principal; suite inside only; 90 m² / 186 m² additional) would govern. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The sub-area name exists only on the board.** The City publishes no "Southwest Maple Ridge" boundary or plan — the planning document that actually governs this ground is the Hammond Area Plan (OCP s. 10.5), and the City names buyers use are Hammond, Port Hammond and Port Haney. A search, a plan check or a permit conversation run under the board name alone will miss the governing documents. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- **The school locator has a documented gap on Lorne Avenue.** The SD42 locator returned no result for 20577 Lorne Avenue — the Hammond Park address — because the address falls between the locator's listed ranges for the street. That is why this file claims no catchment: any acreage or lot marketing for this sub-area that names a catchment school without its own locator run on the exact address is ahead of the evidence. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/ (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/southwest-maple-ridge.md, §4)
- **Hammond began as a railway bargain.** The City records that the Hammond brothers, William and John, donated land to the CPR on the condition the first railway station in Maple Ridge be built in Hammond; the junction (Port Hammond Junction) served as a supply depot as the community grew around the station, yards and a cedar mill — which is why the oldest, largest lots in the sub-area cluster around the historic core and the two West Coast Express stations bookend it today. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/hammond-1st-reading-report
- **The OCP number itself is contested — check before you quote.** The program record carries two Maple Ridge OCP citations (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024) and this file resolves neither; the Hammond Plan's s. 10.5 position and adopted status are the settled facts. Any older page or feature sheet quoting an OCP number for this ground should be verified against the City's current bylaw list before it is relied on. (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/southwest-maple-ridge-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, FILE STATUS)
- **Two Hammond recreation projects went to an assent vote — outcome unconfirmed.** The Hammond Aquatics and Recreation Centre (on the current Hammond Community Park site) and the Hammond Multi-Use Community Park (on the golf course site at 20818 Golf Lane) were put to residents under Loan Authorization Bylaws No. 8123-2026 and No. 8124-2026; the research confirms the votes were called, not their results (UNVERIFIED). If the golf-course project proceeds, the land-use conversation around 20818 Golf Lane changes — but as of this research it is a civic proposal, not a development site for sale. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/what-residents-need-know-oct-17-assent-vote-three-recreation-ready-projects
