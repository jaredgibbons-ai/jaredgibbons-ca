- **Secondary suites (the wider Chilliwack stock a condo buyer compares against):** under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, a secondary suite is an accessory dwelling unit of not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building — the lesser of 90 m² and 40% (s.2); only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot (s.4.06(1)); garden suites are capped at 100 m² gross floor area; and ADU parking is set at s.5.04 (1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite; 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame wherever the comparison stock or any parcel is in the reserve (Chilliwack is farm-adjacent throughout; agriculture is 67% of the city's land base):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. The City adds: an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, for placing fill or removing soil, for non-farm uses, and for subdivision or a boundary adjustment; a Farm Home Plate Development Permit (OCP D-4) is required when the Zoning Bylaw's Farm Home Plate regulations are not met. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR owned ONLY by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug — it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
