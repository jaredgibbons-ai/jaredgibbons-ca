- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Langley?** A principal residence permitted without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted ONLY inside the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a Langley farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and Township bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide an ALR farm parcel?** Not without the ALC's permission: depositing a strata plan IS a subdivision, and ALCA s.21(1) bars subdividing agricultural land unless the Commission permits it or the ALR General Regulation allows it (ALC Bulletin 09, s.6). Do not write "can never be stratified," and do not cite Bulletin 05 for stratification. (§3.)
- **Q: What income does a farm need to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's thresholds are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; applications are due by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31 after purchase.
