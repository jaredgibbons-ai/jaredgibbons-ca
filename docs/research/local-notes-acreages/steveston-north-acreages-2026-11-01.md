## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500** — Single Detached RS1/RS2 (Section 8.1; minimums in Section 2) and SSMUH zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L, RSM/XL as amended. A secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in RS1/RS2, and the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **OCP: City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000**, with the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4) guiding the Steveston planning area including this district's southern streets. https://Richmond.Ca/city-hall/strategies/sale.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Subdivision and servicing — VERIFIED ledger:** Richmond Subdivision Bylaw 8751 and Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 are VERIFIED (carry exactly). Subdivision runs against the Bylaw 8500 zone minimums and Bylaws 8751/8752; confirm the works required for a specific lot with the City. (Standing servicing ledger.)
- **Flood — Bylaw 8204 governs every lot here:** the district is on Lulu Island in the Fraser River floodplain, behind the City perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations; the system is designed for a 1:500 flood event and dikes are being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried for completeness; no Steveston North ALR parcel is documented in this file's research:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps: principal residence maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, and it is expressly NOT documented in this slug's own research — asserted nowhere in this file. No Steveston North parcel is carried as farm-class here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **BC strata block (where a strata purchase is made instead of land — the Heron redirect case):** Form B diligence (SPA s.59, within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (a Heron listing line "Rentals Allwd w/Restrctns" carries a rental-restriction element that is VOID for long-term rentals); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; 45+ does NOT convert). - **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. A $78,000 figure for $3.5M lives ONLY in a townhomes sibling elsewhere and is flagged wherever met as an OPEN reconciliation item, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
