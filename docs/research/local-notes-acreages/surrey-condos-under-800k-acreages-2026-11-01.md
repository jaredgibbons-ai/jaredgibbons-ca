## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Surrey plan frame:** OCP = Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is never cited as current. Zoning is Bylaw 12000. Verify a specific parcel's designation and zone with the City before pricing any use. (Standing Surrey frame.)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (the governing frame for any Surrey ALR parcel; none is documented in this band):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR — stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in this slug's research; it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Suites, SSMUH and short-term rentals (Surrey urban ground):** one secondary suite per dwelling unit as in §2, suite habitable area capped at 40% of the principal unit, no suite within a coach house or garden suite; SSMUH 3 / 4 / 6 units by lot size and bus-stop eligibility as in §2. On ALR land the ALC suite rule (inside the principal residence only) controls instead — the two regimes must not be mixed. Short-term rentals: renting by the owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as primary residence, tenants cannot run one, an apartment unit with one lock-off suite is a permitted type, and a business licence at $358 per year (plus any application fees) is required first — carried with the City's $350 proposal disclosed. A strata can still restrict STRs by bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Subdivision and servicing:** Surrey servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED on City pages. Streamside / riparian protection in Surrey is in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000, with setbacks by stream class — NO single riparian figure is stated in this file, ever. For an ALR parcel, ALC approval is required in addition to the City process.
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors (inputs + tier math shown):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. $500K → $8,000 ($2,000 + $6,000); $700K → $12,000; $800K cap → $14,000 ($2,000 + $12,000) — and at this band's own ceiling the worked example is the anchor itself: an $800,000 purchase, no exemption, pays $14,000; the standing ledger's FTHB figure at the cap is **$6,000** after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies (carried exactly as the ledger states it); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M fully residential → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000 wherever a $78,000 figure for $3.5M is met in a sibling elsewhere in the program (it lives in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m townhomes sibling; flagged there, not followed here). First-time buyers: full exemption requires fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective Apr 1, 2024), exempting the first $500,000, partial exemption only between $835,000 and $860,000, subject to the program's other conditions (principal residence; property 0.5 hectares or smaller — the acreage-relevant condition). Because this band caps at $800,000, a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in the band sits under the full-exemption value ceiling on price alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland is addressed by the CRA in a separate sheet and is usually taxable subject to limited exemptions.
