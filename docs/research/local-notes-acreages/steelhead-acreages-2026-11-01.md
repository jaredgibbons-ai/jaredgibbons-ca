## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020** — the rural zone set RU80 / RU36 / RU16 (each with an "s" secondary-dwelling variant) and RR7 / RR7s, with RU16 carrying a 1.6 ha minimum lot size per a City rezoning notice. Verify a specific parcel's exact zone with the City before pricing any use. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/notice-rezoning-bylaws-june-1-2026
- **OCP: Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026** (standing Mission frame; it replaces and repeals Bylaw 6350-2025). Live guides stating "June 1, 2026" are wrong — that error belongs ONLY to the three guide files named in the FILE STATUS block and is NOT carried as a defect of the standalone steelhead page. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026
- **Secondary suites and detached dwellings:** a Mission secondary suite is a self-contained living space inside a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; a suite is not a coach home or garden cottage (those are detached secondary dwellings); suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; all suites must be registered with the City, new suites go through the building permit process, and an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus utility fees applies. On rural lots a detached second dwelling generally requires the secondary-dwelling ("s") variant of the parcel's zone, which City material shows owners obtaining by rezoning (e.g., RU16 to RU16s). https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rsd25-4
- **SSMUH:** Mission has adopted the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation (incorporation Bylaw 6299-2024, Jul 17, 2024) — zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings as of December 7, 2023 are subject to it, subject to eligibility and servicing; **Mission's unit counts are held UNCONFIRMED in this program and Surrey's figures are never imported.** Steelhead's rural lots sit outside the urban serviced area, so whether SSMUH unit counts apply to a given Steelhead lot is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame wherever a Steelhead parcel is in the reserve:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in Steelhead's own research file — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Subdivision and servicing:** for a non-ALR Steelhead parcel, subdivision runs through the City against the zone minimums and the City's servicing requirements under the **DSCB (Development Servicing and Construction Bylaw) No. 5650-2017 (City source, title confirmed)**; for an ALR parcel, ALC approval is required in addition. Rural servicing reality (well/septic, §2) is part of any subdivision costing — never quote a servicing standard for a rural parcel from a listing or a summary.
- **Wells and septic as rules, not colour:** wells sit in the provincial GWELLS database, and sewerage systems must meet the Sewerage System Regulation's setbacks (including 30 m from a well unless professional advice is obtained) — subject-removal items on every Steelhead acreage deal, not closing-week surprises. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on acreage:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program (south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m) and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on farm and vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland is the caution: the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a SEPARATE info sheet and treats farmland sales as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions — a Steelhead farm parcel must therefore never be assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file; the contract must state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
