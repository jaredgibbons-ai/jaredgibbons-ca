## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Development Permit Area SD2 — the rule that defines this shore.** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 names "SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment)", Category "b" (protection of development from hazardous conditions): the waterfront area is subject to mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence, the ocean-frontage slopes are subject to erosion, and existing vegetation and tree cover on the slopes must be protected. Any building, rebuild or small multi-unit project on a bluff lot is designed inside SD2 first and the zoning second. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SD2 building rules.** No building may sit with the underside of a wooden floor system, or the top of a concrete slab for habitation, business or storage of goods damageable by floodwaters, lower than the Flood Construction Level (unless a lesser elevation is set by the provincial ministry); no building within 7.5 m of the natural boundary of the sea or within 7.5 m of the inboard toe of any dike; the guidelines require a restrictive covenant acknowledging the land may be subject to flooding — it runs with the land and will show on title; and development affected by hazardous conditions is subject to conditions set by a suitably qualified Professional Engineer. The site-specific Flood Construction Level for an individual lot: UNCONFIRMED. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** A single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules. The Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the PREVIOUS OCP, Bylaw No. 3950 — any file or page attributing a "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" to Tsawwassen is committing the known conflation error. Farm land in the plan is designated "Agriculture (A)". https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

- **ALR rules in full — carried for testing a claim (no in-area ALR confirmed, §2).** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALC residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and one additional residence of 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or less / 186 m² or less on parcels over 40 ha. A bluff lot sold by an individual owner will usually sit on the exempt side and a developer-assembled parcel on the taxable side, but the specific sale's treatment is a facts question — anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Property transfer tax on land.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — at bluff values the 3% band and the further 2% are the operative math. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy an acreage on English Bluff?** No — there is no acreage product on this shore. What trades is large fee-simple estate lots (RS3 subdivision minimum 4,000 m²) with houses on them, on a hazard-designated escarpment. A buyer who needs working or hobby acreage is looking at Delta's ALR farm ground, not the bluff. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is there any bare land for sale on the bluff?** No named bare-land or acreage development is verified here at all — the shore is built out in large fee-simple estate lots, so "buying land" means buying a lot with a house on it, or a teardown read through the SD2 rules. (§2 above)
- **Q: How big are the lots, really?** The RS3 zone that fits this shore sets a 4,000 m² minimum subdivision lot area, 30 m width and 30 m average depth — estate scale. Individual lot dimensions are read off title and the City's zoning maps, parcel by parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf
- **Q: Can I subdivide a bluff lot?** A subdivision must first clear the zone's minimums (RS3: 4,000 m² per new lot), then the City's subdivision and servicing standards (Bylaw 8288's adoption is UNCONFIRMED, §3), and on this shore the SD2 hazard permit area sits over everything — a Professional Engineer's conditions, the Flood Construction Level and a registered flood covenant. Treat subdivision here as an engineering file first and a survey file second. (§3 above)
- **Q: Do I own the beach in front of a waterfront lot?** No. The foreshore below the natural boundary is Crown land administered through FrontCounter BC; upland ownership does not include it, and stairs or docks need tenure checked, not assumed. https://www.delta.ca:443/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
- **Q: Can I clear trees to open up the view — or keep horses on a big lot?** Trees: carefully, if at all — the SD2 justification requires protection of existing vegetation and tree cover on the ocean-frontage slopes, and that vegetation is part of what holds the slope. Animals/farming: no agricultural use or farm operation is documented for this shore in the research, and the ground is an urban RS-zone estate fabric inside a hazard DPA — any keeping-of-animals claim for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until the parcel's zone section and Delta's bylaws are read for it. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Is the lot on municipal water and sewer, or well and septic?** Tsawwassen is within the City's municipal water, sewer and drainage systems; no source in this program documents well/septic on the bluff. Confirm the specific parcel's services and connection charges with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: What will SD2 do to my insurance and financing?** The hazard designation is in the City's own plan — mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence — so it will surface in any serious buyer's, lender's or insurer's search; confirm insurability and lender comfort early. Whether a specific insurer will write a specific bluff lot: UNCONFIRMED. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Can a lot take a suite or coach house under SSMUH?** Delta's SSMUH bylaws (8394 and 8395, June 24, 2024) allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide — but on this shore the SD2 hazard conditions are the deciding layer on top of that permission. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Does GST apply when I buy a bluff lot?** Per CRA GI-003, an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt, while developer/business sales and land subdivided into more than two parts are generally taxable — the seller and the land's history decide, not the price. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What does it cost to hold a bluff lot while I design a build?** Delta property taxes on the City's calendar (2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026), plus the geotechnical and engineering work SD2 requires before a permit — budget the engineering as a holding cost, not an afterthought. Parcel-specific carrying figures: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
