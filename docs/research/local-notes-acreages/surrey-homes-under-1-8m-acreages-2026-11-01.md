## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Surrey frame:** OCP = Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — never cite Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. SSMUH (Jul 8, 2024): a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Surrey's SSMUH changes also renamed the former RF-12 / RF-12C zones to the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone and RF-SD to R6 Semi-Detached — older material using RF-12 / RF-SD names is using pre-2024 zone names. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites and coach houses:** one secondary suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit (Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, as verified in the program's rules-gaps pass; no 90 m² cap was found in the current consolidated bylaw text). A coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² only on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR); smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house, and one extra parking space is required. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. Verified on the City's own Land Development / Servicing Agreement pages: the Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw, worked through the City's Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. SSMUH units do not create new lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside / riparian:** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, with different setbacks applied to various types of streams — read per parcel against its stream class in COSMOS; NO single riparian figure is stated in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only — a short-term rental is the renting of a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required before operating. **Licence figure CONFLICT disclosed:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposing the STR amendments put the licence at $350/yr plus fees — $358/yr is carried with the $350/yr + fees figure disclosed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R094.pdf
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — for Surrey's farm / ALR-edge parcels (Cloverdale-farms and Port Kells ground):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Whether any specific parcel is in the ALR: UNCONFIRMED — check parcel by parcel on the ALC map. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR — stated expressly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in this slug's own research; it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — a purchase under $1,800,000 meets only the first two tiers. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math, fully residential, no exemption unless stated): $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000 ($2,000 + 2% × $1,600,000 = $32,000); $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000. A $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program (the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m townhomes sibling — a different slug; this batch's surrey-homes-over-1-8m townhomes sibling follows $93,000) and is flagged wherever met as the OPEN summary-pass reconciliation item, never followed. First-time buyers: where all conditions are met, exempt on the first $500,000, with a full exemption requiring a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024) and a partial exemption between $835,000 and $860,000. Newly built home exemption: full threshold $1,100,000, complete elimination at $1,150,000 (effective April 1, 2024). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Mortgage loan insurance across this band (CMHC):** mortgage loan insurance is required when a homebuyer makes a down payment of less than 20%; homes $500,000 or less require a minimum 5% down payment; homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999 require 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% on any amount over $500,000; homes at $1,500,000 or more require a minimum 20% down payment with insurance not available — this band straddles all three tiers. CMHC premium rates are 0.6% to 4.5% of the total mortgage amount — the correct published range; no different premium range is documented for THIS slug (see §5). https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file, and the GST position of any specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and use are applied. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 is noted in-line in some sibling files — March 13 governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **BC strata block — the form part of this feed actually is:** Form B diligence (the strata paperwork a condo, half-duplex-strata or townhome purchase carries that a detached purchase does not); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions); REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission right on a developer sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners
- **Pad ground:** manufactured-home pad ground is NOT lot stock, land or acreage. Pad tenancy is stated in percentages only (2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount) and NO pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact in this file.
- **Foreclosure / court-ordered sale — where met:** BC court-ordered sales follow the settled Supreme Court process (Rules 21-7 / 13-5, Practice Direction PD-66, Form 60). Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry — the registry mapping for a Surrey parcel is UNVERIFIED in this file's sources (a New Westminster working assumption is carried only where the Surrey research itself states it, and it is not stated for this slug).
