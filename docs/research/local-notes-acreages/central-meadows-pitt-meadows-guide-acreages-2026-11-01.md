## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **City plan and zoning:** OCP "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Bylaw No. 2864, 2020 (adopted September 27, 2022), designates the Town Centre for high and medium density multifamily and mixed-use commercial/residential development. Houses sit under RS Large Lot Residential, R-3, R-4 and R-6 Urban Residential zones in Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 — RS minimum lot area 0.2 ha; R-3 up to 3 units, R-4 up to 4 units and R-6 up to five units (six where adaptable-housing requirements are met on lots close to frequent transit), each with a minimum lot area for subdivision of 1,215 sq m (a subdivision minimum only, not a development minimum on existing lots); RM-1 (townhouse) minimum lot area 0.4 ha. Any specific parcel's zone: UNVERIFIED per parcel. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Suites and SSMUH:** one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit, 33 to 90 sq m, inside the same building, where the zone permits it; garden suites are one per lot at 33 to 90 sq m in the zones listed in the City garden suite guide (including RS), and a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. Under provincial SSMUH applied citywide: lots 280 sq m or less up to 3 units, lots over 280 sq m up to 4 units, and lots over 280 sq m within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units; SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986 was adopted June 25, 2024. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Short-term rentals PROHIBITED:** short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in the City's Zoning Bylaw, per the City's zoning bylaw amendments staff report. Do not price any Central Meadows home on short-term-rental income. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Subdivision / servicing — number UNCONFIRMED, process documented:** Pitt Meadows' subdivision-servicing bylaw number is HELD UNCONFIRMED in the standing ledger — no number is asserted in this file. The process position carried from the siblings: subdivision turns on the zone minimums above (1,215 sq m for R-3/R-4/R-6; 0.2 ha RS; 0.4 ha RM-1) and City approval for the specific parcel; parcel-level servicing requirements are confirmed with the City. A 2026 flat utility fee amount for this area: UNVERIFIED.
- **Flood / dikes:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the floodplain for the Fraser River and, to a smaller degree, the Pitt River — this is the figure these notes retire to. The City is almost entirely protected by 60 km of dikes, plus ditches, pump stations and flood boxes; Central Meadows is in drainage Area 3 (Kennedy, including McKechnie) of the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District. The guide page's "over 85%" Financial Plan figure is a live conflict between two City sources (§5), never followed here. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — ALR ground exists citywide and farm-adjacent questions arise on this ground, so the full frame is carried:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan IS a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 deposit = subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — never the shorthand "cannot be stratified." Residence caps as the ALC states them: a principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite is permitted ONLY when located within the principal residence; one additional residence is permitted up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, and building more than the permitted number, or larger than the size limits, requires ALC approval. City zoning and the ALC frame both apply where the parcel is in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by the horse-properties page only); it is NOT documented in this slug's research. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Quota and board items — UNVERIFIED in every particular in THIS file:** no marketing-board source was opened in this slug's research, so no quota or board position is stated for any Pitt Meadows operation.
- **BC strata block (for any strata lot met, including the cross-referenced townhome stock):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **City of Pitt Meadows property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 (adopted May 5, 2026), with a 5% penalty added after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026; the bylaw also sets a separate residential (Class 1) drainage rate of $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value alongside the general municipal rate of $2.8438 per $1,000. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's personal-use land is generally exempt, with taxable cases including land used primarily in a business, sold in the course of a business, or subdivided into more than two parts. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — CRA governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
