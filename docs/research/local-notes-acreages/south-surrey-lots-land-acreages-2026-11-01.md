## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning + OCP (Surrey):** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — NEVER 18020 as current; Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, with a lot's zone checkable on the City's COSMOS map. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf **White Rock:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2506 (adopted Jun 27, 2024, incorporating SSMUH: up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 over 280 m², up to 6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop). The old-to-new Surrey zone mapping (RF→R3, RH→R1, RQ→R2, one-acre→RA), the White Rock RS-1 minimum-lot dimensions (742 m², 18 m wide, 30.5 m deep) and the Surrey RA minimums (4,050 m², 50 m wide, 60 m deep, Part 12) are carried as site copy in the research — not re-read from either bylaw in this pass. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html (Program rules file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **How many homes (Surrey SSMUH, as the page's copy states it, Jul 8, 2024 incorporation):** inside the urban containment boundary, lots under 4,050 m² may hold 3 units (280 m² or less) or 4 units (larger), up to 6 for some lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; a triplex/fourplex (houseplex) is allowed only in the R3 zone, on a lot with a lane or two road frontages, with no secondary suite (site copy). A secondary suite is capped at **40%** of the principal dwelling's habitable area — the old 90 m² cap is NOT current, never state it; a coach/garden suite runs 35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA/R2/R2-O/R3 lots. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html (Program rules file above.)
- **Subdivision process (Surrey, site copy — UNVERIFIED beyond it):** City planner review, a Preliminary Layout Approval (PLA) letter listing conditions, 12 months to meet them (extension for a fee), conditions that can include road dedication, covenants, tree permits and development cost charges, and a registered land surveyor's final plan to the Land Title Office. No City subdivision page was opened in this pass. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the east-side acreage ground carries ALR parcels:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban; ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 if the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by HORSE-PROPERTIES); it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to it. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Servicing:** Surrey Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 8830 (1986) VERIFIED. The page states that if a new lot would use a well or septic field, Fraser Health testing must be done and the application cannot proceed if standards are not met, and that some acreage land is unserviced (site copy). Parcel-level servicing for any specific lot: UNVERIFIED. Well records sit in GWELLS. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration

- **Short-term rentals:** Surrey — permitted, owner-only hosting from the principal residence, City business licence **$358/yr carried, with the City's $350/yr proposal disclosed**. White Rock's STR rule: UNVERIFIED in this pass. (Program rules file above.)
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — anchors with tier math:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example); a sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland carries the CRA's separate caution. The page's own GST/owner-builder copy was NOT verified against CRA or BC Housing material in the research pass — anything finer than GI-003 (including owner-builder authorization detail) is UNCONFIRMED here and is a question for the buyer's own advisors. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — housing, not bare land:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 noted in-line in some sources — March 13 governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phase-out to $1.5M; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many lots are for sale in South Surrey right now?** UNVERIFIED as a statistic — SnapStats publishes no lots/land category, so no land count or median exists in the source. The live page is a land filter over the whole community; price land off land sales, not house medians. (§1.)
- **Q: How do I find a lot's zone?** On the Surrey side, look the lot up on the City's COSMOS map and read its zone in Zoning Bylaw 12000; White Rock lots sit under White Rock Zoning Bylaw No. 2506. The zone on the specific lot decides which unit counts even apply. (§3.)
- **Q: How many homes can go on a South Surrey lot?** In Surrey, inside the urban containment boundary: 3 units on a lot of 280 m² or less, 4 on a larger lot under 4,050 m², up to 6 for some lots within 400 m of frequent bus service; a houseplex is R3-only with a lane or two frontages and no suite. A suite is capped at 40% of the principal dwelling; a coach/garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA/R2/R2-O/R3). (§3.)
- **Q: Can I stratify an ALR acreage lot into bare-land strata lots?** Not without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; ALCA s.21(1)). It is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and Bulletin 05 is not the source for it. (§3.)
- **Q: What if the lot has no city sewer or water?** The page's copy states a new lot on a well or septic field needs Fraser Health testing before a subdivision can proceed; parcel-level servicing is UNVERIFIED in this research — confirm services with the City and Fraser Health before writing. (§3.)
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same as a freehold lot?** No. A bare-land strata lot is a strata lot (Form B, fees, bylaws) whose lot is essentially the building lot itself — Sea Ridge Bays is the South Surrey case, documented ONCE in its lots homes and cross-referenced in §2. A fee-simple lot has no strata at all. (§2.)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on a lot purchase?** The anchors in §3 — e.g., $1.0M→$18,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 including the over-$3M tier. (§3.)
- **Q: Is GST charged on a vacant lot?** At GI-003 level: personal-use land sold by an individual is generally exempt; business sales and parcels split into more than two lots can be taxable; farmland carries the CRA's separate caution. The FTHB GST rebate is for new HOUSING, not bare land. Confirm with your accountant before writing. (§3.)
- **Q: How long does a Surrey subdivision take?** The page's copy states a PLA carries 12 months to meet its conditions (extension for a fee) — carried as site copy, UNVERIFIED beyond it, because no City subdivision page was opened in this pass. Set financing against the PLA condition list for the specific file, not a general timeline. (§3.)
