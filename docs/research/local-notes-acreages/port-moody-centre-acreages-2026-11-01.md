## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP and zoning:** Port Moody OCP Bylaw 2026, No. 3522 (adopted April 28, 2026; repeals No. 2955), Chapter 14, covers this area in Section 14.6 (Moody Centre — including the Moody Centre Station Transit-Oriented Area, the South of St. Johns Street Special Study Area, Westport Village, the Heritage Commercial District and the Oceanfront District) and Section 14.5 (Inlet Centre and Coronation Park). Zoning Bylaw, 2018, No. 2937 puts single detached homes in RS zones, multi-residential homes in RT and RM zones, and mixed-use development in TC and CRM zones; apartment and mixed-use buildings are concentrated in the Moody Centre and Inlet Centre transit-oriented areas. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733 and https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- **SSMUH / small-lot potential, as the City states it:** the transit-oriented areas are **exempt** from small-scale multi-unit housing rules — an RS lot inside a TOA does not gain SSMUH units, while RS lots outside them can add units. In Moody Centre, RS1 lots in OCP Single Family–Low Density areas can be rezoned to RS1-S for small lots of at least 9 m width and 325 m² area — a rezoning route, not an as-of-right split. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/ and https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
