## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** — farm zones in Section 6; minimum lot areas, lot widths and urban-zone minimums are UNVERIFIED in this slug's pass, so no minimum is quoted from this file — confirm the specific parcel's zone figures on the City record. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 ("Chilliwack 2050")**, including the Schedule B plans listed in §1 and Schedule D development permit areas: D-1 Drinking Water Supply, D-2 Hillside Environment, D-3 Riparian Area, D-4 Farm Home Plate, D-5 Hillside Development, D-6 Downtown, D-7 Mixed-Use Areas, D-8 Residential, D-9 Commercial, D-10 Industrial. Which DPAs apply to a given lot is a per-parcel check. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame wherever Chilliwack land is in the reserve (and the city is farm-adjacent throughout):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. The City adds: an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, for placing fill or removing soil, for non-farm uses, and for subdivision or a boundary adjustment, with ALC applications referred to the City for review; a Farm Home Plate Development Permit (OCP D-4) is required when the Zoning Bylaw's Farm Home Plate regulations are not met. The Agricultural Area Plan was created as part of the OCP in 2012 in partnership with the Chilliwack Agricultural Commission, and the Agricultural and Rural Advisory Committee (ARAC) monitors its implementation. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR owned ONLY by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug — it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **SSMUH — ESTABLISHED:** Chilliwack's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment is Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, which amended Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 and was adopted June 18, 2024, as recorded in Section 14's amendment record in the consolidated bylaw; the City's signed staff report describes it as allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, item 6.)
- **Suites and accessory dwellings:** confirmed in this program's rules-gaps verification (Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, ss. 2, 4.06, 5.04) — a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling, one accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot, garden suites are capped at 100 m², and the bylaw's parking rules sit at s. 5.04. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How much of Chilliwack is agricultural land?** The City states agriculture accounts for 67% of the City's total land base and 29% of its economic activity, and its OCP carries an Agricultural Area Plan for that land — which is why the ALR check comes first on almost any large parcel. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Q: How do I check if a Chilliwack lot is in the ALR?** The City directs owners to the City Webmap; ALR properties are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and regulations, and an ALC application may be required for a new house, fill or soil removal, a non-farm use, or a subdivision or boundary adjustment. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Q: Can a serviced Chilliwack lot carry more than one home?** The SSMUH amendment, Bylaw No. 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024), allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing, per the City's staff-report description — eligibility is parcel-specific and confirmed with the City. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, item 6.)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a large Chilliwack parcel?** Subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer / Preliminary Layout Approval process against the Zoning Bylaw and OCP — and the zone minimums, DCCs and servicing requirements are all UNVERIFIED in this file, so get the City's position on the specific parcel in writing before subjects come off. The servicing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED (§3) — do not let anyone quote "Bylaw 3055" to you as verified from this program. For ALR land, ALC approval is required in addition. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=653
- **Q: What permit areas should I check on a hillside lot?** The OCP's Schedule D lists D-2 Hillside Environment and D-5 Hillside Development, along with D-1 Drinking Water Supply and D-3 Riparian Area; which areas apply to a specific lot is a per-parcel check with the City — and the current OCP list does not use the live page's "DPA2 (Hillside and Upland)" name (§5). https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Q: Are the market numbers in this file land prices?** No — there are no market numbers in this file. Chilliwack is a CADREB market SnapStats does not cover, CADREB publishes board-wide only with no land category, and land-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED here; pricing support comes from Paragon comparables. https://www.cadreb.com/market/
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same diligence as a freehold lot?** No — a bare-land strata purchase adds the BC strata block in §3 (Form B, depreciation reports, CRF), and its community is documented in its lots-and-land home file, not here. A home on a rented pad is NOT lot stock at all.
