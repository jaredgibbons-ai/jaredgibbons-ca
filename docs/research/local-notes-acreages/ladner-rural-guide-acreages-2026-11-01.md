## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Official Community Plan:** the Delta Official Community Plan, **Bylaw No. 8400 (2024)** — a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules; farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" and its Agriculture policies apply to Delta's agricultural land. Any "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" attribution is an ERROR wherever met (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous Bylaw No. 3950, 1985). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted:** City of Delta pages cite the Zoning Bylaw as No. 7600, 2017 (the A1 zone sheet below is a City zoning document), while an internal consolidated title in this program's research says "8400, 2025" — the two collide, and this file asserts neither number as settled. The **A1 Agriculture zone** is the zone intended for farming, breeding pets, kennels and related uses. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **A1 dwelling rules (the housing this ground actually allows):** farming is a principal use, and accessory uses include a farm house accessory to farming, one secondary suite accessory to a farm house, an accessory farm dwelling unit, migrant farm worker housing, and equestrian facilities to a maximum of 40 permanent stalls accessory to farming. One farm house per lot is permitted, maximum total floor area **500 m²**, on a farm home plate of at most **3,600 m²** (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate). One accessory farm dwelling unit per lot — maximum **90 m²** on lots of 40 ha or less, **186 m²** on lots over 40 ha — located on the same farm home plate as the farm house, with ALC approval required for more than one. An additional farm house (180 m² maximum on lots under 8 ha, 233 m² on lots of 8 ha or more) requires a Professional Agrologist's report supporting its necessity and written ALC approval. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Subdivision of farm land (A1 s. 10.10.8):** subdivision is not permitted except where it complies with the Official Community Plan Bylaw and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable; the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is **8 ha**, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. The 8 ha figure is a subdivision floor for NEW lots, not a statement that every existing farm parcel meets it. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **SSMUH (Delta):** Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024, allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies — the A1/ALR frame in this section, not SSMUH, governs farm parcels. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Subdivision and servicing — DSSS Bylaw No. 8288 (2024), adoption UNCONFIRMED:** cited in this program from a civicweb / January 2024 council report — final adoption and parcel-level standards UNCONFIRMED in this file; confirm with the City for the specific parcel. - **ALR RULES IN FULL (this is ALR farm ground — this block governs most of this ground):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). A specific parcel's ALR status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address, with official confirmation from the ALC's Mapping and GIS team. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase.
