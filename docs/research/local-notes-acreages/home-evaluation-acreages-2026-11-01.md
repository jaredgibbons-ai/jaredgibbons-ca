## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **The regulator's framework behind the service:** in British Columbia, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); a licensed real estate professional in BC must give consumers information about commissions and fees, must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party. Disclosure must happen at the outset of dealings; dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. A CMA request through this page is, on the page's own text, a request for an evaluation — the page does not state when agency disclosure occurs in Jared's process: UNVERIFIED. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers

- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — the frame an acreage seller/buyer brings to an evaluation conversation:** 1% first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000.
