- **"Nearly half the city's employment space" is a study geography, not this sub-area's boundary:** the City's employment lands work puts industrial space at nearly half of Burnaby's employment space city-wide, with the Big Bend sub-area accounting for much of it on the City's assessment geography — a figure a seller may quote as if it measured the MLS® sub-area; it does not, and the research flags the distinction itself. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/burnaby-2050-planning-our-city-together/burnaby-2050-policy-guide-share-your-feedback/economic-development-employment-and-industrial-lands
- **A zero-sale month is the normal signal here, not a crash:** 2 detached and 3 attached listings with 0 solds in September 2026 is a thin parcel market doing what it does — the research carries its own small-sample warning, and any "Big Bend price drop" claim built on that month is built on nothing. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report; research file above.)
- **The dyke is also the recreation system:** Burnaby Fraser Foreshore Park sits on the river within the foreshore, and the City's dyke-upgrade program runs along the foreshore reaches within and beside the park — for river-adjacent parcels, the same structure that sets the flood position also sets the amenity frontage. http://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewers/flood-prevention
