## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), carried verbatim with sourcing stated:** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, states that the disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Representation at an open house:** a licensed real estate professional in BC must work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party to the transaction. What that means in practice: chatting factually with the host at an open house does not by itself make you their client — but once you start sharing your motivation, budget or needs with a licensee who represents the seller, the disclosure rules engage. Use the open house for facts; save your story for your own agent. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers

- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,000,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $800,000 ($16,000) = **$18,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling in this program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. The home-based exemptions have land routes — FTHB vacant-lot build at $835,000 or less (partial under $860,000), building and occupying by the first anniversary; Newly Built Home Exemption $1,100,000 / $1,150,000 thresholds. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
