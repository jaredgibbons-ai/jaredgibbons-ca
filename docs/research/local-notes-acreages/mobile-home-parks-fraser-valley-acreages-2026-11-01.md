## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Governing law (the topic rule): the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA).** Manufactured homes are built off-site and transported to a final location for installation, and the MHPTA covers most manufactured home tenancies. A pad tenancy is NOT governed by the Strata Property Act: park rules are not strata bylaws, and there is no Form B, no depreciation report and no contingency reserve fund for a pad-rental site. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Pad-rent increases — PERCENTAGES ONLY, verified live in this slug's research:** a park landlord may increase rent only once every 12 months, only with 3 full months' written notice, and only up to the yearly limit; the landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion of a prior year's limit. **The 2026 limit for manufactured home site tenancies is 2.3% plus a proportional amount, and the 2027 limit is 2.2% plus a proportional amount**; the proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park. **NO pad-rent dollar figure is published in this file as fact, ever** — the ranges on sibling pages of the live site ($650–$950 Langley, $550–$950 Chilliwack, $600–$900 on a further Langley page) are UNVERIFIED site copy with no official source, flagged and never adopted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale (MHPTA s.28):** assignment permanently transfers the site tenancy to the buyer; under s.28(1)(b) a tenant may assign only with the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an order of the director, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it — the Act's word is "director," never "arbitrator"; under s.28(3) the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting to an assignment, and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Park closure / change of use (MHPTA s.42):** where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to another use, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date of the notice; a tenant may end the tenancy early on at least 10 days' written notice, the dispute window for a 12-month notice is 15 days, and additional compensation of the assessed value of the home minus the $20,000 may be ordered where the tenant could not obtain the permits needed to move the home or could not move it to another site within a reasonable distance, with a further $5,000 or 12 months' pad rent, whichever is greater, payable if the landlord does not proceed with the conversion purpose within a reasonable period. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Municipal frames, stated PER municipality and never blended (zoning/servicing follow the park's own municipality):** Township of Langley — Zoning Bylaw 2500; SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024; NO 6-plex tier; suite cap 120 m²; coach house = fee-simple second unit under s.102, NEVER strata; R-CL s.403 = 220 m² min LOT / 464.5 m² max LOT area, the "355 m²" figure NOWHERE in the bylaw); servicing under **Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED**. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: If I buy a home in one of these parks, do I own the land?** No — in a pad-rental park you own the home and rent the site under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement governed by the MHPTA. The exceptions prove the rule: in a bare-land strata community you own the lot (documented once in its lots-and-land home file), and in a co-operative such as Pioneer Park (Aldergrove) the land is held through the co-op and you buy shares. (§2.)
- **Q: How many mobile home parks does this page list?** 73 entries in page order, covering Abbotsford, Agassiz/Kent, Chilliwack, Coquitlam, Cultus Lake, Soowahlie, Hope, Langley, Maple Ridge, Mission, Rosedale, Ruskin and Surrey — with Cedar Grove and Mountainview Estates in Chilliwack each listed twice, so the distinct count is lower, and the list is site copy only because no official park registry exists. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.html
- **Q: How much is pad rent in Fraser Valley parks?** No figure is stated as fact in this file: this page publishes no pad rent for any park, no official source publishes a pad-rent figure, average or range, and the ranges on sibling pages of the live site are UNVERIFIED site copy (§3). The figure that matters is the specific park's current rent in its written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement. (§3.)
- **Q: How much can a park raise pad rent each year?** Up to the MHPTA limit for manufactured home site tenancies: **2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027**, on 3 full months' written notice, once every 12 months, with no rounding up and no carry-forward of an unused portion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What transfers when I buy a park home — do I need the park's consent?** Yes: under MHPTA s.28 the site tenancy assigns only with the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, an order of the director, or an authorizing tenancy agreement); the owner cannot charge a consent fee, and the pad rent and the next increase date carry over unchanged. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: What happens if the park is sold or converted to another use?** Under MHPTA s.42 the landlord must first have all required government permits and approvals, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 on or before the effective date; the tenant may leave early on 10 days' notice, has 15 days to dispute, and may be eligible for additional compensation of the assessed value minus $20,000 through dispute resolution, plus a further $5,000 or 12 months' pad rent (whichever is greater) if the landlord does not proceed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
