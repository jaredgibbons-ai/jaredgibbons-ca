## 2. THE ACREAGE ITSELF

**This file carries NO acreage roster of its own — stated plainly, as required for a TOPIC file. No named acreage subdivision, bare-land strata acreage community or farm parcel is counted here, because a plans guide has no ground of its own. Verified named acreage developments counted here: 0. What §2 documents is the acreage framework the plans create — the Agriculture (A) designation, the A1 zone and the ALR layer — which is the substance an acreage buyer is actually buying into anywhere in Delta. Asserted ONLY as this file's own research documents it.**

- **The designation layer:** farm land in Delta is designated Agriculture (A) in OCP Bylaw 8400 (2024) — s. 1.5.1 sets the designation's purpose as maintaining agricultural lands, applied on Map 2. The OCP is a single consolidated plan: there is NO Schedule D.1 and no Tsawwassen or Ladner Area Plan in Bylaw 8400; those area plans were schedules of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, which Bylaw 8400 superseded. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **The zone layer (A1):** in the A1 Agriculture zone (s. 10.10.8, as cited in this program's Delta research), subdivision is permitted only where it complies with the OCP and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is **8 ha**. That single figure is the acreage-subdivision reality for Delta farm ground: there is no small-lot farm subdivision product under the current plans. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **The residential zone ladder the plans also create (for contrast):** RS1, RS2, RS3, RD2 and RD3 at 4,000 m²; RS4 at 610 m²; RS5 at 550 m² under the Single Family Residential designation or 460 m² otherwise; RS6 at 390 m²; RS7 at 335 m²; RD1 at 836 m²; RD4 at 504 m²; there is no RD5 zone. The 4,000 m² low-density zones are house lots, not acreages — no parcel size is presented here as an acreage size beyond the A1 rule above. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **ALR vs non-ALR split:** no Delta-wide split is stated in this file's research: UNCONFIRMED. The ALR layer applies on top of the OCP designation and zoning where land is in the reserve — the Agricultural Land Commission generally allows no more than one residence per ALR parcel, with a principal residence built without an ALC application capped at 500 m² total floor area and one additional detached residence permitted up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on larger parcels. A specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the ALC maps. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Tenure — strata vs freehold under the plans:** both tenures exist across Delta; no Delta-wide tenure split is stated in this file: UNVERIFIED (no official source read publishes one for this topic). On ALR parcels the strata form is barred outright (§3). DOCUMENT-ONCE discipline: bare-land strata acreage communities are documented ONCE in their lots-and-land home files — none is re-documented or re-counted here.
- **Serviced vs unserviced:** the plans topic states no parcel-level servicing position; the Delta servicing bylaw number is HELD UNCONFIRMED (§3), so servicing is documented as a process (Approving Officer, zone minimums, works-and-services conditions), never as a quoted standard.
- **Hobby vs working farm:** the plans do not distinguish them — the Agriculture (A) designation and A1 zone govern both, and the working-farm difference is made by BC Assessment farm classification (§3), not by the OCP. Farm operations are described in process terms only in this program; a generic feed is NEVER presented as a confirmed operation, and no marketing-board source was opened in this slug's research, so quota/board items stay UNVERIFIED. These are plan directions, documented AS SUCH — never as inventory. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Work in the right order — the three layers:** OCP designation first (s. 1.5.1 / Map 2), then the parcel's zone on the zoning maps, then any Development Permit Area in Appendix B — a use can pass one layer and fail the next. The OCP's Appendix B carries the DPAs and Guidelines, including the Tsawwassen Urban Centre DPA guidelines and the hazard DPAs SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment) and SD3 Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Zoning — NUMBER CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted:** the research states the OCP's designations are implemented through Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, with zoning assigned parcel by parcel on the zoning maps hosted on maps.delta.ca. Program-wide, City pages cite No. 7600, 2017, while an internal title says "8400, 2025" — the collision is flagged here and neither number is asserted as settled; a ruling is needed. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **SSMUH: Delta Bylaws 8394 + 8395, adopted June 24, 2024** — the City has introduced the provincially required zoning updates that allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots; the City frames SSMUH as ground-oriented housing between single-detached homes and condominiums, intended to be attainable for middle-income families. SSMUH does not apply as an acreage lever on A1 farm ground, where the ALC layer governs. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for ALR parcels anywhere in Delta:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR — stated expressly: the flat form is NOT documented in this slug's research; it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer against the zone minimums (A1: 8 ha) and works-and-services conditions, plus ALC approval where the parcel is in the ALR. HELD UNCONFIRMED: Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw 8288 (2024) adoption — sibling lots files asserting "8288, 2024" in governing terms are noted as conflicts wherever met and the number is never asserted here; the process is documented instead.
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, not followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

Pad ground is NOT lot stock / land / acreage; manufactured-home pad tenancy percentages only (2026 = 2.3% + proportionate; 2027 = 2.2% + proportionate) and NO pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is Delta's current Official Community Plan?** Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future", adopted July 8, 2024 — a single consolidated plan whose Land Use Plan (s. 1.5, Map 2) sets the designations for the whole city. Delta was the first municipality in the region to complete its OCP review and update. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Is there still a Tsawwassen Area Plan or a Ladner Area Plan?** No. Those were schedules of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950 (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1). Bylaw 8400 superseded them — there is no Schedule D.1 in the current OCP, and any citation of one is an error. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: How do I find what can be built on a Delta acreage?** In order: the OCP designation (s. 1.5.1 / Map 2), then the parcel's zone on the zoning maps, then whether a Development Permit Area applies — and, if the parcel is in the ALR, the ALC layer on top of all three. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: What does the Agriculture (A) designation mean for a farm purchase?** It is the OCP designation applied on Map 2 to maintain agricultural lands. It does not settle what the ALC allows — residence caps (500 m² principal; suite inside it only; additional residence 90 m² / 186 m²) come from the ALC rules where the land is in the reserve. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Delta farm parcel into acreage lots?** Only within tight limits: a new A1 lot needs a minimum of 8 ha, OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable. There is no small-lot farm subdivision route in the current plans. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can an ALR acreage be stratified or split for family?** Stratified: no — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Split: only with ALC approval and within the A1 8 ha minimum. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What did SSMUH change on Delta lots?** The provincially required updates (Bylaws 8394 + 8395, June 24, 2024) allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots — but servicing, flood and setback rules still apply parcel by parcel, and SSMUH is not an acreage lever on A1 ground. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: What farm income keeps farm class?** All three tiers: $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Do I pay GST on vacant Delta land?** Only the GI-003 position is stated here: most individual personal-use sales are exempt, with taxable exceptions (business use, course of business, subdivision into more than two parts). Finer treatment for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which zoning bylaw number should my documents cite?** The number is CONTESTED in the City's own materials (No. 7600, 2017 on City pages vs an internal title "8400, 2025") — flagged in this file, asserted neither way; confirm the current citation with the City before relying on it in a contract.
