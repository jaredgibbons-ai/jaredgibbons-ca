- **ALR — a live edge-parcel check on this ground:** farm use leases on some Tilbury-area parcels appear in City material, and ALR status for a specific parcel is a check through the Agricultural Land Commission. If a parcel is in the ALR: subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence, and an additional residence capped at 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha) — and an ALR or farm-leased parcel does not price like a clean freehold industrial lot. Farm class, where a parcel's farm use is in issue, runs on the full three-tier test — $2,500 (0.8–4 ha operation), $2,500 plus 5% of actual value over 4 ha (over 4 ha), $10,000 (under 0.8 ha), B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31; a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata industrial purchases — the BC strata block, industrial application:** industrial strata ownership follows the Strata Property Act framework (owners own their strata lots and together own common property as a strata corporation). For strata industrial units, read the strata's depreciation report, insurance and any environmental conditions the way you would a residential strata — depreciation reports run on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, and the minimum CRF contribution is 10% since November 1, 2023 — but add a Phase I environmental site assessment for the unit's and the complex's prior uses. Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 and only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2) — neither point is a Tilbury pricing factor on industrial bays, and neither is dressed up as one here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Property transfer tax — residential schedule stated, commercial treatment UNCONFIRMED for Tilbury:** the residential PTT schedule is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated, RESIDENTIAL basis only: a $1,800,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,600,000 ($32,000) = **$34,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** (tier math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000) — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. **Tilbury caution: most Tilbury parcels are non-residential industrial land, and the PTT treatment of a specific commercial/industrial parcel is UNCONFIRMED in this file where the research is silent — the residential worked example is an anchor for mixed-use comparison only, never a Tilbury quote; confirm the parcel's PTT position before pricing.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
