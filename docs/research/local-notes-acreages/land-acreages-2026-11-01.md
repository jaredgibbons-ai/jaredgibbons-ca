## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **No single municipality's rule governs this page — stated first.** Zoning, servicing and development rules follow the parcel's own jurisdiction (Township of Langley, City of Langley, Surrey, Abbotsford, Mission, Squamish, Gibsons/Sunshine Coast, FVRD and others all appear in this feed's geography). Confirm the rule where the parcel actually sits; nothing below blends jurisdictions.
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — wherever ALR ground exists or ground is farm-adjacent:** subdivision of ALR land requires Agricultural Land Commission permission, apart from the limited cases in the Agricultural Land Reserve General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission. ALR residences cannot be stratified without ALC permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a **permission rule, never an absolute ban**, and Bulletin 05 is never cited for it. Caps: one principal residence, 500 m² or less total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence without an application at 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, or 186 m² or less on larger parcels, where local bylaws also permit it; local governments may regulate further but cannot be more permissive than the ALC. A parcel's ALR status is checked on ALC maps by PID or civic address. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha if over 4 ha; $10,000 if under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by HORSE-PROPERTIES); it is **not documented in this slug's research** and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked examples with inputs + tier math:** general rates are 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% of the portion over $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000, on fair market value at registration unless an exemption applies. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies to a qualifying home purchase); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). - **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Worked example at GI-003 level only: an individual selling a personal-use parcel they never used in a business and never subdivided — generally exempt; the same parcel sold by a developer, or as a newly subdivided lot in the course of a business — generally taxable. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is this page only Fraser Valley land?** No — that is the page's title, not its feed. The first cards include Quesnel, Fort St. John, Gibsons and Squamish alongside Langley, Surrey and Mission parcels, every card labelled "Land" and "Status: Active." https://jaredgibbons.ca/land.html
- **Q: How many homes can I build on ALR land?** Generally one principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite ONLY inside that residence, and at most one additional detached residence at 90 m² or less (40 ha or smaller) or 186 m² or less (larger parcels), where local bylaws also permit it; more requires ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify an ALR parcel into bare-land strata lots?** Not without ALC permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). It is a permission rule, not an absolute ban. (§3.)
- **Q: How do I check whether a lot is in the ALR?** On the ALC's maps, by PID or civic address; subdivision of reserve land requires ALC permission apart from limited approving-officer cases in the ALR General Regulation. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: What PTT applies on a land purchase?** The tier math in §3 — for example $800K→$14,000, $1.5M→$28,000, $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 including the over-$3M tier — on fair market value at registration unless an exemption applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Does GST apply to vacant land?** At GI-003 level only: an individual's personal-use land sale is generally exempt; business/developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. For any specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED here — confirm before writing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What were land prices in September 2026?** UNVERIFIED as a market figure — SnapStats publishes no land category, so no land median, band or share is stated. Feed-card prices at reading are a snapshot, not a statistic. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report.)
- **Q: Can I use the first-time-buyer GST rebate on bare land?** No — the Bill C-4 rebate (Royal Assent March 13, 2026) is for new HOUSING, not bare land itself; the New Housing Rebate is likewise housing-only. HBP ($60,000) and FHSA ($40,000/$8,000) are savings vehicles, not a land exemption. (§3.)
- **Q: Why can't I price a lot off the nearest city's detached median?** Because a detached median describes houses in that community, not parcels, and this feed spans many communities — price a parcel off its own zoning, ALR status, servicing and comparables. (§§1, 3.)
- **Q: What must I verify off the listing card?** Zoning, servicing (water/sewer vs well/septic), ALR status and development claims — listing text is listing-agent copy until checked against the in-force municipal zoning and, for ALR parcels, the ALC rules for that PID. https://jaredgibbons.ca/land.html
