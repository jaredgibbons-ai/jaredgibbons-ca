- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for ALR parcels in this topic:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; one additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, total floor area 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and City of Maple Ridge bylaws must also permit it. Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. One hectare = 2.471 acres. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.nrcan.gc.ca/maps-tools-publications/tools/geodetic-reference-systems/converting-units-measurement/10798
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income requirements at farm gate prices — $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha; farm applications must be submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Stated explicitly for this topic: the flat form is NOT documented in the maple-ridge-farms research file — the three-tier form above is what that file documents; the flat error is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. - **Q: Can I irrigate from a well on the parcel?** Only with a water licence: under the Water Sustainability Act, groundwater used for non-domestic purposes such as irrigation requires a licence and payment of water fees and rentals before lawful use; domestic groundwater use does not require authorization. Confirm the licence position for the parcel before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- **Q: Is the farm serviced?** Assume onsite sewage until proven otherwise: a building with indoor plumbing not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed / installed / inspected only by an Authorized Person. The City's own A1–A3 checklist also records flood plain status and geotechnical items for agricultural properties — run those checks on any lowland parcel. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/a1-a2-a3-agr-zoning-checklist202103081624103844
