## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **No single city's frame is imported — stated first.** This file is provincial in scope. Per-jurisdiction frames are stated AS such for their own ground only: for example, Chilliwack's Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 defines POULTRY as any domesticated fowl including chickens, turkeys, ducks, geese and swans, and lists General Agriculture as a permitted principal use in its AL, AU and AS zones (carried from the 55-plus sibling's opened bylaw source) — that is a Chilliwack position, not a BC rule. Township of Langley, Abbotsford and other municipal frames likewise apply only on their own ground, per their own area files. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (farm files carry the full block):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a PERMISSION rule, never an absolute ban, and ALC Bulletin 05 is never cited for it. Residence caps: one principal residence, maximum 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; at most one additional residence of 90 m² on parcels ≤40 ha or 186 m² on parcels >40 ha; local bylaws may restrict further but not be more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if less than 0.8 ha. Applications by the legislated October 31 deadline for the next assessment/tax year; late applications are not accepted; a buyer continuing to farm must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by HORSE-PROPERTIES) — it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Supply management and quota — board facts ONLY from board sources actually opened in this program (sibling-opened, named in FILE STATUS), with the batch-13 position stated alongside:** BC poultry production is supply-managed. For chicken, quota is the amount of chicken, expressed in kilograms of live weight, that the BC Chicken Marketing Board allots to a grower; the board's General Orders name mainstream and specialty quota classes, treat transfer of quota with registered premises and acquisition or disposition without premises as different cases, and the board approves quota transactions, checking good standing and cycle allocation; growers are licensed by the BCCMB on an annual basis. Hatching-egg quota under the BC Broiler Hatching Egg Commission is hen-based for mainstream (one unit = one placeable broiler breeder hen) and hatched-chick-based for specialty. Turkey quota under the BC Turkey Marketing Board is pounds of turkey meat per specified period. The BC Farm Industry Review Board supervises the regulated marketing system and hears appeals of a board's decision, with appeals to be filed within 30 calendar days. https://bcchicken.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/BCCMB-GENERAL-ORDERS-Aug-26-2011.pdf and https://bcbhec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2022-Annual-Report.pdf and https://bcturkey.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/174_66.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/organizational-structure/ministries-organizations/boards-commissions-tribunals/bc-farm-industry-review-board/npma-appeals **Still UNVERIFIED (genuinely unopened, carried as such):** BC Egg Marketing Board layer transfer rules (including any Quota Exchange / going-concern mechanism); premises/barn-certification wording for chicken; turkey transfer rules beyond the scheme provisions stated above; any quota price or per-unit value in any sector; and, in the batch-13 research pass itself, every board item (that pass opened no board source — the live page's three-board account plus hatching-egg commission account is site copy in that pass). https://jaredgibbons.ca/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are market statistics published for poultry farms in BC?** No figure is stated in this file: SnapStats has no farm category, and no official source opened publishes BC poultry-farm counts, farms-for-sale counts, quota prices or per-acre values — each is UNVERIFIED, and a price opinion stands on the specific property's land, barns, residence and any quota included, checked part by part.
- **Q: What residence can a BC poultry parcel in the ALR have?** One principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha), with local rules possibly more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What income must a poultry operation show to keep farm classification?** $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation; $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha. Applications are due by October 31 for the next tax year, and a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31 after purchase. (§3.)
- **Q: Is quota included when I buy a poultry farm in BC?** Not as a default. The live page states quota is not included unless expressly stated in the Contract of Purchase and Sale (site copy, recorded as such), the one operating farm in its feed states quota is not included, and the BCCMB General Orders (sibling-opened source) treat transfer with registered premises and acquisition/disposition without premises as separate, board-approved cases. Read the specific contract and verify the transfer position with the applicable board before subjects are removed. Any quota price: UNVERIFIED. https://jaredgibbons.ca/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.html and https://bcchicken.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/BCCMB-GENERAL-ORDERS-Aug-26-2011.pdf
- **Q: Does this page list poultry farms across all of BC?** No — as read, its feed showed four cards, all in Burnaby or Abbotsford, and only one described an operating commercial poultry operation. The feed count is a snapshot, not a BC inventory. https://jaredgibbons.ca/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.html
- **Q: How is quota measured?** Differently by sector (sibling-opened board sources): broiler quota in kilograms of live weight per cycle; turkey quota in pounds of turkey meat per specified period; hatching-egg quota in hen units (mainstream) or hatched chicks (specialty). A "quota included" line means different things by sector. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide a poultry parcel into lots?** Only with ALC approval/permission under §3 — subdivision in the ALR requires ALC approval and stratification requires ALC permission (a permission rule, never an absolute ban; Bulletin 05 is not the source).
- **Q: What happens during an avian influenza outbreak?** HPAI is reportable to the CFIA; in a primary control zone, permits are required to move birds, eggs and by-products, and on a quarantined farm nothing bird-related moves without CFIA permission — placements, showings and completion dates can all be delayed. Confirm the property's control-zone status before fixing a completion date. https://www.inspection.gc.ca/en/animal-health/terrestrial-animals/diseases/reportable/avian-influenza/what-expect-your-animals-are-infected
- **Q: If a board refuses my quota transfer, can I appeal?** The BCFIRB supervises the system and hears appeals of board decisions, to be filed within 30 calendar days of the board's final decision (sibling-opened BCFIRB source). Turkey transfer rules beyond the scheme provisions and egg layer transfer rules stay UNVERIFIED (§3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/organizational-structure/ministries-organizations/boards-commissions-tribunals/bc-farm-industry-review-board/npma-appeals
