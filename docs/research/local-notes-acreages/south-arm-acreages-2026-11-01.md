## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500** — Single Detached RS1/RS2 (Section 8.1; minimums in Section 2). A secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in RS1/RS2 — Section 5.4: minimum 33.0 m², maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m²; the City allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **OCP: City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000** — no separate South Arm area plan is confirmed; the district sits in the OCP framework rather than a Schedule 2 area plan of its own. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **Subdivision and servicing — VERIFIED ledger:** Richmond Subdivision Bylaw 8751 and Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 are VERIFIED (carry exactly). Subdivision runs against the Bylaw 8500 zone minimums and Bylaws 8751/8752; confirm the works required for a specific lot with the City. (Standing servicing ledger.)
- **Flood — Bylaw 8204 governs every lot here:** the district is on Lulu Island in the Fraser River floodplain along the South Arm channel, behind the City perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations; the system is designed for a 1:500 flood event and dikes are being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres. Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements — for homes near the south dike, confirm both before planning additions or a rebuild. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — carried for completeness; no South Arm ALR parcel is documented in this file's research:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps: principal residence maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, and it is expressly NOT documented in this slug's own research — asserted nowhere in this file. No South Arm parcel is carried as farm-class here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **BC strata block (where a strata purchase is made instead of land — the Kingz Court redirect case):** Form B diligence (SPA s.59, within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; 45+ does NOT convert). None of it applies to a fee-simple lot purchase itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
