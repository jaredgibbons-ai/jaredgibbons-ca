## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **OCP and zoning (Surrey only):** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is replaced and is NEVER cited as current in this file. Area-level guidance is the adopted South Port Kells Land Use Plan; North Port Kells is primarily light industrial (IL) zoning under **Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000**. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ADOPTEDSOUTHPORTKELLSLANDUSEPLAN.pdf and https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential, as the municipality states it:** Zoning Bylaw No. 12000's SSMUH framework (in force since July 8, 2024) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, on eligible lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary — permissions that do NOT override ALR status on a farm parcel. The City states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey, off-ALR parcels):** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, one per unit, not a separate strata lot, and never inside a coach house or garden suite. ALR parcels follow the ALC rules below instead. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision and servicing — Bylaw No. 8830 (1986), VERIFIED ledger:** Surrey's subdivision/servicing instrument is the **Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** — verified in this program's ledger; servicing runs through the City's Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process, confirmed per parcel. Port Kells also carries its own industrial and commercial DCC schedules (§2). (Standing verified ledger.)
- **Streamside setbacks:** Surrey's streamside setbacks sit in **Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, varying by stream class** — NO single riparian figure is ever stated for a Port Kells parcel; the applicable setback is read off Part 7A for the watercourse class on the specific lot, in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R053.pdf
- **ALR BLOCK IN FULL — ALR ground exists in Port Kells, so this block governs real parcels here:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps: principal residence maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (parcels over 40 ha). Check the ALR boundary on any south-side parcel before pricing. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ADOPTEDSOUTHPORTKELLSLANDUSEPLAN.pdf
- **Farm class — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, and it is NOT documented in this slug's own research — asserted nowhere in this file beyond the three-tier frame. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **BC strata block (where a strata purchase is made instead of land — no such plan verified here):** Form B diligence (SPA s.59, within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; 45+ does NOT convert). On ALR parcels the stratification bar above governs first. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2 (notices sent end of May; if the due date falls on a weekend or statutory holiday, payment the next business day is without penalty), with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply here. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors, tier math shown: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K cap → $14,000 ($6,000 fully qualifying First-Time Home Buyers'); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (1% × $200,000 = $2,000 + 2% × $1,800,000 = $36,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 = $45,000 + further 2% × $500,000 = $10,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; CRA governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land — 100% to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism). ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What acreage or land can I actually buy in Port Kells?** Two kinds, split by the highway: fee-simple light-industrial (IL) land and buildings north of Highway 1, and fee-simple houses and rural parcels in South Port Kells — suburban lots to multi-acre rural/ALR parcels. There is no lot program: no named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata offered as lots, and presale lot offerings verified: zero. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Port Kells really a board sub-area, or just an industrial park name?** Both: it IS a real FVREB sub-area in the board's SnapStats structure AND the City profiles it as a major industrial area; it is not one of Surrey's six town centres, and for community planning it sits within the Guildford area's orbit. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791
- **Q: Is Barnston Island part of Port Kells / Surrey?** No. Barnston Island codes to Port Kells only for board purposes because the free ferry to it sails from the foot of 104 Avenue in Port Kells — the island is NOT Surrey ground, its position is never imported here, and its own lots file carries its land position. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- **Q: Can I subdivide or build a second home on a South Port Kells acreage?** It depends on zoning and, critically, ALR status: the land use plan shows ALR lands inside the residential pocket, subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, and ALR parcels carry the ALC's residence limits — principal ≤500 m², a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, an additional residence capped at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha) — and an ALR residence cannot be stratified at all. Off-ALR, Surrey's suite cap is 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area, and subdivision runs under Bylaw No. 8830. Confirm both before pricing in a second dwelling. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: The land use plan mentions cluster housing — is that a complex I can buy into?** No. "Cluster housing" in the adopted South Port Kells Land Use Plan is a plan designation for the residential pocket, not a named strata complex — no complex name, address or strata plan attaches to it on any source checked. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ADOPTEDSOUTHPORTKELLSLANDUSEPLAN.pdf
- **Q: Are there extra development charges in Port Kells?** Yes — Surrey publishes separate 2025 DCC schedules for Port Kells industrial and commercial development, apart from the city-wide rates. Confirm the schedule early in any development pro forma. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791
- **Q: Is a south-side lot serviced?** UNVERIFIED per parcel in this program — servicing on the rural south side is a parcel-by-parcel check with the City and on the ground (municipal services vs well/septic), and on acreage ground the servicing answer drives value more than the house does. (§2.)
