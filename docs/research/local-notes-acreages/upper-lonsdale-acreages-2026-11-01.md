City Zoning Bylaw 6700 governs City-side parcels. The City legalized secondary suites in all single-family dwellings in 1993; one suite is permitted per single-family property, the owner must reside at the property, and a suite may be combined with an accessory coach house, permitted where the front lot line is at least 10 m (32.81 ft) and requiring a Development Permit and a Building Permit. **On December 8, 2025 the City adopted the Ground Oriented (GO) Zone, increasing permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 residential properties and allowing up to 6 units per lot on approximately 600 properties — City-side only.** https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Official-Community-Plan-2014-No-8400-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9114-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-t.pdf and https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/HTO-Year-2-Progress-Report-July-1,-2025-June-30,-2026.pdf
