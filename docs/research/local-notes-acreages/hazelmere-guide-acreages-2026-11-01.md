## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Plan and zoning:** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw No. 18020 is the predecessor only, never current — with zoning under **Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000**, parcel by parcel. No City land use plan is named for Hazelmere. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- **SSMUH caution for this ground (Surrey, adopted Jul 8, 2024):** Surrey's SSMUH allowances apply to eligible residential-zoned properties under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including lands not connected to community water and sewer — most Hazelmere acreage ground is outside that shape, so the standard 3–4 unit framing must not be applied to Hazelmere lots without a parcel check. Where SSMUH does apply, the general frame is 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey):** a secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area** under Bylaw 12000; a "90 m²" figure is NOT the current Surrey cap and is not used here. On A-1 land the sibling page states one dwelling which may contain one secondary suite — UNVERIFIED on the bylaw text (§2). https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Subdivision / servicing:** **Surrey Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED** for the City of Surrey (verified ledger). Subdivision potential runs against the zone in Bylaw 12000 (A-1 minimums UNVERIFIED on the text, §2) plus Bylaw 8830's servicing standards, confirmed per parcel with the City. (Verified ledger; FILE STATUS.)
- **Streamside / floodplain:** Surrey streamside protection is **Part 7A by stream class — NO single riparian figure** is stated or used here (corrected ledger). For Hazelmere specifically, the guide page states the Little Campbell watershed has no dykes and its floodplain follows the river channel closely (site copy, §1). https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere-guide.html
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (much of the Hazelmere watershed is farmland in the ALR per the guide, and the area is farm ground):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha); local governments may further restrict residences through zoning. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm applies by October 31 after purchase. Whether a specific Hazelmere property carries farm-class taxation is a BC Assessment / parcel question: UNVERIFIED in this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey-wide):** only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at **$358 per year** is required before operating — carried with the City's $350 proposal disclosed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Property taxes (Surrey):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a SEPARATE info sheet, usually taxable subject to limited exemptions. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — CRA governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **BC strata block (for any strata product a Hazelmere buyer later prices — no bare-land strata community is documented in §2):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
