## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning framework:** the Downtown District (DD) schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 governs much of the core — permitted uses are those in the Downtown District Official Development Plan, including commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses; CD-1 and C-3A districts also apply in parts of the area. There is no house-lot minimum table to quote; the parcel's own district governs. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — VERIFIED Vancouver instruments:** subdivision in Vancouver runs under Subdivision By-law No. 5208, and its fees are set by By-law No. 14538 — the verified ledger entry for Vancouver (Subdivision By-law 5208; fees 14538). No other city's servicing number is used for Vancouver ground. The downtown-relevant subdivision form is airspace/strata-plan work under that framework, not lot splits (§2). https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **SSMUH position:** the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing framework is a house-lot framework — Downtown has no house-lot stock for it to attach to (§2), and no Downtown SSMUH position is documented in the research. Density is set by the DD/CD-1 position of the specific parcel, as the City states it for that parcel. (Research file §4.)
- **Heritage controls:** Yaletown, at this page's south side, is a City protected heritage area (HA-3), bordered by Homer and Nelson streets to the north and east, and Cambie, Mainland, and Drake streets to the south and west, with warehouse-era buildings — heritage regulations affect renovation, alteration and any redevelopment of a heritage-district parcel. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **ALR RULES — carried for completeness, no downtown ALR parcel is confirmed here:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and Bulletin 05 is NOT cited for stratification. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² + ≤1,000 m² fill; secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence; one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure (no downtown farm parcel is confirmed; carried so no lesser form is used):** $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha (greater than 4 ha); $10,000 (less than 0.8 ha); applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page only, never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **BC strata block — the operative regime downtown:** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). For strata purchases, review the depreciation report, contingency reserve fund and any special levies before pricing an offer; none of that is in the feed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; farmland caution per CRA's separate info sheet. A downtown strata resale is not a vacant-land sale. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — CRA governs; 100% of GST to $1 million, maximum $50,000, phasing out to $1.5 million; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself; a $1.25M straight-line row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY, applies here, rate 3%:** every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration (due February 3, 2026); the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. A property declared, determined or deemed empty is taxed at 3% of assessed taxable value. The Province's Speculation and Vacancy Tax is separate and in addition. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/evidence-exemptions.aspx
- **Vancouver property taxes:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. **Additional school tax:** 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
