## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815** (adopted June 9, 2026, effective July 1, 2026) — the standing frame; the former 1965 Bylaw No. 4742 is superseded and must never be cited for this area. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 SSMUH District in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified; minimum lot width 10 m, minimum lot area 281 m²; permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP: Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025**, replacing the 1998 OCP and taking precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; the area falls under the Brentwood Town Centre framework within the 2050 OCP. Check the 2050 OCP before assuming assembly or redevelopment value on lots near Lougheed Highway and the stations. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan

- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million.
