- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for ALR parcels:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence (a second, detached, small-scale residence for farm use or other permitted use) capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Agri-tourism and temporary farm worker housing are permitted under conditions on ALR land. One hectare = 2.471 acres. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.nrcan.gc.ca/maps-tools-publications/tools/geodetic-reference-systems/converting-units-measurement/10798
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in Bradner's own research file (standalone or guide) — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. Farm class is the holding-cost difference between a working Bradner parcel and an idle one — confirm the parcel's current class and income record before pricing the tax position. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What am I actually buying when I buy an "acreage" in Bradner?** A whole fee-simple farm or rural-residential parcel under A1 or A2 Agricultural zoning, part of the community in the ALR. - **Q: Is this parcel in the ALR — and what changes if it is?** Confirm the parcel itself on the ALC map; the community is only partly in the reserve. If it is in: subdivision needs ALC approval, the residence cannot be stratified, and the housing caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside that residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I split the parcel, or stratify it later for family?** Stratify: no, if it is ALR — residences in the ALR cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Split: only within the agricultural zone minimums and, in the ALR, only with ALC approval. Do not price a Bradner acreage as if a full second house or a strata exit can be added. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a suite go in a barn or a detached building?** No — under the ALC housing rules a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. A detached second dwelling is the "additional residence," size-capped and tied to farm use or another permitted use. Abbotsford's general suite and coach/garden caps do not override the ALR rule on ALR land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What farm income do I need to keep farm class?** All three tiers, not a flat figure: $2,500 on a 0.8–4 ha farm operation; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A page or listing stating only "$2,500+" understates the test for small and large parcels. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Is the acreage serviced?** Assume well and onsite sewage until proven otherwise — Bradner is outside the urban servicing pattern. Pull the GWELLS well record and the Fraser Health sewerage filing for the parcel before offering. Parcel-level status: UNCONFIRMED in every source checked until those records are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a Bradner farm acreage?** Do not assume either way. The CRA's vacant-land sheet makes most individual personal-use sales exempt, but it treats farmland in a separate sheet as usually taxable with limited exemptions — a working farm parcel is the fact pattern where buyers get surprised. The answer is UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel until vendor status and farm use are applied; the contract must state who bears GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
