## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

**Frames are kept separate in this section. A rule headed "Surrey" governs Surrey ground only; a rule headed "Langley City" governs Langley City ground only. Nothing here is a "regional" rule, and no frame is imported for any other municipality the editorial names (including Chilliwack, for which this file states no frame at all).**

- **Surrey frame — OCP / zoning (per the lots-and-land sibling):** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current; Bylaw No. 18020 is never cited as current. Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000, read per parcel in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/townhomes-under-900000-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **Surrey frame — SSMUH / suites / subdivision:** maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; one secondary suite per dwelling unit, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite; coach/garden suites 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on specified RA/R2 lots below maximum FAR). Subdivision/servicing: zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000 per parcel in COSMOS; Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830, worked through the Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. Surrey property taxes due July 2, 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes (via the lots-and-land sibling)
- **Langley City frame — zoning / suites:** Langley City is a separate municipality from both Surrey and the Township of Langley; its zoning is Langley City Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 (number cited; adoption date conflicted/UNVERIFIED in the program record), under which a secondary suite may not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, and the registered owner must reside on the lot. City SSMUH: Bylaw 3284. Township rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 2500; SSMUH Bylaw 6020, suite cap 120 m², coach house as a fee-simple second unit under s.102) govern Township ground only and do NOT govern a Langley City parcel. No Langley City OCP number, subdivision/servicing bylaw number or tax due date is asserted in this file: UNCONFIRMED here, read from the City for the specific parcel. (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/townhomes-under-900000-condos-2026-11-01.md; standing Langley frame corrections)
- **ALR block (provincial frame, applied only where a parcel IS in the ALR, in any municipality):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, it is NOT documented in this slug's own research (it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page), and no farm-class claim is asserted for this band. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Strata block (built townhome lots in this band, and any bare-land strata lot):** Form B diligence (SPA s.59) — the Province's strata buying guidance directs buyers to the Form B information certificate, the Form F, strata bylaws and rules, fees and special levies; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw; REDMA s.21 7-day rescission for presales. Age restrictions, pet rules and rental restrictions live in the strata documents, not in the listing price. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) across this band (provincial, all frames):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — a purchase under $900,000 meets only the first two tiers. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K cap→$16,000. For completeness beyond the band: $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M lives only in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, not followed. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000, full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024), partial exemption may apply between $835,000 and $860,000 — so part of this band (to $835,000) can sit inside the full-exemption value range on price alone, and part (above $860,000) cannot. Newly Built Home Exemption: full threshold $1,100,000, complete elimination at $1,150,000 (effective April 1, 2024) — a qualifying newly built townhome anywhere on this page is inside that threshold on price alone; it is a new HOME exemption, not bare-land relief. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland caution per GI-003's separate sheet. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED. Built townhome resale GST treatment is a separate, transaction-specific question and is not stated as a band rule here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the housing boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA; March 12 noted in-line where siblings carry it — March 13 governs, never silently overwritten) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out, housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
