- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $2,000,000 British Properties purchase treated as fully residential with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,800,000 ($36,000) = **$38,000**. Second worked example with the top tiers shown: a $3,500,000 purchase treated as fully residential = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,800,000 ($36,000) + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 above $3 million ($10,000) = **$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000.
