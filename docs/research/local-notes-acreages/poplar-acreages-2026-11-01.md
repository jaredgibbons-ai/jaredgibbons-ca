- **Fee-simple rural parcels — the acreage form.** Single-detached houses are the main form in Poplar — on urban lots in the north part of the sub-area and on larger rural lots toward Huntingdon Road and the south edge. The established stock is freehold detached housing; strata in the sub-area is limited to the townhome complexes along King Road and Windsor Street / Marshall Road. Acreage trades in that same fee-simple form: a house (or a buildable envelope) on a large rural parcel, with no strata, no Form B and no strata fees. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014 (research file §5)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What kind of acreage actually comes up for sale in Poplar?** Individual fee-simple rural parcels on the Huntingdon Road / south edge — a house on a large lot, or a buildable rural parcel — not lots in a named acreage subdivision, because none is verified here, in this slug's research or in the guide sibling. - **Q: Is a Huntingdon Road parcel in the ALR?** South-edge parcels are in or beside the ALR — check the specific parcel on the ALC maps; in the ALR, subdivision needs ALC approval, residences cannot be stratified, and the residence caps in Section 3 apply on top of City zoning. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Poplar acreage parcel?** Where the A1 zone applies, the City minimum for a parcel created by subdivision is 8.0 ha — so a parcel has to be very large before any split is even arguable, and an ALR parcel additionally needs ALC approval. Subdivision servicing detail for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED until the City confirms it (no Development Bylaw number is asserted in this file). https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/content/coa/coabylaws/z/zoning/?xsl=/templates/browse.xsl
- **Q: Will a south-edge acreage parcel have city water and sewer?** It may be on well and septic instead. Pull the GWELLS well record and the Fraser Health sewerage filings before subjects — and do not assume it matches a King Road parcel's servicing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners
- **Q: Can I add a second home or a coach house on an acreage parcel?** On City-zoned ground, Abbotsford's rules (Abbotsford ONLY) allow one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings if the criteria are met (400 m² minimum lot, 12.0 m frontage, cap under 50%), and a coach/garden suite at 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m². On ALR ground the ALC caps in Section 3 govern instead — an additional residence is 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha), and a suite must be within the principal residence. The SSMUH bylaw number sometimes cited for Abbotsford ("3560-2024") is UNCONFIRMED — do not rely on it. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Which schools serve a Poplar acreage parcel?** This slug's research documents feeder patterns, NOT a locator run, and states the basis openly: for 32746 Huntingdon Road (South Poplar Traditional Elementary's own address), the Traditional pathway is South Poplar Traditional Elementary → Abbotsford Traditional School (Grades 6–12) — a choice-program entry pattern, NOT the regular catchment, which is UNVERIFIED in this file. For 33165 King Road (Jackson Elementary's own address), the published pattern is Jackson Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary. Run the exact address in the SD34 School Locator before relying on any school. https://www.abbyschools.ca/policy-18-school-catchment-areas and https://ats.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/registration/atms
- **Q: Is Poplar flood risk real for an acreage parcel?** Drainage runs toward the Sumas Prairie system the City calls low-lying and Nooksack-overflow-prone; your parcel's status is a Zoning Bylaw Schedule E / WebMap check, parcel by parcel — UNVERIFIED until done. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- **Q: Can I get farm-class taxes on a Poplar acreage parcel?** Only if the parcel is farm land meeting the full three-tier test in Section 3 ($2,500 / 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; apply by October 31). Acreage alone does not qualify, and this file does not document any Poplar parcel as a working farm — BC Assessment's class for the specific parcel governs. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Will I pay GST on a vacant Poplar acreage parcel?** At CRA GI-003 level: an individual personal-use sale is generally exempt; a business-use, in-the-course-of-business, or more-than-two-part subdivision sale can be taxable. Confirm the vendor's status in writing and get tax advice — anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What PTT would a $900,000 acreage purchase carry?** On the general tiers in Section 3: $16,000 (1% on the first $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% on the next $700,000 = $14,000), before any exemption that actually applies — the home exemptions do not apply to bare land alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
