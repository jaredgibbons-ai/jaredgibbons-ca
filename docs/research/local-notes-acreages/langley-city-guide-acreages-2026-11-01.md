## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **City plans and zoning:** the City's Official Community Plan **Bylaw No. 3200** was adopted November 22, 2021. Zoning is **City of Langley Zoning Bylaw, 2025, No. 3300**, which repeals Zoning Bylaw 1996 No. 2100 — cited by NUMBER only: its exact adoption date is conflicted in this program's sources and is UNVERIFIED, and a live page's stated March 9, 2026 adoption date was NOT adopted. Full land-plan detail is in the langleycity land-plans research (batch-13) and is not duplicated here. Township bylaws are never imported into this City file. (Research file above.)
- **SSMUH (City of Langley ONLY):** the City amended its Zoning Bylaw to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on RS1 and RS2 lots by **Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024** — RS1/RS2 permit **4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop**. That 6-unit tier is a City rule; it does NOT exist in the Township (Township Bylaw 6020 has no 6-plex tier), and neither rule crosses the municipal line. (Research file above.)
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 3300):** a secondary suite must not exceed the **lesser of 100 m² and 40%** of the gross floor area of the building; the registered owner must reside on the lot; at least one extra off-street parking space is required; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. (Research file above.)
- **Short-term rentals in Langley City: UNVERIFIED** — no City of Langley STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page in this program. Do not market a short-term rental on a City rule that cannot be cited.
- **Subdivision / servicing:** no City servicing bylaw number is carried in this slug's research, and the Township's Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 is expressly NOT borrowed into City ground — a City parcel's subdivision and servicing standard is confirmed with the City of Langley. UNVERIFIED as a number in this file. (Research file above; FILE STATUS.)
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (provincial frame — stated so it is never misapplied; no City ALR parcel is documented in §2):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified WITHOUT THE ALC'S PERMISSION — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. No farm-class claim is asserted for any City parcel in this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property taxes (City of Langley):** payments received after the July 2 due date incur a penalty; the City's payment page lists City Hall at 20399 Douglas Crescent and a 2.2% processing fee on credit-card payments. The penalty percentage for 2026: UNVERIFIED — the City page read states a penalty applies but does not state the rate. https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — worked anchors with inputs + tier math:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption, unless stated): $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000) — follow $93,000; a $78,000 figure for $3.5M appears ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling and is flagged wherever met, never followed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
