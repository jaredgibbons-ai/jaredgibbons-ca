## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **City plan:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the **Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current in this file. The Cloverdale Town Centre Plan is being updated (boundary expanded July 22, 2024; Fairgrounds excluded as a Special Study Area). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000.** Its SSMUH framework (in force for this area since **July 8, 2024**) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. The former RF-12 / RF-12C zones were replaced by the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone and RF-SD by R6 Semi-Detached in those changes (§5 defect). The City states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits. On ALR land the ALC rules below govern instead — the two regimes meet at the lowland line and must not be mixed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for Cloverdale's lowland ALR parcels:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum total floor area 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha, where Surrey bylaws also permit it. Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR, and building more than the permitted number, or larger than the size limits, requires ALC approval. Whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence is not permitted outright in the ALR and requires ALC approval; bed-and-breakfast use inside a lived-in principal residence is permitted within the ALC's four-bedroom / 30-day limits. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (the HORSE-PROPERTIES page owns it) — no flat form is documented in this slug's research, and the cloverdale-farms page's "$2,500 to $10,000+" summary is site copy, not adopted in either file. For an edge parcel, farm class is the holding-cost difference between a working parcel and an idle one — confirm the parcel's current class and income record before pricing the tax position. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Suites, coach houses and STRs (urban ground):** one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area**, never inside a coach house or garden suite, and not a separate strata lot; a coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), with lane access required for a coach house on smaller lots and one extra parking space. Short-term rentals are permitted, **owner-only** (the host must be the owner living at the property as their primary residence; tenants cannot operate one) — the licence figure is carried as **$358 per year** per the City's Short-Term Rentals page, with the conflict disclosed: the City's 2024 corporate report proposed **$350 per year plus fees** — confirm the current figure on the City's licence page before quoting it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Subdivision and servicing:** Surrey servicing is **Subdivision and Development Bylaw No. 8830 (1986), VERIFIED on the City's own pages** — carried with that sourcing. For an ALR parcel, ALC approval is required in addition to the City process. **Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated in this file**; flood construction level on a lowland parcel is a per-parcel check (the cloverdale-farms research opened no City floodplain page, so no floodplain figure is asserted for the edge here).
