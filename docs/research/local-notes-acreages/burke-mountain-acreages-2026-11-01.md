## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning: City of Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996** — small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3 with the subdivision minimums stated in Section 2 (650 m² / 18.5 m for R-1 and R-3; 465 m² / 12.5 m for R-2). https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **OCP: Interim OCP Bylaws 5511/5512, adopted April 27, 2026, superseding Bylaw 3479** (standing correction — never cite 3479 as current), with the Northeast Coquitlam Area Plan framework, the adopted Hyde Creek / Smiling Creek / Partington Creek neighbourhood plans, the Northwest Burke Vision and Hazel-Coy neighbourhood planning as the local layers. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision
- **SSMUH, as the City states it:** Coquitlam's adopted SSMUH rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. SSMUH creates no acreage lots — run the math on the exact zone (R-1 / R-2 / R-3) and services first. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Creek / hazard rules — the governing diligence frame for mountain lots:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within **50 metres** of the top of bank of any watercourse in Northeast Coquitlam (30 metres in the rest of Coquitlam); Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and s.523 covers riparian areas. No single riparian figure beyond that stated DPA reach is asserted, and lot-level creek setback and slope status are UNVERIFIED per lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Subdivision and servicing: Coquitlam Subdivision Bylaw 3558, 2003 — VERIFIED** in the program ledger. Subdivision runs through the City against the R-zone minimums and the watercourse DPA above. (Standing ledger entry; research file above.)
- **ALR rules — conditional, stated because the check is parcel-by-parcel:** no ALR parcel is confirmed on Burke Mountain; IF a specific lot were in the ALR, subdivision would require Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² (40 ha or less) / 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR, asserted nowhere here and documented nowhere in Burke Mountain's research. No Burke Mountain parcel is documented as farm-classed. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **BC strata block (the governing block for the mountain's counted townhouse stock):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). The Roxton Avenue fee-simple cluster carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — diligence there is title, municipal rules and registered covenants only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Presale rules, where the mountain's presales are met:** Versant and Rocklin 1295 are REDMA presales counted in the siblings — deposits are held in trust (Versant's price sheet states its 10% structure payable in trust) and completion is as each project's own documents state (Versant Fall 2026–Early 2027; Rocklin 1295 UNCONFIRMED as a single date). Neither presale is land or acreage. (Townhomes sibling, §2.)
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,200,000 fully residential Burke Mountain lot purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,000,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** (a townhomes sibling in this program carries $78,000 for $3.5M — an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag wherever met). Land routes: FTHB vacant-lot-and-build route at $835,000 or less (partial under $860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption refund route at $1,100,000 / $1,150,000 thresholds. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — ONLY at the CRA GI-003 level:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example); a sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file; the contract must state the GST position. Presale GST on Versant/Rocklin homes is a housing question governed by each contract — not a bare-land position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there acreage for sale on Burke Mountain?** No — no acreage parcel, acreage subdivision, bare-land strata or presale acreage offering was verified on any source. The land-form purchases here are detached houses on R-1/R-2/R-3 lots and townhouses. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How big are Burke Mountain lots, and can one be split?** Subdivision minimums are 650 m² (18.5 m width) in R-1 and R-3 and 465 m² (12.5 m width) in R-2 under Zoning Bylaw 3000, 1996 — under Subdivision Bylaw 3558, 2003. Creek setbacks can govern the buildable area on creek-edge lots, so area alone does not settle a split. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Q: What can a house lot hold under SSMUH?** Up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including suites and carriage/laneway homes — but only where zoning and municipal services allow; unserviced lots are outside the rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Q: What creek check applies before I build or add a suite?** The Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area reaches 50 metres from the top of bank of any watercourse in Northeast Coquitlam — double the 30-metre reach in the rest of Coquitlam — and Zoning Bylaw s.519 / s.523 set flood and riparian setbacks. Partington Creek lots can also sit near the secured wildlife corridor lands. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
