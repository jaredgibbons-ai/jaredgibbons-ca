## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Plan framework: the Cambie Corridor Plan (approved May 9, 2011; Phase 3 approved 2018)** guides growth from 16th Avenue to the Fraser River around the Canada Line stations, targeting the Oakridge Municipal Town Centre at 41st and Cambie as its southern anchor. There is no separate "Cambie" OCP — the corridor plan is the framework, layered over the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 district schedules (R1-1 on side streets, C-2 on arterial sites, CD-1 at station areas). Plan designation is not approved zoning — most corridor densities still require a rezoning application. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/cambie-corridor-plan.aspx
- **Subdivision — VERIFIED City instruments:** subdivision in Vancouver runs under the Subdivision By-law No. 5208, and its 2026 fees are set by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026), which substitutes a new Schedule F: Class I (Major) $174,300, Class II (Intermediate) $87,200, Class III (Minor) $15,020, Class V (Air Space) $125,100 where FSR is greater than 3.0, and Class VI (Freehold Rowhouses) $15,020 plus $1,950 per freehold lot. The City's major-subdivision process (sites over 10,000 m², or 10,000–40,000 m² likely to require complex legal agreements) requires a preliminary proposal with a current Land Title Office title search and a lawyer's charge summary, submitted to the Approving Officer. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/major-subdivision.aspx
- **Floodplain diligence:** the City's designated floodplain is a citywide dataset covering coastal and river floodplains, and the corridor's south foot runs down to the Fraser River at Marine Drive; whether a specific corridor parcel is in the designated floodplain: UNVERIFIED — check the parcel in the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **ALR position — stated plainly: no ALR position is documented for this area.** No ALR parcel is documented in the research and no farm adjacency is raised, so no ALR rule is applied to any corridor parcel in this file. For completeness only, the standing conditional rules IF a parcel were ever confirmed ALR are: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; residence caps are a principal residence to 500 m², a suite within the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); and farm class runs on the full three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met, is documented only on the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, and is never attributed to this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC strata block (applies to the corridor's strata stock, and in full to any bare-land strata lot if title ever showed that tenure):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions); strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 purchase treated as fully residential with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** (tier math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 further-tier = $93,000) — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Vancouver taxes — property tax and the 3% Empty Homes Tax (Vancouver ONLY):** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date, and the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. Vancouver owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the Empty Homes Tax rate is 3%, the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date.
