- **Q: What extra units can go on an acreage parcel?** On urban ground: one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings (s.140.3.8), subject to the 400 m² / 12.0 m / Collector-or-Local / no-cul-de-sac-bulb criteria; suite under 50% of the principal dwelling; coach/garden suite 15% of lot area to 100 m² maximum. On ALR ground the ALC caps replace those: principal 500 m², suite inside the principal only, additional residence 90 m² / 186 m² by the 40 ha line. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is this parcel in the ALR — and why does it matter?** Check the parcel on the ALC map: the research does not confirm ALR status parcel-by-parcel inside the built neighbourhoods. If it is in the ALR, subdivision needs ALC approval, residences cannot be stratified, and the ALC residence caps govern. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can I keep farm class on a small acreage?** Only on the full three-tier test — $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha (over 4 ha), $10,000 (under 0.8 ha) — with an application by October 31. Acreage alone does not qualify; a flat "$2,500+" quote understates the test. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: What taxes do I pay on the acreage itself?** PTT at the general rates (worked anchors in Section 3: $1.5M → $28,000, no exemption). GST turns on the seller, not the land: an individual's personal-use sale is generally exempt per CRA GI-003; a business sale or a more-than-two-part subdivision sale can be taxable — have the contract state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What will it cost to hold the acreage while I plan?** Property taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3, second 5% September 16, 2026), plus insurance and any covenant/servicing obligations on title. No strata fee on a fee-simple parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Q: What are the hillside and flood risks on an east-side acreage?** Slope/geotechnical conditions on McKee/Eagle Mountain ground (Steep Slope Development Permit Area; McKee open-space network includes slopes over 40%) and flood exposure on low parcels toward Sumas Prairie (City notices name properties south of the Sumas River and along Whatcom Road).
