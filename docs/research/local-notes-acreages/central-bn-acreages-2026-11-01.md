## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815:** adopted Jun 9, 2026 and in effect Jul 1, 2026, replacing the former Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742), which is never cited as current for Burnaby. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan:** adopted Dec 9, 2025, replacing the 1998 OCP; it takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. Community plans are adopted by Council as policy guidelines for rezoning and development applications. The Royal Oak Community Plan covers the plan area to the south of this sub-area. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and a minimum lot area of 281 m²; permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites. Central has no SkyTrain station, so the 400 m frequent-transit test is a parcel-specific bus-corridor check, not a station-distance assumption. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (carried because the ALR check is mandatory diligence here, even though no ALR acreage ground is documented for Central):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, with applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR wherever met; that flat figure is documented only as the HORSE-PROPERTIES page's own research position and is never attributed to this area's research. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — Burnaby servicing base number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** the former servicing base number was renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024, and no confirmed current base number is quoted in this file; the process is documented instead — subdivision potential runs off the parcel's zoning (the R1 thresholds above), servicing is confirmed with the City parcel by parcel, and streamside checks gate brook-side lots (below). Never quote a Burnaby servicing-bylaw number from a listing or a summary. (Program servicing ledger; ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/central-bn-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §3)
- **Streamside / hazard:** Deer Lake and Deer Lake Brook drain the area's east side; parcel-level streamside setbacks and hazard status are property-specific checks: UNVERIFIED for individual lots. On Deer Lake–edge and brook-side lots, confirm streamside setbacks for the specific parcel before pricing redevelopment value. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- **BC strata block (applies to the strata purchases a Central buyer actually meets — the building stratas cross-referenced in Section 2, not to a fee-simple acreage purchase):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Pad ground:** a manufactured home on a rented pad is NOT lot stock, NOT land, NOT acreage. No pad-rental stock is verified for Central. - **Property transfer tax (PTT):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. - **GST on vacant land — ONLY at the CRA GI-003 level:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland caution: the CRA treats farmland sales in a separate sheet as usually taxable — never assume a farm parcel is GST-exempt on the vacant-land rule alone; parcel-level treatment is UNCONFIRMED, and anything finer than GI-003 is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

A live page's $1.25M straight-line phase-out row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism where met. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
