## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **City plan and zoning (Surrey ground):** OCP = Surrey 2050, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026 — current; Bylaw No. 18020 is never cited as the current OCP in this file. Zoning is under Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, applied parcel by parcel (there is no Serpentine plan or zone set, §1). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame wherever a feed parcel is in the reserve (this feed's farm ground expressly included):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. Stated explicitly for this slug: the flat form is NOT documented in Serpentine's own research file — it is owned by the HORSE-PROPERTIES page, a different slug, and is never attributed to this file. (The feed's blueberry-farm parcels are exactly the ground where the three tiers, not a flat line, decide the tax position.) https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **SSMUH (Surrey, urban lots only — NOT ALR parcels):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (Surrey's SSMUH framework, July 8, 2024). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey, non-ALR):** only one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Coach houses and garden suites (Surrey, non-ALR):** all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; the unit must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house; one extra parking space is required. On ALR parcels the ALC frame above replaces every one of these numbers. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Subdivision and servicing:** Surrey's Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 is VERIFIED as the City's servicing bylaw in this program. Subdivision of ALR land additionally requires ALC approval (above), and rural-parcel servicing is proven on records — GWELLS for wells, Fraser Health for sewerage — not on a card's "services at lot line" wording (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
