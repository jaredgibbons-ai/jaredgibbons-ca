## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT ACREAGES

- **Jurisdiction first:** a Rosedale parcel sits under the FVRD as local government for Electoral Area D. **Which FVRD Official Community Plan and zoning bylaw, by number, govern a specific Rosedale parcel: UNVERIFIED** — no FVRD planning bylaw for Area D was opened in this pass, and no number is asserted. - **ALR RULES IN FULL — Rosedale is farm-adjacent valley ground with ALR parcels, so the full frame applies wherever ALR ground exists:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban; ALC Information Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is not cited for it. Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill, per the ALC housing page as read in the research); a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; local governments may regulate further but not be more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** minimum gross farm income at farm gate prices of $2,500 if the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — new applications by the legislated deadline of October 31, and classification is removed if the requirements stop being met. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR asserted ONLY where a file's own research documents it (owned by HORSE-PROPERTIES); it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to it. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — anchors with tier math:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Rosedale part of Chilliwack?** No. It is an unincorporated community in FVRD Electoral Area D, east of Chilliwack — the FVRD, not the City of Chilliwack, is the local government, and Chilliwack bylaws and tax dates do not govern a Rosedale parcel. (§1.)

- **Q: Can I build a second home on a Rosedale ALR parcel?** Under the ALC frame: one principal residence of 500 m² or less, a suite ONLY inside it, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha). Stratifying or subdividing ALR land requires ALC permission — it is a permission rule, not an absolute ban. (§3.)
- **Q: What farm income keeps farm classification?** $2,500 for an operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value over 4 ha for a larger operation; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. (§3.)
- **Q: When are property taxes due on a Rosedale acreage?** Under the rural regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11): July 2, with 5% added on amounts unpaid after July 2 and a further 5% after October 31, on the Surveyor of Taxes' notice. The City of Chilliwack's dates do not apply. (§3.)
- **Q: Is the Rosedale park a 55+ park?** No. Mt Cheam MHP's own listing states families are allowed — it is mostly senior-occupied by description, but no age restriction is in its rules as listed, and a pad home there is a tenancy, not acreage ownership. (§2.)
- **Q: Is the 52324 Yale Road development verified stock?** No — it is a QUEUE item: a feed card in listing text only, confirmed on no developer, strata or manager source, documented + flagged and counted nowhere. (§2.)
- **Q: Which school serves a Rosedale acreage?** Rosedale Traditional Community (50850 Yale Rd) is the in-community school confirmed in the Ministry file, but the catchment for any specific address is UNVERIFIED until checked with SD33 — no locator run for a Rosedale address is on record. (§1.)
- **Q: What PTT and GST do I budget on a farm parcel?** PTT per the anchors in §3 (e.g., $1.5M→$28,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 including the over-$3M tier; ALR parcels split on the return). GST is a GI-003 question — personal-use land generally exempt, business/subdivision sales taxable, farmland under the CRA's separate caution. (§3.)
