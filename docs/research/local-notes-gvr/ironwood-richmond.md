# Ironwood, Richmond

## Location
- "Ironwood" is an MLS sub-area name; its street boundary is published only on GVR's interactive map and is UNVERIFIED. [1]
- The City has an Ironwood Sub-Area Plan (Schedule 2.8A) listed under the City's Shellmont Area. [2]
- That plan fronts Steveston Highway and No. 5 Road, with Highway 99 on the east edge and Agricultural Land Reserve land north of its Area C. [3]

## Housing and zoning
- Area A (south side of Steveston Highway) is commercial; Area B (north side) is townhouse and multi-family; Area C ("The Gardens") is mixed-use with mid-rise buildings of 4 to 6 storeys. [3]
- A single-family neighbourhood lies north of Area B. [3]
- The plan was adopted in 2001, last amended in 2022, and does not set specific zones; it defers to the Zoning Bylaw. [3]
- The City's 2024 small-scale multi-unit housing zoning (RSM) applies to single-family lots outside the five transit-oriented areas, which surround the Bridgeport, Capstan, Aberdeen, Lansdowne and Brighouse stations. [4]

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- The plan requires at least 0.2 ha of public open space in Area C, a perimeter greenway along Steveston Highway and No. 5 Road, and a proposed City "agricultural-park" north of The Gardens. [3]
- Ironwood Library is at 8200-11688 Steveston Highway. [5]
- Ironwood Plaza is a 169,000 sq ft centre anchored, per its developer, by London Drugs and Overwaitea Food Group. [6]

## Schools
- Kingswood Elementary is at 11511 King Road; whether it is the catchment school for any address is UNVERIFIED. [7]
- The Ironwood plan does not mention schools. [3]
- SD38 sets catchments by Board designation and provides a locator. [8]

## Transit
- No bus route or Canada Line station serving Ironwood was confirmed; UNVERIFIED. [9]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- A 3 m landscaped buffer runs along the north edge of Area C to protect the adjacent ALR. [3]
- A 15 m riparian management area runs along Highway 99. [3]
- Parking in Area C must sit below the lowest permitted habitable storey set by the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw. [3]
- Soil conditions are not addressed in the plan (UNVERIFIED); Richmond Fire-Rescue says pockets of liquefaction are possible in a significant earthquake. [3][10]
- Richmond relies on 49 km of dikes. [11]
