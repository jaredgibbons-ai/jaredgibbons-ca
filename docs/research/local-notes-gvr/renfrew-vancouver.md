# Renfrew, Vancouver

## Location
- "Renfrew VE" is an MLS sub-area name; the City-side name is the Renfrew-Collingwood local area, which the Province also lists as a community health service area. Whether "Renfrew VE" is a City local-area name: not listed in the sources found. [1]
- Exact MLS boundary: UNVERIFIED.
- The Rupert and Renfrew Station Area Plan is focused on the Rupert and Renfrew SkyTrain stations. [2]

## Housing and zoning
- Council approved the Rupert and Renfrew Station Area Plan on July 8, 2025, a 30-year guide: towers up to 45 storeys near stations, villages up to six storeys, and multiplex areas that keep R1-1 zoning. [2]
- In January 2026 Council approved zoning updates for the villages: townhouses and low-rise apartments, generally to four storeys strata, six rental and eight below-market rental or social housing. [3]
- Named villages: Rupert and East 22nd, Renfrew and East 22nd, Renfrew and East 1st, with parts of Nanaimo and East Broadway and Nanaimo and East 1st. [3]
- R1-1 (in effect October 17, 2023) replaced the former RS zones and allows multiplexes of up to 6 strata or 8 rental units. [4]
- The Joyce-Collingwood Station Precinct Plan is a separate 30-year framework near that station, stepping heights down toward single-family areas. [5]
- Which plan applies to a specific lot: UNVERIFIED.

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- Renfrew Park Community Centre is at 2929 East 22nd Avenue and includes a fitness centre and pool. [6]
- The Park Board's plan memo names Renfrew Ravine, Still Creek, Rupert Park, Beaconsfield Park and Slocan Park. [7]
- Shopping: UNVERIFIED beyond the villages named above. [3]

## Schools
- Renfrew Community Elementary, 3315 East 22nd Avenue, K-7, is part of the Windermere Family of Schools; VSB states no catchment boundary and links a locator. [8]

## Transit
- TransLink lists Renfrew and Rupert on the Millennium Line and Joyce-Collingwood on the Expo Line. [9]
- Bus routes: UNVERIFIED.

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- The plan includes nature-based flood management along Still Creek, with parts of the creek to be daylit. [2]
- A Groundwater Protection Area in the Building By-law restricts underground structures to protect Still Creek. [3]
- Empty Homes Tax: annual declaration; 3% of assessed value for properties deemed or declared empty in the 2025 reference year (page as retrieved Nov 2026). [10]
