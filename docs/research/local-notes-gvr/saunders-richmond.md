# Saunders, Richmond

Coverage note: no City, school district or transit source tied this MLS name to a boundary. Most items are UNVERIFIED.

## Location
- "Saunders" is an MLS sub-area name; GVR publishes its boundary only on an interactive map, so the street boundary is UNVERIFIED. [1]
- It is not a plan name in the City's list of area and sub-area plans; the City planning area is UNVERIFIED. [2]
- The only sourced tie is the road: the City's Garden City greenway plan recommends designating Saunders Road as an east-west green link, a medium-term action. [3]

## Housing and zoning
- Housing types: UNVERIFIED.
- City-wide, single-family and duplex lots were moved into RSM zones in June 2024 under Provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules; per-lot zoning needs a parcel check. [4]
- The same report says about 84% of the affected lots were previously RS1/A, RS1/B or RS1/E. [5]

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- Named parks inside the sub-area: UNVERIFIED.
- Nearby on the Garden City Road corridor (position relative to the MLS boundary UNVERIFIED): Garden City Community Park, 6620 Garden City Road, 23.76 acres, with a greenway running south along Garden City Road from Granville Avenue to Williams Road. [6]
- South Arm Community Centre is at 8880 Williams Road. [7]

## Schools

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- Richmond is about 1 m above sea level and relies on 49 km of dikes. [11]
- Bylaw 8204 sets a flood plain construction level in its Schedule B; the number was not legible in the copy fetched and is UNVERIFIED. [12]
- Richmond Fire-Rescue says pockets of liquefaction are possible in a significant earthquake. [13]
