# Steveston Village, Richmond

## Location
- "Steveston Village" is both a Greater Vancouver REALTORS® MLS® sub-area name and a character area in the City's Steveston Area Plan, which also contains the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area. [1]
- Boundary: UNVERIFIED. The Area Plan's heritage map labels Chatham Street, 7th Avenue, Railway Avenue, No. 1 Road and Moncton Street along the South Arm of the Fraser River, but its text does not describe the edges. [1]
- Landmarks: Gulf of Georgia Cannery National Historic Site (12138 Fourth Avenue), Britannia Shipyard Park (5180 Westwater Drive), Imperial Landing Park (4000 Bayview). [2][3][4]

## Housing and zoning
- The Heritage Conservation Area was designated under section 614 of the Local Government Act. Subdividing, building, adding to or altering buildings or landscape features generally needs a Heritage Alteration Permit unless exempt. [1]
- The City's permit guide says Council designated the area in 2009; protected heritage properties need a Heritage Alteration Permit for exterior work, and non-heritage properties need a Development Permit, with a mandatory pre-application meeting. [5]
- Along Moncton Street the Area Plan caps buildings at two storeys and 9 m, and it encourages keeping the small historic lots shown on the 1892 lot-line map. [1]
- The Steveston Commercial zone (CS2, CS3) allows 9 m and two storeys (CS2) or 12 m and three storeys (CS3); apartment housing is a secondary use, not allowed on the ground floor within 9 m of a road. Which lots carry these zones: UNVERIFIED. [6]

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- The Area Plan names Steveston Village as the main commercial centre. [1]
- Steveston Community Park (4011 Moncton Street) and the Community Centre (4320 Moncton Street, replacement project underway) are on Moncton Street. [7][8]
- Garry Point Park is at 12011 7th Avenue. [9]

## Schools
- Manoah Steves Elementary (10111 Fourth Ave) and Steveston-London Secondary (6600 Williams Rd) appear on the BC school directory with Steveston addresses. Proximity is UNVERIFIED; no catchment is claimed. [10]

## Transit
- Steveston Exchange (Chatham and Moncton area) is served by routes 401, 402, 406, 407 and 413. [11]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- Bylaw 8204 section 4.4 relaxes the flood construction elevation in the Steveston Village Area where the floor is at or above the fronting sidewalk. [12]
- The Area Plan supports the commercial fishing fleet's home port. The Steveston Harbour Authority leases the harbour from Fisheries and Oceans Canada. [1][13]
- The Cannery is a National Historic Site operated by a non-profit society for Parks Canada. [14]
- Richmond is about 1 m above sea level; dikes are planned to rise from 3.5 m to 4.7 m. [15]
- Liquefaction pockets are possible in a major earthquake. [16]
