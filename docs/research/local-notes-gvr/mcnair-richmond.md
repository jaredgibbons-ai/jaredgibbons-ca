# McNair, Richmond

Coverage note: the MLS name matches a school and park, not a City planning area. Most items are UNVERIFIED.

## Location
- "McNair" is an MLS sub-area name; GVR publishes its boundary only on an interactive map, so the street boundary is UNVERIFIED. [1]
- It is not a plan name in the City's list of area and sub-area plans; the City planning area is UNVERIFIED. [2]
- McNair Neighbourhood School Park, 9460 No. 4 Road, lies north of Williams Road on No. 4 Road and Albion Road, in the City's South Arm recreation area. [3]
- Fire Hall No. 6 is at 9400 No. 4 Road. [4]

## Housing and zoning
- Housing types: UNVERIFIED.
- City-wide, single-family and duplex lots were moved into RSM zones in June 2024 under Provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules; the per-lot zone for any McNair address needs a parcel check. [5]

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- McNair park is 21.75 acres with two sports fields, four ball diamonds, three tennis courts, a playground and walking paths, next to the secondary school. [3]
- South Arm Community Centre is at 8880 Williams Road, beside South Arm Park, which has an outdoor pool and sports fields. [6]
- Shopping: UNVERIFIED.

## Schools
- Matthew McNair Secondary is at 9500 No. 4 Road. [7]
- Elementary schools next to the area: UNVERIFIED. SD38 sets catchments by Board designation and provides a locator. [8]

## Transit
- Bus routes and nearest Canada Line station: UNVERIFIED. [9]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- ALR proximity east of No. 4 Road: UNVERIFIED for this latitude (a City plan to the north states the ALR lies east of No. 4 Road there). [10]
- Richmond is about 1 m above sea level and relies on 49 km of dikes. [11]
- Bylaw 8204 sets a flood plain construction level in its Schedule B; the number was not legible in the copy fetched and is UNVERIFIED. [12]
- Richmond Fire-Rescue says pockets of liquefaction are possible in a significant earthquake. [13]
