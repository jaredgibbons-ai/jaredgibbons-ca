# West End, Vancouver

## Location
- West End is both an MLS sub-area ("West End VW") and a City local area, west of Downtown with water on three sides: English Bay, Coal Harbour and Lost Lagoon in Stanley Park. [1]
- The West End Community Plan area is bounded by West Georgia Street, Burrard Street, Stanley Park and English Bay, about 204 hectares and 112 blocks. [2] MLS boundary versus plan area: UNVERIFIED.
- The plan names three villages, Robson, Denman and Davie, linked as a "West Loop". [2]

## Housing and zoning
- The plan was completed in late 2013. [1]
- It reported about 44,500 residents in 2011, with 81% of households renting and about one-third of the city's purpose-built rental stock. [2]
- Residential areas are mainly RM zones with a mostly six-storey limit; laneway infill is allowed only on sites built before 1975. [2]
- The plan counted about 124 character houses and 121 Heritage Register buildings. [2]
- A May 2026 Council report proposes rezoning 1030 and 1056 Burnaby Street from RM-5A to CD-1 for a six-storey rental building, which staff call consistent with the West End Community Plan. [3]

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- Stanley Park (400 hectares, seawall about 9 km) and English Bay Beach Park appear on the City parks map. [4]
- The plan also names Nelson Park and Barclay Heritage Square. [2]
- West End Community Centre is on Denman Street, with an ice rink, fitness centre, racquet courts and Joe Fortes Library. [5]
- Denman Street has local shops and restaurants; Robson and Alberni Streets have high-end retail; Davie Village is named by the City. [1]

## Schools
- King George Secondary, 1755 Barclay Street. [6]
- Lord Roberts Elementary and Roberts Annex are named in VSB's downtown catchment review, which is not decided: UNVERIFIED outcome. [7]
- The plan also names Lord Roberts School as a historic school in the area. [2]

## Transit
- TransLink lists Burrard Station on the Expo Line; Burrard Street is a plan boundary, but walking distance from specific addresses: UNVERIFIED. [2][8]
- Bus routes: UNVERIFIED.

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- The plan reported about 6,500 resident parking permits against about 3,500 on-street spaces. [2]
- Rental redevelopment needs a tenant relocation plan under the Tenant Relocation and Protection Policy, with compensation of 4 to 24 months' rent by tenancy length. [9]
- The City says that policy replaced the Rate of Change Guidelines for applications received from February 15, 2016. [10]
- The City lists Downtown, False Creek and the Port Lands as a primary flood zone; West End parcel exposure: UNVERIFIED. [11]
- Empty Homes Tax: annual declaration; 3% of assessed value for properties deemed or declared empty in the 2025 reference year (page as retrieved Nov 2026). [12]
