# Point Grey, Vancouver

## Location
- "Point Grey" is a Greater Vancouver REALTORS® (Vancouver West) sub-area name. The City local area with the closest name is West Point Grey, one of the 22 local areas. [1] The MLS boundary: UNVERIFIED.
- Lord Byng Secondary, at 3939 West 16th Avenue, is described by VSB as in the West Point Grey neighbourhood. [2]
- The Municipality of Point Grey was set up in 1908 by separating from South Vancouver; the City history page gives no modern boundary. [3]
- The Jericho Lands lie near Jericho Beach Park and Trimble Park, with NW Marine Drive, Point Grey Road and West 4th Avenue shown on the City's map. [4]

## Housing and zoning
- Low-density lots are in the City's R1-1 zone, which consolidated nine RS zones into one, covering about 65,000 lots. [5]
- R1-1 permits duplexes and single detached houses outright; multiple dwellings (up to 8 units) are conditional and need rear-lane access and a site outside a designated flood plain. [6]
- Which zone applies to a given lot: UNVERIFIED.
- The City ran a policy planning program for the Jericho Lands; the boards retrieved show 2019 engagement and a target policy statement date of 2020-21. Current status: UNVERIFIED. [4]

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- The City parks map shows Jericho, Locarno and Spanish Banks beach parks; Jericho Beach is one of 11 staffed beaches, and Pacific Spirit Regional Park is run by Metro Vancouver. [7]
- West Point Grey Community Centre is at 4397 West 2nd Avenue, with a gym, fitness centre, pottery studio and Aberthau Mansion. [8]
- The City history page records 40 shops on 10th Avenue between Tolmie and Trimble Streets by 1924; current shopping streets: UNVERIFIED. [3]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- The City lists Jericho-Spanish Banks as one of its three primary flood zones, with planning assumptions of about 50 cm of sea level rise by mid-century and 1 m by 2100. [9] Parcel-level exposure: UNVERIFIED.
- Multiple-dwelling eligibility under R1-1 excludes designated flood plain sites. [6]
- A detention tank requirement applies to new single detached houses, duplexes and multiplexes from July 1, 2025. [5]
- Empty Homes Tax: annual declaration; 3% of assessed value for properties deemed or declared empty in the 2025 reference year (page as retrieved Nov 2026). [10]
