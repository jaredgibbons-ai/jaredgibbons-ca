# Garden City, Richmond

## Location
- "Garden City" is an MLS sub-area name, not a City planning-area name. The MLS boundary appears only on GVR's interactive map, so it is UNVERIFIED. [1]
- The City planning area covering it is UNVERIFIED. Garden City Road is the west edge of the City Centre Area Plan's McLennan South sub-area (bounded by Granville Avenue, Blundell Road, Garden City Road and No. 4 Road) and a named road in McLennan North. [2][3]
- Garden City Lands, 5555 No. 4 Road, lies between Westminster Highway, Alderbridge Way, Garden City Road and No. 4 Road, in the City Centre recreation area. [4]

## Housing and zoning
- McLennan South allows townhouses, duplexes, triplexes and single-family lots at about 2.5 to 3 storeys, with up to 3 storeys over parking along Garden City Road. [2]
- That plan caps convenience shops along Garden City Road at 375 m2 per use. [2]
- Single-family lots elsewhere in Richmond were moved to RSM zones in June 2024; zoning for a specific lot is UNVERIFIED without a parcel check. [5]

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- Garden City Community Park, 6620 Garden City Road, covers 23.76 acres with an arboretum, lake, community garden, dog off-leash area and playground. [6]
- Garden City Lands has perimeter gravel paths, a community garden and a KPU farm school zone; visitors may not enter the bog. [4]
- The City's greenway plan recommends designating Saunders Road as an east-west green link to Garden City Road. [7]

## Schools
- Garden City Elementary is at 8311 Garden City Road. [8]
- The McLennan South plan lists Anderson, DeBeck, Garden City and General Currie as the elementary schools its children attend; this reflects the plan's date and is not a current catchment. [2]
- Anderson Elementary is at 9460 Alberta Road. [9]

## Transit
- The McLennan South plan notes bus stops on Garden City Road with service to Vancouver; route numbers are UNVERIFIED. [2]
- Nearest Canada Line station: UNVERIFIED. [10]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- Garden City Lands sits inside the Agricultural Land Reserve, and the City limits public access. [4]
- The ALR lies east of No. 4 Road next to McLennan South. [2]
- Richmond relies on 49 km of dikes; Bylaw 8204 sets a flood plain construction level in its Schedule B, number UNVERIFIED. [11][12]
- Richmond Fire-Rescue says pockets of liquefaction are possible in a significant earthquake. [13]
