## Housing and zoning
- Housing types in the sub-area: UNVERIFIED.
- City-wide, single-family and duplex lots were rezoned in June 2024 into RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L or RSM/XL zones for small-scale multi-unit housing, assigned by prior zone and lot size; about 84% of affected lots were RS1/A, RS1/B or RS1/E. Which zone applies to a given Lackner lot needs a parcel check. [3][4]
