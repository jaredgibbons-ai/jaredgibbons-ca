# Strathcona, Vancouver

## Location
- Strathcona is the name of both an MLS sub-area and a City neighbourhood; the City describes it as a historic neighbourhood east of Chinatown. [1]
- Boundary: UNVERIFIED. The City's Strathcona/Kiwassa RT-3 Guidelines show the RT-3 areas only on a map; streets such as Jackson Avenue, Hawks Avenue, East Pender Street, Princess Avenue and Keefer Street appear as examples, not as boundaries. [2]
- Chinatown's HA-1 and HA-1A heritage zoning applies to the area bounded by Hastings, Union, Gore and Carrall streets, with Pender Street as its historic core. [3]

## Housing and zoning
- Strathcona's character-protection zoning is the RT-3 district schedule, whose stated intent is to keep neighbourhood and streetscape character, mainly by retaining and renovating character buildings, and which refers to pre-1920 architecture. [4]
- Under RT-3, converting a building to up to two units without exterior change is outright; infill, multiple dwellings and many other uses are conditional approvals decided by the Director of Planning. [4]
- The R1-1 zone replaced former RS zones in 2023; RT-3 is a separate district schedule, so R1-1 multiplex rules should not be assumed for RT-3 lots. [4][5]
- The guidelines were last amended July 9, 2024. [2]
- Which district applies to a given lot: UNVERIFIED.

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- Strathcona Community Centre is jointly run by the Park Board and the Strathcona Community Centre Association and has childcare, a fitness centre and a playground. [1]
- A centre at 601 Keefer Street appears in the City's centre list; the list does not name it, so the address match is UNVERIFIED. [6]
- Strathcona Park and shopping streets: UNVERIFIED.

## Schools
- Lord Strathcona Elementary, 592 East Pender Street, K-7. [7]
- Admiral Seymour Elementary, 1130 Keefer Street, K-7. [8]
- VSB pages state no catchment boundaries and link a locator. [7][8]

## Transit
- TransLink lists Stadium-Chinatown and Main Street-Science World on the Expo Line; walking distance is UNVERIFIED. [9]
- Routes R5, 14, 16 and 20 serve Hastings Street, with stops named at Main, Glen, Hawks, Princess and Jackson. [10]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- The City's coastal flood report says False Creek and the Flats are projected to become vulnerable in future; Strathcona-specific flood exposure is UNVERIFIED. [11]
- Empty Homes Tax: annual declaration; 3% of assessed value for properties deemed or declared empty in the 2025 reference year (page as retrieved Nov 2026). [12]
