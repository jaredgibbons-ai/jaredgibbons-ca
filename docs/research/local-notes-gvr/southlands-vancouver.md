# Southlands, Vancouver

## Location
- Southlands is a Greater Vancouver REALTORS® (Vancouver West) sub-area. In the City's 22 local areas it falls within Dunbar-Southlands. [1] MLS boundary: UNVERIFIED.
- The City's South Lands Plan describes it as a low-lying area of southwest Vancouver along the Fraser River, between Marpole and the University Endowment Lands and south of SW Marine Drive. [2]
- The plan names Blenheim Flats ("Horse Village"), West Southlands and the Angus Lands (Angus West and Angus East) as parts of the area. [2]

## Housing and zoning
- The South Lands Plan was approved by Council on March 8, 1988 and last amended June 30, 2024. [2]
- Over two-thirds of the area is zoned RA-1 (Limited Agricultural); most of the rest is RS-1. The plan aims to keep it low-density and semi-rural. [2]
- Single-family zoning was set for Angus West and light industrial/office for Angus East. [2]
- A significant part has been in the Agricultural Land Reserve since 1973; the plan supports that status. [2]
- A 2019 City memo says RA-1 allows a one-family dwelling up to 280 m² outright, and that ALC rules cap ALR homes at 500 m²; about 160 Southlands ALR parcels are under 2 acres and may be exempt after title review. [3]
- The ALC currently allows one principal residence of up to 500 m² without approval; larger or additional residences may need ALC approval. [4]
- Whether R1-1 applies anywhere in Southlands: UNVERIFIED.

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- Land uses named in the plan are golf courses, equestrian properties and nurseries; the Southlands Riding Club is named. [2]
- The plan (1988 figures) counted about 400 horses, mostly in commercial boarding stables. [2]
- Shopping and community centre: UNVERIFIED.

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- The South Lands Plan states most of the area is in the Fraser River floodplain. [2] The City names the Fraser foreshore as its most flood-exposed area. [6]
- ALR status and RA-1 zoning limit house size, extra dwellings and non-farm use; the City says General Order #291/89, which let it approve RA-1 uses without the ALC, was rescinded June 10, 2019. [3]
- Check the ALC and the City before relying on a second dwelling or subdivision for any lot. [4]
- Empty Homes Tax: annual declaration; 3% of assessed value for properties deemed or declared empty in the 2025 reference year (page as retrieved Nov 2026). [7]
