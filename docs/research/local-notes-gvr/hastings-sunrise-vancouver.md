# Hastings Sunrise, Vancouver

## Location
- Hastings-Sunrise is one of the City of Vancouver's 22 local planning areas, covering 8.0 km2 and about 7% of the city's land area. [1]
- The MLS sub-area name matches the City local area name; the MLS boundary itself is UNVERIFIED. [1]
- The City profile describes the boundary only as running "from Broadway and Nanaimo northeast to the city's limits"; other streets are not given, so boundary: UNVERIFIED. [1]
- Hastings Park (PNE, Playland, the Sanctuary) sits at the northeast corner of the city between McGill Street, Hastings Street, Renfrew Street and the Cassiar Connector; the master plan places the Hastings-Sunrise community to its west and south. [2]

## Housing and zoning
- The R1-1 zone replaced all former RS zones from October 17, 2023. It allows single detached houses with a suite or laneway house, duplexes, character retention, and multiplexes of 3 to 6 strata units or up to 8 rental units. [3]
- Which R1-1 or other district applies to a given lot in Hastings-Sunrise is UNVERIFIED; check the City zoning map.
- Council approved the Vancouver Official Development Plan on March 12, 2026; it does not rezone any property itself. [4]
- The City's 2016 census profile showed about 41% of households renting, against 53% citywide. [1]

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- Hastings Community Centre is listed at 3096 E Hastings. [5]
- It faces the PNE and Hastings Park and has a gym, fitness centre, racquetball court, tennis court, playground and water park; it is associated with Templeton Park Pool. [6]
- Shopping areas: UNVERIFIED (no City source found).

## Schools
- Hastings Community Elementary, 2625 Franklin Street, offers K-7 English and French Immersion; Tillicum Annex serves K-4 in English; the school is in the Templeton Secondary family. [7]
- Templeton Secondary School is at 727 Templeton Drive. [8]
- The VSB pages link a catchment locator and state no boundaries; confirm catchment there. [7][8]

## Transit
- Routes R5, 14 and 16 run on Hastings Street between the Iron Workers Memorial Bridge and Boundary Road. [9]
- Bus stop changes are expected in late 2026; proposed bus lanes are on hold, with Renfrew Street to Boundary Road to be reviewed in a corridor study planned for 2027. [9]
- TransLink lists Nanaimo and 29th Avenue stations on the Expo Line; whether they fall inside the area is UNVERIFIED. [10]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- Vancouver homeowners must declare occupancy status every year; properties deemed or declared empty for the 2025 reference year were taxed at 3% of assessed value (page as retrieved Nov 2026). [11]
- The City names the Fraser River foreshore, Jericho-Spanish Banks and Downtown/False Creek/Port Lands as its three primary flood zones; Hastings-Sunrise is not named, but lot-level flood exposure is UNVERIFIED. [12]
