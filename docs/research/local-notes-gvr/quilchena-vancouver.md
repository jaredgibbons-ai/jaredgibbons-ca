# Quilchena, Vancouver

Note: this is Quilchena on Vancouver's west side, not Quilchena in Richmond. The Quilchena golf club that gave the area its name left Vancouver for Richmond in 1953. [1]

## Location
- Quilchena is a Greater Vancouver REALTORS® (Vancouver West) sub-area name, not one of the City's 22 local areas. The City local area (Arbutus Ridge or Kerrisdale): UNVERIFIED. MLS boundary: UNVERIFIED.
- VSB places Quilchena Elementary (5300 Maple Street) and Maple Grove Elementary in Kerrisdale. [2][3]
- Quilchena Park (about 7.7 hectares) is bounded by Valley Drive, 33rd Avenue, the Arbutus rail corridor and 30th Avenue. [4]
- The original golf club's clubhouse stood at 29th Avenue and East Boulevard; the Park Board says the park was created in the 1960s after the course closed and much of the land was developed for housing. [1][4]

## Housing and zoning
- Low-density lots are in the City's R1-1 zone, which consolidated nine RS zones. [5]
- R1-1 permits duplexes and single detached houses outright; multiple dwellings (up to 8 units) are conditional and need rear-lane access and a site outside a designated flood plain. [6]
- Which zone applies to a given lot: UNVERIFIED.

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- Quilchena Park has two ball diamonds, two soccer fields, a disc golf course, a field house, Hellenic Garden and a perimeter path. [7]
- A skateboard park is at 4590 Magnolia Crescent, at the park's northeast corner. [8]
- VSB names Kerrisdale Arena, Kerrisdale Library, Kerrisdale Pool and the Arbutus Greenway as resources near Maple Grove Elementary. [3]
- Shopping streets: UNVERIFIED.

## Schools
- Quilchena Elementary, 5300 Maple Street, about 320 students, English and French Immersion tracks, next to Point Grey Secondary; VSB states an English catchment of roughly Larch to Granville Street and 33rd to 41st Avenue. [2]
- Maple Grove Elementary, 1924 West 45th Avenue, K-7 with a Montessori program, sharing grounds with Magee Secondary; no catchment stated. [3]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- R1-1 multiple-dwelling sites must have been a single lot of record before October 17, 2023. [6]
- A detention tank requirement applies to new single detached houses, duplexes and multiplexes from July 1, 2025. [5]
