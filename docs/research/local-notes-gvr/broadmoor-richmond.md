# Broadmoor, Richmond

## Location
- "Broadmoor" is both an MLS sub-area name and a City of Richmond planning area. The MLS boundary is published only on GVR's interactive map, so it is UNVERIFIED. [1]
- The City's Broadmoor Area contains three sub-area plans: Ash Street (2.6A), Central West (2.6B) and Sunnymede North (2.6C). [2]
- The Central West sub-area plan is bounded by Williams Road (south), No. 3 Road (east), Gilbert Road (west) and the north side of Afton Drive (north). [3]
- A City age-friendly report on Broadmoor labels Steveston Highway, Blundell, Francis, Williams, Gilbert, No. 3, No. 4 and Garden City roads on its map but does not state a boundary in text. [4]

## Housing and zoning
- The Central West plan designates large-lot single-family (R1/E) in the interior, with townhouse lands on the perimeter under development permit guidelines, and aims to strengthen No. 3 Road as an emerging neighbourhood village. [3]
- The plan was adopted in 2001 under Bylaw 7100, so its zone names predate the current Zoning Bylaw 8500. [3]
- In 2024 the City moved lots zoned RS1/E into the new RSM/L zone for small-scale multi-unit housing; the minimum lot area for RSM/L is roughly 550 m2. [5]
- Sunnymede North applies multi-family development permit guidelines and requires noise assessments. [6]

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- South Arm Community Centre is at 8880 Williams Road beside South Arm Park, which has an outdoor pool, tennis courts and sports fields. [7]
- The City's age-friendly report names South Arm Park and the community centre as local gathering places and mentions a shopping mall parking lot without naming the mall (name UNVERIFIED). [4]

## Schools
- Maple Lane Elementary is at 7671 Alouette Drive. [8]
- Hugh McRoberts Secondary is at 8980 Williams Road, on the south edge of the Central West sub-area. [9][3]
- Whether Maple Lane lies inside the MLS boundary is UNVERIFIED. Catchments are set by the SD38 Board; use the SD38 locator. [10]

## Transit
- The City's age-friendly report says Broadmoor has frequent local bus service and limited east-west service; route numbers are UNVERIFIED. [4]
- Nearest Canada Line station: UNVERIFIED. [11]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- Richmond is about 1 m above sea level and relies on 49 km of dikes. [12]
- Bylaw 8204 sets a flood plain construction level in its Schedule B; the number was not legible in the copy fetched and is UNVERIFIED. [13]
- Richmond Fire-Rescue says pockets of liquefaction are possible in a significant earthquake. [14]
- Central West policy calls for tree retention or replacement and a 6 m setback from No. 3 Road. [3]
