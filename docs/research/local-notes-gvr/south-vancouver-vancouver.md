# South Vancouver, Vancouver

## Location
- "South Vancouver" is an MLS sub-area name, not one of the City's 22 local areas. The City local areas that appear to correspond are Sunset, Victoria-Fraserview and Killarney, but the MLS boundary is UNVERIFIED and no City source defining it by streets was found.
- The City's East Fraserlands Policy Statement refers to a combined "Victoria-Fraserview/Killarney" community. [1]
- Sunset Community Centre is on Main Street near East 49th Avenue. [2]

## Housing and zoning
- R1-1 replaced all former RS zones on October 17, 2023, allowing single detached houses with suite or laneway house, duplexes, character retention, and multiplexes of 3 to 6 strata or up to 8 rental units. [3]
- The Vancouver Plan (July 2022) was converted into the city's first Official Development Plan, approved March 12, 2026; the ODP does not rezone any property and future applications must be consistent with it. [4][5]
- Zoning for any specific lot, and any area plan covering it: UNVERIFIED.

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- Sunset Community Centre is listed at 6810 Main Street; it offers childcare, a fitness centre, a dance studio and a playground, and is associated with Sunset Rink and Moberly Arts and Cultural Centre (7646 Prince Albert Street). [2][6]
- Killarney Community Centre is listed at 6260 Killarney Street. [6]
- Shopping: UNVERIFIED.

## Schools
- John Oliver Secondary School, 530 East 41st Avenue, serves grades 8 to 12; VSB gives no catchment boundary and links a locator. [7]
- Elementary schools: UNVERIFIED (not confirmed from VSB pages).

## Transit
- No TransLink source reviewed for this area: UNVERIFIED.

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- The Fraser River floodplain is the area most vulnerable to flooding in Vancouver, running from Boundary Road to Musqueam; whether an MLS "South Vancouver" lot sits in it is UNVERIFIED. [8]
- Provincial planning guidance asks for about 1 m of sea level rise by 2100. [9]
- Empty Homes Tax: annual declaration; 3% of assessed value for properties deemed or declared empty in the 2025 reference year (page as retrieved Nov 2026). [10]
