# Steveston South, Richmond

## Location
- "Steveston South" is a Greater Vancouver REALTORS® MLS® sub-area name, not a City of Richmond planning-area name. The City plan covering the ground is the Steveston Area Plan. [1]
- Boundary: UNVERIFIED. No City source reviewed defines the MLS sub-area edges. [1]
- Within the Area Plan, the Trites Area is bounded by Trites Road (west), No. 2 Road (east), Andrews Road (south) and Moncton Street (north). Whether it falls inside this MLS area is UNVERIFIED. [1]
- Landmarks on the South Arm waterfront include Garry Point Park (12011 7th Avenue) and the Steveston Harbour Authority site at 12740 Trites Road. [2][3]

## Housing and zoning
- The Area Plan supports two- and three-storey townhouses and affordable housing along the east side of the Trites Area (No. 2 Road), while keeping existing single-family character elsewhere in Steveston. [1]
- The Area Plan limits residential buildings at the BC Packers waterfront node to four storeys over parking. [1]
- Zoning for any specific lot: UNVERIFIED (check the City zoning map and Zoning Bylaw 8500). [4]

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- Garry Point Park is a waterfront park with open lawn, a Japanese-style memorial garden (Kuno Garden), the Fisherman's Memorial Needle and Scotch Pond heritage moorage. The City advises the river is unsafe for swimming. [2]
- Britannia Shipyard Park (5180 Westwater Drive) and Imperial Landing Park (4000 Bayview) are linked riverfront parks with heritage buildings and a boardwalk. [5][6]
- Steveston Village is the main commercial centre under the Area Plan. [1]

## Schools
- Public schools with Steveston-area addresses on the BC school directory: Manoah Steves Elementary (10111 Fourth Ave) and Steveston-London Secondary (6600 Williams Rd). Their position relative to this MLS area is UNVERIFIED; no catchment is claimed. [7]

## Transit
- Steveston Exchange is served by routes 401, 402, 406, 407 and 413 per TransLink's exchange map. [8]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- The Paramount node is a working commercial fishing harbour; the Area Plan supports land uses that service the fleet. [1]
- The Steveston Harbour Authority is a non-profit that leases the harbour from Fisheries and Oceans Canada. [3]
- The Gulf of Georgia Cannery (12138 Fourth Avenue) is a National Historic Site beside the harbour. [9]
- The City describes Richmond as roughly 1 m above sea level, protected by dikes it plans to raise from 3.5 m to 4.7 m. [10]
- Under Bylaw 8204, new buildings must be set back 7.5 m from a standard dike right of way. [11]
- The proposed Steveston Island dike would run about 3.3 km from Garry Point Park to London Landing, with crest heights of 4.7 to 5.5 m and a multi-decade timeline. [12]
- Liquefaction pockets are possible in a major earthquake. [13]
