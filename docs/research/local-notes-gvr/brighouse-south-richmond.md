# Brighouse South, Richmond

## Location
- "Brighouse South" is a Greater Vancouver REALTORS® sub-area; GVR publishes its boundaries only on an interactive map, so the street boundary is UNVERIFIED. [1]
- It is not a City of Richmond planning-area name. The nearest City ground is the City Centre Area Plan, which names Brighouse and Lansdowne as separate villages; which parts of each fall inside the MLS sub-area is UNVERIFIED. [2]
- Minoru Park sits north of Granville Avenue between Minoru Boulevard and Gilbert Road, in the City's City Centre recreation area. [3]

## Housing and zoning
- The City Centre Area Plan describes Brighouse Village as a civic and mixed-use focus of about 141 ha, with a projected 26,000 to 30,000 residents. [4]
- The Brighouse Village transect map sets height tiers of 15 m, 25 m and 45 m (T4, T5, T6) and says the Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development policy applies throughout the village. [5]
- Small-scale multi-unit housing zoning (RSM/S to RSM/XL, adopted June 2024) does not apply inside the five transit-oriented areas, which include Brighouse and Lansdowne. [6]
- The Lansdowne Centre master plan (about 20 years, fourth reading November 2023) is phased, starting in the northeast corner of the parking lot; whether it lies inside this MLS area is UNVERIFIED. [7]

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- Minoru Park, 7191 Granville Avenue, has artificial-turf fields, a track, tennis courts, lawn bowling greens and lakes. [3]
- The City lists Brighouse Library at 7700 Minoru Gate, City Centre Community Centre at 5900 Minoru Boulevard, and Minoru Centre for Active Living at 7191 Granville Avenue. [8]
- CF Richmond Centre is at 6551 No. 3 Road. [9]

## Schools
- Richmond Secondary is at 7171 Minoru Boulevard. [10]
- General Currie Elementary is at 8220 General Currie Road. [11]
- Whether either school is "next to" the MLS boundary is UNVERIFIED. SD38 says students attend the catchment school the Board designates and provides a school locator. [12]

## Transit
- TransLink's Richmond and Delta map shows Richmond-Brighouse and Lansdowne as Canada Line stations; Richmond-Brighouse is grouped with routes 301, 401 to 406, 408, 410, 414, 416 and 430, and Lansdowne with 403, 405, 407, 410 and 416. [13]
- Which stop is nearest to a given address is UNVERIFIED; the CF Richmond Centre site names Richmond-Brighouse as the nearest station to the mall. [9]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- Richmond is about 1 m above sea level and relies on 49 km of dikes, which the City is raising from 3.5 m to 4.7 m. [14]
- Bylaw 8204 requires floor levels and flood-susceptible equipment at or above a flood plain construction level set in its Schedule B; the number was not legible in the copy fetched, so it is UNVERIFIED. [15]
- Richmond Fire-Rescue says pockets of liquefaction are possible in a significant earthquake. [16]
