# Victoria, Vancouver

## Location
- "Victoria VE" is a Greater Vancouver REALTORS® (Vancouver East) sub-area name, not one of the City's 22 local areas. Its boundary: UNVERIFIED. Which City local area it sits in: UNVERIFIED (candidates are the two below).
- Victoria-Fraserview is a City local area running from 41st Avenue south to the Fraser River and from Knight Street west to Earles, Vivian and Elliot Streets east. [1]
- Kensington-Cedar Cottage is the City local area to the north: Fraser Street to Nanaimo Street, with 41st Avenue as its southern edge. [2]
- Trout Lake sits inside John Hendry Park at East 15th Avenue and Victoria Drive, in Kensington-Cedar Cottage. [3]

## Housing and zoning
- Victoria-Fraserview has more ground-oriented housing than the city average; apartments were about 25% of stock, all under five storeys (2016 census data). [1]
- Kensington-Cedar Cottage had about 44% renter households and about 30% apartments, with a high concentration of semi-detached and duplex housing (2016 data). [2]
- The City's R1-1 zone consolidated nine low-density RS zones, about 65,000 lots. [5]
- Under R1-1, duplexes and single detached houses are outright; multiple dwellings (up to 8 units) are conditional and need rear-lane access and a site outside a designated flood plain. [4]

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- Kensington Community Centre is at Knight Street and East 37th Avenue, with a 15-metre indoor pool, fitness centre, seniors' centre and skateboard park. [6]
- John Hendry Park covers 27.21 hectares, includes the Trout Lake Community Centre and a 1.2 km path around the lake. [3]

- TransLink's N20 night route is titled "Victoria" and runs between Howe at Dunsmuir and Marine Drive Station, with a stop at Victoria and 41 Avenue. [9]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- The City identifies the Fraser River foreshore as its most flood-exposed area; whether any Victoria VE parcel falls in it: UNVERIFIED. [11]
- R1-1 multiple-dwelling eligibility excludes designated flood plain sites (see above). [4]
- The Tenant Relocation and Protection Policy applies citywide to rezoning and development permit applications affecting renters; it replaced the Rate of Change Guidelines for applications received from February 15, 2016. [13]
- Empty Homes Tax: annual declaration; 3% of assessed value for properties deemed or declared empty in the 2025 reference year (page as retrieved Nov 2026). [12]
