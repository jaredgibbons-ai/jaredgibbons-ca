# Renfrew Heights, Vancouver

## Location
- "Renfrew Heights" is an MLS sub-area name and is not one of the City's 22 local areas; the City local area expected to contain it is Renfrew-Collingwood, which the Province lists as a community health service area. [1]
- Boundary: UNVERIFIED. No City source found that defines Renfrew Heights by streets.
- Renfrew Park Community Centre is at East 22nd Avenue and Renfrew Street; whether it lies inside the MLS sub-area is UNVERIFIED. [2]

## Housing and zoning
- Former RS-zoned areas became R1-1 on October 17, 2023, allowing single detached houses with a suite or laneway house, duplexes, character retention, and multiplexes of 3 to 6 strata units or up to 8 rental units. [3]
- The Rupert and Renfrew Station Area Plan (approved July 8, 2025) keeps current R1-1 zoning in its "multiplex areas" furthest from the stations, with towers up to 45 storeys near stations and villages up to six storeys. [4]
- January 2026 zoning updates for villages at Renfrew and East 22nd, Rupert and East 22nd, and Renfrew and East 1st allow townhouses and low-rise apartments. [5]
- Which plan area includes a given Renfrew Heights lot: UNVERIFIED.

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- Renfrew Park Community Centre, 2929 East 22nd Avenue, has a fitness centre and pool. [6]
- The Park Board names Renfrew Ravine, Rupert Park, Beaconsfield Park and Slocan Park in the station area plan. [7]
- Shopping: UNVERIFIED.

## Schools
- Renfrew Community Elementary, 3315 East 22nd Avenue, K-7, is part of the Windermere Family of Schools; VSB links a catchment locator and gives no boundary. [8]

## Transit
- TransLink lists Renfrew and Rupert stations on the Millennium Line. [9]
- Walking distance and bus routes from Renfrew Heights: UNVERIFIED.

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- Still Creek flood management and daylighting are part of the station area plan. [4]
- A Building By-law Groundwater Protection Area restricts underground structures to protect Still Creek. [5]
- Empty Homes Tax: annual declaration; 3% of assessed value for properties deemed or declared empty in the 2025 reference year (page as retrieved Nov 2026). [10]
