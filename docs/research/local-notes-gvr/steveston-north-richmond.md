# Steveston North, Richmond

## Location
- "Steveston North" is a Greater Vancouver REALTORS® MLS® sub-area name, not a City of Richmond planning-area name. The City plan that covers Steveston is the Steveston Area Plan. [1]
- Boundary: UNVERIFIED. No City source reviewed defines the MLS sub-area edges. [1]
- For the Bill 44 rezoning deferral, the City describes a single-family area north of the historic commercial district, bounded by Steveston Highway (north), No. 1 Road (east), Chatham Street (south) and 7th Avenue (west). Whether this matches the MLS sub-area is UNVERIFIED. [2]
- Nearby landmark: Steveston Community Park at 4011 Moncton Street, at No. 1 Road. [3]

## Housing and zoning
- The Steveston Area Plan sets a goal of keeping and enhancing the existing single-family character of Steveston. [1]
- On June 24, 2024 Council changed zoning on almost 27,000 single-family and duplex lots to allow small-scale multi-unit housing under provincial Bill 44. [2]
- For the area described above, the Province extended the rezoning deadline to December 30, 2030; the City had asked for time because of its Steveston sanitary sewer replacement program. [2]
- The Zoning Bylaw 8500 residential list includes RS1/RS2 single detached zones and RSM small-scale multi-unit zones. Which zone applies to a given lot: UNVERIFIED (check the City zoning map). [4]

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- Steveston Community Park has ball diamonds, tennis courts, a playground, a seasonal waterplay area, an off-leash dog area and a seasonal outdoor pool. [3]
- Steveston Community Centre is at 4320 Moncton Street; the City lists a replacement project as underway and the original building as demolished. [5]
- The Area Plan names Steveston Village as the main commercial centre. [1]

## Schools
- Public schools with Steveston-area street addresses on the BC school directory: Manoah Steves Elementary (10111 Fourth Ave), Steveston-London Secondary (6600 Williams Rd), Hugh McRoberts Secondary (8980 Williams Rd), Hugh Boyd Secondary (9200 No. 1 Rd). Their position relative to this MLS area is UNVERIFIED, and no catchment is claimed. [6]

## Transit
- Steveston Exchange is served by routes 401, 402, 406, 407 and 413 per TransLink's exchange map. Which routes pass this MLS area: UNVERIFIED. [7]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- Richmond sits on Fraser River delta islands averaging about 1 m above sea level; the City cites flooding from the river, the ocean and heavy rain. [8]
- The City plans to raise dikes from 3.5 m to 4.7 m and designs for a 1-in-500-year flood. [9]
- Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets minimum construction levels (the Schedule B figures were not reviewed) and dike setbacks of 7.5 m where a standard dike exists. [10]
- The Steveston Island dike concept runs about 3.3 km from Garry Point Park to London Landing, with a multi-decade timeline. [11]
- The City says pockets of liquefaction are possible in a major earthquake; not all ground would liquefy. [12]
