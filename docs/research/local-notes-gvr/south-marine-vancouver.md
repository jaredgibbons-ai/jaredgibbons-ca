# South Marine, Vancouver

## Location
- "South Marine" is an MLS sub-area name and is not one of the City's 22 local areas. Boundary: UNVERIFIED. No City source found that defines it by streets.
- The City's East Fraserlands Policy Statement places that site in the southeast corner of the city on the Fraser River, between Kerr Street and Boundary Road and south of Marine Way, within the Victoria-Fraserview/Killarney community. [1]
- Whether East Fraser Lands (River District) is inside the MLS sub-area is UNVERIFIED.

## Housing and zoning
- East Fraser Lands is guided by an Official Development Plan (2006, amended 2017) with separate rezoning policy for Area 1, Area 2 and the Kerr Street properties. [2]
- The 2004 policy statement set a target maximum of 10,000 residents on about 126 acres. [1]
- Former RS-zoned lots elsewhere became R1-1 on October 17, 2023, allowing multiplexes of 3 to 6 strata or up to 8 rental units. [3]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- The Fraser River floodplain is the area most vulnerable to flooding in Vancouver, running from Boundary Road to Musqueam, and is largely non-diked. [5]
- The City's flood assessment describes flood protection near East Fraser Lands as rock revetments and retaining walls, and notes increasing higher-density residential there. [6]
- In 2014 the City adopted a Flood Construction Level for the floodplain with a sea level rise allowance. [5]
- The Province told municipalities in 2011 to plan for about 1 m of sea level rise by 2100. [5]
- Some residents questioned continued floodplain development such as East Fraser Lands. [5]
- Empty Homes Tax: annual declaration; 3% for properties deemed or declared empty in the 2025 reference year (page as retrieved Nov 2026). [7]
