# Westwind, Richmond

## Location
- "Westwind" is a Greater Vancouver REALTORS® MLS® sub-area name. The City's archive describes it as a subdivision created in the late 1970s, which gave its name to Westwind Elementary. It is not a separate City planning area in the sources reviewed; the nearest City plan is the Steveston Area Plan. [1][2]
- Boundary: UNVERIFIED. The City archive page names nearby roads, but that text could not be reconciled with other maps, so no streets are stated here. [1]
- SD38 places Westwind School in southwest Richmond, near Steveston and No. 2 Road. [3]
- The City's roads map labels "Westwind" as a neighbourhood name. Its exact position: UNVERIFIED. [4]

## Housing and zoning
- Housing types: UNVERIFIED for this sub-area. The City archive describes Westwind as a newly created subdivision when its school opened in 1979. [1]
- City-wide, Council amended zoning in June 2024 for almost 27,000 single-family and duplex lots to allow small-scale multi-unit housing. Whether Westwind lots are included: UNVERIFIED. [5]
- Zoning for a specific lot: check the City zoning map; Zoning Bylaw 8500 lists RS1/RS2 single detached and RSM small-scale multi-unit zones. [6]

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- Steveston Community Park (4011 Moncton Street) and Garry Point Park (12011 7th Avenue) are the City parks reviewed in the Steveston area. Distance from Westwind: UNVERIFIED. [7][8]
- Shopping: UNVERIFIED.

## Schools
- Westwind Elementary, 11371 Kingfisher Dr, K-7; SD38 lists about 450 students. [9][3]
- Other public schools with Steveston-area addresses: Steveston-London Secondary (6600 Williams Rd), Hugh Boyd Secondary (9200 No. 1 Rd). No catchment is claimed. [9]

## Transit
- Routes serving Westwind: UNVERIFIED. Steveston Exchange is served by routes 401, 402, 406, 407 and 413. [10]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- Richmond averages about 1 m above sea level, with flood risk from the river, ocean and rainfall. [11]
- The City plans to raise dikes from 3.5 m to 4.7 m. [12]
- Bylaw 8204 sets flood construction levels (Schedule B figures were not reviewed) and dike setbacks. [13]
- The City says pockets of liquefaction are possible in a major earthquake. [14]
