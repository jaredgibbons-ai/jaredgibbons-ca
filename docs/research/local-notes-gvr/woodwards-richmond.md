# Woodwards, Richmond

## Location
- "Woodwards" is a Greater Vancouver REALTORS® MLS® sub-area name, not a City of Richmond planning-area name. Which City planning area covers it: UNVERIFIED. [1]
- Boundary: UNVERIFIED. No City or SD38 source reviewed defines it. [1]
- Caution: City sources use "Woodward" for places that may not be this sub-area. Woodwards Slough is a greenbelt at 9400 Dyke Road between the south end of Garden City Road and No. 4 Road. Woodward Neighbourhood School Park (10300 Seacote Road) is in Shellmont, beside Daniel Woodward Elementary. Neither establishes the MLS boundary. [2][3]
- The BC school directory lists Jessie Wowk Elementary at 5380 Woodwards Rd. [4]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- Richmond averages about 1 m above sea level with river, ocean and rainfall flood risk. [7]
- The City plans to raise dikes from 3.5 m to 4.7 m and designs for a 1-in-500-year flood. [8]
- Bylaw 8204 sets flood construction levels (Schedule B figures were not reviewed) and a 7.5 m setback from a standard dike right of way. [9]
- The City says pockets of liquefaction are possible in a major earthquake; the BC Building Code requires designers to account for soil amplification and liquefaction. [10][11]
