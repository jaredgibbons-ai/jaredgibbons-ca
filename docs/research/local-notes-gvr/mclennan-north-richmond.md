# McLennan North, Richmond

## Location
- McLennan North matches a City planning name: the McLennan North Sub-Area Plan (Schedule 2.10C), part of the City Centre Area Plan. The MLS boundary itself is published only on GVR's interactive map and is UNVERIFIED. [1][2]
- The plan names Garden City Road (west), No. 4 Road (east, next to agricultural land), Westminster Highway, Cook Road and Alberta Road; its exact boundary appears only on a map not readable in the text. [3]
- The plan's community park and school site has frontages on Granville Avenue, No. 4 Road and Alberta Road. [3]

## Housing and zoning
- The plan calls for single-family homes, townhouses and apartments, with apartments up to 45 m along Garden City Road and lower townhouse and single- and two-family forms toward No. 4 Road. [3]
- All multiple-family sites are development permit areas. The plan gives no zoning districts, so zone names for specific lots are UNVERIFIED. [3]
- The plan's land use map was amended in 2022, and a 2022 bylaw redesignated 9511 Granville Avenue as School. [3][4]

## Parks, community facilities and shopping
- The plan requires a community park and school site of at least 83,000 m2, two neighbourhood parks of at least 0.71 ha each, and a public trail at least 5 m wide on the east side of Garden City Road. [3]
- MacNeill Neighbourhood School Park, 6611 No. 4 Road, covers 20.36 acres with sports fields, a track and a community garden. [5]
- A mixed-use area at Cook and Alberta Roads is planned as the gateway to the park. [3]

## Schools
- A.R. MacNeill Secondary is the secondary school beside that park. [5]
- Anderson Elementary is at 9460 Alberta Road, one of the park-site frontages; its status as the plan's elementary school is UNVERIFIED. [6][3]
- Catchments are set by the SD38 Board; use the SD38 locator. [7]

## Transit
- The plan proposes a local shuttle bus and does not mention a Canada Line station. [3]
- Bus routes serving the area: UNVERIFIED. [8]

## Risks and rules a buyer should know
- The No. 4 Road side borders agricultural land; confirm ALR status by parcel. [3]
- Richmond relies on 49 km of dikes; Bylaw 8204 sets a flood plain construction level in its Schedule B, number UNVERIFIED. [9][10]
- Richmond Fire-Rescue says pockets of liquefaction are possible in a significant earthquake. [11]
