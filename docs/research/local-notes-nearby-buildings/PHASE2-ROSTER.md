Built Mon Nov 2, 2026, from the three local-notes program summaries and their count-home files (55-plus, condos, townhomes). This is the definitive pre-production roster for phase 2: one What's Nearby file per named building below, scoped to the building's street address + 1 km walking radius, every measured time starting at the building's address. No phase-2 building files have been written yet — this pass is scoping only.

## How this roster was built

- Every VERIFIED counted building/complex was extracted from its count-HOME file per each program summary's count-once positions: 55-plus 111, condos 583, townhomes 365 located of 366 claimed (see the discrepancy note at the end of this file).
- Names and street addresses are EXACTLY as the notes state them. No address was looked up or invented. Where the notes give no street address the entry reads ADDRESS NOT IN NOTES; where the notes carry conflicting addresses open, the entry reads ADDRESS CONFLICT with both addresses.
- EXCLUDED per the phase-2 rules: queue items, candidates, tenure records, presales, directory-only/unverified names, and excluded-tenure records. That exclusion removes exactly 4 of the 55-plus 111 — the co-op/membership complexes Sullivan Mews (east-newton), Pioneer Park (aldergrove), St. George's Village (cottonwood-mr) and St. Andrew's Place (mission-homes-for-sale). The 55-plus program's 14 manufactured-home parks and 4 life-lease complexes are verified named complexes inside the counted 111, are not in the exclusion list, and ARE on the roster (class noted on each line).
- DEDUPE (count-once): a building counted by two or three programs is ONE roster entry listing all its programs — including component splits the programs count separately (e.g. The Pillars, Muse North Delta, Kings Landing II, Highstreet Village, Vista 29). 59 buildings are multi-program. Where the programs disagree on the home file, the disagreement is recorded on the entry line and in the closing section — it is NOT resolved here. Where townhomes files a shared building in the '-guide' variant of the same area slug, the entry sits under the base slug and the line does not treat that as a disagreement.
- Production order: grouped by home area slug alphabetically; buildings alphabetical within each group (leading 'The' ignored). Numbering is continuous across the file.
