## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Proposed Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) Development Permit Area; 14,000+ eligible lots Township-wide, 2026 report (https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf).
- Undeveloped Willoughby land's SSMUH eligibility depends on rezoning and subdivision (https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf).
- Interior renovations, including a secondary suite, are exempt from that permit (https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf).
Read: 2026-11-03

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Are school catchments changing on the Willoughby slope? **A:** Yes, from September 2027. New catchments take effect then, tied to new Smith schools opening that September: stɑtl̓əw̓ Middle and Emery Dosdall Secondary. Willoughby Elementary then feeds Yorkson Creek Middle and Emery Dosdall Secondary; Josette Dandurand Elementary feeds Yorkson Creek Middle and R.E. Mountain Secondary (https://www.sd35.bc.ca/willoughby-slope-catchment-boundary-changes.21343).
- **Q:** How do I check a specific home's school? **A:** Use the district's school locator, which finds the catchment school from an address or development name. The September 2027 catchments load into it in fall 2026, before January 2027 registration, so confirm which school year a 2026 result belongs to (https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator) (https://www.sd35.bc.ca/willoughby-slope-catchment-boundary-changes.21343).
- **Q:** What is planned for 200 Street? **A:** A bus rapid transit corridor. The Township states TransLink's proposed service along 200 Street drives the Willoughby Community Plan update, the draft corridor plan includes five stations envisioned as mixed-use neighbourhood hubs, and the plan package was deferred to the May 25, 2026 Council meeting (https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/willoughby-community-plan-update.aspx).
- **Q:** What did the Yorkson park upgrade add? **A:** A completed north campus. The Township states the $20.5 million project added a spray park, its largest accessible playground, a synthetic turf field and a 75-stall parking lot at 84 Avenue, complete and open per the Township (https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/yorkson-community-park.aspx).
Read: 2026-11-03

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- 2026 flat-rate utilities, single-family: water $779.96, sewer $716.47; suite adds $233.99 and $214.94 (https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx).
- Taxes and utilities both due July 2, 2026, billed separately (https://www.tol.ca/en/news/information-about-property-taxes-utility-fees.aspx).
- Missed taxes: 5% at July 2, further 5% after September 3, 2026 (https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx).
- Township of Langley is a Speculation and Vacancy Tax taxable area (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas).
Read: 2026-11-03
