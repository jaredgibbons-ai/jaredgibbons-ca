# Webster's Corners — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No rapid-transit station is in this area. Nearest rapid-transit station: Port Haney Station (West Coast Express), 22300 River Road, Maple Ridge, to the west. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Nearest bus exchange: Haney Place, served by TransLink Routes 701 and 749. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt701.pdf
- Route 701 Coquitlam Central Station (TransLink): east on Dewdney Trunk Rd past this area; most trips start at Dewdney Trunk at 248 St, and some weekday trips run from Mission City Station via Haney Place. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt701.pdf
- Route 749 Ruskin/Haney Place (TransLink): loop from Haney Place via Dewdney Trunk Rd and Garibaldi St, through this area, to 280 St at Lougheed Hwy. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt749.pdf

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- BCIT Woodlot at Webster's Corners: a network of mountain-bike trails (City of Maple Ridge). https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Kanaka Creek Regional Park (Metro Vancouver): Canyon Trail to Cliff Falls, Riverfront Trails and the Bell-Irving Hatchery, at the area's west side. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- Maple Ridge Leisure Centre, 11925 Haney Place. https://www.mapleridge.ca/art-parks-rec/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre
- Maple Ridge Library, 130-22470 Dewdney Trunk Road. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations
