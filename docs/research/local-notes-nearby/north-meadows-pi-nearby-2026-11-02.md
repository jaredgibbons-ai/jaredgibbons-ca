# North Meadows (Pitt Meadows) — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No SkyTrain station is in North Meadows; the nearest rail station named on a TransLink timetable read is Pitt Meadows Station (West Coast Express), south of Lougheed Hwy, served by 701. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt701.pdf
- Pitt Meadows Station is a West Coast Express station. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Bus 701 Coquitlam Central Station / Haney Place (TransLink) via Harris Rd and Pitt Meadows Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt701.pdf
- Bus 722 Bonson / Meadowtown (TransLink) via Harris Rd and 122 Ave through north Pitt Meadows. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt722.pdf
- R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus (TransLink) runs along Lougheed Hwy at area's south edge, between Coquitlam Central and Haney Place; TransLink's RapidBus page states routes run at least every 10 minutes at peak and every 15 minutes off-peak. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
