# East Central — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest rapid-transit station: Port Haney Station (West Coast Express), 22300 River Road, Maple Ridge. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Nearest bus exchange: Haney Place, served by TransLink Routes 701 and 745. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt701.pdf
- Route 701 Coquitlam Central Station (TransLink): Haney Place via Maple Meadows Station to Coquitlam Central Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt701.pdf
