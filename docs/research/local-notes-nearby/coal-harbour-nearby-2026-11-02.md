# Coal Harbour — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest rapid-transit stations are Waterfront Station and Burrard Station, south-east of Coal Harbour in downtown Vancouver, shown on TransLink's system map with Expo Line, Canada Line, SeaBus and West Coast Express at Waterfront. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/system-maps/september-2026/metro-vancouver-transit-map.pdf
- Route 5 Downtown, TransLink, runs via Robson St through the downtown core south of Coal Harbour. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/5/direction/0/schedule
- Route 19 Kingsway / Stanley Park, TransLink, serves West Pender St at Burrard St, per TransLink stop information in this pass. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/stop/50179/schedule
