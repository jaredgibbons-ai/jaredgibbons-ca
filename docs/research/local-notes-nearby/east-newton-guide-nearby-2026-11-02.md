# East Newton — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest SkyTrain station: King George Station, on the route 321 corridor through Newton Exchange. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt321.pdf)
- Nearest bus exchange: Newton Exchange, the south terminus of the R1 King George Boulevard RapidBus. (https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects)
- R1 King George Boulevard, TransLink: Newton Exchange to Guildford; RapidBus routes run at least every 10 minutes at peak and every 15 minutes off-peak. (https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects)
- Route 321, TransLink: White Rock / Newton Exchange / King George Station / Surrey Central Station. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt321.pdf)
- Route 324, TransLink: Newton Exchange to Surrey Central Station via 132 Street. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt324.pdf)
