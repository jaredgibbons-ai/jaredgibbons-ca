# Tsawwassen East — What's Nearby: Local Notes

- Nearest bus exchange: South Delta Exchange, terminus on TransLink timetables for routes 601 and 619. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt619.pdf

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Boundary Bay Regional Park, Boundary Bay Road, Delta: Metro Vancouver regional park with Centennial Beach, Dyke Trail (17 km), Savannah and Raptor trails. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park
- Diefenbaker Park, 5579 1 Ave, Delta: City park with picnic shelter, playground, trails, water park. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/diefenbaker-park
- South Delta Recreation Centre, 1720 56 Street, Delta, V4L 2B1: City facility with arena, curling rink, gymnasium. http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/south-delta-recreation-centre
- Tsawwassen Library, 1321A 56th Street, Delta, V4L 2A6: Fraser Valley Regional Library branch. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/tsawwassen-library
