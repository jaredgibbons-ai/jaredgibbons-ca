# Tsawwassen East — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest rapid-transit station reached by bus: Bridgeport Station, Richmond, reached from South Delta Exchange on TransLink route 601. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Nearest bus exchange: South Delta Exchange, named as the terminus on TransLink timetables for routes 601 and 619. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt619.pdf
- Route 601 Bridgeport Station / South Delta, TransLink: selected trips run to Boundary Bay via 12 Avenue, Boundary Bay Road, 3 Avenue and 67 Street, continuing via South Delta Exchange to Bridgeport Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Route 619 Boundary Bay / South Delta Exchange, TransLink: from South Delta Exchange via 12 Avenue, Boundary Bay Road, 3 Avenue and 67 Street to 1A Avenue. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt619.pdf

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Boundary Bay Regional Park, Boundary Bay Road, Delta: Metro Vancouver regional park with Centennial Beach, the Dyke Trail (17 km) and the Savannah and Raptor trails. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park
- Diefenbaker Park, 5579 1 Ave, Delta: City of Delta park with picnic shelter, playground, trails and water park. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/diefenbaker-park
- South Delta Recreation Centre, 1720 56 Street, Delta, V4L 2B1: City of Delta facility with arena, curling rink and gymnasium. http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/south-delta-recreation-centre
- Tsawwassen Library, 1321A 56th Street, Delta, V4L 2A6: Fraser Valley Regional Library branch. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/tsawwassen-library
