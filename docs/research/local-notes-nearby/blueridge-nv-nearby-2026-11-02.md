# Blueridge — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No SkyTrain, West Coast Express or SeaBus station is in Blueridge; the area's bus anchor is Phibbs Exchange, named as the terminus of route 214 in TransLink's timetable. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt214.pdf
- Route 214 Blueridge / Phibbs Exchange / Burrard Station (TransLink) runs from Sechelt at Hyannis via Berkley, Carnation, Emerson, Mt Seymour Parkway, Riverside Dr and Dollarton Hwy to Phibbs Exchange, some morning trips continuing to Vancouver. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt214.pdf
