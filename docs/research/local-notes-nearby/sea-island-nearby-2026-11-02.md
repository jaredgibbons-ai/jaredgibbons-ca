# Sea Island — What's Nearby: Local Notes

- The Canada Line stations on Sea Island are Templeton Station, Sea Island Centre Station and YVR-Airport Station, per TransLink's Buzzer. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2010/01/canada-line-yvr-addfare-coming-mon-jan-18-heres-how-it-works/
