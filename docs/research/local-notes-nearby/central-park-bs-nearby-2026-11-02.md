# Central Park — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Patterson Station (Expo Line) is at the park's north-east corner; Metrotown Station (Expo Line) is east of the park. TransLink lists buses 19, 31, 49, 116 and 130 from the Patterson area to Metrotown Station. https://www.translink.ca/news/2026/october/translink%20replacing%2040-year-old%20elevator%20at%20patterson%20skytrain%20station
- Route 129 Patterson Station / Holdom Station, TransLink, via Sardis St and Moscrop St north of the park. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt129.pdf
- Route 19, TransLink, runs on Kingsway to Metrotown Station, per TransLink. https://www.translink.ca/news/2026/october/translink%20replacing%2040-year-old%20elevator%20at%20patterson%20skytrain%20station

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Central Park, 3883 Imperial St, City of Burnaby park with an outdoor fitness circuit, per the City. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/outdoor-fitness-circuits
- Bonsor Recreation Complex, 6550 Bonsor Avenue, City of Burnaby, at Metrotown east of the park. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/bonsor-recreation-complex
- Bob Prittie Metrotown Branch is one of four Burnaby Public Library branches, per the City of Burnaby. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/city-departments/burnaby-public-library
