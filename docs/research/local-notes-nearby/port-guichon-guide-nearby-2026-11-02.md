# Port Guichon — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest rapid-transit station reached by bus: Bridgeport Station, Richmond, the terminus of TransLink route 601 from Ladner Exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Nearest bus exchange: Ladner Exchange, terminus on TransLink timetables for routes 601, 609 and 616. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt616.pdf
- Route 616 Ladner South / Ladner Exchange, TransLink: loop from Ladner Exchange via 46A Street to River Road West, serving the stop at 46A Street at River Road West. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt616.pdf
- Route 601 Bridgeport Station / South Delta, TransLink: runs via Ladner Exchange, Ladner Trunk Road and Highway 99 to Bridgeport Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Route 609 South Delta Exchange / Ladner Exchange, TransLink: TransLink states all-day 30-minute service, seven days a week, from January 5, 2026. https://www.translink.ca/news/2025/december/translink%20boosts%20winter%20bus%20service%20on%2037%20metro%20vancouver%20routes
