# Eastern Hillsides — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No SkyTrain, West Coast Express or SeaBus station is in Chilliwack. Route 66 Fraser Valley Express (BC Transit) runs Downtown Chilliwack to Lougheed Station (SkyTrain), the nearest rapid-transit station. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/chw_map_66.pdf
- Nearest bus exchange: Downtown Exchange, Chilliwack. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- No route in BC Transit's Chilliwack list runs into the Eastern Hillsides plan area; closest listed service: Route 71 Agassiz-Harrison (BC Transit), along Yale Road at the north foot of the hillsides, and Route 66. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
