# Barnston Island — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No TransLink bus route serving Barnston Island itself was confirmed on a TransLink timetable in this pass; there is no rapid-transit station or bus exchange on the island. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt338.pdf
- Access off the island: the Barnston Island Ferry crosses Parson's Channel between Barnston Island and Port Kells, Surrey, on demand, 5-minute crossing, capacity 5 vehicles and 50 passengers (Province of BC ferry page, search result on gov.bc.ca). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- Nearest bus exchange (off-island): Guildford Exchange, terminus of route 338, TransLink. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt338.pdf
- Route 338 East Fraser Heights / Guildford, TransLink, via 177A Street, 104 Avenue and 160 Street, on the Surrey side near the ferry landing. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt338.pdf

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Barnston Island Regional Park, Electoral Area A, listed on Metro Vancouver's regional park locations page; the park provides riverfront rest areas (Metro Vancouver study, search result on metrovancouver.org). https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park-locations
