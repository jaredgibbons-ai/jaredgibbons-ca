# Port Moody Centre — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Moody Centre Station and Inlet Centre Station are this area's SkyTrain stations; the City of Port Moody names both for the city centre. http://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/discover-port-moody/getting-around/
- TransLink's SkyTrain schedules page describes the Millennium Line as running through Port Moody and Coquitlam. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/skytrain/?page=1
- Moody Centre Station is also a West Coast Express station. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Bus 160 Port Coquitlam Station / Kootenay Loop (TransLink) runs via Inlet Centre Station and Moody Centre Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/210/tt160.pdf
- Bus 183 Moody Centre Station / Coquitlam Central Station (TransLink) runs via Ioco Rd (Inlet Centre Station), St Johns St and Williams St. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt183.pdf
- TransLink's Inlet Centre Station map places the station at 2300 Barnet Hwy, served by routes 160, 179, 183, 184 and N9. http://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/skytrain_station_maps/inlet_centre_station.pdf

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Rocky Point Park, 2800 block Murray Street, is listed by the City of Port Moody among its parks. http://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/discover-port-moody/getting-around/
- Old Orchard Park is named on the City's things-to-do page. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/discover-port-moody/things-to-do/?pcid=9fd962c3-6045-469c-a33e-a690930d453f&cid=c662f58e-6324-4b98-b566-2adbfab619c0&lang=en
- Port Moody Recreation Complex, 300 Ioco Road, has a weight room, curling rink and two ice arenas, per the City. http://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/discover-port-moody/getting-around/
- Port Moody Public Library is at the Civic Centre, 100 Newport Drive, per the City. http://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/discover-port-moody/getting-around/
