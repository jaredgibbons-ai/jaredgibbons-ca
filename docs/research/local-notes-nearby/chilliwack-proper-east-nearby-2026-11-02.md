# Chilliwack Proper East — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- There is no rapid transit in Chilliwack: no SkyTrain, West Coast Express or SeaBus station is in the city. Route 66 Fraser Valley Express (BC Transit) runs Downtown Chilliwack to Lougheed Town Centre Station, the nearest rapid-transit station. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=66
- Nearest bus exchange: Downtown Exchange, Chilliwack. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- Route 57 Broadway (BC Transit): Downtown Chilliwack to Cottonwood Mall via Yale Rd at Menzies St and Young Rd. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=57
- Route 51 Vedder (BC Transit): Downtown Chilliwack to UFV Chilliwack via Yale Rd at Menzies St and Vedder Rd. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=51
