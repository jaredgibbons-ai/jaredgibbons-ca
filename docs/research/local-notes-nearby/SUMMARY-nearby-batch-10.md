## Batch notes
- Walk engine: routing.openstreetmap.de routed-foot used throughout; every kept walk implies ~4.4–4.6 km/h (plausible). No implausible-speed walks were kept; all dropped walks were distance-cap drops and are disclosed in-file.
- Resolved — batch 9's open discrepancy: the two Langley walk figures flagged in the batch-9 summary (Timms Community Centre → Douglas Park Community school) are now settled in favour of the on-disk value. Two independent passes in this batch (city-of-langley-homes-for-sale and langley-condos-for-sale), measured fresh from the same from-point against the school's OSM facility geocode, both returned 9.3 min / 0.69 km, matching the on-disk langleycity file; the 2.6 min / 0.19 km alternate came from a different, superseded geocode of the same school.
- Geocode corrections caught in-pass: Delta Secondary address query mislanded in Tsawwassen (routes discarded, facility-name query landed in Ladner, set re-measured); Yorkson Creek Middle address query mislanded in Glen Valley (discarded, re-measured); D.W. Poppy Secondary address query mislanded on 52 Ave in Langley City (facility point in Hopington used; drive then over cap and dropped); "Langley Centre" address query mislanded on Langley Events Centre in Willoughby (corrected to the Logan Avenue exchange point — the same trap caught in batch 9); Coghlan Fundamental Elementary facility query landed ~4 km north of its address (address point used); "13428 Old Yale Road" landed on an Old Yale segment in Bridgeview (facility-name query → Holland Park); "Sunnyside Elementary, 2828 159 Street" returned only road segments (facility-name query fixed it); Surrey City Hall area and Sunnyside points verified before routing. Address queries that returned no result (several schools/facilities) were resolved with facility-name queries throughout.
- Fallback usage (all disclosed in-file): retailer/centre own-domain search results supplied addresses where store pages rendered without them — Save-On-Foods (Langley ×3 files, Ladner), Urban Fare and Choices (Yaletown), CF Pacific Centre and Central City (centre pages rendered without addresses), Pharmasave (Ladner), Buy-Low Foods and Brookswood Library (FVRL), Otter Co-op Pharmacy, Muriel Arnason Library (FVRL), Thrifty Foods (Morgan Crossing — store page errored on two opens). No banned source was used anywhere.
- 429s: none hit in the entire batch. Two ordinary 404s only (a superseded tol.ca .aspx URL and a guessed Metro Vancouver park URL), neither used.
- Research-vs-assumption outcomes: campbell-valley-acreages-farms-for-sale went FULL against the topic-slug expectation because its research anchors it on the Campbell Valley sub-area/regional-park ground; langley-farms-for-sale-acreages-agricultural-land-mls went all-omitted despite its Langley-only name because its research expressly spans five districts with no single sub-area. Otter District's research held transit UNVERIFIED — this pass confirmed only route 503 on the Fraser Highway edge and claimed nothing stronger. Campbell Valley route 566 was newly sourced from TransLink's own timetable in this pass (the research cited no route), with its seasonal/weekend limits stated in-file. Port Moody's West Coast Express was NOT stated: no operator page read in that pass supported it.
