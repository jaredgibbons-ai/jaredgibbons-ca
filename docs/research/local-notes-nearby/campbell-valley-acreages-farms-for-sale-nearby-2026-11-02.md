# Campbell Valley Acreages & Farms — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No operating SkyTrain, West Coast Express or SeaBus station in or near this area was confirmed on a TransLink page read in this pass.
- Nearest bus exchange: Langley Centre, terminus of route 566 (Bay 6). (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt566.pdf)
- Route 566 Campbell Valley / Langley Centre, TransLink: Langley Centre via 200 Street and 8 Avenue to Campbell Valley Park; weekend and holiday service, June 8 to September 6, 2026. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt566.pdf)
- Metro Vancouver's park page: a bus stop for regular service is near the park entrance at 200th Street and 20th Avenue. (https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park)

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Campbell Valley Regional Park, Metro Vancouver: trails for walkers, cyclists and horseback riders, and the Nature House. (https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park)
- Noel Booth Community Park, Township of Langley, north of the area in Brookswood-Fernridge. (https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/noel-booth-community-park.aspx)
- George Preston Recreation Centre, 20699 42 Avenue, Brookswood. (https://www.tol.ca/en/parks-recreation/george-preston-recreation-centre.aspx)
