# Campbell Valley — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest SkyTrain station: King George Station, via Langley Centre on routes 566 and 503. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf)
- Nearest bus exchange: Langley Centre, terminus of route 566 at Bay 6. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt566.pdf)
- Route 566 Campbell Valley / Langley Centre, TransLink: from Langley Centre Bay 6 via Glover Rd, 203 St, 200 St and 8 Ave to Campbell Valley Park; the timetable states weekend and holiday service runs June 8 to September 6, 2026 inclusive. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt566.pdf)
- A bus stop for regular service is near the park entrance at 200th Street and 20th Avenue, and a route 566 stop is at the South Valley entrance. (https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park)

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Campbell Valley Regional Park (Metro Vancouver): forests, fields and wetlands, a network of trails and a Nature House; Little River Loop is 2.3 km return and Shaggy Mane Trail is an 11 km loop. (https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park)
- George Preston Recreation Centre, 20699 42 Avenue, north of the area: ice arena, hall, Langley Curling Centre. (https://www.tol.ca/en/parks-recreation/george-preston-recreation-centre.aspx)
