# Chilliwack Mountain — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No SkyTrain, West Coast Express or SeaBus station is in Chilliwack. Route 66 Fraser Valley Express (BC Transit) runs Downtown Chilliwack to Lougheed Station (SkyTrain), the nearest rapid-transit station. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/chw_map_66.pdf
- Nearest bus exchange: Downtown Exchange, Chilliwack. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- No route in BC Transit's Chilliwack list runs onto the mountain's residential streets; closest listed service: Route 59 Industrial (Downtown to Cottonwood Mall via Lickman Park & Ride), below the mountain. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Hillkeep Regional Park, at the top of the mountain: about 2 km of trails and a viewing platform (Fraser Valley Regional District). https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/hillkeep.html
- Chilliwack Landing Leisure Centre, 9145 Corbould Street — nearest leisure centre, downtown off the mountain. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=37&prid=544&prshow=details
- Chilliwack Library, 45860 First Avenue. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/chilliwack-library

## 3. SCHOOLS
- No public school is on the mountain in the Ministry Chilliwack list; the nearest listed schools are on the valley floor. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/chilliwack
- La Verendrye, grades K-7, School District 33 Chilliwack, 6610 Lickman Road, below the west side; catchment not confirmed. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/chilliwack
- Bernard Elementary, grades K-5, School District 33 Chilliwack; catchment not confirmed. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/chilliwack
- Chilliwack Secondary, grades 9-12, School District 33 Chilliwack; catchment not confirmed. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/chilliwack

## 4. GROCERY AND SHOPPING ANCHORS
- London Drugs (pharmacy), Cottonwood Centre, 21-45585 Luckakuck Way. https://www.londondrugs.com/stores/cottonwood-mall/s/041
