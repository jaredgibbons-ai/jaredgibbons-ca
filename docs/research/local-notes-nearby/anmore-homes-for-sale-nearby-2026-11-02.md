# Anmore — What's Nearby: Local Notes

Village of Anmore notes, anchored on the civic ground at Anmore Community Hub and Anmore Elementary.

## 1. TRANSIT
- No rapid-transit station is in Anmore. Nearest: Moody Centre Station in Port Moody, the terminus of TransLink route 182; TransLink lists Moody Centre as a West Coast Express station. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt182.pdf)
- No bus exchange is named for Anmore: no operator page read in this pass names an exchange in the village.
- Route 182 Moody Centre Station/Belcarra, TransLink: via East Road and Sunnyside Road, with some trips continuing on Sunnyside to the turnaround at the park entrance (Anmore Grocery). (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt182.pdf)
