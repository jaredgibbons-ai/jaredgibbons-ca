# West Vancouver — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No SkyTrain station is in West Vancouver; nearest rapid transit: SeaBus at Lonsdale Quay, North Vancouver — TransLink states the SeaBus connects Downtown Vancouver to North Vancouver. https://www.translink.ca/translink/schedules-and-maps/seabus
- Nearest bus exchange/terminus: Park Royal, shown as the terminus on TransLink timetables for routes 251 and 255. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt251.pdf
- Route 251 Park Royal / Queens, TransLink: via Marine, Queens, 15th St, Mathers and 17th St to Park Royal. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt251.pdf
- Route 255 Capilano University / Dundarave, TransLink: via Marine, Lonsdale and Park Royal. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt255.pdf
- Routes 250, 253 and 254 also serve Park Royal, per the District of West Vancouver. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/ambleside-park
