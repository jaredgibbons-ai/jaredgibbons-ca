# West Chilliwack — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No SkyTrain, West Coast Express or SeaBus station is in Chilliwack. Route 66 Fraser Valley Express (BC Transit) runs Downtown Chilliwack to Lougheed Town Centre Station via Lickman Park & Ride, the nearest rapid-transit station. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=66
- Nearest bus exchange: Downtown Exchange, Chilliwack. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- Route 52 Evans (BC Transit): Cottonwood Mall to Downtown Chilliwack via Evans Rd at Yale Rd and Hodgins Ave at Ashwell Rd. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=52
- Route 59 Industrial (BC Transit): peak-period weekday trips between Downtown Chilliwack, Cottonwood Mall and Lickman Park & Ride. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=59
