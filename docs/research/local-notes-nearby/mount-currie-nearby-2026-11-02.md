# Mount Currie — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No rapid transit serves Mount Currie; none is named on a BC Transit page read in this pass. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/889/862/pem_rg_sept2020.pdf
- No bus exchange is named for Mount Currie in the BC Transit Pemberton Valley rider's guide read. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/889/862/pem_rg_sept2020.pdf
- Route 100 Pemberton Local, BC Transit: local service within Pemberton connects the communities of Xit'olacw and Mt Currie to the Village of Pemberton; operated by Pacific Western Transportation. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/889/862/pem_rg_sept2020.pdf

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Úll̓us, Mount Currie: the Líl̓wat Nation's administrative building, a block off Main Street, with a gym, banquet hall and Neighbourhood House (Nation's own site; no civic address stated there). https://lilwat.ca/our-community/about-us/
- Lil̓wat7úl Culture Centre, at Ts̓zil Learning Centre, 125 Lillooet Lake Road, Líl̓wat Nation: Nation's culture centre, open Wednesday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., April to September. https://lilwat.ca/our-community/about-us/

## 3. SCHOOLS
- Xet̓ólacw Community School, grades K4-12, Mount Currie: First Nations school, named and graded as the Líl̓wat Nation's own site lists it (the Nation's school, with an Ucwalmícwts immersion program K4 to Grade 7); catchment not confirmed. https://lilwat.ca/our-community/about-us/
- Signal Hill Elementary, grades K-7, 1410 Portage Rd, Pemberton, School District 48 (Sea to Sky); catchment not confirmed. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/048
- Pemberton Secondary, grades 8-12, Pemberton, School District 48; catchment not confirmed. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/048
