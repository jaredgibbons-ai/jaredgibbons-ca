# Kitsilano — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest rapid-transit station: Burrard Station (SkyTrain) downtown; there is no SkyTrain station in Kitsilano itself — https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/194/tt002.pdf
- Nearest named bus loop: Dunbar Loop, on Dunbar Street in Dunbar-Southlands, the west-end terminus of the 2 — https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/194/tt002.pdf
- 2 Macdonald / Burrard Station — TransLink bus from Dunbar Loop via Macdonald Street, Point Grey Road and Cornwall Avenue through Kitsilano to Burrard Station — https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/194/tt002.pdf
- 4 Powell / UBC Exchange — TransLink bus along West 4th Avenue through the middle of Kitsilano — https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/4/direction/1/schedule
- 99 B-Line — TransLink bus along Broadway at the area's south edge, between Commercial-Broadway Station and UBC Exchange — https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt099.pdf
