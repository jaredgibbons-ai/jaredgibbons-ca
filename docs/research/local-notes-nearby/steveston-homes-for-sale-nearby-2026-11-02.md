## 5. MEASURED TRAVEL TIMES
- Measured 2026-11-02 from Steveston Community Centre, Easthope Hub (4320 Moncton Street, Steveston village) using OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoding, OSRM demo for driving and routing.openstreetmap.de routed-foot for walking. OSM data date not published by the service.
- Walk to Steveston Exchange: 8.2 minutes, 0.61 km (routed-foot).
- Drive to Garry Point Park: 4.9 minutes, 1.54 km (OSRM demo).
- Drive to Robert A. McMath Secondary: 3.4 minutes, 1.14 km (OSRM demo).
- Drive to Safeway Seafair: 6.5 minutes, 3.09 km (OSRM demo).
