# Stave Falls — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest rapid-transit station: Mission City Station (West Coast Express), 33200 North Railway Ave, Mission; TransLink states weekday trains run between Mission City and downtown Vancouver in morning and evening rush hours. (https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express)
- Bus exchange: Mission City Station, the loop terminus on BC Transit Route 34. (https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=34)
- Route 34 East Side, BC Transit: Mission City Station via Heritage Park Centre to Dewdney Trunk Rd at Stave Lake St, the timing point toward this area. (https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=34)

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Rolley Lake Park (BC Parks): 23 km northwest of Mission via Dewdney Trunk Road and Bell Street; 64 campsites, swimming, fishing and canoeing on the lake. (https://bcparks.ca/rolley-lake-park/)
- Hayward Reservoir Trail: Hayward Lake Reservoir, part of BC Hydro's Stave River project, with Reservoir Trail (east side) and Railway Trail (west side) (City page, search result). (https://www.mission.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/hayward-reservoir-trail-joint-bchydro-city-mission-trail)
- Mission Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library), 33247 Second Avenue, Mission. (https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations)
