# What's Nearby — Phase 1, Batch N4 Summary

Batch N4: slugs 76–100 of the phase-1 list, in list order. Files dated 2026-11-02.
Counts = sourced-fact bullets per section (a bullet counts when it carries a source URL on an official or operator page read in this pass). "Measured times" = travel times measured in this pass, all dated 2026-11-02; the routing services do not publish an OSM data date, and each file states that.

Method notes for the whole batch:
- Driving was measured with OSRM (router.project-osrm.org). Walking was measured with the OpenStreetMap routed-foot service (routing.openstreetmap.de); kept walks imply ~4.4–5.0 km/h, inside the plausible walking range. The OSRM demo server's foot profile was not used for walking. Walks beyond the 1.5 km walk cap and drives beyond the 10 km cap were dropped and noted in the file.
- School catchments were not confirmed anywhere in this batch: no district catchment locator was run, so files state "catchment not confirmed".
- Bus frequencies are stated only where an operator page read in this pass states them (the SeaBus 15-minute daytime / 10-minute weekday-peak sailings in boulevard and british-properties); otherwise they sit under NOT CONFIRMED.
- Save-On-Foods' own store pages repeatedly rendered without a street address; those stores were left unsourced under NOT CONFIRMED rather than cited from a third party. Two anchors are sourced from an allowed site plan instead: Terra Nova Village's Save-On-Foods from the centre owner's leasing site plan (terra-nova), and Park & Tilford's address from Save-On-Foods' own release (boulevard).
- No fetch 429s were hit anywhere in this batch.
- Two walks were dropped for implausible routed-foot speed and disclosed in their files: Eagle Ridge Elementary → Scott Creek Middle (~6.6 km/h, coquitlam-east) and Forest Grove Elementary → Forest Grove Park (~6.1 km/h, forest-hills-bn); drives were substituted.
- boulevard: Phibbs Exchange was NOT used — no TransLink page read in this pass supported it as this area's anchor; Lynn Valley Centre (route 240 terminus) was used instead. The corridor is named on both the City and District sides of Highway 1, with no split point stated.
- seaisland: Section 3 omitted — no school with a Sea Island address appears on the Ministry SD38 list. south-meadows-pitt-meadows: Section 4 omitted — no in-area supermarket or centre address was readable on an allowed source.
- All 25 files are at or under 400 words including URLs (range 308–399).
