# West Newton — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest SkyTrain station: King George Station, reached from Newton Exchange on route 321 and the R1 King George Boulevard RapidBus. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt321.pdf)
- Nearest bus exchange: Newton Exchange, at the east edge of the area, served by the R1 and R6 RapidBus routes. (https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects)
- R1 King George Boulevard, TransLink: Newton Exchange to Guildford, via King George Station and Surrey Central Station. (https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects)
- R6 Scott Road, TransLink: Scott Road Station to Newton Exchange. (https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects)
- Route 321, TransLink: Surrey Central Station / Newton Exchange / White Rock via King George Boulevard. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt321.pdf)
- Route 324, TransLink: Newton Exchange to Surrey Central Station via 132 Street. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt324.pdf)
