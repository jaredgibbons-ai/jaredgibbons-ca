# Park Royal — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No SkyTrain, SeaBus or West Coast Express station in West Vancouver. TransLink's R2 Marine-Willingdon RapidBus runs from Park Royal to Metrotown Station, every 6 to 7 minutes at weekday peak, 10 to 15 minutes off-peak. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
- Nearest bus exchange BY NAME: Park Royal — TransLink's 250 timetable lists Park Royal with Bay 2 and Bay 5 stops. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/210/tt250.pdf
- Route R2 Marine-Willingdon RapidBus, TransLink: Park Royal to Metrotown Station, one-seat ride. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r2-marine-willingdon-rapidbus
- Route 250 Horseshoe Bay / Dundarave / Vancouver, TransLink: Downtown Vancouver via Marine Dr and Park Royal; some trips continue to Horseshoe Bay Ferry Terminal. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/210/tt250.pdf

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Ambleside Park, 1150 Marine Drive, West Vancouver: District gateway park, east of Park Royal; District page notes buses change at Park Royal or a 5–10 minute walk (address from westvancouver.ca search result, this pass). https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/ambleside-park
- Capilano River Regional Park, across the Capilano River: Metro Vancouver regional park; Capilano Pacific Trail and Coho Loop Trail; hatchery closed to the public from October 1, 2026. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/capilano-river-regional-park
