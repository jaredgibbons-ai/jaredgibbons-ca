# Sea Island — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest rapid station: Templeton Station (Canada Line), on Sea Island north of Grant McConachie Way near Templeton Road, per TransLink's Buzzer blog. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2009/05/sneak-peek-of-templeton-station-on-the-canada-line-thu-may-28-2pm-7pm/
- Nearest bus exchange: Bridgeport Station, the terminus of route 412, which serves the island's south side and Burkeville. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt412.pdf
- Route 412 Sea Island South / Bridgeport Station, via Russ Baker Way, Inglis Drive and Miller Road, TransLink. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt412.pdf

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Sea Island Community Centre, 7140 Miller Road, in Burkeville: City of Richmond facility, per the City's facility address list. https://www.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Adult_and_Tot_Drop-In_Schedule_Summer_202679101.pdf
- McDonald Beach Park, 3500 McDonald Road, on the island's north bank: City of Richmond park. https://richmond.ca/culture/eventplanningresources/parks-outdoor/picnicsites.htm?PageMode=HTML
- Iona Beach Regional Park, 900 Ferguson Road: Metro Vancouver regional park at the island's north-west point. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/iona-beach-regional-park
