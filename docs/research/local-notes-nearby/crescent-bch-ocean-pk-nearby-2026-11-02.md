## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Crescent Beach, South Surrey: swimming, beach volleyball, beachfront pathway. (https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/find-park?community%5B0%5D=121&page=1)
- Blackie Spit Park, South Surrey: picnic areas, nature trails, two fully fenced dog off-leash spaces. (https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/find-park?community%5B0%5D=121&page=1)
- Crescent Park, South Surrey: 52-hectare park, forest trails, ponds, picnic shelters, tennis and pickleball courts, playground. (https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/crescent-park)
- Kwomais Point Park, 1367 128 Street, Surrey: ocean lookout over Semiahmoo Bay. (https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/kwomais-point-park)
- Surrey Libraries Ocean Park Branch, 12854 17 Avenue, Surrey. (https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/staying-safe-winter)
