# Quay — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Rapid-transit stations: New Westminster Station and Columbia Station, both at the top of the waterfront; New Westminster Station is served by the Expo and Millennium Lines — https://anvilcentre.com/
- Bus exchange: the bays at New Westminster Station, the start point of the 102 and 103 — https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt103.pdf
- 103 Quayside / New Westminster Station — TransLink bus looping the Quayside Drive waterfront out to Renaissance Square — https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt103.pdf
- 102 Victoria Hill — TransLink bus; most trips continue on from the 103 — https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt102.pdf

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Westminster Pier Park and the Waterfront Esplanade are named on the City of New Westminster's own community parks list — http://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/community-parks
- Anvil Centre, 777 Columbia Street — https://anvilcentre.com/
- New Westminster Public Library, Main Branch, 716 6th Avenue, Uptown — https://www.nwpl.ca/about-us/hours-and-locations/
