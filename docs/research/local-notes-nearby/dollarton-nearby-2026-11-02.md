# Dollarton — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No SkyTrain, West Coast Express or SeaBus station is in Dollarton, in the District of North Vancouver; the area's bus anchor is Phibbs Exchange, named as the terminus in TransLink's North Vancouver timetable. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2026-schedules/september/nvanp155.pdf
- Route 212 Phibbs Exchange/Deep Cove, TransLink, runs along Dollarton Highway, per that timetable. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2026-schedules/september/nvanp155.pdf
- Route 211 Phibbs Exchange/Burrard Station, TransLink, runs from Deep Cove via Dollarton, Mt Seymour Parkway and Phibbs Exchange to Burrard Station, per its timetable. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt211.pdf

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Cates Park/Whey-ah-Wichen, 4141 Dollarton Hwy: seaside park with 6 km of waterfront trails, playgrounds, beaches, tennis courts and a year-round boat launch, per North Vancouver Recreation & Culture. https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/community-recreation-centres/cates-whey-ah-wichen-picnic-shelter
- Cates Park walking trails are also listed in the NVRC walking guide. https://www.nvrc.ca/programs-memberships/program-directory/walk-hike-run/walking-guide/cates-park
