# Central Abbotsford — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No SkyTrain, West Coast Express or SeaBus station is in Abbotsford; TransLink lists West Coast Express stations from Mission City to Waterfront only. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Nearest rapid-transit station: Mission City Station (West Coast Express), in Mission. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Nearest bus exchange: Bourquin Exchange, shown as a timing point on the BC Transit route 1 map. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2025/12/cfv_map_1.pdf
