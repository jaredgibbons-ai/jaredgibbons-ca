- Route 609 South Delta Exchange / Ladner Exchange, TransLink: via 56 Street, 12 Avenue, 52 Street and Canoe Pass Way through Tsawwassen First Nation; TransLink states all-day 30-minute service, seven days a week, from January 5, 2026. https://www.translink.ca/news/2025/december/translink%20boosts%20winter%20bus%20service%20on%2037%20metro%20vancouver%20routes
