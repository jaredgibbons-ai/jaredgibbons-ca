# Lake City Industrial — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Lake City Way Station (Millennium Line) is in this Burnaby industrial district; TransLink's route 134 timetable names it as a terminus. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt134.pdf
- Route 134 Lake City Station / Brentwood Station, TransLink, runs via Enterprise St and Lake City Way through the district, per its timetable. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt134.pdf
- Route 134 schedule page: https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/134/direction/0/schedule

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Burnaby Lake Regional Park, 4519 Piper Ave, borders the district to the west, per Metro Vancouver. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/burnaby-lake-regional-park
