# Moody Park — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest rapid-transit station: New Westminster Station (SkyTrain), downtown; there is no SkyTrain station in Moody Park itself — https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt105.pdf
- Bus exchange: the bays at New Westminster Station, where the Uptown routes begin — https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt106.pdf
- 105 Uptown / New Westminster Station — TransLink bus linking Uptown, including 8th Street and 6th Avenue stops, with New Westminster Station — https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt105.pdf
- 106 Edmonds Station — TransLink bus from New Westminster Station via Carnarvon Street and 6th Street, at the area's west edge — https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt106.pdf
