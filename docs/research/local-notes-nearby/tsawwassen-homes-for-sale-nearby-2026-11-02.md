# Tsawwassen — What's Nearby: Local Notes

Community-wide notes for Tsawwassen in the City of Delta, anchored on the 56 Street town centre.

## 1. TRANSIT
- No rapid-transit station is in Delta. Nearest: Bridgeport Station (Canada Line), terminus of TransLink routes 601 and 602. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf)
- Nearest bus exchange: South Delta Exchange, on 56 St, served by routes 601, 602 and 609. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf)
- Route 601 Bridgeport Station/South Delta, TransLink: via South Delta Exchange and Ladner Exchange to Bridgeport Station. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf)
- Route 602 Bridgeport Station/Tsawwassen Heights, TransLink: via 56 St (South Delta Exchange) and 12 Ave to Bridgeport Station. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf)
- Route 609 South Delta Exchange/Ladner Exchange, TransLink. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt609.pdf)
