# Capitol Hill — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No SkyTrain station is in the area; the R5 RapidBus on Hastings Street connects to Burrard Station, with stops at Hythe and Holdom below the hill. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/ttR5.pdf
- Nearest bus loop: Kootenay Loop, an R5 stop and the route 130 / 160 terminus on Hastings Street. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt130.pdf
- Route R5 Hastings Street, SFU Exchange to Burrard Station, TransLink. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/ttR5.pdf
- Route 130 Metrotown Station, via Hastings and Willingdon, TransLink. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt130.pdf
- Route 160 Kootenay Loop, via Hastings at Kensington, TransLink. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/210/tt160.pdf
