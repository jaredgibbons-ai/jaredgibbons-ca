# Bayridge — What's Nearby: Local Notes

"Bayridge" is a page name for District of West Vancouver ground on Marine Drive west of 31st Street, not a confirmed official neighbourhood name.

## 1. TRANSIT
- No rapid-transit station serves this area; buses are West Vancouver Transit (Blue Bus) for TransLink.
- Bus exchange on the route timetable: Park Royal (timing point, Bay 2, route 250). https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
- Route 250 Dundarave / Horseshoe Bay, via Lions Gate Bridge and Marine Drive to 25th Street, some trips to Horseshoe Bay Ferry Terminal; West Vancouver Transit (Blue Bus). https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
