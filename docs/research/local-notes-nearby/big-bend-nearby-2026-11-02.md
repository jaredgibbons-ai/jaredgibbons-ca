# Big Bend — What's Nearby: Local Notes

Big Bend is the southernmost part of Burnaby, along the Fraser River south of Marine Drive; mostly farmland and industrial land, with a small rural-residential pocket.

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest rapid stations: Edmonds Station and Metrotown Station (Expo Line), the two termini of route 116, which runs along Marine Way through Big Bend. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt116.pdf
- No bus exchange named for this area on any TransLink page read.
- Route 116 Edmonds Station / Metrotown Station, via Marine Way, North Fraser Way and Byrne Road, TransLink. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt116.pdf

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Burnaby Fraser Foreshore Park, 7751 Fraser Park Drive, Burnaby — riverfront park with trails, picnic area, playground and an outdoor fitness circuit. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/outdoor-fitness-circuits
- Burnaby Public Library, Tommy Douglas Branch, 7311 Kingsway, Burnaby (address from the library's own community-room form). https://bpl.bc.ca/images/documents/BPL-Community-Room-Application-form-all-branches-2025-01-Code-of-Conduct-specified.pdf
