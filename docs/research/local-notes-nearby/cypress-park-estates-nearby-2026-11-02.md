# Cypress Park Estates — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No rapid-transit station is in West Vancouver; the nearest bus exchange is Park Royal, named as the terminus in the route 256 timetable. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt256.pdf
- Route 256 Whitby Estates / Park Royal, TransLink, runs via Chippendale Rd to Westhill, then via 21st St, Gordon Ave, 22nd St and Marine Dr to Park Royal South; timetable shows hourly departures. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt256.pdf
