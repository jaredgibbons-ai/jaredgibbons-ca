# Upper Lonsdale — What's Nearby: Local Notes

Upper Lonsdale runs along Lonsdale Ave from about 25th St to Rockland Ave: south of 29th St is the City of North Vancouver; north of 29th St is the District of North Vancouver.

## 1. TRANSIT
- No rapid-transit station is in Upper Lonsdale. Nearest: the SeaBus at Lonsdale Quay Station, downhill in Lower Lonsdale, with departures to Waterfront (TransLink). https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/station/lonsdale-quay/schedule
- 230 Upper Lonsdale / Lonsdale Quay (TransLink): Lonsdale Quay up Lonsdale Ave to Osborne, then via Princess, Braemar, Lonsdale and Rockland to Prospect. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt230.pdf
- 229 Lonsdale Quay / Lynn Valley (TransLink): via Lonsdale Ave, 29 St and Lynn Valley Rd (search result, this pass). https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt229.pdf

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Delbrook Community Recreation Centre, 851 West Queens Road, on the District side west of the area (operator's accessibility page, search result, this pass). http://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/accessibility
- Mahon Park, on Jones Ave from 15th to 21st, south of the area beside Carson Graham Secondary (City of North Vancouver parks list). https://www.cnv.org/parks-recreation-and-culture/parks-and-greenways/parks-in-the-city

## 3. SCHOOLS
- Larson Elementary, K–7; Braemar Elementary, K–7; Boundary Elementary, K–7 — School District 44 (North Vancouver); catchment not confirmed for each. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044
- Carson Graham Secondary, 8–12; Holy Trinity School, K–7, independent — School District 44 (North Vancouver); catchment not confirmed for each. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044

## 4. GROCERY AND SHOPPING ANCHORS
