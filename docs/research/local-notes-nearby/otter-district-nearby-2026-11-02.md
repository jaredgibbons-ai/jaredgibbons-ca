# Otter District — What's Nearby: Local Notes

- Nearest SkyTrain station: King George Station, on route 503, TransLink, which runs via Fraser Highway and King George Boulevard (King George Station) to Surrey Central Station. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf)
- Nearest bus exchange: Langley Centre, a stop on route 503. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf)
- Route 503, TransLink: Surrey Central Station – Fraser Highway Express, from 272 Street at Fraser Highway via 264 Street, Fraser Highway and Langley Centre, along the district's north edge. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf)
