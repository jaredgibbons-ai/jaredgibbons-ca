# Port Kells — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No SkyTrain or West Coast Express station is located in Port Kells; the area's bus service is TransLink route 388. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt388.pdf
- Bus route 388 88 Avenue (TransLink) runs between Carvolth Exchange and 22nd Street Station via 88 Ave and 168 St, per TransLink's timetable. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt388.pdf
- Carvolth Exchange is the western terminus of the 388, per TransLink's timetable. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt388.pdf
