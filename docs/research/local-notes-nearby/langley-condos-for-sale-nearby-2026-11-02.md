# Langley Condos — What's Nearby: Local Notes

Anchor: Langley City centre — Timms Community Centre, 20399 Douglas Crescent. (https://www.langleycity.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/timms-community-centre)

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest operating SkyTrain station: Surrey Central Station, reached from Langley Centre on TransLink routes 320 and 502. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt320.pdf)
- Nearest bus exchange: Langley Centre; timetables show routes starting "From Langley Centre." (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt320.pdf)
- Route 320, TransLink: Langley Centre to Surrey Central Station via Fleetwood. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt320.pdf)
- Route 502, TransLink: Langley Centre to Surrey Central Station via Fraser Highway and King George Station. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt502.pdf)
- Route 531, TransLink: Willowbrook to White Rock Centre via Langley Centre, 200 Street and 24 Avenue. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt531.pdf)
