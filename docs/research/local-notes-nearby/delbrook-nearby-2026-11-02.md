# Delbrook — What's Nearby: Local Notes

Delbrook is in the District of North Vancouver.

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest rapid transit: SeaBus at Lonsdale Quay — TransLink states the SeaBus connects Downtown Vancouver to North Vancouver, takes 12 minutes, and departs every 15 minutes during the day. https://www.translink.ca/translink/schedules-and-maps/seabus
- Nearest bus exchange: Lonsdale Quay, terminus of route 249. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt249.pdf
- Route 249 Delbrook / Lonsdale Quay, via Delbrook Avenue, Westview Drive and Larson Road, TransLink. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt249.pdf
