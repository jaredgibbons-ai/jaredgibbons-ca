# Boulevard — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest rapid transit: Lonsdale Quay SeaBus terminal, City of North Vancouver — TransLink lists sailings from Lonsdale Quay every 15 minutes during the day, 10 minutes in weekday peaks. https://www.translink.ca/translink/schedules-and-maps/seabus
- Nearest bus exchange: Lynn Valley Centre, terminus of route 240, per TransLink's timetable. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt240.pdf
- Route 240 Lynn Valley / Downtown, via Boulevard Crescent, Grand Boulevard East, 15th Street and Keith Road, TransLink; it runs the corridor on both the City and District sides of Highway 1. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt240.pdf

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Grand Boulevard Park, the median park along Grand Boulevard, City of North Vancouver, per the City. https://www.cnv.org/City-Hall/News-Room/Whats-New/2024/7/3/Pollinator-Meadows-Creating-a-Beautiful-Buzz
- Harry Jerome Community Recreation Centre, 130 East 23rd Street, City of North Vancouver: North Vancouver Recreation & Culture centre. https://www.nvrc.ca/new-harry-jerome
