# Campbell Valley — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest SkyTrain station: King George Station, via Langley Centre on route 503, TransLink. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf)
- Nearest bus exchange: Langley Centre, the terminus of route 566. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt566.pdf)
- Route 566, TransLink: Campbell Valley / Langley Centre, from Langley Centre Bay 6 via 200 Street and 8 Avenue to Campbell Valley Park; the timetable states weekend and holiday service runs June 8 to September 6, 2026. (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt566.pdf)
- Metro Vancouver states a bus stop for regular service is near the park entrance at 200th Street and 20th Avenue, and a route 566 stop is at the South Valley entrance. (https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park)

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Campbell Valley Regional Park, Metro Vancouver: forests, fields and wetlands, a network of trails, and a Nature House. (https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park)
- George Preston Recreation Centre, 20699 42 Avenue, north of the area in Brookswood. (https://www.tol.ca/en/parks-recreation/george-preston-recreation-centre.aspx)
