# Whitby Estates — What's Nearby: Local Notes

Whitby Estates is a small subdivision pocket (Whitby Rd, Folkestone Way, Kadlec Court, Garden Court, Highgrove Mews) above Taylor Way, inside Chartwell / British Properties, West Vancouver. No school, grocery store or rapid-transit station is in the pocket; the anchors below are the nearest supported, each outside the pocket.
