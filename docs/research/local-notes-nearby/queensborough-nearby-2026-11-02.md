# Queensborough — What's Nearby: Local Notes

Queensborough is New Westminster's island part; its SkyTrain station and bus exchange sit on the mainland side of the Queensborough Bridge.

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest rapid station: 22nd Street Station (Expo Line), on the mainland side; routes 104 and 410 run from the island to the station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt104.pdf
- Nearest bus exchange: 22nd Street Station bus exchange, an eight-bay loop below the station, mainland side; refresh completed in 2019. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2019/07/refresh-of-22nd-street-station-bus-exchange-now-complete/
- Route 104 22nd Street Station / Annacis Island, via the Queensborough Bridge, Ewen Avenue and Derwent Way, TransLink. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt104.pdf
- Route 410 Richmond-Brighouse Station / 22nd Street Station, via Ewen Avenue, Howes Street and the Queensborough Bridge, TransLink. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt410.pdf

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Queensborough Community Centre, 920 Ewen Avenue, New Westminster. https://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/recreation/fitness
- New Westminster Public Library, Queensborough Branch, 920 Ewen Avenue, inside the community centre. https://www.nwpl.ca/about-us/hours-and-locations/
- Ryall Park, beside the community centre; City-listed among its spray parks. https://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/amenities/sb_expander_articles/2534.php
- Port Royal Park and Riverfront Walk, access near Ewen Avenue and Salter Street. https://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/neighbourhood-parks/articles/5408.php
