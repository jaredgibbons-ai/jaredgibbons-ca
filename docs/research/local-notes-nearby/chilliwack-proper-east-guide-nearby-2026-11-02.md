# Chilliwack Proper East — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No rapid transit in Chilliwack: no rapid-transit station is in the city. Nearest by bus: Lougheed Town Centre Stn, Route 66 terminus. (https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=66)
- Bus exchange, as BC Transit names it: Downtown Chilliwack, terminus/timing point on routes 52, 53, 66, 71. (https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=52)
- Route 71 Agassiz-Harrison, BC Transit: Downtown Chilliwack east on Yale Road to Rosedale, Agassiz, Harrison Hot Springs. (https://www.bctransit.com/agassiz-harrison/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=71)
- Route 53 Fairfield, BC Transit: Downtown Chilliwack (Yale Rd) loop via Menzies St at Riverside Dr, Young Rd at Clare Ave. (https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=53)
- Route 57 Broadway, BC Transit: shifts from Carleton Street to McNaught Road January 4, 2026; new stops on McNaught and Acorn. (https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/)
- Route 66 Fraser Valley Express, BC Transit: Chilliwack, Abbotsford, Langley, Burnaby; daily. (https://www.bctransit.com/plan-a-trip/interregional-routes/)
