# Chilliwack Proper East — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No rapid-transit station in Chilliwack. Nearest by bus: Lougheed Town Centre Stn, Route 66 terminus. (https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=66)
- Bus exchange (as BC Transit names it): Downtown Chilliwack, terminus on routes 52, 53, 66, 71. (https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=52)
- Route 71 Agassiz-Harrison, BC Transit: Downtown Chilliwack to Harrison Hot Springs via Yale Road, Rosedale, Agassiz. (https://www.bctransit.com/agassiz-harrison/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=71)
- Route 53 Fairfield, BC Transit: Downtown Chilliwack loop via Menzies St, Young Rd. (https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=53)
- Route 57 Broadway, BC Transit: moves to McNaught Road January 4, 2026; new stops on McNaught, Acorn. (https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/)
- Route 66 Fraser Valley Express, BC Transit: Chilliwack, Abbotsford, Langley, Burnaby; daily. (https://www.bctransit.com/plan-a-trip/interregional-routes/)
