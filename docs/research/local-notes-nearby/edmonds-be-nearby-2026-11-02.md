# Edmonds — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest rapid station: Edmonds Station (Expo Line); routes 106 and 112 both terminate at Edmonds Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt106.pdf
- Nearest bus exchange: the Edmonds Station bus loop, terminus of routes 106 and 112. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt112.pdf
- Route 106 Edmonds Station, from New Westminster Station via Edmonds Street and Griffiths Drive, TransLink. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt106.pdf
- Route 112 Edmonds Station, from New Westminster Station via Kingsway, 14th Avenue and Griffiths Drive, TransLink. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt112.pdf
