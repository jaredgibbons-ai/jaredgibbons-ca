# Bridal Falls — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No rapid-transit station serves Bridal Falls; the area is in FVRD Electoral Area D, east of Chilliwack. (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html)
- Nearest bus anchor named by the operator: Downtown Chilliwack, the terminus timing point on Route 71. (https://www.bctransit.com/agassiz-harrison/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=71)
- Route 71 Agassiz-Harrison, BC Transit: Downtown Chilliwack to Harrison Hot Springs via Yale Road and Highway 9; Trip Note B states on-request trips can route to Bridal Falls. (https://www.bctransit.com/agassiz-harrison/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=71)
- The FVRD states it administers Route 71, connecting Agassiz, Harrison Hot Springs and Chilliwack, delivered by BC Transit. (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html)

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Bridal Veil Falls Park (BC Parks): day-use park 16 km east of Chilliwack via Highway 1 Exit 135; the falls tumble 60 metres; a loop trail reaches the viewing platform. (https://bcparks.ca/bridal-veil-falls-park/)
- Cheam Lake Wetlands Regional Park (FVRD), 53480 Elgey Road, Rosedale: 107-hectare park in Electoral Area D, about 2 km of walking trails; pets not permitted. (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/cheam-lake-wetlands.html?media=standard)
