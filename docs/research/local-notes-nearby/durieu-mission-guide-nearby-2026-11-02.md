# Durieu — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest rapid-transit station: Mission City Station (West Coast Express), 33200 North Railway Ave, Mission, southwest of this area. (https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express)
- No FVRD-administered BC Transit route is routed to Durieu: the FVRD administers Route 71 Agassiz-Harrison, Route 72 Hope and Route 66 Fraser Valley Express only. (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html)

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Cascade Falls Regional Park, 36421 Ridgeview Road, Deroche: 22-hectare FVRD park in Electoral Area F northeast of Mission, 30-metre waterfall, 1 km trail. (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/cascade-falls.html)
