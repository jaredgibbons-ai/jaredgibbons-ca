## 5. MEASURED TRAVEL TIMES
- Measured 2026-11-02 from Vancouver City Hall (453 West 12th Avenue) using OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoding, OSRM demo for driving and routing.openstreetmap.de routed-foot for walking. OSM data date not published by the service.
- Walk to Broadway–City Hall Station: 4.3 minutes, 0.32 km (routed-foot).
- Walk to Whole Foods Market Cambie: 6.6 minutes, 0.50 km (routed-foot).
- Drive to Hillcrest Centre: 5.3 minutes, 2.58 km (OSRM demo).
- Drive to Central Library: 4.6 minutes, 2.46 km (OSRM demo).
