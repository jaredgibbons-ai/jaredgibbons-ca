# Sumas Prairie — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No rapid-transit station is in Abbotsford; the FVRD states Route 66 connects Chilliwack and Abbotsford with Langley and Burnaby via Highway 1. (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html)
- Nearest bus exchange: Bourquin Exchange, Abbotsford, named on BC Transit's route 3 map. (https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/cfv_map_3.pdf)
- Route 3 Clearbrook/Huntingdon, BC Transit: runs on Sumas Way, at the west edge of the prairie, to Huntingdon at 2nd and Sumas, per its BC Transit map. (https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/cfv_map_3.pdf)
- Route 66 Fraser Valley Express, BC Transit: Highway 1 service connecting Abbotsford with Langley and Burnaby. (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html)
