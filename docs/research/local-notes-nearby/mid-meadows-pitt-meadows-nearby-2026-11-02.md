# Mid Meadows — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest rapid-transit station: Maple Meadows Station (West Coast Express), just east of the city line in Maple Ridge — https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Pitt Meadows Station, 12280 Harris Road, is the in-city West Coast Express station — https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Bus terminus: Meadowtown Centre, the end point of the 719 and 722 — https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt719.pdf
- 719 Fraser Way / Meadowtown — TransLink bus via Maple Meadows Station, Hammond, Wildwood, Bonson Road and Fraser Way — https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt719.pdf
- 722 Bonson / Meadowtown — TransLink bus via Pitt Meadows Station, Harris, Hammond, Blakely, 116B Ave, Bonson and Fraser Way — https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt722.pdf
