# Burke Mountain — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- Coquitlam Central Station is the bus-exchange and station anchor in TransLink's route 191 timetable; the route also passes Lafarge Lake–Douglas Station (Millennium Line) on Pinetree Way. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt191.pdf
- Route 191 Princeton / Coquitlam Central Station, TransLink: a loop from Coquitlam Central Station via Johnson, Glen, Pinetree and David Ave to Princeton, returning via Princeton, Coast Meridian and David, per its timetable. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt191.pdf

## 2. PARKS AND RECREATION
- Smiling Creek Park: a 3.43-acre park forming part of the Smiling Creek Elementary joint-use site, with sports field, playground and trails, per the City of Coquitlam facility page. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Smiling-Creek-Park-81
- Burke Mountain Pioneer Park: a 4.4-acre community park, per the City of Coquitlam facility page (search result on the City's domain). https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Burke-Mountain-Pioneer-Park-21
