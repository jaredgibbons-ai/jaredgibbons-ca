# Chilliwack Proper South — What's Nearby: Local Notes

## 1. TRANSIT
- No rapid transit in Chilliwack: no rapid-transit station is in the city. Nearest by bus: Lougheed Town Centre Stn, Route 66 terminus. (https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=66)
- Bus exchange, as BC Transit names it: Downtown Chilliwack, terminus/timing point on routes 52 and 66. (https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=52)
- Route 52 Evans, BC Transit: Cottonwood Mall to Downtown Chilliwack, timing point at Evans Rd at Yale Rd. (https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=52)
- Route 51 UFV, BC Transit: January 4, 2026 notice adds later, more frequent Saturday-evening service. (https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/)
- Route 57 Broadway, BC Transit: same notice adds Sunday-evening service. (https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/)
- Route 66 Fraser Valley Express, BC Transit: Chilliwack, Abbotsford, Langley, Burnaby; daily. (https://www.bctransit.com/plan-a-trip/interregional-routes/)
