# Cedardale — What's Nearby: Local Notes

Cedardale is in the District of West Vancouver.

## 1. TRANSIT
- Nearest rapid transit: SeaBus at Lonsdale Quay — TransLink states it connects Downtown Vancouver to North Vancouver, takes 12 minutes, and departs every 15 minutes during the day. https://www.translink.ca/translink/schedules-and-maps/seabus
- Nearest bus exchange: Park Royal, a bay stop on route 250. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
- Route 250 Dundarave / Horseshoe Bay, via Marine Drive and Park Royal, TransLink (West Vancouver Blue Bus). https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
