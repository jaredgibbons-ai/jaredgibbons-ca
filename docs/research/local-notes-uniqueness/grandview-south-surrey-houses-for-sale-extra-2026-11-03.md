## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves a Grandview house? **A:** Usually Grandview Heights Secondary at 16987 25 Avenue, but not for every address in the plan area. The district's December 4, 2020 letter lists six feeder elementary schools, and a district handout notes Edgewood is split by address with Earl Marriott Secondary. Check the Locator. (check current — 2020) https://media.surreyschools.ca/media/Default/medialib/ghss-parent-letter-dec-4-docx12.4f356085095.pdf
- **Q:** Which elementary schools are inside Grandview Heights? **A:** Edgewood and Sunnyside sit inside the plan area, at 16666 23 Avenue and 2828 159 Street, with the secondary school at 16987 25 Avenue. Location is not catchment: the district sets catchment by home address through its School Locator tool, so confirm the exact address before buying. https://www.surreyschools.ca/grandviewheights
- **Q:** Is Grandview Heights finished building? **A:** No — the last plan area only started planning in 2026. Council authorized staff on July 13, 2026 to initiate the Area 5 Neighbourhood Concept Plan, with a Stage 1 draft land use plan expected in early 2027 and Stage 2 servicing targeted for spring 2028. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r144-grandview-heights-area-5-neighbourhood-concept-plan-initiation
- **Q:** When did the local schools open? **A:** Both are new buildings from the growth period: Edgewood Elementary opened in January 2021 and Grandview Heights Secondary in September 2021, per City planning reports, and the district growth plan names a future Redwood Heights Elementary as the east side builds out. https://media.surreyschools.ca/media/Default/medialib/planning-for-growth-final.16b71911523.pdf
Read: 2026-11-03
