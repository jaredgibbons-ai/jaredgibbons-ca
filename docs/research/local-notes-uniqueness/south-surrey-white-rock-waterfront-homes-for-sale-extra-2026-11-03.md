## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Two zoning systems apply: White Rock Zoning Bylaw, 2024, No. 2506 (adopted June 27, 2024), and Surrey's rules at Crescent Beach — confirm the city first. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents
- White Rock's Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw, 2017, No. 2220 includes a Waterfront Development Permit Area. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents
- At Crescent Beach, a geotechnical engineer is required for all floodplain construction, and building below the FCL needs a Development Variance Permit (DVP) plus a title covenant. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/crescent-beach-floodplain-residential-building-permit

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a Crescent Beach house in a floodplain? **A:** Yes — check the lot against the Crescent Beach Floodplain map before offering. Surrey requires the 3.30 m G.S.C. Flood Construction Level, a geotechnical engineer for all floodplain construction, and Council approval plus a title covenant for any build below that level. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/crescent-beach-floodplain-residential-building-permit
- **Q:** Will trains whistle outside a Marine Drive home? **A:** Not routinely at the promenade crossings. Since January 29, 2020, BNSF crews have been under a general order not to sound horns at White Rock's eight promenade crossings, after 16 safety gates were installed; engineers still sound the horn for people or animals on the tracks. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/408/Rail-in-White-Rock
- **Q:** Can a Crescent Beach lot drain into the City storm sewer? **A:** Usually not. Surrey states that for most Crescent Beach properties, connection to the City storm sewer is not feasible, because most streets have no storm sewer mains and existing mains are shallow, so drainage must be settled at the building permit stage. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/crescent-beach-floodplain-residential-building-permit
- **Q:** Which hospital serves a waterfront home here? **A:** Peace Arch Hospital in White Rock. Fraser Health lists it at 15521 Russell Avenue, White Rock, providing emergency, surgery and maternity care for White Rock and South Surrey residents, and states it is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/white-rock/peace-arch-hospital

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The railway runs between Marine Drive homes and the beach: a single track, operating 24 hours a day, used by BNSF freight and Amtrak passenger trains. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/408/Rail-in-White-Rock
- Beach access crosses that railway at eight gated pedestrian crossings only: Bay, Anderson, Oxford and the Pier on West Beach; Cypress, Balsam, Ash and Finlay on East Beach. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/408/Rail-in-White-Rock
- Dogs are not allowed on the White Rock Pier at any time, per the City's Pier and Promenade page. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/401/Pier-Promenade

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check White Rock's OCP floodplain mapping: a City report describes an Ocean Floodplain level of 3.9 m geodetic and a Campbell River Floodplain level of 4.6 m, overlapping in the southeast corner — check current. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1071/OCP-Report---Draft---June-12-2017-PDF
- Check the Crescent Beach Floodplain map for the exact lot: the map, not the street, decides whether the 3.30 m FCL applies. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/crescent-beach-floodplain-residential-building-permit
- Surrey's DMAF reporting treats Mud Bay, Crescent Beach and Semiahmoo Bay as its coastal flood communities, so dike work is an ongoing program, not a finished fix. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R237.pdf
