# Abbotsford East Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: abbotsford-east (Abbotsford East, Abbotsford; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Abbotsford East is the city's east side, running from the McMillan area to the Sumas Mountain slopes. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- It is not one City neighbourhood name; the City plans it through McMillan, Old Clayburn, East Townline, Whatcom and McKee. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- Highway 1 runs along the south side, Old Yale Road and Old Clayburn Road run through the middle, and McKee Road serves the northeast slopes. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie
- People also name its pockets directly: McKee, Auguston, Sandy Hill, Clayburn, Eagle Mountain and McMillan. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- Central Abbotsford and Abbotsford West lie to the west, Sumas Mountain to the east, Sumas Prairie to the southeast and Mission across the Fraser River to the north. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached homes are the main form, from established Old Yale Road streets to newer subdivisions on the McKee and Eagle Mountain slopes. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/
- Zones include RS3 (540 m² minimum interior lot), RS5 (420 m²) and RS6, the compact-lot zone used in newer subdivisions. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/
- Ground-oriented homes are enabled in the McKee plan area under the Urban 2 – Ground Oriented designation. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- The detached stock is freehold and attached product is strata under the Strata Property Act. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/termination
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The east-side angle on the shared Zoning Bylaw facts is slope and heritage; see the central-abbotsford-guide note for the full bylaw, suite and tax facts. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- The McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023), Schedule E of the Official Community Plan, governs the mountainside part. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- The Official Community Plan applies a Steep Slope Development Permit Area to slope land. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- Clayburn Village, at the west foot of Sumas Mountain, is protected through the Clayburn Village Heritage Conservation Area in the Official Community Plan. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- Suite criteria stated in City reports include a lot of at least 400 m², at least 12.0 m frontage, a Collector or Local road and not on a cul-de-sac bulb. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools are physically in Abbotsford East? **A:** Eight named public schools sit inside the area. They include Sandy Hill Elementary at 3836 Old Clayburn Road, Auguston Traditional Elementary at 36367 Stephen Leacock Drive, Clayburn Middle at 35139 Laburnum Avenue, Robert Bateman Secondary at 35045 Exbury Avenue and Yale Secondary at 34620 Old Yale Road. https://sandyhill.abbyschools.ca/
- **Q:** What catchment chain was confirmed here? **A:** Two locator runs return the same middle and secondary schools. Both 36367 Stephen Leacock Drive and 3836 Old Clayburn Road return Clayburn Middle and Robert Bateman Secondary, with Auguston Traditional and Sandy Hill respectively as the elementary schools. https://clayburn.abbyschools.ca/parentsandstudents/registration/clayburn
- **Q:** Does a slope lot need extra reports? **A:** Yes, slope lots can trigger geotechnical review. The McKee plan's open space network includes geotechnical areas and slopes over 40 percent, and the Official Community Plan applies a Steep Slope Development Permit Area, so a buyer should budget for that check. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **Q:** Can exterior work be done freely in Clayburn Village? **A:** No, the village has heritage protection. Clayburn Village is protected through a Heritage Conservation Area in the Official Community Plan, so exterior changes need the heritage rules checked before work is planned or priced. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **Q:** How is Highway 1 reached? **A:** Highway 1 access is at Whatcom Road on the south side. The 66 Fraser Valley Express also uses Highway 1 between Abbotsford, Langley and Chilliwack, giving the south edge a regional bus option. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie
Read: 2026-11-03

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check low lots near the Sumas River and Whatcom Road against City flood notices and the WebMap. City notices name properties south of the Sumas River and along Whatcom Road in evacuation order and alert areas. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie
- Check the Official Community Plan Drainage and Flood Control map (Map 12) and Agricultural Land Reserve map (Map 16) for the specific lot. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- On McKee slope lots, check the Steep Slope Development Permit Area and expect geotechnical conditions where slopes exceed 40 percent. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
