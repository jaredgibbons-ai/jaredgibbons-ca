# Bear Creek / Green Timbers Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: bear-creek-green-timbers-surrey-houses-for-sale (Bear Creek / Green Timbers; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Most of this board area is parkland, not houses: the City tags both Bear Creek Park and Green Timbers Urban Forest Park as Whalley and City Centre on their park pages, while the eastern side falls in the City's Fleetwood plan area, which the City states stretches from Green Timbers Urban Forest in the west towards the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) in the east. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/fleetwood-land-use-plans/fleetwood-town-centre-plan
- The fixed anchors are Green Timbers Urban Forest Park at the 14600 block of 100 Avenue on the north side, Bear Creek Park at 13750 88 Avenue on the southwest side, the future Green Timbers SkyTrain Station at 140 Street and Fraser Highway on the south side, and Enver Creek Secondary at 14505 84 Avenue on the southeast side. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/green-timbers-urban-forest-park
- Surrey-wide community boundaries are in the city note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The detached houses here are the streets left between two large parks, and the Fraser Highway edge is already rezoning to apartments. At 9137, 9129 and 9121 148 Street a City report records three lots, each with a single-family dwelling, zoned Urban Residential Zone (R3), proposed for a six-storey, 78-unit apartment building. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7924-0269-00.pdf
- That 148 Street site is designated Low Rise Residential in the Stage 1 Fleetwood Plan and sits within 800 metres of the future 152 Street SkyTrain station, in a Tier 3 Transit-Oriented Area (TOA) supporting up to eight storeys and 3.0 floor area ratio (FAR) — the plan context a house buyer on the corridor inherits. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7924-0269-00.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A house near the future stations is judged against the Fleetwood Plan, not just its current zone. The Stage 1 Plan was endorsed March 7, 2022, an updated Stage 1 was endorsed October 16, 2023, further changes followed to align with the Province's Transit-Oriented Areas legislation, and Council will consider the updated plan in 2026. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/fleetwood-land-use-plans/fleetwood-town-centre-plan
- Green Timbers itself cannot be built on: Surrey residents voted in 1988 and 1996 to designate part of the park as a protected urban forest, so houses adjoining it face a permanent park edge, not a future subdivision. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/green-timbers-urban-forest-park
- Surrey-wide small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules are in full in the city note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** When does SkyTrain actually reach a house here? **A:** Late 2029 is the anticipated in-service date. The Surrey–Langley SkyTrain project extends the Expo Line 16 kilometres on an elevated guideway from King George Station to 203 Street in Langley City, mainly along Fraser Highway; major construction started in November 2024, and Green Timbers Station at 140 Street and Fraser Highway is the first new station east of King George. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/09/SLS_Monthly-Status-Report_July_2026.pdf
- **Q:** Which schools serve a house between the parks? **A:** Enver Creek Secondary at 14505 84 Avenue is the local secondary. The district's 2026–2027 families document puts Brookside, Green Timbers, Janice Churchill and Maple Green elementaries in the Enver Creek family; Green Timbers Elementary is at 8824 144 Street and Brookside at 8555 142A Street, just south of Bear Creek Park — confirm the exact address in the locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** Will the forest behind a Green Timbers–edge house be developed? **A:** The park side will not, on the City's own history. The City states the forest was completely logged by 1929, replanted and dubbed British Columbia's Birthplace of Reforestation, became a park in 1974, and was voted a protected urban forest in 1988 and 1996 — the private lots outside the park boundary are a separate question. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/green-timbers-urban-forest-park
- **Q:** Does a Fraser Highway house lot have apartment potential? **A:** Near the stations, the plan points that way. The City's 148 Street report records a house-lot assembly rezoning from R3 to a Comprehensive Development (CD) zone for a six-storey, 78-unit building within 800 metres of the future 152 Street station, in a Tier 3 TOA — potential is plan- and site-specific, never automatic. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7924-0269-00.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Bear Creek Park's spray park was rebuilt in 2026 with four colour-coded sensory zones, and Bear Creek Park Stadium is a new destination athletics facility in the park; some track and field amenities were closed for upgrades until spring 2026, per the City park page. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bear-creek-park
- The Surrey Arts Centre — Surrey Art Gallery and Surrey Civic Theatres — sits inside Bear Creek Park at 13750 88 Avenue, the same address as the park, so event traffic shares the park's 88 Avenue entrance. https://www.surrey.ca/arts-culture/surrey-arts-centre
- Green Timbers Lake is shore fishing only: the City states the lake is regularly stocked, with no watercraft, wading or swimming, and the park has over 10 kilometres of nature trails plus the Surrey Nature Centre at 14225 Green Timbers Way. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/green-timbers-urban-forest-park
- École Simon Cunningham Elementary at 9380 140 Street sits at the southwest corner of Green Timbers Urban Forest, so a west-side house can be in a French immersion elementary catchment rather than the Enver Creek family streets to the east. https://www.surreyschools.ca/scunningham

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Today the area runs on buses; SkyTrain arrives late 2029. TransLink route 320 runs Surrey Central Station to Langley Centre via 104 Avenue, 152 Street and Fraser Highway along the south side, and the R1 King George Boulevard RapidBus stops at 88 Avenue by Bear Creek Park on the west side, between Newton Exchange and Guildford Exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/207/tt320.pdf
- The future Green Timbers Station was designed with as small a footprint as possible to minimize impacts on treed areas and local aquatic and fisheries sources, per the project's status report — station construction fronts Fraser Highway at 140 Street, not the park interior. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/09/SLS_Monthly-Status-Report_July_2026.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check any lot touching Bear Creek in COSMOS, the City of Surrey Mapping Online System, first. Bear Creek is a fish-bearing watercourse — the City park page notes spawning salmon or trout a short walk down the trail — and Surrey's Development Permit Areas (DPAs) include streamside protection areas where a permit can be required before land alteration or clearing. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bear-creek-park
- The mapped lowland floodplain is downstream, not in these streets: the City's Serpentine and Nicomekl Lowlands page lists Bear Creek among the Serpentine River's tributary areas, with the designated floodplain in the lowlands south of this upland area, much of it close to sea level behind dykes and sea dams. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
