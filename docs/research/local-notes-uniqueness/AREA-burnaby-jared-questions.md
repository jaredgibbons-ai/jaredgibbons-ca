# Burnaby — Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer, for a voice memo. Muse does not answer these.

1. If a buyer says "Burnaby" and nothing else, how do you sort them between Metrotown, Brentwood, Edmonds and the north-slope neighbourhoods — what questions decide it?
2. Which Burnaby pocket do you think buyers sleep on, and which one do they chase hardest relative to what it delivers?
3. What surprises buyers most across the city: strata levies in the 1990s towers, the pace of the Brentwood and Lougheed redevelopments, or how different the north and south slopes feel?
4. How does the development-charge picture (DCCs and amenity charges on new product) show up in your new-build deals here compared with neighbouring cities?
5. Tell one story that sums up buying in Burnaby in 2026 — a bidding war, a document surprise, or a neighbourhood that won a sceptical buyer over.
