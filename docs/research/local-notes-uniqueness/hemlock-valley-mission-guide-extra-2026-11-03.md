# Hemlock Valley Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: hemlock-valley-mission-guide (Hemlock Valley; Guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Hemlock Valley is not in Mission: it is unincorporated land in Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area C, governed by the FVRD's Hemlock Valley Official Community Plan (November 2019), even though listings carry Mission or Agassiz mailing addresses. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- The plan area is based on the Controlled Recreation Area (CRA) the Province established in 2016 with approval of the Hemlock Valley All Seasons Resort Master Plan, for what is now Sasquatch Mountain Resort. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- The village streets named in the plan area include Sakwi Creek Road, Edelweiss Drive, Mt Downing Road, Enzian Way and Snowflake Crescent, and the resort's own address is 20955 Hemlock Valley Road. https://www.supernaturalbc.com/listings/sasquatch-mountain-resort-900281713/
- Per the OCP, the largest nearby town is Agassiz in the District of Kent, approximately 38 kilometres away; Mission is approximately 55 kilometres west, and the Village of Harrison Hot Springs sits south of the plan area. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Inside the plan area, the OCP names three planning neighbourhoods — Hemlock West (the historic resort community), Hemlock East and Hemlock North — each to be detailed through neighbourhood plans as the resort expands. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The village is small: the FVRD's Hemlock Valley Evacuation Guide describes approximately 200 dwellings, mainly vacation homes and short-term rentals, with Sasquatch Mountain Resort at the village's upper end. http://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD Evacuation Guide - Hemlock Valley.pdf
- The existing resort accommodation base recorded in the OCP is 226 resort residential units equating to 1,072 bed units, about 60 percent of them ski-in/ski-out or within comfortable walking distance of the base area. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Forms in the village are detached cabins and chalets on the named streets, half-duplex product on Snowflake Crescent, and slope-side condo units at 21000 Enzian Way.
- The community has an organised owner body: the Hemlock Valley Homeowners Association, established in 1980, is a non-profit that works on behalf of valley homeowners. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Mission's bylaws do not apply here; Mission city-wide facts are carried in the mission-bc-mission-guide note, and Hemlock Valley runs on FVRD and provincial rules instead.
- The OCP's Development Permit Areas, in its Schedule I, include protection of development from hazardous conditions, so a slope lot can need a development permit from the FVRD before building. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- The OCP's build-out distribution proposes 40 percent public (nightly-rental) bed units, 45 percent private bed units and 15 percent resident/employee deed-restricted housing at full build-out — a plan target, not a description of today's tenure. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- The resort expansion in the provincially approved Resort Master Plan is a phased build-out toward 19,969 bed units and a Balanced Resort Capacity of 16,641 visitors per day — a long-range plan vision longer than the life of the plan, not built product. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Hemlock Valley part of Mission? **A:** No. It is unincorporated Fraser Valley Regional District Electoral Area C land governed by the FVRD's Hemlock Valley Official Community Plan. Real estate reporting groups a "Hemlock" sub-area under the community Mission, and listings carry Mission or Agassiz mailing addresses, but neither city governs the valley: permits, zoning and taxes run through the FVRD and the Province. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Q:** Who do I pay property tax to? **A:** The Province, not a city and not the FVRD. A property outside any municipality is in a rural area: its owner receives the tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5 percent penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5 percent on amounts unpaid on October 31. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area
- **Q:** Where do children go to school? **A:** There is no school in the valley. The OCP states the small permanent population will limit the viability of locating schools and medical services inside the Controlled Recreation Area, so students travel to outside communities. The nearest schools confirmed in the BC Ministry K-12 file are in Agassiz (School District 78) and at Deroche and Dewdney (School District 75), but the catchment for a specific Hemlock Valley address is not confirmed — see NOT CONFIRMED. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Q:** What is the ski hill, in the plan's own numbers? **A:** Sasquatch Mountain Resort, recorded in the OCP as 4 ski lifts servicing 146.8 hectares of developed ski terrain on 35 named trails, on the south and southwest slopes, using a 100 percent natural snowpack, with a Comfortable Carrying Capacity of about 1,150 skiers and boarders per day. The resort's own site describes it as 100 percent Canadian owned and completely smoke-free across all resort areas. https://sasquatchmountain.ca/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- There is no public transit to Hemlock Valley: the FVRD administers three BC Transit routes — Route 66 Fraser Valley Express, Route 71 Agassiz-Harrison and Route 72 Hope — and none is routed to the valley. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html
- The FVRD publishes a Hemlock Valley Evacuation Guide, and its evacuation route runs past Sts'ailes First Nation and Harrison Mills — the valley has one road out. http://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD Evacuation Guide - Hemlock Valley.pdf
- The FVRD's Electoral Area C 2026 residential utility rate sheet lists water systems for Morris Valley and Lake Errock and a Morris Valley sewer system; no Hemlock Valley system appears on it, so valley water and sewer servicing is a parcel-level question, not an FVRD utility bill lookup. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area C - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- The resort is smoke-free: Sasquatch Mountain Resort states it is completely smoke-free, including vaping, across all resort areas. https://sasquatchmountain.ca/
- The Hemlock Valley Homeowners Association has represented valley owners since 1980 and is the community body a new owner will meet first. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Mailing addresses mislead: a Hemlock Valley property can carry a Mission or an Agassiz postal address (V0M 1A1) while sitting in neither municipality. https://www.supernaturalbc.com/listings/sasquatch-mountain-resort-900281713/

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Hemlock Valley All Seasons Resort build-out, under the provincially approved Resort Master Plan and the FVRD OCP: a phased expansion from the existing 226 resort residential units (1,072 bed units) toward 19,969 bed units at full build-out, with Hemlock East and Hemlock North to be detailed through neighbourhood plans as the resort expands. STATUS: long-range approved plan vision, not a construction schedule; no phase timing is stated in the OCP read 2026-11-03. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- No municipal development application exists for the valley, because there is no municipality: FVRD approvals are the pipeline, and no current FVRD application for a Hemlock Valley project was confirmed in this pass.

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Property tax is provincial and rural: the tax notice comes from the Surveyor of Taxes Office, taxes are due July 2, and penalties are 5 percent on amounts unpaid July 2 and a further 5 percent on amounts unpaid October 31, under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, section 11. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax does not apply here: the Province's taxable-areas list names municipalities only — the City of Abbotsford, the City of Chilliwack and the District of Mission are listed, and unincorporated FVRD Electoral Area C is not a taxable area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- FVRD Electoral Area C utility billing, where an FVRD system exists, is quarterly (January, April, July, October) for metered services; no Hemlock Valley system is listed on the Area C 2026 rate sheet read in this pass. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area C - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
Read: 2026-11-03

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Driving is the only way in or out: the valley is reached by road from the Agassiz / Harrison Mills side, and the FVRD evacuation route runs past Sts'ailes First Nation and Harrison Mills. http://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD Evacuation Guide - Hemlock Valley.pdf
- The resort's own site describes Sasquatch Mountain Resort as "just 2 hours from Vancouver" — the operator's description, not a measured time. https://sasquatchmountain.ca/
- The nearest towns for services are Agassiz, approximately 38 kilometres away per the OCP, and Mission, approximately 55 kilometres west. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- No bus, shuttle or park-and-ride service to the valley was confirmed on an operator page; the FVRD's BC Transit routes 66, 71 and 72 do not serve it. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Wildfire interface is a named hazard layer here: the OCP carries a Hazard and Risk Management chapter including a Wildfire Interface Hazard section, and the valley sits in forested mountain terrain. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Check the lot's Development Permit Area status with the FVRD before valuing a slope lot: the OCP's Schedule I Development Permit Areas include protection of development from hazardous conditions. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Evacuation is a real due-diligence item, not a formality: the FVRD publishes a valley-specific evacuation guide with a single route out past Harrison Mills. http://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD Evacuation Guide - Hemlock Valley.pdf
- Servicing must be confirmed parcel by parcel: the OCP's infrastructure chapter covers water supply, sanitary sewer, stormwater, snow clearing and solid waste for the plan area, but which system a given lot is on, and its fees, are not published in the plan. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
