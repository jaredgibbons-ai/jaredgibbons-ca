# Chilliwack Proper East — Extra Local Notes
Page: chilliwack-proper-east (Chilliwack Proper East; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Chilliwack Proper East is the eastern half of Chilliwack Proper, the older urban grid north of the Vedder River in the City of Chilliwack. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- The City does not plan this ground under its board name: it sits inside Schedule B2, the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan, of the 2050 Official Community Plan (Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500). https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- Yale Road is the east–west spine, with Chilliwack Middle School at 46354 Yale Road and Chilliwack Secondary at 46363 Yale Road on it. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- Highway 1 (Trans-Canada Highway) access for this side of the city is at the Prest Road interchange, Exit 123. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- McNaught Road and Acorn Street carry the relocated Route 57 Broadway bus stops from January 4, 2026 — see Section 8. https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock here is mainly freehold houses on the older serviced grid, in zones such as R1-A (Urban Residential), minimum lot area 500 square metres with community water and sanitary sewer and minimum lot width 15 metres — city-wide zone figures are in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- Infill lots here can fall in the R1-C (Urban Infill) Zone, minimum lot area 300 square metres interior and 360 square metres corner — see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note for the full zone table. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A suite here must sit above the flood construction level: the City's small-scale multi-unit housing guide states new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3279/FAQ_-_Small-Scale_Housing_-_Updated_2025_07_28.pdf
- One accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000: a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 square metres and 40 percent of the building's habitable floor space, and garden suites are capped at 100 square metres (sections 2, 4.06 and 5.04). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- Accessory dwelling unit parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom unit or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach house or garden suite, under section 5.04 of the same bylaw. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- City-wide small-scale multi-unit housing and tax rules are written in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note — see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note. https://chilliwack.com/ocp

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which plan actually governs a Proper East lot? **A:** The Schedule B2 plan governs it, not a plan with this area's board name. Schedule B2 is the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan inside the 2050 Official Community Plan, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, and the Schedule D development permit areas (Development Permit Areas D-1 to D-10) apply parcel by parcel on top of it. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Q:** Can I add a suite to a house here? **A:** Yes, within exact size and flood limits. Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 allows one accessory dwelling unit per lot, a secondary suite capped at the lesser of 90 square metres and 40 percent of the home's habitable floor space, and the City's guide states a new suite may not sit below the flood construction level, so a low basement suite may not be legalisable. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q:** Which elementary school will my child attend? **A:** It depends on the exact address, so check it before you write an offer. School District No. 33 (Chilliwack) catchments are address-based, and the schools named for this side of the city include McCammon Elementary at 9601 Hamilton Street, Robertson Elementary at 46106 Southlands Crescent and Little Mountain Elementary at 9900 Carleton Street; no catchment result for a specific address is confirmed in this note. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/education/work/bc_k12_schools.csv
- **Q:** Did the bus on my street change recently? **A:** Yes, Route 57 Broadway changed on January 4, 2026. British Columbia Transit (BC Transit) moved the route's Carleton Street service one block east to McNaught Road, added new stops on McNaught Road and Acorn Street, and added Sunday-evening service on Routes 51, 53 Fairfield and 57 Broadway. https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/
- **Q:** Is a market median published for Chilliwack Proper East? **A:** No sourced median exists for this sub-area. Chilliwack is a Chilliwack and District Real Estate Board (CADREB) market, the board publishes board-wide statistics only, and Chilliwack is not one of the communities in the SnapStats extraction used on this site, so no sub-area figure is stated here. https://www.cadreb.com/market/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Flood level is the first check on any lot here: Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets the buildable floor at the flood construction level plus 0.6 metres of freeboard for new development in the floodplain. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- The same bylaw sets minimum setbacks of 60 metres from the natural boundary of the Fraser River, 75 metres from the Vedder River and 30 metres from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- Riparian lots fall under Development Permit Area 3, which protects fish habitat and riparian areas, and a Development Permit is required before subdivision, construction or land alteration inside a designated area unless an exemption applies. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- Which Schedule D permit areas touch a specific Proper East parcel is a per-parcel check with the City — the plan lists D-1 Drinking Water Supply through D-10 Industrial. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
