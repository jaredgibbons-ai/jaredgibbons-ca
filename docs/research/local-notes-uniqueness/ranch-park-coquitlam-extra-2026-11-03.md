# Ranch Park — Extra Local Notes
Page: ranch-park-coquitlam (Ranch Park; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Ranch Park is a board sub-area in southwest Coquitlam, and no City-published boundary for it was found, so the working check is street names: Spuraway Avenue is the spine, with Daybreak Avenue, Ranch Park Way, Armada Street, Fleet Street and Pasture Circle inside the pocket, Mariner Way on its west side and the slope down toward Como Lake Avenue and Lougheed Highway below it. https://www.bchydro.com/energy-in-bc/projects/partial-cable-replacement-coquitlam.html?fixzoom=true
- The pocket takes its name from Ranch Park itself, a City park the City describes as a local playground and open grass area for residents. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Ranch-Park-71
- Ranch Park Elementary, 2701 Spuraway Avenue, sits inside the pocket and is the street-level anchor most residents use to place the neighbourhood. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/coquitlam

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock here is detached houses on the Spuraway Avenue slope, not strata towers: no named condominium or townhouse complex inside the pocket was confirmed on a City, developer or strata source read for this note. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Ranch-Park-71
- Shared housing-stock facts for Ranch Park houses — build eras, lot patterns and subdivision history — are covered in full in the ranch-park-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; this note takes the street-level angle only (see the ranch-park-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note).
- The slope is the defining physical fact of the stock: British Columbia Hydro (BC Hydro) states the elevation change between the top of the slope at Spuraway Avenue and Pinnacle Street and the Como Lake Substation at Como Lake Avenue and Lougheed Highway is 170 metres, so lots step down the hillside rather than sitting flat. https://www.bchydro.com/energy-in-bc/projects/partial-cable-replacement-coquitlam.html?fixzoom=true

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools are in or beside Ranch Park? **A:** Ranch Park Elementary, at 2701 Spuraway Avenue, is inside the pocket and serves kindergarten to Grade 5. The middle and secondary schools for this part of southwest Coquitlam are Hillcrest Middle School, 2161 Regan Avenue, for Grades 6 to 8, and École Dr. Charles Best Secondary, 2525 Como Lake Avenue, for Grades 9 to 12. School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) assigns by home address, so confirm any specific house in the district locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/coquitlam
- **Q:** What is the BC Hydro work planned in the neighbourhood? **A:** BC Hydro is replacing a 1.3-kilometre section of 1970s underground, oil-insulated power cable with a new oil-free line along the existing utility corridor between Lougheed Highway and Spuraway Avenue. Vegetation clearing runs from summer 2026 into winter 2027, construction is expected to begin in spring 2027 and take about two years, and the new line is scheduled in service in winter 2028. https://www.bchydro.com/energy-in-bc/projects/partial-cable-replacement-coquitlam.html?fixzoom=true
- **Q:** Does the slope affect an older house here? **A:** Yes, in two practical ways a buyer can verify. BC Hydro states past leaks occurred at the existing cable's joints because of pressure from the 170-metre elevation change, which is a reminder of how much grade the pocket carries. The City also flags any lot with a steep slope on or next to it for an early talk with Development Services before a small-scale multi-unit design. https://www.bchydro.com/energy-in-bc/projects/partial-cable-replacement-coquitlam.html?fixzoom=true
- **Q:** Is Ranch Park in a river floodplain? **A:** The City's mapped river floodplains are the Coquitlam, Fraser and Pitt river floodplains, and this pocket sits on the slope above them rather than behind a dike. The local water question is slope drainage and the creek corridors below the pocket, not river flooding. A specific lot still needs checking on City mapping, because the floodplain study is a river study and does not grade individual hillside lots. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/182/Coquitlam-Fraser-and-Pitt-Rivers-Floodplain-Mapping-Report-PDF
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Ranch Park's own park is small and local in scale: the City lists a playground, outdoor fitness equipment and alcohol-permitted status, not sports fields or courts. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Ranch-Park-71
- Childcare has operated at the elementary school for decades: Ranch Park Kids Place, at 2701 Spuraway Avenue, states it has provided care for children ages 2.5 to 12 since 1985, with before- and after-school and summer programs. https://ranchparkkidsplace.com/
- The BC Hydro corridor work will be visible before construction starts: pre-work pruning and vegetation removal along the corridor was expected to resume in August 2026, ahead of the spring 2027 construction start. https://www.bchydro.com/energy-in-bc/projects/partial-cable-replacement-coquitlam.html?fixzoom=true
- The City lists the BC Hydro partial cable replacement among its external partner projects and confirms the same 1.3-kilometre scope, so corridor notices may come from either BC Hydro or the City. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1052/External-Partner-Projects
Read: 2026-11-03

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 153 is the pocket's bus: it runs from Braid Station via Como Lake Avenue and Mariner Way — with stops on Mariner Way at Hawser Avenue and Daybreak Avenue, on the pocket's west edge — to Coquitlam Central Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt153.pdf
- Both ends of route 153 are SkyTrain stations: Braid Station on the Millennium Line and Coquitlam Central Station on the Millennium Line Evergreen Extension, which is also the West Coast Express terminus. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt153.pdf
- Driving access runs downhill: Mariner Way connects the pocket to Barnet Highway, and Como Lake Avenue connects it to Lougheed Highway at the bottom of the slope. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt153.pdf
Read: 2026-11-03

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Slope is the first check on any Spuraway Avenue lot: the City flags lots with a steep slope on or next to them for early Development Services contact before small-scale building, and the pocket's grade is documented by BC Hydro at a 170-metre elevation change. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- Check the title for the BC Hydro transmission corridor and any easement before planning excavation, pools or additions, because the corridor crosses the neighbourhood between Lougheed Highway and Spuraway Avenue and will be an active construction zone from 2027. https://www.bchydro.com/energy-in-bc/projects/partial-cable-replacement-coquitlam.html?fixzoom=true
- River flood risk is checked, not assumed: the City's floodplain mapping covers the Coquitlam, Fraser and Pitt rivers, and the mapping report does not assign a flood level to individual hillside lots in this pocket. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/182/Coquitlam-Fraser-and-Pitt-Rivers-Floodplain-Mapping-Report-PDF
