## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Brentwood Park condos sit inside the Brentwood Town Centre in north Burnaby, one of four designated Town Centres in Burnaby's Official Community Plan, governed locally by the Brentwood Town Centre Development Plan adopted in 1996. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-town-centre-plan-amendments
- The plan area's core corner is Lougheed Highway and Willingdon Avenue: the City states the Brentwood Site, the primary core of the plan area, is at the northeast corner of Lougheed Highway and Willingdon Avenue, and the Buchanan West part of the centre is generally bounded by Lougheed Highway, Gilmore Avenue, Halifax Street and Madison Avenue/Douglas Street. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-town-centre-plan-amendments

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The Brentwood condo stock is high-rise towers over a retail core, not low-rise walk-ups: the City's Burnaby 2050 policy guide states residential development in the Brentwood Town Centre is primarily apartments ranging from high-rise to mid-rise towers. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/burnaby-2050-planning-our-city-together/burnaby-2050-policy-guide-share-your-feedback/growth-scenarios
- The Amazing Brentwood by SHAPE Properties is the anchor project at 4567 Lougheed Highway: SHAPE states it is a 28-acre site with more than 6,000 homes at completion over 11 residential towers, that Neighbourhood One is complete, and that Tower Six, with 369 homes, is the current tower selling in Neighbourhood Two. https://shape.ca/portfolio/the-amazing-brentwood/
- Not every Amazing Brentwood tower is a condo: SHAPE states Tower One's 300 homes are professionally managed rental homes with full-time concierge, so a Brentwood buyer must separate the strata towers from the rental tower in the same project. https://shape.ca/portfolio/the-amazing-brentwood/
- Concord Brentwood by Concord Pacific is the second master-planned cluster, on the south side of Lougheed Highway: its Hillside West homeowner page gives the addresses 4720 Lougheed Highway and 4730 Lougheed Highway, Burnaby, for its two towers. https://www.concordpacific.com/homeowner/hillside-west/
- Concord's next phase is Hillside East: Concord Pacific describes Hillside East as two elevated towers perched above the treetops, steps from SkyTrain and the Brentwood mall, as the newest phase of Concord Brentwood. https://www.concordpacific.com/project/concord-brentwood/
- Older Brentwood condo stock predates these two projects around the station and along Halifax Street and Dawson Street; addresses and build years for those earlier strata buildings could not be confirmed from a municipal or developer source in this pass — see NOT CONFIRMED.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Brentwood Site Conceptual Master Plan, adopted in 2013, is the document that governs the Amazing Brentwood lands: the City states it outlines the principles guiding redevelopment of the site into a commercial and residential hub, and that the Brentwood Town Centre Development Plan is the community plan over it. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-town-centre-plan-amendments
- The plan is being amended for rentals and childcare: the City states recent changes to its Rental Use Zoning Policy and Child Care Action Plan provided the opportunity to revisit the Brentwood Town Centre Development Plan and the Brentwood Site Master Plan to provide more diverse housing options and social amenities. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/brentwood-town-centre-plan-amendments
- One Amazing Brentwood parcel is proposed to change use: the City consultation describes redesignating the parcel at Willingdon Avenue and Lougheed Highway from commercial zoning to a mixed-use residential and commercial zoned site under the amended master plan. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-town-centre-plan-amendments
- A strata in Brentwood cannot ban long-term rentals: the Province states that as of November 24, 2022 no strata corporation is allowed to have a residential rental-restriction bylaw, while short-term rental restrictions still apply and a strata can fine up to $1,000 a day for breaching a short-term rental bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/short-term-rental-bylaws
- Age bylaws in a Brentwood strata are limited the same way: the Province states the only permitted strata age restriction is a 55-and-over bylaw kept to preserve seniors' housing, and an age bylaw setting a minimum below 55 is invalid. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/more-homes-for-people

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is every tower at The Amazing Brentwood a strata condo I can buy? **A:** No. SHAPE states Tower One's 300 homes are professionally managed rental homes, while Tower Six's 369 homes are the current ownership offering in Neighbourhood Two. The project will hold more than 6,000 homes over 11 towers at completion on the 28-acre site at 4567 Lougheed Highway, in a mix of tenures set phase by phase, so the tenure of a specific tower must be checked with the developer, not assumed from the project name. https://shape.ca/portfolio/the-amazing-brentwood/
- **Q:** What is the difference between Brentwood Park and Brentwood Town Centre? **A:** Brentwood Park is the neighbourhood name used for the residential area; Brentwood Town Centre is the City's planning designation. Burnaby states Brentwood is one of four designated Town Centres in its Official Community Plan, intended to accommodate a significant portion of the city's population and job growth, and the Brentwood Town Centre Development Plan (1996) is the community plan for the area. Condos inside the plan boundary follow that plan's density framework. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-town-centre-plan-amendments
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves a Brentwood condo south of Lougheed Highway? **A:** A new one is planned, because today the children cross the highway. The City and the Burnaby School District state the site held for a new elementary school is 4900 Dawson Street, south of Lougheed Highway, so students would not have to cross a busy road to reach current schools north of Lougheed; the attendance area already held more than 400 elementary students when the site was announced. Until it is built, confirm the catchment for the exact tower address with the district. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2023-02-21/city-and-school-district-determine-site-new-school-brentwood-neighbourhood
- **Q:** How busy is Brentwood Town Centre Station for a condo buyer relying on it? **A:** TransLink calls it the third busiest Millennium Line station and states it recorded over 2.2 million boardings in 2024, serves roughly 23,000 residents and 15,000 workers within a 10-minute walk, and is a transfer point for bus routes 25, 123, 130, 134, 136, 222 and the N9. It is also the only SkyTrain station built on a roadway median. https://www.translink.ca/news/2025/march/brentwood%20town%20centre%20skytrain%20station%20upgrades%20complete
- **Q:** Can I rent out a Brentwood condo I buy? **A:** For a long-term tenancy, the strata cannot stop you: provincial law since November 24, 2022 prohibits strata rental-restriction bylaws. Short-term rentals are different — a strata may still limit or ban them and fine up to $1,000 a day, and the provincial Short-Term Rental Accommodations Act applies on top of the strata bylaw, so read both before buying a Brentwood unit for short-term rental use. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/short-term-rental-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The station above the condos was rebuilt while the towers rose: TransLink states the Brentwood Town Centre Station upgrades, completed for customers on March 24, 2025, added a new street-level elevator, two new escalators, an expanded mezzanine, three new fare gates and two glass-enclosed stairwells, a $32 million project. https://www.translink.ca/news/2025/march/brentwood%20town%20centre%20skytrain%20station%20upgrades%20complete
- Brentwood's new elementary school is a held site, not a built school: the City states the site at 4900 Dawson Street coincides with planned park and road works including the Dawson Street extension, and the school district still had to work through the steps to secure the site and request provincial capital funding. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2023-02-21/city-and-school-district-determine-site-new-school-brentwood-neighbourhood
- A railway cuts the neighbourhood in two: the City is studying a pedestrian and cyclist overpass across the active railway in Brentwood at five candidate locations from the Town Centre plan — Madison Avenue, Alaska Street, Willingdon Avenue, Beta Avenue and Douglas Road — because the tracks separate homes from schools, shops and the Central Valley Greenway. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-pedestrian-and-cyclist-overpass-feasibility-study
- Concord Brentwood units were built with building-wide water softening and electric vehicle charging at every residential parking stall: Concord Pacific states all buildings at Concord Brentwood incorporate a water softening system and all residential parking stalls have pre-installed 208 volt, 40 amp EV charging outlets. https://phase1.concordbrentwood.com/
- The mall under the towers is a working construction site in phases: the City held its April 2, 2024 open house on the plan amendments inside The Amazing Brentwood at 4567 Lougheed Highway, second floor Centre Court — the project is still amending its master plan while people live in its first towers. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2024-03-15/city-invites-feedback-proposal-amendments-brentwood-town-centre

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby's total 2026 residential property tax rate is $2.97749 per $1,000 of assessed value on the City's tax information page, made up of a $1.55649 City portion plus school at $1.01300, TransLink at $0.31410, Metro Vancouver at $0.05800 and BC Assessment at $0.03570. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/additional-information
- Utility movement for a Brentwood condo in 2026 is stated by the City: water services increase 0 percent and sewer services increase 3.5 percent, approximately $14 for a condo, under the approved 2026–2030 Financial Plan with its 2.9 percent property tax increase. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2026-01-28/burnaby-keeps-tax-rates-affordable-latest-financial-plan
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in Burnaby: the Province lists the City of Burnaby as a designated taxable area in the Metro Vancouver Regional District, so a Brentwood condo owner must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- Burnaby's rates bylaw for 2026 is Burnaby Rates Bylaw 2026, Bylaw No. 14814, adopted May 12, 2026 — the bylaw to cite when a strata's tax figure is questioned. https://bylaws.burnaby.ca/media/14000/14814.pdf

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Brentwood Town Centre Station on the Millennium Line is the centre's transit anchor: TransLink states it is a transfer point for seven bus routes — 25, 123, 130, 134, 136, 222 and N9 — connecting to UBC, New Westminster, Metrotown, North Vancouver, Coquitlam and late-night service from downtown Vancouver. https://www.translink.ca/news/2025/march/brentwood%20town%20centre%20skytrain%20station%20upgrades%20complete
- The station is the only SkyTrain station built on a roadway median, sitting in the middle of Lougheed Highway east of Willingdon Avenue, with a publicly accessible mezzanine that also works as a pedestrian crossing of the highway. https://www.translink.ca/news/2025/march/brentwood%20town%20centre%20skytrain%20station%20upgrades%20complete
- A future Metrotown–North Shore Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) line is planned through the station: TransLink states the BRT will operate between Metrotown and Park Royal via the Ironworkers Memorial Bridge, with Brentwood Town Centre Station one of the connection points used to set station locations. https://www.translink.ca/news/2025/august/translink%20seeks%20feedback%20on%20metrotown-north%20shore%20bus%20rapid%20transit
- Driving access is Highway 1 (Trans-Canada Highway) immediately south of the plan area and Lougheed Highway through it; the City notes the Brentwood Community Centre site is near rapid transit, Highway 1 and the Central Valley Greenway, the cycling route that runs through the neighbourhood. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/brentwood-community-centre

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check which master-plan phase a tower sits in before relying on a view or amenity: the Brentwood Site Master Plan is under amendment, including a proposed redesignation of the Willingdon Avenue and Lougheed Highway parcel from commercial to mixed-use residential and commercial, so land beside a finished tower can still change use. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-town-centre-plan-amendments
- Check tenure tower by tower in mixed projects: at The Amazing Brentwood the developer states Tower One is rental housing it manages itself, so a resale unit's neighbours and the building's strata documents differ from a pure strata tower — confirm the strata plan and tenure for the specific tower. https://shape.ca/portfolio/the-amazing-brentwood/
- The railway is an active corridor through the plan area: the City's overpass study exists because the tracks are a barrier residents must cross, and condo units facing the corridor on Dawson Street and Willingdon Avenue should be checked for rail noise at viewing, a matter no City page quantifies. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-pedestrian-and-cyclist-overpass-feasibility-study
