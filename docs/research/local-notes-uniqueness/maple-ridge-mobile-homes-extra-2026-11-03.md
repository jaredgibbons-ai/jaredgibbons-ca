# Maple Ridge Mobile Homes — Extra Local Notes
Page: maple-ridge-mobile-homes (Maple Ridge; mobile homes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Maple Ridge's manufactured homes sit in seven park sites across the city, not in one district. A City report counts approximately 205 manufactured homes in home park sites, and says all seven sites would require rezoning before any redevelopment. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/update-tenant-protectionstpb-reportattachmentspdf
- The southwest of the city, including Hammond, is bounded by Pitt Meadows and the Katzie First Nation to the west and by the Fraser River to the south, and Wharf Street in Hammond serves as both a road and a dike. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026-06-25-Hammond-Area-Plan_0.pdf
- Lougheed Highway is the main east–west corridor through the park areas, and it carries the R3 RapidBus between Coquitlam Central Station and Haney Place. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A home on a rented pad here is a tenancy under the provincial Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA): the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad, and the full framework is in the surrey-mobile-homes note — see the surrey-mobile-homes note. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- Maple Ridge's Manufactured Home Park Redevelopment Tenant Assistance Policy No. 6.27, in an updated version effective July 28, 2026, applies where residents are expected to be displaced if the City approves a development application, and it supplements the MHPTA. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/manufactured-home-park-redevelopment-tenant-assistance-policy-no-627pdf
- Policy 6.27 works alongside the City's Tenant Protection Bylaw No. 8106-2026, Policy 6.32 Tenant Relocation Assistance, and Policy 6.40 Rental Protection. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/manufactured-home-park-redevelopment-tenant-assistance-policy-no-627pdf
- Assigning a pad tenancy to a buyer needs the park owner's prior written consent on Residential Tenancy Branch (RTB) Form 10; the owner must respond so the home owner receives it by the 10th day, and no response by the end of day 10 means consent is deemed given. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What happens to me if my Maple Ridge park is redeveloped? **A:** You get two layers of protection, one provincial and one from the City. The MHPTA sets the legal notice and compensation floor, and the City's Policy No. 6.27 adds communication with residents, relocation planning, financial compensation, moving help, a right to return when possible, and replacement-housing support where a development application would displace residents. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/community-planning/housing-action/tenant-relocation-policies
- **Q:** Can the park owner block my purchase of a home on a pad? **A:** The owner can require consent, but cannot simply ignore the request. Assignment needs prior written consent requested on RTB Form 10, the owner's response must reach the home owner by the 10th day, and silence by the end of that day is conclusively deemed consent; no fee may be charged for the consent itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q:** How much can my pad rent go up each year? **A:** Only by a percentage set by the Province, never by an amount the park chooses freely. For 2026 the limit for a manufactured home site is 2.3 percent plus the proportionate increase in local government levies and regulated utility fees for the park's common property, with three full months' notice and only one increase in 12 months. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/rent-rtb/rent-increases
- **Q:** Does a manufactured home here have to meet flood construction rules? **A:** Yes, the same flood rules apply to manufactured homes as to other houses. The City's Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 8121-2026 counts manufactured home pads as structures and requires manufactured homes to meet the same Flood Construction Level as other residential uses, although replacing an existing home in an existing park below that level may be permitted if the non-conformity is not increased and no units are added. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
- **Q:** Is The Maples co-op on Cherrington Avenue a mobile home park? **A:** No, it is a different form of housing entirely. The Co-operative Housing Federation of British Columbia (CHF BC) describes The Maples Housing Co-operative at 21570 Cherrington Avenue as 31 semi-detached co-op homes between Dewdney Trunk Road and Lougheed Highway, which is membership housing, not a pad you rent under a manufactured home park tenancy. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The whole city has only about 205 manufactured homes in parks, so this is a small resale stock rather than a deep market. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/update-tenant-protectionstpb-reportattachmentspdf
- Policy 6.27 was replaced by an updated version effective July 28, 2026 that supersedes the September 26, 2023 version, and it is scheduled for review on July 28, 2028. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/manufactured-home-park-redevelopment-tenant-assistance-policy-no-627pdf
- The City's tenant protection page groups the park policy with a Tenant Protection Bylaw, a Tenant Relocation Assistance policy, and a Rental Protection policy, so park residents sit inside a wider city tenant-protection system. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/community-planning/housing-action/tenant-relocation-policies
- From January 1, 2026, metered water and sewer in Maple Ridge moved to seasonal metered rates, and the sewer flat fee no longer appears on the tax notice for metered customers, while flat-rate customers are still billed water and sewer on the tax notice. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/changes-coming-metered-water-and-sewer-fees-maple-ridge
- The City monitors the North and South Alouette Rivers, Kanaka Creek, and the Fraser River, publishes flood maps, and treats the April-to-July freshet as the main flood season. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/be-flood-ready-guide-fraser-river-city-maple-ridge
- School District 42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows) serves the city, and the provincial school list for the district includes Alouette Elementary at 22155 Isaac Crescent and Maple Ridge Elementary at 20820 River Road. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Flood is the first check: a manufactured home must meet the same Flood Construction Level as any other residence under Bylaw No. 8121-2026, so a buyer should confirm the pad's flood status before removing subjects. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
- Redevelopment risk runs through City Hall: because every park site needs rezoning to redevelop, a rezoning application is the early-warning sign, and Policy 6.27 is the document that sets out what residents then receive. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/update-tenant-protectionstpb-reportattachmentspdf
- A purchase is not complete on the pad until assignment consent is dealt with, so the RTB Form 10 consent step, with its 10-day deemed-consent rule, belongs in the offer timeline. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- Park rules form part of the tenancy agreement under the MHPTA framework summarized in the surrey-mobile-homes note, so a buyer should read the current park rules, including any age or pet rules, before buying rather than rely on how a listing describes them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
