# Clayton Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: clayton (Clayton, Surrey; homes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Clayton is in Surrey, not Langley — the City plans it as Clayton Heights, about 809 hectares from Fraser Highway in the south to the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) in the north and west, bordered by the Township of Langley in the east. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/clayton-general-land-use-plan
- Clayton-shared facts are treated in full in the earlier houses note — see the clayton-cloverdale-houses-for-sale note; this note adds what a general Clayton page needs beyond the houses angle.
- The City's plan neighbourhoods inside Clayton Heights are East Clayton, West Clayton and North Clayton, and a land use plan for North Clayton has not yet been initiated. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/clayton-general-land-use-plan
- The Fraser Valley Real Estate Board (FVREB) sub-area is called Clayton, one of three sub-areas of the board community Cloverdale, alongside Cloverdale and Serpentine — board name and City planning name (Clayton Heights) describe the same ground. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/clayton-general-land-use-plan

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Clayton is a post-1999 planned community — East Clayton was endorsed in 2003 as the first urban neighbourhood, and West Clayton's final Neighbourhood Concept Plan (NCP) was approved in July 2015 as the second. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton
- West Clayton is planned as the future home of over 12,000 Surrey residents, with a mixed-use urban centre beside a future rapid transit station at 184 Street and Fraser Highway. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/west-clayton-ncp
- The civic anchor is Clayton Community Centre, 7155 187A Street, a 75,000 square foot facility integrating arts, recreation, library and parks, officially opened October 16, 2021. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/city-of-surrey-officially-opens-clayton-community-centre-4

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Do not rely on an old NCP label alone — the City records that East and West Clayton land use designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) update, so confirm a lot's current OCP designation. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton
- Surrey-wide zoning, Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH), suite and coach house rules are treated in full in the earliest Surrey note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note; shared Clayton detail is in the clayton-cloverdale-houses-for-sale note.
- Clayton's northern and western edges run to the ALR, so edge lots follow the Agricultural Land Commission housing frame as well as City zoning. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/clayton-general-land-use-plan

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Clayton in Surrey or Langley? **A:** Surrey — homes east of the municipal boundary are in the Township of Langley, not in Surrey Clayton. The City describes East Clayton as north of Fraser Highway along the border with the Township of Langley, so two similar streets can sit in different municipalities, with different bylaws, taxes and school districts; confirm which side of the line a home sits on before comparing it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton
- **Q:** Which schools are in Clayton? **A:** Schools confirmed by name and address in the Clayton and east Cloverdale part of Surrey include Katzie Elementary, Hazelgrove Elementary and Clayton Heights Secondary. Catchments in School District 36 (SD36) are address-based and no Clayton locator run is on record, so check the exact address in the district locator before relying on a school for a specific home. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** When does SkyTrain reach Clayton? **A:** Two named stations serve Clayton directly, and the in-service date is 2029 on the project pages. Hillcrest–184 Street Station sits at 184 Street and Fraser Highway and Clayton Station at the future 190 Street and Fraser Highway, on the 16-kilometre Expo Line extension from King George Station to 203 Street in Langley City — a plan under construction, not an operating line today. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/
- **Q:** What is the Clayton Community Centre? **A:** It is Clayton's combined recreation, arts and library building at 7155 187A Street. The 75,000 square foot centre officially opened October 16, 2021 and includes a fitness centre, a gymnasium and a full-service 14,000 square foot Surrey Libraries branch, which is why it functions as the neighbourhood's civic centre rather than a single-purpose hall. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/city-of-surrey-officially-opens-clayton-community-centre-4

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Station proximity is a plan fact, not yet a service — Clayton Station and Hillcrest–184 Street Station are named project stations with an anticipated in-service date of 2029. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/
- The former RF-12 and RF-SD zone names in older Clayton material were replaced by R4 Small Lot Residential and R6 Semi-Detached in Surrey's July 8, 2024 SSMUH changes — older labels are pre-2024 names. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Current bus detail stated in a 2026 City planning report for east Clayton: the closest bus stops are on 72 Avenue at 191 Street, route 370 runs Willowbrook to Cloverdale and route 372 runs Langley Centre to Clayton Heights. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7924-0277-00
- A 10-metre multi-use pathway is required along the south edge of the 74 Avenue townhouse site in that same report, linking east–west to Salmonberry Park — trail connections here arrive through development conditions, lot by lot. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7924-0277-00

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey's 2026 residential tax rate, parcel taxes and utility charges are treated in full in the earliest Surrey note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note.
- Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5 percent penalty after July 2 and a further 5 percent after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies in Surrey, a designated taxable area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Clayton's current bus spine is Fraser Highway and 72 Avenue — routes 370 and 372 serve the east Clayton stops at 72 Avenue and 191 Street, per the City's 2026 planning report. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7924-0277-00
- Fraser Highway is also the SkyTrain corridor: the extension runs along Fraser Highway from King George Station to Langley City. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/surrey-langley-skytrain

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Confirm ALR status on any lot at Clayton's north or west edge before assuming residential rebuild rights — the plan area runs to the ALR on both edges. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/clayton-general-land-use-plan
- Confirm the current OCP designation, not the superseded NCP label, and check whether a lot falls inside a Transit-Oriented Area (TOA) around the future 184 Street or 190 Street stations, which changes the density frame. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/west-clayton-ncp
- Confirm the municipality on title before an offer near the eastern edge — Surrey and Township of Langley rules diverge at that line. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton
