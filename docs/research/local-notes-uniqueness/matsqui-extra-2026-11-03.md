# Matsqui Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: matsqui (Matsqui; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Matsqui is the Fraser River floodplain prairie in north Abbotsford, running from the Mt. Lehman/Bradner side east toward Sumas Mountain. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- Highway 11 (Abbotsford–Mission Highway) bisects Matsqui Prairie and crosses the Fraser River into Mission at the Mission Bridge. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- Harris Road, Gladwin Road, Riverside Road and Page Road form the prairie road fabric named in City flood and drainage material. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/diking-drainage
- The City lists three floodplains — Matsqui Prairie, Sumas Prairie and Glen Valley — inside its roughly 39,000-hectare area. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58625/

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Homes here sit mainly on the flat prairie floor or in historic Matsqui Village, surrounded by working farms. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- Most prairie parcels are large agricultural lots rather than urban subdivisions, under Agricultural zones in Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58312/
- Matsqui Trail Regional Park became a Fraser Valley Regional District park in 2017 and is operated by the City. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/matsqui-trail-regional-park-grade-railway-crossing-closure
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) and the Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) control most building here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- A principal residence in the ALR is capped at 500 m² total floor area, and a larger home needs a Non-Adhering Residential Use application. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- An owner may generally have no more than one residence per ALR parcel unless the ALR Use Regulation or the Commission permits an additional residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- City policy C010-10 requires City approval before private structures such as bridges, irrigation pipes or field drains are placed in City-maintained ditches and dikes. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coaother/c01010

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Did Matsqui flood in 2021 like Sumas Prairie did? **A:** Sumas Prairie took the main 2021 inundation, while Matsqui roads also closed. The City states the November 2021 Nooksack overflow flooded Sumas Prairie, forced Highway 1 and the border to close, and evacuated over 1,100 properties and over 3,300 people; Matsqui Prairie risk is a separate Fraser River dike risk, which the City says would be even more devastating if the Matsqui Dike breached. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- **Q:** What protects the prairie from the Fraser River? **A:** The Matsqui Dike along the Fraser River is the protection, and the City monitors it every freshet. The City states the Matsqui Dikes are designed to keep Fraser River water out of Matsqui Prairie, but seepage pooling is common during high water, and owners should avoid removing trees, deep cultivation or machine digging near dikes from May to September. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/2022-spring-freshet-update
- **Q:** Can I build a second house on a farm parcel? **A:** Usually only if ALC rules allow it, not by right. Provincial policy states an owner may have no more than one residence per parcel unless the ALR Use Regulation permits an additional residence or the Commission approves a Non-Adhering Residential Use application, and the Commission must not approve an extra residence unless it is necessary for a farm use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- **Q:** Which elementary school is in the village? **A:** Máthxwi Elementary, formerly Matsqui Elementary, at 33661 Elizabeth Avenue. The Abbotsford School District (SD34) school site lists Máthxwi Elementary at 33661 Elizabeth Avenue, Abbotsford, V4X 1T4, phone 604.826.8181, and the provincial school list records it as a K–5 school at that address. Confirm any address catchment with SD34 before removing subjects. https://mathxwi.abbyschools.ca/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Irrigation water levels in Matsqui Prairie are lowered each fall and restored each May. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/diking-drainage
- The City states irrigation levels across Sumas and Matsqui Prairies are gradually reduced from late September and restored in May for the growing season. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/diking-drainage
- The Canadian Pacific Kansas City at-grade crossing on the dike in Matsqui Trail Regional Park was permanently closed to meet Transport Canada rules by end of November 2024, with signed rerouting for trail users. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/matsqui-trail-regional-park-grade-railway-crossing-closure
- Real-time Fraser River levels for this reach are read at the Mission Bridge station (08MH024) named by the City. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/2022-spring-freshet-update

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- The Fraser River bank erosion protection project for the Matsqui Dike is the active infrastructure project here. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/governments-canada-and-british-columbia-invest-fraser-river-bank-erosion
- Canada, British Columbia and the City are investing more than $19 million to stabilise about three kilometres of riverbank along the Matsqui Dike, with Sumas First Nation and Matsqui First Nation involved and fishing-site and habitat restoration in the design. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/governments-canada-and-british-columbia-invest-fraser-river-bank-erosion
- The Clayburn Creek Improvement Project, status Current, widens and deepens about 800 m of lower Clayburn Creek in Matsqui Prairie from below 4315 Wright Street to the west boundary of 34416 Clayburn Road, with offsetting work anticipated in the August–September 2026 fisheries window. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/projects-go/clayburn-creek-improvement-project

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Prairie properties pay extra diking and drainage charges on top of general Abbotsford taxes. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf
- The 2026 Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026 defines a Matsqui Prairie Dyking, Drainage and Irrigation District with its own levies, separate from the Sumas Prairie district. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf
- Effective March 1, 2026 the residential water rate is $1.46/m³ and sewer rate is $1.35/m³; the industrial/agricultural water rate is $1.32/m³ and sewer $1.22/m³. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/water-sewer-rates
- Abbotsford is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so residential owners must declare. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- For the headline Abbotsford residential tax rate, see the sumas-prairie note.

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Highway 11 over the Mission Bridge is the main road link north. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- BC Transit Central Fraser Valley route 31 Abbotsford/Mission runs via Highway 11, with select trips historically diverting via Matsqui Village on Harris Road and Riverside Street; check the current BC Transit schedule for the village trips. https://cptdb.ca/wiki/index.php/Central_Fraser_Valley_Transit_System_route_31_%27Abbotsford_/_Mission%27
- The Matsqui Trail runs on the dike through Matsqui Trail Regional Park, operated by the City for the FVRD. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/matsqui-trail-regional-park-grade-railway-crossing-closure

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the dike, freshet seepage and flood construction rules for the exact lot before offering. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/2022-spring-freshet-update
- The provincial Lower Mainland dike inventory maps show dike infrastructure, pump stations, flood boxes and known hazard concerns, including low dikes and sand boils, for this reach. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/drought-flooding-dikes-dams/integrated-flood-hazard-management/governance/dike-management/dike-inventory
- City Utilities Master Plans work notes parts of the Matsqui Dike are currently lower than the design level for a major 1-in-500-year flood event. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58625/
- Ditch works need City and fisheries approvals under the Watercourse Management Policy; undesignated ditches are kept clean by the adjacent owner. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coaother/c01010
