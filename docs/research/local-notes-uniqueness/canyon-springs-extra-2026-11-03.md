## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Coquitlam-wide zoning, small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH), tax and utility facts are written in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; this note covers what is specific to Canyon Springs. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Pinetree Way address here? **A:** Pinetree Way Elementary, Summit Middle and Pinetree Secondary serve the sample address 1420 Pinetree Way. School District No. 43 (SD43) catchments are address-based in a three-tier system, and Pinetree Way Elementary itself is at 1420 Pinetree Way, so run the exact address in the SD43 School Locator rather than assuming every Canyon Springs street feeds the same three schools. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** Is Canyon Springs an official City neighbourhood? **A:** No. Canyon Springs is the board sub-area name used in market reporting; no City of Coquitlam neighbourhood plan carries that name in sources read. City documents group these streets with the City Centre and the Transit-Oriented Areas around the Evergreen Extension stations, which is the planning frame that actually governs rezoning and density here. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Q:** Which SkyTrain stations are closest? **A:** Lincoln and Lafarge Lake-Douglas, both Millennium Line (Evergreen Extension) stations on Pinetree Way, are the nearest stations named in City Transit-Oriented Areas documents. Coquitlam Central Station, also on the Evergreen Extension, adds West Coast Express service. Which station is walkable depends on the exact street, and no City source assigns a station to the sub-area as a whole. https://coquitlam.ca.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?view_id=2&clip_id=2891&meta_id=82306
- **Q:** What runs through the park side of the area? **A:** Hoy Creek and its linear park trail system run beside Town Centre Park, with trailheads at Pinetree Way and Guildford Way shown on the City's Salmon Come Home event map. Creek-side lots are exactly where the 30-metre Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area and the Zoning Bylaw flood setbacks apply, so a creek address needs a permit check before any addition is designed. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15758/2025_Salmon_Come_Home_Map_11x85in-v5?bidId
Read: 2026-11-03

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check any lot within 30 metres of a watercourse top of bank against the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area before buying; the permit, not the zoning alone, decides what can be built there. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 section 519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and section 523 covers riparian areas — the two sections to read for Hoy Creek and ravine-edge lots. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
