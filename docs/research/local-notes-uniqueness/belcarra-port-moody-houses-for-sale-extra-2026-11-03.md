# Belcarra Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: belcarra-port-moody-houses-for-sale (Belcarra; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Belcarra is its own municipality, the Village of Belcarra, not part of the City of Port Moody: it sits on a narrow peninsula at the mouth of Indian Arm, with a land area of about 5.50 square kilometres and a population of 687 in the 2021 Census; the full shared area facts are in the belcarra-port-moody note. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/emergency-management-service-delivery.pdf
- One road runs the village: Bedwell Bay Road enters from Port Moody, becomes the spine past the Village Hall at 4084 Bedwell Bay Road, and the residential streets split into the Belcarra Bay side (Belcarra Bay Road, Salish Road, Whiskey Cove Lane) and the Bedwell Bay side (Kelly Avenue, Senkler Road, Turtlehead Road). https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2026/07/Public-Notice-of-Zoning-Bylaw-Amendment-463-2026-Readings.pdf
- The village was incorporated on August 22, 1979, and its Indigenous name is təmtəmíxʷtən (Tum-Tumay-Whueton), an ancestral village of the Tsleil-Waututh Nation. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/emergency-management-service-delivery.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is almost entirely detached houses: the Village's tax base is almost all residential, and the zoning that covers most lots is One-Family Residential (RS-1) under Zoning Bylaw No. 510, 2018. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2026/07/2.0-2026-07-27-Regular-Council-Meeting-Agenda-Package.pdf
- The RS-1 zone permits up to one principal single-family house and one accessory unit, either a secondary suite or a coach house, per lot. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2026/07/2.0-2026-07-27-Regular-Council-Meeting-Agenda-Package.pdf
- Named sub-areas in the Official Community Plan (OCP) are Belcarra Bay and Bedwell Bay; Farrer Cove, at the north end, is covered by its own zone, RM-2 (Farrer Cove South), in the zoning bylaw's district list. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2024/10/621-2024-Official-Communitiy-Plan-Bylaw-SCHEDULE-A.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Belcarra runs its own bylaws: the 2024 Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 621, 2024) and Zoning Bylaw No. 510, 2018 govern permits and lots, not Port Moody's bylaws, even though the postal and real-estate area name is Port Moody. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2024/10/621-2024-Official-Communitiy-Plan-Bylaw-SCHEDULE-A.pdf
- Servicing is the binding constraint: houses here rely on on-site septic, and the Village ties any wider permissions to septic and water capacity, while the municipal water system serves the majority of properties in Belcarra Bay and Bedwell Bay. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2026/07/2.0-2026-07-27-Regular-Council-Meeting-Agenda-Package.pdf
- Belcarra is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Belcarra part of Port Moody for taxes and permits? **A:** No. The Village of Belcarra is a separate municipality with its own council, its own 2024 Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 621, 2024) and its own Zoning Bylaw No. 510, 2018, so building permits, tax rates and servicing rules are the Village's, even though the postal and real-estate area name is Port Moody. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2024/10/621-2024-Official-Communitiy-Plan-Bylaw-SCHEDULE-A.pdf
- **Q:** Can I add a suite or coach house on a Belcarra lot? **A:** Yes, one accessory unit: the RS-1 zone permits up to one principal single-family house and one accessory unit, either a secondary suite or a coach house, per lot — but the added unit still has to work on the lot's septic system, and the Village ties wider permissions to septic and water capacity. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2026/07/2.0-2026-07-27-Regular-Council-Meeting-Agenda-Package.pdf
- **Q:** Where do my children go to school? **A:** There is no school building in the Village: the Official Community Plan's community facilities map lists fire protection (the Sasamat Volunteer Fire Department), the Waste and Recycling Depot, the Village Hall, a multi-sport court and the TransLink community shuttle — no school — so Belcarra addresses are served by School District No. 43 (SD43) schools outside the village, confirmed by address with SD43. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2024/10/621-2024-Official-Communitiy-Plan-Bylaw-SCHEDULE-A.pdf
- **Q:** What happens to garbage and recycling? **A:** Belcarra runs a central drop-off, not curbside collection: the Official Community Plan facilities map names solid waste management as a central drop-off Waste and Recycling Depot, and the Village budgets the depot as its own operating fund, separate from water. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2025/10/Village-of-Belcarra-Draft-2026-2040-Financial-Plan.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- təmtəmíxʷtən/Belcarra Regional Park, run by Metro Vancouver, holds White Pine Beach on Sasamat Lake and the Belcarra picnic area on the inlet, with a 3.2-kilometre Sasamat Lake Loop Trail, and park traffic uses the same Bedwell Bay Road residents use. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/t%C9%99mt%C9%99m%C3%ADx%CA%B7t%C9%99n-belcarra-regional-park
- Fire protection is volunteer: the Sasamat Volunteer Fire Department is the village's fire service named in the Official Community Plan. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2024/10/621-2024-Official-Communitiy-Plan-Bylaw-SCHEDULE-A.pdf
- The Village Hall stands at 4084 Bedwell Bay Road on the single spine road, so every civic service is on the one road in and out. https://belcarra.ca/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Belcarra sets its own property taxes as a separate municipality, and the Village budgets its Waste and Recycling Depot as its own operating fund with its own rate increases, separate from water. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2025/10/Village-of-Belcarra-Draft-2026-2040-Financial-Plan.pdf
- The speculation and vacancy tax applies in Belcarra, so owners declare annually. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Bedwell Bay Road is the only road in and out: it enters from Port Moody and runs past the Village Hall at 4084 Bedwell Bay Road to both the Belcarra Bay and Bedwell Bay street systems. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2026/07/Public-Notice-of-Zoning-Bylaw-Amendment-463-2026-Readings.pdf
- A TransLink community shuttle serves the village, per the Official Community Plan's facilities map — there is no conventional bus route or rail station in Belcarra. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2024/10/621-2024-Official-Communitiy-Plan-Bylaw-SCHEDULE-A.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Test the septic system and confirm its capacity before adding any accessory unit, because the Village ties added units to septic and water capacity and there is no municipal sewer. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2026/07/2.0-2026-07-27-Regular-Council-Meeting-Agenda-Package.pdf
- Confirm whether the specific property is connected to the municipal water system, since the Official Community Plan says the system serves the majority of properties in Belcarra Bay and Bedwell Bay, without a lot list. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2024/10/621-2024-Official-Communitiy-Plan-Bylaw-SCHEDULE-A.pdf
