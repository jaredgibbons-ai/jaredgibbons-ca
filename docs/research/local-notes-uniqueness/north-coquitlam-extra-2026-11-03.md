# North Coquitlam Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: north-coquitlam (North Coquitlam; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- North Coquitlam on this page means the Burke Mountain side of northeast Coquitlam, centred on David Avenue, Coast Meridian Road and Princeton Avenue, under the Northeast Coquitlam Area Plan. https://www.coquitlam.ca/422/Burke-Mountain-Village
- David Avenue is the east-west arterial and Coast Meridian Road and Victoria Drive are the other east-west and north-south links the City's Northwest Burke Vision names for northeast Coquitlam, with Pipeline Road as the western north-south access. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/790/Northwest-Burke-Vision-PDF
- The northern edge is not a street but a park boundary: Pinecone Burke Provincial Park includes Burke Mountain in Coquitlam, and BC Parks states the park is west of Pitt Lake and the Pitt River. https://bcparks.ca/pinecone-burke-park/

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Burke Mountain Village is the planned centre of this housing area: the City states its 39 acres are anticipated to include a range of residential dwellings and approximately 120,000 square feet of retail and commercial space, including a grocery store, a large public plaza and a village greenway, with the final unit count to be determined by the developer or builder. https://www.coquitlam.ca/422/Burke-Mountain-Village
- The wider mountain is planned in named neighbourhoods, not one subdivision: the City's plan mapping names Upper Hyde Creek, Lower Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek and Partington Creek neighbourhood plans under the Northeast Coquitlam Area Plan. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1191/Schedule-H-PDF
- Princeton Park, a 0.7-acre park in the Upper Hyde Creek neighbourhood that opened June 20, 2017, shows the scale of the newer pockets: small neighbourhood parks built with the housing, on Princeton Avenue streets. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Princeton-Park-69

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What is Burke Mountain Village, and is it built? **A:** Burke Mountain Village is the planned social and commercial heart of northeast Coquitlam, on 39 acres with housing, about 120,000 square feet of retail including a grocery store, a plaza and a greenway. The City states the Burke Mountain Community Centre and Burke Village Park there are currently under construction, and the final Village unit count will be determined by its developer or builder. https://www.coquitlam.ca/422/Burke-Mountain-Village
- **Q:** Which schools exist on Burke Mountain now? **A:** The City states there are three active and one planned elementary schools within three kilometres of Burke Mountain Village, and Leigh Elementary at 1230 Soball Street is one of the established Burke Mountain schools in the district listings. Smiling Creek Elementary serves the Smiling Creek neighbourhood. Catchment is still by home address, so confirm a David Avenue or Princeton Avenue address in the School District No. 43 School Locator. https://www.coquitlam.ca/422/Burke-Mountain-Village
- **Q:** How do I get into Pinecone Burke Provincial Park from here? **A:** By car, BC Parks directs drivers north on Coast Meridian Road, east on Harper Road for two kilometres, parking near the District Hunting and Fishing Club at the end of Harper Road. A second access reaches Munro Lake via Victoria Drive, which becomes Quarry Road, for seven kilometres. Those trailheads sit above the Burke Mountain housing streets. https://bcparks.ca/pinecone-burke-park/
- **Q:** Is there a community centre on Burke Mountain yet? **A:** Not open yet. The City states the Burke Mountain Community Centre and Burke Village Park completed detailed design in November 2025, with early site work and construction starting in 2026 and the centre and park expected to open in 2029. A December 2025 open house was held at the Burke Mountain Discovery Centre, 3537 Princeton Avenue. https://www.coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1835
- **Q:** What parks do the newer streets already have? **A:** Princeton Park, 0.7 acres in Upper Hyde Creek, opened in 2017 with nature-themed play elements. Burke Mountain Pioneer Park is a 4.4-acre community park with a community garden, a full-sized sport court with basketball and hockey nets, and trail connections with distance markers. Sheffield Park in Smiling Creek and Galloway Park, with a water spray park, are also named Burke Mountain neighbourhood parks in City documents. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Burke-Mountain-Pioneer-Park-21

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Burke Mountain Athletic Park opened September 25, 2026 beside the future middle and secondary school: a 400-metre lit running track, a full-size artificial turf field and four lit tennis courts, developed with School District No. 43 (Coquitlam). http://www.coquitlam.ca/m/NewsFlash/Home/Detail/2056
- The Burke Mountain Loop 2 trail is a 3.2-kilometre mixed-surface loop using sections of David Avenue, Coast Meridian Road, Highland Drive, Wales Avenue and Sheffield Avenue, with some steep sections. https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Archive/Item/122?arcId=1349
- Galloway Park and Smiling Creek Park both host City little libraries under the Adopt-A-Little Library program, alongside Blue Mountain Park in central Coquitlam. https://www.coquitlam.ca/625
- The community centre site will include a dedicated Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area (SPEA) to protect natural watercourses beside Burke Village Park. http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Archive/Item/1947?arcId=2566
- Higher frequencies of human-caused wildfire ignitions are reported along the Coquitlam River valley in the City's wildfire plan, and Coast Meridian Road at David Avenue and the northeast Burke Mountain area are listed interface locations in that plan. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/7234/Community_Wildfire_Resiliency_Plan_2021-FireSmart_Principles

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Coquitlam's 2026 Class 1 Residential municipal rate is $2.2756 per $1,000 of assessed value, in full with utility charges in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026 and utility bills March 31, 2026. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17264/2026_Tax-Rates
- Coquitlam is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so a North Coquitlam owner must complete the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- The Burke-specific cost check is servicing on a growing mountain: the Cedar Drive Upgrade Project, supporting development in northeast Coquitlam, is in full in the river-springs-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note, and a buyer of a new Princeton Avenue home should confirm which road and drainage works are complete for that street. https://www.coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/home/detail/1921

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 191, Coquitlam Central Station to Princeton, is the Burke Mountain bus: TransLink describes it as connecting Burke Mountain residents to the Evergreen Extension and West Coast Express at Coquitlam Central Station, via David Avenue and Princeton Avenue. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2016/12/bus-route-buzz-new-service-191-coquitlam-central-stationprinceton/
- Route 186 runs David Avenue via Pinetree Way, Pipeline Road and Robson Drive, and route 160 runs Victoria Drive and Cedar Drive on the area's east side, per their TransLink timetables. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt186.pdf
- No SkyTrain station is on Burke Mountain; the station connection is Coquitlam Central Station, southwest across the Coquitlam River. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2016/12/bus-route-buzz-new-service-191-coquitlam-central-stationprinceton/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the Interface Wildfire Risk Management Development Permit Area and the Community Wildfire Resiliency Plan 2021 mapping for any lot backing onto forest: the plan lists Coast Meridian Road at David Avenue and the northeast Burke Mountain interface, with continuous forest in Pinecone Burke Provincial Park above the housing. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/7234/Community_Wildfire_Resiliency_Plan_2021-FireSmart_Principles
- Check the 50-metre Northeast watercourse permit distance on Hyde Creek, Partington Creek and Smiling Creek lots, and whether a Riparian Areas Protection Regulation assessment is triggered within 30 metres of a stream. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- Check slope on the City's Schedule B environmental mapping, which maps unstable slopes for the Northeast area; the map shows boundaries, not a geotechnical conclusion for a David Avenue lot. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
