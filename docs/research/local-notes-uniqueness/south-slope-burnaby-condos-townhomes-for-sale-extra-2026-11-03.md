## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The strata stock here is low-rise and mid-rise apartment buildings and townhome complexes on the Nelson Avenue, Patterson Avenue, Sussex Avenue and Station Hill Drive corridors, in freehold strata form. Strata property in British Columbia is governed by the Strata Property Act, and most Burnaby apartment and townhome complexes are freehold strata, where each home is a strata lot on a registered strata plan. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- No named South Slope building with a street address and year built was confirmed on a municipal or strata source in this pass. Building names and years circulating on listing sites were not used — see NOT CONFIRMED.
- Newer ground-oriented strata is the R1 rowhouse form on former house lots. Burnaby's R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District permits rowhouses and townhouses on house lots, with fee-simple rowhouse lots requiring 5 to 8 metres of width depending on lot type. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- Rental and pet rules are building-specific here, not area rules. No South Slope strata page stating a rental restriction, pet rule or age rule was read in this pass, so read the registered bylaws and the Form B Information Certificate for the specific building before offering.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A secondary suite inside a South Slope townhome cannot be sold separately. Burnaby's zoning rewrite confirms that dwelling units classed as secondary suites must stay registered under the same title as the primary unit they sit in, and the strata conversion of existing purpose-built rental housing is not permitted except for duplex or semi-detached units — see the vancouver-heights-burnaby-houses-for-sale note for the city-wide position. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- A strata lot within 30 metres of Byrne Creek or a slope ravine can carry a streamside condition. The City requires an assessment for a property within 30 metres of a stream, ravine or wetland to identify the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area (SPEA) protected from new building and land disturbance. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/development-permits-construction/new-home-construction
- Strata disputes on the slope go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal (CRT). The Province routes many strata bylaw, fee and governance disputes to the CRT under the strata housing framework. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a South Slope townhome on Nelson Avenue? **A:** Check the exact address in the School District 41 (SD41) locator first. Nelson Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 7, is at 4850 Irmin Street, South Slope Elementary is at 4446 Watling Street, and Burnaby South Secondary, Grade 8 to Grade 12, is at 5455 Rumble Street. The district groups Nelson and South Slope elementaries with Burnaby South Secondary in its Kingsway South zone. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** How do I reach SkyTrain from a Station Hill Drive condo? **A:** There is no station on Station Hill Drive itself. The slope's stations are Patterson Station and Royal Oak Station on the Expo Line, north of Rumble Street, and TransLink buses on Nelson Avenue and Patterson Avenue connect the strata corridors to those stations and to Metrotown, one station west of Patterson. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- **Q:** What utility bill does a strata home here pay? **A:** A multiple-family dwelling pays its own Burnaby utility rates. The City's 2026 residential table charges a multiple-family dwelling $374.54 for water and $436.00 for sewer use, with a 5% discount if the notice is paid by the due date, separate from the strata fees the building collects. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Q:** Can the strata restrict rentals or pets in my building? **A:** Yes, if its registered bylaws say so. Rental, pet and age rules in Burnaby strata buildings come from each strata corporation's own bylaws under the Strata Property Act, not from a City rule, and no South Slope building's bylaws were read in this pass. Ask for the current bylaws and Form B for the specific building on Station Hill Drive, Nelson Avenue or Patterson Avenue before writing an offer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Q:** Is a townhome on the slope freehold or strata? **A:** Most are freehold strata, and a newer rowhouse can be fee-simple. Burnaby's R1 district allows fee-simple rowhouse lots, whose homes are not strata lots, alongside strata townhome complexes — the title and any strata plan number on the listing tell you which form a specific South Slope home is. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- South Slope Elementary shares its Watling Street site with the B.C. Provincial School for the Deaf. Heritage Burnaby records that South Slope / B.C. School for the Deaf opened in 1992 at 4446 Watling Street and replaced the former Jericho Hill School for the Deaf in Vancouver. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark843
- Nelson Elementary, at 4850 Irmin Street, is the in-area Kindergarten to Grade 7 school for the Nelson Avenue corridor buildings. SD41 lists Nelson Elementary at that Irmin Street address on its provincial school list. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- The Michael J. Fox Theatre sits on the Burnaby South Secondary campus at 5455 Rumble Street. The secondary school serving the slope's strata homes is also the local community theatre site, north of the Station Hill Drive buildings. https://south.burnabyschools.ca/
- A strata home's sewer parcel tax is shared across its parcel. The City notes the sewer parcel tax is billed with property taxes in May and is shared between the number of properties on each parcel of land, and it moves to the utility notice in 2027. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 8. GETTING AROUND

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the Byrne Creek SPEA before buying a ravine-edge strata home. The Burnaby 2050 OCP's Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area guidelines set how the protected area is measured on a parcel, which affects common-property repairs and any building addition. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-06/Streamside-Protection-and-Enhancement-Development-Permit-Area-Guidelines.pdf
- Check the strata's registered bylaws and Form B, not the listing, for rental and pet rules. The Province's strata framework puts those rules in each corporation's bylaws, and the Civil Resolution Tribunal resolves many disputes over them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- Check slope and title constraints on BurnabyMap and through the Land Title and Survey Authority of British Columbia (LTSA). The City's new-home page directs buyers to title records for easements and rights-of-way, which on a slope lot can include drainage and utility corridors. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/development-permits-construction/new-home-construction
