## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Rentals and pets are set by each strata's filed bylaws, not by the City; the Province directs buyers to the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- Buildings need a form and character permit in Development Permit Area 8 (Residential), per the City's December 2025 guide. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3279/2025%5F12%5FDevelopment%5FPermit%5FBrochure%5F%2D%5FDP%5F6%2D10%2Epdf
- Sardis sites sit on the Vedder River fan, covered by an OCP permit area, so flood design forms part of approvals. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1372

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Are Chilliwack townhomes strata or freehold? **A:** Treat them as strata unless title documents say otherwise. R4 and CD-9 townhome development is built as strata complexes, so provincial strata guidance on bylaws, fees and levies applies. The legal check is the strata plan and Form B for the specific complex, which show its rental and age bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Q:** Where are most townhomes? **A:** On the Sardis side: the Villas at Sardis Park at 45819 Stevenson Road, Iron Horse rowhomes at 46211 Promontory Road, and Garrison Crossing forms under CD-9, all named on City or developer records. North of the river, supply is smaller infill, such as 9119 Broadway Street. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2300/DRAC%5FMinutes%5F%2D%5FJune%5F10%5F%2D%5FSIGNED.pdf
- **Q:** Can I rent out a townhome? **A:** Only if the strata's bylaws allow it. Provincial guidance states strata corporations set rental bylaws, summarized in the unit's Form B, so ask for current bylaws before offering. Purpose-built rentals differ: at York Residences, Campus Drive, the operator sets terms itself and states pets are not allowed. https://www.diverserentals.com/rentals/york
- **Q:** Do Sardis townhomes have flood rules? **A:** At building stage, yes. Sardis sits on the Vedder River alluvial fan, and Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519 sets a 75-metre setback from the Vedder River and floors at flood construction level plus 0.6 metres. For existing complexes, check the depreciation report and strata insurance. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City-wide 2026 rates and charges: see chilliwack-houses-for-sale note.
- Strata fees and levies are set by each strata corporation; no official page states a fee for any Chilliwack complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
