# West End (New Westminster) — Questions for Jared

5 questions only Jared can answer:

1. In West End deals you have handled, what do buyers actually pay a premium for — a rear laneway, a view down the slope to the Fraser River, or infill potential under the new six-unit program — and what do they discount?
2. Who is buying West End houses right now: young families coming from condos in New Westminster or Burnaby, downsizers, or infill builders?
3. What sells fast in the West End and what sits — do character houses from the early 1900s move quickly while plain post-war houses wait, or is it the other way around?
4. What surprises buyers most after they move to the West End — how steep the slope streets feel, Twelfth Street traffic, or the lack of a SkyTrain station in the neighbourhood?
5. Tell one West End story: a deal, a showing, or a house on a named street that taught you something about this neighbourhood that no official page would say.
