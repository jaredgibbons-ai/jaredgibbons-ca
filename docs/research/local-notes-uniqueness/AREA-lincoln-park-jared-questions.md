# Lincoln Park — Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer, for a voice memo. Muse does not answer these.

1. Who buys houses in Lincoln Park — Port Coquitlam families trading up from Mary Hill, or buyers leaving Coquitlam proper for PoCo's smaller-city feel?
2. What sells fast and what sits — the Hyde Creek–side homes, or the older stock along Fraser Avenue?
3. What surprises buyers most: how much of the neighbourhood borders the Hyde Creek nature reserve, flood-watch closures at Birchland Park, or PoCo's suite and SSMUH rules in practice?
4. How do Lincoln Park buyers compare it against Mary Hill below and Citadel Heights above?
5. Tell one story from a Lincoln Park deal — a creek-side surprise in the disclosure, a family outbidding for the school catchment, or a long-held 1960s home selling.
