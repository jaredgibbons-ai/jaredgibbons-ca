# Capilano Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: capilano-nv (Capilano, District of North Vancouver; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Capilano here is the Capilano Road corridor in the District of North Vancouver's west end, between the Upper Levels Highway (Highway 1) and the Cleveland Dam, around the Capilano River canyon. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- District and school sources split the name: Upper Capilano is the plateau north of Highway 1, while Lower Capilano / Lions Gate is the Marine Drive end south of the highway — this page is the corridor itself, not the southern village lands. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Confirmed anchors are Capilano Suspension Bridge Park at 3735 Capilano Road and the Cleveland Dam at the corridor's north end in Capilano River Regional Park; exact boundary streets were not confirmed on a District page read on 2026-11-03. https://www.capbridge.com/faq/
- Across the Capilano River is West Vancouver's Park Royal / Ambleside side; east are Canyon Heights and Edgemont Village across the creek ravines. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Grouse Mountain's address is 6400 Nancy Greene Way at the top of Capilano Road, and Capilano Road is the only road up to it. https://www.grousemountain.com/contact

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The corridor stock is detached houses under the District's R1 and R2 detached residential zones, on canyon-side streets off Capilano Road and plateau streets toward Montroyal Boulevard. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- Zone minimum lots run from R2(S1) at 335 m² with 10 m width to R1 and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² with 30 m width; standard R2 is 660 m², 18 m width, 34 m depth. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- No named condo building or townhome complex on the corridor was confirmed on an official or developer source read on 2026-11-03; the corridor reads as freehold detached. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- A principal house plus a secondary suite plus a coach house — three units total — has been permitted on single-family zoned lots within the Urban Containment Boundary since June 2024. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- The age and style profile of the stock is not stated on an official source read, so no build era is claimed. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Capilano-specific rules start with the canyon: District Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets Streamside Protection, Creek Hazard, Slope Hazard and Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Areas that canyon-edge and ravine lots here fall under. https://dnv-docs.simplicitycms.ca/documents/ocp-schedule-b-dpa.pdf
- The Capilano watershed above the Cleveland Dam is closed to the public to protect drinking water, and the reservoirs and water supply areas are closed except for registered tours — upper-corridor lots sit below a protected reservoir, not a recreation lake. https://metrovancouver.org/services/water/watersheds-reservoirs
- Under Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525, an R2 principal building may include a secondary suite, and a coach house is a dwelling unit accessory to and detached from the principal dwelling. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- The District's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- District-wide zoning detail, taxes and utilities are treated in full — see the edgemont-55-plus-homes-for-sale note.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Capilano Road address? **A:** School District 44 (SD44) catchments are set by exact address, and SD44 has elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 7) and secondary (Grades 8–12) schools only, with no middle tier. The plateau elementaries beside the corridor are Canyon Heights at 4501 Highland Boulevard, Highlands at 3150 Colwood Drive and Cleveland at 1255 Eldon Road, with Handsworth Secondary at 1033 Handsworth Road. Confirm the exact address with SD44. https://www.sd44.ca/school/canyonheights/Pages/default.aspx
- **Q:** Is Capilano Elementary our school? **A:** Do not assume so — Capilano Elementary is at 1230 West 20th Street in the City of North Vancouver lowlands, south of this corridor, despite carrying the Capilano name. Corridor addresses sit beside the Canyon Heights, Highlands and Cleveland elementary areas instead. A catchment is only settled by running the exact address with School District 44. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044
- **Q:** What runs on Capilano Road for transit? **A:** TransLink route 236 runs the length of Capilano Road toward Grouse Mountain and Lonsdale Quay, route 232 runs via Capilano Road at the corridor's east side, and route 246 crosses the corridor via Capilano Road and Montroyal Boulevard with downtown trips over the Lions Gate Bridge. The SeaBus at Lonsdale Quay crosses to Waterfront Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt236.pdf
- **Q:** Can I swim or boat on Capilano Lake? **A:** No — the Capilano Reservoir behind the Cleveland Dam is a drinking-water supply area, and Metro Vancouver states the reservoirs and water supply areas are closed to the public except for registered tours. Visitors can view the reservoir from the Cleveland Dam at the north end of Capilano River Regional Park. Recreation here is the park trails below the dam, not the lake. https://metrovancouver.org/services/water/watersheds-reservoirs
- **Q:** What is the dam at the top of the road? **A:** The Cleveland Dam, completed in 1954 and 91 metres high, holds back Capilano Lake, the drinking-water reservoir above the corridor, and North Vancouver's Capilano Watershed supplies a third of the region's drinking water per Metro Vancouver. Homes on the upper corridor sit downstream of that dam, which is why the District's creek and slope hazard checks in Section 9 matter. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/capilano-river-regional-park
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Capilano River Regional Park, run by Metro Vancouver, follows the river through and above the corridor, with the Capilano Pacific Trail and the Coho Loop Trail. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/capilano-river-regional-park
- Fisheries and Oceans Canada's Capilano River Hatchery, in the regional park below the dam, was completed in 1971 and normally draws about 250,000 visitors a year for the coho and chinook runs. https://www.pac.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/sep-pmvs/hatcheries-ecloseries/capilano-eng.html
- Capilano Suspension Bridge Park at 3735 Capilano Road is a private attraction on the corridor, separate from the free regional park and dam. https://www.capbridge.com/faq/
- All Grouse Mountain and suspension-bridge visitor traffic travels Capilano Road past corridor homes, because Capilano Road is the only road to Grouse Mountain. https://www.grousemountain.com/contact
- Capilano Library at 3045 Highland Boulevard is described by North Vancouver District Public Library as in the heart of Edgemont Village, east of the corridor. https://www.nvdpl.ca/capilano-library
- Delbrook Community Recreation Centre at 851 West Queens Road is the nearest North Vancouver Recreation Commission (NVRC) full-service centre, south-east of the corridor. https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/courts
- Lions Gate Hospital at 231 East 15th Street and the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 221 West Esplanade serve the area. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital
- Secondary French Immersion is offered at Handsworth Secondary in SD44. https://handsworth.sd44.ca/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- District-wide tax and utility treatment is in full — see the edgemont-55-plus-homes-for-sale note. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- The figures that apply to corridor lots: the District's 2026 total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value (municipal portion 1.83419), with taxes due July 2, 2026. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- District single-family utility fees in 2026 include water at $932.80 and sewer / drainage at $1,255.60, with combined single-family utilities of $2,571.20. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- The District of North Vancouver is a listed Speculation and Vacancy Tax taxable area, so owners here file the annual provincial declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Capilano Road is the spine — south to Marine Drive and the Lions Gate Bridge, north to the Cleveland Dam and Grouse Mountain — with the Highway 1 interchange at Capilano Road as the highway access. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt236.pdf
- Route 236 runs Capilano Road between Grouse Mountain and Lonsdale Quay, and route 232 links Grouse Mountain and Phibbs Exchange via the corridor's east side. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt236.pdf
- Route 246 (Downtown / Highland) runs via Marine Drive, Capilano Road, Ridgewood Drive, Edgemont Boulevard, Highland Boulevard and Montroyal Boulevard. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt246.pdf
- The SeaBus at Lonsdale Quay crosses to Waterfront Station in 12 minutes, every 15 minutes during the day, per TransLink timetable material. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt246.pdf
- Drive times to downtown Vancouver and Vancouver International Airport (YVR) are UNCONFIRMED — no official source read states them. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt236.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Run the District GeoWeb hazard layers before pricing any canyon-edge renovation or rebuild. https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- OCP Schedule B sets seven Development Permit Areas, including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard. https://dnv-docs.simplicitycms.ca/documents/ocp-schedule-b-dpa.pdf
- The District publishes property-level GeoWeb reports including modelled flood construction levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- River levels below the Cleveland Dam can change with dam releases, so treat the regional park river edge as a hazard check, not just an amenity, for lower corridor lots. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/capilano-river-regional-park
- Confirm the exact zone through District GeoWeb, because corridor minimum lots range from 335 m² to 4,000 m² by sub-zone. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
