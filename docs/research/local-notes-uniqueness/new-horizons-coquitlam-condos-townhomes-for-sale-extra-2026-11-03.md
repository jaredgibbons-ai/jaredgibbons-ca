# New Horizons Condos and Townhomes — Extra Local Notes
Page: new-horizons-coquitlam-condos-townhomes-for-sale (New Horizons; condos and townhomes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- New Horizons is a small house pocket in east-central Coquitlam whose centre streets are Nestor Street, Harwood Avenue, Toba Drive and Sechelt Drive, with Mariner Way as the through road at its west side; the City publishes no boundary for this board sub-area name, so treat street names, not a map line, as the check. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- The pocket's addressed anchor is École Nestor Elementary at 1266 Nestor Street, the School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) school physically inside the street pattern the houses and attached homes front. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- Mariner Way separates this pocket from the Ranch Park streets to the south-west, and the same Mariner Way corridor carries the buses that serve both pockets. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt152.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is actually inside New Horizons? **A:** École Nestor Elementary, at 1266 Nestor Street, is the School District No. 43 school inside the pocket, listed for kindergarten to Grade 5. The École in its name is the district's own listing, and attendance is assigned by permanent home address rather than by the neighbourhood name, so run the specific address in the SD43 School Locator before relying on Nestor for a Harwood Avenue or Toba Drive home. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** Are there named townhouse complexes to compare in New Horizons? **A:** None could be confirmed from an official source in this pass. No municipal, strata or developer page read names a condo or townhouse complex on Nestor Street, Harwood Avenue, Toba Drive or Sechelt Drive, so attached homes here are verified lot by lot — by civic address, strata plan if one exists, and title — rather than by complex name, amenities list or a shared depreciation report. https://coquitlam.ca/889
- **Q:** How do residents here reach SkyTrain without a car? **A:** By bus down Mariner Way to Coquitlam Central Station. TransLink route 152 runs from Coquitlam Central Station via Lougheed Highway, Dewdney Trunk Road and Mariner Way, and route 153 runs from Braid Station via Como Lake Avenue and Mariner Way to Coquitlam Central Station, where the Millennium Line Evergreen Extension and the West Coast Express stop. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt152.pdf
- **Q:** Can a New Horizons house lot be turned into several attached homes? **A:** Possibly, under the small-scale rules adopted June 9, 2025 and set out in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. The City flags three early checks before designing: a creek on or beside the lot, a steep slope, or a house built before 1945. A multi-unit conversion also needs the City's fire flow form filed with the building permit application, confirming water supply for the lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Q:** Which library branch serves this pocket? **A:** Coquitlam Public Library runs two branches, and the City Centre Branch at 1169 Pinetree Way is the branch on the City Centre side of the city that this pocket's Mariner Way buses run toward; the other branch is Poirier at 575 Poirier Street. Branch programs and hours are the library's own listings, so check them there rather than assuming a neighbourhood branch exists. https://www.coqlibrary.ca/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The pocket's school is a French-language-named district school: the Ministry's school list records it as École Nestor Elementary, kindergarten to Grade 5, at 1266 Nestor Street — a detail a buyer only sees on the district or Ministry listing, not on the street sign. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- Mariner Way is a scheduled bus corridor on this pocket's edge, carrying routes 152 and 153, while Nestor Street, Harwood Avenue and Toba Drive themselves carry no bus route in the timetables read — bus noise is a Mariner Way-fronting check only. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt153.pdf
- Integrated stormwater management applies to small-scale residential projects in Coquitlam, to manage runoff from roofs and driveways and protect local creeks — relevant to any New Horizons lot adding hard surface in a multi-unit conversion. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- The City classifies its creeks for environmental value and monitors flow, level and water quality on various creeks, so a lot draining toward a local creek corridor carries a monitoring and setback context even where no development permit area is mapped on the lot itself. https://coquitlam.ca/217/Creeks-and-Streams

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 152, Coquitlam Central Station to Lougheed Station, runs Mariner Way at the pocket's west side via Dewdney Trunk Road, with some trips via Chilko Drive, then continues via Hickey Drive and Austin Avenue. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt152.pdf
- Route 153, Coquitlam Central Station to Braid Station, runs Mariner Way via Como Lake Avenue, Laurentian Crescent, Austin Avenue and Poirier Street, linking the pocket to New Westminster's Braid Station as well as Coquitlam Central. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt153.pdf
- The rail connection for both routes is Coquitlam Central Station, served by the Millennium Line Evergreen Extension and the West Coast Express. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt153.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the City's environmental development permit mapping, Schedule B, for slope and interface boundaries before assuming a Nestor Street lot is unconstrained; the map shows the layers, not a lot-level geotechnical conclusion. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- Check Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 section 519 flood and slope siting rules and Schedule G, the designated 200-year floodplain of the Brunette, Coquitlam, Fraser and Pitt rivers, even though this pocket sits upland of those rivers. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1170/Part-5-General-Regulations-PDF
- Check the City's floodplain mapping work, which covers the Coquitlam, Fraser and Pitt river floodplains, against the specific lot rather than the neighbourhood name before purchase. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/182/Coquitlam-Fraser-and-Pitt-Rivers-Floodplain-Mapping-Report-PDF
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status per parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission mapping; no ALR land was identified in this part of Coquitlam in the sources read, which is not a parcel clearance. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
