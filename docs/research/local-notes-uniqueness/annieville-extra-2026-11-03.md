# Annieville Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: annieville (Annieville, North Delta; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Annieville is the north-west North Delta neighbourhood on the Fraser River frontage in the City of Delta, with River Road as its frontage street and 116 Street at its east side. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for Annieville; the mapped boundary comes from the former North Delta Area Plan, Schedule C.1 under the previous Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 3950. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- Nordel sits east and south-east, the Tilbury industrial lands run west along River Road, and the Fraser River closes the north side. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations
- Shared City of Delta municipal facts are held in the earliest Delta note — see the ladner-houses-for-sale note; shared Annieville facts are in the annieville-north-delta-houses-for-sale and annieville-guide notes. This note is street-level only.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is predominantly single detached houses, with duplex product appearing through rezonings on the River Road side. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- Lot sizes split sharply by zone: river-side RD3 (Duplex/Single Detached Residential 3) and RS1 lots carry 4,000 square metre minimums, while an RS6 lot after subdivision carries a 390 square metre minimum at 13 metres wide and 30 metres deep. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS6.pdf
- The former North Delta Area Plan identified residential infill east of 116 Street between 90 Avenue and 96 Avenue, at Annieville's east seam. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Two Development Permit Areas shape this neighbourhood in the Delta OCP 2024, Bylaw No. 8400: ND3 covers the Annieville village on River Road, and ND1, the Fraser River Escarpment area, covers the river frontage. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The current OCP, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated the former area plans into a single Land Use Plan; the North Delta Area Plan survives only as the previous plan's Schedule C.1 under Bylaw No. 3950 — check current. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 applies parcel by parcel; the zone ladder read for this area runs from RS1 and RD3 at 4,000 square metres down through RS6 at 390 and RS7 at 335 square metres, and there is no RD5 zone. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS6.pdf
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) in Delta allows combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the bylaw applies. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- A worked example of the infill pattern: the City's file for 10604 River Road proposed rezoning from RD3 to RS6 (Single Detached Residential 6) for a two-lot subdivision under Bylaw No. 7600. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/224579/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve an Annieville address? **A:** It depends on the address. For the sample address 9240 112 Street, the School District No. 37 (Delta) School Locator result is Annieville Elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 7), at that same address, and Delview Secondary (Grades 8 to 12) at 9111 116 Street. The district has no middle-school tier in its locator, and east-side addresses can fall to other schools, so run the exact address. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q:** Is the Fraser River frontage a hazard area? **A:** Yes, on the frontage itself. The ND1 Fraser River Escarpment Development Permit Area covers it, and the OCP lists mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence for that area, with river bank and ravine slopes subject to erosion. A geotechnical review belongs in the subject list for a River Road or slope-side lot, not after removal. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q:** Can a big river-side lot be subdivided? **A:** Sometimes, through rezoning rather than by right. The City's own 10604 River Road file used an RD3-to-RS6 rezoning to create a two-lot subdivision, because the standing RD3 minimum is 4,000 square metres while RS6 allows lots down to 390 square metres. Price a large parcel on its rezoning and servicing answer, not on the house alone. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/224579/
- **Q:** How do buses reach SkyTrain from here? **A:** Two routes terminate at Scott Road Station on the Expo Line. Route 312 runs via Scott Road, 96 Avenue, 114 Street, 92A Avenue, 112 Street and 72 Avenue to Scottsdale Exchange, and route 391 runs via 72 Avenue, 116 Street and 96 Avenue. The 112 Street and 116 Street legs are the Annieville-side segments of each route. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt312.pdf
- **Q:** Where is the nearest library and recreation centre? **A:** Neither sits inside Annieville itself. George Mackie Library, a Fraser Valley Regional Library branch, is at 8440 112th Street just east in Nordel, and the North Delta Recreation Centre at 11415 84 Avenue serves North Delta from Nordel. Annieville's own indoor anchor is its elementary school and Lions Park beside it. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check ND1 first on any frontage lot: the OCP's Fraser River Escarpment area lists mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence, and the City requires geotechnical work under its slope policy. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Confirm the parcel's Development Permit Area status and constraints on DeltaMap; the City states the map is for convenience and requirements should be confirmed with its Application Centre. https://www.delta.ca:443/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
- Riparian and streamside work near the river and its creeks can engage the federal Fisheries Act, the provincial Water Sustainability Act and the Riparian Areas Regulation as well as Delta's bylaws. https://www.delta.ca:443/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
- Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status inside Annieville was not confirmed in the sources read; it is a property-specific check through the Agricultural Land Commission. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Read the title through the Land Title and Survey Authority of British Columbia: the City states title charges can affect a property's use or redevelopment. https://www.delta.ca:443/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
