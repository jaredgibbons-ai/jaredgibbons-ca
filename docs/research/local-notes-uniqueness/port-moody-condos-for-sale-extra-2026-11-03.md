# Port Moody Condos — Extra Local Notes

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Port Moody's condo stock sits in two station areas, not spread across the city: Suter Brook and Newport Village by Inlet Centre Station on Ioco Road, Klahanie along Klahanie Drive, and the St. Johns Street shelf by Moody Centre Station. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/y10lcutj/port-moody-official-community-plan-bylaw-2026-no-3522.pdf
- City-wide boundaries, plan history and tax background are held in full in the heritage-mountain-port-moody-houses-for-sale note; this note covers only the condo stock (see the heritage-mountain-port-moody-houses-for-sale note).
- The two SkyTrain stations that organise the stock are Moody Centre Station at 65 Williams Street and Inlet Centre Station on Ioco Road, with St. Johns Street and Clarke Street as the east–west streets below them. https://maps.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/system-maps/fwc26_regional_transit_connections.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The newest tower stock is at Suter Brook Village: The Grande, by the Onni Group, at 300 Morrissey Road (units also addressed 305 and 308 Morrissey Road), described by the developer as the final offering in Suter Brook Village, with 1-, 2- and 3-bedroom homes. https://onni.com/thegrande/
- Klahanie is a Polygon masterplan on Klahanie Drive mixing apartments and townhomes; its Boardwalk apartment development is at 600 and 700 Klahanie Drive, beside the Canoe Club amenity facility. https://polyhomes.com/masterplan/klahanie/ https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/boardwalk-700-klahanie-drive
- The Moody Centre shelf's named strata is Platform by Aragon at 83 Moody Street, at Moody Street and Clarke Street; Aragon's own communities page lists Platform as its Port Moody project, and the development mixes apartment and townhouse forms at one address. https://aragon.ca/ourcommunities/
- Port Moody condos are freehold strata, not freehold houses: a buyer owns a strata lot and a share of common property, and the Form B (Section 3) is the document that states the strata's position. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Condo zoning is set parcel by parcel under Port Moody Zoning Bylaw, 2018, No. 2937; multi-residential buildings sit in the bylaw's multi-residential and mixed-use zones, checked on the City ViewPort map. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- No Port Moody strata can bar long-term rentals: rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since November 24, 2022, whatever an older building's bylaw package or a directory line still says. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- The only age bylaw a Port Moody strata may enforce is 55-or-older, under sections 123.1 and 123.2 of the Strata Property Act; no building named in Section 2 is stated by a source read to carry one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Stratas with five or more lots in Port Moody must hold a depreciation report renewed every five years since July 1, 2024, with no deferral vote — the report is where a Klahanie or Suter Brook buyer checks planned envelope and elevator work. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which Port Moody condos are newest? **A:** The Grande at 300 Morrissey Road in Suter Brook Village is the developer-described final offering in that village, by the Onni Group, above the village shops by Inlet Centre Station. Older Klahanie and Moody Centre buildings trade beside it, so compare a Suter Brook resale against the depreciation report and contingency reserve fund (CRF) position of its own strata, not against the newest tower. https://onni.com/thegrande/
- **Q:** Is Klahanie all townhomes? **A:** No. Polygon's Klahanie masterplan on Klahanie Drive includes apartment buildings as well as townhomes; Boardwalk, at 600 and 700 Klahanie Drive, is the apartment development, with residents' access to the Canoe Club amenity facility. A Klahanie listing's form — apartment or townhouse — changes the strata plan, the parking and the insurance a buyer reviews, so read the form on the listing against the strata plan. https://polyhomes.com/masterplan/klahanie/
- **Q:** Can I rent out a Port Moody condo? **A:** Yes, long-term. Since November 24, 2022 no British Columbia strata may enforce a rental-restriction bylaw, so an older Port Moody bylaw package that still prints a rental cap does not bind a new owner. Short-term rentals are different: a strata bylaw can still restrict them, and the registered bylaws attached to the Form B are the document that answers that question for a specific building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q:** Which station serves a Suter Brook condo? **A:** Inlet Centre Station, on Ioco Road, is the SkyTrain station for Suter Brook and Newport Village; Moody Centre Station, at Williams Street, serves the St. Johns Street shelf and also carries the West Coast Express. Both are Millennium Line stations on TransLink's regional map. Travel time from a specific building to either station is UNCONFIRMED — no measured time was taken for this note. https://maps.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/system-maps/fwc26_regional_transit_connections.pdf
- **Q:** Does a Moody Centre condo sit in the heritage area? **A:** The Moody Centre heritage conservation area covers the older streets around Clarke Street and Murray Street, where a heritage alteration permit can be required for construction or reconstruction; the strata shelf itself is not the heritage stock, but a Platform-area buyer should still check the property's designation on the City's development pages. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Port Moody's current Official Community Plan (OCP) is Bylaw, 2026, No. 3522, adopted April 28, 2026; it repealed Bylaw No. 2955, which older condo disclosure packages still cite. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/y10lcutj/port-moody-official-community-plan-bylaw-2026-no-3522.pdf
- "50 Electronic" at 55 Klahanie Drive is an address brand, not an age rule: the "50" is 50 Electronic Avenue and "Club 50" is its amenity brand, and no source read states a 50-plus or 55-plus bylaw for it. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/y10lcutj/port-moody-official-community-plan-bylaw-2026-no-3522.pdf
- The public library for every Port Moody condo area is the single Port Moody Public Library at 100 Newport Drive in Newport Village. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Eagle Ridge Hospital, the Tri-Cities community hospital, is inside Port Moody at 475 Guildford Way, east of the condo core. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- School catchments here do not follow village lines: School District No. 43 (SD43) assigns by home address, and a locator run for 100 Newport Drive returned Mountain Meadows Elementary, not the central Moody Elementary a Newport buyer would assume. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Port Moody's 2026 municipal residential general tax rate is 1.61574 dollars per $1,000 of taxable value, under the Annual Property Tax Rates Bylaw, 2026, No. 3541 (full city breakdown in the heritage-mountain-port-moody-houses-for-sale note). http://www.portmoody.ca/media/tiabceqp/2026-mill-rates.pdf
- City utilities are a separate bill from property tax: the 2026 utility bill was due February 27, 2026, while property taxes were due July 2, 2026 — a condo buyer budgets both, on top of strata fees. https://www.portmoody.ca/utilities https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/property-taxes/
- Port Moody is a Speculation and Vacancy Tax taxable area in the Metro Vancouver Regional District, so every residential condo owner must file the provincial declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Moody Centre Station combines Millennium Line SkyTrain, West Coast Express commuter rail and a bus exchange at Williams Street — the only Port Moody station with both rail services. https://maps.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/system-maps/fwc26_regional_transit_connections.pdf
- Inlet Centre Station, on Ioco Road, is the SkyTrain-only station serving the Suter Brook, Newport Village and Klahanie condo stock. https://maps.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/system-maps/fwc26_regional_transit_connections.pdf
- Route 160 links Moody Centre Station with Port Coquitlam Station along St. Johns Street and the Barnet Highway, on TransLink's regional transit map. https://maps.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/system-maps/fwc26_regional_transit_connections.pdf
- The West Coast Express runs weekdays only, westbound in the morning and eastbound in the afternoon, between Waterfront and Mission City through Moody Centre — a condo buyer commuting outside those windows is on SkyTrain or bus. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Creek and slope risk is mapped, not guessed: check Development Permit Area 4 (Environmentally Sensitive Areas) and Area 5 (Hazardous Lands and Steep Slopes) on the City ViewPort Environmentally Sensitive Areas and Watercourses layers before buying below the Heritage Mountain slope or beside Noons Creek. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/environmental-protection-and-requirements/
- The OCP Natural Environment chapter names flooding, debris flows, erosion, landslip and rockfalls on its hazard maps (OCP Maps 14 and 16) and requires geotechnical investigation on steep slopes before clearing. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/lmwddork/chapter-5-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670724
- Rail noise is a Moody Centre shelf item: the Canadian Pacific Railway tracks run between St. Johns Street and the inlet at Moody Centre Station, and no City page read states a noise standard for a specific condo building — check the unit's orientation and the strata minutes. https://maps.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/system-maps/fwc26_regional_transit_connections.pdf
- Forest-interface wildfire risk on the city's north-shore slope is managed under the City Community Wildfire Protection Plan, and the City hazards page points owners to FireSmart Canada. https://www.portmoody.ca/resident-services/hazards/
