# Mission West Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: mission-west-mission-guide (Mission West; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Mission West here means the western part of the City of Mission, Fraser Valley Regional District, west of Hatzic toward the Stave River: Silverdale, Silverhill, Stave Falls' southern approaches and the Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) corridor west of the town core. Hatzic-wide facts are in full in the hatzic note — see the hatzic-55-plus-homes-for-sale note.
- The City's Silverdale Comprehensive Planning Area (SCPA) covers 1,392.16 hectares (3,440.10 acres) in southwest Mission. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/silverdale-mis-environmental
- Mission's new Official Community Plan (OCP) is Bylaw 6350-2025, replacing OCP Bylaw 5670-2017. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/legislated-referral-consultation
Read: 2026-11-03

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Existing stock in Mission West proper is rural and suburban single-detached homes on large lots, including Rural 16 Zone (RU16) parcels such as 30748 Silverhill Avenue, at about 1.95 hectares (4.82 acres). https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rsd25-5
- Silverdale is designated for future urban growth, not yet built out: the SCPA Master Infrastructure Strategy (MIS) was done because the area lacks urban servicing (streets, water, sanitary and storm). https://www.mission.ca/media/file/silverdale-mis-servicing-roads-drainage
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Zoning is under City of Mission Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020; a secondary detached unit in RU16 requires rezoning to the secondary-dwelling form, e.g. RU16 to RU16s at 30748 Silverhill Avenue to permit a coach house. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rsd25-5
- That Silverhill proposal was stated as consistent with the OCP, inside the urban growth boundary, and in the Silverdale Comprehensive Planning Area. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rsd25-5
- The 2025 OCP referral package was sent to the Agricultural Land Commission (ALC), confirming Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) land is part of the planning picture in Mission — check the ALC map for a specific lot. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/legislated-referral-consultation
Read: 2026-11-03

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Where does Mission West end and Hatzic begin? **A:** The City pages read for this note state no single named boundary street between "Mission West" and Hatzic. This note uses the brief's working split — west of Wren Street / Cascade Street toward the Stave River is Mission West, Hatzic is east — and Hatzic-wide facts sit in the hatzic note. Treat the line as a guide convention, not a City boundary. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/legislated-referral-consultation
- **Q:** Can I add a coach house in Silverdale? **A:** Not always as-of-right. At 30748 Silverhill Avenue the owner needed a rezoning from RU16 to RU16s (Rural 16 Secondary Dwelling Zone) specifically to permit a secondary detached unit in coach-house form. Ask Planning which secondary-dwelling zone, if any, already applies to the lot you are buying. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rsd25-5
- **Q:** Is Silverdale serviced for new subdivisions? **A:** Not yet at area scale. The City's Master Infrastructure Strategy for the 3,440-acre SCPA exists precisely because the area lacks urban servicing — streets, water, sanitary and storm systems still have to be built as neighbourhood plans proceed. Existing rural homes should be checked lot by lot for well and septic. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/silverdale-mis-servicing-roads-drainage
- **Q:** Which school district and schools? **A:** School District 75 (Mission). Silverdale Elementary is at 29715 Donatelli Avenue, Mission, and the western side feeds toward Mission's middle and secondary schools in town. Catchments here cover large rural areas, so verify the exact address with the district and ask about bus routing, not just the school name. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/legislated-referral-consultation
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Silverdale's long-range plan splits the SCPA into Central, West and East neighbourhoods; the Central Neighbourhood Plan work includes an Integrated Rainwater Management Plan dated May 31, 2024, prepared for Silverdale Hills GP Inc. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/h1-irmp-master-report-app-g
- The draft 2025 OCP engagement identified a potential future Transit-Oriented Development Area on Lougheed Highway in Silverdale at the heart of a large Community Node, tied to longer-term servicing expansion. https://engage.mission.ca/media/file/phase-2-engagement-public-workbook
- The Stave River forms Mission's western edge: Hayward Lake and Stave Lake are BC Hydro reservoirs upstream of Ruskin Dam, about 60 km east of Vancouver. https://www.bchydro.com/community/recreation_areas/hayward_lake.html
- Mission City Station, in town east of this area, is the eastern terminus of the West Coast Express and the meeting point for TransLink and BC Transit buses. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- BC Transit Central Fraser Valley routes serving Mission City Station include 31 Valley Connector to Abbotsford, 32 West Heights, 33 Cedar Valley, 34 East Side and 35 Hatzic — no route is named for Silverdale or Steelhead. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
Read: 2026-11-03

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Silverdale Comprehensive Planning Area / Central Neighbourhood Plan, southwest Mission (1,392 ha): City of Mission OCP future growth area; Master Infrastructure Strategy adopted 2020, Central Neighbourhood Plan and its May 31, 2024 Integrated Rainwater Management Plan in progress — status read 2026-11-03; no home count or completion date is stated in this note because none was stated on the pages read. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/h1-irmp-master-report-app-g
- Rezoning RSD25-5, 30748 Silverhill Avenue, Silverdale: RU16 to RU16s to permit one secondary detached (coach house) unit on a 1.95 ha lot; information package for the October 6, 2025 meeting. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rsd25-5
- City of Mission OCP Bylaw 6350-2025: new OCP replacing Bylaw 5670-2017, with legislated referral responses dated April 22, 2025 — the plan framework for Silverdale, the Lougheed corridor and a Rural Mission Strategy. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/legislated-referral-consultation
Read: 2026-11-03

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City of Mission 2026 budget presentation (for a home assessed at $1,133,024 in 2025): flat-rate water user fee $627.24 and sewer user fee $776.64 in the 2026 estimate, refuse collection $164.04 and recycling/compost $292.80 — figures are the City's average-home illustration, not a per-lot quote. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/budget-2026-oct-26-meeting-presentation
- Speculation and Vacancy Tax: the District of Mission is listed as a designated taxable area, so owners must declare. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- The City's 2026 presentation shows total municipal and utility charges rising 11.74% on that illustration home — check current before relying on it. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/budget-2026-oct-26-meeting-presentation
Read: 2026-11-03

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- West Coast Express: Mission City Station to Waterfront Station, Vancouver, weekday mornings westbound and weekday afternoons eastbound, no weekend or statutory-holiday service; first westbound departure shown 5:25 a.m. in the January 5, 2026 timetable. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- TransLink bus 701 provides limited weekday service to and from Mission City Station along Lougheed Highway when trains are not running. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- Mission Bridge (Highway 11) south to Abbotsford and Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) west to Maple Ridge are the road links; drive times UNCONFIRMED.
Read: 2026-11-03

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Fraser River flood: in June 2026 the Province released new floodplain maps for the Fraser and Coquihalla rivers from Yale to Mission; use the Flood Study Explorer plus the City's own floodplain designation for a specific lot — provincial maps do not replace local designation. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026WLRS0029-000715
- The Province's official Fraser River flood profile runs from Mission to the sea and a separate Hope-to-Mission profile exists; the 1894 flood was the design flood for Fraser dikes. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/drought-flooding-dikes-dams/integrated-flood-hazard-management/governance/dike-management/flood-profiles-scenarios
- Silverdale slope and rainwater: the Central Neighbourhood Plan required a dedicated Integrated Rainwater Management Plan and geotechnical assessment, a signal to get lot-level slope and drainage advice in the SCPA hillsides. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/h1-irmp-master-report-app-g
