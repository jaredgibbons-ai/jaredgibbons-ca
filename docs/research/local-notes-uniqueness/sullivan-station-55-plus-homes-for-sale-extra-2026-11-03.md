## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The governing plan for this ground changed, not just the zoning: the South Newton plan was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) update and is no longer in effect, development is led by the OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, and on December 2, 2024 Council endorsed Corporate Report R246, which updated South Newton's land-use designations. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- Surrey's Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) rules allow a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 square metres, 4 units on lots larger than 280 square metres, and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 square metres within 400 metres of a frequent bus stop — the route for a Sullivan Station homeowner to add a suite or units for a caregiver or family member on a detached lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- In the neighbouring 55+ complexes, the age rule is a strata bylaw: only a 55+ restriction is legal in British Columbia since November 24, 2022, the bylaw governs who may live in the unit rather than who may own it, and a younger spouse or partner, a live-in caregiver and legacy residents are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Surrey's Manufactured Home Park Policy tells park residents the owner must give 12 months' notice of a final moving date, only after receiving all permits and approvals — a city policy layer that matters to park buyers elsewhere in Surrey, though no park is verified inside this sub-area. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there any 55+ housing in Sullivan Station itself? **A:** No, none was verified. The sub-area's named complexes are family townhouse and condo product with no age restriction in the sources checked for the settled program file. A search that returns 55+ results under a Sullivan Station heading is returning the East Newton complexes to the north or the Panorama Ridge complexes to the south, under three different sub-area labels. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Q:** What about Sullivan Mews — it has Sullivan in the name? **A:** Sullivan Mews at 15020 66A Avenue is a genuine 55+ community, but it is filed under the East Newton sub-area, not Sullivan Station, and it is not a strata. It is a membership purchase, so there is no strata lot, no Form B in the strata sense, and the membership documents are the due diligence. The name, the sub-area and the tenure each point in a different direction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** Where is the nearest actual 55+ purchase? **A:** North in East Newton, at Chelsea Gardens, 13870 and 13888 70 Avenue, and Charleston Green, 6537 138 Street, or south in Panorama Ridge at the two Lakewood complexes on W Boundary Drive. Chelsea Gardens mixes apartment-style condos and ground-level townhouse-style homes in one gated strata, so a buyer who wants a condo form and a buyer who wants a house form are reading different sections of the same complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** Can I create 55+ style living on a Sullivan Station house lot instead? **A:** You can add housing, but not an age restriction of your own making. Surrey's small-scale rules permit three to six units on a detached lot depending on lot size and frequent-bus-stop proximity, which allows a suite or coach-style unit for a caregiver or relative. An age restriction on who may live there is a strata bylaw matter, and a single detached lot is not a strata. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- The main due-diligence risk on this page is a labelling risk, not a physical one: a complex marketed under a Sullivan name or shown in a Sullivan Station search may sit in East Newton or Panorama Ridge and may be a membership rather than a strata, so the civic address, the sub-area and the tenure are each checked separately. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
