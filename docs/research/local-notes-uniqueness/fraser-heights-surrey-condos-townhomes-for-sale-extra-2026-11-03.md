# Fraser Heights Condos and Townhomes — Extra Local Notes
Page: fraser-heights-surrey-condos-townhomes-for-sale (Fraser Heights, Surrey; condos/townhomes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Fraser Heights is a detached-house plateau, so its condo and townhome supply is the exception, not the pattern: the area sits north of Highway 1 in Guildford, with the Fraser River and Surrey Bend Regional Park to the north. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/abbey-ridge-land-use-plan
- The eastern slice is Abbey Ridge, about 175 hectares between 172 Street and the Port Kells industrial area and Golden Ears Connector, running from Highway 1 up to Surrey Bend Regional Park. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/abbey-ridge-land-use-plan
- The one commercial node is 108 Avenue at 160 Street, where the Fraser Heights Shopping Centre at 16033 108 Avenue sits under Comprehensive Development (CD) By-law No. 11302 — attached homes cluster near this node and the 104 Avenue edge, not across the plateau. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7917-0317-00.pdf
- City-wide Surrey narrative (six communities, City Centre's status) is held in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Attached stock here is thin and mostly townhouse form: no apartment-condo building in Fraser Heights was confirmed on a municipal or strata source read in this pass, and that gap is stated plainly in NOT CONFIRMED below. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans
- Abbey Ridge was suburban acreage before its Local Area Plan (LAP), approved February 6, 2017 (Corporate Report R032), so its newer homes — including any attached product — post-date that plan, unlike the 1980s–1990s detached subdivisions around Fraser Glen. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/abbey-ridge-land-use-plan
- The schools that anchor family demand for townhomes are Fraser Heights Secondary at 16060 108 Avenue, Bothwell Elementary at 17070 102 Avenue, Dogwood Elementary at 10752 157 Street, Erma Stephenson Elementary at 10929 160 Street and Fraser Wood Elementary at 10650 164 Street. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- Pacific Academy, a private Junior Kindergarten to Grade 12 school at 10238 168 Street, sits inside the neighbourhood and is named in City reports as a locational landmark for the 104 Avenue commercial pocket. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7914-0151-00.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Every Fraser Heights lot carries a zone under Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000, and the City directs buyers to confirm the current zone in its online mapping (COSMOS — City of Surrey Mapping Online System) rather than rely on an old zone name. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/zoning
- Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) rules adopted July 8, 2024 allow three to four homes on most residential lots, and up to six on lots of 281–4,050 square metres within 400 metres of a frequent bus stop — the 337 and 338 stops on 108 Avenue and 104 Avenue are the stops that matter here. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- In Abbey Ridge, the LAP designations were superseded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan update, so an attached-home proposal there is read against the new Official Community Plan (OCP), not the 2017 plan text alone. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/abbey-ridge-land-use-plan
- A strata townhouse here can be a conventional strata or a bare-land strata; the Province's strata page explains that strata plans filed at the Land Title Office can cover townhouses and bare land, which changes who maintains roads and yards. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/understanding-stratas/kinds-of-stratas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Are there real condos in Fraser Heights, or only townhomes? **A:** Mostly townhomes and detached houses — no apartment-condo building was confirmed here on an official source. Fraser Heights is planned as a low-density plateau in Guildford, and its one planned higher-density pocket is Abbey Ridge in the east under its 2017 Local Area Plan. A buyer set on an apartment should compare Guildford Town Centre stock instead of waiting for a Fraser Heights tower. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/abbey-ridge-land-use-plan
- **Q:** Which elementary school does a townhome on 164 Street feed? **A:** Catchments are address-based, not street-name-based. The Ministry directory places Fraser Wood Elementary at 10650 164 Street, Bothwell Elementary at 17070 102 Avenue and Erma Stephenson Elementary at 10929 160 Street inside the area, but only the School District 36 (Surrey) locator run on the exact unit address settles which school a specific townhome feeds. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** Can a Fraser Heights townhome owner rent the home out long-term? **A:** Yes for long-term tenancies. Since November 24, 2022, British Columbia strata corporations cannot enforce rental-restriction bylaws against long-term rentals, though short-term rentals can still be restricted and the strata's own bylaws at 108 Avenue or 104 Avenue complexes still govern pets and other rules, so read the registered bylaws, not a listing summary. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q:** Does the slope at the east end affect an attached-home purchase? **A:** It can. City reporting describes the northeast Fraser Heights slope above 104 Avenue and 172 Street as a high environmentally sensitive area with streams, next to the Canadian National (CN) intermodal yards — a townhome or lot on that slope needs the hazard and streamside checks in Section 9, not just a strata document review. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/RPT_2000-C014.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Route 337 is the lifeline bus: TransLink's 337 Fraser Heights / Surrey Central Station terminates at 168 Street and 104 Avenue and runs through Guildford Exchange, so townhome blocks near 104 Avenue have a different commute from blocks up at 112 Avenue. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt337.pdf
- Route 338 East Fraser Heights / Guildford terminates at 177A Street and 101A Avenue and is the only bus serving the Abbey Ridge side — frequency there is a plan detail, not a SkyTrain substitute. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/210/tt338.pdf
- TransLink's own South of Fraser engagement records riders asking for more peak frequency on the 337 and 338 and an extension of the 337 to serve Pacific Academy, Fraser Heights Secondary and the Fraser Heights Recreation Centre at 10588 160 Street — crowding at school times is documented by the operator. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/plans-and-projects/area-planning/south-of-fraser/sofe-ph1-public-engagement-summary.pdf
- Surrey Bend Regional Park, on the Fraser River below the plateau, is floodplain forest and marsh managed by Metro Vancouver — homes at the north edge sit above it, but trail access, not building land, is what the park offers. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/surrey-bend-regional-park
- The Abbey Ridge park site known as 27D, between Lyncean Drive and 179 Street, is a 5.2-acre neighbourhood park still in design — the City issued a design request for proposals closing July 9, 2025, so east-side townhome buyers are buying beside a planned park, not a finished one. https://www.surrey.ca/business-economy/tenders-rfqs-rfps/design-and-construction-administration-of-3-new-neighbourhood-parks

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey's total 2026 residential (Class 1) tax rate is $3.42731 per $1,000 of assessed value ($1.84251 municipal plus $1.58480 other authorities) — the full city-wide breakdown is held in the surrey-houses-for-sale note; the same rate applies to a Fraser Heights strata unit. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- A Fraser Heights home with a legal secondary suite is billed extra utilities for the suite: for 2026, $454.00 water and $821.71 sewer for the suite, plus $172.04 waste — relevant to townhomes and houses marketed with a suite. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill
- Metered Surrey homes are billed water and sewer every four months, with sewer charged at 88 percent of water supplied — the billing cycle a strata buyer sees passed through in fees. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies: the Province lists the City of Surrey as a designated taxable area, so a vacant Fraser Heights unit still needs the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Two TransLink routes serve the whole neighbourhood: the 337 (Fraser Heights / Surrey Central Station, terminus 168 Street at 104 Avenue) and the 338 (East Fraser Heights / Guildford, terminus 177A Street at 101A Avenue); there is no SkyTrain station in Fraser Heights. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt337.pdf
- Highway 1 interchanges at 160 Street and 176 Street are the driving exits; the Golden Ears Way connector at the east edge leads to the Golden Ears Bridge toward Maple Ridge — travel time to Maple Ridge is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/abbey-ridge-land-use-plan
- Guildford Exchange and Guildford Town Centre at 10355 152 Street are the transfer and shopping anchor below the plateau, reached by the 337 and 338. https://www.guildfordtowncentre.com/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the lot in COSMOS first: Surrey's mapping shows zoning, flood and hazard layers for the exact address, and the northeast slope above 104 Avenue carries streams and a high environmentally sensitive area rating in City reporting. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/zoning
- For a unit near Bon Accord or the ravine creeks feeding Surrey Bend, confirm streamside setbacks under Part 7 (Streamside Protection) of Zoning By-law No. 12000 — the City applies a setback and no-build covenant pattern on streamside lots elsewhere in North Surrey. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7917-0186-00.pdf
- Confirm Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status per property on the Agricultural Land Commission map before assuming a large Abbey Ridge-edge lot can be subdivided — the plan area borders acreage and industrial land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- The City page does not tell a buyer a specific strata's finances: for any Fraser Heights townhome, the Form B Information Certificate and depreciation report are the documents that show planned levies — request them for the exact strata plan. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
