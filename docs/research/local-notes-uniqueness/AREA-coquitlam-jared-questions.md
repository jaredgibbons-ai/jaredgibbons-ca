# Coquitlam — Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer, for a voice memo. Muse does not answer these.

1. Across a whole year of Coquitlam deals, which pocket gives buyers the most house for the money right now — and which pocket do they overpay for without realising?
2. When a family tells you "Coquitlam" with no neighbourhood named, what do you ask first to sort them — and where do they usually end up?
3. What is the most common surprise in a Coquitlam purchase: strata documents in the big Glen Drive towers, slope and creek rules on the north side, or West Coast Express versus SkyTrain commute reality?
4. How has Burke Mountain changed the rest of the city's market — does it pull buyers out of the older neighbourhoods or push prices up in them?
5. Tell one story that sums up Coquitlam — a deal that shows what buying here is really like in 2026.
