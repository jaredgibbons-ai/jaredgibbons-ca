# Heritage Mountain Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: heritage-mountain-port-moody (Heritage Mountain; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Heritage Mountain is a City of Port Moody neighbourhood grouping, not a separate municipality: Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw, 2026, No. 3522, section 14.3 covers Heritage Mountain together with the Twin Creeks, Noons Creek, Mountain Meadows and Heritage Woods sub-areas. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- The street pattern is the point here: Ravine Drive, Noons Creek Drive and Turner Creek Drive climb off Heritage Mountain Boulevard, and David Avenue is the east-west exit toward Ioco Road or Coquitlam. https://translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/182/direction/0/schedule
- The north edge is forest, not houses: Bert Flinn Park backs the upper streets, and the Village of Anmore sits beyond the city boundary on East Road. https://www.portmoody.ca/news-notices-and-community-matters/posts/cougar-encounter-in-bert-flinn-park/

- City-wide zoning, secondary-suite and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) rules for these streets are covered in full in the heritage-mountain-port-moody-houses-for-sale note; the neighbourhood-level point is that a registered building scheme on title can block an SSMUH application here. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Bert Flinn Park off-leash? **A:** Only in part. Dogs may be off leash at any time on Bert Flinn Park's wide gravel trail, and from 6 a.m. to 10 a.m. only on the Morning Off-Leash Mini Loop, a one-year pilot the City is monitoring through late 2026; everywhere else in the park is on-leash, and non-compliance can draw a ticket under Bylaw No. 3343. https://www.portmoody.ca/news-notices-and-community-matters/posts/city-pilots-expansion-of-dog-off-leash-area-in-bert-flinn-park-with-addition-of-morning-only-mini-loop-trail/
- **Q:** Can visitors park all day on the streets below Bert Flinn Park? **A:** No. The City reminds park drivers that a vehicle may not be parked adjacent to another person's residential property for more than three hours between 7 a.m. and 7 p.m., Monday to Friday, under Bylaw No. 1528 — a rule that affects the Heritage Mountain Boulevard and David Avenue trailhead streets. https://www.portmoody.ca/news-notices-and-community-matters/posts/city-pilots-expansion-of-dog-off-leash-area-in-bert-flinn-park-with-addition-of-morning-only-mini-loop-trail/
- **Q:** Is wildlife a real issue on the upper streets? **A:** Yes, on the City's own record. The City posted a notice after the British Columbia Conservation Officer Service received reports of an encounter with two cougars, an adult and a young cougar, in the Bert Flinn Park area, and directs sightings in urban areas to the Report All Poachers and Polluters line at 1-877-952-7277. https://www.portmoody.ca/news-notices-and-community-matters/posts/cougar-encounter-in-bert-flinn-park/
- **Q:** Does one bus route cross the whole neighbourhood? **A:** Yes — route 182, Moody Centre Station/Belcarra, loops through Heritage Mountain on Heritage Mountain Boulevard, Ravine Drive and Turner Creek Drive, then continues via David Avenue and Aspenwood Drive into Anmore and Belcarra, so the same bus serves both the school-run streets and the inlet villages. https://translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/182/direction/0/schedule

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Heritage Mountain Elementary at 125 Ravine Drive sits inside the neighbourhood; the secondary building buyers picture, Heritage Woods Secondary at 1300 David Avenue, is in Heritage Woods, not here. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- There is no library branch in Heritage Mountain; the single Port Moody Public Library is at 100 Newport Drive in Inlet Centre. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- OCP policy for section 14.3 restricts vehicle access to local roads and prohibits access from arterial roads, which shapes driveway approvals on Heritage Mountain Boulevard and David Avenue lots — see the heritage-mountain-port-moody-houses-for-sale note. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Port Moody was declared a Bear Smart Community in 2024, and landscaping beside sensitive areas here must use the City Preferred Plant List with bear-resistant waste enclosures. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/environmental-protection-and-requirements/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Taxes here are City of Port Moody taxes, charged city-wide with no Heritage Mountain local-area levy found: the 2026 municipal residential general rate of 1.61574 per $1,000 and the $1,892 single-family utility bill are set out in full in the heritage-mountain-port-moody-houses-for-sale note. http://www.portmoody.ca/media/tiabceqp/2026-mill-rates.pdf
- Port Moody is a Speculation and Vacancy Tax taxable area, so every residential owner here must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 182 is the through-route: Moody Centre Station/Belcarra, crossing Heritage Mountain on Ravine Drive and Turner Creek Drive before David Avenue. https://translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/182/direction/0/schedule
- Routes 183 and 184 link the neighbourhood's edges to Inlet Centre Station and Moody Centre Station on the Millennium Line; route detail is in the heritage-mountain-port-moody-houses-for-sale note. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt184.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check a lot against Development Permit Area 4 and Area 5 on the City's ViewPort map before buying a Noons Creek or slope lot; the general City pages do not settle an individual lot's status — see the heritage-mountain-port-moody-houses-for-sale note for the map layers. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
