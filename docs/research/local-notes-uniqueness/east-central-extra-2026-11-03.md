# East Central Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: east-central (East Central, Maple Ridge; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- "East Central" is a Paragon (MLS) sub-area name only for ground in the City of Maple Ridge: it is not a City plan or neighbourhood name, no City plan named "East Central" exists, and the downtown part of this sub-area is covered by the City's Town Centre Area Plan. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- The City publishes no boundary for this sub-area, so the boundary is UNVERIFIED; this page's working scope, from the live page, is the ground between 222 Street and 232 Street, with the Town Centre core at its west side and residential streets east and north of downtown toward 232 Street and 124 Avenue. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- East Central sits east of West Central and the Town Centre seam at 222–224 Street, west of Cottonwood, with Southwest Maple Ridge (Port Haney) at its southwest corner and Albion further east along Lougheed Highway; residents and the City usually call its west end Downtown Maple Ridge, Haney, or the Town Centre. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods/be-downtown

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- This sub-area holds two stocks side by side: freehold single-detached houses on the residential streets east and north of downtown, and strata condos concentrated in the Town Centre core along 224 Street, 226 Street, 227 Street and 121 Avenue. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods/be-downtown
- The condo cluster exists because the plan allows it: the Town Centre Core designation supports higher densities in a transit-oriented downtown anchored by the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- The City names a downtown location "Brickwater" at 11897 227 Street, but official City pages do not state its building type or developer: UNVERIFIED beyond its name and address; no named townhome complex in the sub-area was confirmed on an official source. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods/be-downtown
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Maple Ridge-wide zoning, suite and tax facts are written in full in the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note — see that note; East Central's own layer is the Town Centre Area Plan (Official Community Plan section 10.4), written in full here because the downtown ground is this page's. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- The Town Centre Area Plan update expanded the plan boundaries to incorporate River Bend, consolidated the land use designations, and added a Town Centre Core designation supporting higher densities around the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024 covers the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station transit-oriented areas inside this plan area, which changes the parking and density rules a downtown buyer or builder meets. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- Town Centre parcels carry Town Centre commercial, multi-family and comprehensive development zones under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019, separate from the house zones on the residential streets. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is East Central the same as downtown Maple Ridge? **A:** Only at its west end. "East Central" is the board's sub-area name; the City calls the western part Downtown Maple Ridge, Haney or the Town Centre and plans it under the Town Centre Area Plan, while the streets east and north of downtown toward 232 Street are residential ground under the same sub-area label. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods/be-downtown
- **Q:** Which schools are physically in East Central? **A:** Golden Ears Elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 7) at 23124 118 Avenue, Harry Hooge Elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 7) at 12280 230 Street, Thomas Haney Secondary at 23000 116 Avenue, ci:tməxw Environmental Community (Kindergarten to Grade 8) and District 42 Alternative Secondary, both at 23125 116 Avenue; catchments are address-based, so run the exact address in the School District No. 42 locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- **Q:** What school would a downtown address actually be assigned? **A:** It depends on the address, and one downtown result is on record: for the sample address 11897 227 Street, the district locator gives Eric Langton Elementary and Thomas Haney Secondary, with French Immersion options at Maple Ridge Elementary (early), Eric Langton Elementary (late) and Maple Ridge Secondary. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q:** What did the Town Centre plan update change for a downtown condo buyer? **A:** The update expanded the plan to incorporate River Bend, consolidated its land use designations and added a Town Centre Core designation for higher densities around the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station — the two transit anchors downtown buildings are planned around. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Memorial Peace Park, on 224 Street between Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road, is surrounded by City Hall, the Leisure Centre, the Greg Moore Youth Centre, The ACT Arts Centre and Haney Place Mall, holds the Cenotaph and the Bandstand, and hosts the Haney Farmers Market — downtown condos face event and market activity, not just retail. https://www.mapleridge.ca/art-parks-rec/recreation-facilities/memorial-peace-park
- The Maple Ridge Leisure Centre at 11925 Haney Place, at the west edge of this sub-area, has an indoor pool, fitness centre, gymnasium and sport courts. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre
- Maple Ridge Public Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library) at 130 – 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road is inside this sub-area and is the only Fraser Valley Regional Library branch in Maple Ridge. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/maple-ridge-public-library
- The Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is inside Haney Place Mall at 121 – 11900 Haney Place, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m.; Ridge Meadows Hospital itself is west of this sub-area at 11666 Laity Street. https://www.healthlinkbc.ca/clinic/ridge-meadows-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- Port Haney, at the sub-area's southwest corner, was historically the steamboat landing and Canadian Pacific Railway station for the area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Downtown shopping is three named centres in the City's downtown guide: Haney Place Mall at 11900 Haney Place, Valley Fair Mall at 22709 Lougheed Highway, and Maple Ridge Square Shopping Mall at 22441 Dewdney Trunk Road; grocery tenants inside them were not confirmed on an official source. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods/be-downtown

- East Central has no separate tax or utility rate: Maple Ridge's residential tax rate, utility charges and due dates are city-wide and are written in full in the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note — see that note. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Haney Place Exchange is in this sub-area; routes on TransLink's Haney Place route diagram include the R3, 701, 733, 741, 743, 744, 745, 746, 748, 749 and 791, and the 733 serves the residential area north of downtown. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/194/r748.pdf
- The R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus runs from Coquitlam Central Station to Haney Place, giving this sub-area the city's rapid-bus terminus. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
- Port Haney Station at 22300 River Road, at the sub-area's southwest corner, runs West Coast Express trains on weekdays between Waterfront Station (downtown Vancouver) and Mission City. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) runs along the south side, Dewdney Trunk Road runs east–west through downtown, 224 Street and 226 Street are the main north–south streets through the core, and the Haney Bypass links Lougheed Highway to Port Haney at the southwest corner. https://www.mapleridge.ca/art-parks-rec/recreation-facilities/memorial-peace-park

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check floodplain status on the south edge: along River Road and Port Haney the flood construction level is 6.7 metres geodetic including freeboard, no habitable floor space may sit below it on affected lots, a covenant on title identifies floodplain construction, and suites are prohibited on floodplain properties. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- Check escarpment status at the west edge: the Fraser River Escarpment policy area reaches this sub-area (its east boundary is 224 Street), and lots within 300 metres of the crest require a geotechnical report attached to a Section 219 covenant before a building permit. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- No Agricultural Land Reserve land in East Central was identified in the official sources checked; parcel status is a per-address check on the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
