# Tsawwassen Central Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: tsawwassen-central-guide (Tsawwassen Central, Tsawwassen; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Tsawwassen Central is centred on the junction of 56 Street and 12 Avenue, around the Tsawwassen Town Centre site; it is a board sub-area name, and the City publishes no boundary for it. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009764?tool=survey_tool
- South Delta Recreation Centre at 1720 56 Street and South Delta Exchange beside it mark the area's south end, and Winskill Park at 5575 9 Avenue marks its east side. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/winskill-park

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The housing mix is the widest in Tsawwassen: older detached streets around 12 Avenue, apartments and seniors housing on 56 Street, and mixed-use buildings proposed at the town centre. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921
- KinVillage on 54A Street is the area's seniors campus, and its proposed second phase alone is a six-storey building of 143 seniors affordable rental units with a community centre on the main floor. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The current Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 8400, 2024, designates the centre as Neighbourhood Centre and Corridors (NC), intended for multi-unit residential and mixed uses; there is no Tsawwassen Area Plan in Bylaw 8400 — that plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921
- Because these proposals are consistent with the OCP, provincial legislation says no public hearing is held for their rezonings, so comment happens through the City's application pages instead. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921
- Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017 is the zoning bylaw every application here amends, including parking variances such as KinVillage's request to go from 143 parking spaces to 72. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there still a Tsawwassen Area Plan? **A:** No, not in the current plan. The Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 3950; the current consolidated OCP, Bylaw No. 8400, 2024, contains no Tsawwassen Area Plan and instead designates this centre Neighbourhood Centre and Corridors (NC). Older documents quoting Schedule D.1 are out of date. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921
- **Q:** Which schools serve a central Tsawwassen address? **A:** Verify the exact address in the district locator, because the centre sits between catchments. Cliff Drive Elementary at 5025 12 Avenue and South Park Elementary at 735 Gilchrist Drive are the two elementary schools flanking the centre, and South Delta Secondary at 750 53 Street is the secondary school; the district directs families to confirm catchment by address. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/news-events/news/registration-information-for-students-who-are-new-to-delta-2/
- **Q:** Will the pool and gym be closed for years? **A:** The old Winskill centre is being replaced, not just repaired. Construction started in summer 2025 on a larger facility with a ten-lane, 25-metre lap pool, leisure pool, teach pool and gymnasium, and the City's May 2025 release put completion at the end of 2027. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/sports-activities/recreation-facilities/winskill-renewal-project
- **Q:** How do I get downtown without a car? **A:** Take a bus from South Delta Exchange to Bridgeport Station and change to the Canada Line. Route 601 is the all-day service, and routes 602, 603 and 604 are the peak-period expresses from the Tsawwassen neighbourhoods to Bridgeport. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Winskill Park holds the Lions Wellness Park, which the City describes as British Columbia's first playground for older adults. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/winskill-park
- Overnight fibre-optic cable work ran along 56 Street and 12 Avenue in early 2026, between 8:00 p.m. and 5:00 a.m., with right-lane closures — a sample of the utility work that comes with a growing centre. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/construction-notice-tsawwassen-fibre-optic-cable
- Tsawwassen Library is at 1321A 56 Street, inside the town centre, and the South Delta Recreation Centre is at 1720 56 Street by the exchange. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/heat-warning-issued-city-delta
- A new 3.5-metre multi-use pathway is being built along 56 Street from 6 Avenue to Winskill Park, extended along 8A Avenue and 55A Street. https://letstalk.delta.ca/56stmup?tool=survey_tool
- South Delta Recreation Centre at 1720 56 Street is also a listed City cooling centre in heat warnings, with the Tsawwassen Library at 1321A 56 Street on the same list. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/heat-warning-issued-city-delta

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Winskill Renewal Project, Winskill Park: replacement aquatic and fitness centre approved by Council in spring 2024, designs approved fall 2024, construction started summer 2025, with a fourth baseball diamond added and the disc golf course moved to Diefenbaker Park. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/sports-activities/recreation-facilities/winskill-renewal-project
- KinVillage Phase 2, 1050 54A Street (file LU009921): one six-storey building, 143 seniors affordable rental units including 131 adaptable units, plus a 786.2 square metre community centre; rezoning and a parking variance were at the feedback stage when read on 2026-11-03. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921
- The Park, 5360 12 Avenue and 1146, 1156 and 1170 53A Street (file LU009764): revised application for a six-storey mixed-use building with ground-floor commercial and 67 apartment units, up from 65 units and five storeys in the original; not yet to Council when read. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009764?tool=survey_tool
- Malcolm Centre, 1505 56 Street (file LU009955): six-storey mixed use with 45 rental apartments and four ground-floor retail units; a public information meeting was held April 9, 2026. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009955
Read: 2026-11-03

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta's 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after that date and another 5 percent after September 2, 2026; Council's 2026 plan set a 2.9 percent City tax increase. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- For 2026, Delta's flat-rate utility bill for a single-family home is $1,658, up $51 from 2025, covering garbage and recycling plus water and sewer where not metered separately. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Delta is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT), so residential owners must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- South Delta Exchange at 56 Street and 18 Avenue, beside the recreation centre, is the hub for routes 601, 602, 603, 604 and the local 609 to Ladner Exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf
- Highway 17 south from 56 Street reaches the British Columbia Ferries (BC Ferries) Tsawwassen terminal, and Highway 17A and Highway 99 run north through the George Massey Tunnel to Richmond. https://www.bcferries.com/travel-boarding/terminal-directions-parking-food/vancouver-tsawwassen/TSA

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Construction disruption is the near-term risk in the centre: the Winskill rebuild, the 56 Street pathway and several six-storey applications all sit within a few blocks, so check the City's application and project pages for the street you are buying on. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/sports-activities/recreation-facilities/winskill-renewal-project
