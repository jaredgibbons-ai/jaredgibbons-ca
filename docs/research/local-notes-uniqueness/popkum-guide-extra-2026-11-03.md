# Popkum Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: popkum-guide (Popkum, Chilliwack; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Popkum is not in the City of Chilliwack — it is an unincorporated community in Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area D, Popkum/Bridal Falls/Wahleach. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area D - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- The FVRD describes Area D as bounded by the Fraser River to the northwest, Electoral Area B to the north and east, Electoral Area E to the south, and the City of Chilliwack to the west; no official published boundary for Popkum itself was found — see the popkum note. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html
- The settled corridor runs along Yale Road east of Chilliwack, south of Highway 1, divided by Highway 9 into West and East Popkum in the Popkum-Bridal Falls Official Community Plan (OCP). https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Housing here is mainly single-detached homes and manufactured homes on rural and suburban lots along Yale Road and Popkum Road, not strata subdivisions — see the popkum note for lot detail. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html
- Cheam Lake Wetlands Regional Park, at 53480 Elgey Road, sits at the Popkum/Rosedale edge and limits building on its wetland lands. http://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/cheam-lake-wetlands-regional-park.html

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Land use is governed by the FVRD, not City of Chilliwack bylaws, because Popkum is in Electoral Area D. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html
- Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) land in the plan area is subject to Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) rules; check a specific parcel on the ALC map before assuming a non-farm use is allowed. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules allowing secondary suites province-wide also apply in regional district electoral areas, per the Province. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/local-governments-and-housing/housing-initiatives/smale-scale-multi-unit-housing

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Who provides local government services in Popkum? **A:** The FVRD provides local services in Electoral Area D, while the Province collects rural property tax. The FVRD bills some utilities directly by service area, and not all taxpayers in the same electoral area are in the same service, so a buyer should confirm which services a specific lot actually receives before closing. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html
- **Q:** Is there a school in Popkum itself? **A:** No school is located in Popkum itself. School District No. 33 (SD33) transportation lists the Rosedale/Popkum area as Rosedale Traditional Community School and Chilliwack Secondary School, so most Popkum families travel west toward Rosedale and Chilliwack Proper for school. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2023-03/611%20AP%20Transportation%20-%20Operating%20Instructions.pdf
- **Q:** How do I get a bus from Popkum? **A:** BC Transit route 71, Agassiz-Harrison, runs along Yale Road through Rosedale and Popkum between downtown Chilliwack and Harrison Hot Springs. It is the only BC Transit route named for this corridor in the Chilliwack riders' guide list, which also lists route 66 and route 51 for connections in Chilliwack itself. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/riders-guide/
- **Q:** Can I visit Minter Gardens? **A:** No. The University of the Fraser Valley (UFV) states Minter Gardens, in rural Popkum in Electoral Area D, closed in 2013 after 33 years and the property was put up for sale. Do not treat it as an operating attraction when valuing a nearby home. https://blogs.ufv.ca/blog/2014/07/ufv-students-work-fvrd-imagine-new-future-minter-gardens/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Pets are not permitted in Cheam Lake Wetlands Regional Park, a 107-hectare FVRD park with about 2 kilometres of trails and a floating boardwalk, because of its sensitive ecosystem. http://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/cheam-lake-wetlands-regional-park.html
- Bridal Veil Falls Provincial Park is immediately east at the Highway 1 and Highway 9 junction, a day-use park centred on Bridal Veil Falls. https://bcparks.ca/bridal-veil-falls-park/
- FVRD utility billing in Area D is quarterly, in January, April, July and October, unlike City of Chilliwack quarterly billing under city bylaws. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area D - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- No Fraser Valley Regional Library branch is in Popkum; the nearest branches listed are in Chilliwack and Agassiz — see the popkum note. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html
- Highway 1 noise and interchange traffic affect lots closest to the Highway 9 interchange and Yale Road — no official noise map for Popkum was read, so this is a site-visit check, not a mapped zone. https://bcparks.ca/bridal-veil-falls-park/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Popkum does not pay City of Chilliwack tax rates. In FVRD Area D, 2026 residential Water - Area D is metered at a $77.00 flat/minimum with $0.96 per cubic metre over 100 cubic metres, billed quarterly; Sewer - Area D and Drainage - West Popkum show no utility billing and are funded through taxation instead. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area D - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) taxable-areas list names the City of Chilliwack, not FVRD Electoral Area D; check the provincial map for a specific Popkum parcel rather than assuming SVT applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- BC Transit route 71 connects Popkum along Yale Road to downtown Chilliwack, Agassiz and Harrison Hot Springs — see the popkum note for timing points. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/riders-guide/
- Highway 1 access is at the Highway 9 interchange by Bridal Falls, east of Popkum, which is also the route north across the Fraser River toward Agassiz. https://bcparks.ca/bridal-veil-falls-park/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check slope and slide history near the escarpment south of Highway 1: the Popkum-Bridal Falls plan area includes waterfall and slide terrain by Bridal Veil Falls — confirm a lot-specific geotechnical report, as no lot-level hazard map was read this pass. https://bcparks.ca/bridal-veil-falls-park/
- Check ALR status, water service area and septic arrangements per lot, because FVRD services differ by service area within Area D. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html
- Check whether a lot is in the West Popkum drainage service area, which is funded through taxation, not utility billing. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area D - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
