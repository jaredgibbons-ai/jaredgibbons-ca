## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The provincial age-restriction law — a strata may only restrict age at 55 or older, with caregiver, prior-resident, child and younger-spouse exemptions — is written in full in the chilliwack-55-plus-townhomes-for-sale note; see that note. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- Specific to these buildings: the registered bylaw of the individual building states who may occupy a unit, and the Form B Information Certificate is where a buyer confirms it for Ivy Green, Spadina Regency, Knight Lodge, The Sapphire or Mountainview Estates. No building's pet or rental rule is stated here, because no registered bylaw set was read in this pass.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which 55+ condos are downtown and which are in Sardis? **A:** Ivy Green at 45775 Spadina Avenue and Spadina Regency at 45765 Spadina Avenue are downtown, near the hospital precinct. Knight Lodge at 45660 Knight Road and The Sapphire at 7685 Amber Drive are in Sardis, south of Highway 1 and west of Vedder Road, on the shopping and recreation side around Vedder and Tyson roads. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/buildings/chilliwack?filter_titletoland=FREEHOLD%2BSTRATA&page=4&sortby=street_name
- **Q:** Can I rent out a 55+ condo if I move into care? **A:** British Columbia law removed strata rental-restriction bylaws, so a strata cannot ban long-term renting, but a valid 55+ age bylaw still controls who may live there, including a tenant. The tenant must meet the age rule or an exemption. Read the building's registered bylaws before promising a family member or tenant that they can move in. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/more-homes-for-people
- **Q:** How close is a downtown 55+ condo to the hospital? **A:** Chilliwack General Hospital is at 45600 Menholm Road, in the same Chilliwack Proper precinct as the Spadina Avenue buildings; Fraser Health calls it a community hospital providing primary, core secondary and some specialty services. No walking time is stated, because no source measured one for a specific building — check the route, including the Hodgins Avenue entrance Fraser Health directs outpatients to. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital
- **Q:** Is there urgent care on the Sardis side? **A:** No separate Sardis centre was found. Fraser Health's Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #104 – 7955 Evans Road, on the west side, co-located with the rural primary care centre. A Knight Road or Amber Drive buyer uses that centre and the Menholm Road hospital, the same services a downtown buyer uses. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/Chilliwack-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre

- Two 55+ buildings sit ten numbers apart on one street — 45765 and 45775 Spadina Avenue — while 45749 Spadina Avenue, Chilliwack Gardens, is not a 55+ building in the earlier program's checks. Check the exact civic address, not the street. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/buildings/chilliwack?filter_titletoland=FREEHOLD%2BSTRATA&page=4&sortby=street_name
- The Sardis 55+ buildings sit west of Vedder Road, while Sardis Sports Complex, 5725 Tyson Road, and Sardis Library, 5819 Tyson Road, sit east of the Knight Road area — recreation is a drive or bus ride across Vedder Road. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=117
- Curbside collection changed May 1, 2026, to weekly compostables and alternating bi-weekly garbage and recycling; an apartment strata normally handles waste internally, so this matters mainly when comparing a condo with a Cottage Grove rancher. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=818

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Chilliwack's 2025 total residential tax rate was 4.50189 per $1,000 of assessed value; full breakdown in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2527/Tax%5FRates%5Ffor%5Fthe%5FWebtugnc2fs1l9d%2Epdf
- City utilities are billed quarterly; in an apartment condo, water, sewer and garbage are commonly carried inside strata fees instead — a building-by-building document question. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in the City of Chilliwack, a listed taxable area, so an empty 55+ condo still needs the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Floodplain Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets floodplain areas and Flood Construction Levels city-wide; for an existing apartment, the practical check is the parkade, storage and flood history in the strata documents. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- The Sardis-Vedder Aquifer is a drinking-water Development Permit Area under Schedule D-1 of the 2050 OCP, relevant to any redevelopment of a Sardis strata site. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SD1%5FDPA%5FDrinking%5FWater%5FSupply%2Epdf
