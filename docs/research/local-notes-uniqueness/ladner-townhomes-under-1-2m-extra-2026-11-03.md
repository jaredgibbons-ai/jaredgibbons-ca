## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A strata can still ban short-term rentals, though not long-term rentals. A Delta council report states rental-restriction bylaws were ended provincially, but bylaws prohibiting short-term accommodations remain permitted in all strata corporations. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-02/David%20Eby_Premier_Strata%20Property%20Act%20Amendments_Feb%2013%202023_0.pdf
- The only strata age restriction now permitted is 55-and-over for seniors' housing, per the same report. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-02/David%20Eby_Premier_Strata%20Property%20Act%20Amendments_Feb%2013%202023_0.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves a Ladner townhome address? **A:** It depends on the exact address; the district locator is the check. The Delta School District groups Ladner Elementary's catchment from the Hawthorne, Holly, Ladner, Port Guichon and Neilson Grove areas. Ladner Elementary is Kindergarten to Grade 7 at 5016 44 Avenue. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/french-immersion-applications/early-french-immersion/
- **Q:** Who collects garbage and food scraps at a complex? **A:** Garbage follows Delta's weekly system, out by 7:00 a.m. on collection day. Food scraps differ: Delta states multi-family collection is arranged with private haulers. Ask the strata who its private hauler is, what the contract costs the complex each year, and whether that cost sits inside the monthly strata fee. https://www.delta.ca/services/garbage-recycling/green-can-compost
- **Q:** Can I run a short-term rental in a Ladner townhome? **A:** Check the strata bylaws first; a ban can still be valid. Delta's council report states long-term rental restrictions were ended provincially, but short-term accommodation prohibitions remain permitted in every strata corporation. A townhome that is rentable on a long-term lease may therefore still be barred from nightly or weekly rental use by its own bylaws. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-02/David%20Eby_Premier_Strata%20Property%20Act%20Amendments_Feb%2013%202023_0.pdf
- **Q:** What watering rules bind the complex's lawns? **A:** Metro Vancouver's regional rules bind strata common property like a house lot. Stage 1 restrictions run May 1 to October 15, allow lawn watering once weekly on a designated day by address and type, and are enforced through local bylaws. https://metrovancouver.org/media-room/media-release/970
