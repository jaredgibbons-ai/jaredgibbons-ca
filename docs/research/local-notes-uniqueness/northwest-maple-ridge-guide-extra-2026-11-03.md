# Northwest Maple Ridge Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: northwest-maple-ridge-guide (Northwest Maple Ridge; Guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Northwest Maple Ridge has no City-published boundary; it is the board sub-area "Northwest" on Maple Ridge's west side. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The working scope is north of Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road, west of about 224 Street, to the Pitt Meadows boundary. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Laity Street is the main north-south spine, with 123 Avenue and 124 Avenue running east-west through the housing. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt744.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is mainly freehold detached houses on RS-1 lots, with a small attached pocket near Lougheed Highway. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 sets RS-1 subdivision minimums at 668.0 square metres area, 18.0 metres width and 27.0 metres depth, with 40% maximum lot coverage. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- Infill lots on Hartnell Avenue show the current build form here: new detached houses on subdivided RS-1 lots, not strata product. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- One secondary suite per lot is allowed inside the detached house, to 40% of gross floor area, and not in the floodplain area — see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note for the full city rules. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- Eligible detached and duplex lots here may reach three, four or six units under the City's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) bylaws. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- No neighbourhood area plan covers the northwest housing area; the Official Community Plan (OCP) governs, updated November 25, 2025. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is there a train station in Northwest Maple Ridge? **A:** Yes — Maple Meadows Station is the west-side station for this area. It is at 20010 Dunn Avenue and West Coast Express trains run on weekdays between Waterfront in downtown Vancouver and Mission City, so the service is a commuter service, not an all-day service. Check the current timetable before relying on a specific train. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
**Q:** Which elementary school will my child attend? **A:** It depends on the exact address, not the street name alone. School District 42 (SD42) catchments are address-based, elementary school is Kindergarten to Grade 7, and Laity View Elementary at 21023 123 Avenue, Glenwood Elementary at 21410 Glenwood Avenue and Fairview Elementary at 12209 206 Street are the schools in this part of the city. Run the address in the district locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
**Q:** Is my lot in the floodplain? **A:** Only the exact lot lookup answers that, because low ground near the Alouette rivers is treated differently from the higher streets. The City publishes North and South Alouette River and Fraser River floodplain maps, the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 metres geodetic including freeboard, and no habitable floor space may sit below that level on an affected lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
**Q:** Can I add a suite for rental income? **A:** A suite is allowed only inside the house and only outside the floodplain area. The City allows one secondary suite per lot, capped at 40% of the building's gross floor area, and Zoning Bylaw section 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation on floodplain properties, so a low lot can lose the suite option entirely. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Maple Meadows Station, not Port Haney, is the practical West Coast Express station for most northwest addresses. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Route 744 runs Meadowtown to Haney Place via 123 Avenue, Laity Street and 207 Street, linking the area to both stations and the Town Centre exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt744.pdf
- Route 701 runs from Coquitlam Central Station via Maple Meadows Station and Haney Place along the Lougheed corridor south of the area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt701.pdf
- Merkley Park is listed by the City at 22008 124 Avenue; an earlier City page gave a different address, so confirm the park entrance by street, not by memory. https://www.mapleridge.ca/art-parks-rec/arts-theatre/public-art/field-dreams
- Ridge Meadows Hospital at 11666 Laity Street is in this quadrant of the city, on Laity Street itself. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- SD42 has no middle school tier, so children move from a Kindergarten to Grade 7 elementary school directly to a Grade 8 to 12 secondary school. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Maple Ridge's 2026 general residential rate is $2.8643 per $1,000 of taxable value — full city tax detail is in the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- Maple Ridge 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- The City of Maple Ridge is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so residential owners must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- West Coast Express from Maple Meadows Station is the rapid transit here, running weekday trains between Waterfront and Mission City. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Haney Place is the bus exchange for the area, served by routes 701, 743 and 744 on the Laity Street and Lougheed corridors. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt744.pdf
- TransLink's Langley to Haney Place Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) is in detailed planning at 22 kilometres with peak service every 10 minutes; designs, costs and timelines are not yet determined. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/bus-rapid-transit

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the City floodplain maps for the exact lot before removing subjects, because the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 metres geodetic on affected lots. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status parcel by parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission map for any lot at the northern farmland edge. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- The floodplain maps do not set a suite value: suites are prohibited on floodplain properties, so a below-grade suite on a low lot needs a separate bylaw check. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0

## 10. HOW IT COMPARES
- Northwest Maple Ridge has a West Coast Express station inside its working scope; Whonnock, to the east, has no station. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Northwest Maple Ridge is governed by the OCP alone; Albion, to the east, also has its own area plan in OCP Chapter 10.2. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId=
- Both areas are in School District 42, where elementary school is Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary school is Grades 8 to 12. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/
