# New Westminster Lots and Land — Extra Local Notes
Page: new-westminster-lots-land (New Westminster; lots and land).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- New Westminster land is a whole-city market in BC's smallest city by area: a mainland part on the north bank of the Fraser River and Queensborough, on the eastern tip of Lulu Island, across the river and reached by the Queensborough Bridge. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- The board community "New Westminster" is the city itself, with 14 sub-areas from Brunette and Sapperton in the east to Queensborough across the river; city zoning, not board lines, decides what a lot can hold. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Attachment_C2___Small_Scale_Multi_Unit_Housing_Legislation_and_Implementation_Summary.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- New Westminster lots are fee-simple house lots in an almost fully built-out city; the land trade is teardown, infill and assembly lots, not greenfield subdivision. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- Since June 2026 the infill frame is city-wide: zones RGO-A (up to four units) and RGO-B (up to six units) permit infill housing on single detached and duplex properties across most of the city without a site-specific rezoning. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- Townhouse ground sits in the RT districts of Zoning Bylaw No. 6680 (2001): RT-2, RT-2A, RT-2B, RT-2C and RT-3, RT-3A, RT-3B only — there are no RT-A or RT-B districts in that bylaw. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Attachment_C2___Small_Scale_Multi_Unit_Housing_Legislation_and_Implementation_Summary.pdf
- Subdivision and servicing run under Bylaw 7142, 2007; parcel-level works and servicing conditions are confirmed with the City for the specific parcel. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH = small-scale multi-unit housing) needed little change here: most New Westminster residential properties already permitted a house plus a secondary suite and an accessory dwelling unit (a laneway or carriage house), so their zoning was not a provincial "restricted zone." https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Attachment_C2___Small_Scale_Multi_Unit_Housing_Legislation_and_Implementation_Summary.pdf
- The exception was duplex ground: Bylaw No. 8453, 2024 rezoned the roughly 160 "restricted zone" duplex properties to a new RD-1 district that permits duplexes to add rental secondary suites. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Attachment_C2___Small_Scale_Multi_Unit_Housing_Legislation_and_Implementation_Summary.pdf
- Queensborough runs on a different clock: the Province extended Queensborough's SSMUH compliance date to May 4, 2029, so the mainland infill frame and the Queensborough frame are not the same at the same time. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Attachment_C2___Small_Scale_Multi_Unit_Housing_Legislation_and_Implementation_Summary.pdf
- A secondary suite cannot be strata-titled, sold separately or used for a short-term rental, and every property with a suite pays annual secondary suite water and sewer charges. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Secondary_Suite_Guide_August_2025.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** How many homes can one New Westminster lot hold?
**A:** Up to six, depending on the lot's zone. In June 2026 the City adopted Official Community Plan and Zoning Bylaw amendments permitting up to six units of infill housing — secondary suites, laneway and carriage houses, triplexes, quadplexes and other multiplex forms — on single detached and duplex properties across most of the city, under RGO-A (up to four units) and RGO-B (up to six units), with no site-specific rezoning. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects

**Q:** Can I subdivide a New Westminster lot?
**A:** Subdivision is the narrow route here, not the main one. Subdivision and servicing run under Bylaw 7142, 2007 on top of Zoning Bylaw No. 6680 (2001), and most added density now comes from the RGO infill zones on the existing lot rather than from splitting it. Confirm the parcel's zone and its servicing conditions with the City before pricing a split. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects

**Q:** Is a Queensborough lot under the same rules as a mainland lot?
**A:** Not on the same timeline. Queensborough sits across the Fraser River in floodplain Zone 1, and the Province extended its SSMUH compliance date to May 4, 2029, while the mainland runs on the June 2026 RGO frame. Price and plan a Queensborough lot on its own floodplain zone and its own infill timeline, never on a mainland comparable's rules. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Attachment_C2___Small_Scale_Multi_Unit_Housing_Legislation_and_Implementation_Summary.pdf

**Q:** Can a suite on the lot be sold or rented short-term?
**A:** No. The City's Secondary Suite Guide states suites cannot be strata-titled, sold or used for short-term rentals, and all properties with suites are subject to annual secondary suite water and sewer charges. A suite adds rental use inside the one title; it never creates a second title or a short-term rental right. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Secondary_Suite_Guide_August_2025.pdf

**Q:** Which floodplain zone is my lot in?
**A:** It depends on the sub-area, and the zones are never blended: Queensborough is Zone 1, the North Arm is Zone 2, the Quay and Downtown are Zone 3, and Brunette is Zone 4. Confirm the parcel's zone before subjects come off, because flood requirements follow the zone, not the city's average position. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Columbia Station is the Expo Line branch point, so a Sapperton or Braid lot and a 22nd Street lot ride different branches of the same line into downtown. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/skytrain/?page=1
- Royal Columbian Hospital at 330 E Columbia St is the only hospital in the Lower Mainland immediately adjacent to a SkyTrain station (Sapperton), which is part of why Sapperton lots carry assembly interest. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/New-Westminster/royal-columbian-hospital#.XSdr3flKhGM
- Suites are not free to carry: the City's user-fee schedule sets an annual charge for a secondary suite ($652.72 basic rate in the Engineering User Fees and Rates Bylaw schedule, as amended by Bylaw No. 8496, 2024) on top of the main dwelling's charge. https://pub-newwestcity.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=20265
- Application review currently runs under an Interim Development Review Framework, first endorsed in 2024 and updated in June 2026, while provincially driven policy changes are underway — expect review practice to keep moving. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- School District No. 40 (SD40) catchments are address-based and no catchment lookup for a specific lot address is on record in this program: run the exact address with SD40 before marketing a school. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Secondary_Suite_Guide_August_2025.pdf
Read: 2026-11-03

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- New Westminster's total 2026 residential tax rate is 4.79312 per $1,000 of assessed value on the City's 2026 mill-rate schedule, with the municipal portion at about 3.10387 per $1,000. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/26_CNW_Mill_Rates_.pdf
- 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 3, 2026. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php
- The City's user-fee schedule sets an annual utility user charge of $1,305.46 (basic rate) for a single detached dwelling and $652.72 for a secondary suite. https://pub-newwestcity.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=20265
- New Westminster is inside the Metro Vancouver taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so owners file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The Expo Line serves New Westminster at five stations — 22nd Street, New Westminster, Columbia, Sapperton and Braid — and Columbia is where the line branches. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/skytrain/?page=1
- Queensborough lots depend on the Queensborough Bridge to the mainland city and the Pattullo Bridge corridor toward Surrey; there is no SkyTrain station in Queensborough. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/skytrain/?page=1

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Floodplain position is set by sub-area zone — Queensborough Zone 1, North Arm Zone 2, Quay and Downtown Zone 3, Brunette Zone 4 — and the zone decides the flood diligence for the lot; the map does not set the flood construction level for a specific building, which is a parcel-level confirmation with the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- A suite's legality is a title-and-bylaw check, not a listing claim: suites must meet the Zoning Bylaw, the Building Code and the Tree Protection and Regulation Bylaw, and they carry the annual suite charges in §7. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Secondary_Suite_Guide_August_2025.pdf
