# West Newton — Questions for Jared
Area: West Newton, Surrey (detached houses).

## 5 questions only Jared can answer
1. Who is actually buying detached houses in West Newton in your deals — families moving within Newton, buyers coming from Vancouver or Richmond for the lot size, or builders and investors targeting the RA acreage lots along Highway 10 and 128 Street for small-lot subdivision?
2. What sells fast and what sits in West Newton: do the newer RF-10 and RF-13 small-lot houses with coach houses or suites move first, while the older original houses on acreage lots wait for a builder buyer — and what is the one reason a West Newton listing stalls most often?
3. What surprises West Newton buyers most once they are looking seriously — that three different neighbourhood concept plans cover the area, that Princess Margaret, Frank Hurt and Tamanawis catchments split the area three ways, or the Cougar Creek setback on a lot they liked?
4. In your deals, how much does the east–west split matter — houses near the R1 on King George Boulevard and Newton Exchange versus houses near the R6 on Scott Road — in who shows up, how fast it sells, and what buyers will pay for?
5. Tell one West Newton deal story: a sale, a subdivision, or a problem you solved around 128 Street, 72 Avenue or the Highway 10 edge that shows a buyer or seller something no listing or plan map could.
