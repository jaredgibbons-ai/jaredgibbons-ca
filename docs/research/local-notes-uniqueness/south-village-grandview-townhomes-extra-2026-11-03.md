# South Village, Grandview Heights Townhomes — Extra Local Notes
Page: south-village-grandview-townhomes (Grandview Heights, South Surrey; townhomes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- South Village stands on 23A Avenue at 164 Street, south of 24 Avenue, inside the Sunnyside Heights plan area. https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp
- The Sunnyside Heights Neighbourhood Concept Plan (NCP) area is bordered by 168 Street to the east, 24 Avenue to the north and the Highway 99 Corridor Plan to the west, with the protected Fergus Watershed Biodiversity Preserve to the south. https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp
- The plan area covers about 153 hectares (379 acres) and is the second neighbourhood plan area planned within Grandview Heights. https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp
- Area-wide Grandview Heights plan facts are in full in the grandview-south-surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the grandview-south-surrey-houses-for-sale note.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The municipal record for this site is an assembled four-parcel approval: 2348-164 Street plus 16437, 16457 and 16467-23 Avenue, rezoned to permit a 74-unit townhouse development. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_RCLU_2013-12-16.pdf
- Council approved that rezoning on July 22, 2013, changing the Official Community Plan (OCP) designation from Suburban to Urban at the same time. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_RCLU_2013-12-16.pdf
- The applicant named in the City minutes is 0966540 B.C. Ltd., care of Legendary Developments Ltd., not the builder name used in later marketing — see NOT CONFIRMED for the builder attribution. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_RCLU_2013-12-16.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- South Village's zone is Multiple Residential 30 (RM-30) under Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000, set by Amendment By-law, 2013, No. 18042, with the companion OCP amendment in By-law No. 18041. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_RCLU_2013-12-16.pdf
- The Sunnyside Heights land-use designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update, so a rebuild or addition question for this complex is answered from the OCP and the RM-30 zone, not the original NCP. https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** The City approved 74 townhomes here, so why do listings describe a smaller complex? **A:** The 74-unit figure is the whole assembled site. City minutes for application 7913-0065-00 cover four parcels — 2348-164 Street and 16437, 16457 and 16467-23 Avenue — rezoned RA to RM-30 for a 74-unit townhouse development. A single strata phase at one civic address is only part of that approval, so compare a unit against its own strata plan, not the 74-unit site total. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_RCLU_2013-12-16.pdf
- **Q:** Which elementary school is within walking distance of South Village? **A:** Edgewood Elementary at 16666 23 Avenue is the elementary school in this pocket, a few blocks east on the same avenue system. The British Columbia school list records it in School District 36 (Surrey). Proximity is not catchment: confirm 16467 23A Avenue in the district school locator before writing an offer that depends on Edgewood. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** What is south of South Village if I keep walking? **A:** Protected watershed land, not more housing. The Sunnyside Heights plan area ends at the Fergus Watershed Biodiversity Preserve, which the City names as the plan's southern boundary. That preserve edge is why the streets in this pocket stop at the plan boundary instead of continuing south as a grid. https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp
- **Q:** Who actually built South Village — the City file names a different company? **A:** The approval and the builder are two different records. City minutes name the 2013 applicant as 0966540 B.C. Ltd., care of Legendary Developments Ltd., for the rezoning. Later marketing names Red Boutique Properties as the builder of the townhomes and of a separate row-home section. No page on Red's own site confirming the complex was found in this pass, so verify the builder warranty record in the strata documents. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_RCLU_2013-12-16.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Edgewood Park, a City park at 2284-165 Street, is the neighbourhood park for this pocket, east of the complex. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/edgewood-park
- Ta'talu Elementary at 1970 165A Street is a second, newer elementary school in the wider Sunnyside Heights pocket, recorded in the British Columbia school list for School District 36. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- Council amended the Sunnyside Heights plan in December 2017, changing land uses in the south portion of the plan area, so two homes a few streets apart here can sit under different plan vintages. https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp
- The Grandview Heights Aquatic Centre at 16855-24 Avenue, with a 10-lane, 50-metre FINA-standard (International Swimming Federation standard) competition pool, is this pocket's recreation anchor at the top of 168 Street. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/grandview-heights-aquatic-centre?viewTab=2

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check any lot or strata parcel near the south edge against the Fergus Watershed Biodiversity Preserve boundary: the City names the preserve as the Sunnyside Heights plan boundary, and land beside a protected watershed can carry riparian and tree-protection conditions a listing will not state. https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp
- Read the depreciation report and minutes before subjects: strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report under British Columbia's strata framework, and disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- Confirm which approval phase a unit belongs to: the City file covers a 74-unit, four-parcel site, so a unit's strata plan may cover only part of the land the rezoning describes. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_RCLU_2013-12-16.pdf
