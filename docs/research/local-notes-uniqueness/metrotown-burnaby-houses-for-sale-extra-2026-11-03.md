# Metrotown Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: metrotown-burnaby-houses-for-sale (Metrotown, Burnaby; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Metrotown is Burnaby's downtown and its regional city centre, serving the southwest quadrant of the city. The City of Burnaby states Metrotown is the largest of its four town centres — Metrotown, Brentwood, Lougheed and Edmonds — and is designated Burnaby's downtown in the Metrotown Downtown Plan (2017). https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- The detached and low-rise housing sits inside that downtown plan area, in the Maywood neighbourhood. City rezoning reports place Maywood addresses on Telford Avenue, Silver Avenue, Sussex Avenue and Willingdon Avenue inside the Metrotown Downtown Plan area, south and west of the Metropolis at Metrotown core. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=49857
- Maywood is described by the City as the residential heart of Metrotown, in the report for 6620 Sussex Avenue. The same report states the Maywood neighbourhood is intended to be the residential "heart" of Metrotown, with opportunities to live, gather and socialize, which is the plan role the low-rise streets around Maywood Park carry. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=52009
- Silver Avenue's plan edge is Maywood Park, because the City's 2026 report for 6470 and 6508 Silver Avenue states the site is located along Silver Avenue across from Maywood Park. A house or low-rise lot on that block therefore faces a park frontage inside a High-Rise Apartment 1 designation. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/media/Hpo/_Data/_CouncilMinutesAndReports/Unrestricted/2026/05.26/95553.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The Maywood stock a house buyer actually sees is mostly older low-rise rental and strata buildings on residential-district lots, not detached houses on large lots. At 6450, 6486, 6488 and 6508 Telford Avenue, the City lists a three-storey, 33-unit strata apartment at 6450 Telford (Multiple Family Residential District RM3), a two-storey, eight-unit rental apartment at 6486 Telford (Residential District R5), a three-storey, five-unit strata apartment at 6488 Telford (RM3), and a vacant R5 lot at 6508 Telford. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=49857
- The Willingdon Avenue stock is 1960s walk-up rental: the City states the site at 6622 and 6688 Willingdon Avenue is improved with two three-storey rental apartment buildings with a total of 72 units, constructed in the 1960s. The report places that site in the Maywood neighbourhood area of the Metrotown Downtown Plan. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=52541
- Sussex Avenue carries the same era of stock, because the City states 6620 Sussex Avenue is improved with a 53-unit, two- to three-storey low-rise rental apartment building constructed in 1965, on five legal lots under a single civic address. Its prevailing zoning at the time of the report was RM3 Multiple Family Residential District. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=52009
- Silver Avenue's current buildings are also low-rise rental, because the City's 2026 report for 6470 and 6508 Silver Avenue states the two lots are improved with low-rise residential rental buildings containing 83 rental units. Those 83 units are the existing stock on a site designated High-Rise Apartment 1 in the Official Community Plan (OCP). https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/media/Hpo/_Data/_CouncilMinutesAndReports/Unrestricted/2026/05.26/95553.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The plan that governs these streets has been partly superseded, because the City states Burnaby City Council gave final reading and adoption to the Burnaby 2050 OCP on December 9, 2025, and that supported future land use in the new OCP Land Use Map now takes precedent over individual neighbourhood-wide community plan land use maps. Where a community plan such as the Metrotown Downtown Plan does not align with the OCP, the City states the OCP is the most up-to-date direction. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- Inside the plan area, designation decides what a low-rise lot can become: the City's Silver Avenue report states the 6470 and 6508 Silver Avenue site is designated High-Rise Apartment 1 within the OCP, and its current zoning is RM3 Multiple Family Residential District, with a proposed Comprehensive Development (CD) district based on the R7 High-Rise Apartment 1 District and the Metrotown Downtown Plan as guidelines. Existing zoning and plan designation are therefore two different numbers on the same Maywood lot. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/media/Hpo/_Data/_CouncilMinutesAndReports/Unrestricted/2026/05.26/95553.pdf
- Rental replacement rules shape any redevelopment of the Maywood walk-ups, because City reports for Maywood rezonings cite the Rental Use Zoning Policy (2020) and Tenant Assistance Policy (2020) among the adopted policies the proposal must align with. A buyer of a tenanted low-rise building on Telford, Sussex or Willingdon carries those two policies in the file from the start. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=52541
- Mixed-designation lots have a stated density rule: the City's Sussex Avenue report states that, per the Metrotown Downtown Plan, consolidated sites with multiple residential designations are to have their densities calculated consistently with the relative proportions of the land use designations that comprise the consolidated site. A Maywood assembly does not get the highest designation applied to the whole parcel. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=52009
- Burnaby-wide zoning, tax and utility basics: see the vancouver-heights-burnaby-houses-for-sale note.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Are there still detached houses in Metrotown, or is it all towers?
**A:** The plan area's residential streets in Maywood are mainly low-rise buildings on small residential lots, based on the City's own site descriptions. On Telford Avenue the City lists R5 Residential District lots holding a two-storey, eight-unit rental building and a vacant lot beside RM3 strata walk-ups, so the ground-oriented stock here is small multi-unit buildings and single lots inside a downtown plan, not a detached-house suburb. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=49857

**Q:** What does the Metrotown Downtown Plan mean for a house on Silver Avenue?
**A:** It means the lot's future is set by its OCP designation, which for 6470 and 6508 Silver Avenue is High-Rise Apartment 1, across from Maywood Park. The City's 2026 report for that site recommends advancing a rezoning for 32-storey and 28-storey apartment buildings to first and second reading, so a low-rise property on this street sits in an active high-rise rezoning corridor. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/media/Hpo/_Data/_CouncilMinutesAndReports/Unrestricted/2026/05.26/95553.pdf

**Q:** Which elementary school serves the Maywood streets?
**A:** Maywood Community School, at 4567 Imperial Street, is the School District No. 41 (Burnaby) elementary school listed by the Province for the Maywood area, at Kindergarten to Grade 7. Chaffey-Burke Elementary at 4404 Sardis Street is the other district elementary school listed on the east side of Metrotown, so the exact Maywood address should be run through the district's catchment process before a buyer relies on either name. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/burnaby

**Q:** If a Maywood rental building is redeveloped, what happens to tenants?
**A:** Burnaby's Rental Use Zoning Policy and Tenant Assistance Policy are the two adopted policies City reports apply to Maywood rezonings, including the Willingdon Avenue and Telford Avenue applications. Those policies, cited in the City's own rezoning reports for these streets, set the rental replacement and tenant assistance framework a redevelopment proposal is measured against, which matters to both building buyers and sitting tenants. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=52541

**Q:** How fast is the SkyTrain from Metrotown Station?
**A:** TransLink's Expo Line timetable gives station-to-station times from Metrotown of 2 minutes to Patterson, 20 minutes to Waterfront on the Waterfront–King George line, and 1 minute to Royal Oak eastbound. Metrotown sits between Patterson and Royal Oak on that line, which is why both the Maywood streets west of the station and the Sussex Avenue streets east of it use the same station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt991.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Metropolis at Metrotown is listed by the City as a Metrotown anchor, alongside the Hilton Vancouver Metrotown, Holiday Inn Express Metrotown, Element by Westin and head or corporate offices including Interfor, TELUS and Sun Life Financial. The downtown's office and hotel stock sits in the same town centre as the Maywood housing streets. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Metrotown has Burnaby's first pedestrian scramble crossing, at the intersection of Central Boulevard and the Metrotown underground parkade access, near Metrotown SkyTrain station. The City states the scramble improves safety and movement through the area, which is the main walking route between the station, the mall and the Maywood streets. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects-direct-link
- A district energy utility is planned to serve this town centre first: the City states it is creating a District Energy Utility using thermal energy from Metro Vancouver's Waste-to-Energy facility in south Burnaby, with service slated to be introduced in Metrotown and Edmonds, where almost half of Burnaby's residential growth over the next 20 years is expected. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/climate-action/resilient-neighbourhoods
- The City has considered moving City Hall into this town centre, because Council identified Metrotown as the preferred location for a new City Hall, as it is designated Burnaby's downtown in the 2017 Metrotown Downtown Plan and a regional city centre under Metro Vancouver's Regional Growth Strategy. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2023-05-31/burnaby-seeking-input-location-new-city-hall-metrotown-area
- Maywood Park is a plan landmark, not just a local park: the City's Silver Avenue report locates the 6470 and 6508 Silver Avenue site across from Maywood Park, and the Sussex Avenue report calls Maywood the residential heart of Metrotown. Houses and walk-ups facing Maywood Park face the neighbourhood the plan is organized around. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=52009
- Growth is concentrated here by policy: the City's town centre table projects Metrotown growing from 25,800 people in 2011 to 66,800 in 2041, and from 13,300 dwelling units in 2021 to 31,800 in 2041, the largest of the four town centres. Those figures are the City's OCP projections, stated as general directions and targets. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Metrotown Station is on the Expo Line between Patterson and Royal Oak, and TransLink's timetable lists both the Waterfront–King George and Waterfront–Production Way–University services through it. The same timetable gives Metrotown to Waterfront as 20 minutes station to station, which is the rail baseline for every Maywood street. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt991.pdf
- The town centre is served by two SkyTrain lines at the network level, because the City states its town centres have SkyTrain stations served by two lines — Expo and Millennium — and high-frequency and local bus routes. For Metrotown itself the station is an Expo Line station serving the town centre it is named for. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Walking across Central Boulevard at the station uses the pedestrian scramble, because the City states the Metrotown pedestrian scramble is at Central Boulevard and the Metrotown underground parkade access, near Metrotown SkyTrain station, and is Burnaby's first pedestrian scramble crossing. That crossing is on the route between the station and the Maywood streets south of Central Boulevard. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects-direct-link

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the OCP designation before the community plan, because the City states the Burnaby 2050 OCP, adopted December 9, 2025, takes precedent over individual community plan land use maps where they do not align. A Metrotown Downtown Plan (2017) density figure for a Maywood lot is not the final word after that adoption. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- On a consolidated Maywood site, verify the designation proportions lot by lot, because the City's Sussex Avenue report states densities on consolidated sites with multiple residential designations are calculated from the relative proportions of each designation in the site. An assembly priced on the tallest designation alone is mispriced under the plan's own rule. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=52009
- For any tenanted walk-up on Telford, Sussex, Silver or Willingdon, read the Rental Use Zoning Policy and Tenant Assistance Policy before valuing redevelopment, because City reports for these streets list both as adopted policies the proposal must align with. The existing rental counts are documented in the same reports — 72 units at 6622 and 6688 Willingdon Avenue and 83 units at 6470 and 6508 Silver Avenue. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=52541
- Check the current rezoning file number, not only the address, because the Silver Avenue site has had a predecessor rezoning bylaw: the City's 2026 report for REZ #25-12 recommends abandoning Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, Amendment Bylaw No. 27, 2023 (Bylaw No. 14600) contingent on second reading of the new rezoning bylaw. An older approval reference for 6470 and 6508 Silver Avenue is not the live file. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/media/Hpo/_Data/_CouncilMinutesAndReports/Unrestricted/2026/05.26/95553.pdf
