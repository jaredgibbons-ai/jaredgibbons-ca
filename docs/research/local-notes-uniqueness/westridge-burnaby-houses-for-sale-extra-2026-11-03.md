## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Slope is a planning rule here: Burnaby's 2050 policy guide says steep slopes are managed through Development Permit Areas (DPAs), restricting development on them and varying setbacks at slope tops. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/burnaby-2050-planning-our-city-together/burnaby-2050-policy-guide-share-your-feedback/climate-change-and-natural-hazards
- A geotechnical report is required only if a property is flagged as floodplain or slope instability area, per the City's application form. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2021-07/ERC%20Type%202%20Application%20Form.pdf
- Lots near a stream or ravine can trigger a Streamside Protection Development Permit, needing an Environmental Assessment Report by a Qualified Environmental Professional. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-06/Preparing-Environmental-Assessment-Report.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is the west side of Westridge the same as the east side? **A:** No. Inlet Drive divides the neighbourhood into a 1950s single-family subdivision to the west and a 1970s subdivision with larger lots and some low-rise units to the east, so lot size, house era and street pattern all change at Inlet Drive. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark791
- **Q:** Does the slope change what I can build? **A:** On flagged lots, yes. Burnaby's policy is to restrict development on steep slopes through Development Permit Areas and vary setbacks at slope tops, and the City requires a geotechnical report where a property is flagged for slope instability or floodplain. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/burnaby-2050-planning-our-city-together/burnaby-2050-policy-guide-share-your-feedback/climate-change-and-natural-hazards
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves Westridge? **A:** Westridge Elementary, at 510 Duncan Avenue, kindergarten to Grade 7, is the neighbourhood school on the Province's Burnaby list. Burnaby School District No. 41 (SD41) states that its School Locator finds the school a child can attend based on the home address, so confirm the exact address there. https://burnabyschools.ca/elementary-school-addresses/
- **Q:** What is at Barnet Marine Park, below Westridge? **A:** The City describes it as a foreshore park on Burrard Inlet with a sandy beach, launch facilities for canoes, kayaks and sailing craft, shore fishing with a valid licence, a seasonal cafe, picnic areas, playgrounds, and a designated dog off-leash area. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/barnet-marine-park
