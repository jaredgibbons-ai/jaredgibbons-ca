## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- White Rock's current zoning bylaw is Zoning Bylaw 2024, No. 2506, adopted June 27, 2024 to incorporate small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) regulations required by provincial legislation. https://pub-whiterockcity.escribemeetings.com:443/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11428
- A 2025 housekeeping amendment to Bylaw 2506 states that residential ceiling heights over 5 metres count as two floors in the gross floor area calculation — a rule that reaches double-height top-floor units directly. https://pub-whiterockcity.escribemeetings.com:443/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11428
- The same amendment work adds semi-detached and rowhouse structures to the general regulations and prohibits mechanical equipment projecting into a required setback, both form rules for top-floor and rooftop elements. https://pub-whiterockcity.escribemeetings.com:443/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11428
- White Rock runs two form-based permit areas a penthouse buyer will meet in the documents: the Town Centre Development Permit Area and the Waterfront Development Permit Area. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Does White Rock have a penthouse or view-protection bylaw?
**A:** No penthouse-specific bylaw was found in the City material read. What exists is the general framework: Zoning Bylaw 2024, No. 2506 for height and form, plus the Town Centre and Waterfront Development Permit Areas for design review. A view from a top-floor unit is therefore a matter of the building's own zoning and what is approved next door, checked on the City's development portal, not a protected right. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/512/Development

**Q:** Can a future building block a White Rock penthouse view?
**A:** Check the portal before relying on the view: it lists all active and completed development applications since 2023, is updated daily, and keeps projects visible for about two years after a Council decision. If a taller form is already approved beside the building, the disclosure is there. The City also keeps approved major projects on its Construction Information Portal. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/512/Development

**Q:** Why does a double-height penthouse ceiling matter in White Rock?
**A:** Because the City counts it as floor area: the Bylaw 2506 housekeeping amendment states that a residential ceiling height over 5 metres is considered two floors and is included in calculating residential gross floor area. That affects what alterations or enclosures a top-floor owner can later be permitted, and it is a City rule, not a strata rule, so it applies whatever the bylaws say. https://pub-whiterockcity.escribemeetings.com:443/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11428

**Q:** Surrey side or White Rock side — which rules govern a South Surrey penthouse?
**A:** The municipality the building sits in governs, full stop. White Rock buildings answer to Zoning Bylaw 2024, No. 2506 and White Rock's permit areas; a building a few streets north in Surrey answers to Surrey's zoning bylaw and Surrey's plan areas instead. Confirm the municipality from the civic address and tax notice before applying any height or view statement to a unit. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents
