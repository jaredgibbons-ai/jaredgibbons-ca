# Vedder Crossing Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: vedder-crossing (Vedder Crossing; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Vedder Crossing sits on the north bank of the Vedder River in the City of Chilliwack, at the junction of the Chilliwack and Vedder Rivers at its southern boundary, with Stevenson Road to the north. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/
- Vedder Road is the neighbourhood's spine: it carries traffic from Sardis south across the Vedder River toward Cultus Lake, and the commercial corner of Vedder Road and Keith Wilson Road anchors the area's shops. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/
- The neighbourhood is built on the former Canadian Forces Base (CFB) Chilliwack: the base was established about 1941 between Watson Road and Keith Wilson Road and closed in 1997, and its lands became the Garrison Crossing subdivision and the Canada Education Park. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/
- South of Vedder Road the City plans the adjoining district as the South Vedder Neighbourhood (Official Community Plan Schedule B-6), bordered by the Chilliwack/Vedder River to the south, Promontory to the south-east and Ch'iyáqtel (Tzeachten) First Nation lands to the east, with Vedder Road as its western boundary. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6734

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The housing is planned, newer stock rather than an old townsite grid: Garrison Crossing is a residential subdivision built on the former base lands, and Canada Education Park covers 200 acres of the 400-acre former base for institutional, commercial and residential development. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/
- On the campus side of Vedder Road, cafés and commercial uses sit under upper-storey residential along the south part of Vedder Road, with more commercial space at Vedder Road and Keith Wilson Road. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/
- The neighbourhood's schools are on the Vedder side: Vedder Elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 5) at 45850 Promontory Road, Vedder Middle School (Grades 6–8) at 45560 South Sumas Road, and G.W. Graham Secondary (Grades 9–12) at 45955 Thomas Road, all in Chilliwack School District (SD33). https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/chilliwack

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Land use south of the river crossing follows the South Vedder Neighbourhood Plan, Schedule B-6 of Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 (adopted April 2022, updated 2025); the City's plan envisions ground-level commercial buildings of 4 storeys, mixed commercial-residential buildings of 6 storeys, and residential buildings of 3–6 storeys along the Vedder Road corridor. https://www.engagechilliwack.com/southvedder/widgets/95165/faqs
- The Vedder River floodplain is designated floodplain under Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519, so flood construction levels apply to new building near the river — see the chilliwack-proper-south note for the full bylaw treatment. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- Part of the former base is still military: the Chilcotin Training Area (the OPSEE Area — Operator Special Engineering Equipment Training Area) remains operational for Primary Reserve field training and firing ranges beside the redeveloped lands. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What is the Canada Education Park next door? **A:** It is the education campus built on the former CFB Chilliwack lands after the base closed in 1998: the University of the Fraser Valley (UFV), the RCMP Pacific Region Training Centre and the Canada Border Services Agency are partners there, on 200 acres of the old 400-acre base beside the Vedder River. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/
- **Q:** Which schools serve Vedder Crossing? **A:** Vedder Elementary at 45850 Promontory Road for Kindergarten to Grade 5, Vedder Middle School at 45560 South Sumas Road for Grades 6–8, and G.W. Graham Secondary at 45955 Thomas Road for Grades 9–12 — but SD33 assigns by street-address catchment, so confirm the exact address with the district. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/chilliwack
- **Q:** Is there riverside trail access from the neighbourhood? **A:** Yes. The Vedder River Rotary Trail runs about 15 kilometres along the Vedder River, and the Garrison Crossing Legacy Trail links north through the neighbourhood to the Trans Canada Trail along the river, with an east-west pedestrian spine through Canada Education Park. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/
- **Q:** How do I get to Highway 1 or downtown without driving? **A:** BC Transit Route 51 (Vedder) runs from Downtown Chilliwack down Vedder Road to the UFV Chilliwack campus in Canada Education Park — it is the neighbourhood's own route — and Route 66 Fraser Valley Express runs from the Downtown Exchange to Abbotsford, Langley and Lougheed Station. https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/
- **Q:** Is the Vedder River a flood risk for these homes? **A:** The river is a designated floodplain under Bylaw 4519, which sets flood construction levels for new construction; the City's guide requires habitable floors at the flood construction level plus 0.6 metres of freeboard in the general floodplain area. An existing home's build level is a building-file question, not something the neighbourhood page shows. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- UFV preserved the base's saluting base in its Parade Square, repainted with commemorative plaques and metal fixtures spelling "ubique" — the military history is built into the campus landscaping, not erased. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/
- The Cheam Leisure Centre sits inside Garrison Crossing, giving the neighbourhood its own pool and recreation centre rather than a drive to Sardis. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/
- BC Transit added later weekday and weekend service and more Saturday evening trips on Route 51 from January 4, 2026, because the UFV run is the corridor's main route. https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/
- G.W. Graham Secondary had a 450-seat addition completed in the early 2020s, bringing capacity to about 1,400 seats — growth pressure on the Vedder-side schools is a standing SD33 capital issue. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/CPL/221125/CPLN221125_A10.pdf
- The City-wide Chilliwack tax and transit facts are carried in full in the chilliwack-proper-south note; this note covers only what is specific to the Vedder Crossing side of the river.

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 51 (Vedder) is the local bus: BC Transit describes the Chilliwack system as running Route 51 from Downtown to the UFV Chilliwack Campus via Vedder Road, which passes directly through Vedder Crossing. https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/
- Vedder Road north leads to Sardis and the Highway 1 interchanges at Vedder Road and Lickman Road; the Route 66 Fraser Valley Express map shows its Chilliwack stops at the Downtown Exchange, Eagle Landing and Lickman Road. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/chw_map_66.pdf
- The trail network is a real second option here: the Trans Canada Trail along the Vedder River and the Garrison Crossing Legacy Trail link the neighbourhood to the river trails on foot and by bike. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check any lot near the river against Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519, Schedule A, which maps the floodplains of the Vedder River and Vedder Canal and sets the flood construction levels for them. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- Dike protection is the working assumption, not a guarantee: the City guide's flood rules for dike-protected ALR areas still require floors 1.2 metres above natural ground with a flood covenant on title, which shows the City prices residual risk into construction even behind dikes. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- Ask whether a Garrison Crossing home's title carries base-era covenants or a building scheme from the Canada Lands Company redevelopment — the whole subdivision is one master-planned project on former federal land. Status of any specific covenant: UNCONFIRMED from the sources read; it is a title-search item.
