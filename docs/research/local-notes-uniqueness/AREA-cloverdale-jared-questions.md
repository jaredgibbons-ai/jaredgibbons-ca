# Cloverdale — Questions for Jared

1. When a Cloverdale farm parcel comes up — say on 176 Street or Colebrook Road — who is actually buying it: working farmers expanding, families wanting a house on acreage, or investors holding ALR land? What split do you see?
2. What surprises Cloverdale farm buyers most after they move in — the Serpentine lowland drainage and ditches, farm traffic and machinery on the roads, or the ALR limits on a second house and subdivision?
3. Which sells faster in Cloverdale in your experience: a smaller A-1 lot near the town centre that a family can live on, or a larger lowland parcel a working farm can use? What sits?
4. Have you seen a Cloverdale deal change or fall apart over an ALC issue — a house over the size cap, a second dwelling, a non-farm use — and what happened?
5. Tell one Cloverdale farm story only you would know: a parcel, a buyer, or a showing that taught you something about this farm belt that no bylaw page would show.
