## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Foreclosure venue follows the land. Section 21 of the Law and Equity Act requires filing in the land's municipality or judicial district, deeming Vancouver and New Westminster the same registry. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol28/consol28/00_96253_01
- The redemption period is six months unless the court orders otherwise, and the court may extend it before an order absolute, under the same Act. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol28/consol28/00_96253_01
- Flood rules follow an as-is purchase: Delta records a permit area protecting development from flood hazards in Ladner East Rural (LV5). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/228964/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which court handles a Ladner foreclosure? **A:** The British Columbia Supreme Court, by petition. The Law and Equity Act ties venue to the land's municipality or judicial district, treating Vancouver and New Westminster as one registry. Confirm the registry named on the petition itself, because that is where the court file, the order nisi and any sale approval application will be heard and recorded. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol28/consol28/00_96253_01
- **Q:** How long can the owner still redeem? **A:** Six months, unless the court orders shorter or longer. The Law and Equity Act sets that default and allows an extension before an order absolute. Check the order nisi in the court file for the actual redemption date and the amount the court has fixed as owing under the mortgage. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol28/consol28/00_96253_01
- **Q:** Is Goods and Services Tax (GST) charged on a court-ordered sale? **A:** It depends on the property and parties. The Canada Revenue Agency treats a creditor's seizure of real property as a deemed sale by the debtor, with a possible rebate for a non-registrant seller. Confirm the tax status in writing before completion, and ask who collects or remits any tax payable on the particular sale being approved. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/gst-hst-rebates/reason-code-7/eligibility.html
- **Q:** Does buying "as is" change the flood checks? **A:** No, they matter more. Delta records flood-proofing covenants on Ladner-area lots and a flood-hazard permit area in Ladner East Rural. A court sale does not waive a covenant already registered on title, so the covenant wording should be read before any offer is written. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/228964/
