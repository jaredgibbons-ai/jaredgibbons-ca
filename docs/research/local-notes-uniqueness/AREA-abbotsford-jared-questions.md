# Abbotsford — Questions for Jared

## Questions only Jared can answer

1. Which Abbotsford pocket do your buyers ask for first — Clearbrook/Mill Lake, West Abbotsford acreages, Matsqui Prairie, or the newer east-side subdivisions — and has that changed in the last couple of years?
2. How do your buyers weigh Abbotsford against Langley and Chilliwack — is it price per square foot, lot size, the commute on Highway 1, or farm and ALR ground they can't find further west?
3. What surprises Abbotsford buyers most after they commit — Matsqui Prairie flood and dike issues, well and septic on acreage properties, airport noise near YXX, or how different the east and west sides of the city feel?
4. How do the Abbotsforward 2050 plan changes and the City Centre densification come up in your deals — do sellers near the core hold out for assembly value, and do buyers ask about it?
5. Tell one Abbotsford deal story that shows what buying here is really like — a floodplain or insurance surprise, an ALR farm deal, or a sale that taught you what to check first in this city.
