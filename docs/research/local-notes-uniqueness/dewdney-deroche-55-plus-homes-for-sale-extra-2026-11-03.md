## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Dewdney-Deroche part of Mission? **A:** No, it is unincorporated FVRD Electoral Area G, even though the mailing address and the board grouping say Mission. That changes who issues permits, who sets zoning and who provides water and garbage services. A buyer should direct land-use questions to the FVRD for the parcel's Parcel Identifier (PID), not to City of Mission counters, and should expect school service to still come from School District 75, which is a separate boundary. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/garbage-recycling/facilities.html
- **Q:** Is Oasis Country Estates a 55+ park? **A:** Not verifiably, on the sources available to this note. The index describes an adult side alongside a family section and states no age number for the adult side, and no park rules document was opened in this pass. Strata law cannot fill the gap, because a manufactured-home park is not a strata corporation. Get the park rules and the tenancy agreement in writing before writing an offer on any home there. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** Which elementary schools serve Dewdney and Deroche? **A:** Each community has its own School District 75 (Mission) elementary school site, and Dewdney Elementary's own district site is dewdney.mpsd.ca. The index and Ministry-derived records in the earlier file give Dewdney Elementary at 37151 Hawkins Pickle Rd and Deroche Elementary at 10340 N Deroche Rd, but catchment for a specific home was not run in this pass, so confirm the exact address with the district before relying on either school. https://dewdney.mpsd.ca/
- **Q:** Where does garbage go if there is no city collection contract for my lot? **A:** The FVRD lists the Sylvester Road Transfer Station at 10033 Sylvester Road, Dewdney, serving Electoral Areas F and G, open Saturday, Sunday and Monday from 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. on the page read. It also lists the Mission Landfill as a facility for Area G. Fees on that page include $2.00 per bag not exceeding 0.15 cubic metres, so a rural buyer should budget for self-haul or a private hauler where collection does not apply. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/garbage-recycling/facilities/sylvester-road-transfer-station.html?media=contrast
- **Q:** Is transit available for a senior without a car here? **A:** BC Transit's Central Fraser Valley page states handyDART is an accessible door-to-door service for people with disabilities that prevent using fixed-route transit without assistance, and that system is the operator page read for the Mission-Abbotsford area. No fixed-route stop, route number or frequency for a Dewdney or Deroche address was confirmed from a BC Transit route page in this pass, so a car-free plan is UNCONFIRMED for a specific lot. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/
