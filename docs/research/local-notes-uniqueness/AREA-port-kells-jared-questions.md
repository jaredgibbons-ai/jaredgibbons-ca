# Port Kells — Questions for Jared
Area: Port Kells (Surrey). First program page: port-kells-surrey-houses-for-sale.

## Questions (voice memo)
1. When a buyer asks about a Port Kells house, what do they actually want — the rural lot, the highway access, or a home-based business setup?
2. How do buyers react when they see the mix of houses beside industrial yards and truck traffic on 192 Street and 88 Avenue?
3. What comes up in deals here that never comes up in other Surrey areas — wells, septic, ALR edges, rail noise, flood paperwork?
4. Who buys in Port Kells, and what do they do with the land once they own it?
5. Tell me about one Port Kells deal that taught you something about the area.

## Shot list
- The Highway 1 and 176 Street interchange from the 88 Avenue side — wide shot showing how close the houses sit to the freeway corridor.
- Tynehead Regional Park's open meadows — ground-level shot down the park path to show the green edge beside the subdivision streets.
- A Port Kells farm/industrial side street off 192 Street — street-level shot showing the house-and-yard mix a buyer will actually see.
