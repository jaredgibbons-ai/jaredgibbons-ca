# Sunshine Hills / Woods Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: sunshine-hills-woods-guide (Sunshine Hills / Woods, North Delta; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sunshine Hills and Sunshine Woods are the treed streets around Bond Boulevard and Watershed Park, between 72 Avenue, 64 Avenue and Scott Road (120 Street); street-level detail is in the sunshine-hills-woods-north-delta-houses-for-sale note — see the sunshine-hills-woods-north-delta-houses-for-sale note. https://www.delta.ca:443/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search?page=8
- The City's own naming is layered: its tree-protection work lists Sunshine, Sunshine Woods, Sunshine Hills, Sunshine Village, Westview and Cougar Canyon as separate neighbourhoods from the 2011 North Delta Area Plan. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-sunshine-hills-neighbourhood-tree-protection

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Tree protection is the live local rule: in March 2026 Council directed staff to the next engagement phase of a Sunshine Hills Neighbourhood Tree Protection Pilot Program, after 2,693 properties were surveyed and 564 owners responded. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-sunshine-hills-neighbourhood-tree-protection
- If adopted, the pilot's bylaw amendments would be reviewed after two years; Cougar Canyon, Sunshine Hills and Scottsdale showed the highest interest in taking part. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-sunshine-hills-neighbourhood-tree-protection
- Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) also applies on these lots, so a coach house or garden suite proposal and the tree rules can land on the same lot at once. https://letstalk.delta.ca/ssmuh

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve Sunshine Hills? **A:** Verify the exact address in the district locator. Sunshine Hills Elementary is in the neighbourhood at 11285 Bond Boulevard, and Seaquam Secondary, in the Sunshine Hills community, is its secondary school and home of the district's International Baccalaureate (IB) program; catchment remains address-based, so confirm before you buy. https://se.deltasd.bc.ca/our-school/about-our-school/
- **Q:** Can I remove the big trees on a lot? **A:** Ask the City before assuming yes. Council launched the tree-protection pilot process in October 2025 because of concern about mature canopy loss in Sunshine Hills, and owners in Cougar Canyon, Sunshine Hills and Scottsdale are now being asked whether they support bylaw amendments for their neighbourhoods. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-sunshine-hills-neighbourhood-tree-protection
- **Q:** What is the Watershed, exactly? **A:** It is Delta's largest park, at 11600 Kittson Parkway, with trails and a viewpoint, and its streams host an annual release of 25,000 chum salmon fry each April with tree planting and exhibitors including the Cougar Creek Streamkeepers. Lots backing onto it trade a park boundary for stream and trail rules. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/events-calendar/watershed-fish-release
- **Q:** Where is the nearest big grocery and mall shopping? **A:** On Scott Road, at the area's east edge. The Delta Walmart on that corridor is store #1207 at 7155 120 Street; no City page read in this pass names a shopping centre inside the Sunshine Hills grid itself, so daily shopping is a Scott Road trip. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-sunshine-hills-neighbourhood-tree-protection

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Seaquam Secondary students from Sunshine Hills Elementary walk to the secondary school for orientation — the two schools are close enough that the district treats the trip as a walking field trip needing no consent form. https://sh.deltasd.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/5/2019/06/Seaquam-Orientation.pdf
- Burns Bog, the largest raised bog on the west coast of the Americas, sits north of this area; over 2,300 hectares of the bog and Delta Nature Reserve were added to Canada's protected areas database in 2025. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/burns-bog-and-delta-nature-reserve-added-canadas-protected
- The City has built nearly 500 dams in Burns Bog to raise the water table and reduce wildfire risk — the bog's fire risk is managed, not absent. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-commemorates-20th-anniversary-burns-bog-purchase
- Cougar Canyon and Watershed Park are named by the City among Delta's key natural areas, alongside the Delta Nature Reserve and Burns Bog. https://admin.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2022-08/Natural%20Areas%20-Yard%20Waste%202022_0.pdf
- Survey invitations for the tree pilot went to 2,693 properties across seven North Delta neighbourhoods, so a Sunshine Hills buyer is voting on rules alongside Westview, Cougar Canyon and Scottsdale owners. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-sunshine-hills-neighbourhood-tree-protection

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta's 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after that date and another 5 percent after September 2, 2026; Council's 2026 plan set a 2.9 percent City tax increase. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- For 2026, Delta's flat-rate utility bill for a single-family home is $1,658, up $51 from 2025, covering garbage and recycling plus water and sewer where not metered separately. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Delta is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT), so residential owners must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Scott Road (120 Street) is the transit spine: the R6 Scott Road RapidBus links Scott Road Station and Newton Exchange every 7.5 minutes at peak and 15 minutes off-peak, from 5 a.m. to 1 a.m. on weekdays. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus
- Route 340 runs from Scottsdale Exchange to 22nd Street Station via 64 Avenue, Kittson Parkway and Highway 91 over the Alex Fraser Bridge, along this area's south edge. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Ravine and stream rules are the lot-level check here: Cougar Canyon and the Watershed's streams mean setbacks and tree retention can limit a building envelope, and the pending tree-protection pilot could add rules after you buy. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-sunshine-hills-neighbourhood-tree-protection
- Wildfire interface is a real, managed risk beside Burns Bog and the canyon forests; the City's bog dams exist specifically to mitigate it. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-commemorates-20th-anniversary-burns-bog-purchase
