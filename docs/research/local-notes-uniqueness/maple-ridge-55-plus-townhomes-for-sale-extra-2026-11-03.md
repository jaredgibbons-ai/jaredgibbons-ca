## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Haney Pioneer Village, 11830 230 Street, is an 84-unit townhouse co-operative for active seniors aged 60-plus, with one- and two-bedroom units, private back yards and a recreation hall laundry room; it is 60-plus, never 55-plus, and members hold co-operative membership, not a strata lot. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- St. George's Village Seniors Housing Co-operative, 52–23580 Dewdney Trunk Road, is the city's one verified 55-plus co-operative; its own membership application states the age qualification for membership is 55 years, a dependent aged 19 or older may live with a member, and the co-operative has clear title to its land. http://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/

- The Maples Housing Co-operative at 21570 Cherrington Avenue has 31 semi-detached homes between Dewdney Trunk Road and Lougheed Highway and no seniors designation in any source checked. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Only a 55-plus age bylaw is lawful in a British Columbia strata, under sections 123.1 and 123.2 of the Strata Property Act; any bylaw set below 55 became invalid on November 24, 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- The age bylaw restricts who may live in a unit, not who may own it, so a person under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55-plus corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Exemptions in Strata Property Regulation 7.01 cover a resident who lived in the unit when the bylaw passed, a live-in caregiver, and, from May 1, 2023, a younger spouse or partner and children in the specified resident's care. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Co-operative housing at Haney Pioneer Village and St. George's Village is not strata law at all: occupancy follows the co-operative's membership rules and share purchase, and there is no Form B Information Certificate. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I buy a 55-plus townhouse in Maple Ridge? **A:** No strata townhouse purchase with a verified 55-plus bylaw was found anywhere in the city. The ground-oriented seniors options are co-operatives: St. George's Village at 23580 Dewdney Trunk Road, which is 55-plus by its own membership form, and Haney Pioneer Village at 11830 230 Street, which is 60-plus. A buyer who must hold strata title should look at the Fraserview Village apartments covered on the 55-plus condos page. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q:** Is Haney Pioneer Village a 55-plus complex? **A:** No, its age bar is higher, at 60-plus, on every source checked, including the Co-operative Housing Federation of BC listing that describes an 84-unit townhouse complex for active seniors aged 60-plus. Calling it 55-plus would misstate the rule a 56-year-old buyer would face. It is also a co-operative, so a purchase is a membership and share transaction under the co-operative's rules, not a strata lot purchase with a Form B. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q:** My partner is under 55. Could we live in a 55-plus strata here? **A:** Yes, if a 55-plus strata is the purchase, because provincial exemptions cover a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement, as well as live-in caregivers and legacy residents who lived in the unit when the bylaw passed. That exemption is a Strata Property Act rule, so it helps in Fraserview Village apartments; it does not change a co-operative's own membership age rule at St. George's Village or Haney Pioneer Village. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** Are the Alouette townhomes on 232 Street age-restricted? **A:** No age restriction was found for Alouette Estates at 11870 232 Street in any source checked in this program. The similar name is the trap: the Alouette Retirement Mobile Home Park at 12868 229 Street is the 55-plus property, and it is a manufactured-home park where a resident owns the home and rents the pad. A buyer should confirm the exact civic address and tenure before assuming any Alouette property is 55-plus. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
