# Canyon Springs Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: canyon-springs-coquitlam-houses-for-sale (Canyon Springs; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Canyon Springs is a small board sub-area on the south slope below Burke Mountain, around Walton Avenue and the streets south of David Avenue — and the City publishes no boundary for it. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- The fixed point inside the pocket is Walton Elementary at 2960 Walton Avenue, a School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) (SD43) kindergarten to Grade 5 school on Walton Avenue. https://catalogue.data.gov.bc.ca/dataset/bc-schools-k-12-with-francophone-indicators
- Leigh Elementary at 1230 Soball Street sits just north of David Avenue at the top of this slope, which is why David Avenue is the working north edge used for this page. https://catalogue.data.gov.bc.ca/dataset/bc-schools-k-12-with-francophone-indicators
- Nothing in this note is about Balmoral House or Kirkstone Gardens; those buildings were verified in an earlier program to be in Lynn Valley, North Vancouver, not in Coquitlam's Canyon Springs.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses here are the detached stock on the Walton Avenue side streets below David Avenue, under the City's small-scale residential zones. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId
- Subdivision minimums that shape any rebuild or split here are 650 square metres with 18.5 metres of width in zones R-1 and R-3, and 465 square metres with 12.5 metres of width in R-2, under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId
- No City, SD43 or BC Assessment public page read for this note states a typical build decade, typical lot size or named subdivision for Canyon Springs houses, so check the individual lot on the City zoning viewer and the title. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The rule that matters most on this slope is position, because lots here sit between David Avenue above and the creek and slope lands below. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- City-wide small-scale multi-unit housing rules are in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; the Canyon Springs angle is the City's own early-check list — a creek on or next to the lot, a steep slope, or a house built before 1945 each mean contact Development Services before designing. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- Land within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of a watercourse needs a Watercourse Protection Development Permit before land is altered, which is the permit to price into any creek-edge lot here. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Where exactly is Canyon Springs? **A:** Treat Walton Avenue as the centre, not a City line. The City of Coquitlam publishes no neighbourhood plan or boundary for the name Canyon Springs, so the working pocket is the house streets around Walton Elementary at 2960 Walton Avenue, south of David Avenue and below Burke Mountain. Confirm any individual listing's sub-area label against the street address itself. https://catalogue.data.gov.bc.ca/dataset/bc-schools-k-12-with-francophone-indicators
- **Q:** Which elementary school is in the pocket? **A:** Walton Elementary, at 2960 Walton Avenue, is the School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) school physically on Walton Avenue. A school being on the street is not an assignment for your house: district catchment is by permanent home address, so run the exact address in the School District No. 43 School Locator before relying on Walton or on Leigh Elementary at 1230 Soball Street. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** Can I add units to a house lot here? **A:** Possibly, under the small-scale rules in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. The local limits come first: subdivision minimums are 650 square metres in zones R-1 and R-3 and 465 square metres in R-2, and a creek or steep slope on or beside the lot triggers an early call to the City and may need a watercourse permit. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId
- **Q:** How do I get to SkyTrain from here? **A:** By bus or car, not on foot to a station in the pocket. Route 186 runs David Avenue at the top of the slope via Pinetree Way and Pipeline Road to Coquitlam Central Station, which is served by the Millennium Line Evergreen Extension and the West Coast Express. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt186.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Walton Elementary is the pocket's landmark, at 2960 Walton Avenue; school traffic on Walton Avenue is part of daily life on these streets. https://catalogue.data.gov.bc.ca/dataset/bc-schools-k-12-with-francophone-indicators
- Library service for this side of Coquitlam is the Coquitlam Public Library City Centre branch at 1169 Pinetree Way, plus the Library Link mobile service, until the future Burke Mountain Village library opens. https://www.coqlibrary.ca/
- New small-scale builds here must file the City's Small-Scale Residential Fire Flow Requirements Form with the building permit application, confirming available fire flow for the lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- The City applies integrated stormwater management to small-scale residential projects, to manage runoff and protect local creeks — a real design cost on sloped lots draining toward the creek lands below David Avenue. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the creek layer first: any lot within 30 metres of a watercourse top of bank or ravine bank can need a Watercourse Protection Development Permit, and the guide does not set the final setback for a specific lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- Check slope on the City's environmental development permit mapping, which also maps unstable slopes and the interface wildfire risk boundary for the northeast side of the city. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- Check the Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 Part 5 flood and slope siting rules even though this pocket is upland of the mapped river floodplains. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1170/Part-5-General-Regulations-PDF
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status per parcel on Agricultural Land Commission mapping; no ALR land was identified here in the sources read, which is not a parcel clearance. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca
