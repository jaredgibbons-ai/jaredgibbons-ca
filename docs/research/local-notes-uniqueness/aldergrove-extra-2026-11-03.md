# Aldergrove Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: aldergrove (Aldergrove, Township of Langley; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Aldergrove's core is centred on Fraser Highway and 272 Street, in the Township of Langley at the Township's east end. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/Att-B---Aldergrove-CP---Redlined.pdf
- The Aldergrove Core Area is bounded by Bertrand Creek to the north and east, 29 Avenue to the south and 270 Street to the west. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/Att-B---Aldergrove-CP---Redlined.pdf
- The Township's own eastern boundary is 276 Street at the City of Abbotsford, and its southern boundary is the United States border, so Aldergrove is the last Township community before Abbotsford. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The core plan describes residential land here as large-lot single-family homes, with some townhouses and apartments including fourplexes on 30 Avenue west of 272 Street. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/Att-B---Aldergrove-CP---Redlined.pdf
- The named new condo form in the core is The Hart at Alder Grove Town Centre, whose buildings are named for Aldergrove pioneer families, with The Hart the fifth building after Shortreed, Jackman and Vanetta. https://fifthave.ca/the-vibrant-new-hart-of-aldergrove-town-centre/
- Lot size steps up fast outside the core: Township minimums run from 464.5 square metres for R-1B and R-1C urban lots to 8,094 square metres for SR-2 suburban lots, with rural land beyond. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Township-wide zoning, Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH), tax and utility facts are in full in the murrayville note — see the murrayville note. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- Aldergrove's own angle is the core plan: the Aldergrove Community Plan and the Aldergrove Core Area Plan govern the community, and the Township's Official Community Plan update includes an Aldergrove component. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/community-planning.aspx
- Bertrand Creek is a rule, not just a view: it bounds the core to the north and east, and the Township directs applicants to check a property's watercourses in GeoSource before applying. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Much of the land around the core is in the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR), and the Township directs owners to check a specific property's ALR designation in GeoSource. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve an Aldergrove address? **A:** It depends on the exact address. For the sample address 26850 29 Avenue, the School District 35 School Locator returns Parkside Centennial Elementary, Betty Gilbert Middle School and Aldergrove Community Secondary; schools physically in Aldergrove also include Shortreed Community Elementary at 27330 28 Avenue and Coghlan Fundamental Elementary at 4452 256 Street. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- **Q:** Is there a pool and arena in Aldergrove itself? **A:** Yes — at one address. The Aldergrove Credit Union Community Centre at 27032 Fraser Highway holds pools, the Otter Co-op Outdoor Experience waterpark, an ice arena, a fitness centre and an indoor walking track; its arena is NHL-size (National Hockey League regulation size) with 500 seats and opened August 18, 2018. https://www.tol.ca/en/parks-recreation/aldergrove-community-centre.aspx
- **Q:** Can I get to Surrey Central by transit? **A:** Yes, by one express bus, and it takes over an hour. TransLink route 503 runs from 272 Street at Fraser Highway via Langley Centre to Surrey Central Station, and its timetable shows westbound trips of about 84 to 98 minutes; BC Transit route 21 connects the same stop east to Abbotsford, and there is no SkyTrain here. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf
- **Q:** Where is the library and the regional park? **A:** Both are in the community. Aldergrove Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library) is at 26770 29 Avenue, and Aldergrove Regional Park (Metro Vancouver) has its main entrance at 27240 8 Avenue, south of the core toward the border. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/aldergrove-regional-park

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 503 is the spine: TransLink's Aldergrove/Langley – Fraser Hwy Express runs from 272 Street at Fraser Highway to Surrey Central Station via Langley Centre and King George Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf
- BC Transit route 21 (Aldergrove Connector) links Aldergrove with Abbotsford from the same 272 Street at Fraser Highway stop, making this a two-system transfer point. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf
- Highway 1 (Trans-Canada Highway) runs through the Township north of Aldergrove, and Fraser Highway is the direct road west to Langley and east to Abbotsford. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- Driving commute times are UNCONFIRMED — no official source read in this pass publishes them. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve status first on any lot outside the core: much Township land is in the ALR, and the Township directs the check to its GeoSource viewer. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Check watercourses the same way: Bertrand Creek bounds the core, and riparian setbacks can remove part of a creek-side lot from the buildable area. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Match the lot to its zone minimum before pricing a split: Township minimums span 464.5 square metres to 8,094 square metres depending on zone, and the rural zone minimums could not be confirmed from a readable bylaw section in this pass. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
