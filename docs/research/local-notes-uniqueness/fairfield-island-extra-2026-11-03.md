# Fairfield Island — Extra Local Notes
Page: fairfield-island (Fairfield Island; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Fairfield Island is a residential neighbourhood in the City of Chilliwack, north of Chilliwack Proper, reached by Young Road North, Menzies Road, Hope River Road, Clare Avenue, Bell Road and Ballam Road. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- The City plans it together with Chilliwack Proper: Schedule B2 of the 2050 Official Community Plan (Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500) is the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- Cartmell Road is the park road at the north end: Island 22 Regional Park is at 44955 Cartmell Road, on the Fraser River. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/island-22.html
- Strathcona Road carries the neighbourhood's elementary school, Strathcona Elementary at 46375 Strathcona Road. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/education/work/bc_k12_schools.csv

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- No official source read for this note publishes a Fairfield Island housing-stock profile, so no lot-size range, build era or strata split is stated here — that gap is recorded in NOT CONFIRMED. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- The one housing fact the plan frame settles is tenure context: this is a Schedule B2 residential neighbourhood inside the city-wide zoning frame of Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, with the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) administration the City describes applying to farm parcels on its edges — see Section 3. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- Island 22 Regional Park, the area's largest single land use, is a 132-hectare park with an equestrian area, a boat launch, walking trails, an off-leash dog park, a bike skills park and a picnic shelter. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/island-22.html

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Dike and flood construction rules are the defining buyer rule here: the City's Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 designates floodplain land and sets flood protection requirements, and its Schedule A floodplain map is where a Fairfield Island parcel is checked. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- New building in the floodplain must sit at the flood construction level plus 0.6 metres of freeboard, and the freeboard may be waived only if the owner registers a Section 219 covenant on title and provides a qualified professional's report that the property may be safely used for the intended purpose. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- On land protected by standard dikes inside the ALR, flood construction levels have generally been standardized at 1.2 metres above natural ground elevation, with a flooding covenant required on title. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- ALR parcels follow a separate approval path: properties in the ALR are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act, Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) approval may be required before the City can approve related development, and an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, placing fill, a non-farm use, or a subdivision or boundary adjustment. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- A Development Permit is required on farm-edge parcels when the Farm Home Plate regulations in the Zoning Bylaw are not met, under Development Permit Area 4 – Farm Home Plate. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- City-wide suite and small-scale multi-unit housing rules are written in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note — see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3279/FAQ_-_Small-Scale_Housing_-_Updated_2025_07_28.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Fairfield Island really an island, and what does that mean for a purchase? **A:** What matters for a purchase is the dike and floodplain frame, whatever the name. The neighbourhood sits in the Fraser River floodplain area the City regulates under Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519: new building must meet the flood construction level plus 0.6 metres freeboard, setbacks apply from the Fraser River's natural boundary, and a covenant can be registered on title where freeboard is waived. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves a Fairfield Island address? **A:** Check the exact address, because School District No. 33 catchments are address-based. The school located in the neighbourhood is Strathcona Elementary at 46375 Strathcona Road, and the other schools named for this side of the city are Little Mountain Elementary at 9900 Carleton Street, A D Rundle Middle School at 45660 Hocking Avenue and Chilliwack Secondary at 46363 Yale Road; no catchment result is confirmed here. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/education/work/bc_k12_schools.csv
- **Q:** Can I build or add a suite on an ALR-edge parcel here? **A:** Only through the ALC process where the parcel is in the Agricultural Land Reserve. The City states an Agricultural Land Commission application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, for placing fill or removing soil, for non-farm uses and for subdivision, and it directs owners to check ALR status on the City Webmap before applying. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Q:** Is there a bus, or is this a drive-everywhere area? **A:** One named bus route serves the island: British Columbia Transit (BC Transit) Route 53 is the Fairfield route, and BC Transit's January 4, 2026 service changes added Sunday-evening service on Route 53 Fairfield along with Routes 51 and 57 Broadway. Everything else — shopping, the hospital, Highway 1 — is off the island, so most trips still start with a car. https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/
- **Q:** What does Island 22 charge, and when? **A:** Parking fees at Island 22 Regional Park are seasonal, not year-round. The Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) states the gatehouse operates daily from June 29 to October 25, 2026, and its 2026 fee table lists a vehicle towing a horse or boat trailer at $10 and a commercial vehicle towing a boat trailer at $100. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/island-22.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Island 22 is owned as a City park but run regionally: it is a 132-hectare Fraser Valley Regional District park at 44955 Cartmell Road, and the Island 22 Equestrian Society maintains the equestrian area, including a cross-country field, jumps, riding rings, trails and corrals. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/island-22.html
- The park connects to a section of the Experience the Fraser Canyon to Coast Trail, so dike-top trail traffic passes the north end of the neighbourhood. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/island-22.html
- Below-flood-level living space is restricted: the area below the flood construction level in a residential building may be used only for uses such as a utility room, workshop or indoor recreation, and its floor must sit at least 0.3 metres above the crown of the road. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- An older home here can be partly outside today's flood rules: the bylaw's elevation requirements do not apply to a renovation or addition that increases the building by less than 25 percent of the floor area that existed when the previous floodplain bylaw was adopted in March 1997. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 53 Fairfield is the island's bus, running between the neighbourhood and Downtown Chilliwack, and it gained added Sunday-evening service on January 4, 2026. https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/
- Downtown Chilliwack is the exchange where Route 53 connects to the rest of the BC Transit Chilliwack system, including Route 66 Fraser Valley Express toward Langley and Burnaby. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- The Canyon to Coast Trail section at Island 22 gives a dike-top walking and cycling link along the Fraser River from the north end of the neighbourhood. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/island-22.html

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the floodplain map: Schedule A of Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 designates the floodplain areas, and the bylaw's flood construction levels are read off that schedule for the specific parcel. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- The bylaw sets a minimum setback of 60 metres from the natural boundary of the Fraser River for buildings requiring floodproofing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- The City warns that its published floodplain elevation figures are estimates: the floodplain elevations map states the figures carry a margin of error of plus or minus 20 centimetres, that flood construction levels inside the dike are rough estimates of an 1894-magnitude flood, and that no one should rely on them alone to estimate or mitigate flood risk. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/1912/Floodplain%20elevations%20April%202012%2Epdf
- Riparian parcels fall under Development Permit Area 3, which protects fish habitat and riparian areas, and ALR status for any parcel is checked on the City Webmap before an application. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
