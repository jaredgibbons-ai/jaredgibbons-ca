# Cultus Lake — Questions for Jared

## Questions

1. Who buys at Cultus Lake in your deals — recreational and second-home buyers, full-time residents, or investors counting on rental income? How does the mix split between the Park (north) and Lindell Beach (south)?
2. What sells fast and what sits — Park leasehold homes, Lindell Beach freehold lots, or the bare land strata cottage communities like Creekside Mills, The Cottages and Aquadel Crossing?
3. What surprises buyers — that Park homes are leasehold and not freehold, the sewer service area boundaries, short-term rental limits, or summer traffic on the single road in?
4. How do lenders and insurers treat the Park leasehold homes compared with freehold at Lindell Beach, and does that change who can buy?
5. One story: a Cultus Lake deal that taught you something — a lease, septic or strata surprise — and what you check first now.

## Shot list

1. Main Beach and the docks at the north end, from the foreshore looking south down the lake — the Park Board side, the swimming area and the lake's length in one frame.
2. Sunnyside Boulevard at Columbia Valley Highway, the Village Centre corner — the commercial heart of the Park and the redevelopment site.
3. The Lindell Beach shoreline and residents' docks at the south end, looking north toward the Park — the quieter freehold shore for comparison with Main Beach.
