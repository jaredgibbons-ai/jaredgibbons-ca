# English Bluff Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: english-bluff (English Bluff; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- English Bluff is the bluff on the west shore of Tsawwassen in the City of Delta, along English Bluff Road above the Strait of Georgia. https://admin.delta.ca/media/283
- The pocket runs roughly from 12 Avenue south to 1 Avenue along English Bluff Road, around English Bluff Elementary at 402 English Bluff Road; exact City boundary lines are not published in the sources read. https://admin.delta.ca/media/5293
- Fred Gingell Park at 253 English Bluff Road is the named park on the bluff, listed by the City with picnic tables, trails and a viewpoint. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search?page=3
- Tsawwassen Heights and Pebble Hill sit inland and uphill to the east, and Beach Grove is the next shore pocket north along the water. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search?page=3

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- This is the only English Bluff page in the program, so the stock facts are stated here in full.
- The confirmed stock is freehold detached houses under the RS zones of Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 along English Bluff Road and its side streets, including bluff-top and waterfront lots on the Strait of Georgia shore. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The RS3 Single Detached Residential 3 zone sheet describes a zone for single detached housing in the vicinity of Georgia Strait, with a minimum subdivision lot area of 4,000 square metres, lot width 30 metres and average lot depth 30 metres; zoning is assigned parcel by parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf
- No condo or townhouse complex inside English Bluff was confirmed on a City or developer source read for this note; the area's named buildings are its elementary school and its parks, not strata complexes.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Development Permit Area SD2, English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment), is the defining rule on this shore: the Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw No. 8400 states the waterfront area is subject to mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence, and the ocean-frontage slopes require protection of existing vegetation and tree cover. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- SD2 building rules include a Flood Construction Level for habitable floors, no building within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of the sea or within 7.5 metres of the inboard toe of any dike, and a restrictive covenant on title acknowledging the land may be subject to flooding. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The City's Form S2 guide for sloped lands states a development permit is required for subdivision in SD2, for soil removal or deposit over 2 cubic metres per year on or within 5 metres of the crest of the slope, and for swimming pools unless set back at least 10 metres or two-thirds of the slope height, whichever is greater, from the crest. https://admin.delta.ca/media/283
- A Delta building official can require a geotechnical study wherever, in the official's opinion, land is or is likely to be subject to erosion, land slip, rock falls or avalanche, whether or not a development permit is required. https://admin.delta.ca/media/283
- Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing zoning allows up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, but on this shore the SD2 hazard conditions sit on top of that permission. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Does the bluff erosion issue affect my lot, or only the waterfront row? **A:** Read the permit area, not the street name. SD2 covers the Tsawwassen Escarpment lands the OCP designates for hazardous conditions, and the City's guide triggers a development permit for works near the crest of the slope, including soil movement within 5 metres of the crest and pools close to it. A lot away from the crest can still need a permit, so check the parcel on DeltaMap and ask the City before designing. https://admin.delta.ca/media/283
- **Q:** Which schools serve an English Bluff address? **A:** The recorded School District No. 37 (SD37) locator result for 402 English Bluff Road is English Bluff Elementary for Kindergarten to Grade 7 and South Delta Secondary, 750 53 Street, for Grades 8 to 12. Catchments are address-based and SD37 publishes Tsawwassen Area catchment maps, so run the specific address rather than relying on the sample. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/ https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/student-registration/catchment-area-maps/
- **Q:** How do I get to the beach from the bluff? **A:** At Fred Gingell Park, 253 English Bluff Road. The City lists the park with trails and a viewpoint, and a City report states stairs at Fred Gingell Park run from the upper park down to the beach. Diefenbaker Park at 5579 1 Avenue, with picnic tables, playgrounds, trails, a viewpoint, washrooms and a water park, is the other named park on this shore. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search?page=3
- **Q:** Who owns the beach below a bluff house? **A:** The foreshore below the natural boundary is Crown land, and Delta states foreshore and water use is interjurisdictional — federal, provincial and City rules all apply. Upland ownership does not include the beach below the natural boundary, and stairs or docks need their tenure checked before a seller markets them as private. https://www.delta.ca:443/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
- **Q:** Is there a bus on English Bluff Road? **A:** Yes. TransLink route 604, English Bluff to Bridgeport Station, runs via 56 Street, 16 Avenue, Beach Grove Road, 12 Avenue, English Bluff Road and 1 Avenue, and the 614 English Bluff community shuttle loops via 1 Avenue, English Bluff Road and 12 Avenue from South Delta Exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt603.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Irrigation is a slope issue here, not just a garden choice: the City's sloped-lands guide discourages irrigation systems because leaks can affect slope stability, although systems applying moderate amounts of water do not require a development permit. https://admin.delta.ca/media/283
- Cutting up to two trees can be done without a sloped-lands development permit only if at least 0.6 metres of stump remains standing and the root structure stays intact — and tree cutting is still subject to the Tree Cutting Regulation Bylaw. https://admin.delta.ca/media/283
- Surface water runoff must not be discharged over the crest of the slope for a driveway or path to stay exempt from a development permit under the City's guide. https://admin.delta.ca/media/283
- Emergency slope or flood work does not need a permit first, but the emergency must be reported to the City's Director of Engineering within 48 hours. https://admin.delta.ca/media/283
- South Delta Recreation Centre at 1720 56 Street and the Tsawwassen Library at 1321A 56 Street are the recreation and library services for this shore, both inland in the town centre. http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/south-delta-recreation-centre

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check SD2 status for the exact parcel on DeltaMap before offering: the permit area, the Flood Construction Level and the 7.5 metre setbacks are parcel questions, and the City's guide does not certify any individual lot. https://www.delta.ca/about-delta/deltamap?viewer=DeltaMapExternal.viewer/
- Read the geotechnical file, not just the disclosure: the City's steep-slope guideline requires a signed and sealed study confirming the land may be used safely for the intended development over its projected life, with a construction management and monitoring plan. https://admin.delta.ca/media/283
- Check the title for the SD2 restrictive covenant acknowledging flood risk, and check any existing geotechnical covenant or report registered against the lot. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
