# Bear Creek Green Timbers Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: bear-creek-green-timbers-guide (Bear Creek / Green Timbers, Surrey; Guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Bear Creek Green Timbers is one Fraser Valley board sub-area with a compound name — not two separate neighbourhoods — joining the Bear Creek Park ground in the west with the Green Timbers Urban Forest ground in the east. (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report)
- The City does not use the name as a community: the City lists seven communities (City Centre, Cloverdale, Fleetwood, Guildford, Newton, South Surrey, Whalley) and places Bear Creek Park in Whalley, so this board sub-area lies over ground the City frames through Whalley, Newton, Fleetwood and Guildford. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- The two name-giving green spaces face each other across the sub-area: Bear Creek Park at 13750 88 Avenue in the west, and Green Timbers Urban Forest at the 14600 block of 100 Avenue in the east. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bear-creek-park
- Facts this sub-area shares with Queen Mary Park next door (the two-RapidBus framing, suite and added-unit rules, hospital access) are written in full in the queen-mary-park-surrey-guide note — see the queen-mary-park-surrey-guide note.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is detached-led: established houses on larger, tree-covered lots around the two parks, with a condo cluster on 81a Avenue and townhouse complexes toward the Fleetwood and Guildford edges. (program research file: batch-11/bear-creek-green-timbers.md, read 2026-11-01)
- Suites are prominent in the detached stock, with houses described as carrying basement suites — the stock the City's one-suite rule (written out in the queen-mary-park-surrey-guide note) most affects. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- The board boundary is not published at street level, so whether a given complex falls inside this sub-area or in Bolivar Heights, Fleetwood Tynehead or Guildford is a Paragon map question, not something a listing label settles. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bear-creek-green-timbers.html

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Green Timbers is a protected urban forest by public vote: Surrey residents voted in 1988 and again in 1996 to designate part of the park as an urban forest, a protected natural legacy — lots at its edge sit beside land the City holds as protected, not as a future subdivision. https://www.surrey.ca/activities-parks-recreation/parks/green-timbers-urban-forest
- Bear Creek is a fish-bearing creek: the City's park page notes spawning salmon or trout visible from the trail, so lots backing onto the creek corridor can carry riparian setback and tree rules — check the parcel in the City's COSMOS map. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bear-creek-park
- City-wide zoning, suite, coach house and small-scale multi-unit rules for this sub-area are written in full in the queen-mary-park-surrey-guide note and the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see those notes. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Bear Creek Green Timbers one neighbourhood or two? **A:** One board sub-area with a compound name. Board data carries a single "Bear Creek Green Timbers" sub-area of Surrey, joining the Bear Creek Park ground — which the City places in Whalley, beside the Surrey Arts Centre — with the Green Timbers Urban Forest ground to the east. Buyers say the two park names separately, but listings and statistics use the single combined label. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- **Q:** When does SkyTrain reach Green Timbers? **A:** Green Timbers Station is a named station on the Surrey Langley SkyTrain, at the intersection of 140 Street and Fraser Highway on the westernmost edge of Green Timbers Urban Forest. TransLink states an anticipated in-service date of 2029 for the 16-kilometre Expo Line extension from King George Station to Langley City. Until then the station is a plan fact, not a service. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/
- **Q:** What is at Green Timbers besides the forest? **A:** A health and public-safety cluster on Green Timbers Way. The SkyTrain project states Green Timbers Station will facilitate access to the Jim Pattison Outpatient Care and Surgery Centre, the BC RCMP E-Divisional Headquarters and Surrey Memorial Hospital — the institutions that share the forest's western edge along Fraser Highway. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/
- **Q:** Which schools are in the sub-area? **A:** Schools confirmed by address in and around this ground include Bear Creek Elementary (13780 80 Ave), Green Timbers Elementary (8824 144 St), Frank Hurt Secondary (13940 77 Ave) and Enver Creek Secondary (14505 84 Ave), all School District 36. Catchments are address-based and which schools fall inside the board sub-area line is not published, so run the exact address in the district locator. (BC Ministry K-12 schools file, 2025/2026)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Green Timbers Urban Forest holds over 10 kilometres of nature trails, a regularly stocked fishing lake (fishing from shore only — no watercraft, wading or swimming), picnic areas and the Surrey Nature Centre, all in second-growth forest. https://www.surrey.ca/activities-parks-recreation/parks/green-timbers-urban-forest
- The forest is called British Columbia's "Birthplace of Reforestation": the land was completely logged by 1929, then replanted and run as a forestry school and nursery before becoming a park in 1974, and a glacial erratic — a boulder carried by glacial ice — sits in its northwestern trails. https://www.surrey.ca/activities-parks-recreation/parks/green-timbers-urban-forest
- Bear Creek Park's walking track is a 365-metre, 4-lane rubberized track at the 88 Avenue entrance, lit for evening walkers and open year-round. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bear-creek-park
- The Surrey Arts Centre shares Bear Creek Park's address, 13750 88 Avenue — the park is also the city's arts venue, with the Bear Creek Stadium athletics facility on the same ground. https://www.surrey.ca/arts-culture/surrey-arts-centre
- Bear Creek Park is at capacity for large-scale community events: the City states no community event applications are being accepted for the park at this time, and certain track and field amenities are being upgraded, closed until spring 2026. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bear-creek-park
- The park's spray park was rebuilt in 2026 in four colour-coded sensory zones, designed with the Surrey Accessibility Leadership Team — a new facility fact for families on the surrounding streets. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bear-creek-park
- A heritage marker in Bear Creek Park shows a historic map of local landowners, self-titled "stump farmers," from around 1940, and acknowledges the forced removal of Japanese Canadians during the Second World War. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bear-creek-park

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- This sub-area pays the city-wide Surrey rate: the 2026 total residential (Class 1) rate is $3.42731 per $1,000 of assessed value, with the full schedule in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies: the Province lists the City of Surrey as a designated taxable area, so owners here file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 325 runs between Surrey Central Station and Newton Exchange via 72 Ave, 140 St and 102 Ave, crossing the Green Timbers side of the sub-area on 140 Street. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt325.pdf
- Route 321 runs on King George Boulevard west of the sub-area, from Surrey Central Station via Newton Exchange to White Rock Centre. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt321.pdf
- The nearest SkyTrain today is King George Station; measured from Bear Creek Park (13750 88 Avenue) in the What's Nearby pass, it is a 3.9-minute drive (2.84 km) and Surrey Central Station a 5.7-minute drive (3.76 km), OSRM routing measured 2026-11-02. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/parking-streets-transportation/walking-cycling-transit/transit-in-surrey
- From 2029 (anticipated), Green Timbers Station at 140 Street and Fraser Highway puts the sub-area's east side on the Expo Line — the first rail service inside the sub-area. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Bear Creek's salmon and trout spawning makes its corridor a riparian check: any lot backing onto the creek should be tested for riparian setbacks and tree-protection rules in COSMOS before a buyer prices a coach house or an addition. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bear-creek-park
- Green Timbers Lake is for wildlife and fishing only — no watercraft, wading or swimming, and dogs must stay on leash and out of the water — which sets what "lakefront" recreation actually means on the forest's edge streets. https://www.surrey.ca/activities-parks-recreation/parks/green-timbers-urban-forest
- Station construction is the near-term disruption: the Surrey Langley SkyTrain is being built along Fraser Highway at the sub-area's east edge with an anticipated 2029 opening, so Fraser Highway-fronting lots carry construction and guideway effects the park pages do not describe — check the project site for the current works map. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/
