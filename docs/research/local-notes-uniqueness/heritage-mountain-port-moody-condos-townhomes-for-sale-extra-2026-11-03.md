## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- For the city-wide Port Moody zoning bylaw, small-scale multi-unit housing, suite, tax and utility position, see the heritage-mountain-port-moody-houses-for-sale note, which states them in full for this city; strata rules below are the attached-housing addition. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- Strata rental-restriction bylaws are void across British Columbia since November 24, 2022, so a Heritage Mountain strata cannot ban long-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- A registered building scheme on title in this grouping takes precedence over small-scale multi-unit entitlements, and the City states it cannot process an application the scheme blocks — read title, not just zoning, on any townhouse lot assembly idea. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is beside the Panorama Drive townhouses? **A:** Aspenwood Elementary at 2001 Panorama Drive stands on the same street shelf as the Panorama Drive and Panorama Place complexes. School District No. 43 assigns by home address and the Aspenwood, Heritage Mountain and Mountain Meadows catchments meet in this grouping, so confirm the complex's exact address in the School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** Is there a SkyTrain station for these townhouses? **A:** No station is in Heritage Mountain. Routes 183 and 184 connect the Noons Creek Drive, Panorama Drive and David Avenue stops to Inlet Centre Station and Moody Centre Station on the Millennium Line, so a strata buyer here still plans on a bus leg or a drive to the station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt184.pdf
- **Q:** Can I rent out a Heritage Mountain townhouse long-term? **A:** Yes as a matter of provincial law, because strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022. Still read the complex's own bylaws and the Rental Disclosure Statement attached to the Form B, because pet, parking and move rules in those documents still bind the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q:** What should I read in the depreciation report for a creek-side complex? **A:** Read the drainage, retaining and road items first. Since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, and on Noons Creek Drive and Ravine Drive sites the report is where slope and watercourse maintenance funding appears. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Heritage Mountain Community Centre at 200 Panorama Place sits inside the townhouse streets themselves, not down the hill — the one civic facility in this grouping at walking scale. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/
- Dogs may be off leash only on Bert Flinn Park's wide gravel trail from Heritage Mountain Boulevard and David Avenue, plus the morning mini-loop pilot — relevant to every ground-floor townhouse backing onto that forest. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/parks/off-leash-dog-parks/
- Port Moody was declared a Bear Smart Community in 2024, and waste enclosures beside sensitive areas must be bear-resistant — a practical strata bylaw and garbage-room point in this forested grouping. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/environmental-protection-and-requirements/
- No library branch sits in Heritage Mountain; the single Port Moody Public Library is at 100 Newport Drive in Inlet Centre. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check Development Permit Area 4 and Area 5 on a Noons Creek or Ravine Drive complex: watercourse setbacks under Zoning Bylaw section 5.4 and steep-slope review apply to strata common property, not only to house lots. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/environmental-protection-and-requirements/
- Check the Form B Information Certificate for approved special levies still payable, the contingency reserve fund balance and insurance summary before removing subjects on any unit here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- Check forest-interface wildfire position under the City Community Wildfire Protection Plan for complexes backing onto Bert Flinn Park forest. https://www.portmoody.ca/resident-services/hazards/
