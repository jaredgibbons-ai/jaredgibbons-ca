# Sullivan Station Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: sullivan-station-surrey-houses-for-sale (Sullivan Station; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sullivan Station houses sit inside the City's South Newton plan area: about 485 hectares (1,200 acres), generally north of Highway 10, east of King George Boulevard and west of 152 Street, bordered by the East Newton neighbourhood concept plans (NCPs) to the northwest. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- The plan names the area's two commercial nodes: Panorama Heights at 152 Street and Highway 10, and Sullivan Heights at 64 Avenue and 144 Street — Sullivan Park at 6272 152 Street and Sullivan Elementary at 6016 152 Street sit on the 152 Street east edge. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- Serpentine is a Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area name in this part of Surrey; it is a board geography label, not a City of Surrey plan name — the City plan for these streets is South Newton. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- South Newton houses include a small-lot single-family pattern the City added on purpose: in December 2004 Council approved a single-family flex (small-lot) designation and guidelines in the South Newton NCP, on top of the plan's standard single-family and suburban half-acre and quarter-acre designations. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- The current infill pattern is two-lot splits of suburban parcels: at 5744 146A Street a City report dated November 3, 2025 records a site zoned Suburban Residential Zone (R1), designated Suburban Residential Half-Acre, proposed for subdivision into two single-family lots under a Comprehensive Development (CD) zone based on R2. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7925-0172-00.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The South Newton NCP no longer governs a purchase here. The City states South Newton was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) update and is no longer in effect, and that development in the area is now led by the OCP; on December 2, 2024 Council endorsed Corporate Report R246 updating land use designations to reflect current market conditions. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- A half-acre–looking lot is not automatically splittable: the 146A Street applications in section 2 and section 6 each needed an NCP amendment from Suburban Half-Acre to Suburban Quarter-Acre plus rezoning, even though the proposal complied with the OCP's Urban designation. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0317-00
- Surrey-wide small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules are in full in the city note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves a Sullivan Station house, and is it full? **A:** Sullivan Heights Secondary at 6248 144 Street is the local secondary, and it is over capacity. A City planning report dated November 3, 2025 states that, as of September 2025, Sullivan Heights was operating at 123 percent capacity despite a 700-capacity addition that opened in September 2022, with no further capital requests currently planned. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7925-0172-00.pdf
- **Q:** Which elementary schools feed Sullivan Heights? **A:** Seven elementaries are in the Sullivan Heights family for 2026–2027: Cambridge, Goldstone Park, Hyland, McLeod Road Traditional, Snokomish, Sullivan and Woodward Hill. Note the name is McLeod Road Traditional, not McLeod Road Elementary, and catchment is still by exact address, so confirm the house in the district school locator. https://media.surreyschools.ca/media/Default/medialib/families-of-schools-2026-2027.299e54207481.pdf
- **Q:** Is Sullivan Station a SkyTrain station, present or planned? **A:** No — no source read names a Sullivan Station stop. The name is a board sub-area label for the Sullivan / Panorama part of southeast Newton; the area's confirmed transit is bus routes 342, 345 and 364, and King George Boulevard bus rapid transit (BRT) planning on the west boundary, with station locations not yet determined in the sources read. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2023/11/three-new-rapid-transit-corridors-coming-to-metro-vancouver/
- **Q:** Can I split a large Sullivan lot into two house lots? **A:** Only with City approval, on the recent evidence. At 5669 146A Street a May 11, 2026 planning report sought subdivision into two residential lots, requiring an NCP amendment from Suburban Residential 1/2 Acre to Suburban Residential 1/4 Acre and rezoning from R1 to a CD zone based on R2 — budget for that process, not a simple subdivision. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0317-00

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Sullivan Park is an 18.5-hectare (45-acre) community park at 6272 152 Street overlooking agricultural land east of Newton, with parking off 152 Street south of the train tracks or off 62A Avenue east of 152 Street — the rail line crosses the park's south access. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/sullivan-park
- Sullivan Heights Park at 14426 64 Avenue is a 7.5-hectare park with soccer, rugby and cricket fields and a kabaddi field, and Sullivan Heights Secondary and the Bell Performing Arts Centre sit directly south of it at 6248 144 Street. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/sullivan-heights-park
- There is no Surrey Libraries branch and no City recreation centre inside Sullivan Station; the nearest branches listed by Surrey Libraries are Newton at 13795 70 Avenue and Strawberry Hill at 7399 122 Street. https://www.surreylibraries.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/SurreyLibrariesAnnualReport2025.pdf
- The Sullivan Heights Secondary addition that opened in September 2022 cost $34.3 million, added 700 students of capacity and brought the school to 1,700, per the Province's September 6, 2022 release — and the 2025 City report in section 4 shows enrolment has since passed that capacity again. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2022ECC0064-001329.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Sullivan Station houses pay the City of Surrey residential rate and utilities in full in the city note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note for the 2026 rate per $1,000, the capital and drainage parcel taxes, flat-rate water and sewer, and the secondary-suite levy. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- The local point is east-edge land: Sullivan Park overlooks agricultural land at 152 Street, so a house on the east edge can sit beside Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) farmland while remaining a standard Surrey residential tax property — ALR status is parcel-specific and is checked in section 9. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/sullivan-park

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Three bus routes frame the area, per TransLink timetables effective January 5, 2026: route 342 (Newton Exchange / Langley Centre) runs through the area via Highway 10, 152 Street, 60 Avenue, 142 Street, 57 Avenue, 144 Street and 72 Avenue; route 345 runs the length of 152 Street on the east edge; route 364 runs along 64 Avenue on the north edge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt342.pdf
- There is no SkyTrain station in or directly serving Sullivan Station at present, and no library-branch transit hub inside the area — Newton Exchange, reached by the 342, is the transfer point. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt342.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check creek lots first: the City's South Newton page states the area contains several fish-bearing creeks, including the headwaters of Archibald Creek, Sullivan Heights Creek, and tributaries of Hyland Creek, and Surrey's streamside Development Permit Areas (DPAs) can require a permit before land alteration or clearing near a watercourse. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- Sullivan Heights Creek is a fish-supporting stream through Sullivan Heights Park that connects to the Serpentine River, so a lot backing onto the park's natural area is also backing onto a protected watercourse corridor — confirm setbacks in COSMOS, the City of Surrey Mapping Online System, per address. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/sullivan-heights-park
- Check ALR status on any 152 Street east-edge lot in COSMOS: the City describes farmland immediately east of the area, and no ALR land inside the South Newton plan area was confirmed from an Agricultural Land Commission source read for this note. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/sullivan-park
