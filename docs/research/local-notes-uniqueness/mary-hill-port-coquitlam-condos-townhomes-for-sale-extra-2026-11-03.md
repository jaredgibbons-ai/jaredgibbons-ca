- Attached homes here are strata townhouses and low-rise strata units, not freehold houses: Zoning Bylaw No. 3630 provides townhouse zones RTh1, RTh2 and RTh3 and a rowhouse zone RRh for this form in Port Coquitlam. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2025-08/3630%20-%20Zoning%20Bylaw%202008.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- For the city-wide zoning bylaw, small-scale multi-unit housing, tax and utility position, see the mary-hill-port-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; strata rules below are the Mary Hill attached-housing addition. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Strata rental-restriction bylaws are void across British Columbia since November 24, 2022, so a Mary Hill strata cannot ban long-term rentals by bylaw; short-term rental rules are separate and must be checked in the bylaws themselves. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- The only age bylaw a British Columbia strata may now keep is a 55-or-older residency bylaw under Strata Property Act sections 123.1 and 123.2, with the exemptions in Strata Property Regulation 7.01. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there a bus inside Mary Hill for a townhouse buyer without a car? **A:** Yes, one loop. TransLink route 170 runs from Port Coquitlam Station via Pitt River Road, then Eastern Drive to Western Drive, returning via Western Drive and Mary Hill Road. That loop is the only in-neighbourhood service; a complex off that loop still needs a walk to Eastern Drive or Western Drive. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt170.pdf
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves a Mary Hill townhouse? **A:** École Mary Hill Elementary at 1890 Humber Crescent is the elementary school physically inside the hillside. School District No. 43 assigns by home address, so confirm the designated school for the complex's exact address in the district School Locator before relying on it. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-coquitlam
- **Q:** Can I rent out a Mary Hill townhouse long-term? **A:** Yes as a matter of provincial law, because strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022. Still read the strata's own bylaws and any Rental Disclosure Statement attached to the Form B, because parking, pet and move rules in those documents still bind the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q:** What strata paperwork matters most on a hillside complex? **A:** The Form B Information Certificate matters most, because it states strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, the contingency reserve fund balance and insurance, with the budget, rules and most recent depreciation report attached. On a sloped Mary Hill site, read the depreciation report's drainage and retaining items closely. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Routley Park at 1570 Western Drive and Robert Hope Park at 2137 Lamprey Drive, with their outdoor pools, are the in-area recreation anchors for Mary Hill strata families. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/recreation-parks/parks-trails/parks-directory?page=2
- Waste collection is by City zone, not by complex name: Port Coquitlam has five collection zones, green carts weekly and recycling and garbage carts on alternating weeks, so a complex address must be checked on the zone map. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/waste-collection/collection-schedule-zone-map
- Since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots in British Columbia must obtain a depreciation report every five years with no deferral vote — ask for the current report on any Mary Hill complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- The 170 loop means Western Drive and Eastern Drive carry scheduled bus traffic past the complexes on those streets, while interior crescents do not. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt170.pdf

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 170 is the Mary Hill loop from Port Coquitlam Station via Eastern Drive and Western Drive; routes 173 and 174 stay on the north side and do not enter Mary Hill. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt170.pdf
- Port Coquitlam Station at the hill's north edge is the West Coast Express stop, a weekday peak-direction commuter service to Waterfront Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- The Mary Hill Bypass (Highway 7B) along the south and west edges is the driving exit for a Mary Hill strata owner heading to the Pitt River Bridge or Highway 1. https://portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check flood status per property on the City flood page: the hill rises above the Fraser lowland, but low lots near Pitt River Road and the bypass need the same Fraser/Pitt River floodplain check as any river-side lot. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/flood
- Check the strata depreciation report for slope works: retaining walls, drainage and roadways inside a hillside complex are common property, and their funding shows in that report, not in the listing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Check Tree Bylaw, 2019, No. 4108 before assuming a complex can clear trees for parking or amenity work. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/4108-tree-bylawpdf
