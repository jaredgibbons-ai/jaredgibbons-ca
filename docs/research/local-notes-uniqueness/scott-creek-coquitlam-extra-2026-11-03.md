# Scott Creek Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: scott-creek-coquitlam (Scott Creek; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Scott Creek the neighbourhood is the Lansdowne Drive pocket in northwest Coquitlam, and its defining line is the creek itself, not a City boundary: the City publishes no boundary for this sub-area name, so the creek corridor and the Lansdowne Drive and Falcon Drive streets are the check. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Scott-Creek-Linear-Park-78
- Scott Creek Linear Park, at 2695 Runnel Drive, runs along a portion of Scott Creek on the pocket's west side, and its trails connect neighbourhoods through the creek corridor. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Scott-Creek-Linear-Park-78
- The plan map and the city-wide zoning, tax and small-scale housing facts for these streets are in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; this note covers the creek, park and school-campus angle only. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/3487/City-Centre-Area-Plan-Servicing-Assessment-PDF

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The creek rule is the rule that matters most on Runnel Drive: land within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of a watercourse in the City Centre Area Plan needs a Watercourse Protection Development Permit — in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note, applied here to the Scott Creek Linear Park corridor. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- Riparian edges are a protected feature, not landscaping a buyer can clear: the City's Scott Creek Linear Park page explains that the edges of wetlands and waterways are riparian areas, and that restoration projects in the creek improve habitat for coho and chum salmon, cutthroat and steelhead trout. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Scott-Creek-Linear-Park-78

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What fish are in Scott Creek itself? **A:** Scott Creek supports coho and chum salmon, cutthroat and steelhead trout, according to the City's Scott Creek Linear Park page. The creek is part of the waterway network feeding the Coquitlam River and the Lower Coquitlam Watershed, and ongoing restoration and enhancement projects are improving fish habitat along this Runnel Drive corridor. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Scott-Creek-Linear-Park-78
- **Q:** Is the creek park separate from Eagle Ridge Park? **A:** Yes, they are two different City parks serving this pocket. Scott Creek Linear Park, at 2695 Runnel Drive, is the trail corridor along Scott Creek itself. Eagle Ridge Park, at the Lansdowne Drive school campus, is the sports park — the two are covered separately in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note, and a buyer should check which one a specific lot actually faces. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Scott-Creek-Linear-Park-78
- **Q:** Which schools anchor this neighbourhood? **A:** Three SD43 schools sit on the Lansdowne Drive and Falcon Drive corridor: Eagle Ridge Elementary at 1215 Falcon Drive, Scott Creek Middle at 1240 Lansdowne Drive and Gleneagle Secondary at 1195 Lansdowne Drive. That list is in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. Catchment is by permanent home address, so run the exact address in the SD43 School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** Does living by Scott Creek mean river floodplain risk? **A:** No river dike system is the issue on these streets. The City's floodplain mapping covers the Coquitlam, Fraser and Pitt rivers, and this upland pocket sits above them; the local check is the Scott Creek corridor and its 30-metre watercourse permit distance, in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/182/Coquitlam-Fraser-and-Pitt-Rivers-Floodplain-Mapping-Report-PDF

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Scott Creek Linear Park's address is 2695 Runnel Drive, a side street off the Lansdowne Drive corridor, not a main-road entrance. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Scott-Creek-Linear-Park-78
- The creek beside these houses is a salmon creek, not a ditch: the City states Scott Creek feeds the Coquitlam River and supports the Lower Coquitlam Watershed. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Scott-Creek-Linear-Park-78
- Hoy Creek Linear Park, south of this pocket in City Centre, is the paired creek park: the City states its trails connect neighbourhoods and that coho and chum salmon are raised at the Hoy Creek Fish Hatchery there. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Hoy-Creek-Linear-Park-47
- The Coquitlam Crunch Trail access points on Lansdowne Drive and at Eagle Ridge Park, and the Scott Creek Community Garden between the middle and elementary schools, are in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/coquitlamcrunchtrail-27
- Salmon viewing for Scott Creek is from public areas beside Guildford Way and off Lansdowne Drive, per the Hoy-Scott watershed reporting — a seasonal creek activity on this pocket's south and west edges. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Hoy-Creek-Linear-Park-47

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Coquitlam's 2026 Class 1 Residential municipal rate is $2.2756 per $1,000 of assessed value, in full with utility charges and due dates in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17264/2026_Tax-Rates
- Coquitlam is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so a Scott Creek owner must complete the annual declaration; reserve, treaty and self-governing Indigenous Nation lands are excluded. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- The neighbourhood-specific cost check is the creek permit: a Runnel Drive or Lansdowne Drive lot within 30 metres of the Scott Creek bank can carry Watercourse Protection Development Permit conditions that an interior lot does not. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 185, Coquitlam Central Station to Lansdowne, is this pocket's bus: it runs Lansdowne Drive through the neighbourhood — routing in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. http://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/192/tt185.pdf
- Route 186 runs David Avenue at the pocket's north side and route 187 runs Johnson Street at its east side, per their TransLink timetables. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt186.pdf
- No SkyTrain station is in Scott Creek; the rail connection for routes 185, 186 and 187 is Coquitlam Central Station, south of the pocket, on the Millennium Line Evergreen Extension and the West Coast Express. http://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/192/tt185.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area layer for any lot backing onto Scott Creek Linear Park: within 30 metres of top of bank or ravine bank in this plan area the permit can set riparian, runoff and slope conditions, and the guide does not set a final lot-level setback. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- Check slope and wildfire layers on the City's Schedule B environmental development permit mapping, which maps unstable slopes and the Interface Wildfire Risk Management boundary for the City Centre and Northeast areas; it shows boundaries, not a lot conclusion. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- Check Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 section 519 and Schedule G floodplain mapping even on upland lots, and check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status per parcel on Agricultural Land Commission mapping — no ALR land was identified here in the sources read, which is not a parcel clearance. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1170/Part-5-General-Regulations-PDF
