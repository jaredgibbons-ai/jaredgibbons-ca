# Bridgeview Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: bridgeview-guide (Bridgeview, Surrey; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Bridgeview has no City-published boundary of its own; the City of Surrey calls it a historic neighbourhood in Whalley, anchored by Bridgeview Park at 12560 115 Avenue. The City describes the park as located in the historic neighbourhood of Bridgeview. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bridgeview-park
- The plan area beside it is South Westminster, bordered by the Fraser River in the west, King George Boulevard in the north and 96 Avenue in the south, and described by the City as the largely industrial area along the river between Scott Road SkyTrain Station and the Surrey Fraser Docks (Port of Vancouver). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/south-westminster
- History in brief: the South Westminster Dyking District was formed in 1920 to protect this low Fraser River bench, and the area flooded again in 1972 before the dyking district transferred to Surrey in 1975 under an agreement to improve the dyke system. https://surrey.ca/city-services/3655.aspx
- Uphill to the east sits South Westminster Heights, about 30.86 hectares bounded by Old Yale Road (north), 128 Street (east), 104 Avenue (south) and 125B Street (west) — a separate infill plan area, not part of the riverfront lowland. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/south-westminster-heights

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The residential stock is an older lowland neighbourhood beside industrial land, not a planned new subdivision. The City's industrial overview states Bridgeview is predominantly zoned for residential and industrial uses, with a mix of light industrial, commercial and residential zones. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SouthwestminterBridgeviewIndustrialAreas.pdf
- The adjoining South Westminster Heights area is described by the City as a more traditional single-family area with older lots and mature trees, which is the character of the streets rising east of the lowland. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/south-westminster-heights
- Community anchors are civic, not strata: Bridgeview Community Centre at 11475 126A Street has one gymnasium and limited program hours (Tuesday and Thursday 5–8 p.m., Wednesday 6–8 p.m., weekends 9 a.m.–3 p.m.). https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/bridgeview-community-centre
- Bridgeview Park at 12560 115 Avenue carries the outdoor stock: playground, water park, picnic shelters, two softball diamonds, a multi-use field, and tennis, pickleball and basketball courts. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bridgeview-park

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The South Westminster Neighbourhood Concept Plan (NCP), approved in December 2003, is the plan that governs the riverfront side, and its update was endorsed on March 10, 2025. The 2025 update concluded the Stage 2 land use for the mainly industrial portion and named a Transit Village Study Area at Scott Road Station and a Waterfront District Study Area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/south-westminster
- Flood building rules apply on the lowland lots: the City's Bridgeview Floodplain guide requires a geotechnical engineer for construction in the floodplain, sets a Flood Construction Level (FCL), and allows no habitable area below the FCL, enforced by a restrictive covenant on title. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/BridgeviewFloodplain.pdf
- One in-area application shows the elevation rule in numbers: a City planning report for 11444–124 Street states the site is within the Fraser River floodplain and requires a Minimum Building Elevation of 4.8 metres geodetic. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7924-0145-00.pdf
- City-wide Surrey zoning, secondary suite and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) rules are written in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Bridgeview its own plan area, or part of somewhere else? **A:** It has no plan of its own. No City page read names a Bridgeview neighbourhood plan; the riverfront is planned as South Westminster under its Neighbourhood Concept Plan, first approved in 2003 and updated in March 2025, and the streets uphill east fall under the South Westminster Heights infill plan approved in 2013. A buyer's lot can sit in either frame, so check which plan the address falls under. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/south-westminster
- **Q:** Which schools serve Bridgeview? **A:** Bridgeview Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 7, at 12834 115A Avenue is the in-area school. The provincial school list for School District 36 (Surrey) gives that address and grade range, and lists Kwantlen Park Secondary, Grades 8–12, at 10441 132 Street. Catchments are address-based, so confirm the exact house address in the district school locator before an offer that depends on a school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** Can I use the basement as a suite or bedroom? **A:** Not below the flood construction level. The City's Bridgeview Floodplain guide states habitable area is not permitted below the FCL and that a restrictive covenant on title enforces the ban, so a low basement in the floodplain is storage or utility space, not living space, even if a previous owner finished it. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/BridgeviewFloodplain.pdf
- **Q:** Will the land beside the houses stay industrial? **A:** Mostly, with two exceptions now in study. The March 2025 South Westminster update kept Mixed Employment and Industrial designations for the Stage 2 lands, while naming a Transit Village of housing and retail at Scott Road Station and a Waterfront District feasibility study — both study areas, not adopted residential designations yet. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/transit-village-near-scott-road-skytrain-envisioned-newly-approved-south-westminster-ncp
- **Q:** Is truck traffic on the residential streets a documented issue? **A:** Yes, the City has recorded it. A 2025 planning report for 124 Street in Bridgeview records truck drivers ticketed for driving through the residential neighbourhood, states Bridgeview roads were historically built on softer base materials not intended for heavy trucks, and notes the site has direct access to the South Fraser Perimeter Road as the designated truck route. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7924-0145-00.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The river beach is Brownsville Bar Park, 11931 Old Yale Road, one of the only Fraser River locations with a sandy beach and about 300 metres of waterfront, sitting between the Pattullo and SkyTrain bridges at the end of the Tannery Greenway. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/brownsville-bar-park
- The old crossing here is a registered heritage site: Brownsville Park and Sandbar, City heritage Site No. 223 in the 1100 block of Portage Road, registered January 12, 2004, marking the early Brownsville ferry landing at the foot of Old Yale Road. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/heritage-conservation/heritage-sites/whalley/brownsville-park-and-sandbar
- There is no Surrey Libraries branch inside Bridgeview; the City system lists ten branches city-wide, and the Whalley-area branch is City Centre at 10350 University Drive. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/surrey-libraries
- Soil is a lot-level check here: the City floodplain guide flags methane gas from decomposing organic soils common to Bridgeview, which is why a geotechnical report is required for floodplain construction. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/BridgeviewFloodplain.pdf
- The community centre's hours are part-time, not full-week: programs only on Monday and Friday, with public hours on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday evenings and weekend days. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/bridgeview-community-centre

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey-wide property tax rates, utility charges and suite levies are written in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- The Bridgeview-specific charge is the area Development Cost Charge (DCC) schedule: the City publishes separate 2025 Bridgeview / South Westminster DCC rates, including an industrial total of $108,406 per acre and a commercial rate of $14.58 per square foot of building area (excluding several commercial zones), so a rebuild or new project here is costed under that schedule. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SouthwestminterBridgeviewIndustrialAreas.pdf

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- No SkyTrain station sits inside the residential streets; Scott Road Station on the Expo Line is the area's rapid-transit anchor, named by the City as the station the South Westminster plan is built around. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/south-westminster
- The in-neighbourhood bus is route 371, Surrey Central Station to Scott Road Station, which TransLink's timetable routes through Bridgeview on Bridgeview Drive, 115 Avenue, 128 Street, 132 Street, 114 Avenue and 112 Avenue. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt371.pdf
- The R6 Scott Road RapidBus launched January 2, 2024, runs between Scott Road Station and Newton Exchange from 5 a.m. to 1 a.m. daily, with 15-minute-or-better service all day, and route 319 continues alongside it serving local stops. https://www.translink.ca/news/2024/january/new%20rapidbus%20slashes%20travel%20times%20in%20surrey%20and%20delta
- The river crossing at the area's west edge is now the four-lane stal̕əw̓asəm (Riverview) Bridge to New Westminster, fully opened in February 2026; measured drive times from Bridgeview are UNCONFIRMED. https://www.pattullobridgereplacement.ca/app/uploads/sites/637/2026/09/PBR_Monthly-Status-Report_July_2026.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Flood is the first check: the City states Surrey's Fraser River floodplain covers 776 hectares of developed land with a mix of industrial and residential properties, protected by dykes, concrete flood walls and four pump stations that drain land behind the dykes during tides, storm surge and freshet. https://surrey.ca/city-services/3655.aspx
- Look a specific lot up in the City's Bridgeview Floodplain guide and COSMOS mapping before buying: the guide sets the lot's Flood Construction Level and covenant position, and states lots from a recent subdivision can carry minimum building elevations set by that subdivision's engineer, which the guide itself does not list. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/BridgeviewFloodplain.pdf
- The South Westminster shore is described by the City as a very low flood bench that flooded in 1972, so an older house's as-built elevation is a document to ask for, not an assumption. https://surrey.ca/city-services/3655.aspx
