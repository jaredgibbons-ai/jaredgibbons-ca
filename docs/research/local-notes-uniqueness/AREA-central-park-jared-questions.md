# Central Park — Questions for Jared

1. In Central Park area deals, what do you see differ between the detached houses south of the park along Imperial Street and the condos and townhomes on the Patterson Avenue and Central Boulevard side toward Metrotown?
2. Who buys around Central Park: commuters using Patterson Station, families who want the park's forest trails at their door, or investors playing the Metrotown edge — and what do they say tipped it?
3. What sells fast here and what sits: do houses backing onto the Central Park forest move first, while houses fronting Kingsway wait — and how do the older low-rise buildings near Patterson compete with the Metrotown towers?
4. What surprises Central Park buyers: the Expo Line running through the park's northeast corner beside Patterson Station, that Boundary Road is the Vancouver border, or Imperial Street traffic on the park's south edge?
5. Tell one Central Park story: a deal on Patterson Avenue or Imperial Street where the park frontage, the station, or a teardown-and-rebuild plan changed the price, the subjects, or the outcome.
