# Garrison Crossing Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: garrison-crossing-guide (Garrison Crossing, Chilliwack; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Garrison Crossing is in Sardis, on the south side of Chilliwack, within the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan area — see the garrison-crossing note for street-level boundaries. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- The Sardis plan names Vedder Road as the primary north-south spine of Sardis, connecting its sub-areas including Garrison Crossing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Canada Education Park lies south of Garrison Crossing along the same Vedder Corridor, per the Sardis plan. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The guide-level stock is a planned mix of detached houses, townhomes and condominiums/apartments around a commercial core, built as the residential redevelopment of the former Canadian Forces Base (CFB) Chilliwack lands — street-level detail is in the garrison-crossing note. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/
- That redevelopment is described by Chilliwack Economic Partners Corporation as including Garrison Crossing within 400 acres total of institutional, commercial and residential development on the former base. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Sardis land uses were incorporated into city-wide designations in the 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, which updated the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan adopted October 2020. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- City-wide small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules require up to 4 units on single-detached or duplex lots over 280 square metres inside the urban growth boundary, affecting about 11,000 Chilliwack properties; exclusions can apply. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3279/FAQ_-_Small-Scale_Housing_-_Updated_2025_07_28.pdf
- Strata bylaws for a specific Garrison building were not read this pass — obtain the strata's own bylaws and Form B before subject removal.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What was here before Garrison Crossing? **A:** Canadian Forces Base Chilliwack was here before. Chilliwack Economic Partners Corporation states CFB Chilliwack closed its 400-acre site in 1998, and in 2003 the City, Canada Lands Company and partners launched Canada Education Park on 200 acres of the former base, adjacent to the Vedder River, with Garrison Crossing as its residential part. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/
- **Q:** What is Canada Education Park? **A:** It is a multi-agency education campus south of Garrison Crossing. Its partners listed include the University of the Fraser Valley (UFV), the Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP) Pacific Region Training Centre, and the Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA), so students and trainees are a regular presence on nearby streets. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/
- **Q:** Is there frequent transit? **A:** Yes, on Vedder Road. The Sardis Neighbourhood Plan states there is a frequent bus line with 15-minute service connecting Sardis to downtown, Garrison Crossing and Canada Education Park to the south along the Vedder Corridor. Confirm the current route and timetable on BC Transit before relying on a specific departure. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- **Q:** Which schools serve Garrison? **A:** School District No. 33 (SD33) lists Vedder Elementary, Vedder Middle and G.W. Graham Secondary among its south-side schools, but the catchment for a specific Garrison address was not confirmed this pass. Use SD33's school locator for the exact address rather than assuming a school from proximity alone. https://sd33.bc.ca/schools
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The former base parade-square saluting base off Caen Avenue was preserved by UFV, with commemorative plaques and metal fixtures shaped as the word "ubique." https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/
- UFV's Chilliwack campus address in Canada Education Park is 45190 Caen Avenue, a useful landmark for locating Garrison's south edge. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/
- City curbside collection in Chilliwack includes weekly compostables, bi-weekly recycling and garbage, and monthly glass and flexible plastics; 2026 fees range from $27.15 to $34.80 per month by cart size. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1893
- Sardis plan policy prioritizes a neighbourhood park in North Sardis and a corridor plan for Vedder Road, which affect streets north of Garrison as well. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Luckakuck Creek runs through the Sardis plan area near Garrison — check riparian setbacks for lots backing onto it. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City of Chilliwack 2025 residential property tax total was 4.50189 per $1,000 of assessed taxable value, including municipal, school and other authorities, per the City's 2025 rate table; drainage charges on land only are extra where they apply. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2527/Tax%5FRates%5Ffor%5Fthe%5FWebtugnc2fs1l9d%2Epdf
- The City of Chilliwack is a designated taxable area for the Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT), so Garrison owners must complete the annual SVT declaration if they own residential property there. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- Utility rates are billed quarterly by the City, under the Waterworks Regulation Bylaw and Sanitary Sewer System Regulation Bylaw named on its Tax and Utility Rates page. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
Read: 2026-11-03

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- BC Transit route 51, Vedder, runs downtown to the UFV Chilliwack campus via Vedder Road, serving the Garrison corridor. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/riders-guide/
- BC Transit route 55, Yarrow-Greendale, also starts at the UFV Chilliwack campus and passes the Vedder Road corridor west toward Yarrow. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/riders-guide/
- Route 66, Fraser Valley Express, connects Chilliwack to Lougheed Station via Highway 1 for regional trips — boarding point for a Garrison address was not confirmed this pass. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/riders-guide/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the City's floodplain maps for any lot near Luckakuck Creek or the Vedder River lowlands; the Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets flood construction levels in standard-dike areas. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- Check title for former-base covenants, statutory building schemes and easements, which are common in planned redevelopments — no Garrison-specific covenant was read this pass.
- Confirm strata documents directly: this guide note did not read any Garrison strata's bylaws, depreciation report or parkade/age rules.
