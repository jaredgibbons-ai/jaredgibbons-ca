# Matsqui — Questions for Jared

## Questions
1. What kinds of Matsqui deals do you actually see — working farms, acreages with houses, or village homes — and which is most common?
2. Who is buying in Matsqui Prairie: farm operators expanding, families wanting acreage, or buyers from Mission/Abbotsford trading up?
3. What sells fast versus slow on the prairie — for example village lots versus large ALR parcels?
4. What surprises Matsqui buyers most: the dike and freshet seepage, the ALR house-size and second-home rules, or the irrigation ditch system?
5. Tell one Matsqui story — a deal, a flood-season showing, or a farm sale — that shows what buying here is really like.
