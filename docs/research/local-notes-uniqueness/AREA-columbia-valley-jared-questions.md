# Columbia Valley — Questions for Jared

1. In Columbia Valley deals you have handled, what is the first servicing question buyers ask — septic versus the FVRD or Corix sewer areas, well water, or the volunteer fire department — and how does the answer change the offer?
2. Who buys in Columbia Valley: Cultus Lake people who want out of the Park Board lease area, acreage buyers priced out of Chilliwack, or recreational buyers who end up living there full time — and what do they say tipped it?
3. What sells fast in the valley and what sits: Lindell Beach lots near the lake, Sleepy Hollow and valley-floor acreages, or the Corix-serviced subdivisions like Aquadel Crossing and The Cottages?
4. What surprises Columbia Valley buyers most after subjects: no FVRD garbage pickup, the single road out on a summer weekend, school busing to Cultus Lake Elementary, or the Province — not a city — sending the tax bill?
5. Tell one Columbia Valley story: a deal where a septic field, a fire service area boundary, or an evacuation-route conversation changed the subjects, the price, or whether the deal firmed at all.
