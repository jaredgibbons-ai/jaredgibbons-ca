# Websters Corners Area Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: websters-corners-guide (Websters Corners; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Websters Corners is near the border of Maple Ridge and Mission, just off Dewdney Trunk Road — that Mission-border position is how the City itself locates it. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The City spells the name with an apostrophe — Webster's Corners — while the page slug and board sub-area use Websters Corners without one. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Webster's Corners Park is at 25496 Dewdney Trunk Road, next to Webster's Corners Elementary School — the Dewdney Trunk Road address that anchors the community. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/websters-corners-park
- The North 256 Street study area description places Webster's Corners south of the Alouette Valley bench, at elevations mainly between 60 and 160 metres above sea level in that study's geography. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/251127-environmental-and-hydrogeological-assessment-north-256-street-industrial-lands

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Large-lot and farm rules are parcel-specific in Websters Corners: the City's suite summary lists Agricultural (A-1, A-2, A-3) zones among zones where a secondary suite is permitted under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019, subject to the one-suite and 40% Gross Floor Area limits. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-03/Secondary-Suite-Brochure-2021.pdf
- Kanaka Creek watershed rules matter here: the North 256 Street environmental assessment states the southeast portion of that study area, north of Websters Corners, is part of the Kanaka Creek watershed, with tributaries to North Kanaka Creek. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/251127-environmental-and-hydrogeological-assessment-north-256-street-industrial-lands
- A suite must remain under the same legal title and cannot be a short-term or vacation rental — a limit that applies to a Websters Corners acreage suite as well. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-03/Secondary-Suite-Brochure-2021.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is in Websters Corners? **A:** Webster's Corners Elementary (Kindergarten to grade 7) is at 25554 Dewdney Trunk Road on the provincial School District 42 list. Webster's Corners Park, at 25496 Dewdney Trunk Road, sits next to the school, so school and park traffic share the same Dewdney Trunk Road frontage. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/school/04242020
- **Q:** Why do people mention film production in Websters Corners? **A:** Because the City itself calls Webster's Corners an active hub for film production in Maple Ridge, alongside its British Columbia Institute of Technology (BCIT) Woodlot mountain-bike trail network. A buyer should expect occasional production activity and trail-user parking near the Woodlot and Dewdney Trunk Road, rather than a quiet purely farm road at all times. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- **Q:** What is the Kanaka Creek connection? **A:** Kanaka Creek Regional Park flanks both sides of Kanaka Creek for about 11 kilometres from the Fraser River to just south of Websters Corners, and the park hosts the Bell-Irving Hatchery and Kanaka Creek Watershed Stewardship Centre. Creek setbacks and watershed rules are therefore a real check for lots draining toward Kanaka Creek. http://www.metrovancouver.org/services/parks/ParksPublications/KanakaCreekParkMap.pdf
- **Q:** Is Websters Corners close to Mission services? **A:** It is near the Maple Ridge–Mission border just off Dewdney Trunk Road, per the City, but it remains a City of Maple Ridge community in School District 42 — not a Mission community in School District 75. A buyer should not assume Mission schools, Mission taxes or Mission utilities apply to a Websters Corners address. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Webster's Corners Park is 1.267 hectares and lists drinking water, a fitness circuit, open green space, playground, parking and washrooms on the City's facility page. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/websters-corners-park
- The park page was last updated October 24, 2024 on the City site — check current facility status before relying on an amenity list. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/websters-corners-park
- The British Columbia Institute of Technology Woodlot trail network is the mountain-bike feature the City names for Webster's Corners — trail access is through the Woodlot, not through the neighbourhood park. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Flood-hazard creeks named in the North 256 Street assessment for the area north of Websters Corners include Mudd, Sawdust, Websters and North Kanaka Creeks, plus the Alouette River floodplain. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/251127-environmental-and-hydrogeological-assessment-north-256-street-industrial-lands
- Aquifer No. 38 underlies the western side of that northern study area and is recharged from the Alouette River — groundwater is a documented feature of the wider Websters Corners / 256 Street geography. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/251127-environmental-and-hydrogeological-assessment-north-256-street-industrial-lands

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check creek flood hazard first: the City-commissioned assessment identifies flood-hazard areas along Mudd, Sawdust, Websters and North Kanaka Creeks and in low-lying areas along the southern boundary of the northern study area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/251127-environmental-and-hydrogeological-assessment-north-256-street-industrial-lands
- Groundwater and springs are a documented risk factor north of Websters Corners: where ground drops sharply to the Alouette River floodplain, springs feeding small creeks are common, per the same assessment. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/251127-environmental-and-hydrogeological-assessment-north-256-street-industrial-lands
- The City monitors Kanaka Creek during high streamflow events and maintains dikes and pump stations — a Websters Corners buyer near Kanaka Creek should read MapleRidge.ca/Flood for the specific lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/high-streamflow-advisory-1
