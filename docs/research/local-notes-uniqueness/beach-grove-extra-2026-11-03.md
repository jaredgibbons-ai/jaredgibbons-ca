# Beach Grove Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: beach-grove (Beach Grove; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Beach Grove is the waterfront pocket of Tsawwassen in the City of Delta, on the Boundary Bay shore south of 12 Avenue along Beach Grove Road. https://letstalk.delta.ca/beachgroveseawall?tool=qanda
- The City's own flood project defines the pocket's shore: the Beach Grove Seawall runs approximately 1.2 kilometres between 12 Avenue and 17A Avenue along Boundary Bay's western shorefront. https://letstalk.delta.ca/beachgroveseawall?tool=qanda
- Beach Grove Park at 6051 17A Avenue and Beach Grove Elementary at 5955 17A Avenue anchor the inland side of the pocket. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/beach-grove-park https://admin.delta.ca/media/5293
- Boundary Beach and Point Roberts, in the United States, lie to the south, and Tsawwassen Central is inland to the west. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- This is the earliest Beach Grove page in the program, so shared facts are stated here in full rather than by pointer.
- The waterfront lots are zoned RD2 (Duplex/Single Detached Residential 2) at the confirmed sample, 1728 Beach Grove Road: the Delta zone sheet states RD2 is the zone for waterfront lots in Beach Grove and carries a 4,000 square metre minimum lot size for subdivision. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf
- Zoning is otherwise assigned parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps, so the inland streets are not all RD2 and each lot's zone must be read on DeltaMap. https://www.delta.ca/about-delta/deltamap?viewer=DeltaMapExternal.viewer/
- The City's seawall design form refers to a "Revised Design Height for Beach Grove Seawall" study and to Boundary Bay Village erosion studies, which is the City's own record that this shore's houses sit behind an engineered seawall rather than a natural beach bank. https://admin.delta.ca/media/287

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Hazard Development Permit Area SD3, Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands, in Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw No. 8400, Appendix B, sets a minimum floor level of 2.9 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum, a minimum land surface elevation of 2.3 metres around construction, and no building within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, section 6.2.3, sets a waterfront setback for lots fronting Boundary Bay along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue, under which a seawall may encroach into the minimum front setback; the bylaw states no Beach Grove flood construction level. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- A new or replacement seawall must meet the City's seawall design requirements: crest elevation of 4.1 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum, designed so the crest can be raised a further 1.4 metres in the future, a wave deflector, erosion protection at the toe, and construction on private property. https://admin.delta.ca/media/287
- Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing zoning, adopted June 24, 2024, allows up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the bylaw and to any hazard permit area on the lot. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a Beach Grove house behind a dike or a seawall? **A:** Behind a seawall on this shore. The City states the Beach Grove Seawall runs approximately 1.2 kilometres between 12 and 17A Avenues and provides essential flood protection for the neighbourhood, and it ran a condition assessment and concept design project for future upgrades funded through Emergency Management BC's National Disaster Mitigation Program. Check the individual lot's seawall and any flood covenant on title. https://letstalk.delta.ca/beachgroveseawall?tool=qanda
- **Q:** What happens here in a storm surge? **A:** The City treats Beach Grove as a named flood-risk community. Its December 27, 2022 advisory expected high water with strong southeast winds to elevate flood risk for Beach Grove and Boundary Bay, moved the area to Stage 3 of its Staged Flood Preparedness Plan with portable pumps and generators, and placed sandbags at Seaview Road, the 1A Avenue boat launch, 3rd Avenue and 16th Avenue beach accesses. http://www.delta.ca/node/1209
- **Q:** Which elementary school is in the pocket? **A:** Beach Grove Elementary at 5955 17A Avenue is the School District No. 37 (SD37) school inside Beach Grove. Catchments are address-based and SD37 publishes Tsawwassen Area catchment boundary maps, so confirm the exact address against the current map or the SD37 locator; being inside the pocket does not by itself prove the assignment. https://admin.delta.ca/media/5293 https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/student-registration/catchment-area-maps/
- **Q:** Who owns the beach in front of a waterfront house? **A:** The Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore, and private moorage or dock use sits on tenure by lease or licence through FrontCounter BC, under General Permission or Specific Permission — not on land the seller owns. Budget and title checks for a waterfront lot should include the foreshore tenure position, not just the upland parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **Q:** Can I walk the shore from Beach Grove? **A:** Yes, on the dike trail system. Metro Vancouver's Boundary Bay Regional Park includes Centennial Beach and the Dyke Trail, which runs along the north shore of Boundary Bay for 17 kilometres between 17A Avenue and Mud Bay Park, and Delta's own birding guide describes a 20-kilometre dyke trail around Boundary Bay between Beach Grove and Mud Bay Park in Surrey. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park https://www.delta.ca/media/34

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Beach Grove Park at 6051 17A Avenue is listed by the City with dawn-to-dusk hours and picnic tables, playgrounds, sport courts, tennis courts, trails, washrooms and an off-leash area. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/beach-grove-park
- The City is the diking authority here and operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure across Delta. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Route 601 serves Boundary Bay on trips via 12 Avenue, Boundary Bay Road, 3 Avenue and 67 Street to 1A Avenue, continuing via South Delta Exchange and Ladner Exchange to Bridgeport Station on the Canada Line. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- A seawall on a Beach Grove lot is the owner's structure, not the City's: the City's design form requires the seawall to be constructed on private property and certified by a structural engineer for flood protection integrity. https://admin.delta.ca/media/287

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check on DeltaMap whether the specific lot falls inside Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 before offering; DeltaMap shows flood protection, permit areas, Agricultural Land Reserve status and lot dimensions by parcel. https://www.delta.ca/about-delta/deltamap?viewer=DeltaMapExternal.viewer/
- Ask for the seawall's engineer certification and any flood covenant on title: the City requires certification of structural and flood protection integrity for existing seawalls, and its general pages do not certify any individual lot's wall. https://admin.delta.ca/media/287
- Check title through the Land Title and Survey Authority: the City states title charges can affect a property's use or redevelopment. https://www.delta.ca:443/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
