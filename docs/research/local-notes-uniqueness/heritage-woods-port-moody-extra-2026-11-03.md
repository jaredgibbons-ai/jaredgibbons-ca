# Heritage Woods Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: heritage-woods-port-moody (Heritage Woods, Port Moody; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Heritage Woods is the eastern part of Port Moody's Heritage Mountain grouping on the north shore, below Anmore and beside the Coquitlam border, centred on David Avenue east of Heritage Mountain Boulevard. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- The City publishes no street-by-street boundary for Heritage Woods alone; the Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw No. 3522, Chapter 14, Section 14.3 treats it with Heritage Mountain, Twin Creeks, Noons Creek and Mountain Meadows. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Its house streets named on TransLink route 183 are Forest Park Way and Panorama Drive; David Avenue continues east into Coquitlam to Pinetree Way. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt183.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The OCP describes this grouping as single detached homes and townhouses plus a small number of apartment units; shared stock and rules facts are written in full in the heritage-woods-port-moody-houses-for-sale note. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- The landmark public building is Heritage Woods Secondary School at 1300 David Avenue, with North Shore Community Park's fields at the same address. http://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/facilities-fields-and-rentals/sports-fields/
- Aspenwood Elementary, 2001 Panorama Drive, and Aspenwood Park, 200 Panorama Place, sit inside the Panorama Drive loop on the neighbourhood's east side. http://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/facilities-fields-and-rentals/sports-fields/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Port Moody-wide zoning, small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH), tax and utility facts are written in full in the heritage-mountain-port-moody-houses-for-sale note; Heritage Woods specifics follow. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- Heritage Woods is outside the Inlet Centre and Moody Centre Transit-Oriented Areas, so the SSMUH position that applies here is the standard RS and RT zone position, not the transit-area exemption. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- Environmentally sensitive and steep-slope development permit areas can apply on this hillside, and creek lots need a riparian assessment with Zoning Bylaw No. 2937 Section 5.4 setbacks. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Heritage Woods Secondary inside the neighbourhood? **A:** Yes. Heritage Woods Secondary, Grades 9 to 12, is at 1300 David Avenue inside Heritage Woods, and TransLink route 183 names its stop on David Avenue. Catchment still depends on the home address in School District No. 43 (SD43), so verify the exact house in the School Locator rather than assuming the nearest secondary school is the assigned one. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-moody
- **Q:** Aspenwood or Heritage Mountain elementary for my street? **A:** It depends on the address. Aspenwood Elementary is at 2001 Panorama Drive on the Heritage Woods side and Heritage Mountain Elementary at 125 Ravine Drive to the west, and an earlier SD43 locator run for 1300 David Avenue returned Heritage Mountain Elementary. The two catchments meet inside this area, so the street alone does not settle it. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-moody
- **Q:** Where do students go for middle school? **A:** Eagle Mountain Middle, in Anmore, is the middle school returned for this catchment family. It sits outside Port Moody, north of David Avenue, so the middle-school trip crosses into the Village of Anmore within the same SD43. Buyers moving from a one-school city should confirm the middle and secondary pair for their exact address before subjects are removed. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** Can I walk to SkyTrain from Heritage Woods? **A:** No station is in Heritage Woods. Route 183 runs from Coquitlam Central Station via Panorama Drive, Forest Park Way and David Avenue to Inlet Centre and Moody Centre stations, so the rail link is a bus ride east or west along David Avenue, not a walk. Plan the commute around the bus, and check the current 183 timetable for frequency. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt183.pdf
- **Q:** Where does the Bert Flinn trail system start? **A:** At David Avenue and Heritage Mountain Boulevard, at the neighbourhood's west edge. The City lists Bert Flinn Park's wide gravel trail from that entrance as an all-day off-leash area, with a Morning Off-Leash Mini Loop pilot trail open to off-leash dogs between 6 a.m. and 10 a.m. only; dogs must be on leash everywhere else in the park. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/parks/off-leash-dog-parks/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Dogs off leash outside the designated Bert Flinn trails can draw a ticket under Bylaw No. 3343; in all other Port Moody public spaces dogs must be on leash. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/parks/off-leash-dog-parks/
- Heritage Mountain Community Centre is at 200 Panorama Place, inside Heritage Woods beside Aspenwood Park — despite carrying the Heritage Mountain name. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/
- North Shore Community Park is listed at 1400 David Avenue for courts and 1300 David Avenue for a lit artificial turf field and a grass baseball and soccer field. http://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/facilities-fields-and-rentals/sports-fields/
- Port Moody Public Library is at 100 Newport Drive, south of the inlet; no library branch or grocery store inside Heritage Woods was verified on an official source. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/
- David Avenue runs east into Coquitlam as well as west into Port Moody, so east-end homes use Coquitlam Central Station as readily as the Port Moody stations. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt183.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Hillside lots here can carry both environmentally sensitive and steep-slope development permit areas; both still apply to SSMUH units, so a coach house design needs the permit-area check first. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- Homes backing onto the Bert Flinn Park forest are forest-interface lots; the City's hazards page notes Port Moody is surrounded by forests and parks and directs owners to FireSmart Canada for wildfire preparation. https://www.portmoody.ca/resident-services/hazards/
- Creek lots need a riparian assessment and Zoning Bylaw No. 2937 Section 5.4 setbacks confirmed before any addition is designed. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
