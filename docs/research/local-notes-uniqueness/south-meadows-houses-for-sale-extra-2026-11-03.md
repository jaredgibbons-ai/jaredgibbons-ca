## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- South Meadows is the board's name; the City calls this pocket South Bonson, the Bonson Road corridor south of Hammond Road and the Canadian Pacific Kansas City rail tracks to the Fraser River; the full shared area facts are in the south-meadows-pitt-meadows note. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/council-supports-business-park-land-use
- No official boundary is published for the board name: the working limits are Hammond Road and the rail line to the north, the Fraser River to the south, the Bonson Road corridor in the middle, and the Golden Ears Business Park lands toward Harris Road to the west. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- Osprey Village, at Fraser Way and Barnston View Road, is the pocket's village centre. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/council-supports-business-park-land-use

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses sit under the RS Large Lot Residential, R-3, R-4 and R-6 Urban Residential zones in Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011: RS has a minimum lot area of 0.2 hectares, and residential buildings must be sited at least 15.0 metres from watercourses and key or constructed ditches. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- The unit counts step up by zone: R-3 allows up to 3 units per lot, R-4 up to 4 units, and R-6 up to five units, or six where at least one unit meets the adaptable housing requirements in the BC Building Code on lots close to frequent transit. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- The townhouse zones sit beside the house zones in the same bylaw: RM-1 Multi-Family Residential 1 (townhouse) carries a minimum lot area of 0.4 hectares, and RM-2 (townhouse and low-density apartment) a minimum lot area of 3,035 square metres. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- One secondary suite is allowed per principal dwelling unit, at 33 to 90 square metres, inside the same building, where the zone permits it; garden suites are one per lot at 33 to 90 square metres in the zones listed in the City's garden suite guide, including RS, and a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- Under the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules applied citywide, lots of 280 square metres or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 square metres up to 4 units, and lots over 280 square metres within 400 metres of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units; the City's SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986 was adopted June 25, 2024. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- The governing plan is the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022, which repealed the former Official Community Plan Bylaw 2352, 2007. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- Pitt Meadows is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve South Bonson? **A:** For the sample address 10932 Barnston View Road, the School District No. 42 (SD42) locator result is Davie Jones Elementary for Kindergarten to Grade 7 and Pitt Meadows Secondary for Grades 8 to 12, because SD42 has no middle-school tier; no public school building stands inside the sub-area, and the closest listed elementaries are Edith McDermott Elementary at 12178 Bonson Road and Pitt Meadows Elementary at 11941 Harris Road. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q:** Can I add a suite and a garden suite? **A:** Not both: one secondary suite per principal dwelling is allowed where the zone permits it, at 33 to 90 square metres inside the same building, and garden suites run one per lot at 33 to 90 square metres in the listed zones including RS — but a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Q:** What does the 1,215-square-metre figure mean? **A:** It is a subdivision minimum only: the R-3, R-4 and R-6 zones carry a minimum lot area for subdivision of 1,215 square metres, and the City's SSMUH implementation update clarifies that figure does not apply to development on existing lots. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **Q:** Where is the nearest hospital? **A:** Ridge Meadows Hospital, at 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge, is the Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency care serving Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows, and the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 121-11900 Haney Place, Maple Ridge. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the ditch and watercourse setback before planning: residential buildings in these zones are sited at least 15.0 metres from watercourses and key or constructed ditches, and much of the pocket is low dyke-protected ground by the Fraser River. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- Confirm the zone on the specific lot — RS, R-3, R-4 or R-6 — because the unit count and the subdivision minimum turn on it. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- Confirm the SD42 catchment for the exact address in the district locator, since no public school building stands inside the sub-area. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
