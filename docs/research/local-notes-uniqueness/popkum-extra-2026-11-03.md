# Popkum — Extra Local Notes
Page: popkum (Popkum; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Popkum is an unincorporated community in Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area D (Popkum–Bridal Falls), east of the City of Chilliwack — it is not inside the City, and the FVRD, not the City, is its local government. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area D - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- Yale Road is the community's main street: the active development file in Popkum covers 52285, 52375, 52425 and 52445 Yale Road. https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/popkum-major-ocp-amendment-development-proposal
- Statistics Canada's 2021 Census designated-places list, as carried in an FVRD document, names Popkum as a designated place (hamlet) within Electoral Area D. http://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Tenders~and~RFPs/RFP-26012 - Preparedness Resiliency and Emergency Program.pdf
- Elgey Road, off Popkum Road North, leads to Cheam Lake Wetlands Regional Park at 53480 Elgey Road, Rosedale. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/cheam-lake-wetlands.html?media=standard
- No official boundary for the Popkum community itself was found in the sources read — Electoral Area D's boundaries are not a community boundary; see NOT CONFIRMED. https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/popkum-major-ocp-amendment-development-proposal

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The housing question in Popkum is lot size under the Official Community Plan (OCP): the FVRD's engagement page states the OCP's expressed minimum single-family lot size here is 800 square metres, and the current Yale Road proposal seeks 69 of its 108 lots at 600 square metres, below that standard. https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/popkum-major-ocp-amendment-development-proposal
- The existing stock the FVRD documents describe is rural and acreage housing plus a serviced subdivision area: the FVRD lists a West Popkum drainage service area and an Area D integrated water system among its Electoral Area D services. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area D - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- No official source read for this note publishes a Popkum build-era, dwelling-count or strata-versus-freehold profile, so none is stated — see NOT CONFIRMED. https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/popkum-major-ocp-amendment-development-proposal

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The plan is the FVRD's, not the City's: the Official Community Plan for Popkum–Bridal Falls is FVRD Bylaw No. 1501, 2018, and the FVRD's bylaw library carries it as the Area D OCP bylaw. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws.html?media=contrast
- City of Chilliwack zoning does not apply in Popkum: the FVRD is the local government for its electoral areas, providing planning and development approvals, building inspection and bylaw enforcement, while member municipalities govern themselves. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area D - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- Suites are part of the current Yale Road proposal's form: the revised 2026 proposal describes 108 single-family lots "with suites," which is a proposal position, not an approval — the file's status is in Section 6. https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/popkum-major-ocp-amendment-development-proposal
- Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) parcels here follow the provincial frame: one principal residence at a maximum of 500 square metres, a suite only inside that principal residence, and at most one additional detached residence of 90 square metres (parcels of 40 hectares or less) or 186 square metres (over 40 hectares) without an Agricultural Land Commission application. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Roads are not an FVRD service: an FVRD document states roads in the electoral areas are under the jurisdiction of the provincial Ministry of Transportation and Transit, not the regional district. http://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Tenders~and~RFPs/RFP-26012 - Preparedness Resiliency and Emergency Program.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Do I pay City of Chilliwack taxes in Popkum? **A:** No — there is no municipal property tax bill from the City here, because Popkum is not in the City. Electoral Area D owners pay for the services their area receives through the provincial rural property tax system and through FVRD utility billing; the FVRD's 2026 Area D sheet shows water billed by user fee while sewer and West Popkum drainage show no utility billing and are funded through taxation instead. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area D - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- **Q:** What does water cost, and how is it billed? **A:** Area D water is metered and billed quarterly, in January, April, July and October. The Fraser Valley Regional District's 2026 residential rates set the Area D metered water flat rate at $77.00 covering up to 100 cubic metres, with an overage rate above that volume, under the Water Supply Systems Fees and Charges Amendment Bylaw No. 1824, 2026. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Sewer~and~Water/Water Systems fees and charges amendment BL 1824, 2026.pdf
- **Q:** Which school serves Popkum, and is there one in the community? **A:** No school is located in Popkum itself. The nearest district school confirmed by name and address is Rosedale Traditional Community at 50850 Yale Road, Rosedale, in School District No. 33 (Chilliwack); catchments are address-based, so confirm the exact Popkum address, including the secondary school, in the district's school locator before relying on it. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/education/work/bc_k12_schools.csv
- **Q:** Can I take a dog to Cheam Lake Wetlands? **A:** No — pets are not permitted in that park. Cheam Lake Wetlands Regional Park is a 107-hectare lake, marsh and forest park in Electoral Area D at 53480 Elgey Road, 184 bird species have been recorded there, and the FVRD states pets and other domestic animals are not permitted under FVRD Bylaw No. 1500, 2018. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/cheam-lake-wetlands.html?media=standard
- **Q:** Is the big Yale Road subdivision approved? **A:** Not as of the last FVRD update read for this note. On May 28, 2026 the FVRD Board considered the major OCP amendment and rezoning for 52285 to 52445 Yale Road and resolved to refer the application back to staff to work with the applicant on options for residential lots of 800 square metres — the file was sent back, not approved. https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/popkum-major-ocp-amendment-development-proposal

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Utility bills arrive four times a year, not monthly: in Electoral Area D all residential utilities are billed every three months, in January, April, July and October, using a mixture of metered and flat rates plus taxation for some service areas. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area D - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- Sewer and drainage may not appear on a utility bill at all: the FVRD's 2026 Area D rate sheet shows Sewer – Area D and Drainage – West Popkum with "No Utility Billing," funded through taxation instead. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area D - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- The wetlands park's boardwalk is being renewed: an FVRD request for quotations dated May 14, 2026 covers environmental assessment and monitoring for replacing two floating boardwalk segments totalling about 66 metres at Cheam Lake Wetlands Regional Park, 53480 Elgey Road. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Tenders~and~RFPs/RFQ 26015 - Cheam Environmental Assessment - Monitoring.pdf
- Popkum is inside a specified area for the additional property transfer tax: the Province's list of areas for the additional Property Transfer Tax names Fraser Valley Regional District Electoral Area D, including Bridal Falls and Popkum. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/additional-property-transfer-tax/bc-areas

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Popkum's active file is the Yale Road major OCP amendment and rezoning: applicant Norah Properties Land Corporation proposes to redevelop 52285, 52375, 52425 and 52445 Yale Road, Area D. The 2026 revised proposal is 108 single-family lots with suites (39 lots at 800 square metres and 69 lots at 600 square metres), 1.02 hectares (2.5 acres) of commercial outdoor storage, removal of the earlier townhome and row-home component, and 4 percent total park dedication. STATUS: referred back by the FVRD Board on May 28, 2026 for work on 800-square-metre lot options, after the May 14, 2026 Electoral Area Services Committee — not approved, read 2026-11-03. https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/popkum-major-ocp-amendment-development-proposal
- The FVRD notes parts of the revised proposal remain non-compliant with key OCP expectations, particularly the commercial storage use and lot sizes below the 800-square-metre single-family standard, and that a new public information meeting is the next step before any first reading. STATUS: pre-first-reading, read 2026-11-03. https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/popkum-major-ocp-amendment-development-proposal

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- There is no City of Chilliwack tax bill in Popkum: it is an FVRD electoral area, and each electoral area pays property taxes through the provincial government according to the particular services it receives. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area D - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- 2026 Area D water: metered users pay a $77.00 quarterly charge covering up to 100 cubic metres, and $1.13 per cubic metre over 100 cubic metres, under FVRD Bylaw No. 1824, 2026, adopted March 26, 2026. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Sewer~and~Water/Water Systems fees and charges amendment BL 1824, 2026.pdf
- Area D also carries its own development cost charges: the FVRD bylaw library lists an Area D Water System Development Cost Charge bylaw and Development Cost Charges for the Area D Integrated Water System (Bylaw 1096, 2011), plus a West Popkum Storm Drainage development cost charge bylaw. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws.html?media=contrast
- Whether the Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies to a Popkum address is not confirmed in this note: the Province's taxable-areas list names municipalities including the City of Chilliwack, and Popkum is outside that city — see NOT CONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Highway 1 is the corridor: Cheam Lake Wetlands access directions route drivers from Highway 1 at Exit 138 to Yale Road East, or Exit 135 to Highway 9 and then east on Yale Road to Popkum Road. https://www.orniverse.com/site/11904/cheam-lake-wetlands-regional-park

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Confirm servicing parcel by parcel: Area D services are a mixture of metered water, tax-funded sewer and a named West Popkum drainage service area, so which services a specific lot actually has is a check with the FVRD, not an assumption. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area D - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- Confirm ALR status on the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR maps before valuing any second-dwelling, farm-use or non-farm-use story — ALR status is parcel-specific. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- Confirm the parcel's zone and OCP designation with the FVRD: the governing documents are the Popkum–Bridal Falls OCP, Bylaw No. 1501, 2018, and the FVRD zoning bylaw for Electoral Area D, not any City of Chilliwack bylaw. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws.html?media=contrast
- Check site-specific hazard mapping with the FVRD before building or buying: no area-level flood or debris-flow statement for Popkum is carried in this note, because none was confirmed on an FVRD page read for it — see NOT CONFIRMED. https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/popkum-major-ocp-amendment-development-proposal
