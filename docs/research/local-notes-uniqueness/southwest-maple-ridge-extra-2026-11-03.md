# Southwest Maple Ridge Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: southwest-maple-ridge (Southwest Maple Ridge, Maple Ridge; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Southwest Maple Ridge is a board sub-area name only; the City's own name for this corner is Hammond, bounded west by Pitt Meadows and the Katzie First Nation, south by the Fraser River, and north by the Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road commercial areas. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- Port Hammond and Port Haney are the other City neighbourhood names buyers use inside and at the east edge of this sub-area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Hammond's stock is older single-detached houses around a historic commercial area, with mainly single-family development east toward Port Haney; Maple Ridge-wide stock facts are in the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note — see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- The standard house zone is RS-1 (Single Detached Residential) in Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019, with subdivision minimums of 668.0 square metres lot area, 18.0 metres width and 27.0 metres depth. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- One secondary suite per lot is permitted inside the house, to a maximum of 40% of gross floor area, and not within the floodplain area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- A Hammond Development Permit Area applies to properties within the Hammond Area Plan, on top of the base zoning. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- Escarpment lots carry extra rules: within the Fraser River Escarpment policy area, drainage from new construction must connect to the City storm system, and lots within 300 metres of the crest need a geotechnical report on a Section 219 covenant before a building permit. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools are in Southwest Maple Ridge? **A:** Hammond Elementary, 11520 203 Street, Maple Ridge Elementary, 20820 River Road, and Westview Secondary, 20905 Wicklund Avenue, are physically in the area. School District No. 42 has no middle tier — elementary is Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary is Grades 8 to 12 — and catchment is by address, so check the exact address in the district locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- **Q:** Did the Hammond recreation projects pass? **A:** No — on October 17, 2026 voters rejected both Hammond borrowing questions: the Aquatics and Recreation Centre bylaw drew 6,841 Yes votes against 14,218 No, and the Multi-Use Community Park bylaw drew 6,209 Yes against 14,797 No. The City states it did not receive elector approval to adopt any of the loan bylaws or proceed with the borrowing. https://www.mapleridge.ca/art-parks-rec/recreation-ready
- **Q:** Can a floodplain lot here have a rental suite? **A:** No — Zoning Bylaw section 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation on floodplain properties, and no habitable floor space may sit below the Fraser River flood construction level of 6.7 metres geodetic on affected lots. Hammond fronts the Fraser River, so check the lot's flood status before counting any suite income. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q:** How do trains serve this corner? **A:** Two West Coast Express stations bracket the area: Maple Meadows Station, 20010 Dunn Avenue, on the west side, and Port Haney Station, 22300 River Road, at the east edge. Trains run on weekdays between Waterfront Station in downtown Vancouver and Mission City, so the service is a commuter service, not all-day transit. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Hammond began as Port Hammond Junction: the Hammond brothers donated land to the Canadian Pacific Railway on condition the first Maple Ridge station be built in Hammond, and the community grew around the station, yards and a cedar mill. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/hammond-1st-reading-report
- Hammond Park, 20577 Lorne Avenue, carries a playground, sport court, tennis court and pickleball court, while Hammond Community Park holds the Larry Walker and Hammond Stadium ballfields and the Hammond Outdoor Pool. https://www.mapleridge.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Hammond-Park-58
- The Maple Ridge Golf Course, 20818 Golf Lane, is the site the rejected community park bylaw proposed to convert — it remains a golf course site after the vote. https://www.mapleridge.ca/art-parks-rec/recreation-ready
- In the escarpment policy area, City policy says septic fields are to be avoided and new residences require sanitary sewers, so confirm sewer connection on older Hammond houses. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Buses 743 and 744 loop through Hammond streets — 207 Street, Lorne Avenue and the Hammond area — between Meadowtown and Haney Place via Maple Meadows Station, and route 701 runs on Dewdney Trunk Road at the area's north edge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt744.pdf
- The Golden Ears Bridge at the area's west side is the road link to Langley and Surrey across the Fraser River. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/maintenance-and-upgrade-program/bridge-projects

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the Fraser River Escarpment layer first: the policy area runs from 207 Street west to 224 Street east, 124 Avenue north to the escarpment crest south, and lots within 100 metres of the crest need a detailed geotechnical report on seismic slope failure. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- Check flood construction level on any river-side lot: the level is 6.7 metres geodetic including freeboard, a covenant on title identifies floodplain construction, and the City page does not clear a specific lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
