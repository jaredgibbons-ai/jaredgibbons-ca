## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- In the Township, lands in the Willowbrook Transit-Oriented Area are exempt from required off-street parking for residential uses, except parking for persons with disabilities, under the amendment to Zoning Bylaw 1987, No. 2500. https://strongtownslangley.org/campaigns/tol/toolkit/ssmuhplanner/sources/2024-11-18%20Langley%20(Township)%20SSMUH%20Bylaw.pdf
- In the City, all lands designated Transit-Oriented Residential are subject to a development permit for building form and character, so top-floor form is set site by site in a CD zone, not by a general penthouse rule. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3261
- The CD92 Eastleigh approval required 203 parking spaces and provided 169, including visitor spaces, a variance pattern a penthouse buyer should expect in City CD zones. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3261

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is there a Langley bylaw that defines a penthouse?
**A:** No penthouse definition was found in the City or Township material read. Both municipalities handle tall or top-floor apartment form through site-specific Comprehensive Development zones and development permits instead. In the City, the Eastleigh Crescent buildings were each rezoned to their own CD zone, CD92 and CD95, with height, coverage and parking set in that bylaw. A buyer reads the building's own CD bylaw, not a citywide penthouse rule. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3277-zoning-bylaw-1996-no-2100-amendment-no-202-2024-no-3277

**Q:** Does a top-floor unit in the Willowbrook area come with parking?
**A:** Not by right under the Township rule read: lands designated in the Willowbrook Transit-Oriented Area are exempt from required off-street parking for residential uses, except accessible parking. If parking is provided, the spaces must meet the electric vehicle charging requirements in Section 107.3 of the Township bylaw. Confirm the specific unit's parking on title and in the strata plan. https://strongtownslangley.org/campaigns/tol/toolkit/ssmuhplanner/sources/2024-11-18%20Langley%20(Township)%20SSMUH%20Bylaw.pdf

**Q:** City or Township — why does it matter for a penthouse purchase?
**A:** Because the two Langleys run different zoning bylaws, different development permit areas and different tax notices. The City uses Zoning Bylaw 1996, No. 2100 and the Township uses Zoning Bylaw 1987, No. 2500. The same street name can sit in either municipality, so the civic address's municipality, stated on the tax notice and BC Assessment record, is the first check before applying any rule. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3261

**Q:** What should a buyer verify about a claimed penthouse in Langley?
**A:** That the word comes from the developer or strata, not a listing. In this pass no developer or strata page read used the term for a Langley building, so any penthouse label here is UNCONFIRMED until the strata plan, the building's CD bylaw and the developer's own material agree on the top-floor unit's layout and exclusive-use areas. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3261
