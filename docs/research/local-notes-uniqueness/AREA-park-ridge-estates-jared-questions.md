# Park Ridge Estates — Questions for Jared

1. In Park Ridge Estates deals — the established houses south of David Avenue, west of Hyde Creek — what do you see differ from deals for the brand-new builds higher up Burke Mountain in Smiling Creek and Partington Creek: condition fights, subjects, or price per foot?
2. Who buys in Park Ridge Estates: move-up families wanting Burke Mountain without a new-build price, buyers aimed at Leigh Elementary or École Nestor Elementary, or people priced out of Westwood Plateau — and what do they say tipped it?
3. What sells fast here and what sits: do houses backing onto the Hyde Creek greenbelt move first, while houses fronting David Avenue or sitting under the slope wait — and how do the 1990s builds compete with newer Burke Mountain stock?
4. What surprises Park Ridge Estates buyers: that there is no school inside the pocket itself, the drive down David Avenue and Coast Meridian Road to Coquitlam Central Station, or that the Burke Mountain Community Centre on Princeton Avenue is still years from opening?
5. Tell one Park Ridge Estates story: a deal along the David Avenue corridor or backing Hyde Creek where the creek, the slope, or the coming Burke Mountain Village changed the price, the subjects, or the outcome.
