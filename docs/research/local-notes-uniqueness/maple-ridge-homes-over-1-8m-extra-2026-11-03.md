## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- This band has no streets of its own: it is a price filter applied across the whole City of Maple Ridge, not a neighbourhood, plan area or board designation, so the boundary a buyer checks is the municipal boundary and the parcel's own zone, not a band boundary. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The City's own neighbourhood list names the east-side areas where this band's rural and acreage stock sits: Webster's Corners, Whonnock, Ruskin, Thornhill and Silver Valley, alongside Albion/Kanaka, Port Hammond, Port Haney, Town Centre and Yennadon. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Webster's Corners sits near the border of Maple Ridge and Mission, just off Dewdney Trunk Road, according to the City's neighbourhood page. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Ruskin is described by the City as an area along the Stave River, and Silver Valley as located on the north side of the Alouette River. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Above this band the stock is defined less by house style than by land: the rural zones in Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 set subdivision minimums far above urban lots — A-1 (Small Holding Agricultural, s.501) 2.0 hectares, A-2 (Upland Agricultural, s.502) 4.0 hectares and A-3 (Extensive Agricultural, s.503) 8.0 hectares, each with the lot width and depth set out in Schedule B of the bylaw. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- The two rural residential zones sit below the agricultural zones in lot size: RS-2 (Single Detached Suburban Residential, s.610) has a subdivision minimum of 0.4 hectares, and RS-3 (Single Detached Rural Residential, s.611) has a minimum of 0.8 hectares where a community water system is available and 2.0 hectares where it is not, per Schedule B and s.611.4 of the same bylaw. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- Servicing follows the zone, not the price: the bylaw states the RS-2 zone is outside the urban area boundary and may be connected to the community water system but may not be connected to the community sanitary sewer system, and the RS-3 zone is outside the urban area boundary and may not be connected to either system. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- In the A-1 and A-3 zones, single detached residential use is limited to one dwelling per lot, and buildings for that use are capped at 10% lot coverage under ss.501.5–501.6 and 503.5–503.6 of the bylaw. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- Thornhill, one of the east-side areas this band reaches, is described by the City as a heavily forested, mainly residential area with several farms and nurseries. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Urban RS-1 subdivision minimums for this city are stated in full in the maple-ridge-homes-under-1-8m note — see the maple-ridge-homes-under-1-8m note. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Agricultural zoning carries its own residential limits: in the A-1, A-2 and A-3 zones the permitted principal uses are Agricultural, Cannabis Commercial Production, Forest Resource and Single Detached Residential, as ss.501.2, 502.2 and 503.2 of Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 state them. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- On a lot inside the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR), the City's Farm Home Plate requirements in s.402 of the zoning bylaw apply, as s.501.11 of the bylaw states for A-1 lots. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- The Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) states that land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless the parcel qualifies for an additional residence, and that local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR through zoning. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- The ALC sets a size ceiling on that one residence: a principal residence in the ALR is permitted without an ALC application only at a total floor area of 500 square metres or less, with no more than 1,000 square metres of fill placed for its construction. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Three city-wide rules are stated in full in the companion note and are named here once only: the one-secondary-suite rule, the small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) position, and Transit-Oriented Area Bylaw No. 8001-2024 — see the maple-ridge-homes-under-1-8m note. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Does a home over $1.8M in Maple Ridge sit in one particular area? **A:** No. The band is a price filter over the whole city, and the City's neighbourhood list runs from Port Hammond and Port Haney in the southwest to Webster's Corners, Whonnock and Ruskin in the east. A buyer in this band is therefore choosing first between serviced urban lots and the rural east-side areas, and only then between individual parcels and their zones. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
**Q:** What zones cover the acreage homes in this band, and how large are the lots? **A:** The rural families in Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 are the agricultural zones A-1, A-2 and A-3, with subdivision minimums of 2.0, 4.0 and 8.0 hectares, and the rural residential zones RS-2 at 0.4 hectares and RS-3 at 0.8 hectares with community water or 2.0 hectares without it. Those minimums govern subdivision; an existing lot's size is read from its title and survey. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
**Q:** Can I add a second house on an ALR acreage in this band? **A:** Only within the ALC's additional-residence rules. The Commission states an additional residence may be permitted without an application where there is only one existing residence when construction begins, the additional residence is detached, and its total floor area is no more than 90 square metres on parcels of 40 hectares or less, or 186 square metres on parcels over 40 hectares. A Notice of Intent may still be required if fill is needed. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
**Q:** How does Property Transfer Tax change above this band? **A:** The Province applies the general rate in steps: 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000. A purchase in this band therefore reaches the 3% step on any portion above $2,000,000, and a further tax described in section 7 can apply above $3,000,000 of residential value. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
**Q:** Will a principal residence in this band still receive the home owner grant in full? **A:** Not necessarily, because the grant turns on assessed value, not purchase price. The Province states the grant threshold for 2026 is $2,075,000, that the grant is reduced by $5 for each $1,000 of assessed value over that threshold, and that the regular grant is $0 if the residential or partitioned value is over $2,189,000. Partitioning may apply where a property holds more than one separate residence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Thornhill has a mountain biking and hiking trail network on 256 Street, which the City counts at 17 trails, described as moderately difficult and typically taking an hour to complete. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Ruskin still has a number of shake and sawmills active along the Stave River, and the Ruskin Community Hall remains a meeting place for the local community, according to the City. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Whonnock Lake Park, in the Whonnock neighbourhood, has a small sandy beach, a swimming area and hiking trails, and the Ridge Canoe and Kayak Club is based on Whonnock Lake. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Webster's Corners holds the BCIT Woodlot trail network used by mountain bikers, and the City also names the area as an active hub for film production in Maple Ridge. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Kanaka Creek Regional Park, on the Albion side of the city, holds the Bell-Irving Hatchery and fish fence, which Metro Vancouver states support salmonid enhancement for chum and coho salmon and cutthroat trout in Kanaka Creek. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- In the ALR, a secondary suite is permitted only when it is entirely contained within the principal residence; a separate or detached dwelling cannot be used as the suite, under the ALC's housing rules. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Replacing a house on ALR land has a sequencing rule: the ALC states a new principal residence may be built without an application if the existing one is demolished and removed first, but ALC approval is required to keep living in the existing residence while the new one is constructed. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Property Transfer Tax (PTT) is the cost that changes shape in this band: the Province states the general rate is 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- Above $3,000,000 a further tax applies: the Province states that if a property has residential property worth over $3,000,000, a further 2% tax is applied to the residential property value greater than $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- On a mixed-class property that further 2% applies only to the residential portion, and where land is classed as farm only because it is used for an owner's or farmer's dwelling, up to 0.5 hectares is treated as residential property — a point that bears directly on farm-classed acreages in this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- PTT is calculated on the fair market value of the property, meaning land and improvements, on the day it is registered at the Land Title Office, and the Province states it is not the same as annual property taxes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- No first-time buyer relief reaches this band: the Province's first time home buyers' program requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less for a full exemption, effective April 1, 2024, with a partial exemption only where fair market value is more than $835,000 and less than $860,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- A foreign national, foreign corporation or taxable trustee must also pay the additional property transfer tax on the fair market value of the residential portion of a property within a specified area of British Columbia, as the Province's PTT page states. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check ALR status parcel by parcel before anything else on a rural purchase: the ALC directs buyers to its maps and online mapping tools to answer whether a property is in the ALR, and no area-wide statement substitutes for that parcel check. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- Check what the ALR status does to the building plan: a residence larger than the ALC's permitted size, or more residences than permitted, requires ALC approval, and the Commission states approval for an additional residence beyond what is permitted can only be given where the residence is necessary for farm use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Check fill before planning construction on ALR land: the ALC lists a Notice of Intent to place fill among the approvals that may be required, alongside local government zoning and building permits and health authority approval for septic systems. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Check servicing on RS-3 land specifically: the zoning bylaw states that zone may not be connected to the community water system or the community sanitary sewer system, so well and septic condition are parcel-level checks, not assumptions. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- Check creek-adjacent parcels against the watercourse itself: Metro Vancouver states Kanaka Creek provides habitat for a variety of wildlife including salmon, and that steep, slippery cliffs occur in many places along the creek, especially at Cliff Falls. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- Two hazard checks are stated in full in the companion note and are named here once only: the Fraser River floodplain construction level with Zoning s.402.8(h), and the Fraser River Escarpment geotechnical rule — see the maple-ridge-homes-under-1-8m note. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
