# Thornhill Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: thornhill-mr (Thornhill, Maple Ridge; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Thornhill is a heavily forested, mainly residential area in east Maple Ridge with several farms and nurseries. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Thornhill sits east of the Albion planning area, in School District 42's (SD42) East Capital Zone, which Kanaka Creek bisects with limited bridge crossings. https://facilitiesreview.sd42.ca/east-capital-zone/
- Its working corridor is Dewdney Trunk Road and 256 Street, with 100 Avenue and 98 Avenue through the housing on the Thornhill bus loop. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt748.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is rural-residential: detached houses on large parcels, farms and nurseries, not urban subdivisions. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Much of Thornhill carries an Urban Reserve designation, holding it in low-density form until the section 3 tests are met. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/OCP-2025-Chapter-3.pdf
- Farm-edge lots fall under the RS-3 (Agricultural) and A1–A3 zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019; the City's RS-3 checklist requires a lot over 0.4 hectares for an agricultural use, tied to Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) land or an agricultural Official Community Plan (OCP) designation. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/rs-3-agr-zoning-checklist202103081630532233
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Urban development is not supported yet: OCP policy 3-24 keeps Thornhill a long-term Urban Reserve until city population exceeds 100,000 and capacity inside the existing urban area approaches build-out. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/OCP-2025-Chapter-3.pdf
- Policy 3-25 first requires a Metro Vancouver-approved Regional Context Statement amendment, a Fraser Sewerage Area extension, and an adopted Area Plan including an aquifer groundwater study and an agricultural impact assessment. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/OCP-2025-Chapter-3.pdf
- Until then, policy 3-26 sets a minimum parcel size for subdivision (figure in NOT CONFIRMED); city-wide suite and zoning rules sit in the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/03-ocp-chapter-3202107081353392009

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is there a Thornhill Elementary school? **A:** No — no SD42 school carries that name. Thornhill is in the East Capital Zone, whose six elementary schools include Blue Mountain and Webster's Corners, with Samuel Robertson Technical and Garibaldi as the secondary schools. Catchments are address-based, so run the exact address in the district locator. https://facilitiesreview.sd42.ca/east-capital-zone/ https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
**Q:** Can I subdivide an acreage into urban lots? **A:** Not under current policy. The OCP blocks urban-level densities in the reserve until an Area Plan is adopted, and first requires regional, sewerage, groundwater and farm-impact approvals. A rural split meeting today's minimum parcel size is a separate, parcel-specific question for the City. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/OCP-2025-Chapter-3.pdf
**Q:** Is Thornhill on city sewer? **A:** No urban sewer serves a developed Thornhill today. The OCP makes a Fraser Sewerage Area extension, approved by the Greater Vancouver Sewerage and Drainage District Board, a precondition of urban development, so the reserve still runs on rural servicing. Confirm any single lot with the City. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/OCP-2025-Chapter-3.pdf
**Q:** Is my lot in the ALR? **A:** Some Thornhill-edge parcels are, and zoning alone does not answer it. The Agricultural Land Commission publishes the ALR boundary map, and the City's agricultural checklists ask whether a property is in the ALR and whether structures need ALC approval. Check the parcel before writing an offer. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Thornhill's bus, the 748 Thornhill/Haney Place loop, is a limited community-shuttle service, not a frequent route. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt748.pdf
- The City counts a mountain biking and hiking network on 256 Street: 17 trails, moderately difficult, typically about an hour each. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Kanaka Creek Regional Park's Canyon Trail links the Bell-Irving fish hatchery to Cliff Falls, 1.2 kilometres one way; the hatchery supports chum and coho salmon enhancement. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- Rural lots can depend on wells; the Province's well records service is searchable for a property's well. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 748 loops from Haney Place via 240 Street, Jackson Road and 100 Avenue, then 256 Street, 98 Avenue, 264 Street and 268 Street, some trips via Port Haney Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt748.pdf
- There is no West Coast Express station in Thornhill; Port Haney, reached on some 748 trips, is the rail connection. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check slope and creek setbacks lot by lot: City agricultural checklists flag geotechnical reports, fish and wildlife review and a DP30 area check here. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/a1-a2-a3-agr-zoning-checklist202103081624103844
- Check ALR status on the ALC map, and the well record for any lot not on community water; neither map shows parcel-level flood or slide risk. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
