# Central Port Coquitlam Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: central-port-coquitlam (Central Port Coquitlam; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Central Port Coquitlam is downtown Port Coquitlam, around Shaughnessy Street, Wilson Avenue and McAllister Avenue, with Port Coquitlam Station at 2125 Kingsway Avenue. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- The City's downtown civic centre area is bounded by McAllister Avenue, Shaughnessy Street, Wilson Avenue and Donald Street. http://portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects/revitalizing-our-downtown
- The Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 3838, identifies Downtown as a mixed-use neighbourhood, which is the plan name for the core of this sub-area. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Exact board sub-area boundary roads are not published by the board, so street-level edges here are UNCONFIRMED — see NOT CONFIRMED.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Central is the city's apartment core: Zoning Bylaw No. 3630 concentrates apartment zones RA1 (floor area ratio 1.0, rising to 1.5 with underground or structured parking and 2.0 with a $50 per square foot community amenity contribution) and RA2 (up to 2.5) downtown and along main roads. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf
- The bylaw also names site-specific Comprehensive Development zones downtown, including CD2 for PoCo Place Mall at 2755 Lougheed Highway and CD10 for Shaughnessy Station at 2850 Shaughnessy Street. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf
- Older detached homes remain on the central side streets off Hawthorne Avenue and Mary Hill Road, inside the same sub-area as the RA-zoned blocks. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Downtown is a named Development Permit Area in the OCP: OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 4373 lists the Downtown development permit area at section 9.3, separate from the Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing permit area at section 9.12. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11285
- Apartments are permitted above ground-floor office and retail uses in the Downtown and other commercial areas under the OCP housing policy replaced by Bylaw No. 4373. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11285
- The City is undertaking an OCP update to align with Metro 2050 and provincial housing legislation, after 2024–2025 work on small-scale multi-unit housing designations and Transit Oriented Areas — the current OCP was adopted in 2005 and substantially updated in 2013. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/reports-plans-publications/official-community-plan

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Does Central Port Coquitlam have a SkyTrain station? **A:** No, there is no SkyTrain station inside Port Coquitlam. The Millennium Line Evergreen Extension station is Coquitlam Central, in Coquitlam; what Central has in the sub-area itself is Port Coquitlam Station on the West Coast Express, at 2125 Kingsway Avenue, between Coquitlam Central and Pitt Meadows stations. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Wilson Avenue address downtown? **A:** Central Community Elementary is the catchment elementary for 2150 Wilson Avenue. A School District 43 School Locator run for the Port Coquitlam Community Centre address returned Central Community Elementary, École Pitt River Middle and École Riverside Secondary; Central Elementary is also listed by the district as at or near capacity for cross-catchment applications. http://sd43.bc.ca/cross-catchment
- **Q:** Is downtown in the floodplain? **A:** Yes, the low-lying downtown lands are part of the city's floodplain picture. The City states a significant portion of Port Coquitlam, protected by dyke infrastructure, is within the Fraser or Pitt River flood plain; a condo or house buyer here should ask the City about the specific lot and any flood construction requirements. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/city-port-coquitlam-prepares-potential-flooding
- **Q:** What is at 2150 Wilson Avenue? **A:** The Port Coquitlam Community Centre, holding the Terry Fox Library, is at 2150 Wilson Avenue downtown. Gates Park is at 2300 Reeve Street, the other named central park in the City parks directory. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/recreation/parks-sports-fields-trails/parks-directory/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Port Coquitlam is Terry Fox's hometown, and the Terry Fox: Inspiring Local Champions exhibit is at the Port Coquitlam Community Centre downtown. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/terry-fox-inspiring-local-champions-exhibit-opens-port-coquitlam
- Leigh Square is the downtown open-concept festival space, and Veterans Park in the heart of downtown was redesigned as an open concept space in the civic centre redevelopment. http://portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects/revitalizing-our-downtown
- McAllister Avenue's streetscape was designed for walking, socializing and shopping, and an extension of the Donald Pathway is part of the same civic centre project. http://portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects/revitalizing-our-downtown
- The City's capital list marks Downtown Lighting complete in 2025, covering Shaughnessy Street string lights from Kingsway Avenue to Wilson Avenue and McAllister Avenue post lights. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects?construction_year=All&page=2
- Central Elementary at 2260 Central Avenue is on the district's at-or-near-capacity list, which limits cross-catchment moves into it. http://sd43.bc.ca/cross-catchment

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Port Coquitlam is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so a residential owner in Central must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- The City's Fees and Charges Bylaw, 2025, No. 4415 took effect January 1, 2026, and its Schedule D sets development application fees, including a $2,500 minimum fee for an Official Community Plan amendment with rezoning for rowhouse, townhouse and apartment zones. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/4415-fees-and-charges-bylawpdf
- The City states Port Coquitlam has the lowest average property taxes and utility fees in Metro Vancouver, with a 1.95 percent property tax increase for the average home in 2026. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/2026tax-newsletter-finalpdf
- The tax rate per $1,000 (Bylaw No. 4441) is in the mary-hill-port-coquitlam note and the utility levy amounts are in the oxford-heights-port-coquitlam and birchland-manor-port-coquitlam notes in this cluster.

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The West Coast Express from Port Coquitlam Station runs five weekday westbound trains at 6:04, 6:34, 7:04, 7:34 and 8:04 a.m., arriving at Waterfront Station 36 minutes later, with no weekend or statutory holiday service. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- Five eastbound trains leave Waterfront Station at 3:50, 4:20, 4:50, 5:30 and 6:20 p.m. and stop at Port Coquitlam Station on the way to Mission City. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- The R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus runs along Lougheed Highway across the north edge of the central area, connecting toward Coquitlam Central Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- Measured drive times from downtown Port Coquitlam are UNCONFIRMED — no operator page states them.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check flood status lot by lot on the City flood page: flood plain maps and preparedness resources are at portcoquitlam.ca/flood, and the City patrols and maintains dikes and pump stations during high water. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/city-port-coquitlam-prepares-potential-flooding
- Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) land in the city is in the south, including land in and around Colony Farm Regional Park at Central's west edge on the Coquitlam River — relevant to lots near the river, not to downtown strata lots. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- The flood and OCP pages do not state a flood construction level for an individual downtown lot; that figure comes from the City for the specific address.
