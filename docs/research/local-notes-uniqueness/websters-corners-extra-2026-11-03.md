# Websters Corners — Extra Local Notes
Page: websters-corners (Websters Corners; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Websters Corners is the rural acreage area at Maple Ridge's east edge, near the Mission border just off Dewdney Trunk Road. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The City spells the name Webster's Corners, with an apostrophe; the board sub-area drops the apostrophe. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The main corridors are Dewdney Trunk Road east–west, with 248 Street, 256 Street and 264 Street north–south and 128 Avenue on the north side. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The City publishes no boundary for this neighbourhood; the street scope above is a working description, not a boundary citation. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Shared area facts are in full in the websters-corners-guide note (earlier in program order); this note adds street-level specifics.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is rural detached homes and acreages, with no condo or townhome complex confirmed inside the sub-area on an official source. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- One secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, inside the single detached building. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- A suite may be at most 40% of gross floor area and may not be in the Floodplain Area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- On Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) land, the provincial ALR Use Regulation also applies: one suite in the principal residence under Section 31, and an additional residence only under Section 32 conditions. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- The area lies outside the Urban Area Boundary, under Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools are physically in this area? **A:** Three public schools sit inside this area on the Ministry file. Webster's Corners Elementary is at 25554 Dewdney Trunk Road. Blue Mountain Elementary is at 12453 248 Street on the Ministry file. Garibaldi Secondary is at 24789 Dewdney Trunk Road. Catchments are still address-based. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q:** What catchment did the sample address return? **A:** For 25554 Dewdney Trunk Road, the recorded locator result is Webster's Corners Elementary and Garibaldi Secondary. School District 42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows) catchments are address-based through its School Locator. French Immersion schools shown for that address are programs of choice, not catchment schools. Check any other address directly. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q:** Can an acreage add a suite or second home? **A:** One suite may fit inside the main house; a separate second home is much narrower. The City permits one suite per lot in the agricultural zones, capped at 40% of floor area and outside the floodplain. On ALR land the provincial regulation governs as well. An additional separate residence needs its Section 32 conditions met. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q:** What is the 256 Street plan I keep hearing about? **A:** The North 256 Street Industrial Lands Area Plan covers both sides of 256 Street north of 128 Avenue. Council approved the Official Community Plan amendments for about 500 acres. The plan sets future designations only. It does not itself approve any construction on a parcel. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-approves-new-500-acre-256-street-industrial-area-plan-support-jobs-and-economic
- **Q:** Is there a train or rapid transit here? **A:** There is no West Coast Express station or rapid transit inside this sub-area. The nearest West Coast Express stations are Port Haney and Maple Ridge to the west and Mission City to the east. Bus route 701 runs east on Dewdney Trunk Road past this area. Most east trips end at 248 Street. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The British Columbia Institute of Technology (BCIT) Woodlot here carries a network of mountain-bike trails, and the City calls the area an active film-production hub. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Kanaka Creek Regional Park sits at the west side, with the Canyon Trail to Cliff Falls, Riverfront Trails and the Bell-Irving Hatchery. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- No City recreation centre or library branch sits inside this sub-area; the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre is at 11925 Haney Place in the Town Centre. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre
- School District 42 has no middle tier: elementary is Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary is Grades 8 to 12. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/
- Ridge Meadows Hospital, at 11666 Laity Street, is the community hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows with 24/7 emergency care. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check ALR status per parcel in the Agricultural Land Commission map finder before valuing subdivision or a second home. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- Pull well records in the provincial GWELLS database on every acreage purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- Onsite sewerage must meet the Sewerage System Regulation, including a 30 metre setback from a well unless professional advice is obtained. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004
- Check the City floodplain framework for the lot, covering Fraser River, Alouette system, steep creeks and groundwater flooding; lot-level effect is per parcel. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-climate-action-flood-resilience-environmental-protection-and-mckenney-creek
