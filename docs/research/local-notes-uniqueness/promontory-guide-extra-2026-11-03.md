# Promontory Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: promontory-guide (Promontory; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Promontory has no official boundary: the City of Chilliwack does not publish one for the Promontory sub-area, and the closest official placement is the City's South Vedder Neighbourhood Plan page, which borders that plan area by the Promontory neighbourhood to the southeast. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2975
- The neighbourhood is the hillside above the Sardis and Vedder flats, east of Vedder Road, and Promontory Road is its main road: Vedder Elementary School sits at 45850 Promontory Road at the base of the hill, and BC Transit Route 54 climbs Promontory Road and runs along Teskey Way and Sylvan Drive. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/033 https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=54
- The City itself uses the neighbourhood name for its parks: its Jinkerson Park page describes the park as located in the Promontory neighborhood of Chilliwack. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=67
- Its neighbours are Sardis and the Vedder flats below to the north and west, the South Vedder neighbourhood to the west across Vedder Road, Tzeachten First Nation lands further east on the flats, and Mount Thom Park on its eastern side. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2975 https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=92

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Promontory is a planned hillside residential area, not an old townsite: under the previous 2040 Official Community Plan, growth was directed to designated hillsides governed by Comprehensive Development Area Plans, with Promontory named as one of them, and the current Chilliwack 2050 OCP carries the hillside rules forward. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/common/downloads/publication.pdf https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- The one school-aged anchor dates the build-out: Promontory Heights Elementary Community School at 46200 Stoneview Drive was built in 2000, and the district reports the Promontory catchment held about 850 students aged 5 to 10 as of 2025. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2022-03/SD33%20LRFP%202021-2025.pdf https://www.sd33.bc.ca/news/board-education-approves-purchase-promontory-property
- No official source read publishes a Promontory-wide statement of home types or a strata-versus-freehold split, so the mix on a specific street is a property-by-property check, not a neighbourhood figure. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Slope is the governing rule on this hillside: Promontory is a designated hillside and upland area under Development Permit Area 2 (DPA2) on OCP Schedule E, Map 14, and a DPA2 permit is required before subdivision, construction, land clearing or earthworks. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- A DPA2 application requires a geotechnical report from a qualified professional, and the City's DPA2 guide states the report must cover hazard and slope assessment, setbacks and cut and fill considerations, site stability, and drainage and soil conditions; an environmental assessment and a hydrology report may also be required. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- A second hillside layer can apply to the same lot: Development Permit Area 5 applies to all lands in the city with slopes of 20% or more, and a development permit is required for subdivision of land with 20% or greater slopes or new construction or earthworks on those slopes. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881 https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- Proposals on the steepest ground get a third-party review: the OCP Schedule D-5 guidelines require a third-party review of the application where the development proposal involves slopes of 30% or greater. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- The stated objectives of the hillside permit areas are to reduce slope hazards, erosion and flooding, and to ensure development responds to natural terrain and drainage patterns. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- City-wide suite and small-scale multi-unit rules apply on the flatter lots here as they do across the city and are written in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note; the hillside permit layers above are the Promontory-specific addition. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves Promontory, and is it full? **A:** Promontory Heights Elementary Community School at 46200 Stoneview Drive is the catchment school, and it is over capacity on the district's own figures: capacity 580 with enrolment of 603, and about 850 students aged 5 to 10 in the Promontory catchment as of 2025. SD33 also states there is no opportunity to expand the existing facility because of site limitations and structural constraints, which is why the district bought a second school site on the hill. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/news/board-education-approves-purchase-promontory-property
- **Q:** Where do Promontory students go after elementary school? **A:** The district's bus-route groupings in SD33 Administrative Procedure 611 place Promontory with Stitó:s Lá:lém Totí:lt Elementary/Middle School (Kindergarten to Grade 8) and G.W. Graham Secondary (Grades 9 to 12), and the district's June 2025 Promontory question-and-answer document confirms no boundary change for 2025/26 or 2026/27. Catchments are address-based, so confirm the exact address with the district's school locator before relying on a listing's school claim. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2025-06/Promontory%20Q%2BA%20-%20Preliminary%20Boundary%20Discussions.pdf
- **Q:** Does building or adding on here need a geotechnical report? **A:** Yes, where the lot is in Development Permit Area 2, which covers Promontory as a designated hillside and upland area: a geotechnical report from a qualified professional is required with each application, covering slope and hazard assessment, setbacks, cut and fill, site stability and drainage. Land with slopes of 20% or more also falls under Development Permit Area 5, and slopes of 30% or more trigger a third-party review. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881 https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- **Q:** Is there a bus off the hill? **A:** Yes: BC Transit Route 54 Promontory runs between Cottonwood Mall and Promontory via Vedder Road, Promontory Road, Teskey Way and Sylvan Drive, with timing points at Vedder and Promontory, Teskey and Promontory, and Sylvan and Vista. BC Transit added Sunday-evening service to Route 54 in the January 4, 2026 service change, but the hill still has the one route, so a car-free household should check the current Rider's Guide times. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=54 https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/
- **Q:** Is a second Promontory elementary school coming? **A:** A site is secured but the school is not yet funded: SD33's Board unanimously approved the purchase of a 6.8-acre property at 5303 Promontory Road for a future 500-student elementary school, after environmental and geotechnical testing confirmed the site is suitable. Ministry approvals and funding are still required for construction, and the district states a project of this size typically needs one to two years for design and approval, then two to three years of construction. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/news/board-education-approves-purchase-promontory-property

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Jinkerson Park at 5869 Jinkerson Road carries the neighbourhood's recreation: roughly 600 metres of walking trails, outdoor fitness equipment, multiple playgrounds, a multi-use games area, a pump track, off-leash dog areas with separate big-dog and small-dog areas, and a drinking fountain. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=67
- There is a separate Jinkerson Dog Park address, 5855 Jinkerson Road, in the City's parks listing, distinct from the main park address. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=67
- Mount Thom Park is partly in Promontory: the City describes a 70-hectare wooded park including the summit and slopes of the 484-metre Mount Thom, with the Thom Creek Trailhead at Sylvan Drive and Thom Creek Drive and the Lookout Loop Trail joining from the Promontory side. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=92
- There is no City recreation centre and no library branch on the hill: the nearest Fraser Valley Regional Library branch is Sardis Library at 5819 Tyson Road. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/sardis-library
- The school at the base of the hill is not the catchment school for the hilltop: Vedder Elementary at 45850 Promontory Road serves the Vedder flats, while Promontory addresses feed Promontory Heights Elementary — confirm by address, not by proximity. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/033
- Promontory Heights Elementary cannot simply be expanded to meet growth: SD33 states expansion is not possible due to site limitations and structural constraints, including building upwards. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2025-06/Promontory%20Q%2BA%20-%20Preliminary%20Boundary%20Discussions.pdf
- The City cannot stop new building on the hill until a second school exists: SD33's Promontory question-and-answer document states the City has no role in land acquisition for school sites and cannot prevent new builds for that reason. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2025-06/Promontory%20Q%2BA%20-%20Preliminary%20Boundary%20Discussions.pdf

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Future Promontory elementary school site, 5303 Promontory Road — Chilliwack School District 33 project: the Board of Education unanimously approved the purchase of this 6.8-acre property at a special regular board meeting on November 20 (district news page, read 2026-11-03) for a future 500-student elementary school; environmental and geotechnical consultants confirmed the site is suitable, the funding request had been in the district's 5-year capital plan for over 10 years, and the Board borrowed to buy the site outside the Ministry's regular funding cycle. STATUS: site purchased; Ministry approvals and funding for construction still required, read 2026-11-03. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/news/board-education-approves-purchase-promontory-property

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Promontory is in the City of Chilliwack, whose 2026 residential property-tax rate is 4.66727 per $1,000 of assessed value in total — see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note for the full breakdown, utility charges and billing cycle. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2527/Tax%5FRates%5Ffor%5Fthe%5FWeb%2Dmou9gntw%5Fc%2Epdf
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies here: the Province's taxable-areas list names the City of Chilliwack. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- The hillside-specific cost a buyer meets here is professional, not municipal: a DPA2 application must include a geotechnical report from a qualified professional, and the City does not publish the consultant's fee — budget for the report itself, not a City charge. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 54 Promontory is the hill's only bus route: BC Transit runs it between Cottonwood Mall and Promontory via Vedder Road, Promontory Road, Teskey Way and Sylvan Drive. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=54
- Route 54 received additional Sunday-evening service in BC Transit's January 4, 2026 Chilliwack service change. https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/
- Connections off the hill are at Vedder Road: Route 51 Vedder runs from Downtown Chilliwack down Vedder Road to the University of the Fraser Valley Chilliwack campus, and Route 66 Fraser Valley Express connects Chilliwack to Abbotsford, Langley and Lougheed Town Centre Station. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=51 https://www.bctransit.com/plan-a-trip/interregional-routes/
- Walking and trail links are recreational rather than commuter routes here: the Thom Creek Trail and Lookout Loop Trail connect Promontory into Mount Thom Park. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=92

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check both hillside permit layers for the lot, not just the zone: a parcel can be governed by two or more Development Permit Area designations, and the City states that a separate development permit is required for each designation that applies. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- Slope hazard is the documented risk here, not river flood: the DPA2 objectives are reducing slope hazards, erosion and flooding and making development respond to natural terrain and drainage patterns, and the supporting reports cover site stability and soil conditions. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- Retaining walls and grading are part of the permit picture on steep lots: the DPA5 guidelines address cut and fill, post-development contours, retaining walls and slope stabilization in the detailed grading plan an application must show. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP5%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- What the City pages read do not tell a buyer: whether a specific existing Promontory home was built with the geotechnical work its slope would require today — that is a building-file and permit-history check for the individual property.
