# North Coquitlam — Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer, for a voice memo. Muse does not answer these.

1. When you sell a condo or townhome around Pinetree Way and Glen Drive, who is actually buying — SFU families, investors, downsizers, or first-time buyers priced out of Burnaby?
2. What sells fast in North Coquitlam and what sits — older low-rises near David Avenue, or the newer towers by Coquitlam Centre?
3. What surprises buyers most here: the walk to the Evergreen Extension stations, strata fees in the big towers, or how the area changes between the mall core and the quieter streets north of David?
4. Do buyers compare North Coquitlam against Port Moody or Burke Mountain more often, and what usually wins the argument?
5. Tell one story from a North Coquitlam deal — a bidding situation, a strata surprise in the documents, or a client who changed their mind about the area.
