# Chilliwack River Valley — Questions for Jared

## Questions

1. Who buys in the Chilliwack River Valley in your deals — outdoor and fishing buyers, acreage buyers priced out of Chilliwack, or people who work in the valley's recreation economy?
2. What sells fast and what sits — Baker Trails and the lower-valley homes near Vedder Crossing, or the upper-valley properties past the cell-service cutoff toward Chilliwack Lake?
3. What surprises buyers — the slope and debris hazards, that most parcels are well and septic with only Bell Acres water and Baker Trails sewer as FVRD services, or having no bus and no cell reception?
4. How do the FVRD's slope stability assessment and the Wilson Road dike recovery work come up in your deals — do buyers, lenders or insurers ask about them?
5. One story: a valley deal that taught you something — a hazard report, a service surprise, or a property you now approach differently — and what you check first now.

## Shot list

1. Chilliwack Lake Road at the Vedder Crossing Bridge, looking east up the valley — the valley entrance, the river and the road corridor in one frame.
2. Thompson Regional Park at 48600 Chilliwack Lake Road, looking across the park to the river and valley walls — the community's one FVRD park.
3. Chilliwack Lake from the provincial park boat launch, looking south down the lake — the head of the valley and the recreation draw that fills the road in summer.
