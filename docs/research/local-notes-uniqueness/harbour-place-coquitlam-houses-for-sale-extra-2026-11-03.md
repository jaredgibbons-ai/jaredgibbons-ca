# Harbour Place Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: harbour-place-coquitlam-houses-for-sale (Harbour Place; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Harbour Place is not a harbour or riverfront pocket in the City sources: it is a slope house pocket north of Austin Avenue and Como Lake Avenue, around Baker Drive and King Albert Avenue, below Como Lake, and the City publishes no boundary for the multiple listing service sub-area name. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- The two addressed anchors inside the pocket are Baker Drive Elementary at 885 Baker Drive and Blue Mountain Park at 975 King Albert Avenue, at the foot of Blue Mountain. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Blue-Mountain-Park-18
- Como Lake Park, 700 Gatensbury Street, sits just north of the pocket, and TransLink's route 153 stop at Como Lake Avenue and Baker Drive marks the pocket's north edge. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/153/direction/0/schedule

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses are the stock here, on the Baker Drive and King Albert Avenue slope streets; no City, School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) or BC Assessment public page read for this note states a typical build decade, lot size, subdivision name or house style for Harbour Place, so none is claimed. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- The one housing-form fact the City does state nearby is plan-level, not stock-level: on May 27, 2026 Council kept areas east of Blue Mountain Street out of the Southwest Corridors land-use changes, and areas no longer proposed for townhouses in that review remain Small-Scale Residential. http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1968

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Harbour Place on the water? **A:** No harbour or river frontage is stated for this pocket in any City source read. It is a slope neighbourhood north of Austin Avenue and Como Lake Avenue, around Baker Drive and King Albert Avenue, below Como Lake. The named water is Como Lake itself, in Como Lake Park at 700 Gatensbury Street just north of the houses, with a 1.2-kilometre Lakeside Loop trail, a fishing dock, wetlands and a lake the City states is stocked regularly in spring. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Como-Lake-Park-26
- **Q:** Which elementary school is in the pocket? **A:** Baker Drive Elementary, kindergarten to Grade 5, at 885 Baker Drive, is the school physically inside Harbour Place. Middle and secondary options named in district listings around it are École Montgomery Middle at 1900 Edgewood Avenue, Hillcrest Middle at 2161 Regan Avenue and Centennial Secondary at 570 Poirier Street, but no official page read assigns a specific Harbour Place address to any of them. Catchment is by permanent home address, so confirm the exact house address in the School District No. 43 School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** Will the Austin Avenue plan changes rezone my house lot? **A:** Not automatically. Council adopted the Southwest Shoulders and Corridors amendments on May 27, 2026 after a May 27 public hearing, updating land use around the Burquitlam and Lougheed Town Centre stations and along Austin Avenue, and the City states all proposed land-use changes east of Blue Mountain Street were removed during engagement. A Baker Drive or King Albert Avenue lot's designation still has to be read lot by lot in the adopted plan maps, not assumed from the corridor headline. http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1968
- **Q:** Is there a bus on the interior streets? **A:** No interior-street bus routing is published for Baker Drive or King Albert Avenue. TransLink routes 153, 156 and 157 all run Como Lake Avenue on the pocket's north side; route 153, Coquitlam Central Station to Braid Station, also runs Poirier Street and Austin Avenue and stops at Como Lake Avenue and Baker Drive. House buyers here should plan on walking to Como Lake Avenue, Austin Avenue or Poirier Street for a stop, based on the published routings. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/153/direction/0/schedule
- **Q:** Where is the nearest full recreation complex? **A:** Poirier Sport and Leisure Complex, 633 Poirier Street, southwest of the pocket, is the nearest major City recreation complex in the City's facility listings. The in-pocket amenity is smaller: Blue Mountain Park at 975 King Albert Avenue, which the City lists with a spray park at the same address, and Como Lake Park just north for trails, fishing and the viewing dock. https://coquitlam.ca/384/Poirier-Sport-and-Leisure-Complex

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Blue Mountain Park, 975 King Albert Avenue, is the park the pocket sits around, at the foot of Blue Mountain, and its spray park shares the 975 King Albert Avenue address. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Blue-Mountain-Park-18
- The nearest library branch is Coquitlam Public Library's Poirier Branch at 575 Poirier Street, not the City Centre Branch at 1169 Pinetree Way. http://www.coqlibrary.ca/about/hours-locations
- École Montgomery Middle, the middle school listed at 1900 Edgewood Avenue near this pocket, is itself a seismic replacement project: School District No. 43 states the building permit was approved and the project issued for tender in September 2026. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/ecole-montgomery-middle
- The Austin Heights sewer work south of the pocket is in its final phase in summer and fall 2026, installing a new sewer main on Casey Street between Brunette Avenue and Cartier Avenue over about two months, weather dependent. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1266/Rochester-to-Austin-Sewer-Upgrade

- Southwest Shoulders and Corridors amendments, Southwest Coquitlam including the Austin Avenue corridor on this pocket's south side, City of Coquitlam plan amendment, mixed medium-density housing, townhousing and local commercial: the City states the adopted concepts are expected to add approximately 2,300 additional homes across the review area and add a commercial node near Austin Avenue and Blue Mountain Street — STATUS: adopted May 27, 2026 after a public hearing that day, read 2026-11-03. http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1968

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 153, Coquitlam Central Station to Braid Station, is the pocket's route: it runs Poirier Street, Austin Avenue and Como Lake Avenue, with a stop at Como Lake Avenue and Baker Drive on the north side. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/153/direction/0/schedule
- Routes 156 and 157 also run Como Lake Avenue on the north side, per their TransLink timetable routings; neither is routed onto Baker Drive or King Albert Avenue. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/192/tt156.pdf
- No SkyTrain station is located in Harbour Place; the Millennium Line Evergreen Extension, opened December 2, 2016, serves stations in neighbouring areas, reached from here by the Como Lake Avenue and Austin Avenue buses. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2017/12/evergreen-extension-anniversary-a-year-ago-today-it-opened/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check flood information at city scale, not pocket scale: the City states Coquitlam fronts four major rivers — Brunette, Coquitlam, Pitt and Fraser — that Coquitlam River flood levels use the 200-year design flood, and that dikes and pump stations protect low-lying properties; no City flood page specific to the Baker Drive slope was found. https://www.coquitlam.ca/224/Floodplain-Dikes
- Check the Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 section 519 floodplain setbacks anyway: no building within 30 metres of the natural boundary of the Brunette, Coquitlam, Fraser or Pitt rivers, or within 15 metres of another qualifying watercourse, and no building below the flood construction level in designated floodplain. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1170/Part-5-General-Regulations-PDF
- Check slope and tree cover lot by lot on King Albert Avenue and Baker Drive: this is a slope pocket at the foot of Blue Mountain, and no City geotechnical page specific to Harbour Place was found in the sources read, so a slope conclusion needs a lot-level City map check, not this note. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Blue-Mountain-Park-18
