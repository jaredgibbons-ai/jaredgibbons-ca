# Glenwood Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: glenwood-port-coquitlam-houses-for-sale (Glenwood; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Glenwood is the north Port Coquitlam house pocket around Glen Elementary, at 3064 Glen Drive, and it is not named as an established neighbourhood in the City's Official Community Plan (OCP) — it sits inside the north-side residential area. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/birchland/
- Lougheed Highway forms the pocket's south boundary, and Coast Meridian Road and Lincoln Avenue are its north–south routes. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Citadel lies to the east and Riverwood beyond it, with Central Port Coquitlam to the south across Lougheed Highway. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is detached houses on the north-side streets around Glen Drive, under the City's RS zones in Zoning Bylaw No. 3630: minimum lot width is 15 metres in RS1, 12 metres in RS2, 30 metres in RS3 and 9.5 metres in RS4. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Evergreen Park, at 3500 Cedar Drive, is the addressed park inside the pocket's street system. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Port Coquitlam's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules allow 3 to 6 units on RS lots without rezoning, depending on lot size, location and transit access. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve Glenwood? **A:** The School District No. 43 (SD43) locator result recorded for 3064 Glen Drive is Glen Elementary, École Maple Creek Middle and Pinetree Secondary, and Glen Elementary itself stands at that Glen Drive address; catchments for other addresses must be checked in the locator. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** How many units can a Glenwood house lot take? **A:** Under the City's small-scale multi-unit housing rules, 3 to 6 units are allowed on RS lots without rezoning, with the count depending on lot size, location and transit access, so the lot's own figures decide the number. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q:** Is Glenwood a City neighbourhood? **A:** Not by name in the Official Community Plan: the pocket is a board sub-area inside the City's north-side residential area, so City documents discuss the north side rather than a Glenwood neighbourhood plan. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- **Q:** When are Port Coquitlam property taxes due? **A:** Property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after July 2 and a further 5 percent after September 15, 2026, and utility charges are flat annual water and sewer fees billed separately, due March 31, 2026. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The pocket's school chain runs to Pinetree Secondary, not to Terry Fox Secondary in Riverwood or Riverside Secondary in Citadel, per the recorded locator result for the Glen Drive address. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- Evergreen Park at 3500 Cedar Drive is the pocket's addressed park, on the Cedar Drive street system west of Glen Drive. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Lougheed Highway is a hard south edge: everything south of it is a different part of the city, so a Glenwood address is by definition a north-side address. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Port Coquitlam property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after July 2 and a further 5 percent after September 15, 2026, and the City's 2026 increase was 1.95 percent for the average home. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes
- The City states it has the lowest average property taxes and utility fees in Metro Vancouver, and utilities are flat annual water and sewer fees billed separately from taxes, due March 31, 2026. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes
