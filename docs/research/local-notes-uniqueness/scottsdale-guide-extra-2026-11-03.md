# Scottsdale Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: scottsdale-guide (Scottsdale, North Delta; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Scottsdale is the North Delta area around Scott Road (120 Street) and 72 Avenue, named for the Scottsdale Exchange and Scottsdale Centre area; street-level detail is in the scottsdale-north-delta-houses-for-sale note — see the scottsdale-north-delta-houses-for-sale note. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus
- The exchange itself sits just across the Surrey border, and TransLink notes the R6 stops on-street at Scott Road and 72 Avenue for Scottsdale Centre and Strawberry Hill rather than looping into the exchange. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The guide-level pattern is post-war and later detached streets west of Scott Road, with seniors apartment buildings on 119 Street and newer corridor apartments proposed on Scott Road itself. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- Two named seniors buildings anchor the rental stock: Scott Road Lodge at 7666 119 Street, 42 one-bedroom subsidized units for ages 55 and over, and Strawberry Hill Manor at 7667 119A Street, 48 studio and one-bedroom units, also 55 and over. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Transit-oriented density is the defining rule: the 2024 Official Community Plan (OCP) designates a Scottsdale Exchange Transit Oriented Development (TOD) Area, and provincial Bill 47 requires minimum densities within 400 metres of the exchange. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus
- Scottsdale is also in phase two of the Sunshine Hills tree-protection pilot engagement, so corridor density and tree rules are both in play on the same streets. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-sunshine-hills-neighbourhood-tree-protection
- Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) applies on the detached streets away from the corridor, as it does citywide. https://letstalk.delta.ca/ssmuh

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Why is Scott Road changing so fast? **A:** Because it is the region's busiest south-of-river corridor, by TransLink's own account. TransLink calls Scott Road the busiest corridor south of the Fraser and notes the old 319 was the fourth busiest bus route in Metro Vancouver in 2023, which is why the R6 RapidBus and the transit-oriented density rules both landed here. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Scottsdale address? **A:** Verify the exact address in the district locator. Earlier verified program research for a Scottsdale sample address returned Heath Elementary and Seaquam Secondary, and Pinewood Elementary also serves North Delta streets in this grid; catchment is address-based and the district directs families to confirm it, so do not rely on a listing's school line. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/news-events/news/registration-information-for-students-who-are-new-to-delta-2/
- **Q:** Does the RapidBus stop at Scottsdale Exchange? **A:** No, and that surprises people. TransLink states the R6 does not enter Scottsdale Exchange, to keep travel times faster; riders connect to local routes at shared stops on Scott Road and 72 Avenue, beside Scottsdale Centre and Strawberry Hill. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus
- **Q:** Is there seniors housing in Scottsdale itself? **A:** Yes, on 119 Street. The City's April 2026 affordable housing guide lists Scott Road Lodge at 7666 119 Street and Strawberry Hill Manor at 7667 119A Street, both for residents aged 55 and over and both applied for through the British Columbia Housing (BC Housing) registry. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The R6 was a $33 million joint investment by TransLink, Surrey and Delta, and TransLink calls it its largest service expansion since 2020. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus
- Chalmers Park at 11400 76A Avenue is the area's sports park, with pickleball, tennis and sport courts, fields and trails on the City's listing. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/chalmers-park
- Deltassist's North Delta office is at 9097 120 Street, on the corridor itself — a practical landmark for the services concentrated on Scott Road. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2021-06/SocialServicesInventory%20CS.pdf
- The Delta Walmart on the corridor is store #1207 at 7155 120 Street, at the Scottsdale area's south end — carried from the program's settled record, not a store page read in this pass. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus
- Route 319 still runs the local Scott Road service under the R6, so Scottsdale stops have both the limited-stop RapidBus and the all-stops local bus on the same corridor. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta's 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after that date and another 5 percent after September 2, 2026; Council's 2026 plan set a 2.9 percent City tax increase. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- For 2026, Delta's flat-rate utility bill for a single-family home is $1,658, up $51 from 2025, covering garbage and recycling plus water and sewer where not metered separately. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Delta is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT), so residential owners must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The R6 runs every 7.5 minutes at peak and 15 minutes off-peak between Scott Road Station and Newton Exchange, 5 a.m. to 1 a.m. weekdays and 6 a.m. to 1 a.m. weekends. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus
- Local routes from Scottsdale Exchange include the 310 to Ladner, the 312 and 391 to Scott Road Station, the 316 and 329 to Surrey Central, the 340 to 22nd Street Station via Highway 91, and the 364 to Langley Centre. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus
Read: 2026-11-03

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Corridor change is the practical risk and opportunity: a lot within 400 metres of Scottsdale Exchange sits inside the transit-oriented area, so check the property's designation before assuming today's house form is the long-term plan for the street. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus
- Scott Road traffic and busway construction are part of daily life on the corridor; a house fronting 120 Street and one two streets west are different purchases, so view at peak hour. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus
