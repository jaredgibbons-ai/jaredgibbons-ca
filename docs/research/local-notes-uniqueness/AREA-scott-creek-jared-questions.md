# AREA — Scott Creek, Coquitlam — Questions for Jared

1. In Scott Creek deals you have done around Lansdowne Drive and Falcon Drive, what is the one lot feature — creek frontage, slope, or lot size — that most changes how fast a house sells and what it sells against?
2. Who is actually buying Scott Creek houses: families moving for Scott Creek Middle and Gleneagle Secondary, upsizers from Coquitlam condos and townhomes, or buyers coming over from Port Moody and Burnaby — and what do they say tipped it?
3. What sells fast here and what sits: do renovated houses near Eagle Ridge Park move first, while original-condition houses on the busier Lansdowne Drive stretch wait, or is it the other way around in your deals?
4. What surprises Scott Creek buyers after they move in — the Coquitlam Crunch foot traffic and parking at Eagle Ridge Park, creek-lot building limits, school catchment lines, or something else you hear about after completion?
5. Tell one Scott Creek story: a deal on a Lansdowne, Falcon or side-street lot where local knowledge changed the outcome — a creek setback, a suite or carriage-house check, or a catchment call.
