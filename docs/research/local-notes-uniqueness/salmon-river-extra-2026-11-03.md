# Salmon River Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: salmon-river (Salmon River; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Salmon River is a rural neighbourhood in the Township of Langley, named for the Salmon River that crosses its northern side; in the Township's Official Community Plan (OCP) the planned community here is called the Salmon River Uplands, designated in the Township's Rural Plan. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=7382BC38-B075-496D-975D-7969EB5087F3&folderId=07725C200520083849425410
- The working street grid is 240 Street running north–south, with 48 Avenue, 52 Avenue, 56 Avenue and Fraser Highway crossing east–west; the Fraser Highway Employment Lands plan area beside it runs along Fraser Highway roughly from 228 Street to 240 Street, bounded by 48 Avenue to the north and Old Yale Road and 40 Avenue to the south. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Reference-Plans/Fraser-Highway-Employment-Lands-Reference-Plan.pdf
- Much of the land around the settled lots is in the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR): a Township council report describes the Salmon River Uplands properties at 5990 – 240 Street and the 6000 block of 240 Street as ALR land designated Salmon River Uplands in the Rural Plan, adopted in 1993. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=CE3A90E0-2C64-4B0E-A06B-810C099710DA&folderId=45573C170725113654260000

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is single-family houses on large rural lots: the Township's council reports describe the existing pattern in the uplands as Suburban Residential Zone SR-1 lots of roughly 0.38 to 0.81 hectares (about 0.93 to 2.0 acres) along 240 Street, 248 Street and the avenues. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=CE3A90E0-2C64-4B0E-A06B-810C099710DA&folderId=45573C170725113654260000
- New supply comes as acreage subdivisions, not townhouses: current Township applications in the uplands propose 56-lot and 58-lot single-family subdivisions under the Suburban Residential SR-3 zone (see Section 6). https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=7382BC38-B075-496D-975D-7969EB5087F3&folderId=07725C200520083849425410
- The Salmon River Uplands Engineering Servicing Plan (2024) plans municipal water and sanitary sewer extensions for the area — many existing homes predate those services, so servicing varies lot by lot. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Reference-Plans/Fraser-Highway-Employment-Lands-Reference-Plan.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Rural Plan policy for the Salmon River Uplands is one sentence long in effect: the Township's report quotes it as the area "shall be maintained for rural residential and agricultural uses" — there is no urban neighbourhood plan promising higher density here. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=7382BC38-B075-496D-975D-7969EB5087F3&folderId=07725C200520083849425410
- Minimum lot sizes are set by zone: Rural Zone RU-1 requires 1.7 hectares (4.2 acres) for a new lot, and Suburban Residential SR-1 requires 3,716 square metres (0.91 acres), per the Township Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 figures quoted in council reports. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=CE3A90E0-2C64-4B0E-A06B-810C099710DA&folderId=45573C170725113654260000
- ALR land cannot be excluded or subdivided for housing without the Agricultural Land Commission (ALC): in 2018 Township Council refused to even refer an exclusion application for 5990 – 240 Street and the 6000 block of 240 Street to the ALC, because it conflicted with the OCP, the Rural Plan and the regional growth strategy. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=CE3A90E0-2C64-4B0E-A06B-810C099710DA&folderId=45573C170725113654260000
- The Salmon River itself is protected where it crosses a development site: the Township requires the river corridor to be dedicated and protected as a non-disturbance Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area (SPEA) under Schedule 3 of the Langley OCP. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=7382BC38-B075-496D-975D-7969EB5087F3&folderId=07725C200520083849425410
- Hatchery Creek (the Nicomekl River headwaters, also called Hatchery Creek in Township studies) is a Class A watercourse, and subdivision layouts near it must respect top-of-bank setbacks confirmed by a qualified professional. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=83651C260821100407897159&folderId=83651C260821095819365148

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Salmon River in the Agricultural Land Reserve? **A:** Partly — and the line runs through the neighbourhood, not around it. Township reports describe ALR parcels on 240 Street inside the Salmon River Uplands designation, while other uplands lots are zoned SR-1 and SR-3 suburban residential outside the ALR. Check the specific parcel on the ALC's mapping and the Township's zoning, because ALR status decides whether subdivision or a non-farm use is even possible. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=CE3A90E0-2C64-4B0E-A06B-810C099710DA&folderId=45573C170725113654260000
- **Q:** How big are the lots, and can they be split? **A:** Existing SR-1 lots run about 0.38 to 0.81 hectares, the RU-1 rural zone sets a 1.7-hectare minimum for new lots, and new subdivisions here are being approved at SR-3 density only through rezoning — a 58-lot proposal at the 23700 block of 56 Avenue went to Council in December 2025. Splitting an existing acreage is a rezoning or ALC question, not a right. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=7382BC38-B075-496D-975D-7969EB5087F3&folderId=07725C200520083849425410
- **Q:** Is there municipal water and sewer, or well and septic? **A:** It depends on the street. The Township's Salmon River Uplands Engineering Servicing Plan (2024) plans municipal water and sanitary sewer for the area, and recent subdivision approvals have required sewerage service as a condition — older homes away from the new mains may still be on well and septic, so verify the specific lot's services with the Township. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Reference-Plans/Fraser-Highway-Employment-Lands-Reference-Plan.pdf
- **Q:** Which schools serve the area? **A:** Langley School District (SD35) assigns by street address and tells families to use its online School Locator for the catchment school; there is no school inside the Salmon River Uplands itself, so children are bused or driven to schools in the surrounding Murrayville, Otter and Aldergrove areas — confirm the exact schools for the address before subjects. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- **Q:** Will the area urbanize like Willoughby? **A:** The Township's own plans say no in the near term: the Rural Plan keeps the uplands for rural residential and agricultural uses, Metro Vancouver designates the area rural in the regional growth strategy, and the new Fraser Highway Employment Lands plan is an employment area along Fraser Highway, not a housing plan for the uplands. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=7382BC38-B075-496D-975D-7969EB5087F3&folderId=07725C200520083849425410

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Community amenity contributions are negotiated subdivision by subdivision in the uplands: the Township's December 2025 report records $7,500,000 in contributions for the 56-lot project at 5759 – 240 Street under the previous policy (about $133,929 per lot), and $125,000 for the single added lot at 25027 – 52 Avenue — there is no single standard charge a buyer can assume across the area. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=7382BC38-B075-496D-975D-7969EB5087F3&folderId=07725C200520083849425410
- The 56-lot subdivision at 5759 – 240 Street was required to keep 22.3% of its developable area as open space and a 15-metre treed greenspace buffer after a July 2023 Council resolution cut its lot count — the look of the new streets here is set by that negotiation, lot by lot. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=7382BC38-B075-496D-975D-7969EB5087F3&folderId=07725C200520083849425410
- Water quality and capacity in the area's five aquifers are named in the Township's Fraser Highway Employment Lands plan as primary concerns — a well-served lot here needs its water source checked, not assumed. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Reference-Plans/Fraser-Highway-Employment-Lands-Reference-Plan.pdf
- Hatchery Creek supports salmonid species and Best Creek (also called Trigg Creek) supports coho salmon and trout, so creek-side lots carry streamside setbacks that can shrink the buildable area well inside the lot lines. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Reference-Plans/Fraser-Highway-Employment-Lands-Reference-Plan.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The Township of Langley's total 2025 residential tax rate was 3.34910 per $1,000 of assessed value, of which the total municipal rate under Bylaw 6128 was 1.87580 per $1,000 — the Township's own 2025 Property Tax Rates table. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/property-taxes/document-feed/2025-Tax-Rates.pdf
- Township utility charges (water, sewer, solid waste) are billed separately from the tax notice — the Township began mailing utility fees on a separate statement in 2025 — and the tax due date is July 2, with a 5% penalty on current-year taxes unpaid after that date. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies: the Township of Langley is a Metro Vancouver municipality on the Province's taxable-areas list. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Driving is the area's transport: Fraser Highway and 240 Street are the through routes named in the Township's plans for this area, and measured drive times from the uplands to Highway 1 or Langley Centre are UNCONFIRMED. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Reference-Plans/Fraser-Highway-Employment-Lands-Reference-Plan.pdf
- No TransLink fixed-route bus was found serving the Salmon River Uplands streets (240 Street, 48–56 Avenues) in the operator material read; the nearest transit exchanges are in Langley Centre and Aldergrove. Treat any transit claim for a specific lot as UNCONFIRMED until checked on translink.ca.
- School transport is by SD35 bus or car for most uplands addresses, since no school sits inside the neighbourhood. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Creek setbacks are the lot-level risk: the Salmon River must be dedicated as a non-disturbance Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area where it crosses a site, and Hatchery Creek is a Class A watercourse with top-of-bank setbacks — check the Township's streamside mapping (OCP Schedule 3) for the specific lot. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=7382BC38-B075-496D-975D-7969EB5087F3&folderId=07725C200520083849425410
- Check ALR status on the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping before assuming a second dwelling, subdivision or non-farm business: Council's 2018 refusal to refer the 240 Street exclusion application shows exclusion is not a formality here. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=CE3A90E0-2C64-4B0E-A06B-810C099710DA&folderId=45573C170725113654260000
- Servicing is the third check: confirm whether the lot is on municipal water and sewer under the Salmon River Uplands Engineering Servicing Plan (2024) or on a private well and septic, and whether connection charges apply. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Reference-Plans/Fraser-Highway-Employment-Lands-Reference-Plan.pdf
- What the Township pages do not tell a buyer: the yield or water quality of an individual well, or a septic system's condition — those need the well log, Fraser Health records and an inspection, not the plan documents.
