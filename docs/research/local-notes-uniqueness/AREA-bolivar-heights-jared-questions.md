# Bolivar Heights — Questions for Jared
Area: Bolivar Heights, Surrey (condos/townhomes first program page; houses and guide also in the program).

## 5 questions only Jared can answer
1. In your Bolivar Heights deals, what do buyers pay up for — the hill and the views, a Crestview Drive or Bentley Road lot, or the City Centre Plan townhome and low-rise designations — and what do they discount?
2. Who is buying in Bolivar Heights right now in your deals — City Centre renters moving to ground-oriented homes, downsizers from the detached streets, or small builders assembling lots under the 2022 plan — and how does that split between houses and the few attached sales?
3. What sells fast and what sits in Bolivar Heights: do renovated view houses move first while original-condition homes on the ravine streets wait, and are buyers pricing in the planned mixed-use village or ignoring it?
4. What surprises buyers most once they are under contract in Bolivar Heights — the Bon Accord and Invergarry ravine setbacks, how far the SkyTrain stations actually feel from the hill, or what the City Centre Plan designation does and does not allow on their lot?
5. Tell one Bolivar Heights deal story: a sale, a near-miss, or a problem you solved on the hill (Crestview Drive, Surrey Road, or the Bolivar Park side) that shows a buyer or seller something no listing could.
