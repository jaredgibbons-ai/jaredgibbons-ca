# City of Langley — Questions for Jared

1. In City of Langley deals you have handled, how often does a buyer — or their lender or notary — mix up City and Township bylaws, taxes or utilities, and what does the mix-up cost when it surfaces?
2. Who is buying in the City right now: first-time buyers into the apartment stock, downsizers from Township houses, or investors positioning around the 203 Street SkyTrain terminus — and what do they say tipped it?
3. What sells fast in the City and what sits: walkable downtown condos, Uplands detached houses, or older townhouses along 200 Street and 208 Street?
4. What surprises City of Langley buyers most: metered water billed once a year on the tax notice, the Nicomekl floodplain line on south-side lots, SkyTrain construction on Fraser Highway, or how small the city actually is once they drive it?
5. Tell one City of Langley story: a deal where the City-versus-Township line, a floodplain check, or a station-area density rule changed the subjects, the price, or whether the deal firmed at all.
