## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Connaught Heights is the City of New Westminster's west-end mainland neighbourhood, around 22nd Street Station, London Street and Marine Way — the City name and the board sub-area name match, and the City documents Connaught Heights in its Historic Neighbourhood Context Statements. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php
- The West End and Moody Park lie to the east, Burnaby is across the city's west boundary, and Queensborough is across the Queensborough Bridge to the south-east; the precise Official Community Plan boundary roads for Connaught Heights were not confirmed on a City page read for this note. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses are effectively the whole stock here: single-detached houses under the RS (Single Detached Residential) districts of Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001, with secondary suites and laneway or carriage house potential under the same bylaw — no condo building or townhome complex name in Connaught Heights was confirmed on a developer or strata source read for this note. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- The RS-1 lot standard is the measurable lot fact: a minimum site area of 557.40 square metres (6,000 square feet) and a floor space ratio cap of 0.5; the zone and dimensions of a specific lot are confirmed parcel by parcel with the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- No official source read states the age or style split of the detached stock, so none is claimed. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- New Westminster-wide zoning, 2026 tax and utility figures and Speculation and Vacancy Tax applicability are stated in full in the queensborough-new-westminster-houses-for-sale note; this note covers only what touches Connaught Heights lots directly. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- The 22nd Street Station area is a Frequent Transit Development Area in Our City 2041, the Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 7925, 2017): the City directs higher-density, transit-supportive growth there, so lots near the station carry a different planning future than the interior London Street house streets. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- Secondary suites are allowed in the RS districts and laneway and carriage houses are regulated under Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001; townhouse districts RT-A and RT-B, where designated, require a minimum site area of 836.1 square metres (9,000 square feet) and minimum frontage of 20 metres. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- Connaught Heights houses also sit in a documented heritage context: the neighbourhood is in Volume 3 of the City Heritage Resource Inventory (Sapperton, Victory Heights, Queensborough, Connaught Heights, West End, Kelvin and Glenbrooke). https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/585.php

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which SkyTrain station serves Connaught Heights? **A:** 22nd Street Station on the Expo Line is at the neighbourhood, and the Official Community Plan identifies its surroundings as a Frequent Transit Development Area. TransLink routes from the station include the 100 to Marpole, the 101 to Lougheed Station, the 104 to Annacis Island via Queensborough, and the 128 to Braid Station. For a house buyer, the station is the area's defining amenity — and the reason lots near it are planned differently from the interior streets. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Connaught Heights address? **A:** For the sample address 2201 London Street — Connaught Heights Elementary itself — the School District No. 40 locator result is Connaught Heights Elementary (kindergarten to Grade 6), Fraser River Middle (Grades 6 to 8) and New Westminster Secondary (Grades 9 to 12). The locator also shows Montessori (kindergarten to Grade 5) at Connaught Heights Elementary and Early French Immersion at École Lord Tweedsmuir Elementary. Catchments are address-based, so confirm the exact house. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- **Q:** Can a house here add a suite or a laneway house? **A:** Yes, under the same bylaw that zones the lot. The RS districts of Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 allow secondary suites, and laneway and carriage houses are regulated under that bylaw; RS-1 sets a 557.40 square metre (6,000 square foot) minimum site area and a 0.5 floor space ratio, which frames how much floor area a lot can carry. Permitted space should be documented from City permits before it is priced into a purchase. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Q:** Is Connaught Heights in the floodplain? **A:** No floodplain zone for its residential streets is named in the City's floodplain study. The study zones only the city's low-lying edges — Zone 1 Queensborough, Zone 2 the west-end Fraser River industrial land, Zone 3 the Quay waterfront and Zone 4 the Brunette River industrial area. That is a study-level answer, not a lot-level one: a specific lot's status is checked with the City, especially on the low ground near Marine Way. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Q:** What shopping is inside the neighbourhood? **A:** Very little, on the City's own record. The City's consultation for its Connaught Heights small-sites work recorded that the area has minimal retail services, and no shopping centre inside Connaught Heights was confirmed on an official source read for this note. Daily shopping is a trip out of the neighbourhood; the offsetting fact is the Expo Line station at 22nd Street. https://www.newwestcity.ca/housing/affordable-housing/connaught-heights-small-sites/sb_expander_articles/1840.php

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The elementary school shares its park with the public, with a catch: Connaught Heights Park is shared with Connaught Heights Elementary, and the City's small-sites consultation heard that the shared park is not always accessible to the community during the school day; a recommended action in the City's Comprehensive Parks Plan is to acquire more parkland in Connaught Heights. http://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/community-parks
- Grimston Park is the community park at the west end of the city, the larger park serving these house streets. http://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/community-parks
- The nearest City recreation facilities are east of the area: Moody Park Arena at 701 8th Avenue, with the Moody Park outdoor pool and spray park, and the təməsew̓txʷ Aquatic and Community Centre at 65 East Sixth Avenue, the city's main aquatic centre. https://www.newwestcity.ca/facilities
- Electricity is a City bill, not a BC Hydro bill: New Westminster owns and operates its own electric utility under the Utility Commission Bylaw No. 8029, 2018, and the Utility Commission page lists the outage line at 604-239-6100 and billing at 604-527-4555. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/electrical-utility/sb_expander_articles/469.php
- The Main Branch of New Westminster Public Library is at 716 6th Avenue in Uptown, east of the area — there is no library branch in Connaught Heights. https://www.nwpl.ca/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the floodplain study zones before assuming the west end is clear: the City's study zones the low-lying edges only — Zone 1 Queensborough, Zone 2 west-end Fraser River industrial land, Zone 3 the Quay waterfront, Zone 4 the Brunette River industrial area — and no zone is named for Connaught Heights' residential streets, which settles the study map, not an individual low lot near Marine Way. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve status lot by lot if a property's use matters: no Agricultural Land Reserve land is identified in Connaught Heights in the sources read, and a specific property's status is checked in the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- Check the station-area designation before pricing a lot as a house lot: the 22nd Street Station Frequent Transit Development Area in the Official Community Plan directs higher-density growth near the station, so a lot's plan designation and zoning should both be confirmed with the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
