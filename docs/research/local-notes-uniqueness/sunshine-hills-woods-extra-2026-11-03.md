# Sunshine Hills Woods Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: sunshine-hills-woods (Sunshine Hills Woods, North Delta; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sunshine Hills Woods is a Fraser Valley Real Estate Board (FVREB) sub-area name that combines two City of Delta neighbourhoods; Delta-wide facts are in the tsawwassen-houses-for-sale note and Sunshine-Hills-shared facts in the sunshine-hills-woods-north-delta-houses-for-sale note — see those notes — and this note adds street-level detail only.
- The two City neighbourhoods are Sunshine Hills (neighbourhood 12 of 14) and Sunshine Woods (neighbourhood 13 of 14) on the neighbourhood map of the former North Delta Area Plan, Schedule C.1 under the previous Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 3950. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- The working street grid for the combined area is 72 Avenue north, 64 Avenue south, Watershed Park and Lyon Road west, and Scott Road (120 Street) east — a research grid, because the City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for either neighbourhood. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- The current plan framework is the Delta Official Community Plan 2024, Bylaw No. 8400, a single consolidated plan with no area-plan schedules; its Map 2 shows a Small Scale Residential designation for this part of North Delta. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is predominantly freehold single-detached houses under the Small Scale Residential designation, with strata townhomes at the edges — the 72 Avenue corridor and the south side — and no condo building inside the grid confirmed on a City source. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- Lot size is set parcel by parcel under Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600: single-detached minimums run from RS7 at 335 square metres (m²) (11 m width) and RS6 at 390 m² up to RS1 at 4,000 m², and duplex minimums run from RD4 at 504 m² to RD1 at 836 m², with no RD5 zone in the bylaw. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) is permitted on all single-detached and duplex lots citywide in Delta, so a Sunshine Hills Woods house lot can carry additional small units where the parcel's zone under Bylaw No. 7600 allows the combination. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/
- Delta's SSMUH regulations allow suites, coach houses and garden suites in combination with detached and duplex dwellings where the zoning applies — the parcel's zone sheet, not the street, decides which combination a lot gets. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- Cougar Creek is a salmon-bearing watercourse running through this area, and streamside setback detail is lot-specific — a lot backing Cougar Canyon does not build like an interior lot of the same size. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is actually inside the neighbourhood? **A:** Sunshine Hills Elementary, at 11285 Bond Boulevard, teaching Kindergarten to Grade 7. It sits inside the working grid and shares its address with the School District 37 (SD37) school locator's sample result for this area. The paired secondary school for that sample address is Seaquam Secondary, 11584 Lyon Road, Grades 8 to 12 — but catchments are address-based, so confirm any other address in the locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/037 https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q:** Is "Sunshine Hills Woods" a name the City uses? **A:** No — it is the board sub-area name, not a City neighbourhood. Delta's own former North Delta Area Plan names two separate neighbourhoods, Sunshine Hills (12 of 14) and Sunshine Woods (13 of 14), and the current OCP, Bylaw No. 8400, carries no area-plan schedule for either. City documents, permits and zoning maps will not use the combined name. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- **Q:** What is the big forest on the west side? **A:** Watershed Park, at 11600 Kittson Parkway. The City describes it as Delta's largest park, with eleven kilometres of gravel trails for walking, cycling, jogging and horseback riding. Homes on the park's east edge back onto that trail network rather than onto another street, which is the defining feature of the Woods side of the area. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/watershed-park
- **Q:** Can a house lot here add a garden suite or coach house? **A:** Yes, where the parcel's zoning allows the combination. Delta's SSMUH rules permit garden suites and coach houses in combination with single-detached and duplex dwellings, and SSMUH applies on all single-detached and duplex lots citywide. The minimum lot sizes in Bylaw No. 7600 run from 335 m² to 4,000 m² across the zone ladder, so the zone sheet is the deciding document. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Cougar Canyon Environmental Reserve is at 11300 72 Avenue, at the area's north-west corner, and Westview Park is at 6500 Westview Drive on the south side. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/watershed-park
- Both neighbourhood schools double as City voting places: the City's North Delta voting-location list puts a voting place at Sunshine Hills Elementary, 11285 Bond Boulevard, and at Seaquam Secondary, 11584 Lyon Road. https://admin.delta.ca/media/5297
- Pinewood Elementary, at 11777 Pinewood Drive, is the other elementary school on the City's North Delta voting list for this side of the park — a reminder that west-edge addresses do not all feed Sunshine Hills Elementary, so run the exact address in the SD37 locator. https://admin.delta.ca/media/5297 https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- There is no recreation centre inside the working grid; the North Delta Recreation Centre at 11415 84 Avenue serves North Delta. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/north-delta-recreation-centre
- The nearest library is George Mackie Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library) at 8440 112 Street, north of the area in Nordel. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations
- The nearest hospital is Delta Hospital at 5800 Mountain View Boulevard in Ladner (Fraser Health), and the North Delta Public Health Unit is at 11245 84 Avenue. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- Kittson Parkway is both a street and a park road: it cuts through the Watershed Park area and carries bus route 340, so park-edge homes sit on a bus route, not a cul-de-sac. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta's 2026 flat-rate utility fee is $1,658 for a single-family home, billed by the City separately from the property tax notice. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- City of Delta 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on balances outstanding after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 2, 2026; the approved 2026 Financial Plan included a 2.9% property tax increase for City operational services. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- The City of Delta is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so an owner here files the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 340 is the through-route: Scottsdale Exchange to 22nd Street Station via Scott Road, 64 Avenue, Kittson Parkway, Highway 91 and the Alex Fraser Bridge, running through the Watershed Park edge of the area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf
- Route 364 runs Langley Centre to Scottsdale Exchange via 64 Avenue and Scott Road along the south edge, and route 319 runs Scott Road Station to Newton Exchange via 72 Avenue along the north edge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf
- The R6 RapidBus runs on Scott Road at the area's east side, the fastest bus link from the grid to Scott Road Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf
- Highway 91 access is via 64 Avenue at the south edge — the same corridor route 340 uses to reach the Alex Fraser Bridge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Creek setbacks are the lot-level risk: Cougar Creek is salmon-bearing, the City has done creek bank stabilization work in Cougar Canyon, and the buildable area of a canyon-edge lot is set by its streamside setback, which no area page can state — it is confirmed lot by lot. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- No floodplain designation specific to the residential streets was confirmed in the OCP read; a buyer checks the specific lot rather than relying on the area's upland position beside Watershed Park. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status is a parcel check, not an area assumption: OCP Map 2 shows no Agricultural designation for this part of North Delta, and property-level ALR status is confirmed through the Agricultural Land Commission. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
