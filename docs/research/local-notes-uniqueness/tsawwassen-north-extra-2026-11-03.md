# Tsawwassen North Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: tsawwassen-north (Tsawwassen North, City of Delta; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Tsawwassen North is the north side of Tsawwassen in the City of Delta, along Highway 17 and 52 Street toward Ladner; this note covers the ground north of the Tsawwassen Springs–Beach Grove side of town, at street level. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- "Tsawwassen North" is a Paragon (Fraser Valley Real Estate Board) sub-area name, not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name, and the City publishes no boundary for it. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/215646/
- Tsawwassen First Nation Lands border the north side to the west and north, and Ladner lies across the farm belt to the north, with Tsawwassen Central to the south. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Shared Tsawwassen-wide facts are held in the earlier notes — see the tsawwassen-houses-for-sale note and its sibling notes; shared Delta municipal facts are in the ladner-houses-for-sale note. This note keeps only north-side specifics.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The north side mixes freehold detached houses under the Single Detached Residential (RS) and Duplex (RD) zones of Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, on streets south of Highway 17, with newer strata housing around Springs Boulevard. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- Tsawwassen Springs condo buildings stand at 5011 Springs Boulevard and 5055 Springs Boulevard (developer source). https://www.tsawwassensprings.ca/page/34/?post_type=page&p=0
- Urban subdivision minimums in the applicable ladder run from RS7 at 335 square metres to RD1 at 836 square metres, with RS4 at 610, RS5 at 550 or 460 and RS6 at 390 square metres between them. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- Lots fronting the farm belt and the Highway 17 corridor can be larger rural parcels rather than urban lots — confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City of Delta zoning does not apply on Tsawwassen Lands: Tsawwassen First Nation is self-governing, with jurisdiction over land management on over 724 hectares transferred under the Tsawwassen First Nation Final Agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- The City side is planned under the Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), which consolidated the former area plans into a single Land Use Plan. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Settled fact, stated plainly: the current consolidated OCP contains no Tsawwassen Area Plan. The old "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw No. 8400" attribution is wrong — Schedule D.1 belonged to the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950 — and farm land here is designated Agriculture (A) in the current plan. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Confirm a parcel's zone on the City zoning maps before pricing in suite or subdivision potential, because Bylaw No. 7600 minimums range from 335 to 836 square metres in this ladder alone. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- Where a property borders farm land, Agricultural Land Commission rules apply to land in the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR), including one-residence-per-parcel limits. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Canoe Pass Way address? **A:** Ladner's, on the sample checked. For 5000 Canoe Pass Way, the School District No. 37 (Delta) locator result is Ladner Elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 7) at 5016 44 Avenue and Delta Secondary (Grades 8 to 12) at 4615 51 Street. North-side addresses can fall into Ladner catchments, so check the exact address instead of assuming a Tsawwassen school. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q:** Is a home beside Tsawwassen Lands under City rules? **A:** Only if it sits on the City side of the boundary. Tsawwassen First Nation holds jurisdiction over land management on its treaty lands, over 724 hectares under the Final Agreement, separate from City of Delta zoning. Tenure, servicing and resale handling differ on Tsawwassen Lands, so establish which side of the boundary the property is on before anything else. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **Q:** What bus runs along the north side? **A:** Route 609 is the north-side route: it runs from South Delta Exchange via 56 Street, 12 Avenue, 52 Street and Canoe Pass Way, through Tsawwassen First Nation, to Ladner Exchange. Route 601 serves 56 Street and South Delta Exchange toward Bridgeport Station for Canada Line connections, but it does not trace the Canoe Pass Way corridor. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- **Q:** Where is urgent care on this side of town? **A:** Fraser Health lists a South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 1826–4949 Canoe Pass Way, on the north side's own corridor. The nearest hospital is Delta Hospital at 5800 Mountain View Boulevard in Ladner. Keep both addresses: the Canoe Pass Way centre is after-hours primary care, not a hospital emergency department. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/7/8/Service---South-Delta-After-Hours-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre
- **Q:** Is there a grocery store on the north side itself? **A:** Not one confirmed with an address by an official source. The named grocery in the town-centre sources is Thrifty Foods on the Tsawwassen Town Centre Mall site, whose City-listed addresses are 1219 56 Street, 5499 12 Avenue and 1236 55 Street — south of this note's ground, in the centre of town. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- South Delta Recreation Centre at 1720 56 Street, at South Delta Exchange, is the community recreation centre serving the north side. http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/south-delta-recreation-centre
- Tsawwassen Library, a Fraser Valley Regional Library branch at 1321A 56 Street, serves the north side from the town centre. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations
- The City of Delta is the diking authority here and operates over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and about 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- The BC Ferries Vancouver (Tsawwassen) terminal sits at the southwest end of Highway 17, with sailings to Swartz Bay, Duke Point and Departure Bay (Nanaimo) and the Southern Gulf Islands — ferry traffic is part of Highway 17's rhythm past the north side. https://www.bcferries.com/travel-boarding/terminal-directions-parking-food/vancouver-tsawwassen/TSA
- Highway 17A, reached from the Highway 17 corridor, leads to Highway 99 and the George Massey Tunnel toward Richmond and Vancouver. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Establish the jurisdiction first: a lot on Tsawwassen Lands follows Tsawwassen First Nation land law, not Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, and that changes tenure and servicing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- For strata product around Springs Boulevard, review the strata documents under the Strata Property Act framework; British Columbia strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- For farm-belt lots, check Agricultural Land Reserve status before picturing a second dwelling — Commission rules include one-residence-per-parcel limits on ALR land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Flood protection here is a City system, not a lot feature: Delta operates the dikes, pump stations and drainage network, so ask what protects the specific parcel rather than the community generally. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
