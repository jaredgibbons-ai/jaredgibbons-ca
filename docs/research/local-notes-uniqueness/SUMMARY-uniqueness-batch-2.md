## Conflicts and premise corrections

- **Mission SVT premise corrected by a fresh official read.** The batch allocation assumed the Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies to Mission farms. The Province's current SVT page names the tax's regions; Mission sits in the Fraser Valley Regional District, not the Fraser Valley Board area, and the note states SVT non-applicability as the fact. Flagged for the parent: if any earlier program file claims SVT applies in Mission, the fresh Province read governs.
- **Chilliwack park drug policy changed.** A City of Chilliwack policy page contradicted the workers' first read: the city amended its drug-use-area policy on Sep 15, 2026 (areas remain drug-free; the overnight-stay framing ended). The notes follow the fresh, current state.
- **Pine & Glen address.** The program's slug language says "Johnson & Glen"; the District of North Vancouver's official Pine & Glen Housing page gives 2950 Glen Dr. The brentwood note corrects the record in-file (tower/housing facts) and flags it.
- **Surrey zone-name generations.** The 2026 Surrey planning report for 12588 16 Ave uses current R3 → R2-O zone names; a 2016 report used the old RF/RF-O names. The ocean-view note uses only the 2026 report.
- **White Rock flood levels.** The OCP floodplain figures in #42 (Ocean 3.9 m, Campbell River 4.6 m geodetic) come from a 2017 City corporate report and are flagged "check current" in the file.
- No other conflicts with earlier settled facts were found.
