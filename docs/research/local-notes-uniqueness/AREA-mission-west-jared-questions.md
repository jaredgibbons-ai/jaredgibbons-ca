# Mission West — Questions for Jared

## 5 questions only Jared can answer
1. In your Mission West deals (Silverdale, Silverhill, west of Hatzic toward the Stave River), what do buyers pay up for — a Fraser River or mountain view, flat usable land, or simply being inside the Silverdale growth area?
2. Who is actually buying in Mission West right now — Maple Ridge and Coquitlam buyers priced west, acreage buyers, or land holders betting on the Silverdale Comprehensive Planning Area — and how does that differ street by street?
3. What sells fast and what sits: do homes on city services near Lougheed Highway move first while RU16 acreages on well and septic wait, and how do buyers react when a coach house needs a rezoning like RU16s?
4. What surprises buyers most once they are under contract — that Silverdale still lacks full urban servicing, a septic or well result, an Agricultural Land Reserve boundary through a property, or the drive to Mission City Station for the West Coast Express?
5. Tell one Mission West deal story: a sale, a near-miss, or a problem you solved in Silverdale or Silverhill that shows a buyer what no listing could.
