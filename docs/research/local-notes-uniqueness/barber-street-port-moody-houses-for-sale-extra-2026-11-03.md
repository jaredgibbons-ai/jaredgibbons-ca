## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Barber Street is a board sub-area name inside the City of Port Moody's Pleasantside neighbourhood, not a City neighbourhood name: the City's Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw, 2026, No. 3522 covers this shore in section 14.2, Pleasantside and April Road; the full shared area facts are in the barber-street-port-moody note. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- The name comes from one street: Barber Street runs off Ioco Road on the north shore of the Port Moody Arm, and Pleasantside Elementary School sits on it at 195 Barber Street. http://www.sd43.bc.ca/school/pleasantside/Pages/default.aspx
- The working street set is the shore strip below Ioco Road — Barber Street, Alderside Road and the lanes off them — with Ioco Road as the only through road and Old Orchard Park at the west end of the shoreline. https://engage.portmoody.ca/rocky-point-and-old-orchard-parks-master-plans/news_feed/background-rocky-point-today

- The stock is detached houses on the shore strip below Ioco Road, on Barber Street, Alderside Road and the lanes off them, in the Pleasantside section of the Port Moody 2050 Official Community Plan. https://engage.portmoody.ca/rocky-point-and-old-orchard-parks-master-plans/news_feed/background-rocky-point-today

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Port Moody 2050, the City's Official Community Plan, was adopted by Council on April 28, 2026, and Chapter 14 covers Pleasantside and April Road in section 14.2. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/business-and-development/official-community-plan.aspx
- The RS1-S small-lot rezoning route is available in Pleasantside — one of the few Port Moody areas where it is offered — with minimum lot figures of 9 metres width and 325 square metres. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Small-scale multi-unit housing is permitted in the RS and RT zones outside the City's transit-oriented areas, under the provincial housing legislation the City has implemented. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- Port Moody is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves Barber Street? **A:** Pleasantside Elementary School stands on the street itself, at 195 Barber Street, per the school's own School District No. 43 (SD43) page; SD43 assigns catchments by address, so confirm the assigned middle and secondary schools for a specific address with the district. http://www.sd43.bc.ca/school/pleasantside/Pages/default.aspx
- **Q:** Can a Barber Street lot be split or densified? **A:** Lot-by-lot routes are the change mechanism here: the RS1-S small-lot program in Pleasantside (minimum 9 metres width and 325 square metres) and small-scale multi-unit housing in the RS zones are the Official Community Plan's stated densification routes for this neighbourhood, rather than project rezonings. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- **Q:** Is Barber Street a City neighbourhood? **A:** No: it is a real-estate board sub-area name inside the City's Pleasantside neighbourhood, planned in section 14.2 (Pleasantside and April Road) of the Port Moody 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw, 2026, No. 3522, so City documents are found under Pleasantside. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- **Q:** What is at the end of the shoreline? **A:** Old Orchard Park, at the west end of the shoreline below Ioco Road, is the shore's park anchor, and the City is running master plans for Rocky Point and Old Orchard parks together. https://engage.portmoody.ca/rocky-point-and-old-orchard-parks-master-plans/news_feed/background-rocky-point-today

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The sub-area is named for a single short street, so a "Barber Street" listing is on or just off Barber Street and Alderside Road, not in a large district. http://www.sd43.bc.ca/school/pleasantside/Pages/default.aspx
- Ioco Road is the only through road: every shore street hangs off it, so road work on Ioco Road affects the whole strip. https://engage.portmoody.ca/rocky-point-and-old-orchard-parks-master-plans/news_feed/background-rocky-point-today
- Pleasantside is one of the few Port Moody areas where the RS1-S small-lot program is offered, alongside Moody Centre and Seaview — and not in College Park. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Confirm the lot's RS1-S eligibility before assuming a small-lot split, because the program's minimums are 9 metres width and 325 square metres and it is offered only in named areas. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Confirm the SD43 catchment for the exact address, since the elementary school on the street does not settle the middle and secondary assignments. http://www.sd43.bc.ca/school/pleasantside/Pages/default.aspx
- Check shoreline and slope conditions with the City for a waterfront-side lot, since the shore strip sits below Ioco Road on the Port Moody Arm. https://engage.portmoody.ca/development-applications
