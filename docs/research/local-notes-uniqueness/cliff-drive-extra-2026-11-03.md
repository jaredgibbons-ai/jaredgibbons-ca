# Cliff Drive Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: cliff-drive (Cliff Drive, Tsawwassen; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Cliff Drive is a pocket of north-west Tsawwassen in the City of Delta, and the City publishes no official boundary for the name. It is a Paragon sub-area name for the streets around 12 Avenue, Cliff Drive and English Bluff Road, anchored by Cliff Drive Elementary at 5025 12 Ave (https://cd.deltasd.bc.ca/).
- The City plans this ground as part of Tsawwassen, one of Delta's three communities (Ladner, Tsawwassen and North Delta), under the Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) (https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future).
- Brandrith Park, 5093 12 Ave, sits inside the pocket on the City's park search, which places the working centre of the area on 12 Avenue west of 56 Street (https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/brandrith-park).
- English Bluff is west, Tsawwassen Central is east at 56 Street, and Pebble Hill is south-east — see the tsawwassen-central note for the centre (https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/brandrith-park).

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock here is freehold single detached houses and duplexes, because zoning is parcel by parcel under Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 and the zones found in Tsawwassen are the RS single detached and RD duplex families (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf).
- Subdivision minimums in those zones run from RS7 at 335 m² and RS6 at 390 m² up to RS4 at 610 m² and RD1 duplex at 836 m², so lot pattern is set by the parcel's zone, not by the sub-area name (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf).
- No condo or townhome complex with a street address in Cliff Drive was confirmed on a City, developer or strata source — see NOT CONFIRMED (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Delta's zoning umbrella is Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, and this note carries the city-wide rules in full for the Delta cluster. The bylaw's zone sheets set each zone's uses and minimum lot sizes, and the parcel's zone is read off the City zoning maps (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf).
- Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies (https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future).
- There is no Tsawwassen Area Plan in the current OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) is a single consolidated plan, and the former Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the superseded OCP, Bylaw No. 3950 (https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future).
- On the pocket's western edge, hazard Development Permit Area SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment) applies to parcels shown on the OCP maps, so escarpment-edge lots need a parcel-level check before building plans are priced (https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf).
- Any Delta property within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area (https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf).
- Delta permits 2 to 4 hens for personal use on a lot in any RS or RD zone, with coop and run at most 10 m², no roosters, and an annual licence under the Animal Control Bylaw (https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is Cliff Drive an official neighbourhood?
**A:** No — Cliff Drive is a Paragon sub-area name only, and the City of Delta publishes no boundary for it. The City plans the ground as part of Tsawwassen under the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). Describe any property by its street, its zone on the Bylaw No. 7600 maps and its school catchment, not by the sub-area label alone (https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future).

**Q:** Which schools serve a Cliff Drive home?
**A:** Catchments are address-based in School District 37, with elementary at Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary at Grades 8 to 12 and no middle tier. For the sample address 1299 56 St, the district locator result is Cliff Drive Elementary, 5025 12 Ave, and South Delta Secondary, 750 53 St. Check the exact address in the locator, because one result does not bind the next street (https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/).

**Q:** Can a house here add a suite or extra units?
**A:** Yes, where the zoning applies — Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots. That is a zoning permission, not a building approval: the parcel's zone under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, servicing and any Development Permit Area on the lot still govern what can actually be built (https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future).

**Q:** Does the escarpment affect homes on the west side?
**A:** It can — the OCP's hazard Development Permit Area SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment) covers parcels shown on the OCP maps at the pocket's western edge. A development permit and geotechnical input may be required before construction there. Which specific Cliff Drive parcels fall inside SD2 is read parcel by parcel off the OCP maps, not from the sub-area name (https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf).

**Q:** Is Cliff Drive behind dikes?
**A:** Delta's low land is protected by a City-run system, and the City operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure — dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection — plus 31 drainage pump stations and about 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure. This pocket sits on the upland side of Tsawwassen, but a parcel's flood construction level is property-specific and is confirmed with the City (http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage).

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- This note carries the City of Delta cost facts in full for the Delta cluster. Delta Council approved a 2.9 per cent property tax increase for 2026 on December 8, 2025, and the 2026 tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026 (https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes).
- The 2026 flat rate utility bill for a single-family home is $1,658, up $51 from $1,607 in 2025; residential owners receive the annual flat rate bill at the end of February for garbage and recycling, with water and sewer on that bill or on separate quarterly metered bills (http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities).
- Delta's utility bylaws are Bylaw No. 8537 (water service), Bylaw No. 8538 (sewer rates) and Bylaw No. 8539 (garbage collection) (http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities).
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies here: the Province lists the City of Delta among the Metro Vancouver Regional District taxable areas, and residential owners in a taxable area must complete the annual declaration (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas).
- The exact 2026 residential tax rate per $1,000 is in NOT CONFIRMED — the City's rate schedule document could not be opened in this pass (https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes).

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- There is no rapid transit in Tsawwassen; buses from South Delta Exchange are the transit link. TransLink route 601 runs South Delta / Boundary Bay to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line) via 56 Street, and route 602 runs Tsawwassen Heights to Bridgeport Station, both via South Delta Exchange at 56 Street and 18 Avenue (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf).
- Route 620 links the BC Ferries Tsawwassen terminal to Bridgeport Station via Highway 17, Highway 17A, Ladner Exchange and Highway 99 (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt620.pdf).
- The BC Ferries Vancouver (Tsawwassen) terminal is at the end of Highway 17, with sailings to Swartz Bay (Victoria), Duke Point and Departure Bay (Nanaimo) and the Southern Gulf Islands (https://www.bcferries.com/travel-boarding/terminal-directions-parking-food/vancouver-tsawwassen/TSA).
- By road, 56 Street north reaches Highway 17, then Highway 17A and Highway 99 through the George Massey Tunnel toward Richmond and Vancouver (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf). Driving times are UNCONFIRMED — no operator page states them.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the parcel against hazard Development Permit Area SD2 English Bluff on the OCP maps before buying on the western edge — the map, not the listing, shows whether the lot is in the escarpment area (https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf).
- Check streamside status: any lot within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, which can limit building placement (https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf).
- Check the parcel's zone and flood construction level with the City: zoning is parcel by parcel under Bylaw No. 7600, and the City is the diking authority for Delta's flood protection system (http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage).
- The OCP and zoning maps do not tell a buyer a lot's soil, drainage history or a building's condition — those need a survey, a geotechnical report and an inspection, none of which a map layer replaces (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf).
