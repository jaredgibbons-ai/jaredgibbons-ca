# Lake Errock Area Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: lake-errock-mission-guide (Lake Errock; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Lake Errock is an unincorporated lakeside community in Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area C, east of Deroche near Harrison Mills — not inside the City of Mission and not inside the District of Kent. The FVRD states its Area C plan area spans from the Harrison River to east of Deroche and includes Morris Valley, Harrison Mills, Lake Errock and the eastern Deroche Bench (https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/areacocp?tool=news_feed).
- The settled streets sit around the lake off Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and Morris Valley Road, including Bayview Road and Malcolm Road on the lake side (https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/lake-errock).
- Lake Errock has its own named district identity: the FVRD lists Electoral Area C as Sasquatch Country, covering Harrison Mills, Hemlock Valley and Lake Errock (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/contact/your-electoral-area-director.html).

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is lake and rural housing — waterfront and view houses on Bayview Road, rural parcels on Malcolm Road and Morris Valley Road, and manufactured homes near Highway 7 — with no strata complex confirmed on an official source read for this note (https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/lake-errock).
- Servicing is lot-by-lot rather than municipal: the FVRD ran a Lake Errock Neighbourhood Plan servicing feasibility study and a local sanitary servicing plan process as part of the Area C plan work, because new Lake Errock services had to be studied before growth (https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/areacocp?tool=news_feed).
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A new community plan now governs Lake Errock: the FVRD Electoral Area C Official Community Plan, Bylaw 1747, 2024, adopted September 18, 2025, includes a dedicated Lake Errock Neighbourhood Plan and replaced the former plan, Bylaw No. 0020, 1998 (https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/areacocp?tool=news_feed).
- FVRD Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021 permits one secondary suite or one accessory dwelling unit per lot, not both, in almost all electoral areas, subject to lot size and servicing (https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/adu).
- On Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) parcels the Agricultural Land Commission allows one principal residence up to 500 square metres plus one additional residence of 90 square metres up to 40 hectares, or 186 square metres above 40 hectares (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is Lake Errock part of Mission? **A:** No, it only carries a Mission mailing address. Lake Errock is an unincorporated FVRD Electoral Area C community, so FVRD zoning, FVRD permits and provincial rural taxes apply. Neither City of Mission nor District of Kent bylaws or tax dates govern a Lake Errock lot (https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/areacocp?tool=news_feed).
**Q:** Which school district serves Lake Errock? **A:** School District No. 75 (Mission), not School District No. 78. The FVRD's election notice places Harrison Mills–Lake Errock Area C voters in the District No. 75 rural trustee election, and there is no school in Lake Errock itself on the provincial school list; the nearest listed schools are Deroche and Dewdney elementary (https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075).
**Q:** What happened to the big Harrison Rise development? **A:** The rezoning is withdrawn and no longer active. The FVRD states GWEB Holdings Ltd. withdrew its zoning amendment application for Harrison Rise at 43837 and 43873 Lougheed Highway on March 7, 2025, and the FVRD no longer has an active rezoning application for it (https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/areacocp?tool=news_feed).
**Q:** Can I put a dock or boat on Lake Errock? **A:** That is unconfirmed on any official page read for this note, so do not rely on listing wording. Confirm frontage, dock tenure and any lake boating rule on title and with the FVRD and the Province for the specific lot before paying for water access (https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/lake-errock).
**Q:** What does the new Lake Errock Neighbourhood Plan change? **A:** It sets the land-use direction for the community inside the new Area C plan. The FVRD adopted Bylaw 1747, 2024 on September 18, 2025 with dedicated neighbourhood plans for Lake Errock and Harrison Mills, so a lot's designation must now be read in that bylaw, not the 1998 plan it replaced (https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/areacocp?tool=news_feed).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The community's growth debate was driven by a former gravel pit: the FVRD's project page is titled Lake Errock Gravel Pit Redevelopment, and the Harrison Rise proposal for that site included 11 three-unit multiplexes (33 dwelling units) in the material summarized by the FVRD (https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/lake-errock).
- Fire protection here has a named local investment: the FVRD's Canada Community-Building Fund report lists a North Fraser Fire Lake Errock gear storage renovation and Lake Errock Community Park upgrades as funded projects (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Financial~Reporting/CWF Annual Expenditure Report for Reporting Year 2025.pdf).
- A Morris Valley Roadside Trail Extension and Shelter is listed in the same FVRD fund report for Electoral Area C, on the road serving Lake Errock's east side (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Financial~Reporting/CWF Annual Expenditure Report for Reporting Year 2025.pdf).
- School District No. 75 assigns Deroche and Dewdney elementary catchments to Hatzic Middle School, which is the middle-school path Lake Errock families check first for a specific address (https://media.mpsd.ca/media/Default/medialib/merged-agenda-package-cotw_dec03_2024.054b732103.pdf).
- The nearest hospital named on an official health page is Mission Memorial Hospital, 7324 Hurd Street, Mission, with a 25-bed medical inpatient unit, a 20-bed Patient Assessment and Transition Home unit and a 10-bed hospice (https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/mission/mission-memorial-hospital).

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Lake Errock owners pay property tax to the Province, not to a municipality, because Lake Errock is a rural area; the Province states rural owners receive their notice from the Surveyor of Taxes Office (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area).
- Rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5 percent penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5 percent on amounts unpaid on October 31, under section 11 of the Taxation (Rural Area) Act (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01).
- Electoral Area C has its own FVRD sewer service area for Morris Valley (service 444 in the FVRD financial plan), which is an Area C cost structure that does not exist in Areas F or G; whether a Lake Errock lot pays it is a parcel check (https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=29616).
- City of Mission figures do not apply here; see the mission-farms note for the city-wide figures.
Read: 2026-11-03

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) is the through route, west to Deroche and Mission and east across the Harrison River toward Agassiz; BC Transit and the FVRD consulted in spring 2025 on a proposed Highway 7 bus between downtown Mission and downtown Agassiz that would serve this corridor if funded (https://www.bctransit.com/have-your-say-in-the-future-of-transit-along-highway-2025-04-25/).
- No TransLink service reaches Lake Errock; the West Coast Express commuter trains run from Mission City Station in the City of Mission, west of Deroche (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf).

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Read the lot's designation and any development permit areas in the new Bylaw 1747, 2024 Lake Errock Neighbourhood Plan schedules, because the 1998 plan's hazard and environmentally sensitive permit areas no longer govern (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20C%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%2020%20-%20Morris%20Valley,%20Harrison%20Mills%20and%20Lake%20Errock.pdf).
- Confirm water source, septic or sewer connection and perc results lot by lot on Bayview Road and Malcolm Road; the FVRD's own servicing feasibility work for the neighbourhood plan is the starting document, not listing text (https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/areacocp?tool=news_feed).
- Check ALR status by parcel identifier number (PID) on the Agricultural Land Commission maps before assuming a second home or subdivision is possible (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
