## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** How do I tell which rules apply to a townhome in West Central versus Silver Valley?
**A:** Start with the City's two overlays, not the sub-area name: whether the site is inside a Transit-Oriented Area, and whether it is inside the Urban Containment Boundary. The City's proposed zoning focuses density near transit and the Town Centre, while edge sub-areas follow the base zones. The Urban Infill checklist works through those questions lot by lot before a permit is filed. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-12/Residential%20Zoning%20Checklist%20-%20Urban%20-%20Fillable.pdf

**Q:** Will the single-detached lots beside my townhome complex change?
**A:** They can: eligible single-family and duplex lots can now hold up to three, four or six units, and the City lists townhouse among the multi-unit forms needing a development permit first. That does not approve any specific neighbouring project. Check the specific lot against the checklist figures — lot area, Urban Containment Boundary position and the 400-metre prescribed bus stop test. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new

**Q:** What changed in Maple Ridge taxes for 2026?
**A:** Council adopted a 3.5 percent property tax increase for 2026, and water and sewer utility rates rose 4.5 percent and 6.05 percent; the City estimates the combined increase for an average home with water and sewer service at $181, or 4.1 percent. A strata townhouse sees the utility part through its own meter or flat-rate account, so check which billing the complex uses. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/have-your-say-draft-2026-budget-focused-affordability-march-29

**Q:** When are Maple Ridge property taxes due?
**A:** On the first business day in July, which for 2026 is July 2. The City bills flat-rate water and sewer on the same tax notice, while metered utility customers are billed quarterly instead. A townhouse buyer closing near July should confirm which cycle the unit is on, because the July notice can carry both tax and utilities. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/video-transcript-understanding-your-property-taxespdf-0

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Maple Ridge's 2026 residential mill rate totals 4.53578 per $1,000 of assessed value, with the municipal general portion at 2.86430. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
