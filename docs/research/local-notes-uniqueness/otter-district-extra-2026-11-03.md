# Otter District Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: otter-district (Otter District; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Otter District is the rural farming district around 248 Street and Fraser Highway on Langley's Aldergrove side. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/additional-naming-sponsor-announced-for-new-aldergrove-credit-union-community-centre.aspx
- The Otter Co-op retail centre at 3650 248 Street is the district namesake landmark. https://www.angryotterliquor.crs/wcm/connect/sites/6220d307-766a-404a-a9ce-d8f1062f13f8/OtterFarm100y+FINAL.pdf?MOD=AJPERES&CACHEID=ROOTWORKSPACE.Z18_2IKA1G82M08S50ANCIK0482G01-6220d307-766a-404a-a9ce-d8f1062f13f8-oxkGaH9
- 40 Avenue and 248 Street form the local grid named in the assignment brief; the Salmon River (Langley) crosses the district's north side toward Fort Langley.
- Aldergrove lies east on Fraser Highway and the Salmon River Uplands lie north and west.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Housing is detached farmhouses and rural homes on acreage lots, with farm buildings as the dominant built form. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Most Otter parcels are in the ALR, so ALC residence rules apply on top of Township zoning. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- Principal residences in the ALR are capped at 500 m² total floor area and additional residences at the sizes in the ALR Use Regulation (90 m² cited in ALC correspondence), with larger or extra homes needing Commission approval. https://webfiles.islandstrust.bc.ca/islands/local-trust-areas/south-pender/current-projects/Minor%20LUB%20Amendments%20Project/8%20-%20Public%20Hearing%20Binder/05.2_SP-BL-129_Bylaw%20referral_Responses.pdf
- Placing a mobile home on private land, including as a temporary accessory dwelling, is subject to Township Zoning Bylaw rules and, in the ALR, to ALC provisions. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/resources/building/bulletins-forms-resources/Document-Feed-Building-Forms/PLI---Mobile-Homes-on-Private-Property.pdf
- The Township's Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing amendments under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 (Bylaw No. 6020, 2024) address suites, accessory dwelling units and coach houses in restricted residential zones, with Farm Bylaw and ALR conditions noted. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/Report-to-Council----Small-Scale,-Multi-Unit-Housing,-June-10,-2024.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Why is it called Otter District? **A:** The name comes from the Otter District Farmers' Institute, formed here in 1922. The Co-op's own history states twenty-five local farmers formed the Institute in 1922 to buy supplies together, it converted to a co-operative in 1979, and the retail centre at 3650 248 Street still carries the Otter name a century later. https://www.angryotterliquor.crs/wcm/connect/sites/6220d307-766a-404a-a9ce-d8f1062f13f8/OtterFarm100y+FINAL.pdf?MOD=AJPERES&CACHEID=ROOTWORKSPACE.Z18_2IKA1G82M08S50ANCIK0482G01-6220d307-766a-404a-a9ce-d8f1062f13f8-oxkGaH9
- **Q:** Is there still an Otter Elementary School? **A:** No — the historic Otter Elementary closed in June 2004, per the Langley Retired Teachers' history. That history records the first Otter school built in 1889, enrolment of 205 pupils by 1983, and closure in 2004 with students disbursed to nearby schools including North Otter and Coghlan; buyers must use the current SD35 locator for today's catchments. https://bcrta.ca/langley/history/otter-elementary-school/
- **Q:** Which schools serve Otter today? **A:** SD35 schools named in district lists for this side of Langley include North Otter Elementary and Coghlan Fundamental Elementary, feeding Aldergrove-area secondaries. SD35 states catchments are set by its maps and School Locator Tool, so the exact 248 Street or 40 Avenue address must be checked there before subject removal. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/catchment-maps
- **Q:** Can I put a mobile home on my acreage as a second dwelling? **A:** Only with permits and, in the ALR, within ALC limits. The Township states a building permit is required to place a mobile home, placement as a single-family or temporary accessory dwelling is regulated by the Zoning Bylaw, and ALC regulations may affect allowances on ALR land; the home must meet CSA A277 or Z240 standards. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/resources/building/bulletins-forms-resources/Document-Feed-Building-Forms/PLI---Mobile-Homes-on-Private-Property.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The school emblem, the Otter, was moved to the Otter Co-op in 2004 when the school closed, and a commemorative plaque was unveiled at the original school site. https://bcrta.ca/langley/history/otter-elementary-school/
- The Co-op sponsored the outdoor portion of the Aldergrove Credit Union Community Centre at 27032 Fraser Highway as the "Otter Co-op Outdoor Experience," with an initial $250,000 contribution for the first five-year period. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/additional-naming-sponsor-announced-for-new-aldergrove-credit-union-community-centre.aspx
- The Co-op CEO stated in that Township release that Aldergrove has been Otter's home since 1922. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/additional-naming-sponsor-announced-for-new-aldergrove-credit-union-community-centre.aspx

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Rural Township homes on flat-rate utilities pay the same published Township schedule as other single-family homes. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- The 2026 flat rates are $779.96 water and $716.47 sewer per year for single-family, plus a $214.94 sewer and $233.99 water additional fee where a secondary suite exists. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- Garbage is $226.00 base plus $200.00 for a 240 L cart or $100.00 for a 120 L cart in 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- The Township of Langley is a designated Speculation and Vacancy Tax taxable area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- For Township of Langley headline tax rates, see the langley-farms note.

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Fraser Highway is the east–west route through Aldergrove, and 248 Street runs north–south through the district. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/additional-naming-sponsor-announced-for-new-aldergrove-credit-union-community-centre.aspx
- BC Transit route 21 Aldergrove/Abbotsford connects the Aldergrove side to Abbotsford in the Central Fraser Valley route list. https://www.cptdb.ca/wiki/index.php?title=Central_Fraser_Valley_Transit_System&diff=808384&oldid=581076
