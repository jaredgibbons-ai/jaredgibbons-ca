# East Chilliwack Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: east-chilliwack-guide (East Chilliwack, Chilliwack; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- East Chilliwack is the valley-floor farm and rural-residential area east of Chilliwack Proper, toward Rosedale — see the east-chilliwack note for street-level detail. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1618
- Its school corridor is Chilliwack Central Road: East Chilliwack Elementary is at 49190 Chilliwack Central Road, per the Province's school list. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/033
- The City classifies its neighbourhood plans as urban, hillside and rural; East Chilliwack's farmland falls under the Agricultural Area Plan, Schedule B1 of the 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP), not a standalone neighbourhood plan. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Housing is mainly detached homes on rural and farm lots in the valley bottom, with farm buildings on working Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) parcels — see the east-chilliwack note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1618
- The City states nearly 70% of land in Chilliwack is in the ALR and not available for development, which shapes why East Chilliwack stock stays low-density. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1618
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Agricultural Area Plan, Schedule B1 of OCP Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, is the plan covering East Chilliwack farmland. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
- Development Permit Area D-4, Farm Home Plate, requires residential buildings on valley-floor farmland to cluster in a farm home plate, leaving the balance for agriculture, where siting does not conform to zoning. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
- Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 applies: in standard-dike areas habitable floors must meet the flood construction level for the Fraser, Chilliwack and Vedder rivers, plus 0.6 metres freeboard, unless a covenant and qualified professional's report support a waiver. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve East Chilliwack? **A:** School District No. 33 (SD33) transportation lists the Eastern Hillside/East Chilliwack area as East Chilliwack Elementary School, Rosedale Traditional Community School, and Chilliwack Secondary School. That three-school pathway, elementary on Chilliwack Central Road, middle in Rosedale, secondary in Chilliwack Proper, is the pattern a buyer should verify for their exact address. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2023-03/611%20AP%20Transportation%20-%20Operating%20Instructions.pdf
- **Q:** Will the new east-side elementary school change East Chilliwack catchments? **A:** Possibly for nearby areas, but not confirmed for East Chilliwack itself. In May 2026 SD33 approved splitting Cheam Elementary's catchment between Little Mountain, Strathcona and the new east-side elementary opening September 2027, and moving Rosedale Kindergarten-Grade 5 students there. East Chilliwack Elementary was not named in that decision. https://sd33.bc.ca/news/board-approves-changes-some-schools
- **Q:** Can I subdivide a farm lot in East Chilliwack? **A:** Subdivision needs City Approving Officer approval and, inside the ALR, Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) rules also apply. The City's subdivisions page lists creating two or more lots as requiring a subdivision application. Do not assume a large lot can be split until both the City zoning and ALR status are checked for that parcel. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=653
- **Q:** Is there a bus through East Chilliwack? **A:** BC Transit route 71 runs on Yale Road, north of the Chilliwack Central Road farm area, between downtown Chilliwack and Harrison Hot Springs. No BC Transit route through the East Chilliwack farm interior was listed in the riders' guide read this pass, so most East Chilliwack homes rely on driving to Yale Road or into Chilliwack Proper. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/riders-guide/
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- East Chilliwack Elementary offers before/after care through Little Valley 123 Child Care, listed on SD33's early-learning site. https://earlylearning.sd33.bc.ca/after-school-care-sites
- The City's flood FAQ describes an ongoing $30 million program to raise the dike system to the current provincial elevation and states the East Dyke has been upgraded. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1912
- Fraser River flood response phases are keyed to the Mission gauge, at about 5.5 metres bankfull and 6.0 metres for minor flooding in unprotected areas, per the City flood information read in earlier work and its Flood Protection page. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1912
- Hillside land in Chilliwack is defined by the City for subdivision purposes as land with slopes of 20 percent or more — relevant only at East Chilliwack's upland edges, not the flat farm floor. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=653
- Most subdivisions require a tree and green space management plan under Tree Management (Land Development) Bylaw 2008, No. 3585, before final approval. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=653
Read: 2026-11-03

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City of Chilliwack 2025 residential property tax total was 4.50189 per $1,000 of assessed taxable value, per the City's 2025 rate table; the table notes local area service charges may apply to specific properties in addition. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2527/Tax%5FRates%5Ffor%5Fthe%5FWebtugnc2fs1l9d%2Epdf
- The City of Chilliwack is a designated taxable area for the Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT), so East Chilliwack owners must complete the annual declaration for residential property. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- City utility rates are billed quarterly; farm properties may be on well or septic rather than city services — confirm servicing on the Tax and Utility Rates page by address, as no East Chilliwack-specific rate was published there. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
Read: 2026-11-03

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Highway 1 access for East Chilliwack is from the Prest Road and Annis Road interchanges on the city's east side — interchange numbers were not stated on an official page read this pass, so they are not given here.
- BC Transit route 71 on Yale Road is the nearest listed bus corridor, running downtown Chilliwack to Harrison Hot Springs through Rosedale. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/riders-guide/
- Route 66, Fraser Valley Express, provides the regional Highway 1 bus link from Chilliwack toward Lougheed Station. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/riders-guide/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the City's floodplain maps and Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 for the specific lot's flood construction level before buying a valley-floor home. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- Check ALR status with the ALC and farm home plate siting with the City before planning a second dwelling, suite or non-farm building. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
- Check well water and septic, drainage and dyking charges per lot, because East Chilliwack services vary between farm and rural-residential parcels and no single service map was read this pass. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
