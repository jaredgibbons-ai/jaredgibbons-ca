## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Land near Stoney Creek is the key local rule: Burnaby's Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area rules in Zoning Bylaw section 6.23 require setbacks near streams, ravines, wetlands and lakeshores. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/construction-and-renovation/green-building-and-land-development/environmental-review-committee
- A buyer planning an addition or redevelopment within 30 metres of a stream should expect a streamside leave area of 15 to 30 metres for a Class A fish-bearing watercourse, set by the Planning Department for the specific site. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2021-08/Streams-and-Ravines-Informational-Guide.pdf
- The City's Environmental Review Committee can consider relaxing a streamside setback where full protection is not feasible, for a stated application fee. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/construction-and-renovation/green-building-and-land-development/environmental-review-committee

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which SkyTrain lines serve Sullivan Heights condos? **A:** Two lines serve it at Lougheed Town Centre Station. TransLink lists Lougheed Town Centre as a transfer station between the Expo Line and the Millennium Line, and the Millennium Line runs from VCC-Clark through Burnaby to Lafarge Lake–Douglas in Coquitlam, so a resident can ride west toward Vancouver or east toward Coquitlam from the same station. http://retrosearch.org/browse?url=https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/skytrain
- **Q:** What is happening to the Cameron Community Centre during construction? **A:** A temporary centre is operating inside The City of Lougheed Shopping Centre. The City states the temporary Cameron Community Centre is on the main level of the shopping centre at 110-9855 Austin Avenue while the replacement centre and library are built, and extra fitness and children's programs run at nearby Charles Rummel Centre. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/cameron-recreation-complex
- **Q:** Does the streamside rule affect a townhome purchase here? **A:** It can affect future work, not the existing home. Burnaby requires setbacks for development near streams and ravines under Zoning Bylaw section 6.23, and the City directs owners near a stream to contact Planning about setback requirements that may affect their property before planning additions or redevelopment. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/construction-and-renovation/green-building-and-land-development/environmental-review-committee
- **Q:** Which secondary school zone is this part of Burnaby in? **A:** This area falls in the district's Cariboo Lougheed zone, whose secondary school is Burnaby Mountain Secondary. The Burnaby Board of Education lists Burnaby Mountain Secondary with Cameron, Lyndhurst, Seaforth and Stoney Creek Community elementary schools in that zone; the exact catchment for an address must still be confirmed with the district's school locator. https://burnabyschools.ca/board/trustees/
