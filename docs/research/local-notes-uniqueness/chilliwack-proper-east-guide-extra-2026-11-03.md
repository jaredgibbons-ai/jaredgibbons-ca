# Chilliwack Proper East Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: chilliwack-proper-east-guide (Chilliwack Proper East; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Chilliwack Proper East has no official boundary either: it is the eastern part of Chilliwack Proper, and the City publishes no boundary for the sub-area name — the shared Proper facts are written in full in the chilliwack-proper-west-guide note, and this note carries the east side's own angle. https://www.cadreb.com/market/
- Yale Road and Portage Avenue are the main streets named in official sources for this part of Chilliwack Proper, and the Downtown Neighbourhood Plan description — the original town site between the Fraser River to the north and the Vedder River to the south — is the orientation the guide page uses. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- The plan frame is the same Schedule B2 Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan as the west side — see the chilliwack-proper-west-guide note for the plan history and designations in full. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- Kinsmen Park on Portage Avenue, at 46490 Portage Avenue, is this side's named City park, a 2-hectare park northeast of downtown. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=104&backTo=%26dowhat%3DamenityView%26paID%3D26%26plID%3D192

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The east side's distinct rule is river flood exposure: the Downtown Neighbourhood Plan position carried in the guide research is that Chilliwack Proper is subject to the flood risk of the Fraser River, the dyke system is built to a 1-in-200-year flood standard, and at higher river stages areas outside the City's dyking system flood. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- New construction here is governed by Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519, with habitable floors at the Flood Construction Level plus 0.6 metres of freeboard — the full bylaw treatment is in the chilliwack-proper-west-guide note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- Suite, coach house and small-scale multi-unit rules are city-wide and are written in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note, with the Proper-specific flood angle in the chilliwack-proper-west-guide note; this note does not repeat them. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- School catchments are a moving rule on this side: SD33 catchment changes approved May 12, 2026 take effect in September 2027 and redraw which elementary school several east-side addresses feed into — see section 5. https://sd33.bc.ca/news/board-approves-changes-some-schools

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Proper East address? **A:** The elementary schools named for this side are Bernard Elementary at 45465 Bernard Avenue, Robertson Elementary at 46106 Southlands Crescent and Strathcona Elementary at 46375 Strathcona Road, all Kindergarten to Grade 5, with Chilliwack Middle School at 46354 Yale Road for Grades 6 to 8 and Chilliwack Secondary at 46363 Yale Road for Grades 9 to 12. SD33 assigns by street address, so confirm the exact address in the SD33 School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/033
- **Q:** Are the Yale Road schools full? **A:** Yes, on the district's own figures: SD33's enrolment-balancing material reported Chilliwack Secondary at 135% of functional capacity and Chilliwack Middle at 122%, with the district overall at 103% of functional capacity. That pressure is why the district ran a formal catchment consultation from January 28 to April 10, 2026 and approved changes in May 2026 that take effect in September 2027. https://sd33.bc.ca/news/board-consider-consultation-draft-plan-balance-school-enrolment
- **Q:** Will my child's elementary catchment change? **A:** It can: the Board approved changes on May 12, 2026, effective September 2027, under which the Cheam Elementary catchment is split between Little Mountain Elementary, Strathcona Elementary and the new east side elementary school, and the Early French Immersion program moves from Cheam Elementary to the new east side elementary school. Check the exact address against the 2027 catchments, not the current ones. https://sd33.bc.ca/news/board-approves-changes-some-schools
- **Q:** Is Chilliwack Proper East in a floodplain? **A:** The area is subject to Fraser River flood risk on the Downtown Neighbourhood Plan's own description: the dyke system is built to a 1-in-200-year flood standard, and areas outside the dyking system flood at higher river stages. Flood status is set lot by lot under Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519, so the parcel's flood construction level is a City check for the specific address before subjects are removed. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- **Q:** What bus runs east from here? **A:** Route 71 Agassiz-Harrison runs from Downtown Chilliwack east along Yale Road through Rosedale and Agassiz to Harrison Hot Springs, and Route 53 Fairfield loops from Downtown Chilliwack via Menzies Street and Young Road. BC Transit also moved Route 57 off Carleton Street onto McNaught Road on January 4, 2026, with new stops on McNaught and Acorn, which changed the walk to a bus stop for streets on this side. https://www.bctransit.com/agassiz-harrison/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=71 https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Catchments on this side change in September 2027: Rosedale catchment Kindergarten to Grade 5 students will attend the new east side elementary school when it opens that month, and the district's north side Early and Late French Immersion programs move from Chilliwack Middle School to Rosedale Traditional Community School for 2027-28. https://sd33.bc.ca/news/board-approves-changes-some-schools
- Kinsmen Park's tennis courts have a name of their own: the Dorothy Kostrzewa Tennis Courts, 4 courts at 46490 Portage Avenue, with 4 more school courts at Chilliwack Secondary. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=104&backTo=%26dowhat%3DamenityView%26paID%3D26%26plID%3D192
- There is no City recreation centre inside this sub-area: the nearest are the Chilliwack Landing Leisure Centre at 9145 Corbould Street on the west side and the Cheam Leisure Centre at 45501 Market Way in Sardis. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2246
- The nearest library is the Chilliwack Library at 45860 First Avenue, in the downtown core west of this area. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations
- Route 57's January 4, 2026 move from Carleton Street to McNaught Road means older transit maps and listing copy naming Carleton Street stops are out of date for this side. https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/
- Strathcona Park is listed in the City's parks directory for this area with no street address, so a listing that names it as a landmark gives no distance a buyer can verify from the City page. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=104&backTo=%26dowhat%3DamenityView%26paID%3D26%26plID%3D192

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- There is no rapid transit in Chilliwack: Route 66 Fraser Valley Express is the interregional link, serving Chilliwack, Abbotsford, Langley and Burnaby with daily service. https://www.bctransit.com/plan-a-trip/interregional-routes/
- Route 71 Agassiz-Harrison is this side's own route, running from Downtown Chilliwack east along Yale Road to Rosedale, Agassiz and Harrison Hot Springs. https://www.bctransit.com/agassiz-harrison/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=71
- Route 53 Fairfield loops from Downtown Chilliwack via Menzies Street and Young Road, and it received additional Sunday-evening service in the January 4, 2026 service change. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=53 https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the dyke question first: on the Downtown Neighbourhood Plan description carried in the guide research, areas outside the City's dyking system flood at higher river stages, so whether a lot sits inside or outside the dyke line matters more than its distance from the river. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519 sets the construction elevation for new building, and the parcel's own Flood Construction Level is not published on a neighbourhood page — it is a per-lot City check. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) edges touch the larger lots on this side of the city: the City states agriculture accounts for 67% of the City's total land base, and ALR status for a specific parcel is a parcel-by-parcel check on the Agricultural Land Commission map. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- What the pages read do not tell a buyer: whether an existing east-side house was built to the current flood construction level — that is a building-file question for the City, not something the flood guide page answers for an existing home.
