# Ryder Lake Area Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: ryder-lake-guide (Ryder Lake; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Ryder Lake is an upland rural community inside the City of Chilliwack, sitting on Mount Thom east of Promontory — it is a city neighbourhood, not an electoral area, so City of Chilliwack bylaws and taxes apply. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/basic-html/page-26.html
- Its working streets are Elk View Road, Ryder Lake Road, Extrom Road, Forester Road, Churchill Parkway and Ross Road, reached from Highway 1 at Prest Road and Bailey Road; the City's trail list anchors Ross Road in Ryder Lake by name. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754.+&dowhat=locationView&plID=184
- The name on the map is a lake, but the access a buyer actually gets is a mountain: Mount Thom Park, the City park on the Mount Thom summit and slopes, is the public land in the community, and its trails — not a lakeshore — are what the City documents for Ryder Lake. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754.+&dowhat=locationView&plID=184
- Its community anchor is Ryder Lake Hall at 49265 Elk View Road, the hall the Ryder Lake community association rents out for local events. https://www.ryderlake.com/

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is rural estate acreages, and the City plans to keep it that way: the 2040 Official Community Plan (OCP) policy is to "maintain the current rural estate density of Ryder Lake" and "limit small acreage rural and suburban residential development to the current zoning designations and discourage rezoning for a higher density." https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/basic-html/page-26.html
- No City or School District No. 33 (Chilliwack) page read states a typical lot size, build decade or house style for Ryder Lake, so acreage size and era are per-lot title and BC Assessment checks, not a neighbourhood average. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- The one building pattern the City documents here is detached accessory dwellings: the City's residential form-and-character guidelines expressly exempt detached accessory dwelling units in the Ryder Lake neighbourhood from that development permit — an exemption Promontory and the other hillside areas do not get. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8156

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City-wide Chilliwack zoning, tax and general rules are in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note; this note keeps only the Ryder Lake-specific rules below. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- Slope rule: Ryder Lake is inside Development Permit Area 2 – Hillside and Upland Areas, which the City says "protects developments from hazardous hillside conditions and environmentally sensitive areas," and a geotechnical report from a qualified professional is required with each application. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- Second slope layer: Development Permit Area 5 – Hillside Development applies to all lands in Chilliwack with slopes of 20% or more, so a steep Ryder Lake lot can sit in both permit areas at once. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- Long-range rule: the 2040 OCP designates Ryder Lake a "medium to long term development reserve," preserving "this upland area for development prospect consideration beyond 2040" — rezoning hopes before that horizon run against the plan's own wording. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/basic-html/page-26.html
- Farm rule: the same OCP policy supports "appropriate agricultural activities on ALR parcels to maintain agricultural production" in Ryder Lake, so Agricultural Land Reserve parcels here are a working-farm designation, checked per parcel on Agricultural Land Commission mapping. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/basic-html/page-26.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there a school in Ryder Lake? **A:** No operating school sits in the community today — the small elementary school is a former school. School District No. 33's transportation procedure sets regular bus routes for the "Ryder Lake area" serving Vedder Elementary School, Mount Slesse Middle School and G.W. Graham Secondary School, all down in Sardis. District policy provides busing where the catchment school is beyond 3.0 kilometres for elementary students and 4.0 kilometres for middle and secondary students. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2023-03/611%20AP%20Transportation%20-%20Operating%20Instructions.pdf
- **Q:** Can I build or clear land on a sloped Ryder Lake lot without extra permits? **A:** Often not. Development Permit Area 2 covers the hillside and upland areas including Ryder Lake, and the City requires a geotechnical report with each development permit application; Development Permit Area 5 separately captures any land at 20% slope or more anywhere in the city. The older OCP text also required a permit for removing trees over 30 centimetres diameter and for installing a septic field within 61 metres of a lake's natural boundary — confirm the current wording with the Land Development Department before clearing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- **Q:** Will Ryder Lake be subdivided into city lots soon? **A:** The plan says the opposite. The 2040 OCP directs the city to maintain Ryder Lake's current rural estate density, discourage rezoning to higher density, and hold the upland as a development reserve for consideration beyond 2040, while directing designated hillside growth instead to serviced areas such as Promontory, Eastern Hillsides, Chilliwack Mountain and Little Mountain. A purchase priced on near-term subdivision is priced against the adopted plan. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/basic-html/page-26.html
- **Q:** Is there bus service to Ryder Lake? **A:** No Chilliwack Transit route in the BC Transit service-change notices read serves Ryder Lake itself; the system's rural reach is routes such as 55 Yarrow–Greendale and the summer-only 56 Cultus Lake. The one transit service the community does get is the School District No. 33 school bus network for the Ryder Lake area. Daily life here is car-based, via Elk View Road down to Prest Road and Highway 1. https://www.bctransit.com/summer-service-change-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2026-06-15/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Karver's Trail, named after Karver Morford, connects Ross Road in Ryder Lake with the Thom Creek Trail in Mount Thom Park — 1.3 kilometres with 170 metres of elevation gain, gravel and dirt, rated moderate by the City. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754.+&dowhat=locationView&plID=184
- Mount Thom Park is the community's park: a City park on the 484-metre Mount Thom summit and slopes above Ryder Lake, with trailheads on the Ryder Lake side including Churchill Parkway and Ross Road as well as from Promontory below. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754.+&dowhat=locationView&plID=184
- The creek name to know here is Thom Creek, which drains the Mount Thom slopes through the park's trail system — lots backing onto the park's ravines carry the Development Permit Area 2 environmental checks, not just slope checks. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- The road pattern is rural lanes, not a subdivision grid — and the OCP's growth map is why it stays that way: unlike Promontory below, Ryder Lake has no comprehensive development area plan and no municipal growth boundary drawn around it. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/basic-html/page-26.html
- Detached accessory dwelling units are treated differently here than anywhere else in Chilliwack: the City's form-and-character development permit guidelines list the Agricultural Land Reserve and the Ryder Lake neighbourhood as the two exemptions. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8156

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City-wide Chilliwack figures are in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note: the City's published 2026 Class 1 Residential municipal rate is 3.00943 per $1,000 of assessed value, with a total residential rate of 4.66727 per $1,000 plus a drainage charge of 0.12024 per $1,000 on land only — see that note for the breakdown. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2527/Tax%5FRates%5Ffor%5Fthe%5FWeb%2Dmou9gntw%5Fc%2Epdf
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in the City of Chilliwack, which includes Ryder Lake: the Province's taxable-areas list names the City of Chilliwack, so a Ryder Lake owner files the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- The Ryder Lake-specific cost check is servicing, not tax: whether a given Elk View Road or Ryder Lake Road lot is on a community water system or a private well and septic field is not stated in any City page read for this note — see NOT CONFIRMED — and a septic field's distance from the lake is itself a permit trigger under the hillside rules. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Driving is the only year-round option: the route down is Elk View Road and Ryder Lake Road to Bailey Road and Prest Road, meeting Highway 1 at Exit 123 (Prest Road) — the same corridor the Mount Thom trailhead directions use. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754.+&dowhat=locationView&plID=184
- The regional bus link is one valley away: BC Transit Route 66 Fraser Valley Express serves Chilliwack, Abbotsford, Langley and Burnaby (Lougheed Station) daily, reached by driving down to the Chilliwack exchanges. https://www.bctransit.com/plan-a-trip/interregional-routes/
- School transport is the exception to the no-bus rule: School District No. 33 runs regular school bus routes for the Ryder Lake area to Vedder Elementary, Mount Slesse Middle and G.W. Graham Secondary. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2023-03/611%20AP%20Transportation%20-%20Operating%20Instructions.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with Development Permit Area 2 mapping: the City's development permits page is the entry point for the hillside and upland layer covering Ryder Lake, and a geotechnical report is required with each application — the page states the requirement, not a lot-level stability conclusion. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- Add Development Permit Area 5: any Ryder Lake land at 20% slope or more is captured city-wide, so measure the building site's slope, not the driveway's. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- Wildfire interface is a live check, not a label: the hillside permit framework exists to protect development from hazardous conditions, and the form-and-character guidelines require hillside site grading to integrate with the slope and preserve natural features — confirm the current FireSmart or interface conditions with the City's Land Development Department for the specific lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8156
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve status per parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission mapping before assuming farm or non-farm use; the 2040 OCP expressly supports agricultural activity on Ryder Lake's ALR parcels. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/basic-html/page-26.html
- The City of Chilliwack subdivisions webpage's reference to a "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" is an open conflict with earlier verified research holding it was never adopted — do not rely on it as governing for any Ryder Lake subdivision question; see NOT CONFIRMED. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
