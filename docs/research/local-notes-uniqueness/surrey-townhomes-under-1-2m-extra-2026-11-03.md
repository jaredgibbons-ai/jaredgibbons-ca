## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A short-term rental in a Surrey townhome is owner-only: only the property owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot operate one, and a principal-residence whole townhouse is one of the permitted forms. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- Hosting needs a City business licence first, at $358 per year plus any applicable application fees; the provincial frame since May 1, 2024 also limits rentals to the host's principal residence plus one suite or accessory unit on the same property. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/surrey-has-new-short-term-rental-requirements

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Does the first-time home buyers' program help at $1.2 million? **A:** No, the band's top end is past its reach. The Province's program page states a full property transfer tax exemption needs a fair market value of $835,000 or less, effective April 1, 2024, exempting the tax on the first $500,000, and a partial exemption runs only to less than $860,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q:** What if the townhome is newly built? **A:** A new townhome can escape the tax entirely at the right price. The Province's newly built home exemption page states the full exemption threshold rose to $1,100,000 on April 1, 2024, the exemption phases out over the next $50,000, and it is completely eliminated at $1,150,000, so a new townhome between $1,150,000 and $1.2 million gets none. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Q:** Is there GST relief on a new townhome here? **A:** Yes, and this band is where it is strongest. The Canada Revenue Agency guide states first-time home buyers may be eligible, combined with the new housing rebate, for a rebate of 100 percent of the Goods and Services Tax (GST) on homes valued at or under $1 million, gradually reduced between $1 million and $1.5 million, with none at $1.5 million or more. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/rc4028/gst-hst-new-housing-rebate.html
- **Q:** Can I rent the townhome short-term while living elsewhere? **A:** No, Surrey ties the rental to the owner's own home. The City requires the host to be the property owner, requires that owner to reside at the property as their primary residence, and bars tenants from operating a short-term rental, so an investor-held townhome in this band cannot be run as one. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Q:** Which schools serve a townhome address in this band? **A:** The address decides, not the complex or the price. Surrey Schools (School District 36) maintains a School Catchment Locator page for looking up the catchment schools for a specific home address, and a buyer should run the exact townhome address through it before removing subjects on an offer. https://www.surreyschools.ca/page/78827/school-catchment-locator

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- A townhome strata cannot meter on its own: the City's water meter program states multi-family homes such as townhomes must go through the building permit process to switch to a water meter, and metered homes are then billed every four months on consumption. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/voluntary-water-meter-program
- A larger strata can be paid to meter: the program offers up to $1,500 in rebates for multi-family stratas with more than 15 units, for the first 30 strata properties switching by the end of 2026. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/voluntary-water-meter-program

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The band straddles the newly built ceiling: below $1,100,000 a qualifying new townhome pays no property transfer tax, between $1,100,000 and $1,150,000 the exemption is proportionally reduced, and at $1,150,000 it is gone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption/exemption-amounts
- A first-time buyer's GST saving tops out inside this band: the Department of Finance states the rebate saves up to $50,000, applies to agreements entered into on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031, and its bill received Royal Assent on March 12, 2026. https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2026/03/legislation-to-make-life-more-affordable-receives-royal-assent.html
- If the strata meters, 2026 metered rates are $1.4116 per cubic metre for water and $2.3593 for sewer, sewer volume set at 88 percent of water supplied; Surrey's tax rate itself is held in the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- A newly built exemption must be kept, not just claimed: the Province requires the buyer to move in within 92 days of registration at the Land Title Office and keep occupying the home as a principal residence for the rest of the first year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- The GST rebate has a contract-date window: the Canada Revenue Agency's registrant guide states it applies where the builder agreement was entered into on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031, with construction substantially completed before 2036, so an older presale contract in this band may fall outside it. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/rc4022/general-information-gst-hst-registrants.html
