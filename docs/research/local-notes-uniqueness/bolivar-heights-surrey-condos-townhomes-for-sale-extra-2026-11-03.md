# Bolivar Heights Condos and Townhomes — Extra Local Notes

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Bolivar Heights is a City Centre Plan district on the hill west of King George Boulevard, and it is the one Whalley neighbourhood where the City has pre-planned townhouses and low-rise apartments into an existing single-family area. https://engage.surrey.ca/city-centre-plan/news_feed/council-approves-land-use-designation-changes-in-bolivar-heights-and-green-timbers
- The landmarks that fix the area are Bolivar Park at 13591 Crestview Drive (vehicle access from 115 Avenue) and Invergarry Park at 11285 Surrey Road, which protects 37.5 hectares of Bon Accord Creek forest and ravine. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bolivar-park
- The City publishes no street boundary for the Bolivar Heights board sub-area; the working edges in City material are the City Centre Plan district, King George Boulevard to the east and the Bon Accord / Invergarry ravines to the west — stated as NOT CONFIRMED in the research file and repeated in NOT CONFIRMED below. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/city-centre-plan

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The existing attached stock is small, but the plan entitlement is the story: on July 11, 2022 Council approved City Centre Plan changes (Corporate Report R137; 2022) adding townhouses and low-rise apartments, plus multiplexes, to a district that was single-family houses. https://engage.surrey.ca/city-centre-plan/news_feed/council-approves-land-use-designation-changes-in-bolivar-heights-and-green-timbers
- The same approval adds a new urban mixed-use village with shops and services, and neighbourhood parks within a short walk of all residents — the commercial node future townhome buyers here are actually buying into. https://engage.surrey.ca/city-centre-plan/news_feed/council-approves-land-use-designation-changes-in-bolivar-heights-and-green-timbers
- The City Centre Plan itself was approved in January 2017 (Corporate Report R014) and establishes transit-oriented development around the SkyTrain stations east of the hill — Bolivar Heights is the plan's low-rise, ground-oriented district, not a tower district. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/city-centre-plan
- Schools inside or serving the hill are James Ardiel Elementary at 13751 112 Avenue and Surrey Traditional at 13875 113 Avenue, with Kwantlen Park Secondary at 10441 132 Street just south of the area. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A Bolivar Heights lot's future is set by two documents: its current zone under Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000, and its City Centre Plan designation — the 2022 designation change does not itself rezone a lot, so a townhome project still needs its own application. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/city-centre-plan
- Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) adopted July 8, 2024 permits three to four homes on most Surrey residential lots without rezoning, and up to six near frequent bus stops — on this hill that is the multiplex route, separate from the plan's townhouse and low-rise apartment designations. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Lots backing onto Bon Accord Creek in Invergarry Park carry streamside protection under Part 7 (Streamside Protection) of Zoning By-law No. 12000 — setbacks and no-build covenants can remove part of a ravine lot from the buildable area. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/invergarry-park
- The finer-grained, better-connected road network approved in the 2022 update means some existing streets in the district are planned to be extended or connected as redevelopment proceeds — check the plan's road network map against a specific lot. https://engage.surrey.ca/city-centre-plan/news_feed/council-approves-land-use-designation-changes-in-bolivar-heights-and-green-timbers

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Bolivar Heights actually getting townhouses, or is that just a plan? **A:** It is an approved plan, not yet a built neighbourhood. Council approved the land use changes on July 11, 2022, introducing townhouses and low-rise apartments alongside houses and multiplexes, plus a mixed-use village. Each project still needs rezoning and permits under Zoning By-law No. 12000, so today's attached listings are few and most of the change is still in the application stage. https://engage.surrey.ca/city-centre-plan/news_feed/council-approves-land-use-designation-changes-in-bolivar-heights-and-green-timbers
- **Q:** How close is a Bolivar Heights townhome to SkyTrain? **A:** The stations are east of the hill in City Centre, not in Bolivar Heights itself. The City Centre Plan builds transit-oriented development around those stations, and TransLink route 373 links the neighbourhood — it runs from Surrey Central Station via Gateway Station, 112 Avenue, 136 Street and Surrey Road to Guildford. Travel time to a station is UNCONFIRMED; measure it from the specific address. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt373.pdf
- **Q:** Does the ravine behind a Bolivar Heights home limit what I can build? **A:** Yes, on creek lots. Invergarry Park protects 37.5 hectares of natural forest and riparian Bon Accord Creek habitat, and streamside lots fall under Part 7 streamside protection in the zoning bylaw, which can require setbacks and a registered no-build covenant. A ravine-edge townhome or lot needs the streamside map checked before a buyer prices in an addition or a suite. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/invergarry-park
- **Q:** Which library and hospital serve this side of Whalley? **A:** There is no library branch in Bolivar Heights; the City Centre Branch at 10350 University Drive is the Whalley branch. Surrey Memorial Hospital at 13750 96 Avenue is the area hospital, and the Surrey Urgent and Primary Care Centre (UPCC) at 9639 137A Street is in Whalley itself. https://surreylibraries.ca/locations/city-centre-branch

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Bolivar Park's parking is reached from 115 Avenue even though the park address is 13591 Crestview Drive, and the park holds baseball fields, a field house and a two-acre fenced dog off-leash area — noise and parking on game days concentrate on the Crestview Drive side. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bolivar-park
- Invergarry Mountain Bike Park is entered from Wallace Drive and Surrey Road inside Invergarry Park — a draw for some buyers and a weekend-traffic fact for homes on Surrey Road. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/invergarry-park
- Route 373 is the neighbourhood's cross-town link: Surrey Central Station to Guildford Recreation Centre via 112 Avenue, 136 Street, 113 Avenue, Surrey Road, Wallace Drive and 116A Avenue — the streets it uses are the streets townhome buyers will also drive. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt373.pdf
- The City Centre Branch library at 10350 University Drive sits 150 metres from Surrey Central Station and is served by the R1 RapidBus and routes 314, 316, 320, 321, 323, 324, 325, 326, 329, 335, 337, 371, 373 and 501–509 series buses — the practical transit hub for the hill. https://surreylibraries.ca/locations/city-centre-branch
- Whalley holds Surrey's first Urgent and Primary Care Centre, opened November 2018, with a second centre added in 2020 — Fraser Health's own reporting, and the walk-in option Bolivar Heights buyers actually use. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2020/May/second-urgent-and-primary-care-centre-opens-in-surrey

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Bolivar Heights pays the City of Surrey rate: the total 2026 residential (Class 1) rate is $3.42731 per $1,000 of assessed value, with the full breakdown held in the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- A strata unit here is billed City utilities through the strata; a house or duplex with a legal suite adds the 2026 suite levies of $454.00 water and $821.71 sewer, plus $172.04 waste, on the owner's utility account. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies in Surrey, which the Province lists as a designated taxable area — a vacant Bolivar Heights unit still requires the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- No SkyTrain station sits in Bolivar Heights: Gateway and Surrey Central stations on the Expo Line, east of King George Boulevard, are the rail access, and the City Centre Plan's transit-oriented districts are built around them. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/city-centre-plan
- TransLink route 373 (Guildford / Surrey Central Station) crosses the neighbourhood on 112 Avenue, Surrey Road and Wallace Drive — the only through bus route confirmed on an operator timetable for these streets. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt373.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Slope and ravine are the local hazards: homes on the Bon Accord and Invergarry ravine edges need the hazard-lands and streamside layers checked in the City of Surrey Mapping Online System (COSMOS) for the exact lot before an offer. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/invergarry-park
- Surrey's Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's north edge with dikes and four pump stations; Bolivar Heights sits on the hill above that floodplain, but a specific low lot near 108 Avenue still needs its own flood-layer check — the City flood page does not settle an individual lot. http://www.surrey.ca/city-services/3655.aspx
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status per property on the Agricultural Land Commission map if a lot touches the district's rural edge — not assumed for any Bolivar Heights address in this note. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- For any strata purchase, the depreciation report and Form B Information Certificate state the building's planned work — provincial law now requires stratas of five or more lots to obtain a depreciation report every five years. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
