## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Sardis sites sit on the Vedder River alluvial fan, covered by an OCP development permit area, so drainage and flood design are reviewed. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1372
- Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519: 75-metre setback from the Vedder River's natural boundary, plus fan flood construction levels. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
Read: 2026-11-03

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Are Garrison townhomes zoned differently? **A:** Yes. Garrison Crossing uses one comprehensive zone, CD-9, instead of R4. Townhouses there must meet R4 standards — 1,200-square-metre site, 30-metre width — while rowhouses have a 170-square-metre minimum lot and detached houses 240. The mix was set when the neighbourhood was zoned, not lot by lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q:** What is happening on Keith Wilson Road? **A:** A lot at 44877 Keith Wilson Road is being rezoned from R1-A to R4 (Low Density Multi-Unit Residential) for a future townhouse development, under application RZ001689, applicant Alex Mochtchenkov, per the City's own hearing notice; the hearing was December 2, 2025. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/CPL/260306/CPLN260306_A36.pdf
- **Q:** Which schools does a Sardis townhome feed? **A:** It depends on the side of Sardis. The district locator for 45170 South Sumas Road returned Tyson Elementary, Vedder Middle and Sardis Secondary; a Promontory complex falls toward Promontory Heights Elementary and G.W. Graham Secondary (sample: 46200 Stoneview Drive). Run the civic address. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q:** Do Sardis townhomes face wildlife rules? **A:** Promontory ones do. The City designates hillside neighbourhoods including Promontory as Wildlife Issue Areas under the Solid Waste Management Bylaw: wildlife-resistant carts, nothing at the curb before 5:00 a.m., removal by 10:00 p.m. Stevenson and Tyson Road complexes are outside the designated areas. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2936
Read: 2026-11-03
