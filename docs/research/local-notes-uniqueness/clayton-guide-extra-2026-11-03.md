# Clayton Heights Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: clayton-guide (Clayton Heights, Surrey; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Clayton Heights covers about 809 hectares (2,000 acres), from Fraser Highway in the south to the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) in the north and west, bordered by the Township of Langley in the east. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/clayton-general-land-use-plan
- East Clayton, the first urban neighbourhood developed in Clayton Heights, sits north of Fraser Highway along the Langley border. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton
- West Clayton is the second urban neighbourhood, adjacent to East Clayton and the ALR. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/west-clayton-ncp
- Clayton is part of Cloverdale in City planning; its land-use plans sit under Cloverdale land-use plans. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Clayton was planned from 1999 as a compact community where amenities and services are within walking distance of homes. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/clayton-general-land-use-plan
- East Clayton covers about 314 hectares (776 acres) across four plan areas, including a transit-oriented area on Fraser Highway. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton
- West Clayton covers about 288 hectares (713 acres) north of Fraser Highway, with a mixed-use centre planned at a future rapid transit station at 184 Street and Fraser Highway. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/west-clayton-ncp

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The East Clayton and West Clayton Neighbourhood Concept Plan (NCP) designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) update; the OCP now guides development. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton
- The ALR borders Clayton on the north and west, so lots on those edges adjoin protected farm land. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/clayton-general-land-use-plan
- A land-use plan for North Clayton has not yet been initiated, per the City. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/clayton-general-land-use-plan

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is Clayton the same as Cloverdale? **A:** Clayton Heights is a newer community inside Cloverdale. The City files its plans under Cloverdale land-use plans, and the 1999 Clayton General Land Use Plan set the vision for Clayton Heights itself, with East Clayton approved in 2003 as the first phase and West Clayton following in 2015. The older Cloverdale town centre on 176 Street is south of Fraser Highway, outside Clayton. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/west-clayton-ncp
**Q:** Will SkyTrain serve Clayton? **A:** Yes, Clayton Station is one of eight new stations on the Surrey-Langley SkyTrain extension, located in east Clayton at 189 Street and Fraser Highway. The project runs 16 kilometres from King George Station to Langley City Centre, major construction started in November 2024, and the anticipated in-service date is late 2029. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/04/SLS_Monthly-Status-Report_March_2026.pdf
**Q:** Which secondary schools serve Clayton? **A:** The Ministry list for Surrey School District 36 names Clayton Heights Secondary at 7003 188 Street, inside Clayton itself. The district also lists École Salish Secondary on an extended-day schedule to manage enrolment in the area. Catchment depends on the exact address, so confirm with the district School Locator before buying for a specific school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
**Q:** Why do Clayton streets use lanes and porches? **A:** East Clayton was planned as a sustainable, compact neighbourhood under the 1999 General Land Use Plan, with the Neighbourhood Concept Plan approach used for each phase. That planning produced the lane-served, porch-front form that distinguishes East Clayton from older Cloverdale subdivisions south of Fraser Highway, which were not built under this plan. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Clayton plan area was divided using elementary school catchments, with boundaries on major roads so students would not need to cross them walking to school. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/RPT_2010_R080.pdf
- Hillcrest-184 Street Station, west of Clayton Station, also serves the Clayton edge at 184 Street and Fraser Highway. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/04/SLS_Monthly-Status-Report_March_2026.pdf
- The East Clayton Transit Oriented Area plan guides development that integrates with future rapid transit on Fraser Highway. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton
- 72 Avenue widening from 188 Street to 196 Street was anticipated complete in fall 2026, on Clayton's north route. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/capital-projects/72-avenue-improvements-extensions?check_logged_in=1
- The future 72 Avenue extension from 152 Street to Highway 15 will cross south of Clayton, with bridges over Bear Creek and the Serpentine River. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/capital-projects/72-avenue-improvements-extensions?check_logged_in=1
