## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Tenure first: a home on a rented pad is not land ownership. The buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad (the manufactured home site) from the park landlord under a written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement, governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- Settled fact, restated exactly: the allowable pad-rent increase is 2.3% plus the proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027. No pad-rent dollar figure is published as fact anywhere, and none is stated in this note. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- What the rent covers is set park by park in the written agreement, not by a provincial list: the Province's rent-increase page treats services included in the rent as the agreement's own terms, and an additional increase can only be sought for costs related to services included in the rent. Ask for the agreement, not a verbal list of inclusions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Assignment on resale, MHPTA section 28: a tenant may assign the site tenancy only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an order of the director, or where the agreement authorizes it; before asking, the seller must give the buyer written copies of the park rules and the relevant parts of the tenancy agreement, the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent, and on assignment the pad rent and the scheduled date of the next increase stay the same. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- Disputes go to the Residential Tenancy Branch (RTB) by dispute resolution: a tenant who is charged above the legal limit does not have to pay the excess, can deduct an overpayment from future rent with a written explanation, or can apply for dispute resolution for an order that the landlord comply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- A park committee and park rules operate under the MHPTA framework the Province publishes for manufactured home parks; the rules for the specific Langley park are part of the documents the seller must hand the buyer before assignment consent is sought. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- Township permit layer: placing a home in a Township park needs a building permit, the Park Manager's acceptance and signature on the building plans, and compliance with the Township's Mobile Home Park Bylaw, Building Bylaw and, in some parks, development permits. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/building-development/resources/building/bulletins-forms-resources/Document-Feed-Building-Guidelines/PLI---Guide-Mobile-Home-Park.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** How much is pad rent in Langley? **A:** No official source publishes a Langley pad rent, average or range, so no dollar figure is stated here. The rent is set in the specific park's written site tenancy agreement. What the Province does publish is how that rent may change: 2.3% plus the proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027, once every 12 months on three full months' notice. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes

**Q:** Does my pad rent jump to a market rent when I buy? **A:** No, not on an assignment. Under section 28 of the MHPTA the tenancy is permanently transferred to you, the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, and the scheduled date of a later increase does not reset. That is why the assignment consent, not a new agreement at a new rent, is the document to secure before completion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf

**Q:** What is the proportional amount in plain terms? **A:** It is the park's share-out of two cost changes: the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park. Only levies and regulated fees for the park's common property count, including school taxes, hospital levies and garbage fees on a tax notice, and electricity, gas, water, telephone, cable and internet charges. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes

**Q:** What happens if my Langley park closes for redevelopment? **A:** The landlord must first have all necessary government permits and approvals, then give a 12-month notice covering a full year and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date. You may leave early on at least 10 days' written notice, dispute the notice within 15 days, and in set circumstances claim added compensation of the home's assessed value minus the $20,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Two different RTB forms do two different jobs: the Notice of Standard Rent Increase, Manufactured Home Site (Form RTB-11a), covers the standard increase, and a landlord seeking an increase above the limit applies on the Application for Additional Rental Increase Form (RTB Form 52), which cannot be filed online. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- A landlord cannot round up the increase, cannot carry forward an unused part of a past year's limit, and an increase given on short notice simply takes effect on the earliest date that would comply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- Dispute deadlines are short and different: 5 days for a 10-day notice for non-payment, 10 days for a one-month notice, and 15 days for a 12-month park-conversion notice, each counted from receiving the notice. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- If the landlord does not take steps toward the stated conversion within a reasonable period after a 12-month notice, the landlord must pay the greater of $5,000 or 12 months' rent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- A Township home must meet CSA (Canadian Standards Association) A277 or CAN/CSA Z240 MH Series standards, and in older parks a setback that cannot be met can require a Board of Variance application before a permit issues. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/building-development/resources/building/bulletins-forms-resources/Document-Feed-Building-Guidelines/PLI---Guide-Mobile-Home-Park.pdf
- The sale itself is only effective once registered with the Manufactured Home Registry, through a lawyer or notary filing the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership forms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Property tax on the home: the Province states a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value does not include the value of the land where the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- Municipal rates for context, 2026: the City of Langley's total residential rate is 4.2067 per $1,000 of taxable value on its 2026 Property Tax Rates sheet. The Township of Langley's 2026 residential rate per $1,000 was not read on a Township page in this pass and is not stated here. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/2026taxrates
- Speculation and Vacancy Tax: both the City of Langley and the Township of Langley are on the Province's list of taxable areas in the Metro Vancouver Regional District, as is the City of Maple Ridge; whether the tax applies to a specific park home depends on the owner's declaration and exemptions, not on the pad. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- Property Transfer Tax (PTT) on a pad-home resale: UNCONFIRMED. Earlier programs left the PTT treatment of a manufactured home sold on a rented pad unsettled, and no provincial page read in this pass settles it, so no PTT position is stated here.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Verify the home, not the land: search the Manufactured Home Registry for the registered owner and the home's record, because the purchase is a chattel transfer registered there, not a land title transfer. The Registry record does not state the pad rent or the park's zoning. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- Verify the park's status in the municipality the park sits in: Township parks are checked against the Township's zoning (MH-1 in the Booth plan area) and its Mobile Home Park Bylaw permit route; City of Langley parks are checked against the City's Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300, whose park zone section was not opened in this pass. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Neighbourhood-Plan-Bylaws/Booth-Neighbourhood-Plan-(No.-6008).pdf
- Moving the home out is its own permit: a transport permit comes from the Manufactured Home Registry, a tax certificate for transport is required (with an exception for a move to a different pad in the same park), and the permit expires 30 days after issue. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- Check the CSA label: a home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal, and a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
