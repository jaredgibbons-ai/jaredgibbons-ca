# Langley Foreclosures and Court-Ordered Sales — Extra Local Notes
Page: langley-foreclosures-court-ordered-sales (Langley; foreclosures and court-ordered sales).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- "Langley" on this page means two separate municipalities — the City of Langley and the Township of Langley — and each runs its own tax sale, its own tax roll and its own collector; the generic British Columbia court-ordered-sale process is treated in full in the missionforeclosuresandcourtsales note — see that note.
- The Township of Langley states it is required by the Local Government Act to sell at its annual tax sale every property whose taxes have not been paid for three years. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/tax-sale.aspx
- Which single Supreme Court registry a Langley foreclosure petition is assigned to could not be verified on an official British Columbia (BC) Courts source — see NOT CONFIRMED. The Province's courthouse list names the New Westminster Law Courts, Begbie Square, 651 Carnarvon Street, New Westminster, as a Supreme Court location serving the region. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/justice/courthouse-services/courthouse-locations?keyword=court&keyword=registries

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock this topic touches spans both Langleys: City of Langley strata condos and townhomes around the Langley City core, and Township detached houses, acreages and farm parcels — foreclosure follows the registered mortgage, not the housing type. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- Township tax-sale lists show the range directly: the Township's 2026 public notice lists properties by street address, parcel identifier (PID) and legal description across rural and urban Township lots, including strata-plan lots. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/public-notices/public-notices-2026/2026-09-14-Public-Notice.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A BC foreclosure must be started by petition under Supreme Court Civil Rule 21-7, with every person whose interest is to be extinguished made a respondent; the Langley-specific work is in the municipal tax-sale rules below, not in a different court process. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- The Township sells tax-sale properties "as is" without warranty or guarantee, and states it makes no representation about the condition or quality of a property sold. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/tax-sale.aspx
- The Township's upset price — the floor bid — equals all outstanding taxes, penalties, interest, 5% tax sale costs, and Land Title Office fees. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/tax-sale.aspx
- A petitioner who registers a certificate of pending litigation (CPL) against a Langley title binds anyone who later registers an interest in that property. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** If a property is in "Langley," which tax sale do I watch? **A:** The one run by the municipality the property actually sits in. The City of Langley and the Township of Langley are separate municipalities with separate tax rolls; the Township holds its auction at its Civic Facility, 20338 – 65 Avenue, and a City property never appears on the Township's list, or the reverse. Check the municipal address, not the mailing city name. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/tax-sale.aspx
- **Q:** When is the Township of Langley tax sale and how do I bid? **A:** The Township holds it as a public auction on the last Monday in September at 10 a.m. — September 28 in 2026 — in Council Chambers at the Civic Facility, 20338 – 65 Avenue. Bidders pre-register that morning, between 9:15 and 9:50 a.m. for the 2026 sale, and bidding is accepted on all listed properties. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/tax-sale.aspx https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/public-notices/public-notices-2026/2026-09-14-Public-Notice.pdf
- **Q:** Can the owner get a Township tax-sale property back? **A:** Yes — the owner has one year after the sale to redeem it. During that year the purchaser holds a sale that the owner can unwind by paying what the Local Government Act requires, which is why a tax-sale purchase is not the same as a completed court-ordered sale with court-approved title. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/tax-sale.aspx
- **Q:** What if my delinquent taxes are in the Township — is there a deadline before the auction? **A:** Yes. The Township states delinquent taxes must be paid by August 31 of the calendar year to keep the property out of that year's tax sale process, ahead of the late-September auction date. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/tax-sale.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Township must advertise its tax sale in a local paper not less than 3 and not more than 10 days before the sale, so the confirmed property list appears only shortly before auction day. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/tax-sale.aspx
- The Township's sale is held under Part 16 of the Local Government Act and Section 254 of the Community Charter — the 2026 notice is issued under both. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/public-notices/public-notices-2026/2026-09-14-Public-Notice.pdf
- A purchase at a BC municipal tax sale is subject to property transfer tax on the fair market value of the property — the Township states this in its own tax-sale material. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/tax-sale.aspx
- Both the City of Langley and the Township of Langley are designated taxable areas for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so an empty court-bought home in either municipality still needs the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- A court-ordered sale runs through an application in the foreclosure file: the Supreme Court fee schedule charges $80 to file a notice of application, plus $7 per package filed electronically through Court Services Online. https://free.bcpublications.ca/civix/document/id/roc/roc/168_2009_06

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Court filing fees are provincial: $80 to file a notice of application in a foreclosure proceeding and $7 per document package sent through Court Services Online, under Appendix C, Schedule 1 of the Supreme Court Civil Rules. https://free.bcpublications.ca/civix/document/id/roc/roc/168_2009_06
- The Township's tax-sale costs are built into its upset price: outstanding taxes, penalties, interest, 5% tax sale costs and Land Title Office fees. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/tax-sale.aspx
- Costs in an uncontested Rule 21-7 foreclosure are assessed under Scale A, the lowest scale in the rules' costs appendix. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/roc/roc/168_2009_05

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Confirm the municipality first: a legal description in the Township's notice is stated in New Westminster District terms (for example "LT 1 SEC 2 TWP 7 NWD"), and searching the wrong municipality's tax-sale list is the most basic Langley error. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/public-notices/public-notices-2026/2026-09-14-Public-Notice.pdf
- Township tax-sale properties are sold "as is" with no representation about condition or quality — inspection and title diligence happen before the auction, not after. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/tax-sale.aspx
- In a court-ordered sale, the amount due and the redemption period are set by the court in the Rule 21-7 proceeding; neither is stated on any municipal page, so the court file is the diligence source. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
