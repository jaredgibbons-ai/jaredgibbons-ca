## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Zoning Bylaw, 2018, No. 2937 puts detached homes in Single Detached Residential (RS) zones; check a lot on ViewPort. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH), Bylaw No. 3460 (2024): four units on lots 280–4,050 square metres, six within 400 metres of frequent bus stops; title building schemes override SSMUH. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- Secondary suite: maximum 90 square metres, 40 percent of floor space, never stratified; allowed since 2004. https://media-002-ca.cdn.govstack.com/portmoody-014-ca/media/rdtbhokg/a_guide_to_secondary_suites.pdf
- Small-lot subdivision (RS1-S, 9 metres, 325 square metres) applies only in Moody Centre, Seaview, Pleasantside. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary serves Ravine Drive? **A:** Heritage Mountain Elementary at 125 Ravine Drive is the named Kindergarten to Grade 5 school here, but School District No. 43 (SD43) catchment follows the permanent home address, so please confirm the exact address in the SD43 School Locator first. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** Can I add a coach house? **A:** Yes, if the lot meets the SSMUH RS1 rules: a laneway, coach house or accessory dwelling unit may be built with no lane access required, each suite counts toward the lot total, and only one suite is allowed per primary unit. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- **Q:** Does a creek lot change plans? **A:** Yes. A Qualified Environmental Professional must assess the watercourse, identify top of bank and apply Zoning Bylaw section 5.4 riparian setbacks; Development Permit Area 4 (Environmentally Sensitive Areas) and Area 5 (Hazardous Lands and Steep Slopes) also apply on creek lots. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- **Q:** Is there a SkyTrain station here? **A:** No station is in Heritage Mountain. Buses 183 and 184 connect to Inlet Centre and Moody Centre stations on the Millennium Line; route 184 runs via Noons Creek Drive and Panorama Drive to Lansdowne Drive, so buyers plan a bus leg. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt184.pdf
- **Q:** Can I remove a large tree? **A:** Not freely on a development site: a tree removal permit and arborist report are required, Tree Protection Bylaw, 2026, No. 3531 regulates cutting on private and public property, and clearing March 1 to August 31 may require a nesting survey. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/environmental-protection-and-requirements/
