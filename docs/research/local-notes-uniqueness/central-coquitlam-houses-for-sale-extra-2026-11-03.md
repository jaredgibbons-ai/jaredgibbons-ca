# Central Coquitlam Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: central-coquitlam-houses-for-sale (Central Coquitlam; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Central Coquitlam is the established central part of the City of Coquitlam, around Como Lake Avenue, Austin Avenue and the City Centre / Pinetree Way edge — it is a board sub-area name, and no City neighbourhood plan boundary for "Central Coquitlam" is stated on a City page read for this note. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- The main roads through the area are Como Lake Avenue, Austin Avenue, Pinetree Way and Guildford Way; Coquitlam West and Southwest Coquitlam lie to the west, City Centre to the north-east, Maillardville to the south and Port Moody beyond the city's west side. https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1838

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses here are freehold detached homes on the established central streets, in the City's small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3 under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996, with strata apartment buildings clustered toward the Pinetree Way and City Centre station edge rather than on the house streets. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- Subdivision minimums under that bylaw are 650 square metres with 18.5 metres of width in R-1 and R-3, and 465 square metres with 12.5 metres of width in R-2; no official source read states a typical build decade or house style for the sub-area, so none is claimed. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Central Coquitlam house? **A:** School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) assigns schools by exact address. The district locator result verified in the research for 1169 Pinetree Way is Glen Elementary, École Maple Creek Middle and Pinetree Secondary, and the district also names Central Elementary School, Hillcrest Middle School and École Dr. Charles Best Secondary School for the central area — a different street can feed a different set, so run the house address in the locator before writing an offer. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** Can I add a suite or a carriage house to a house here? **A:** The City's small-scale multi-unit housing rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 square metres or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 square metres and under 4,050 square metres, including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes, where zoning and municipal services allow. Lots not connected to municipal water or sewer fall outside the rules — full text is in the chineside-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Q:** How close is SkyTrain to the house streets? **A:** The stations are at the area's east edge, not on the interior streets. Coquitlam Central, Lincoln and Lafarge Lake-Douglas stations on the Millennium Line Evergreen Extension serve the City Centre and Pinetree Way edge of the sub-area, and Coquitlam Central also has West Coast Express commuter rail service. Most Central Coquitlam houses reach a station by bus or car, so check the route from the specific address. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/station/coquitlam-central/schedule
- **Q:** Is there a neighbourhood plan for Central Coquitlam? **A:** No plan carries the Central Coquitlam name. The Interim Official Community Plan's Schedule J lists neighbourhood plans for Austin Heights, Burquitlam-Lougheed, City Centre, Hyde Creek, Maillardville, Partington Creek, Smiling Creek and Waterfront Village Centre — the City Centre plan and the Transit-Oriented Areas framework are what touch this area's east edge, and the interior house streets are governed by the city-wide plan and Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17458
- **Q:** What library and recreation serve the area? **A:** Coquitlam Public Library's City Centre Branch is at 1169 Pinetree Way, at the area's east edge, and the Poirier Branch at 575 Poirier Street serves the west side of the city; the Library Link mobile service also runs. City Centre Aquatic Complex and Evergreen Cultural Centre are in the City Centre area at the sub-area's east edge. https://www.coqlibrary.ca/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Central Coquitlam is a three-tier school district area: School District No. 43 runs elementary, middle and secondary schools, so a house move can change three school assignments at once, not one. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- Town Centre Park and Como Lake Park are the City park names in City records for this part of Coquitlam; their street addresses were not confirmed in the sources read for this note. https://www.coqlibrary.ca/
- The nearest hospital is Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody, on the Port Moody–Coquitlam border — Central Coquitlam has no hospital of its own. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- Coquitlam's Voluntary Residential Water Metering Program bills metered homes every four months instead of the annual flat rate, so a house's water billing cycle depends on whether the owner opted in. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17426/2026-Budget-Highlights

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check creek and ravine setbacks before assuming a lot can be rebuilt or subdivided: the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers land within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- Check the Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 section 519 floodplain setbacks anyway: no building within 30 metres of the natural boundary of the Brunette, Coquitlam, Fraser or Pitt rivers, or within 15 metres of another qualifying watercourse, and no building below the flood construction level in designated floodplain. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1170/Part-5-General-Regulations-PDF
- A Fraser River floodplain designation for Central Coquitlam's residential streets was not confirmed in the sources read — the City states dikes and pump stations protect low-lying properties elsewhere in Coquitlam, which does not settle any individual lot here. https://www.coquitlam.ca/224/Floodplain-Dikes
