# Abbotsford West Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: abbotsford-west (Abbotsford West incl. Clearbrook; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Abbotsford West is anchored by Clearbrook, whose name became official in 1948; the area was earlier called Sandy Flats or West Abbotsford. https://clearbrook.abbyschools.ca/school-name-history-8
- Clearbrook Road is the spine, named because it led from town down to Clearbrook, Washington, along a route first used by the Stó:lō people. https://clearbrook.abbyschools.ca/school-name-history-8
- The City's Primary Transit Corridor runs along South Fraser Way between Clearbrook and McCallum Road, putting Clearbrook at the corridor's west end. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan
- The City Centre Neighbourhood Plan (Official Community Plan Bylaw 2600, Schedule C) covers the Clearbrook-side commercial core: Sevenoaks, West Oaks and Clearbrook Town Square. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2016b2600schedulec

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The west side mixes older Clearbrook detached housing with City Centre apartment and townhouse stock around Mill Lake and South Fraser Way. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2016b2600schedulec
- Laurel on Mill Lake, by Heinrichs Developments, is 42 townhomes and apartments at 33160 Plaxton Crescent on the Mill Lake shore, and the developer states the homes are finished. https://heinrichsdevelopments.com/pages/laurel-on-mill-lake-by-heinrichs-developments
- The Abbotsforward 2050 OCP adds townhouse and apartment areas near mixed-use centres and along major roads, the policy direction for the west side's next attached housing. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Abbotsforward 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) is approved and being implemented, with more flexible Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing options, including on west-side detached lots. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Which elementary school serves Clearbrook? **A:** École Clearbrook Elementary at 3614 Clearbrook Road, Kindergarten to Grade 5, a dual-track French Immersion school since 1985. School District 34 (SD34) enrols by home-address catchment under Policy 18 and Administrative Procedure 336, and out-of-catchment enrolment needs spare capacity, so confirm the exact address with the district. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/abbotsford https://uppersumas.abbyschools.ca/administrative-procedures/AP336
**Q:** Where do Clearbrook children go after elementary school? **A:** Colleen and Gordie Howe Middle School at 3174 Clearbrook Road, Grades 6 to 8, sits on Clearbrook Road itself. SD34 elementary schools run to Grade 5 and its middle schools cover Grades 6 to 8, so a Clearbrook child changes schools twice before secondary. The catchment for a specific address is the district's call. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/abbotsford
**Q:** Is there a real transit exchange on the west side? **A:** Yes — the Highstreet Transit Exchange opened for bus service on September 6, 2026, on Highstreet Access Road between Mt. Lehman Road and Cardinal Avenue. It opened with five bus bays serving local and regional routes including the Fraser Valley Express, with a sixth bay still being completed. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/new-transit-exchange-opening-near-highstreet-shopping-centre
**Q:** What is happening on Highway 1 at the west end? **A:** The Fraser Valley Highway 1 Corridor Improvement Program is widening the highway through Abbotsford in phases. The Province's September 2026 fact sheet lists 264th Street to Mt. Lehman Road as under construction, completion anticipated 2029, and Mt. Lehman Road to Highway 11 under construction, completion anticipated 2031. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/transportation-infrastructure-projects/highway-1-fraser-valley/documents/2026-09-22-september-fraser-valley-hwy1-fact-sheet.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Abbotsford's 2026 rates are set by Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026, per $1,000 of taxable value — a city-wide fact carried here because the Abbotsford houses page note is later in program order. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf
- Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a further 5% on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- The City of Abbotsford is a designated Speculation and Vacancy Tax area, so west-side residential owners file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Routes 1, 2 and 66 Fraser Valley Express serve the Highstreet exchange, linking the west side across Abbotsford and to Langley and Chilliwack. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/projects-go/highstreet-transit-exchange
- The 66 runs on Highway 1 between Lougheed Station, Carvolth Exchange, Highstreet and McCallum Park and Ride, then Chilliwack. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/692/890/chw_map_66.pdf
- The Central Fraser Valley Transit System serves Abbotsford and Mission with connections to Aldergrove, Chilliwack, Langley and the West Coast Express. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/transportation-roads/transit

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check Matsqui Prairie-edge lots individually: the City maintains the rivers, creeks, ditches and dykes in Matsqui and Sumas Prairies, and private structures in those watercourses need City approval. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coaother/p00111
- Under Watercourse Management Policy C010-10, ditches not on Map D-660 are kept clean by the adjacent owner — a cost a prairie-edge buyer inherits. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coaother/c01010
- Highway 1 construction runs along the area's south edge to 2031 on the provincial timeline; check DriveBC patterns before subject removal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/transportation-infrastructure-projects/highway-1-fraser-valley/documents/2026-09-22-september-fraser-valley-hwy1-fact-sheet.pdf
