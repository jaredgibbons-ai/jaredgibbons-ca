# Tsawwassen Central Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: tsawwassen-central (Tsawwassen Central, City of Delta; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Tsawwassen Central is the ground around the junction of 56 Street and 12 Avenue in Tsawwassen, City of Delta, and the City publishes no boundary for the name. It is a Paragon sub-area name; the planning frame is the Tsawwassen Urban Centre in the Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) (https://delta.civicweb.net/document/215646/).
- The centre's landmark site is Tsawwassen Town Centre Mall, whose City-listed addresses are 1219 56 Street, 5499 12 Avenue and 1236 55 Street (https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/).
- Tsawwassen Library is at 1321A 56 St on the mall site, and South Delta Recreation Centre is at 1720 56 St beside South Delta Exchange (http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/south-delta-recreation-centre).
- Cliff Drive is north-west, Tsawwassen East and Pebble Hill are south-east, and Ladner is north via 56 Street and Highway 17A — Tsawwassen-shared facts are written in full in the cliff-drive note; this note covers the centre itself.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The centre's housing is freehold detached and duplex houses under the RS and RD zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, on in-town lots — subdivision minimums run from RS7 at 335 m² and RS6 at 390 m² up to RD1 at 836 m² (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf).
- Strata condominium buildings stand in the town centre beside the mall, but the building names carried in earlier program files came from a banned source and are not re-cited here — see NOT CONFIRMED (https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/).
- South of the centre, the housing is newer: Southlands Tsawwassen's homes are all freehold, in a mix the developer lists as condo flats, townhomes, non-strata rowhomes, duplexes, courtyard cottages and detached estate cottages — full project entry in §6 (https://discoversouthlands.ca/faq/).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is Tsawwassen Central an official City neighbourhood?
**A:** No — it is a Paragon sub-area name, and the City publishes no boundary for it. The official planning frame is Tsawwassen and the Tsawwassen Urban Centre under the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). In practice the name means the streets around 56 Street and 12 Avenue and the Town Centre Mall site (https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf).

**Q:** Which schools serve a home in the centre?
**A:** Catchments are address-based in School District 37, elementary Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary Grades 8 to 12. For the sample address 1299 56 St, the locator result is Cliff Drive Elementary, 5025 12 Ave, and South Delta Secondary, 750 53 St. Two district schools sit in the wider centre: Pebble Hill Traditional Elementary, 246 52A St, and South Park Elementary, 735 Gilchrist Dr — attendance still follows the address catchment (https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/).

**Q:** What is happening with the Town Centre Mall site?
**A:** The site is not approved for redevelopment — the developer's own project page records that Century Group's revised rezoning application was rejected by the City of Delta at the October 6, 2025 council meeting, after the original October 2024 application. The mall and its Thrifty Foods store continue on the site; any future application would start again — full entry in §6 (http://tsawwassentowncentre.ca/project-updates/).

**Q:** Where do buses leave from in central Tsawwassen?
**A:** South Delta Exchange, at 56 Street and 18 Avenue beside South Delta Recreation Centre at 1720 56 St, is the centre's bus hub. TransLink route 601 runs from the exchange area via 56 Street to Bridgeport Station, and route 602 runs Tsawwassen Heights to Bridgeport Station via the exchange (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf).

**Q:** Is there urgent care in Tsawwassen, or only the hospital in Ladner?
**A:** Both exist — Fraser Health lists a South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 1826 – 4949 Canoe Pass Way in Tsawwassen, on the Tsawwassen First Nation lands west of the centre, while Delta Hospital at 5800 Mountain View Blvd in Ladner is the community hospital for Delta (https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/7/8/Service---South-Delta-After-Hours-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Pebble Hill Traditional Elementary, 246 52A St, is described on its School District 37 site as Delta's first Traditional School, with school uniforms and formalized routines — a program detail that affects school planning for centre families (http://ph.deltasd.bc.ca/).
- English Bluff Elementary, 402 English Bluff Rd, is an International Baccalaureate Primary Years Program school in the district's international programs listing, west of the centre (https://www.godelta.ca/about-delta/schools/english-bluff-elementary/).
- The Thrifty Foods grocery store sits on the Town Centre Mall site, and a City council report names the store on the site whose addresses are 1219 56 Street, 5499 12 Avenue and 1236 55 Street (https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/).
- Tsawwassen's Spring Clean-Up day in 2026 was Monday, March 30, under the City's annual curbside program (http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-city-delta-announces-2026-spring-clean).
- South Park Elementary, 735 Gilchrist Dr, is listed for School District 37 in the Province's school directory, alongside Pebble Hill Traditional Elementary at 246 52a St and English Bluff Elementary at 402 English Bluff Rd (https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/037).

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Tsawwassen Town Centre Mall redevelopment — Century Group Lands Corporation; site addresses 1219 56 Street, 5499 12 Avenue, 1236 55 Street and an unaddressed parcel; City File LU009754. STATUS, read 2026-11-03: the original rezoning application (October 2024) proposed about 1,433 homes; a revised, reduced application (September 2025, about 600 homes) was rejected by Delta Council on October 6, 2025, per the developer's own project updates page — the project is not approved (http://tsawwassentowncentre.ca/project-updates/).
- Southlands Tsawwassen — Century Group; sales centre at #101 – 6388 Market Ave. STATUS, read 2026-11-03: under construction and selling in phases; the developer states about 950 freehold homes at completion, home sales began in 2020, and full build-out is expected to take about 10 to 15 years, around a Market District anchored by Market Square and the Red Barn (https://discoversouthlands.ca/faq/).
- Century Group's own project page describes Southlands as 950 homes on 537 acres along Boundary Bay, with working farmland, forest and trails connecting to Boundary Bay Park (https://centurygroup.ca/projects/southlands-tsawwassen/).
- The Fraser River Tunnel Project (Highway 99 crossing) touches this area's commute; it is named here once and covered in the cliff-drive note (https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future).
Read: 2026-11-03

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- South Delta Exchange is the transit hub, at 56 Street and 18 Avenue beside South Delta Recreation Centre: routes 601 and 602 run from the exchange area to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line) (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf).
- Route 620 links the BC Ferries Tsawwassen terminal to Bridgeport Station via Highway 17, Highway 17A and Ladner Exchange (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt620.pdf).
- The ferry terminal is at the end of Highway 17 south of the centre, with sailings to Swartz Bay, Duke Point, Departure Bay and the Southern Gulf Islands (https://www.bcferries.com/travel-boarding/terminal-directions-parking-food/vancouver-tsawwassen/TSA).
- By road, 56 Street and 12 Avenue are the centre's junction; Highway 17 is reached north on 56 Street (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf). Driving times are UNCONFIRMED.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Near 56 Street and 12 Avenue, ask about the Town Centre file before buying: the LU009754 applications were rejected in 2025, so no approved redevelopment timeline exists for the mall site, and a future application could change the site again (http://tsawwassentowncentre.ca/project-updates/).
- For renovations or additions in the centre, check the Tsawwassen Urban Centre Development Permit Area guidelines in the OCP, which speak to siting, streetscape, massing and landscaping (https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf).
- Confirm the parcel's zone on the City zoning maps before assuming suite or subdivision potential — minimums differ sharply between zones such as RS7 (335 m²) and RD1 (836 m²) under Bylaw No. 7600 (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf).
- Check the exact school catchment by address in the SD37 locator; the Cliff Drive Elementary result in §4 is for one sample address only (https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/).
