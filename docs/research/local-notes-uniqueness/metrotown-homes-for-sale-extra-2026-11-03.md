## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Metrotown is designated in Burnaby's Official Community Plan (OCP) as a Regional City Centre. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/gzbb96lh2d/lum---metrotown
- The Metrotown Downtown Plan was adopted by Council on July 24, 2017. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/gzbb96lh2d/lum---metrotown
- The plan boundary runs from Boundary Road west to Royal Oak Avenue east and Imperial Street south, with a northern edge on local streets including Bond and Dover Streets; Central Park is inside the western part. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/gzbb96lh2d/lum---metrotown
- Kingsway, Central Boulevard, Beresford Street and Willingdon Avenue are the plan's main streets. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/gzbb96lh2d/lum---metrotown

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Community plans in Burnaby are adopted by Council as a policy guideline for considering rezoning applications, and designations can be changed by Council. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/gzbb96lh2d/lum---metrotown
- Site-specific zoning sits in the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw and Comprehensive Development plans, not in the Downtown Plan map alone. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/gzbb96lh2d/lum---metrotown
- Proposed streets, lanes and linkages on the plan map are diagrammatic and may be adjusted for a specific proposal. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/gzbb96lh2d/lum---metrotown

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is Metrotown Burnaby's downtown? **A:** Yes, in City planning Metrotown is Burnaby's downtown and a Regional City Centre. The Metrotown Downtown Plan adopted July 24, 2017 replaced the earlier community plan framework for the area bounded by Boundary Road, Royal Oak Avenue, Imperial Street and the Bond and Dover Street edge, and it sets the land-use direction a rezoning is judged against. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/gzbb96lh2d/lum---metrotown
**Q:** What are the neighbourhoods inside Metrotown? **A:** The Downtown Plan divides the area into six neighbourhoods: Metro Downtown, Maywood, Central Park North, Central Park East, Marlborough and The Ridge. A home's neighbourhood matters because density and form expectations differ by neighbourhood in the plan, even though all six sit inside the same Regional City Centre boundary and share the same SkyTrain station area. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/gzbb96lh2d/lum---metrotown
**Q:** Does the plan guarantee a tower can be built on a lot? **A:** No, the City states community plans are a policy guideline for considering rezoning applications and are subject to change by Council. The general land-use map shows the designation, but site-specific land uses, guidelines, desired assemblies and road dedications are defined in the plan text, the Zoning Bylaw and Comprehensive Development plans for the site. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/gzbb96lh2d/lum---metrotown
**Q:** Where is Central Park in relation to homes here? **A:** Central Park forms the western part of the plan area along Boundary Road, and the Patterson SkyTrain station sits at its northeast fringe at Patterson Avenue and Central Boulevard. Homes in Central Park North and Central Park East neighbourhoods adjoin the park, while Metro Downtown homes sit east of Willingdon Avenue around Kingsway and Central Boulevard. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/gzbb96lh2d/lum---metrotown

- The plan area includes about 82 hectares of Central Park inside its roughly 297-hectare boundary — see the metrotown-burnaby-houses-for-sale note for the sourced figure check before reuse.
- Non-market and market rental homes can be on any residentially designated site, so rental buildings are not confined to one block. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/gzbb96lh2d/lum---metrotown
- New institutional uses are considered site by site on any mixed-use designated site, not pre-mapped. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/gzbb96lh2d/lum---metrotown
- Metrotown Station is at 4401 Beresford Street, between Patterson Station toward Waterfront and Royal Oak Station toward King George. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/gzbb96lh2d/lum---metrotown
- Burnaby's sewer parcel tax moves from the property tax notice to the utility notice starting in 2027. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Metrotown Station is an elevated Expo Line station on Central Boulevard, with Patterson Station to the west and Royal Oak Station to the east. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/gzbb96lh2d/lum---metrotown
- Kingsway is the east-west arterial through the plan area, linking to Vancouver at Boundary Road and to New Westminster to the southeast. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/gzbb96lh2d/lum---metrotown
