# Greendale — Extra Local Notes
Page: greendale (Greendale; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Greendale is a rural farm community on the west side of the City of Chilliwack, inside the city boundary but planned as farmland, not suburb. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8630
- Sumas Prairie Road is the community's spine, carrying Greendale Elementary Community School at 6621 Sumas Prairie Road. https://greendale.sd33.bc.ca/
- The historic settlement block was bounded by Adams Road, Hopedale Road, South Sumas Road and Sumas Prairie Road, the grid the farm roads here still follow. https://gameo.org/index.php?title=Greendale_(Chilliwack,_British_Columbia,_Canada)
- The name is recent: the settlement was originally called Sumas and was renamed Greendale around 1950 to avoid confusion with the nearby town in Washington State. https://gameo.org/index.php?title=Greendale_(Chilliwack,_British_Columbia,_Canada)
- Chadsey Road is the other named local road: Chadsey Elementary School stood on it from the 1960s until it closed in 2002 and its grades 1 and 2 students moved to Greendale Elementary. https://gcs.sd33.bc.ca/index.php/school-history

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The housing here is farm housing on Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) parcels, under the City's AL (Agricultural Lowland) Zone frame, where the minimum lot area for subdivision is 7.5 hectares — the full zone table is in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- The scale of the farm frame is city-wide context: Chilliwack contains 41,862 acres of land in the ALR, and about two-thirds of the city's land base falls within the ALR, most of it Fraser River valley-bottom farmland. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8630
- The original farm settlement pattern was large lots: in 1930 the Northern Construction Company sold 274 hectares (686 acres) here divided into 28 lots of 18 to 25 acres each. https://gameo.org/index.php?title=Greendale_(Chilliwack,_British_Columbia,_Canada)
- No official source read for this note publishes a Greendale build-era or dwelling-count profile, so none is stated — see NOT CONFIRMED. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8630

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- ALR rules govern what can be built: on ALR land a principal residence is limited to 500 square metres total floor area, a secondary suite is permitted only when entirely contained within the principal residence and local bylaws permit it, and one additional detached residence is permitted without an Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) application at up to 90 square metres on parcels of 40 hectares or less, or 186 square metres on larger parcels. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- The City reviews ALR change applications locally: the City is responsible for reviewing applications for non-farm uses, exclusions, inclusions, subdivisions and boundary adjustments in the ALR. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8630
- A Development Permit is required on farm parcels when the Farm Home Plate regulations in the Zoning Bylaw are not met, under Development Permit Area 4 – Farm Home Plate, which exists to reduce the long-term impact of residential uses on agricultural land. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- Flood construction levels on ALR land protected by standard dikes have generally been standardized at 1.2 metres above natural ground elevation, with a flooding covenant registered on title. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- City-wide suite and subdivision rules are written in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note — see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note. https://chilliwack.com/ocp

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I subdivide a Greendale farm into smaller lots? **A:** Not as a standard subdivision, because the land is Agricultural Land Reserve farmland. The City's AL (Agricultural Lowland) Zone sets a 7.5-hectare minimum lot area for subdivision, and any ALR subdivision or boundary adjustment also goes through the Agricultural Land Commission application process, which the City reviews; a small-lot split needs approvals a normal residential lot does not. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q:** Can I build a second house or a suite for family or farm help? **A:** Within the ALR housing frame only. A principal residence may be up to 500 square metres, a suite is permitted only inside that principal residence, and one additional detached residence is allowed without an ALC application at up to 90 square metres on parcels of 40 hectares or less, or 186 square metres above that size, subject to City bylaws. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q:** Which school do Greendale children attend? **A:** The school in the community is Greendale Elementary Community School at 6621 Sumas Prairie Road, a Kindergarten to Grade 5 school in School District No. 33 (Chilliwack). Catchments are address-based, so confirm the exact address with the district; Chadsey Elementary on Chadsey Road closed in 2002 and no longer operates as a district school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/school/03333042
- **Q:** Does flood risk affect a farm purchase here? **A:** Yes — check the flood construction level before pricing any building plan. Greendale sits in the valley-bottom floodplain frame the City regulates under Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519, and on ALR land behind standard dikes the flood construction level is generally 1.2 metres above natural ground elevation, with a flooding covenant on title. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- **Q:** Is there a bus into Chilliwack from Greendale? **A:** Yes — BC Transit Route 55 Yarrow–Greendale loops from Vedder Road at South Sumas Road through the UFV Chilliwack campus and Yarrow to Greendale, with four weekday departures and three on Saturday in the operator's schedule, and no Sunday trips shown. It is a backup, not a commute plan — daily life still runs on a vehicle. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=55

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The elementary school is also the community hall: Greendale Elementary is a community school, and its history page records that most of the school was destroyed by fire in September 2000 and the damaged portion was rebuilt and reopened in September 2002. https://gcs.sd33.bc.ca/index.php/school-history
- Drainage is farmed, not ornamental: Council minutes record the $2.5 million Collinson Pump Station upgrade, which almost doubled pumping capacity for a 1-in-100-year rainfall event and benefits over 750 hectares of agricultural land in Greendale plus its homes and outbuildings. https://www.chilliwack.com/apps/council/meetings/minutes/reg/2015.12.15 Regular Council Minutes.pdf
- Small businesses in the historic settlement were established mainly along Sumas Prairie Road, which is still where the community's non-farm frontage sits. https://gameo.org/index.php?title=Greendale_(Chilliwack,_British_Columbia,_Canada)
- Farm classification is a tax file, not an automatic status: buyers pricing a Greendale parcel should confirm the parcel's ALR status on the City Webmap and check Agricultural Land Commission requirements before assuming any non-farm use. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- BC Transit Route 55 Yarrow–Greendale serves Greendale on a Vedder Road–UFV Chilliwack campus–Yarrow loop (four weekday and three Saturday departures in the schedule read, none on Sunday), but most daily life here is still car-based. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=55
- The road grid is the farm grid: Sumas Prairie Road runs through the community, with Adams Road, Hopedale Road, South Sumas Road and Chadsey Road forming the local block pattern. https://gameo.org/index.php?title=Greendale_(Chilliwack,_British_Columbia,_Canada)
- Downtown Chilliwack is the transit exchange for the city system, for any trip that starts with a drive into town. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check ALR status first: the City directs owners to the City Webmap for ALR status, and an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, fill or soil removal, a non-farm use, or a subdivision or boundary adjustment. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- Check the flood construction level on Schedule A of Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 before valuing any building or addition. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- Check drainage, not just river flood: the Collinson Pump Station record shows Greendale's protection depends on pumped drainage for heavy rainfall as well as on dikes, so ditch and pump service to the specific parcel is a diligence item. https://www.chilliwack.com/apps/council/meetings/minutes/reg/2015.12.15 Regular Council Minutes.pdf
- Riparian ditches and creeks fall under Development Permit Area 3, which protects fish habitat and riparian areas across the city. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
