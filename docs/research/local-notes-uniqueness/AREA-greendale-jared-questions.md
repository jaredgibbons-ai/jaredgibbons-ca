# Greendale — Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer from his own deal experience here. Do not answer them from research.

1. Who buys in Greendale in your deals — working farmers, hobby-farm families, or buyers holding the land for the long term — and what size of parcel do they actually close on?
2. What sells fast here and what sits — do parcels with a good house and farm buildings move first, while bare or oddly configured ALR parcels wait?
3. What surprises buyers most — the ALR limits on a second dwelling, drainage and ditch obligations, or how far daily services actually are?
4. In your deals, how do farm classification, water licences and drainage/pump questions show up in subjects, and which one most often changes the price?
5. Story prompt: tell one Greendale farm deal — the parcel, the ALR or drainage wrinkle, and how it closed — that shows a buyer what buying farmland here really involves.
