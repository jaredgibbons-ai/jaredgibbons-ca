# Fraser Heights Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: fraser-heights-surrey-houses-for-sale (Fraser Heights; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Fraser Heights houses sit north of Highway 1, under the Fraser River bluff; the general area boundaries are in the earlier note — see the fraser-heights-surrey-condos-townhomes-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/abbey-ridge-land-use-plan
- The house-only eastern pocket is Abbey Ridge: about 175 hectares (430 acres) north of Highway 1, between 172 Street west and the Port Kells Industrial Area and Golden Ears Connector east. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/abbey-ridge-land-use-plan
- Surrey Bend Regional Park lies east of the residential streets, on the Fraser River floodplain side of the bluff. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/surrey-bend-regional-park

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- East Fraser Heights houses started as suburban acreage lots, and the Abbey Ridge plan keeps the area largely residential, with industrial designations only along the Golden Ears Connector and a small commercial area on Barnston Drive. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/abbey-ridge-land-use-plan
- A current Abbey Ridge example shows the lot pattern: at 10187 173 Street a City report describes a site zoned One-Acre Residential Zone (RA), designated Suburban Residential 2–4 units per acre, proposed for rezoning to Quarter Acre Residential Zone (RQ) and Single Family Residential Zone (RF) for one suburban and four urban single-family lots. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7921-0282-00.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- An Abbey Ridge house lot can carry an acreage zone, not a normal urban zone; the 173 Street report above records RA zoning and a Half-Acre Residential Zone (RH) history on nearby Abbey Ridge sites, so the zone on the title search matters more than the street's look. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7921-0282-00.pdf
- Surrey-wide small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) and suite rules for detached lots are in full in the city note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note; here, check first whether the lot is an RA or RH acreage parcel in Abbey Ridge, because those parcels follow the plan's suburban designations. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/abbey-ridge-land-use-plan
- The Abbey Ridge plan's land use designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) update, and the plan now guides amenities and infrastructure only. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/abbey-ridge-land-use-plan

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is an east Fraser Heights house on a normal city lot? **A:** Not always — the east side was suburban acreage. The Abbey Ridge plan area covers about 175 hectares north of Highway 1, and City reports for 173 Street record one-acre RA zoning being rezoned in pieces to RQ and RF urban lots, so two neighbouring houses can sit on very different lot rules. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7921-0282-00.pdf
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves a Fraser Heights house? **A:** Fraser Heights Secondary at 16060 108 Avenue serves the area. The School District No. 36 (Surrey) family for 2026–2027 groups Bothwell, Dogwood, Erma Stephenson and Fraser Wood elementary schools with it, but catchment is by exact address, so confirm the house in the district school locator before relying on a school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** Does the Highway 1 side of a lot change a rebuild? **A:** It can, through noise and buffer rules. A City report for a 100 Avenue project in Abbey Ridge records a 15-metre landscape buffer in the plan to reduce Highway 1 noise and visual impact along the south property line, with the applicant seeking a narrower buffer — a lot-by-lot check, not a street-wide rule. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7918-0145-00.pdf
- **Q:** Is there a SkyTrain station for Fraser Heights houses? **A:** No — there is no station in Fraser Heights. TransLink route 337 is named Fraser Heights / Surrey Central Station and ends at 168 Street at 104 Avenue via Guildford Exchange, and route 338 links Guildford Exchange to East Fraser Heights; rail access is by bus to Surrey Central or King George stations. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/337/direction/0/schedule

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The local recreation centre has no pool; Fraser Heights Recreation Centre at 10588 160 Street offers two gymnasiums, a fitness studio and an about 5,000 square foot weight room, and shares its site with Fraser Heights Park's tennis courts, turf fields and skateboard plaza. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/fraser-heights-recreation-centre
- There is no Surrey Libraries branch in Fraser Heights; the nearest branch east is Port Kells Branch at 18885 88 Avenue, which Surrey Libraries states opened in 1970 in the former community post office and is its oldest active library building. https://surreylibraries.ca/locations/port-kells-branch
- Harold Bishop Elementary at 15670 104 Avenue sits at the south edge but is grouped with Johnston Heights Secondary, not Fraser Heights Secondary, in the district's 2026–2027 families document — a south-edge house is not automatically in the Fraser Heights family. https://media.surreyschools.ca/media/Default/medialib/families-of-schools-2026-2027.299e54207481.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Fraser Heights houses pay the same City of Surrey residential tax rate, parcel taxes, flat-rate water and sewer, and secondary-suite utility levy as every Surrey house — the full 2026 figures are in the city note, see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- The houses-specific point is the acreage edge: an RA or RH parcel in Abbey Ridge is still a Surrey residential property for tax purposes, but its servicing and subdivision costs follow the Abbey Ridge engineering strategy in the plan, not the urban RF streets west of 172 Street. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/abbey-ridge-land-use-plan

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Buses, not SkyTrain, serve Fraser Heights houses. Route 337 runs Surrey Central Station to Fraser Heights (terminus 168 Street at 104 Avenue) via Guildford Exchange, and the 338 community shuttle, launched January 6, 2025, runs Guildford Exchange to East Fraser Heights (terminus 177A Street at 101A Avenue) every 30 minutes via 104 Avenue, 160 Street and the Golden Ears Connector. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/337/direction/0/schedule
- Highway 1 is the south boundary and the Golden Ears Connector frames the east side, which is why Abbey Ridge streets connect through 172 Street and the connector rather than across the highway. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/abbey-ridge-land-use-plan

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the bluff and creek lots in the City's Development Permit Areas (DPAs) first. Surrey's Official Community Plan includes hazard DPAs: steep-slope areas over 20 percent gradient, and flood-prone areas inside the 200-year floodplain, both as shown in the OCP figures and in COSMOS, the City of Surrey Mapping Online System. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/surrey-official-community-plan/development-permit-areas
- The low land east of the houses floods seasonally: Metro Vancouver states sections of the Parsons and Pacific trails in Surrey Bend Regional Park may be flooded and closed during high tides or heavy rainfall — that floodplain is parkland, and the page does not state the flood status of any house lot. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/surrey-bend-regional-park
