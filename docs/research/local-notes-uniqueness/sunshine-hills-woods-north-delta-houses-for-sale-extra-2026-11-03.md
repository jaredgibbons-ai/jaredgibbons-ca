## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses here are mainly freehold single detached homes under a Small Scale Residential designation in Delta's 2024 Official Community Plan (OCP). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Subdivision minimums vary widely by zone, from RS7 at 335 m² to RS1 at 4,000 m², so lot size is a zone question, not an area average. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- Duplex minimums in the bylaw ladder include RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m². https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Delta allows up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots under its small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) updates. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- Delta's SSMUH rules allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- Delta is proposing to consolidate 12 small-scale residential zones into one and permit houseplexes of three or four units with separate exterior entrances; this was a proposal at engagement stage, not an adopted rule. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-city-delta-launches-engagement-small-scale
- Zoning is parcel-by-parcel under Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve my address? **A:** Catchment depends on the exact address, so check it before you buy. For 11285 Bond Blvd the School District 37 (SD37) locator result is Sunshine Hills Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 7, at that same address, and Seaquam Secondary, Grades 8 to 12, at 11584 Lyon Rd. SD37 has no middle-school tier. Other streets in the area can fall in different catchments, so run each address in the locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q:** What is Watershed Park, next to these houses? **A:** Watershed Park is Delta's largest park and sits on the west side of this area. The City gives its address as 11600 Kittson Pkwy and describes eleven kilometres of gravel trails. Houses backing onto the park or Cougar Canyon trade a greenbelt edge for lot-by-lot streamside checks, described in section 9. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/watershed-park
- **Q:** Can I add a coach house or garden suite? **A:** Yes, in the combinations Delta's SSMUH rules allow, if your parcel's zoning permits it. Delta states its regulations allow single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites in combination. Confirm the parcel's zone sheet under Bylaw No. 7600 first, because minimum lot sizes differ sharply between zones. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q:** Where does the drinking water come from? **A:** Most Delta water comes from Metro Vancouver, and about 1% comes from Delta wells near Watershed Park. The City's 2024 water report says well water is pumped from deep wells in Sunshine Hills into the 64 Avenue Reservoir and supplied by gravity to the lowland area south of 64 Avenue. https://www.delta.ca/media/3310

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta's 2026 flat-rate utility total is $1,658 for a single-family home, up from $1,607 in 2025. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Flat-rate utility bills are issued at the end of February and cover garbage and recycling, with water and sewer either on that bill or on separate quarterly metered bills. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Delta's 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- The approved 2026 Financial Plan included a 2.9% property tax increase for City operational services; Delta does not publish the rate as a single per-$1,000 figure on the page read. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- The City of Delta is listed as a taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT), so owners must complete the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check Cougar Creek setbacks before valuing a lot's buildable area. Cougar Creek is a salmon-bearing watercourse through Surrey and Delta, the City has done bank-stabilization work in Cougar Canyon, and streamside setback detail is lot-specific. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status parcel by parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission map; the OCP map shows no Agricultural designation for this part of North Delta, which is not a parcel-level ruling. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Confirm the parcel zone under Bylaw No. 7600 before assuming suite, coach-house or subdivision rights, because zone minimums range from 335 m² to 4,000 m². https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
