# West Central Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: west-central (West Central, Maple Ridge; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- "West Central" is a Paragon (MLS) sub-area name only: it is not a City of Maple Ridge plan or neighbourhood name, and the City does not list "West Central" among its neighbourhoods. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The City publishes no boundary for this sub-area, so the boundary is UNVERIFIED; this page's working scope, from the live page only, is the residential ground between 207 Street and 222 Street, with corridors Lougheed Highway, Dewdney Trunk Road, Abernethy Way, 207 Street, 216 Street and 222 Street — a working scope, never an official boundary. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- West Central sits west of East Central and the Town Centre, northeast of Southwest Maple Ridge (Hammond / Port Hammond), and east of the Pitt Meadows municipal boundary. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- One seam to know: Eric Langton Elementary at 12138 Edge Street sits on the seam between this page's working scope and East Central's, and both pages' research names schools on that seam — see the east-central note for the Town Centre ground east of it. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is "West Central" a real Maple Ridge neighbourhood? **A:** Not as a City name. "West Central" is the Paragon sub-area name used for listings west of the Town Centre; the City does not list it among its neighbourhoods and publishes no boundary for it, so residents usually describe where they live by street and school names instead. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- **Q:** Which schools serve West Central streets? **A:** Schools named for this area, with addresses confirmed against the British Columbia Ministry school file, are Alouette Elementary at 22155 Isaac Crescent, Glenwood Elementary at 21410 Glenwood Avenue, Laity View Elementary at 21023 123 Avenue, Maple Ridge Secondary at 21911 122 Avenue and Westview Secondary at 20905 Wicklund Avenue; catchments are address-based, so run the exact address in the School District No. 42 (SD42) locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q:** Does the Lougheed corridor plan change what my lot can become? **A:** It can on the corridor edges. The adopted Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan directs growth around future Bus Rapid Transit stations along Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road, so a corridor lot's plan designation should be checked before it is valued as a plain house lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- **Q:** Is there a recreation centre or library in West Central? **A:** No. No City recreation centre is located in West Central — the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre at 11925 Haney Place is in the Town Centre to the east — and the Maple Ridge Public Library at 130 – 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road is also in the Town Centre. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/maple-ridge-public-library
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Ridge Meadows Hospital at 11666 Laity Street is west of the Town Centre and is the community hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows, with 24/7 emergency care; its Laity Street address puts it on this side of the city. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- School District No. 42 has no middle tier: elementary is Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary is Grades 8 to 12, so children here change schools after Grade 7. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/
- Three parks named on the live page — Merkley Park (21690 124 Avenue), Reg Franklin Park (21358 Dewdney Trunk Road) and Brickwood Park (12162 223 Street) — were not confirmed on a City facility page in the research behind this note: UNVERIFIED as City facility listings.

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Maple Meadows Station at 20010 Dunn Avenue serves the west side of Maple Ridge, and Port Haney Station at 22300 River Road sits at the east edge of the wider west side; West Coast Express trains run weekdays between Waterfront Station (downtown Vancouver) and Mission City. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Haney Place Exchange, in the Town Centre to the east, is the bus hub; the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus runs from Coquitlam Central Station to Haney Place. ## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check floodplain status on south-edge lots: the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 metres geodetic including freeboard, no habitable floor space may sit below it on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw section 402.8(h) prohibits suites on floodplain properties. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- Check escarpment status near 224 Street: lots within 300 metres of the Fraser River Escarpment crest need a geotechnical report on a Section 219 covenant before a building permit — a teardown-and-rebuild cost item, not a formality. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- Because no official sub-area boundary exists, confirm any "West Central" claim in a listing against the street address itself — the label alone does not fix a school catchment, a plan designation or a floodplain status. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
