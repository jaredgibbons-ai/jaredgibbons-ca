# King George Corridor Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: king-george-corridor-south-surrey-houses-for-sale (King George Corridor, South Surrey; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The corridor runs on King George Boulevard from 8 Avenue to the Nicomekl River, about 7 kilometres and about 490 hectares (1,210 acres), with Highway 99 to the east, the Nicomekl River to the north and 8 Avenue to the south. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south
- This is the south segment only: the same road continues through Newton to Surrey City Centre, but the City plan area, and this page, stop at the Nicomekl River. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south
- The City page states no west boundary road for the plan area, so a house west of King George Boulevard is not confirmed inside the plan area by that page alone. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached houses here sit in a corridor the City planned for mixed development type and density, parks and trails, a transportation network and engineering infrastructure — not as a single subdivision. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south
- At the north end, land at 3600 King George Boulevard is zoned General Agriculture Zone (A-1) and lies in the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR), so the corridor's detached stock includes acreage-edge lots beside farmland, not only urban lots. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0403-00
- The south end, between 16 Avenue and 24 Avenue, falls under the Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan as well as the corridor plan, so houses there sit beside the town-centre density the corridor plan alone does not describe. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/semiahmoo-town-centre-plan

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Do not rely on the 1995 corridor designations: on December 2, 2024 Council endorsed Corporate Report R246 updating them, and the City states the corridor's land use designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) update. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south
- For the city-wide Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH), secondary suite and Zoning By-law No. 12000 position, see the surrey-houses-for-sale note; the corridor-specific point is that A-1 / ALR lots at the Nicomekl end, such as 3600 King George Boulevard, are treated as agricultural land in the City report, not as ordinary urban house lots. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0403-00

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a King George Corridor house in Newton or South Surrey? **A:** South Surrey, on this page. The City plan area runs from 8 Avenue to the Nicomekl River, bordered by Highway 99 to the east. King George Boulevard continues north through Newton to City Centre, but that northern stretch is outside this plan area and outside this page, so check which side of the Nicomekl River a listing sits on before comparing houses. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south
- **Q:** Can I rely on the old King George Corridor plan for what can be built? **A:** No. The planning process completed in 1995 produced a land use and development concept plan, but the City states those designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update, and Council endorsed updated designations in Corporate Report R246 on December 2, 2024. Use the current OCP designation for the specific lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south
- **Q:** Does the corridor have rapid transit now? **A:** Not on this south segment. TransLink's R1 RapidBus runs on King George Boulevard only between Newton Exchange and Guildford Exchange, north of the Nicomekl River. The King George Boulevard Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) project toward Semiahmoo Town Centre is a separate, later project covered in Section 6, so a corridor house today does not have a RapidBus stop on its own segment. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
- **Q:** Is the north end of the corridor a flood concern? **A:** Check the specific lot. The corridor ends at the Nicomekl River, and the City maintains a Serpentine–Nicomekl lowlands flood-control page for this river system. A house near the river or on the A-1 land at 3600 King George Boulevard needs the floodplain and ALR check in Section 9 before an offer, not a corridor-wide assumption. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The corridor is about 7 kilometres long but has no City-stated west boundary road, so two houses both described as “King George Corridor” can sit well apart, near 8 Avenue or near the Nicomekl River. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south
- Highway 99, not a local street, is the plan area's east border, so east-side corridor houses back toward a provincial highway corridor rather than a neighbourhood street. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south
- The Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan overlaps only the 16 Avenue to 24 Avenue stretch, so school, shopping and density expectations differ between the corridor's south end and its Nicomekl end. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/semiahmoo-town-centre-plan

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Highway 99 is the corridor's east edge and Highway 10 (56 Avenue / 58 Avenue corridor) is reached north of the Nicomekl River crossing — the plan page itself names only Highway 99, the river and 8 Avenue as borders. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south
- TransLink RapidBus routes run at least every 10 minutes at peak and every 15 minutes off-peak, 6 a.m. to midnight, but the R1 on King George Boulevard runs Newton Exchange to Guildford Exchange, north of this corridor. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
- Route 321 (Surrey Central Station to White Rock) runs on King George Boulevard through South Surrey — see the pacific-douglas-south-surrey-houses-for-sale note for the White Rock end of that route. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt321.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check a Nicomekl-end lot on the City's Serpentine–Nicomekl lowlands flood-control page and in the City floodplain mapping before an offer; the corridor plan page does not state a flood construction level for any specific lot. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status for lots near 3600 King George Boulevard: the City report for that site states the site falls within the ALR and is zoned A-1, which limits non-farm uses. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0403-00
