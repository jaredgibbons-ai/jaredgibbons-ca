- A Ladner East-Urban (LV4) development permit controls form and character for new East Ladner townhouses. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009770

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is Hampton Cove strata or freehold, and what do owners get?
**A:** Hampton Cove's collections, including Westhampton by Polygon, are strata townhouses with exclusive access to The Hampton Club. Polygon describes over 12,000 square feet with pool, spa, fitness studio, gymnasium and guest suites. Central Ladner townhouses do not include that club. https://polyhomes.com/community/westhampton/

**Q:** Which school serves a townhouse in Neilsen Grove?
**A:** Neilson Grove Elementary is the school inside Neilsen Grove, and Polygon uses the walk to it as a selling point. School District No. 37 (Delta) catchments are address-based, so confirm the exact unit address in the School Locator before you buy. https://polyhomes.com/community/westhampton/

**Q:** What is being built on Central Avenue?
**A:** Wildwood, 40 four-bedroom, three-bathroom townhomes by Shato Holdings Ltd. at 4900 Central Avenue. The developer's site lists show homes as open. Read the disclosure statement and the developer's own project details on the site, not a listing summary, before buying in central Ladner. https://wildwoodladner.com/

**Q:** Did the 57 Street townhouses get approved?
**A:** Yes, the rezoning was adopted. The City page for 4876 to 4938 57 Street, file LU009582, applicant Maple Leaf Homes Ladner Ltd., states Council adopted the bylaw on March 3, 2025, for 40 units. Adoption is not a building permit. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009582?tool=survey_tool

Read: 2026-11-03
