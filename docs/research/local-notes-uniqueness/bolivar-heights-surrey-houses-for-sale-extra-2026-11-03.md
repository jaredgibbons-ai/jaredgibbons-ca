# Bolivar Heights Houses — Extra Local Notes

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Bolivar Heights is a North Surrey neighbourhood inside the City of Surrey's City Centre Plan area, north of the City Centre core. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/city-centre-plan
- Two City parks mark the area's extent: Bolivar Park at 13591 Crestview Drive and Invergarry Park at 11285 Surrey Road. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bolivar-park https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/invergarry-park
- Bolivar Park's forested south and east areas sit at higher elevation with views of New Westminster and the Fraser River, which is the bluff position the Heights name describes. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bolivar-park
- Shared area facts — boundaries, schools context and plan history — are carried in the bolivar-heights-surrey-condos-townhomes-for-sale and bolivar-heights-guide notes; this note keeps the houses angle only.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The City Centre Plan update now underway allows for growth in the Bolivar Heights area, including a mixed-use neighbourhood centre, so a house lot's future land use here is set by that update, not only by its current zoning. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/city-centre-plan
- Lots near the Bon Accord ravine in Invergarry Park adjoin protected riparian creek habitat, and Surrey may require a development permit in its Steep Slope and Sensitive Ecosystem Development Permit Areas. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/invergarry-park https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Bolivar Heights part of the City Centre plan area? **A:** Yes. The City's City Centre Plan page states that the plan update now underway allows for growth in the Bolivar Heights area, including a mixed-use neighbourhood centre. That matters for a house buyer because the plan, updated after Council endorsed an Entertainment District proposal in October 2025, sets the direction for land use change around these established house streets. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/city-centre-plan
- **Q:** Which elementary schools are in Bolivar Heights itself? **A:** Two public schools are physically in the area: James Ardiel Elementary at 13751 112 Avenue and Surrey Traditional School at 13875 113 Avenue, both in the BC Ministry of Education list for School District 36. Surrey Traditional is a choice program, and regular catchments are assigned by exact address through the district's School Locator, so confirm the address rather than assuming the in-area school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036 https://www.surreyschools.ca/ellendale/school-registration
- **Q:** Does a SkyTrain station serve Bolivar Heights? **A:** No station is inside Bolivar Heights. The one TransLink route confirmed running through its streets is bus 373 between Surrey Central Station and Guildford, which passes Gateway Station on the Expo Line and runs via 112 Avenue, 136 Street, 113 Avenue, Surrey Road and Wallace Drive. Station access for a house here means a bus ride or drive to City Centre, not a walk to a platform. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt373.pdf
- **Q:** What is the ravine land in the middle of the area? **A:** It is Invergarry Park, which protects over 37.5 hectares of natural forest and riparian creek habitat in the Bon Accord ravine and creek system, with trail access from Gladstone Drive, Currie Drive, 111 Avenue, 114 Avenue and Surrey Road. Houses backing onto it sit beside protected creek habitat, which is why ravine-edge lots need a setback and permit check before any addition or rebuild. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/invergarry-park

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Bolivar Park includes a two-acre fenced dog off-leash area east of the main parking lot, plus baseball fields, a field house and forest trails; parking is accessed from 115 Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bolivar-park
- The eastern part of Invergarry Park, around Wallace Drive and Surrey Road, holds the Invergarry Mountain Bike Park, with trails offering up to 25 metres of vertical and drops up to 3 metres on expert terrain. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/invergarry-park
- Invergarry Park dates to 1913, when the Surrey Municipality began consolidating land to conserve the Bon Accord ravine and creek system — the ravine is protected parkland, not a future development parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/invergarry-park

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Bolivar Heights houses pay City of Surrey property taxes, due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- The provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies: the Province lists the City of Surrey as a designated taxable area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check any lot backing onto the Bon Accord ravine for steep-slope and riparian constraints before planning an addition: the park protects the ravine and creek system, and Surrey's Steep Slope and Sensitive Ecosystem Development Permit Areas can require a permit. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/invergarry-park https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Confirm a specific lot's floodplain status in the City's COSMOS mapping rather than from the neighbourhood's bluff position: Bolivar Heights sits on the upland above the Fraser River floodplain, and floodplain status is shown per address. https://www.surrey.ca/city-services/3655.aspx
