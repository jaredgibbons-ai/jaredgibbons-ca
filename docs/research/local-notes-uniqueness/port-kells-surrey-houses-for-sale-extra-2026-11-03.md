# Port Kells Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: port-kells-surrey-houses-for-sale (Port Kells, Surrey; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Port Kells splits at Highway 1: the industrial area sits north of it by the Fraser River, and the house streets sit south of it toward the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR). https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791
- The Anniedale-Tynehead Neighbourhood Concept Plan (NCP) area, the planned part of south Port Kells, covers about 408 hectares (ha), bounded by Highway 1 and 96 Avenue north, 168 Street west, the ALR south and Harvie Road east. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/anniedale-tynehead-ncp
- 176 Street (Highway 15) is the area's north-south truck route, and 88 Avenue is the east-west street carrying Port Kells Elementary at 19076 88 Avenue. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/school/03636002

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses here are mostly on acreage lots: the City first described Anniedale-Tynehead as rural acreage lots and agricultural uses, now designated to support urban development. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/anniedale-tynehead-ncp
- Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 zone names for this stock include RA (One-Acre Residential), RH (Half-Acre Residential) and A-1 (General Agriculture), per the Development Cost Charge (DCC) bylaw zone list. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21174.pdf
- North of Highway 1 the stock changes completely: the Port Kells industrial area spans 781 acres and is primarily zoned for light industrial use. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The ALR edge is the controlling line: the NCP plan area stops at the ALR in the south, so a lot's ALR status decides whether farm rules or the NCP govern it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/anniedale-tynehead-ncp
- Protected watercourses cross the plan area, including the Serpentine River and several tributaries, and the City calls them significant and protected. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/anniedale-tynehead-ncp
- Stage 2 of the Anniedale-Tynehead NCP is still underway, to develop a servicing and financial plan and refine land uses, so servicing for new lots is not settled. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/anniedale-tynehead-ncp

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is a Port Kells house in the floodplain?
**A:** Part of the area is: Port Kells is named among the tributary areas of the Serpentine River, whose lowland floodplain covers 59 square kilometres (km2), and much of that lowland sits very close to sea level. Ask the City which side of the floodplain line the lot falls on, and check the Serpentine and Nicomekl Lowlands page for the lot's reach of river. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands

**Q:** Which elementary school serves Port Kells houses?
**A:** Port Kells Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 7, at 19076 88 Avenue, is the neighbourhood school named for the area. It is also a heritage building: the current school opened in 1907 on the south side of 88 Avenue, across from Port Kells Church, and was formally recognized in 2000. Confirm the exact address catchment with School District No. 36 (Surrey) before relying on it. https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=11415

**Q:** Can I subdivide an acreage lot south of Highway 1?
**A:** Only as the Anniedale-Tynehead NCP and servicing allow: Council endorsed the Stage 1 Plan for the whole NCP in September 2024, and Stage 2, which sets the servicing and financial plan, is still underway. Until Stage 2 lands, a subdivision answer for one lot on 92 Avenue or 184 Street is a City file review, not a bylaw read alone. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/anniedale-tynehead-ncp

**Q:** What sits between the houses and Tynehead?
**A:** Tynehead Regional Park, run by Metro Vancouver, is the green block at the area's west side, entered from 168 Street. The practical point for a buyer is that park land, not more houses, forms the western edge of the house streets. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/tynehead-regional-park

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Port Kells Elementary opened in 1907, and the first Port Kells school opened in 1891 on the Telegraph Trail, then inside Langley's boundaries. https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=11415
- The Serpentine River is controlled by a sea dam at King George Highway, first built in the 1912-1913 period with the Nicomekl dam, to keep salt water out of farm irrigation. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain
- South Port Kells was only picked for new development in 2003, with a General Land Use Plan approved in 2005, which is why the house streets still read rural. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/anniedale-tynehead-ncp
- The plan area is expected to hold up to 20,000 Surrey residents once built out, on gently sloping highlands above the agricultural lowlands. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/anniedale-tynehead-ncp
- The industrial area's 2025 DCC schedule has separate Port Kells industrial and commercial rates, a cost signal that the north side is planned as employment land. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey-wide residential tax rate, utility billing and Speculation and Vacancy Tax treatment: see the surrey-houses-for-sale note.
- Port Kells carries its own DCC geography: the City's DCC bylaw lists the Anniedale-Tynehead and Port Kells industrial areas as separate charge areas from general Surrey zones. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21174.pdf
- Acreage lots under the RA and RH zones are charged DCCs by zone at subdivision, so a split on 88 Avenue prices differently from an RF-zone lot elsewhere in Surrey. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21174.pdf

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Highway 1 and 176 Street (Highway 15) do the heavy lifting: the NCP area is bounded by Highway 1 and 96 Avenue in the north, so most trips start at those two interchanges. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/anniedale-tynehead-ncp
- Trails are part of the plan, not an afterthought: the NCP network links neighbourhood parks, schools and Tynehead Regional Park, with an overpass connection across Highway 1 toward Fraser Heights. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/anniedale-tynehead-ncp

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with water: the Serpentine catchment is 334 km2, its river is tide-influenced, and the lowland was historically an intertidal zone, so drainage on a low Port Kells lot needs a file check. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- Check ALR status before any farm-use assumption: the NCP boundary uses the ALR as its south edge, and lots can sit on either side of that line on the same street. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/anniedale-tynehead-ncp
- Check servicing timing: Stage 2 of the NCP sets the servicing and financial plan, and it was still underway on the City page read 2026-11-03. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/guildford-land-use-plans/anniedale-tynehead-ncp
