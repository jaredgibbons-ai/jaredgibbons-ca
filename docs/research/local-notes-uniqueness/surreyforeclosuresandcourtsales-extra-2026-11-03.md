# Surrey Court-Ordered Sales — Extra Local Notes
Page: surreyforeclosuresandcourtsales (Surrey; court-ordered sales).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The shared BC court-ordered-sale process (petition, Order Nisi and redemption period, conduct of sale, court approval, Order Absolute) is written in full in the missionforeclosuresandcourtsales note — see the missionforeclosuresandcourtsales note. This note covers Surrey only.
- A Surrey foreclosure is filed at the New Westminster Registry, not in Surrey: in Samra Enterprises Inc. v. Lally, 2025 BCSC 1674, a foreclosure of land in South Surrey, the court file is New Westminster Registry Docket H250369 and the hearing was in New Westminster. https://www.bccourts.ca/jdb-txt/sc/25/16/2025BCSC1674.htm
- The venue rule treats two registries as one: section 21 (3) of the Law and Equity Act deems the Vancouver and New Westminster registries to be the same registry for foreclosure venue. https://ltpm.ltsa.ca/21-venue-foreclosure-proceedings

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- A Surrey foreclosure file can run for years before a sale completes: in the Samra file, the orders were made in sequence — Order Nisi of Foreclosure on July 26, 2023, Order for Conduct of Sale on April 23, 2024, and Order Approving Sale on March 25, 2025. https://www.bccourts.ca/jdb-txt/sc/25/16/2025BCSC1674.htm
- The Samra orders show the buyer's title being cleared by the approval order itself: the order approving sale directed that charges, liens, encumbrances, caveats, mortgages and certificates of pending litigation listed in it be cancelled for the purpose of issuing title to the mortgaged property. https://www.bccourts.ca/jdb-txt/sc/25/16/2025BCSC1674.htm

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Surrey's tax sale is a separate City process, not a foreclosure: the City states it is required by the Local Government Act to sell at tax sale all properties whose taxes have not been paid for three years. https://www.Surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/municipal-tax-sale
- The City sells at the Upset Price: the tax sale page states the Collector may sell a property for the Upset Price, which equals all outstanding taxes, penalties, interest, 5% tax sale costs, and Land Title Office fees. https://www.Surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/municipal-tax-sale
- The City gives no warranty on a tax sale property: the tax sale page states all property sales are "as is" without warranty or guarantee by the City. https://www.Surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/municipal-tax-sale

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which court registry holds the file for a Surrey foreclosure? **A:** The New Westminster Registry holds it, because Surrey has no Supreme Court registry of its own. A 2025 BC Supreme Court decision on a foreclosure of South Surrey land, Samra Enterprises Inc. v. Lally, is a New Westminster Registry file, Docket H250369, and the hearing was held in New Westminster, so file searches and hearings run through that registry. https://www.bccourts.ca/jdb-txt/sc/25/16/2025BCSC1674.htm
- **Q:** When is Surrey's tax sale, and how do I bid? **A:** Surrey holds its annual tax sale at 10:00 a.m. on the last Monday of September in Council Chambers at Surrey City Hall, 13450 104 Avenue. Bidders register at the Property & Payment Services counter at City Hall starting 8:30 a.m. that day, and registration closes at 9:59 a.m., with late registration not permitted. https://www.Surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/municipal-tax-sale
- **Q:** If I buy at the Surrey tax sale, is the property mine right away? **A:** No, the sale sits under a one-year redemption right. The City states the current owner has one year after the property is sold at tax sale to pay all taxes, interest, and charges to retain ownership of the property, so a tax sale purchaser waits out that year before the purchase is secure. https://www.Surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/municipal-tax-sale
- **Q:** How do I keep a Surrey property out of the tax sale list? **A:** Pay the delinquent taxes before the deadlines the City publishes. The City states properties that may be included in the tax sale are listed in the local newspaper the week before the sale, that paying delinquent taxes by September 2 avoids the newspaper listing, and that paying before 10:00 a.m. on tax sale day removes the property from the list. https://www.Surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/municipal-tax-sale

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Surrey publishes its tax sale list as a City document: the City's Notice of Tax Sale for Monday, September 29, 2025 lists each property by civic address and legal description, under section 645 of the Local Government Act. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Tax_Sale.pdf
- The City itself can bid at its tax sale: the 2025 notice states the Manager, Realty Services, or designate, authorized by Council under section 648 of the Local Government Act, may bid on behalf of the City on properties at the tax sale. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Tax_Sale.pdf
- A Surrey foreclosure file can be searched before bidding: Court Services Online eSearch covers Supreme Court civil files, "Foreclosure" is a selectable file class, and viewing one file's details costs $6.00. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The tax sale happens at Surrey City Hall: the City's 2025 notice places the auction in the Council Chambers at Surrey City Hall, 13450 104 Avenue, Surrey. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Tax_Sale.pdf
- The foreclosure hearing happens in New Westminster: the Samra foreclosure of South Surrey land was heard in New Westminster, B.C., where its registry file is kept. https://www.bccourts.ca/jdb-txt/sc/25/16/2025BCSC1674.htm

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- A lowland Surrey property can sit in the coastal floodplain: the City states its coastal floodplain covers about 20% of Surrey's land area, stretching from Boundary Bay and Mud Bay along the Nicomekl and Serpentine Rivers toward Cloverdale and Newton, and is subject to coastal and river flooding. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain
- Most of that floodplain sits behind dykes, not above the water: the City states most of Surrey's floodplain, coastal and inland, is protected by dykes, with sea dams on the Nicomekl and Serpentine Rivers first built in 1912 and 1913. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain
- A tax sale title carries the redemption risk for a year: the City states the owner may redeem within one year of the tax sale by paying all taxes, interest, and charges. https://www.Surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/municipal-tax-sale
