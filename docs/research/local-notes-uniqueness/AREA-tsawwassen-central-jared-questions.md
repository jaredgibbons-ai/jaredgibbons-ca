# Tsawwassen Central — Questions for Jared

For Jared to answer by voice memo. Muse does not answer these.

1. When a buyer says "Tsawwassen" but means the central part around 56 Street and 12 Avenue, what are they actually picturing — and how does it differ from what Beach Grove or English Bluff buyers want?
2. Who buys in central Tsawwassen — downsizers into the condos and townhomes near the Town Centre, families after the detached streets, or people leaving Ladner and Richmond?
3. What do buyers get wrong about central Tsawwassen before they look closely — the dike and flood question, flight paths, how far the beach really is?
4. Which product is hardest to buy well in Tsawwassen Central right now — the older detached homes, the low-rise condos, or the townhomes — and why?
5. Tell one story: a Tsawwassen deal that taught you something about the town that a buyer from outside Delta would never guess.
