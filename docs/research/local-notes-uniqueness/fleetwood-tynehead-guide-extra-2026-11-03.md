## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Fleetwood Town Centre is centred at Fraser Highway and 160 Street and 84 Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/RPT_2000-C016.pdf
- The Town Centre planning area is bounded by 88 Avenue north, 80 Avenue south, 156 Street west and 168 Street east. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/RPT_2000-C016.pdf
- Fleetwood is a designated Urban Centre in Metro Vancouver's Regional Growth Strategy (RGS). https://fleetwoodplan.surrey.ca/get-involved/widgets/65368/faqs
- Tynehead is the eastern part of the guide area toward 168 Street and Tynehead Regional Park; the City plan name for the whole area is Fleetwood. https://fleetwoodplan.surrey.ca/get-involved?tool=qanda

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** When does SkyTrain reach Fleetwood? **A:** The provincial project states service is expected at the end of 2029, with major construction started in November 2024. The Surrey-Langley SkyTrain (SLS) project extends the Expo Line 16 kilometres on an elevated guideway from King George Station to Langley City Centre, mainly along Fraser Highway, with eight new stations. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/04/SLS_Monthly-Status-Report_March_2026.pdf
**Q:** Which stations are in Fleetwood? **A:** Three stations serve this guide area in the project list: 152 Street, Fleetwood at 160 Street and Fraser Highway in the heart of Fleetwood Town Centre, and Bakerview-166 Street in east Fleetwood beside the Surrey Sports and Leisure Complex. The project also lists a bus exchange at Bakerview-166 Street. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/04/SLS_Monthly-Status-Report_March_2026.pdf
**Q:** Will there be a park-and-ride at a Fleetwood station? **A:** No dedicated park-and-ride lot is planned. The City's Fleetwood Plan answers state there will be on-street parking of about 300 spaces throughout the extension in Surrey, no dedicated lot, and TransLink is expected to update bus routes to connect with each new station once the line operates, with bus exchanges at some stations including Bakerview-166 Street. https://fleetwoodplan.surrey.ca/get-involved?tool=qanda
**Q:** Does the Fleetwood Plan force my house to be redeveloped? **A:** No, the City states redevelopment happens only with property owner consent and owners determine timing. The plan guides what the City would support over 30 years; areas near stations will likely change sooner, some areas may not redevelop at all, and change depends on willing buyers, sellers and developers coming together. https://fleetwoodplan.surrey.ca/get-involved/widgets/65368/faqs

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Fleetwood Station is described by the project as serving a community centre with library and gym facilities plus local retail at 160 Street. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/04/SLS_Monthly-Status-Report_March_2026.pdf
- 160 Street between 88 Avenue and 96 Avenue is planned for widening as a short-term priority, anticipated 2026-2027, with timelines subject to change. https://fleetwoodplan.surrey.ca/get-involved?tool=qanda
- No timelines have been determined for 156 Street improvements, per the City's Fleetwood Plan answers. https://fleetwoodplan.surrey.ca/get-involved?tool=qanda
- Early SkyTrain construction preparation has started in Fleetwood and will ramp up; the Province offers construction updates by email signup. https://fleetwoodplan.surrey.ca/get-involved?tool=qanda
- Fleetwood Park Secondary is one of six Surrey secondaries on an extended-day schedule to manage enrolment, and funding for a Fleetwood Park addition has been announced. https://www.surreyschools.ca/_ci/p/176056

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Surrey-Langley SkyTrain extension: 16 kilometres, eight stations, three transit exchanges, in-service target late 2029, major construction started November 2024 — full project entry is on this guide note as the area's defining project. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/04/SLS_Monthly-Status-Report_March_2026.pdf
- All eight stations were under construction by May 2026, with more than 30 percent of the elevated guideway complete, per the Province. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0022-000515

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Fraser Highway is the transit spine: the extension runs on it from King George Station through Fleetwood to Langley City Centre. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/04/SLS_Monthly-Status-Report_March_2026.pdf
- The project includes 14 kilometres of new separated multi-use pathways connecting to stations, per the federal-provincial release. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0022-000515
- Until opening in late 2029, Fraser Highway remains a bus corridor; station-area bus routes will change when the line opens. https://fleetwoodplan.surrey.ca/get-involved?tool=qanda
