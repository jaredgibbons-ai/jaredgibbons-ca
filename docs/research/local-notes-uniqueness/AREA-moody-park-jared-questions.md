# Moody Park — Questions for Jared

1. In Moody Park deals, what do you see differ between the older character houses on the streets facing the park — Eighth Street, Sixth Avenue, Tenth Avenue — and the smaller postwar houses toward Twelfth Street and Sixth Street?
2. Who buys in Moody Park: families targeting Lord Kelvin Elementary, downsizers who want Century House and the Uptown shops on Sixth Street close by, or buyers priced out of Queen's Park — and what do they say tipped it?
3. What sells fast here and what sits: do renovated park-facing houses move first, while unrenovated houses on busier Sixth Avenue or Eighth Street wait — and how do suites in the older houses change the math?
4. What surprises Moody Park buyers: that the SkyTrain stations are a downhill drive, not a walk, how much daily life runs through Moody Park Arena, the outdoor pool and Century House, or the hill grades on the numbered streets?
5. Tell one Moody Park story: a deal on a park-facing street where the park frontage, a heritage detail, or a suite changed the price, the subjects, or the outcome.
