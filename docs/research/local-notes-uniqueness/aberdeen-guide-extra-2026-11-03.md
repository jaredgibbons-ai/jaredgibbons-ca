# Aberdeen Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: aberdeen-guide (Aberdeen; Guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Aberdeen is a rural community in the City of Abbotsford around Bradner Road, south of Fraser Highway and west of Clearbrook, north of the Abbotsford International Airport (YXX) lands. https://aberdeen.abbyschools.ca/
- "Aberdeen" is an official City name as well as a board sub-area name: it is profile area 6, "Aberdeen," in the City's 2020 Demographic Profiles, separate from Bradner – Mt. Lehman (area 1) and West Abbotsford (area 7). https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2020%20McKee%20Profile%20-%20spreads%20final.pdf
- Bradner and Mt. Lehman lie west and northwest, Abbotsford West and Clearbrook east, Poplar northeast, and Aldergrove (Township of Langley) further west along Fraser Highway. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- No exact boundary roads for the Aberdeen sub-area are published in text form on a City source; the Bradner Road / Fraser Highway framing is the sourced orientation.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is single-detached houses on rural and agricultural lots; the area is rural in character with working farm parcels, under Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- Lot sizes are agricultural in scale: the minimum subdivision lot area is 8.0 hectares in the Agricultural One Zone (A1) and 16.0 hectares in the Agricultural Two Zone (A2). https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- Homes are effectively all freehold farm and rural residential lots; the only strata housing confirmed inside the wider board sub-area is the Lantern Park townhome complex at 27640 Lantern Avenue, at the western edge by the Aldergrove border.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City-wide Abbotsford zoning, Official Community Plan (OCP) and small-scale multi-unit housing rules are written in full in the abbotsford-homes-for-sale note; see that note for the city-wide frame.
- In the A1 zone, one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit is permitted per lot in accordance with the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the Agricultural Land Commission, on a lot of at least 0.4 hectares, to a maximum floor area of 90 square metres on lots of 40 hectares or less and 186 square metres on lots over 40 hectares, plus one secondary suite per lot to a maximum of 100 square metres. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- Land inside the Abbotsford Airport Zoning Regulations (AZR), SOR/83-253, shown on City drawing M-500, is subject to height limits, and NAV CANADA and Transport Canada approvals are required before the City issues a building permit on AZR land. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- Zoning Bylaw Schedule E sets floodplain boundaries; whether a single Aberdeen lot is in the floodplain is a lot-by-lot check in the City's WebMap. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is inside Aberdeen? **A:** Aberdeen Elementary at 2975 Bradner Road is the public elementary school physically inside the area. A School District No. 34 (SD34) School Locator run for that address returned Aberdeen Elementary, then Eugene Reimer Middle, then Rick Hansen Secondary. Catchments are set under SD34 Policy 18 and are address-specific, so any other Aberdeen address needs its own locator run. https://aberdeen.abbyschools.ca/
- **Q:** Can a second home be added on an Aberdeen farm lot? **A:** One additional farm dwelling can be permitted, within limits. The A1 zone allows one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot of at least 0.4 hectares under the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation or with Agricultural Land Commission approval, capped at 90 square metres on lots of 40 hectares or less and 186 square metres on larger lots, plus one secondary suite per lot to 100 square metres. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Q:** Does the airport restrict building here? **A:** It can, on the south side of the area near the airport lands. Where a lot is inside the Airport Zoning Regulation area on City drawing M-500, building height may not exceed the approach, outer or transitional surfaces, and NAV CANADA and Transport Canada approvals are needed before a City building permit. Check the lot against drawing M-500 before planning a tall barn or shop. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- **Q:** Is there bus service through Aberdeen? **A:** No route runs through the interior rural roads. BC Transit route 21 (Aldergrove / Abbotsford) runs on Fraser Highway along the north edge of the area, between Aldergrove and Bourquin Exchange via Highstreet Exchange. Aberdeen addresses otherwise rely on the Fraser Highway stops at the area's north edge. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=21&schedule=2026-04-05

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Aberdeen Elementary, 2975 Bradner Road, is the only school confirmed physically inside the area; there is no middle or secondary school inside Aberdeen. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/034
- No City park inside Aberdeen was verified by name and address on the City parks list; the nearest verified City facilities are Matsqui Recreation Centre, 3106 Clearbrook Road, and Clearbrook Park, 3680 Clearbrook Road, to the east. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds
- The nearest libraries are Fraser Valley Regional Library branches outside the area: Mt. Lehman Library, 5875 Mt. Lehman Road, and Clearbrook Library, 32320 George Ferguson Way. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/
- Rural lots outside the urban boundary may be on wells and onsite sewage rather than community water and sewer; the City does not charge water or sewer utility fees to properties not connected to City infrastructure. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing
- The nearest hospital is Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre, 32900 Marshall Road, a Fraser Health hospital with 24-hour emergency service. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre
- Huntingdon Road and King Road run south from the area toward Abbotsford International Airport and the Huntingdon / Sumas border crossing corridor. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Aberdeen owners pay City of Abbotsford taxes: the City's 2026 general municipal residential rate is 1.08284 dollars per $1,000 of taxable value, before school, regional and other levies — city-wide detail in the abbotsford-homes-for-sale note. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Summary%20May%2022.pdf
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in Abbotsford: the Province's taxable-areas list names the City of Abbotsford. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 21 Fraser Hwy is the area's bus service, on Fraser Highway at the north edge, between Aldergrove and Bourquin Exchange via Highstreet Exchange. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=21&schedule=2026-04-05

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the lot's zone and floodplain designation in the City's WebMap before offering: Zoning Bylaw Schedule E floodplain boundaries are lot-specific and do not follow from the street address alone. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- For a well, look up the well record in the Province's GWELLS registry; for septic, Fraser Health states an onsite sewerage system must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation and be filed by an Authorized Person. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- On Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) parcels, subdivision, non-farm use and added dwellings need both City zoning compliance and, where the ALR Use Regulation requires it, Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR status is parcel-specific. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Airport height surfaces are a separate check from zoning: a lot can be zoned for a tall farm building and still be capped by the Airport Zoning Regulations on City drawing M-500. https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/sor-83-253/FullText.html?wbdisable=true
