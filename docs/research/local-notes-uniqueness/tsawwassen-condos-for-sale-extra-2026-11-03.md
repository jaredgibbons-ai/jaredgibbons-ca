## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Are Southlands condo flats leasehold? **A:** No — they are freehold strata. Century Group's Southlands FAQ states every Southlands home is freehold, never leasehold, and that condo flats at Market Commons and the other Southlands buildings are freehold strata homes whose monthly fee covers common-area maintenance, landscaping and snow removal. https://discoversouthlands.ca/faq/
- **Q:** Where are the new rental apartments in Tsawwassen? **A:** Clara Apartments, at 1467 56 Street, is a purpose-built rental building under construction near Tsawwassen Town Centre. Its site lists 48 homes — studios, one- and two-bedrooms — with completion and first move-ins stated as Spring 2027. The City also notes periodic single-lane closures on 56 Street, between 9 a.m. and 3 p.m., during that construction work. https://claraapartments.ca/
- **Q:** Which schools serve a 56 Street condo? **A:** South Delta Secondary is the public secondary school for Tsawwassen addresses. Delta School District No. 37 publishes Tsawwassen-area catchment maps and notes boundaries are reviewed and occasionally adjusted, so verify a building's exact address in the district School Locator before enrolment. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/student-registration/catchment-area-maps/
- **Q:** Is a home on Tsawwassen First Nation lands taxed like a Delta condo? **A:** No — treaty lands are outside the Speculation and Vacancy Tax areas. The Province states reserve lands, treaty lands and lands of self-governing Indigenous Nations are not part of the taxable areas, while the City of Delta is listed as taxable. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Bus route 620 links Bridgeport Station (Canada Line) directly to the Tsawwassen ferry terminal. BC Ferries' own directions tell foot passengers to take the Canada Line to Bridgeport, then the 620 bus to the terminal. https://www.bcferries.com/travel-boarding/coach-bus-shuttle
