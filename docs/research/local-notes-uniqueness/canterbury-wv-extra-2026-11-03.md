# Canterbury, West Vancouver — Extra Local Notes
Page: canterbury-wv (Canterbury; West Vancouver neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Canterbury is a residential pocket on the British Properties hillside in the District of West Vancouver, above Ambleside and the Upper Levels Highway (Highway 1). https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- Canterbury is not a named neighbourhood in the District's Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 4985, 2018; the OCP's centres are the Ambleside Municipal Town Centre, the Park Royal regional shopping centre, and the village centres of Dundarave, Caulfeild Village and Horseshoe Bay, and in District planning terms this hillside sits inside the wider British Properties residential area. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- The neighbouring areas are Chartwell to the east, also in the British Properties, Ambleside to the south below the highway, Westcot and the lower British Properties to the west, Sentinel Hill to the south-west, and the upper British Properties benches to the north. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The confirmed stock is detached houses on hillside lots in the RS single-family dwelling zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- Minimum site areas in Section 200 of the bylaw are 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, with RS6 using the area of the site of record; the larger RS1 and RS2 minimums are the ones associated with the British Properties benches. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- No condominium building or townhome complex inside Canterbury was confirmed on a District, developer or strata source; the stock confirmed here is freehold detached houses. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- Secondary suites and a detached secondary suite are among the permitted uses the bylaw lists for the single-family zones. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- District-wide West Vancouver plan, tax and utility treatment is in the west-vancouver-homes-for-sale note; see the west-vancouver-homes-for-sale note.
- The lot-by-lot question in Canterbury is which RS zone applies, because minimum site areas run from 488 m² in RS5 to 8,094 m² in RS1; the District's Zoning Map confirms the zone for a specific lot. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- The bylaw's ground-oriented dwelling rules allow 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH): the Province requires zoning to allow a minimum of 4 dwelling units on single-family and duplex parcels greater than 280 m², and a minimum of 6 units on such parcels greater than 280 m² near frequent bus service, defined as bus stops with at least one route stopping at least every 15 minutes on average. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/local-governments-and-housing/housing-initiatives/smale-scale-multi-unit-housing
- On July 21, 2025, the Province directed West Vancouver to amend its OCP to provide increased density in the single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave, and at Park Royal–Taylor Way, by December 31, 2025 — check current; Canterbury sits above Ambleside, so whether its lowest streets fall inside the adjacent areas must be verified per property. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Canterbury an official West Vancouver neighbourhood? **A:** No — Canterbury is not a named neighbourhood in the District's Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. The plan's centres are Ambleside, Park Royal, Dundarave, Caulfeild Village and Horseshoe Bay, and in District planning terms this hillside sits inside the wider British Properties residential area. Buyers use Canterbury and British Properties as search names, but permits and plan rules are set at District level for the lot. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- **Q:** Which school will a Canterbury address feed into? **A:** Run the exact address through the School District No. 45 (SD45) school locator — catchments are address-based and no Canterbury catchment is confirmed for a sample address. The nearby schools are Chartwell Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 7, at 1300 Chartwell Drive, Sentinel Secondary, Grades 8 to 12, at 1250 Chartwell Drive, and Westcot Elementary at 760 Westcot Road. SD45 has no middle-school tier. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/ https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/chartwell/
- **Q:** Can I add a suite, a coach house or extra dwellings on a Canterbury lot? **A:** Two District rules apply to the lot. Section 200 of Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses in the single-family zones, and the ground-oriented rules allow 4 dwellings maximum per lot, or 6 if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop. The lot's RS zone, shown on the District Zoning Map, decides which figures apply. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **Q:** Does the Province's 2025 density directive affect a Canterbury lot? **A:** It may, and only District mapping for the specific lot settles it. On July 21, 2025 the Province directed West Vancouver to amend its Official Community Plan to provide increased density in single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave, and at Park Royal–Taylor Way, by December 31, 2025. Canterbury sits above Ambleside, so whether its lowest streets fall inside the adjacent areas must be verified per property — check current. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698
- **Q:** What hazard permits can affect work on a Canterbury hillside lot? **A:** Yes — two permit triggers are measured from natural features on or near the lot. An Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit is required for work within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface. A Foreshore Development Permit covers waterfront and coastal-flood-risk properties and does not ordinarily reach this upper hillside. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- School District No. 45 (SD45), West Vancouver Schools, has no middle-school tier: elementary is Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary is Grade 8 to Grade 12. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/
- Chartwell Elementary, at 1300 Chartwell Drive, is described by SD45 as situated in the British Properties overlooking Vancouver and Burrard Inlet, and Sentinel Secondary, at 1250 Chartwell Drive, sits beside it. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/chartwell/
- Westcot Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 7, at 760 Westcot Road, is described by SD45 as situated at the foot of the British Properties, west of Canterbury. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/
- No park inside Canterbury was confirmed on a District source; the closest named District waterfront park is Dundarave Park, 150 25th Street, west along the waterfront below the hillside. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/dundarave-park
- The Centennial Seawalk was built in 1967 for Canada's centennial and runs roughly 1.7 km east from Dundarave Park to Ambleside, below the Canterbury hillside. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/dundarave-park
- The District facilities serving Canterbury are downhill in Ambleside: the West Vancouver Community Centre and Aquatic Centre at 2121 Marine Drive, and West Vancouver Memorial Library at 1950 Marine Drive. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/facilities/community-centre https://westvancouver.ca/arts-culture/west-vancouver-memorial-library
- Park Royal, at Marine Drive and Taylor Way east and downhill of Canterbury, is recognized in the District's OCP as the regional shopping centre, and Ambleside is the Municipal Town Centre; no shopping inside Canterbury itself was confirmed on a District source. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Wildfire interface: a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit is required for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface; check the specific lot on the District's development-permits page, which states the trigger distance but does not confirm whether a given Canterbury lot is inside the area. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- Watercourses: an Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank, so creek-side hillside lots need that check before a rebuild or addition is planned. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- Foreshore and coastal flooding: a Foreshore Development Permit is required for work within the Foreshore Development Permit Area, for waterfront property or other properties at risk of coastal flooding, which does not ordinarily reach this upper hillside. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- Title covenants: whether a Canterbury lot carries British Properties building-scheme or covenant restrictions beyond District zoning sits on the individual title, not in the zoning bylaw — pull the title for the specific lot; the bylaw alone does not state any covenant term. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- Zone confirmation: confirm the lot's RS zone and minimum site area on the District's Zoning Map before relying on suite, coach-house or subdivision potential; the street's other homes do not state the entitlement. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
