# Steelhead Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: steelhead-mission-guide (Steelhead; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Steelhead is a rural community in northwest Mission, City of Mission, Fraser Valley Regional District, in the Stave Lake / Stave River uplands north of Silverdale and west of the town core, reached via Stave Lake Street and Dewdney Trunk Road toward Stave Falls.
- The City's own application files locate Steelhead by address, e.g. 33105 Thomas Avenue is filed as "LOCATION: Steelhead" in a City rezoning package. https://www.mission.ca/sites/default/files/2024-03/Information%20Package%20-%20Notice%20of%20Rezoning%20Bylaw%20-%20R18-023.pdf
- Draft OCP engagement identified Rural Centres in both Stave Falls and Steelhead, where limited commercial growth could support local needs. https://engage.mission.ca/media/file/phase-2-engagement-public-workbook

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Zoning is under City of Mission Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020; secondary dwellings in Steelhead's rural zones are not automatic — the Thomas Avenue file required rezoning from RU36 to RR7s to allow a secondary dwelling on each new lot. https://www.mission.ca/sites/default/files/2024-03/Information%20Package%20-%20Notice%20of%20Rezoning%20Bylaw%20-%20R18-023.pdf
- A 2026 City report on shipping containers grouped Steelhead with Stave Falls, Ferndale and Keystone as larger-lot rural areas, and recommended limiting residential shipping-container storage to lots of 0.2 hectares (0.49 acres) or larger in suburban and rural zones. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rao26-2

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Steelhead on city water and sewer? **A:** Plan for private services until you prove otherwise for the specific lot. Steelhead is a rural area outside Mission's serviced town core, and the City files read here deal in rural zones (RU36, RR7s) with secondary dwellings, not urban servicing extensions. Get a well potability/flow test and a septic inspection by an authorized person as subjects. https://www.mission.ca/sites/default/files/2024-03/Information%20Package%20-%20Notice%20of%20Rezoning%20Bylaw%20-%20R18-023.pdf
- **Q:** Can each lot have a second home? **A:** Only if the zone says so. At 33105 Thomas Avenue the owner applied to rezone from RU36 to RR7s specifically "to allow for the subdivision of single family lots with a secondary dwelling on each lot." A plain RU36 lot should not be assumed to carry that right. https://www.mission.ca/sites/default/files/2024-03/Information%20Package%20-%20Notice%20of%20Rezoning%20Bylaw%20-%20R18-023.pdf
- **Q:** What is the dam and lake context? **A:** Steelhead sits in the Stave system: Stave Lake Reservoir is held by Stave Falls Dam, Hayward Lake Reservoir below it is held by Ruskin Dam, and BC Hydro warns that water releases create dangerous currents above and below both dams. Recreation rules differ by lake — Hayward is canoes and non-motorized boats (electric motors permitted), Stave permits motorized boats. https://www.bchydro.com/community/recreation_areas/stave_lake.html?fixZoomMobile=true
- **Q:** Is there a bus to Steelhead? **A:** No BC Transit route is named for Steelhead. Mission routes from Mission City Station are 31, 32, 33, 34, 35 and 39, plus TransLink 701 and the West Coast Express; a car is the practical link from Steelhead to that station. Check BC Transit for any rural stop on Stave Lake Street before relying on it. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Construction of Stave Falls Dam and Powerhouse began in 1909 and was completed by January 1, 1912; Ruskin Dam construction began in 1929, 5.6 km downstream, creating Hayward Lake Reservoir. https://www.BChydro.com/community/recreation_areas/ruskin_dam.html
- The Power House at Stave Falls Visitor Centre, Hayward Lake recreation area and Stave Lake boat launch are all about 60 km east of Vancouver and are run as BC Hydro recreation areas. https://www.bchydro.com/community/recreation_areas/hayward_lake.html
- The Stave Lake Reservoir lodge and campsite is closed indefinitely while the area and structures undergo an engineering and structural assessment, per BC Hydro. https://www.bchydro.com/community/recreation_areas/stave_lake.html?fixZoomMobile=true
- Steelhead Falls is a named viewpoint on the Hayward Lake reservoir trail system in BC Hydro's recreation area network. https://www.bchydro.com/community/recreation_areas/hayward_lake.html
- School District 75 (Mission) serves Steelhead; there is no school located in Steelhead named on the pages read, so families should verify catchment and bus routing by exact address with the district.
- A January 2000 upgrade to Stave Falls Dam and Powerhouse was completed to increase efficiency and ensure long-term hydroelectric service. https://www.bchydro.com/community/recreation_areas/hayward_lake.html

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Road access is Stave Lake Street / Dewdney Trunk Road south to Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and Mission City Station; drive times UNCONFIRMED.
- West Coast Express from Mission City Station runs weekday mornings to Waterfront, Vancouver, and weekday afternoons back, with no weekend or statutory-holiday service. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- Ruskin Dam is a road crossing of the Stave River between Mission (Stave Falls side) and Maple Ridge (Ruskin side), downstream of Hayward Lake. https://www.BChydro.com/community/recreation_areas/ruskin_dam.html

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Dam and reservoir safety: BC Hydro states hydroelectric operations create special concerns — avoid designated hazard areas above and below Stave Falls and Ruskin Dams, obey posted signs, and note swimming, boating or fishing in those areas is extremely dangerous because of releases and currents. https://www.bchydro.com/community/recreation_areas/stave_lake.html?fixZoomMobile=true
- Hayward Lake rule: canoeing or swimming within 1 km of Ruskin Dam is prohibited in BC Hydro's recreation guidance for the reservoir system — confirm current signage on site. https://www.bchydro.com/community/recreation_areas/hayward_lake.html
- Fraser flood maps do not settle a Steelhead lot: the Province's 2026 floodplain release covers the Fraser from Yale to Mission, but Steelhead's local risks are slope, creek and reservoir-related — get lot-level geotechnical and riparian advice. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026WLRS0029-000715
- Septic/well: no City page read confirms servicing for a given Steelhead lot; require well and septic records and Fraser Health / authorized-person inspection as subjects.
