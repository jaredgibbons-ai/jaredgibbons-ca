# Oxford Heights Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: oxford-heights-port-coquitlam (Oxford Heights; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Oxford Heights is the north-side Port Coquitlam neighbourhood around Oxford Street, Coquitlam Avenue and the Oxford Connector, west of Coast Meridian Road. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt173.pdf
- Castle Park Elementary at 1144 Confederation Drive is the named school address used for the Oxford Heights catchment run in section 5. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-coquitlam
- The Oxford Connector is the road link from Oxford Street west to Shaughnessy Street and Lougheed Highway, and it is the western edge most drivers use for this area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt173.pdf
- Exact board sub-area boundary roads are not published by the board, so street-level edges here are UNCONFIRMED — see NOT CONFIRMED.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Oxford Heights is a detached-house pocket: its September 2026 board figures are not used here because this program carries no listing or sales data in these notes — see NOT CONFIRMED for what could not be sourced.
- The housing form is set by the Residential Small Scale (RS) zones in Zoning Bylaw No. 3630, the same bylaw that sets RS3 minimum lot width at 30 metres, the widest of the four RS families. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf
- Confederation Drive and Castle Crescent are the Castle Park street names around Castle Park Elementary, the subdivision streets at the centre of this pocket. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-coquitlam

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- An Oxford Heights RS lot is inside the Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) Development Permit Area, which covers RS1, RS2, RS3, RS4 and Comprehensive Development zones under OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 4373. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11285
- The SSMUH permit checklist requires a site survey prepared by a British Columbia Land Surveyor, so an infill plan on an Oxford Street lot starts with a survey, not a sketch. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/application-form-dp-ssmuh-2024
- Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) size and siting rules for RS lots are covered in full in the mary-hill-port-coquitlam note in this cluster — see that note.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools does a Confederation Drive address feed? **A:** Castle Park Elementary is the catchment elementary for 1144 Confederation Drive. A School District 43 (SD43) School Locator run for that address returned Castle Park Elementary, École Citadel Middle and École Riverside Secondary; catchments are address-based, so confirm any Oxford Heights home by its exact address in the locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-coquitlam
- **Q:** How do I get downtown without a car from Oxford Heights? **A:** By bus to a train station, not by train from the neighbourhood itself. TransLink routes 173 Cedar and 174 Rocklin run the loop through Oxford Street, Coquitlam Avenue and Coast Meridian Road to Port Coquitlam Station and Coquitlam Central Station, where the West Coast Express and SkyTrain connect. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt173.pdf
- **Q:** Is Oxford Heights in the floodplain like the south side? **A:** The City's flood warning is city-wide, not neighbourhood-specific. The City states a significant portion of Port Coquitlam is in the Fraser or Pitt River flood plain behind dykes; Oxford Heights sits on the raised north side, but no City page read gives this neighbourhood a flood clearance, so check the specific lot on the City flood page. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/city-port-coquitlam-prepares-potential-flooding
- **Q:** Can an Oxford Heights lot take more than one home? **A:** Yes, in principle, under the SSMUH rules for RS zones. The lot still needs a Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing development permit, and the City's application form lists options including a new house with secondary suite and Accessory Dwelling Unit, a duplex, a triplex, or an ADU only. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/application-form-dp-ssmuh-2024
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The 173/174 loop is one circular service split in two: westbound 173 trips continue as 174 Rocklin trips at Victoria Drive, running via Cedar Drive, Prairie Avenue, Coast Meridian Road, Coquitlam Avenue, Oxford Street and the Oxford Connector. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt173.pdf
- École Citadel Middle at 1265 Citadel Drive is the middle school returned for the Confederation Drive address, in the Citadel pocket north-west of Oxford Heights. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-coquitlam
- The City's 2026 capital list names Oxford Connector resurfacing from the Esso station to Lougheed Highway, a current road-work item on this area's main exit. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects?construction_year=All&page=2
- The same capital list names 2026 streetlights on Eastern Drive at Western Drive and at Pooley Avenue, on the Citadel edge above Oxford Heights. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects?construction_year=All&page=2
- Minnekhada Middle is not the middle school for the Confederation Drive run — Citadel Middle is — a common wrong assumption for north-side Port Coquitlam addresses. http://sd43.bc.ca/cross-catchment

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Port Coquitlam utility bills are flat annual fees, due March 31, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after March 31, 2026 and a further 5 percent after June 1, 2026. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills
- The 2026 annual water rate for a single residential dwelling unit is $646.00 and the annual sewer rate for a single residential dwelling unit is $648.00. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Routes 173 Cedar and 174 Rocklin are the Oxford Heights buses, running daily as a combined circular service between this area, Port Coquitlam Station and Coquitlam Central Station, per TransLink's timetable effective June 8, 2026. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt173.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Look the lot up on the City's flood resources before subjects are removed: the City publishes flood plain maps and preparedness resources at portcoquitlam.ca/flood, and states the dike level was raised throughout the community in 2007. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/city-port-coquitlam-prepares-potential-flooding
- The City's flood modelling, in its Flood Mapping Update Report, shows protection against the 1:200-year flood event but dike overtopping predicted for the 1:500-year event with 1 metre of sea level rise by 2100 — the page does not give an Oxford Heights lot a pass or a fail. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/FileStream.ashx?DocumentId=5334
- A Maple Creek Drainage Pump Station design item appears in City capital planning for the north side drainage system; a buyer near a north-side creek should ask the City which pump station drains the lot. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=2906
