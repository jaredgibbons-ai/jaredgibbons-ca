# Columbia Valley — Extra Local Notes
Page: columbia-valley (Columbia Valley; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Columbia Valley is in Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area H, not in the City of Chilliwack, and Electoral Area H consists of Cultus Lake and Columbia Valley. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11249
- Electoral Area H borders Electoral Area E to the west, the City of Chilliwack to the north, the City of Abbotsford to the west, and the United States border to the south. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11249
- Columbia Valley sits south of Cultus Lake along Columbia Valley Highway, reaching toward the United States border, and is distinct from Cultus Lake Park itself. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Area%20H.pdf
- The FVRD's Electoral Area H director page groups the area as Columbia Valley, Cultus Lake and Lindell Beach together under one director. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/contact/your-electoral-area-director.html
- Columbia Valley Road is the road the FVRD evacuation guide names as serving much of the populated area around Cultus Lake. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Area%20H.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Columbia Valley housing sits inside an electoral-area planning system, not a city zoning system: the FVRD's planning page lists the Official Community Plan (OCP) for Electoral Areas E and H, Bylaw No. 1115, covering Columbia Valley, Lindell Beach and the Chilliwack River Valley, and the consolidated Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021 for the electoral areas. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws/planning-land-use.html
- The FVRD states that Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021 consolidates the nine existing zoning regulations for all of its electoral areas into one region-wide electoral area zoning bylaw, standardising regulations without significantly altering the regulations for a particular parcel or zone type. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2022/consolidated-zoning-bylaw-approved.html?media=contrast
- No FVRD, provincial or federal source read for this note publishes a Columbia Valley-wide build decade, lot-size or style profile, so none is claimed; shared Columbia Valley guide facts are in full in the columbia-valley-guide note — see the columbia-valley-guide note. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws/planning-land-use.html
- A buyer should read the specific lot's zone under Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021 rather than assume a city-style residential zone applies. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws/planning-land-use.html

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The plan for this area is the FVRD Official Community Plan for Electoral Areas E and H, Bylaw No. 1115, 2011, as amended, which guides land-use planning and management for Cultus Lake and Columbia Valley in Electoral Area H. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws/planning-land-use.html https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11249
- Small-scale multi-unit housing is an open FVRD work item, not a settled Columbia Valley rule in the sources read: the FVRD's December 2025 electoral-area services papers include a small-scale multi-unit housing implementation and servicing review, with a direction to bring forward an implementation strategy addressing potential groundwater risks in early 2026. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/Meeting.aspx?Id=7b549cf8-d9da-4976-a7cf-79fe2f1719cd&Agenda=Agenda&lang=English&Item=46&Tab=attachments
- Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) rules matter here because the FVRD's Area H evacuation guide notes that a development of emergency access routes through ALR land would require an application to the Agricultural Land Commission to approve ALR land as a transportation corridor. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Area%20H.pdf
- A buyer should confirm the lot's ALR status on the Agricultural Land Commission map and its zone under Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021 before assuming a second dwelling or subdivision is permitted. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws/planning-land-use.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Columbia Valley in the City of Chilliwack? **A:** No, it is in the Fraser Valley Regional District. The FVRD's Official Community Plan amendment record states that Electoral Area H consists of Cultus Lake and Columbia Valley, and the FVRD director page groups Columbia Valley with Cultus Lake and Lindell Beach under Electoral Area H. That means services, bylaws and tax bills come from the regional district and the Province, not from Chilliwack City Hall. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11249 https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/contact/your-electoral-area-director.html
- **Q:** Who picks up garbage in Columbia Valley? **A:** The FVRD does not provide it here. The FVRD facilities page states the FVRD does not provide garbage and recycling services in Electoral Areas D, E or H, and that residents may arrange pickup through a private waste hauler or take garbage to the Bailey Landfill in Chilliwack. A buyer should therefore confirm private hauler availability for the specific road before assuming curbside collection. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/garbage-recycling/facilities.html
- **Q:** Is there one road in and out? **A:** In practical terms, yes for the lake-side populated area. The FVRD's Electoral Area H evacuation guide states that Columbia Valley Road, serving much of the populated area around Cultus Lake, provides one-way in and one-way out access only, and that communities on the southern end of Cultus Lake may be unable to use Columbia Valley Highway in an emergency. A buyer should read the evacuation guide for the specific property. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Area%20H.pdf
- **Q:** Is there a fixed bus into Columbia Valley? **A:** No fixed route into Columbia Valley itself is stated in the BC Transit Chilliwack Rider's Guide read for this note. The guide's seasonal Route 56 Cultus Lake serves Cultus Lake in the July 1 to Labour Day period, which is the lake end of the corridor, not the Columbia Valley side south of the lake, so a buyer should plan on driving and confirm any seasonal service at the time of purchase. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chw_rg_jan2024-V1.1.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Columbia Valley Community Hall at 1202 Kosikar Road is the named community hall for this valley, used for Columbia Valley ratepayers' meetings in the FVRD director's Area H updates. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/2022-Fall-Update-FVRD-Letterhead.pdf
- The Columbia Valley Volunteer Fire Department is the named emergency response organisation for the valley in the FVRD's Area H evacuation guide, alongside the Cultus Lake Volunteer Fire Department, BC Ambulance, the Royal Canadian Mounted Police and Search and Rescue. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Area%20H.pdf
- Electoral Area H spans nearly 110 square kilometres and includes Columbia Valley, Cultus Lake and Lindell Beach, with a permanent population of over 2,500 people largely located in and around Cultus Lake, per the FVRD evacuation guide. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Area%20H.pdf
- Parking on Columbia Valley Highway is prohibited because it presents a hazard to drivers and pedestrians and prevents emergency services from moving safely, per the FVRD's Cultus Lake parking notice, so a buyer should not assume highway-shoulder parking is available at a lake-side home. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2022/plan-ahead-for-long-weekend-parking-at-cultus-lake.html
- The Cultus Lake Park office, at 4165 Columbia Valley Highway, is the administration office for Cultus Lake Park, a separate jurisdiction from the Columbia Valley electoral-area lots south of the park. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Welcome-to-Cultus-Lake-Park-2022.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The FVRD's 2026 Area H residential utility sheet lists only Cultus Lake service areas: Water – Cultus Lake is listed as no utility billing, Sewer – Cultus Lake North is a flat rate of $1,369.00, and Sewer – Cultus Lake South is listed as no utility billing, with utility bills issued annually in January in arrears through utility billing user fees in addition to taxation. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html
- No Columbia Valley water or sewer service area is listed on that 2026 Area H sheet, so a Columbia Valley buyer should confirm whether the specific lot is on a private well and septic system and budget for that servicing rather than assume an FVRD utility connection. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html
- Garbage is a private cost here because the FVRD does not provide garbage and recycling services in Electoral Area H, as stated on its facilities page. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/garbage-recycling/facilities.html
- The Province requires owners of residential property in a designated taxable area to file a speculation and vacancy tax declaration every year by March 31; whether a specific Columbia Valley electoral-area lot falls in a designated taxable area is unconfirmed in the sources read for this note — see NOT CONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/speculation-and-vacancy-tax/declaration

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Columbia Valley Highway is the corridor out of the valley north toward Cultus Lake and Chilliwack, and it is the highway named in the FVRD's Cultus Lake parking notice as the route where parking is prohibited. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2022/plan-ahead-for-long-weekend-parking-at-cultus-lake.html
- The FVRD evacuation guide states Columbia Valley Road provides one-way in and one-way out access for much of the populated area around Cultus Lake, and notes limited alternative emergency egress routes were explored in a 2012 traffic study. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Area%20H.pdf
- Seasonal bus service reaches Cultus Lake only in the BC Transit guide: Route 56 Cultus Lake is listed for the July 1 to Labour Day period, and no fixed route into Columbia Valley itself is stated in that guide. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chw_rg_jan2024-V1.1.pdf
- No source read for this note states a measured drive time from Columbia Valley to Chilliwack, Cultus Lake or the United States border, so none is claimed. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Area%20H.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Read the FVRD Electoral Area H evacuation guide for the specific property before removing subjects: the guide covers evacuation assumptions, narrow road corridors, traffic congestion, and the single-access character of the Columbia Valley Road area. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Area%20H.pdf
- Confirm water and sewer servicing lot by lot: the 2026 Area H utility sheet lists only Cultus Lake service areas, so a Columbia Valley lot may depend on a private well and septic system whose condition and capacity need their own checks. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html
- Check wildfire interface and FireSmart readiness: the FVRD director's Area H updates record FireSmart demonstrations for the area, including a demonstration site at Twin Alders across from the Cultus Lake Community Hall, and the FVRD's preparedness materials list Columbia Valley among its electoral-area communities. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/May-Area-H-Report.pdf
- Check ALR status and zone under Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021 on the Agricultural Land Commission map and the FVRD planning page, because rural lots here can carry both ALR and rural-zone constraints. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws/planning-land-use.html
