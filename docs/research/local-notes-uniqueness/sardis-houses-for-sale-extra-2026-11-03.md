# Sardis Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: sardis-houses-for-sale (Sardis; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sardis is a Chilliwack neighbourhood south of Highway 1 and the Vedder River, under the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan, Schedule B5 of the 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- The plan borders it with Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) land west and east, First Nations lands south and east, and Vedder south. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Vedder Road is the corridor plans use to divide east and west Sardis. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Garrison Crossing (Tamihi Way, Market Way, Chehalis Drive) is in east Sardis; Promontory is its southeast hillside. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2246

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Older streets east of Vedder Road use R1-A and R3: R3 (Small Lot Residential) sets a 360-square-metre minimum lot, 12-metre width, and permits an accessory dwelling unit. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- Garrison Crossing houses use CD-9: minimum lots of 240 square metres detached, 480 duplex, 9-metre width. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- West of Vedder Road, West Evans, between Stevenson Road and Evans Road, is ALR along the urban boundary. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Promontory-edge houses use HR (Hillside Residential): 0.2-hectare minimum lot with community water. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Sardis sits on the Vedder River alluvial fan, and the OCP's fan permit area covers Sardis, including Garrison Crossing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1372
- Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 designates the Vedder floodplain and fans as floodplain; the setback is 75 metres from the Vedder River's natural boundary. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Sardis Secondary or G.W. Graham? **A:** It depends on the middle school. The District No. 33 locator for 45501 Market Way returned Watson Elementary, then a choice of Mount Slesse or Vedder Middle, then G.W. Graham or Sardis Secondary matching that choice. Run the exact address. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q:** Is a Sardis house on a flood fan? **A:** Most of Sardis sits on the Vedder River alluvial fan. The OCP carries a permit area for the fan, and Bylaw No. 4519 designates the fan as floodplain, with a 75-metre setback and floors set by the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- **Q:** How are Garrison Crossing houses different? **A:** They were built under one comprehensive zone, CD-9, not the standard R1-A or R3 zones. CD-9 sets minimums — 240 square metres for a detached house lot, 170 for a rowhouse — and allows townhouse and apartment forms under R4 and R5 standards. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q:** Can I add a suite? **A:** Yes, on small-lot streets: R3 lists an accessory dwelling unit as a permitted ancillary use. City-wide small-scale multi-unit rules (see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note) allow up to four units on qualifying lots. Check the flood design first, because fan rules apply there. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
