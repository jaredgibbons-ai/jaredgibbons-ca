# Upper Deer Lake — Extra Local Notes
Page: upper-deer-lake-burnaby (Upper Deer Lake; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Upper Deer Lake is the residential slope on the south side of Deer Lake, between the lake and Kingsway: its working streets are Sperling Avenue, Canada Way, Deer Lake Avenue, Burris Street and Buckingham Avenue. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt144.pdf
- The civic anchor sits inside the pocket, not downtown: Burnaby City Hall is at 4949 Canada Way, and TransLink's route 144 timetable names a Burnaby City Hall bus loop on Deer Lake Parkway. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt144.pdf
- Deer Lake Park, at 5435 Sperling Avenue, forms the pocket's north edge, with the Burnaby Art Gallery, Burnaby Village Museum and Shadbolt Centre for the Arts on the lake's north side. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Lakeshore and creek lots are the constrained ones: Burnaby's Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area (SPEA) guidelines apply to any parcel containing a stream or within 30 metres of the top of bank of a stream, and the City's streams guide extends the same leave-area logic to freshwater shorelines and wetlands. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-06/Streamside-Protection-and-Enhancement-Development-Permit-Area-Guidelines.pdf
- City-wide zoning, including the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) district and the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 in effect July 1, 2026 — see the vancouver-heights-burnaby-houses-for-sale note. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is City Hall really in this neighbourhood? **A:** Yes, the civic campus is part of the pocket's daily geography. Burnaby City Hall stands at 4949 Canada Way, TransLink's route 144 timetable lists a Burnaby City Hall bus loop on Deer Lake Parkway, and the same route stops on Deer Lake Avenue at the Shadbolt Centre for the Arts before continuing to Canada Way and Burris Street. Expect civic traffic on Canada Way on council and event days. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt144.pdf
- **Q:** Which secondary school is beside the lake? **A:** Burnaby Central Secondary, Grades 8 to 12, at 6011 Deer Lake Parkway, is the School District No. 41 (Burnaby) secondary school on the lake parkway itself. Its address puts it on the pocket's north side, beside Deer Lake Park. Being beside the school is not the same as being assigned to it: the district assigns by home address, so confirm the specific Upper Deer Lake address with the district. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** What is in Deer Lake Park for a homeowner, day to day? **A:** The City describes Deer Lake Park as a lake, trails and culture hub: a flat boardwalk and lakeside trails, kayak rentals in summer, and concerts and festivals on the Festival Lawn, with the Burnaby Art Gallery, Burnaby Village Museum and Shadbolt Centre for the Arts to the north of the lake. For a south-shore homeowner that means event-day parking pressure on Sperling Avenue and Deer Lake Avenue, not just quiet trails. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- **Q:** Can I extend a house toward the lake or a gully? **A:** Not without a streamside check first. Burnaby's streams and ravines guide applies to anyone developing within 30 metres of a stream or freshwater wetland or shoreline, with streamside leave areas of 15 to 30 metres for Class A fish-bearing watercourses and 5 to 30 metres for Class B, set by the Planning Department for the specific site. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2021-08/Streams-and-Ravines-Informational-Guide.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby-wide tax rates, utility charges and billing dates apply here as across the city — see the vancouver-heights-burnaby-houses-for-sale note. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- The detached-house utility line for this pocket in the City's 2026 table is a $652.33 annual water charge for a detached single-family dwelling, with sewer billed through the sewer use and parcel charges, and a 5% discount if the full utility payment is received by the due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- A billing change is already scheduled: starting in 2027, the City moves the Sewer Parcel Tax from the property tax notice onto the utility notice, combining water and sewer charges on one bill. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the shoreline and stream setbacks before pricing a renovation: the City's streams guide states the federal Fisheries Act protects fish habitat on these watercourses, and Burnaby's Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area bylaw sets the development setbacks the Planning Department applies site by site. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2021-08/Streams-and-Ravines-Informational-Guide.pdf
- Check the top of bank, not the water's edge: the same guide explains the top of bank of a ravine can sit a greater distance from the stream than the visible bank, and in flat areas it is taken as the edge of the active floodplain, which can move the leave area well into a rear yard. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2021-08/Streams-and-Ravines-Informational-Guide.pdf
