# Little Mountain Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: little-mountain (Little Mountain; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Little Mountain is a small hillside neighbourhood on the north side of the City of Chilliwack, at the east end of the downtown townsite, reached by Yale Road East, Quarry Road and Prest Road. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=amenityView&plID=199&backTo&paID=46
- The City lists Little Mountain itself as a nature park and Little Mountain Park as a neighbourhood park in its parks inventory — the hill is a named City park and natural area, not just a marketing name. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=amenityView&plID=199&backTo&paID=46
- Carleton Street runs along the base of the hill: Little Mountain Elementary is at 9900 Carleton Street, which fixes the neighbourhood's south-western anchor point. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/school/03333016
- Highway 1's Prest Road interchange (Exit 123) is the neighbourhood's highway door, at the east end of Yale Road East. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=5211

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is detached houses on sloped lots above the valley floor — the City treats the area as hillside, so homes step up the slope rather than sitting on the flat townsite grid of Chilliwack Proper below. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- Little Mountain Elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 5, 9900 Carleton Street) sits at the base of the hill and is the neighbourhood's named school in Chilliwack School District (SD33). https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/school/03333016
- Because the lots are hillside, driveways, retaining walls and drainage are part of the housing stock's character: the City's hillside rules cap driveway width at 3.5 metres at the curb face to limit grading on sloped lots. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Slopes of 20% or more put a Little Mountain lot in Development Permit Area 5 (Hillside Development) under Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, Schedule D-5: a development permit is required for subdivision, earthworks and construction on those slopes. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- Development Permit Area 2 (Hillside and Upland Areas) also applies to hillside land here, and the City states a geotechnical report from a qualified professional must be submitted with each application under it. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- The hillside guidelines in Schedule D-5 steer most building to slopes under 30% and preserve open space on slopes of 30% or more, and they prohibit large cuts and fills to create flat yards. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- Buildings on the slope must run parallel to the natural contours under guideline HD-38, to reduce grading and avoid high wall faces on the downhill side. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Do I need a special permit to build or add on here? **A:** On any part of the lot with a slope of 20% or more, yes: Development Permit Area 5 requires a City development permit before subdivision, earthworks or construction, and Development Permit Area 2 requires a geotechnical report with the application. Flat portions under 20% slope are exempt from the DPA 5 permit. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- **Q:** Which school catchment is a Little Mountain address in? **A:** This is in flux, and the district's own final decision was not captured in this pass. A community petition responding to SD33's proposed north-side catchment changes states the proposal would reassign Little Mountain students to Little Mountain Elementary and Chilliwack Middle School for 2027/2028, replacing the Cheam Elementary and Rosedale Traditional pathway — treat the proposal details as UNCONFIRMED until checked on SD33's own pages. https://www.petitions.com/little_mountain_chilliwack_against_sd33_proposed_north_side_catchment_changes
- **Q:** Is slope stability a real concern or paperwork? **A:** The City treats it as a real hazard class: Development Permit Area 2 land is identified in the OCP as having serious hazards from slope instability and soil erosion, and proposals need a hydro-geotechnical study covering slope stability, rockfall and drainage before a permit issues. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8292
- **Q:** Can I flatten the lot for a yard or pool? **A:** Not by large earthworks: the Schedule D-5 hillside guidelines say yard areas must be kept in a natural slope condition and large cuts and fills to achieve flat yards will not be permitted. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- **Q:** How do I get to the bus from the hill? **A:** BC Transit Route 57 (Broadway) runs along the base of the hill on the Yale Road East side; from January 4, 2026 its Carleton Street service shifted one block east to McNaught Road with new stops on McNaught Road and Acorn. Homes higher on the slope are a walk downhill to those stops. https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Little Mountain is in the City of Chilliwack, whose 2025 residential tax rate was 4.50189 per $1,000 of assessed value all-in (municipal total 2.83455 per $1,000) — see the chilliwack-proper-south note for the full rate breakdown. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2527/Tax%5FRates%5Ffor%5Fthe%5FWebtugnc2fs1l9d%2Epdf
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies: the Province's taxable-areas list names the City of Chilliwack. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- A cost specific to hillside lots: where a development permit is triggered, the City requires professional reports (geotechnical, and on steeper proposals a third-party review for slopes of 30% or more) at the owner's expense. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/basic-html/page-94.html

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Yale Road East and Prest Road are the driving routes; the Prest Road interchange is Highway 1 Exit 123, the closest freeway access for the north side's east end. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=5211

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Slope hazard is the headline risk: check the lot against Development Permit Area 2 (Hillside Environment, OCP Schedule D-2, Map 15) and Development Permit Area 5 (slopes of 20% or more, Schedule D-5) on the City's OCP maps before writing an offer. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8292
- The Schedule D-2 guidelines require a qualified professional's geotechnical study addressing landslide, earthflow and rockfall hazard for hillside development — ask for any existing report on title or in the City's building file. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8292
- Drainage runs downhill: the guidelines require storm drainage control, interceptor ditches above steep slopes where required, and erosion control, so a buyer should check where the lot above drains to. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8292
- What the OCP maps do not tell a buyer: the stability history of one specific lot — that is in the geotechnical reports and covenants registered on that lot's title, not on the neighbourhood page.
