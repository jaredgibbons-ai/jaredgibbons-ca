# Maple Ridge Duplexes — Extra Local Notes

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- A Maple Ridge duplex lot is dealt with under the City's provincial-housing pages, which treat single-family and duplex lots as one eligible class across the city. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- City-wide Maple Ridge facts — boundaries, history, employers: see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note.
- The City sorts duplex potential by position, not by neighbourhood name: inside or outside the Urban Containment Boundary, and within or beyond 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-12/Residential%20Zoning%20Checklist%20-%20Urban%20-%20Fillable.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The duplex here is one option inside a wider small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) menu: the City lists secondary suites, detached garden suites, duplexes, triplexes and fourplexes as the forms an eligible lot can take. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- Under the City's Urban Infill Residential use, a lot currently zoned Single Detached or Two-Unit (Duplex) Residential, inside the Urban Containment Boundary and under 4,050 square metres, can qualify for the infill use in Zoning Bylaw Section 402.29. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-12/Residential%20Zoning%20Checklist%20-%20Urban%20-%20Fillable.pdf
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Eligible single-family and duplex lots can now have up to three, four, or six units of SSMUH, provided they meet zoning, bylaw and building code requirements. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- The unit count is mechanical: under 281 square metres of lot area allows up to three dwelling units; 281 to 4,050 square metres allows up to four; and the same size range within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop allows up to six. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-12/Residential%20Zoning%20Checklist%20-%20Urban%20-%20Fillable.pdf
- A building permit is required for SSMUH work, and construction must comply with the BC Building Code and the City's Building Bylaw; most developments under the new rules do not require a rezoning. https://mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-08/FLYER-CMR%20Housing%20regulations-PLANN-240729%20V5.pdf
- If the proposal is a multi-unit development — triplex, fourplex, courtyard or townhouse form — an approved development permit is required before a building permit application. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-12/Residential%20Zoning%20Checklist%20-%20Urban%20-%20Fillable.pdf
- A detached garden suite is governed by Section 402.11 of the Zoning Bylaw, and a principal-to-accessory building distance of 4.5 metres applies in the R-3 zone under Section 603.7. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-12/Residential%20Zoning%20Checklist%20-%20Urban%20-%20Fillable.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is a Maple Ridge duplex strata or fee-simple?
**A:** It depends on how the two units were created, and the City material read does not settle it for any specific property. A duplex built as one building on one lot is a single title; two separately titled halves exist only where a strata plan or a subdivision was registered. Check the title and any strata plan at purchase, because SSMUH permission changes unit count, not title. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new

**Q:** Can I add units to a duplex lot I buy?
**A:** Possibly, within the SSMUH counts: an eligible lot can reach three, four or six units depending on lot area and distance to a prescribed bus stop. The City's checklist is the working tool, and it requires the lot to be zoned Single Detached or Two-Unit (Duplex) and inside the Urban Containment Boundary for the infill use. A building permit is still required. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-12/Residential%20Zoning%20Checklist%20-%20Urban%20-%20Fillable.pdf

**Q:** Do I need a public hearing to convert a duplex into more units?
**A:** No hearing is held for the SSMUH route itself: under the new provincial legislation, public hearings are no longer held for rezoning of residential developments that align with the Official Community Plan, and most SSMUH work needs no rezoning at all. A development permit can still be required for multi-unit forms, which is a staff and Council process, not a hearing. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new

**Q:** What is the first permit step for a duplex project?
**A:** Confirm eligibility with the Planning Department, then apply for a building permit; the City's information sheet directs applicants to its online building permit application through MapleRidge.ca/eservices. If the design is a triplex, fourplex, courtyard or townhouse form, the development permit comes first. Keep the checklist figures for your lot size in the file, because the unit cap follows from them. https://mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-08/FLYER-CMR%20Housing%20regulations-PLANN-240729%20V5.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The City publishes an Urban Infill Residential checklist that works a lot through its own yes/no questions before a buyer pays for drawings. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-12/Residential%20Zoning%20Checklist%20-%20Urban%20-%20Fillable.pdf
- The 400-metre frequent-transit test is the single fact that moves a mid-size lot from four units to six, so measure it to the prescribed bus stop, not to the nearest stop of any kind. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-12/Residential%20Zoning%20Checklist%20-%20Urban%20-%20Fillable.pdf
- Planning for Growth is the City's live process: an open house on June 4, 2026 at the Maple Ridge Library covered the Transit-Oriented Area and SSMUH updates that duplex lots fall under. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-maple-ridge-invites-residents-learn-more-about-proposed-housing-zoning-changes

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Maple Ridge's 2026 residential mill rate totals 4.53578 per $1,000 of assessed value on the City's rate summary, with the municipal general portion at 2.86430. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- The 2026 flat water fee on the same City summary is $859.25, with an additional-unit flat fee of $429.60 — the line a duplex owner with a suite should check. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- Maple Ridge is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Lots outside Maple Ridge's Urban Area are treated separately in the City's SSMUH material, with a complete list held by the Planning Department, so a rural duplex lot needs a direct City confirmation. https://mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-08/FLYER-CMR%20Housing%20regulations-PLANN-240729%20V5.pdf
- The checklist warns that meeting the unit count does not settle siting: setback exceptions sit in Section 403.2 of the Zoning Bylaw and must be checked lot by lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-12/Residential%20Zoning%20Checklist%20-%20Urban%20-%20Fillable.pdf
