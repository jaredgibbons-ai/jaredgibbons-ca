# Delta Manor Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: delta-manor-guide (Delta Manor, Ladner; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Delta Manor is the central Ladner grid around Delta Secondary School and Memorial Park, not an official City neighbourhood name; the full boundary discussion is in the delta-manor note — see the delta-manor note. https://de.deltasd.bc.ca/
- Its anchor addresses are Delta Secondary at 4615 51 Street and Memorial Park at 5010 47 Avenue, with Hawthorne to the north and Holly to the east. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/delta

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The guide-level pattern is an older central-Ladner street grid of detached homes around the school and park, with townhouse and apartment forms near Ladner Trunk Road; complex-by-complex detail is in the delta-manor note — see the delta-manor note. https://de.deltasd.bc.ca/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) is the main rule change here: Delta adopted zoning on June 24, 2024 permitting suites, coach houses and garden suites on single-detached and duplex lots without rezoning. https://letstalk.delta.ca/ssmuh
- Older Delta Manor lots can carry restrictive covenants that prohibit secondary suites; Delta's 2020 amendment process dealt with discharging those covenants where no variance is needed. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/january-18-2021-virtual-public-hearing-amendments-secondary

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Delta Manor address? **A:** Catchment is address-based, so check the exact address before you buy. The district says families verify their catchment school through its School Locator or the board office, and Delta runs elementary schools from kindergarten to grade 7 and secondary schools from grade 8 to grade 12, with no middle-school tier. Delta Secondary itself stands inside this area at 4615 51 Street. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/news-events/news/registration-information-for-students-who-are-new-to-delta-2/
- **Q:** Is Delta Manor an official neighbourhood? **A:** No official City boundary was confirmed for the name. It is a board sub-area name for central Ladner, anchored by Delta Secondary and Memorial Park at 5010 47 Avenue, so two listings both labelled Delta Manor can sit on quite different streets. Ask which street, not just which sub-area name. https://de.deltasd.bc.ca/
- **Q:** Can I add a suite or coach house? **A:** Possibly, without a rezoning. Delta's Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) rules, adopted June 24, 2024, let owners apply at building-permit stage for secondary suites, garden suites and coach houses on single-detached and duplex lots. A title covenant can still block a suite, so read the title before counting on rental income. https://letstalk.delta.ca/ssmuh
- **Q:** What is changing nearby? **A:** Ladner Village is the change zone next door. The City is rezoning seven Chisholm Street and Delta Street properties for a future mixed-use development of up to six storeys, and a 146-unit non-market rental building is proposed at 5625 Ladner Trunk Road; details are in section 6 below. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009635

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Delta Secondary has stood on its 51 Street site in some form since the Ladner school centre opened there, making the school, not a shopping street, the area's landmark. https://de.deltasd.bc.ca/
- Ladner Leisure Centre at 4600 Clarence Taylor Crescent and the Ladner Pioneer Library at 4683 51 Street are the daily-life anchors just west of the grid. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations
- Delta Hospital at 5800 Mountain View Boulevard is Fraser Health's community hospital for Delta, and the Ladner Exchange bus loop sits next door. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- The Delta Manor and Laburnum re-consolidation question is a known open item in this program; no City page read in this pass settled it, so it is listed under NOT CONFIRMED. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- Route 618 is the Ladner North loop from Ladner Exchange via Ladner Trunk Road, Central Avenue, Westminster Avenue and Crescent Drive — the local circulator for the streets north of the village. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Area-plan items for this guide's own area come first: the Ladner Village (LV1) Development Permit Area governs form and character for new multi-unit buildings at the village edge of Delta Manor. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009635

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta's 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after that date and another 5 percent after September 2, 2026; Council's 2026 plan set a 2.9 percent City tax increase. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- For 2026, Delta's flat-rate utility bill for a single-family home is $1,658, up $51 from 2025, covering garbage and recycling plus water and sewer where not metered separately. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Delta is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT), so residential owners must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
Read: 2026-11-03

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 601 is the spine: it runs from South Delta to Bridgeport Station on the Canada Line, stopping at Ladner Exchange beside Delta Hospital. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf
- Route 609 links Ladner Exchange to South Delta Exchange via Arthur Drive and Ladner Trunk Road, which is the direct Ladner-to-Tsawwassen bus from this area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Flood is the first check in low-lying Ladner: Delta sits between the Fraser River, Boundary Bay and the Strait of Georgia and maintains over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection; ask which side of the dike a lot sits on. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Check the title for suite-prohibiting covenants and any flood covenant before removing subjects; the City page on suite amendments explains why those covenants exist on older lots. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/january-18-2021-virtual-public-hearing-amendments-secondary
