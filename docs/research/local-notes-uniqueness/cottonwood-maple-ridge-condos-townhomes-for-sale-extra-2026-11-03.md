## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Creek-side strata lots carry a watercourse check other Maple Ridge townhome areas do not: Kanaka Creek is a salmon-bearing watercourse protected inside Kanaka Creek Regional Park, and Metro Vancouver warns of steep, slippery cliffs along the creek, especially at Cliff Falls. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Are there any apartment condos in Cottonwood?
**A:** None were confirmed on an allowed source in this pass. The City publishes no Cottonwood neighbourhood or area plan, and no municipal, developer or strata page read for this note names an apartment building inside the sub-area. The attached homes that do exist are townhome complexes along the Cottonwood Drive and Kanaka Way corridors, plus one housing co-operative on Dewdney Trunk Road, so a "condo" search here should be read as a townhome search. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods

**Q:** Which schools serve a Cottonwood townhome?
**A:** Two elementary schools sit inside the area, Kanaka Creek Elementary at 11120 234A Street and Alexander Robinson Elementary at 11849 238B Street, and School District No. 42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows) catchments are address-based. For the Kanaka Creek Elementary address itself, the district locator returns Kanaka Creek Elementary and Samuel Robertson Technical Secondary, but a complex on 240 Street or 119 Avenue can fall in a different elementary catchment, so run the exact unit address in the locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/

**Q:** What is different about buying at St. George's Village?
**A:** It is a co-operative, not a strata. The co-op's own membership form states the age qualification is 55 years, that the co-op holds clear title to the land, and that a $3,500 non-refundable transfer fee is payable when a share transfers. There is no strata lot, no Form B Information Certificate and no strata fee structure; approval runs through the co-op's membership process instead. https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf

**Q:** Does the creek affect what a strata can do with its land?
**A:** Yes, at the edges. Kanaka Creek Regional Park protects the creek corridor through this part of Maple Ridge, the Bell-Irving Hatchery in the park supports chum and coho salmon and cutthroat trout, and complexes backing onto the creek or its tributaries can carry riparian setbacks that limit fences, sheds and tree removal. The strata's registered plan and the City's flood and watercourse maps are where a buyer confirms which part of a complex's common property is setback land. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park

**Q:** How do I get downtown from a Cottonwood townhome without driving?
**A:** By bus to Haney Place, then the West Coast Express or the R3 RapidBus. TransLink's route diagram names the 745 Haney Place / Cottonwood as this area's route, and the 745 and 746 interline at Jackson Road at 103 Avenue, forming one circular service through Cottonwood and Albion. No West Coast Express station sits in Cottonwood; the stations are in the Town Centre and at Port Haney. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/194/r748.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The hatchery is a working salmon facility, not just a park feature: Kanaka Creek Regional Park's Bell-Irving Hatchery supports chum and coho salmon and cutthroat trout. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- Two named trail systems start inside the sub-area's parkland: the Canyon Trail runs 1.2 kilometres one way from the hatchery to Cliff Falls, and the Riverfront Trails run 3 kilometres one way at the creek oxbow and the Fraser River. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- No City recreation centre sits in Cottonwood: the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre is at 11925 Haney Place in the Town Centre, west of every Cottonwood complex. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre
- No library branch sits in Cottonwood either: Maple Ridge Public Library is at 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road in the Town Centre. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/
- School District No. 42 has no middle tier: elementary schools run Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary schools Grades 8 to 12, so a Cottonwood townhome family changes schools once, after Grade 7. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools
- Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, is the community hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows, with 24/7 emergency care. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
