# Simon Fraser University Area Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: simon-fraser-university-area-burnaby-houses-for-sale (Simon Fraser University Area; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- This area is the top of Burnaby Mountain: the Simon Fraser University (SFU) Burnaby campus and the UniverCity residential community beside it, reached by Burnaby Mountain Parkway and Gaglardi Way; the full shared area facts are in the simon-fraser-university-area-burnaby note. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/
- UniverCity occupies about 65 hectares south and east of the campus, in neighbourhoods the developer materials name West Highlands, East Highlands, High Street and the Slopes. https://intergulf.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/B1709_1060_Arbour_Feature-Sheet_v4.pdf
- The campus and community are ringed by the Burnaby Mountain Conservation Area, whose trails climb from about 25 metres above sea level to the 366-metre summit, and UniverCity is now home to over 5,000 residents. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/walking-hiking-and-biking/burnaby-mountain-conservation-area-trails

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The mountaintop's homes are strata apartments and townhomes at UniverCity, not a detached-house subdivision: the newest product named in developer materials is Arbour at SFU, 178 homes in 4 buildings of 1, 2 and 3 bedroom condominiums and 3 bedroom stacked townhomes in the Slopes neighbourhood. https://intergulf.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/B1709_1060_Arbour_Feature-Sheet_v4.pdf
- No detached-house subdivision on the mountaintop was found in the sources read; buyers set on a detached house near SFU look to the slope neighbourhoods below the campus, such as Forest Grove and Westridge. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- UniverCity homes are strata homes, so a purchase carries strata documents, bylaws and fees rather than the City lot rules that govern a detached Burnaby house; the strata angle is covered in the simon-fraser-university-area-burnaby-condos-townhomes-for-sale note. https://intergulf.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/B1709_1060_Arbour_Feature-Sheet_v4.pdf
- Burnaby 2050, the City's official community plan, was adopted December 9, 2025 and sets the land-use direction for the city including the mountain's slope neighbourhoods. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- Burnaby is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Are there detached houses at the top of Burnaby Mountain? **A:** No detached-house subdivision was found in the sources read: the residential community beside the campus is UniverCity, about 65 hectares of strata neighbourhoods named West Highlands, East Highlands, High Street and the Slopes, with product such as Arbour's condominiums and stacked townhomes rather than fee-simple houses. https://intergulf.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/B1709_1060_Arbour_Feature-Sheet_v4.pdf
- **Q:** How many people live at UniverCity? **A:** Over 5,000 residents, per the community's own site, in the neighbourhoods south and east of the SFU Burnaby campus on the mountaintop. https://univercity.ca/
- **Q:** Is there an elementary school on the mountain? **A:** Yes: University Highlands Elementary is the School District No. 41 (SD41) elementary school listed for the UniverCity community on the district's elementary addresses page; SD41 assigns catchments by address, so confirm the assigned school for a specific address. https://burnabyschools.ca/elementary-school-addresses/
- **Q:** What is the 2026 residential tax rate? **A:** Burnaby's 2026 residential mill rate totals 3.28373 per 1,000 dollars of assessed value, and gross taxes are the assessed value multiplied by the mill rate and divided by 1,000, before local area service, sewer parcel and business improvement area charges. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The board prints this sub-area's name in full as "Simon Fraser University SFU," and the neighbouring sub-area "Simon Fraser Hills" covers the Forest Grove side of the mountain and is a different area. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/burnaby
- The Burnaby Mountain Conservation Area rings the campus and community, so the mountaintop's open space is a conservation area with trails from about 25 metres to the 366-metre summit rather than a set of neighbourhood parks. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/walking-hiking-and-biking/burnaby-mountain-conservation-area-trails
- Arbour at SFU sits beside a new roughly 30,000-square-foot Slopes Neighbourhood Park in UniverCity's Slopes neighbourhood. https://intergulf.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/B1709_1060_Arbour_Feature-Sheet_v4.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby's 2026 residential tax rate is 3.28373 per 1,000 dollars of assessed value, and the average residential assessment in Burnaby decreased by 5.29 percent in 2026, which does not necessarily mean an individual bill falls, because taxes depend on the change in a property's assessment relative to the class average. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates
- Property taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after the July due date and a further 5 percent after the September due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes
- Assessment notices arrive from BC Assessment each January, and the assessed value on the notice is the value the mill rate is applied to. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Burnaby Mountain Parkway and Gaglardi Way are the two roads up the mountain to the campus and UniverCity. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/
- The proposed Burnaby Mountain Gondola would run from Production Way–University Station to a terminal near the SFU bus exchange, which is the station the mountain's transit plan is built around; the gondola is not approved or funded. https://www.translink.ca/news/2023/november/translink%20seeking%20feedback%20on%20burnaby%20mountain%20gondola

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Treat a UniverCity purchase as a strata purchase: read the strata documents, bylaws and fee schedule, because the mountaintop stock is strata product rather than detached houses. https://intergulf.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/B1709_1060_Arbour_Feature-Sheet_v4.pdf
- Confirm the SD41 catchment for the exact address, since the mountain's assigned secondary school is not stated in the sources read. https://burnabyschools.ca/elementary-school-addresses/
- Do not price in the Burnaby Mountain Gondola: TransLink carries it as a proposal requiring a future investment plan plus TransLink Board and Mayors' Council approval. https://www.translink.ca/news/2023/november/translink%20seeking%20feedback%20on%20burnaby%20mountain%20gondola
