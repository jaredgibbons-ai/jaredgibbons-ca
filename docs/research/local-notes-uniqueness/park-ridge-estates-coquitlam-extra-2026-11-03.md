# Park Ridge Estates Coquitlam Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: park-ridge-estates-coquitlam (Park Ridge Estates, Coquitlam; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Park Ridge Estates is the established subdivision at the southern base of Burke Mountain in northeast Coquitlam, centred on the David Avenue and Coast Meridian Road corner; no City-published boundary for the subdivision name was found. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision
- The City frames Burke Mountain growth through three neighbourhood plans — Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek and Partington Creek — and Park Ridge Estates sits in the built, lower part of that planning area, below the newer Smiling Creek streets. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock here is built-out detached houses and townhouse clusters from Burke Mountain's first building phase, not presale product: the new-build supply on Burke Mountain is concentrated in the Smiling Creek and Partington Creek plans above and east of this subdivision. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision
- The named elementary school on the subdivision's own streets is Leigh Elementary, 1230 Soball Street, School District No. 43 (Coquitlam). https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve Park Ridge Estates? **A:** Leigh Elementary, 1230 Soball Street, is the elementary school on the subdivision's streets, and the Burke Mountain Middle/Secondary School off David Avenue east of Coast Meridian Road is under construction. School District No. 43 catchment is by permanent home address, so run the exact address in the district locator rather than assuming the closest school is the assigned one. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** Is Park Ridge Estates the same as Burke Mountain? **A:** No — it is one built subdivision at Burke Mountain's southern base, while Burke Mountain is the whole mountainside planning area the City guides through the Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek and Partington Creek neighbourhood plans. Services marketed to the wider mountain, including Burke Mountain Village, are north and east of this subdivision, not inside it. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision
- **Q:** What is being built near the subdivision? **A:** The Burke Mountain Community Centre and Burke Village Park broke ground in May 2026, with design complete and full building construction following in 2026, beside a village centre planned at about 120,000 square feet of retail including a grocery store. Those amenities serve the whole mountain; nothing under construction inside the subdivision itself was confirmed. https://www.coquitlam.ca/422/Burke-Mountain-Village
- **Q:** Can I add units to a house lot here? **A:** Possibly, under Coquitlam's small-scale multi-unit housing rules, which are stated in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. The local checks come first: a creek on or beside the lot, a steep slope, or a house built before 1945 each trigger a Development Services conversation before design, and slopes and creek corridors are common on Burke Mountain's lower streets. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- There is no SkyTrain station on Burke Mountain; rail access is from the Millennium Line stations below the mountain, reached by bus or car down David Avenue and Coast Meridian Road. https://www.coquitlam.ca/422/Burke-Mountain-Village
- David Avenue is the subdivision's east–west spine across the mountain's base, and Coast Meridian Road is its north–south link down to Lougheed Highway and Port Coquitlam. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the City's environmental development permit mapping for any lot here: it maps unstable slopes and watercourse areas, and Burke Mountain lots can carry slope or creek conditions the mapping shows but does not resolve for a specific lot. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
