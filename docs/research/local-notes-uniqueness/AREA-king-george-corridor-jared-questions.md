# King George Corridor, South Surrey — Questions for Jared
Area: King George Corridor, South Surrey. First program page for this area in this set. Questions only — do not answer here.

## Questions (voice memo)
1. On King George Boulevard itself versus the side streets west of it — which houses do buyers actually pay up for, and which sit because of highway frontage?
2. At the Nicomekl River end, do buyers raise the floodplain and ALR farmland next door, or does it never come up until the inspection?
3. Has the King George Boulevard BRT talk changed buyer interest in the corridor, or do buyers treat it as years away?
4. Who is buying corridor houses — families headed for Semiahmoo Town Centre schools, or investors watching the OCP density?
5. What surprises buyers most here — lot depth off the Boulevard, highway noise, or how different the 8 Avenue end feels from the 32 Avenue end?
