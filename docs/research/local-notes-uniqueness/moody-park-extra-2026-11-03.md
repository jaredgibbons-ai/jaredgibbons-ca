# Moody Park Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: moody-park (Moody Park, New Westminster; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Moody Park is the neighbourhood centred on Moody Park itself, bounded by 8th Avenue, 8th Street, 10th Street and 6th Avenue in the Uptown-west area of New Westminster — the park is the boundary anchor, not just a feature inside it. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php
- The City name and the board name match: the City lists Moody Park among its Historic Neighbourhood Context Statements, and "Moody Park" is also the sub-area name used in board statistics. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php
- What adjoins it: Uptown to the east, Brow of the Hill to the west, Glenbrooke North to the north, and Downtown and the Quay down the hill to the south. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php
- City-wide New Westminster facts are treated in full elsewhere — see the the-heights-new-westminster-houses-for-sale note; the house-specific facts for this neighbourhood are in the moody-park-new-westminster-houses-for-sale note.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is freehold houses on the residential streets around the park, with strata apartment buildings on the 6th Street, 6th Avenue and 8th Avenue edges — the park-facing blocks are houses, the corridor blocks are apartments. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- One edge building is confirmed on its property manager's own document: The Diplomat, 707 Eighth Street, strata plan NW920, managed by Quay Pacific Property Management, on the Eighth Street edge of the park. https://www.quaypacific.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/AGM-18-12-13-Notice-of-AGM-FINAL.pdf
- The neighbourhood's landmark buildings are civic, inside or on the park: Moody Park Arena at 701 8th Avenue and the Moody Park outdoor pool at 701 10th Street. https://www.newwestcity.ca/facilities https://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/book-facilities-fields-and-parks/articles/6965.php
- The replacement New Westminster Secondary School at 820 Sixth Street, at the area's east edge, opened to students on January 11, 2021 — a 1,900-seat building that is School District 40's only secondary school. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2021EDUC0003-000034.htm

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Moody Park is documented in a City Historic Neighbourhood Context Statement but is not a Heritage Conservation Area (HCA), so the context statement records the area's history without an HCA permit layer on ordinary homes — heritage status is still checked property by property through the City's heritage program. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php
- A demolition permit in New Westminster has a heritage step: before a demolition permit is accepted for a building older than 50 years, the City initiates a review of the building to identify any heritage value, and a tree permit application must be submitted with every demolition application. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Demolition_Permit_Application_Package_June_2025.pdf
- City-wide infill rules changed in June 2026: Council adopted Official Community Plan (OCP) and zoning amendments permitting up to six units of infill housing — secondary suites, laneway and carriage houses, triplexes, quadplexes and other multiplex forms — on single-detached and duplex properties across most of the city, under new RGO-A (up to four units) and RGO-B (up to six units) zoning; the full city-wide treatment is in the the-heights-new-westminster-houses-for-sale note. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- House zoning numbers for Moody Park streets (RS districts, suite and laneway rules) are treated in full in the moody-park-new-westminster-houses-for-sale note.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Moody Park address? **A:** School District 40 catchments are set by exact address in the district's school locator. For the sample address 1714 Eighth Avenue, the locator result is École Lord Tweedsmuir Elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 6, which also offers Early French Immersion), Fraser River Middle (Grades 6–8) and New Westminster Secondary (Grades 9–12). The district operates only one secondary school, and its long-range facilities plan notes that school is already operating above capacity, so the elementary catchment is the real differentiator between Moody Park streets. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- **Q:** What is actually in Moody Park itself? **A:** More than a green: the City lists Moody Park with an outdoor pool, spray park and sports fields, and Moody Park Arena stands at 701 8th Avenue at the park. The outdoor pool at 701 10th Street is heated to 27–28 degrees Celsius, has a slide for anyone over 48 inches tall, and stays open in the rain, closing only during lightning events. A few blocks east, the təməsew̓txʷ Aquatic and Community Centre at 65 East Sixth Avenue fully opened in May 2024 with an eight-lane 50-metre pool. https://www.newwestcity.ca/outdoor-pools https://www.newwestcity.ca/facilities
- **Q:** Is there a SkyTrain station in Moody Park? **A:** No — there is no station inside the neighbourhood. The nearest stations are New Westminster Station and Columbia Station on the Expo Line, downtown at the foot of the hill. TransLink route 105 (Uptown / New Westminster Station) and route 106 (Edmonds Station / New Westminster Station) run the Uptown corridors adjoining the area and connect it to those stations, so a Moody Park commute is a bus-to-station trip, not a walk-to-station trip. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf
- **Q:** Can I tear down an older Moody Park house and rebuild? **A:** Not without clearing two City steps first: for any building older than 50 years, the City reviews heritage value before a demolition permit is accepted, and a tree permit application must accompany every demolition application under the City's tree protection rules. The neighbourhood's Historic Neighbourhood Context Statement means older homes here are documented, so a teardown plan should start with the City's heritage and tree desks, not with a builder's quote. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Demolition_Permit_Application_Package_June_2025.pdf
- **Q:** Could a Moody Park house lot hold more homes under the new infill rules? **A:** City-wide, yes in principle: in June 2026 Council adopted amendments permitting up to six units of infill housing on single-detached and duplex properties across most of the city, and the City publishes a map and list of properties zoned RGO-A (up to four units) or RGO-B (up to six units). Whether a specific Moody Park lot is RGO-A or RGO-B is a property-level check on that City map, and heritage or tree constraints on the lot can still shape what gets built. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Moody Park outdoor pool can be rented privately on weekend mornings in July and August, with City fees starting at $77.62 per hour for up to 30 people — the pool at 701 10th Street is a bookable neighbourhood facility, not just a drop-in one. https://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/book-facilities-fields-and-parks/articles/6965.php
- New Westminster Secondary School is the district's only secondary school and is already operating above capacity, per School District 40's long-range facilities plan — every Moody Park teenager in the public system is headed to the same building at 820 Sixth Street. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2021EDUC0003-000034.htm
- The Main Branch of the New Westminster Public Library is at 716 6th Avenue, at the neighbourhood's east edge in Uptown — inside the Moody Park street grid, not downtown. https://www.nwpl.ca/
- The City's floodplain study zones only the low-lying edges of New Westminster — Queensborough, the west-end Fraser River industrial land, the Quay waterfront and the Brunette River industrial area; no floodplain zone is named for the Moody Park plateau, though lot-level status is still confirmed with the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- New Westminster runs its own electrical utility, unlike most British Columbia municipalities on BC Hydro — city-wide detail is in the the-heights-new-westminster-houses-for-sale note. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php
- Uptown adjoining Moody Park is identified in the Official Community Plan as a Frequent Transit Development Area, which is why the apartment form clusters on the 6th Street and 6th Avenue edges rather than inside the park blocks. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- No SkyTrain station is inside Moody Park; the working transit link is TransLink route 105 (Uptown / New Westminster Station) and route 106 (Edmonds Station / New Westminster Station) on the adjoining Uptown corridors, feeding New Westminster and Columbia stations on the Expo Line. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf
- 8th Street and Royal Avenue are the driving link from the neighbourhood down to Downtown and the Quay; 6th Avenue and 8th Avenue carry east-west traffic across the top and bottom of the park. https://www.newwestcity.ca/facilities
- The Rotary Crosstown Greenway's 5th-to-8th Street segment and the BC Parkway corridor at Seventh Avenue West are the named cycling routes touching the neighbourhood, both on the City's active projects list. https://www.newwestcity.ca/planning-building-and-development/projects-on-the-go/main/88/articles/page2.php?status=active

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check heritage before planning work on an older home: the neighbourhood has a Historic Neighbourhood Context Statement, the City runs a heritage register and permit process, and demolition of a building over 50 years old triggers a City heritage review before a permit is accepted. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Demolition_Permit_Application_Package_June_2025.pdf
- Check the tree rules before assuming a lot can be cleared: a tree permit application must be submitted with every demolition permit application in New Westminster, and retained trees on and beside the site are protected during construction. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Demolition_Permit_Application_Package_June_2025.pdf
- Check the flood layer anyway: the City's floodplain study names zones only at the city's low-lying edges, but the study is a city-level document — it does not certify any individual Moody Park lot, which is confirmed with the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- For an edge apartment, run the strata file: British Columbia strata corporations must obtain depreciation reports, and buyers can read the Form B information certificate — the provincial strata pages set out both. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
