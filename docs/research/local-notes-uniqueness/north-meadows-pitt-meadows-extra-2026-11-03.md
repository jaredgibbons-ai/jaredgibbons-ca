# North Meadows — Extra Local Notes
Page: north-meadows-pitt-meadows (North Meadows; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- North Meadows is the part of Pitt Meadows north of Lougheed Highway, and the City publishes no boundary for the sub-area name, so use its parts: the Highland Park and Wildwood residential areas, Harris Road running north, and the rural Pitt Polder farmland beyond the urban edge. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/community-projects-and-initiatives/projects-and-studies/north-lougheed-area-plan
- The closest official planning name for the sub-area's south edge is the North Lougheed Area: about 50 hectares (125 acres) on the north side of Lougheed Highway between Meadows Gardens Golf Course and Harris Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/community-projects-and-initiatives/projects-and-studies/north-lougheed-area-plan
- Harris Road is the main north–south road, with Advent Road, Ford Road, McNeil Road and Old Dewdney Trunk Road as the other named streets in the rural north. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/harris-road-park

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock splits three ways: detached houses in Highland Park and Wildwood, farm houses on large agricultural parcels in the Pitt Polder, and one named condominium complex on the golf course edge. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- The Fairways, 19673 and 19677 Meadow Gardens Way, is the sub-area's named condominium complex: the developer, Magusta Development, describes a 268-home resort-style condominium complex on the edge of Meadow Gardens Golf Course. https://magusta.com/our-portfolio/the-fairways/
- Lot scale is set by zoning minimums, in full in section 3: residential lots start at a 0.2-hectare minimum in the RS (Large Lot Residential) zone and run to 8 and 16 hectares in the agricultural zones. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A garden suite is the second-dwelling route here, not a secondary suite on the same lot: the City's garden suite guide permits one garden suite per lot in the A-1 and A-2 agricultural zones, the RR-1 to RR-5 rural residential zones and the RS and R-1 urban zones, sized between 33 and 90 square metres, and a property may have a garden suite or a secondary suite, not both. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- On Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) land there is a second provincial route: the Agricultural Land Commission allows one additional detached residence without an application where one residence already exists when construction begins, to a maximum of 90 square metres on properties of 40 hectares or less. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Short-term rentals are prohibited everywhere in Pitt Meadows: the City states short-term rental units such as Airbnb and VRBO are not permitted anywhere in the city, while bed and breakfast operations are permitted only under section 5.6 of Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/171
- Watercourse setbacks bite on farm lots: the zoning bylaw requires residential and agricultural buildings to be sited 15 metres from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches in the A-1 and A-2 zones. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- City-wide Pitt Meadows tax, utility and general zoning facts are covered in full in the pitt-meadows-condos-for-sale note (see the pitt-meadows-condos-for-sale note).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a North Meadows address? **A:** Highland Park Elementary, 18961 Advent Road, is inside the sub-area and serves kindergarten to Grade 7, and Pitt Meadows Secondary, 19438 116B Avenue, serves Grades 8 to 12 — School District No. 42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows) has no middle tier in Pitt Meadows. For the sample address 18961 Advent Road the district locator returned those two schools, but catchments are address-based, so a rural Harris Road or Ford Road address must be run in the locator itself. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- **Q:** Is North Meadows behind dikes, and what does that mean for a house? **A:** Yes — the City is almost entirely protected by 60 kilometres of dikes, and the northern drainage areas, including the Pitt Polder catchment, are drained by floodboxes and pump stations because of their low elevation. A buyer is therefore relying on a maintained dike, ditch and pump system rather than on high ground, and should ask for the property's dike and drainage details and any flood construction requirements before removing subjects. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Q:** Can I rent a suite or cabin here short-term? **A:** No. The City states that short-term rental units such as Airbnb and VRBO are not permitted anywhere in Pitt Meadows, and its zoning staff report confirms short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011. That prohibition applies the same in the rural north as in town, so no North Meadows property — house, garden suite or recreational cabin — should be priced on short-term rental income. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/171
- **Q:** Can a farm lot be subdivided or carry a second house? **A:** Subdivision is tightly limited by the agricultural zones: the minimum lot area is 8 hectares in A-1 General Agricultural and 16 hectares in A-2 Large Lot Agricultural under Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011. A second dwelling is more realistic than a split: one garden suite of 33 to 90 square metres is permitted in the A-1, A-2, RR and RS zones, and ALR land has the Agricultural Land Commission's additional-residence route on top of that. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Q:** What changed with the North Lougheed Area Plan? **A:** The plan is now adopted, not proposed. Council adopted the North Lougheed Area Plan on September 29, 2026 through Official Community Plan Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026, covering about 50 hectares north of Lougheed Highway between Meadows Gardens Golf Course and Harris Road. The City states the area is expected to develop gradually over many years and that future development still requires additional planning, technical studies and Council approvals before specific projects proceed. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/community-projects-and-initiatives/projects-and-studies/north-lougheed-area-plan

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Harris Road Park, 12474 Harris Road, is the sub-area's recreation core: the City lists a concession, spray park, skate park, playground, walking trail, baseball and softball diamonds, an outdoor pool and a basketball court. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/harris-road-park
- The rural north has registered heritage: the City heritage register includes the Japanese Canadian Hall at 19089 Advent Road and the Pitt Meadows School House at 13414 Harris Road, described by the City as the most intact of the three early school houses remaining in the city. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/arts-culture-heritage/heritage
- Codd Island Wetlands, on the Alouette River in the rural north, are undiked floodplain wetlands on the City heritage register — a reminder that not every part of the north side sits behind a dike. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/arts-culture-heritage/heritage
- The Pitt Polder Pump Station is new infrastructure, not legacy: the City states work on the new station was completed in 2021, it meets current provincial standards with fish-friendly pumps, and it provides flood mitigation to over 1,650 hectares of agricultural land. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/about-pitt-meadows/location-maps/pitt-polder-pump-station
- There is no library branch in the sub-area: the city's branch is Pitt Meadows Public Library, 200-12099 Harris Road, south of Lougheed Highway. https://fvrl.bc.ca/locations/pitt-meadows-public-library
- The nearest hospital is in the next city: Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge, is a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency care. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Bus service hugs the south edge and Harris Road: route 701 runs via Harris Road and Hammond Road along the south edge, stopping at Pitt Meadows Station, the West Coast Express station south of Lougheed Highway. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt701.pdf
- Route 719 serves the Wildwood pocket inside the sub-area: it runs Wildwood Crescent and Hammond Road as part of the combined 719/722 service. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt719.pdf
- No bus route serving the rural Pitt Polder itself was confirmed on an official source read for this note — the confirmed routes are on the urban south edge and in Wildwood only. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt719.pdf
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) is the driving spine: it is the sub-area's south edge and the east–west route across the Pitt River Bridge toward Port Coquitlam and Maple Ridge. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/community-projects-and-initiatives/projects-and-studies/north-lougheed-area-plan

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Floodplain is the defining risk check: the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95 per cent of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the floodplain for the Fraser River and, to a smaller degree, the Pitt River — use that current City figure, not the roughly 85–86 per cent figures in older City documents. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- Know which drainage area a lot sits in: the City divides the municipality into four dike drainage areas, and North Meadows lots fall mainly in Area 1 (Alouette Pump Station catchment) and Area 4 (Pitt Polder catchment), each discharged by floodboxes and pump stations because of low elevation. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- ALR status is per-parcel: the same Business Plan puts 78 per cent of the city's land in the Agricultural Land Reserve, so a North Meadows lot's ALR status, farm-use rules and second-dwelling route must be confirmed for the specific parcel, not inferred from the street. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- Servicing is per-lot in the rural north: whether a given lot is on municipal water and sewer or on a well and septic system is not published per lot, so check the well record in the provincial GWELLS application and confirm the onsite sewerage system's certification before writing an offer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
