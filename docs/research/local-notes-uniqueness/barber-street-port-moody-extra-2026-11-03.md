# Barber Street Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: barber-street-port-moody (Barber Street; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Barber Street is a board sub-area name inside the City of Port Moody's Pleasantside neighbourhood, not a City neighbourhood name: the City's Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw, 2026, No. 3522 covers this shore in section 14.2, Pleasantside and April Road. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- The name comes from one street: Barber Street runs off Ioco Road on the north shore of the Port Moody Arm, and Pleasantside Elementary School sits on it at 195 Barber Street. http://www.sd43.bc.ca/school/pleasantside/Pages/default.aspx
- The working street set is the shore strip below Ioco Road — Barber Street, Alderside Road and the lanes off them — with Ioco Road as the only through road and Old Orchard Park at the west end of the shoreline. https://engage.portmoody.ca/rocky-point-and-old-orchard-parks-master-plans/news_feed/background-rocky-point-today

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is primarily single-detached houses, with a small number of townhouse developments along Ioco Road — the OCP's description of the Pleasantside and April Road area this page sits in. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- The OCP keeps the expectation low-rise: the lower-density character of Pleasantside and April Road is anticipated to be retained, with gentle densification from Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) over time rather than redevelopment of the shore strip. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Pleasantside is one of only three Port Moody neighbourhoods where small-lot subdivision is allowed: an eligible Single Detached Residential (RS1) lot can be rezoned to Single Detached Residential – Small Lot (RS1-S), with a minimum lot width of 9 metres and minimum lot area of 325 square metres, in the Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside neighbourhoods only. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- The same page sets the clock: small-lot subdivision approval typically takes between twelve and twenty-four months, and a minor development permit and building permit are still required before building on a new RS1-S lot. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- City-wide suite and SSMUH rules for Port Moody lots are covered in full in the barber-street-port-moody-houses-for-sale note; the Barber Street difference is the RS1-S eligibility above, which the Heritage Mountain and College Park areas do not have. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which school is actually on Barber Street? **A:** Pleasantside Elementary School, at 195 Barber Street, a School District No. 43 (SD43) school with office hours 8:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. Catchment is still set by the permanent home address, so a buyer on Alderside Road should confirm the exact address with SD43 rather than assume the closest building is the assigned school. http://www.sd43.bc.ca/school/pleasantside/Pages/default.aspx
- **Q:** Can I split a large Ioco Road lot into small lots? **A:** Possibly — Pleasantside is a designated area for the RS1-S small-lot program. The lot must be designated Single Family-Low Density in the OCP and meet the 9-metre width and 325-square-metre area minimums, the City's process runs twelve to twenty-four months in most cases, and planning staff ask applicants to consult before submitting. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- **Q:** Why is traffic the first question on this shore? **A:** Because the OCP says so directly. Ioco Road is the primary traffic corridor through Pleasantside and April Road and part of the regional Major Road Network, carrying traffic to and from Anmore and Belcarra, and the City records traffic on Ioco Road as a continuing challenge in this neighbourhood. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- **Q:** Is there a beach in the neighbourhood? **A:** Yes — Old Orchard Park, just under two hectares at the west end of this shore, has Port Moody's only swimming beach (salt-water), and its beach area is used for launching non-motorized boats such as kayaks and stand-up paddleboards, depending on tides. https://engage.portmoody.ca/rocky-point-and-old-orchard-parks-master-plans/news_feed/background-rocky-point-today

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Old Orchard Park connects to the larger Old Mill Site Park/Shoreline Park, together 12.2 hectares, and both are starting points for the Shoreline Trail toward Rocky Point Park. https://engage.portmoody.ca/rocky-point-and-old-orchard-parks-master-plans/news_feed/background-rocky-point-today
- The community association here is Pleasantside's: the City lists the Pleasantside Community Association, which runs the Penguin Plunge and Boat Festival, and The Residents of Pleasantside IOCO Communities Association (TROPICA) for this shore. http://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/discover-port-moody/community-groups-and-clubs/
- Heritage stock clusters on Ioco Road, not Barber Street itself: the Pleasantside and April Road area carries five properties on the Port Moody Heritage Register in the earlier OCP account, including the Pleasantside Grocery at 631 Ioco Road — check current register status with the City before relying on it. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- There is no SkyTrain station on this shore; the bus and station detail for Port Moody is in the barber-street-port-moody-houses-for-sale note.

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Barber Street lots pay City of Port Moody taxes at the city-wide rates — the 2026 mill rates and the single-family utility bill are set out in full in the heritage-mountain-port-moody-houses-for-sale note, and no Pleasantside local-area levy was found in the sources read. http://www.portmoody.ca/media/tiabceqp/2026-mill-rates.pdf
- Port Moody is a Speculation and Vacancy Tax taxable area, so every residential owner on Barber Street and Alderside Road must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Ioco Road is the only through route: it is designated part of the regional Major Road Network and is the corridor TransLink route 182 uses between Moody Centre Station and the Anmore/Belcarra shore. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf https://translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/182/direction/0/schedule
- The Shoreline Trail is the off-road link: Old Orchard Park is a starting point for walking or cycling the trail toward Rocky Point Park and the Moody Centre station area. https://engage.portmoody.ca/rocky-point-and-old-orchard-parks-master-plans/news_feed/background-rocky-point-today

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Shoreline lots need the foreshore check: the OCP's environmentally sensitive area and watercourse layers apply along the Port Moody Arm shore, and a lot's status is checked parcel by parcel on the City ViewPort map under Planning and Zoning layers, not on the general pages. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- Slope is the second check on the uphill side of Ioco Road: Development Permit Area 5 (Hazardous Lands and Steep Slopes) is the City layer to confirm for a specific lot before designing a rebuild — see the barber-street-port-moody-houses-for-sale note for the city-wide hazard detail. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
