# Chilliwack Mountain Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: chilliwack-mountain (Chilliwack; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Chilliwack Mountain is a named mountain and hillside neighbourhood on the south side of the Fraser River, west of Chilliwack Creek and west of the downtown core. https://apps.gov.bc.ca/pub/bcgnws/names/5861.html
- The City publishes no street-by-street boundary for the Chilliwack Mountain sub-area; the residential streets climb from Chilliwack Mountain Road and Schweyey Road, with Grandview Drive rising to Hillkeep Place at the top. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3237 https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/hillkeep.html
- Below the homes sits the Industrial Way / Progress Way industrial area on the valley floor; downtown and Chilliwack Proper West are east, Little Mountain is southeast, and Fairfield Island is across the river north. https://chilliwack.com/ocp

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City-wide zoning, small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) and suite rules are in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note; see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note.
- Mountain-specific lot minimums differ by zone and slope: Hillside Residential (HR) requires 0.2 hectares (ha) with community water or 0.4 ha otherwise; Rural Hillside (RH) requires 8 ha; Suburban Hillside Residential (SHR) rises from 360 square metres (m²) in the lowest slope band to 990 m² in the highest; Suburban Residential (SR) starts at 650 m² fully serviced. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- No neighbourhood plan for the mountain appears in Schedule B of the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP); the mountain is covered by the hillside permit areas instead. https://chilliwack.com/ocp

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Do I need a special permit to build or clear land on Chilliwack Mountain? **A:** Yes, in most cases. The whole mountain sits in Development Permit Area D-2, Hillside Environment, and a permit with a geotechnical hazard and slope assessment is required before subdivision, construction, land clearing or earthworks. Budget for the qualified professional's report and the permit timeline before committing to a build, an addition or major landscaping on any mountain lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- **Q:** Is there a bus or a school on the mountain itself? **A:** No to both. No British Columbia Transit (BC Transit) route in the Chilliwack list runs onto the mountain's residential streets, and no School District No. 33 (SD33) public school is located on the mountain in the provincial school directory. Families drive to valley-floor schools, and the catchment for any address is confirmed only in the SD33 School Locator. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/ https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/chilliwack https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q:** Is the mountain on City sewer? **A:** Partly, and the service line is documented. The City states the sanitary forcemain along Schweyey Road provides sewage services for Chilliwack Mountain and portions of Industrial Way and Progress Way. That statement does not confirm connection for a specific parcel, so verify water and sewer status for the exact lot with the City before writing an offer. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3237
- **Q:** Why does lot size change so much from street to street? **A:** Because slope sets the minimum in the SHR zone. The minimum lot area starts at 360 m² in the lowest slope band and rises to 990 m² in the highest band, so two lots of the same size can have different subdivision or rebuild potential. A lot's measured slope, not just its area, decides what the zoning allows. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Hillkeep Regional Park, a 36-acre nature reserve at the top of the mountain on Hillkeep Place, has about 2 kilometres of hiking trails, a viewing platform, limited parking, and hours of 7 a.m. to sunset. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/hillkeep.html
- Chilliwack Mountain is a designated Wildlife Issue Area: carts may not go to the curb before 5:00 a.m. on collection day and wildlife-resistant carts are required from May 1, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2936
- The nearest City leisure centre is off-mountain: Chilliwack Landing Leisure Centre, 9145 Corbould Street. https://www.ymcabc.ca/locations/chilliwack-landing-leisure-centre
- The nearest library branch is Chilliwack Library, 45860 First Avenue, downtown. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/chilliwack-library
- Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Road, is the hospital serving the mountain. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- No bus route climbs the mountain; the closest service is BC Transit Route 59 Industrial, which links Downtown Chilliwack and Cottonwood Mall via the Lickman Park & Ride in peak-period trips shown on the route schedule. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=59
- Highway 1 access is at the Lickman Road interchange south of the mountain, where the Lickman Park & Ride sits. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=59
- Drive times to Abbotsford or Vancouver are UNCONFIRMED; no operator page states them.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Slope is the main check: Development Permit Area D-2 covers land identified with slope-instability and soil-erosion hazards, and Development Permit Area D-5 applies to slopes of 20 percent or more; look the lot up in the OCP Schedule D maps. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SD2%5FDPA%5FHillside%5FEnvironment%2Epdf https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- A qualified professional must submit a geotechnical study under the Engineers and Geoscientists British Columbia landslide-assessment guidelines before development proceeds in D-2. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SD2%5FDPA%5FHillside%5FEnvironment%2Epdf
- At the mountain's base, the Chilliwack Creek corridor carries riparian and fish-habitat constraints; a federal assessment for a Schweyey Road stormwater outfall into Chilliwack Creek addressed designated critical habitat for Salish Sucker. https://www.iaac.gc.ca/050/evaluations/proj/88951
- Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status is parcel-specific; check any lot on Agricultural Land Commission mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
