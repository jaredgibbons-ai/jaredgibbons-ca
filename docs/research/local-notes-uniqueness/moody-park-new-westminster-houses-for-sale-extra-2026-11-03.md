## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses here are early stock: residential construction along Sixth Avenue began shortly after the park was cleared in 1889, and the neighbourhood was largely developed and serviced by streetcar by the First World War. https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=16762
- A dated example survives at 1013 Sixth Avenue, a two-storey front-gabled house built between 1892 and 1902, on the Community Heritage Register since April 27, 2009. https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=16768

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What is inside Moody Park itself? **A:** Moody Park holds the neighbourhood's main outdoor recreation in one block. The City lists an outdoor pool at 701 Tenth Street, three tennis courts shared with three pickleball courts, a full and a half basketball court, and a 2,430-square-metre off-leash dog area added in August 2015 with three gated entrances. Its spray park is currently closed and listed as reopening in summer 2027. https://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/amenities/sb_expander_articles/3440.php
- **Q:** What is Century House? **A:** Century House is the City's membership centre for adults aged 50 and over, beside Moody Park. The City states annual membership is $25 and covers social and activity clubs, fitness, education, counselling and peer support, with trained senior volunteers providing one-to-one peer support at no cost. It is a seniors' activity centre, not a care home or a housing complex. https://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/facilities/century-house/1000
- **Q:** What replaced the Canada Games Pool? **A:** The replacement is the təməsew̓txʷ Aquatic and Community Centre at 65 East Sixth Avenue, just east of the Moody Park area. The City states the name is the hən̓q̓əmin̓əm̓ word for “sea otter house,” chosen by a Naming Advisory Panel of local First Nations and urban Indigenous representatives, and that the facility replaced Canada Games Pool and the Centennial Community Centre. https://youractivenw.ca/new-aquatic-and-community-centre-to-be-named-t%c9%99m%c9%99sew%cc%93tx%ca%b7-aquatic-and-community-centre/
- **Q:** Which elementary schools sit in or beside the area? **A:** Two School District No. 40 (New Westminster) elementaries are in the wider pocket: Lord Kelvin Elementary, kindergarten to Grade 5, at 1010 Hamilton Street, and Lord Tweedsmuir Elementary, kindergarten to Grade 5, at 1714 Eighth Avenue. Proximity is not assignment: School District No. 40 assigns by home address, so confirm the catchment for the specific house before relying on either school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/new-westminster
