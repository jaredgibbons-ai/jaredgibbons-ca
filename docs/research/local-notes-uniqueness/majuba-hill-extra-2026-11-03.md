# Majuba Hill Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: majuba-hill (Majuba Hill; neighbourhood).

Shared facts for this area (boundaries, the park, the village schools and library, and plan history) are in full in the majuba-hill-guide note — see the majuba-hill-guide note. This note covers the hill itself only.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Majuba Hill Road is the hill's named access road, meeting Wilson Road at the City's one facility on the hill. The City lists Majuba Heritage Park at 4132 Wilson Rd, at Wilson Road and Majuba Hill Road. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=84
- The hill rises directly above the village of Yarrow, below Vedder Mountain, and no City neighbourhood plan covers the hill by name; the Yarrow Neighbourhood Plan, Schedule B7, covers the village below, not the hill. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2983

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The housing pattern on the hill is rural acreage, not urban lots. The former 2040 Official Community Plan (OCP) "Rural" designation described rural residential subdivision at a 1-hectare minimum and rural acreage subdivision at a 4-hectare minimum where permitted, and its "Forest Resource" designation covered rural hillsides including Majuba Hill, excluding Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) lands. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/common/downloads/publication.pdf
- No official source gives typical lot sizes or a home-type breakdown for existing Majuba Hill properties, so none is stated here. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/common/downloads/publication.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A development permit is required before subdivision, construction, land clearing or earthworks on the hill. Majuba Hill is a designated hillside area in the City's Hillside Environment Development Permit Area (Development Permit Area 2), and the City's DP2 Guide, March 2026, requires a geotechnical report with a hazard and slope assessment with the application. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- Slopes of 20% or more trigger the separate Hillside Development permit area. OCP Schedule D-5 states a development permit is required for subdivision of land with 20% or greater slopes, and for new construction or earthworks on 20% or greater slopes, with third-party review where a proposal involves 30% or greater slopes. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- Forest fire risk is assessed lot by lot in this permit area. OCP Schedule D-2 states land in the area may be subject to high forest fire risks and that a Fire Risk Assessment Analysis Report must identify and manage the forest fire interface. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8292
- Driveway width on a hillside lot is capped at 3.5 m at the curb face under guideline HD-37 of Schedule D-5, to minimise grading. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I clear and build on a Majuba Hill lot as of right? **A:** No — the hillside permit comes first. The City's DP2 Guide confirms Majuba Hill is a designated hillside/upland area, and a development permit is required before subdivision, construction, land clearing or earthworks. The application needs a geotechnical report with a hazard and slope assessment, so budget the report into the purchase, not after it. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- **Q:** Is there a school on the hill? **A:** No public school is located on Majuba Hill. The nearest school is Yarrow Community School, Kindergarten to Grade 5, at 4595 Wilson Rd N in the village below the hill, and SD33 catchments are address-based, so a specific Majuba Hill address must be checked in the district's catchment maps or locator. https://yarrow.sd33.bc.ca/
- **Q:** Which secondary school do hill students bus to? **A:** SD33's transportation procedure groups the Yarrow and Vedder Mountain Road areas for bus routes serving Yarrow Elementary School, Stitó:s Lá:lém Ta:lílé Middle School and GW Graham Secondary School. That is a bus-route grouping in the district's Administrative Procedure 611, not a catchment guarantee for a specific address, which the district locator decides. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2023-03/611%20AP%20Transportation%20-%20Operating%20Instructions.pdf
- **Q:** Is the hill on City water and sewer? **A:** The village below is not on the municipal network, and the City states there are no plans to extend municipal services to Yarrow. The Yarrow Neighbourhood Plan states Yarrow is serviced by Yarrow Water Works, a community-based water system, and private lot-by-lot septic systems, and that no municipal sewer or water extension is planned; a hill lot's own servicing must be verified per parcel. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799
- **Q:** Does snow change school busing here? **A:** Yes — Majuba Hill is one of the named higher-elevation areas in SD33's snow plan. The district's inclement weather guidelines state a Transportation Snow Plan is in place for students who bus and live in higher elevations such as Chilliwack Mountain, Little Mountain, Majuba Hill, Ryder Lake and some areas of Columbia Valley and the Eastern Hillsides. https://sd33.bc.ca/inclement-weather-school-closure-guidelines
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Part of the hill drains toward a municipal drinking-water watershed area: the former 2040 OCP development permit mapping covered "Municipal Watershed: Volkert Creek, Yarrow, Majuba Hill." https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/common/downloads/publication.pdf
- A storm water management plan is part of a hillside application: Schedule D-2 guideline HE-9 requires on-site drainage that does not adversely affect adjacent properties. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8292
- Fencing in the hillside environment area must be designed to allow the movement of wildlife, under guideline HE-2 of Schedule D-2. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8292
- No BC Transit route serves the hill itself; Route 55 Yarrow–Greendale serves the village below, with a stop at Yarrow Central Rd at Kehler St. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=55
- The nearest library branch is the Yarrow Library, 4670 Community Street, at the base of the hill. http://fvrl.bc.ca/locations/yarrow-library

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Majuba Hill Road and Wilson Road are the driving access, and there is no transit on the hill. BC Transit's Chilliwack route list contains no route serving Majuba Hill; Route 55 Yarrow–Greendale is the service in the village below. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/
- BC Transit's Chilliwack and Fraser Valley Regional District Transit Future Action Plan (2024) contemplates restructuring routes 55 and 59, which would affect Yarrow-area service generally; no Majuba Hill-specific service is proposed in it. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/2024-05-21-Chilliwack-and-FVRD-TFAP_final.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Get the geotechnical report before removing subjects on a sloped lot. Schedule D-2 guideline HE-8 requires a Qualified Professional's geotechnical study, following the Engineers and Geoscientists BC landslide assessment guidelines, certifying the development will not be endangered by rock fall, landslides, earthflow or other slope instability. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8292
- Wildfire interface is a named risk in this permit area, not a general caution: Schedule D-2 states the area may be subject to high forest fire risks and requires a Fire Risk Assessment Analysis Report. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8292
- The hillside schedules do not tell a buyer a lot's exact slope; Schedule D-5 sets the permit trigger at 20% slopes and third-party review at 30%, so a survey is what settles a specific lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- ALR parcels exist within and around the hillside area and remain designated Agricultural under the OCP, so check ALR status per address with the Agricultural Land Commission. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/common/downloads/publication.pdf
