## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The Heights sits at the 28 Avenue and 156 Street corner, on the seam between two City plan areas. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
- South of 28 Avenue is Morgan Heights, the first Grandview Heights neighbourhood plan area, bounded by 28 Avenue to the north, 24 Avenue to the south and 164 Street to the east. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The Heights is one mixed approval, not two separate complexes: City minutes for application 7908-0254-00 record 39 townhouse units plus 66 units in a four-storey apartment building on the assembled site at 2827, 2853 and 2871-156 Street. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_RCLU_2011-07-11.pdf
- The applicant named in those minutes is Lakewood Management Limited / Lakewood Heights Development Ltd., and the approval raised the apartment height in the neighbourhood plan from three storeys to four storeys. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_RCLU_2011-07-11.pdf
- This page covers only the 39 townhomes; the 66 apartment homes on the same approval are the subject of the grandview-heights-condos note — see the grandview-heights-condos note.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Heights was rezoned from One-Acre Residential (RA) to Comprehensive Development (CD), based on the Multiple Residential 30 (RM-30) zone, so its densities and setbacks live in its own CD bylaw, not in the standard RM-30 text. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_RCLU_2011-07-11.pdf
- The same approval amended the Official Community Plan (OCP) designation from Suburban to Multiple Residential, which is why this corner carries apartment height beside one-acre lots. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_RCLU_2011-07-11.pdf
- City-wide zoning, small-scale multi-unit housing and suite rules are in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Are The Heights townhomes and the condos beside them one strata or two? **A:** They were approved as one development. City minutes for application 7908-0254-00 describe a single proposal of 39 townhouse units and 66 units in a four-storey apartment building at 2827, 2853 and 2871-156 Street, by Lakewood Management Limited / Lakewood Heights Development Ltd. Read the strata plan and Form B for the specific lot to confirm how the two forms share common property and fees. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_RCLU_2011-07-11.pdf
- **Q:** Why does a four-storey building stand on a street of acreage lots? **A:** Council amended the plan to allow it. The 2011 approval for The Heights included a neighbourhood plan amendment increasing the apartment building height from three storeys to four storeys, an OCP amendment from Suburban to Multiple Residential, and rezoning from RA to CD based on RM-30. The taller form is a site-specific approval, not the pattern for the surrounding RA lots. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_RCLU_2011-07-11.pdf
- **Q:** Which elementary school is closest, and is it full? **A:** Sunnyside Elementary at 2828-159 Street is the nearby elementary school, and a 2018 City report records it at 24 classrooms including 7 portables, with a variance sought for two more portables. Portable count is a crowding signal, not a catchment ruling. Confirm the exact address in the Surrey Schools (School District 36) locator before relying on a school. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7918-0335-00.pdf
- **Q:** Is this Morgan Heights or North Grandview Heights? **A:** The complex sits on the boundary street between them. Morgan Heights runs from 24 Avenue to 28 Avenue, while North Grandview Heights runs from 28 Avenue to 32 Avenue, east of 156 Street. A lot's plan area follows its exact parcel, so check the specific strata lot in City mapping rather than relying on the corner description used in marketing. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/north-grandview-heights

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Sunnyside Elementary's site at 2828-159 Street is designated Elementary School in the North Grandview Heights Neighbourhood Concept Plan (NCP), even though the school serves the Morgan Heights side of 28 Avenue as well. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7918-0335-00.pdf
- Morgan Heights is centred on Southridge School, the independent school at 2656 160 Street, and its plan provided three new neighbourhood parks for this side of Grandview Heights. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
- The Morgan Heights plan is now largely built out and has been rescinded through the Surrey 2050 OCP update, so current land-use questions for this corner are answered from the OCP, not the old NCP. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
- Grandview Heights Secondary at 16987 25 Avenue was built at 1,500-student capacity to relieve overcrowding at Earl Marriott and Semiahmoo secondary schools. https://www.surreyschools.ca/capitalprojectoffice/page/181989/grandview-heights-secondary-new-school-site-177

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the CD bylaw, not just the RM-30 zone: The Heights' approval is a Comprehensive Development zone based on RM-30, so parking, setbacks and density for this site are in the site-specific bylaw attached to application 7908-0254-00. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_RCLU_2011-07-11.pdf
- Read the depreciation report and minutes before subjects: strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report under British Columbia's strata framework, and disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- Confirm the school portable picture at Sunnyside Elementary does not change your catchment: the 2018 variance file shows a school already using portables, and catchment is set by exact address in the district locator, not by distance. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7918-0335-00.pdf
