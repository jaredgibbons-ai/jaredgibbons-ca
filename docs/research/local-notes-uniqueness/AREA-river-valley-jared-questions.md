# River Valley — Questions for Jared

## Questions only Jared can answer

1. Who buys in the River Valley (Chilliwack River Valley, FVRD Electoral Area E) in your deals — fishing and recreation buyers, acreage buyers priced out of Chilliwack and Sardis, or full-time residents who work in the city?
2. What sells fast and what sits — homes in the serviced pockets (Baker Trails sewer, Bell Acres water) or the lower valley near Vedder Crossing, versus properties up past Post Creek toward Chilliwack Lake?
3. What surprises River Valley buyers most after they commit — that the FVRD, not the City of Chilliwack, is their local government, that most homes are on a well and septic, that there is no bus service, or that cell reception ends partway up the road?
4. How do the erosion setback line, the slope stability assessment and the Wilson Road dike work come up in your deals — do buyers, lenders or insurers ask for hazard reports before subjects come off?
5. Tell one River Valley deal story that shows what buying on Chilliwack Lake Road is really like — a servicing surprise, a hazard or insurance issue, or a sale that taught you what to check first up the valley.

## Shot list — photos Jared could take

1. Chilliwack Lake Road at Baker Trails, shot from street level along the settlement with the river and the valley slopes behind it, to show the serviced lower-valley end where most homes sit.
