Albion Station is at 10151 240 Street, Spencer Brook Estates at 23986 104 Avenue (strata plan EPS763, built 2015), and Rydge at 10640 248 Street has 61 townhomes by Raicon Developments, counted under Thornhill because the board classifies it there. https://pub-mapleridge.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=7611

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Townhouse zoning here is RM-1 (Low Density Townhouse Residential) under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019. A City public-hearing notice rezoned 11646–11654 228 Street from RS-1 to RM-1 for an 8-unit market strata townhouse development, which shows the usual route for new townhouse sites. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/241115/MRNN241115_A28.pdf
- Every complex above is strata, so the Form B Information Certificate, depreciation report and contingency reserve fund balance are the purchase documents; there is no Form B for a fee-simple home. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- No Maple Ridge strata townhouse complex is verified as 55-plus; the city's verified 55-plus strata are apartment buildings in Fraserview Village — see the maple-ridge-55-plus-condos-for-sale note. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Are Maple Ridge townhomes strata or freehold? **A:** They are strata in every complex verified in this program, and no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified city-wide. That means a strata corporation, monthly strata fees, bylaws and a Form B Information Certificate on a purchase at complexes such as Spencer Brook Estates, 23986 104 Avenue, and Brighton, 13819 232 Street. A seller who says a townhome is fee-simple should be checked on the title search before subjects are removed. https://pub-mapleridge.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=7611
- **Q:** Why is Rydge sometimes called Albion and sometimes Thornhill? **A:** The board classification governs here, and Rydge at 10640 248 Street is classified Thornhill even though its developer marketed it as an Albion project. The practical point for a buyer is catchment, tax and comparable selection: use the Thornhill sub-area for Rydge, not Albion, and confirm the School District 42 catchment for the exact address in the district's school locator before writing an offer. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- **Q:** Can I rent out a Maple Ridge townhome I buy? **A:** Yes for long-term rentals, because British Columbia made residential rental-restriction bylaws in strata corporations void on November 24, 2022. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals by bylaw, so a buyer planning short stays must read the current bylaws for that complex, for example at Cottonwood Terrace, 11355 Cottonwood Drive, rather than relying on an old building summary that still says rentals are not allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** Is any Maple Ridge townhouse complex adults-only? **A:** No verified 55-plus strata townhouse complex exists in Maple Ridge in this program. Haney Pioneer Village at 11830 230 Street is townhouse-shaped but is an 84-unit housing co-operative for seniors aged 60-plus, not a strata a buyer can purchase, and St. George's Village at 23580 Dewdney Trunk Road is also a co-operative. Buyers who need a strata purchase should look at Fraserview Village apartments instead. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- School catchments are address-based in School District 42, and no catchment for a specific townhouse address was verified in this program, so run the exact address, for example 23986 104 Avenue, in the district locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- Six Cottonwood-area complexes in the earlier program have no public source that states strata versus fee-simple tenure, so tenure there is a title-search question, not a listing question. https://pub-mapleridge.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=7611
- The Fraserview Village buildings near 116 Avenue are apartments, not townhouses, even when a listing photo makes them look like ground-oriented homes. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- Ridge Meadows Hospital at 11666 Laity Street is the Fraser Health community hospital serving both Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows, with 24-hour emergency service. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- Websters Corners and Whonnock recorded no attached housing stock in the September 2026 market data used by this program, because that rural east side is acreage housing under Agricultural Land Reserve rules. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Maple Ridge's 2026 rate summary lists the Class 1 Residential general municipal rate at 2.8643 per $1,000 of assessed value and a total residential mill rate of 4.53578 per $1,000; full city detail is in the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- For a multi-residential property the City's 2026 utility schedule lists a flat water fee of $815.62 and a flat sewer fee of $680.91, with metered customers billed quarterly. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in the City of Maple Ridge, which the Province lists as a designated taxable area in the Metro Vancouver Regional District. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Haney Place Exchange in the town centre is the hub for townhouse areas, with the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus to Coquitlam Central Station and route 701 toward Coquitlam Central and Maple Ridge East. https://www.transit.land/explore/operators/o-c28-translink
- The West Coast Express runs five weekday trains each way between Mission City Station and Waterfront Station, stopping at Maple Meadows Station, 20030 Dunn Avenue, and Port Haney Station, 22288 River Road; door-to-station time from any complex is UNCONFIRMED. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- Route 745 serves Cottonwood from Haney Place and route 746 serves Albion from Haney Place, which are the local links for the two biggest townhouse clusters. https://www.transit.land/explore/operators/o-c28-translink

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Flood status is now governed by the City of Maple Ridge Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 8121-2026, which designates floodplain in Schedule A and sets flood construction levels and setbacks; check a townhouse lot against Schedule A, especially near River Road and Port Haney. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
- The City states the new bylaw addresses the Fraser River, the Alouette River system, steep creeks such as Kanaka Creek, and groundwater flooding, and that a site-specific exemption process exists. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-climate-action-flood-resilience-environmental-protection-and-mckenney-creek
- Silver Valley and Thornhill townhouse sites sit below hillside and creek areas, so the bylaw's Debris Flow and Alluvial Fan Hazard Area in Schedule A is the layer to check for a specific lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
