# Cottonwood Maple Ridge Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: cottonwood-maple-ridge-houses-for-sale (Cottonwood, Maple Ridge; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Cottonwood is a board sub-area name only — the City does not list Cottonwood among its neighbourhoods and publishes no Cottonwood area plan or street-by-street boundary. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The area's official anchors are Kanaka Creek Elementary at 11120 234A Street and Alexander Robinson Elementary at 11849 238B Street, west of 240 Street and east of the Town Centre, with Kanaka Creek running through it to the Fraser River. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/
- Albion lies east beyond 240 Street, the Town Centre and East Central lie west, and Thornhill lies north. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- With no area plan, Cottonwood's detached stock is ordinary RS-zone subdivision housing under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019, organised around its two elementary schools and the Kanaka Creek corridor — for RS-1 lot minimums see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- No secondary school stands inside the sub-area; the two schools inside it are both Kindergarten to Grade 7 elementaries, Kanaka Creek and Alexander Robinson. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The rule that matters most on Cottonwood lots is the creek: Kanaka Creek is a salmon-bearing watercourse protected inside Kanaka Creek Regional Park, so lots backing onto the creek corridor carry watercourse setbacks that interior lots do not. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- There is no Cottonwood plan to add density: rezoning or added-unit expectations here rest on the Official Community Plan's general policies and the city-wide suite and SSMUH rules in the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note, not on a neighbourhood plan. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q: Which secondary school follows Kanaka Creek Elementary?** **A:** For the Kanaka Creek Elementary address itself, 11120 234A Street, the School District 42 locator gives Samuel Robertson Technical Secondary, at 10445 245 Street, for Grades 8 to 12. Catchments split by street in this sub-area, so that pairing is an example, not a promise for every Cottonwood address — run the exact address in the locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: Is there a bus named for this area?** **A:** Yes — TransLink's route 745 is the Haney Place / Cottonwood service on the Haney Place route diagram. It interlines with the 746 Haney Place / Albion at Jackson Road at 103 Avenue, forming one circular service, so a Cottonwood rider's trip to the Town Centre exchange runs through or toward Albion depending on direction. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/194/r748.pdf
- **Q: What does backing onto Kanaka Creek actually mean?** **A:** It means the lot borders a regional park corridor on a salmon-bearing creek. Metro Vancouver's park follows Kanaka Creek through this part of Maple Ridge with the Canyon Trail to Cliff Falls and the Bell-Irving Hatchery, and it warns of steep, slippery cliffs along the creek, especially at Cliff Falls — so setbacks, slope and drainage need checking, not just the view. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a Cottonwood house?** **A:** Yes, under the city-wide rule: one suite per lot, inside the single detached building, at most 40% of the building's gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area, with a building permit required. The Cottonwood-specific caution is to confirm the lot is outside the mapped floodplain and creek setbacks before counting on rental income. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The waste and recycling address for this side of town is in the sub-area's backyard: Metro Vancouver's Maple Ridge Recycling and Waste Centre and the free Ridge Meadows Recycling Depot are both at 10092 236 Street. https://www.mapleridge.ca/resident-services/waste-and-recycling-services-maple-ridge
- Kanaka Creek Regional Park's trails are the local recreation system here — the Canyon Trail runs from the fish hatchery to Cliff Falls at 1.2 km one way and the Riverfront Trails run 3 km one way at the creek oxbow and the Fraser River. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- No City recreation centre or library branch sits in Cottonwood; the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre is at 11925 Haney Place and Maple Ridge Public Library is at 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road, both in the Town Centre. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Cottonwood's transit is the 745 Haney Place / Cottonwood circular, interlining with the 746 to Albion at Jackson Road at 103 Avenue — there is no West Coast Express station in the sub-area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/194/r748.pdf
- Local streets named in TransLink routing for this area include 232 Street, 238B Street, Kanaka Way, 240 Street and 104 Avenue, linking the house streets to Haney Place Exchange in the Town Centre. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/194/r748.pdf
- East–west driving is on Lougheed Highway to the south and Dewdney Trunk Road to the north of the sub-area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/194/r748.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Creek-side lots are the due-diligence priority: Metro Vancouver flags steep, slippery cliffs along Kanaka Creek at Cliff Falls, and the park corridor means setback and slope questions on any lot backing onto the creek. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- Flood status for a specific Cottonwood lot is read under Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 8121-2026 and its Schedule A — see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note; no Cottonwood-wide flood finding is made here. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
- No Agricultural Land Reserve land inside Cottonwood was identified on the sources read; ALR status remains a per-parcel check on the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
