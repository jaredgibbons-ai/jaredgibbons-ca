# Hatzic 55+ Homes — Extra Local Notes
Page: hatzic-55-plus-homes-for-sale (Hatzic; 55+ homes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Hatzic is two governments under one page name: the bench streets inside the City of Mission (Ferguson Avenue, Jasper Avenue, McEwen Avenue, Ferndale Avenue and Dewdney Trunk Road) sit beside Hatzic Prairie and Hatzic Island, which are Fraser Valley Regional District electoral-area ground, all carrying Mission mailing addresses. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf
- The lake itself is the landmark: Hatzic Lake lies east of Mission proper, with the bench above and west of it, Hatzic Island in its centre, and Hatzic Prairie to the north; the west shore is in the City of Mission and the rest is unincorporated regional-district land. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf
- The Fraser Valley Regional District plan for Hatzic Valley bounds that valley by the City of Mission in the west, Hatzic Lake to the south, Durieu Ridge to the east and Stave Lake to the north. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf
- School District No. 75 (Mission) serves Hatzic, with Hatzic Elementary at 8465 Draper Street and Hatzic Middle School at 34875 Moffat Avenue in the bench area, but a specific lake or prairie address still needs a district catchment check. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The only verified 55+ purchase product actually in Hatzic is a manufactured-home park, not a strata: Mission Mobile Home Park at 34519 Lougheed Highway, where park material describes a 55+ adult-oriented park and the buyer owns the home and rents the pad. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf
- No age-restricted strata complex with a Hatzic address was verified in this pass; the nearest verified 55+ strata ownership sits in Mission's urban core, Cherry Lane at 32165 7th Avenue and Luther Place at 7525 Martin Place, so a Hatzic buyer who wants strata 55+ is shopping outside Hatzic itself. https://www.missionseniors.ca/our-communities/cedar-valley-manor/
- The nearest verified life-lease seniors option is also in the core, not Hatzic: Cedar Valley Manor at 8358 Cedar Street, described by its operator as a life lease for people aged 55 and over, which is an occupancy interest rather than freehold ownership. https://www.missionseniors.ca/our-communities/cedar-valley-manor/
- Bench houses on city lots in Hatzic carry no age rule in any source read; they are ordinary freehold houses, and lake resort and park names in listing text (Everglades Resort, Green Acres Mobile Park) are UNCONFIRMED as 55+ on any park or resort source. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The general British Columbia Strata Property Act 55+ framework is not repeated here; see the chilliwack-55-plus-townhomes-for-sale note for the full framework, including that only 55+ age bylaws are now legal in strata and that rental-restriction bylaws are void. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Hatzic's 55+ rule works differently because it is a park rule, not a strata bylaw: pad tenancies fall under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, with rent rises limited to once in 12 months on at least three full months' notice, capped at the provincial limit (2026 = 2.3% plus a proportional share of levy and regulated-utility changes; 2027 = 2.2% plus a proportional share). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- Inside the City of Mission, a secondary suite must be registered with the City and sit inside a single-family home, and suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; in Electoral Area F a suite must sit within the principal dwelling and a detached garden suite is not the Area F suite route. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- Farmland on the prairie side in the Agricultural Land Reserve follows the Agricultural Land Commission housing frame of one principal residence plus at most one additional residence, sized by parcel size, so a second dwelling needs an Agricultural Land Commission check for the parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What 55+ home can I actually buy in Hatzic? **A:** A manufactured home on a rented pad at Mission Mobile Home Park at 34519 Lougheed Highway is the only verified 55+ purchase in Hatzic itself. You own the home, not the land under it, and the park's own rules — not a strata bylaw — set who may live there, so read those rules and the pad tenancy terms before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Q:** Is Hatzic part of the City of Mission? **A:** Only partly. The bench is inside the City, while Hatzic Prairie is Electoral Area F and Hatzic Island is Electoral Area G of the Fraser Valley Regional District, so tax, zoning and bylaw answers change with the parcel even though every address carries a Mission mailing address. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf
- **Q:** Who do I pay property tax to in Hatzic? **A:** It depends on the parcel. Bench properties pay the City of Mission, with a flat 10% penalty on balances outstanding after the July due date, while rural prairie and island properties are taxed by the Province's Surveyor of Taxes, with 5% penalties after July 2 and a further 5% after October 31. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- **Q:** Can my spouse under 55 live with me in the park? **A:** Park rules decide, not the strata exemption. The younger-spouse exemption in the strata regulation does not automatically apply inside a manufactured-home park, so get the park's current written rule for a younger spouse before you write an offer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** Is a Hatzic Lake property flood-exposed? **A:** Treat it as a parcel question. The valley has a documented 1948 flood in which a dyke along Hatzic Lake broke and Hatzic Prairie was largely under water, and low ground here sits behind the Dewdney Dyke system, so check the specific lot against current floodplain mapping and dyke protection. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The mailing address misleads on governance: a Ferguson Avenue house and a Shook Road island property both say Mission, yet one pays City tax under City bylaws and the other pays rural provincial tax, so budget the wrong regime if you assume the address settles it. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf
- Mission Memorial Hospital at 7324 Hurd Street in Mission provides the nearest acute care, with a 25-bed medical inpatient unit, a 20-bed Patient Assessment and Transition Home unit and a 10-bed hospice, while no assisted-living residence with a Hatzic address appears in the provincial registry. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/mission/mission-memorial-hospital
- Three tenures sit on one page: a freehold bench house, a resort use-area lot and a pad-rented manufactured home are different assets with different financing and resale structures, so tenure must come from park or resort documents, not a listing card. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf
- The City's current plan is Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026, which replaced Bylaw 6350-2025, but that City plan does not govern the regional-district parts of Hatzic at all. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan
- Hatzic Lake access is not a title right by default: dock rights, moorage and beach tenure for any specific Shook Road property are UNCONFIRMED on any official source read, so confirm shoreline tenure on title before paying for water access. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The City of Mission's tax-rates page explains that rates are set by bylaw before May 15 each year, that municipal tax funds police, fire, street lighting, roads and parks, and that additional taxes are levied for diking, but the exact 2026 residential rate per $1,000 was not readable from the municipal rate schedule in this pass and is UNCONFIRMED here. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- Rural Hatzic properties are taxed under the provincial rural system, not the City system, with the Surveyor of Taxes notice and the 5% plus 5% penalty pattern described in section 4, so a single Hatzic tax figure cannot cover both sides of the line. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in the District of Mission, which the Province lists as a designated taxable area, so an owner in the City part of Hatzic must file the annual declaration even if exempt as a principal residence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The rail link is Mission City Station on the West Coast Express, with five weekday westbound trains in the early morning and five eastbound returns and no weekend or holiday service, per TransLink's timetable for the line serving Mission. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) is the road spine through Hatzic, crossing the southern end of Hatzic Lake beside the Canadian Pacific main line, so highway and rail noise are a lot-specific check on the southern bench and island approaches. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Flood is the headline risk: Hatzic Prairie and the lake lowlands sit in Fraser River floodplain ground behind the Dewdney Dyke system, with the 1948 dyke-break history in section 4, so the buyer looks up the specific lot in current floodplain mapping and confirms dyke protection rather than relying on the page name. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf
- The Agricultural Land Reserve covers much of the inhabited Hatzic Valley, so a prairie parcel may carry Agricultural Land Commission housing limits that cap a second residence, and the map does not tell a buyer whether a specific lot is in the reserve without a parcel check. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Septic and well rules are a rural-lot question on the prairie and island sides, where municipal sewer and water cannot be assumed; the servicing for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED on any source read and must be confirmed with the regional district for the parcel. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf
