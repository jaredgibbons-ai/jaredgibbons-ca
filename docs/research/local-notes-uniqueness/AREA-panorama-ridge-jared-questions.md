# Panorama Ridge — Questions for Jared

1. In Panorama Ridge deals, what do you see differ between the half-acre ridge lots along 125A Street and Southridge Drive and the smaller lots north of Highway 10 toward 64 Avenue — subjects, view premiums, or how hard buyers push on the ridge addresses?
2. Who buys on Panorama Ridge: families targeting Colebrook Elementary and École Panorama Ridge Secondary, downsizers staying on the ridge, or builders hunting older houses on large lots — and what do they say tipped it?
3. What sells fast here and what sits: do the south-facing view lots looking toward Mud Bay move first, while houses backing onto Highway 10 or 152 Street wait — and do older ranchers sell as houses or as land?
4. What surprises Panorama Ridge buyers: the drop from the ridge into the Colebrook Road farmland and floodplain below, Highway 10 noise on the north edge, or having Panorama Ridge Riding Club and the equestrian trails in Joe Brown Park as neighbours?
5. Tell one Panorama Ridge story: a deal on Southridge Drive or 125A Street where the view, the lot size, or frontage onto Joe Brown Park changed the price, the subjects, or the outcome.
