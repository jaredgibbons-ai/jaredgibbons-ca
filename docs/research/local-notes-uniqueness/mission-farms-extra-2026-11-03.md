## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Mission farm land is the rural east and north of the city, not the urban core: the farm districts are Dewdney–Deroche, Durieu, Hatzic, Lake Errock and Stave Falls, reached along Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and the Dewdney Trunk Road corridor. ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/mission-farms-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md
- This page's ground straddles two governments, and the split is exact: Stave Falls and Steelhead are inside the City of Mission, Hatzic Bench is inside the City, Hatzic Prairie is Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area F, and Hatzic Island is FVRD Electoral Area G. ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/mission-farms-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md
- The Fraser River is the farm belt's south edge: the Silverdale Comprehensive Planning Area, at the city's west end, is bordered to the south by the Fraser River and Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) lands. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/lan64c-silverdale-comprehensive-planning-area-neighbourhood-planning-terms-referencepdf
- Mission is in the Fraser Valley Regional District, not Metro Vancouver, which is why its transit, regional taxes and planning referrals run through the FVRD and BC Transit rather than Metro Vancouver and TransLink. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- Farm families are served by School District No. 75 (Mission) rural schools including Deroche Elementary at 10340 N Deroche Rd, Dewdney Elementary at 37151 Hawkins-Pickle Rd and Hatzic Elementary at 8465 Draper St, with catchments set by home address. ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/mission-farms-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Mission farms trade as whole fee-simple farm parcels — working farms, bare agricultural land and rural acreages with farm potential — bought whole with their buildings, water and septic position, not as serviced lots. ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/mission-farms-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md
- There is no bare-land strata farm product here, and on ALR parcels the strata form is barred outright: residences in the ALR cannot be stratified without the Agricultural Land Commission's permission, because depositing a strata plan is a subdivision. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- The province's own farm inventory describes Mission's pattern: the BC Ministry of Agriculture's Agricultural Land Use Inventory (2011 inventory, published 2013) found a greater number of smaller farms than larger operations in Mission, across a variety of livestock and crop operations. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/farming-natural-resources-and-industry/agriculture-and-seafood/agricultural-land-and-environment/strengthening-farming/land-use-inventories/missionlui2011rprt_2013-08-26_final.pdf
- The ALR share is small and dated, so check current: that same provincial inventory puts Mission's ALR at 1,539 hectares, nearly 7% of the District of Mission's 22,422 hectare land area at the time of the 2011 inventory — check current, as the figure is more than 12 months old. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/farming-natural-resources-and-industry/agriculture-and-seafood/agricultural-land-and-environment/strengthening-farming/land-use-inventories/missionlui2011rprt_2013-08-26_final.pdf
- No presale lot offering and no bare-land subdivision product is verified for Mission farm land; the one verified Mission presale in this program's research is an urban apartment project, not farm stock. ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/mission-farms-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City-side zoning is Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020, as amended — the City's 2025 housekeeping package amends it as Bylaw 6354-2025-5949(187) — while FVRD-side parcels are zoned under FVRD bylaws, so the governing bylaw is a jurisdiction check first. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-zoning-bylaw-housekeeping-amendments
- The current Official Community Plan is OCP Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026, which replaced and repealed OCP Bylaw 6350-2025 with the vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction unchanged and a Regional Context Statement added. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026
- The City's Rural Mission Strategy — its plan to recognize, protect and enhance Mission's rural areas, building on the OCP — is finalized and posted by the City. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/rural-mission-strategy-now-live
- ALR subdivision requires Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) approval, and the residence caps are fixed: one principal residence per parcel at a maximum 500 square metres, a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, and one additional residence without an ALC application where conditions are met — capped at 90 square metres on parcels of 40 hectares or less and 186 square metres on larger parcels, if local bylaws also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Subdivision and servicing on the City side runs under the Development and Subdivision Control Bylaw 5650-2017, listed in the City's own bylaws library. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/bylaws-policies?field_types_target_id=All&page=2
- Flood rules are bylaw-set on the low ground: Mission's Floodplain Management Bylaw 4027-2007 specifies floodplain setbacks and flood construction levels, including a 60 metre setback from the natural boundary of the Fraser River, 15 metres from any other stream, and 7.5 metres from the inboard toe of a dyke. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/silverdale-mis-geotech
- Farm classification has three income tiers, never one flat figure: $2,500 of gross farm income at farm gate prices on a farm operation of 0.8 to 4 hectares, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 hectares above that, and $10,000 on operations under 0.8 hectares, with applications by October 31 for the next taxation year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is this farm inside the City of Mission or the FVRD — and why does it matter? **A:** It decides the tax penalty, the zoning bylaw and the planning authority. Stave Falls, Steelhead and Hatzic Bench are City ground, where a flat 10% penalty applies to unpaid taxes after July 2; Hatzic Prairie (Area F) and Hatzic Island (Area G) are FVRD rural ground under the separate rural regime of 5% after July 2 plus 5% after October 31. A Mission mailing address settles nothing — check the parcel's tax folio. ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/mission-farms-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md
- **Q:** Can I build a second house on a Mission farm? **A:** On ALR land, one additional residence is permitted without an Agricultural Land Commission application where its conditions are met: detached, and capped at 90 square metres on parcels of 40 hectares or less or 186 square metres on larger parcels. The principal residence itself is capped at 500 square metres, a secondary suite is permitted only inside it, and City of Mission or FVRD bylaws must also allow the additional residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q:** How much income must the farm produce to keep farm classification? **A:** It depends on the operation's size, in three tiers: $2,500 of gross farm income at farm gate prices for an operation of 0.8 to 4 hectares; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 hectares for a larger operation; and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 hectares. A continuing farmer applies by October 31 for the next taxation year, and a parcel that stops meeting its tier loses the classification. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q:** Can I irrigate from the well or creek? **A:** Only with the licence position confirmed. Under the Water Sustainability Act, groundwater used for a non-domestic purpose such as irrigation requires a water licence, with water fees and rentals payable, before the water can lawfully be used; domestic groundwater use does not require authorization. Ask for the licence, or evidence a licence is not needed, in the diligence package for any farm that depends on irrigation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- **Q:** Does the Speculation and Vacancy Tax apply in Mission? **A:** Yes. The Province's taxable-areas page lists the District of Mission as a designated taxable area for the Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT), so owners of residential property there must complete the annual declaration, with reserve, treaty and self-governing Indigenous Nation lands excluded. Farm parcels classed and used as farm land are a separate assessment question — confirm the specific property's class and any exemption with the Province rather than assuming the farm use removes the declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Q:** Is the low farm in the floodplain? **A:** The low districts are the check. Mission's Floodplain Management Bylaw 4027-2007 sets flood construction levels and setbacks — 60 metres from the Fraser River's natural boundary — and on Hatzic Island the FVRD's Floodplain Management Bylaw 0681, 2005 sets a flood construction level of 9.3 metres geodetic. A parcel's level against those figures is a survey question, not a listing statement. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/silverdale-mis-geotech

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City of Mission property taxes for 2026 were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, and a flat 10% penalty is added to any current balances outstanding, including unclaimed home owner grants, on July 3, 2026; the City states it has no authority to waive or modify the penalty. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- Tax rates are set by class each year under the City's tax rates framework, with municipal rates set by the City, school rates set by the Province, regional district rates set by the Fraser Valley Regional District, and additional taxes levied for diking. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- Development cost charges (DCCs) are imposed under Development Cost Charge Bylaw 6348-2025, adopted May 5, 2025, and apply to every person who obtains approval of a subdivision, a building permit for a commercial, industrial or institutional use, or a building permit for a residential building with more than one dwelling unit other than a secondary suite. https://www.mission.ca/planning/development-amenity-cost-charges
- The DCC bylaw's stated purposes are the capital costs of sewage, water, drainage, fire protection, police, highway and solid waste and recycling facilities, and providing and improving park land, to service the development charged. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/6348-2025-development-cost-charge-0
- Amenity Cost Charges (ACCs) are imposed under Amenity Cost Charge Bylaw 6349-2025, also adopted May 5, 2025, replacing the Community Amenity Contribution for developers, payable at subdivision approval or building permit issuance. https://www.mission.ca/planning/development-amenity-cost-charges
- Mission is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax — the Province's list names the District of Mission — so residential owners must complete the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- Mission has no Supreme Court registry, so a court-ordered farm sale's filing venue is a separate check and is UNVERIFIED in this program's registry ledger. ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/mission-farms-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Mission City Station is the eastern terminus of the West Coast Express, on the line running through Port Coquitlam, Coquitlam Central, Moody Centre and Port Moody-side stations to Waterfront — the commuter rail link for the farm districts west of the city. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- Local buses are BC Transit, not TransLink: the Central Fraser Valley Transit System serves Mission, and its Mission routes include the 35 Hatzic route into the farm district east of downtown. https://bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley
- The Valley Connector bus routes link Mission and Abbotsford across the Fraser River on the Abbotsford–Mission Highway (Highway 11), the same crossing a farm operator uses for the Abbotsford side. https://bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley
- Highway 7 (Lougheed Highway) is the farm belt's east–west road through Dewdney, Deroche and Hatzic; no BC Transit or TransLink page read for this note states a drive time from the farm districts, so times are UNCONFIRMED. https://bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the flood construction level and setback for the parcel first on low ground: Mission's Floodplain Management Bylaw 4027-2007 governs City-side parcels, and in the Silverdale area the bylaw's Fraser River flood construction level is approximately 9.1 metres geodetic at the east side and 8.6 metres at the west side. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/silverdale-mis-geotech
- Check the FVRD regime on the other side of the boundary: Hatzic Island, in Electoral Area G, sits inside the Fraser River floodplain under the FVRD Floodplain Management Bylaw 0681, 2005, with a flood construction level of 9.3 metres geodetic and a minimum ponding elevation of 5.2 metres. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=23504
- Check ALR status per parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission mapping by parcel identifier (PID) or civic address before pricing any residence, subdivision or non-farm use; the ALC states its online tools are for general reference, with official confirmation from its Mapping and GIS team. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- Check soil and agricultural capability per parcel in the BC Soil Information Finder: no soil class for any specific Mission farm parcel is stated in the sources read for this note. ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/mission-farms-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md
- Check the water and septic file with the offer, not after it: irrigation water needs a licence position under the Water Sustainability Act, and a non-sewered farm home's onsite system must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation and be handled by an Authorized Person. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
