## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Matsqui is the board sub-area of Abbotsford on the Fraser River's south bank, and its shared area-wide facts are carried in the earlier matsqui page note — this note keeps only the houses, lot and flood angle. (See the matsqui note.)
- The City of Abbotsford's planning name for the same ground is Matsqui Prairie, and its one settled centre is Matsqui Village on Riverside Road, where Matsqui Village Park sits at 6074 Riverside Road. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds
- Highway 11 is the area's north-south road, and its bus route runs from Bourquin Exchange through Matsqui Village on Riverside Road toward Mission City Station. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=31

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK

- Houses here are single-detached homes on agricultural and rural lots, and the agricultural zones set the lot floor: the Agricultural One (A1) zone's minimum subdivision size is 8.0 ha and the Agricultural Two (A2) zone's is 16.0 ha. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- The housing is freehold farm and rural-residential land, and the City's AgRefresh work records the agricultural pattern as two designations, Agriculture 1 – Uplands on A1 patterns and Agriculture 2 – Lowlands on A2 patterns. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- The village's own school-sized anchor is Máthxwi Elementary at 33661 Elizabeth Avenue, which the school's site states serves approximately 150 students and their families. https://mathxwi.abbyschools.ca/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE

- The one-residence rule governs most prairie parcels, because land in the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless the owner qualifies for an additional residence under the Agricultural Land Commission rules. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- A principal residence in the ALR is capped in size, and the Commission states it must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- A suite in the ALR can only sit inside the principal residence, because the Commission states a secondary suite is permitted in the ALR only when it is entirely contained within the principal residence and local bylaws permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- City-wide zoning treatment is carried in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note. (See the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note.)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

**Q:** Can I build a second house on a Matsqui farm lot?
**A:** One additional detached residence is possible, but only within the Commission's size caps. The Agricultural Land Commission states that a property may have one additional residence if there is only one existing residence when construction begins, capped at 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, and City zoning must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

**Q:** What floodproofing applies to a house on the prairie floor?
**A:** Matsqui Prairie is floodproofing Area "E" in the Zoning Bylaw. Section 140.7 sets full floodproofing at 9.8 m GSC for Area "E" on Schedule "E", with reduced floodproofing at 6.0 m GSC, 1.2 m above ground level and 0.3 m above the crown of the nearest road, for buildings behind standard dykes; buildings not behind dykes fall under the full provisions. Read the lot's position against the dyke before designing a build. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/42052/

**Q:** Which school is in Matsqui Village?
**A:** Máthxwi Elementary is the school in the village, at 33661 Elizabeth Avenue. The school's own site gives that address and states it serves approximately 150 students and their families, and the district's catchments page states that each student's catchment school is determined by the address of their primary home, checked through the School Locator. https://mathxwi.abbyschools.ca/ https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments

**Q:** Can I subdivide a prairie parcel for a family member?
**A:** Subdivision on the prairie runs into the agricultural minimums first. The City's AgRefresh report records the Zoning Bylaw minimum subdivision sizes as 8.0 ha in the A1 zone and 16.0 ha in the A2 zone, and subdivision inside the ALR also involves the Agricultural Land Commission, which can approve or refuse the application forwarded by Council. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf

**Q:** Is there a bus through the village?
**A:** Yes — only the 31A trips stop in the village, not every route 31 trip. BC Transit's route 31 schedule runs between Bourquin Exchange and Mission City Station, and its timetable shows a Matsqui Village stop on 31A trips in both directions, while 31 and 31X trips pass the stop without a listed time. Confirm the variant before planning a trip around the bus. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=31

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- Matsqui Village Park is the village's park, at 6074 Riverside Road, listed in the City's parks list with a ball diamond, basketball and sports court, tennis court, playground, picnic shelter, washrooms, trails and a parking lot. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds
- Rural water service is capped by bylaw, and the City's Waterworks Regulation Bylaw limits water service connections for properties outside the Urban Development Boundary to a maximum of 25 mm in diameter. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/55400/
- A registered suite is taxed on the annual bill, and the City states registered owners are billed annually for property taxes, solid waste collection, secondary suites and other charges in one billing. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS

- The Fraser River Bank Erosion Protection project is the major infrastructure work touching Matsqui housing land, and the City states it will stabilize approximately three kilometres of the Fraser River riverbank along the northern boundary of Abbotsford to reduce flood risk for the Matsqui Prairie. Status read 2026-11-03: funded and announced, with more than $19 million committed by the governments of Canada and British Columbia with the City of Abbotsford. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/governments-canada-and-british-columbia-invest-fraser-river-bank-erosion

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE

- The full city tax treatment is carried in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note; the Matsqui angle is the dyking bill on top of it. (See the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note.)
- Properties in the Matsqui Prairie Dyking, Drainage and Irrigation District pay a district tax rate of 0.71196 per $1,000 of assessed value for 2026, under Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf
- The district also levies per-acre parcel taxes, set for 2026 at $28.00 per acre in the Matsqui Prairie Dyking Area and $9.33, $32.71 and $35.13 per acre in irrigation Zones 2, 3 and 4. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf
- Abbotsford's 2026 residential (Class 1) municipal rates are 1.08284 per $1,000 for police and 1.78296 per $1,000 for general and debt servicing, before the dyking amounts above. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE

- Check the lot's floodproofing area first, because Matsqui Prairie is Area "E" on Schedule "E" of the Zoning Bylaw, with full floodproofing at 9.8 m GSC and reduced provisions only for buildings behind standard dykes. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/42052/
- Check the setbacks from water and dykes before siting any building, because Zoning Bylaw section 140.7 sets a 60.0 m setback from the Fraser River within the Fraser River floodplain, 15.0 m from the inboard toe of a dyke, and 7.5 m from ditches. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/42052/
- Check the dyke's condition as part of diligence, because the City states the Matsqui Dike protects the prairie and that bank erosion could create a dike breach, which is the risk the Fraser River Bank Erosion Protection project is funded to reduce. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/governments-canada-and-british-columbia-invest-fraser-river-bank-erosion
- Confirm ALR status before valuing a second dwelling, because the Commission's one-residence rule and size caps apply on ALR parcels whether or not the listing mentions them. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
