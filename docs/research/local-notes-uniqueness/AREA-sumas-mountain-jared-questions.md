# Sumas Mountain, Abbotsford — Questions for Jared
Area: Sumas Mountain, Abbotsford (detached houses).

## 5 questions only Jared can answer
1. In your Sumas Mountain deals, who buys where — do families take the serviced Auguston and McKee bench subdivisions while acreage buyers take Straiton and the upper mountain, and where do the two groups overlap?
2. What sells fast and what sits on the mountain: do view houses on the lower benches move first, while well-and-septic acreages wait, and how much do slope, driveway grade and quarry truck routes matter in the final price?
3. What surprises mountain buyers most after acceptance — a septic or well result, a steep-slope or creek setback that kills an addition, an informal trail crossing the lot, or a lot that turns out to be in FVRD (Fraser Valley Regional District) Area H, not the City?
4. How is the McKee Neighbourhood Plan build-out changing your deals on the lower mountain — are buyers paying for the coming neighbourhood, or discounting for years of construction and trail changes above them?
5. Tell one Sumas Mountain deal story: a sale, a near-miss, or a problem you solved on a Sumas Mountain Road, Dawson Road, Straiton Road or Whatcom Road property that shows a buyer something no listing could.
