# East Delta — Questions for Jared

Area: East Delta (City of Delta — the rural/farm side east of the Highway 91 corridor). Five questions only Jared can answer from deals he has actually done or shown in the area. Do not answer these from research.

1. When you work an East Delta farm or rural-residential deal, who is on the other side — working farmers, hobby-farm buyers, or land bankers waiting on the city to grow east?
2. What do buyers underestimate most out here — water and drainage, septic, ALR (Agricultural Land Reserve) limits on what they can build, or how the farm-use rules actually bite?
3. How do East Delta deals differ from your normal residential files — longer due diligence, different lenders or appraisers, more conditions falling apart?
4. Which East Delta properties move and which sit for you — road frontage, soil quality, an existing house, or a clean rectangular parcel?
5. Tell one East Delta story: a farm deal, a failed condition, or a buyer surprise that captures what buying land out here is really like.

## Photo / drone shot list for Jared

1. Ladner Trunk Road (Highway 10) farm fields looking east toward the Highway 91 corridor — the scale of the remaining farmland in one frame.
2. A Burns Bog edge shot from an East Delta road allowance — the bog is the area's defining neighbour and buyers should see it.
3. A working-farm streetscape on one of the numbered east–west roads (for example 64 Avenue or 72 Street), shot low so the ditch, field and farmhouse read together.
