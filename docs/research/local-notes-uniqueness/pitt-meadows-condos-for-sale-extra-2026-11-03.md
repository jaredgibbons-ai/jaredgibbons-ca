## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Every Pitt Meadows condo purchase runs on the same two documents: the Form B Information Certificate and the depreciation report the strata must now renew every five years. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- City-wide strata and short-term rental rules are written in full in the south-meadows-condos-townhomes-for-sale note; they apply unchanged to a Harris Road apartment. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- A 55-plus bylaw is the only age bylaw a British Columbia strata may still enforce, under sections 123.1 and 123.2 of the Strata Property Act — no city condo above is published as 55-plus in the sources read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Where are Pitt Meadows condos actually located? **A:** Almost all of them stand on or just off Harris Road, not spread across the city. The named buildings run from Origin at 11950 Harris Road in the centre to Liv42 at 12409 Harris Road and Cedar Downs at 12565 190A Street in the north pocket, with The Fairways alone on Meadow Gardens Way by the golf course. A condo listing with a Bonson Road address is almost certainly a townhome instead. {Research file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-condos-2026-11-01.md}
- **Q:** How old is the condo stock? **A:** The dated buildings in the city file span 2007 to 2015, so there is no pre-2000 apartment stock in the verified list. The Fairways and Origin date to 2007 and 2008, Solaris to 2010 and Liv42 to 2015 in the earlier program records. That age band is why the first mandatory depreciation reports matter here: every one of these stratas is now in the five-year reporting cycle. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q:** Can I buy a condo and rent it out long-term? **A:** A strata rental cap cannot stop you, because long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable province-wide since November 24, 2022. Short-term rental use is the opposite case in Pitt Meadows, where the City prohibits it under its zoning bylaw. Read the south-meadows note for both layers in full before underwriting any rental income on a Harris Road unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q:** Is there a new condo presale in Pitt Meadows? **A:** No current residential presale project in Pitt Meadows was verified on a developer or municipal page in this program's passes. The development section below lists the municipal projects that were found instead, which are an area plan, a school replacement and road works. A presale marketed under a Pitt Meadows name should be checked against the developer's own disclosure statement before any deposit. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/39

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Pitt Meadows has one public secondary school only, Pitt Meadows Secondary at 19438 116B Avenue, so every condo address in the city feeds the same grades 8 to 12 school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- The city has four public elementary schools in the district list: Pitt Meadows Elementary (11941 Harris Road), Davie Jones (12030 Blakely Road), Edith McDermott (12178 Bonson Road) and Highland Park (18961 Advent Road). https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- A replacement Pitt Meadows Secondary is in design and development after August 2024 provincial funding, per School District 42's facilities plan page. https://www.sd42.ca/board-of-education/strategic-facilities-plan/
- The airport, Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK) at 18799 Airport Way, sits in the city's south-west, under the flight paths a west-side condo buyer should ask about. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport
- City-wide tax and dike detail: see the south-meadows-condos-townhomes-for-sale note. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
