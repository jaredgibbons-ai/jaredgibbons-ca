# Belcarra Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: belcarra-port-moody (Belcarra; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Belcarra is its own municipality, the Village of Belcarra, not part of the City of Port Moody: it sits on a narrow peninsula at the mouth of Indian Arm, northwest of Port Moody and Anmore, with a land area of about 5.50 square kilometres and a population of 687 in the 2021 Census. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/emergency-management-service-delivery.pdf
- One road runs the village: Bedwell Bay Road enters from Port Moody, becomes the spine past the Village Hall at 4084 Bedwell Bay Road, and the residential streets split into the Belcarra Bay side (Belcarra Bay Road, Salish Road, Whiskey Cove Lane) and the Bedwell Bay side (Kelly Avenue, Senkler Road, Turtlehead Road). https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2026/07/Public-Notice-of-Zoning-Bylaw-Amendment-463-2026-Readings.pdf
- The village was incorporated on August 22, 1979, and its Indigenous name is təmtəmíxʷtən (Tum-Tumay-Whueton), an ancestral village of the Tsleil-Waututh Nation. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/emergency-management-service-delivery.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is almost entirely detached houses: the Village's tax base is almost all residential, and the zoning that covers most lots is One-Family Residential (RS-1) under Zoning Bylaw No. 510, 2018. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2026/07/2.0-2026-07-27-Regular-Council-Meeting-Agenda-Package.pdf
- Named sub-areas in the Official Community Plan (OCP) are Belcarra Bay and Bedwell Bay; Farrer Cove, at the north end, is covered by its own zone, RM-2 (Farrer Cove South), in the zoning bylaw's district list. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2024/10/621-2024-Official-Communitiy-Plan-Bylaw-SCHEDULE-A.pdf
- Waterfront lots on Bedwell Bay and Belcarra Bay carry marine zoning (W-1, W-2, W-3) for the water side of the property, separate from the house zone. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2019/05/ZAC_Draft_Zoning_Bylaw_510_2018-01-02.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- An RS-1 lot allows one principal single-family house plus one accessory unit — a secondary suite or a coach house — or one duplex; anything denser needs a site-specific Comprehensive Development (CD) rezoning. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2026/07/2.0-2026-07-27-Regular-Council-Meeting-Agenda-Package.pdf
- There is no municipal sewer: sewage is handled on site by septic systems, and the Village's own regional context statement says it will only widen suite, coach house and low-density multi-unit permissions where septic and water provisions are available. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2026/07/2.0-2026-07-27-Regular-Council-Meeting-Agenda-Package.pdf
- Drinking water is municipal but imported: the Village has had a water services agreement with the District of North Vancouver since 2008, and that district has been the primary source of potable water since 2011, serving most properties in the Belcarra Bay and Bedwell Bay areas. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2024/10/621-2024-Official-Communitiy-Plan-Bylaw-SCHEDULE-A.pdf
- The current plan is the 2024 OCP, Bylaw No. 621, 2024; zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 510, 2018 — both are Belcarra's own bylaws, not Port Moody's. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2024/10/621-2024-Official-Communitiy-Plan-Bylaw-SCHEDULE-A.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Belcarra part of Port Moody for taxes and permits? **A:** No. The Village of Belcarra is a separate municipality with its own council, its own 2024 Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 621, 2024) and its own Zoning Bylaw No. 510, 2018, so building permits, tax rates and servicing rules are the Village's, even though the postal and real-estate area name is Port Moody. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2024/10/621-2024-Official-Communitiy-Plan-Bylaw-SCHEDULE-A.pdf
- **Q:** Can I add a suite or coach house on Bedwell Bay Road? **A:** Yes, one accessory unit. The RS-1 zone permits up to one principal single-family house and one accessory unit, either a secondary suite or a coach house, per lot — but the added unit still has to work on the lot's septic system, and the Village ties any wider permissions to septic and water capacity. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2026/07/2.0-2026-07-27-Regular-Council-Meeting-Agenda-Package.pdf
- **Q:** Where do my children go to school? **A:** There is no school building in the Village. The OCP community facilities map lists fire protection (Sasamat Volunteer Fire Department), the Waste and Recycling Depot, the Village Hall, a multi-sport court and the TransLink community shuttle — no school — so Belcarra addresses are served by School District No. 43 (SD43) schools outside the village, and the exact school must be confirmed by address with SD43. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2024/10/621-2024-Official-Communitiy-Plan-Bylaw-SCHEDULE-A.pdf
- **Q:** What happens to garbage and recycling? **A:** Belcarra runs a central drop-off, not curbside collection. The OCP facilities map names solid waste management as a central drop-off Waste and Recycling Depot (the WARD), and the Village budgets the depot as its own operating fund with its own rate increases, separate from water. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2025/10/Village-of-Belcarra-Draft-2026-2040-Financial-Plan.pdf
- **Q:** Does the regional park change daily life? **A:** Yes in summer. təmtəmíxʷtən/Belcarra Regional Park, run by Metro Vancouver, holds White Pine Beach on Sasamat Lake and the Belcarra picnic area on the inlet; Metro Vancouver posts washroom closures there and a 3.2-kilometre Sasamat Lake Loop Trail, and park traffic uses the same Bedwell Bay Road residents use. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/t%C9%99mt%C9%99m%C3%ADx%CA%B7t%C9%99n-belcarra-regional-park

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Fire protection is the Sasamat Volunteer Fire Department, shared with Anmore — the Village Hall and fire service are not Port Moody Fire Rescue. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2024/10/621-2024-Official-Communitiy-Plan-Bylaw-SCHEDULE-A.pdf
- Water bills carry a long tail: a water parcel tax pays the debt on the potable water system built under Bylaw No. 413, 2008, started with the first debt payment in 2013 and runs for the 25-year term of that debt unless prepaid. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2024/06/2023-Financial-Statements.pdf
- Dogs are not allowed at White Pine Beach, on the swimming decks of the Sasamat Lake floating walkway, or at most pocket beaches on Sasamat Lake, and must be leashed elsewhere in the regional park. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/t%C9%99mt%C9%99m%C3%ADx%CA%B7t%C9%99n-belcarra-regional-park
- The park's name changed on October 8, 2021 to təmtəmíxʷtən/Belcarra Regional Park under a 2020 co-operation agreement between Metro Vancouver and Tsleil-Waututh Nation — signage and older documents use both names. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/t%C9%99mt%C9%99m%C3%ADx%CA%B7t%C9%99n-belcarra-regional-park
- Street lighting in the village is limited to areas next to bus shelters, per the OCP community facilities map. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2024/10/621-2024-Official-Communitiy-Plan-Bylaw-SCHEDULE-A.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Belcarra sets its own tax rates: the 2025 rates were set by Village of Belcarra 2025 Tax Rates Bylaw No. 634, 2025, and the Province's compiled 2025 total combined residential rate for Belcarra was 3.38873 per $1,000 of assessed value; the 2026 rate is in NOT CONFIRMED below. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2025/05/2.0-2025-05-12-Regular-Council-Meeting-Agenda-Package.pdf https://www.civicinfo.bc.ca/data?surveyid=603
- Water and the Waste and Recycling Depot (WARD) are charged as separate Village funds: the draft 2026 financial plan recommended a 4 percent water rate increase and a 5 percent WARD increase for 2026. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2025/10/Village-of-Belcarra-Draft-2026-2040-Financial-Plan.pdf
- The Village of Belcarra is listed by the Province as a Speculation and Vacancy Tax taxable area, so every residential owner must declare. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- One bus serves the village: TransLink route 182, Moody Centre Station/Belcarra, a community shuttle that runs Bedwell Bay Road to Belcarra Bay Road at Whiskey Cove Lane. https://translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/182/direction/0/schedule
- Bedwell Bay Road is the only road in and out, continuing as Sunnyside Road through Anmore; the OCP shows transit as the TransLink Community Shuttle Bus only. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2024/10/621-2024-Official-Communitiy-Plan-Bylaw-SCHEDULE-A.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Septic condition is the first check: with no municipal sewer, a buyer should confirm the on-site system's age, capacity and any Village or health-authority records for the specific lot; the Village pages do not report an individual system's state. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2026/07/2.0-2026-07-27-Regular-Council-Meeting-Agenda-Package.pdf
- Wildfire interface is a named planning item: the OCP is informed by a Wildfire Protection Plan and the earlier OCP carried wildland fire hazard protection policies for the forested peninsula. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2019/10/Bylaw-435-2011-Official-Community-Plan-Consolidated.pdf
- Foreshore and moorage are separately regulated: the Bedwell Bay Sustainability Plan sits inside the OCP as Schedule D, with policies on use of foreshore and residential moorage, so a dock or buoy is not settled by the house zone alone. https://belcarra.ca/assets/media/2019/05/vob-bylaw-435_official-community-plan.pdf
