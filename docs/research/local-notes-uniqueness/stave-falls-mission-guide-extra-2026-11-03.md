# Stave Falls Area Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: stave-falls-mission-guide (Stave Falls; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Stave Falls is a rural community inside the City of Mission, around the Stave River below Stave Lake — unlike Dewdney, Deroche and Lake Errock to the east, City of Mission bylaws apply here. The City's Rural Mission Strategy names Stave Falls first: "From Stave Falls, Steelhead and Silverdale to our other rural neighbourhoods" (https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy).
- The community centres on Dewdney Trunk Road, Wilson Street and Brackley Avenue, with the Powerhouse at Stave Falls at 31338 Dewdney Trunk Road (https://www.bchydro.com/community/recreation_areas/visitor-centres/stave-falls-visitor-centre.html).
- Stave Falls Elementary, 30204 Brackley Avenue, is the in-community school building the City used for the Rural Mission Strategy open house on October 7, 2025 (https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy).

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is acreage-led rural housing on Dewdney Trunk Road, Wilson Street, Berg Avenue, Carr Street and Rolley Lake Street, plus manufactured homes in parks on Wilson Street; no strata complex was confirmed on an official source read for this note (https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy).
- The setting is a working hydro reservoir system, not a natural lakefront: BC Hydro describes water stored in Alouette Lake running into Stave Lake, through the Stave Falls powerhouse into Hayward Lake, then through Ruskin Dam to the Fraser River (https://www.BChydro.com/news/conservation/2023/visit-stave-falls.html?fixZoomMobile=true).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City of Mission planning governs Stave Falls, under the new Official Community Plan adopted in 2025 as Bylaw 6350-2025, with a replacement Bylaw 6405-2026 put to public hearing on April 7, 2026 to formally add the Regional Context Statement (https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/notice-public-hearing-april-7-2026).
- Detached secondary homes in Mission's rural zones go through a secondary-dwelling zone: City rezoning files rezone Rural Residential 7 (RR7) lots to RR7 Secondary Dwelling (RR7s) under Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 to allow a coach house or garden cottage (https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rsd26-2).
- Mission updated Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 on July 17, 2024 to incorporate provincial small-scale multi-unit housing in urban residential zones, under Bylaw 6299-2024-5949(163); rural Stave Falls lots are outside those urban zones (https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-zoning-bylaw-housekeeping-amendments).
- All secondary suites inside the City must be registered with the City, a suite must be a self-contained living space in a single-family home, and suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhouses (https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is Stave Falls part of Mission? **A:** Yes, Stave Falls is inside the City of Mission, so City zoning, City servicing decisions and City tax dates apply. That is the opposite of Dewdney, Deroche and Lake Errock east on Highway 7, which are unincorporated Fraser Valley Regional District ground with provincial rural taxes (https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy).
**Q:** What is the Powerhouse at Stave Falls? **A:** It is BC Hydro's original 1912 powerhouse at 31338 Dewdney Trunk Road, once British Columbia's biggest hydroelectric power source, now a Visitor Centre and National Historic Site of Canada. BC Hydro lists it as currently closed, reopening March 1, 2027, with adult admission of $8 (https://www.bchydro.com/community/recreation_areas/visitor-centres/stave-falls-visitor-centre.html).
**Q:** Which school is in Stave Falls? **A:** Stave Falls Elementary, kindergarten to grade 6, at 30204 Brackley Avenue, is listed by the Province for School District No. 75 (Mission). District policy assigns the Stave Falls Elementary catchment to Hatzic Middle School for grades 7 to 9 (https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075).
**Q:** Is Stave Lake waterfront a private dock right? **A:** No dock or moorage right is confirmed by any source read for this note. Stave Lake and Hayward Lake are BC Hydro reservoirs in a three-station system, so shoreline use, docks and moorage must be confirmed on title and with BC Hydro for the specific property (https://www.BChydro.com/news/conservation/2023/visit-stave-falls.html?fixZoomMobile=true).
**Q:** What recreation is at the door? **A:** Rolley Lake Provincial Park is the named park in the Stave Falls area, and the Stave West Forest and Recreation Area covers the Tree Farm Licence 26 forest west of Stave Lake, managed by the City with Kwantlen, Matsqui and Leq'á:mel First Nations and the Province (https://www.mission.ca/community-environment/municipal-forest/visiting-working-forest/stave-west-forest-recreation-area).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Rolley Lake Park's campground grew in 2026: the Province states 32 new drive-in sites opened in May 2026, bringing drive-in sites to 92, in a $4-million project with electrical and wastewater upgrades (https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026ENV0027-000617).
- BC Hydro lists the Stave Lake boat launch, Hayward Lake Reservoir Recreation Area and Ruskin Dam Recreation Area as the recreation sites beside the Powerhouse on Dewdney Trunk Road (https://www.bchydro.com/community/recreation_areas/visitor-centres/stave-falls-visitor-centre.html).
- Stave West has over 200 campsites across four campgrounds managed by Recreation Sites and Trails BC, including Kearsley Creek with equestrian corrals and Rock Creek for recreational-vehicle groups (https://www.mission.ca/community-environment/municipal-forest/visiting-working-forest/stave-west-forest-recreation-area).
- The City ran design work for a Stave Falls Community Park on Brackley Avenue, beside Stave Falls Elementary, through its engagement site; its built status is in NOT CONFIRMED (https://engage.mission.ca/projects/stave-falls-community-park-design-concepts).
- Hayward Lake gets busy in summer with swimmers and paddlers, per BC Hydro, which is the seasonal traffic pattern on Dewdney Trunk Road past Wilson Street (https://www.BChydro.com/news/conservation/2023/visit-stave-falls.html?fixZoomMobile=true).

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Stave Falls has no separate rural tax system, because it is inside the City of Mission; see the mission-farms note for the city-wide residential tax, utility and penalty figures that apply to a Stave Falls property.
- No sub-area-specific utility charge for Stave Falls was stated on a City page read for this note; servicing on acreage lots here is confirmed lot by lot in Section 9 (https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy).

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Dewdney Trunk Road is the through route, west to Maple Ridge and east into central Mission; no TransLink or BC Transit route summary for Stave Falls was stated on an operator page read for this note (https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy).
- The West Coast Express runs from Mission City Station in central Mission, five weekday westbound trains with no weekend service, per the TransLink timetable (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf).

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Confirm servicing lot by lot on Wilson Street and Dewdney Trunk Road: acreage properties here use wells and septic systems where City services do not reach, and no City page read states servicing for a specific Stave Falls lot (https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy).
- Treat every waterfront, dock and moorage claim as a title and BC Hydro check, because the shoreline is a working reservoir system running Alouette Lake to Stave Lake to Hayward Lake to Ruskin Dam (https://www.BChydro.com/news/conservation/2023/visit-stave-falls.html?fixZoomMobile=true).
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve status by parcel identifier number (PID) on the Agricultural Land Commission maps before assuming a second home is possible on a rural lot (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
