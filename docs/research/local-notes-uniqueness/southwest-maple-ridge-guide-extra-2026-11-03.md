# Southwest Maple Ridge Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: southwest-maple-ridge-guide (Southwest Maple Ridge, Maple Ridge; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Southwest Maple Ridge is a board sub-area name; the City's planning frame for this corner is Hammond, in the southwest corner of Maple Ridge — see the southwest-maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026-06-25-Hammond-Area-Plan_0.pdf
- Hammond is bounded west by Pitt Meadows and the q̓ic̓əy̓ (Katzie) First Nation, south by the Fraser River, and north by the Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road commercial areas. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026-06-25-Hammond-Area-Plan_0.pdf
- The Hammond plan divides the area into four precincts: North Hammond, Upper Hammond, Lower Hammond and the Maple Meadows Business Park. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026-06-25-Hammond-Area-Plan_0.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Hammond's stock includes early-1900s single-family houses, a historic commercial area, a 5.4-hectare baseball park and a 16-hectare golf course, per the area plan. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/10-ocp-chapter-105-hammond-area-plan202212201605219163

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The updated Hammond plan focuses growth near Maple Meadows Station and introduces Maple Meadows Residential and Maple Meadows Mixed-Use designations with increased density near transit. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-advances-housing-strategy-completion-area-plan-updates
- Near the station, the plan allows 8–12 storey mixed-use buildings, while protecting employment lands in the Maple Meadows Business Park precinct. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-ocp-and-area-plan-updates-increase-housing-and-align-legislation
- A Hammond Heritage Character Area and a Hammond Development Permit Area apply inside the plan area; Upper Hammond carries the heritage precinct policies. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026-06-25-Hammond-Area-Plan_0.pdf
- Ground-Oriented Residential lots here can support single-detached, duplex, triplex and fourplex forms, with up to six units within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-03/Attachment-A-Official-Community-Plan-Amending-Bylaw-No-8057-2025.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Did the Hammond recreation projects get approved? **A:** No — on October 17, 2026 voters rejected both Hammond borrowing bylaws: the Aquatics and Recreation Centre at 11509 207 Street / 20601 Westfield Avenue (6,841 yes, 14,218 no) and the Multi-Use Community Park at 20818 Golf Lane (6,209 yes, 14,797 no). The City states the projects will not proceed as proposed. https://www.mapleridge.ca/art-parks-rec/recreation-ready
- **Q:** Is Hammond in the floodplain? **A:** Partly — portions of Lower Hammond are in the Fraser River floodplain, and all of the Maple Meadows Business Park precinct is in the floodplain, per the area plan. The plan carries a Hammond Area Floodplain figure, and the City's 2026 floodplain bylaw sets construction levels for designated lots, so check the specific address. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-03/Attachment-A-Official-Community-Plan-Amending-Bylaw-No-8057-2025.pdf
- **Q:** Which train stations serve this side of Maple Ridge? **A:** Two — Maple Meadows Station at the west side and Port Haney Station at the east edge both sit on the West Coast Express line between Mission City and Waterfront. Trains run Monday to Friday, westbound in the morning and eastbound in the afternoon, with no weekend or statutory-holiday service. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt997.pdf
- **Q:** What changed in the Hammond plan in 2025? **A:** Council adopted the Hammond update on November 25, 2025 as part of the city-wide area-plan package. It directs growth around Maple Meadows Station, adds the Maple Meadows Residential and Mixed-Use designations, and increases density near transit as provincial legislation requires, while protecting employment lands. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-advances-housing-strategy-completion-area-plan-updates

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Hammond began as Port Hammond Junction: the Hammond brothers donated land to the Canadian Pacific Railway on condition the first Maple Ridge station was built here (detail in the southwest-maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note). https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026-06-25-Hammond-Area-Plan_0.pdf
- Part of the q̓ic̓əy̓ Slough sits at the western edge of the Business Park precinct and provides natural drainage for the area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-03/Attachment-A-Official-Community-Plan-Amending-Bylaw-No-8057-2025.pdf
- Hammond Elementary at 11520 203 Street doubles as a local voting place, and the plan names it inside the heritage-side precincts. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-urges-residents-plan-municipal-voting-due-overlapping-provincial-election
- The golf course lands at 20818 Golf Lane were the proposed Multi-Use Community Park site — that proposal ended with the October 2026 vote. https://www.mapleridge.ca/art-parks-rec/recreation-ready
- Golden Ears Way, at this area's west side, is the road TransLink is widening from two lanes to four between the Highway 7 interchange and 210 Street. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/city-projects/golden-ears-way-widening

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Maple Ridge's 2026 total residential mill rate is 4.53578 per $1,000 of assessed value, including the municipal general rate of 2.86430 (2026 Property Tax Rate Summary). https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- The 2026 flat water fee is $859.25; Maple Ridge is also a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so residential owners must declare. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Maple Meadows and Port Haney are consecutive West Coast Express stations; the full run is 69 kilometres and about 75 minutes end to end, per TransLink. https://www.translink.ca/news/2025/november/all%20aboard%20translink%20celebrates%2030%20years%20of%20west%20coast%20express
- Golden Ears Way is a 12.6-kilometre corridor linking Surrey to Maple Ridge via the Golden Ears Bridge; its northern two-lane segment is the widening project in Section 6. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/city-projects/golden-ears-way-widening
- The 791 bus links Haney Place to Braid Station via Maple Meadows Station, Hammond Road and Harris Road, per the TransLink timetable. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt791.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Floodplain is the first check in Lower Hammond and the Business Park: Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 8121-2026 designates floodplain, sets flood construction levels and setbacks, and regulates fill placement. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-climate-action-flood-resilience-environmental-protection-and-mckenney-creek
- No habitable area may be built with its floor system below the Flood Construction Level on a designated lot under that bylaw. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
- Heritage rules are the second check in Upper Hammond: confirm whether the lot sits in the Hammond Heritage Character Area figure in the plan before planning exterior changes. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026-06-25-Hammond-Area-Plan_0.pdf
