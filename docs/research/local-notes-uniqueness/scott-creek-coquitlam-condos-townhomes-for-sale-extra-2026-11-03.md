## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The creek rule is the rule that matters for attached lots here: land within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of a watercourse in the City Centre Area Plan needs a Watercourse Protection Development Permit before land is altered — relevant to Lasalle Place units backing onto the Hoy Creek green corridor. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there a condo tower in Scott Creek? **A:** No tower was found on the sources read. The attached stock named for this pocket is townhouse-form: the 42-unit project at 1251–1271 Lasalle Place from the City's 1988 Design Committee minutes, plus the 1135 Lansdowne Drive townhouses counted under Eagle Ridge. A buyer who wants a high-rise apartment is shopping City Centre or Burquitlam, not this pocket, and should check the coquitlam-condos-for-sale note for that stock. http://searcharchives.coquitlam.ca/uploads/r/city-of-coquitlam-archives/7/c/7/7c71a5957f8798da515b191032bd1603df68a9e685856005244e3413b40b1469/Design_Committee_-_1988.pdf
- **Q:** How old is the Lasalle Place townhouse complex? **A:** It was already built by 1988. City Design Committee minutes of July 12, 1988 review a roofing-material change for the 42-unit townhouse project at 1251–1271 Lasalle Place, which puts the complex in the late-1980s building cohort. For a unit there, the depreciation report and the strata's roof and envelope history matter more than the original build date, and neither is published on a page read for this note. http://searcharchives.coquitlam.ca/uploads/r/city-of-coquitlam-archives/7/c/7/7c71a5957f8798da515b191032bd1603df68a9e685856005244e3413b40b1469/Design_Committee_-_1988.pdf
- **Q:** Does the creek behind Lasalle Place limit what the strata can do? **A:** Yes, at the lot line. In the City Centre Area Plan, land within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of a watercourse is in the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area, and altering land there needs that permit, with conditions for riparian protection, water quality and slope stability. Townhouse units backing onto the Hoy Creek corridor are the ones to measure against that 30-metre line before assuming a deck or fence project is simple. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- **Q:** Which bus serves the townhouses, not just the houses? **A:** Route 185 is the Lansdowne Drive route. TransLink's timetable for route 185, Coquitlam Central Station to Lansdowne, runs the length of Lansdowne Drive through the pocket via Mariner Way and Barnet Highway to Panorama Drive, then continues as the 184 toward Moody Centre Station — that is the stop pattern for the Lansdowne-edge attached stock, while Lasalle Place buyers use the Pinetree Way side. http://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/192/tt185.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The City's trail guide places a community garden on Lasalle Place — the Scott Creek Community Garden sits between Scott Creek Middle and Eagle Ridge Elementary, beside the Lasalle Place townhouses. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1591/Explore-the-Great-Outdoors---Trail-Maps-and-Guide-PDF
- Scott Creek's own drainage is actively maintained at the townhouse edge: the City's instream-works list names Scott Creek at Runnel Drive (box culvert clean and inspect), Scott Creek at Lansdowne Drive (box culvert) and Scott Creek at Eagleridge Drive (box culvert) as work sites. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/14428/25-026-RFP---Field-Monitoring-of-Instream-Works?bidId=
- The three SD43 schools on the Lansdowne and Falcon corridor — Eagle Ridge Elementary at 1215 Falcon Drive, Scott Creek Middle at 1240 Lansdowne Drive and Gleneagle Secondary at 1195 Lansdowne Drive — are listed in full in the houses note; catchment for a Lasalle Place address is by permanent home address in the SD43 School Locator, not by proximity. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- Eagle Ridge Outdoor Pool, an eight-lane 25-metre outdoor pool at Eagle Ridge Park, is seasonal — its listed 2026 season ran May 30 to September 7 — so a townhouse buyer does not get year-round swimming at that facility. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/-91
- The Coquitlam Crunch Trail starts at Eagle Ridge Park on Runnel Drive and climbs 2.2 kilometres with 894 steps to Westwood Plateau, which is the trail access the Lasalle Place and Lansdowne townhouses share with the houses. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/coquitlamcrunchtrail-27

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area layer first for any Lasalle Place or Lansdowne unit near Hoy Creek or Scott Creek: within 30 metres of top of bank or ravine bank, the permit can add riparian, runoff and erosion conditions, and the guide does not set the final setback for a specific lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- Check slope and wildfire layers on the City's Schedule B Environmental Development Permit Areas map, which maps unstable slopes and the Interface Wildfire Risk Management boundary for the City Centre and Northeast areas; it shows layers, not a lot-level geotechnical conclusion. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- Check the strata's own documents for the 1988-era Lasalle Place complex — depreciation report, envelope and roof history — because no municipal page publishes the building's condition. http://searcharchives.coquitlam.ca/uploads/r/city-of-coquitlam-archives/7/c/7/7c71a5957f8798da515b191032bd1603df68a9e685856005244e3413b40b1469/Design_Committee_-_1988.pdf
