## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Burnaby's condo stock is organized by four town centres, because the City's growth framework concentrates apartment growth there: Metrotown is the City Centre, and Brentwood, Lougheed and Edmonds are the Town Centres. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/burnaby-2050-planning-our-city-together/burnaby-2050-policy-guide-share-your-feedback/growth-scenarios
- Each centre sits on a SkyTrain station of the same name: Metrotown and Edmonds on the Expo Line, Brentwood on the Millennium Line, and Lougheed Town Centre where the two lines meet. https://www.translink.ca/news/2026/october/translink%20replacing%2040-year-old%20elevator%20at%20patterson%20skytrain%20station
- Neighbourhood-level stock detail lives in the sibling notes: Capitol Hill and Central Park are named and pointed to, not repeated, below.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The framework sets the built form by tier: residential development in the City Centre of Metrotown is primarily high-rise, while the Town Centres of Brentwood, Lougheed and Edmonds are primarily apartments ranging from high-rise to mid-rise towers. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/burnaby-2050-planning-our-city-together/burnaby-2050-policy-guide-share-your-feedback/growth-scenarios
- Metrotown's Hazel Street corridor carries the densest named cluster in the program index — Sussex at 4711 Hazel Street, The Sovereign at 4508 Hazel Street and Centrepoint at 4808 Hazel Street among them — fronting the Metrotown Station and Central Boulevard area. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2024-05-23/streetfest-returns-burnaby-canada-day-headline-performer-Dear-Rouge

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City-wide, every strata of five or more lots must hold a depreciation report on a five-year cycle since July 1, 2024, with no deferral vote; that duty reaches every tower in all four town centres. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Rezoning bylaws for new towers are now amendments to the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, and since the 2023 provincial changes, public hearings are neither required nor prohibited for specified residential rezonings, though public notice still applies before first reading. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/meetings-and-public-hearings/public-hearings/zoning-bylaw-amendments

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Which town centre has the tallest condo stock?
**A:** Metrotown, because the City's growth framework gives the City Centre the highest density forms of development, with residential development primarily in high-rise buildings. Brentwood, Lougheed and Edmonds are Town Centres, where residential development is primarily apartments ranging from high-rise to mid-rise towers — a tier below Metrotown in the same framework. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/burnaby-2050-planning-our-city-together/burnaby-2050-policy-guide-share-your-feedback/growth-scenarios

**Q:** How much growth is Burnaby planning for, and where does it go?
**A:** The City forecasts over 100,000 new residents, over 55,000 new homes and over 50,000 new jobs by 2050, and states that for three decades it has focused new growth in the four Town Centres and the Urban Villages. A condo purchase in any centre is therefore inside the City's designated growth geography, not at its edge. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/burnaby-2050-planning-our-city-together/burnaby-2050-policy-guide-share-your-feedback/growth-scenarios

**Q:** Does a presale tower still need a public hearing?
**A:** Not necessarily. Burnaby's Zoning Bylaw Amendments page states that 2023 amendments to the Local Government Act no longer require or prohibit public hearings for specific residential rezoning applications, while public notice requirements — publishing and mailing before first reading — continue to apply. Check the specific Rezoning Reference (REZ) file for the hearing status of one project. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/meetings-and-public-hearings/public-hearings/zoning-bylaw-amendments

**Q:** What one document matters most in a 1990s tower purchase?
**A:** The depreciation report. Every Burnaby strata with five or more lots must obtain one on a five-year cycle, stratas can no longer defer it by annual three-quarter vote, and Metro Vancouver stratas whose last report predates December 31, 2020 had to obtain a new one by July 1, 2026 — so a missing or stale report is itself a finding. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Burnaby has no single downtown: the framework names Metrotown alone as City Centre, above the three Town Centres. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/burnaby-2050-planning-our-city-together/burnaby-2050-policy-guide-share-your-feedback/growth-scenarios
- The same framework lists Rapid Transit Urban Villages — Bainbridge, Broadview, Garden Village, the Heights, Holdom, Lake City, Lochdale and Royal Oak — as a separate tier below the Town Centres. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/burnaby-2050-planning-our-city-together/burnaby-2050-policy-guide-share-your-feedback/growth-scenarios
- School District No. 41 (SD41) serves the whole city; its elementary list runs from Chaffey-Burke at 4404 Sardis Street to Edmonds Community at 7651 18th Avenue. https://burnabyschools.ca/elementary-school-addresses/
- New strata buildings begin tax life under a developer's parent folio until the City's apportionment process creates child-unit tax notices. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/additional-information
- StreetFest on Central closes Central Boulevard and Beresford Street between Patterson and Willingdon Avenues, below the Metrotown towers, each Canada Day. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2024-05-23/streetfest-returns-burnaby-canada-day-headline-performer-Dear-Rouge

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due July 3, 2026, after which a 5% late penalty applies, with a further 5% after the September due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- The 2026 tax sale was set for September 28, 2026 at Burnaby City Hall, for parcels with taxes unpaid for the two prior years — the end point of the penalty ladder a strata owner ignores. http://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-sale

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with rental replacement: the Beresford Street application in the Central Park note includes 112 required rental replacement units, a condition type that signals an older rental building is being redeveloped — check whether a target building sits in a rezoning assembly. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/meetings-and-public-hearings/public-hearings/zoning-bylaw-amendments
