## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK

- Detached houses here sit mainly on urban residential lots, and the zoning floor for a new interior lot is 540 m² in the RS3 zone and 420 m² in the RS5 zone, as a City council report applying Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 states. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/
- Westerleigh is the west side's named master-planned community, and developer Polygon Homes describes it as an established community beside the Discovery Trail, the all-season corridor connecting the east and west ends of the city, with the Club West clubhouse at its centre. https://polyhomes.com/community/elmstone/
- Blue Jay Elementary at 30995 Southern Drive opened in September 1988, and the school's own history page records that the surrounding streets are named after birds — Robin, Sandpiper, Sparrow, Curlew, Osprey, Cardinal, Swallow, Heron and Mallard. https://bluejay.abbyschools.ca/school-name-history-5

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE

- A secondary suite must sit inside the principal dwelling and be registered with the City, and registration confirms the suite complies with the BC Building Code and the Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw, 2014. https://abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites
- Suite eligibility has street-level conditions, and City policy criteria applied in council reports require a lot of at least 400 m², at least 12.0 m of frontage, a Collector or Local road, and no location on a cul-de-sac bulb. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/
- A house with a suite needs extra parking, and the Zoning Bylaw requires a minimum of two parking spaces for a single family house plus one additional space if a secondary suite is constructed. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/
- City-wide zoning, small-scale multi-unit housing and suite size rules are treated in full in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note. (See the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note.)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

**Q:** Is the basement suite in a west-side house legal?
**A:** A legal suite is one registered with the City, not just one shown in a listing. The City's secondary suites page states that suites are required to be registered, that registration confirms compliance with the BC Building Code and the Zoning Bylaw, 2014, and that a suite is a portion of a single detached home with cooking facilities and a separate exterior entrance. Ask the seller for the registration before you value the rental income. https://abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites

**Q:** Which school will my child attend?
**A:** The catchment school is set by your home address, not by the nearest school building. Abbotsford School District No. 34 (SD34) states that students attend the neighbourhood catchment school determined by their primary home address, and it recommends using its School Locator tool and catchment maps together to confirm a boundary before relying on it. https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments

**Q:** Can I add a suite to a house on a cul-de-sac?
**A:** A suite on a cul-de-sac bulb does not meet the City's suite criteria as applied in council reports. The criteria require a minimum lot size of 400 m², a minimum frontage of 12.0 m, and a Collector or Local road, and they state the suite must not be on a cul-de-sac bulb. Check the lot against all four points before assuming suite income. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/

**Q:** Is floodproofing a factor for houses near Fishtrap Creek?
**A:** Yes — land in the Fishtrap Creek floodplain carries its own floodproofing area in the Zoning Bylaw. Section 140.7 designates Area "H" as the lands within the 100-year floodplain of Fishtrap Creek, as delineated in the Master Drainage Plan for Fishtrap Creek (Map D-340), with the floor of a dwelling required to sit 0.6 m above the 100-year surface water level shown on that map. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/42052/

**Q:** How do I get to Vancouver without a car?
**A:** The Highstreet Transit Exchange is the west side's transit hub, and the City states the site currently accommodates the #1, #2 and #66 Fraser Valley Express bus routes. The 66 is the express service toward Lougheed Station, and route 21 also stops at Highstreet Exchange on its run between Aldergrove and Bourquin Exchange via Fraser Highway. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/projects-go/highstreet-transit-exchange https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=21

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- Non-catchment school requests are restricted at W.J. Mouat Secondary and all its feeder schools, and SD34 states it has restricted those requests to reduce enrolment over time — a west-side address outside the Mouat catchment is not a reliable route into that school. https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments
- Owners are billed annually for solid waste collection and secondary suites on the property tax notice, and the City states registered owners are billed for property taxes, solid waste collection, secondary suites and other charges in one annual billing. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- Westerleigh houses sit beside the Discovery Trail, which Polygon Homes describes as an all-season corridor connecting the east and west ends of the city. https://polyhomes.com/community/elmstone/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE

- The full city tax treatment is carried in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note; the west-side angle is the bill itself. (See the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note.)
- Abbotsford's 2026 residential (Class 1) municipal rates are 1.08284 per $1,000 for police and 1.78296 per $1,000 for general and debt servicing under Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026, before regional and library levies. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE

- Check any lot near Fishtrap Creek against floodproofing Area "H" before building or adding a suite, because Zoning Bylaw section 140.7 sets minimum floor elevations for lands in the creek's 100-year floodplain from the Master Drainage Plan Map D-340. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/42052/
- Check the lot's zone and floodplain status for the specific parcel rather than the street, because the bylaw's floodproofing areas are mapped on Schedule "E" and apply by mapped area, not by neighbourhood name. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/42052/
- Confirm a suite is registered, not merely finished, because the City requires registration and a registered suite is the one confirmed against the BC Building Code and the Zoning Bylaw. https://abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites
