# Vancouver Heights, Burnaby — Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer, for a voice memo. Do not answer these from research.

1. In the deals you have done or toured in Vancouver Heights, how much of the price is the Burrard Inlet and downtown view, and how much is the house and lot under it?
2. Who is actually buying in Vancouver Heights — families chasing the Rosser and Confederation Park catchments, buyers leaving Vancouver for Burnaby taxes, or builders pricing the R1 multiplex potential?
3. What sells fast and what sits in Vancouver Heights — do the older character houses on Pandora and Albert move quicker than the newer custom rebuilds higher on the slope?
4. What surprises buyers most here — that "Vancouver Heights" is Burnaby and not Vancouver, the steep-lot engineering costs, or something else you keep having to explain?
5. Tell one story from a Vancouver Heights deal — a showing, an offer night, or a street — that sums up what this neighbourhood is really like to buy in.
