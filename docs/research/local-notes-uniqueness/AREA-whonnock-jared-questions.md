# Whonnock — Questions for Jared

## Questions
1. What kinds of Whonnock properties do you actually get asked to sell or show — lakefront on Whonnock Lake, acreages along Dewdney Trunk Road, or smaller rural lots — and how often does Whonnock come up in a year?
2. Who is buying in Whonnock: acreage buyers wanting privacy, hobby farmers, or commuters trading size for the drive into Maple Ridge and Mission?
3. What sells fast out here and what sits — does Whonnock Lake frontage move quickly, and do buyers hesitate over wells, septic systems or the distance to services?
4. What surprises buyers most when they first tour Whonnock — the rural road conditions, the lack of municipal sewer, school bus distances, or how quiet it is compared with west Maple Ridge?
5. Can you tell me about one Whonnock deal that sticks in your mind — a tough inspection, an unusual property, or a buyer who fell for the lake or the valley setting?
