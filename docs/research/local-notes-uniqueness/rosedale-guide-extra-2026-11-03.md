# Rosedale Area Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: rosedale-guide (Rosedale, Chilliwack; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Rosedale is a rural village community at the City of Chilliwack's east end, centred on Yale Road; its addresses read as their own place — the school district directory lists its school at 50850 Yale Road, Rosedale, BC V0X 1X2. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2026-09/Chilliwack%20School%20District%20Directory%202026-27.pdf
- Yale Road is the spine: Rosedale Traditional Community School sits at 50850 Yale Road, and Highway 9 joins Yale Road at a roundabout just north of the village on the way to the Agassiz-Rosedale Bridge. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2026-09/Chilliwack%20School%20District%20Directory%202026-27.pdf https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation-projects/other-transportation-projects/agassiz-rosedale-bridge
- Rosedale uses rural addressing: the school district's directory lists the community's postal code as V0X 1X2, the rural format, not a Chilliwack V2P/V2R urban code. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2026-09/Chilliwack%20School%20District%20Directory%202026-27.pdf
- The Fraser River and its dike system form Rosedale's northern edge, with Cheam First Nation lands beside the Highway 9 corridor; the FVRD holds a transit service agreement with Cheam First Nation for Route 71, which runs through Rosedale. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/about-the-fvrd/projects-initiatives/transit-planning.html

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Rosedale's stock is a village core on Yale Road ringed by Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) farmland: older village houses along Yale Road near McGrath Road, with farm parcels on the rural roads beyond it, including McGrath Road, where the new east side elementary school is being built at 10125 McGrath Road. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387 https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/administration/capital/major-capital-projects/new-east-side-elementary-school
- Rosedale's housing stays low-density by policy: the City's neighbourhoods page states its growth management strategy limits growth in rural settlements by retaining the existing rural settlement boundaries, rather than extending serviced subdivisions into them. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1618
- Growth is directed by the 2050 Official Community Plan's agricultural framework (Schedule B1) around the village, which protects the ALR land base rather than extending the village into it. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Most land around the village is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, so the Agricultural Land Commission's housing rules govern farm parcels: one principal residence of 500 square metres or less in total floor area on most parcels, with a secondary suite permitted only inside the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 applies to Rosedale's Fraser River frontage: new building floor elevation is set at the flood construction level plus 0.6 metres of freeboard, checked lot by lot on the City's floodplain maps. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- The 2050 OCP's Farm Home Plate Development Permit Area (DPA 4) applies to housing on ALR valley-floor properties of 2 hectares or more, which covers much of Rosedale's farm ring. http://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2182
- City-wide zoning and small-scale multi-unit housing rules are written in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note; see that note.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What happens to Rosedale's school in 2027? **A:** Rosedale Traditional Community School changes twice over. In May 2026 the Chilliwack School Board approved reconfiguring it from a Kindergarten-to-Grade-8 school to a Grades 6–8 middle school effective 2027–28, moving the district's north-side French Immersion program into it, and sending Rosedale catchment Kindergarten-to-Grade-5 students to the new east side elementary school opening September 2027. https://sd33.bc.ca/news/board-approves-changes-some-schools
- **Q:** Is Rosedale in the Fraser River floodplain? **A:** The village sits on the river's south bank behind the City's dike system. The City states a large portion of Chilliwack lies within the Fraser River floodplain and that it maintains the dikes as the local diking authority; Rosedale lots also face the Floodplain Regulation Bylaw's flood construction level plus 0.6 metres of freeboard for new building. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2825
- **Q:** How do I get across the river from Rosedale? **A:** Highway 9 crosses the Fraser River on the Agassiz-Rosedale Bridge, a 1,870-metre two-lane bridge opened in 1956 that carries nearly 15,000 vehicles a day between Highway 1 and Highway 7. A provincial rehabilitation project began in September 2026 and is expected to run about three years, with single-lane alternating traffic through work zones. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation-projects/other-transportation-projects/agassiz-rosedale-bridge
- **Q:** Is there a bus in Rosedale? **A:** Yes, but it is a regional route, not a local one: BC Transit Route 71 Agassiz–Harrison runs from Downtown Chilliwack through Rosedale on Yale Road and Highway 9 to Agassiz and Harrison Hot Springs, administered by the FVRD with the City of Chilliwack as a cost-sharing partner. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Rosedale Traditional Community School is also the community hall: its Community School Society (RTCSS) runs adult drop-in sports, yoga, youth programs and birthday parties at 50850 Yale Road, and the district rents the facility for community events. https://sd33.bc.ca/node/596
- Rosedale addresses read as their own place, not "Chilliwack": the district directory addresses the school to "Rosedale, BC V0X 1X2", and Canada Post rural addressing applies to the village. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2026-09/Chilliwack%20School%20District%20Directory%202026-27.pdf
- The bridge work is the area's multi-year disruption: the Province's Agassiz-Rosedale Bridge page warns of nightly lane closures and single-lane alternating traffic through 150- to 300-metre work zones from September 2026 to 2029. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation-projects/other-transportation-projects/agassiz-rosedale-bridge
- Highway 1 access is at Exit 135, where Highway 9 leaves the freeway at the village's north edge; the Province's numbered-routes list describes Highway 9 as running from the junction with Route 1 at Exit 135 north to Highway 7 in Agassiz and on to Harrison Hot Springs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/transportation-reports-and-reference/reference-information/numbered-routes

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- New East Side Elementary School, 10125 McGrath Road, Rosedale: a three-storey, 500-seat Kindergarten-to-Grade-5 school with a neighbourhood learning centre for child care, built by the Province with School District No. 33. STATUS: under construction (started May 2025); provincial occupancy milestone April 2027, district opening September 2027, read 2026-11-03. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/administration/capital/major-capital-projects/new-east-side-elementary-school
- Agassiz-Rosedale Bridge rehabilitation (Highway 9 at Rosedale): a $64-million provincial project to address structural steel and concrete deterioration, improve drainage and extend the bridge's service life by an estimated 50 years; the $33-million major works contract went to Graham Infrastructure LP. STATUS: construction began September 21, 2026, completion expected 2029, read 2026-11-03. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation-projects/other-transportation-projects/agassiz-rosedale-bridge
- Rosedale Traditional Community School reconfiguration: approved by the SD33 Board on May 12, 2026 — the school becomes a Grades 6–8 middle school and takes the north-side French Immersion program in 2027–28. STATUS: approved, effective September 2027, read 2026-11-03. https://sd33.bc.ca/news/board-approves-changes-some-schools
- City-wide and multi-area projects are covered in the chilliwack-homes-for-sale note.

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 71 Agassiz–Harrison is Rosedale's only bus: Downtown Chilliwack to Harrison Hot Springs via Yale Road and Highway 9, with fares on a zonal system and free transfer to the Chilliwack Transit System for Agassiz–Harrison passengers. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html
- Highway 9 is the cross-river link: from its junction with Highway 1 at Exit 135 it runs north over the Agassiz-Rosedale Bridge to Agassiz, Highway 7 and Harrison Hot Springs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/transportation-reports-and-reference/reference-information/numbered-routes
- Yale Road is the local link west into Chilliwack Proper and Downtown Chilliwack, and east toward Popkum and Bridal Falls. https://sd33.bc.ca/schools

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Fraser River freshet is Rosedale's defining risk: the City's Fraser River Flood Response Plan keys its response stages to the Mission gauge, with dike patrols starting at bank-full conditions (5.5 metres) and 24-hour patrols at 7.0 metres; unprotected areas outside the dike system flood first. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=346
- Check whether a Rosedale lot is inside or outside the dike system before anything else: the City's flood protection page distinguishes protected floodplain from unprotected areas, and the OCP discourages development in unprotected floodplain areas. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2825
- Farm purchases need an ALR status check on the exact parcel plus a Farm Home Plate (DPA 4) review for any new house siting on parcels of 2 hectares or more. http://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2182
