# Oaklands — Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer, for a voice memo. Muse does not answer these.

1. Who buys the low-rise condos and townhomes in Oaklands — Royal Oak station commuters, Deer Lake families, or investors?
2. What sells fast and what sits in this small pocket — and does the stock turn over rarely enough that every listing draws a crowd?
3. What surprises buyers most: how close Deer Lake Park is on foot, strata fees in the older low-rises, or noise from Royal Oak and the SkyTrain guideway?
4. When a buyer picks Oaklands over Metrotown or Edmonds, what is the real reason?
5. Tell one story from an Oaklands deal — a long-waited listing, a rental-restriction surprise in the bylaws, or a buyer who came for Deer Lake.
