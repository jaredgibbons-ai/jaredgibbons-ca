# Harbour Place Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: harbour-place-coquitlam (Harbour Place; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Harbour Place is a slope neighbourhood around Baker Drive and King Albert Avenue, north of Austin Avenue and Como Lake Avenue and below Como Lake, and the City publishes no boundary for the sub-area name. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Blue-Mountain-Park-18
- Blue Mountain Park, at 975 King Albert Avenue at the foot of Blue Mountain, is the addressed park inside the pocket, and its spray park shares the same 975 King Albert Avenue address. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Blue-Mountain-Spray-Park-93
- Como Lake Park, at 700 Gatensbury Street just north of the houses, is the neighbourhood's lake edge, reached from Baker Drive via Como Lake Avenue. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Como-Lake-Park-26

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What is in Blue Mountain Park, at 975 King Albert Avenue? **A:** Blue Mountain Park is this pocket's main park, at the foot of Blue Mountain on King Albert Avenue. The City lists a separate Blue Mountain Spray Park at the same 975 King Albert Avenue address, and City picnic listings for the park give a 500-person maximum capacity for its picnic area, with washrooms, water and electrical available with a rental. https://www.coquitlam.ca/879/Picnic-Outdoor-Spaces
- **Q:** What can you do at Como Lake, just north of the houses? **A:** Como Lake Park, at 700 Gatensbury Street, offers the Lakeside Loop trail around the lake, a fishing dock and wetlands, and the City states the lake is stocked regularly in spring. It is a park lake north of the Baker Drive houses, not frontage any Harbour Place lot owns — the harbour name does not mean waterfront, as the harbour-place-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note sets out. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Como-Lake-Park-26
- **Q:** Which elementary school is inside the pocket? **A:** Baker Drive Elementary, kindergarten to Grade 5, at 885 Baker Drive, is the school physically inside Harbour Place — in full in the harbour-place-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. Middle and secondary assignment for a specific Baker Drive or King Albert Avenue address is not stated on any official page read, so run the address in the School District No. 43 School Locator before relying on a school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** Where do residents here swim indoors and borrow books? **A:** Poirier Sport and Leisure Complex, at 633 Poirier Street southwest of the pocket, is the nearest major City recreation complex in the City's facility listings. The nearest library branch is Coquitlam Public Library's Poirier Branch at 575 Poirier Street, not the City Centre Branch at 1169 Pinetree Way. http://www.coqlibrary.ca/about/hours-locations
- **Q:** Will Austin Avenue redevelopment change this neighbourhood? **A:** The adopted Southwest Shoulders and Corridors changes touch the pocket's south edge on Austin Avenue, including a neighbourhood commercial node near Austin Avenue and Blue Mountain Street, and are in full in the harbour-place-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. Areas east of Blue Mountain Street were kept out of the land-use changes, so a Baker Drive lot's designation must still be read lot by lot. http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1968

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Blue Mountain Park's picnic area is bookable and large: City picnic listings set a 500-person maximum, with a fee for groups of 1 to 100 people and a damage deposit, for the 975 King Albert Avenue site. https://www.coquitlam.ca/879/Picnic-Outdoor-Spaces
- Blue Mountain Park hosts a City little library under the Adopt-A-Little Library program, one of the listed park locations alongside Galloway Park and Smiling Creek Park on Burke Mountain. https://www.coquitlam.ca/625
- The Rose Garden near this side of Coquitlam, at 624 Poirier Street, features over 800 roses with brick pathways and raised planters, per the City's picnic and outdoor spaces page — a Poirier Street amenity, not a Harbour Place park. https://www.coquitlam.ca/879/Picnic-Outdoor-Spaces
- Como Lake's fish are a managed park feature: the City states the lake in Como Lake Park is stocked regularly in spring, with a fishing dock on the Lakeside Loop. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Como-Lake-Park-26
- École Montgomery Middle, at 1900 Edgewood Avenue near this pocket, is a seismic replacement project with its building permit approved and the project issued for tender in September 2026 — in the harbour-place-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/ecole-montgomery-middle

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Coquitlam's 2026 Class 1 Residential municipal rate is $2.2756 per $1,000 of assessed value, in full with utility charges in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17264/2026_Tax-Rates
- Coquitlam is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so a Harbour Place owner must complete the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- The local cost check is park-adjacent, not a fee: Blue Mountain Park picnic rentals at 975 King Albert Avenue carry City rental fees and a damage deposit, which matters to a buyer planning large gatherings there, not to the house tax bill. https://www.coquitlam.ca/879/Picnic-Outdoor-Spaces

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 153, Coquitlam Central Station to Braid Station, is the pocket's bus: it runs Poirier Street, Austin Avenue and Como Lake Avenue, with a stop at Como Lake Avenue and Baker Drive — in full in the harbour-place-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/153/direction/0/schedule
- Routes 156 and 157 also run Como Lake Avenue on the pocket's north side, per their TransLink timetables; no route is published on Baker Drive or King Albert Avenue themselves. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/192/tt156.pdf
- No SkyTrain station is in Harbour Place; buses on Como Lake Avenue and Austin Avenue connect to the Millennium Line Evergreen Extension stations in neighbouring areas. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2017/12/evergreen-extension-anniversary-a-year-ago-today-it-opened/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check flood information at city scale: the City states Coquitlam River flood levels use the 200-year design flood and that dikes and pump stations protect low-lying properties, and Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 section 519 sets 30-metre and 15-metre watercourse setbacks — both in full in the harbour-place-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. https://www.coquitlam.ca/224/Floodplain-Dikes
- Check Como Creek drainage for lots between Baker Drive and Como Lake: Como Lake is the headwater of the Como Creek watershed, so a lot draining toward the lake needs a City map check, not an assumption from its slope position. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Como-Lake-Park-26
- Check slope lot by lot on King Albert Avenue at the foot of Blue Mountain: no City geotechnical page specific to Harbour Place was found, so no slope conclusion is stated here. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Blue-Mountain-Park-18
