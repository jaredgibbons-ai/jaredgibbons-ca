# Silver Valley Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: silver-valley-guide (Silver Valley, Maple Ridge; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Silver Valley sits north of the Alouette River on Maple Ridge's north side, and the City's Silver Valley Area Plan is Chapter 10.3 of the Official Community Plan — see the silver-valley-maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/silver?bidId=
- Its forested hillsides are gateways to Golden Ears Provincial Park, and its creeks are among the richest salmon habitat in the Lower Mainland, per the City plan page. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/silver?bidId=
- Access today runs south across the Alouette River toward Abernethy Way and Lougheed Highway; a second arterial access is only a planned capital item (Section 6). https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-04/REVISED-Capital-Plan-for-Council-Review-April-21-2026.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Silver Valley is the newer master-planned side of Maple Ridge: the plan guiding it dates to 2002 and was modernized in 2025 because the original designations no longer matched what was being built. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/silver?bidId=
- The 2025 update adds new residential and mixed-use forms intended to support walkable neighbourhoods, rather than only detached lots. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-ocp-and-area-plan-updates-increase-housing-and-align-legislation

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Secondary suites are not permitted on lots zoned RST-SV (a Silver Valley zone) under the City's suite guide list of excluded zones, which also lists R-2, R-3, RST, RT and RM. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2023-11-import/Sec%20Suite%20Guide%20New_202103081701305643.pdf
- The 2025 Silver Valley update consolidates land-use designations, adds a zoning matrix, and incorporates the added density required by provincial legislation — check the matrix for the lot's designation before assuming a form. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/silver?bidId=
- Creek and conservation lands are a plan feature here, not an edge condition: the plan's stated aim is that growth preserve and protect the valley's natural elements. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/silver?bidId=

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there a Silver Valley elementary school? **A:** No — School District 42 records Silver Valley as entirely within the Yennadon Elementary catchment, and Yennadon is at 23347 128 Avenue, south of the valley. The district's older Silver Valley report proposed future school sites in the hamlets, but no school has been built inside Silver Valley on the sources read this pass. https://www.sd42.ca/assets/media/SD42-Blaney-Hamlet-Property-Acquisition.pdf https://elementary.sd42.ca/yennadon/first-week-of-school/
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves Silver Valley? **A:** Garibaldi Secondary, at 24789 Dewdney Trunk Road, is the north-side secondary school for this zone, not Samuel Robertson Technical, which serves the Albion side of east Maple Ridge. Garibaldi is an International Baccalaureate World School offering Middle Years and Diploma programmes for Grades 8 to 12. https://www.sd42.ca/secondary-school/garibaldi-secondary/
- **Q:** Will my child get a school bus to Garibaldi? **A:** Not on the regular service if you live in Silver Valley: School District 42's transportation list covers Garibaldi Secondary catchments excluding students residing in the Silver Valley / Rockridge area. Families here should plan on driving or using TransLink buses, and confirm eligibility with the district for the specific address and school year. https://www.sd42.ca/news/announcements/page/2/
- **Q:** Is there a second way out of the valley? **A:** Not yet — the City's 2026–2030 capital plan lists a 240 Street bridge north of 124 Avenue, extending 240 Street across the Alouette River to Fern Crescent and 128 Avenue, expressly as a second arterial access for Silver Valley and Golden Ears Park. It is at pre-planning and design stage, not built. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-04/REVISED-Capital-Plan-for-Council-Review-April-21-2026.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- There is no school inside Silver Valley itself; elementary children travel to Yennadon, south of the Alouette River. https://www.sd42.ca/assets/media/SD42-Blaney-Hamlet-Property-Acquisition.pdf
- The original plan proposed four schools — one elementary in each of the Blaney, Forest and Horse hamlets and a secondary in Rivers Village — none confirmed built this pass. https://www.sd42.ca/assets/media/SD42-Blaney-Hamlet-Property-Acquisition.pdf
- A new one-acre neighbourhood park is under construction at 13650 232A Street, between 232A Street and Blaney Road, beside a conservation area and the trail system. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/SVNewPark
- That park site slopes downhill from Blaney Road to 232A Street and keeps a stand of large spruce trees in its north-east corner. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/SVNewPark
- New trails, pedestrian bridges and trail improvements for Silver Valley are planned under the area plan, per the City's engagement listing. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/projects
- The Albion industrial lands and Kanaka Creek edges sometimes assumed for this area were not confirmed as Silver Valley features on sources read this pass — see NOT CONFIRMED.

- Guide-specific item: the Silver Valley Area Plan update was adopted November 25, 2025, modernizing the 2002 plan with simplified designations and a zoning matrix. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/silver?bidId=

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Maple Ridge's 2026 total residential mill rate is 4.53578 per $1,000 of assessed value, including the municipal general rate of 2.86430 (2026 Property Tax Rate Summary). https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- The 2026 flat water fee is $859.25, billed on the tax notice for flat-rate customers; metered customers are billed quarterly. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- Maple Ridge is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so residential owners must declare. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Silver Valley has no rail station: the West Coast Express stops at Port Haney and Maple Meadows, both south of the Alouette River, Monday to Friday only. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt997.pdf
- Abernethy Way is being widened and extended east in phases (210 Street to 240 Street, future connection to 256 Street), and the City calls it a critical secondary route for Silver Valley. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/city-projects/abernethy-way-corridor-improvements
Read: 2026-11-03

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check creek-side lots against Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 8121-2026, approved July 2026: it covers steep-creek watercourses and groundwater flooding as well as the Fraser and Alouette rivers, and sets flood construction levels and setbacks. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-climate-action-flood-resilience-environmental-protection-and-mckenney-creek
- Land less than 1.5 metres above the natural boundary of an adjacent watercourse is designated floodplain under that bylaw — relevant on Kanaka Creek and Alouette tributary lots. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
