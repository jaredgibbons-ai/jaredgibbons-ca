# Altamont Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: altamont (West Vancouver; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Altamont is a residential hillside in the District of West Vancouver, above Marine Drive between Dundarave to the east and Caulfeild to the west, below the British Properties. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- The District publishes no street-by-street boundary and no Official Community Plan (OCP) neighbourhood name for Altamont; the District's named centres are Ambleside, Park Royal, Dundarave, Caulfeild Village and Horseshoe Bay. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- The streets buyers use to place it are the avenues climbing from Marine Drive toward Queens Avenue, with Gisby Street holding the neighbourhood park named in Section 5. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/PARKS_MASTER_PLAN_FINAL.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Altamont's homes are detached houses on hillside lots in the RS single-family dwelling zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- No condominium building or townhome complex inside Altamont was confirmed on a District, developer or strata source; the confirmed stock is freehold detached houses. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- Secondary suites and a detached secondary suite (coach house) are among the permitted uses the bylaw lists for the single-family zones. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- District-wide West Vancouver tax, utility and plan treatment is in the west-vancouver-homes-for-sale note; see the west-vancouver-homes-for-sale note.
- Altamont's lot-by-lot question is which RS zone applies: minimum site areas run from 488 square metres (m²) in RS5 to 8,094 m² in RS1, with RS2 at 1,625 m², RS3 at 975 m² and RS4 at 731 m²; the District's Zoning Map confirms the zone for a specific lot. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- The bylaw's ground-oriented dwelling rules allow 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop — check current: the Province directed the District on July 21, 2025 to increase density in areas adjacent to Dundarave by December 31, 2025, and Altamont sits directly above Dundarave. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Altamont an official West Vancouver neighbourhood? **A:** Not in the District's plan. The name is used for this hillside in property searches, but it does not appear as a named neighbourhood in the District's Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, whose centres are Ambleside, Park Royal, Dundarave, Caulfeild Village and Horseshoe Bay. Plan and permit questions for an Altamont lot are answered at District level, not by a local area plan. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- **Q:** Which school will an Altamont address feed into? **A:** Only the locator can say. School District No. 45 (SD45), West Vancouver Schools, sets catchments by address and directs families to the school locator on its admissions page. The nearest elementary schools by address are West Bay Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 7, at 3175 Thompson Place to the west, and Irwin Park Elementary in Dundarave to the east; neither is confirmed as the catchment for a specific Altamont address. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/ https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/045
- **Q:** Can I add a coach house or extra dwellings on an Altamont lot? **A:** Possibly, within two layers of rules. Section 200 of the zoning bylaw lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses in the single-family zones, and the ground-oriented rules allow up to 4 dwellings per lot, or 6 within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop. The lot's RS zone and the post-directive amendments decide the real number — check current. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **Q:** Do creek or wildfire permits affect a rebuild here? **A:** They can, and both triggers are measured in metres. An Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit is required for work within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface. On a sloped, treed Altamont lot, either trigger can add a qualified professional and permit time to a rebuild. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Altamont Park is a District neighbourhood park: the District's Parks Master Plan lists Altamont Park among its neighbourhood parks and in its park-protection schedule. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/PARKS_MASTER_PLAN_FINAL.pdf
- SD45 has no middle-school tier: elementary is Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary is Grade 8 to Grade 12. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/
- The closest District waterfront park is Dundarave Park, 150 25th Street, with more than 50 metres of sandy beach at the east end of the Centennial Seawalk, which runs about 1.7 kilometres east to Ambleside. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/dundarave-park
- The District's main indoor recreation facility serving Altamont is the West Vancouver Community Centre and Aquatic Centre, 2121 Marine Drive, and its library is West Vancouver Memorial Library, 1950 Marine Drive, both in Ambleside. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/facilities/community-centre https://westvancouver.ca/arts-culture/west-vancouver-memorial-library
- Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, is the Vancouver Coastal Health hospital serving West Vancouver. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 251 Queens / Park Royal, operated as West Vancouver Blue Bus, serves the Queens Avenue corridor below the hillside, running from Marine Drive at 25th Street to Park Royal via 27th Street, Queens Avenue, 15th Street, Mathers Avenue, Burley Drive, Kings Avenue, 11th Street, Fulton Avenue and 17th Street. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt251.pdf
- Route 250 runs on Marine Drive below the hillside between Horseshoe Bay, Dundarave, Park Royal and downtown Vancouver via Georgia Street, the Stanley Park Causeway and the Lions Gate Bridge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
- Taylor Way, east of Altamont, connects Marine Drive to Highway 1 (Upper Levels Highway); no rapid transit serves the District, and drive times are UNCONFIRMED.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Wildfire interface: the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, approved December 14, 2020, covers properties within 100 metres of a forested area — about 50 percent of District land — and a permit is needed before constructing most new homes and coach houses in it. https://westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/natural-environment-and-hazard-development-permit-areas/wildfire-hazard-development-permit
- Creeks: in Existing Neighbourhoods, development within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse requires an Environmental Development Permit, and development is defined to include vegetation removal, soil disturbance and hazard-tree work. https://westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/natural-environment-and-hazard-development-permit-areas/environmental-development-permits
- Foreshore: tree work within 15 metres of the natural boundary (high tide mark) of the ocean needs a foreshore tree permit, relevant to Altamont's lowest Marine Drive lots. https://www.westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/trees/tree-cutting-permits/foreshore-protection-area
- Confirm the lot's RS zone and permit areas on the District's Zoning Map and WESTMap before relying on suite, coach-house or subdivision potential; the map, not the street's largest homes, states the entitlement.
