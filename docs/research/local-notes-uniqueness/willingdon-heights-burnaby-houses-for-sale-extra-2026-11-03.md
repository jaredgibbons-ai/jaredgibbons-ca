## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Willingdon Heights is the house pocket between Boundary Road on the west and Willingdon Avenue on the east, with Hastings Street on the north and Lougheed Highway on the south; the strata stock on Hastings Street is covered in the willingdon-heights-burnaby-condos-townhomes-for-sale note. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Brentwood Town Centre, one of Burnaby's four City-designated town centres, sits immediately southeast across Lougheed Highway, so the pocket's south edge is a town-centre edge rather than a town-centre address. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Gilmore Avenue is the interior school street: Kitchener Elementary stands at 1351 South Gilmore Avenue and Gilmore Community School at 50 South Gilmore Avenue, both inside the Boundary Road to Willingdon Avenue frame. https://burnabyschools.ca/elementary-school-addresses/

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The interior streets are detached houses, while the Hastings Street north edge carries the apartment buildings and the pocket's strata stock, which the condos-townhomes note covers in full. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Detached lots here fall under the City's R1 small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) district, so a house lot can be redeveloped as up to 3 to 6 dwelling units depending on lot area and proximity to a frequent transit network bus stop. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I build a multiplex on a Willingdon Heights house lot? **A:** Possibly, under the R1 SSMUH district: newly rezoned R1 lots are eligible for up to 3 to 6 dwelling units depending on lot area and proximity to a frequent transit network bus stop, and the unit count is inclusive of any secondary suites, so tenure does not change the permitted number. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q:** Is this pocket inside Brentwood Town Centre? **A:** No. Brentwood is a City-designated town centre immediately southeast across Lougheed Highway, while Willingdon Heights itself is the house and low-rise pocket north of the highway; the town-centre towers begin south of Lougheed Highway. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves the Gilmore Avenue streets? **A:** Kitchener Elementary, at 1351 South Gilmore Avenue, is the School District No. 41 (SD41) elementary school on that street, with Gilmore Community School at 50 South Gilmore Avenue nearby; SD41 assigns catchments by address, so confirm the assigned school for a specific address before relying on it. https://burnabyschools.ca/elementary-school-addresses/
- **Q:** What is the 2026 residential tax rate? **A:** Burnaby's 2026 residential mill rate totals 3.28373 per 1,000 dollars of assessed value, and gross taxes are the assessed value multiplied by the mill rate and divided by 1,000, before local area service, sewer parcel and business improvement area charges. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The pocket's two Gilmore Avenue schools sit on the same street a few blocks apart, so school drop-off traffic concentrates on South Gilmore Avenue rather than on Hastings Street. https://burnabyschools.ca/elementary-school-addresses/
- Willingdon Avenue is the dividing line: the pocket ends at it, and the Capitol Hill area begins one block east of Willingdon Avenue at the Hastings Street corner. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Hastings Street is the commercial edge, not a residential through street for the interior: the interior house streets run between Hastings Street and Lougheed Highway without a town-centre address of their own. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby's 2026 residential tax rate is 3.28373 per 1,000 dollars of assessed value, and the average residential assessment in Burnaby decreased by 5.29 percent in 2026, which does not necessarily mean an individual bill falls, because taxes depend on the change in a property's assessment relative to the class average. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates
- Property taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after the July due date and a further 5 percent after the September due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes
- Assessment notices arrive from BC Assessment each January, and the assessed value on the notice is the value the mill rate is applied to. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Confirm the lot's R1 SSMUH eligibility and unit count with the City before assuming a multiplex, because the count turns on lot area and on distance to a frequent transit network bus stop. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- Confirm the SD41 catchment for the exact address, since elementary assignments follow the address rather than the neighbourhood name. https://burnabyschools.ca/elementary-school-addresses/
- Confirm whether a Hastings Street frontage lot is zoned for multi-family use rather than R1, because the apartment edge and the house interior carry different rules. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
