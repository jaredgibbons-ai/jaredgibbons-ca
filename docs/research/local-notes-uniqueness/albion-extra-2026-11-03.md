# Albion Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: albion (Albion, Maple Ridge; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Albion is the east Maple Ridge growth area around 240 Street and Lougheed Highway (Highway 7), and it is one of the few Maple Ridge names that matches at every level: the City lists the neighbourhood as "Albion/Kanaka," the Official Community Plan (OCP) carries an Albion Area Plan, and "Albion" is the Paragon sub-area name. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The City publishes no boundary for the Multiple Listing Service (MLS) sub-area as such, so any Albion boundary is UNVERIFIED as an official line; the Albion Area Plan's Schedule 1 map is the closest official boundary document, and this page's working scope is the residential corridors on 240 Street, 102 Avenue and 104 Avenue, the Albion Fairgrounds and Sports Complex lands by the Fraser River, and farmland in South Albion. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/albion-area-plan-schedule-1pdf-1
- Albion sits next to Cottonwood and East Central to the west along Lougheed Highway, Thornhill to the northeast, Kanaka Creek ground to the north, and the Fraser River along its south edge. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Albion's planned stock is a mix of house forms set by its own Area Plan, not a single house type: the plan's residential designations are Low Density Residential (single detached at an urban standard), Ground-Oriented Residential, Cluster Residential and Townhouse, plus an Albion Village Commercial node. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/albion-area-plan-schedule-1pdf-1
- The growth pattern behind the newer houses here is the Area Plan itself: Council's October 28, 2025 update supports growth of local commercial nodes and adds buffers where urban development meets farmland, across a range of housing styles. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId=
- Named complexes and presale projects inside Albion were not confirmed on an official, developer or strata source in the research behind this note: UNVERIFIED — see the albion-maple-ridge-condos-townhomes-for-sale note for attached stock.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Maple Ridge-wide zoning, suite and small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules are written in full in the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note; Albion's own layer on top of them is the Albion Area Plan (OCP Chapter 10.2), so a lot's plan designation here must be checked as well as its zone. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId=
- South Albion lots meet farmland: the 2025 plan update introduces buffers where urban development meets farmland, and Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status is a per-parcel check on the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- On the Fraser River edge, Albion lots can sit in the floodplain: the flood construction level is 6.7 metres geodetic including freeboard, no habitable floor space may sit below it on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw section 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Albion an official neighbourhood or just a listing label? **A:** It is both, which is unusual in Maple Ridge. The City lists "Albion/Kanaka" among its neighbourhoods, the Official Community Plan carries an Albion Area Plan for the same ground, and the board uses "Albion" as the sub-area name — unlike West Central and East Central, which are board names only. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- **Q:** What changed in the 2025 Albion Area Plan update? **A:** Council adopted the update on October 28, 2025. It enhances walking and cycling connections, supports growth of local commercial nodes, introduces buffers where urban development meets farmland, and balances new residential development against protection of environmentally sensitive areas. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId=
- **Q:** Which schools are physically in Albion? **A:** Albion Elementary at 10031 240 Street and c'usqunela Elementary at 24093 104 Avenue are in the area, with Samuel Robertson Technical Secondary at 10445 245 Street and Kanaka Creek Elementary at 11120 234a Street at the west edge; addresses are from the British Columbia Ministry school file, and catchments are address-based, so run the exact address in the School District No. 42 (SD42) locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q:** Can a South Albion house have a suite? **A:** Not if the lot is in the floodplain area. Maple Ridge's suite rules bar suites on floodplain properties under Zoning Bylaw section 402.8(h), and no habitable floor space may sit below the 6.7-metre flood construction level on affected lots, so check the covenant on title before counting suite income. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Albion Fairgrounds hosts events year-round, including Country Fest, the Caribbean Festival and the Home Show, and the Albion Sport Complex beside it is home to Planet Ice (ice rink) and the Golden Ears Winter Club (curling) — event-day traffic on the surrounding streets is part of living here. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Kanaka Creek Regional Park (Metro Vancouver) runs along Albion's west side, with the Canyon Trail to Cliff Falls and the Bell-Irving Hatchery. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- No library branch sits in Albion; the Maple Ridge Public Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library) is at 130 – 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road in the Town Centre. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/maple-ridge-public-library
- Two school addresses on the live Albion page do not match the Ministry school file (c'usqunela Elementary and Kanaka Creek Elementary) — quote school addresses from the district or Ministry file, not the listing page. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- School District No. 42 has no middle tier: elementary schools are Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary schools are Grades 8 to 12, so Albion children change schools after Grade 7. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) runs along Albion's south side and 240 Street is its main north–south road, with 102 Avenue and 104 Avenue running east–west through the residential area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Haney Place Exchange, in the Town Centre to the west, is the bus hub for this side of the city; the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus runs from Coquitlam Central Station to Haney Place. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
- West Coast Express trains run weekdays between Waterfront Station (downtown Vancouver) and Mission City, with Port Haney Station at 22300 River Road and Maple Meadows Station at 20010 Dunn Avenue serving the west side of the city — no station sits in Albion. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check floodplain status lot by lot on the south edge: the City's floodplain construction document sets the 6.7-metre flood construction level, a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, and which Albion lots are affected is per-parcel only. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve status lot by lot in South Albion on the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping before assuming a house lot can be subdivided, filled or given a second dwelling. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- Check the Albion Area Plan Schedule 1 designation for any lot near 248 Street or a commercial node: plan designations here were updated October 28, 2025, and a lot's designation, not its current house, decides what can be built next. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/albion-area-plan-schedule-1pdf-1
