- College Park is an established residential area at Port Moody's west end, next to Glenayre and Seaview near the Burnaby border. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/city-hall/resources/Documents/OCP-neighbourhood-plan-areas_-_edited_for_EngagePM_website.pdf

- The Seaview sub-area beside it extends from College Park Way to Ingersoll Street and from Seaforth Way to Glenayre Drive. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050/news_feed/seaview

- Barnet Highway (Highway 7A) and St. Johns Street run east–west through the city, and College Park is the closest Port Moody neighbourhood to the Burnaby border on that route. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The College Park-specific trap is the small-lot rule: RS1-S small lot subdivision, minimum 9 m width and 325 m², is available only in Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside OCP Single Family–Low Density areas — College Park is not on that list. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- Port Moody Zoning Bylaw No. 2937 puts single detached homes in RS zones and multi-residential homes in RT and RM zones. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) is permitted in RS and RT zones outside the transit-oriented areas. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- The Suncor refinery lands beside College Park and Seaview are designated a Special Study Area in the OCP, so land use next door is an open planning question. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Port Moody-wide tax, utility and Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) treatment is in full — see the heritage-mountain-port-moody-houses-for-sale note.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I split a College Park lot into small lots? **A:** Not under the RS1-S small lot rule as published — that subdivision option, minimum 9 m width and 325 m², is limited to Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside OCP Single Family–Low Density areas, and College Park is not listed. Treat any lot-split value as unproven until the City confirms a route in writing for the exact lot. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- **Q:** What is the Special Study Area next door? **A:** It is the Suncor refinery lands beside College Park and Seaview, designated a Special Study Area in the Official Community Plan. That designation means the future land use has not been decided in the plan, so buyers should ask what is known, disclose the uncertainty, and check the current study status with the City rather than assuming either industry forever or redevelopment soon. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- **Q:** Which schools serve the area? **A:** School District 43 (SD43) catchments are set by exact address in the SD43 School Locator. District schools in this part of Port Moody include Glenayre Elementary and Seaview Community School, with Moody Middle and Port Moody Secondary serving central Port Moody. The status of the former College Park Elementary building at 300 Princeton Avenue is not confirmed on an SD43 source, so do not rely on the neighbourhood name for a school. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** Will this area redevelop quickly? **A:** The Official Community Plan does not expect that — it states widespread redevelopment is not likely in College Park, Glenayre and Seaview, with change expected mainly as gentle densification from small-scale multi-unit housing on individual lots. Existing lot patterns are expected to be retained. The one large exception in the grouping is the Woodland Park site, covered in Section 6. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- **Q:** How do I reach SkyTrain? **A:** By bus to Moody Centre Station — TransLink buses connect west Port Moody to Moody Centre Station on the Millennium Line Evergreen Extension, which also serves the West Coast Express. No station is inside College Park, and specific route numbers on College Park streets were not confirmed on a TransLink page read, so check the trip planner for the exact address. https://maps.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/system-maps/fwc26_regional_transit_connections.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Glenayre Centre at 492 Glencoe Drive, in adjoining Glenayre, is the local community centre; the Port Moody Recreation Complex is at 300 Ioco Road and the library at 100 Newport Drive. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/
- Several College Park, Glenayre and Seaview streets are adjacent to the Suncor Refinery lands, per the OCP. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- The OCP's Woodland Park sub-area policies call for a new local street connection from Mount Royal Drive to Seaview Drive, an appropriately sized park, and protection of the South Schoolhouse Creek Tributary riparian area. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Rocky Point Park and the Shoreline Trail at the head of Burrard Inlet are the city's waterfront park system, east of this area. https://engage.portmoody.ca/rocky-point-and-old-orchard-parks-master-plans/news_feed/background-rocky-point-today
- Eagle Ridge Hospital is in Port Moody at 475 Guildford Way, east of College Park. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- No shopping centre inside College Park was verified on an official source; the OCP notes only some commercial development on Clarke Road for the grouping. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
