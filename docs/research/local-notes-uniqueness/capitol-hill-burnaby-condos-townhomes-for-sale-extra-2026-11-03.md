## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Capitol Hill's condo and townhome stock sits on and below Hastings Street, not on the hilltop house streets: the City's major-projects list files the area's one current strata project under the Hastings plan area at 4472 Hastings Street, at Willingdon Avenue. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2024-08/Major-Development-Projects.pdf
- The neighbourhood's heritage anchor is the Capitol Hill Community Hall, built in 1947–1948 on a triangular lot in Dynes Park beside Hastings Street at the terminus of the former Hastings streetcar line, per the City's Heritage Burnaby record. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark563

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- New multi-family buildings in Burnaby are judged under the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (Bylaw No. 14815), whose district list separates a Townhouse District (R2) from Low-Rise Apartment (R3, R4), Mid-Rise Apartment (R5, R6) and High-Rise Apartment (R7, R8) districts — so a Hastings Street low-rise and an Ellesmere Avenue townhouse sit in different districts with different rules. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-07/Zoning-Bylaw-Consolidated-Without-Schedules.pdf
- A strata rental-restriction bylaw cannot be enforced here: the Province states that as of November 24, 2022 no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw, all such bylaws are invalid, and only a 55-or-older age-restriction bylaw is allowed; short-term rental bylaws are still allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- The same provincial page states that from November 1, 2023 a strata must contribute a minimum of 10% of its annual operating fund to the contingency reserve fund (CRF) when it approves its budget — the figure to check on the Form B of any small Hastings Street strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- Burnaby 2050, adopted December 9, 2025, now takes precedence over older community-plan land-use maps where they conflict, so pre-2026 plan wording for the Hastings corridor is not the current direction. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is there a SkyTrain station for Capitol Hill condos?
**A:** No station is in Capitol Hill; the rapid transit is bus on Hastings Street. TransLink's R5 Hastings Street RapidBus runs from Burrard Station to Simon Fraser University (SFU), and since September 7, 2026 the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus also runs on Hastings Street and down Willingdon Avenue to Metrotown Station, giving the Willingdon corner a one-seat ride to the North Shore and to Metrotown. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects

**Q:** What is actually being built for strata buyers on the hill?
**A:** One five-storey mixed-use strata building at 4472 Hastings Street, at Willingdon Avenue. The City's list describes 34 strata homes over ground-floor commercial retail with underground parking, by Capitol Hill (4472 Hastings) Ltd. Partnership, and records the rezoning at Final Adoption on June 10, 2025. It is a low-rise over retail, so a buyer is comparing it with the existing small Hastings buildings, not with a town-centre tower. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2024-08/Major-Development-Projects.pdf

**Q:** Which elementary school is in the neighbourhood itself?
**A:** Capitol Hill Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 7, at 350 South Holdom Avenue, is the in-area School District No. 41 (Burnaby) school on the Province's school list, and Burnaby North Secondary, Grades 8 to 12, is at 751 Hammarskjold Drive. Catchment is address-based, so a Hastings Street address west of Holdom should still be checked with the district before subjects are removed. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041

**Q:** Can a strata here stop me renting out my unit long-term?
**A:** No. The Province states that as of November 24, 2022 all strata residential rental-restriction bylaws are invalid, and no bylaw change was required for that to take effect. A Capitol Hill strata can still have a bylaw banning or limiting short-term rentals, so an owner planning short stays must read the registered bylaws, not the rental clause. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The R2 extension changed this page's transit on a specific date: TransLink states the extended R2 began September 7, 2026, runs 5 a.m. to 2 a.m. seven days a week, every 6 to 7 minutes at peak and every 10 to 15 minutes off-peak, and replaced the peak-only 222 Willingdon Express. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- The hill's community hall predates every strata here: Heritage Burnaby records the Capitol Hill Community Hall as built in 1947–1948 as a non-partisan public space, on a triangular lot in Dynes Park named for William Dynes, the hall association member who was foreman of its construction. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark563
- A second City-listed project touches the neighbourhood's east side: a non-market senior citizens and non-market rental development proposed at 6005 Pandora Street (rezoning REZ #21-14, architect Ankenman Marchand), recorded at Initial Report on June 14, 2021 — rental, not strata, so it adds neighbours, not competing condos. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2024-08/Major-Development-Projects.pdf
- Burnaby's residential water-meter program reaches strata homes too: the City states installation began in June 2026 with about 5,500 meter-ready homes, meters are mandatory and installed free outside the home, and flat-rate billing stays in place while data is collected. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewers/residential-water-meter-program

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Slope is this page's lot-level check: Capitol Hill's strata buildings sit on or below a steep hill, and the Zoning Bylaw 2026's Form and Character Development Permit Area guidelines are the document that governs multi-family form and character — a buyer of a hillside townhouse should confirm whether the lot is in a development permit area on the City's zoning map before relying on a renovation plan. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-07/Zoning-Bylaw-Consolidated-Without-Schedules.pdf
- For any strata purchase, read the depreciation report cycle: the Province's strata legislation page tracks the updated depreciation report requirements effective July 1, 2024, and the October 27, 2025 expansion of who may prepare them — a small strata's report date is a Form B question, not a listing fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
