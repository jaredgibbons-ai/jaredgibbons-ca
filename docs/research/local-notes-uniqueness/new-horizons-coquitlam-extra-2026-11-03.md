# New Horizons — Extra Local Notes
Page: new-horizons-coquitlam (New Horizons; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- New Horizons is a board sub-area in Coquitlam's city centre area, and no City-published boundary for it was found, so use its named anchors: the Windsor Gate community on the street called Windsor Gate, off Glen Drive and Pipeline Road, with Glen Park beside it and Pinetree Way and the two SkyTrain stations to the west. https://polyhomes.com/masterplan/windsor-gate/
- The sub-area name is a real-estate board name, not a City neighbourhood name: the City plans this ground as part of its city centre, while the developer, Polygon, calls the master-planned community here Windsor Gate. https://polyhomes.com/masterplan/windsor-gate/
- Glen Drive is the east–west street through the pocket: École Glen Elementary School is at 3064 Glen Drive, and the Windsor Gate towers stand on Windsor Gate just off Glen Drive. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Windsor Gate is the defining stock here: Polygon states it is a 25-acre master-planned community begun in 2008, built in a Frank Lloyd Wright-inspired style with wide, landscaped boulevards, and mixing high-rise towers with low-rise apartment buildings around a residents-only club. https://polyhomes.com/community/the-lloyd/
- The Lloyd, Windsor Gate, Polygon, concrete high-rise tower: Polygon describes solid concrete construction, cantilevered lines, brick detailing and decorative metal screens, with interiors built around overheight ceilings. https://polyhomes.com/community/the-lloyd/
- Shared housing-stock facts for New Horizons condos, townhomes and houses are covered in full in the new-horizons-coquitlam-condos-townhomes-for-sale and new-horizons-coquitlam-houses-for-sale notes; this note takes the street-level neighbourhood angle only (see the new-horizons-coquitlam-condos-townhomes-for-sale note).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City-wide zoning, Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) and the Interim Official Community Plan (OCP) are covered in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note (see the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note); the rules below are the ones specific to this pocket.
- Windsor Gate ownership carries the Nakoma Club with it: Polygon states homeowners in Windsor Gate get membership to the residents-only, 18,000-square-foot Nakoma Club, so a buyer here is buying into a shared amenity facility as well as a strata unit, and the club's costs sit inside the strata's finances. https://polyhomes.com/community/the-lloyd/
- The whole city is now covered by the Interim OCP, Bylaw No. 5511, 2026, adopted April 27, 2026, which replaced the former citywide plan and carries the former area plans in as schedules — check current, because older plan references for the city centre area are superseded. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What exactly is the Nakoma Club, and who can use it? **A:** The Nakoma Club is Windsor Gate's private, residents-only clubhouse. Polygon describes 18,000 square feet with an outdoor pool, a landscaped terrace, a fully equipped fitness centre, a gymnasium, a screening room, guest suites and a concierge. It is an amenity for Windsor Gate homeowners, not a public recreation centre, so a buyer outside Windsor Gate cannot use it, and a buyer inside it should read how it is funded in the strata documents. https://polyhomes.com/community/the-lloyd/
- **Q:** Which schools serve the Windsor Gate pocket? **A:** Polygon names the schools in this area as Westwood Elementary, École Glen Elementary School, École Maple Creek Middle School and Pinetree Secondary. Glen Elementary is at 3064 Glen Drive, in the pocket, and Pinetree Secondary is at 3000 Pinewood Avenue. School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) assigns students by permanent home address, so a tower address and a detached address on the same street can fall in different catchments and must be checked separately. https://polyhomes.com/masterplan/windsor-gate/
- **Q:** How close is rapid transit, in the operator's own terms? **A:** Two Millennium Line stations sit on Pinetree Way at the west side of the pocket: Lincoln Station and Lafarge Lake-Douglas Station. TransLink's route 186 timetable names both stations on its run along Pinetree Way past Glen Drive, and route 188 uses the same two stations. There is no station inside Windsor Gate itself; the stations are the pocket's western edge, reached via Glen Drive and Pinetree Way. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt186.pdf
- **Q:** What major road and water work is happening on Pipeline Road? **A:** Two projects overlap on the pocket's east side. The City is upgrading Pipeline Road from Guildford Way to David Avenue to four lanes with a pedestrian pathway and a two-way micromobility lane, and Metro Vancouver is building the Coquitlam Water Main, whose Robson to Guildford section runs along Pipeline Road between Robson Drive and Guildford Way, with construction started in fall 2023 and estimated completion in fall 2026. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1052/External-Partner-Projects
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Glen Park is the pocket's public park, and it is still changing: the City states it is working towards the next phase of improvements for Glen Park, with the project page last updated October 20, 2026. https://letstalkcoquitlam.ca/projects
- Coquitlam River Park is one block from Windsor Gate, by the developer's own description, which puts a river park at the east edge of a high-density pocket. https://polyhomes.com/masterplan/windsor-gate/
- Douglas College's Coquitlam campus and the Evergreen Cultural Centre are part of the pocket's daily geography: Polygon lists both among the amenities of this area, along with Lafarge Lake and Coquitlam Centre. https://polyhomes.com/masterplan/windsor-gate/
- Pipeline Road construction is not finished at the pocket's edge: the City is also running Pipeline Road active transportation and road safety improvements from Guildford Way to Lincoln Avenue, a segment that passes the Windsor Gate area. https://letstalkcoquitlam.ca/projects
Read: 2026-11-03

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Pipeline Road Upgrade Project, Pipeline Road from Guildford Way to David Avenue, City of Coquitlam capital project, road and active-transportation work: expansion to four lanes, a pedestrian pathway, two-way micromobility lane, intersection upgrades, lighting and boulevard trees — STATUS: under construction in phases through 2026, read 2026-11-03. https://www.coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/home/detail/1921
- Coquitlam Water Main, Robson to Guildford section, Pipeline Road between Robson Drive and Guildford Way, Metro Vancouver, drinking-water main: this section is 1.4 kilometres long and 3.2 metres in diameter; construction started in fall 2023 with estimated completion in fall 2026, and later sections run to 2034 — STATUS: under construction, read 2026-11-03. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1052/External-Partner-Projects

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City-wide Coquitlam tax rates, utility charges and billing dates are covered in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note (see the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note).
- Coquitlam is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so a residential owner in New Horizons must complete the annual declaration, including an owner holding a Windsor Gate strata unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 186 runs past the pocket on Pinetree Way: from Coquitlam Central Station via Pinetree Way — serving Lincoln Station and Lafarge Lake-Douglas Station and the Pinetree Way stop at Glen Drive — then David Avenue and Pipeline Road toward Hampton Park. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt186.pdf
- Route 188 uses the same Pinetree Way stations and then runs David Avenue and Coast Meridian Road to Port Coquitlam Station, giving the pocket a one-bus link east into Port Coquitlam. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt188.pdf
- The two SkyTrain stations on the pocket's west edge, Lincoln Station and Lafarge Lake-Douglas Station, are both on the Millennium Line Evergreen Extension. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt186.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- River-edge lots need a flood check, not an assumption: the City's floodplain mapping work covers the Coquitlam River floodplain, and the detached streets at the east side of this sub-area sit near that river corridor, so a specific lot's flood status is read from City mapping, not from the tower addresses on Windsor Gate. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/182/Coquitlam-Fraser-and-Pitt-Rivers-Floodplain-Mapping-Report-PDF
- Strata buyers here carry a second building to diligence: the Nakoma Club is a shared, residents-only facility, so its operating costs, reserves and any special levies flow through Windsor Gate strata budgets and should be read in the strata documents alongside the individual building's. https://polyhomes.com/community/the-lloyd/
- Construction exposure is current and dated: Pipeline Road carries both a City road widening and a Metro Vancouver water main through 2026, so access, noise and detours on the pocket's east side should be checked against the project pages before completion dates are assumed. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1052/External-Partner-Projects
