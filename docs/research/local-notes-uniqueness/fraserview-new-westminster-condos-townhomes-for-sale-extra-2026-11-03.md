# Fraserview New Westminster Condos and Townhomes — Extra Local Notes
Page: fraserview-new-westminster-condos-townhomes-for-sale (Fraserview, New Westminster; condos and townhomes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Fraserview is the south slope above the North Arm of the Fraser River, and it is a board sub-area name, not one of the ten neighbourhoods on the City's Official Community Plan (OCP) residential neighbourhoods map. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/OUR_CITY_2041_Official_Community_Plan_New_Westminsters_Residential_Neighbourhoods_Map.pdf
- The slope runs along Stewardson Way and the Waterfront Esplanade (North Arm) at its base, with Jamieson Court, Ross Drive and Francis Way carrying the strata stock above it, per the City's consolidated land use designations map. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/CNW_Interactive_Land_Use_Map___(Consolidated_September_2026).pdf
- The City publishes no historic neighbourhood context statement for Fraserview: its heritage context statement list covers Brow of the Hill, Connaught Heights, Downtown, Glenbrooke North, Massey-Victory Heights, McBride-Sapperton and the West End, not Fraserview. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php
- City-wide basics in full: see the-heights-new-westminster-houses-for-sale note.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Victoria Hill is the slope's tower cluster: the program index counts The Lookout at 22 East Royal Avenue, Victoria Hill at 15 Royal Avenue, The Carlyle at 280 Ross Drive, The Grove at 265 Ross Drive, The Whittaker at 225 Francis Way and Parkside at Victoria Hill at 271 Francis Way, most of them one developer's phases on the hill. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/CNW_Interactive_Land_Use_Map___(Consolidated_September_2026).pdf
- The waterfront edge holds the low-rise stock: Palace Quay at 69 and 71 Jamieson Court is the largest index building on the court, with Jamieson Place townhouses at 72 Jamieson Court beside it. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/CNW_Interactive_Land_Use_Map___(Consolidated_September_2026).pdf
- Two index entries sit off the hill — Queen's Park Place at 55 Blackberry Drive and Fraserview Park at 74 Miner Street — and the counted townhouse stock is thin (245 Francis Way, 72 Jamieson Court), because most Fraserview strata are apartment-form. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/OUR_CITY_2041_Official_Community_Plan_New_Westminsters_Residential_Neighbourhoods_Map.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Ground-oriented additions on this slope are design-controlled: OCP Development Permit Area (DPA) 1.2, Ground Oriented Housing, and DPA 1.3, Townhouses and Rowhouses, set the guidelines infill townhouse and rowhouse work must follow. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/OCP_DPA_1.3_Townhouses_and_Rowhouses_(Consolidated_April_2023).pdf
- The foreshore itself is a protected designation: the OCP land use map carries an Intertidal designation for the Fraser River foreshore, to preserve it in a predominantly natural state, with lookouts, trails and docks as complementary uses. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/CNW_Interactive_Land_Use_Map___(Consolidated_September_2026).pdf
- Every Fraserview strata of five or more lots must hold a depreciation report on the provincial five-year cycle in force since July 1, 2024, with no deferral vote. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is Fraserview an official City neighbourhood?
**A:** No. The City's OCP residential neighbourhoods map names ten neighbourhoods and Fraserview is not among them; the name comes from the real estate board sub-area system. Planning treatment for a Jamieson Court or Ross Drive address therefore runs through the OCP mainland schedules and the Downtown and Quayside designations nearby, not a Fraserview area plan. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/OUR_CITY_2041_Official_Community_Plan_New_Westminsters_Residential_Neighbourhoods_Map.pdf

**Q:** Which schools serve a Fraserview address?
**A:** School District No. 40 (SD40) catchments are address-based. The locator result recorded in the program research for 1010 Hamilton Street is Lord Kelvin Elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 5), Fraser River Middle (Grades 6 to 8) and New Westminster Secondary (Grades 9 to 12); run any other Jamieson Court or Francis Way address in the SD40 registration process before relying on it. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/

**Q:** Is the Fraserview waterfront in the floodplain?
**A:** Parts of the mainland lowland are. The City's flood strategy material states about 460 hectares, roughly 30% of the city, lies within the Fraser River floodplain, and that Queensborough is the part protected by a perimeter dyke — the mainland waterfront along Stewardson Way is not described as diked, so elevation and flood construction level are unit-level checks here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf

**Q:** How do I get downtown from Jamieson Court without a car?
**A:** By TransLink route 103, which runs the Quayside and Fraserview waterfront between the Quay and New Westminster Station on the Expo Line. There is no SkyTrain station inside Fraserview itself; New Westminster Station and 22nd Street Station are the nearest Expo Line stations for the slope's buildings. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Lookout Greenspace at Mayes Street and Stewardson Way is named for its vantage point over the North Arm of the Fraser River, with trees, lawns, paths, picnic tables and benches. https://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/neighbourhood-parks/articles/5400.php
- The Grove at Victoria Hill is a phase, not one building: index listings place it at 250, 265, 270 and 275 Ross Drive and Francis Way. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/CNW_Interactive_Land_Use_Map___(Consolidated_September_2026).pdf
- Fraser Mews at 340, 365 and 385 Ginger Drive is filed as townhouse in some listings but is a four-storey elevator apartment building in built form, and the index counts it as an apartment. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/CNW_Interactive_Land_Use_Map___(Consolidated_September_2026).pdf
- The Lookout's commonly quoted 295 Francis Way address is its sales centre; the buildings stand at 22 East Royal Avenue. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/OUR_CITY_2041_Official_Community_Plan_New_Westminsters_Residential_Neighbourhoods_Map.pdf
- Palace Quay carries two civic addresses, 69 and 71 Jamieson Court, for one building. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/CNW_Interactive_Land_Use_Map___(Consolidated_September_2026).pdf

- The 2026 home owner grant threshold on the City's page is $2,075,000 of assessed value, with the basic grant up to $570 and the additional grant up to $845, claimed from the Province rather than at City Hall, 511 Royal Avenue. https://www.newwestcity.ca/propertytaxes-utilities/sb_expander_articles/713.php

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 103 is the waterfront bus: it links the Jamieson Court and Quayside streets to New Westminster Station, per TransLink's Burnaby and New Westminster schedule book. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start at the river: the OCP's Intertidal designation and the City's floodplain figures mean a Jamieson Court ground-floor unit needs its flood construction level and strata insurance terms checked, not assumed from the hill above it. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- Check the phase, not the project name, at Victoria Hill: buildings sharing the Boiler House amenity centre and the Victoria Hill name are separate stratas on Ross Drive, Francis Way and East Royal Avenue, with separate depreciation reports. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
