## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Central Coquitlam-specific rule is proximity: lots near the Pinetree Way stations sit inside the City's Transit-Oriented Areas framework, while Como Lake streets do not. https://coquitlam.ca.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?view_id=2&clip_id=2891&meta_id=82306
- The Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers land within 30 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse, and Zoning Bylaw sections 519 and 523 set flood and riparian setbacks. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- Coquitlam-wide zoning, small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH), suites and carriage homes are treated in full — see the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Central Coquitlam address? **A:** School District 43 (SD43) catchments are set by exact address in a three-tier system of elementary, middle and secondary schools. The verified locator result for 1169 Pinetree Way is Glen Elementary, École Maple Creek Middle and Pinetree Secondary, while other central addresses feed schools including Central Elementary, Hillcrest Middle and École Dr. Charles Best Secondary. Run the exact address. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** Which SkyTrain stations can I use? **A:** Three Evergreen Extension (Millennium Line) stations serve the City Centre edge of Central Coquitlam: Coquitlam Central, Lincoln and Lafarge Lake-Douglas, on Pinetree Way and Barnet Highway. Coquitlam Central also offers West Coast Express commuter rail. Interior streets around Como Lake and Austin Avenue have no station inside the neighbourhood, so most trips start with a bus or drive to a station. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/station/coquitlam-central/schedule
- **Q:** What recreation is actually in the area? **A:** Town Centre Park and Como Lake Park are the City park names recorded for this part of Coquitlam, and the City Centre Aquatic Complex and Evergreen Cultural Centre sit at the City Centre edge. Como Lake Park is at 700 Gatensbury Street. Addresses for the other facilities were not confirmed on the sources read, so check the City facility pages before a showing trip. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Como-Lake-Park-26
- **Q:** Can an older house lot add units? **A:** Yes, under Coquitlam's small-scale multi-unit housing rules, which allow added units including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes where zoning and municipal services allow. Lots not connected to municipal water or sewer fall outside the rules. The full unit-count and lot-size table is in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; confirm zone and services for the specific lot with the City. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Q:** Which library serves the area? **A:** Coquitlam Public Library's City Centre branch is at 1169 Pinetree Way and its Poirier branch is at 575 Poirier Street, with a Library Link mobile service as well. Both branches serve Central Coquitlam addresses. The City Centre branch address is also the sample address used for the verified school locator result on this page. https://www.coqlibrary.ca/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- SD43 is a three-tier district — elementary, middle and secondary — unlike two-tier districts nearby. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- Programs of Choice and cross-catchment moves in SD43 go through application and a lottery / random draw with in-district priority, not catchment rights. http://sd43.bc.ca/cross-catchment
- Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody, is the Fraser Health hospital on the border serving this area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- Coquitlam Central is both a SkyTrain station and a West Coast Express station, so downtown commuter-rail trips start there. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/station/coquitlam-central/schedule

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check creek and ravine setbacks before pricing any addition or rebuild on a watercourse lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- The Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area reaches 30 metres from the top of bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- Confirm the exact zone and municipal services before advertising suite, carriage-home or multi-unit potential, because unserviced lots fall outside the small-scale multi-unit housing rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
