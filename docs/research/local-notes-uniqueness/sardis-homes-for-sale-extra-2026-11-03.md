# Sardis Homes — Extra Local Notes
Page: sardis-homes-for-sale (Sardis; homes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sardis is a neighbourhood inside the City of Chilliwack, not a separate municipality: the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan states Sardis is centrally located in Chilliwack, immediately south of Highway 1, bordered by the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) west and east, First Nations lands south and east, and the Vedder neighbourhood to the south. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- The governing plan is Schedule B5, Sardis Neighbourhood Plan, of the 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw 2025, No. 5500; the Sardis plan was adopted in October 2020 and updated in 2025. Street-level boundaries, catchments and parks belong in full to the sardis-houses-for-sale note — see that note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Sardis is not one housing era: sub-areas run from the older streets around Sardis Park and Knight Road, through the former military married quarters rebuilt as Garrison Crossing, to Promontory and Vedder Crossing toward the Vedder River. Plan names divide it into Sardis West Vedder, Sardis East Vedder, Sardis South, Garrison Crossing, Promontory and Vedder Crossing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Garrison Crossing alone holds more than 1,700 residential units, including refurbished homes and new townhouses and condominium apartments, on a 153-acre site, according to Canada Lands Company. Home-type detail is in the sardis-condos-for-sale, sardis-townhomes-for-sale and sardis-houses-for-sale notes. https://www.clc-sic.ca/node/69

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Two neighbourhood plans touch a Sardis purchase: the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan, Schedule B5, and the South Vedder Neighbourhood Plan, Schedule B6, adopted April 2022 and updated 2025, covering the Vedder Crossing side around Garrison Crossing and the Vedder River. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6734
- The South Vedder plan's direction is denser, walkable growth: density increasing along Vedder Road through infill and redevelopment, building a complete community near Garrison Crossing with housing variety, parks, transit and schools within walking distance. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6734
- The Sardis-Vedder Aquifer is protected drinking water: Schedule D-1 of the 2050 OCP designates it Development Permit Area 1, so subdivision, construction and new private wells in the mapped area can require a development permit. City-wide rules are in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale and chilliwack-duplexes-for-sale notes. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SD1%5FDPA%5FDrinking%5FWater%5FSupply%2Epdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Sardis its own town? **A:** No. Sardis is a neighbourhood of the City of Chilliwack, planned through Schedule B5 of the City's 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500. A Sardis buyer pays City of Chilliwack taxes, follows Chilliwack bylaws, and is served by School District No. 33 (SD33) and BC Transit. The name survives from the farming community that predates amalgamation, which is why it still appears in school names and board sub-area labels. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- **Q:** What is Garrison Crossing, and why do its streets look different? **A:** Garrison Crossing is the redevelopment of part of former Canadian Forces Base Chilliwack, which Canada Lands Company acquired from the federal government in 2011. Canada Lands states it preserved elements of the base, including refurbished Permanent Married Quarters with original fir floors, and built the Legacy Walk honouring those who lived and worked at the base from 1942 to its closure in 1995. https://www.clc-sic.ca/node/69
- **Q:** Which university is in Sardis? **A:** The University of the Fraser Valley (UFV) Chilliwack campus at Canada Education Park, 45190 Caen Avenue, opened in September 2012, beside the Trades and Technology Centre opened in 2007. The City's UFV page states UFV enrolls more than 16,000 students a year across its campuses. The campus sits on the Garrison and Vedder Crossing side of Sardis. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=77
- **Q:** What is planned for the Vedder Road side of Sardis? **A:** The South Vedder Neighbourhood Plan, Schedule B6, adopted April 2022 and updated 2025, frames growth around Vedder Road and Garrison Crossing as infill and redevelopment that adds density, improves mobility, and protects the Sardis-Vedder Aquifer. A buyer on Vedder Road or Keith Wilson Road should read both plans. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6734
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Garrison Crossing's street names are a memorial: Veterans Affairs Canada records that the original base streets were given names derived from the Halq'eméylem language of the Stó:lō First Nations — Chehalis, Coquitlam, Cowichan, Matsqui, Nicomen, Qualicum, Shawnigan, Soowahlie and Tamihi — and Sappers Way was named for the engineers who served at the base. https://www.veterans.gc.ca/en/remembrance/memorials/canada/garrison-crossing-street-names-plaque
- Sardis Library, 5819 Tyson Road, and the Sardis Sports Complex, 5725 Tyson Road, sit a few numbers apart on one street — Tyson Road is Sardis's civic spine east of Vedder Road. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/
- Elim Village, 45412 Chehalis Drive, is a large seniors campus inside Garrison Crossing, so Sardis carries seniors housing and services in the middle of a family neighbourhood. https://elimvillage.com/chilliwack/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- A Sardis home is taxed as a City of Chilliwack home: the 2025 total residential rate was 4.50189 per $1,000 of assessed value; full breakdown in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2527/Tax%5FRates%5Ffor%5Fthe%5FWebtugnc2fs1l9d%2Epdf
- Utilities are the City's, billed quarterly under the bylaws on the City's rates page; no separate Sardis utility or improvement-district charge was found in any source read. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in the City of Chilliwack, which includes Sardis, because the Province lists the city as a taxable area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- BC Transit, not TransLink, serves Sardis. Route 51 Vedder runs Downtown to the University of the Fraser Valley (UFV) campus via Vedder Road — Sardis's main north-south transit corridor. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=51&schedule=2024-07-05
- Route 52 Evans connects Cottonwood Mall, on Luckakuck Way in Sardis, with Downtown via Evans Road, a second corridor that does not use Vedder Road. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=52
- Highway 1 forms Sardis's north edge, per the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan; no drive time is stated, because no operator source measured one. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- The Vedder River is Sardis's southern flood source: Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets Flood Construction Levels and a 75-metre building setback from the river's natural boundary, mattering most in Vedder Crossing and south Sardis. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- The Downtown Neighbourhood Plan notes much of Sardis and Vedder remains above the historic flood zones, while dyked areas are protected to a 1-in-200-year flood standard — the parcel's own flood construction level is the check to make. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3556
- Former-base land is the Garrison Crossing check: Canada Lands records the base operated from 1942 to closure in 1995, so read title and any environmental or heritage notations for the specific lot. https://www.clc-sic.ca/node/69
