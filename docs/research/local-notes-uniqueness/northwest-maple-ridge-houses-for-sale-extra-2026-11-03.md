# Northwest Maple Ridge Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: northwest-maple-ridge-houses-for-sale (Northwest Maple Ridge; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Northwest Maple Ridge has no official City boundary — it is a board sub-area name, and the City's neighbourhood list does not include it. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Its house streets run west of the Town Centre toward the Pitt Meadows border, around 206 Street, 123 Avenue and Abernethy Way, with Fairview Elementary at 12209 206 Street and Laity View Elementary at 21023 123 Avenue as the street anchors. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/
- Maple Meadows Station at 20010 Dunn Avenue sits at this area's southwest side, on the Pitt Meadows side of the municipal edge of the wider west side. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The detached stock splits in two: RS-1 suburban house streets around 206 Street and 123 Avenue in the south, and larger rural and farm lots to the north, where the bylaw's RS-2, RS-3 and A-1 to A-3 minimums apply — see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note for those figures. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- Westview Secondary at 20905 Wicklund Avenue is the secondary school standing in these house streets. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The dividing rule here is servicing and land designation, read lot by lot: northern lots can fall in the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR), where the Agricultural Land Commission limits a parcel to one principal residence plus at most one additional smaller residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Suites and SSMUH unit counts are city-wide — see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note; the Northwest angle is that a suite is not permitted in the Floodplain Area, which matters on the low northern lots near the Alouette River system. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q: Is a Northwest Maple Ridge lot on city water and sewer, or on a well and septic?** **A:** It depends on the lot, and no official page read in this pass settles it street by street. The southern RS-1 streets around 206 Street are the urban side of the sub-area, while the northern rural and farm lots use the bylaw's larger RS-2, RS-3 and A-zone minimums, so confirm servicing with the City for the exact parcel. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Q: Which schools serve the area?** **A:** Fairview Elementary at 12209 206 Street and Laity View Elementary at 21023 123 Avenue stand in these streets, and Westview Secondary at 20905 Wicklund Avenue is the local secondary school. Catchment in School District 42 is by exact address, and the district warns a catchment school at capacity may not take the student, so run the address in the School Locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: Can I build a second house on a large northern lot?** **A:** On Agricultural Land Reserve land, generally no second full house. The Agricultural Land Commission frame allows one principal residence plus at most one additional residence, sized at up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 hectares or less and up to 186 m² on larger parcels. Check the parcel's ALR status on the Commission's mapping first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How do I commute downtown from here?** **A:** The closest rail is Maple Meadows Station at 20010 Dunn Avenue, at this area's southwest side. TransLink's West Coast Express runs weekdays between Waterfront in downtown Vancouver and Mission City, stopping at Maple Meadows and Port Haney in Maple Ridge, so this is a drive-or-bus-to-the-station commute, not a walk-to-rail one for most streets. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Davie Jones Elementary at 12030 Blakely Road is in Pitt Meadows, not Maple Ridge, even though it is one of the schools buyers ask about for streets near the municipal border — do not assume a Maple Ridge address falls in its catchment. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/
- Ridge Meadows Hospital at 11666 Laity Street is south-east of these streets, and the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is inside Haney Place Mall at 11900 Haney Place. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- Bear rules bite hardest on the northern, treed lots: garbage carts go out no earlier than 5:00 a.m. and come in by 7:00 p.m. on collection day under the City's Wildlife and Vector Control Bylaw guidance. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-maple-ridge-reminds-residents-keep-bears-and-other-animals-wild

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Maple Meadows Station is this area's transport anchor, at 20010 Dunn Avenue, with West Coast Express trains running weekdays toward Waterfront and Mission City. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- The first check on a northern lot is floodplain: Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 8121-2026 covers the Alouette River system as well as the Fraser River, and designates land within its Schedule A hazard areas and land less than 1.5 metres above a watercourse's natural boundary. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
- ALR status is the second check, and it is per parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping — a lot can look like a normal house lot and still carry ALR housing limits. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Treed lots at the urban edge can fall in the City's Wildfire Development Permit Area, where a development permit, or a restrictive covenant on design and landscaping for a single detached home, can be required. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/wildfire-development-permit-construction-requirements
