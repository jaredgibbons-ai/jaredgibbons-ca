## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A park's 55+ rule can lawfully stop a sale: under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation, a landlord in a 55+ park may refuse an assignment of the pad tenancy where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement — a refusal power with no equivalent in a strata sale. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- Assignment of the pad tenancy needs the landlord's prior written consent under section 28 of the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA), the landlord cannot charge a fee for that consent, and on assignment the pad rent and the date of the next increase stay the same for the buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- Pad rent increases are capped by the Province: for manufactured home site tenancies the 2026 limit is 2.3 percent plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees, with three full months' written notice and only one increase per 12 months. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- A manufactured home sale is only effective if registered with the Manufactured Home Registry, and a home cannot be sold without its original legible Canadian Standards Association (CSA) label — Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular homes; a lost label needs Technical Safety BC re-certification. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- In a strata, by contrast, only a 55-or-older residency bylaw is legal (Strata Property Act sections 123.1 and 123.2) — that bylaw restricts who may live in a unit, not who may own it, which is the core legal difference between Brookswood's park homes and a 55+ strata elsewhere in Langley. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** My spouse is under 55 — can we both live in a Brookswood park home? **A:** Not automatically. A park's 55+ rule sits in the tenancy regulation and the park's own rules, and a landlord may refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the age requirement; the strata exemptions for younger spouses do not govern a pad tenancy. Ask Cedar Creek Estates, Fernridge Park or Pineridge for its occupancy rule in writing before writing an offer. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q:** How much can my pad rent go up each year? **A:** Up to the provincial limit for manufactured home site tenancies: 2.3 percent plus a proportional amount for 2026. The proportional amount is the park's own change in local government levies plus regulated utility fees divided by its number of sites, so the same headline limit produces a different dollar increase at each Brookswood park. Increases need three full months' notice, once per 12 months. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q:** Does the park raise my pad rent when I buy the home? **A:** No. On an assignment under section 28 of the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same and the scheduled date of a later increase does not move. The landlord's consent is required first, the landlord cannot charge a fee for it, and the sale itself must be registered with the Manufactured Home Registry to take effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q:** What happens if the park is converted to another use? **A:** The landlord must first hold every government permit and approval, then give 12 months' notice and pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date, under section 42 of the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, with defined additional-compensation routes where the home cannot be moved. That conversion risk is the long-term tenure question behind every Brookswood park purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q:** Do I pay property tax on a park home I own? **A:** Yes, on the home itself. The Province states a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential owners, but the assessed value excludes the land's value where the home sits in a manufactured home park — the pad land is the landlord's. Township taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5 percent penalties at midnight July 2 and again at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The proportional amount makes two parks' increases differ in the same year, because it is each park's own levy and utility change divided by its own site count — ask the specific park for its last proportional-amount calculation, not a Langley average. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- Pet rules split the three parks on the listing record: Cedar Creek Estates allows pets with restrictions, Fernridge Park states no pets, and Pineridge's pet rule is NOT CONFIRMED — confirm the current rule with the park, because park rules change by landlord notice. https://www.estateblock.com/langley/5-3031-200-st-1968212
- George Preston Recreation Centre at 20699 42 Avenue and the Brookswood branch of the Fraser Valley Regional Library at 20045 40 Avenue are the public facilities Brookswood seniors actually use; there is no seniors centre inside the three parks' own streets beyond Cedar Creek's in-park community centre and Fernridge's clubhouse.
- Langley Memorial Hospital at 22051 Fraser Highway is the Langleys' hospital, a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency — Brookswood has no hospital of its own. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital
- The Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan protects trees and watercourses while previously undeveloped areas grow — the plan frame, not an age rule, is what shapes what gets built around the parks, including on the Agricultural Land Reserve edges at the community's south side. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/brookswoodfernridge-community-plan-adopted.aspx

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Tenure is the first risk: a park home is a Manufactured Home Registry asset on a rented pad, so the diligence set is the pad tenancy agreement, the park's written age and pet rules, the last proportional-amount calculation and the home's CSA label status — not strata documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- Check the parcel's designations in the Township's GeoSource mapping before assuming anything about the land under or beside a park: the Township directs applicants there for Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) and floodplain designations, and Brookswood's south and east edges meet ALR farmland and Murray Creek. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Park conversion is a statutory, compensated event (section 42 MHPTA: permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation) — the protection exists, but a buyer should still read the Brookswood-Fernridge plan designation for their park, because the plan is what a conversion application is judged against. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- What the Township pages do not tell a buyer: a specific park's current pad rent — no park-operator source stating a current pad rent for any Brookswood park was obtained in this program, so every pad figure must come from the park in writing at purchase.
