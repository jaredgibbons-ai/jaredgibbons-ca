## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What is actually inside Central Park? **A:** Central Park is an 86-hectare City park with a lake, Douglas fir, cedar and vine maple trees, tennis courts, an outdoor swimming pool, a horseshoe pitch, a playground, a spray park and a pitch-and-putt golf course, listed in the City of Burnaby's own film kit at 3883 Imperial Street. The pitch-and-putt is a City golf facility at the same Imperial Street address. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-02/Burnaby-Film-Office-Film-Kit.pdf
- **Q:** What is Swangard Stadium used for now? **A:** Swangard Stadium, at Central Park's northwest corner, is a City venue booked for sporting, cultural, corporate and fundraising events, and the City's Canada Day StreetFest puts its mainstage acts in Central Park beside it. The City also lists designated parking for permit holders at the main stadium parking lot, reached eastbound on Kingsway east of Boundary Road, during major park events. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2024-05-23/streetfest-returns-burnaby-canada-day-headline-performer-Dear-Rouge
- **Q:** Which SkyTrain station serves these houses? **A:** Patterson Station, on the Expo Line at Patterson Avenue and Central Boulevard, is the station at the park's northeast corner, and Metrotown Station is the next station east. The City directs festival visitors to both stations and closes parts of Patterson Avenue and Central Boulevard for its Central Park events, so station-adjacent houses also carry event-day road closures. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2024-05-23/streetfest-returns-burnaby-canada-day-headline-performer-Dear-Rouge
- **Q:** Does the Kingsway corridor change daily life on these streets? **A:** Yes, on event days most of all. Kingsway is the park's north frontage and the Metrotown shopping district's main street, and the City's event pages show road closures and parking restrictions on Patterson Avenue and Central Boulevard for park events, with a bike valet at the tennis courts at Patterson and Mayberry during StreetFest. Ordinary-day Kingsway traffic volumes are UNCONFIRMED from sources read. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2024-05-23/streetfest-returns-burnaby-canada-day-headline-performer-Dear-Rouge

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- A perimeter trail circles the park: the City's 2018 annual report records the first phase of an accessible four-metre-wide, multi-use Central Park Perimeter Trail along Boundary Road to Imperial Street. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2021-05/2018-Annual-Municipal-Report.pdf
- Central Park Outdoor Pool and Swangard Stadium both hold City-placed nasal naloxone kits inside their automated external defibrillator cases, part of a pilot the City launched in 2020. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/programs-and-policies/nasal-naloxone-kits
- There is no smoking in Burnaby parks: the City states Central Spark is non-smoking and that no smoking is allowed in Burnaby parks, with no designated smoking areas at the event. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/events/central-spark
- The park's pitch-and-putt parking and lower pond are reached from Imperial Street, on the houses' side of the park. https://www.golfburnaby.ca/golf/central-park
