# Port Moody Lots and Land — Extra Local Notes
Page: port-moody-lots-land (Port Moody; lots and land).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Port Moody is a whole-city land market: lots trade inside the City of Port Moody only, between Burrard Inlet to the north and the Coquitlam city boundary to the east and south. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- The ground splits in two: flat Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside lots along St Johns Street and Clarke Street, and hillside lots in the Heritage Mountain grouping (Heritage Mountain, Twin Creeks, Noons Creek, Mountain Meadows, Heritage Woods) named in Chapter 14 of the Official Community Plan (OCP). https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- "Port Moody" is also a Greater Vancouver board community name, but that community groups in the separate villages of Anmore and Belcarra; a lot inside the city limits follows City of Port Moody bylaws, not village rules. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Most Port Moody lots sit in single detached residential (RS) zones under Zoning Bylaw, 2018, No. 2937, and the city's own small-lot route is the Single Detached Residential – Small Lot (RS1-S) zone. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- An RS1-S lot needs at least 325 square metres (m²) of area and 9 metres (m) of width, and small-lot subdivision is only open in the Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside neighbourhoods on land designated Single Family-Low Density in the OCP. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- The scale is real but modest: at 2622 Henry Street an approximately 809 m² RS1 lot was rezoned RS1-S to split into two lots of about 404.6 m² each, each meeting the 9 m minimum width. https://pub-portmoody.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=24757
- City lots are on municipal water and sewer; there is no well-and-septic lot stock inside Port Moody's urban fabric. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/property-taxes/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Port Moody replaced its OCP in 2026: OCP Bylaw 2026, No. 3522 was adopted April 28, 2026 and repeals Bylaw No. 2955, so any land package still citing 2955 is out of date. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH = small-scale multi-unit housing) is permitted in RS and multi-residential (RT and RM) zones outside the transit-oriented areas, including coach houses and accessory dwelling units; the transit-oriented areas are exempt from the SSMUH rules. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- Moody Centre and the Ioco townsite are heritage conservation areas, where a heritage alteration permit can be required before new construction or reconstruction on a lot. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/
- Creek lots need a riparian assessment and the Zoning Bylaw Section 5.4 setbacks, and environmentally sensitive and steep-slope development permit areas (DPA = development permit area) can apply on the north-shore hillsides. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Can I split my Port Moody lot into two smaller lots?
**A:** Only through the RS1-S small-lot route, and only in Moody Centre, Seaview or Pleasantside on land designated Single Family-Low Density in the OCP. Each new lot needs at least 325 m² and 9 m of width, the rezoning to RS1-S comes first, and the Approving Officer considers the subdivision only after the rezoning is in place, as the 2622 Henry Street split shows. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/

**Q:** Can I put a coach house or extra units on a Port Moody lot instead of subdividing?
**A:** Yes, in most of the city: SSMUH, including coach houses and accessory dwelling units, is permitted in RS and RT zones outside the transit-oriented areas, with at least a three-unit minimum on RS lots. Near the two stations the lot sits in a transit-oriented area, which is exempt from SSMUH, so density there comes from its OCP and zoning position instead. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/

**Q:** Does heritage status change what I can build on a Moody Centre lot?
**A:** Yes. Moody Centre and the Ioco townsite are heritage conservation areas, and the City can require a heritage alteration permit for new construction, reconstruction or changes on included properties. That permit sits on top of zoning, so answer the permit question before pricing a teardown or new build on a heritage-area lot. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/

**Q:** What extra diligence does a hillside or creek lot need?
**A:** More than its lot size suggests. Watercourse lots need a riparian assessment and must meet the Zoning Bylaw Section 5.4 setbacks, and environmentally sensitive and steep-slope development permit areas can apply on hillside ground. The buildable area after setbacks and permit conditions, not gross lot area, decides the project. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Small-lot subdivision is a three-neighbourhood tool only: a lot in College Park, Glenayre or Heritage Woods cannot use the RS1-S route no matter how wide it is, because the zone is limited to Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- A Port Moody address can catchment into Coquitlam: a recorded School District No. 43 (SD43) locator run for 100 Newport Drive returned Gleneagle Secondary in Coquitlam, so run the exact lot address in the SD43 School Locator before marketing a school. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- The utility bill and the tax bill land months apart: the 2026 utility bill was due February 27, 2026, while 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/property-taxes/
- Port Moody's second station matters to land value: Inlet Centre Station is the station the Coronation Park / Inlet District lands sit beside, and both Port Moody stations are on the Millennium Line. https://engage.portmoody.ca/coronation-park-3-ocp-amendment-and-rezoning
- An RS1-S rezoning does not need a public hearing where the City proceeds without one: the 120 April Road RS1-S rezoning notice states no public hearing would be held under section 464(2) of the Local Government Act. https://www.portmoody.ca/news-notices-and-community-matters/posts/notice-of-proposed-zoning-amendment-bylaw-without-public-hearing-october-14-2025/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Port Moody's 2026 residential municipal rates under Annual Property Tax Rates Bylaw, 2026, No. 3541 are 1.61574 per $1,000 for general municipal purposes, plus 0.70211 police and 0.24678 asset renewal levy per $1,000. http://www.portmoody.ca/media/tiabceqp/2026-mill-rates.pdf
- The 2026 utility bill for a detached home totals $1,892 for water, sewer, garbage, green waste and recycling, and was due February 27, 2026. https://www.portmoody.ca/utilities
- 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding amounts after that date and a further 5% after September 15, 2026. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/property-taxes/
- Port Moody is a listed Speculation and Vacancy Tax area in Metro Vancouver, so a vacant lot held by a non-exempt owner is inside the declaration system. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
