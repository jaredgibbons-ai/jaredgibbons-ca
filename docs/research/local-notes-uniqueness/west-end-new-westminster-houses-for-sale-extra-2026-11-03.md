# West End Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: west-end-new-westminster-houses-for-sale (West End; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The West End is the City of New Westminster's western residential neighbourhood: the City's west-side study area pairs the West End with Connaught Heights, bordered by Tenth Street to the north, Fenwick Avenue to the west, Stewardson Way and Sixth Avenue to the south, and Twelfth Street to the east; the full shared area facts are in the west-end-new-westminster note. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/Westside%20Child%20Development%20Hub%20New%20West.pdf
- Twelfth Street is the neighbourhood's commercial spine and its eastern edge, and the ground slopes down south and west to the Fraser River. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/220121_CNW_Street_Tree_Master_Plan_Final_reduced(1).pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The West End is predominantly single-family in character, with commercial and multi-family uses only on sections of Twelfth Street, Sixth Street and Eighth Street, per the City's Street Tree Master Plan. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/220121_CNW_Street_Tree_Master_Plan_Final_reduced(1).pdf
- The build era is early: the West End developed in the late 1800s and early 1900s, and many residential streets have rear laneways, which is the physical basis for the City's infill and laneway rules here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/220121_CNW_Street_Tree_Master_Plan_Final_reduced(1).pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The City's Infill Housing Program, adopted June 2026 through Official Community Plan and Zoning Bylaw amendments, permits up to six infill units on most single detached and duplex properties, with the RGO-A and RGO-B property map published. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- New Westminster's zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001, and its RS districts allow secondary suites, with laneway and carriage houses regulated under the same bylaw. https://www.newwestcity.ca/planning-building-and-development/zoning
- The City commissioned a Twelfth Street Heritage Inventory in 2003 because the street was expected to see new commercial and residential development, so heritage checks matter on the Twelfth Street edge. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/CNW_DOCS_201027_v2_Twelfth_Street_Heritage_Inventory(1).pdf
- New Westminster is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** How many homes can I put on a West End house lot now? **A:** Up to six infill units on most single detached and duplex properties, under the Infill Housing Program's Official Community Plan and Zoning Bylaw amendments adopted June 2026; the City has published the RGO-A and RGO-B property map that shows where the infill zones apply. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- **Q:** Do West End streets have laneways? **A:** Many do: the City's Street Tree Master Plan records that the West End developed in the late 1800s and early 1900s and that many residential streets have rear laneways, and laneway and carriage houses are regulated under Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/220121_CNW_Street_Tree_Master_Plan_Final_reduced(1).pdf
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves the West End? **A:** New Westminster Secondary School, at 820 Sixth Street, is School District No. 40's (SD40) only secondary school, serving the whole city; the recorded locator chain for 835 Eighth Street on the area's east side is Lord Kelvin Elementary, Fraser River Middle and New Westminster Secondary. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- **Q:** When are New Westminster property taxes due? **A:** Property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty on July 3 and a second 5 percent penalty on September 3, 2026. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the RGO-A and RGO-B property map before assuming six infill units, because the program permits up to six on most, not all, single detached and duplex properties. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- Check the Twelfth Street Heritage Inventory and the City's heritage register before planning exterior work on a Twelfth Street property. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/CNW_DOCS_201027_v2_Twelfth_Street_Heritage_Inventory(1).pdf
- Confirm the SD40 catchment for the exact address, since the city's single secondary school does not settle the elementary and middle assignments. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
