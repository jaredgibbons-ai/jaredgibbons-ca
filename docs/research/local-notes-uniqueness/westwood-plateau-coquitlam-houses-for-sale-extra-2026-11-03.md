- Westwood Plateau is a master-planned residential neighbourhood on Eagle Mountain in northern Coquitlam, bordering Port Moody and Anmore to the west, covering 2,309 hectares (5,706 acres) with almost 19,775 people, per the City's Westwood Plateau Community Profile 2019. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/214/2019-Westwood-Plateau-PDF?bidId=
- The house streets sit above the attached stock, which clusters on Plateau Boulevard, Parkway Boulevard, Paddock Drive and Dayanee Springs Boulevard below them; the strata stock is covered in the westwood-plateau-coquitlam-condos-townhomes-for-sale note. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/214/2019-Westwood-Plateau-PDF?bidId=
- Westwood Plateau Village, the local shops, is at the intersection of Parkway Boulevard and Johnson Street, as stated in the City profile. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/214/2019-Westwood-Plateau-PDF?bidId=

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The plateau's stock is a master-planned mix: detached-house streets above and townhouse and condo clusters on Plateau Boulevard, Parkway Boulevard, Paddock Drive and Dayanee Springs Boulevard below, all inside the one 2,309-hectare community the City profile describes. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/214/2019-Westwood-Plateau-PDF?bidId=
- Detached lots here sit in Coquitlam's small-scale residential zones under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996, and the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules as adopted in Coquitlam apply to them. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquitlam

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Coquitlam's SSMUH rules allow up to 3 units on lots of 280 square metres or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 square metres and under 4,050 square metres, and the City states it currently has no bus stops meeting the provincial frequent-service criteria, so the 6-unit tier does not apply. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquitlam
- Small-scale housing in Coquitlam can include secondary suites, garden cottages and carriage houses as accessory units, which the City groups with duplexes, triplexes and fourplexes as Small-Scale Residential. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquitlam
- Coquitlam is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

- **Q:** How big is Westwood Plateau? **A:** The City's community profile gives 2,309 hectares (5,706 acres) and almost 19,775 people for the master-planned neighbourhood on Eagle Mountain, which is why its house streets and its strata clusters read as separate pockets inside one sub-area name. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/214/2019-Westwood-Plateau-PDF?bidId=
- **Q:** Where are the local shops? **A:** Westwood Plateau Village, at the intersection of Parkway Boulevard and Johnson Street, is the local shopping node named in the City profile; the profile is the source for the community's layout of village, schools and parks. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/214/2019-Westwood-Plateau-PDF?bidId=
- **Q:** How many units can a plateau house lot take under the small-scale rules? **A:** Up to 3 units on lots of 280 square metres or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 square metres and under 4,050 square metres, and because Coquitlam currently has no bus stops meeting the provincial frequent-service criteria, the provincial 6-unit tier near frequent transit does not apply in the city. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquitlam
- **Q:** When are Coquitlam property taxes due? **A:** Property tax payment was due July 2, 2026, covering January 1 through December 31, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5 percent penalty on any unpaid balance after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
