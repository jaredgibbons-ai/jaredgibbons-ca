# Walnut Grove Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: walnut-grove (Walnut Grove; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Walnut Grove is the Township of Langley community between Highway 1 and the Fraser River, on the 200 Street / 208 Street / 88 Avenue grid. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/service-changes/2026/2026_fall_service_change.pdf
- 200 Street runs the length of Langley from the US border (as Carvolth Road) north through Walnut Grove to the Golden Ears Bridge.
- Derby Reach Regional Park at 21801 Allard Crescent is the Fraser riverfront park on Walnut Grove's north side. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/derby-reach-regional-park
- Carvolth Exchange, the Township's largest transit hub, sits next to Highway 1 on 202 Street at the community's south edge.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Walnut Grove is a planned suburban mix of detached homes and strata townhouse clusters built around its schools and parks.
- Walnut Grove Secondary School at 8919 Walnut Grove Drive describes itself as having an enrolment of almost 2,000 students, making it the largest school in Langley. http://wgss.ca/
- Metro Vancouver describes Derby Reach as a riverside park with the Fort-to-Fort Trail and the 38-site Edgewater Bar Campground. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/derby-reach-regional-park

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, as amended by the Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing Bylaw No. 6020 (2024), now allows combinations of suites, accessory dwelling units and small multi-unit forms on eligible single-family lots. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/Report-to-Council----Small-Scale,-Multi-Unit-Housing,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- That report states Bill 44 requires two to six units in restricted residential zones depending on lot and servicing, with Township zoning updated by June 30, 2024. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/Report-to-Council----Small-Scale,-Multi-Unit-Housing,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- Riverfront lots sit behind the Township's Fraser River dike system; see Section 9 for the dike check. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/drought-flooding-dikes-dams/integrated-flood-hazard-management/governance/dike-management/dike-inventory

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** How does the 555 bus work for commuting? **A:** The 555 Port Mann Express runs between Carvolth Exchange and Lougheed Station over Highway 1 and the Port Mann Bridge. TransLink's Fall 2026 service changes add weekday afternoon trips toward Carvolth Exchange every 10 minutes from 3:30–4 p.m. and every 8 to 9 minutes from 4–6 p.m., shortening waits for the return commute to Langley. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/service-changes/2026/2026_fall_service_change.pdf
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves Walnut Grove? **A:** Walnut Grove Secondary School on Walnut Grove Drive is the community's secondary school. The school states it is located in Walnut Grove with almost 2,000 students, offers English and French academic studies, and is the largest school in Langley; SD35 still assigns catchments by exact address through its locator, so verify a specific home there. http://wgss.ca/
- **Q:** What is at Derby Reach? **A:** Derby Reach Regional Park is the Fraser River park at 21801 Allard Crescent. Metro Vancouver lists it as a nature preserve and recreation park with hiking trails and scenic viewpoints, open 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. in the listing read, and its Fort-to-Fort Trail and Edgewater Bar Campground are the features residents use for riverfront walks and camping. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/derby-reach-regional-park
- **Q:** Are elementary catchments fixed across Walnut Grove? **A:** No, they vary by street across the community's several elementary schools. SD35 publishes individual catchment maps for schools including Alex Hope, Dorothy Peacock, Topham and West Langley elementaries and for Walnut Grove Secondary, and directs families to its School Locator Tool or the board office at 604-534-7891 if unsure. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/catchment-maps

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Walnut Grove homes pay Township flat-rate utilities where on the flat-rate system, billed separately from taxes. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- The 2026 single-family flat rates are $779.96 water and $716.47 sewer; multi-family rates are $667.22 water and $609.00 sewer. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- A secondary suite adds $233.99 water and $214.94 sewer in 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- Unpaid metered balances after the due date carry a 10% penalty, and metered bills go out three times a year. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- The Township of Langley is a designated Speculation and Vacancy Tax taxable area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- For Township of Langley headline tax rates, see the langley-farms note.

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Highway 1's 200 Street interchange and the Golden Ears Bridge via 200 Street are the community's freeway links.
- TransLink's 555 Port Mann Express connects Carvolth Exchange to Lougheed Station, with increased Fall 2026 afternoon frequency toward Carvolth. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/service-changes/2026/2026_fall_service_change.pdf
- Local routes 562 (Langley Centre/Walnut Grove) and 595 (Langley Centre/Maple Meadows Station) serve Carvolth Exchange per transit records read.
- The Fort-to-Fort Trail through Derby Reach gives a riverfront walking and cycling route toward Fort Langley. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/derby-reach-regional-park
