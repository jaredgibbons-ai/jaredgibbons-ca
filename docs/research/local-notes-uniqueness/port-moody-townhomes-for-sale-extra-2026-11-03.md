## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Since 2024 the provincial Transit-Oriented Area rules (Bill 47) set minimum densities around Moody Centre Station: up to 20 storeys within 200 metres, 12 within 400 metres, 8 within 800 metres. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050?tool=qanda
- Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw, 2026, No. 3522, Chapter 14 groups Heritage Mountain with Twin Creeks, Noons Creek, Mountain Meadows and Heritage Woods, separately from the station areas. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Tree removal on a development site needs a permit and arborist report under Tree Protection Bylaw, 2026, No. 3531 — in full in the heritage-mountain-port-moody-houses-for-sale note. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/environmental-protection-and-requirements/
- Province-wide: long-term rental-restriction bylaws are unenforceable since November 24, 2022; the only enforceable age bylaw is 55-plus (55+). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Which SkyTrain stations serve Port Moody townhouses?
**A:** Two, both on the Millennium Line's Evergreen Extension: Moody Centre Station, at the transit exchange beside the West Coast Express tracks, and Inlet Centre Station, under the Barnet Highway overpass at Ioco Road. Klahanie and Suter Brook sit between them. TransLink announced the final station names in March 2013. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2013/03/evergreen-line-extension-station-names-announced/

**Q:** What is The Canoe Club, and what does it mean for strata fees?
**A:** The Canoe Club is Klahanie's shared amenity building: Polygon describes a 15,000-square-foot facility with outdoor pool, hot tub, tennis courts, gym, theatre and guest suites, serving the whole master plan. Part of a Klahanie fee buys into that shared structure — compare it on that basis, using the Form B. https://polyhomes.com/masterplan/klahanie/

**Q:** Can I rent out a Klahanie townhouse long-term?
**A:** Yes. No British Columbia strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, in force since November 24, 2022, even where older bylaws record restrictions. Short-term rentals can still be restricted, and the master plan's own documents should be read alongside the strata bylaws before a tenant is placed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

**Q:** How does the West Coast Express differ from the SkyTrain at Moody Centre?
**A:** They share the station site, not the service pattern. The West Coast Express is TransLink's weekday commuter railway between Waterfront Station and Mission City, stopping at Moody Centre; the Millennium Line SkyTrain there runs all day, every day. Off-peak commuters are SkyTrain and bus riders. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

**Q:** Which schools serve Klahanie and Suter Brook?
**A:** Port Moody is in School District No. 43 (Coquitlam), with elementary, middle (Grades 6 to 8) and secondary schools and address-based catchments. Run the townhouse's exact address in the district's catchment tools: the Klahanie, Suter Brook and Heritage Mountain catchments divide within a few blocks of each other. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Shoreline Park's trail network runs about 6 kilometres along the Burrard Inlet shore below Klahanie, linking Rocky Point Park eastward. https://polyhomes.com/masterplan/klahanie/
- Suter Brook Village and Newport Village, either side of Ioco Road, are the two planned shopping villages serving this stock. https://polyhomes.com/masterplan/klahanie/
- The Moody Centre TOD vision dates to 2017; the 2024 provincial Transit-Oriented Area designation (Bill 47) now overlays the City's framework on the same ground. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050?tool=qanda
- South of St. Johns Street is a designated Special Study Area in the OCP — studied for change, not yet re-planned. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/regional-context-statement-port-moody-2026.pdf
- Klahanie's building names (The Tides, Salal, Inglenook) repeat across phases — the strata plan number, not the building name, identifies the legal complex. https://polyhomes.com/masterplan/klahanie/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- In full in the heritage-mountain-port-moody-houses-for-sale note: Bylaw, 2026, No. 3541 sets the 2026 municipal general rate at 1.61574 per $1,000, plus police 0.70211 and asset renewal 0.24678; taxes were due July 2, 2026. http://www.portmoody.ca/media/tiabceqp/2026-mill-rates.pdf
- The 2026 single-family utility bill (water, sewer, garbage, green waste, recycling) is $1,892, due February 27, 2026; strata figures are set complex by complex. https://www.portmoody.ca/utilities
- Port Moody is a listed taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax; owners must declare annually. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Slope is a build fact: the 3180 St. Johns Street file records its sloping site pushing parking above grade and adding four storeys of effective height — check grading and drainage on any St. Johns–edge townhouse likewise. https://engage.portmoody.ca/3180-st-johns-street-rezoning
- Klahanie's first phases date to the mid-2000s, inside the mandatory depreciation-report cycle; read the report's funding models against the contingency reserve fund on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Hillside complexes back onto Noons Creek and Twin Creeks ravines: confirm creek setbacks and the Tree Protection Bylaw, 2026, No. 3531 position before exterior work. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/environmental-protection-and-requirements/
