## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is in Bradner itself? **A:** Bradner Elementary, at 5291 Bradner Road, is the in-community school. The district describes it as a community school whose roots reach back into the late 1800s, with many past pupils still living and working in the area. Catchments are address-based in District 34, so confirm the property address (https://yale.abbyschools.ca/schools/bradner).
- **Q:** Where do Bradner students go after elementary school? **A:** The settled feeder pattern for the Bradner Elementary address is Eugene Reimer Middle School, at 3433 Firhill Drive, and that school's pages name former students now at Rick Hansen Secondary School. District 34 catchments are address-based, so run the specific address through the district locator (https://eugenereimer.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/aboutus/eugenereimer).
- **Q:** Is Bradner part of urban Abbotsford? **A:** No. The City profiles Bradner with Mt. Lehman as small historic rural centres surrounded by agricultural lands in the northwest corner. That is why agricultural zoning and Agricultural Land Reserve rules, not urban infill rules, frame most buying questions here (https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/2014%20Bradner-Mt.Lehman%20Demographic%20Profile_spreads.pdf).
- **Q:** What is Bradner Hall used for? **A:** Bradner Hall, at 5305 Bradner Road, is the community hall: the City used it for a Bradner agriculture open house, and the City profile names it as the hall where the annual Bradner Daffodil Show is held, beside the elementary school (https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/AgRefresh%20Stage%203%20Winter%202017-18%20Engagement%20Results.pdf).
