# Scott Creek Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale (Scott Creek; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Scott Creek has no City-published boundary, so treat street names, not a map line, as the check: the house pocket centres on Lansdowne Drive in northwest Coquitlam, roughly Guildford Way south, David Avenue north, Johnson Street and Pipeline Road east, and Falcon Drive and Creekside Drive toward the Port Moody side west, with Scott Creek itself running through it in a green corridor. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- Three School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) (SD43) schools anchor the pocket on one corridor: Eagle Ridge Elementary, 1215 Falcon Drive, Scott Creek Middle, 1240 Lansdowne Drive, and Gleneagle Secondary, 1195 Lansdowne Drive, across from the middle school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- The City Centre Area Plan Schedule A map is the plan map that actually shows these streets: it includes Falcon Drive, Scott Creek Middle and Gleneagle Secondary, with David Avenue at the top of the mapped area. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/3487/City-Centre-Area-Plan-Servicing-Assessment-PDF

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses here are the low-density stock around the Lansdowne Drive and Falcon Drive school campus, not apartment or townhouse stock: the City Centre Area Plan Schedule A legend designates this part of the plan area "One Family Residential" and "Compact One Family Residential," alongside apartment designations closer to City Centre. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/3487/City-Centre-Area-Plan-Servicing-Assessment-PDF
- No City, SD43 or BC Assessment public page read for this note states a typical build decade, typical lot size or named subdivision for Scott Creek houses, so era and lot size are lot-by-lot checks on the City's QtheMap zoning viewer and the title, not a neighbourhood average. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- What the City does publish for these lots is the rebuild route: most low-density lots now sit in one of four small-scale residential zones, R-1, R-2, R-3 or R-4, each with its own minimum lot size for adding units, from 465 square metres in R-2 to 930 square metres in R-4. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City-wide, in full here only: Coquitlam's zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996, and on June 9, 2025 Council adopted zoning changes for Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH), with supporting amendments June 23, 2025. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- City-wide SSMUH numbers: up to 3 units on lots of 280 square metres or smaller, and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 square metres but under 4,050 square metres; the 6-unit tier near frequent-service bus stops does not apply because the City states Coquitlam currently has no bus stops meeting the provincial frequent-service criteria. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- City-wide forms allowed include secondary suites, carriage houses, laneway homes, garden cottages, duplexes, triplexes and fourplexes, and units may be stratified and sold or kept as rentals, subject to the zone. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- Scott Creek-specific trigger: the City tells owners to contact Development Services before designing if there is a creek on or next to the lot, a steep slope on or next to it, or the house was built before 1945 — all three are live questions on creek-side and slope lots off Lansdowne Drive. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- Creek rule: land within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of a watercourse in the City Centre Area Plan needs a Watercourse Protection Development Permit before land is altered or developed. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- The whole city is now covered by the Interim Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 5511, 2026, adopted April 27, 2026, which replaced Citywide OCP Bylaw No. 3479, 2001 and carries the former area plans in as Schedule J schedules — check current, as plan references in older documents are superseded. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools are actually in Scott Creek? **A:** Three SD43 schools sit inside the pocket, all on the Lansdowne and Falcon corridor: Eagle Ridge Elementary at 1215 Falcon Drive for kindergarten to Grade 5, Scott Creek Middle at 1240 Lansdowne Drive for Grades 6 to 8, and Gleneagle Secondary at 1195 Lansdowne Drive for Grades 9 to 12. Being inside the pocket is not the same as being assigned: SD43 catchment is by permanent home address, so run the specific address in the SD43 School Locator before relying on any of the three. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** Can I add a suite, carriage house or extra units to a Scott Creek house lot? **A:** Possibly, under the small-scale rules adopted June 9, 2025. Lots of 280 square metres or smaller may allow up to 3 units and larger lots under 4,050 square metres up to 4 units, in zones R-1 through R-4. The City flags three early checks for this area: a creek on or beside the lot, a steep slope, or a house built before 1945 — any one means talk to Development Services first. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Q:** Does Scott Creek itself restrict building near my lot? **A:** Yes, if the lot is close enough. In the City Centre Area Plan, land within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of a watercourse falls in the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area, and altering land there needs that permit, with conditions to protect riparian area, water quality and slope stability. Scott Creek runs through this neighbourhood in a ravine corridor, so creek-adjacent lots on the side streets off Lansdowne Drive are the ones to measure first. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- **Q:** Is Scott Creek in a river floodplain? **A:** The mapped river floodplains are elsewhere. The City's floodplain mapping work covers the Coquitlam, Fraser and Pitt river floodplains, and this upland Lansdowne Drive pocket sits above them; the local water risk here is the Scott Creek corridor itself, not a river dike system. The City classifies its creeks for environmental value and monitors flow, level and water quality on various creeks, and a specific lot still needs checking on City mapping before purchase. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/182/Coquitlam-Fraser-and-Pitt-Rivers-Floodplain-Mapping-Report-PDF
- **Q:** What recreation is walkable from these houses? **A:** Eagle Ridge Park, entrance at 2635 Runnel Drive and fronting Lansdowne Drive at the schools, is the centre: natural grass soccer and rugby fields, baseball and softball diamonds, a lacrosse box with four pickleball courts, four lit tennis courts, playground, trails and washrooms. The City also places the Scott Creek Community Garden between Scott Creek Middle and Eagle Ridge Elementary, and lists the park as the starting point of the Coquitlam Crunch Trail. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Eagle-Ridge-Park-36

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Coquitlam Crunch Trail climbs 2.2 kilometres on the British Columbia Hydro right-of-way from this pocket, with two stair sets totalling 894 steps and 242 metres of elevation gain to the top of Westwood Plateau; access points include Eagle Ridge Park, Guildford Way, two on Lansdowne Drive, David Avenue and Bramble Park on Panorama Drive. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/coquitlamcrunchtrail-27
- Eagle Ridge Outdoor Pool, a subfacility of Eagle Ridge Park, is an eight-lane, 25-metre outdoor pool with 1-metre and 3-metre diving boards, and its listed 2026 season ran May 30 to September 7 — seasonal, not year-round. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/-91
- New small-scale builds here must file the City's Small-Scale Residential Fire Flow Requirements Form with the building permit application, confirming available fire flow for the lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City-wide, in full here only: the City's Summary of 2026 Tax Rates sets the total municipal rate for Class 1 Residential at $2.2756 per $1,000 of assessed value, made up of $2.1993 municipal services plus $0.0763 drainage, and the grand total with other governments at $3.8520 per $1,000. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17264/2026_Tax-Rates
- City-wide: 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026 for January 1 to December 31, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
- City-wide utilities: the 2026–2030 Financial Plan limits 2026 utility increases to 2% for water, 4% for sewer and drainage and 1% for solid waste, and estimates total municipal taxes and utilities for an average residential home at about $4,558; utility bills were due March 31, 2026. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17424/2026_Financial_Plan_Public_Book
- City-wide: Coquitlam is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so residential owners there must complete the annual declaration; reserve, treaty and self-governing Indigenous Nation lands are excluded. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area layer first for any lot near Scott Creek: within 30 metres of top of bank or ravine bank in this plan area, the permit can add conditions on riparian protection, runoff quality and erosion or landslip risk, and the guide does not itself set the final setback for a specific lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- Check slope on the City's environmental development permit mapping, which also maps unstable slopes and the Interface Wildfire Risk Management boundary for the City Centre and Northeast areas; the page shows the layers, not a lot-level geotechnical conclusion. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- Check the Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 section 519 flood and slope siting rules and Schedule G, the designated 200-year floodplain of the Brunette, Coquitlam, Fraser and Pitt rivers, even though this pocket is upland of those rivers. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1170/Part-5-General-Regulations-PDF
