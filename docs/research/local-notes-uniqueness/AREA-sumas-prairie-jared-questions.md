# Sumas Prairie — Questions for Jared

1. When you work a Sumas Prairie farm listing, what do buyers ask about first after the November 2021 and December 2025 floods — the dike, the pump station, or insurance?
2. Who is actually buying on the prairie now — working farm families expanding, or lifestyle buyers who want the big flat lot?
3. Does being on the Inner Prairie (behind the Sumas Dike) versus the Outer Prairie change how a property sells, in your experience?
4. What surprises Prairie buyers most after they move in — drainage and dyking charges on the tax bill, farm smells and noise, or something else?
5. Tell one story from a Sumas Prairie deal — a flood question, an ALR ruling, or a farm sale that taught you something about the area.

Shot list:
1. The Sumas Dike and Barrowtown Pump Station from the dike crest, looking across the flat lakebed toward Sumas Mountain — shows the whole flood-protection story in one frame.
2. A straight farm road on the lakebed (Tolmie Road or No. 3 Road) shot low and long, fields on both sides to the horizon — the flatness is the area's signature.
3. Drone shot over the Vedder Canal where it meets the prairie edge, looking west across the fields toward Abbotsford — marks the Abbotsford/Chilliwack split of the prairie.
