## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A second dwelling in the ALR is size-capped, not discretionary: the Agricultural Land Commission (ALC = Agricultural Land Commission) referral letter states residences must comply with 500 square metres (m²) for a principal residence and 90 m² for an additional residence, and non-compliant residences require Commission approval by a non-adhering residential use application. https://webfiles.islandstrust.bc.ca/islands/local-trust-areas/south-pender/current-projects/Minor%20LUB%20Amendments%20Project/8%20-%20Public%20Hearing%20Binder/05.2_SP-BL-129_Bylaw%20referral_Responses.pdf
- Some ALR subdivision steps sit with the Approving Officer, not the Commission: Delta's and Langley's reports both work from the rule that ALC approval is required except in prescribed circumstances; the 753 – 264 Street file shows a consolidation the Approving Officer could consider while the road dedication still needed the ALC. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=26B98BED-94CB-48F1-8BA9-7AC2502E0AA4&folderId=89097C230327150547589611
- Soil and fill are regulated in the ALR, not free: the ALC letter notes Section 35 of the ALR Use Regulation permits certain fill for farm use and for construction of a farm building and a principal residence, and fill is not outright permitted beyond that. https://webfiles.islandstrust.bc.ca/islands/local-trust-areas/south-pender/current-projects/Minor%20LUB%20Amendments%20Project/8%20-%20Public%20Hearing%20Binder/05.2_SP-BL-129_Bylaw%20referral_Responses.pdf
- Local bylaws must match provincial farm law: Section 46 of the Agricultural Land Commission Act requires local governments to ensure their bylaws are consistent with the Act, the ALR regulations and Commission orders. https://webfiles.islandstrust.bc.ca/islands/local-trust-areas/south-pender/current-projects/Minor%20LUB%20Amendments%20Project/8%20-%20Public%20Hearing%20Binder/05.2_SP-BL-129_Bylaw%20referral_Responses.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** I need a house for a farm worker or parent — what fits without an application?
**A:** One additional residence of 90 m² or less, if the principal residence is within its 500 m² cap and local zoning allows it. Anything larger is a non-adhering residential use application to the ALC, filed through the ALC Portal via the Township, as the ALC's referral letter sets out. https://webfiles.islandstrust.bc.ca/islands/local-trust-areas/south-pender/current-projects/Minor%20LUB%20Amendments%20Project/8%20-%20Public%20Hearing%20Binder/05.2_SP-BL-129_Bylaw%20referral_Responses.pdf

**Q:** Does ALR land automatically get farm tax class?
**A:** No. BC Assessment states Class 9, Farm requires the land to produce a prescribed amount of qualifying primary agricultural products for sale, and that farm buildings sit in Class 1 — ALR inclusion alone does not classify the land, and a non-producing acreage is taxed as residential land. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/understanding-property-classes-and-exemptions?TermStoreId=2e29bc59-558d-448c-9c19-d11b487dbf89&TermSetId=0a8def97-2239-4f44-b0fc-f2f10a0ea2d4&TermId=582130f6-038f-4842-ada7-0166ce03a246

**Q:** Can I irrigate from the well or creek on the property?
**A:** Only under a water licence for farm use. The Province states a licence is required to divert, store or use surface or groundwater for non-domestic purposes including agriculture, and non-domestic groundwater has required authorization since the Water Sustainability Act took effect February 29, 2016. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights

**Q:** Can I bring in fill to raise a building site?
**A:** Only within the ALR Use Regulation limits. The ALC notes Section 35 permits certain fill for farm use and for construction of a farm building and a principal residence, and warns fill placement or removal in the ALR must comply with the Act, Regulation and Commission resolutions. https://webfiles.islandstrust.bc.ca/islands/local-trust-areas/south-pender/current-projects/Minor%20LUB%20Amendments%20Project/8%20-%20Public%20Hearing%20Binder/05.2_SP-BL-129_Bylaw%20referral_Responses.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Irrigation can run around the clock lawfully: the Province's irrigation factsheet states irrigation equipment may operate 24 hours a day during the crop-growing season. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/farming-natural-resources-and-industry/agriculture-and-seafood/agricultural-land-and-environment/strengthening-farming/farm-practices/2026/irrigation_2026.pdf
- Water applied has a ceiling in practice: the same factsheet states the amount applied should not exceed the climatic moisture deficit or what the soil can store within the crop rooting depth. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/farming-natural-resources-and-industry/agriculture-and-seafood/agricultural-land-and-environment/strengthening-farming/farm-practices/2026/irrigation_2026.pdf
- Intake screens are a legal detail on fish-bearing water: the factsheet states the federal Fisheries Act requires irrigation pumps and intakes on fish-bearing sources to be properly screened. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/farming-natural-resources-and-industry/agriculture-and-seafood/agricultural-land-and-environment/strengthening-farming/farm-practices/2026/irrigation_2026.pdf
- A farmer's dwelling gets a specific tax treatment: BC Assessment notes a farmer's dwelling in a rural area is exempt from provincial general property taxes but subject to school taxes. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/understanding-property-classes-and-exemptions?TermStoreId=2e29bc59-558d-448c-9c19-d11b487dbf89&TermSetId=0a8def97-2239-4f44-b0fc-f2f10a0ea2d4&TermId=582130f6-038f-4842-ada7-0166ce03a246

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The 2025 total tax rate a Langley buyer can verify is 3.34910 per $1,000 for Class 1 Residential, with the municipal portion at 1.87580, per the Township 2025 tax rates sheet. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/property-taxes/document-feed/2025-Tax-Rates.pdf
- Class 9 land gets a school-tax break: 50 per cent of the assessed value of a Class 9 parcel is exempt from school taxes, per BC Assessment citing the School Act. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/understanding-property-classes-and-exemptions?TermStoreId=2e29bc59-558d-448c-9c19-d11b487dbf89&TermSetId=0a8def97-2239-4f44-b0fc-f2f10a0ea2d4&TermId=582130f6-038f-4842-ada7-0166ce03a246
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) taxable areas list includes the Township of Langley, so a residential portion on a farm parcel can still require an annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- Water carries an annual rental: the Province states most water users must apply for the right to use water and pay an annual rental fee for that use. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check soil capability on the parcel, not the listing: no Township page read on 2026-11-03 publishes a soil-class map for individual lots, so a buyer's soil claim needs a parcel-level capability lookup — see NOT CONFIRMED.
- Check the water licence's purpose and quantity against the farm plan: licences are purpose-specific under the Water Sustainability Act, and a domestic-only authorization does not cover irrigation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- Check existing fill and soil removal for compliance: the ALC warns uses and fill placement or removal in the ALR must comply with the Act, Regulation and Commission resolutions, so undocumented fill is a title-and-compliance risk. https://webfiles.islandstrust.bc.ca/islands/local-trust-areas/south-pender/current-projects/Minor%20LUB%20Amendments%20Project/8%20-%20Public%20Hearing%20Binder/05.2_SP-BL-129_Bylaw%20referral_Responses.pdf
