# Burnaby Hospital Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: burnaby-hospital (Burnaby Hospital area, Burnaby; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The Burnaby Hospital area is the residential ground around the Burnaby Hospital campus at 3935 Kincaid Street in central Burnaby, south of Canada Way and west of Deer Lake. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- "Burnaby Hospital" is a board sub-area name taken from the hospital, not a City of Burnaby community-plan name; the City publishes no street-by-street boundary for it. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- Burnaby City Hall at 4949 Canada Way and Deer Lake Park at 6450 Deer Lake Avenue sit just east of the area, and Cascade Heights is the neighbourhood at its south side. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- Shared City of Burnaby facts — zoning, tax, utility and plan rules that apply city-wide — are in full in the brentwood-homes-for-sale note; this note covers only the hospital-area angle. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The housing around the campus is mostly detached houses on the Kincaid Street and Canada Way side streets, and no City or BC Assessment public page read for this note names a residential condo or townhome complex inside the sub-area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- The dominant building in the area is the hospital itself: a new six-storey health care pavilion with 83 beds, mostly in single-patient rooms, opened on the campus in 2026. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026INF0014-000256.htm
- No official source read states typical lot sizes, build decades or house styles for these streets, so none is claimed. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City-wide Burnaby rules are in the brentwood-homes-for-sale note; the area-specific rule here is plan-level: the Cascade Heights Community Plan, for the neighbourhood at this sub-area's south side, was officially adopted by the City in March 2025. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- The Burnaby 2050 OCP, adopted December 9, 2025, now takes precedence over the Cascade Heights plan's land use map where the two conflict, so a south-side lot's density is read in the 2050 OCP first. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- Hospital construction is a live local condition, not a bylaw: Fraser Health advises that sections of some hospital corridors close temporarily overnight for construction, and its July 2025 notice put single-lane alternating traffic on Kincaid Street with the eastbound bus stop at MacDonald Avenue temporarily relocated. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2025/Jul/Burnaby-Hospital-redevelopment-project-Construction-notice-July-23

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What is Burnaby Hospital, and what care does it provide? **A:** Burnaby Hospital at 3935 Kincaid Street is a large Fraser Health community hospital providing primary and secondary acute care. Fraser Health lists 24/7 emergency care, general and internal medicine, general surgery, orthopedics, gynecology, urology, level one and two obstetrics, neonatal intensive care, tertiary palliative care, adult psychiatry and outpatient clinics among its services. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- **Q:** What opened at the hospital in 2026? **A:** A new six-storey health care pavilion and an expanded support facilities building opened to patients on March 10, 2026. The Province states the pavilion holds 83 beds, primarily in single-patient rooms with ensuite washrooms, and that the expanded support facilities building includes a larger community emergency department adding 23 treatment spaces and a dedicated mental-health zone. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026INF0014-000256.htm
- **Q:** What happens next in the redevelopment? **A:** The Province announced a renewed plan on September 21, 2026, with $210 million for an improved and expanded emergency department and surgical unit going ahead immediately. Expanded cancer care and a new acute-care tower are to follow, building on the March 2026 pavilion opening; the announcement gives no completion date for the tower. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026INF0056-001108.pdf
- **Q:** Which schools serve a hospital-area address? **A:** It depends on the exact address, because School District No. 41 (SD41) catchments are address-based. The SD41 schools in or beside this sub-area on the Province's list are Douglas Road Elementary (K–7) at 4861 Canada Way, Cascade Heights Elementary (K–7) at 4343 Smith Avenue, and Burnaby Central Secondary (8–12) at 6011 Deer Lake Parkway; confirm any address in the SD41 locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** Is there a SkyTrain station in this area? **A:** No SkyTrain station sits inside the Burnaby Hospital sub-area. Fraser Health directs bus travellers to use the West Wing Entrance and notes redevelopment work affects travel to the campus; the nearest Millennium Line station on TransLink's station mapping is Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station, north-east across the Deer Lake side, and any walking time from a specific house is UNCONFIRMED. https://alz.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/skytrain-accessible-entrance-maps/sperling_burnaby_lake_station_elevator_map.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Hospital entrances changed in 2026: between 6:00 a.m. and 10:00 p.m. visitors use the West Wing Entrance or the new East and South Entrances, and bus travellers are directed to the West Wing Entrance. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- After hours, between 10:00 p.m. and 6:00 a.m., entry is through the Emergency entrance, with parking in the Emergency Parking Lot, which holds five accessible stalls available 24/7. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- Deer Lake Park, 6450 Deer Lake Avenue, is the area's large park, with walking trails connecting the lake to the Burnaby Art Gallery, Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, Burnaby Village Museum and Hart House restaurant. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- Part of the Deer Lake boardwalk by the boat launch is closed from September 21, 2026 for a full replacement, with completion scheduled by winter 2027 and a signed detour via Sperling Avenue and Deer Lake Drive. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- Wesburn Community Centre, 4781 Parkwood Avenue in Wesburn Park south of the area, has a meeting and activity room for 100 people, a spray park, a baseball diamond and a basketball court. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/wesburn-community-centre
- The Burnaby Public Library branch for this quadrant of the city is the Bob Prittie Metrotown Branch, south-west at Metrotown; the library runs four branches, one in each quadrant. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Burnaby Hospital Redevelopment Phase 1, 3935 Kincaid Street, Fraser Health and the Province of BC, hospital campus: a new six-storey health care pavilion with 83 beds and an expanded support facilities building with a new energy centre, at an estimated cost of $633 million funded by the Province and the Burnaby Hospital and Community Foundation — STATUS: opened to patients March 10, 2026, with renovations to existing buildings continuing, read 2026-11-03. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026INF0014-000256.htm
- Burnaby Hospital renewed plan (Phase 2), 3935 Kincaid Street, Province of BC and Fraser Health, hospital expansion: $210 million for an improved and expanded emergency department and surgical unit, followed by expanded cancer care and a new acute-care tower — STATUS: announced September 21, 2026 as going ahead immediately for the emergency and surgical work; no tower construction date stated, read 2026-11-03. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026INF0056-001108.pdf
- Brentwood-area projects are in full in the brentwood-homes-for-sale note and are not repeated here. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-town-centre-plan-amendments

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Canada Way is the main east–west road at the area's north edge, Kincaid Street serves the hospital campus, and Deer Lake Parkway and Sperling Avenue link east to Deer Lake Park and City Hall at 4949 Canada Way. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2025/Jul/Burnaby-Hospital-redevelopment-project-Construction-notice-July-23
- Buses serve Kincaid Street at the campus, and Fraser Health directs bus travellers arriving at the hospital to the West Wing Entrance. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- No SkyTrain station is inside the sub-area; the nearest station on TransLink's mapping is Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station on the Millennium Line, and station walking times from specific addresses are UNCONFIRMED. https://alz.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/skytrain-accessible-entrance-maps/sperling_burnaby_lake_station_elevator_map.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check construction exposure on Kincaid Street before buying on the campus edge: Fraser Health's construction notices are the running record of lane closures, paving and bus stop moves during the redevelopment. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2025/Jul/Burnaby-Hospital-redevelopment-project-Construction-notice-July-23
- Check the Burnaby 2050 OCP map and the March 2025 Cascade Heights Community Plan together for a south-side lot, because the 2050 OCP governs where the maps conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- No area-wide hazard designation for these residential streets was confirmed in the sources read; flood, slope and Agricultural Land Reserve status are parcel-level checks, with ALR status checked in the Agricultural Land Commission's map finder. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
