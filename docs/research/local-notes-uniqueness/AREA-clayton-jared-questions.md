# Clayton — Questions for Jared
Area: Clayton / Clayton Heights (Cloverdale, Surrey). First program page: clayton-cloverdale-houses-for-sale.

## Questions (voice memo)
1. Clayton has been building out for years — what do buyers get wrong about the newer east side versus the older Clayton Heights streets?
2. In your deals, what matters more to Clayton buyers: Clayton Heights Secondary catchment, the Fraser Highway commute, or lot size?
3. What surprises buyers after they move in — parking on the narrow streets, strata-like density on detached streets, construction still going on around them?
4. Who is buying in Clayton now, and what are they moving from?
5. Tell me about one Clayton deal that shows how the area has changed.
