# New Westminster — Questions for Jared
Area: New Westminster (city-wide). First program page: new-westminster-condos-for-sale.

## Questions (voice memo)
1. Across your New West deals, which neighbourhood's condos move fastest — Downtown, the Quay, Sapperton, Uptown — and why?
2. What do buyers get wrong about New Westminster strata — older building condition, rental rules, or the hill?
3. How much does SkyTrain access actually drive New West condo and townhome deals compared with price per square foot?
4. Who is buying in New West now, and what are they leaving behind — Burnaby towers, Surrey, Vancouver?
5. Tell me about one New Westminster deal that taught you something about the city.
