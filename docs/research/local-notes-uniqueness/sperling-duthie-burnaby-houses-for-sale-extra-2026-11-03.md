# Sperling-Duthie Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: sperling-duthie-burnaby-houses-for-sale (Sperling-Duthie; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sperling-Duthie is the central-Burnaby house area around Sperling Avenue and Duthie Avenue, west and north of Deer Lake and crossed by Canada Way; the full shared area facts are in the sperling-duthie-burnaby note. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- Sperling Avenue is the spine: it runs from Lougheed Highway and the SkyTrain station at its north end down to Deer Lake Park at its south end. http://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/skytrain_station_maps/sperling_burnaby_lake_station.pdf
- Buckingham Heights, the slope east of Deer Lake, sits within and beside the sub-area's south-east side, and Burnaby Lake Regional Park lies immediately north of Lougheed Highway. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/burnaby-lake-regional-park

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is almost entirely detached houses: post-war bungalows on the west side, and large custom and estate homes in the Buckingham Heights pocket on the slope east of Deer Lake. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- Detached lots here fall under the City's R1 small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) district, so a house lot can be redeveloped as up to 3 to 6 dwelling units depending on lot area and proximity to a frequent transit network bus stop. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Burnaby's new Zoning Bylaw 2026 was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026, replacing the former Zoning Bylaw 1965, and the R1 SSMUH district dates to the June 2024 rezoning of all R district lots. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- Burnaby 2050, the City's official community plan, was adopted December 9, 2025 and sets the land-use direction for the whole city including this area. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- Woodward House, at 5195 Sperling Avenue and built in 1912, is on the City's heritage register, so heritage rules can touch a purchase on that street. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/
- Burnaby is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve Sperling Avenue addresses? **A:** École Sperling Elementary, at 2200 Sperling Avenue, is the School District No. 41 (SD41) elementary school on the spine, and the SD41 locator result recorded for 2200 Sperling Avenue is Sperling Elementary for Kindergarten to Grade 7 and Burnaby North Secondary for Grades 8 to 12, not Burnaby Central Secondary; confirm the assigned schools for a specific address before relying on them. https://burnabyschools.ca/elementary-school-addresses/
- **Q:** Is the Deer Lake boardwalk open? **A:** Not the boat-launch section: the boardwalk section by the boat launch in Deer Lake Park has been closed from September 21, 2026 for full replacement, with completion scheduled for winter 2027 and a detour via Sperling Avenue and Deer Lake Drive. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- **Q:** Can I build a multiplex on a Sperling-Duthie house lot? **A:** Possibly, under the R1 SSMUH district: newly rezoned R1 lots are eligible for up to 3 to 6 dwelling units depending on lot area and proximity to a frequent transit network bus stop, and the count is inclusive of any secondary suites. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q:** What is happening at Burnaby Hospital? **A:** Burnaby Hospital, at 3935 Kincaid Street, is being redeveloped in phases: Phase 1 is a six-storey 83-bed pavilion, and Phase 2 is a 160-bed acute care tower with a cancer centre, with Phase 2 dates still to be determined. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/capital-projects/projects/burnaby-hospital/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby's 2026 residential tax rate is 3.28373 per 1,000 dollars of assessed value, and the average residential assessment in Burnaby decreased by 5.29 percent in 2026, which does not necessarily mean an individual bill falls, because taxes depend on the change in a property's assessment relative to the class average. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates
- Property taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after the July due date and a further 5 percent after the September due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes
- Assessment notices arrive from BC Assessment each January, and the assessed value on the notice is the value the mill rate is applied to. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates
