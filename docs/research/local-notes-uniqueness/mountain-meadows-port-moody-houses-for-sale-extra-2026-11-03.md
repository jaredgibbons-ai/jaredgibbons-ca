# Mountain Meadows Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: mountain-meadows-port-moody-houses-for-sale (Mountain Meadows; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Mountain Meadows is a north-slope Port Moody neighbourhood built around one street, Noons Creek Drive: TransLink's route 184 timetable lists its stops along Noons Creek Drive at Falcon Drive, Thurston Terrace, Maude Court, Barberry Place, David Avenue, Shale Court and Sandstone Crescent; the full shared area facts are in the mountain-meadows-port-moody note. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt184.pdf
- The City plans it as part of a five-neighbourhood group, not on its own: Chapter 14 of Port Moody 2050, the Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw, 2026, No. 3522, groups Heritage Mountain, Twin Creeks, Noons Creek, Mountain Meadows and Heritage Woods in one section. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- David Avenue is the top-edge street: the 184 turns from Noons Creek Drive onto Panorama Drive at the David Avenue end, tying the pocket to the Coquitlam side of the ridge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt184.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is detached houses along Noons Creek Drive and its terraces — Falcon Drive, Thurston Terrace, Maude Court, Barberry Place, Shale Court and Sandstone Crescent — the street set the 184 bus serves. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt184.pdf
- Chapter 14 of Port Moody 2050 sets this group's future vision as small-scale, multi-unit housing (SSMUH) gentle densification along existing lot patterns, so the house lots themselves are the densification surface here. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Port Moody 2050, the City's Official Community Plan, was adopted by Council on April 28, 2026, and its Chapter 14 vision for this five-neighbourhood group is SSMUH gentle densification along existing lot patterns. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050?page=2&tool=story_telling_tool
- Small-scale multi-unit housing is permitted in the RS and RT zones outside the City's transit-oriented areas, under the provincial housing legislation the City has implemented. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- The RS1-S small-lot rezoning route is not offered in Mountain Meadows: the City's list is Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside only. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Port Moody is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** How does the bus serve a one-street neighbourhood? **A:** Route 184 runs the spine: its timetable lists stops along Noons Creek Drive at Falcon Drive, Thurston Terrace, Maude Court, Barberry Place, David Avenue, Shale Court and Sandstone Crescent, then turns onto Panorama Drive at the David Avenue end toward the Coquitlam side of the ridge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt184.pdf
- **Q:** What densification applies to Mountain Meadows houses? **A:** Gentle densification along existing lot patterns: Chapter 14 of Port Moody 2050 gives this five-neighbourhood group — Heritage Mountain, Twin Creeks, Noons Creek, Mountain Meadows and Heritage Woods — a future vision of small-scale, multi-unit housing added along the existing lot pattern, rather than tower or project rezoning. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- **Q:** Which elementary school is named for this side of the city? **A:** Mountain Meadows Elementary is the School District No. 43 (SD43) elementary school carrying this neighbourhood's name, and the recorded locator chain for a central Port Moody address runs Mountain Meadows Elementary, Scott Creek Middle and Gleneagle Secondary; SD43 assigns catchments by address, so confirm the chain for a specific Noons Creek Drive address with the district. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** Is there a small-lot split option here? **A:** No: the RS1-S small-lot rezoning is offered only in Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside, and Mountain Meadows is not on the City's list, so added units here come through small-scale multi-unit housing in the RS zones instead. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
Read: 2026-11-03
