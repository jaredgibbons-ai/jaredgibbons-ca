## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The houses here are governed by the City's RS zones in Zoning Bylaw No. 3630: minimum lot width is 15 metres in RS1, 12 metres in RS2, 30 metres in RS3 and 9.5 metres in RS4, and the zone on the specific lot decides what the lot can hold. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Apartments sit in the RA1 and RA2 zones and townhouses in the RTh1 to RTh3 zones under the same bylaw, so the detached stock is the RS-zone stock by definition. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The City's utility model is unusual in the region: water and sewer are flat annual fees billed separately from property taxes, due March 31, 2026, rather than metered charges on the tax bill. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes
- The City states it has the lowest average property taxes and utility fees in Metro Vancouver, a city-wide claim that applies to this sub-area's bills as well. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes
- The RS zone widths vary widely inside one bylaw — 9.5 metres in RS4 to 30 metres in RS3 — so two Woodland Acres lots can carry very different subdivision potential. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
