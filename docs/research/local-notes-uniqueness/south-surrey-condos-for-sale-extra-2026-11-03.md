- South Surrey's condo stock concentrates in the Semiahmoo Town Centre and the King George Boulevard corridor, not evenly across the peninsula — shared South Surrey facts are spread across earlier notes, starting with the sunnyside-park-south-surrey-condos-townhomes-for-sale note and the king-george-corridor-south-surrey-houses-for-sale note; this note stays condo-specific. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7924-0284-00
- The City's planning report for a Semiahmoo Town Centre site describes the town centre designations at 24 Avenue and 152 Street as Town Centre and Multiple Residential in the Official Community Plan (OCP) and Low-rise Mixed-use and Low-rise Residential in the Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan — the designation pair that governs most apartment sites on this page. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7924-0284-00
- White Rock is a separate municipality inside this page's search area, with its own bylaws; a "South Surrey" condo with a White Rock civic address is not under Surrey's zoning or tax rules. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan was amended in 2026 to align with the Surrey 2050 OCP and with the King George Boulevard Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) terminus at 152 Street and 16 Avenue: Council approved those amendments on July 27, 2026, so older town centre plan excerpts quoted for a condo site may already be superseded. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/council-minutes/regular-council-public-hearing-minutes-july-27-2026
- The City planning report for the 152A Street site records its zoning as Comprehensive Development Zone (CD), which means the permitted uses and densities for that condo site come from its own CD bylaw, not from a standard apartment zone — the same check applies to other CD-zoned buildings on this page. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7924-0284-00
- Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void in every South Surrey strata, because the Province removed strata rental restrictions effective November 24, 2022; a building's older bylaw text does not revive them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- Surrey-wide zoning, suite and tax rules are stated in full in the surrey-condos-for-sale note — see the surrey-condos-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Where in South Surrey are the apartment condos, as opposed to townhomes? **A:** In two clusters: the Semiahmoo Town Centre around 152 Street and 24 Avenue, where the City plan designates Low-rise Residential and Low-rise Mixed-use sites, and the King George Boulevard corridor south of 24 Avenue, where King + Crescent's four six-storey buildings stand. Most of the rest of South Surrey's attached stock is townhome complexes. A unit outside those two clusters that is marketed as an apartment condo should be checked against its strata plan form. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7924-0284-00
- **Q:** Is King + Crescent still a presale? **A:** No. The developer, Zenterra, lists King + Crescent as a completed, sold-out project: four six-storey buildings of 1, 2 and 3-bedroom homes at King George Boulevard in South Surrey, with civic addresses on 146A Street. Purchases there are now resales under the strata's own documents, not developer contracts, so the diligence is the Form B Information Certificate, the depreciation report and the registered bylaws rather than a disclosure statement. https://zenterra.ca/location_project/30-minute-hit/
- **Q:** What changed for Semiahmoo Town Centre condos in 2026? **A:** The plan itself changed. On July 27, 2026 Council approved Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan amendments updating the land use map and road network map to align with the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan and with the King George Boulevard BRT terminus being at 152 Street and 16 Avenue. A condo bought on the strength of the older plan's road network or a different terminus location should be re-checked against the amended plan. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/council-minutes/regular-council-public-hearing-minutes-july-27-2026
- **Q:** Does the hospital in Cloverdale affect a South Surrey condo purchase? **A:** Only at one remove. Peace Arch Hospital at 15521 Russell Avenue in White Rock remains the Fraser Health community hospital serving South Surrey, providing emergency, surgery and maternity care 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The new Surrey hospital is in Cloverdale, covered in full in the cloverdale-condos-for-sale note — see that note — and is not a South Surrey facility. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/white-rock/peace-arch-hospital

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The BRT terminus location moved within the town centre plan: the City's April 2026 report states the originally anticipated terminus near 16 Avenue and 156 Street informed parts of the plan's road network and land use, and the finalized station is at 16 Avenue and 152 Street, on the north side of 16 Avenue west of 152 Street, with layover for three buses. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2026-R073_2026-05-11.pdf
- The same report directs staff to bring forward land-use changes in the Semiahmoo Town Centre as a result of that terminus — so the plan amendments of July 2026 may not be the last change for buildings near 152 Street and 16 Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2026-R073_2026-05-11.pdf
- The planning report for the 152A Street development names the neighbouring stock a buyer is actually comparing: Peninsula Village across 24 Avenue, zoned C8 and RM15, and Semiahmoo House across 153 Street — context that does not appear in a listing. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7924-0284-00
- King George Boulevard BRT is a design-stage project, not a funded line: the City's September 2025 report advanced it from 10 percent to 30 percent design, evaluating station concepts at Surrey Central, Newton Town Centre and Semiahmoo Town Centre. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R204.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey's 2026 residential tax rate, utility charges and suite levies are city-wide and are stated in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-condos-for-sale note for the condo application. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies to South Surrey condos because the Province lists the City of Surrey as a designated taxable area in the Metro Vancouver Regional District; a White Rock-address unit is separately listed under the City of White Rock on the same provincial page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The Highway 99 corridor buses are the rapid link for South Surrey condos: TransLink route 351 runs White Rock Centre to Bridgeport Station, and only the 351 stops at Highway 10 and 96 Street for pick-up and drop-off on the Bridgeport-bound trip. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt354.pdf
- TransLink route 354 runs from White Rock Centre via 152 Street, Johnston Road, 16 Avenue, King George Boulevard, 24 Avenue and 160 Street to South Surrey Park and Ride, then via Highway 99 to Bridgeport Station — the route that passes the Semiahmoo Town Centre condo cluster. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt354.pdf
- TransLink route 352 runs from White Rock Centre via 152 Street, 16 Avenue, 128 Street and Crescent Road, serving the Ocean Park edge of this page, then via South Surrey Park and Ride and Highway 99 to Bridgeport Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt352.pdf
- South Surrey Park and Ride, at King George Boulevard and Highway 99, is the park-and-ride for these routes — the exchange a condo buyer on 152 Street or King George Boulevard will actually use. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt354.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- For a low-lying condo near the Nicomekl River, check the provincial flood work before relying on it: a provincial release describes Nicomekl River dike rehabilitation and raising protecting more than 50 buildings and parts of 48 Avenue and 184 Street, scheduled for completion in summer 2027 — the release does not state the flood position of any specific building. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026EMCR0038-000983.pdf
- For a CD-zoned building, read the site's own Comprehensive Development bylaw rather than a standard zone summary: the 152A Street report shows a Semiahmoo site split-designated Town Centre and Multiple Residential with its permissions in its CD bylaw, and the report does not tell a buyer what an older building's CD bylaw permits today. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7924-0284-00
- For any building near 152 Street and 16 Avenue, check the amended Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan rather than pre-2026 plan excerpts, because the July 27, 2026 amendments changed both the land use map and the road network map around the BRT terminus. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/council-minutes/regular-council-public-hearing-minutes-july-27-2026
