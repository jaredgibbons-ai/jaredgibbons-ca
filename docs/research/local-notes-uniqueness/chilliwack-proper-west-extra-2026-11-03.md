# Chilliwack Proper West Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: chilliwack-proper-west (Chilliwack Proper West; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The City publishes no boundary for this sub-area, so treat any west/east line as orientation only. The covering plan is Schedule B2, the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan, in the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw 2025, No. 5500. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
- The west side is the Young Road, Corbould Street and airport-side part of Chilliwack Proper, west of the downtown core. City facility addresses anchor it: Chilliwack Landing Leisure Centre, Unit #1, 9145 Corbould St, and Chilliwack Airport, 46244 Airport Road. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=46 https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=34
- Downtown itself is a separate plan area, Schedule B3, the Downtown Neighbourhood Plan, first adopted June 2010 and updated 2025; shared downtown facts are in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note — see that note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3556

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The base housing form here is one- and two-family homes, with attached housing and narrow-lot infill in parts. A City public hearing notice for a rezoning inside the plan area names the designations "Residential 1 – One and Two Family Housing" and "Residential 2 – Attached Housing and Narrow Lot Infill." https://www.chilliwack.com/main/notices/RZ1394 - 9388 & 9374 Ashwell Road.pdf
- The Mountain View area holds early-20th-century and mid-century houses in Edwardian, Edwardian Foursquare, Colonial Revival, Craftsman, French Storybook and Minimal Traditional styles, per the City's heritage conservation area schedule. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8127
- The plan's stated planning issue is residential infill in an established area, not greenfield subdivision; the 2018 notice adding the plan said it guides decision-making on residential infill development. https://gov.chilliwack.bc.ca/main/notices/OCP00033.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A Heritage Alteration Permit (HAP) may be required before you alter, demolish or subdivide in the Mountain View Heritage Conservation Area. The City's HAP guide says an owner needs a HAP before subdivision, construction or alteration if the property is heritage-designated and/or inside a heritage conservation area. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3279/Guide%5Fto%5FHAPs%5F%2D%5F2026%5F03%5F02%2Epdf
- Within that area, category decides the permit trigger: for minor additions or alterations only Heritage Protected and Heritage Interest properties need a HAP, while demolition, new construction and subdivision need one in every category. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3232/2025%2D07%2D17%5F%2D%5FFAQ%5F%2D%5FMtn%5FView%5FHCA%2Epdf
- Downtown development is also form-and-character controlled under Development Permit Area D-6, Downtown, in OCP Schedule D-6. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8154
- Habitable floors must sit at or above the Flood Construction Level plus 0.6 m freeboard in dike-protected areas, under Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is downtown inside Chilliwack Proper West? **A:** Downtown is planned separately, even though it sits beside the west-side grid. The 2050 OCP gives downtown its own Schedule B3 Downtown Neighbourhood Plan and gives Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Schedule B2, so a downtown lot and a Corbould Street lot are read under different neighbourhood plans, with downtown form-and-character rules added under Development Permit Area D-6. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
- **Q:** Can I renovate a Mountain View house freely? **A:** Not always — check the heritage category first. Mountain View is a designated Heritage Conservation Area under OCP Schedule C, facilitated under sections 614 and 615 of the Local Government Act. Demolition, new construction and subdivision need a Heritage Alteration Permit for every property category, and minor alterations need one for Heritage Protected and Heritage Interest properties. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8127
- **Q:** Which elementary schools are physically on the west side? **A:** Four elementary schools are addressed on the west side in the SD33 directory. They are Bernard Elementary, 45465 Bernard Ave; Central Elementary Community School, 9435 Young Rd; McCammon Elementary, 9601 Hamilton St; and Leary Integrated Arts & Technology Elementary, 9320 Walden St, all Kindergarten to Grade 5. Catchment for a specific address still needs the district's school locator. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2025-01/Chilliwack%20School%20District%20Directory%202024-25.pdf
- **Q:** Is this area behind a dike? **A:** Yes, the valley-floor part is dike-protected, and the City is raising the system. The City's floodplain FAQ describes an ongoing $30M program to raise the dyke system to the current provincial elevation and states the Young Road dyke has been upgraded; flood response phases are keyed to the Mission gauge. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=4330
- **Q:** Will I hear the airport? **A:** The airport is inside this side of the city, so check a lot's position against Airport Road. The City's airport page describes a 130-acre airport at 46244 Airport Road with a 1,219 m paved, lit runway, a parallel taxiway, and accommodation for 75 private and commercial aircraft including helicopters. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=34

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Hope River runs along Corbould Street here, which is why the flood check is street-specific. The City lists Hope River Corbould Park at 9899 Corbould St in its parks listing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=61
- Evergreen Hall, 9291 Corbould St, is a City hall with the Cheam, Slesse and Minto rooms, booked through Recreation and Culture. http://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=218
- The Landing site also carries the skateboard park and pump track beside the leisure centre pools, per the City recreation page. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=46
- There is no Fraser Valley Regional Library branch on the west side; the Chilliwack Library is downtown at 45860 First Avenue. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/chilliwack-library
- Kwíyeqel Secondary, 8855 Elm Dr, Grades 9–12, is the secondary school addressed on the west side in the SD33 directory. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2025-01/Chilliwack%20School%20District%20Directory%202024-25.pdf

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Main Street Improvements are the active City project touching this area, with completion expected summer 2027. The City project page states work began in October, replacing water, sanitary and storm mains along Yale Road and Main Street from Alexander Avenue to Wellington Avenue, then rebuilding Main Street from Yale Road to Kipp Avenue with a stamped concrete boulevard, street trees, ornamental lights and a rebuilt signal at Spadina Avenue and Main Street. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3274
- District 1881 is the completed multi-phase downtown redevelopment beside this area. The Chilliwack Economic Partners Corporation (CEPCO) downtown page states the City and CEPCO assembled the site bounded by Young Road, Yale Road, Princess Avenue and Empress Lane, Council awarded the 3.75-acre redevelopment to Algra Bros. Developments Ltd., and the project combines retail, commercial, residential and mixed-use development. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/downtown/

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 52 Evans is the west side's main local bus, running from the Downtown Exchange through the Young Road south and Evans Road corridor past the Landing Leisure Centre, per the BC Transit Chilliwack route maps. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- Route 57 Broadway serves the airport side, with timing points at Yale/Airport and Young Road on its run from the Downtown Exchange. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- Route 59 Industrial is peak-period only, with two weekday morning and two weekday afternoon trips between Downtown Chilliwack and Cottonwood Mall via the Lickman Park & Ride. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=59

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check floodplain status per address, not by neighbourhood name. The City directs buyers to its floodplain maps, and Bylaw No. 4519 sets the Flood Construction Level plus 0.6 m freeboard in standard-dike areas, with a waiver path only by covenant supported by a qualified professional's report. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- Check heritage category before pricing a renovation, because the HCA FAQ states the permit trigger changes by category and by whether the work is minor or major. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3232/2025%2D07%2D17%5F%2D%5FFAQ%5F%2D%5FMtn%5FView%5FHCA%2Epdf
- The flood FAQ does not set a lot's elevation; it states response phases keyed to the Mission gauge, with bankfull about 5.5 m and minor flooding at 6.0 m in unprotected areas. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=4330
