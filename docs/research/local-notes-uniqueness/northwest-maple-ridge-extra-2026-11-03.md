# Northwest Maple Ridge Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: northwest-maple-ridge (Northwest Maple Ridge; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Northwest Maple Ridge is the west-side area of the City of Maple Ridge, framed in the live program by Dewdney Trunk Road, Abernethy Way, 203 Street, 210 Street and Old Dewdney Trunk Road. https://jaredgibbons.ca/northwest-maple-ridge.html
- No street-level board boundary is published, so that corridor framing is a working scope, not an official boundary. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan
- Hammond adjoins it to the south-west and has its own area plan, updated in the same November 25, 2025 OCP package. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-ocp-and-area-plan-updates-increase-housing-and-align-legislation
- The Hammond plan states Hammond sits in the south-west corner of Maple Ridge, bounded west by Pitt Meadows and the Katzie First Nation and south by the Fraser River — the same river edge that shapes this area's south side. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026-06-25-Hammond-Area-Plan_0.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Hammond Area Plan directs growth around Maple Meadows Station and allows 8–12 storey mixed-use near transit while protecting employment lands — the rule set on this area's south-west edge. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-ocp-and-area-plan-updates-increase-housing-and-align-legislation
- Wharf Street in Lower Hammond serves as both road and dike, and homes on the Fraser River side of it do not get the dike's flood protection — a title-and-survey check for any river-edge lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026-06-25-Hammond-Area-Plan_0.pdf
- Maple Ridge Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 8121-2026 designates flood hazard areas and connected riverine hazard areas, and bars new buildings outside dike protection in a connected riverine area unless a site-specific exemption is granted. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
- A secondary suite is not permitted on a floodplain property unless the underside of the suite's finished floor system is above the flood construction level. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2023-11-import/Sec%20Suite%20Guide%20New_202103081701305643.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary schools serve the west side? **A:** Two Maple Ridge schools are confirmed by name and address, plus one across the city line. Fairview Elementary is at 12209 206 Street and Laity View Elementary at 21023 123 Avenue in Maple Ridge, while Davie Jones Elementary at 12030 Blakely Road sits in Pitt Meadows; SD42 catchments are address-based, so run the exact address in the district locator before relying on any one school. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves Northwest Maple Ridge? **A:** Westview Secondary is the west-side secondary named in district records. It sits at 20905 Wicklund Avenue in Maple Ridge, and SD42 runs secondary as Grades 8 to 12 with no middle tier; as with elementary schools, the catchment is decided by the exact home address in the SD42 locator, not by the neighbourhood name alone. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q:** How does the Golden Ears Bridge route work from here? **A:** The west-side roads feed the bridge corridor through Pitt Meadows and the Lougheed corridor. Maple Meadows Station on the West Coast Express and Haney Place Exchange with the R3 RapidBus are the transit anchors for this side of the city, and TransLink's Langley–Haney Place bus rapid transit line is in detailed planning between Willowbrook and Haney Place. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- **Q:** Is the land north of Abernethy Way protected farmland? **A:** Much of the northern framing is ALR acreage, but the exact line is parcel-specific. The confirmed facts are the bylaw minimums — A-1 at 2.0 hectares, A-2 at 4.0 hectares and A-3 at 8.0 hectares — and the program could not confirm the live page's sharp Urban Area Boundary dividing line on a City document, so check the individual parcel's zone and ALR status. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- One named elementary school for this area is in a different municipality: Davie Jones Elementary is in Pitt Meadows, not Maple Ridge, even though SD42 serves both cities. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- The Hammond plan records that Lower Hammond has fewer mature trees than Upper Hammond, which it links to lower elevation and drainage — a visible clue to which lots sit low. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026-06-25-Hammond-Area-Plan_0.pdf
- SD42 does not list Hammond-area catchments among its regular school-bus catchments, so most west-side students are not on a district bus route. https://www.sd42.ca/student-transportation/
- The City's 2026 capital program includes rail safety upgrades at the 203 Street/Maple Crescent and Port Haney crossings on this side of the city. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-moves-key-infrastructure-projects-forward-2026-2030-capital-plan

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- A new Protective Services Building in West Maple Ridge is starting construction under the adopted 2026–2030 Capital Plan. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-moves-key-infrastructure-projects-forward-2026-2030-capital-plan
- The Hammond Aquatics and Recreation Centre and a 40-acre multi-use community park at the current Maple Ridge Golf Course site are planned on this side of the city, with park construction estimated to begin in 2027 and the aquatics centre in 2029 — see the southwest-maple-ridge note for the Hammond project detail. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-shares-designs-and-funding-strategy-three-new-recreation-facilities
- Abernethy Way is being extended from 232 Street to 240 Street, east of this area, to improve east–west flow and prepare for future bus rapid transit on Lougheed Highway. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-moves-key-infrastructure-projects-forward-2026-2030-capital-plan

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The area-specific cost split is municipal: a Pitt Meadows address and a Maple Ridge address on adjoining streets carry different city tax notices, even inside one school district. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- Maple Ridge metered water and sewer customers moved to seasonal metered rates on January 1, 2026, with the former sewer parcel tax folded into the sewer rate. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/changes-coming-metered-water-and-sewer-fees-maple-ridge

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Maple Meadows Station is the west-side West Coast Express station, on the weekday Waterfront to Mission City commuter line. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Haney Place Exchange carries the R3 Lougheed Highway RapidBus to Coquitlam Central Station. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/bus-rapid-transit
- The Pitt Meadows/Maple Ridge Diking District's east edge is a rise of land in Maple Ridge at approximately 201 Street and the Canadian Pacific Railway alignment — the same corridor the west-side roads and rail crossings use. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the dike question: the diking district covers 410 hectares in Maple Ridge, bounded by the Fraser, Pitt and Alouette River dikes, and a lot's position relative to the dike decides its protection. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- Then check the floodplain bylaw map: land less than 1.5 metres above the natural boundary of an adjacent watercourse is designated floodplain under Bylaw No. 8121-2026. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
- On the Port Haney river edge, the program's verified flood construction level is 6.7 metres geodetic including freeboard, with a covenant on title and no habitable floor space below that level on affected lots. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
