# Ladner Acreages — Extra Local Notes
Page: ladner-acreages-for-sale (Ladner, Delta; acreages).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The acreage ground is the farm fabric south and west of Ladner village: parcels along Ladner Trunk Road, 64 Street and the roads toward Westham Island, in the City of Delta. Delta-wide Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) and zoning rules are in full in the ladner-farms note; this note is the buyer angle only. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The board sub-areas here are Ladner Rural and East Delta; Westham Island is reached only by the Westham Island Bridge over Canoe Pass, off Westham Island Road. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-farms.html

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Parcels are fee-simple farm lots under the A1 Agriculture zone, not lots in a named subdivision; no new named acreage subdivision was verified here, matching the earlier acreages program. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- A1 allows one farm house per lot, maximum 500 square metres (m²), on a farm home plate capped at 3,600 m², or 5,000 m² where an additional farm house also sits on the plate. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- Water for fields comes from the City ditch system in summer irrigation mode; homes not on community sewer use an onsite system under the Sewerage System Regulation, per Fraser Health. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Subdivision in A1 needs a minimum new lot of 8 hectares (ha), Official Community Plan (OCP) compliance and, in the ALR, Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) approval, apart from the ALC home site severance exception. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- A second dwelling is one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot: 90 m² maximum on lots of 40 ha or less, 186 m² over 40 ha, on the same farm home plate; more needs ALC approval. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- Non-domestic groundwater, including irrigation from a well, needs a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act before lawful use; domestic use does not. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Can I build a second full house for family on a Ladner acreage? **A:** No, not as of right. Delta's A1 zone allows one accessory farm dwelling unit, capped at 90 m² on lots up to 40 ha and 186 m² above that, on the same farm home plate as the farm house. A separate additional farm house needs a Professional Agrologist's necessity report and written ALC approval. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf

**Q:** Why does my farmhouse plan need a flood level in Ladner? **A:** Ladner acreages sit in the diked Fraser River and Boundary Bay floodplain. The City runs over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, but the Flood Construction Level for a house is set lot by lot, so a new farmhouse is built to that lot's level, often on fill or a raised foundation, not to a neighbourhood-wide figure. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage

**Q:** Can I split a 20-acre parcel into two acreages? **A:** Only if each new lot still meets the A1 minimum of 8 ha, the OCP, and ALC approval where the land is in the ALR. Twenty acres is about 8.09 ha, so a two-lot split of that size fails the 8 ha-per-lot test on its face. Get the ALC map check and the City subdivision answer before pricing any split. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf

**Q:** Do I farm it myself to keep the low farm tax? **A:** No. Farm classification is earned on income, in three tiers, and leased land can qualify where the owner applies, a lease is in place, the leased land contributes to the farm operation, and the lessee-farmer meets the income test. Applications are due by October 31 for the following tax year, so a lease to a Delta farmer is a documented route. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Delta's farm ditches work two ways: drainage mode in winter, irrigation mode in summer, with City control gates regulating water levels for crops. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- The City operates 31 drainage pump stations and about 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure across Delta, including the agricultural area. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Migrant farm worker housing in A1 needs the employing farm to be at least 8 ha and the lot at least 4 ha, with farm classification and written ALC approval; new buildings must be manufactured homes on a temporary foundation. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- Equestrian use in A1 is capped at 40 permanent stalls as an accessory use to farming. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- No soil capability class for any Ladner parcel was confirmed from an official text source in this program, so soil class stays parcel-specific and unconfirmed until an official capability record is read. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta's approved 2026 Financial Plan raised City property taxes 2.9%; taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties after July 2 and after September 2, 2026. The per-$1,000 residential rate is in the City's 2026 Tax Rates Schedule, linked from this page; the figure itself was not read in this pass, so it is not stated here. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- Flat-rate utilities for 2026 total $1,658 for a single-family home, up $51 from 2025; metered water and sewer are billed quarterly. Farm parcels on wells or septic do not carry the same utility bill, so confirm which services the parcel actually has. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Development Cost Charges (DCCs) apply on subdivision or a non-residential building permit under DCC Bylaw No. 7560; the intensive-agriculture rate is charged per square metre of gross floor area, so a large barn or greenhouse carries a DCC cost a house lot does not (check current schedule). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/135287

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check ALR status parcel by parcel on the ALC maps by parcel identifier (PID) or civic address; the ALC states its online tools are for general reference, with official confirmation from its Mapping team. The map does not state a parcel's Flood Construction Level or farm-class record. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- Check title for a flood-proofing Section 219 covenant and ask the City for the lot's Flood Construction Level; neither appears on the ALC map. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Check the well and water licence record before valuing irrigation: an unlicensed non-domestic groundwater use is not a lawful irrigation supply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
