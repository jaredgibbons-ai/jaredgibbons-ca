# North Delta Lots and Land — Extra Local Notes
Page: north-delta-lots-land (North Delta; lots and land).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- North Delta is the City of Delta's northern community, on the south bank of the Fraser River, north of Burns Bog and south of the river, with Scott Road (120 Street) as its eastern edge against Surrey. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Land here trades inside four board sub-areas — Annieville, Nordel, Scottsdale and Sunshine Hills Woods — with River Road and Annieville on the river side, Nordel at the Burns Bog edge, and the Scott Road corridor carrying the growth designations. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Delta's current Official Community Plan (OCP) is Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), one consolidated plan with no area-plan schedules; North Delta's development permit areas (DPA = development permit area) sit in its Appendix B. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- North Delta lots are established fee-simple house lots — teardown, infill and resale parcels on serviced streets — and no named fee-simple subdivision selling serviced vacant lots was verified here. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- The Scott Road corridor is the exception to the house-lot pattern: the OCP's Scott Road Corridor designation supports apartment rezoning of house lots, as application LU009880 shows (lots zoned Single Detached Residential 1 (RS1), Duplex/Single Detached Residential 3 (RD3) and a comprehensive development zone proposed for Apartment Residential 6 (RA6)). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/6717/8051/2986/Second_PIM_notice.pdf
- Nordel holds North Delta's one verified bare-land strata plan, Sunbury Terrace (Plan NWS2806, 12 units, 1989), documented in the nordel lots-and-land program file; a bare-land strata lot is strata tenure on the lot itself, not fee-simple. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/burns-bog-ecological-conservancy-area
- Delta-wide zoning mechanics (zone families, minimum lot sizes, suite and coach-house rules) are covered in full in the ladner-farms note; this note keeps only the North Delta angle.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Three Appendix B permit areas shape North Delta lots: ND1 Fraser River Escarpment (the OCP lists mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence on the river frontage), ND3 Annieville (the River Road village) and ND12 Nordel Social Heart (centred on 84 Avenue and 112 Street). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH = small-scale multi-unit housing) runs under Delta Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024: suites, coach houses and garden suites are permitted in combination where the zoning applies — added units on an existing lot, not a subdivision right. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/
- The west edge is protected, not developable: Burns Bog is held as the Burns Bog Ecological Conservancy Area by Metro Vancouver, and Delta Nature Reserve access is at 10388 Nordel Court. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/delta-nature-reserve
- Subdivision servicing standards are unsettled on this record: the adoption and parcel standards of the proposed Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 are UNCONFIRMED, so confirm the governing standard with the City before pricing a subdivision. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** What makes a Scott Road corridor lot different from an interior North Delta lot?
**A:** Its plan designation, not its house. Corridor lots carry the OCP's Scott Road Corridor designation, and the City is processing apartment rezonings of RS1 and RD3 house lots there — application LU009880 would rezone corridor lots to RA6 for two six-storey buildings. An interior lot has no such designation and prices as a house lot. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/6717/8051/2986/Second_PIM_notice.pdf

**Q:** Does the Burns Bog edge help or hurt a Nordel lot?
**A:** Both, and neither is expansion value. The Bog is a protected Ecological Conservancy Area under Metro Vancouver, so a west-edge lot backs onto land that will not be developed — but the same edge means checking permit and environmental conditions early, and no part of the conservancy is buildable lot area. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/burns-bog-ecological-conservancy-area

**Q:** Can I subdivide a large North Delta lot?
**A:** Only against the parcel's zone minimums and the servicing standard the City confirms for the file. The adoption status of the Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 is UNCONFIRMED in this program, so get the City's governing standard in writing before counting a second lot in your price. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/

**Q:** Can I add a suite or coach house instead of subdividing?
**A:** That is the route Delta actually opened. Under SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395 (adopted June 24, 2024), secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites are permitted in combination on single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies. Confirm the parcel's zone first; the added-unit right does not split the title. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/

**Q:** Is my North Delta lot in a development permit area?
**A:** Check Appendix B of OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024): ND1 Fraser River Escarpment on the river frontage, ND3 Annieville, and ND12 Nordel Social Heart around 84 Avenue and 112 Street. A lot inside one carries design and hazard review an interior lot outside them does not. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Delta's 2026 flat-rate utility fee is $1,658 for a single-family home, covering garbage and recycling and, in the City's own words, water and sewer "where applicable" — so servicing on a specific vacant parcel is a City confirmation, not an assumption. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- The former North Delta Area Plan no longer exists as a schedule: it was Schedule C.1 under the repealed OCP Bylaw No. 3950, and Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) has no lettered schedules — a package citing one is citing a repealed plan. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Delta's zoning bylaw number is contested on City sources (pages cite No. 7600, 2017; an internal title reads "8400, 2025," which is the OCP's number) — take the parcel's zone from the City's zoning maps for the address, never from a bylaw number quoted in a listing. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- School District No. 37 (SD37) catchments are address-based across Annieville, Nordel, Scottsdale and Sunshine Hills Woods, and no locator run for a North Delta address is on record: run the exact address before marketing a school. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Tree removal on a Delta lot is governed by the Delta Tree Protection and Regulation Bylaw No. 7969, 2021, including replacement planting or cash in lieu — see the ladner-farms note for the Delta-wide treatment.
Read: 2026-11-03

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta's 2026 Financial Plan set a 2.9% property tax increase for City operational services, and 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties after July 2 and after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- The 2026 flat-rate utility fee is $1,658 for a single-family home; Delta bills flat-rate utilities in late February and metered homes quarterly. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Highway 91 and the Alex Fraser Bridge are North Delta's freeway link to Richmond and Vancouver, and Highway 99 runs along its south side past Burns Bog; Scott Road (120 Street) is the Surrey boundary arterial and the corridor the OCP designates for growth. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- The ND1 Fraser River Escarpment permit area is the named hazard frame for river-side lots: the OCP lists mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence there, and the permit-area map shows where the area applies — it does not certify any one lot safe, which needs a parcel-level geotechnical answer. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Burns Bog edge lots sit beside a protected peat bog conservancy: check development permit and environmental conditions early, because setback or covenant conditions surface late in deals. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/burns-bog-ecological-conservancy-area
- Whether any specific North Delta parcel is in the Agricultural Land Reserve is a parcel check on the Agricultural Land Commission maps by PID or civic address; no North Delta ALR split is published. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
