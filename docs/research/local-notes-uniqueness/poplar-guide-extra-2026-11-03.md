# Poplar Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: poplar-guide (Poplar, City of Abbotsford; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Poplar is south Abbotsford around Huntingdon, King and Gladwin Roads, south of Highway 1; City profile name: "South Poplar" (https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/2014%20Bradner-Mt.Lehman%20Demographic%20Profile_spreads.pdf).
- South Poplar Traditional Elementary sits at Gladwin and Huntingdon Roads (https://southpoplar.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/aboutus/southpoplar).
- The 1933 school was named for poplar trees planted by Will Hill-Tout (https://southpoplar.abbyschools.ca/school-name-history-26).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is South Poplar Traditional a catchment or choice school? **A:** It is a choice program with a kindergarten lottery. District 34's procedure states the Traditional lottery covers King and South Poplar, applications run from the fourth Monday to the fourth Friday of January, seats are assigned in February by random selection proportional by catchment area, and siblings get priority (https://uppersumas.abbyschools.ca/administrative-procedures/AP336).
- **Q:** What happens after South Poplar Traditional Elementary? **A:** Students from the two feeder schools, King Traditional and South Poplar Traditional, are automatically registered at Abbotsford Traditional School, and most of its Grade 6 students come from those schools. It is one combined Grades 6–12 school at 2272 Windsor Street, formed in September 2023 (https://ats.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/registration/atms) (https://ats.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/about/ats).
- **Q:** Can a family outside Poplar apply? **A:** Yes. Abbotsford Traditional School describes itself as a non-catchment school of choice, and states any child in the district may apply to a Traditional school, space permitting, from any catchment area. Regular catchments, by contrast, are set by home address through the district locator (https://ats.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/about/ats).
- **Q:** How old is the South Poplar school? **A:** The district history states it opened in 1933, a new building went up the hill in the early 1950s, and in fall 2003 it became South Poplar Traditional Elementary, after enrolment fell to ninety-two students; by 2005 portables were needed for over two hundred sixty students (https://southpoplar.abbyschools.ca/school-name-history-26).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- South Poplar Traditional is Kindergarten to Grade 5, provincial curriculum (https://southpoplar.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/aboutus/southpoplar).
- Abbotsford Traditional School is a closed campus at lunch (https://ats.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/about/ats).
- The old school building was donated in 2012 to the museum society (https://southpoplar.abbyschools.ca/school-name-history-26).
- Only two Traditional elementary schools: King and South Poplar (https://kingtraditional.abbyschools.ca/parentsstudents/whatistraditional/schools/kingtraditional).
- A student planner is used daily in Grades 1–5 (https://southpoplar.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/aboutus/southpoplar).
