# Pitt Meadows — Questions for Jared
Area: Pitt Meadows, City of Pitt Meadows. Five questions only Jared can answer from his own deals, plus a photo shot list. Do not answer these from research.

## Questions for Jared
1. Across the whole city, what is the one thing out-of-town buyers most often get wrong about Pitt Meadows before their first tour — the dikes, the farmland share, or the commute?
2. Who is moving to Pitt Meadows now — Maple Ridge and Coquitlam buyers trading across, downsizers staying in the city, or farm families — and which group is growing?
3. City-wide, what sells fast and what sits — Harris Road condos, Osprey Village townhomes, or acreages — and what is the pattern you see deal after deal?
4. What surprises buyers after they move in — living behind dikes, CPKC train frequency at Harris Road, airport activity from Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK), or farm traffic in season?
5. Tell one Pitt Meadows deal story from any sub-area that captures how this city actually trades.

## Shot list
1. Drone shot from the Fraser River dike at Shoreline Park, 51 Bonson Road, looking north over Osprey Village rooftops to the Golden Ears mountains — the city's river-to-mountain frame.
2. Pitt Meadows Station on Harris Road at a weekday morning West Coast Express departure, platform level, showing the commuter core buyers are paying for.
3. Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK), 18799 Airport Way, from Airport Way with runways and the control tower in frame and Harris Road farmland behind — the asset no other Fraser Valley city page can show.
