1. When buyers look at Dewdney or Deroche, do they split cleanly into pad-rent park buyers on Lougheed Highway and fee-simple acreage buyers — or do the same buyers cross-shop both?
2. What breaks deals in this corridor — financing on manufactured homes, well or septic results, flood insurance on the lowland, or pad-rent increases?
3. Which village draws more of your business, Dewdney around Hawkins-Pickle Road or Deroche around Taylor Road, and why?
4. What surprises buyers here — that Mission bylaws do not apply, that Deroche water is flat-rate while Dewdney water is metered, or the freshet history on Nicomen Island?
5. Tell one Dewdney or Deroche deal story: a sale where the FVRD Area G rules or the river changed the outcome.
