# Cedar Hills Surrey Condos and Townhomes — Extra Local Notes
Page: cedar-hills-surrey-condos-townhomes-for-sale (Cedar Hills, Surrey; condos and townhomes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Cedar Hills is a residential pocket of Whalley in north Surrey, centred on the 96 Avenue and 128 Street corner, where the City itself names the Cedar Hills Shopping Centre at 12830 96 Avenue as the area's landmark site. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/26.06.26-City-of-Surrey-Land-Exchange-Final.pdf
- Cedar Hills Elementary at 12370 98 Avenue is the school that carries the area's name, two blocks north of 96 Avenue. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/surrey
- Cedar Hills area facts in full: see the cedar-hills-guide note. Surrey-wide basics: see the surrey-houses-for-sale note.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Cedar Hills is mostly detached houses, and that is the finding of this pass: no named strata condominium or townhouse complex inside Cedar Hills was verified from an official, developer or strata source, and the program's Surrey condos assembly likewise records no verified named-building roster for the Cedar Hills sub-area. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7912-0028-00.pdf
- What strata-form product exists sits on the 96 Avenue corridor at the area's edges: a City planning report for 13314, 13326 and 13344 96 Avenue proposed about 22 townhouse units by rezoning from Single Family Residential Zone (RF) to Comprehensive Development Zone (CD) based on a multiple residential zone. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7912-0028-00.pdf
- The one large multi-unit building with a Cedar Hills address, Pacific Park Apartments at 9450 128 Street, is a rental apartment building operated by Mainstreet Equity Corp., not a strata: it cannot be bought unit by unit. https://www.mainst.biz/apartments/surrey/pacific-park-apartments?%3Futm_source=loknow&utm_id=120222188457440339

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The ground under Cedar Hills houses is mid-transition: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000 now carries provincial Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) zones, and Corporate Report R083 of May 11, 2026 closes and replaces in-stream single-family rezoning applications in batches to match. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7921-0104-00-and-7921-0104-01
- The 96 Avenue corridor carries City Centre density expectations at its east end: City planning material states lands within City Centre east of 134 Street at 96 Avenue are deemed appropriate for multiple residential development up to a floor area ratio (FAR) of 2.5 under the City Centre Land Use and Density Concept. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7912-0028-00.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Are there any condos actually in Cedar Hills?
**A:** Almost none that verify: this pass found no named strata complex inside Cedar Hills from an official, developer or strata source, and the area's one large multi-unit building, Pacific Park Apartments at 9450 128 Street, is rental housing run by Mainstreet Equity Corp. Buyers set on a Cedar Hills address are really choosing between detached houses, corridor townhouse product on 96 Avenue, and strata stock in neighbouring Whalley and Bolivar Heights. https://www.mainst.biz/apartments/surrey/pacific-park-apartments?%3Futm_source=loknow&utm_id=120222188457440339

**Q:** Which elementary school serves a home near 96 Avenue and 128 Street?
**A:** Cedar Hills Elementary at 12370 98 Avenue, Kindergarten to Grade 7, is the named school inside the area, in School District No. 36 (Surrey), called SD36. École K.B. Woodward Elementary at 13130 106 Avenue serves addresses further west. Catchment follows the exact address in the SD36 School Locator, and the streets around 128 Street sit near catchment edges, so run the specific address rather than relying on the Cedar Hills name. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/surrey

**Q:** What is happening with the Cedar Hills Shopping Centre?
**A:** The City's June 26, 2026 land exchange document lists the Cedar Hills Shopping Centre at 12830 96 Avenue, a 14.6-acre Comprehensive Development (CD) zoned site at the southeast corner of 96 Avenue and 128 Street, as one of three disposition properties in a proposed arena and entertainment hub land exchange. It is a proposal in a City report, not an approved redevelopment, so no demolition or rezoning should be assumed from it. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/26.06.26-City-of-Surrey-Land-Exchange-Final.pdf

**Q:** How do I reach SkyTrain from Cedar Hills?
**A:** By bus down 96 Avenue: TransLink's Surrey schedule book lists route 326, Surrey Central Station to Guildford, and route 314, Sunbury to Surrey Central Station via Scott Road at 96 Avenue, as the routes working the 96 Avenue corridor. Both end at Surrey Central Station on the Expo Line, which is the practical transit pattern for the whole pocket: bus first, SkyTrain second. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2026-schedules/september/surrey-p170.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Bolivar Park at 13445 115 Avenue, at Cedar Hills' northeast edge, is the area's sports park, with baseball fields in the City's park listing. http://surrey.ca/culture-recreation/2075.aspx
- The Bolivar Dog Off-leash Area is a named City off-leash site inside that same park system on 115 Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/activities-parks-recreation/parks/things-to-do-in-parks/dog-off-leash-areas
- Cedar Hills Elementary hosts a StrongStart early learning centre, listed by SD36 at 12370 98 Avenue in its 2025–26 StrongStart locations. https://media.surreyschools.ca/media/Default/medialib/strongstart-locations-map-2025-2026.698a83189774.pdf
- The shopping centre site a buyer sees at 96 Avenue and 128 Street is 14.6 acres — larger than most Surrey neighbourhood centres — because it was assembled as one Comprehensive Development (CD) parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/26.06.26-City-of-Surrey-Land-Exchange-Final.pdf
- École K.B. Woodward Elementary at 13130 106 Avenue, west of the pocket, is the dual-track school whose catchment reaches toward Cedar Hills' western streets. https://www.surreyschools.ca/kbwoodward/contact-us
