# Woodland Acres — Extra Local Notes
Page: woodland-acres-port-coquitlam (Woodland Acres; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Woodland Acres is a small board sub-area on Port Coquitlam's west side, against the Coquitlam city boundary, and no City-published boundary for it was found, so the working check is its street pattern: Westwood Street on the west, Lougheed Highway at the south, and interior streets including McRae Crescent, Scott Place and Battistoni Place. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt173.pdf
- Westwood Street is the boundary street in practical terms: TransLink's route 173 timetable runs buses down Westwood Street between Dewdney Trunk Road in Coquitlam and Kingsway Avenue in Port Coquitlam, which is the pocket's western edge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt173.pdf
- The pocket sits between two better-known neighbours: Glenwood to the east inside Port Coquitlam, and Coquitlam's city centre across Westwood Street to the west. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt173.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Woodland Acres house? **A:** This pocket's catchment is genuinely not settled on the sources read, and a buyer should run the exact address in the School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) locator before relying on any school name. École Westwood Elementary, at 3610 Hastings Street in Port Coquitlam, is the elementary school on the pocket's own street pattern, but an earlier locator sample for this sub-area used an address on Confederation Drive — a different part of the city — so that result does not settle these streets. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** How do I get to SkyTrain from Woodland Acres? **A:** There is no SkyTrain station in Port Coquitlam, so the trip is bus first. Routes 173 and 174 run along Westwood Street on the pocket's west edge, linking Port Coquitlam Station and Coquitlam Central Station, where the Millennium Line and the West Coast Express run. Route 173 continues through Port Coquitlam via Kingsway Avenue and Wilson Avenue, and route 174 returns via Shaughnessy Street and David Avenue, so the two routes form a loop through both cities. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt173.pdf
- **Q:** Can I add units to a detached lot here? **A:** Possibly, under the city-wide small-scale rules. Port Coquitlam permits three to six small-scale multi-unit housing units on most RS-zoned lots without a rezoning, with the exact number depending on the lot's size, its location and its proximity to transit. Development cost charges apply to new small-scale units but are not applied to new secondary suites or accessory buildings. The zone and lot dimensions of the specific lot still govern, so confirm both with the City before pricing in added units. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q:** What is being built in or beside the pocket? **A:** No active residential application inside Woodland Acres was found on the City's capital projects listing read for this note. The nearest City road work in the program is on Kingsway Avenue, the east–west street routes 173 and 174 use south of the pocket, where a phase of intersection improvements was scheduled to start in early June 2026. Buyers should re-check the capital projects page, because project status there changes as phases move from design to construction. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects?construction_year=All&page=1

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The two bus routes on Westwood Street interline: a bus that leaves Coquitlam Central Station as route 173 Cedar arrives at its terminus and continues as route 174, and vice versa, so the route number on the same corridor changes mid-trip. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt173.pdf
- École Westwood Elementary, 3610 Hastings Street, is a French immersion elementary school inside the pocket's street pattern — the "École" in its name is the marker, and immersion entry is by the district's program rules, not by catchment alone. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-coquitlam
- The pocket's south edge is a highway commercial frontage, not houses: Lougheed Highway bounds the area to the south, so interior crescents sit one or two streets back from a major arterial. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt173.pdf
- Port Coquitlam's 2026 utility bills were due March 31, a separate and earlier bill than the July 2, 2026 property tax due date — a closing adjustment point buyers in this pocket share with the rest of the city. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/2026utilities85x11-finalpdf

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Routes 173 and 174 are the pocket's buses: both run the Westwood Street corridor between Coquitlam Central Station and Port Coquitlam Station, with route 173 continuing via Kingsway Avenue, Wilson Avenue, Prairie Avenue and Cedar Drive, and route 174 via Shaughnessy Street, David Avenue and Oxford Street. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt173.pdf
- Port Coquitlam Station, the south end of both routes, is the West Coast Express commuter rail station for the city. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt173.pdf
- Lougheed Highway at the pocket's south edge is the driving link east to the Mary Hill Bypass and Highway 1 and west into Coquitlam. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt173.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- School catchment is the pocket's open due-diligence item: conflicting sample results exist for this sub-area, so a school-dependent offer should be conditional on a locator run for the exact address, not on the neighbourhood name. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- Creek and hazard permit areas are a lot-by-lot check here: the City's OCP carries watercourse and hazard development permit areas, and the OCP, not the sub-area name, is where a buyer confirms whether a specific Westwood Street or McRae Crescent lot is inside one. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Boundary confusion is a real paperwork risk: postal addresses, school listings and services around Westwood Street mix Coquitlam and Port Coquitlam names, so the municipality on the tax notice — Port Coquitlam — is the authority for permits, utilities and bylaws. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/2026tax-newsletter-finalpdf
