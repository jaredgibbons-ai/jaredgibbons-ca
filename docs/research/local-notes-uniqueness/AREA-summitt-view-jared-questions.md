# Summitt View — Questions for Jared

## Questions
1. When buyers say "Summitt View" to you, which streets do they actually mean — and is that the same pocket the board data uses for the name?
2. In your deals on the lower Burke Mountain slopes, who is buying — first-time buyers priced out of Burke Mountain proper, move-up families, or investors?
3. What do buyers weigh most here in your showings: the drive down David Avenue to Coquitlam Central Station, the creek and slope lots, or being early in a still-building-out area?
4. Which homes sell fast and which sit in this pocket — the townhouse stock, the detached homes on flatter lots, or the view lots?
5. Tell one story from a deal or showing in this pocket — a catchment surprise, a creek setback that changed a plan, or a buyer who chose these streets over Burke Mountain or Westwood Plateau, and why.
