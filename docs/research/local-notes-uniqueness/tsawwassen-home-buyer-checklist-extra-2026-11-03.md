# Tsawwassen Home Buyer Checklist — Extra Local Notes
Page: tsawwassen-home-buyer-checklist (Tsawwassen; buyer checklist).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The first checklist step in Tsawwassen is confirming which government governs the lot: City of Delta bylaws cover Tsawwassen proper, while Tsawwassen First Nation (TFN) lands northwest of the community are under TFN's own laws — see the tsawwassen-houses-for-sale note for the shared boundary and treaty facts.
- TFN runs its own building permit system on Tsawwassen Lands: TFN's Building Regulation (2015), last amended November 26, 2024, regulates construction on Tsawwassen Lands and provides for building permits and an occupancy permit under TFN law, not Delta's bylaws. https://tsawwassenfirstnation.com/pdfs/TFN-Laws-Regulations-Policies/Regulations/Building_Regulation_26_Nov_2024.pdf
- TFN's land base is still growing under its treaty: TFN's 2025 Land Use Plan records the expansion of the land base from 724 hectares to 804 hectares since the 2009 Final Agreement took effect. https://tsawwassenfirstnation.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Land-Use-Plan-2025.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The checklist differs by tenure: most Tsawwassen homes are freehold under Delta zoning (see the tsawwassen-houses-for-sale note), while homes on Tsawwassen Lands are leasehold residential projects on land TFN continues to own. https://tsawwassenfirstnation.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Land-Use-Plan-2025.pdf
- TFN's plan counts leasehold homes as a distinct housing form: the 2025 Land Use Plan lists "leasehold residential, commercial and industrial projects" among the development delivered on Tsawwassen Lands since the treaty. https://tsawwassenfirstnation.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Land-Use-Plan-2025.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Check the suite's registration, not just its existence: Delta's secondary suite registration form requires the suite to meet the BC Building Code and Delta Building/Plumbing Bylaw No. 6060, 2002 for health, fire and life safety, and only one secondary suite is permitted on a property. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/48825/
- A Delta suite carries the house's address with a letter added: Delta's suite program states no separate address is assigned, the suite uses the principal dwelling's address with the letter "B" added, and an indicator must be placed at the front of the home so emergency personnel can find the suite. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/48825/
- Suite size and parking are capped in Delta: the registration requirements set a suite's floor area between 33 m² and 90 m² and at no more than 40% of the dwelling's habitable floor space, with an off-street parking space for the suite in addition to the two required for the principal dwelling. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/48825/
- A detached coach house or garden suite follows Delta's accessory dwelling unit (ADU) rules: the City's October 2025 ADU brochure states up to two detached ADUs are permitted on the majority of urban lots with single-family or duplex dwellings, and its concierge team helps owners through the permit process. https://www.delta.ca/media/4304
- ADU heights are set by type in Delta: the brochure sets a coach house at a maximum of 2 storeys, 6.75 m to mid-roof or flat-roof top and 8 m to a pitched roof ridge, and a garden suite at 1.5 storeys. https://www.delta.ca/media/4304
- A foreshore lot in the Boundary Bay / Southlands area has a seawall standard: Delta's seawall design form for the SD3 Boundary Bay Foreshore and Southlands development permit area requires the seawall crest at 4.1 m geodetic elevation, designed so the crest can later be raised by 1.4 m. https://admin.delta.ca/media/5541

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is the basement suite in a Tsawwassen house legal? **A:** Check its registration and its address, not the listing description. Delta registers a secondary suite only after it meets the BC Building Code and Delta Building/Plumbing Bylaw No. 6060, 2002, and a registered suite is identified by the letter "B" added to the house's address, with an indicator at the front of the home for emergency responders. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/48825/
- **Q:** Is this home on Tsawwassen First Nation land, and does that change the purchase? **A:** Yes, it changes which rules apply to the home. Land northwest of Tsawwassen proper is Tsawwassen Lands under TFN's own laws: construction there is governed by TFN's Building Regulation rather than Delta bylaws, and the housing is leasehold on land TFN owns, under TFN's 2025 Land Use Plan. Confirm the boundary before assuming City of Delta rules, taxes or permits apply. https://tsawwassenfirstnation.com/pdfs/TFN-Laws-Regulations-Policies/Regulations/Building_Regulation_26_Nov_2024.pdf
- **Q:** The lot backs onto Boundary Bay — what flood checks apply? **A:** Check the lot's dike and seawall position before offering. Delta received a $2 million provincial grant in 2023 to raise a section of the Boundary Bay dike, and DeltaMap shows flood protection and dike elevations parcel by parcel; a foreshore property in the SD3 permit area must also meet the City's seawall design form, including a 4.1 m crest elevation. https://admin.delta.ca/media/5541
- **Q:** Can I add a coach house after I buy? **A:** In Delta, a building permit is the route, and no rezoning is needed on most urban lots. The City's ADU brochure states up to two detached accessory dwelling units are permitted on the majority of urban lots with single-family or duplex dwellings, applications are made through the City's online permit portal, and the free ADU Concierge Service answers questions at 604-946-3380. https://www.delta.ca/media/4304

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- A registered Delta suite adds an annual utility charge: Delta's suite program states that once the building inspector issues a final occupancy permit, the suite is registered with the City and the owner pays additional utility fees each year. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/48825/
- Boarders are not allowed in a Delta house that has a suite: the City's suite requirements state boarders and lodgers are not permitted in a single family house that contains a secondary suite. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/48825/
- Heritage checks in Delta run through a register set up in 1999: a BC environmental assessment document records that Delta Council established the Delta Heritage Register, the official register of sites of heritage significance in Delta, on March 9, 1999, and that listed properties are referred to the Heritage Advisory Commission when an application affects them. https://projects.eao.gov.bc.ca/api/document/5cb8fe80dd75c10024d0476d/fetch/7.1_Heritage.pdf
- Tsawwassen has no Ladner-style heritage conservation area in the current plan: Delta's Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 maps its two Heritage Conservation Areas in Ladner (Maps IIE and IIF) and maps Tsawwassen under Development Permit Areas instead (Map IIB). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the dike before the house: Delta's 2023 dike announcement states, in the mayor's words, that with future sea levels expected to rise it is imperative that the dike be raised to protect the community from future flooding, and the grant-funded pilot raises the Boundary Bay dike section adjacent to Boundary Bay Airport between 72 Street and 88 Street. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-receives-provincial-grant-boundary-bay-dike
- Check the lot on DeltaMap first: the City's map shows flood protection, dike elevations, permit areas and lot dimensions by parcel — see the tsawwassen-houses-for-sale note for the DeltaMap link and the English Bluff geotechnical permit area.
- Check the seawall paperwork on a foreshore lot: Delta's seawall form requires either a new engineered seawall design or, for an existing seawall, certification by a Professional Engineer of its structural and flood protection integrity. https://admin.delta.ca/media/5541
