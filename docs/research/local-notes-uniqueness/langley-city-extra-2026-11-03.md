## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools are physically inside the City? **A:** Six School District No. 35 (Langley) schools sit inside the City: Douglas Park Community at 5409 206 Street, Nicomekl Elementary at 20050 53 Avenue, Blacklock Fine Arts Elementary at 5100 206 Street, Uplands Elementary at 4471 207a Street, H D Stafford Middle at 20441 Grade Crescent, and Langley Secondary at 21405 56 Avenue. Catchments are address-based, so confirm the exact address with the district. https://www.langleycity.ca/city-hall/reports-plans-strategies/strategic-vision
- **Q:** Is the hospital inside the City? **A:** No. Langley Memorial Hospital at 22051 Fraser Highway is the hospital for both Langleys, and Fraser Health lists its address in the Township of Langley, immediately outside the City boundary. It provides 24/7 emergency, general and internal medicine, general surgery and maternity services, so City buyers use it daily without it being a City building. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital
- **Q:** When does SkyTrain actually reach the City? **A:** The Surrey Langley SkyTrain project extends the Expo Line 16 kilometres from King George Station to 203 Street in Langley City, with eight stations and a transit exchange at Langley City Centre. The project's current anticipated in-service date is late 2029. Until then the City is bus-served, so price a home on today's transit, not the station rendering. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/project-overview
- **Q:** Are City taxes the same as Township taxes? **A:** No, the two municipalities set separate rates and send separate notices. The City's 2026 total residential rate and utility structure are stated in full in the langleycity note. A Township rate applied to a City home, or the reverse, gives the wrong carrying cost, so check the municipality on the tax notice itself. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/2026taxrates
- **Q:** Where do I check the plan rules for a City house? **A:** On the City's land-use page, not this homes page. The langleycity note carries the Official Community Plan, Zoning Bylaw No. 3300, Transit-Oriented Area and suite rules in full. This page's job is the homes themselves: stock, schools, transit and daily life inside the City boundary. https://www.langleycity.ca/index.php/business-development/planning-zoning/official-community-plan
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- City Hall is at 20399 Douglas Crescent, and the City charges a 2.2 percent processing fee on credit-card property tax payments. https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes
- Property tax payments received after the July 2 due date incur a penalty in the City; the percentage is not stated on the payment page read. https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes
- Blacklock is a fine arts elementary school and Douglas Park is a community school — program labels a catchment search alone will not explain; confirm placement with School District No. 35. https://www.langleycity.ca/city-hall/reports-plans-strategies/strategic-vision
- The City's vision page promises SkyTrain by the end of 2029 while the project page says late 2029 — the same target in two wordings; use the project page for status. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/project-overview
- Langley City Centre station will include one of the extension's three transit exchanges, so bus routes will re-orient to 203 Street when the line opens — check current. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/project-overview

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Confirm the municipality before anything else: a City address follows City bylaws and City tax rates, and a Township address a few streets away follows the Township's — the boundary, not the postal code, decides. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-city.html
- For a home with a suite, ask for the City registry record and the building permit, not the listing text — the registry requirement is stated in the langleycity note.
- For a lot near the future station, check the Transit-Oriented Area land-use update that came with the 2025–2026 bylaw package before assuming current density is final — check current. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/public-hearing-noticepdf
- School catchments are address-based in School District No. 35 and no district catchment lookup for a City address is on record in this program; run the exact address before writing a school claim.
