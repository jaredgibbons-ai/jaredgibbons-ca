# Yarrow Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: yarrow-guide (Yarrow, Chilliwack; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Yarrow is a rural community in the west of the City of Chilliwack, covered by its own Yarrow Neighbourhood Plan, adopted June 2022 and updated 2025 as Schedule B7 of the 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799
- The City's flood-alert description bounds the core alert area from Boundary Road to Browne Road, and from the Vedder River to Majuba Hill Road — a practical picture of the settled village area, not a legal boundary. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=37&prshow=details&prID=490
- Yarrow sits outside the Urban Growth Boundary, which is why the plan envisions minimal growth there — see the yarrow note for street detail. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The Yarrow Neighbourhood Plan states the neighbourhood is defined predominantly by single-detached dwellings on large rural lots. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799
- Newer homes here tend to be built higher than older ones: the plan notes floodplain requirements typically push new development to the maximum 10-metre height limit in residential zones, while pre-floodplain homes were much lower. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Flood rules are specific to Yarrow: under Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519, in the Yarrow and Greendale areas a building's lowest habitable floor must be at least 1.2 metres above natural ground elevation, or 0.3 metres above the crown of the adjacent road, whichever is greater, with a qualified professional's report and a covenant absolving the City. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- Servicing limits growth: the plan states groundwater is high year-round and soil and the high water table cannot support a significant increase in septic system loading. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799
- Much surrounding land is Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR); the City notes nearly 70% of land in Chilliwack is in the ALR and not available for development. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1618

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Why is growth so limited in Yarrow? **A:** The plan gives three reasons together: Yarrow's rural setting outside the Urban Growth Boundary, servicing constraints, and floodplain constraints. That combination, not a single rule, is why buyers should not assume a large rural lot here can be subdivided or densified like an urban Sardis lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799
- **Q:** How serious is flood risk in Yarrow? **A:** The plan states Yarrow is within the floodplains of both the Fraser River and the Vedder River, protected by an extensive City-managed diking system, and that rural communities like Yarrow are at highest risk, with an average projected flood depth of 4 metres if a dike fails. Read the plan and the flood bylaw for any specific lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799
- **Q:** Which schools do Yarrow children attend? **A:** School District No. 33 (SD33) lists the Yarrow/Vedder Mountain Road area as Yarrow Elementary School, Stitó:s Lá:lém Totí:lt Elementary/Middle School, and Sardis Secondary School. In May 2026 SD33 confirmed no change to that pathway: Greendale and Yarrow students continue to Stitó:s Lá:lém Totí:lt, then Sardis Secondary in Grade 9. https://sd33.bc.ca/news/board-approves-changes-some-schools
- **Q:** Is Yarrow on city water and sewer? **A:** Not uniformly. The City's utilities page lists a separate Yarrow Water Works, and the neighbourhood plan stresses septic limits from a high water table, so a buyer must confirm, for the exact lot, whether it is on community water, a well, city sewer or a septic system. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=70
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Yarrow Community School is at 4605 Wilson Road, per the Province's school list for SD33. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/033
- The City issued an evacuation alert for Yarrow in November 2021, from Boundary Road to Browne Road — flood alerts here are a lived event, not only a map layer. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=37&prshow=details&prID=490
- In April 2023 Chilliwack signed the Sumas River Watershed Flood Mitigation Collaborative Framework with the Province, Semá:th, Mathxwí and Leq'á:mel First Nations and the City of Abbotsford to make Sumas Prairie more resilient — the watershed west of Yarrow. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1912
- BC Transit route 55 is named Yarrow-Greendale and runs weekdays and Saturdays only in the route description read — check the current riders' guide before planning a commute. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/riders-guide/
- New secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level, per the City's small-scale housing FAQ referencing the Floodplain Regulation Bylaw. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3279/FAQ_-_Small-Scale_Housing_-_Updated_2025_07_28.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City of Chilliwack 2025 residential property tax total was 4.50189 per $1,000 of assessed taxable value, per the City's 2025 rate table; the table includes a dyking component, relevant in a diked community like Yarrow. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2527/Tax%5FRates%5Ffor%5Fthe%5FWebtugnc2fs1l9d%2Epdf
- The City of Chilliwack is a designated taxable area for the Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT), so Yarrow owners in the city must complete the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- City utility rates are billed quarterly, but Yarrow Water Works is listed separately on the City utilities page — confirm which billing applies to a specific lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- BC Transit route 55, Yarrow-Greendale, runs from the Vedder Road / University of the Fraser Valley (UFV) Chilliwack campus area to Yarrow and Greendale via Vedder Mountain, Yarrow Central, Keith Wilson Road and South Sumas Road. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/riders-guide/
- Route 51, Vedder, connects the UFV campus to downtown Chilliwack, giving Yarrow riders a transfer point at the campus. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/riders-guide/
- Road access west is by Keith Wilson Road and Vedder Mountain Road; in November 2021 the City directed drivers to Keith Wilson Road when Vedder Mountain Road closed for landslide-related work. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=37&prid=495&prshow=details

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with flood: Yarrow lies in both Fraser and Vedder floodplains, with separate City Flood Response Plans for each river, posted on the City's Flood Protection page. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1912
- Check the Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 Schedule A map and the Yarrow-specific elevation rule in section 11 before planning any build or suite. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- Check septic capacity and groundwater on site: the plan states high year-round groundwater limits added septic loading, which can affect a suite or second dwelling plan. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799
- Check ALR status per lot with the Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) before assuming a non-farm use. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1618
