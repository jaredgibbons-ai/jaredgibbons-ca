## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools are in Elgin Chantrell itself? **A:** Chantrell Creek Elementary and Elgin Park Secondary both sit inside the area, at 2575 137 Street (Kindergarten to Grade 7) and 13484 24 Avenue (Grades 8 to 12), per the Ministry's district list. A school inside the area is not the same as catchment, which the district sets by home address. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** Can I subdivide an acreage lot into small lots? **A:** No — the RA zone keeps subdivision at acreage scale. Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 sets the minimum lot at 4,050 square metres, with 50-metre width and 60-metre depth minimums and a maximum density of 2.5 lots per hectare, so the lot does not split into city-size lots. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **Q:** Is a low river-edge lot a normal lot? **A:** No — treat it as a floodplain lot until checked. The City states much of the Nicomekl lowland is very close to sea level because it was historically an intertidal zone, and it has built dikes and pump stations under its lowlands flood plan since 1997. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **Q:** What is the heritage building on Crescent Road? **A:** The Historic Stewart Farm inside Elgin Heritage Park, at 13723 Crescent Road. The Canadian Register lists an 1894 farmhouse with seven associated farm buildings there, formally recognized in 1985, as the only intact farm complex from the late nineteenth century remaining in Surrey. http://historicplaces.ca/fr/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=2590&pid=0
Read: 2026-11-03
