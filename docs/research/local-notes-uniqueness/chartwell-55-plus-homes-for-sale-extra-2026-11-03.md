## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Only a 55+ age bylaw is legal in a British Columbia strata now: Strata Property Act sections 123.1 and 123.2 permit a restriction to age 55 or older, and any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since November 24, 2022 — and that framework does not apply to a rental retirement residence at all, because a residence is not a strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- The bylaw restricts who may live in a strata unit, not who may own it, and Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse or partner, and children in the defined care cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- West Vancouver zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010: the single-family zones in section 200 set minimum site areas from 488 square metres in RS5 to 8,094 square metres in RS1, with secondary suites and detached secondary suites permitted — the Chartwell hillside is large-lot single-family zoning, not apartment zoning. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- Assisted-living suites in the Ambleside residences are registered with British Columbia's Assisted Living Registry, and publicly funded long-term care is allocated through Vancouver Coastal Health after an assessment — neither is bought, and neither uses a strata age bylaw. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there a Chartwell retirement residence in West Vancouver? **A:** No. Chartwell has no West Vancouver residence on its own British Columbia finder, and its only North Shore residence is Churchill House at 150 West 29th Street in North Vancouver, a rental independent and assisted living residence. A search result pairing Chartwell with West Vancouver is pairing the British Properties neighbourhood name — Chartwell Drive and Chartwell Elementary — with the city, not naming a residence. https://chartwell.com/find-a-residence/british-columbia
- **Q:** What 55+ homes can I buy in West Vancouver, then? **A:** The verified age-restricted strata set in the settled roster is three Ambleside buildings: Westview Terrace at 1390 Duchess Avenue, Tudor Gardens at 843 22nd Street and Lincoln Gardens at 2110 Marine Drive. All three are on the Ambleside flat near Marine Drive, not in Chartwell and not on the hillside, and each building's registered age bylaw is confirmed on its Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** What are the Chartwell-style options that are actually in West Vancouver? **A:** The same product type — rental retirement living with care on site — is offered by Amica West Vancouver at 659 Clyde Avenue, Amica Lions Gate at 701 Keith Road and Hollyburn House at 2095 Marine Drive, with independent-living rental at Westerleigh PARC. All are rental, none is Chartwell, and none is strata ownership, so the comparison is between tenancy and care agreements, not purchase contracts. https://www.amica.ca/west-vancouver
- **Q:** Where is the hospital, and where would long-term care be? **A:** Lions Gate Hospital at 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, serves the whole North Shore under Vancouver Coastal Health. Inside West Vancouver itself, Inglewood Care Centre at 725 Inglewood Avenue is the District's long-term care home, carried in the settled file as 230 publicly funded beds opened in 1979 and operated by the Baptist Housing Society, with access through Vancouver Coastal Health. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- All of the District's care capacity is on the Ambleside flat: the registered assisted-living residences, the one long-term care home and all three verified 55+ strata buildings sit within a few blocks of Marine Drive between Clyde Avenue and 22nd Street, and the hillside and western villages have no registered seniors housing. https://www.amica.ca/lions-gate/suites
- One Ambleside address carries two products: at 959 21st Street the Kiwanis society describes its own 55+ independent living, while a registered assisted-living residence at the same address is published by another operator — treat Kiwanis Manor as two products at one address until the operators confirm the split. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-manor/
- Operator brands change faster than registries: Hollyburn House at 2095 Marine Drive is currently marketed under a different brand name than its Assisted Living Registry legal operator, so the registry entry, not the brand, is the stable fact to cite. https://cogirseniorliving.ca/retirement-home-west-vancouver-british-columbia/venvi-hollyburn-house
- West Vancouver's additional school tax is a routine line at hillside values: 0.2 percent on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4 percent above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3 percent and 0.6 percent on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5 percent on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026, and water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- District of West Vancouver property taxes and utility fees are set by the District on its taxes and utility fees pages: 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026 with the two 5 percent penalty dates in section 5, and water and sewer are metered and billed quarterly rather than charged as a flat annual utility bill. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- The primary risk on this page is a name-collision risk: a buyer who searches the operator name and lands on a hillside house, or who searches the neighbourhood name and expects a residence, is reading two different subjects, and the civic address and the operator's own finder are the checks that separate them. https://chartwell.com/find-a-residence/british-columbia
- For a hillside lot, confirm the zone and minimum site area in Zoning Bylaw No. 4662 section 200 and look the parcel up in the District's mapping before assuming a suite, a detached secondary suite or a subdivision is available, because RS zone minimums in the British Properties range up to 8,094 square metres in RS1. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- For the three Ambleside 55+ strata buildings, the registered age bylaw and pet rule come from each building's Form B; the settled roster's age evidence for two of the three rests on a North Shore building guide rather than a strata page, so it is treated as roster evidence, not as a bylaw read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
