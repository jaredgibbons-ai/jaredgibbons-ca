# Montecito Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: montecito-burnaby-houses-for-sale (Montecito; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Montecito is a detached-house area in northeast Burnaby, below Burnaby Mountain and above Lougheed Highway. The City also calls this area Sperling-Broadway for planning purposes, and Burnaby Lake Regional Park sits directly south across Lougheed Highway. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/burnaby-lake-sports-complex
- Kensington Avenue forms the west edge and Halifax Street runs across the north side. Duthie Avenue, Sperling Avenue and Greystone Drive run through the house streets, and Burnaby Mountain Parkway climbs from the east side to Simon Fraser University (SFU). https://translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/september-2026/burnaby-and-new-westminster-map.pdf
- Lochdale and Parkcrest sit to the west and Sperling-Duthie sits to the south across Lougheed Highway. Metro Vancouver describes Burnaby Lake Regional Park as a wildlife sanctuary with a rowing course, Nature House, viewing towers and docks. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/burnaby-lake-regional-park

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached houses on sloping streets below the mountain are the stock this page covers. A small pocket of low-rise apartments sits around Halifax Street at Phillips Avenue, but the house streets on Duthie Avenue, Halifax Street and Greystone Drive are freehold detached lots. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- Bill Copeland Sports Centre at 3676 Kensington Avenue anchors the west edge, inside the Burnaby Lake Sports Complex. The City lists an ice rink with 1,984 seats there, plus five lit artificial turf fields in the wider complex. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/bill-copeland-sports-centre

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Most Montecito house lots sit in Burnaby's R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District. Burnaby replaced its former single- and two-family districts with R1 in June 2024: a lot can hold 3 units if it is up to 280 square metres, 4 units on a larger lot, and 6 units on a lot of at least 281 square metres within 400 metres of a frequent-transit stop. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- The new Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 took effect July 1, 2026, so check the current bylaw, not an older summary, before designing a rebuild. The City's zoning rewrite page is the starting point and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) now governs density expectations on large lots. Council adopted it on December 9, 2025; it replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves a Montecito house? **A:** Montecito Elementary is the regular-program catchment for the Montecito address checked. A School District 41 (SD41) locator run for 2176 Duthie Avenue returned Montecito Elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 7) and Burnaby North Secondary (Grades 8 to 12). Sperling Elementary appeared for that address only as Early French Immersion, so do not assume Sperling is the regular catchment. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** Can I add units to a Montecito lot? **A:** Yes, under the R1 rules stated in section 3, subject to lot size and transit distance. A standard lot allows 3 to 4 homes in total, counting a suite or coach house, and only a larger lot within 400 metres of a frequent-transit stop reaches 6. Confirm the lot area and the transit test with the City before pricing in that value. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q:** Is there SkyTrain in Montecito itself? **A:** No station sits inside Montecito; the station is at the south edge on Lougheed Highway. Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station on the Millennium Line connects with bus routes 110, 134 and 144, and the 144 runs between Metrotown Station and SFU. North-end houses on Halifax Street are a bus or drive from that station, not a short walk. https://alz.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/skytrain-accessible-entrance-maps/sperling_burnaby_lake_station_elevator_map.pdf
- **Q:** What is at Burnaby Lake for a house buyer? **A:** Burnaby Lake Regional Park gives Montecito houses a large protected park at the south door. Metro Vancouver lists walking trails, viewing towers and docks, a Nature House, and a rowing course with a pavilion and public canoe launch at the Burnaby Lake Sports Complex on Sperling Avenue. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/burnaby-lake-regional-park

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Sperling Elementary is an Early French Immersion school, not the default catchment for every Montecito address. The SD41 locator result for 2176 Duthie Avenue lists Sperling only under Early French Immersion and Montecito Elementary as the regular program, so check each address separately. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- Burnaby Lake Sports Complex fields 1 and 2 were closed for turf upgrades from May to September 2026 and were listed open again in the City's October 29, 2026 field-status update. The complex is reached from Kensington Avenue and Sperling Avenue, at Montecito's south-west corner. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/burnaby-lake-sports-complex
- The library branch for this quadrant is the temporary Cameron Branch inside The City of Lougheed Shopping Centre at 9855 Austin Avenue. Burnaby Public Library runs four branches, one in each quadrant of the city. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/city-departments/burnaby-public-library
- You can walk into Burnaby Lake Regional Park directly from Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station. Metro Vancouver also notes the park's Sprott Street sports-field entrance on Sperling Avenue is served by bus routes 110 and 144. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/burnaby-lake-regional-park

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026. A 5 percent penalty applies to outstanding current taxes and to an unclaimed home owner grant after the July due date, with a further 5 percent after the September due date, and Council approved a 2.9 percent tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- The 2026 water charge for a detached single-family dwelling is $652.33 for the year. Burnaby's residential utility notice also covers annual sewer use and a garbage toter fee, rates carry a 5 percent discount if paid by the due date, and single-family sewer is billed as a sewer parcel tax on the property tax notice. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- Starting in 2027 the sewer parcel tax moves off the property tax notice and onto the utility notice. The City tells owners on the tax deferment program or with mortgage-paid taxes to check their 2026 notice for the amount that will move. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
