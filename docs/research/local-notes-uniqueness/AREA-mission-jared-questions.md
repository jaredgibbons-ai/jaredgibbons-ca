# Mission — Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer, for a voice memo. Muse does not answer these.

1. When a buyer looks at farm land in Mission — Hatzic Prairie, Dewdney–Deroche, the Stave Falls side — are they working farmers, hobby-farm buyers, or land bankers waiting on the ALR?
2. What sells fast in Mission's farm country and what sits for a year — small acreages with a house, or bare ALR parcels?
3. What surprises farm buyers most here: floodplain and dike rules on the Fraser flats, well and septic realities, or how much of Hatzic Prairie and Hatzic Island sits in the regional district rather than the city?
4. How do buyers compare Mission farm land against Abbotsford and Langley — what does Mission give them for the lower profile?
5. Tell one story from a Mission farm deal — a soil or water surprise, an ALC ruling that changed a deal, or a family farm changing hands.
