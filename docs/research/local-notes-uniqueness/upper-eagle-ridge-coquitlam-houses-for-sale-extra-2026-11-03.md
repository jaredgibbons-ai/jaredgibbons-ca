## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Upper Eagle Ridge is the upper, northern part of the Eagle Ridge house area on the slope below Westwood Plateau, and the City publishes no official boundary for the multiple listing service sub-area name. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/coquitlamcrunchtrail-27
- Its centre streets are Falcon Drive, Charter Hill Drive and the upper part of Lansdowne Drive, roughly Guildford Way south, David Avenue north and Johnson Street west. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- Addressed anchors are Eagle Ridge Elementary at 1215 Falcon Drive, Eagle Ridge Park, and Coquitlam Crunch Trail access points at Eagle Ridge Park, on Lansdowne Drive and at David Avenue. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/coquitlamcrunchtrail-27
- The City Centre Area Plan Schedule A map includes the Falcon Drive and Lansdowne Drive area, with David Avenue at the north edge of the mapped area. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/3487/City-Centre-Area-Plan-Servicing-Assessment-PDF

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses here are the one-family stock on the Falcon Drive, Charter Hill Drive and upper Lansdowne Drive slope; the City Centre Area Plan Schedule A legend shows "One Family Residential" and "Compact One Family Residential" designations in this part of the plan area, with apartment and townhousing designations closer to City Centre. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/3487/City-Centre-Area-Plan-Servicing-Assessment-PDF
- No City, School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) or BC Assessment public page read for this note states a typical build decade, lot size, subdivision name or house style for Upper Eagle Ridge itself, so none is claimed; era and lot size are title and QtheMap checks per lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- The slope is the stock's defining physical fact in official sources: the City's Crunch Trail page records 242 metres of elevation gain from the Lansdowne Drive and Eagle Ridge Park side to the top of Westwood Plateau, the plateau this pocket climbs toward. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/coquitlamcrunchtrail-27

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City-wide zoning, small-scale multi-unit housing, 2026 tax and utility figures and Speculation and Vacancy Tax applicability are in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; this note covers the slope, creek and wildfire rules on Falcon Drive and Charter Hill Drive lots. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- Creek rule at the City Centre distance: land within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of a watercourse in the City Centre Area Plan needs a Watercourse Protection Development Permit before land is altered, and Scott Creek runs through the wider Eagle Ridge area in a green corridor. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- Wildfire rule: Coquitlam has an Interface Wildfire Risk Management Development Permit Area, with FireSmart landscaping and exterior design guidelines in Part 4 of the former Citywide Official Community Plan, and the City's Community Wildfire Resiliency Plan 2021 states about 23% of the Coquitlam wildland urban interface has a moderate wildfire behaviour threat or higher. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/7234/Community_Wildfire_Resiliency_Plan_2021-FireSmart_Principles
- Plan frame, check current: no plan is named for Eagle Ridge; the whole city is under the Interim Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 5511, 2026, adopted April 27, 2026, and the Northwest Coquitlam Area Plan, carried in as a Schedule J schedule, covers the Westwood Plateau lands immediately above this pocket. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve Upper Eagle Ridge — is it Dr. Charles Best and Minnekhada? **A:** No official source read supports that pairing for this pocket. The schools physically in the area, all School District No. 43, are Eagle Ridge Elementary at 1215 Falcon Drive, kindergarten to Grade 5, Scott Creek Middle at 1240 Lansdowne Drive, Grades 6 to 8, and Gleneagle Secondary at 1195 Lansdowne Drive, Grades 9 to 12. École Dr. Charles Best Secondary is listed at 2525 Como Lake Avenue in central Coquitlam and Minnekhada Middle in Port Coquitlam; catchment is by home address, so run the specific address in the district School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** How steep is the slope above these houses? **A:** The City measures the climb beside the pocket, not each street. The Coquitlam Crunch Trail, which starts at Eagle Ridge Park below these streets, climbs 2.2 kilometres on the British Columbia Hydro right-of-way with two stair sets totalling 894 steps and 242 metres of elevation gain to the top of Westwood Plateau, with access at Eagle Ridge Park, Guildford Way, two points on Lansdowne Drive, David Avenue and Bramble Park on Panorama Drive. A specific Charter Hill Drive lot's grade still needs its own survey. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/coquitlamcrunchtrail-27
- **Q:** Does wildfire planning affect a Falcon Drive house? **A:** It can, at the permit stage. Coquitlam regulates high-risk interface areas through an Interface Wildfire Risk Management Development Permit Area, and its Community Wildfire Resiliency Plan 2021 records that FireSmart landscaping and exterior finish guidelines sit in Part 4 of the former Citywide plan. The City also describes a wildfire buffer in northwest Coquitlam and FireSmart property assessments on interface properties. Whether a specific lot is inside the permit boundary is a City map check, not a neighbourhood label. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/7234/Community_Wildfire_Resiliency_Plan_2021-FireSmart_Principles
- **Q:** Is Upper Eagle Ridge in a floodplain? **A:** The City's floodplain mapping covers the Coquitlam, Fraser and Pitt river floodplains, and this slope pocket is upland of them; no floodplain or slope-hazard statement specific to Upper Eagle Ridge was found in the official sources read. The local water check is different: Scott Creek runs through the wider Eagle Ridge area, the City classifies its creeks for environmental value and monitors creek levels, and lots within 30 metres of a watercourse bank in this plan area fall in the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area. https://coquitlam.ca/217/Creeks-and-Streams
- **Q:** What recreation sits at the foot of the pocket? **A:** Eagle Ridge Park, entrance at 2635 Runnel Drive and fronting the Lansdowne Drive school campus, lists a baseball and softball diamond, community garden, lacrosse box with four pickleball courts, playground, playing fields, four lighted tennis courts, trails and washrooms. Its subfacility, Eagle Ridge Outdoor Pool at 2689 Guildford Way, is an eight-lane, 25-metre pool with 1-metre and 3-metre diving boards, with a listed 2026 season of May 30 to September 7. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Eagle-Ridge-Outdoor-Pool-91

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the City's environmental development permit mapping, Schedule B, which maps unstable slopes and the Interface Wildfire Risk Management boundary for the City Centre and Northeast areas; the map shows the boundaries, not a lot-level slope or fire conclusion. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- Check the Community Wildfire Resiliency Plan 2021 threat mapping: about 23% of Coquitlam's wildland urban interface rates moderate wildfire behaviour threat or higher, and the plan's FireSmart actions are aimed at homes backing onto natural green space in interface neighbourhoods. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/7234/Community_Wildfire_Resiliency_Plan_2021-FireSmart_Principles
- Check creek setbacks on any lot draining to Scott Creek: within 30 metres of top of bank or ravine bank in this plan area, the Watercourse Protection Development Permit can impose conditions on riparian protection, runoff quality and erosion or landslip. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
