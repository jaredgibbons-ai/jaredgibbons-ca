# Coquitlam West — Questions for Jared

1. In Coquitlam West deals, what do you see differ between the older detached houses on Foster Avenue, Robinson Street and the Como Lake Avenue streets and the new condos and townhomes around Burquitlam Station on Clarke Road?
2. Who buys in Coquitlam West / Burquitlam: Simon Fraser University students, staff and investors riding the 143 bus from the station, families aimed at the Como Lake schools, or commuters splitting the Burnaby–Coquitlam border — and what do they say tipped it?
3. What sells fast here and what sits: do houses within a short walk of Burquitlam Station move first, while houses up the Como Lake hill wait — and how did the Evergreen Extension opening change what sells on Clarke Road?
4. What surprises Coquitlam West buyers: that North Road is the Burnaby border and Lougheed Town Centre is a different city, SkyTrain guideway noise near Clarke Road, or the grades on the streets climbing off Como Lake Avenue?
5. Tell one Coquitlam West story: a deal on Clarke Road or Como Lake Avenue where the station, a rezoning, or the Burnaby border changed the price, the subjects, or the outcome.
