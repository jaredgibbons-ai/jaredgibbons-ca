## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Pacific Douglas is the board name for the City of Surrey neighbourhood the City calls Douglas, and the City plan area is only about 61 hectares (150 acres) east of Highway 99, west of Highway 15 and north of the United States border. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/douglas-ncp
- The attached stock on this page sits mostly north of that small plan pocket, on 4 Avenue, 19 Avenue and 174 Street, so a Pacific Douglas condo or townhome address is not automatically inside the Douglas plan area — see the pacific-douglas-south-surrey-houses-for-sale note for the neighbourhood-shared boundary detail. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/douglas-ncp
- The plan area's north edge is Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) land and The Glades Garden Park, which is why the streets just north of the pocket change from strata complexes to farm parcels. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/douglas-ncp

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The 1999 Douglas plan designations no longer govern these complexes, because the City states Douglas's land use designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan update, with zoning parcel by parcel under Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 — city-wide Surrey rules are in full in the surrey-condos-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/douglas-ncp
- A 55+ bylaw is the only age bylaw a strata here may enforce: the Province states a strata may restrict residency to people age 55 or older, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and younger spouses or partners — the frame for The Greens at Douglas, to be confirmed on that strata's Form B Information Certificate. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void in every strata on this page, because the Province removed strata rental restrictions effective November 24, 2022; pet bylaws remain enforceable and differ by complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- On the ALR land at the north edge, the Agricultural Land Commission housing frame is one principal residence of at most 500 square metres plus at most one additional residence of 90 square metres on parcels of 40 hectares or less, or 186 square metres on larger parcels — relevant to any bare-land strata parcel touching that edge. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I buy an apartment condo in Pacific Douglas? **A:** No completed one is verified in the sub-area. The strata stock that trades here is townhome and rancher complexes — Douglas Point at 17516 4 Avenue, Berkeley Village at 16433 19 Avenue and the 55+ Greens at Douglas at 350 174 Street — plus presale product still under construction. A listing that calls a Pacific Douglas unit an apartment condo should be checked against its strata plan and civic address before it is treated as apartment stock. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/53-17516-4-ave-1934378
- **Q:** Is Douglas Point a seniors complex like The Greens at Douglas? **A:** No, and the two are easy to confuse because both are gated rancher-style stratas minutes apart. The Greens at Douglas carries a published 55+ age restriction in its building record, while no source read states an age restriction for Douglas Point. Under provincial law only a 55-or-older bylaw is enforceable at all, and the registered bylaw on the Form B — not a listing summary — is what governs who may live in either complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** Which elementary school is inside the neighbourhood? **A:** Douglas Elementary, at 17325 2 Avenue, is the Kindergarten to Grade 7 school physically inside this ground, listed in the British Columbia Ministry school list for School District 36 (Surrey). Catchment assignment is by exact home address and the district has the final word, so run the specific unit's address through the Surrey Schools locator before writing an offer that depends on this school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** A project at 18 Avenue is labelled Pacific Douglas but its own copy says Grandview Heights — which is it? **A:** Unsettled, and the label should not be relied on. That project is Harlowe at 16485 18 Avenue, covered in the development section below; the board feed files it under Pacific Douglas while the project's own description places it in Grandview Heights. Confirm the sub-area from the civic address and the disclosure statement, and value the unit on its own strata documents rather than on either neighbourhood label. https://www.livabl.com/surrey-bc/harlowe

- Surrey's 2026 residential tax rate, utility charges and suite levies are city-wide and are stated in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note and the surrey-condos-for-sale note — see the surrey-condos-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates

## 8. GETTING AROUND

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check ALR status parcel by parcel at the north edge in the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR maps, because the plan area is bordered by the reserve and ALR status changes what may be built, subdivided or stratified on a parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- For a rancher strata in the older pocket, confirm current water and sewer servicing for the specific lot: the only servicing statement found is the City's 2007 report that about half the Douglas lands were on the City sanitary system, and that page does not tell a buyer what an individual complex is connected to today. (check current) https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/RPT_2007-L001.pdf
- For The Greens at Douglas, read the registered age bylaw and the exemptions in the strata package: the provincial page explains who a 55+ bylaw may and may not exclude, but it does not reproduce this strata's own bylaw text. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Flood protection work is active on the nearby Nicomekl River system: a provincial release describes dike rehabilitation and raising protecting buildings, agricultural land and parts of 48 Avenue and 184 Street, scheduled for completion in summer 2027 — check the specific complex against the City's flood layers before relying on that work. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026EMCR0038-000983.pdf
