## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which public schools are beside this pocket? **A:** Buckingham Elementary and Burnaby Central Secondary are the public schools beside the pocket. The Province lists Buckingham Elementary (K–7) at 6066 Buckingham Ave and Burnaby Central Secondary (8–12) at 6011 Deer Lake Parkway. Catchments are address-based, so confirm the exact house in the locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** Is Deer Lake SDA School public? **A:** No, Deer Lake SDA School is an independent school. The Province lists it separately from public schools, at 5550 Gilpin St, serving kindergarten to grade 12. Public catchment for a Gilpin Street house still comes from School District 41's address-based locator, not this school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** Can you swim at Deer Lake? **A:** You can launch a canoe or kayak, but the City does not treat Deer Lake as a swimming lake. It states the lake suits secondary contact such as watercraft, not swimming, with launches off Sperling Avenue. Fishing is allowed with a BC freshwater licence. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- **Q:** Can park visitors park on these streets? **A:** No, the City directs park visitors to designated lots only. Its park page states parking is not permitted in residential areas around Deer Lake Park, and that its lots, off Deer Lake, Sperling and Royal Oak avenues, close at 10 pm. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
