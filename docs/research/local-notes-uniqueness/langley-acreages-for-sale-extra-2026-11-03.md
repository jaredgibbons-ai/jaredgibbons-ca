# Langley Acreages — Extra Local Notes
Page: langley-acreages-for-sale (Langley Township; acreages).
## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- This page covers acreages across the whole Township of Langley, not one valley, and the Township is a separate municipality from the City of Langley. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- A parcel's community and neighbourhood plan area is a GeoSource lookup, and the Township warns GeoSource property details are for reference only. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Rural land use designations are their own GeoSource layer, separate from zoning, and both must be read for an acreage. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The Township-wide stock is individual fee-simple acreage parcels; no new named acreage subdivision or bare-land strata was verified across the Township in this pass. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Parcel fabric is checked the same way everywhere in the Township: GeoSource shows approximate lot dimensions, road access and site features before a buyer relies on them. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- What varies by parcel is servicing: small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) servicing rules turn on whether municipal water and sanitary capacity already exists at the lot. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- This note owns the Township-wide subdivision and second-dwelling framework that the langley-farms note does not: subdivision minimums sit in Section 110 of Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 (lot area, frontage and depth). https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- The Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw sets servicing and tree protection requirements for a Township subdivision, as the development applications page states. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Second dwellings outside the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) follow the Township's one-unit-plus-one rule where SSMUH does not apply: one residential unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit (ADU), depending on zoning. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- Inside the ALR, the Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) layer applies on top: principal residence at most 500 square metres (m²), suite only inside that residence, and one additional detached residence only within the ALC size limits. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- SSMUH eligibility is narrow by design: inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to both municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit Oriented Area, and not larger than 4,050 m² (1 acre). https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I subdivide a Langley acreage into two lots? **A:** Only if the resulting lots meet the zone's minimums. The Township puts minimum lot area, frontage and depth in Section 110 of Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, servicing and tree protection in the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw, and — inside the Agricultural Land Reserve — the Agricultural Land Commission must also be satisfied, so all three are checked before an offer firms up. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- **Q:** How many homes can I have on one acreage? **A:** It depends on size and servicing, not on the word acreage. Lots over 4,050 m² are not SSMUH-eligible; a non-eligible lot is limited to one unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit depending on zoning, and an ALR lot is further limited by the Commission's residence and additional-residence rules. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q:** Can I build the second home first and replace the house later? **A:** In the ALR, not by staying in the old house while a new principal residence is built. The Commission states that needs its approval, because two principal residences would exist during construction, and approval can require a covenant to remove the old residence and financial security to ensure removal. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q:** Do I need a development permit as well as a building permit? **A:** Sometimes, yes. Since April 27, 2026 the Township has an Official Community Plan Schedule 4 Development Permit Area for SSMUH form and character, a development permit is required for SSMUH unless an exemption applies, and a building permit is always required for SSMUH buildings. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- A pre-application discussion is the Township's stated first step: a Development Planner gives initial feedback within 30 days if the question is not answered online. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Application fees must be paid within 7 days of acceptance or the application may be cancelled, as the Township states. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- SSMUH lots require a minimum of one parking space per unit, with a minimum of two spaces per lot. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- The BC Building Code allows only one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit in the Township, as the Township's SSMUH page states. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- School catchment for any Township acreage is by street address in School District 35's School Locator Tool. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- Township-wide farm facts are owned by the langley-farms note — see the langley-farms note for that shared frame. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Township tax statements are mailed or emailed by the end of May each year, and a new owner must confirm with their notary or lawyer who pays by the due date. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- Penalties cannot be waived: 5% at midnight July 2 and a further 5% at midnight September 3 on current-year taxes, including an unclaimed home owner grant. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- Land-specific charges here are servicing-driven: where water or sanitary capacity does not exist, upgrades required to service a development are determined under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw and must be completed before service is provided. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) taxable-area list includes the Township of Langley. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- No per-$1,000 residential rate is stated on the Township page read today, so none is quoted — see NOT CONFIRMED. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The Township has one Transit Oriented Area, on 196 Street in Willowbrook, within 800 metres of the proposed Surrey-Langley SkyTrain station, designated by provincial order on May 2, 2025. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- Inside that area the Province sets minimum allowable density of 8–20 storeys by distance tier, which is why Willowbrook acreages face different questions than rural ones. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- Outside Willowbrook, GeoSource is the Township's stated source for road access as a parcel site feature; no rapid transit serves rural Township acreages. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the Environment layer in GeoSource for watercourses and classifications, and the Planning layer for development permit areas, before buying any acreage. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Confirm whether the parcel is in the Agricultural Land Reserve using the regional and provincial check the Township itself directs applicants to make. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Metro Vancouver's Regional Growth Strategy designation also applies to a Township proposal and is part of the Township's own pre-application checklist. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Septic, fill and floodplain approvals can each be separate in the ALR: the Commission lists health authority approval for septic and a Notice of Intent for fill among them. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
