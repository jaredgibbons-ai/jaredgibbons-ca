# Sardis South Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: sardis-south-guide (Sardis South; Guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sardis South has no City-published boundary; it is the southern part of Sardis in the City of Chilliwack, around Tyson Road, South Sumas Road and Watson Road, roughly south of Stevenson Road. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- The whole of Sardis, including Sardis South, sits inside Schedule B5 (Sardis) of the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- Sardis South is a Chilliwack and District Real Estate Board (CADREB) market name, not a Fraser Valley board sub-area, and CADREB publishes statistics board-wide only. https://www.cadreb.com/market/
- Vedder Road is the eastern edge: Sardis East Vedder and Promontory lie east of it, Sardis West Vedder west of it, and Vedder Crossing and Garrison Crossing lie to the south toward the Vedder River. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=51

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- One accessory dwelling unit (ADU) is the limit per lot: a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 square metres and 40 percent of the building's habitable floor space, and a garden suite is capped at 100 square metres, under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) allows 3 to 4 units on eligible single-detached and duplex zoned lots over 280 square metres inside the urban growth boundary, under Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3279/FAQ_-_Small-Scale_Housing_-_Updated_2025_07_28.pdf
- New secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level, which limits basement suites on the Vedder River side of Sardis South. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3279/FAQ_-_Small-Scale_Housing_-_Updated_2025_07_28.pdf
- Short-term rentals are a regulated use under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141, not an as-of-right use of a house. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Sardis South address? **A:** Schools are assigned by street address, not by the Sardis South name. The schools physically on this side of Sardis are Tyson Elementary, 45170 South Sumas Road; Watson Elementary, 45305 Watson Road; Mount Slesse Middle School, 5871 Tyson Road; and GW Graham Secondary, 45955 Thomas Road, all in School District No. 33 (SD33). Run the exact address in the SD33 School Locator before relying on any one of them. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q:** Is Sardis South in the floodplain? **A:** The southern lots sit toward the Vedder River, so check the specific lot. Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets building setbacks of 75 metres from the natural boundary of the Vedder River and requires new floor elevation at the flood construction level plus 0.6 metres of freeboard. The City publishes floodplain maps showing the level for each area. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- **Q:** Can I add both a suite and a coach house? **A:** No, not as two separate accessory units. Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 allows only one accessory dwelling unit on a lot, in the form of a secondary suite, a coach house or a garden suite. The separate small-scale multi-unit housing rules can allow 3 to 4 total units on an eligible lot inside the urban growth boundary, which is a different approval path from the one-ADU rule. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q:** What is Watson Glen Park? **A:** It is the main City park in Sardis South, at Cumberland and Tyson Avenues beside the Twin Rinks Arena. The City lists it at 11.12 hectares with a 2.0 kilometre perimeter trail. The Sardis Sports Complex at 5725 Tyson Road and the Sardis Library at 5819 Tyson Road are on the same Tyson Road corridor, within the same part of Sardis. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=153&backTo=%26dowhat%3DamenityView%26paID%3D51%26plID%3D121

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- Sardis Library, 5819 Tyson Road, is a Fraser Valley Regional Library branch, not a City library. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations
- Sardis Sports Complex is at 5725 Tyson Road, on the City's facility list, a short walk from Mount Slesse Middle School at 5871 Tyson Road. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=37&prshow=details&prID=409

- The Vedder River dike and the river's alluvial fan sit south of the neighbourhood; flood construction levels are lot-specific, not street-specific. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the lot against the City's floodplain maps under Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519 before subjects: the bylaw sets a 75-metre setback from the Vedder River's natural boundary and flood construction levels vary by area. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- Lots near creeks fall under Development Permit Area 3, which protects riparian areas and fish habitat; a development permit can be required before building. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- The floodplain map tells a buyer the flood construction level for the area; it does not tell them the finished floor elevation of an existing house, which needs a survey or City file check. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
