# Silver Valley Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: silver-valley (Silver Valley; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Silver Valley is a City of Maple Ridge neighbourhood governed by its own area plan inside the Official Community Plan (OCP). https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/silver?bidId=
- The City publishes no street-level boundary for the board sub-area, so no exact boundary line is stated here. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan
- The plan frames Silver Valley as a gateway to the Malcolm Knapp Research Forest and Golden Ears Park. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/Attachment-A2-Silver-Valley-Area-Plan-Clean-Copy.pdf
- Kanaka Creek runs along the area's south and east side, with Kanaka Creek Regional Park following the creek corridor. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park-locations

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Silver Valley Area Plan was modernised by an amending bylaw adopted November 25, 2025, replacing the 2002 plan structure with simplified designations and a zoning matrix. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/silver?bidId=
- Properties in the wildfire interface need a Wildfire Development Permit, or a restrictive covenant with wildfire construction requirements where an exemption applies. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/wildfire-development-permit-construction-requirements
- Wildfire rules require a minimum 10-metre setback from the top of ridgelines, cliffs or ravines unless a hazard assessment justifies a change. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/dp-checklist-wildfire201703031133332241
- Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 sets parcel rules; rural zones in the bylaw schedule include RS-2 at 0.4 hectares and RS-3 at 0.8 or 2.0 hectares depending on servicing. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Silver Valley on a current area plan or an old one? **A:** A current one governs it. Council adopted the updated Silver Valley Area Plan on November 25, 2025; the City says the update modernises the 2002 plan, consolidates land-use designations, adds a zoning matrix and adds density required by provincial legislation, so buyers should read the 2025 plan text, not summaries of the old plan. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/silver?bidId=
- **Q:** Does the wildfire permit area change how a house must be built? **A:** Yes, it can change materials and landscaping. The City requires a development permit for development in mapped wildfire risk areas, and its construction sheet requires ember protection on vents and ignition-resistant treatment for decks, balconies and overhangs; some small projects proceed only with a covenant recording those requirements instead. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/wildfire-development-permit-construction-requirements
- **Q:** Which schools serve Silver Valley? **A:** Catchments are address-based, so confirm the exact address first. The schools named for the area in district records are Yennadon Elementary at 23347 128 Avenue and Garibaldi Secondary at 24789 Dewdney Trunk Road, in School District 42 (SD42), which runs elementary Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary Grades 8 to 12 with no middle tier; no Silver Valley locator result is on record. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q:** Is there a school bus for Silver Valley secondary students? **A:** No regular district bus serves that trip now. SD42 lists regular transportation only for named catchments including Whonnock Elementary and Garibaldi Secondary excluding students residing in the Silver Valley/Rockridge area, and the district states those Silver Valley routes were discontinued in the 2025/26 budget for low ridership and available public transit alternatives. https://www.sd42.ca/student-transportation/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The district bus change is the detail most families miss: Silver Valley/Rockridge Garibaldi students are expressly excluded from regular SD42 transportation. https://www.sd42.ca/student-transportation/
- Kanaka Creek Regional Park is a Metro Vancouver regional park, not a City park, so its rules and hours come from Metro Vancouver. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park-locations
- The 2025 plan update was part of a citywide package aligning the OCP and the Town Centre, Hammond and Silver Valley area plans with provincial housing legislation. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-ocp-and-area-plan-updates-increase-housing-and-align-legislation
- A full OCP review is planned by 2030 using 2026 Census data, so current designations are not the final word for undeveloped parcels. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Area-specific cost here is servicing type, not a different tax rate: metered customers moved to seasonal water rates on January 1, 2026, with lower rates outside May to September and a lower fixed base rate. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/changes-coming-metered-water-and-sewer-fees-maple-ridge
- Most Maple Ridge utility charges are billed on the annual property tax notice, while metered water and sewer customers receive a quarterly metered utility bill. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/utilities

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- There is no rail station in Silver Valley; the city's West Coast Express stations are Maple Meadows and Port Haney, reached by road or bus. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Haney Place Exchange is the city's bus hub, with the R3 Lougheed Highway RapidBus to Coquitlam Central Station — see the northwest-maple-ridge note for the west-side station detail. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/bus-rapid-transit
- TransLink's Langley–Haney Place bus rapid transit project is in detailed planning at 22 kilometres between Willowbrook and Haney Place, with designs, costs and timelines not yet determined. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/bus-rapid-transit

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the wildfire map first: the City's Wildfire Development Permit Area map decides whether a permit or covenant applies to the lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/wildfire-development-permit-construction-requirements
- Check creek setbacks second: the City's floodplain bylaw designates land near watercourses as floodplain and sets floodplain setbacks, with the greatest applicable setback governing. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
- The floodplain open-house material names steep creeks such as Kanaka Creek as a fast-water and sediment hazard during heavy rain — relevant to ravine lots here. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/Floodplain-Bylaw-Open-House-36-x-48-in%29.pdf
