# Willingdon Heights Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: willingdon-heights-burnaby (Willingdon Heights; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Willingdon Heights is the residential grid around Willingdon Avenue south of Hastings Street, and Heritage Burnaby describes it as a primarily residential commuter neighbourhood shaped by its closeness to the Trans-Canada Highway, Lougheed Highway and Hastings Street. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?objectType_facet=Architectural%20Drawing&objectType_facet=History%20Links&ps=50&q=&sort=date_sort%20asc&decade_facet=2000&decade_facet=1850&onlineMediaType_facet=Image&p=1
- Willingdon Heights Park, at 1491 Carleton Avenue, is the in-area park, and its outdoor fitness circuit is one of the few City-listed facilities inside the grid itself. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/outdoor-fitness-circuits
- Kitchener Elementary, at 1351 Gilmore Avenue, serves the area's west side; Burnaby North Secondary, at 751 Hammarskjold Drive, is the secondary school northeast of the grid. https://kitchener.burnabyschools.ca/
- The Brentwood Town Centre lands at the northeast corner of Lougheed Highway and Willingdon Avenue form the area's southern commercial edge. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-town-centre-plan-amendments

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is detached houses on the Carleton Avenue, Gilmore Avenue and Parker Street grid, with the Brentwood towers rising at the Lougheed Highway edge — two housing worlds on one street pattern. Heritage Burnaby's record frames the area as primarily residential and commuter-oriented. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?objectType_facet=Architectural%20Drawing&objectType_facet=History%20Links&ps=50&q=&sort=date_sort%20asc&decade_facet=2000&decade_facet=1850&onlineMediaType_facet=Image&p=1
- Willingdon Heights Park recently gained new basketball courts under the City's park reinvestment program, alongside its vintage outdoor bodyweight fitness stations — push-up bars, parallel bars and chin-up bars. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2026-04-23/burnaby-building-our-legacy-become-canadas-competition-capital
- Confederation Park, a short way northwest, is the area's full-service park: 3 tennis courts, 10 pickleball courts and the covered Sohen Gill Sports Box. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/outdoor-courts

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Lots near Brentwood Town Centre Station sit in a Frequent Transit Network Area pattern: the City states that within those areas in the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District, up to 6 dwelling units may be built on lots of 281 square metres or more, and off-street parking is optional. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- The Brentwood Town Centre Development Plan (1996) is the City's community plan for the town centre at this area's south edge, and Council authorised a consultation in January 2024 to amend it and the Brentwood Site Conceptual Master Plan (adopted 2013). https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-town-centre-plan-amendments
- Where the 1996 plan and the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) disagree, the OCP's land-use map now takes precedence — the City's community plans page states this applies city-wide since the OCP's adoption on December 9, 2025. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- Hastings Street storefront properties here can also carry the Hastings Business Improvement Area (BIA) local service tax, which the Burnaby Rates Bylaw 2026 levies under section 216 of the Community Charter. https://bylaws.burnaby.ca/media/14000/14814.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What changed on Willingdon Avenue transit in fall 2026? **A:** The R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus was extended from Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station on September 7, 2026, running via Hastings Street and Willingdon Avenue past this area. TransLink states it runs 5 a.m. to 2 a.m. daily, every six to seven minutes at peak, and it replaced the peak-only 222 Willingdon Express, which was discontinued. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- **Q:** Is the 130 bus still the local option on Willingdon? **A:** Yes, with a change: TransLink's 2026 fall service changes keep the 130 providing local service on Willingdon Avenue, but most trips now start or end at Kootenay Loop, with only select weekday peak trips continuing to Phibbs Exchange. Its timetable runs from Phibbs Exchange via Hastings Street, Willingdon Avenue (Brentwood Station) and Central Boulevard to Metrotown Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt130.pdf
- **Q:** Why is there talk of an overpass near Brentwood? **A:** The City is studying a pedestrian and cyclist overpass across the active railway in Brentwood, at five candidate locations from the Brentwood Town Centre Development Plan: Madison Avenue, Alaska Street, Willingdon Avenue, Beta Avenue and Douglas Road. Phase 1 is complete and Phase 2 design feedback is scheduled for late 2026, so no crossing is built yet. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-pedestrian-and-cyclist-overpass-feasibility-study
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Carleton Avenue home? **A:** Kitchener Elementary at 1351 Gilmore Avenue is the west-side elementary, and Burnaby North Secondary at 751 Hammarskjold Drive is the area's secondary school building. Burnaby catchments are assigned by civic address through SD41's School Locator, and this grid sits near catchment edges, so verify the exact address rather than assuming the nearest building — especially for homes east of Willingdon Avenue. https://kitchener.burnabyschools.ca/
Read: 2026-11-03

- A new Confederation Park Community Centre is in planning northwest of the area: the City describes it as a new centre within Confederation Park to meet growing recreation needs in northwest Burnaby, with the project page updated October 27, 2026. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/projects
- The railway at the area's south cuts the grid off from Brentwood Town Centre on foot — that severance is the whole reason the City is studying the five overpass locations named in section 4. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-pedestrian-and-cyclist-overpass-feasibility-study
- The R2 extension brought eight new bus stops and Expo and Millennium Line connections to the Willingdon corridor, per TransLink's launch summary. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- The active railway south of the grid is the named constraint: it is why the City is studying overpasses, and a buyer on the streets nearest it (Parker Street, Georgia Street) should expect train noise that no City page quantifies — the overpass study page discusses crossings, not noise levels for individual lots. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-pedestrian-and-cyclist-overpass-feasibility-study
- Plan-change risk is live here: the Brentwood plan amendments could redesignate the Willingdon-and-Lougheed parcel to mixed-use residential and commercial, changing what can be built at the area's south edge — check the current plan status before pricing a lot on development potential. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-town-centre-plan-amendments
- Still Creek runs along the area's southeast near Brentwood Station; lots toward Still Creek Drive should be screened on the City's Riparian Management Areas layer for a Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area before planning an addition. https://gis.burnaby.ca/arcgis/rest/services/Other/BBOT/MapServer/25
