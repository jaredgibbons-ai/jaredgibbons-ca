## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Are Ladner's new apartment buildings rentals or strata condos?
**A:** Both current corridor projects are rental, not strata. Ladner Willows at 5625 Ladner Trunk Road is a 146-unit, five-storey non-market rental for Red Door Housing Society, and Ladner Landing II at 5737 Ladner Trunk Road is 78 market rental units. Strata buyers shop resale buildings. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009635

**Q:** What should I read before buying a strata condo in Ladner?
**A:** Read the strata bylaws, rules, fees and any special levies before removing subjects, per the Province of British Columbia's strata buying guidance. Ladner's older low-rise buildings sit near the floodplain village core, so the depreciation report shows how the strata plans for major work. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners

**Q:** Which secondary school serves a Ladner condo?
**A:** Delta Secondary School at 4615 51 Street is Ladner's secondary school, and School District No. 37 (Delta) catchments are address-based. Run the exact condo address in the School Locator, because elementary catchments change by block across Ladner, street by street. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/

**Q:** Is there a hospital near Ladner's condo buildings?
**A:** Yes, Delta Hospital at 5800 Mountain View Boulevard is in Ladner, with a 24/7 emergency department. Fraser Health states Ladner Exchange is next door and parking is free, with some parking redirected since May 12, 2025 during Beedie Long-term Care Centre construction. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
