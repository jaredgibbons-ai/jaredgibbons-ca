## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Short-term rentals are not a permitted use under Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 anywhere in Pitt Meadows, so an Osprey Village townhome cannot be used as a short-term rental even if its strata bylaws are silent. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- Long-term rental caps in strata bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022, which is a different rule from the City short-term ban above. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- Every strata with five or more lots, including the Osprey Village complexes, must now obtain a depreciation report every five years with no deferral vote. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there a condo apartment I can buy in South Meadows? **A:** No apartment-condo building was verified in South Meadows, so a buyer searching this page is really shopping townhomes. The verified attached form is the Osprey Village townhome cluster on Fraser Way, Sutton Avenue and Barnston View Road, headed by Nature's Walk on the developer's own page. Check the mid-meadows note for the full cluster file before assuming a South Meadows listing label means a different complex. https://onni.com/blog/introducing-natures-walk/
- **Q:** Can I run an Airbnb from an Osprey Village townhome? **A:** No, because the City prohibition applies city-wide, not complex by complex. A staff report to Council states short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use under the Zoning Bylaw, and the Province's principal-residence requirement also lists Pitt Meadows. A strata that does not mention short-term rentals does not override either layer, so underwrite the home as a residence or a long-term rental only. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves a Bonson Road address? **A:** Check the exact address, because no public school building sits inside South Meadows itself. School District 42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows) runs elementary schools at kindergarten to grade 7 and one Pitt Meadows secondary school, Pitt Meadows Secondary at 19438 116B Avenue, for grades 8 to 12. Edith McDermott Elementary at 12178 Bonson Road and Davie Jones Elementary at 12030 Blakely Road are the Bonson-area schools listed for the district. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- **Q:** Is this area behind a dike? **A:** Yes, South Meadows sits behind the Fraser River dike in the City's urban drainage area, and Shoreline Park itself sits on the dike. The City's Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 sets an Urban Floodplain designated flood level of 5.15 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum. Ask the City for the flood construction level covenant on the specific strata lot's title before removing subjects. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 - Consolidated.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Pitt Meadows charges a separate drainage rate on top of its general municipal rate, and both are set in the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- The City states taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5 percent after August 1, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- The City collects property taxes and utility fees for water, drainage, diking, sewer, solid waste and green waste in one combined bill. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/Tax-insert-2026.pdf
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies in the City of Pitt Meadows, which the Province lists as a taxable area in the Metro Vancouver Regional District, so every residential owner must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- Permit and licence fee figures for a specific suite or renovation: see the City fee schedule linked from the taxes and development pages — no fee figure is stated in the sources read for this note. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/Tax-insert-2026.pdf

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- No West Coast Express station sits in South Meadows; the station for this area is Pitt Meadows Station on Harris Road, reached by the 719/722 Bonson Road loop. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain
- Route 701 runs on Hammond Road at the north edge toward Coquitlam Central Station and Haney Place. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 first: its Schedule B Urban Floodplain designated flood level is 5.15 metres, and Schedules A and B map which floodplain covers a lot. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 - Consolidated.pdf
- The Drainage System Protection Bylaw No. 2266, 2007 requires a City permit before building within seven metres of the toe of a dike or six metres of the top of bank of a key ditch. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2023-04/Drainage%20System%20Protection%20Bylaw%20No.%202266%2C%202007.pdf
- Most of Pitt Meadows outside the urban core is Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) land; Osprey Village itself is urban, but lots backing onto farm parcels to the east sit at the ALR edge — confirm the boundary on the City's official community plan (OCP) maps. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- Strata lots here are on municipal sewer; a septic system on a South Meadows strata lot was not stated in any source read — treat septic claims as UNCONFIRMED.
