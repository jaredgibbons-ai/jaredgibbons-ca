# Morgan Crossing Homes for Sale — Extra Local Notes
Page: morgan-crossing-homes-for-sale (Morgan Crossing, South Surrey; Homes for Sale).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Morgan Crossing is a retail village, not the Morgan Creek golf community. It sits at 24 Avenue and Croydon Drive in Grandview Heights, South Surrey, with homes above and beside the shops on Croydon Drive and 26 Avenue addresses, listed on the board mainly under the Grandview sub-area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
- The wider plan ground is Morgan Heights, Grandview Heights Neighbourhood Concept Plan (NCP) Area 1, bordered by 28 Avenue north, 24 Avenue south, 164 Street east and the Highway 99 corridor plan west, about 80 hectares. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
- The Shops at Morgan Crossing describe themselves as an open-air, pedestrian-friendly urban village with over 60 shops and services — that village is the landmark buyers mean by the name. http://www.theshopsatmorgancrossing.com/

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The homes here are mainly strata condos in low-rise buildings over and beside the village shops. The Morgan Heights plan that framed this ground features a mix of housing types centred on Southridge Private School, and the City states the plan is now largely built out. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
- Grandview Corners and Morgan Crossing together form the commercial core the Orchard Grove plan describes as about 92,900 square metres (one million square feet) of commercial space near 24 Avenue and 168 Street. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OrchardGroveNCPStage2Report2012.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Morgan Heights NCP no longer governs on its own. The City states it was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan update and development is now led by the Official Community Plan (OCP). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
- Surrey-wide strata, suite and small-scale rules are in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. A Morgan Crossing unit is strata, so the building's own bylaws and depreciation report, not the NCP, set rental, pet and parking rules for that unit. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Morgan Crossing the same as Morgan Creek? **A:** No, they are different places. Morgan Crossing is the shops-and-condos village at 24 Avenue and Croydon Drive in Grandview Heights, while Morgan Creek is the separate golf-course community to the north. Listings here fall mainly under the Grandview board sub-area, so do not price or search this village using Morgan Creek results. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves a Morgan Crossing unit? **A:** Check the unit's exact address, because two secondaries are named for this cluster. Grandview Heights Secondary at 16987 25 Avenue opened in September 2021 beside the aquatic centre, but individual listings here have also named Semiahmoo Secondary, and no district locator result for a Morgan Crossing address is on record, so run the address in the locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** What recreation is walkable from the village? **A:** Grandview Heights Aquatic Centre is the anchor, at 16855 24 Avenue. The City lists it as a one-stop facility for lane, public and leisure swimming and diving, with a weight room, cafe and outdoor deck, at the corner of 24 Avenue and 168 Street east of the village. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/grandview-heights-aquatic-centre?viewTab=2
- **Q:** Is there rapid transit at Morgan Crossing? **A:** No, there is no SkyTrain or RapidBus station in the village. The practical transit spine is bus service on 24 Avenue, with Highway 99 and the South Surrey Park and Ride handling the regional trip via Route 351 to Bridgeport Station, so confirm the current stop for the building. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt351.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The aquatic centre and the secondary school share one corner, at 24 Avenue and 168 Street, so school and pool traffic peaks overlap on weekday mornings and afternoons. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/grandview-heights-aquatic-centre?viewTab=2
- Elementary schools confirmed for the wider Grandview ground include Pacific Heights Elementary (17148 26 Avenue), Edgewood Elementary (16666 23 Avenue), Sunnyside Elementary (2828 159 Street) and Morgan Elementary (3366 156A Street) — the assigned school depends on the unit address. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- Southridge Private School is the centre the Morgan Heights plan was built around, so independent-school traffic is part of the local pattern separate from the public catchments. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
- The village streetscape is open-air, so a unit over Croydon Drive faces shop, restaurant and parking activity rather than a closed mall — check the unit's floor and outlook against the village plan for that building. http://www.theshopsatmorgancrossing.com/
- Grandview Heights was guided first by the 2005 General Land Use Plan, so building ages cluster in planned phases rather than spreading evenly by street. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
