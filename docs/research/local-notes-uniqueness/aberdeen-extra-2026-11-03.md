# Aberdeen Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: aberdeen (Aberdeen, Abbotsford; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Aberdeen is the rural community around Bradner Road, south of Fraser Highway and north of the Abbotsford International Airport (YXX) lands. https://aberdeen.abbyschools.ca/
- Aberdeen is an official City name as well as a board name: it is profile area 6, Aberdeen, in the City's 2020 Demographic Profiles, separate from Bradner – Mt. Lehman and West Abbotsford. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2020%20McKee%20Profile%20-%20spreads%20final.pdf
- Bradner Road runs north–south through the area, Fraser Highway forms the north edge, and Huntingdon Road and King Road run along the south toward YXX. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- Bradner and Mt. Lehman lie to the west and northwest, Abbotsford West and Clearbrook to the east, and Poplar to the northeast. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- There is no Aberdeen neighbourhood plan; Abbotsforward 2050 (Bylaw 2600-2016) governs. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Housing here is overwhelmingly single detached homes on rural and agricultural lots, not strata product. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- Lot scale is agricultural: the minimum subdivision lot area is 8.0 hectares (ha) in the Agricultural One Zone (A1) and 16.0 ha in the Agricultural Two Zone (A2). https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- Homes are effectively all freehold farm and rural residential lots. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- One townhome complex is confirmed at the western edge by the Aldergrove border: Lantern Park, 27640 Lantern Avenue, built by the Reimer Group. https://www.readkong.com/page/built-27640-lantern-avenue-aldergrove-abbotsford-8573656
- Rural lots outside the urban boundary may use wells and onsite sewage rather than community water and sewer. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Aberdeen angle on the shared city facts is farm zoning and the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR); see the central-abbotsford-guide note for the full urban bylaw, tax and utility facts. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- In the A1 zone, one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit is permitted per lot on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, to a maximum of 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, plus one secondary suite per lot to a maximum of 100 m². https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- That farm dwelling permission applies in accordance with the ALR Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the Agricultural Land Commission. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- Land inside the Abbotsford Airport Zoning Regulations (AZR), SOR/83-253, shown on City drawing M-500, is subject to height limits, and NAV CANADA and Transport Canada approvals are required before the City issues a building permit. https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/sor-83-253/FullText.html?wbdisable=true
- Floodplain boundaries for any lot are set by Zoning Bylaw Schedule E and must be checked for that lot. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is inside Aberdeen? **A:** Aberdeen Elementary at 2975 Bradner Road is the public elementary school inside the area. A locator run for that address returns Aberdeen Elementary, then Eugene Reimer Middle and Rick Hansen Secondary, but any other address still needs its own locator run under Policy 18. https://aberdeen.abbyschools.ca/
- **Q:** Can a farm lot be subdivided into small lots? **A:** No, subdivision minimums are agricultural in scale. A1 lots subdivide at a minimum of 8.0 ha and A2 lots at 16.0 ha, so future subdivision value is limited by the zone rather than by frontage alone. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **Q:** Can a second home be added for family or workers? **A:** One Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit may be permitted on a qualifying lot. The A1 section requires a lot of at least 0.4 ha and caps the unit at 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less, with ALR rules applying where the lot is in the reserve. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Q:** Does the airport limit building height here? **A:** Yes, on land inside the AZR area on City drawing M-500. Height may not exceed the approach, outer or transitional surfaces, and federal approvals are needed before a City permit, which matters for tall shops and barns. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- **Q:** Is there a bus through the interior roads? **A:** No interior route was verified. BC Transit route 21 (Aldergrove/Abbotsford) runs on Fraser Highway at the north edge toward Bourquin Exchange, and the system map shows no route through the interior rural roads. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Aberdeen Elementary is the only public school confirmed inside the area, at 2975 Bradner Road. https://aberdeen.abbyschools.ca/
- No City park inside Aberdeen was verified by name and address; the nearest verified facilities are Matsqui Recreation Centre at 3106 Clearbrook Road and Clearbrook Park at 3680 Clearbrook Road, both to the east. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds
- Mt. Lehman Library at 5875 Mt. Lehman Road is to the northwest and Clearbrook Library at 32320 George Ferguson Way to the east. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/
- No shopping centre or grocery store inside Aberdeen was verified on an official source; Highstreet Shopping Centre at Mt. Lehman Road is to the northwest. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/projects-go/highstreet-transit-exchange
- Well records are held in the provincial GWELLS database, and onsite sewage filings are made through Fraser Health by an Authorized Person. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Confirm ALR status and the lot's zone in the City WebMap before offering, because both are lot-specific. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- Check whether the lot falls inside the AZR area on City drawing M-500 before planning any tall building. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- Pull the well record in GWELLS and confirm the septic filing under the Sewerage System Regulation for any rural lot. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- Check floodplain status against Zoning Bylaw Schedule E for the specific lot. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
