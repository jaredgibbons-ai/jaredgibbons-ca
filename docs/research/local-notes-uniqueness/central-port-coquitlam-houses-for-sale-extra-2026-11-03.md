# Central Port Coquitlam Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: central-port-coquitlam-houses-for-sale (Central Port Coquitlam; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Central Port Coquitlam is the downtown and central area around Shaughnessy Street and Wilson Avenue, between the Coquitlam River to the west and Mary Hill to the east; the City publishes no surveyed boundary for this board sub-area. https://portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Port Coquitlam Station at 2125 Kingsway Avenue and the Port Coquitlam Community Centre at 2150 Wilson Avenue anchor the core; Shaughnessy Street is the main north–south downtown corridor. https://www.translink.ca/parkandride?utm_source=direct&utm_medium=shorturl&utm_campaign=parkandride
- The Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw, 2013, No. 3838 identifies Downtown as a mixed-use neighbourhood inside this central area, distinct from the established neighbourhoods named in the plan. https://portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached houses here are the older central side-street stock off Shaughnessy Street, Elgin Avenue and Donald Street, sitting beside the downtown apartment zones rather than in a post-war subdivision pattern like Mary Hill. https://portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Apartment zones RA1 and RA2 in Zoning Bylaw No. 3630 are concentrated downtown and along main roads, so a detached house on a Shaughnessy Street or Kelly Avenue lot can carry apartment zoning, not a house zone. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2025-08/3630%20-%20Zoning%20Bylaw%202008.pdf
- Detached houses are freehold; the zone and Official Community Plan designation for a central lot must be checked parcel by parcel in PoCoMAP, because house and apartment designations meet street by street downtown. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/reports-plans-publications/official-community-plan

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- For the city-wide zoning bylaw, small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH), tax and utility position, see the mary-hill-port-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; this note adds only the central-area rules below. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- The area around Port Coquitlam Station is one of the City's Transit-Oriented Areas in the Official Community Plan, so lots near Wilson Avenue and Kingsway Avenue sit under transit-oriented policies, not only house policies. https://portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- RA1 allows a floor area ratio of 1.0, rising where parking is underground or in a structure; RA2 allows up to 2.5, per Zoning Bylaw No. 3630 — confirm the current zone for a Shaughnessy Street lot before assuming house-only use. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2025-08/3630%20-%20Zoning%20Bylaw%202008.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a downtown house on Wilson Avenue? **A:** Central Community Elementary, École Pitt River Middle and École Riverside Secondary serve the sample address 2150 Wilson Avenue. School District No. 43 (SD43) assigns by permanent home address, so run the exact house address in the SD43 School Locator before relying on that trio for any other central street. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** Is a central house in the floodplain? **A:** Possibly — check the lot, not the neighbourhood name. The City states a significant portion of Port Coquitlam, including low central land, lies in the Fraser and Pitt River floodplain behind dike infrastructure, and floodplain maps are published on the City flood page for a property-level check. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/flood
- **Q:** Can a Shaughnessy Street house lot become apartments? **A:** On some lots, yes, because RA1 and RA2 apartment zoning is concentrated downtown and along main roads under Zoning Bylaw No. 3630. That does not make every house lot an apartment site: confirm the zone, the Official Community Plan designation and any development permit area with the City first. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2025-08/3630%20-%20Zoning%20Bylaw%202008.pdf
- **Q:** Does the West Coast Express run all day from here? **A:** No. The West Coast Express is a weekday commuter service, westbound to Waterfront Station in the morning and eastbound in the afternoon, with no weekend or statutory-holiday service. Port Coquitlam Station is in this area, but the timetable, not the station's presence, sets the commute. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf

- The Traboulay PoCo Trail loops through the central riverfront, and ƛ̓éxətəm Regional Park (formerly Colony Farm) sits on the Coquitlam River at the area's west edge. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/recreation-parks/parks-trails/parks-directory?page=1
- Port Coquitlam Station's park-and-ride is listed by TransLink at 267 spaces, open 7 days a week, 4 a.m. to 2 a.m. — useful for a house buyer who will drive to the train, not walk. https://www.translink.ca/parkandride?utm_source=direct&utm_medium=shorturl&utm_campaign=parkandride

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Port Coquitlam Station in this area is a West Coast Express stop between Coquitlam Central Station and Pitt Meadows Station, on the Waterfront Station to Mission City line. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- Routes 173 and 174 serve the north side from Port Coquitlam Station via Wilson Avenue and Shaughnessy Street; route 170 stays on the Mary Hill side and does not serve the downtown house streets. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt174.pdf
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and the Mary Hill Bypass (Highway 7B) cross the city at this area's edges, and the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus runs along Lougheed Highway. https://portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check flood status per lot on the City flood page: Port Coquitlam has two floodplains, the Fraser/Pitt River zone and the Coquitlam River zone, and the City patrols and maintains dikes and pump stations. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/flood
- Check the Watercourse Development Permit position before clearing or building near the Coquitlam River or a ditch: work in a designated watercourse area needs a permit or a City-confirmed exemption. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/development-permits/watercourse-development
- Check Tree Bylaw, 2019, No. 4108 before removing a mature central-lot tree: a cutting permit can be refused in bird nesting season without a biological survey. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/4108-tree-bylawpdf
