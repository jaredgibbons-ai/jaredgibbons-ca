# East Delta Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: east-delta (East Delta, City of Delta; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- East Delta is the farm belt and eastern Ladner fringe of the City of Delta, and the City publishes no boundary for the name. It is a Paragon sub-area name for the rural land east of the Ladner village core, around Ladner Trunk Road, 60B Street and Highway 17A (https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219618/).
- The urban edge was formerly covered by the East Ladner Area Plan, Schedule B.1 under the previous Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 3950; the current OCP, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated the former area plans into a single Land Use Plan (https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219618/).
- Fraser Health describes this ground plainly as the farmlands of East Delta, noting the Good Samaritan Delta View Care Centre is located there (https://www.fraserhealth.ca/your-community/delta/locations-in-delta).
- Ladner's village sub-areas (Hawthorne, Holly, Neilsen Grove, Port Guichon) are west; North Delta is across the farm belt to the north-east; Tsawwassen is south-west (https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219618/).

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is farm houses on agricultural land, with one farm house per lot under the A1 Agriculture zone of Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf).
- On the urban East Ladner edge, housing takes the Ladner RS and RD zones, and one townhome project is recorded on a developer source only: Skye at Holly Park, 4760 60B Street (https://skyehomes.ca/).
- The one large institution in the farmland is the Good Samaritan Delta View Care Centre, a long-term care home at 9321 Burns Drive, Delta, V4K 3N3 (https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/73617/).
- No condo building with a street address in East Delta was confirmed on an official source — see NOT CONFIRMED (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- This note carries the Delta farm-zoning and Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) rules in full for the cluster; city-wide zoning, tax and dike facts are in the cliff-drive note — see the cliff-drive note.
- In the A1 zone, subdivision is permitted only where it complies with the OCP and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 hectares, with a home site severance exception under Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) policy (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf).
- The A2 zone also sets an 8-hectare minimum for a lot created by subdivision, and the A3 zone does not permit subdivision (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A2.pdf).
- ALC housing rules cap an ALR parcel at one principal residence of at most 500 m² built without an ALC application, a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, and one additional detached residence of up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 hectares or less or 186 m² on larger parcels — anything beyond that needs ALC approval (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- Delta's A1 zone mirrors those limits: a farm house maximum of 500 m², a farm home plate maximum of 3,600 m², one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit within the same 90 m² / 186 m² size limits (https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf).
- Farm land here is designated "Agriculture (A)" in the OCP, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), whose Agriculture policies (OCP s. 2.6) apply to Delta's agricultural land (https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is East Delta an official neighbourhood?
**A:** No — the City of Delta plans the city as Ladner, Tsawwassen and North Delta, and "East Delta" is a Paragon sub-area name for the farm area east of Ladner. The farm part is governed by the OCP's municipal-wide Agriculture policies under Bylaw No. 8400 (2024); the urban edge was formerly under the East Ladner Area Plan, Schedule B.1 under the previous Bylaw No. 3950 (https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf).

**Q:** Can a farm parcel here have a second dwelling?
**A:** Yes, within fixed caps — an ALR parcel may carry one principal residence plus one additional detached residence of up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 hectares or less, or 186 m² on larger parcels, and Delta's A1 zone allows one accessory farm dwelling unit within those same limits on the farm home plate. More than that needs Agricultural Land Commission approval (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Q:** What is the smallest new farm lot that can be created?
**A:** Eight hectares — Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 sets the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision in the A1 zone at 8 hectares, subject to Official Community Plan compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, apart from the home site severance exception under ALC policy (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf).

**Q:** Which schools serve East Delta?
**A:** No sample catchment is stated for this sub-area, because School District 37 catchments are address-based and no East Delta address result is published in the program research. Elementary runs Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary Grades 8 to 12 with no middle tier; run the exact address in the district's School Locator before relying on any school in an offer (https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/).

**Q:** How do I check whether a parcel is in the ALR?
**A:** Check it on the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR maps by PID (parcel identifier) or civic address. The ALC states its online tools are for general reference only, with official confirmation available from its Mapping and GIS team — so a map result is a screening step, not a legal confirmation (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Delta's agricultural ditches work two ways: in the farm area the ditches and pump stations run in drainage mode in winter and irrigation mode in summer, when ditch water is used to irrigate crops, with City control gates regulating levels (http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage).
- A farm property's well is searched in the provincial Groundwater Wells and Aquifers (GWELLS) registry, and wells built before the Water Sustainability Act came into force may not appear in it (https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems).
- A building with indoor plumbing that is not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite sewage system complying with the provincial Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person, per Fraser Health (https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems).
- The Boundary Bay Dyke Trail runs from 17A Avenue to Mud Bay in Boundary Bay Regional Park (Metro Vancouver), along the south side of the Delta farm area (https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park).
- No shopping centre inside East Delta was confirmed on an official source; the OCP's Ladner Village Core material places this area's shopping in Ladner Village, west of Chilukthan Slough (https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf).
- Ladner Pioneer Library, 4683 51 Street, is the library serving this farm area (https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations).

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta's 2026 tax increase, due dates, flat rate utility bill and Speculation and Vacancy Tax position are city-wide and are written in full in the cliff-drive note — see the cliff-drive note (https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes).
- The farm-area difference is how water and sewer are billed and supplied: Delta bills water and sewer either on the annual flat rate utility bill or in separate quarterly metered bills, and unserviced farm parcels rely on wells and onsite sewage instead (http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities).

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- No bus route serving the interior farm roads of East Delta was confirmed; the confirmed services run on the area's edges. TransLink route 601 runs via Ladner Exchange, Ladner Trunk Road, Highway 17A and Highway 99 to Bridgeport Station (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf).
- Route 620 (Tsawwassen Ferry – Bridgeport Station) runs via Ladner Exchange, Highway 17A and Highway 17 (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt620.pdf).
- By road, Highway 17A, Ladner Trunk Road (Highway 10) and Highway 99 through the George Massey Tunnel are the main links, with Highway 17 (South Fraser Perimeter Road) to the south (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf). Driving times are UNCONFIRMED.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with ALR status and the housing caps: reserve status controls the 500 m² principal-residence cap and the additional-residence limits, and it is checked parcel by parcel on the ALC maps (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps).
- Check flood protection requirements and streamside rules with the City before development near a regulated water body — Delta's low farm land is diked, and the City lists flood protection among its development checks (https://www.delta.ca/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations).
- Diligence the water and septic: search the well in GWELLS (older wells may be missing) and ask for the Authorized Person's sewerage filings for any onsite system (https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems).
- Check the farm home plate, not just the house: the A1 maximum is 3,600 m² for the residential footprint, which caps where the farm house and accessory buildings can sit (https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf).
