# Northeast Maple Ridge Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: northeast-maple-ridge-guide (Northeast Maple Ridge; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Northeast Maple Ridge here means the rural northeast inside the City of Maple Ridge, Metro Vancouver Regional District: Websters Corners and the east side of Whonnock, Kanaka Creek, and the 256 Street / Thornhill edge, running east to the Maple Ridge–Mission municipal boundary near Dewdney Trunk Road. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The City describes Webster's Corners as near the Maple Ridge–Mission border, just off Dewdney Trunk Road; Whonnock is centred on Whonnock Lake and 112 Avenue. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Thornhill is only the southern edge of this guide — heavily forested, mainly residential, with farms and nurseries. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
Read: 2026-11-03

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The fabric is rural single-detached homes on large lots, farms and nurseries in Thornhill, not suburban subdivisions; the City lists no named subdivision for Websters Corners or Whonnock on its neighbourhoods page. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Silver Valley, north of the Alouette River, is a separate newer growth area — see the silver-valley note; it is not counted as housing stock here. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Secondary suites: one per lot, inside the same building as the single-detached house, maximum 40% of total gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area, under Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH): eligible single-family and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units, subject to zoning, bylaw and building code requirements; rural lots outside serviced areas are not automatically eligible — confirm with Planning. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- Kanaka Creek bisects the east side and is a natural barrier with limited bridge crossings, which shapes riparian setbacks on creek lots. https://facilitiesreview.sd42.ca/east-capital-zone/
Read: 2026-11-03

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is this Silver Valley? **A:** No. Silver Valley sits north of the Alouette River and is a separate guide area. Northeast Maple Ridge here is the older rural east — Websters Corners, Whonnock and the Thornhill edge — with large lots, farms and forest, not the Silver Valley subdivision fabric. Confirm the neighbourhood name on the City map before comparing homes. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- **Q:** Can I add a suite? **A:** One secondary suite per lot is permitted inside the main house, capped at 40% of gross floor area, and suites are not allowed in the Floodplain Area. A building permit is required for new construction or renovation of a suite, and renting both floors triggers a business licence. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q:** Which schools serve the east side? **A:** School District 42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows) lists Webster's Corners Elementary, 25554 Dewdney Trunk Road, Kindergarten to Grade 7, Whonnock Elementary, 27471 112 Avenue, Kindergarten to Grade 7, and Garibaldi Secondary, 24789 Dewdney Trunk Road, Grades 8–12. Check the exact address in the district locator, because Kanaka Creek limits crossings. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- **Q:** Is there a wildfire or creek check? **A:** Yes. Kanaka Creek runs through this area and the School District 42 facilities review calls it a significant natural barrier. For a specific lot, check the City's watercourse and slope maps and title, not the park map — the regional park protects the creek corridor, it does not certify a private lot as buildable or flood-free. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park?TermStoreId=3a6ffa4a-86cb-4ceb-8bd3-9be3deff9313&TermSetId=dbdd44c3-bd06-4e43-b96b-cfedeb17b17a&TermId=e93060f2-4d03-4877-8e6f-229c1fef4b3e

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Kanaka Creek Regional Park: Metro Vancouver's Canyon Trail runs 1.2 km one way from the fish hatchery to Cliff Falls; the Bell-Irving Hatchery and fish fence support chum and coho salmon and cutthroat trout. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park?TermStoreId=3a6ffa4a-86cb-4ceb-8bd3-9be3deff9313&TermSetId=dbdd44c3-bd06-4e43-b96b-cfedeb17b17a&TermId=e93060f2-4d03-4877-8e6f-229c1fef4b3e
- Webster's Corners is an active film-production hub and has mountain-bike trails in the British Columbia Institute of Technology (BCIT) Woodlot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Thornhill has a 256 Street mountain-biking and hiking network of 17 trails, described by the City as moderately difficult and about an hour each. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Whonnock Lake Park has a small sandy beach, swimming area, trails and a new playground; the Ridge Canoe and Kayak Club is based on Whonnock Lake. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- School District 42's East Capital Zone runs from central Maple Ridge to the Mission district boundary and contains six elementary and two secondary schools. https://facilitiesreview.sd42.ca/east-capital-zone/
- Ruskin, at the southeast edge, still has active shake and sawmills along the Stave River and the Ruskin Community Hall. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
Read: 2026-11-03

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City of Maple Ridge 2025 tax-rate summary: municipal general rate for residential (Class 1) is 2.6648 per $1,000 of assessed value, and the provincial school levy for Class 1 is 1.1194 per $1,000 (2025). https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2025-05/2025%20Property%20Tax%20Rates_1.pdf
- For 2025 the City removed the recycling levy after curbside recycling moved to Recycle BC at no cost to residents; sewer rates rose 23.5% and water 4.5% for the average household (2025 budget). http://www.mapleridge.ca/news/2025-budget-invests-community-priorities-while-reducing-tax-rate-increase
- Speculation and Vacancy Tax: the City of Maple Ridge is listed as a designated taxable area, so owners must declare. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- Renting both a main floor and a secondary suite requires a business licence, with a one-time $500 Application Review Fee stated by the City. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
Read: 2026-11-03

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- No West Coast Express station or SkyTrain is in northeast Maple Ridge; the City's transit-oriented planning is centred elsewhere, so driving via Dewdney Trunk Road and Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) is the practical route — travel times UNCONFIRMED. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- Kanaka Creek's limited bridge crossings, noted by School District 42, affect east–west trips inside the area itself. https://facilitiesreview.sd42.ca/east-capital-zone/
Read: 2026-11-03

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Floodplain: secondary suites are prohibited in the Floodplain Area, a signal to check the City floodplain map for the specific lot before assuming a suite or addition is possible. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- Kanaka Creek riparian land: the regional park protects the creek, hatchery and fish fence corridor; a park boundary does not tell a buyer a private lot's setback — get the lot's riparian assessment and title. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park?TermStoreId=3a6ffa4a-86cb-4ceb-8bd3-9be3deff9313&TermSetId=dbdd44c3-bd06-4e43-b96b-cfedeb17b17a&TermId=e93060f2-4d03-4877-8e6f-229c1fef4b3e
- Provincial floodplain maps do not replace local-government floodplain designation and flooding may occur outside mapped areas — stated on the Province's emergency-planning maps page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/drought-flooding-dikes-dams/integrated-flood-hazard-management/governance/flood-hazard-land-use-management/floodplain-mapping/emergency-planning
