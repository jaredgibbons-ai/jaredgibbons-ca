# Hawthorne Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: hawthorne (Hawthorne, Ladner, Delta; homes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Hawthorne is a Ladner pocket in the City of Delta, not a Langley neighbourhood — it is a Paragon sub-area name in Ladner, built around Hawthorne Elementary at 5160 Central Avenue and Hawthorne Park at 5151 56 Street. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/hawthorne-park
- Hawthorne-shared facts are treated in full in the earlier guide note — see the hawthorne-guide note; this note adds street-level detail beyond the guide.
- Hawthorne is not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name — the ground falls under the Delta Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Neilsen Grove is to the north, Delta Manor to the south around Delta Secondary and Memorial Park, and Holly to the east.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Hawthorne's townhouse stock concentrates on one corridor, Central Avenue — Central Park at 4910 Central Avenue and Central Park West at 4887 Central Avenue face each other across the 4900 block, and Keira Gardens sits just east at 4808 Linden Drive.
- Every completed townhouse complex verified for Hawthorne is strata tenure, and no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in the sub-area.
- The detached streets around that corridor are where Delta's 2024 small-scale multi-unit housing changes apply, not the existing strata complexes.
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Delta adopted its Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) zoning on June 24, 2024, in Bylaws 8394 and 8395, allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- The current OCP is a single consolidated plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), with no lettered schedules — the former Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, and farm land in Delta is designated Agriculture (A). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Any property within 30 metres of a stream, slough or ditch in Delta falls in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — a lot-level check for Hawthorne's ditch-lined streets. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Hawthorne an official neighbourhood? **A:** No — Hawthorne is a real-estate board sub-area name, not a City of Delta neighbourhood name. The City plans this part of Ladner under the single consolidated OCP, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), so a buyer searching City documents for a Hawthorne area plan will not find one; the operative documents are the OCP, the zoning map and the lot's own records. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q:** Which schools serve Hawthorne? **A:** The School District 37 (SD37) locator result for the sample address 5160 Central Avenue is Hawthorne Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 7, and Delta Secondary, Grades 8 to 12, at 4615 51 Street. Catchments are address-based, so run the exact home address in the district locator before an offer that depends on either school. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q:** Are Hawthorne townhouses strata or freehold? **A:** Strata — every completed townhouse complex verified on Central Avenue and Linden Drive is strata tenure, and no fee-simple complex was verified in the sub-area. That means strata fees, registered bylaws, a Form B Information Certificate and a depreciation report on every townhouse purchase here; a listing that calls a Hawthorne townhouse fee-simple should be checked against title. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q:** Is Hawthorne in a flood area? **A:** Ladner is low land protected by the City's flood protection system, not open floodplain building. Delta maintains over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, 31 drainage pump stations and roughly 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure — flood construction requirements are still confirmed lot by lot with the City before a rebuild or addition. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Hawthorne Park, 5151 56 Street, carries picnic tables, sports fields, tennis courts and washrooms, per the City's park search — it is the sub-area's main green, directly across from the elementary school catchment anchor. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/hawthorne-park
- Ladner Leisure Centre at 4600 Clarence Taylor Crescent and Ladner Pioneer Library at 4683 51st Street are the civic anchors serving Hawthorne, both south of the pocket in Delta Manor's orbit. http://delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/ladner-leisure-centre
- Delta Hospital at 5800 Mountain View Boulevard sits beside Ladner Exchange, the bus exchange every Hawthorne transit trip starts from. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- There is no Hawthorne row in the standard SnapStats pack — South Delta is not covered, so any quoted Hawthorne median from that source does not exist.

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta's 2026 flat-rate utility total for a single-family home is $1,658, up $51 from $1,607 in 2025, billed annually at the end of February for garbage and recycling with water and sewer on the flat bill or in quarterly metered bills. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Council approved a 2.9 percent property tax increase for 2026 on December 8, 2025 — about $8 per month on the average home assessed at $1.3 million in 2025, plus about $4 per month in utility fees. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-2026-budget-approved-bringing-investments-every
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies in Delta, a designated taxable area, so a Hawthorne owner files the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
Read: 2026-11-03

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 618 is Hawthorne's own loop — it runs from Ladner Exchange via Harvest Drive, Ladner Trunk Road (Highway 10), Central Avenue, Linden Drive, Westminster Avenue and Ferry Road. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt618.pdf
- Route 601 runs from Ladner Exchange to Bridgeport Station on the Canada Line — the rail connection for the whole sub-area, since no rapid transit station sits in Delta. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Ladner Exchange, at Clarence Taylor Crescent and Harvest Drive, is the transfer point for both routes. https://www.translink.ca/rider-guide/driving/park-and-ride

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the dike and drainage question, not the house — confirm the lot's flood construction requirements with the City, because Ladner lots sit behind the dike system described in section 4. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Check whether the lot falls within 30 metres of a stream, slough or ditch, which triggers the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- For any Central Avenue strata purchase, read the depreciation report and the Form B insurance summary — the corridor's complexes date from 2007 to 2011, so roof, envelope and deck items lead the repair list. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
