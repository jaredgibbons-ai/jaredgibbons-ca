# Langley Farms — Extra Local Notes
Page: langley-farms (Township of Langley; farms).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The farm area is township-wide, not one neighbourhood: the Township of Langley covers 316 square kilometres (km²), bounded by the Fraser River to the north and the United States border along 0 Avenue to the south. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- Most farm land sits outside the six urban centres, in the rural area governed by the Rural Plan, Bylaw No. 3250. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- The Fraser Valley Real Estate Board area is Langley; the municipality for these farms is the Township, not the separate City of Langley. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Three-quarters of the Township is farm land by designation: 75 per cent, or 235 km², is in the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR = Agricultural Land Reserve). https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- Parcels are mostly small for farm land: 73 per cent of Township ALR parcels are smaller than 4 hectares (ha), and only 14 per cent are larger than 8 ha, per the Agricultural Viability Strategy (check current). https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/agricultural-viability-strategy.aspx
- The main rural zone is Rural Zone RU-2 in Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, shown in a Council report as an 8.0 ha minimum lot size at 753 – 264 Street. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=26B98BED-94CB-48F1-8BA9-7AC2502E0AA4&folderId=89097C230327150547589611
- Half of all farms in Metro Vancouver are in this Township, per the Township agriculture page. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/agriculture.aspx

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Subdivision, exclusion and non-farm use in the ALR need an Agricultural Land Commission (ALC = Agricultural Land Commission) application: the owner applies in the ALC Portal, the local government reviews it first, and the ALC decides if it is forwarded. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/application-process/
- A principal residence in the ALR is capped at 500 square metres (m²), and an additional residence at 90 m², per the ALC's bylaw referral letter citing the ALR Use Regulation. https://webfiles.islandstrust.bc.ca/islands/local-trust-areas/south-pender/current-projects/Minor%20LUB%20Amendments%20Project/8%20-%20Public%20Hearing%20Binder/05.2_SP-BL-129_Bylaw%20referral_Responses.pdf
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) does not open ALR farm land to multiplexes: ALR use stays under the Agricultural Land Commission Act and the Township rural zones above. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/application-process/
- Normal farm practice is protected: a neighbour aggrieved by odour, noise or dust from a farm business applies to the BC Farm Industry Review Board (BCFIRB) for a normal farm practice determination. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/agriculture-seafood/agricultural-land-and-environment/farm-practices-protection/resolving-farm-concerns-and-complaints

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Can I subdivide a Langley farm into smaller lots?
**A:** Only with ALC approval and only down to the zone minimum. An application goes through the ALC Portal to the Township first, and RU-2 land at 753 – 264 Street carries an 8.0 ha minimum lot size, so most parcels here cannot be split at all. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=26B98BED-94CB-48F1-8BA9-7AC2502E0AA4&folderId=89097C230327150547589611

**Q:** How big a house can I build on ALR land in Langley?
**A:** The principal residence is capped at 500 m² total floor area under the ALR Use Regulation, as the ALC states in its referral letter. A larger house needs a non-adhering residential use application to the ALC, decided after the Township reviews it. https://webfiles.islandstrust.bc.ca/islands/local-trust-areas/south-pender/current-projects/Minor%20LUB%20Amendments%20Project/8%20-%20Public%20Hearing%20Binder/05.2_SP-BL-129_Bylaw%20referral_Responses.pdf

**Q:** Can I run a non-farm business from the farm?
**A:** Not as of right on ALR land. A non-farm use needs an ALC application, filed in the ALC Portal and sent first to the local government, which decides whether to forward it; the ALC then decides. Plan the purchase on farm use only until approval is in hand. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/application-process/

**Q:** Can neighbours stop manure spreading or early-morning machinery?
**A:** Usually not if it is a normal farm practice. The Province's farm practices page explains the Farm Practices Protection (Right to Farm) Act route: concerns go first through an informal process, then formally to BCFIRB, which dismisses the complaint if the practice is normal farm practice. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/agriculture-seafood/agricultural-land-and-environment/farm-practices-protection/resolving-farm-concerns-and-complaints

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Irrigation water is licensed, not automatic: the Water Sustainability Act requires a licence to divert, store or use surface or groundwater for non-domestic use, including agricultural use. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- Irrigation equipment may lawfully operate 24 hours a day in the growing season, per the Province's Farm Practices Reference Guide irrigation factsheet. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/farming-natural-resources-and-industry/agriculture-and-seafood/agricultural-land-and-environment/strengthening-farming/farm-practices/2026/irrigation_2026.pdf
- The Township runs an Ecological Services Initiative with the Langley Sustainable Agriculture Foundation to help farmers bear the cost of keeping waterways and forests clean. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/agriculture.aspx
- Farm questions go to a named Township contact: the Agricultural Planner, at cpp@tol.ca, per the Township agriculture page. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/agriculture.aspx

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The Township's total 2025 tax rate for Class 1 Residential was 3.34910 per $1,000 of assessed value, of which the total municipal rate was 1.87580, per the Township 2025 tax rates sheet. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/property-taxes/document-feed/2025-Tax-Rates.pdf
- Farm land is Class 9 only if it actually produces: BC Assessment states Class 9, Farm requires the land to produce a prescribed amount of qualifying primary agricultural products for sale; farm buildings fall in Class 1. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/understanding-property-classes-and-exemptions?TermStoreId=2e29bc59-558d-448c-9c19-d11b487dbf89&TermSetId=0a8def97-2239-4f44-b0fc-f2f10a0ea2d4&TermId=582130f6-038f-4842-ada7-0166ce03a246
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) taxable areas list includes the Township of Langley, per the Province's taxable areas page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check ALR status parcel by parcel before subjects: the ALC application process page is the entry point, and the Township's own report for 753 – 264 Street confirms ALR status is stated separately from zoning and Rural Plan designation. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=26B98BED-94CB-48F1-8BA9-7AC2502E0AA4&folderId=89097C230327150547589611
- Check the water licence, not just the well: non-domestic groundwater use has required a licence since the Water Sustainability Act came into force on February 29, 2016. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- The ALR map does not grant farm tax class: BC Assessment classifies on actual production for sale, not on ALR inclusion alone. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/understanding-property-classes-and-exemptions?TermStoreId=2e29bc59-558d-448c-9c19-d11b487dbf89&TermSetId=0a8def97-2239-4f44-b0fc-f2f10a0ea2d4&TermId=582130f6-038f-4842-ada7-0166ce03a246
