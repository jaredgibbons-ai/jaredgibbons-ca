# Cloverdale Neighbourhood Guides — Extra Local Notes

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Cloverdale is Surrey's historic town centre, and this hub page covers the whole community rather than one pocket. The City states its western border runs along 160 Street to 48 Avenue as the southern border, Langley is east, and Guildford and Fleetwood are north, separated by 84 Avenue and 76 Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/cloverdale
- The town-centre plan area sits at Highway 10 and Highway 15 (Pacific Highway, 176 Street), the crossing that anchors the historic main street. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
- Inside the hub, the onward pockets are the historic town centre itself, Clayton Heights to the north, and the fairgrounds and hospital ground to the south — use the sibling Cloverdale pages for street-level detail on each. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/cloverdale

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The town-centre plan is being updated, so check the current plan stage before assuming density. A plan endorsed November 18, 2019 is under update after Council endorsed Corporate Report R176 on October 30, 2023 to support the new hospital and BC Cancer Centre, and Council expanded the boundary northward on July 22, 2024. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
- The fairgrounds are planned separately from the town centre. The City is running an independent master planning and feasibility study for the Cloverdale Fairgrounds as a Special Study Area, with its own timelines. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Why does Cloverdale feel different from other Surrey centres? **A:** It was Surrey's historic centre first. The City describes western and railroad character, one of Surrey's most recognizable main streets, the Museum of Surrey, the Surrey Archives in the 1912 Municipal Hall, and many heritage buildings, with commercial growth following its railway connections. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/cloverdale
- **Q:** What happens at the fairgrounds, and when? **A:** The fairgrounds host Canada's second-largest rodeo. The City states the Cloverdale Fairgrounds have hosted the Cloverdale Rodeo and Country Fair since 1938 as a site, with the rodeo held annually since 1945, and venues there include the Agriplex and Alice McKay Building for year-round events. http://surrey.ca/news-events/events/cloverdale-fairgrounds
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves the town centre? **A:** Lord Tweedsmuir Secondary serves the town-centre elementary catchments. District material groups A.J. McLellan, George Greenaway, Surrey Centre, Martha Currie, Cloverdale Traditional and Don Christian elementaries into the Lord Tweedsmuir catchment family, while Clayton feeds Clayton Heights and Salish secondaries, so confirm the exact address. https://placespeak.com/uploads/1484/Cloverdale-Clayton_Catchment_Boundaries_2018-19.pdf
- **Q:** Will SkyTrain reach Cloverdale? **A:** Three stations are planned for Cloverdale on the approved expansion, per the City. The City's Cloverdale page states three new stations are planned with the approved SkyTrain expansion connecting Surrey to Langley, which is the Surrey-Langley extension along Fraser Highway — a planned service, not a current station. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/cloverdale
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Cloverdale hosts the largest Canada Day celebration in Western Canada each year, per the City, with free music, activities and food — expect town-centre closures that day. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/cloverdale
- A.J. McLellan Elementary is at 16545 61 Avenue and Cloverdale Traditional, a Kindergarten to Grade 7 choice program, is at 17857 56 Avenue — two different admission routes in the same hub area. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- Cloverdale Learning Centre, for Grades 10 to 12, is at 5658 176 Street on the main street itself. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- The Surrey Archives sit inside the 1912 Municipal Hall, a working heritage building a buyer can visit to check a town-centre property's history. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/cloverdale
- The Cloverdale Business Improvement Association (BIA) runs the town-centre business directory and annual events, separate from the City. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/cloverdale
- Fair week changes the area: the rodeo and country fair fill the fairgrounds each Victoria Day period, with event traffic on 176 Street and 62 Avenue. http://surrey.ca/news-events/events/cloverdale-fairgrounds

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- New Surrey Hospital and BC Cancer Centre, 5500 180 Street, Fraser Health — 168 inpatient beds, five operating rooms and a 55-space emergency department as described in the City transportation report; STATUS: construction began September 2023, completion anticipated 2030, City report dated December 1, 2025, read 2026-11-03. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025_R264.pdf
- Cloverdale Town Centre Plan update — land use, parks and transportation update supporting the hospital; STATUS: Step 3 developing the update through winter 2026, Step 4 completing spring to summer 2027, City plan page read 2026-11-03. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
- Hospital transportation works, 180 Street and Highway 10 area, City of Surrey and Fraser Health — new west-side multi-use path on 180 Street between 55 Avenue and 56 Avenue and new bikeways to the hospital; STATUS: endorsed December 2, 2025, to be delivered for the 2030 opening, read 2026-11-03. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/city-of-surrey-clears-way-future-hospital-growth

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 342 links Cloverdale west to Newton and east to Langley Centre. TransLink's timetable runs it via Highway 10, 176 Street (Highway 15), 56 Avenue and 203 Street to Langley Centre. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt342.pdf
- Highway 15 (176 Street) is the north-south driving spine through the town centre to the Pacific Highway border crossing, and Highway 10 is the east-west spine to Langley and Delta. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
