# Salmon River Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: salmon-river-houses-for-sale (Salmon River, Township of Langley; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Salmon River is rural Township of Langley: the Salmon River itself flows north to the Fraser River, and the Township's Rural Plan lists it among the river systems draining the municipality. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Community-Neighbourhood-Plans/Community/Rural-Community-Plan-Bylaw-(No.-3250).pdf
- The upland house area is named Salmon River Uplands in Township plans, sitting above the river flats between Fort Langley to the northwest and Murrayville to the south. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Reference-Plans/Fraser-Highway-Employment-Lands-Reference-Plan.pdf
- Williams Park marks the middle of the uplands: 36.5 acres, bounded to the east by 238 Street and to the south by 64 Avenue. http://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/water/water-quality/2021-Parks-WQR.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses here are rural residential on large lots: the Township's Official Community Plan (OCP) policy for Salmon River Uplands says the area shall be maintained for rural residential and agricultural uses. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Community-Neighbourhood-Plans/Community/Rural-Community-Plan-Bylaw-(No.-3250).pdf
- Zoning runs through the Rural (RU) zones in Section 200 of Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, which applies to the whole Township and sets permitted uses, lot coverage and building restrictions parcel by parcel. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- Servicing is part of the stock's character: residents not served by the Township water utility get water from private or community wells, per the Township's water quality report. http://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/water/water-quality/2023-Annual-Water-Quality-Report.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) boundary and the Rural designation do not line up here: the Rural Plan names Salmon River Uplands as an area where the ALR boundary and the OCP designation conflict, to be reviewed with the Agricultural Land Commission. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Community-Neighbourhood-Plans/Community/Rural-Community-Plan-Bylaw-(No.-3250).pdf
- Streamside Protection and Enhancement Areas (SPEAs) apply outside the ALR: Schedule 3 of the OCP sets non-disturbance zones around watercourses, with setback width set by a Qualified Environmental Professional based on the watercourse class. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-permits.aspx
- Flowing artesian wells are a named local hazard: controlling artesian flow is required under the Water Sustainability Act, Sections 52 and 53, and the Groundwater Protection Regulation. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/provincial-advisory-for-control-of-flowing-wells-in-the-township.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is a Salmon River house on a well and septic system?
**A:** Many are: the Township states that residents not serviced by its water utility access water from private or community wells, and its own Salmon River Uplands parks, Williams and Brown, run on dedicated wells with no connection to the municipal system. Ask for the well record and septic file for the specific lot on 240 Street or 64 Avenue, because servicing changes lot by lot. http://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/water/water-quality/2021-Parks-WQR.pdf

**Q:** Can I farm or subdivide ALR land in Salmon River Uplands?
**A:** Not on the strength of the OCP label alone: the Rural Plan records that the ALR boundary and the Rural designation conflict in Salmon River Uplands, and says the boundary should be reconsidered with the British Columbia Agricultural Land Commission. Until that review resolves a parcel, the ALR rules on the title govern what a buyer can do with the land. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Community-Neighbourhood-Plans/Community/Rural-Community-Plan-Bylaw-(No.-3250).pdf

**Q:** Which schools serve Salmon River houses?
**A:** School District No. 35 (Langley), called SD35, runs the area's schools, and its nearest named village schools are in Fort Langley: Fort Langley Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 7, at 8877 Bartlett Street, and Langley Fine Arts School, Kindergarten to Grade 12, at 9096 Trattle Street. Catchments are address-based, so confirm the exact Salmon River address with SD35 before purchase. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/035

**Q:** What watercourses affect building near the Salmon River?
**A:** The Salmon River and its tributaries carry SPEA protection outside the ALR: the setback is a non-disturbance zone whose width a Qualified Environmental Professional sets from the watercourse classification. The Township's development permit page also points buyers to the GeoSource Environment layer, which maps watercourses and classifications lot by lot before an application. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-permits.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Brown Park, 12.9 acres off 240 Street south of 51 Avenue, is in Salmon River Uplands and runs on its own well, not Township water. http://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/water/water-quality/2021-Parks-WQR.pdf
- Williams Park and Brown Park wells are tested weekly for E. coli, coliforms, turbidity, temperature and pH, and twice a year for arsenic and other metals. http://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/water/water-quality/2021-Parks-WQR.pdf
- The Fraser Highway Employment Lands plan area, about 152 hectares (ha) from 228 Street to 240 Street, sits beside Salmon River Uplands and is surrounded by the ALR but includes no ALR land itself. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Reference-Plans/Fraser-Highway-Employment-Lands-Reference-Plan.pdf
- The Nicomekl River, locally named Hatchery Creek in that plan area, flows west through its northern portion in a ravine between Fraser Highway, 232 Street and 236 Street. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Reference-Plans/Fraser-Highway-Employment-Lands-Reference-Plan.pdf
- Best Creek, also called Trigg Creek, is a primary tributary to Murray Creek and supports coho salmon and trout, per the same Township reference plan. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Reference-Plans/Fraser-Highway-Employment-Lands-Reference-Plan.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the aquifer map: the Township's artesian advisory maps areas under potential artesian pressure, and a qualified professional or certified well-driller must advise before drilling. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/provincial-advisory-for-control-of-flowing-wells-in-the-township.aspx
- Check ALR status in GeoSource before any use assumption: the Township's application guide tells buyers to determine whether a property is within the ALR as a first review step. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Check the floodplain and the river setback together: the Rural Plan places a floodplain area south and west of Fort Langley in the same drainage system the Salmon River feeds. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/the-township/resources/plans-reports-strategies/Community-Neighbourhood-Plans/Community/Rural-Community-Plan-Bylaw-(No.-3250).pdf
