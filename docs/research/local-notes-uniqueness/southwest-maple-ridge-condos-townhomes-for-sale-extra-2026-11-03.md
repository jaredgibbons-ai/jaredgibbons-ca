## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Southwest Maple Ridge is a board sub-area name only: the City of Maple Ridge publishes no boundary or plan under that name, and its neighbourhoods page instead lists Port Hammond and Port Haney in this corner of the city. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The official planning frame is Hammond: the Hammond Area Plan describes Hammond as bounded on the west by the City of Pitt Meadows and the Katzie First Nation, on the south by the Fraser River, to the north by Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road commercial areas, and to the east by mainly single-family development. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- Port Haney, at the sub-area's east edge, was historically the steamboat landing and the Canadian Pacific Railway station area, and heritage buildings from around the city were relocated there. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Fraser River Escarpment policy area covers much of the ground an attached project would be built on: it is bounded by 207 Street to the west, 124 Avenue to the north, 224 Street to the east and the escarpment crest along the south, and properties within 300 metres of the crest require a geotechnical report attached to a Section 219 covenant before a building permit. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- Within 100 metres of the crest, a detailed geotechnical report addressing potential seismic slope failure is required, all drainage from new construction must connect to the City storm drainage system, and septic fields are to be avoided in the policy area. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- Floodplain rules bind the riverfront edge: the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 metres geodetic including freeboard, no habitable floor space may sit below that level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- A Hammond Development Permit Area applies to properties within the Hammond Area Plan, including heritage form-and-character review. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- City of Maple Ridge zoning and small-scale multi-unit housing rules in full: see the maple-ridge-condos-for-sale note.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is there a train station near Southwest Maple Ridge townhomes?
**A:** Yes, two, which no other Maple Ridge sub-area in this set matches. Maple Meadows Station, 20010 Dunn Avenue, serves the west side of the sub-area and Port Haney Station, 22300 River Road, sits at its east edge. West Coast Express trains run on weekdays between Waterfront Station in downtown Vancouver and Mission City, so the service is a weekday commuter service, not an all-day or weekend service. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

**Q:** Can a Hammond townhome have a secondary suite?
**A:** Not on a floodplain property. The City's floodplain material states Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation on floodplain properties, and no habitable floor space may be located below the 6.7-metre geodetic flood construction level on affected lots. A townhome strata's own bylaws are a second, separate check, because strata suite and rental rules are registered per complex and no complex-specific bylaw was confirmed for this sub-area in this pass. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0

**Q:** Why do Hammond building permits ask for geotechnical reports?
**A:** Because of the Fraser River Escarpment. The City's escarpment information package states properties within 300 metres of the crest require a geotechnical report attached to a Section 219 covenant before a building permit, and properties within 100 metres require a detailed report addressing potential seismic slope failure. A buyer in a newer attached project here should expect that covenant on title and read it before removing subjects. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package

**Q:** What plan governs new attached housing in Hammond?
**A:** The Hammond Area Plan, s. 10.5 of the Official Community Plan. The City states the plan has been given final reading and adopted, and its amending-bylaw update enables mixed-use, higher-density buildings in the plan area and incorporates the density provincial legislation requires. That adopted plan, not a rezoning rumour, is the document that sets where strata and mixed-use forms can go in Southwest Maple Ridge. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan

**Q:** Which schools serve the Hammond side of the sub-area?
**A:** Three public schools sit inside the sub-area: Hammond Elementary, 11520 203 Street, Maple Ridge Elementary, 20820 River Road, and Westview Secondary, 20905 Wicklund Avenue. School District No. 42 catchments are address-based and it has no middle tier, with elementary Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary Grades 8 to 12, so run the exact complex address in the district locator rather than assuming the nearest school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Hammond Park, 20577 Lorne Avenue, has benches, a playground, a sport court, a tennis court and a pickleball court. https://www.mapleridge.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Hammond-Park-58
- Hammond Community Park is the site of the Larry Walker and Hammond Stadium ballfields, Hammond Hall and the Hammond Outdoor Pool, and the Maple Ridge Golf Course is at 20818 Golf Lane in Hammond. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- Hammond began as Port Hammond Junction: the City records that the Hammond brothers donated land to the Canadian Pacific Railway on the condition the first railway station in Maple Ridge be built in Hammond. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/hammond-1st-reading-report
- No library branch sits in the southwest area: Maple Ridge Public Library is at 130 – 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road in the Town Centre. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/maple-ridge-public-library
- The Golden Ears Way expansion is a live road project on the sub-area's west side: the City states Golden Ears Way will be expanded from two lanes to four between the Highway 7 interchange and 210 Street, with detailed design underway. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/expansion-golden-ears-way-address-major-traffic-bottleneck-maple-ridge

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Escarpment is the first check here: the City's escarpment package maps the policy area between 207 Street, 124 Avenue, 224 Street and the crest, and states the geotechnical and covenant requirements that follow a lot inside it. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- Flood is the second: Hammond fronts the Fraser River to the south, and the City's floodplain material sets the 6.7-metre flood construction level and the covenant that identifies floodplain construction on title. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- Heritage review is the third: a Hammond Development Permit Area applies within the Hammond Area Plan, so exterior changes in older strata or mixed-use buildings can need a permit a newer sub-area would not. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
