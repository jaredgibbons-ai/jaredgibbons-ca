# Chilliwack Proper West — Questions for Jared

## Questions

1. Who buys on the west side of Chilliwack Proper in your deals — first-time buyers in the older grid, investors buying infill and suite potential, or downsizers who want the Landing Leisure Centre and downtown close?
2. What sells fast and what sits — Mountain View heritage houses, narrow-lot infill, or homes near the airport and the industrial side? Where do buyers draw the line on aircraft noise?
3. What surprises buyers — the Mountain View Heritage Conservation Area permit rules on renovations, Flood Construction Levels behind the dike, or how different the streets feel block by block west of downtown?
4. When a buyer compares Chilliwack Proper West with Chilliwack Proper East and South, what do you tell them about schools, the downtown plan area and District 1881?
5. One story: a Proper West deal that taught you something — a heritage house, an infill lot or an airport-side property you now recommend or avoid, and why.

## Shot list

1. Five Corners and District 1881 from Yale Road at Young Road — the downtown edge of this area, heritage façades and the rebuilt core in one frame.
2. Corbould Street at the Chilliwack Landing Leisure Centre, 9145 Corbould St — the pools, skateboard park and Hope River side that anchor the west-side grid.
3. Mountain View heritage streetscape near Gore Avenue and Nowell Street, looking toward the mountains the area is named for — the early-1900s houses in their street setting.
