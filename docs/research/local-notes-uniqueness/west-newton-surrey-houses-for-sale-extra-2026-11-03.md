# West Newton Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: west-newton-surrey-houses-for-sale (West Newton; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- West Newton houses are covered by three City plans, not one boundary: West Newton / Highway 10 is the residential neighbourhood north of Highway 10 and west of King George Boulevard; West Newton North is west of 128 Street and north of 64 Avenue; West Newton South is west of 128 Street and south of 64 Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans
- The City also names a Newton Cultural Commercial District in West Newton at 128 Street and 80 Avenue, which puts the planning area's north reach at 80 Avenue, and the Imagine Scott Road vision covers the Scott Road–72 Avenue corridor on the west side (Scott Road is 120 Street). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans
- Surrey-wide community boundaries are in the city note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The Highway 10 side of West Newton is small-lot infill built from acreage parcels. At 5913 and 5927 128 Street a City report records rezoning from One-Acre Residential Zone (RA) to Single Family Residential (10) Zone (RF-10) and Single Family Residential (13) Zone (RF-13) to subdivide about 0.87 hectares (2.16 acres) into 16 single-family small lots. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7916-0353-00.pdf
- Coach houses are part of that pattern on the east side of 128 Street: the same report describes the lots across 128 Street as single-family small lots with coach houses, zoned for small lots with a lane at 13 units per acre. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7916-0353-00.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Which West Newton plan covers a house changes what can be built on it. The City lists all three West Newton neighbourhood concept plans (NCPs) as approved, and states there are currently no ongoing land use plans in Newton, so a house is judged against its approved NCP plus current zoning, not a plan in progress. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans
- Surrey-wide small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules are in full in the city note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note; the West Newton point is that many Highway 10–edge lots are already RF-10 or RF-13 small lots, or RA acreage awaiting rezoning, so the starting zone differs street by street. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7916-0353-00.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a West Newton house on a big lot a subdivision candidate? **A:** Sometimes, but only through rezoning. The 128 Street example needed an NCP amendment from Proposed Single Family (6 units per acre) to Small Lot (10 units per acre) plus rezoning from RA to RF-10 and RF-13 for 16 lots, so an acreage-sized lot alone does not grant small-lot density. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7916-0353-00.pdf
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves a West Newton house? **A:** It depends which side of the area the house is on. Princess Margaret Secondary at 12870 72 Avenue sits inside West Newton, Frank Hurt Secondary at 13940 77 Avenue serves addresses at the east edge, and Cougar Creek Elementary at 12236 70A Avenue is listed in the Tamanawis Secondary family — confirm the exact address in the district locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** Is there really no East Newton Elementary for the Newton side of Surrey? **A:** Correct — no such school exists. The British Columbia Ministry list for School District 36 (Surrey) contains no East Newton Elementary; West Newton elementaries on that list include Dr. F.D. Sinclair at 7480 128 Street and Strawberry Hill at 7633 124 Street, and any page naming an East Newton Elementary is wrong. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** How do I reach SkyTrain from a West Newton house? **A:** By RapidBus to a station, not by walking to one. TransLink's R6 Scott Road RapidBus runs Scott Road Station to Newton Exchange via Scott Road and 72 Avenue along the west side, and the R1 King George Boulevard RapidBus runs Newton Exchange to Guildford Exchange via King George and Surrey Central stations on the east side. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Newton Athletic Park at 7395 128 Street is inside the area, while the Newton Recreation Centre and Newton Wave Pool at 13730 72 Avenue and Newton Exchange sit just east in Newton Town Centre — the houses west of King George Boulevard use a town-centre hub that is outside their plan area. https://surreylibraries.ca/locations/newton-branch
- Unwin Park at 13313 68 Avenue, at the east edge, has a 12,000-square-foot universally accessible playground, plus an outdoor pool and spray park, per the City's accessibility page. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/accessibility-services
- There are two Surrey Libraries branches serving the area: Newton Branch at 13795 70 Avenue, about 120 metres from the route 321 and 324 stops, and Strawberry Hill Branch at 7399 122 Street inside West Newton. https://surreylibraries.ca/locations/newton-branch
- Cougar Creek runs through the southwest part of the area near Cougar Creek Elementary; a house backing onto the creek can carry streamside setbacks — see section 9. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/surrey-official-community-plan/development-permit-areas

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- West Newton houses pay the City of Surrey residential rate and utility charges set out in full in the city note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note for the 2026 tax rate per $1,000, parcel taxes, flat-rate water and sewer, and the secondary-suite levy. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- The houses-specific cost is on the Highway 10 acreage edge: a lot rezoned from RA to small-lot RF zones, like the 128 Street subdivision, moves through the West Newton / Highway 10 NCP servicing plan, so servicing and development cost charges are set by that plan process, not by the older acreage servicing. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/RPT_2004-C008.pdf

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Two RapidBus lines frame West Newton houses: the R6 on Scott Road and 72 Avenue to the west and the R1 on King George Boulevard to the east, both described by TransLink on its RapidBus projects page. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
- Newton Exchange at 72 Avenue and 137 Street, just east of the area, is the local hub; Surrey Libraries' Newton Branch page lists the routes accessible there as R1, R6, 301, 319, 321, 322, 323, 324, 325, 335, 341, 342, 393, 394 and 864. https://surreylibraries.ca/locations/newton-branch
- The Surrey–Langley SkyTrain extension does not pass through Newton; it starts at King George Station and runs east along Fraser Highway — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check any creek-side or low lot in COSMOS, the City of Surrey Mapping Online System, before an offer. Surrey's Development Permit Areas (DPAs) include streamside protection areas and flood-prone land inside the 200-year floodplain, and a development permit can be required before subdivision, land clearing or tree removal in a DPA. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/surrey-official-community-plan/development-permit-areas
- The flood risk here is downstream, not street-wide: the City's Serpentine and Nicomekl Lowlands page states the Serpentine River's tributary areas include Newton Town Centre and Bear Creek, and that 59 square kilometres of the 334 square kilometre catchment is lowland floodplain close to sea level behind dykes and sea dams — West Newton itself is on the upland side. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
