# Montecito — Questions for Jared

For Jared Gibbons to answer by voice memo. Do not answer these from research — only Jared's deal experience counts.

## Questions
1. Who actually buys in Montecito — local move-up families, or Simon Fraser University (SFU) staff and families coming from outside Burnaby?
2. Does being walkable to Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station actually add value here, or do most buyers still drive everywhere?
3. What sells fast on the Halifax Street and mountain side versus the Lougheed Highway side — and what sits?
4. What surprises buyers most after they move in — the slope, the sports-complex traffic on Kensington Avenue, or something else?
5. Tell one Montecito deal story: a house that priced or sold in a way that taught you something about this pocket.
