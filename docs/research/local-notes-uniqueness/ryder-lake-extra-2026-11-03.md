## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Ryder Lake is an upland community above Promontory, and the City itself numbers it as a neighbourhood. The City's Greenspace Plan 2020 lists Ryder Lake as one of the city's numbered neighbourhoods, neighbourhood 47. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2650/2021-05-28%20GSP%20MASTER%202020%20-%20Urban%20Format.pdf
- The City's Mount Thom Park page places Ryder Lake on the upland slopes around Mount Thom, with park parking areas in Ryder Lake on Forester Road and Churchill Parkway, and on Ross Road. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=92

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Rural estate density is the official description of the housing form here. The former 2040 Official Community Plan (OCP) directed that Ryder Lake's current rural estate density be maintained and that small-acreage rural and suburban residential development stay within the existing zoning designations. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/common/downloads/publication.pdf
- Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) parcels exist inside the area, and the same OCP policy supported appropriate agricultural activities on ALR parcels in Ryder Lake to maintain agricultural production. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/common/downloads/publication.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A hillside development permit is required before subdivision, construction, land clearing or earthworks. Ryder Lake is a designated hillside/upland area under Development Permit Area 2 (DPA2), shown on OCP Schedule E, Map 14, per the City's DP2 Guide. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- A DPA2 application requires a geotechnical report by a registered geotechnical engineer, including a hazard and slope assessment, setbacks and cut/fill considerations, site stability, and drainage and soil conditions; an environmental assessment and a hydrology report may also be required. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- The stated objectives of DPA2 include reducing slope hazards, erosion and flooding, and ensuring development responds to natural terrain and drainage patterns. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- On ALR parcels, the Agricultural Land Commission caps a principal residence at 500 m² total floor area without ALC approval, with one additional residence limited to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there a Ryder Lake elementary school my child would attend in the community? **A:** No — there is no operating public school located in Ryder Lake today. SD33's Long Range Facilities Plan profiles every district school and contains no Ryder Lake school. The district's transportation procedure instead groups the Ryder Lake area for bus routes serving Vedder Elementary, Mount Slesse Middle School and GW Graham Secondary School, which is a bus grouping, not an in-community school. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2022-03/SD33%20LRFP%202021-2025.pdf
- **Q:** Are homes here on City water and sewer? **A:** Do not assume municipal servicing on an upland lot — verify the parcel. Fraser Health states a building not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite sewerage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, and the Province's GWELLS registry is where a well's record is checked. No City page read in this pass confirms municipal water or sewer for a specific Ryder Lake parcel. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q:** Does snow affect the school run from up here? **A:** Yes — Ryder Lake is named in SD33's Transportation Snow Plan. The district's inclement weather guidelines state the snow plan applies to bus students living in higher elevations such as Chilliwack Mountain, Little Mountain, Majuba Hill, Ryder Lake and some areas of Columbia Valley and the Eastern Hillsides, and closures are announced by 6:00 a.m. https://sd33.bc.ca/inclement-weather-school-closure-guidelines
- **Q:** Can I build or clear first and permit later? **A:** No — the DPA2 permit comes before the work. The City's DP2 Guide states a permit is required before subdivision, construction, land clearing or earthworks in a designated hillside/upland area, and the application requires a geotechnical report including a hazard and slope assessment, setbacks, site stability and drainage and soil conditions. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- **Q:** Is there a bus to town? **A:** No BC Transit route serves Ryder Lake. The official Chilliwack route list is routes 51 Vedder, 52 Evans, 53 Fairfield, 54 Promontory, 55 Yarrow–Greendale, 57 Broadway, 58 Tyson, 59 Industrial, 66 Fraser Valley Express, 71 Agassiz-Harrison and 72 Hope, and none goes to Ryder Lake; Route 54 Promontory, below the upland, is the closest listed service. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The City counted only 2.5 ha of neighbourhood park area in Ryder Lake in 2018, in its Greenspace Plan 2020, which also pictures "Ryder Lake Park." https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2650/2021-05-28%20GSP%20MASTER%202020%20-%20Urban%20Format.pdf
- Mount Thom Park is 70 ha on the 484 m Mount Thom, and its Ryder Lake side trails are the Horse Loop, Lookout Loop and Ridgeline trails together at 2.6 km, plus Karver's Trail at 1.3 km connecting Ross Road to the Thom Creek Trail. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=92
- There is no City recreation centre and no library branch in Ryder Lake; the nearest branch is the Sardis Library, 5819 Tyson Road. https://fvrl.bibliocommons.com/locations/SR
- A historic Ryder Lake school building is not a current school: no Ryder Lake site appears in SD33's school profiles, so buyers should not rely on any "Ryder Lake Elementary" name as a current catchment. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2022-03/SD33%20LRFP%202021-2025.pdf

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Driving is the practical access, because no BC Transit route serves the area. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/
- School travel is by SD33 bus for the named grouping, under the district's transportation procedure for the Ryder Lake area: Vedder Elementary, Mount Slesse Middle School and GW Graham Secondary School. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2023-03/611%20AP%20Transportation%20-%20Operating%20Instructions.pdf
- Route 54 Promontory is the closest BC Transit service, ending in the Promontory neighbourhood below Ryder Lake. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Wildfire and slope are permit-level checks here, through DPA2: Schedule D-2 of the OCP states hillside land may be subject to high forest fire risks requiring a Fire Risk Assessment Analysis Report, and land in the area has been identified as having hazards from slope instability and soil erosion. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8292
- Test the well and inspect the septic filing before removing subjects: well records sit in the Province's GWELLS registry, and Fraser Health requires an onsite sewerage system to comply with the Sewerage System Regulation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- Floodplain Bylaw No. 4519 is a valley-floor check, not an upland one: Ryder Lake sits above the floodplain areas the bylaw regulates, so the lot-level hazards here are slope, drainage and fire rather than dike elevation. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
