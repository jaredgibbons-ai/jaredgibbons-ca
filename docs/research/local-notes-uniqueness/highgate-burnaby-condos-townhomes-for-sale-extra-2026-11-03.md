## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Highgate is the Kingsway and Edmonds Street node inside Burnaby's Edmonds Town Centre, in southeast Burnaby. The City's Kingsway + Edmonds master plan site is at the southwest corner of Kingsway and Edmonds, in the heart of the Edmonds Town Centre. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/kingsway-edmonds-conceptual-master-plan
- The Edmonds Community Plan, adopted in March 2025, is the plan that governs growth here over the next several decades. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/edmonds-community-plan
- The plan document describes today's taller building pockets as focused around the Kingsway and Edmonds Street intersection, Highgate Village and west of the Edmonds SkyTrain station. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=80931
- The area is in School District 41's Kingsway South zone, whose secondary school is Byrne Creek Community Secondary. https://burnabyschools.ca/board/trustees/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Edmonds Community Plan is the controlling local plan, officially adopted in March 2025 after a process launched in 2023 with three phases of public consultation. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/edmonds-community-plan
- Future height at the Town Centre node is the highest in Edmonds: the plan material describes the Kingsway and Edmonds node as the community's main civic, cultural and commercial mixed-use hub, with the detailed height ranges set out in the adopted plan document. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=80931
- Byrne Creek Ravine Park land west of the node is a constraint on expansion in that direction: the East and West Edmonds material in the plan treats the ravine and Taylor Park green spaces as continuing parkland. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=80930
- Streamside setbacks under Zoning Bylaw section 6.23 apply near Byrne Creek and its ravine, as they do near any Burnaby stream or ravine. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/construction-and-renovation/green-building-and-land-development/environmental-review-committee

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What is the Hall Towers site across Kingsway, and will it displace tenants? **A:** It is a City and BC Housing redevelopment of publicly owned land, and the City states it will replace the aging Hall Towers buildings, with 331 units, without displacing tenants. The conceptual master plan proposes over 1,400 purpose-built rental units and approximately 1,000 market strata units on the 4.5-acre site, plus a public plaza, so Highgate buyers should expect a long construction period at the node. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/kingsway-edmonds-conceptual-master-plan
- **Q:** Which SkyTrain station serves Highgate? **A:** Edmonds Station on the Expo Line is the station for this area. TransLink lists Edmonds among the Expo Line stations between Royal Oak and 22nd Street, and the Expo Line connects downtown Vancouver with Burnaby, New Westminster and Surrey; there is no Millennium Line station at Highgate itself. http://retrosearch.org/browse?url=https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/skytrain
- **Q:** Did the new Edmonds plan change what can be built beside existing Highgate towers? **A:** Yes, the plan reset the long-term height framework in March 2025. The City states the adopted Edmonds Community Plan will manage growth in Edmonds over the next several decades, and its height material concentrates the tallest forms at the Town Centre, Station and Southgate nodes rather than across the whole neighbourhood. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/edmonds-community-plan
- **Q:** Which schools are in this zone? **A:** This is the district's Kingsway South zone, led by Byrne Creek Community Secondary. The Board's zone list places Byrne Creek with Edmonds Community, Stride Avenue Community and Taylor Park schools in Kingsway South; a specific Highgate address still needs an address-level catchment confirmation from the district, which was not read for this note. https://burnabyschools.ca/board/trustees/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- The master plan advanced to a public hearing on May 30, 2023 and received second reading on June 5, 2023, and the City states next steps will be aligned with the new Official Community Plan. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/kingsway-edmonds-conceptual-master-plan

- The City provides weekly recycling collection for apartments, condos and townhomes that receive City service, arranged through the Solid Waste and Recycling Division. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/recycling-and-garbage/apartments-condos-and-townhouses
