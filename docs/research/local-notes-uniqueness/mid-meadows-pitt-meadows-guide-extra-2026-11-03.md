# Mid Meadows Pitt Meadows Guide — Extra Local Notes

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Mid Meadows is a board sub-area name; the City's own labels for this ground are North Bonson, South Bonson and Osprey Village, along Bonson Road south of the Canadian Pacific Kansas City (CPKC) rail line — see the mid-meadows-houses-for-sale note. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/pitt-meadows-athletic-park
- The Fraser River dike and the Pitt River Greenway form the south edge; Harris Road is the west side and the Maple Ridge boundary the east. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/pitt-meadows-athletic-park
- Pitt Meadows Athletic Park at 11431 Bonson Road sits in the South Bonson area, beside Pitt Meadows Arena. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/pitt-meadows-athletic-park

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Osprey Village is the area's multi-family node: a village centre of shops and homes on Barnston View Road, with the South Bonson Community Centre at 10932 Barnston View Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/events/community-calendar/fraser-way-roundabout-and-park-public-engagement-meeting

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Pitt Meadows' small-scale multi-unit housing (Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing) zones cap units by zone: up to three in R-3, four in R-4, and six in R-6 where the sixth unit is an adaptable unit. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- One secondary suite per principal dwelling is allowed in the RS, R-3, R-4 and R-6 zones, sized 33 to 90 square metres, inside the same building. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/Guide-to-Secondary-Suites.pdf
- A lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite, per the City's suite guides (detail in the mid-meadows-houses-for-sale note). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/Guide-to-Secondary-Suites.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Mid Meadows an official neighbourhood name? **A:** No — it is the board sub-area label. The City names this ground North Bonson, South Bonson and Osprey Village, and its plans and park pages use those names. When you search City documents for a Bonson Road property, search the Bonson and Osprey names, not Mid Meadows. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/pitt-meadows-athletic-park
- **Q:** How flood-exposed is a Bonson Road home? **A:** Treat flood as a core check: over 95% of Pitt Meadows' land base lies in the Fraser River floodplain, and this area sits behind the dike at the city's south edge. The City is protected by about 60 kilometres of dikes, with pump stations and flood boxes, and its floodplain bylaw sets a 5.15-metre designated flood level for the urban floodplain. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Q:** What are the parking rules in Osprey Village? **A:** The village lot's four-hour limit now runs Monday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., and the highlighted part of Barnston View Road is two-hour parking Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Elsewhere, including Fraser Way and Bonson Road, the City bylaw allows parking up to 72 hours at a time. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/283
- **Q:** Which schools serve the Bonson area? **A:** Pitt Meadows has one secondary school, Pitt Meadows Secondary, and elementary catchments are address-based in School District 42. Davie Jones Elementary at 12030 Blakely Road and Highland Park Elementary at 18961 Advent Road serve the city's east and north sides; run the exact Bonson address in the district locator, because no catchment result was confirmed for it this pass. https://elementary.sd42.ca/daviejones/schools-out/ https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-school/highland-park-elementary/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Fraser Way roundabout in Osprey Village has a fountain at its centre, and the City has consulted on safety and maintenance changes because paths bring pedestrians through traffic. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/events/community-calendar/fraser-way-roundabout-and-park-public-engagement-meeting
- Pitt Meadows Art Gallery reopened inside the South Bonson Community Centre, with a Katzie First Nation mural on its front entrance. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/4901
- Athletic Park holds five regular and two mini soccer fields, four ball diamonds, and parking for 344 vehicles — expect event traffic on Bonson Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/pitt-meadows-athletic-park
- The Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District covers this south-central ground, defined by the Fraser, Pitt and Alouette river dikes and a rise of land near 201 Street in Maple Ridge. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- The city's urban drainage here is Area 3 (Kennedy catchment), discharging to the Alouette, Pitt and Fraser rivers through flood boxes and pump stations. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Pitt Meadows collects property taxes and utility fees in one combined bill; the 2026 increase for the average single-family home is $319 combined, including a $137 utility increase. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/Tax-insert-2026.pdf
- The 2026 single-family flat water fee rises from $836 to $917, and utility fees here include a drainage and diking charge — a cost line most cities do not bill separately. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/Staff-Report-2026-Budget-Financial-Plan-Overview.pdf
- Pitt Meadows is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so residential owners must declare. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
Read: 2026-11-03

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- This area has no rail station of its own; Pitt Meadows Station on the West Coast Express is north of the rail line, and the City states the train reaches downtown Vancouver in about 45 minutes. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain
- Route 701 buses on Harris Road link this side of the city to Coquitlam Central Station and to Haney Place in Maple Ridge (detail in the mid-meadows-houses-for-sale note). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-06/Parking%20Study.pdf
- The Golden Ears Bridge, reached via Airport Way and Bonson Road, is this area's direct road link to Maple Ridge and Langley — timing unconfirmed.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the floodplain bylaw: Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 sets a 5.15-metre geodetic designated flood level for the Schedule B Urban Floodplain, and habitable space below the required flood level is not permitted. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/Floodplain%20Designation%20and%20Construction%20Control%20Bylaw%20No.%202384%2C%202008%20-%20Consolidated.pdf
- Check title for a flood protection covenant before valuing a suite: the City's suite guide warns that covenants can bar suites, and construction below the flood level is prohibited. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/Guide-to-Secondary-Suites.pdf
- The dike system does not currently meet modern standards for climate change, sea-level rise and seismic design, per the City's flood mitigation release — ask about dike upgrade levies and flood insurance for the specific lot. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/city-receives-412000-flood-mitigation-plan
