# Annieville Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: annieville-guide (Annieville, North Delta; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Annieville is North Delta's riverfront neighbourhood along River Road, centred on 112 Street and Annieville Elementary; street-level detail is in the annieville-north-delta-houses-for-sale note — see the annieville-north-delta-houses-for-sale note. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/annieville-lions-park
- Annieville Lions Park at 9150 112 Street, beside the elementary school, is the neighbourhood's named landmark and covers just over 33,000 square metres. https://letstalk.delta.ca/annievillelionspark

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The guide-level pattern is older detached homes on the slope above the Fraser River, with larger River Road lots that have drawn small-lot subdivision proposals; the lot and zone detail is in the annieville-north-delta-houses-for-sale note — see the annieville-north-delta-houses-for-sale note. https://letstalk.delta.ca/annievillelionspark
- The Annieville commercial village on River Road is a designated development permit area (ND3 in the 2024 Official Community Plan (OCP)), which is why the riverfront shops look different from the housing streets behind them. https://letstalk.delta.ca/annievillelionspark

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) applies on Annieville's single-detached and duplex lots: Delta's June 24, 2024 zoning lets owners seek suites, garden suites and coach houses at building-permit stage without rezoning. https://letstalk.delta.ca/ssmuh
- River Road frontage brings the river into the file: check flood, dike and streamside conditions for the specific lot, because Delta's flood system is built around the Fraser River frontage on this side of the city. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve Annieville? **A:** Verify the exact address in the district locator first. Annieville Elementary stands in the neighbourhood at 9240 112 Street, and Delview Secondary at 9111 116 Street is the secondary school on the Annieville side of North Delta; the district says catchment is address-based and directs families to confirm it through the locator or the board office. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/news-events/news/registration-information-for-students-who-are-new-to-delta-2/
- **Q:** Is Annieville changing? **A:** Yes, at its edges rather than all at once. The City describes Annieville Lions Park as about one kilometre from the growing Scott Road Corridor, and it endorsed a full master plan for the park in December 2025, with detailed design in winter 2026 and construction from fall 2026. https://letstalk.delta.ca/annievillelionspark
- **Q:** What is the park plan actually adding? **A:** Replacement, not just repair, is the theme. The endorsed master plan includes a fenced spray park, picnic shelter, multi-use games area and field plaza, because the existing splash pad and washrooms are reaching the end of their lifespan. Expect construction activity beside the elementary school from fall 2026. https://letstalk.delta.ca/annievillelionspark
- **Q:** How do I reach SkyTrain from here? **A:** By bus to Scott Road Station on the Expo Line. Route 312 runs Scott Road Station to Scottsdale Exchange through the North Delta grid including 112 Street, and route 391 runs the same corridor via 116 Street; both feed the station rather than running downtown themselves. https://letstalk.delta.ca/nordelwaymup?tool=survey_tool

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Annieville Lions Park was built through the efforts of local residents and is tied to baseball; the City calls it a gathering place for generations of North Delta families. https://letstalk.delta.ca/annievillelionspark
- The Nordel Way multi-use pathway gives a protected cycling route from the Alex Fraser Bridge to River Road, tying into the on-street bike lane on River Road itself. https://letstalk.delta.ca/nordelwaymup?tool=survey_tool
- The City received $760,000 in provincial active-transportation funding in 2025, part of it for protected cycling lanes on River Road connecting Ladner and North Delta in phases. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-city-delta-receives-760000-provincial-funding
- George Mackie Library at 8440 112 Street is the nearest Fraser Valley Regional Library branch, just south in Nordel. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations
- The park's splash pad and washrooms are at the end of their lifespan, which is why the master plan rebuild, not a light refresh, is what residents will see from fall 2026. https://letstalk.delta.ca/annievillelionspark

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta's 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after that date and another 5 percent after September 2, 2026; Council's 2026 plan set a 2.9 percent City tax increase. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- For 2026, Delta's flat-rate utility bill for a single-family home is $1,658, up $51 from 2025, covering garbage and recycling plus water and sewer where not metered separately. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Delta is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT), so residential owners must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- River Road is the east-west route along the Fraser, and 112 and 116 Streets run south to Nordel Way and Highway 91 for the Alex Fraser Bridge. https://letstalk.delta.ca/nordelwaymup?tool=survey_tool
- Cycling is unusually direct here: the Nordel Way pathway connects the Alex Fraser Bridge to River Road and was co-funded by TransLink's Bicycle Infrastructure Capital Cost Share program. https://letstalk.delta.ca/nordelwaymup?tool=survey_tool

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- River frontage means flood homework: Delta maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection where the Fraser River meets the city, so ask for any flood covenant on title and the lot's relation to the dike. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Slope and streamside conditions vary by street on the Annieville hillside; no City page read in this pass maps them lot by lot, so a geotechnical report is a subject condition to consider, not an assumption. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
