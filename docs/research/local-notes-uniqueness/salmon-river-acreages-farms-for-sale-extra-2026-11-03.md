# Salmon River Acreages and Farms — Extra Local Notes
Page: salmon-river-acreages-farms-for-sale (Salmon River, Langley Township; acreages and farms).
## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Salmon River is a Langley Township watershed of about 77 square kilometres (km²) that drains from Abbotsford through the Township to the Fraser River's Bedford Channel. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- The river's outlet is controlled, not free-flowing: it discharges through the Salmon River Pump Station outlet flood boxes at the Fraser River. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- Tidal influence reaches far inland here — the Township states Fraser River tidal influence extends as far upstream as Highway 1 because of the river's flat gradient. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- The Uplands fabric beside the valley is the small-lot acreage pattern buyers see on the ground; its plan status is read in the Township's Rural Plan layer in GeoSource, not from a neighbourhood plan. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is individual fee-simple acreage and farm parcels along the river and in the Uplands, with no new named acreage subdivision or bare-land strata verified in this pass. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Land use in the watershed mixes agricultural activities and residential areas, each with distinct drainage and flood needs, as the Township states. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- Low-lying parcels are part of the working drainage system: the Township notes standing fish can be left in agricultural fields and other low-lying areas after flood events. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Zoning is Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, and Section 110 sets minimum subdivision requirements for lot area, frontage and depth. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- A Salmon River proposal is checked in GeoSource against rural land use designations, development permit areas, watercourses and trees before an application is made. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- In the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR), a principal residence is capped at 500 square metres (m²) and a secondary suite must sit entirely within that principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) does not apply to lots over 4,050 m² (1 acre), and eligible lots need both municipal water and sewer — most valley acreages fail one or both tests. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Does a Salmon River acreage flood? **A:** Low-lying land here can flood, and the mechanism is specific. When Fraser River levels rise, the pump station flood box gates close to stop upstream flooding; the Salmon River then cannot drain by gravity, so water accumulates until tides drop or pumps run — check the parcel's elevation and the floodplain layer in GeoSource before subjects. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Q:** Are the pumps protecting the valley year-round? **A:** Only partly, today. The Township states the fish-friendly screw pump operates year-round, but the three older non-fish-friendly axial pumps may operate only during freshet (May to August) under Fisheries and Oceans Canada restrictions — which is exactly why the upgrade project in section 6 exists. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Q:** Can I add a second detached house for family? **A:** In the ALR, only within the Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) limits: one additional detached residence, up to 90 m² on properties 40 hectares or less, if there is only one existing residence when construction begins, and only if Township zoning also allows it on that lot. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q:** How do I check a specific lot's constraints? **A:** Start with the Township's GeoSource mapping tool. The Township directs users to view zoning, approximate lot dimensions, community and neighbourhood plan area, development permit areas, and site features like watercourses, trees and road access there — then confirm accuracy with a British Columbia land surveyor (BCLS). https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The original Salmon River Pump Station was built in 1949, a larger axial pump replaced one unit in 1984, and a new station with a fish-friendly screw pump was built beside it in 1997. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- Flood boxes, not pumps, move most water: the Township states the six flood boxes often handle more water than all the drainage pumps combined when Fraser levels are low. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- Peak flows pull in opposite seasons — peak Fraser River flow is in June, peak Salmon River flow is around November, per the Township's pump-operation data. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- The river provides spawning, rearing and overwintering habitat for numerous fish species, which is why pump operation is federally restricted outside freshet. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- School catchment is by street address in School District 35's (Langley) School Locator Tool, not by the Salmon River name. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Salmon River Pump Station upgrades are in design and tendering: Council authorized staff to proceed with detailed design and tendering in March 2026, with construction expected in Spring 2027, as read on 2026-11-03. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- The project installs a second fish-friendly screw pump (adding 2,000 litres/second, doubling screw capacity to 4,000 litres/second) and replaces the 1984 axial pump with a fish-friendly axial pump (1,500 litres/second). https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Township tax statements arrive by the end of May, and unpaid current-year taxes draw a 5% penalty at midnight July 2 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- A Salmon River tax notice includes drainage among its levies, alongside roads, solid waste and capital projects — drainage is a real line in a flood-valley watershed. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) list includes the Township of Langley, so residential owners here are in a designated taxable area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- Headline civic facts are in the earliest sibling note — see the langley-farms note; no per-$1,000 residential rate is stated on the Township page read today, so none is quoted. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Movement here is by road to the river crossings and Highway 1; the Township's own flood description uses Highway 1 as the upstream marker for tidal influence in the valley. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- Property information for a trip or a viewing starts in GeoSource, which shows road access as a site feature for each parcel. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- No rapid transit serves the Salmon River acreages; the Township's only Transit Oriented Area is on 196 Street in Willowbrook for the proposed Surrey-Langley SkyTrain station. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Flood is the headline risk: the Township models reduced flood extents for 10-year and 200-year winter storm events only after the pump upgrades, so today's protection is the existing system. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- When flood boxes stay closed for hours or several days on the tidal cycle, water accumulates in the Salmon River — a buyer should ask for the parcel's flood history in a winter event, not only freshet. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- Watercourse and development permit layers are checked in GeoSource's Environment and Planning layers before any building or subdivision plan. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- In the ALR, septic needs health authority approval and fill may need an Agricultural Land Commission Notice of Intent, both listed by the ALC as separate approvals. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
