## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The oldest stock is the park-side heritage houses: the Moore House at 5145 Sperling Avenue, built 1941, sits in the Deer Lake Park Heritage Precinct. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?objectType_facet=Landmark%20Description%20&objectType_facet=Bylaws%20-%20Repealed&ps=50&q=&decade_facet=2010&decade_facet=1940&sort=score%20desc&p=1
- Deer Lake Park was established in 1949 on the lake's eastern shore; the area retained much of its rural character as Burnaby urbanised. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark692
- The 1965 Baldwin House at 6543 and 6545 Deer Lake Drive, designed by Arthur Erickson, is the newest protected building in Burnaby's heritage inventory. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/programs-and-policies/heritage-planning-program

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Heritage compliance is monitored: Burnaby checks heritage sites against its bylaws, Heritage Revitalization Agreements and conservation standards. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/programs-and-policies/heritage-planning-program
- Inventory properties qualify for Heritage Planning Program incentives; the inventory, used since 1989, tracks significant buildings not protected by designation bylaws. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/programs-and-policies/heritage-planning-program
- Lots near the lake or its creeks can fall in the Streamside Protection Development Permit system, needing a Qualified Environmental Professional's report on the setback area. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-06/Preparing-Environmental-Assessment-Report.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What is actually in Deer Lake Park, beside these houses? **A:** It is Burnaby's cultural centre, not just a lake. The City lists trails connecting the lake, Burnaby Art Gallery, the Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, Burnaby Village Museum and Hart House restaurant, at 6450 Deer Lake Ave, reached from Canada Way via Sperling Ave. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park?sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwjLkNCwnraGAxUUDNQBHdBIJfsQyK4DegQIBBAt
- **Q:** Which schools serve an Upper Deer Lake address? **A:** Buckingham Elementary, at 6066 Buckingham Avenue, kindergarten to Grade 7, and Burnaby Central Secondary, at 6011 Deer Lake Parkway, Grades 8 to 12, are the schools on the Province's Burnaby list beside this neighbourhood; Burnaby School District No. 41's School Locator decides catchment by address. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** Is there a bus through Upper Deer Lake itself? **A:** Yes. TransLink's route 133 timetable, effective September 7, 2026, runs from Edmonds Station via Imperial, Walker, Stanley, Sperling and Canada Way, with some trips via Deer Lake Parkway and Burnaby City Hall, then continues as route 129 at Holdom Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt133.pdf
- **Q:** Does living beside the park mean events next door? **A:** The park is a venue as well as a nature park. The City lists a Festival Lawn at Deer Lake Park as a performance venue overlooking the lake in the city's arts precinct, and states the park is open from dawn until dusk year-round. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park?sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwjLkNCwnraGAxUUDNQBHdBIJfsQyK4DegQIBBAt

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Search the Heritage Burnaby database for the specific house before offering: an inventoried house can carry heritage expectations without a designation bylaw on title. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/programs-and-policies/heritage-planning-program
- Check streamside title charges near the lake: a Streamside Restoration Plan must be registered on title as part of a Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area covenant and development permit. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-06/Preparing-SPEA-Restoration-and-Enhancement-Plan.pdf
