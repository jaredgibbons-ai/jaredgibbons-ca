# Forest Glen Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: forest-glen-burnaby-houses-for-sale (Forest Glen; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Forest Glen is a Burnaby South house area between Metrotown and Edmonds, named for Forest Glen Park at 4855 Bond Street. Its house streets run around Kingsway, Willingdon Avenue and the Bond Street and Nelson Avenue blocks, east of Metrotown. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- The board label adds a Burnaby South tag, written as Forest Glen BS in listing area labelling. The name itself is Forest Glen; the BS tag is an area code, not part of the neighbourhood's name. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- Metrotown and Patterson sit to the west, and Burnaby Hospital and Cascade Heights sit to the north-east. Edmonds and South Slope lie to the east and south-east, and Deer Lake lies to the north. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached houses on the Bond, Nelson and Sussex-side streets are the stock this page covers, separate from the corridor towers. The Kingsway frontage through Forest Glen carries taller corridor buildings that belong to the town-centre corridor, while the interior streets hold the freehold house lots. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Wesburn Community Centre at 4781 Parkwood Avenue serves the south side of the area in Wesburn Park. The City lists a 700 square metre meeting and activity room for 100 people there, with no regular office hours and visits by arrangement. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/wesburn-community-centre
- Forest Glen Park at 4855 Bond Street is the named neighbourhood park on the City's parks list. The larger green spaces at the edges are Central Park to the west and Deer Lake Park to the north. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Interior house lots follow the citywide R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) rules — see the montecito-burnaby-houses-for-sale note for the full unit counts. The Forest Glen caution is zoning by street: Kingsway and Willingdon corridor parcels are not R1 house lots, so the SSMUH counts apply only to R1 lots on the interior streets and must be confirmed parcel by parcel. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- The Cascade Heights Community Plan covers the neighbourhood north-east of Forest Glen toward Burnaby Hospital. The City adopted it in March 2025, under the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) adopted December 9, 2025. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/cascade-heights-community-plan

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary schools sit in or beside Forest Glen? **A:** Four elementary schools sit in or beside the house streets, so the catchment depends on the address. The Province's School District 41 (SD41) list names Clinton Elementary at 5858 Clinton Street, École Inman Elementary at 3963 Brandon Street, Nelson Elementary at 4850 Irmin Street and École Marlborough Elementary at 6060 Marlborough Avenue. Check the exact address in the SD41 locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** Which bus runs on the house streets themselves? **A:** Route 110 runs on Bond Street and Nelson Street themselves, not only on Kingsway. TransLink's timetable shows the 110 linking Metrotown Station to Lougheed Station via Bonsor Avenue, Bennett Street, Nelson Avenue, Bond Street, Willingdon Avenue, Deer Lake Parkway, Sperling Station, Winston Street and Government Road, giving the house streets a two-direction service. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt110.pdf
- **Q:** Where is the SkyTrain for a Forest Glen house? **A:** Metrotown Station and Patterson Station, both on the Expo Line, sit at the area's west side. The City lists Burnaby's Expo Line stations as Patterson, Metrotown, Royal Oak and Edmonds. A house on the east side of Forest Glen is a bus ride on the 110 to a station, not a station-front address. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- **Q:** Can a Forest Glen house lot add a suite or extra homes? **A:** Yes on an R1 interior lot, within the citywide counts carried in the montecito-burnaby-houses-for-sale note. A rented suite also changes the utility bill: the City's 2026 table charges a rented suite in a single-family dwelling $326.17 for water and $403.00 for sewer use, on top of the main dwelling charges. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Forest Glen's detached streets sit between two town centres on one corridor. The City states its town centres combine transport and social infrastructure with homes and jobs and are served by SkyTrain plus high-frequency and local buses, and Kingsway through Forest Glen is the corridor between Metrotown to the west and Edmonds to the east. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- The Edmonds Urgent and Primary Care Centre (UPCC) is at #201-7315 Edmonds Street, east of Forest Glen in the Edmonds town centre. Fraser Health lists it as the urgent and primary care option on this side, separate from Burnaby Hospital to the north-east. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/burnaby/edmonds-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- Deer Lake Park at 6450 Deer Lake Avenue, north of the area, is open dawn to dusk year-round. The City describes trails connecting the lake, Burnaby Art Gallery, Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, Burnaby Village Museum and Hart House, with a sandy beach at the lake's east end. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- The specific Burnaby Public Library branch assignment for a Forest Glen address was not confirmed in the sources read. The City confirms only the four-branch, one-per-quadrant structure, with the nearest town-centre branch node at Metrotown to the west. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/city-departments/burnaby-public-library

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Citywide Burnaby tax figures are carried in full in the montecito-burnaby-houses-for-sale note — see that note. The Forest Glen-specific cost is the suite charge: for 2026 the City charges a two-family dwelling, entire property, $978.50 for water, and a rented suite in a single-family dwelling $326.17 for water plus $403.00 for sewer use. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- All residential utility rates carry a 5 percent discount if paid by the due date. Cross-connection charges are added where they apply, and prices on the City's table include tax. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 110 is the defining transit service for Forest Glen houses, running on Bond and Nelson themselves. Its published path runs between Metrotown Station and Lougheed Station via Willingdon Avenue, Deer Lake Parkway, Royal Oak Avenue, Gilpin Street, City Hall, Sprott Street, Sperling Station, Winston Street, Government Road and Eastlake Drive. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt110.pdf
- Kingsway, Willingdon Avenue and Imperial Street are the main roads through the area. Canada Way lies to the north-east and Marine Drive and Marine Way lie to the south toward the Fraser River. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt116.pdf
