# Cultus Lake Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: cultus-lake (Cultus Lake; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Cultus Lake is not a municipality: the lake sits in Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area H, south of the City of Chilliwack, and the FVRD lists Columbia Valley, Cultus Lake and Lindell Beach as Area H's communities. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/about-the-fvrd/electoral-areas/electoral-area-h.html
- The north end is Cultus Lake Park, 640 acres governed by the Cultus Lake Park Board under the Cultus Lake Park Act, while the south shore, Lindell Beach, is outside the Park Board lands and governed directly by the FVRD — the same lake has two different land systems. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/ https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/about-the-fvrd/electoral-areas/electoral-area-h.html
- Columbia Valley Highway and Columbia Valley Road are the only road in and out, running north to Highway 1 at the Sardis or Yarrow exits. https://bcparks.ca/cultus-lake-park/
- Soowahlie First Nation (IR 14) is shown on the PlanCultus plan map at the south end of the lake. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/CLP-Plan-Bylaw-1080-hig-res.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Inside the Park, homes sit on leased lots: the Park Board states the Park is home to more than 1,500 residential leaseholders and 21 commercial businesses, and the tenure is leasehold, not freehold. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/ https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/new-resident-information/
- Park land use runs on PlanCultus, Bylaw No. 1080, 2016, which designates the existing residential neighbourhoods as Residential, and the FVRD's Cultus Lake Park Zoning Bylaw No. 1375, 2016: R-1 allows one single-detached dwelling per lot, and R-2 allows one single-detached dwelling plus one secondary suite. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/CLP-Plan-Bylaw-1080-hig-res.pdf https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Area-H-Zoning-Bylaw-1375-Cultus-Lake-Park-Amended.pdf
- The south shore is freehold and bare land strata instead: named communities include Creekside Mills at Cultus Lake, strata plan EPS3777 (FVRD Board record), The Cottages at Cultus Lake, 1777 Columbia Valley Rd (developer source), and Aquadel Crossing, 1885 Columbia Valley Rd (developer source). https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=15206 http://www.cultuslakecottages.com/ https://www.aquadelcrossing.com/
- One freehold condo building is named on a developer source: Lakeside at Cultus Lake, 3175 Columbia Valley Highway, 21 homes at the north end, developed by Alture Properties. https://altureproperties.com/2017/06/05/lakeside-cultus-lake-takes-location-everything/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The first rule is tenure: a Park home purchase buys the home and the leasehold interest under the Park Board's lease system, so the lease terms and Park bylaws are part of the purchase documents. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/new-resident-information/
- Outside the Park, zoning is the FVRD Consolidated Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021, and under the Official Community Plan for Electoral Areas E and H, Bylaw No. 1115, the only residential use permitted under the plan designations is single family residential. https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/zoning https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA Housing Needs Report.pdf
- Short-term rentals are controlled through zoning: Bylaw No. 1638 excludes "Temporary Tourist Accommodation" from Residential use except where a zone expressly permits it, and the FVRD has specifically enabled Electoral Area H to address short-term vacation rentals. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=22845
- Alluvial fan development outside the Park lands must meet the Flood Construction Level and setback set by a site-specific geohazard assessment under the FVRD Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 1669, 2022. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Floodplain%20Management%20Bylaw%20No.%201669.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a Cultus Lake home freehold or leasehold? **A:** It depends which shore. Homes inside Cultus Lake Park at the north end sit on lots leased from the Cultus Lake Park Board, so the buyer purchases the home and the leasehold interest, not the land. Homes at Lindell Beach and the south shore are outside the Park boundary and are freehold or bare land strata under FVRD bylaws. The two systems sit minutes apart, so check the tenure on the specific property first. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/new-resident-information/
- **Q:** Is the lake on sewer, or is it septic country? **A:** Both, by service area. The FVRD operates a sewer system for Cultus Lake in Electoral Area H and publishes a service area map; its new North Cultus Wastewater Treatment Plant was commissioned in April 2025 to produce Class A effluent with phosphorus removal. Lots outside the mapped sewer service area use private septic systems regulated by the Fraser Health Authority, so confirm the exact lot's service area before assuming a connection. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/sewer-septic.html?media=standard https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2025/north-cultus-wastewater-treatment-plant-comes-online.html
- **Q:** Does the bus run to Cultus Lake in winter? **A:** No. BC Transit route 56 Cultus Lake runs between Chilliwack and the Cultus Lake Marina stop via Vedder Road and Columbia Valley Highway in the summer months only, and it does not extend to Lindell Beach at the south end. The route page read on 2026-11-03 shows no current schedule, which matches the off-season. Year-round living at the lake means driving. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=56
- **Q:** Which schools serve the lake? **A:** The only public school at the lake is Cultus Lake Swílhcha Community School, Kindergarten to Grade 5, at 71 Sunnyside Blvd inside the Park. The SD33 School Locator result for that address returns Mount Slesse Middle School and G.W. Graham Secondary, with the locator note that Mount Slesse students must attend G.W. Graham. Catchments are address-based, so run the exact address for a south-shore property rather than relying on the Park result. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/school/03333009 https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q:** What does it cost to park at Main Beach? **A:** Paid parking is in effect in the Park's public lots, and the Park Board states the Park receives no funding at the local, provincial or federal level and is entirely self-sustaining, which is why lot fees and lease charges carry its operations. Residents' guests, renters and buyers visiting listings in summer should expect the public lots at Main Beach to be pay lots in season. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Sunnyside Campground, 4165 Columbia Valley Highway, has close to 600 campsites, so summer traffic past the north-end homes is campground traffic as much as beach traffic. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/
- The Park Board's September 9, 2026 meeting included a review of its Noise Regulation Bylaw No. 1147, 2019 for daytime noise disturbances — noise rules inside the Park are the Board's, not the City's. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026.09.09-Regular-Meeting-Recap-3.pdf
- Cultus Lake Community Hall is at 4220 Columbia Valley Highway, and there is no Fraser Valley Regional Library branch at the lake — the nearest is Sardis Library, 5819 Tyson Road, Chilliwack. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/ https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/
- There is no FVRD garbage or recycling collection service in Electoral Area H, unlike some other electoral areas — disposal is a resident arrangement. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Strategic~Plans/2020%203rd%20Quarter%20Update.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Area H utility bills are issued annually in January, in arrears, and the FVRD's 2026 residential rates for Cultus Lake are: Sewer – Cultus Lake North (service 441) at a flat $1,369.00 per year plus property taxes, while Water – Cultus Lake (485) and Sewer – Cultus Lake South (448) have no utility billing and are funded through property taxes. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area H - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- Electoral Area H properties pay provincial rural property tax, not a municipal tax: under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- Park lease lots pay Park Board lease charges rather than a standard municipal tax bill, and the South Cultus Lake sewer parcel tax (Bylaw No. 1497, 2018, as amended by Bylaw No. 1779, 2025) is the annual requisition divided equally among the parcels on the roll. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/new-resident-information/ https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com:443/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=30839

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 56 Cultus Lake is the only bus: Chilliwack (Cottonwood Mall and Promontory area) to the Cultus Lake Marina stop via Vedder Road and Columbia Valley Highway, summer months only. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=56
- Columbia Valley Road is one road in and one road out for the whole lake, north to Highway 1 at the Sardis or Yarrow exits — summer congestion and any road closure affect every property on the lake. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/FVRD-Area-H-Update-with-Emergency-and-Evacuation-Plans-May-14-2025.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Read the FVRD Evacuation Guide for Area H before buying: it lists flooding, landslide and slope stability, interface fire, debris slides and flash flooding among the area's hazards, and the FVRD runs a Flood and Debris Control service in Area H. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/FVRD-Area-H-Update-with-Emergency-and-Evacuation-Plans-May-14-2025.pdf
- At the south end, the Frosst Creek flood protection works — two debris basins and two dikes on the north and south sides of the creek — protect the community built on the Frosst Creek alluvial fan, and fan lots fall under Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 1669, 2022. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/flood-protection.html https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Floodplain%20Management%20Bylaw%20No.%201669.pdf
- Wildfire is a named planning layer here: the FVRD's Area H page links a Community Wildfire Resiliency Plan and an area Hazard Report for residents to check. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/about-the-fvrd/electoral-areas/electoral-area-h.html
