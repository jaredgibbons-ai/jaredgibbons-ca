## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- City-wide, Pitt Meadows houses are low-lying, dike-protected stock: the City states it is almost entirely protected by 60 kilometres of dikes, with ditches, pump stations and flood boxes as its flood infrastructure. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- The City divides the municipality into four drainage areas, defined by dikes and served by flood boxes and pump stations, including the Alouette, Fenton and Kennedy catchments and the Pitt Polder catchment. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Flood construction is the city-wide house rule: Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 regulates construction in the floodplain, and its Schedule A and Schedule B maps show the designated areas. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 - Consolidated.pdf
- The same bylaw defines a Standard Dike as a dike built to a minimum crest elevation equal to the Flood Construction Level, so a house's protection depends on the dike standard, not on being inside the city boundary alone. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 - Consolidated.pdf
- Drainage is also regulated house by house: Drainage System Protection Bylaw No. 2266, 2007 regulates discharge into and obstruction of the municipal drainage system and imposes a drainage charge on real property. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-05/Drainage System Protection Bylaw No. 2266, 2007 - Consolidated Version.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What is the flood risk for a Pitt Meadows house? **A:** The risk is river flooding held back by dikes, not ocean flooding. The City states it is almost entirely protected by 60 kilometres of dikes, ditches, pump stations and flood boxes, and its Floodplain Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 maps the designated floodplain in Schedules A and B. A buyer should still check the specific lot against those schedules and the dike standard, because protection is a dike crest level, and the bylaw itself allows exemption applications with a report. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Q:** What is Pitt Meadows Regional Airport, and does it affect houses? **A:** Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK) sits north of the Fraser River between the Pitt River and Golden Ears bridges, first opened in 1963, and is owned and operated by the Pitt Meadows Airport Society, a not-for-profit. It has one staffed control tower, a terminal building, a restaurant, three paved runways and a water runway, and it runs the Fly YPK Neighbourly Program to reduce aircraft disturbance, so flight noise is a live question for houses near Airport Way. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport
- **Q:** Who are the Katzie First Nation in relation to Pitt Meadows? **A:** Katzie First Nation, q̓ic̓əy̓, states its traditional territory includes the present-day municipalities of Pitt Meadows, Maple Ridge, Surrey, Langley and Delta, with Pitt Lake at its centre, and that most of its 614 members live in that territory. A Pitt Meadows house therefore sits on land Katzie describes as its traditional territory, and reserve lands themselves are separate from the city's residential lots. https://katzie.ca/who-we-are/
- **Q:** How do people commute from Pitt Meadows? **A:** By commuter rail and road bridge. Pitt Meadows Station is a West Coast Express station on TransLink's system, and the airport page places the city between the Pitt River and Golden Ears bridges, giving the two road crossings of the Pitt and Fraser river system. A house buyer should confirm the current West Coast Express timetable on TransLink's station page rather than assuming all-day trains. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/station/pitt-meadows/map

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Pitt Polder Pump Station, replaced in a project completed in 2024, quadrupled pumping capacity from 1.2 to at least 4.8 cubic metres per second, with its electrical components set above the 200-year flood elevation level. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/flood-projects-table.pdf
- The Diking District covers 3,546 hectares, of which 3,136 hectares are in Pitt Meadows and 410 hectares in Maple Ridge, while the drainage basin feeding it is 4,062 hectares. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- Pitt Meadows collects property taxes and utility fees in one combined bill, covering water, drainage, diking, sewer, solid waste and green waste. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/Tax-insert-2026.pdf
- The airport's Neighbourly Program is the City's own named channel for aircraft-disturbance concerns, rather than a general complaints line. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The 2026 municipal residential rate is $2.8438 per $1,000 of taxable value for general purposes plus $0.1082 per $1,000 for the drainage levy, under 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026, with taxes due on or before July 2, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- Pitt Meadows is a Metro Vancouver municipality, so the same bylaw adds a $0.0583 per $1,000 Metro Vancouver Regional District levy for residential property in 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the floodplain schedules first: look up the lot on Schedules A and B of Bylaw No. 2384, 2008, and confirm the Flood Construction Level and any exemption route, because the bylaw allows exemption applications with a supporting report. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 - Consolidated.pdf
- Check the airport zoning layer too: federal Pitt Meadows Airport Zoning Regulations (C.R.C., c. 102) limit building heights on lands adjacent to or in the vicinity of the airport, so a tall rebuild near the airport needs a height check against those surfaces. https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._102/page-1.html?txthl=b
- Check drainage charges and discharge rules under Bylaw No. 2266, 2007 before altering ditches, culverts or drainage on a lot. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-05/Drainage System Protection Bylaw No. 2266, 2007 - Consolidated Version.pdf
