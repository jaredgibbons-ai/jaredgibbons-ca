# Majuba Hill Area Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: majuba-hill-guide (Majuba Hill, Chilliwack; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Majuba Hill is a rural hillside sub-area inside the City of Chilliwack, on the lower slopes of Vedder Mountain above Yarrow, reached by Majuba Hill Road off Yarrow Central Road. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799
- The Yarrow Neighbourhood Plan (OCP Schedule B7, adopted June 2022, updated 2025) treats Yarrow as two sub-areas — Yarrow Town Centre and Majuba Hill — surrounded by Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) and Forest Resource land. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799
- Agricultural lands in the ALR lie to the north and west of Majuba Hill, and Forest Resource lands that are part of Vedder Mountain lie to the south and east, so the sub-area is an island of rural homes between farmland and forest. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799
- The plan's detailed policies apply specifically to Yarrow Town Centre; Majuba Hill is mapped in the plan but is not the focus of its development policies. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Majuba Hill's stock is large-lot rural homes, not subdivisions: the Yarrow Neighbourhood Plan characterises the sub-area as residential uses on large, rural properties. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799
- The City's engagement material describes rural Chilliwack settlements such as Majuba Hill as predominantly large rural residential estates and vacant acreages in a natural state, outside the urban corridors. https://www.engagechilliwack.com/yarrowplan?tool=qanda
- Settlement here is old by Chilliwack standards: the Chadsey family moved up to Majuba Hill after the 1894 flood, and William Chadsey opened a post office in his home on Majuba Hill Road, near Robinson Road, in 1900. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&paID=18&plID=84

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The ALR boundary is the controlling rule around Majuba Hill: lands outside the sub-area to the north and west are in the Agricultural Land Reserve, where the Agricultural Land Commission's housing rules limit most parcels to one principal residence of 500 square metres or less in total floor area. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799 https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Farm Home Plate rules can apply on the valley-floor edge: the 2050 OCP's Development Permit Area 4 (Farm Home Plate) applies to housing on ALR farmland for properties of 2 hectares or more. http://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2182
- Hillside rules apply on the slope itself: the 2050 OCP sets a Hillside Environment Development Permit Area (DPA 2) and a Hillside Development Permit Area (DPA 5), both listed on the City's development permits page. http://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2182
- City-wide zoning and small-scale multi-unit housing rules are written in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note; see that note.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Majuba Hill part of Yarrow? **A:** For planning, yes and no. The Yarrow Neighbourhood Plan names Majuba Hill as one of Yarrow's two sub-areas, but the plan's policies apply specifically to Yarrow Town Centre, and the City's engagement answers state Majuba Hill is technically outside the Yarrow neighbourhood boundary used for the plan's mapping. Practically, residents use Yarrow's services and shops. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799 https://www.engagechilliwack.com/yarrowplan?tool=qanda
- **Q:** Which schools serve Majuba Hill? **A:** School District No. 33's transportation procedure groups the "Yarrow/Vedder Mountain Road areas" for busing to Yarrow Elementary School, Stitó:s Lá:lém Totí:lt Elementary/Middle School and Sardis Secondary School. Catchments are set by exact address, so confirm the property in the district's School Locator before relying on that grouping. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2023-03/611%20AP%20Transportation%20-%20Operating%20Instructions.pdf
- **Q:** Can I farm or subdivide land next to Majuba Hill? **A:** The farmland to the north and west is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, and subdivision inside the ALR is restricted under provincial rules administered by the Agricultural Land Commission; the forested land to the south and east is Forest Resource land on Vedder Mountain. Check the specific parcel's ALR status on Commission mapping before assuming any development option. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799
- **Q:** What is at Majuba Hill itself — shops or services? **A:** Nothing commercial: the Yarrow Neighbourhood Plan states Majuba Hill has a lack of commercial and institutional uses, so residents rely on access to Yarrow Town Centre, on Yarrow Central Road below the hill, for daily services. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Majuba Hill is the gateway to the Vedder Mountain trail network: the Yarrow plan describes the sub-area as serving as a gateway to recreational trails on Vedder Mountain, so recreational traffic passes the neighbourhood. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799
- Majuba Heritage Park marks the point where Vedder's Road diverged from the New Westminster–Yale Wagon Road, two of the oldest roads in the Chilliwack area; the site was declared a municipal heritage site in 1982 and opened as a park in 1986. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&paID=18&plID=84
- Before Sumas Lake was drained in 1924, the view from Majuba Heritage Park looked out over the lake; the drained lakebed below became the farmland that now surrounds the hill. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&paID=18&plID=84
- School transport is by district bus: SD33 lists the Yarrow/Vedder Mountain Road areas among the catchment areas its regular bus routes are set up to serve, rather than a no-ride walk zone. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2023-03/611%20AP%20Transportation%20-%20Operating%20Instructions.pdf

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 55 Yarrow–Greendale is the nearest bus service, running from Yarrow Central Road in Yarrow Town Centre to the University of the Fraser Valley Chilliwack campus; Majuba Hill homes are above the Yarrow Central Road stops, so the route serves the foot of the hill, not the hill itself. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- Yarrow Central Road is the single access corridor: it connects Majuba Hill Road down to Vedder Road and Highway 1 for trips into Sardis and Chilliwack Proper. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Hillside hazard review is the first check: Majuba Hill sits on Vedder Mountain's slope, inside the hillside framework of the 2050 OCP (Hillside Environment DPA 2 and Hillside Development DPA 5), so building or clearing can require a hillside development permit and geotechnical input. http://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2182
- The farmland below the hill is floodplain farmland: the ALR lands north and west of Majuba Hill sit on the former Sumas Lake bed and the Vedder River floodplain, so a combined purchase of hill and flat land carries both hillside and floodplain checks. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799
- Wildfire interface applies where homes meet the Vedder Mountain Forest Resource lands to the south and east; the applicable permit area guidelines are in the 2050 OCP's Schedule D Development Permit Areas. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
