# Maple Ridge Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: maple-ridge-houses-for-sale (Maple Ridge; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Maple Ridge is a single city on the north bank of the Fraser River, bounded west by Pitt Meadows and east by Mission, with Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and Dewdney Trunk Road as its two east–west house corridors. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The City's own neighbourhood list names Albion/Kanaka, Port Hammond, Port Haney, Ruskin, Silver Valley, Thornhill, Town Centre, Webster's Corners, Whonnock and Yennadon — board sub-area names such as West Central and Cottonwood do not appear on that City list. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached houses in Maple Ridge sit mainly in the RS (Single Detached Residential) zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019; the RS-1 schedule sets a minimum lot area of 668.0 m², minimum width 18.0 m, minimum depth 27.0 m and maximum lot coverage of 40%. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- Rural house lots use larger RS-2 and RS-3 minimums and farm lots use A-1 (2.0 ha), A-2 (4.0 ha) and A-3 (8.0 ha) minimums in the same bylaw schedule. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City-wide, one secondary suite per lot is permitted, inside the single detached building, at no more than 40% of the building's total gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area; a building permit is required. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- Eligible single-detached and duplex lots can now have up to three, four, or six units of small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) — secondary suites, detached garden suites or coach houses, duplexes, triplexes and fourplexes — if zoning, bylaw and building code rules are met. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- A business licence is required if two rental units on one Maple Ridge property are both rented, and the City states a one-time $500 Application Review Fee for rental licensing on its secondary-suite page. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q: Can I add a coach house to a Maple Ridge house lot?** **A:** Yes, on an eligible lot a detached garden suite or coach house is one of the SSMUH forms the City now allows. Eligible single-detached and duplex lots can reach three, four, or six total units depending on the lot, provided zoning, bylaw and building code requirements are all met, and a building permit is required. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- **Q: How big can a secondary suite be?** **A:** A suite can be at most 40% of the building's total gross floor area. Only one suite per lot is permitted, it must sit inside the same building as the single detached house, it cannot be in the Floodplain Area, and new or renovated suites need a building permit. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q: Which school will my child attend?** **A:** Catchment is decided by your exact street address in School District 42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows). The district's School Locator shows the designated catchment school for an address, but it warns a child may not be assigned to that school if the building is at capacity, so confirm with the school principal. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: Does the Speculation and Vacancy Tax apply in Maple Ridge?** **A:** Yes, the City of Maple Ridge is listed as a taxable area in the Metro Vancouver Regional District. Only owners of residential property in a designated taxable area must complete the annual declaration, and reserve, treaty and self-governing Indigenous Nation lands are excluded from the taxable areas. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Maple Ridge households arrange their own garbage disposal; the City does not provide curbside recycling, which Recycle BC provides at no cost to residents, and the Maple Ridge Recycling Depot at 10092 236th Street is run by Ridge Meadows Recycling Society. https://www.mapleridge.ca/resident-services/waste-and-recycling-services-maple-ridge
- Garbage carts may go out no earlier than 5:00 a.m. and must be removed by 7:00 p.m. on collection day under the City's bear-aware guidance, because garbage and compost draw bears into Maple Ridge neighbourhoods. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-maple-ridge-reminds-residents-keep-bears-and-other-animals-wild
- Ridge Meadows Hospital at 11666 Laity Street is the Fraser Health community hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- City-level: Council adopted the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan on January 27, 2026, directing most new housing and commercial growth around future Bus Rapid Transit stations on Lougheed Highway at 203 Street, 207 Street, Laity Street and 216 Street; the plan does not itself change current zoning. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- City-level: updates to the Official Community Plan and the Town Centre, Hammond and Silver Valley area plans were adopted November 25, 2025, and the Albion Area Plan update was adopted in October 2025, to meet the Province's December 31, 2025 housing-legislation deadline. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan
- City-level: on October 17, 2026 voters did not approve borrowing for the three Recreation Ready projects — Hammond Aquatics and Recreation Centre, Hammond Multi-Use Community Park and Albion Twin-Rink Arena Expansion — so those projects cannot proceed under that funding plan. https://www.mapleridge.ca/art-parks-rec/recreation-ready

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The City's 2026 tax-rate summary lists the Class 1 Residential general municipal rate at 2.86430 per $1,000 of assessed value, within a total Class 1 mill rate of 4.53578 per $1,000 including other taxing authorities. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- The 2026 summary lists the flat-rate water charge at $859.25, or $191.65 per parcel where that basis applies, and flat-rate customers are billed water and sewer on the tax notice while metered customers are billed quarterly. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- Maple Ridge property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on balances outstanding July 3, 2026 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in Maple Ridge — see section 4. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Maple Ridge has two West Coast Express stations, Maple Meadows and Port Haney, on TransLink's weekday commuter line between Waterfront in downtown Vancouver and Mission City. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- TransLink's Langley–Haney Place Bus Rapid Transit line is planned at 22 kilometres with 13 stations, running via 200 Street, the Golden Ears Bridge and Lougheed Highway, connecting to Haney Place, Ridge Meadows Hospital and the future Surrey Langley SkyTrain at Willowbrook. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/city-council/maple-ridge-moves
- On May 12, 2026 Council endorsed TransLink's 30% concept design for that BRT line, with five Maple Ridge stations: Lougheed Highway at 203, 207, Laity and 216 Streets, plus the Haney Place terminus. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-supports-concept-design-langley-haney-place-bus-rapid-transit

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- The first check for any Maple Ridge lot is the new Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 8121-2026, approved July 28, 2026, which designates floodplain areas in its Schedule A and sets flood construction levels and setbacks for the Fraser River, the Alouette River system, steep creeks and groundwater flooding. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-climate-action-flood-resilience-environmental-protection-and-mckenney-creek
- Land less than 1.5 metres above the natural boundary of an adjacent watercourse, lake, pond, swamp, marsh or reservoir is designated floodplain under Bylaw No. 8121-2026, and no building or fill may go inside a Floodplain Setback. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
- Properties in the City's Wildfire Development Permit Area need a development permit for development, subdivision or building permits, and a single detached home may instead need a restrictive covenant covering building design and landscaping. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/wildfire-development-permit-construction-requirements
- Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status is per parcel; on ALR land the Agricultural Land Commission frame is one principal residence plus at most one additional smaller residence, with size limits that depend on parcel size. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
