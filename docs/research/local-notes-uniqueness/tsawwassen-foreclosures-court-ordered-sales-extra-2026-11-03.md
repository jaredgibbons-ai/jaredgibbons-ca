## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A transfer on Tsawwassen Lands needs a Tsawwassen Law certificate first. Chapter 5 of the Final Agreement requires a Certificate of Transfer issued under Tsawwassen Law to be submitted to the Registrar before a fee simple transfer may be registered. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/07039_08
- Title is registered in the British Columbia Land Title Office, with a notation that the parcel is Tsawwassen Lands, per the Final Agreement. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/07039_08
- Only Tsawwassen First Nation can cancel an indefeasible title to a Tsawwassen Lands parcel, per the same chapter. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/07039_08
- TFN's Land Act governs dispositions (enacted 2009, consolidated 2024). https://tsawwassenfirstnation.com/pdfs/TFN-Laws-Regulations-Policies/Laws/Laws/Land_Act_2024.03.14.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Does a court-ordered sale work the same on Tsawwassen Lands? **A:** Not exactly, because the title step differs. Registration stays in the British Columbia Land Title Office, but a fee simple transfer first needs a Certificate of Transfer under Tsawwassen Law. Have a lawyer confirm how a court order interacts with that requirement. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/07039_08
- **Q:** Who makes land law on Tsawwassen Lands? **A:** Tsawwassen First Nation, through its Legislature. The Nation became self-governing on April 3, 2009, and the official Registry of Tsawwassen Laws is held in hard copy at the TFN Administration Office. Delta's zoning bylaw does not govern these parcels, and neither does Delta council act as the land-use authority for Tsawwassen Lands. https://tsawwassenfirstnation.com/governance-overview/laws/
- **Q:** Is a Tsawwassen Lands home in the Speculation and Vacancy Tax area? **A:** No, treaty lands are excluded. The Province lists Delta as a taxable area, then states reserve lands, treaty lands and lands of self-governing Indigenous Nations are not part of the taxable areas. A Delta-side Tsawwassen home and a Tsawwassen Lands home therefore sit on opposite sides of that tax line. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Q:** What is a Tsawwassen Fee Simple Interest? **A:** It is the individual fee simple holding created under TFN's Land Act. The Final Agreement requires a Certificate of Transfer under Tsawwassen Law before it can be transferred at the Land Title Office. Permitted transferees are TFN law, not Delta rules. https://tsawwassenfirstnation.com/pdfs/TFN-Laws-Regulations-Policies/Laws/Laws/Land_Act_2024.03.14.pdf

## 10. HOW IT COMPARES
- The registration office is shared; the land law and taxing government are not.

| Fact | Delta-side Tsawwassen | Tsawwassen Lands (TFN) | Ladner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Land registration | Land Title Office | Land Title Office, with Tsawwassen Lands notation; Certificate of Transfer required | Land Title Office |
| Governing land law | Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017 | TFN Land Act; Land Use Planning and Development Act | Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017 |
| Speculation and Vacancy Tax | Delta is a taxable area | Treaty lands are not taxable areas | Delta is a taxable area |
