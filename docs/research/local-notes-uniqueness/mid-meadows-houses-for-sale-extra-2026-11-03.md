# Mid Meadows Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: mid-meadows-houses-for-sale (Mid Meadows, Pitt Meadows; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Mid Meadows has no City-published boundary — the City of Pitt Meadows publishes no official boundaries for MLS sub-areas, and no plan names "Mid Meadows." https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- The detached houses in this note are the Bonson Road area south of the Canadian Pacific Kansas City rail line and Hammond Road and north of the Fraser River dike, between Harris Road to the west and the Maple Ridge boundary to the east — a street pattern taken from the official addresses of the facilities in Sections 2 and 5, not an official boundary.
- The area sits inside drainage Area 3 of the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District, which the City calls the urban development area. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewer/dikes
- Pitt Meadows city-wide facts (zoning bylaw, suites and garden suites, tax rate, utilities, Speculation and Vacancy Tax) were covered in full in the south-meadows-houses-for-sale note — see that note.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The named residential enclave at the south end is Osprey Village in South Bonson, on the Fraser River dike, with Shoreline Park at 51 Bonson Road and the South Bonson Community Centre at 10932 Barnston View Road as its civic anchors. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/shoreline-park
- The recreation block that defines the middle of the area is Pitt Meadows Athletic Park at 11431 Bonson Road and Pitt Meadows Arena at 11435 Bonson Road, which the City places "in the South Bonson area of the City." https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/pitt-meadows-athletic-park
- North Bonson, around Davie Jones Elementary on Blakely Road, is the quieter north end: North Bonson Park is listed by the City with baseball and softball diamonds, a playground and an off-leash dog park, plus leash-optional dike trails toward the Pitt River Bridge and the Pitt Meadows Marina. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/north-bonson-park
- No official source states build decades or typical lot sizes for Mid Meadows detached houses — see NOT CONFIRMED; no MLS figures are used here.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The governing plan is the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," adopted September 27, 2022, which covers the whole city including the Bonson Road area; no neighbourhood plan specific to Bonson Road was found on a City page. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- Dike and drainage rules are the local constraint that replaces a floodplain bylaw search: this area drains through Area 3 (Kennedy, including McKechnie) to the Alouette, Pitt and Fraser Rivers, and because of the low elevation its catchments are drained by floodboxes and pump stations when river levels are high. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewer/dikes
- City-wide Pitt Meadows zoning, suite and Agricultural Land Reserve rules are in the south-meadows-houses-for-sale note — see that note; the Mid Meadows-specific point is that the Bonson urban area itself had no ALR parcels confirmed on a City page in this pass, while the City states 78% of Pitt Meadows land overall is in the Agricultural Land Reserve. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary schools serve a Bonson Road house? **A:** Two School District 42 elementaries sit inside this area: Davie Jones Elementary (K–7) at 12030 Blakely Road and Edith McDermott Elementary (K–7) at 12178 Bonson Road. Secondary students attend Pitt Meadows Secondary at 19438 116B Avenue, north of Lougheed Highway. Catchment is by exact address, so confirm a Hammond Road or Bonson Road address in the district locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- **Q:** Why do two addresses appear for Edith McDermott / Somerset Park? **A:** They are two different addresses for one joint site. The school address is 12178 Bonson Road on School District 42 and BC Government listings, while the City lists the joint Somerset Park / Edith McDermott school site at 12778 Bonson Road for the soccer field and baseball and softball diamonds. Use 12178 for the school and 12778 for the park booking. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/sports-fields
- **Q:** How much does the train at Harris Road affect a Mid Meadows house? **A:** The City states trains block the Harris Road and Kennedy Road crossings for up to 3.5 hours per day on one of the busiest Canadian Pacific segments in British Columbia. Harris Road is the west-side route out of the Bonson area, so a Mid Meadows commute that depends on that crossing is directly affected until the planned underpass is built. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/railways
- **Q:** Is a Bonson Road house in a floodplain? **A:** It is behind dikes, which is the local form of the question. The City states Pitt Meadows is almost entirely protected by 60 kilometres of dikes, sits between the Pitt River to the west and the Fraser River to the south, and this area is in the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District. Ask for the dike and drainage information for the specific lot rather than a generic floodplain answer. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewer/dikes

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Harris Landing / Pitt River Regional Greenway at Harris Road and Fraser Way is the dike-trail head for this area: the City states the Greenway's first phase is open at 10.2 kilometres along the Fraser and Pitt River dikes, with about 30 kilometres planned at full build-out to Grant Narrows Regional Park. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/pitt-river-regional-greenway
- Bonson Park at 11546 Bonson Road is the disc golf site, and the South Bonson Community Centre at 10932 Barnston View Road also houses the Pitt Meadows Art Gallery — both from the City's facility listings.
- The McMyn/Mason House heritage entry ties a current street corner to this area: the City heritage register states the small farmhouse was originally on the McMyn farm at the southwest corner of Park Road and Bonson Road, "now the Somerset area," and is now at its relocated site at 19175 122nd Avenue. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/arts-culture-heritage/heritage
- There is no library branch in Mid Meadows; Pitt Meadows Public Library is at 200 – 12099 Harris Road in Central Meadows. https://fvrl.bc.ca/locations/pitt-meadows-public-library
- Somerset Park at the Edith McDermott site is a City sports-field location (soccer, baseball and softball), so school-field use on Bonson Road is booked through the City, not only the school. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/somerset-parkedith-mcdermott-school

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Harris Road Underpass / Pitt Meadows Road and Rail Improvements Project — at Harris Road, the west-side exit for Mid Meadows: the Vancouver Fraser Port Authority, with the City and Canadian Pacific Kansas City, leads a project to separate road and rail traffic with a four-lane underpass and a shared multi-use path. On April 18, 2023 Council re-affirmed full commitment but declined to become a 25% funding partner (up to $49.6 million), and the City's 2025–2026 letters to federal and provincial ministers seek funding. Status read 2026-11-03: planned / funding advocacy, not under construction on the City page read. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/city-planning-projects/pitt-meadows-road-and-rail-improvements-project/past-project
- Bonson Road and Hammond Road capital works — the City's 2026 Engineering Business Plan lists design work for Hammond Road from Blakely Road to Bonson Road, and Bonson Road from Hammond Road to Bruce Drive including a pedestrian crossing upgrade at Pitt Meadows Athletic Park, both seeking partial grant funding. Status read 2026-11-03: in the 2026 draft business plan (meeting November 24, 2025). https://pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/Staff-Report-2026-Business-Plan-Engineering.pdf
- Fraser Way Dike raising — the same 2026 Engineering plan lists Fraser Way Dike as a continued multi-year project: raising the dike, repaving, adding a multi-use path and extending the road east — the dike at the south end of the Bonson / Osprey Village area. Status read 2026-11-03: continued multi-year design in the 2026 plan. https://pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/Staff-Report-2026-Business-Plan-Engineering.pdf
- Harris Road Complete Street — the City is exploring transforming Harris Road into a "Complete Street" as recommended in its Active Transportation Plan; Harris Road is the western boundary street for this area. Status read 2026-11-03: listed as a City planning project, no construction date stated. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/city-planning-projects

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Routes 719 and 722 are the Bonson Road buses: TransLink's timetable runs them as a combined circular community shuttle — 722 via Bonson Road, 116B Avenue, Blakely Road and Hammond Road to Pitt Meadows Station, and 719 via Fraser Way, Bonson Road, Wildwood Crescent and Hammond Road to Maple Meadows Station and Meadowtown Centre. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/194/tt719.pdf
- Route 701 runs along Hammond Road at the north edge, stopping at Pitt Meadows Station, on the Coquitlam Central Station to Haney Place / Maple Ridge East route. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/194/tt701.pdf
- There is no West Coast Express station in Mid Meadows; Pitt Meadows Station is in Central Meadows, and the Bonson buses connect to it and to Maple Meadows Station in Maple Ridge. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with dikes: the City states the current dike system was built after the 1948 Fraser River flood and rebuilt to current standards from 1968, and the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District covers 3,546 hectares (3,136 hectares in Pitt Meadows) defined by the Fraser, Pitt and Alouette River dikes. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewer/dikes
- Check rail noise and crossing delay for any house using Harris Road: the City states the Harris–Kennedy rail segment blocks crossings up to 3.5 hours per day, and the City has no authority over rail operations because they are federally regulated. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/railways
- The dike page does not give a single lot's flood construction level or drainage status — that requires a per-address check with the City for a Bonson Road, Blakely Road or Hammond Road lot. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewer/dikes
