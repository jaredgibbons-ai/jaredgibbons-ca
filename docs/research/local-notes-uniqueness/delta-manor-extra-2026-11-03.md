# Delta Manor — Extra Local Notes
Page: delta-manor (Delta Manor; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Delta Manor is the central Ladner grid around Delta Secondary School and Memorial Park, in the City of Delta. https://de.deltasd.bc.ca/
- Delta Manor is not an official City neighbourhood name; it is the board sub-area name for that central grid. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/137892/
- Memorial Park is at 5010 47 Avenue, and Delta Secondary is at 4615 51 Street, both inside this pocket. http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/list-of-parks/lists/all-parks/memorial-park
- The governing plan is the Delta Official Community Plan (OCP) 2024, Bylaw No. 8400; the former Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the superseded Bylaw No. 3950. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Up to four units are allowed on single-detached and duplex lots under Delta's Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) updates. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- Combinations can include single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- Any property within 30 metres of a stream, slough or ditch is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- Confirm the parcel's zone on the City zoning maps before assuming a suite or coach house fits. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Delta Manor address? **A:** Catchments are address-based, so check the exact address first. For the sample address 4600 53 Street, the district locator result recorded in this program is Ladner Elementary at 5016 44 Avenue and Delta Secondary at 4615 51 Street. West-Ladner addresses can fall to a different elementary school. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q:** Is Delta Manor an official neighbourhood? **A:** No, Delta Manor is a board name, not a City neighbourhood name. It covers the central Ladner grid around Delta Secondary and Memorial Park. The governing document is the consolidated Delta OCP 2024. The old Ladner schedule no longer exists as a separate schedule. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q:** Can I add a suite or coach house? **A:** A suite or coach house may be allowed, depending on the parcel's zone. Delta's SSMUH amendments cover secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies. The RS1 material lists a secondary suite as an accessory use. Check the zoning map for the lot before counting on added rent. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Q:** How does transit connect to Richmond? **A:** Transit connects through Ladner Exchange on route 601 to Bridgeport Station. Route 601 runs from South Delta to the Canada Line. Route 618 loops Ladner North from Ladner Exchange via Ladner Trunk Road, Central Avenue, Westminster Avenue and Crescent Drive. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- **Q:** Where is the library and hospital? **A:** The Ladner library and Delta Hospital serve this pocket from central Ladner. Ladner Pioneer Library is at 4683 51st Street. Delta Hospital is at 5800 Mountain View Boulevard with 24/7 emergency care, next to Ladner Exchange. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- For 2026 the flat-rate utility total is $1,658 for a single-family home, billed annually at the end of February, with metered water and sewer billed quarterly where metered. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- The City of Delta is a listed taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
Read: 2026-11-03

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check streamside setbacks before planning an addition or coach house, because the 30-metre permit area can limit building work. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- Confirm flood construction requirements for the specific lot with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- For any strata purchase, review bylaws, rules, fees and special levies before removing subjects, per provincial strata buying guidance. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners
