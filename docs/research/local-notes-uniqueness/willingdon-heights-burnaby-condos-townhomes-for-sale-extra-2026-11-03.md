## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A secondary suite inside a Willingdon Heights strata townhome cannot be sold on its own title. Burnaby's zoning rewrite confirms secondary suites must stay registered under the same title as the primary unit, and strata conversion of existing purpose-built rental housing is not permitted except for duplex or semi-detached units — see the vancouver-heights-burnaby-houses-for-sale note for the city-wide position. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- A house lot behind the Hastings Street buildings can now hold a stratified multiplex. Lots in the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District with two or more primary dwelling units may be stratified, which is the route by which new ground-oriented strata homes appear on Willingdon Heights side streets. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- Strata bylaw and fee disputes here go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal (CRT). The Province's strata framework routes many of those disputes to the CRT rather than to court. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which SkyTrain stations serve a Willingdon Heights condo? **A:** Brentwood Town Centre Station and Gilmore Station, both on the Millennium Line, are the closest stations, south and southeast of the area across Lougheed Highway. Hastings Street and Willingdon Avenue buses connect the area's buildings to those stations, and Brentwood is the City-designated town centre for this side of Burnaby. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Hastings Street condo family? **A:** Check the exact address in the School District 41 (SD41) locator, because the area sits between two zones. Parkcrest Elementary is at 6055 Halifax Street and Burnaby North Secondary is at 751 Hammarskjold Drive, and the district groups the North Burnaby elementary schools, including Westridge and Parkcrest, with Burnaby North Secondary in its Brentwood North zone material. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** What City utility charges does a strata home pay here? **A:** A multiple-family dwelling pays Burnaby's 2026 charges of $374.54 for water and $436.00 for sewer use, with a 5% discount if the utility notice is paid by its due date. The sewer parcel tax is billed with property taxes in May and shared between the properties on the parcel, moving to the utility notice in 2027. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Q:** Is the new building at Willingdon and Hastings part of Brentwood? **A:** No, 4488 Hastings Street is north of Lougheed Highway, in Willingdon Heights at the Capitol Hill edge, not in the Brentwood Town Centre plan area south of the highway. The Brentwood towers and The Amazing Brentwood mall are a separate town-centre stock — compare them on the brentwood-park-condos-for-sale page rather than treating a Hastings Street low-rise as a Brentwood tower home. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- **Q:** Can a Willingdon Heights strata ban rentals or pets? **A:** Yes, if its registered bylaws say so. Rental, pet and age rules are set by each strata corporation under the Strata Property Act, not by the City, and no building's bylaws on Hastings Street or Willingdon Avenue were read in this pass. Order the current bylaws and Form B Information Certificate for the specific building before writing the offer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check Hastings Street traffic and title charges before buying a low-rise unit on the corridor. The City's new-home page directs buyers to the Land Title and Survey Authority of British Columbia (LTSA) for easements and rights-of-way, and to BurnabyMap for zoning and heritage flags by address. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/development-permits-construction/new-home-construction
- Check the streamside rule on any side-street townhome lot near a culverted creek. A property within 30 metres of a stream, ravine or wetland needs an assessment setting the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area (SPEA) before new building or land disturbance. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/development-permits-construction/new-home-construction
- Read the strata's registered bylaws and Form B for rental, pet and renovation rules. The Province's strata framework puts those rules in the corporation's own bylaws, with the Civil Resolution Tribunal resolving many disputes about them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
