# Maple Ridge Homes for Sale — Extra Local Notes
Page: maple-ridge-homes-for-sale (City of Maple Ridge; Homes for Sale).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Maple Ridge is one city with two halves: an urban southwest around Haney and the Town Centre, and a rural east and north in Whonnock, Thornhill and the farm belt. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The Fraser River forms the south boundary, Pitt Meadows is the west boundary, Mission is the east boundary, and Golden Ears Provincial Park closes the north. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The board divides the city into 12 sub-areas, including Albion, Cottonwood, East Central, North, Northeast, Northwest, Silver Valley, Southwest, Thornhill, Websters Corners, West Central and Whonnock. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Haney is the historic name for the downtown core; the City's current name for the same ground is Town Centre, around Haney Place and Dewdney Trunk Road. https://www.mapleridge.ca/art-parks-rec/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- City housing splits by geography: urban detached, townhouse and condominium product in the southwest and Albion, and acreage houses on rural and farm zones in the east and north. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- The standard urban house zone is RS-1 in Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019, with subdivision minimums of 668.0 square metres, 18.0 metres width and 27.0 metres depth. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- Rural zone minimums in the same bylaw reach 2.0 hectares in A-1, 4.0 hectares in A-2 and 8.0 hectares in A-3, which is why east-side lots cannot be compared with Town Centre lots. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 is the current city zoning bylaw for every Maple Ridge home type — this note carries the city-wide rules in full for the Maple Ridge group. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- One secondary suite per lot is allowed inside the detached house, to 40% of gross floor area, and not within the floodplain area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- Eligible detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) bylaws. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- Council adopted Official Community Plan (OCP) updates on November 25, 2025, and updated area plans for Town Centre, Hammond, Silver Valley and Albion in the same 2025 cycle. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** What is the Lougheed Transit Corridor plan, and is it adopted? **A:** It is a corridor plan for higher-density housing along Lougheed Highway tied to Bus Rapid Transit, and it was not adopted in January 2026. The City's 2025 year-end review states Council gave second reading to the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan to forward it to a public hearing, so a corridor lot's added density is a plan-stage question, not a current right. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-maple-ridge-looks-back-2025-year-review-highlights-infrastructure-housing-and-community
**Q:** Where does the city want growth to go? **A:** Growth is directed to the Town Centre, the Lougheed corridor and the established area plans, not to the rural east. The City states its 2025 OCP and area-plan updates align land use with provincial housing legislation and focus growth close to transit service, including the Lougheed Transit Corridor and the Bus Rapid Transit route. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-maple-ridge-looks-back-2025-year-review-highlights-infrastructure-housing-and-community
**Q:** How many schools and which district serve the whole city? **A:** All public schools in Maple Ridge are in School District 42, shared with Pitt Meadows. The district has no middle tier, so elementary school is Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary school is Grades 8 to 12, and every catchment is address-based in the district's School Locator, which matters in a city this spread out. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/
**Q:** Is Maple Ridge in the Speculation and Vacancy Tax area? **A:** Yes — the City of Maple Ridge is listed by the Province as a designated taxable area. That means every residential owner must complete the annual speculation and vacancy tax declaration, even when an exemption applies, and reserve, treaty and self-governing Indigenous Nation lands are excluded from the taxable areas. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Ridge Meadows Hospital at 11666 Laity Street is the Fraser Health community hospital for both Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows, with 24/7 emergency care. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- The Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is inside Haney Place Mall at 121 – 11900 Haney Place, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- Maple Ridge Leisure Centre is at 11925 Haney Place and Maple Ridge Public Library is at 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road, both in the Town Centre. https://www.mapleridge.ca/art-parks-rec/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre
- The city has two West Coast Express stations, Port Haney at 22300 River Road and Maple Meadows at 20010 Dunn Avenue, not one. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- A "North" address and a "Northwest" address are different board sub-areas in Maple Ridge, so a listing search under one name misses the other. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Golden Ears Way is the City-named approach route to the Golden Ears Bridge, and its expansion is a TransLink-owned project the City advocates for in Maple Ridge Moves. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-maple-ridge-advances-next-phase-abernethy-way-corridor-improvements

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Abernethy Way Corridor Improvements, 210 Street to 256 Street — City transportation project, current: four-lane widening east to 240 Street with a future 256 Street connection; the 232 to 240 Street phase has design and acquisition in 2025–2026 and construction beginning in 2027 (status read November 3, 2026). https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-maple-ridge-advances-next-phase-abernethy-way-corridor-improvements
- 240 Street Bridge north of 124 Avenue — City capital item, pre-planning: a second Alouette River crossing aligned with 240 Street, with a $55 million initial Class B estimate in the 2026–2030 capital plan, supporting access to Silver Valley. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/Attachment-A-Updated-2026-2030-Capital-Plan-1%29.pdf
- Langley to Haney Place Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) — TransLink project, detailed planning: 22 kilometres between Willowbrook and Haney Place with peak service every 10 minutes; designs, costs and timelines are not yet determined. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/bus-rapid-transit
- North 256 Street Industrial Lands Area Plan — City plan, in development: about 600 acres of industrial land whose opening depends on the Abernethy Way extension to 256 Street. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-maple-ridge-advances-next-phase-abernethy-way-corridor-improvements

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Maple Ridge's 2026 general residential property-tax rate is $2.8643 per $1,000 of taxable value, under Property Tax Rates Bylaw No. 8099-2026, adopted May 12, 2026. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- The full 2026 residential mill rate in the City's rate summary is $4.53578 per $1,000 including school, BC Assessment, Metro Vancouver and TransLink levies. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- Council set the 2026 property-tax increase at 3.5%, about $101 for a property assessed at the city average of $1.04 million. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/video-transcript-understanding-your-property-taxespdf-0
- 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% if unpaid by September 1–2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- West Coast Express runs weekday trains between Waterfront and Mission City through Port Haney and Maple Meadows stations. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- The R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus runs Haney Place to Coquitlam Central Station, and route 701 runs Coquitlam Central via Haney Place, Maple Meadows and Pitt Meadows stations. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt701.pdf
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) is the east-west highway, Dewdney Trunk Road is the parallel Town Centre arterial, and Golden Ears Way leads to the Golden Ears Bridge to Langley and Surrey. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/city-projects/abernethy-way-corridor-improvements

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the City's floodplain maps for any lot near the Fraser, Alouette or Kanaka Creek rivers, because the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 metres geodetic on affected lots. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- Check ALR status parcel by parcel in the east and north farm belt on the Agricultural Land Commission map; city zoning alone does not settle farm use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- Check creek and steep-slope ground in Silver Valley, Thornhill and the Kanaka Creek corridor against the OCP development-permit areas for the exact lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan
