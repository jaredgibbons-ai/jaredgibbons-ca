- Deer Lake houses ring a park, not a shopping street: Deer Lake Park at 6450 Deer Lake Avenue is the centre, and the City calls the park the cultural centre of Burnaby. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Park hours shape the streets: Deer Lake Park is open from dawn until dusk year-round, so the lake roads carry event and visitor traffic in daylight hours only. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** What is actually inside Deer Lake Park?
**A:** A working cultural precinct on the lake's north side: the Burnaby Art Gallery, the Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, Burnaby Village Museum and Hart House restaurant, linked by walking trails with scenic views around the lake. The City lists the Festival Lawn, overlooking Deer Lake, as the outdoor performance venue for concerts and festivals in the same precinct. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park

**Q:** What does Burnaby Village Museum cost to enter, and what is in it?
**A:** Admission is free, per the City's arts and culture facilities page. It is an open-air museum showing life in the village of Burnaby in the early 20th century, with costumed interpreters, a farmhouse, a street of homes and shops, a blacksmith forge, and the C.W. Parker Carousel as the highlight exhibit. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/arts-and-culture-facilities

**Q:** What is the Shadbolt Centre, and how big is it?
**A:** A City-owned multi-use arts facility of 3,252 square metres (m2) (35,000 square feet) in Deer Lake Park, designed in 1995 by Hotson Bakker Architects and named for painter Jack Shadbolt and his wife Doris Shadbolt. It houses a recital hall, theatres, dance and pottery studios and music rehearsal rooms, and runs theatre presentation, arts learning and festivals. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/arts-and-culture-facilities/shadbolt-centre-for-the-arts/about-the-shadbolt

**Q:** Is the Burnaby Art Gallery a general art museum?
**A:** No: the City states it is the only public art museum in Canada dedicated to works of art on paper, with a program of contemporary and historical visual art. For a Deer Lake Avenue house, that means a national-scope gallery, not a community hall, is the neighbour across the park road. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The boardwalk by the boat launch is being fully replaced: that section closed beginning September 21, with completion scheduled by winter 2027 and a signed detour via Sperling Avenue. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- The City monitors waterway health at Deer Lake with the Pacific Streamkeepers, a volunteer organization working on watershed education and clean-ups. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- BC Spirits Square, built in 2010 beside the Shadbolt Centre, holds the City's first piece of public art: carved stone monoliths by Coast Salish artist Thomas Cannell. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/arts-and-culture-facilities/shadbolt-centre-for-the-arts/about-the-shadbolt
- The Shadbolt building won the Canadian Wood Council Award of Merit in 1996 for its creative wood and stone design. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/arts-and-culture-facilities/shadbolt-centre-for-the-arts/about-the-shadbolt
- The City's film kit lists Deer Lake Park and the Shadbolt Centre among Burnaby's popular filming locations, which explains periodic production closures on Deer Lake Avenue. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-02/Burnaby-Film-Office-Film-Kit.pdf
