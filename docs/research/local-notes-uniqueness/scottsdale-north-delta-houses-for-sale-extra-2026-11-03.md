## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Scottsdale is the south-east corner of North Delta in the City of Delta, along Scott Road at the Surrey border. Newton, in Surrey, is across Scott Road to the east. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf

The research grid is 72 Avenue south, 84 Avenue north, 112 Street west and Scott Road (120 Street) east — a working grid, not an official boundary. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached houses and duplexes on the interior grid are Scottsdale's house stock, with strata towers toward Scott Road and 80 Avenue. Delta's 2024 Official Community Plan (OCP) shows Small Scale Residential and Mixed Residential designations in and around the area. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- A Scottsdale duplex can be strata or non-strata, and the title form changes the obligations. The Province states strata housing can include duplexes, with owners holding their strata lot plus a share of common property. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Lots within 400 metres of Scottsdale Exchange sit in a transit-oriented area with minimum densities. Delta states the provincial Bill 47 Transit Oriented Development Area extends 400 metres from the exchange. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- A suite, coach house or garden suite can be combined with a house or duplex here under Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules. Delta adopted those rules city-wide on June 24, 2024 — see the nordel-north-delta-houses-for-sale note for the Delta-wide SSMUH detail. https://letstalk.delta.ca/ssmuh
- Zoning is lot by lot, so two similar houses can carry different expectations. Confirm the lot's zone and any transit-oriented overlay on DeltaMap before pricing development potential. https://www.delta.ca/about-delta/deltamap?viewer=DeltaMapExternal.viewer/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Scottsdale house? **A:** It depends on the exact address, so check the locator before you buy. For 7031 120 Street, School District No. 37 (SD37) gave Heath Elementary, 11364 72 Avenue, for Kindergarten to Grade 7 and Seaquam Secondary, 11584 Lyon Road, for Grades 8 to 12. Scottsdale has no middle-school tier in SD37. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q:** Does the transit-oriented area change what I can build? **A:** Yes, near the exchange it can, because minimum densities apply close to transit. Delta states Bill 47 requires minimum densities within 400 metres of Scottsdale Exchange. A lot inside that line and a lot just outside it can face different density expectations, so measure the lot against the official area map. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q:** Is Scottsdale Exchange in Delta? **A:** No, the exchange itself is on the Surrey side of Scott Road, per the City of Delta. Scottsdale houses are in Delta and pay Delta taxes and utilities, while the buses at the exchange are TransLink services. Do not rely on a listing's "steps to the exchange" wording without checking the lot's side of Scott Road. http://www.delta.ca/node/1838
- **Q:** Is a Scottsdale duplex always a strata? **A:** No, a duplex here may be strata-titled or non-strata, and you must check the title. The Province's strata guidance lists duplexes as a form strata housing can take. A strata duplex has a strata corporation and common property; a non-strata duplex does not, which affects insurance, repairs and financing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta flat-rate utilities for a single-family home are $1,658 in 2026 — the houses-specific angle is that a Scottsdale house pays the same Delta flat rate as a Nordel house. The City bills flat-rate utilities at the end of February. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Delta property taxes were due July 2, 2026. The approved 2026 Financial Plan included a 2.9 percent increase for City operational services. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in the City of Delta. The Province lists Delta as a Metro Vancouver taxable area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The R6 Scott Road RapidBus is Scottsdale's rapid transit link, running from Scott Road Station to Newton Exchange. TransLink lists the R6 among its six RapidBus routes. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
- Scottsdale Exchange is the local bus hub for the area. TransLink timetables list routes 312, 316, 319, 322, 329, 340, 364 and 391 from Scottsdale — see the scottsdale-guide note for the full route table. https://www.translink.ca/r6
- Scott Road Station, at the north end of the R6, connects to the Expo Line SkyTrain. The R6 timetable starts at Scott Road Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/ttR6.pdf
