## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Boundary Beach is the south Tsawwassen pocket on Boundary Bay beside the Canada–United States border at Point Roberts, in the City of Delta. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park
- Centennial Beach, at 570 Boundary Bay Road, is the beach inside Boundary Bay Regional Park next to these homes. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park

- The governing plan is the Delta Official Community Plan (OCP) 2024, Bylaw No. 8400, adopted July 8, 2024, with no Tsawwassen area-plan schedule; the former plan was Schedule D.1 under Bylaw No. 3950. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Foreshore homes face Hazard Development Permit Area SD3, which sets a 2.9 metre Geodetic Survey of Canada datum minimum floor level. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- SD3 also sets a 2.3 metre minimum land surface elevation around construction and a 7.5 metre setback from the natural boundary of a tidal area. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- The Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore, and private moorage is held by lease or licence through FrontCounter BC. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- Whether SD3 covers a specific lot is a parcel-level check; do not assume it from the street alone. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What park serves this pocket? **A:** Boundary Bay Regional Park serves this pocket, with Centennial Beach at 570 Boundary Bay Road. Metro Vancouver lists the Dyke Trail, the Savannah and Raptor trails and Cammidge House in the park. The park is an internationally recognized Important Bird Area. Most of Centennial Beach is closed to dogs, with signed posts showing where. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park
- **Q:** Which schools serve the beach address? **A:** For the sample address 570 Boundary Bay Road, the recorded locator result is South Park Elementary and South Delta Secondary. South Park Elementary is at 735 Gilchrist Drive for Kindergarten to Grade 7. South Delta Secondary is at 750 53 Street for Grades 8 to 12. Check any other address in the locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q:** What flood rules affect a waterfront build? **A:** The SD3 foreshore rules affect a waterfront build through floor level, land elevation and setback. No habitable floor may sit below 2.9 metres datum. Land around construction must be at least 2.3 metres datum. No building may sit within 7.5 metres of the tidal natural boundary. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q:** Who owns the beach in front of a home? **A:** The Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore, not the upland owner. Tenure for docks or moorage is by lease or licence only, through FrontCounter BC. Private moorage sits under a General Permission or Specific Permission. Value beach access on that tenure basis. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **Q:** How does the bus reach the Canada Line from here? **A:** Route 601 reaches the Canada Line at Bridgeport Station from Boundary Bay. Boundary Bay trips run via 12 Avenue, Boundary Bay Road, 3 Avenue and 67 Street to 1A Avenue. The route continues via South Delta Exchange and Ladner Exchange. Check the current timetable for times. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
