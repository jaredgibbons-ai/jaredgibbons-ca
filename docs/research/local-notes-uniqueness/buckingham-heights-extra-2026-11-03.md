# Buckingham Heights Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: buckingham-heights (Buckingham Heights, Burnaby; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Buckingham Heights is the detached-house slope east and south-east of Deer Lake, surrounding Buckingham Elementary School between Deer Lake Park and Royal Oak Avenue in south-central Burnaby. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- Its working streets are Buckingham Drive and Buckingham Avenue on the slope, with Sperling Avenue running down the park's east side and Canada Way along the north. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- It is a separate board sub-area from Deer Lake, Deer Lake Place and Upper Deer Lake, which border it, even though all four share the same lake. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for the sub-area; the anchor address is Buckingham Elementary at 6066 Buckingham Avenue. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is detached single-family housing only: the sub-area's market record shows detached homes on the slope streets and no attached product. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools does a Buckingham Avenue address actually feed to? **A:** For the sample address 6066 Buckingham Avenue, the School District 41 (Burnaby) locator returns Buckingham Elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 7) and Burnaby Central Secondary (Grades 8 to 12), so the elementary school on the slope is also the catchment school for its own address. Catchments remain address-based across the sub-area, so run any other address in the locator before marketing school appeal. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** Is there a SkyTrain station in Buckingham Heights? **A:** No station sits inside the sub-area. Royal Oak Station on the Expo Line, to the south-west, is the nearest station for this side of the lake, and TransLink's Burnaby system map shows bus routes 110 and 144 running the Deer Lake Parkway corridor north of the slope, so most transit trips here start with a bus or a drive to a station. https://translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/september-2026/burnaby-and-new-westminster-map.pdf
- **Q:** What is happening at the Deer Lake boat launch? **A:** The boardwalk section by the boat launch is closed for a full replacement. The City states the closure began September 21, 2026, work is scheduled to complete by winter 2027, a small section of the nearby picnic area is also closed, and the signed detour runs via Sperling Avenue and Deer Lake Drive at about five extra minutes of walking — worth knowing before a buyer prices in the trail loop. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- **Q:** Are there condos or townhomes to buy in Buckingham Heights? **A:** Effectively none in the current market record. This is a detached, freehold slope: the attached options near the lake sit in other sub-areas, so a buyer set on strata product should look at the Deer Lake Place and Metrotown-side pages rather than wait for a Buckingham Heights listing. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- **Q:** What happens at Deer Lake Park through the year? **A:** The park is Burnaby's cultural centre and event ground. The City's 2026 special events program lists Burnaby Blooms (May 3), Symphony and Opera in the Park (July 18–19) and the Burnaby Blues + Roots Festival (August 8) at Deer Lake Park, with the Haunted Village at Burnaby Village Museum in October, so event-day traffic and parking on the park streets are part of living on this slope. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2026-04-17/burnaby-unveils-2026-civic-special-events-program

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Deer Lake Park is open dawn to dusk year-round and its address is 6450 Deer Lake Avenue; its trails connect the lake, Burnaby Art Gallery, Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, Burnaby Village Museum and Hart House restaurant. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- Canoes, kayaks and paddleboards are allowed on Deer Lake, with launch access from the boat launch and beach area off Sperling Avenue at the east end of the lake — the same area affected by the boardwalk closure. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- The library branch serving this quadrant is the Bob Prittie Metrotown Branch, south-west at Metrotown; Burnaby Public Library runs four branches, one in each quadrant of the city. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/city-departments/burnaby-public-library
- Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid Street (Fraser Health), is north-west of the sub-area across Canada Way. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- Burnaby City Hall is at 4949 Canada Way, north-west of the slope at Deer Lake — this is the City's governmental and cultural precinct, not a retail district. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-09/Notice-of-Election-by-Voting-2026-10-01.pdf
- There is no enclosed mall or shopping centre inside the sub-area; Metrotown, south-west, is designated the city's downtown and regional city centre and is this area's main shopping destination. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- No SkyTrain station sits inside Buckingham Heights; Royal Oak Station (Expo Line) to the south-west is the nearest station for this side of Deer Lake. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- TransLink's September 2026 Burnaby system map shows bus routes 110 and 144 on the Deer Lake Parkway corridor north of the slope, linking the area to Metrotown Station and to Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station and Simon Fraser University (SFU). https://translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/september-2026/burnaby-and-new-westminster-map.pdf
- Canada Way runs along the north side, Sperling Avenue down the park's east side, and Royal Oak Avenue along the west side toward Kingsway. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the lakeshore file on park-edge lots: the sub-area's north-west edge is the lake's wet shoreline, and the City is replacing the boat-launch boardwalk outright, so confirm streamside setbacks and any hazard notation for the specific parcel. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- Check the heritage register for Sperling Avenue-edge properties before planning exterior work, as precinct properties carry heritage review. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark496
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status for a specific parcel in the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder rather than assuming from the sub-area. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
