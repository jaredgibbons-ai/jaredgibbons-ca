# West Central Maple Ridge Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: west-central-maple-ridge-houses-for-sale (West Central Maple Ridge; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- West Central has no official City boundary — it is a board sub-area name, and the City's neighbourhood list does not include it. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The streets that define this page in practice are Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road running east–west, with 207 Street, Laity Street and 216 Street as the north–south house streets between the Pitt Meadows border and 221 Street at the Town Centre. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- The Lougheed Transit Corridor, which covers this area's two main roads, runs from Maple Ridge's western boundary to the Town Centre at 221 Street. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-shares-highlights-lougheed-transit-corridor-area-plan-engagement

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The rule that changes houses here is the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan, adopted January 27, 2026, which supports increased residential and commercial density on lots near Lougheed Highway and the future Bus Rapid Transit stations; the City states the plan does not immediately change any property's zoning. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- West Central's eastern edge falls in the Town Centre's transit-oriented area framework: the Town Centre Area Plan, updated November 25, 2025, builds in the Transit-Oriented Areas designated by Bylaw No. 8001-2024 around Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town?bidId=
- For suites and SSMUH unit counts, see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note — the West Central angle is only that corridor lots near the 207, Laity and 216 Street stations are the ones the corridor plan targets for added density. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q: Will the Lougheed corridor plan rezone my house lot automatically?** **A:** No, not automatically. The City states the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan is a long-term planning document that does not result in any immediate impacts on the current zoning of properties in the area. It directs future growth around the Bus Rapid Transit stations at 203, 207, Laity and 216 Streets instead. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- **Q: Which elementary schools serve these streets?** **A:** The schools in or beside the house grid are Alouette Elementary at 22155 Isaac Crescent, Glenwood Elementary at 21410 Glenwood Avenue and Laity View Elementary at 21023 123 Avenue. Catchment is still address-based in School District 42, so run the exact address in the district School Locator before relying on any one school. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/
- **Q: Where is the nearest hospital?** **A:** Ridge Meadows Hospital is at 11666 Laity Street, on this area's Laity Street corridor, and Fraser Health describes it as the community hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows. That puts the hospital inside the wider West Central house area rather than across town, which matters for buyers who need emergency care close by. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- **Q: Is there a train station in West Central?** **A:** No West Coast Express station sits inside the house streets themselves. The two Maple Ridge stations are Maple Meadows, serving the west side, and Port Haney at the Town Centre edge, on TransLink's weekday Waterfront to Mission City line, so most West Central commuters drive or bus to one of those two stations. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The hospital is a local traffic fact, not just a service: Ridge Meadows Hospital sits at 11666 Laity Street, and Laity Street is also one of the four future Bus Rapid Transit station streets named in the corridor plan. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- Secondary options at the area's edges are Maple Ridge Secondary at 21911 122 Avenue and Westview Secondary at 20905 Wicklund Avenue; School District 42 has no middle-school tier, with elementary Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary Grades 8 to 12. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/
- No City recreation centre sits in West Central; the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre is at 11925 Haney Place in the Town Centre to the east. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Full entry (this note owns the corridor): the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was adopted January 27, 2026 and the City directed staff to accept pre-applications and development inquiries in the corridor at the end of 2025; growth is directed around the Bus Rapid Transit stations at 203, 207, Laity and 216 Streets. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- On May 12, 2026 Council endorsed TransLink's 30% concept design for the Langley–Haney Place Bus Rapid Transit line, including centre-running dedicated bus lanes on Lougheed Highway between Maple Meadows Way and 222 Street at the Haney Bypass, and directed staff to continue to detailed design. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-supports-concept-design-langley-haney-place-bus-rapid-transit
- Town Centre boundary expansion to include River Bend (adopted November 25, 2025) touches this area's eastern edge only — see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note for the city-level plan set. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town?bidId=

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- West Central houses pay the same City of Maple Ridge rates as every Maple Ridge house — the 2026 residential rate, utility charges, due dates and Speculation and Vacancy Tax position are in full in the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- The neighbourhood-specific cost angle is licensing: a West Central owner renting both a main house and a suite needs a City business licence, with the $500 one-time Application Review Fee stated on the City's suite page. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The planned rapid transit for these streets is bus, not rail: four of the five Maple Ridge Bus Rapid Transit stations — 203 Street, 207 Street, Laity Street and 216 Street on Lougheed Highway — sit in or beside this area, with the terminus at Haney Place Transit Exchange. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-supports-concept-design-langley-haney-place-bus-rapid-transit
- TransLink's Haney Place diagram shows the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus and routes 701, 733, 741, 743, 744, 745, 746, 748, 749 and 791 using Haney Place Exchange, one exchange east of this area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/194/r748.pdf
- Drivers use Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and Dewdney Trunk Road east–west and the Golden Ears Bridge from the city's west side to Langley and Surrey — see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/city-council/maple-ridge-moves
