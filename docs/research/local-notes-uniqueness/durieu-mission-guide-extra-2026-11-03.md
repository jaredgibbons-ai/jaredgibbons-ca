# Durieu Area Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: durieu-mission-guide (Durieu; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Durieu is an unincorporated farming settlement in the Hatzic Valley, not inside the City of Mission. The Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) lists Durieu among the historic settlement areas of Electoral Area F in the Hatzic Valley, with Hatzic Prairie, Miracle Valley and McConnell Creek (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).
- The valley floor runs between Hatzic Lake to the south and Stave Lake to the north, reached by Sylvester Road from the south and Stave Lake Road from the west, on streets including Durieu Road, Farms Road, Sward Road and Seux Road (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Almost all Durieu homes are single detached houses and farm dwellings on acreages, because most inhabited valley land is farmland. The FVRD states the majority of the inhabited Hatzic Valley is in the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR), with commercial and hobby farming alongside sparse residential use (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).
- No strata complex or subdivision in Durieu was confirmed on an official source read for this note; the stock is lot-by-lot rural housing on Sylvester Road and Stave Lake Road parcels (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- FVRD rules, not City of Mission bylaws, govern building and zoning in Durieu, under the Official Community Plan for Hatzic Valley, Electoral Area F, Bylaw No. 0999, 2010, and FVRD Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021 (https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=30556).
- A secondary suite in Electoral Area F must sit inside the main house, not in a separate building. The FVRD records suites as freely permitted in Area F but only within the principal dwelling, not as a detached garden suite (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).
- On ALR land the Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) allows one principal residence up to 500 square metres plus one additional residence of 90 square metres on parcels up to 40 hectares, or 186 square metres above 40 hectares (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is Durieu part of Mission? **A:** No, Durieu only carries a Mission mailing address. It is an unincorporated community in FVRD Electoral Area F in the Hatzic Valley, so FVRD zoning, FVRD permits and provincial rural taxes apply, and City of Mission bylaws and tax dates do not (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).
**Q:** Can I build a detached coach house on a Durieu acreage? **A:** Not as a secondary suite, because Area F suites must be inside the main house. A separate small home is only possible as the one additional residence the ALC allows on ALR land, at 90 or 186 square metres by parcel size, confirmed for the parcel (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
**Q:** Which schools serve a Durieu address? **A:** No school with a Durieu address is listed by the Province for School District No. 75 (Mission). The district list names Hatzic Elementary at 8465 Draper Street and Hatzic Middle School at 34875 Moffat Avenue in Mission, but the catchment for a specific Durieu address is unconfirmed (https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075).
**Q:** Is Durieu flood-exposed? **A:** The valley floor has flooded before, so check the parcel, not the area name. The FVRD records that in 1948 a dyke break on Hatzic Lake grew the lake to 25 square kilometres and left Hatzic Prairie largely under water, south of Durieu (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).
**Q:** Who supplies water on Sylvester Road? **A:** Only some Durieu-area lots are on a community system, so confirm the service area for the lot. The FVRD runs the Hatzic Prairie water system in Area F and publishes a water system map showing which properties are connected (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/water.html).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Cascade Falls Regional Park, an FVRD regional park in the Hatzic Valley northeast of Durieu, is the valley's named day-use park, listed in the FVRD parks snapshot (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/RGS/OutdoorRecSnapshot.pdf).
- Davis Lake Provincial Park sits at the north end of the valley off Sylvester Road and is listed by the FVRD among provincial parks in the regional district (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/RGS/OutdoorRecSnapshot.pdf).
- School District No. 75 assigns middle school by elementary catchment: Deroche, Dewdney, Hatzic and Stave Falls elementary catchments all feed Hatzic Middle School under district policy (https://media.mpsd.ca/media/Default/medialib/merged-agenda-package-cotw_dec03_2024.054b732103.pdf).
- Fill placed on ALR land in Area F needs Agricultural Land Commission approval, and FVRD staff reports for Stave Lake Road and Dale Road parcels in Area F show fill applications being refused forwarding to the ALC (https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=30556).
- The Hatzic Prairie drainage history in Section 4.4 of the Hatzic Valley Official Community Plan is the document FVRD staff cite for drainage issues on valley-floor parcels (https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=30556).

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Durieu owners pay property tax to the Province, not to a city, because Durieu is a rural area. The Province states rural owners get their tax notice from the Surveyor of Taxes Office (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area).
- Rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5 percent penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5 percent on amounts unpaid on October 31, under section 11 of the Taxation (Rural Area) Act (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01).
- City of Mission tax and utility figures do not apply here; see the mission-farms note for the city-wide figures.
Read: 2026-11-03

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- There is no confirmed scheduled bus service in Durieu itself; the road link is Sylvester Road south to Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and Stave Lake Road west into Mission, as the FVRD describes valley access (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).
- BC Transit and the FVRD consulted in April–May 2025 on a proposed new Highway 7 bus between Mission and Agassiz, which would be the first fixed route along the corridor east of Mission if funded (https://www.bctransit.com/have-your-say-in-the-future-of-transit-along-highway-2025-04-25/).

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the parcel in the Riparian Areas Development Permit Area before planning work near a creek, because FVRD staff reports state all properties in the Hatzic Valley Official Community Plan area sit in that permit area, with farm activities exempt from a permit (https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=30556).
- Check ALR status by parcel identifier number (PID) on the ALC maps, because most inhabited valley land is in the ALR and ALR status controls second homes and subdivision (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- Confirm well or community-water service and septic feasibility lot by lot on Sylvester Road and Stave Lake Road; no official page read states servicing for a specific Durieu lot (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/water.html).
