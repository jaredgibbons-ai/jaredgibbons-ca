## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Small Norfolk Street stratas are not exempt from depreciation reports: every British Columbia (BC) strata with five or more lots must obtain one on a five-year cycle since July 1, 2024, and only stratas of four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- A seven-unit self-managed strata, like the 3058 Smith Avenue complex in this frame, carries the same report duty: the provincial page makes no self-management exception for stratas of five or more lots. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Rezonings here proceed under the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, the bylaw amended by the applications on the City's current Zoning Bylaw Amendments page. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/meetings-and-public-hearings/public-hearings/zoning-bylaw-amendments

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is Central Burnaby a town centre like Metrotown?
**A:** No. Burnaby's growth framework names Metrotown as the City Centre and Brentwood, Lougheed and Edmonds as the Town Centres; Central Burnaby appears in none of those tiers, so its Norfolk Street and Canada Way stock changes by individual rezoning, not by a town centre plan. That is why the area has townhouse clusters instead of a tower district. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/burnaby-2050-planning-our-city-together/burnaby-2050-policy-guide-share-your-feedback/growth-scenarios

**Q:** Why are there two Winchelsea buildings?
**A:** The program index records Winchelsea at two Norfolk Street addresses, 3738 and 3733, as separate entries. They are different stratas across the same street, so a Form B Information Certificate, depreciation report or bylaw set pulled for one address does not describe the other. Confirm the civic address and strata plan number before subjects are removed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

**Q:** Does a small townhouse strata still need a depreciation report?
**A:** Yes, if it has five or more lots. Since July 1, 2024 every BC strata of five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report on a five-year cycle and can no longer defer it by annual three-quarter vote; Metro Vancouver stratas without a current report had a July 1, 2026 deadline. Only four-or-fewer-lot stratas are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

**Q:** How do I reach SkyTrain from Norfolk Street?
**A:** There is no station on Norfolk Street or Canada Way; the frame's station is Royal Oak, on the Expo Line south of the Canada Way corridor, named in TransLink's Expo Line station sequence east of Patterson alongside Metrotown and Edmonds. Canada Way buses link the Norfolk Street cluster to it. https://www.translink.ca/news/2026/october/translink%20replacing%2040-year-old%20elevator%20at%20patterson%20skytrain%20station

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date, on unpaid taxes and on an unclaimed home owner grant alike. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- Utility notices in Burnaby are payable by the annual March due date for the discounted amount, and unpaid utility fees transfer to property taxes as arrears after December 31 — a strata owner's unit-level bill, separate from strata fees. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
