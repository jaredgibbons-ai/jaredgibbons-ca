# Glenbrooke North Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: glenbrooke-north (Glenbrooke North, New Westminster; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Glenbrooke North is a mainland residential neighbourhood north of Queen's Park, wrapped around Glenbrook Ravine, Jamieson Court and Park Crescent in New Westminster — the ravine, not a main road, is its organising feature. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php
- Watch the spelling: the City spells the neighbourhood "Glenbrooke North" (with the second e) but the ravine, park and drive inside it are "Glenbrook" — Glenbrook Ravine Park, Glenbrook Drive — and the middle school is École Glenbrook Middle. https://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/community-parks/articles/5427.php
- What adjoins it: the Queen's Park Heritage Conservation Area to the south, Sapperton to the east across McBride Boulevard, Massey Victory Heights beyond Sapperton, and Downtown and Uptown to the south-west. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php
- No City page read states a surveyed boundary for this sub-area, so treat street-level edges as approximate and confirm a specific address's sub-area with the City — see NOT CONFIRMED.
- City-wide New Westminster facts are treated in full elsewhere — see the the-heights-new-westminster-houses-for-sale note; house-specific facts are in the glenbrooke-north-new-westminster-houses-for-sale note.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is single-detached houses on quiet residential streets, and the combined Glenbrooke North / Queen's Park context work documents some of the oldest dwellings in the city in this area — age is a feature of the housing stock here, not an exception. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Queens_Pk_NHood_Ctxt_Stmt_14_April_2009%281%29.pdf
- The one large civic building inside the area is a school: École Glenbrook Middle School at 701 Park Crescent, which offers Late French Immersion as a Program of Choice. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- Queens Park Care Centre, a Fraser Health facility at 315 McBride Boulevard, sits at the Queen's Park edge of the area — the institutional neighbour on the area's south-east corner. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/New-Westminster/royal-columbian-hospital
- No condo building or townhome complex name inside Glenbrooke North was confirmed on a developer, strata or property manager source in this pass — the strata share here is small and no building is named as fact; see NOT CONFIRMED.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Glenbrooke North is not inside the Queen's Park Heritage Conservation Area (HCA): the HCA adjoins it to the south, and no source read extends its Heritage Alteration Permit rules into Glenbrooke North — do not price in HCA constraints, or market HCA protection, for a Glenbrooke North address without a City confirmation for that address. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- Glenbrook Ravine is a watercourse corridor in the City's Green Zone, so ravine-edge lots can carry streamside setbacks that reduce the buildable area — the setback for a specific lot is confirmed parcel by parcel with the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/2_4 Parks and Open Space.pdf
- The area is documented in the City's Historic Neighbourhood Context Statements — context documentation is not the same as HCA protection, and it does not by itself restrict a renovation. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Queens_Pk_NHood_Ctxt_Stmt_14_April_2009%281%29.pdf
- City-wide infill changes adopted in June 2026 (up to six units on single-detached and duplex properties across most of the city, under RGO-A and RGO-B zoning) apply here as they do city-wide; the full treatment is in the the-heights-new-westminster-houses-for-sale note, and the ravine setback is the lot-level limit that matters most in this neighbourhood. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Glenbrooke North inside the Queen's Park Heritage Conservation Area? **A:** No — the Heritage Conservation Area covers the Queen's Park neighbourhood to the south, and Glenbrooke North only adjoins it. Glenbrooke North is documented in the City's Historic Neighbourhood Context Statements, which record its history, but no Heritage Alteration Permit layer applies on the sources read. That distinction changes what a buyer can do with an older house here, so confirm the specific address with the City if a listing leans on heritage either way. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Glenbrooke North address? **A:** School District 40 catchments are set by exact address in the district's school locator. For the sample address 91 Courtney Crescent, the locator result is F.W. Howay Elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 5), École Glenbrook Middle (Grades 6–8) and New Westminster Secondary (Grades 9–12). Glenbrook Middle School itself is inside the neighbourhood at 701 Park Crescent, and its Late French Immersion is a Program of Choice entered by application — it is not a catchment entitlement for local addresses. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- **Q:** What is Glenbrook Ravine like to live beside? **A:** It is a City park and trail corridor running through the neighbourhood, with the park entrance at 76 Jamieson Court and further access points at Jamieson Court (south) and via Kwantlen Court and Glenbrook Drive. Metro Vancouver's Glenbrook Sewer Upgrade has been working in the ravine, with site restoration — irrigation, signage, new asphalt and planting — under way in 2026 and short temporary trail-entrance closures, so a home beside the ravine trades a green setting for periodic utility construction. https://metrovancouver.org/services/liquid-waste/glenbrook-sewer-upgrade-glenbrook-ravine-section?utm_source=newwestanchor.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=more-construction-on-the-way
- **Q:** How do I get to SkyTrain from Glenbrooke North? **A:** There is no station in the neighbourhood; the nearest are Columbia Station and Sapperton Station on the Expo Line. TransLink route 123 (New Westminster Station / Brentwood Station) and route 102 (Victoria Hill / New Westminster Station) serve the north mainland corridors around the area. In practice that makes Glenbrooke North a bus-or-drive-to-station neighbourhood, unlike the Downtown and Quay neighbourhoods at the stations themselves. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf
- **Q:** Does the ravine restrict what I can build on an edge lot? **A:** It can: Glenbrook Ravine is a watercourse corridor in the City's Green Zone, and streamside setbacks are lot-specific, so the buildable envelope of a ravine-edge lot can be smaller than the zoning alone suggests. The zoning numbers are in the glenbrooke-north-new-westminster-houses-for-sale note; the setback for the specific lot has to be confirmed with the City before a buyer prices an addition or a rebuild on that lot. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/2_4 Parks and Open Space.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Glenbrook Ravine Park's listed address is 76 Jamieson Court, and its parking and washrooms are up at the nearby təməsew̓txʷ Aquatic and Community Centre, with limited accessible parking on Jamieson Court itself. https://www.newwestcity.ca/calendar-of-events/events/8701/2026-04.php
- The ravine is actively restored by volunteers: the Glenbrook Ravine Restoration weed pulls, led by the Invasive Species Council of Metro Vancouver with City Parks and Recreation and New Westminster Environmental Partners, target Himalayan blackberry in the ravine's middle section. https://www.newwestcity.ca/calendar-of-events/events/8675.php
- Metro Vancouver's sewer work has closed ravine access points in turn — trail access via Kwantlen Court was closed for approximately two months and intermittently through the project, and the Jamieson Court trail entrance closed temporarily in February 2026 during site restoration — so which entrance is open changes with the project phase, not permanently. https://metrovancouver.org/services/liquid-waste/glenbrook-sewer-upgrade-glenbrook-ravine-section?utm_source=newwestanchor.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=more-construction-on-the-way
- The nearest hospital is Royal Columbian Hospital, 330 East Columbia Street in Sapperton, east of the area across McBride Boulevard — the hospital is also a redevelopment site on the City's projects list (named in Section 6). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/New-Westminster/royal-columbian-hospital
- The nearest library branch is the Main Branch of the New Westminster Public Library at 716 6th Avenue, south-west in Uptown. https://www.nwpl.ca/
- Queen's Park — arena, sportsplex, stadium and sports fields — is at the area's south edge, so Glenbrooke North's arena and stadium access runs through the Queen's Park complex, not a facility of its own. http://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/community-parks

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Ravine-edge lots are the due-diligence item here: Glenbrook Ravine is a watercourse corridor, streamside setbacks are lot-specific, and the City source does not give a setback for any individual lot — get the lot's setback and slope conditions from the City before pricing an addition or rebuild. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/2_4 Parks and Open Space.pdf
- Utility construction in the ravine is a live, multi-year file: Metro Vancouver states construction for the Glenbrook sewer system will occur in multiple phases over several years, so ravine-adjacent buyers should check the project page for the current phase and trail closures. https://metrovancouver.org/services/liquid-waste/glenbrook-sewer-upgrade-glenbrook-ravine-section?utm_source=newwestanchor.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=more-construction-on-the-way
- Draw the Heritage Conservation Area line precisely: the Queen's Park HCA adjoins the area, and a listing that claims HCA protection — or a buyer who assumes HCA restrictions — both need an address-level confirmation from the City, because the boundary does not follow the neighbourhood's marketing edges. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- A floodplain designation for this area was not confirmed in the sources read: the City's floodplain study zones are at the city's low-lying edges, and lot-level status for a Glenbrooke North property is confirmed with the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
