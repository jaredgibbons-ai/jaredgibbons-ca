# South Surrey / White Rock — Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer, for a voice memo. Muse does not answer these.

1. Who is buying waterfront on Marine Drive and in Crescent Beach right now — local downsizers, Vancouver sellers cashing out, or out-of-province buyers?
2. What sells fast on the waterfront and what sits — true water's-edge lots, or view homes one street back that trade on the view alone?
3. What surprises waterfront buyers most: the BNSF rail line along the shore, flood construction levels and insurance, or how the Surrey and White Rock sides of the strip differ in rules?
4. For ocean-view (non-waterfront) homes, what do buyers actually pay for — the white-water view, the sunset angle, or the street itself?
5. Tell one story from a waterfront or view deal — a view blocked or protected, a dike or foreshore issue in the documents, or a bidding war over a view corridor.
