# Port Moody Centre Condos and Townhomes — Extra Local Notes
Page: port-moody-centre-condos-townhomes-for-sale (Port Moody Centre, Port Moody; condos and townhomes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Port Moody Centre is the south-shore centre of Port Moody, around two SkyTrain stations: Moody Centre Station on the St. Johns Street corridor and Inlet Centre Station at Barnet Highway and Ioco Road. http://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/skytrain_station_maps/inlet_centre_station.pdf
- The City's Moody Centre Station Transit-Oriented Development (TOD) Area currently extends from the Canadian Pacific Railway line to St. Johns Street and from Electronic Avenue to Moody Street, with Moody Centre Station (SkyTrain, bus, West Coast Express) as its focal point. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050?tool=qanda
- The combined Inlet Centre and Coronation Park area extends from Noons Creek Drive to Murray Street, and from St. Johns Street and Barnet Highway to the Coquitlam municipal border. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050?page=2&tool=story_telling_tool
- Port Moody–shared facts are carried in the port-moody-condos-for-sale note and the neighbourhood description in the port-moody-centre-houses-for-sale note; this note is centre-specific only.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Height rules split at the station: the provincial Transit-Oriented Area (TOA) around Moody Centre Station sets minimum heights the City must allow — up to 20 storeys within 200 metres, 12 storeys within 400 metres and 8 storeys within 800 metres. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050?tool=qanda
- The City's own designation goes higher in the core: the Moody Centre Station Transit-Oriented Area – Core designation in Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw, 2026, No. 3522 supports high-density, mixed-use development around the station with building heights up to 26 storeys, with site-specific proposals above that considered under the Moody Centre TOD Guidance Framework. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/m3sbv1j5/draft-official-community-plan-bylaw-2026-no-3522-amendment-bylaw-no-1-2026-no-3547-density-bonus.pdf
- Coronation Park has its own height sections: Area A mixed use up to 31 storeys and Area B residential up to 26 storeys under the plan vision the City carried into Port Moody 2050, with a third Area C designated multi-residential up to 8 storeys subject to a master plan. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050?page=2&tool=story_telling_tool
- Port Moody 2050, the new OCP, was adopted by Council on April 28, 2026 — plan references dated before that adoption are superseded and should be checked current. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050?page=2&tool=story_telling_tool

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Port Moody Centre one neighbourhood or two station areas? **A:** Two station areas inside one centre. Moody Centre Station anchors the TOD Area between the rail line and St. Johns Street, from Electronic Avenue to Moody Street, while Inlet Centre Station anchors the Suter Brook, Newport and Coronation Park side. A condo's station, and its distance band from that station, decides which height and density rules apply to its site. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050?tool=qanda
- **Q:** What does the Coronation Park rezoning mean for nearby townhomes? **A:** It replaces a house neighbourhood with a transit-oriented district over time. Wesgroup's application, rezoning file REZ00030, covers 59 properties in Coronation Park currently zoned One-Family Residential (RS1), proposed for a Comprehensive Development zone; the rezoning bylaw received three readings after the October 3, 2023 public hearing. Existing townhomes nearby are not rezoned by it, but construction and land assembly are a due-diligence item. https://engage.portmoody.ca/coronation-park-rezoning
- **Q:** Do strata townhomes here pay different city utilities than condos? **A:** Yes — Port Moody bills utilities by housing type. For 2026 the City lists total utility rates of $1,662 for a townhouse and $1,116 for a condo or apartment with full collection services, covering water, sewer, recycling, green waste, glass and garbage. The 2026 utility bill was due February 27, 2026, separate from property taxes. https://www.portmoody.ca/utilities
- **Q:** Which schools are in the centre itself? **A:** Moody Elementary at 110 Buller Street (Kindergarten to Grade 5) and École Moody Middle School of the Arts at 3115 St. Johns Street (Grades 6 to 8) sit inside the centre, with Port Moody Secondary at 300 Albert Street. School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) catchment is by home address, so a specific condo or townhouse still needs the district's address check. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Moody Centre Station is also a West Coast Express station, so the same stop gives downtown Vancouver commuter rail as well as SkyTrain — Inlet Centre Station does not. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Rocky Point Park, in the 2800 block of Murray Street, is the centre's waterfront park, and the Port Moody Recreation Complex is at 300 Ioco Road by Inlet Centre. http://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/discover-port-moody/getting-around/
- Utilities and taxes are billed separately in Port Moody: utility bills are due in February and property taxes in July, and the Province will not defer utility fees under the tax deferment program. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Port Moody's 2026 municipal tax rate for Class 1 Residential is $2.71318 per $1,000 of assessed value, and the grand total with other governments is $4.29125 per $1,000, per the City's Summary of 2026 Tax Rates. http://www.portmoody.ca/media/tiabceqp/2026-mill-rates.pdf
- 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 15, 2026. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/property-taxes/
- Type-specific utilities for 2026: townhouse $1,662 and condo or apartment with full collection services $1,116 for water, sewer, garbage, green waste, recycling and glass collection combined. https://www.portmoody.ca/utilities
- Port Moody is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Inlet Centre Station, 2300 Barnet Highway, is served by bus routes 160, 179, 183, 184 and N9 per TransLink's station map, as well as the Millennium Line. http://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/skytrain_station_maps/inlet_centre_station.pdf
- Route 160 runs Port Coquitlam Station to Kootenay Loop via both Inlet Centre and Moody Centre stations, and Route 183 runs Moody Centre Station to Coquitlam Central Station via Inlet Centre Station and St. Johns Street. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt183.pdf
- Barnet Highway and St. Johns Street are the two road corridors through the centre, joining at the east end toward Coquitlam; no measured drive time is stated here.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check which TOA distance band a building sits in before assuming its redevelopment value: the 200, 400 and 800 metre bands around each station carry different minimum heights, and the band is measured from the station, not the neighbourhood edge. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050?tool=qanda
- Check the rail corridor: the Moody Centre TOD Area runs from the Canadian Pacific Railway line south to St. Johns Street, so the northern tier of buildings sits beside an active rail line — a noise and vibration item for strata minutes and depreciation reports. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050?tool=qanda
- Check creek and inlet frontage per lot on City mapping: Noons Creek forms the west edge of the Inlet Centre area description, and no lot-level flood or slope conclusion is given by the plan pages read — that check remains with the buyer.
