# Sardis East / Vedder Area Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: sardis-east-vedder-guide (Sardis East / Vedder, Chilliwack; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sardis East and Vedder sit on Chilliwack's south side, east of Vedder Road: Sardis is the plan area immediately south of Highway 1, and Vedder (South Vedder) continues south from Sardis to the Chilliwack/Vedder River. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- The Sardis Neighbourhood Plan (OCP Schedule B5, adopted October 2020, updated 2025) bounds Sardis by the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) to the west and east, First Nations lands to the south and east, and the Vedder neighbourhood to the south. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- The South Vedder Neighbourhood Plan (OCP Schedule B6, adopted April 2022, updated 2025) puts Vedder Road as South Vedder's western boundary, with the Chilliwack/Vedder River to the south, Promontory to the south-east and Ch'íyáqtel (Tzeachten) First Nation lands to the east. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6734
- The City notes the boundary between Sardis and Vedder was fluid for years; the OCP fixed the two neighbourhood boundaries to support detailed neighbourhood planning. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6734

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- East Sardis housing clusters around named plan sub-areas in the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan, including Wells Landing, south of Wells Road between the ALR lands and Vedder Road, which the plan describes as a mix of residential uses of varying age and form. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- South Vedder's stock mixes older single-family homes along Vedder Road and the river with the newer master-planned streets of Garrison Crossing, where Canada Lands Company refurbished former CFB Chilliwack married quarters and added new townhouses and condominium apartments. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6734 https://www.clc-sic.ca/node/69
- Sardis-general housing stock facts shared with the earlier Sardis pages are written in the sardis-houses-for-sale note; this note covers only the east side and Vedder.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Two different neighbourhood plans govern this one guide area: Sardis properties follow the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan (OCP Schedule B5) and Vedder properties the South Vedder Neighbourhood Plan (Schedule B6), so the plan that applies depends on which side of the Sardis–Vedder boundary the lot sits. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230 https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6734
- The South Vedder Plan area east of Vedder Road shares a boundary with Tzeachten First Nation along Keith Wilson Road, so lots at the east edge sit beside First Nation lands that the City does not zone. https://www.engagechilliwack.com/southvedder/widgets/95165/faqs
- City-wide zoning, small-scale multi-unit housing and floodplain rules for Sardis and Vedder are written in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note; see that note.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Vedder part of Sardis or its own neighbourhood? **A:** It is its own plan area now. The City's Official Community Plan fixed separate boundaries for Sardis (Schedule B5) and Vedder after years of fluid use of the two names, and South Vedder has its own neighbourhood plan, adopted April 2022. In daily use many residents still say "Sardis" for the whole south side, but permits and plans follow the OCP boundary. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6734
- **Q:** Which schools serve the east side of Sardis? **A:** Sardis Elementary is at 45775 Manuel Road and Sardis Secondary at 45460 Stevenson Road, both in east Sardis, but School District No. 33 sets catchments by street address, so confirm the exact address in the district's School Locator before relying on either school. Vedder addresses may instead fall to Vedder Elementary at 45850 Promontory Road or Vedder Middle School at 45560 South Sumas Road. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/033
- **Q:** What is Garrison Crossing, and why do its streets look different? **A:** Garrison Crossing is the redevelopment of the former CFB Chilliwack married quarters by Canada Lands Company: a 153-acre community of more than 1,700 units where refurbished military homes keep original fir floors and street names, and a new road, Sappers Way, was named for the engineers who served at the base from 1942 until its closure. https://www.clc-sic.ca/node/69 https://www.veterans.gc.ca/en/remembrance/memorials/canada/garrison-crossing-street-names-plaque
- **Q:** Does the Vedder River flood the Vedder neighbourhood? **A:** The river is the neighbourhood's south boundary and its main hazard check. The City closes parts of the Vedder Rotary Trail during its Vedder River sediment removal program, which it runs to restore the river's capacity to contain floodwater; check the lot against the City's floodplain mapping and Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 before buying. http://chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3171
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Sardis Park, a 5-hectare former gravel pit at 6898 School Lane in the park inventory since 1971, has a 655-metre outer looping trail around its pond and hosts the Eco Market on Wednesday nights from May until October. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=117
- The Vedder Rotary Loop Trail runs more than 20 kilometres around the Vedder River from Vedder Park, crossing the Vedder Canal on the Keith Wilson Bridge and passing the Great Blue Heron Nature Reserve at kilometre 6.5. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=144
- Vedder Park parking lots and trail sections close intermittently for the City's river sediment removal and trail restoration work; the Lickman Road lot was listed closed until November 13, 2026. http://chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3171
- The Luckakuck Creek Trail corridor runs through north-east Sardis, and the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan's North Evans sub-area includes a portion of that trail corridor along Evans Road. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Garrison Crossing's Cheamview Crescent loop was deliberately kept at its original PMQ (Permanent Married Quarters) road width, with no curbs, gutters or sidewalks, to preserve how the base streets looked. https://www.veterans.gc.ca/en/remembrance/memorials/canada/garrison-crossing-street-names-plaque

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 51 Vedder is the main bus spine for this area, running between Downtown Chilliwack and the University of the Fraser Valley Chilliwack campus via Vedder Road, with timing points at Vedder Road at Promontory Road and Vedder Road at Luckakuck Way. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=51&schedule=2024-07-05
- Route 66 Fraser Valley Express stops at Vedder Road at Luckakuck Way, giving east Sardis a direct regional bus to Abbotsford, Langley and Lougheed Town Centre Station. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- The Vedder Rotary Loop Trail gives the area a 20-plus-kilometre off-road cycling and walking loop, with parking at Vedder Park, Peach Park, Lickman Road and Hopedale Road. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=144

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Vedder River sediment is this area's live flood issue: the City states that extreme weather has deposited more sediment in the Vedder River and Canal than typical, raising the river's height and increasing flood risk to the city, which is why it removes sediment in the August–September in-stream works window. http://chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3171
- The OCP treats the Sardis–Vedder area as an exception to Chilliwack's floodplain pattern — the plan states that, except for the hillsides and the Sardis–Vedder area, Chilliwack is situated on floodplains — but lots fronting the Vedder River and Luckakuck Creek still need a lot-specific floodplain and riparian (DPA 3) check. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/basic-html/page-54.html
