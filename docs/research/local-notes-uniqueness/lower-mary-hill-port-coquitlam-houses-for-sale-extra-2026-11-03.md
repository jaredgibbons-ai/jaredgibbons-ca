## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Lower Mary Hill in the floodplain? **A:** Treat every lot as a flood check: much of Port Coquitlam is within the Fraser or Pitt River flood plain behind dikes raised in 2007, and this sub-area's low Pitt River Road ground is that setting. Confirm the specific lot on City flood maps. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/city-port-coquitlam-prepares-potential-flooding
- **Q:** Which schools are closest on the official list? **A:** École Mary Hill Elementary, kindergarten to Grade 5, 1890 Humber Crescent; École Pitt River Middle, Grades 6–8, 2070 Tyner Street; École Riverside Secondary, Grades 9–12, 2215 Reeve Street. School District No. 43 (SD43) assigns by address; confirm catchment in its School Locator Tool. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-coquitlam
- **Q:** Can a Pitt River Road lot be split? **A:** It has been applied for here: at 1612 Pitt River Road, Bylaw No. 4243 (RS1 to RS4) reached third reading subject to demolition and off-site works including a fire hydrant and Pitt River Road pedestrian access. Each lot needs its own approval. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/FileStream.ashx?DocumentId=8497
- **Q:** What bus runs on Pitt River Road? **A:** Two TransLink routes: route 170, the Port Coquitlam South loop, via Pitt River Road toward Eastern and Western Drives, and route 188 on Pitt River Road toward Coast Meridian Road and Coquitlam Central Station. Check the timetable for the nearest stop. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt170.pdf
