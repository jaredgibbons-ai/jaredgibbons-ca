## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A foreclosure in British Columbia (BC) must be started by petition under Supreme Court Civil Rule 21-7, and every person whose interest in the mortgaged property is to be extinguished must be made a respondent. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- The court may fix a redemption period and, if the amount due is not paid in that period, grant a final order of foreclosure; a party of record may also apply at any time for an order that the property be sold, which is where court-ordered sales come from. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- A petitioner who registers a CPL against the Maple Ridge title binds anyone who later registers an interest, and that person need not be served with the petition. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a Maple Ridge tax sale the same as a court-ordered sale? **A:** No — they are two different sales run by two different bodies. The City's annual Tax Sale auctions properties with three years of unpaid taxes, while a court-ordered sale is a Supreme Court sale inside a foreclosure petition under Rule 21-7. A buyer researching one should not use the other's dates, deposits or redemption rules. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/property-taxes/tax-sale https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q:** How long does a Maple Ridge owner get to redeem after the City's tax sale? **A:** One year from the auction date. To redeem, the owner must pay all three years of taxes plus fees, penalties and interest; if the year passes unredeemed, the tax-sale purchaser moves toward title, and property transfer tax is calculated on the property's market value at that transfer. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/property-taxes/tax-sale
- **Q:** What is the lowest bid the City can accept at its tax sale? **A:** The upset price, and nothing below it. The City defines the upset price as all outstanding taxes plus penalties and interest, a 5% administration fee, and Land Title Office fees, and the City itself states it will be an active bidder at the sale. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/property-taxes/tax-sale
- **Q:** Where is a Maple Ridge foreclosure file physically handled? **A:** The land-title side is certain and the court-registry side is not confirmed here. Title documents register in the New Westminster Land Title District, but no official BC Courts source read for this note assigns Maple Ridge Supreme Court filings to a single named registry, so a buyer should have their lawyer confirm the registry from the filed petition itself. https://ltpm.ltsa.ca/node/105

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Maple Ridge's 2026 Tax Sale was scheduled for Monday, September 28, 2026, and an owner could pull a property off the list by paying all delinquent taxes by 10 a.m. that day. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/property-taxes/tax-sale
- Tax-sale purchases in Maple Ridge must be paid in certified funds within two hours of the auction ending — a buyer who wins cannot go away and arrange financing afterwards. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/property-taxes/tax-sale
- Maple Ridge property taxes are due the first business day in July (July 2 in 2026), and the City puts flat-rate utilities on the same single property tax notice, so arrears on one combined notice are what feed a future tax sale. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/property-taxes/understanding-property-taxes
- A court-ordered sale approval hearing is an application filing: the Supreme Court Civil Rules fee schedule charges $80 to file a notice of application, plus $7 per document package sent through Court Services Online electronic filing. https://free.bcpublications.ca/civix/document/id/roc/roc/168_2009_06
- Costs in an uncontested foreclosure under Rule 21-7 are assessed under Scale A, the lowest costs scale in the rules' costs appendix. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/roc/roc/168_2009_05

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Court filing fees are provincial, not municipal: commencing documents and applications in a foreclosure are priced from Appendix C, Schedule 1 of the Supreme Court Civil Rules — $80 to file a notice of application and a $7 electronic filing fee per package through Court Services Online. https://free.bcpublications.ca/civix/document/id/roc/roc/168_2009_06
- The City's own sale costs sit in the tax-sale upset price: outstanding taxes, penalties and interest, plus a 5% administration fee and Land Title Office fees. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/property-taxes/tax-sale
- Maple Ridge is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so a buyer who leaves a court-bought home empty still owes the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Title is the first check: a Maple Ridge foreclosure title sits in the New Westminster Land Title District, and the CPL registered by the petitioner is what binds later interests — order the title and the court file before bidding. https://ltpm.ltsa.ca/node/105 https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- A tax-sale property is a separate risk: the owner keeps a one-year redemption right after the auction, so a tax-sale buyer does not get an unassailable title on sale day. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/property-taxes/tax-sale
- The court file, not the listing, states the amount due and the redemption period the court fixed — both come from the petition record under Rule 21-7, and neither appears on a marketing page. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
