## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Pioneer Park, 27111 0 Avenue, Aldergrove: a manufactured-home housing co-operative, carried on the settled roster as 55+. The buyer owns the manufactured home and holds co-operative shares and occupancy rights — the land is held through the co-operative, so this is neither fee-simple land ownership, nor a strata lot, nor a Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) pad tenancy. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- Verified pad-rental parks in Aldergrove: none. Pioneer Park is Aldergrove's only verified manufactured-home community in the settled program, and its tenure is co-operative — a buyer cross-shopping Brookswood's pad parks is comparing a different legal form, not a different park brand. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- Pioneer Park's age and acceptance position is UNRESOLVED in the roster: its home file carries two different listing wordings for the 55+ rule, and listings state a buyer must be accepted by the co-operative and purchase shares — no co-operative or Township source read on 2026-11-03 states the current rule, share position or monthly charge, so all three must be obtained from the co-operative in writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Not Aldergrove stock, by name: Creekside Villa, 27435 29A Avenue, is a gated 55+ rancher townhouse strata, and Alderwood Manor, 27090 32 Avenue, is built townhouse form — neither is a manufactured-home park, and Langley Grove Estates is Otter District ground, not Aldergrove. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/Att-B---Aldergrove-CP---Redlined.pdf
- Tenure line: a home on a rented pad is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad. That line describes Brookswood's parks; at Pioneer Park there is no pad and no landlord, and the co-operative's share and occupancy documents replace the pad tenancy entirely. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The MHPTA pad-rent caps do not set Pioneer Park's monthly charge: the co-operative is not a pad tenancy, so the provincial caps stated in full in the brookswood-mobile-homes-for-sale note (2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount) govern Brookswood's pads, not a co-op fee — see the brookswood-mobile-homes-for-sale note, and see the cost-of-mobile-home-pad-rental-fees-in-langley note for the full pad-rent cost rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- A Pioneer Park purchase has two records to diligence, not one: the provincial Manufactured Home Registry record for the home itself, and the co-operative's share and occupancy record — the Registry transfer alone does not transfer co-op membership or acceptance. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- The Strata Property Act's 55+ bylaw exemptions do not automatically answer a Pioneer Park occupancy question: a co-operative sets its own occupancy rules in its own documents, so a strata age-bylaw page is background only and the co-op's current written rule governs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Township frame for Aldergrove ground: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 and the Aldergrove Community Plan govern the land under and around Pioneer Park, and the Township's small-scale multi-unit housing Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024, no 6-plex tier) creates no added-unit right inside a co-operative park — Township-wide detail is in the brookswood-mobile-homes-for-sale note. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Pioneer Park a pad-rental park like the Brookswood parks? **A:** No, and the difference changes the whole purchase. At Cedar Creek Estates or Fernridge Park you own the home and rent a pad from a landlord under provincial tenancy law. At Pioneer Park, 27111 0 Avenue, you own the home, buy co-operative shares, pay a monthly co-op charge, and must be accepted by the co-operative — there is no landlord, no pad rent and no tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Q:** Does the provincial pad-rent cap protect my monthly charge at Pioneer Park? **A:** No. The cap — one increase a year on 3 full months' notice, 2026 at 2.3% plus a proportional amount — applies to manufactured home site tenancies. A co-operative charge is set under the co-operative's own documents and bylaws, not the tenancy cap, so ask the co-operative how its charge is set and how it has changed, in writing, before an offer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q:** What happens if Pioneer Park ever winds up or closes? **A:** Not the provincial park-closure route. The 12-month notice and $20,000 compensation rules govern a landlord converting a pad park to another use; a co-operative's winding-up position lives in its own documents and co-operative law, and no source read on 2026-11-03 states Pioneer Park's position. Read the co-op documents, not the tenancy pages, for this question. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q:** Which school would a child at Pioneer Park attend? **A:** Unconfirmed for that address. School District No. 35 (Langley) settles catchments by address on its School Locator, and its settled chain for the sample address 26850 29 Avenue is Parkside Centennial Elementary, Betty Gilbert Middle School and Aldergrove Community Secondary — but no locator run for 27111 0 Avenue is documented, and Pioneer Park is carried as a 55+ community in any case. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- **Q:** Can I just move a manufactured home onto a rural Aldergrove lot instead? **A:** That is a land question, not a park question. Rural Aldergrove lots sit under Township zoning and, for many parcels, Agricultural Land Reserve rules — the Agricultural Land Commission's housing-in-the-ALR pages govern what housing the reserve allows, and the home itself still needs its Manufactured Home Registry record and CSA (Canadian Standards Association) label to be sold or moved. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Pioneer Park runs on a third legal system: not strata (no Form B, no strata bylaws) and not a pad tenancy (no pad-rent cap, no Residential Tenancy Branch pad process) — its acceptance, share and occupancy documents are the whole rulebook, and diligence done in either of the other two forms asks the wrong questions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- Pioneer Park's address is the border: 27111 0 Avenue is Aldergrove's south edge on the United States boundary, a rural setting apart from the Fraser Highway core — the opposite corner of the community from the Aldergrove Credit Union Community Centre at 27032 Fraser Highway. https://www.tol.ca/en/parks-recreation/aldergrove-community-centre.aspx
- Bertrand Creek bounds the Aldergrove Core Area to the north and east, so core-adjacent lots — not the 0 Avenue park site — are the ones where a watercourse and riparian check matters most; the Township directs applicants to check watercourses in its GeoSource mapping before applying. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/Att-B---Aldergrove-CP---Redlined.pdf
- Schools physically in Aldergrove, School District No. 35: Parkside Centennial Elementary (3300 270 Street), Shortreed Community Elementary (27330 28 Avenue), Coghlan Fundamental Elementary (4452 256 Street), North Otter Elementary (5370 248 Street), Betty Gilbert Middle School (26845 27 Avenue) and Aldergrove Community Secondary (26850 29 Avenue) — catchments are by address on the district locator, never by community name. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- The nearest hospital confirmed on a health-authority source is Langley Memorial Hospital, 22051 Fraser Highway, west of Aldergrove in Murrayville; no urgent care centre or public health unit inside Aldergrove itself was verified. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital
- Much of the Township's land is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, and Aldergrove's rural fringe is where that bites: a manufactured home on an owned rural lot near Aldergrove can face reserve housing rules that a park home never meets. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

- Co-operative documents are the diligence: for Pioneer Park, obtain the co-operative's current share, acceptance, occupancy and monthly-charge documents in writing — no provincial tenancy or strata page substitutes for them, and the roster's listing-stated figures for shares, charges and pets are dated listing text, never carried as fact in this note. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
