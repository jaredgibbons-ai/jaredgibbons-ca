# Eastern Hillsides Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: eastern-hillsides (Eastern Hillsides, Chilliwack; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Eastern Hillsides is a hillside plan area in southeast Chilliwack, inside the City of Chilliwack. https://www.chilliwack.com/ocp
- Its edges are the Ryder Lake uplands to the west, Lookout Ridge and Elk Mountain to the south, the Cascade foothills and Fraser Valley Regional District boundary to the east, and Highway 1 (Trans-Canada Highway) and valley-floor farmland to the north. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- Marble Hill is a named slope area inside the plan area, and internal streets named in City park listings include Allan Road, Rowanna Crescent and Marble Hill Road. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- It sits across the valley floor from Promontory and Sardis to the northwest, with Rosedale and Bridal Falls east along Highway 1. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The plan describes early development as large semi-serviced lots with single-family homes, with small-lot zoning in later development cells. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- Development is organized into development cells with unit allocations, and those allocations still regulate how many units may be developed in the plan area. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- Lot size is set by zone and slope together: Suburban Hillside Residential (SHR) minimum lot area runs 360 square metres to 990 square metres under the Zoning Bylaw slope table. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- No condo building or townhome complex inside the plan area was named with an address on a City list read in this pass; the established stock the plan describes is freehold detached housing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The governing plan is the Eastern Hillsides Neighbourhood Plan, Schedule B4 of the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan (Bylaw 2025, No. 5500). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- Zoning is City of Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000: Small Lot Residential (R3) minimum lot is 360 square metres, Hillside Residential (HR) is 0.2 hectares with a community water system and 0.4 hectares otherwise, and Rural Hillside (RH) is 8 hectares. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- Subdivision of land with slopes of 20 percent or more needs a development permit under the 2050 Official Community Plan's Hillside Development Permit Area (Schedule D-5). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- The plan was amended in 2017 specifically to protect future development from rock fall, landslide and earthflow where conditions are unstable. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1962
- The historic wagon road, Browlee Road, is identified in the plan as a heritage asset, and wagon roads in the area include Nixon, Ruddock, Allan and Hinkley. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there a school in Eastern Hillsides? **A:** No — there is no school inside the plan area, and the Neighbourhood Plan states that directly. School District 33 (Chilliwack) catchments are address-based through the district School Locator; the recorded sample for 51314 Rowanna Crescent is East Chilliwack Elementary, Rosedale Traditional Community School and Chilliwack Secondary, so every child travels off the hillside. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- **Q:** What is the Marble Hill slide issue? **A:** Part of Marble Hill is treated as undevelopable land. The City's Greenspace Plan identifies the lower and upper Marble Hill slide areas as undevelopable, and Council directed an expanded Marble Hill development moratorium area in 2017 after continuing ground movement was reported, so a Marble Hill lot needs its geotechnical record checked before any build is assumed. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2650/2021-05-28%20GSP%20MASTER%202020%20-%20Urban%20Format.pdf
- **Q:** Does slope change the minimum lot size? **A:** Yes — slope alone can almost triple it. Under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 the Suburban Hillside Residential minimum lot area ranges from 360 square metres to 990 square metres by the bylaw's slope table, with a 12-metre minimum width and 20-metre minimum depth, so the zone name alone does not settle what a lot can do. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q:** Is there bus service on the hillsides? **A:** No route runs into the plan area. BC Transit's Chilliwack route overview lists routes 51, 52, 53, 54, 55, 57, 58, 59, 66, 71 and 72 for the city system, and none of them serves the Eastern Hillsides plan area, so daily trips are by car. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Utilities and solid waste are billed quarterly in Chilliwack, not on the tax notice. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- Water rates sit in the Waterworks Regulation Bylaw 2004, No. 2995 and sewer rates in the Sanitary Sewer System Regulation Bylaw 2010, No. 3702, both linked from the City's Tax and Utility Rates page. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- Municipal tax revenue in Chilliwack also carries drainage and dyking levies on top of the general rate, per the City's Tax and Utility Rates page. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies in the City of Chilliwack, which the Province lists as a designated taxable area, so residential owners must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Geotechnical hazard is the first check here: steep slopes, main watercourses and ravines cross the plan area, and the plan identifies geotechnical hazard areas. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1962
- Look the lot up against the Hillside Development Permit Area (Schedule D-5) in the 2050 Official Community Plan, because subdivision at 20 percent slope or more requires a permit. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status for any lot at the valley-floor north edge in the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder, because farmland borders the plan area there. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- The City pages do not give a lot's slide history or covenant terms; those come from the property's own geotechnical reports and title documents. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2650/2021-05-28%20GSP%20MASTER%202020%20-%20Urban%20Format.pdf
