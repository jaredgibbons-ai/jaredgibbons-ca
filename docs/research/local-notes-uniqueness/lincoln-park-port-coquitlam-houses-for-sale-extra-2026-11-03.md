# Lincoln Park Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: lincoln-park-port-coquitlam-houses-for-sale (Lincoln Park; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Lincoln Park is an established residential neighbourhood on Port Coquitlam's north side, around Lincoln Avenue and Lincoln Drive, Laurier Avenue and Coast Meridian Road, with Fraser Avenue at its south side; the City publishes no street-by-street boundary for this sub-area, so treat street limits as a working orientation, not an official line. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-03/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan%201.pdf
- The City's Official Community Plan (OCP) groups Lincoln Park with Mary Hill as established residential neighbourhoods which developed from the 1950s onwards, distinct from the newer Riverwood and Citadel areas. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-03/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan%201.pdf
- Hyde Creek runs through the neighbourhood's east side: the City lists Hyde Creek Nature Reserve at 1457 Lincoln Drive, inside the street pattern the houses front. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/recreation-parks/parks-trails/parks-directory/hyde-creek-nature-reserve

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Lincoln Park houses follow the established pattern the OCP describes for this neighbourhood — a neighbourhood developed from the 1950s onwards — rather than a new-subdivision pattern, so era and condition are house-by-house checks, not a single build period. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-03/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan%201.pdf
- The subdivision floor for the main house zones is set city-wide in Zoning Bylaw No. 3630 and is stated in full in the mary-hill-port-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; Lincoln Park lots are checked against those Residential Small-Scale zone minimums lot by lot. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf
- In-area anchors are the two School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) schools inside the streets: Birchland Elementary at 1331 Fraser Avenue and Minnekhada Middle School at 1390 Laurier Avenue, with Hyde Creek Recreation Centre at 1379 Laurier Avenue between them on the same street as the middle school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City-wide rules — Zoning Bylaw, 2008, No. 3630, small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH), the OCP Bylaw, 2013, No. 3838 frame, 2026 tax and utility figures — are in full in the mary-hill-port-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; this note covers the Lincoln Park-specific checks. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Work near Hyde Creek or another watercourse, ditch or adjacent environmentally sensitive land in a designated area needs a Watercourse Development Permit, or a City-confirmed exemption, before filling, clearing vegetation, paving or building — the first permit check for a Lincoln Drive or Laurier Avenue lot backing onto the creek corridor. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/development-permits/watercourse-development
- The OCP designates Transit-Oriented Areas (TOAs) in Port Coquitlam, including lands adjacent to the Lincoln SkyTrain Station across the Coquitlam border, with prescribed residential density and height tiers — relevant context for the west edge of this neighbourhood closest to that station, with the tier map in the OCP itself. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-03/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan%201.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools are physically inside Lincoln Park? **A:** Two School District No. 43 schools sit inside the neighbourhood: Birchland Elementary at 1331 Fraser Avenue for kindergarten to Grade 5, and Minnekhada Middle School at 1390 Laurier Avenue for Grades 6 to 8. No secondary school sits inside the sub-area. Attendance is assigned by permanent home address, so run the specific address in the district School Locator before relying on either school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** What is at Hyde Creek Recreation Centre, and where exactly is it? **A:** The City lists Hyde Creek Recreation Centre at 1379 Laurier Avenue, eleven numbers from Minnekhada Middle School on the same street, with pool, fitness, children and youth programs and racquet courts in the City's Fall 2026 Leisure Guide, open Monday to Friday 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. and Saturday and Sunday 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. It is this neighbourhood's own recreation centre, not a downtown facility. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/cityofpocofall26leisureguide03singlepagesaug-24pdf
- **Q:** Is Lincoln Park in the floodplain? **A:** Flood status is per property here, not per neighbourhood. The City states Port Coquitlam has two floodplains, the Fraser/Pitt River zone and the Coquitlam River zone, and its flood-watch notice closed Birchland Park, beside this neighbourhood's Fraser Avenue side, during a high-water event. Check the specific lot on the City flood page rather than assuming the north-side streets are all clear. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/notices/flood-watch-rainfall-warning-issued
- **Q:** Which buses serve Lincoln Park's streets? **A:** TransLink routes 173 and 174 are the neighbourhood's pair, and each continues as the other. Route 173 runs via Coast Meridian Road, Coquitlam Avenue and Oxford Street, and route 174 runs via Coast Meridian Road, Greenmount Avenue, Wellington Street and Oxford Street, both linking Port Coquitlam Station with Coquitlam Central Station. Interior streets such as Lincoln Avenue itself carry no route in the timetables read. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt173.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Hyde Creek Nature Reserve at 1457 Lincoln Drive covers 539,110 square metres with walking trails, per the City's parks directory — the largest single green space inside this house pocket, and the reason Lincoln Drive's east side fronts forest and creek, not more houses. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/recreation-parks/parks-trails/parks-directory/hyde-creek-nature-reserve
- Laurier Avenue carries the neighbourhood's two civic anchors eleven numbers apart: Hyde Creek Recreation Centre at 1379 Laurier Avenue and Minnekhada Middle School at 1390 Laurier Avenue, so school and pool traffic concentrate on that one street. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/city-facilities/hyde-creek-recreation-centre
- Dogwood Manor at 3155 Seymour Street, on the neighbourhood's Seymour Street, is subsidised rent-geared-to-income independent-living apartments for seniors run by the Port Coquitlam Senior Citizens' Housing Society: 24 units, built 1960, per the City's Social Housing and Services Resource Guide — a 2020 guide, so check current status before relying on it. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/social-housing-services-resource-guide-2020v4
- Waste collection is by zone, not by neighbourhood name: Port Coquitlam has five collection zones, green carts are weekly, and blue recycling and grey garbage carts alternate weeks, so a Lincoln Park address must be looked up on the zone map. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/waste-collection/collection-schedule-zone-map

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 173, Cedar to Coquitlam Central Station, runs the neighbourhood's west and north edges via Coast Meridian Road, Coquitlam Avenue and Oxford Street, through Port Coquitlam Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt173.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check flood status per lot on the City flood page: Port Coquitlam has two floodplains, the Fraser/Pitt River zone and the Coquitlam River zone, and the City patrols and maintains dikes and pump stations; the neighbourhood name alone does not clear a low lot near Birchland Park or the creek. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/flood
- Check the City's flood-watch history for the creek and park edge: a City notice closed Birchland Park during a high-water event while crews monitored drainage systems, culverts and pump stations, which is the documented behaviour of this neighbourhood's low ground. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/notices/flood-watch-rainfall-warning-issued
- Check watercourse rules before altering a creek-side lot: filling, clearing vegetation, paving or building near a watercourse, ditch or adjacent environmentally sensitive land in a designated area needs a Watercourse Development Permit or a City-confirmed exemption. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/development-permits/watercourse-development
- Check tree rules before clearing: Tree Bylaw, 2019, No. 4108 regulates tree removal, and a tree cutting permit can be refused during the active bird nesting season unless a biological survey by a qualified environmental professional is submitted. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/4108-tree-bylawpdf
