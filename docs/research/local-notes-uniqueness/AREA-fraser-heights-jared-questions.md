Area: Fraser Heights, Surrey (condos/townhomes first program page; houses and guide also in the program).

4. What surprises buyers most once they are under contract in Fraser Heights — the single commercial node at 108 Avenue and 160 Street, the bus-only transit on the 337 and 338, or slope and creek conditions on the east-side lots above 104 Avenue?
