# Bridal Falls — Questions for Jared

## Questions

1. Who buys in Bridal Falls in your deals — Chilliwack buyers wanting acreage, highway-corridor commuters, or recreation buyers around the falls and Cheam Lake Wetlands?
2. What sells fast and what sits — the bare-land strata resort lots at 53480 Bridal Falls Road, or the rural homes toward Rosedale and Popkum? How do the resort lots actually trade?
3. What surprises buyers — that Bridal Falls is FVRD Electoral Area D and not the City of Chilliwack, the on-request-only bus, or water and sewer service areas?
4. What is the actual legal structure at 53480 Bridal Falls Road — what does a buyer own, what do fees cover, and how do lenders treat those lots?
5. One story: a Bridal Falls or Popkum deal that taught you something — and whether you would keep Bridal Falls as its own market or fold it into Rosedale and Popkum.

## Shot list

1. Bridal Veil Falls from the BC Parks viewing platform, looking up at the 60-metre falls — the landmark the whole community is named for.
2. Highway 1 at Exit 135, looking east toward Mount Cheam — the interchange, the highway-services frontage and the Cheam backdrop in one frame.
3. Cheam Lake Wetlands Regional Park from the Elgey Road entrance, looking across the marsh — the 107-hectare park most buyers do not know is here.
