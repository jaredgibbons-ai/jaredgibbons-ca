# Mary Hill Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: mary-hill-port-coquitlam (Mary Hill; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Mary Hill is the south-side Port Coquitlam neighbourhood around Pitt River Road, Mary Hill Road and Humber Crescent, below the Mary Hill Bypass (Highway 7B). https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- École Mary Hill Elementary at 1890 Humber Crescent is the named anchor school inside the area. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/maryhill/
- The City names Mary Hill as an established neighbourhood in Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw No. 3838, separate from Lower Mary Hill to its east. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Exact board sub-area boundary roads are not published by the board, so street-level edges here are UNCONFIRMED — see NOT CONFIRMED.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Mary Hill housing is mainly ground-oriented homes on Residential Small Scale (RS) lots, not the apartment zones RA1 and RA2 that sit downtown — see the central-port-coquitlam note for those zones. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf
- Zoning Bylaw No. 3630 sets minimum lot widths from 15 metres in RS1 and 12 metres in RS2 down to 9.5 metres in RS4, so lot width on the title tells a buyer which RS family a Mary Hill lot sits in. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf
- Townhouse zoning families RTh1, RTh2 and RTh3 in Bylaw No. 3630 are the route used for townhouse assemblies on Pitt River Road, as the Harbour Street application in section 6 shows. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A detached Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) — a coach house — is capped at 90 square metres (968 square feet) in Port Coquitlam, may be rented, cannot contain a secondary suite, and cannot be strata subdivided. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/development-permits/accessory-dwelling-units
- RS1, RS2, RS3 and RS4 lots are designated a Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) Development Permit Area, so a new multi-unit build on a Mary Hill RS lot needs a development permit for form and character, not only a building permit. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11285
- Secondary suites are permitted in single-detached houses, duplexes and triplexes under the OCP amendment in Bylaw No. 4373, and ADUs are permitted accessory to single-detached houses. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11285
- City-wide SSMUH unit counts (3 to 6 units by lot size and transit proximity) are covered in full in the mary-hill-port-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; this note covers only the Mary Hill permit-area angle.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools does a Humber Crescent address feed? **A:** École Mary Hill Elementary is the catchment elementary for 1890 Humber Crescent. A School District 43 (SD43) School Locator run for that address returned École Mary Hill Elementary, École Pitt River Middle and École Riverside Secondary; catchments are address-based, so run the exact address of any Mary Hill home before relying on it. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/maryhill/
- **Q:** Is Mary Hill in the Pitt River floodplain? **A:** Parts of low-lying Mary Hill near Pitt River Road are in the floodplain. Council records for 1155 Pitt River Road state a minimum flood construction level of 5.15 metres for lots on that site; a buyer must ask the City for the flood construction level of the specific lot, because it is not on the listing. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11402
- **Q:** Can I add a coach house behind a Mary Hill house? **A:** Yes, on a qualifying RS-zoned lot, as an ADU. The City caps an ADU at 90 square metres, requires it to sit separate from the principal dwelling at the rear of the lot, and states pre-fabricated ADUs must be sprinklered and meet the British Columbia Building Code. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/development-permits/accessory-dwelling-units
- **Q:** Is Mary Hill the same as Lower Mary Hill? **A:** No, they are separate board sub-areas and separate pages. The OCP names Mary Hill as its own established neighbourhood; Lower Mary Hill is the pocket further east toward the Pitt River, covered on its own page in the internal links below. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Robert Hope Park outdoor pool is at 2137 Lamprey Drive, on the Mary Hill side of the city. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/recreation/parks-sports-fields-trails/parks-directory/
- The City's 2026 capital list includes sanitary sewer upgrades on Kelly Avenue from Mary Hill Road to the west lane, so road work on that stretch is a current, dated item, not a rumour. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects?construction_year=All&page=2
- École Pitt River Middle is at 2070 Tyner Street and École Riverside Secondary at 2215 Reeve Street, the middle and secondary schools returned for the Mary Hill sample address. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-coquitlam
- SD43 cross-catchment applications for September 2026 are closed, and the 2027–2028 window runs February 5–10, 2027, so a Mary Hill buyer cannot assume a transfer into another catchment mid-year. http://sd43.bc.ca/cross-catchment
- Mary Hill Sanitary Pump Station work appears by name in the City's capital planning, a reminder that low Mary Hill lots drain by pump station, not only by gravity. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=2906

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The 170 Port Coquitlam Station / Port Coquitlam South bus is the TransLink route family serving the south side including Mary Hill, connecting at Port Coquitlam Station — route list as shown on the TransLink station record. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- Mary Hill's highway exit is the Mary Hill Bypass (Highway 7B), which runs along the Fraser River between the Pitt River Bridge and the Port Mann Bridge — named in the City's OCP road network. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the lot on the City's flood resources at portcoquitlam.ca/flood before removing subjects: the City states a significant portion of Port Coquitlam sits in the Fraser or Pitt River flood plain behind dyke infrastructure, raised in 2007. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/city-port-coquitlam-prepares-potential-flooding
- The City's dike system covers the Coquitlam, Fraser and Pitt Rivers, is built to the 1:200-year flood standard, and is backed by nine drainage pump stations — that is system protection, not a lot-by-lot guarantee. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/FileStream.ashx?DocumentId=5334
- The flood page does not tell a buyer the flood construction level for one Mary Hill lot; the Harbour Street record shows levels are set lot by lot (5.15 metres on part of that site). https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11402
