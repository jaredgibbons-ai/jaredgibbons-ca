# Chilliwack Mountain — Questions for Jared

Area file for Chilliwack Mountain (City of Chilliwack hillside neighbourhood). Five questions only Jared can answer from deals he has actually done or shown in the area. Do not answer these from research.

1. Which elementary and secondary schools do mountain addresses actually feed into in your deals, street by street — and how often does the catchment surprise a buyer?
2. In your experience on the mountain, how many homes are on full City water and sewer versus wells and septic, and has servicing ever changed a deal's price or conditions?
3. What do buyers pay the premium for up there — the view, the lot size, or the newer builds — and what sells slowly on the mountain?
4. Has a geotechnical report or the hillside development permit process ever delayed, repriced or killed a mountain deal for you? What happened?
5. Tell one Chilliwack Mountain story: a showing, an inspection surprise, or a deal that captures what buying on this hillside is really like.

## Shot list for Jared

1. Hillkeep Regional Park viewing platform at the top of the mountain, looking north across the Fraser Valley — the view the whole neighbourhood is sold on, clear morning.
2. Grandview Drive switchbacks looking up toward Hillkeep Place, showing the slope buyers actually drive every day.
3. Chilliwack Mountain Road at Schweyey Road looking east toward the downtown core — the mountain-to-downtown relationship in one frame.
