# Salmon River Condos and Townhomes — Extra Local Notes
Page: salmon-river-langley-condos-townhomes-for-sale (Salmon River, Township of Langley; condos and townhomes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Salmon River is the rural district along the Salmon River in the Township of Langley's northwest, north of the Otter area and south of Fort Langley, and the Township publishes no street-level boundary for the board sub-area — the district follows the river valley between 232 Street and 264 Street, from the Fraser River side south toward 40 Avenue. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- Neighbourhood-shared facts for this district are written in full in the salmon-river note (section F) — see the salmon-river note; Township of Langley–wide facts are in the earliest Township page note in program order, the langley-farms note — see the langley-farms note.
- The upland edge of the district runs on 56 Avenue and Rawlison Crescent, and that upland/valley split is the geography that decides whether any attached product could exist here at all. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The housing stock is effectively all detached, and that is the central fact of this page: in September 2026 the board snapshot for the Salmon River sub-area recorded 22 detached listings against 4 attached listings, with no attached sales. (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report)
- No named condo building is verified inside the district: the Township development applications route was checked in this program's research and no condo project inside Salmon River was named. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- No named townhome complex is verified inside the district either: the same Township route named no townhome project, and the district's homes are farm and acreage houses on large rural lots along the valley and the 56 Avenue upland. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Most Salmon River properties are non-strata freehold acreages, so a unit marketed here as a condo or townhome should be checked against its title and strata plan before it is treated as strata stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Rural land here is under Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, and zone assignments for a specific parcel come from the bylaw map schedules through the Township's GeoSource mapping, not from a listing's zone label. https://www.tol.ca/at-your-service/bylaw-enforcement/bylaws/
- The Township's small-scale multi-unit housing position does not create townhome product in the valley: properties that do not meet the eligibility criteria — including lots larger than 4,050 square metres — are eligible for one residential unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit (ADU), and Salmon River acreages are generally above that lot-size line. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- On Agricultural Land Reserve parcels in the valley, the Agricultural Land Commission permits one additional residence without an application where its size rules are met — up to 90 square metres on properties of 40 hectares or less and up to 186 square metres on larger properties — subject to Township bylaws. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- If a strata parcel does surface here, provincial strata law is the frame: rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022, and the Form B Information Certificate is the diligence document for fees, levies and the depreciation report. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Are there any condos or townhomes to buy in Salmon River? **A:** None are verified as named stock. The district's housing is farm and acreage houses, the Township development applications route names no condo or townhome project inside the district, and the September 2026 board snapshot counted only 4 attached listings against 22 detached. A buyer set on an attached form should treat any Salmon River condo or townhome listing as an exception to verify from title and strata documents, not as a local market segment. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Salmon River address? **A:** Catchments are address-based in School District 35 (Langley), and addresses in this district split. For the sample upland address 4452 256 Street, the district School Locator returns North Otter Elementary and D.W. Poppy Secondary, with no middle school shown. Coghlan Fundamental Elementary at the same address is a district school of choice, which is a different admission route from a catchment school — run the exact address in the locator before relying on either. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- **Q:** Is a Salmon River property in the floodplain? **A:** The valley floor is Fraser River floodplain land, and the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station was built to help mitigate flooding of the surrounding agricultural lands during freshet and provides flood protection when Fraser River levels rise, with flood box gates that close as levels rise. Upland parcels on the 56 Avenue side sit in a different position from valley-floor parcels, so the check is parcel by parcel, starting with the pump station and dike file. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Q:** Can an acreage here add a second dwelling for family or farm help? **A:** Sometimes, within two separate frames. On Agricultural Land Reserve land the Agricultural Land Commission permits one additional residence at up to 90 square metres (40 hectares or less) or 186 square metres (over 40 hectares) without an application, subject to Township bylaws; off the reserve, the Township's small-scale rules allow one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit in addition to the primary unit on lots larger than 4,050 square metres, depending on zoning. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Salmon River is a salmon-bearing watercourse, and Metro Vancouver watershed material covers the Salmon River watershed — riparian setbacks can limit the building envelope on parcels near the river, separate from the flood question. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- Rural properties here may use wells: the provincial GWELLS database holds registered well records and mapped aquifers, and well reports are due within 90 days of constructing, altering or decommissioning a well — pull the parcel's record before closing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers
- Septic is the other rural servicing check: Fraser Health requires an authorized person to file a record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair of an onsite system, and a letter of certification after construction — those filings are the paper trail for a Salmon River acreage. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons
- There is no recreation centre or library branch inside the district on the Township facility list; the nearest Township facilities are in Fort Langley and Walnut Grove. https://www.tol.ca/en/parks-recreation/community-and-recreation-centres.aspx
- Langley Memorial Hospital, 22051 Fraser Highway, is the Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency serving the area, southwest of the district. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Township of Langley property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty on current taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5 percent at midnight September 3, 2026, and utility fees are billed separately from the tax statement. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- The Township's 2026 budget raised property taxes 3.97 percent in total — 0.90 percent for core municipal services, 1.59 percent for RCMP, 1.26 percent for fire and 0.22 percent for bylaws — per the Township's 2026 property tax newsletter. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/services/resources/property-taxes/2026-Property-Tax-Newsletter.pdf
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies in the Township of Langley: the Province lists Langley (city and township) among the Metro Vancouver taxable areas, so even a rural Salmon River owner must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- A Salmon River acreage on well and septic does not pay the Township flat-rate water and sewer charges a serviced strata unit pays — servicing, not the tax rate, is the cost split between the valley acreages and Township townhome product elsewhere. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the pump station and dike file for any valley-floor parcel: the Township states the pump station provides flood protection when Fraser River levels rise and its flood box gates close as river levels rise, and upgrades are in detailed design — the project page does not give a flood level for a specific lot. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- Confirm Agricultural Land Reserve status on the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR maps for the exact parcel, because valley farm land is in the reserve only in places and ALR status governs second dwellings and subdivision. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- Pull the GWELLS well record and the Fraser Health sewerage filings for the parcel: a well or septic system with no filings is a servicing risk that no listing document will surface. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons
- Check riparian setbacks with the Township before planning any build near the river: the Salmon River is salmon-bearing, and the watershed material does not state a setback distance for a specific parcel. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
