# Murrayville Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: murrayville (Murrayville, Township of Langley; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Murrayville sits on both sides of the Fraser Highway immediately east of the City of Langley, inside the Township of Langley. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Community-Plan-Bylaws/Murrayville-Community-Plan-(Bylaw-No.-2661).pdf
- Its civic core is the Fraser Highway corridor around 216 Street and 222 Street, where Langley Memorial Hospital at 22051 Fraser Highway and W.C. Blair Recreation Centre at 22200 Fraser Highway stand a block apart. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital
- Old Yale Road runs along its south side, with James Hill Elementary at 22144 Old Yale Road on that road. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Community-Plan-Bylaws/Murrayville-Community-Plan-(Bylaw-No.-2661).pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Township-wide rules are stated in full in this note, because Murrayville is the earlier Langley page in program order. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- The Murrayville Community Plan is Amendment Bylaw 1988 No. 2661 to the Langley Official Community Plan Bylaw 1979 No. 1842, adopted April 17, 1989, and amended by the 2025 Official Community Plan Update, Bylaw No. 6200, on December 1, 2025. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Community-Plan-Bylaws/Murrayville-Community-Plan-(Bylaw-No.-2661).pdf
- Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) in the Township runs under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 Amendment (SSMUH) Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024: an eligible lot may hold 3 units at 280 square metres or smaller, or 4 units between 280 and 4,050 square metres. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- Eligibility is narrow: the lot must be inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on both municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated and not in a Transit-Oriented Area, and lots over 4,050 square metres are not eligible; there is no 6-unit transit tier in the Township. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- Township zoning minimums from Zoning Bylaw 2500, Section 110.1, run from 464.5 square metres for R-1B and R-1C urban lots to 8,094 square metres for SR-2 suburban lots. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** How many units can my Murrayville lot hold under the new provincial rules? **A:** At most four, and only if the lot qualifies. Under Township Bylaw No. 6020 an eligible lot holds 3 units at 280 square metres or less and 4 units up to 4,050 square metres, provided it is inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated and not in a Transit-Oriented Area. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q:** Is the hospital actually in Murrayville? **A:** Yes — it anchors the neighbourhood's civic core. Fraser Health lists Langley Memorial Hospital at 22051 Fraser Highway as a community hospital with 24/7 emergency, general medicine, general surgery and maternity services, and W.C. Blair Recreation Centre at 22200 Fraser Highway is in the same block. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital
- **Q:** Which schools have Murrayville addresses? **A:** Four public schools are listed in the British Columbia Ministry school file for this area: James Hill Elementary at 22144 Old Yale Road, Langley Fundamental Elementary at 21789 50 Avenue, Langley Fundamental Middle/Secondary at 21250 42 Avenue and Langley Secondary at 21405 56 Avenue. A school's address is not a catchment promise, so confirm the home's address in the School District 35 locator. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- **Q:** When are Township taxes due, and what happens if they are late? **A:** They were due Thursday, July 2, 2026. The Township applies a 5 percent penalty at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5 percent at midnight September 3, 2026, and utility fees are now billed on a separate statement from the tax notice. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- A secondary suite adds its own utility charges in the Township: for 2026 the flat-rate additions are $233.99 for water and $214.94 for sanitary sewer on top of the house rates. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- Metered Township homes are billed three times a year, with meters read in April, August and December, sewer volume charged at 80 percent of water supplied, and a 10 percent penalty on unpaid metered balances after the due date. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- Garbage is charged as a $226.00 base rate plus a cart charge for 2026 — $200.00 for a 240-litre cart or $100.00 for a 120-litre cart — and only properties receiving municipal collection pay it. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- The Township's 2026 budget raised property taxes 3.97 percent, split as 0.90 percent core services, 1.59 percent RCMP (Royal Canadian Mounted Police), 1.26 percent fire and 0.22 percent bylaw enforcement. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/important-2026-budget-investments-approved-by-township-of-langley-council.aspx
- School District 35 catchments for a specific Murrayville address were not confirmed by a locator run in this program, so no catchment is promised in this note. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The 2026 flat-rate utility bill for a Township single-family home is $779.96 for water plus $716.47 for sanitary sewer, before garbage, and utilities are billed separately from property taxes. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- Multi-family homes pay less under the split rates adopted for 2026: $667.22 for water and $609.00 for sanitary sewer. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- Township property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5 percent penalties at midnight July 2 and midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies in the Township of Langley, which the Province lists as a designated taxable area in the Metro Vancouver Regional District. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Fraser Highway is the through route, running east-west through Murrayville toward Aldergrove and Abbotsford and west into Langley City and Surrey. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Community-Plan-Bylaws/Murrayville-Community-Plan-(Bylaw-No.-2661).pdf
- No SkyTrain or West Coast Express station is in Murrayville; the Surrey-Langley SkyTrain extension now under construction ends at 203 Street in Langley City, west of Murrayville. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status and watercourses in the Township's GeoSource viewer before assuming a south or east Murrayville lot can be built on or split, because much Township land is in the ALR. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Check heritage designation before pricing in extra units: a heritage-designated lot is not eligible for Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing under Bylaw No. 6020. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- Confirm municipal water and sewer connection for the exact lot, because SSMUH eligibility requires both services and lots over 4,050 square metres fall outside the program entirely. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- The Township pages read in this pass do not publish a Murrayville flood, slope or aquifer map layer for a specific lot; that check is a per-property GeoSource and title search. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
