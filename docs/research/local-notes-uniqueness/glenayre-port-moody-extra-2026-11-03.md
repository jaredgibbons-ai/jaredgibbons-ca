# Glenayre Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: glenayre-port-moody (Glenayre; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Glenayre is a City of Port Moody neighbourhood grouped with College Park, Harbour Heights and Seaview in section 14.1 of the Port Moody 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw, 2026, No. 3522. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- The pocket sits between Clarke Road and the Burnaby city line, on Glenayre Drive and its side streets; the adjoining Seaview sub-area is described by the City as extending from College Park Way to Ingersoll Street and from Seaforth Way to Glenayre Drive. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050/news_feed/seaview
- École Glenayre Elementary at 495 Glencoe Drive and the Glenayre Centre at 492 Glencoe Drive are the two named public buildings inside the pocket. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/school/glenayre/pages/newsitem.aspx?ItemID=236&ListID=f15d990e-aa01-4292-bc78-c4216fe95d8b&TemplateID=Announcement_Item https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Stock facts shared with the houses page are in full in the glenayre-port-moody-houses-for-sale note; this note does not repeat them.
- What the OCP states for this grouping is that Glenayre, Seaview and College Park are predominantly residential, with a mix of single detached homes, townhouses and apartments and some commercial development on Clarke Road. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- The OCP also states most existing housing in Glenayre, Seaview and College Park was built within the last 40 to 50 years, and that widespread redevelopment is not likely, with gentle densification expected from Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH). https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Zoning for a Glenayre lot is set parcel by parcel under Zoning Bylaw, 2018, No. 2937, and is checked on the City ViewPort map under the Planning and Zoning layers. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- Small-lot subdivision to Single Detached Residential – Small Lot (RS1-S), minimum 9 metres width and 325 square metres, is limited to Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside — Glenayre is not on that list, so a Glenayre lot split needs a different route confirmed with the City. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- Several areas in this grouping are adjacent to the Suncor Refinery lands, which the OCP treats as a Special Study Area, so land use on that edge is an open planning question to check with the City. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Slope and creek lots can fall in Development Permit Area 4 (Environmentally Sensitive Areas) or Area 5 (Hazardous Lands and Steep Slopes), and a Qualified Environmental Professional must assess any watercourse and apply Zoning Bylaw section 5.4 riparian setbacks. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/environmental-protection-and-requirements/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is in Glenayre itself? **A:** École Glenayre Elementary at 495 Glencoe Drive is the school inside the pocket, and it is a dual-track school with English and French immersion classes. School District No. 43 (SD43) catchment is set by the permanent home address, not by the neighbourhood name, so run the exact address in the SD43 School Locator before relying on Glenayre as the assigned school. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/school/glenayre/pages/newsitem.aspx?ItemID=236&ListID=f15d990e-aa01-4292-bc78-c4216fe95d8b&TemplateID=Announcement_Item https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** How do I reach SkyTrain from Glenayre Drive? **A:** By bus or by driving to a station, because no station sits in the neighbourhood. TransLink route 180 runs from Lougheed Station via Burquitlam Station on Clarke Road, then along Glenayre Drive, Harvard Drive and Princeton Avenue through College Park to Moody Centre Station, so one bus connects Glenayre homes to two Millennium Line stations. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/206/tt180.pdf
- **Q:** Can I split a Glenayre lot into small lots? **A:** Not under the RS1-S small-lot rule as the City publishes it. That subdivision option, minimum 9 metres width and 325 square metres, is limited to Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside Single Family–Low Density areas, and Glenayre is not named. SSMUH infill on a qualifying lot is a separate route; confirm the lot's zone and any registered building scheme on title first. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- **Q:** Does the refinery next door affect a purchase here? **A:** It is a planning fact to check, not a rumour: the OCP states several areas in the College Park grouping are adjacent to the Suncor Refinery lands and designates those lands a Special Study Area. Ask what the current study status is, and check the specific lot's distance and any title charges, before writing an offer on the western edge streets. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Glenayre Centre at 492 Glencoe Drive is the City community centre for this pocket, listed on the City recreation facilities page alongside the Port Moody Recreation Complex at 300 Ioco Road. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/
- The City replaced more than 1.5 kilometres of watermain in Glenayre, College Park and Seaview in 2023, with full roadway asphalt replacement and sidewalk, curb and gutter restoration, per the City 2023 Annual Report highlights. https://www.portmoody.ca/news-notices-and-community-matters/posts/2023-annual-report-highlights-achievements-connected-to-council-strategic-plan-objectives/
- The City Trail Network Plan, endorsed by Council in 2025, proposes improved urban multi-use paths and bike routes connecting Glenayre, Seaview and College Park with Moody Centre, Burnaby Mountain and Coquitlam trails — a plan item, not built infrastructure. https://engage.portmoody.ca/trail-network-plan
- Port Moody has more than 60 kilometres of trails and pathways managed by the City and the Tri-Cities Off Road Cycling Association, per the Trail Network Plan page. https://engage.portmoody.ca/trail-network-plan

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 180 is the pocket's bus: Moody Centre Station to Lougheed Station via St. Johns Street, Clarke Road and Burquitlam Station, then Glenayre Drive, Harvard Drive, Princeton Avenue, Washington Drive, College Park and Cecile Drive. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/206/tt180.pdf
- Clarke Road is the south edge road out of the pocket, running to Burquitlam Station and toward Burnaby along the city line side of the grouping. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- The two SkyTrain stations serving this side of Port Moody are Burquitlam, reached on Clarke Road, and Moody Centre, the route 180 terminus; Inlet Centre Station is farther east and is not the station for Glenayre streets. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/206/tt180.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check Development Permit Area 4 and Area 5 on the City ViewPort Environmentally Sensitive Areas and Watercourses layers before buying a slope or creek lot on the pocket's upper streets; the general pages do not settle a specific lot's status. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/environmental-protection-and-requirements/
- Check tree rules before planning an extension: Tree Protection Bylaw, 2026, No. 3531 regulates cutting on private and public property, and a development site needs a tree removal permit and an arborist report. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/environmental-protection-and-requirements/
- Check title for a registered building scheme: the City states a building scheme that blocks SSMUH takes precedence over SSMUH entitlements and it cannot process an SSMUH application on a lot it blocks. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
