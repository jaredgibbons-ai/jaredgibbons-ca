# Vedder Mountain Area Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: vedder-mountain-guide (Vedder Mountain, Chilliwack; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Vedder Mountain is split between two governments: the homes along the western end of Vedder Mountain Road, reached from Yarrow, are inside the City of Chilliwack, while the eastern side of the mountain above Cultus Lake is Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area H (Cultus Lake–Columbia Valley). https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Area%20H.pdf
- The FVRD's own meeting record places 43241 Vedder Mountain Road in Area H, so an address on this one road can be in the regional district even though it carries a Chilliwack-area setting. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/Meeting.aspx?Id=7b549cf8-d9da-4976-a7cf-79fe2f1719cd&Agenda=Agenda&lang=English&Item=46&Tab=attachments
- Vedder Mountain Road runs east–west along the mountain's north side and meets Columbia Valley Road at a roundabout; the FVRD's Area H evacuation guide gives daily traffic on Vedder Mountain Road at approximately 7,000 vehicles. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Area%20H.pdf
- On the city side, the Yarrow Neighbourhood Plan treats the Forest Resource lands south and east of Majuba Hill as part of Vedder Mountain, making the mountain the backdrop to both Yarrow and Majuba Hill. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Housing here is a thin rural strip, not a subdivision pattern: the City's engagement material describes Chilliwack's rural hillside settlements like the Vedder Mountain side as predominantly large rural residential estates and vacant acreages in a natural state. https://www.engagechilliwack.com/yarrowplan?tool=qanda
- Majuba Hill, on the mountain's western shoulder inside the city, is the built-up face of this area: the Yarrow Neighbourhood Plan characterises it as residential uses on large, rural properties, serving as a gateway to the recreational trails on Vedder Mountain. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799
- There are no commercial or institutional uses on the mountain itself; the Yarrow plan notes Majuba Hill residents rely on services outside the sub-area, in Yarrow Town Centre and Sardis. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6799

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Which rulebook applies depends on which side of the boundary the lot is on: City of Chilliwack zoning and its Hillside Development Permit Area apply on the city side, while FVRD bylaws and the Electoral Area H framework apply on the Cultus Lake side — confirm the jurisdiction for the exact address before relying on any rule. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Area%20H.pdf
- Crown land on Vedder Mountain is managed under the FVRD's Crown Land Referral guidelines as a recreation area in which forestry activities and recreation uses coexist and are coordinated, so working forest use beside the trails is expected, not an exception. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=27689
- The same FVRD guidelines require resource plans on the north side of Vedder Mountain, which drains into the City of Abbotsford or the City of Chilliwack, to be referred to the municipality. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=27689
- City-side hillside building rules (Hillside Environment DPA 2 and Hillside Development DPA 5 under the 2050 OCP) are listed on the City's development permits page; see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note for city-wide zoning detail. http://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2182

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a Vedder Mountain home in the City of Chilliwack or the regional district? **A:** It depends on the address. The FVRD's records place 43241 Vedder Mountain Road in Electoral Area H, while the western end of the road at Yarrow is inside the City of Chilliwack. The distinction changes who issues your building permit, who provides services and which official community plan applies, so verify the jurisdiction for the specific property first. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/Meeting.aspx?Id=7b549cf8-d9da-4976-a7cf-79fe2f1719cd&Agenda=Agenda&lang=English&Item=46&Tab=attachments
- **Q:** Which schools do Vedder Mountain Road children attend? **A:** School District No. 33's transportation procedure groups the "Yarrow/Vedder Mountain Road areas" together for school busing: Yarrow Elementary School, Stitó:s Lá:lém Totí:lt Elementary/Middle School and Sardis Secondary School. Catchments are still set by exact address, so confirm in the district's School Locator. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2023-03/611%20AP%20Transportation%20-%20Operating%20Instructions.pdf
- **Q:** Can I use the mountain trails from home? **A:** The mountain's trail network is the area's defining amenity, and it is a managed recreation area, not a park with a single gate: the FVRD's referral guidelines say Vedder Mountain should be managed as a recreation area where forestry and recreation coexist. The Vedder Rotary Loop Trail also passes the mountain's foot at its kilometre 20.2 mark, on Vedder Mountain Road. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=27689
- **Q:** How do residents get out in an emergency? **A:** The FVRD's Electoral Area H evacuation guide plans around two exits: west on Vedder Mountain Road to Abbotsford, or north via Vedder Road into Chilliwack, with the City of Chilliwack's after-hours line notified so it can control traffic at the roundabout. Columbia Valley Road carries about 12,000 vehicles a day, more in peak season. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Area%20H.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Part of Vedder Mountain's Crown land sits outside the firearms prohibition area: City Parks and Trails Advisory Committee minutes record that hunting and gun use are permitted on a large Crown parcel, and that the City is working with the FVRD and the Province to have the area restricted because of high trail use. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2833/CPTAC - May 14 minutes.pdf
- The mountain bike parking for the trail network is on Parmenter Road, on the Area H (Cultus Lake/Columbia Valley) side, not on the Chilliwack side of the mountain. http://www.fvmba.com/
- School bus service is a planned route here, not a walk-to-school pattern: SD33's transportation procedure lists the Yarrow/Vedder Mountain Road areas among the catchment areas its regular bus routes are set up to serve. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2023-03/611%20AP%20Transportation%20-%20Operating%20Instructions.pdf
- The Vedder River Campground and Browne Creek Wetlands sit at the mountain's north-west foot on the Vedder Rotary Loop Trail (kilometres 13.4 to 16.2), so summer visitor traffic shares Vedder Mountain Road with residents. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=144

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City-side properties pay City of Chilliwack taxes and utilities; the city-wide figures are written in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note — see that note.
- Area H properties are not City of Chilliwack taxpayers: the FVRD is their local government, and FVRD documents (its Electoral Area H evacuation guide and Area H planning files) are the service reference for the Cultus Lake side of the mountain. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Area%20H.pdf

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- There is no BC Transit route on Vedder Mountain Road itself; the nearest named services are Route 55 Yarrow–Greendale at Yarrow Town Centre for the west end and Route 56 Cultus Lake on the Cultus Lake side. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- Driving is the practical default: Vedder Mountain Road west leads to Yarrow and Vedder Road, and east it meets Columbia Valley Road at a roundabout for Cultus Lake, per the FVRD's Area H evacuation guide. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Area%20H.pdf
- The Vedder Rotary Loop Trail's kilometre 20.2 point is the Vedder Mountain Road sidewalk, linking the mountain's foot to Vedder Park and the Keith Wilson Bridge by trail. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=144
Read: 2026-11-03

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Wildfire and evacuation are the headline checks here: the FVRD publishes a dedicated Electoral Area H evacuation guide covering the Cultus Lake and Vedder Mountain Road area, with only two road exits — Vedder Mountain Road west to Abbotsford, or Vedder Road north to Chilliwack. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD%20Evacuation%20Guide%20-%20Area%20H.pdf
- Slope and drainage run downhill to two cities: the FVRD's referral guidelines note the mountain's north side drains into the City of Abbotsford or the City of Chilliwack, so hillside lots need the geotechnical review required by the applicable hillside development permit area. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=27689
- Trail-adjacent lots sit beside an active recreation and forestry area where, per City committee minutes, firearms use is still permitted on a large Crown parcel outside the prohibition area; check the parcel's exact position against that boundary. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2833/CPTAC - May 14 minutes.pdf
