# Thornhill Area Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: thornhill-maple-ridge-guide (Thornhill; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Thornhill is a heavily forested, mainly residential area on the south slope of Grant Hill, with the City describing several farms and nurseries selling fresh produce in peak seasons. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The Thornhill trail network the City names is on 256 Street — 17 trails the City calls moderately difficult and typically about an hour to complete. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Thornhill is listed by the City as one of its notable neighbourhoods and historical areas, many of which the City says are well over 100 years old. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A secondary suite in Thornhill follows Maple Ridge's city-wide suite rules under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019: one suite per lot, contained within the same building as the single detached home, and not over 40% of the building's total Gross Floor Area (total floor area). https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- A suite must not be situated within the Floodplain Area, and a Business Licence is required if there are two rental units on the property — both stated on the City's secondary suites page. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- Slope and creek Development Permit Areas (DPAs) for a specific Thornhill parcel: the City's soil bylaw work references Watercourse Protection and Natural Features Development Permit Areas in the Official Community Plan, with setbacks within 30 metres of a watercourse top of bank — check the parcel on City mapping. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/1353
- General Maple Ridge zoning position is written in full in the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note — see that note.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there a Thornhill Elementary School today? **A:** No current Thornhill elementary appears on the provincial School District 42 (SD42) school list read for this note. Thornhill had its own elementary only from 1959 to 1969 at Spilsbury Street and 96th Avenue, and Thornhill children attend Whonnock Elementary — so a buyer should confirm the current catchment for the exact address with SD42, not assume a Thornhill school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- **Q:** What is the trail access actually like in Thornhill? **A:** The City of Maple Ridge describes a large mountain-biking and hiking trail network on 256 Street with 17 trails, rated moderately difficult and typically taking about an hour to complete, usable year-round. That 256 Street network, not a central park, is the recreation feature the City itself names for Thornhill. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- **Q:** Can I add a suite to a Thornhill house? **A:** Possibly, under the city-wide rules: Maple Ridge permits one secondary suite per lot inside the same building as the single detached home, capped at 40% of total Gross Floor Area, with a building permit required for new construction or renovation of a suite. A suite cannot be in the Floodplain Area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q:** Is Thornhill urban or rural for servicing? **A:** The City describes Thornhill as heavily forested and mainly residential with several farms and nurseries, which signals a rural-residential setting rather than a Town Centre setting. Whether a specific lot is on municipal water and sewer, or on a well and septic system, is parcel-specific and is UNCONFIRMED in this note — verify it with the City for the exact lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Slope is the first check in Thornhill: the City's soil regulation references slopes steeper than 20 percent (five metres horizontal to one metre vertical) and land within 30 metres of the crest or toe of such slopes inside Natural Features and Watercourse Development Permit Areas. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/1353
- Creek setbacks are the second check: the same City material references land within 30 metres of any top of bank or high water mark of a watercourse, ravine, lake, wetland or drainage ditch. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/1353
- Flood risk in Maple Ridge is creek-specific as well as river-specific: the City's high streamflow advisory says it monitors the North and South Alouette, Kanaka Creek and Fraser Rivers and inspects dikes and pump stations. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/high-streamflow-advisory-1
- A Thornhill lot's wildfire interface, well and septic status is UNCONFIRMED here — check the City's flood page at MapleRidge.ca/Flood and parcel mapping before subjects. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/high-streamflow-advisory-1
