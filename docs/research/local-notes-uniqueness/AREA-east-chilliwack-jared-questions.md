# East Chilliwack — Questions for Jared

## Questions

1. Who buys in East Chilliwack in your deals — working farmers, hobby-farm buyers from the coast, or families who want a house on acreage east of the city core? What are they leaving behind?
2. What sells fast and what sits — smaller Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) parcels on Chilliwack Central Road, larger farm operations on Prest Road and Gibson Road, or rural-residential lots at the edge of the serviced area?
3. What surprises buyers — the farm home plate limits on where a house and yard can go, the 500 m² principal-residence cap on ALR land, well and septic servicing, or Fraser River freshet and dike questions?
4. When a buyer compares East Chilliwack with Rosedale and with Greendale, what do you tell them about schools, servicing and how farm the area still is?
5. One story: an East Chilliwack deal that taught you something — an ALR application, a septic or well surprise, or a farm home plate problem you now check first, and why.

## Shot list

1. Chilliwack Central Road at East Chilliwack Elementary, 49190 Chilliwack Central Rd — the area's spine road, its one school and the farm fields in one frame.
2. Prest Road looking north across the Fraser flats toward the dike — the farm grid, the flat valley floor and the flood landscape buyers need to understand.
3. Gibson Road / Prairie Central Road farm intersection at field level — barns, fields and a farm home plate showing how houses sit clustered on working land.
