# East Chilliwack Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: east-chilliwack (East Chilliwack; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The City publishes no boundary for this sub-area, so the useful official frame is farmland, not a line on a map. East Chilliwack is the rural, farm-side area east of Chilliwack Proper toward Rosedale, covered by the Agricultural Area Plan, Schedule B1 of the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP). https://www.chilliwack.com/ocp
- Chilliwack Central Road is the area's spine road, shown by its one school's address, 49190 Chilliwack Central Rd. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2025-01/Chilliwack%20School%20District%20Directory%202024-25.pdf
- Prairie Central Road and Gibson Road are the farm-grid roads named in district planning for this side: SD33's transportation procedure names a "Prairie Central Road – Gibson/Hanford Roads Area" in its historic school-site planning list. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2023-03/611%20AP%20Transportation%20-%20Operating%20Instructions.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses here sit on farmland first and residential lots second, under the Agricultural Area Plan. That plan states 67% of Chilliwack's total land area (17,322 ha) is in the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR), and the Fraser River valley bottom portion is about 14,000 ha. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1_Agricultural_Area_Plan_1.pdf
- The City's zoning for this fabric is its agricultural zone family: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 lists AL (Agricultural Lowland), AU (Agricultural Upland), AS (Agricultural Small Lot), AR (Agricultural Residential), AC and AFP among its agricultural zones. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- In the AL zone, the bylaw's permitted principal uses include conditional agriculture, general agriculture, a manufactured home and a single detached dwelling, with a maximum of one structure for principal residential use per lot. https://pub-chilliwack.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=2198

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Every house here is clustered onto a farm home plate if the parcel is valley-floor farmland of 2 hectares or more. Development Permit Area 4 (DPA 4), Farm Home Plate, applies to housing on valley floor farmland within the ALR for properties with 2 hectares or more, per the City's development permits page. http://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2182
- The farm home plate cap is 4,000 m² for a single family dwelling, plus an additional 1,000 m² if an additional dwelling applies, per the City's December 2025 DPA 4 guide. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3279/2025%5F12%5FDPA%5F4%5F%2D%5FFarm%5FHome%5FPlate%2Epdf
- On ALR land the province caps the principal residence at 500 m² total floor area without Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) approval, and an additional residence is limited to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or 186 m² on larger parcels. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- ALC approval may be required before the City can approve a related development application on an ALR property, per the City's agriculture page. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- A secondary suite on ALR land is permitted only inside the principal residence, and only where local bylaws allow it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I subdivide a farm parcel here into house lots? **A:** Not as a right — ALR status controls first. The City's agriculture page states ALR properties are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALR regulations, and ALC approval may be required for subdivision and other applications before the City can approve them. Check the parcel's ALR status on the City's Webmap or with the ALC before assuming any lot creation. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Q:** How big a house can I build on ALR land? **A:** The provincial cap is 500 m² of total floor area for the principal residence, and the City then clusters it onto a farm home plate. The ALC states a larger residence needs ALC approval, and the City's DPA 4 guide caps the farm home plate at 4,000 m² for the dwelling, with residential buildings, septic tanks and related uses clustered inside it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q:** Is there a second-dwelling option for family or farm help? **A:** Yes, one additional residence, within strict size limits. The ALC permits an additional residence of up to 90 m² total floor area on a parcel of 40 hectares or less, or up to 186 m² on a larger parcel, and local government zoning can further restrict number, size and siting. A detached building cannot be used as a secondary suite instead. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q:** Which school is actually in East Chilliwack? **A:** East Chilliwack Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 5, at 49190 Chilliwack Central Rd, is the one school located in the area. No middle or secondary school is located in East Chilliwack in the SD33 directory; Rosedale Traditional Community School, Kindergarten to Grade 8, is east of the area in Rosedale, and address-level catchment must be confirmed with the district locator. https://eastchilliwack.sd33.bc.ca/
- **Q:** Is this area flood-prone? **A:** The valley floor is dike-protected Fraser River floodplain, so the lot check is elevation, not distance. The City's floodplain FAQ states the East Dyke has been upgraded under its $30M dyke-raising program, and Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519 requires habitable floors at or above the Flood Construction Level plus 0.6 m freeboard in standard-dike areas. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=4330

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- There is no City park or recreation centre located in East Chilliwack in the City's listings, and no Fraser Valley Regional Library branch; the nearest branch is the Chilliwack Library, 45860 First Avenue. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754 https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/chilliwack-library
- No fixed-route BC Transit bus runs into East Chilliwack's rural roads on the Chilliwack system route list; Route 71 Agassiz-Harrison runs along Yale Road East through Rosedale, east of the area. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- Septic, not city sewer, is the default assumption to verify on a farm parcel: Fraser Health states a building not on a community system must use an onsite sewerage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- Well records for a property are held in the Province's GWELLS registry, which is where a buyer checks a drilled well's record before relying on it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- SD33's bus procedure groups the Eastern Hillside area with East Chilliwack Elementary School and Rosedale Traditional Community School for bus routes, which is a transport grouping, not a catchment guarantee. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2023-03/611%20AP%20Transportation%20-%20Operating%20Instructions.pdf

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- A new east side elementary school in Rosedale is the active project that changes schooling east of this area, with opening anticipated in September 2027. SD33's Board-approved changes split the Cheam Elementary catchment between Little Mountain Elementary, Strathcona Elementary and the new east side elementary school from September 2027, and move the Early French Immersion program from Cheam Elementary to the new school. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/news?page=3
- No East Chilliwack-specific development application was found on the City's Highlighted Projects page read on 2026-11-03; that page lists city-wide engineering projects, none addressed in East Chilliwack. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2773

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Farm-parcel costs here turn on servicing and ALR filings, not strata fees; the city-wide tax rate and utility billing cycle are in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note — see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2527/Tax%5FRates%5Ffor%5Fthe%5FWeb%2Dmou9gntw%5Fc%2Epdf

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Driving is the practical mode here, because no BC Transit route on the Chilliwack system list enters the rural roads of East Chilliwack. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- Route 71 Agassiz-Harrison is the nearest named service, running along Yale Road East through Rosedale with stops including Yale Rd at Hwy 9 and Yale Rd at McGrath Rd, per BC Transit's route overview. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- Route 66 Fraser Valley Express is the regional link from Downtown Chilliwack to Lougheed Town Centre Station, with Chilliwack stops at Downtown Chilliwack, Vedder Rd at Luckakuck Way and the Lickman Park & Ride. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=66

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the dike and Flood Construction Level for the specific lot under Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519; the bylaw sets habitable floors at the applicable level for the Fraser, Chilliwack and Vedder rivers plus 0.6 m freeboard in standard-dike areas. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- Check riparian setbacks before planning a build, because Development Permit Area 3 (Riparian Area) is listed among the OCP's development permit areas covering farmland watercourses. https://www.chilliwack.com/ocp
- Check the farm home plate on title plans and site plans: DPA 4 states a farm home plate includes septic tanks and related facilities but excludes sewerage disposal fields, so the field can sit outside the plate. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SD4%5FDPA%5FFarm%5FHome%5FPlate%2Epdf
- Freshet timing is a Fraser River issue: the City's flood FAQ ties its Fraser River Flood Response Plan phases to the Mission gauge, with bankfull about 5.5 m. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=4330
