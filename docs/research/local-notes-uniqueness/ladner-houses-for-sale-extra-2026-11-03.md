## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Ladner Village is in the Fraser River floodplain behind dikes, so building work must address flood risk despite the dikes. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is Ladner behind a dike, and what does that mean for a purchase?
**A:** Yes, Ladner sits in the Fraser River floodplain behind dikes, and the City states the Village could reasonably be expected to flood if the dike system failed. Check the flood-proofing covenant on title and ask what flood construction level applied when the house was built. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf

**Q:** Can I add a suite or coach house to a Ladner lot?
**A:** A secondary suite is an accessory use in the RS1 zone, and Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing amendments allow duplexes, suites, coach houses and garden suites where zoning applies. Confirm the parcel's zone on the City maps and its flood conditions before planning the work. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf

**Q:** Which elementary school serves a house near Ladner Village?
**A:** Ladner Elementary at 5016 44 Avenue is the village-area school, dual-track English and French Immersion, Kindergarten to Grade 7. School District No. 37 (Delta) catchments are address-based, so run the exact house address in the School Locator before relying on it. https://le.deltasd.bc.ca/our-school/about-our-school/

**Q:** What changes if a house is on Arthur Drive?
**A:** Arthur Drive, built in the 1880s beside Chilukthan Slough, was designated Ladner Heritage Conservation Area 2 (LHCA 2 – Arthur Drive), so exterior work faces heritage guidelines, not just zoning (check current: designated under former Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 3950, replaced by Bylaw No. 8400, 2024). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/42939/

**Q:** How do I reach Westham Island farmland from Ladner?
**A:** By the Westham Island Bridge over Canoe Pass, TransLink's oldest bridge, opened March 29, 1910. It is partially single-lane, swings 90 degrees for boats, and its 50-tonne limit excludes semi-trucks, so expect alternating one-way traffic on the way to the island farms. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2013/07/roads-bridges-getting-to-know-westham-island/
