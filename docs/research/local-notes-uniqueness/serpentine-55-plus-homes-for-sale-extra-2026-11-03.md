## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there a 55+ complex in Serpentine itself? **A:** No verified 55+ complex on this ground was confirmed in this pass. The index file and the official pages opened agree that naming one here would be a guess. A buyer should ask for a named complex, its street address and its filed bylaws, and treat any claim without those three items as unconfirmed until the strata documents are produced. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/serpentine-wildlife-management-area
- **Q:** Can my younger spouse live with me if I buy in a 55+ strata nearby? **A:** Yes, the Province requires an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a specified resident. A specified resident is a resident who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption, and the exemption covers a spouse married to, or living in a marriage-like relationship with, that resident. Confirm the complex's own bylaws as well, because a corporation can add further exemptions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** Does the Serpentine River affect a purchase here? **A:** Yes, flood and drainage checks matter on the lowland ground. Surrey states its floodplain is subject to coastal and river flooding, and most of the floodplain is protected by dykes rather than being outside the floodplain. Ask which dyke, flood box and pump system serves the lot, and get the flood terms for that parcel in writing before removing subjects. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain
- **Q:** Which schools should a grandchild's family check? **A:** School District 36 (Surrey) catchments are address-based, so no school should be promised from the Serpentine label alone. The Province's school listing confirms Earl Marriott Secondary at 15751 16 Ave, Surrey, in School District 36, as one south Surrey school in that listing. A family moving with a buyer, or buying for family use, must run the exact address through the district before relying on any catchment. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Serpentine Fen nature trail is about 3.5 kilometres long and the City allows about 1.5 hours for the walk. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/serpentine-wildlife-management-area
- The Fen covers 150 hectares with many habitats and is home to over 200 bird species, with September and October the busiest months for spotting birds. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/serpentine-wildlife-management-area
- The Wildlife Management Area is managed by Ducks Unlimited Canada, not as a normal City neighbourhood park operation. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/serpentine-wildlife-management-area
- Park hours listed by the City are dawn until dusk for the Serpentine Wildlife Management Area. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/serpentine-wildlife-management-area
- Sea dams on the Serpentine were first built in 1913, and the gates close when the tide comes in and open again to let river water exit. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain
- A spillway is a lower part of a river dyke where flood water can spill into a holding cell, typically on agricultural fields, and then drain back after the event. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Flood is the first check here, because Surrey states the coastal floodplain makes up about 20 percent of the city's land area and is subject to both coastal and river flooding. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain
- Most of Surrey's floodplain is protected by dykes, which means protection depends on the dyke system, not on the lot being outside a flood area. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain
- During high tide or flood events, electrically powered pumps help push water out to sea or into the rivers, so a buyer should ask about pump and flood box service for the parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain
