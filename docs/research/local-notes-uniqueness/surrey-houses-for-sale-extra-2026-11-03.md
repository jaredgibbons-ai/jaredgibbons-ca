## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000. July 8, 2024: 20 single-family and duplex zones consolidated into nine small-scale zones. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations
- Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH): over 72,000 lots under 4,050 square metres inside the urban containment boundary — suite, garden suite, coach house, duplex, houseplex, no rezoning where allowed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations
- One-third of Surrey is Official Community Plan (OCP) Agriculture, 95 percent Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) — A-1 lots carry ALR limits. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7918-0236-00.pdf
- Crescent Beach Flood Construction Level (FCL): 3.30 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada (GSC); below it needs a Development Variance Permit (DVP). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/crescent-beach-floodplain-residential-building-permit

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I add a coach house or houseplex? **A:** Often yes, without rezoning. The July 8, 2024 Zoning By-law No. 12000 changes permit Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing on over 72,000 lots under 4,050 square metres inside the urban containment boundary, including suites, coach houses, duplexes and houseplexes. Fit depends on zone, lot size and frequent bus stop status — check the lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations
- **Q:** Is a low Bridgeview or Mud Bay house normal? **A:** No — it is a floodplain purchase. A City report maps Fraser River freshet to Bridgeview, Port Mann, Port Kells and South Westminster, tides to Crescent Beach, Mud Bay and Colebrook Road, and winter rain to the Serpentine, Nicomekl and Little Campbell lowlands. Bridgeview sits behind dikes. (check current — 2007 report) https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/RPT_2007-C013.pdf
- **Q:** Does East Newton Elementary exist? **A:** No — it does not exist. The Ministry list for School District 36 (Surrey) records Tamanawis Secondary at 12600 66 Avenue and McLeod Road Traditional, Kindergarten to Grade 7, at 6325 142 Street. The district sets catchments — confirm the address in its school locator before a school-dependent offer. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** What does a legal suite cost yearly? **A:** For 2026 a secondary suite adds $454.00 water and $821.71 sewer, $1,275.71 total, plus $172.04 for garbage, recycling and organics collection each year. An owner who removed a suite must arrange an inspection, or the additional levy stays on the account for that tax year. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill

- Metered billing: every four months; sewer at 88 percent of water supplied; 5 percent penalty after due date. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill
- King George Boulevard Bus Rapid Transit (BRT): 30 percent designed, not fully funded — City update, October 20, 2025. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R216.pdf
- Serpentine dike raising: summer 2027. New pump station near 16000 block of 60 Avenue: late 2027. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026EMCR0038-000983.pdf
- Additional school tax applies to detached homes assessed at $3 million and over — City tax page. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- City floodplain map first — Crescent Beach buyers unsure of the boundary are directed to the City Floodplain map and guide. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/crescent-beach-floodplain-residential-building-permit
- The permit page does not state an existing house's build level — verify slab versus 3.30 metres GSC. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/crescent-beach-floodplain-residential-building-permit
- Most river lowland is ALR farmland — confirm reserve status before assuming non-farm use. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7918-0236-00.pdf
- SSMUH stops at 4,050 square metres and outside the urban containment boundary. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations
