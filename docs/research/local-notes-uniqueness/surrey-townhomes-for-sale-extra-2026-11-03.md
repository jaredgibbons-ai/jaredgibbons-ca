# Surrey Townhomes — Extra Local Notes
Page: surrey-townhomes-for-sale (Surrey; townhomes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Surrey townhomes are a city-wide search, not one neighbourhood: the City divides Surrey into six communities — Cloverdale, Fleetwood, Guildford, Newton, South Surrey and Whalley — and townhouse stock sits in all six, heaviest where neighbourhood concept plans designated townhouse land. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- The townhome belt a buyer actually tours runs along Fraser Highway through Fleetwood and Clayton, along 74 Avenue in West Clayton, and along 168 Street in Darts Hill and Grandview Heights in South Surrey — each cluster is named in a current City planning report below. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0268-00
- Surrey is in the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board (FVREB) area, not the Greater Vancouver board area, so a Surrey townhome search uses FVREB sub-areas such as Fleetwood Tynehead, Clayton and Grandview Heights. https://www.fvreb.bc.ca/about/about-fvreb/

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The current Surrey townhome form is a strata cluster built under a townhouse or multiple-residential zone, not a row of freehold houses: a May 2026 City report describes a 45-unit townhouse development at 1928 168 Street rezoned from Acreage Residential (RA) to Multiple Residential 30 (RM-30). https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0306-00
- West Clayton is the largest single townhome frontier: one application at 18980 and 18990 74 Avenue proposes 111 townhouse units rezoned to High Density Townhouse Zone (MR4), and an older file at 18702, 18726 and 18738 74 Avenue covers 166 townhouse units on three lots plus a park lot conveyed to the City. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0268-00
- Smaller infill clusters are the other Surrey pattern: 14 townhouse units at 10195 160 Street in Fleetwood, on a site rezoned from Urban Residential (R3) to Comprehensive Development (CD), and 12 units at 13054 112 Avenue near Scott Road, rezoned to Medium Density Townhouse Zone (MR2). https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0171-00
- Unit mix in the newest Clayton file is stated by the City: at 5880 192 Street all 34 proposed units are three-bedroom plus den, with 106 square metres of outdoor amenity space meeting the Zoning Bylaw requirement. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0283-00
- Named completed complexes with developer or strata pages stating addresses and build years could not be confirmed from official sources in this pass — see NOT CONFIRMED.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City-wide zoning, tax and Speculation and Vacancy Tax facts for Surrey are written in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note; the townhome-specific rules below are the zone and amenity rules that apply only to this stock. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- Surrey's 2026 zoning update renames the townhouse zones: the City's May 25, 2026 corporate report lists MR1 as Low Density Townhouse Zone and MR2 as Medium Density Townhouse Zone, alongside RM-10, RM-15 and RM-23 multiple-residential zones, in amendments to Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2026-R103.pdf
- A townhome complex must provide amenity space under the RM-30 rules the City applies: 3.0 square metres of indoor amenity space per dwelling unit, with a minimum of 37 square metres on site before cash-in-lieu can be considered, and cash-in-lieu priced at $3,000 per unit in the 10195 160 Street file. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0171-00
- Streamside setbacks can shrink a townhome site: the 10195 160 Street proposal needed a Development Permit for Hazardous Lands and Sensitive Ecosystems and a variance to streamside setbacks, because part of the Fleetwood site sits in a protected riparian corridor. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0171-00
- Side-yard setbacks in a CD townhouse zone can be tighter than the RM-30 standard: the same Fleetwood file reduces side yards from 6.0 metres under RM-30 to a minimum of 3.0 metres, which is why two Surrey complexes that look alike can have different gaps between buildings. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0171-00

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a Surrey townhome strata or freehold? **A:** Most newer Surrey townhomes are strata, built as one project under a townhouse zone such as RM-30, MR2 or MR4 after a rezoning and a form-and-character development permit. The City reports above describe each cluster as a single development application — 45 units at 1928 168 Street, 111 units at 18980 74 Avenue — so buyers get strata bylaws, a depreciation report and common-property amenity space, not a freehold lot. Check the Form B for the specific complex. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0306-00
- **Q:** Why are so many Surrey townhomes in Clayton? **A:** Because the West Clayton Neighbourhood Concept Plan (NCP) designated the land for cluster townhousing before it was built. City reports for 74 Avenue sites describe designations such as Medium Density Cluster and Townhouse 25–30 units per acre net, with open-space and park dedications built into each project, including a park lot conveyed to the City in the 166-unit file at 18702–18738 74 Avenue. The form is planned, not incidental. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7916-0221-00-and-7916-0221-01
- **Q:** Which school does a Clayton or Fleetwood townhome feed into? **A:** The catchment is set by the exact address, not by the complex name. Surrey Schools (School District 36) states that a catchment school is determined by the address and the district's School Boundaries Map, and directs families to its School Locator tool before registering. Two complexes on the same street can split between schools, so run the unit's civic address through the locator before writing an offer that depends on a school. https://www.surreyschools.ca/page/78827/school-catchment-locator
- **Q:** What amenity space should a newer Surrey townhome complex have? **A:** Under the RM-30 rules the City applies, a complex needs 3.0 square metres of indoor amenity space per unit and outdoor amenity space on top of that; where a small site cannot provide it, the developer pays cash-in-lieu at $3,000 per unit, as in the 14-unit Fleetwood file. An older complex with no amenity building is not breaking a rule — it was approved under the standards of its day — but the comparison matters when fees are similar. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0171-00
- **Q:** Will the Surrey-Langley SkyTrain change townhome areas? **A:** Yes, along Fraser Highway specifically. The extension runs from King George Station along Fraser Highway with Surrey stations at Green Timbers (140 Street), 152 Street, Fleetwood (160 Street), Bakerview–166 Street, Hillcrest–184 Street and Clayton (189 Street), so Fleetwood and Clayton townhome clusters sit on the corridor the line serves. Full project detail is in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/09/SLS_Monthly-Status-Report_July_2026.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- A townhome pays the multiplex waste rate, not the detached rate alone: the City charges waste collection of $344.08 for 2026 to a single-family house, duplex or multiplex, collected with property taxes — a strata townhome's share flows through that billing structure. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill
- Metered Surrey homes are billed for water and sewer every four months and sewer volume is charged at 88 percent of water supplied, with a 5 percent penalty on an unpaid metered balance after the due date — relevant in complexes where utilities are metered per unit rather than covered by strata fees. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill
- A townhome built beside a creek may carry a conveyed green corridor: the 13054 112 Avenue project conveys about 0.12 acres (500 square metres) to the City as open space to preserve the Green Infrastructure Network corridor along the watercourse, land the strata does not own or maintain. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0384-00
- In West Clayton, a townhome's street may not be finished: the 166-unit file required relocating the future 186A Street to protect an off-site stream, so road alignments in newer Clayton complexes were still being set by development files, not by the original grid. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7916-0221-00-and-7916-0221-01

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey's 2026 tax rate, parcel taxes and Speculation and Vacancy Tax position are city-wide facts written in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- The townhome-specific charge a buyer can verify is waste: $344.08 for 2026 for a single-family house, duplex or multiplex, collected with the property tax notice due July 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill
- A secondary suite levy does not apply to a standard townhome unit: the City's suite charge is defined for a self-contained unit inside a detached, semi-detached or duplex house, so a townhome buyer should not budget the $1,275.71 suite utility levy unless the unit itself contains a separately levied suite, which the utility page does not describe for townhomes. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Fleetwood and Clayton townhomes sit on the Fraser Highway corridor served today by bus and, on completion of the Surrey-Langley SkyTrain extension, by the Fleetwood (160 Street), Bakerview–166 Street, Hillcrest–184 Street and Clayton (189 Street) stations named in the project status report — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note for the full entry. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/09/SLS_Monthly-Status-Report_July_2026.pdf
- North Surrey townhomes near Scott Road use the R6 Scott Road RapidBus from Scott Road Station to Newton Exchange along Scott Road and 72 Avenue, and RapidBus routes run at least every 10 minutes at peak and every 15 minutes off-peak, 6 a.m. to midnight, on TransLink's project page. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
- The 12-unit project at 13054 112 Avenue is described by the City as close to both Gateway and Scott Road SkyTrain stations and about 150 metres outside the City Centre Plan boundary — the transit position for the Bridgeview-edge townhome cluster. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0384-00

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the flood and hazard layers for low-lying townhome clusters the same way as for houses — full method in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note; the townhome-specific check is the development permit record for the complex itself. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- A complex approved with a Hazardous Lands and Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit, like 10195 160 Street, carries streamside setbacks that limit what the strata can build or pave in the corridor — read the permit in the City file before assuming a yard or amenity area can be extended. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0171-00
- In a complex approved under a Comprehensive Development (CD) zone, the rules are the CD bylaw for that site, not the standard RM-30 zone — setbacks, amenity space and parking can all differ from the complex across the street, so the strata's Form B and the CD bylaw are the documents to read. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0171-00
