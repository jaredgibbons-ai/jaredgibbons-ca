# Brentwood Park Townhomes — Extra Local Notes

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Brentwood Park's townhome question is a street question: the ground-oriented stock sits on the established streets south of Lougheed Highway — Springer Avenue is the street the City's own applications name for street-oriented townhouses — while the towers rise on the town-centre lots north and east of those streets. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2024-08/Major-Development-Projects.pdf
- Brentwood is one of Burnaby's four town centres, which the City's community-plans page describes as combining transportation and social infrastructure with residential and employment opportunities and higher-density housing. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- Brentwood-shared facts (boundaries, condo towers, station, Amazing Brentwood in full): see the brentwood-park-condos-for-sale note. City-wide Burnaby facts: see the vancouver-heights-burnaby-houses-for-sale note.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Standalone townhouse complexes are the minority form here: most "townhouse" units in Brentwood Park are street-fronting townhouse units at the base of tower projects, a form the City's applications state expressly — for example, the Boffo Developments phase at Springer Avenue and Lougheed Highway is a high-rise building "with street orientated townhouses atop underground parking." http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2024-08/Major-Development-Projects.pdf
- The same pattern appears at the town centre's west edge, where a Dawson Street application is described by the City as a multi-family residential building "with street fronting townhouses, and above and below grade structured parking." http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2024-08/Major-Development-Projects.pdf
- A buyer here is therefore usually buying a strata lot inside a tower strata, with the tower's Form B, depreciation report and parkade — not a lot in a townhouse-only complex. Named standalone complexes with addresses and years built could not be confirmed on an allowed source in this pass; see NOT CONFIRMED.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Townhouse-form homes have their own district in the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (Bylaw No. 14815): the district list names a Townhouse District (R2), with an R2r rental variant, separate from the Low-Rise, Mid-Rise and High-Rise Apartment districts (R3 to R8) that govern the tower bases most Brentwood townhouse units sit in. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-07/Zoning-Bylaw-Consolidated-Without-Schedules.pdf
- A tower-base townhouse is still a strata lot: the Province states all strata residential rental-restriction bylaws are invalid as of November 24, 2022, only a 55-or-older age bylaw is allowed, and short-term rental bylaws remain allowed — so the registered bylaws of the tower strata, not a townhouse-complex rulebook, govern the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- The Zoning Bylaw 2026's Form and Character Development Permit Area guidelines exempt R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing uses proposed in an R2 Townhouse district from a form and character permit, except supportive housing — a small-infill rule that matters on the Springer Avenue house streets. https://bylaws.burnaby.ca/media/14000/14815.pdf
- Burnaby 2050, adopted December 9, 2025, takes precedence over older community-plan land-use maps where they conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is there a townhouse complex in Brentwood Park, or is it all towers?
**A:** The confirmed form is mostly townhouse units inside tower projects, not standalone complexes. The City's current applications describe street-oriented townhouses at the base of a Springer Avenue high-rise (Boffo Developments, REZ #21-30) and street-fronting townhouses in a Dawson Street mixed-use building. A buyer who wants a townhouse-only strata should verify the strata plan of any listing, because the address alone does not tell them which form they are buying. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2024-08/Major-Development-Projects.pdf

**Q:** What is Brentwood Block, and does it include townhouses?
**A:** Brentwood Block is Grosvenor's 7.9-acre pedestrian-focused master plan at Alpha Avenue. Grosvenor states construction has started on Phase 1: a 41-storey residential strata tower, two rental towers, a new 100,000 square foot Brentwood Community Centre and 180,000 square feet of commercial, with 1,279 rental homes in Phase 1. It is tower product, not a townhouse complex, and it is written in full in Section 6 of this note. https://www.grosvenor.com/news-insights/grosvenor-starts-construction-on-burnaby-s-first-pedestrian-focused-master-plan,-brentwood-block

**Q:** Which stations serve the townhouse streets?
**A:** Two Millennium Line stations bracket the area: Brentwood Town Centre Station at Lougheed Highway and Willingdon Avenue, and Gilmore Station at Lougheed Highway and Gilmore Avenue, with Holdom Station next east. Since September 7, 2026 the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus also stops at Brentwood Town Centre on its Park Royal to Metrotown run, so the Springer Avenue streets have a RapidBus connection as well as the SkyTrain. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects

**Q:** Can I rent out a Brentwood townhouse long-term?
**A:** Yes, under provincial law: the Province states no strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw and all such bylaws are invalid as of November 24, 2022. The limit to check is the short-term rental bylaw of the specific tower strata, because short-term rental bylaws are still allowed and are building-specific. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Two projects with near-identical names are not the same tenure: SHAPE's own page for The Amazing Brentwood states Tower ONE's 300 rental homes are professionally managed by SHAPE, while the same master plan's other towers are strata — a townhouse or condo buyer must check which tower a listing is in. https://shape.ca/portfolio/the-amazing-brentwood/
- SHAPE states The Amazing Brentwood is a 28-acre master plan at 4567 Lougheed Highway with over 6,000 homes over 11 residential towers at completion, SkyTrain connected on site, and homes in Neighbourhood Two now selling — the construction context around the established townhouse streets. https://shape.ca/portfolio/the-amazing-brentwood/
- The federal contribution to Brentwood Block is on the public record: Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation (CMHC) states the two buildings at 2132 Alpha Avenue and 4608 Lougheed Highway are part of the 7.9-acre Brentwood Block site, with 1,279 rental units, 160,000 square feet of commercial space and 451 condominiums in Phase 1. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/media-newsroom/news-releases/2025/canada-invests-new-rental-housing-units-burnaby
- Brentwood Block's civic piece is a City-owned building, not a strata amenity: Grosvenor states the new 100,000 square foot Brentwood Community Centre will be City-owned, with an anticipated completion of late 2029, and that the master plan also includes a daycare. https://www.grosvenor.com/news-insights/grosvenor-starts-construction-on-burnaby-s-first-pedestrian-focused-master-plan,-brentwood-block

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby's 2026 municipal residential rate and city-wide utility and Speculation and Vacancy Tax treatment: see the vancouver-heights-burnaby-houses-for-sale note.
- A Hastings Business Improvement Area (BIA) levy exists in north Burnaby but not in Brentwood: the Burnaby Rates Bylaw 2026 (Bylaw No. 14814) sets a Hastings BIA rate of 0.65242 per $1,000 on Class 6 (Business and Other) only, and notes the Burnaby North Road BIA expired March 31, 2026 — a Brentwood townhouse owner does not pay a BIA levy on a residential unit. https://bylaws.burnaby.ca/media/14000/14814.pdf
- The multi-family utility line a townhouse strata budgets from is the City's 2026 Multiple Family Dwelling rate: water $374.54 and sewer use $436.00 per year, before the 5% early-payment discount. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Brentwood Town Centre Station (Millennium Line) sits above Lougheed Highway east of Willingdon Avenue, between Gilmore Station west and Holdom Station east, and is the station for both the tower lots and the Springer Avenue streets. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
- The R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus added a Brentwood stop on September 7, 2026: TransLink states the extension runs from Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station via Hastings Street and Willingdon Avenue, all day, seven days a week, and replaced the peak-only 222 Willingdon Express. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- Highway 1 access is south of the townhouse streets via Willingdon Avenue and Sprott Street; a measured drive time is UNCONFIRMED rather than estimated.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Streamside setback is the south-edge check to ask about: a ground-oriented lot at the area's south edge should be checked for a streamside protection area on the City's mapping before an addition is planned — a lot-level check a tower strata unit does not carry.
- Construction adjacency is a dated, checkable fact here, not a general warning: Brentwood Block Phase 1 and the Springer Avenue towers are active files on the City list updated October 27, 2026, so a buyer on Alpha Avenue or Springer Avenue should read the current application status for the lot beside the complex, not a past summary. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2024-08/Major-Development-Projects.pdf
- For a tower-base townhouse, the depreciation report belongs to the whole tower strata: the Province's updated depreciation report requirements (effective July 1, 2024) apply to the tower's systems — parkade, elevators, envelope — which the townhouse owner shares. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
