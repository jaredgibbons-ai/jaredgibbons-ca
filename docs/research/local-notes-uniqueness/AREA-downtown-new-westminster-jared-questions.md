Area file for Downtown New Westminster, owned by uniqueness batch U15 — the first Downtown New Westminster page in program order is `downtown-new-westminster-condos-townhomes-for-sale` (#359). Five questions only Jared can answer, for a voice memo. Muse does not answer these.

## Questions

1. Who buys downtown New Westminster condos in your deals — first-time buyers, downsizers leaving houses in the Heights and Sapperton, or investors? What draws them to the downtown core specifically?
2. What sells fast and what sits — river-view towers at the Quay, the Columbia Street buildings, or the older low-rises on Carnarvon and Ash? What makes the difference?
3. What surprises buyers — freight-train noise along the riverfront, strata parking, the hillside streets, or the difference between the Quay end and the Columbia Street end?
4. When a buyer is choosing between downtown New West and Uptown or Burnaby's Highgate, what do you tell them?
5. One story: a downtown New West deal that taught you something — a building you now recommend or avoid, and why.
