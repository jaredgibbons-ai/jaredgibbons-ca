- Houses sit in the R1 SSMUH District: 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, 6 within 400 m of frequent transit; suites count in the total. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary schools sit here? **A:** Two elementary schools sit on this corridor. École Seaforth Elementary (K–7) is at 7881 Government Rd, and Stoney Creek Community Elementary (K–7) is at 2740 Beaverbrook Cres. School District 41 catchments are address-based, so run the exact address through the district locator first. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** Can a house lot hold more homes? **A:** Yes, an R1 SSMUH lot can hold more homes without rezoning. Burnaby permits three units on lots up to 280 square metres, four on larger lots, and six on larger lots inside a Frequent Transit Network Area. BurnabyMap shows a specific lot's maximum. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q:** Is Burnaby Lake a swimming lake? **A:** No swimming claim is made in the sources read. Metro Vancouver describes Burnaby Lake Regional Park for level trails, wildlife, a dock, and canoeing or kayaking, with dogs leashed and prohibited at Piper Spit, not a swimming beach for houses nearby. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/burnaby-lake-regional-park
- **Q:** What replaced the Cameron centre? **A:** A replacement Cameron Community Centre and Library is under construction in Lougheed Town Centre. The City states it will be four times larger than the original, with pools, an expanded library and gym, and gives an estimated completion date of 2028. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2026-03-09/burnabys-once-generation-civic-building-program-reaches-50-completion
Read: 2026-11-03
