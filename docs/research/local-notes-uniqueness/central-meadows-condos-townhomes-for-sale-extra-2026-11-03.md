## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Central Meadows is Pitt Meadows' civic core, and its condo stock stands on one street: Harris Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- The City's official name for this ground is the Municipal Town Centre in the official community plan (OCP), around Harris Road, City Hall at 12007 Harris Road, Lougheed Highway at the north edge and the CPKC rail line along Hammond Road to the south. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A co-operative home at 19032 Advent Road has no Form B Information Certificate, because the Strata Property Act diligence package does not apply to a housing co-operative the way it does to Solaris or Origin. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- Meadowland Village's 55-plus position comes from listing records, not a strata page read here, so confirm the registered age bylaw in the Form B package before relying on it. {Research file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/central-meadows-pitt-meadows-condos-2026-11-01.md}

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What is the difference between Meadowland Village and the Meadowlands co-op? **A:** They are different tenures on the same Advent Road pocket, and only one is a strata purchase. Meadowland Village at 18960 Advent Road is a freehold strata townhome complex where a buyer gets title to a strata lot and a Form B package. Meadowlands at 19032 Advent Road is a housing co-operative listed by CHF BC, where the buyer holds membership shares instead of strata title. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves a Harris Road condo? **A:** For the City Hall sample address at 12007 Harris Road, the earlier program file records Pitt Meadows Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary as the catchment pair. Pitt Meadows Elementary stands at 11941 Harris Road, on the same corridor as Solaris and Origin, and teaches kindergarten to grade 7. Always rerun the exact unit address in the School District 42 locator, because catchments here are address-based. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- **Q:** Is Solaris pet-free? **A:** The building directory in the earlier program file records pets not allowed at Solaris at 12069 Harris Road, while Origin's pet position is listing-level only. Directory lines are not bylaws, so a pet owner should read the registered bylaws attached to the Form B for the specific building rather than relying on either line. The two Harris Road buildings do not share one pet rule. {Research file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/central-meadows-pitt-meadows-condos-2026-11-01.md}
- **Q:** How close is the train? **A:** Pitt Meadows Station is on Harris Road in this sub-area, which is why Central Meadows is the city's commuter core. The City's transportation page describes West Coast Express service from that station toward Waterfront Station in Vancouver. No other Pitt Meadows sub-area contains a station, so a Harris Road condo and a Bonson Road townhome have materially different commutes on the same city page set. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Even the civic core is mapped floodplain work: check a Harris Road lot against Floodplain Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 Schedule B and its 5.15-metre urban designated flood level — full wording in the south-meadows note. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 - Consolidated.pdf
- The CPKC rail line along Hammond Road bounds this sub-area to the south, so south-facing Harris Road units carry a rail-noise question the strata minutes, not a map, will answer. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- For a 2008–2010 building like Origin or Solaris, the first mandatory depreciation report and the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance on the Form B are the levy-risk documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
