# Neilsen Grove Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: neilsen-grove (Neilsen Grove, Ladner; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Neilsen Grove is a north Ladner pocket in the City of Delta, and the spelling is the first fact: the AREA is "Neilsen Grove" and the SCHOOL is "Neilson Grove Elementary" — opposite spellings, both official (https://ng.deltasd.bc.ca/).
- The pocket sits on and around the Marina Garden Estates lands on Admiral Way and Admiral Boulevard, named in City of Delta council reports; the City publishes no neighbourhood boundary for the name (https://delta.civicweb.net/document/202969/).
- Neilson Grove Elementary is physically in the area at 5500 Admiral Boulevard (https://ng.deltasd.bc.ca/).
- Hawthorne is south, Delta Manor and the Ladner village core are south-west, and Ladner Harbour is west, at Ladner Harbour Park, 4905 McNeelys Way (https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/ladner-harbour-park).
- Ladner-shared facts for the Delta cluster are carried in full in this note; the ladner-rural note points here for them.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The pocket mixes freehold detached houses with strata townhouse and apartment buildings in the Hampton Cove / Marina Garden Estates development on Admiral Way and Admiral Boulevard (https://delta.civicweb.net/document/202969/).
- Polygon's Hampton Cove buildings, from the developer's own pages: Pilothouse, a condominium building at 5535 Admiral Way (https://polyhomes.com/community/pilothouse/); Fairwinds townhomes at 5550 Admiral Way (http://www.polyhomes.com/community/fairwinds); Beaufort Landing townhomes at 5551 Admiral Way (https://www.polyhomes.com/community/beaufortlanding/); and Westhampton townhomes at 5311 Admiral Way (https://www.polyhomes.com/community/westhampton/).
- A City council report names Marina Garden Estates at 5311 Admiral Way as a townhouse development, and a City memorandum describes a multi-family apartment building within the same application lands (https://delta.civicweb.net/document/84874/).
- Detached lots follow the Ladner zone ladder in Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 — RS5 minimum lot area 550 m² (460 m² outside the Single Family Residential designation), RS6 at 390 m² and RS7 at 335 m² for new lots created by subdivision (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City-wide Delta zoning, SSMUH, tax and dike facts are written in full in the cliff-drive note — see the cliff-drive note. This section covers what is specific to this pocket.
- Strata purchases here follow the Province's strata rules: a buyer should review the strata bylaws, rules, fees, special levies and strata records before removing subjects, because most of the Hampton Cove stock is strata tenure (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners).
- Lots near the harbour shore can fall in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, which covers any Delta property within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch (https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf).
- The RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use to a single detached dwelling, which is the suite route for the pocket's detached lots where RS1 applies (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** How do you spell it — Neilsen or Neilson Grove?
**A:** Both spellings are correct, for different things — the area is "Neilsen Grove" and the school is "Neilson Grove Elementary", at 5500 Admiral Blvd on its School District 37 site. Use the area spelling in searches and contracts for the neighbourhood, and the school spelling for the school; mixing them up is the common error here (https://ng.deltasd.bc.ca/).

**Q:** Which schools serve a Neilsen Grove home?
**A:** Catchments are address-based in School District 37, with elementary at Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary at Grades 8 to 12. For the sample address 5500 Admiral Boulevard, the locator result is Neilson Grove Elementary for K–7 and Delta Secondary, 4615 51 St, for 8–12. Other addresses in the pocket must be run in the locator individually (https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/).

**Q:** Is Hampton Cove strata or freehold?
**A:** The Hampton Cove buildings are strata product — Polygon's own pages present Pilothouse at 5535 Admiral Way as a condominium building and Fairwinds, Beaufort Landing and Westhampton as townhome communities, while the pocket's detached houses on the surrounding streets are freehold under the RS zones. Confirm the tenure of a specific unit from title and the strata plan, not the marketing name (https://polyhomes.com/community/pilothouse/).

**Q:** Which bus runs through Neilsen Grove?
**A:** TransLink route 618, the Ladner North loop from Ladner Exchange, runs through this part of Ladner — out via Central Avenue, Linden Drive, Westminster Avenue and Ferry Road to Commodore Drive, and back via Ferry Road, Admiral Way, Admiral Boulevard, Westminster Avenue, Crescent Drive and 57 Street. It is the pocket's own loop service (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt618.pdf).

**Q:** Is this pocket behind dikes?
**A:** Yes — Ladner is low land protected by the City of Delta's dike, seawall and drainage-pump system; the city-wide figures are in the cliff-drive note. A specific lot's flood construction level is property-specific and is confirmed with the City, not from the sub-area name (http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage).

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta's 2026 tax increase, due dates, flat rate utility bill and Speculation and Vacancy Tax position are city-wide and are written in full in the cliff-drive note — see the cliff-drive note (https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes).
- The pocket-specific point is billing type: urban Ladner homes here are on City water and sewer, billed on the annual flat rate utility bill or quarterly metered bills, unlike the well-and-septic checks that apply on Delta farm parcels (http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities).

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check streamside setbacks near the harbour and sloughs: any lot within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area (https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf).
- For a Hampton Cove strata unit, the due diligence is the strata record set — bylaws, rules, fees, levies and depreciation information — under the Province's strata buying guidance (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners).
- Check the exact school catchment by address: the Neilson Grove Elementary result in §4 is for the school's own address as a sample, and catchments are address-based (https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/).
- Confirm the parcel's flood construction level with the City: Ladner is diked low land, and the level is property-specific (http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage).
