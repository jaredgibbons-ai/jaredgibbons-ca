# East Burnaby Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: east-burnaby-houses-for-sale (East Burnaby; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- East Burnaby is a broad quadrant label for Burnaby's eastern side, not one small neighbourhood. It runs from the Edmonds town centre and the Kingsway and Canada Way corridors east to the City of New Westminster border, taking in the Cariboo-Armstrong streets, the Second Street and Twelfth Avenue school neighbourhoods and the Robert Burnaby Park lands. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/city-departments/burnaby-public-library
- Edmonds, one of Burnaby's four town centres, anchors the west side, and the New Westminster border anchors the east. Smaller board sub-areas, including Edmonds, Cariboo and The Crest, sit inside the geography a buyer would call east Burnaby, so name the street and school node, not only the quadrant. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Highgate and Edmonds sit to the west and south-west, and South Slope sits to the south-west. The City of New Westminster lies across 10th Avenue and the eastern border. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached houses on the interior east-side streets are the stock this page covers. The attached stock concentrates toward the Edmonds and Highgate nodes, which carry their own sub-area rows, while the Second Street, Twelfth Avenue and Cariboo Hill streets hold the house lots this page is about. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Robert Burnaby Park is the east side's large park, and its courts give the house streets a named recreation anchor. The City's outdoor courts list gives the park 3 tennis courts and 10 pickleball courts, with a practice wall. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/outdoor-courts
- Rosemary Brown Recreation Centre at 7789 18th Street, at the area's south side, is a year-round multi-sport facility. The City lists 2 National Hockey League (NHL)-sized rinks and 2 multipurpose rooms there, with public skates, lessons and hockey, figure skating and ringette. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/rosemary-brown-recreation-centre

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- House lots here follow the citywide R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) rules — see the montecito-burnaby-houses-for-sale note for the full unit counts. The east-side caution is the creek network: lots near Robert Burnaby Park and the ravine lands can carry watercourse setbacks, so confirm setbacks for the exact lot before designing added units. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- The Edmonds Community Plan covers the town-centre part of the east side and was officially adopted in March 2025. Burnaby is organized into 20 Community Plan Areas under the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP), adopted December 9, 2025. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/official-community-plan
- Cariboo Hill Secondary's heritage record dates the school to 1961, built for the growing Armstrong, Seaforth and Lyndhurst elementary enrolments on the east side. That date anchors how long the surrounding house neighbourhoods have been established, without stating an age for any individual house. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark837

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools are on Burnaby's east side? **A:** Four schools named in the research serve the broad east side, and the catchment depends on the address. The Province's School District 41 (SD41) list names Second Street Community Elementary at 7502 2nd Street, Twelfth Avenue Elementary at 7622 12th Avenue, Taylor Park Elementary at 7590 Mission Avenue and Cariboo Hill Secondary at 8580 16th Avenue. Check each address in the SD41 locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** What is Robert Burnaby Park to a house buyer? **A:** Robert Burnaby Park is the large forested park at the centre of the east-side house area. The City describes many creeks and streams running through it and over half its area as critical wildlife habitat, and its restoration project is removing invasive species and planting 3,000 new trees, with some trails and areas closed during the work. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/ecosystem-restoration-robert-burnaby
- **Q:** Where is the recreation centre for the south side of East Burnaby? **A:** Rosemary Brown Recreation Centre at 7789 18th Street serves the south side with 2 NHL-sized rinks. At the west edge, Edmonds Community Centre at 7433 Edmonds Street adds a 6-lane pool, a leisure pool with waterslides and a lazy river, 2 gyms and the Edmonds Centre for 55+. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/edmonds-community-centre
- **Q:** Where do I go for urgent care on the east side? **A:** The Edmonds Urgent and Primary Care Centre (UPCC) at #201-7315 Edmonds Street serves the west side of East Burnaby. Fraser Health lists it separately from Burnaby Hospital at 3935 Kincaid Street, which sits north-west of East Burnaby across the city. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/burnaby/edmonds-urgent-and-primary-care-centre

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The library branch for this quadrant is the Tommy Douglas Library at the Kingsway town-centre node in Edmonds. Burnaby Public Library runs four branches, one in each quadrant of the city, matching the quadrant structure the City itself uses. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/city-departments/burnaby-public-library
- Route 116 links the area's southern streets to two SkyTrain stations. TransLink's timetable runs the 116 between Metrotown Station and Edmonds Station via Imperial Street, Sussex Avenue, Marine Drive, Marine Way, Byrne Road, Southridge Drive, Griffiths Drive and 18th Avenue. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt116.pdf
- Edmonds Station on the Expo Line serves the west side of the east-side house area. The City lists Burnaby's Expo Line stations as Patterson, Metrotown, Royal Oak and Edmonds, and states its town centres are served by SkyTrain plus high-frequency and local bus routes. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Shopping for East Burnaby houses concentrates at the Edmonds and Highgate node on Kingsway and at 10th Avenue and Canada Way local shops. There is no enclosed shopping centre on the interior house streets themselves. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Citywide Burnaby tax and utility figures are carried in full in the montecito-burnaby-houses-for-sale note — see that note. Burnaby is a Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) area question every east-side buyer should settle separately: the Province lists taxable areas by municipality, and reserve lands, treaty lands and lands of self-governing Indigenous Nations are not part of the taxable areas. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- The 2026–2030 Financial Plan adds a Growth Infrastructure Investment Levy of 1.9 percent on top of the 2.9 percent property tax increase. The City states the levy is about a $39 increase for an average home, separate from the roughly $61 average-home figure for the tax increase itself. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2026-01-28/burnaby-keeps-tax-rates-affordable-latest-financial-plan

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Edmonds Station is the rapid-transit access point for the west side of East Burnaby. It sits on the Expo Line, and route 116 connects the southern house streets to both Edmonds Station and Metrotown Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt116.pdf
- Canada Way, Kingsway, Edmonds Street and 10th Avenue are the main east–west roads. Sixth Street, 2nd Street and Cariboo Road run north–south toward Lougheed Highway and New Westminster. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/ecosystem-restoration-robert-burnaby

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check watercourse setbacks early on a park-edge or ravine-edge lot near Robert Burnaby Park. Creeks and streams run through the park and the east-side ravine lands, and over half the park is critical wildlife habitat, so a lot that backs onto it needs a setback check before a rebuild is designed. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/ecosystem-restoration-robert-burnaby
- No floodplain designation for East Burnaby's residential streets was confirmed in the sources read. Parcel-level hazard, watercourse-setback and Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status are property-specific checks, with ALR status checked through the Agricultural Land Commission. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
