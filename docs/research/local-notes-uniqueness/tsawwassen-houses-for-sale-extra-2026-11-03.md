## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Delta's zoning is Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017 (verified on delta.ca, 2026-11-03). It limits use, density, size, height and siting. http://www.delta.ca/node/763
- Delta adopted small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) zoning on June 24, 2024, for single-detached and duplex lots city-wide. A houseplex zone is being explored. https://letstalk.delta.ca/ssmuh
- Delta's Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 8400, 2024, contains no Tsawwassen Area Plan. Farm land is designated Agriculture (A) — check current. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I add a suite or coach house? **A:** Yes, in many cases, because Delta's June 24, 2024 small-scale multi-unit housing changes allow a building permit for a secondary suite, garden suite or coach house on a single-detached lot without rezoning. Confirm the lot's unit count on DeltaMap first. https://letstalk.delta.ca/ssmuh
- **Q:** Is a Beach Grove house flood-exposed? **A:** Low-lying Beach Grove lots sit behind the Boundary Bay dike, so check the individual lot first. DeltaMap shows flood protection, dike elevations and lot dimensions by parcel, and Delta received $2 million in provincial funding in 2023 for Boundary Bay dike raising. https://www.delta.ca/about-delta/deltamap?viewer=DeltaMapExternal.viewer/
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves my street? **A:** South Delta Secondary serves Tsawwassen addresses, but check the exact street before relying on it. Delta School District No. 37 (SD37) publishes Tsawwassen catchment maps, notes that boundaries are reviewed and occasionally adjusted, and directs families to its School Locator for verification. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/student-registration/catchment-area-maps/
- **Q:** Is nearby land Tsawwassen First Nation land? **A:** It can be on the northwest side, and City rules do not govern it. Tsawwassen First Nation holds jurisdiction over its 724-hectare treaty land base under its 2009 Final Agreement, separate from Delta bylaws, DeltaMap zoning and Delta property taxes. https://tsawwassenfirstnation.com/about-tfn/our-nation/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta property tax is charged per $1,000 of net taxable value, due July 2, 2026, with a 2.9 percent 2026 City increase. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- The 2026 provincial school tax for School District No. 37 (Delta) is $1.0997 per $1,000 of residential value, set provincially. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/oic/OIC_CUR/0124_2026
- Flat-rate utilities for a single-family home total $1,658 in 2026. Delta bills them in late February; metered homes pay quarterly. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in Delta, a listed taxable area. Treaty lands of self-governing Indigenous Nations are excluded. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
