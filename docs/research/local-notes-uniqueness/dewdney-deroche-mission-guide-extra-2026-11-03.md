# Dewdney & Deroche Area Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: dewdney-deroche-mission-guide (Dewdney & Deroche; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Dewdney and Deroche are two separate unincorporated villages on Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) east of Mission, not one town and not inside Mission. The Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) lists Electoral Area G as Nicomen Island, Deroche, Dewdney and Hatzic Island (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).
- Deroche's eastern bench ground crosses into Electoral Area C, because the FVRD's Area C plan area runs from the Harrison River to east of Deroche and includes the eastern portions of the Deroche Bench (https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/areacocp?tool=news_feed).
- Corridor streets named on official sources include Lougheed Highway, Taylor Road in Deroche, Hawkins-Pickle Road in Dewdney and North Deroche Road in Deroche (https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075).

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The corridor mixes fee-simple acreage houses with manufactured homes on rented pads, two different ownership types on one page. FVRD bylaws provide separate community water systems for Deroche and for Dewdney in Electoral Area G, serving the settled lots along Highway 7 (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/water.html).
- Each village has its own elementary school and hall-scale services rather than a shared town centre: Dewdney Elementary at 37151 Hawkins-Pickle Road and Deroche Elementary at 10340 North Deroche Road (https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- FVRD zoning, not City of Mission zoning, governs both villages, under FVRD Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021; a Thompson Road parcel in Area G is shown in that bylaw as Agricultural 4 (AG-4), permitting farm and residential use (https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=26940).
- Bylaw No. 1638, 2021 permits one secondary suite or one accessory dwelling unit per lot, not both, in almost all FVRD electoral areas, subject to lot size and servicing (https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/adu).
- On Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) parcels the Agricultural Land Commission allows one principal residence up to 500 square metres plus one additional residence of 90 square metres up to 40 hectares, or 186 square metres above 40 hectares (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- Norrish Creek, north of Dewdney, is a protected community drinking-water watershed: a provincial assessment states Norrish Creek is a principal water supply for Abbotsford and Mission through the FVRD, with the community intake about 9 kilometres upstream of the Fraser River (https://a100.gov.bc.ca/pub/acat/public/viewReport.do?reportId=9433).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Are Dewdney and Deroche part of Mission? **A:** No, both carry Mission mailing addresses but sit in unincorporated FVRD Electoral Area G. Zoning, building permits, community water and provincial rural taxes run through the FVRD and the Province, so City of Mission bylaws, suite rules and tax dates do not apply to a Highway 7 lot here (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).
**Q:** Which elementary school does each village have? **A:** Each village has its own School District No. 75 school: Dewdney Elementary, kindergarten to grade 6, at 37151 Hawkins-Pickle Road, and Deroche Elementary, kindergarten to grade 6, at 10340 North Deroche Road. Both catchments feed Hatzic Middle School under district policy (https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075).
**Q:** Is there a bus to Mission or Agassiz? **A:** Not yet on an operator page read for this note. BC Transit and the FVRD consulted in spring 2025 on a proposed Highway 7 route between downtown Mission and downtown Agassiz through Deroche, with an open house at Deroche Community Hall, 41555 North Nicomen Road (https://www.bctransit.com/have-your-say-in-the-future-of-transit-along-highway-2025-04-25/).
**Q:** Does flooding affect the lowland lots? **A:** Yes, the lowland has had freshet alerts, so check the parcel. The FVRD has issued freshet evacuation alerts for Area G ground including Nicomen Island and the Taylor Road area in Deroche, and has listed Dewdney Regional Park among flood-related park closures (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2022/freshet-update.html).
**Q:** Is a manufactured home here the same as owning land? **A:** No, a park home sits on a rented pad while an acreage house is fee simple, and the two finance and resell differently. Confirm the current pad rent, park rules and approval process in the park's own documents before writing an offer on any Highway 7 park home (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/water.html).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Deroche water is billed quarterly on a flat rate: the FVRD's 2026 Area G rates list Water – Deroche at a $102.00 flat rate, with utility bills issued in January, April, July and October (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html).
- Dewdney water is metered, not flat: FVRD Bylaw No. 1824, 2026 sets the Electoral Area G Dewdney system on a minimum quarterly charge plus per-cubic-metre consumption tiers (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Sewer~and~Water/Water Systems fees and charges amendment BL 1824, 2026.pdf).
- The FVRD publishes separate water system maps for Deroche and for the Dewdney Community Water System showing exactly which properties are connected (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/water.html).
- Area G services named by the FVRD include the Dewdney and Deroche community water systems, North Fraser fire protection, north side street lighting and north side garbage (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html).
- Nicomen Slough and Deroche Creek both have federal hydrometric stations at Deroche and Dewdney, a sign of how closely water levels on this lowland are monitored (https://wateroffice.ec.gc.ca/station_metadata/reference_index_e.html?stnNum=08MH085).

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Owners here pay property tax to the Province, not to Mission, because Dewdney and Deroche are rural areas; the Province states rural owners receive their notice from the Surveyor of Taxes Office (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area).
- Rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5 percent penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5 percent on amounts unpaid on October 31, under section 11 of the Taxation (Rural Area) Act (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01).
- Deroche and Dewdney water charges in Section 5 are the sub-area-specific costs here; City of Mission figures do not apply — see the mission-farms note for the city-wide figures.

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) is the only through road, running west to Mission and east toward Harrison Mills and Agassiz, and the proposed BC Transit Highway 7 route would run on it between downtown Mission and downtown Agassiz (https://www.bctransit.com/have-your-say-in-the-future-of-transit-along-highway-2025-04-25/).
- The nearest commuter rail is in Mission: the West Coast Express leaves Mission City Station on five weekday westbound trains, with no weekend service; no operator page read states a Dewdney or Deroche stop (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf).

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check flood status by parcel identifier number (PID) before valuing a lowland lot, because the Taylor Road and Nicomen Island ground has recorded FVRD freshet evacuation alerts and the Fraser River floodplain covers the corridor floor (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2022/freshet-update.html).
- Check ALR status by PID on the Agricultural Land Commission maps, because ALR parcels carry the 500 square metre principal-residence limit and the 90 or 186 square metre additional-residence limit (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- Confirm whether the lot is inside the Deroche or Dewdney water service area on the FVRD system maps, or on a private well and septic system, before assuming community servicing (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/water.html).
