# Beach Grove — Questions for Jared

## Questions
1. In your Beach Grove deals, how much do buyers actually pay for direct Boundary Bay frontage over a non-waterfront street in the same pocket, and what evidence do you use to show it?
2. Who buys in Beach Grove in your experience — Tsawwassen locals moving within town, downsizers after the beach, or buyers coming from outside Delta?
3. What surprises buyers here after they write — the seawall's condition or certification, flood covenants on title, insurance, or the SD3 permit rules when they plan work?
4. Which Beach Grove homes sell fast and which sit — is it the waterfront row, the cottage-era stock, or the larger inland lots that move first?
5. Tell one Beach Grove story: a deal, a showing at high tide or in a storm, or a seawall conversation that captures what buying on this shore is really like.
