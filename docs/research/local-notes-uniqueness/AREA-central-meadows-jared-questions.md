# Central Meadows — Questions for Jared
Area: Central Meadows, City of Pitt Meadows. Five questions only Jared can answer from his own deals, plus a photo shot list. Do not answer these from research.

## Questions for Jared
1. When a buyer tours Solaris at 12069 Harris Road versus Origin at 11950 Harris Road, what actually decides it for them — height and concrete, price per door, or the pet rules?
2. Who buys on the Harris Road corridor — West Coast Express commuters, downsizers from Pitt Meadows houses, or first-time buyers — and has that mix changed?
3. What sells fast and what sits in Central Meadows, and does a unit facing the CPKC rail line or Hammond Road take measurably longer?
4. What surprises buyers about the Advent Road pocket — that Meadowland Village is 55-plus strata but the Meadowlands next door is a co-op you cannot buy the same way?
5. Tell one Central Meadows deal story — a Harris Road condo or an Advent Road rancher — that a buyer would not learn from the listing.
