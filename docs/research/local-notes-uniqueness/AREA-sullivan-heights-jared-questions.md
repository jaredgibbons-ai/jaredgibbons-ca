# Sullivan Heights — Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer, for a voice memo. Muse does not answer these.

1. Who buys the condos and townhomes in Sullivan Heights — Lougheed Town Centre workers, SFU-connected buyers, or people trading down from houses on the North Slope?
2. What moves fastest here: the older walk-ups near Cameron, or the townhome complexes along the Lougheed corridor?
3. What surprises buyers most — how much of the neighbourhood is about to change with the Cameron Community Centre and library replacement and the Lougheed Village rental project, or the strata fees in the 1970s stock?
4. How do buyers weigh Sullivan Heights against Brentwood or Metrotown when the SkyTrain ride is nearly the same?
5. Tell one story from a Sullivan Heights deal — a document surprise, a renovation reveal, or a buyer who only wanted this pocket.
