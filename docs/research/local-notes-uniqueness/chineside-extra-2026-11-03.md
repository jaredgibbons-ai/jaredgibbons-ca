## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Chineside in Port Coquitlam? **A:** No, Chineside is in Coquitlam. Market reporting carries Chineside only under the Coquitlam community, and City of Coquitlam sources place it in central Coquitlam around Austin Avenue and Como Lake Avenue. The distinction changes the tax bill, the utility bill and the council whose bylaws apply, even though the school district is School District No. 43 (SD43) on both sides of the city line. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Q:** Which schools will my children attend? **A:** The catchment must be checked by exact address in the SD43 School Locator, because no Chineside address-level result is verified yet. SD43 names Alderson Elementary among its Coquitlam schools and runs Como Lake Middle School at 1121 King Albert Avenue, but neither fact makes either school the catchment school for a given Chineside house — only the locator run for that address settles it. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** Can we apply to a French Immersion or Montessori school instead? **A:** Yes, but those are applications, not catchment rights. SD43 Programs of Choice — French Immersion, Mandarin Bilingual and Montessori — are entered through the district's program application process, and cross-catchment applications are decided by a lottery or random draw with in-district priority. A buyer should not price a Chineside house on a choice school place that has not been offered. http://sd43.bc.ca/cross-catchment
- **Q:** Is Chineside the same market as Harbour Chines? **A:** No, they are separate sub-areas with separate reporting rows, despite the shared name. Treat any figure quoted for "the Chines" with caution until it is clear which of the two sub-areas it covers; the City publishes no neighbourhood plan for either name, so the board boundary, not a City plan, is what separates them in practice. https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1838
