# Cloverdale Acreages — Extra Local Notes
Page: cloverdale-acreages-for-sale (Cloverdale, Surrey; acreages).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Cloverdale acreage sits on the lowland edges of the Cloverdale community in the City of Surrey: the Serpentine and Nicomekl River lowlands west and south of the town centre, along 176 Street (Highway 15), Colebrook Road and the 64 Avenue corridor. Cloverdale farm facts are in full in the cloverdale-farms note; this note is the acreage angle. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/cloverdale
- The City describes Cloverdale's borders as 160 Street to the west and 48 Avenue to the south, with Langley to the east; the acreage pockets lie inside the southern and western lowland part of that frame, not in Clayton's built subdivisions. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/cloverdale
- Serpentine is a Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area; the cloverdale-farms feed's visible farm listings were Serpentine listings on 176 Street. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-farms.html

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The farm zones are confirmed on a City of Surrey bylaw: the Development Cost Charge (DCC) Bylaw No. 21174 zone list names A-1 General Agriculture and A-2 Intensive Agriculture in Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. The A-1 subdivision minimums were not read on a City-hosted zoning text in this pass, so no minimum lot size is stated as fact here. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21174.pdf
- Acreage homes here are single dwellings on farm parcels in the lowlands, not lots in a named acreage subdivision; no new named acreage subdivision was verified, matching the earlier acreages program. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- Servicing is parcel by parcel in the lowlands: the City's lowlands system drains farm fields through ditches, flood boxes and pump stations, which is drainage servicing, not proof of municipal water or sewer at a lot line. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) rules govern first on ALR parcels: subdivision needs Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) approval, a principal residence is capped at 500 square metres (m²) without an ALC application with no more than 1,000 m² of fill, a suite is allowed only inside that residence, and one additional residence is capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 hectares (ha) or less or 186 m² over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Streamside setbacks in Surrey are in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and vary by stream class, so no single setback figure applies to a Cloverdale acreage crossed by a ditch or creek. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21174.pdf
- Surrey's sea dams on the Nicomekl and Serpentine Rivers, first built in 1912 and 1913, keep saline water out of farm irrigation water; a lowland parcel's drainage and irrigation both depend on that system, not only on its own ditch. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is a Cloverdale acreage on 176 Street in the ALR? **A:** Check the parcel itself, not the street. The ALR edge runs through the lowlands around 176 Street and 64 Avenue, and parcels on the same road can fall either side of it. Use the ALC maps search by parcel identifier (PID) or civic address, then confirm with the City zone, because ALR status controls subdivision, the second dwelling and whether the home can ever be stratified. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps

**Q:** Can I put a coach house on a Cloverdale ALR acreage? **A:** Do not count on Surrey's urban coach house rules here. On ALR land the ALC frame governs: a suite is permitted only inside the principal residence, and a detached second home is the ALC additional residence, capped at 90 m² on parcels up to 40 ha. Whether a Surrey coach house form can serve as that additional residence on one parcel is a parcel-level ALC and City check. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

**Q:** Why is a flat lowland acreage not simply cheap building land? **A:** Much of it is protected farm land inside Surrey's coastal floodplain, which covers about 20% of the city and includes over 60 square kilometres of agricultural land. Dykes, sea dams, ditches and pumps hold the water back, and a spillway cell can be a farm field itself, designed to hold flood water in an event before it drains back to the river. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain

**Q:** What income keeps farm class on a small Cloverdale acreage? **A:** The test scales with the operation's size: $10,000 of farm income under 0.8 ha, $2,500 between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, and $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that size. Apply by October 31 for the next tax year. A small acreage therefore faces the highest income hurdle per hectare, not the lowest. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Without the Serpentine and Nicomekl sea dams and dykes, parts of Cloverdale and Highways 10, 15 and 99 would flood twice daily at high tide, per the City's July 28, 2025 dyking district report. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R174.pdf
- The sea dams also store fresh water for agricultural irrigation, so the same gates that block salt water are part of a lowland farm's summer water supply. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- The lowlands were reclaimed for farming from the late 1800s by dyking and canals, and the Surrey Dyking District was formed in 1910 to build the two sea dams. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- Farm neighbours have a working landscape: dredging to maintain dykes and drainage, and flood spillway cells on agricultural fields, are normal operations in the Serpentine lowland, not future development signals. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain
- School catchments for a lowland address are address-based in School District No. 36 (Surrey); no catchment for a Cloverdale farm address was run in this program, so no school is assigned to a parcel here. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/cloverdale

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey's 2026 total residential (Class 1) tax rate is 3.42731 per $1,000 of assessed value, made up of a municipal total of 1.84251 plus school, TransLink, Metro Vancouver, BC Assessment and Municipal Finance Authority charges; the Class 9 Farm total on the same schedule is 12.57740 per $1,000, applied to farm-class assessed values. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- A Class 1, 8 or 9 parcel in Surrey also pays a $300 capital parcel tax collected with property taxes, and drainage parcel taxes are collected with property taxes. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- DCCs are imposed under Surrey Development Cost Charge Bylaw, 2024, No. 21174, payable at subdivision or building permit as that bylaw specifies; an acreage buyer planning a new farm building should price the A-1 or A-2 DCC line from that bylaw's schedule (check current). https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21174.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the City's coastal floodplain page and the parcel's flood construction level: the page describes the system that protects the lowlands, but it does not give a lot-level building elevation. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain
- Check ALR status on the ALC maps by PID or civic address before valuing a second dwelling or a split; the map does not state the City zone or the streamside setback for the parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- Check for a spillway cell or drainage statutory right of way on title: lowland flood works can sit on farm fields by design, and a title search shows what a listing does not. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain
