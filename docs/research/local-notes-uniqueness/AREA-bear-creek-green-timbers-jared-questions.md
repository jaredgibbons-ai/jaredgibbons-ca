# Bear Creek / Green Timbers — Questions for Jared
Area: Bear Creek / Green Timbers, Surrey (detached houses).

## 5 questions only Jared can answer
1. Who is actually buying the detached houses between Bear Creek Park and Green Timbers in your deals — families who want the park edge, buyers holding for the Fleetwood Plan and the future Green Timbers and 152 Street SkyTrain stations, or a mix — and has that mix changed since SkyTrain construction started on Fraser Highway?
2. What sells fast and what sits here: do houses backing onto Green Timbers Urban Forest or Bear Creek Park move first, while houses fronting Fraser Highway or 88 Avenue wait — and what is the one reason a listing in this pocket stalls most often?
3. What surprises buyers most once they are under contract or close to it — that so much of the board area is parkland and public land rather than houses, the Bear Creek streamside setback, or that Enver Creek's elementary family splits between the Fleetwood streets and Simon Cunningham on the west side?
4. In your deals, what does the Fleetwood Plan actually do to a house sale near 140 Street or 152 Street and Fraser Highway — do sellers price in the Transit-Oriented Area tiers, do buyers believe the late-2029 station date, and have you seen an assembly sale like the 148 Street lots?
5. Tell one Bear Creek / Green Timbers deal story: a sale, a near-miss, or a problem you solved on a park-edge or Fraser Highway street that shows a buyer or seller something no plan map or listing could.
