# East Central Maple Ridge Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: east-central-maple-ridge-houses-for-sale (East Central Maple Ridge; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- East Central has no City boundary — it is a Paragon (MLS) sub-area name only, and the City publishes no plan named "East Central"; the downtown part is covered by the Town Centre Area Plan. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- The Town Centre core in the plan was first described as bounded by Ontario Street (224 Street), Dewdney Trunk Road, Hinch Road (225 Street) and Lougheed Highway, later expanded north to 124 Avenue, west to 221 Street and east to Burnett Street. http://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- The wider detached-house streets run east and north of that core toward 232 Street and 124 Avenue; the City publishes no separate boundary for them, so a listing's "East Central" label is a board label, not a City line. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- City-wide Maple Ridge facts (Zoning Bylaw, small-scale multi-unit housing, suites, taxes, utilities, Speculation and Vacancy Tax): see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached houses here sit in two settings: older residential streets east and north of downtown, and the denser Town Centre edge where the 2025 plan directs higher-density, mixed-use growth around Haney Place Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station. http://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- The 2025 Town Centre update consolidated land-use designations from 11 to 7 and added a Town Centre Core designation, so a detached house near 224 Street can sit beside land the plan now treats as core, not low-density residential. http://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- Fletcher Park at 12260 Fletcher Street is the named neighbourhood park inside the residential part, listed by the City with open green space, benches, a playground and wheelchair access — a marker for the established-house streets north of downtown. https://www.mapleridge.ca/art-parks-rec/recreation-facilities/fletcher-park

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Town Centre Area Plan (Official Community Plan s. 10.4), updated November 25, 2025 by Bylaw 8060-2025, is the plan that governs the downtown edge of East Central, including the River Bend addition. http://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station are designated Transit-Oriented Areas under Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024, adopted June 25, 2024 — check a house near 223 Street or River Road against that bylaw, not just its zone. http://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- On Fraser River floodplain lots at the south edge, no habitable floor space may sit below the flood construction level, set at 6.7 metres geodetic (including freeboard) in the City's 2008 study. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on a property situated in the floodplain — a suite that is legal on a 124 Avenue lot may be prohibited on a River Road lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is my East Central house actually in the Town Centre plan? **A:** Only the downtown edge is. The Town Centre Area Plan, updated November 25, 2025, covers the core around 224 Street, Dewdney Trunk Road and Lougheed Highway, expanded to 124 Avenue, 221 Street and Burnett Street. Houses further east toward 232 Street are in the MLS sub-area but outside that plan area, so plan policies on density do not apply the same way. http://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves a house on Edge Street or 230 Street? **A:** Catchment is by exact address in School District 42, not by the East Central label. Schools physically in or beside this area include Eric Langton Elementary at 12138 Edge Street, Golden Ears Elementary at 23124 118 Avenue and Harry Hooge Elementary at 12280 230 Street. Confirm the address in the district locator before relying on any one school. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools
- **Q:** Can I add a suite to a house near Port Haney? **A:** Not if the lot is in the Fraser River floodplain. The City's floodplain document states Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation on a floodplain property, and habitable floors must sit at or above the 6.7-metre flood construction level. Check the flood map for the specific River Road or 223 Street address first. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q:** How close is the train, and how often does it run? **A:** Port Haney Station at 22300 River Road is the West Coast Express stop for this area. TransLink lists Port Haney on the weekday commuter line between Waterfront in downtown Vancouver and Mission City, with five trains each way on weekdays — westbound in the morning, eastbound in the afternoon and evening. Houses on River Road sit closest to that line. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Memorial Peace Park on 224 Street, between Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road, hosts the Haney Farmers Market and Remembrance Day services, and is ringed by City Hall, the Leisure Centre, the Greg Moore Youth Centre, The ACT Arts Centre and Haney Place Mall. https://www.mapleridge.ca/art-parks-rec/recreation-facilities/memorial-peace-park
- Maple Ridge Leisure Centre at 11925 Haney Place is the indoor pool, fitness centre and gymnasium for East Central houses — it is in the downtown core, not a separate neighbourhood facility. http://www.mapleridge.ca/1459
- The only Fraser Valley Regional Library branch in Maple Ridge is Maple Ridge Public Library at 130 – 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road, inside this area. http://fvrl.bc.ca/locations/maple-ridge-public-library
- Thomas Haney Secondary at 23000 116 Avenue and ci:tməxw Environmental Community (K–8) at 23125 116 Avenue share the east-side school site; Maple Ridge Secondary at 21911 122 Avenue sits at the west edge, so two East Central addresses can feed different secondaries. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/maple-ridge
- The City mails its "Be Flood Ready" guide to properties near the Fraser River floodplain and states it maintains dikes, pump stations and drainage systems and monitors river levels during freshet — relevant to River Road houses only, not the 124 Avenue streets. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-maple-ridge-advises-residents-prepare-freshet-season

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Haney Place Transit Exchange is in this area, and TransLink names these routes from Haney Place: R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus, 701 to Coquitlam Central Station / Maple Ridge East, 733 Rock Ridge, 741 Anderson Creek, 745 Cottonwood and 746 Albion. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/08/more-bus-service-coming-to-21-routes-for-better-commutes-this-fall/
- West Coast Express at Port Haney Station, 22300 River Road, runs weekdays between Waterfront and Mission City with five trains each way. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Planned Langley–Haney Place Bus Rapid Transit is a 22-kilometre, 13-station line between Willowbrook in Langley and Haney Place, with peak service every 10 minutes as planned, connecting at Haney Place to the R3; TransLink lists it in detailed planning with roadway designs, costs and timelines not yet determined. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/bus-rapid-transit

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check any River Road or Port Haney lot on the City's Fraser River floodplain mapping: the flood construction level here is 6.7 metres geodetic and suites are prohibited on floodplain lots under Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h). https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- Fraser River Escarpment lands along the river carry hazards the City lists as flooding, mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence, and development there requires a Section 219 covenant and a professional engineer's report. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- The flood and escarpment documents are area-level only — neither tells a buyer the status of one lot; that requires a per-address check with the City. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
