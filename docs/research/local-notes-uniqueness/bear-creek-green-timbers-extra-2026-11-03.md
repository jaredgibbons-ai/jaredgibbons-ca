- This neighbourhood is named for two parks: Bear Creek Park at 13750 88 Avenue and Green Timbers Urban Forest Park in the 14600 block of 100 Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/green-timbers-urban-forest-park?sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwjn5JOy9b6IAxVOS6sCHRlmDLoQyK4DegQIBBAt

- Housing here wraps around protected parkland, rather than sitting inside a single plan area. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/green-timbers-urban-forest-park?sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwjn5JOy9b6IAxVOS6sCHRlmDLoQyK4DegQIBBAt

- Green Timbers itself is second-growth forest: the land was completely logged by 1929, replanted as British Columbia's first reforestation project, and became a park in 1974. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/green-timbers-urban-forest-park?sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwjn5JOy9b6IAxVOS6sCHRlmDLoQyK4DegQIBBAt

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is Green Timbers Lake a swimming lake? **A:** No, the City allows year-round shore fishing only, and states no watercraft, wading or swimming is permitted, with dogs kept on leash and out of the water. The lake sits inside Green Timbers Urban Forest Park, which also holds over 10 kilometres of nature trails, picnic areas and the Surrey Nature Centre. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/green-timbers-urban-forest-park?sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwjn5JOy9b6IAxVOS6sCHRlmDLoQyK4DegQIBBAt
**Q:** Will there be a SkyTrain station here? **A:** Yes, Green Timbers Station is planned at 140 Street and Fraser Highway, on the westernmost edge of Green Timbers Urban Forest. The project states the station will serve the Jim Pattison Outpatient Care and Surgery Centre, the British Columbia Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP) E-Division headquarters and Surrey Memorial Hospital, with 152 Street and Fleetwood stations next east. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/
**Q:** Which schools are near the parks? **A:** Bear Creek Elementary at 13780 80 Avenue and Green Timbers Elementary at 8824 144 Street are the park-named elementary schools, and Enver Creek Secondary is at 14505 84 Avenue. Those are locations from the British Columbia Ministry school list for Surrey School District 36 (SD36), not catchment assignments; SD36 assigns schools by exact address, so run the address in the district School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
**Q:** What work is happening in Bear Creek Park? **A:** The City's park projects list shows an electrical service upgrade and public-address system installation at Bear Creek Park, 13750 88 Avenue, completed in October 2026, after splash-park equipment and surfacing upgrades in May 2026. Those dates are the City's project-list entries, read on the date below, not a promise of further work. http://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/park-projects

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- A glacial erratic, a large rock carried by glacial ice, sits on the trails in the northwestern part of Green Timbers park. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/green-timbers-urban-forest-park?sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwjn5JOy9b6IAxVOS6sCHRlmDLoQyK4DegQIBBAt
- The Surrey Nature Centre sits inside Green Timbers, beside an arboretum of over 75 species and the 1930 Inaugural Plantation. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/trees-yard-garden/discover-surreys-trees
