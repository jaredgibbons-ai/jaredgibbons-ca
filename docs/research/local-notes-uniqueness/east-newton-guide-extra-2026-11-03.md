# East Newton Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: east-newton-guide (East Newton, Surrey; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- East Newton is the Chimney Hill area east of 144 Street, north of 64 Avenue and Hyland Creek, and south of Bear Creek. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- The guide area splits into three plans: East Newton North, East Newton South and East Newton Business Park. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- The Business Park sits east of 152 Street and north of 64 Avenue; the two residential plans sit east of 144 Street, split by 72 Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is there an East Newton Elementary? **A:** No, there is no school by that name. The British Columbia (BC) Ministry school list for Surrey School District 36 (SD36) names Chimney Hill Elementary at 14755 74 Avenue and McLeod Road Traditional at 6325 142 Street, and no East Newton Elementary appears. Check any address in the district School Locator before relying on a catchment. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
**Q:** Which secondary school serves East Newton? **A:** Tamanawis Secondary at 12600 66 Avenue is the area secondary named in district planning, and the district lists funding for a Tamanawis addition creating classrooms with Fleetwood Park, Kwantlen Park and Guildford Park. Catchment still depends on the exact address, so confirm with the SD36 School Locator rather than assuming the nearest school is the assigned school. https://www.surreyschools.ca/_ci/p/176056
**Q:** Is East Newton still growing under its old plans? **A:** No, the City describes the three East Newton plans as now largely built out. Their land-use designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update, so a buyer checking redevelopment potential should read the current OCP designation for the lot, not the rescinded North, South or Business Park NCP maps. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
**Q:** What separates East Newton from West Newton? **A:** The dividing line in City planning is 144 Street. East Newton is defined as east of 144 Street around Chimney Hill, between Bear Creek to the north and Hyland Creek and 64 Avenue to the south. West Newton lies on the other side of 144 Street toward King George Boulevard, under different Newton land-use plans. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Chimney Hill is the local name; East Newton is the City planning name for the same ground. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- McLeod Road Traditional is a Program of Choice, not a regular catchment school; the district consulted in 2024 on converting its three traditional schools to catchment schools. https://www.surreyschools.ca/surreytraditional/_ci/p/178598
- Bear Creek forms the north edge and Hyland Creek the south edge, so creek setbacks can affect lots on those edges. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- SD36 is short more than 6,500 student seats district-wide, so catchment and closed-school lists change; the district publishes closed out-of-catchment schools each year. https://www.surreyschools.ca/surreytraditional/_ci/p/178598
- The Business Park plan was amended in 2020 to reinforce employment land and remove live/work expectations. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans
Read: 2026-11-03

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Guide-level item only: the Newton Town Centre Plan, adopted July 13, 2020, covers 76 Avenue to 68 Avenue, King George Boulevard to 138 Street, west of this guide area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/newton-town-centre-plan
- No ongoing land-use plan is listed by the City for Newton at the time of reading. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans
- The City is widening 72 Avenue from 144 Street to 152 Street, anticipated complete in 2027, on the East Newton north-south route. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/capital-projects/72-avenue-improvements-extensions?check_logged_in=1

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey's 2026 municipal total residential rate is $1.84251 per $1,000 of assessed value, before other authorities. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- Surrey's 2025 flat rate for a single-family home was water $1,139 plus sewer $1,638, total $2,777; the 2026 flat total shown is $2,856. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill
- The City of Surrey is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- 72 Avenue is the main east-west route through East Newton, and the City plans it as a continuous route from 120 Street to 196 Street. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/capital-projects/72-avenue-improvements-extensions?check_logged_in=1
- There is no SkyTrain station in East Newton; the Surrey-Langley extension runs on Fraser Highway north of here — see the fleetwood-tynehead-guide note. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/04/SLS_Monthly-Status-Report_March_2026.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check lots near Bear Creek and Hyland Creek against City riparian and drainage requirements; both creeks bound the plan area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- Do not rely on the rescinded NCP maps for permitted use; the City states the OCP now leads development in all three plan areas. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
