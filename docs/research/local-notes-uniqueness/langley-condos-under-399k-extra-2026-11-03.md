## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The under-$399K band is mostly older low-rise strata stock in the City of Langley plus newer low- and mid-rise product in the Township, and the age gap matters because of one provincial deadline: strata corporations in Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley that had no depreciation report, or a report received before December 31, 2020, had to obtain a current one by July 1, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Every strata corporation in British Columbia, of any size, must carry property and liability insurance for the strata corporation, so an older building in this band should have a current policy and a stated deductible position a buyer can read before subject removal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances-and-insurance/strata-corporation-insurance
- Township of Langley condos are billed utilities at the Township's multi-family flat rate, while City of Langley condos sit on metered water — the same band therefore carries two different utility structures depending on which side of the municipal boundary the building stands. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- No named building in this band was confirmed on a strata corporation or property manager's own page in this pass, so no building name, year built or strata plan number is stated here — see NOT CONFIRMED.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A depreciation report is now mandatory on a five-year cycle for every British Columbia strata corporation with five or more strata lots, and stratas can no longer defer it by an annual three-quarter vote — in this band that rule lands hardest on the older City of Langley low-rise buildings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Rental, pet and age rules for a Langley condo come from that building's own registered strata bylaws and the seller's Form B information certificate, not from either Langley municipality; the Province's strata housing pages are the official starting point for what those documents must disclose. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- In the City of Langley, land designated Mid Rise Residential is subject to a development permit for building form and character, which is the rule shaping the new apartment buildings joining this stock — the City's Bylaw No. 3303 (2025) rezoned 20239–20249 54A Avenue from RM1 Multiple Residential Low Density to CD105 Comprehensive Development on that basis. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3303pdf
- The seller-side rules for Langley — permits, closing adjustments and the Agricultural Land Reserve — are treated in full in the sell-my-house-in-langley note; this note covers the buyer and strata side only.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Why does the July 1, 2026 depreciation report deadline matter for a cheap Langley condo? **A:** Because the deadline applied exactly to this band's older buildings: strata corporations in the Fraser Valley with no report, or one received before December 31, 2020, had to obtain a current report by July 1, 2026. A fresh report shows the building's projected repair and replacement costs over 30 years and how the contingency reserve fund (CRF) is funded, which is where special levies in older low-rise buildings come from. Ask for the report and the CRF balance before subject removal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q:** Is a condo in the City of Langley billed for utilities the same way as one in the Township? **A:** No — the two municipalities bill differently. City of Langley properties are on water meters: the 2026 water rate is $2.06 per cubic metre plus a $75 fee, and sewer is charged on 80 percent of consumption at $2.41 per cubic metre plus $75. Township of Langley condos instead pay a flat annual multi-family rate — $667.22 for water and $609.00 for sanitary sewer in 2026 — billed on a separate utility statement. https://www.langleycity.ca/PropertyTaxes/utilities https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Willoughby Heights condo, and is that changing? **A:** Catchments in School District 35 (Langley) are set by exact address through the district's school locator, and Willoughby Slope catchments change in September 2027 when two new schools open — stɑtl̓əw̓ Middle and Emery Dosdall Secondary. Under the new map, Donna Gabriel Robins Elementary (East) feeds stɑtl̓əw̓ Middle then Emery Dosdall Secondary, while Donna Gabriel Robins (West) feeds Peter Ewart Middle then R.E. Mountain Secondary, so two units on the same street can split. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/willoughby-slope-catchment-boundary-changes.21343
- **Q:** Can I rent out a condo I buy in this band? **A:** The answer sits in the building's own documents, not in a municipal rule. Rental restrictions, pet limits and any age bylaw are set by the strata corporation's registered bylaws, and the seller's Form B information certificate is the document that discloses the bylaws and rules that apply to the specific strata lot. No strata page read in this pass stated a rental rule for a named Langley building, so read the Form B and bylaws for the exact building before writing an offer that depends on renting the unit out. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Q:** Will the SkyTrain change anything for Langley City condos in this band? **A:** Yes, on the Province's current timeline: TransLink states the Surrey-Langley SkyTrain is a 16-kilometre Expo Line extension along Fraser Highway from King George Station to 203 Street in Langley City, with an anticipated in-service date of 2029. City of Langley buildings near the 203 Street terminus area are the part of this band closest to the line; Township buildings in Willoughby and Aldergrove are not on the route. Read the project page for the current status rather than a sales centre map. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/surrey-langley-skytrain

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Township of Langley utility fees no longer ride on the property tax statement: the Township has mailed a separate flat-rate utility statement since 2025, due July 2, 2026, and a 10 percent penalty applies to unpaid utility balances after the due date. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/separate-utility-statements-mailed-to-property-owners.aspx
- City of Langley residential water meters are read once a year in March and the charge is billed on the June tax notice, so a City condo's water cost arrives with taxes, not as a monthly bill. https://www.langleycity.ca/PropertyTaxes/utilities
- H.D. Stafford Middle School in the City of Langley takes students from six named elementary catchments — Blacklock Fine Arts, Douglas Park, James Hill, Nicomekl, Simonds and Uplands — and its Grade 8s transition to Langley Secondary School, which is the school path behind most City of Langley condo addresses. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/hdsms/welcome-to-h-d--stafford-middle-school.18702
- Langley Memorial Hospital, the Fraser Health community hospital for both Langleys, is at 22051 Fraser Highway — inside the City of Langley condo corridor, not out in the Township. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital
- A buyer can subscribe to the Township's Development Activity Portal to get email notices of new applications near a specific building, because applications become public within about three weeks of acceptance. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-activity.aspx
- The City of Langley's 2026 single-family solid waste charge is $432, but multi-family buildings are billed under the City's metered utility structure instead — do not carry a house utility figure into a condo budget. https://www.langleycity.ca/PropertyTaxes/utilities

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The condo-specific Township of Langley charge is the multi-family utility flat rate: $667.22 for water plus $609.00 for sanitary sewer per year in 2026, $1,276.22 in total, with a garbage base rate of $226.00 where Township collection applies. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- The condo-specific City of Langley charge is metered: water at $2.06 per cubic metre plus a $75 fee, and sewer on 80 percent of water consumption at $2.41 per cubic metre plus a $75 fee, for 2026. https://www.langleycity.ca/PropertyTaxes/utilities

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the depreciation report: for any building in this band, ask whether the strata met the July 1, 2026 Fraser Valley deadline and read the report's funding models, because an underfunded contingency reserve in an older low-rise building is where a special levy comes from after you buy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Read the strata's insurance position on the Province's strata insurance page and in the Form B: the strata corporation must insure the common property at full replacement value, and the deductible an owner can be charged back after a water loss is a building-level figure no municipal page states. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances-and-insurance/strata-corporation-insurance
- Confirm the school catchment for the exact unit address in School District 35's locator, and re-check it against the September 2027 Willoughby Slope boundary changes before relying on a school name in a listing. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/catchment-maps
- Strata disputes in British Columbia can be taken to the Civil Resolution Tribunal, which is the official route if a strata records or bylaw dispute follows a purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
