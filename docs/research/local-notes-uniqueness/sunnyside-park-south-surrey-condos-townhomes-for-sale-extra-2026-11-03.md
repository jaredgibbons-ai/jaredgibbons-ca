# Sunnyside Park Condos and Townhomes — Extra Local Notes
Page: sunnyside-park-south-surrey-condos-townhomes-for-sale (Sunnyside Park, South Surrey; condos/townhomes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sunnyside Park is the board name for the Sunnyside pocket of South Surrey between 16 Avenue and 24 Avenue, and the City itself just calls it Sunnyside — the hall, park and forest all carry the shorter name. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bakerview-park
- Do not confuse it with Sunnyside Heights, a separate Grandview Heights neighbourhood plan area near 168 Street, 24 Avenue and Highway 99 — a townhome in one is not in the other, and the plan rules differ. https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp
- The landmarks that fix the area are Bakerview Park at 1845 154 Street (home to Sunnyside Community Hall), Sunnyside Acres Urban Forest Park at 2598 144 Street, and the South Surrey Recreation and Arts Centre at 14601 20 Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bakerview-park
- The Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan area, 16 Avenue to 24 Avenue from Martin Drive to Earl Marriott Secondary School, borders this sub-area on the east — the town centre's density is planned there, not inside Sunnyside's residential streets. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/semiahmoo-town-centre-plan
- Shared Sunnyside Park area facts are held in full in the sunnyside-park-south-surrey-houses-for-sale note — see that note; this file carries only the condo and townhome specifics.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The attached stock is real here, unlike most of South Surrey: the September 2026 SnapStats split for the sub-area recorded 28 condos and townhomes listed against 46 detached (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, cited in the area research file).
- Named complexes on the 24 Avenue and 22 Avenue corridors include Meridian Pointe at 14968 24 Avenue (townhomes, strata plan LMS4558, built 2003), Meridian Park Estates at 14888 24 Avenue (built 1995) and Semiahmoo Trail at 15128 24 Avenue (built 2014) — years and plan numbers are from the building directory and are flagged in NOT CONFIRMED. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/buildings/surrey/sunnyside-park-surrey?sortby=status_sync
- The 22 Avenue cluster holds Villa Pacific at 15155 22 Avenue (built 1994), Edencrest at 15168 22 Avenue (built 1999) and Muirfield Gardens at 15188 22 Avenue (built 2003) — a 1990s–2000s stratum of ground-oriented strata housing south of 24 Avenue. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/buildings/surrey/sunnyside-park-surrey?sortby=status_sync

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Surrey's small-scale multi-unit zones (RA through R6 under Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000) set what a house lot beside these complexes can become: up to three homes on lots under 280 square metres, up to four on larger lots, and up to six on eligible lots within 400 metres of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- The Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan was updated to align with King George Boulevard Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) and the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) designations — lots at Sunnyside's east edge near Martin Drive take their future from that plan, not from the residential streets' zoning alone. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/semiahmoo-town-centre-plan
- For a strata purchase here, the Province's buyer paperwork path is the check: Form B Information Certificate, Form F, the strata's registered bylaws and rules, fees and any special levies, confirmed against filings at the Land Title Office. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers
- Long-term rental restriction bylaws in these 1990s complexes are unenforceable since November 24, 2022, but short-term rental and pet bylaws can still be enforced — read the registered bylaws for Meridian Pointe or Villa Pacific, not a directory summary. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a Sunnyside Park townhome the same area as Sunnyside Heights? **A:** No. Sunnyside Heights is a Grandview Heights plan area near 168 Street and Highway 99, while Sunnyside Park's facilities — Bakerview Park and Sunnyside Community Hall at 1845 154 Street — sit west of 152 Street between 16 and 24 Avenues. The two names pull different listings, different schools and different plan rules, so confirm which one a listing actually means before comparing prices. https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp
- **Q:** Which schools serve a townhome at 148 Street and 20 Avenue? **A:** The district locator result on record for the sample address 1785 148 Street gives H. T. Thrift Elementary and Semiahmoo Secondary, which itself stands at 1785 148 Street. Catchments are address-based in School District 36, and the boundary does not follow the board sub-area line, so run the exact complex address — a unit at 15550 26 Avenue can feed a different elementary school. https://www.surreyschools.ca/semiahmoo/_ci/p/174781
- **Q:** What recreation is actually inside the sub-area? **A:** More than most South Surrey pockets. Bakerview Park holds Sunnyside Community Hall, a garden, playground, baseball diamonds and a soccer field; Sunnyside Acres Urban Forest Park at 2598 144 Street is about 140 hectares of second-growth forest with roughly four kilometres of trails, including the Wally Ross universal access trail; and the South Surrey Recreation and Arts Centre is at 14601 20 Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/sunnyside-acres-urban-forest-park
- **Q:** Where is the nearest hospital for a Sunnyside condo buyer? **A:** Peace Arch Hospital at 15521 Russell Avenue in White Rock, which Fraser Health lists as a community hospital with emergency, surgery and maternity care. There is no hospital inside Sunnyside itself; the recreation centre and indoor pool at 14601 20 Avenue and 14655 17 Avenue are the area's public facilities instead. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/white-rock/peace-arch-hospital

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Semiahmoo Trail runs through this part of South Surrey and crosses 148 Street — City planning material also records a King George Highway pedestrian overpass built to connect into Sunnyside, so trail access is a street-by-street fact, not an area-wide one. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/KingGeorgeCorridorStudyDecember1995.pdf
- Semiahmoo Library at 1815 152 Street is the nearest Surrey Libraries branch — it sits on the 152 Street corridor east of the sub-area's complexes, not inside them. https://www.surreylibraries.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/SurreyLibrariesAnnualReport2025.pdf
- Semiahmoo Shopping Centre at 1711 152 Street is the named retail centre beside this sub-area (per Fraser Health) — no grocery store inside the sub-area itself was verified on an official source. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2026/Apr/Improving-access-to-team-based-care-for-people-in-Surrey

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 321 is the through bus: TransLink's timetable runs it from Surrey Central Station to White Rock Centre and White Rock South via King George Boulevard and 152 Street — the corridor at Sunnyside's east edge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt321.pdf
- King George Boulevard Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) is planned, not built: the Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan was updated to align with it, so a 152 Street or King George corridor condo bought for BRT is buying a plan alignment. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/semiahmoo-town-centre-plan
- The streets that serve the complexes are 148 Street, 152 Street, 20 Avenue, 24 Avenue and King George Boulevard. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/KingGeorgeCorridorStudyDecember1995.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check flood and Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status lot by lot: the research file confirms neither for any Sunnyside parcel, and the Agricultural Land Commission map is where a specific lot is looked up — the area's south and west edges approach farmland. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/permitted-uses-in-the-alr/living-in-the-alr-information/
- For the 1990s strata stock (Sunnyside Gate 1992, Villa Pacific 1994, Meridian Park Estates 1995), the depreciation report is the key diligence document — provincial law requires stratas of five or more lots to obtain one every five years, and it is where roof and envelope levies first appear. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Confirm the zone and hazard layers for the exact address in the City of Surrey Mapping Online System (COSMOS) before relying on a complex's redevelopment value — SSMUH tiers turn on lot size and the 400-metre frequent-bus-stop test. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
