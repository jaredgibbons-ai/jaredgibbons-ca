## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** What does a Maclure Road depreciation report show?
**A:** It shows the strata's projected common-property maintenance, repair and replacement costs over 30 years. A strata with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, with no deferral vote, and the newest report attaches to the Form B certificate. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

**Q:** Which bus serves George Ferguson Way?
**A:** Route 1, signed Highstreet/University of the Fraser Valley (UFV), links Highstreet Exchange, Bourquin Exchange and UFV past Mill Lake and Ware Street, on the BC Transit map. Streets south of Highway 1 use other routes, so check the map stop by stop. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2025/12/cfv_map_1.pdf

**Q:** Is a central townhome flood-exposed?
**A:** Sumas Prairie, the City's named flood area, sits in east Abbotsford, not the central corridors. Its flood page describes a 90 square-kilometre low-lying area flooded by Nooksack River overflow, and names three floodplains: Matsqui Prairie, Sumas Prairie and Glen Valley. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse

**Q:** Which plan covers Historic Downtown townhomes?
**A:** The Historic Downtown Neighbourhood Plan, Bylaw 2920-2019, on the City's neighbourhood plans page, beside the City Centre Neighbourhood Plan, Bylaw 2913-2019. Under the Abbotsforward 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP), those plans direct ground-oriented multi-family housing, so the plan area matters. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
Read: 2026-11-03
