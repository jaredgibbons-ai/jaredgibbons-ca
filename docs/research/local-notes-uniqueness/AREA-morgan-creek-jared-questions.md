# Morgan Creek — Questions for Jared

1. In Morgan Creek deals, what do you see differ between the houses fronting the golf course on Morgan Creek Way and the Rosemary Heights houses off the course around 154 Street and 32 Avenue — do fairway addresses negotiate differently?
2. Who buys in Morgan Creek: golfers, downsizers moving from larger South Surrey houses, or families targeting Morgan Elementary, Rosemary Heights Elementary and Southridge — and what do they say tipped it?
3. What sells fast here and what sits: do fairway-backing houses move first, while interior houses without a view wait — and how do the original 1990s builds compete once they are renovated?
4. What surprises Morgan Creek buyers: that the golf course is public and living here is not tied to the course, the creek and greenbelt setbacks in Rosemary Heights, or the drive to Morgan Crossing and Grandview Corners for daily shopping?
5. Tell one Morgan Creek story: a deal on Morgan Creek Way where the fairway frontage, a view, or something about the course changed the price, the subjects, or the outcome.
