# Simon Fraser University Area Condos and Townhomes — Extra Local Notes
Page: simon-fraser-university-area-burnaby-condos-townhomes-for-sale (Simon Fraser University Area, Burnaby; condos and townhomes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- UniverCity is the condo and townhome community on top of Burnaby Mountain, beside the Simon Fraser University (SFU) campus inside its Ring Road, in the City of Burnaby. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/
- The community covers about 65 hectares around the campus, planned as neighbourhoods south and east of SFU, reached by University Drive and Gaglardi Way from below the mountain. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/
- Shared SFU and Simon Fraser Hills facts are carried in full in the simon-fraser-hills-burnaby-condos-townhomes-for-sale note; this note covers only the UniverCity condo and townhome angle. City of Burnaby–wide facts are in the vancouver-heights-burnaby-houses-for-sale note.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Every UniverCity home sits on SFU-owned land: buyers own the strata unit, and the land is held under a prepaid 99-year leasehold, not freehold. https://univercity.ca/planning-development/for-developers/
- Named buildings verified in the local index include Altitude, 9060 and 9080 University Crescent; Origin, 9150 University High Street; The Nest by Mosaic, 9250 University High Street; The Hub by Liberty Homes, 9009 Cornerstone Mews; and Centre Block, 9393 Tower Road — addresses as indexed, to be confirmed on title for any specific unit. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/
- The Trust states it has developed homes for more than 5,000 UniverCity residents with Lower Mainland developers, in low-rise and tower forms around University High Street and University Crescent. https://univercity.ca/planning-development/for-developers/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Tenure is the first rule here: SFU retains ownership of the land, and serviced, subdivided parcels go to developers through 99-year lease agreements, so a purchase is a leasehold strata interest. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/
- Development form is set by the Simon Fraser University Official Community Plan, adopted September 9, 1996 and amended April 15, 2002, which forms part of Burnaby's Official Community Plan (OCP) and is the basis for rezoning land within the Ring Road for leasehold subdivision for market housing. https://univercity.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/Simon_Fraser_University_Official_Community_Plan.pdf
- The plan allows up to 4,536 residential units, so remaining density is a plan figure, not a per-building promise; check the specific strata plan and lease for the building. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a UniverCity condo freehold? **A:** No — the land stays in SFU ownership. The SFU Community Trust prepares zoned, serviced, subdivided sites for private developers on a prepaid, 99-year leasehold basis, and home buyers hold strata title in the building on that leasehold land. A buyer should have a lawyer review the head lease, the remaining lease term and any lease terms that run with the strata before removing subjects. https://univercity.ca/planning-development/for-developers/
- **Q:** Which elementary school is on the mountain? **A:** University Highlands Elementary, at 9388 Tower Road, is the on-mountain school. It is a Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design (LEED) Gold school inside UniverCity, designed so surrounding residences are within about a five-minute walk, with limited parking and a two-minute parking zone on University High Street. Catchment for a specific address is still a Burnaby School District check. https://universityhighlands.burnabyschools.ca/frequently-asked-questions/
- **Q:** How do residents get off the mountain without a car? **A:** Buses from SFU Exchange are the current link. TransLink lists the R5 Hastings Street RapidBus to Burrard Station, Route 143 to Burquitlam Station, Route 144 to Metrotown Station and Route 145 to Production Way–University Station from the exchange. There is no SkyTrain station on the mountain; the rail connection is at the bottom of the bus ride. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
- **Q:** What heats these buildings? **A:** District energy, in connected buildings. The Burnaby Mountain District Energy Utility's biomass Renewable Energy Centre, completed in 2020, provides heat and hot water to most of the SFU campus and about half of UniverCity, using locally sourced wood waste with natural gas boilers for peaking and backup. Whether a specific building is connected is a strata-documents check. https://www.corix.com/systems/burnaby-mountain/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Heat and hot water billing in connected UniverCity buildings runs through the Corix district energy system, not individual gas or electric baseboards — confirm the utility charge in the strata budget. https://www.corix.com/systems/burnaby-mountain/
- The on-mountain elementary school leases its building from SFU Community Trust: University Highlands was converted from an existing office building and is leased to the Burnaby School District by the Trust. https://liveatsimonfraser.ca/simon-fraser-schools.html
- Burnaby Mountain Conservation Area surrounds the community: the City lists 34 multi-use trails over about 28 kilometres across 578 hectares, with the summit at 366 metres. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/walking-hiking-and-biking/burnaby-mountain-conservation-are
- Winter road access is a mountain issue: both road routes down, University Drive via Gaglardi Way and the Hastings route, are exposed hill roads, and the proposed gondola is not built — see Section 6.

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby-wide tax and utility figures are carried in full in the vancouver-heights-burnaby-houses-for-sale note; this note does not repeat them.
- Burnaby is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so a residential owner in UniverCity must complete the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- The UniverCity-specific cost is district energy: connected buildings buy heat and hot water from the Corix Burnaby Mountain District Energy Utility rather than a municipal utility. https://www.corix.com/systems/burnaby-mountain/

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- SFU Exchange is the on-mountain transit hub named on TransLink's R5 timetable, and Production Way–University Station on the Millennium Line is the rail station the mountain buses feed. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/ttR5.pdf
- Route 145 runs Production Way Station to SFU Exchange, and Route 143 runs SFU Exchange to Burquitlam Station, per TransLink timetables. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt145.pdf
- The R5 Hastings Street RapidBus runs Burrard Station to SFU, at least every 10 minutes at peak and 15 minutes off-peak, per TransLink. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the lease first: the due-diligence document for any UniverCity unit is the 99-year ground lease and the strata's leasehold terms, because SFU retains the land — a freehold title search alone does not describe this tenure. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/
- Check slope and watercourse conditions against the SFU Official Community Plan, which requires subdivision proposals to include a topographic survey showing watercourses and significant trees and preliminary watercourse protection proposals. https://univercity.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/Simon_Fraser_University_Official_Community_Plan.pdf
- Check wildfire and forest interface exposure on the ground: the community is surrounded by the Burnaby Mountain Conservation Area forest, and no UniverCity-specific hazard map was read for this note — that gap is listed under NOT CONFIRMED.
