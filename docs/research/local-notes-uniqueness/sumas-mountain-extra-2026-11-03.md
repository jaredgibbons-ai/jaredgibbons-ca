# Sumas Mountain Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: sumas-mountain (Sumas Mountain, Abbotsford; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sumas Mountain is split between two governments: the built west and south slopes are in the City of Abbotsford, and the upper and eastern rural part is unincorporated Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) land toward Chilliwack. http://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/sumas-mountain.html
- The streets buyers actually use are Sumas Mountain Road, Straiton Road, Dawson Road, Batt Road, Ward Road, Ledgeview Drive and Whatcom Road at Highway 1 Exit 95. https://jaredgibbons.ca/sumas-mountain.html
- Inside the City slopes sit Auguston, McKee and the Eagle Mountain side, with Clayburn Village at the northwest foot and Sumas Prairie below to the south. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Two housing worlds share this page: freehold detached houses in Auguston, McKee and Eagle Mountain, and rural homes on large lots on the upper FVRD roads. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- City minimum lots frame the slope stock: 0.4 hectares in Suburban Residential (SR), 2.0 hectares in Country Residential (CR) and 4.0 hectares in Rural Residential (RR) under Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- One named strata exists on the mountain: the Straiton strata at 4595 Sumas Mountain Road (Strata Plan EPS1299) is 26 single-family strata lots south of Dawson Road, not a townhome or condo building. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/45965/
- Ground-oriented homes are planned, not yet built, under the McKee plan's Urban 2 – Ground Oriented designation. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Check which government governs the lot first, because City zoning and the FVRD plan are different rule sets. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- On the City side the McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023), Schedule E of the Official Community Plan, guides the west slope, inside Abbotsforward 2050, Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2600-2016. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- On the rural side the FVRD Sumas Mountain Official Community Plan, Consolidated Bylaw No. 584, applies, and homes there use wells and onsite septic because there is no public water or sewer at rural density. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- Slope land carries a Steep Slope Development Permit Area in the City Official Community Plan, and the McKee plan protects unstable areas and slopes over 40 percent as open space. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
Read: 2026-11-03

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there one school catchment for the mountain? **A:** No — the mountain splits in two. A School District 34 (Abbotsford) locator run for 36367 Stephen Leacock Drive gave Auguston Traditional Elementary, Clayburn Middle and Robert Bateman Secondary, while 2540 Eagle Mountain Drive gave Irene Kelleher Totí:ltawtxw Elementary, William A. Fraser Middle and Yale Secondary. Run the exact address before relying on any school. https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments
- **Q:** Which elementary schools sit on the slopes? **A:** Four do. Auguston Traditional Elementary at 36367 Stephen Leacock Drive, Mountain Elementary at 2299 Mountain Drive, Irene Kelleher Totí:ltawtxw Elementary at 2540 Eagle Mountain Drive and Sandy Hill Elementary at 3836 Old Clayburn Road are on the slopes, with Clayburn Middle at 35139 Laburnum Avenue at the northwest foot. https://mountain.abbyschools.ca/schoolplan/
- **Q:** Is the school still called Matsqui Elementary? **A:** No — that name is gone. The Abbotsford Board of Education approved renaming Matsqui Elementary to Mathxwí Elementary in December 2021, with the renaming ceremony in September 2022, to reflect the Halq'eméylem language of the Stó:lō First Nations; the school is at 33661 Elizabeth Avenue. https://indigenous.abbyschools.ca/blog/renaming-and-intertwining-history
- **Q:** Can I build freely on a steep lot? **A:** No — expect a geotechnical step. The City applies Steep Slope Development Permit guidelines on slope land, and the McKee plan puts unstable geotechnical areas and slopes over 40 percent into protected open space, so part of a steep lot may be unbuildable. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- No secondary school sits on the mountain itself; secondary students travel to Robert Bateman Secondary at 35045 Exbury Avenue or Yale Secondary at 34620 Old Yale Road at the foot. https://clayburn.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/codeofconduct/clayburn
- Five quarries operate on the mountain and gravel trucks travel to and from them on an access road through the Lightning Rock site, per Semá:th (Sumas) First Nation. https://sumasfirstnation.com/press-releases/sacred-land-returned-to-semath/
- Sumas Mountain Regional Park at 39100 Sumas Mountain Forest Service Road is open 7 a.m. to sunset, and its Chadsey Lake Trail runs from the upper parking lot to Chadsey Lake. http://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/sumas-mountain.html
- City parks on the slopes include Lower Sumas Mountain Park at 2850 Whatcom Road and 36151 Walter Road, McKinley Park at 35575 McKee Road and Eagle Mountain Park at 2570 Eagle Mountain Way. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds
- No recreation centre or library branch is on the mountain; the nearest City recreation centre is Abbotsford Recreation Centre at 2499 McMillan Road at the west foot. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-recreation-culture/facilities-hours
Read: 2026-11-03

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City-side taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5 percent penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5 percent penalty on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- Abbotsford Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026 sets the Class 1 residential general municipal (police) rate at 1.08284 per $1,000 of taxable value for 2026. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf
- Rural-side owners pay the Province, not the City: a property outside a municipality receives its notice from the Surveyor of Taxes Office, with rural taxes due July 2 and 5 percent penalties on July 2 and October 31 under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies in the City of Abbotsford, which the Province lists as a designated taxable area, so City-side residential owners must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Bus service is foot-of-mountain only: BC Transit Central Fraser Valley route 7 (Sumas Mountain) runs downtown Abbotsford to Sumas Mountain, route 9 (McKee) runs to McKee, and route 15 (Auguston) serves Auguston. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=7
- Highway 1 access is at the Whatcom Road interchange at the south foot of the mountain. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/new-park-dianne-brook-ave
- No bus route to the upper mountain or the regional park trailheads was confirmed on an operator page read in this pass. http://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/sumas-mountain.html

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with slope, not flood: the mountain itself is above the floodplain, and the floodplain land is at its foot in Sumas Prairie, the low area named in City flood notices. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status parcel by parcel: the Official Community Plan's Map 16 shows an Agriculture designation on the east side of the mountain. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- On a rural lot, check the well record in the provincial GWELLS application and confirm the septic system was installed by an Authorized Person under the Sewerage System Regulation, because the rural part has no public water or sewer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- The City and FVRD pages do not tell a buyer a specific lot's geotechnical history; that comes from the lot's own reports and title covenants. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
