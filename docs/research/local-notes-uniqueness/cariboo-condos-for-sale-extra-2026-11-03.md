# Cariboo Condos — Extra Local Notes
Page: cariboo-condos-for-sale (Cariboo, Burnaby; condos).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Burnaby uses the Cariboo name two ways, and this page's condos belong to the northern one: the condo stock stands on Manchester Drive, just south of Lougheed Highway and west of Cariboo Road, while the City's own planning material also names a Cariboo-Armstrong Area in southeast Burnaby around Cariboo Hill Secondary School at 8580 16th Avenue. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark837
- School District No. 41 (Burnaby), called SD41, bridges the two: its trustee zone for both is the single Cariboo Lougheed Zone, grouping École Cariboo Hill Secondaire with Armstrong, Second Street Community and Twelfth Avenue schools. https://burnabyschools.ca/board/trustees/
- The Manchester Drive cluster's streets — Manchester Drive, Morrey Court, Horne Street and Carrigan Court — all appear on the City's map for Rezoning Reference #24-23, the Lougheed Town Centre file that surrounds them. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/media/Hpo/_Data/_CouncilMinutesAndReports/Unrestricted/2026/03.24/95478.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The rule hanging over the Manchester Drive cluster is the Lougheed Town Centre Plan: the City's rezoning notice for the neighbouring site states its Comprehensive Development (CD) district is based on the RM5s Multiple Family Residential District, the C3 General Commercial District, the Lougheed Core Area Master Plan and the Lougheed Town Centre Plan as guidelines. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/xmsfvc8kcj/notice-rezoning-bylaw-for-first-and-second-reading-rez-24-23-2026-04-08
- Inside any Manchester Drive building, provincial strata law governs the purchase: the Form B Information Certificate states fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, the contingency reserve fund balance and an insurance summary, with the depreciation report attached. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Which Cariboo does a Manchester Drive condo belong to?
**A:** To the board's Cariboo sub-area along Cariboo Road under Lougheed Highway — not to the Cariboo Hill school neighbourhood in southeast Burnaby. The City itself separates them: its heritage record places Cariboo Hill Secondary School, 8580 16th Avenue, in the Cariboo-Armstrong planning study area, while the Manchester Drive buildings appear on Lougheed Town Centre rezoning maps. SD41 still groups both under one Cariboo Lougheed trustee zone. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark837

**Q:** How old are the Manchester Drive buildings, and what should I check?
**A:** Brookside Park, the cluster's documented example, was completed in 1982 as a four-storey wood-frame building of 98 units under strata plan NWS1579. For any building of that era, the depreciation report is the key document: strata corporations with five or more lots must now obtain one every five years, and it is attached to the Form B, so read its envelope and roof timelines before subjects come off. https://vadapm.com/building-data/brookside-park-9620-manchester-drive

**Q:** What is being built next to the Manchester Drive cluster?
**A:** A rezoning amendment, Reference #24-23, covers 3900 Grand Promenade, 9850 Austin Road and 9858 and 9898 Gatineau Place for the Pinnacle Lougheed development. The City notice states the earlier rezoning, Reference #18-32, already permitted two market strata towers of 80 and 73 storeys, and the 2026 amendment replaces parts of that scheme including a hotel and restaurant component. Expect construction staging on the cluster's north side. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/xmsfvc8kcj/notice-rezoning-bylaw-for-first-and-second-reading-rez-24-23-2026-04-08

**Q:** Which schools serve a Manchester Drive address?
**A:** Run the exact address in the SD41 school locator: the district's Cariboo Lougheed Zone names Armstrong Elementary at 8757 Armstrong Avenue and École Cariboo Hill Secondary at 8580 16th Avenue as its schools, but both stand in southeast Burnaby, and no published catchment assigning Manchester Drive to them was read in this pass. Treat any listing's school claim for a Manchester Drive unit as unverified until the locator confirms it. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/burnaby

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Cariboo Hill Secondary School was built in 1961 and opened with 410 students, after Armstrong, Seaforth and Lyndhurst elementary schools filled the northeast Burnaby area it serves, per the City's heritage record. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark837
- Armstrong Elementary at 8757 Armstrong Avenue is the elementary school that shares the Cariboo-Armstrong name, one block from the secondary school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/burnaby
- The SD41 trustee zone name, Cariboo Lougheed, is the official hint that the district treats the Lougheed corridor and Cariboo Hill as one zone, which is why school paperwork for Manchester Drive uses the Cariboo name at all. https://burnabyschools.ca/board/trustees/
- Cariboo Hill School Park at 8670 16th Avenue adjoins the secondary school in southeast Burnaby — a park carrying the Cariboo name that has nothing to do with the Manchester Drive condos. http://burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- The City's rezoning map for Reference #24-23 labels Manchester Drive, Morrey Court and Cariboo Road together, placing the condo cluster inside the Lougheed Town Centre plan's street grid. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/media/Hpo/_Data/_CouncilMinutesAndReports/Unrestricted/2026/03.24/95478.pdf
