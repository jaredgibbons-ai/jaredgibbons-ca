# Northeast Maple Ridge — Questions for Jared

## 5 questions only Jared can answer
1. In your northeast Maple Ridge deals (Websters Corners, Whonnock, the Thornhill edge), what do buyers pay up for — usable flat acreage, a shop or barn, creek frontage on Kanaka Creek, or just distance from neighbours?
2. Who is actually buying out here right now — horse and hobby-farm buyers, trades owners who need yard and shop space, or families leaving Silver Valley and Albion for land — and where are they coming from?
3. What sells fast and what sits: do serviced lots with municipal water move first while well-and-septic acreages wait, and does a house on Dewdney Trunk Road behave differently from one up 256 Street or on 112 Avenue in Whonnock?
4. What surprises buyers most once they are under contract — a well flow or potability result, a septic field, a riparian setback on Kanaka Creek or Whonnock Creek, or finding out how limited the bridge crossings and school bus routes are?
5. Tell one northeast Maple Ridge deal story: a sale, a near-miss, or a problem you solved in Websters Corners, Whonnock or Thornhill that shows a buyer what no listing could.
