- Mary Hill is the south-side hillside above the Fraser River lowland, around Mary Hill Road, Western, Eastern and Lamprey Drives; no City sub-area boundary is published. https://portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses follow the City-described 1950s Mary Hill pattern: larger lots, curving streets, no rear lanes. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/housing-action-plan
- Zoning Bylaw No. 3630 minimums: RS1 500 square metres / 15 metres; RS2 375 / 12; RS4 300 / 9.5. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City-wide, in full here only: Zoning Bylaw, 2008, No. 3630 governs house lots, in Residential Small-Scale zones RS1–RS4. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH): three to six units without rezoning on RS1–RS4 lots; number set by lot size, location and transit. A duplex, larger project or accessory dwelling unit needs an SSMUH Development Permit first. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- No separate Mary Hill plan: Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2013, No. 3838 (2013). https://portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is in Mary Hill? **A:** École Mary Hill Elementary, 1890 Humber Crescent, serves kindergarten to Grade 5 inside this hillside pattern. School District No. 43 (SD43) assigns children by home address, so confirm the designated school for a specific address in the district School Locator Tool. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-coquitlam
- **Q:** Can I add a suite or coach house? **A:** Possibly: RS lots can hold three to six units without rezoning, depending on size, location and transit proximity. A duplex, larger project or accessory dwelling unit needs an SSMUH Development Permit first; Development Cost Charges apply except to new suites and accessory buildings. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q:** Is Mary Hill in the floodplain? **A:** The hill rises above the lowland, but about 45 percent of Port Coquitlam lies in the Pitt, Fraser and Coquitlam River floodplain, and new development must meet flood-proofing based on the 200-year flood level. Check the lot on the City flood page. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/flood
- **Q:** What bus runs through Mary Hill? **A:** TransLink route 170, the Port Coquitlam South loop, runs from Port Coquitlam Station via Pitt River Road, Eastern Drive and Western Drive, returning via Mary Hill Road. Routes 173 and 174 stay on the north side and do not enter Mary Hill. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt170.pdf
