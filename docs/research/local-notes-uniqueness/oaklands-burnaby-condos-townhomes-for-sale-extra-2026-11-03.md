## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Oaklands inside the Edmonds plan, or is it a separate neighbourhood plan? **A:** It is inside the Edmonds plan area, not a separate plan. The City states the Edmonds Community Plan was officially adopted in March 2025 to manage growth in the Edmonds neighbourhood in southeast Burnaby over the next several decades, and no separate Oaklands community plan page was found on the City project list read for this note. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/edmonds-community-plan
- **Q:** Which SkyTrain station should an Oaklands buyer plan around? **A:** Edmonds Station on the Expo Line is the named station for this area. TransLink lists Edmonds on the Expo Line between Royal Oak and 22nd Street, and the Expo Line connects downtown Vancouver with Burnaby, New Westminster and Surrey; the exact walk or bus link from a specific Oaklands street is UNCONFIRMED in this note. http://retrosearch.org/browse?url=https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/skytrain
- **Q:** Will the Kingsway and Edmonds redevelopment change this pocket directly? **A:** It changes the node beside it, not the pocket's own lots, on the City page read. The City and BC Housing project is on publicly owned land at the southwest corner of Kingsway and Edmonds, 4.5 acres in the heart of the Edmonds Town Centre, proposing over 1,400 rental units and about 1,000 strata units; its full entry is in the highgate-burnaby-condos-townhomes-for-sale note. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/kingsway-edmonds-conceptual-master-plan
- **Q:** Which secondary school zone is Oaklands in? **A:** The Kingsway South zone, whose secondary school is Byrne Creek Community Secondary. The Board lists Byrne Creek with Edmonds Community, Stride Avenue Community and Taylor Park in that zone, and the Province lists Byrne Creek at 7777 18th Street; an individual address still needs district locator confirmation, which was not read here. https://burnabyschools.ca/board/trustees/
