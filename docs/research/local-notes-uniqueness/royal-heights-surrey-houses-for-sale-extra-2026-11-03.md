# Royal Heights Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: royal-heights-surrey-houses-for-sale (Royal Heights, Surrey; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Royal Heights has no City boundary for the board sub-area name, but a City corporate report describes a Royal Heights enclave bounded by Royal Heights Park to the north, 116 Street to the east, properties on 96A Avenue to the south and Princess Drive to the west, with 127 lots. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2026-R017.pdf
- Royal Heights Park, at 9797 115A Street, is the area's defining park, listed by the City under Whalley and City Centre. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/royal-heights-park
- Royal Heights Elementary, at 11665 97 Avenue, sits inside that enclave pattern at its south side, on 97 Avenue east of 116 Street. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/school/03636077

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The enclave described in Corporate Report R017 holds 127 lots — a small, defined subdivision pattern, not an open-ended suburb — bounded by Royal Heights Park, 116 Street, 96A Avenue properties and Princess Drive. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2026-R017.pdf
- Royal Heights Park itself is over 2.2 hectares, with forest on the west side and an open field on the east, established in the mid-1960s during the subdivision of the surrounding houses. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/royal-heights-park
- Park access streets name the subdivision's interior pattern: 97A Avenue at 115A Street, Crown Crescent, Princess Drive and Royal Crescent. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/royal-heights-park

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** How big is Royal Heights, actually? **A:** Small and defined at its core. A City corporate report describes the Royal Heights enclave as 127 lots bounded by Royal Heights Park to the north, 116 Street to the east, properties on 96A Avenue to the south and Princess Drive to the west. Houses outside those four edges may carry the board sub-area name without sitting in the enclave the report describes. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2026-R017.pdf
- **Q:** Which elementary school is in Royal Heights? **A:** Royal Heights Elementary, at 11665 97 Avenue. The British Columbia Ministry school page lists it as a Kindergarten to Grade 7 public school in School District 36 (Surrey), school ID 03636077, with phone 604-581-7622. Catchments are address-based, so confirm the exact house address in the district locator before relying on it. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/school/03636077
- **Q:** Did the zoning here really change recently? **A:** Yes. Surrey Council minutes for April 13, 2026 record adoption of Bylaw No. 21930 rezoning this Royal Heights enclave from Comprehensive Development (CD) to a small-scale residential zone, with no public hearing under the cited Local Government Act provision. A seller's older CD zoning printout is therefore out of date for these lots. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/council-minutes/regular-council-public-hearing-minutes-april-13-2026
- **Q:** Is Royal Heights in the Nicomekl floodplain? **A:** The enclave sits above the lowland, around 97 Avenue and 115A Street by Royal Heights Park, and no floodplain designation for the enclave is stated in Corporate Report R017 or the park page read in this pass. That is not a lot-level clearance — check the specific lot in the City floodplain mapping before an offer. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2026-R017.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Royal Heights Park was established in the mid-1960s during the subdivision itself, so the park's forest west side and open field east side are original subdivision features, not later additions. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/royal-heights-park
- The park's access points are at 97A Avenue at 115A Street, Crown Crescent, Princess Drive and Royal Crescent — four interior subdivision streets, not an arterial entrance. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/royal-heights-park
- Royal Kwantlen Park, at 13035 104 Avenue north of Royal Heights, is a separate, much larger City park — do not confuse a “Royal” park listing with Royal Heights Park at 9797 115A Street. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/royal-kwantlen-park

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check elevation and drainage lot by lot: Royal Heights sits above the Serpentine / Nicomekl lowland to the south, but neither Corporate Report R017 nor the Royal Heights Park page states a flood, slope or geotechnical status for any specific lot — use the City floodplain and hazard mapping for the address. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2026-R017.pdf
- Check the current zone, not the historic CD zone: Bylaw No. 21930 (adopted April 13, 2026) changed the enclave's zoning, so a pre-2026 zoning printout for a Royal Heights lot is superseded. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/council-minutes/regular-council-public-hearing-minutes-april-13-2026
