## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Greenway is the second phase of Adera's South Ridge Club, a collection of 67 townhomes in 3- and 4-bedroom plans ranging from 1,550 to just under 2,000 square feet, per the developer. https://adera.com/finalists-in-6-georgie-award-categories/
- Every Greenway home has private outdoor space of up to 750 square feet, including a private rooftop lanai and deck space, per Adera. https://adera.com/finalists-in-6-georgie-award-categories/
- The City's February 22, 2016 planning report for 2825 159 Street proposed rezoning from Acreage Residential (RA) to a Comprehensive Development (CD) zone based on RM-30 to permit exactly 67 townhouse units, with Greenway Adera Ventures Ltd. as owner. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7915-0244-00.pdf
- The shared amenity is the South Ridge Club clubhouse, described by Adera as a 6,500 square foot building with an outdoor pool, hot tub, kitchen, lounge, exercise room, full-size gymnasium and movie theatre. https://adera.com/2017/02/udi-development-of-the-month-south-ridge-club/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Greenway is a strata purchase, so the Form B and depreciation report govern the diligence — stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report on a five-year cycle and can no longer defer it by vote. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- The site-specific CD zoning, not a standard townhouse zone, is what permits Greenway's form — a buyer checking additions or exterior changes starts from the CD bylaw and the strata bylaws, not from a generic Surrey townhouse rule. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7915-0244-00.pdf
- Surrey-wide suite, coach house and short-term rental rules do not create rights inside a strata townhome complex — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note for the citywide frame.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Greenway one phase or part of something larger? **A:** It is one phase of a larger club development. Adera describes Greenway as the second release of homes at South Ridge Club, following Willsbrook and preceding the Grandview phase, so owners share the South Ridge Club clubhouse and amenities with the sister phases rather than owning a standalone complex amenity building of their own. https://adera.com/finalists-in-6-georgie-award-categories/
- **Q:** How big are the Greenway homes and outdoor spaces? **A:** The developer states 3- and 4-bedroom homes from 1,550 to just under 2,000 square feet. Each home carries private outdoor space of up to 750 square feet, including a private rooftop lanai — that rooftop space is the defining layout feature here and the item to inspect closely for membrane, drainage and railing condition on a resale purchase. https://adera.com/finalists-in-6-georgie-award-categories/
- **Q:** What does the clubhouse actually include? **A:** Adera describes a 6,500 square foot members-only clubhouse with an outdoor pool, hot tub, barbecue area, kitchen, lounge, exercise room, a full-size gymnasium and a movie theatre. Because the amenity is shared across the South Ridge Club phases, a buyer should confirm in the strata documents how clubhouse costs are shared and who may use it. https://adera.com/2017/02/udi-development-of-the-month-south-ridge-club/
- **Q:** Which schools serve 2825 159 Street? **A:** Catchments in School District 36 (SD36) are address-based, so the exact unit address must be run in the district locator. Grandview Heights Secondary is at 16987 25 Avenue in the same plan area; do not rely on a listing's school line — confirm the catchment for 2825 159 Street itself before an offer that depends on a school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
Read: 2026-11-03
