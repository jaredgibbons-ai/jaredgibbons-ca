# Poplar Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: poplar (Poplar, Abbotsford; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Poplar sits south of Highway 1, around Huntingdon Road, King Road and Gladwin Road. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- The board name is Poplar, while the City's official demographic profile name for the same ground is South Poplar, profile area 5. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2020%20McKee%20Profile%20-%20spreads%20final.pdf
- The area runs west of the Marshall Road and McCallum Road side and east toward Clearbrook Road. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- Clearbrook lies to the northwest, the City Centre and South Fraser Way corridor lie north across Highway 1, and Sumas Prairie lies to the east and southeast. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2006%20Commission%20-%20PRC%2028%20Abbotsford%20Community%20Heritage%20Register%20Addition....pdf
- No Poplar-specific neighbourhood plan is listed in the Official Community Plan; Abbotsforward 2050 governs. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- The community was named Poplar for the poplar trees planted on the top of the hill, and the Old South Poplar School is on the Abbotsford Community Heritage Register. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2006%20Commission%20-%20PRC%2028%20Abbotsford%20Community%20Heritage%20Register%20Addition....pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Single detached houses are the main form, on urban lots in the north part and larger rural lots toward Huntingdon Road and the south edge. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- Urban minimums are RS3 at 540 m² and RS5 at 420 m² minimum interior lots in City reports, while rural A1 land subdivides at a minimum of 8.0 ha. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/56399/
- Townhomes occur along King Road and around Windsor Street and Marshall Road; the named project is Kings Crossing, covered in full in section 6. https://thekingscrossing.ca/contact-us/
- The established stock is freehold detached housing, with strata limited to the townhome complexes noted above. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- Few or no condo buildings inside Poplar were verified; low-rise condos concentrate north of Highway 1 along South Fraser Way. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Poplar angle on the shared city facts is the urban-to-rural split on one page; see the central-abbotsford-guide note for the full Zoning Bylaw, suite and tax facts. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- South-edge lots in or beside the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) follow the Agricultural Land Commission Act, the ALR Use Regulation and City zoning for subdivision and added dwellings. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/content/coa/coabylaws/z/zoning/?xsl=/templates/browse.xsl
- Lots inside the Airport Zoning Regulation area on City drawing M-500 face height surfaces and NAV CANADA and Transport Canada approvals before a City permit, because Poplar is east of Abbotsford International Airport (YXX). https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- Floodplain status for a single lot is checked against Zoning Bylaw Schedule E Floodplain Boundaries in the City WebMap. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools are physically in Poplar? **A:** Three public schools sit inside the area. South Poplar Traditional Elementary is at 32746 Huntingdon Road, Jackson Elementary at 33165 King Road and Abbotsford Traditional School at 2272 Windsor Street, and the catchment for any single address must still be checked in the locator. https://southpoplar.abbyschools.ca/
- **Q:** Is South Poplar the catchment school for its own address? **A:** No, not for the regular stream. A locator run for 32746 Huntingdon Road returns Jackson Elementary as the regular catchment elementary, with Abbotsford Middle and Abbotsford Senior Secondary following, while South Poplar feeds the separate Traditional pathway. https://ats.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/registration/atms
- **Q:** How does the Traditional pathway work here? **A:** Abbotsford Traditional School runs Grades 6 to 12 and feeds from two elementary schools. The school states the majority of its Grade 6 students come from King and South Poplar, and feeder students are automatically registered. https://ats.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/registration/atms
- **Q:** What buses serve Poplar? **A:** Four routes serve the area. Route 3 runs Clearbrook/Huntingdon, route 5 runs Clearbrook/Bourquin via Hospital on Marshall Road at the east edge, route 6 runs the Gladwin corridor and route 12 links the University of the Fraser Valley and Bourquin Exchange. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- **Q:** Is Poplar in a floodplain? **A:** A lot-level answer is not confirmed here. Poplar drains toward the Sumas Prairie system the City describes as low-lying and subject to Nooksack River overflow, so the Schedule E map in WebMap must be checked for the specific lot. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Both locator-run addresses in Poplar return the same regular chain: Jackson Elementary, Abbotsford Middle and Abbotsford Senior Secondary, including Jackson Elementary's own address at 33165 King Road. https://abbymiddle.abbyschools.ca/node/10331
- Huntingdon Park is at 34779 3rd Avenue and Berry Park at 33928 Fern Street at the north edge toward Highway 1. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds
- The nearest City recreation centres are outside the area: Matsqui Recreation Centre at 3106 Clearbrook Road to the northwest and Abbotsford Recreation Centre at 2499 McMillan Road to the northeast. https://www.abbotsford.ca/Aquatics
- Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre at 32900 Marshall Road sits immediately north and northeast of Poplar across Highway 1. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre
- No shopping centre inside Poplar was verified; Sevenoaks Shopping Centre on South Fraser Way at Gladwin Road, north of Highway 1, is the nearest centre named in the City study. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/HDNP%2BCommercial%2BMarket%2BStudy.pdf
- South-edge rural lots may be on wells and onsite sewage, recorded in the provincial GWELLS database and under the Sewerage System Regulation through Fraser Health. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners
- A district-wide school renaming note for nearby Matsqui-area buyers: the former Matsqui Elementary is now Mathxwí Elementary at 33661 Elizabeth Avenue, renamed at the end of 2021 in consultation with local First Nation communities. https://indigenous.abbyschools.ca/blog/renaming-and-intertwining-history

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City-wide tax rates, water and sewer rates and Speculation and Vacancy Tax applicability are written in full in the central-abbotsford-guide note; see that note. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes
- The Poplar point is the tax formula itself: Abbotsford tax is the BC Assessment value multiplied by a rate per $1,000 of net assessed value, and about 35 cents of each tax dollar passes to other taxing authorities. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check floodplain status lot by lot against Zoning Bylaw Schedule E in WebMap, because drainage runs toward the Sumas Prairie system. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- Confirm ALR status before counting on subdivision or an extra dwelling on a south-edge lot, where the A1 minimum is 8.0 ha. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/content/coa/coabylaws/z/zoning/?xsl=/templates/browse.xsl
- Check AZR status on City drawing M-500 before planning additions on lots east of YXX. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- On rural lots, pull the GWELLS well record and confirm septic filings before removing subjects. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners
