## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The age rule on every complex above comes from the same provincial frame: under the Strata Property Act sections 123.1 and 123.2 a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older, and any bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since November 24, 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- The bylaw restricts who may live in the townhouse, not who may own it: the Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it, and the exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse or partner, and defined children cases expanded on May 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Listing wording is not the bylaw: Willow Park Estates' "at least one owner must be 55" and Chelsea Green's "only one occupant to be 55+" are listing summaries, and the registered bylaw read with the Form B Information Certificate is what governs a purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- A long-term rental ban cannot be enforced in any of these complexes, because strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since November 24, 2022 — an older "rentals not allowed" line in a building record for Willow Park Estates cannot be relied on; the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- Township townhome suites carry a live source conflict, flagged not resolved: Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, section 102 as amended in July 2025, states a secondary suite may be located within a townhouse, rowhouse or manorhome, capped at 120 square metres, one per principal dwelling unit, while the Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhouses. https://www.tol.ca/at-your-service/bylaw-enforcement/bylaws/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** My spouse is under 55 — can we both live in a 55+ townhouse in Langley? **A:** Yes, in a strata townhouse. Provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption, and children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. The exemption comes from the Strata Property Regulation, so a complex's own bylaw cannot remove it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** Is Chartwell Green the same as Chartwell Langley Gardens? **A:** No, and the shared name causes real search confusion in Walnut Grove. Chartwell Green at 8737 212 Street is a 55+ townhouse complex a buyer can purchase into. Chartwell Langley Gardens at 8888 202 Street is a Chartwell rental continuum-of-care residence that cannot be bought; Fraser Health records 92 long-term care beds there, of which 73 are publicly subsidized. Check the civic address and the tenure, not the Chartwell name. https://chartwell.com/en/bc/langley/langley-gardens
- **Q:** Where is the deepest choice of 55+ townhouses in Langley? **A:** Walnut Grove, in the Township. Four verified complexes sit there — Churchill Park (147 duplex-style townhouses, built 1993), Spencer Green (97 townhomes, built 1991), Chartwell Green and Chelsea Green — all gated, all ground-oriented, and all from the early 1990s. Willoughby, the Township's newer townhouse area, has only one verified 55+ complex, Willow Park Estates, and it was built in 1987, before the current Willoughby build-out. https://www.tmbrealestate.com/walnut-grove-townhomes/churchill-park
- **Q:** Can I buy a 55+ townhouse for my parent if I am under 55? **A:** Ownership itself is not age-restricted. The Province states that people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in it; a parent who is 55 or older can be the resident who satisfies the bylaw. The practical check is the registered bylaw and the exemptions in the strata package, because a listing's one-line age summary does not reproduce either. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The two municipalities bill differently, and six of the seven complexes sit in the Township: Township taxes and utility fees were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty on current taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5 percent at midnight September 3, 2026, and utility fees are billed on a separate statement. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- For the one City of Langley complex, Huntsfield Green: the City's official 2026 rate sheet sets the total residential (Class 1) rate at $4.2067 per $1,000 of taxable value, made up of a $2.5937 general municipal rate, $1.1606 school, $0.3586 TransLink (GVTA), $0.0381 BC Assessment and $0.0555 Metro Vancouver (GVRD) and $0.0002 Municipal Finance Authority components. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/2026taxrates
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies in both municipalities: the Province lists Langley (city and township) as a taxable area, so a 55+ townhouse left vacant still requires the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Read the registered age bylaw, not the listing: two complexes in this note are listing-verified only (Chartwell Green and Chelsea Green), so their age rule rests on listing statements until the bylaw and Form B are read for the specific complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- Riparian and flood checks are parcel-level in this stock: for any complex near Bertrand Creek or another watercourse, confirm the setback and flood position for the specific unit with the Township in addition to the strata documents.
- Age-restricted stock narrows the resale pool mechanically: the next buyer must also be able to live in the unit under the bylaw — be 55 or older, hold an exemption as a spouse, partner, caregiver or legacy resident, or buy without residing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Do not price a manufactured-home park unit as a townhouse: Langley's 55+ parks run on the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation, under which a landlord in a 55+ park may refuse an assignment where the purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement — a consent step with no equivalent in a strata townhouse sale. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
