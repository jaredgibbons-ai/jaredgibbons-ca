# East Newton Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: east-newton-surrey-houses-for-sale (East Newton; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- East Newton is the City of Surrey's geographic name for the neighbourhoods around Chimney Hill, east of 144 Street, north of 64 Avenue and Hyland Creek, and south of Bear Creek. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- The City's three East Newton plan areas designate land uses for the broader Chimney Hill community, generally south of Bear Creek and east of 144 Street, bordered by the Chimney Hill residential estate in the north-east and Hyland Creek in the south. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- The City also names Valley View Cemetery as a landmark inside the area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- East Newton's houses were built out under 1990s subdivision plans, and the City states the three plan areas are now largely built out. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- The East Newton Business Park plan area at the north end was designated predominantly business park and employment land, unlike the residential North and South plan areas. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Read the current Official Community Plan (OCP) designation, not the old plan label: East Newton North was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update and is no longer in effect, and East Newton South's and the Business Park's land use designations were superseded by that update. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- The East Newton South and Business Park plans still guide implementation of public amenities and infrastructure even though their land use designations were superseded. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- Surrey-wide rules for houses — secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites, and small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) unit counts by lot size — are carried in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note; see that note for the figures.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What plans govern an East Newton house lot today? **A:** The Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan does. The area was planned through the 1993 Local Area Plan and three Neighbourhood Concept Plans approved in 1996, 1997 and 1999, but the City states East Newton North was later rescinded and the South and Business Park designations were superseded by the OCP update, so a lot's current OCP designation is the one to check. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- **Q:** Which schools are physically in or beside East Newton? **A:** Chimney Hill Elementary at 14755 74 Avenue and T E Scott Elementary at 7079 148 Street are named by the City for this area, and Tamanawis Secondary sits at 12600 66 Avenue at the area's south-west edge. A nearby school is not an assigned school: Surrey Schools sets catchments by exact address in its School Locator, and no locator result for an East Newton address is on record. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036 https://www.surreyschools.ca/ellendale/school-registration
- **Q:** Is there a school called East Newton Elementary? **A:** No — no school by that name exists. It appears in neither the BC Ministry of Education school list nor the Surrey Schools directory, and there is no school at 7235 144 Street. The name came from a district web address that renders a generic page for any name typed into it. The elementary schools confirmed by name and address here are Chimney Hill, M B Sanford and T E Scott. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** What changed in the East Newton Business Park in 2020? **A:** In March 2020 Council approved amendments that replaced the live-work designation south of 66 Avenue, and its park and buffer designations, with the business park designation. The City records that most business-park land has since been developed or is under application, while the live-work land had not been developed or shown market feasibility. Houses near the north end sit beside employment land, not future housing land. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- McLeod Road Traditional, at 6325 142 Street west of 144 Street, is a choice program school whose correct name is McLeod Road Traditional — not "McLeod Road Elementary" as some listings call it. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- The City's East Newton page names Regent Christian Academy among the area's schools; it is an independent school, separate from the public SD36 catchment system. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- Two creeks frame the area — Bear Creek to the north and Hyland Creek to the south — and the City names both in the area's geographic definition. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- The main planning change touching East Newton houses is the March 2020 Business Park amendment (Corporate Report R051, March 9, 2020), which converted the undeveloped live-work land south of 66 Avenue to business park designation. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- The Surrey Langley SkyTrain extension runs along Fraser Highway north and east of East Newton, with no station inside this area; project detail is named once here and carried in the east-newton-guide and east-newton notes. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/
- No house-scale development application inside East Newton's residential streets was confirmed on a City page read for this note; see NOT CONFIRMED.

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- East Newton houses pay City of Surrey property taxes, due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- The provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies: the Province lists the City of Surrey as a designated taxable area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- Surrey's 2026 residential tax rate and flat utility charges are carried in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note; see that note for the figures.

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 342 runs along East Newton's west and south edges: TransLink's timetable shows it travelling via 72 Avenue, 144 Street, 57 Avenue, 142 Street, 60 Avenue and 152 Street between Newton Exchange and Langley Centre. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt342.pdf
- Route 393 links Newton Exchange and Surrey Central Station via 72 Avenue, King George Boulevard, 64 Avenue, 128 Street and 104 Avenue, passing south and west of East Newton. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt393.pdf
- No SkyTrain station is inside East Newton; the area is bus-served, with Newton Exchange as its named TransLink terminus. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt342.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check creek setbacks on lots backing onto Hyland Creek or Bear Creek: Surrey may require a development permit in its Sensitive Ecosystem and Flood Plain Development Permit Areas, and both creeks bound this area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- Do not rely on a 1990s Neighbourhood Concept Plan label for redevelopment potential: the North plan was rescinded and the South and Business Park designations superseded, so verify the lot's current OCP designation and zone with the City. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
