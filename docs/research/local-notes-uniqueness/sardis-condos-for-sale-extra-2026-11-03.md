## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Andmar, 46185 Thomas Road: the developer's statement describes 6 mixed-use and 4 residential buildings, about 120,000 square feet of commercial space, 461 units. https://www.liveatandmar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Andmar-Complete-Binder-June-2026.pdf
- Andmar sits on ten acres of Crown land held in reserve for the Tzeachten First Nation, leased 109 years to May 31, 2128 — its condos are leasehold, not freehold strata. https://www.liveatandmar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Andmar-Complete-Binder-June-2026.pdf
- York Residences, 45511, 45505 and 45497 Campus Drive: three buildings run as rentals by Diverse, not sold strata. https://www.diverserentals.com/rentals/york
- Elim Village Garrison Crossing, 45412 Chehalis Drive: retirement community; buildings named The Redwood, Sequoia, Douglas, Maple and Hawthorn. https://elimvillage.com/chilliwack/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Andmar buyers take subleases under the 109-year head lease ending May 31, 2128, so title, mortgage and resale differ from freehold strata. https://www.liveatandmar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Andmar-Complete-Binder-June-2026.pdf
- Garrison apartments sit in CD-9, where apartments meet R5 standards: minimum lot 1,000 square metres, width 30 metres. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is an Andmar condo freehold? **A:** No. The developer's statement states the project sits on Crown land held in reserve for the Tzeachten First Nation, leased to Andmar Development Corp. for 109 years ending May 31, 2128; buyers take subleases, so the statement and sublease need review before purchase. https://www.liveatandmar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Andmar-Complete-Binder-June-2026.pdf
- **Q:** Can I buy a Campus Drive condo? **A:** York Residences, 45511, 45505 and 45497 Campus Drive are rental buildings. Diverse lists York A, B and C as rental homes in Garrison Campus, pets not allowed. Buying in Garrison Crossing means a strata building elsewhere, or Elim Village on Chehalis Drive. https://www.diverserentals.com/rentals/york
- **Q:** What is the retirement option in Sardis? **A:** Elim Village Garrison Crossing, 45412 Chehalis Drive — a retirement and assisted living community in five operator-named buildings (listed in section 2 above). Because it is operator-run, not standard strata, purchase and monthly charges come from the operator's own documents, not a Form B. https://elimvillage.com/chilliwack/
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Sardis condo? **A:** Catchments are address-based, and Sardis splits. The locator for 45501 Market Way returned Watson Elementary, a Mount Slesse or Vedder Middle choice, then G.W. Graham or Sardis Secondary. For Thomas Road: 46200 Stoneview Drive returned Promontory Heights Elementary and G.W. Graham Secondary. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
Read: 2026-11-03
