# Hatzic Area Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: hatzic-mission-guide (Hatzic; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Hatzic is three places under one mailing name: the Hatzic Bench inside the City of Mission, Hatzic Prairie in Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area F, and Hatzic Island in Electoral Area G. The FVRD lists Area F as McConnell Creek and Hatzic Prairie, and Area G as Nicomen Island, Deroche, Dewdney and Hatzic Island (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).
- Bench streets inside the City include Ferguson Avenue, Jasper Avenue, McEwen Avenue, Ferndale Avenue and Dewdney Trunk Road, while Shook Road serves the Hatzic Lake and Hatzic Island side (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).
- The Hatzic Valley Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 0999, 2010, covers the Area F valley floor between Hatzic Lake and Stave Lake, including Hatzic Prairie and Durieu (https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=30556).

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The Bench holds city-lot houses near Hatzic Elementary and Hatzic Middle School, while the lake side on Shook Road holds cabins, park homes and resort use-area lots — three tenure types on one guide page (https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075).
- Hatzic Prairie holds farm and acreage housing on valley-floor parcels, most of the inhabited valley being in the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) with commercial and hobby farming (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).
- For 55-plus stock in Hatzic, see the hatzic-55-plus-homes-for-sale note; its stock list is not repeated here.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Which rulebook applies depends on which side of the City and FVRD line the parcel sits on: City of Mission zoning and suite registration on the Bench, FVRD Bylaw No. 0999, 2010 and FVRD Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021 on the Prairie, confirmed parcel by parcel (https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=30556).
- Inside the City, all secondary suites must be registered with the City and suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhouses; in Electoral Area F, a suite must sit inside the main house and cannot be a detached garden suite (https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program).
- On ALR parcels the Agricultural Land Commission allows one principal residence up to 500 square metres plus one additional residence of 90 square metres up to 40 hectares, or 186 square metres above 40 hectares (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- Flood protection here is run by the Dewdney Area Improvement District (DAID), which owns and operates the Hatzic pump station and electrical kiosks in Hatzic Slough, per the FVRD's joint release (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2024-1/10m-for-flood-mitigation-projects-benefitting-communities-north-of-the-fraser-river.html).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is Hatzic part of Mission? **A:** Only the Bench is. Hatzic Prairie is FVRD Electoral Area F and Hatzic Island is Electoral Area G, so a Shook Road or Prairie property follows FVRD rules and provincial rural taxes while a Ferguson Avenue house follows City bylaws. Confirm which side of the line the parcel identifier number (PID) sits on before quoting any rule (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).
**Q:** How is Hatzic Prairie protected from flooding? **A:** By a dike and pump system run by the Dewdney Area Improvement District, not by the City alone. A $5-million provincial project funds upgrades to the Hatzic pump station and electrical kiosks in Hatzic Slough, raising the kiosk to a higher flood-resistant level and adding a fish-friendly pump to increase capacity (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2024-1/10m-for-flood-mitigation-projects-benefitting-communities-north-of-the-fraser-river.html).
**Q:** Which schools serve Hatzic? **A:** Hatzic Elementary, kindergarten to grade 6, at 8465 Draper Street, and Hatzic Middle School, grades 7 to 9, at 34875 Moffat Avenue, both School District No. 75 schools on the Bench. District policy feeds the Hatzic Elementary catchment to Hatzic Middle School; a lake or Prairie address still needs an address-level catchment check (https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075).
**Q:** What did the 1948 flood do here? **A:** It put most of Hatzic Prairie under water. The FVRD records that a dyke break on Hatzic Lake in 1948 grew the lake five times in size to 25 square kilometres. A specific property's current flood status and dike protection are still a parcel-level check, not a history answer (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).
**Q:** Who supplies water in Hatzic Prairie? **A:** The FVRD's Hatzic Prairie water system serves part of Area F on metered billing, while Bench homes are on City services and other Prairie lots use private wells. Confirm the service area for the lot on the FVRD water system maps before assuming community water (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/water.html).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Hatzic Prairie water is billed twice a year, not monthly: the FVRD's 2026 Area F rates list Water – Hatzic Prairie (service 491) as metered, billed semi-annually in April and October, with a $335.00 minimum charge (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html).
- The Hatzic Lake pump station was rebuilt with three submersible fish-friendly pumps from Bedford Pumps, the first of that type installed in the Lower Fraser, serving the whole Hatzic Lake watershed (https://www.resilientwaters.ca/_files/ugd/9288ab_80f4f16b64aa43339c59286f00b5c890.pdf).
- A local state of emergency was declared for the Hatzic Lake area during the November 2021 atmospheric river, which is the event the current pump and watershed funding follows (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2024-1/10m-for-flood-mitigation-projects-benefitting-communities-north-of-the-fraser-river.html).
- Neilson Regional Park, an FVRD regional park on the west side of Hatzic Lake, is the lake's named public park in the FVRD parks snapshot (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/RGS/OutdoorRecSnapshot.pdf).
- The Hatzic Watershed Stewardship Team behind the flood work includes Leq'á:mel First Nation, the FVRD, the DAID, the City of Mission and provincial ministries (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2024-1/10m-for-flood-mitigation-projects-benefitting-communities-north-of-the-fraser-river.html).

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Hatzic Pump Station and Electrical Kiosk Upgrades, Hatzic Slough: $5 million, funded by the Province through the Fraser Valley Flood Mitigation Program, including the Hatzic Flood Mitigation and Watershed Master Plan, a raised electrical kiosk and an added fish-friendly pump; owner-operator is the Dewdney Area Improvement District, status as announced in the FVRD joint release read on 2026-11-03 (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2024-1/10m-for-flood-mitigation-projects-benefitting-communities-north-of-the-fraser-river.html).

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Hatzic runs two tax systems: Bench properties inside the City pay the City — see the mission-farms note for the city-wide figures — while Hatzic Prairie and Hatzic Island properties are rural and pay the Province, receiving their notice from the Surveyor of Taxes Office (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area).
- Rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5 percent penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5 percent on amounts unpaid on October 31, under section 11 of the Taxation (Rural Area) Act — different from the City's single penalty (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01).
- The Hatzic Prairie water charge in Section 5 ($335.00 semi-annual minimum, 2026, service 491) is the sub-area-specific utility cost here (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html).
Read: 2026-11-03

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The Bench connects west on Dewdney Trunk Road into central Mission, while Hatzic Prairie and the lake are reached by Sylvester Road and Shook Road; no bus route summary for the lake or Prairie was stated on an operator page read for this note (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/water.html).
- The West Coast Express runs from Mission City Station with five weekday westbound trains at 5:25 to 7:25 a.m. reaching Waterfront in 75 minutes, five eastbound returns and no weekend service, per the TransLink timetable (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf).
- BC Transit and the FVRD consulted in spring 2025 on a proposed Highway 7 bus between Mission and Agassiz that would serve the Dewdney Trunk Road and Lougheed Highway corridor south of Hatzic if funded (https://www.bctransit.com/have-your-say-in-the-future-of-transit-along-highway-2025-04-25/).

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check flood status, dike protection and flood-construction level by PID for any Prairie, Island or Shook Road lot, because the 1948 dyke break flooded the Prairie and the 2021 atmospheric river brought a local state of emergency at Hatzic Lake (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).
- Check whether the lot sits behind DAID dike works or outside them, and confirm any DAID parcel tax or drainage charge for the PID with the improvement district (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2024-1/10m-for-flood-mitigation-projects-benefitting-communities-north-of-the-fraser-river.html).
- Check ALR status by PID on the Agricultural Land Commission maps, because most inhabited Prairie land is in the ALR and ALR status controls second homes and subdivision (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- Confirm dock, beach and lake-access tenure on title for any Shook Road property; no official page read confirms a private dock right on Hatzic Lake (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/RGS/OutdoorRecSnapshot.pdf).
