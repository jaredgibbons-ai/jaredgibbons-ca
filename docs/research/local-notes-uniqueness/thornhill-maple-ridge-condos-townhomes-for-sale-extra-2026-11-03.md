## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Slope is the governing rule on Thornhill: the City's Natural Features Development Permit (NFDP) applies to any site with slopes of 15% or greater, and to sites within 50 metres (m) of a park boundary or floodplain boundary. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2023-11-import/Natural%20Features%20Development%20Permit%20Guidelines%20%28Initial%20Checklist%29_202103241909465463.pdf
- The City's sloping-sites bulletin requires a Geotechnical Letter of Assurance for development on a slope greater than 10%, and a full geotechnical report on or within 5 m of a slope greater than 20%; development is prohibited on slopes greater than 25% by the Zoning Bylaw. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Sloping%20Sites%20-%20Geotechnical%20Requirements_201309060639219195.pdf
- Wildfire is the second overlay: the City's peer review of its Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area records the proposed Thornhill polygon at over 580 hectares, with hazardous fuel types across the slope and critical telecommunications infrastructure on it, and puts construction in the area under National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) standards NFPA-1144 and NFPA-1141. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2023-11-import/Wildfire_rpt_2014_peer_review_201406051235208899.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is Rydge in Thornhill or Albion?
**A:** Both names are in print for the same hillside: Raicon Developments markets Rydge at 10640 248 Street as an Albion community, while its listings classify it Thornhill MR (Maple Ridge). The program's notes follow the board classification, so Rydge counts as Thornhill stock here. For a buyer, the practical point is to search both area names, because the 248 Street pocket is marketed under Albion and filed under Thornhill. https://rydge.ca/register/

**Q:** What geotechnical paper should exist for a Thornhill townhouse?
**A:** Under the City's sloping-sites bulletin, a complex on a slope over 10% needed a Geotechnical Letter of Assurance at building permit, and one on or near a slope over 20% needed a full geotechnical report as well. If a geotechnical covenant is already on title, the permit file includes a sealed letter from the engineer confirming they read it, so ask for the covenant and the report together when reviewing the strata documents. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Sloping%20Sites%20-%20Geotechnical%20Requirements_201309060639219195.pdf

**Q:** Does wildfire risk change what a Thornhill strata can do?
**A:** Yes, at the building-envelope level: the City's wildfire guidelines put design and construction inside the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area under NFPA-1144 and NFPA-1141 standards, which reach roofing, siding and landscaping choices a strata makes at renewal time. The proposed Thornhill polygon is over 580 hectares, so the 248 Street complexes sit inside the mapped interface, not at its edge. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2023-11-import/Wildfire_rpt_2014_peer_review_201406051235208899.pdf

**Q:** Which schools serve the 248 Street complexes?
**A:** The complexes sit in Albion's school orbit: Albion Elementary at 10031 240 Street and Samuel Robertson Technical Secondary at 10445 245 Street are the named schools on the west side of Thornhill in School District No. 42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows), called SD42. Catchment is by exact address, and SD42's facilities review treats Thornhill itself as a future growth area, so confirm the 248 Street address in the district locator rather than assuming the Albion names. https://www.sd42.ca/assets/media/Strategic-Facilities-Review-June-2021.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The City's 2026–2030 Capital Plan replaces the 248 Street drinking water pump station, with planning, design and construction commencing in 2028 — the station that serves the townhouse pocket's slope. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-03/Approved-December-15-2025-2026-2030-Capital-Plan.pdf
- The same capital plan twins the supply main between 248 Street and the Albion Reservoir in 2027 and 2028 to support growth in the Albion and Thornhill service area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-03/Approved-December-15-2025-2026-2030-Capital-Plan.pdf
- Thornhill Park at 26007 98 Avenue is the named park on the hill's north side, used in SD42's environmental school program calendar. https://es.sd42.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/SD42-Calendar-Linear-2023_2024.pdf
- SD42's Strategic Facilities Review expects Thornhill development to begin around 2030, as Albion and Silver Valley approach buildout — the planning fact behind the holding pattern on the acreages above the townhouses. https://www.sd42.ca/assets/media/Strategic-Facilities-Review-June-2021.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Read title for a Section 219 geotechnical covenant: the City's bulletin states a covenant restricting the use of land may be required where a Qualified Professional reports on landslide-prone land under Section 56 of the Community Charter. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Sloping%20Sites%20-%20Geotechnical%20Requirements_201309060639219195.pdf
- Check the slope map before planning any addition: the NFDP checklist requires a colour-coded slope assessment of slopes over 15%, and the City does not support development where average natural slopes exceed 25%. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2023-11-import/Natural%20Features%20Development%20Permit%20Guidelines%20%28Initial%20Checklist%29_202103241909465463.pdf
