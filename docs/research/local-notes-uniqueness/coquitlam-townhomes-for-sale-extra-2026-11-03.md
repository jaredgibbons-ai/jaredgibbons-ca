## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Townhouses in Coquitlam are a multi-family form that needs a Form and Character Development Permit: the City requires that permit before subdividing, constructing or adding to a building in a Development Permit Area, covering design, landscaping, siting, parking and finishes. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- The current building-permit list shows the form still being built at scale: a 142-unit, 3-storey townhouse project at 1395 Pollard Street on Burke Mountain, in 24 buildings, and 16 townhouses in 3 blocks at 3469 Baycrest Avenue, both with applications dated August 2026. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/193/Current-Multiple-Family-Commercial-Industrial-and-Institutional-Building-Permit-Applications-PDF
- Older infill townhomes arrive one rezoning at a time: at 586 Thompson Avenue the City's notice proposed rezoning from RS-1 One-Family Residential to RT-2 Townhouse Residential for three 3-storey townhouse buildings holding nine units. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/13815/Notice-of-Zoning-Amendment---586-Thompson-Avenue?bidId

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Townhouse zoning is RT-2 Townhouse Residential under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 — the City's own land-sale document for 3596 Innes Court confirms that site is zoned RT-2 for townhouse development. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15590/25-012-RFO---Land-Sale---Townhouse-Development-Site-at-3596-Innes-Crt?bidId
- One zone is in flux: the City states the R-4 Intensive Small-Scale Residential zone will be reviewed as part of its upcoming Townhouse and Rowhouse Review, so R-4 townhouse-form rules may change — check current. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId
- Creek-side townhouse sites can also sit in a Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area, where a permit is required before developing, subdividing, constructing or altering land. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- City-wide small-scale multi-unit housing rules are in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note and are not repeated here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Are Coquitlam townhomes strata or freehold? **A:** Both forms exist, and the title decides. The City's small-scale guide notes dwelling units can be stratified and sold or kept unstratified as rentals, and townhouse complexes built under RT-2 are typically strata; a fee-simple rowhouse has no strata corporation. Read the title, the strata plan if any, and the Form B before assuming which one a listing is. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId
- **Q:** What should I check on a Burke Mountain townhouse near a creek? **A:** Check the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area first. Coquitlam requires that permit before land in the area is developed or altered, and Burke Mountain townhouse projects are being approved alongside creek corridors — the Partington Creek projects secure a 50-metre wildlife corridor, widening to 100 metres at a wildlife hot spot. Setbacks and common-property drainage are strata-document checks. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Q:** Can a townhouse complex here be redeveloped? **A:** Possibly, but two reviews govern it. Townhouses need Form and Character Development Permit compliance for any rebuild form, and the City is reviewing its townhouse and rowhouse framework, including the R-4 zone, so today's density is not a guaranteed future density. Confirm current zoning on the City's mapping and the Official Community Plan (OCP) designation for the complex address. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Q:** Which SkyTrain station do most Coquitlam townhouse areas feed? **A:** Coquitlam Central Station is the hub the northern bus routes feed. Routes 185 and 186 both run from Coquitlam Central Station into the Lansdowne Drive and David Avenue neighbourhoods, and the station is served by the Millennium Line Evergreen Extension and the West Coast Express. Burke Mountain townhouses are bus-fed to that station, not station-adjacent; Burquitlam Station serves the west side. http://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/192/tt185.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The City sells townhouse land itself: its 2025 request for offers covered a 3.60-acre RT-2 townhouse site at 3596 Innes Court on Burke Mountain, bounded by Mitchell Street and Brownlee Avenue, beside a future park of about 2.0 acres. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15590/25-012-RFO---Land-Sale---Townhouse-Development-Site-at-3596-Innes-Crt?bidId
- Waste rules are written into approvals: a 2021 zoning text amendment updated Section 509 of the bylaw to add waste-collection requirements for townhouse and rowhouse developments, so garage and bin storage is a bylaw matter in newer complexes. https://www.coquitlam.ca/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Minutes/_03152021-510
- School catchment is by permanent home address in School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) (SD43); being near a school named for the neighbourhood is not an assignment — run the townhouse address in the SD43 School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check creek proximity on any Burke Mountain or ravine-edge complex: the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area layer shows where alteration needs a permit, and strata depreciation reports are where retaining walls and drainage show up for existing complexes. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- Check slope on the City's environmental development permit mapping, which also maps unstable slopes for the City Centre and Northeast areas; the map does not give a lot-level geotechnical conclusion. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- Check the zoning in force at purchase, not at listing: the Townhouse and Rowhouse Review, including the R-4 zone, is an open City project, so townhouse-form rules are a check-current item. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId
