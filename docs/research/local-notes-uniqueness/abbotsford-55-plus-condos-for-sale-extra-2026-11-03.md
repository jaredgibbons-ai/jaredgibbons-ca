## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A 55+ condo here is governed by a strata age bylaw: Strata Property Act sections 123.1 and 123.2 permit a restriction to age 55 or older only, and any age bylaw below 55 — including older 45+ wording still found in stale building directories for Abbotsford towers — has been invalid since November 24, 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- The bylaw restricts who may live in the unit, not who may own it, and Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse or partner, and children in the defined care cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, section 140.3.8, permits one secondary suite in a single detached dwelling subject to its lot and frontage criteria — the suite route is a house rule, not a condo rule, and no condo buyer can add a suite inside a strata lot on that basis. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes
- A life lease such as Cascade Court has no Form B, no depreciation report and no strata council: an up-front capital payment buys lifetime occupancy, monthly charges apply, and the operator redeems the lease on departure under its own terms, which vary by operator. https://gardenparktower.ca/life-lease

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Where in Abbotsford are the 55+ condos, in one sentence? **A:** They are in Abbotsford West and Central Abbotsford, not in Abbotsford East. The western cluster runs along Old Yale Road and Tims Avenue around Evergreen Village, Elmwood Manor and Royal Court, and the central cluster sits around Mill Lake on Gladwin Road, Bourquin Crescent and George Ferguson Way. Abbotsford East's only verified 55+ product in the settled roster is the Cascade Court life lease, which is a different tenure. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes
- **Q:** A directory calls a tower 45+. Can it still enforce that? **A:** No. Since November 24, 2022 the only age a British Columbia strata bylaw may set is 55 or older, and every 19+, 40+ or 45+ bylaw became invalid that day. Stale directory entries for Abbotsford buildings still showing a lower age are pre-2022 wording; the registered bylaw and the Form B for the specific unit are the documents that state the current rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** How is Cascade Court's life lease different from buying a condo? **A:** A life lease buys occupancy, not title. The standard British Columbia model described by a life lease operator is an up-front capital payment for lifetime occupancy, monthly maintenance charges, and redemption by the operator on departure under the lease terms. There is no strata lot to resell on the open market, no Form B and no depreciation report, so the lease document itself is the due diligence. https://gardenparktower.ca/life-lease
- **Q:** Where is care if a condo owner needs support later? **A:** British Columbia's Assisted Living Registry lists Abbotsford residences including Bevan Village at 33386 Bevan Avenue, and the Menno Place campus in Central Abbotsford provides assisted living and care through its registered buildings — all rental or care admission, not a purchase, and separate from the strata condos in section 2. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3AAbbotsford&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check dyking and drainage exposure before treating two Abbotsford condos as equivalent: the City levies separate Matsqui Prairie and Sumas Prairie dyking taxes under its tax bylaw, and its strategic work includes a Flood Mitigation Strategy — the prairie-floor areas and the Mill Lake upland are different risk files. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf
- For any roster building, the registered age bylaw, pet rule and fee inclusions come from that building's Form B and registered bylaws; no strata own page stating them for these buildings was read in this pass, and stale directories still carry pre-2022 age wording. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
