# Vedder Crossing Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: vedder-crossing-guide (Vedder Crossing; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Vedder Crossing takes its name from a bridge: the Province of BC uses Vedder Crossing Bridge, where Vedder Road crosses the river, as the dividing point between the Chilliwack River upstream of the bridge and the Vedder River between the bridge and the Vedder Canal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/flood-projects/vedder-canal
- The City does not publish an MLS-style boundary for Vedder Crossing: the closest official description is the South Vedder Neighbourhood Plan area, bordered by the Chilliwack/Vedder River to the south, the Promontory neighbourhood to the southeast, Tzeachten First Nations Lands to the east, Vedder Road to the west and the broader Vedder Neighbourhood to the north. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2975
- The South Vedder Neighbourhood Plan was adopted by Council in 2022 and updated in 2025 as part of the Official Community Plan update, and the City describes South Vedder as part of the broader Vedder Neighbourhood at the south end of Chilliwack. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2975
- Vedder Park, on the river at 45450 Petawawa Road, sits on the paved Legacy Trail, connects to the Garrison Crossing community to the north, and is within walking distance of the University of the Fraser Valley (UFV) Chilliwack campus. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=144

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The guide-level stock is planned and newer rather than an old townsite grid: the housing, campus and commercial blocks around Vedder Road were built on the former Canadian Forces Base Chilliwack lands, and the street-level treatment of that redevelopment is in the vedder-crossing note — this guide note covers the river, trail and plan frame. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/canada-education-park/
- The plan for the corridor's next buildings is set by the South Vedder Neighbourhood Plan: ground-level commercial buildings of 4 storeys, mixed commercial-residential buildings of 6 storeys, and residential buildings of 3 to 6 storeys along the Vedder Road corridor. https://www.engagechilliwack.com/southvedder/widgets/95165/faqs

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The river sets the building rule here: the City states there are floodplain areas in the city for both the Fraser River and the Vedder River, and the provincial dike standard and floodplain mapping for the Vedder River are based on the 1-in-200-year flood event. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1912
- New building near the river is governed by Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519, which sets flood construction levels for the Vedder River and Vedder Canal floodplains — the bylaw's full residential treatment is in the chilliwack-proper-west-guide note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- Land use on the east side of Vedder Road follows the South Vedder Neighbourhood Plan, Schedule B-6 of Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — the plan's corridor heights are in section 2. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6734
- City-wide suite, zoning and small-scale multi-unit rules are written in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note and are not repeated here. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Why does the river change its name at the bridge? **A:** Because the Province draws the line at Vedder Crossing Bridge: the watercourse is generally called the Chilliwack River upstream of the bridge, and the Vedder River between the Vedder Canal and Vedder Crossing Bridge. The distinction matters when a buyer reads flood documents, because City and provincial pages use both names for the same river system on either side of the bridge. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/flood-projects/vedder-canal
- **Q:** Is there riverside trail access from the neighbourhood? **A:** Yes: Vedder Park sits on the Vedder Greenway and Rotary Trail along the river, and the park itself offers dog off-leash areas totalling nearly a hectare, washrooms, a playground and a pump track at 45450 Petawawa Road. The City posts trail closure notices for flood-recovery work on its Vedder Trail page, so trail access at a given address can be interrupted by river work even in a normal year. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=144 https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=37&prshow=details&prID=577
- **Q:** Is the Vedder River a flood risk for these homes? **A:** The river is a managed flood system, not an unmanaged one: the City states the Vedder River floodplain mapping and dike standard are based on the 1-in-200-year flood event, and its flood protection work includes raising the West Dike (right bank) by approximately one metre, designed for that event with 0.75 metres of freeboard. An individual home's protection still depends on its own flood construction level under Bylaw No. 4519, which is a building-file check. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1912 https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1372
- **Q:** Which schools serve Vedder Crossing? **A:** The Vedder-side schools are Vedder Elementary at 45850 Promontory Road for Kindergarten to Grade 5, Vedder Middle School at 45560 South Sumas Road for Grades 6 to 8, and G.W. Graham Secondary at 45955 Thomas Road for Grades 9 to 12, with Watson Elementary at 45305 Watson Road also on the south side. SD33 assigns by street-address catchment, so confirm the exact address with the district rather than from the school's proximity. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/033
- **Q:** How do I get downtown or to Metro Vancouver without driving? **A:** BC Transit Route 51 Vedder is the corridor's Frequent Transit Network route, running between the UFV Chilliwack Campus and Downtown Chilliwack via Vedder Road, with timepoints at Vedder Road at Promontory Road and Vedder Road at Luckakuck Way. From the Downtown Chilliwack exchange, Route 66 Fraser Valley Express runs to Abbotsford, Langley and Lougheed Town Centre Station, and it gained more trips on all days of the week from January 4, 2026. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=51 https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The river fills with sediment and the City removes it as flood work: the City states sediment removal from the Vedder River restores the river's capacity to contain and convey floodwater, and that the November 2021 event deposited 10 times the typical annual amount of sediment. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=37&prshow=details&prID=577
- The Province puts a number on that event: the November 2021 atmospheric river deposited 440,000 cubic metres of sediment in the Vedder River between Vedder Crossing and the Highway 1 bridge, reducing flood conveyance capacity. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/flood-projects/vedder-canal
- Vedder flood timing is the opposite of the Fraser River's: the Province states Vedder River and Canal flood flows are typically governed by fall and winter storm events rather than freshet. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/flood-projects/vedder-canal
- There is a named river gauge for this crossing: a Water Survey of Canada gauge named "Chilliwack River Near Vedder Crossing" is listed on the City's Vedder River Flood Protection page. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2055
- Qoqó:lem Park at 45076 Vedder Mountain Road is the second river-side park on this side, named in a City press release alongside the Vedder Park works. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=37&prshow=details&prID=591
- The Vedder Park site itself is leftover base land: the City states the site was previously used as a military base, and Garrison Crossing is the community to its north. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=144
- The Cheam Leisure Centre at 45501 Market Way is the recreation centre for this side of the city, in Sardis north of Vedder Crossing, and there is no Fraser Valley Regional Library branch in Vedder Crossing itself — the Sardis Library at 5819 Tyson Road is the south-side branch. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/sardis-library
- Route 56 Cultus Lake is a summer-only service (July 1 to Labour Day) and stops on Vedder Road at Watson Road, so it is not a year-round option for a car-free household here. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=56

STATUS: City flood protection program as described on the City's page, read 2026-11-03. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1372

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Vedder Crossing is in the City of Chilliwack, whose 2026 residential property-tax rate is 4.66727 per $1,000 of assessed value in total — see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note for the full breakdown, utility charges and billing cycle. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2527/Tax%5FRates%5Ffor%5Fthe%5FWeb%2Dmou9gntw%5Fc%2Epdf

- City utility and solid waste charges here are billed quarterly, under the same City bylaws as the rest of Chilliwack. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 51 Vedder is the local bus and the system's Frequent Transit Network route: it runs between the UFV Chilliwack Campus and Downtown Chilliwack via Vedder Road, through the middle of this neighbourhood. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=51
- BC Transit added later weekday and weekend service and more frequent Saturday-evening service on Route 51 from January 4, 2026. https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/
- The system's other south-side routes also run from this corridor: Route 54 Promontory, Route 55 Yarrow-Greendale and Route 58 Tyson all serve the Sardis and Vedder side of the city. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/2024-05-21-Chilliwack-and-FVRD-TFAP_final.pdf
- BC Transit's Chilliwack Rider's Guide map labels the "VEDDER CROSSING" stop area at the south end of Vedder Road, at the river crossing itself. https://lite.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/chw_rg_june2026-1.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the dike and sediment record, not the map alone: the Province states that high water levels and inadequate dike freeboard could compromise the protection the Vedder River dikes offer, which is why the dike-raising and sediment-removal program in section 6 exists. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/flood-projects/vedder-canal
- Check any lot near the river against Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519 and its floodplain mapping for the Vedder River and Vedder Canal, and confirm the parcel's flood construction level with the City. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- River work can close the amenity a buyer is paying for: the City posts Vedder Trail closure notices for flood-recovery work, so current trail status is a check at offer time, not a permanent feature. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=37&prshow=details&prID=577
- What the pages read do not tell a buyer: the flood construction level an existing Vedder Crossing home was actually built to — that is in the property's building file, not on any neighbourhood or flood-program page.
