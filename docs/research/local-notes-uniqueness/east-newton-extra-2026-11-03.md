# East Newton Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: east-newton (East Newton, Surrey; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- East Newton is the Chimney Hill ground east of 144 Street, north of 64 Avenue and Hyland Creek, and south of Bear Creek. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- It splits into three plan areas: East Newton North, East Newton South and East Newton Business Park, with the two residential plans split by 72 Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- The Business Park sits east of 152 Street and north of 64 Avenue, and the Chimney Hill residential estate sits at the northeast. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- This is a largely built-out planned suburb, not a town centre: the City describes the three plan areas as now largely built out. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- Schools the City names in the area are Chimney Hill Elementary, T.E. Scott Elementary and Regent Christian Academy, alongside Valley View Cemetery. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- Chimney Hill Elementary is at 14755 74 Avenue and T.E. Scott Elementary is at 7079 148 Street, per the British Columbia Ministry school list for Surrey School District 36 (SD36). https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036

- For Surrey-wide zoning, suites, small-scale multi-unit housing and coach houses, and for Newton-wide facts shared with West Newton, see the west-newton note, where those rules are stated in full.
- The local rule is plan currency: East Newton North was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) update, and the South and Business Park designations were superseded by it, so the OCP now guides development. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- A 2020 amendment removed the Business Park's live-work designation south of 66 Avenue and replaced it with business park designation, reinforcing employment-only use. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is there an East Newton Elementary? **A:** No, there is no school by that name. The British Columbia Ministry school list for SD36 names Chimney Hill Elementary at 14755 74 Avenue for this ground, and no East Newton Elementary appears in the Ministry file or the district directory. Do not rely on any listing or page that names one; check an address in the SD36 School Locator instead. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
**Q:** Is there a secondary school inside East Newton? **A:** No secondary school inside the plan area is confirmed in this note. The nearest named secondary in district planning, Tamanawis Secondary at 12600 66 Avenue, sits west of 144 Street, outside the East Newton boundary. SD36 assigns students by exact home address, not by plan boundary, so confirm any specific address in the district School Locator before buying for a school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
**Q:** What is McLeod Road Traditional? **A:** McLeod Road Traditional is the correct name of the school at 6325 142 Street, west of 144 Street at the area's edge; it is a traditional program school, not a school named McLeod Road Elementary. The district consulted in 2024 on converting its three traditional schools to regular catchment schools, so its admission basis should be checked with SD36 directly. https://www.surreyschools.ca/surreytraditional/_ci/p/178598
**Q:** Can I rely on the old East Newton plan maps for a building plan? **A:** No, the City states the three East Newton plans are largely built out and their land-use designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update, with East Newton North rescinded outright. Read the current OCP designation for the lot, and use the old Neighbourhood Concept Plan (NCP) maps only as history of how the subdivision was laid out. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Chimney Hill is the local name and East Newton is the City planning name for the same ground. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- Valley View Cemetery sits inside the plan area, named by the City alongside the area's schools. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- A new neighbourhood park, Woodward Hill Park at 6083 144 Street, is in planning and design on the City's park projects list, at the area's west edge. http://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/park-projects

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Full entry for this cluster: the City is widening 72 Avenue from 144 Street to 152 Street, the main east-west route through East Newton, as part of making 72 Avenue a continuous route from 120 Street to 196 Street. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/capital-projects/72-avenue-improvements-extensions?check_logged_in=1
- The City lists no ongoing land-use plan for Newton, so no new neighbourhood plan is in progress for this area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Newton Exchange, west of King George Boulevard, is the exchange for Newton, confirmed by TransLink as the terminus of the R1 King George Boulevard and R6 Scott Road RapidBus routes. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
- 72 Avenue is the through-route, and the City plans it as a continuous route from 120 Street to 196 Street. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/capital-projects/72-avenue-improvements-extensions?check_logged_in=1
- No SkyTrain station is in East Newton; the Surrey–Langley extension stations run on Fraser Highway, north of this area. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check lots on the north and south edges against creek requirements: Bear Creek bounds the area north and Hyland Creek bounds it south. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- Do not rely on a rescinded or superseded NCP map for permitted use; the City states the OCP now leads development in all three plan areas. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
