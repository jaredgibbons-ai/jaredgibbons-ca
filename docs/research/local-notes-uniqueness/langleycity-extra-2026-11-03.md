## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The City of Langley is a separate municipality from the Township of Langley, with its own council, Official Community Plan (OCP) and zoning bylaw — a Township rule never governs a City lot. https://www.langleycity.ca/city-hall/reports-plans-strategies/strategic-vision
- This page is the City's land-use page, not a listing feed: it covers OCP maps, zoning and development direction for the City only, and points to the separate homes page for inventory. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langleycity.html
- City Hall sits at 20399 Douglas Crescent, the civic anchor for every plan, hearing and tax question in this note. https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes
- Fraser Highway runs through the City as its main east–west corridor, and the future SkyTrain terminus is planned at 203 Street in Langley City Centre. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/project-overview

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The plan frame, not the feed, shapes this page's stock story: the OCP sets where apartment, townhouse and plex-home growth goes, and the zoning bylaw turns that into buildable rules lot by lot. https://www.langleycity.ca/index.php/business-development/planning-zoning/official-community-plan

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The OCP is Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 3200, given final adoption on November 22, 2021, and built on the Nexus of Community vision adopted July 23, 2018 for the next 25-plus years. https://www.langleycity.ca/index.php/business-development/planning-zoning/official-community-plan
- A new Zoning Bylaw No. 3300 replaces Zoning Bylaw No. 2100, adopted in 1996; the City's OCP page states the new bylaw was adopted in March 2026 — check current. https://www.langleycity.ca/index.php/business-development/planning-zoning/official-community-plan
- OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3319 was brought forward with Bylaw No. 3300 to keep the OCP consistent with the zoning bylaw, align Transit-Oriented Areas (TOAs) around SkyTrain stations, and add the Townhomes and Plexhome Best Practices Guide to the Development Permit Area guidelines. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/public-hearing-noticepdf
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH): Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024, permits 4 units on RS1 and RS2 lots, and 6 units within 400 metres of a frequent bus stop. https://www.langleycity.ca/city-hall/reports-plans-strategies/strategic-vision
- Secondary suites under Bylaw No. 3300: a suite may not exceed the lesser of 100 square metres and 40 percent of the building's gross floor area, the registered owner must live on the lot, one extra off-street parking space is required, and the suite must be permitted, inspected and entered in the City's secondary suites registry. https://www.langleycity.ca/city-hall/reports-plans-strategies/strategic-vision
- Garden suites and carriage homes in the R1 zone under Bylaw No. 3300 are capped at 100 square metres of ground floor area, with total accessory building floor area on an R1 lot capped at 60 square metres and owner occupancy required. https://www.langleycity.ca/city-hall/reports-plans-strategies/strategic-vision

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is the City of Langley the same as the Township of Langley? **A:** No. The City is its own municipality with its own council, its OCP Bylaw No. 3200 adopted November 22, 2021, and its own Zoning Bylaw No. 3300. The Township surrounds it and runs on different bylaws, tax notices and plans, so confirm which municipality a listing address sits in before using any rule or rate from either one. https://www.langleycity.ca/index.php/business-development/planning-zoning/official-community-plan
- **Q:** What does the OCP mean for a house I buy today? **A:** The OCP sets the long-term land-use direction for housing, streets, parks and neighbourhoods, while zoning sets what is allowed today. A lot's OCP designation can support a different future use than its current house, especially near downtown and the planned 203 Street station, so read both documents before assuming today's use is the ceiling. https://www.langleycity.ca/index.php/business-development/planning-zoning/official-community-plan
- **Q:** How many homes can go on a single-detached lot? **A:** Up to four units on RS1 and RS2 lots under Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024, and up to six units where the lot is within 400 metres of a frequent bus stop. That is a zoning permission, not a building permit: servicing, parking and Development Permit Area rules still apply to the specific lot. https://www.langleycity.ca/city-hall/reports-plans-strategies/strategic-vision
- **Q:** Is a suite in a City house automatically legal? **A:** No. Under Bylaw No. 3300 a legal suite stays under the lesser of 100 square metres and 40 percent of the building's floor area, the registered owner lives on the lot, an extra off-street parking space is provided, and the suite is permitted, inspected and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. Ask for the registry record, not just the listing wording. https://www.langleycity.ca/city-hall/reports-plans-strategies/strategic-vision
- **Q:** Why is there a second OCP amendment with the new zoning bylaw? **A:** Because the Local Government Act requires the OCP and zoning to stay consistent. Amendment Bylaw No. 3319 updates land use for the provincial Transit-Oriented Area rules around stations, aligns the plan with provincial housing legislation, and brings the Townhomes and Plexhome Best Practices Guide into the Development Permit Area guidelines. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/public-hearing-noticepdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The vision behind the plan has a name and a date: Nexus of Community, adopted July 23, 2018, is the document the 2021 OCP implements. https://www.langleycity.ca/city-hall/reports-plans-strategies/strategic-vision
- The OCP carries two appendices a buyer may meet in a disclosure: Appendix A, the Nicomekl River District Neighbourhood Plan, and Appendix B, District Policies. https://www.langleycity.ca/index.php/business-development/planning-zoning/official-community-plan
- Provincial Transit-Oriented Area rules limit the City's ability to set minimum residential parking in areas covered by the station-area tiers, which is why parking counts differ by block — check current. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/public-hearing-noticepdf
- Langley Memorial Hospital at 22051 Fraser Highway serves both Langleys but sits in the Township, outside the City boundary. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital
- Credit-card payment of City property taxes carries a 2.2 percent processing fee on the City's payment page. https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The City's total residential tax rate for 2026 is $4.2067 per $1,000 of taxable value, of which the City general rate is $2.5937 per $1,000, with school, TransLink, Metro Vancouver and other levies making up the rest. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/2026taxrates
- Property tax payments received after the July 2 due date incur a penalty; the City page read states a penalty applies but does not state the percentage. https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes
- City water charges in the 2026 rate sheet combine a $75.00 annual flat fee with consumption charged per cubic metre; sewer and solid waste are billed under the same City utility structure — see the rate sheet for the current figures. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/2026taxrates
- Whether the Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies is set by the Province's taxable-area list; Langley City buyers should confirm the property's status on the provincial page for the declaration year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- There is no SkyTrain station in the City today; the Surrey Langley SkyTrain project extends the Expo Line 16 kilometres on an elevated guideway from King George Station to 203 Street in Langley City. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/project-overview
- The extension includes eight stations and three transit exchanges, at Bakerview–166 Street, Willowbrook and Langley City Centre. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/project-overview
- The City's own vision page states SkyTrain is coming to Langley City by the end of 2029 — the project page's current wording is late 2029. https://www.langleycity.ca/city-hall/reports-plans-strategies/strategic-vision
- Current bus route numbers and frequencies for the City: see the langley-city note; confirm any route on TransLink before relying on it.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the lot against both documents: the OCP designation in Bylaw No. 3200 and the zone in Bylaw No. 3300, because the November 2025 amendment package changed land use on some parcels for Transit-Oriented Area consistency — check current. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/public-hearing-noticepdf
- The Nicomekl River District is a named plan area inside the OCP (Appendix A), so river-adjacent lots carry a second layer of policy beyond the base zone. https://www.langleycity.ca/index.php/business-development/planning-zoning/official-community-plan
- A flood, dike or geotechnical layer specific to a City lot was not stated on the City pages read for this note; confirm the parcel in person with Development Services at 20399 Douglas Crescent. https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes
