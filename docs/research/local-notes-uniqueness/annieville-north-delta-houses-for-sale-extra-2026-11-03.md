## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Annieville is the riverfront end of North Delta: River Road runs along the Fraser River through its industrial strips and the Annieville houses, then meets Nordel Way for Highway 91 access. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90186/
- 112 Street is the neighbourhood spine: Annieville Elementary is at 9240 112 Street and Annieville Lions Park is at 9150 112 Street, a few doors apart. https://letstalk.delta.ca/annievillelionspark
- River Road at Centre Street is gated to general traffic: City material says the gate allows bus, emergency vehicle, bike and pedestrian traffic only, so through-drivers use Nordel Way. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90186/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The River Road gate shapes daily life: residents heading to the Alex Fraser Bridge, Highway 91 or Tilbury must use Nordel Way, because River Road at Centre Street is closed to general traffic. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90186/
- Delta's OCP includes a Riverside Development Permit Area (Map IID), so riverfront-adjacent work in North Delta can need a development permit on top of zoning. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Which elementary school serves an Annieville house?
**A:** Annieville Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 7, at 9240 112 Street, is the school named for the neighbourhood, and it also hosts Delta's Home Quest provincial online school on its bottom floor. Because SD37 enrols by catchment and moves students when a school fills, run the exact address in the SD37 School Locator rather than assuming from 112 Street alone. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/programs/home-quest/

**Q:** Why is River Road blocked at Centre Street?
**A:** The City planned the gate so regional traffic would leave River Road once the South Fraser Perimeter Road opened: the gate passes buses, emergency vehicles, bikes and pedestrians only. Annieville and Sunbury residents were consulted in 2005 to 2007, and the City record states drivers bound for the Alex Fraser Bridge must use Nordel Way instead. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90186/

**Q:** What is happening at Annieville Lions Park?
**A:** Council approved the final Annieville Lions Park Master Plan on December 8, 2025, with detailed design next and construction planned for fall 2026. The plan adds a barrier-free path network linking 112 Street to 114 Street, a fenced spray park, a fieldhouse and a multi-use games area, and the 2026 Financial Plan carries a $7 million budget for the project. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-exciting-updates-approved-annieville-lions-park

**Q:** How big is Annieville Lions Park, and what is in it now?
**A:** Just over 33,000 square metres (m2), beside Annieville Elementary at 9150 112 Street and about one kilometre from the Scott Road Corridor. The City lists a spray park, Bob Bunnett Field and ball diamonds among its current features, and notes the splash pad and washrooms are reaching the end of their lifespan, which is why they are being replaced. https://letstalk.delta.ca/annievillelionspark

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Nordel Way is the commuter route: City material directs Annieville drivers bound for the Alex Fraser Bridge, Highway 91 or Tilbury Park to Nordel Way because of the River Road gate. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90186/
- River Road still works for local trips on foot or bike: the Centre Street gate passes pedestrians and cyclists, so the riverfront stays reachable without a car. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90186/
- The South Fraser Perimeter Road (Highway 17) is the reason River Road changed: the City record ties the gate decision to regional traffic moving to that freeway when it opened. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90186/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the dike and drainage file for River Road lots: Delta maintains over 63 kilometres (km) of flood protection infrastructure city-wide, and Annieville sits on the Fraser River bank behind that system. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Check which side of the Centre Street gate a house sits on before assuming a River Road commute; the wrong side adds a Nordel Way loop to every bridge trip. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90186/
- Check the OCP Riverside Development Permit Area map (Map IID) for any lot near the river before planning an addition or rebuild. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
