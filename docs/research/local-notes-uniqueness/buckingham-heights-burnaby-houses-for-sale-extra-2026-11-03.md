# Buckingham Heights Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: buckingham-heights-burnaby-houses-for-sale (Buckingham Heights; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Buckingham Heights is a detached-house slope east and south-east of Deer Lake in south-central Burnaby. Its core streets, Buckingham Drive and Buckingham Avenue, surround Buckingham Elementary School at 6066 Buckingham Avenue, between Deer Lake Park and Royal Oak Avenue. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- Deer Lake and Deer Lake Park lie to the north-west and Upper Deer Lake lies to the south-east. Canada Way runs along the north side and Sperling Avenue runs down the park's east side toward the lake. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- The board treats Buckingham Heights as its own sub-area, separate from Deer Lake, Deer Lake Place and Upper Deer Lake. No City of Burnaby community plan using the Buckingham Heights name was confirmed in the sources read. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is inside Buckingham Heights? **A:** Buckingham Elementary is the school inside the sub-area, at 6066 Buckingham Avenue. It is a Kindergarten to Grade 7 school in School District 41 (SD41). Catchments are still address-based, so confirm the exact house address in the SD41 locator before relying on this school in an offer. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** Which secondary schools are nearest? **A:** Burnaby Central Secondary and Moscrop Secondary are the nearest secondaries named in the research. Burnaby Central is at 6011 Deer Lake Parkway, north of the lake, and Moscrop Secondary is at 4433 Moscrop Street, to the west. Which one serves a given Buckingham address is a locator check, not a neighbourhood assumption. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** Are there condos or townhomes in Buckingham Heights? **A:** No attached stock was identified for this sub-area in the research; it reads as a detached, freehold slope. A buyer who needs a strata home should look at the neighbouring sub-areas instead of waiting for attached listings here. Any new attached project would need a City application source, and none inside the sub-area was confirmed. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- **Q:** What happens at Deer Lake Park through the year? **A:** Deer Lake Park hosts the City's returning 2026 special events on the lawn beside the lake. The City lists Burnaby Blooms on May 3, Symphony and Opera in the Park on July 18 to 19, and the Burnaby Blues and Roots Festival on August 8, with the Haunted Village at Burnaby Village Museum in late October. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2026-04-17/burnaby-unveils-2026-civic-special-events-program

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Deer Lake boat-launch boardwalk is closed for a full replacement until winter 2027. The City gives a signed detour via Sperling Avenue and Deer Lake Drive, adding about five minutes of walking, and a small section of the nearby picnic area is also closed during the work. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- Deer Lake Park is open dawn to dusk year-round, and the City calls it the cultural centre of the city. Its trails connect the lake, Burnaby Art Gallery, Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, Burnaby Village Museum and Hart House restaurant from the park address at 6450 Deer Lake Avenue. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- No City recreation centre sits inside Buckingham Heights in the sources read. The library branch for this quadrant is the Bob Prittie Metrotown Branch, south-west at Metrotown, one of four Burnaby Public Library branches, one in each quadrant. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/city-departments/burnaby-public-library
- Burnaby City Hall is at 4949 Canada Way, north-west of the slope at Deer Lake. Permit, tax and grant business for a Buckingham house is handled there, not at a south Burnaby office. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-09/Notice-of-Election-by-Voting-2026-10-01.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Citywide Burnaby tax and utility figures are carried in full in the montecito-burnaby-houses-for-sale note — see that note. The penalty rule matters on a high-value detached purchase: a 5 percent penalty applies to outstanding current taxes and to an unclaimed home owner grant after the July due date, and the City states it has no authority to waive or modify the penalty, even for a payment one day late. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- The home owner grant must be claimed by the property tax July due date to avoid penalty. The City notes financial institutions no longer accept grant applications, so a new owner claims online or by phone directly. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/how-to-pay
