## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Township frame, in full here: the Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs land use, and the Township's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) bylaw is Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024, with no 6-plex tier — SSMUH creates no added-unit right inside a manufactured home park, and a pad tenancy is not a fee-simple lot. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- Township servicing is confirmed under Township Bylaw No. 5382 in the program's verified ledger — whether a specific Brookswood park is on municipal sewer or on a private system is a park-documents question, not stated for either park in any source read. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- Assignment in a Township pad park: selling the home assigns the existing pad tenancy to the buyer — it is not a new tenancy. The seller must give the proposed buyer the written tenancy agreement and park rules first, assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB (Residential Tenancy Branch) Form 10, silence by the end of the 10th day after the request is received is deemed consent, and no fee may be charged for consent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- Pad-rent frame in brief: one increase every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped at the inflation rate plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated park utilities — 2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount, percentages only. For the full pad-rent cost rules, see the cost-of-mobile-home-pad-rental-fees-in-langley note. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- Park rules in a Township park are part of the tenancy agreement: a landlord may change them only once a year with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough to follow, and does not change a material term of the agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Cedar Creek Estates protected by the community plan? **A:** Its site at 3031 200 Street is designated Manufactured Home Park in the Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan itself, which is a stronger paper position than a park that exists only under zoning. A designation is not a guarantee — a plan can be amended — but any redevelopment of this site must deal with that designation, the Township process, and provincial closure law together. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/brookswoodfernridge-community-plan-adopted.aspx
- **Q:** Can the park refuse my purchase of a home at Fernridge Park? **A:** The park owner must consent to the assignment of the pad tenancy, in writing, before you become the tenant. Ask early: the request goes on RTB Form 10, the owner has until the 10th day after receiving it to respond, silence is deemed consent, and on assignment you take the tenancy on the same terms and the same pad rent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q:** Are Brookswood's parks really 55+? **A:** Both verified parks — Cedar Creek Estates on 200 Street and Fernridge Park on 24 Avenue — are carried as 55+ on the settled roster, but that position is listing-stated in their home files, not read on a Township or operator page. In a pad park the age rule lives in the park rules and the assignment consent, not in a strata bylaw, so get the current written park rules before writing an offer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Q:** What happens if a Brookswood park closes for redevelopment? **A:** Provincially, the owner must first have all necessary permits and approvals, then give 12 months' notice and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may leave early on at least 10 days' written notice. Whether the Township adds its own relocation conditions through a rezoning is an application-by-application question — no Township manufactured-home-park policy was read on 2026-11-03. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q:** Do I pay tax on the land under my Brookswood park home? **A:** No. The Province assesses a manufactured home like other residential property but excludes the land value when the home sits in a manufactured home park on land owned by someone else. The home must be a principal residence in the registered owner's name to qualify for the Home Owner Grant, claimed each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Park-status check: confirm each Brookswood park's zoning under Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 and search the Township's development applications page for the park's civic address before buying — zoning and the plan designation describe the land, not the park owner's plans, and neither tells you the current pad rent or park rules. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Manufactured Home Registry check: a manufactured home sale is effective only when registered in the provincial Manufactured Home Registry through a lawyer or notary, with the original signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the home's CSA (Canadian Standards Association) label (Z-240 mobile, A-277 modular) must be attached and legible. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- Watercourse check: the Township directs applicants to check a property's Agricultural Land Reserve designation and watercourses in its GeoSource mapping before applying — use the same lookup for a Brookswood lot near Murray Creek or the Little Campbell River system; the map shows designations, not a home's drainage history. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Closure-risk check: read the RTB end-of-tenancy page before any Brookswood pad purchase — permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation, plus an added-compensation route assessed on the home's value where the RTB conditions are met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
