# River Springs Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: river-springs-coquitlam (River Springs; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- River Springs is a river pocket on Shaughnessy Street on the east bank of the Coquitlam River in northeast Coquitlam, with David Avenue on its south side and the Coquitlam River and Coquitlam River Park on its west, and the City publishes no official boundary for the sub-area name. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Coquitlam-River-Park-28
- Hockaday Park, on the river at the north end of the pocket, is the City page that names the River Springs area directly and describes its riverfront setting. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/hockadaypark-46
- The City's instream-works program names this reach precisely: "Coquitlam River at Oxbow, River Springs" and "Coquitlam River at Hockaday Park" are two separate listed work sites on this pocket's riverfront. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/14428/25-026-RFP---Field-Monitoring-of-Instream-Works?bidId=

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The houses on the Shaughnessy Street pocket are the stock here, and their documented history is in the street names: the City records that several River Springs streets were named after 1930s Hollywood visitors to the Steelhead Lodge fishing lodge on this riverfront — in full in the river-springs-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/hockadaypark-46
- No City, School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) or BC Assessment public page read states a build decade, lot size, subdivision name or developer for the wider neighbourhood, so none is claimed; the pocket is an existing river neighbourhood under the Northeast Coquitlam Area Plan, not a Burke Mountain neighbourhood plan area. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/4149/How-Development-Happens-PDF

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What is Hockaday Park, at the north end of River Springs? **A:** Hockaday Park is a riverfront City park on the Coquitlam River with a viewing platform, a small sand beach and interpretive signage, and a playground built into the natural terrain with climbing structures, swings and open grass. It is the park the City uses to tell this pocket's history, including the 1930s Steelhead Lodge era on the Oxbow Ranch site. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/hockadaypark-46
- **Q:** Why do River Springs streets have Hollywood names? **A:** The City states that in the 1930s Karl Jacobs and actress Clara Guiol bought the Oxbow Ranch, renamed it Steelhead Lodge and built cabins as a fishing lodge and Hollywood hideaway, rumoured to have been visited by film stars of the era. Several streets in the River Springs area were named after those famous visitors, which is why the street names here differ from Burke Mountain's. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/hockadaypark-46
- **Q:** How big is the river parkland beside these houses? **A:** Coquitlam River Park, at 1190 Shaughnessy Street, holds a network of forest walking trails extending 9.8 kilometres within 165 acres of municipal parkland along the Coquitlam River — in full in the river-springs-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. Hockaday Park at the north end adds the viewing platform, sand beach and playground on the same riverfront. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Coquitlam-River-Park-28
- **Q:** Does the river flood or change beside River Springs? **A:** The City states the Coquitlam River is at its highest and most dynamic from November to March, and its flood levels are designed on the 200-year design flood — both in full in the river-springs-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. The City also recorded trail erosion repairs on the river's east side below David Place in August 2026, so the bank beside this pocket is monitored and repaired, not static. https://www.coquitlam.ca/224/Floodplain-Dikes
- **Q:** Which schools serve a River Springs address? **A:** No School District No. 43 school building sits inside the pocket in the provincial directory. The schools listed around it, including Leigh Elementary at 1230 Soball Street on Burke Mountain and École Coquitlam River Elementary at 4250 Shaughnessy Street in Port Coquitlam, are in full in the river-springs-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; catchment for a specific address must be run in the district School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Coquitlam's 2026 Class 1 Residential municipal rate is $2.2756 per $1,000 of assessed value, in full with utility charges in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17264/2026_Tax-Rates
