# Cloverdale Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: cloverdale-houses-for-sale (Cloverdale; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Cloverdale's town-centre plan area is centred on the crossroads of Highway 10 (56 Avenue) and Highway 15 (176 Street). The City's plan material gives the plan area as 297 hectares (733 acres), running from the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) in the south to the Cloverdale Fairgrounds (60 Avenue) in the north, and from 172 Street in the west to 184 Street in the east. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2023-R176_0.pdf
- Cloverdale is one of the City of Surrey's six communities, and its historic town centre grew around 176 Street as its main commercial street. The plan describes the town centre as the primary commercial, institutional and civic heart of the Cloverdale community, with two mainly residential areas flanking it east and west and a business park south of Highway 10. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2023-R176_0.pdf
- Farm and acreage edges of Cloverdale are covered in the cloverdale-farms and cloverdale-acreages-for-sale notes — see the cloverdale-farms note and the cloverdale-acreages-for-sale note; this note covers the town's detached-house streets inside and around the plan area. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2023-R176_0.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached houses are an explicit plan category here, not a leftover. The Cloverdale Town Centre Plan's land-use legend lists Detached Residential and Semi-detached Residential alongside townhouse and low-rise forms, with the detached stock in the residential areas flanking the historic core. https://www.surrey.ca/files/Cloverdale%20TCP%20Plan%20Summary%202019Apr12%20(for%20web).pdf
- Infill on the town's house streets is active at the smallest scale: a January 2026 City planning report approved the pattern in principle for 18130 60 Avenue, a subdivision of one residential property into two lots, which staff said complied with the Official Community Plan (OCP) direction encouraging infill residential development near schools and parks. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0096-00
- Heritage houses are part of the stock, not decoration: Surrey's heritage material for Cloverdale (summarised in City-linked permit guidance from the City's Heritage Register) lists the 1881 Town Hall at 17635 60 Avenue and the 1912 Municipal Hall at 17671 56 Avenue under heritage designation bylaws, with the Clova Theatre at 5732 176 Street and Cloverdale United Church at 17575 58A Avenue under heritage revitalization agreements — verify any specific house on the City's Heritage Register before assuming it can be altered or demolished. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2023-R176_0.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Town Centre Plan, not just the zoning bylaw, shapes what a house lot can become. Council approved the current plan on November 18, 2019; it concentrates higher-density uses in the historic core and along 176 Street, with a secondary mixed-use node at 180 Street and Highway 10 beside the Kwantlen Polytechnic University (KPU) Tech campus, and a gradual transition of heights and densities outward to the detached streets. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2023-R176_0.pdf
- That plan is being rewritten around the hospital: in October 2023 Council authorized an update to the Cloverdale Town Centre Plan focused on supporting the planned new hospital and BC Cancer Centre, including medically supportive land uses — so designations on streets near 180 Street should be checked against the update, not only the 2019 plan. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2023-R176_0.pdf
- The plan area was also expanded: City-linked plan summaries record that in July 2024 Council expanded the plan area north to 64 Avenue between 175/175A Street and 180 Street, bringing more detached streets under the town-centre plan. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2023-R176_0.pdf
- Surrey-wide suite, coach house and small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules are written in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves a Cloverdale house, and is it full? **A:** Lord Tweedsmuir Secondary at 6151 180 Street, Grades 8 to 12, is Cloverdale's secondary school, and it is over capacity. A City planning report records School District 36 advising that Lord Tweedsmuir was operating at 133% capacity as of September 2024, with a 600-capacity addition requested in the district's 2026/2027 Capital Plan, targeted to open in 2029 but not yet approved by the Ministry. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0096-00
- **Q:** Which elementary school would a new house on 60 Avenue feed? **A:** On the City's own evidence for 18130 60 Avenue, the answer is Martha Currie Elementary. The January 2026 planning report for that subdivision records the district's expected enrolment as 2 elementary students at Martha Currie Elementary and 1 secondary student at Lord Tweedsmuir Secondary — other Cloverdale streets feed other elementaries, so confirm by address in the locator. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0096-00
- **Q:** When does the new hospital open, and where is it? **A:** The new Surrey Hospital and BC Cancer Centre is at 5500 180 Street, directly south of the KPU Cloverdale campus. The City states construction began in September 2023 with completion anticipated in 2030, and that its supporting transportation works are planned to be delivered by 2030 to support opening day. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025_R264.pdf
- **Q:** Will SkyTrain reach Cloverdale houses? **A:** Yes, at the town's northern edge, on the Province's current timeline. The Province states all eight stations of the 16-kilometre Surrey–Langley SkyTrain extension are under construction and the extension is expected to enter operation in late 2029, extending the Expo Line from King George Station to Langley City Centre through Cloverdale along Fraser Highway. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0022-000515

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Two bus routes already serve the hospital site, with stated frequencies. The City's hospital report records route 370 (Cloverdale to Willowbrook) operating every 30 minutes from 6 a.m. to 8 p.m., and route 342 (Langley to Newton) every 20 minutes from 5 a.m. to 11:30 p.m., and says a direct hospital–184 Street SkyTrain station–Campbell Heights bus connection is being investigated, not yet in service. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025_R264.pdf
- 60 Avenue is a real bus street for the house area: the City's 18130 60 Avenue report places bus routes 370 and 320 (Langley–Fleetwood–Surrey Central Station) on 60 Avenue, with the nearest stops at 182 Street, about 150 metres from that site. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0096-00
- The town centre's older plan bus picture has changed: the 2019 plan recorded Cloverdale Town Centre as served by routes 320, 342 and 370 — route lists on older plan documents should not be used as the current network. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2023-R176_0.pdf
- Cloverdale Traditional, at 17857 56 Avenue, Kindergarten to Grade 7, is a choice-program school inside the town — a traditional-program option distinct from the neighbourhood catchment elementaries. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey-wide property tax rates, utility billing and the suite fee are written in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- The Cloverdale-specific point is plan-driven servicing: the town-centre plan material states that when new development occurs in an area with a land use plan, the developer must make contributions toward streets, parks and amenities — a cost that lands on new Cloverdale builds and subdivisions, not on a resale house purchase. https://www.surrey.ca/files/Cloverdale%20TCP%20Plan%20Summary%202019Apr12%20(for%20web).pdf
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies here because the City of Surrey is a designated taxable area, so every residential owner files the yearly declaration; houses south of the town toward the ALR edge are still inside the City of Surrey for this tax. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Cloverdale's two highways cross inside the town: Highway 10 (56 Avenue) runs east–west and Highway 15 (176 Street, the Pacific Highway) runs north to the United States border — the plan area is centred on their crossroads. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2023-R176_0.pdf
- Current bus anchors are routes 370, 342 and 320 (see section 5 for the City-stated frequencies on 370 and 342); the rapid-transit change is the Fraser Highway SkyTrain extension, expected in operation in late 2029. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0022-000515
- 64 Avenue is the northern east–west alternative: the City has widened 64 Avenue to a continuous four-lane corridor from 120 Street to 196 Street, linking Newton and Cloverdale, with the final 152-to-164 Street phase completing in 2026. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/surrey-breaks-ground-final-phase-of-64-avenue-road-widening

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Heritage status is the Cloverdale-specific title check. Before planning a teardown or major alteration on an older town-centre house, check the City's Community Heritage Register and whether the property carries a heritage designation bylaw or a heritage revitalization agreement, both of which the City uses in Cloverdale. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2023-R176_0.pdf
- The ALR edge is the southern due-diligence line: the plan area runs south to the Agricultural Land Reserve, so a house lot near that edge needs an ALR check in the Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) finder — farm-use and subdivision rules change at that boundary. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2023-R176_0.pdf
- School capacity is a live risk, not a formality: with Lord Tweedsmuir at 133% capacity in September 2024 and its addition unapproved, a catchment address should be confirmed with School District 36 for space, not just for boundaries. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7925-0096-00
