# Glenbrooke North Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: glenbrooke-north-new-westminster-houses-for-sale (Glenbrooke North, New Westminster; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Glenbrooke North lies between Sixth Avenue and Tenth Avenue, with Sixth Street on the west and McBride Boulevard on the east, per the City's Historical Neighbourhood Context Statement for Glenbrooke North. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Glenbrooke_N_NHood_Ctxt_Stmt_14_April_2009.pdf
- The City spells the name Glenbrooke North; the board report spelling GlenBrooke North, with a capital B, is the same neighbourhood, north of Queen's Park around Glenbrook Ravine, Jamieson Court and Park Crescent. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php
- Second Street and Fifth Street boulevards run north from Queen's Park into Glenbrooke North, and the filled Glenbrooke Creek bed cuts across the neighbourhood's northeast corner, per the City context statement. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Glenbrooke_N_NHood_Ctxt_Stmt_14_April_2009.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses here span the Edwardian-era fabric on Fifth Street south of Eighth Avenue to the 1950s subdivision character along Eighth Avenue and east of Fourth Street, per the City context statement. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Glenbrooke_N_NHood_Ctxt_Stmt_14_April_2009.pdf
- Detached houses are freehold under the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series) in Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001; RS-1 sets a minimum site of 557.40 square metres with a floor space ratio of 0.5. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- École Glenbrook Middle School at 701 Park Crescent and Herbert Spencer Elementary at 605 Second Street are the school buildings inside the house streets, not commercial anchors. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/new-westminster

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- For the city-wide New Westminster zoning, tax and Speculation and Vacancy Tax position, see the the-heights-new-westminster-houses-for-sale note; the Glenbrooke North additions follow. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp
- The Official Community Plan designation for most Glenbrooke North house lots is Residential – Detached and Semi-Detached Housing (RD), whose stated intent is houses, secondary suites, laneway or carriage houses and duplexes. https://pub-newwestcity.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11794
- RS-1 zoning permits a single detached dwelling with a secondary suite and a detached accessory dwelling unit, so most lots here can hold three homes — house, suite and laneway or carriage house — before any infill program is counted. https://pub-newwestcity.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11794
- Glenbrooke North is not a Heritage Conservation Area: the Queen's Park Heritage Conservation Area adjoins it to the south, and its permit rules do not extend into Glenbrooke North on any source read. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- The City's infill housing work expressly names Glenbrooke North among the low-density neighbourhoods whose Detached and Semi-Detached and Ground Oriented Infill designations cover houses, suites, laneway and carriage houses, small lots, duplexes and triplexes. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-revitalization-agreement-policy-refresh/sb_expander_articles/2231.php

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Glenbrooke North house? **A:** Check the exact address, because catchments split here. A School District No. 40 locator result recorded for a nearby address returned F.W. Howay Elementary, École Glenbrook Middle and New Westminster Secondary, while Herbert Spencer Elementary at 605 Second Street stands inside this neighbourhood and offers Early French Immersion as a program of choice. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- **Q:** Is Glenbrooke North under Queen's Park heritage rules? **A:** No. Glenbrooke North has a Historic Neighbourhood Context Statement, which documents its history, but it is not a Heritage Conservation Area. The Queen's Park Heritage Conservation Area, with its protection for existing buildings and design guidelines, adjoins it to the south and stops at that boundary. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **Q:** What is the ravine behind the Park Crescent houses? **A:** Glenbrook Ravine Park, 13.38 acres established in 1991, with access points on Jamieson Court, Blackberry Drive and Glenbrooke Drive, each with winding staircases and hard-packed trails, plus a pond, falls and bridge in its cultivated section. Lots backing onto it trade a park edge for a steep slope. https://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/community-parks/articles/5427.php
- **Q:** Can I add a laneway house on an Eighth Avenue lot? **A:** On most lots, yes in principle. RS-1 zoning permits a detached accessory dwelling unit alongside the house and a secondary suite, and the lot sits in the Laneway and Carriage House Development Permit Area, so a development permit and the design guidelines apply before a building permit. https://pub-newwestcity.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11794

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Terry Hughes Park, between Seventh and Eighth Avenues adjoining McBride Boulevard, was leased from the federal penitentiary site in 1956 — the green edge on this area's east side is former federal land, per the City context statement. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Glenbrooke_N_NHood_Ctxt_Stmt_14_April_2009.pdf
- The BC Penitentiary Cemetery near 245 Francis Way, at the area's southeast edge, was established in 1913, used until 1968, and restored and unveiled by the City on August 29, 2018. https://www.newwestcity.ca/2018/08/27/city-of-new-westminster-to-unveil-restored-bc-penitentiary-cemetery.php
- Glenbrook Ravine was reserved by Colonel Moody in 1859 as Queen's Ravine, and the City's park history calls it the first public park in the Colony of British Columbia. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Glenbrook_Ravine_History.pdf
- The təməsew̓txʷ Aquatic and Community Centre at 65 East Sixth Avenue, whose aquatic portion opened May 14, 2024, is the nearest major recreation centre, east of this area. http://newwestcity.ca/facilities
- Cherry trees line the north end of the Second Street blocks by Queen's Park, a context-statement detail that marks the boulevard streets at this area's south edge. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Glenbrooke_N_NHood_Ctxt_Stmt_14_April_2009.pdf

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 109, New Westminster Station / Lougheed Station, runs via Richmond Street and Jamieson Court, turning around on Jamieson Court — the Glenbrook Ravine access street itself carries the bus loop. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/192/tt109.pdf
- Route 102, Victoria Hill, returns via Blackberry Drive, another ravine access street, then Francis Way and Memorial Drive; route 155 runs on Richmond Street along the area's east side. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/192/tt102.pdf
- No SkyTrain station is inside Glenbrooke North: the City lists five stations in New Westminster — 22nd Street, New Westminster, Columbia, Sapperton and Braid — and the closest depends on the address. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Guide_to_Transit_Oriented_Development_Areas_in_New_Westminster_Updated_Nov_5_2024.pdf
- McBride Boulevard on the east edge and Eighth Avenue and Eighth Street carry driving traffic south to Downtown and the Pattullo Bridge approach and north to Sapperton. http://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/community-parks

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check slope and ravine setbacks for any lot backing onto Glenbrook Ravine: the ravine is a steep, forested watercourse corridor in the City Green Zone, and the park page's staircases and trails describe grade, not a lot's buildable area. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Glenbrook_Ravine_History.pdf
- Check flood status per address on the City Flood Susceptibility Map: the City states five of its twelve square kilometres lie within the provincial floodplain boundaries, covering Queensborough and sections of the mainland — Glenbrooke North's hill position must still be verified per lot. http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- Check the filled creek bed in the northeast corner: the City context statement records that Glenbrooke Creek's ravine was filled in 1914 for a combined sewer, so ground conditions there differ from the surrounding streets. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Glenbrooke_N_NHood_Ctxt_Stmt_14_April_2009.pdf
