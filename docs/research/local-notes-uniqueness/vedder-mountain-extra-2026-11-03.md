## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Vedder Mountain is the mountain and its residential slopes on the south side of the City of Chilliwack, above the Vedder River and beside Cultus Lake — shared area facts (the mountain's recreation frame, Cultus Lake context and the wider South Vedder plan picture) are written in full in the vedder-mountain-guide note; this note adds street-level specifics and points to it for shared facts. https://chilliwack.com/ocp

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Slope is the trigger: Development Permit Area 5 (Hillside Development) applies to all lands within the City of Chilliwack with slopes of 20 percent or more, and a Development Permit is required before subdivision, construction or land alteration inside a designated area unless an exemption applies. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- Development Permit Area 2 (Hillside and Upland Areas) protects development from hazardous hillside conditions and environmentally sensitive areas, and a geotechnical report from a qualified professional is required with each Development Permit Area 2 application. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- Development Permit Area 1 covers the City's drinking-water supply lands — the municipal watersheds and aquifers — which is the permit area to check on upland parcels above the city's water supply areas. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- Development Permit Area 3 protects riparian areas and fish habitat on creek crossings, which matters on the mountain's creek lots. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- City-wide zoning, suite and small-scale multi-unit housing rules are written in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note — see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3279/FAQ_-_Small-Scale_Housing_-_Updated_2025_07_28.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Does my lot's slope change what I can build? **A:** Yes — at 20 percent slope a second permit layer applies. Development Permit Area 5 covers all City of Chilliwack land with slopes of 20 percent or more, and Development Permit Area 2 can also apply to hillside and upland land, with a geotechnical report required from a qualified professional as part of the application, before subdivision, construction or land alteration proceeds. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves the Cultus Lake side of the mountain? **A:** The school located there is Cultus Lake Swílhcha Community School at 71 Sunnyside Blvd, Cultus Lake, a public elementary school in School District No. 33. Catchments are address-based, so confirm the exact address in the district's school locator — a Vedder Mountain address on the Sardis side may not fall in the same catchment, and no locator result is confirmed in this note. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/education/work/bc_k12_schools.csv
- **Q:** Is there a bus at the foot of the mountain? **A:** Yes, on Vedder Road. BC Transit Route 51 Vedder runs between Downtown Chilliwack and the University of the Fraser Valley Chilliwack campus via Vedder Road, with timing points including Vedder Road at Promontory Road and Vedder Road at Luckakuck Way; the mountain's residential streets themselves have no confirmed route in this note. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=51&schedule=2024-07-05
- **Q:** What flood rules apply near the Vedder River at the mountain's foot? **A:** The City's floodplain bylaw sets the setback from this river. Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets a minimum setback of 75 metres from the natural boundary of the Vedder River and 30 metres from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal for buildings requiring floodproofing, with floor levels set by the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- **Q:** Are the mountain's trails and recreation areas covered here? **A:** Not in this note — they are covered once, in the guide note. The vedder-mountain-guide note carries the shared recreation and mountain facts for this area; this neighbourhood note is limited to street-level, school, permit and access specifics, so the two pages do not repeat the same trail content. https://chilliwack.com/ocp

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- A building permit here can carry a geotechnical condition a flatland lot never sees: construction on land that may be subject to flooding, mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip, rockfalls, subsidence or avalanche may not be permitted unless a report certified by a registered professional geotechnical engineer states the land may be safely used as intended. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=639
- Hillside neighbourhoods are Wildlife Issue Areas for garbage: the City designates hillside areas including Chilliwack Mountain and Little Mountain, where carts may not go to the curb before 5:00 a.m. on collection day and wildlife-resistant carts are required from May 1, 2026 — whether a specific Vedder Mountain street is designated is a check on the City page. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2936
- City-wide collection and billing details (weekly compostables, alternating garbage and recycling, quarterly utility billing) are written in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=818

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check slope before anything else: Development Permit Area 5 applies at slopes of 20 percent or more anywhere in the city, and Development Permit Area 2 requires a geotechnical report with the application. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- Check the floodplain at the mountain's foot: Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets the 75-metre Vedder River setback and the flood construction levels shown on its Schedule A map. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- Check drinking-water lands: Development Permit Area 1 covers the surface water supply areas for the City of Chilliwack, and its checklist must accompany a permit application on those lands. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- Check riparian creeks: Development Permit Area 3 protects fish and fish habitat and riparian areas on creek-crossed lots. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
