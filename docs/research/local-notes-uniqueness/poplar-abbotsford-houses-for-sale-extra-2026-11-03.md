# Poplar Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: poplar-abbotsford-houses-for-sale (Poplar, Abbotsford; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Poplar is the south-west Abbotsford house area below Highway 1, around Clearbrook Road, Peardonville Road and Marshall Road. Its southern streets — King Road and Huntingdon Road — run toward the United States border at the Sumas–Huntingdon crossing on Highway 11.
- Poplar houses sit inside the City of Abbotsford; the city-wide boundary, plan and tax facts are in full in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note — see the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note. The City's neighbourhood plans page is the index for any plan covering a Poplar street. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- South Poplar Traditional Elementary, at 32746 Huntingdon Road, anchors the south end of the area and carries "Poplar" in its name. https://southpoplar.abbyschools.ca/
- The Abbotsford School of Integrated Arts (ASIA) North Poplar campus, at 32041 Marshall Road, sits at the north end of Poplar. http://northpoplar.abbyschools.ca/

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Poplar's detached stock mixes suburban houses near Clearbrook Road with rural houses and small acreages toward King Road and Huntingdon Road. Rural lots at the south edge toward Huntingdon Road are in or beside the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) — see the sumas-prairie note for the prairie-wide farm picture (program order #189).
- Houses on the north side of Poplar sit near the Marshall Road corridor; the ASIA North Poplar campus address, 32041 Marshall Road, marks that built-up northern edge. http://northpoplar.abbyschools.ca/
- Detached-house servicing changes at the city edge: acreage parcels on the rural edges use wells and onsite sewage where outside the urban boundary — see the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A Poplar house near the airport flight paths can face federal height limits. Poplar is east of Abbotsford International Airport (YXX); where a lot falls inside the Airport Zoning Regulation area on City drawing M-500, height limits and NAV CANADA / Transport Canada approvals apply before a City permit. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- A suite in a Poplar detached house follows the city-wide zoning rule — one secondary suite is permitted in all single detached dwellings under Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 section 140.3.8; the full small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) wording is in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- Farm use, subdivision and an added dwelling on an ALR lot in south Poplar follow the Agricultural Land Commission Act and the ALR Use Regulation as well as City zoning. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a South Poplar house in the flood area? **A:** South Poplar sits on higher ground than Sumas Prairie, but the lot still needs its own check. The City's flood pages focus on the Sumas and Matsqui prairie dike areas east and north of Poplar, so do not assume a Huntingdon Road lot carries the prairie flood covenant — check the parcel on the City WebMap and ask what is on title. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- **Q:** My child wants the traditional program — does living in Poplar guarantee a seat? **A:** No, a traditional school seat is by application, not by address alone. Abbotsford School District No. 34 (SD34) states its traditional schools are schools of choice that any child in the district may apply to attend, space permitting; South Poplar Traditional Elementary is at 32746 Huntingdon Road. Auguston Traditional is the exception, run as a closed catchment. https://frasermiddlehockey.abbyschools.ca/programs/traditional-schools
- **Q:** Which water bill will a Poplar house get? **A:** It depends which side of the Clearbrook Waterworks District line the house is on. The City bills water and sewer bi-monthly, except customers in the Clearbrook Waterworks District, who are billed quarterly for sewer. Poplar's Clearbrook Road streets are where that district boundary matters, so confirm the billing district for the exact address at closing. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/current-utility-bill
- **Q:** Can I build taller or add a unit near the airport side of Poplar? **A:** Height is capped first by the federal airport rules, before City zoning. Where a lot is inside the Airport Zoning Regulation area on City drawing M-500, approach, outer and transitional surface limits apply and federal approvals come before a City permit. Get the lot checked against drawing M-500 before designing an addition. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Poplar houses pay the same City of Abbotsford tax bylaw as the rest of the city — Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3643-2025 sets the 2025 rates per $1,000 of taxable value; the mechanics are in full in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2025-05/Bylaw%203643-2025%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202025.pdf
- The Poplar-specific cost difference is the water district, not the tax rate. Houses in the Clearbrook Waterworks District are billed quarterly for sewer; other Abbotsford houses are billed bi-monthly for water and sewer. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/current-utility-bill
- City-wide 2026 utility rates apply in Poplar from March 1, 2026: residential water $1.46 per cubic metre and sewer $1.35 per cubic metre. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/water-sewer-rates
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in Abbotsford, including Poplar. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the airport zoning overlay before any addition on a west Poplar lot. The City's YXX guidelines tie height to the Airport Zoning Regulations and drawing M-500. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- Check ALR status before assuming a south Poplar acreage can be split or gain a second house. Housing in the ALR follows the Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) housing rules as well as City zoning. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Check wells and septic on rural Poplar lots outside the urban boundary, using the Province's well records. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- Check the parcel on City WebMap for the lot's own layers; the map does not replace a title search. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
