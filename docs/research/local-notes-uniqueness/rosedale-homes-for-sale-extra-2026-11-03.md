## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- As a City of Chilliwack area, Rosedale is under the 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, whose Schedule B includes the Agricultural Area Plan as B1 — relevant to Rosedale's farm-edge lots. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
- Farm home plate rules apply on valley-floor farmland: Development Permit Area D-4, Farm Home Plate, in the 2050 OCP governs clustering residential buildings so the balance stays in farm use. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
- In the Agricultural Lowland (AL) zone read this pass, a farm home plate allows a principal residential use of 4,000 square metres plus 1,000 square metres for an additional residential use, with subdivision inside the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) generally at 7.5 hectares minimum unless excluded or approved — confirm current zoning on the City site, as the copy read was a third-party posting of the bylaw. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Rosedale in Chilliwack or the regional district? **A:** Rosedale itself is in the City of Chilliwack, so City tax, utility and bylaw rules apply to a Rosedale address. The confusion comes from its eastern neighbour: Popkum and Bridal Falls, further east on Yale Road, are in Fraser Valley Regional District Electoral Area D, with different taxes and utility billing. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html
- **Q:** Which schools serve Rosedale homes? **A:** School District No. 33 (SD33) transportation lists the Rosedale/Popkum area as Rosedale Traditional Community School for Kindergarten to Grade 8 and Chilliwack Secondary School for Grades 9 to 12. The school itself is at 50850 Yale Road in Rosedale. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2023-03/611%20AP%20Transportation%20-%20Operating%20Instructions.pdf
- **Q:** Are Rosedale school catchments changing? **A:** Yes, a change is approved and dated. In May 2026 SD33 approved moving north-side French Immersion to Rosedale Traditional Community School in 2027-28, transitioning it from a Kindergarten-Grade 8 school to a middle school, with Rosedale catchment Kindergarten-Grade 5 students moving to a new east-side elementary school opening September 2027. https://sd33.bc.ca/news/board-approves-changes-some-schools
- **Q:** Can I still visit Minter Gardens in Rosedale? **A:** No. The University of the Fraser Valley states Minter Gardens closed in 2013 after 33 years and the property, described as in rural Popkum in Electoral Area D at the foot of Mount Cheam, was put up for sale. The separate Minter Country Garden store on Young Road is a different business. https://blogs.ufv.ca/blog/2014/07/ufv-students-work-fvrd-imagine-new-future-minter-gardens/
Read: 2026-11-03

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City of Chilliwack 2025 residential property tax total was 4.50189 per $1,000 of assessed taxable value, per the City's 2025 rate table, and utility rates are billed quarterly under the City's water and sewer bylaws. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2527/Tax%5FRates%5Ffor%5Fthe%5FWebtugnc2fs1l9d%2Epdf
- The City of Chilliwack is a designated taxable area for the Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT); a Rosedale home inside the city is in that list, while a Popkum home in Electoral Area D is not named — check the provincial map by address. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- BC Transit route 71, Agassiz-Harrison, runs along Yale Road through Rosedale between downtown Chilliwack and Harrison Hot Springs, and is the main bus serving Rosedale homes. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/riders-guide/
- Highway 9, reached east on Yale Road, crosses the Fraser River to Agassiz and connects to Highway 1 at Bridal Falls. https://bcparks.ca/bridal-veil-falls-park/
- Route 66, Fraser Valley Express, is the regional connection from Chilliwack toward Lougheed Station — Rosedale riders first use route 71 or drive into Chilliwack. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/riders-guide/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check floodplain status per lot: the City's Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 requires habitable floors at or above the flood construction level plus 0.6 metres freeboard in standard-dike areas. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999

- Confirm which side of the City/FVRD boundary a listing address falls on, because tax, utility billing and subdivision authority change at that line. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html
