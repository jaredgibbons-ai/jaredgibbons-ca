# New Westminster Townhomes — Extra Local Notes
Page: new-westminster-townhomes-for-sale (New Westminster; townhomes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- New Westminster's townhome stock clusters in Queensborough, the island neighbourhood on the eastern tip of Lulu Island reached by the Queensborough Bridge, where the Ewen Avenue corridor of strata complexes stands; smaller pockets sit at Victoria Hill, Queen's Park and downtown Royal Avenue. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/CNW_Interactive_Land_Use_Map___(Consolidated_September_2026).pdf
- The City's Official Community Plan (OCP) residential neighbourhoods map names ten neighbourhoods — Queensborough, Connaught Heights, West End, Moody Park, Brow of the Hill, Glenbrooke North, Queen's Park, Massey Victory Heights, McBride Sapperton and Downtown including Quayside. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/OUR_CITY_2041_Official_Community_Plan_New_Westminsters_Residential_Neighbourhoods_Map.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Ewen Avenue in Queensborough holds the counted run: Portside at 488 Furness Street in Port Royal, Coopers Landing at 935 Ewen Avenue, English Mews at 1111 and 1135 Ewen Avenue, Ewen at 843 Ewen Avenue, Alexander Walk at 1211 Ewen Avenue and Oxford Townhomes at 728 Ewen Avenue. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/CNW_Interactive_Land_Use_Map___(Consolidated_September_2026).pdf
- Salter Street adds two more Queensborough complexes in the index: The Peninsula at 210 Salter Street and Paddler's Landing at 250 Salter Street, with Coracle at 260 Ewen Avenue. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/CNW_Interactive_Land_Use_Map___(Consolidated_September_2026).pdf
- On the mainland, the index counts Clinton Place at 114 Park Row in Queen's Park (strata plan NWS262, 14 units, built 1974), Brickstone Walk II at 838 Royal Avenue downtown, the Victoria Hill townhouses at 245 Francis Way and Jamieson Place at 72 Jamieson Court. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/OUR_CITY_2041_Official_Community_Plan_New_Westminsters_Residential_Neighbourhoods_Map.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Townhouse form is design-controlled city-wide: Development Permit Area (DPA) 1.3, Townhouses and Rowhouses, in the OCP sets guidelines that shape infill townhouse and rowhouse development, and DPA 1.2 covers ground oriented housing. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/OCP_DPA_1.3_Townhouses_and_Rowhouses_(Consolidated_April_2023).pdf
- New townhouse zoning is being rewritten: the City's Interim Development Review Framework, updated June 22, 2026, states townhouse development is expected to proceed as guided by the new RT-A and RT-B zoning districts. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Interim_Development_Review_Framework_Updated_22_June_2026(1).pdf
- Queensborough townhouses sit on regulated flood plain land: Flood Plain By-law No. 5095, 1978 sets the habitable floor elevation on a gradient from 4.15 metres at the Queensborough Railway Bridge to 4.4 metres at the Patullo Bridge. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/5095_flood.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Why does Queensborough dominate the townhome stock?
**A:** Because Queensborough was planned as a ground-oriented neighbourhood on Lulu Island, and its Ewen Avenue and Salter Street corridors hold the city's counted strata townhouse complexes — Portside, Coopers Landing, English Mews, Alexander Walk and others — while mainland neighbourhoods like Sapperton and Connaught Heights hold only scattered complexes among detached houses. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/CNW_Interactive_Land_Use_Map___(Consolidated_September_2026).pdf

**Q:** What does the flood plain bylaw mean for an Ewen Avenue townhouse?
**A:** Flood Plain By-law No. 5095, 1978 requires the underside of the floor system of any habitable, business or storage area on the designated lands to sit no lower than an even gradient from 4.15 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada (G.S.C.) datum at the Queensborough Railway Bridge to 4.4 metres at the Patullo Bridge. Ask for the flood construction level documentation with the strata records. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/5095_flood.pdf

**Q:** Are the zoning district names in older strata documents still current?
**A:** Not reliably. The City's Interim Development Review Framework, updated June 22, 2026, states townhouse development is expected to proceed under new RT-A and RT-B zoning districts, while older program material names RT-2 through RT-2C on the mainland and RT-3, RT-3A and RT-3B in Queensborough under Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001. Confirm the current district for the lot with the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Interim_Development_Review_Framework_Updated_22_June_2026(1).pdf

**Q:** Can a townhouse project skip a public hearing?
**A:** Often, yes. The City's rezoning page states public hearings will not be held for zoning amendment bylaws consistent with the OCP where residential use is the majority, and the Interim Development Review Framework repeats that hearings are prohibited for Official Community Plan-consistent majority-residential amendments — consultation still applies before the hearing-not-held step. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Interim_Development_Review_Framework_Updated_22_June_2026(1).pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Brickstone Walk at 828 Royal Avenue looks like a townhouse complex but is an apartment-form building in the index, while Brickstone Walk II at 838 Royal Avenue is the counted townhouse strata. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/OUR_CITY_2041_Official_Community_Plan_New_Westminsters_Residential_Neighbourhoods_Map.pdf
- Clinton Place at 114 Park Row dates to 1974 — older than the Queensborough stock by decades — under strata plan NWS262. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/OUR_CITY_2041_Official_Community_Plan_New_Westminsters_Residential_Neighbourhoods_Map.pdf
- English Mews carries two civic addresses, 1111 and 1135 Ewen Avenue, for one complex. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/CNW_Interactive_Land_Use_Map___(Consolidated_September_2026).pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The provincial home owner grant is claimed from the Province, not the City: the City page states the 2026 threshold is $2,075,000 of assessed value, the basic grant is up to $570 and the additional grant for owners 65 and older is up to $845. https://www.newwestcity.ca/propertytaxes-utilities/sb_expander_articles/713.php
- The City's pre-authorized pre-payment plan for property taxes and annual utilities runs ten equal withdrawals from August 1 to May 1, a schedule a townhouse owner can set alongside strata fee payments. https://www.newwestcity.ca/citypage/index/articles/7562.php

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- TransLink route 103, Quayside to New Westminster Station, serves the waterfront street grid below the Victoria Hill and Jamieson Court townhouses. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf
- Queensborough townhouses depend on the Queensborough Bridge: it is the island neighbourhood's road link to the mainland city and to Highway 91A at the Stewardson Way interchange. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/5095_flood.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with flood geography: the City's flood strategy material states about 460 hectares, roughly 30% of the city, lies within the Fraser River floodplain, and Queensborough is protected by the shared perimeter dyke around Lulu Island while mainland lowlands are not diked. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- Check the habitable floor elevation against By-law No. 5095, 1978 on any Queensborough purchase, and ask the strata for flood-related records and insurance terms for the Ewen Avenue or Salter Street complex. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/5095_flood.pdf
