# North Shore Port Moody Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: north-shore-port-moody-houses-for-sale (North Shore Port Moody; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- North Shore is a board sub-area name, not a City neighbourhood name: the City's Official Community Plan (OCP) areas on this shore are Pleasantside and April Road, plus Ioco and the Heritage Mountain grouping. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/city-hall/resources/Documents/OCP-neighbourhood-plan-areas_-_edited_for_EngagePM_website.pdf
- The house strip runs along the north shore of Burrard Inlet on Ioco Road, Alderside Road, Bentley Road, April Road and Barber Street, with Burrard Inlet to the south and the Ioco lands to the west. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Ioco Road is the primary traffic corridor here and part of the regional Major Road Network, carrying traffic to and from Belcarra and Anmore, per OCP section 14.2. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses here are primarily single-detached homes, with a small number of townhouse developments along Ioco Road, per OCP section 14.2 for Pleasantside and April Road. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Detached houses are freehold; the Ioco townsite houses at the west end are early refinery-worker homes inside a heritage conservation area, a different stock from the later hillside subdivisions above Alderside Road. https://www.portmoody.ca/arts-culture-and-heritage/heritage-and-history/al-sholund-s-historical-insights/
- Pleasantside Elementary at 195 Barber Street is the in-area school building anchoring the Barber Street and April Road house streets. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- For the city-wide Port Moody zoning bylaw, small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH), tax and utility position, see the heritage-mountain-port-moody-houses-for-sale note; the north-shore additions follow. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- Small-lot subdivision to RS1-S, minimum 9 metres width and 325 square metres, is available in Pleasantside where the Official Community Plan designates Single Family – Low Density — Pleasantside is one of only three areas named for it, with Moody Centre and Seaview. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- OCP section 14.2 policy for Pleasantside and April Road is to retain the lower-density character with gentle densification, reduce driveway access points onto Ioco Road, and improve pedestrian and cycling links between Knowle Street and First Avenue. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- In the Ioco townsite heritage conservation area, a heritage alteration permit can be required for exterior changes to a house. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves a Bentley Road house? **A:** Pleasantside Elementary at 195 Barber Street is the elementary school in this shore area, and a School District No. 43 locator run for Old Orchard Hall, 646 Bentley Road, returned Pleasantside Elementary. Middle school returned both Eagle Mountain Middle and École Moody Middle, so confirm the exact address. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** Is there a beach for north shore house owners? **A:** Yes, at Old Orchard Park, 630 Alderside Road. The City and its parks master-plan work describe Old Orchard Park as holding Port Moody's only swimming beach, with the Shoreline Trail starting there toward Rocky Point Park at the head of the inlet. https://engage.portmoody.ca/rocky-point-and-old-orchard-parks-master-plans/news_feed/background-rocky-point-today
- **Q:** Can I split a Pleasantside lot into small lots? **A:** Possibly, and only in Pleasantside on this shore. The City allows subdivision from RS1 to the small-lot RS1-S zone, minimum 9 metres width and 325 square metres per lot, only where the Official Community Plan designates Single Family – Low Density in Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- **Q:** Is there a SkyTrain station on the north shore? **A:** No station is on this shore. Buses 181 and 182 run the Ioco Road corridor to Moody Centre Station, which carries both the Millennium Line and the West Coast Express. A north shore house commute therefore always starts with a bus leg or a drive down Ioco Road. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt181.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Old Orchard Hall at 646 Bentley Road is the in-area community hall, and Official Community Plan policy promotes it for community events and childcare. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Barber Street Park at 94 April Road is the small neighbourhood playground inside the April Road house streets. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/recreation-parks-and-environment/parks.aspx
- The Old Orchard Park Master Plan was endorsed by Council in 2024 and guides park work over a three- to five-year period, so beach and hall facilities here are mid-renewal, not finished. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/recreation-parks-and-environment/parks.aspx
- No recreation complex or library branch sits on this shore: the Port Moody Recreation Complex is at 300 Ioco Road south of the inlet side, and the single Public Library is at 100 Newport Drive. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 181, Moody Centre Station / Ioco, runs this shore on Ioco Road via Murray Street and the Moody overpass, with some trips starting from Belcarra. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt181.pdf
- Route 182, Belcarra / Moody Centre Station, also runs Murray Street and Ioco Road before turning onto Heritage Mountain Boulevard toward Anmore and Belcarra. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/206/tt182.pdf
- The Shoreline Trail runs along this shore between Old Orchard Park and Rocky Point Park and Inlet Park, giving the house streets a waterfront walking and cycling link the hillside areas do not have. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/recreation-parks-and-environment/parks.aspx

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check Development Permit Area 4 (Environmentally Sensitive Areas) and Area 5 (Hazardous Lands and Steep Slopes) before buying a shore or slope lot, using the City ViewPort Environmentally Sensitive Areas and Watercourses layers. http://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/environmental-protection-and-requirements/
- Check heritage status in the Ioco townsite before planning work: it is a heritage conservation area, and the general zoning page does not settle a specific house's permit position. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/
- Check the foreshore lot against the City hazards page: the Official Community Plan names flooding, erosion and landslip among Port Moody hazards, and a shoreline address needs its own check, not a neighbourhood assurance. https://www.portmoody.ca/resident-services/hazards/
