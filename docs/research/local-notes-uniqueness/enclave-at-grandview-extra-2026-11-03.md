# Enclave at Grandview Townhomes — Extra Local Notes
Page: enclave-at-grandview (Grandview Heights, South Surrey; townhomes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Enclave's pocket is the west side of Grandview Heights, on 156 Street inside the Morgan Heights plan area, the first neighbourhood planned in Grandview Heights. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
- Morgan Heights covers about 80 hectares (196 acres), bounded by 28 Avenue to the north, 24 Avenue to the south and 164 Street to the east, with the Highway 99 corridor plan area to the west. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
- The complex address carried in this program's research index, 2979 156 Street, sits just north of the 28 Avenue line that separates Morgan Heights from North Grandview Heights — see NOT CONFIRMED for the status of the complex's own facts. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/north-grandview-heights

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Morgan Heights' land-use rules no longer come from its own plan: the City states the Morgan Heights plan was rescinded through the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) update and is no longer in effect, and development in the area is led by the OCP. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
- The same rescission matters north of 28 Avenue: North Grandview Heights' designations have also been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update, so a lot on either side of the avenue is read from the OCP today. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/north-grandview-heights

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Who built Enclave, and how many homes does it have? **A:** This could not be confirmed on an allowed source, and this note does not guess. The live page attributes the complex to Citimark and gives a unit count and a strata plan number, but searches of the developer's own site and the wider web in this program returned no Citimark, strata or manager page for this complex. Treat any builder, count or plan figure for Enclave as unverified until the strata documents for a specific unit confirm it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
- **Q:** Which elementary schools are in Enclave's pocket? **A:** Two School District 36 elementary schools stand in this pocket: Sunnyside Elementary at 2828-159 Street and Morgan Elementary at 3366-156A Street, both recorded in the British Columbia school list. A district consultation package listed Sunnyside and Morgan among the proposed feeder schools for Grandview Heights Secondary. Catchment is still by exact address, so run 2979 156 Street in the district locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** Is Enclave in Morgan Heights or North Grandview Heights? **A:** It sits on the seam, and the plan answer changed recently either way. Morgan Heights runs from 24 Avenue to 28 Avenue; North Grandview Heights runs from 28 Avenue to 32 Avenue, east of 156 Street. Both plans' designations have now been superseded or rescinded in favour of the Surrey 2050 OCP, so the practical rule for a buyer is the OCP designation and the lot's zone, not the old plan name. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/north-grandview-heights
- **Q:** How does drainage run through this pocket? **A:** Northward, following the slope. The City's terms of reference for the Morgan Heights plan state the area drains to the north, following the natural topography through the North Grandview Heights area. For a specific Enclave lot, that means checking the City's ecosystem and drainage mapping for the parcel rather than assuming the street's grading tells the whole story. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/CRGrandviewHeightsArea1TermsofReference.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Morgan Heights is centred on Southridge School at 2656 160 Street, the independent school the City names as the plan's centre — the landmark locals use for this pocket. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp
- Sunnyside Elementary was already at 24 classrooms including 7 portables in 2018, when the school district sought a parking variance for two more portables — a crowding marker for this pocket's families. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7918-0335-00.pdf
- Grandview Heights Secondary at 16987 25 Avenue was built at 1,500-student capacity specifically to address overcrowding in South Surrey and White Rock. https://www.surreyschools.ca/capitalprojectoffice/page/181989/grandview-heights-secondary-new-school-site-177
- The Morgan Heights plan is described by the City as largely built out, so new supply in this pocket now comes as individual rezonings, not as a further plan phase. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/morgan-heights-ncp

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- For a lot near the north edge of Morgan Heights, check the City's sensitive-ecosystem mapping: a 2023 application at 16487-28 Avenue in North Grandview Heights required a Development Permit for Sensitive Ecosystems, which shows ecosystem permits are live on this side of Grandview Heights. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7922-0233-00.pdf
- Read the depreciation report and minutes for the specific strata before subjects: strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report under British Columbia's strata framework, and disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- Do not rely on any Enclave specification, amenity or year-built figure from a listing: none was confirmed on a developer, strata or manager source in this pass, so the strata disclosure documents are the only reliable record for this complex.
