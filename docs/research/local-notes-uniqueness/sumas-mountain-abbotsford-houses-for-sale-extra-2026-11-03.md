# Sumas Mountain Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: sumas-mountain-abbotsford-houses-for-sale (Sumas Mountain, Abbotsford; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sumas Mountain houses are the hillside and acreage houses on Abbotsford's east side, along Sumas Mountain Road, Dawson Road, Straiton Road and Whatcom Road. Whatcom Road connects Highway 1 with McKee Road, and its connector segment opened in February 2012. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/39154
- The mountain is split between governments, and that split is the first boundary fact. The City of Abbotsford covers the west and south slopes inside the city; the Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area H Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 584, 2003, covers the Sumas Mountain area outside the city. http://www.smrpoa.ca/media/Sumas+Mountain+Official+Community+Plan+(Bylaw+No.+584-2003).pdf
- Sumas Mountain Regional Park, at 39100 Sumas Mountain Forest Service Road, occupies the top and east side of the mountain. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/sumas-mountain.html
- The Vicarro Ranch Planning Area sits near the eastern edge of the city's urban boundary on Sumas Mountain, made up of three parcels of about 155 hectares (383 acres). https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/39154

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Sumas Mountain detached stock splits into two kinds: serviced subdivision houses on the lower McKee and Auguston benches, and rural acreage houses higher up and in Straiton. The McKee Neighbourhood Plan covers about 842 hectares on Sumas Mountain with steep forested slopes and headwater tributaries. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-09/NEDP%20Application%20Guide_Appendix%20A_August%202026%20%5BFinal%5D.pdf
- Auguston Traditional Elementary, at 36367 Stephen Leacock Drive, marks the Auguston subdivision bench on the lower mountain. https://auguston.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/registration/auguston
- Mountain Elementary is at 2299 Mountain Drive, on the mountain's lower west side (address carried in the Nov 1 research from the district school plan page — check current). https://mountain.abbyschools.ca/schoolplan/
- Upper-mountain and Straiton houses are acreage houses, not subdivision houses: servicing, access and slope differ lot by lot, so the plan and zone for the exact parcel govern — see section 3.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The McKee Neighbourhood Plan is the governing plan for new houses on the lower mountain. It was adopted by Council in 2023, covers about 842 hectares on Sumas Mountain, and its environmental policies guide development to maintain McKee's environmental integrity. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-09/NEDP%20Application%20Guide_Appendix%20A_August%202026%20%5BFinal%5D.pdf
- The plan is law, not a concept: OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3400-2023 added the McKee Neighbourhood Plan as Schedule E to OCP Bylaw No. 2600-2016, adopted June 26, 2023 after a public hearing June 12, 2023. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2023-07/ADOPTED%203400-2023%20Official%20Community%20Plan%20Amendment%20Bylaw%20No.%20021.pdf
- Development inside McKee must also align with the McKee plan's own policies, on top of the OCP. The City's Natural Environment Development Permit (NEDP) guide states that where development is proposed within the McKee Neighbourhood, it is expected to also align with the McKee Neighbourhood Plan. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-09/NEDP%20Application%20Guide_Appendix%20A_August%202026%20%5BFinal%5D.pdf
- The OCP includes an Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) map (Map 16) and shows an Agriculture designation on the east side of the mountain; whether any single parcel is in the ALR must be checked parcel by parcel. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- City-wide suite and small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) wording is in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note — see the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note. Hillside lots can still be limited by slope, creek and servicing rules even where the city-wide permission exists.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a Sumas Mountain house on City water and sewer, or on a well and septic? **A:** It depends which bench the house is on, and you must check the lot. Lower McKee and Auguston subdivision houses are the serviced form, while upper-mountain and Straiton acreage houses are the rural form. Fraser Health publishes the rules for onsite sewerage systems, and the Province keeps well records, so pull both for a rural lot. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q:** What is being planned above my street in McKee? **A:** A full new neighbourhood is planned, not just a few lots. The McKee Neighbourhood Plan was adopted in June 2023 as Schedule E to the Official Community Plan, and the City is now developing a McKee Trail Strategy for the neighbourhood's trail network. Ask what phase and what trail alignment sit above the lot you are buying. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **Q:** Will quarry trucks pass my house? **A:** Ask about quarry truck traffic on the mountain roads before you buy. Five quarries operate on Sumas Mountain and their gravel trucks use an access road to Highway 1 through the Lightning Rock site, as Semá:th (Sumas First Nation) states in its land-return release. https://sumasfirstnation.com/press-releases/sacred-land-returned-to-semath/
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves an Auguston house? **A:** Auguston Traditional Elementary is the Auguston bench school, at 36367 Stephen Leacock Drive, but it is not a normal open catchment. The district states Auguston Traditional is a catchment school with a defined catchment and sibling priority, and it is excluded from the open traditional choice list. Confirm the exact address in the School District No. 34 (SD34) locator. https://auguston.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/registration/auguston
- **Q:** Is the trail behind my house an official City trail? **A:** Not necessarily, and that matters for privacy and for future building. The City's McKee Trail Strategy work notes the existing network is mostly informal, built incrementally over years on privately owned land. A worn path behind a lot is not proof of a public right of way, so check title and the trail strategy maps. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- The regional park is bigger on some pages than others — use the FVRD figure. The FVRD manages three hiking trails on the eastern flank: Centennial Trail, Chadsey Lake Trail and Eastern Lookout Trail, in a 1,471-hectare park. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=24348
- Sandy Hill Elementary sits at the mountain's west foot on Old Clayburn Road, and its own school site is the district page for that catchment side. https://sandyhill.abbyschools.ca/
- The McKee trail network is being formalised now: the City's Let's Talk Abbotsford page for the McKee Trail Strategy is the live consultation record. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- A new City park on Dianne Brook Avenue, on the lower mountain side, is on the City's Let's Talk projects list. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/new-park-dianne-brook-ave

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Sumas Mountain houses inside the city pay the city-wide Tax Rates Bylaw — Bylaw No. 3643-2025 for 2025, per $1,000 of taxable value; full mechanics in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2025-05/Bylaw%203643-2025%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202025.pdf
- A new detached house on the mountain carries the rural-residential or single-detached development cost charge (DCC) class that fits its form: the City summary effective February 1, 2026 lists rural residential per square metre of gross floor area and single detached per lot, totalling $64,860 per lot for single detached. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-04/DCC-ACC%20Rates%20Summary%20Bylaw%20No.%203633-2025_0.pdf
- Houses outside the city boundary, in FVRD Electoral Area H, do not pay City of Abbotsford taxes at all — they fall under the provincial rural property tax system. The Province's rural area property tax page is the starting point for those lots. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area
- City-serviced mountain houses pay the 2026 city utility rates from March 1, 2026 (water $1.46 per cubic metre, sewer $1.35 per cubic metre); a well-and-septic lot pays neither. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/water-sewer-rates
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in the City of Abbotsford, including its Sumas Mountain neighbourhoods. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check slope and creek rules first on any hillside lot. The McKee plan area is described by the City as steep forested slopes with headwater tributaries, and development must align with the McKee plan and the Natural Environment Development Permit (NEDP) guide. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-09/NEDP%20Application%20Guide_Appendix%20A_August%202026%20%5BFinal%5D.pdf
- Check which government governs the lot before anything else. A lot east of the city boundary sits in FVRD Electoral Area H under its own OCP (Bylaw No. 584, 2003), with different subdivision, tax and permit rules than a City lot. http://www.smrpoa.ca/media/Sumas+Mountain+Official+Community+Plan+(Bylaw+No.+584-2003).pdf
- Check ALR status parcel by parcel on the east side of the mountain, using OCP Map 16 and the City WebMap. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- Check the well record and the septic file for a rural lot: the Province keeps well records and Fraser Health sets onsite sewerage requirements. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
