# Sardis West Vedder — Extra Local Notes
Page: sardis-west-vedder (Sardis West Vedder; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sardis West Vedder is the part of Sardis west of Vedder Road, and Vedder Road is the line that separates it from Sardis East Vedder and Promontory. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- The Sardis Neighbourhood Plan covers the whole Sardis neighbourhood, not just this side: Sardis is centrally located in Chilliwack, immediately south of Highway 1, bordered by the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) to the west and east, First Nations lands to the south and east, and the Vedder neighbourhood to the south. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- The plan's own sub-areas on this side are North Evans and Wells Landing, and the plan states these sub-area boundaries do not formally exist and were created only to characterise parts of the neighbourhood. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- North Evans extends along Evans Road, north of Wells Road and south of Knight Road. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Wells Landing is south of Wells Road, between the ALR lands and Vedder Road, with access to the Valley Rail Trail and to Vedder Road shops and services. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- West Evans, at the west edge, is ALR land between Stevenson Road and Evans Road, along the boundary of urban development. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The housing on the Evans Road side is a mix of forms, not one subdivision type: North Evans is characterised in the plan by single-detached homes, townhouses, secondary suites, mobile home parks and seniors' housing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Wells Landing is described as a mix of residential uses of varying age and form, rather than a single building era. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Sardis as a whole has no vacant greenfield land, so new housing here comes from infill and redevelopment: subdivision of standard lots into two smaller single-detached lots and small-scale multi-unit housing are the anticipated forms in the plan. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Shared Sardis housing-stock facts are in full in the sardis-houses-for-sale note; this note adds only the west-of-Vedder sub-area detail above. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The plan that covers this area is the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan, Schedule B5 of Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, adopted October 2020 and updated 2025. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387 https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- A drinking-water development permit can be required on this side because the Sardis-Vedder Aquifer protection area is Development Permit Area (DPA) 1: a permit is required for work including a new private well, a septic field, fuel storage tanks, or excavation larger than 20 square metres and deeper than 0.5 metres inside the aquifer or Vedder River Fan protection areas. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8128
- Expansion of urban development into the West Evans ALR land is not contemplated by the Sardis plan and would require further discussion with the Agricultural Land Commission and a comprehensive plan. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- City-wide zoning, suite-size caps and small-scale multi-unit housing rules for Chilliwack are in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note — see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Sardis West Vedder a City-drawn neighbourhood? **A:** No City boundary exists for the name Sardis West Vedder itself. The City's Sardis Neighbourhood Plan covers Sardis as one neighbourhood south of Highway 1, and its Evans Road sub-areas — North Evans, Wells Landing and West Evans — are described in the plan as informal character areas whose boundaries do not formally exist. Use Vedder Road as the practical east line and the plan map for the Sardis boundary. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- **Q:** Which elementary schools sit on this side? **A:** Evans Elementary is the school named inside North Evans in the Sardis plan, and Unsworth Elementary at 5685 Unsworth Road is the second elementary on this side in School District No. 33 (SD33) listings. Catchments are set by home address, so a buyer should run the exact address in the SD33 School Locator rather than assume the nearest school is the catchment school. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230 https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2026-09/Chilliwack%20School%20District%20Directory%202026-27.pdf
- **Q:** Why does a well or septic change need a permit here? **A:** Because this side sits over the Sardis-Vedder Aquifer, which the City protects as DPA 1 for drinking water supply. The City's DPA 1 text lists a new private well, a septic field installation and larger excavation inside the aquifer protection area as activities requiring a development permit, so a buyer planning those works should confirm the permit before subjects are removed. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8128
- **Q:** Is there a bus on Evans Road? **A:** Yes, Route 52 Evans is the Evans Road route in the BC Transit Chilliwack Rider's Guide, listed between downtown and Cottonwood Mall via Evans Road in the guide's route list. The same guide lists Route 51 Vedder on Vedder Road at the east edge of this area, so most west-side homes are served from either Evans Road or Vedder Road rather than from interior streets. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chw_rg_jan2024-V1.1.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Luckakuck Creek runs through the north side of this area, and the plan describes the creek and its riparian area, along the Coqualeetza Lands, as having a trail system that is a major asset to North Sardis / Luckakuck. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- The Coqualeetza Lands are a 23-hectare (58-acre) property in the centre of Sardis, managed by Stó:lō Nation, with public access welcomed and walking trails valued as a neighbourhood asset; the lands are not subject to the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- The City's Sardis-Vedder parks list for this side includes the Luckakuck Creek Trail, the Valley Rail Trail and the Sheffield Dog Off-Leash Park. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=areaView&plID=165&backTo&areaID=6
- One recorded SD33 locator result exists for this corridor: 7600 Evans Road returned Evans Elementary, Vedder Middle School and Sardis Secondary, and that result applies to that address only — see the sardis-houses-for-sale note for shared Sardis school detail. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- Sardis growth is contained by the ALR between Evans Road to the west and Chilliwack River Road to the east, a pattern the plan says continues to shape development here. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 52 Evans is the bus route for the Evans Road corridor, and Route 51 Vedder runs the Vedder Road edge; both are listed in the BC Transit Chilliwack Rider's Guide route list. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chw_rg_jan2024-V1.1.pdf
- The Sardis plan describes a frequent bus line on the Vedder Corridor, stated in the plan as 15-minute service, connecting Sardis to downtown, Garrison Crossing and the Canada Education Park to the south. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Highway 1 is the north boundary of Sardis, and Vedder Road is the main north-south connection within and out of Sardis, per the plan's Vedder Corridor description. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- The Valley Rail Trail serves the Wells Landing part of this side for walking and cycling, per the Sardis plan. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the aquifer layer first on this side: the Sardis-Vedder Aquifer, Vedder River Fan and other protection areas are mapped as DPA 1 in Schedule D-1 of the OCP, and the map for a specific lot is read through the City's OCP and webmap resources. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8128 https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
- Check Luckakuck Creek frontage lots against the OCP's Schedule D-3 Riparian Area development permit area, listed in the OCP's Schedule D contents. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
