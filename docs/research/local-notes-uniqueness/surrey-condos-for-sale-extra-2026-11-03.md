## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Where do towers stand, versus low-rise? **A:** Towers stand in Whalley, around City Parkway, 104 Avenue and Surrey Central Station, where the City describes emerging residential high rises at a transit hub. Low-rise wood-frame is the form elsewhere — King + Crescent in South Surrey is four six-storey buildings. Guildford and Newton buyers should expect low-rise, older strata and confirm form by address. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- **Q:** Is PARKWAY Aspect move-in ready? **A:** The developer page read 2026-11-03 lists Aspect homes as move-in ready, estimated move-in early 2027, in Bosa Properties' four-tower, over 2,000-home masterplan at City Parkway and 104 Avenue. That is the developer's wording. A unit's completion, disclosure and deposit terms still need reading in its own contract documents. https://parkway.bosaproperties.com/Own-a-Home/parkway-aspect
- **Q:** What City bill sits beyond strata fees? **A:** A flat-rate strata unit pays $454 water and $571 sewer in 2026, $1,025 total, plus $46.55 recycling and organics on City collection. Those are City charges only. Strata fees, insurance and contingency contributions are set by the strata corporation and appear only in its documents. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill
- **Q:** Can I rent it out, or keep a large dog? **A:** The strata decides, not the City. No source read stated a rental, pet or age rule for a named Surrey building, so the answer is in that building's bylaws, rules and Form B information certificate. Obtain them for the exact building before an offer depending on renting or pets. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Metered strata buildings bill every four months; sewer is 88 percent of water supplied. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill
- Metro Vancouver bulk costs are 75 to 86 percent of utility charges per the City — why strata sewer is $571 versus $1,661 per house, 2026. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill
- Additional school tax applies to stratified condominium units assessed at $3 million and over — City tax page. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- Holland Park, beside the Whalley towers, hosts the annual Fusion Festival. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- R6 Scott Road is Surrey's newest RapidBus, on a region's-busiest corridor — City transit update, 2025. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R216.pdf
