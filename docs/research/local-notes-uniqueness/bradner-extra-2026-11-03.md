Page: bradner (Bradner; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Bradner is a rural acreage community in west Abbotsford centred on Bradner Road. https://bradner.abbyschools.ca/
- Bradner Elementary at 5291 Bradner Road and Bradner Hall at 5305 Bradner Road sit side by side and anchor the village core. https://bradner.abbyschools.ca/
- The community runs toward Mt. Lehman and the Township of Langley line on its west side, with Matsqui Prairie to the north and east. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- Highway 1 access for the west side of Abbotsford is via the 264 Street interchange in Langley; the exact drive time from Bradner is UNCONFIRMED.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- ALR rules cap house size and second homes on farm parcels here, as across Abbotsford. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- A principal residence in the ALR is capped at 500 m² total floor area; exceeding it needs a Non-Adhering Residential Use application to the Commission. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- An additional residence beyond what the ALR Use Regulation permits also needs Commission permission, and the Commission must not grant it unless it is necessary for a farm use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- Abbotsford farm parcels here fall under the Agricultural zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, including the A2 zone referenced in current council reports. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58312/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there a community hall and what happens there? **A:** Yes, Bradner Hall at 5305 Bradner Road is the community centre. Its own site states the Bradner Community Club was established in 1924, the present hall was erected by local volunteers in 1900, the hall has been home to the Bradner Flower Show since 1932, and the facility holds up to 350 people for catered functions and local groups including 4-H clubs. http://www.bradnerhall.ca/
- **Q:** Which school do Bradner children attend first? **A:** Bradner Elementary on Bradner Road is the local elementary school. The Abbotsford School District lists Bradner Elementary School at 5291 Bradner Rd, Abbotsford, beside the hall, with the school site at bradner.abbyschools.ca. Catchments in rural Abbotsford go by street address, so confirm the exact address with SD34 before removing subjects on any purchase. https://bradner.abbyschools.ca/
- **Q:** Can I add a second dwelling for family on an acreage? **A:** Only within the ALR residence rules, which are restrictive. Provincial policy allows generally one residence per parcel, permits an additional residence only where the ALR Use Regulation conditions are met, and otherwise requires a Non-Adhering Residential Use application; the Commission must not approve an additional residence unless it is necessary for a farm use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- **Q:** Is Bradner on city water and sewer? **A:** Rural acreage servicing must be verified lot by lot and was not confirmed on the pages read. What is confirmed is the municipal utility pricing structure: from March 1, 2026 Abbotsford residential water is $1.46/m³ and sewer is $1.35/m³ where City services exist. Lots outside service areas rely on private well and septic arrangements, which need their own inspections. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/water-sewer-rates
Read: 2026-11-03

- The 2026 Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026 sets separate dyking and drainage districts for the prairies; whether a Bradner lot is inside one must be checked on its tax notice. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf
