# Sperling-Duthie Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: sperling-duthie-burnaby (Sperling-Duthie, Burnaby; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sperling-Duthie is a central-Burnaby residential area around Sperling Avenue and Duthie Avenue, west and north of Deer Lake and crossed by Canada Way. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- Sperling Avenue is the area's spine: it runs from Lougheed Highway and the SkyTrain station at its north end down to Deer Lake Park at its south end. http://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/skytrain_station_maps/sperling_burnaby_lake_station.pdf
- Buckingham Heights, the slope east of Deer Lake, sits within and beside this sub-area's south-east side, and Burnaby Lake Regional Park lies immediately north of Lougheed Highway. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/burnaby-lake-regional-park
- Burnaby City Hall is at 4949 Canada Way, just west of the sub-area at Deer Lake. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-09/Notice-of-Election-by-Voting-2026-10-01.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is almost entirely detached houses, running from post-war bungalows on the flatter western streets to large custom homes in Buckingham Heights on the slope above Deer Lake. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- The Sperling Avenue lakefront holds Burnaby's documented early housing: the City's heritage register lists the Woodward Cottage at 5141 Sperling Avenue, built 1904 and described as the oldest house in the Deer Lake neighbourhood. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark496
- Beside it stand three more register houses from one family group: the Moore House at 5145 Sperling Avenue (built 1941), the Rosalie (Moore) Barrett Residence at 5155 Sperling Avenue (built 1942) and the Montague and Marcia Moore Residence at 5165 Sperling Avenue (built 1943, Tudor Revival). https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark499
- There are effectively no named condo or townhome complexes inside the sub-area; attached homes, when they appear, are one-off low-rise units on the Canada Way and Lougheed Highway edges. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- For lot, zoning and suite detail on the detached stock, see the sperling-duthie-burnaby-houses-for-sale note.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Sperling Avenue's lakefront lots sit inside the Deer Lake Park Heritage Precinct, so a renovation or rebuild there starts with the City's heritage register, not just the zoning bylaw. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark496
- The register houses on Sperling Avenue were formally recognized on the Community Heritage Register by Council resolution dated May 26, 2003, and several are now owned by the City of Burnaby as part of the precinct. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark496
- The precinct is described in the City's heritage records as Burnaby's most significant collection of historic sites, which is the context a buyer should expect for design review on the lakefront strip. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark496

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Does a SkyTrain station actually serve Sperling-Duthie? **A:** Yes. Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station, an elevated Millennium Line station at 2800 Sperling Avenue and Lougheed Highway, stands at the top of Sperling Avenue, and TransLink's station map shows buses 110, 134 and 144 connecting there, the 144 running to Metrotown Station and to Simon Fraser University (SFU). Homes at the Deer Lake end of the sub-area use those buses to reach it rather than walking. http://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/skytrain_station_maps/sperling_burnaby_lake_station.pdf
- **Q:** Is French Immersion available at the local elementary school? **A:** Yes. École Sperling Elementary, 2200 Sperling Avenue, is a dual-track school offering Kindergarten to Grade 7 programs in both English and Early French Immersion, and the district hosts its French Immersion parent information evenings at Sperling. Because immersion is a program of choice, confirm entry and catchment through School District 41 (Burnaby) before relying on a place. https://sperling.burnabyschools.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Sperling-School-Plan-2025.pdf
- **Q:** Which secondary school does a Sperling Avenue address feed to? **A:** For the sample address 2200 Sperling Avenue, the School District 41 locator returns Sperling Elementary for Kindergarten to Grade 7 and Burnaby North Secondary for Grades 8 to 12 — not Burnaby Central Secondary, even though Burnaby Central is the secondary school located in the Deer Lake area at 6011 Deer Lake Parkway. Catchments are address-based, so run the exact address in the locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/burnaby
- **Q:** What do the heritage houses on Sperling Avenue mean for a buyer nearby? **A:** They signal a protected streetscape, not just old houses. The City's register places the 1904 Woodward Cottage, the 1941 Moore House and the 1942 and 1943 Moore family houses within the Deer Lake Park Heritage Precinct on lakefront lots, so exterior changes and rebuild plans on that strip carry heritage review on top of normal permits. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark499
- **Q:** Is anything being built in the sub-area? **A:** One application is on the City's books at its north end. The City of Burnaby Major Projects list, updated to October 27, 2026, carries rezoning reference 23-03 by Doxa Development Limited for 1030 Sperling Avenue, to permit market strata and non-market rental homes, at initial report stage since June 19, 2023, so it is an application record, not an approval or a construction start. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2024-08/Major-Development-Projects.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Deer Lake boat-launch boardwalk replacement, with its signed detour via Sperling Avenue and Deer Lake Drive, runs along this area's park frontage until winter 2027 — full entry in the buckingham-heights note. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- Charles Rummel Centre, 3630 Lozells Avenue, is the small City community centre just east of the sub-area, with a main hall seating up to 100, a playground and a spray park. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/charles-rummel-centre
- You can walk into Burnaby Lake Regional Park directly from Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station, and the park's Sprott Street sports-field entrance on Sperling Avenue is served by buses 110 and 144. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt110.pdf
- The Woodward House at 5195 Sperling Avenue, built 1912 with British Arts and Crafts influences, was built for Maud and Harriet Woodward, one of Burnaby's first teachers among them, and is on the Community Heritage Register. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?objectType_facet=Landmark%20Description%20&objectType_facet=History%20Links&ps=50&q=&sort=title_sort%20asc&creator_facet=johnreid000000%7CJohn%20Reid&creator_facet=bernardrhill000000%7CBernard%20R.%20Hill&place_facet=sperlingavenue000000%7CSperling%20Avenue&p=1
- The Burnaby Lake line fostered this area's growth: the City's heritage record notes the British Columbia Electric Railway's Burnaby Lake interurban line opened June 12, 1911 and fostered Deer Lake's development as a residential neighbourhood. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?objectType_facet=Landmark%20Description%20&objectType_facet=History%20Links&ps=50&q=&sort=title_sort%20asc&creator_facet=johnreid000000%7CJohn%20Reid&creator_facet=bernardrhill000000%7CBernard%20R.%20Hill&place_facet=sperlingavenue000000%7CSperling%20Avenue&p=1
- There is no enclosed mall or town centre inside the sub-area; Brentwood to the north-west and Metrotown to the south are its designated shopping town centres. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station serves the area's north end with Millennium Line trains and bus routes 110, 134 and 144 at its bays. http://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/skytrain_station_maps/sperling_burnaby_lake_station.pdf
- Route 144 links the station to Metrotown Station in one direction and to SFU in the other, passing the Deer Lake Parkway and Canada Way corridor on the way. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt144.pdf
- Route 110 runs from the station area via Sperling Avenue, Kensington and Deer Lake Parkway toward Metrotown Station, covering the sub-area's north–south spine by bus. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt110.pdf
- Canada Way crosses the sub-area east–west and is its main road link to Burnaby City Hall at 4949 Canada Way and to the Burnaby Hospital lands. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-09/Notice-of-Election-by-Voting-2026-10-01.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the heritage register before pricing a renovation on the Sperling Avenue lakefront: register properties inside the Deer Lake Park Heritage Precinct carry heritage review, and the register entry for each address is the lookup. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark496
- Check the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) parcel map before assuming density on a large lot: the OCP was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and its land use map takes precedence over older community plan maps where they conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- Run the exact address in the School District 41 locator: the confirmed sample address feeds to Burnaby North Secondary, not the closer Burnaby Central, so catchment cannot be read off distance. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/burnaby
