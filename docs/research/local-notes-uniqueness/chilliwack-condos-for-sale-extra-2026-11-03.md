## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Strata Property Act governs these buildings; provincial guidance covers bylaws, fees and depreciation reports. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- Part of downtown sits in the Mountain View Heritage Conservation Area, OCP Schedule C, adding heritage review to exterior changes. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- Downtown buildings are in Development Permit Area 6 (Downtown) for form and character (City guide, December 2025). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3279/2025%5F12%5FDevelopment%5FPermit%5FBrochure%5F%2D%5FDP%5F6%2D10%2Epdf
- Downtown flood proofing requires habitable rooms above the flood construction level, behind a 1-in-200-year dyke (Downtown Plan). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3556

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Where are the condo buildings? **A:** In two clusters. Downtown, records name Newmark at 46289 Yale Road and Mountainview Lane at 8497 Young Road, around Five Corners. In Sardis, the Campus Drive buildings and Andmar on Thomas Road form the second cluster. Elsewhere, apartments are scattered singles in R5 and R6 zones. https://www.chilliwack.com/apps/council/meetings/minutes/reg/20191015%20-%20Regular%20Council%20Meeting.pdf
- **Q:** Is a downtown condo in a heritage area? **A:** Part of downtown is. The Mountain View Heritage Conservation Area, Schedule C of the Official Community Plan, covers part of the downtown residential area, described in the Downtown Plan as one of the city's first neighbourhoods. Buildings inside it face heritage controls on exterior work, beyond strata bylaws. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Q:** Can I rent it out, or keep a pet? **A:** Both answers come from the building's strata bylaws, summarized in the Form B the Province directs buyers to read for that building. One Sardis exception: at York Residences the operator's page states pets are not allowed, as Diverse-run rentals, not strata. https://www.diverserentals.com/rentals/york
- **Q:** What transit does downtown get that Sardis does not? **A:** The Downtown Chilliwack exchange itself. BC Transit lists it as terminus for city routes, including Route 66 to Lougheed Town Centre Station, Route 57 to Cottonwood Mall and Route 71 to Agassiz. A Sardis condo relies on Route 51 or 54 to reach it. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
Read: 2026-11-03

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Downtown sits in the Fraser River floodplain: Bylaw No. 4519 sets a 60-metre setback and floors at flood construction level plus 0.6 metres. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- Heritage Conservation Area buildings carry controls beyond strata rules; check OCP Schedule C. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- Read the depreciation report, Form B and strata insurance before buying (provincial guidance). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
