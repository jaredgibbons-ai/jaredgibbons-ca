## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- A Chilliwack duplex search covers the whole City of Chilliwack, in the Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD), from Chilliwack Proper north of Highway 1 to Sardis south of it. Its duplex history is written in rezoning notices — for example, 10494 McDonald Road in 2017, rezoning one lot to allow one duplex. https://www.chilliwack.com/apps/notices/noticeView.cfm?attachID=2003
- Chilliwack is in the Chilliwack and District Real Estate Board (CADREB) area; CADREB publishes no area statistics, so none are cited. City-wide facts in full are in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Chilliwack duplexes come in two ownership forms, separated in the City's own reporting: its building permit report has distinct categories for "New Two-Family Duplex (Fee simple)" and "New Two-Family Duplex (Strata)." A fee-simple half is a separate lot; a strata half carries strata documents. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2187/03%5FMARCH%5F2026%5FBuilding%5FMonth%5FEnd%5FReport%2Epdf
- Older stock was built one rezoning at a time: public-hearing notices from 2017 to 2019 show lots rezoned from R1-A (One Family Residential) to R1-B (One and Two Family Residential) "to facilitate construction of a duplex," including 46916 Acorn Avenue and 7167 Ramsay Place. https://www.chilliwack.com/apps/notices/noticeView.cfm?attachID=2003
- No named duplex subdivision or developer-built duplex complex was confirmed from a municipal or developer source; Chilliwack duplexes are individual buildings on individual lots, and none is invented here.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Chilliwack's zoning is "Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000," as amended, and the pre-2024 duplex zone was R1-B (One and Two Family Residential): an R1-A lot needed a rezoning, with a public hearing, before a duplex could be built. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- That changed with the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules: the City must now allow up to four units on single-detached or duplex-zoned lots greater than 280 square metres inside the urban growth boundary, affecting approximately 11,000 properties; duplexes are a listed form. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3279/FAQ_-_Small-Scale_Housing_-_Updated_2025_07_28.pdf
- Council adopted the amendments reluctantly and kept parking at two off-street spaces per unit, instead of the province's recommended 0.5 to 1 — a new duplex should expect four spaces to be the local test. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=37&prshow=details&prID=574
- The City's small-scale guide caps floor area ratio at 0.75, keeps the lot's own zone rules, and records R1-B as "Duplex (no suites)," while an R1-A house may take a suite or coach house. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3279/FAQ_-_Small-Scale_Housing_-_Updated_2025_07_28.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Do I still need a rezoning to build a duplex in Chilliwack? **A:** Usually not, if the lot qualifies for small-scale housing. The City must allow up to four units on single-detached or duplex-zoned lots over 280 square metres inside the urban growth boundary, and duplexes are a listed form. Before 2024 the same project on an R1-A lot required an R1-B rezoning and a public hearing, as the McDonald Road notice shows. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3279/FAQ_-_Small-Scale_Housing_-_Updated_2025_07_28.pdf
- **Q:** Can each side of a duplex also have a suite? **A:** Not on the City's published table for the R1-B duplex form, recorded as "Duplex (no suites)." Other combinations exist in other zones — a house plus a suite, or a house plus a coach house — and the City says to confirm all other zones with Planning staff. Get that answer in writing for the address before counting suite income. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3279/FAQ_-_Small-Scale_Housing_-_Updated_2025_07_28.pdf
- **Q:** What is the difference between a fee-simple and a strata duplex here? **A:** Chilliwack's permit report tracks them separately — "New Two-Family Duplex (Fee simple)" and "New Two-Family Duplex (Strata)" — because the ownership differs. A fee-simple half is a separate lot with its own title; a strata half is a strata lot with bylaws and fees. The building can look identical, so title settles the form. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2187/03%5FMARCH%5F2026%5FBuilding%5FMonth%5FEnd%5FReport%2Epdf
- **Q:** How much parking must a new Chilliwack duplex provide? **A:** Two off-street spaces per unit — the standard the City maintained when it adopted the provincial amendments, rather than the recommended 0.5 to 1 space per unit. For a duplex that is four spaces, and parking is often the constraint on a narrow Chilliwack Proper lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=37&prshow=details&prID=574

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- A pre-SSMUH duplex may carry its own rezoning in its history, so the neighbouring house on the same street may not show the same permission in its file, even though current small-scale rules now cover both lots. https://www.chilliwack.com/apps/notices/noticeView.cfm?attachID=2003
- The 2019 duplex rezoning at 7167 Ramsay Place needed a concurrent development variance permit to reduce the R1-B minimum lot width from 20 metres to 19.5 metres — lot width, not just area, has decided real Chilliwack duplex applications. https://chilliwack.com/main/notices/RZ1364%20%20DVP1084%20-%207167%20Ramsay%20Place1.pdf
- Collection changed May 1, 2026: compostables weekly, garbage and recycling alternating bi-weekly, monthly glass and flexible plastics — a two-household duplex shares one collection day and one curb space for both units' carts. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=818

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- BC Transit, not TransLink, runs Chilliwack transit. Route 51 Vedder runs Downtown to the University of the Fraser Valley (UFV) campus via Vedder Road through Sardis, and Route 52 Evans runs Downtown to Cottonwood Mall via Evans Road — the two corridors most duplex buyers will use. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=51&schedule=2024-07-05
- Highway 1 is the city's east-west highway, with Sardis south of it and Chilliwack Proper north; no drive time is stated, because no operator source measured one. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Floodplain status is the main Chilliwack duplex risk: much of the city lies in the floodplain of the Fraser, Vedder or Sumas rivers or a local creek, and Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets the Flood Construction Level plus 0.6 metres freeboard for new construction. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- The same bylaw sets a 75-metre building setback from the natural boundary of the Vedder River and 60 metres from the Fraser River — limits a duplex addition must respect. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- Subdivision carries an open conflict: the City's subdivisions webpage names a "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055," while the verified ledger holds it was never adopted. Confirm the governing bylaw in writing before planning a fee-simple split — see NOT CONFIRMED. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
