# Central Abbotsford Area Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: central-abbotsford-guide (Central Abbotsford, Abbotsford; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Central Abbotsford is the city core centred on South Fraser Way, not a single official City neighbourhood. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/HDNP%2BCommercial%2BMarket%2BStudy.pdf
- The City Centre is bordered approximately by Peardonville Road and Mill Lake to the south, Ware Street, Mill Lake Road and Horn Street to the east, Parkview Street and Tims Street to the west, and a line roughly parallel to George Ferguson Way and Maclure Road to the north. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/HDNP%2BCommercial%2BMarket%2BStudy.pdf
- The Historic Downtown focused redevelopment area is bounded by Pine Street, Cyril Street, McDougall Avenue and McCallum Road. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/Historic%20Downtown%20Neighbourhood%20Plan%20Final%20Event%20Boards.pdf
- Two adopted plans cover the core: the City Centre Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2913-2019) and the Historic Downtown Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2920-2019), under Abbotsforward 2050. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- Abbotsford West lies to the west, Abbotsford East to the east, and Poplar to the northwest. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Zoning Bylaw 2400-2014 governs all of Abbotsford, and this note carries the shared city-wide facts in full for the cluster. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- Section 140 allows 3 units on lots 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots greater than 280 m² in the stated conditions. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- Section 140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- The small-scale multi-unit housing amendment is Bylaw No. 3560-2024, adopted June 25, 2024. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/
- A secondary suite must be less than 50% of the principal unit, and garden suites and coach houses may occupy up to 15% of the lot to a maximum of 100 m². https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- Historic Downtown designations include Urban 1 – Midrise up to 6 storeys and Urban 2 – Ground Oriented for rowhouses, townhouses, multiplexes or duplexes. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/Historic%20Downtown%20Neighbourhood%20Plan%20Final%20Event%20Boards.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which plans govern Central Abbotsford? **A:** Two adopted neighbourhood plans govern the core. The City Centre Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2913-2019) and the Historic Downtown Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2920-2019) sit under Abbotsforward 2050, and a buyer should check which plan area the lot falls in before assuming height or form. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- **Q:** Can a detached home here have a suite? **A:** Yes, one secondary suite is permitted in all single detached dwellings. Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8 states the permission, subject to bylaw conditions including size limits, so a buyer should confirm the suite meets the current section before counting rental use. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Q:** How is the school catchment checked? **A:** Enter the primary home address in the School District 34 (SD34) School Locator. SD34 states the locator determines the neighbourhood catchment school and registration starts at that school, so street reputation alone does not settle the assignment for any central address. https://swift.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/kindergartenregistration/swift
- **Q:** What did the locator return for central test addresses? **A:** Both test addresses return the same chain. 33130 Bevan Avenue returns Godson Elementary, Abbotsford Middle and Abbotsford Senior Secondary, and 32161 Dormick Avenue returns Dormick Park Elementary with the same middle and secondary schools. https://abbymiddle.abbyschools.ca/node/10331
- **Q:** Is the core in the Sumas Prairie floodplain? **A:** No, the central upland is not Sumas Prairie. The City describes Sumas Prairie as low-lying land in East Abbotsford, but Willband Creek runs through the central area, so a parcel-level floodplain check is still required for a specific lot. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Four public schools share the Bevan Avenue corridor in the core. Godson Elementary is at 33130 Bevan Avenue, Dormick Park Elementary at 32161 Dormick Avenue, Abbotsford Middle at 33231 Bevan Avenue and Abbotsford Senior Secondary at 33355 Bevan Avenue. https://godson.abbyschools.ca/
- Mill Lake Park is accessed from Bevan Avenue and Emerson Street, and Centennial Outdoor Pool is at 2310 Emerson Street. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds
- Clearbrook Library is at 32320 George Ferguson Way, and the Abbotsford library branch shares 33355 Bevan Avenue with Abbotsford Senior Secondary. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/
- The Gur Sikh Temple at 33089 South Fraser Way is designated a National Historic Site and is described by Parks Canada as the oldest surviving Gurdwara. https://www.pc.gc.ca/culture/designation/lieu-site/temple-sikh-abbotsford-sikh-temple
- Abbotsford Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at Unit 100, 2692 Clearbrook Road, at the west side of the central area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Abbotsford's 2026 residential tax rate is set by Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026, expressed per $1,000 of taxable value. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf
- For Class 1 residential, the bylaw's Table 1 components are 1.08284, 1.78296, 0.08582, 0.11583 and 0.08836 per $1,000. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf
- Property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- Effective March 1, 2026, the residential water rate is $1.46 per cubic metre and the sewer rate is $1.35 per cubic metre, after a Council-approved 4% increase to each in January 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/water-sewer-rates
- Water and sewer customers are billed bi-monthly, except Clearbrook Waterworks District customers, who are billed quarterly for sewer. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/current-utility-bill
- Starting January 2027, solid waste fees move onto the utility bill alongside water and sewer and no longer appear on the annual Property Tax Notice. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing
- The City of Abbotsford is listed by the Province as a designated taxable area for the Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so owners must complete the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Bourquin Exchange is the central transit exchange. BC Transit Central Fraser Valley routes named for the core include 1 (Highstreet/University of the Fraser Valley), 2, 4, 5 (Clearbrook/Bourquin via Hospital), 12, 17 and 21 (Aldergrove/Abbotsford). https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- Highway 1 interchanges at McCallum Road and Clearbrook Road serve the central area, west toward Langley and Surrey and east toward Chilliwack. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/HDNP%2BCommercial%2BMarket%2BStudy.pdf
- Highway 11 north from the core leads to Mission. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/HDNP%2BCommercial%2BMarket%2BStudy.pdf
- There is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in Abbotsford. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check floodplain status lot by lot in the City WebMap, even in the upland core. Willband Creek runs through the Historic Downtown and central area, and the City flood page separates this area from Sumas Prairie without clearing any single parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- Check the plan designation before a rebuild in Historic Downtown, because Urban 1, Urban 2 and Urban 3 designations set different expected forms. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/Historic%20Downtown%20Neighbourhood%20Plan%20Final%20Event%20Boards.pdf
- Confirm Agricultural Land Reserve status in the Official Community Plan maps and WebMap for any edge lot; no central parcel was confirmed as ALR-free here. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
