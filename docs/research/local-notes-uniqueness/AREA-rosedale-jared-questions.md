# Rosedale — Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer, for a voice memo. Muse does not answer these.

## Questions

1. Who buys in Rosedale in your deals — farm families who need ALR land, buyers priced out of Chilliwack Proper and Sardis who still want a house, or Agassiz and Harrison commuters? What are they leaving behind?
2. How do buyers react when they learn Rosedale Traditional Community School becomes a Grades 6–8 middle school in September 2027 and the K–5 kids move to the new McGrath Road elementary — does the school change help or hurt a Rosedale listing?
3. What sells fast and what sits — village houses on Yale Road, acreages on McGrath Road, or working ALR farms? Where do buyers draw the line on farm smells, dike-side lots and properties outside the dike system?
4. What surprises buyers — the Agassiz-Rosedale Bridge rehabilitation running to 2029 with single-lane alternating traffic, rural V0X addressing and services, or how thin the Route 71 bus service is compared with in-town Chilliwack?
5. One story: a Rosedale deal that taught you something — a farm or village property you now recommend or would steer a buyer away from, and why.
