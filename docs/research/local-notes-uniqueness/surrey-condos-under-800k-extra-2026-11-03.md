## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- This band covers the whole City of Surrey, not one neighbourhood — Surrey runs from the Fraser River in the north to the United States border in the south, between Delta and Langley. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- Surrey-wide tax, zoning and utility facts are treated in full in the earliest Surrey note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note.
- The City divides Surrey into six communities — Cloverdale, Fleetwood, Guildford, Newton, South Surrey and Whalley — and condos in this band sit in all six, with the tower stock concentrated in Whalley/City Centre. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- In this band the stock is strata apartment condos, not detached houses — the buyer owns a strata lot and shares the common property through the strata corporation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- City Centre in Whalley is where the City identifies emerging residential high rises, while the other five communities stay primarily lower-density — so a sub-$800k search mixes Whalley towers with low-rise buildings elsewhere in Surrey. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Every strata with five or more lots must now carry a current depreciation report — stratas in Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley without one, or with one from before December 31, 2020, had to obtain one by July 1, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- A depreciation report projects maintenance, repair and replacement costs over 30 years, and stratas may no longer defer getting one by an annual three-quarter vote. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What does the $800k band actually buy in Surrey? **A:** It buys a strata condo, and the documents matter as much as the unit. Because almost every home in this band is a strata lot, the purchase turns on the strata's depreciation report, its contingency reserve position and its repair history over the next 30 years, not only on the suite's finishes or floor plan. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q:** Can a strata still ban long-term rentals? **A:** No — British Columbia (BC) strata rental-restriction bylaws for residential lots are void, although a strata may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. A listing or an old bylaw package that says rentals are not allowed for a Surrey condo in this band is out of date for long-term renting; check the current registered bylaws instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q:** Is there an age-restricted building risk in this band? **A:** Only a 55-and-over bylaw can still restrict who lives in a BC strata. Any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since November 24, 2022, under sections 123.1 and 123.2 of the Strata Property Act, with exemptions for spouses, caregivers and legacy residents — so ask specifically whether a building is 55-plus, not whether it is adult-oriented. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** Does the Speculation and Vacancy Tax apply to a Surrey condo? **A:** Yes — the Province lists the City of Surrey as a designated taxable area, so every residential owner, including a condo owner in this band, must file the annual Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) declaration even when an exemption applies. Budget for the declaration step every year, not only in the purchase year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- A strata's annual budget must contribute at least 10 percent of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund (CRF) — read the CRF balance in that light before judging whether fees look low. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- The Form B Information Certificate must disclose strata fees, amounts owed, approved-but-unpaid special levies, the CRF balance, parking and storage allocations and an insurance summary, and the strata must provide it within seven days of a request. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- King George Boulevard Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) is at 30 percent design and not yet fully funded, per the City's October 20, 2025 transit update — do not price a City Centre condo as if the BRT were operating. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R216.pdf
- Newton hosts a Vaisakhi Parade the City says draws more than 400,000 visitors a year, with closures around 128 Street and 72 Avenue on parade day — relevant to Newton condos in this band. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities

## 8. GETTING AROUND

- TransLink's R1 King George Boulevard RapidBus runs Newton Exchange to Guildford Exchange via Surrey Central and King George stations, at least every 10 minutes at peak and 15 minutes off-peak, 6 a.m. to midnight. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the depreciation report, not the listing photos — it is the document that shows the 30-year repair list and the funding models behind any future special levy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Check the Form B insurance summary for the water-damage deductible, because deductibles are routinely charged back to the unit a leak started in. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
