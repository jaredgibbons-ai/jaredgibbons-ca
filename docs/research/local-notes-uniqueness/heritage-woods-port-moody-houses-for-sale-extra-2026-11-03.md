## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is the secondary school in Heritage Woods? **A:** Yes. Heritage Woods Secondary, Grades 9 to 12, is at 1300 David Avenue inside the neighbourhood, and route 183 names its stop David at Heritage Woods Secondary. Catchment still depends on the home address in School District No. 43 (SD43). https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043 https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt183.pdf
- **Q:** Aspenwood or Heritage Mountain elementary? **A:** Both border this area: Aspenwood Elementary is at 2001 Panorama Drive on the Heritage Woods side, Heritage Mountain Elementary at 125 Ravine Drive west. SD43 catchment is address-based, and locator research for 1300 David Avenue returned Heritage Mountain Elementary, so check it. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** Where is middle school? **A:** Eagle Mountain Middle, Grades 6 to 8, at 110 Dogwood Drive in Anmore is the middle school in the provincial list for this catchment family. It sits outside Port Moody, north of David Avenue, so the trip crosses into Anmore. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** Can I walk to SkyTrain? **A:** No station is in Heritage Woods. Route 183 runs from Coquitlam Central Station via Panorama Drive, Forest Park Way, David Avenue and Heritage Mountain Boulevard to Inlet Centre and Moody Centre stations, so rail is a bus ride east or west. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt183.pdf
- **Q:** Where is the Bert Flinn trailhead here? **A:** The Bert Flinn Loop Trail starts at Heritage Woods Secondary and North Shore Community Park, 1300 David Avenue, with the park entrance at David Avenue and Heritage Mountain Boulevard, at Heritage Woods' west edge, not at the inlet, for these houses. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Bert Flinn-backing houses are forest-interface lots: OCP interface management applies under the Community Wildfire Protection Plan. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/lmwddork/chapter-5-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670724
- Development Permit Area 5 (Hazardous Lands, Steep Slopes) applies with Area 4 on hillside lots. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- Creek lots need a Qualified Environmental Professional report, top-of-bank survey, section 5.4 setbacks before design. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- City hazards page: Port Moody is surrounded by forests, so wildfire risk applies; see FireSmart Canada. https://www.portmoody.ca/resident-services/hazards/
