# Hatzic — Questions for Jared

1. In the Hatzic deals you have actually seen, which side sells — bench houses inside the City of Mission, Hatzic Prairie acreages, or manufactured homes on pads — and does the buyer usually understand before viewing that half of "Hatzic" is not in the City at all?
2. Who is buying the 55+ manufactured homes at Mission Mobile Home Park in your experience — local downsizers from Mission and Abbotsford, or people coming from further away for the price point — and what do they get wrong about pad tenancy before you explain it?
3. What surprises Hatzic buyers most after they buy: the dyke and flood insurance conversation, the rural tax notice coming from the Province instead of the City, school catchment for a lake or prairie address, or something else you hear repeatedly?
4. How do Hatzic Lake and Hatzic Island properties actually trade — do docks, beach access and seasonal-use limits at the resorts hold value the way listing text claims, or do buyers discount them once they read the tenure documents?
5. Tell one Hatzic story only you could tell: a deal, a showing or a listing moment on the bench, the prairie or the island that captures what selling in Hatzic is really like.
