## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is in Oxford Heights? **A:** École Irvine Elementary, kindergarten to Grade 5, at 3862 Wellington Street, inside this pattern. School District No. 43 (SD43) assigns by permanent home address and cross-catchment approval is not guaranteed, so confirm the designated school for the exact address in the School Locator Tool. http://sd43.bc.ca/cross-catchment
- **Q:** Is Castle Park Elementary the Oxford Heights school? **A:** No — checked and not confirmed. Castle Park Elementary is at 1144 Confederation Drive, with Hazel Trembath Elementary at 1278 Confederation Drive and École Citadel Middle at 1265 Citadel Drive: the Citadel pattern on the west side of the city, not Wellington or Oxford Street. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-coquitlam
- **Q:** Which buses run on its streets? **A:** Routes 173 (Cedar) and 174 (Rocklin). The 173 runs via Oxford Street, Coquitlam Avenue and a Wellington Street stop toward Prairie Avenue and Cedar Drive; the 174 returns via Greenmount, Wellington, Mason and Oxford. Both link Port Coquitlam Station and Coquitlam Central Station in each direction. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt174.pdf
- **Q:** What recreation centre serves this side? **A:** Hyde Creek Recreation Centre, 1379 Laurier Avenue, beside Minnekhada Middle School, 1390 Laurier Avenue, and Hyde Creek Nature Reserve, 1457 Lincoln Drive. The City lists Hyde Creek among its facility addresses; confirm current programs on the City facility page before visiting. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/recreation-parks/parks-trails/parks-directory?page=1
