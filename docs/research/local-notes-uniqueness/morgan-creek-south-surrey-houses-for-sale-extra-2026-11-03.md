## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary schools serve Morgan Creek? **A:** Rosemary Heights and Morgan elementary schools serve this area, per the City, at 15516 36 Avenue and 3366 156A Street respectively. Confirm the exact address in the district School Locator before buying, because catchment follows the address, not the neighbourhood name or the nearest school building. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/rosemary-heights-ncps
- **Q:** Which secondary school do students attend? **A:** Grandview Heights Secondary, on the district's own letter: its December 4, 2020 letter lists Morgan and Rosemary Heights among the feeder elementary schools making up the catchment for the secondary school at 16987 25 Avenue. (check current — letter dated 2020) https://media.surreyschools.ca/media/Default/medialib/ghss-parent-letter-dec-4-docx12.4f356085095.pdf
- **Q:** Is the golf course private for residents? **A:** No — it is a public course. Morgan Creek Golf Course describes itself as open to the public year-round at 3500 Morgan Creek Way, so non-residents play the same fairways that residents back onto, and living beside it is not required to play. http://morgancreekgolf.com/
- **Q:** How do I confirm a catchment? **A:** Use the district School Locator tool. Surrey Schools states your catchment school is determined by your address and where it falls within the district's School Boundaries Map, and directs families to input the address into the Locator on its registration page. https://www.surreyschools.ca/chs/registration-1
Read: 2026-11-03
