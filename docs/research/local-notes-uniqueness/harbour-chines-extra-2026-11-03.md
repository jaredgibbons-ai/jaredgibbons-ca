## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The rule that matters most on creek and ravine lots here is the watercourse permit area. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- Coquitlam's Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers land within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- Zoning Bylaw section 519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and section 523 covers riparian areas. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH), suites and carriage homes, tax, utilities and Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) rules for Coquitlam are treated in full — see the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; the Harbour Chines angle is only that lot size and municipal services decide eligibility lot by lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Harbour Chines in Port Coquitlam? **A:** No — Harbour Chines is in the City of Coquitlam, on the Chines slope around Como Lake Avenue in central Coquitlam. The name misleads buyers because it contains the word Harbour, but no source read places it in Port Coquitlam and it has no waterfront. File taxes, schools and permits with Coquitlam and School District 43 (SD43). https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Q:** Which schools will my child attend? **A:** Catchments in SD43 are set by exact address, so no Harbour Chines catchment is named until the address is run in the SD43 School Locator. A nearby City Centre sample address returned Glen Elementary, École Maple Creek Middle and Pinetree Secondary, but that sample is not confirmed as a Harbour Chines catchment. Run the exact address before relying on any school name. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** Can I add a suite or carriage home? **A:** Possibly — Coquitlam's small-scale multi-unit housing rules allow added units including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes where zoning and municipal services allow, and lots not connected to municipal water or sewer fall outside the rules. The full unit-count table is in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; confirm the lot's zone and services with the City first. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Q:** Do I need a development permit near a creek? **A:** Yes, if the work is within 30 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse, a Watercourse Protection Development Permit can be required in this part of Coquitlam. Zoning Bylaw sections 519 and 523 also set flood and riparian setbacks. Check the lot on the City's permit pages before pricing a renovation, addition or rebuild. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Q:** Is Como Lake Park in this area? **A:** Como Lake Park is the named City park for the Como Lake area of central Coquitlam, at 700 Gatensbury Street. It is the local green space buyers associate with the Como Lake slope streets. Confirm which park serves a specific Harbour Chines street with the City, because no park inside the sub-area boundary itself was confirmed on a City page. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Como-Lake-Park-26

- School District 43 Programs of Choice — French Immersion, Mandarin Bilingual and Montessori — are entered by application, not by catchment. http://sd43.bc.ca/cross-catchment
- Cross-catchment applications in SD43 are decided by a lottery / random draw with in-district priority. http://sd43.bc.ca/cross-catchment
- The nearest library branch is Coquitlam Public Library City Centre at 1169 Pinetree Way, with the Poirier branch at 575 Poirier Street. https://www.coqlibrary.ca/
