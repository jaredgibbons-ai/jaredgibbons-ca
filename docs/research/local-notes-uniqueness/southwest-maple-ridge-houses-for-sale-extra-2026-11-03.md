# Southwest Maple Ridge Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: southwest-maple-ridge-houses-for-sale (Southwest Maple Ridge; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Southwest Maple Ridge has no City plan under that name — the official frame is Hammond, in the southwest corner, bounded on the west by the City of Pitt Meadows and the Katzie First Nation, on the south by the Fraser River, and on the north by Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road commercial areas, with single-family development to the east. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- A second official frame is the Fraser River Escarpment policy area, bounded by 207 Street, 124 Avenue, 224 Street and the crest of the Fraser River Escarpment — that box covers most of the detached-house streets in this sub-area. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- In street terms the sub-area runs from about 203 Street at the Pitt Meadows boundary east to about 224 Street, and from Dewdney Trunk Road and 124 Avenue south to River Road and the Fraser River. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- City-wide Maple Ridge facts: see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Hammond's named character is early-1900s single-detached houses and a historic commercial area, alongside a 5.4-hectare baseball park and a 16-hectare golf course at 20818 Golf Lane — the plan's own list of prominent features. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- The 2025 Hammond update changes the edge, not the core: it introduces Maple Meadows Residential and Maple Meadows Mixed-Use designations with increased density near Maple Meadows Station, while the interior Hammond streets remain single-family in the plan description. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-advances-housing-strategy-completion-area-plan-updates
- Hammond Park at 20577 Lorne Avenue, with the Hammond outdoor pool, is the named park inside the house streets; Maple Ridge Golf Course at 20818 Golf Lane is the large open-space block on the south side. https://www.mapleridge.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Hammond-Park-58

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Hammond Area Plan (Official Community Plan Chapter 10.5) was adopted January 24, 2017, and its update was adopted November 25, 2025, enabling mixed-use, higher-density buildings near transit — check whether a house is in the Maple Meadows station-area designations or the interior residential area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- The Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan, adopted January 27, 2026 (Official Community Plan Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021), covers Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road from the western boundary to 221 Street and directs growth around future Bus Rapid Transit stations at 203 Street, 207 Street, Laity Street and 216 Street; the City states it does not change current zoning by itself. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- Inside the escarpment policy area (207 Street to 224 Street), storm drainage must be carried to ditches or sewers, no discharge is allowed into river bank ravines or over river bank slopes except by continuous pipe to river level, and new construction requires sanitary sewers rather than septic fields. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- The City ran a Hammond Heritage Character Area process in 2021 to look at conserving heritage character in Hammond; it concluded July 13, 2021 — heritage is a live local issue for the early-1900s houses, even though no separate Southwest plan exists. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a Hammond house in a floodplain? **A:** Hammond fronts the Fraser River to the south, and the Hammond Area Plan includes a Hammond Area Floodplain map, so flood status is address-specific here. The City also publishes a citywide flood preparedness guide covering Fraser River flooding and river monitoring. Ask for the floodplain map check for the exact Lorne Avenue, Westfield Avenue or River Road address before removing subjects. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- **Q:** Which schools serve Southwest Maple Ridge houses? **A:** The School District 42 schools physically in this area are Hammond Elementary (K–7) at 11520 203 Street, Maple Ridge Elementary (K–7) at 20820 River Road, and Westview Secondary (Grades 8–12) at 20905 Wicklund Avenue. Catchment is still by exact address, so confirm a 207 Street or 216 Street address in the district locator rather than assuming the nearest school. https://elementary.sd42.ca/hammond/
- **Q:** Will density near Maple Meadows Station change my street? **A:** Only the station-area designations changed in the 2025 Hammond update. The City states the update focuses growth near Maple Meadows Station with Maple Meadows Residential and Mixed-Use designations and increased density near transit, while protecting employment lands. An interior Hammond street away from the station is not given the same designation. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-advances-housing-strategy-completion-area-plan-updates
- **Q:** Can I use a septic field on an escarpment lot? **A:** No for new construction in the escarpment policy area. The City's Fraser River Escarpment package, for the area bounded by 207 Street, 124 Avenue, 224 Street and the escarpment crest, states new construction requires sanitary sewers rather than septic fields, and storm water must be piped rather than discharged over the bank. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Hammond Multi-Use Park with baseball fields and Maple Ridge Golf Course share the address 20818 Golf Lane in City listings — the golf course lands are also the site the City is planning as a new multi-use community park (see Section 6). https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/city-projects
- Port Haney, at this area's east edge, was historically the steamboat landing and the Canadian Pacific Railway station for the area, with heritage buildings relocated there for preservation, per the City's neighbourhoods page. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- West Coast Express serves this area at two stations, Maple Meadows Station and Port Haney Station, and the City states the line runs five trips daily. https://www.mapleridge.ca/node/3153
- Route 744 is the Hammond loop bus: TransLink's timetable runs it between Haney Place and Meadowtown via Port Haney and Maple Meadows stations, looping through Hammond via 224 Street, 216 Street, 207 Street, Laity Street and Dewdney Trunk Road. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt744.pdf
- There is no library branch in the southwest; Maple Ridge Public Library is at 130 – 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road in the Town Centre. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/maple-ridge-public-library

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- Hammond Aquatics and Recreation Centre — planned at Hammond Community Park (City project listing: 20601 Westfield Avenue): the City's June 19, 2026 Recreation Ready update gives schematic designs for a new year-round aquatics and recreation centre in Hammond, with construction of the aquatics centre phase estimated to start in 2029, after the multi-use park in 2027 and the Albion arena expansion in 2028. Status read 2026-11-03: schematic design, funding decision process under way. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-shares-designs-and-funding-strategy-three-new-recreation-facilities
- Hammond Multi-Use Community Park — at the current Maple Ridge Golf Course site, 20818 Golf Lane: the City describes a 40-acre multi-use park relocating two baseball fields from Hammond Community Park, with Phase 1 construction estimated to begin in 2027. Status read 2026-11-03: planned, schematic design published. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-shares-designs-and-funding-strategy-three-new-recreation-facilities
- Recreation Ready assent vote — the three projects (Hammond Aquatics and Recreation Centre, Hammond Multi-Use Community Park Phase 1 at $23 million preliminary estimate, and the Albion arena expansion) were directed to an October 17, 2026 assent vote on long-term borrowing, with a total preliminary estimate of $393 million for all three. Status read 2026-11-03: vote directed; result not stated on the City page read. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/be-recreation-ready-and-stay-informed-oct-17-assent-vote
- Lougheed Transit Corridor applications — from December 9, 2025 Council directed staff to begin accepting and processing development applications and pre-application requests in the corridor; the plan itself was adopted January 27, 2026. Status read 2026-11-03: applications open, no individual Southwest project named on the plan page. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the Fraser River Escarpment Construction Guidelines (Council policy, approved March 23, 2004) for any lot between 207 Street and 224 Street above the river: they control surface and groundwater discharge to protect the slopes, and require an engineer's approach to drainage and construction. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- Check the Hammond Area Floodplain map in the Hammond Area Plan for any lot south toward River Road — the plan maps floodplain inside Hammond itself, not just along the river bank. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- Escarpment hazards the City lists for river-bank lands include flooding, mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence, with a Section 219 covenant and professional engineer's report required for development on those lands (see the East Central note for the same package applied at Port Haney). http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
