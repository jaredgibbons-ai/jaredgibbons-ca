## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Townhouse stock sits in four pockets: Citadel (Citadel Drive), Riverwood (Fremont Street), Glenwood (Prairie Avenue) and Central (Atkins Avenue). https://portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- The Canadian Pacific Railway yard and Lougheed Highway split the city; Coast Meridian Road crosses both on the Coast Meridian Overpass, opened March 2010. https://www.canadianconsultingengineer.com/features/award-of-excellence-transportation-coast-meridian-overpass/

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Citadel Pointe, 678 and 688 Citadel Drive, is the verified anchor: freehold strata, Plan LMS3766, built 1999, gated duplex-style townhouses. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/development-permits/development-permit
- Shaughnessy Park, 3655 Shaughnessy Street, is a 32-home townhouse complex carried in the Zoning Bylaw as Comprehensive Development Zone CD3, named by that address. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf
- Most townhouse sites are zoned site-specifically under Zoning Bylaw, 2008, No. 3630 — CD zones are listed by address (CD24: 1485 Coast Meridian; CD8: 2211–17 Prairie Avenue); the general townhouse zone is RTh1. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Every townhouse project sits in a form-and-character Development Permit Area: design, landscaping, siting and finishes are regulated by Development Permit under the Official Community Plan. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/development-permits/development-permit
- Lots near a watercourse need a Watercourse Development Permit too — the creek-side complexes in Riverwood and along Hyde Creek are the ones to check. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/development-permits/watercourse-development
- New townhouse construction carries an Amenity Cost Charge under Bylaw, 2026, No. 4449, which defines "Ground-Oriented Multi Family" to include townhouse and rowhouse forms. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/4449-amenity-cost-charge-bylawpdf
- A Development Permit cannot be approved until any rezoning or Official Community Plan amendment for the project has final approval. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/land-use-subdivision/rezoning-application

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Which schools serve a townhouse in Citadel?
**A:** School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) lists Castle Park Elementary, 1144 Confederation Drive (Kindergarten to Grade 5), and École Citadel Middle, 1265 Citadel Drive (Grades 6 to 8), both on Citadel's own streets. Catchments are address-based, so confirm the exact townhouse address with the district. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-coquitlam

**Q:** How does the West Coast Express commute work from PoCo?
**A:** Port Coquitlam Station, off Kingsway Avenue south of the Canadian Pacific Railway tracks, opened in 1995 with the West Coast Express line to Waterfront Station, and TransLink records 280 park-and-ride spaces there. Trains are weekday commuter service; off-peak trips are buses and the Mary Hill Bypass. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

**Q:** Bylaws say rentals are allowed "with restrictions." Can my tenant be blocked?
**A:** No, not for a long-term tenancy. Rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable in British Columbia since November 24, 2022, whatever the registered text says; the line recorded for Citadel Pointe is old bylaw text. A 55-plus age bylaw and short-term rental bylaws still bind. Confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

**Q:** Why do north-side and south-side townhouses feel like different cities?
**A:** Because one bridge joins them. The Coast Meridian Overpass, a 580-metre cable-stayed structure over the rail yard and Lougheed Highway, opened in March 2010 as the city's largest-ever transportation project; every north-side school, shopping and station trip still runs over it. https://www.canadianconsultingengineer.com/features/award-of-excellence-transportation-coast-meridian-overpass/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Traboulay PoCo Trail is a roughly 25-kilometre loop encircling the city, passing the Riverwood and Citadel townhouse pockets. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/explore-poco/tourist-information/
- PoCo Place, 2755 Lougheed Highway, is zoned CD2 — a site-specific zone, so any redevelopment there needs its own rezoning. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf
- Mayfair Terrace, 2266 Atkins Avenue, carries a mandatory base service plan on top of strata fees: the same complex operates as a registered retirement residence. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/mayfair-terrace-2266-atkins-ave
- Kew Lifestyles at Montrose, 2430 Mary Hill Road, is a 55-plus rental building completed in 2023 — nothing in it can be bought. https://www.soarchitects.com/portfolio/kew-lifestyles-at-montrose-condominiums/
- Citadel Middle School's athletic field, 1265 Citadel Drive, is in the City's parks directory — a City-booked venue, not just a school ground. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/recreation/parks-sports-fields-trails/parks-directory/
- Gates Park, 2300 Reeve Street, is mid-upgrade as the city's soccer hub; Phase 2 was anticipated by September 2026 in the City's capital program. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/2026-2027-capital-program-focuses-critical-infrastructure

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- In full in the mary-hill-port-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note: the 2026 residential rate under Annual Tax Rates Bylaw, 2026, No. 4441 is $2.1985 per $1,000 (general municipal); taxes were due July 2, 2026. https://portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/4441%20-%20Annual%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw.pdf
- Utilities are flat: a single unit pays $646 per year for water and $648 for sewer in 2026; each additional unit pays $481 water and $483 sewer — the line that matters when a townhouse carries a suite. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills
- Suited homes are billed for both the main dwelling and the suite; exemption applications are due annually by December 31. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills/secondary-suite-exemptions
- Port Coquitlam is a listed taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax; owners must declare annually. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Port Coquitlam Station is the rail anchor: West Coast Express trains to Waterfront Station, 280 park-and-ride spaces off Kingsway Avenue. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- The Coast Meridian Overpass carries Coast Meridian Road over the rail yard and Lougheed Highway to the Mary Hill Bypass (Highway 7B) and Highway 1. https://www.canadianconsultingengineer.com/features/award-of-excellence-transportation-coast-meridian-overpass/
- There is no SkyTrain station inside Port Coquitlam; the nearest are Coquitlam Central and Moody Centre, in neighbouring cities. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2013/03/evergreen-line-extension-station-names-announced/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the watercourse line first: a Watercourse Development Permit area covers land near the city's creeks, and the permit process must run before development proceeds. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/development-permits/watercourse-development
- For 1990s strata stock (Citadel Pointe, 1999), read the depreciation report's 30-year projection against the contingency reserve fund balance on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Riverwood and Citadel sit between the Pitt River and Fraser River dike systems the Traboulay PoCo Trail follows — confirm dike and drainage position with the City. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/explore-poco/tourist-information/
