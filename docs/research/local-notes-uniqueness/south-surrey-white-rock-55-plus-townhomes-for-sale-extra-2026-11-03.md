## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- This page spans two municipalities: South Surrey is part of the City of Surrey, while White Rock is its own city, so a townhome on the Surrey side follows Surrey bylaws and taxes and a townhome a few blocks away in White Rock follows White Rock's, even though both use School District No. 36 (Surrey). https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/seniors/age-friendly-strategy-action-plan
- The commercial and services core is the Semiahmoo Town Centre area around 152 Street and 16 Avenue in South Surrey, where the City's town centre planning has focused apartment and townhouse growth, with single-family pockets between 154 and 157 Streets and 17 and 18 Avenues debated in that plan process. https://www.surrey.ca/files/STCP%20July%202019%20Engagement%20Summary.pdf
- South Surrey Recreation and Arts Centre at 14601 20 Avenue is the recreation anchor for the area, and White Rock Community Centre at 15154 Russell Avenue sits just across the municipal line in White Rock. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/January-2026-South-Newsletter.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a South Surrey 55+ townhome in Surrey or White Rock for taxes? **A:** Check the municipal line, not the page name. South Surrey townhomes pay City of Surrey tax, whose 2026 municipal total for residential property is $1.84251 per $1,000 of assessed value on the City's rate page, while a White Rock address pays White Rock's separate rate, which is UNCONFIRMED in this pass. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- **Q:** Where are seniors programs for a 55+ townhome owner? **A:** At South Surrey Recreation and Arts Centre at 14601 20 Avenue. The City's South Surrey newsletter lists a Seniors Services membership at $30.00 per year for registered volunteer-led drop-in activities, events and clubs, plus discount pricing on select 55+ registered programs. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/January-2026-South-Newsletter.pdf
- **Q:** Is Amica White Rock a place I can buy a townhome? **A:** No. Amica White Rock at 15331 16 Avenue is a seniors' residence, named as such on the City of Surrey's own page for the entrance sculpture there, so it is rental residence living near the townhome area, not strata townhome ownership. https://www.surrey.ca/arts-culture/surrey-public-art/permanent-public-art-collection/waters-edge
- **Q:** Can I join a seniors program if I live on the White Rock side? **A:** Yes, for at least the phone-based art program. The City of Surrey states its We're All Artists telephone art program is for those age 55 plus living in Surrey and White Rock, run with Seniors Come Share Society, so that program crosses the municipal line even though taxes do not. https://www.Surrey.ca/arts-culture/surrey-art-gallery/learning/video-library/art-motion/were-all-artists-seniors-program
- **Q:** Will the Semiahmoo Town Centre change my street? **A:** Slowly, on the City's own account. Engagement material for the town centre records gradual change over many decades and strong resident concern about density, traffic and parking, so a buyer should read the current town centre plan for their block rather than assume the complex will be redeveloped soon (check current). https://www.surrey.ca/files/STCP%20July%202019%20Engagement%20Summary.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey's 2026 residential municipal total is $1.84251 per $1,000 of assessed value, made up on the City's rate page of a general municipal total of $1.68947 plus a roads and traffic levy of $0.15304, before school, TransLink and other levies added on the same page. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- White Rock's residential rate is a separate municipal figure and is UNCONFIRMED in this pass, so a single tax number cannot cover this whole page; confirm the side of the line before budgeting. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
