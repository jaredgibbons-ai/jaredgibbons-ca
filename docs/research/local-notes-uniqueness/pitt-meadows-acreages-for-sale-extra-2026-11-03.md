# Pitt Meadows Acreages — Extra Local Notes
Page: pitt-meadows-acreages-for-sale (Pitt Meadows; acreages).
## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Pitt Meadows is a majority-farm city, unlike its neighbours. The City states 78% of Pitt Meadows is agricultural land, and its Official Community Plan (OCP) states Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) lands comprise 78% of all land in the city. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/4740
- The farm fabric surrounds a compact urban town centre and is bounded by the Fraser, Pitt and Alouette rivers. The City's pump-station release describes a continuous dike system protecting the city at the confluence area of those rivers. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/new-9-million-pitt-polder-pump-station-complete
- Harris Road is a farm-area road the City itself uses as an address reference, for example for Grabenhorst Garden at 13895 Harris Road in the ALR and for the West Coast Express station at 12280 Harris Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/pitt-meadows-secures-80634-federal-funding-community-orchard-and

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The parcel fabric is large working farm parcels, not small estate lots. The City's A-1 General Agricultural zone requires a minimum lot area of 8 hectares for subdivision, which keeps the existing fabric in large holdings. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- The crops named by the City are dairy, cranberries, blueberries, nursery stock and greenhouse crops. The City also states Pitt Meadows ranks sixth among British Columbia municipalities for agricultural production and contributes 20% of British Columbia's overall blueberry production. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/4740
- A City-owned ALR example is Grabenhorst Garden at 13895 Harris Road, described by the City as a five-acre property within the ALR. It is a community garden and orchard, not a private acreage, but it shows the Harris Road farm fabric inside the city. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/pitt-meadows-secures-80634-federal-funding-community-orchard-and
- No named bare-land strata acreage subdivision was confirmed from a City page read for this note, so none is named here.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A-1 General Agricultural is the main farm zone, and its stated intent is to protect farming areas for food production consistent with the Agricultural Land Commission Act. The zone allows one farm house per lot with a maximum gross floor area of 500 square metres. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- Extra farm houses are tied to farm size, not to family preference. The A-1 rules in the consolidated zoning bylaw excerpt allow one additional farm house for farm operations over 8 hectares and under 40 hectares, and two additional farm houses for operations of 40 hectares or more. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- A garden suite in A-1 or A-2 has a lot-size split. The City's garden suite guide permits garden suites in A-1 and A-2 on lots under 2 acres, requires the suite to be 33 to 90 square metres and no more than 20% of lot area in A-1 and A-2, and says a suite on a lot over 2 acres in those zones needs Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) approval for a non-farm use. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- A garden suite must be on municipal services. The same City guide requires one additional off-street parking space and connection to municipal water, sewer and drainage utilities. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- Short-term rentals are not a fallback use for a farm house. The City states short-term rental units such as Airbnb and VRBO are not permitted anywhere in Pitt Meadows, while bed and breakfast operations must comply with Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011, section 5.6. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/171

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I subdivide a Pitt Meadows farm into smaller acreages? **A:** Not under the main farm zone as of right. The A-1 General Agricultural zone sets a minimum lot area of 8 hectares for subdivision, and most of the city is also in the ALR, where subdivision needs ALC approval. A buyer counting on two or three 2-hectare lots from one farm parcel is counting on approvals the City zoning does not grant by itself. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Q:** How much of this city is actually floodplain? **A:** Most of it, and the City says so directly. Its Pitt Polder Pump Station release states 86% of Pitt Meadows is in the floodplain, the area is divided into four drainage areas served by six pump stations, and protection relies on a continuous dike system of ditches, flood boxes and pumping stations. Any acreage offer should include dike, drainage and flood-insurance checks. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/new-9-million-pitt-polder-pump-station-complete
- **Q:** Can I add a detached suite for a parent or worker? **A:** Possibly, but lot size decides the approval path. The City allows a garden suite in A-1 and A-2 on lots under 2 acres at 33 to 90 square metres, while a lot over 2 acres in those zones needs ALC approval for a non-farm use, and in all cases the suite must connect to municipal water, sewer and drainage. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Q:** What crops will my neighbours actually be growing? **A:** Berry and dairy operations are the City's own description of its farm base. Pitt Meadows lists dairy production, cranberries and blueberries, nursery stock and greenhouse crops, ranks sixth in British Columbia for agricultural production and produces 20% of the province's blueberries, so spray, pollination, harvest traffic and farm hours are normal working conditions, not exceptions. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/4740

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Pitt Polder Pump Station protects more than 1,650 hectares of agricultural land. The City states the rebuilt station meets current provincial standards, has fish-friendly pumps, and included dike improvements and culvert replacements. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/new-9-million-pitt-polder-pump-station-complete
- Soil and fill on farm land is a City and ALC file, not a handshake. The City's agriculture page states it works closely with the ALC and other agencies on Soil and Fill Permit Applications for ALR land. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/4740
- The City has an Agricultural Viability Strategy to strengthen its farm sector. The City describes it as a comprehensive agricultural planning document for the long-term sustainability of local agriculture and the agri-food sector. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/4740
- Pitt Meadows supplies potable water for agricultural use, which is unusual in Metro Vancouver. An older City OCP section states agriculture is the largest non-residential water consumer and the three largest water users are agricultural operators — verify current service for a specific parcel with the City. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/our-community/community-plan/section_4_-_policies_-_4_10_utilities.pdf
- School catchment for a farm address is checked through School District 42's locator, the same district as Maple Ridge. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- Generic civic facts for Pitt Meadows are in the pitt-meadows-houses-for-sale note; this note keeps only farm, dike and ALR facts.

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- The land-relevant City project is the North Lougheed Area Plan, adopted September 29, 2026 by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026. The City states the area is about 50 hectares (125 acres) on the north side of Lougheed Highway between Meadows Gardens Golf Course and Harris Road, and that future development still needs further planning, technical studies and Council approvals. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/community-projects-and-initiatives/projects-and-studies/north-lougheed-area-plan
- That plan is explicitly tied to protecting surrounding farms. The City states it preserves surrounding agricultural properties and supports building the North Lougheed Connector to improve traffic flow, enable traffic calming on Old Dewdney Trunk Road and improve road safety in the agricultural area. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/community-projects-and-initiatives/projects-and-studies/north-lougheed-area-plan

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Development Cost Charges (DCCs) for a new single-family home are $23,183 per unit in total in the City's DCC rate summary. That summary is headed Development Cost Charges Bylaw 2995, 2024, adopted September 10, 2024, and breaks the single-family total into components including $13,876 and $6,371. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-04/v17-2010_DCC_Rate_Summary.pdf
- An Amenity Cost Charge (ACC) is charged on top of DCCs. The same City summary gives the City of Pitt Meadows Amenity Cost Charge Bylaw 2987, 2024 rate for a single-family dwelling as $8,480 per dwelling unit for April 1, 2026 to March 31, 2027, and $3,560 for a secondary suite or garden suite. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-04/v17-2010_DCC_Rate_Summary.pdf
- Subdividing land over 3 lots triggers a parkland rule in that summary. It states Parkland Dedication under Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2589 is 5% of the land being proposed for subdivision, only where the land subdivided is greater than 3 lots. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-04/v17-2010_DCC_Rate_Summary.pdf
- The City publishes its current rates in the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026, listed on its bylaw finder. A total residential rate per $1,000 was not reproduced in the City pages read for this note, so no total rate is asserted here — see NOT CONFIRMED. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-hall/bylaw-services/find-bylaw?page=0
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies in Pitt Meadows. The Province lists the City of Pitt Meadows as a taxable area in the Metro Vancouver Regional District. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The West Coast Express (WCE) Pitt Meadows Station is the rail link for farm-area commuters. TransLink lists its park and ride at 12280 Harris Road with 150 spaces, open 7 days a week from 4 a.m. to 2 a.m. https://www.translink.ca/parkandride?utm_source=direct&utm_medium=shorturl&utm_campaign=parkandride
- Lougheed Highway is the east-west route along the south edge of the farm area, and the proposed 2-kilometre North Lougheed Connector would link Abernethy Way to Lougheed Highway. The City states staff are working with the Ministry of Transportation and Transit and TransLink on how it connects to Golden Ears Way and Lougheed Highway. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/community-projects-and-initiatives/projects-and-studies/north-lougheed-area-plan
- Pitt Meadows Airport (YPK) is a local aviation facility inside the city. The City publishes an airport page for it, which is relevant to noise checks for nearby parcels. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Dike and drainage failure is the defining risk for a Pitt Meadows acreage. With 86% of the city in the floodplain and four drainage areas served by six pump stations, a buyer should confirm which drainage area a parcel is in and what its flood construction requirements are. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/new-9-million-pitt-polder-pump-station-complete
- Ditch setbacks reduce buildable area on farm parcels. The A-1 rules require residential buildings to be sited 15 metres from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches, in the consolidated zoning bylaw excerpt read for this note. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- Soil deposit or removal needs a permit path on ALR land. The City states it works with the ALC on Soil and Fill Permit Applications, so imported fill for a house site or barn should be priced with that approval in mind. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/4740
- Check the City's flood preparation information before insuring a farm property. The City directs residents to its flood preparation page from its pump-station release. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/new-9-million-pitt-polder-pump-station-complete
