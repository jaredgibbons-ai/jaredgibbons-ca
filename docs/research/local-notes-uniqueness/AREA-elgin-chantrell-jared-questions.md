# Elgin Chantrell — Questions for Jared

1. In Elgin Chantrell deals, what do you see differ between the large-lot houses along Crescent Road and the subdivision houses in the Chantrell Creek pockets off 137 Street and 24 Avenue — land value versus house value, and how subjects change?
2. Who buys in Elgin Chantrell: estate and acreage buyers who want a rural feel inside the city, families targeting Chantrell Creek Elementary and Elgin Park Secondary, or buyers who need room for horses, shops, or toys — and what do they say tipped it?
3. What sells fast here and what sits: do Nicomekl River-side lots and Crescent Road addresses move first, while deep forest lots in the interior wait — and do older houses on one-acre lots sell to end users or to builders?
4. What surprises Elgin Chantrell buyers: the Agricultural Land Reserve and floodplain along the Nicomekl River, septic and well questions on the big lots, or how dark and quiet Crescent Road and the interior streets are at night?
5. Tell one Elgin Chantrell story: a deal on Crescent Road or 137 Street where the lot size, the river, or a heritage or tree issue changed the price, the subjects, or the outcome.
