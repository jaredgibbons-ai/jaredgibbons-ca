## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Slope and creek rules are the local issue: Burnaby's Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area rules in Zoning Bylaw section 6.23 require setbacks near streams, ravines, wetlands and lakeshores. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/construction-and-renovation/green-building-and-land-development/environmental-review-committee
- Development within 30 metres of a stream can require a streamside leave area of 15 to 30 metres for a Class A fish-bearing watercourse, determined site by site by the Planning Department. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2021-08/Streams-and-Ravines-Informational-Guide.pdf
- A setback relaxation goes through the City's Environmental Review Committee, which reviews development applications in environmentally sensitive areas. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/construction-and-renovation/green-building-and-land-development/environmental-review-committee

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves Simon Fraser Hills? **A:** Burnaby Mountain Secondary is the secondary school for this district zone. The Burnaby Board of Education lists Burnaby Mountain Secondary in the Cariboo Lougheed zone together with Stoney Creek Community, Cameron, Lyndhurst and Seaforth elementary schools, and the Province lists the school at 8800 Eastlake Drive; confirm an individual address with the district locator before relying on it. https://burnabyschools.ca/board/trustees/
- **Q:** Is there a SkyTrain station in Simon Fraser Hills itself? **A:** No station inside the hillside area is stated on the TransLink page read. TransLink lists Lougheed Town Centre and Production Way–University as the Millennium and Expo Line stations serving the wider Lougheed area, with Lougheed Town Centre as an Expo–Millennium transfer station; the bus or walk connection from a specific complex is UNCONFIRMED here. http://retrosearch.org/browse?url=https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/skytrain
- **Q:** Why do creek setbacks matter for a townhome on a slope? **A:** Because Burnaby applies streamside setbacks on sloped creek land, not only beside flat watercourses. The City's guide covers land within 30 metres of a stream or ravine and states the Planning Department determines the leave area for the specific site, so a buyer planning an addition, deck or redevelopment should check the exact requirement for that lot first. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2021-08/Streams-and-Ravines-Informational-Guide.pdf
- **Q:** What civic facility change affects this area most? **A:** The Cameron Community Centre and Library replacement in Lougheed Town Centre is the main one. The City states the new facility will be four times larger than the original 1988 facility, with completion estimated in 2028, and a temporary centre is operating inside The City of Lougheed Shopping Centre in the meantime. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2026-03-09/burnabys-once-generation-civic-building-program-reaches-50-completion

- The temporary Cameron Community Centre is at 110-9855 Austin Avenue inside The City of Lougheed Shopping Centre during construction of the replacement facility. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/cameron-recreation-complex
