# Ladner Farms — Extra Local Notes
Page: ladner-farms (Ladner, Delta; farms).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Ladner's farm land wraps the village on the City of Delta's Fraser River floodplain, including Westham Island and the Ladner Trunk Road corridor east of the village. https://admin.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-08/Agricultural%20Plan.pdf
- Ladner is the main hub of Delta's agriculture industry, with Westham Island offering some of Delta's most popular farms and roadside farmstands, per the City's 2024 Annual Report. https://www.delta.ca/media/4044
- Delta-wide farm facts are in full in this note, as the earliest Delta farm page in program order; ladner-acreages will point here.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Delta has 8,465 hectares (ha) of active farmland, per the City's 2024 Annual Report. https://www.delta.ca/media/4044
- The farm zone is A1 Agriculture in Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 2750, 1977: the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision in A1 is 8 ha, subject to approval under the Agricultural Land Commission Act. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219068/
- Farm parcels here are large by Lower Mainland standards: the 2150 56 Street subdivision file describes a 23 ha lot proposed to become two lots of 11.5 ha each. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219068/
- Migrant farm worker housing is a permitted A1 use with conditions, not a rezoning: at 9230 Ladner Trunk Road, a 16 ha ALR site zoned A1, the City required a Section 219 covenant setting the housing conditions. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/110332/
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Subdividing ALR land needs Agricultural Land Commission (ALC = Agricultural Land Commission) approval, except in prescribed circumstances where the municipal Approving Officer can grant it, per Delta's Council report on 2150 56 Street. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219068/
- Meeting the 8 ha A1 minimum does not guarantee a split: Delta's report on 2150 56 Street warns the two-lot proposal would still result in fragmentation of agricultural land, and Council forwarded it to the ALC with no comment. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219068/
- A principal residence in the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR = Agricultural Land Reserve) is capped at 500 square metres (m²) and an additional residence at 90 m² under the ALR Use Regulation, per the ALC's referral letter. https://webfiles.islandstrust.bc.ca/islands/local-trust-areas/south-pender/current-projects/Minor%20LUB%20Amendments%20Project/8%20-%20Public%20Hearing%20Binder/05.2_SP-BL-129_Bylaw%20referral_Responses.pdf
- Flood proofing follows the lot into farm building: Delta subdivision files require compliance with the Delta Subdivision and Development Standards Bylaw, including a flood proofing covenant. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219068/
Read: 2026-11-03

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Can I split a Ladner farm into two lots if both stay over 8 ha?
**A:** Not on zoning alone. At 2150 56 Street, two proposed 11.5 ha lots met the A1 8 ha minimum, but Delta still treated the split as fragmentation of agricultural land and ALC approval was required; Council forwarded the application to the ALC with no comment. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219068/

**Q:** Can I house seasonal farm workers on the farm?
**A:** Yes, as a permitted A1 use if the conditions are met and a covenant is registered. At 9230 Ladner Trunk Road, housing for eight migrant farm workers on a 16 ha ALR farm required a Section 219 covenant covering floor area, setbacks, farm size and amenity space; no rezoning was needed. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/110332/

**Q:** Is Ladner farmland protected from flooding, or exposed to it?
**A:** It is protected by infrastructure that has to be maintained. Delta's Agricultural Plan 2023 states almost all Delta farmland is protected by dikes, field drainage is pumped through the ditch system into the ocean or Fraser River, and ponding is common because of the high water table and winter rainfall. https://admin.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-08/Agricultural%20Plan.pdf

**Q:** What does Delta's farm plan say buyers should expect next?
**A:** Continued pressure points, not a quiet status quo. The Delta Agricultural Plan 2023 records non-farm use of agricultural land, conflicts with wildlife and waterfowl, climate change threats and urban development pressure as live issues, with diking and drainage maintenance a named direction. https://admin.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-08/Agricultural%20Plan.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Delta maintains 67 kilometres (km) of dikes and seawall to protect the city from flooding, per the 2024 Annual Report — the farm purchase sits behind that system. https://www.delta.ca/media/4044
- Fields sit at or below sea level at high tide: the Agricultural Plan states Delta farmland is in the Fraser River floodplain and lowlands at or below sea level during high tides. https://admin.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-08/Agricultural%20Plan.pdf
- Westham Island dike work is a named gap: the Plan states more flood infrastructure work is needed particularly on Westham Island, following the City's 2019 Flood Management Study. https://admin.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-08/Agricultural%20Plan.pdf
- The Westham Island bridge constrains farm equipment: the Plan records issues with the bridge for larger agricultural equipment and weight load requirements. https://admin.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-08/Agricultural%20Plan.pdf
- Waterfowl conflict is a Delta-specific farm cost: the Plan lists conflicts with wildlife and waterfowl among the sector's ongoing issues. https://admin.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-08/Agricultural%20Plan.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the dike and drainage file, not the house: the Agricultural Plan states field water is moved through the ditch system and pumped out, and ponding from the high water table is a common issue on Delta farms. https://admin.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-08/Agricultural%20Plan.pdf
- Check title for farm-housing covenants: the 9230 Ladner Trunk Road file shows a Section 219 covenant can bind present and future owners to the A1 migrant housing conditions. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/110332/
- Check ALR status and the subdivision history together: Delta's 2150 56 Street report separates OCP designation, A1 zoning and ALC approval into three distinct checks. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219068/
