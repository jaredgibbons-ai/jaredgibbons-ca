# Lincoln Park — Extra Local Notes
Page: lincoln-park-port-coquitlam (Lincoln Park; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Lincoln Park is an established neighbourhood on Port Coquitlam's north side, and the City's Official Community Plan (OCP) counts Lincoln Park among the city's established neighbourhoods dating from the 1950s. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Coast Meridian Road is the pocket's western spine and Lincoln Avenue its namesake street: Hyde Creek Nature Reserve is at 1457 Lincoln Drive, and the Hyde Creek Recreation Centre and Minnekhada Middle School stand side by side on Laurier Avenue at 1379 and 1390 Laurier Avenue. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/recreation/parks-sports-fields-trails/parks-directory/
- Hyde Creek runs through the pocket, which is why so much of Lincoln Park's public land is creek corridor rather than built lots. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects?construction_year=All&page=1

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is an established post-war detached-house pocket: the City's OCP places Lincoln Park's origins in the 1950s, which makes it one of the older house areas on Port Coquitlam's north side rather than a new subdivision. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Shared housing-stock facts for Lincoln Park houses are covered in full in the lincoln-park-port-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; this note takes the street-level angle only (see the lincoln-park-port-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note).
- The pocket's non-house stock clusters on its edges: the recreation centre, the middle school and the creek reserve occupy the Laurier Avenue and Lincoln Drive frontage, so the interior streets are houses while the services sit on the creek corridor. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/city-facilities/hyde-creek-recreation-centre

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- City-wide Port Coquitlam zoning, tax and utility facts are covered in full in the mary-hill-port-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note (see the mary-hill-port-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note); the rules below are the Lincoln Park-specific ones.
- Hyde Creek brings a development permit check with it: the City's OCP carries watercourse and hazard development permit areas, and lots touching the Hyde Creek corridor are the ones in this pocket where a permit area can apply before land is altered. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Small-scale multi-unit housing is a city-wide permission with a creek exception to test here: the City permits three to six small-scale units on most RS (Residential Small-Scale) lots without rezoning, with the number set by lot size, location and transit proximity — but a creek-side Lincoln Park lot still has to clear its watercourse conditions first. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Lincoln Park house? **A:** For the sample address 1379 Laurier Avenue — the Hyde Creek Recreation Centre address — the School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) locator returned Leigh Elementary for kindergarten to Grade 5, Minnekhada Middle School, 1390 Laurier Avenue, for Grades 6 to 8, and Terry Fox Secondary, 1260 Riverwood Gate, for Grades 9 to 12. Catchments are address-based, so run the specific house address in the locator rather than relying on the neighbourhood name. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** What is at the Hyde Creek Recreation Centre? **A:** The centre at 1379 Laurier Avenue is the pocket's recreation anchor, standing next to Minnekhada Middle School on the same street. It is a City facility, listed by the City among its recreation centres serving the north side of Port Coquitlam. Buyers should check the City's facility page for the current program and pool schedule, because the capital program also carries drainage and culvert work on Hyde Creek at Coast Meridian Road and Lincoln Avenue that can affect the area around the centre. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/city-facilities/hyde-creek-recreation-centre
- **Q:** Does Hyde Creek flood or restrict building near my lot? **A:** Hyde Creek is an active drainage corridor, not just a park feature. The City's capital program includes Hyde Creek drainage and culvert upgrades, including bridges over Coast Meridian Road and Lincoln Avenue and storm, sanitary and water main replacement, in design with a 2027 construction year. The OCP's watercourse development permit area can also apply to creek-adjacent lots, so a buyer near the creek should check both the permit area and the lot's drainage before planning additions. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects?construction_year=All&page=1
- **Q:** How do I get downtown or to SkyTrain from Lincoln Park? **A:** Route 188 is the pocket's bus: it runs along Coast Meridian Road with stops at Lincoln Avenue and Laurier Avenue, connecting Port Coquitlam Station in one direction and Coquitlam Central Station — with the Millennium Line and the West Coast Express — in the other, via David Avenue and Pinetree Way. Port Coquitlam Station also gives West Coast Express commuter rail. There is no SkyTrain station in Port Coquitlam itself. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt188.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Two City institutions share one street: the Hyde Creek Recreation Centre at 1379 Laurier Avenue and Minnekhada Middle School at 1390 Laurier Avenue face each other across the pocket's main service corridor. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-coquitlam
- Hyde Creek Nature Reserve, at 1457 Lincoln Drive, is listed in the City's parks directory — the creek corridor is protected parkland, not leftover vacant land awaiting development. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/recreation/parks-sports-fields-trails/parks-directory/
- Leigh Elementary, the elementary school returned for the Laurier Avenue sample address, is itself across the city boundary at 1230 Soball Street, Coquitlam — a Lincoln Park child's elementary school can sit in the neighbouring city while remaining in the same school district. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-coquitlam
- Terry Fox Secondary, the secondary school for the pocket, is not in the pocket: it stands at 1260 Riverwood Gate, in the Riverwood area to the southeast. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-coquitlam

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 188 is the pocket's spine bus: from Port Coquitlam Station via Coast Meridian Road — stopping at Lincoln Avenue, Patricia Avenue and Laurier Avenue — then David Avenue and Pinetree Way, past Lafarge Lake-Douglas and Lincoln SkyTrain stations, to Coquitlam Central Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt188.pdf
- Route 173 reaches the pocket's southwestern streets: it runs via Westwood Street and Kingsway Avenue to Port Coquitlam Station, then continues via Prairie Avenue and Cedar Drive to Rocklin, interlining with route 174. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt173.pdf
- Coast Meridian Road is the driving spine: it connects the pocket south to Lougheed Highway and Port Coquitlam Station and north to David Avenue and Coquitlam. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt188.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Creek proximity is the first check: lots near Hyde Creek can fall in the OCP watercourse development permit area, and the City is designing drainage and culvert upgrades on this creek precisely because it carries stormwater through the pocket. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Drainage work is scheduled, not hypothetical: the Hyde Creek project's bridge, storm main and watermain scope at Coast Meridian Road and Lincoln Avenue carries a 2027 construction year, so buyers on those frontages should expect construction and check the capital projects page for status changes. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects?construction_year=All&page=1
- Older-house checks apply here: because the OCP dates this neighbourhood to the 1950s, a buyer should expect era-typical servicing and materials questions on interior lots and confirm permit history with the City rather than assuming upgrades were permitted. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
