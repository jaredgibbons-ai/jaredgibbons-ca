## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is The Wedgewood confirmed as 55+? **A:** Not on an official page opened in this pass, so treat it as unconfirmed until the filed bylaws are seen. The 55-plus index lists 2626 Countess St as a 55+ gated townhouse complex, but an index entry is not a bylaw. Ask the seller for the current bylaws filed at the Land Title Office and the Form B, and check whether the age bylaw, pet rule and rental wording in those documents match the listing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/amending-bylaws-and-rules
- **Q:** Can my 52-year-old partner live with me in a 55+ townhome? **A:** Yes, if you are the specified resident, the Province requires an exemption for your younger spouse or partner. A specified resident meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption, and the family exemptions added on May 1, 2023 cover younger spouses or partners and defined children cases. The complex can also add its own further exemptions in its bylaws, so read both the provincial rule and the filed bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** Can I rent out a 55+ townhome I buy in Abbotsford? **A:** A strata cannot ban long-term renting through a rental-restriction bylaw, because the Province states no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw after November 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, because the age bylaw governs who may reside. A strata may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw, which is a different rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q:** How close is hospital care for a 55+ buyer? **A:** Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre at 32900 Marshall Road provides emergency, surgery and maternity care and is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, as Fraser Health states. That does not measure distance from any one complex, so a buyer should map the specific townhome address to Marshall Road themselves. Fraser Health also lists seniors parking permits at that hospital, at $18.75 for one week and $51.75 for one month. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre
- **Q:** What new care capacity is being added in Abbotsford? **A:** Fraser Health states a new five-storey long-term care facility will be built on vacant land beside Maplewood House and the Abbotsford Hospital and Cancer Centre at 1919 Jackson St. It will replace 109 beds at Cottage and Worthington Pavilion and add 91 net new beds. The page read for this note gave a procurement-phase status with construction expected to begin in 2025 and complete in 2027, so check the current status before relying on the dates. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2023/Jun/new-long-term-care-home-coming-to-abbotsford

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- The biggest due diligence risk in a 55+ townhome is trusting a listing label instead of the filed bylaws, because new and changed bylaws do not take effect until filed at the Land Title Office, with the pet-bylaw exception the Province notes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/amending-bylaws-and-rules
- The Land Title Office does not review filed bylaw documents, and filing alone does not mean a bylaw is enforceable, as the Province warns on its amending-bylaws page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/amending-bylaws-and-rules
- A bylaw is not enforceable to the extent it contravenes the Strata Property Act, the Strata Property Regulation, the Human Rights Code or any other law, as the Province states. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/amending-bylaws-and-rules
