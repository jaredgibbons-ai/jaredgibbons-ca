# Cariboo — Questions for Jared
Area: Cariboo (Burnaby). First program page: cariboo-condos-for-sale.

## Questions (voice memo)
1. Cariboo is one of Burnaby's quieter condo pockets — who actually buys there, and what are they choosing it over?
2. In your deals, how much do the Lougheed corridor and the town centre plans matter to Cariboo buyers versus simple price?
3. What comes up on Cariboo-area strata buildings that buyers do not expect — age of the buildings, rental rules, parking?
4. How do Cariboo condos sell compared with similar units in Brentwood or Metrotown — faster, slower, different buyer entirely?
5. Tell me about one Cariboo-area deal or listing that stuck with you.
