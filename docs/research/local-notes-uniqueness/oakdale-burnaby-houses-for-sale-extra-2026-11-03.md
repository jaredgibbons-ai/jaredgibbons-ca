## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary schools are closest? **A:** Cameron Elementary and Lyndhurst Elementary are the closest elementary schools named here. The Province lists Cameron Elementary (K–7) at 9540 Erickson Dr and Lyndhurst Elementary (K–7) at 9847 Lyndhurst St. Catchments are address-based, so run the exact address through the locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** Is Oakdale in Burnaby or Coquitlam for schools? **A:** This Oakdale is in Burnaby for schools. Its houses fall under School District 41, with elementary schools (K–7) and secondary schools (8–12) and no middle-school tier. That differs from Coquitlam across North Road, so do not apply its school list here. https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/
- **Q:** Which secondary school is named here? **A:** Burnaby Mountain Secondary is the secondary school named for this area. The Province lists it at 8800 Eastlake Dr (8–12), on the same corridor as the area's elementary schools. Catchment is address-based, so confirm the specific street in the locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** How does a house here reach SFU? **A:** A house here reaches Simon Fraser University (SFU) by bus from Burquitlam Station. TransLink's 143 runs via Clarke Road, Como Lake Avenue, Broadway and Gaglardi Way to the SFU Exchange. A proposed gondola from Production Way is not yet approved or funded. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt143.pdf
