# Westridge Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: westridge-burnaby (Westridge; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Westridge is split almost in half by Inlet Drive: Heritage Burnaby's neighbourhood record describes a typical 1950s single-family subdivision west of Inlet Drive and a newer 1970s subdivision with larger lots and some low-rise units to the east, climbing Burnaby Mountain. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark791
- The west half drops to the Burrard Inlet shoreline and the railway line; the east half rises toward Simon Fraser University, linked by Burnaby Mountain Parkway. The City's Trans Canada Trail page runs its Burnaby section from Barnet Highway in the east across this north slope. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/walking-hiking-and-biking/trans-canada-trail
- Westridge Elementary, at 510 Duncan Avenue, is the in-area SD41 school and a 2026 civic voting place — the neighbourhood's fixed point on Cliff Avenue's slope streets. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-09/Notice-of-Election-by-Voting-2026-09-24.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Two building eras define the stock: 1950s single-family homes west of Inlet Drive, and 1970s homes on larger lots, plus some low-rise units, east of it. Heritage Burnaby's record adds that a 1980 City study characterised Westridge as a stable community with little need for direct municipal involvement in growth. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark791
- Lou Moro Park — formerly Westridge Park, west of Inlet Drive near Hastings Street — carries 2 tennis courts and 8 pickleball courts, the largest court count of any park in this cluster of notes. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/outdoor-courts
- Barnet Marine Park, on the Burrard Inlet shore below the west half, is the area's waterfront: a foreshore park with a sandy beach, canoe and kayak launch and shore fishing, on a former logging mill camp site. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/barnet-marine-park

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Slope is the governing rule on the east half: the City's Burnaby Mountain trail page records grades climbing from about 25 metres above sea level to the 366-metre summit, and development on steep lots is where geotechnical review is most likely to be demanded — confirm the lot's status with the Planning Department. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/walking-hiking-and-biking/burnaby-mountain-conservation-area-trails
- Detached lots here are R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing lots — see the suncrest-burnaby note for the full unit-count rules; the transit-area parking relief described in the willingdon-heights-burnaby note does not automatically apply on these interior streets.
- The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, in effect July 1, 2026, reassigned park and natural-area lands — including forests and riparian areas — to the new Parks, Open Space and Natural Areas (P1) District, which matters for lots backing onto the conservation area. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- Barnet Marine Park's parking rules are a bylaw in daily life here: from May 1 to September 30, on weekends and holidays, time limits apply because the park draws over 1,900 visitors and about 1,000 vehicles a day. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/barnet-marine-park

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Does a bus still run through Westridge itself? **A:** No through-neighbourhood bus loop operates today; the practical service is the R5 Hastings Street RapidBus on the area's south edge. TransLink's R5 timetable runs from SFU Exchange via Burnaby Mountain Parkway and Hastings Street to Burrard Station, with stops at Duthie Avenue and Kensington Avenue below the Westridge slope. Budget a walk down Cliff Avenue or Inlet Drive to Hastings for the bus. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/ttR5.pdf
- **Q:** What happened at Barnet Highway in 2007 that buyers still hear about? **A:** A pipeline rupture during City sewer work in July 2007 spilled oil through this neighbourhood to Burrard Inlet, damaging homes, the forested slope and marine wildlife, and closing the Barnet Highway for months of cleanup. That history is why a buyer on the Inlet Drive slope should ask for environmental disclosure and check title and permit history rather than relying on the quiet current streetscape. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?objectType_facet=Landmark%20Description%20&objectType_facet=Architectural%20Drawing&ps=50&q=&sort=date_sort%20asc&onlineMediaType_facet=Image&repository_facet=Burnaby%20Heritage%20Planning&repository_facet=Burnaby%20Village%20Museum&decade_facet=1960&p=1
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves a Duncan Avenue home? **A:** Westridge Elementary is the in-neighbourhood school, at 510 Duncan Avenue, listed by the City among its 2026 voting locations drawn from SD41 facilities. Catchments in Burnaby are set by civic address in SD41's School Locator, and the slope streets east of Inlet Drive should be checked individually, because proximity to the building alone does not settle which side of a catchment line a lot sits on. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-09/Notice-of-Election-by-Voting-2026-09-24.pdf
- **Q:** Is the beach at Barnet Marine Park swimmable and boat-accessible? **A:** The City describes a sandy shore for strolling and launching paddleboards, canoes and kayaks, with launch facilities for canoes, kayaks and sailing craft and shore fishing with a licence. It does not publish a swimming-water quality result on the park page, so check Metro Vancouver and health-authority postings in season rather than assuming the beach is a designated swimming beach. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/barnet-marine-park

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Barnet Marine Park is now co-managed: on June 18, 2025, səlilwətaɬ (Tsleil-Waututh Nation) and the City of Burnaby signed a co-management agreement for the park, which the Nation calls saltaɬ, in its traditional territory. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-06-19/slilwtal-tsleil-waututh-nation-and-city-burnaby-sign-agreement-co-manage
- The park's main parking lot sits on the south side of the rail line and the beach side is pedestrian only — the City specifically warns visitors not to park on the rail line. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/barnet-marine-park
- The Velodrome Trail from the Harry Jerome Sports Centre side climbs 240 vertical metres at an average 20% grade, starting with a 500-tread timber stairway — and that trail section is currently closed indefinitely for erosion and aging steps. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/walking-hiking-and-biking/burnaby-mountain-conservation-area-trails
- Westridge Park was renamed Lou Moro Park; City court listings use the new name, which can confuse a buyer searching the old one. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/outdoor-courts
- The Trans Canada Trail segment from Burnaby Mountain to Barnet Marine Park drops about 2 kilometres up and 4 kilometres back down to sea level — the City itself flags this portion as not one of its accessible routes. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/walking-hiking-and-biking/trans-canada-trail

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE

- Garbage disposal for 2026 is priced by toter size: $73 for the 120-litre small toter, $159 for the default 180-litre, $169 for 240 litres and $447 for the 360-litre large toter, with a one-time $52 exchange fee per address. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The R5 Hastings Street RapidBus is the spine: TransLink lists R5 among its six RapidBus routes, running at least every 10 minutes at peak and every 15 minutes off-peak, daily 6 a.m. to midnight, from Burrard Station to SFU. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
- The R5 timetable shows the Westridge-side stops at Hastings Street at Duthie Avenue and at Kensington Avenue, then Burnaby Mountain Parkway to SFU Exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/ttR5.pdf
- Burnaby Mountain Parkway links the east half of Westridge directly to SFU's campus roads — it is the route the R5 itself uses above Hastings Street. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/ttR5.pdf
- Travel time from Cliff Avenue to Burrard Station on the R5: UNCONFIRMED — TransLink's timetable gives stop times, not a door-to-door figure for this area.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Slope and erosion are the named checks: the City has closed the Velodrome Trail indefinitely for dirt erosion and aging wood steps on this same mountainside, which tells a buyer the slope moves — but the trail page does not rate any individual lot, so a geotechnical report is still a private purchase. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/walking-hiking-and-biking/burnaby-mountain-conservation-area-trails
- Pipeline history is the second check: the 2007 Inlet Drive rupture reached homes and the Burrard Inlet shore, so ask for the property's environmental and permit history; no City page gives a lot-by-lot spill map.
- The West Coast Express railway runs along the shore below the west half but has no station in Westridge — the City's park page treats the rail line as a boundary to cross on foot, not a service to board here. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/barnet-marine-park
