# Uptown Houses — Extra Local Notes

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Uptown is the Sixth Street and Sixth Avenue plateau neighbourhood in New Westminster, designated a Frequent Transit Development Area in the City's Official Community Plan (OCP), "Our City 2041," Bylaw No. 7925, 2017. https://www.newwestcity.ca/planning-building-and-development/zoning
- City Hall, at 511 Royal Avenue, sits at the area's south edge, and the New Westminster Public Library's main branch is at 716 Sixth Avenue. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- Moody Park lies at the neighbourhood's north-west edge, and the Queen's Park Heritage Conservation Area, the city's one conservation area, lies just east of Uptown. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is detached houses on the plateau streets under the RS districts of Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001: the RS-1 district requires a minimum site area of 557.40 square metres (6,000 square feet) with a floor space ratio of 0.5. https://www.newwestcity.ca/planning-building-and-development/zoning
- RS districts allow secondary suites, and laneway and carriage houses are regulated under the same bylaw, so the house stock carries suite and laneway potential by zone rather than by exception. https://www.newwestcity.ca/planning-building-and-development/zoning
- New Westminster Secondary School, at 820 Sixth Street, is the city's single secondary school and the plateau's largest institution. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The City's Infill Housing Program, adopted June 2026, permits up to six infill units on most single detached and duplex properties city-wide, including Uptown; the full entry is in the west-end-new-westminster note. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- Uptown's Frequent Transit Development Area designation in Our City 2041 is the plan frame for added density around the Sixth Street corridor. https://www.newwestcity.ca/planning-building-and-development/zoning
- The Queen's Park Heritage Conservation Area is the city's one conservation area and sits just east of Uptown, so heritage conservation rules apply across the east edge rather than inside Uptown itself. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage
- New Westminster is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve Uptown? **A:** School District No. 40 (SD40) runs a single secondary school, New Westminster Secondary at 820 Sixth Street, whose replacement school opened January 11, 2021 at a cost of 106.5 million dollars with 1,900 seats; recorded locator chains include Lord Kelvin Elementary, Fraser River Middle and New Westminster Secondary for 835 Eighth Street, and École Lord Tweedsmuir Elementary, Fraser River Middle and New Westminster Secondary for 1714 Eighth Avenue. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- **Q:** Is there a SkyTrain station in Uptown? **A:** No: the nearest stations are New Westminster and Columbia, on the Expo Line below the plateau, and Uptown is served by buses including the 105 (Uptown and New Westminster Station), the 106 (Edmonds and New Westminster Station), the 103 and the 123 on the Sixth Street and Sixth Avenue corridors. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/skytrain
