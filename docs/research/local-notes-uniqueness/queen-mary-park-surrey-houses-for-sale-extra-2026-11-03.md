## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Queen Mary Park the park sits at 8972 Queen Mary Boulevard, north of 88 Avenue and west of 132 Street, and the City lists it under Whalley and City Centre. https://www.surrey.ca/activities-parks-recreation/parks/queen-mary-park
- The house area takes its name from that park and Queen Mary Boulevard: its east side reaches King George Boulevard and Bear Creek Park at 13750 88 Avenue, and its west side reaches Scott Road (120 Street). https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bear-creek-park
- Bear Creek Park, at the area's northeast at 13750 88 Avenue, is a separate, larger City park — not part of Queen Mary Park itself. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bear-creek-park

- Detached houses here surround a park that is mostly natural area, not playing fields: the City states the majority of Queen Mary Park is natural areas established to protect Healy Creek, with trails and a playground reached from the trails. https://www.surrey.ca/activities-parks-recreation/parks/queen-mary-park

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves Queen Mary Park houses? **A:** Kennedy Trail Elementary is the area's named elementary, at 8305 122A Street, and the district page confirms it as a School District 36 school with phone 604-590-1198. Catchments in Surrey are address-based, so confirm the exact house address in the district school locator before writing an offer that depends on Kennedy Trail. https://www.surreyschools.ca/kennedytrail
- **Q:** What is inside Queen Mary Park itself? **A:** Mostly protected natural area. The City states the majority of the park is natural areas established to protect Healy Creek, a tributary to Bear Creek and the Serpentine River. Natural trails loop through the northwest portion, entered from Queen Mary Boulevard at 130B Street and from the end of Lanark Place, with a playground in the middle reached from the trails. https://www.surrey.ca/activities-parks-recreation/parks/queen-mary-park
- **Q:** How close is Bear Creek Park? **A:** Bear Creek Park is at 13750 88 Avenue, at the northeast of this house area across King George Boulevard from the Queen Mary Boulevard streets. It is a separate City park from Queen Mary Park at 8972 Queen Mary Boulevard, so a listing that names both parks is naming two different parks on opposite sides of the area. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bear-creek-park
- **Q:** Does this area have RapidBus on both sides? **A:** Yes, on its two edges. The R6 Scott Road RapidBus runs on Scott Road (120 Street) on the west side, from Scott Road Station to Newton Exchange, and the R1 King George Boulevard RapidBus runs on King George Boulevard on the east side, from Newton Exchange to Guildford Exchange. Neither route runs through the interior Queen Mary Boulevard streets. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Queen Mary Park is open dawn until dusk, at 8972 Queen Mary Boulevard, with park contact 604-501-5050 — the park has no sports-field lighting or evening facility use stated on its City page. https://www.surrey.ca/activities-parks-recreation/parks/queen-mary-park
- Trail access is from two specific points only on the City page: the intersection of Queen Mary Boulevard and 130B Street, and the end of Lanark Place east of Queen Mary Boulevard. https://www.surrey.ca/activities-parks-recreation/parks/queen-mary-park

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The R6 Scott Road RapidBus connects Scott Road Station to Newton Exchange along Scott Road and 72 Avenue, serving the area's west edge at 120 Street. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus
- The R1 King George Boulevard RapidBus connects Newton Exchange to Guildford Exchange along King George Boulevard, serving the area's east edge, and RapidBus routes run at least every 10 minutes at peak and every 15 minutes off-peak, 6 a.m. to midnight. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
