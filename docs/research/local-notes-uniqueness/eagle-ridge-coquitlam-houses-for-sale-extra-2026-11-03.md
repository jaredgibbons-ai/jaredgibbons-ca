# Eagle Ridge Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: eagle-ridge-coquitlam-houses-for-sale (Eagle Ridge; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Eagle Ridge is the established residential area in north-central Coquitlam along Guildford Way and Lansdowne Drive, below Westwood Plateau and beside the Port Moody border; no City neighbourhood plan boundary for Eagle Ridge on its own was confirmed, so the working anchors are Guildford Way and Lansdowne Drive. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- Eagle Ridge Hospital, at 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody, stands at the area's west side, on the Port Moody and Coquitlam border — the facility the area shares its name with. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- Westwood Plateau lies above to the north, Upper Eagle Ridge is the separate board sub-area upslope, and Central Coquitlam and City Centre lie to the south and east. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquitlam

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is established detached houses on the Guildford Way and Lansdowne Drive corridors, in a bench neighbourhood that predates the Westwood Plateau master plan above it; the two Lansdowne Drive strata complexes at the Guildford Way end are covered in the eagle-ridge-coquitlam-condos-townhomes-for-sale note. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Eagle-Ridge-Park-36
- The small-scale residential zones here are R-1, R-2 and R-3 under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996, and the minimum lot size for subdivision is 650 square metres with 18.5 metres width for R-1 and R-3, and 465 square metres with 12.5 metres width for R-2. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Coquitlam's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules allow up to 3 units on lots of 280 square metres or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 square metres and under 4,050 square metres, including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- The City states it currently has no bus stops meeting the provincial frequent-service criteria, so the 6-unit tier near frequent transit does not apply in Coquitlam. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquitlam
- The Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- Coquitlam is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve Eagle Ridge? **A:** For the sample address 1195 Lansdowne Drive, the School District No. 43 (SD43) locator result recorded is Eagle Ridge Elementary, Scott Creek Middle and Gleneagle Secondary, and Gleneagle Secondary itself stands at that Lansdowne Drive address; catchments for other addresses must be checked in the locator. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** How small can an Eagle Ridge lot be subdivided? **A:** The minimum lot size for subdivision is 650 square metres with 18.5 metres width in the R-1 and R-3 zones, and 465 square metres with 12.5 metres width in the R-2 zone, under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Q:** Is the hospital in Coquitlam or Port Moody? **A:** Port Moody: Eagle Ridge Hospital is at 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody, on the municipal border at this area's west side, and it is the closest hospital for Eagle Ridge. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q:** When are Coquitlam property taxes due? **A:** Property tax payment was due July 2, 2026, covering January 1 through December 31, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5 percent penalty on any unpaid balance after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area before planning work near a creek or ravine, since it covers land within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse here. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- Confirm whether the lot is connected to municipal water and sewer before assuming SSMUH units, because lots not connected are outside the SSMUH rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- Confirm the board sub-area on the specific property: Lansdowne Drive addresses are filed under Eagle Ridge, Upper Eagle Ridge and Westwood Plateau. http://searcharchives.coquitlam.ca/uploads/r/city-of-coquitlam-archives/9/2/8/928c605cc00a7d36fe6b1e528d6f6c09799dadfb8f8f89fdddbeee2c24bb9842/Subdivision_Committee_-_1980.pdf
