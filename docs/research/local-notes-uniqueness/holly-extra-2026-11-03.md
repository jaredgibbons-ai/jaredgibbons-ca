# Holly, Ladner — Extra Local Notes
Page: holly (Holly, Ladner, City of Delta; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Holly Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 7 in School District 37 (Delta), is at 4625 62 Street, Delta, V4K 3L8. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/school/03737035
- The City's Ladner page says "East Ladner" is the common name for the part of Ladner east of Highway 17A. Highway 17A runs north-south from Highway 99 to the BC Ferries terminal, and Ladner Trunk Road is the main east-west artery. https://www.delta.ca/Ladner
- Delta publishes no Holly boundary. The Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw No. 8400 page lists no planning areas or neighbourhoods, and the City's 2024 OCP fact sheet never mentions Holly, so Holly is not an official City of Delta planning area. https://www.delta.ca/city-hall/bylaws/official-community-plan-bylaw
- The City's parks map lists a Holly Park in Ladner, with no address given. https://delta.ca/sites/default/files/2021-09/parks_map.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Under Bill 44, Delta had to change its zoning before June 30, 2024. Council gave the amendments first to third readings on June 3, 2024, and cannot hold a public hearing on them. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/zoning-amendment-bylaws-introduced-permit-small-scale-multi
- Limits are three units on lots up to 280 m² and four units on lots over 280 m². Six units apply only within 400 m of frequent transit along Scott Road, which is in North Delta, so that tier does not reach Ladner. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/building-renovating/residential/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Each unit needs one on-site parking space, except units under 33 m². Compliant projects skip rezoning and Council, but covenants on title, steep slopes and nearby streams can still limit a lot. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/building-renovating/residential/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Council adopted the OCP on July 8, 2024. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- Delta applies a Development Permit Area for Streamside Protection and Enhancement in some areas. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/environment-conservation/environmentally-sensitive-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Holly an official Delta neighbourhood? **A:** No. Delta's OCP bylaw page lists no planning areas, and Delta's own file for 60B Street calls the area East Ladner. Ask the City about the exact lot's zone and designation. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009770
- **Q:** Which school is Holly's? **A:** Holly Elementary, 4625 62 Street, Kindergarten to Grade 7. Confirm the catchment for the exact address with School District 37 before an offer relies on it. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/delta
- **Q:** Can I add a suite or coach house? **A:** Usually by building permit. Delta allows a garden suite and/or coach house (maximum two) plus suites, with one single detached or duplex per lot, subject to title covenants and streams. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/building-renovating/residential/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q:** Do dikes protect this part of Ladner? **A:** Delta runs over 63 km of flood protection and 31 drainage pump stations, and the Province maps Delta's dikes under the Corporation of Delta. Ask what floor elevation applied to the house. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- 2026 property tax was due July 2, 2026, with a 2.9 percent City increase; penalties are 5 percent after July 2 and a further 5 percent after September 2. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- Flat-rate utilities for a single-family home are $1,658 in 2026, up from $1,607. Metered water is $1.47 per m³ for the first 125 m³ and $1.97 above that. Sewer is $2.13 per m³ on 80 percent of water use, with $25 quarterly minimums. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Home Owner Grant: basic maximum $570, additional $275. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- A new secondary suite requires a water meter, so the house moves to quarterly metered billing. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/building-renovating/secondary-suites
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax lists the City of Delta as a taxable area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
