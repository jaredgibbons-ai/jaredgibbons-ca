# Holly Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: holly (Holly; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The City of Langley does not list a "Holly" neighbourhood among its six official neighbourhoods. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/nicomekl-neighbourhood-profile
- The City states its six neighbourhoods are centred on its elementary schools: Alice Brown, Blacklock, Douglas Park, Nicomekl, Simonds and Uplands. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/nicomekl-neighbourhood-profile
- The pocket sold as "Holly" near 200 Street and 53 Avenue falls inside the City's Nicomekl neighbourhood as the City defines it: north of the Nicomekl River (roughly 52 Avenue to 62 Avenue) and west of 203 Street, around Nicomekl Elementary at 20050 53 Avenue. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/nicomekl-neighbourhood-profile
- The newer Nicomekl River District plan area runs from 196 Street west to 208 Street east. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/appendix-nicomekl-river-district-neighbourhood-planconsolidation-february-2026pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Housing in this pocket is a mix of detached homes, townhomes and apartment buildings, as the City describes the Nicomekl plan area edges. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/appendix-nicomekl-river-district-neighbourhood-planconsolidation-february-2026pdf
- The City's Nicomekl profile describes the neighbourhood as one of the City's oldest, with a busy commercial corridor on 200 Street and medium-density townhomes among its housing forms. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/nicomekl-neighbourhoodprofile-2019-web-ready
- Brydon Park and Brydon Lagoon are the named green spaces the City lists for this neighbourhood. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/nicomekl-neighbourhood-profile

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Nicomekl River District Neighbourhood Plan (Appendix A to the City's plan consolidation, February 2026) is the detailed plan for this pocket. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/appendix-nicomekl-river-district-neighbourhood-planconsolidation-february-2026pdf
- The Nicomekl River spans 34 km through three jurisdictions — Township of Langley, Langley City and Surrey — and flows through the centre of Langley City. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/appendix-nicomekl-river-district-neighbourhood-planconsolidation-february-2026pdf

- Floodplain policy for the Nicomekl corridor applies to low lots near the river and Brydon Creek/Baldi Creek and Murray Creek as mapped in City material. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/appendix-b-district-policiesconsolidation-february-2026pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Holly an official Langley City neighbourhood? **A:** No, not in the City's own neighbourhood system. The City profiles six neighbourhoods centred on elementary schools — Alice Brown, Blacklock, Douglas Park, Nicomekl, Simonds and Uplands — and the 200 Street/53 Avenue pocket sits inside Nicomekl, which the City delineates around Nicomekl Elementary at 20050 53 Avenue. Use "Holly" as a local sales name only. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/nicomekl-neighbourhood-profile
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves this pocket? **A:** Nicomekl Elementary is the school the City centres this neighbourhood on. The City profile gives its address as 20050 53 Avenue and places the neighbourhood north of the Nicomekl River and west of 203 Street; SD35 catchments still go by exact street address, so confirm the specific home in the SD35 School Locator before removing subjects. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/nicomekl-neighbourhood-profile
- **Q:** What is the Nicomekl floodplain risk here? **A:** Low lots near the river sit in the Nicomekl floodplain trail and park system the City manages. The City's plan describes the river surfacing from a culvert under Fraser Highway and 208 Street and running west through parkland to Surrey, with the floodplain forming the green heart of the city; buyers should check the City's floodplain mapping for the exact lot. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/appendix-nicomekl-river-district-neighbourhood-planconsolidation-february-2026pdf
- **Q:** What community program is running in Nicomekl? **A:** Nicomekl is a pilot for the City's Resilient Neighbourhood Networks. The City announced June 29, 2026 that Nicomekl, which received the highest community vote, launches first in summer 2026, with Douglas following later in 2026, to help neighbours connect and prepare together. https://www.langleycity.ca/news/langley-city-launches-resilient-neighbourhood-networks-announces-nicomekl-and-douglas-pilot

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Langley City water is metered and user-pay, unlike the Township's flat rates. https://www.langleycity.ca/PropertyTaxes/utilities
- The 2026 water rate is $2.06 per cubic metre plus a $75 fee, with residential meters read annually in March and billed on the June tax notice. https://www.langleycity.ca/PropertyTaxes/utilities
- Sewer is charged on 80% of water consumption at $2.41 per cubic metre plus a $75 fee, and the 2026 single-family solid waste charge is $432. https://www.langleycity.ca/PropertyTaxes/utilities
- The City of Langley is a designated Speculation and Vacancy Tax taxable area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- For City of Langley headline tax rates, see the city-of-langley-homes-for-sale note.
