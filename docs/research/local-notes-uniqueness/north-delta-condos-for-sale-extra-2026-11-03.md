# North Delta Condos — Extra Local Notes

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Condo stock follows two corridors: Scott Road (120 Street) from 72 Avenue to Nordel Way, and 84 Avenue through the Nordel Social Heart. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 8400, 2024, plans most growth in the Scott Road Corridor: about 14,000 people in about 6,100 new households, North Delta reaching an estimated 87,000 people by 2051. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Scott Road is the municipal boundary — its east side is Surrey, which is why the Scottsdale Exchange hub serving North Delta addresses sits in Surrey. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Delta-wide basics (Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017, taxes, utilities): see the tsawwassen-houses-for-sale note.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Delta Rise, 11967 80 Avenue, is North Delta's only high-rise condo tower: 38 storeys, built 2017, at Scott Road and 80 Avenue. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Chancellor Place, 11910 and 11920 80 Avenue, is one complex in two concrete towers, built 1989/1990 — one strata question, not two buildings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- Delta Cedars, 8635 120 Street, is the corridor's oldest stock: built 1972/1973, 96 units, outdoor pool and shared laundry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Muse North Delta, 9015 120 Street, mixes apartment and townhouse units under one strata and one address; The Residences at Sunstone Village, 8360 Delsom Way, are the Nordel-side apartments (54 condominiums at launch). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Scott & Nicholson, 6950 and 6960 Nicholson Road, is a two-building apartment development of 188 units, approved under Bylaws 7818 and 7819 in 2019. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/185685/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- New Scott Road buildings are judged in the Scott Road Corridor (SRC) Development Permit Area: form and character are regulated by development permit under OCP Bylaw No. 8400, 2024. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/6717/8051/2986/Second_PIM_notice.pdf
- The 2024 OCP has no lettered area-plan schedules: the old North Delta Area Plan (Schedule C.1 to former OCP Bylaw No. 3950) does not carry into Bylaw No. 8400; Development Permit Areas sit in Appendix B. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017 sets the parcel's zone; the Brixton application (§6) rezones to Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) from RS1 and Comprehensive Development Zone No. 414 (CD414). http://www.delta.ca/node/763
- Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are unenforceable since November 24, 2022 (Delta Cedars is the local directory example); the only enforceable age bylaw is 55-plus (55+). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** How does the R6 Scott Road RapidBus actually run?
**A:** The R6 links Scott Road Station (Expo Line) with Newton Exchange along Scott Road and 72 Avenue, stopping at Nordel Way, 84 Avenue, 80 Avenue and 75A Avenue in North Delta. TransLink runs it every 7.5 minutes at peak, 15 minutes off-peak, saving up to 10 minutes each direction. It does not enter Scottsdale Exchange. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus

**Q:** Is a unit at Muse North Delta a condo or a townhouse?
**A:** It can be either. Muse, 9015 120 Street, holds apartment and townhouse units under one strata corporation and one civic address; Delta Gardens pairs 84 Avenue apartments with 115 Street townhomes the same way. The unit's own strata lot and form on the strata plan decide — never the complex name alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

**Q:** Is Delta Cedars a 55+ building?
**A:** No source read for this note says so. Its directory record says "age restrictions" without stating any age, and the only age bylaw British Columbia law permits is 55 or older, under Strata Property Act sections 123.1 and 123.2. Read the registered bylaw in the strata documents, not the directory line. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

**Q:** What taxes hit a brand-new Scott Road condo that a resale does not?
**A:** Two. A new build carries 5 percent Goods and Services Tax (GST), which a resale does not; and Property Transfer Tax applies unless the buyer qualifies for the Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000) or the First-Time Home Buyers' Program. The federal GST/HST rebate can stack separately. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The R6 deliberately skips Scottsdale Exchange; TransLink states riders connect to local routes at shared on-street stops on Scott Road and 72 Avenue instead. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus
- A lake once sat under the Scott & Nicholson site: the City's 2019 hearing record notes Scott Lake used to flow through the Nicholson Road area — hence its stormwater conditions. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/185685/
- Arland Mews Co-operative, 11865 80 Avenue, sits in the same Scottsdale blocks as the towers but is a housing co-operative — membership and occupancy, not strata ownership. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- Sunstone's 55-plus building, 10775 Delsom Crescent, is a 70-unit rental block beside the strata Residences — its age rule is a tenancy rule, not a strata bylaw. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- The R6 was a $33 million joint TransLink, Surrey and Delta investment with a dedicated busway near 72 Avenue; TransLink calls Scott Road the busiest corridor south of the Fraser River. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta-wide, in full in the tsawwassen-houses-for-sale note: property tax is charged per $1,000 of net taxable value; 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- The 2026 provincial school tax for School District No. 37 (Delta) is $1.0997 per $1,000 of residential value, set by provincial order — the same rate lands on a North Delta condo's notice as on a Tsawwassen house's. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/oic/OIC_CUR/0124_2026
- The $1,658 flat-rate utility figure in the tsawwassen-houses-for-sale note is the single-family figure; a condo's water and sewer usually sit inside strata fees, as the Form B states. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Delta is a listed taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax; owners must declare annually. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The R6 is North Delta's spine: Scott Road Station to Newton Exchange, 13 stops, with North Delta stops at Nordel Way, 84 Avenue, 80 Avenue, 75A Avenue and 72 Avenue. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/ttR6.pdf
- Scott Road Station, at the corridor's north end, is the Expo Line SkyTrain connection; the R6 exists because no SkyTrain line runs down Scott Road itself. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/r6-scott-road-rapidbus
- The OCP's growth plan is built around this corridor and the provincially designated Transit-Oriented Area around Scottsdale Exchange, across Scott Road in Surrey. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Age splits the risk: Delta Cedars (1972/1973) and Chancellor Place (1989/1990) are inside the mandatory depreciation-report cycle on roof, piping and parkade years the 2017–2023 buildings have not reached — compare contingency reserve fund balances, not headline fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Check the Form B for approved special levies still payable and the insurance summary; its water-damage deductible is the figure a unit-owner policy is usually matched against. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- Scott Road frontage is a construction corridor: with about 6,100 new households planned along it, check a corridor-facing unit against the Scott Road Height Map in OCP Bylaw No. 8400. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
