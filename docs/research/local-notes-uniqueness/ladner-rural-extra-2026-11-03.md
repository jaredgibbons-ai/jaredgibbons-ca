# Ladner Rural Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: ladner-rural (Ladner Rural, City of Delta; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Ladner Rural is the farm and river pocket west and south-west of Ladner village in the City of Delta, including the Westham Island side, and the City publishes no boundary for the name. It is a Paragon sub-area name, not a City planning name (https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-city-delta-celebrates-reopening-westham-island).
- The pocket has two distinct parts: inland farm parcels on roads such as 34B Avenue and 64 Street, and float homes on West River Road at Canoe Pass — the sub-area's own listing feed carries both, per the program's read of the live page (https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-rural.html).
- Westham Island is reached only by the Westham Island Bridge over Canoe Pass, off River Road west of Ladner — the bridge is the island's only land access (http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-city-delta-celebrates-reopening-westham-island).
- Ladner village and its sub-areas are east, East Delta is beyond them, and Tsawwassen is south. Ladner-shared urban facts are written in full in the neilsen-grove note — see the neilsen-grove note.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The inland stock is farm houses and bare agricultural land under the "Agriculture (A)" designation in the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), and A1 Agriculture zoning where that zone applies (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf).
- The A1 zone is intended for farming, breeding pets, kennels and related uses, and its accessory uses include equestrian facilities to a maximum of 40 permanent stalls accessory to farming (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf).
- The river stock is float homes on West River Road, several naming the Canoe Pass float-home community and private boat slips in their listing text — that is feed composition from the page itself, not a tenure finding (https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-rural.html).
- Migrant farm worker housing is a permitted A1 use only under set criteria, including Agricultural Land Reserve status, farm classification under the Assessment Act, written ALC approval, and a farm of at least 8 hectares on a lot of at least 4 hectares, in manufactured homes on temporary foundations (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Agricultural Land Commission housing caps and the A1/A2/A3 subdivision rules are written in full in the east-delta note — see the east-delta note. This section covers what is specific to this pocket's parcels.
- Delta's A1 zone permits one farm house per lot at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², on a farm home plate of at most 3,600 m² (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate) (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf).
- One accessory farm dwelling unit per lot is permitted, at 90 m² or less on lots of 40 hectares or less and 186 m² or less on larger lots, on the same farm home plate as the farm house; an additional farm house needs a Professional Agrologist's report on necessity and written ALC approval (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf).
- A parcel's Agricultural Land Reserve status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address, and the ALC states its online tools are for general reference only (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps).
- For a float home, the legal structure is the rule that matters and it is not established by listing text — see NOT CONFIRMED (https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-rural.html).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is Westham Island part of Ladner Rural?
**A:** The island is the rural pocket's western side in working geography, but the boundary is not official — "Ladner Rural" is a Paragon sub-area name with no City-published boundary, and the program's read of this page's own feed found no Westham Island label on its cards. Treat island addresses as Westham Island, Delta, and confirm how a specific listing is labelled before relying on the sub-area name (https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-rural.html).

**Q:** What happened with the Westham Island Bridge in 2026?
**A:** The bridge was closed to vehicles for 67 days after a barge strike on January 20, 2026, and reopened to vehicle traffic on March 29, 2026, when Delta Council marked the reopening with a tractor parade led by a Westham Island farmer. The City states repair work continued after reopening and the navigation channel stayed closed to marine traffic until further notice (http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-city-delta-celebrates-reopening-westham-island).

**Q:** How big can a house be on a farm parcel here?
**A:** Delta's A1 zone permits one farm house per lot at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², on a farm home plate of at most 3,600 m² — or 5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate. The plate, not just the house footprint, is the cap a buyer's designer has to work within (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf).

**Q:** Can a farm here add a second dwelling for family or workers?
**A:** Yes, within limits — one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot, at 90 m² or less on lots of 40 hectares or smaller and 186 m² or less on larger lots, on the same farm home plate as the farm house. More than one accessory unit needs Agricultural Land Commission approval, and an additional farm house needs an agrologist's necessity report plus written ALC approval (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf).

**Q:** What is the tenure of the Canoe Pass float homes?
**A:** That is unconfirmed — the float homes on West River Road are described in listing text with boat slips and strata-style language, but no official, developer or strata source for the community's legal structure was read in this program. Establish what is actually owned — the float, the slip, any lease or strata interest — from title and the community's own documents before an offer (https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-rural.html).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The George C. Reifel Migratory Bird Sanctuary sits at the north end of Westham Island at 5191 Robertson Road, and its own visitor page sets out hours, admission and directions over the bridge from Ladner (https://www.reifelbirdsanctuary.com/planning.html).
- TransLink owns the Westham Island Bridge and ran the repair program: its update states Pier 5, one of the bridge's primary supports, was severely damaged in the January 20, 2026 vessel strike, and repairs included lifting a bridge section and replacing piles and beams (https://www.translink.ca/news/2026/january/westham%20island%20bridge%20repair).
- A replacement bridge is in early planning: TransLink states it is working with funding partners on a 2027 Investment Plan expected to fund construction of a new Westham Island Bridge, with engineering design work under way (https://www.translink.ca/news/2026/january/westham%20island%20bridge%20repair).
- In Delta's agricultural area the ditches and pump stations run in drainage mode in winter and irrigation mode in summer, when ditch water irrigates crops under City control gates (http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage).
- School catchments for rural addresses are address-based in School District 37 and no locator result was run for any Ladner Rural address in this program; SD37 publishes Ladner Area catchment maps (updated October 2017) beside the locator (https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/student-registration/catchment-area-maps/).
- Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, is Fraser Health's community hospital for Delta with 24/7 emergency care, beside Ladner Exchange (https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital).

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta's 2026 tax increase, due dates, flat rate utility bill and Speculation and Vacancy Tax position are city-wide and are written in full in the cliff-drive note — see the cliff-drive note (https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes).
- The rural difference is servicing: farm parcels here can sit outside City water and sewer billing entirely, on wells and onsite sewage, while serviced homes are billed on the annual flat rate bill or quarterly metered bills (http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities).

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- There is no confirmed bus route on West River Road or Westham Island itself; the pocket's transit is Ladner Exchange. TransLink route 601 runs from Ladner Exchange via Ladner Trunk Road, Highway 17A and Highway 99 to Bridgeport Station (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf).
- Route 620 runs from Ladner Exchange via Highway 17A and Highway 17 to the BC Ferries Tsawwassen terminal and to Bridgeport Station (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt620.pdf).
- By road, everything on Westham Island crosses the single-lane Westham Island Bridge at River Road — the only land access — which is why the 67-day closure in early 2026 cut the island off from vehicle access (http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-city-delta-celebrates-reopening-westham-island).
- Ladner's local loop, route 618 from Ladner Exchange, serves the village side; its full street path is in the neilsen-grove note (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt618.pdf).

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Confirm ALR status first: it controls the farm house cap, the accessory dwelling limits and most use permissions, and it is checked parcel by parcel on the ALC maps (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps).
- Establish the parcel's flood construction level before designing a farm house: Delta's lowland is diked floodplain, the City maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, and the level is property-specific (http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage).
- For a Westham Island purchase, diligence the bridge: it is the only land access, it reopened March 29, 2026 after a 67-day closure, repair work continued after reopening, and a replacement is only at the planning stage (https://www.translink.ca/news/2026/january/westham%20island%20bridge%20repair).
- For a float home, diligence tenure, moorage and insurance before the lifestyle: none of that is established by listing text, and no source for the Canoe Pass community's structure is carried in this note (https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-rural.html).
- Get the parcel's ditch, drainage and irrigation position from the City: agricultural ditches switch between drainage and irrigation modes under City control gates (http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage).
