# Altamont (West Vancouver) — Questions for Jared

Area file for Altamont (District of West Vancouver hillside above Dundarave, at the British Properties edge). Five questions only Jared can answer from deals he has actually done or shown in the area. Do not answer these from research.

1. Where do your buyers draw Altamont's lines — which streets do they treat as Altamont versus Dundarave, West Bay or the British Properties?
2. In the Altamont deals you have seen, how much of the value is the view corridor versus lot size — and what sits or sells slowly here?
3. Do Altamont sellers price in suite, coach-house or multi-unit potential, or is it still a single-family story in practice?
4. Have the wildfire-interface or creek-setback permits, or a difficult slope, ever changed a deal on this hillside? What happened?
5. Tell one Altamont story: a showing, a view, or a deal that captures buying on this part of the West Vancouver hillside.
