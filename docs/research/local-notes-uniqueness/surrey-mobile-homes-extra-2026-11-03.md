## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- All Surrey manufactured-home parks named in the settled program sit in Newton, Fleetwood or South Surrey — none is verified in Whalley, Guildford or Cloverdale — and every park below is inside the City of Surrey, in Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-areas Fleetwood Tynehead, Queen Mary Park, West Newton, East Newton and King George Corridor. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- Westwood Court, 8670 156 Street, sits in Fleetwood east of 156 Street's Fraser Highway corridor; Westwood Estates, on 134 Street (listings use both 8224 and 8254 — confirm the civic address), sits in Queen Mary Park west of King George Boulevard. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- Newton Mobile Home Park (Newton MHP), 6571 King George Boulevard, and Crispen Bays, 7790 King George Boulevard, and Crestway Bays, 8220 King George Boulevard, form a King George Boulevard strip in Newton; Sea Ridge Bays, 15875 20 Avenue, is the South Surrey outlier, south toward the Nicomekl lowlands. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Surrey regulates manufactured home parks through Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 — check the park's zone and the bylaw's manufactured-home-park provisions for the specific address before relying on continued park use. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- Surrey's Manufactured Home Park Redevelopment and Strata Conversion Policy O-34A adds a City layer on top of provincial law when a Surrey park is redeveloped or strata-converted: the City brochure states the owner must notify residents of the application and the Council meeting, and the policy addresses relocation of existing residents and a right of first refusal to purchase. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- Surrey Tenant Protection Bylaw No. 21788 (2025) does NOT cover these homes: the City states it does not apply to property governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — Surrey pad tenants rely on the provincial Act plus Policy O-34A, not Bylaw 21788. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/submitting-a-development-application/rental-housing-redevelopment
- Provincial frame in brief: the Residential Tenancy Branch (RTB) caps a pad-rent increase at once every 12 months with at least 3 full months' notice — 2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount — and a park closure for another use needs all permits first, 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation. For the full pad-rent cost rules, see the cost-of-mobile-home-pad-rental-fees-in-langley note. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Are all Surrey parks seniors parks? **A:** No. The settled roster carries Westwood Court in Fleetwood, Westwood Estates in Queen Mary Park and Newton MHP on King George Boulevard as 55+, but Crispen Bays and Crestway Bays on King George Boulevard are carried as all-ages tenure records. Treat every age position as unconfirmed until the park's written rules are in hand, because no City or operator page read on 2026-11-03 states a Surrey park's age rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Q:** If a Surrey park is sold for redevelopment, what protects me? **A:** Two layers, and neither is Surrey's Tenant Protection Bylaw, which excludes manufactured home park property. Provincially, closure needs all permits in place, 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation; in Surrey, Policy O-34A also requires the owner to notify residents of the application and Council meeting and addresses relocation and first refusal to purchase. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- **Q:** Does buying the home transfer the pad to me automatically? **A:** No — the existing site tenancy is assigned to you, and assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on RTB Form 10; if the owner does not respond by the 10th day after receiving the request, consent is deemed given, and the pad rent stays the same on assignment. Get the park rules and the written tenancy agreement from the seller before asking. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q:** Is Sea Ridge Bays in South Surrey the same kind of purchase as Westwood Court? **A:** No. Sea Ridge Bays, 15875 20 Avenue, is bare-land strata — you own the home and the strata lot, and strata law governs. Westwood Court, 8670 156 Street, is a rented pad — you own only the home as a chattel. The two Surrey forms have different taxes, different sale paperwork and different protection on closure, so never compare them on price alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q:** Do I pay property tax on the land under a Surrey park home? **A:** No. The Province states a manufactured home is assessed and taxed like other residential property, but the assessed value does not include the land value where the home sits in a manufactured home park on land owned by someone else. A principal-residence home can still qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Two Surrey parks differ by one word and sit in two different communities: Westwood COURT is Fleetwood (8670 156 Street) and Westwood ESTATES is Queen Mary Park (134 Street) — an alert or a park-rule request sent to the wrong one diligence-checks the wrong park. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- Surrey school catchments are address-based in School District No. 36 (Surrey): the district states your catchment school is set by your address on its School Boundaries Map and directs families to its School Locator tool — no Surrey park is promised a named school in this note, because no park-address locator run was completed on 2026-11-03. https://www.surreyschools.ca/ellendale/school-registration
- South Surrey's Sea Ridge Bays pocket at 15875 20 Avenue sits in the low ground south of the Nicomekl River system: Surrey's lowlands there are diked and drained by pump stations under the Serpentine–Nicomekl lowlands flood control works, a different flood setting from the raised Newton and Fleetwood park sites — check the specific lot, not the city. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_AFPC_2023_09_05.pdf
- Surrey bills utilities two ways: a property without a water meter pays an annual flat rate once a year, and a metered property is billed every 4 months — which system a park passes through to residents is a park-documents question, not stated on the City page for any named park. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/paying-your-utility-bill
- A Surrey park home has no strata fee even when a listing form labels its monthly park charge that way — the charge is pad rent under a site tenancy, and the RTB, not a strata council, is the dispute route. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- A Surrey manufactured home cannot be sold without its original legible CSA (Canadian Standards Association) certification label — Z-240 for a mobile home, A-277 for a modular home — and a lost label needs Technical Safety BC re-certification before a sale can register. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City of Surrey 2026 residential (Class 1) property tax: $3.42731 per $1,000 of assessed value in total, of which the City's own municipal total is $1.84251 per $1,000 (general municipal $1.68947 plus roads and traffic $0.15304), with school, TransLink, Metro Vancouver, BC Assessment and Municipal Finance Authority levies making up the rest. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- Surrey 2026 annual flat-rate water and sewer for a single-family dwelling is $1,195 water plus $1,661 sewer, $2,856 in total; metered water is $1.4116 per cubic metre and metered sewer $2.3593 per cubic metre in 2026 — these are City dwelling rates, and a park's own billing to pad tenants is set by the park, not this table. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill
- BC Assessment treatment: a manufactured home in a Surrey park is assessed on the home only, excluding the park land value, because the land is owned by someone other than the home owner. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in the City of Surrey: the Province lists the City of Surrey as a designated taxable area, and only owners of residential-class property there must complete the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- Property Transfer Tax on a pad-park home resale in Surrey: UNCONFIRMED — no provincial page read settles the treatment of a chattel home sold with an assigned pad tenancy, so confirm with the lawyer or notary handling the Manufactured Home Registry transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Flood check for the South Surrey pocket: Surrey City Policy O-55, Development within the Nicomekl and Serpentine River Floodplains (adopted 2008 — check current), regulates development and filling in the 200-year floodplain, and City minutes record proposals inside the Serpentine 200-year floodplain being tested against O-55 — look up the Sea Ridge Bays lot's floodplain position with the City; the policy tells you the development rules, not whether a specific home has flooded. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_AFPC_2023_09_05.pdf
- Park-status check: confirm a Surrey park's zoning in Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and ask the City whether any Policy O-34A redevelopment or strata-conversion application is active for that address — zoning alone does not tell you a park owner's intentions. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- Manufactured Home Registry check: a sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is effective only if registered in the provincial Manufactured Home Registry, filed through a lawyer or notary with the original signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership — the Registry confirms the home's ownership record, not the pad tenancy's park-rule position. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- Closure-risk check: before buying on any Surrey pad, read the RTB end-of-tenancy page for the conversion rules — permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 on or before the effective date, and an added-compensation route where the home cannot be moved and no site is available within a reasonable distance. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
