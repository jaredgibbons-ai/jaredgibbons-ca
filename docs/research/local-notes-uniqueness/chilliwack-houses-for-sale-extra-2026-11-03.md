## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I add a suite or coach house? **A:** Often yes. Small-scale multi-unit rules require up to four units on lots over 280 square metres inside the urban growth boundary, as a suite, coach house, duplex, triplex or fourplex. Floodplain lots face extra limits: no new suites below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3279/FAQ_-_Small-Scale_Housing_-_Updated_2025_07_28.pdf
- **Q:** Is this house in the floodplain? **A:** Check the lot. Much of Chilliwack lies in the floodplain of the Fraser, Vedder or Sumas Rivers or hillside creeks, and Bylaw No. 4519 sets floor elevation at the flood construction level plus 0.6 metres freeboard, with City maps showing each area's level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- **Q:** Which schools will my children attend? **A:** School District No. 33 (SD33) sets catchments by street address, so use its School Locator for the exact address. One verified result: 8550 Young Road returned Robertson Elementary, A.D. Rundle Middle and Chilliwack Secondary. Nearby addresses can differ, even on the same street. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q:** Can a farm-edge parcel hold a second home? **A:** Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) rules limit most parcels to one residence unless another qualifies; a principal residence must be 500 square metres or less, with any suite inside it. Check the parcel on Agricultural Land Commission mapping before assuming a second home is possible. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
