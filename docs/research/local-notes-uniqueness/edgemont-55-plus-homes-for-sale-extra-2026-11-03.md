# Edgemont 55+ Homes — Extra Local Notes

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Edgemont Village is in the District of North Vancouver, not the City of North Vancouver: it is one of the District's six village centres in its Official Community Plan (OCP), centred on Edgemont Boulevard at Highland Boulevard in the Upper Capilano area. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- The Capilano Library at 3045 Highland Boulevard sits in the heart of Edgemont Village, and the village core is the Edgemont Boulevard strip around that library, the grocery and the cafés. https://www.nvdpl.ca/capilano-library
- No earlier Edgemont page exists in this program, so this note carries the Edgemont and District of North Vancouver facts in full.
- The two places 55+ housing actually sits are a short walk apart in the village core: Connaught Crescent for the owned strata, and Highland Boulevard for the rental care residence named in section 2. https://www.amica.ca/edgemont-village

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Edgemont Villa at 3151 Connaught Crescent is the one verified age-restricted strata ownership building in Edgemont: a small freehold strata built in 2003 and described in the settled roster in this program as a 55+ building of about 20 condos, with elevator, underground parking and storage. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Amica Edgemont Village at 3225 Highland Boulevard is the village's rental senior-living residence, registered with British Columbia's Assisted Living Registry with operator Edgemont Senior Living Inc., offering independent living, assisted living, memory care and licensed residential care — rental and care only, nothing to buy. https://www.amica.ca/edgemont-village
- Connaught, the Grosvenor project at the Edgemont Boulevard and Ridgewood Drive gateway, is 82 concrete condominiums and townhomes with ground-floor retail anchored by Thrifty Foods, construction commenced 2016 and completed 2019 — general-market stock, with no age restriction stated by the developer. https://www.grosvenor.com/property/property-america/connaught
- No subsidized seniors rental building in Edgemont was verified in the sources checked in the settled program file.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Only a 55+ age bylaw is legal in a British Columbia strata now: Strata Property Act sections 123.1 and 123.2 permit a restriction to age 55 or older, and any bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since November 24, 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- The bylaw governs who may live in the unit, not who may own it, and Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse or partner, and children in the defined care cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- District zoning is Zoning Bylaw 3210, and since June 2024 the District permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on single-family zoned properties inside the Urban Containment Boundary, which is the route to a caregiver suite on an Edgemont house lot. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- Schedule B of the District OCP establishes Development Permit Areas for streamside protection, wildfire hazard, creek hazard and slope hazard, and Mackay Creek and Mosquito Creek run through the neighbourhood's ravines — a permit layer on ravine-edge lots, not on the village-core strata. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What 55+ home can I actually buy in Edgemont Village? **A:** On the settled evidence, one building: Edgemont Villa at 3151 Connaught Crescent, a small freehold strata built in 2003 and carried as 55+ in this program's roster. Because the supply is one small building, resales are occasional rather than continuous, and the exact age-bylaw wording for a unit is confirmed from the strata documents and Form B for that unit, not from a listing summary. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** Is Amica Edgemont Village a purchase? **A:** No. Amica Edgemont Village at 3225 Highland Boulevard is a rental residence registered with the provincial Assisted Living Registry, with independent living, assisted living, memory care and licensed residential care in one building. A buyer who wants ownership in the village is looking at Edgemont Villa; a resident who wants care on site is looking at a tenancy and care agreement at Amica, not a strata lot. https://www.amica.ca/edgemont-village
- **Q:** Can I add a suite for a caregiver instead of buying the strata? **A:** In many cases, yes, as a housing form. Since June 2024 the District permits a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite plus a coach house on single-family zoned lots inside the Urban Containment Boundary, subject to the lot's zone and to any creek or slope development permit area. That three-unit permission is District-wide, so the specific lot's permit areas are the deciding check. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Q:** Where is the hospital for Edgemont? **A:** Lions Gate Hospital at 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, is the North Shore hospital under Vancouver Coastal Health, in the City of North Vancouver east of the village. Edgemont itself has no hospital; its in-village health-adjacent anchor is the care ladder in section 2, from the owned strata on Connaught Crescent to the registered care suites on Highland Boulevard. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The village grocery site was rebuilt as the Connaught project: the retail that Edgemont Villa and Amica residents walk to is the renewed Edgemont Boulevard and Ridgewood Drive core with Thrifty Foods at ground level, completed in 2019. https://www.grosvenor.com/property/property-america/connaught
- The elementary catchment split runs through the neighbourhood — the Highland Boulevard side and the Colwood Drive side sample to different elementary schools in the School District 44 (North Vancouver) locator, both feeding Handsworth Secondary — a street-by-street fact that affects resale to families. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044
- District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after July 2 and a further 5 percent after September 1, 2026. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf
- A live-in caregiver is exempt from a 55+ age bylaw by provincial regulation, which is the legal basis for ageing in place with care inside an Edgemont Villa unit rather than moving to the Amica building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The District of North Vancouver's total 2026 residential tax rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value, made up in the District's rate sheet of a $1.83419 municipal rate plus school, TransLink, Metro Vancouver, British Columbia Assessment and other levies. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- The District's 2026 utility sheet sets single-family water at $932.80 and a combined single-family utility total of $2,571.20; strata units carry their own utility arrangements, confirmed on the Form B. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies in the District of North Vancouver as a Metro Vancouver taxable area on the Province's list, so an owner here files the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
