# Whonnock Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: whonnock (Whonnock; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Whonnock is a City of Maple Ridge neighbourhood and a board sub-area name in the rural east of the city, centred on Whonnock Lake. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The City publishes no boundary for the neighbourhood, so the working scope used here is the live page's framing only: roughly 264 Street east to the Mission border at 288 Street. https://jaredgibbons.ca/whonnock.html
- Webster's Corners and Thornhill sit to the west and north-west, Ruskin to the east inside Maple Ridge, and the City of Mission lies across the eastern municipal boundary. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The east–west corridors are Lougheed Highway (Highway 7), Dewdney Trunk Road and 112 Avenue, with 272 Street and 280 Street running north–south. https://jaredgibbons.ca/whonnock.html

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is rural residential and farm parcels, not urban subdivisions: the zoning schedule that governs most lots is the rural and agricultural end of Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- Minimum lot areas in that schedule are A-1 at 2.0 hectares, A-2 at 4.0 hectares, A-3 at 8.0 hectares, RS-2 at 0.4 hectares and RS-3 at 0.8 or 2.0 hectares depending on the zone text. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- Whonnock Elementary at 27471 112 Avenue is the village anchor building, a Kindergarten to Grade 7 school in School District 42 (SD42). https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/school/04242040
- Whonnock Lake Park at 27871 113 Avenue is the City's recreation site on the lake, listed by the City as a park and picnic ground. https://www.mapleridge.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Whonnock-Lake-114
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) parcels carry the agricultural zone minimums above, and a subdivision of ALR land also needs Agricultural Land Commission approval — the City states that test when it reports on rural applications. https://pub-mapleridge.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=6908
- The same City report states the RS-3 minimum lot area is 0.8 hectares, so a 1.5-hectare RS-3 parcel cannot subdivide without rezoning. https://pub-mapleridge.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=6908
- City policy in OCP Section 6.2.2 discourages subdividing agricultural land into smaller parcels, which is the policy test a Whonnock subdivision application faces. https://pub-mapleridge.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=6908
- A detached garden suite in the RS-3 zone may reach 7.5 metres in height under a 2021 zoning amendment the City cites in its rural application reports. https://pub-mapleridge.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=6908

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Does Whonnock have city sewer? **A:** No municipal sewer statement could be confirmed on a City page in this pass, so treat servicing as a per-lot check. What is confirmed is the rural zoning pattern — RS-3's minimum rises to 2.0 hectares without community water in the bylaw summary — which is why buyers here budget for a septic inspection and a well potability and flow test on any acreage purchase. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Q:** Which school do Whonnock children attend? **A:** Whonnock Elementary is the in-village school, at 27471 112 Avenue, Kindergarten to Grade 7. SD42 catchments are address-based and no locator result for a Whonnock address is on record in this program, so confirm the exact address in the district locator; the nearby named schools are Webster's Corners Elementary at 25554 Dewdney Trunk Road and Garibaldi Secondary at 24789 Dewdney Trunk Road. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q:** Is there a school bus in Whonnock? **A:** Yes — Whonnock is one of the few Maple Ridge catchments with regular district busing. SD42 lists Whonnock Elementary among the schools with regular transportation, for Kindergarten to Grade 3 students living farther than 4 kilometres from the school and Grade 4 to 12 students farther than 4.8 kilometres, with registration deadlines each year. https://www.sd42.ca/student-transportation/
- **Q:** Can I subdivide a Whonnock acreage? **A:** Only if the zone minimums and, on ALR land, the province's process both allow it. The bylaw schedule sets A-2 at 4.0 hectares and A-3 at 8.0 hectares, the City applies OCP policy discouraging agricultural subdivision, and ALR subdivision needs an Agricultural Land Commission application on top of any City rezoning. https://pub-mapleridge.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=6908
- **Q:** What is Whonnock Lake used for? **A:** It is the neighbourhood's recreation centre of gravity. The City describes Whonnock Lake Park as a popular destination with a small sandy beach, a swimming area, hiking trails and a playground, with the Ridge Canoe and Kayak Club based on the lake; students from Whonnock Elementary walk to the lake for the district's Coldest Night fundraiser route. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Whonnock Elementary doubles as a polling place and community hall substitute — it hosted the Whonnock voting location for the October 17, 2026 municipal election. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- There is no City recreation centre or library branch inside Whonnock; the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre at 11925 Haney Place and the library at 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road are in Town Centre to the west. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre
- TransLink route 701 runs east on Dewdney Trunk Road, but most east trips end at 248 Street and only some weekday trips continue non-stop from Haney Place to Mission City Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/194/tt701.pdf
- There is no West Coast Express station in Whonnock; the nearest stations are Port Haney/Maple Meadows to the west and Mission City to the east. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The rural cost difference here is servicing: lots without community water fall under the larger RS-3 minimum, and well and septic work is a private cost rather than a City utility bill. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- Maple Ridge utility charges are mostly billed on the annual property tax notice, with metered customers on quarterly bills — most Whonnock homes are not on that metered system. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/utilities
- For the Maple Ridge headline residential tax rate, see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note — the rate is stated in full there and is not restated here.

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) is the through route west to Town Centre and east to Mission, with Dewdney Trunk Road as the parallel local route. https://jaredgibbons.ca/whonnock.html
- Route 749 (Ruskin) is the local bus named for the adjoining Ruskin area; its timetable details are unconfirmed below. https://www.sd42.ca/student-transportation/
- Driving is the default here: SD42's bus eligibility distances (4 and 4.8 kilometres) exist because rural Whonnock addresses sit far from the school. https://www.sd42.ca/student-transportation/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the wildfire permit map: Whonnock's forested lots fall under the same Wildfire Development Permit Area framework, requiring a permit or a covenant with wildfire construction standards. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/wildfire-development-permit-construction-requirements
- Check Whonnock Creek and lake-edge setbacks: the floodplain bylaw designates land within its setbacks and land under 1.5 metres above a watercourse's natural boundary as floodplain. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
- Check the farm home plate and accessory-building limits before planning a shop: a City rural report shows variances being sought to deepen a farm home plate and raise accessory height, which signals how tight the default limits are. https://pub-mapleridge.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=6908
