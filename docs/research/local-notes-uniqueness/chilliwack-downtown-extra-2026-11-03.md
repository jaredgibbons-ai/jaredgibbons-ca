# Chilliwack Downtown Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: chilliwack-downtown (Chilliwack; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Downtown is the historic core of Chilliwack Proper, centred on Five Corners, where Yale Road and Young Road intersect with Wellington Avenue. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/downtown/
- The downtown core centres on Main Street, Yale Road East and Young Road, with First Avenue and Princess Avenue framing the District 1881 blocks. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/downtown/
- Municipally this ground is planned as Schedule B3, the Downtown Neighbourhood Plan, inside the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP); it is a Chilliwack and District Real Estate Board (CADREB) market, not a Fraser Valley board market. https://chilliwack.com/ocp https://www.cadreb.com/market/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which plan actually governs a downtown purchase? **A:** The Downtown Neighbourhood Plan, Schedule B3 of the Chilliwack 2050 OCP. Adopted in June 2010 and updated in 2025, it sets the vision for the Downtown Plan Area over 30 years, covering land use, housing, commercial vitality, open space, transportation and the form and character of both public and private development. Read the parcel's designation in B3 before valuing any infill potential. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3556
- **Q:** What is District 1881, and who built it? **A:** District 1881 is the redevelopment of the 3.75-acre downtown site bounded by Young Road, Yale Road, Princess Avenue and Empress Lane. The City of Chilliwack and the Chilliwack Economic Partners Corporation (CEPCO) assembled the properties, and Council awarded the redevelopment contract to Algra Bros. Developments Ltd. The project combines retail, commercial, residential and mixed-use buildings across multiple phases. https://www.businessinchilliwack.com/downtown/
- **Q:** Can I change the exterior of a downtown building freely? **A:** No. Development in the mapped downtown area is subject to the D-6 form-and-character permit guidelines, which exist to facilitate the Downtown Design Guidelines. New work is expected to fit the core's form, proportion and detailing, and buildings of heritage value are encouraged to be restored with historically accurate materials, so exterior plans need a permit check first. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8154
- **Q:** Which schools are in the downtown core? **A:** Four School District No. 33 (SD33) schools sit in the core corridor: Chilliwack Central Elementary Community, 9435 Young Road; Bernard Elementary, 45465 Bernard Avenue; Chilliwack Middle School, 46354 Yale Road; and Chilliwack Secondary, 46363 Yale Road. Catchments are address-based, so confirm the exact address in the SD33 School Locator rather than relying on the downtown label. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/chilliwack https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Downtown Chilliwack is the main British Columbia Transit (BC Transit) exchange and terminus for local routes 51 to 59, 71 and 72. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- Route 66 Fraser Valley Express runs from Downtown Chilliwack to Lougheed Town Centre Station, with Chilliwack stops including Vedder Road at Luckakuck Way and the Lickman Park & Ride. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- BC Transit's December 18, 2025 service notice, effective January 4, 2026, added Sunday-evening service for Route 57 Broadway, Route 53 Fairfield and Route 51. https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/
- Highway 1 access for the core is via the Yale Road / Young Road corridor interchanges; drive times to Abbotsford or Vancouver are UNCONFIRMED, as no operator page states them.
