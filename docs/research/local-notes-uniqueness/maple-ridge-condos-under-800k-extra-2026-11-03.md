## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Every strata with five or more lots must now have a depreciation report on a five-year cycle, with no deferral vote. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- The most recent depreciation report must be attached to the Form B Information Certificate given to buyers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Form B is the disclosure a buyer prices from: the Province's Form B page covers strata fees, amounts owing, levies and the attached budget, rules and depreciation report. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- Rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable for long-term rentals since November 24, 2022, whatever an older building directory says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024 covers the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station areas, which is the planning frame for new core buildings. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What does the Form B tell me that the listing does not? **A:** It tells you the money facts the listing omits. The Province requires the certificate to carry the unit's strata fees and amounts owing and to attach the rules, the current budget and the most recent depreciation report, so special levies, reserve funding and parking and storage allocation are checked there before subject removal, not after. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q:** How current must a building's depreciation report be? **A:** On a five-year cycle, with no opt-out vote. Since July 1, 2024 every strata corporation with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, the report projects maintenance and replacement over 30 years, and corporations with four or fewer lots are the only ones exempt — so a missing report on a larger Maple Ridge building is a red flag, not a normal gap. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q:** Can a first-time buyer use the property transfer tax (PTT) exemption in this band? **A:** Yes, on the Province's stated thresholds. The Province says qualifying first-time buyers save up to $8,000, with tax eliminated on the first $500,000 for an existing home worth up to $835,000 — a ceiling above this page's whole band — provided the buyer meets the program's citizenship, residency and never-owned conditions and keeps the home as a principal residence. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/more-homes-for-people
- **Q:** How do I check a building's pet and parking rules? **A:** Read them in the strata documents, in this order. Ask for the Form B first, because it states how parking and storage are allocated to the unit, then read the registered bylaws and rules attached to it for the pet bylaw; a directory summary or a listing remark is not the bylaw, and only the filed bylaw text governs what a buyer can keep or park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q:** Which stations serve the downtown condo core? **A:** Port Haney and Maple Meadows, both on the West Coast Express. TransLink runs the weekday commuter line between Waterfront in downtown Vancouver and Mission City with those two Maple Ridge stops, and Haney Place Exchange in the core carries the R3 RapidBus toward Coquitlam Central — the two anchors the Town Centre plan builds its density around. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Four Fraserview Village buildings are separate strata corporations sharing one village amenity building, so a unit's fees and votes sit with its own strata, not the village.
- The core's catchment check is address-based: the program's recorded locator sample for a downtown address (11897 227 Street) returned Eric Langton Elementary and Thomas Haney Secondary, with French Immersion shown as choice programs, not catchments. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- Stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget since November 1, 2023 — a line to verify in any older building's budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Maple Ridge property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026 — the adjustment dates a condo buyer sees at completion. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- PTT is the band's main closing tax: the Province's general rates and the first-time buyer thresholds in Section 4 decide the bill, and the exemption is claimed by the buyer's legal professional on the PTT return. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- Maple Ridge utility charges are billed mainly on the annual tax notice, with metered accounts billed quarterly from January 1, 2026 under seasonal rates. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/utilities
- For the Maple Ridge headline residential tax rate, see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note — the rate is stated in full there and is not restated here.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check flood status for river-edge buildings: on the Port Haney/River Road edge the verified flood construction level is 6.7 metres geodetic including freeboard, with a covenant on title and no habitable floor space below that level on affected lots. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- The current floodplain framework is Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 8121-2026, which designates flood hazard and connected riverine areas across the city. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
- In an older building, the depreciation report's 30-year projection and the contingency reserve fund balance are the levy-risk check, and both must be in the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
