## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Cottonwood is a named Maple Ridge neighbourhood in the City's own planning geography, listed among the Engage Maple Ridge neighbourhood names alongside Albion, Thornhill, Town Centre and Webster's Corners. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/about
- Cottonwood's working centre is the Cottonwood Drive / Dewdney Trunk Road area east of the Town Centre — the exact City-stated boundary streets were not returned in this pass, so the boundary is UNCONFIRMED here (see NOT CONFIRMED).
- Cottonwood sits between the Town Centre to the west and the Kanaka Creek / Albion side of Maple Ridge to the east, on the Dewdney Trunk Road corridor toward Websters Corners.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- One secondary suite per lot is the Cottonwood rule under the city-wide summary: the suite must be inside the same building as the single detached home and must not exceed 40% of total Gross Floor Area (total floor area). https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- Secondary suites are permitted in zones the City lists as A-1, A-2, A-3, A-4, R-1, R-4, RS-1, RS-1a, RS-1b, RS-1c, RS-1d, RS-2, RS-3 and others — a Cottonwood buyer must confirm the lot's own zone in Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-03/Secondary-Suite-Brochure-2021.pdf
- A suite must not be in the Floodplain Area unless built above the established flood construction level, per the City's suite summary. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-03/Secondary-Suite-Brochure-2021.pdf
- General Maple Ridge zoning position is in the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note — see that note.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** How do I get to the Golden Ears Bridge from Cottonwood? **A:** The planned Langley–Haney Place Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) route shows the corridor logic for this side of Maple Ridge: it connects Maple Ridge with the Township of Langley via 200 Street, the Golden Ears Bridge and Lougheed Highway, over a 22-kilometre route with 13 stations. Drivers use the same Golden Ears Way / Golden Ears Bridge corridor south from the Lougheed Highway area. https://www.translink.ca/brt?utm_source=direct&utm_medium=shorturl&utm_campaign=brt&_gl=1%2Ajci4xc%2A_ga%2AMTQwNDM4NTgyMi4xNzIyMzczNTEz%2A_ga_QGWL01WSZ6%2AMTcyMjM3MzUxMi4xLjAuMTcyMjM3MzUyMC41Mi4wLjA.
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Cottonwood address? **A:** Cottonwood is in School District 42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows). The provincial list gives Thomas Haney Secondary at 23000 116 Avenue and Maple Ridge Secondary at 21911 122 Avenue among the district's secondary schools, but the exact elementary catchment for a Cottonwood street was not confirmed from a district locator in this pass, so confirm it with SD42 for the address. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- **Q:** Can a Cottonwood house have a legal suite? **A:** Yes, if the zone and building allow it: Maple Ridge permits one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, capped at 40% of total Gross Floor Area, with a building permit required for construction or renovation, and a Business Licence required if there are two rental units on the property. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q:** Is Cottonwood affected by the Lougheed corridor plan? **A:** Cottonwood is east of the Lougheed Transit Corridor's Town Centre end at 221 Street. The corridor plan itself runs from the city's western boundary to the Town Centre along Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road, so Cottonwood buyers should check whether a Dewdney Trunk Road lot falls inside a corridor or Town Centre policy area on City mapping. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Kanaka Creek Regional Park runs about 11 kilometres up Kanaka Creek from the Fraser River to just south of Websters Corners, past the Albion side of Maple Ridge — the park corridor east of Cottonwood. http://www.metrovancouver.org/services/parks/ParksPublications/KanakaCreekParkMap.pdf
- The City monitors Kanaka Creek levels during high streamflow events, along with the North and South Alouette and Fraser Rivers. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/high-streamflow-advisory-1
- Sandbags for flood-prone Maple Ridge residents are made available at the City Operations Centre at 23925 Dewdney Trunk Road when risk increases. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/high-streamflow-advisory-1
- A secondary suite must remain under the same legal title as the main home and cannot be used for short-term or vacation rentals, per the City's suite summary. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-03/Secondary-Suite-Brochure-2021.pdf
- Maple Ridge's draft Capital Plan includes a 240 Street Bridge north of 124 Avenue as a second Alouette River crossing — a north-side project Cottonwood buyers watching Abernethy Way traffic should know is in pre-planning, not built. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/Attachment-A-Updated-2026-2030-Capital-Plan-1%29.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check Kanaka Creek flood information first for low Cottonwood lots: the City's floodplain work states mapping will include minimum Flood Construction Levels for the Fraser River and parts of the Alouette River, with other systems to follow in 2027. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/Floodplain-Bylaw-Open-House-36-x-48-in%29.pdf
- The City's flood material distinguishes creek flooding from river flooding — steep creeks such as Kanaka Creek can carry fast water, sediment and debris onto alluvial fans below. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/Floodplain-Bylaw-Open-House-36-x-48-in%29.pdf
- Report blocked drains or debris to the City's 24-hour line at 604-463-9581 or MapleRidge.ca/Report, per the City's streamflow advisory — a useful number for a Cottonwood owner near a creek. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/high-streamflow-advisory-1
