# Forest Hills Condos and Townhomes — Extra Local Notes
Page: forest-hills-burnaby-condos-townhomes-for-sale (Forest Hills, Burnaby; condos/townhomes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Forest Hills is a small attached-housing pocket on the lower south-west slope of Burnaby Mountain, built around Forest Grove Drive, Ash Grove Crescent and Beaverbrook Crescent, below Simon Fraser University (SFU). https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- The City-facing name for the pocket is Forest Grove — Forest Grove Drive, Forest Grove Elementary at 8525 Forest Grove Drive and Forest Grove Park at 8505 Forest Grove Drive — while "Forest Hills" is the board sub-area name. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041 http://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- Gaglardi Way is the road up Burnaby Mountain to SFU east of the pocket, and Lougheed Highway runs along its south side toward Lougheed Town Centre. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt136.pdf
- The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for the Forest Hills board sub-area in the sources read — the exact edge streets are in NOT CONFIRMED. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The verified attached stock here is 1980s townhome complexes on Forest Grove Drive, not apartment towers: no apartment-condo building inside Forest Hills was verified on an official source in this pass. https://bchousing.org/publications/Zone-5-Choices-for-Families.pdf
- Wembley Estates, 8187 Forest Grove Drive at Underhill Avenue, is a 28-unit townhouse complex, freehold strata, whose own strata council filing in the Trans Mountain hearing record places it roughly 50 metres from the entrance to the Burnaby Terminal. https://docs2.cer-rec.gc.ca/ll-eng/llisapi.dll/fetch/2000/90464/90552/548311/956726/2392873/2451003/2482312/B173-1_-_Trans_Mountain_Response_to_Wembley_Estates_IR_No._1_-_A3Y3W9.pdf?nodeid=2482313&
- Henley Estate, 8210 Forest Grove Drive, is a townhome strata, plan NW2129, named as "Henley Estate Strata NW2129" in a Canada Energy Regulator intervenor record. https://docs2.cer-rec.gc.ca/ll-eng/llisapi.dll/fetch/2000/90464/90552/548311/956726/2392873/2449981/2445932/A14%2D3_%2D_Appendix_I_%2D_List_of_Participants_%2D_Part_A_%E2%80%93_Intervenors_%2D_Revised_16_February_2016_%2D_A3V6I6.pdf?nodeid=2445934&vernum=14
- Three of the pocket's largest multi-unit sites are not strata at all: Hillside Place Housing Co-operative at 8400 Forest Grove Drive, Whattlekainum Co-operative at 8740 Forest Grove Drive and Pine Ridge Co-operative at 8763 Ash Grove Crescent are membership housing in BC Housing's directory. https://bchousing.org/publications/Zone-5-Choices-for-Families.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Burnaby's current zoning bylaw is the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026 and in effect July 1, 2026, replacing the former 1965 bylaw — any Forest Hills source still citing Bylaw No. 4742 is superseded. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-07/Zoning-Bylaw-Consolidated-Without-Schedules.pdf
- The 2026 bylaw carries a Townhouse District (R2) and apartment districts (R3 to R8) in Part 3, and its Schedule II sets the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area guidelines that apply near Stoney Creek. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-07/Zoning-Bylaw-Consolidated-Without-Schedules.pdf
- No Forest Hills strata can bar long-term rentals: rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since November 24, 2022, whatever a 1980s complex's original bylaw package says. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- Stratas with five or more lots here must hold a depreciation report renewed every five years since July 1, 2024 — for 1980s townhome stock, that report is where envelope, roofing and roadway work is scheduled. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- A co-operative unit is not bought under the Strata Property Act: the three Forest Grove and Ash Grove co-operatives in Section 2 have no Form B, no depreciation-report regime and no market resale. https://bchousing.org/publications/Zone-5-Choices-for-Families.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Are there apartment condos in Forest Hills? **A:** None was verified on an official source in this pass. The pocket's buyable attached stock is townhome complexes on Forest Grove Drive — Wembley Estates at 8187 Forest Grove Drive and Henley Estate at 8210 Forest Grove Drive are the two named on federal hearing records — and the large sites a search surfaces on Forest Grove Drive and Ash Grove Crescent are housing co-operatives, not condos for sale. https://bchousing.org/publications/Zone-5-Choices-for-Families.pdf
- **Q:** Is the complex at 8400 Forest Grove Drive a condo I can buy? **A:** No. 8400 Forest Grove Drive is Hillside Place Housing Co-operative in BC Housing's Zone 5 directory: membership housing applied for through the co-op and Housing Registry route, not a strata purchase. The same applies at Whattlekainum Co-operative, 8740 Forest Grove Drive, and Pine Ridge Co-operative, 8763 Ash Grove Crescent. None of the three produces a Form B or a market listing a buyer can act on. https://bchousing.org/publications/Zone-5-Choices-for-Families.pdf
- **Q:** How close is Wembley Estates to the Burnaby Terminal? **A:** About 50 metres from the terminal entrance, on the strata council's own evidence: its filing in the Trans Mountain Expansion Project hearing record describes the 28-unit townhouse complex at the corner of Underhill Avenue and Forest Grove Drive as roughly 50 metres from the entrance to the Burnaby Terminal, with additional tanks proposed uphill of the complex in that application. Read that filing before offering on a Wembley unit. https://docs2.cer-rec.gc.ca/ll-eng/llisapi.dll/fetch/2000/90464/90552/548311/956726/2392873/2451003/2482312/B173-1_-_Trans_Mountain_Response_to_Wembley_Estates_IR_No._1_-_A3Y3W9.pdf?nodeid=2482313&
- **Q:** Will the Burnaby Mountain Gondola pass over my townhome? **A:** It is proposed, not built or funded. TransLink's preferred straight route from Production Way–University Station to SFU would pass above the Forest Grove neighbourhood, and the City of Burnaby's project page records that Forest Grove residents have raised noise, privacy, visual and property-impact concerns; the project still needs inclusion in a future investment plan. Do not pay today for a station that does not exist. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/burnaby-mountain-gondola
- **Q:** Which schools are inside the pocket? **A:** Two School District No. 41 (SD41) elementaries sit inside it: Forest Grove Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 7, at 8525 Forest Grove Drive, and Stoney Creek Community Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 7, at 2740 Beaverbrook Crescent, with Burnaby Mountain Secondary, Grades 8 to 12, at 8800 Eastlake Drive the nearest secondary. Catchments are address-based — run the exact address with SD41 before relying on either elementary. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The library serving this pocket is a temporary location: the Cameron Branch of Burnaby Public Library operates inside The City of Lougheed Shopping Centre at 9855 Austin Avenue during the Cameron rebuild. http://burnaby.ca/CameronLougheed
- Forest Grove Park at 8505 Forest Grove Drive, beside the elementary school of the same name, is the pocket's park and court site — the amenity core here is school-and-park, with no shopping centre inside the sub-area. http://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- Stoney Creek runs through the pocket toward Burnaby Lake, and the City lists a Stoney Creek Stormwater Management Plan among its watershed plans — a creek-side townhome's setbacks are a parcel-level check, not a listing detail. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/construction-and-renovation/green-building-and-land-development/environment-and-eco-system-protection
- TransLink's gondola engagement record commits to compensation for two multi-family property complexes for aerial passage if Route 1 proceeds — a disclosure item specific to Forest Grove complexes under the line. https://www.llbc.leg.bc.ca/public/pubdocs/bcdocs2022_2/727263/727263_Burnaby_Mountain_Gondola_responding_to_key_engagement_themes.pdf
- UniverCity and SFU product is a different sub-area: leasehold and university-land homes on the mountain top are not Forest Hills stock and are not counted here. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby's 2026 residential tax rates, per the City's tax page: the City of Burnaby portion is 1.55649 dollars per $1,000 of assessed value, and the total residential rate across all taxing authorities shown is 2.97749 per $1,000. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/additional-information
- Burnaby utility charges for a multiple family dwelling in 2026 are $374.54 for water and $436.00 for sewer use, on the City's residential utility fees page, with a 5 percent discount if paid by the due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- In 2026 Burnaby held water at a 0 percent increase and sewer at 3.5 percent, using reserves against higher Metro Vancouver charges, per the City's financial plan release. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2026-01-28/burnaby-keeps-tax-rates-affordable-latest-financial-plan
- The sewer parcel tax still sits on the 2026 property tax notice; the City states it moves to the utility notice starting in 2027. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- Burnaby is a Speculation and Vacancy Tax taxable area, listed by the Province in the Metro Vancouver Regional District, so every residential owner here must file the declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 136 is the pocket's bus: Lougheed Station to Brentwood Station via Forest Grove Drive, Underhill Avenue and Production Way, per TransLink's timetable — it is the link from a Forest Grove Drive townhome to the SkyTrain network. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt136.pdf
- The nearest rapid station is Production Way–University Station on the Millennium Line, reached on route 136; there is no SkyTrain station inside Forest Hills. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt136.pdf
- Lougheed Station's bus exchange is route 136's terminus, connecting the pocket to both the Millennium and Expo Lines. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt136.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Streamside rules are the first check on a creek-side lot: Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, Schedule II, sets the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area guidelines, and a development on a parcel subject to it needs an Environmental Assessment Report by a Qualified Environmental Professional confirming the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area boundary. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-06/Preparing-Environmental-Assessment-Report.pdf
- Steep-slope risk is policy-mapped in the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan work: the City's climate and natural hazards directions restrict development on steep slopes through Development Permit Areas and vary setbacks at the top of slopes — relevant on the pocket's sloped lots below the Burnaby Mountain Conservation Area. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/burnaby-2050-planning-our-city-together/burnaby-2050-policy-guide-share-your-feedback/climate-change-and-natural-hazards
- Tank-farm proximity is a Wembley Estates-specific check: the complex sits roughly 50 metres from the Burnaby Terminal entrance on its strata council's own filing, which is the document a buyer reads for the expansion history. https://docs2.cer-rec.gc.ca/ll-eng/llisapi.dll/fetch/2000/90464/90552/548311/956726/2392873/2451003/2482312/B173-1_-_Trans_Mountain_Response_to_Wembley_Estates_IR_No._1_-_A3Y3W9.pdf?nodeid=2482313&
- Wildfire interface status for a specific Forest Grove Drive lot is not stated on any City page read — the pocket borders the forested Burnaby Mountain Conservation Area, and a buyer checks the lot's status with the City directly. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/burnaby-mountain-gondola
