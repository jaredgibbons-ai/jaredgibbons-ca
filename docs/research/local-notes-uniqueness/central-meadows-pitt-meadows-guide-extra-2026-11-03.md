# Central Meadows Pitt Meadows Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: central-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide (Central Meadows, Pitt Meadows; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Central Meadows is a board sub-area name; the City's planning name for this ground is the Municipal Town Centre, covering the Harris Road corridor, the North Lougheed area and the Civic Centre — see the central-meadows-houses-for-sale note. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/our-community/community-plan/section_5_-_land_use_designations.pdf
- Harris Road is the main street: City Hall is at 12007 Harris Road and Pitt Meadows Elementary at 11941 Harris Road. https://elementary.sd42.ca/pittmeadows/events/category/district-calendar/day/2026-11-03/
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) is the north edge and the Canadian Pacific Kansas City (CPKC) rail line along Hammond Road is the south edge. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/our-community/community-plan/section_5_-_land_use_designations.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The Official Community Plan's Town Centre Commercial designation permits high-density mixed commercial and residential uses in the central area, and does not include highway commercial uses. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/our-community/community-plan/section_5_-_land_use_designations.pdf
- Around that core, Residential – Medium Density permits townhouse-form housing, and Residential – Low Density permits forms from single-detached houses to townhouses at up to 30 units per hectare, per the plan. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/our-community/community-plan/section_5_-_land_use_designations.pdf
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The City's 2025 Official Community Plan update (Amendment Bylaw No. 3034, 2025) concentrates higher-density residential and commercial development along Harris Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Oct_28_2025_Staff_Report_OCP_Update_2025_and_OCP_Amendment_Bylaw_3034_2025.pdf
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing) caps are zone-based: up to three units in R-3, four in R-4, and six in R-6 where the sixth unit is adaptable; lots over 280 square metres within 400 metres of a frequent transit stop can reach six. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- One secondary suite per principal dwelling is allowed in the RS, R-3, R-4 and R-6 zones, sized 33 to 90 square metres; in R-3, R-4 and R-6 no extra off-street parking space is required for the suite. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/Guide-to-Secondary-Suites.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What is actually being built at the Civic Centre? **A:** Heron's Nest, at 19085 119B Avenue: a Metro Vancouver Housing non-market rental building with a ground-floor childcare facility, serving seniors, families and people with disabilities. The City's staff report counts 115 apartments, including 16 accessible units, with roughly half the homes as two- or three-bedroom family units; construction runs about two and a half years. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/herons-nest-non-market-housing-and-child-care-project https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/city-hall/hearings/Council%20Report%20-%20January%2024%2C%202023%20-%2019085%20119B.pdf
- **Q:** Is the secondary school being replaced? **A:** Yes — the Ministry of Education and Child Care funded the Pitt Meadows Secondary replacement, including a neighbourhood learning centre, in August 2024, and the new school is anticipated to open in September 2029. Site preparation began in 2026, with soil compaction work along the south side of the existing building in October 2026. https://www.sd42.ca/replacement-secondary-meadows/ https://secondary.sd42.ca/pmss/pmss-replacement-project-update-october-16-2026/
- **Q:** How flood-exposed is the Town Centre? **A:** Less than the lowlands, but check the lot: over 95% of Pitt Meadows' land base lies in the Fraser River floodplain, while the City's plan notes most of the urban area sits outside the defined floodplain on higher ground. The floodplain bylaw still sets a 5.15-metre urban designated flood level, so confirm which schedule covers the property. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/our-community/community-plan/section_4_-_policies_-_4_1_natural_environment.pdf
- **Q:** How do I reach downtown Vancouver or SkyTrain from here? **A:** Pitt Meadows Station is in this central area: the West Coast Express runs Monday to Friday at peak periods, five trains each way, and the City puts the ride to downtown Vancouver at about 45 minutes. Bus route 701 and the RapidBus connection toward Coquitlam Central Station, about 20 minutes, are the off-peak links. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Heron's Nest construction displaces the Seniors Activity Centre parking lot; a temporary gravel lot sits west of the 119B Avenue and 190A Street roundabout, reserved for seniors centre users Monday to Saturday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/herons-nest-non-market-housing-and-child-care-project
- Pitt Meadows Elementary at 11941 Harris Road sits directly in the Civic Centre cluster, beside the recreation centre and library. https://elementary.sd42.ca/pittmeadows/events/category/district-calendar/day/2026-11-03/
- The Civic Centre cluster — recreation centre, elementary school, seniors centre, library, shops and transit — is the stated reason the Heron's Nest site was chosen. http://pittmeadows.ca/herons-nest
- Harris Road bus stops in front of the Civic Centre are served by route 701, a frequent transit network route, and route 791 to Braid Station, per the City-commissioned parking study. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-06/Parking%20Study.pdf
- The 119B Avenue frontage is a designated neighbourhood bikeway with direct connections to the Harris Road and Hammond Road bike lanes. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-06/Parking%20Study.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Pitt Meadows collects property taxes and utility fees in one combined bill; the 2026 increase for the average single-family home is $319 combined, on an average assessed value of $1,184,823 stated in the City's tax insert. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/Tax-insert-2026.pdf
- Taxes under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after August 1, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- Pitt Meadows is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so residential owners must declare. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The West Coast Express runs Monday to Friday in peak periods only — five trains westbound in the morning and eastbound in the afternoon — on Canadian Pacific track through Pitt Meadows Station. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain
- Route 701 is this area's frequent route, running about every 15 minutes through the day in the City-commissioned study, linking Coquitlam Central Station, Harris Road, Pitt Meadows Station and Maple Ridge. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-06/Parking%20Study.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008: Schedule A Rural Floodplain designated level is 1.85 metres geodetic and Schedule B Urban Floodplain is 5.15 metres — which schedule covers the lot changes the buildable floor height. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/Floodplain%20Designation%20and%20Construction%20Control%20Bylaw%20No.%202384%2C%202008%20-%20Consolidated.pdf
- The city is almost entirely protected by 60 kilometres of dikes, with four drainage areas served by flood boxes and pump stations; a dike or drainage charge is part of the utility bill in Section 7. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- Construction noise is a live due-diligence item near the secondary school site through 2030, and near Heron's Nest for about 2.5 years from its start. https://secondary.sd42.ca/pmss/pmss-replacement-project-update-october-16-2026/
