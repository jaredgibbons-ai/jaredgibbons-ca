# Ladner Elementary Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: ladner-elementary (Ladner Elementary; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The Ladner Elementary area is the residential district around Ladner Elementary School in Ladner, one of the three communities of the City of Delta, on the south shore of the Fraser River's main arm. https://le.deltasd.bc.ca/our-school/about-our-school/
- The school itself — École Élémentaire Ladner Elementary — is at 5016 44 Avenue, on 44 Avenue at the 50 Street crosswalk, in the middle of the Ladner townsite grid of numbered streets and avenues. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/delta
- The school's own page places it "in the heart of Ladner" in Delta School District No. 37 (Delta), and the neighbourhood takes its search name from the school's catchment rather than from a City of Delta plan name. https://le.deltasd.bc.ca/our-school/about-our-school/
- Ladner Village and its Chisholm Street and Elliott Street wharves on the river are the area's commercial edge to the north-west; Ladner Trunk Road (Highway 10) and Harvest Drive are the main road links east. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-ladner-village-revitalization-projects

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock around the school is ground-oriented Ladner housing: single-detached houses on the townsite grid, with townhouses and low-rise apartments on the busier edges — a City report for 4501 Arthur Drive, in this district, describes townhouses and low-rise apartments north of 45 Avenue beside the detached streets. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/9117/8363/3826/Rezoning_Development_Variance_Permit_and_Heritage_Alteration_Permit_for_Townhouses_at_4501_Arthur_Drive.pdf
- Arthur Drive carries registered heritage houses: the 4501 Arthur Drive site holds the Westholme – David McKee Residence, a heritage dwelling on a 3,291-square-metre lot, inside Ladner Heritage Conservation Area 2 (LHCA-2 Arthur Drive). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/7417/8164/3520/New_App_Report_9952.pdf
- The school the area is named for is a dual-track Kindergarten to Grade 7 school of about 590 students, with roughly half in French Immersion — the building runs two complete programs, English and French, under one roof. https://le.deltasd.bc.ca/our-school/about-our-school/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Delta's Zoning Bylaw has been updated for Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH): up to 4 dwelling units per lot are permitted through most of the city (6 near rapid transit on Scott Road, which does not apply in Ladner), using combinations of a single-detached house or duplex, a secondary suite, and garden suites or coach houses. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/5217/7676/6835/91f9a7bfa59936d6e76ff00fb0d988ac_SSMUH_Brochure_28Nov_202429.pdf
- Garden suites and coach houses must sit at least 1.5 metres from side and rear property lines and from the main building, behind the principal dwelling, and each is allowed up to 42 square metres (about 452 square feet) of additional floor area. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/5217/7676/6835/91f9a7bfa59936d6e76ff00fb0d988ac_SSMUH_Brochure_28Nov_202429.pdf
- Impermeable surfaces (buildings, patios, driveways) are still capped at 60% of the lot under Delta's SSMUH rules, and lot coverage rules are unchanged — infill here is a unit-count change, not a coverage change. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/5217/7676/6835/91f9a7bfa59936d6e76ff00fb0d988ac_SSMUH_Brochure_28Nov_202429.pdf
- On Arthur Drive, the Ladner Heritage Conservation Area 2 (LHCA-2) guidelines apply: demolishing the registered heritage house at 4501 Arthur Drive requires a Heritage Alteration Permit, not just a demolition permit. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/7417/8164/3520/New_App_Report_9952.pdf
- East Ladner infill runs through named development permit areas: the 60B Street townhouse application required a Development Permit for form and character in the Ladner East-Urban (LV4) Development Permit Area. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/4617/7676/7348/3ff51f67a63aa5b3830e9b533891cc59_LU009770_Resident20Notice20V4.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Ladner Elementary a French Immersion school? **A:** It is dual-track: the school runs a complete English program and a complete French Immersion program from Kindergarten to Grade 7 in the same building, with about 590 students and roughly 50% in French Immersion. For French Immersion, École Ladner Elementary is the attendance-area school for families in the Hawthorne, Holly, Ladner, Port Guichon and Neilson Grove catchments. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/french-immersion-applications/early-french-immersion/
- **Q:** Can I add a suite or a coach house to a house here? **A:** Delta's updated zoning permits up to 4 units on most lots, and a garden suite or coach house is one of the allowed forms: it must sit behind the main house, at least 1.5 metres from the lot lines and the main building, with up to 42 square metres of bonus floor area. The lot's total unit count and the 60% impermeable-surface cap still apply. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/5217/7676/6835/91f9a7bfa59936d6e76ff00fb0d988ac_SSMUH_Brochure_28Nov_202429.pdf
- **Q:** Is this area behind the dike, and does that affect building? **A:** Ladner sits on low-lying delta land protected by Delta's dike system, and the City's flood work is ongoing: the River Road West dike in Ladner (the Port Guichon side) is being raised in phases toward a 4.1-metre level intended to address sea level rise to 2050. Flood construction requirements are checked lot by lot through the City's mapping when you build or add on. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/185358/
- **Q:** What is happening in Ladner Village? **A:** The City is mid-way through a multi-year Ladner Village Revitalization plan: Council approved the detailed design for the Chisholm Street Wharf and construction was under way in 2026 with completion expected by late spring 2026, a one-way concept is being consulted on for Chisholm Street, and the Bridge Street Pop-Up Park is relocating to the City-owned site at Delta Street and Chisholm Street. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-ladner-village-revitalization-projects
- **Q:** Which secondary school follows Ladner Elementary? **A:** Delta Secondary School is Ladner's secondary school (Grades 8–12) in School District 37; Ladner Elementary feeds the Ladner family of schools, but catchment is assigned by home address, so confirm the pairing for the exact street in the district's school locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/delta

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The school at 5016 44 Avenue is one of only a few Delta elementaries running both English and French Immersion tracks, which is why its catchment matters to buyers well beyond the surrounding blocks. https://le.deltasd.bc.ca/our-school/about-our-school/
- Many properties along River Road West, on the Port Guichon edge of Ladner, sit OUTSIDE Delta's dike system and are shallow lots dating to the late 1800s — the City's dike report singles this strip out from the rest of Ladner. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/185358/
- The City ran a Ladner Village Market and a pop-up park as part of the village revitalization, and the village brand theme chosen by the City is "Shaped by the River." http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-ladner-village-revitalization-projects
- The 4501 Arthur Drive townhouse application includes a condition worth knowing on heritage streets: the existing house must first be offered for relocation at the owner's cost for $1.00 for 120 days before demolition. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/9117/8363/3826/Rezoning_Development_Variance_Permit_and_Heritage_Alteration_Permit_for_Townhouses_at_4501_Arthur_Drive.pdf
- Delta collects property tax separately from utilities, and its tax due date is July 2, with a 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta's property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on balances outstanding after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026; the approved 2026 Financial Plan included a 2.9% property tax increase for City services. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- Delta sets its rates each year by bylaw — for 2025 the rate bylaws were Bylaw No. 8500 (municipal), No. 8501 (Metro Vancouver Regional District) and No. 8502 (Tsawwassen Business Improvement Association), adopted April 2025. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/238234/
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies in Delta: Delta is a Metro Vancouver municipality on the Province's taxable-areas list, so residential owners file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Ladner Exchange is the local transit hub: TransLink's South Delta route group serving Ladner includes the 601 (South Delta / Bridgeport Station), the 606 (Ladner Ring), the 616 (Ladner South / Ladner Exchange), the 618 (Ladner North) and the 310 (Scottsdale / Ladner). https://www.transit.land/explore/operators/o-c28-translink
- The 601–604 group runs from South Delta through Ladner to Bridgeport Station in Richmond via Highway 99; TransLink's timetable states that toward Bridgeport, buses make all local stops up to Highway 17A at Ladner Trunk Road and at Massey Exchange (Highway 99 at Steveston Highway), and that 602, 603 and 604 trips stop at Massey Exchange only on request, for drop-off only. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Dike and sea level rise is the long-term risk file: the City states Delta's coastal dikes stand at about 3.5 metres and do not meet the Province's seismic guidelines, and its flood strategy raises dike sections — including the Boundary Bay dike in east Ladner — toward the 2050 flood design level of 4.1 metres. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/200879/
- Check whether a specific property sits inside or outside the dike system: the City's River Road West report states that many Port Guichon-area properties are located outside Delta's dike system, which changes the flood picture completely for those lots. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/185358/
- Heritage is a second check on the older streets: a house on Arthur Drive can be inside Ladner Heritage Conservation Area 2 (LHCA-2), where demolition or alteration needs a Heritage Alteration Permit and must follow the area's heritage guidelines. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/7417/8164/3520/New_App_Report_9952.pdf
- Use the City of Delta's DeltaMap tool to check a lot's zoning, flood construction level and development permit areas; what the public pages do not show is a specific home's past flood history or its geotechnical (preload/soil) report — those come from the seller's disclosure and the City building file.
