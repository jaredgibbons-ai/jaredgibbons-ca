# AREA — Surrey — Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer. Do not answer them here.

1. City-wide, Surrey deals: when a detached house and a condo in the same Surrey neighbourhood are both in front of you, what difference do you actually see in how the deals go — conditions, subjects, deposit behaviour, fall-throughs — in Whalley/City Centre versus Newton versus South Surrey?
2. Who buys where in Surrey: who is really buying the detached houses in Fleetwood, Newton and Panorama Ridge, and who is buying the Whalley tower condos and the South Surrey low-rise condos — first-time buyers, families moving within Surrey, downsizers, investors, newcomers — and has that mix changed in the last year?
3. What sells fast and what sits in Surrey: which house types and which condo buildings or pockets move quickly across the city, and which ones sit — by neighbourhood, by lot or building type — and what is the one reason you see most often when a Surrey listing stalls?
4. What surprises Surrey buyers most: what catches buyers off guard after they start looking — suite and coach-house rules, floodplain lots in Bridgeview or the lowlands, strata documents in older low-rise buildings, school catchments — and what do you wish every Surrey buyer checked before the first showing?
5. One Surrey story: tell one deal from anywhere in Surrey — a house or a condo — that taught you something about this city that no bylaw or listing sheet shows, with the street or building named if you can share it.
