# Panorama Ridge Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: panorama-ridge (Panorama Ridge, Surrey; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Panorama Ridge has no stand-alone City boundary; it sits inside Surrey's South Newton plan area, north of Highway 10, east of King George Boulevard and west of 152 Street. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- Its named commercial node is Panorama Heights, at 152 Street and Highway 10, at the area's southeast corner. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached houses are the base residential form here; the attached stock is a smaller, newer layer on the Colebrook and 152 Street edges — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note for Surrey-wide stock facts. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- The ridge's subdivision lots follow the R-series zones in Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000; the research records an R3 minimum subdivision lot area of 660 square metres in this plan area. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The South Newton neighbourhood plan no longer governs here; it was rescinded, so land use follows the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan and Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note for the bylaw detail. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- Creek-side lots carry riparian rules because Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries run through the plan area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools are in Panorama Ridge? **A:** Panorama Ridge Secondary, 13220 64 Avenue, and Colebrook Elementary, 5404 125A Street, are in the area, and McLeod Road Traditional, 6325 142 Street, also serves it. Catchment is by home address, not by neighbourhood name, so run the exact address in the School District No. 36 (Surrey) School Locator before relying on any school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** What plan governs a rebuild or addition here? **A:** The Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan and Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 govern, because the South Newton neighbourhood plan was rescinded in the Official Community Plan update. A seller or neighbour quoting South Newton plan densities is quoting a document that is no longer in effect, so check the current zoning for the lot instead. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Q:** Can a house here add units? **A:** Yes, within Surrey's small-scale multi-unit housing limits, which are stated in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note. The Panorama-specific point is servicing and creeks: Archibald Creek, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries cross this area, and a creek-side lot can lose building area to riparian setbacks before unit count is even considered. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Q:** What sits at the south edge of the ridge? **A:** Highway 10 and the Serpentine lowlands sit below the south edge, so south-edge lots face a highway corridor rather than houses. The plan area's creeks drain toward those lowlands, which is why drainage and riparian checks matter more on the slope and Colebrook Road side than on the flat top of the ridge. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The test address run in earlier research, 13220 64 Avenue, returned North Ridge Elementary and École Panorama Ridge in the district locator — not the elementary school physically closest to it — so catchment cannot be guessed from distance. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- No library or recreation centre inside the sub-area was confirmed; Newton Athletic Park, 7395 128 Street, west of the area, is the major sports park serving it. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks
- Surrey-Newton Urgent and Primary Care Centre, 6830 King George Boulevard, is the named urgent-care site serving this part of Newton. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Surrey/surrey-newton-urgent-and-primary-care-centre

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Buses on the 152 Street, 132 Street and 64 Avenue corridors link the ridge to Newton Exchange and Surrey Central; routes named in TransLink's Surrey schedule are 321, 322 and 324, and the R1 RapidBus runs on King George Boulevard west of the area. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2026-schedules/april/surrey-p170.pdf
- Highway 10 is the east–west route at the south edge, toward Langley and Cloverdale eastbound and Delta westbound. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check riparian setbacks first on any creek-side lot: Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries are fish-bearing, and setbacks can shrink the building envelope; the plan page names the creeks, not a lot's setback. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- Check whether a south-edge lot sits on the upland side of Highway 10 or toward the Serpentine lowlands before subjects, using City mapping for that lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
