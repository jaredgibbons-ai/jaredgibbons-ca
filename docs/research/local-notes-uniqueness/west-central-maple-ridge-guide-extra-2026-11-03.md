# West Central Area Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: west-central-maple-ridge-guide (West Central; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- West Central is Maple Ridge's Town Centre side: the City describes its Town Centre as walkable and vibrant with shops, services, entertainment and recreational activities. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The Town Centre's civic address anchor is Haney Place — City planning correspondence uses 11995 Haney Place, Maple Ridge, and the transit exchange is Haney Place Exchange. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-08/Planning-Application-Submission-Checklist-and-Details-2026-08-14%29-Fillable.pdf
- The Lougheed Transit Corridor reaches the Town Centre at 221 Street, following Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road from the city's western boundary — 221 Street is the corridor's stated Town Centre end. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- West Central's future stock is higher-density by plan: the updated Town Centre plan reinforces the core as a primary location for higher-density residential and mixed-use development, anchored by Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney West Coast Express station. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/invest-maple-ridge/development-opportunities
- The updated Town Centre boundary was expanded to include adjacent areas such as River Bend, and land-use designations were reorganised to clarify allowable development types and densities. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/invest-maple-ridge/development-opportunities

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- West Central development is checked against Town Centre Development Permit Area guidelines, which the City's application checklist splits into precincts including Civic Core, Downtown West, Downtown East, South of Lougheed, Port Haney, and North View and South View. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-08/Planning-Application-Submission-Checklist-and-Details-2026-08-14%29-Fillable.pdf
- The Lougheed Transit Corridor Development Permit Area also appears on the City's checklist — a West Central lot on Lougheed Highway or Dewdney Trunk Road can carry both Town Centre and corridor design review. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-08/Planning-Application-Submission-Checklist-and-Details-2026-08-14%29-Fillable.pdf
- A secondary suite in a West Central house follows the city-wide rule: one per lot, inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of total Gross Floor Area, and not in the Floodplain Area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What changed in the Town Centre plan in 2025? **A:** The City updated the Town Centre Area Plan as part of its 2025 Official Community Plan and area plan revisions to align with provincial housing requirements. The City says the updated plan expands the Town Centre boundary to include areas such as River Bend and reinforces the core for higher-density residential and mixed-use development around its transit hubs. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/invest-maple-ridge/development-opportunities
- **Q:** Which trains and buses serve West Central? **A:** West Central is anchored by two transit hubs the City itself names: Haney Transit Exchange for buses including the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus, and Port Haney West Coast Express station for commuter rail. Maple Meadows Station at 20010 Dunn Avenue, with 435 Park and Ride spaces, is the larger Park and Ride west of the core. https://www.translink.ca/parkandride?utm_source=direct&utm_medium=shorturl&utm_campaign=parkandride
- **Q:** What schools are in or near West Central? **A:** School District 42's provincial list gives Maple Ridge Elementary (Kindergarten to grade 7) at 20820 River Road and Maple Ridge Secondary (grades 8–12) at 21911 122 Avenue, with Westview Secondary at 20905 Wicklund Avenue. Confirm the catchment for the exact West Central address with SD42, as catchments are address-specific. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- **Q:** Does the Lougheed corridor plan rezone my West Central lot? **A:** No — the City states the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan is a long-term planning document that does not result in immediate impacts on current zoning and does not approve construction. It sets land-use designations, policies and Development Permit Area guidelines that future applications must follow. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Haney Place Exchange opened August 27, 2008 and is described as the easternmost major transit exchange in Metro Vancouver — West Central buyers are at the end of the R3 line, not mid-route. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/city-council/maple-ridge-moves
- The Town Centre plan update was done as part of the City's 2025 Official Community Plan revisions — a buyer reading an older Town Centre plan should check for the 2025 version. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/invest-maple-ridge/development-opportunities
- Maple Ridge Secondary at 21911 122 Avenue is the district's French Immersion secondary noted on the provincial district list context — confirm programme entry with SD42. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- Port Haney's rail crossing is a planned capital item: the City's capital plan lists a Port Haney Canadian Pacific Kansas City (CPKC) Crossing Upgrade tied to a future Port Haney Waterfront Park access. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/Attachment-A-Updated-2026-2030-Capital-Plan-1%29.pdf
- The 203 Street and Maple Crescent rail crossing is also a priority capital project to meet Transport Canada railway clearance rules — relevant to west-side West Central traffic. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/Attachment-A-Updated-2026-2030-Capital-Plan-1%29.pdf

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- The Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was adopted January 27, 2026 (Official Community Plan Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021), with direction for staff to begin accepting pre-applications and development inquiries in the corridor. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP
- The plan directs growth around four future Bus Rapid Transit stations on Lougheed Highway at 203 Street, 207 Street, Laity Street and 216 Street, as part of TransLink's Langley–Haney Place Bus Rapid Transit line. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- The updated Town Centre Area Plan (2025 Official Community Plan revisions) expanded the Town Centre boundary to include River Bend — the city-major plan change that most affects West Central land. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/invest-maple-ridge/development-opportunities

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus runs between Coquitlam Central Station and Haney Place Exchange along Lougheed Highway — West Central is at its eastern terminus. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/city-council/maple-ridge-moves
- The future Langley–Haney Place Bus Rapid Transit line will be 22 kilometres with 13 stations, connecting Willowbrook in Langley with Haney Place via 200 Street, the Golden Ears Bridge and Lougheed Highway, with peak service every 10 minutes on TransLink's page. https://www.translink.ca/brt?utm_source=direct&utm_medium=shorturl&utm_campaign=brt&_gl=1%2Ajci4xc%2A_ga%2AMTQwNDM4NTgyMi4xNzIyMzczNTEz%2A_ga_QGWL01WSZ6%2AMTcyMjM3MzUxMi4xLjAuMTcyMjM3MzUyMC41Mi4wLjA.
- West Coast Express serving West Central is via Port Haney station in the Town Centre and Maple Meadows Station at 20010 Dunn Avenue (435 spaces, 4 a.m. to 2 a.m.). https://www.translink.ca/parkandride?utm_source=direct&utm_medium=shorturl&utm_campaign=parkandride

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- South West Central lots near the Fraser River need a flood check: the City's floodplain work sets Flood Construction Levels and setbacks from rivers and creeks for areas at risk, with Fraser River mapping in the first phase. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/Floodplain-Bylaw-Open-House-36-x-48-in%29.pdf
- The City monitors the Fraser River and inspects dikes and pump stations during high streamflow events — Port Haney and River Road properties should read the City's flood page before buying. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/high-streamflow-advisory-1
- Rail noise is a West Central due-diligence item: the Town Centre sits by the Canadian Pacific Kansas City railway, and the City's capital plan funds crossing upgrades at Port Haney and at 203 Street and Maple Crescent. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/Attachment-A-Updated-2026-2030-Capital-Plan-1%29.pdf
