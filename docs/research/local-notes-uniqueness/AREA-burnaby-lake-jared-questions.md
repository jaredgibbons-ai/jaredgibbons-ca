# Burnaby Lake — Questions for Jared

1. In Burnaby Lake area deals, what do you see differ between the detached houses south of the lake around Lakefield Drive and Kensington Avenue and the houses and townhomes on the north shore near Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station and Winston Street?
2. Who buys around Burnaby Lake: families targeting Sperling Elementary and Burnaby Central Secondary, sports families living at the Burnaby Lake Sports Complex, or Millennium Line commuters — and what do they say tipped it?
3. What sells fast here and what sits: do streets with direct trail access into Burnaby Lake Regional Park move first, while houses backing onto Highway 1 or the Winston Street industrial side wait?
4. What surprises Burnaby Lake buyers: that the lake is a nature park, not a swimming lake, Highway 1 noise across the water, or Still Creek low spots and what they mean for a specific lot?
5. Tell one Burnaby Lake story: a deal on Sperling Avenue or Lakefield Drive where the lake access, the station, or the sports complex changed the price, the subjects, or the outcome.
