# Surrey Lots and Land — Extra Local Notes
Page: surrey-lots-land (Surrey; lots and land).
## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- This page is City of Surrey-wide land, and Surrey is its own municipality with its own zoning bylaw, separate from Langley Township to the east. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Surrey's land splits into upland lots and lowland floodplain: the Serpentine and Nicomekl lowlands cover 59 square kilometres (km²) of a 334 km² catchment, as the City states. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- A land buyer's status check is the City's Online Development Inquiry, which needs the property address, development project number or building permit number. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/development-building-inquiries

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The land stock here is serviced urban lots, assembly parcels and rural Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) pockets under one city-wide search, not one subdivision. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- No new named bare-land subdivision or bare-land strata was verified for Surrey in this pass; the earlier program's one named fee-simple subdivision, Whitby Estates, is documented in the lots program and is not restated as new stock here. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/development-building-inquiries
- Lot size drives the zone label after the 2024 rezoning: for example RA (Acreage Residential Zone) covers lots over 4,050 square metres (m²) in the City's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) table. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- This note owns Surrey land facts in full, as no earlier Surrey land page exists: zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, amended for SSMUH on July 8, 2024. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- The 2024 changes replaced older zones — RA became Acreage Residential, RH (Half-Acre) became R1 Suburban Residential, and RF/RF-SS became R3 Urban Residential — so an old zone name on a document must be translated. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Over 4,600 Comprehensive Development (CD) lots keep their site-specific CD regulations; added SSMUH permissions apply by lot size under Part 52 of the zoning bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Coach houses and garden suites are 35 m² minimum and 75 m² maximum, excluded from floor area ratio (FAR), rising to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots where the main house is below maximum FAR. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- In the ALR, the Agricultural Land Commission caps a principal residence at 500 m² and requires a suite to be inside that residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** When are Development Cost Charges (DCCs) actually paid on a Surrey lot? **A:** At subdivision approval or building permit issuance, not before approvals. The City's 2025 DCC report states the bylaw was amended so a DCC must be paid at approval of the subdivision or issue of the building permit, and developers may defer 75% until occupancy or within four years, whichever comes first. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R173.pdf
- **Q:** Does a garden suite or coach house attract its own DCC? **A:** Yes, as an additional unit, with caps. The City states the first SSMUH residential unit is exempt because DCCs were paid at original subdivision, additional units are charged by square footage at a rate comparable to townhouses, and garden and coach house DCCs are capped at 800 square feet. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R173.pdf
- **Q:** How much is the DCC example the City itself publishes? **A:** The City's worked examples in its July 28, 2025 report put a duplex at $31,517.50 (down from $49,480.00 in the March bylaw) and a garden or coach house at $9,104 — City portion only, before Metro Vancouver and TransLink charges, and rates change by bylaw, so confirm the current schedule for your lot. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R173.pdf
- **Q:** Can a large Surrey lot use SSMUH for 4–6 units? **A:** No if it is over 4,050 m². Surrey states SSMUH applies to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary; 3 units are possible under 280 m², 4 units over 280 m², and 6 units only on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 metres of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q:** Are farm buildings charged DCCs like a house? **A:** No — the City proposes a different treatment. Its 2025 report states agricultural properties are charged similar to single-family without the sanitary component because agricultural lands are not serviced, with reductions in Collector Road and Parkland DCCs, plus a square-footage rate for ancillary buildings. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R173.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- About 13,000 eligible Surrey lots (around 18% of SSMUH lots) sit within frequent bus stop areas with 15-minute service, where up to 6 units and no off-street parking may apply. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- A coach house must be attached to a garage, and smaller R3–R6 lots need lane access for one; without a lane, a garden suite may be the route instead. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- A coach house or garden suite needs one extra parking space beyond the spaces required for the house, duplex and any secondary suite. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- Existing restrictive covenants under section 219 of the Land Title Act still apply and take precedence over SSMUH permissions, as the City states. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- SSMUH does not retroactively legalize a suite or coach house built without permits; owners must still go through permit and approval channels. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey's 2026 total residential (Class 1) tax rate is $3.42731 per $1,000 of assessed value, of which the Municipal Total is $1.84251 per $1,000. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- Farm (Class 9) is taxed differently in the same 2026 schedule: Municipal Total $4.48750 per $1,000 and Total tax rate $12.57740 per $1,000 — a land buyer must use the class that applies to the parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- A Capital Parcel Tax of $300 is collected with property taxes for Class 1, 8 and 9 properties in 2026, and drainage parcel taxes are also collected with property taxes. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- Property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty after midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) taxable-area list includes the City of Surrey. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- DCCs are the land-specific charge: see section 4 for the City's duplex and garden/coach house examples and the payment-timing rule. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R173.pdf

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Frequent bus stop areas are a planning fact, not just transit: lots within 400 metres of a stop with 15-minute service can qualify for 6 SSMUH units. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- To check a project's servicing and permit status, the City's Online Development Inquiry shows rezoning, subdivision, development permit, engineering and building permit details by address or project number. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/development-building-inquiries

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Lowland land is near sea level because it was historically an intertidal zone, and both the Serpentine and Nicomekl are controlled by sea dams — flood construction is a lot-by-lot check. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- The City has built dykes, pump stations and conveyance improvements in the agricultural lowlands since its 1997 Lowland Flood Control Strategic Plan, and states flooding has been significantly reduced — not eliminated. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- Hazard lands can be exempt from SSMUH, and Steep Slope, Flood Plain, Sensitive Ecosystem and Farm Protection Development Permit Areas may require a development permit. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- At application, the City requires the lot's own sewer, water and drainage servicing capacity to be considered for additional housing. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
