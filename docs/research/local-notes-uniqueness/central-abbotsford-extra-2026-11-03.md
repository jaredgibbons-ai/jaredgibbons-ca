# Central Abbotsford Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: central-abbotsford (Central Abbotsford, Abbotsford; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- At street level, Central Abbotsford is the Bevan Avenue school corridor and the streets around Mill Lake and South Fraser Way. https://godson.abbyschools.ca/
- The neighbourhood sits inside the City Centre and Historic Downtown plan areas described in the central-abbotsford-guide note; see that note for the plan boundary lines. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- People call this ground both Central Abbotsford and Downtown Abbotsford, and also name it by Mill Lake and Sevenoaks. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/HDNP%2BCommercial%2BMarket%2BStudy.pdf
- Matsqui Recreation Centre at 3106 Clearbrook Road sits at the west edge of the central area. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached streets here include infill lots created under the RS7 zone. RS7 allows new interior lots down to 300 m², smaller than the RS3 minimum of 540 m² and the RS5 minimum of 420 m². https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58178/
- The RS3-i (Urban Residential Zone, Infill) zone is named in City council material for Maple Street in the Historic Downtown plan area. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58178/
- Attached housing concentrates around South Fraser Way and Gladwin Road, including Mahogany at Mill Lake at 2180 Gladwin Road; see the central-abbotsford-guide note for its full entry. https://www.ventanaconstruction.com/multi-family-residential/mahogany-at-mill-lake?itemId=l75nwgoa191q6nwa2steub8dcjgrvn
- Detached homes are freehold, while condos and townhomes are strata under the Strata Property Act. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary schools feed Abbotsford Middle here? **A:** Godson and Dormick Park are among the feeder schools. Abbotsford Middle states feeder students living in its catchment are automatically registered for September, and its family groups with Abbotsford Senior Secondary, so the Bevan corridor chain is consistent across both test addresses run. https://abbymiddle.abbyschools.ca/node/10331
- **Q:** How small can an infill lot be on these streets? **A:** New RS7 interior lots can go down to 300 m². City material states a 10.0 m minimum width and 22.5 m minimum depth for RS7, which is materially smaller than older RS3 lots, so frontage and depth must both be measured on the specific lot. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58178/
- **Q:** Is Mill Lake the recreation centre of this neighbourhood? **A:** Mill Lake Park is the central green space, with access from Bevan Avenue and Emerson Street. Centennial Outdoor Pool at 2310 Emerson Street sits in the Mill Lake area, and Matsqui Recreation Centre at 3106 Clearbrook Road serves the west edge. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds
- **Q:** Where is the hospital from these streets? **A:** Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre is at 32900 Marshall Road, east of the central area. The closer walk-in option inside the area is the Abbotsford Urgent and Primary Care Centre at Unit 100, 2692 Clearbrook Road. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Abbotsford Middle and Abbotsford Senior Secondary sit on the same Bevan Avenue corridor as Godson and Dormick Park elementaries. Godson is at 33130 Bevan Avenue and Abbotsford Senior Secondary at 33355 Bevan Avenue. https://godson.abbyschools.ca/
- The Abbotsford library branch shares the 33355 Bevan Avenue address with Abbotsford Senior Secondary. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/
- Sevenoaks Shopping Centre on South Fraser Way is named in the City study as the City Centre's major retail centre, with West Oaks Mall and Clearbrook Town Square also named as City Centre malls. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/HDNP%2BCommercial%2BMarket%2BStudy.pdf
- Willband Creek runs through the Historic Downtown and central area, so creek-adjacent lots need a parcel check even away from any floodplain name. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- Bourquin Exchange, the city's central bus exchange, sits inside this neighbourhood's street pattern. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
