# Clayton Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: clayton-cloverdale-houses-for-sale (Clayton (Clayton Heights), Surrey; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Clayton Heights is a roughly 809-hectare plan area from Fraser Highway in the south to the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) in the north and west, with the Township of Langley on its east side, under the City of Surrey's Clayton General Land Use Plan (GLUP), completed in 1999. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/clayton-general-land-use-plan
- East Clayton is the south-eastern piece, about 314 hectares north of Fraser Highway and west of the Langley border, split into four plan areas: the main East Clayton Neighbourhood Concept Plan (NCP), extensions west of 188 Street and north of 72 Avenue, and a transit-oriented area on Fraser Highway. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton
- West Clayton is the second piece, about 288 hectares north of Fraser Highway and east of the ALR, bordered by the East Clayton NCP to the east, with a future North Clayton NCP to the northeast. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/west-clayton-ncp
- 188 Street is the line the extensions are named against, because the East Clayton NCP Extension West of 188 Street was approved as a Stage 1 and Stage 2 report on April 4, 2005. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/RPT_2005-RC003.pdf
- 64 Avenue is the southern drainage edge, not just a street: the East Clayton servicing report states Catchment A drains south to discharge near 68 Avenue and 64 Avenue and on to Logan Creek, and is tributary to the Nicomekl River. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r156-east-clayton-neighbourhood

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Clayton's houses were planned as compact cluster and ground-oriented forms, not large-lot subdivisions. The West Clayton Stage 2 concept lists Suburban Residential, Urban Transition, Low, Medium and High Density Cluster categories, plus Townhouse Residential at 22 units per acre plus a 5-unit bonus. http://surrey.ca/files/West_Clayton_Stage2_Land_Use_Concept_FINAL.pdf
- Lot siting varies by watercourse, because the same West Clayton legend sets riparian setbacks at 30 metres and 15 metres from the centrelines of Class A and Class B watercourses, including Clayton Creek and North Creek. http://surrey.ca/files/West_Clayton_Stage2_Land_Use_Concept_FINAL.pdf
- East Clayton was the first urban neighbourhood developed in Clayton Heights, north of Fraser Highway along the Langley border, and its four plan areas went through separate NCP processes, so house form changes between the main NCP and the west and north extensions. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton
- Aloha Estates is a separate infill area inside the Clayton Heights study area, listed by the City alongside the East and West Clayton NCPs under the 1999 GLUP, so an Aloha Estates house was not built under those NCP rules. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/clayton-general-land-use-plan

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The biggest current change is that the East Clayton designations no longer govern on their own: the City states the East Clayton NCP, its west and north extensions and the transit-oriented area were rescinded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) Update and are no longer in effect, and development there is led by the OCP. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton
- West Clayton carries the same change in softer form: the City states its land use designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP Update, land use requirements are guided by the OCP, and West Clayton continues to guide public amenities and infrastructure. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/west-clayton-ncp
- Fraser Highway houses face a newer plan layer, because the Clayton Corridor Plan covers parts of East and West Clayton and North Cloverdale around future SkyTrain stations at 184 Street and 190 Street, with a new village, Clayton Centre, at the 190 Street station and Hillcrest Village at the 184 Street station. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/city-asks-surrey-residents-provide-feedback-draft-clayton-corridor-plan
- The west edge of West Clayton is an ALR edge with a stated buffer treatment, because the West Clayton Stage 2 concept shows an ALR buffer and landscape setback along that boundary, which a buyer should confirm on plan documents rather than from the fence line. http://surrey.ca/files/West_Clayton_Stage2_Land_Use_Concept_FINAL.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Which plan actually governs a house in East Clayton now — the NCP or the OCP?
**A:** The Official Community Plan governs, because the City states the East Clayton Neighbourhood Concept Plan and its extensions were rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP Update and are no longer in effect. The NCP documents still explain how the streets and lots were laid out, but what can be built today is read from the parcel's OCP designation. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton

**Q:** Which secondary school serves a house on the Hazelgrove side of Clayton?
**A:** Clayton Heights Secondary at 7003 188 Street is the secondary school whose catchment, in School District No. 36 (Surrey)'s Cloverdale/Clayton boundary document, consists of the Hazelgrove, Katzie, Latimer Road and Sunrise Ridge elementary areas. The same document assigns the Clayton, Port Kells, Adams Road and Hillcrest elementary areas to École Salish Secondary, so the exact address decides between the two. https://placespeak.com/uploads/1484/Cloverdale-Clayton_Catchment_Boundaries_2018-19.pdf

**Q:** Is there an elementary school inside Clayton itself?
**A:** Yes, Hazelgrove Elementary is a Clayton school in School District No. 36 (Surrey), listed by the Province at 7057 191 Street, Surrey. Hazelgrove is also one of the four elementary catchments feeding Clayton Heights Secondary at 7003 188 Street, subject to the district's current boundary map. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/surrey

**Q:** Will the SkyTrain change the Fraser Highway end of Clayton?
**A:** Yes, on the corridor the City is planning around two stations, because the Clayton Corridor Plan covers Fraser Highway in East and West Clayton and North Cloverdale around future stations at 184 Street and 190 Street. The City states Clayton Centre will form at the 190 Street station and Hillcrest Village at the 184 Street station. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/city-asks-surrey-residents-provide-feedback-draft-clayton-corridor-plan

**Q:** How do I confirm the catchment school for a specific Clayton house?
**A:** Use the Surrey Schools School Locator tool for the exact address, because the district states catchment is determined by address and where it falls within the district's School Boundaries Map. Clayton is the clearest case for checking, since its elementary areas feed two different secondary schools — Clayton Heights Secondary and École Salish Secondary — under the district's Cloverdale/Clayton boundary document. https://www.surreyschools.ca/chs/registration-1

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Clayton Community Centre at 7155 187A Street is Clayton's combined recreation, arts and library building, officially opened October 16, 2021, described by the City as a 75,000-square-foot fully accessible facility with a gymnasium, fitness centre, studios, teaching kitchen and preschool space. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/city-of-surrey-officially-opens-clayton-community-centre-4
- The Surrey Libraries Clayton Branch sits inside that same 7155 187A Street building as a full-service 14,000-square-foot library, and the City states it was the tenth branch added to the Surrey Libraries network. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/city-of-surrey-officially-opens-clayton-community-centre-4
- The community centre is within a 10-minute walk of two high schools, according to the City's facility award release, and Clayton Heights Secondary itself is at 7003 188 Street. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/city-of-surrey-wins-bcrpa-facility-excellence-award-clayton-community-centre
- North Clayton is still the unbuilt third of the plan area: a 2018 City report states the Clayton GLUP area was about 39 percent built out at the end of 2017, East Clayton was nearing full build-out at 95 percent, and North Clayton held about one third of the developable land. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/RPT_2018-R183.pdf

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Today Fraser Highway buses are Clayton's transit, because TransLink's Langley transit priorities work focuses bus speed and reliability on Fraser Highway routes 502 and 503 while SkyTrain is extended on the same corridor, with the 503 redesigned as high-frequency, limited-stop service between Surrey Central and Langley Centre. https://translinklistens.ca/langley-transit-priorities
- The future stations are named by street: the City states the Clayton Corridor will have two future SkyTrain stations at 184 Street and 190 Street on Fraser Highway, so a house's station is decided by which of those intersections it is closer to. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/city-asks-surrey-residents-provide-feedback-draft-clayton-corridor-plan
- Construction is a current condition on Fraser Highway east of Highway 15, where the project states segment delivery brings temporary access changes for roads, intersections, sidewalks and driveways while the gantry works above. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/construction-update-extended-hours-fraser-highway-east-of-highway-15/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the rescission, because a plan search that stops at the NCP gives the wrong answer today: the City states the East Clayton plans were rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP Update and development is led by the OCP, so confirm the current OCP designation and zoning for the exact parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton
- Check which drainage catchment the house sits in, because the servicing report splits East Clayton into Catchment A, tributary to the Nicomekl River, and Catchment B, draining to North Cloverdale Creek and the Serpentine River via the Fry's Corner pump station. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r156-east-clayton-neighbourhood
- Near Fraser Highway, check the Clayton Corridor Plan boundary as well, because the corridor plan overlaps portions of the East Clayton NCP, the East Clayton Transit Oriented Area Plan, the West Clayton NCP and the North Cloverdale plans near the 184 Street and 190 Street stations. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/city-asks-surrey-residents-provide-feedback-draft-clayton-corridor-plan
