# Sardis East Vedder Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: sardis-east-vedder (Sardis East Vedder; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sardis East Vedder is the part of Sardis east of Vedder Road, and the City publishes no boundary for the sub-area name — it sits inside the Schedule B5 Sardis Neighbourhood Plan area of Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- Vedder Road is the west edge, and BC Transit's east-side timing points sit on it at Britton Avenue and at Promontory Road. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=54
- Stevenson Road is the main east–west street, with the Wiltshire Street at Stevenson Road bus timing point beside the Sardis Park area and Sardis Secondary at 45460 Stevenson Rd. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=54
- The south edge is the Vedder River, where Vedder Park is kilometre 0 of the City's Vedder Rotary Loop Trail and the Vedder Bridge crosses back to the north bank. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754.&dowhat=locationView&plID=167
- The ground rises east to Promontory, where the bus timing points change to Teskey Way and Sylvan Drive on Promontory Road — the hillside edge covered in the sardis-east-vedder-guide note. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=54

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- One accessory dwelling unit per lot is the cap under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 — the full suite, coach house and small-scale multi-unit rules are in the sardis-houses-for-sale note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools are actually on the east side of Vedder Road? **A:** Six district schools are confirmed on this side by name and address in the BC Ministry file: Sardis Elementary, Sardis Secondary, Vedder Middle School and Stitó:s Lá:lém Totí:lt on the flat side, plus Vedder Elementary and Promontory Heights Community Elementary on Promontory. Catchments are set by street address, not by this list, so run the exact address in the SD33 School Locator before relying on any one school. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q:** Is the Promontory bus a real service or a shuttle? **A:** It is a full BC Transit loop. Route 54 runs between Cottonwood Mall and Promontory roughly hourly from early morning to late evening, using timing points at Britton Avenue at Vedder Road, Wiltshire Street at Stevenson Road, Promontory Road at Vedder Road, Teskey Way and Sylvan Drive, so both the flat Sardis Park streets and the Promontory hillside are on the same loop. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=54
- **Q:** Can I put a suite in the basement of a house near the river? **A:** Not below the Flood Construction Level. The City's small-scale housing guide states new secondary suites are not permitted below that level, and on alluvial fan lots a Qualified Professional's site-specific assessment sets the building setback and elevation first, with an engineered concrete foundation at least 1.0 m above grade. Check the lot's flood layer before pricing any suite income. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- **Q:** Where does Sardis East Vedder end and Garrison Crossing begin? **A:** There is no official line, because the City does not publish a boundary for the sub-area name. The physical break is the Vedder River at the Vedder Bridge, and the plan frame changes at the same time: this side is Schedule B5 Sardis, while the ground south toward the river and Garrison Crossing falls under the Schedule B6 South Vedder plan area. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Q:** What changed at Sardis Secondary? **A:** The school grew by 400 seats. School District 33 states the two-storey mass timber addition was completed on schedule in August 2026, raising capacity to 1,600 students, and that the added space let the district remove 11 portable classrooms from the site and relocate them to other schools. https://sd33.bc.ca/news/sardis-celebrate-70th-anniversary-new-addition

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Vedder Rotary Loop Trail starts at your south edge: the City marks kilometre 0 at Vedder Park, and the south-bank Vedder Rotary Trail South runs 5.8 km from Wilson Road back to the Vedder Bridge. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754.&dowhat=locationView&plID=167
- Promontory is a designated Wildlife Issue Area, so garbage carts may not go to the curb before 5:00 a.m. on collection day and wildlife-resistant carts are required from May 1, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2936
- The Sardis Secondary addition added a new bus and drop-off loop and staff parking south of the school, which changes traffic patterns on the Stevenson Road frontage at school hours. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/administration/capital/major-capital-projects/sardis-secondary-school

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the lot against the alluvial fan rules first: fan lots need a site-specific Qualified Professional assessment for setback and elevation before building, under the Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519 guide. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- River-adjacent lots carry a 75-metre setback from the natural boundary of the Vedder River in the City's floodplain materials, and hillside lots on Promontory need a geotechnical report with a Development Permit Area 2 application — both in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999 https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
