## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A self-managed eight-home strata like Willow Creek still owes the same Form B package on a resale as a large complex; small size changes who prepares it, not whether it exists. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt from the five-year depreciation-report rule; none of the named Pitt Meadows townhome complexes is that small on the figures read, and Willow Creek at eight homes is not exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Bonson by Mosaic the complex or the neighbourhood? **A:** Both names are in use, which causes search confusion in this city. Bonson is the Bonson Road corridor the City uses in names like South Bonson and the South Bonson Community Centre, while Bonson by Mosaic is one townhouse complex at 19696 Hammond Road built in 2021 in the city file. A listing that says only Bonson may be anywhere on the corridor, including Osprey Village at its south end. {Research file: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-townhomes-2026-11-01.md}
- **Q:** Are Pitt Meadows townhomes freehold or strata? **A:** The named complexes in the city file are strata purchases, including Meadowland Village, which listing records describe as freehold strata — meaning titled strata lots, not fee-simple row houses. Willow Creek is recorded as a self-managed strata and the Osprey Village complexes as strata complexes. A truly fee-simple townhome with no strata was not verified in Pitt Meadows in this program, so check title on any home marketed that way. {Research file: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-townhomes-2026-11-01.md}
- **Q:** Which townhome pocket is closest to the train? **A:** The Hammond Road pocket is closest, because 19696 Hammond Road sits just south of the CPKC line and Pitt Meadows Station is on Harris Road to its west. Osprey Village at the south end of Bonson Road relies on the 719/722 bus loop to reach either station. No measured walking time to a station is stated in a source read, so treat any minute figure in a listing as UNCONFIRMED. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain
- **Q:** What does a 1989 complex like Willow Creek mean for levies? **A:** Age is the point: a 1989 eight-home strata spreads any roof or envelope project across very few lots, so the depreciation report and contingency reserve fund balance matter more, not less, than in a large complex. British Columbia now requires stratas with five or more lots to renew a depreciation report every five years, and Willow Creek is inside that rule. Read both documents in the Form B package before subjects expire. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Hammond Road townhomes sit directly south of the CPKC rail line, so train noise is a Hammond Road question, not an Osprey Village one. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- The 719/722 loop is the only transit inside Osprey Village; Hammond Road has route 701 on the street itself. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain
