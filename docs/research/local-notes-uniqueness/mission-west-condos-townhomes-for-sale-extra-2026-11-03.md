# Mission West Condos and Townhomes — Extra Local Notes
Page: mission-west-condos-townhomes-for-sale (Mission West, Mission; condos and townhomes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Mission West is a board sub-area for the western side of the City of Mission: the Silverdale area and the rural fringe toward Stave Falls and Ruskin, on streets including Silverdale Avenue, Gunn Avenue, Nelson Street and Townshipline Avenue, all inside the City of Mission. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies
- The City's own name for the growth ground is the Silverdale Comprehensive Planning Area (SCPA), a 3,440-acre area sometimes known as Southwest Mission, designated in the Official Community Plan for urban growth. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies
- The SCPA is bordered by Silverdale Creek to the east, rural, agricultural and Kwantlen First Nations lands to the north, Silvermere Lake to the west and the Fraser River and Agricultural Land Reserve lands to the south. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/lan64c-silverdale-comprehensive-planning-area-neighbourhood-planning-terms-referencepdf
- Neighbourhood-shared facts for Mission West: see the mission-west-houses-for-sale note.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- There is no built townhome or condo roster to describe yet in Mission West: no named attached complex was confirmed on a municipal, developer or strata source in this pass, because the Silverdale ground is still in the neighbourhood-plan stage that must come before development. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/lan64c-silverdale-comprehensive-planning-area-neighbourhood-planning-terms-referencepdf

- The existing stock the plan will sit beside is rural: the City's Rural Mission Strategy covers the City's rural communities, naming Silverdale among them, building on the new Official Community Plan. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The West neighbourhood cannot jump the queue: the City's Silverdale terms of reference state Council will not consider initiation of neighbourhood planning for either the West or East neighbourhoods until 65% of the land area of the South Slopes of the Central Neighbourhood Plan is under application, or engineering services have been committed. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/lan64c-silverdale-comprehensive-planning-area-neighbourhood-planning-terms-referencepdf
- Rainwater rules are written before the homes are: the Silverdale Central Neighbourhood Plan's Integrated Rainwater Management Plan applies the City's Development and Subdivision Control Bylaw 5650-2017 goals, including drainage protection for life, livelihood and property and control of surface ponding. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/h1-irmp-master-report-app-g
- A townhouse, once built here, still cannot take a secondary suite: the City's secondary suites program states suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes anywhere in Mission. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Why are there so few townhomes for sale in Mission West?
**A:** Because most of the growth ground is still in planning, not construction. Mission West's urban growth area is the 3,440-acre Silverdale Comprehensive Planning Area, and the City requires a neighbourhood plan, with technical due diligence and a financial strategy for servicing, before development proceeds under it. The Central Neighbourhood Plan is the active plan area; the West and East neighbourhoods are gated behind its progress, so attached supply here is a future plan outcome. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/lan64c-silverdale-comprehensive-planning-area-neighbourhood-planning-terms-referencepdf

**Q:** Which elementary school is physically in Mission West?
**A:** Silverdale Elementary, 29715 Donatelli Avenue, Kindergarten to Grade 6, is the elementary school on the Silverdale side of Mission West. West Heights Elementary, 32065 Van Velzen Avenue, serves the city's west heights area. School District No. 75 catchments are address-based, and no district catchment lookup for a specific Mission West address is on record, so confirm the exact address with the district before relying on either school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075

**Q:** Which bus route serves the west side of Mission?
**A:** BC Transit route 32, named West Heights. Mission's local buses are BC Transit's Central Fraser Valley system, and its routes are 31 Valley Connector, 32 West Heights, 33 Cedar Valley, 34 East Side, 35 Hatzic and 39 Shopper Shuttle. The 31 Valley Connector is the intercity link to Abbotsford, and Mission City Station, on the east side of downtown, is where Mission West riders connect to the West Coast Express. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps

**Q:** Is Mission West inside the City of Mission for taxes and bylaws?
**A:** Yes. The Silverdale Comprehensive Planning Area is defined in the City of Mission's own Official Community Plan and plans material, and the board sub-area's streets carry Mission mailing addresses, so City of Mission zoning, subdivision and tax rules are the municipal frame, not a rural regional-district frame. City-wide Mission tax and utility facts are held in the mission-condos-for-sale note rather than repeated here. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies

**Q:** What should I check on a Silvermere Lake or acreage-edge attached property?
**A:** Shoreline and servicing, in writing. The Silverdale planning area is bordered by Silvermere Lake to the west and Agricultural Land Reserve lands to the south, and no official source read in this pass confirms dock, moorage or shoreline-tenure rights, or whether a specific Mission West parcel is on City water and sewer versus well and septic. Those are title and City servicing-map questions for the specific parcel, not listing statements. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/lan64c-silverdale-comprehensive-planning-area-neighbourhood-planning-terms-referencepdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Silverdale Elementary, 29715 Donatelli Avenue, is one of only three elementary schools the district's facilities plan identifies as serving Mission's rural areas, alongside Dewdney and Deroche elementaries in the east. https://media.mpsd.ca/media/Default/medialib/long-range-facility-plan-february-5-2019.03bc531128.pdf
- Mission Senior Secondary, 32939 7th Avenue, is the district's single Grades 10 to 12 school, so every Mission West student travels east across the city for senior secondary. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075
- Mission Memorial Hospital, 7324 Hurd Street, has a 25-bed medical inpatient unit, a 20-bed Patient Assessment and Transition Home unit and a 10-bed hospice, and provides primary acute care for the city. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/mission/mission-memorial-hospital
- The West Coast Express timetable effective January 5, 2026 runs five weekday trains from Mission City Station between 5:25 and 7:25 a.m., arriving at Waterfront Station 75 minutes later, with no weekend or statutory holiday service; station-to-door times for Mission West addresses are not stated by any official source. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- Homes outside Mission's waste collection area pay a $36.00 rural recycling fee on the tax notice instead of the garbage and recycling charges, $62.28 with a secondary dwelling. https://www.mission.ca/services/utility-bills

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve status parcel by parcel: the Silverdale planning area borders Agricultural Land Reserve lands to the south, and any parcel inside the reserve follows the Agricultural Land Commission's housing frame, with status confirmed on the Commission's mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Check rainwater and drainage designations before assuming a lot is buildable as shown: the Central Neighbourhood Plan's map layers include watercourses, biophysical constraints, designated environmentally sensitive areas and flood construction levels. https://esri.mission.ca/ags/rest/services/Geocortex/Planning/MapServer/4796
- Check servicing at the parcel, not the sub-area: whether a Mission West property is on City water and sewer versus well and septic was not confirmed for any parcel in this pass. https://www.mission.ca/services/water-sewer/water/water-meters
