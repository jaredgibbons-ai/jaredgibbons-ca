# South Meadows, Pitt Meadows Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: south-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide (South Meadows, City of Pitt Meadows; Guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- South Meadows is the board name for the South Bonson end of Bonson Road, and the City publishes no boundary for the sub-area. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- The working scope is Bonson Road south of Hammond Road and the Canadian Pacific Kansas City (CPKC) rail line, down to the Fraser River, including Fraser Way and Barnston View Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- The City itself calls this ground South Bonson: its community centre and art gallery are the South Bonson Community Centre at 10932 Barnston View Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- Mid Meadows and Osprey Village are north, Central Meadows is northwest across Harris Road, and Maple Ridge is east. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- South Meadows is governed city-wide by the "I See Pitt Meadows 2040" Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022 — city-wide Pitt Meadows detail is in the pitt-meadows-houses-for-sale note. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- Short-term rentals are prohibited in Pitt Meadows: short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in the Zoning Bylaw. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- A lot here can have one secondary suite at 33 to 90 square metres inside the house, or one garden suite at 33 to 90 square metres where the zone lists it, but never both on one lot. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- Residential buildings in the RS zone must be sited at least 15.0 metres from watercourses and key or constructed ditches. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 - Consolidated Version_0.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Why do trains block Harris Road, and will it change? **A:** Harris Road crosses the CPKC main line at grade, on one of the busiest rail segments in British Columbia. The City states trains block the Harris Road and Kennedy Road crossings up to 3.5 hours per day, and the Pitt Meadows Road and Rail Improvements Project would replace the Harris Road crossing with a four-lane underpass, with the Kennedy Road overpass work paused in favour of Harris Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/railways
**Q:** Is South Meadows behind a dike? **A:** Yes — the whole pocket sits behind the Fraser River dike in drainage Area 3. The City states over 95% of Pitt Meadows lies within the Fraser and Pitt River floodplain, the city is almost entirely protected by 60 kilometres of dikes with ditches, pump stations and flood boxes, and Shoreline Park at 51 Bonson Road sits on the dike itself. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
**Q:** Which elementary school serves a Bonson Road address? **A:** It depends on the exact address, and no public school building sits inside South Meadows itself. A School District 42 locator run recorded for 10932 Barnston View Road returned Davie Jones Elementary at 12030 Blakely Road and Pitt Meadows Secondary at 19438 116b Avenue, while Edith McDermott Elementary at 12178 Bonson Road serves Bonson Road addresses further north, so check every address. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
**Q:** How close is the airport, and is there aircraft noise? **A:** Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK) is west of South Meadows across Harris Road, and the City maintains a dedicated page for airport safety and noise concerns. No official noise-zone map covering this sub-area was confirmed for this note, so a buyer concerned about flight paths should ask the City and the airport directly rather than rely on distance alone. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Shoreline Park at 51 Bonson Road is on the Fraser River dike, so the dike top is the neighbourhood's riverfront trail. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- Bonson Park at 11546 Bonson Road contains a disc golf course, a use few buyers expect inside a residential pocket. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- Pitt Meadows Athletic Park is at 11431 Bonson Road, next to the arena complex with three sheets of ice. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- Osprey Village at Fraser Way and Barnston View Road is the local commercial pocket, with the larger Meadowtown centre at 19800 Lougheed Highway north of the rail line. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- Route 719 runs Meadowtown to Fraser Way via Hammond Road, Bonson Road and Fraser Way to the Barnston View roundabout, then continues as route 722. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt719.pdf
- The city's only library branch is north in Central Meadows at 200-12099 Harris Road, not in South Meadows. https://fvrl.bc.ca/locations/pitt-meadows-public-library
- Drainage Area 3, which includes South Meadows, is the city's urban drainage area, serviced by ditches, culverts and storm sewers together. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163

- Pitt Meadows Road and Rail Improvements Project, Harris Road at the CPKC main line — port authority, City and CPKC project, in design and funding correspondence in 2025–2026: a new four-lane underpass below the rail crossing at Harris Road, the north gate to South Meadows (status read November 3, 2026). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/city-planning-projects/pitt-meadows-road-and-rail-improvements-project/past-project

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Pitt Meadows' 2026 general residential rate is $2.8438 per $1,000, plus a separate residential drainage rate of $0.1082 per $1,000, under Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 — see the pitt-meadows-houses-for-sale note for full city detail. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- 2026 Pitt Meadows taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- The City of Pitt Meadows is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so owners file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- No West Coast Express station sits in South Meadows; Pitt Meadows Station at 12280 Harris Road, north of the rail line, is the city's station. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Routes 719 and 722 form the Bonson Road loop: 719 runs via Bonson Road and Fraser Way, and 722 returns via Harris Road, Ford Road and Hammond Road. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt719.pdf
- Harris Road is the main north-south road out of the pocket, and its at-grade CPKC crossing is the delay point the underpass project targets. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/railways
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and Golden Ears Way run along the city's north side, linking the area to Maple Ridge and the Golden Ears Bridge. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check floodplain, dike and drainage detail for the exact lot with the City, because the pocket is low land behind the Fraser River dike in drainage Area 3 (Kennedy, including McKechnie). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- Check the 15.0 metre RS siting setback from watercourses and key or constructed ditches before planning any addition near a ditch. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 - Consolidated Version_0.pdf
- Check rail noise and crossing delay on streets near the CPKC line and Harris Road; the City's rail page, not a listing, is the source for blockage times. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/railways
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status at the pocket's edges parcel by parcel, because 78% of the city's land base is in the ALR city-wide. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf

## 10. HOW IT COMPARES
- South Meadows sits behind the Fraser River dike with no school inside it; Central Meadows holds the Town Centre, library and secondary school ground. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- South Meadows and Mid Meadows share the same Pitt Meadows tax bylaw and School District 42; they differ in that South Meadows is south of the rail line and Mid Meadows is north of it. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/railways
- Maple Ridge across the east boundary uses a different tax bylaw, at a 2026 general residential rate of $2.8643 per $1,000 against Pitt Meadows' $2.8438 plus drainage. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
