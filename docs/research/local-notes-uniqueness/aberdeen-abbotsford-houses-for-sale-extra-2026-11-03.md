## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK

- Houses here are single-detached homes on rural and agricultural lots, and the agricultural zones set the lot floor: the Agricultural One (A1) zone's minimum subdivision size is 8.0 ha and the Agricultural Two (A2) zone's is 16.0 ha. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- The stock is freehold farm and rural-residential land, and the City's AgRefresh work records the agricultural pattern as two designations, Agriculture 1 – Uplands on A1 patterns and Agriculture 2 – Lowlands on A2 patterns. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- Rural houses here are privately serviced in the rural pattern, and a City council report describing rural residential development in Abbotsford states that such homes are usually serviced by wells and septic fields, as urban services are not located within the vicinity. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/42563/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE

- The one-residence rule governs most farm parcels, because land in the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless the owner qualifies for an additional residence under the Agricultural Land Commission rules. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- A principal residence in the ALR is capped in size, and the Commission states it must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Airport height rules apply on land under the Airport Zoning Regulation (AZR), and the City's guidelines state that no building, structure or object may exceed in elevation any approach surface, outer surface or transitional surface at that location. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- City-wide zoning treatment is carried in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note. (See the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note.)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

**Q:** Can I build a second house on an Aberdeen farm lot?
**A:** One additional detached residence is possible, but only within the Commission's size caps. The Agricultural Land Commission states that a property may have one additional residence if there is only one existing residence when construction begins, capped at 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, and City zoning must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

**Q:** Does the airport limit what I can build?
**A:** Yes, on land shown on the City's drawing M-500 as under the Airport Zoning Regulation. The City's YXX guidelines state that a developer on such land must seek approvals directly from both NAV CANADA and Transport Canada, and that the City releases the building permit only once copies of those approvals are presented. Height may not exceed the approach, outer or transitional surfaces. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf

**Q:** Which school is in Aberdeen?
**A:** Aberdeen Elementary is the school inside the area, at 2975 Bradner Road. The district's catchments page states that each student's catchment school is determined by the address of their primary home and is checked through the School Locator tool, so a Bradner Road address should still be run through the locator rather than assumed from the school's location alone. https://aberdeen.abbyschools.ca/ https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments

**Q:** Can I subdivide a farm parcel for a family member?
**A:** Subdivision runs into the agricultural minimums first. The City's AgRefresh report records the Zoning Bylaw minimum subdivision sizes as 8.0 ha in the A1 zone and 16.0 ha in the A2 zone, and subdivision inside the ALR also involves the Agricultural Land Commission, which can approve or refuse the application forwarded by Council. Most existing Aberdeen parcels cannot be split further. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf

**Q:** Is there a bus serving Aberdeen?
**A:** The bus service is on Fraser Highway at the north edge, not on the interior rural roads. BC Transit's route 21 runs between Aldergrove and Bourquin Exchange via Highstreet, and its timetable lists a stop at Fraser Highway at Lefeuvre Road in both directions, along with Highstreet Exchange and South Fraser Way at Clearbrook Road. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=21

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- Rural water service is capped by bylaw, and the City's Waterworks Regulation Bylaw limits water service connections for properties outside the Urban Development Boundary to a maximum of 25 mm in diameter, and bars their use for open field crop irrigation. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/55400/
- NAV CANADA assessment takes time, and the City's YXX guidelines state that NAV CANADA attempts to respond within 8 to 12 weeks of receiving a complete land use proposal — a tall shop or barn near the airport is not a quick permit. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- The Abbotsford School District's own acknowledgement names the Máthxwi First Nation and the Semá:th First Nation as the Nations on whose traditional and unceded territory the district, including Aberdeen Elementary, sits. https://aberdeen.abbyschools.ca/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE

- The full city tax treatment is carried in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note; the Aberdeen angle is the farm-class and servicing picture. (See the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note.)
- Abbotsford's 2026 residential (Class 1) municipal rates are 1.08284 per $1,000 for police and 1.78296 per $1,000 for general and debt servicing under Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026, before regional and library levies. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf
- Farm-class (Class 9) land is taxed on a different municipal schedule in the same bylaw, and a mixed house-and-farm property can carry both classes on one notice, so the class split on the BC Assessment record is part of the cost check. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf
- Where a rural Aberdeen property is on City water, the connection is capped at 25 mm outside the Urban Development Boundary, and metered residential water is $1.46 per cubic metre and sewer $1.35 per cubic metre effective March 1, 2026. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/55400/ https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/water-sewer-rates
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies here, because the Province's taxable-areas list names the City of Abbotsford. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE

- Check whether the lot is inside the Airport Zoning Regulation area on City drawing M-500 before planning any tall building, because the City will not release a building permit for AZR land until NAV CANADA and Transport Canada approvals are presented. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- Confirm ALR status before valuing a second dwelling, because the Commission's one-residence rule and the 90 m² / 186 m² additional-residence caps apply on ALR parcels whether or not the listing mentions them. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Pull the well and septic records for the specific parcel, because a City report describes rural residential homes in Abbotsford as usually privately serviced by wells and septic fields where urban services are not in the vicinity — servicing is a parcel check, not an area assumption. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/42563/
- Check the lot's floodplain status on Schedule "E" of the Zoning Bylaw, because the bylaw's floodproofing areas are mapped and apply by mapped area, not by the Aberdeen name. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/42052/
