## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Nordel infill is judged inside a named permit area: the 11220 and 11230 83 Avenue files need Development Permits for form and character in the Nordel Social Heart (ND12) Development Permit Area. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/249457
- Those files rezone from Single Detached Residential 1 (RS1) to Townhouse Residential 62 (RT62) under Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017, Amendment Bylaws No. 8494 and 8495, 2026, for four three-storey units on each lot. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/249457
- Any lot within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch falls in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, which reaches ordinary North Delta ditches. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Which secondary school serves a townhouse on 84 Avenue in Nordel?
**A:** North Delta's secondary schools are North Delta Secondary at 11447 82 Avenue and Sands Secondary at 10840 82 Avenue, both Grades 8 to 12 in School District No. 37 (Delta), called SD37. Catchments are address-based and 84 Avenue sits between the two, so run the exact address through the SD37 School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/delta

**Q:** What documents should I read before buying a Sunstone townhouse?
**A:** Ask for the Form B Information Certificate, which states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, the contingency reserve fund balance and an insurance summary, with rules, budget and the most recent depreciation report attached. On Delsom Crescent complexes, that report shows coming roof and envelope work. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

**Q:** Can a developer replace an older North Delta townhouse complex?
**A:** It is already proposed on 92 Avenue: the 11944 92 Avenue file would demolish 34 townhouses and build a 140-unit six-storey apartment plus 51 three-storey townhouses. In an older complex near Nordel Way, check the City's current applications, because a strata wind-up vote decides a complex's end. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/7717/8466/1706/Item_10_-_Six-Storey_Apartment_and_Three-Storey_Townhouse_Proposal_at_11944_92_Avenue_compressed.pdf

**Q:** How do I get downtown from a Nordel townhouse without a car?
**A:** Route 312 is the spine: from Scottsdale Exchange via Scott Road, 72 Avenue, 112 Street, 92A Avenue, 114 Street and 96 Avenue to Scott Road Station on the Expo Line. From the Baker Road side, route 316 on 116 Street links toward Surrey Central Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt312.pdf
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- North Delta Recreation Centre at 11415 84 Avenue is the civic anchor of the Nordel pocket, two blocks from the Delsom Crescent complexes. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/north-delta-recreation-centre
- Sunstone Park at 10400 Delsom Crescent lists playgrounds, sport courts, trails and a viewpoint in the City park search, and hosts the City's Luminary Festival each September. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search?keywords&region=All&amenities%5B74%5D=74&page=2
- Seaquam Secondary at 11584 Lyon Road, an International Baccalaureate (IB) World School, serves Delta's Sunshine Hills edge, not the Nordel pocket. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/delta
- The City is rebuilding 84 Avenue between Delsom Crescent and 112 Street, with sanitary work anticipated in 2026 and roadway construction in 2027. https://letstalk.delta.ca/84averoadway
- No TransLink route runs on 84 Avenue itself in the current timetable: the pocket's stops are on 112 Street, on route 312. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt312.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta's 2026 residential tax rate and utility charges: see the tsawwassen-houses-for-sale note.
- British Columbia (BC) Property Transfer Tax (PTT) applies at 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million and 3% above $2 million, with first-time buyer and newly built home exemptions stated on the Province's page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- Strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, a cost carried in fees at every Delsom Crescent complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the 30 m streamside line: ditches count under the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area guide, and Sunstone streets drain to open ditches. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- Check whether a complex sits inside the Nordel Social Heart (ND12) Development Permit Area before planning exterior changes; the 83 Avenue files show permits are required there. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/249457
- For older complexes near 92 Avenue, ask about wind-up discussions: the 11944 92 Avenue proposal shows the City will entertain redevelopment of a 34-unit townhouse site. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/7717/8466/1706/Item_10_-_Six-Storey_Apartment_and_Three-Storey_Townhouse_Proposal_at_11944_92_Avenue_compressed.pdf
