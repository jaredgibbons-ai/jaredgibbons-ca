## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK

- That conversion history is the stock's defining fact: a converted building carries conversion-era construction and a strata corporation formed from a rental building, so the depreciation report, the strata's Form B information certificate and the conversion disclosure are the documents that describe this complex, not a developer's sales material. http://searcharchives.coquitlam.ca/uploads/r/city-of-coquitlam-archives/9/2/8/928c605cc00a7d36fe6b1e528d6f6c09799dadfb8f8f89fdddbeee2c24bb9842/Subdivision_Committee_-_1980.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there really strata housing in Upper Eagle Ridge, or is it all houses? **A:** The verified strata anchor is at the foot of the slope, not on the upper streets. City archives record a strata title conversion application for 1170 Lansdowne Drive by Cressey Development Corporation in 1980, which makes that complex a converted rental building. No official source read names a condo or townhouse complex on Falcon Drive or Charter Hill Drive themselves, so treat any upper-street strata claim as unconfirmed until a strata plan is produced. http://searcharchives.coquitlam.ca/uploads/r/city-of-coquitlam-archives/9/2/8/928c605cc00a7d36fe6b1e528d6f6c09799dadfb8f8f89fdddbeee2c24bb9842/Subdivision_Committee_-_1980.pdf
- **Q:** What does the 1980 conversion at 1170 Lansdowne Drive mean for a buyer today? **A:** It means the building started as rental housing and was sold unit by unit under a conversion undertaking that existing tenants would not be ejected. For a resale buyer the practical checks are the strata's depreciation report, contingency reserve fund position and Form B, because a conversion-era building's remaining life on roof, plumbing and envelope systems is a document question, not something a listing states. http://searcharchives.coquitlam.ca/uploads/r/city-of-coquitlam-archives/9/2/8/928c605cc00a7d36fe6b1e528d6f6c09799dadfb8f8f89fdddbeee2c24bb9842/Subdivision_Committee_-_1980.pdf
- **Q:** Can I rely on a published rental or pet bylaw for the Lansdowne Drive complex? **A:** No published bylaw set for 1170 Lansdowne Drive was confirmed on a strata, developer or municipal page read for this note. Strata rental and pet rules live in the strata corporation's own bylaws filed at the Land Title Office and summarised on the Form B a seller must provide, so the Form B and the current bylaw set are the only reliable source for this complex. http://searcharchives.coquitlam.ca/uploads/r/city-of-coquitlam-archives/9/2/8/928c605cc00a7d36fe6b1e528d6f6c09799dadfb8f8f89fdddbeee2c24bb9842/Subdivision_Committee_-_1980.pdf
- **Q:** Which schools sit below these strata units? **A:** Three School District No. 43 schools sit at the foot of the pocket on the Lansdowne and Falcon corridor: Eagle Ridge Elementary at 1215 Falcon Drive for kindergarten to Grade 5, Scott Creek Middle at 1240 Lansdowne Drive for Grades 6 to 8, and Gleneagle Secondary at 1195 Lansdowne Drive for Grades 9 to 12. Catchment is assigned by permanent home address, so run the unit's address in the district School Locator before relying on any of them. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** Does the wildfire permit area affect a townhouse renovation on this slope? **A:** It can, at the permit stage. Coquitlam regulates high-risk interface areas through an Interface Wildfire Risk Management Development Permit Area, and its Community Wildfire Resiliency Plan 2021 records FireSmart landscaping and exterior finish guidelines for interface properties. Whether a specific Lansdowne Drive strata lot sits inside the permit boundary is a City map check on the environmental development permit mapping, not a neighbourhood label. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the City's environmental development permit mapping, Schedule B, which maps unstable slopes and the Interface Wildfire Risk Management boundary for the City Centre and Northeast areas; the map shows the boundaries, not a lot-level slope or fire conclusion for a strata lot. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- Check the Community Wildfire Resiliency Plan 2021 threat mapping: about 23 percent of Coquitlam's wildland urban interface rates moderate wildfire behaviour threat or higher, and its FireSmart actions target homes backing onto natural green space. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/7234/Community_Wildfire_Resiliency_Plan_2021-FireSmart_Principles
- Check creek setbacks on any lot draining to Scott Creek: within 30 metres of top of bank or ravine bank in this plan area, the Watercourse Protection Development Permit can impose conditions on riparian protection, runoff quality and erosion or landslip. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve status per parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission mapping; no ALR land was identified in this part of Coquitlam in the sources read, which is not a parcel clearance. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
