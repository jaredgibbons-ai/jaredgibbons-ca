# Birchland Manor Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: birchland-manor-port-coquitlam (Birchland Manor; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Birchland Manor is the small north-east Port Coquitlam pocket around Fraser Avenue and Prairie Avenue, east of Coast Meridian Road. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/birchland/
- Birchland Elementary at 1331 Fraser Avenue is the named school on Fraser Avenue inside the pocket. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/birchland/
- Prairie Avenue is the east–west street the TransLink 173/174 loop uses at Coast Meridian Road, on Birchland Manor's west edge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt173.pdf
- Terry Fox Secondary at 1260 Riverwood Gate sits north of the pocket in Riverwood, the secondary school named for this side of the city. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/terryfox
- Exact board sub-area boundary roads are not published by the board, so street-level edges here are UNCONFIRMED — see NOT CONFIRMED.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Birchland Manor is a detached-house pocket with a small attached component: no municipal source read publishes a build-era or lot-size profile for this pocket alone — see NOT CONFIRMED.
- The pocket's housing form is governed by the Residential Small Scale (RS) zones in Zoning Bylaw No. 3630, not by the townhouse RTh or apartment RA zones used on the city's main roads. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf
- Fraser Avenue is also a street name in the City's rezoning history: a separate Fraser Avenue rezoning at 2260 Fraser Avenue, south in Central, is not in this pocket and is not carried here — see the central-port-coquitlam note.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A Birchland Manor RS lot can hold a secondary suite inside the house: the Official Community Plan policy replaced by Amendment Bylaw No. 4373 permits secondary suites in single-detached houses, duplexes and triplexes. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11285
- A suite changes the utility bill: the City bills additional dwelling units at their own water and sewer rates, set out in section 7. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills
- Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing permit-area rules and Accessory Dwelling Unit (coach house) rules for RS lots are covered in full in the mary-hill-port-coquitlam and oxford-heights-port-coquitlam notes in this cluster — see those notes.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is in Birchland Manor itself? **A:** Birchland Elementary, at 1331 Fraser Avenue, is in the pocket. It is a Kindergarten to Grade 5 school in School District 43 (SD43); because SD43 catchments are based on the permanent home address, a buyer should still run the exact Fraser Avenue or Prairie Avenue address in the district School Locator before relying on this catchment. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/birchland/
- **Q:** Where do Birchland Manor students go for secondary school? **A:** Terry Fox Secondary at 1260 Riverwood Gate is the secondary school serving this side of Port Coquitlam, named for Terry Fox, a 1976 graduate of the school under its former name. No SD43 locator run for a Birchland Manor address is on record in this program, so the catchment itself is UNCONFIRMED here — run the address in the locator. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/terryfox
- **Q:** Does a suite in a Birchland Manor house cost more in utilities? **A:** Yes, the City charges for the extra unit. For 2026, each additional dwelling unit is billed $481.00 for water and $483.00 for sewer on top of the principal unit, under the City's flat annual utility rates. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills
- **Q:** Is Birchland Manor on the floodplain side of the city? **A:** The City's flood statement is not pocket-specific. The City says a significant portion of Port Coquitlam is within the Fraser or Pitt River flood plain and protected by dykes; Birchland Manor is on the raised north side, but no City page read clears an individual lot, so check the address on the City flood page. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/city-port-coquitlam-prepares-potential-flooding

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Solid waste fees are not on the utility bill: the City states solid waste fees are billed on the Annual Property Tax Notice, while water and sewer are billed separately by the end of February. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills
- Multi-family homes in Port Coquitlam only pay solid waste fees if they have opted in to City waste collection services. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills
- The 173/174 TransLink loop stops on Prairie Avenue at Coast Meridian Road, the closest named loop stop to Fraser Avenue in the timetable. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt173.pdf
- SD43's cross-catchment process for the 2027–2028 school year runs February 5–10, 2027, and approval is only for the one designated school requested, not a general transfer. http://sd43.bc.ca/cross-catchment
- Blakeburn Elementary at 1040 Riverside Drive and Blakeburn Lagoons Park are in the adjoining Riverwood pocket north-east of Birchland Manor, not inside it. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-coquitlam

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The 2026 annual water rate for each townhouse unit is $590.00, and for each apartment unit $456.00 for water and $458.00 for sewer, under the City's flat annual utility rates. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills
- Utility bills are mailed by the end of February and due March 31, 2026, and water and sewer are provided on a cost recovery basis. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills
- The municipal tax rate (Bylaw No. 4441) is in the mary-hill-port-coquitlam note, single-dwelling utility rates are in the oxford-heights-port-coquitlam note, and the Speculation and Vacancy Tax position is in the central-port-coquitlam note in this cluster.

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Birchland Manor has no train station: its TransLink service is the bus network on Prairie Avenue and Coast Meridian Road, connecting west to Port Coquitlam Station and Coquitlam Central Station on the 173/174 loop. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt173.pdf
- Coast Meridian Road is one of the city's two main north–south routes named in the Official Community Plan, with the Coast Meridian Overpass connecting the north side to Lougheed Highway. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Look the lot up on the City's flood resources at portcoquitlam.ca/flood: the City's own modelling shows protection against the 1:200-year flood but dike overtopping predicted in a 1:500-year event with 1 metre of sea level rise by 2100, and the flood page does not rate an individual Birchland Manor lot. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/FileStream.ashx?DocumentId=5334
- The City operates nine drainage pump stations that pump creek and drainage water over the dikes when river levels are high and flood boxes close — a north-side buyer should ask which drainage system serves the lot. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/FileStream.ashx?DocumentId=5334
- Suite legality is a separate check from zoning: a second kitchen or separate entrance does not by itself prove a Birchland Manor suite was permitted, so ask the City for the permit record for the specific address — UNCONFIRMED for any individual home here.
