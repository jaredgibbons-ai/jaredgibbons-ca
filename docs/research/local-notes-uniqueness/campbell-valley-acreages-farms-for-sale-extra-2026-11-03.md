# Campbell Valley Acreages and Farms — Extra Local Notes
Page: campbell-valley-acreages-farms-for-sale (Campbell Valley, Langley Township; acreages and farms).
## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Campbell Valley is the rural valley in south Langley Township around 200 Street and 16 Avenue, and its centrepiece is parkland, not a subdivision. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- The Little Campbell River runs through the park, and the Little River Loop trail crosses it twice on raised boardwalks. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- The Township's GeoSource tool is where a buyer checks a Campbell Valley parcel's zoning, plan area and watercourses lot by lot. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock here is individual fee-simple acreage parcels, with no new named acreage subdivision or bare-land strata verified in this pass. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Park land is not housing stock: the Annand/Rowlatt Farmstead (buildings from 1898) and the Lochiel Schoolhouse (built 1924, moved to the park in 1988) sit inside Campbell Valley Regional Park as heritage sites. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- Equestrian use shapes the parcels: Metro Vancouver lists equestrian facilities, a riding ring and horse unloading in the park, and nearly 17 kilometres (km) of horse trails. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Zoning is Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, which applies to the whole Township and sets permitted uses, lot coverage and building restrictions parcel by parcel. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- Section 110 of that bylaw sets the minimum subdivision requirements for lot area, frontage and depth, so a Campbell Valley split is read there first. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- Where a parcel is in the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR), the Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) caps a principal residence at 500 square metres (m²) total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Township-wide farm and acreage facts are owned in full by the langley-farms note — see the langley-farms note for the shared Township frame. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I build a second full house on a Campbell Valley acreage? **A:** Not as of right on most parcels. In the ALR the ALC allows one principal residence, plus one additional detached residence only if conditions are met — up to 90 m² on properties 40 hectares (ha) or less — and Township zoning may be stricter, so confirm both layers for the specific lot before you buy. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q:** Can I put a suite in a detached barn or shop? **A:** No, not as a secondary suite in the ALR. The ALC permits a secondary suite only when it is entirely contained within the principal residence; a separate or detached building cannot be used as the suite, and Township bylaws must also permit suites on that lot. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q:** Why do so many listings mention horses? **A:** Because the park next door is built for them. Metro Vancouver lists nearly 17 km of equestrian trails, the 11 km Shaggy Mane Trail shared with pedestrians, horse-trailer parking at Campbell Downs Equestrian Centre, and a riding ring — so trailer access and fencing on the parcel itself are practical checks, not extras. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- **Q:** Does small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) give me 3–4 units here? **A:** Usually no on a large rural lot. The Township states lots larger than 4,050 m² (1 acre) are not eligible for SSMUH, and eligible lots must be inside the Urban Containment Boundary and connected to both municipal water and sewer; other lots get one unit plus one suite or accessory dwelling unit (ADU), depending on zoning. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Dogs must be on leash throughout Campbell Valley Regional Park except in the leash-optional area next to 4 Avenue, which affects acreages that back onto the park. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- Cycling is allowed only on the Perimeter Trail in the park, and cycling is not permitted on the Shaggy Mane Trail at all. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- McLean Pond is closed to fishing by provincial regulation dating to April 1, 2017, to protect native animals. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- School catchment is address-based: School District 35 (Langley) says its School Locator Tool determines the catchment school from a street address or housing development name. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- Park hours change with the season — Metro Vancouver lists 7:00 am–6:00 pm from November 3 to December 31 — so evening trail traffic beside nearby parcels is seasonal. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Township property tax statements are mailed or emailed by the end of May, with a 5% penalty at midnight July 2 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026, as the Township states. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- The Township tax notice bundles general Township taxes plus levies for roads, drainage, solid waste and capital projects, and taxes collected for other authorities — drainage and solid waste matter on acreages. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) taxable-area list includes the Township of Langley, so a residential parcel owner there must check the declaration duty on the Province's page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- Headline civic tax facts are in the earliest sibling note — see the langley-farms note; the Township page read today states no per-$1,000 residential rate, so no rate is quoted here. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Transit reaches the park, not most acreages: Metro Vancouver states a regular bus stop is near the park entrance at 200th Street and 20th Avenue, Langley. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- TransLink route 566 serves the South Valley entrance on weekends and holidays from June 8 to September 6, 2026, as Metro Vancouver states. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- Cyclists can connect from the park to the South Langley Regional Trail at its south-east corner, and to the Fernridge Trail and High Point multi-use trail nearby. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Flood and watercourse checks start in GeoSource: the Township directs buyers to activate the Environment layer to view watercourses and classifications for a parcel. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- The Little Campbell River is a working watercourse through the valley — the park's Little River Loop crosses it twice — so riparian setbacks on a bordering parcel need a lot-specific check. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- In the ALR, building can trigger a Notice of Intent for fill, health authority approval for septic, and floodplain or riparian approvals, as the ALC lists. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
