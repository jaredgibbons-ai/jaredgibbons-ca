# Ranch Park Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: ranch-park-coquitlam-houses-for-sale (Ranch Park; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Ranch Park is the house pocket around Spuraway Avenue and the north side of Mariner Way in central Coquitlam, with Mundy Park's forest as its south edge; the City publishes no boundary for this board sub-area name, so treat street names, not a map line, as the check. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1591/Explore-the-Great-Outdoors---Trail-Maps-and-Guide-PDF
- The pocket's addressed anchor is Ranch Park Elementary at 2701 Spuraway Avenue, the School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) school inside the street pattern. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- The City's trail guide maps Spuraway Avenue and Mariner Way along Mundy Park's north side, with the park's parking lot and community path reached from those streets. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1591/Explore-the-Great-Outdoors---Trail-Maps-and-Guide-PDF

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses here are the low-density stock between the Spuraway Avenue school site and the Mundy Park forest edge, not apartment or townhouse stock; the attached-housing route for these lots is the City's small-scale framework, in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId
- What the City publishes for these lots is the rebuild frame: most low-density lots now sit in one of four small-scale residential zones, R-1, R-2, R-3 or R-4, each with its own minimum lot size for adding units, from 465 square metres in R-2 to 930 square metres in R-4. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId
- No City, SD43 or BC Assessment public page read for this note states a typical build decade, typical lot size or named subdivision for Ranch Park houses, so era and lot size are lot-by-lot checks on the City's QtheMap zoning viewer and the title, not a neighbourhood average. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is in Ranch Park itself? **A:** Ranch Park Elementary, at 2701 Spuraway Avenue, serves kindergarten to Grade 5 and is the School District No. 43 school physically inside this pocket. Attendance is assigned by permanent home address, not by the Ranch Park name, so confirm the designated school for a specific Spuraway Avenue or Mariner Way address in the district School Locator before relying on it. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** How big is the park these houses back onto? **A:** The City's trail guide describes Mundy Park as a 178-hectare park and trail system, among the community's most treasured assets, offering a rare urban forest experience on Ranch Park's south edge. Its Mundy Park Community Path is a 5.5-kilometre paved multi-use trail encircling the park, and shorter interior trails loop around Mundy Lake and lead to viewing benches at Lost Lake. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1591/Explore-the-Great-Outdoors---Trail-Maps-and-Guide-PDF
- **Q:** What bus serves a Ranch Park house? **A:** TransLink route 152 is the Mariner Way service: it runs from Coquitlam Central Station via Lougheed Highway, Dewdney Trunk Road and Mariner Way, past the Spuraway Avenue side of the pocket, and continues via Hickey Drive and Austin Avenue to Lougheed Station. Route 153 also runs Mariner Way, linking Coquitlam Central Station with Braid Station in New Westminster via Como Lake Avenue. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt152.pdf
- **Q:** Can I add units to a Ranch Park house lot? **A:** Possibly, under the small-scale rules adopted June 9, 2025 and set out in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. For this pocket the City's three early flags matter most: a creek on or beside the lot, a steep slope, or a house built before 1945, any of which means talking to Development Services before designing, especially on lots touching the park forest. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Dogs must be on leash at all times on the Mundy Park Community Path, and the trail guide directs dog owners to the park's separate off-leash rules — a daily-life detail for houses whose yards open toward the park. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1591/Explore-the-Great-Outdoors---Trail-Maps-and-Guide-PDF
- The Mundy Park Community Path can be completed on foot within an hour and is a paved multi-use trail suitable for a variety of recreational pursuits, so the park edge carries steady walker and cyclist traffic past the Spuraway Avenue side. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1591/Explore-the-Great-Outdoors---Trail-Maps-and-Guide-PDF
- Mundy Park holds an outdoor pool, listed as Spani Pool on the City's trail guide map, with playground, washrooms and parking inside the park — seasonal recreation on this pocket's doorstep, not a year-round facility. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1591/Explore-the-Great-Outdoors---Trail-Maps-and-Guide-PDF
- Riverview Forest, beside Mundy Park, is a dedicated urban forest with trails for walking and jogging in a forested natural setting, extending the green edge east of the Ranch Park streets. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1591/Explore-the-Great-Outdoors---Trail-Maps-and-Guide-PDF

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the City's environmental development permit mapping, Schedule B, for slope and interface boundaries on park-edge lots; the map shows the layers, not a lot-level geotechnical conclusion. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- Check Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 section 519 flood and slope siting rules and Schedule G, the designated 200-year floodplain of the Brunette, Coquitlam, Fraser and Pitt rivers, even though this pocket sits upland of those rivers. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1170/Part-5-General-Regulations-PDF
- Check creek status on any lot draining toward Mundy Park: the City classifies its creeks for environmental value and monitors flow, level and water quality on various creeks, and a park-edge lot still needs its own mapping check before purchase. https://coquitlam.ca/217/Creeks-and-Streams
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status per parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission mapping; no ALR land was identified in this part of Coquitlam in the sources read, which is not a parcel clearance. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
