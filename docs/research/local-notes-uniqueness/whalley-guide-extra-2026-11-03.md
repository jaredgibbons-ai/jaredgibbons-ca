# Whalley Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: whalley-guide (Whalley / Surrey City Centre, Surrey; Guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Whalley is the City community and "City Centre" is its downtown core — the City lists the community as "City Centre (Whalley)," and downtown addresses sit around King George Boulevard, City Parkway and 104 Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/whalley
- The board's name for this ground is Whalley, not Surrey City Centre: board reporting for Surrey carries a Whalley sub-area and no City Centre sub-area, so listings and statistics for the downtown are filed under Whalley. (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report)
- Guildford is east of Whalley, Newton is south, and Bridgeview and the Fraser River are north, with Scott Road and North Delta to the west. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/whalley

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The core's stock is strata condominiums in concrete towers at the stations, with mid-rise and townhouse product at the core's edges and older single-detached houses on the surrounding Whalley blocks. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/whalley
- King George Hub, at King George Station, is the named master-planned cluster on the south end of the core: its residential towers are Hub One (13615 Fraser Hwy), Hub Two (13655 Fraser Hwy), The Line rental tower (13639 George Junction) and Plaza One & Two (13725 & 13745 George Junction), by PCI Developments. https://kinggeorgehub.com/
- Two building waves define the area: 1970s-era low-rise rental and detached stock outside the core, and post-2000 tower construction inside it that accelerated after the 2017 City Centre Plan. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/city-centre-plan
- Central City, 13450 102 Avenue, is the core's landmark mixed complex — shopping centre, office tower and Simon Fraser University's Surrey campus stacked over Surrey Central Station. https://www.sfu.ca/surrey/about/our-locations/sfu-at-central-city.html

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- What can be built beside a Whalley home is set by the City Centre Plan, approved in 2017 and now under update: the update establishes a Central Business District, identifies a future Entertainment District, enables more transit-oriented development around SkyTrain stations and allows growth in Bolivar Heights. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/city-centre-plan
- City-wide zoning, suite and small-scale multi-unit rules are Surrey-wide and are written in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note; the Whalley-specific layer is the City Centre Plan designation on the parcel, checked in the City's COSMOS map. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Density-bonus money changed regime in 2026: the City states Community Amenity Contributions were superseded by its Amenity Cost Charge and bonus density provisions as of June 15, 2026, with the old page kept only as a historic reference for in-stream applications — relevant to anyone pricing a core site on bonus density. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/community-amenity-contributions

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Whalley the same place as Surrey City Centre? **A:** City Centre is the downtown core inside the larger Whalley community. The City lists it as "City Centre (Whalley)," one of its communities, while the real estate board's sub-area name for the whole area is Whalley. A buyer searching only "City Centre" will miss board data and listings filed under Whalley. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/whalley
- **Q:** Which SkyTrain stations serve Whalley? **A:** Four Expo Line stations, and they are the only SkyTrain stations in Surrey: Scott Road, Gateway, Surrey Central and King George. Surrey Central and King George anchor the City Centre core itself; Gateway and Scott Road serve the wider Whalley blocks to the north. The R1 King George Blvd RapidBus also runs through the core between Newton Exchange and Guildford Exchange. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/parking-streets-transportation/walking-cycling-transit/transit-in-surrey
- **Q:** Which schools serve a City Centre address? **A:** The in-area public schools are A.H.P. Matthew Elementary (13367 97 Ave) and École Kwantlen Park Secondary (10441 132 St), which hosts French Immersion and the Inter-A program, both in School District 36. Catchments are address-specific: a district locator run for 13367 97 Ave returned A.H.P. Matthew Elementary and Queen Elizabeth Secondary, so run the exact address before relying on a school. https://www.surreyschools.ca/kwantlenpark
- **Q:** Is downtown Whalley in a floodplain? **A:** No area-specific flood regime applies to the core. City Centre is upland, away from Surrey's floodplain lowlands, which the City places along the Nicomekl and Serpentine Rivers toward Cloverdale and Newton and on the Little Campbell River in the south. The core is also outside the Agricultural Land Reserve. Confirm any single parcel in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Simon Fraser University's Surrey campus is inside the Central City shopping centre at 250 - 13450 102 Avenue, next to Surrey Central Station; its first building opened above the mall in fall 2006 and was designed by architect Bing Thom. https://www.sfu.ca/surrey/about/our-locations/sfu-at-central-city.html
- Kwantlen Polytechnic University also has a Civic Plaza campus in the core, and Surrey City Hall and the City Centre Library (10350 University Drive) complete the civic cluster around the stations. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/whalley
- Holland Park, 13428 Old Yale Road, is the downtown's central green space and festival site at 10 hectares. https://www.surrey.ca/activities-parks-recreation/parks/holland-park
- Chuck Bailey Recreation Centre, 13458 107A Avenue, is the core's recreation centre, and the City is expanding it. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/whalley
- Central City shopping centre, 10153 King George Blvd, is anchored by T&T Supermarket, Winners, Shoppers Drug Mart and Walmart — the core's full-service grocery anchor is a mall tenant, not a standalone store. https://centralcity.ca/
- Buses listed at Surrey Central Station include routes R1, 320 and 321; no operator page read names a "Surrey Central bus exchange," so treat the station forecourt as a station stop cluster, not a named exchange. http://www.surreylibraries.ca/locations/city-centre-branch

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Whalley pays the city-wide Surrey rate: the 2026 total residential (Class 1) rate is $3.42731 per $1,000 of assessed value, with the full schedule in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies: the Province lists the City of Surrey as a designated taxable area, so Whalley owners file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Whalley holds all four of Surrey's Expo Line stations — Scott Road, Gateway, Surrey Central and King George — making it the only Surrey community on the SkyTrain network today. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/parking-streets-transportation/walking-cycling-transit/transit-in-surrey
- The R1 King George Blvd RapidBus runs from Newton Exchange to Guildford Exchange via Surrey Central Station and King George Station, so both ends of the core have a rapid-bus spine as well as rail. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
- Route 321 links Surrey Central Station south along King George Boulevard through King George Station toward Newton Exchange and White Rock Centre. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt321.pdf
- The Surrey Langley SkyTrain extends the Expo Line east from King George Station along Fraser Highway, with an anticipated in-service date of 2029 — King George stays the transfer point between the two lines' Surrey segments. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/surrey-langley-skytrain
Read: 2026-11-03

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- The core's main due-diligence layer is the plan, not a hazard map: the City Centre Plan update is redrawing districts around the stations, so check a parcel's current and proposed designation in COSMOS and the plan update before valuing redevelopment potential. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/city-centre-plan
- Flood and ALR checks come back clean for the core itself — it is upland and outside the Agricultural Land Reserve — but outer Whalley blocks near the Nicomekl and Serpentine lowlands are a different file: use the City's floodplain pages for any low-lying address. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain
- For strata towers, the plan pages do not state a building's rental or pet bylaws, depreciation report position or special levies — those come only from the strata's own documents for the specific building. https://kinggeorgehub.com/
