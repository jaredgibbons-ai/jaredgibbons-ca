# Mission Townhomes — Extra Local Notes
Page: mission-townhomes-for-sale (City of Mission; townhomes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Mission townhomes sit inside the City of Mission, under the Official Community Plan's Urban Residential Townhouse designation, which is the plan category that separates townhouse ground from the Urban Residential and Suburban Residential designations around it. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan
- The growth corridor for the form is Cedar Valley, in the city's north-central area: the City's Cedar Valley Local Area Plan (November 2019) is one of the planning documents the City's Development Cost Charge background report builds its growth projections on. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/2024-development-cost-charge-background-report
- City of Mission–wide facts and Mission condo facts: see the mission-condos-for-sale note; this note is the townhome-specific angle only.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Mission creates townhouse zoning project by project: at 32909, 32919 and 32939 Cherry Avenue in Cedar Valley, the City processed a new Comprehensive Development 53 Zone (CD53) to allow 74 homes consisting of townhouses and back-to-back townhouses, on land zoned Suburban 20 (S20) under an Attached Multiunit Residential Official Community Plan designation. https://pub-mission.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11475
- Back-to-back townhouses are a distinct form in Mission: City staff brought the form to Council in September 2021 as a new housing form for Mission, with examples from neighbouring municipalities, before the Cherry Avenue application used it. https://pub-mission.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11475

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A Mission townhouse cannot take a secondary suite: the City's secondary suites program states suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes, while a suite in a single detached house must be registered with the City and carries an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus applicable utility user fees on the tax notice. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- Zoning is under City of Mission Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020, and townhouse projects that do not fit an existing zone proceed by a site-specific comprehensive development zone, as CD53 did on Cherry Avenue. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning
- Zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted, which affects the detached streets around a townhouse complex rather than the strata complex itself. https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Can I add a rental suite to a Mission townhouse?
**A:** No. The City of Mission's secondary suites program states secondary suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes. Suites belong to single detached houses in Mission, must be registered with the City, go through the building permit process when new, and carry an annual $63 secondary suite fee plus utility user fees. A townhome buyer who needs rental income has to price that from the strata's rental position in its registered bylaws instead, which is a separate document. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program

**Q:** Which bus serves Cedar Valley townhomes?
**A:** BC Transit route 33, named Cedar Valley. Mission's local buses are BC Transit's Central Fraser Valley system, not TransLink, and its Mission routes are 31 Valley Connector, 32 West Heights, 33 Cedar Valley, 34 East Side, 35 Hatzic and 39 Shopper Shuttle. A Cedar Valley townhome rider connects at Mission City Station for the West Coast Express or the 31 Valley Connector to Abbotsford. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps

**Q:** How are schools structured for a Mission townhome family?
**A:** In three tiers, unlike Maple Ridge next door. School District No. 75 (Mission) runs elementary schools Kindergarten to Grade 6, middle schools Grades 7 to 9, and Mission Senior Secondary, 32939 7th Avenue, for Grades 10 to 12. Cedar Valley addresses feed elementary schools such as Albert McMahon Elementary, 32865 Cherry Avenue, and Edwin S Richards Elementary, 33419 Cherry Avenue, but catchments are address-based, so confirm the complex address with the district. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075

**Q:** What is a back-to-back townhouse, and does Mission have them?
**A:** A back-to-back townhouse shares a rear wall with the unit behind it, so it has no rear yard. City of Mission staff introduced the form to Council in September 2021 as a new housing form for Mission, and the Cherry Avenue application at 32909 to 32939 Cherry Avenue then proposed 74 homes consisting of townhouses and back-to-back townhouses under a new CD53 zone. A buyer comparing the two forms on the same street should check which form a specific unit is. https://pub-mission.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11475

**Q:** Is there a train to Vancouver from Mission?
**A:** Yes, on weekday rush hours only. Mission City Station, 33200 North Railway Avenue, is the eastern terminus of TransLink's West Coast Express, and the timetable effective January 5, 2026 runs five weekday trains westbound from Mission City at 5:25 to 7:25 a.m. and five eastbound trains returning in the afternoon, with no service on weekends or statutory holidays. Limited weekday 701 bus service to and from Mission City Station is also available. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City of Mission tax rates and Speculation and Vacancy Tax treatment in full: see the mission-condos-for-sale note.
- The townhome-specific utility point is what does not apply: Mission's 2026 flat-rate utility fees total $1,892.28 per single family home (water $641.76, sanitary sewer $786.60, garbage $166.92, recycling and compostables $297.00), and the higher with-secondary-dwelling total of $3,207.60 cannot apply to a townhouse, because suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes in Mission. https://www.mission.ca/services/utility-bills
- Mission bills utilities as a flat rate on the annual property tax notice for serviced homes, rather than metering most homes, and metered homes are billed water and sewer quarterly. https://www.mission.ca/services/water-sewer/water/water-meters

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the dike and flood frame on south-side complexes: the Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's south edge, low areas such as Hatzic Prairie are protected by the Dewdney Dyke system, and the City's tax rates page states additional taxes are levied for diking on top of the municipal, school, regional district and BC Assessment levies. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- Check the zone, not the marketing form name: a Mission townhouse project may sit in a site-specific comprehensive development zone such as CD53, whose permitted forms (including back-to-back units) are written in that zone's own text in Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020. https://pub-mission.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11475
- Agricultural Land Reserve status on the city's rural edges is per-parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca
