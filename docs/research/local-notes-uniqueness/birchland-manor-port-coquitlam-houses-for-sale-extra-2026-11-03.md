## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is in Birchland Manor? **A:** Birchland Elementary, at 1331 Fraser Avenue, stands inside the pocket on Fraser Avenue; School District No. 43 (SD43) assigns catchments by address, so confirm the assigned elementary, middle and secondary schools for a specific address in the district locator. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/birchland/
- **Q:** How many units can a Birchland Manor house lot take? **A:** Under the City's small-scale multi-unit housing rules, 3 to 6 units are allowed on RS lots without rezoning, with the count depending on lot size, location and transit access, so the lot's own figures decide the number. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q:** Which bus serves the pocket? **A:** The TransLink 173 and 174 loop runs at Prairie Avenue and Coast Meridian Road on the pocket's west edge, per the route timetable, tying the Fraser Avenue streets to the Coast Meridian corridor. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt173.pdf
- **Q:** When are Port Coquitlam property taxes due? **A:** Property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after July 2 and a further 5 percent after September 15, 2026, and utility charges are flat annual water and sewer fees billed separately, due March 31, 2026. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes
