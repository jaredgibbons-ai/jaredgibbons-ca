# Deer Lake Place Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: deer-lake-place (Deer Lake Place, Burnaby; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Deer Lake Place is the small residential pocket at the west end of Deer Lake in central Burnaby, off Deer Lake Parkway near Gilpin Street, beside the City's civic and cultural precinct at Deer Lake Park. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- Canada Way and Deer Lake Parkway serve the pocket's north side, and Gilpin Street connects it south toward Kingsway and Metrotown. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- It is a distinct board sub-area from Deer Lake, Upper Deer Lake and Buckingham Heights — the four are listed separately, and this pocket is the west-end one. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- Burnaby City Hall is at 4949 Canada Way, just north-west of the pocket — the Deer Lake area is the City's governmental precinct as well as its cultural one. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-09/Notice-of-Election-by-Voting-2026-10-01.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Deer Lake Place the same area as Deer Lake? **A:** No. They are separate board sub-areas with separate market records, and Deer Lake Place is specifically the small pocket at the west end of the lake, off Deer Lake Parkway near Gilpin Street, while Deer Lake covers the streets around the rest of the park. Using the wrong name in a price opinion imports the wrong pocket's comparables, so confirm which sub-area a listing actually sits in. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- **Q:** Is the school in the pocket the public school for my child? **A:** No. The school physically in the pocket, Deer Lake SDA School at 5550 Gilpin Street, is an independent school, not the public catchment school. The nearby public schools on the Province's School District 41 list are Buckingham Elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 7), 6066 Buckingham Avenue, and Burnaby Central Secondary (Grades 8 to 12), 6011 Deer Lake Parkway, and the catchment for a specific address must be run in the district locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** What is actually beside the pocket? **A:** Deer Lake Park, directly east. The City describes it as the cultural centre of the city, where trails connect the lake, the Burnaby Art Gallery, the Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, Burnaby Village Museum and Hart House restaurant; the park is open dawn to dusk year-round and its address is 6450 Deer Lake Avenue, so the pocket's amenity is a working arts precinct, not just a green space. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- **Q:** Is there SkyTrain in Deer Lake Place? **A:** No station sits inside the pocket. Metrotown Station on the Expo Line, south-west at Metrotown, is the nearest station for this pocket, and TransLink's September 2026 Burnaby system map shows bus routes 110 and 144 running the Deer Lake Parkway corridor at the pocket's north side, which is how transit trips here connect to the rail network. https://translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/september-2026/burnaby-and-new-westminster-map.pdf
- **Q:** What is the Burnaby Art Gallery, specifically? **A:** A one-of-a-kind public gallery in the park at the lake's north side. The City describes it as dedicated to collecting, preserving and presenting contemporary and historical visual art, and as the only public art museum in Canada dedicated to works of art on paper — one of the cultural facilities, with the Shadbolt Centre and its Festival Lawn performance venue, that sit a short walk east of this pocket. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Burnaby Village Museum is at 6501 Deer Lake Avenue in the park's north-east corner, recreating the village of Burnaby in the early 20th century with costumed interpreters, a farmhouse, and a street of homes, shops and a blacksmith forge. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- Baldwin House and Eagles Estate, heritage homes around the lake, are part of the Deer Lake Artist Residency program, about a 10-minute walk from the Shadbolt Centre and the Burnaby Art Gallery. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- The City's 2026 special events program brings Burnaby Blooms (May 3), Symphony and Opera in the Park (July 18–19) and the Burnaby Blues + Roots Festival (August 8) to Deer Lake Park, so event days change parking and traffic at the pocket's doorstep. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2026-04-17/burnaby-unveils-2026-civic-special-events-program
- The Deer Lake boat-launch boardwalk replacement closes that trail section until winter 2027, with a detour via Sperling Avenue and Deer Lake Drive — full entry in the buckingham-heights note. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- The library branch serving this quadrant is the Bob Prittie Metrotown Branch, south-west at Metrotown, one of Burnaby Public Library's four quadrant branches. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/city-departments/burnaby-public-library
- Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid Street (Fraser Health), is immediately north-west of the pocket across Canada Way. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Bus routes 110 and 144 run the Deer Lake Parkway corridor at the pocket's north side, per TransLink's September 2026 Burnaby system map, linking it toward Metrotown Station and toward Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station and Simon Fraser University (SFU). https://translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/september-2026/burnaby-and-new-westminster-map.pdf
