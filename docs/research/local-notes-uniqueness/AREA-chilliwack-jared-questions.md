# AREA — Chilliwack: Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer from his own deals and showings in Chilliwack. Do not answer from research.

1. When you sell a house in Chilliwack Proper versus one in Sardis, who is actually buying each side — local move-ups, Fraser Valley commuters, Alberta or Ontario arrivals, investors — and has that mix changed in the last two years?
2. What sells fast and what sits in Chilliwack right now: do suited houses, small-lot R3 infill, or acreage and ALR-edge properties move first, and which product do you find hardest to price?
3. What surprises buyers most after they move here — the floodplain and flood-covenant paperwork, the school capacity and catchment changes, the Vedder River fan rules, or the Highway 1 commute — and which one has actually killed or reshaped a deal for you?
4. You have the city-wide view from your listings: where do you see the 4,594-home provincial target actually landing on the ground — downtown Yale Road, Sardis infill, or the hillsides — and is that where your buyers want to be?
5. Tell one Chilliwack deal story only you can tell: a house with a hidden suite, flood history, ALR problem or bidding situation that taught you something you now check on every Chilliwack file.
