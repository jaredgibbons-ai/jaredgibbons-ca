# South Surrey Acreages — Extra Local Notes
Page: south-surrey-acreages-for-sale (South Surrey, Surrey; acreages).
## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- South Surrey acreages in this note are City of Surrey land — Hazelmere valley and the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) pockets south toward the border — not Langley Township land. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- The municipal border matters street by street: the same rural roads continue east into the Township of Langley, where Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, not Surrey's bylaw, applies. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- Hazelmere sits in the Little Campbell River side of south Surrey, while Surrey's named flood lowlands are the Serpentine and Nicomekl system to the north-west of the acreage pockets. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- Which side of the border a parcel is on is a title and mapping question: Surrey buyers use the City's Online Development Inquiry by address, Langley buyers use the Township's GeoSource. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/development-building-inquiries

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is individual fee-simple acreage parcels in Surrey's rural pockets, with no new named acreage subdivision or bare-land strata verified in this pass. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/development-building-inquiries
- In Surrey's current zone language the large-lot end is RA, Acreage Residential Zone, for lots over 4,050 square metres (m²) in the small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) table. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- A detached second unit is sized like an accessory building here: coach houses and garden suites run 35 m² to 75 m², up to 120 m² on RA lots where conditions are met. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Surrey-side parcels follow Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 as amended July 8, 2024; Langley-side parcels follow Township Bylaw No. 2500 — do not carry one municipality's rule across the border. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- SSMUH in Surrey applies only to eligible lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, so a true South Surrey acreage over that size is outside the 3–6 unit framework. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Properties not connected to community water and sewer, and certain hazard lands, may be exempt from SSMUH — a servicing check is part of the zoning check on rural Surrey lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- In the ALR south of Highway 99, the Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) caps a principal residence at 500 m² total floor area and limits fill for it to 1,000 m². https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Farm Protection is a named Development Permit Area type in Surrey that can require a development permit on farm-edge lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a Hazelmere acreage in Surrey or Langley? **A:** Hazelmere as covered here is City of Surrey, and that decides the rules. Surrey's zoning bylaw, Surrey's 2026 tax schedule and Surrey's Development Cost Charges (DCCs) apply on the Surrey side; a parcel east of the municipal border is Township of Langley, under Bylaw No. 2500 and Township servicing bylaws, so confirm the municipality on the specific parcel before comparing two listings. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- **Q:** Can I add a coach house on a South Surrey acreage? **A:** Possibly, and RA lots have the widest route. Surrey states larger RA, R1, R2 and R2-O properties may place a coach house off the main driveway instead of a lane if setbacks, separation and driveway width are met; the unit is 35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA where the main house is below maximum floor area ratio. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Q:** Does the ALR stop me building a second home for family? **A:** It limits, rather than simply stops, a second home. The Commission allows one additional detached residence without an application if there is only one existing residence when building starts — up to 90 m² on properties 40 hectares or less — and Surrey's bylaws must also allow it, which is a separate check. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q:** Is Hazelmere in the floodplain I hear about in South Surrey? **A:** Not in the same system as the lowlands, and the distinction matters. The City's flood page describes the Serpentine and Nicomekl lowlands — 59 square kilometres of near-sea-level former intertidal land behind sea dams — while Hazelmere drains to the Little Campbell River; a buyer checks the specific parcel's flood and Development Permit Area status rather than assuming either answer. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Surrey's sea dams work on the tide: Serpentine and Nicomekl gates open at low tide to let fresh water reach Mud Bay and close at high tide to keep salt water out of farm land. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- Farmers began reclaiming Surrey's lowlands with dykes and canals in the late 1800s, and the Surrey Dyking District was formed in 1910 to build the sea dams. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- A coach house or garden suite project must consider service connection cost and feasibility first — sanitary sewer, storm sewer and water fees are investigated through the City's Engineering Front Counter before a permit application. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- If a South Surrey parcel has more than 5 trees, an arborist report is part of the coach house / garden suite document package the City lists. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- A restrictive covenant under section 219 of the Land Title Act still overrides SSMUH permissions in Surrey, so title is checked before design. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey's 2026 total residential rate is $3.42731 per $1,000 of assessed value, and the Farm (Class 9) total in the same schedule is $12.57740 per $1,000 — classification changes the bill on an acreage. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- The 2026 Capital Parcel Tax is $300 for Class 1, 8 and 9, collected with property taxes, and drainage parcel taxes are collected the same way. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- Land-specific charges are Surrey's Development Cost Charges: the City's published examples and payment-at-subdivision-or-permit timing are owned in full by the surrey-lots-land note — see the surrey-lots-land note. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R173.pdf
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) list includes the City of Surrey, and also includes the Township of Langley for a parcel across the border. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- Taxes are due July 2 in Surrey, with 5% penalties after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Access is by the arterial grid the City names in its flood work — King George Boulevard and Elgin Road frame the Nicomekl sea dam area west of the acreage pockets. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- Frequent bus stop status changes what a smaller South Surrey lot may build: within 400 metres of a 15-minute-service stop, an eligible lot may reach 6 SSMUH units. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Project and permit status for a specific acreage is tracked in the City's Online Development Inquiry by address, project number or permit number. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/development-building-inquiries

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Flood risk is system-specific: lowland parcels sit behind dykes, pump stations and sea dams built under the City's Lowland Flood Control Strategic Plan from 1997, which the City says significantly reduced flooding. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- Flood Plain, Steep Slope, Sensitive Ecosystem and Farm Protection Development Permit Areas can each require a development permit in Surrey before a building permit. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- In the ALR, a buyer may face separate approvals for fill (Agricultural Land Commission Notice of Intent), septic (health authority) and floodplain or riparian requirements. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Servicing capacity is decided lot by lot at application: sewer, water, drainage and other utility requirements for the individual lot must be considered, as the City states. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
