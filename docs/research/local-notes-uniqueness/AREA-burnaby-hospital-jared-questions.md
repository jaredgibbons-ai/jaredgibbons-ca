# Burnaby Hospital Area — Questions for Jared

For Jared Gibbons to answer by voice memo. Do not answer these from research — only Jared's deal experience counts.

## Questions
1. Do hospital staff and other health-care buyers actually pay more to live within walking distance of the Burnaby Hospital campus, or is that overstated?
2. How do buyers react to the hospital redevelopment — do they see the new pavilion and planned tower as upside, or the years of Kincaid Street construction as a negative?
3. Which elementary catchment do your family buyers ask about most here — Douglas Road, Cascade Heights or Chaffey-Burke?
4. What sells slowly around the hospital, and what moves — houses on Kincaid Street itself, or the quieter interior streets?
5. Tell one deal story from this area: a sale where the hospital helped or hurt the result in a way buyers would not expect.
