## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- West Newton is the Newton land west of King George Boulevard and north of Highway 10. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps
- Its City plans run generally south of 68 Avenue to Highway 10, in three areas: West Newton North, West Newton South and West Newton / Highway 10. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps
- Scott Road (120 Street) is the west edge toward Delta, and the wider Newton community is bordered by Delta west, 160 Street east and Whalley and Fleetwood north. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/newton

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Surrey-wide rules are stated in full in this note for the Surrey cluster. Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 allows one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex unit, no suite inside a coach house or garden suite, and a suite floor area of at most 40% of the principal unit. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) in Surrey allows at most 3 units on lots under 280 square metres, 4 units on larger lots, and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 square metres within 400 metres of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- A coach house or garden suite is allowed in residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes, sized 35 to 75 square metres, rising to 120 square metres on some larger zones, with one extra parking space. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- A short-term rental in Surrey is an owner-hosted rental under 90 consecutive days, the owner must live there as a primary residence, and a business licence at $358 per year is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- West Newton South was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) update, so that area is now led by the OCP, not the old plan. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Which schools are actually in West Newton? **A:** The City names J.T. Brown and Panorama Park elementary schools and Tamanawis Secondary inside the West Newton plan areas. That is a location fact, not a catchment promise, because Surrey School District 36 (SD36) assigns schools by exact address. Confirm any home address in the SD36 School Locator before relying on a school. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps
**Q:** Can I add a suite or a coach house? **A:** Yes, within Surrey-wide limits stated in full in section 3 of this note: one secondary suite per qualifying unit at most 40% of the principal unit's floor area, and a coach house or garden suite of 35 to 75 square metres in zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes, with one extra parking space. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
**Q:** Is West Newton still guided by its 1990s plans? **A:** Only in part. Council approved West Newton South in July 1995 and West Newton North in July 1996, but West Newton South was later rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update and is no longer in effect. A buyer checking redevelopment should read the current OCP designation for the lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps
**Q:** What transit serves the area today? **A:** Two RapidBus routes frame the area: the R1 King George Boulevard runs from Newton Exchange to Guildford Exchange along the east edge, and the R6 Scott Road runs from Scott Road Station to Newton Exchange along the west edge and 72 Avenue. TransLink states RapidBus runs at least every 10 minutes at peak and 15 minutes off-peak. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Newton takes its name from Elias John Newton, whose homestead sat along 72 Avenue near 124 Street. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/newton
- The Newton Cultural Commercial District strategy covers a commercial district at 128 Street and 80 Avenue in West Newton. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans
- Newton Elementary opened in 1914 at what is now King George Boulevard and 72 Avenue, the corner that became Newton's main commercial district after King George Boulevard was completed in 1940. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/newton

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey-wide costs are stated in full in this note. Surrey's 2026 municipal total residential rate is $1.84251 per $1,000 of assessed value, before other authorities' rates. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- The 2026 annual flat rate for a single-family home is water $1,195 plus sewer $1,661, total $2,856; a secondary suite adds $1,275.71. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/understanding-your-utility-bill
- Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- The City of Surrey is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- The south end drains toward the Serpentine and Nicomekl lowlands, the dyke-protected floodplain south of Newton; check a specific lot on the City's lowlands floodplain pages. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- Do not rely on the rescinded West Newton South plan for permitted use; the City states the OCP now leads development there. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps
