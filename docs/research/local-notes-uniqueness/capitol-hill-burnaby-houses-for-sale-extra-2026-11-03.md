## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Capitol Hill is the hill north of Hastings Street in northwest Burnaby: its houses climb from the Hastings Corridor shopping street up toward Burrard Inlet. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-burnaby-yourvoice-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3117/4406/8249/2-OCP_PartB_Land_Use_Framework.pdf
- Willingdon Avenue is the west anchor: Confederation Park, the McGill Library and the Eileen Dailly pool cluster sit at Willingdon Avenue and Albert Street. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/confederation-park-community-centre
- Pandora Street carries two of the hill's institutions: Confederation Park Elementary at 4715 Pandora Street and the Capitol Hill Children's Centre at 5750 Pandora Street. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses on the hill sit behind a low-rise main street: the City's land use framework pairs the walkable Hastings Corridor with the residential streets of the Heights and Capitol Hill plan area above it. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-burnaby-yourvoice-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3117/4406/8249/2-OCP_PartB_Land_Use_Framework.pdf
- The current zoning frame is the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, cited in the bylaw as replacing the 1965 bylaw, with R1 as the Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing District (SSMUH). https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-07/Zoning-Bylaw-Consolidated-Without-Schedules.pdf
- One Hastings-fronting lot shows the transition stock: 4472 Hastings Street, a former service station site, was drawn for about 5,760 square feet of retail plus 34 strata units under a Comprehensive Development (CD) zone. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/FileStream.ashx?DocumentId=69510

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Which elementary schools serve Capitol Hill houses?
**A:** Two School District No. 41 (Burnaby) schools are named for the hill: Capitol Hill Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 7, at 350 Holdom Avenue, and Confederation Park Elementary, Kindergarten to Grade 7, at 4715 Pandora Street. Which one takes a given house is a catchment question, so confirm the exact address, for example on Cambridge Street or Eton Street, with the district before subjects come off. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041

**Q:** What is in Confederation Park for a house buyer on the hill?
**A:** The City calls it one of Burnaby's largest parks, with softball, lawn bowling and skateboarding, a 400 square metre (m2) plaza-style water play area, and the heritage-designated cenotaph at its north end, listed on Burnaby's Community Heritage Register. The Eileen Dailly Centre adds a beach-entry leisure pool and a main pool, beside the McGill Branch library off Albert Street. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/confederation-park

**Q:** Is the new Confederation Park Community Centre going ahead?
**A:** Not on its original timetable: the City project page states the Confederation Park Community Centre replacement project has been deferred. The plan was a new three-level facility at Willingdon Avenue and Albert Street, retaining the McGill Library, built 2001, and the Eileen Dailly pool, built 1992, and replacing the 1971 seniors centre inside the new building. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/confederation-park-community-centre

**Q:** How do I reach Simon Fraser University (SFU) or downtown from the hill?
**A:** On the R5 Hastings Street RapidBus, which TransLink lists as running from Burrard Station to SFU along Hastings Street, past the foot of Capitol Hill. TransLink describes RapidBus as limited-stop service with transit priority, and its fall 2026 service changes add service on the R5 for SFU trips. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Confederation Park's cenotaph, at the park's north end, holds a Heritage Designation and is listed on Burnaby's Community Heritage Register. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/confederation-park
- The McGill Branch library at Albert and Alpha streets sits kitty-corner to the Eileen Dailly Leisure Pool and Fitness Centre, inside the park edge. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/confederation-park
- The Confederation Community Centre for seniors, built in 1971 and expanded in 1988, is the building slated for replacement inside the deferred new complex. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/confederation-park-community-centre
- Capitol Hill Children's Centre, at Capitol Hill Elementary, 5750 Pandora Street, provides 25 child care spaces and is run by the Lower Mainland Purpose Society. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/seniors-and-family-support/children-and-child-care
- The R2 Marine-Willingdon RapidBus now runs to Metrotown, giving Willingdon Avenue at Hastings a one-seat ride between the North Shore and Metrotown every 6 to 7 minutes at weekday peak. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects

## 8. GETTING AROUND

- The R2 Marine-Willingdon RapidBus extension, launched September 7, adds a frequent all-day link from Willingdon Avenue to Park Royal and Metrotown. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects
- Local streets on the hill were studied as one system: the North Burnaby Traffic Calming Study covered Burnaby Heights and Capitol Hill together for pedestrian, cyclist and driver safety. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects?page=3

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the slope on the specific street: the 4472 Hastings Street file records even a main-street site sloping about 3 feet west to east, and hill lots above Hastings slope more. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/FileStream.ashx?DocumentId=69510
- Check which zoning bylaw version applies to a rebuild: the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (Bylaw No. 14815) renames districts, including R1 for small-scale multi-unit housing, so an old R5 or RS reference on a listing may be stale. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-07/Zoning-Bylaw-Consolidated-Without-Schedules.pdf
- Check the deferred centre's effect on Albert Street plans: the Confederation Park Community Centre project is deferred, so do not price in a new facility opening beside Willingdon Avenue. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/confederation-park-community-centre
