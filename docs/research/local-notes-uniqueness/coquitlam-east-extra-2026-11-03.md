## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Coquitlam East is the established east side of the City of Coquitlam, around David Avenue, Coast Meridian Road and the Eagle Ridge and Lansdowne Drive slopes below Burke Mountain. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision
- It sits with Central Coquitlam west, Burke Mountain and Northeast Coquitlam (Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek, Partington Creek) north-east, Port Coquitlam across the Coquitlam River east, and Port Moody west; no City page read states an exact board boundary. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision
- "Eagle Ridge" and "Lansdowne" are the local names used on these streets, while "Coquitlam East" is the Greater Vancouver REALTORS® (GVR) sub-area name. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Lansdowne Drive address? **A:** Eagle Ridge Elementary, Scott Creek Middle and Gleneagle Secondary serve the sample address 1195 Lansdowne Drive. Gleneagle Secondary is itself at 1195 Lansdowne Drive and Scott Creek Middle at 1240 Lansdowne Drive, so this catchment cluster sits inside the neighbourhood; School District No. 43 (SD43) catchments are still address-based, so run the exact house address in the locator. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** Does the Burke Mountain plan cover my lot? **A:** Not automatically. The Northeast Coquitlam neighbourhood plans — Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek and Partington Creek — cover the north-east part of the city above this sub-area, and no Coquitlam East plan was confirmed in City sources. A lot's permissions come from its zoning, its development permit areas and the city-wide Official Community Plan (OCP), not from a Burke Mountain plan name. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision
- **Q:** Why does the creek setback differ on the east side? **A:** Because the City sets a wider permit area in the north-east. The Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area reaches 50 metres from the top of bank of any watercourse in Northeast Coquitlam, versus 30 metres elsewhere in Coquitlam, and Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 sections 519 and 523 add flood and riparian setbacks. Ravine lots here need the permit check earlier, not later. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Q:** Which station do east-side residents use? **A:** Coquitlam Central Station is the main station for east Coquitlam, on the Millennium Line (Evergreen Extension) at Lougheed Highway and Pinetree Way, and it also carries West Coast Express service. Interior-street bus route numbers were not confirmed on a TransLink page read, so check the station page for the current route list for a specific street. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/station/coquitlam-central/schedule

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Three SD43 schools stand within the sub-area's streets: Eagle Ridge Elementary, 1215 Falcon Drive; Scott Creek Middle, 1240 Lansdowne Drive; Gleneagle Secondary, 1195 Lansdowne Drive. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- David Avenue and Coast Meridian Road are the east-west and north-south spines; Lougheed Highway is the southern through-route to Coquitlam Central Station. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision
- City Centre recreation — the City Centre Aquatic Complex and Town Centre Park — sits west of the sub-area in the City Centre, not inside Coquitlam East. https://www.coqlibrary.ca/
- Coquitlam Public Library's City Centre branch, 1169 Pinetree Way, and Poirier branch, 575 Poirier Street, serve the city, with the Library Link mobile service. https://www.coqlibrary.ca/
- Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody, is the Fraser Health hospital on the Port Moody / Coquitlam border serving this area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Apply the 50-metre Northeast Coquitlam watercourse permit distance, not the 30-metre city standard, when a lot sits near a creek on the sub-area's north-east edge — confirm which side of that line the parcel falls on with the City. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 section 519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and section 523 covers riparian areas; both matter on the Coquitlam River side and the ravine streets. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
