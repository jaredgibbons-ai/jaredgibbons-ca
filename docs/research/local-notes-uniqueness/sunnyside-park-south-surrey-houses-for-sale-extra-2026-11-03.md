# Sunnyside Park Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: sunnyside-park-south-surrey-houses-for-sale (Sunnyside Park, South Surrey; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sunnyside Park the place is anchored by Sunnyside Park itself at 15455 – 26 Avenue, a 6-hectare (15-acre) neighbourhood park in South Surrey with vehicle access from 154 Street and from Cranley Drive. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/sunnyside-park
- “Sunnyside Park” is the board sub-area name; the City uses “Sunnyside” for facilities here, including Sunnyside Park and Bakerview Park on 154 Street — there is no City plan boundary for this sub-area name. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bakerview-park
- Do not confuse it with Sunnyside Heights: that is a separate Grandview Heights plan area bordered by 168 Street in the east, 24 Avenue in the north and the Highway 99 Corridor Plan in the west, with the Fergus Watershed Biodiversity Preserve to the south. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The detached stock is the established housing around Sunnyside Park (15455 – 26 Avenue) and Bakerview Park on 154 Street, north of the Semiahmoo Town Centre — the City plan that governs new density nearby is the town centre plan, not a Sunnyside Park subdivision plan. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SemiahmooPlanSummary.pdf
- Sunnyside Park's own northeast corner holds a grove of second-growth Douglas-fir trees, and the park provides the outdoor pool, sports fields, softball diamonds, tennis courts and two playgrounds the surrounding houses use. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/sunnyside-park
- Sunnyside Heights, by contrast, is a planned 153-hectare (379-acre) neighbourhood concept plan (NCP) area in Grandview Heights — its newer planned stock is not the Sunnyside Park stock on this page. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The neighbouring plan that changes density near Sunnyside Park houses is the Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan: its summary covers a 136-hectare (336-acre) plan area in the established heart of South Surrey. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SemiahmooPlanSummary.pdf
- Sunnyside Heights' land use designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) update — that statement is about the Grandview Heights plan area, and a buyer should not apply Sunnyside Heights NCP densities to a Sunnyside Park house lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Sunnyside Park the same as Sunnyside Heights? **A:** No. Sunnyside Heights is a separate plan area in Grandview Heights, bordered by 168 Street, 24 Avenue and the Highway 99 Corridor Plan, totalling about 153 hectares. Sunnyside Park is the established South Surrey area around Sunnyside Park at 15455 – 26 Avenue and Bakerview Park on 154 Street, well west of 168 Street. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp
- **Q:** What did the City just rebuild at Sunnyside Park? **A:** The ball diamonds. The City states replacement of Fields #1 to #4 at Sunnyside Park was completed in spring 2026 to improve safety and playability, with new irrigated turfgrass, foul-line and home-run fencing, team dugouts, bleacher seating and field lighting for Field #1 for evening play. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/sunnyside-park
- **Q:** Which town centre plan affects houses just south of here? **A:** The Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan. Its summary describes a 136-hectare plan area guiding growth and development in the established heart of South Surrey, south of the Sunnyside Park houses around 24 Avenue and 152 Street. Confirm a specific lot's designation in that plan before assuming town-centre density reaches it. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SemiahmooPlanSummary.pdf
- **Q:** Is there a forest park within walking distance of these houses? **A:** Yes — Sunnyside Acres Urban Forest Park, reached from 144 Street and 24 Avenue west of the Sunnyside Park houses, is the City's urban forest park for this part of South Surrey. Check the City park page for its trail entrances before assuming a specific street connects directly into it. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/sunnyside-acres-urban-forest-park

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Sunnyside Park is open dawn until dusk, at 15455 – 26 Avenue, and its parking is reached from both 154 Street and Cranley Drive — two different house streets feed the same park. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/sunnyside-park
- Bakerview Park on 154 Street carries the Sunnyside name in City facility listings, so “Sunnyside” on a City page can mean Bakerview Park or Sunnyside Community Hall, not the board sub-area boundary. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bakerview-park
- The South Surrey Recreation & Arts Centre at 14601 20 Avenue is the recreation centre serving Sunnyside Park houses, west along 20 Avenue from 154 Street. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/south-surrey-recreation-arts-centre

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 321 (Surrey Central Station to White Rock) on King George Boulevard and 152 Street is the TransLink spine east of Sunnyside Park houses; the City's Sunnyside Heights report notes the closest bus route to that separate plan area is on 24 Avenue, nearly one kilometre north of 20 Avenue. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt321.pdf
- Highway 99 is reached south and east via 16 Avenue and King George Boulevard — the Sunnyside Heights NCP's west border is the Highway 99 Corridor Plan, which shows how close the highway corridor sits to this part of South Surrey. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check whether a specific lot falls under the Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan or the Surrey 2050 OCP only, because the Sunnyside Heights NCP — often confused with this area — does not apply here: its area is bordered by 168 Street, 24 Avenue and the Highway 99 Corridor Plan. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/sunnyside-heights-ncp
- For lots backing onto Sunnyside Acres Urban Forest Park or Sunnyside Park, confirm tree and park-edge rules on the City park pages; those pages state park hours and amenities, not the tree-protection rules for a private lot. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/sunnyside-acres-urban-forest-park
