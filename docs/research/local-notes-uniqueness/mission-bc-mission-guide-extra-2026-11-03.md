# Mission Area Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: mission-bc-mission-guide (Mission; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Mission sits on the north bank of the Fraser River, with its downtown and waterfront below 7th Avenue and the Waterfront Comprehensive Planning Area designated in the 2020 Official Community Plan (OCP) update. https://www.mission.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/Waterfront-Revitalization-Master-Plan_0.pdf
- The city's Welton Common office is at 7337 Welton Street downtown, the address the City uses for Official Community Plan engagement drop-offs. https://engage.mission.ca/media/file/phase-2-engagement-public-workbook
- Mission's growth core in the draft OCP work is Lower Mission south of 7th Avenue plus the Waterfront, identified as the Core Area for prioritised future growth. https://engage.mission.ca/media/file/phase-2-engagement-public-workbook
Read: 2026-11-03

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Mission's housing is directed by plan, not by a single subdivision: the Waterfront, Downtown and Hospital Hub are identified for the greatest intensity of future development to support transit service. https://engage.mission.ca/media/file/phase-2-engagement-public-workbook
- The Waterfront itself is described by the City as the largest undeveloped river frontage in the Lower Mainland, so most waterfront housing is future plan area rather than built stock today. http://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/waterfront-revitalization-master-plan
- A potential future Transit-Oriented Development Area on Lougheed Highway in Silverdale is identified at the heart of a large Community Node in the OCP engagement material. https://engage.mission.ca/media/file/phase-2-engagement-public-workbook
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Mission's current plan framework is Official Community Plan Bylaw 5670-2017, amended to incorporate the Waterfront Revitalization Master Plan as a Reference Plan for the Waterfront Comprehensive Planning Area. https://www.mission.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/Waterfront-Revitalization-Master-Plan_0.pdf
- Zoning is under City of Mission Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020, cited in the City's 2025 zoning amendment packages for waterfront-area properties accessed from London Avenue. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rao25-6
- For city-wide zoning and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) detail, see the mission-farms note — this guide note does not repeat it.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What is the Mission Waterfront plan, in plain terms? **A:** It is a 40-year set of long-term strategies inside the City's Official Community Plan for the riverfront between Mission Raceway and the Canadian Pacific Railway bridge. The City says it aims to deliver jobs, a unified urban heart between Downtown and the Waterfront, and renewed natural spaces, with new homes, shops and restaurants over time. http://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/waterfront-revitalization-master-plan
- **Q:** Where will most new density go in Mission? **A:** The draft Official Community Plan engagement material identifies the Waterfront, Downtown and Hospital Hub for the greatest intensity of future development, and the entirety of Lower Mission south of 7th Avenue plus the Waterfront as the Core Area where growth is prioritised, specifically to support transit service. https://engage.mission.ca/media/file/phase-2-engagement-public-workbook
- **Q:** Which hospital serves Mission? **A:** Mission Memorial Hospital at 7324 Hurd Street is Fraser Health's hospital for Mission. Buyers near Hurd Street should expect hospital traffic and services clustered there, and should confirm current services on Fraser Health's own location page rather than relying on a listing description. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Mission/mission-memorial-hospital#.X855oshKhaQ
- **Q:** Is there a university campus in Mission? **A:** Yes — the University of the Fraser Valley (UFV) Mission campus was revitalised and reopened in September 2024 as the home of UFV's School of Education. The Province states the two-storey building includes a library and community spaces on the main floor, with 264 seats in teacher and early childhood education training. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024psfs0043-001450
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Mission Memorial Hospital is at 7324 Hurd Street, not on First Avenue — the Hurd Street cluster is the health-services address buyers should map. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Mission/mission-memorial-hospital#.X855oshKhaQ
- École Heritage Park Middle School (School District 75, grades 7–9) is at 33700 Prentis Avenue, the same Prentis Avenue address area as the UFV Mission campus. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075
- Mission Senior Secondary (grades 10–12) is at 32939 7th Avenue, and Mission Central Elementary (Kindergarten to grade 6) is at 7466 Welton Street. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075
- Mission City Station's Park and Ride is at 7218 Mershon Street with 254 spaces, a $3 daily rate and $60 monthly rate on TransLink's page. https://www.translink.ca/parkandride?utm_source=direct&utm_medium=shorturl&utm_campaign=parkandride
- The UFV Mission campus renewal was funded with $8 million total — $2.3 million from the Province and $5.7 million from UFV — and was designed in partnership with local Stó:lō community leaders. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024psfs0043-001450

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- The Waterfront Revitalization Master Plan (June 2022) is the city's major long-term project: strategies integrated within the Official Community Plan to guide waterfront growth over the next 40 years. http://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/waterfront-revitalization-master-plan
- The City is reviewing its city-wide Official Community Plan — the Let's Plan Mission process — with draft directions identifying the Waterfront, Downtown and Hospital Hub for greatest intensity. https://engage.mission.ca/media/file/phase-2-engagement-public-workbook
- The UFV Mission campus revitalisation was completed and announced September 6, 2024, making the campus the new home for UFV's School of Education. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024psfs0043-001450
- Rural Mission community-level projects are covered in the durieu, dewdney-deroche, lake-errock, stave-falls and hatzic notes — this city guide does not repeat them.

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- City-wide Mission tax and utility figures are written in full in the mission-farms note — see the mission-farms note for the rate and charges.
- Mission is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT): the Province lists the District of Mission among taxable areas, so residential owners there must complete the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- The Mission City Park and Ride cost is area-specific for commuters: $3 daily and $60 monthly at 7218 Mershon Street on TransLink's page. https://www.translink.ca/parkandride?utm_source=direct&utm_medium=shorturl&utm_campaign=parkandride
Read: 2026-11-03

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Mission City Station is Mission's West Coast Express terminus, with its Park and Ride at 7218 Mershon Street (254 spaces, hours 4 a.m. to 2 a.m., 7 days per week). https://www.translink.ca/parkandride?utm_source=direct&utm_medium=shorturl&utm_campaign=parkandride
- Mission City Station parking is not overnight: TransLink states none of its Park and Ride locations permit overnight parking, and Mission City's lot hours are 4 a.m. to 2 a.m., 7 days per week. https://www.translink.ca/parkandride?utm_source=direct&utm_medium=shorturl&utm_campaign=parkandride
- Highway access is via Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and the Abbotsford–Mission Highway (Highway 11) corridor — drive times to Vancouver are UNCONFIRMED and are not stated here.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Waterfront and low-lying Mission lots sit behind a dike on the Fraser River: the Waterfront plan itself notes the dike protecting the city from Fraser River flooding, so a buyer should check the parcel against the City's flood and dike information for the Waterfront Comprehensive Planning Area. https://www.mission.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/Waterfront-Revitalization-Master-Plan_0.pdf
- The Waterfront Master Plan states it preserves and enhances natural elements and improves ecological integrity where diminished — a buyer planning waterfront work should expect environmental review, not a simple building permit. http://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/waterfront-revitalization-master-plan
- Parcel-level floodplain, Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) and servicing status for a specific Mission lot is UNCONFIRMED in this note — check it on the City's mapping before subject removal.
