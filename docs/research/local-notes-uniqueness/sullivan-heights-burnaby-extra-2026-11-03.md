# Sullivan Heights Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: sullivan-heights-burnaby (Sullivan Heights; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sullivan Heights sits north of the Lougheed Highway and east of Government Road, below the SFU campus, with North Road as its eastern edge against Coquitlam. The City of Burnaby's Heritage Planning record for the neighbouring Lyndhurst area places the Sullivan Heights single-family subdivision east of Stoney Creek Park, inside the Lougheed Town Centre area. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark823
- Stoney Creek runs through the middle of the area, splitting the older subdivision in the east from the multi-family streets around Cameron Street and Beaverbrook Crescent in the west. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark823
- The area's civic address anchor is 9855 Austin Avenue — The City of Lougheed shopping centre site, where the temporary Cameron Community Centre now operates at unit 110. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2024-09-03/temporary-cameron-community-centre-now-open

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The original Sullivan Heights subdivision is 1950s single-family housing, built east of Stoney Creek Park after the Lougheed Highway was completed in 1953. Heritage Burnaby's Burquitlam record dates the subdivision to that decade and notes Lyndhurst School opened in 1954 to serve it. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?ps=50&q=&onlineMediaType_facet=Image&onlineMediaType_facet=Video&decade_facet=1940&decade_facet=1730&p=1
- West of Stoney Creek Park the stock changes: Heritage Burnaby records a higher-density residential area of multi-family buildings developed after 1974. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark823
- The newest stock is The City of Lougheed: developer SHAPE states Neighbourhood One is complete with 1,500 homes, a 22,000-plus square foot amenity facility and retail anchored by PriceSmart Foods, on the 37-acre site at 9855 Austin Avenue. https://shape.ca/portfolio/the-city-of-lougheed/
- Cameron Elementary, the area's in-neighbourhood SD41 school, is at 9540 Erickson Drive. https://burnabyschools.ca/elementary-school-addresses/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Lougheed Town Centre Plan framework governs the tower lands, and the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP), adopted December 9, 2025, now takes precedence over older community-plan land-use maps where they conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- A new Tenant Protection Bylaw now covers renters displaced by redevelopment in areas like this one; the City states it replaced the Tenant Assistance Policy in place until June 30, 2026. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/programs-and-policies/housing
- Streamside rules bite along Stoney Creek: development proposed within 30 metres of the top of bank triggers the City's Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area review — see the simon-fraser-hills-burnaby note for the full stream-setback item. https://burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2021-07/Guide%20for%20Developing%20Near%20Streams.pdf
- Detached lots on the 1950s streets carry R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zoning — see the suncrest-burnaby note for the full rules and the willingdon-heights-burnaby note for the transit-area unit counts.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Cameron Park's recreation centre open during construction? **A:** No — the old Cameron Recreation Complex at 9523 Cameron Street closed on August 18, 2024, and programs moved to the Temporary Cameron Community Centre inside The City of Lougheed Shopping Centre at 110-9855 Austin Avenue. The City expects construction of the replacement to take about three years from fall 2024, with work mainly Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/cameron-community-centre-and-library/questions-and-answers
- **Q:** Which SkyTrain lines actually serve Lougheed Town Centre Station? **A:** Three lines meet at the station beside this area, according to developer SHAPE: the site is described as a rare 37-acre location where three SkyTrain lines meet. That is why The City of Lougheed master plan plans more than 20 towers and over 10,000 residents on the former mall lands at 9855 Austin Avenue. https://shape.ca/portfolio/the-city-of-lougheed/
- **Q:** Does a Sullivan Heights house have a suite charge on its utility bill? **A:** A rented suite inside a single-family dwelling has its own 2026 utility lines: $326.17 for water and $403.00 for sewer use, separate from the main house charge. The City also gives a 5% discount on utility rates paid in full by the due date. Check whether a suite is registered before assuming that charge is lawful income support. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves the Cameron Street side? **A:** Sullivan Heights sits in SD41's Cariboo-Lougheed zone, whose secondary schools are Burnaby Mountain, at 8800 Eastlake Drive, and Cariboo Hill. Cameron Elementary at 9540 Erickson Drive is in that zone's elementary list. The zone grouping does not replace the address-level catchment, so run the civic address through SD41's School Locator before relying on either school in an offer. https://mountain.burnabyschools.ca/blog/2017/11/07/sogi-education-in-burnaby-schools-parent-info-session-november-22nd/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The temporary Cameron library opened April 29, 2024, inside the shopping centre, after the 9523 Cameron Street library closed March 29, 2024 — library users now go to the mall, not Cameron Park. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2024-02-14/cameron-library-move-temporary-location-spring
- Cameron Park's spray park stays open during construction, but the City warns street parking for it is limited while site fencing and demolition proceed. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2024-09-03/temporary-cameron-community-centre-now-open
- A new two-directional, 3-metre-wide cycle track is being built with the Cameron project, connecting existing bike routes at Beaverbrook Drive and Morey Court. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/cameron-community-centre-and-library/questions-and-answers
- Stoney Creek Park, at the area's west edge, has 2 tennis and 2 pickleball courts listed by the City. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/outdoor-courts

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- The City of Lougheed, 9855 Austin Avenue, developer SHAPE Properties: 37-acre master plan, 20-plus towers, 1.3 million square feet of retail, 5-plus acres of parks, over 10,000 homes at completion. STATUS: Neighbourhood One complete (1,500 homes, including Tower Four, a 237-home rental tower); later neighbourhoods in sales and planning; read 2026-11-03. https://shape.ca/portfolio/the-city-of-lougheed/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby's city-wide 2026 residential tax rate and detached-home utility charges are covered in full in the simon-fraser-hills-burnaby note; this note adds the suite and strata figures that apply on these streets.
- A strata or multi-family unit's 2026 charges are $374.54 for water and $436.00 for sewer use — the figures a City of Lougheed tower buyer will see, before the 5% early-payment discount. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- 2026 is the last year the Sewer Parcel Tax appears on the property tax notice; from 2027 all water and sewer charges combine on the utility notice. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in the City of Burnaby, listed by the Province as a Metro Vancouver taxable area — see the simon-fraser-hills-burnaby note. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Lougheed Town Centre Station is this area's rapid-transit hub, on the lands SHAPE is rebuilding as The City of Lougheed at 9855 Austin Avenue. https://shape.ca/portfolio/the-city-of-lougheed/
- The 145 bus to SFU leaves from Production Way–University Station, one stop west, via Broadway and Gaglardi Way — the route SFU-bound riders from Cameron Street use after a short SkyTrain hop. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt145.pdf
- The 110 bus (Lougheed Station to Metrotown Station) runs the Lougheed Highway along the area's south edge; its route name is listed in TransLink's route network. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/146/direction/1/map

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Stoney Creek is the named hazard check here: the City's Riparian Management Areas geographic layer flags lots crossed by a 10-metre or 30-metre Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area, and Stoney Creek Park's subdivision edge is exactly where those lots sit. https://gis.burnaby.ca/arcgis/rest/services/Other/BBOT/MapServer/25
- Construction exposure is the second check: the Cameron site at 9523 Cameron Street and the multi-phase City of Lougheed site both run for years, and the City's own Q&A sets Cameron construction hours at 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. on weekdays — the page does not tell you a specific lot's noise or shadowing outcome. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/cameron-community-centre-and-library/questions-and-answers
- For a tower purchase, the City pages do not state a building's rental or pet bylaws; those come from the strata's registered bylaws and Form B, which no municipal page publishes.
