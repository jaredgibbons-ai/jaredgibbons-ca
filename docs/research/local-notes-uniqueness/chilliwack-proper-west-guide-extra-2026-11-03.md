# Chilliwack Proper West Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: chilliwack-proper-west-guide (Chilliwack Proper West; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Chilliwack Proper West has no official boundary: it is a Chilliwack and District Real Estate Board (CADREB) sub-area name for the western part of Chilliwack Proper, and the City of Chilliwack publishes no boundary for it. https://www.cadreb.com/market/
- The working orientation is the grid west of the downtown core, around Young Road, Corbould Street and the airport side of Chilliwack Proper, taken from the addresses of the schools and facilities on that side rather than from any City map. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/033
- The closest official planning area is larger than the sub-area: the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan covers the established residential area of Chilliwack Proper as a whole, and it is Schedule B2 of Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- Its neighbours are Chilliwack Proper East and downtown Chilliwack (District 1881) to the east, Fairfield Island to the north, and Sardis and Vedder Crossing south of Highway 1. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=136&backTo=%26dowhat%3DamenityView%26paID%3D29%26plID%3D130
- Townsend Park, a 15-hectare City park west of downtown, is the area's western green anchor. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=136&backTo=%26dowhat%3DamenityView%26paID%3D29%26plID%3D130

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is an older urban grid, not a single subdivision: the neighbourhoods plan designations that cover it are Residential 1 (one and two family housing) and Residential 2 (attached housing and narrow lot infill), quoted in a City public hearing notice dated May 4, 2021 for a rezoning inside the plan area. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- Detached houses and ranchers on the older streets sit alongside low-rise condominium buildings on Robson Street, Cook Street and Brett Avenue and townhome units on Wolfe Road, all named in the guide page's own reading of the area. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/033
- Infill is the live building story on this side: the Schedule B2 plan's stated purpose is to guide future decision making on residential infill development, and it was first added to the 2040 Official Community Plan (OCP) as Schedule D through Bylaw 2018, No. 4533. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- No official source publishes a build-era or strata-versus-freehold split for this sub-area, so any age profile for a specific street is a property-by-property check, not a neighbourhood figure. https://www.cadreb.com/market/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Flood rules come first on this side of the city: under the City's Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519, the underside of the lowest habitable floor must be built to the Flood Construction Level (FCL) plus 0.6 metres of freeboard, and no basement is allowed (a crawlspace to 1.5 metres deep is the maximum, subject to drainage rules). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- The 0.6 metre freeboard can be waived only if the owner registers a Section 219 covenant on title and provides a qualified professional's report that the property may be safely used for the intended purpose. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- A suite here is capped and singular: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 allows only 1 accessory dwelling unit per lot, a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 square metres and 40% of the building's habitable floor space, and a garden suite is capped at 100 square metres. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- A suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level — the rule that bites hardest in this floodplain-side grid. https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- City-wide rules that also apply here — the small-scale multi-unit housing position in Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 and the zoning bylaw itself — are written in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note; this note carries only the Proper West angle. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- Short-term rentals are a regulated operation under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141, adopted November 24, 2021, which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- Creek-side lots can trigger a second permit layer: Development Permit Area 3 (Riparian Areas) requires a Riparian Areas Protection Regulation assessment report from a qualified professional before subdivision, construction or land alteration in a designated riparian area. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Where exactly is Chilliwack Proper West? **A:** It is the part of Chilliwack Proper west of the downtown core, around Young Road, Corbould Street and the airport side of the city. That is an orientation taken from school and facility addresses, not a boundary: the City publishes no boundary for the sub-area name, and the planning document that covers the ground is the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan, Schedule B2 of the 2050 OCP. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Proper West address? **A:** The schools on this side are Bernard Elementary at 45465 Bernard Avenue, Chilliwack Central Elementary Community at 9435 Young Road, McCammon Elementary at 9601 Hamilton Street and Leary Integrated Arts & Technology at 9320 Walden Street, all Kindergarten to Grade 5, plus Kw'íyeqel Secondary at 8855 Elm Drive for Grades 9 to 12. Chilliwack School District 33 (SD33) assigns by street address, so confirm the exact address in the SD33 School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/033
- **Q:** Can I add a suite or a coach house to a house here? **A:** One accessory dwelling unit per lot is the limit where the zone permits it: a secondary suite capped at the lesser of 90 square metres and 40% of the building's habitable floor space, a coach house, or a garden suite capped at 100 square metres. The unit needs a building permit, must meet the BC Building and Fire Code and flood protection requirements, and a new suite cannot sit below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q:** Is this area in the floodplain? **A:** Much of the city is: the City states that much of Chilliwack lies in the floodplain of the Fraser River to the north, the Vedder River to the south, the Sumas River to the west or one of several creeks entering the valley from the hillsides. For any one lot, the working rule is the Flood Construction Level plus 0.6 metres of freeboard under Bylaw No. 4519, and the parcel's own FCL is a City check before subjects are removed. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- **Q:** How do I get to Metro Vancouver without driving? **A:** BC Transit Route 66, the Fraser Valley Express, runs from Chilliwack to Lougheed Town Centre Station in Burnaby, with Chilliwack stops at Downtown Chilliwack, Vedder Road at Luckakuck Way and the Lickman Park and Ride, and BC Transit expanded Route 66 trips on all days of the week from January 4, 2026. Local routes 52 Evans and 57 Broadway connect this side of the city to the Downtown Chilliwack exchange. https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The area's recreation centre is the Chilliwack Landing Leisure Centre at Unit #1, 9145 Corbould Street, with pools, a fitness centre, an outdoor water playground, a skateboard park and a pump track, with The Landing park beside it. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2246
- Hope River Corbould Park at 9899 Corbould Street and Evergreen Hall at 9291 Corbould Street put a river-side park and a community hall on the same Corbould Street corridor as the leisure centre. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2246
- Chilliwack General Hospital, the city's community hospital at 45600 Menholm Road, sits on this west side of Chilliwack Proper, in the Hodgins Avenue and Menholm Road precinct. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital
- Route 59 Industrial is a peak-period service only: the guide page records two morning trips from Downtown Chilliwack and two afternoon trips from Cottonwood Mall, so it is not an all-day option for a car-free household. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=52
- SD33 runs a three-tier structure here — elementary Kindergarten to Grade 5, middle Grades 6 to 8 and secondary Grades 9 to 12 — and no SD33 locator run for a Proper West address is on record in this program, so a listing's school claim is unverified until the address is run. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- The nearest library branch is outside the sub-area: Chilliwack Library at 45860 First Avenue, in the downtown core to the east. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations
- A legal suite or coach house gets its own civic address through the City's alias address process, which requires an application form and a site plan showing where the suite or coach house sits. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2235

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The City of Chilliwack's 2026 residential property-tax rate is 4.66727 per $1,000 of assessed value in total — see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note for the full rate breakdown, utility charges and billing cycle. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2527/Tax%5FRates%5Ffor%5Fthe%5FWeb%2Dmou9gntw%5Fc%2Epdf
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies here: the Province's taxable-areas list names the City of Chilliwack, so residential owners in Proper West file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- City utility and solid waste charges on this side are billed quarterly, under the Waterworks Regulation Bylaw 2004, No. 2995, the Sanitary Sewer System Regulation Bylaw 2010, No. 3702 and the Solid Waste Management Bylaw 2019, No. 4660. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- There is no rapid transit in Chilliwack: the closest SkyTrain connection is by bus, on BC Transit Route 66 Fraser Valley Express to Lougheed Town Centre Station. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=66
- Route 52 Evans runs from Downtown Chilliwack to Cottonwood Mall via Evans Road, with a timing point at Hodgins Avenue at Ashwell Road, serving the south-western streets of this area. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=52
- Route 57 Broadway runs from Downtown Chilliwack to Cottonwood Mall via Young Road, with timing points near the airport and at Young Road. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=57&schedule=2024-06-30
- BC Transit added Sunday-evening service on Routes 52, 53, 54, 57 and 58 and expanded Route 66 on all days of the week in the January 4, 2026 service change. https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the flood layer: Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519 sets construction elevation requirements for new development in the floodplain, and the City's guide page for the bylaw is the first document to read for a Proper West lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- Alluvial fan areas are a separate check on the creek-side streets: a site-specific assessment by a qualified professional is required to set building setback and elevation, and an engineered concrete foundation must extend a minimum of 1.0 metre above grade at any point on the building perimeter. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- An addition can lose its exemption at a set size: the bylaw's elevation requirements apply to a renovation or addition that increases a building's size at its original non-conforming elevation by 25% or more of the floor area that existed at the adoption of the previous floodplain bylaw in March 1997. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- What the City pages read do not tell a buyer: the Flood Construction Level for a specific lot — that figure is parcel-specific and comes from the City's Development and Regulatory Services Department, not from a neighbourhood page.
