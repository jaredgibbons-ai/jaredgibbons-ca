# Whonnock Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: whonnock-guide (Whonnock, Maple Ridge; Guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Whonnock is Maple Ridge's rural east edge, and the City publishes no boundary for the neighbourhood or the board sub-area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods/whonnock
- The working scope runs from about 264 Street east to the Mission border at 288 Street, between the Fraser River lowland and the northern hills. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods/whonnock
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and Dewdney Trunk Road are the east-west roads, with 272 Street, 280 Street and 112 Avenue as the local grid. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt749.pdf
- The City lists Selvey Park, Whonnock Lake Park and Whonnock Lake Centre as the Whonnock facilities on its neighbourhood page. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods/whonnock

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is freehold detached houses on acreages, not strata housing, with barns, workshops and horse facilities on the larger parcels. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods/whonnock
- Zone minimum lot areas under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 run from RS-2 at 0.4 hectares and RS-3 at 0.8 or 2.0 hectares to A-1 at 2.0 hectares, A-2 at 4.0 hectares and A-3 at 8.0 hectares. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- No condominium building or townhome complex in Whonnock was confirmed on an official, developer or strata source read for this note. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods/whonnock
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- East Maple Ridge rural rules, not urban rules, govern most Whonnock lots (full detail here; the albion-guide note points back to this note for shared east-side context). https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- One secondary suite per lot is permitted in zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, inside the detached house, to 40% of gross floor area, and not in the floodplain area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- On Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) land the provincial ALR Use Regulation also applies: one secondary suite in the principal residence under section 31, and an additional residence only if the section 32 conditions are met. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- No Whonnock area plan exists; the Official Community Plan (OCP) and its rural designations govern, updated November 25, 2025. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Is Whonnock on city sewer and water? **A:** No municipal sewer should be assumed on a Whonnock acreage, so the septic system and well are core diligence items, not closing details. Wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database, and an onsite sewerage system must meet the Sewerage System Regulation, including the 30-metre setback from a well unless professional advice is obtained. Pull both records before subject removal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
**Q:** Can I subdivide a large Whonnock lot? **A:** Only if the zone minimum and the ALR status both allow it, which is a parcel-by-parcel answer. The zoning schedule sets minimums as large as 4.0 hectares in A-2 and 8.0 hectares in A-3, and ALR land is governed by the Agricultural Land Commission, so a large lot alone does not create a subdivision right. Check the zone and the ALR map first. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
**Q:** Which schools serve Whonnock, and is there a bus? **A:** Whonnock Elementary at 27471 112 Avenue is in the area and Garibaldi Secondary at 24789 Dewdney Trunk Road is the east-side secondary school. School District 42 catchments are address-based, and the district lists regular school bus service for specific schools including Whonnock Elementary and Garibaldi Secondary, so confirm both the catchment and bus eligibility for the exact address. https://www.sd42.ca/student-transportation/
**Q:** Is there transit in Whonnock? **A:** Yes, but it is a single loop bus, not a station service. Route 749 runs Ruskin to Haney Place via Dewdney Trunk Road, 284 Street, 116 Avenue and 280 Street, returning via Lougheed Highway, 272 Street and River Road through Whonnock, and there is no West Coast Express station in the area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt749.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Whonnock Lake Park has a small sandy beach, swimming area, hiking trails and a playground, with the Ridge Canoe and Kayak Club based on Whonnock Lake. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods/whonnock
- Whonnock Lake Centre is at 27871 113 Avenue, the local hall for an area with no recreation centre or library branch of its own. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-10/Booklet-WhonnockLakeCentreHandbook-Letter-RECRE-241031-V1.pdf
- The nearest library and leisure centre are in the Town Centre to the west: Maple Ridge Public Library at 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road and the Leisure Centre at 11925 Haney Place. https://www.mapleridge.ca/art-parks-rec/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre
- Route 701's east trips mostly end at Dewdney Trunk Road at 248 Street, west of Whonnock, so route 749 is the in-area bus. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt701.pdf
- A property described as Ruskin in its own text can still sit in the Whonnock board sub-area at the east edge, so search both names. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods/whonnock
- Heritage buildings stand on River Road, 272 Street and the Lougheed Highway frontage; a heritage check belongs in diligence for older houses on those roads. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods/whonnock

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Maple Ridge's 2026 general residential rate is $2.8643 per $1,000 of taxable value — see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note for the full city tax schedule. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- The City of Maple Ridge is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so a Whonnock owner must file the annual declaration even for ALR land with a house. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- Farm-class and ALR status change the tax picture parcel by parcel, so the rate above is the residential starting point only, not a farm bill. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 749 is the only bus routed through Whonnock itself, looping between Ruskin and Haney Place. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt749.pdf
- The nearest West Coast Express station is Port Haney Station at 22300 River Road, west of the area in the Town Centre direction. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) is the through route east to Mission and west to the Town Centre and the Golden Ears Bridge approach. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt749.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check ALR status for the exact parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder before valuing any future use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- Check the well record in GWELLS and the septic filing against the Sewerage System Regulation's 30-metre well setback on every acreage purchase. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004
- Check City floodplain mapping for low land near the Fraser River and Whonnock Creek, because suites are prohibited in the floodplain area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- Neither the ALR map nor the floodplain map states well yield or septic condition; both need property-level records and inspection. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration

## 10. HOW IT COMPARES
- Whonnock lots start at rural zone minimums of 0.4 hectares and up; Albion lots follow the urban RS-1 minimum of 668.0 square metres. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- Whonnock has no area plan; Albion has the Albion Area Plan in OCP Chapter 10.2, adopted in updated form on October 28, 2025. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId=
- Whonnock has an in-area elementary school and no secondary school; Albion has both an elementary school and a secondary school inside its plan area. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/
