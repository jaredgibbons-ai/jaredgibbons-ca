# Vedder Crossing — Questions for Jared

1. In Vedder Crossing and Garrison Crossing, what is the buyer really paying for — the newer planned streets, the river trails, or the UFV and Canada Education Park next door?
2. Who buys here — military and RCMP families connected to the old base and the training centre, UFV staff and students' families, or commuters?
3. Do the Vedder River floodplain rules or the strata rules in the newer complexes come up more in your deals on this side of the river?
4. What surprises buyers most — how much of the area is one master-planned former-base project, the OPSEE training area still operating beside it, or the Keith Wilson Road traffic?
5. Tell one story from a Vedder Crossing deal — a Garrison townhouse, a rental bought for the UFV market, or a sale where the river location decided it.
