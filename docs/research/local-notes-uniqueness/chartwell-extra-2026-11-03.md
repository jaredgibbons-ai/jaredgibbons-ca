# Chartwell Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: chartwell (West Vancouver; neighbourhood — detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Chartwell is a residential enclave in the British Properties, centred on Chartwell Drive above Ambleside in the District of West Vancouver. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/chartwell/
- Its anchors are on one street: Chartwell Elementary at 1300 Chartwell Drive and Sentinel Secondary at 1250 Chartwell Drive sit beside each other on Chartwell Drive itself. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/045
- The District's Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, guides land use district-wide; its named centres are the Ambleside Municipal Town Centre, Park Royal, and the village centres of Dundarave, Caulfeild Village and Horseshoe Bay — Chartwell is not a named District neighbourhood in that plan. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- Nearby enclaves and areas: Canterbury to the west, the upper British Properties benches to the north, Ambleside below Highway 1 to the south, and Park Royal downhill via Taylor Way to the south-east. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- For the 55+ angle on this enclave, see the chartwell-55-plus-homes-for-sale note; this note is the general enclave. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/chartwell/

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is detached houses on hillside lots in the RS single-family dwelling zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010, with secondary suites and detached secondary suites (coach houses) among the permitted uses in those zones. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- Lot size is set zone by zone: Section 200 states minimum site areas of 8,094 square metres in RS1, 1,625 in RS2, 975 in RS3, 731 in RS4 and 488 in RS5, with RS6 using the site of record. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- No condo or townhome complex inside Chartwell is confirmed on an official source; the enclave's named buildings are its two public schools, not residential complexes. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/chartwell/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A Wildfire Hazard Development Permit is required for work within the District's Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area — within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface, an area approved December 14, 2020 that covers about 50 percent of District land. https://westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/natural-environment-and-hazard-development-permit-areas/wildfire-hazard-development-permit
- An Environmental Development Permit is required for development within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse in existing neighbourhoods, and lands in the District's future neighbourhoods generally north of Highway 1 carry a 30-metre setback from a watercourse bank or wetland edge — check which regime a Chartwell lot falls under. https://westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/natural-environment-and-hazard-development-permit-areas/environmental-development-permits
- The Province's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) legislation requires zoning to allow a minimum of 4 units on single-family and duplex parcels over 280 square metres, and 6 units on such parcels near frequent bus service; West Vancouver's local implementing status for these benches is carried in NOT CONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/local-governments-and-housing/housing-initiatives/smale-scale-multi-unit-housing
- On July 21, 2025 the Province directed West Vancouver to amend its OCP for increased density in single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave and at Park Royal–Taylor Way by December 31, 2025; whether Chartwell's lowest streets are inside the "adjacent" areas is a per-property check. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698
- Any British Properties building scheme or covenant sits on the individual title, in addition to District zoning; no covenant term is stated here because none was confirmed. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools are actually in Chartwell? **A:** Two, on the same street. Chartwell Elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 7) is at 1300 Chartwell Drive and Sentinel Secondary (Grades 8 to 12) is at 1250 Chartwell Drive. School District 45 (West Vancouver Schools) has no middle-school tier — elementary runs to Grade 7 and secondary starts at Grade 8 — but catchments are still address-based, so confirm your exact address. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/
- **Q:** Does buying on Chartwell Drive guarantee the Chartwell and Sentinel catchments? **A:** No. School District 45 directs families to the school locator tool on its admissions page to find their local catchment school, because catchments follow the exact address, not the street name or the enclave name. Run the specific address in the locator before writing an offer that depends on either school. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/
- **Q:** Is there a bus on Chartwell Drive itself? **A:** Yes. TransLink's route 254 (British Properties / Park Royal) timetable, effective June 8, 2026, routes buses from Park Royal via Taylor Way, Southborough and Eyremount onto Chartwell and Crestwell before looping through the upper properties to Glenmore. Selected trips continue downtown via the Lions Gate Bridge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt254.pdf
- **Q:** What extra tax should I budget for at British Properties values? **A:** The Province's additional school tax, where the residential assessment passes $3 million. For 2026 it is 0.2 percent on the portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4 percent on the portion above $4 million, rising to 0.3 percent and 0.6 percent on January 1, 2027. It is charged on top of the District tax bill. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Chartwell Elementary is described by School District 45 as sitting in the British Properties overlooking Vancouver and Burrard Inlet, with two playground structures, a natural gravel field, upper soccer and basketball courts, and the Properties Family Hub offering multi-age programming for area residents. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/chartwell/
- The District's school program links the enclave's two schools: Chartwell's Grade 6/7 Pre-AP Capstone Inquiry Project is described as preparation for the Advanced Placement opportunities at Sentinel Secondary next door. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/chartwell/
- Westcot Elementary, 760 Westcot Road, is the neighbouring elementary school at the foot of the British Properties, west of Chartwell. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/chartwell/
- The confirmed waterfront below the hillside is the Centennial Seawalk, built in 1967 and running about 1.7 kilometres from Dundarave Park to Ambleside. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/dundarave-park
- Indoor recreation and the library are downhill in Ambleside: the West Vancouver Community Centre and Aquatic Centre at 2121 Marine Drive, and West Vancouver Memorial Library at 1950 Marine Drive. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/facilities/community-centre
- The hospital serving the North Shore, including West Vancouver, is Lions Gate Hospital at 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5 percent on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes
- About 50 percent of the tax bill is collected by the District and remitted to other taxing authorities. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes
- Water, sewer and garbage utilities are metered and billed quarterly; the District gives a 10 percent discount when payment is received on or before the due date. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- West Vancouver is in a Speculation and Vacancy Tax area administered by the Province; the District's tax page directs owners to the provincial declaration and collection process. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the lot against the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area first: it covers properties within about 100 metres of a forested area — roughly half the District — and the permit process typically takes 4 to 6 weeks. https://westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/natural-environment-and-hazard-development-permit-areas/wildfire-hazard-development-permit
- Check creek setbacks on the District's environmental permit mapping: 15 metres from top of bank in existing neighbourhoods, 30 metres in the future neighbourhoods generally north of Highway 1. https://westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/natural-environment-and-hazard-development-permit-areas/environmental-development-permits
- Pull the title through the Land Title and Survey Authority of British Columbia (LTSA) for British Properties building-scheme or covenant charges before planning a rebuild; those terms are not in the zoning bylaw. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- A Foreshore Development Permit applies to waterfront or coastal-flood-risk property; the District notes it does not ordinarily reach this upper hillside, but confirm per lot. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
