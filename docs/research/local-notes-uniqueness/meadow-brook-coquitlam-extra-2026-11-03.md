## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Meadow Brook is the City's "Meadowbrook" neighbourhood, and the City gives it an exact boundary: south of Dewdney Trunk Road to Greene Street, between Irvine Street and east of Cherrybrook Place. https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/854?arc=1086
- Cherrybrook Place, inside that boundary, is a named street in the replacement zoning schedules, with addresses such as 991, 993, 995 and 997 Cherrybrook Place listed parcel by parcel in the draft Comprehensive Development zone. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-coq-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8316/9139/4004/0f60821c01f71f2185f312c8a6612536_Draft_Meadowbrook_Comprehensive_Development_28CD29_Zone.pdf
- Meadowbrook Elementary, at 900 Sharpe Street, is the addressed school anchor for this side of Coquitlam, on Sharpe Street south of the Dewdney Trunk Road boundary. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Meadowbrook's stock is zero-lot-line one-family housing under its replacement zone: the City's draft Comprehensive Development Zone-29 (CD-29) states its intent is zero-lot-line development for one-family residential uses on fee simple lots, with limited accessory uses. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-coq-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8316/9139/4004/0f60821c01f71f2185f312c8a6612536_Draft_Meadowbrook_Comprehensive_Development_28CD29_Zone.pdf
- The CD-29 lot rules are small-lot rules: a one-family use is not permitted on a lot under 270 square metres, the minimum lot width is 9.0 metres and the minimum lot depth is 30.0 metres. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-coq-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8316/9139/4004/0f60821c01f71f2185f312c8a6612536_Draft_Meadowbrook_Comprehensive_Development_28CD29_Zone.pdf
- Building size is capped in the same zone: the maximum floor area of the principal building on each lot is 225 square metres, lot coverage must not exceed 45 percent, and no more than one principal building is permitted per lot. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-coq-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8316/9139/4004/0f60821c01f71f2185f312c8a6612536_Draft_Meadowbrook_Comprehensive_Development_28CD29_Zone.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Meadowbrook was Coquitlam's last Land Use Contract (LUC) area: the City states Meadowbrook was the last remaining LUC in Coquitlam, and that provincial changes to the Local Government Act required all LUCs in British Columbia to end by June 30, 2024, with new zoning in place by June 30, 2022. https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/854?arc=1086
- The replacement zoning was written to mirror the old contract, not to open the area up: the City states the goals were to protect existing uses and character by mirroring, as closely as possible, what the LUC had allowed on a property. https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/854?arc=1086
- A buyer cannot assume the city-wide small-scale rules apply the same way on a CD-29 lot: the zone is site-specific, it sets its own exterior side-yard setback of a minimum 3.0 metres abutting Dewdney Trunk Road, Irvine Street and Greene Street, and attached party-wall development must be developed together as a single development with a development permit. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-coq-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8316/9139/4004/0f60821c01f71f2185f312c8a6612536_Draft_Meadowbrook_Comprehensive_Development_28CD29_Zone.pdf
- City-wide Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) and Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 facts are in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; whether a specific Meadowbrook lot is in CD-29 or a small-scale zone is a QtheMap check on that lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What is a Land Use Contract, and does one still govern my Meadowbrook lot? **A:** No, the contract itself has ended. The City explains that instead of zoning, a Land Use Contract regulated all properties within a specific boundary area, and Meadowbrook was the last remaining one in Coquitlam. Provincial law required all such contracts to end by June 30, 2024, so a Cherrybrook Place or Greene Street lot is now governed by its replacement zoning. https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/854?arc=1086
- **Q:** How small are Meadowbrook lots allowed to be? **A:** Smaller than a typical Coquitlam house lot, under the draft CD-29 zone the City prepared for this area. It sets a minimum lot area of 270 square metres, a minimum width of 9.0 metres and a minimum depth of 30.0 metres for a one-family use, with a maximum principal-building floor area of 225 square metres and 45 percent lot coverage. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-coq-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8316/9139/4004/0f60821c01f71f2185f312c8a6612536_Draft_Meadowbrook_Comprehensive_Development_28CD29_Zone.pdf
- **Q:** Can two Meadowbrook houses share a party wall? **A:** Yes, in a limited form stated in the CD-29 zone. Where a principal building is to attach to the building on the abutting lot through a shared party wall, the lots are to be developed together as a single development, the use and gross floor areas must comply with CD-29, and a development permit is required in keeping with the two-family dwelling requirements. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-coq-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8316/9139/4004/0f60821c01f71f2185f312c8a6612536_Draft_Meadowbrook_Comprehensive_Development_28CD29_Zone.pdf
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves a Meadowbrook address? **A:** Meadowbrook Elementary, kindergarten to Grade 5 at 900 Sharpe Street, is the named elementary school for this area in the provincial school directory. That does not assign a specific Dewdney Trunk Road or Cherrybrook Place address to it: School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) catchment is by permanent home address, so run the exact address in the district School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** Did the rezoning change what I can build? **A:** The City's stated intent was the opposite of a density lift. It initiated the Meadowbrook rezoning in December 2021 to comply with provincial legislation, and stated the new zoning should protect existing uses and character by mirroring the Land Use Contract as closely as possible. Any addition or rebuild still has to meet the replacement zone's lot, coverage and setback rules for that parcel. https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/854?arc=1086

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The spelling differs by source: the City and the school use one word, "Meadowbrook," while this site's page uses two words, "Meadow Brook" — search City documents under Meadowbrook or a bylaw lookup will miss the area. https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/854?arc=1086
- At least 10.0 square metres of the front yard of a CD-29 lot must not be occupied by any building or structure or used for off-street parking, and must be landscaped, per the draft zone text. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-coq-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8316/9139/4004/0f60821c01f71f2185f312c8a6612536_Draft_Meadowbrook_Comprehensive_Development_28CD29_Zone.pdf

- Coquitlam's 2026 Class 1 Residential municipal rate is $2.2756 per $1,000 of assessed value, in full with utility charges in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17264/2026_Tax-Rates
- Coquitlam is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so a Meadowbrook owner must complete the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the title and the replacement zone together on any Meadowbrook lot: the former Land Use Contract ended June 30, 2024, but older documents and listings may still describe the lot by contract terms, and the governing rules are now the replacement zoning for that parcel. https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/854?arc=1086
- Check the exact zone on the City's QtheMap viewer before assuming small-scale multi-unit rights: CD-29 is a site-specific comprehensive development zone with its own lot, coverage and floor-area caps, in the draft text the City published for this area. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-coq-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8316/9139/4004/0f60821c01f71f2185f312c8a6612536_Draft_Meadowbrook_Comprehensive_Development_28CD29_Zone.pdf
