# North Shore Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: north-shore-port-moody (North Shore, Port Moody; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- North Shore is the board (Greater Vancouver REALTORS®) sub-area name for Port Moody's residential shore on the north side of the Port Moody Arm of Burrard Inlet; it is not an official City neighbourhood name. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/city-hall/resources/Documents/OCP-neighbourhood-plan-areas_-_edited_for_EngagePM_website.pdf
- The official Official Community Plan (OCP) areas along this shore are Ioco, Pleasantside and April Road, plus the Heritage Mountain grouping above them; no single official boundary exists for the board sub-area. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/city-hall/resources/Documents/OCP-neighbourhood-plan-areas_-_edited_for_EngagePM_website.pdf
- Ioco Road is the one through-corridor, running from the Burnaby border and Ioco in the west, below the Heritage Woods hillside, toward Anmore and Belcarra in the north and east. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The OCP describes Pleasantside and April Road as primarily single-detached homes, with a small number of townhouse developments along Ioco Road in Pleasantside; shared stock facts are in full in the north-shore-port-moody-houses-for-sale note. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- The oldest stock is the Ioco townsite: early-1900s homes built for Imperial Oil refinery workers after a spur track to the refinery was completed, now inside a heritage conservation area. https://www.portmoody.ca/arts-culture-and-heritage/heritage-and-history/al-sholund-s-historical-insights/
- Apartment buildings are concentrated at Inlet Centre and Moody Centre south of the inlet rather than on the north shore itself. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Port Moody-wide zoning, small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH), tax and utility facts are written in full in the heritage-mountain-port-moody-houses-for-sale note; North Shore specifics follow. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- Small-lot subdivision from RS1 to RS1-S — minimum 9 metre width and 325 square metre lot area — is available in Pleasantside only where the OCP designates Single Family–Low Density (also Moody Centre and Seaview). https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- In the Ioco townsite heritage conservation area, a heritage alteration permit can be required for changes to a home. https://www.portmoody.ca/arts-culture-and-heritage/heritage-and-history/al-sholund-s-historical-insights/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is North Shore an official Port Moody neighbourhood? **A:** No, it is the board sub-area name. The City's OCP areas on this shore are Ioco, Pleasantside and April Road, and the Heritage Mountain grouping on the hills above. That matters because plan policies, heritage rules and small-lot permissions attach to the OCP area and the zoning, not to the North Shore label a listing uses. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/city-hall/resources/Documents/OCP-neighbourhood-plan-areas_-_edited_for_EngagePM_website.pdf
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Bentley Road address? **A:** The School District No. 43 (SD43) locator run for 646 Bentley Road returned Pleasantside Elementary, both Eagle Mountain Middle and École Moody Middle for middle school, and Heritage Woods Secondary. Pleasantside Elementary itself is at 195 Barber Street on this shore. Because two middle schools were returned for one address, check the exact house rather than assuming a single middle school. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** Can I alter an Ioco townsite house freely? **A:** No. The Ioco townsite is a heritage conservation area, and a heritage alteration permit can be required for changes. The townsite was built for Imperial Oil refinery workers in the early 1900s, so exterior work, additions and demolition questions start with the City's heritage process, not just a building permit. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/
- **Q:** Is there a swimming beach on this shore? **A:** Yes. Old Orchard Park, on the north shore, has Port Moody's only swimming beach (salt-water) and is a starting point for the Shoreline Trail. Rocky Point Park, at the head of the inlet, adds a pier, boat launch, outdoor pool and skate park, so the two parks carry different parts of a waterfront household's routine. https://engage.portmoody.ca/rocky-point-and-old-orchard-parks-master-plans/news_feed/background-rocky-point-today
- **Q:** Can a Pleasantside lot be split into small lots? **A:** Possibly, in the designated areas only. Small-lot subdivision from RS1 to RS1-S needs at least 9 metres of width and 325 square metres per lot, and it is available in Pleasantside only where the OCP designates Single Family–Low Density. Confirm the parcel's designation with the City before pricing any subdivision potential into an offer. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Old Orchard Hall is at 646 Bentley Road, and the Port Moody Recreation Complex is at 300 Ioco Road — the two City facilities on this shore. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/
- SD43 elementary schools on or above this shore include Pleasantside (195 Barber Street), Aspenwood (2001 Panorama Drive), Heritage Mountain (125 Ravine Drive) and Mountain Meadows (999 Noons Creek Drive). https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/port-moody
- Ioco Road is the only through-corridor for these neighbourhoods, so school runs, showings and the commute all share one road. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- No shopping centre or grocery store on the north shore was verified on an official source; Port Moody Public Library is at 100 Newport Drive, south of the inlet. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/
- Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, is Fraser Health's in-city hospital, south of the inlet. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital

## 8. GETTING AROUND

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check heritage status before any exterior plan in the Ioco townsite: the heritage conservation area permit sits on top of normal building permits. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/
- Shorefront and creek lots need the riparian and foreshore position checked: the sub-area fronts the Burrard Inlet foreshore, the Shoreline Trail runs along the water, and no Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) land was confirmed on the north shore. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- The City's hazards page notes Port Moody is surrounded by forests and parks, so wildfire and interface preparation applies to the hillside-edge streets above Ioco Road as well. https://www.portmoody.ca/resident-services/hazards/
