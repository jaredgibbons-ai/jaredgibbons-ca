# Albion Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: albion-maple-ridge-houses-for-sale (Albion, Maple Ridge; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Albion is east Maple Ridge, around 240 Street and Lougheed Highway. The City lists the neighbourhood as "Albion/Kanaka" and its Official Community Plan (OCP) carries an Albion Area Plan, Chapter 10.2. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The North East Albion Area is bound by Kanaka Creek Regional Park to the north, existing Albion residential developments and conservation areas to the southwest, and rural residential developments to the east. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/10-ocp-chapter-102-albion202211091803097864
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) runs along the south side, 240 Street is the main north-south road, and 102 Avenue and 104 Avenue run east-west through the residential area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId=
- Cottonwood and East Central lie west along Lougheed Highway, Thornhill lies northeast, and the Fraser River forms the south edge. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The standard urban house zone here is RS-1 Single Detached Residential in Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019, section 605, with a minimum lot area of 668.0 m², minimum width 18.0 m, minimum depth 27.0 m and maximum lot coverage 40%. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- The Albion Area Plan's Low Density Residential designation corresponds to single detached development at a low-density urban standard with lot sizes at 2,000 m². https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/albion-area-plan-3rd-reading-websitepdf
- The plan's Low-Medium Density Residential designation covers single detached or duplex housing with lot sizes from 891 m² down to 557 m². https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/10-ocp-chapter-102-albion202211091803097864
- North East Albion's Single Family designation allows secondary suites and/or detached garden suites where zoning permits, with larger lots placed beside the eastern plan boundary. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/10-ocp-chapter-102-albion202211091803097864

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- One secondary suite per lot is permitted in Maple Ridge, inside the same building as the single detached house. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- A suite must not exceed 40% of the total gross floor area of the building, must not be situated within the Floodplain Area, and needs a building permit; a business licence is required if there are two rental units on the property. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- Eligible single-family and duplex lots can now have up to three, four or six units of small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH), if zoning, bylaw and building code requirements are met. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- Any new development within 50 metres of a stream, wetland, pond or ditch needs a Watercourse Protection Development Permit, under Watercourse Protection Bylaw No. 6410-2006. https://www.mapleridge.ca/resident-services/water-sewer/water-protection-management
- On floodplain lots the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic including freeboard, no habitable floor space may sit below it, and Zoning Bylaw section 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation on floodplain properties. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves Albion houses? **A:** Samuel Robertson Technical Secondary at 10445 245 St is the secondary school physically in Albion. It is a School District 42 (SD42) school, and SD42 has no middle tier: elementary is Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary is Grades 8 to 12. Catchments are address-based, so run the exact address in the SD42 School Locator before relying on any school. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q:** What is Kanaka Creek to an Albion house buyer? **A:** Kanaka Creek Regional Park runs along the area's west and north side, so creek rules affect nearby lots. Metro Vancouver's park includes the Canyon Trail to Cliff Falls and the Bell-Irving Hatchery. The City also requires a watercourse permit for development within 50 metres of a water feature, so a creek-side lot is not a standard lot. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- **Q:** Can I put a suite in an Albion house? **A:** Yes, one suite, inside the main house, if the lot is not in the floodplain. Maple Ridge permits one secondary suite per lot contained within the single detached building, capped at 40% of the building's gross floor area. Suites must not be situated within the Floodplain Area, and building permits are required. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q:** Is Albion still changing? **A:** Yes, the area plan was updated in 2025. Council adopted updates to the Albion Area Plan on October 28, 2025, enhancing walking and cycling connections, supporting growth of local commercial nodes, and introducing buffers where urban development meets farmland. The southern part of North East Albion, south of 110 Avenue, was the focus of the land use and servicing review behind it. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId=
- **Q:** Which elementary schools are in the area? **A:** Albion Elementary at 10031 240th St and c'usqunela Elementary at 24093 104 Avenue are the elementary schools physically in Albion, with Kanaka Creek Elementary at 11120 234a St at the west edge. Those addresses are from the British Columbia Ministry Kindergarten to Grade 12 school file. Catchment for a specific house is still an SD42 locator check. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Maple Ridge's 2025 total residential mill rate is 4.1895 per $1,000 of assessed value on the City's 2025 Property Tax Rate Summary, of which the City general rate is 2.6648. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2025-05/2025%20Property%20Tax%20Rates_1.pdf
- Maple Ridge's 2025 water flat fee on the same summary is $822.25 per parcel, with an additional-unit flat fee of $411.10. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2025-05/2025%20Property%20Tax%20Rates_1.pdf
- For 2025 the City raised sewer rates by 23.5%, about $136 more for the average household, and water rates by 4.5%, about $35 more, citing Metro Vancouver regional wastewater costs. http://www.mapleridge.ca/news/2025-budget-invests-community-priorities-while-reducing-tax-rate-increase
- Maple Ridge property taxes were due July 2, 2025, and the tax notice includes charges for utilities. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/watch-your-property-tax-notices-save-time-pay-online-july-2-2025
- The City of Maple Ridge is listed as a taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT), so owners must complete the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check floodplain status lot by lot on the south edge, along the Fraser River and Kanaka Creek. A covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, and no habitable floor space may be located below the 6.7 m flood construction level on affected lots. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- Check watercourse setbacks before planning an addition: a Watercourse Protection Development Permit is required within 50 metres of a stream, wetland, pond or ditch. https://www.mapleridge.ca/resident-services/water-sewer/water-protection-management
- Check ALR status for South Albion and Albion Flats parcels on the Agricultural Land Commission map; the Flats are mostly ALR and outside the Urban Area Boundary. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-12/06%20OCP%20Chapter%206%20-%20AL%20EDITS.pdf
