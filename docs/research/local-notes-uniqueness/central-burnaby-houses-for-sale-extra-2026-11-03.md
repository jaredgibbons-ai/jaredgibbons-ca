# Central Burnaby Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: central-burnaby-houses-for-sale (Central Burnaby; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Central Burnaby is the house pocket around Deer Lake and Burnaby City Hall, framed by Canada Way and Deer Lake Parkway, with the Norfolk Street strata cluster inside the same frame covered in the central-burnaby-condos-townhomes-for-sale note. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/meetings-and-public-hearings/public-hearings/zoning-bylaw-amendments
- Burnaby City Hall stands at 4949 Canada Way, just west of the Deer Lake side of the pocket. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-09/Notice-of-Election-by-Voting-2026-10-01.pdf
- Deer Lake Park is the pocket's southern anchor, and Sperling Avenue runs from Lougheed Highway and the SkyTrain station at its north end down to Deer Lake Park at its south end. http://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/skytrain_station_maps/sperling_burnaby_lake_station.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The house stock is detached houses on the Canada Way and Deer Lake Parkway frame streets, while the Norfolk Street cluster between Canada Way and Deer Lake Parkway holds the strata buildings, including two buildings named Winchelsea at 3738 and 3733 Norfolk Street. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/meetings-and-public-hearings/public-hearings/zoning-bylaw-amendments
- Detached lots here fall under the City's R1 small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) district, so a house lot can be redeveloped as up to 3 to 6 dwelling units depending on lot area and proximity to a frequent transit network bus stop. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Burnaby's new Zoning Bylaw 2026 was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026, replacing the former Zoning Bylaw 1965, and the R1 SSMUH district dates to the June 2024 rezoning of all R district lots. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- Burnaby 2050, the City's official community plan, was adopted December 9, 2025 and sets the land-use direction for the whole city including this pocket. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- Burnaby is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which secondary school is in Central Burnaby? **A:** Burnaby Central Secondary, at 6011 Deer Lake Parkway, is the School District No. 41 (SD41) secondary school on the Deer Lake Parkway side of the pocket, while Burnaby South Secondary at 5455 Rumble Street serves the south side; SD41 assigns catchments by address, so confirm the assigned school for a specific address. https://south.burnabyschools.ca/
- **Q:** Is Deer Lake Park part of the neighbourhood? **A:** Yes as the southern anchor: Deer Lake Park sits at the south end of the Sperling Avenue spine, and the boardwalk section by the boat launch has been closed from September 21, 2026 for full replacement, with completion scheduled for winter 2027 and a detour via Sperling Avenue and Deer Lake Drive. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- **Q:** Can I build a multiplex on a Central Burnaby house lot? **A:** Possibly, under the R1 SSMUH district: newly rezoned R1 lots are eligible for up to 3 to 6 dwelling units depending on lot area and proximity to a frequent transit network bus stop, and the count is inclusive of any secondary suites. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q:** What is the 2026 residential tax rate? **A:** Burnaby's 2026 residential mill rate totals 3.28373 per 1,000 dollars of assessed value, and gross taxes are the assessed value multiplied by the mill rate and divided by 1,000, before local area service, sewer parcel and business improvement area charges. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- City Hall at 4949 Canada Way is inside the pocket's orbit, so council, tax and permit business is a short trip rather than a trip across the city. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-09/Notice-of-Election-by-Voting-2026-10-01.pdf
- The Norfolk Street strata cluster is a pocket within the pocket: two separate buildings share the Winchelsea name at 3738 and 3733 Norfolk Street, so the civic address, not the building name, decides which building a unit is in. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/meetings-and-public-hearings/public-hearings/zoning-bylaw-amendments
- Burnaby Hospital, at 3935 Kincaid Street, is the major institution on the pocket's north side, with its redevelopment in phases. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/capital-projects/projects/burnaby-hospital/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby's 2026 residential tax rate is 3.28373 per 1,000 dollars of assessed value, and the average residential assessment in Burnaby decreased by 5.29 percent in 2026, which does not necessarily mean an individual bill falls, because taxes depend on the change in a property's assessment relative to the class average. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates
- Property taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after the July due date and a further 5 percent after the September due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes
- Assessment notices arrive from BC Assessment each January, and the assessed value on the notice is the value the mill rate is applied to. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Confirm the lot's R1 SSMUH eligibility and unit count with the City before assuming a multiplex, because the count turns on lot area and on distance to a frequent transit network bus stop. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- Confirm the SD41 catchment for the exact address, since Burnaby Central Secondary and Burnaby South Secondary both touch this pocket. https://south.burnabyschools.ca/
- Confirm whether a Norfolk Street frontage lot is strata or detached-house zoning, because the two Winchelsea buildings and the house streets carry different rules. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/meetings-and-public-hearings/public-hearings/zoning-bylaw-amendments
