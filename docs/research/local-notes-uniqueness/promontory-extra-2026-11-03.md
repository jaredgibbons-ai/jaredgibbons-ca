# Promontory — Extra Local Notes
Page: promontory (Promontory; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Promontory is the Sardis hillside east of Vedder Road, reached by Promontory Road, and that hillside position east of the Vedder corridor is what separates it from the flat Sardis streets. https://www.bctransit.com/agassiz-harrison/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2024/02/chw_map_54.pdf
- The BC Transit Route 54 map names the Promontory timing points a buyer will use to place the area: Vedder Road at Promontory Road at the bottom of the hill, and Teskey Way at Promontory Road on the hillside. https://www.bctransit.com/agassiz-harrison/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2024/02/chw_map_54.pdf
- Vedder Road is the primary transportation spine of the Sardis neighbourhood, providing the main north-south connection within and out of Sardis, and Promontory Road branches east from it. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- The City's Official Community Plan (OCP) lists the Eastern Hillsides Neighbourhood Plan as Schedule B4 and the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan as Schedule B5 as separate schedules, so a Promontory lot's plan cover must be read lot by lot rather than assumed. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A hillside development permit is the rule that defines building on Promontory: under Schedule D-5 of OCP Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, a development permit is required for subdivision of land with 20% or greater slopes and for new construction or earthworks on 20% or greater slopes. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- A third-party review of the application is required where the development proposal involves 30% or greater slopes, per the City's hillside permit text referring to its Development Approval Information Bylaw. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- Work confined to slopes under 20% is exempt in the cases the City lists, including where the property contains only areas with natural slopes less than 20%, or where construction involves only fences or single-tier retaining walls of less than 1.2 metres in height. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- The stated objectives of the hillside permit area are that new hillside development positively contributes to Chilliwack's hillside character and maintains unique hillside features such as rock outcrops, watercourses and ravines. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- City-wide zoning, suite and small-scale multi-unit housing rules for Chilliwack are in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note — see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Does every Promontory lot need a hillside permit? **A:** No, the trigger is slope, not the neighbourhood name. The City's Schedule D-5 text requires a development permit for subdivision, new construction or earthworks on 20% or greater slopes, and exempts development restricted to areas with natural slopes under 20%. A buyer should have the lot's slope read from the City's mapping and permit office before assuming a permit, a geotechnical report or neither is needed. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- **Q:** Which bus climbs the hill? **A:** Route 54 Promontory is the hillside route in the BC Transit Chilliwack Rider's Guide, listed as Cottonwood Mall to Promontory via Promontory Road. The Route 54 map shows the climb from Vedder Road at Promontory Road up to the Teskey Way and Promontory Road timing point, with the Tyson and Watson area and Sardis Secondary passed on the valley-floor part of the routing. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chw_rg_jan2024-V1.1.pdf https://www.bctransit.com/agassiz-harrison/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2024/02/chw_map_54.pdf
- **Q:** Is there an elementary school on the hill? **A:** Yes, Promontory Elementary is an SD33 school: the district's trustee liaison list names Promontory Elementary among the schools assigned to a trustee, alongside Sardis Secondary. Catchment assignment is still by home address, so a buyer should run the exact Promontory address in the SD33 School Locator rather than assume the hillside school is the catchment school for every hillside street. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2026-09/Chilliwack%20School%20District%20Directory%202026-27.pdf https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q:** What is different about buying on the hill versus flat Sardis? **A:** The permit layer is different. Flat Sardis lots are governed mainly by zoning and the Sardis plan, while a Promontory lot at or over 20% slope also needs a hillside development permit, and a proposal involving 30% or greater slopes needs a third-party review of the application, per the City's Schedule D-5 text. That adds a professional report step a flat-lot buyer does not face. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The hillside permit guidelines require manufactured slopes to be re-vegetated to reflect natural conditions, and state that rock cuts are an acceptable alternative to retaining walls. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- Lot grading on a hillside development must be provided on a consistent, comprehensive basis throughout the whole development, per the City's hillside guidelines. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- The OCP also designates a separate Schedule D-2 Hillside Environment development permit area, listed beside the D-5 Hillside Development area, so a hillside lot can carry both an environment and a development permit layer. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
- The City's Sardis-Vedder parks list does not name a community park inside Promontory itself; its listed community parks for the Sardis-Vedder area are All Sappers Memorial Park, Cheam Centre Park, Sardis Park, Vedder Park and Watson Glen Park on the valley floor. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=areaView&plID=165&backTo&areaID=6
- Chilliwack General Hospital at 45600 Menholm Road is the Fraser Health community hospital serving this hillside. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Slope is the first due diligence item on Promontory: read the lot against Schedule D-5 Hillside Development in the OCP, confirm whether any part of the building site is at or over 20% slope, and whether the 30% third-party review trigger applies. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295 https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
- Check the Schedule D-2 Hillside Environment layer as well as D-5, because the OCP lists both hillside permit areas and a lot can be in either or both. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
- Check watercourses and ravines on the lot, which the hillside objectives name as features to be maintained, and check any creek against the Schedule D-3 Riparian Area permit area. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295 https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
- The hillside permit text does not give a lot-specific stability conclusion; a buyer still needs the lot's own geotechnical advice where the City requires it, rather than relying on the neighbourhood's general hillside designation. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
