# Grandview South Surrey Condos and Townhomes — Extra Local Notes

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Grandview Heights covers about 1,050 hectares (2,600 acres) from 16 Avenue north to 28 Avenue, bounded by the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) on the east, under the Grandview Heights General Land Use Plan (GLUP) the City of Surrey endorsed in June 2005. https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/grandview-heights-general-land-use-plan
- The strata stock sits inside named Neighbourhood Concept Plan (NCP) areas — Morgan Heights (Area 1), Sunnyside Heights (Area 2), Darts Hill (Area 3), Redwood Heights (Area 4), Orchard Grove (Area 5a) and Grandview Heights Area 5 — each with its own land use and servicing rules. https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/grandview-heights-general-land-use-plan

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Servicing is the rule that bites in Grandview: Grandview Heights Area 5 will rely on utility servicing through the adjacent North Grandview, Redwood Heights and Darts Hill NCP areas, which the City states are still in planning and have not begun construction. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r144-grandview-heights-area-5-neighbourhood-concept-plan-initiation
- Applications inside Area 5 can proceed at the same time as its NCP, but the City holds them at third reading until the servicing and financing stage is endorsed. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r144-grandview-heights-area-5-neighbourhood-concept-plan-initiation
- Strata rules that apply in every Grandview complex — Form B disclosure, depreciation reports and the ban on rental-restriction bylaws — are provincial: see the surrey-condos-for-sale note.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Which elementary school does a townhouse at 2350 165 Street feed into?
**A:** Schools physically in Grandview Heights are Edgewood Elementary at 16666 23 Avenue, Sunnyside Elementary at 2828 159 Street and Pacific Heights Elementary at 17148 26 Avenue, all Kindergarten to Grade 7 in School District No. 36 (Surrey), called SD36. Catchment is set by home address through the SD36 School Locator, and the locator result for 2350 165 Street was not verified in this pass, so run the exact address before subjects come off. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/surrey

**Q:** Is there a secondary school inside Grandview Heights?
**A:** Yes: Grandview Heights Secondary at 16987 25 Avenue, beside the Grandview Heights Aquatic Centre at 16855 24 Avenue. It is the SD36 secondary school built for this plan area, so most Grandview townhouse addresses are closer to it than to the older South Surrey secondaries, but catchment still follows the address, not the plan boundary, and should be confirmed in the School Locator. https://www.surreyschools.ca/grandviewheights

**Q:** Why do some Grandview phases take so long to start?
**A:** Because servicing runs ahead of building in the newer NCP areas: the City states Grandview Heights Area 5 depends on utility servicing delivered through the North Grandview, Redwood Heights and Darts Hill plans, none of which had begun construction when Corporate Report R144 was written in July 2026. A presale contract in those areas carries that servicing timeline, so read the developer's disclosure statement completion dates rather than the sales centre estimate. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r144-grandview-heights-area-5-neighbourhood-concept-plan-initiation

**Q:** Can I rent out a Grandview townhouse?
**A:** Provincial law governs first: amendments to the Strata Property Act ended strata rental-restriction bylaws, so a Grandview strata cannot ban long-term rentals, though it can still ban short-term rentals such as nightly stays. The individual complex's registered bylaws and any Rental Disclosure Statement attached to the Form B are the documents to read, because listing text about rentals is not the legal position. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Grandview Heights Aquatic Centre at 16855 24 Avenue is the City's aquatic facility for the whole plan area, at the northeast quadrant of 24 Avenue and 168 Street. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/grandview-heights-aquatic-centre?viewTab=2
- Grandview Heights Secondary at 16987 25 Avenue stands directly beside the aquatic centre, so the 25 Avenue frontage carries school and pool traffic at the same hours. https://www.surreyschools.ca/grandviewheights
- Fergus Creek's habitat preservation area adjoins the Sunnyside Heights (Area 2) plan area south of 16 Avenue, and City plan material requires connections into it from 168 Street and the Pioneer Greenway. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2007-C012.pdf
- Redwood Park, at 17900 20 Avenue on the area's eastern side, is a City-listed South Surrey heritage and nature park tied to the Redwood Heights plan name. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/heritage-conservation/heritage-sites/south-surrey/redwood-park
- Area 5 drains two ways: northern slopes flow to the Nicomekl River via Burrows Ditch and Erickson Creek, and southern slopes to Sam Hill Creek and the Little Campbell River, per Corporate Report R144. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r144-grandview-heights-area-5-neighbourhood-concept-plan-initiation

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 531 is Grandview's bus spine: White Rock Centre to Willowbrook via 152 Street and the full length of 24 Avenue, with stops at 160 Street, 164 Street and 168 Street serving the townhouse corridors and the aquatic centre. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt531.pdf
- There is no SkyTrain in South Surrey: route 531's endpoints, White Rock Centre and Willowbrook, are bus exchanges, and the 24 Avenue stops are the transfer points for both. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt531.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with servicing status: in Darts Hill, Redwood Heights and Area 5, the City's own report ties development timing to servicing that had not begun construction in July 2026, so a presale's outside completion date matters more here than in built-out Morgan Heights. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r144-grandview-heights-area-5-neighbourhood-concept-plan-initiation
- Check creek setbacks on the Area 5 and Darts Hill edges: Erickson Creek, Burrows Ditch, Sam Hill Creek and Fergus Creek all carry riparian areas inside the plan boundaries. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2007-C012.pdf
