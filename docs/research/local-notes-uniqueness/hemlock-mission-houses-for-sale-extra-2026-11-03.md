# Hemlock Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: hemlock-mission-houses-for-sale (Hemlock / Hemlock Valley; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Hemlock Valley is not in Mission — it is unincorporated land in Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area C. The FVRD's Hemlock Valley Official Community Plan (OCP) governs the plan area and the FVRD administers it. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20C%20%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%201626%20-%20Hemlock%20Valley.pdf
- The plan area follows the Controlled Recreation Area (CRA) set by the Province in 2016 for the Hemlock Valley All Seasons Resort Master Plan. That resort is now Sasquatch Mountain Resort. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- The nearest town is Agassiz, in the District of Kent, about 38 kilometres away by the OCP's measure. Mission is about 55 kilometres west — a reporting grouping, not the government here. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- House streets named in the OCP area include Sakwi Creek Road, Edelweiss Drive and Snowmist Place. Village roads and resort streets, not Mission city streets. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached cabins and chalets are the house stock in a small resort village of about 200 dwellings. The FVRD evacuation guide describes those dwellings as mainly vacation homes and short-term rentals, with the resort at the village's upper end. http://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD Evacuation Guide - Hemlock Valley.pdf
- The OCP counted 226 resort residential units, equal to 1,072 bed units, in the existing base. About 60 percent were ski-in/ski-out or within comfortable walking distance of the base area. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- The OCP splits the village into Hemlock West, Hemlock East and Hemlock North planning areas. Each is to be detailed through a neighbourhood plan as the resort expands. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- FVRD bylaws, not Mission bylaws, set the land rules for a Hemlock house. The current OCP is FVRD Bylaw No. 1626, 2021, for Hemlock Valley in a portion of Electoral Area C, adopted March 17, 2022. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20C%20%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%201626%20-%20Hemlock%20Valley.pdf
- Development Permit Areas in the OCP include protection of development from hazardous conditions. Confirm a lot's permit-area status with the FVRD before valuing a slope lot. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Rental use is the village pattern, not a guarantee for any one house. Parcel-level rental permission, strata rules and licensing still need confirmation for the specific property. http://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD Evacuation Guide - Hemlock Valley.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a Hemlock house in Mission? **A:** No, Hemlock Valley is not inside the City of Mission, even though board reporting groups it under Mission. It is unincorporated FVRD Electoral Area C land governed by the FVRD's Hemlock Valley OCP. Title, permits and taxes run through the FVRD and the Province, so do not apply Mission bylaws or Mission tax rates to a Hemlock purchase. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20C%20%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%201626%20-%20Hemlock%20Valley.pdf
- **Q:** Who do I pay property tax to? **A:** You pay the Province, not a city, because Hemlock Valley is a rural area. The Province states a rural-area owner gets the tax notice from the Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province, not the regional district. That is different from a Mission house, where the city collects the tax. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area
- **Q:** Where do children go to school? **A:** There is no school in the valley, so children travel out for school. The OCP states the small permanent population limits the viability of a school inside the Controlled Recreation Area. Which district and school a specific Hemlock address is assigned to was not confirmed from a district catchment source in this pass — confirm it in writing before buying as a full-time family home. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Q:** Can I rent the house out nightly? **A:** The village pattern is vacation and short-term rental use, but that does not grant a right for your lot. The FVRD guide describes the dwellings as mainly vacation homes and short-term rentals. Check the lot's zoning, any strata bylaws, and any licence the FVRD or Province requires for that address. http://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD Evacuation Guide - Hemlock Valley.pdf
- **Q:** What happens in an emergency or evacuation? **A:** Hemlock Valley has its own FVRD evacuation guide, because it is a single-road resort village. The guide describes about 200 dwellings and an evacuation route past Sts'ailes First Nation and Harrison Mills. Read the guide for the lot, keep the route in mind in winter, and plan for the village to be on its own longer than a city neighbourhood. http://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD Evacuation Guide - Hemlock Valley.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Hemlock Valley Homeowners Association was established in 1980 and works for valley homeowners. The OCP records it as a non-profit. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Water, sewer and other services here are resort-community systems, not City of Mission services. The OCP's services chapter covers water supply, sanitary sewer, utilities, stormwater, snow clearing and solid waste for the plan area. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Private utility service is a live due-diligence item for Hemlock houses — see the hemlock-valley-mission-guide note for the utility detail. Confirm the provider, the bill and any upgrade charges for the specific lot before removing subjects. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- The ski hill recorded in the OCP has 4 lifts and 146.8 hectares of developed terrain on 35 named trails. It uses a 100 percent natural snowpack, on the south and southwest slopes. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- A Hemlock house pays provincial rural tax, not a Mission municipal rate. The Province states rural-area owners receive the notice from the Surveyor of Taxes Office and pay the Province. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area
- Rural taxes are due July 2, with penalties for late payment under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act. The Act sets the July 2 payment date in section 11. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax does not list FVRD Electoral Area C as a taxable area. The Province's taxable-area list names municipalities such as the City of Delta and the District of Mission, not Hemlock Valley's rural area — check the Province map for the lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The valley is reached by road from the Agassiz and Harrison Mills side, with no public transit confirmed. The FVRD evacuation route runs past Sts'ailes First Nation, Harrison Mills and sites along that route. http://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/FVRD Evacuation Guide - Hemlock Valley.pdf
- Agassiz is the service town for a Hemlock house, about 38 kilometres away per the OCP. Everyday trips for school, medical care and shopping start with that road trip. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
