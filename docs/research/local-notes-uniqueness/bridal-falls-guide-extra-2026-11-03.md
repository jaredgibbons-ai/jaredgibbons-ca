# Bridal Falls Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: bridal-falls-guide (Bridal Falls, Fraser Valley Regional District; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Bridal Falls is unincorporated, in Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area D, "Popkum, Bridal Falls" (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/contact/your-electoral-area-director.html).
- It sits at the Highway 1 and Highway 9 interchange (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Water~Systems/Area%20D%20Integrated%20Water%20System%20Map.pdf).
- It is not inside the City of Chilliwack — government detail, see the bridal-falls note.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Who handles zoning and building permits here? **A:** The Fraser Valley Regional District, not the City of Chilliwack. Bridal Falls is in Electoral Area D, and the FVRD library holds its bylaws: Official Community Plan Bylaw 1501 for Popkum and Bridal Falls and the consolidated Zoning Bylaw 1638, 2021. Confirm a parcel's zone with the FVRD first (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws/planning-land-use.html).
- **Q:** Is there a bus to Bridal Falls? **A:** Yes, on request on some trips. BC Transit's Rider's Guide, Trip Note B, states on-request Route 71 Agassiz-Harrison trips can route to Bridal Falls. Route 71 runs between Chilliwack and Harrison Hot Springs, and its Sunday service was discontinued September 8, 2026 (https://www.bctransit.com/agassiz-harrison/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2026/06/chw_rg_june2026.pdf) (https://www.bctransit.com/fall-changes-in-chilliwack-and-the-fraser-valley-regional-district-2026-08-24/).
- **Q:** Which school serves this side of the district? **A:** Rosedale Traditional Community School, Kindergarten to Grade 8, School District 33, at 50850 Yale Road, Rosedale, is the school on this side, with about 530 students. No school is in Bridal Falls itself; confirm catchments with District 33 (https://rosedale.sd33.bc.ca/index.php/node/6).
- **Q:** Can I take a dog into the parks nearby? **A:** It depends on the park. At Cheam Lake Wetlands Regional Park, pets, including dogs, cats and horses, are not permitted at all, under FVRD Bylaw No. 1500, 2018. Where FVRD parks permit dogs, they must be on-leash, on designated trails (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/cheam-lake-wetlands.html?media=standard).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Falls trails were temporarily closed after a rockfall, August 2, 2026 notice (https://seatoskyparks.com/parks/bridal-veil-falls-park/).
- Park gate season: April 1 to October 31; day use free (https://seatoskyparks.com/parks/bridal-veil-falls-park/).
- The falls park is day-use, 16 kilometres east of Chilliwack (https://seatoskyparks.com/parks/bridal-veil-falls-park/).
- Unlicensed dogs draw a $200 fine in the electoral areas (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/animal-control/faq.html?media=standard).
- Utility bills unpaid December 31 transfer to taxes (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/utility-bills.html).

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Area D water, 2026: $77.00 per quarter to 100 cubic metres, then $1.13 per cubic metre (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Sewer~and~Water/Water Systems fees and charges amendment BL 1824, 2026.pdf).
- Rural taxes: the Province collects them; the FVRD cannot process tax payments (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/board-of-directors/how-the-fvrd-is-governed.html?media=standard).
- No single Area D rural rate published — see NOT CONFIRMED.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 1669, 2022 near lowland or watercourses (https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws/planning-land-use.html).
- Falls area is a rockfall zone; barriers mark closed ground (https://seatoskyparks.com/parks/bridal-veil-falls-park/).
- Confirm the lot is inside the integrated water area (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Electoral%20Area%20D%20Integrated%20Water%20System%20DCC%20Amendment%20Bylaw%20No.%201096-2011.pdf).
