## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Are Southlands townhomes strata or non-strata? **A:** They are freehold strata, so a monthly strata fee applies. Century Group's FAQ states Southlands townhomes and condo flats are freehold strata homes, with fees covering common-area maintenance, landscaping and snow removal, while its rowhomes, duplexes and estate cottages offer non-strata ownership instead. https://discoversouthlands.ca/faq/
- **Q:** How much visitor parking do new townhome projects provide? **A:** Less than buyers may expect at Eden Crescent, where the proposal varies visitor parking down to zero. The City's application page lists 27 three-bedroom townhomes with 42 garage spaces, nine new on-street spaces on Eden Crescent and 10A Avenue, and a variance reducing on-site visitor spaces from three to none. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009888
- **Q:** What is a stacked townhome at Dennison Gate? **A:** A stacked townhome is a unit stacked above or below another, rather than side by side. The Dennison Gate proposal at 52 Street and 7A Avenue lists 50 stacked units of one to four bedrooms in seven three-storey buildings, at a floor space ratio of 0.6. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009901
- **Q:** Which schools serve the 52 Street townhomes? **A:** South Delta Secondary is the public secondary school for Tsawwassen addresses, including the 52 Street and 7A Avenue area. Delta School District No. 37 publishes Tsawwassen catchment maps, notes boundaries are reviewed and occasionally adjusted, and directs families to verify the exact address in its School Locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/student-registration/catchment-area-maps/
Read: 2026-11-03
