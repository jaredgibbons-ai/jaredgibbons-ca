# North Meadows — Questions for Jared
Area: North Meadows, City of Pitt Meadows. Five questions only Jared can answer from his own deals, plus a photo shot list. Do not answer these from research.

## Questions for Jared
1. What do buyers get wrong about The Fairways on Meadow Gardens Way when they first tour it — the golf-course setting, the size of the complex, or who actually lives there?
2. Who is buying in North Meadows — Fairways condo buyers versus the farm and acreage buyers on Richardson Road and Hale Road — and are they different households entirely?
3. What sells fast and what sits in North Meadows, and does the Lougheed Highway divide change how long a Fairways unit takes versus a house south of the highway?
4. What surprises buyers about living north of Lougheed Highway — dike and floodplain questions, farm neighbours, or the drive to Pitt Meadows Station?
5. Tell one North Meadows deal story — a Fairways sale or a Polder acreage — that shows what this sub-area is really like to buy in.

## Shot list
1. The Fairways courtyard and Residents' Club from Meadow Gardens Way, shot across the Meadow Gardens Golf Course fairway so the course-edge setting reads in one frame.
2. Harris Road looking north across Lougheed Highway toward Highland Park and Highland Park Elementary on Advent Road, early morning, showing the highway as the sub-area's south edge.
3. Drone shot north up the Pitt Polder farm roads (Richardson Road / Hale Road) toward Pitt Lake, showing dikes and farmland with no attached housing — the honest picture of most of North Meadows.
