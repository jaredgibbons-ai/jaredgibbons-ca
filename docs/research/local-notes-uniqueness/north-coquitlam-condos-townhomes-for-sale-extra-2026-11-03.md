# North Coquitlam Condos and Townhomes — Extra Local Notes
Page: north-coquitlam-condos-townhomes-for-sale (North Coquitlam; condos and townhomes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- North Coquitlam is the board name for Coquitlam's City Centre tower district, so expect the same streets to be called City Centre or Town Centre in City documents: the condo and townhouse stock clusters on Pinetree Way, Glen Drive, Atlantic Avenue and Pinewood Avenue, around Coquitlam City Hall at 3000 Guildford Way. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Directory.aspx?DID=33
- Town Centre Park at 1299 Pinetree Way is the district's north anchor, a 100-acre park holding Lafarge Lake, Percy Perry Stadium and the City Centre Aquatic Complex area, with Pinetree Secondary School beside it. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/towncentrepark-84
- The district's project architect for its newest tower site describes 1175 Pinetree Way as a block away from Coquitlam City Hall and Coquitlam Town Centre Park, which is the working centre of this page's area. https://www.locidesign.ca/all-projects/pinetree-way
- Coquitlam Public Library's City Centre Branch at 1169 Pinetree Way sits inside the cluster, on the same street as the towers and the park. https://www.coqlibrary.ca/

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- MThree at 1188 Pinetree Way is the tower whose facts the developer itself publishes: Cressey's own project page confirms a 48-storey tower with 319 homes, completed in 2016, with The Summit Club, 9,000 square feet of amenities, at the top of the building. https://cressey.com/residential/mthree/
- The City Centre Area Plan Schedule A legend is the plan source for the district's form mix: it designates apartment and townhousing areas closer to City Centre, alongside one-family designations farther out, which is why this page's stock is towers and podium townhomes rather than detached houses. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/3487/City-Centre-Area-Plan-Servicing-Assessment-PDF
- The next stock arriving is mixed tenure, not all strata: Onni Group's own leasing material for Pine & Glen at 3022 Glen Drive states over 900 residential units with 35,000 square feet of office space and 13,000 square feet of ground-floor retail, so a share of the district's new homes will be rental-only and never appear as strata resales. https://onni.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Availability-Brochures-OFFICE-COPY-ONLY.pptx-1-1.pdf
- No developer, strata or municipal page read for this note publishes a verified year built, unit count or restriction set for the Glen Drive and Atlantic Avenue towers other than MThree, so those buildings are named by address only in NOT CONFIRMED and no year is claimed for them here. https://cressey.com/residential/mthree/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is North Coquitlam the same place as Coquitlam City Centre? **A:** Yes, for this housing stock. North Coquitlam is the board sub-area name, while the City calls the same Pinetree Way, Glen Drive and Guildford Way district City Centre or Town Centre in its plans and facility pages. A buyer searching only one name misses listings filed under the other, so check both labels for the tower cluster around Town Centre Park and City Hall. https://www.locidesign.ca/all-projects/pinetree-way
- **Q:** Which SkyTrain stations serve a Pinetree Way condo? **A:** Two stations bracket the cluster. The City places the Lafarge Lake–Douglas Station terminus of the Millennium Line Evergreen Extension in the southwest corner of Town Centre Park, and Onni's own material for Pine & Glen describes the site as within walking distance of both Coquitlam Central Station and the Lafarge Lake/Douglas Station. Lincoln Station sits between them on the same line. https://onni.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Availability-Brochures-OFFICE-COPY-ONLY.pptx-1-1.pdf
- **Q:** Which schools are physically inside this condo district? **A:** School District No. 43 (Coquitlam), called SD43, lists Pinetree Secondary at 3000 Pinewood Avenue for Grades 9 to 12, Pinetree Way Elementary at 1420 Pinetree Way for kindergarten to Grade 5, and Glen Elementary at 3064 Glen Drive, all inside the tower district's streets. Attendance is assigned by permanent home address, so run the specific unit's address in the SD43 School Locator before relying on any of the three. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** What will Pine & Glen change for existing condo owners here? **A:** Onni Group's own leasing brochure describes Pine & Glen on Glen Drive as a mixed-use development in the heart of Coquitlam with over 900 residential units, 35,000 square feet of office space and 13,000 square feet of ground-floor retail, with retail units available in the first quarter of 2027. That is new supply, new retail and new office workers arriving beside the existing Glen Drive towers. https://onni.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Availability-Brochures-OFFICE-COPY-ONLY.pptx-1-1.pdf
- **Q:** Is Lafarge Lake a natural lake? **A:** No. The City states Town Centre Park was a gravel plant and pit thirty years ago, and Lafarge Lake is a man-made excavated lake donated to the City by the Lafarge company in the mid-1980s; the park officially opened in May 1989. The lake is regularly stocked with rainbow trout between March and November by the Fraser Valley Trout Hatchery, and fishing is from the pier. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/towncentrepark-84

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Town Centre Park is undergoing upgrades guided by the Town Centre Park Master Plan, approved in 2018 to guide improvements for 15 to 20 years, and the City warns some trails close while work is under way — a live construction factor for park-facing units. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/towncentrepark-84
- The park's Community Plaza, on the eastern shore of Lafarge Lake, seats up to 1,300 people and is one of the region's outdoor performance and event venues, so event noise is a Lafarge Lake-facing unit check, not a district-wide one. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/towncentrepark-84
- Percy Perry Stadium's track and the park's five lit synthetic turf fields and eight lit tennis courts put evening sports lighting on the park's edge, closest to the Pinetree Way towers. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/towncentrepark-84
- The City Centre Branch library at 1169 Pinetree Way and the City archives at 1171 Pinetree Way share the tower district's main street, so strata records research and library services are both on Pinetree Way itself. https://www.coquitlam.ca/683/City-Archives

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- The Millennium Line Evergreen Extension is this district's rapid transit: the City places the Lafarge Lake–Douglas terminus in Town Centre Park's southwest corner, and Coquitlam Central Station, with West Coast Express service, sits at the district's south end. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/towncentrepark-84
- Route 186, Coquitlam Central Station to Hampton Park, runs Pinetree Way and David Avenue at the district's north side via Pipeline Road and Robson Drive. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt186.pdf
- Route 187, Coquitlam Central Station to Parkway, runs Johnson Street west of the district via Plateau Boulevard and Paddock Drive to Parkway Boulevard. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt187.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area for any low-rise or townhouse site in this plan area: within 30 metres of a watercourse bank, the permit can add conditions on riparian protection, runoff quality and erosion or landslip risk, and the guide does not set the final setback for a specific lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- Check the City's environmental development permit mapping, Schedule B, which maps unstable slopes and the Interface Wildfire Risk Management boundary for the City Centre and Northeast areas; it shows boundaries, not a lot-level conclusion. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- Check Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 section 519 flood and slope siting rules and Schedule G, the designated 200-year floodplain of the Brunette, Coquitlam, Fraser and Pitt rivers, even though the tower district itself is upland of those rivers. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1170/Part-5-General-Regulations-PDF
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status per parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission mapping; no ALR land was identified in this district in the sources read, which is not a parcel clearance. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
