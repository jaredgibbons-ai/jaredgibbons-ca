## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- North Meadows' only verified attached pocket is Meadow Gardens Way, beside Meadow Gardens Golf Course, north of Lougheed Highway (Highway 7). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/north-lougheed-area-plan
- North Meadows is a board sub-area name for Pitt Meadows north of Lougheed Highway, covering Highland Park, Wildwood, Harris Road north and the rural Pitt Polder farm roads beyond; the City publishes no boundary for the name. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/north-lougheed-area-plan
- The City's closest official geography is the North Lougheed Area, about 50 hectares on the north side of Lougheed Highway between Meadows Gardens Golf Course and Harris Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/north-lougheed-area-plan

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A 55-plus age bylaw for The Fairways is UNCONFIRMED; no source read publishes one, so do not buy or sell it as a 55-plus complex without reading the registered bylaws on the Form B Information Certificate. https://magusta.com/our-portfolio/the-fairways/
- Long-term rental caps in strata bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022, and Pitt Meadows separately prohibits short-term rental accommodation under Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 — see the south-meadows-condos-townhomes-for-sale note for the city-wide wording. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- The Fairways, built in 2007 per listing records in the earlier program file, falls in the first mandatory five-year depreciation-report cycle for stratas with five or more lots. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is The Fairways a 55-plus building? **A:** No source checked states a 55-plus bylaw for The Fairways, so treat the age position as unconfirmed, not as a yes. The developer describes 268 resort-style homes with a Residents' Club, exercise facilities and a games room, and earlier listing records show family amenities. Only the registered bylaws attached to the Form B settle the age question for a specific purchase. https://magusta.com/our-portfolio/the-fairways/
- **Q:** Are there townhomes to buy in North Meadows? **A:** None were verified on a developer or strata source, so a townhome search for this sub-area will mostly return detached houses and farm properties. The one attached address buyers actually tour is The Fairways on Meadow Gardens Way, which is apartment-condo form. If a portal labels a Meadow Gardens Way gated complex a townhome, ask for the strata plan number before treating the form as settled. https://magusta.com/our-portfolio/the-fairways/
- **Q:** Which school serves Meadow Gardens Way? **A:** Highland Park Elementary at 18961 Advent Road is the Pitt Meadows elementary school on the north side, listed for School District 42 at kindergarten to grade 7, and all Pitt Meadows addresses feed Pitt Meadows Secondary at 19438 116B Avenue for grades 8 to 12. Catchments in this district are address-based, so run the exact Meadow Gardens Way address in the district locator rather than assuming the nearest school building is the catchment school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- **Q:** Does the golf course affect the condos? **A:** The Fairways was built as a golf-course-edge complex, with its Residents' Club overlooking Meadow Gardens Golf Course according to the developer. That setting is the product: courtyard homes oriented to the course rather than to a town centre. Noise, ball-strike and course-hours questions are not answered on the developer page read, so raise them with the strata through the Form B and minutes. https://magusta.com/our-portfolio/the-fairways/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the lot against Floodplain Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 Schedules A and B before assuming the golf-course pocket and the Pitt Polder farm roads carry the same flood level — the bylaw sets different designated levels for its rural and urban schedules. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 - Consolidated.pdf
- Richardson Road and Hale Road acreages sit in the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR); a condo buyer at The Fairways is urban, but any North Meadows land purchase needs an ALR check on the City OCP maps. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- Building within seven metres of a dike toe needs a permit under Drainage Bylaw No. 2266, 2007 — relevant to Polder lots, not to a Fairways strata lot. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2023-04/Drainage%20System%20Protection%20Bylaw%20No.%202266%2C%202007.pdf
