## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses are the main land use here, because the plan says the existing small-scale residential areas are the largest land use element in Austin Heights and are to be respected. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17458/Official-Community-Plan---Schedule-J-PDF

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Austin Heights plan, not the board label, is the rulebook for these lots, and it now sits as Schedule J-1 of the Interim Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 5511, 2026. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17458/Official-Community-Plan---Schedule-J-PDF
- The plan requires new medium-density development near the centre to transition in massing to the lower-density houses beside it, respecting slope, privacy and building massing. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17458/Official-Community-Plan---Schedule-J-PDF

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is my house inside the Austin Heights plan area? **A:** Check the street, not the listing label. The plan area runs on either side of Austin Avenue, roughly Foster Avenue north to Rochester Avenue south, and Blue Mountain Street and Joyce Street west to Linton Street east. A house inside those lines is read against the Austin Heights Neighbourhood Plan as well as the city-wide Official Community Plan (OCP). https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17458/Official-Community-Plan---Schedule-J-PDF
- **Q:** Which schools sit on or beside these streets? **A:** Mundy Road Elementary is at 2200 Austin Avenue, on Austin Avenue itself, and École Rochester Elementary is at 411 Schoolhouse Street, with École Montgomery Middle at 1900 Edgewood Avenue. Those are locations, not assignments: School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) catchment is by permanent home address, so run the exact address in the district School Locator. https://catalogue.data.gov.bc.ca/dataset/bc-schools-k-12-with-francophone-indicators
- **Q:** Will the Austin Avenue core change my side street? **A:** The plan aims the change at the core, not the side streets. It transitions the commercial core to a high-density mixed-use area, adds medium-density housing next to it as a buffer, and keeps small-scale residential as part of the mix, so a detached lot two blocks off Austin Avenue is planned differently from one on it. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17458/Official-Community-Plan---Schedule-J-PDF
- **Q:** Is this a floodplain area? **A:** The mapped river floodplains are the lowlands, not this slope. The City's floodplain mapping covers the Coquitlam, Fraser and Pitt rivers, while Austin Heights sits on the slope above the Brunette and Fraser lowlands; the local check for a house lot here is slope and any creek on or beside the lot, through the City's development permit mapping. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/182/Coquitlam-Fraser-and-Pitt-Rivers-Floodplain-Mapping-Report-PDF

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Austin Avenue is part of TransLink's regional Major Road Network, so it carries regional traffic through the middle of this house area, and the plan's road work aims to keep that role while improving the pedestrian experience. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17458/Official-Community-Plan---Schedule-J-PDF
- The plan protects the Poirier Leisure Precinct south of the plan area as an area-wide recreation resource and commits the City to master-plan it, which is the recreation anchor for these houses. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17458/Official-Community-Plan---Schedule-J-PDF
- The nearest library branch for most of these streets is the Coquitlam Public Library Poirier branch at 575 Poirier Street; the City Centre branch is at 1169 Pinetree Way. https://www.coqlibrary.ca/
- Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way in Port Moody, is the Fraser Health hospital on the Port Moody and Coquitlam border serving this side of the city. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check slope first on these lots, using the City's environmental development permit mapping, which maps unstable slopes and watercourse areas; the map shows the layer, not a lot-level geotechnical answer. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- Check any creek on or beside the lot against the Watercourse Protection Development Permit rules before assuming a suite or carriage house fits. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- Check the Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 flood and slope siting rules in Part 5 for any lot at the foot of the slope toward the Brunette lowlands. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1170/Part-5-General-Regulations-PDF
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status per parcel on Agricultural Land Commission mapping; no ALR land was identified in this part of Coquitlam in the sources read, which is not a parcel clearance. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca
