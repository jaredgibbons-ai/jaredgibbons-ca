## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses here are estate and acreage houses: detached homes on large rural lots, with barns and outbuildings on the equestrian and hobby-farm parcels. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- The Township's zoning bylaw separates Rural (RU) Zones in Section 200 from Suburban Residential zones in Section 300 and Residential zones in Section 400, and a Campbell Valley lot can fall under different sections from its neighbour. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- Section 110 of the bylaw sets minimum subdivision requirements for lot area, frontage and depth, which is the first check for any estate lot's future potential. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- No area-typical lot size or build era for Campbell Valley houses was stated on a Township source read for this note; see NOT CONFIRMED.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- On ALR land there may generally be no more than one residence per parcel unless the parcel qualifies for an additional residence, and local government zoning may further restrict residences. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- An ALR principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 square metres or less, with no more than 1,000 square metres of fill placed for its construction. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- An ALR secondary suite is permitted only when it is entirely contained within the principal residence; a detached dwelling cannot be used as the suite, and Township bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Building more than the permitted number of ALR residences, or a larger residence than the size limits, requires Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission, under ALC Information Bulletin 09, section 6. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- The Township adopted small-scale multi-unit housing Bylaw 6020 on November 18, 2024: three units on lots of 280 m² or less, four units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², and lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible, so most Campbell Valley acreage parcels fall outside it. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- A building permit is required for all secondary suites in the Township, and suites must conform to municipal bylaws and provincial health and safety standards. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/secondary-suites.aspx?_mid_=41367

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can an estate lot here carry a second detached dwelling? **A:** On ALR land, generally no, unless the parcel qualifies for one additional residence. The ALC frame allows one principal residence per parcel, plus at most one additional residence of up to 90 square metres on properties 40 hectares or less, or 186 square metres on properties over 40 hectares. Township zoning may be more restrictive and cannot be more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q:** Can I subdivide a Campbell Valley lot? **A:** Subdivision is a two-gate question here, Township and, on ALR land, the ALC. The Township bylaw's Section 110 sets minimum lot area, frontage and depth, and its Section 200 covers the Rural zones. In the ALR, a plan that causes subdivision, including a strata plan, may not be registered without ALC approval or a permitted exception. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- **Q:** Does a house here come on sewer and city water? **A:** Do not assume it, because rural lots can be on well and septic. The Township's utility statements cover properties that receive water, sanitary sewer and garbage services, and owners receive a statement only for the services that apply to their property. Ask which services the specific lot actually has, and price well and septic checks into due diligence. http://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-fees.aspx
- **Q:** What does a secondary suite cost each year here? **A:** A suite adds a set percentage to Township utility fees, on top of the permit. Secondary suite water and sewer fees are 30% of the annual flat-rate fees, and from 2025 properties with suites are also subject to an Annual Secondary Suite Infrastructure Fee collected with property taxes. A permit is required before the suite is built. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/secondary-suites.aspx?_mid_=41367
- **Q:** If I keep farming the land, does the farm tax class carry over? **A:** No, a buyer who plans to continue farming must apply. BC Assessment's farm classification thresholds are $2,500 of income for a farm operation of 0.8 to 4 hectares, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value above 4 hectares, and $10,000 under 0.8 hectares, with applications due by October 31 for the next tax year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

- The Township separated utility billing from property taxes in 2025, so a Campbell Valley owner now receives a separate annual utility statement for the services that apply. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/township-separates-billing-of-utility-fees-and-property-taxes.aspx

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The Township's total 2025 residential tax rate is 3.34910 per $1,000 of assessed value, of which the total municipal rate under Bylaw 6128, including the library, is 1.87580. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/property-taxes/document-feed/2025-Tax-Rates.pdf
- The Township's 2026 flat-rate fees are water $779.96 and sanitary sewer $716.47 for a single-family dwelling, with secondary suite fees of $233.99 for water and $214.94 for sewer. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- Garbage in 2026 is a $226.00 base rate plus a cart charge of $200.00 for a 240-litre cart or $100.00 for a 120-litre cart, for properties receiving municipal collection. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- Township property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, and utility fees are no longer included on the property tax statement. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/information-about-property-taxes-utility-fees.aspx
- The Township of Langley is listed as a taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT), so owners must complete the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Confirm ALR status first, because it caps residences, suite form and subdivision. The ALC states local governments may further restrict ALR residences through zoning, including number, size limits and siting. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Check well and septic records for any rural lot not on Township water and sewer; the Township's utility pages describe serviced properties only and do not certify a specific lot's systems. http://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-fees.aspx
- Check farm classification status before closing if the tax bill assumes it, because a continuing farmer must file a new BC Assessment application by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- Confirm the parcel's zone and Section 110 subdivision minimums with the Township before valuing any future lot creation, because zoning varies property to property. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
