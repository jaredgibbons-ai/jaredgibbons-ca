# Campbell Valley — Extra Local Notes
Page: campbell-valley (Campbell Valley; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Campbell Valley is a rural sub-area in south Township of Langley, centred on Campbell Valley Regional Park. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- The park's Nature House is at the Rowlatt Farmstead, 20285 8 Avenue, reached by the South Valley Entrance. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- The park's north entrance is on 16th Avenue and its equestrian centre access is on 208th Street south of 16th Avenue. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- A board sub-area label does not settle a parcel's farm, Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) or park-edge status; that is parcel by parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- On ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) application, to a maximum total floor area of 500 square metres. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- A secondary suite on ALR land is permitted only when located within the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- At most one additional residence is permitted on ALR land: up to 90 square metres on properties 40 hectares or less, or up to 186 square metres over 40 hectares. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission, under ALC Information Bulletin 09, Section 6. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- Township of Langley Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) under Bylaw 6020 makes lots over 4,050 square metres ineligible, so most acreage parcels here fall outside it. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What homes can an ALR parcel here have? **A:** An ALR parcel can have one principal residence, a suite inside it, and at most one small additional residence. The principal residence is capped at 500 square metres with no more than 1,000 square metres of fill. The additional residence is capped at 90 or 186 square metres by parcel size. Local bylaws may be stricter. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q:** Can I strata-title or subdivide an acreage? **A:** Strata titling counts as subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission first. Information Bulletin 09, Section 6 states that rule directly. Outside the ALR, subdivision still needs Township approval and a proven water supply. Confirm the parcel's ALR status before valuing any split potential. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q:** How do I keep farm classification after buying? **A:** File the farm classification application with BC Assessment by October 31 after purchase. A buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit the General Application for Farm Classification by that date. Income thresholds vary by operation size, starting at $2,500 for operations between 0.8 and 4 hectares. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q:** What trails and horse facilities are in the park? **A:** The park offers 29 kilometres of trails, with about 14 kilometres designated for equestrian use. Walkers, cyclists and horseback riders share the network. The park holds the Campbell Valley Equestrian Society and Campbell Downs Equestrian Centre. The Nature House sits in the heritage Red Barn at the Rowlatt Farmstead. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Campbell Valley address? **A:** No catchment is stated here, because no district locator run for a Campbell Valley address is on record. Schools in the wider south Langley area on the Ministry file include Brookswood Secondary at 20902 37a Avenue. Check the exact address in the School District 35 (Langley) locator before relying on a school. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Little River Loop Trail is 2.2 kilometres with boardwalks across wetlands and little elevation gain. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- The Shaggy Mane Trail is the outer perimeter trail shared with horseback riders. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- The Nature House is open from Victoria Day to Labour Day, with free admission, per the park visitor information. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- General property transfer tax (PTT) is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, and 3% above that, with a further 2% on residential value above $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
