# Highgate — Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer, for a voice memo. Muse does not answer these.

1. Who is buying the condos and townhomes around Highgate Village — first-time buyers using the Edmonds SkyTrain station, investors, or downsizers from South Slope houses?
2. What sells fast here and what sits — the towers by Kingsway, or the low-rises and townhomes on the quieter streets behind Edmonds?
3. What surprises buyers most: the Kingsway and Edmonds redevelopment scale the City and BC Housing are planning, or the age and strata fees of the 1990s stock?
4. How does Highgate compete with Metrotown two stations away — what does a buyer give up, and what do they gain?
5. Tell one story from a Highgate deal — a first-time buyer bidding war, a special levy in the minutes, or a seller who watched the area change.
