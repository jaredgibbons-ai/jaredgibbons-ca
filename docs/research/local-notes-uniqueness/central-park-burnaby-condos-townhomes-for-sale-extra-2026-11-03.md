## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Every strata here with five or more lots must now hold a current depreciation report: since July 1, 2024 the Province requires reports on a five-year cycle, with no deferral vote, and Metro Vancouver stratas without one had until July 1, 2026 to comply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Old rental-restriction bylaws in the Sardis Street and Patterson Avenue buildings are not enforceable: provincial changes removed strata long-term rental restrictions, while short-term rental rules can still apply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- New rezonings on the Kingsway and Wilson Avenue edges now proceed under the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, the bylaw the City's current amendment list amends. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/meetings-and-public-hearings/public-hearings/zoning-bylaw-amendments

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Which SkyTrain station serves a Wilson Avenue tower?
**A:** Patterson Station, on the Expo Line at Central Boulevard and Patterson Avenue, one stop west of Metrotown Station. TransLink closed its elevator on November 3 for replacement until spring 2027, so step-free access at this station is disrupted this winter. https://www.translink.ca/news/2026/october/translink%20replacing%2040-year-old%20elevator%20at%20patterson%20skytrain%20station

**Q:** Two buildings are called Central Park Place — which one am I buying?
**A:** There are two: 5652 Patterson Avenue and 4160 Sardis Street, different stratas with different ages, finances and bylaws. Grand Central Park Place at 6128 Patterson Avenue is a third, separate building. Match the strata plan number and civic address on the Form B Information Certificate to the unit, not the marketing name. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

**Q:** Which elementary school serves the Sardis Street buildings?
**A:** Chaffey-Burke Elementary at 4404 Sardis Street is the in-area school in the Burnaby School District No. 41 (SD41) list, and Maywood Community School at 4567 Imperial Street serves the south side. Catchments are address-based, so run the exact unit address in the SD41 School Locator before relying on either name. https://burnabyschools.ca/elementary-school-addresses/

**Q:** What does the Patterson Station elevator closure change for me?
**A:** TransLink advises riders needing step-free access to allow 30 extra minutes during the closure, using buses 19, 31, 49, 116 or 130 from Central Boulevard and Willingdon Avenue to Metrotown Station, or the accessible Metrotown entrance via the BC Parkway. https://www.translink.ca/news/2026/october/translink%20replacing%2040-year-old%20elevator%20at%20patterson%20skytrain%20station

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due July 3, 2026, and late strata owners face the same penalty ladder as house owners: 5% after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date, applied also to an unclaimed home owner grant. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- New strata units can start life under one parent tax folio: Burnaby's apportionment process splits a developer's parent property into child strata units, and requests received after April 15 are processed after the July tax due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/additional-information

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the depreciation report date: a Sardis Street or Patterson Avenue strata with no report, or one from before December 31, 2020, was required to obtain a new one by July 1, 2026 under the provincial rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Check which Central Park Place you are in: the 5652 Patterson Avenue and 4160 Sardis Street stratas share a name, so a documents package pulled for the wrong one is a real error risk here. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/events/central-spark
