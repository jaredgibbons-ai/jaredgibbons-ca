# Mission West Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: mission-west-houses-for-sale (Mission West, Mission; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Mission West is the western side of the City of Mission, not a separate municipality. Shared area facts are carried by the earlier `mission-west-mission-guide` note; this note keeps the houses angle.
- Its ground includes Silverdale and the rural fringe toward Stave Falls and Ruskin, on streets such as Silverdale Avenue, Gunn Avenue, Hayward Street and Keystone Avenue.
- The Silverdale Comprehensive Planning Area is a 3,440-acre area, sometimes known as Southwest Mission, designated in the City's Official Community Plan (OCP) for urban growth. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies
- The board publishes no street-level boundary for the Mission West sub-area, so no municipal boundary is substituted for it.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses here mix established single-family homes on the west-side streets with acreage houses on the rural fringe toward Stave Falls. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy
- The City's Rural Mission Strategy covers the rural communities from Stave Falls, Steelhead and Silverdale to the other rural neighbourhoods, which is the planning frame for the acreage end of this stock. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy
- Silverdale is the growth end of the stock: it is designated for urban growth, with a Central Neighbourhood Plan process and a Master Infrastructure Strategy underway. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies
- Age, style and freehold-versus-strata splits for Mission West houses were not published on an official source read for this note; see NOT CONFIRMED.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Mission must allow up to four housing units on most single-family properties inside the Urban Growth Boundary, as small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH). https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/provincial-housing-targets
- SSMUH combinations in Mission can include secondary suites in single-family dwellings and detached accessory units such as garden suites or coach homes, and a building permit is still required for a new unit. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/provincial-housing-targets
- Every secondary suite in Mission must be registered with the City, suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes, and an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus applicable utility fees is applied to the property tax notice. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- Mission's zoning bylaw is Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020, which the City is amending to comply with provincial housing legislation. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/provincial-housing-targets
- A Transit-Oriented Area (TOA) applies within 400 metres of the West Coast Express station, with minimum densities and significantly reduced parking requirements; Mission West houses are outside that radius. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/provincial-housing-targets

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a suite in a Mission West house legal if it already exists? **A:** Only a registered suite is compliant, so check the registration before you rely on the income. The City requires all secondary suites to be registered, a new suite goes through the building permit process, and an unregistered suite can trigger a bylaw investigation. The annual fee is $63 plus applicable utility user fees on the tax notice. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **Q:** Which elementary school is physically on the Silverdale side? **A:** Silverdale Elementary at 29715 Donatelli Ave is the elementary school physically in the Silverdale side of this area. It is listed in the British Columbia Ministry Kindergarten to Grade 12 school file for School District 75 (SD75) Mission. Catchments are address-based, so confirm any specific address with SD75 rather than assuming this school. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy
- **Q:** Can I add a garden suite or coach home? **A:** Possibly, under Mission's SSMUH rules, if the lot is inside the Urban Growth Boundary. The City must allow up to four units on most single-family properties there, including detached accessory units such as garden suites or coach homes. Construction still needs a building permit from the City before proceeding. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/provincial-housing-targets
- **Q:** How does the West Coast Express help a Mission West house? **A:** It gives a weekday commuter train from Mission City Station, on the far side of the city from Mission West. TransLink's timetable runs five weekday westbound trains from Mission City at 5:25 to 7:25 a.m., arriving Waterfront 75 minutes later, with no weekend or statutory-holiday service. Station-to-door time for a Mission West address is not stated by an official source. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- **Q:** Is a parcel here in the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR)? **A:** Check the parcel, because ALR status changes what you can build. On ALR land the Agricultural Land Commission allows one principal residence of 500 square metres or less, a secondary suite only inside that residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 square metres on parcels of 40 hectares or less. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Mission Memorial Hospital is at 7324 Hurd Street, with a 25-bed medical inpatient unit, a 20-bed Patient Assessment and Transition Home unit and a 10-bed hospice. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/mission/mission-memorial-hospital
- Mission's water system began metering all new homes in 2009, and a voluntary meter program for homes built before 2009 was introduced in 2025. https://www.mission.ca/services/water-sewer/water/water-meters
- Unmetered homes pay a flat rate on the annual tax notice, while metered homes pay by volume on a quarterly utility bill. https://www.mission.ca/services/water-sewer/water/water-meters

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Mission's 2026 total residential tax rate is 4.4368 per $1,000 of assessed value on the City's 2026 tax rates schedule. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/2026-Tax-Rates
- Mission's 2026 flat-rate utilities for a single-family home total $1,892.28: water $641.76, sanitary sewer $786.60, garbage $166.92 and recycling and compostables $297.00. https://www.mission.ca/services/utility-bills
- A home with a secondary dwelling pays $3,207.60 in total 2026 flat-rate utilities on the same City schedule. https://www.mission.ca/services/utility-bills
- Properties inside the Mission or Silverdale Diking District pay an additional diking levy, shown as 0.0923 per $1,000 on the 2026 schedule. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/2026-Tax-Rates
- Mission's 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 10% penalty on current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- The District of Mission is listed as a taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT), so owners must complete the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Local buses in Mission are BC Transit's, in the Central Fraser Valley system. Route 32 West Heights and route 33 Cedar Valley both start at Mission City Station. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/
- Route 31 Valley Connector links Mission and Abbotsford and is listed by BC Transit in the same Central Fraser Valley route set. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/
- BC Transit notes Mission Exchange was closed from August 26, 2024 with buses servicing the Mission City Station terminal instead, anticipated until October 2026. https://www.bctransit.com/hope/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=32
- The West Coast Express is TransLink's, not BC Transit's: Mission City Station is its eastern terminus, with five weekday trains each way and limited weekday 701 bus service also available. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check diking status before buying a low-lying Mission West parcel. Mission levies additional diking taxes for properties inside the Mission Diking District or the Silverdale Diking District, which is the City's own signal that flood protection is a parcel-level issue here. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/2026-Tax-Rates
- Check ALR status parcel by parcel; subdivision and extra residences on ALR land need Agricultural Land Commission approval beyond the permitted frame. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Verify water and sewer servicing for acreage lots property by property; the City's meter and flat-rate pages describe serviced homes, and no official source read confirms servicing for a specific rural parcel. https://www.mission.ca/services/water-sewer/water/water-meters
- Confirm suite registration with the City, because an unregistered suite can require a decommission permit. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
