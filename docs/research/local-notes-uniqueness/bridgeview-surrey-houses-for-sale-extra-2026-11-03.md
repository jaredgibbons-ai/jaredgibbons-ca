# Bridgeview Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: bridgeview-surrey-houses-for-sale (Bridgeview; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Bridgeview has no City-published neighbourhood boundary of its own. The City of Surrey calls it a historic neighbourhood, and the adjoining South Westminster Neighbourhood Concept Plan (NCP) area is bordered by the Fraser River in the west, King George Boulevard in the north and 96 Avenue in the south. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/south-westminster
- The house streets sit east of that industrial plan area, around 115 Avenue, 128 Street and Old Yale Road. Anchors the City names are Bridgeview Park at 12560 115 Avenue and Bridgeview Community Centre at 11475 126A Street. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bridgeview-park
- Bridgeview is in the City of Surrey, in Whalley, north of South Westminster's port and industrial lands. The City's industrial overview puts Bridgeview (307 acres) east of King George Boulevard / the Pattullo Bridge and South Westminster (1,041 acres) west of it. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SouthwestminterBridgeviewIndustrialAreas.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached houses here are the older, smaller-lot stock of north Surrey, not a new subdivision. The nearby South Westminster Heights infill area beside Bridgeview records lot sizes from 700 square metres up to 4.12 acres across about 182 properties, which shows how mixed the old lot pattern is — check each Bridgeview lot on the City's COSMOS map rather than assuming a standard size. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/south-westminster-heights
- Houses share the area with light industrial and commercial zoning. The City states Bridgeview is predominantly zoned for residential and industrial uses, so a house can sit a street away from a work yard. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SouthwestminterBridgeviewIndustrialAreas.pdf
- Rebuilds are shaped by flood rules, not just zoning: many replacement houses sit raised, because habitable floors must meet the flood construction level (see section 3). https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/BridgeviewFloodplain.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The floodplain rule is the rule that matters most for a Bridgeview house. The City's Bridgeview Floodplain building guide requires a geotechnical engineer for construction in the floodplain, sets a Flood Construction Level (FCL), allows no habitable area below the FCL, and requires a restrictive covenant on title barring habitable floor area below the FCL. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/BridgeviewFloodplain.pdf
- The same guide flags two lot-level checks most buyers miss: methane gas from decomposing organic soils common to Bridgeview, and a Metro Vancouver sanitary trunk sewer and right-of-way crossing the floodplain area. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/BridgeviewFloodplain.pdf
- City-wide zoning, secondary-suite and small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules for Surrey houses are written in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. The Bridgeview difference is that floodplain lots also need the development permit and FCL steps above before those unit counts can be used. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/BridgeviewFloodplain.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I finish the basement of a Bridgeview house? **A:** Not as living space if it sits below the flood construction level. The City's Bridgeview Floodplain guide says habitable area is not permitted below the FCL, a restrictive covenant on title enforces that ban, and only non-habitable buildings such as garages and sheds may sit below the FCL — so a low basement is storage, not a suite. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/BridgeviewFloodplain.pdf
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Bridgeview house? **A:** Bridgeview Elementary is the in-area school, at 12834 115A Avenue, Kindergarten to Grade 7. The provincial school list gives that address and grade range; the district's family-of-schools grouping in earlier research paired it with Kwantlen Park Secondary at 10441 132 Street, but confirm the exact secondary catchment for the address in the Surrey Schools (School District 36) locator before relying on it. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** Why do some Bridgeview houses look raised or three storeys tall? **A:** Because the floor has to start at the flood construction level. City Board of Variance minutes record a Fraser River floodplain FCL of 4.4 metres with no habitable space below it, and note that houses built to that level can look three storeys high while containing two — height appeals for Bridgeview rebuilds are a known result. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/minutes/MIN_BOV_2020-03-11.pdf
- **Q:** Is truck traffic a real issue on the house streets? **A:** Yes, on some streets it is documented, not just complained about. A 2025 City planning report for 124 Street in Bridgeview records truck drivers ticketed for driving through the residential neighbourhood, heavy loads damaging ditches and roads built on softer base materials, and vibration concerns from neighbours. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7924-0145-00.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The riverfront park in the area is a construction zone during bridge removal. Brownsville Bar Park at 11931 Old Yale Road sits between the Pattullo and SkyTrain bridges, and the bridge project used the park for crane pads and a temporary access road during deconstruction preparation. https://www.pattullobridgereplacement.ca/app/uploads/sites/637/2025/11/Removal-of-the-Pattullo-Bridge-Information-Sheet.pdf
- There is no Surrey Libraries branch inside Bridgeview. Earlier City research names the City Centre branch at 10350 University Drive as the nearest branch — treat the branch, not the neighbourhood, as the library trip. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bridgeview-park
- Recreation is closer than the map suggests: the North Surrey Sport and Ice Complex at 10950 126A Street is in Bridgeview, and Bridgeview Park itself lists a spray park, two softball diamonds, a multi-use field, and tennis, pickleball and basketball courts. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bridgeview-park
- A heritage site marks the old river crossing: Brownsville Park and Sandbar, City heritage Site No. 223 in the 1100 block of Portage Road, registered January 12, 2004, at the early Brownsville ferry landing at the foot of Old Yale Road. https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/bridgeview-park

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- The Pattullo Bridge Replacement is the project that changes this area, and its new bridge is already open. The Province states all four lanes of the stal̕əw̓asəm (Riverview) Bridge opened to traffic on Saturday, February 14, 2026, the old Pattullo Bridge then closed, and its deconstruction will take approximately two years — status read 2026-11-03. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0025-000178
- On the Surrey side, deconstruction includes removing the section over Highway 17, preparation works in Brownsville Bar Park, a new Highway 17 off-ramp opening in summer 2026, and the east multi-use path opening in May 2026 with the west path by summer 2026 — all dates stated as approximate by the project. https://www.pattullobridgereplacement.ca/app/uploads/sites/637/2026/03/Upcoming-Construction-Activities-in-Surrey-Information-Sheet-Mar-10-1.pdf
- The City is also replanning the land beside the houses: on March 10, 2025 Council endorsed Corporate Report R048 concluding the South Westminster plan update for the mainly industrial lands and naming two study areas — a Waterfront District and a Transit Village at Scott Road Station. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/south-westminster

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey-wide property tax rates, utility billing and the suite fee are written in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- The Bridgeview-specific cost is development cost charges (DCCs): the City publishes a separate Bridgeview / South Westminster DCC schedule, so a rebuild, subdivision or suite project here is charged under that area schedule, not only the city-wide one. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SouthwestminterBridgeviewIndustrialAreas.pdf
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies here because the City of Surrey is listed as a designated taxable area in the Metro Vancouver Regional District, so every residential owner must file the yearly declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Flood is the first check on any Bridgeview lot. Look the address up against the City's Bridgeview Floodplain guide and the zoning bylaw's floodproofing part: habitable floors must meet the provincial FCL, a geotechnical engineer is required, and a title covenant may already ban living space below the FCL. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/BridgeviewFloodplain.pdf
- Soil is the second check: the City guide states methane gas is produced from decomposing organic soils common to Bridgeview, and a planning report for the area records roads built on softer base materials — both are reasons a geotechnical report matters here more than on Surrey's upland lots. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7924-0145-00.pdf
- The flood guide does not tell a buyer the lot's exact FCL number: it states that lots created by a recent subdivision carry minimum building elevations set by that subdivision's engineer of record, which can differ from the record-lot FCL — the figure must be confirmed lot by lot with the City. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/BridgeviewFloodplain.pdf
