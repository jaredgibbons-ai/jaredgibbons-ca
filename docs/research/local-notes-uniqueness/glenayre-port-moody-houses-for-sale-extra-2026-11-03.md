- Glenayre is a City of Port Moody neighbourhood grouped with College Park, Harbour Heights and Seaview in section 14.1 of the Port Moody 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw, 2026, No. 3522; the full shared area facts are in the glenayre-port-moody note. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Port Moody 2050, the City's Official Community Plan, was adopted by Council on April 28, 2026, and section 14.1 groups Glenayre with College Park, Harbour Heights and Seaview. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/business-and-development/official-community-plan.aspx
- The RS1-S small-lot rezoning route is not offered in Glenayre: the City's list is Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside only, so Glenayre lots use the standard RS rules and small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) instead. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Small-scale multi-unit housing is permitted in the RS and RT zones outside the City's transit-oriented areas, under the provincial housing legislation the City has implemented. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- Port Moody is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which school is in Glenayre? **A:** École Glenayre Elementary, at 495 Glencoe Drive, is the pocket's own School District No. 43 (SD43) elementary school, with the Glenayre Centre at 492 Glencoe Drive across the street; SD43 assigns catchments by address, so confirm the assigned middle and secondary schools for a specific address with the district. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/
- **Q:** Can I split a Glenayre lot under the small-lot program? **A:** No: the RS1-S small-lot rezoning is offered only in Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside, and Glenayre is not on the City's list, so the small-lot route does not apply here; small-scale multi-unit housing in the RS zones is the densification route that does. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- **Q:** What recreation is inside the pocket? **A:** The Glenayre Centre, at 492 Glencoe Drive, is the City recreation facility inside the pocket, listed on the City's recreation facilities and locations page. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/
- **Q:** Is Glenayre in Burnaby or Port Moody? **A:** Port Moody: the pocket sits between Clarke Road and the Burnaby city line, so its west edge touches Burnaby while its taxes, permits and bylaws are the City of Port Moody's under the Port Moody 2050 Official Community Plan. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Do not assume the small-lot program applies: RS1-S is not offered in Glenayre, and a purchase priced on a small-lot split would rest on a route the City does not offer here. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Confirm the SD43 catchment for the exact address, since the pocket's elementary school does not settle the middle and secondary assignments. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/
- Confirm which side of the Burnaby line a west-edge property sits on, because the municipality decides the tax and permit regime. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
