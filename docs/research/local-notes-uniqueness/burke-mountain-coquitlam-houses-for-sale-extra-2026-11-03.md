# Burke Mountain Houses — Extra Local Notes

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Burke Mountain is the City's Northeast Coquitlam growth area, and it is one of the few board sub-areas here where the board name and the City planning name are the same. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision
- The City plans it through adopted neighbourhood plans for Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek and Partington Creek, the Northwest Burke Vision for its northwest area, and Burke Mountain Village in Partington Creek. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision
- Partington Creek, the southeast plan area, is about 240 hectares (595 acres), bounded by Burke Mountain Creek, Partington Creek, Victoria Drive and an irregular boundary north of Harper Road. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17458/Official-Community-Plan---Schedule-J-PDF
- The Northwest Burke Vision area is bordered by the Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek and Partington Creek neighbourhoods to the south and Pinecone Burke Provincial Park to the north and east. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Single-detached houses and townhouses are the planned and built forms in the Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek and Partington Creek plans, with apartment form planned at the Burke Mountain Village centre. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision
- Partington Creek is the steeper plan area: the plan says it has steeper topography than the other northeast Coquitlam neighbourhoods planned to date, with views of the Fraser Valley and the Cascade Mountains. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17458/Official-Community-Plan---Schedule-J-PDF
- Subdivision minimums are 650 square metres with 18.5 metres of width in zones R-1 and R-3, and 465 square metres with 12.5 metres of width in R-2, under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996, and creek setbacks can govern the buildable area on creek-edge lots. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Creek and slope rules, not just the zoning, decide what a Burke Mountain house lot can do. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- Partington Creek has an Integrated Watershed Management Plan to retain and enhance Partington Creek and the smaller fish-bearing watercourses, which is why creek-edge lots carry setbacks that can be larger than the zoning suggests. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17458/Official-Community-Plan---Schedule-J-PDF

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary schools are actually on Burke Mountain? **A:** Three School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) elementary schools are on the mountain itself: Leigh Elementary at 1230 Soball Street, Coast Salish Elementary at 3538 Sheffield Avenue, and Smiling Creek Elementary at 3456 Princeton Avenue. None is an assignment for your address: district catchment is by permanent home address, so run the exact address in the School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** When does the village centre arrive? **A:** In stages, and the community centre is the anchor. Road construction and servicing have been under way since 2019, the Burke Mountain Discovery Centre at 3537 Princeton Avenue opened in 2022, and construction of the Burke Mountain Community Centre broke ground on May 12, 2026, with the City planning an opening in 2029. https://burkevillage.ca/village-timeline/
- **Q:** Is there a middle or secondary school on the mountain yet? **A:** The combined school is being built, but it is not the current assignment. School District No. 43 states the Burke Mountain Middle and Secondary School project, off David Avenue east of Coast Meridian Road, was about 70 percent complete in August 2026. Until it opens, a Burke Mountain address is assigned by the district locator, not by the construction site. https://sd43.bc.ca/burke-mountain-middle-secondary-school
- **Q:** What does a creek-edge lot cost me in buildable area? **A:** Potentially a lot, because Partington Creek is salmon habitat under an Integrated Watershed Management Plan. The plan protects Partington Creek and the smaller fish-bearing watercourses, and the first phase of the Partington Creek wildlife corridor is a 50-metre corridor widening to 100 metres at a wildlife hot spot — measure the setback before valuing the lot. http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/2009

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Pinecone Burke Provincial Park borders Burke Mountain to the north and east, with primary access through Harper Road off Coast Meridian Road. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision
- The Burke Mountain Discovery Centre at 3537 Princeton Avenue is a City facility already open in the village area, with the Ibex Café, and is where the City presents the future village. https://burkevillage.ca/village-timeline/
- Library service is the Coquitlam Public Library Library Link mobile service and the City Centre branch at 1169 Pinetree Way until the library inside the future community centre opens. https://www.coqlibrary.ca/
- The village is planned for more than 120,000 square feet of retail plus a public plaza and neighbourhood park, which is a plan figure, not an open shopping centre. https://burkevillage.ca/village-timeline/

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- No SkyTrain station is inside Burke Mountain; the nearest Millennium Line Evergreen Extension stations are Coquitlam Central, Lincoln and Lafarge Lake-Douglas, and Coquitlam Central also has West Coast Express service. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/station/coquitlam-central/schedule
- Route 186 runs David Avenue along the foot of the mountain via Pinetree Way, Pipeline Road and Robson Drive to Coquitlam Central Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt186.pdf
- Route 187 runs Johnson Street and the Plateau Boulevard and Paddock Drive side, from Coquitlam Central Station to Parkway Boulevard. http://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/199/tt187.pdf
- Coast Meridian Road and David Avenue are the two road connections down the mountain that the City's own school and village projects are addressed from. https://sd43.bc.ca/burke-mountain-middle-secondary-school

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the wildlife and creek corridor before the floor plan: Partington Creek is salmon habitat, and corridor and setback land is not buildable yard. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17458/Official-Community-Plan---Schedule-J-PDF
- Check slope on the City's environmental development permit mapping, which maps unstable slopes, watercourse areas and the interface wildfire risk boundary for the northeast side of the city — Burke Mountain houses sit against a forested provincial park interface. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- Check any watercourse edge against the Watercourse Protection Development Permit rules before assuming a suite, carriage house or addition fits. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status per parcel on Agricultural Land Commission mapping; no ALR land was identified on the mountain in the sources read, which is not a parcel clearance. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca
