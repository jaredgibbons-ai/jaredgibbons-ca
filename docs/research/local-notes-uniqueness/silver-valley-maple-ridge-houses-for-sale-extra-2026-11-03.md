# Silver Valley Maple Ridge Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: silver-valley-maple-ridge-houses-for-sale (Silver Valley, Maple Ridge; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Silver Valley is a real City planning area in north Maple Ridge, not just a board name: it has its own area plan in Section 10.3 of the Official Community Plan, and the City describes its forested hillsides as gateways to Golden Ears Provincial Park. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/silver?bidId=
- The plan is built around hamlets — its adopted figures name Blaney Hamlet, Forest Hamlet and Horse Hamlet — with 136 Avenue, Blaney Road, 232A Street and 256 Street as the roads that organise the house streets. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/silver-valley-figure2apdf
- The South Alouette River and Maple Ridge Park at 13180 232 Street mark the area's southern side, in Yennadon. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/projects

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Silver Valley's house forms are set by its own plan designations — Eco-Cluster Residential, Ground-Oriented Residential and Medium Density Residential — rather than by the standard RS-1 grid used in central Maple Ridge. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/Attachment-A2-Silver-Valley-Area-Plan-Clean-Copy.pdf
- The plan's highest-density form, River Village Mixed-Use, is capped at four storeys and is meant for land on collector or arterial roads, so the detached stock stays ground-oriented outside that node. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/Attachment-A2-Silver-Valley-Area-Plan-Clean-Copy.pdf
- About 80% of Silver Valley remained wooded in the plan's physical-context section, which is why so many house lots back onto treed or creek land rather than other houses. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/Attachment-A2-Silver-Valley-Area-Plan-Clean-Copy.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The governing document is the Silver Valley Area Plan itself: first adopted October 22, 2002 by Bylaw No. 6067-2002, and re-adopted in updated form by Bylaw No. 8061-2025 on November 25, 2025, with simplified designations and a new zoning matrix. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/silver-valley-figure2apdf
- The 2025 update modernises the 2002 plan, consolidates land-use designations and adds density required by provincial housing legislation, so a lot's plan designation — not just its zoning — has to be read before assuming a rebuild or added unit works. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/silver?bidId=
- Suites and SSMUH counts are city-wide — see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note; the Silver Valley angle is the plan's Eco-Cluster and conservation designations, which can sit over or beside a house lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/Attachment-A2-Silver-Valley-Area-Plan-Clean-Copy.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q: Is there a school inside Silver Valley?** **A:** No school building inside the plan area was confirmed on a district source in this pass. The plan itself contains a school-sites section and the City is building park amenities first; the school serving the area on district lists is Yennadon Elementary at 23347 128 Avenue, south of the plan area. Run any exact address in the School District 42 locator before relying on a catchment. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: Why do so many lots back onto green space?** **A:** Because the plan was written that way. The Silver Valley Area Plan carries Conservation and Open Space designations alongside its residential ones, names watercourses on its hamlet figures, and its context section records that about 80% of the area remained wooded. That setting is also why creek setbacks and slope checks matter more here than in central Maple Ridge. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/Attachment-A2-Silver-Valley-Area-Plan-Clean-Copy.pdf
- **Q: What is being built for residents right now?** **A:** A new one-acre neighbourhood park at 13650 232A Street, at Blaney Road. The City says the site slopes downhill from Blaney Road toward 232A Street, sits beside a conservation area to the north with trail access to the south, is in the 2025 Capital Plan, and has an estimated completion of early 2027. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/city-projects/silver-valley-new-park-development-blaney-rd-232a-st
- **Q: Is Silver Valley on the city's growth path or a finished area?** **A:** It is still a growth area under an active plan. Council adopted the updated Silver Valley Area Plan on November 25, 2025 to modernise the 2002 plan and add provincially required density, and the City's project list still shows new trails, pedestrian bridges and trail improvements planned for the Silver Valley area under that plan. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/silver?bidId=

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Blaney Hamlet Park at 13712 230A Street is the existing hamlet park, and the new park at 232A Street and Blaney Road is a separate, additional one-acre site — two different parks on the same Blaney corridor. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/blaney-hamlet-park
- Maple Ridge Park, at 13180 232 Street on the South Alouette River, is having its picnic shelters, washrooms and parking upgraded, with construction that began the week of March 23, 2026 and the south (232 Street) parking lot reopened while the north (132 Avenue) lot closes. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/city-projects/maple-ridge-park-picnic-shelters-washrooms-parking
- The City calls Silver Valley's creeks and rivers some of the richest salmon habitat in the Lower Mainland, so a creek-side lot here carries a working fish stream, not just a view. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/silver?bidId=

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Silver Valley houses pay standard City of Maple Ridge taxes and utilities — the 2026 residential rate, water charge, due dates and Speculation and Vacancy Tax position are in full in the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Silver Valley has no West Coast Express station and no rapid-transit station; its roads out are 232 Street and 256 Street south to Dewdney Trunk Road and Lougheed Highway, and the stations are Maple Meadows and Port Haney, across town. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- The plan area's own figures show its internal structure as hamlet centres within a 200-metre, five-minute-walk neighbourhood radius, which is a plan concept for local trips, not a transit service. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/silver-valley-figure2apdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Slope is the first Silver Valley check: the City's own new-park page describes its Blaney Road site sloping downhill toward 232A Street beside a conservation area, and the plan area is hillside above the South Alouette River. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/city-projects/silver-valley-new-park-development-blaney-rd-232a-st
- Wildfire interface is the second check: lots in the City's Wildfire Development Permit Area need a development permit for building or subdivision, or a restrictive covenant on design and landscaping for a single detached home. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/wildfire-development-permit-construction-requirements
- Creek and flood status is read under Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 8121-2026, which covers steep creek watercourses as well as the Alouette and Fraser systems — see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-climate-action-flood-resilience-environmental-protection-and-mckenney-creek
