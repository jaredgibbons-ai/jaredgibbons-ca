# Port Guichon — Extra Local Notes
Page: port-guichon (Port Guichon; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Port Guichon is the west Ladner pocket around Port Guichon Elementary and West River Road, in the City of Delta. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/037
- The school inside the pocket is Port Guichon Elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 7) at 4381 46a Street. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/037
- Ladner is one of Delta's South Delta communities, and Port Guichon is governed by the Delta Official Community Plan (OCP) 2024, Bylaw No. 8400, adopted July 8, 2024, with no separate Ladner area-plan schedule. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The pocket is low-density and ground-oriented, with detached houses, half duplexes and river-side homes. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- Delta's Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) framework already shows here as coach houses above rear-lane garages on Savoy Street homes. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- No townhome complex inside Port Guichon was confirmed on an official or developer source in this pass. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Up to four units are allowed on single-detached and duplex lots under Delta's SSMUH zoning updates. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- The City supports accessory dwelling units including coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- Any lot within 30 metres of a stream, slough or ditch is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area in OCP Appendix B. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- Farm land in Delta is designated Agriculture (A) in the OCP, Section 1.5.1. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is inside Port Guichon? **A:** Port Guichon Elementary is inside the pocket, at 4381 46a Street for Kindergarten to Grade 7. The British Columbia (BC) Ministry of Education list for School District 37 (Delta) gives that name and address. Whether a specific home address falls in its catchment must still be checked by address, because district catchments are address-based. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/037
- **Q:** How do I confirm my catchment? **A:** Check the exact address in the district School Locator before relying on a school. School District 37 directs families to the Baragar Online School Locator by residential address or postal code. The district also publishes Ladner Area catchment boundary maps, updated October 2017. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q:** Can I add a coach house or suite? **A:** A coach house or suite may be possible, subject to a parcel-level City check. Delta's SSMUH updates allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots. The City also runs help for owners creating coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites. River-side lots need extra checks for streamside and flood rules. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q:** How do residents reach the Canada Line? **A:** Residents reach the Canada Line by TransLink route 601 to Bridgeport Station. The route runs via South Delta Exchange, Highway 17, Ladner Exchange, Ladner Trunk Road, Highway 17A and Highway 99. Check the current timetable for the stop nearest a specific address. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- **Q:** What plan governs building here? **A:** The Delta OCP 2024 governs building here, as one consolidated plan. Council adopted Bylaw No. 8400 on July 8, 2024. Its Land Use Plan in Section 1.5 carries the designations. There is no separate Ladner schedule in that bylaw. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
Read: 2026-11-03

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- For 2026 the flat-rate utility total is $1,658 for a single-family home, up from $1,607 in 2025, billed annually at the end of February for garbage and recycling, with water and sewer on the flat bill or quarterly metered bills. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- The City of Delta is a listed taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so residential owners must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check flood construction requirements for the specific lot with the City, because Ladner is low land behind dikes. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Check whether the lot is within 30 metres of a stream, slough or ditch, which triggers the Streamside Development Permit Area. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- On West River Road homes, verify foreshore tenure, docks and moorage from title and the City, not from listing text. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
