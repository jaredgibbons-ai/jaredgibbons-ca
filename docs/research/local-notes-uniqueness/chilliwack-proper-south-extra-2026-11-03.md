# Chilliwack Proper South Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: chilliwack-proper-south (Chilliwack Proper South; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Chilliwack Proper South is the southern part of Chilliwack Proper, the city's original townsite north of Sardis, in the City of Chilliwack; the City plans the whole district as one area in the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan, Schedule B-2 of Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw 2025, No. 5500. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=5211
- The City plan divides Chilliwack Proper into four sub-areas — Mountainview (east of downtown), Riverside (north of downtown), Townsend (west of downtown) and Fairfield Island — and defines the planning area as the land around the Downtown plan area, bounded by First Nations land to the west and the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) to the north and east. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=5211
- The working streets of the southern Proper are Yale Road, Young Road, Hodgins Avenue and Bernard Avenue, with Highway 1 along the south edge; the City plan names Hodgins Avenue and the Ashwell/Evans Road route as the area's strong links to downtown and to Highway 1. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=5211
- A Canadian National railway line runs along the southern boundary of the plan area, and the City plan records that it reduces street connectivity and requires a 30-metre setback from residences. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=5211

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is older detached houses on a townsite grid plus infill: the City plan notes several streets here were built as cul-de-sacs in the 1980s, which limits through-connections compared with the older grid streets. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=5211
- Infill is the growth pattern the City plans for: the plan lists opportunities for infill development near Bernard Elementary School, Townsend Park and Chilliwack General Hospital, close to downtown. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=5211
- The area's schools sit inside the neighbourhood: Bernard Elementary at 45465 Bernard Avenue and McCammon Traditional Elementary at 9601 Hamilton Street, both Kindergarten to Grade 5 in Chilliwack School District (SD33); Robertson Elementary is at 46106 Southlands Crescent. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/chilliwack
- Chilliwack Secondary School, the area's high school, is at 46363 Yale Road. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/chilliwack

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Most detached lots here are zoned R1-A (Urban Residential) under Chilliwack's Zoning Bylaw, and the City is actively rezoning individual R1-A lots — an April 2026 notice proposes 46510 Brooks Avenue from R1-A to R4 (Low Density Multi-Unit Residential) for a townhouse development. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/notices/Notice_of_Application_-_RZ1803.pdf
- Land use is set by the 2050 OCP, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, and the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan (adopted May 2018, updated 2025) is its Schedule B-2 for this district. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=5211
- New construction in the protected floodplain here must meet Chilliwack's Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 — see Section 9; city staff reports describe Yale Road properties in Chilliwack Proper as lying within the protected floodplain. https://pub-chilliwack.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11619
- Development Permit Area No. 5 (Urban Corridor) applies on the Yale Road corridor: the City required a development permit for the supportive housing project at 45466 Yale Road because the property is in that area. https://pub-chilliwack.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11619

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school will my child attend? **A:** It depends on the exact street address, and three SD33 elementaries serve parts of the Proper: Bernard Elementary (45465 Bernard Avenue), McCammon Traditional Elementary (9601 Hamilton Street) and Robertson Elementary (46106 Southlands Crescent). Chilliwack School District assigns by catchment, so confirm the address in the district's school locator before removing subjects. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/chilliwack
- **Q:** Is this area in the floodplain? **A:** Parts of it are. The City's floodplain guide states that much of Chilliwack lies in the floodplain of the Fraser River, the Vedder River, the Sumas River or valley creeks, and Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets the building elevations for floodplain lots. Chilliwack Proper properties can sit inside the protected (diked) floodplain, which still carries flood construction levels. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- **Q:** Can I add a suite or redevelop an older Proper lot? **A:** Sometimes — the City plan supports infill near downtown, and Council hears R1-A rezoning applications for exactly that, such as the 46510 Brooks Avenue application to move from R1-A to the R4 multi-unit zone for townhouses. Permission is lot-by-lot through rezoning or the zoning bylaw, not automatic, so check the specific lot with the Planning Department. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/notices/Notice_of_Application_-_RZ1803.pdf
- **Q:** How do I get downtown or to the highway without a car? **A:** BC Transit Route 51 (Vedder) runs the Yale Road–Vedder Road spine between Downtown Chilliwack and the University of the Fraser Valley (UFV) Chilliwack campus, and Route 66 Fraser Valley Express leaves the Downtown Exchange for Abbotsford, Langley and Lougheed Station. Route 52 (Evans) serves the Evans Road side toward Cottonwood Mall. https://www.bctransit.com/service-improvements-in-chilliwack-and-agassiz-harrison-2025-12-18/
- **Q:** What is the railway I can see south of the neighbourhood? **A:** It is the rail line the City plan maps along the southern boundary of Chilliwack Proper; the plan requires a 30-metre setback from residences next to it, and it is why several Proper streets end as cul-de-sacs instead of connecting south. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=5211

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Chilliwack's 2025 residential tax rate was 4.50189 per $1,000 of assessed value all-in, of which the municipal total was 2.83455 per $1,000 — the City's own 2025 Property Class Tax Rates table breaks that municipal total into general government, services, culture and dyking components. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2527/Tax%5FRates%5Ffor%5Fthe%5FWebtugnc2fs1l9d%2Epdf
- The City of Chilliwack publishes its approved tax rates by year on its Tax & Utility Rates page, and states that Council must adopt the tax bylaw each year before May 15. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies in Chilliwack: the Province's taxable-areas list names the City of Chilliwack, so residential owners here file the annual SVT declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- BC Transit runs Chilliwack's local system: Route 51 (Vedder) links Downtown to the UFV Chilliwack campus via Vedder Road, Route 52 (Evans) links Downtown to Cottonwood Mall via Evans Road, and Routes 53 (Fairfield), 57 (Broadway) and 58 (Tyson) serve the north-side streets. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chw_rg_jan2024-V1.1.pdf
- Route 66 Fraser Valley Express is the regional link: the Fraser Valley Regional District states it connects Chilliwack and Abbotsford with Langley and Burnaby via Highway 1, and BC Transit expanded its Saturday and Sunday trips from Chilliwack in January 2026. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html
- Chilliwack local transit fares in the January 2024 Rider's Guide were $2.00 adult single ride and $5.00 for a DayPASS, marked "subject to change" — check the current guide before relying on them. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chw_rg_jan2024-V1.1.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 is the key document: it designates the floodplains of the Fraser River, Chilliwack River, Vedder River and Vedder Canal as floodplain and sets Flood Construction Levels (FCL) on its Schedule A map. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- The City's residential floodplain guide states the build rule plainly: the underside of the lowest habitable floor must reach the FCL plus 0.6 metres of freeboard, basements are not permitted (a crawlspace of at most 1.5 metres may be), and the freeboard can only be waived with a Section 219 covenant on title plus a qualified professional's report. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- On ALR land protected by standard dikes, the guide states FCLs have generally been standardized at 1.2 metres above natural ground, with a flood covenant registered on title. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- Look a lot up on the City's floodplain map (Schedule A to Bylaw 4519) and on title for a flood covenant; what the bylaw does not tell you is whether a specific existing house was built to the current level — that needs the building file and a survey.
- The subdivisions reference to a "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" is CONTESTED: the City's webpage names it, but it is held in the verified program ledger as never adopted, so treat any reliance on it as UNCONFIRMED.
