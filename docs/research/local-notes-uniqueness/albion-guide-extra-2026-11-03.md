# Albion Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: albion-guide (Albion, Maple Ridge; Guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Albion is the east Maple Ridge growth area around 240 Street and Lougheed Highway, listed by the City as "Albion/Kanaka." https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The Albion Area Plan, Chapter 10.2 of the Official Community Plan (OCP), is the closest official boundary document, including the North East Albion area and the Albion Flats next to Lougheed Highway and 240 Street. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId=
- 240 Street runs north-south, 102 Avenue and 104 Avenue run east-west through the housing, and the Fraser River forms the south edge. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId=
- Kanaka Creek runs along the west side, with Cottonwood and East Central to the west and Thornhill to the northeast. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Albion's housing is newer detached houses plus ground-oriented and townhouse product, matching the plan's own designation mix. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/albion-area-plan-schedule-1pdf-1
- The Area Plan designations are Low Density Residential, Ground-Oriented Residential, Cluster Residential and Townhouse, plus an Albion Village Commercial node. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/albion-area-plan-schedule-1pdf-1
- The industrial lands sit in the Albion Flats by Lougheed Highway, separate from the residential corridors on 102 Avenue and 104 Avenue. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/socioeconomictrendsimplicationsgp-rollosept2010

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Why is Albion called Albion, and what was the ferry? **A:** The Albion name survives in the ferry landing that served this riverfront for over 50 years. A City-commissioned study of the Albion Flats records that the six-lane Golden Ears Bridge replaced the Albion Ferry service in 2009, and that in its last years the ferry carried about 1.5 million vehicles and 4.0 million passengers a year, with waits over an hour at busy times. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/socioeconomictrendsimplicationsgp-rollosept2010
**Q:** What changed in the 2025 Albion Area Plan update? **A:** The update, adopted October 28, 2025, changed how the growth edge is handled, not the area's basic residential role. The City states it enhances walking and cycling connections, supports growth of local commercial nodes, introduces buffers where urban development meets farmland, and balances new housing against protection of environmentally sensitive areas. Check a lot's current designation before assuming added density. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId=
**Q:** Which schools are actually in Albion? **A:** Three schools sit inside the area, with a fourth at its west edge. Albion Elementary is at 10031 240 Street, c'usqunela Elementary is at 24093 104 Avenue, Samuel Robertson Technical Secondary is at 10445 245 Street, and Kanaka Creek Elementary is at 11120 234a Street; School District 42 catchments are address-based, so run the exact address in the district locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
**Q:** Is the south edge in the floodplain? **A:** The Fraser River edge lots are the ones to check, because the flood rule is elevation-based. The flood construction level is 6.7 metres geodetic including freeboard, no habitable floor space may sit below it on an affected lot, a covenant on title identifies floodplain construction, and suites are prohibited on floodplain properties. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Albion Community Centre at 24165 104 Avenue is co-located with c'usqunela Elementary at 104 Avenue and 240 Street, and opened June 22, 2022. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/city-projects/albion-community-centre-development
- The centre replaced the historic Albion Hall on 240 Street, built by volunteers in 1923 and demolished after a 2010 inspection found it at end of life. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2022-06-20-albion-community-centre-our-new-multi-use-gatheringpdf
- Kanaka Creek Regional Park runs 400 hectares along 12 kilometres of Kanaka Creek, with the Canyon Trail to Cliff Falls and the Bell-Irving Hatchery. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- The Albion Fairgrounds and Albion Sports Complex at 23778 104 Avenue host Planet Ice and the Golden Ears Winter Club curling rink. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Route 748 loops Haney Place via Lougheed Highway, River Road, 240 Street and 100 Avenue through Thornhill and Albion streets. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt748.pdf
- There is no library branch in Albion; Maple Ridge Public Library is at 130 – 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road in the Town Centre. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/maple-ridge-public-library
Read: 2026-11-03

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Maple Ridge's 2026 general residential rate is $2.8643 per $1,000 of taxable value — see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note for the full city tax schedule. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- The City of Maple Ridge is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so Albion owners file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- No West Coast Express station sits in Albion; Port Haney Station at 22300 River Road and Maple Meadows Station serve the city from the west side. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Route 748 is the Albion loop bus to Haney Place, via 240 Street and 100 Avenue. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt748.pdf
- The Golden Ears Bridge, west of Albion, replaced the Albion Ferry in 2009 and is now the Fraser River crossing to Langley and Surrey. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/socioeconomictrendsimplicationsgp-rollosept2010
Read: 2026-11-03

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check floodplain status and the title covenant for any lot near the Fraser River or Kanaka Creek, using the City's floodplain construction guide and maps. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- Check ALR status parcel by parcel for South Albion lots at the farmland edge, where the 2025 plan adds urban-farmland buffers. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- Kanaka Creek is a riparian corridor, so lots backing onto the creek need a development-permit and tree check the plan map alone does not settle. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
Read: 2026-11-03

## 10. HOW IT COMPARES
- Albion has its own area plan; Whonnock and Northwest Maple Ridge do not, and are governed by the OCP alone. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId=
- Albion has a community centre inside the area, opened in 2022; Whonnock's local facility is the smaller Whonnock Lake Centre. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/city-projects/albion-community-centre-development
- All three areas are in School District 42, with address-based catchments checked in the same district locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
