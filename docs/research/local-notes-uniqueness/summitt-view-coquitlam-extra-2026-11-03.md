## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Summitt View has no City-published neighbourhood boundary in the sources read, so this note anchors the name to the northeast Coquitlam corridor the City does plan in writing: the lower Burke Mountain slopes around David Avenue, Coast Meridian Road and Pinetree Way. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/790/Northwest-Burke-Vision-PDF
- The City's Northwest Burke Vision states the Burke area's north-south access is provided by Coast Meridian Road and Pipeline Road, with David Avenue and Victoria Drive as the important east-west arterials. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/790/Northwest-Burke-Vision-PDF
- Westwood Plateau is the planned area to the west of the Burke lands, and the Coquitlam River escarpment bisects the western portion of the Northwest Burke Vision area. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/790/Northwest-Burke-Vision-PDF

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The whole city is covered by the Interim Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 5511, 2026, adopted April 27, 2026, which carries four development permit areas: Urban Design, Watercourse Protection, Wildfire Hazard and Unstable Slopes. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17000/Official-Community-Plan---Schedule-A-PDF
- Northeast Coquitlam has the wider creek rule: a Watercourse Protection Development Permit is required before altering land within 50 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse — the rest of Coquitlam uses 30 metres. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- City-wide Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) rules, zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 and the June 2025 unit counts are in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; the local trigger here is the same one the City flags — a creek or a steep slope on or next to the lot means contact Development Services before designing. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- The Northwest Burke Vision names the watercourses buyers on this slope meet by name: the Coquitlam River, Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek and Burke Mountain Creek, each with Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area setbacks. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/790/Northwest-Burke-Vision-PDF

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Summitt View an official City neighbourhood? **A:** No City source read for this note defines "Summitt View" as a neighbourhood with boundaries. The name is used for this page's pocket on the lower Burke Mountain slopes, but the documents that govern a lot here are the City-wide Interim OCP, the Northwest Burke Vision for the Burke lands, and the parcel's zone — so verify any claim about the pocket against those documents, not the name. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/790/Northwest-Burke-Vision-PDF
- **Q:** Is Summit Middle School in this pocket, and what is its address? **A:** Summit Middle School is an SD43 school at 1450 Parkway Boulevard, on Westwood Plateau — not at a Summit Drive address, and not on Burke Mountain. A recorded SD43 School Locator run for 1420 Pinetree Way, in the neighbouring Canyon Springs pocket, returned Pinetree Way Elementary, Summit Middle and Pinetree Secondary, which shows plateau schools serving Pinetree Way addresses; it does not assign a Summitt View address. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/summit https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** How do residents reach SkyTrain? **A:** By bus to Coquitlam Central Station, because there is no rapid transit on this slope. TransLink route 186 runs David Avenue via Pinetree Way, Pipeline Road and Robson Drive between Coquitlam Central Station and Hampton Park, which is the David Avenue corridor service for the lower Burke Mountain streets. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt186.pdf
- **Q:** Does a creek setback change what I can build? **A:** Yes, if the lot is close enough to a watercourse. In Northeast Coquitlam the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area extends 50 metres from the top of bank of any watercourse, and altering land inside it needs the permit first, with conditions for riparian protection and slope stability. Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek and Burke Mountain Creek all cross this slope, so measure the lot against the creek map first. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Q:** Where are the local shops and services? **A:** At the David Avenue and Coast Meridian Road corner, on the evidence of the City's own Burke planning documents: the Northwest Burke Vision describes commercial nodes at that intersection offering professional services, restaurants and convenience stores, and identifies Burke Mountain Village as the planned larger centre for the mountain. Anything beyond that description needs a current City project page. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/790/Northwest-Burke-Vision-PDF
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- The Northwest Burke Vision plans improved access to Pinecone Burke Provincial Park from this side of the city, as a planning direction rather than a built road. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/790/Northwest-Burke-Vision-PDF
- The Coquitlam River floodplain sits at the foot of this slope in the Vision's mapping, even though the house streets are upland — the flood check and the creek check are two different lookups here. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/790/Northwest-Burke-Vision-PDF

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the Watercourse Protection layer first: the 50-metre Northeast Coquitlam permit area is wider than the city norm, and the permit guide does not itself set the final setback for a specific lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
