## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- College Park is an established residential area at Port Moody's west end, next to Glenayre and Seaview near the Burnaby border; the full shared area facts are in the college-park-port-moody note. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/city-hall/resources/Documents/OCP-neighbourhood-plan-areas_-_edited_for_EngagePM_website.pdf
- The City's Port Moody 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP), Chapter 14, groups College Park with Harbour Heights, Glenayre and Seaview as one neighbourhood plan section. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- The Seaview sub-area beside it extends from College Park Way to Ingersoll Street and from Seaforth Way to Glenayre Drive, and exact board sub-area boundary roads for College Park itself are not stated on a City page read on 2026-11-03. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050/news_feed/seaview

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is detached houses on the Clarke Road and College Park Way side of the city's west end, under the RS zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 2937. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/city-hall/resources/Documents/OCP-neighbourhood-plan-areas_-_edited_for_EngagePM_website.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The RS1-S small-lot rezoning route is NOT available in College Park: the City's list is Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside only, so a College Park lot cannot use the small-lot program available two neighbourhoods east. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) is permitted in the RS and RT zones outside the City's transit-oriented areas, which is the densification route that does apply here. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- Development Permit Area 4 (environmentally sensitive) and Development Permit Area 5 (hazardous lands and steep slope) apply at this end of the city, and a riparian assessment can be required under section 5.4 of the Zoning Bylaw. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Port Moody is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I split a College Park lot under the small-lot program? **A:** No: the RS1-S small-lot rezoning is offered only in Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside, and College Park is not on the City's list, so the small-lot route used elsewhere in Port Moody does not apply here; small-scale multi-unit housing in the RS zones is the route that does. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- **Q:** What is the Suncor lands issue beside College Park? **A:** The Suncor refinery lands beside College Park and Seaview are an Official Community Plan Special Study Area, which makes them the main planning item over this end of the city: any change there runs through a special study rather than a routine application, and buyers should read the study-area status before relying on the lands staying as they are. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- **Q:** Is there a school in College Park? **A:** A school building stands at 300 Princeton Avenue, but it does not appear in School District No. 43's current Port Moody school list, so its status is unverified — confirm the catchment schools for the exact address with SD43 rather than assuming the building assigns them. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/city-hall/resources/Documents/OCP-neighbourhood-plan-areas_-_edited_for_EngagePM_website.pdf
- **Q:** Where is the nearest recreation centre? **A:** The Glenayre Centre, at 492 Glencoe Drive, is the recreation facility in adjoining Glenayre, and École Glenayre Elementary stands at 495 Glencoe Drive beside it. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- College Park is the closest Port Moody neighbourhood to the Burnaby border on the Barnet Highway (Highway 7A) and St. Johns Street route, so its west edge is a municipal boundary, not just a street. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- The City plans College Park together with Harbour Heights, Glenayre and Seaview in one Chapter 14 section, so Official Community Plan reading for this pocket is a four-neighbourhood section, not a College Park chapter. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Seaview's stated extent — College Park Way to Ingersoll Street, Seaforth Way to Glenayre Drive — is the closest thing to a boundary the City publishes for this cluster. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050/news_feed/seaview

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Do not assume the small-lot program applies: RS1-S is not offered in College Park, and a purchase priced on a small-lot split would rest on a route the City does not offer here. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Check Development Permit Area 4 and 5 status and any riparian assessment under Zoning Bylaw section 5.4 for lots near creeks or steep slopes at this end of the city. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Confirm the SD43 catchment for the exact address, since the Princeton Avenue school building's status is unverified. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/city-hall/resources/Documents/OCP-neighbourhood-plan-areas_-_edited_for_EngagePM_website.pdf
