# Edmonds Condos — Extra Local Notes
Page: edmonds-condos-for-sale (Edmonds, Burnaby; condos).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Edmonds' plan area is bounded by Imperial Street to the north, Canada Way to the east, 10th Avenue to the south, and Gilley Avenue/Marine Drive to the west, per the Edmonds Community Plan's plan-area boundary section — all lands inside that boundary are considered part of Edmonds under the plan. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-04/Edmonds-Community-Plan.pdf
- The Edmonds Community Plan was adopted in March 2025, and Edmonds is one of Burnaby's four town centres; the plan area is about 908 acres with three nodes — the Town Centre at Kingsway and Edmonds Street, the Station node at Edmonds Station, and Southgate City — and six districts. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects-direct-link?page=2
- The plan's three nodes are where the condo stock concentrates: Kingsway corridor mid- and high-rises at the Town Centre node, towers at the Station node, and the Southgate City master plan at the south node. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-04/Edmonds-Community-Plan.pdf City-wide Burnaby facts: see the vancouver-heights-burnaby-houses-for-sale note.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Edmonds Community Plan (adopted March 2025) is the current plan for this ground, and Burnaby 2050, adopted December 9, 2025, takes precedence over older community-plan land-use maps where they conflict — so pre-2025 Edmonds plan wording is not the current direction for a Kingsway lot. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- Zoning for the corridor stock is in the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (Bylaw No. 14815): its district list runs Low-Rise Apartment (R3, R4), Mid-Rise Apartment (R5, R6) and High-Rise Apartment (R7, R8), with a Townhouse District (R2) for ground-oriented forms. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-07/Zoning-Bylaw-Consolidated-Without-Schedules.pdf
- An Edmonds strata cannot enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw: the Province states that as of November 24, 2022 all strata residential rental-restriction bylaws are invalid, only a 55-or-older age bylaw is allowed, and short-term rental bylaws remain allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- The strata budget rule to check on a Form B is the contingency reserve fund (CRF): the Province states a strata must contribute a minimum of 10% of its annual operating fund to the CRF when it approves its budget (from November 1, 2023). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** What is Southgate City, and how big is it?
**A:** Southgate City is a 60-acre master-planned community by Ledingham McAllister, one of the three nodes in the March 2025 Edmonds Community Plan. On the City's major-projects list its Gateway neighbourhood phases (REZ #22-30 and #22-31B) total 819 market strata homes plus 115 non-market rental homes in one phase alone, with towers, podiums and low-rise buildings — so an Edmonds condo buyer is often buying beside an active multi-phase build, not into a finished neighbourhood. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2024-08/Major-Development-Projects.pdf

**Q:** Which station serves an Edmonds condo?
**A:** Edmonds Station, on the Expo Line, serves the Station node, and the 116 bus runs between Metrotown Station and Edmonds Station through the area's streets. The Kingsway corridor stock is a bus ride or a walk from the station depending on the block, and the Edmonds Community Plan's three-node structure — Town Centre, Station, Southgate City — is the map a buyer should use to judge that distance. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-04/Edmonds-Community-Plan.pdf

**Q:** What is being planned at Kingsway and Edmonds Street itself?
**A:** A conceptual master plan by the City with BC Housing for the southwest corner of Kingsway and Edmonds Street (REZ #20-32, applicant Aplin and Martin Consultants Ltd., addresses 7252 and 7282 Kingsway and 7255 Edmonds Street). The City's list describes 1,076 market strata homes and 233 market median rental homes and records the file at Second Reading on June 5, 2023 — a concept-stage file, not a sales program. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2024-08/Major-Development-Projects.pdf

**Q:** Is the Edmonds Community Centre part of the condo area?
**A:** Yes: the Edmonds Community Centre at 7433 Edmonds Street sits in the Town Centre node and houses a 6-lane swimming pool, a leisure pool with waterslides and a lazy river, a steam room and sauna, two gymnasiums, and the Edmonds Centre for 55+ inside the same building, per the City's facility page. It is the civic anchor the Kingsway corridor condos use day to day. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/edmonds-community-centre

**Q:** Can an Edmonds strata stop me renting my unit out?
**A:** No, not long-term: the Province states no strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw and all such bylaws are invalid as of November 24, 2022. A building can still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw, and only a 55-or-older age-restriction bylaw is allowed — so read the registered bylaws for those two clauses, not for a rental ban. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Edmonds plan names a creek daylighting move: the plan's big moves include supporting efforts to daylight Powerhouse Creek as future development occurs, and enhancing parks and trails into a blue-green space network — a creek fact that affects lots near the corridor, not only ravine lots. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-04/Edmonds-Community-Plan.pdf
- Byrne Creek runs south through the area: the Province's dike list names a Byrne Creek dike, 4.35 kilometres, administered by the City of Burnaby — the document a buyer of a lot near Byrne Creek Ravine Park starts from. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/air-land-water/water/integrated-flood-hazard-mgmt/dikes_listed_by_riverwatercoursepdf.pdf
- A Kingsway rental tower is part of the current file set, not strata competition: Intracorp's Kingsway Balmoral project at 6535 Kingsway (REZ #25-09) is a 12-storey mixed-use building with 290 rental units, at Third Reading October 27, 2026 on the City list. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2024-08/Major-Development-Projects.pdf
- Townhouse-form stock exists in Edmonds but is not this page's product: Mosaic Homes' townhouse project on Bevan Street and Stride Avenue addresses (REZ #22-04) combines market and below-market rental townhouses and reached Final Adoption January 27, 2026 — it belongs to the Edmonds townhomes page, and is named here once. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2024-08/Major-Development-Projects.pdf
- Burnaby's water-meter program began in June 2026 with about 5,500 meter-ready homes: meters are mandatory and installed free, and flat-rate billing continues while data is collected, per the City. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewers/residential-water-meter-program

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby's 2026 municipal residential rate and city-wide utility and Speculation and Vacancy Tax treatment: see the vancouver-heights-burnaby-houses-for-sale note.
- The condo utility figure is the City's 2026 Multiple Family Dwelling line: water $374.54 and sewer use $436.00 per year, with a 5% discount if the full payment is received before the due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- The City states a change in how the sewer parcel tax is billed: it appears on the property tax notice each May, and starting in 2027 it moves to the utility notice — a billing-location change a strata owner will see in 2027, not a new charge. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- The 2026 financial plan held water at 0% and raised sewer about $14 for a condo. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2026-01-28/burnaby-keeps-tax-rates-affordable-latest-financial-plan

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Edmonds Station (Expo Line) is the rapid-transit anchor for the Station node; the City is also running an Edmonds Town Centre Cycling Network project, listed on its projects page, which is the document for street-by-street cycling changes. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects-direct-link?page=2
- Route 116 runs between Metrotown Station and Edmonds Station via Griffiths Drive, Southridge Drive and 18th Avenue, which is the bus a Southgate City or 18th Avenue owner uses to reach either station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt116.pdf
- Kingsway is the corridor arterial: the Edmonds plan's big moves include transforming Kingsway into a safer, more pedestrian-friendly boulevard and Edmonds Street into a mixed-use Character Street, which is the plan context for corridor construction in coming years. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-04/Edmonds-Community-Plan.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Creek and ravine lots carry the area's lot-level check: Byrne Creek and Powerhouse Creek cross the plan area, the Province's dike list records a 4.35-kilometre Byrne Creek dike administered by the City of Burnaby, and the Edmonds plan proposes daylighting Powerhouse Creek — a buyer near either creek should confirm the lot's streamside and flood status with the City before subjects are removed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/air-land-water/water/integrated-flood-hazard-mgmt/dikes_listed_by_riverwatercoursepdf.pdf
- Tenure is the building-level check on Kingsway: the corridor holds both strata towers and non-market rental towers, and the City's project list is the fastest public check of what a given address was approved as. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2024-08/Major-Development-Projects.pdf
- For any strata purchase, the depreciation report and Form B are the systems check: the Province tracks updated depreciation report requirements effective July 1, 2024, so a report dated before that was prepared under the older requirements. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
