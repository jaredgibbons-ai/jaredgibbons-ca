## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A sign that needs a permit in Maple Ridge goes through the City's Sign Permit Application under Sign Bylaw No. 7620 – 2020, which asks for the sign type (including directional), the project address, the zone, and whether the sign is illuminated — an illuminated sign also needs an electrical permit application. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2023-11-import/Sign%20Permit%20Application_202211071748268179.pdf
- Off-site directional signs pointing to a development are a separate, permitted category: the City's Temporary Construction Sign Application covers requests to install directional signs to developments only, and off-premises construction signs identifying a development's location need the municipal engineer's approval under section 6.9 and clauses 12.2.1 to 12.2.3 of the sign bylaw. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/sign-permit-application-development-directionalpdf
- The City publishes set locations for development directional signs — location D1 is the corner of 201st Street and Lougheed Highway — so directional signage is placed at city-approved corners, not wherever a seller chooses. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/sign-permit-application-development-directionalpdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can my agent put directional signs anywhere to guide people to my Maple Ridge open house? **A:** No — directional signage is a regulated category here, not a free choice. Signs are governed by Maple Ridge Sign Bylaw No. 7620 – 2020, development directional signs go through the City's Temporary Construction Sign Application, and the City publishes approved sign locations such as the corner of 201st Street and Lougheed Highway. The specific open-house sandwich-board rules were not confirmed — see NOT CONFIRMED. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/sign-permit-application-development-directionalpdf
- **Q:** What does a directional sign permit cost the seller? **A:** The City's temporary construction sign permit requires a $500 deposit. The applicant deposits $500 in cash or cheque with the City to guarantee the permit's terms are fulfilled, and the permit can be cancelled if the holder does not comply with Sign Bylaw No. 7620 – 2020. That deposit is for development directional signage; a single-home open house may not need this permit class at all. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/sign-permit-application-development-directionalpdf
- **Q:** How do I reach a Maple Ridge open house without a car? **A:** Use the West Coast Express or the 791 bus. Maple Ridge has two West Coast Express stations, Maple Meadows and Port Haney, and TransLink's route 791 runs from Haney Place through Maple Meadows Station and Pitt Meadows Station to Braid Station, connecting to the SkyTrain network. Check the current timetable before a weekend viewing. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt791.pdf
- **Q:** Does the City charge for a basic sign permit application? **A:** The application itself is filed with Bylaw and Licencing Services on the City's form. The form requires the contractor's current Maple Ridge trade licence number and two sets of scaled metric drawings for fascia, awning and projecting signs. Fees for a residential open-house sign specifically were not stated on the sources read — see NOT CONFIRMED. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2023-11-import/Sign%20Permit%20Application_202211071748268179.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Maple Ridge City Hall, which administers sign permits, is at 11995 Haney Place, Maple Ridge — the address printed on the City's sign permit and directional sign applications. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2023-11-import/Sign%20Permit%20Application_202211071748268179.pdf
- Sign permit drawings must be in metric: the City requires two sets of scaled drawings in metric for fascia, awning and projecting signs, including a site plan showing the sign against the parcel boundaries. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2023-11-import/Sign%20Permit%20Application_202211071748268179.pdf
- A directional sign permit holder must repair any municipal infrastructure affected by the sign work and return it to an equal or better condition within 24 hours, to the City's satisfaction. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/sign-permit-application-development-directionalpdf
- Permit enquiries go to the City's Bylaw and Licencing Services Department, where sign plans and specifications are deposited and approved before the permit issues. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/sign-permit-application-development-directionalpdf
- Maple Ridge property taxes are due the first business day in July (July 2 in 2026) — a possession date set just after an open-house purchase can land on the tax due date, which affects adjustments. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/property-taxes/understanding-property-taxes

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The one sign-related dollar figure the City's own form states is the $500 cash-or-cheque deposit guaranteeing a temporary construction (directional) sign permit. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/sign-permit-application-development-directionalpdf
- Maple Ridge is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax — relevant to a buyer who purchases at an open house and leaves the home empty. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- The City's 2026 property tax increase was set at 3.5%, and taxes are due the first business day in July. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/property-taxes/understanding-property-taxes

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Two West Coast Express stations sit inside Maple Ridge — Maple Meadows Station and Port Haney Station — on the Waterfront-to-Mission commuter line. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt791.pdf

- Lougheed Highway is the east-west road most directional signage points from — the City's first listed development directional sign location, D1, is at 201st Street and Lougheed Highway. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/sign-permit-application-development-directionalpdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- A sign placed without the required permit can be cancelled by the City: the directional sign permit states it is subject to cancellation if the holder fails to observe Sign Bylaw No. 7620 – 2020. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/sign-permit-application-development-directionalpdf
- An open house shows the house, not the file: zoning, permits and flood or Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status for a Maple Ridge lot are checked with the City and on title, not from anything on display at a viewing — see the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note for the diligence layers.
- Strata open houses carry strata rules: for a strata home, the Form B Information Certificate and the strata's bylaws, not the open-house feature sheet, state rental, pet and parking rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
