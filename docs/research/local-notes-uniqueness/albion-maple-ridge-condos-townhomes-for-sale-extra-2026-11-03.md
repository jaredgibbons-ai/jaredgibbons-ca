- Albion matches at every level: the City of Maple Ridge lists the neighbourhood as Albion/Kanaka, and its Official Community Plan carries an Albion Area Plan as Chapter 10.2. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The North East Albion plan area is bound by Kanaka Creek Regional Park to the north, existing Albion residential developments and conservation areas to the southwest, and rural residential developments to the east. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/albion-area-plan-2026pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Townhouse is a named plan designation in Albion, not just a housing form: the Albion Area Plan's Townhouse designation permits multi-unit housing forms in areas with access to major corridors, commercial areas and public amenities. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/albion-area-plan-2026pdf
- Cluster Residential is the second attached designation: it permits clustered ground-oriented multi-unit forms, including townhouse, courtyard and fourplex units, in environmentally sensitive areas where integrated site design is required. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/albion-area-plan-2026pdf
- In North East Albion, the plan states townhouses will consist primarily of two- to three-storey building forms, and encourages townhouse or strata developments to locate adjacent to Kanaka Creek Regional Park and sensitive streams for stewardship of the environmental buffer. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/albion-area-plan-2026pdf
- The built example is Kanaka Springs: developer Epic Homes describes it as a 70-acre masterplan community of townhomes and single-family homes, surrounded by over 20 acres of dedicated environmental space and linked to 1.2 kilometres of neighbourhood trail systems. https://kanakasprings.com/master-plan/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The plan steers strata townhouses to the park edge on purpose: North East Albion policy encourages townhouse or strata developments adjacent to Kanaka Creek Regional Park and sensitive streams, so creek-side complexes here carry riparian and buffer obligations a mid-block complex does not. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/albion-area-plan-2026pdf
- Flood rules bind the south edge: the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 metres geodetic including freeboard, no habitable floor space may sit below it on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- Farmland interface is a plan rule, not a neighbour dispute: the October 28, 2025 Albion Area Plan update introduces buffers where urban development meets farmland, and Agricultural Land Reserve parcels sit in South Albion. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId=

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** What did the October 2025 Albion Area Plan update change for townhome buyers?
**A:** Council adopted updates to the Albion Area Plan on October 28, 2025. The City states the updates respond to regional-partner feedback, enhance walking and cycling connections, support the growth of local commercial nodes, and introduce buffers where urban development meets farmland. For a townhome buyer, the practical effect is that new attached projects are planned around commercial nodes and trail links, with farmland buffers at the urban edge. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId=

**Q:** How tall are Albion townhouses allowed to be?
**A:** In North East Albion, primarily two to three storeys. The Albion Area Plan states North East Albion townhouses will consist primarily of two- to three-storey building forms. Outside the North East plan area, the Townhouse designation still permits multi-unit forms near corridors, commercial areas and amenities, but the two-to-three-storey statement is a North East Albion policy, so check which schedule of the plan a specific complex falls under. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/albion-area-plan-2026pdf

**Q:** Which elementary schools are in Albion?
**A:** Two sit inside the area: Albion Elementary at 10031 240 Street and c̓əsqənelə Elementary at 24093 104 Avenue, with Kanaka Creek Elementary at 11120 234A Street at the west edge. School District No. 42 catchments are address-based, secondary students go to schools including Samuel Robertson Technical Secondary at 10445 245 Street, and the district locator, not the nearest-school guess, settles a specific townhome address. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/

**Q:** Is Kanaka Springs a strata, and what comes with it?
**A:** Kanaka Springs is a master-planned community by Epic Homes mixing townhomes and single-family homes on 70 acres. The developer's master plan page states it includes a village hub with mixed-use commercial space and a neighbourhood park, a 1.2-acre future central community park, over 20 acres of dedicated environmental space, and a future elementary school planned around the corner. Individual phases carry their own strata plans, so the Form B for the specific phase governs fees and bylaws. https://kanakasprings.com/master-plan/

**Q:** Does Albion have a transit route of its own?
**A:** Yes, the 746 Haney Place / Albion. TransLink's Haney Place diagram shows the 746 interlining with the 745 Cottonwood service at Jackson Road at 103 Avenue, forming one circular service, and the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus runs on the Lougheed Highway corridor along Albion's south side to Haney Place. There is no West Coast Express station in Albion; Port Haney and Maple Meadows stations serve the city's west side. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/194/r748.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Albion Fairgrounds hosts events year-round, including Country Fest, the Caribbean Festival and the Home Show, so 240 Street and Jim Robson Way carry event traffic on fair weekends. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The Albion Sport Complex is home to Planet Ice (ice rink) and the Golden Ears Winter Club (curling club). https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- No library branch sits in Albion: Maple Ridge Public Library is at 130 – 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road in the Town Centre. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/maple-ridge-public-library
- Kanaka Creek Regional Park runs along the area's north and west sides, with the Canyon Trail to Cliff Falls and the Bell-Irving Hatchery supporting chum and coho salmon and cutthroat trout. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- Quote school addresses from the district or the BC Ministry list, not from listing copy: Albion Elementary is 10031 240 Street and Samuel Robertson Technical Secondary is 10445 245 Street. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Flood is a south-edge question: Albion fronts the Fraser River, the flood construction level is 6.7 metres geodetic, and which lots are affected is per-parcel on the City's flood maps. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- Riparian land is a north-edge question: complexes beside Kanaka Creek Regional Park and its tributary streams sit next to protected watercourse corridors, and the plan's Cluster Residential designation exists precisely for environmentally sensitive ground. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/albion-area-plan-2026pdf
- Agricultural Land Reserve status for South Albion parcels is per-parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping, and the 2025 plan update's farmland buffers apply where urban development meets that land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
