# Pitt Meadows 55+ Condos — Extra Local Notes
Page: pitt-meadows-55-plus-condos-for-sale (Pitt Meadows; 55+ condos).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Pitt Meadows condos cluster along Harris Road and Lougheed Highway in the urban south of the city, inside a municipality where designated Agricultural Land Reserve land covers most of the ground outside that urban core, with the Pitt River to the west and the Fraser River to the south. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/Tax-insert-2026.pdf
- The City's Official Community Plan is Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, amended by Bylaw No. 3034, 2025 to add Small-Scale, Multi-Unit Housing as-of-right and to designate Transit-Oriented Areas beside the Pitt Meadows and Maple Meadows West Coast Express stations. http://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026-06-23-3034%2C-2025-OCP-Amendment-Bylaw-Provincial-Housing-Legislation.pdf
- The North Lougheed Area, about 50 hectares (125 acres) on the north side of Lougheed Highway between Meadows Gardens Golf Course and Harris Road, is now covered by its own area plan, adopted September 29, 2026 by Official Community Plan Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/community-projects-and-initiatives/projects-and-studies/north-lougheed-area-plan

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there a true 55+ condo building in Pitt Meadows? **A:** No named one was verified in this pass. The verified 55+ product here is rancher townhouses in Central Meadows and rental or care residences such as The Wesbrooke at 12000 190A Street, so a buyer set on a condo apartment should confirm any claimed age bylaw in the specific strata's filed bylaws before relying on it. https://www.thewesbrooke.com
- **Q:** Why do taxes and utilities arrive together here? **A:** Pitt Meadows sends one combined bill. The City collects property taxes and utility fees in a single annual bill, and its 2026 insert states the average single-family home was assessed at $1,184,823 with a total property tax and utility increase of $319 for 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/Tax-insert-2026.pdf
- **Q:** Will my condo area change? **A:** Yes, in the designated spots. The North Lougheed Area Plan was adopted September 29, 2026 for about 50 hectares north of Lougheed Highway, and the City states future projects there still need further planning, technical studies and Council approvals, so change is planned but gradual, not immediate. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/community-projects-and-initiatives/projects-and-studies/north-lougheed-area-plan
- **Q:** How do I get downtown without driving? **A:** Use the West Coast Express station in Pitt Meadows. The City's Official Community Plan amendment designates Transit-Oriented Areas beside the Pitt Meadows and Maple Meadows stations, which is the City's own confirmation that the commuter rail station is the transit anchor for condo locations near Harris Road; exact train times are UNCONFIRMED here. http://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026-06-23-3034%2C-2025-OCP-Amendment-Bylaw-Provincial-Housing-Legislation.pdf
- **Q:** Is the Harris Road rail crossing a real issue? **A:** Yes, and the City says so. City business-plan material tracks Harris Road grade separation advocacy, a rail monitoring and alert system for blockages at the Harris Road crossing, and a Harris Road Complete Street study, so a buyer near Harris Road should expect rail blockages to shape daily driving. https://pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/Staff-Report-2026-Business-Plan-Engineering.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Pitt Meadows taxes were the region's lowest on the City's own comparison: the 2026 tax insert states that in 2025 Pitt Meadows had the lowest property taxes in the region at $5,002 for the average single-family home, and the second lowest combined with utility fees at $6,721. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/Tax-insert-2026.pdf
- Most of the city's land is farm-designated: the same insert charts designated Agricultural Land Reserve land at 78% for Pitt Meadows, the highest share shown, which is why condo supply stays inside a small urban footprint. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/Tax-insert-2026.pdf
- Utility fees in Pitt Meadows cover water, drainage, diking, sewer, solid waste and green waste as City services in the combined bill, so diking is a line item a condo owner pays even in an apartment building. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/Tax-insert-2026.pdf
- The City is actively redesigning its main street: the Harris Road Complete Street feasibility study was completed in 2025, covering a multi-use path, the Harris and Hammond intersection and protected bike lanes, to guide future capital work a Harris Road condo owner will live through. https://pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/Staff-Report-2026-Business-Plan-Engineering.pdf
- Seniors services sit at the civic core: Pitt Meadows Recreation Centre at 12027 Harris Road hosts seniors recreation beside The Wesbrooke residence, so the Harris Road condo cluster is the walkable seniors-services cluster in this city. https://www.thewesbrooke.com

- Heron's Nest, 19085-119B Avenue: 115 affordable rental homes plus a child care centre for 150 children, on City-owned land leased to Metro Vancouver Housing Corporation with provincial funding through British Columbia Housing. STATUS: project announced and in design on the British Columbia Housing page read 2026-11-03, with construction timing to be confirmed there (check current). https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/more-than-110-new-affordable-homes-child-care-centre-coming-to-pitt-meadows
- Katzie First Nation homes, 19676, 19679 and 19696 Salish Road: 40 affordable and supportive homes in three buildings, including 14 independent affordable homes for Elders and families, built with Katzie First Nation Affordable Housing Development Society, Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation and British Columbia Housing. ## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The exact Pitt Meadows residential tax rate per $1,000 was not stated in the municipal tax insert read in this pass and is UNCONFIRMED here; what the City states is the combined-bill structure and the 2025 comparison figures in section 5. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/Tax-insert-2026.pdf

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Commuter rail is the anchor: the West Coast Express serves Pitt Meadows station, and the City designates Transit-Oriented Areas beside it and Maple Meadows station, which is how the City itself frames condo transit access. http://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026-06-23-3034%2C-2025-OCP-Amendment-Bylaw-Provincial-Housing-Legislation.pdf
- Lougheed Highway and Harris Road are the road spine, and the proposed 2-kilometre North Lougheed Connector would link Abernathy Way to Lougheed Highway to calm traffic on Old Dewdney Trunk Road, per the adopted area plan. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/community-projects-and-initiatives/projects-and-studies/north-lougheed-area-plan

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Diking and drainage are a standing cost and a standing risk: the City's utility list includes diking and drainage as core services in a floodplain city between two rivers, so a ground-level condo buyer should ask the strata about flood insurance and drainage work, not just the building envelope. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/Tax-insert-2026.pdf
- Rail noise and blockage are mapped by the City's own work: Harris Road rail-crossing monitoring and the grade-separation project exist because Canadian Pacific Kansas City trains block and sound through the Harris Road corridor beside the condo cluster. https://pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/Staff-Report-2026-Business-Plan-Engineering.pdf
- Agricultural Land Reserve edges matter: with 78% of the city designated as reserve land on the City's chart, a condo on the urban edge can sit beside working farm land, with farm noise, spraying and restricted neighbouring development as normal conditions. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/Tax-insert-2026.pdf
