## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Meadowland Village, 18960 Advent Road, is a freehold strata, plan NWS2727, self-managed, made of ground-level rancher-style townhomes with no stairs and no unit above or below, beside Hoffman Park. https://www.meadowlandscoop.ca/
- Meadowlands Housing Co-operative, 19032 Advent Road, is a separate 55-plus co-operative with 16 four-unit single-floor row-home buildings, 64 homes in total (32 single-occupancy and 32 double-occupancy), a community activity centre and on-site maintenance; members hold shares, so there is no strata lot to buy. https://www.meadowlandscoop.ca/
- Meadow Highlands Mobile Home Co-operative on Hammond Road, with park addresses on Pinyon Lane, Planetree Lane and Poplar Drive, is a manufactured-home co-operative with a clubhouse and indoor pool and no age restriction in its own materials, so it is not 55-plus stock. https://www.meadowlandscoop.ca/
- No 55-plus townhouse strata was verified in South Meadows, Mid Meadows, North Meadows or West Meadows; Osprey Village and Bonson Landing townhomes on Fraser Way and Hammond Road carry no age restriction in any source checked. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Meadowland Village's 55-plus bylaw is lawful because British Columbia permits only an age bylaw set at 55 or older, under sections 123.1 and 123.2 of the Strata Property Act. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- The co-operative next door runs on co-operative membership rules instead: the Co-operative Housing Federation of BC states all Meadowlands units are designated for adults 55 years and up only, and one indoor cat is permitted. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- Meadowland Village is self-managed, so there is no management company portal; the strata's own records, bylaws and Form B Information Certificate are obtained through the strata council on a purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since November 24, 2022, so a Meadowland Village owner may rent long-term, while a short-term rental bylaw could still apply and must be checked in the current bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What 55-plus townhome can I actually buy in Pitt Meadows? **A:** One complex: Meadowland Village at 18960 Advent Road, a self-managed freehold strata, plan NWS2727, of ground-level rancher townhomes beside Hoffman Park. Nothing else in the city is a verified age-restricted strata townhouse. The co-operative at 19032 Advent Road is also 55-plus and ground-oriented, but it is membership housing applied for through the co-operative, not a unit listed for sale, so the two should never be priced or compared as the same product. https://www.meadowlandscoop.ca/
- **Q:** Is Meadowland Village the same as Meadowlands? **A:** No, and the near-identical names on the same street are a genuine trap. Meadowland Village, 18960 Advent Road, is the strata: you buy a strata lot, pay strata fees and get a Form B. Meadowlands Housing Co-operative, 19032 Advent Road, is a 64-home co-operative where members hold shares and pay monthly housing charges. Checking the civic address, 18960 versus 19032, is the fastest way to tell which property a listing or a neighbour is describing. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q:** How many homes are in Meadowland Village, and when was it built? **A:** Both figures are in conflict in the sources, so treat them as unconfirmed: listing text says 26 homes while a building source header says a 20-unit development, and the build year appears as both 1988 and 1989. The strata plan NWS2727 and the Form B are the records that settle the count, and the strata plan's filing date settles the year. Do not rely on either number in an offer until those documents are in hand. https://www.meadowlandscoop.ca/
- **Q:** My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Meadowland Village? **A:** Yes, because the provincial exemptions to a 55-plus bylaw include a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement, plus live-in caregivers and residents who lived in the unit when the bylaw passed. Children under 19 in that resident's care, and adult children the resident cared for before they turned 19, were added to the exemptions on May 1, 2023. The exemption follows the qualifying resident, so it should be confirmed against the strata's registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Pitt Meadows Seniors Activity Centre is at 19065 119B Avenue, run by the Ridge Meadows Seniors Society for adults 55-plus, with membership stated at $35 a year and free at age 90-plus in the bc211 listing; it is a place to go, not housing. https://bc.211.ca/agency-details/ridge-meadows-seniors-society-46654954/
- The Wesbrooke at 12000 190A Street is the city's only full-service seniors residence, offering independent and assisted living with 24-hour staffing; it is rental and care housing, and its total suite count is UNCONFIRMED on its current site. https://www.thewesbrooke.com
- Heron's Nest at 19085 119B Avenue is a Metro Vancouver Housing project of 115 homes for families and individuals with childcare, so a new building near the seniors centre is not seniors housing. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain
- Highland Park Elementary at 18961 Advent Road is directly on the Meadowland Village street, which is why school traffic and crossing activity are part of daily life on Advent Road despite the 55-plus designation. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- There is no hospital in Pitt Meadows; Ridge Meadows Hospital at 11666 Laity Street in Maple Ridge is the Fraser Health hospital for both cities, with 24-hour emergency service. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- Ford Road Housing Co-operative at 19041 Ford Road is listed in BC Housing's seniors directory but its own application package shows an adult building, a family building and three- and four-bedroom townhouses, so it is a mixed co-operative, not seniors-only. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Pitt Meadows' 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 sets the Class 1 Residential general municipal rate at 2.8438 per $1,000, plus a drainage levy of 0.1082 per $1,000 and a Metro Vancouver Regional District levy of 0.0583 per $1,000; taxes under that bylaw were due July 2, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- The City collects property taxes and utility fees in one combined annual bill, and its utility fees cover water, drainage, diking, sewer, and solid and green waste, which is why a Pitt Meadows bill includes a diking charge a Maple Ridge bill does not itemize the same way. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/Tax-insert-2026.pdf
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in the City of Pitt Meadows, listed by the Province as a designated taxable area in the Metro Vancouver Regional District. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Flood and dike detail for the whole city is written in full in the south-meadows-condos-townhomes-for-sale note — see that note; the lot-level check for an Advent Road townhouse is the City's floodplain and drainage material, because the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95 percent of Pitt Meadows is in the floodplain behind about 60 kilometres of dikes. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/city-receives-412000-flood-mitigation-plan
- The Province's Lower Mainland dike inventory maps show dike infrastructure, pump stations, flood boxes and areas of hazard concern for the Pitt and Fraser river frontages, and state they support emergency planning rather than replacing a designated floodplain map. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/drought-flooding-dikes-dams/integrated-flood-hazard-management/governance/dike-management/dike-inventory
