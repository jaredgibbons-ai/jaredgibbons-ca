# Sapperton Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: sapperton-new-westminster-houses-for-sale (Sapperton; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sapperton is the slope neighbourhood above the Fraser River between McBride Boulevard and Brunette Creek, taking in Royal Columbian Hospital, the Sapperton and Braid SkyTrain stations, Hume Park and the Brewery District. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- The name comes from the Royal Engineers' Columbia Detachment camp: "Sappers' Town," from 1858, is the origin of the Sapperton name. https://www.newwestcity.ca/planning-building-and-development/zoning
- East Columbia Street is the spine, carrying the 109 bus between New Westminster Station and Lougheed Station. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/skytrain

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is detached houses on the slope streets under the RS districts of Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001, with the RT-A and RT-B townhouse districts applying where the Official Community Plan designates them. https://www.newwestcity.ca/planning-building-and-development/zoning
- The station areas at Sapperton and Braid are a Frequent Transit Development Area, so the slope's house streets sit beside a designated growth area rather than inside a static enclave. https://www.newwestcity.ca/planning-building-and-development/zoning
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The City's Infill Housing Program, adopted June 2026, permits up to six infill units on most single detached and duplex properties city-wide; the full entry is in the west-end-new-westminster note. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- Flood rules bite at the bottom of the slope, not on it: the Brunette River industrial land is Floodplain Zone 4, below the Flood Construction Level, while the residential slope above is not the risk pocket. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- New Westminster is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve Sapperton? **A:** The School District No. 40 (SD40) locator result recorded for 331 Richmond Street is Skwo:wech Elementary for Kindergarten to Grade 5, École Glenbrook Middle for Grades 6 to 8 and New Westminster Secondary for Grades 9 to 12; Skwo:wech Elementary itself stands at 331 Richmond Street. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- **Q:** What is the new elementary school's story? **A:** Skwo:wech Elementary opened June 6, 2022 at a cost of 34.98 million dollars for more than 400 Kindergarten to Grade 5 students, replacing Richard McBride Elementary, and its name is the halq'eméylem word for sturgeon. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- **Q:** What changed at Royal Columbian Hospital? **A:** Royal Columbian Hospital, at 330 East Columbia Street, is Fraser Health's regional referral hospital for cardiology, neurosurgery, high-risk obstetrics and neonatal intensive care, and its Jim Pattison Acute Care Tower opened to patients May 31, 2026 — 10 storeys, 388 beds, with a new emergency department and entrance. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/New-Westminster/royal-columbian-hospital

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Hume Park and Lower Hume Park, by the Brunette River, hold the pool, lacrosse box and off-leash area, and the Brunette-Fraser Greenway and Sapperton Landing Park tie the slope to the Fraser waterfront. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- Sapperton Park dates to 1907, making it one of the city's oldest park sites. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage
- The Brewery District is the former Labatt brewery site on East Columbia Street, redeveloped by Wesgroup as 1.4 million square feet across 8 buildings, 7 of them complete. https://wesgroup.ca/brewery-district/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check flood position by elevation: the Brunette River industrial land at the slope's foot is Floodplain Zone 4 below the Flood Construction Level, so a low-lying lot needs a flood check even though the residential slope is not the risk pocket. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- Expect hospital-area construction and traffic through the later redevelopment phases at Royal Columbian Hospital. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/New-Westminster/royal-columbian-hospital
