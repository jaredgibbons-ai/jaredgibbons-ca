# Port Moody Centre Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: port-moody-centre-houses-for-sale (Port Moody Centre; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Port Moody Centre is the south-shore centre of Port Moody, around two SkyTrain stations: Moody Centre Station on the St. Johns Street corridor and Inlet Centre Station at Barnet Highway and Ioco Road. http://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/skytrain_station_maps/inlet_centre_station.pdf
- The City's Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw No. 3522 covers the centre in Chapter 14 as section 14.6, Moody Centre — including the Moody Centre Station Transit-Oriented Area, the South of St. Johns Special Study Area, Westport Village, the Heritage Commercial District and the Oceanfront District — with Inlet Centre and Coronation Park in section 14.5; the board name "Port Moody Centre" is not itself an Official Community Plan name. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- The Moody Centre Station transit-oriented development area currently extends from the Canadian Pacific Railway line to St. Johns Street and from Electronic Avenue to Moody Street, with the station as its focal point. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050?tool=qanda

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The centre's house stock is the detached streets south of St. Johns Street, under the RS zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 2937, while the RT and RM zones carry the townhouse and apartment stock and the TC and CRM zones the mixed-use stock around the stations. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Moody Centre is a heritage conservation area, so the older house stock here carries heritage rules the newer suburbs do not. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Rocky Point Park, with its pier, boat launch, pool and the start of the Shoreline Trail, is the centre's waterfront anchor. https://engage.portmoody.ca/rocky-point-and-old-orchard-parks-master-plans/news_feed/background-rocky-point-today

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Port Moody 2050, the City's Official Community Plan, was adopted by Council on April 28, 2026, with Moody Centre in section 14.6 and Inlet Centre and Coronation Park in section 14.5 of Chapter 14. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/business-and-development/official-community-plan.aspx
- The RS1-S small-lot rezoning route is available in Moody Centre's single-family low-density areas, with minimums of 9 metres width and 325 square metres — the small-lot option the west-end neighbourhoods do not have. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Transit-oriented areas are exempt from the small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules, and SSMUH applies in the RS and RT zones outside them, so a centre lot's position relative to the station areas decides which regime governs it. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- Because Moody Centre is a heritage conservation area, a heritage alteration permit may be required for work on a house here. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Port Moody is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve Port Moody Centre? **A:** The School District No. 43 (SD43) locator result recorded for 100 Newport Drive is Mountain Meadows Elementary, Scott Creek Middle and Gleneagle Secondary, and Moody Elementary and Moody Middle with Port Moody Secondary, at 300 Albert Street, also serve central Port Moody; confirm the chain for a specific address with the district. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** Can a centre house lot be split into small lots? **A:** The RS1-S small-lot rezoning is available in Moody Centre's single-family low-density areas, at minimums of 9 metres width and 325 square metres, which is the small-lot route the City offers here and in Seaview and Pleasantside but not in College Park or Glenayre. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- **Q:** What civic facilities are in the centre? **A:** The Recreation Complex is at 300 Ioco Road, Kyle Centre at 125 Kyle Street and the Port Moody Public Library at 100 Newport Drive, and Rocky Point Park holds the pier, boat launch, pool and Shoreline Trail start on the waterfront. https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/
- **Q:** Does the heritage conservation area affect a house purchase? **A:** Yes: Moody Centre is a heritage conservation area, so a heritage alteration permit may be required for alterations, and buyers of older houses here should check the permit position before planning exterior work. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Moody Centre Station is a three-mode station: SkyTrain on the Millennium Line, the West Coast Express commuter rail, and a bus loop share the one St. Johns Street corridor site. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050?tool=qanda
- The combined Inlet Centre and Coronation Park area extends from Noons Creek Drive to Murray Street, and from St. Johns Street and Barnet Highway to the Coquitlam municipal border, so the centre's east side runs to another city's line. https://engage.portmoody.ca/portmoody-2050?page=2&tool=story_telling_tool
- The centre's plan geography has five named parts in section 14.6 alone — the station transit-oriented area, the South of St. Johns Special Study Area, Westport Village, the Heritage Commercial District and the Oceanfront District — so a centre address's rules depend on which part it falls in. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Port Moody property taxes are set city-wide, and the speculation and vacancy tax applies in Port Moody, so owners declare annually. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Moody Centre Station (SkyTrain, West Coast Express and bus loop) and Inlet Centre Station are the centre's two stations, on the Millennium Line, with the West Coast Express running between downtown Vancouver and Mission on weekdays in the morning and evening rush. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- St. Johns Street and Barnet Highway (Highway 7A) are the centre's east–west corridors, and the Shoreline Trail starts at Rocky Point Park on the waterfront. https://engage.portmoody.ca/rocky-point-and-old-orchard-parks-master-plans/news_feed/background-rocky-point-today

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check heritage status before planning work: Moody Centre is a heritage conservation area and a heritage alteration permit may be required. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf
- Confirm whether the lot sits inside a transit-oriented area, because those areas are exempt from the SSMUH rules that apply elsewhere in the centre. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- Confirm the SD43 catchment for the exact address, since two school chains serve central Port Moody. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
