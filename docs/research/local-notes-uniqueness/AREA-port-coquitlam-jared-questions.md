# Port Coquitlam — Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer, for a voice memo. Muse does not answer these.

1. Who is choosing Port Coquitlam over Coquitlam or Port Moody right now — and what is the deciding factor, price, the downtown core, or the small-city feel?
2. What sells fast in PoCo and what sits — Mary Hill character homes, Citadel new builds, or the north-side family pockets?
3. What surprises buyers most: the West Coast Express commute pattern, flood and creek rules along the Pitt River side, or how much downtown PoCo is changing?
4. When you price a PoCo listing, which neighbouring city's sales do sellers try to use — and which ones actually hold up?
5. Tell one story from a PoCo deal — a first-time buyer who landed here after losing in Coquitlam, or a long-time local selling the family home.
