# Abbotsford Homes for Sale — Extra Local Notes
Page: abbotsford-homes-for-sale (City of Abbotsford; Homes for Sale).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Abbotsford is a single city in the Fraser Valley Regional District, crossed east–west by Highway 1 and Fraser Highway, with the Huntingdon / Sumas border crossing at its south end on the Highway 11 corridor. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- The City publishes its communities as demographic profile areas, including Bradner – Mt. Lehman (area 1), Aberdeen (area 6) and West Abbotsford (area 7); board sub-area names and City profile names overlap but are not the same list. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2020%20McKee%20Profile%20-%20spreads%20final.pdf
- Abbotsford International Airport (YXX) sits in the southwest of the city, south of Fraser Highway and west of Clearbrook. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- Mission lies across the Fraser River to the north, the Township of Langley to the west, Chilliwack to the east, and the United States border to the south.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The city-wide stock is governed by Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw, 2014, No. 2400-2014, whose zone families run from the Section 200 agricultural zones (A1, A2) through urban residential zones such as RS3 and the infill RS7 zone to apartment and mixed-use zones. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- Infill subdivision is an active form: Council reports in 2025 repeatedly rezoned single lots from Urban Residential Zone, Infill (RS3-i) to Infill Residential Zone (RS7) for two-, three- and four-lot subdivisions, for example at 2272 Lynden Street and 2330 Fir Street. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58344/
- The rural half of the city is farm lots at agricultural scale: minimum subdivision lot area 8.0 hectares in the A1 zone and 16.0 hectares in the A2 zone. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The governing plan is Abbotsforward 2050, the City's updated Official Community Plan (OCP): the City's laws site now carries the OCP as Bylaw No. 3690-2025, replacing the Bylaw No. 2600-2016 cited in earlier research — check current. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) is built into the OCP update: the draft framework received by Council on May 13, 2025 proposed an SSMUH 1 designation enabling small-scale multi-unit housing near the urban core and the Primary Transit Corridor, limited to three storeys and a maximum of four dwelling units per lot. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58283/
- In the A1 agricultural zone, one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot is permitted under the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or with Agricultural Land Commission approval, on a lot of at least 0.4 hectares, capped at 90 square metres (lots of 40 hectares or less) or 186 square metres (larger lots), plus one secondary suite per lot to 100 square metres. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- Development works and services are secured under the City's Development Bylaw, 2022, which Council reports cite for road and utility upgrades, road widening dedication and corner truncations on rezoning files. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58283/
Read: 2026-11-03

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which plan governs growth in Abbotsford? **A:** Abbotsforward 2050, the updated Official Community Plan, governs. The City's laws site carries it as OCP Bylaw No. 3690-2025, with neighbourhood plans for City Centre, Historic Downtown, UDistrict and McKee named inside it. A Stage 3 draft of the update was received by Council on May 13, 2025, alongside matching zoning bylaw changes, so confirm the current bylaw version for any specific lot. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **Q:** Can a city lot be split or built out to four units? **A:** Often yes, through infill zoning or the new small-scale multi-unit housing framework. Council approved repeated RS3-i to RS7 rezonings in 2025 for two- to four-lot subdivisions, and the OCP update's SSMUH 1 designation contemplates up to four dwelling units per lot near the urban core and Primary Transit Corridor, at up to three storeys. Each route carries Development Bylaw, 2022 works and services conditions. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58344/
- **Q:** How are schools assigned? **A:** By street address, under School District No. 34 (SD34) Policy 18. Catchment areas are set by the district and checked address by address in the SD34 School Locator; a neighbourhood name does not guarantee a school. As one verified example, the locator run for 2975 Bradner Road returned Aberdeen Elementary, Eugene Reimer Middle and Rick Hansen Secondary. Run the exact address of any home before relying on a catchment. https://www.abbyschools.ca/policy-18-school-catchment-areas
- **Q:** Does the airport restrict what I can build? **A:** Only on land inside the airport zoning area in the southwest of the city. Land covered by the Abbotsford Airport Zoning Regulations, SOR/83-253, shown on City drawing M-500, is subject to height limits under the approach, outer and transitional surfaces, and NAV CANADA and Transport Canada approvals are required before the City issues a building permit on that land. https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/sor-83-253/FullText.html?wbdisable=true

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Water and sewer are billed bi-monthly, not monthly, and a 5 percent discount applies if payment is received by the due date printed on the bill. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/current-utility-bill
- From January 2027, solid waste fees move off the annual property tax notice and onto the utility bill alongside water and sewer; collection days do not change. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing
- Properties not connected to City water or sewer — well and septic properties — are not charged those utility fees. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing
- Any utility account balance outstanding at December 31 is transferred to the property tax account and subject to daily interest, per the City's current utility bill page (stated for the 2025 balance). https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/current-utility-bill
- Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre, 32900 Marshall Road, is the city's hospital, with 24-hour emergency, surgery and maternity services, and the urgent and primary care centre is at 100-2692 Clearbrook Road. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre
- Property tax notices are billed annually for January to December, with an estimated delivery date of May 22, 2026 for the 2026 notice. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- The City's 2026 Tax Rates Summary sets the Class 1 Residential general municipal rate at 1.08284 dollars per $1,000 of taxable value; the full 2026 rate is set by Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026 and adds school, Fraser Valley Regional District, hospital and library levies. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Summary%20May%2022.pdf
- All 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5 percent penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5 percent penalty on September 16, 2026, including on unclaimed Home Owner Grant amounts. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- Effective March 1, 2026, the residential water rate is $1.46 per cubic metre and the sewer rate is $1.35 per cubic metre, after a Council-approved 4 percent increase to each in January 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/water-sewer-rates
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in Abbotsford: the Province's taxable-areas list names the City of Abbotsford, so owners of residential property must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- BC Transit's Central Fraser Valley system, not TransLink, runs Abbotsford buses; route 21 Fraser Hwy links Aldergrove and Bourquin Exchange via Highstreet Exchange. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=21&schedule=2026-04-05
- Route 66 Fraser Valley Express runs from Downtown Chilliwack through Abbotsford to Lougheed Town Centre Station — see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note for its Chilliwack stops. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- Highway 1 interchanges serving the city include Mt. Lehman Road and Clearbrook Road; the Huntingdon / Sumas border crossing is at the south end of the Highway 11 corridor. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- Abbotsford International Airport (YXX) is inside the city, in the southwest, under its own federal Airport Zoning Regulations, SOR/83-253. https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/sor-83-253/FullText.html?wbdisable=true
- There is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in Abbotsford; the nearest West Coast Express station is Mission City Station. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the specific lot in the City's WebMap for its zone and any Zoning Bylaw Schedule E floodplain designation before offering; both are lot-specific. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status is parcel-specific across the rural half of the city; subdivision, non-farm use and added dwellings can need Agricultural Land Commission approval on top of City zoning. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Airport height surfaces are a separate layer from zoning and floodplain: land on City drawing M-500 can be height-capped even where zoning allows a taller building. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- For rural homes off City sewer, Fraser Health states an onsite sewerage system must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation, and well records sit in the Province's GWELLS registry. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
