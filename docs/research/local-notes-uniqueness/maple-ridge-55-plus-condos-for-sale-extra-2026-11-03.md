## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A Fraserview Village age bylaw is lawful only because it is set at 55-plus, the sole age threshold the Strata Property Act now permits, in sections 123.1 and 123.2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- A building summary that still says rentals are not allowed at Rosewood Court is out of date for long-term rentals, because British Columbia voided strata rental-restriction bylaws on November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Unit counts and strata plan numbers for Rosewood Court, Emerald Manor, The Willows and Castlemount are UNCONFIRMED from public sources, so the Form B and the strata plan are the documents that settle them on a purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which Maple Ridge condos can I buy that are truly 55-plus? **A:** The verified purchase stock is the four Fraserview Village strata apartment buildings: Rosewood Court at 22611 116 Avenue, Emerald Manor at 11609 227 Street, The Willows at 11578 225 Street and Castlemount at 11601 227 Street. No other Maple Ridge condo building has a verified 55-plus bylaw in this program, and a listing that calls a downtown unit suitable for a retiree is not an age restriction. Confirm the bylaw on the Form B before removing subjects. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/250926/MRNN250926_Z37.pdf
- **Q:** What do I get for the strata fee at Fraserview Village? **A:** The shared amenity centre at 22610 116 Avenue is the centre of the purchase, described as an indoor pool, hot tub, exercise and recreation centre, clubhouse, workshop and a guest suite residents can rent. A listing for Emerald Manor also stated gas and hot water included in the strata fee and RV parking possible, but those are listing statements, so a buyer should confirm the current fee inclusions in the strata budget and Form B for the specific building. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/250926/MRNN250926_Z37.pdf
- **Q:** Is there a 55-plus condo called Hillcrest in Maple Ridge? **A:** No complex by that name was confirmed on a developer, strata or manager source in this program, even though a listing feed card used the name for an East Central 55-plus condo. Treat Hillcrest as an unverified feed name, not a building a buyer can search for, and use the four Fraserview Village addresses instead. If a seller uses the name, ask for the strata plan number and civic address and check them against the Land Title record. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/250926/MRNN250926_Z37.pdf
- **Q:** How close is care if my health changes after I buy at Fraserview? **A:** Two options sit on 224 Street, a short distance west of 116 Avenue: Chartwell Willow at 12275 224 Street offers independent living, assisted living and private long-term care in one building, and Maple Ridge Seniors Village at 22141 119 Avenue offers independent living, assisted living and complex care. Both are rental and care housing, so moving there is a new tenancy, not a transfer of the condo, and Ridge Meadows Hospital is at 11666 Laity Street. https://chartwell.com/bc/maple-ridge/willow

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Maple Ridge tax and utility detail is in the maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note — see that note; a Fraserview condo is taxed at the Class 1 Residential general rate of 2.8643 per $1,000 for 2026. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- For multi-residential properties the City's 2026 schedule lists a flat water fee of $815.62, which is the utility line a condo strata's budget reflects rather than a bill the owner pays directly. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in Maple Ridge as a designated taxable area, so a 55-plus owner who moves into care and leaves the condo empty must still file the declaration and check the care-related exemptions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Fraserview Village sits above River Road in the Port Haney area, so a buyer should check the lot against Schedule A of Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 8121-2026, which sets flood construction levels and setbacks city-wide. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
- Buildings from 1991 to 1997 are at the age where the depreciation report and contingency reserve fund matter most, so read both in the Form B package rather than relying on the current strata fee alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Pet rules differ by building in the earlier program's listings, for example a one-pet limit stated at Emerald Manor, and pet bylaws remain enforceable even though rental bylaws were voided, so confirm the current bylaw set. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
