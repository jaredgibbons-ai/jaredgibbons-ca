# Yarrow — Questions for Jared

## Questions

1. Who buys in Yarrow in your deals — families priced out of Sardis, acreage buyers, or people who specifically want the village? What are they leaving behind?
2. What sells fast and what sits — village lots on Yarrow Central Road, or the ALR acreage ring around it? Where do buyers draw the line on septic and well?
3. What surprises buyers after they move in — the high water table and floodproofing rules, the hydrogeological assessment needed for a suite, or how limited the bus and services are?
4. When a buyer compares Yarrow with Greendale and with Sardis, what do you tell them about the trade-off?
5. One story: a Yarrow deal that taught you something — a property, a buyer reaction, or a rule you now check first — and what you do differently now.

## Shot list

1. Yarrow Central Road at Kehler Street, looking along the village main street — the commercial frontage, the bus stop and the village scale in one frame.
2. The Vedder River and Great Blue Heron Nature Reserve from the Rotary Trail at the Wilson Road trailhead, looking north across the wetlands to the river — the floodplain setting the village sits in.
3. Majuba Hill and Vedder Mountain backdrop from Yarrow Pioneer Park, looking east and south over the village rooftops — the two landmarks that frame the village.
