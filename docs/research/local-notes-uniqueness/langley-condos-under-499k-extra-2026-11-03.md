# Langley Condos by Price Band — Extra Local Notes
Page: langley-condos-under-499k (Langley; condos by price band).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- This page is a price-band filter over both Langleys, not one municipality: the band topic is condos under $499K across the City of Langley and the Township of Langley, which have separate councils, bylaws and tax notices.
- The older resale cluster is in the City core along Fraser Highway and the 205/206 Street blocks, while the Township stock in this band concentrates in Willoughby along 200 Street and 207 Street and at Aldergrove Town Centre on Fraser Highway at 272 Street.
- Langley is School District 35 (SD35), and catchments are address-based through the district locator — a City address and a Township address a few blocks apart can feed different schools.
- No count of listings in this band is stated in this note, because no official source publishes a band count.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Form B is issued under Strata Property Act section 59 and must be provided within seven days of request; it carries fees, amounts owing, levies and the contingency reserve fund balance, with rules, budget and the most recent depreciation report attached. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- Every strata with five or more lots must hold a depreciation report on a five-year cycle with no deferral vote, projecting common-property costs over 30 years. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Rental-restriction bylaws are unenforceable for long-term rentals anywhere in BC since November 24, 2022, so an older "rentals not allowed" line in a Langley City building's documents does not bind a buyer; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- The only age bylaw a strata may still enforce is 55 or older, under Strata Property Act sections 123.1 and 123.2, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and defined family cases — the live question in the 1988–1990 City buildings marketed as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Two zoning bylaws split this band: City of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300 for City buildings and Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 for Township buildings, with different suite and small-scale multi-unit housing rules on each side.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Does under $499K clear the first-time buyer property transfer tax (PTT) test on price alone? **A:** Yes — price is not the barrier in this band. The Province states qualifying first-time buyers pay no tax on the first $500,000 and can save up to $8,000 on an existing home worth up to $835,000, so every home under this page's ceiling sits inside the value range; the buyer must still meet the citizenship, BC residency and never-owned-a-principal-residence conditions. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/more-homes-for-people
- **Q:** The documents say rentals are not allowed — can I rent the unit out? **A:** For a long-term tenancy, yes. Provincial changes made strata rental-restriction bylaws unenforceable from November 24, 2022, and the Province's strata pages record that change directly; what can still bind is a short-term rental restriction and, at a 55+ building, the age bylaw, which limits who may live in the unit even when renting is allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q:** How do I check pet and parking rules before I offer? **A:** Start with the Form B, then the filed bylaws. The certificate states how parking and storage are allocated to the specific unit and attaches the strata's rules and budget, while the pet rule lives in the registered bylaws; read both before subject removal, because a listing summary is not the bylaw and only the filed text governs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q:** Why does the same price buy a 1989 City unit or a new Township unit? **A:** Because this band spans two different stocks in two municipalities. The City side is the three-building 1988–1990 low-rise roster on 205/206 Street, while the Township side is Willoughby and Aldergrove Town Centre product built decades later; the comparison that matters is the strata documents — depreciation report age, reserve fund and fee inclusions — not the list price alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q:** What transit serves this band today? **A:** Buses today, SkyTrain in delivery. Carvolth Exchange on 202 Street by Highway 1 is the Township's park-and-ride hub with the 555 Port Mann Express toward Lougheed Town Centre, and TransLink states the Province is delivering a 16-kilometre Expo Line extension along Fraser Highway to 203 Street in Langley City, with the Langley City Centre Station east of 203 Street and Industrial Avenue.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Shared band-process facts are in full in this note because this page sits earlier in program order than langley-condos-under-399k; that later page should point here for the Form B, depreciation-report and PTT process.
- A 55+ bylaw does not stop a younger buyer from owning in every case: the provincial age-bylaw page sets out exemptions, and whether the buyer may occupy, or only own and rent to an eligible occupant, turns on the registered bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Fee inclusions differ inside this band: some older City buildings include heat and hot water in the strata fee per their documents, which changes the real monthly comparison against a newer Township unit — confirm inclusions on the Form B, not the listing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- Stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund each year since November 1, 2023 — the first budget line to check in a 1988–1990 building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Huntsfield Green at 19649 53 Avenue in Langley City is a townhouse complex, not an apartment condo, and never joins the City's three-building apartment roster.

- The tax split in this band is municipal: a City of Langley unit and a Township of Langley unit at the same price carry different city tax notices, rates and utility charges.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- In a 1988–1990 building, read the depreciation report's 30-year projection before the price: roofs, piping and parkade work concentrate in that age group, and the report must be attached to the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Confirm tenure on title: corporation-share, leasehold, co-op and pad-rent product can surface at this price point in Langley feeds and is not freehold strata condo stock; the Form B and title search separate them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- Check which municipality the unit is actually in before closing adjustments: City and Township tax and utility figures differ, and the listing's "Langley" label does not say which one applies.
