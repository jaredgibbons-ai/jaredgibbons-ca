# County Line / Glen Valley Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: county-line-glen-valley (County Line / Glen Valley; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- County Line / Glen Valley is the Fraser River floodplain strip in northeast Langley Township at the Abbotsford line. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/drought-flooding-dikes-dams/integrated-flood-hazard-management/governance/dike-management/dike-inventory
- 272 Street is the County Line boundary with Abbotsford, and River Road runs along the Fraser through Glen Valley. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/glen-valley-regional-park
- The provincial dike inventory lists a separate Glen Valley Dyking District map sheet (sheet 19). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/drought-flooding-dikes-dams/integrated-flood-hazard-management/governance/dike-management/dike-inventory
- Fort Langley lies west along River Road, and Trinity Western University's Langley campus at 22500 University Drive sits south of the valley floor. https://www.twu.ca/about-us/locations

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Housing is detached homes and farmhouses on large rural and floodplain lots, not strata complexes. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- Historic Hassall House, built in 1917 inside Glen Valley Regional Park, is described by Metro Vancouver as a typical 1920s rural farm residence and the site of Glen Valley's first store, post office and wharf. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/Documents/glenn-valley-regional-park-brochure.pdf
- Township zoning for floodplain parcels includes the Rural Floodplain Zone RU-4 named in Township council reports. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=0B34D4F4-4515-447D-9D5B-C7F255C8E3BA&folderId=96641C240325124044497307

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- ALR rules govern most valley-floor parcels, including house size and extra residences. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- The principal residence cap in the ALR is 500 m² total floor area, with larger homes needing a Non-Adhering Residential Use application. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- Township Official Community Plan Schedule 3 Streamside Protection and Enhancement areas require habitat protection along creeks, as applied in Township reports for RU-4 land near the Salmon River. https://e-connect.tol.ca/OurCity/Prospero/FileDownload.aspx?fileId=0B34D4F4-4515-447D-9D5B-C7F255C8E3BA&folderId=96641C240325124044497307

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What is being done about Glen Valley flooding? **A:** A multi-phase Township project with Kwantlen First Nation and the Rivershed Society of BC is upgrading flood infrastructure. Reporting of the provincial announcement states the work repairs dike banks, replaces culverts, improves ditch networks and adds pumping capacity at the Salmon River Pump Station with fish-friendly pumps, funded with $5 million from the Province's Fraser Valley Flood Mitigation Program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/drought-flooding-dikes-dams/integrated-flood-hazard-management/governance/dike-management/dike-inventory
- **Q:** Can I fish or launch a boat in the park? **A:** Yes, within Metro Vancouver park rules at Glen Valley Regional Park. Metro Vancouver lists fishing, canoeing/kayaking and a canoe/kayak launch among the park's features, and its brochure states fishing needs a Tidal Waters Sport Fishing Licence and car-top boats can launch at the Poplar Bar parking lot area. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/glen-valley-regional-park
- **Q:** Which school district serves the valley? **A:** School District 35 (Langley) serves both Langley Township and Langley City. SD35 publishes catchment maps and a School Locator Tool, and buyers should run the exact address through the locator because rural east Langley catchments differ by street and feed different elementary and secondary schools. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/catchment-maps
- **Q:** Can I build a second residence on an ALR lot? **A:** Only under the ALC's additional-residence rules. The Commission's policy allows generally one residence per parcel, an additional residence only where the ALR Use Regulation permits it, and otherwise a Non-Adhering Residential Use application that the Commission must not grant unless the extra residence is necessary for a farm use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Glen Valley Regional Park is split by the municipal line: Poplar Bar, Duncan Bar and Crescent Island are part of the park within the City of Abbotsford, managed by the Fraser Valley Regional District and operated by Abbotsford. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/Documents/glenn-valley-regional-park-brochure.pdf
- Metro Vancouver warns the riverbank in the park is eroding and undercut, water levels are tidal and can change rapidly, and dogs must be leashed. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/Documents/glenn-valley-regional-park-brochure.pdf
- Hassall House is closed to the public and rented as a private residence. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/Documents/glenn-valley-regional-park-brochure.pdf
- Nathan Creek in Glen Valley has been a focus of habitat work along the dike near the end of 80 Avenue, per Township-related reporting read.

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Township flat-rate utilities are billed separately from property taxes. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- For 2026, the Township flat rate is $779.96 for water and $716.47 for sanitary sewer for a single-family home, with garbage base rate $226.00 plus cart charges. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- Metered water and sewer are billed three times a year, with meters read in April, August and December, and sewer volume calculated at 80% of water supplied. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/utility-rates.aspx
- The Township of Langley is a designated Speculation and Vacancy Tax taxable area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- For Township of Langley headline tax rates, see the langley-farms note.

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- River Road and 272 Street are the through routes; there is no rapid transit on the valley floor. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/glen-valley-regional-park
- The nearest regional transit hub is Carvolth Exchange, where TransLink added weekday afternoon service on the 555 Port Mann Express toward Carvolth in its Fall 2026 changes. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/service-changes/2026/2026_fall_service_change.pdf
- Highway 1 access is via the 264 Street and 232 Street interchanges west and east of the valley; drive times UNCONFIRMED.
