# Central Meadows Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: central-meadows-pitt-meadows (Central Meadows, Pitt Meadows; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Central Meadows has no official City boundary; the closest official planning name is the Official Community Plan (OCP) Municipal Town Centre. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- The Town Centre covers the Harris Road corridor and the North Lougheed Area and the Civic Centre, with Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) at the north edge and the Canadian Pacific Kansas City (CPKC) rail line along Hammond Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/city-hall/hearings/2020-11-05_-2869_2020_north_lougheed_ocp_amendment_bylaw.pdf
- Harris Road is the main street, and City Hall at 12007 Harris Road sits in the civic core beside the recreation centre and library. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-hall/property-taxes/payment-options
- Mid Meadows lies southeast, West Meadows west, North Meadows across Lougheed Highway, and Maple Ridge east. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is an established Town Centre mix: detached houses on the interior streets, with multifamily buildings concentrated on Harris Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- The OCP designates the Town Centre for high and medium density multifamily and mixed-use commercial and residential development, so the Harris Road frontage differs from the interior streets. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- House lots sit under RS (Large Lot Residential), R-3, R-4 and R-6 zones in Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011, with subdivision minimums from 1,215 square metres in R-3, R-4 and R-6 to 0.2 hectares in RS. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- No condo building or townhome complex name in Central Meadows was confirmed on an official, developer or strata page read in this pass. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The governing plan is "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022, which repealed the former OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/media/8989
- One secondary suite per principal dwelling is allowed where the zone permits it, at 33 to 90 square metres inside the same building. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- A garden suite is one per lot at 33 to 90 square metres in the zones listed in the City garden suite guide, including RS, and a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- Under the City's Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) rules, lots of 280 square metres or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 square metres up to 4 units, and lots over 280 square metres within 400 metres of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units; the amending Bylaw No. 2986 was adopted June 25, 2024. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- The 1,215 square metre figure in the R zones is a subdivision minimum only and does not apply to development on existing lots, per the City's SSMUH implementation update. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Central Meadows home? **A:** Pitt Meadows Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary, for the sample address checked. School District 42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows) has no middle tier — elementary is Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary is Grades 8 to 12 — and its locator result for 12007 Harris Road is Pitt Meadows Elementary at 11941 Harris Road and Pitt Meadows Secondary at 19438 116B Avenue, the city's only public secondary school. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q:** Is this area in a floodplain? **A:** Yes — treat flood as standard due diligence here. The City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95 percent of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the floodplain of the Fraser River and, to a smaller degree, the Pitt River, and the city is almost entirely protected by 60 kilometres of dikes, ditches, pump stations and flood boxes. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Q:** Can I have both a suite and a garden suite? **A:** No — the City allows one or the other. Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 allows one secondary suite per principal dwelling at 33 to 90 square metres inside the same building where the zone permits it, and garden suites in the listed zones including RS, but a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Q:** What is the extra drainage charge on the tax bill? **A:** It is a separate levy inside the tax bylaw. The 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 sets the residential drainage rate at $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value, alongside the general municipal rate of $2.8438 per $1,000, reflecting this area's position in an urban drainage catchment. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Central Meadows drains through urban drainage Area 3 (Kennedy, including McKechnie) of the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District, serviced by ditches, culverts and storm sewers discharging to the Alouette, Pitt and Fraser Rivers. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- The civic core is walkable in one block: sxwhekwnes (Spirit Square) at 11985 Harris Road, Pitt Meadows Family Recreation Centre at 12027 Harris Road and Pitt Meadows Public Library at 200-12099 Harris Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/sxwhekwns-spirit-square
- Harris Road holds registered heritage buildings, including the Pitt Meadows General Store and Post Office at 12294 Harris Road, now the Pitt Meadows Museum. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/arts-culture-heritage/heritage
- Meadowtown Shopping Centre at 19800 Lougheed Highway, at the area's northeast edge, holds the Real Canadian Superstore grocery store named in the City's commercial lands analysis, while Meadowvale Shopping Centre on Harris Road North holds Save-On-Foods. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/city-hall/hearings/2025-11-04-Public-Hearing-Notice-3031%2C-2025-19800-Lougheed_Highway-Daycare.pdf
- Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK) is west of the area, and the City maintains a page for airport safety and noise concerns. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/arts-culture-heritage/heritage
- The nearest hospital is Ridge Meadows Hospital at 11666 Laity Street in Maple Ridge, a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency care. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Pitt Meadows' 2026 general municipal residential rate is $2.8438 per $1,000 of taxable value, plus the $0.1082 drainage rate, under Bylaw No. 3047, 2026, adopted May 5, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty added after July 2, 2026 and a further 5 percent after August 1, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies in the City of Pitt Meadows, which the Province lists as a designated taxable area in the Metro Vancouver Regional District. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- A 2026 flat utility fee amount for Pitt Meadows homes was not confirmed on a City page read in this pass, so none is stated here. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/67

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Pitt Meadows Station on Harris Road is the transit anchor: West Coast Express trains run to Waterfront Station, Vancouver, Monday to Friday in peak periods. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain
- The City states the West Coast Express trip from Pitt Meadows to downtown Vancouver is about 45 minutes. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain
- Bus connections at the station include route 701 to Coquitlam Central Station and Haney Place via Harris Road and Hammond Road, route 791 to Braid Station and Haney Place, the 719/722 Meadowtown–Fraser Way circular service, and the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus stop at Harris Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain
- Golden Ears Way and the Golden Ears Bridge, east of the area, are the driving route toward Langley and Surrey. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start on the City's Dikes page: the city is almost entirely protected by 60 kilometres of dikes, and the Diking District is defined by the Fraser, Pitt and Alouette River dikes. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- Check the lot against the Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008: its Designated Flood Level is 1.85 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum for the Schedule A Rural Floodplain and 5.15 metres for the Schedule B Urban Floodplain. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/Floodplain%20Designation%20and%20Construction%20Control%20Bylaw%20No.%202384%2C%202008%20-%20Consolidated.pdf
- Note the superseded figure: an older City document stated about 86 percent of the city in the floodplain; the City's 2020 Business Plan figure of over 95 percent is the current one and governs. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status per address: the City states 78 percent of its land base is in the ALR, although Town Centre parcels are a per-address check on Agricultural Land Commission maps. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- The dike pages do not tell a buyer an individual home's flood construction level or insurance position; that comes from the property's own permit history. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
