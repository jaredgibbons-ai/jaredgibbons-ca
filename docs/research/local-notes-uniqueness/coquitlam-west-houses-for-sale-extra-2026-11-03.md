## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The TOA tiers are the controlling extra rule: within 800 metres of a rapid transit station, Tier 1 (200 metres or less) requires allowing at least 5.0 floor area ratio (FAR) and up to 20 storeys, Tier 2 (over 200 to 400 metres) 4.0 FAR and up to 12 storeys, and Tier 3 (over 400 to 800 metres) 3.0 FAR and up to 8 storeys. https://coquitlam.ca.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?view_id=2&clip_id=2891&meta_id=82306
- Inside a TOA the City cannot require residential parking spaces other than parking for people with disabilities, and those residential parking rates have been removed from the Zoning Bylaw. https://coquitlam.ca.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?view_id=2&clip_id=2891&meta_id=82306
- City-wide small-scale multi-unit rules, Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 and tax facts for Coquitlam houses: see the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Burquitlam Station on the Millennium Line? **A:** Yes. Burquitlam Station is one of the eight SkyTrain stations in or near Coquitlam designated as transit stations for the provincial TOA rules, on the Millennium Line's Evergreen Extension, and Lougheed Town Centre Station is the next designated station south of it. A house's TOA tier is measured from the station, so two houses on the same street can sit in different tiers. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-coq-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2617/2719/6074/Project_Background_Document_-_Transit-Oriented_Areas.pdf
- **Q:** What does a TOA tier mean for the house lot I am buying? **A:** It sets the minimum density and height the City must allow, not a promise of approval. Tier 3, the tier covering most house streets here at 400 to 800 metres from Burquitlam or Lougheed Town Centre Station, requires allowing 3.0 FAR and up to 8 storeys on residential or mixed residential parcels, and the City cannot refuse a proposal on density or height alone up to those minimums. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-coq-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2617/2719/6074/Project_Background_Document_-_Transit-Oriented_Areas.pdf
- **Q:** What is Como Lake, for a house buyer? **A:** Como Lake is the small lake and park on Como Lake Avenue in the middle of this area, shown as Como Lake Park in the Burquitlam-Lougheed servicing assessment, with the Como Lake citywide greenway running from Clarke Road to the Burnaby border. It is a neighbourhood park and greenway anchor, not a swimming lake designation in the City documents read, so check current park rules before assuming any water use. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/482/Burquitlam-Lougheed-Neighbourhood-Plan-Servicing-Assessment-PDF?bidId=
- **Q:** Is the Lougheed Town Centre redevelopment in Burnaby or Coquitlam? **A:** The shopping centre site is in Burnaby, at 9855 Austin Avenue, while its station and TOA affect Coquitlam West houses across North Road. SHAPE Properties, the developer, describes The City of Lougheed as a 37-acre master plan on that site, with 20 residential towers and over 10,000 residents on completion, so Coquitlam West buyers get the station and the construction, but the towers themselves are a Burnaby project. https://shape.ca/commercial-leasing/the-city-of-lougheed/

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Measure the TOA tier before valuing a lot: the tier is set by distance from the station parcel by parcel — 200, 400 and 800 metres — and exempt lands include federally regulated properties and First Nation reserve lands, so a map average for the street is not a lot answer. https://coquitlam.ca.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?view_id=2&clip_id=2891&meta_id=82306
- Check creek and slope triggers before designing a small-scale rebuild, as the City flags both for lots in this part of Coquitlam: see the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note for the city-wide wording.
