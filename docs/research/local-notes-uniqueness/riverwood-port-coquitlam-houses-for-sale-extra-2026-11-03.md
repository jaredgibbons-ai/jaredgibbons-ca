## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Riverwood is the north-east Port Coquitlam house area by the Pitt River, around Riverwood Gate and Coast Meridian Road, with Terry Fox Secondary at 1260 Riverwood Gate as its named anchor. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/terryfox
- Terry Fox Secondary is named for Port Coquitlam's own Terry Fox, so the area's landmark school carries the city's best-known name. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/terryfox
- Citadel lies to the west, and the Pitt River and its trail system form the area's east side. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is detached houses on the Riverwood Gate and Coast Meridian Road streets, under the City's RS zones in Zoning Bylaw No. 3630: minimum lot width is 15 metres in RS1, 12 metres in RS2, 30 metres in RS3 and 9.5 metres in RS4. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Blakeburn Elementary, at 1040 Riverside Drive, and Fortress Park, at 2698 Fortress Court, are the addressed anchors inside the area's street system. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve Riverwood? **A:** The School District No. 43 (SD43) locator result recorded for 1260 Riverwood Gate is Blakeburn Elementary, Minnekhada Middle and Terry Fox Secondary, and Terry Fox Secondary itself stands at that Riverwood Gate address; catchments for other addresses must be checked in the locator. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** How many units can a Riverwood house lot take? **A:** Under the City's small-scale multi-unit housing rules, 3 to 6 units are allowed on RS lots without rezoning, with the count depending on lot size, location and transit access, so the lot's own figures decide the number. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q:** What trail system runs by Riverwood? **A:** The Traboulay PoCo Trail runs on the Pitt River side of the area, tying the riverfront streets to the city's trail network, and Fortress Park at 2698 Fortress Court is the addressed neighbourhood park. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- **Q:** When are Port Coquitlam property taxes due? **A:** Property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after July 2 and a further 5 percent after September 15, 2026, and utility charges are flat annual water and sewer fees billed separately, due March 31, 2026. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Port Coquitlam property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after July 2 and a further 5 percent after September 15, 2026, and the City's 2026 increase was 1.95 percent for the average home. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check flood status lot by lot: much of Port Coquitlam is Fraser and Pitt River floodplain behind dykes, and Riverwood sits on the Pitt River side, so a lot's dyke and flood-construction position needs confirming with the City. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Confirm the RS zone and its minimum lot width for the specific lot in Zoning Bylaw No. 3630, because RS1 through RS4 carry different widths from 9.5 to 30 metres. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes
- Confirm the SD43 catchment for the exact address in the district locator, since assignments follow the address. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
