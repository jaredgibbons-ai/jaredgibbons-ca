## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Abbotsford's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) amendment is Bylaw No. 3560-2024, "Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw, 2014, No. 2400, Amendment Bylaw, 2024, No. 3560," adopted June 25, 2024: accessory units count toward a lot's total permitted units — up to 4 units on one lot in SSMUH areas, being 3 units on lots 280 square metres or less and 4 units on lots bigger than 280 square metres. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/57884/ https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/2025-12-16%20-%20SSMUH%20Application%20Guide_1.pdf
- A secondary suite is a dwelling unit accessory to, and within, a principal dwelling unit, part of the same real estate entity; suites must be registered with the City, and the size cap is now less than 50 percent of the size of the principal unit. https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites
- A coach house is a dwelling unit accessory to a single detached dwelling or duplex on the same lot, on the second storey of an accessory building, and a garden suite is the single-storey at-grade form; both may occupy up to 15 percent of the lot area to a maximum of 100 square metres, maximum height is 7.5 metres or 2 storeys for a coach house and 4.5 metres or 1 storey for a garden suite, accessory units cannot be sold independently from the principal unit, and parking is 1 required stall for each accessory unit type under Zoning Bylaw Section 150. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/2025-12-16%20-%20SSMUH%20Application%20Guide_1.pdf
- On ALR land at the band's rural edge, a principal residence is capped at a total floor area of 500 square metres or less, with no more than 1,000 square metres of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence; and one additional residence is permitted up to 90 square metres on properties of 40 hectares or less and up to 186 square metres on properties over 40 hectares. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Subdivision on the farm edge is effectively closed at this band's scale: the City's AgRefresh parcel-size work records a minimum subdivision lot area of 8.0 hectares in the Agricultural One Zone (A1) and 16.0 hectares in the Agricultural Two Zone (A2). https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** How many Abbotsford homes are actually for sale under $1.8M? **A:** No sourced number exists, and any figure quoted for the band should be treated as unverified. - **Q:** Does a purchase under $1.8M reach the top property transfer tax tier? **A:** No. The Province's general rates are 1 percent on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2 percent on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3 percent only on the portion above $2,000,000, with a further 2 percent on the residential portion of value above $3,000,000 — so a purchase under $1,800,000 meets only the first two tiers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q:** Is a suite in an Abbotsford house in this band automatically legal? **A:** No — legality is a City-records question, not a listing-text question. A secondary suite must sit within the principal unit, be part of the same real estate entity, be registered with the City, and be less than 50 percent of the principal unit's size, so a marketed "mortgage helper" still needs its registration and permit history checked. https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites
- **Q:** Can a farm-edge property in this band add a second house? **A:** Only within tight Agricultural Land Commission limits. A suite is permitted only inside the principal residence, one additional residence is capped at 90 square metres on properties of 40 hectares or less or 186 square metres over 40 hectares, and City zoning also applies, with A1 subdivision minimums of 8.0 hectares and A2 at 16.0 hectares. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q:** Which school will an Abbotsford address in this band be assigned? **A:** It is address-based, and only recorded locator results should be quoted. For example, 33130 Bevan Avenue returns Godson Elementary, Abbotsford Middle School and Abbotsford Senior Secondary School, and 33661 Elizabeth Avenue returns Mathxwí Elementary, Clayburn Middle School and Robert Bateman Secondary; any other address must be run in the School District No. 34 (SD34) locator — run 2026-11-01, check current (SD34 School Locator, mybaragar.com).
