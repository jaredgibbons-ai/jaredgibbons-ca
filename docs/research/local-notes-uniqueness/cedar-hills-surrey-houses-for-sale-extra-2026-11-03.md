## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Cedar Hills has no City-published neighbourhood boundary. The City of Surrey does not publish Cedar Hills as an official neighbourhood name; the name is anchored on the ground by Cedar Hills Elementary School at 12370 98 Avenue, in the Whalley side of north Surrey near 124 Street and 100 Avenue. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- The school itself confirms the address: Surrey Schools' Cedar Hills page lists 12370 98 Avenue, Surrey, postal code V3V 2K3, and names the school Cedar Hills Elementary. https://www.surreyschools.ca/schools/cedarhill
- Houses here sit between the Bolivar Heights slope to the south and the Fraser River lowland to the north, inside the City of Surrey — but the exact streets the market calls Cedar Hills are UNCONFIRMED from any municipal source (see NOT CONFIRMED), so this note does not invent a boundary line. https://www.surreyschools.ca/schools/cedarhill

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The zoning check that matters is the lot's zone in Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, read lot by lot. Surrey replaced its old single-family zones in the July 8, 2024 rewrite: most former Single Family Residential (RF) lots are now R3 Urban Residential, intended for small-scale multi-unit housing on urban lots typically 560 square metres or larger, holding 2 to 6 dwelling units depending on lot size and location. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r128-proposed-amendments-surrey-zoning-law-1993-no-12000
- Subdivision minimums in R3 are a lot area of 560 square metres and a lot width of 15 metres, so an older Cedar Hills lot's split potential turns on whether it clears both figures — measure the surveyed lot, not the assessed frontage. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r128-proposed-amendments-surrey-zoning-law-1993-no-12000
- A houseplex (three or four units in one house-form building) is not a right on every lot: Surrey limits that form to the R3 zone on a lot with rear lane access or two street frontages, and a secondary suite is not permitted inside a houseplex. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r128-proposed-amendments-surrey-zoning-law-1993-no-12000

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves a Cedar Hills house? **A:** Cedar Hills Elementary is the named in-area school, at 12370 98 Avenue, Kindergarten to Grade 7. Surrey Schools lists that address on the school's own page, and the provincial school list confirms the grade range — but catchment is set by the exact home address, so run the address in the district's School Locator before treating any one house as in-catchment. https://www.surreyschools.ca/schools/cedarhill
- **Q:** Which secondary school will my child attend? **A:** That is not confirmed for Cedar Hills in this note, on purpose. Earlier area research paired the Cedar Hills name with Tamanawis Secondary, but Tamanawis is at 12600 66 Avenue — far south in Newton — while Cedar Hills Elementary is at 98 Avenue in north Surrey, so that pairing is not repeated here; confirm the secondary catchment for the specific address in the School District 36 locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** How many homes can go on my lot now? **A:** It depends on the zone and the lot, not on the Cedar Hills name. Under Surrey's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules as written into Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, an eligible R3 lot under 280 square metres is capped at 3 units, a larger eligible lot at 4, and a lot of at least 281 square metres inside a Frequent Bus Stop Area at up to 6 — setbacks, parking and lane access still decide what actually fits. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r128-proposed-amendments-surrey-zoning-law-1993-no-12000
- **Q:** How does registration and catchment actually work in Surrey? **A:** Catchment is address-based and capacity can override it. Surrey Schools states that your catchment school is determined by your address on the district's School Boundaries Map, that the School Locator returns it, and that Grade 7 students in a district elementary school are registered into their feeder secondary school automatically — enrolment at a school of choice still depends on availability. https://www.surreyschools.ca/chs/registration-1

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The district runs Kindergarten to Grade 7 elementary schools and Grade 8 to 12 secondary schools, with no middle-school tier. Surrey Schools states that structure directly, which matters for a Cedar Hills family planning a single elementary run at Cedar Hills Elementary followed by one secondary move. https://www.surreyschools.ca/cedarhill/_ci/p/174781
- Summer Learning runs at named sites, not at every school: the district's Summer Learning list includes Bridgeview Elementary at 12834 115A Avenue and Panorama Park Elementary at 12878 62 Avenue among its elementary sites — Cedar Hills Elementary is not listed as a Summer Learning site on that page. https://www.surreyschools.ca/summerlearning/class-sites
- Because the City publishes no Cedar Hills plan, there is no neighbourhood concept plan (NCP) amenity package to expect: unlike NCP areas, no plan document read in this pass promises Cedar Hills a future park, road or school contribution package — check the specific street, not a plan promise. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans

- The first due-diligence step here is identity, not hazards: confirm which Cedar Hills the listing means. The verified school anchor is at 12370 98 Avenue in north Surrey; material online that pairs "Cedar Hills" with Newton schools around 66 Avenue describes different ground and must not be used for a 98 Avenue house. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
