## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- This page is a price band over one municipality, the City of Abbotsford: the $800K ceiling is the page's own filter, and the condo stock it draws on sits in the city's urban core, not its rural sub-areas. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- The core the condos cluster in is planned, not accidental: the City Centre Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2913-2019) and the Historic Downtown Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2920-2019) are adopted neighbourhood plans under the city-wide Abbotsforward 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP), and they cover the Mill Lake, South Fraser Way and Historic Downtown ground where apartment buildings stand. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- The OCP's growth direction for that core is a new mixed-use centre between the hospital and Mill Lake Park and a Primary Transit Corridor along South Fraser Way between Clearbrook and McCallum Road. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan
- Abbotsford-wide facts are treated in full elsewhere — see the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note; this note covers the condo stock and strata specifics only.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Abbotsford's tallest condo stock is one named tower: Mahogany at Mill Lake, 2180 Gladwin Road — a 27-storey concrete residential tower with 152 suites above three storeys of commercial space and a two-level underground parkade, developed by Quantum Properties Mahogany LP and built by Ventana Construction. https://www.ventanaconstruction.com/multi-family-residential/mahogany-at-mill-lake?itemId=l75nwgoa191q6nwa2steub8dcjgrvn
- Part of that tower is not strata resale stock at all: floors five through ten of Mahogany are operated by Aging in Place at Mahogany as private, full-sized rental suites for independent senior living — six floor plans from 674 to 1,661 square feet — so a buyer searching the building will meet both strata units and rental suites at the same address. https://www.aipcl.ca/
- The low-rise end of the stock is planned at six storeys and under: the Historic Downtown plan's Urban 1 – Midrise designation allows up to 6 storeys, and its Urban 2 – Ground Oriented designation enables rowhouses, townhouse, multiplex or duplex housing. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/Historic%20Downtown%20Neighbourhood%20Plan%20Final%20Event%20Boards.pdf
- Abbotsford condos are freehold strata, and the provincial strata framework — depreciation reports, strata records and the Form B information certificate — is the document set that describes any specific building in this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A depreciation report is mandatory on a five-year cycle for every British Columbia strata corporation with five or more strata lots, with no deferral vote, and strata corporations in the Fraser Valley that had no report, or one received before December 31, 2020, had to obtain a current one by July 1, 2026 — the first document to ask for in any Abbotsford building in this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- New condo form in the core is set by the two adopted neighbourhood plans: the City Centre plan (Bylaw 2913-2019) and Historic Downtown plan (Bylaw 2920-2019) designations decide where midrise and mixed-use buildings can go, under the Abbotsforward 2050 OCP. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- One utility rule splits Abbotsford condo addresses in two: water and sewer customers are billed bi-monthly, except customers in the Clearbrook Waterworks District, who are billed quarterly for sewer services — two buildings in this band can be on different City billing cycles. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/current-utility-bill
- Rental, pet and age rules are building-level strata bylaws, not City rules: no Abbotsford building's registered bylaws were read in this pass, so any rental or 55+ claim for a specific building must be read in that building's own documents — see NOT CONFIRMED.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** As a first-time buyer, does the property transfer tax (PTT) exemption reach condos in this band? **A:** Yes, on the Province's published thresholds: the first time home buyers' program's full exemption runs to a fair market value of $835,000 or less and exempts the tax on the first $500,000 of the purchase price, with a partial exemption only above $835,000 and below $860,000, subject to the program's other personal and property conditions. Because this page's band caps at $800,000, a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in the band is under the full-exemption value ceiling — confirm eligibility with a legal professional before writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q:** What is unusual about the Mahogany at Mill Lake tower? **A:** Two things a buyer would not expect. It is a 27-storey concrete tower of 152 suites at 2180 Gladwin Road, developed by Quantum Properties Mahogany LP, standing above three storeys of commercial space beside Mill Lake. And floors five through ten are not strata lots for sale — the building's own operator runs them as private rental suites for independent senior living, in six floor plans from 674 to 1,661 square feet. Check which tenure a specific suite is before comparing it to strata resales in the same building. https://www.ventanaconstruction.com/multi-family-residential/mahogany-at-mill-lake?itemId=l75nwgoa191q6nwa2steub8dcjgrvn https://www.aipcl.ca/
- **Q:** How does the City bill a condo for water and sewer? **A:** By metered consumption, billed bi-monthly for most of the city: effective March 1, 2026, the residential water rate is $1.46 per cubic metre and the sewer rate is $1.35 per cubic metre, after Council approved a 4 percent increase to both in January 2026. Paying by the due date printed on the bill earns a 5 percent discount. The exception is the Clearbrook Waterworks District, where sewer is billed quarterly — so the building's district, not just its strata fees, decides the utility rhythm a buyer inherits. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/water-sewer-rates https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/current-utility-bill
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Central Abbotsford condo? **A:** School District 34 catchments are address-based and set through the district's School Locator portal for the primary home address. In locator runs recorded in this program for Central Abbotsford addresses, 33130 Bevan Avenue returns Godson Elementary, then Abbotsford Middle, then Abbotsford Senior Secondary, and 32161 Dormick Avenue returns Dormick Park Elementary, then Abbotsford Middle, then Abbotsford Senior Secondary. A condo building's own address must be run before any school is marketed with the unit. https://swift.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/kindergartenregistration/swift
- **Q:** What strata document matters most in an older Abbotsford building? **A:** The depreciation report. Strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain one on a five-year cycle from a qualified provider, projecting maintenance, repair and replacement costs over 30 years, and Fraser Valley stratas without a current report had to obtain one by July 1, 2026. Read it together with the contingency reserve fund position and the seller's Form B information certificate: in this band, the building's funding for roofs, elevators and parkades is a bigger price factor than the unit's finishes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Abbotsford gives a 5 percent discount on the utility bill if payment is received by the due date printed on the bill — a small, automatic saving that only exists if the bill is paid on time. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/current-utility-bill
- Any utility account balance still outstanding at December 31 is transferred to the property tax account and charged daily interest, so a condo seller's unpaid City utility bill becomes a tax-account problem for the property. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/current-utility-bill
- Mill Lake Park, the green edge of the central condo stock, is accessed from Bevan Avenue and Emerson Street. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds
- Bourquin Exchange is the central transit exchange, and BC Transit's Central Fraser Valley network names routes 1 (Highstreet/UFV), 2 (Highstreet/McMillan), 5 (Clearbrook/Bourquin via Hospital), 12 (UFV/Bourquin) and 21 (Aldergrove/Abbotsford) among the services tying the condo core to the University of the Fraser Valley (UFV), the hospital and Highstreet. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre is at 32900 Marshall Road, and the Abbotsford Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at Unit 100, 2692 Clearbrook Road — the two Fraser Health anchors at either end of the central condo corridor. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre
- Clearbrook Library is at 32320 George Ferguson Way and the Abbotsford branch library is at 33355 Bevan Avenue, both inside the central area the condo stock sits in. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/

- Abbotsford property tax rates and the full tax picture are city-wide facts treated in full in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note; the dates a condo owner works to: property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5 percent penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5 percent penalty on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Abbotsford has no SkyTrain; the condo core runs on BC Transit buses through Bourquin Exchange, including route 1 (Highstreet/UFV), route 2 (Highstreet/McMillan), route 5 (Clearbrook/Bourquin via Hospital), route 12 (UFV/Bourquin) and route 31 (Abbotsford/Mission). https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- South Fraser Way is the commercial backbone of the City Centre, and Highway 1 interchanges at McCallum Road and Clearbrook Road serve the central condo area, with Highway 11 running north to Mission. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/HDNP%2BCommercial%2BMarket%2BStudy.pdf
- The OCP designates South Fraser Way between Clearbrook and McCallum Road as a Primary Transit Corridor — the planning commitment behind bus priority on the street most central condos front. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Read the depreciation report and contingency reserve position before subject removal: Fraser Valley stratas had a July 1, 2026 deadline for a current report, and a building that just commissioned its first report in years is a building whose levy schedule is about to be written. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Confirm the tenure of the specific suite in a mixed building: at Mahogany at Mill Lake, floors five through ten are operator-run rental suites, not strata lots, so a "unit in the tower" is not automatically a strata purchase. https://www.aipcl.ca/
- British Columbia strata disputes can be resolved through the Civil Resolution Tribunal, and the Province's strata housing pages set out how strata ownership, records and dispute resolution work — the official route if a records dispute follows a purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- Flood and Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) checks are Abbotsford-wide files treated in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note; apartment buildings in the urban core are not the ALR stock, but a buyer's lawyer still confirms the parcel's designations.
