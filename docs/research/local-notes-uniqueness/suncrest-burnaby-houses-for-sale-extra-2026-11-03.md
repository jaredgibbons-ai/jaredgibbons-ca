# Suncrest Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: suncrest-burnaby-houses-for-sale (Suncrest; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Suncrest is the western, all-detached pocket of south Burnaby below Metrotown, bordered by South Slope to the east; the full shared area facts are in the suncrest-burnaby note. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/lewarne-park-playground
- Rumble Street is the spine: TransLink's 146 Metrotown Station and Suncrest loop turns through the area on Rumble Street between Joffre Avenue and Patterson Avenue, which is the clearest official trace of the neighbourhood's extent. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt146.pdf
- Burnaby South Secondary, the area's secondary school, stands at 5455 Rumble Street at the neighbourhood's northeast corner, and Clinton Elementary stands at 5858 Clinton Street on the area's south side. https://south.burnabyschools.ca/

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is all detached houses, which is what sets Suncrest apart from the tower stock of Metrotown immediately to the north and from the mixed stock of the Royal Oak station area to the east. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/lewarne-park-playground
- Detached lots here fall under the City's R1 small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) district, so a house lot can be redeveloped as up to 3 to 6 dwelling units depending on lot area and proximity to a frequent transit network bus stop. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Burnaby's new Zoning Bylaw 2026 was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026, replacing the former Zoning Bylaw 1965, and the R1 SSMUH district dates to the June 2024 rezoning of all R district lots. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- Burnaby 2050, the City's official community plan, was adopted December 9, 2025 and sets the land-use direction for the whole city including this pocket. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- Burnaby is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve Suncrest? **A:** Clinton Elementary, at 5858 Clinton Street, is the School District No. 41 (SD41) elementary school on the area's south side, and Burnaby South Secondary, at 5455 Rumble Street, is the secondary school at the neighbourhood's northeast corner; SD41 assigns catchments by address, so confirm the assigned schools for a specific address. https://burnabyschools.ca/elementary-school-addresses/
- **Q:** Can I build a multiplex on a Suncrest house lot? **A:** Possibly, under the R1 SSMUH district: newly rezoned R1 lots are eligible for up to 3 to 6 dwelling units depending on lot area and proximity to a frequent transit network bus stop, and the count is inclusive of any secondary suites. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q:** Is Suncrest part of Metrotown? **A:** No. Suncrest is the all-detached pocket below Metrotown, and Metrotown's tower stock sits to the north; the pocket's own spine is Rumble Street, not the town-centre streets. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/lewarne-park-playground
- **Q:** What is the 2026 residential tax rate? **A:** Burnaby's 2026 residential mill rate totals 3.28373 per 1,000 dollars of assessed value, and gross taxes are the assessed value multiplied by the mill rate and divided by 1,000, before local area service, sewer parcel and business improvement area charges. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates
Read: 2026-11-03

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby's 2026 residential tax rate is 3.28373 per 1,000 dollars of assessed value, and the average residential assessment in Burnaby decreased by 5.29 percent in 2026, which does not necessarily mean an individual bill falls, because taxes depend on the change in a property's assessment relative to the class average. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates
- Property taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after the July due date and a further 5 percent after the September due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes
- Assessment notices arrive from BC Assessment each January, and the assessed value on the notice is the value the mill rate is applied to. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Confirm the lot's R1 SSMUH eligibility and unit count with the City before assuming a multiplex, because the count turns on lot area and on distance to a frequent transit network bus stop. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- Confirm the SD41 catchment for the exact address, since Clinton Elementary and Burnaby South Secondary are the named schools but assignments follow the address. https://burnabyschools.ca/elementary-school-addresses/
- Confirm whether a lot on the Rumble Street edge falls in the Royal Oak Community Plan's station-area rules rather than the standard R1 rules. http://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-04/Royal-Oak-Community-Plan.pdf
