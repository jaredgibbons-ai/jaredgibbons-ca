# West Vancouver — Questions for Jared

For Jared to answer by voice memo. Muse does not answer these.

1. When a buyer asks you about West Vancouver versus North Vancouver, what differences do you actually see in the deals — lot sizes, view premiums, how fast the good listings move?
2. Who is buying in West Vancouver right now — downsizers from the same area, families moving up from Vancouver, out-of-province buyers — and who is selling?
3. What surprises buyers most once they are serious about a West Vancouver house — renovation and demolition rules, septic versus sewer pockets, school catchment expectations, insurance?
4. Ambleside, Dundarave, Horseshoe Bay, British Properties: which pocket would you send a buyer to first for value, and which one do you find hardest to buy well in, and why?
5. Tell one story: a West Vancouver deal that taught you something about the area that no listing sheet shows.

## Shot list for Jared

1. Ambleside beach and the seawall looking east toward the Lions Gate Bridge and downtown Vancouver — late-afternoon light, from the foot of 14th Street.
2. Marine Drive in Dundarave village looking west along the shops toward the mountains — morning, before the lunch crowd.
3. Horseshoe Bay from the ferry terminal viewpoint looking across the bay to the marina and the Sea to Sky mountains — clear day, mid-morning.
