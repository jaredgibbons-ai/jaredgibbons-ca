## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- No verified 55+ strata or freehold ownership complex exists in West Chilliwack: the settled roster in this program records none, and the one strata building on Evans Road, Creekside Estates at 7694 Evans Road, is recorded in that roster as having no age restrictions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Cottonwood Village (Cottonwood Mobile Home Park) at 7610 Evans Road is the area's one verified 55+ product: a manufactured-home park carried in the settled roster as 143 units with a clubhouse, on First Nations leased land, where the buyer purchases the manufactured home and rents the pad. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- No 55+ rental building, retirement residence or subsidized seniors building with a West Chilliwack address was verified in the settled program file; the city's registered assisted-living residences and subsidized seniors addresses in that file are on the east and downtown side. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=pique%20newsmagazine&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&f%5B0%5D=ha%3AFraser%20Health&page=1&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A manufactured-home park is not a strata: pad tenancies at Cottonwood Village are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, and a sale is an assignment of the pad tenancy that requires the park owner's consent, with the seller required to provide the written tenancy agreement and park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- The assignment rules recognise a park where every pad is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55, or two or more tenants of whom at least one has reached 55, under section 10(2)(b)(i) of the Human Rights Code — so a park can refuse an assignment where no purchaser meets the age requirement. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- First Nations leased land adds a head lease above the pad tenancy at Cottonwood Village: the settled roster records a lease expiry in 2076 with an option to extend, stated in listing-derived sources, so the lease term and extension mechanics — not a strata Form B — are the documents to review. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol20/consol20/02077_pit
- Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, in the Agricultural Lowland (AL) zone that frames the rural west, sets a minimum lot area of 7.5 hectares for land excluded or exempted from the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR), 1 hectare for a boundary adjustment, and 0.2 hectares for an approved homesite severance. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there a 55+ strata condo or townhome I can buy in West Chilliwack? **A:** No, on the settled evidence. No age-restricted strata or freehold ownership complex with a West Chilliwack address was verified, and the one strata building on the Evans Road corridor, Creekside Estates at 7694 Evans Road, is recorded as having no age restrictions. A buyer who needs strata ownership in Chilliwack is directed to the downtown and central complexes in the city 55+ notes, not to this area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** What can a 55+ buyer buy on Evans Road, then? **A:** A manufactured home in Cottonwood Village at 7610 Evans Road. The buyer owns the home, rents the pad, and holds a pad tenancy under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act on First Nations leased land. That is leasehold park tenure, not strata or freehold ownership, and the purchase closes as an assignment of the pad tenancy with the park owner's consent. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Q:** My spouse is under 55. Does the park test work differently from a strata? **A:** Yes. In a 55+ strata, a younger spouse or partner is exempt by provincial regulation. In a park, the assignment framework is written around at least one of two or more tenants having reached 55, and the park's own rules govern the pad tenancy. Read the park rules the seller must provide before writing an offer, because the strata exemption list does not apply to a park. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Q:** Is there health care inside this area? **A:** Yes, one service is inside the corridor: the Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #104 – 7955 Evans Road, co-located with the Chilliwack and Fraser Health Rural Primary Care Centre. Chilliwack General Hospital is outside the area, so urgent and primary care on Evans Road is the local service, and hospital care is a trip east. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/Chilliwack-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Two neighbouring Evans Road addresses are opposite products: 7610 Evans Road is 55+ leasehold park housing, and 7694 Evans Road, a few doors from Evans Elementary, is a general-market strata recorded with no age restrictions — a corridor search blurs that distinction. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- Board listing data classifies 7610 Evans Road as sub-area Sardis West Vedder, while this site's West Chilliwack research treats the Evans Road corridor as the core of West Chilliwack — the two systems name the same ground differently. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- School District 33 (Chilliwack) catchments are address-based: the locator result recorded in the settled file for 7600 Evans Road is Evans Elementary, then Vedder Middle, then Sardis Secondary. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- Chilliwack property tax notices are due the first working day after July 1, with a 5 percent penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5 percent after August 4, 2026, plus separate drainage and dyking levies on top of the municipal rates. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Chilliwack's property tax rates are the sum of each taxing authority's levy, and the City states that additional taxes are levied for drainage and dyking on top of the municipal rates — a standing extra on lowland west-side properties. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- A Cottonwood Village buyer pays pad rent under a pad tenancy rather than strata fees, and the amount is set by the park's tenancy documents; no current pad figure is stated in this note because no park page stating one was read in this pass. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- The provincial property tax deferment program is for people 55 and older, which is the age group this page serves. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check flood setbacks on any rural west-side lot under the City's Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519, which sets minimum building setbacks of 60 metres from the Fraser River, 75 metres from the Vedder River and 30 metres from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- At Cottonwood Village the due-diligence file is the head lease and the park rules, not a depreciation report: the remaining lease term, the extension option and the park owner's assignment consent decide what a lender, an insurer and the next buyer will accept. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- Confirm Agricultural Land Reserve status for any lot outside the park: most of the surrounding west-side ground is farm land under the Agricultural Area Plan, and ALR use limits apply to a house lot there. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf
