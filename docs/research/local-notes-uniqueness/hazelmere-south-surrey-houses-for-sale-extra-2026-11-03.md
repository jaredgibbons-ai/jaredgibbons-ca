# Hazelmere Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: hazelmere-south-surrey-houses-for-sale (Hazelmere; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Hazelmere is the rural south-east corner of Surrey's South Surrey community, inside the City of Surrey. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- The older name is Hall's Prairie: the City's South Campbell Heights plan records that Hall's Prairie was renamed Hazelmere after the rail station named for Henry Thrift's homestead. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SouthCampbellHeightsLocalAreaPlan.pdf
- The South Campbell Heights plan area sits immediately north of Hazelmere's southern lands: about 249 hectares centred around 16 Avenue and 192 Street, south of the Campbell Heights Business Park. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/south-campbell-heights-display-boards.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The housing stock is detached houses on rural and agricultural acreage lots, not subdivisions. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SouthCampbellHeightsLocalAreaPlan.pdf
- The City's plan document records that Hazelmere largely maintained its rural, agricultural character over the past century. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SouthCampbellHeightsLocalAreaPlan.pdf
- The ground is a mix of agricultural fields and second-growth forest on gently sloping land on either side of the Little Campbell River, which flows in a shallow ravine. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/south-campbell-heights-display-boards.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- On Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) land, the Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) generally allows one residence per parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- An ALR principal residence may be up to 500 m² total floor area without an ALC application, and one additional detached residence may be permitted: up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 hectares or less, or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 hectares. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- A secondary suite on ALR land is permitted only inside the principal residence, and only where local bylaws allow it; Surrey's zoning can be more restrictive than the ALC frame. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Surrey's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) allowances do not automatically reach Hazelmere acreages: they apply to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, and properties not connected to community water and sewer are among the exemptions. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Surrey-wide zoning, suite and coach house rules are carried in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note; see that note before quoting them for a Hazelmere lot.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I build a second house or coach house on a Hazelmere acreage? **A:** Only within the ALR limits if the parcel is in the Agricultural Land Reserve: one principal residence up to 500 m², a suite only inside that residence, and at most one additional detached residence of 90 m² or less on parcels up to 40 hectares. Surrey zoning can restrict further, so the parcel's ALR status and zone decide. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q:** Is Hazelmere in Surrey or Langley? **A:** Hazelmere is in the City of Surrey, in its South Surrey community, at the city's south-east corner. The Township of Langley and Campbell Valley lie east of the Surrey boundary, outside Hazelmere. The confusion comes from the rural character and the Langley border, but taxes, zoning, permits and services here are Surrey's. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- **Q:** Which school is in Hazelmere itself? **A:** Xw'epiteng Elementary, at 18035 8 Avenue, is the school physically in Hazelmere, listed in the BC Ministry of Education school list for School District 36 (Surrey). Being the closest school does not assign a child to it: Surrey Schools assigns catchment schools by exact home address through its School Locator tool, so check the parcel's address before relying on any school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036 https://www.surreyschools.ca/ellendale/school-registration
- **Q:** Can I add the usual Surrey SSMUH units to a Hazelmere lot? **A:** Generally not on the standard framing. Surrey's SSMUH allowances cover eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, and lands not connected to community water and sewer are exempt. Most Hazelmere acreage sits outside that shape, so any added-unit claim needs a parcel-level check of zone, containment boundary and servicing first. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Well records for older Hazelmere properties may simply not exist in the provincial database: wells built before 2016, when registration was voluntary, might not be captured in GWELLS (Groundwater Wells and Aquifers). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers
- A septic system is one of the approvals the ALC lists for ALR residential work: health authority approval for septic systems may be required alongside zoning and building permits. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- The nearest confirmed TransLink bus service is route 531, which runs along 24 Avenue and 192 Street at Hazelmere's northern edge; no route runs through the Hazelmere valley roads. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt531.pdf

## 6. DEVELOPMENT AND NEW PROJECTS
- The South Campbell Heights Local Area Plan, immediately north of Hazelmere's southern lands, was approved by Surrey Council on December 16, 2024 (Corporate Report R265), concluding that planning process. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/south-campbell-heights
- On February 25, 2022, the Metro Vancouver Regional District Board adopted a regional growth strategy amendment for South Campbell Heights that changed about 228 hectares of Rural land to Mixed Employment, Conservation and Recreation, and Agricultural designations, and extended the Urban Containment Boundary by about 223 hectares. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/south-campbell-heights
- An Integrated Stormwater Management Plan for the South Campbell Heights area is in progress, aimed at protecting the health of the Little Campbell River watershed and the Brookswood Aquifer while development proceeds. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/south-campbell-heights-display-boards.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2; Hazelmere properties pay City of Surrey taxes. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- The provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies here: the Province lists the City of Surrey as a designated taxable area, so owners file a declaration each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- Surrey's 2026 residential tax rate and flat utility charges are carried in full in the surrey-houses-for-sale note; see that note for the figures.

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the parcel's ALR status before assuming any non-farm use or second dwelling: ALR status is parcel-specific, and the ALC's residence size limits apply to any parcel inside the reserve. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- A development permit may be required in Surrey's Steep Slope, Flood Plain, Sensitive Ecosystem or Farm Protection Development Permit Areas, which matters on Hazelmere's creek and ravine lots along the Little Campbell River. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Search GWELLS for the property's well record before closing, and treat a missing record as unproven servicing, not as no well: pre-2016 wells were registered voluntarily and may be absent from the database. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers
