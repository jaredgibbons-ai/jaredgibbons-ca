# Cape Horn Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: cape-horn-coquitlam-houses-for-sale (Cape Horn; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Cape Horn is the established residential slope in south Coquitlam above the Cape Horn interchange, where Highway 1 (the Trans-Canada Highway), Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and the Mary Hill Bypass (Highway 7B) meet at the Port Mann Bridge approach. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/291/Cape-Horn-C15-C16-PDF?bidId=
- The City tracks the area as planning areas 15 and 16 in its development statistics, rather than as a named neighbourhood plan. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/291/Cape-Horn-C15-C16-PDF?bidId=
- Cape Horn Avenue and Mariner Way are the residential roads connecting the slope to the interchange, with United Boulevard the light-industrial and commercial road below it. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/291/Cape-Horn-C15-C16-PDF?bidId=
- Next to it: Maillardville and Coquitlam West to the west and north-west, Central Coquitlam up Mariner Way to the north, Port Coquitlam across the Coquitlam River and the Mary Hill Bypass to the east, and Surrey across the Port Mann Bridge to the south. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses here are freehold-led detached stock on a slope, with the City's own permit record for the area in the statistics year entirely in single-family, suite and carriage-house form, and no townhouse or apartment units created. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/291/Cape-Horn-C15-C16-PDF?bidId=
- Subdivision minimums are 650 square metres with 18.5 metres of width in zones R-1 and R-3, and 465 square metres with 12.5 metres of width in R-2, under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId
- No City, School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) (SD43) or BC Assessment public page read for this note states a typical build decade or typical lot size for Cape Horn houses, so era and lot size are per-lot checks. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The slope is the rule, not the interchange, because a lot's rebuild depends on its grade, its creek edge and its access, not on how close it sits to Highway 1. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- City-wide small-scale multi-unit housing rules are in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; the Cape Horn angle is that the City's early-check list — a creek on or next to the lot, a steep slope, or a house built before 1945 — is routine, not exceptional, on these slope streets. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- Land within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of a watercourse needs a Watercourse Protection Development Permit before land is altered, and Cape Horn Creek gives this rule a local name. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is actually in Cape Horn? **A:** Cape Horn Elementary, at 155 Finnigan Street, is the School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) elementary school inside this area, with phone 604-526-4428. Its presence is not a catchment promise: district catchment is by permanent home address, so run the exact address in the district School Locator, and check whether the middle and secondary assignment follows École Montgomery Middle and Centennial Secondary. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/school/capehorn/Parents/
- **Q:** Will I hear the highway? **A:** Plan on it and check the specific lot. The slope sits directly above the interchange where Highway 1, Lougheed Highway and the Mary Hill Bypass meet, so highway and bridge noise is a property-by-property question — the City and TransLink pages read for this note give no lot-level noise contour, and no distance claim should be quoted without a site visit. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/291/Cape-Horn-C15-C16-PDF?bidId=
- **Q:** Can I add a suite or carriage house to a Cape Horn house? **A:** Possibly, under the small-scale rules in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. The Cape Horn-specific limit is the lot itself: subdivision minimums of 650 square metres in R-1 and R-3 and 465 square metres in R-2, plus the creek, slope or pre-1945-house check, and a watercourse permit for creek-edge lots. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId
- **Q:** Is Cape Horn in the Fraser floodplain? **A:** Not in the way a riverfront lot is. The City's floodplain mapping covers the Coquitlam, Fraser and Pitt river floodplains, while Cape Horn sits on the slope above the interchange lowlands; the water risk to check lot by lot is Cape Horn Creek and slope drainage, not a river dike. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/182/Coquitlam-Fraser-and-Pitt-Rivers-Floodplain-Mapping-Report-PDF

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check highway and rail noise lot by lot: the City and TransLink pages read give no Cape Horn-specific noise contour, so a site visit and a check of the lot's position above the interchange are the due diligence, not a published zone. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/291/Cape-Horn-C15-C16-PDF?bidId=
