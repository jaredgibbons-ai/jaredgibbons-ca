## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Council adopted Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing (Bill 44) zoning on June 24, 2024; houseplexes of three or four units become a permitted use, and secondary suites are not allowed inside a houseplex. https://letstalk.delta.ca/ssmuh
- Delta's announcement states a minimum of four units on lots over 280 m² and six units within 400 m of frequent transit. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/zoning-amendment-bylaws-introduced-permit-small-scale-multi
- The Province defines frequent transit as a route every 15 minutes on average, weekdays 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. and weekends 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/local-governments-and-housing/housing-initiatives/smale-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Delta's current limits: 9.5 m sloped-roof height, 1.5 m side yard, one on-site space per unit; proposed changes are 10.5 m height, 40% coverage and 15 m frontage for new lots. https://letstalk.delta.ca/ssmuh
- Strata corporations cannot pass rental-restriction bylaws, but can restrict or ban short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/renting-in-stratas/landlords-and-strata-corporations
- Strata pet bylaws can limit or ban pets, except certified guide and service dogs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/pet-bylaws

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which school is Hawthorne Elementary? **A:** Hawthorne Elementary, 5160 Central Avenue, is a K-7 school, phone 604-946-7601; catchments are address-based, so confirm a specific home with School District 37. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/school/03737025
- **Q:** Can a Hawthorne lot take a suite, coach house or houseplex? **A:** Delta's June 2024 zoning allows these on single-detached and duplex lots, but each lot's size, transit distance and flood elevation decide the count; ask Delta's Application Centre at development@delta.ca or 604-946-3380. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
- **Q:** Does Delta set a minimum floor elevation? **A:** Delta shows minimum flood elevations on DeltaMap, and a restrictive covenant may set a minimum building elevation for habitable space, or one may be required at permit stage. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
- **Q:** Can a Hawthorne-area strata ban rentals or pets? **A:** It cannot ban long-term rentals, but it can restrict short-term rentals and set pet limits, so read the filed bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/renting-in-stratas/landlords-and-strata-corporations

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- 2026 residential municipal rate is 2.4065 per $1,000 (General 2.2925, Neighbourhood Road Improvements 0.0574, Library 0.0566), up from 2.2403 in 2025; specified-area drainage adds 0.0944, school 1.0997 and TransLink 0.3586. https://www.delta.ca/media/4005
- 2026 property tax was due July 2, 2026; the basic Home Owner Grant maximum is $570 and the additional grant $275. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- 2026 flat-rate utilities for a single-family home are $1,658, up from $1,607; water is $1.47 per m³ to 125 m³ a quarter then $1.97; sewer is $2.13 per m³ on 80% of water use. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Apartments and townhouses pay flat-rate utilities per dwelling unit; no separate rate is stated. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Delta is a Speculation and Vacancy Tax area; 2026 rates are 1% for Canadian citizens and permanent residents and 3% for foreign owners. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/tax-rates

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Delta runs 31 drainage pump stations, about 538 km of drainage infrastructure and over 63 km of flood protection, with riverside and coastal dikes protecting the lowlands. https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Check DeltaMap and the title for a flood covenant before removing subjects. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
- Projects disturbing soil need a provincial Site Disclosure Statement where contamination is likely; Delta's page does not mention peat or water table. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
- Delta notes floodplain conditions often make deep basements unfeasible. https://letstalk.delta.ca/ssmuh
