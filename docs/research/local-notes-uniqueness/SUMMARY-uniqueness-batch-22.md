## Conflicts with earlier settled facts / premise corrections

1. **Tsawwassen Lands registration — premise corrected against the primary source.** The working premise (Tsawwassen Lands homes are registered in the Nation's own register, not the BC Land Title Office) is contradicted by Chapter 5 (Land Title) of the Tsawwassen First Nation Final Agreement (BC Laws): interests in Tsawwassen Lands ARE registered in the Land Title Office under the Land Title Act, with a Tsawwassen Lands notation on title; Tsawwassen Law adds a required Certificate of Transfer before a fee simple transfer can be registered, and only TFN can cancel an indefeasible title. The note is written to the official text and the correction is flagged in its NOT CONFIRMED. **Earlier program files that assert a separate TFN lands register should be checked against this.**
2. **Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** — carried as an open ruling in earlier programs; confirmed as read on delta.ca's zoning page in this pass and used as read in the Delta notes.
3. **District domains:** Surrey Schools' live official site is surreyschools.ca (cited for the catchment locator), and the Delta School District's is deltasd.bc.ca; the brief's sd36.bc.ca / sd37.bc.ca addresses are not the districts' live sites.
4. **Mission Sign Bylaw No. 1662-1987 contains no open-house provision at all** (full text searched). The Mission note therefore rests on what the bylaw does state: §7.4/§7.5 prohibitions on third-party/portable and highway signs, a $250 illegal-sign ticket on city property, and §9.2(a) permitting a temporary on-site sale sign with no permit. Any assumption that Mission has a codified open-house sign rule is not supported.
5. **Delta's 2026 municipal residential tax rate per $1,000 could not be extracted** (the schedule linked from delta.ca's tax page is a bylaw PDF with no extractable text; two reads failed). It is recorded "unconfirmed" in two §10 tables and the NOT CONFIRMED sections — never estimated. A Metro Vancouver levy component was read but is not comparable to a municipal total and was not used.
6. **Search-text reliance (disclosed):** three facts rest on search-result text of the official page after browser transport failures — the Surrey SSMUH zoning page, Mission's sign-fee schedule page (the fee AMOUNT was not read and is unconfirmed in-file), and the Law and Equity Act text via BC Laws search content. No other fact gaps resulted.
