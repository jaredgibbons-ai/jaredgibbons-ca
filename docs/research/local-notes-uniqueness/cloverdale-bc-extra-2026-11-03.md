# Cloverdale Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: cloverdale-bc (Cloverdale, Surrey; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Cloverdale's town centre sits at the crossroads of Highway 10 and Highway 15 (Pacific Highway / 176 Street). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
- The July 22, 2024 plan expansion runs from 60 Avenue south to 64 Avenue north, between 175 / 175A Street west and 180 Street east, plus one lot at 18120 56 Avenue. https://engage.surrey.ca/cloverdale-town-centre-plan?tool=qanda
- The Cloverdale Fairgrounds are a separate Special Study Area, not inside the Town Centre Plan's scope. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The expansion area is established housing: its 145 properties have dwelling ages from 1923 to 2023, with 1988 the average year of construction, per the City's plan-update page. https://engage.surrey.ca/cloverdale-town-centre-plan?tool=qanda
- The plan area mixes commercial, industrial and residential neighbourhoods around a pedestrian-oriented core on 176 Street. https://www.surrey.ca/files/Cloverdale%20TCP%20Plan%20Summary%202019Apr12%20(for%20web).pdf
- Schools confirmed by address include Cloverdale Traditional at 17857 56 Avenue, A.J. McLellan Elementary at 16545 61 Avenue and Lord Tweedsmuir Secondary at 6151 180 Street. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- For Surrey-wide zoning, suites, small-scale multi-unit housing, coach houses and short-term rentals, see the west-newton note, where those rules are stated in full.
- The local rule is heritage form on 176 Street: the Town Centre Plan's urban design guidelines maintain 2-storey building heights on 176 Street inside the Heritage Character Area. https://www.surrey.ca/files/Cloverdale%20TCP%20Plan%20Summary%202019Apr12%20(for%20web).pdf
- The Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) edges the community's lowland side; for ALR parcels the Agricultural Land Commission (ALC) frame is one principal residence of at most 500 square metres plus at most one additional residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- The Town Centre Plan update includes a review of sensitive ecosystems and riparian areas, which can reclassify land near creeks. https://engage.surrey.ca/cloverdale-town-centre-plan?tool=qanda

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Why is the Town Centre Plan being updated again? **A:** A new hospital changed the plan's assumptions. Council endorsed an update on October 30, 2023 to support the planned new hospital and British Columbia Cancer Centre, then expanded the boundary northward on July 22, 2024. The update reconsiders land uses, transportation routes and parks around that medical growth. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
**Q:** Where is the new hospital going? **A:** The new Surrey hospital and British Columbia Cancer Centre will be built in Cloverdale beside the Kwantlen Polytechnic University campus at 5500 180 Street. Fraser Health states the cancer centre will be British Columbia's seventh regional cancer centre, and the City's plan page refers to the groundbreaking of the Cloverdale hospital as a driver of the plan update. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2020/Aug/new-surrey-hospital-to-include-bc-cancer-centre
**Q:** Is the Fairgrounds part of the town centre plan? **A:** No, the Fairgrounds are designated a Special Study Area under their own City master planning and market feasibility study, with separate timelines and objectives. The Town Centre Plan considers the areas around the Fairgrounds, and may adjust if the Fairgrounds Master Plan is released, but the Fairgrounds themselves are out of its scope. https://engage.surrey.ca/cloverdale-town-centre-plan?tool=qanda
**Q:** Does Cloverdale have its own community centre and library? **A:** Yes, the Clayton Community Centre at 7155 187A Street integrates arts, recreation and a library in one building in Cloverdale. That address is east of the historic core in Clayton, so a buyer in the old town centre should not assume it is a walk from every Cloverdale street; check the specific address. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/cloverdale

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Downtown Cloverdale is described by the City as the historical centre of Surrey, with a heritage character area extended north along 176 Street to 58A Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/files/Cloverdale%20Town%20Centre%20Plan%20Survey%20Results%202019Apr25.pdf
- Existing surface parking lots on 176A Street were preserved in the plan after open-house input, a detail that affects core redevelopment parcels. https://www.surrey.ca/files/Cloverdale%20Town%20Centre%20Plan%20Survey%20Results%202019Apr25.pdf
- One lot northeast of the Kwantlen Polytechnic University Tech campus, at 18120 56 Avenue, was added to the plan expansion on its own, outside the main rectangle. https://engage.surrey.ca/cloverdale-town-centre-plan?tool=qanda
