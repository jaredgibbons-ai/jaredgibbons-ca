## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The band is a price filter, not a neighbourhood: it applies across the whole City of Maple Ridge, whose named neighbourhoods run from Port Hammond and Port Haney in the southwest to Webster's Corners, Whonnock and Ruskin in the east. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The City describes Silver Valley, on the north side of the Alouette River, as a gateway to recreation areas, and Albion/Kanaka by the Albion Fairgrounds and Albion Sports Complex. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Maple Ridge is in Metro Vancouver and in School District 42 (SD42), and its hospital, Ridge Meadows Hospital, is at 11666 Laity Street. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The band's urban detached stock sits mainly on RS-1 (Single Detached Residential) lots: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019, section 605, sets a subdivision minimum of 668.0 square metres lot area, 18.0 metres width and 27.0 metres depth, with 40% maximum lot coverage. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- The band also reaches the Town Centre, where the Town Centre Area Plan supports higher densities in a transit-oriented core anchored by the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- Rural-edge homes in Webster's Corners, Thornhill and Whonnock sit under the City's rural zones rather than RS-1, so lot rules differ by address, not by price band. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- One secondary suite is allowed per lot, inside the single detached building, at most 40% of gross floor area, and not within the floodplain area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- Eligible single-family and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) bylaws, depending on lot and transit criteria the City states. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024 covers the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station transit-oriented areas (TOAs), which changes what can be built near those two hubs. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** Does the band mean one kind of home? **A:** No, the band is only a price ceiling, so it spans freehold detached homes on RS-1 lots in the urban area, denser Town Centre homes near the Haney Transit Exchange, and rural-zone homes in Webster's Corners, Thornhill and Whonnock. What a buyer gets for the band depends on the neighbourhood and zone, which is why the zone check comes before the price check. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
**Q:** How do school catchments work in Maple Ridge? **A:** Catchments are address-based in SD42, which has no middle tier: elementary schools run Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary schools run Grades 8 to 12. One recorded district locator run, for 11897 227 Street, returned Eric Langton Elementary and Thomas Haney Secondary; that result is for that address only, and every other address needs its own locator run. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
**Q:** Can a home in this band have a legal suite? **A:** Yes, if the lot qualifies: the City allows one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, at most 40% of the gross floor area. The exception is floodplain land, where suites are not permitted, so a suite advertised on a Fraser River floodplain lot needs a permit check before a buyer relies on the income. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
**Q:** What extra charges apply if I build or subdivide in this band? **A:** Development Cost Charges (DCCs) apply to new development, not to a resale purchase. The City adopted an updated DCC Bylaw on September 24, 2024, its first rate revision since 2017, to fund roads, sewers, drainage, water and parks for growth, and notes changes to the DCC instalments regulation starting January 1, 2026. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/construction-development-permits/development-cost-charges

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check Fraser River floodplain lots first: the flood construction level is 6.7 metres geodetic including freeboard, a covenant on title identifies floodplain construction, no habitable floor space may sit below that level, and Zoning Bylaw section 402.8(h) prohibits suites on floodplain properties. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- Check escarpment lots second: lots within 300 metres of the Fraser River Escarpment crest, whose policy area's east boundary is 224 Street, require a geotechnical report attached to a Section 219 covenant before a building permit. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- Check rural-edge lots for Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status on Agricultural Land Commission mapping; status is parcel-specific. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
