## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The conversion history is the rule that shapes diligence at 1170 Lansdowne Drive: because the complex began as rental housing and was converted to strata under the 1980 application, its strata documents and building systems history read differently from a purpose-built strata of the same era. http://searcharchives.coquitlam.ca/uploads/r/city-of-coquitlam-archives/9/2/8/928c605cc00a7d36fe6b1e528d6f6c09799dadfb8f8f89fdddbeee2c24bb9842/Subdivision_Committee_-_1980.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What attached stock does Eagle Ridge actually have? **A:** Two City-documented complexes on Lansdowne Drive. Eagle Ridge Court at 1170 Lansdowne Drive is a strata conversion approved through the City's Subdivision Committee process in 1980, applied for by Cressey Development Corporation, and the townhouse project at 1135 Lansdowne Drive is a 16-unit project the City's Design Committee reviewed in 1988. That is the verified attached roster for this page; anything else marketed as Eagle Ridge attached should be checked against its sub-area field. http://searcharchives.coquitlam.ca/uploads/r/city-of-coquitlam-archives/9/2/8/928c605cc00a7d36fe6b1e528d6f6c09799dadfb8f8f89fdddbeee2c24bb9842/Subdivision_Committee_-_1980.pdf
- **Q:** Why does Eagle Ridge Court's paperwork mention tenants? **A:** Because it started as a rental building. The July 3, 1980 Subdivision Committee minutes record the strata title conversion application for 1170 Lansdowne Street, the undertaking that existing tenants would not be ejected to ease sale of the units, and a recommended waiver of the 3.5% vacancy guideline. A buyer should expect conversion-era disclosure in the strata documents and should read the depreciation report with that history in mind. http://searcharchives.coquitlam.ca/uploads/r/city-of-coquitlam-archives/9/2/8/928c605cc00a7d36fe6b1e528d6f6c09799dadfb8f8f89fdddbeee2c24bb9842/Subdivision_Committee_-_1980.pdf
- **Q:** Is Eagle Ridge Hospital in Coquitlam? **A:** No — it is in Port Moody. Fraser Health lists Eagle Ridge Hospital at 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody, which is the hospital at the west end of this Coquitlam neighbourhood's main street. For a Lansdowne Drive townhouse buyer, the practical point is that the closest hospital named by the health authority sits across the municipal border, not inside Coquitlam. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q:** Which schools are beside these complexes? **A:** Three schools on one corridor. School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) (SD43) listings name Eagle Ridge Elementary at 1215 Falcon Drive, Scott Creek Middle at 1240 Lansdowne Drive and Gleneagle Secondary at 1195 Lansdowne Drive, all by Eagle Ridge Park. Catchment is still by permanent home address in the SD43 School Locator — a 1135 or 1170 Lansdowne address must be run individually, not assumed from the walk. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Eagle Ridge Park, entrance at 2635 Runnel Drive and fronting Lansdowne Drive at the schools, holds natural grass soccer and rugby fields, baseball and softball diamonds, a lacrosse box with four pickleball courts, four lit tennis courts, a playground, trails and washrooms — the recreation base directly in front of the Lansdowne complexes. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Eagle-Ridge-Park-36
- Eagle Ridge Outdoor Pool is a subfacility of that park: an eight-lane, 25-metre outdoor pool with 1-metre and 3-metre diving boards, with a listed 2026 season of May 30 to September 7 — seasonal, not year-round. https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/-91
- The Coquitlam Crunch Trail starts at Eagle Ridge Park and climbs 2.2 kilometres with 894 steps and 242 metres of elevation gain to Westwood Plateau, so the Lansdowne townhouses sit at the trail's bottom access point. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/coquitlamcrunchtrail-27
- Scott Creek's drainage work touches this corridor directly: the City's instream-works list names Scott Creek at Lansdowne Drive and Scott Creek at Eagleridge Drive as box-culvert clean-and-inspect sites. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/14428/25-026-RFP---Field-Monitoring-of-Instream-Works?bidId=
- The Scott Creek Community Garden sits between Scott Creek Middle and Eagle Ridge Elementary, on the same campus frontage the complexes face. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Eagle-Ridge-Park-36
