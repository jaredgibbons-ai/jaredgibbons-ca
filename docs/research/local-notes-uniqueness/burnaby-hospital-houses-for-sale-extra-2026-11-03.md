# Burnaby Hospital Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: burnaby-hospital-houses-for-sale (Burnaby Hospital area; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The Burnaby Hospital house area is the residential streets around the hospital campus in central Burnaby. Burnaby Hospital sits at 3935 Kincaid Street, south of Canada Way and west of Deer Lake, and Fraser Health describes it as one of the largest hospitals outside the City of Vancouver. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- Canada Way runs along the north edge and Kincaid Street serves the campus itself. Deer Lake Parkway and Sperling Avenue link the east side to Deer Lake and to Burnaby City Hall at 4949 Canada Way. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2025/Jul/Burnaby-Hospital-redevelopment-project-Construction-notice-July-23
- Cascade Heights and Forest Glen sit to the south and west, and Sperling-Duthie and Deer Lake sit to the east. Deer Lake Park at 6450 Deer Lake Avenue is immediately east of the house streets. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached houses on the streets around the campus are the stock this page covers. The dominant building is not housing at all: the hospital's new six-storey health care pavilion with 83 beds, mostly single-patient rooms, opened in March 2026 beside an expanded support facilities building. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2026/Sep/Moving-forward-on-renewed-plan-to-redevelop-Burnaby-Hospital
- Wesburn Community Centre at 4781 Parkwood Avenue serves the south side of the area. The City lists a meeting and activity room for 100 people in Wesburn Park, with a playground, spray park, baseball diamond and basketball court in the park. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/wesburn-community-centre

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- House lots here follow the same citywide R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) rules as the rest of Burnaby — see the montecito-burnaby-houses-for-sale note for the full unit counts. The houses-specific point here is location: lots on the Canada Way edge sit closest to the hospital employment centre, so confirm the zone and the frequent-transit test for the exact lot before assuming a higher unit count. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- The Cascade Heights Community Plan covers the neighbourhood at this area's south side. The City adopted that plan in March 2025, and the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) adopted December 9, 2025 takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a house near Burnaby Hospital? **A:** It depends on the exact address, because catchments are address-based. A School District 41 (SD41) locator run for 4861 Canada Way returned Douglas Road Elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 7) and Burnaby Central Secondary (Grades 8 to 12). Cascade Heights Elementary at 4343 Smith Avenue sits at the south side, so a street one block south can fall in a different elementary catchment. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** Is there a SkyTrain station in this area? **A:** No station sits inside the Burnaby Hospital house area. Buses serve Kincaid Street at the campus, and Fraser Health directs riders to TransLink for bus information during hospital construction. The nearest Millennium Line station named in the research is Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station, north-east across the Deer Lake side, and its walking distance is address-specific and UNCONFIRMED. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2025/Jul/Burnaby-Hospital-redevelopment-project-Construction-notice-July-23
- **Q:** Will hospital construction affect my street? **A:** It can, on and near Kincaid Street, while the redevelopment continues. Fraser Health's July 2025 construction notice covered paving and repainting on Kincaid Street at the campus, with single-lane alternating traffic and a temporarily relocated eastbound bus stop on Kincaid at MacDonald Avenue. Ask for current construction notices for the specific street before completing. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2025/Jul/Burnaby-Hospital-redevelopment-project-Construction-notice-July-23
- **Q:** What is Deer Lake Park to a house buyer here? **A:** Deer Lake Park is the cultural centre at this area's east door. The City describes walking trails connecting the lake with the Burnaby Art Gallery, Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, Burnaby Village Museum and Hart House restaurant. One boardwalk section by the boat launch is closed from September 21, 2026 for replacement, with completion scheduled by winter 2027. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Burnaby Hospital is a University of British Columbia (UBC)-affiliated teaching hospital, not only a community hospital. Fraser Health lists general surgery, medicine, perinatal care, a neonatal intensive care unit, critical care and emergency services, serving Burnaby and East Vancouver. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- The library branch for this quadrant is the Bob Prittie Metrotown Branch, south-west at Metrotown. Burnaby Public Library runs four branches, one in each quadrant of the city. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/city-departments/burnaby-public-library
- Burnaby City Hall is at 4949 Canada Way, just east of the house area at Deer Lake. That puts permit and tax counters a short drive east on Canada Way rather than at Metrotown. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-09/Notice-of-Election-by-Voting-2026-10-01.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Citywide Burnaby tax and utility figures are carried in full in the montecito-burnaby-houses-for-sale note — see that note. The change that matters most to a house buyer here starts in 2027: the sewer parcel tax moves from the property tax notice to the utility notice, so a buyer comparing a 2026 tax notice with a 2027 utility notice will see the same charge in a new place. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- Utility notices are payable by the annual March due date to receive the discounted amount, and unpaid utility fees transfer to property taxes as arrears after December 31. The City states partial payments do not receive the discount. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- There is no SkyTrain station inside this house area, so buses are the transit service on its own streets. Fraser Health confirms buses operate on Kincaid Street at the campus and directs riders to TransLink during construction. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2025/Jul/Burnaby-Hospital-redevelopment-project-Construction-notice-July-23
- Canada Way is the main east–west road on the north edge. Kincaid Street serves the hospital, and Deer Lake Parkway and Sperling Avenue link east to Deer Lake, City Hall and the Burnaby Lake side. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2025/Jul/Burnaby-Hospital-redevelopment-project-Construction-notice-July-23
