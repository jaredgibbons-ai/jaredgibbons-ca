# The Crest Burnaby Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: the-crest-burnaby-houses-for-sale (The Crest, Burnaby; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The Crest is a house neighbourhood in southeast Burnaby around 10th Avenue, 6th Street and Edmonds Street, at the New Westminster border. The name matches The Crest Centre, the neighbourhood shopping plaza in the 10th Avenue and Canada Way area — the City does not publish a street-by-street boundary for the name. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Edmonds, a City-designated town centre on Kingsway, is the larger centre west of The Crest. The City lists Edmonds among Burnaby's four town centres, and the Edmonds Community Plan area sits west of the Crest house streets. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Griffiths Drive and 18th Avenue form the southeast edge toward Byrne Creek Ravine Park. TransLink route 106 runs through the area via 6th Street, Edmonds Street, Griffiths Drive and 18th Avenue. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt106.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached houses on side streets are the core stock in The Crest. The Province's School District 41 (SD41) list and the district's own pages place two elementary schools inside the house area, Second Street Community and Morley, which is the pattern of an established single-family district rather than a tower district. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- Houses here are freehold lots under the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District. Burnaby rezoned its house districts into R1 in June 2024, and the district's permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- Low-rise strata buildings sit near The Crest Centre and along 10th Avenue, outside this page's house stock. No condo building or townhome complex name in The Crest was confirmed on a municipal or strata source in this pass — see NOT CONFIRMED.
- Multiplex infill is the change coming to these lots. Lots in the R1 district with two or more primary dwelling units may be stratified and sold individually, while secondary suites stay on the primary unit's title. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A Crest house lot allows 3 to 4 homes, and 6 only near frequent transit. Under the R1 SSMUH District, lots up to 280 square metres allow 3 units, larger lots allow 4, and lots of at least 281 square metres within 400 metres of a frequent-transit stop allow 6 — see the vancouver-heights-burnaby-houses-for-sale note for the city-wide position. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- A subdividable SSMUH lot needs at least 10 metres of width and 281 square metres of area. Fee-simple rowhouse lots are the exception, at 5 to 8 metres of width depending on lot type. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- A lot within 30 metres of Byrne Creek or another stream, ravine or wetland needs a streamside assessment. The City uses that assessment to set the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area (SPEA) where new building and land disturbance are kept out. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/development-permits-construction/new-home-construction
- The Edmonds Community Plan, adopted by Council in March 2025, governs the town centre west of The Crest, and the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) takes precedence over older plan maps where they conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school does a Crest address feed into? **A:** Check the exact address, because two elementary schools sit in the area. Second Street Community Elementary is at 7502 2nd Street and Morley Elementary is at 7355 Morley Street, both Kindergarten to Grade 7 in SD41. Catchments are address-based through the SD41 School Locator, and the nearest secondary on the provincial list is Byrne Creek Community Secondary at 7777 18th Street. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** Is there a SkyTrain station in The Crest? **A:** No, there is no station inside The Crest. Edmonds Station on the Expo Line, west of the area, is the SkyTrain link, and TransLink route 106 runs through the Crest streets via 6th Street, Edmonds Street, Griffiths Drive and 18th Avenue to connect the houses to Edmonds and New Westminster stations. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt106.pdf
- **Q:** Can I add a suite or split my house lot into a multiplex? **A:** Yes, under the R1 SSMUH District. A standard lot allows 3 to 4 homes including a secondary suite in the count, lots of at least 281 square metres near frequent transit allow 6, and a lot with two or more primary units can be stratified. A suite itself cannot be stratified off its primary unit. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q:** What recreation is inside the neighbourhood? **A:** The Rosemary Brown Recreation Centre at 7789 18th Street is the in-area facility, a two-rink centre on the southeast edge of The Crest. Robert Burnaby Park, north of the area, is described by the City as urban forest with over half its area as critical wildlife habitat. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/rosemary-brown-recreation-centre
- **Q:** Does Byrne Creek affect a house on Griffiths Drive or 18th Avenue? **A:** It can on lots close to the ravine. A property within 30 metres of a stream, ravine or wetland requires an assessment that identifies the protected streamside area before new building, so a ravine-edge lot can have a smaller buildable area than its lot size suggests. Confirm the lot's status with the City before designing an addition. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/development-permits-construction/new-home-construction

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Second Street is a community school, not just a catchment school. SD41 names it Second Street Community Elementary at 7502 2nd Street, and the City's Southeast Community children's programs run at both Second Street and Morley Elementary, 7355 Morley Street. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- The Tommy Douglas Branch of Burnaby Public Library, at 7311 Kingsway in Edmonds, is the library branch serving this quadrant of the city. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/city-departments/burnaby-public-library
- The Edmonds Urgent and Primary Care Centre (UPCC) is at #201-7315 Edmonds Street, west of The Crest, and Burnaby Hospital is at 3935 Kincaid Street. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- SD41 has no middle-school tier anywhere in Burnaby. Elementary schools run Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary schools run Grade 8 to Grade 12, so a Crest child moves straight from Second Street or Morley to secondary. https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/
- A rented suite in a Crest house is billed separately for utilities. The City's 2026 table charges a rented suite in a single-family dwelling $326.17 for water and $403.00 for sewer use. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 106 is the through bus for The Crest. TransLink's timetable runs route 106, Edmonds Station to New Westminster Station, via 6th Street, Edmonds Street, Griffiths Drive and 18th Avenue. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt106.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the Byrne Creek ravine edge on BurnabyMap before valuing a lot on Griffiths Drive or 18th Avenue. The Burnaby 2050 OCP's Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area guidelines govern how the protected area is measured on the parcel. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-06/Streamside-Protection-and-Enhancement-Development-Permit-Area-Guidelines.pdf
- Check title charges through the Land Title and Survey Authority of British Columbia (LTSA) before a rebuild. The City's new-home page notes that easements and rights-of-way can cross a lot and limit where a new house or multiplex can sit. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/development-permits-construction/new-home-construction
- Check protected trees before assuming a lot is clearable. The City grants tree-cutting permits only against its criteria, and replacement trees for trees removed for development must be shown on the site plan. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/trees/tree-removal-and-replacement
