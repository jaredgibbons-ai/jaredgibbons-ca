## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The attached stock in Westridge is townhome complexes on the Cliff Avenue, Duncan Avenue and Barnet Highway slope streets, in freehold strata form, with detached houses making up most of the remaining streets. Strata homes in British Columbia are strata lots under the Strata Property Act, each with a share of the complex's common property. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- No named Westridge townhome complex with a street address and year built was confirmed on a municipal or strata source in this pass. Complex names and years on listing sites were not used — see NOT CONFIRMED.
- Newer attached homes here can also be R1 rowhouses on former house lots. Burnaby's R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District permits rowhouses and townhouses on house lots, including fee-simple rowhouse lots of 5 to 8 metres width that are not strata lots. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A Westridge lot near a mountain creek can need a streamside assessment before a strata complex adds or rebuilds. The City requires an assessment for a property within 30 metres of a stream, ravine or wetland to set the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Area (SPEA) kept clear of new building — see the vancouver-heights-burnaby-houses-for-sale note for the city-wide position. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/development-permits-construction/new-home-construction
- A suite inside a Westridge strata townhome cannot be stratified off. Burnaby's zoning rewrite states that secondary suites must remain on the same title as the primary unit, and that strata conversion of existing purpose-built rental housing is not permitted except for duplex or semi-detached units. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- Tree removal on a sloped Westridge complex lot needs a City permit decision. The City assesses protected-tree removal against its Tree Bylaw criteria, and replacement trees must be shown on the site plan where trees are removed for development. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/trees/tree-removal-and-replacement

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves a Westridge townhome? **A:** Westridge Elementary is the in-area school, at 510 Duncan Avenue, Kindergarten to Grade 7 in SD41. The district groups Westridge Elementary with Burnaby North Secondary, at 751 Hammarskjold Drive, in its Brentwood North zone, but catchments are address-based, so run the complex's exact address in the SD41 School Locator before relying on the assignment. https://westridge.burnabyschools.ca/
- **Q:** What sits below the Barnet Highway townhomes? **A:** Barnet Marine Park and the Westridge Marine Terminal both sit on the Burrard Inlet shore below Westridge. The City park is at 8181 Barnet Road, and Trans Mountain, the terminal operator, places the Westridge Marine Terminal at 7065 Bayview Drive, where tankers load at the end of its pipeline system. https://www.transmountain.com/westridge-marine-terminal
- **Q:** Is there SkyTrain in Westridge? **A:** No, there is no SkyTrain station in Westridge. The nearest stations are on the Millennium Line south of the slope, including Sperling–Burnaby Lake and Holdom, and TransLink buses on Barnet Highway and Hastings Street connect the Cliff Avenue and Duncan Avenue complexes to those stations and to downtown Vancouver. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- **Q:** What does a Westridge strata home pay the City for utilities? **A:** A multiple-family dwelling pays the City's 2026 rates of $374.54 for water and $436.00 for sewer use, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. That City utility notice is separate from the monthly strata fees the townhome complex collects for common property. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Q:** Can a Westridge strata restrict rentals or pets? **A:** Yes, if the complex's registered bylaws say so. Those rules come from each strata corporation under the Strata Property Act, not from a City bylaw, and no Westridge complex's bylaws were read in this pass. Get the current bylaws and the Form B Information Certificate for the specific Cliff Avenue or Duncan Avenue complex before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Westridge Park is at 320 Cliff Avenue, on the same street as the slope's townhome complexes. The City lists Westridge Park among its parks, separate from the waterfront Barnet Marine Park below Barnet Highway. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- The Westridge Marine Terminal at 7065 Bayview Drive is an operating oil terminal, not a marina for residents. Trans Mountain's own page describes the terminal as its marine loading facility in Burnaby, at the shore below the Westridge streets. https://www.transmountain.com/westridge-marine-terminal
- Barnet Marine Park parking and beach access are on Barnet Road, not through the townhome streets. The City's park page places the park at 8181 Barnet Road, reached from Barnet Highway east of the Westridge slope. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/barnet-marine-park
- SD41 has no middle-school tier for Westridge children. Elementary schools run Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary schools run Grade 8 to Grade 12, so a Westridge Elementary student moves directly to secondary. https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Barnet Highway is the through road along the bottom of the Westridge slope, linking the townhome streets east toward Port Moody and west toward Hastings Street and downtown Vancouver. Barnet Marine Park, at 8181 Barnet Road, sits on that corridor. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/barnet-marine-park
- Hastings Street is the southern through road for Westridge, with the Brentwood Town Centre town centre west along Lougheed Highway. The City counts Brentwood among its four town centres, and its SkyTrain stations are the rapid-transit option west of Westridge. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Route 136 shows the bus pattern south of the mountain: TransLink runs it from Lougheed Station via Production Way, Forest Grove Drive, Broadway and Halifax Street to Brentwood Station, serving the streets below the Westridge slope. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt136.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the streamside area on any Westridge complex backing onto a ravine or creek. The Burnaby 2050 OCP's Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area guidelines govern how the protected area is measured on the parcel, which can limit additions and common-property work. http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-06/Streamside-Protection-and-Enhancement-Development-Permit-Area-Guidelines.pdf
- Check title and slope constraints before a strata complex plans retaining-wall or drainage work. The City's new-home page directs owners to the Land Title and Survey Authority of British Columbia (LTSA) for easements and rights-of-way, and BurnabyMap flags heritage and zoning constraints by address. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/development-permits-construction/new-home-construction
- Read the strata's bylaws and depreciation material, not the listing, for rental and pet rules. The Province's strata framework places those rules in each corporation's registered bylaws, with many disputes resolved by the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
