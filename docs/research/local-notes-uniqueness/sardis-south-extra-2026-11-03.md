# Sardis South — Extra Local Notes
Page: sardis-south (Sardis South; neighbourhood page).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sardis South is the southern part of Sardis around Tyson Road, South Sumas Road and Watson Road, roughly south of Stevenson Road and north of the Vedder neighbourhood. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- The Sardis Park sub-area in the City's Sardis Neighbourhood Plan is the southeast quadrant of the Sardis neighbourhood, and Stevenson Road is that sub-area's most diverse street. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Sardis as a whole is centrally located in Chilliwack, immediately south of Highway 1, bordered by the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) to the west and east, First Nations lands to the south and east, and the Vedder neighbourhood to the south. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- No City boundary is published for the name Sardis South itself; the Tyson Road and Watson Road framing above is the orientation used in this program's research, not a City boundary. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The Sardis Park side is predominantly established single-detached housing, and the plan states that pattern directly: the sub-area is characterised by established single-detached residential uses, secondary suites and some limited apartment development. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Larger lots are a defining feature on this side because of past servicing limits: the plan states there are many larger lots in Sardis Park, reflective of the area's past servicing limitations which required many homes to be on septic systems. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Stevenson Road itself holds the mixed edge of the stock: the plan lists a gated community, apartment buildings, duplexes, new infill development and many single-detached homes on that one street. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Shared Sardis housing-stock facts are in full in the sardis-houses-for-sale note; this note adds only the south-side, street-level detail above. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The plan that covers this area is the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan, Schedule B5 of Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, adopted October 2020 and updated 2025. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387 https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Redevelopment pressure on the larger Sardis Park lots is a stated plan fact, not a rumour: the plan records pressure to convert these larger lots, particularly along Stevenson Road, into small-scale multi-unit housing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- A drinking-water development permit can apply on the south side as well, because Development Permit Area (DPA) 1 protects the Sardis-Vedder Aquifer and Vedder River Fan and lists septic field installation and new private wells among the activities requiring a permit inside the protection areas. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8128
- City-wide zoning, suite-size caps, short-term rental and small-scale multi-unit housing rules for Chilliwack are in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note — see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Why are lots bigger on the Sardis Park side? **A:** The larger lots reflect how the area was serviced when it was built. The City's Sardis plan states that many larger lots in Sardis Park exist because past servicing limitations required many homes to be on septic systems. A buyer should confirm whether a specific lot is now on City sewer or still on a septic system, because that affects suite and redevelopment options. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- **Q:** Which schools are physically on this side? **A:** Tyson Elementary at 45170 South Sumas Road and Watson Elementary are the elementary schools on the Tyson and Watson side in School District No. 33 (SD33) listings, with Vedder Middle School and Mount Slesse Middle School and Sardis Secondary at 45460 Stevenson Road also named for the wider Sardis set. Catchments are address-based, so run the exact address in the SD33 School Locator before relying on any one school. https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2026-09/Chilliwack%20School%20District%20Directory%202026-27.pdf https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q:** Is there a commercial centre inside Sardis South? **A:** Yes, at the east edge on Vedder Road. The Sardis plan names the Vedder Pointe commercial centre at the intersection of Vedder Road and Stevenson Road, with a variety of services for local residents. Homes deeper on Tyson Road, South Sumas Road and Watson Road are residential streets, so day-to-day shopping is at that Vedder and Stevenson corner or north toward Luckakuck Way. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- **Q:** Which bus serves Tyson Road? **A:** Route 58 Tyson is the Tyson Road route in the BC Transit Chilliwack Rider's Guide, listed as Cottonwood Mall to Tyson via Evans and Tyson roads in the guide's route summaries. Route 51 Vedder runs Vedder Road at the east edge of this area, and Route 54 Promontory also passes the Vedder Road side, so the east-edge homes have more route choice than the interior Tyson streets. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chw_rg_jan2024-V1.1.pdf

- The City's Sardis-Vedder parks list names Watson Glen Park as a community park and Manuel Park, South Sumas Park and Higginson Park among the smaller parks on this side. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=areaView&plID=165&backTo&areaID=6
- The Vedder Rotary Loop Trail and Peach Creek Rotary Trail are both listed in the City's Sardis-Vedder trails list and are reached from the south end of this area toward the Vedder River. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=areaView&plID=165&backTo&areaID=6

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 58 Tyson is the interior route for this side, and Routes 51 Vedder and 54 Promontory serve the Vedder Road edge; all three are listed in the BC Transit Chilliwack Rider's Guide. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/chw_rg_jan2024-V1.1.pdf
- Vedder Road is the primary transportation spine of the Sardis neighbourhood, providing the main north-south connection within and out of Sardis, per the Sardis plan. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- The BC Transit Route 54 map shows the Tyson and Watson timing point and the Sardis Secondary area on the Sardis routing, which is the map a Tyson-side buyer should read for the actual stop pattern. https://www.bctransit.com/agassiz-harrison/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2024/02/chw_map_54.pdf
- Southbound, Watson Road and Keith Wilson Road lead from this area toward Garrison Crossing and the Vedder River crossings; no source read for this note states a measured drive time, so none is claimed. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=areaView&plID=165&backTo&areaID=6

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check septic history on Sardis Park lots first: the plan's statement that many homes required septic systems means a buyer should obtain the servicing record for the specific lot and confirm whether a suite or added unit is supportable. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- Check the aquifer layer for well and septic work: the Sardis-Vedder Aquifer and Vedder River Fan protection areas are Development Permit Area 1 in Schedule D-1 of the OCP, read through the City's OCP page and webmap. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8128 https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
- Check creek-side lots at the south end against the OCP's Schedule D-3 Riparian Area development permit area, listed in the OCP's Schedule D contents. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
- City-wide Chilliwack floodplain and flood construction level checks are in full in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note — see the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2387
