## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is detached houses held for decades under a land use contract (LUC), which is why this pocket's rules story is a zoning-replacement story rather than a standard subdivision story: the City replaced the contract zoning parcel by parcel, with Cherrybrook Place addresses listed one by one in the draft Comprehensive Development zone. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-coq-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8316/9139/4004/0f60821c01f71f2185f312c8a6612536_Draft_Meadowbrook_Comprehensive_Development_28CD29_Zone.pdf
- The replacement zoning is now the governing zoning for these lots, ahead of the land use contract's June 30, 2024 end date. https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/854?arc=1086

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Land Use Contract replacement rezoning was initiated December 6, 2021, with new zoning required by June 30, 2022 ahead of the June 30, 2024 contract end, so a Meadowbrook house is bought under the replacement zone, not under the old contract. https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/854?arc=1086
- Coquitlam's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules allow up to 3 units on lots of 280 square metres or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 square metres and under 4,050 square metres, and the City states it currently has no bus stops meeting the provincial frequent-service criteria, so the 6-unit tier does not apply. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquitlam
- Coquitlam is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What is a land use contract and why does it matter here? **A:** Meadowbrook's houses sat under a land use contract, a legacy zoning contract the City had to replace before the contract's June 30, 2024 end: the City initiated the replacement rezoning on December 6, 2021, required new zoning by June 30, 2022, and completed the process by the provincial deadline, so buyers now check the replacement zone for what a lot allows. https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/854?arc=1086
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves Meadowbrook? **A:** Meadowbrook Elementary, at 900 Sharpe Street, is the addressed School District No. 43 (SD43) school anchor south of the Dewdney Trunk Road boundary; SD43 assigns catchments by address, so confirm the assigned school for a specific address in the district locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** How many units can a Meadowbrook house lot take under the small-scale rules? **A:** Up to 3 units on lots of 280 square metres or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 square metres and under 4,050 square metres, and because Coquitlam currently has no bus stops meeting the provincial frequent-service criteria, the provincial 6-unit tier near frequent transit does not apply in the city. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquitlam
- **Q:** When are Coquitlam property taxes due? **A:** Property tax payment was due July 2, 2026, covering January 1 through December 31, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5 percent penalty on any unpaid balance after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
