# Sullivan Station Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: sullivan-station-guide (Sullivan Station, Surrey; Guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Sullivan Station has no separate City plan boundary of its own. The City ground here is South Newton, a large neighbourhood in southeast Newton with commercial nodes at Panorama Heights (152 Street and Highway 10) and Sullivan Heights (64 Avenue and 144 Street). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- The South Newton plan area covers about 485 hectares, generally north of Highway 10, east of King George Boulevard and west of 152 Street, bordered by the East Newton plans to the northwest. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- People say Sullivan Station, Sullivan or Sullivan Heights for this ground, while the City names are Sullivan, Sullivan Heights and South Newton. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Housing here mixes detached houses with townhouse and low-rise infill around the two nodes. The City describes South Newton as having a variety of housing types, and a 2004 plan amendment added small-lot single-family designations. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- A current infill shape is at 14188 62 Avenue, where a planning report proposes a 4-storey mixed-use building with 27 market rental homes and 513 square metres of ground-floor commercial space in South Newton. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7925-0147-00.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The South Newton plan no longer governs on its own. It was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP) update, development is led by the OCP, and Council endorsed updated land-use designations on December 2, 2024 in Corporate Report R246. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) sets the unit counts here: up to 3 units on lots under 280 square metres, 4 units on larger lots, and up to 6 on eligible lots of at least 281 square metres within 400 metres of a frequent bus stop — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note for Surrey-wide zoning detail. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Fish-bearing creeks cross this ground, so creek lots need a setback check. South Newton holds the headwaters of Archibald Creek, Sullivan Heights Creek and tributaries of Hyland Creek. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves Sullivan Station? **A:** Sullivan Heights Secondary at 6248 144 Street is the secondary school in this ground. School District 36 (Surrey) lists it among its Surrey schools, but assignment is by home address through the district locator, so confirm the exact address before relying on this school for an offer. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q:** Is there a new elementary school here? **A:** Yes, a new elementary school opened in January 2026. École Snokomish Elementary at 14778 58B Avenue is a 27-classroom school for up to 655 Kindergarten to Grade 7 students, drawing from the Cambridge, Goldstone Park, Sullivan and Woodward Hill areas and taking over Woodward Hill's French Immersion program, so nearby catchments changed at opening. https://www.surreyschools.ca/-it-s-a-beautiful--beautiful-school----cole-snokomish-elementary-opens-for-students-in-sullivan.197621
- **Q:** Can I add units to a detached lot here? **A:** Often yes, within the small-scale limits. Surrey allows up to 4 units on a lot over 280 square metres and up to 6 on an eligible lot near a frequent bus stop, and the City states these changes do not retroactively legalize a suite or coach house built without permits, so check permits on any existing suite. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q:** Does Sullivan Station have SkyTrain? **A:** No, there is no SkyTrain station in Sullivan Station. Service is by bus on the 152 Street and 64 Avenue corridors, with the R1 RapidBus on King George Boulevard west of the area linking Newton Exchange and King George Station, so a commute plan here starts with a bus leg. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Catchments moved in January 2026, so last year's school answer may now be wrong. Snokomish Elementary opened that month for students from four existing school areas, which is why the locator, not the nearest school building, decides placement. https://www.surreyschools.ca/-it-s-a-beautiful--beautiful-school----cole-snokomish-elementary-opens-for-students-in-sullivan.197621
- Six School District 36 schools sit in or serve this ground: Sullivan Elementary (6016 152 Street), Cambridge Elementary (6115 150 Street), Goldstone Park Elementary (6287 146 Street), Woodward Hill Elementary (6082 142 Street), Snokomish Elementary (14778 58B Avenue) and Sullivan Heights Secondary (6248 144 Street). https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- Sullivan Heights Secondary holds about 1,550 students in Grades 8 to 12 and has the Bell Performing Arts Centre on site. https://www.surreyschools.ca/sullivanheights/our-school
- The 64 Avenue and 144 Street node is the walk-to node for the west side, while Panorama Heights at 152 Street and Highway 10 serves the east side — the two nodes split daily shopping for the area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- Creek headwaters run through the plan area, so a lot that looks like a normal rear yard can carry riparian limits on additions or a rebuild. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey's 2026 total residential rate is $3.42731 per $1,000 of assessed value — see the surrey-houses-for-sale note for the full Surrey breakdown. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 342 is the through route for this ground. TransLink's timetable runs it from Newton Exchange via 72 Avenue, 144 Street, 60 Avenue, 152 Street and Highway 10 to Langley Centre. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt342.pdf
- Route 345 runs on 152 Street between King George Station and White Rock Centre via Fraser Highway and Highway 10, giving the east edge a direct link to SkyTrain at King George Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt345.pdf
- Highway 10 is the south driving edge and Highway 15 (176 Street) is the next corridor east toward Cloverdale and the border — see the cloverdale-neighbourhood-guides note for that corridor. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check creek setbacks first on any lot touching Archibald Creek, Sullivan Heights Creek or a Hyland Creek tributary, using City mapping for the specific lot — the plan page names the creeks but does not set a lot's buildable area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- Confirm Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status lot by lot south of Highway 10, where the Serpentine lowlands begin — no ALR or floodplain designation inside Sullivan Station itself was confirmed on a City page read for this note. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
