# The Heights Houses — Extra Local Notes

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- The Official Community Plan (OCP) names it "Massey Victory Heights," residential neighbourhood 8 of 9 on the mainland; buyers shorten it to "The Heights." https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/OUR_CITY_2041_Official_Community_Plan_New_Westminsters_Residential_Neighbourhoods_Map.pdf
- It runs from East Tenth Avenue (the Burnaby border) south to East Eighth Avenue, east of McBride Boulevard — the line the City's Sapperton / Massey-Victory Heights Transportation Plan uses for this part of the city. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Sapperton_Massey_Victory_Heights_Transportation_plan___Summary_Report___V01b_for_web.pdf
- Neighbours: McBride Sapperton (OCP neighbourhood 9) east, Glenbrooke North west, Queen's Park south-west. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/OUR_CITY_2041_Official_Community_Plan_New_Westminsters_Residential_Neighbourhoods_Map.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses sit in the OCP designation "(RD) Residential – Detached and Semi-Detached Housing": detached houses and duplexes, plus a secondary suite and a laneway or carriage house per lot. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/CNW_Interactive_Land_Use_Map___(Consolidated_September_2026).pdf
- Base zoning is the Single Detached Residential District (RS-1): single detached dwellings, secondary suites, and laneway or carriage houses. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/300_One_and_Two_Unit_Residential___As_of_June_8_2026.pdf
- Character is guarded: the City's heritage consultant confirmed the decorative street lights have heritage value — five originals on Churchill Avenue will be refurbished — and the neighbourhood has its own City Historic Neighbourhood Context Statement. https://www.newwestcity.ca/planning-building-and-development/projects-on-the-go/articles/8271.php?status=all https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php
- Curving Mott Crescent and Courtney Crescent anchor the west; York Street, Massey Street, William Street and Churchill Avenue run east. https://www.newwestcity.ca/planning-building-and-development/projects-on-the-go/articles/8271.php?status=all

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- June 2026 Infill Housing Program: up to six units (suites, laneway and carriage houses, triplexes, quadplexes) on single detached and duplex lots across most of the city, mapped RGO-A (up to four units) or RGO-B (up to six), no site-specific rezoning. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects
- The Province's small-scale multi-unit housing law needed no broad rezoning here: most house lots already allowed a house plus suite and laneway house. About 160 duplex lots were rezoned to RD-1 by Bylaw No. 8453, 2024, to add rental secondary suites. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Attachment_C2___Small_Scale_Multi_Unit_Housing_Legislation_and_Implementation_Summary.pdf
- The Queen's Park Heritage Conservation Area — the city's only one — does not cover The Heights, but a Heritage Alteration Permit can still apply to a house protected by its own designation, Heritage Revitalization Agreement or covenant; check the Heritage Protection Map. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-protection-and-development/sb_expander_articles/582.php https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-protection-and-development/sb_expander_articles/1626.php

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I have both a basement suite and a laneway house? **A:** Yes — that pairing is the pattern the City planned for. The Residential – Detached and Semi-Detached designation lets a principal unit include a secondary suite and a detached house add a laneway or carriage house, and RS-1 zoning allows all three on one lot. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/CNW_Interactive_Land_Use_Map___(Consolidated_September_2026).pdf
- **Q:** Is The Heights inside the Queen's Park Heritage Conservation Area? **A:** No. New Westminster has exactly one Heritage Conservation Area, covering Queen's Park only, so Heights houses escape its Protected and Non-protected classes and design guidelines. A permit applies only where the individual property carries a designation, Heritage Revitalization Agreement or covenant. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-protection-and-development/sb_expander_articles/582.php
- **Q:** Which school will my child actually get into? **A:** Placement is by catchment, and catchment is not a guarantee. School District 40 places students where space is available by catchment, runs a lottery when in-catchment demand far exceeds room, and sends overflow students to the next available school. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- **Q:** Will the City be digging up my street? **A:** On the named streets, yes, in sequence. The City is replacing the end-of-life street lighting: conduit and bases first, old lights kept on until fixtures arrive, Zone 1 (Mott Crescent, Courtney Crescent) first, then Zone 2 (York Street, Massey Street, William Street, Churchill Avenue). https://www.newwestcity.ca/planning-building-and-development/projects-on-the-go/articles/8271.php?status=all

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Total 2026 residential (Class 1) property tax rate: $4.79312 per $1,000 of assessed value, per the City's 2026 mill rate schedule. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/26_CNW_Mill_Rates_.pdf
- Utilities are a separate annual flat-rate bill: a single family dwelling pays $2,753.73 net for 2026 after the 5% discount (water $881.20, sewer $1,364.21, solid waste $508.32); a suite raises it to $4,143.46 net. Mailed late February; discount needs payment by May 4, 2026. https://www.newwestcity.ca/propertytaxes-utilities/sb_expander_articles/1271.php
- 2026 Home Owner Grant: up to $570 basic, $845 at 65-plus, threshold $2,075,000, claimed with the Province. https://www.newwestcity.ca/propertytaxes-utilities/sb_expander_articles/713.php
- The City of New Westminster is a listed Speculation and Vacancy Tax area (Metro Vancouver Regional District), so owners file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Lots near the Brunette River / Hume Park edge: check Development Permit Area 7.1 (Brunette River), whose guidelines lead with geotechnical hazard mitigation for steep bank slopes. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/OCP_DPA_7.1_Brunette_River_(Consolidated_April_2023).pdf
- The Sapperton Reach, from Hume Park's east boundary to the Braid Station SkyTrain overpass, has a south-bank embankment steeper than 30 percent — a corridor-lot issue, not an interior-street one. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Brunette_River_Master_Plan_Sapperton_Reach_.pdf
- Heritage designation and Heritage Revitalization Agreements supersede Transit Oriented Development Area height and density entitlements — check the Heritage Protection Map before pricing a rebuild. https://www.newwestcity.ca/planning-building-and-development/new-westminsters-official-community-plan/provincial-housing-legislation-implementation/sb_expander_articles/7565.php
