# Capitol Hill — Questions for Jared
Area: Capitol Hill (Burnaby). First program page: capitol-hill-burnaby-houses-for-sale.

## Questions (voice memo)
1. What sells a Capitol Hill house — the downtown and inlet views, the Hastings Street shops, or the SFU commute?
2. How do buyers react to the refinery and pipeline infrastructure on the north slope — deal-breaker, discount, or non-issue?
3. What do you check on a Capitol Hill lot that you would not check in flat parts of Burnaby — slope, view protection, older retaining walls?
4. Who buys on Capitol Hill, and how fast do the view homes move compared with the non-view streets?
5. Tell me about one Capitol Hill deal that taught you something about the hill.

## Shot list
