Five questions only Jared can answer about Forest Hills (the Forest Grove Drive / Ash Grove Crescent / Beaverbrook Crescent pocket below Simon Fraser University). Not answered here — for a voice memo.

3. What sells fast there and what sits — end units, units backing onto Stoney Creek or the greenbelt, units on Forest Grove Drive itself?
4. What surprises buyers once they dig in — the co-operatives mixed in with the strata complexes, the Burnaby Terminal next to Wembley Estates, the gondola route over Forest Grove, or the depreciation reports on 1980s stock?
