# Surrey Duplexes — Extra Local Notes
Page: surrey-duplexes-for-sale (Surrey; duplexes).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- A Surrey duplex can sit in any of the city's six communities, because this page covers a housing form, not a neighbourhood; city boundaries are in the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- Duplex permissions follow two maps, not streets: about 72,000 eligible lots inside the Urban Containment Boundary carry Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) rights, and lots within 400 metres of a frequent bus stop, mapped in Schedule F of the zoning bylaw, carry higher counts. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r162-small-scale-multi-unit-housing-review-framework

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Duplexes are the small-scale form Surrey actually builds: City committee minutes record 133 SSMUH permits issued in 2025, of which 129 were duplexes and four were multiplexes of three units or more. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/council-minutes/development-approval-process-improvement-committee-minutes-january-20-2026
- A Surrey duplex may be side-by-side or up-and-down, and more than 4,600 Comprehensive Development (CD) zoned lots based on single-family or duplex zones also pick up the new permissions under Part 52 of the zoning bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A duplex no longer needs a duplex zone: the July 8, 2024 amendments to Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000 replaced the old single-family and duplex zones with new small-scale zones (R1 to R6 in the City's CD-zone table), and unit counts now turn on lot size. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- Six units is the ceiling, only near frequent transit: eligible lots of 281 to 4,050 square metres within 400 metres of a frequent bus stop may hold 6 units with no off-street parking required; about 13,000 lots, roughly 18 percent of SSMUH lots, qualify. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- A suite can sit inside a duplex, and servicing is sized for it: suites are permitted within a duplex, triplex or fourplex and must be registered, and the City's SSMUH framework sets one water service per property, 19 to 38 millimetres for duplexes and houseplexes. http://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/secondary-suites

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can I build a duplex without rezoning? **A:** Yes, a building permit alone is usually enough. The City's SSMUH review report states the amended zoning bylaw lets an owner obtain a building permit to construct duplexes, and houseplexes with garden suites or coach houses, up to four units, and a maximum of six units in frequent bus stop areas. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r162-small-scale-multi-unit-housing-review-framework
- **Q:** How is water billed for two households on one lot? **A:** Surrey bills the lot, not the unit. City committee minutes state one water meter per legal lot is required, leaving owners or strata managers to split the bill; the SSMUH framework adds that strata duplex billing is split equally among the strata units, up to four, on a flat rate. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r162-small-scale-multi-unit-housing-review-framework
- **Q:** What does the building permit cost? **A:** The fee follows the declared value of construction, not the home type. Under the City of Surrey Building Bylaw, 2012, No. 17850 schedule effective April 14, 2026, the non-refundable flat fee at application is $150 for work valued at $20,000 to $99,999 and $700 for $100,000 to $999,999, with the balance due before issuance. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/BuildingFeeSchedule.pdf
- **Q:** Do lowland duplex lots carry flood rules? **A:** Yes, the rules follow the land, not the building form. The City's lowlands page states the Nicomekl and Serpentine catchment is 334 square kilometres, of which 59 square kilometres is lowland floodplain, much of it very close to sea level behind dykes and sea dams, so a duplex lot there needs the flood check in section 9. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- A duplex owner can be paid to meter: the City's pilot offers the first 1,000 single-family or duplex homes switching by the end of 2026 a free meter and setup valued at $450 plus a $400 rebate, and a one-time credit if the first metered bill beats the old flat bill. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/utility-billing-services/voluntary-water-meter-program
- An unregistered suite inside a duplex costs the owner: the City bills $1,000 for each unregistered secondary suite it becomes aware of, and registration can be checked with its Secondary Suite Fee Web Inquiry tool. http://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/secondary-suites

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Development Cost Charges (DCCs) size a duplex like a townhouse: the City's report on Surrey Development Cost Charge Bylaw, 2025, No. 21598 caps DCCs for additional primary units such as a duplex or triplex at 1,750 square feet, consistent with townhouses, with no specific additional DCC for a secondary suite. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R173.pdf
- A suite inside a duplex adds a set yearly fee: the Secondary Suite Service Fee is $893 per suite under the Secondary Suite Service Fee By-law, 2011, No. 17356; Surrey's 2026 tax rate and utility totals are held in the surrey-houses-for-sale note. http://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/secondary-suites

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Frequent transit unlocks the six-unit duplex lot: the City identified its frequent bus stops with TransLink as stops providing 15-minute service, and a council report names the R6 Scott Road RapidBus as the kind of service enabling six units within 400 metres. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R146.pdf
- The wider RapidBus network duplex buyers use, including the R1 King George Boulevard RapidBus, is set out in the surrey-houses-for-sale note. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start a lowland duplex check with the floodplain: the City's coastal floodplain page states it covers about 20 percent of Surrey, stretching from Boundary Bay and Mud Bay along the Nicomekl and Serpentine Rivers towards Cloverdale and Newton, subject to coastal and river flooding, and mostly protected by dykes, with sea dams first built in 1912 and 1913. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain
- Confirm the lot is SSMUH-eligible before counting units: the zoning schedules exempt land not connected to a City or regional water or community sewer system, heritage-protected land, and land in zones with a minimum subdivision lot size of 4,050 square metres. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zoning_12000_Schedules_0.pdf
