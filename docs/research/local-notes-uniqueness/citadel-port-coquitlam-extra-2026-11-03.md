# Citadel Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: citadel-port-coquitlam (Citadel, Port Coquitlam; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Citadel is an established residential neighbourhood on the north side of Port Coquitlam, around Citadel Drive and Mary Hill Road, beside Castle Park on the Pitt River side. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- The City's Official Community Plan (OCP) calls this same neighbourhood Citadel Heights, while the board sub-area name is Citadel. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- No City neighbourhood map stating boundary roads for Citadel / Citadel Heights was found on a City page read on 2026-11-03. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Neighbours are Oxford Heights and Glenwood to the west and south, Riverwood to the east, Central Port Coquitlam to the south, and Pitt Meadows across the Pitt River. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- Coast Meridian Road and the Coast Meridian Overpass connect north Port Coquitlam to Lougheed Highway and the Mary Hill Bypass. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Citadel's detached stock sits under the RS1, RS2, RS3 and RS4 small-lot residential zones in Zoning Bylaw No. 3630. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2025-08/3630 - Zoning Bylaw 2008.pdf
- Minimum lot widths in those zones are at least 15 m (RS1), 12 m (RS2), 30 m (RS3) and 9.5 m (RS4). https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2025-08/3630 - Zoning Bylaw 2008.pdf
- Citadel Pointe, 678 and 688 Citadel Drive, is a gated townhome complex with its own strata website, managed by FirstService Residential. https://www.citadelpointe.com/info.html
- Detached homes here are freehold and attached homes are strata titled. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- A verified age and style breakdown is not stated in the OCP, which describes Citadel Heights only as an established residential neighbourhood. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is it called Citadel or Citadel Heights? **A:** Both names describe the same north Port Coquitlam neighbourhood — the board sub-area is Citadel and the City's Official Community Plan uses Citadel Heights. Search and paperwork use both, so check a listing, tax record and school lookup against the street address on Citadel Drive or Mary Hill Road rather than the neighbourhood name alone. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Citadel home? **A:** School District 43 (SD43) catchments are set by exact address. The locator result for the sample address 1265 Citadel Drive — Citadel Middle School's own address — is Hazel Trembath Elementary, École Citadel Middle and École Riverside Secondary. Castle Park Elementary and Hazel Trembath Elementary are the other north-side elementaries named on district material. Run the exact home address. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q:** Can a house lot hold more than one home? **A:** Yes — Port Coquitlam permits small-scale multi-unit housing of 3 to 6 units on RS-zoned lots without rezoning, with the count depending on lot size, location and proximity to transit. That makes the lot's zone, width and transit distance part of the price discussion for older Citadel houses. Confirm the count for the specific lot with the City before writing it into an offer. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q:** Is Citadel in the floodplain? **A:** Not automatically — a significant portion of Port Coquitlam sits in the Fraser and Pitt River floodplain behind dyke infrastructure, and Citadel Heights is elevated ground, but the City does not clear a specific lot by neighbourhood name. Check the lot's floodplain status with the City lot by lot, especially on lower streets toward the Pitt River and Castle Park. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/port-coquitlam-reminds-residents-prepare-possible-flooding
- **Q:** What recreation is on the north side? **A:** Hyde Creek Recreation Centre at 1379 Laurier Avenue is the north-side recreation centre, and the Terry Fox Library is at the Port Coquitlam Community Centre, 2150 Wilson Avenue. Castle Park sits beside Citadel on the Pitt River side, and the Traboulay PoCo Trail network crosses the city. Those four facilities frame daily life on the north side. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/recreation/parks-sports-fields-trails/traboulay-poco-trail/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Port Coquitlam-wide rates and utilities are treated in full — see the mary-hill-port-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes
- The Citadel-applicable figure is the citywide 2026 residential municipal rate of 2.1985 per $1,000 in Annual Tax Rates Bylaw, 2026, No. 4441, with taxes due July 2, 2026. https://portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/4441%20-%20Annual%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw.pdf
- Port Coquitlam states its 2026 increase was 1.95% for the average home and that utility bills are flat annual water and sewer fees billed separately, due March 31, 2026. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes
- Port Coquitlam is a listed Speculation and Vacancy Tax taxable area, so owners here file the annual provincial declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Citadel Drive and Mary Hill Road serve the neighbourhood, with Coast Meridian Road and its overpass as the link south to Lougheed Highway and the Mary Hill Bypass. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- TransLink buses serve north Port Coquitlam from Coquitlam Central Station and Port Coquitlam Station, and the West Coast Express stops at Port Coquitlam Station. https://maps.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/system-maps/fwc26_regional_transit_connections.pdf
- No SkyTrain station is in Port Coquitlam; rail access is by bus to Coquitlam Central or by West Coast Express at Port Coquitlam Station. https://maps.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/system-maps/fwc26_regional_transit_connections.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check floodplain status lot by lot, even on the heights — the City warns that a significant portion of Port Coquitlam is Fraser and Pitt River floodplain protected by dykes. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/port-coquitlam-reminds-residents-prepare-possible-flooding
- No Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) land was confirmed inside Citadel on a source read, so do not assume ALR either way for an edge lot. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- For gated strata such as Citadel Pointe, read the strata's own bylaws and gate / parking rules on the strata site before an offer. https://www.citadelpointe.com/info.html
- Confirm the small-scale multi-unit count for the exact lot with the City, because it turns on size, location and transit proximity. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
