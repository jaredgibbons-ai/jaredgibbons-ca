# Coquitlam Lots and Land — Extra Local Notes
Page: coquitlam-lots-land (Coquitlam; lots and land).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- This page covers the whole City of Coquitlam, so its land is not one pocket: the City's own plan maps split it into the City Centre area around Lansdowne Drive and Falcon Drive, the Northeast (Burke Mountain) area, and the Southwest, and each side carries different lot rules. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/3487/City-Centre-Area-Plan-Servicing-Assessment-PDF
- The whole city now sits under one plan: the Interim Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 5511, 2026, adopted April 27, 2026, replaced Citywide OCP Bylaw No. 3479, 2001 and carries the former area plans in as Schedule J schedules — check current. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review
- Three rivers frame the city's low land: the City's floodplain mapping work covers the Coquitlam, Fraser and Pitt river floodplains, while the upland lot fabric sits above them. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/182/Coquitlam-Fraser-and-Pitt-Rivers-Floodplain-Mapping-Report-PDF
- School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) (SD43) serves the whole city, and catchment is by permanent home address, so a lot's schools are an address check, not a city-wide fact. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Land here trades as individual fee-simple lots, not as a bare-land subdivision product: no bare-land strata plan for Coquitlam is verified in this program's research, and no named bare-land subdivision or presale lot offering is verified city-wide. ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/coquitlam-homes-for-sale-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md
- What a fee-simple lot can be split into runs off its small-scale residential zone: the minimum subdivision lot size is 650 square metres with 18.5 metres of width in zones R-1 and R-3, and 465 square metres with 12.5 metres of width in R-2, under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId
- The City's own 2026 fee schedule treats bare-land strata as its own subdivision category — "Conventional, Site Configuration, and Bare Land Strata (three or fewer lots)" — so the form exists in Coquitlam's process even though no community is verified as lot stock. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/374/Development-Planning-Application-Fees-Schedule-PDF
- Fraser Mills, on United Boulevard at King Edward Street, is a development site, not a lot offering: its buildings are presale towers documented in the condos program, and the site is preloaded to stand 20 feet above median sea level because it sits on the Fraser River floodplain. ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/coquitlam-homes-for-sale-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- General Coquitlam zoning and tax facts are written in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note (#10 in this program); this note covers only the land-specific rules below. ~/workspace/local-notes/uniqueness/scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale-extra-2026-11-03.md
- Subdivision servicing runs under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 3558, 2003: the consolidated bylaw requires the owner to grant or acquire statutory rights-of-way for the works and services needed to serve a subdivision, and to pay for traffic-control and street-name signage the City installs, before subdivision approval or building permit issue. https://publicdocs.coquitlam.ca/coquitlamdoc/getdocIF.asp?doc=6328713
- Adding homes without subdividing uses the small-scale zones: each of R-1, R-2, R-3 and R-4 sets its own minimum lot size for adding units, from 465 square metres in R-2 to 930 square metres in R-4. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId
- Creek distance decides whether a permit comes first: land within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of a watercourse in the City Centre Area Plan needs a Watercourse Protection Development Permit, and in Northeast Coquitlam the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area reaches 50 metres from the top of bank. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- Trees on a development lot are regulated before clearing: private trees are managed under the Tree Management Bylaw 4091, a permit may be required for removal, replacement trees may be required, and subdivision applicants will likely be asked for an arborist report as part of their submissions. https://northeastsector.ca/560/Trees-Development
- Flood and riparian siting rules sit inside the zoning bylaw itself: section 519 covers flood protection and section 523 covers riparian areas, with Schedule G mapping the designated 200-year floodplain of the Brunette, Coquitlam, Fraser and Pitt rivers. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1170/Part-5-General-Regulations-PDF

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** How small can a Coquitlam lot be subdivided? **A:** It depends on the zone, and the zone is the first check. Under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996, the minimum subdivision lot is 650 square metres with 18.5 metres of frontage width in zones R-1 and R-3, and 465 square metres with 12.5 metres of width in R-2. Servicing for any split then runs under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 3558, 2003, confirmed with the City for the specific parcel. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId
- **Q:** Is there bare-land strata I can buy a vacant lot in? **A:** None is verified in Coquitlam. The City's fee schedule has a bare-land strata subdivision category, so the tenure form is possible here, but this program found no Coquitlam bare-land strata community on a City, strata or developer source, and the verified bare-land strata roster for the whole region holds none in Coquitlam. If a lot's title does show strata tenure, the lot itself carries strata fees, bylaws and Form B diligence. ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/coquitlam-homes-for-sale-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md
- **Q:** What does a creek on or beside the lot change? **A:** It can add a permit before anything else happens. In the City Centre Area Plan, land within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of a watercourse is in the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area; in Northeast Coquitlam that distance is 50 metres. The City also tells small-scale applicants to contact Development Services early where there is a creek on or next to the lot, or a steep slope. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- **Q:** Can I clear the trees on a vacant lot before I build? **A:** Not automatically. Private property trees are managed under Coquitlam's Tree Management Bylaw 4091, a tree cutting permit may be required, and replacement trees may be required. On a subdivision or land amalgamation the City says an applicant will likely need an arborist report from a consulting arborist, and where clearing creates a new forest edge, a windthrow analysis by a Registered Professional Forester may be asked for. https://northeastsector.ca/560/Trees-Development
- **Q:** When are development cost charges (DCCs) charged on a new lot? **A:** Coquitlam imposes DCCs under its Development Cost Charges Bylaw No. 5411, 2025, listed in the City's own frequently requested bylaws, and the City's DCC guidelines for external parties, updated March 13, 2026, explain how the rates in that bylaw are calculated, including city-wide versus area-specific charges and instream protection. Read the guideline for the lot's area before pricing a subdivision. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/8846/Development-Cost-Charges-DCC-Guidelines-for-External-Parties-PDF

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- A new small-scale build on a vacant lot must file the City's Small-Scale Residential Fire Flow Requirements Form with the building permit application, confirming available fire flow for that lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- The City applies integrated stormwater management to small-scale residential projects, to manage runoff from roofs and driveways and protect local creeks — a design cost on lots adding hard surface near a watercourse. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- The City measures a tree's Critical Root Zone as its dripline, and its Minimum Protection Zone as six times the tree's diameter at breast height — the distances that decide how much of a treed lot is actually buildable. https://northeastsector.ca/560/Trees-Development
- Multi-stem trees count as protected size in Coquitlam if the largest two stems' diameters at breast height, added together, exceed 20 centimetres. https://northeastsector.ca/560/Trees-Development
- The City's zoning viewer, QtheMap, is the lot-level check the City itself points buyers to for a parcel's zone, rather than any neighbourhood average. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Coquitlam's general 2026 tax rates are written in full in the scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale note; the land-specific charges below are this page's costs. ~/workspace/local-notes/uniqueness/scott-creek-coquitlam-houses-for-sale-extra-2026-11-03.md
- Development cost charges are imposed under Development Cost Charges Bylaw No. 5411, 2025, as listed in the City's frequently requested bylaws. https://coquitlam.ca/556/Frequently-Requested-Bylaws
- Amenity Cost Charges (ACCs) sit on top of DCCs for new homes: the City's ACC program was adopted July 7, 2025, and the low-rise residential rate — which the City defines to include single-detached and small-scale residential — is $21,205 per dwelling unit, effective July 7, 2025. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1399/Amenity-Cost-Charges
- Subdivision application fees, 2026 fee schedule effective January 1, 2026: a conventional, site configuration or bare land strata subdivision of three or fewer lots costs $5,691.40, plus $209.40 for every lot created beyond three. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/374/Development-Planning-Application-Fees-Schedule-PDF
- A Watercourse Protection Development Permit for a single-family development costs $811.40 under the same 2026 schedule, and $1,908.20 for developments other than single family. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/374/Development-Planning-Application-Fees-Schedule-PDF
- Coquitlam is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT), so a residential lot owner there must complete the annual declaration; reserve, treaty and self-governing Indigenous Nation lands are excluded. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the flood layer first on low land: the City's floodplain mapping covers the Coquitlam, Fraser and Pitt rivers, and Zoning Bylaw Schedule G maps the designated 200-year floodplain — the mapping report does not give a lot-level flood construction level for a specific parcel. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/182/Coquitlam-Fraser-and-Pitt-Rivers-Floodplain-Mapping-Report-PDF
- Check the environmental development permit layers, which map watercourses, unstable slopes and the Interface Wildfire Risk Management boundary for the City Centre and Northeast areas; the map shows the layers, not a lot-level geotechnical conclusion. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status per parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission mapping before pricing any edge-of-city parcel; no ALR parcel in Coquitlam was identified in the sources read for this note, which is not a parcel clearance. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- Check the tree position before valuing a treed lot: the Tree Management Bylaw 4091 permit and replacement rules, and the arborist report a subdivision will likely require, can change the buildable area the price assumed. https://northeastsector.ca/560/Trees-Development
