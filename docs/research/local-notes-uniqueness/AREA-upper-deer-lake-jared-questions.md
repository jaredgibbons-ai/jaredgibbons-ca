# Upper Deer Lake — Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer, for a voice memo. Muse does not answer these.

1. Who buys houses around Upper Deer Lake — families who want the park and the museum village next door, or buyers trading the lake for a quieter street than Metrotown offers?
2. What sells fast and what sits — the heritage-era homes near the lake's west end, or the postwar stock on the upper streets?
3. What surprises buyers most: heritage protection on individual houses, the Shadbolt Centre and boardwalk work changing the park, or lot shapes on the older subdivisions?
4. How do buyers price the Deer Lake address against Buckingham Heights or Burnaby Lake streets nearby?
5. Tell one story from an Upper Deer Lake deal — a protected-house restriction discovered late, a park-side premium, or a long-held family home coming to market.
