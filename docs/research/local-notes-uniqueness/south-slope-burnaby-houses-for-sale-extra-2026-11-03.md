# South Slope Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: south-slope-burnaby-houses-for-sale (South Slope; houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- South Slope is the house pocket on the hill between Rumble Street at the top and Marine Drive at the bottom: the City's Royal Oak Community Plan map draws this slope with Rumble Street, Royal Oak Avenue, Marine Drive and Gilley Avenue as its working street frame; the full shared area facts are in the south-slope-burnaby note. http://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-04/Royal-Oak-Community-Plan.pdf
- The plan map names the slope's creeks, which double as its internal boundaries: Frogger's Creek, Gray Creek, John Matthews Creek and Byrne Creek all cross the plan area between Rumble Street and Marine Drive. http://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-04/Royal-Oak-Community-Plan.pdf
- The older name for this hill is Alta Vista: Heritage Burnaby files South Slope Elementary under the Alta Vista historic neighbourhood, in the Sussex-Nelson planning study area. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark843

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is detached houses on the slope streets between Rumble Street and Marine Drive, with the Royal Oak Station area at the slope's northern half rather than inside the house blocks themselves. http://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-04/Royal-Oak-Community-Plan.pdf
- Detached lots here fall under the City's R1 small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) district, so a house lot can be redeveloped as up to 3 to 6 dwelling units depending on lot area and proximity to a frequent transit network bus stop. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
Read: 2026-11-03

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Royal Oak Community Plan, adopted March 2025, covers the Station Area District between Nelson Avenue, MacPherson Avenue, Imperial Street and Rumble Street, and the slope streets down to Marine Drive, so a lot's plan rules depend on whether it sits in the station district or on the slope below it. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects-direct-link?page=2
- Burnaby's new Zoning Bylaw 2026 was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026, replacing the former Zoning Bylaw 1965, and the R1 SSMUH district dates to the June 2024 rezoning of all R district lots. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- Burnaby is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Does the Royal Oak Community Plan cover my South Slope lot? **A:** It covers the slope's northern half and the Royal Oak Station area, with the Station Area District drawn between Nelson Avenue, MacPherson Avenue, Imperial Street and Rumble Street and the slope streets running down to Marine Drive, so check the plan map for the lot's position before assuming station-area rules apply. http://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-04/Royal-Oak-Community-Plan.pdf
- **Q:** Do creeks affect South Slope lots? **A:** Four named creeks cross the plan area between Rumble Street and Marine Drive — Frogger's Creek, Gray Creek, John Matthews Creek and Byrne Creek — so a lot near one of them needs a watercourse setback check with the City, since the creeks are the slope's internal boundaries. http://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-04/Royal-Oak-Community-Plan.pdf
- **Q:** Can I build a multiplex on a South Slope house lot? **A:** Possibly, under the R1 SSMUH district: newly rezoned R1 lots are eligible for up to 3 to 6 dwelling units depending on lot area and proximity to a frequent transit network bus stop, and the count is inclusive of any secondary suites. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q:** What is the 2026 residential tax rate? **A:** Burnaby's 2026 residential mill rate totals 3.28373 per 1,000 dollars of assessed value, and gross taxes are the assessed value multiplied by the mill rate and divided by 1,000, before local area service, sewer parcel and business improvement area charges. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby's 2026 residential tax rate is 3.28373 per 1,000 dollars of assessed value, and the average residential assessment in Burnaby decreased by 5.29 percent in 2026, which does not necessarily mean an individual bill falls, because taxes depend on the change in a property's assessment relative to the class average. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates
- Property taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after the July due date and a further 5 percent after the September due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes
- Assessment notices arrive from BC Assessment each January, and the assessed value on the notice is the value the mill rate is applied to. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/tax-rates

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Confirm the lot's R1 SSMUH eligibility and unit count with the City before assuming a multiplex, because the count turns on lot area and on distance to a frequent transit network bus stop. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- Check for a watercourse setback if the lot sits near Frogger's Creek, Gray Creek, John Matthews Creek or Byrne Creek, since all four cross the plan area. http://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-04/Royal-Oak-Community-Plan.pdf
- Confirm whether the lot falls in the Royal Oak Station Area District or on the slope below it, because the plan rules differ between the two. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects-direct-link?page=2
