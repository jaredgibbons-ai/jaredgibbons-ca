## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Burnaby's house lots sit in the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District, which allows 3 units on lots up to 280 square metres, 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 square metres within 400 metres of a frequent-transit stop. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- The new Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026, replacing the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 — older R-zone descriptions no longer govern a Brentwood lot. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- Lots with two or more primary units in the R1 District may be stratified, but a secondary suite must stay on the same title as its primary unit and cannot be stratified. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- Inside a Frequent Transit Network Area or a transit-oriented area, no off-street parking is required; outside them, a new build needs 1 space for the first unit plus 0.5 spaces per added unit, rounded up. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- The Burnaby 2050 OCP, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What plan governs new towers in Brentwood? **A:** The Brentwood Town Centre Development Plan (1996) is the City's community plan for the centre, and the Brentwood Site at Lougheed Highway and Willingdon Avenue is guided by the Brentwood Site Conceptual Master Plan adopted in 2013. On January 29, 2024 Council authorized public consultation on amendments to both plans to add more diverse housing, rental housing, green space and child care. https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-town-centre-plan-amendments
- **Q:** Can a Brentwood house lot hold more than one home? **A:** Yes, under the R1 SSMUH District most house lots can hold 3 to 4 homes, and a lot of at least 281 square metres within 400 metres of a frequent-transit stop can hold 6. Permitted forms include single-family homes with laneway homes, duplexes, multiplexes, cottage clusters and rowhouses, and a secondary suite counts toward the total unit count for the lot. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q:** Which elementary schools sit in the Brentwood Park side of this page? **A:** Brentwood Park Elementary at 1455 Delta Avenue is the in-area elementary school on the Province's SD41 list, with Gilmore Community Elementary at 50 South Gilmore Avenue and Kitchener Elementary at 1351 Gilmore Avenue also in or beside the area. Catchments are address-based, so the catchment for a specific home is confirmed in the SD41 school locator for that exact address, not from this list. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** What changed for Brentwood transit in 2026? **A:** The R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus was extended from Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station via Hastings Street and Willingdon Avenue, serving Brentwood Town Centre directly. TransLink states the extension began in September 2026, runs all day seven days a week, uses 60-foot buses, and runs every 6 to 7 minutes in peak periods, replacing the peak-only 222 Willingdon Express. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/04/translink-fast-tracks-major-rapidbus-expansion-from-north-shore-to-metrotown/
- **Q:** Is there a house rental licence in Burnaby? **A:** Yes, an owner who rents out a whole house and does not live on the premises must buy a City of Burnaby House Rental Licence instead of paying additional utility fees. Owners of single- and two-family homes must also file a Supplementary Utility Fees Declaration stating whether the home or a suite is rented, and file a new declaration within 30 days if the rental status changes. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- SD41 has no middle-school tier: elementary is kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary is Grade 8 to Grade 12, in 41 elementary and 8 secondary schools city-wide. https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/
- A rented suite in a Burnaby single-family dwelling carries its own 2026 utility charges — $326.17 for water and $403.00 for sewer use — on top of the main dwelling's charges. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- Starting in 2027 the Sewer Parcel Tax moves off the Burnaby property tax notice onto the utility notice, so it can no longer be deferred under the provincial tax deferment program and a mortgage company will no longer pay it with the taxes. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- The 180-litre garbage toter is Burnaby's default size, at a 2026 annual disposal fee of $159; the 360-litre large toter costs $447 a year. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- Burnaby City Hall and Revenue Services are at 4949 Canada Way, east of Brentwood across the Deer Lake side of the city. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Burnaby's 2026 municipal residential rate is $1.72223 per $1,000 of taxable value in total for Class 1 (residential) under the Burnaby Rates Bylaw 2026, Bylaw No. 14814, adopted May 12, 2026, including a $0.05920 Growth Infrastructure Investment component. https://bylaws.burnaby.ca/media/14000/14814.pdf
- Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan, about $61 for the average home, plus the 1.9% Growth Infrastructure Investment Levy of about $39 for an average home. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2026-01-28/burnaby-keeps-tax-rates-affordable-latest-financial-plan
- Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding current taxes after the July due date and a further 5% penalty after the September due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- The 2026 annual water charge for a detached single-family dwelling is $652.33, utility rates carry a 5% discount if paid by the due date, and sewer for single-family homes is billed as a sewer parcel tax on the property tax notice in 2026. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies in the City of Burnaby: the Province lists Burnaby among the Metro Vancouver Regional District taxable areas, so a residential owner here must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check Still Creek on the south side of the Brentwood area lot by lot: streamside setbacks are parcel-specific, and no City page read gives a Brentwood-wide setback figure. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- Check the lot's Frequent Transit Network Area status on the City's BurnabyMap viewer before pricing added units, because the 6-unit tier and the no-parking-required rule both turn on the 400-metre frequent-transit test. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status for any edge parcel in the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder; the Brentwood core itself is urban, but the check is the official route for any lot in question. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- Check the Burnaby 2050 OCP land use map, not the 1996 plan map alone, before assuming redevelopment value near the stations, because the 2025 OCP takes precedence where the maps conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
