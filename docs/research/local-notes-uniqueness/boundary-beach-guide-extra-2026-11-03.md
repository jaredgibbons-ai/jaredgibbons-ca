# Boundary Beach Guide — Extra Local Notes

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Boundary Beach is the beachfront enclave at Tsawwassen's south end, running along Boundary Bay Road, Centennial Parkway and 66 Street toward the United States border; the street-level boundary detail is in the boundary-beach note — see the boundary-beach note. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park
- Its public anchor is Boundary Bay Regional Park and Centennial Beach at 570 Boundary Bay Road, run by Metro Vancouver. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The guide-level pattern is detached homes on the streets behind the dike, mixing older beach cottages with newer raised houses built to flood levels; lot-by-lot detail is in the boundary-beach note — see the boundary-beach note. https://admin.delta.ca/media/5541
- Waterfront homes here sit behind engineered seawalls on private property, not a public promenade, which is why two houses on the same street can have very different flood works. https://admin.delta.ca/media/5541

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- The Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands (SD3) Development Permit Area is the controlling rule: the City's seawall form requires a crest elevation of 4.1 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada (GSC) datum, designed so it can be raised a further 1.4 metres, with a wave deflector and erosion protection at the toe. https://admin.delta.ca/media/5541
- A new seawall needs an engineered design by a structural engineer, and an existing seawall needs an engineer's certification of its structural and flood-protection integrity. https://admin.delta.ca/media/5541

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What protects these houses from the sea? **A:** A dike and private seawalls protect them, and both are regulated. Delta maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection, and the City's seawall form for this foreshore requires a 4.1-metre GSC crest that can later be raised 1.4 metres. Ask for the seawall's engineering certification before you buy, not after. https://admin.delta.ca/media/5541
- **Q:** Can I build or rebuild at beach level? **A:** No — floor levels are set by the SD3 permit area. Earlier program research on the City's Official Community Plan (OCP) recorded a minimum habitable floor elevation of 2.9 metres GSC and land surface of 2.3 metres GSC around construction in SD3; confirm the current figures for your lot with the City, because a parcel-level check decides. https://admin.delta.ca/media/5541
- **Q:** Who owns the beach in front of a waterfront house? **A:** The Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore in British Columbia. Private moorage and foreshore use are by provincial permission, lease or licence through FrontCounter BC, so a dock or steps over the foreshore are a tenure question to check on title and with the Province. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **Q:** Which elementary school serves the beach streets? **A:** Check the exact address in the district locator before relying on any name. South Park Elementary at 735 Gilchrist Drive is the school on the south Tsawwassen side nearest these streets, and South Delta Secondary at 750 53 Street is the area's secondary school; catchment is address-based and the district directs families to verify it. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/news-events/news/registration-information-for-students-who-are-new-to-delta-2/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Most of Centennial Beach is closed to dogs, with signed marker posts showing where dogs are prohibited — relevant if you are buying for daily dog walks. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park
- The park is an internationally recognized Important Bird Area and a Pacific Flyway stop, so seasonal bird closures and crowds are part of beach life here. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park
- Delta received a $2 million provincial grant in 2023 for Boundary Bay dike improvements, with a pilot raising a dike section by Boundary Bay Airport between 72 Street and 88 Street. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-receives-provincial-grant-boundary-bay-dike
- Route 601 runs Boundary Bay trips via 12 Avenue, Boundary Bay Road, 3 Avenue and 67 Street, so the beach streets do have a bus, but it is the long way to Bridgeport Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf
- The Dyke Trail runs through Boundary Bay Regional Park, so the dike top doubles as the neighbourhood's walking and cycling route, not just flood works. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta's 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after that date and another 5 percent after September 2, 2026; Council's 2026 plan set a 2.9 percent City tax increase. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- For 2026, Delta's flat-rate utility bill for a single-family home is $1,658, up $51 from 2025, covering garbage and recycling plus water and sewer where not metered separately. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Delta is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT), so residential owners must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Seawall condition is the lot-specific risk: get the engineer's certification the City requires for existing seawalls, and price future raising, because the design standard assumes the crest may need to go 1.4 metres higher. https://admin.delta.ca/media/5541
- Sea-level rise is the long-term driver: the City states future sea levels are expected to rise and its dike must be raised to protect the community, which is why flood covenants and floor levels follow these lots on title. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-receives-provincial-grant-boundary-bay-dike
