## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Duplex-form strata homes at Nature's Walk follow the same Form B and depreciation-report rules as any strata lot; duplex form does not make a home a freehold purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- City short-term rental prohibition applies on Bonson Road exactly as elsewhere in Pitt Meadows — see the south-meadows-condos-townhomes-for-sale note for the bylaw wording. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- Building within seven metres of the Fraser River dike toe on a Bonson Road lot needs a permit under Drainage Bylaw No. 2266, 2007, which matters for fences and additions in dike-adjacent complexes like Shoreline at 19452 Fraser Way. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2023-04/Drainage%20System%20Protection%20Bylaw%20No.%202266%2C%202007.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Why does my Osprey Village listing say South Meadows if this page says Mid Meadows? **A:** Because the board label and the program's geography file the same ground under two names, and both appear in a buyer's search. Listings classify Coho, Coho II, Shoreline and Nature's Walk as South Meadows, while the research file places Bonson Road south of the rail line in Mid Meadows. The complex, address and strata are identical either way — search both labels so no Osprey Village home is missed. {Research file: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows-townhomes-2026-11-01.md}
- **Q:** Is Shoreline actually on the water? **A:** Shoreline at 19452 Fraser Way stands at the Fraser River edge of Osprey Village, behind the dike, with the dike-top trail at Shoreline Park as its waterfront. Homes are not on unprotected riverbank: the dike and the City's floodplain bylaw sit between the complex and the river. Check the lot's flood construction level covenant and the strata's flood insurance in the Form B package. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 - Consolidated.pdf
- **Q:** Which school serves Barnston View Road? **A:** No school building stands in Osprey Village, so catchment is an address lookup, not a walk-to-school assumption. The district list places Edith McDermott Elementary at 12178 Bonson Road and Davie Jones Elementary at 12030 Blakely Road as the city's Bonson-side elementary schools, with Pitt Meadows Secondary at 19438 116B Avenue for grades 8 to 12. Run the exact Barnston View Road address in the School District 42 locator before writing subjects. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- **Q:** How do I commute from Fraser Way without a car? **A:** Use the 719/722 circular service, which loops Bonson Road and Fraser Way to Meadowtown, Pitt Meadows Station and Maple Meadows Station for the West Coast Express. There is no station on Bonson Road itself, and route 701 on Hammond Road is the north-edge alternative. Frequencies are stated on TransLink timetables, not in the sources read here, so check the current timetable for the sailing you need. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Fraser Way lots sit behind the Fraser River dike in the urban floodplain: Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 Schedule B sets the 5.15-metre designated flood level that governs flood construction levels here. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 - Consolidated.pdf
- Dike setbacks under Drainage Bylaw No. 2266, 2007 (seven metres from the dike toe) can limit additions in the dike-adjacent complexes. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2023-04/Drainage%20System%20Protection%20Bylaw%20No.%202266%2C%202007.pdf
- East of Bonson Road the urban corridor meets Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) farm parcels; confirm the ALR edge for any lot backing onto farmland on the City OCP maps. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
