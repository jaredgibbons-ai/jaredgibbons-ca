## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Off-site directional signs on city property are not allowed. Sign Bylaw No. 1662-1987, section 7.4, prohibits third party and portable signs except as specifically permitted, and section 7.5 bars signs within or over a highway; crews dispose of illegal signs, $250.00 ticket. http://www.mission.ca/media/file/sign-bylaw-1662-1987pdf-1 https://www.mission.ca/community-environment/environment-sustainability/signs-city-property
- An on-site sale sign needs no permit: section 9.2(a) allows a temporary, non-illuminated sign advertising the parcel's sale, removed the date it sells. http://www.mission.ca/media/file/sign-bylaw-1662-1987pdf-1

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can the agent put directional signs on boulevards? **A:** No, not on city property. Sign Bylaw No. 1662-1987 prohibits third party and portable signs except as specifically permitted and bars signs within or over a highway; crews dispose of illegal signs and a $250.00 ticket applies. Plan the trip by street address instead. http://www.mission.ca/media/file/sign-bylaw-1662-1987pdf-1 https://www.mission.ca/community-environment/environment-sustainability/signs-city-property
- **Q:** Does the home's for-sale sign need a permit? **A:** No. Section 9.2(a) of Sign Bylaw No. 1662-1987 permits temporary, non-illuminated signs advertising the sale, lease or rental of the parcel they sit on, in all zones, if the sign otherwise complies and is removed the date the parcel sells. http://www.mission.ca/media/file/sign-bylaw-1662-1987pdf-1
- **Q:** How do I check an address's school catchment? **A:** Use the district's own locator, not a listing site. Mission Public Schools, School District No. 75, tells parents to find the catchment school with the School Locator Tool on its website, under Schools, then Locate Your School, bringing proof of address to register. https://media.mpsd.ca/media/Default/medialib/mpsd-k-12-registration-document-guide-august-2025.c7ffd45837.pdf
- **Q:** Can I take the West Coast Express to a weekend open house? **A:** No, it does not run weekends. TransLink's timetable, effective January 5, 2026, lists Monday to Friday service from Mission City Station and states no service on weekends or statutory holidays, so use a BC Transit bus or drive on open-house days. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
Read: 2026-11-03

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Buses run reduced weekend service, shaping an open-house run. BC Transit says Central Fraser Valley buses run 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. weekdays, less on weekends; route 35 Hatzic is peak-only, no midday trips. https://engage.bctransit.com/cfv2024/news_feed/service-today
- Mission is a Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) area for residential property. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

- Two transit systems serve Mission; only buses run weekends. The West Coast Express links Mission City Station to Waterfront Station weekdays only; BC Transit runs routes 31 Abbotsford-Mission, 33 Cedar Valley and 35 Hatzic. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf https://lite.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/10534_CFV_RG_Jan2025-v9.pdf

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- A Hatzic open house needs a flood and dike check first. Hatzic Lake drains through the Fraser River dike, the Dewdney Area Improvement District (DAID) is diking authority for the Hatzic reach, and the November 2021 atmospheric river flooded lower Hatzic Valley. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/flood-projects/hatzic-valley-emergency-works
- Forested-edge homes need a wildfire check: Mission Fire Rescue Service offers free FireSmart assessments; its Home Ignition Zone runs 30 metres out. https://www.mission.ca/services/public-safety/fire-rescue-service/firesmart-mission
Read: 2026-11-03
