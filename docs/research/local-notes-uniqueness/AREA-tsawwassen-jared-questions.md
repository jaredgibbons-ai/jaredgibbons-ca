# Tsawwassen — Questions for Jared
Area: Tsawwassen (houses, condos, townhomes).

## 5 questions only Jared can answer
1. In Tsawwassen deals you have actually worked, what do you see that the listings and City pages never show — in Beach Grove, English Bluff, Pebble Hill and Southlands?
2. Who is buying in Tsawwassen right now, by home type — who takes the houses, who takes the Tsawwassen Springs and 56 Street condos, and who takes the townhomes?
3. What sells fast in Tsawwassen and what sits — which streets, building types or lot types move, and which ones stall?
4. What surprises Tsawwassen buyers most after they start looking seriously — the dike and flood checks, the Tsawwassen First Nation boundary, the ferry traffic, or something else you hear repeatedly?
5. Tell one Tsawwassen deal story (no names needed) that shows how buying there really works — a subject, an inspection, a strata or a flood issue that changed the deal.

## Shot list — 3 photos / drone shots for Jared to take
1. Diefenbaker Park, 5579 1 Avenue — from the park viewpoint looking west over the Strait of Georgia, late afternoon, to show the bluff setting above the Beach Grove houses.
2. Market Avenue in Southlands, at Market Square and the Red Barn — street-level shot looking down Market Avenue with the townhomes and condo flats on both sides.
3. Boundary Bay Road at 3 Avenue, Southlands edge — drone shot looking north over the Southlands farmland toward the Tsawwassen houses, with Boundary Bay in frame.
