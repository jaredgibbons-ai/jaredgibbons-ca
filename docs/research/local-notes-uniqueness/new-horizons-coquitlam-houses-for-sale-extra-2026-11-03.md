## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Windsor Gate is the defining stock here: Polygon states it is a 25-acre master-planned community begun in 2008, mixing high-rise towers with low-rise apartment buildings around a residents-only club, so the sub-area's homes are strata product rather than detached houses. https://polyhomes.com/community/the-lloyd/
- A buyer searching this page for a detached house is searching the wrong stock: the named community's buildings are towers and low-rise apartments in a Frank Lloyd Wright-inspired style with wide, landscaped boulevards. https://polyhomes.com/community/the-lloyd/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Coquitlam's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules allow up to 3 units on lots of 280 square metres or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 square metres and under 4,050 square metres, and the City states it currently has no bus stops meeting the provincial frequent-service criteria, so the 6-unit tier does not apply. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquitlam
- Small-scale housing in Coquitlam can include single-detached dwellings, secondary suites, garden cottages and carriage houses, duplexes, triplexes, fourplexes and multiplexes, which the City groups as Small-Scale Residential. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquitlam
- Coquitlam is inside the speculation and vacancy tax taxable area, so residential owners here must declare each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
