# North Maple Ridge Area Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: north-maple-ridge-guide (North Maple Ridge; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- North Maple Ridge has no City-published boundary — the City of Maple Ridge neighbourhood list names Silver Valley, Yennadon, Thornhill, Albion/Kanaka, Webster's Corners and Whonnock on the north side, and no neighbourhood called "North Maple Ridge," so this guide's ground is the rural north built from those named neighbourhoods. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The Alouette River is the area's dividing line: Silver Valley sits on the north side of the Alouette River and is the gateway to the recreation areas and trail networks beyond it, while Yennadon south of the river is an active agricultural production area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Thornhill, at the area's west side, is a heavily forested, mainly residential area with farms and nurseries, and its mountain bike and hiking trail network runs on 256 Street with 17 trails. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The working roads are Dewdney Trunk Road along the south edge and 232 Street and 240 Street running north — the same 232 Street spine that carries Maple Ridge Park at 232 Street and 132 Avenue in Yennadon. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock splits between small urban-fringe lots and true acreages, and Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 sets the split: RS-1 (Single Detached Residential) section 605 requires a minimum lot area of 668.0 square metres, 18.0 metre width and 27.0 metre depth, while the rural zones in the same bylaw require A-1 at 2.0 hectares, A-2 at 4.0 hectares, A-3 at 8.0 hectares and RS-2 at 0.4 hectares. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- North of the Alouette River the form is set by the Silver Valley Area Plan, which plans housing as clusters — Eco-Cluster Residential, Ground-Oriented Residential and Medium Density Residential around hamlets named Blaney Hamlet, Forest Hamlet and Horse Hamlet, plus a River Village Mixed-Use designation added in the 2025 update. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-advances-housing-strategy-completion-area-plan-updates
- In Yennadon the houses sit inside a working farm area the City describes as an active agricultural production area, so acreage homes there neighbour farm operations rather than other subdivisions. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A secondary suite is allowed once per lot and only inside the same building as the single detached house, capped at 40 percent of the building's total gross floor area, and a suite must not be situated within the Floodplain Area — all under Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019 as summarized on the City's secondary suites page. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- Renting both floors costs a licence: a business licence is required if there are two rental units on a Maple Ridge property, and short-term rental use also requires a business licence, per the City's secondary suites page. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status is per parcel, not per neighbourhood: the City calls Yennadon an active agricultural production area, but a specific lot's ALR status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping before assuming farm or second-dwelling rights. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is "North Maple Ridge" an official neighbourhood with set boundaries? **A:** No — the City publishes no boundary for that name. Its own neighbourhood list for the north side names Silver Valley north of the Alouette River, Yennadon, Thornhill, Albion/Kanaka, Webster's Corners and Whonnock. A listing labelled North Maple Ridge can sit in any of those, so price and rule comparisons should be made against the named City neighbourhood the lot actually falls in, not the guide name. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- **Q:** Can I put a suite in a house near the Alouette River? **A:** Only if the lot is outside the floodplain area. Maple Ridge allows one secondary suite per lot, inside the same building as the single detached house and no larger than 40 percent of the building's gross floor area, but the City's rules state a suite must not be situated within the Floodplain Area. River-adjacent lots along the North and South Alouette need the floodplain check in section 9 before a suite is counted as income. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q:** Which schools serve a house on 232 Street or in Silver Valley? **A:** School District 42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows) assigns schools by address, not by guide name. The district runs elementary schools from Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary schools from Grade 8 to Grade 12, with no middle-school tier, and the exact catchment for a north Maple Ridge address is confirmed in the district's School Locator before an offer depends on it. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q:** What changes under the updated Silver Valley Area Plan? **A:** The plan now plans for a complete community, not just acreage lots. The update adopted November 25, 2025 modernizes the 2002 Silver Valley plan, consolidates land use designations, adds a zoning matrix, incorporates added density required by provincial legislation, and introduces a River Village Mixed-Use designation. Lots north of the Alouette River should be read against Section 10.3 of the Official Community Plan (OCP), not the pre-2025 plan. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/silver?bidId=

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Maple Ridge Park, 13180 232 Street, sits on the South Alouette River in Yennadon with a playground, spray park and picnic shelter — it is the municipal park for the 232 Street corridor, not a Silver Valley park. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Kanaka Creek Regional Park (Metro Vancouver) runs along Kanaka Creek at the area's east side, with the Canyon Trail to Cliff Falls and the Bell-Irving Hatchery. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- No City recreation centre or library branch sits in the north: the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre is at 11925 Haney Place and the Maple Ridge Public Library at 130 – 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road, both in the Town Centre to the south. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre
- Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, is the community hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows with 24/7 emergency care — the hospital run from a Silver Valley acreage goes south through the Town Centre, not east. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- BC Hydro makes controlled releases at the Alouette Dam that raise levels on the South Alouette River: in March 2026 the City advised residents in flood-prone areas below the dam along the South Alouette River to prepare for possible flooding and evacuation. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/bc-hydro-planning-controlled-release-alouette-dam

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Two community shuttle routes serve the north side: TransLink route 733 (Rock Ridge / Haney Place) runs two-way service on 232 Street to 239B Street and 133 Avenue, and route 741 (Anderson Creek / Haney Place) runs via 227 Street, 128 Avenue, Foreman Drive and Silver Valley Road, both introduced September 4, 2017 to replace the C47 and extend service to Silver Valley. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2017/08/bus-route-buzz-surrey-langley-maple-ridge/
- Route 733's current timetable (effective June 8, 2026) runs from Haney Place via 228 Street, Dewdney Trunk Road and 232 Street to 133 Avenue at Rock Ridge Drive, with limited morning trips continuing to Port Haney Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt733.pdf
- The rail link is the West Coast Express at Port Haney and Maple Meadows stations, TransLink's weekday commuter train between Waterfront Station in downtown Vancouver and Mission City — there is no station in the north itself. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the lot against the City's flood maps first: Maple Ridge publishes North and South Alouette River and Fraser River floodplain mapping, and the City's flood page directs residents to MapleRidge.ca/Flood to determine whether a property is in a flood-prone area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/be-flood-ready-guide-fraser-river-city-maple-ridge
- The new standard is Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 8121-2026: its designated flood event is the 1-in-200-year event for the Alouette River systems and the 1894 flood (about 1-in-500-year) for the Fraser River, and no building, habitable area or fill is permitted within the bylaw's Floodplain Setbacks. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
- The underlying study behind the Alouette maps is the North and South Alouette Rivers floodplain analysis, whose updated 200-year floodplain area is bounded by Park Lane, 236th Street, 127th Avenue and 136th Avenue — lots inside that box carry modelled flood depths, not just a floodplain label. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/north-and-south-alouette-rivers-floodplain-study
- Confirm ALR status parcel by parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission map for any Yennadon or Thornhill acreage — the City neighbourhood description does not settle an individual lot's reserve status. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
