## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Cottage Grove, 8500 Young Road, is the clearest buyable ground-oriented 55+ product: a gated bare-land strata of detached one-level ranchers, each on its own strata lot. Confirm the registered age bylaw on the Form B before relying on it. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/buildings/chilliwack?filter_titletoland=FREEHOLD%2BSTRATA&page=4&sortby=street_name
- Chartwell Birchwood, 45650 Patten Avenue, offers seniors' townhouses beside its retirement residence — two bedrooms, full kitchen, in-suite laundry, balcony and patio, in four floor plans. It is rental retirement living, not a strata you can buy. https://chartwell.com/bc/chilliwack/birchwood/senior-townhouses
- Elim Village, 45412 Chehalis Drive in Garrison Crossing, Sardis, is a seniors village including The Redwood, a four-storey independent-living building of 64 suites. Elim's own pages describe rental and life-lease style housing, not freehold townhome strata. https://elimvillage.com/chilliwack/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A 55+ bylaw is now the only age restriction a British Columbia strata may have: effective November 24, 2022, a strata may only restrict age at not less than 55 years, with exemptions for some caregivers and for residents who already lived in the lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- From May 1, 2023, further exemptions cover children, including adult children, and younger spouses or partners of an exempt resident — the complex's registered bylaw still states how the rule is applied. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- Rental-restriction bylaws are no longer enforceable in British Columbia stratas, but a valid 55+ bylaw still controls who may live in the unit, including a tenant. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/more-homes-for-people

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Can my 52-year-old spouse live with me in a 55+ townhome? **A:** Possibly, under the provincial exemptions. Since May 1, 2023, British Columbia law exempts younger spouses or partners, and children including adult children, of a qualifying resident from a 55+ age-restriction bylaw. The strata's registered bylaw is still the document to read before offering, because it states how this complex applies the rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q:** Is Cottage Grove a townhome, a house or a condo? **A:** It is a bare-land strata of detached ranchers at 8500 Young Road, which is why it appears on ground-oriented searches. You own a strata lot and a one-level detached home; the strata carries the common property and the gate. That differs from a freehold Young Road house and from an apartment condo, and the strata documents settle fees and responsibilities. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/buildings/chilliwack?filter_titletoland=FREEHOLD%2BSTRATA&page=4&sortby=street_name
- **Q:** Are the Chartwell Birchwood townhouses for sale? **A:** No. Chartwell presents its townhouses at 45650 Patten Avenue as seniors' townhouses inside a retirement residence, with weekly housekeeping and emergency response included, beside the main residence near Chilliwack General Hospital. They are rented as retirement living. A buyer who wants to own should look at strata product such as Cottage Grove, and treat Birchwood as the rental alternative in the same decision. https://chartwell.com/bc/chilliwack/birchwood/senior-townhouses
- **Q:** Which hospital serves these homes? **A:** Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Road, is the city's Fraser Health community hospital, providing primary, core secondary and some specialty services. The Patten Avenue seniors cluster sits in its precinct; Cottage Grove and Elim Village are south of Highway 1 in Sardis, so their hospital trip crosses the highway interchange. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Chilliwack's 2025 total residential tax rate was 4.50189 per $1,000 of assessed value, including a total municipal rate of 2.83455; the full breakdown is in the chilliwack-houses-for-sale note. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2527/Tax%5FRates%5Ffor%5Fthe%5FWebtugnc2fs1l9d%2Epdf
- City utility and solid-waste charges are billed quarterly, under the bylaws named on the City's rates page; a strata may carry some through strata fees. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in the City of Chilliwack, a listed taxable area, so an empty 55+ townhome still needs the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets Flood Construction Levels (FCLs) in Chilliwack; the City guide states new construction generally needs the lowest habitable floor at FCL plus 0.6 metres freeboard. Check the parcel, not the neighbourhood. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=637
- The same bylaw sets a 75-metre building setback from the natural boundary of the Vedder River, relevant to south-side lots. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- The Sardis-Vedder Aquifer is Development Permit Area 1 in the 2050 Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, Schedule D-1, so construction or a new well in the mapped area can require a development permit. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SD1%5FDPA%5FDrinking%5FWater%5FSupply%2Epdf
