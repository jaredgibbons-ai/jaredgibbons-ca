# Coquitlam Mobile Homes for Sale — Extra Local Notes
Page: coquitlam-mobile-homes-for-sale (City of Coquitlam; Mobile Homes for Sale).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Coquitlam's manufactured-home ground is three named parks, not a city-wide scatter. The settled program roster is Hideaway Park in Central Coquitlam and Wildwood Park and Mill Creek Village in Maillardville, in Coquitlam West, all in the City of Coquitlam and School District 43 (Coquitlam). https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/4210/Mobile-Home-Guide-PDF
- A home on a rented pad is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad. That tenure line applies to every park in this note. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/4210/Mobile-Home-Guide-PDF

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Hideaway Park is at 4200 Dewdney Trunk Road in Central Coquitlam, a pad-rental park recorded in the program as a tenure record. A home there is bought as a manufactured home on a rented pad, not as land. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/4210/Mobile-Home-Guide-PDF
- Wildwood Park is at 201 Cayer Street in Maillardville, a pad-rental, non-strata park described in the program record as family and all-age. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/4210/Mobile-Home-Guide-PDF

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Coquitlam has a dedicated mobile-home park zone. Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 lists RMH-1, Mobile Home Park, among the City's zones, and a home's siting must comply with that bylaw and the Building Bylaw. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1168/Part-3-Basic-Provisions-PDF--Introduction-to-how-Zoning-is-prescribed-and-enforced
- Moving or placing a home needs City permits and provincial documents. The City's Mobile Home Guide requires a building permit to move a home into or within a Coquitlam park, a current Manufactured Home Registry document, proof of Canadian Standards Association (CSA) Z240 compliance, the park manager's written approval, and a provincial transit permit under the Mobile Home Act. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/4210/Mobile-Home-Guide-PDF
- Recreational trailers are not homes here: the City guide states recreational-type trailers are not permitted to be used as a residence. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/4210/Mobile-Home-Guide-PDF
- Provincial park tenancy rules, including pad-rent caps, are in full in the surrey-mobile-homes note — see the surrey-mobile-homes note. The Coquitlam angle is section 7 below. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2025HMA0067-000786.htm

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** What do I actually own when I buy in a Coquitlam park? **A:** You own the home, not the land under it. A home on a rented pad is not land ownership — the buyer owns the manufactured home as a chattel and rents the pad from the park owner, under a Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act tenancy, so the purchase, financing and resale all run through the Manufactured Home Registry, not a land title. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/4210/Mobile-Home-Guide-PDF
- **Q:** How much can my pad rent go up? **A:** By the provincial cap plus a proportional amount, once a year. For an increase taking effect in 2026 the cap is 2.3 percent plus the proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees, and for 2027 it is 2.2 percent plus the proportional amount — percentages only, with no pad-rent dollar figure stated here. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2025HMA0067-000786.htm
- **Q:** Can I move a different home onto a Coquitlam pad? **A:** Only with permits and approvals in place first. The City requires a building permit, the Manufactured Home Registry document, CSA Z240 compliance evidence and the park manager's letter of approval, plus a demolition permit for the site the home leaves and a provincial transit permit for the move. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/4210/Mobile-Home-Guide-PDF
- **Q:** Do I pay Coquitlam property tax on a manufactured home? **A:** Yes, the City taxes the home even though the pad is rented. Coquitlam property tax for 2026 was due July 2, 2026, with a 5 percent penalty after July 2 and a further 5 percent after September 15, and the annual utility bill was due March 31 with penalties after March 31 and October 1. https://www.coquitlam.ca/568/Late-Payment-Penalties

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Hideaway Park carries a documented redevelopment tension: program material describes the Dewdney Trunk Road site both as a park community and as a transit-oriented redevelopment opportunity near the Evergreen Line — ask about park tenure security before subjects. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/4210/Mobile-Home-Guide-PDF
- Assignment of a pad tenancy needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on Residential Tenancy Branch Form 10, with silence after the set response window treated as consent in the program record — build that window into subject dates. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/4210/Mobile-Home-Guide-PDF
- A sale is only effective once registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, filed through a lawyer or notary with the signed bill of sale — a missing CSA certification label on the home is a financing and resale flag. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/4210/Mobile-Home-Guide-PDF
- Even Hideaway Park's postal code varies across listing sources for 4200 Dewdney Trunk Road, so verify the civic address against City or registry records, never a listing. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/4210/Mobile-Home-Guide-PDF
- Coquitlam utility penalties run on a different calendar from taxes: 5 percent after March 31 and again after October 1 for utilities, versus July 2 and September 15 for taxes. https://www.coquitlam.ca/568/Late-Payment-Penalties

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Coquitlam property tax was due July 2, 2026 for the January 1 to December 31, 2026 year, per the City's property tax page — the City's exact 2026 residential rate per $1,000 is in its linked 2026 Tax Rates document and is left in NOT CONFIRMED below because that document was not extracted in this pass. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Maillardville's two parks sit on Cayer Street and King Edward Street, in the southwest city near the Lougheed corridor, while Hideaway Park sits on Dewdney Trunk Road in Central Coquitlam — two separate transit grounds, so check the route for the specific park address. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/4210/Mobile-Home-Guide-PDF
- Coquitlam's transit is TransLink, including the Evergreen Extension of the Millennium Line — the program record places Hideaway Park in a shoulder station area near that line. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/4210/Mobile-Home-Guide-PDF

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check park conversion risk first, because it is the tenure risk unique to this home type. For a conversion of all or a significant part of a park, the program record states the landlord must hold all permits first, give 12 months' notice and pay compensation — confirm the current Residential Tenancy Branch wording before relying on any figure. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2025HMA0067-000786.htm
- Verify the home's Registry record and CSA label before financing, because the City will not permit a home without CSA Z240 compliance evidence and the sale is not effective until registered. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/4210/Mobile-Home-Guide-PDF
