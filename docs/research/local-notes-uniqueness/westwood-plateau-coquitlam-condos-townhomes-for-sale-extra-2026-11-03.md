## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Westwood Plateau a tower area or a townhouse area? **A:** A townhouse and house area. The City's Westwood Plateau Community Profile 2019 describes a master-planned residential neighbourhood on Eagle Mountain of 2,309 hectares with almost 19,775 people, two golf courses and an extensive trail system, and no City page read for this note names an apartment tower on the Plateau. A buyer who wants a concrete high-rise is shopping City Centre or Burquitlam; a buyer here is choosing among low-rise strata complexes and houses. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/214/2019-Westwood-Plateau-PDF?bidId=
- **Q:** Which schools sit on the Plateau itself? **A:** The Plateau schools named in School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) (SD43) listings include Panorama Heights Elementary at 1455 Johnson Street and Bramblewood Elementary at 2875 Panorama Drive. Being on the Plateau is not the same as being assigned: SD43 catchment is by permanent home address, so run the specific townhouse address in the SD43 School Locator before relying on either school. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- **Q:** Is there a SkyTrain station for these townhouses? **A:** No station is on the Plateau. The TransLink route that serves the townhouse streets is route 187, Coquitlam Central Station to Parkway, which runs Johnson Street, Plateau Boulevard and Paddock Drive to Parkway Boulevard — a bus ride down the hill to Coquitlam Central Station, which is served by the Millennium Line Evergreen Extension and the West Coast Express. http://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/199/tt187.pdf
- **Q:** What recreation is inside the neighbourhood, not down the hill? **A:** Golf and trails, per the City profile. The profile states green space abounds in Westwood Plateau, from neighbourhood parks to an extensive trail system that links to the backcountry trails on Eagle Mountain, and two golf courses; the City trail guide also maps the Westwood Plateau Golf Academy trail. That is the amenity base a townhouse buyer here uses daily, rather than the City Centre facilities. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/214/2019-Westwood-Plateau-PDF?bidId=

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Coquitlam Crunch Trail tops out in this neighbourhood: it climbs 2.2 kilometres on the British Columbia Hydro right-of-way with 894 steps and 242 metres of elevation gain, with access points at David Avenue and Bramble Park on Panorama Drive at the Plateau end. https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/coquitlamcrunchtrail-27
- Westwood Plateau Village at Parkway Boulevard and Johnson Street is the in-neighbourhood shopping node the City profile names, separate from Coquitlam Centre down the hill on Barnet Highway. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/214/2019-Westwood-Plateau-PDF?bidId=
- The City trail guide maps a named loop on the Plateau streets — the Westwood Plateau Trails and the Golf Academy trail around Plateau Boulevard, Panorama Drive and Johnson Street — which is the trail network directly behind many strata complexes. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1591/Explore-the-Great-Outdoors---Trail-Maps-and-Guide-PDF
- Panorama Heights Elementary at 1455 Johnson Street and Bramblewood Elementary at 2875 Panorama Drive put two elementary schools on the Plateau itself, which is not true of every Coquitlam bench neighbourhood. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/043
- The profile's figures are from Census Canada 2016 as printed in the 2019 profile — check current before quoting the 19,775 population or the 64% postsecondary figure from that document. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/214/2019-Westwood-Plateau-PDF?bidId=

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check the Interface Wildfire Risk Management boundary and unstable-slope layers on the City's Schedule B Environmental Development Permit Areas map for any complex backing onto Eagle Mountain greenbelt; the map shows the layers, not a lot-level conclusion. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/1527/Schedule-B---Environmental-Development-Permit-Areas-PDF?bidId=
- Check watercourse setbacks for complexes near the Plateau's creek corridors under the Watercourse Protection Development Permit rules: within 30 metres of top of bank or ravine bank in the plan area, land alteration needs that permit. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/316/Watercourse-Protection-Development-Permit-Application-Guide-PDF
- Check the strata's depreciation report for envelope and roof condition on 1990s–2000s low-rise complexes; no municipal page publishes building condition for a Plateau complex. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/214/2019-Westwood-Plateau-PDF?bidId=
