## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which elementary school is on these streets? **A:** Morley Elementary is the elementary school on Highgate's own streets. The Province lists Morley Elementary (K–7) at 7355 Morley St, and Edmonds Community Elementary at 7651 18th Ave nearby. School District 41 catchments are address-based, so check the exact address in the locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** Which secondary school serves Highgate? **A:** Byrne Creek Community Secondary is the secondary school in the Edmonds area. The Province lists it at 7777 18th St (8–12), while Cariboo Hill Secondary at 8580 16th Ave serves East Burnaby. Confirm a specific house in the locator before treating either as its catchment. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q:** How close is recreation here? **A:** Edmonds Community Centre is the recreation centre for these streets. The City lists it at 7433 Edmonds Street with a 6-lane pool, leisure pool with waterslides, 2 full-size gyms, a youth lounge, the Edmonds Centre for 55+, childminding, and a seniors lounge. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/edmonds-community-centre
- **Q:** Does Highgate get SkyTrain or only buses? **A:** A Highgate house gets both SkyTrain and buses. Edmonds Station is this centre's Expo Line station, and the City states town centres are served by SkyTrain plus high-frequency and local buses. The 119 also runs Kingsway between Metrotown and Edmonds stations. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
