## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A 55+ condo here is a strata lot governed by a strata age bylaw: under the Strata Property Act sections 123.1 and 123.2 a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older, and any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since November 24, 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- The bylaw restricts who may live in the unit, not who may own it, and Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse or partner, a live-in caregiver, legacy residents, and children in the defined care cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- White Rock condos sit under City of White Rock Zoning Bylaw 2024, No. 2506 and Official Community Plan (OCP) Bylaw 2017, No. 2220, while Surrey-side condos sit under Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000 — the same street can cross from one bylaw set to the other. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/695/Property-Tax-Rates-Estimator
- Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void in every strata in both cities since November 24, 2022, but a tenant must still meet the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata may still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a White Rock 55+ condo taxed and zoned by Surrey? **A:** No. White Rock is its own city, with Zoning Bylaw No. 2506 and its own annual property tax rates bylaw — Bylaw No. 2581 for 2026 — while a building a few blocks north in Sunnyside Park is in the City of Surrey under Surrey's bylaws and tax rates. Check which side of the municipal boundary the building's civic address falls on before comparing two otherwise similar units. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/695/Property-Tax-Rates-Estimator
- **Q:** Which 55+ buildings here are apartment condos rather than townhouses? **A:** The apartment form is the low-rise strata buildings named in section 2: Semiahmoo Estates at 2239 152 Street and 13965 16 Avenue on the Surrey side, and Monticello Place, Capistrano and Seawynd Manor on the White Rock side. The gated rancher complexes often shown in the same search are townhouses, and they are dealt with in the townhomes note for this area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** Can my younger spouse live with me in a 55+ condo? **A:** Yes, under the provincial exemptions. A younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement is exempt under Strata Property Regulation 7.01, as are live-in caregivers and legacy residents who were living in the unit when the bylaw passed. The exemption is provincial, so it applies in both Surrey and White Rock buildings, but the building's own registered bylaw wording is still read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q:** Where is hospital care for these buildings? **A:** Peace Arch Hospital at 15521 Russell Avenue, White Rock, is Fraser Health's hospital serving South Surrey and White Rock, and it sits in White Rock itself, south of most of the Sunnyside Park condo cluster. Surrey Memorial Hospital is the larger Surrey hospital, but for a buyer in these buildings the local hospital of record is Peace Arch. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/white-rock/peace-arch-hospital

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Surrey's total 2026 residential (Class 1) property tax rate is $3.42731 per $1,000 of assessed value, including $1.84251 municipal and $1.58480 for other authorities; Surrey-side buildings also pay the $300.00 Capital Parcel Tax for 2026. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/property-tax-rates
- White Rock sets its own rates under its Annual Property Tax Rates Bylaw, 2026, No. 2581, which the City's council summary states reflects an average property tax increase of 5.04% over 2025 rates; the City publishes a residential property tax estimator on its tax page. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/5115
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT) applies in Surrey: the Province lists the City of Surrey as a designated taxable area, so a Surrey-side owner must file the annual declaration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
