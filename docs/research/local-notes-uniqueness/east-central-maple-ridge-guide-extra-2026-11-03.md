# East Central Maple Ridge Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: east-central-maple-ridge-guide (East Central Maple Ridge, Maple Ridge; area guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- East Central is a board sub-area name, not a City plan name; its downtown west end is governed by the City's Town Centre Area Plan (Official Community Plan Chapter 10.4). https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town?bidId=
- The Town Centre plan area expanded in 2025 to include River Bend, and earlier plan text puts its edge at 124 Avenue north, 221 Street west and Burnett Street east. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/attachment-tcap-official-community-plan-amending-bylaw-no-8060-2025pdf
- Lougheed Highway and the Haney Bypass form the south edge; Dewdney Trunk Road runs east–west through the residential streets north and east of downtown — see the east-central-maple-ridge-houses-for-sale note. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town?bidId=

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock splits in two: freehold houses on the streets east and north of downtown, and strata condos in the Town Centre core. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town?bidId=
- The 2025 plan adds a Town Centre Core designation supporting higher density in a walkable downtown anchored by Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town?bidId=

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Secondary suites must not exceed 40% of the building's total gross floor area (Gross Floor Area) and must not sit in the Floodplain Area, under Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- Provincial small-scale multi-unit housing (Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing) rules allow up to three, four or six units on eligible single-family and duplex lots in Maple Ridge. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- Two Transit-Oriented Areas (Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station) fall inside the Town Centre plan area under Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town?bidId=

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is East Central an official neighbourhood? **A:** No — East Central is the board sub-area label used for listings, while the City plans this ground as the Town Centre plus the residential streets east of it. A buyer should check which City plan and zone covers the exact lot, because downtown parcels follow Town Centre designations, not house-lot rules. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town?bidId=
- **Q:** Which schools sit inside this area? **A:** Golden Ears Elementary at 23124 118 Avenue and Thomas Haney Secondary at 23000 116 Avenue are physically inside East Central. Catchments in School District 42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows) are address-based, so a house two streets away can map to a different elementary school; run the exact address in the district locator before subjects. https://www.sd42.ca/announcement/registration-summer-learning-starts/
- **Q:** Can a downtown house lot take more units? **A:** Possibly — provincial rules require Maple Ridge to allow up to three, four or six small-scale units on eligible single-family and duplex lots, with the count set by lot size and distance to frequent transit. Lots inside the Town Centre Core designation follow the plan's higher-density designations instead. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- **Q:** Does flood risk apply this far from the river? **A:** At the south edge, yes — the City's Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 8121-2026, approved July 28, 2026, designates floodplain areas, sets flood construction levels and setbacks, and regulates fill. It addresses Fraser River, Alouette River, steep-creek and groundwater flooding, so check Schedule A for the specific lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-climate-action-flood-resilience-environmental-protection-and-mckenney-creek

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The Town Centre was formerly called Central Haney and grew around the Port Haney station and steamboat landing. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/attachment-tcap-official-community-plan-amending-bylaw-no-8060-2025pdf
- Land-use designations here were consolidated from 11 to 7 in the November 25, 2025 plan update, so older plan maps no longer match. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town?bidId=
- A City land-use review covers Cliff Avenue, Selkirk Avenue and 119 Avenue properties between 221 Street and 222 Street, between this plan and the draft Lougheed Transit Corridor plan. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/landusereview
- Detached garden suites are capped at 140 square metres or 15% of lot area, whichever is less, outside the Agricultural Land Reserve. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/detached-garden-suites
- Golden Ears Elementary hosts the district's Late French Immersion intake, which is program-based, not catchment-based. https://www.sd42.ca/announcement/registration-summer-learning-starts/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Maple Ridge's 2026 total residential mill rate is 4.53578 per $1,000 of assessed value, including the municipal general rate of 2.86430 (2026 Property Tax Rate Summary). https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- The 2026 flat water fee is $859.25; flat-rate water and sewer appear on the tax notice, while metered customers are billed quarterly. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2026-property-tax-rates
- Maple Ridge is a designated taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so residential owners must declare. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Port Haney Station, at this area's southwest corner, is a West Coast Express stop between Mission City and Waterfront, Monday to Friday only, with no weekend or statutory-holiday service. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt997.pdf
- Haney Place Exchange is the local bus hub; the planned Langley–Haney Place Bus Rapid Transit line would run about 22 kilometres with 13 stations and peak service every 10 minutes. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/plans-and-projects/rapid-transit/bus-rapid-transit/brt-phase-3-engagement-summary.pdf
Read: 2026-11-03

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check any lot against Schedule A of Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 8121-2026: no habitable area may be built with its floor system below the Flood Construction Level. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
- The bylaw also designates land less than 1.5 metres above the natural boundary of an adjacent watercourse as floodplain — creek-side lots away from the Fraser are not exempt. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/maple-ridge-floodplain-management-bylaw-no-8121-2026pdf
