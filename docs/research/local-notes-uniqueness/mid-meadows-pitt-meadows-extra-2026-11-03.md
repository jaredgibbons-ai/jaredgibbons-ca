# Mid Meadows Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: mid-meadows-pitt-meadows (Mid Meadows, Pitt Meadows; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Mid Meadows is the Paragon sub-area name for the Bonson Road ground in the City of Pitt Meadows, south of the Canadian Pacific Kansas City (CPKC) rail line and Hammond Road and north of the Fraser River dike. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/harris-landing-pitt-river-greenway
- The City publishes no official neighbourhood boundary or official name for "Mid Meadows": its own labels for this ground are North Bonson, South Bonson and Osprey Village, and the City heritage register calls the Park Road / Bonson Road area "Somerset." https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp https://www.pittmeadows.ca/arts-culture-heritage/heritage
- From official facility addresses, the area runs along Bonson Road roughly between Harris Road in the west and the Maple Ridge boundary in the east, with Central Meadows (the Town Centre) to the northwest, West Meadows farm and airport lands to the west, and South Meadows lands toward the Fraser River. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Pitt Meadows city-wide tax and utility facts are written in full in the pitt-meadows-houses-for-sale note — see that note for §7; the rules below are the ones that land specifically on Bonson Road lots.
- Houses here sit under RS Large Lot Residential, R-3, R-4 and R-6 Urban Residential zones in Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011: RS has a minimum lot area of 0.2 hectares, R-3 allows up to 3 units per lot, R-4 up to 4, and R-6 up to five, or six where at least one unit meets the adaptable housing requirements in the British Columbia Building Code on lots close to frequent transit; RM-1 Multi-Family Residential 1 (townhouse) sites have a minimum lot area of 0.4 hectares. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- The 1,215 square metre figure in those zones is a subdivision minimum only and does not apply to development on existing lots, per the City's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) implementation update — the newer source governs over the older bylaw reading. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- A Bonson Road lot can have a suite or a garden suite, never both: one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit, 33 to 90 square metres, inside the same building where the zone permits it, and one garden suite per lot at 33 to 90 square metres in the zones listed in the City garden suite guide, including RS. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- The governing plan is the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022; no neighbourhood plan specific to the Bonson Road area was found in official sources. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/media/8989

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is Mid Meadows the same as Bonson Road? **A:** Yes, in local terms. "Mid Meadows" is the Paragon sub-area name; the City calls the same ground North Bonson, South Bonson and Osprey Village, it runs along Bonson Road south of the rail line to the Fraser River dike, and the heritage register's name for the Park Road / Bonson Road corner is Somerset. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/arts-culture-heritage/heritage
- **Q:** Which elementary school will my child attend — Davie Jones or Edith McDermott? **A:** It depends on the address, not on the street's side of the area alone. Both schools sit inside the sub-area — Davie Jones Elementary at 12030 Blakely Road and Edith McDermott Elementary at 12178 Bonson Road — and for the sample address 10932 Barnston View Road the district locator gives Davie Jones Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary, so run the exact address before relying on either. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q:** Can I add both a secondary suite and a garden suite? **A:** No. City rules allow one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit at 33 to 90 square metres inside the same building where the zone permits it, or one garden suite per lot in the listed zones including RS — and a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Q:** How many units can a house lot here hold under the new small-scale rules? **A:** Up to six in the right spot. Under the provincial rules applied citywide, lots of 280 square metres or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 square metres allow up to 4, and lots over 280 square metres within 400 metres of a frequent transit bus stop allow up to 6; the City's implementing Bylaw No. 2986 was adopted June 25, 2024. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Pitt Meadows Athletic Park at 11431 Bonson Road, beside Pitt Meadows Arena in South Bonson, has soccer fields, baseball and softball diamonds, and tennis and sport courts; Somerset Park is at the Edith McDermott school site, 12778 Bonson Road, and North Bonson Park is at the Davie Jones school site on Blakely Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/pitt-meadows-athletic-park
- Shoreline Park at 51 Bonson Road sits in Osprey Village on the Fraser River dike, Bonson Park is at 11546 Bonson Road, and the Harris Landing / Pitt River Regional Greenway at Harris Road and Fraser Way is a Metro Vancouver greenway with a first phase of 10.2 kilometres along the Fraser and Pitt River dikes. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/harris-landing-pitt-river-greenway
- The South Bonson Community Centre is at 10932 Barnston View Road, and Osprey Village shops and services sit at Fraser Way and Barnston View Road beside it, including Osprey Village Market as a grocery tenant in the City's commercial lands analysis. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/283
- The McMyn/Mason House was originally on the McMyn farm at the southwest corner of Park Road and Bonson Road, now the Somerset area, per the City heritage register. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/arts-culture-heritage/heritage
- Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK) is west of the area across Harris Road, and the City keeps a page for airport safety and noise concerns — the west-side streets are the ones closest to that flight activity. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/arts-culture-heritage/heritage
- No library branch is in this sub-area; Pitt Meadows Public Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library) is at 200-12099 Harris Road in Central Meadows. https://fvrl.bc.ca/locations/pitt-meadows-public-library

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 722 runs Meadowtown to Bonson / Fraser Way via Harris Road, Hammond Road, Blakely Road, 116B Avenue, Bonson Road and Fraser Way, stopping at Pitt Meadows Station and continuing as route 719; route 719 runs Meadowtown to Fraser Way via Maple Meadows Station, Hammond Road, Wildwood, Bonson Road and Fraser Way to the Barnston View roundabout, continuing as 722 — together they are the circular bus service through this sub-area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt722.pdf
- Route 701 runs along Hammond Road at the north edge to Coquitlam Central Station and Haney Place; no West Coast Express station is in this sub-area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt722.pdf
- Bonson Road is the main road through the area, with Hammond Road and the rail line at the north edge, Harris Road at the west edge, and Fraser Way and Barnston View Road in Osprey Village; Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and Golden Ears Way north of the area serve Maple Ridge, Coquitlam and, via the Golden Ears Bridge, Langley. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Floodplain is the first check here: the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95 percent of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the floodplain for the Fraser River and, to a smaller degree, the Pitt River, and the city is almost entirely protected by 60 kilometres of dikes — check the specific lot with the City rather than relying on the dike alone. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- This sub-area is in drainage Area 3 (Kennedy, including McKechnie) of the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District, the urban development area, and RS zoning requires buildings 15 metres from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status is a per-address check on Agricultural Land Commission maps; the City states 78 percent of Pitt Meadows land is in the ALR citywide, but ALR parcels inside the urban Bonson Road area were not confirmed. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- Rail and airport exposure are street-specific here: the CPKC rail line forms the north edge and Pitt Meadows Regional Airport sits west across Harris Road, so check the exact street for both before purchase. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain
