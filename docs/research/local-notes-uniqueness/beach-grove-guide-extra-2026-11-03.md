# Beach Grove Guide — Extra Local Notes
Page: beach-grove-guide (Beach Grove, Delta / Tsawwassen; Guide).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Beach Grove is a waterfront pocket inside Tsawwassen, in the City of Delta, on the Boundary Bay side east of 56 Street. Its own landmarks are Beach Grove Elementary at 5955 17A Avenue and Beach Grove Park at 6051 17A Avenue. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/beach-grove-park
- Delta-wide facts are in full in the tsawwassen-houses-for-sale note — see the tsawwassen-houses-for-sale note. This note covers only the Beach Grove pocket: the dike, the beach access points and the foreshore rules. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/beach-grove-park
- South Delta Secondary at 750 53 Street is the public secondary school for Tsawwassen addresses, with catchments set by School District 37 (Delta) by street address. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/student-registration/catchment-area-maps/

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The stock is mainly detached houses on low, flat lots behind the Boundary Bay dike, split between shoreline homes on the bay and interior streets around 17A Avenue. Delta's Official Community Plan (OCP), Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), applies to the pocket. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- Beach Grove Park and the elementary school share the 17A Avenue ground, so the interior streets orient to a school-and-park block rather than a commercial street. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/beach-grove-park

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Foreshore building levels are set by a hazard permit area, not by the dike alone. Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 in the OCP sets a minimum floor level of 2.9 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada (GSC) datum, a minimum land surface of 2.3 metres GSC around construction, and no building within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- A waterfront setback rule covers Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue. Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, section 6.2.3, sets that setback and allows a seawall to encroach into the minimum front setback, and the bylaw states no Beach Grove flood construction level. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- Added units follow Delta's small-scale rules, in full in the tsawwassen-houses-for-sale note — see the tsawwassen-houses-for-sale note. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a Beach Grove house protected from flooding? **A:** It sits behind a dike, but that is not the whole answer. The shore faces Boundary Bay behind the Boundary Bay dike maintained by the City as diking authority, and foreshore lots also fall under Hazard Permit Area SD3 floor and setback levels, so check the specific lot on DeltaMap and ask about any flood covenant on title. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q:** Which elementary school is in Beach Grove? **A:** Beach Grove Elementary, at 5955 17A Avenue, is the school inside the pocket. School District 37 catchments are address-based and its Tsawwassen area maps are the boundary documents, so confirm the exact address rather than assuming the in-pocket school is the assigned school. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/student-registration/catchment-area-maps/
- **Q:** What beach access does the pocket have? **A:** Beach access is along the Boundary Bay dike and foreshore, with Centennial Beach in Boundary Bay Regional Park to the south. Metro Vancouver runs that regional park at 570 Boundary Bay Road, with the Dyke Trail along the shore, so the pocket's beach routine runs south to the regional park facilities. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park
- **Q:** Why do beach access points close in winter? **A:** Winter storms drive the closures. City flood-preparedness material for Beach Grove and Boundary Bay Village describes temporary works each fall, with beach access points secured with concrete blocks or sandbags against southeasterly winter storms, and a berm where no seawalls exist. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/96845/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- The City carries out staged flood preparedness in Beach Grove every fall, a routine a summer buyer never sees. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/96845/
- Delta received a $2 million provincial grant in 2023 for Boundary Bay dike improvements, with a pilot raising a dike section by the airport between 72 Street and 88 Street — the same dike system that protects this shore. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-receives-provincial-grant-boundary-bay-dike
- The City operates over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure and 31 drainage pump stations across Delta, so drainage here is pumped infrastructure, not gravity alone. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Beach Grove Park sits beside the elementary school on 17A Avenue, so park and school traffic share one block. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/beach-grove-park
- A Beach Grove seawall repair was a named flood project: the Province funded replacement of a deteriorating, cracked seawall section in Beach Grove as part of its 2016 flood-mitigation work. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2016TRAN0151-001116
- Flat-rate utilities are city-wide in Delta, so the Beach Grove bill follows the Delta figure in section 7, not a pocket rate. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 601 is the Tsawwassen bus spine, and its Boundary Bay trips serve this shore. TransLink's timetable runs Boundary Bay trips via 12 Avenue, Boundary Bay Road, 3 Avenue and 1A Avenue, continuing via South Delta Exchange to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line). https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- The Dyke Trail is the walking and cycling link along the shore, running through Boundary Bay Regional Park at Centennial Beach south of the pocket. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check Hazard Permit Area SD3 for the specific lot before any build or addition, because its 2.9 metre floor level and 7.5 metre tidal setback can change a plan — the OCP sets the levels, but only a parcel check says whether the lot is inside the area. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- Check the dike and drainage position on DeltaMap for the parcel, and ask about flood covenants on title — the dike is maintained infrastructure with an active raising program, not a guarantee for a single lot. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-receives-provincial-grant-boundary-bay-dike
