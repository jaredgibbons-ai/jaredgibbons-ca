# Central Meadows Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: central-meadows-houses-for-sale (Central Meadows; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Central Meadows is the Harris Road town-centre pocket of Pitt Meadows, and the City publishes no official neighbourhood boundary for the name. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- The closest official planning name is the Official Community Plan (OCP) Municipal Town Centre, which embraces Harris Road and the North Lougheed Area and includes the Civic Centre. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/city-hall/hearings/2020-11-05_-2869_2020_north_lougheed_ocp_amendment_bylaw.pdf
- City Hall at 12007 Harris Road is the civic anchor of the pocket, on the main street the Town Centre is built around. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-hall/property-taxes/payment-options
- The working edges for a house buyer are Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) at the north, the Canadian Pacific Kansas City (CPKC) rail line along Hammond Road through the middle, and Harris Road as the main street; Mid Meadows lies to the south-east, North Meadows across Lougheed Highway, and Maple Ridge to the east. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Houses here are the detached homes on the side streets off Harris Road, inside the urban core the City concentrates growth in, surrounded by farmland outside the Urban Containment Boundary. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- City-wide Pitt Meadows housing and suite rules are in full in the central-meadows-condos-townhomes-for-sale note; the detached angle here is lot-by-lot, because the City publishes no typical lot size or build decade for Central Meadows houses. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- The City's 2025 amendment work states the direction plainly: most new homes are expected to be multi-unit dwellings, and the proportion of single-detached dwellings is expected to decline as they are gradually replaced by denser housing types. http://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026-06-23-3034%2C-2025-OCP-Amendment-Bylaw-Provincial-Housing-Legislation.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Flood and dike rules come first for a Central Meadows house, ahead of the zoning. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- About 78 percent of Pitt Meadows' land base is in the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) and over 85 percent lies in the Fraser, Pitt and Alouette river floodplains, so a house lot's dike, drainage and floodplain status is a City check, not an assumption. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- Suite potential is one suite, not two: the City's garden suite information sheet allows one secondary suite (33 to 90 square metres) or one garden suite per lot where the zone allows, never both — full city-wide suite and small-scale multi-unit housing rules are in the central-meadows-condos-townhomes-for-sale note. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- The Town Centre is also a Development Permit Area: Development Permit Area No. 8, Town Centre, applies in this pocket, so exterior and site changes on a house lot can need a development permit as well as a building permit. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools does a Central Meadows house feed into? **A:** Do not assume it from the neighbourhood name. The School District No. 42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows) locator result recorded for the sample address 12007 Harris Road is Pitt Meadows Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary. The district has no middle-school tier — elementary is kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary is Grades 8 to 12 — so run every address in the district locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q:** Why does the train matter so much here? **A:** The rail line splits the town centre, and Harris Road is the crossing. The City's Harris Road project aims to separate road and rail traffic at Harris Road to make commutes faster and safer and improve emergency response times, which tells a buyer the current at-grade crossing is a daily delay and a planning priority, not background noise. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/39
- **Q:** Is a Central Meadows house in a floodplain? **A:** Treat it as a yes until the lot check says otherwise. Over 85 percent of Pitt Meadows lies in the Fraser, Pitt and Alouette floodplains behind about 60 kilometres of dikes, and the City runs four dike drainage areas, including Area 3, Kennedy, for the urban core — confirm the specific lot's floodplain, dike and drainage details with the City. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Q:** Can I add a garden suite to a house lot? **A:** One suite only, and only where the zone allows it. The City allows one secondary suite of 33 to 90 square metres or one garden suite per lot, never both, and small-scale multi-unit housing unit counts of 3, 4 or 6 turn on the 280 square metre lot threshold and a frequent-transit test — see the central-meadows-condos-townhomes-for-sale note for the full rules. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Budget the drainage levy as its own line, because Pitt Meadows charges it separately from the general rate. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- The 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 sets a separate drainage rate in Schedule A alongside the general municipal rate, and taxes under that bylaw were due July 2, 2026, with 5 percent added after July 2, 2026 and a further 5 percent after August 1, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- Full Pitt Meadows tax, utility and Speculation and Vacancy Tax treatment is in the central-meadows-condos-townhomes-for-sale note; the detached-house point is that taxes and utilities arrive in one combined City bill. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/Tax-insert-2026.pdf

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Harris Road is the spine: the Town Centre was planned around high-frequency transit services along Harris Road and Lougheed Highway and walking distance to the West Coast Express station. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/city-hall/hearings/2020-11-05_-2869_2020_north_lougheed_ocp_amendment_bylaw.pdf
- Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) is the east-west highway at the north edge of the pocket, toward Maple Ridge east and the Pitt River Bridge west. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- Per-route bus numbers and West Coast Express times are operator facts: check TransLink for the current timetable rather than any listing sheet — no route-level timetable was confirmed for a Central Meadows house street in the sources read. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/39

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check flood, dike and drainage status for the specific lot with the City first: over 85 percent of the city is floodplain and the urban core drains through the Area 3 Kennedy dike drainage area. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) status per parcel: about 78 percent of the city's land base is ALR, and a house lot at the urban edge can sit next to, or partly in, farm land. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- Check rail noise and crossing delay for any lot near Hammond Road or Harris Road: the City's own grade-separation project exists because road and rail traffic currently cross at grade at Harris Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/39
- Check Development Permit Area No. 8, Town Centre, before planning exterior work: the Official Community Plan (OCP) applies a town-centre development permit area in this pocket. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
