# Nordel Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: nordel-north-delta-houses-for-sale (Nordel, North Delta; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Nordel is a North Delta neighbourhood in the City of Delta, centred on 84 Avenue and 112 Street. The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary, so check a lot on DeltaMap. https://www.delta.ca/about-delta/deltamap?viewer=DeltaMapExternal.viewer/
- The Nordel Social Heart is the name for that 84 Avenue and 112 Street core in Delta's 2024 Official Community Plan (OCP). It is Development Permit Area ND12. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009611?tool=survey_tool
- Burns Bog and the Delta Nature Reserve sit at Nordel's west edge, accessed at 10388 Nordel Court. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/delta-nature-reserve

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Detached houses on freehold lots are Nordel's core stock, with strata townhomes in Sunstone on Delsom Crescent at the south side. Polygon is Sunstone's developer. https://polyhomes.com/masterplan/sunstone/
- Lot size depends on the zone for that lot, not the neighbourhood name. Delta's small-lot zones run down to RS7 at a 335 square metre subdivision minimum — check current. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A suite, coach house or garden suite can be sought by building permit on many Nordel lots, with no rezoning. Delta adopted small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) changes on June 24, 2024 for single-detached and duplex lots city-wide. https://letstalk.delta.ca/ssmuh
- Houses inside ND12 need a development permit for form and character. The City lists ND12 as a required permit for multi-unit work in the Social Heart. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009689?tool=survey_tool
- Delta states its zoning bylaw controls use, density, size, height and siting for each property. Cite Delta zoning only as Delta's own pages state it. http://www.delta.ca/node/763

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Which schools serve a Nordel house? **A:** It depends on the exact address, so check the locator before you buy. For 11415 84 Avenue, School District No. 37 (SD37) gave Richardson Elementary, 11339 83 Avenue, for Kindergarten to Grade 7 and North Delta Secondary, 11447 82 Avenue, for Grades 8 to 12. Other Nordel streets can fall in a different catchment. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q:** Can I add a garden suite? **A:** Yes, in many cases, by building permit and without rezoning. Delta's June 24, 2024 SSMUH adoption lets owners apply for a secondary suite, detached garden suite or coach house on a single-detached lot. How many units fit depends on lot size and site rules, so confirm the address with the City first. https://letstalk.delta.ca/ssmuh
- **Q:** What is the Social Heart, and does it affect my lot? **A:** The Social Heart is Delta's planned focus for North Delta, centred on 84 Avenue and 112 Street. Lots inside Development Permit Area ND12 face form-and-character permit review for new multi-unit work. Lots a few blocks away do not carry that same ND12 step, so locate the lot against the ND12 map. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009611?tool=survey_tool
- **Q:** Is Nordel in a flood area? **A:** Nordel's houses sit inland, north of Burns Bog, not behind the Boundary Bay dike. Delta directs buyers to check flood protection, development permit areas and lot details lot by lot on DeltaMap. Do not assume a flood covenant or its absence from the neighbourhood name — search title and DeltaMap for the lot. https://www.delta.ca/about-delta/deltamap?viewer=DeltaMapExternal.viewer/

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Delta flat-rate utilities for a single-family home are $1,658 in 2026. The City bills flat-rate utilities at the end of February; metered homes are billed quarterly. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- Delta property taxes were due July 2, 2026, per $1,000 of net taxable value. The approved 2026 Financial Plan included a 2.9 percent increase for City operational services. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in the City of Delta. The Province lists Delta as a Metro Vancouver taxable area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
