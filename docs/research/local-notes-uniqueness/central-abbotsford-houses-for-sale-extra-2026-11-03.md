# Central Abbotsford Houses — Extra Local Notes
Page: central-abbotsford-houses-for-sale (Central Abbotsford; detached houses).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- Central Abbotsford is the older middle of the city, around Mill Lake, South Fraser Way, Bevan Avenue and Gladwin Road. Two adopted neighbourhood plans cover its core: the City Centre Neighbourhood Plan and the Historic Downtown Neighbourhood Plan.
- "Central Abbotsford" is not confirmed as an official City neighbourhood name. The City planning areas covering the central area are the City Centre Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2913-2019) and the Historic Downtown Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2920-2019). https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- Abbotsford Middle School, at 33231 Bevan Avenue, and Abbotsford Senior Secondary, at 33355 Bevan Avenue, sit side by side in the centre (addresses from the provincial school list read this session). https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/034
- Godson Elementary, at 33130 Bevan Avenue, is the central elementary on the same Bevan Avenue corridor. https://godson.abbyschools.ca/

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- Central detached houses are the city's older stock, on the established streets around Mill Lake and Bevan Avenue, rather than in a new subdivision. Centennial Park Elementary, at 2527 Gladwin Road, marks the Gladwin Road side of that older grid (address from the provincial school list). https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/034
- The Historic Downtown plan area holds the oldest house streets, next to the downtown commercial core. The City's Historic Downtown Neighbourhood Plan final event boards describe that plan area. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/Historic%20Downtown%20Neighbourhood%20Plan%20Final%20Event%20Boards.pdf
- New central homes arrive mainly as infill and added units on existing lots, under the zoning bylaw's section 140 — see section 3 and the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- A central house can sit in a heritage-sensitive downtown plan area. The Historic Downtown Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2920-2019) is the adopted plan for the downtown core, listed by the City with its other neighbourhood plans. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- A central house can also sit in the City Centre plan area, under the City Centre Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2913-2019). A rebuild or added unit has to fit the plan that covers the lot, so confirm which plan area the address is in before designing. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- The city-wide suite and small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rule is stated in full in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note — see the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note. Central lots are where that infill permission is most often used, because the lots are already serviced.
- The Gur Sikh Temple area carries a heritage listing: the temple on South Fraser Way is a National Historic Site of Canada, designated by Parks Canada. https://www.pc.gc.ca/culture/designation/lieu-site/temple-sikh-abbotsford-sikh-temple

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is a central Abbotsford house in a heritage area? **A:** Some are, near the historic downtown core, and the plan is the first check. The Historic Downtown Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2920-2019) covers the downtown area, and the City also keeps a Community Heritage Register. A heritage listing or a plan rule can limit demolition and exterior changes, so check the register and the plan for the exact address before planning a teardown. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- **Q:** Which middle school serves a Bevan Avenue house? **A:** Abbotsford Middle School is the central middle school, at 33231 Bevan Avenue, beside Abbotsford Senior Secondary at 33355 Bevan Avenue. Catchment still goes by exact street address in Abbotsford School District No. 34 (SD34), so run the address through the SD34 School Locator rather than assuming the nearest school is the catchment school. https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments
- **Q:** Can I add a suite to an older central house? **A:** A secondary suite is permitted in all single detached dwellings under the zoning bylaw, section 140.3.8 — see the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note for the full wording. Older central houses still need building-code, parking and servicing checks, and a house in the Historic Downtown plan area can face added plan rules, so confirm both before an offer. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Q:** Is a central house in the flood zone? **A:** Central Abbotsford is on the bench above the prairie floor, not behind the Sumas dike. The City's flood response pages cover the Sumas and Matsqui prairie areas; a Mill Lake–area lot is checked instead for creek and stormwater layers on City WebMap. Do not carry a prairie flood assumption into a central offer, and do not skip the parcel check either. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Central houses pay the city-wide Tax Rates Bylaw — Bylaw No. 3643-2025 for 2025, per $1,000 of taxable value; full mechanics in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2025-05/Bylaw%203643-2025%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202025.pdf
- A downtown-area house can also carry a Business Improvement Area (BIA) levy context: the City's 2025 tax report states Abbotsford Downtown Business Improvement Area taxes are calculated as part of the annual tax rates work. That levy applies to the BIA's own properties, so check the tax notice for the specific lot rather than assuming it. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58124
- Central houses are on the standard City billing cycle: bi-monthly water and sewer, with the 5 percent on-time discount — see the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/current-utility-bill
- 2026 city-wide utility rates apply from March 1, 2026: water $1.46 per cubic metre, sewer $1.35 per cubic metre. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/water-sewer-rates
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies in Abbotsford, including the centre. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Check heritage status before planning work on a downtown-edge house. The City keeps a Community Heritage Register, and the Historic Downtown plan adds its own rules — see section 3. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- Check creek and stormwater layers around Mill Lake on City WebMap; a central lot is not in the prairie dike area, but creek setbacks can still apply. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- Check the Natural Environment Development Permit (NEDP) guide where a central lot touches a watercourse. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-09/NEDP%20Application%20Guide_Appendix%20A_August%202026%20%5BFinal%5D.pdf
- Check older-house servicing on the title and permit record, not on the listing: central houses predate current code, and the City WebMap does not show permit history.
