# North Maple Ridge — Questions for Jared
Area: North Maple Ridge (area guide).

## 5 questions only Jared can answer
1. In your own North Maple Ridge deals, what do buyers pay up for — a Silver Valley lot north of the Alouette River, a Yennadon acreage, or a smaller RS-1 lot closer to 232 Street and Dewdney Trunk Road?
2. Who is actually buying in the north right now in your deals — acreage and horse buyers, families trading up from the Town Centre, or buyers leaving Pitt Meadows and Port Coquitlam for land — and does that differ between Silver Valley and Yennadon?
3. What sells fast and what sits in North Maple Ridge: do serviced acreages near 232 Street move first while remote 256 Street or Webster's Corners properties wait, and how does the updated Silver Valley Area Plan change what buyers ask for?
4. What surprises buyers most once they are under contract in the north — the floodplain line on an Alouette River lot and the new Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 8121-2026, a suite that cannot legally sit in the floodplain, ALR status on a Yennadon parcel, or well and septic findings on an acreage?
5. Tell one North Maple Ridge deal story: a sale, a near-miss, or a problem you solved on an acreage or river lot (Silver Valley, Yennadon, Thornhill or Webster's Corners side) that shows a buyer or seller something no listing could.
