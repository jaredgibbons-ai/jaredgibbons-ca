# East Burnaby — Questions for Jared

For Jared Gibbons to answer by voice memo. Do not answer these from research — only Jared's deal experience counts.

## Questions
1. When a buyer says "East Burnaby," which streets do they actually mean — the Second Street side, Cariboo-Armstrong, or anywhere east of Edmonds?
2. Does the New Westminster border help or hurt pricing on the 10th Avenue side of East Burnaby?
3. How much does a Robert Burnaby Park or ravine edge add to a house, against the setback and habitat questions it raises?
4. What sells fast and what sits on the east side — and does the Edmonds Station walk change that?
5. Tell one East Burnaby deal story: a sale where the park, the border or a school catchment changed the result.
