# Lower Landing (London Landing, Steveston) Neighbourhood — Extra Local Notes
Page: lower-landing (London Landing, Steveston, Richmond; neighbourhood).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES
- No City of Richmond source uses the name "Lower Landing"; the official name for this ground is London Landing, and this note is written on that basis. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/ZMU2033849.pdf
- London Landing is the waterfront pocket at the east edge of Steveston in the City of Richmond, at the south foot of No. 2 Road where London Road and Dyke Road meet the South Arm of the Fraser River. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=136
- The City publishes no boundary for a London Landing neighbourhood; the sourced anchors are London's Wharf Park at 6062 Dyke Road, London Road, Princess Street and the No. 2 Road addresses now in rezoning. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=136
- Steveston Village lies west along the South Arm waterfront, Steveston North sits across Steveston Highway, and the pocket sits inside the Steveston planning area under the Steveston Area Plan, Schedule 2.4 of the Official Community Plan (OCP). https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails-cycling/trails-map/steveston.htm
- The name is a settler-family landing name: in 1881 brothers Charles and William London bought 200 acres at the south end of No. 2 Road, at what became known as London's Landing. https://stevestonheritage.ca/about/
- No earlier Richmond sibling exists in this program, so Richmond and London Landing facts are carried in full in this note.

## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK
- The pocket's core product is strata: low- and mid-rise mixed-use buildings under the site-specific London Landing (Steveston) zones, townhouses, and detached houses in Single Detached (RS1 and RS2) zones at the edges. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/ZMU2033849.pdf
- The Pier at London Landing, 6160 London Road, is the named waterfront building, an Oris Development project named in City council material. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=41751
- Harbour Walk at 13040 No. 2 Road is the complex on the site rezoned in 2014 for a four-storey building of about 55 residential units with about 348.5 square metres of commercial space. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/agendafiles/Public_Hearing_5-20-2014.pdf
- London Landing townhouses at 13200 Princess Street were built by London Landing Development Corp. at Steveston's east edge. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/agendas/archives/dpp/2002/103002_minutes.htm
- Detached lot minimums at the edges vary by subzone in Zoning Bylaw 8500 Section 8.1.8 — for example RS1/B at 360.0 square metres, RS1/E at 550.0, RS1/F at 828.0 and RS1/K at 315.0 square metres. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Zoning Bylaw 8500 Section 20.20 is the site-specific Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU20) – London Landing (Steveston) zone, created by Bylaw 8818 on September 24, 2012, to provide for commercial, residential and industrial uses in the Steveston area. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/ZMU2033849.pdf
- ZMU20 caps density at a floor area ratio (FAR) of 1.2 plus 0.1, lot coverage at 76 percent and height at 21.0 metres. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/ZMU2033849.pdf
- A second site-specific zone, Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU24) – London Landing (Steveston), was created for 13040 No. 2 Road by Bylaw 9094, with a public hearing on May 20, 2014. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/agendafiles/Public_Hearing_5-20-2014.pdf
- Bylaw 8500 Part C also carries site-specific London Landing single-detached and two-unit zones, including Single Detached Heritage zones ZS1 and ZS11 and Heritage Two-Unit Dwelling zones ZD1 and ZD4 — heritage wording in a zone name is a rule, not a style note. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/bylaws/zoning-bylaw-8500/zoning8500PartC.htm
- Detached houses near the pocket sit in RS1 and RS2 zones, with secondary suite rules in Section 5.4 and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL added under provincial Bill 44 legislation. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- New construction is subject to flood construction levels and dike setbacks under the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is "Lower Landing" a real Richmond place name? **A:** No City of Richmond source read uses it. The official name for this Steveston waterfront pocket is London Landing: it appears in Zoning Bylaw 8500 as the "London Landing (Steveston)" zones and on the ground at London's Wharf Park, 6062 Dyke Road. Search and title work should use London Landing, with the slug's wording treated as a label, not a place. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/ZMU2033849.pdf
- **Q:** What homes are actually in London Landing? **A:** Strata-led waterfront product. The named anchors are The Pier at London Landing at 6160 London Road, Harbour Walk at 13040 No. 2 Road — a four-storey, roughly 55-unit mixed-use building approved in 2014 — and townhouses at 13200 Princess Street, with detached houses in RS1 and RS2 zones at the pocket's edges. There is no detached subdivision at the core. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/agendafiles/Public_Hearing_5-20-2014.pdf
- **Q:** Which schools serve a London Landing address? **A:** That is address-specific and not confirmed for this pocket. School District No. 38 (Richmond) catchments are address-based, with elementary at Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary at Grades 8 to 12 and no middle-school tier. Schools in the wider Steveston district include Lord Byng Elementary, Steves Elementary, Westwind Elementary and R.A. McMath Secondary — run the exact address in the district locator before relying on any of them. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Q:** What is being proposed on No. 2 Road? **A:** A five- and six-storey residential and commercial mixed-use project. City staff reported on July 8, 2026 that Jim Pattison Developments' application RZ 25-009451 would rezone 13131, 13111, 13031, 12931 and 12771 No. 2 Road from Light Industrial (IL) to a new Residential / Limited Commercial (ZMU63) – London Landing (Steveston) zone. It is an application in process, not an approval. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Application_by_Jim_Pattison_Developments_for_Rezoning_at_13131__13111__1303179429.pdf
- **Q:** What waterfront recreation is at the pocket's front door? **A:** London's Wharf Park at 6062 Dyke Road: just under 4 acres with the No. 2 Road Pier, river views, a playground, open lawn and trail connections to the South Arm Dyke and Steveston Greenways networks. The City's Greenways material also describes the historic London's Landing fishing pier area, with a fishing float, picnic tables, washrooms and interpretation signage. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=136

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- Richmond's 2026 residential rate is $1.94985 per $1,000 of assessed value for the total municipal portion, and $3.53838 per $1,000 for the total tax rate including school and other levies. http://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/about.htm
- City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5 percent penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5 percent on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- Homes in the pocket are on City water and sewer services; a 2026 utility dollar figure for the pocket was not confirmed on a City source — see NOT CONFIRMED.

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Route 401 (One Road) links Steveston Exchange on Chatham Street to Richmond–Brighouse Station via No. 1 Road, the pocket's Canada Line connection. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt401.pdf
- No. 2 Road runs north from the wharf to Steveston Highway, London Road and Dyke Road run along the waterfront, and Steveston Highway runs east to Highway 99. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/CNCL_062209_Bylaw_840323090.pdf
- The South Arm Dyke Trail and the Steveston Greenways give the pocket its walking and cycling link west into Steveston Village. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails-cycling/trails-map/steveston.htm

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Richmond is islands in the Fraser River floodplain at an average of 1 metre above sea level, protected by 49 kilometres of dikes and 39 drainage pump stations designed for a 1:500 flood event, with dikes being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- Confirm the flood construction level and dike setback for the specific lot under Bylaw 8204 before pricing in a rebuild or addition — this pocket sits directly behind the South Arm dike. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- In mixed-use ZMU buildings, read the strata depreciation report, contingency fund and commercial-component cost sharing early, because the zone permits residential over commercial and industrial uses; British Columbia strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- The Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area sits west of this pocket and carries Heritage Alteration Permit requirements under the Steveston Area Plan; whether any London Landing parcel sits inside its boundary was not confirmed — check the parcel. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
