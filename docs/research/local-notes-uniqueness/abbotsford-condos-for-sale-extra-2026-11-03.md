## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE
- Rental-restriction bylaws are unenforceable: since November 24, 2022 no strata may restrict long-term rentals, even where a directory still records "rentals not allowed" (Regency Park, Lakewood Gardens). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- The only enforceable age bylaw is 55-plus (55+), under Strata Property Act sections 123.1 and 123.2; any lower stated age is invalid. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs; the 2025 updates raised the Mid Rise Apartment Zone (RMM) to 2.5 Floor Space Ratio and stopped counting hallways, elevators and stairwells as floor area. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Zoning%20Bylaw%20Updates%20Overview%202025.pdf
- Stratas with five or more lots must hold a depreciation report every five years (no deferral since July 1, 2024) and contribute at least 10 percent of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund (CRF). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
**Q:** A directory says my Gladwin Road building bans rentals and pets. What binds me?
**A:** The rental ban binds no one: long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022. A pet bylaw still binds, and a registered 55+ bylaw still governs who may live in the unit. The registered bylaws, checked against the Form B Information Certificate, decide — not the directory. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

**Q:** How exposed am I to a special levy in a 1989–1993 building?
**A:** Read the depreciation report before the strata fee. Lakewood Gardens (1989), Regency Park (1990) and Town Centre Tower (1993) sit inside the first mandatory five-year cycle; the report's 30-year projection and three CRF funding models show whether roofs and parkades are funded by fees or levies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

**Q:** Can a first-time buyer get the new federal GST rebate on a new condo?
**A:** Yes, if eligible and the agreement was signed on or after March 20, 2025: the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate returns 100 percent of the 5 percent GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million. The Canada Revenue Agency's News No. 122 records Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

**Q:** Which schools serve a condo on Gladwin Road?
**A:** School District No. 34 catchments are address-based. Recorded locator runs for the Bevan Avenue corridor return Godson Elementary, 33130 Bevan Avenue, then Abbotsford Middle, 33231 Bevan Avenue, then Abbotsford Senior Secondary, 33355 Bevan Avenue; the Ware Street side returns Dormick Park Elementary instead — run the exact address. https://godson.abbyschools.ca/

**Q:** What is the strata insurance deductible risk?
**A:** A strata's water-damage deductible can be charged back to the owner whose unit caused the loss. The amount sits in the strata's insurance summary inside the document package, not the listing; buyers commonly match their own unit policy limit to that figure before removing subjects. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE
- 2026 property tax rates are set by Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026, per $1,000 of taxable value; a strata unit is taxed on its own assessed value. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf
- Water and sewer are metered: from March 1, 2026, water is $1.46 per cubic metre and sewer $1.35; whether strata fees include them is stated on the Form B. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/water-sewer-rates
- Abbotsford is a listed taxable area for the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax; every residential owner must declare annually. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- New condos carry 5 percent Goods and Services Tax (GST); resales do not. The Newly Built Home Exemption removes Property Transfer Tax in full up to $1,100,000, partially to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Downtown condos anchor on Bourquin Exchange, where BC Transit routes 1, 2, 4, 5, 12, 17, 21 and 31 meet; route 5 (Clearbrook / Bourquin via Hospital) is the Gladwin Road hospital link. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- Route 66, the Fraser Valley Express, connects Abbotsford with Langley and Burnaby (Lougheed Station) via Highway 1 and expanded service on January 4, 2026. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html
- Highstreet Exchange construction began January 4, 2026; routes 1, 2 and 66 were detoured to a temporary exchange on Cardinal Avenue. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the depreciation report on any pre-2000 building: the first mandatory cycle lands on the Gladwin Road and Simon Avenue stock's roof, piping and parkade years. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Check the Form B for approved special levies still payable and the CRF balance; the strata must provide it within seven days, maximum $35 plus copying. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- Flood geography matters at the city's edges: Sumas Prairie flooded in December 2025 (485 properties under Evacuation Order), and the City states a Matsqui Prairie flood would be worse. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
