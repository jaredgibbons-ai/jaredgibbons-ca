- South Meadows is the board's name; the City calls this pocket South Bonson, the Bonson Road corridor south of Hammond Road and the Canadian Pacific Kansas City rail tracks to the Fraser River. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/council-supports-business-park-land-use

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK
- **Q:** Is there a school inside South Meadows? **A:** No public school building sits inside this pocket. The closest elementary schools are Edith McDermott Elementary, 12178 Bonson Road, and Pitt Meadows Elementary, 11941 Harris Road, both north of the rail tracks, and Pitt Meadows Secondary, 19438 116B Avenue, is the city's only public secondary school. Catchment is by address in School District No. 42, which has no middle tier. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- **Q:** Which catchment did a South Bonson address return? **A:** For the sample address 10932 Barnston View Road — the South Bonson Community Centre — the district locator returned Davie Jones Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary, with French Immersion options at Pitt Meadows Elementary, Eric Langton Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary. That result is for that address only; other Bonson Road addresses can return a different elementary school, so run each address. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q:** What is Osprey Village? **A:** Osprey Village is the commercial and community node at Fraser Way and Barnston View Road, holding the South Bonson Community Centre and the Pitt Meadows Art Gallery at 10932 Barnston View Road, plus the village shops the City's commercial lands analysis names, including Osprey Village Market. It is the services centre for the houses south of the rail tracks. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- **Q:** Is this area in a floodplain? **A:** Yes — the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of Pitt Meadows lies within the Fraser River and Pitt River floodplain, protected by about 60 kilometres of dikes. This pocket sits behind the Fraser River dike in drainage Area 3 of the local diking district, and the dike itself carries Shoreline Park and the dyke-top trails, so flood and dike checks belong in every purchase here. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW
- Shoreline Park, 51 Bonson Road, sits on the Fraser River dike, and the dyke-top trails run along the river through the pocket. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- Bonson Park, 11546 Bonson Road, holds a disc golf course, and Pitt Meadows Athletic Park, 11431 Bonson Road, holds soccer fields and ball diamonds, with the community garden at 11410 Bonson Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- The Pitt Meadows Arena complex, with three sheets of ice, is in this pocket, while the Pitt Meadows Family Recreation Centre, 12027 Harris Road, is north of it. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK) is west of the pocket across Harris Road, and the City keeps a page for airport safety and noise concerns; no official airport noise-zone map covering this pocket was confirmed. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport

## 8. GETTING AROUND
- Routes 719 and 722 run as a combined circular service through Bonson Road and Fraser Way: 719 runs Meadowtown to Fraser Way via Maple Meadows Station, Hammond Road and Bonson Road, continuing as 722, which returns via Harris Road and Hammond Road. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt719.pdf
- Route 701 runs along Hammond Road at the pocket's north edge to Coquitlam Central Station and Haney Place via Pitt Meadows Station, and the West Coast Express runs weekday trains from Pitt Meadows Station, north of the pocket. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE
- Start with the dike and drainage file for the specific lot: the city is almost entirely protected by 60 kilometres of dikes plus ditches, pump stations and flood boxes, and this pocket drains in Area 3 (Kennedy, including McKechnie), discharging to the Alouette, Pitt and Fraser Rivers. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- Check Agricultural Land Reserve status per parcel at the pocket's edges: the City states 78% of Pitt Meadows is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, even though the built South Bonson pocket itself is developed village land. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
