# Vedder Mountain — Questions for Jared

Five questions only Jared can answer, for a voice memo. Muse does not answer these.

## Questions

1. When a Vedder Mountain Road property comes up, how do buyers react to the split between City of Chilliwack lots on the west end and FVRD Electoral Area H lots toward Cultus Lake — does the jurisdiction change what they will pay or how fast it sells?
2. Who buys on Vedder Mountain in your deals — trail riders who want the mountain bike network at the door, acreage buyers leaving Sardis, or people who work in Chilliwack and want out of the subdivisions? What are they leaving behind?
3. What surprises buyers after they move in — well and septic servicing, the firearms use still permitted on the Crown land parcel, summer Cultus Lake traffic on Vedder Mountain Road, or having only two road exits in an evacuation?
4. What sells fast and what sits — the flatter acreages near Yarrow, or the steep view lots higher on the road? Where do buyers draw the line on slope and driveway access?
5. One story: a Vedder Mountain deal that taught you something — a lot you now recommend or would steer a buyer away from, and why.
