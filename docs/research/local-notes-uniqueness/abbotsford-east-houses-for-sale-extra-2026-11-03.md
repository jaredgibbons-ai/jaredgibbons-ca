## 2. THE HOUSING STOCK

- Detached houses dominate the east side, and the zoning floor for a new interior lot is 540 m² in the RS3 zone and 420 m² in the RS5 zone, as a City council report applying Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 states. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/
- The east side's newer detached product sits in the McKee plan area, and the City's McKee Neighbourhood Plan covers approximately 842 hectares on Sumas Mountain, described as a landscape of steep forested slopes and headwater tributaries. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2023-07/ADOPTED%203400-2023%20Official%20Community%20Plan%20Amendment%20Bylaw%20No.%20021.pdf
- Older established streets run along Old Yale Road and Old Clayburn Road, and the route 9 schedule reads the east side through McMillan Road at Old Yale Road, McKee Drive at McKee Road, and Old Clayburn Road at Sandy Hill Road. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=9

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT BUYERS HERE

- A secondary suite must sit inside the principal dwelling and be registered with the City, and registration confirms the suite complies with the BC Building Code and the Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw, 2014. https://abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites
- Suite eligibility has street-level conditions, and City policy criteria applied in council reports require a lot of at least 400 m², at least 12.0 m of frontage, a Collector or Local road, and no location on a cul-de-sac bulb. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/
- Slope lots carry a hazard rule, and a City council report for an east-side lot records a geotechnical covenant on title requiring no buildings within 1.5 m of the crest of the slope at the rear of the property. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/
- City-wide zoning, small-scale multi-unit housing and suite size rules are treated in full in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note. (See the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note.)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

**Q:** Can I build a suite on a hillside lot in McKee?
**A:** A suite must be inside the principal dwelling and registered with the City, and a slope lot may also carry a geotechnical covenant. The City's suites page requires registration confirming BC Building Code and Zoning Bylaw compliance, and a City report for an east-side lot records a covenant barring buildings within 1.5 m of the slope crest at the rear. Confirm both before planning a suite on a slope lot. https://abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/

**Q:** Which secondary school will my child attend?
**A:** The catchment school is set by your home address, not by the nearest school building. SD34 states that students attend the neighbourhood catchment school determined by their primary home address, and it recommends using its School Locator tool and catchment maps together to confirm a boundary. Non-catchment requests are also restricted at Robert Bateman Secondary and Yale Secondary and all their feeder schools. https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments

**Q:** Does flooding affect houses at the south edge near Whatcom Road?
**A:** Yes — the low land toward the Sumas River is a mapped floodproofing area. Zoning Bylaw section 140.7 designates Area "G" on Schedule "E" as the Sumas River floodplain, where full floodproofing provisions apply at 11.1 m GSC, 4.0 m above the natural boundary of the watercourse and 1.2 m above ground, with no reduced-floodproofing option. Check the lot against Schedule "E" before offering. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/42052/

**Q:** What sets a McKee house apart in the rules?
**A:** The McKee Neighbourhood Plan is the governing plan for the mountainside, and it was adopted as Official Community Plan Amendment Bylaw No. 3400-2023 on June 26, 2023, adding the plan as Schedule "E" of the Official Community Plan. The plan area covers approximately 842 hectares on Sumas Mountain, so a McKee house is bought under a neighbourhood plan, not only the city-wide bylaw. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2023-07/ADOPTED%203400-2023%20Official%20Community%20Plan%20Amendment%20Bylaw%20No.%20021.pdf

**Q:** How do I get to central Abbotsford without a car?
**A:** Route 9 runs the east side between Montrose Exchange and McKee via McMillan Road and Old Yale Road, with stops at McMillan Road at Old Yale Road, McKee Drive at McKee Road, and Old Clayburn Road at Sandy Hill Road, and some trips continue to Bourquin Exchange. That loop is the east side's local link to the city centre. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=9

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- Non-catchment requests are restricted at Robert Bateman Secondary and Yale Secondary and all their feeder schools, and SD34 states it has restricted those requests to reduce enrolment over time — an east-side address outside the Bateman or Yale catchment is not a reliable route into those schools. https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments
- A house near a creek carries a fixed setback, and Zoning Bylaw section 140.7 sets a 30.0 m minimum setback from Clayburn Creek outside the Fraser River floodplain to the east of the intersection of Old Clayburn Road with Clayburn-Straiton Road. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/42052/
- Owners are billed annually for solid waste collection and secondary suites on the property tax notice, and the City states registered owners are billed for property taxes, solid waste collection, secondary suites and other charges in one annual billing. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 7. COSTS AND TAXES HERE

- The full city tax treatment is carried in the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note; the east-side angle is the south-edge levy. (See the abbotsford-houses-for-sale note.)
- Abbotsford's 2026 residential (Class 1) municipal rates are 1.08284 per $1,000 for police and 1.78296 per $1,000 for general and debt servicing under Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026, before regional and library levies. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf
- Houses inside the Sumas Prairie Dyking and Drainage Area pay an additional residential rate, set for 2026 at 0.58353 per $1,000 for Class 1 residential properties, so a south-edge house can carry a dyking line that a hillside house does not. https://abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%203727-2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Bylaw%202026%20%28Adopted%20%2B%20Signed%29%202026%2005%2012.pdf
- Metered water costs $1.46 per cubic metre and sewer $1.35 per cubic metre for residential customers, effective March 1, 2026, after a Council-approved 4% increase to each rate in January 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/utility-billing/water-sewer-rates
- The Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies here, because the Province's taxable-areas list names the City of Abbotsford. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

- Highway 1 runs along the south side of the east side, and route 9 links the mountainside to Montrose Exchange and, on some trips, Bourquin Exchange, via McMillan Road and Old Yale Road. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=9
- Whatcom Road is the east side's highway connection, and the route 9 schedule's east-side stops run from McMillan Road at Old Yale Road through McKee Drive at McKee Road to Old Clayburn Road at Sandy Hill Road. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=9
- The east side's regional trips run by bus through the exchanges, and route 9's schedule shows Bourquin Exchange as the end point of its through trips from Montrose Exchange. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=9

## 9. RISKS AND DUE DILIGENCE

- Check any lot near the Sumas River against floodproofing Area "G" before building or adding a suite, because Zoning Bylaw section 140.7 applies full floodproofing provisions there at 11.1 m GSC with no reduced option. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/42052/
- Check slope lots for a geotechnical covenant, because a City report for an east-side lot records a covenant barring buildings within 1.5 m of the slope crest at the rear of the property. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/
- Check the creek setback for lots near Clayburn Creek, because Zoning Bylaw section 140.7 sets a 30.0 m minimum setback from that creek outside the Fraser River floodplain east of Old Clayburn Road and Clayburn-Straiton Road. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/42052/
- Confirm a suite is registered, not merely finished, because the City requires registration and a registered suite is the one confirmed against the BC Building Code and the Zoning Bylaw. https://abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites
