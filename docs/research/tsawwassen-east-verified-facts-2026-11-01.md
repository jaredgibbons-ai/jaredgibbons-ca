## 1. Identity

- Municipality: City of Delta, BC. Tsawwassen East is the Boundary Bay side of the Tsawwassen community, east of 56 Street, taking in Beach Grove and the Boundary Bay shore, planned under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — the current OCP consolidated the former area plans into a single Land Use Plan (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950) (https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf, read 2026-11-01).

- Nearby areas: Tsawwassen Central to the west, Boundary Beach and Point Roberts (USA) to the south, Ladner to the north (https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park, read 2026-11-01).
- Search / alternate names: "Boundary Bay" (the name used by Metro Vancouver for Boundary Bay Regional Park and by TransLink for the 601's Boundary Bay routing); "Beach Grove" for the waterfront pocket inside the east side; "South Delta" generally (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf, read 2026-11-01).

## 5. Housing profile

- Typical home types: Single detached and duplex houses; the Beach Grove waterfront pocket is zoned RD2, a zone the bylaw states is for waterfront lots in Beach Grove (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf, read 2026-11-01).

## 6. Buying and selling tips

- On any Beach Grove or Boundary Bay-fronting lot, check whether Development Permit Area SD3 applies to the specific parcel: SD3 sets a 2.9 m GSC datum floor level, a 2.3 m minimum land elevation around construction, and a 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of a tidal area — whether a given lot falls inside SD3 needs a DeltaMap check for that lot (https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf, read 2026-11-01).
- Waterfront buyers should understand the foreshore is provincial Crown land: docks and moorage sit on tenure by lease or licence via FrontCounter BC, under General Permission or Specific Permission — not on land the seller owns outright (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage, read 2026-11-01).
- Read the Bylaw No. 7600 waterfront setback rule (s. 6.2.3) before planning additions on Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue — a seawall may encroach into the minimum front setback, and the bylaw states no Beach Grove flood construction level (https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf, read 2026-11-01).
- Confirm the parcel's zone before assuming duplex or subdivision potential: 1728 Beach Grove Road is confirmed RD2 with a 4,000 m² subdivision minimum, but zoning is assigned parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps (https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf, read 2026-11-01).
- Verify the exact SD37 catchment for the address — the sample address on Boundary Bay Road maps to South Park Elementary and South Delta Secondary, but catchments are address-based (https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/, read 2026-11-01).

## 7. FAQ

Q1: Is Tsawwassen East an official City of Delta neighbourhood?
A: No. It is a Paragon sub-area name for the Boundary Bay side of Tsawwassen, planned under the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), and no official boundary exists for the name (https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf, read 2026-11-01).

Q2: What beach and trails are on the east side?
A: Boundary Bay Regional Park and Centennial Beach (Metro Vancouver) are on Boundary Bay Road, with the Dyke Trail and the Savannah and Raptor trails, and Cammidge House in the park (https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park, read 2026-11-01).

Q3: Which schools serve the east side?
A: Catchments are address-based in SD37. For the sample address 570 Boundary Bay Rd, the locator result is South Park Elementary (K–7) and South Delta Secondary (8–12), with no middle-school category (https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/, read 2026-11-01).

Q4: What flood rules apply to a waterfront home in Beach Grove?
A: Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 sets a 2.9 m Geodetic Survey of Canada datum minimum floor level and a 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of a tidal area for the Boundary Bay foreshore, and the shore sits behind the Boundary Bay dike maintained by the City of Delta (https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf, read 2026-11-01).

Q5: What bus serves Boundary Bay?
A: TransLink route 601 runs Boundary Bay trips via 12 Avenue, Boundary Bay Road, 3 Avenue and 67 Street to 1A Avenue, continuing through Ladner Exchange to Bridgeport Station (https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf, read 2026-11-01).
