## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **The $1.5 million line is also the federal insured-mortgage line.** CMHC states mortgage loan insurance is required when a buyer makes a down payment of less than 20%; for homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999 the minimum down payment is 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% on any amount over $500,000; homes $1,500,000 or more require a minimum 20% down payment and mortgage loan insurance is not available. CMHC puts premium rates at 0.6% to 4.5% of the total mortgage amount, payable in full at purchase or added to the mortgage. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **BC property transfer tax across this band:** PTT is payable on a purchase registered at the Land Title Office unless an exemption applies, based on fair market value on the day of registration, at 1% up to and including $200,000, 2% on the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% above $2,000,000 — so nothing in this band reaches the 3% tier, and the further 2% on residential property worth over $3,000,000 never applies in this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First time home buyers' program (BC Gov):** where all conditions are met, the purchaser is exempt from PTT on the first $500,000 of the purchase price; a full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024), a property of 0.5 hectares or smaller containing only residential improvements and used only as the buyer's principal residence, and a partial exemption may apply between $835,000 and $860,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Newly built home exemption (BC Gov):** effective April 1, 2024, the fair market value threshold for a full exemption is $1,100,000, with a partial exemption phasing out over the $50,000 above it and complete elimination at $1,150,000 for qualifying purchasers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Manufactured-home pads follow tenancy law, not strata law:** pad rent can be increased only once every 12 months, with at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for increases taking effect in 2026) plus a proportional levy/utility amount. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Why does this page stop at $1.5 million — is that just an asking-price filter?** It is a price filter, but the line also matches a federal financing rule: CMHC states homes $1,500,000 or more require a minimum 20% down payment and mortgage loan insurance is not available, while homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999 need only 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% on the rest. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Q: What kinds of 55+ homes exist as ownership product in this market?** Three kinds, per the sibling files: low-rise strata condos in established 55+ buildings (deepest in Abbotsford), gated 55+ townhouse/rancher strata complexes (deepest in Langley and South Surrey), bare land strata rancher communities, and — as a separate tenure — manufactured homes you own on a leased pad in a 55+ park. Section 2 names the verified complexes by type; no complex is priced in these notes. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is a specific 55+ complex under $1.5 million right now?** UNCONFIRMED in these notes by design — no source in this program states a current price for a specific complex, prices change, and the live feed (which stamps its own update time and carries the boards' no-warranty Reciprocity notice) is the only place to check a current asking price. https://jaredgibbons.ca/homes-under-1500000.html
- **Q: How many homes are for sale under $1.5M right now?** The live feed showed 238 listings at the crawl moment (November 1, 2026), but that is the feed's own changing count, not a published statistic — SnapStats publishes no price-band cut, so no sourced band count, share or median exists. https://jaredgibbons.ca/homes-under-1500000.html
- **Q: Are these Jared Gibbons' own listings?** No. The cards are MLS® Reciprocity listings from many brokerages; the page footer states the data comes in part from the Reciprocity program of GVR, FVREB or CADREB. Jared's own listings are on the site's our-listings page. https://jaredgibbons.ca/homes-under-1500000.html
- **Q: Besides the strata fee, can a 55+ building charge a second mandatory monthly amount?** At least one verified building does: listing information for Mayfair Terrace in Port Coquitlam states a mandatory base service plan on top of strata fees — $715/mo for the first resident, $400 for a second (MLS R3179064, Oct 2026) — covering meals, housekeeping, emergency response, activities and transportation. Treat the figures as listing-stated and confirm the current amounts in the strata documents and service agreement. Source: ~/workspace/local-notes/port-coquitlam-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: If I buy a manufactured home in a 55+ park, what do I actually own?** The home, not the land: the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act covers the pad tenancy, pad rent can rise only once every 12 months on at least three full months' notice within the provincial cap, and at Maplewood Estates in Abbotsford the park owner must approve the buyer before purchase. There is no strata Form B — the park's tenancy documents are the due-diligence set. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/poplar-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: How much property transfer tax would I pay inside this band?** On the general tiers: 1% on the first $200,000 plus 2% on the portion from $200,000 to the purchase price — nothing in this band reaches the 3% tier or the further 2% over $3,000,000; a BC exemption (first-time buyer to $835,000 full, newly built to $1,100,000 full) could reduce the bill further if you qualify. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **$1.5M is a financing cliff, not just a budget line.** Just under the line a buyer can purchase with less than 20% down (paying a CMHC premium of 0.6% to 4.5% of the mortgage amount); at $1,500,000 or more, 20% down is mandatory and insurance is unavailable — which is why sellers pricing just under the line keep the insured-buyer pool and sellers at or over it lose it. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **The visible page sits at the top of its own band.** The 12 first-page cards at the crawl moment ran $985,000 to $1,499,900 — the band's lower end (condos and townhomes well under those prices) does not appear on page one, and the sort order that produces that is not stated on the page. No price figure here is a claim about any 55+ complex. https://jaredgibbons.ca/homes-under-1500000.html
- **Whole-community medians sit under the ceiling, but that proves nothing about the band.** Surrey detached median $1,215,000, Langley detached median $1,345,000, Surrey condos & townhomes median $512,944 and Langley condos & townhomes median $611,000 (September 2026, SnapStats FVR report) all sit below $1,500,000 — community context only; SnapStats publishes no price-band cut, so how much of either market the band holds cannot be quantified and is not estimated here. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/homes-under-1500000.md (Section 2).
- **In the older 55+ buildings, the strata fee often buys utilities, not just maintenance.** Elmwood Manor's fee is recorded as covering electricity, heat and water; Fernwood Manor's and Alameda Court's as covering heat and hot water; Lakewood Gardens' as covering gas, heat and hot water — a monthly-cost comparison against a general-market condo that excludes utilities misleads if this is not netted out. Sources: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/abbotsford-west-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/central-abbotsford-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Suites are a defining feature of this band's detached stock as displayed.** Seven of the 12 first-page cards describe a basement suite, mortgage helper or separate-entry rental space in their listing text — extended-family and income-helper layouts, which interact with the 55+ exemption cases (a suite occupant still has to fit the strata bylaw analysis only in strata product; a detached house carries no strata age bylaw at all). https://jaredgibbons.ca/homes-under-1500000.html
