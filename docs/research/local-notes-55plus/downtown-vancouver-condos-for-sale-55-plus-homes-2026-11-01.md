# Downtown Vancouver Condos — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. This file covers the City of Vancouver's Downtown local area in its downtown-condo framing (the board's Downtown VW feed ground, with Yaletown and Coal Harbour as their own board sub-areas inside / beside it), consolidating the Downtown VW, Downtown VE and Yaletown files in this series.

**ZERO VERIFIED 55+ OWNERSHIP STOCK DOWNTOWN — stated plainly at the top, as this file's Section 2 heading requires:** no age-restricted strata ownership building was verified anywhere in the Downtown local area in any source checked in this series. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. Downtown's relevance is negative: the law permits a downtown 55+ building, and no verified one exists — the absence is a market fact, not a legal one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. This ownership / residency distinction is what a buyer would use in a real 55+ building elsewhere — downtown, there is no verified building to apply it to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw, and in Vancouver the City's own short-term rental rules also apply — a downtown feed card stating that Airbnb / short-term rentals are permitted in a building is listing copy only and UNVERIFIED in this series. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://jaredgibbons.ca/downtown-vancouver-condos-for-sale.html
- **Tenure varies by building downtown:** freehold strata and leasehold both exist in the wider downtown stock. One feed card describes The Admiralty (1600 Howe Street) as "prepaid Crown lease to 2084" — that is one listing's text about one building, flagged as site editorial only and NOT adopted as a verified tenure fact here. Building-by-building tenure for any building in this feed is UNVERIFIED — read it off title and the strata documents for the exact unit. https://jaredgibbons.ca/yaletown-condos-for-sale.html
- **City of Vancouver zoning:** Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, with the Downtown District (DD) schedule governing much of the core and CD-1 and C-3A districts in parts; Yaletown is also a protected heritage area (HA-3), which adds heritage controls to its warehouse-era buildings. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **Vancouver property taxes and the Empty Homes Tax:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. Every Vancouver owner must also submit an annual Empty Homes Tax property status declaration (due February 3, 2026); the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date. The current Empty Homes Tax rate is UNVERIFIED in this series. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Additional school tax (provincial):** can apply at downtown price points — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Subsidized seniors housing downtown runs through BC Housing's Zone 6 directory and the Housing Registry** — the Downtown South / Yaletown buildings in Section 2 are applied for, not bought or market-rented. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo building I can buy in downtown Vancouver?** No verified one. Across the Downtown VW, Downtown VE and Yaletown checks in this series, no downtown building's sources state a 55+ age bylaw. A buyer who needs the bylaw itself has to look outside the Downtown local area; a buyer who needs downtown and single-level strata living is buying a general-market condo and getting the layout, not the age rule. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: A listing calls a downtown building "55+" / "adult-oriented." Should I believe it?** Not without the registered bylaws. Downtown is the clearest case in this series of the marketing-words trap: amenity sets that read as retirement living (Yaletown Ltd's pool, recreation centre and sauna) sit in buildings whose recorded restrictions are "pets allowed, rentals allowed" and nothing about age. Ask for the Form B and the registered bylaw set; if the age bylaw is not in them, it does not exist for that building. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/yaletown-ltd-1018-cambie-street
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 / I'm under 55 buying for a parent — how do the exemptions work?** In a genuine BC 55+ strata: a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is exempt (expanded May 1, 2023, with defined child and caregiver exemptions), and ownership itself is never age-restricted — an under-55 buyer can own and a 55+ parent can reside. Downtown, this is academic until a verified building exists; it is the rule set to carry to a 55+ purchase in Surrey, Delta or elsewhere in this series. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a downtown condo, or run it short-term?** Long-term: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022. Short-term: a strata can still restrict it, the City of Vancouver has its own short-term rental rules, and any feed card claiming a building permits Airbnb is unverified listing copy — verify from the strata documents and the City before pricing any short-term income. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What does a downtown buyer pay beyond the purchase price and strata fee?** The Vancouver calendar: advance taxes each February and main taxes each July (2026: Feb 3 and Jul 3, 5% penalty after each), the annual Empty Homes Tax declaration every owner must file whether or not the home is empty, and — at higher price points — the Province's additional school tax on residential assessment above $3 million. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Q: Is a downtown condo freehold or leasehold?** It depends on the building, and downtown has both in its wider stock. No general claim is made here and even a specific feed claim (The Admiralty's "prepaid Crown lease to 2084") is carried as unverified listing text. Tenure comes off title for the exact unit — a leasehold's remaining term and lease terms change the financing and resale conversation completely. https://jaredgibbons.ca/yaletown-condos-for-sale.html
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Inside the area: St. Paul's Hospital, 1081 Burrard Street (Providence Health Care). Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, is across False Creek in Fairview. For care housing rather than a hospital, Yaletown House (1099 Cambie Street) is the area's long-term care home, accessed through the care system. https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/clinics/rapid-access-stroke-prevention-clinic and https://travelclinic.vch.ca/en/location/yaletown-house
- **Q: What schools serve a downtown condo?** The VSB lists Elsie Roy Elementary (150 Drake St) and šxʷməqʷəθət Crosstown Elementary (55 Expo Blvd) under Downtown; no public secondary inside the Downtown local area was confirmed, and the nearest named secondary in the sources, King George Secondary (1755 Barclay St), is in the West End. Catchment for a specific address is UNVERIFIED until run in the VSB School Locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods and https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
- **Q: Can I get downtown market numbers for these condos?** Not from SnapStats: Vancouver is not one of its 14 September 2026 communities, so this series states no downtown median, sales ratio or trend. The feed's asking prices are listing copy; pricing support has to come from Paragon comparables. Checked against the SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report files in ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/stats/.
- **Q: If I can't buy 55+ downtown, what are the seniors options actually in the area?** Non-profit subsidized rental in Downtown South / Yaletown (the Seymour, Granville, Davie, Richards and Pacific Boulevard buildings in Section 2, applied for through BC Housing's system) and long-term care at Yaletown House. Both are needs- and income-based systems, not market purchases — a downtown senior who wants to buy is buying general-market strata. Sources: Section 2 above.
