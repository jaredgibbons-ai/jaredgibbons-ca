## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The governing law for a rented pad is provincial, not municipal.** Manufactured home site tenancies are governed by BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) wherever the park sits; in that tenure the tenant owns the manufactured home and rents the site under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement, and landlords and tenants must have a written tenancy agreement for the site. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **The 2026 pad-rent increase limit: 2.3% plus a proportional amount.** For manufactured home site tenancies the 2026 limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; the 2027 limit is 2.2% plus the proportional amount. The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **How an increase may be given:** only with 3 full months' written notice, only once every 12 months, and only up to the yearly limit; the landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion of a prior year's limit, and if proper notice was not given the increase takes effect on the earliest date that would comply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Above-limit increases need an order, not a notice.** A landlord may apply to the Residential Tenancy Branch (RTB Form 52) for an additional increase for financial loss from increased operating or financing costs, or for substantial and essential repairs or renovations that are reasonable, necessary and not expected to recur. Tenants do not have to pay an increase above the legal amount and may deduct an overpayment from future rent or apply for dispute resolution. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Buying a park home does not reset the pad rent (MHPTA s. 28 assignment).** Assignment permanently transfers the site tenancy to the buyer; a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an order of the director, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it; before asking, the seller must give the proposed buyer written copies of the park rules and the relevant parts of the tenancy agreement; the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent; and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **A park's 55+ rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003) expressly recognises a park where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (per Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i)) and allows a park landlord to refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement. The Strata Property Act exemptions below do not answer a park occupancy question — get the park's rules in writing. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s. 42):** where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to another use, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; the tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, may dispute within 15 days, may in defined circumstances apply for additional compensation of the home's assessed value minus the $20,000, and if the landlord does not take steps toward the stated conversion within a reasonable period the landlord must pay the greater of $5,000 or 12 months' rent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Registration and the CSA label gate every park purchase.** In BC the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered with the Manufactured Home Registry, through a lawyer or notary filing the original signed and witnessed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership forms; a home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular), and a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification. A permit to move a home is issued by the Registry, a tax certificate for transport purposes is required (with exceptions including a move to a different pad within the same park), and the transport permit expires 30 days after issue. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Tax on a park home excludes the land.** The Province states a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **What pad rent includes is park-by-park: UNCONFIRMED as a general Langley statement.** Inclusions (utilities, garbage, water/sewer, amenities) are set in the specific park's written site tenancy agreement; the live page's "usually includes / usually does not include" lists are site copy, not an official source. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cost-of-mobile-home-pad-rental-fees-in-langley.html
- **For comparison — the strata rules that apply to Langley's strata 55+ complexes, not its parks:** under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, only a 55+ age bylaw is valid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and since May 1, 2023 also a younger spouse/partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and strata rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term rental bylaws remain allowed. None of that governs a pad tenancy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Two zoning bylaws, kept separate:** Langley City complexes and any City park sit under Langley City Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 (City SSMUH Bylaw 3284, adopted June 17, 2024; City suite cap is the lesser of 100 m² and 40%); Township parks — including all three verified Brookswood parks and Pioneer Park — sit under Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 (Township SSMUH Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024: 3 units on lots ≤280 m², 4 on lots to <4,050 m², larger lots ineligible, no 6-unit tier; Township suite cap 120 m²). The manufactured-home-park zone name and rules in either bylaw: UNVERIFIED — neither bylaw's park-zone section was opened in this pass. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How much is pad rent in Langley?** No official source publishes a Langley pad-rent figure, average or range, so no figure is stated here — and the $650–$950 range in the live page's copy is UNVERIFIED site copy, not an official statistic. A pad rent is set park by park in the written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement; the figure that matters is the specific park's current rent, obtained from the park and the tenancy agreement before an offer. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cost-of-mobile-home-pad-rental-fees-in-langley.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up each year?** Up to the yearly limit set by the Residential Tenancy Branch: for 2026, 2.3% plus a proportional amount; for 2027, 2.2% plus the proportional amount. The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of sites in the park. Increases require 3 full months' notice and can occur only once every 12 months; anything above the limit requires an RTB order. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park raise my pad rent when I buy the home?** No. On assignment under s. 28 of the MHPTA, the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same and the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase does not change; the landlord's prior written consent is required (or deemed given) and the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: Which Langley parks are actually 55+?** Three verified pad-rental parks, all in Brookswood in the Township: Cedar Creek Estates (3031 200 St), Fernridge Park (20071 24 Ave) and Pineridge (2270 196 St). Pioneer Park in Aldergrove is 55+ but is a co-operative, not a pad-rental park. Other Langley parks — Langley Grove Estates and Aldergrove Mobile Home Park — state no age restrictions in their listings. https://www.estateblock.com/langley/5-3031-200-st-1968212 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3109616-33-20071-24-avenue-langley-british-columbia-v2z2a1-99000/brochure and https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/24330-fraser-highway/52
- **Q: Can a park refuse me because of age?** In a 55+ park, yes on assignment: the MHPTA Regulation recognises a park where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 and allows the landlord to refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement. That is a park rule under tenancy law — the strata exemptions for younger spouses, caregivers and legacy residents do not automatically apply, so ask the park for its rule in writing. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q: What does pad rent include in a Langley park?** UNCONFIRMED as a general statement — inclusions are set park by park in the written site tenancy agreement. Do not budget from a generic "usually includes" list; read the specific agreement for utilities, garbage, water/sewer and amenity charges before writing. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cost-of-mobile-home-pad-rental-fees-in-langley.html
- **Q: What is the monthly cost stack besides pad rent?** In structure, not figures: pad rent under the regulated formula, plus home insurance, plus property tax on the home's assessed value excluding the land in a park, plus utilities as the specific park's agreement allocates them. No dollar figure for any element is stated here, because no official source publishes a Langley figure for any of them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Q: What happens if the park is sold or converted to another use?** Under MHPTA s. 42: the landlord must first have all necessary permits and approvals, then give 12 months' notice and pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date; the tenant may leave early on 10 days' written notice, may dispute within 15 days, and may in defined circumstances claim additional compensation of the home's assessed value minus the $20,000; if the landlord does not proceed within a reasonable period, a further payment of the greater of $5,000 or 12 months' rent applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How is Pioneer Park's $510/month different from pad rent?** It is a co-op fee, not pad rent — and it is listing-stated, not a published tariff. Listings state the Pioneer Park buyer must be accepted by the co-op, must purchase co-op shares ($9,200 in one listing, refunded upon sale) and pays a monthly co-op fee ($510 in the same listing) rather than pad rent to a landlord. Confirm the current figures with the co-op. https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form?_pg=3
- **Q: Do I pay property tax on a manufactured home in a Langley park?** Yes, on the home: the Province states a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value does not include the value of the land where the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **There is no Langley pad-rent average to look up — and a page that prints one has not sourced it.** Neither the Province, the Township, the City nor SnapStats publishes a park-level or average pad rent for Langley; SnapStats publishes no manufactured-home category and no rent figure of any kind. The only official "number" in this topic is the increase formula. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **The proportional amount makes two parks' increases differ in the same year.** It is the park's own levy plus regulated-utility change divided by that park's own site count — so the same 2.3% headline limit for 2026 produces a different dollar increase at Cedar Creek Estates than at Fernridge Park or Pineridge. Ask the specific park for its last proportional-amount calculation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **All of Langley's verified 55+ pad-rental parks are in one Township pocket.** Cedar Creek Estates, Fernridge Park and Pineridge are all in Brookswood; there is no verified 55+ pad-rental park in Langley City, Walnut Grove, Willoughby or Fort Langley in the roster. A buyer fixed on a 55+ park in Langley is, in practice, shopping Brookswood — or the Aldergrove co-op. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **A transfer does not trigger a market re-pricing of the pad.** Unlike a strata purchase, where fees are whatever the current budget says, an MHPTA assignment carries the existing pad rent and the existing next-increase date to the new tenant — the seller's rent history is part of what a buyer is acquiring, and it is documented in the tenancy agreement the seller must share before consent is sought. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **The CSA label is a hard gate, not a detail.** A manufactured home without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 mobile, A-277 modular) cannot be sold until Technical Safety BC re-certification — label status is a price and timeline fact on every Langley park purchase, and it is the dividing line between renovated, readily saleable park homes and original-condition fixer product. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **"55+" in a Langley park and "55+" in a Langley strata are different legal objects.** The park rule sits in tenancy regulation and park rules and can support refusing an assignment; the strata rule sits in a registered bylaw under ss. 123.1/123.2 with Regulation 7.01 exemptions. A couple who qualify to live in a Parkway Estates strata unit on a spousal exemption have no automatic equivalent right in a Brookswood park. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Township taxes have two penalty steps that park owners still pay on the home.** Township of Langley property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026, with a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026, and utility fees are billed separately. All three verified 55+ parks are in the Township, so this calendar — not Langley City's — is the one their owners follow. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
