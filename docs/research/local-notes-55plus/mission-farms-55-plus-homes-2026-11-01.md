# Mission Farms — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Plain finding up front: the 55+ angle on Mission farm and ALR land is thin, and this file says so with sources. No 55+ strata, co-operative, rental or care complex was verified on Mission farm land. The one rural product with an age dimension is manufactured-home parks on the Lougheed Highway corridor — Mission Mobile Home Park in Hatzic (listing-stated 55+) and Oasis Country Estates in Dewdney Deroche (an "adult side" whose exact age number no source states — UNCONFIRMED as 55+). Mission's verified 55+ strata and life-lease stock is urban, on the 7th Avenue bench — listed in Section 2 for contrast.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The strata age-bylaw rules, for the record — with no farm-belt strata to apply them to.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; age bylaws below 55 invalid since Nov 24, 2022, under Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term restrictions remain allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR housing caps are the rules that actually bind a 55+ farm buyer.** Land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The second-dwelling rule is the ageing-in-place rule on a farm.** The ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, with a total floor area of 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and Mission's bylaws must also permit it. Anything larger needs an ALC application. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law.** Pad tenancies at Mission Mobile Home Park and Oasis Country Estates fall under BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act: the resident owns the home and rents the pad, pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, and the raise is capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. A park's age rule operates through its park rules and assignment consent (the MHPTA assignment rules recognise parks reserved for tenants who have reached 55, per Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i)) — which is why Oasis's unstated "adult side" number matters and stays UNCONFIRMED. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020** is Mission's zoning bylaw; rural zones such as RU16 carry a 1.6 ha minimum lot size in City notices — relevant to the rural sub-areas, where there is no strata stock at all. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning
- **Current plan: Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026** replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025, keeping the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction with a Regional Context Statement added; the City's Rural Mission Strategy, now finalized, is the rural companion document. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/rural-mission-strategy-now-live
- **Farm classification is a continuing obligation.** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income requirements at farm gate prices are $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha (over 4 ha), and $10,000 (under 0.8 ha). A downsizing buyer who stops producing can lose the classification. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property taxes:** Mission's 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 (Mission has no staged 5% + 5% scheme); the City also levies additional taxes for diking. The Province's regular property tax deferment program is for people 55 and older. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Flood:** the Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's south edge and low areas such as Hatzic Prairie — where Mission Mobile Home Park sits — are protected by the Dewdney Dyke system; flood status is parcel-specific. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ farm community or seniors farm complex in Mission?** No — none was verified on any source. Farm parcels are freehold, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. Mission's verified 55+ purchases are urban (Cherry Lane, Luther Place, Cedar Valley Manor life lease); the rural belt's age-flavoured product is manufactured-home parks. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is Oasis Country Estates a 55+ park?** Only partly, and the number is unproven: listings distinguish an "adult side" from a "family section," and no source checked states the adult side's exact age. Treat "55+ at Oasis" as UNCONFIRMED until the park's own rules state it. Mission Mobile Home Park in Hatzic is the park whose listings state 55+ outright. https://www.zolo.ca/mission-real-estate/41168-lougheed-highway/13
- **Q: I'm 55+ and buying a farm to downsize onto. What limits the house I can build?** On ALR land, a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. Mission's rural zones also carry large minimum lot sizes (RU16: 1.6 ha in City notices). Confirm the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can my adult child or a caregiver live in a second dwelling on the farm?** The ALC permits one additional residence without an application where its size rules are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels, detached — and City bylaws must also permit it. There is no strata-style caregiver exemption because there is no strata. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What does a pad cost in the Hatzic park, and can it jump?** Pad rent is quoted per listing ($650 and $671.29 on two current-era listings at Mission Mobile Home Park) — UNCONFIRMED park-wide; confirm the specific pad in the tenancy documents. Under the MHPTA, pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional share of levy/utility changes. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85367/FullPublic.pdf?i=979581669 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: If I stop farming, do I keep farm-class taxes?** Not automatically — BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income thresholds ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha) must keep being met, by you or by a producing lessee. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Where is the hospital for a rural Mission address?** Mission Memorial Hospital, Fraser Health's community hospital for Mission, in the urban core. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Mission/mission-memorial-hospital
- **Q: Does the farm belt flood?** Low areas such as Hatzic Prairie sit behind the Dewdney Dyke system on the Fraser River floodplain, and the City levies additional taxes for diking; flood status is parcel-specific — check the specific parcel. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The Rural Mission Strategy is finished, not draft.** The City confirms the strategy — its plan to recognize, protect and enhance Mission's rural areas, building on the OCP — is now finalized and posted; older copy describing it as in-progress is stale. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/rural-mission-strategy-now-live
- **Mission's OCP number changed in 2026.** OCP Bylaw 6405-2026 replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025, keeping the same vision and land use framework with a Regional Context Statement added — citations to the older number are superseded. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026
- **The rural park with the age claim is split in two.** Oasis Country Estates' "adult side / family section" split (41168 Lougheed Hwy) means two homes in the same gated park can carry different occupancy rules — the unit's section, not the park's name, is what a buyer is actually purchasing under. https://www.zolo.ca/mission-real-estate/41168-lougheed-highway/13
- **Mission's tax penalty is flat, unlike its neighbours.** A flat 10% penalty applies to current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 — there is no staged 5% + 5% scheme — and diking taxes are levied on top of the municipal, school, regional district and BC Assessment levies, which matters on dyke-protected farm parcels. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
