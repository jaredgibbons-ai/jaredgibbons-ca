## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** is Chilliwack's zoning bylaw. Its Section 2 defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit with a gross floor area of not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building, located within a building containing only 1 principal dwelling unit — the cap is the lesser of 90 m² and 40%. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Accessory dwelling units:** Section 4.06(1) of the same bylaw permits only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits an ADU; the listed types are secondary suite, coach house and garden suite, garden suites are capped at a maximum gross floor area of 100 m², and off-street parking for accessory units is addressed in Section 5.04. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Suites policy:** the City's Secondary Suites Policy permits the rental of secondary suites within any single detached dwelling in the City regardless of zone; a suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level under the Floodplain Regulation Bylaw. https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 is the City's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment to Bylaw No. 5000, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing; Bylaw 5395 was adopted June 18, 2024. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3090/Signed%20Staff%20Report%20%2D%20RZ001761%2Epdf
- **Short-term rentals:** the City regulates short-term rental operations under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **City plan: Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500** is the current city-wide plan (cross-file confirmation against the City's OCP page — see the note in Section 1). https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Flood:** flood hazard zoning exists in the bylaw structure (RS-FHA reserve zone, Section 12 of Bylaw No. 5000) and the City applies flood protection requirements and a Floodplain Regulation Bylaw; parcel-level Flood Construction Levels for Proper South lots: UNVERIFIED. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **Property taxes:** City of Chilliwack property tax notices are mailed annually at the end of May and are due the first working day after July 1 of every calendar year; a 5% late payment penalty is applied for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% for taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually BUY in Chilliwack Proper South?** One verified age-restricted ownership complex, with a boundary flag: Cottage Grove (8500 Young Rd — gated ranchers, built 1989 in the listing checked, "Senior Community: Yes" in listings), which this page's feed labels Chilliwack Proper South even though the address is at the south end of Young Road on the Sardis side. No verified 55+ condo building inside the page's north-of-Highway-1 corridors was found — the Spadina condo pair is counted on the Proper West / city-wide files, not here. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Cottage Grove really in Chilliwack Proper South?** By board feed label, yes — its unit appeared in this page's feed under "Chilliwack Proper South." By the page's own geography, no — the scope is described as Young Road north of Highway 1, distinct from Sardis south of Highway 1, and 8500 Young Road is the south end of the road. The conflict is flagged in this file rather than resolved; buy the complex for what and where it is, not for the sub-area label. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Cottage Grove?** Yes in a strata — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. Confirm Cottage Grove's own registered bylaws on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ complex for a parent, or to move into later?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ complex?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals; Chilliwack also regulates short-term rental operations under its own Bylaw No. 5141. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **Q: Can a detached house here take a suite or a coach house for a caregiver or family?** One accessory dwelling unit per lot where the zone permits it — a secondary suite (capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space), a coach house, or a garden suite (capped at 100 m²) — under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, ss. 2 and 4.06; the City's policy also permits renting a secondary suite in any single detached dwelling regardless of zone, with a building permit and flood-protection compliance, and no new suite below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in this part of Chilliwack?** Not inside this scope — no care residence or subsidized seniors building was verified on the Proper South corridors. The ladder is a move within the city: independent strata here or downtown → subsidized independent rental (Jubilee Place, 45440 Hodgins Ave, 36 units, 55+ low-income) → retirement rental and registered assisted living in the hospital precinct (the Chartwell residences and The Cascades) — plus Waverly Seniors Village, a registered assisted-living residence at 8445 Young Rd, close to Cottage Grove at the south end of Young Road. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is there a bus I can rely on if I stop driving?** The live page names BC Transit Routes 51, 52, 57 and 58 along Yale Road and Young Road with a Route 66 connection at the Downtown Exchange, but route-level detail is UNVERIFIED in the research file — check the current BC Transit schedule for the specific corridor before relying on it. Route 66 Fraser Valley Express itself is confirmed as daily service between Chilliwack, Abbotsford, Langley and Burnaby. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-proper-south.html and https://www.bctransit.com/plan-a-trip/interregional-routes/
- **Q: Why are there no market numbers for this area?** Chilliwack is a CADREB market that SnapStats does not cover, and CADREB publishes statistics board-wide only with no sub-area tables — so any Chilliwack Proper South statistic is UNVERIFIED here rather than borrowed from a neighbouring board. https://www.cadreb.com/market/
- **Q: When are Chilliwack property taxes due, and what is the penalty?** Notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1 each year; for 2026, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
