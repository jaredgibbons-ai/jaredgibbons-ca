## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. No Greentree Village strata checked has used this power — the absence of a 55+ bylaw is the verified fact here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning bylaw — the NEW Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026.** The City states the new Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 was adopted June 9, 2026, replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, and took effect July 1, 2026. Do not cite the 1965 bylaw as current. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **R1 SSMUH zoning:** Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district in June 2024 — 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. This is the route by which a house lot around the pocket — not the strata townhome parcels themselves — becomes a suite or multi-generational property. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP: the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict — which is why the 1972 Greentree Community Plan is a history and boundary source here, not current development policy. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Property taxes:** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; the detached single-family water utility charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Greentree Village?** None that any source verifies. The sub-area's September 2026 listings were 1 detached and 2 condos & townhomes, and the one named complex — the Greentree Village townhomes on Garden Grove Drive — is a general-market 1970s strata with no age restriction in any source. A senior who wants a legal 55+ purchase is looking at other Burnaby or Tri-Cities sub-areas. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file; building checks Nov 1, 2026.)
- **Q: The Greentree townhomes look quiet and low-rise — isn't that a seniors complex?** No. Listings describe a private community centre with an indoor pool, gym and recreation hall and call the wider community "family-oriented"; the 1975 townhouse listing records ordinary strata fees and no age restriction of any kind. Quiet and treed is not the same as age-restricted — check the Form B and registered bylaws on any unit. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/4384-garden-grove-drive and https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/4698-garden-grove-drive
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Burnaby?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which schools serve the Greentree Village pocket?** A current Garden Grove Drive listing states Gilpin Elementary and Moscrop Secondary as the catchments for the community. The École Marlborough name in the area research file belongs to its conflicting south-slope placement (see the conflict note at the top) — confirm any address in the SD41 locator before relying on either. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3110321-3848-garden-grove-drive-burnaby-british-columbia-v5g4a7-1498000/brochure and https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/
- **Q: Can a house near the pocket work as a multi-generational home instead?** That is the realistic in-area route: Burnaby's R1 SSMUH district allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots and 6 near frequent transit, in forms including secondary suites, and multi-unit lots may be stratified. Confirm the lot's size and transit test before pricing suite potential in. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St, is the city's hospital; the Metrotown Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 102-4555 Kingsway (sources via the area research file). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** There is no SkyTrain station in the pocket, and which station serves it is entangled in the boundary conflict flagged at the top of this file — station access for a specific Greentree Village address: UNVERIFIED. Check the exact address's bus service before buying on a no-car plan.
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Greentree Village?** Not in a seniors building — there is none in the sub-area. The in-city ladder sits elsewhere in Burnaby: registered assisted living in the Edmonds and Brentwood areas, long-term care including the George Derby campus in The Crest / Edmonds area, and the 55+ rental at Derby Manor (see The Crest file). https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The sub-area's own research file puts it in the wrong place (flagged Nov 1, 2026):** the research file's Marlborough Avenue / Sussex Avenue placement and its École Marlborough school point conflict with the City's Greentree Community Plan — a 50-acre site bounded by Wayburne Drive, Woodsworth Street, Forest Lawn Cemetery and Moscrop Street — and with every current listing under the Greentree Village label, which clusters on Garden Grove Drive and Canada Way behind BCIT in the Gilpin / Moscrop catchments. Until the board boundary is confirmed against a primary source, any page copy that mixes the two geographies will be wrong somewhere. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/nkpx5fd25q/greentree-village-community-plan-1972 and https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/4384-garden-grove-drive
- **The "village" feel is a 1972 plan, not a marketing invention:** the community was comprehensively planned as a single 50-acre proposal in 1972 (Rezoning #1/72), with the strata townhomes and their private recreation centre — indoor pool, fitness centre, social room — built into the concept from the start; the detached houses on Garden Grove Drive date to the same era (a current house listing records a 1973 build). That single-plan origin is why the pocket feels like a complex even where the homes are freehold. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/nkpx5fd25q/greentree-village-community-plan-1972 and https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/4698-garden-grove-drive
- **Never quote the "100% sales ratio" for this sub-area:** Greentree Village had exactly 2 condos & townhomes listings in September 2026 and both sold, while its single detached listing did not — a 2-listing artefact, not a market verdict. Price off Burnaby community evidence (detached median $1,825,000; condos & townhomes median $686,000 in September 2026) and a multi-month window. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **The recreation centre is private strata amenity, not a City facility:** listings describe the Greentree Recreation Centre — indoor pool, fitness centre and social room, recently renovated per one listing — as the complex's own community centre across the street from the townhomes. It is not a City of Burnaby recreation centre and not a seniors centre; access runs with the strata, so confirm what a given unit's fees actually cover in the strata documents. https://storage.ubertor.com/cl2904/content/featuresheet_2854_document/568028.pdf and https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/4384-garden-grove-drive
- **Burnaby replaced its entire zoning bylaw in 2026:** the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (adopted June 9, 2026, in force July 1, 2026) repealed and replaced the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 — documents or summaries that still cite the 1965 bylaw for this pocket are out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
