## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Westham Island is a real island in the Fraser River estuary off Ladner, in the City of Delta. Environment and Climate Change Canada places the island's Alaksen National Wildlife Area just under 10 km west of Ladner, reached by crossing the bridge to Westham Island and following Westham Island Road. The Province states the Westham Island Bridge is the island's only road access point. https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/national-wildlife-areas/locations/alaksen.html and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026AF0010-000103.htm
- The bridge (TransLink): the Westham Island Bridge officially opened March 29, 1910; it is the oldest of the five bridges TransLink owns and operates, operated by TransLink since 1999, the narrowest TransLink bridge, and one of few remaining bridges containing wood components in Metro Vancouver; it crosses Canoe Pass, connects Westham Island with Ladner, operates with partial single-lane traffic and a swing span that swings 90 degrees to let boats pass, opening roughly seven or eight times a day, mainly for fishing boats; its load limit is 50 tons. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2013/07/roads-bridges-getting-to-know-westham-island/
- The island's land base is farmland: the Province states Westham Island produces more than 25% of B.C.'s seed potatoes, plus other fruit and vegetables, honey and livestock, on more than 700 hectares, with crops including seed potatoes, vegetables and berries, plus forage and pasture. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026AF0010-000103.htm
- The governing plan is the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), adopted July 8, 2024 — a single consolidated plan with no lettered area-plan schedules; its designation list (s. 1.5.1) does not include "Single Family Residential," and farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)." https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Schools: no school on Westham Island appears in the BC Ministry of Education K-12 file used in this program — a search for "Westham" in that file returns no school rows. The Ladner schools in that file, which serve the island side of Delta, are Ladner Elementary, 5016 44 Ave; Hawthorne Elementary, 5160 Central Ave; Port Guichon Elementary, 4381 46a St; Neilson Grove Elementary, 5500 Admiral Blvd; and Delta Secondary, 4615 51 St, all in Delta. The guide page names Delta Secondary as the secondary school in Ladner. Catchments are address-based in School District 37, and no SD37 locator run for a Westham Island address is on record in this program — the catchment for any specific island address is UNCONFIRMED. (Program file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/education/work/bc_k12_schools.csv) and https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Wildlife areas on the island: Alaksen National Wildlife Area, 349 ha, established 1976, mainly four former deltaic islands joined by causeways and dykes, part of the Fraser River Delta Ramsar Site, managed by the Canadian Wildlife Service, open 8 a.m. to 3 p.m., Monday to Friday excluding statutory holidays; a section of Alaksen overlaps with the George C. Reifel Migratory Bird Sanctuary, reached through its gate at 5191 Robertson Rd (the NWA gate is at 5421 Robertson Rd). The Sanctuary's own site describes nearly 300 hectares of managed wetlands, natural marshes and low dykes — the guide page's figure of 648 hectares for the Sanctuary does not match the Sanctuary's own figure and is not adopted here. https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/national-wildlife-areas/locations/alaksen.html and https://www.reifelbirdsanctuary.com/about.html and https://jaredgibbons.ca/westham-island-guide.html
- Flood context: the island sits behind dikes on the Fraser River estuary. The City of Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Health care: the nearest hospital is Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner (Fraser Health) — across the bridge on the mainland side. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- Transit serving Westham Island itself: UNCONFIRMED — no TransLink route serving the island was verified in this research, and no official source publishes driving commute times from the island.
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: not on Westham Island. No verified 55+ ownership, rental, retirement or subsidized building with a Westham Island address was found — see Section 2. The seniors housing, care and seniors-centre services for island residents are all in Ladner, across the bridge.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now — and the island has no strata for one to attach to.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. On Westham Island there is no strata stock in any source checked, so a buyer here is under farm and ALR rules, not strata age rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **For reference, where a 55+ bylaw does exist** (mainland strata), it restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it, with Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19; and rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Housing in the ALR (Agricultural Land Commission) — the rules that actually govern an island purchase:** a principal residence in the ALR is limited to 500 m² or less total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only within the principal residence; and one additional residence may be permitted at up to 90 m² total floor area on properties of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, with local government rules also applying. ALR status is parcel by parcel — check the exact address on the ALC map. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **One verified island sample:** a City of Delta report records 2194 Westham Island Road (Reynelda Farms Ltd.) as zoned Agriculture (A 1) and located within the Agricultural Land Reserve, a 65.2 ha site entirely within the ALR. The ALR status and zoning of any other specific island parcel: UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/220325/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024** is Delta's current consolidated OCP — it has no lettered area-plan schedules (the former Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950 (1985)); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1). **Zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017**, applied parcel by parcel. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Added units outside the ALR frame:** Delta's SSMUH zoning allows up to 4 units on single-detached and duplex lots, with City support for accessory dwelling units such as coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites — on ALR land the ALC residence rules above govern first, and Delta's housing page does not override them. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **The guide page's home-plate figures are UNCONFIRMED:** the guide states the farm home plate can be up to 3,600 m² with one farm house, or 5,000 m² with an Additional Farm House — those figures were not checked against the City's Agricultural Plan or Zoning Bylaw in this research and are a different measure from the ALC floor-area limits above. Do not quote them as rules. https://jaredgibbons.ca/westham-island-guide.html
- **Bridge status is a live diligence item, not a settled fact:** the TransLink-owned bridge was struck by a marine vessel on January 20, 2026 and closed to vehicles due to safety concerns — a primary support structure sustained significant damage and a bearing connecting the deck to the pier was damaged — and the Province and TransLink funded emergency barge service during repairs. The guide page states the bridge reopened to vehicles, pedestrians and cyclists on March 28, 2026 (site copy); no current official source confirming the reopening date or the bridge's present status and limits was opened in this research, so the bridge's current status is UNCONFIRMED here — check the City of Delta's Westham Island updates page before relying on access. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026AF0010-000103.htm and https://www.delta.ca/westhamislandupdates and https://jaredgibbons.ca/westham-island-guide.html
- **Property taxes and 55+ relief:** Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. The provincial regular property tax deferment loan program covers homeowners 55 and older, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) covers seniors 65+. Property tax or levy notes specific to Westham Island: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Subsidized seniors rental applications** for Delta run through BC Housing's Housing Registry (Zone 3 — Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025) — every Delta entry in it is on the mainland. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing on Westham Island itself?** None verified — no strata, park, co-op, rental, retirement or subsidized building with an island address in any source checked. The island's housing is farmhouses, acreage homes and feed-listed float homes, none with an age bylaw. A buyer who needs a verified 55+ community has to look to the mainland: Ladner for care and subsidized rental, Tsawwassen Central for buyable 55+ strata. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: If I buy a farm on the island and age in place, where is care?** Across the bridge in Ladner: Augustine House (3820 Arthur Dr, retirement and assisted living, Assisted Living Registry class SN) and Delta Hospital (5800 Mountain View Blvd). There is no care facility, seniors centre or school on the island itself — every service trip crosses the Westham Island Bridge. http://www.augustinehouse.ca/ and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **Q: Can I build a suite or a second home for family or a caregiver on an island farm?** Only inside the ALC frame: a secondary suite is permitted only within the principal residence (which is capped at 500 m² total floor area), and one additional residence is capped at 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha), with Delta's bylaws also applying. Anything beyond that needs ALC approval — there is no strata-exemption route on farmland because there is no strata. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What happens if the bridge closes again?** It already has: struck by a vessel on January 20, 2026, it closed to vehicles for weeks while the Province and TransLink funded emergency barge service. The bridge is the island's only road access, it is a partial single-lane swing bridge with a published 50-ton load limit, and its current status after the 2026 repairs is UNCONFIRMED in this research — read the City of Delta's updates page before booking inspections, appraisals or a move date. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026AF0010-000103.htm and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2013/07/roads-bridges-getting-to-know-westham-island/ and https://www.delta.ca/westhamislandupdates
- **Q: Are the float homes a cheaper way into the island — and are they 55+?** No age restriction is stated for them in any source, and their moorage tenure, financing and insurance facts are UNCONFIRMED in this research — a float home is not a strata lot and not farmland, and none of the usual strata documents exist to check. Treat a float-home purchase as its own tenure investigation, not as a shortcut. https://jaredgibbons.ca/westham-island-guide.html
- **Q: Is the whole island in the ALR?** ALR status is set property by property — check the exact address on the ALC map. The one official sample in this research (2194 Westham Island Road) is zoned Agriculture (A 1) and entirely within the ALR, and the island's documented land base is more than 700 hectares of farmland, but no island-wide ALR percentage is stated in any source used here. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/220325/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What flood questions should I ask on an island property?** The island is dike-protected low land in the Fraser River estuary and Delta is the diking authority; the guide page's own checklist tells buyers to ask whether the property is in a floodplain, what dike protects it, and to get insurance quotes before removing subjects. Lot-level flood status: UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel here. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://jaredgibbons.ca/westham-island-guide.html
- **Q: Which schools would island children attend?** There is no school on the island in the BC Ministry K-12 file; Ladner's schools serve the island side of Delta, and the guide page names Delta Secondary (4615 51 St) as the secondary school in Ladner. Catchments are address-based and the catchment for a specific island address is UNCONFIRMED — check it in the SD37 locator. (Program file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/education/work/bc_k12_schools.csv) and https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Does a 55+ buyer get any tax break on an island farmhouse?** The provincial tools are age-based, not place-based: property tax deferment as a low-interest loan from age 55, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in this region) from age 65, both for a principal residence. Farm-class taxation is a separate BC Assessment question — no farm-class rate or rule is stated here: UNCONFIRMED from the sources used. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The island's only road in is a 1910 swing bridge that closes for boats seven or eight times a day.** Partial single-lane, 50-ton load limit, wood components, the oldest and narrowest of TransLink's five bridges — every showing, delivery, ambulance trip and care commute from an island home runs over it. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2013/07/roads-bridges-getting-to-know-westham-island/
- **The 2026 closure is the stress test that already happened.** A vessel strike on January 20, 2026 closed the bridge to vehicles for weeks, with emergency barge service funded by the Province and TransLink — a buyer pricing single-bridge access should price it as a demonstrated event, not a hypothetical. The guide page's March 28, 2026 reopening date is site copy, UNCONFIRMED against a current official source in this research. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026AF0010-000103.htm and https://jaredgibbons.ca/westham-island-guide.html
- **No published market statistics cover the island.** SnapStats publishes no South Delta figures — its Delta coverage is North Delta only — so no Westham Island, Ladner or Tsawwassen median, inventory or sales figure can be stated from the sources used here, and North Delta figures must never be presented as island figures. (Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, as recorded in the Westham Island area research file.)
- **The guide page overstates the bird sanctuary by more than double.** It gives the George C. Reifel Migratory Bird Sanctuary as 648 hectares; the Sanctuary's own About page describes nearly 300 hectares of managed wetlands, natural marshes and low dykes. The Alaksen National Wildlife Area that overlaps it is 349 ha (Canada.ca) — three different figures for two overlapping areas, and only the operators' own figures are carried here. https://www.reifelbirdsanctuary.com/about.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/national-wildlife-areas/locations/alaksen.html and https://jaredgibbons.ca/westham-island-guide.html
- **A quarter of a province's seed potatoes grow beside these homes.** The Province states the island produces more than 25% of B.C.'s seed potatoes on more than 700 hectares — the "quiet rural" purchase here is a working farm landscape with irrigation, spraying and harvest traffic, which the guide page's own framing also flags. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026AF0010-000103.htm and https://jaredgibbons.ca/westham-island-guide.html
- **The seniors infrastructure island buyers imagine is all in Ladner.** Augustine House for care, McKee House Seniors Society for programs, Delta Hospital for emergencies, the Ladner schools for grandchildren's visits — every one of them is on the mainland side of Canoe Pass. The island offers the farm; Ladner offers the services. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?address=&f%5B0%5D=city%3ADelta&f%5B1%5D=city%3AGolden&f%5B2%5D=city%3ASalmon%20Arm&f%5B3%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc and https://mckeesociety.com/
- **Delta's flood system is city-scale:** over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure maintained by the City — the island's dikes are one piece of that system, and parcel-level flood status still has to be confirmed per property. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
