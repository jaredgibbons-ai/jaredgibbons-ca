# Cultus Lake East — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

Jurisdiction note: Cultus Lake East is not in the City of Chilliwack proper — the east shore is Cultus Lake Provincial Park, Cultus Lake Park lease lots and FVRD Electoral Area H land south of the City of Chilliwack, per the area research file. The title follows that research file's verified geography. Sibling files: Cultus Lake North (Park lease lots at the north end) and Cultus Lake South (Lindell Beach, freehold/bare-land strata) cover the same lake — complexes are filed in one sub-area file only and cross-referenced in the others, never double-counted.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. There is no strata on the east shore for this to attach to — it is included because buyers cross-shop the lake's south-end strata communities, where the same law governs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The strata bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in strata exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. These exemptions do not govern a manufactured-home park tenancy or a Park Board lease. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide in strata.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **A manufactured-home park's age rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw** — the frame that applies to Cultus Lake Village. The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003) expressly recognises a park where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (per Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i)) and allows a park landlord to refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement. That is why the park's own written rule — not a listing's 45+ or 55+ label — decides who can buy into Cultus Lake Village. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park tenancy law:** manufactured home site tenancies are governed by BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act; the tenant owns the manufactured home and rents the site under a written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement. For 2026, the pad-rent increase limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees, with 3 full months' written notice and increases only once every 12 months. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park-lands zoning: FVRD Cultus Lake Park Zoning Bylaw No. 1375, 2016** sets the zones for the Cultus Lake Park lease lands, including R-3 Waterfront Residential and R-4 Hillside Residential on the Lakeshore Drive / Munroe Avenue side; in R-3 the permitted primary uses are a single-detached dwelling or a manufactured home, one dwelling per lot, for lots fronting on Cultus Lake. Amendment Bylaw No. 1672 added R-5 Lower Lakeshore Drive and R-6 Upper Lakeshore Drive zones and amended R-4 height. Minimum lot area and frontage figures for R-3 and R-4 are UNVERIFIED. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20H%20-%20Zoning%20Bylaw%201375%20-%20Cultus%20Lake%20Park.pdf
- **Plans / OCP:** PlanCultus, Cultus Lake Park Plan Bylaw No. 1080, 2016, covers Cultus Lake Park including the east-side residential lots; the FVRD Official Community Plan for Electoral Areas E and H, Bylaw No. 1115, covers Area H land outside the Park. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/CLP-Plan-Bylaw-1080-hig-res.pdf
- **Suites:** PlanCultus designates the Park's existing residential neighbourhoods as Residential, with single-family residential use and, subject to Park bylaws, secondary suites or coach houses. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/
- **Lease charges and rural taxes:** Park lease lots pay Park Board lease charges rather than a standard municipal tax bill; the current lease fee schedule and per-lot levies are UNVERIFIED. For non-Park Electoral Area H land, taxes are provincial rural taxes: under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31 — the FVRD rural regime, which differs from the City of Chilliwack's own dates. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/new-resident-information/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ home I can buy on the east shore?** Nothing verified. The only age-restricted product is Cultus Lake Village, a manufactured-home park at 3942 Columbia Valley Road where you buy the home and rent the pad — and its listings conflict on whether the park rule is 45+ or 55+, so the threshold is UNCONFIRMED until the park states its own rule in writing. There is no 55+ strata, rental or care product on the east shore in any source checked. https://www.zolo.ca/cultus-lake-real-estate/3942-columbia-valley-road/28 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3107057-28-3942-columbia-valley-roadcultus-lake-east-cultus-lake-british-columbia-v2r5b1-109900/brochure
- **Q: Is housing on the east shore freehold or leasehold?** Homes inside Cultus Lake Park are on leased lots under the Cultus Lake Park Board, and the FVRD zoning bylaw for the Park sets the R-3 and R-4 residential zones for those lots. Cultus Lake Village, by contrast, is a privately owned manufactured-home park — a pad tenancy, not a Park Board lease and not freehold land. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Area-H-Cultus-Lake-Zoning-Bylaw-1375-2016.pdf and https://www.zolo.ca/cultus-lake-real-estate/3942-columbia-valley-road/28
- **Q: What does the pad rent at Cultus Lake Village cover?** One listing states $715/month including septic, garbage, water and snow removal on the hill and park roads. That figure is listing-stated for that home at that date — confirm the current amount and inclusions with the park, and note the provincial cap on increases (2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026). https://www.zolo.ca/cultus-lake-real-estate/3942-columbia-valley-road/28 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can the park refuse me as a buyer because of age?** In a park whose sites are reserved for tenants who have reached 55, the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation allows the landlord to refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement. Because Cultus Lake Village's own threshold is UNCONFIRMED in public sources, get the park's written consent position before writing. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Cultus Lake Village?** Listing sources differ in detail: one states dogs allowed to a 14-inch maximum at the shoulder; another records cats and dogs OK, one pet, with restrictions. Confirm the current pet rule with the park. https://www.zolo.ca/cultus-lake-real-estate/3942-columbia-valley-road/28 and https://www.zolo.ca/cultus-lake-real-estate/3942-columbia-valley-road/65
- **Q: Is there bus service to the east shore?** Route 56 Cultus Lake runs along Columbia Valley Highway on the east-shore side of the lake between Chilliwack (Cottonwood Mall / Promontory area) and the Cultus Lake Marina stop, and service is seasonal — summer only, with no year-round transit service to the Cultus Lake community. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=56
- **Q: Which schools serve an east-shore address?** No catchment is confirmed for the east shore: the sample address 3499 Columbia Valley Hwy was run in the SD33 School Locator and returned no result, because the locator's listed ranges for the road do not include that number. At area level, SD33 Administrative Procedure 611 groups the "Columbia Valley/Cultus Lake/Chilliwack Lake Road area" as Cultus Lake Elementary School → Mount Slesse Middle School → G.W. Graham Secondary School — an area pattern, not an address-level result. Run the exact address. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school and https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2023-03/611%20AP%20Transportation%20-%20Operating%20Instructions.pdf
- **Q: What hazards are documented for the east shore?** BC Parks documents an active landslide area on the Cultus Lake Horse Trail along the east side, with warning signage and advice not to cross during intense rainfall, plus steep, dangerous drop-offs along the lakeshore and a sudden change in water depth close to shore. The FVRD provides a Flood and Debris Control service in Electoral Area H. https://bcparks.ca/cultus-lake-park/
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Rd, Chilliwack, with the Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre at #104 - 7955 Evans Road, Chilliwack. There is no hospital or care home at the lake. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay at Cultus Lake?** No care or assisted-living residence at a Cultus Lake address was verified in this pass — the ladder runs to Chilliwack and Abbotsford residences. The in-lake options are independent homes only: Park leasehold houses, or a manufactured home in Cultus Lake Village if the park's age rule fits. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence
