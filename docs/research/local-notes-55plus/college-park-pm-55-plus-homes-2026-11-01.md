## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: City of Port Moody Zoning Bylaw, 2018, No. 2937.** Single-detached homes sit in RS zones and multi-residential homes in RT and RM zones. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- **OCP: Port Moody Official Community Plan Bylaw 2026, No. 3522** (current OCP; Chapter 14 sets neighbourhood policy). The Suncor refinery lands beside College Park and Seaview are designated a Special Study Area in the OCP — the main planning item hanging over this end of the city. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- **Secondary suites:** Port Moody's secondary suite guide states the City amended its Zoning Bylaw in 2004 to allow secondary suites in all single-detached residential zones (except manufactured home parks), and in 2018 to permit suites in the duplex zone and remove the maximum-size restriction in that context; a suite may not be stratified, and a building permit process applies. https://api.ghdcdn.com/portmoody-edocs/v1/eDocs/Get?docnumber=632150
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing:** SSMUH is permitted in RS and RT zones outside the transit-oriented areas; under the June 2024 Zoning Bylaw amendment (Amendment Bylaw No. 94, 2024, No. 3460), the City must allow up to four units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², and up to six units on such lots within 400 m of frequent bus stops. The City adopted A Guide to SSMUH on July 22, 2025. http://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- **Small lots — a College Park trap:** RS1-S small lot subdivision (minimum 9 m width and 325 m²) is available only in Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside OCP Single Family–Low Density areas — College Park is not on that list, so confirm any lot-split idea with the City before relying on it. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- **Manufactured-home parks:** Port Moody's secondary suite guide carves manufactured home parks out of the suite rules, but no manufactured-home park inside College Park was verified on an official source: UNCONFIRMED for this sub-area.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing I can buy in College Park?** No — not on any source checked. No College Park strata is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw; the sub-area's attached stock is two general-market townhome complexes on Seaview Drive (Stone Ridge, 2023; Cedar Ridge, 2020). https://www.zolo.ca/port-moody-real-estate/50-seaview-drive/6 and https://www.zolo.ca/port-moody-real-estate/70-seaview-drive/14
- **Q: Is the Westport Village seniors housing something I can buy or move into?** Not now. Westport Village received final (fourth reading) approval on July 25, 2023; its second phase includes a 12-storey tower with seniors housing and care facilities, but the City's May 14, 2024 housing progress report notes Phase II had only residential square footage, not a unit count, and phase timing depends on development permits and a development partner. Treat it as a future planning item, not stock. https://www.centralcharts.com/en/26450-andrew-peller-ltd-ord-a/news/4341326-andrew-peller-limited-receives-final-approval-for-port-moody-development-plans and https://pub-portmoody.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=22690
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I own a unit in a 55+ building without living there?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted anywhere in BC — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a College Park townhome?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Stone Ridge and Cedar Ridge?** Stone Ridge listings record pets allowed with restrictions; Cedar Ridge listings record cats OK, dogs OK with a two-pet number limit and restrictions. Confirm the current bylaws on the Form B for the specific unit. https://www.zolo.ca/port-moody-real-estate/50-seaview-drive/6 and https://www.zolo.ca/port-moody-real-estate/70-seaview-drive/14
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to a College Park house for a parent?** Secondary suites are allowed in all single-detached residential zones (except manufactured home parks) under the City's suite rules, and SSMUH — including coach houses and accessory dwelling units — is permitted on qualifying RS and RT lots outside the transit-oriented areas. A suite may not be stratified. https://api.ghdcdn.com/portmoody-edocs/v1/eDocs/Get?docnumber=632150 and https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- **Q: Can I split my College Park lot into small lots?** Not under the RS1-S rule as published — that option is limited to Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside. Get City confirmation before pricing in subdivision value. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Eagle Ridge Hospital is in Port Moody at 475 Guildford Way (Fraser Health), east of this area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q: How do residents get to SkyTrain?** TransLink buses connect west Port Moody to Moody Centre Station on the Millennium Line Evergreen Extension, which also serves the West Coast Express. Specific route numbers on College Park streets: UNVERIFIED. https://maps.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/system-maps/fwc26_regional_transit_connections.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Westport Village is approved but its seniors tower is Phase 2, with no unit count on record.** Andrew Peller Limited announced final approval on July 31, 2023 for the five-acre former Andrés Wines site at Clarke Street and the Barnet Highway (2120 Vintner Street; 2110–2136 Clarke Street); Phase 1 is a 31-storey tower with 263 condos and 16 market rentals plus an arts centre and childcare, while Phase 2 holds the rental tower and the 12-storey seniors housing tower. The City's May 14, 2024 progress report records Phase II as residential square footage only — 120,366 sq ft of market rental entitlement — with no unit count at that time, and the developer said it would seek development partners. No completion date for the seniors component is published: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.centralcharts.com/en/26450-andrew-peller-ltd-ord-a/news/4341326-andrew-peller-limited-receives-final-approval-for-port-moody-development-plans and https://pub-portmoody.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=22690 and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/westport-village-port-moody-presale-condos/
- **The Suncor lands question sits directly beside this neighbourhood.** The refinery lands beside College Park and Seaview are an OCP Special Study Area, meaning the land use next door is an open planning question — disclose it and check the current study status with the City. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- **Port Moody's council is actively hunting for affordable seniors housing sites — after turning one down.** In October 2025 reporting on council decisions, a Kyle Seniors Housing proposal was declined at second reading (voted 5-1 on Oct 14), and at another meeting council defeated a St. George Street / Mary Street rezoning and directed staff to investigate opportunities for affordable seniors housing, particularly for those at risk of or experiencing homelessness. Those sites are in Moody Centre, not College Park — but they show the city's seniors-housing supply is still at the proposal stage. https://sam4pomo.beehiiv.com/p/monthly-update-october-2025
- **Kyle Centre, the older-adult hub for this side of the city, is being replaced.** Kyle Centre at 125 Kyle Street (built 1977, 11,000 sq ft) is at end of life; the City has approved a redevelopment plan whose first phase — conceptual design for a new facility proposed at 39,000 sq ft, plus plaza and expanded Kyle Park — began in summer 2025 with $515,000 from the Community Amenity Contribution Reserve, and the site planning keeps room for potential future affordable housing at the west end of the City-owned lands under a separate project. https://www.portmoody.ca/news-notices-and-community-matters/posts/city-launches-plan-to-redevelop-kyle-centre-area-conceptual-design-for-new-facility-is-first-step/ and http://www.portmoody.ca/city-government/city-projects/kyle-centre-redevelopment/
- **Attached homes moved faster than detached here in September 2026.** College Park condos & townhomes ran a 22% sales ratio (18 listed, 4 sold) against 14% for detached (14 listed, 2 sold) — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
- **Hazard permit areas can apply even without a floodplain.** The OCP provides environmentally sensitive area and hazardous lands / steep slope development permit areas (DPA 4 and DPA 5), and watercourse lots need riparian assessment under Zoning Bylaw Section 5.4; no floodplain or ALR land was confirmed in College Park. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
