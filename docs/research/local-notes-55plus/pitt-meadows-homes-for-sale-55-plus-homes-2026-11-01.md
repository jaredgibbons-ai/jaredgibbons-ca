# Pitt Meadows — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. This file covers the whole city (the pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale slug is city-wide); the board sub-areas inside it are Central Meadows, Mid Meadows, North Meadows, South Meadows and West Meadows.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Pitt Meadows is bounded by the Pitt River to the west, the Fraser River to the south, the City of Maple Ridge to the east, and Pitt Lake, the Pitt River and agricultural and conservation land to the north; its land area is 85.38 km² (2016 Census figure quoted in the City's 2020 Business Plan). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163 and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- Harris Road is the main north–south street, and Harris Road at Lougheed Highway is the main town centre junction; Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) crosses the city east–west over the Pitt River Bridge, and Golden Ears Way leads to the Golden Ears Bridge over the Fraser River to Langley and Surrey. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt791.pdf
- The civic and shopping core sits along Harris Road: Pitt Meadows Family Recreation Centre (12027 Harris Road), Pitt Meadows Public Library (200–12099 Harris Road), Meadowtown Shopping Centre (19800 Lougheed Highway, with Real Canadian Superstore), Meadowvale Shopping Centre on Harris Road North (Save-On-Foods), and Osprey Village on Fraser Way at Barnston View Road. Pitt Meadows Arena is at 11435 Bonson Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/drop-schedules-hours-rates and https://fvrl.bc.ca/locations/pitt-meadows-public-library and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/dmlive-155673-v1-gpra_report_to_pitt_meadows_20190405_v2_pdf.pdf
- Transit: Pitt Meadows Station is on the West Coast Express Mission City–Waterfront line; the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus stops at Harris Road; the 701, 791, 595 (to Langley Centre via the Golden Ears Bridge) and the 719/722 loop serve the city. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt997.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt701.pdf
- School District 42 serves the city with four elementary schools (Davie Jones, 12030 Blakely Road; Edith McDermott, 12178 Bonson Road; Highland Park, 18961 Advent Road; Pitt Meadows Elementary, 11941 Harris Road) and one secondary school, Pitt Meadows Secondary (19438 116B Avenue); there is no middle tier. https://elementary.sd42.ca/daviejones/ and https://secondary.sd42.ca/pmss/
- The Pitt River Greenway's first phase runs 10.2 km west from Harris Landing (Harris Road at Fraser Way) to Ferry Slip Road along the Fraser and Pitt River dikes — the dike-top trail system is part of daily life on the city's south and west edges. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/harris-landing-pitt-river-greenway
- Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK) is in the south of the city, owned and operated by the Pitt Meadows Airport Society, with a noise concern process — relevant to homes in South Meadows. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: one tight pocket in Central Meadows — Advent Road, where the Meadowland Village strata (18960) and the Meadowlands Housing Co-operative (19032) sit side by side near Hoffman Park and Highland Park Elementary — plus The Wesbrooke seniors residence at 12000 190A Street in Mid Meadows. The city's big manufactured home park, Meadow Highlands on Hammond Road, is NOT age-restricted (see "Checked and NOT 55+"). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Flood frame for the whole city (details and a source conflict in Sections 3 and 5): most of Pitt Meadows lies within the Fraser, Pitt and Alouette river floodplains, protected by a 60 km dike system, and 78% of the land base is in the Agricultural Land Reserve. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/city-receives-412000-flood-mitigation-plan and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. Meadowland Village's recorded "Age Restricted 55+" wording is consistent with the current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. These are strata rules; the Meadowlands Co-operative's "adults 55 years and up only" designation is the co-op's own membership rule (CHF BC), and how it treats younger spouses or caregivers is a co-op-document question — UNCONFIRMED from public sources. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. A tenant in a 55+ strata must still satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Floodplain bylaw: Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008.** The bylaw designates the floodplain areas on its Schedules A and sets the Flood Construction Level (FCL) as the Designated Flood Level plus a freeboard of 0.6 metres; the FCL establishes the elevation of the underside of a wooden floor system or the top of a concrete slab for habitable buildings — and for a manufactured home, the ground level or top of a pad must be no lower than that elevation. Specific designated flood levels are set in the bylaw's schedules per area — read the lot's own figure from the bylaw/city mapping rather than assuming a single city-wide number. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 - Consolidated.pdf
- **Flood protection and the drainage levy:** most of the city lies within the Fraser, Pitt and Alouette river floodplains behind a 60 km dike system, and property taxes carry a separate drainage levy — under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026, the Class 1 Residential drainage levy is $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value (2026 figure, bylaw-stated). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/city-receives-412000-flood-mitigation-plan and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011.** Urban houses sit in the R-3, R-4 and R-6 zones (up to three, four and six units; five in R-6 unless a unit meets adaptable housing requirements); townhouses are a permitted form in R-3, R-4, R-6 and RM-1; apartment buildings are a permitted form in R-4 and R-6 and the multi-family zones. Secondary suites and garden suites are accessory uses in the RS, RR, R-4 and R-6 zones, with the City's garden suite guide setting one suite per lot at 33 sq m to 90 sq m. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Manufactured homes got their own zone in 2024 — because the old contract expired.** The City's notice for Zoning Amendment Bylaw 2991, 2024 explains that Meadow Highlands Co-operative's land use was governed by a "land use contract" that expired June 30, 2024 (as mandated by the Province), that the underlying zoning was RM-1 Multi-Family Residential — which does not permit manufactured homes — and that the amendment rezoned the property to a new Residential Mobile Home (RMH) zone to match the existing use. Pad tenancies in BC parks are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act; Meadow Highlands' co-operative tenure (members own shares/equity in the land) is documented in its listings. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/240531/MRNN240531_A13.pdf and https://www.jagsidhu.com/listing/r3095727-19630-pinyon-lane-77-pitt-meadows-bc-v3y-1z3/brochure
- **Official Community Plan:** "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," OCP Bylaw No. 2864, 2020 (adopted September 27, 2022); the North Lougheed Area Plan was adopted by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/official-community-plan-0
- **ALR is a standing check:** 78% of the city's land base is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, and A-1/A-2 subdivision minimums (8 ha and 16 ha) plus Agricultural Land Commission rules govern rural lots — relevant to any North or West Meadows acreage a downsizer considers. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Pitt Meadows?** Two products, side by side on Advent Road: a freehold strata rancher townhome in Meadowland Village (18960 Advent Rd, recorded "Age Restricted 55+"), or — by application and membership, not MLS purchase — a unit in the Meadowlands Housing Co-operative (19032 Advent Rd, "adults 55 years and up only" per CHF BC). The Wesbrooke is rental/care, and the big Meadow Highlands park is not age-restricted at all. https://www.estateblock.com/pitt-meadows/7-18960-advent-rd-39761 and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: Meadowland Village or Meadowlands Co-op — aren't they the same place?** No. Meadowland Village (18960 Advent Rd) is a freehold strata of rancher townhomes built in 1988 that you buy into; Meadowlands Housing Co-operative (19032 Advent Rd) is a 64-unit housing co-operative where you apply for membership and occupancy. Different tenure, different seller/application route, nearly identical names, same street. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3118677-19-18960-advent-road-pitt-meadows-british-columbia-v3y2g4-599900/brochure and https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/150403/MRNN150403_A03.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in Meadowland Village?** Yes — in a strata, provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. For the Meadowlands Co-operative, the household rules are the co-op's own and are not published in the sources checked — UNCONFIRMED, ask the co-op. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in Meadowland Village as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my Meadowland Village unit?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** At Meadowland Village, listing data records pets allowed with restrictions — cats OK, dogs OK, number limit one. At the Meadowlands Co-operative, CHF BC states one indoor cat is permitted. Confirm both against the current bylaws/rules before purchase or application. https://www.estateblock.com/pitt-meadows/7-18960-advent-rd-39761 and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: Is Pitt Meadows in a floodplain — and does it change what I can build or insure?** Largely yes, and it is a standard subject here, not an exception: most of the city is floodplain behind a 60 km dike system; the City's Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 sets a Flood Construction Level of the designated flood level plus 0.6 m freeboard (for a manufactured home, the pad top must meet it), and taxes carry a separate drainage levy ($0.1082 per $1,000 for Class 1 Residential in 2026). Check the specific lot's designated flood level, any covenant on title, and insurance pricing early. The City's own documents put the floodplain share at over 95% (2020 Business Plan) — one superseded City document said about 86%; see the conflict note in Section 5. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 - Consolidated.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Pitt Meadows has no hospital of its own. Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street in Maple Ridge, is the hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows, with 24/7 emergency, maternity and general surgery; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre (121–11900 Haney Place, Maple Ridge) serves Maple Ridge, Pitt Meadows and Katzie First Nation residents, open 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., 7 days a week. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- **Q: What transit and seniors services are actually in town?** The West Coast Express stops at Pitt Meadows Station (weekday commuter service to Waterfront), the R3 RapidBus stops at Harris Road, and the 719/722 loop serves the Bonson and Fraser Way streets. The Pitt Meadows Seniors Activity Centre is listed at 19065 119B Ave in the city's Community Check-In resource guide (phone 604-457-4771 in the regional Seniors Resource Directory). https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt997.pdf and https://pubhtml5.com/gbwun/lyir//basic/ and https://www.seniors-network.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/MRNN240927_Z_Seniors_prf3.pdf
- **Q: Does the airport affect the Advent Road area?** Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK) is in the south of the city; the Advent Road complexes are in Central Meadows, north of the airport lands. Noise exposure is location-specific within the south end — check the specific address against the airport's flight paths and the City's noise concern process rather than assuming a city-wide answer. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Pitt Meadows?** The in-city ladder is short: independent strata or co-op living on Advent Road, then The Wesbrooke (12000 190A St) for independent living with services and assisted living. For long-term care, residents look to Maple Ridge facilities (e.g., the care campuses in the East Central file) — no long-term care home inside Pitt Meadows appears in any source checked: UNCONFIRMED as in-city supply. https://www.thewesbrooke.com

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The two Advent Road complexes are the most confusable pair in the two cities' seniors housing.** Meadowland Village (strata, buy, 18960 Advent Rd) and Meadowlands Housing Co-operative (co-op, apply, 19032 Advent Rd) sit on the same block by Hoffman Park, both 55+, with names differing by one letter. Showings, applications and even listing remarks mix them; verify which legal entity a unit belongs to before anything else. https://www.estateblock.com/pitt-meadows/7-18960-advent-rd-39761 and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **SOURCE CONFLICT — the city's floodplain share:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the Fraser and (to a smaller degree) Pitt river floodplains; the superseded OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007 put the share at about 86%. Both are City figures from different documents and dates — use "over 95% (City 2020 Business Plan)" and do not average them. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/our-community/community-plan/section_2_-_community_overview.pdf
- **Meadow Highlands ran on an expiring land use contract until 2024.** The park's land use was governed by a provincially-mandated-to-expire land use contract (expired June 30, 2024) over an RM-1 zone that does not permit manufactured homes; Zoning Amendment Bylaw 2991, 2024 created a Residential Mobile Home (RMH) zone for it. A park whose zoning had to be invented to match its existing use is a tenure story a buyer should understand before assuming all parks sit on standard zoning. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/240531/MRNN240531_A13.pdf
- **The Wesbrooke used to house the seniors centre — it doesn't now.** Operator ads in 2013–2014 billed The Wesbrooke as "Home of Pitt Meadows Senior Rec. Centre"; the current Pitt Meadows Seniors Activity Centre is listed at 19065 119B Ave. Buyers touring The Wesbrooke on the strength of old advertising should not expect the centre inside the building. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/140226/MRNN140226_Z02.pdf and https://pubhtml5.com/gbwun/lyir//basic/
- **There is no SkyTrain and no hospital on any funded timeline in the sources checked** — the city's rapid transit is the R3 bus and the West Coast Express, and hospital care is in Maple Ridge. A funded replacement is in design for Pitt Meadows Secondary School (with a neighbourhood learning centre) on SD42's Strategic Facilities Plan — a school project, not a health one. https://www.sd42.ca/board-of-education/strategic-facilities-plan/ and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- **Sub-area markets move very differently, and the 55+ pocket sits in the strongest one for attached product.** In September 2026, Central Meadows — where both Advent Road complexes sit — carried 32 active Condos & Townhomes listings with 5 sold, while South Meadows detached ran a 45% sales ratio against Mid Meadows detached at 11% — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
- **The dike is also the trail system.** The Pitt River Greenway runs 10.2 km along the Fraser and Pitt River dikes from Harris Landing toward Ferry Slip Road — the flood infrastructure doubles as the city's signature walking route, which is why dike-adjacent streets market the trail, not the floodplain, first. Flood status still has to be checked lot by lot under Bylaw 2384. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/harris-landing-pitt-river-greenway and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 - Consolidated.pdf
