## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; sub-55 bylaws invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rental bylaws are still allowed. This overrides the "rentals not allowed" wording in older Langley building records such as Parkway Estates'. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR frame — the rule that governs this band's acreages.** Land in the Agricultural Land Reserve may generally have no more than one residence per parcel; a principal residence may be up to 500 m² without ALC application; one additional detached residence may be permitted of up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence and only where local bylaws allow it. Whether any specific parcel in this feed is in the ALR is parcel-specific: UNVERIFIED for any specific listing. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Township SSMUH does not rescue acreages.** The Township's Nov 18, 2024 amendments (Bylaw No. 6020) allow 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², subject to eligibility (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area, not larger than 4,050 m²), with no 6-unit transit tier currently applying. Most parcels in this band exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by size alone. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Suite caps differ by municipality.** Township: a secondary suite may not exceed 120 m² gross floor area, one per principal dwelling unit, and on ALR lands a suite must comply with ALC regulations (Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 definitions). City of Langley (Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300): a secondary suite may not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, and the registered owner must reside on the lot. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Property transfer tax figures: UNVERIFIED in the research pass** — the Province's PTT page could not be opened (fetch 429, stopped), so no PTT tier figure, including any over-$2M or over-$3M tier, is stated here. At this band's price level that omission matters and is flagged rather than filled from memory. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/langley-homes-over-1-8m.md

- **Q: Can an over-$1.8M acreage add another home for a parent or caregiver?** It depends on ALR status and zone, not on price. - **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a Langley 55+ strata?** Yes — the provincial younger-spouse/partner exemption applies, along with the under-19 child, adult-child and caregiver exemptions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a 55+ unit in Langley?** Long-term rental bans are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022 — even where an older building record still says "rentals not allowed." The tenant must still meet the age bylaw or an exemption; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
