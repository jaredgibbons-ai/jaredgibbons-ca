## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. There is no strata in Steelhead for this to attach to today — it is the rule a buyer would meet in the city's two 55+ strata complexes in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020** divides Mission's rural area into Rural zones RU80, RU36 and RU16, each with a Secondary Dwelling ("s") variant (RU80s, RU36s, RU16s), and Rural Residential zones RR7 / RR7s; a City rezoning notice describes RU16 as carrying a 1.6 ha minimum lot size. Any Steelhead parcel's actual zone is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/notice-rezoning-bylaws-june-1-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning
- **Secondary suites and second dwellings:** a Mission secondary suite is a self-contained living space inside a single family home; suites are not coach homes or garden cottages (those are detached secondary dwellings, which on rural lots generally require the "s" variant of the parcel's zone); suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; all suites must be registered with the City, new suites go through the building permit process, and an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus utility fees applies. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rsd25-4
- **Current plan: Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026** replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025, keeping the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction with a Regional Context Statement added. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan
- **SSMUH:** Mission has adopted the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation for zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings as of December 7, 2023, subject to eligibility and servicing; Steelhead's rural lots sit outside the urban serviced area, so whether SSMUH unit counts apply to a given lot is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **Wells, septic and ALR are the working rules on this ground:** ALR status is per-parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission's map finder; wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database; onsite sewerage must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation, including the 30 m setback from a well unless professional advice is obtained. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004
- **Property taxes:** City of Mission 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding (including unclaimed home owner grants) on July 3, 2026 — a single flat step, not a staged 5% + 5%. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ complex in Steelhead itself?** No. No age-restricted strata, 55+ park, seniors rental or care residence with a Steelhead address was verified in any source checked, and the sub-area recorded zero listings and zero sales in both splits in September 2026. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: The acreage listings include manufactured homes — is one of them a 55+ park?** No. The Steelhead feed's manufactured homes sit on owned land — one listing states expressly "on its own land (not in a park, no pad rent)". Mission's verified 55+ park is Mission Mobile Home Park in Hatzic, at 34519 Lougheed Hwy, across the city. https://jaredgibbons.ca/steelhead.html and https://www.cotala.com/tours/85367/FullPublic.pdf?i=979581669
- **Q: Where is the nearest actual 55+ ownership stock?** In urban Mission: Cherry Lane (32165 7th Ave, 20 townhouses, 1989, NWS2971) and Luther Place (7525 Martin Place, NWS2729, 1988), both with "Restricted Age: 55+" recorded in listings. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/cherry-lane-32165-seventh-ave
- **Q: Can I age in place on a Steelhead acreage and add a suite or coach house for a caregiver or family?** An in-house secondary suite can be registered with the City (building permit for a new suite; $63 annual suite fee plus utility fees), but a detached coach home or garden cottage is a secondary detached dwelling — on rural lots that generally requires the secondary-dwelling ("s") variant of the parcel's zone, which City material shows owners obtaining by rezoning (e.g. RU16 to RU16s). Servicing is well and septic, not city services. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rsd25-4
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata (in the city complexes)?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is there bus service or a care home out here if I stop driving?** No bus route into Steelhead was confirmed on a transit source: UNVERIFIED, and no care or assisted-living residence in Steelhead appears in BC's Assisted Living Registry — the registered Mission residences are at urban addresses. The West Coast Express at Mission City Station is a weekday commuter service, not local transit. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=buffer&f%5B0%5D=city%3AMission&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Mission Memorial Hospital, 7324 Hurd St, in the urban core southeast of Steelhead, provides 24/7 emergency stabilization and care, general medicine and ambulatory care (Fraser Health). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/your-community/mission/locations-in-mission
- **Q: What diligence matters most on a Steelhead acreage?** The well, the septic and the zone: the feed's own listings run on drilled wells and septic systems with propane heat, wells sit in the provincial GWELLS database, sewerage systems must meet the Sewerage System Regulation's setbacks (including 30 m from a well unless professional advice is obtained), ALR status is per-parcel, and the rural zones span very different minimum lot sizes (RU16 minimum 1.6 ha). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/notice-rezoning-bylaws-june-1-2026 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The board's Steelhead shelf and the live feed disagree, openly.** SnapStats published 0 detached and 0 attached listings for Steelhead in September 2026, while the live page's feed showed 8 active listings on November 1, 2026 (houses, manufactured homes on owned land, and bare land). The sources are a month apart and the feed includes land the residential splits may not count the same way; the exact reconciliation is UNVERIFIED — do not quote a Steelhead "market" from either source alone. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, and https://jaredgibbons.ca/steelhead.html
- **The Steelhead/Silverdale line is drawn differently by the feed than by the place names.** One of the feed's eight listings is a small lot on Silverdale Avenue described in its own text as being in the Silverdale lands, yet the feed labels it "Steelhead" — where the board actually draws the line is an open question in the research file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/steelhead.html
- **No school sits inside Steelhead, and its two nearest elementaries are in two different neighbouring communities** — Stave Falls Elementary to the northeast and Silverdale Elementary to the south — so a "Steelhead catchment" cannot be read off the community name; SD75 catchments are policy-set and address-based, and no Steelhead catchment was confirmed in this research. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075
- **Hydro history shaped this valley.** Construction of the Stave Falls Dam and Powerhouse began in 1909 and was completed by January 1, 1912 (BC Hydro), and the Hayward Lake reservoir Steelhead recreation runs on is still an active hydroelectric facility — which is why the City restricts parking on the roads leading to it at busy times. https://www.bchydro.com/community/recreation_areas/hayward_lake.html
- **A detached second home here usually starts as a rezoning, not a permit.** The secondary-dwelling ("s") zone variant is what permits a coach home or garden cottage on a rural Mission lot, and City application material for the neighbouring Stave Falls area shows RU16 owners rezoning to RU16s for exactly that purpose — while an in-house suite needs no rezoning but must be registered and carries the $63 annual fee plus utility fees. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rsd25-4 and https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **Mission's tax penalty is a single flat step.** Taxes were due July 2, 2026 and a flat 10% penalty landed on outstanding current balances on July 3, 2026 — there is no staged 5% + 5% pattern in Mission, so a one-day miss costs the full 10%. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
