# Sullivan Station, Surrey — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Plain finding up front: there is no verified 55+ housing of any kind inside the Sullivan Station sub-area. The sub-area's named complexes are family townhouse and condo product. The trap is built into the geography: the one "Sullivan"-named 55+ complex — Sullivan Mews, 15020 66A Ave — is placed by MLS in the East Newton sub-area, not this one, and is documented in the East Newton file. The nearest verified 55+ stock is Chelsea Gardens and Charleston Green in East Newton (north) and the Lakewood pair in Panorama Ridge (south).**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000** governs the sub-area; MLS data for the neighbouring Chelsea Gardens records its zoning as RM45. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf and https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/201-13870-70-ave-60859
- **Plans: the South Newton plan no longer exists as a governing document.** It was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan update (OCP Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026, replacing Bylaw 18020), and development in this area is led by the OCP; on December 2, 2024 Council endorsed Corporate Report R246, which updated South Newton's land use designations to reflect current market conditions. Read the current OCP and Zoning Bylaw 12000, not the rescinded plan. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH):** Surrey allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — the route for a Sullivan Station 55+ homeowner to add a suite or units on a detached lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Manufactured-home parks in Surrey carry a City policy layer on top of the provincial Act:** the City's Manufactured Home Park Policy brochure tells park residents the property owner must give 12 months' notice of a final moving date, can only give that notice after receiving all permits and approvals, and must pay $20,000 on or before the move-out date. Surrey's Tenant Protection Bylaw, 2025, No. 21788 does NOT apply to property governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. (No park is verified inside this sub-area; the policy is the city-wide frame.) https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21788.pdf
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. The Province's regular property tax deferment program is for people 55 and older. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing in Sullivan Station itself?** No — none was verified in any source. The sub-area's named complexes (The Uplands, Seneca Woods, Ashbury Hill, Altura, Panorama West Coast Living, Cambridge Park, Creekside Terrace) are family product with no age restriction in any source checked. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What about Sullivan Mews? It has "Sullivan" in the name.** Sullivan Mews (15020 66A Ave) is a genuine 55+ complex — but MLS places it in the East Newton sub-area, and it is not a strata: it is a membership purchase ("not mortgages possible," cash purchase, monthly fee covering taxes, heat, electricity, management, insurance and water, per the listing). It is documented in the East Newton file. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure
- **Q: Where is the nearest actual 55+ purchase to Sullivan Station?** North, in East Newton: Chelsea Gardens (13870 / 13888 70 Ave, gated, plan LMS1416) and Charleston Green (6537 138 St, gated townhouses, built 1988). South, in Panorama Ridge: Lakewood Heights (6211 W Boundary Dr) and Lakewood Place (6061 W Boundary Dr). Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption), with further defined exemptions for children and live-in caregivers. At Lakewood Heights the listing formulation is that only one occupant needs to meet the age requirement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://sothebysrealty.ca/en/property/british-columbia/region-fraser-valley/surrey-real-estate/3076580/6211-w-boundary-drive-no13/
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I add a suite or extra units to a Sullivan Station detached house for a caregiver or family member?** Surrey's SSMUH rules allow 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Is the old South Newton plan still the rulebook for this area?** No. The South Newton plan was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update and is no longer in effect; the OCP (Bylaw No. 21900) leads, with designations updated by Corporate Report R246 (endorsed December 2, 2024). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool (in the neighbouring complexes)?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or a buyer who purchases without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The sub-area's governing plan was rescinded, not amended.** The South Newton plan is no longer in effect at all — the Surrey 2050 OCP update removed it, and Corporate Report R246 (December 2, 2024) redrew the designations. - **The "Sullivan" 55+ complex is not in Sullivan Station.** Sullivan Mews sits at 15020 66A Ave and MLS files it under East Newton; the name, the sub-area and the tenure (membership, not strata) all mislead in different directions, and all three are corrected in Section 2. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure

Sources: Section 2 above.
