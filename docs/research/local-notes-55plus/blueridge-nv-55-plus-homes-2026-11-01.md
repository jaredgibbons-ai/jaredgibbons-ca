## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **District zoning (Bylaw 3210, s. 525):** R1 minimum lot 4,000 m² / 30 m width / 34 m depth; R2 minimum lot 660 m² / 18 m / 34 m, with modifiers R2(S1) 335 m², R2(S2) 1,100 m² and R2(S3) 4,000 m². Since June 2024, a principal house plus a secondary suite plus a coach house (3 units) is permitted on single-family lots inside the Urban Containment Boundary, and small-scale multi-unit housing allows up to 6 units on lots over 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **District OCP (Bylaw 7900), Schedule B** sets Streamside, Creek, Slope and Wildfire development permit areas — the live checks on Blueridge lots near the Seymour River and on the Berkley escarpment. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/blueridge-nv.md)
- **District taxes and utilities 2026:** taxes due Jul 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after Jul 2 and a further 5% after Sep 1; municipal rate 1.83419 and total residential rate 3.28195 per $1,000. Single-family utility charges total $2,571.20 (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

**Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Blueridge?**
A: None were verified. Blueridge has no confirmed condo building, townhome complex or age-restricted product of any kind — the stock is freehold detached houses protected as predominantly single-family by the Seymour Local Plan. A buyer who needs a true 55+ strata has to look outside the Seymour community (the District's verified example is Queensbrook in Delbrook, 678 West Queens Rd). (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/blueridge-nv.md) and https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/real-estate-FAQS/55-age-restricted-stratas-in-north-west-vancouver/

**Q: Is there any seniors rental in Blueridge?**
A: No — none verified inside the pocket. The nearest independent-living rental is Cedar Springs PARC, 3633 Mt Seymour Pkwy at Parkgate (outside the area), a PARC Retirement Living rental residence across from Parkgate Village. https://parcliving.ca/blog/the-best-senior-living-in-north-vancouver-cedar-springs-parc/

**Q: Is there subsidized seniors housing nearby?**
A: Not in Blueridge. The nearest non-profit seniors rental is across the Seymour River in Lynn Valley: Kiwanis Lynn Woods (2551 Whiteley Ct, operator-labelled 60+) and Kiwanis Lynn Manor (2555 Whiteley Ct, 65+), both Kiwanis North Shore Housing Society below-market rental. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/lynn-woods/ and https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/lynn-manor/

**Q: If I need assisted living later, where is it?**
A: Not in the Seymour community. The Assisted Living Registry's North Vancouver seniors residences are Cedar Garden (1250 Cedar Village Close), Amica Edgemont Village (3225 Highland Blvd) and Chartwell Churchill House (150 West 29th St) — all west-side District locations. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc

**Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to a Blueridge house so family or a caregiver can live with me?**
A: Since June 2024 the District permits a principal house plus a secondary suite plus a coach house (3 units) on single-family lots inside the Urban Containment Boundary; base zoning is R1 (min 4,000 m²) or R2 (min 660 m²) under Bylaw 3210 s. 525. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf

**Q: My spouse is under 55 — how do the provincial exemptions work if we buy into a 55+ strata elsewhere on the North Shore?**
A: The May 1, 2023 expansion of Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse/partner of the specified 55+ resident, children under 19 in that resident's care, adult children the resident cared for before age 19, live-in caregivers, and residents who lived in the unit when the bylaw passed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm

**Q: Where is the hospital?**
A: Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, is the Vancouver Coastal Health hospital serving the North Shore; the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 2nd floor, 221 West Esplanade. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre

**Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?**
A: Route 214 from Sechelt Drive at Hyannis Drive, through Berkley Road and Carnation Street to Phibbs Exchange, with some trips through to Burrard Station; at Phibbs the R2 RapidBus now runs one-seat to Metrotown (since September 7, 2026). There is no SkyTrain on the North Shore. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt214.pdf and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/

**Q: Do hazard rules affect Blueridge lots?**
A: They can be the defining check: District landslide work names the Berkley escarpment for landslide risk, managed proactively since the January 19, 2005 Berkley Escarpment landslide and updated in the 2026 Landslide Risk Assessment; OCP Schedule B also sets streamside, creek, slope and wildfire permit areas. Pull the District GeoWeb hazard layers for the specific lot. http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-1-district-landslide.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/

**Q: What do District taxes and utilities run?**
A: 2026 total residential tax rate 3.28195 per $1,000 (municipal portion 1.83419), due Jul 2, 2026, with 5% penalties after Jul 2 and after Sep 1; single-family utilities total $2,571.20 (water $932.80, sewer/drainage $1,255.60). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The Seymour Local Plan is why there is no 55+ stock here.** The plan deliberately manages slow, limited growth and maintains the predominantly single-family character of Blueridge, Seymour Heights, Riverside East and Windsor Park — attached and seniors forms concentrated instead near Parkgate Village and along Mount Seymour Parkway, outside this pocket. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/SLP_2003_6.0.pdf
- **The townhouse co-op at Parkgate that directories file under "independent living" is all-ages.** Mount Seymour Park Housing Co-operative (3822 Brockton Cres, outside Blueridge) states in its own material that it is home to "people and families of all ages" — a directory label is not an age restriction. https://chfbc.papercrane.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/application.MT_.SEYMOUR-PARK-HOUSING-COOP-2019-03-25.pdf
- **The whole Seymour side of the District has no registered assisted living.** All three of the registry's North Vancouver seniors residences sit west of Lynn Creek (Cedar Village Close, Highland Blvd, West 29th St) — a Blueridge owner who needs care later is planning a move across the District, not within the neighbourhood. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc
- **Two elementary schools, one pocket, address-based split.** Blueridge Elementary (2650 Bronte Dr) and Seymour Heights Elementary (2640 Carnation St) both sit inside this small pocket, and the boundary between them runs through it — never name a catchment school from the street alone; SD44 decides by exact address. https://blueridge.sd44.ca/ and https://seymourheights.sd44.ca/
- **Dorothy Lynas is not the catchment school.** The Seymour-area French Immersion elementary at 4000 Inlet Crescent, north of the area, is a choice program entered by SD44 registration, offered across the district regardless of geographical catchment. https://www.sd44.ca:443/registration/french-immersion/?month=09&year=2026&search=month
- **(June 2024)** The District's three-unit rule (house + suite + coach house) inside the Urban Containment Boundary dates from June 2024 — for a senior downsizing within Blueridge, the realistic "55+ strategy" here is a house with a suite or coach house for family or a caregiver, not an age-restricted purchase. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
