# Neilsen Grove — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

Spelling rule used throughout this file: the AREA is "Neilsen Grove"; the SCHOOL is "Neilson Grove Elementary".

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Neilsen Grove is a Paragon (MLS) sub-area name, not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name; it sits in north Ladner on and around the Marina Garden Estates / Hampton Cove lands, under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/202969/
- The school physically in the area is Neilson Grove Elementary — spelled "Neilson" on its School District 37 site, while the area name is spelled "Neilsen Grove" — at 5500 Admiral Boulevard. https://ng.deltasd.bc.ca/
- Admiral Boulevard, Admiral Way, Westminster Avenue, Ferry Road and Ladner Trunk Road are the streets named on TransLink's route 618 loop through this pocket; route 618 is the Ladner North loop from Ladner Exchange, returning via Ferry Road, Admiral Way, Admiral Boulevard, Westminster Avenue, Crescent Drive and 57 Street. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt618.pdf
- Ladner Harbour Park, 4905 McNeelys Way, is the City-listed park serving this shore of Ladner. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/ladner-harbour-park
- Ladner Leisure Centre (4600 Clarence Taylor Crescent), Ladner Pioneer Library (4683 51st Street) and McKee Seniors Recreation Centre (5155 47 Avenue) are the Ladner facilities serving this sub-area; Delta Hospital (5800 Mountain View Blvd, 24/7 emergency) sits beside Ladner Exchange. http://delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/ladner-leisure-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- Route 601 runs South Delta to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line) via Ladner Exchange, Ladner Trunk Road, Highway 17A and Highway 99 — the commuter link for this sub-area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Flood context: Ladner is low land protected by the City of Delta's flood protection system — over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, 31 drainage pump stations and roughly 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure. This sub-area is the harbour/marsh edge of Ladner, so the dike and slough context is closest to the surface here. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates in this sub-area: a single complex — South Pointe, the gated bare land strata on the Regatta Way / Jibset Bay / Tidewater Bay streets off Ferry Road, at the marsh and waterway edge. The much larger Hampton Cove / Marina Garden Estates strata pocket on Admiral Way is not age-restricted (see Section 2). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. For a bare land strata like South Pointe, the same Act and the same age-bylaw rules apply as for a condo strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Bare land strata specifics that matter at South Pointe:** in a bare land strata the owner typically insures and maintains the house itself while the strata maintains common property (gates, internal roads, landscaping). Listings for South Pointe describe the monthly fee as a "bare land" fee — the 5359 Jibset Bay listing recorded $113/mo and the 5379 Regatta Way listing data recorded $173/mo, at their respective listing dates. Treat both as dated listing figures; the current fee, and what it covers, comes from the Form B and the strata's financial statements. https://plsrealestate.com/british_columbia/house/delta/R2435695-5359_jibset_bay and https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/5379-regatta-way
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** assigns zoning parcel by parcel; South Pointe's listing data records zoning CD98A — a site-specific comprehensive development zone, which means the rules for adding a sunroom, suite or outbuilding are the CD zone's own, not the standard RS zones'. Confirm any addition against the CD98A zone and the strata bylaws together. https://www.zolo.ca/ladner-real-estate/5371-regatta-way
- **Seniors congregate housing in Delta's zoning:** the Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone added to Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 in 2024 lists "seniors congregate housing" as a permitted principal use for six-storey apartment development under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation — the City's route for future seniors apartment projects, none of which is proposed in Neilsen Grove in any source checked. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")**, adopted July 8, 2024, is Delta's consolidated OCP covering Ladner. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/ and http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Suites and SSMUH on the detached lots around South Pointe:** the RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use, and Delta's SSMUH amendments allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — but inside a bare land strata, the strata bylaws sit on top of the zoning, so a suite or coach house needs both to allow it. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Property tax deferment at 55+:** BC's regular deferment program lets a homeowner 55 or older during the current year defer annual property taxes on a principal residence as a provincial loan; Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026 (5% penalty after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026). Bare land strata lots are titled lots, so the same program logic applies as for any Delta house — confirm eligibility on the Province's page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What exactly is South Pointe?** A gated bare land strata (plan NWS2528) of detached single-level ranchers on the Regatta Way / Jibset Bay / Tidewater Bay streets off Ferry Road, homes built in 1989. Three separate listing sources call it Ladner's / a "55+ gated community," and one states the rule directly: "Age restriction is 55+." It is Neilsen Grove's only verified 55+ ownership complex. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3075824-5371-regatta-way-delta-bc-v4k-4r9 and https://eugenek.ca/mylistings.html/SearchResults.form?_pg=8
- **Q: How is bare land strata different from a condo at Delta Green?** At South Pointe you own a lot and a detached house on it — your own front door, yard and garden — and pay a comparatively small bare land fee for gates, roads and common areas; in a condo strata the building envelope and systems are the strata's. The trade-off: at South Pointe the roof, exterior and insurance of the house itself are generally the owner's problem. Confirm the exact division in the bylaws and Form B. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/5379-regatta-way
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at South Pointe?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy at South Pointe for a parent, or to live in later?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a South Pointe home?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets and gardening allowed?** A South Pointe listing records "pets allowed and tons of parking" and describes gardening as a reason buyers choose the complex ("downsizing without sacrificing your love of gardening"); a second listing describes a large private garden on one of the bigger lots. Pet numbers/sizes are bylaw-specific — confirm in the registered bylaws. https://eugenek.ca/mylistings.html/SearchResults.form?_pg=8 and https://plsrealestate.com/british_columbia/house/delta/R2435695-5359_jibset_bay
- **Q: Is Hampton Cove (Pilothouse, Fairwinds, etc.) also 55+? It has a clubhouse.** No. No source states an age restriction for any Hampton Cove building; Pilothouse listings describe a pet- and rental-friendly waterfront condo building marketed to executives and families, with no Restricted Age field. The Hampton Club is an amenity clubhouse, not an age bylaw. https://www.zolo.ca/ladner-real-estate/5535-admiral-way/308
- **Q: What transit serves South Pointe day to day?** Route 618, the Ladner North loop, runs through Neilsen Grove via Ferry Road, Admiral Way and Admiral Boulevard to Ladner Exchange; from the Exchange, route 601 runs to Bridgeport Station on the Canada Line. The West Coast Express does not serve Delta — Ladner Exchange buses are the transit option. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt618.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- **Q: South Pointe backs onto marsh and waterways — what about flood risk?** The complex sits at Ladner's low harbour edge, behind the City's dike system (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 pump stations, ~538 km of drainage). Lots within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch are also in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. Flood construction requirements are lot-specific — check the lot on DeltaMap and search title for flood covenants before subjects come off. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection
- **Q: Where are the hospital and seniors centre for this sub-area?** Delta Hospital (5800 Mountain View Blvd, 24/7 emergency) beside Ladner Exchange; McKee Seniors Recreation Centre (5155 47 Ave) runs programs for adults 50+ and is the home base of the free Delta Seniors Bus. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2021-06/SocialServicesInventory%20CS.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The spelling trap is real in searches.** The area is "Neilsen Grove" and the school is "Neilson Grove Elementary" (5500 Admiral Blvd, per SD37) — listings and searches split across both spellings, so a buyer watching only one spelling will miss stock, including South Pointe listings filed under the sub-area name. https://ng.deltasd.bc.ca/
- **South Pointe is 1980s stock, not new build.** Listing data dates the homes to 1989 — the same era as Ladner's other verified 55+ ownership option is not: Delta Green was built in 2021. A South Pointe purchase is a house purchase (roof, drainage, envelope) plus a strata purchase; budget and inspect for both. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3075824-5371-regatta-way-delta-bc-v4k-4r9
- **Two listing eras give two different fee figures — neither is current.** $113/mo (Jibset Bay listing) and $173/mo (Regatta Way listing data) appear in sources from different years. Anyone quoting a South Pointe fee without a date is quoting a stale number; the Form B is the only current source. https://plsrealestate.com/british_columbia/house/delta/R2435695-5359_jibset_bay and https://www.zolo.ca/ladner-real-estate/5371-regatta-way
- **The Hampton Cove pocket next door is the sub-area's growth story — and it is all general-market.** City council reports name the Marina Garden Estates townhouse development at 5311 Admiral Way and a multi-family apartment building within the Marina Garden Estates application; Polygon's Hampton Cove buildings (Pilothouse, Fairwinds, Beaufort Landing, Westhampton, Charterhouse) followed. None carries a 55+ bylaw in any source — the sub-area's new supply does not add to its 55+ supply. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/202969/ and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/84874/
- **The marina/harbour setting is the sub-area's defining feature — and its diligence item.** Ladner Harbour Park is at 4905 McNeelys Way, listings for South Pointe describe homes backing onto "the peaceful greenery of Ladner marsh and waterway," and the City directs owners to DeltaMap for streamside areas, diked channels and parcel-level flood information. Beauty and flood paperwork come from the same geography. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/ladner-harbour-park and https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection
- **There is no 55+ rental or subsidized option in this sub-area at all.** A Neilsen Grove senior who needs rental or income-based housing has to look to the Evergreen Lane cluster or Marina Park in central Ladner (City of Delta Affordable Housing Guide, April 2026) — or to Tsawwassen's KinVillage, which is a different community. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **Route 618 is a loop, and direction matters.** TransLink's timetable shows the Ladner North loop leaving Ladner Exchange via Central Avenue and Linden Drive and returning through this sub-area via Ferry Road, Admiral Way and Admiral Boulevard — the same street can be early in the trip in one direction and late in the other, which changes the real ride time to the Exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt618.pdf
