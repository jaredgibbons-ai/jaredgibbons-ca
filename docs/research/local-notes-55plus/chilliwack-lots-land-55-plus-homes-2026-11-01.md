## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **A vacant lot has no strata age bylaw to check — the building rules arrive with the building.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; age bylaws below 55 invalid since Nov 24, 2022, under Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term restrictions remain allowed. None of that machinery exists on freehold vacant land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR administration is the first land check.** Properties in the Agricultural Land Reserve are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALR regulations, ALC approval may be required before the City can approve related development applications, and the City directs owners to check ALR status on the City Webmap; an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, for placing fill or removing soil, for non-farm uses, and for subdivision or a boundary adjustment, with ALC applications referred to the City for review. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **ALC house-size rules on ALR land:** a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional residence is permitted without an application where its conditions are met — detached, 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and Chilliwack's bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm Home Plate:** the OCP includes Development Permit Area 4 – Farm Home Plate, to reduce the long-term impact of residential uses on agricultural land, and a Development Permit is required when the Farm Home Plate regulations in the Zoning Bylaw are not met. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **SSMUH — the added-unit rule for serviced urban lots:** Chilliwack's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment is Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, which amended Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 and was adopted June 18, 2024, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing, per the City's staff-report description. Eligibility is parcel-specific. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3090/Signed%20Staff%20Report%20%2D%20RZ001761%2Epdf
- **Suite and ADU figures (Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, ss. 2, 4.06, 5.04):** a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling, one accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot, garden suites are capped at 100 m², and the bylaw's parking rules sit at s. 5.04 — the added-unit side of what a serviced lot can carry for a caregiver or family member. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Development Permit Areas — wording conflict flagged:** the live page describes "Development Permit Area 2 (Hillside and Upland)" geotechnical requirements for hillside lots. The OCP Schedule D list names D-2 as "Hillside Environment" and carries a separate D-5 "Hillside Development" — the live page's DPA2 name and framing are UNVERIFIED against the current OCP list and should not be copied. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-lots-land.html and https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **What is UNVERIFIED for land in this program:** Zoning Bylaw minimum lot areas, lot widths and urban-zone minimums; the City subdivision process and Approving Officer requirements; development cost charges, frontage/off-site works and servicing requirements; water and septic servicing as a general pattern for rural lots; and parcel-level floodplain status and Flood Construction Levels for any specific lot. None of those figures is stated in this file — get the City's position on the specific parcel in writing before subjects come off. Sources: the UNVERIFIED list in ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/chilliwack-lots-land.md
- **Taxes on a land purchase:** the Province's general property transfer tax rates are 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion of value above $3,000,000. GST treatment of a vacant-lot purchase and any first-time-buyer treatment for a lot-plus-build: UNVERIFIED in this file, so no GST figure is stated here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Property taxes:** City of Chilliwack property tax notices are mailed annually at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% late payment penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law** (relevant if the "land" plan is a pad in a park instead): pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, and the assignment rules expressly recognise a park where every pad is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55, per s. 10(2)(b)(i) of the Human Rights Code — Cottonwood Village in Section 2 is such a park; Mt Cheam is not, because its listing states families are allowed. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol20/consol20/02077_pit

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a Chilliwack lot that is already "55+"?** No verified product of that kind exists. A vacant freehold lot carries no strata age bylaw, and no source records a Chilliwack lot marketed with a verified 55+ restriction of its own. Age restriction in Chilliwack attaches to the five built strata complexes and the one park in Section 2 — not to land. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: How much of Chilliwack is agricultural land?** The City of Chilliwack states agriculture accounts for 67% of the City's total land base and 29% of its economic activity, and its Official Community Plan carries an Agricultural Area Plan for that land. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Q: How do I check if a Chilliwack lot is in the ALR?** The City directs owners to the City Webmap to check ALR status; ALR properties are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and regulations, and an ALC application may be required for a new house, fill or soil removal, a non-farm use, or a subdivision or boundary adjustment. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Q: I'm 55+ and building on ALR land. What limits the house?** A principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; the OCP's Farm Home Plate permit area (D-4) also controls siting, and a Development Permit is required when the Zoning Bylaw's Farm Home Plate regulations are not met. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Q: Can a caregiver or my adult child live in a second dwelling on the parcel?** The ALC permits one additional residence without an application where its size rules are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels, detached — and City bylaws must also permit it. On a non-ALR serviced lot, the city-wide suite/ADU caps in Section 3 apply instead (suite at the lesser of 90 m² and 40%; one ADU per lot; garden suite capped at 100 m²). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: Can a serviced Chilliwack lot carry more than one home?** The City's SSMUH amendment, Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024), allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing, per the City's staff-report description — eligibility is parcel-specific and is confirmed with the City. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3090/Signed%20Staff%20Report%20%2D%20RZ001761%2Epdf
- **Q: What permit areas should I check on a hillside lot?** The OCP's Schedule D lists D-2 Hillside Environment and D-5 Hillside Development among its development permit areas, along with D-1 Drinking Water Supply and D-3 Riparian Area; which areas apply to a specific lot is a per-parcel check with the City. Note the current OCP list does not use the live page's "DPA2 (Hillside and Upland)" name. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Q: If I build a rancher and later want single-level seniors living in a complex instead, what exists?** The built product in Section 2: five verified 55+ strata complexes (Ivy Green and Spadina Regency on Spadina Avenue, Knight Lodge on Knight Road, Mountainview Estates on Yale Road, Cottage Grove on Young Road) and one 55+ manufactured-home park (Cottonwood Village on Evans Road, on First Nations leased land). Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Are the numbers on this page land prices?** No — there are no market numbers in this file. Chilliwack is a CADREB market that SnapStats does not cover, CADREB publishes statistics board-wide only with no land category, and land-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED here; pricing support comes from Paragon comparables. https://www.cadreb.com/market/
- **Q: What taxes and charges should I budget on a lot purchase, beyond the price?** The general PTT tiers are stated in Section 3, but GST treatment, first-time-buyer treatment for a lot-plus-build, land-financing terms, DCCs and servicing costs are all UNVERIFIED in this file — have a lawyer, a lender and the City confirm them for the specific parcel before writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

- **The slope, not the floorplan, is the due-diligence item on a hillside lot.** In the Chilliwack Mountain file's zoning research, the SHR (Suburban Hillside Residential) zone sets minimum lot area by slope band, from 360 m² in the lowest band to 990 m² in the highest, and DPA D-2 requires a geotechnical hazard and slope assessment before subdivision, construction, clearing or earthworks — a lot's rebuild or suite potential depends on its measured slope, not just its size or view. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- **The plan is brand new, and the agricultural plan sits inside it.** The Chilliwack 2050 OCP, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, carries the Agricultural Area Plan as Schedule B1 — older references to a standalone agricultural plan now sit inside the 2050 OCP. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf and https://chilliwack.com/ocp
