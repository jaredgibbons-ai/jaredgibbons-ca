# Quay (New Westminster) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The City of New Westminster calls this neighbourhood "Quayside"; the Paragon sub-area name is "Quay." It is the Fraser River waterfront strip along Quayside Drive, west and east of the downtown stations — high-rise and low-rise strata on a reclaimed riverfront bench, with the Westminster Pier Park and the Waterfront Esplanade/boardwalk as its front yard. http://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/community-parks and http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- Westminster Pier Park is at 600 Quayside Drive; the esplanade and boardwalk run the length of the neighbourhood along the river. http://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/community-parks
- Transit: two Expo Line stations serve the downtown end — New Westminster Station and Columbia Station — and bus route 103 runs along Quayside Drive itself. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf
- Schools: for the sample address 800 Carnarvon Street, the district pattern is École Qayqayt Elementary, Fraser River Middle School and New Westminster Secondary; School District 40 catchments are address-based. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- The market here is condos, full stop: in September 2026 the sub-area had 0 detached listings, with 59 condos & townhomes listed, 16 sold and a 27% sales ratio (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).
- Flood is the defining physical fact: the City's floodplain study puts the Quay waterfront in Zone 3, and the City's flood management material applies a Flood Construction Level to waterfront development — many newer buildings place habitable floors above that level while the boardwalk and egress sit below it. http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- Health: Royal Columbian Hospital, 330 East Columbia Street in Sapperton, is the city's Fraser Health regional referral and teaching hospital — its 388-bed Jim Pattison Acute Care Tower opened to patients May 31, 2026, with Phase 3 upgrades expected to complete in 2029. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/New-Westminster/royal-columbian-hospital and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/accessing-health-care/capital-projects
- The building stock spans two eras: late-1980s/1990s towers and courts (Anchor Pointe, Riviera, Tiffany Shores, The Excelsior, Laguna Landing, Quay West, Quayside Terrace, The Promenade) and 2000s–2010s product (The Q, Murano Lofts, Trapp + Holbrook on Columbia Street, RiverSky 1 and 2, Pier West). Every one of them was checked for an age restriction for this file — see Section 2.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**The Quay has NO verified 55+ housing of any tenure — no 55+ strata among its many towers, no retirement residence, no assisted-living registration and no seniors-specific subsidized building with a Quayside Drive address was found, despite the densest condo stock in the city.** The buildings were checked one by one (see the Checked list). Do not market any Quayside building as 55+.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None. Every named Quayside strata checked — Pier West, RiverSky 1 and 2, Anchor Pointe, Riviera, Tiffany Shores, The Q, Murano Lofts, Quay West, The Promenade, Quayside Terrace, Laguna Landing, The Excelsior, Trapp + Holbrook — shows pet and/or rental rules in its sources but no age restriction anywhere.

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- None. No purpose-built 55+ rental building with a Quay address appears in any source checked, including BC Housing's Zone 5 directory for Burnaby and New Westminster. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076

### Retirement residences and care (rental/care — NOT strata ownership)

- None in the sub-area. BC's Assisted Living Registry lists exactly two registered seniors assisted-living residences in New Westminster — Thornebridge Gardens, 649 8th Avenue (Moody Park/Uptown edge), and Victoria Heights, 230 Ross Drive (Victoria Hill) — and neither has a Quayside address. The city's long-term care home, AgeCare Royal City (77 Jamieson Court), is also east of the Quay. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANew%20Westminster&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc and https://www.agecare.ca/community/agecare-royalcity/

### Subsidized seniors rental (non-profit, income-based — not ownership)

- None seniors-specific in the sub-area. The Quay's two non-market co-ops (below) are mixed-tenure — the City's non-market inventory shows Westminster Landing's 84 units and Riverbend's 72 units spread across seniors, single, disabled and family columns rather than designated seniors-only. Citywide context: the City reports 599 independent-seniors non-market units (2023), but they are in other neighbourhoods. https://newwestcity.ca/database/rte/Housing%20web%20update%20-%20Non-Market%20details.pdf and https://www.ubcm.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/New Westminster.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. No Quayside strata checked has used this power — the absence of a 55+ bylaw is the verified fact here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why listing-data lines like Laguna Landing's "rentals allowed w/restrictions" have to be read against the current bylaws: a long-term rental ban or cap cannot stand, whatever the database says. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/laguna-landing-10-court
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001** governs the Quayside high-rise and low-rise districts; the Quay's form is set by that bylaw together with the downtown/Quayside plan designations. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Plan: Our City 2041, the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 7925, 2017**, covers the Quayside waterfront. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **Flood Construction Level — the Quay's defining rule:** the City's floodplain study places the Quay waterfront in Floodplain Zone 3, and City flood management applies a Flood Construction Level to waterfront buildings. For a buyer, the working questions are whether the unit and its parking/storage sit above the FCL, what the strata's flood and insurance documents say, and what the depreciation report budgets for envelope and parkade water risk. http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Strata document diligence on older towers:** BC strata law runs on the Form B, the registered bylaws, two years of minutes and the depreciation report — on the Quay's 1986–1997 stock (Quay West, Anchor Pointe, The Excelsior, Laguna Landing), the depreciation report and the strata's insurance summary are where a 55+ buyer's real risk sits, not in any age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **City taxes:** New Westminster 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applying July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 3, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are any Quayside Drive condos 55+?** No — none verified. The towers and courts were checked one by one (Pier West, both RiverSky towers, Anchor Pointe, Riviera, Tiffany Shores, The Q, Murano Lofts, Quay West, The Promenade, Quayside Terrace, Laguna Landing, The Excelsior, Trapp + Holbrook); pet and rental rules show up, age restrictions do not. Listing data for Pier West states it outright: "Senior Community: No." https://www.zolo.ca/new-westminster-real-estate/680-quayside-drive/3302
- **Q: But the Quay feels like a retirement area — is it just marketing?** The feel is real (flat boardwalk living, two SkyTrain stations, small units) but the legal fact is not: no building here has a 55+ bylaw in any source checked, so neighbours of any age can buy and rent in every building. Buy the lifestyle if it suits you — do not pay a "55+ community" premium for it. (Research statement: sources checked Nov 1, 2026.)
- **Q: What does the Flood Construction Level mean for my purchase?** The Quay is Floodplain Zone 3 in the City's study. Newer buildings generally keep habitable space above the FCL while the boardwalk sits below it; older buildings vary. Check the unit's floor level against the FCL, the strata's insurance and depreciation report, and the parkade's flood history — in the strata documents, not the listing. http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Q: Are the two Quayside co-ops seniors co-ops?** No. Westminster Landing (13 K de K Court, 84 units) and Riverbend (1050 Quayside Dr, 72 units) are mixed-tenure co-ops whose City inventory entries span seniors, singles, people with disabilities and families. They are not seniors-designated, and co-op membership is not a purchase. https://newwestcity.ca/database/rte/Housing%20web%20update%20-%20Non-Market%20details.pdf
- **Q: Where is the nearest retirement residence or care to the Quay?** East, not on the waterfront: Thornebridge Gardens (649 Eighth Ave, registered assisted living and independent living) and Victoria Heights (230 Ross Dr, registered assisted living), with AgeCare Royal City long-term care at 77 Jamieson Court. None is in the Quay. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANew%20Westminster&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc and https://www.agecare.ca/community/agecare-royalcity/
- **Q: Can I rent out a Quay condo?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, whatever a building database still prints ("rentals allowed w/restrictions" at Laguna Landing, for example); short-term rentals can still be restricted. Confirm the building's current bylaws and any City short-term rental rules before counting on income. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/laguna-landing-10-court
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the older Quayside buildings?** Building by building: Laguna Landing's record shows pets allowed with restrictions; others vary. Pet rules are bylaws, so the Form B and current bylaw set for the specific building govern — not the listing summary. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/laguna-landing-10-court
- **Q: If I buy here and want a quieter, age-restricted building later, what are my options?** Inside New Westminster, none verified for purchase — the city's seniors stock is rental and care (Thornebridge Gardens, the non-profit towers). A 55+ purchase means looking outside the city; wherever you find one, the same provincial rules apply: the bylaw governs who may live there (not who may own), with exemptions for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers and defined cases for children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Two stations serve the Quay — does it matter which end I buy?** Both New Westminster and Columbia stations are at the downtown (east) end of the waterfront; route 103 runs the length of Quayside Drive. The west-end courts are the quieter, more bus-dependent end — a real consideration for a car-free buyer. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf
- **Q: What should I scrutinize on a 1980s–90s Quayside tower?** The strata package: depreciation report currency, contingency reserve balance, insurance summary and deductible allocation, parkade and envelope work history — the standard BC strata document set (Form B, bylaws, minutes, depreciation report). On waterfront stock of that era, those documents matter more than any single listing feature. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The densest condo neighbourhood in the city has zero age-restricted buildings:** 59 condos & townhomes were listed in the Quay in September 2026 — more choice than the rest of the city's sub-areas — and not one building carries a 55+ bylaw in any source checked (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report; building checks Nov 1, 2026).
- **The river is both the amenity and the file's biggest caveat:** the Quay is Floodplain Zone 3, the boardwalk and esplanade sit at river level below the Flood Construction Level, and two otherwise identical units can differ materially by floor height relative to the FCL. Flood questions here are answered by the strata's documents and the City's flood material, not by the view. http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- **Two of the Quay's "seniors-looking" buildings are mixed co-ops:** Westminster Landing and Riverbend appear in the City's non-market inventory with units across seniors, single, disabled and family columns — directory adjacency to genuine seniors buildings (Dunwood, Legion Manor) makes them easy to misread as seniors co-ops. They are not. https://newwestcity.ca/database/rte/Housing%20web%20update%20-%20Non-Market%20details.pdf
- **The city's actual seniors map points inland:** both registered assisted-living residences (Thornebridge Gardens, Victoria Heights) and the long-term care home (AgeCare Royal City) sit east and uphill of the Quay — a senior who buys on Quayside Drive for the lifestyle is still travelling across town for every seniors-specific service. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANew%20Westminster&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc
- **Database rental lines lag the law:** building databases still print "rentals allowed w/restrictions" for Quayside buildings years after Nov 24, 2022, when long-term strata rental restrictions became void province-wide. The registered bylaws and the Form B are the only reliable rental answer. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/laguna-landing-10-court and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **"Quay" vs "Quayside":** the board calls it the Quay; the City, the street signs and the park call it Quayside. Searching only one spelling misses listings and City documents filed under the other. http://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/community-parks
