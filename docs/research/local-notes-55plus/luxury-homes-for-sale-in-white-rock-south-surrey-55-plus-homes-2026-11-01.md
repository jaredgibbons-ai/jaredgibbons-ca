## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). This provincial rule applies identically in White Rock and Surrey — it is the one rule that does cross the municipal line. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rental bylaws are still allowed. This is why Capistrano listings' "no rentals" wording cannot be taken at face value for a long-term rental. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **White Rock zoning: White Rock Zoning Bylaw, 2024, No. 2506** (adopted June 27, 2024, incorporating the provincial SSMUH regulations), and **Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2017, No. 2220** — both confirmed on the City of White Rock's own bylaw resources page in this pass. The research file's page-copy detail for Bylaw 2506 (RS-1/RS-2/RS-3 minimum lot sizes, RE-1/RE-2 estate zones, suite caps) was NOT confirmed against the bylaw text in the research pass (fetch 429) and stays UNVERIFIED — do not quote those figures as bylaw fact. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/luxury-homes-for-sale-in-white-rock-south-surrey.md
- **Surrey zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000** governs only the Surrey side. Surrey SSMUH: maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; one secondary suite per dwelling unit capped at 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area. None of these Surrey figures applies to a White Rock address. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Surrey OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900** (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Surrey side only; White Rock's OCP is Bylaw No. 2220 above. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Waterfront permit and flood statements — UNVERIFIED.** The page's claims about White Rock OCP Waterfront and Environmental Development Permit Areas and a Crescent Beach flood construction level of 3.30 m GSC under Surrey's R2-O zone were not confirmed against official text in the research pass (fetch 429, stopped). They are exactly the items a waterfront buyer's lawyer and insurer will test — confirm against the current OCP/bylaw text and the covenant on title. https://jaredgibbons.ca/luxury-homes-for-sale-in-white-rock-south-surrey.html

White Rock's due dates and penalty schedule are UNVERIFIED in this pass; Surrey's dates were not substituted across the line. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ product in the luxury feed itself?** No. The visible feed is detached White Rock houses with no age restriction in any source checked. The verified 55+ ownership stock for this page's wider scope is older strata product — Capistrano in White Rock and the South Surrey cluster in Section 2 — a different shelf from the oceanfront detached stock entirely. https://jaredgibbons.ca/luxury-homes-for-sale-in-white-rock-south-surrey.html

The two sides of this page run on two different rulebooks. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents and https://jaredgibbons.ca/luxury-homes-for-sale-in-white-rock-south-surrey.html
- **Q: Is there a published "luxury" market statistic for this market?** No. SnapStats publishes no luxury segment — the available figures are whole-community context (S Surrey White Rock, September 2026: detached median $1,628,000; Condos & Townhomes median $700,000, all price points). ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/luxury-homes-for-sale-in-white-rock-south-surrey.md
- **Q: 1441 vs 1442 Blackwood Street — are both 55+?** No. Capistrano at 1441 Blackwood St is described as 55+ in multiple listings; Blackwood Manor at 1442 Blackwood St states in its listing "There are no age restrictions." One digit changes the buyer pool completely. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3112172-104-1441-blackwood-street-white-rock-british-columbia-v4b3v6-600000/brochure and https://www.zolo.ca/white-rock-real-estate/1442-blackwood-street/119
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata on either side?** Yes — the provincial younger-spouse/partner exemption applies in both municipalities, along with the under-19 child, adult-child and caregiver exemptions in Regulation 7.01. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a 55+ unit?** Long-term rental bans are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022 — even where a listing still says "no rentals," as Capistrano's do. The tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Does Amica White Rock sit in White Rock?** No — its address, 15333 16 Ave, is in Surrey, despite the name. It is also rental/care, not ownership. https://www.amica.ca/white-rock
- **Q: Are the new White Rock towers (Miramar, Semiah, Foster Martin, Monaco) 55+?** No source checked states an age restriction for any of them, and a Miramar listing states the opposite ("No age restrictions"). They are general-market product named on the page for their price point, not their age rule. https://jaredgibbons.ca/luxury-homes-for-sale-in-white-rock-south-surrey.html
- **Q: What should a waterfront buyer verify that a normal buyer would not?** The Development Permit Area and flood-construction-level position — which in this research remain UNVERIFIED against official text, so they must be confirmed against the current White Rock OCP / Surrey bylaw text and the covenant on title before subjects come off. https://jaredgibbons.ca/luxury-homes-for-sale-in-white-rock-south-surrey.html
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale pool?** Yes, mechanically: the next buyer must be 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. That trade-off is part of why the verified 55+ stock here is older condo product rather than the detached luxury shelf. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
