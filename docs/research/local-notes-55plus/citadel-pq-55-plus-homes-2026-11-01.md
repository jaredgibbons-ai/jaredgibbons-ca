## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. This matters in Citadel precisely because no complex here is verified to have one — a seller marketing a Citadel townhome as "55+" would need the actual bylaw, not a site-influence tag. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. Note that older MLS sheets for Citadel complexes still print rental-restriction fields; those bylaws no longer have effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw, 2008, No. 3630.** Citadel's detached homes sit under the RS-1 to RS-4 zones; apartment zones RA1/RA2 and townhouse zones RTh1/RTh2/RTh3 exist in the bylaw, but no Citadel apartment building was confirmed from an official source. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2013, No. 3838** describes Riverwood and Citadel Heights as established residential neighbourhoods on the north side, and supports an appropriate neighbourhood commercial development in the Citadel area. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- **Secondary suites:** the City permits secondary suites only in the RS-1, RS-2, RS-3 and RS-4 zones, and a building permit is required — the main route to a caregiver or family suite in Citadel's detached stock. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/secondary-suites
- **Suite utility-charge exemptions that matter to seniors:** the City waives 50% of the extra suite utility levy when the suite is occupied by a parent or grandparent of the owner, and 100% when a paid caregiver (minimum $150/month) lives in the suite to care for a permanently disabled owner or family member; applications are annual, due by Dec 31. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills/secondary-suite-exemptions
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing:** the City permits 3 to 6 SSMUH units on RS-zoned lots without rezoning, depending on lot size, location and transit proximity. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Manufactured-home parks:** none inside Port Coquitlam's city limits on any source found, so no park rules apply in Citadel.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing I can buy in Citadel?** No — not on any source checked. No Citadel strata is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw. Port Coquitlam's one verified 55+ strata is Mayfair Terrace downtown (2266 Atkins Ave, Central Port Coquitlam). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/mayfair-terrace-2266-atkins-ave
- **Q: A listing calls Citadel Pointe "adult oriented." Does that make it 55+?** No. "Adult oriented" is an MLS site-influence tag, not a bylaw. The MLS sheet checked for Citadel Pointe lists pet bylaws only, with no age restriction — and under BC law the only valid strata age bylaw is a 55+ bylaw under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2. https://www.cotala.com/tours/84964/FullPublic.pdf?i=320027865 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in PoCo?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Citadel townhome?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, whatever an older MLS sheet or bylaw package still prints. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed in Citadel's townhome complexes?** At Citadel Pointe, the MLS sheet checked lists cats OK, dogs OK, with a two-pet number limit and restrictions. Other complexes: check the specific strata's bylaws on the Form B — pet rules are building-by-building. https://www.cotala.com/tours/84964/FullPublic.pdf?i=320027865
- **Q: Can I add a suite in a Citadel house for a parent or caregiver?** Secondary suites are permitted in the RS-1 to RS-4 zones with a building permit, and the City halves the extra suite utility levy when the suite is occupied by a parent or grandparent of the owner, and waives it entirely when a paid caregiver lives in the suite to care for a permanently disabled owner or family member. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/secondary-suites and https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills/secondary-suite-exemptions
- **Q: Where is the nearest seniors housing if I need care later?** The in-city care options are downtown: Hawthorne Seniors Care Community (2111 Hawthorne Ave, assisted living and long-term care through Fraser Health) and the registered assisted-living residences on Kelly Avenue. None are in Citadel. https://hawthornecare.com/ and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3APort%20Coquitlam&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASupportive%20Recovery&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Eagle Ridge Hospital at 475 Guildford Way in Port Moody is the Tri-Cities hospital; Royal Columbian Hospital in New Westminster is the larger regional hospital. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q: Is Citadel in a floodplain?** Citadel Heights is elevated ground, but a significant portion of Port Coquitlam is within the Fraser and Pitt River floodplain behind dykes — whether a specific Citadel lot is in the floodplain is UNVERIFIED and must be checked lot by lot with the City. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/port-coquitlam-reminds-residents-prepare-possible-flooding
- **Q: What transit serves Citadel?** TransLink buses serve north Port Coquitlam from Coquitlam Central Station and Port Coquitlam Station; the West Coast Express is commuter-only from Port Coquitlam Station downtown. Specific route numbers stopping inside Citadel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **There is no SkyTrain coming to Port Coquitlam on any funded timeline.** TransLink's Transport 2050: 10-Year Priorities commits only to an "exploratory business case" for a Millennium Line extension to Port Coquitlam, still simply "underway" in the Mayors' Council's Feb 19, 2026 work plan — no route, stations, funding or dates approved. Citadel buyers commute by bus to Coquitlam Central or by the commuter-only West Coast Express. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/about-translink/governance-and-board/council-minutes-and-reports/2026/february/report--2026-mc-budget--feb-2026.pdf
- **The OCP leaves a door open at Citadel's edges.** The Official Community Plan supports an appropriate neighbourhood commercial development in the Citadel area — worth a City conversation for corner or edge lots, and a land-use watch item for nearby owners. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf
- **Flood works are underway elsewhere in the city.** The City's Cedar Drainage Pump Station, part of the Pitt River dike system, is being rebuilt with construction scheduled summer 2026 to summer 2027 — a reminder that the dike system behind the city's low-lying areas is active infrastructure, even for buyers on Citadel's high ground checking lot-level status. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects/cedar-drainage-pump-station-0
- **Detached and attached homes here move at very different speeds.** In September 2026 Citadel detached homes posted a 45% sales ratio (20 listed, 9 sold) while condos & townhomes sat at 4% (23 listed, 1 sold) — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
