## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The strata age-bylaw rules, for the record — with no local 55+ strata to apply them to.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; age bylaws below 55 invalid since Nov 24, 2022, under Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term restrictions remain allowed. A Ladner Rural farm buyer gets none of this machinery, because a farm purchase is freehold land, not a strata lot — and the one strata in the sub-area, Canoe Pass Village, has no verified age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR housing caps are the rules that actually bind a 55+ farm buyer.** Land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. ALR residences cannot be stratified. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The second-dwelling rule is the ageing-in-place rule on a farm.** The ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, with a total floor area of 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and Delta's bylaws must also permit it. That is the legal route to a caregiver's or family member's dwelling on a Ladner Rural farm; anything larger needs an ALC application. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Delta's A1 zone states the same limits in the bylaw:** one farm house per lot, maximum total floor area 500 m², on a farm home plate of at most 3,600 m² (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate); one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot at the 90 m² / 186 m² limits on the same farm home plate; an additional farm house requires a Professional Agrologist's necessity report and written ALC approval; and subdivision in A1 is not permitted except with OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, with a minimum lot size of 8 ha apart from the home site severance exception. A parcel's ALR status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address, and the ALC states its online tools are for general reference only. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Float-home tenure is a separate diligence file.** The West River Road cards are float homes in a marina setting per their listing texts, with boat slips described as part of, or rented through, the complex. The tenure, moorage and legal structure of the Canoe Pass float-home community: UNVERIFIED — no official, developer or strata source for that community was opened for this file, and listing text alone does not establish tenure, moorage cost or how financing and insurance treat a float home. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-rural-guide.html
- **Farm classification is a continuing obligation, not a one-time label.** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income requirements at farm gate prices are $2,500 if the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha. A downsizing buyer who stops producing can lose the classification and its tax treatment. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Water, drainage and flood are operating rules here, not background:** the City of Delta operates and maintains 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure, and over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure — dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection; in the agricultural area the ditches and pump stations function in drainage mode in winter and in irrigation mode in summer, with control gate structures regulating water levels. Parcel-level flood construction levels: UNVERIFIED for any parcel. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Property taxes:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026; the Province's regular property tax deferment program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ home I can actually buy in Ladner Rural or on Westham Island?** No — none was verified in any source. There is no age-restricted strata, park, co-op or life lease in either part of this guide's scope; the housing is freehold farm parcels and the all-ages Canoe Pass float homes on West River Road. Ladner's verified 55+ purchases (Delta Green, Laburnum Park Place, South Pointe) are in Ladner proper. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: The guide title names Westham Island. Where is its 55+ section?** There is none in the guide page text read for the research file — no Westham Island section and no Westham Island-labelled listing card appears, despite the title. That is a flagged live-page gap, not a finding that Westham Island stock was checked and described on the page. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-rural-guide.html
- **Q: Is Canoe Pass Village a 55+ community?** No source states an age restriction for it. It is a 43-home floating-home strata community per the Floating Homes Association listing, in the Ladner Rural sub-area per listing data — a marina lifestyle community, not an age-restricted one. https://www.floatinghomesbc.ca/links-and-contacts and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3075702-13-3459-w-river-road-delta-bc-v4k-4y6
- **Q: I'm 55+ and buying a farm to downsize onto. What limits the house I can build?** On ALR land, a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; Delta's A1 zone repeats the 500 m² farm house cap and the farm home plate maximum of 3,600 m². Confirm the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps before designing anything. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can my adult child or a caregiver live in a second dwelling on the farm?** The ALC permits one additional residence without an application where its size rules are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels, detached — and Delta's bylaws must also permit it; Delta's A1 zone places that accessory farm dwelling unit on the same farm home plate as the farm house. There is no strata-style caregiver exemption here because there is no strata; the land rules are the whole answer. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Ladner Rural farm parcel for family lots?** Only within tight limits: in the A1 zone a new lot created by subdivision needs a minimum of 8 ha, Official Community Plan compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: If I stop farming, do I keep farm-class taxes?** Not automatically: BC Assessment requires a producing farm to keep meeting minimum gross farm income thresholds that scale with the operation's size ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha). Plan the production, or lease the land to someone who produces, before assuming the classification survives. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: What do I actually own with a West River Road float home, and can I finance it?** UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked: the listings describe float homes with boat slips in a strata-style marina community, but no official or strata source for the Canoe Pass community's tenure, moorage or legal structure was opened, and listing text does not establish what is owned, what moorage costs, or how lenders and insurers treat the home. Diligence the tenure documents before the lifestyle. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-rural-guide.html
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+ on a farm property?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. Farm classification is a separate BC Assessment matter and is not affected by deferment. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and what transit serves a rural address?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner Village — Fraser Health's community hospital for Delta with a 24/7 emergency department. For transit, route 601 serves Ladner Exchange beside the hospital via Ladner Trunk Road and Highway 99 to Bridgeport Station; a bus route serving West River Road itself is UNVERIFIED. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
