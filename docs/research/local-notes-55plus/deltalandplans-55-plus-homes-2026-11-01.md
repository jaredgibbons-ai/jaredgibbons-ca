# Delta Land Plans (Delta) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Framing note: this is a TOPIC page, not an area page — a Delta-only editorial guide to the City's Official Community Plan, Zoning Bylaw and SSMUH rules ("Delta Land Plans, Zoning & OCP Guide"). The 55+ angle on Delta's land plans is specific: Delta delivers almost all of its seniors housing through site-specific comprehensive development zones and, since the RA6 zone was added, through a named "seniors congregate housing" use in its apartment zones — while the OCP that agents still cite with area-plan schedules (Bylaw No. 3950) no longer exists. Section 2 lists Delta's verified 55+ complexes city-wide as the built evidence of those plan rules.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. The age rule lives in the strata's bylaws, not in the City's land plans — the plans decide where seniors buildings may be built, not who may live in them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. The bylaw restricts residency, not ownership: under-55s can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** as of Nov 24, 2022; stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future"), adopted July 8, 2024** — a single consolidated plan. There is NO Schedule D.1 and no Tsawwassen or Ladner Area Plan in Bylaw No. 8400; those area plans were schedules of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, which 8400 superseded. The OCP designates farm land Agriculture (A) (s. 1.5.1) and carries Agriculture policies at s. 2.6. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017.** Seniors housing in Delta is typically delivered through site-specific comprehensive development zones — The Waterford's site is CD Zone No. 276 (seniors' congregate housing and a seniors' congregate care facility); South Pointe sits in CD98A; the KinVillage Phase 2 application would rezone 1050 54A Street from CD289 to a new comprehensive development zone. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/71542/ and https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921
- **"Seniors congregate housing" is a named use in Delta's newer apartment zones.** The Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone added to Bylaw No. 7600 lists apartment building and seniors congregate housing as principal uses, for six-storey residential development along arterial and collector streets under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation — the as-of-right route for future seniors buildings, replacing the one-off CD rezoning. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing:** Delta's SSMUH rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot — the plan-level route for a 55+ homeowner to add a suite or coach house. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **ALR housing caps bind the farm belt, not the villages.** On ALR land a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area with a suite only inside the principal residence, and one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at 90 m² or less (parcels of 40 ha or smaller) or 186 m² or less (larger parcels), where Delta's bylaws also permit it; ALR residences cannot be stratified. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property taxes:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026; the Province's regular property tax deferment program is for people 55 and older. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does Delta's OCP still have a Tsawwassen or Ladner area plan I should read?** No. Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) is a single consolidated plan with no area-plan schedules; the schedules in older documents belonged to the superseded OCP Bylaw No. 3950. Read Bylaw 8400 and the Zoning Bylaw for the parcel. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: How does a new seniors building get approved in Delta?** Historically, by site-specific comprehensive development zoning — The Waterford's CD Zone No. 276 permits seniors' congregate housing and a seniors' congregate care facility, and KinVillage Phase 2 is proceeding as a CD rezoning (City File LU009921). The RA6 apartment zone now also lists seniors congregate housing as a principal use along designated corridors. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/71542/ and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/ and https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921
- **Q: Can I build a 55+ complex on a Delta farm parcel?** No — the farm belt is designated Agriculture (A) in the OCP and zoned A1; ALR residences cannot be stratified, and the ALR housing rules cap residences per parcel. Age-restricted strata is a village / town-centre product in Delta. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to my Delta house for a caregiver or my parents?** Delta's SSMUH rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the zoning bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot. On ALR land the provincial caps in Section 3 apply instead. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption), with further defined exemptions for children and live-in caregivers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner — Fraser Health's community hospital for Delta with a 24/7 emergency department. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **Q: Are Delta's low areas flood-protected?** The City is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure — lot-level flood construction requirements still have to be checked parcel by parcel, and hazard permit areas can limit SSMUH additions. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: What seniors housing is actually in the pipeline?** KinVillage Phase 2 in Tsawwassen: a proposed six-storey building with 143 seniors affordable rental units plus a replacement community centre at 1050 54A Street, in the rezoning process as City File LU009921, with the units to be secured as affordable seniors rental under an operating agreement with BC Housing. It is a proposal, not available housing. https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The OCP most agents still describe no longer exists.** Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) superseded Bylaw No. 3950 outright — there is no Schedule D.1 and no Tsawwassen or Ladner Area Plan in the current OCP, and any page or listing still citing them is citing a repealed plan. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Delta's seniors housing was built one rezoning at a time.** CD276 (The Waterford), CD98A (South Pointe), CD289 → proposed new CD (KinVillage Phase 2): the pattern in the built stock is site-specific comprehensive development zoning, which is why two Delta seniors buildings blocks apart can sit under entirely different zoning rules. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/71542/ and https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921
- **The RA6 zone changed the route.** Seniors congregate housing is now a named principal use in the RA6 apartment zone for six-storey development along arterial and collector streets under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation — future seniors buildings in Delta may not need a one-off rezoning. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **This page and land-use-plans overlap, and the program says so openly.** deltalandplans is Delta-only with a full editorial guide; land-use-plans is titled for four municipalities and showed no editorial body in its fetch. They are not the same page, and the duplication question sits with Jared in the research file rather than being papered over. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/deltalandplans.md
- **Delta publishes its own Affordable Housing Guide** (April 2026 edition), which lists the city's below-market and seniors product building by building — Evergreen Court, Evergreen Plaza, Marina Park and the Augustine House assisted living program among them — and is the fastest official cross-check on any Delta seniors claim. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
