# Riverdale (Richmond) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Riverdale is a small established residential pocket in north-west Richmond on Lulu Island, in the City's Thompson planning and recreation area: the Riverdale Drive pocket west of No. 2 Road and south of Westminster Highway, running south toward Granville Avenue, with the Middle Arm / River Road dyke country at its north side. The City's park page for McCallan Neighbourhood Park, 6360 Coltsfoot Drive, locates that park "West of No. 2 Road and South of Westminster Hwy., off Riverdale Drive and Larkspur Avenue" and lists its recreation area as Thompson. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=79
- Planning status, stated openly: "Riverdale" is not a City of Richmond OCP area or sub-area plan name in the City publications checked — the City plans and serves this ground as part of Thompson. "Riverdale RI" is the board-style sub-area name, and board sources draw it wider than the Riverdale Drive streets: listings file Lynas Lane (from The Hamptons at 5500 Lynas Lane, by Dover Park at the Westminster Highway end, south to the 6655 Lynas Lane condos beside Thompson Community Centre) and Tiffany Boulevard in Riverdale RI. No City boundary for a Riverdale neighbourhood is published — UNVERIFIED as a bounded area. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm and https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/5500-lynas-lane/410
- Nearby areas: Terra Nova to the west across the dyke-side streets, Thompson / Granville and Quilchena to the south and east, the City Centre / Brighouse to the east along Westminster Highway, and Sea Island across the Middle Arm to the north. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/centres/thompson.htm
- Parks and trails: McCallan Neighbourhood Park, 6360 Coltsfoot Drive, is a 3.514-acre park inside the pocket with a sports field, basketball half-court, playground, dogs off-leash area and trails, beside the McCallan Road Trail — the greenway connecting the Railway Avenue cycling corridor and the Terra Nova / West Dyke Trails to the west with the Middle Arm Dyke Trails to the east. Listings for The Hamptons also place Dover Neighbourhood Park directly across from 5500 Lynas Lane at the pocket's north end. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=79 and https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/5500-lynas-lane/410
- Recreation: Thompson Community Centre, 5151 Granville Avenue, is the community centre for the Thompson area, at the pocket's south-east side; the Lynas Lane condos sit beside it. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/centres/thompson.htm
- Schools: School District 38 (Richmond) catchments are address-based with no middle-school tier (elementary K–7, secondary 8–12). Listings for the pocket's complexes name Archibald Blair Elementary and J.N. Burnett Secondary (5011 Granville Avenue) as the catchments for the Lynas Lane streets — listing-stated; the SD38 locator result for any specific Riverdale address is UNVERIFIED, so check the exact address. There is no SD38 school named Riverdale Elementary in Richmond — Riverdale Elementary is in Surrey, under Surrey Schools (SD36). https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r2953004-6651-lynas-lane-309-richmond-bc-v7c-3k8/brochure and https://www.surreyschools.ca/Riverdale
- Library and health care: no library branch sits in the pocket; Richmond Public Library's nearest branches are the Brighouse (Main) Branch, 100-7700 Minoru Gate, and the Steveston Branch (Easthope Hub), 4320 Moncton Street. The nearest hospital is Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway (Vancouver Coastal Health); Richmond City Centre Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #110, 4671 No. 3 Road. https://rpl.yourlibrary.ca/about/hours-locations and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/richmond-city-centre-urgent-and-primary-care-centre/plan-your-visit
- Transit: TransLink route 401 (One Road) runs along Westminster Highway at the pocket's north edge, from Richmond–Brighouse Station (Canada Line) via No. 3 Road and Westminster Highway to Steveston via No. 1 Road; TransLink's published SkyTrain timetable effective September 7, 2026 gives 26 minutes from Richmond–Brighouse Station to Waterfront Station. Route-level detail for the pocket's interior streets is UNVERIFIED. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt401.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt991.pdf
- Where the seniors housing actually is: **nowhere verified inside the pocket.** Riverdale's low-rise condo stock on Lynas Lane looks like classic downsizer product — small one- and two-bedroom units beside a community centre — but a listing for the cluster states "No age restriction" in plain words, and no 55+ strata, 55+ rental, retirement residence or subsidized seniors building with a Riverdale address was verified in any source checked (Section 2). The pocket's one seniors-adjacent institution, the Granville Gardens housing co-operative at 6800 Lynas Lane, is an all-ages co-op on its operator's own description, not seniors housing and not ownership.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **The City's own seniors threshold is 55.** Richmond's 2019 Housing Needs and Co-Location staff report discusses seniors' housing need with seniors defined as individuals aged 55 years and older. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_2_HousingNeeds_CoLocationOpp_PLN_071619_54160.pdf
- **City zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500, Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1)** are the pocket's documented house zones, with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m², RS1/K 315.0 m² and RS1/G 2,000 m², and a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones; Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL also apply in the bylaw as amended. The wide subzone range (360 m² to 2,000 m²) is why lot-by-lot verification matters more in this pocket than most. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Suites — the actual ageing-in-place mechanism here:** a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in RS1/RS2 — Section 5.4 states a suite minimum of 33.0 m² and a maximum of the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m² — and the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit such as a coach house per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. A suite or coach house is how a caregiver or family member lives on a Riverdale lot. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Plan: the City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000,** covers the pocket as part of Thompson; no plan or approved major project specific to a Riverdale pocket was confirmed. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **Flood — Richmond is a dike-protected island city:** Richmond is made up of islands within the floodplain of the Fraser River at an average of 1 metre above sea level; the City's flood protection system includes 49 km of dikes and 39 drainage pump stations, is designed to handle a 1:500 flood event, and dikes are being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. New construction is subject to flood construction levels and dike setbacks under the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 — directly relevant at the pocket's dyke-side north end, on the Middle Arm. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Aircraft noise — the Sea Island side of Richmond:** aircraft noise policy for Richmond is set out in City Bulletin DEVAPPS-05 and the Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy Area; a street-by-street noise contour finding for Riverdale is UNVERIFIED, but this pocket sits on the YVR side of the city and the question belongs in every purchase file here. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/devapps0534378.pdf
- **Property taxes and utilities:** City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. Richmond's 2026 typical single-family metered utility total is $2,251 per year, including a $342 flood protection line. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm and https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/finance/utility-fees/utilityrates.htm
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** (Zone 3: Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025). https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Riverdale?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata in the pocket — the Lynas Lane condos that look like downsizer stock carry no age restriction (one listing says so in plain words), and the rest of the pocket is detached houses and family townhouses. A 55+ buyer here is buying a regular house, townhome or condo with no age bylaw, for the dyke trails and the Thompson Community Centre. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: The Lynas Lane condos beside Thompson Community Centre must be a seniors complex — aren't they?** No. The 90-unit low-rise at 6655 Lynas Lane (built 1981) is marketed to first-time buyers, downsizers and investors alike, with rentals and pets allowed; a listing for the adjoining building at 6651 Lynas Lane states "No age restriction, pets allowed, and rentals permitted" and calls it a family neighbourhood. Small units beside a community centre are not an age bylaw. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r2953004-6651-lynas-lane-309-richmond-bc-v7c-3k8/brochure and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3113027-114-6655-lynas-lane-richmond-british-columbia-v7c3k8-468000/brochure
- **Q: Is Granville Gardens at 6800 Lynas Lane seniors housing? It is in BC Housing's seniors directory.** On the operator's own description, no: Granville Gardens Housing Co-operative describes itself as a 94-home resident-run co-operative founded in 1982, open in its amenities to "residents of all ages," with income-based housing charges — and co-op membership is not ownership in any case. The BC Housing directory placement and the operator's description conflict; the operator is the source relied on here, so it is NOT counted as 55+ housing. https://www.granvillegardenshousingcoop.ca/ and https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: Is the new Rio Vista building on No. 2 Road a seniors building?** No — it is mixed affordable rental. The City describes Rio Vista (5491 No. 2 Road, opened March 2026) as 80 homes for "single people, families and seniors," from studios to three-bedroom units, at low- and moderate-income rents. Seniors can live there; it is not age-restricted, and it is rental only. https://RICHMOND.CA/services/affordable-housing/HousingProjects/pathways.htm
- **Q: How do seniors actually age in place in Riverdale?** In their own houses, using the suite rules: RS1/RS2 lots allow a secondary suite as a secondary use (33.0 m² minimum; maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling or 110 m²), and the City allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit such as a coach house per lot on covered lots — space for a caregiver or family member on the same lot. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If we buy into a 55+ strata elsewhere in Richmond, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: Is Riverdale in a floodplain?** All of Richmond sits behind the perimeter dike system; Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and a flood construction level, and this pocket's north end is the Middle Arm dyke itself — the McCallan Road Trail runs on that system between the West Dyke and Middle Arm Dyke Trails. Get the parcel-level dike and flood-construction position early on any purchase here. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm and https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=79
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Riverdale?** No retirement residence, assisted-living residence or care home with a Riverdale address was verified — the care step means leaving the pocket for Richmond's City Centre / Steveston / Hamilton residences or long-term care elsewhere in the city, with subsidized rental matched city-wide through BC Housing's Housing Registry. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=city%3ARichmond&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The board's Riverdale is bigger than the City's Riverdale streets.** The City evidence anchors the name to Riverdale Drive, off No. 2 Road south of Westminster Highway — but board sources file addresses from 5500 Lynas Lane (at Dover Park, by the Westminster Highway dyke end) all the way south past Granville-side streets in Riverdale RI. A buyer searching "Riverdale" is shopping a wider strip than the pocket the City park page describes, and neither boundary is officially published. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=79 and https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/5500-lynas-lane/410
- **The pocket's newest housing opened in March 2026 — and it is not seniors housing.** Rio Vista, the six-storey, 80-home City / BC Housing / Pathways Clubhouse building at 5491 No. 2 Road, was completed and officially opened in March 2026 for singles, families and seniors at low- and moderate-income rents. It is the most likely building a "Riverdale seniors" search will surface this year, and it is mixed rental. https://RICHMOND.CA/services/affordable-housing/HousingProjects/pathways.htm
- **The co-op in the seniors directory is not taking members anyway.** Granville Gardens Housing Co-operative's own site states membership is not currently being accepted (checked November 2026) — on top of describing the 94-home co-op as all-ages. Both facts have to be corrected before anyone sends a senior there from the BC Housing directory listing. https://www.granvillegardenshousingcoop.ca/
- **There is no Riverdale school in Riverdale.** Riverdale Elementary is in Surrey (SD36); Richmond's SD38 has no school by that name, and the pocket's listing-stated catchments are Blair Elementary and Burnett Secondary. School marketing that trades on the neighbourhood name is a trap in both directions. https://www.surreyschools.ca/Riverdale and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r2953004-6651-lynas-lane-309-richmond-bc-v7c-3k8/brochure
- **The Hamptons' build year is not settled in the sources:** listings give 1995 and 1996 for 5500 Lynas Lane in different places. Minor — but check it against the strata plan before repeating it, the same caution as the pocket's other listing-stated details (its rainscreen, window, metal-roof and EV-charging upgrades are likewise listing-stated). https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/5500-lynas-lane/410 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3105349-306-5500-lynas-lane-richmond-british-columbia-v7c5r5-749900/brochure
- **The dike is part of the utility bill:** Richmond's 2026 typical single-family metered utility total of $2,251 per year includes a $342 flood protection line — in Riverdale that charge buys the Middle Arm dike at the end of the pocket's streets. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/finance/utility-fees/utilityrates.htm
- **Richmond's two money dates:** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% on any outstanding balance after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
