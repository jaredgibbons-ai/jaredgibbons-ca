## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. This is the test the Section 2 flags turn on: a complex marketed as "age restricted" without a stated threshold — Ocean Park Village — cannot be assumed to meet it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This matters directly on this strip: building directories still record "rentals not allowed" for Ocean Park Village and Ocean Park Courtyards, which is pre-2022 directory language — the provincial rule, not the directory line, governs long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/ocean-park-village-12928-17-ave
- **Plans and zoning:** the current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 (finally adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the replaced Bylaw No. 18020 as current. Surrey's zoning bylaw is Zoning By-law No. 12000, and the Crescent Beach Local Area Plan guides development in Crescent Beach itself. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans
- **Crescent Beach floodplain building rule:** applications for properties in the Crescent Beach floodplain should meet the Provincial Flood Construction Level of 3.30 m G.S.C., measured to the top of slab or underside of the floor system, a geotechnical engineer (P.Eng.) is required for construction within a Surrey floodplain, and for most Crescent Beach properties connection to the City storm sewer is not feasible — most streets have no storm sewer mains. This governs building and major renovation work, lot by lot, on the Crescent Beach side. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/crescent-beach-floodplain-residential-building-permit
- **Secondary suites in Surrey:** a secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area under Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000; only one suite is permitted per dwelling unit, and a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing in Surrey:** the City adopted its SSMUH zoning changes on July 8, 2024 — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
