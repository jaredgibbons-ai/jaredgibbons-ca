# Tsawwassen Farms, Delta — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- This is a topic page, not a neighbourhood: "Tsawwassen farms" is the agricultural fringe of Tsawwassen in the City of Delta — the farm land on the Tsawwassen edges (the Southlands side and the farm belt between Tsawwassen and Ladner), not the urban town centre. Delta's Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), designates that farm land Agriculture (A), a designation whose stated purpose is to maintain agricultural lands (OCP s. 1.5.1, Map 2). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Tsawwassen is one of Delta's three communities (Ladner, Tsawwassen and North Delta), planned under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024); the current OCP consolidated the former area plans — the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950 — into a single Land Use Plan, and its Agriculture policies (OCP s. 2.6) apply to Delta's agricultural land. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Tsawwassen First Nation Lands sit on Tsawwassen's western edge. Tsawwassen First Nation is self-governing, with jurisdiction over land management on Tsawwassen Lands — over 724 hectares transferred under the Tsawwassen First Nation Final Agreement — so City of Delta zoning and Delta farm parcels do not extend onto Tsawwassen Lands. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- There is no single MLS sub-area for this topic; Tsawwassen farm parcels sit at the edges of the board's Tsawwassen sub-areas and in the Ladner-side farm belt, and a specific parcel's sub-area label is a listing question, not a planning fact. Source: the topic research file for tsawwassen-farms (batch 7), based on the OCP above. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Flood context: the City of Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure; the City is raising sections of the Boundary Bay dike between 72 Street and 88 Street with provincial grant funding. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- School District 37 (Delta) catchments are address-based; for the sample address 1299 56 St the SD37 School Locator result is Cliff Drive Elementary (K–7), 5025 12 Ave, and South Delta Secondary (8–12), 750 53 St — a town sample, not a farm-parcel catchment, and any farm address must be run in the locator itself. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: not on the farm fringe. No 55+ complex of any kind was verified on the Agriculture (A)-designated farm edges; Tsawwassen's verified 55+ stock is in Tsawwassen Central, around 54A Street, 55 Street, 56 Street and 10–12 Avenues — see Section 2.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** is Delta's zoning bylaw, and the **Official Community Plan is Bylaw No. 8400 (2024)**; farm land on the Tsawwassen edges is designated Agriculture (A) in the OCP (s. 1.5.1), and the OCP's Agriculture policies (s. 2.6) recognize farming as the primary use of agricultural land and protect the Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) and soil-based agriculture. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **ALR housing rules (Agricultural Land Commission):** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. A specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Second dwelling on ALR land:** the ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, with a total floor area of 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and Delta bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Delta farm zoning (A1 Agriculture zone, Bylaw No. 7600):** one farm house per lot, a farm house maximum of 500 m², a farm home plate maximum of 3,600 m², one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit of maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on lots over 40 ha. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **Subdivision of farm land:** in the A1 zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is permitted only where it complies with the Official Community Plan Bylaw and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy; A3 zoning does not permit subdivision. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Delta secondary suites and added units off the ALR:** Delta's 2023 amendment to Bylaw No. 7600 (Bylaw No. 8242) removed the minimum and maximum floor area requirements for secondary suites and set the suite floor area at less than 50% of the gross floor area of the single detached dwelling (excluding an attached garage); Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area — on ALR land the ALC rules above sit on top of these. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/218413/ and http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands):** no building with a habitable floor below 2.9 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum, a minimum land surface elevation around construction of 2.3 metres GSC datum, and no building within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area — directly relevant to Southlands-side farm parcels. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Streamside rules on farm land:** all properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — farm ditches count. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Property taxes and 55+ relief:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. The provincial regular property tax deferment loan program covers homeowners 55 and older, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) covers seniors 65+. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Subsidized seniors rental applications** for Delta run through BC Housing's Housing Registry (Zone 3 — Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025). https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ home I can buy on a Tsawwassen farm or acreage?** None verified. The farm fringe is Agriculture (A)-designated freehold land with no strata, so no age bylaw can attach to it, and no 55+ strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source. Tsawwassen's buyable 55+ stock is two condo buildings in Central: The Lexington and Tsawwassen Greene. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street
- **Q: How do I check whether a Tsawwassen-edge parcel is in the ALR?** On the Agricultural Land Commission's maps, by PID or civic address — reserve status controls residences (the 500 m² principal cap), the one-additional-residence size limits, and most use permissions. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I build a second home on an ALR farm for a family member or caregiver?** One additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and Delta bylaws must also permit it; the A1 zone allows one accessory farm dwelling unit within those same size limits. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I put a suite in the farm house?** On ALR land, a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; Delta's A1 zone likewise provides one secondary suite inside the farm house. Off the ALR, Delta's Bylaw No. 8242 suite rules (less than 50% of the dwelling's gross floor area, excluding an attached garage) are the ones that apply. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/218413/
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Delta farm parcel into smaller lots?** Only within tight limits: in the A1 zone a new lot created by subdivision needs a minimum of 8 ha, OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy; A3 zoning does not permit subdivision. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata like The Lexington?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: What income does a farm need to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income (farm gate) thresholds are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care for a farm buyer?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd in Ladner, is the nearest hospital (Fraser Health). In Tsawwassen, Fraser Health lists a South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 1826–4949 Canoe Pass Way. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The farm topic here is a fringe, not a belt of its own.** It is the southern end of Delta's agricultural land — Agriculture (A)-designated edges around a residential community, plus the shared farm belt toward Ladner — with ALC housing and use rules layered over Delta's A-zone zoning where a parcel is in the ALR. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Farm ditches trigger a development permit area.** Any Delta property within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, so the setback check applies on working farm land as well as urban lots. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Southlands-side parcels carry tidal numbers, not just farm rules.** Hazard DPA SD3 sets a 2.9 m GSC datum floor level, a 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation and a 7.5 m tidal setback on the Boundary Bay foreshore and the Southlands; the City records the Southlands lands as the Southlands Properties of Century Industries Ltd., File LU006390. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/109911/
- **Water is a diligence item, not an assumption.** The provincial GWELLS application is the registry for recorded wells and aquifers, older wells may not appear in it, and under the Water Sustainability Act non-domestic groundwater use, including irrigation, requires a water licence, while domestic use does not. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Soil class is parcel-specific and was not confirmed for this fringe.** No agricultural capability class for the Tsawwassen-edge farm land was confirmed from a text source in this research; capability is mapped per parcel in the BC Soil Information Finder — UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel class. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/resources/soils-and-agricultural-capability/
- **The treaty-land boundary decides which rulebook a western-edge parcel sits under.** City of Delta zoning and ALR farm rules describe Delta parcels; Tsawwassen Lands next door (over 724 hectares under the Final Agreement) are under Tsawwassen First Nation jurisdiction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **Delta's flood system is city-scale:** over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure maintained by the City as diking authority — lot-level flood construction requirements still have to be checked parcel by parcel. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
