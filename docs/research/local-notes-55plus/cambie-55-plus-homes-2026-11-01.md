# Cambie Corridor (Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Cambie" is a corridor and village name, NOT one of the City of Vancouver's 22 official local areas. The corridor runs along Cambie Street and the Canada Line from West 16th Avenue to the Fraser River at Marine Drive, through the three official areas of South Cambie, Riley Park and Oakridge. Corridor boundary roads from a City text source: UNVERIFIED — it is not a local area and has no single boundary. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/cambie-corridor-plan.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- The framework is the Cambie Corridor Plan, approved by Council on May 9, 2011, with Phase 3 (approved 2018) extending policy to the neighbourhood streets within walking distance of the Canada Line stations at King Edward, Oakridge–41st Avenue, Langara–49th Avenue and Marine Drive. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/cambie-corridor-plan.aspx
- The corridor's south anchor is the Oakridge Municipal Town Centre at Cambie Street and West 41st Avenue; Cambie Village shopping sits in South Cambie at the north end. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/cambie-corridor-plan.aspx
- Schools located in the corridor's official areas (VSB, SD39; catchments are address-based — check the VSB locator for any specific home): South Cambie — Carr Elementary, 4070 Oak Street, and Cavell Elementary, 500 West 20th Avenue; Riley Park — Livingstone Elementary, 315 East 23rd Avenue, and Wolfe Elementary, 4251 Ontario Street; Oakridge — Osler Elementary, 5970 Selkirk Street, and Van Horne Elementary, 5855 Ontario Street. Sir Winston Churchill Secondary is at 7055 Heather Street. SD39 has no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12). https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods and https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
- Health care is the corridor's defining feature at its north end: the City states the South Cambie portion is known for its medical facilities, including BC Children's Hospital and BC Women's Hospital and Health Centre at 4480 Oak Street, GF Strong Rehabilitation Centre and Canadian Blood Services. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/south-cambie.aspx
- Parks and recreation: Queen Elizabeth Park, 52 hectares at 125 metres above sea level, the highest point in the city, anchors the corridor's north-east side in Riley Park; Douglas Park Community Centre, 801 West 22nd Avenue, serves the north end and Hillcrest Centre, 4575 Clancy Loranger Way, serves the east side; Oakridge Branch of Vancouver Public Library serves the south end. https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/queen-elizabeth-park.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/riley-park.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/oakridge.aspx
- Transit: the Canada Line runs the corridor's whole length, and TransLink route 41 runs via West 41st Avenue and route 49 runs from Metrotown Station to UBC via 49th Avenue, crossing the corridor at its two southern avenues. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/cambie-corridor-plan.aspx and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt041.pdf
- Where the seniors housing actually is: there is no 55+ ownership building anywhere on the corridor (Section 2). The care and subsidized stock clusters in two places — the Oak Street / West 41st Avenue medical-and-care campus in Oakridge (Louis Brier, Weinberg, Brock Fahrni) and the Heather Street care buildings beside Queen Elizabeth Park (Youville, Honoria Conway-Heather) — plus one subsidized seniors building at West 45th Avenue and the Pearson Dogwood care site at the corridor's south foot at West 57th Avenue. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**No verified 55+ strata (ownership) stock exists on the Cambie Corridor.** No age-restricted ownership building appears in any source checked — the corridor's named new strata buildings (Cambie Gardens, Chelsea by Cressey, Claridge House, Forty Nine West) carry no verified 55+ bylaw. The corridor's verified seniors supply is care homes, one assisted-living campus and one subsidized seniors rental building, all below.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None verified on the corridor. If a listing calls a Cambie Corridor building "55+," treat the age claim as UNCONFIRMED until the registered strata bylaws say so — see the Forty Nine West conflict in the Checked list below.

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- No stand-alone market 55+ rental building was verified on the corridor — the rental-form seniors stock is the subsidized building and the care residences below.

- **Youville Residence — 4950 Heather Street (Riley Park).** Providence Health Care describes it as a multi-level care facility in the Riley Park–Little Mountain area, home to older residents, offering specialized mental health services, on the edge of Queen Elizabeth Park. A City report records Dogwood Lodge was constructed in 1974 for long-term senior residential intermediate care. Care only — and note it is an adults-19+ care facility, not a 55+ housing product. https://bc.211.ca/result/dogwood-lodge-9492135/ and https://council.vancouver.ca/20210210/documents/pspc3.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **How the City creates new 55+ rental — and why it is never strata:** Vancouver's Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy (approved by Council July 23, 2024; last amended May 19, 2026) treats "Social Housing (where occupancy is restricted to older adults 55+)" as social housing that meets the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 definition and is secured by a housing agreement restricting occupancy to residents aged 55 or older, owned and operated by a non-profit or government; for seniors supportive or independent living housing, a housing agreement is used to restrict occupancy to those aged 55 or older and to secure the units as rental housing, with no stratification and no separate sales covenants, for 60 years or the life of the building, whichever is greater. In other words, new City-secured 55+ housing on this corridor will arrive as rental, by design. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-rezoning-seniors-housing.pdf
- **The older City definition behind the existing stock:** the Zoning and Development By-law has long defined congregate housing as a Special Needs Residential Facility — a building containing residential units specifically to accommodate seniors as they age, with separate common areas for dining and socializing, and where support services are provided — under Congregate Housing for Seniors Guidelines adopted February 19, 2002. https://council.vancouver.ca/20040224/ph5.htm
- **Corridor zoning:** side streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a 3–4-unit multiple dwelling, 464 m² for 5 units and 557 m² for 6 to 8 units; a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units, and a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house. The C-2 District Schedule applies to arterial sites on Cambie Street. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **The plan, not just the zoning, drives corridor land:** the Cambie Corridor Plan guides growth from 16th Avenue to the Fraser River around the Canada Line stations, and most corridor densities still require a rezoning application — check a lot's plan designation before pricing it on redevelopment value. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/cambie-corridor-plan.aspx
- **Tax calendar:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax requires an annual property status declaration; the 2025 payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy on the Cambie Corridor?** None that any source verifies. No age-restricted strata ownership building was found anywhere from West 16th Avenue to Marine Drive — the corridor's named strata buildings carry no verified 55+ bylaw. A buyer set on the corridor buys a general-market house, duplex or condo; a buyer set on 55+ ownership buys in a different neighbourhood. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: A listing database says Forty Nine West at 6383 Cambie is a "senior community." Can I rely on that?** No — not on that field alone. The same listing's own text states no age restriction, markets a den that fits a crib, and says pets and rentals are allowed. That is a source conflict, and the age claim stays UNCONFIRMED until the registered strata bylaws for the building are read. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/6383-cambie-street/205
- **Q: What seniors housing does exist on the corridor?** Care and subsidized rental only: the Louis Brier / Weinberg campus at West 41st Avenue and Osler Street (long-term care and registered assisted living), Brock Fahrni long-term care at 4650 Oak Street, Youville and Honoria Conway-Heather on Heather Street, the German-Canadian Care Home temporarily at Dogwood Lodge at West 57th Avenue, and Oakridge House, a 77-unit non-profit seniors rental at 625 West 45th Avenue in BC Housing's Zone 6 directory. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/our-facilities-locations/our-long-term-care-facilities
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a BC 55+ strata elsewhere?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: How do I get into Oakridge House or a care home on the corridor?** Oakridge House is a non-profit seniors rental listed in BC Housing's Zone 6 seniors directory (May 2025) — subsidized seniors housing in the city is applied for through BC Housing's Housing Registry or directly with the non-profit operator, and the City points seniors to the Seniors Services Society's Seniors Housing Directory of BC. The care homes (Louis Brier, Brock Fahrni, Youville, German-Canadian) are reached through the Vancouver Coastal Health home and community care assessment process, not by applying to buy or rent a suite. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/find-supportive-housing-for-seniors.aspx and https://bc.211.ca/result/dogwood-lodge-9492135/
- **Q: Can a corridor house lot hold a suite or laneway house for a caregiver or family?** In the forms the R1-1 schedule states: a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units — the practical aging-in-place route in a corridor with no 55+ strata to buy. Confirm the parcel's schedule first. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Inside the corridor itself: BC Children's Hospital and BC Women's Hospital and Health Centre are at 4480 Oak Street in South Cambie; Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, is north of the corridor in Fairview. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/south-cambie.aspx and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital
- **Q: Is the corridor's south foot in the floodplain?** The corridor runs down to the Fraser River at Marine Drive and the City's designated floodplain is a citywide dataset covering coastal and river floodplains; whether a specific parcel near West 57th Avenue or Marine Drive is in it: UNVERIFIED — check the parcel in the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The corridor's biggest seniors story in 2026 is a care home that moved:** the German-Canadian Care Home's June 4, 2026 update records that it successfully moved its operations to two temporary locations in March 2026 — Dogwood Lodge (500 West 57th Avenue) and Central City Lodge — while its own site is redeveloped; the new home will accommodate 178 residents in individual rooms, 46 more than current capacity, in 15 households of 12 residents each, and the operator says "We look forward to welcoming you to our beautiful new space in 2029." https://www.gcch.ca/redevelopment-proposal/
- **Two of the corridor's care addresses are one campus:** Louis Brier Home & Hospital (1055 West 41st Avenue, 215 residents) and the Weinberg Residence (5650 Osler Street, registered assisted living) together form the Dr. Irving & Phyliss Snider Campus for Jewish Seniors — long-term care and assisted living side by side at the corridor's West 41st Avenue anchor, steps from Oakridge–41st Avenue Station. https://LOUISBRIER.COM/ and http://weinbergresidence.com/
- **The City's own count of Oakridge House is unusually precise:** the City's FOI release for its social-housing data lists Oakridge House, 625 W 45th Ave, as 77 units, operator Chishaun Housing Society, CD-1 zoning, built 1985 — the corridor's only subsidized seniors rental building carries a site-specific CD-1 zone created for it, the same pattern as the care campuses. https://Vancouver.ca/files/cov/2021-258-release.pdf
- **New 55+ housing here will be rental by law, not by accident:** under the Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy as amended May 19, 2026, City-secured 55+ social and supportive housing is locked in by housing agreement as rental — no stratification, no separate sales — for 60 years or the life of the building. A buyer waiting for a new 55+ strata project on Cambie Street is waiting for a product the City's own policy does not produce. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-rezoning-seniors-housing.pdf
- **The Pearson Dogwood site has been a care site since the 1950s:** the City's street-naming report records the site was developed in the 1950s for institutional healthcare purposes — Dogwood Lodge constructed in 1974 for long-term senior residential intermediate care and the George Pearson Centre constructed in 1952 as a tuberculosis sanatorium, later used during the polio epidemic. https://council.vancouver.ca/20210210/documents/pspc3.pdf
- **The corridor plan is still the value driver off the care campuses:** the Cambie Corridor Plan (2011, Phase 3 in 2018) is actively replacing arterial frontage while the side streets keep their house stock — a Cambie Street lot and an interior R1-1 lot two blocks apart are priced off different documents, and neither is priced off any 55+ premium, because there is no 55+ ownership product here to carry one. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/cambie-corridor-plan.aspx
