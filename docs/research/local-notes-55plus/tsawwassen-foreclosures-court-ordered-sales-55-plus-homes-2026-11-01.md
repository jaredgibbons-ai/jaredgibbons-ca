# Tsawwassen Foreclosures & Court-Ordered Sales, Delta — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- This is a topic page, not a neighbourhood: it covers properties anywhere in Tsawwassen — the South Delta community of the City of Delta — being sold under the BC Supreme Court foreclosure process. In BC a "court-ordered sale" is a sale conducted under a court order inside a foreclosure proceeding; it is not a separate process. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- Tsawwassen is one of Delta's three communities (Ladner, Tsawwassen and North Delta), planned under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), which consolidated the former area plans into a single Land Use Plan (the old Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The streets that carry Tsawwassen's verified 55+ stock are in the centre: 54A Street and 55 Street (The Lexington, Tsawwassen Greene, the KinVillage campus) and 56 Street (The Waterford, The Wexford) — sources in Section 2. A court-ordered sale, by contrast, can involve any mortgaged property type anywhere in the community, because Rule 21-7 by its terms covers "mortgaged property" without a property-type limit. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- South Delta Recreation Centre is at 1720 56 St, beside South Delta Exchange at 56 Street and 18 Avenue; Tsawwassen Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library) is at 1321A 56 St. http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/south-delta-recreation-centre
- School District 37 (Delta) catchments are address-based; for the sample address 1299 56 St the SD37 School Locator result is Cliff Drive Elementary (K–7), 5025 12 Ave, and South Delta Secondary (8–12), 750 53 St, with no middle-school category. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Court records: Court Services Online is British Columbia's electronic court registry; its eSearch services are available to anyone with access to the Internet, the file details it shows for Supreme Court civil files include the file number, registry location, names of parties and list of filed documents, and its file classes include "H - Foreclosure." https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do and https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/index.do

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. A court-ordered purchase does not remove the bylaw — a buyer of a Lexington or Greene unit through a court sale must still be able to reside under it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **How a BC foreclosure starts (Supreme Court Civil Rules, Rule 21-7):** a proceeding for foreclosure of the equitable right to redeem mortgaged property, or for redemption, must be started by petition in the Supreme Court of British Columbia — Rule 21-7 (1); all persons whose interest in or claim to the mortgaged property is sought to be extinguished must be made respondents — Rule 21-7 (2); a petitioner who serves the petition must include the court's informational notice for foreclosure proceedings, Form 125 — Rule 21-7 (4.1). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **The order, the redemption period and the final order:** the court may order that a respondent pay what is due under the mortgage and for costs within a redemption period that the court may fix, and that in default of payment the respondent is foreclosed of the equity of redemption — Rule 21-7 (5) (b); the Rules state no set length for that period. In default of payment within the redemption period, a final order of foreclosure may be granted on the petitioner's application — Rule 21-7 (6). A party of record may apply at any time for an order that the mortgaged property be sold — Rule 21-7 (7). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Confirming a sale:** even though the time for redemption has not expired, the person having conduct of a sale may apply to the court for an order confirming the sale, directing the disposition of the proceeds and vesting title in the purchaser — Rule 21-7 (9). The detailed mechanics in Rule 13-5 (conduct of sale, court directions, reserve or minimum price, bid and offer rights) and in practice direction PD-66 were NOT retrieved from an official source in this research — every Rule 13-5 and PD-66 mechanic is UNCONFIRMED here. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** is Delta's zoning bylaw; seniors housing in Delta is typically delivered through site-specific comprehensive development zones — The Waterford's site is CD Zone No. 276 (seniors' congregate housing and congregate care). **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024** is Delta's current OCP. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/71542/ and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta secondary suites and added units:** Delta's 2023 amendment to Bylaw No. 7600 (Bylaw No. 8242) removed the minimum and maximum floor area requirements for secondary suites and set the suite floor area at less than 50% of the gross floor area of the single detached dwelling (excluding an attached garage); Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/218413/ and http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Flood:** Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands) sets a 2.9 m Geodetic Survey of Canada datum minimum habitable floor level, a 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation around construction, and a 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of a tidal area; the City of Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Property taxes and 55+ relief:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. The provincial regular property tax deferment loan program covers homeowners 55 and older, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) covers seniors 65+. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How does a BC foreclosure start?** By petition in the Supreme Court of British Columbia under Rule 21-7 (1); everyone whose interest in the property is sought to be extinguished must be made a respondent, and the petitioner must serve the court's foreclosure information notice, Form 125, with the petition. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: How long is the redemption period?** There is no fixed length in the Rules — the redemption period is the period the court fixes in its order under Rule 21-7 (5) (b). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: What does court confirmation mean for my offer on a Tsawwassen home?** The person with conduct of sale applies under Rule 21-7 (9) for an order confirming the sale, directing the disposition of the proceeds and vesting title in the purchaser — and that application can be made even before the redemption period expires, so until the order is made the sale is not final. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: Can someone outbid my accepted offer?** UNCONFIRMED as a stated process rule in this file — the bid-rights wording in Rule 13-5 and the PD-66 bid timing were not retrieved from an official source in this research. Treat a competing bid at the approval stage as a real possibility to plan for, and get the bid instructions for the specific file from the seller's counsel in writing before spending on inspections. Source for the gap: the topic research file for tsawwassen-foreclosures-court-ordered-sales (batch 7). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: Where can I check the court file for a Tsawwassen foreclosure?** Court Services Online (https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/index.do) offers eSearch to anyone with Internet access; Supreme Court civil file details include the file number, registry, party names and filed documents, and foreclosure files are class "H - Foreclosure." Which Supreme Court registry serves Tsawwassen/Delta as a stated mapping: UNCONFIRMED — the court's locations list states no service areas. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- **Q: If I buy a unit in The Lexington or Tsawwassen Greene through a court sale, does the 55+ bylaw still apply to me?** Yes. The bylaw restricts who may live in the unit, not who may own it, and a court vesting order transfers title — it does not remove a strata age bylaw. The buyer (or their resident) must meet the 55+ requirement or fit an exemption: a younger spouse or partner, a live-in caregiver, defined child cases, or a legacy resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Are court-ordered sales "bank sales" with a set discount?** No on both counts as stated here. In BC the sale runs through the court inside a foreclosure proceeding, not through the bank, and no official source publishes Tsawwassen foreclosure counts or discount-to-market statistics — any discount figure for Tsawwassen is UNVERIFIED. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- **Q: Can I rent out a 55+ unit I buy this way?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: What taxes do I pay on a court-ordered purchase?** UNVERIFIED in this file — property transfer tax and GST treatment for the specific property were not read off an official source in this research. Have your lawyer cost both before you set your ceiling; do not rely on a general rate quoted without that check.
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd in Ladner, is the nearest hospital (Fraser Health). In Tsawwassen, Fraser Health lists a South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 1826–4949 Canoe Pass Way. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Bank foreclosure" is a misnomer in BC.** Sales run through the Supreme Court under Rule 21-7, with the court confirming the sale and vesting title — the process facts, not a bank's listing decision, decide the timeline. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03 and https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- **A sale can be confirmed before the redemption period even expires.** Rule 21-7 (9) allows the person with conduct of sale to apply for confirmation, disposition of proceeds and vesting of title while redemption time remains — which is why "which stage is this file at?" is the first diligence question, before any spending. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **There are no published Tsawwassen foreclosure numbers to lean on.** No official source publishes Tsawwassen foreclosure counts, and the market reports held for this program publish no foreclosure category and no South Delta community section — North Delta figures must never be presented as Tsawwassen figures. Source: the topic research file for tsawwassen-foreclosures-court-ordered-sales (batch 7), coverage checked against the SnapStats September 2026 reports.
- **The two 55+ ownership buildings sit one block apart, and the building between them is not 55+.** The Lexington (1150 54A St) and Heron Place (1153 54A St) face each other on the same street; only The Lexington carries the 55+ bylaw, while Tsawwassen Greene (1140 55 St) is around the corner — so a court sale on 54A Street is not automatically a 55+ purchase. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/heron-place-1153-54-a-street
- **KinVillage is mid-rebuild, in phases.** The Tides (152 homes) replaced the former North Court building; Phase 2 at 1050 54A Street (143 more affordable seniors rental units plus a replacement community centre) is in the rezoning process as City File LU009921. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-seniors-housing-breaks-ground-in-delta and https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921
- **The town centre itself is in a redevelopment process.** The Tsawwassen Town Centre Mall site (1219 56 Street, 5499 12 Avenue, 1236 55 Street) is the subject of a Century Group redevelopment application, City File LU009754 — the commercial heart around the 55+ buildings will change. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/
- **Delta's flood system is city-scale:** over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure maintained by the City — lot-level flood construction requirements still have to be checked parcel by parcel, including on a court-sale property where information is thin. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
