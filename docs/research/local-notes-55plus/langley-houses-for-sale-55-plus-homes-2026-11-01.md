## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. This is the test for Highgate Terrace above: its record says "age restrictions" without a number — if the registered bylaw sets any age other than 55, it cannot be valid in that form today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. Read listing wordings like Willow Park Estates' "at least one owner must be 55" against these exemptions: a listing summary is not the bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. Several Langley buildings above still carry "rentals not allowed" in their building records (Parkway Estates, Parkway II, Willow Park Estates) — for a long-term strata rental that line cannot be relied on; in a park, renting runs through park rules instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Two municipalities, two zoning bylaws — neither creates a 55+ status.** City of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 applies to the entire area within the City of Langley and repealed Zoning Bylaw 1996 No. 2100; the Township's complexes sit under Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 (1987). In every complex above, the age rule comes from a strata bylaw, park rule or co-op rule, not from zoning. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/schedule-zoning-bylaw-2025-no-3300pdf and https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- **Township SSMUH (settled position):** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 Amendment (SSMUH) Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — an eligible lot (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on both municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units if 280 m² or smaller, or 4 units if between 280 m² and 4,050 m² (1 acre); lots larger than 4,050 m² are not eligible. There is no 6-unit tier in the Township. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **City SSMUH (settled position):** City of Langley Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 — RS1/RS2 lots permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3284
- **Township secondary suites:** Zoning Bylaw s.102, as amended (including by Bylaw #6147, July 21, 2025), caps a secondary suite at 120 m² maximum gross floor area, one per principal dwelling unit; the Township's January 2025 guide states the same cap — the program carries a noted conflict between that guide and the July 2025 s.102 text on suites in townhouses/rowhouses, with both documents reported by date. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-100-Administration.pdf
- **City secondary suites (Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300):** a suite must not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area; the registered owner must reside on the lot (s.2.4); a carriage/garden ground-floor unit is capped at 100 m². Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/langley-city.md
- **Manufactured-home parks and the co-op run on their own rules, not strata law:** pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), whose assignment rules expressly recognise a park where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55, and allow a park landlord to refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement. Pioneer Park's rules are co-op rules attached to share ownership. Get the park's or co-op's current rules in writing. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Property taxes — two calendars:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026; the City of Langley also sets a July 2, 2026 due date, with payments received after it incurring a penalty — the City's penalty percentage is UNVERIFIED, as the City page read does not state the rate. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx and https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes
- **Short-term rentals in either municipality: UNVERIFIED** — no City or Township STR rule was read on an official municipal page in this program. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-houses-for-sale.md
- **Tax relief at 55+:** the Province's regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy across Langley?** Fifteen verified entries: 11 strata complexes (Huntsfield Green, Parkway Estates, Parkway II and Glenmont Park in the City; Willow Park Estates, Churchill Park, Spencer Green, Chartwell Green, Chelsea Green, Creekside Villa and Waterfront at Bedford Landing in the Township), manufactured homes on pads at Cedar Creek Estates, Fernridge Park and Pineridge, and a home-plus-shares purchase at Pioneer Park co-operative. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: This page is for detached houses — are any of the 55+ options actually houses?** Yes, in form: Huntsfield Green and Creekside Villa are gated rancher townhouse communities, Churchill Park and Spencer Green are duplex-style townhouse communities, and the Brookswood parks sell detached manufactured homes on pads. A freehold detached house on its own lot has no strata and therefore no 55+ bylaw — the 55+ detached-form product in Langley is strata, park or co-op product. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a Langley 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases, and live-in caregivers are exempt. In a park or the Pioneer Park co-op, that community's own rules govern instead of Regulation 7.01. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Is Morningside Estates a sixteenth option?** UNCONFIRMED. The name and the 5365 205 St address circulate as a 55+ complex and units at that address exist, but no source found states both the name and a 55+ restriction for 5365 205 St. Its verified neighbour is Glenmont Park at 5375 205 St. https://kw.com/property/20307-53-AVENUE-28-Langley-BC-V3A6S8/2035969692624078
- **Q: The building records say "rentals not allowed" at Parkway Estates and Willow Park. Can I rent my unit out?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, whatever an older bylaw set or a building-data site still displays. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Building by building, and the City cluster is restrictive: building sources record pets not allowed at Parkway Estates and Parkway II, and a current Glenmont Park listing describes it as a no-pet complex; Creekside Villa and Waterfront at Bedford Landing record pets allowed with restrictions (2 pets, with size limits in listings); Fernridge Park records no pets. Read the specific listing and the registered bylaws together. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: How many units can a Langley house lot hold, if I want family or a caregiver nearby instead of a 55+ complex?** It depends on the municipality first: Township Bylaw 6020 gives an eligible lot 3 units at ≤280 m² and 4 units at 280–4,050 m² with no 6-unit tier, while City Bylaw 3284 gives RS1/RS2 lots 4 units, or 6 within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx and https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3284
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and is it in the City?** Langley Memorial Hospital (22051 Fraser Highway) is the Langleys' hospital, with 24/7 emergency — and its own Fraser Health listing places it in the Township, immediately outside the City boundary, a short walk from Sunridge Gardens in Murrayville. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/langley/langley-memorial-hospital
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or a buyer who will not live in the unit. Langley's pool is deeper than most Fraser Valley communities because the stock is spread across 11 strata complexes in two municipalities, but it is still an age-gated pool. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Langley" is two legal worlds on one feed.** The same search returns City product under Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 (suites capped at the lesser of 100 m² and 40%, owner must reside on the lot) and Township product under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 (suites capped at 120 m²) — and the SSMUH answers differ too (City 4 or 6 units; Township 3 or 4, no 6-unit tier). Establish the municipality from the address before pricing any suite, caregiver or plex potential. Sources: Section 3 above.
- **Three of the City's four 55+ buildings stand within one block — and the fourth address in that cluster is a ghost.** Parkway Estates (5360 205 St), Glenmont Park (5375 205 St) and Parkway II (5363 206 St) are neighbours; between them sits 5365 205 St, the address attached to the unverified "Morningside Estates" name. A search that mixes up 5365 and 5375 files the wrong building's bylaws on the purchase. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Walnut Grove holds four of the Township's seven 55+ strata — and a fifth name there is single-source.** Churchill Park, Spencer Green, Chartwell Green and Chelsea Green are all in Walnut Grove; Twin Lakes, also in Walnut Grove, rests on one listing only and stays UNCONFIRMED. A Walnut Grove-only search still needs the second-source check. Sources: Section 2 above.

- **The Fort Langley pair is a one-number trap.** Waterfront at Bedford Landing (23215 Billy Brown Rd) is 55+; its sister building, The Village at Bedford Landing (23255 / 23285 Billy Brown Rd), states "No age restrictions." Same road, same name family, opposite age status. https://www.tmbrealestate.com/310-23285-billy-brown-rd
- **The newest seniors building in Langley is subsidized rental, not strata.** The Maple (101 homes, opened per the Province's May 11, 2026 release) replaced a fire-destroyed 66-unit building on the Langley Lions campus — Langley's recent 55+ growth is on the non-market and rental side (also Harrison Landing, Magnolia Gardens, Sunridge Gardens), while the ownership stock remains mostly 1987–1993-era complexes plus the 2009 Waterfront building. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0053-000516
