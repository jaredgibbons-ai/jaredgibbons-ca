## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- What the page is: a live feed page that displays Maple Ridge listings carrying an OPEN HOUSE label, each with a date and start and end times shown; the page states it is updated every 15 minutes, and its own copy names the sub-areas the feed spans — Albion, Silver Valley, Cottonwood, East Central, West Central, Southwest Maple Ridge, Northwest Maple Ridge, Websters Corners, Thornhill and Whonnock. https://jaredgibbons.ca/maple-ridge-open-houses.html

- General market context for pricing what you see: Maple Ridge detached — median sale price $1,131,000, 97% sale-to-list, 29 days on market; condos & townhomes — median $525,000, 94%, 49 days, on 33 solds (small-sample warning: the attached median was $640,000 on 47 solds in August 2026) (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).
- Health care on the run: Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, is the community hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows with 24/7 emergency care; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is inside Haney Place Mall, 121–11900 Haney Place, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- A Maple Ridge–specific check on any rural-edge or river-edge open house: properties along the Fraser River frontage sit in a floodplain where the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, and no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: East Central — the Fraserview Village pocket around 116 Avenue and 225–227 Streets, plus the Alouette park and Haney Pioneer co-op east of downtown and the Legion buildings on 224 Street — with St. George's Village co-op on Dewdney Trunk Road at the Cottonwood edge and Val Maria park on Lougheed Highway in the southwest. A 55+ open house anywhere else in the city should be treated as a claim to verify, not a given. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. An open-house sheet that says "45+" or "adult-oriented" is describing a bylaw that no longer exists in that form — the registered bylaw set is what counts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Representation at an open house:** in BC, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); a licensed professional must work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/real-estate-bc/buying-selling/buying-and-selling-home
- **The open-house exception in the disclosure rules:** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report, states that disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **The strata documents are the buyer's proof, and the Province names them:** for strata purchases the Province's buyer paperwork path is the Form B (Information Certificate) and Form F, the strata bylaws and rules, fees and special levies, with filings confirmed at the Land Title Office — the Form B is where the age bylaw for any complex in Section 2 is actually proved. (Surrey notes in this series state the same provincial path; it is province-wide.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers
- **Depreciation reports:** in BC all strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain depreciation reports, on a five-year cycle, and may no longer defer by holding an annual 3/4 vote; a depreciation report tells the corporation what common property and assets it has and the projected maintenance, repair and replacement costs over 30 years, and the most recent depreciation report, if any, must be attached to the Form B provided to buyers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019** (verified on the City's planning pages Nov 1, 2026 — not 7600-2020); **Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 7060-2014** is the OCP bylaw cited on the City's own OCP chapter documents (not 7601-2020). https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/land-development/planning-application-forms-procedures and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/01ocp-chapter-1201402201612431396
- **Manufactured home parks run on different law.** Pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003); the Regulation contemplates parks reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or two or more tenants, at least one of whom has reached 55), and a landlord may refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the age requirement in such a park. There is no Form B for a park home — the tenancy agreement and park rules are the documents. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Identification or sign-in for open-house visitors:** NOT stated in any BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source found — UNVERIFIED as a rule; a host may still ask, on the seller's instructions. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED** — no live CRA page was read for this file, so no FHSA or HBP limit is stated here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Do I need my own agent to attend a Maple Ridge open house?** No official source requires it. BCFSA's rules excuse disclosure of representation where a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or answering general factual questions — but the moment you share your motivation, financial qualifications or needs, that changes, and BCFSA requires unrepresented consumers to be told the risks of dealing with a licensee who represents the other party. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Q: The open-house listing says "55+". How do I verify that at the open house?** Ask three things on the spot: (1) the exact complex name and civic address, checked against Section 2 of these notes — in Maple Ridge the verified strata list is only the four Fraserview Village buildings; (2) the strata plan number, which separates look-alike complexes; and (3) whether the listing's "55+" comes from a registered age bylaw or from marketing language. Then prove it off the floor: the age bylaw is proved in the strata's registered bylaws and reflected in the Form B, not in the feature sheet. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave
- **Q: What should I ask for in the strata documents after the open house?** The Form B (Information Certificate) with its attachments — the strata's rules, current budget and the most recent depreciation report, if any, must be attached to the Form B provided to buyers — plus the registered bylaw set, two years of minutes, the insurance summary and any Form F (Certificate of Payment) position on the unit. Read the age-bylaw wording itself: since Nov 24, 2022 only a 55+ bylaw is valid, and older "19+", "45+" or "adult-oriented" wording in a summary is not the current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What does the depreciation report tell me that the open house cannot?** What common property and assets the strata has and the projected maintenance, repair and replacement costs over a 30-year span — the levy risk behind the fresh paint. Stratas with five or more lots must obtain one on a five-year cycle and can no longer vote to defer it. At Fraserview Village (1990s buildings) that report, not the amenity tour, is where the real comparison between buildings happens. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: How do I know the open-house time is current?** Each listing on the page carries an OPEN HOUSE label with a date and start and end times, and the page states the feed updates every 15 minutes — check the page the day you plan to go. How those times are entered on the brokerage side is UNVERIFIED on official sources. https://jaredgibbons.ca/maple-ridge-open-houses.html
- **Q: Do I have to sign in or show ID at an open house?** No BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states an identification or sign-in requirement for open house visitors — UNVERIFIED as a rule; an individual host may still ask, on the seller's instructions. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in the unit we saw?** If it is a strata with a 55+ bylaw, yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). In a manufactured-home park the Regulation's model is a site held by two or more tenants at least one of whom has reached 55, but the park's own written rule governs; in a co-op, the co-op's membership documents govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q: The host said rentals are not allowed in the building. Is that true?** For long-term rentals, no — every residential strata rental-restriction bylaw in BC became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, even where a building summary still records the old wording (Rosewood Court's building source still does). The tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave
- **Q: Which Maple Ridge areas can I cover in one open-house trip?** Group by the listing pools: East Central and West Central held the deepest inventory of both property types in September 2026 (82 detached / 120 attached and 66 detached / 78 attached respectively), with Albion, Cottonwood and Silver Valley as the eastern detached group — and the whole verified 55+ strata stock sits inside the East Central leg of that run (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).
- **Q: Is the host at an open house the seller's agent?** The host is a licensee connected to the listing side of the transaction; BCFSA requires a licensee to work for either the buyer or the seller and to inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients — assume anything non-factual you tell the host can reach the seller. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Use the open house for facts, save your story for your own agent.** The BCFSA rules only excuse disclosure of representation at an open house while the host is giving factual answers to general questions — once you discuss your motivation, finances or needs with the seller's licensee, you are sharing with the other side's representative. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **"Ideal for retirees" on a feature sheet is not an age bylaw.** The downtown towers (Era, Inspire, Brickwater) are general-market buildings whose listings market to retirees among others, with no 55+ bylaw in any source checked; only a registered bylaw (proved on the Form B / bylaw set) makes a building 55+. https://vancouvernashdom.com/maple-ridge-apartments-for-sale/SearchResults.form/?_pg=6 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers
- **Two Maple Ridge namesakes will waste an open-house trip if you mix them up.** Alouette Retirement Mobile Home Park (12868 229 St) is the verified 55+ park; Alouette Estates (11870 232 St) is a family townhouse complex with no age restriction. Check the civic address, not the name, before driving out. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3067037-12868-229th-street-18-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-6r1/brochure and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3105915-24-11870-232-street-maple-ridge-british-columbia-v2x6s9-711888/brochure
- **A 60+ co-op can show up in a 55+ buyer's search results.** Haney Pioneer Village (11830 230 St) is a seniors townhouse co-op whose own federation listing states 60+ — a 57-year-old who drives to its open house on a "seniors" search has already lost the trip. Its bar is higher than the page's topic. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **The depreciation-report clock changed recently, and older Fraserview reports may be stale on the new cycle.** Since the strengthened requirements took effect July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain depreciation reports on a five-year cycle and can no longer hold an annual 3/4 vote to defer — so at the open house, ask for the report's date, not just whether one exists. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Open-house counts and conversion statistics for Maple Ridge do not exist on any official source** — UNVERIFIED. SnapStats publishes no open-house category, so any claim about how many open houses a weekend holds, or how traffic converts, is site copy or agent experience, not a published figure (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).
- **The rural open houses are the rare events, and they carry rural checks.** Websters Corners (8 detached listings), Thornhill (13), Whonnock (2) and Northeast (2) held thin pools in September 2026, and a rural-edge showing adds the floodplain check (6.7 m flood construction level on Fraser frontage) that a central condo showing does not (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report) and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
