# Park Royal — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

> **BOUNDARY FLAG — read first:** The Park Royal sub-area straddles a tenure boundary no other West Vancouver sub-area has. Part of the Park Royal shopping centre lands sit on **Capilano Indian Reserve No. 5 (X̱wemelch'stn), Squamish Nation land**, held under the Park Royal Lease (indenture dated April 1, 1974), where the Squamish Nation exercises land-use and zoning control; the rest is District of West Vancouver municipal land under Zoning Bylaw 4662 / OCP Bylaw 4985. Every property here needs its tenure confirmed on title before anything else — see Section 3. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Park Royal is the MLS sub-area around the Park Royal shopping centre at Marine Drive and Taylor Way — West Vancouver's eastern gateway, at the foot of the Lions Gate Bridge and the mouth of the Capilano River; the District's OCP classifies Park Royal as West Vancouver's regional shopping centre. The centre opened in 1950 and operates as North and South malls divided by Marine Drive. http://parkroyal.ca/ and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- Residential form here is the most urban in West Vancouver: condos and apartments in and around the centre (including the Village at Park Royal residences on Arthur Erickson Place and Klahanie Court), townhouses near Taylor Way (the WestRoyal at 328/338 Taylor Way), and detached houses on the streets south of Marine Drive toward Ambleside, including Keith Road on the West Vancouver side. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/park-royal.md
- Park Royal Exchange is the western terminus of the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus, which since September 7, 2026 runs one-seat from Park Royal through Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station (roughly 5 a.m.–2 a.m., every 6–7 minutes at peak); the exchange is also the hub for West Vancouver's Blue Bus routes, including the 250/250A downtown–Horseshoe Bay/Dundarave service via the Lions Gate Bridge. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/ and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/210/tt250.pdf
- The nearest SD45 elementary schools are Irwin Park Elementary (K–7, 2455 Haywood Ave) toward Ambleside/Dundarave and Westcot Elementary (K–7, 760 Westcot Rd); the east-side catchment secondary school is West Vancouver Secondary (1750 Mathers Ave). Confirm catchments by exact address with West Vancouver Schools. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/045
- Ambleside Park and the east end of the Centennial Seawalk are immediately east; Capilano River Regional Park (Metro Vancouver) runs up the canyon across the river, with the Cleveland Dam (completed 1954) and the Capilano River Hatchery (completed 1971; reported closed to the public for construction starting October 1, 2026, until further notice). https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/capilano-river-regional-park and https://www.pac.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/sep-pmvs/hatcheries-ecloseries/capilano-eng.html
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: **there is no 55+ ownership concentration in Park Royal.** The one seniors-specific building inside the sub-area is a rental retirement/care residence on Keith Road (Amica Lions Gate, Section 2). West Vancouver's verified 55+ strata buildings are all west of here, in Ambleside and Dundarave. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-plus-buildings-west-vancouver/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. Note the WestRoyal source above still describes its rentals as "allowed with restrictions" — under current provincial law a long-term rental-restriction bylaw is void regardless of what a building summary says; confirm the current bylaws on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Tenure split — the Park Royal rule that matters most.** Homes on the municipal side are freehold under District of West Vancouver **Zoning Bylaw No. 4662** and **OCP Bylaw No. 4985, 2018**; homes and centre lands on Capilano IR No. 5 are leasehold interests under the 1974 Park Royal Lease, where the Squamish Nation controls zoning and West Vancouver provides building design review and inspection services on the leasehold lands. The two tenures can be streets apart — confirm on the individual title, and flag leasehold tenure to the lender at offer stage. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/development-regulations/other-developments/rezoning-and-ocp-amendments
- **Density is in active transition here.** On July 21, 2025 the Province issued ministerial directives requiring West Vancouver to amend its OCP to increase density in the Park Royal–Taylor Way area by December 31, 2025; the District missed that deadline, a provincial order effective April 7, 2026 implemented the neighbouring Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan, and the Park Royal–Taylor Way directive remains outstanding with quarterly provincial progress reports required through 2026 and 2027. The Park Royal Shopping Centre lands are also a named Form & Character Development Permit area in the OCP. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698 and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0038-000377 and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Secondary suites and coach houses:** both are permitted in the District and require building permits, compliance with Zoning Bylaw s. 130 and the BC Building Code, and a business licence. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/development-regulations/single-family-lots
- **Taxes:** municipal-side homes pay District of West Vancouver taxes — the 2026 payment was due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026; water/sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. The Province's additional school tax also applies at West Vancouver values: 0.2% on the residential portion assessed between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. Homes on reserve land are under a different tax/tenure regime — UNCONFIRMED per title. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo I can buy at Park Royal?** No verified one. The sub-area's condos — the WestRoyal on Taylor Way, the Village at Park Royal towers — are general-market strata with no age bylaw in any source checked. West Vancouver's verified 55+ strata buildings (Westview Terrace, Tudor Gardens, Lincoln Gardens) are in Ambleside and Dundarave. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-plus-buildings-west-vancouver/ and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/the-westroyal-338-taylor-way
- **Q: A listing called the WestRoyal "perfect for downsizing." Does that mean it is 55+?** No. "Downsizer" and "adult oriented" in listing copy describe the likely buyer, not a bylaw. Building sources for 338 Taylor Way record pets and rentals with restrictions and no age restriction. The legal test is a 55+ bylaw in the strata's filed bylaws — confirm on the Form B before assuming any building is age-restricted. https://www.zolo.ca/west-vancouver-real-estate/338-taylor-way/15b
- **Q: What seniors housing IS in Park Royal?** One building: Amica Lions Gate at 701–727 Keith Rd, a rental retirement residence with Independent Living, registered Assisted Living, Long-term Care and Memory Care under one roof. It is a monthly rental with services, not a purchase. https://www.amica.ca/lions-gate
- **Q: Is a home near Park Royal freehold or leasehold?** It depends on the parcel. Municipal-side homes are freehold; lands on Capilano IR No. 5 are leasehold under the 1974 Park Royal Lease, with the Squamish Nation controlling zoning. Confirm tenure on title before pricing or financing discussions — lenders treat reserve-land leaseholds individually. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata in Ambleside or Dundarave?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a 55+ unit as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How is transit if I give up driving?** Park Royal is West Vancouver's transit hub: Park Royal Exchange is the R2 RapidBus terminus (one seat to Lonsdale Quay, Phibbs Exchange and Metrotown since September 2026) and the main Blue Bus exchange, including downtown express buses over the Lions Gate Bridge. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th St, North Vancouver, across the Capilano River — the North Shore's full-service hospital under Vancouver Coastal Health. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital
- **Q: Will the Park Royal–Taylor Way density fight change what my condo is worth?** It is unresolved. The Province's July 2025 directive required more density here by the end of 2025; the District missed the deadline and the item remains outstanding with quarterly progress reports through 2026–2027, while a separate early-stage Park Royal North plan vision (reported May 2026) contemplates up to 27 towers. Treat any density-based value claim as UNCONFIRMED until an adopted bylaw names the parcel. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0038-000377 and https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/new-developments/west-vancouvers-park-royal-north-plan-signals-majo/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Canada's first covered shopping mall is also a First Nations leasehold story.** Park Royal opened in 1950; the centre lands are split between the District and Capilano IR No. 5 under a lease dated April 1, 1974, and on the reserve portion the Squamish Nation — not the District — is the zoning authority, with the District providing building design review and inspection so construction meets municipal standards. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do and http://parkroyal.ca/
- **The province has already overridden West Vancouver once next door.** When the District missed its December 31, 2025 directive deadlines (and delivered 58 of its 220-unit year-one housing target), a provincial order effective April 7, 2026 amended the District's bylaw to implement the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan itself. The Park Royal–Taylor Way directive is the same instrument, still outstanding — buyers here are pricing inside an unresolved provincial process. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0038-000377
- **The salmon hatchery across the river is closed for construction.** The Capilano River Hatchery (completed 1971, normally ~250,000 visitors a year) was reported closed to the public starting October 1, 2026, until further notice — relevant to anyone buying on the river trails' amenity value this season. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/capilano-river-regional-park
- **West Vancouver's OCP is Bylaw 4985, 2018 — and it was amended again on December 1, 2025.** Council adopted OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 5406, 2025 to align the OCP with the 2024 Interim Housing Needs Report, after the Province required OCP/zoning reviews against 20-year housing needs by December 31, 2025. Always cite the current consolidated OCP, not the 2018 original. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/housing/official-community-plan-housing-policy-update
- **A suite in a Park Royal–area house needs a business licence, not just a permit.** The District's own guidance: secondary suites and rental coach houses require building permits, Zoning Bylaw s. 130 and BC Building Code compliance, and a business licence to operate. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/development-regulations/single-family-lots
- **SnapStats publishes no West Vancouver figures at all.** The GVR September 2026 report covers Burnaby, New Westminster, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Pitt Meadows and Maple Ridge only — there is no SnapStats median for Park Royal or West Vancouver; sub-area medians are Paragon-only. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/park-royal.md
- **The additional school tax is a real carrying cost at these values.** On top of District taxes, the Province charges 0.2% on the assessed portion between $3M and $4M and 0.4% above $4M in 2026, rising to 0.3% / 0.6% on January 1, 2027 — a line item downsizers moving from a long-held house often meet for the first time. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
