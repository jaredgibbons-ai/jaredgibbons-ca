This is a house neighbourhood, not a strata market. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. In Parkcrest this law is academic for buyers — the sub-area has no strata stock — but it governs any 55+ purchase a Parkcrest downsizer makes elsewhere in Burnaby. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **City zoning: the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026** is the current bylaw — adopted June 9, 2026 and in effect July 1, 2026. (The old 1965 zoning bylaw is not current; do not cite it as such.) https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (R1 SSMUH District, in place since June 2024):** this is the rule that actually touches Parkcrest, because the sub-area is detached houses — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Plans:** the Lochdale Urban Village Community Plan, for the urban village east of Parkcrest along Hastings / Lougheed, was adopted by Council on July 25, 2022; the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict, and organizes Burnaby into 20 Community Plan Areas. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- **Property taxes:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan, about $61 for the average home. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **Utilities:** Burnaby's 2026 residential utility notice covers annual water, annual sewer use, a cross-connection device charge where applicable, and a residential garbage toter fee; the detached single-family dwelling water charge is $652.33, rates carry a 5% discount if paid by the due date, and for single-family homes sewer is billed as a sewer parcel tax on the property tax notice. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing** — the Housing Registry and the Zone 5 (Burnaby and New Westminster) seniors directory, whose edition located for this research is dated January 2021. Maggie's is supportive housing through Atira, outside that registry route, and Fellburn's long-term care beds are publicly subsidized through Fraser Health. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076 and https://atira.bc.ca/what-we-do/housing/margarets-housing-for-older-women-maggies

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo or townhouse I can buy in Parkcrest?** No — there is effectively nothing attached to buy at all: the sub-area recorded 0 condos and townhomes listed and 0 sold in September 2026, and no strata in it carries a verified 55+ bylaw. A Parkcrest downsizer who wants to stay in a house keeps the house (or adds units under the R1 rules); a buyer who wants a 55+ strata has to look outside this sub-area. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report)
- **Q: What is Maggie's at 1010 Sperling Ave — can any woman 55+ apply?** It is supportive housing, not a rental listing: Atira Women's Resource Society runs 26 units there for women aged 55+ who have been impacted by violence, with communal meals, advocacy, referrals, activities and 24/7 support staff. Eligibility is specific — it is not general-market 55+ rental, and there is nothing to buy. https://atira.bc.ca/what-we-do/housing/margarets-housing-for-older-women-maggies and https://bc.211.ca/agency-details/atira-womens-resource-society-9488521/
- **Q: Is there long-term care in the neighbourhood?** Yes — Fellburn Care Centre, 6050 East Hastings St, on the sub-area's north boundary street: Fraser Health owned and operated, 81 publicly subsidized long-term care beds, plus sub-acute short stays and a Day Program for Older Adults. It is public care housing, not a purchase. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/6/4/fellburn-care-centre
- **Q: The Swedish residence is on Halifax Street — isn't that Parkcrest?** No. The Swedish Assisted Living Residence is at 7230 Halifax St, the east end of Halifax in Montecito; Parkcrest's Halifax blocks are the 6000 blocks around Parkcrest Elementary at 6055 Halifax St. It is also assisted living (rental/care), not ownership. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2009-2013/2013emng0044-000426.htm and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: What transit serves the south side of the neighbourhood?** Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station (Millennium Line) is directly south of the sub-area, with routes 110, 134 and 144 connecting there; the station claim is honest for the Sperling-side streets, not for the north end by Hastings. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt110.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt134.pdf
- **Q: Can a Parkcrest house lot add units instead of selling?** Under Burnaby's R1 SSMUH District, a house lot may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and multi-unit lots may be stratified — the practical "age in place and add a suite/multiplex" route in a sub-area with no strata product. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St, south-west across the city. Parkcrest's own care facility is Fellburn's long-term care home on Hastings, not a hospital. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
