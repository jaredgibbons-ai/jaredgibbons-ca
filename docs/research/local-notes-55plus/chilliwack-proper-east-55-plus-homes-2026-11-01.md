## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is the current-law reading that overrides the older "rentals not allowed" directory line recorded against Mountainview Estates. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** is Chilliwack's zoning bylaw. Its Section 2 defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit of not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building, located within a building containing only 1 principal dwelling unit — the cap is the lesser of 90 m² and 40%. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Accessory dwelling units:** Section 4.06(1) of the same bylaw permits only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits an ADU; the listed types are secondary suite, coach house and garden suite, garden suites are capped at a maximum gross floor area of 100 m² by Section 4.06(1)(g), and ADU parking is set at s.5.04 (1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite; 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Suite legality:** no secondary suite is legal unless built with a building permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 is the City's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment to Bylaw No. 5000, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing; Bylaw 5395 was adopted June 18, 2024. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **City plan: Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500** is the current city-wide plan; its Schedule D development permit areas run D-1 Drinking Water Supply through D-10 Industrial, and which areas apply to a given Proper East parcel is a per-parcel check: UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **ALR housing frame, for parcels at this sub-area's eastern edge:** ALR parcels follow the ALC housing frame of one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). ALR status itself is a parcel-by-parcel check. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property taxes:** City of Chilliwack property tax notices are mailed annually at the end of May and are due the first working day after July 1 of every calendar year; a 5% late payment penalty is applied for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% for taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually BUY in Chilliwack Proper East?** One verified age-restricted strata building: Mountainview Estates (46966 Yale Rd — listings record 55+, no pets; a directory records age restrictions in its bylaws; built 1989 in listings / 1988 in the directory, conflict flagged). A second building on Margaret Avenue is a flagged conflict only and is not counted — see the next question. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Mountview Apartments the same building as Mountainview Estates?** No — they are two different buildings with near-identical names, both labelled Chilliwack Proper East: Mountainview Estates at 46966 Yale Rd, and Mountview Apartments at 46374 Margaret Ave. Listings call the Margaret Avenue building a 55+ community and a directory records age restrictions, but no registered bylaws or Form B were checked for it, so it is not verified as 55+ in this file. Confirm from the strata documents before treating it as a 55+ purchase. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Mountainview Estates?** Yes in a strata — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent, or to move into later?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: A directory says Mountainview Estates does not allow rentals. Can I rent out my unit?** That directory line is older than the current law: long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Mountainview Estates?** Listings record no pets for this building. Pet rules remain a valid bylaw matter, building by building — confirm against the registered bylaws and Form B for the specific unit. https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/46966-yale-road/104
- **Q: Can a detached house here take a suite or a coach house for a caregiver or family?** One accessory dwelling unit per lot where the zone permits it — a secondary suite (capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space), a coach house, or a garden suite (capped at 100 m²) — under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, ss. 2 and 4.06; a suite must be built with a permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and flood protection requirements, and new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in this part of Chilliwack?** The assisted-living option at this scope's western edge is The Cascades (45586 McIntosh Dr, registered assisted living, class SN — boundary flagged in Section 2); the fuller ladder — the three Chartwell retirement residences, Jubilee Place subsidized rental and Chilliwack General Hospital at 45600 Menholm Rd — sits on the west / downtown side of the city-proper, a short move from Proper East rather than inside it. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Why are there no market numbers for this area?** Chilliwack is a CADREB market that SnapStats does not cover, and CADREB publishes statistics board-wide only with no sub-area tables — so any Chilliwack Proper East statistic is UNVERIFIED here rather than borrowed from a neighbouring board. https://www.cadreb.com/market/
- **Q: When are Chilliwack property taxes due, and what is the penalty?** Notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1 each year; for 2026, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
