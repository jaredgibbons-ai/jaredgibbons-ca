# Tsawwassen North, Delta — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Tsawwassen North is a Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name for the north side of the Tsawwassen community, along Highway 17 and 52 Street toward Ladner; it is not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name and the City publishes no boundary for it. The City of Delta side is planned under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), which consolidated the former area plans into a single Land Use Plan (the old Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The sub-area sits beside Tsawwassen First Nation Lands. Tsawwassen First Nation is self-governing, with jurisdiction over land management on Tsawwassen Lands (over 724 hectares under the Tsawwassen First Nation Final Agreement), so City of Delta zoning does not apply on Tsawwassen Lands — a boundary that decides tenure, servicing and which rulebook a "Tsawwassen North" home actually sits under. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- Main roads: Highway 17 (to the BC Ferries Tsawwassen terminal and to Highway 17A), 52 Street, 56 Street and Canoe Pass Way; Highway 17A leads to Highway 99 and the George Massey Tunnel. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- The newer development node is Springs Boulevard at 52 Street: Tsawwassen Springs (Shato Holdings / Talisman Homes) and a City-listed townhouse project at 4800 Springs Boulevard. https://www.tsawwassensprings.ca/about/team/ and http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/2026-01-15%20Delta%20Housing%20Snapshot.pdf
- School District 37 (Delta) catchments are address-based and they cross the marketing boundary here: for the sample address 5000 Canoe Pass Way, the SD37 School Locator result is Ladner Elementary (K–7), 5016 44 Ave, and Delta Secondary (8–12), 4615 51 St — Ladner schools, not Tsawwassen schools. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- South Delta Recreation Centre, 1720 56 St, and Tsawwassen Library, 1321A 56 St, serve the north side from the town centre. http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/south-delta-recreation-centre
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: not on the north side. No verified 55+ strata, retirement residence or subsidized seniors building was found in Tsawwassen North; the concentration is in Tsawwassen Central (KinVillage campus, The Lexington, Tsawwassen Greene, The Waterford) — see Section 2.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** The Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1/123.2, allows a strata age bylaw only at 55 or older; lower age bylaws have been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). Two limits on the north side: the Act governs strata corporations, and homes on Tsawwassen Lands sit under Tsawwassen First Nation law rather than the City of Delta's rulebook — confirm which framework a specific property's documents actually use. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** Under-55s can own a lot in a 55+ strata corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a specified resident who qualifies or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents lawfully in place when a 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and, from May 1, 2023, a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children the resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** in BC strata since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rental bylaws remain permitted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Jurisdiction rule first, zoning second:** Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017 applies only to the City of Delta part of the north side. Its residential ladder includes RS4 at a 610 m² subdivision minimum, RS5 at 550 m² (or 460 m²), RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m², RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m², confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024** governs the City side; Tsawwassen Lands beside it are under the Tsawwassen First Nation Final Agreement framework (over 724 hectares), not the OCP. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **"Seniors congregate housing" is a permitted use in Delta's RA6 apartment zone** (apartment building, seniors congregate housing, and townhouse where an apartment building is provided; six storeys, FSR 3.0) under Bylaw No. 7600 — a route for future seniors product on the City side, not a description of anything built on the north side to date. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **Farm belt rules:** farm and ALR land lies between Tsawwassen and Ladner, and Agricultural Land Commission rules apply to land in the ALR, including one-residence-per-parcel limits — relevant to a 55+ buyer picturing a second dwelling or caregiver suite on a large north-side lot. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Flood:** the City of Delta is the diking authority for the City side and operates and maintains over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Property taxes and 55+ relief:** Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026 (5% penalty after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026). The provincial regular property tax deferment loan program covers homeowners 55 and older on a principal residence, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) covers seniors 65+ — provincial programs; how taxes are administered on a specific Tsawwassen Lands property is a separate question to confirm for that property. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is the manufactured-home park near Tsawwassen Mills a 55+ park?** No. Tsawwassen RV Resort, 4761 Nulelum Way, is listed by its park source as an all-ages, land-lease manufactured home community. No verified 55+ manufactured-home park was found anywhere in Tsawwassen. https://www.mhbo.com/mobile-home-park/83798-tsawwassen-rv-resort-4761-nulelum-way-tsawwassen-bc-v4m-4g9
- **Q: Is Tsawwassen Springs a seniors community?** No verified age bylaw exists for it. Building sources summarize its bylaws as pets and rentals allowed with restrictions and smoking restrictions, with no age restriction, across roughly 490 units built 2011–2018. It is general-market product that downsizers buy, which is not the same thing as a 55+ community.
- **Q: Where is the buyable 55+ stock, then?** Tsawwassen Central: The Lexington (1150 54A St, 38 units, built 1988, 55+) and Tsawwassen Greene (1140 55 St, 27 units, built 1991, 55+). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street and https://www.realtor.ca/real-estate/29373447/204-1140-55-street-delta
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we live in a 55+ strata together?** Yes, where a 55+ bylaw applies: provincial law exempts a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident, and also covers live-in caregivers, children under 19 in the resident's care, adult children cared for before 19, and legacy residents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I buy in a 55+ building before I turn 55?** Ownership is not age-restricted; residency is. An under-55 buyer can own the lot but may not be able to live in it until they qualify or fit an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a north-side strata unit long-term?** In a BC strata, rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, so a long-term ban cannot be enforced; short-term rentals can still be restricted. For a property on Tsawwassen Lands, confirm the tenure and the governing documents for that specific project instead of assuming the provincial strata answer carries over unchanged. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which schools would a grandchild living with us attend?** Address decides, and the north side surprises people: the SD37 locator result for 5000 Canoe Pass Way is Ladner Elementary and Delta Secondary. Run the exact address — do not assume South Delta Secondary. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: What bus runs along the north side?** Route 609 runs from South Delta Exchange via 56 Street, 12 Avenue, 52 Street and Canoe Pass Way, through Tsawwassen First Nation, to Ladner Exchange; route 601 serves 56 Street and South Delta Exchange toward Bridgeport Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital and after-hours care?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd in Ladner, is the nearest hospital. On the north side itself, the South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 1826–4949 Canoe Pass Way on the Canoe Pass Way corridor. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/7/8/Service---South-Delta-After-Hours-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre
- **Q: Can I add a suite for a caregiver on a large lot here?** On the City side, check the parcel's Bylaw No. 7600 zone first (the residential ladder runs from RS7 at 335 m² to RD1 at 836 m² subdivision minimums); on ALR land, Agricultural Land Commission rules, including one-residence-per-parcel limits, can decide the question before the City does. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The treaty-land boundary is the single biggest fact on the north side.** Homes a few blocks apart can sit under entirely different governments: City of Delta zoning and bylaws on one side, Tsawwassen First Nation jurisdiction over land management on the other (724+ hectares under the Final Agreement). Establish which side a property is on before pricing tenure, servicing or resale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **The "seniors park" assumption is wrong here.** The only manufactured-home community found on the north side markets itself as all-ages and family-friendly; Tsawwassen has no verified 55+ park, so a buyer set on park living at 55+ is looking outside Tsawwassen. https://www.mhbo.com/mobile-home-park/83798-tsawwassen-rv-resort-4761-nulelum-way-tsawwassen-bc-v4m-4g9
- **The north side's new supply pipeline is rental and townhouse, not seniors.** The City's housing snapshot lists a townhouse project at 4800 Springs Boulevard (application covering 4800 and a portion of 5133 Springs Boulevard), and Tsawwassen Springs continues as general-market strata — no seniors-designated project on the north side was verified. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/2026-01-15%20Delta%20Housing%20Snapshot.pdf
- **Ferry access is the north side's real amenity for retirees who travel.** Highway 17 runs from this sub-area to the BC Ferries Tsawwassen terminal, with published crossings of 1 hour 35 minutes to Swartz Bay (Victoria) and 2 hours to Duke Point (Nanaimo). https://www.bcferries.com/current-conditions/departures?redir=301
- **A "Tsawwassen" address here can mean Ladner services.** Beyond the school catchment surprise, the bus that serves Canoe Pass Way (route 609) terminates at Ladner Exchange, and the nearest hospital is in Ladner — day-to-day life on the north side faces both directions. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf and https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
