### Checked and NOT 55+ (the standing warnings this page's method produces)

- **A map layer is not an age bylaw.** GIS layers show zoning, flood and ALR data; no layer in any tool named here states a strata's age restriction. A building is 55+ only when its registered bylaws say so — the proof chain is bylaws, then Form B (see the resources file's checklist). ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/resources-for-buyers-and-sellers-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **A clean GIS screen is not a clean title.** The page itself states not every encumbrance shows up on a map; easement and right-of-way questions are settled by the title search, not by the absence of a line on a map. https://jaredgibbons.ca/geographic-information-systems-gis.html

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. No GIS layer records this — it is read off the strata's registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning versus OCP — the two layers a GIS check reads:** in the page's plain-language framing, zoning is the current rule for what may be built today, and the Official Community Plan is the municipality's long-term direction over the next 10–30 years. Those are the page's simplified definitions, recorded as site copy, not bylaw-verified definitions. https://jaredgibbons.ca/geographic-information-systems-gis.html
- **Flood plain status is a financing fact, not just a map colour:** on the page's account, a flood plain location changes insurance, lending and rebuilding rules — which is why the check is run before subjects, whichever tool supplies the layer. https://jaredgibbons.ca/geographic-information-systems-gis.html
- **Manufactured-home pads follow tenancy law, not strata law:** the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act covers most manufactured home tenancies in BC; pad rent can be increased only once every 12 months with at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for increases taking effect in 2026) plus a proportional levy/utility amount. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Tax relief that matters at 55+:** the Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can a GIS map tell me if a building is 55+?** No. The layers the page names — zoning, lot dimensions, flood plain status, ALR boundaries, easements — say nothing about a strata's bylaws. The 55+ test is documentary: the registered bylaws first, then the Form B for the specific lot. https://jaredgibbons.ca/geographic-information-systems-gis.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How do I find out if a property is in a flood plain?** The page's route: toggle the flood plain layer on a municipal GIS map — it names Chilliwack MapOnline, Abbotsford CityMap and Delta's map as the regional tools — with BC iMap (iMapBC) holding provincial flood data; and it warns that flood status affects insurance, lending and rebuilding rules. The current names and layers of those municipal tools are UNVERIFIED in this program — none was opened in this pass. https://jaredgibbons.ca/geographic-information-systems-gis.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/data/geographic-data-services/web-based-mapping
- **Q: Can GIS tell me about easements and rights-of-way?** Sometimes, on the page's own answer: layers often show registered easements and obvious utility rights-of-way, but not every encumbrance appears on a map — use GIS as a first look and confirm with the title search, "the source of truth." https://jaredgibbons.ca/geographic-information-systems-gis.html
- **Q: Do I need GIS skills to buy a 55+ condo?** On the page's framing, no — for a standard unit in an established building GIS is rarely critical. It matters most for the ground-oriented end of the 55+ market: bare-land strata ranchers, manufactured homes on rural-edge pads, and lots near water or ALR edges. https://jaredgibbons.ca/geographic-information-systems-gis.html
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children and live-in caregivers are exempt in defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: A listing says "no rentals allowed" in a 55+ building. Can I rent my unit out?** Yes for long-term tenancies: every strata rental-restriction bylaw in BC has been void since Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What does the zoning-versus-OCP check actually change for a downsizer?** On the page's framing: zoning states what can be built on and around the property today; the OCP states where the municipality wants the area to go over 10–30 years. A 55+ rancher street designated for denser use in the OCP is a different long-term hold than one that is not — verify the specific designations with the municipality rather than the explainer's shorthand. https://jaredgibbons.ca/geographic-information-systems-gis.html
- **Q: In a manufactured-home park, what do I actually own?** The manufactured home itself; the pad is rented under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, with pad rent increases limited to once a year on at least three full months' notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus the proportional levy/utility amount. A GIS/ALR check still matters: many parks sit on rural-edge land where the ALR boundary runs close. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The page promises a walkthrough it never gives.** Its intro promises "a quick walkthrough of the GIS tools I use most for BC properties," but the body is a definition plus a four-question FAQ — no tool is walked through, no steps appear, and no tool is linked. A reader finishes knowing what GIS is, with no route into any actual map. https://jaredgibbons.ca/geographic-information-systems-gis.html
- **The page names no tool for the core market.** Chilliwack, Abbotsford and Delta tools are named; Langley, Surrey, South Surrey and White Rock — where most of the verified 55+ stock in Section 2 sits — have no tool named on the page at all. https://jaredgibbons.ca/geographic-information-systems-gis.html
- **The provincial tool's real name is iMapBC.** The page calls it "BC iMap"; the Province styles its viewer iMapBC, and the same provincial page points to B.C.'s Map Hub (the official BC presence in ArcGIS Online) and the BC Geographical Names Office. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/data/geographic-data-services/web-based-mapping
- **The ALR check the page advertises has a dedicated provincial owner the page never names:** ALR boundaries are an Agricultural Land Commission matter, and the page lists ALR boundaries among its headline GIS uses without naming the ALC's own mapping — flagged in this program's GIS research file as a content gap, with the ALC tool itself UNVERIFIED in this pass. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/geographic-information-systems-gis.md
- **Flood risk and 55+ stock overlap in this market.** Delta's verified 55+ stock (Ladner, Tsawwassen) sits inside a diked flood-plain system, and Abbotsford's 55+ corridor sits on the flats — for those purchases the flood layer is not an academic check; on the page's own account it changes insurance, lending and rebuilding rules. https://jaredgibbons.ca/geographic-information-systems-gis.html (stock locations: the Delta and Abbotsford files in ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/)
