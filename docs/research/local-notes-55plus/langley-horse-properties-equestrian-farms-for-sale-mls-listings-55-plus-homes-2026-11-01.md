## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The strata age-bylaw rules, for the record — with no equestrian strata to apply them to.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; age bylaws below 55 invalid since Nov 24, 2022, under Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term restrictions remain allowed. A horse-property buyer gets none of this machinery, because the purchase is freehold land, not a strata lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR housing caps are the rules that actually bind a 55+ horse-property buyer.** Most Langley horse properties sit in the ALR, per the page's own framing: one principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and one additional residence at up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; more or larger requires ALC approval, and local government bylaws may be more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-horse-properties-equestrian-farms-for-sale-mls-listings.html
- **The additional residence is the groom / caregiver / family dwelling rule.** That 90 m² / 186 m² additional residence is the legal route to a second dwelling on an ALR horse property — for a groom, a trainer, an adult child or a caregiver. A barn conversion or a second house beyond that frame needs ALC approval, and Township bylaws must also permit the dwelling. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm buildings are a separate question from residences — and the page's answer is UNVERIFIED.** The live page's FAQ states farm buildings — barns, stables, arenas, hay storage — are "generally permitted on ALR land without ALC approval" provided Township zoning and building permits are met. The ALC housing page governs residences, not farm buildings, and no ALC farm-building source was opened in the research pass: UNVERIFIED as stated. Confirm permits for every barn, arena and suite with the Township and the ALC for the specific parcel. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-horse-properties-equestrian-farms-for-sale-mls-listings.html
- **Farm classification is a continuing obligation, not a one-time label.** BC Assessment's thresholds are $2,500 in farm-gate sales for a farm operation area of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for larger operations; $10,000 if the total area is under 0.8 ha; applications must be delivered to BC Assessment by October 31. A retired owner who stops producing — including a boarding operation that winds down — can lose the classification. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo89/loo89/411_95_pit and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **The page's flat farm-class line is wrong as stated — LIVE-PAGE ERROR flagged in the research file.** The page's buyer's guide states farm class "applies to properties generating $2,500+ in qualifying annual farm income" as a flat rule; the regulation's thresholds scale by operation size, as above. Do not rely on the page's line. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-horse-properties-equestrian-farms-for-sale-mls-listings.html and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-horse-properties-equestrian-farms-for-sale-mls-listings.md
- **Township frame:** rural Langley sits under Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500; Township SSMUH (Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024) requires an eligible lot of 4,050 m² or smaller, inside the Urban Containment Boundary and on municipal water and sewer — most horse properties fail on size and servicing alone. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Stocking, water and financing claims on the live page are UNVERIFIED as stated.** The page's "approximately 1 horse per acre" guideline with "the Township does not set a specific maximum," its "a horse requires 30–50 litres of water per day" figure, and its "many major banks require 25–35% down on farm-classified properties" claim each cite no source on the page, and no Township, official or lender source for any of them was opened in the research pass. Treat all three as UNCONFIRMED and verify each for the specific property and lender. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-horse-properties-equestrian-farms-for-sale-mls-listings.html
- **Property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies at midnight July 2, 2026, and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. Farm classification, above, is separate from the municipal tax notice and is where the larger tax exposure sits for an equestrian parcel. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **Tax relief at 55+:** the Province's regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
