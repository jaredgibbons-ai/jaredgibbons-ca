# Annacis Island — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Annacis Island is an island in the Fraser River in the City of Delta, in the Highway 91 / Alex Fraser Bridge corridor; the current Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), treats it as an industrial area. It is not one of the neighbourhoods named in the former North Delta Area Plan (Schedule C.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- Street access is by Derwent Way, Cliveden Avenue, Eaton Way and Audley Boulevard; road access is Highway 91 via the Cliveden Interchange and the Alex Fraser Bridge. https://www.delta.ca/ and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf
- The island's named occupants are industrial and regional: the Annacis Business Park, the Port of Vancouver's Annacis Auto Terminal at 820 Dock Road, and Metro Vancouver's Annacis Island Wastewater Treatment Plant. https://www.portvancouver.com/sites/default/files/wp-permits/wpid-48523/2022-09-26-WEB-COPY_PER-21-019-Issued-Project-Permit-and-Report-VFPA-obo-WWL-Annacis-Auto-Terminal-Optimization.pdf and https://metrovancouver.org/services/liquid-waste/wastewater-treatment-plants-and-processes
- No public school, City of Delta park, recreation centre, Fraser Valley Regional Library branch, shopping centre or grocery store on Annacis Island was confirmed in official sources. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search and https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Transit: TransLink route 340 runs Scottsdale Exchange to 22nd Street Station via Highway 91, the Alex Fraser Bridge and the Cliveden Interchange, serving the Annacis Island industrial area edge; no bus exchange on the island itself was confirmed. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf
- The nearest hospital is Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner; the North Delta Public Health Unit is at 11245 84 Avenue. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- Flood context: the island is riverfront industrial land surrounded by Fraser River channels; the City of Delta is the diking authority and maintains over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, 31 drainage pump stations and roughly 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure citywide. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates on Annacis Island: nowhere. There is no housing on the island to concentrate (see Section 2).

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**No 55+ housing of any kind on Annacis Island — and no housing of any kind.** The Delta Official Community Plan 2024 (Bylaw No. 8400) names Annacis Island with River Road East, Tilbury Industrial Park and Roberts Bank as where Delta's industries are primarily located, and the area research confirmed no residential housing mix on the island — no houses, no condos, no townhomes. What trades instead is freehold industrial lots, strata industrial units in the Annacis Business Park, and port-terminal land. Do not put Annacis Island on a 55+ homes page as a place to live. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None. There is no residential strata on the island.

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- None. There is no rental housing on the island.

### Retirement residences and care (rental/care — NOT strata ownership)

- None on the island. The City of Delta Affordable Housing Guide (April 2026) lists subsidized assisted living in Delta only at Augustine House in Ladner and the Residences at KinVillage in Tsawwassen — neither is on or near Annacis Island. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf

### Subsidized seniors rental (non-profit, income-based — not ownership)

- None on the island. The City guide's North Delta non-market seniors buildings — Scott Road Lodge, Strawberry Hill Manor and White Birch Manor — are all on the mainland in the Scottsdale sub-area (see the Scottsdale file). http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf

### Checked and NOT 55+ (do not put these on a 55+ page)

- **Annacis Business Park strata industrial units.** These are industrial strata lots under the Strata Property Act framework — owners own their strata lots and together own common property as a strata corporation — but they are warehouse/industrial premises, not homes, and no source states any residential or age-restricted use. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **KinVillage and The Wexford / The Waterford (Bria)** are in Tsawwassen, not on Annacis Island — they do not belong on any Annacis page. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **Manufactured-home parks:** no source found for any manufactured-home park on Annacis Island.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

These rules are stated here because buyers reach this page looking for 55+ housing in Delta; on Annacis Island itself there is no housing for them to apply to.

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** assigns zoning parcel by parcel; Annacis Island carries an Industrial designation in the OCP, and the industrial zone names and minimum lot standards for Annacis parcels were not confirmed from a City zone sheet — UNCONFIRMED. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")** is Delta's adopted OCP, adopted July 8, 2024; it covers land use across Delta including its industrial areas — there is no separate Annacis Island area plan and no residential designation for the island. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Port land is a separate approval path.** The Annacis Auto Terminal at 820 Dock Road is designated "Port Terminal" under a Vancouver Fraser Port Authority project permit (tenant WWL Vehicle Services Canada, Ltd.) — parcels under port jurisdiction do not follow the City residential path at all. https://www.portvancouver.com/sites/default/files/wp-permits/wpid-48523/2022-09-26-WEB-COPY_PER-21-019-Issued-Project-Permit-and-Report-VFPA-obo-WWL-Annacis-Auto-Terminal-Optimization.pdf
- **Property tax deferment at 55+** exists for Delta homeowners generally — BC's regular Property Tax Deferment Program lets a homeowner who is 55 or older during the current year defer annual property taxes on a principal residence — but it has no application on Annacis Island, where there are no principal residences. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a 55+ home on Annacis Island?** No. There are no homes on the island — no houses, condos, townhomes or manufactured homes. The OCP designates the island industrial; its occupants are the Annacis Business Park, the Port of Vancouver auto terminal and Metro Vancouver's wastewater treatment plant. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Is there any rental or seniors housing on the island?** No. The City of Delta Affordable Housing Guide (April 2026) lists Delta's non-market seniors buildings in Ladner, Tsawwassen and North Delta (Scottsdale) — none on Annacis Island. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **Q: Could a caretaker or live-work unit on an industrial lot be a way to live there?** No source checked states any residential use, caretaker suite or live-work permission on Annacis Island parcels — UNCONFIRMED, and the OCP designation is industrial. Do not market an island unit as livable without the parcel's zone sheet and a City answer in hand. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Where is the closest 55+ housing to Annacis Island?** On the mainland in North Delta: the verified 55+ rentals are Sunstone 55+ Apartments in Nordel (Sunstone/Delsom Crescent) and, in Scottsdale, Scott Road Lodge, Strawberry Hill Manor and White Birch Manor — see those files. No verified 55+ ownership strata was found anywhere in North Delta in this research. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf and https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/sunstone-apartments/1370
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Delta?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is there a bus or seniors bus serving the island?** Route 340 serves the island's industrial edge via the Cliveden Interchange; there is no bus exchange on the island. Separately, the City runs a free Delta Seniors Bus for Delta residents aged 65+ (note: 65+, not 55+), with curbside pick-up and drop-off inside Delta and booking up to 7 days ahead — it is a mainland residential service, not an island service. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/seniors-services/seniors-bus
- **Q: Where is the hospital for this area?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner — Fraser Health's community hospital for Delta with 24/7 emergency care. The North Delta Public Health Unit is at 11245 84 Avenue. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The island's biggest neighbour is a sewage plant, by design.** Metro Vancouver's Annacis Island Wastewater Treatment Plant is one of the region's secondary treatment plants and serves the Fraser Sewerage Area — a permanent, high-activity industrial use in the middle of the island. https://metrovancouver.org/services/liquid-waste/wastewater-treatment-plants-and-processes
- **The auto terminal got bigger, not smaller.** The Vancouver Fraser Port Authority's Annacis Auto Terminal Optimization Project consolidated the Annacis and Richmond auto terminals into the Annacis facility, enabling it to handle up to 480,000 vehicles annually — a 36% increase in capacity — with on-terminal works complete in early 2025. https://www.portvancouver.com/project/annacis-auto-terminal-optimization-project
- **Delta's industrial land base is regionally significant, and this island is part of it.** The OCP cites Metro Vancouver's 2020 Regional Industrial Lands Inventory: Delta's industrially zoned and designated land totaled 1,527 hectares, approximately 13% of the region's inventory — which is why the island's industrial designation is not a placeholder awaiting housing. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **"Annacis" showing up in a housing search is a map artifact, not a neighbourhood.** The name buyers see is the Paragon (FVREB MLS) sub-area name and the Annacis Business Park name; the former North Delta Area Plan did not list Annacis Island among its residential neighbourhoods, and the 2024 OCP carries no residential designation for it. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- **Flood homework is the first subject on any island parcel, home or not.** The island is surrounded by Fraser River channels inside the City's dike system; Flood Construction Level, dike setbacks and any flood covenants on title have to be confirmed parcel by parcel. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
