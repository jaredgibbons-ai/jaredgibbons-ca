# Agassiz — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Read with the Kent file:** Agassiz is not a municipality — it is the main townsite inside the District of Kent. Every complex in this file is legally in the District of Kent; the companion file (kent-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md) covers the same buildings from the district-wide angle plus Mount Woodside and the rural areas, and the two files cross-reference rather than double-count.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Agassiz is the principal community and service centre of the District of Kent, on the north bank of the Fraser River along Highway 7 (Lougheed) and Highway 9. Municipal Hall is at 7170 Cheam Avenue; Pioneer Avenue is the main street, with the grocery, pharmacy and services in the townsite. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/vpwaiuh5/2026-01-21-dok-2025-ocp-update.pdf
- The townsite is compact and flat, ringed by ALR farmland; growth is deliberately directed into the existing townsite to keep it compact and protect farmland. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf
- Highway 9 crosses the Fraser at the Agassiz-Rosedale Bridge to Chilliwack and Highway 1, and runs north to Harrison Hot Springs. BC Transit Route 71 (Agassiz–Harrison) runs Chilliwack–Rosedale–Agassiz–Harrison Hot Springs and Route 72 (Hope) runs Agassiz–Hope via Highway 7; Agassiz (Pioneer Ave at Evergreen Dr) is the transfer point between them. Service is limited — check schedules per trip. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html
- Schools are School District 78 (Fraser-Cascade): Kent Elementary School (K–6) at 7285 McCullough Road, and Agassiz Elementary-Secondary School (grades 7–12) at 7110 Cheam Avenue, which also serves Harrison Hot Springs. https://sd78.bc.ca/kent/ and https://sd78.bc.ca/agassiz-elementary/
- Healthcare is a Fraser Health community cluster, not a hospital: the Agassiz Public Health Unit at 7243 Pioneer Avenue, the Agassiz Community Health Centre at 7040 Cheam Avenue, plus Agassiz Home Health and the Agassiz Mental Health and Substance Use Centre. No hospital in Agassiz. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/your-community/agassiz/services-in-agassiz
- Where 55+ and seniors housing actually concentrates: the MacKay Crescent / Morrow Road / McCaffrey Road side of the townsite. The two Park Place care campuses (Agassiz Seniors Community at 1525 MacKay Cres, Glenwood at 1458 Glenwood Dr), the subsidized seniors buildings (Dogwood Manor on Morrow Rd, Hazelnut Grove on McCaffrey Rd) and several of the townsite's strata complexes (Village Gate and The Langtry on Mackay Crescent) all sit in the same few blocks. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Agassiz has ZERO verified 55+ strata (ownership) complexes.** The townsite has real strata complexes (Section 2, "Checked and NOT 55+"), but no public source checked states a 55+ age bylaw for any of them — age restrictions for those buildings are UNCONFIRMED, not verified, and must not be marketed as 55+ without the strata's own bylaws in hand. What Agassiz does have, in quantity for its size, is seniors rental and care: two Park Place campuses and two subsidized seniors buildings.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None. Zero verified. No source found states a 55+ age restriction bylaw for any strata complex in Agassiz.

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- No purpose-built market 55+ rental building was verified in Agassiz. The seniors rental stock that does exist is the subsidized non-profit housing below and the independent-living suites inside the Park Place care campus (next subsection) — both rental, neither is strata ownership.

### Retirement residences and care (rental/care — NOT strata ownership)

- **Agassiz Seniors Community (Park Place Seniors Living) — 1525 MacKay Crescent.** A multi-level care campus: 50 one- and two-bedroom independent living suites (private-pay rental) plus 68 single-occupancy long-term care rooms (publicly funded placements run through Fraser Health). The operator states the building is pet-friendly for small and medium pets in Independent Living only, with weekly housekeeping, dining room, hairdresser/barber, library and an emergency alert system in each suite. Park Place acquired the campus (previously Cheam Village) in 2018, describing a 4.7-acre site. Year built: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/find-a-location/british-columbia/lower-mainland/agassiz/ and https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/park-place-acquires-4-new-locations-in-agassiz-bc-and-grande-prairie-ab/
- **Glenwood Seniors Community (Park Place Seniors Living) — 1458 Glenwood Drive.** Assisted living plus long-term care: the operator lists 30 one- and two-bedroom assisted living suites and 37 single-occupancy long-term care rooms. It is registered with BC's Assisted Living Registry as a Seniors and Persons with Disabilities (class SN) residence, legal operator Glenwood Seniors Community Partnership. Park Place's 2018 acquisition note breaks the campus down further (publicly funded and private-pay beds/suites) — treat the operator's current page counts above as the headline figures. Year built: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/find-a-location/british-columbia/lower-mainland/glenwood/ and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3AAgassiz&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Municipal law here is District of Kent law, not "Agassiz" law.** Zoning is the District of Kent Zoning Bylaw No. 1219, 2001 (as amended); the plan is the District of Kent Official Community Plan 2025, which designates the townsite core "Commercial – Town Centre" and directs growth into the townsite. https://www.kpu.ca/isfs/foodpolicydatabase/district-kent-zoning-bylaw-no-1219-2001 and https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH):** Kent Bylaw No. 1737, adopted June 10, 2024, created the RS1 zone, under which serviced residential lots in Agassiz can take up to four dwelling units without rezoning, subject to conditions — the gentle-density route the 2025 OCP update relies on for townsite infill. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/tdabxdtl/district-of-kent-ssmuh-information-guide-final.pdf and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/vpwaiuh5/2026-01-21-dok-2025-ocp-update.pdf
- **Secondary suites:** the District amended its Zoning Bylaw to permit secondary suites in single-family homes in all residential zones — the standard way to house a parent or caregiver in the townsite. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf
- **Floodplain rules:** Agassiz sits on the Fraser River floodplain behind dikes. Flood Protection & Erosion is OCP Section 5.5; the District has a Floodplain Bylaw that applications must comply with, plus area-specific flood construction levels — there is no single townsite-wide FCL in the sources checked, so the FCL is a per-property check. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf and https://www.kentbc.ca/en/business-and-development/resources/Documents/2025/Business-Licence-Application_Agritourism-Accommodation---Fillable.pdf
- **ALR rules:** the townsite is ringed by ALR farmland, and some townsite-edge "Residential – Future" lands are still in the ALR and stay in farm use until an ALC exclusion is approved (a Council-approved Neighbourhood Plan is required first). On ALR parcels the ALC housing frame also applies on top of Kent zoning. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/vpwaiuh5/2026-01-21-dok-2025-ocp-update.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Taxes are District of Kent taxes** — there is no separate "Agassiz" tax bill. Notices are mailed in early June and due July 15 (or the next business day), with a separate dyking line on the rate schedule (2026 Class 1 Residential dyking rate 0.03076 per $1,000 of taxable value). Kent is a municipality with its own due date — do not apply the City of Mission penalty or the FVRD rural-area penalty regime to a townsite property. https://www.kentbc.ca/living-here/home-and-property/property-taxes/ and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/mxrbvfky/tax-rates-summary-2026.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo or townhouse I can buy in Agassiz?** No verified one. Agassiz has townhouse and low-rise strata complexes (Village Gate, The Langtry, Fraser Green and others), but no public source states a 55+ age bylaw for any of them — treat any "55+" claim for an Agassiz strata as UNCONFIRMED until the strata's registered bylaws are in hand. The verified seniors stock in town is rental and care, not ownership. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What seniors housing does exist in town?** A full ladder in a few blocks: subsidized non-profit rental (Dogwood Manor, 11 units, listed 60 & older; Hazelnut Grove, 36 units), independent-living rental suites with services (Agassiz Seniors Community, 50 suites), assisted living (Glenwood, 30 suites), and long-term care (68 rooms at Agassiz Seniors Community, 37 rooms at Glenwood). https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/find-a-location/british-columbia/lower-mainland/agassiz/ and https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/find-a-location/british-columbia/lower-mainland/glenwood/ and https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-7-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes, where a valid 55+ bylaw exists — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a strata unit in Agassiz?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. If the building has a valid 55+ bylaw, the tenant still has to satisfy it or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How do I get into the subsidized seniors buildings?** Apply directly to the operator — Dogwood Manor and Hazelnut Grove are both run by the Agassiz-Harrison Senior Citizens Housing Society (10 – 2008 McCaffrey Road, Agassiz) and are listed in BC Housing's Zone 7 directory as apply-directly non-profit developments, not Housing Registry buildings. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-7-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: How does long-term care placement work at the Park Place campuses?** Funded bed placement starts with Fraser Health (the operator directs applicants to Fraser Health at 604-520-7204); the independent-living suites at Agassiz Seniors Community are private-pay rental arranged with the residence directly. https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/find-a-location/british-columbia/lower-mainland/agassiz/
- **Q: Are pets allowed at the care campus?** At Agassiz Seniors Community, yes within limits — the operator states the community is pet-friendly for small and medium pets, in Independent Living only. https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/find-a-location/british-columbia/lower-mainland/agassiz/
- **Q: Is Agassiz in a floodplain?** Yes — the townsite is diked Fraser River floodplain. The local system includes Maria Slough and the Hammersley, Kilby and Bateson pump stations, the District has a Floodplain Bylaw, and diking is a visible separate line on the tax notice. Confirm the flood construction level for the specific property before writing. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/mxrbvfky/tax-rates-summary-2026.pdf
- **Q: Can I add a suite for family in a townsite house?** The District permits secondary suites in single-family homes in all residential zones, and the RS1 (SSMUH) zone allows up to four units on serviced lots that qualify — all subject to the Floodplain Bylaw and building rules for the specific lot. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/tdabxdtl/district-of-kent-ssmuh-information-guide-final.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The new aquatic centre just opened.** The Lets'emot Regional Recreation & Aquatic Centre (LRRAC), beside the Community Recreation and Cultural Centre at 6660 Pioneer Avenue — a 27,000 sq ft expansion with an 8-lane lap pool, leisure pool with lazy river, whirlpool, steam room and sauna — opened to the public on September 25, 2026, replacing the seasonal Ferny Coombe Pool that served the town for nearly 50 years. https://www.kentbc.ca/news/posts/september-9-2026-council-highlights/ and https://www.kentbc.ca/news/posts/press-release-2025-projects-funded-by-44m-from-canada-community-building-fund/
- **Both care campuses changed hands in 2018.** Park Place Seniors Living acquired Agassiz Seniors Community (previously Cheam Village) and Glenwood Seniors Community on September 6, 2018 — so pre-2018 directories listing "Cheam Village" or "Glenwood Care Centre" are describing these same two buildings under old names. https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/park-place-acquires-4-new-locations-in-agassiz-bc-and-grande-prairie-ab/
- **Diking is a line item, not a surprise.** The District's 2026 Tax Rates Summary carries a dedicated "Dyking" column charged per $1,000 of taxable value — 0.03076 for Class 1 Residential — on top of the total municipal rate of 2.80493 (Residential). https://www.kentbc.ca/media/mxrbvfky/tax-rates-summary-2026.pdf
- **The pump station west of town was rebuilt with provincial money.** The Province funded a $4.17 million upgrade of the Hammersley Pump Station, about 6 km west of Agassiz, which conveys Mountain Slough watershed runoff and protects agricultural lands and nearby federal correctional infrastructure — part of the flood system the townsite depends on. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2017TRAN0139-000928
- **The District is planning for a lot more homes — mostly in this townsite.** Kent's Interim Housing Needs Report (2024), as presented in its OCP update, found 495 new homes needed immediately and 1,695 more by 2041, with growth directed mainly to the Agassiz townsite and Mount Woodside. https://forms.kentbc.ca/_Print/Index/6a0b39a0-ebb5-4763-89dd-b3690173f2af?PDF=True and https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf
- **The OCP is mid-update.** The District of Kent Official Community Plan 2025 is the current plan and an update process continued into early 2026, with housing — including housing that supports seniors — as a stated survey and policy topic. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/vpwaiuh5/2026-01-21-dok-2025-ocp-update.pdf and https://forms.kentbc.ca/_Print/Index/6a0b39a0-ebb5-4763-89dd-b3690173f2af?PDF=True
- **There are no board statistics for Agassiz to lean on.** SnapStats' Fraser Valley report has no Agassiz or Kent row, and CADREB (the Chilliwack and District board, whose territory includes Agassiz) publishes board-wide statistics only — pricing a 55+ search here rests on comparable sales work, not a stats sheet.
