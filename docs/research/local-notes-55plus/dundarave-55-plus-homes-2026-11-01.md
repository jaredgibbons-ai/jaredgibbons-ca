## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Dundarave is the District of West Vancouver's western village centre, built around the 2400 block of Marine Drive (24th and 25th Streets), with Dundarave Park and the pier at the foot of 25th Street. https://timewalk.westvancouver.ca/routes/dundarave/dundaraveDirectionsPrint.html
- In the District's OCP, Dundarave is one of West Vancouver's village centres, alongside the Ambleside Municipal Town Centre, the Park Royal regional shopping centre, and the village centres of Caulfeild Village and Horseshoe Bay. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- Dundarave Park (150 25th Street) offers more than 50 metres of sandy beach, a playground, washrooms and a seasonal concession; the Centennial Seawalk runs about 1.7 km east from the park toward Ambleside. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/dundarave-park and https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/centennial-seawalk
- Nearby areas are Ambleside (east), Altamont (west), the British Properties (north, uphill) and Chartwell (northeast) — per the area research file.
- The Dundarave exchange at Marine Drive and 25th Street is served by the 250 Horseshoe Bay/Dundarave/Vancouver and 250A Dundarave/Vancouver on the Frequent Transit Network, the 255 Dundarave–Capilano University, the 251/252 locals to Park Royal, and the 253 (Caulfeild). https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/210/tt250.pdf
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: it does not concentrate in Dundarave Village itself. No source checked places any 55+ strata, 55+ rental, retirement residence or subsidized seniors building inside the village core at 24th–25th and Marine. The nearest retirement residence, Westerleigh PARC at 725 22nd Street, describes itself as sitting "on the edge of the Dundarave neighborhood" — east of the village, toward Ambleside. Sources: Section 2 below, and https://parcliving.ca/blog/the-best-senior-living-location-in-west-vancouver-bc/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **District zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010** is the District of West Vancouver's zoning bylaw; the District states the zoning established by Bylaw No. 4662 specifies how land can be used and regulates intensity, height, density, setbacks and lot coverage. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/bylaws-licensing/find-bylaw/zoning-bylaw-no-4662-2010 and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/development-regulations/other-developments/rezoning-and-ocp-amendments
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018** is West Vancouver's OCP; the District states Council adopted OCP Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, Amendment Bylaw No. 5406, 2025 on December 1, 2025, bringing the OCP into alignment with the 2024 Interim Housing Needs Report. Dundarave is treated in the OCP as a village centre with a named Form & Character Development Permit area (Dundarave Village Neighbourhood Centre), so new development, additions and renovations in the village core are reviewed against OCP design guidelines. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/housing/official-community-plan-housing-policy-update and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Secondary suites (West Vancouver):** the District permits secondary suites under Zoning Bylaw No. 4662 with its own registration process; a secondary suite is a separate residential unit accessory to a single-family or two-family dwelling, only one per lot, not detached from the principal building, with the registered owner occupying either the principal dwelling or the suite. Verify the current registration and fee position with the District for a specific property, as secondary sources summarizing the 2010 legalization differ on current detail. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/real-estate-FAQS/secondary-suites-in-west-vancouver/
- **Foreshore and creek permits affect Dundarave lots directly:** District-wide, a Foreshore Development Permit applies to waterfront properties and others at risk of coastal flooding, and an Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of a watercourse bank. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ condo I can buy in Dundarave Village?** No verified one. No public source checked states a 55+ age-restriction bylaw for any Dundarave strata building — including the village's best-known strata buildings, Villa Maris and Seastrand. A buyer who needs a legally age-restricted building has to confirm a bylaw in the strata documents for a specific building, or look outside Dundarave. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Is Westerleigh PARC in Dundarave?** At its edge, by its operator's own wording — PARC says the building at 725 22nd Street "sits on the edge of the Dundarave neighborhood, a short walk from Dundarave Village." It is rental independent senior living, not a strata you can buy. https://parcliving.ca/blog/the-best-senior-living-location-in-west-vancouver-bc/ and https://parcliving.ca/westerleigh-parc/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022 — but the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to a Dundarave house for a caregiver or family member?** Secondary suites are permitted in West Vancouver under Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, one per lot, within the principal building, with the owner occupying one of the units and a District registration process; waterfront and village-core lots may also need Foreshore or Form & Character development permits. Confirm the specific lot with the District. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/real-estate-FAQS/secondary-suites-in-west-vancouver/ and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Q: Which schools serve Dundarave?** Irwin Park Elementary (K–7, 2455 Haywood Avenue) is in Dundarave itself; the catchment secondary school is West Vancouver Secondary (Grades 8–12, 1750 Mathers Avenue). Confirm by exact address with West Vancouver Schools. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/045
- **Q: Where is the hospital and the seniors centre?** West Vancouver has no hospital of its own — that fact is UNCONFIRMED from a single official source in this file and should be checked before page copy uses it. The verified seniors programming anchor near Dundarave is the West Vancouver Seniors' Activity Centre, across from Hollyburn House at 2095 Marine Drive, between Ambleside and Dundarave. https://cogirseniorliving.ca/retirement-home-west-vancouver-british-columbia/venvi-hollyburn-house
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** Marine Drive buses (West Vancouver Blue Bus, owned and operated by the District under contract to TransLink) link Dundarave to Park Royal, Ambleside, downtown via the Lions Gate Bridge and Horseshoe Bay; the 250/250A at Marine and 25th are on the Frequent Transit Network. There is no SkyTrain on the North Shore. https://www.westvancouver.ca/transportation-roads/blue-bus and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/210/tt250.pdf
- **Q: Is Dundarave densifying — will the village change?** By provincial push: on July 21, 2025, the Province directed West Vancouver to amend its OCP to provide increased density in the single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave (and at Park Royal–Taylor Way), by December 31, 2025. The District's final adopted zoning amendments for the Dundarave-adjacent areas were still outstanding in the area research file — verify current zoning per property. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The "Dundarave" retirement supply is actually at 22nd Street.** Westerleigh PARC's own marketing places it "on the edge of the Dundarave neighborhood" — a few blocks east of the 24th–25th Street village, next to the West Vancouver Community Centre and Seniors' Activity Centre. Buyers searching "Dundarave retirement" are usually being shown this building. https://parcliving.ca/blog/the-best-senior-living-location-in-west-vancouver-bc/
- **The seawalk is the flat route between the two villages.** The Centennial Seawalk runs about 1.7 km east from Dundarave Park to Ambleside — the level, paved waterfront route that connects Dundarave on foot to the Ambleside amenities, library and seniors centre corridor. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/centennial-seawalk
- **Village-core changes are design-reviewed, not just permit-checked.** The Dundarave Village Neighbourhood Centre is a named Form & Character Development Permit area in the OCP, so exterior work in the core is reviewed against design guidelines. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **West Vancouver taxes carry two penalty dates, and high-value homes carry a provincial top-up.** District property taxes for 2026 were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026; the Province's additional school tax applies at Dundarave price points — 0.2% on the residential portion assessed between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **The Blue Bus is municipal.** West Vancouver's bus system is owned and operated by the District of West Vancouver under contract to TransLink — it is not a TransLink-operated service, which is why Dundarave's Marine Drive service has its own local character. https://www.westvancouver.ca/transportation-roads/blue-bus
- **Water and sewer are metered and billed quarterly in West Vancouver** — a different utility pattern from the annual-bill municipalities many downsizers move from. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
