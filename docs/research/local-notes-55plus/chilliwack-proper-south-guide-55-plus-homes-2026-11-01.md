## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** is Chilliwack's zoning bylaw. Its Section 2 defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit with a gross floor area of not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building, located within a building containing only 1 principal dwelling unit — the cap is the lesser of 90 m² and 40%. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Accessory dwelling units:** Section 4.06(1) of the same bylaw permits only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits an ADU; the listed types are secondary suite, coach house and garden suite, garden suites are capped at a maximum gross floor area of 100 m², and off-street parking for accessory units is addressed in Section 5.04. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Suites policy:** the City's Secondary Suites Policy permits the rental of secondary suites within any single detached dwelling in the City regardless of zone; a suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level under the Floodplain Regulation Bylaw. https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 is the City's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment to Bylaw No. 5000, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing; Bylaw 5395 was adopted June 18, 2024. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3090/Signed%20Staff%20Report%20%2D%20RZ001761%2Epdf
- **Short-term rentals:** the City regulates short-term rental operations under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141, adopted November 24, 2021, which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **City plan: Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500** is the current city-wide plan (cross-file confirmation against the City's OCP page — see the flagged adoption-date note in Section 1). https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Flood:** flood hazard zoning exists in the bylaw structure (RS-FHA reserve zone, Section 12 of Bylaw No. 5000) and the City applies flood protection requirements and a Floodplain Regulation Bylaw; parcel-level Flood Construction Levels for Proper South lots: UNVERIFIED. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **Property taxes:** City of Chilliwack property tax notices are mailed annually at the end of May and are due the first working day after July 1 of every calendar year; a 5% late payment penalty is applied for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% for taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **GST on new construction — live on this corridor because of the 2025-built condos at 8497 Young Road:** new homes carry GST where resale homes do not. Two federal rebates are verified from the CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 (April 2026): (1) the GST/HST new housing rebate — generally 36% of the GST or federal part of the HST paid, to a maximum of $6,300, gradually reduced for homes valued between $350,000 and $450,000 and unavailable at $450,000 or more; and (2) the first-time home buyers' (FTHB) GST/HST rebate under Bill C-4, which received royal assent on March 13, 2026 — together with the new housing rebate it rebates 100% of the GST on a newly constructed or substantially renovated home valued up to $1 million, gradually reduced between $1 million and $1.5 million and unavailable at $1.5 million or more, for purchase agreements in the March 20, 2025–2031 window. Home Buyers' Plan and First Home Savings Account figures: UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
