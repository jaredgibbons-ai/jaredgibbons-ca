# Scottsdale — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Scottsdale is the south-east corner of North Delta, in the City of Delta, along Scott Road at the Surrey border. It is both a Paragon (FVREB MLS) sub-area name and a City of Delta neighbourhood name — neighbourhood 11 of the 14 named neighbourhoods in the former North Delta Area Plan (Schedule C.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950). The City does not publish a street-by-street written boundary; the working grid used in research is 72 Avenue south, 84 Avenue north, 112 Street west, Scott Road (120 Street) east — a working grid, not an official boundary. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- Scott Road (120 Street) is the east edge and the Delta–Surrey border. Scottsdale Exchange, the area's bus hub, itself sits on the Surrey side of Scott Road, per the City. https://www.delta.ca/ and http://www.delta.ca/node/1838
- The 80 Avenue / Scott Road node is the sub-area's strata cluster: Chancellor Place (11910 and 11920 80 Avenue), Delta Rise (11967 80 Avenue), White Birch Manor (11905 80 Avenue) and Arland Mews Co-op (11865 80 Avenue) all sit within two blocks of each other on or off 80 Avenue. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- The second seniors cluster is the Strawberry Hill pocket at the south end, on 119A Street just off Scott Road near 76 Avenue: Scott Road Lodge (7666 119 Street / 119A Street — address conflicts between sources, see Section 2) and Strawberry Hill Manor (7667 119A Street) face each other across the street. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- Chalmers Park is at 11400 76A Ave and Gunderson Park at 7630 117 St. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/chalmers-park
- School District 37 catchments are address-based. For the sample address 7031 120 St, the SD37 School Locator result is Heath Elementary (K–7), 11364 72 Ave., and Seaquam Secondary (8–12), 11584 Lyon Rd. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Transit: Scottsdale Exchange is the hub — routes include 310, 312, 316, 319, 322, 329, 340, 364 and 391, plus the R6 Scott Road RapidBus between Scott Road Station and Newton Exchange. The 2024 OCP designates a Scottsdale Exchange TOD Area, and the City states Bill 47 requires minimum densities within a Transit Oriented Development Area extending 400 metres from Scottsdale Exchange. https://www.translink.ca/r6 and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- George Mackie Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library), 8440 112th Street, is at the north-west edge of the area in Nordel; the North Delta Recreation Centre at 11415 84 Avenue serves North Delta. The nearest hospital is Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner; the North Delta Public Health Unit is at 11245 84 Avenue. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates in Scottsdale: this sub-area holds all three of North Delta's non-market 55+ buildings (Section 2), but there is no verified 55+ ownership stock here — the Scott Road towers buyers assume are 55+ are not verified as such.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**No verified 55+ ownership stock in Scottsdale.** Every strata complex checked in this sub-area either states no age restriction in its sources or carries only an old sub-55 age claim that has been legally void since Nov 24, 2022 (Chancellor Place, below). Do not market any Scottsdale strata as 55+ without the strata's own bylaws in hand. The verified 55+ housing in this sub-area is non-market rental (below) — this is where North Delta's subsidized 55+ supply actually is.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. This is the rule that decides Chancellor Place: its old 19+ claim is not a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** applies parcel by parcel. Single-detached minimum lots run RS1 at 4,000 m², RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² or 460 m², RS6 at 390 m² and RS7 at 335 m²; duplex zones run RD1 at 836 m², RD2 and RD3 at 4,000 m², and RD4 at 504 m²; there is no RD5 zone. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD1.pdf
- **Seniors congregate housing is a named use in Delta's apartment zones.** The City's Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone, added to Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 in 2024 for six-storey apartment development under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation, lists "seniors congregate housing" as a permitted principal use alongside apartment buildings. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **Transit-Oriented Area rules override the older pattern near the exchange.** The 2024 OCP designates a Scottsdale Exchange TOD Area, and Bill 47 requires minimum densities within 400 metres of Scottsdale Exchange — the corridor where any future seniors apartment product in this sub-area would most likely be proposed. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")** is Delta's adopted OCP, adopted July 8, 2024; it consolidated the former area plans into a single Land Use Plan, and Map 2 shows Small Scale Residential and Mixed Residential designations in and around this area. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **Secondary suites and small-scale multi-unit housing:** Delta's SSMUH regulations allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites, with reduced parking requirements — the route by which a Scottsdale house lot adds a suite for a parent or caregiver. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Property tax deferment at 55+:** BC's Property Tax Deferment Program (regular program) lets a homeowner who is 55 or older during the current year defer annual property taxes on a principal residence as a provincial loan; the City of Delta's tax page links the program and the Home Owner Grant. Delta's 2026 tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Subsidized seniors rental runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry and the City guide.** All three buildings in Section 2 apply through the Housing Registry (call 604-433-2218 per BC Housing's Zone 2 directory), and the City of Delta Affordable Housing Guide (April 2026) is the City's own list of the buildings, including the SAFER (Shelter Aid for Elderly Renters) rent supplement for seniors aged 60+ paying more than 30% of income on rent. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf and https://www.bchousing.org//publications/Zone-2-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo I can buy in Scottsdale?** No verified one. Chancellor Place, the tower usually named, carries only an old 19+ listing claim — invalid since Nov 24, 2022 — and no source states a 55+ bylaw. Delta Rise and the other Scott Road buildings state no age restriction at all. Scottsdale's verified 55+ housing is rental, and subsidized rental at that. https://www.richmondcondoshomes.com/solds/listing.r2215734-1405-11910-80-avenue-delta-v4c-8e3.71280028
- **Q: What 55+ housing does exist here, then?** Three non-profit rentals: Scott Road Lodge (42 rent-geared-to-income 1-bedrooms, 55+), Strawberry Hill Manor (48 units, studio–1 bedroom, 55+) and White Birch Manor (48 rent-geared-to-income 1-bedrooms, 55+ or under 55 with a disability). All are rentals with eligibility and application processes — there is nothing to buy in any of them. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **Q: How do I apply for Scott Road Lodge, Strawberry Hill Manor or White Birch Manor?** All three apply through BC Housing's Housing Registry (604-433-2218 per the Zone 2 directory). Scott Road Lodge and White Birch Manor are rent-geared-to-income, so income rules apply on top of the 55+ age requirement; the City guide lists Strawberry Hill Manor's rent type as "Market rent" despite the Registry application route — confirm the current rent basis with Affordable Housing Societies (604-596-0066 per the City guide) before assuming a subsidy. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf and https://www.bchousing.org//publications/Zone-2-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Delta?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. For the rental buildings in Section 2, household age rules are set by the operator and the program — ask the Registry or the operator, because the strata exemptions do not govern a tenancy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. Note White Birch Manor's rental rule runs the other way as well: it also admits a person under 55 with a disability. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, which is also why Chancellor Place's old "No Rentals" wording no longer holds. Two catches remain where a valid 55+ bylaw exists: the tenant still has to satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day, and is there a seniors bus?** Scottsdale Exchange is the hub for this side of North Delta: nine local routes plus the R6 Scott Road RapidBus to Scott Road Station (Expo Line) and Newton Exchange. Separately, the City runs a free Delta Seniors Bus for Delta residents aged 65+ — note the 65+ threshold, not 55+ — with curbside pick-up/drop-off inside Delta, medical appointments given priority, and booking up to 7 days ahead. https://www.translink.ca/r6 and http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/seniors-services/seniors-bus
- **Q: Is there a seniors centre in the sub-area?** The North Delta seniors centre is Kennedy Seniors Recreation Centre at 11760 88 Avenue, just north of the sub-area in the Kennedy cluster — the City bills it as recreation for 50+, run with the Kennedy Seniors Society. Chancellor Place listings themselves pitch the building as a short distance to Kennedy Seniors Centre and Sungod Recreation Centre. http://www.delta.ca/services/seniors/seniors-recreation-centres/lists/seniors-recreation-centres/kennedy-seniors-recreation-centre and https://www.richmondcondoshomes.com/solds/listing.r2215734-1405-11910-80-avenue-delta-v4c-8e3.71280028
- **Q: Does the exchange change what my lot is worth or what can be built?** It can: Bill 47 minimum densities apply within 400 metres of Scottsdale Exchange per the City, so two similar lots can sit under different density expectations. Confirm which side of the TOD line a property is on before pricing development potential — and note the exchange itself is on the Surrey side of Scott Road. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and http://www.delta.ca/node/1838
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes here at 55+?** Yes, if the home is your principal residence — BC's regular deferment program is open at 55 or older during the current year (also to surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). Delta's tax page links the program; the City's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **North Delta's entire subsidized 55+ stock is three buildings, and all three are in this one sub-area.** Scott Road Lodge (42 units), Strawberry Hill Manor (48) and White Birch Manor (48) total 138 units — the complete North Delta list in the City's Affordable Housing Guide (April 2026). Annieville, Nordel and Sunshine Hills Woods have none. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **Two of those buildings face each other, and their addresses are routinely swapped in directories.** Scott Road Lodge and Strawberry Hill Manor sit across 119A Street from each other in the Strawberry Hill pocket; the City guide addresses the Lodge to 119 Street where the operator and BC Housing say 119A Street, and one seniors directory gives the Lodge Strawberry Hill's 7667 number. Verify the building by name and operator (both are Affordable Housing Societies, 604-596-0066), not by the street number alone. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf and https://www.bchousing.org//publications/Zone-2-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **White Birch Manor changed operators, and the directories have not caught up with each other.** New Vista Society states it took over the 48-unit building on February 1 for people 55 and older, chosen by BC Housing; the City guide (April 2026) reflects New Vista, while BC Housing's Zone 2 directory (May 2025) still lists the previous society and a different phone number. https://newvista.bc.ca/affordable-housing/housing-rentals/ and https://www.bchousing.org//publications/Zone-2-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Chancellor Place's "adult" reputation is a pre-2022 artifact.** The tower's social activities, indoor pool and seniors-centre proximity are real listing facts, but its only documented age claim is a 19+ bylaw that has been unenforceable since Nov 24, 2022 — reputation is not a bylaw, and no source shows a 55+ conversion vote. https://www.richmondcondoshomes.com/solds/listing.r2215734-1405-11910-80-avenue-delta-v4c-8e3.71280028 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The co-op on 80 Avenue welcomes seniors but restricts nothing.** Arland Mews (44 units) lists "No age requirement" in the City guide while a co-op directory describes it as welcoming seniors over 55 — a buyer who needs the certainty of an age-restricted building does not get it from a welcome statement. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **A Delta Shoppers Mall redevelopment concept shown to Council in November 2022 included 124 seniors housing units in a 10-storey midrise** alongside 624 market condos and 65 purpose-built rentals — but the project was still in the design phase with no development application submitted at that presentation. It is a concept, not a building: do not count it as supply. Status since that presentation: UNCONFIRMED. http://globalawards.news/seniors-housing-to-be-interwoven-within-high-rise-shopping-mall/
