# West Vancouver — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- West Vancouver is one municipality — the District of West Vancouver, between the Capilano River and Horseshoe Bay — governed by Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. Unlike "North Vancouver," there is no second municipality hiding inside the name. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- The OCP emphasizes the Ambleside Municipal Town Centre as the heart of West Vancouver, with retail, services, restaurants, arts and culture, offices, civic services and waterfront recreation; Park Royal, at Marine Drive and Taylor Way on the District's east edge, is recognized in the OCP as the regional shopping centre. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- Marine Drive is the District's east–west main road; the Lions Gate Bridge at its east end connects West Vancouver to downtown Vancouver, and Highway 1 (Upper Levels) crosses the District's upper bench to Horseshoe Bay. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
- Where the 55+ stock actually sits: it is a Marine Drive / village-centre pattern, not a District-wide spread. The verified strata buildings are in Ambleside (Westview Terrace, 1390 Duchess Ave) and beside the West Vancouver Community Centre / Dundarave edge (Tudor Gardens, 843 22nd St; Lincoln Gardens, 2110 Marine Dr). The Kiwanis seniors rental cluster is one block west of the Community Centre, on 21st Street (959, 975 and 999 21st St) and Gordon Avenue. The care residences line Marine Drive and Clyde Avenue. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- School District 45 (West Vancouver) serves the District, with no middle tier — elementary Kindergarten to Grade 7, secondary Grade 8 to Grade 12 — and address-based catchments confirmed through the district's school locator. The Ambleside schools are Hollyburn Elementary, 1329 Duchess Ave; Irwin Park Elementary, 2455 Haywood Ave; and West Vancouver Secondary, 1750 Mathers Ave. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/ and https://westvancouverschools.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Location-Address.pdf
- Transit: route 250 runs from downtown Vancouver via the Lions Gate Bridge along Marine Drive to Dundarave, with some trips continuing to Horseshoe Bay Ferry Terminal; routes 251, 253, 254 and 255 feed the neighbourhoods into the Park Royal exchange. There is no SkyTrain and no SeaBus in West Vancouver — the bus over the bridge is the transit trip. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
- Health care: Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street in North Vancouver (Vancouver Coastal Health), is the hospital serving West Vancouver; the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at Suite 200, 221 West Esplanade, North Vancouver. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital
- Recreation anchors for the 55+ clusters: West Vancouver Community Centre and Aquatic Centre, 2121 Marine Drive, which also houses the Seniors' Activity Centre, and West Vancouver Memorial Library, 1950 Marine Drive — both within a block or two of Tudor Gardens, Lincoln Gardens and Hollyburn House. https://westvancouver.ca/arts-culture/west-vancouver-memorial-library and https://parcliving.ca/blog/the-best-senior-living-location-in-west-vancouver-bc/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why directory lines saying "rentals not allowed" at Tudor Gardens cannot be read at face value. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **District zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010.** Section 200 (single family dwelling zones) states minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, and lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses in those zones; apartment areas in Ambleside are zoned RM1 and RM2. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Ground-oriented density in the bylaw:** the District's ground-oriented dwelling rules state 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop. Note, following the batch-2 triple-check: provincial SSMUH figures are framed by the Province as minimums a municipality must allow; West Vancouver's own final bylaw position on those lots is a separate check — do not quote a West Van SSMUH maximum from the provincial numbers alone. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **Foreshore and flood rules reach the Ambleside flats.** A Foreshore Development Permit is required for work in the Foreshore Development Permit Area (waterfront property or property at risk of coastal flooding); new habitable space must be built to the Flood Construction Level where the property is at risk of coastal flooding, and work within 15 metres of the natural boundary (high tide mark) must follow the Foreshore Protection Area guidelines. An Environmental Development Permit is required within 15 metres of the top of a watercourse bank. https://www.westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/natural-environment-and-hazard-development-permit-areas/foreshore-development-permits
- **Density direction beside the villages:** a July 21, 2025 provincial ministerial directive requires increased density in the Park Royal–Taylor Way area and in single-family/duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave — the two village areas where all of this file's strata 55+ buildings sit. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698
- **Kiwanis eligibility is not strata law.** The Manor's 55+ age is an operator eligibility rule for below-market rental housing (with independent-living and application requirements set by the society), not a strata age bylaw, and no unit in the cluster can be bought. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-manor/
- **Taxes:** the District sets its own rates; current-year due dates, penalty dates and mill rates are UNVERIFIED in this file.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in West Vancouver?** Three strata buildings were verified in this research: Westview Terrace (1390 Duchess Ave, Ambleside), Tudor Gardens (843 22nd St) and Lincoln Gardens (2110 Marine Dr) — and Lincoln Gardens carries the flagged 55+ / 60+ source conflict in Section 2, so its Form B bylaw is the deciding document. That is the whole verified ownership list for the District in this pass. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-plus-buildings-west-vancouver/
- **Q: Is Wedgewood Terrace on Duchess Avenue also 55+?** Not verified. A building directory records "Age restrictions" among its bylaws, but no source checked states the age is 55+ — do not treat a Duchess Avenue listing there as a 55+ purchase until the bylaw itself is read. http://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/wedgewood-terrace-1730-duchess-ave
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55?** Provincially, no: a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt, as are live-in caregivers, legacy residents, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children the resident cared for before 19. Confirm the specific building's bylaw wording in the Form B — the listings checked for Westview Terrace and Tudor Gardens state the restriction without the one-occupant detail. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** It runs opposite ways in the two verified Ambleside/Dundarave buildings checked: Tudor Gardens listings state no pets, while the Wedgewood Terrace directory entry (not a verified 55+ building) records pets allowed with restrictions. Kiwanis states pets are not permitted in its seniors housing communities. Check the individual bylaw every time. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3120687-101-843-22nd-street-west-vancouver-british-columbia-v7v4c1-739900/brochure and https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/lynn-woods/
- **Q: What is the difference between Westerleigh, Amica and buying at Tudor Gardens?** Tudor Gardens is a strata — you buy a unit and the age rule is a strata bylaw. Westerleigh PARC (725 22nd St) and Amica West Vancouver (659 Clyde Ave) are rental retirement residences; Amica also carries registered assisted living and long-term care, and Hollyburn House (2095 Marine Dr) is a registered seniors assisted-living residence. You rent a suite and services in the residences; there is nothing to buy and no resale. https://parcliving.ca/westerleigh-parc/ and https://www.amica.ca/west-vancouver
- **Q: Is the Kiwanis cluster an option if I cannot afford Ambleside strata prices?** It is the below-market rental route: Kiwanis Manor at 959 21st St is 55+ independent living, and the Court, Cypress and Terrace buildings stand in the same 21st Street / Gordon Avenue cluster, each with its own age rule (UNCONFIRMED per building in this pass). Applications are through the society, eligibility applies, and nothing is purchased. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-manor/
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** There is no hospital in West Vancouver. Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street in North Vancouver, serves the District, and the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 221 West Esplanade — both are a Marine Drive bus or drive east, on route 250's corridor. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. In a District where the verified 55+ ownership stock is three buildings, that pool question matters more, not less. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **West Vancouver's verified 55+ ownership stock is three buildings, all within about a kilometre of Marine Drive.** Westview Terrace in Ambleside, and Tudor Gardens and Lincoln Gardens beside the Community Centre — there is no verified 55+ strata in Dundarave village proper, Horseshoe Bay, the British Properties or the Cypress side in any source checked. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-plus-buildings-west-vancouver/
- **The newest Kiwanis buildings on the same street are not seniors housing at all.** Kiwanis Village West (950 and 970 22nd St, 156 homes, opened late 2025) is all-ages workforce and family rental at about 75% of market rents — a buyer or renter who assumes "Kiwanis on 22nd" means seniors will be reading the wrong building. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/opening-more-homes-in-west-vancouver
- **Two different "Hollyburn" seniors names are not housing you can buy.** Hollyburn House (2095 Marine Dr) is a registered assisted-living retirement home, and Hollyburn Elementary (1329 Duchess Ave) is a school — neither is a condo building, and neither the Ambleside "Hollyburn" listings language nor a directory's seniors label makes a strata 55+. https://cogirseniorliving.ca/retirement-home-west-vancouver-british-columbia/venvi-hollyburn-house/ and https://westvancouverschools.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Location-Address.pdf
- **The 55+ buildings sit exactly where the province is pushing more density.** The July 21, 2025 ministerial directive targets the Park Royal–Taylor Way area and the single-family/duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave — the same two villages that hold Westview Terrace, Tudor Gardens and Lincoln Gardens — so the streets around these buildings are the District's named change area, not its quiet backwater. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698
- **One old listing can keep a wrong age alive for years.** Lincoln Gardens was marketed in 2007 as 60+ and is listed today on a 55+ buildings page; since Nov 24, 2022 only a 55+ bylaw is a valid strata age bylaw in BC, so an old "60+" figure in a search result is a history artifact, not a rule a buyer can rely on — the filed bylaw in the strata documents is the only deciding source. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/NSR/070118/NSRR070118_A09.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Ambleside's apartment zoning is the reason the 55+ stock looks the way it does.** The District's apartment areas are zoned RM1 and RM2 under Bylaw No. 4662, with form-and-character development permit guidelines in the Ambleside Apartment Area — the low-rise concrete buildings on Duchess Avenue and Marine Drive, 55+ and general-market alike, are that zoning's product, and no new age-restricted strata project appears in any source checked. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
