## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. That is why the Manor Ridge directory line "rentals not allowed" cannot be read at face value. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Short-term rentals in Surrey:** the City allows them only for an owner living at the property as their primary residence, with a business licence, and the strata can still restrict them by bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000** governs Cloverdale. Plans: the current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the replaced Bylaw No. 18020 as current; the Cloverdale Town Centre Plan update (Section 1) is the local plan in motion. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH):** Surrey allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — context for the detached ground around the condo stock, not a condo-building rule. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Zoning Bylaw No. 12000):** one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite, and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. (Area research file, Section 4.)
- **Property taxes (city-wide, not area-specific):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **First-time-buyer programs:** any FHSA / Home Buyers' Plan dollar limit is UNVERIFIED in this file — no live CRA page was read in this pass, and no limit is stated.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ condos can I actually buy in Cloverdale under this band?** One building: Carriage House (17695 58 Ave, a 1987 downtown condo building whose listings state 55+ in terms, with no pets permitted). The community's other verified 55+ complex, Manor Ridge (6140 192 St), is townhouses — real supply for a Cloverdale downsizer, but outside a condos-only feed. Whether any given Carriage House unit is listed under $800,000 at a given moment is a live-feed question no published source answers in advance. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: How many Cloverdale condos under $800K are 55+?** That count cannot be stated from any published source: SnapStats publishes no price-band cut and no condo-only cut (its Cloverdale attached figure is 194 combined Condos & Townhomes listings in September 2026, median $740,000). What can be stated is the building list in Section 2. (Area research file, Sections 1–2.)
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55?** At Manor Ridge, listings state 1 occupant must be 55+. Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt in any case — confirm Carriage House's own bylaw wording in its Form B, because no listing checked for that building stated the one-occupant detail in terms. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I have a pet?** The two Cloverdale complexes are opposites in the sources checked: Carriage House listings state "No pets permitted," while Manor Ridge listings allow 1 pet with restrictions. City-wide, Fleetwood Gardens records pets allowed with restrictions and Chelsea Gardens records pets allowed with restrictions. There is no Surrey-wide pet rule for 55+ housing — read the individual complex's bylaws. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8 and https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/311-16068-83-ave-1729077
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside; the Province states under-55s can own but may not be able to live in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit, or run an Airbnb?** Long-term: yes in a strata — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022; the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption. Short-term: Surrey allows it only for an owner living at the property as their primary residence, with a business licence, and the strata can still restrict short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Q: Is Bethshan Gardens a cheaper way into a Cloverdale "condo"?** No — it is not a condo at all. Bethshan Gardens (17528 59 Ave) is a society-owned independent-living rental residence; you rent there, you do not buy, and its numeric age eligibility is UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked, so ask the society directly. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/CLR/230928/CLRN250928_A11.pdf
- **Q: Why do Clayton buildings show up on a Cloverdale condos page?** Because the feed's working scope is the whole Cloverdale board community, Clayton included — the research file's identity call, from the feed's own cards. Clayton attached product also moved faster in September 2026 (21% sales ratio, 20 solds on 96 listings) than the Cloverdale sub-area's (17%, 17 on 98) — but none of it is verified 55+. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-condos-under-800k.html (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report)
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Will the SkyTrain change things for these complexes?** The project names Hillcrest-184 Street and Clayton (190 Street) stations with an anticipated in-service date of 2029, on the Clayton (northern) edge of the community — neither verified Cloverdale 55+ complex is on Fraser Highway: Carriage House is in the downtown core and Manor Ridge is on 192 Street. Price the plan, not operating service. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/ and https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/surrey-langley-skytrain
