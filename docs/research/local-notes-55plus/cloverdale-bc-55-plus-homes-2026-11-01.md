# Cloverdale, Surrey — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Cloverdale is one of the seven communities the City of Surrey lists — City Centre, Cloverdale, Fleetwood, Guildford, Newton, South Surrey and Whalley — and the City describes it as Surrey's historic centre and its third largest community. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities and https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/cloverdale
- City-stated edges: the western border runs along 160 Street and connects with 48 Avenue, which is the southern border; Langley is to the east, and Guildford and Fleetwood are to the north, separated by 84 Avenue and 76 Avenue. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/cloverdale
- Board geography differs from City geography: in the SnapStats data, "Cloverdale" is its own Fraser Valley board community made up of three sub-areas — Clayton, Cloverdale and Serpentine (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the Cloverdale area research file).

The Town Centre plan update is being driven in part by the planned new Cloverdale hospital and BC Cancer Centre; the hospital's address, opening date and services are UNVERIFIED on a Fraser Health page opened in this pass. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the Manor Ridge directory line "rentals not allowed" cannot be read at face value. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000** governs Cloverdale, including the small-scale multi-unit housing framework: up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. The former RF-12 / RF-12C zones were replaced by the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone and RF-SD by R6 Semi-Detached in the July 8, 2024 zoning changes — zone names on older Cloverdale listings may be pre-2024 names. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites:** only one secondary suite is permitted per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite, and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. Coach houses and garden suites run 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), with lane access required for a coach house on smaller lots and one extra parking space. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Short-term rentals in Surrey:** a short-term rental is under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required before operating — on top of any strata short-term rental bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **OCP: "Surrey 2050" Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900** is the current OCP — finally adopted June 15, 2026, replacing the 2013 OCP Bylaw No. 18020. The Cloverdale Town Centre Plan update runs inside that framework. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **ALR edge:** Cloverdale includes ALR farm parcels on its lowland side; the ALC housing frame for an ALR parcel is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). Parcel-level detail is in the Cloverdale farms notes. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **First-home programs:** the First Home Savings Account and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file — no live CRA page stating the current limits was read in this pass, so no figure is published here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Cloverdale?** Two strata complexes verified in this research: Carriage House (17695 58 Ave, a 1987 condo building in downtown Cloverdale) and The Estates at Manor Ridge (6140 192 St, a 1989 townhouse complex). No 55+ manufactured-home park, membership complex or subsidized ownership product was verified inside Cloverdale. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55?** At Manor Ridge, listings state 1 occupant must be 55+. Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt in any case — confirm Carriage House's own bylaw wording in its Form B, because no listing checked for that building stated the one-occupant detail in terms. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I have a pet?** The two complexes are opposites in the sources checked: Carriage House listings state "No pets permitted," while Manor Ridge listings allow 1 pet with restrictions. There is no Cloverdale-wide pet rule — read the individual complex's bylaws. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ complex as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, including any older "rentals not allowed" record for Manor Ridge. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Bethshan Gardens something I can buy into?** No. Bethshan Gardens (17528 59 Ave) is an independent-living rental residence owned by the Cloverdale Seniors Citizen Housing Society — you rent there; you do not buy a unit. Its numeric age eligibility is UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked, so ask the society directly. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/CLR/230928/CLRN230928_A11.pdf
- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing in Clayton?** None was verified in any source checked in this pass. Clayton held about half the Cloverdale community's attached inventory in September 2026 (96 of 194 Condos & Townhomes listings, SnapStats FVR September 2026), but that stock is general-market in the sources checked — a Clayton listing that reads "downsizer-friendly" is not an age bylaw (Cloverdale area research file, Sections 2 and 5).
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** There is no hospital inside Cloverdale today; the planned new Cloverdale hospital and BC Cancer Centre are part of why the Town Centre Plan is being updated, and their address, opening date and services are UNVERIFIED on a Fraser Health page opened in this pass. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
- **Q: Will the SkyTrain change things for these two complexes?** TransLink states the Surrey-Langley SkyTrain runs along Fraser Highway with an anticipated in-service date of 2029, and the City states three stations are planned for Cloverdale — but the station names and locations were not confirmed on an official page in this pass (UNVERIFIED), and neither verified complex is on Fraser Highway: Carriage House is in the downtown core and Manor Ridge is on 192 Street near Highway 1. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/surrey-langley-skytrain
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Cloverdale's whole verified 55+ ownership supply is two complexes, and they are different products.** A 1987 downtown condo building with a no-pets rule (Carriage House) and a 1989 townhouse complex on 192 Street that allows a pet (Manor Ridge). A buyer who wants 55+ in Cloverdale is choosing between those two forms, not shopping a deep bench. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **The city-wide Surrey 55+ notes understate Cloverdale.** The Surrey city-wide file in this series lists Manor Ridge as Cloverdale's only verified complex; Carriage House was verified separately in this pass from three independent listings (R3164723, R3080712, R3089060) that all state 55+ in terms. A Cloverdale-only search run off the city-wide file alone misses half the community's verified stock.
- **Cloverdale's seniors rental sits one block from its seniors condo.** Bethshan Gardens (17528 59 Ave, society-owned rental) and Carriage House (17695 58 Ave, strata ownership) are both in the downtown core — the local ladder from owned condo to rental-with-community exists inside two blocks, but the rental side is a society residence, not a strata you can buy later. https://www.apartments.com/profile/8z5mld1/
- **The board's Cloverdale is not the City's Cloverdale.** SnapStats splits the board community into Clayton, Cloverdale and Serpentine, and Serpentine recorded 0 listings and 0 sales in both residential splits in September 2026 — the farm feed that trades under "Cloverdale" sits in Serpentine (see the Cloverdale farms notes), so residential statistics for "Cloverdale" silently exclude the farm market (SnapStats FVR September 2026, via the Cloverdale area research file).
- **Old zone names still circulate on Cloverdale listings.** Surrey replaced RF-12 / RF-12C with R4 and RF-SD with R6 on July 8, 2024 — a Cloverdale listing or seller sheet quoting RF-12 coach-house potential is quoting a repealed zone name; the lot's current zone has to be checked in Zoning Bylaw 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **The Town Centre plan boundary moved after many sellers' material was printed.** Council expanded the Cloverdale Town Centre Plan boundary north to 64 Avenue on July 22, 2024, and the update is being built around the planned hospital — a lot's plan position should be read off the current plan page, not an older brochure. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
