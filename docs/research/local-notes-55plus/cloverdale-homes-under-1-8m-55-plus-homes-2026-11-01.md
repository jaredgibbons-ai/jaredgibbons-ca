## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. A detached freehold house — this slug's product — has no strata and therefore no strata age bylaw at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, including any older "rentals not allowed" record for Manor Ridge. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000** governs Cloverdale, and its zone names changed on July 8, 2024: RF-12 / RF-12C / RF-13 became R4, RF-9 / RF-9C / RF-10 became R5, RF / RF-SS became R3, and RF-SD became R6. The live page's FAQ still quotes the pre-2024 names ("RF-12c or RF-13") for Clayton coach-home lots — out of date against the current bylaw, flagged here and in the research file. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Plans:** the current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the replaced Bylaw No. 18020 as current.
- **Secondary suites, coach houses and SSMUH (the detached-stock rules in this band):** one secondary suite per dwelling unit, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area and never inside a coach house or garden suite; a coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), with lane access required for a coach house on smaller lots and one extra parking space required; Surrey allows 3–4 SSMUH units on most lots by size band, up to 6 near a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **ALR housing frame (Cloverdale's rural fringe):** on ALR parcels, one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), with local zoning able to be more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property taxes (city-wide, not area-specific):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55?** At Manor Ridge, listings state 1 occupant must be 55+. Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt in any case — confirm Carriage House's own bylaw wording in its Form B, because no listing checked for that building stated the one-occupant detail in terms. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I have a pet?** The two complexes are opposites in the sources checked: Carriage House listings state "No pets permitted," while Manor Ridge listings allow 1 pet with restrictions. There is no Cloverdale-wide pet rule. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: I'm selling a Cloverdale house under $1.8M to downsize into 55+ — how fast is each side moving?** SnapStats publishes no band cut; at sub-area level in September 2026, Clayton detached sold at a 29% ratio (34 listings, 10 solds) against the Cloverdale sub-area's 13% (167 listings, 22 solds), and the community detached median was $1,212,500 — $587,500 below this page's ceiling by arithmetic comparison only. The buy side (the two complexes) has no published pace figure at all. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ complex, or rent out the house I keep?** In a strata: long-term rental bans no longer exist — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022; the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. A detached house you keep is not strata — its rental position is tenancy law and City rules, not a bylaw question. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: The page FAQ mentions RF-12c / RF-13 coach-home zoning — can I rely on it for a Clayton lot in this band?** No. Those are pre-2024 zone names; Surrey's July 8, 2024 changes replaced them with the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone, and a coach house today runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² only on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), with lane access required on smaller lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Q: Is Bethshan Gardens something I can buy into with the proceeds of my house sale?** No. Bethshan Gardens (17528 59 Ave) is an independent-living rental residence owned by the Cloverdale Seniors Citizen Housing Society — you rent there; you do not buy a unit, and its numeric age eligibility is UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/CLR/230928/CLRN250928_A11.pdf
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool at Manor Ridge or Carriage House?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. That trade-off is built into every BC 55+ strata since 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Will the coming SkyTrain stations help a Cloverdale downsizer?** The stations serving this community — Hillcrest-184 Street and Clayton (190 Street), anticipated 2029 — are both on the Clayton side, near neither verified 55+ complex. They are a plan fact for Clayton sellers in this band, not a present amenity for a Carriage House or Manor Ridge buyer. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/
