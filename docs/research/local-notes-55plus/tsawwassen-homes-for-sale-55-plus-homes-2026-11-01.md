# Tsawwassen — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. This is the community-wide Tsawwassen file (City of Delta, plus the Tsawwassen First Nation lands in Tsawwassen North) — it consolidates the Tsawwassen sub-area files in this series (Tsawwassen Central, Tsawwassen East / Pebble Hill, Beach Grove, English Bluff, Cliff Drive, Boundary Beach, Tsawwassen North).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Tsawwassen is one of Delta's three communities (with Ladner and North Delta; Tsawwassen + Ladner = South Delta) — the coastal peninsula between the Strait of Georgia, Boundary Bay and the Point Roberts (USA) border. Its areas are English Bluff, Cliff Drive, Tsawwassen Central, Tsawwassen East (Pebble Hill), Beach Grove, Boundary Beach and Tsawwassen North, plus Tsawwassen Springs. https://jaredgibbons.ca/tsawwassen-homes-for-sale.html
- Governing plan (City of Delta side): Delta's Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future," adopted July 8, 2024) — a single consolidated plan with NO lettered area-plan schedules; the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 of the PREVIOUS OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, 1985; farm land in Bylaw 8400 is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s.1.5.1), and the Tsawwassen Urban Centre Development Permit Area IS in Bylaw 8400, Appendix B. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Tenure split: the freehold City of Delta neighbourhoods sit south of 28 Avenue; Tsawwassen North (Tsawwassen Shores / Tsawwassen Landing, and Tsawwassen Springs product on the north side) sits on Tsawwassen First Nation lands under TFN jurisdiction, not City of Delta zoning. The live page states the North product is held under 99-year prepaid residential leases — that lease-term claim is site copy in this program and is UNVERIFIED against a TFN or title source; confirm it off the lease documents for the specific unit. https://jaredgibbons.ca/tsawwassen-homes-for-sale.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- 56 Street is the town-centre spine: the verified 55+ ownership buildings, the KinVillage campus, The Waterford and The Wexford all sit on or just off 56 Street / 54A Street / 10 Avenue in Tsawwassen Central. Sources: Section 2 below.
- Schools (SD37 Delta, confirmed against the BC Ministry K-12 file): Beach Grove Elementary, 5955 17a Ave; Cliff Drive Elementary, 5025 12 Ave; English Bluff Elementary, 402 English Bluff Rd; Pebble Hill Traditional Elementary, 246 52a St; South Park Elementary, 735 Gilchrist Dr; South Delta Secondary, 750 53 St. Catchment for a specific address is UNVERIFIED until checked on SD37's catchment maps. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/student-registration/catchment-area-maps/
- Health care: the nearest hospital is Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner (Fraser Health); Fraser Health also lists a South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 1826–4949 Canoe Pass Way, Tsawwassen. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- Transit and ferries: TransLink route 601 runs Boundary Bay trips via 12 Avenue, Boundary Bay Road, 3 Avenue and 67 Street, continuing via South Delta Exchange and Ladner Exchange to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line). BC Ferries publishes 1 hour 35 minutes for Tsawwassen–Swartz Bay (Victoria) and 2 hours for Tsawwassen–Duke Point (Nanaimo). https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf and https://www.bcferries.com/current-conditions/departures?redir=301
- Foreshore flood rules: Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands) in Bylaw 8400 sets a habitable floor level no lower than 2.9 metres GSC datum, minimum land surface elevation around construction of 2.3 metres GSC datum, and no building within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. Whether a specific lot is inside SD3 is UNVERIFIED — it requires a DeltaMap check for that lot. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: entirely in Tsawwassen Central. English Bluff, Cliff Drive, Beach Grove, Pebble Hill / Tsawwassen East, Boundary Beach and Tsawwassen North each have ZERO verified 55+ ownership stock in this series. Sources: Section 2 below and the sub-area files in this folder.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership) — the whole of Tsawwassen: 2 buildings, both in Central

- **The Lexington — 1150 54A Street (Tsawwassen Central).** Freehold strata, plan NWS2700, built 1988, 38 units, self-managed, at 54A Street and 11 Avenue. Building sources record an adult-oriented community with an age restriction of 55+, one pet allowed but no dogs, and rentals not permitted in the building's own bylaw summary; amenities are a workshop, storage locker and recreation room. Current MLS listings state the rule as "Age restriction 55+, one occupant must be 55+," with a community room, bike room and workshop. Read the rental line against Section 3: provincial law voided strata rental-restriction bylaws in 2022, so confirm the current position on the Form B. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street and https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/1150-54a-street/201
- **Tsawwassen Greene — 1140 55 Street (Tsawwassen Central).** Freehold strata condo, 27 units in the building source, built 1991 (one building source lists 1990 — build year conflict flagged). Current MLS listing text (MLS R3091035) states "Age restricted 55+," with fees including secure underground parking and a storage locker, and one dog or cat allowed to 12 inches at the shoulder — a listing-stated pet rule, confirm it in the strata bylaws. https://www.realtor.ca/real-estate/29373447/204-1140-55-street-delta and https://sothebysrealty.ca/en/property/british-columbia/region-fraser-valley/delta-real-estate/2914521/1140-55-street-no204/

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- **The Tides at KinVillage — 1070 54A Street (Central).** The first phase of the KinVillage campus redevelopment: 152 homes in total — 106 subsidized homes for seniors with low incomes and 46 market rental suites for moderate incomes — all designed adaptable or accessible, with a 450-square-metre day program on the main floor; owned and operated by the KinVillage Association. Rental only — nothing to buy. https://canada.constructconnect.com/joc/news/projects/2025/11/seniors-find-new-homes-as-152-units-open-in-delta and https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-seniors-housing-breaks-ground-in-delta
- **KinVillage independent-living courts — campus around 54A Street and 10 Avenue.** KinVillage's own application page lists South Court (11 units, 430–550 sq ft) and Vidal Court (30 units, primarily 578 sq ft, some to 813 sq ft; BC Housing's directory gives Vidal Court's address as 5492 11 Ave); North Court (86 units) is the building the City's Phase 2 report records as demolished and replaced through The Tides and the Phase 2 site. KinVillage states it provides subsidized and market rental housing for independent older adults, that subsidized rent is 30% of household gross income through BC Housing, that eligibility includes being age 55 or older (or a person with a disability who can live independently), and that pets and smoking are not allowed in the buildings. Rental only. https://www.kinvillage.org/independent-living and https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. The Lexington's older building summary says rentals are not permitted; read that line against this rule and confirm the current position on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Delta plan and zoning (City side):** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, no lettered schedules — any reference to a "Tsawwassen Area Plan" is to Schedule D.1 of the repealed Bylaw No. 3950. Tsawwassen First Nation lands in the North are under TFN jurisdiction, not Delta zoning. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Foreshore development permits:** DPA SD3 in Bylaw 8400 sets the 2.9 m GSC datum habitable floor level, 2.3 m minimum land elevation and 7.5 m tidal setback for the Boundary Bay foreshore and Southlands — check whether the parcel is inside SD3 before pricing a rebuild or addition. Source: Section 1 above.
- **Manufactured-home pad rules:** the one manufactured-home community found in Tsawwassen research (Tsawwassen RV Resort, on TFN lands) is all-ages, so there is no 55+ park product here. Where a BC manufactured-home site tenancy does apply, the 2026 increase limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees, once every 12 months on three full months' notice, under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act framework. Whether that provincial framework applies to a site on Tsawwassen Lands is UNVERIFIED — TFN law governs its own lands. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Delta property taxes (City side):** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. That calendar does not by itself state the tax treatment of TFN leasehold lands in Tsawwassen North. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many 55+ homes can I actually buy in Tsawwassen?** Two buildings in the whole community, both in Central: The Lexington (38 units, built 1988) and Tsawwassen Greene (27 units, built 1991). Everything else age-restricted in Tsawwassen is rental, care or subsidized. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in The Lexington or Tsawwassen Greene?** Yes — provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); the Lexington's own listing wording is "one occupant must be 55+." Caregivers, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/1150-54a-street/201
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit for a parent, or to hold?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside, not who may own. A 55+ parent can be the resident of a unit owned by a younger buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Older summaries for The Lexington that say "rentals not permitted" predate or ignore that change — confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Building by building, and the two verified buildings differ: The Lexington's building source records one pet allowed but no dogs; a Tsawwassen Greene listing states one dog or cat to 12 inches at the shoulder. KinVillage's rental buildings allow neither pets nor smoking. Confirm the current bylaw for the specific building before an offer. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Monterra, the gated rancher community by Centennial Beach, a 55+ complex?** Not verified as one. Its own MLS data sheet calls it "Adult Oriented, Gated Complex" in site influences but lists no age restriction in its bylaw line, and no other source checked states one. Treat it as general-market unless the registered bylaws / Form B for a specific purchase say otherwise. https://www.cotala.com/tours/72783/FullPublic.pdf?i=1871186173
- **Q: What is different about buying in Tsawwassen North?** Tenure. The North product sits on Tsawwassen First Nation lands, not City of Delta freehold — the live page describes 99-year prepaid residential leases, but that term was not verified against a TFN or title source in this program (UNVERIFIED), and none of the North product carries a verified 55+ bylaw in any case. Price and finance it off the lease documents and title, not off a comparison with freehold Central. https://jaredgibbons.ca/tsawwassen-homes-for-sale.html
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or a buyer who will not live in the unit. With only two small buildings carrying the restriction in Tsawwassen, the pool effect and the scarcity effect run at the same time. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, is in Ladner — the drive north on 56 Street / Highway 17 corridor. Inside Tsawwassen, Fraser Health lists the South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 1826–4949 Canoe Pass Way. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Tsawwassen?** The in-community ladder is unusually complete for a community this size, and it is all in Central: independent strata living (Lexington, Tsawwassen Greene), independent rental with a subsidized stream (KinVillage courts and The Tides), independent living residences (The Waterford, The Wexford), assisted living (KinVillage, 68 subsidized suites; the Waterford Care Centre, 36 licensed suites), then long-term care (KinVillage West Court, 100 beds). Sources: Section 2 above.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Tsawwassen's entire verified 55+ ownership supply is 65 units in two 1988–1991 buildings on adjoining Central streets** — 38 units at The Lexington (54A Street) and 27 at Tsawwassen Greene (55 Street). There is no newer age-restricted strata product anywhere in the community in any source checked, and no 55+ stock at all in the beach pockets buyers picture when they picture Tsawwassen. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The building across the street from a 55+ building is not 55+.** Heron Place (1153 54A St) faces The Lexington and expressly has no age restrictions — the 54A Street "seniors cluster" is a cluster of seniors services (KinVillage), not a street of age bylaws. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/heron-place-1153-54-a-street
- **KinVillage is mid-rebuild, and the phases matter.** North Court was demolished and replaced through The Tides (152 homes, opened per the November 2025 construction reporting in this series) and a Phase 2 proposal for 143 more affordable seniors rental units at 1050 54A Street is in the rezoning process — the campus a buyer visits today is not the campus of five years ago, and Phase 2 is a proposal, not available housing. https://canada.constructconnect.com/joc/news/projects/2025/11/seniors-find-new-homes-as-152-units-open-in-delta and https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921
- **"Adult oriented" and "gated" are marketing words, not bylaws — Tsawwassen has the textbook case.** Monterra's data sheet uses both words and records no age restriction; Tsawwassen RV Resort, the park a buyer is most likely to assume is a seniors park, is expressly all-ages. The registered bylaws / Form B, or the park's own listing, settle it — not the listing adjectives. Sources: Section 2, closing list.
- **SnapStats publishes no South Delta figures at all** — its September 2026 communities include North Delta but not Tsawwassen or Ladner — so there is no Tsawwassen median, sales ratio or trend to quote from that source; pricing support has to come from Paragon comparables. Checked against the SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report files in ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/stats/.
- **The seniors-services centre of Tsawwassen is a community centre, not a residence.** The KinVillage Community Centre at 5430 10th Ave is one of Delta's three seniors recreation facilities (operated by the Kinsmen Seniors Society), and KinVillage residents get a free membership — it is part of why the Central pocket works for aging in place, and it is not housing. https://www.delta.ca/city-hall/working-delta/volunteering/kinvillage-community-centre
- **Two of the community's three Bria / care addresses answer to "56 Street" but sit in different pockets** — The Waterford at 1345 56 Street in the town centre, The Wexford at 1737 56 Street at 18 Avenue by the South Delta Recreation Centre. A care search that treats "56 Street" as one location will misplace one of them. https://briacommunities.ca/communities/waterford/ and https://briacommunities.ca/communities/wexford/
