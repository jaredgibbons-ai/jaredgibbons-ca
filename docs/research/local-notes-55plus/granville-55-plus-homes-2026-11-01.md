# Granville (South Granville, Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- For this area, "Granville" means **South Granville** — the Granville Street corridor on Vancouver's west side from the Granville Bridge to West 16th Avenue — not the downtown Granville entertainment corridor. The City lists "South Granville" as a recognised smaller area; it is not one of the City's 22 distinct local areas, and the corridor runs through the Fairview local area, with Shaughnessy at its south end. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/fairview.aspx
- The South Granville Business Improvement Association district runs along Granville Street from the Granville Bridge to 16th Avenue, branded "Gallery Row" for its concentration of art galleries alongside fashion and home-décor shops; the residential side streets run east toward Oak Street and west toward Burrard Street. Exact board boundary roads are UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/fairview.aspx
- The corridor's landmark is the Stanley Theatre (the Arts Club's Stanley stage), 2750 Granville Street at West 12th Avenue — a 650-seat venue in an art deco building that opened in 1931. https://artsclub.com/about/venue-rentals/stanley-industrial-alliance-stage
- Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, operated by Vancouver Coastal Health, sits a few blocks east of the corridor and is BC's referral centre for many provincial programs as well as a teaching hospital. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital
- Schools: L'École Bilingue Elementary, 1166 West 14th Avenue, is the SD39 school listed under Fairview, the local area covering the corridor — a Kindergarten-to-Grade 7 single-track French Immersion school. Catchments are address-based; SD39 has no middle-school tier. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods and https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
- Transit today is buses, including the 99 B-Line along West Broadway; the Broadway Subway — the 5.7 km Millennium Line extension with six new underground stations including South Granville Station — is scheduled to enter service in fall 2027. Granville Park, 3001 Fir Street, is the corridor's main neighbourhood park. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025TT0110-001048 and https://covapp.vancouver.ca/ParkFinder/parkdetail.aspx?inparkid=44
- Where the seniors housing actually is: one block west of Granville Street, on West 12th and West 14th Avenues — Granville Gates supportive housing at 1450 West 12th Avenue and South Granville Park Lodge long-term care at 1645 West 14th Avenue, with the South Granville Seniors Centre (a centre, not housing) at 1420 West 12th Avenue between them. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**No verified 55+ strata ownership complex was found in the South Granville corridor.** The corridor's seniors supply is one long-term care home and one supportive-housing building — neither is a home anyone can buy, and neither is an open-market 55+ rental.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None verified in this area.

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- None verified in this area. No purpose-built market 55+ rental apartment building was found on the corridor in the sources checked; the corridor's apartment stock on Granville Street and West Broadway is general-market strata and rental.

### Retirement residences and care (rental/care — NOT strata ownership)

- **South Granville Park Lodge — 1645 West 14th Avenue, V6J 2J4.** Privately operated, family-owned long-term care home one street west of Granville Street. The operator states it has served seniors for more than 50 years and provides 24-hour registered nursing care, with intermediate care levels I–III, extended care, extended care plus, and convalescent and palliative options, in furnished private rooms. Care housing — admission is a care placement, not a purchase or an open-market rental. Operator rates are published on its accommodation page; they are care fees, change on assessment, and are not reproduced here. https://www.sgplodge.com/ and https://www.sgplodge.com/accommodation

### Subsidized seniors rental (non-profit, income-based — not ownership)

- **Granville Gates Supportive Housing — 1450 West 12th Avenue (the former Chalmers Lodge building).** Supportive housing operated by the non-profit Connective, providing housing for seniors and equity-denied populations including Indigenous people and women, with 24/7 staffing and support services including meal programs, homecare services and cultural supports; residents apply through BC Housing, and priority is given to seniors who are already BC Housing tenants wanting a more seniors-focused building. SOURCE CONFLICT, flagged: Connective describes a 114-unit program, while BC Housing's April 11, 2023 release describes the Province purchasing a 115-unit building at this address — the one-unit difference is unresolved in the sources. This is supportive housing for people exiting or at risk of homelessness, not a general seniors rental a buyer can simply apply to rent at market. http://connective.ca/services/1450-w12/ and https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/more-than-200-homes-coming-to-vancouver

### Checked and NOT 55+ (do not put these on a 55+ page)

- **Chalmers Lodge no longer exists as a seniors' residence at this address.** BC Housing records that the Chalmers Foundation previously operated a seniors' care facility at 1450 West 12th Avenue; the building was aging and near the end of its lifespan, it closed in 2022, and its residents were relocated to the Rideau Residence at 1850 Rosser Avenue in Burnaby under a 2022 Province / BC Housing partnership. Any directory still listing "Chalmers Lodge, 1450 West 12th" as current Vancouver seniors housing is out of date — the building is now Granville Gates (above). https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/more-than-200-homes-coming-to-vancouver and https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/province-to-finance-affordable-seniors-housing-in-burnaby
- **Granville Gardens Retirement Residence — Suite 100, 1550 West 49th Avenue — is not in this area.** Despite the Granville name, its address is at West 49th Avenue, far south of this corridor's West 16th Avenue end, on the Kerrisdale / Marpole stretch of Granville Street. It is a registered assisted-living residence (class SN, operator Verve Senior Living) — but for a different Granville page area, not South Granville. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=May5&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&f%5B0%5D=city%3AVancouver
- **Douglas Lodge (1507–1509 West 12th Avenue, also listed as 2799 Granville Street) and Crescent Court (1504–1512 West 14th Avenue, also listed as 3003–3007 Granville Street)** are heritage apartment buildings on the City's Heritage Register at the Granville Street corners. No source checked records any age restriction for either — they are general rental apartments, not 55+ buildings. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-vancouver-heritage-register.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **The Broadway Plan governs this corridor's future form.** The plan covers Clark Drive to Vine Street and 1st to 16th Avenues, was approved by Council on June 22, 2022 and updated in December 2024, and provides for a South Granville Station area with buildings up to 30–40 storeys near the station (a 40-storey maximum in the Granville/Burrard Slopes – Area A station-area policy). The plan is a policy framework — most higher-density forms still require rezoning, so plan heights are not as-of-right zoning. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx and https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-broadway.pdf
- **City of Vancouver Seniors Housing Strategy (approved July 23, 2024):** the City's first seniors housing strategy, with a Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy to enable more seniors housing across all neighbourhoods and a Resident Relocation Policy adding protections for seniors in community care and assisted living residences in the event of a redevelopment — the exact situation the Chalmers Lodge closure (Section 2) put its residents through in 2022, before this policy existed. https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/seniors-housing-strategy.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/seniors-housing-strategy-address-needs-of-older-adults-july-2024.aspx
- **Side-street zoning:** for detached-house lots off the corridor, By-law No. 13817 replaced the old RS districts with R1-1 — a house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 strata units or up to 8 secured rental units, subject to the district schedule. The Granville Street frontage itself sits under commercial / mixed-use districts; the exact schedule for a given frontage parcel is UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **Tax calendar:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. The Empty Homes Tax matters more here than in most areas because of the corridor's second-home and investor stock: an annual declaration is required, and the tax is 3% of assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in South Granville?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata building on the corridor; its seniors buildings are a long-term care home and a supportive-housing program. A 55+ buyer here is buying a regular Granville / Broadway condo or a side-street house with no age bylaw — the downsizer appeal is the shops, the hospital and the coming subway station, not an age restriction. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is this the downtown Granville Street strip?** No. This area is South Granville, from the Granville Bridge to West 16th Avenue, inside the Fairview local area — Gallery Row, the Stanley Theatre and house-and-apartment side streets. The downtown entertainment corridor is across False Creek and is a different market. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/fairview.aspx
- **Q: Can I still move into Chalmers Lodge on West 12th?** No. Chalmers Lodge closed in 2022; its residents were relocated to the Rideau Residence in Burnaby, the Province bought the building, and it now operates as Granville Gates supportive housing under Connective. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/more-than-200-homes-coming-to-vancouver
- **Q: Who can live at Granville Gates, 1450 West 12th?** People referred through BC Housing for supportive housing — the program is designed for seniors and equity-denied populations including Indigenous people and women, prioritises seniors already in BC Housing tenancies, and provides 24/7 staffing, meals and homecare supports. It is not an open-market rental and not a purchase. http://connective.ca/services/1450-w12/
- **Q: Can I buy a suite at South Granville Park Lodge?** No. It is a family-owned long-term care home at 1645 West 14th Avenue providing 24-hour registered nursing across intermediate, extended and convalescent levels of care. Admission is a care decision; there is nothing to buy. https://www.sgplodge.com/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Vancouver?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a corridor condo I buy?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022 — and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Purpose-built rental buildings on the corridor also carry tenant-relocation implications under the Broadway Plan's renter protections, which an investor should verify before purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Q: When does the subway actually reach South Granville?** On the Province's schedule, fall 2027: the Broadway Subway is a 5.7 km Millennium Line extension with six new underground stations including South Granville Station. Price and plan on that stated date, not on rumour. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025TT0110-001048
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue — a few blocks east of Granville Street, operated by Vancouver Coastal Health and BC's referral centre for many provincial programs. For a senior buyer, this corridor has the shortest hospital distance of the four areas in this batch. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital
- **Q: If I hold a corridor home vacant or as a second home, what does it cost me?** Budget for the Empty Homes Tax: an annual property status declaration is required, and homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year are taxed at 3% of assessed taxable value, on top of the ordinary tax calendar (advance taxes February 3, 2026; main taxes July 3, 2026). https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The corridor's seniors story is a closure, not a building.** Chalmers Lodge operated at 1450 West 12th Avenue for decades as non-profit seniors housing; BC Housing records it as aging and near the end of its lifespan, its residents were moved to Burnaby in 2022, and the Province then bought the building for about $54.6 million committed toward purchase and renovations and reopened it as supportive housing with seniors given priority in tenanting. A buyer researching "seniors housing in South Granville" will still find Chalmers Lodge in older directories — it is the same address as Granville Gates, under a new operator and a different program. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/more-than-200-homes-coming-to-vancouver and https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/province-to-finance-affordable-seniors-housing-in-burnaby
- **There is a seniors centre on the same block — but it is not housing.** The South Granville Seniors Centre at 1420 West 12th Avenue works with adults aged 55+ on well-being, friendship and programs. It sits doors from Granville Gates, which is why the West 12th block reads as a seniors district, but the centre itself has no homes. http://southgranvilleseniors.ca/
- **The one-unit disagreement is real and unresolved.** Connective, the operator, calls Granville Gates a 114-unit program; BC Housing's purchase release calls the building 115 units. Both are cited in Section 2 rather than averaged — anyone quoting a unit count for this building should say which source they are using. http://connective.ca/services/1450-w12/ and https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/more-than-200-homes-coming-to-vancouver
- **South Granville Park Lodge has outlasted most of the corridor's retail.** The operator states it has provided care for over 50 years from its West 14th Avenue site, one street west of Granville, in private rooms with 24-hour registered nursing — it is a care home embedded in a residential block, not an apartment building with services. https://www.sgplodge.com/
- **Plan policy is not zoning on Granville Street.** The Broadway Plan contemplates 30–40 storeys near the future South Granville Station, but the plan itself is a policy framework approved in 2022 and updated in December 2024; most higher-density forms still require a rezoning. Do not price a corridor lot as if tower density were already zoned. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-broadway.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-updates-new-homes-over-30-years-dec-2024.aspx
