# Kent (District of Kent) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Geography note — read first:** Kent is the municipality; Agassiz is the townsite inside it. Every seniors building in the District stands in the Agassiz townsite and is written up in full in the companion file (agassiz-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md). This file covers the District as a whole — Mount Woodside, Harrison Mills, Ruby Creek and the farm plain — and **cross-references the Agassiz buildings instead of counting them twice**. The Village of Harrison Hot Springs is a separate municipality carved out of Kent and has its own file; nothing in Harrison Hot Springs is counted here.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The District of Kent covers 19,374 hectares (the District's own stated total area) on the north side of the Fraser River, running from Harrison Mills in the west about 22 km northeast to Ruby Creek, taking in the Fraser Valley floodplain and the slopes around the lower end of Harrison Lake. (Statistics Canada's 2021 Census land area for the District is 168.59 km² — a different measurement basis, not interchangeable.) https://www.kentbc.ca/media/0enenmdy/doc_636900753607636141.pdf
- Communities inside Kent: the Agassiz townsite (the main centre), Harrison Mills, Ruby Creek, Mount Woodside, and rural farm areas. Highway 7 (Lougheed) runs the length of the District; Highway 9 connects Agassiz south over the Fraser to Chilliwack/Highway 1 and north to Harrison Hot Springs. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/0enenmdy/doc_636900753607636141.pdf and https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf
- Services concentrate in Agassiz: Municipal Hall at 7170 Cheam Avenue, the Community Recreation and Cultural Centre at 6660 Pioneer Avenue, the Agassiz Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library) at 7140 Cheam Avenue, and the Fraser Health Agassiz cluster on Pioneer and Cheam Avenues. Outside the townsite there is no comparable service node. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/agassiz-library and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/your-community/agassiz/services-in-agassiz
- Transit is two BC Transit routes administered by the FVRD: Route 71 (Chilliwack–Agassiz–Harrison Hot Springs) and Route 72 (Agassiz–Hope via Hwy 7), with Agassiz as the in-District hub. Rural Kent outside the townsite has no transit stop network to speak of — service is limited even on the routes that exist. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html
- The District also holds two federal institutions near Agassiz — Kent Institution (maximum security, opened 1979, rated capacity 378) and Mountain Institution (medium security, opened 1962, rated capacity 440) — a major local employer and a land use rural buyers near Agassiz should know is there. https://www.canada.ca/en/correctional-service/corporate/facilities-security/institutional-profiles/pacific/kent-institution.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/correctional-service/corporate/facilities-security/institutional-profiles/pacific/mountain-institution.html
- Where 55+ and seniors housing concentrates: entirely in the Agassiz townsite (MacKay Crescent / Morrow Road / McCaffrey Road side). No source places any 55+ complex, care home, subsidized seniors building or manufactured-home park at Mount Woodside, Harrison Mills or Ruby Creek. Sources: Section 2 below and the Agassiz companion file.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning: District of Kent Zoning Bylaw No. 1219, 2001 (as amended).** Its land-use designations under the OCP run Agricultural, Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Institutional, Parks/Recreation/Open Space and Resource Management. On ALR lots the bylaw's farm home plate rules apply: the farm residence, farm employee residence, home occupation and accessory residential facilities must sit within the farm home plate area (maximum 2,023 m² / 0.5 acre for a plate containing one farm residence), subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act. https://www.kpu.ca/isfs/foodpolicydatabase/district-kent-zoning-bylaw-no-1219-2001 and https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf
- **OCP: District of Kent Official Community Plan 2025** — directs most growth into the Agassiz townsite, with Mount Woodside as a satellite growth village, and makes agriculture a headline priority (Section 4.2). An OCP update process continued into early 2026. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/vpwaiuh5/2026-01-21-dok-2025-ocp-update.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Bylaw No. 1737, adopted June 10, 2024, created the RS1 zone allowing up to four dwelling units on single-dwelling lots in the RS1 zone. Note the District's own guide is explicit: there are no RS1 properties outside Agassiz and Harrison Highlands — rural Kent lots do not get the four-unit option. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/tdabxdtl/district-of-kent-ssmuh-information-guide-final.pdf
- **Secondary suites:** the District amended the Zoning Bylaw to permit secondary suites in single-family homes in all residential zones, and reduced the minimum residential lot size from 550 m² to 440 m² — both recorded in the OCP update as actions taken to reduce housing need. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf
- **ALR rules:** about 50% of the District's land area (excluding forest reserves, Crown land, roads and highways) is in the Agricultural Land Reserve per OCP §4.2; a 2013 provincial Agricultural Land Use Inventory measured 6,502 ha of ALR in Kent on a different basis — the two figures use different denominators and are not interchangeable. ALC housing rules apply on top of Kent zoning on ALR land. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Flood/diking:** Flood Protection & Erosion is OCP Section 5.5. The District's flat lands are Fraser/Harrison floodplain behind dikes; the District maintains 135 km of drainage ditches, and diking is a separate assessment-based rate line on the tax notice (2026 Class 1 Residential: 0.03076 per $1,000 of taxable value). Mount Woodside is the exception area — slope and geotechnical hazards there are covered by OCP Sections 5.2–5.3 instead. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/0enenmdy/doc_636900753607636141.pdf and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/mxrbvfky/tax-rates-summary-2026.pdf
- **Taxes:** District of Kent municipal taxes, notices mailed in early June, due July 15 (or the next business day), rates set before May 15 each year. Kent properties are inside a municipality — neither the City of Mission penalty (flat 10% after Jul 2, Mission only) nor the FVRD rural-area penalty regime (5% after Jul 2 + 5% after Oct 31, for unincorporated electoral-area properties) applies to a Kent tax bill. https://www.kentbc.ca/living-here/home-and-property/property-taxes/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is "Kent" even a place I can search for homes?** As a municipality, yes; as a board area name, no. "Kent" appears on no board list — the board (CADREB) name for this land is Agassiz, and the MLS® taxonomy carries the District under the Agassiz area. Searches, schools and daily life say Agassiz; taxes, bylaws, permits and the OCP say District of Kent. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md, Section 1.
- **Q: Is there a 55+ place to buy anywhere in the District?** No verified one — zero 55+ strata complexes were verified anywhere in Kent. The District's seniors stock is rental, care and subsidized housing, all in Agassiz (see the Agassiz file). A buyer who must own in a 55+ building is looking at Chilliwack or Abbotsford product, not Kent. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes, where a valid 55+ bylaw exists — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a strata unit in Kent?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. If the building has a valid 55+ bylaw, the tenant still has to satisfy it or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I put a suite or a second small house on a rural Kent property for my parents?** It depends which regime the land sits in. In residential zones, secondary suites are permitted in single-family homes in all residential zones. The four-unit SSMUH option exists only in the RS1 zone, and the District confirms there are no RS1 properties outside Agassiz and Harrison Highlands. On ALR land, farm home plate rules and ALC housing rules apply on top of zoning. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/tdabxdtl/district-of-kent-ssmuh-information-guide-final.pdf and https://www.kpu.ca/isfs/foodpolicydatabase/district-kent-zoning-bylaw-no-1219-2001
- **Q: Is Mount Woodside a seniors area?** No. It is the District's designated newer growth area — a mix of single-family, semi-detached and townhouse housing (e.g., Harrison Highlands) with no age restriction verified for any complex there. Its practical senior issue is different: slopes, stairs and geotechnical policy (OCP §§5.2–5.3), versus the flat, walkable townsite. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf
- **Q: Is Kent flood-prone?** The flat lands are — Fraser/Harrison River floodplain behind dikes, with a dedicated OCP flood section (§5.5), 135 km of District-maintained drainage ditches, and diking charged as its own line on the tax notice. Verify dike status, drainage and flood construction requirements for the specific parcel. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/0enenmdy/doc_636900753607636141.pdf and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/mxrbvfky/tax-rates-summary-2026.pdf
- **Q: Where would we actually go for care or a hospital?** In-District care is in Agassiz: assisted living and long-term care at the two Park Place campuses, plus the Fraser Health Agassiz community-health cluster. There is no hospital in the District — Fraser Health lists community and public-health services in Agassiz only. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/your-community/agassiz/services-in-agassiz and https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/find-a-location/british-columbia/lower-mainland/glenwood/
- **Q: Is Harrison Hot Springs part of Kent for housing purposes?** No — it is a separate village municipality carved out of Kent, with its own OCP, zoning, taxes and (proposed) seniors housing project. Do not carry Harrison buildings or Harrison rules onto a Kent page, or the reverse. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/0enenmdy/doc_636900753607636141.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The District's recreation centrepiece just opened in Agassiz.** The Lets'emot Regional Recreation & Aquatic Centre — a 27,000 sq ft expansion attached to the Community Recreation and Cultural Centre at 6660 Pioneer Ave, with an 8-lane lap pool, leisure pool with lazy river, whirlpool, steam room and sauna — opened to the public September 25, 2026, replacing the roughly 50-year-old seasonal Ferny Coombe Pool. https://www.kentbc.ca/news/posts/september-9-2026-council-highlights/ and https://www.kentbc.ca/news/posts/press-release-2025-projects-funded-by-44m-from-canada-community-building-fund/
- **The District has quantified its housing gap.** Kent's Interim Housing Needs Report (2024), as presented in the OCP update, found 495 new homes needed immediately and 1,695 more by 2041 — against a 2021 Census base of about 6,300 residents and 2,518 total private dwellings (2,351 occupied). https://forms.kentbc.ca/_Print/Index/6a0b39a0-ebb5-4763-89dd-b3690173f2af?PDF=True and https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/dp-pd/prof/details/page.cfm?Lang=E&DGUIDlist=2021A00055909032
- **Two of the District's biggest land uses are prisons and research farms.** Kent Institution (the only maximum-security facility in CSC's Pacific Region) and Mountain Institution sit on shared Federal Reserve land near Agassiz, and the OCP also names Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada's Agassiz Research and Development Centre and UBC's Dairy Education and Research Centre as major floodplain land uses. https://www.canada.ca/en/correctional-service/corporate/facilities-security/institutional-profiles/pacific/kent-institution.html and https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf
- **The province paid to upgrade the pump station that protects the west side.** A $4.17 million provincially funded expansion and upgrade of the Hammersley Pump Station, about 6 km west of Agassiz, conveys Mountain Slough watershed runoff and protects agricultural lands and the nearby federal correctional infrastructure. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2017TRAN0139-000928
- **"Lets'emot Regional Park" does not exist as an FVRD park.** No FVRD source for a park by that name was found in this program — "Lets'emot" is the District's new Regional Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Agassiz. Do not put a Lets'emot park on a Kent page. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md, Section 4.
- **There are no board statistics for Kent.** SnapStats' Fraser Valley report has no Kent or Agassiz row, and CADREB publishes board-wide statistics only — any "Kent market median" is UNVERIFIED and must not be printed. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md, Section 2.
