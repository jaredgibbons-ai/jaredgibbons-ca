## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500, Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1)** are Seafair's documented zones, with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m² and a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones; Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL also apply in the bylaw as amended. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Suites — the actual ageing-in-place mechanism here:** a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in RS1/RS2 — Section 5.4 states a suite minimum of 33.0 m² and a maximum of the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m² — and the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. A suite is how a caregiver or family member lives on a Seafair lot. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Plan: the City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000,** covers Seafair, with Schedule 2 area and sub-area plans addressing neighbourhood issues; a Seafair-specific area plan name or approved major project: UNVERIFIED. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **Flood — Richmond is a dike-protected island city:** Richmond is a low-lying island city in the Fraser River floodplain, protected by a perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations including the west dike beside Seafair — 49 km of dikes and 39 pump stations city-wide, designed for a 1:500 flood event, with dikes being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. The Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements, and all lands in Richmond are designated flood plain under it. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **Property taxes and utilities:** City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. Richmond's 2026 typical single-family metered utility total is $2,251 per year, including a $342 flood protection line. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm and https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/finance/utility-fees/utilityrates.htm
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** (Zone 3: Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025). https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Seafair?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata building inside Seafair's boundaries; the strata complexes that do exist here (Apple Greene/Boyd Park on No. 1 Road, Edgewater Park on Williams Road) carry no age restriction in any source checked. A 55+ buyer in Seafair is buying a regular house, townhome or condo with no age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Covenant Court on No. 2 Road a Seafair 55+ building?** It is a real seniors complex, but it is not in Seafair — 9260 No. 2 Road is east of Seafair's Railway Avenue boundary, in the Woodwards neighbourhood on building sources — and no source checked states its exact age-restriction figure, so "55+" for that building is UNCONFIRMED until the strata documents are read. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r2898499-1089260-no-2-road-richmond-bc-v7e-2c8
- **Q: How do seniors actually age in place in Seafair?** In their own houses, using the suite rules: RS1/RS2 lots allow a secondary suite as a secondary use (33.0 m² minimum; maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling or 110 m²), and the City allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot on covered lots — space for a caregiver or family member on the same lot. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If we buy into a 55+ strata elsewhere in Richmond, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Seafair in a floodplain?** All of Richmond is designated flood plain under Bylaw 8204, behind a 49 km dike system designed for a 1:500 event with dike raising from 3.5 m to 4.7 m under way — and Seafair's west edge is the dike itself. Habitable floors must meet the lot's flood construction level unless exempt, which is a real cost item on any rebuild. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **Q: What transit and recreation can I use day to day?** Route 401 on No. 1 Road connects Seafair to Richmond–Brighouse Station and Steveston; Hugh Boyd Community Park and West Richmond Community Centre (fitness centre, gymnasium, squash, badminton) are inside the area, and the West Dyke Trail runs the area's full west edge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt401.pdf and https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=8
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Seafair?** No care home, assisted-living residence or retirement residence with a Seafair address was verified. Richmond's registered assisted-living residences are in Steveston, Hamilton and the City Centre, and the west-side long-term care name is Rosewood Manor, 6260 Blundell Rd, south of the area. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=city%3ARichmond&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc
