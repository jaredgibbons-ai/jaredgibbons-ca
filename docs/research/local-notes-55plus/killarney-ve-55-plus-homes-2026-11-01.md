## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** Since Nov 24, 2022 no strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw; stratas may still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Leasehold wrinkle specific to Champlain Heights:** the majority of the Champlain Heights area is leasehold property — pre-paid 99-year leases from the City of Vancouver expiring in the 2080s — not freehold. A buyer comparing a Champlain Heights townhouse to a freehold Killarney house is comparing different tenure, financing and resale mechanics, whatever the age profile of either complex. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champlain_Heights
- **City zoning: Vancouver Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575.** The house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex, 464 m² for a 5-unit multiple dwelling, 557 m² for 6 to 8 units; a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. East Fraser Lands parcels are under CD-1 districts; parcel-level zone is UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plan: the East Fraser Lands Official Development Plan (River District)** applies at the south edge — a mainly residential community with a variety of housing types and a commercial centre on the former Canadian White Pine Sawmill site, with Wesgroup Properties owning the majority of the land. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/east-fraser-lands-river-district-documents-and-policies.aspx
- **Flood:** the City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain along the Fraser River in South Vancouver, and parcels in it are subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for setbacks and flood construction levels — directly relevant to Fraserlands / River District purchases. A specific parcel's status: check the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Vancouver tax calendar:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; owners must also file the annual Empty Homes Tax property status declaration (the 2025 tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% late penalty). https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Killarney VE?** None that any source verifies. No age-restricted strata ownership building was found in Killarney, Champlain Heights or the River District. The purchasable stock is freehold houses on the R1-1 streets, leasehold Champlain Heights townhomes and co-ops, and general-market River District strata — none of it age-restricted. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/killarney.aspx and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: What seniors housing does exist in Killarney?** One verified facility: Sunrise of Killarney, 6325 Clarendon St — a private long-term care and memory care home (43 long-term care suites, 52 memory care suites, up to 105 residents) that opened in September 2024. It is care housing, not independent living and not a purchase. https://Www.newswire.ca/news-releases/sunrise-senior-living-expands-canadian-presence-with-the-grand-opening-of-sunrise-of-killarney-846096322.html
- **Q: Is there independent or assisted living in Killarney for someone not ready for long-term care?** None verified. BC's Assisted Living Registry and BC Housing's Zone 6 directory show no independent-living retirement residence or subsidized seniors building with a Killarney address; the area's seniors infrastructure for independent seniors is the Killarney Seniors Centre's programs at 6260 Killarney Street. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://www.canada.ca/en/housing-infrastructure-communities/news/2017/01/ground-breaking-new-killarney-seniors-centre-southeast-vancouver.html
- **Q: Are the River District towers a 55+ community in practice?** No source supports that. They are general-market Wesgroup and Polygon buildings built under the East Fraser Lands Official Development Plan, with no age restriction recorded for any tower — a downsizer buying there is buying a regular strata lot under regular bylaws. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/east-fraser-lands-river-district-documents-and-policies.aspx
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a BC 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What is different about buying in Champlain Heights?** Tenure: the majority of Champlain Heights is pre-paid 99-year leasehold from the City of Vancouver, expiring in the 2080s — not freehold. That affects financing, remaining lease value and resale, and it applies to the co-op and townhouse stock a senior might otherwise treat as the area's "affordable" option. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champlain_Heights
- **Q: Is the Fraser River floodplain a factor here?** Yes at the river edge: the designated floodplain covers the Fraser River frontage, and River District parcels in it carry Vancouver Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements. The established house streets sit inland; check the specific parcel in the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** Buses only — there is no SkyTrain station in Killarney. Route 49 runs 49th Avenue between Metrotown Station and UBC, route 26 runs to Joyce Station, and route 100 runs Marine Drive to 22nd Street Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt049.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Killarney's care home is brand new and it skipped the middle rung:** Sunrise of Killarney opened September 10, 2024 as the area's first verified seniors facility — and it is long-term care and memory care only. The local ladder has a top rung (105-resident care home) and a bottom rung (seniors centre programs), with no verified independent or assisted living in between. https://Www.newswire.ca/news-releases/sunrise-senior-living-expands-canadian-presence-with-the-grand-opening-of-sunrise-of-killarney-846096322.html
- **The "Killarney" care home sits on Clarendon Street, at the area's western edge:** the operator's Killarney name is the placement evidence for this file, but a buyer checking City local-area maps should know the address (6325 Clarendon St) is on the Victoria-Fraserview side of the neighbourhood seam — the exact boundary at this address is UNVERIFIED. https://www.sunriseseniorliving.ca/communities/bc/sunrise-of-killarney
- **The Seniors Centre was a three-government build:** the Killarney Seniors Centre's $7.5 million cost was shared, with the Government of Canada contributing up to $2.5 million through the Building Canada Fund — it was purpose-built because, per the 2017 announcement, southeast Vancouver's "growing senior population" needed programs and support services the area lacked. https://www.canada.ca/en/housing-infrastructure-communities/news/2017/01/ground-breaking-new-killarney-seniors-centre-southeast-vancouver.html
- **Champlain Heights was one of Vancouver's last greenfield neighbourhoods:** it was planned in the early 1970s and developed in the early 1980s on a then-undeveloped 614-acre tract, as a showcase community mixing housing types and income levels around Everett Crowley Park — itself a former city landfill, now the city's fifth-largest park at 40 hectares. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champlain_Heights
- **Killarney's history sets its housing age:** the City records that Killarney remained second-growth forest and farms until after WWII, and its first non-native settler, William Rowling (1868), came to own 3.5 km of Fraser River shoreline including all of Killarney's riverfront — the reason the established stock is post-war houses and the only new product is the River District on the old sawmill lands. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/killarney.aspx
- **The Empty Homes Tax declaration is an annual chore every Vancouver owner carries:** a Killarney owner who leaves a property vacant between homes, or holds it as a second property, must still file the yearly property status declaration — the 2025 payment deadline was April 16, 2026 with a 5% late penalty. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
