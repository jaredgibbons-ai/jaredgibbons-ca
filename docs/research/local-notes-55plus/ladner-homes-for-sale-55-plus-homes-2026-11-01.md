# Ladner — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. This is the community-wide Ladner file (City of Delta) — it consolidates the Ladner sub-area files in this series (Neilsen Grove, Delta Manor, Hawthorne, Holly, Port Guichon, Ladner Elementary, Ladner Rural / East Delta).

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Ladner is one of Delta's three communities (Ladner, Tsawwassen, North Delta; Ladner + Tsawwassen = South Delta), centred on the historic village core at 48 Avenue and Delta Street on the South Arm of the Fraser River. Its Paragon sub-areas are Ladner Elementary, Hawthorne, Holly, Delta Manor, Port Guichon, Neilsen Grove (area spelling; the school is Neilson Grove Elementary) and Ladner Rural / East Delta. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-homes-for-sale.html
- Governing plan: Delta's Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future," adopted July 8, 2024) — a single consolidated plan with NO lettered area-plan schedules; the Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 of the PREVIOUS OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, 1985, and farm land in Bylaw 8400 is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s.1.5.1). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Delta's zoning bylaw is Zoning Bylaw No. 7600; parcel zones are read off the City's bylaw maps (an RS1 map is cited in the Hawthorne file in this series). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- Schools (SD37 Delta, confirmed against the BC Ministry K-12 file by name and address): Ladner Elementary, 5016 44 Ave; Hawthorne Elementary, 5160 Central Ave; Holly Elementary, 4625 62 St; Neilson Grove Elementary, 5500 Admiral Blvd; Port Guichon Elementary, 4381 46a St; Delta Secondary, 4615 51 St. Catchment for a specific address is UNVERIFIED until checked on SD37's catchment maps — this program holds no Ladner locator runs. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/student-registration/catchment-area-maps/
- Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, is in Ladner, beside Ladner Exchange — Fraser Health's community hospital for Delta, with 24/7 emergency care. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- Transit: TransLink route 601 runs from South Delta via Ladner Exchange to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line); route 618 is the Ladner North loop from Ladner Exchange; route 609 runs Ladner Exchange to South Delta Exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt618.pdf
- Flood: Ladner sits on the Fraser River delta floodplain; the City of Delta is the diking authority and operates over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure. A specific lot's Flood Construction Level is UNVERIFIED — it requires a City check for that lot. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: the village core and its edges — 51 Street (Delta Green), the Laburnum Park / 52A Street area (Laburnum Park Place), Neilsen Grove off Ferry Road (South Pointe), the Evergreen Lane subsidized cluster and Augustine House on Arthur Drive. Hawthorne, Holly, Port Guichon and Ladner Rural / East Delta each have ZERO verified 55+ ownership stock in this series. Sources: Section 2 below and the sub-area files in this folder.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. A freehold detached house has no strata and therefore no strata age bylaw at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. Where an older Ladner building summary says "rentals not allowed," read it against this rule and confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Bare-land strata is still strata.** South Pointe and Laburnum Park Place are bare-land stratas: the buyer owns the lot and the house on it and pays strata fees for the common property — the 55+ bylaw works the same way as in a condo building, through the registered bylaws. Tenure for a specific lot is proved on title and the strata plan. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Delta plan and zoning:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, no lettered schedules; Zoning Bylaw No. 7600. Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" in the OCP (s.1.5.1), and ALR housing rules cap farm dwellings — one principal residence (500 m² cap without an application) plus, in some cases, one additional detached residence up to 90 m² (parcels 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (larger parcels). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Manufactured-home parks:** no manufactured-home park inside the City of Delta was verified from any source in this series — if one exists, its name, address and age rule are UNCONFIRMED here. Where a BC park pad tenancy does apply, the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act pad-rent increase limit for 2026 is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees, once every 12 months on three full months' notice. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Delta property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Subsidized seniors housing in Delta runs through BC Housing and the City's Affordable Housing Guide** — the Evergreen Lane and Marina Park buildings above are applied for as the guide directs, not bought. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many 55+ homes can I actually buy in Ladner?** Three verified ownership complexes in the whole community: Delta Green (24 strata condos, built 2021), Laburnum Park Place (bare-land strata ranchers) and South Pointe (gated bare-land strata, built 1989). Everything else age-restricted in Ladner is rental, care or subsidized. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a Ladner 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); caregivers, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in Delta Green or South Pointe for a parent, or to hold?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside, not who may own. A 55+ parent can be the resident of a unit owned by a younger buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ Ladner building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata rancher the same as owning a house?** Close, but not identical: you own the lot and the home, the strata owns and maintains the common property (roads, gate, landscaping in common areas), you pay a strata fee, and the strata's bylaws — including the 55+ bylaw — bind the lot. South Pointe's listing-stated fee was $113/mo at its listing date; confirm the current fee and what it covers on the Form B. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/5379-regatta-way
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or a buyer who will not live in the unit. That trade-off is built into every BC 55+ strata since the 2022 law confined age bylaws to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and what seniors services are in the village?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, is in Ladner itself, beside Ladner Exchange. The McKee Seniors Recreation Centre is at 5155 47 Avenue; its society's newsletter describes City of Delta seniors centres as serving people aged 50+, and the Delta Seniors Bus as a free curbside service within Delta for residents 65+. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital and https://www.mckeesociety.com/s/McKee-August-2026-Newsletter.pdf
- **Q: Is Ladner in a floodplain?** Yes — it is dike-protected Fraser River floodplain. The City maintains over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure and 31 drainage pump stations; the Flood Construction Level for a specific lot is parcel-specific and UNVERIFIED until checked with the City for that lot. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the 55+ complexes?** Building by building. A South Pointe listing records pets allowed; Delta Green's and Laburnum's pet rules are UNCONFIRMED from the sources in this series — read the registered bylaws and the Form B for the specific complex. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3075824-5371-regatta-way-delta-bc-v4k-4r9
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Ladner?** The in-community ladder is: independent strata living (the three complexes above), non-profit retirement / assisted living rental (Augustine House, Arthur Drive), subsidized independent rental (Evergreen Court, Evergreen Plaza, Marina Park), then long-term care (Delta View in East Delta, 292 beds). Augustine House's assisted living program is accessed through a Fraser Health case manager. Sources: Section 2 above.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Ladner's 55+ map is drawn by listing sub-areas, not by the village as a whole.** The three verified ownership communities each sit in a different Paragon sub-area — Delta Green in Ladner Elementary, Laburnum Park Place in Delta Manor, South Pointe in Neilsen Grove — and Hawthorne, Holly and Port Guichon, between and around them, have none. A search filtered to one sub-area will miss stock a few blocks away. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The newest 55+ ownership product in Ladner is also its only condo-form product.** Delta Green (built 2021, 24 adaptable units) sits beside the Ladner Lawn Bowling Club, Ladner Pioneer Library (4683 51st Street) and the McKee Seniors Centre — its developer markets that seniors-services cluster, not the building alone, as the product. The other two complexes are 1980s bare-land strata ranchers. https://en.youlive.ca/delta-building/13200-delta-green
- **The "55+" field in listing data can be an empty box, not a bylaw.** River West's aggregator page shows an "Age Restricted (55+)" field with no value in it, while its current listings record no age bylaw at all — the same trap as a gated, adult-oriented marketing line (Monterra in Tsawwassen is the twin case). The registered bylaws and Form B are the only proof. https://www.estateblock.com/ladner/202-4743-w-river-rd-52494
- **Delta Green's civic address is recorded two ways in this series** — 4686 51 Street in its listing and City hearing sources, 4674–4684 51 Street in one sub-area file. Small, but it is exactly the kind of detail to settle from the strata plan before a search alert is built on the address. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **There is no manufactured-home park product in Delta in this research.** Unlike Surrey and Langley, Delta produced no verified park — 55+ or all-ages — from any source checked, so a Ladner buyer who wants the own-the-home / rent-the-pad form has to leave the municipality. UNCONFIRMED whether any Delta park exists at all. Source: the East Delta file in this series.
- **SnapStats publishes no South Delta figures at all** — its September 2026 communities include North Delta but not Ladner or Tsawwassen — so there is no Ladner median, sales ratio or trend to quote from that source; pricing support for a Ladner 55+ purchase has to come from Paragon comparables.
