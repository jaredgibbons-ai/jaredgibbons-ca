## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017.** The Beach Grove waterfront pocket is zoned RD2 (Duplex/Single Detached Residential 2) at the confirmed sample, 1728 Beach Grove Road — a zone the bylaw states is for waterfront lots in Beach Grove, with a 4,000 m² minimum lot size for subdivision. Section 6.2.3 sets a waterfront setback for lots fronting Boundary Bay along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue, under which a seawall may encroach into the minimum front setback, and the bylaw states no Beach Grove flood construction level. Zoning is otherwise parcel by parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")** is Delta's current consolidated OCP — it has no lettered area-plan schedules. Its Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands) provides: no building with a habitable floor below 2.9 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum, a minimum land surface elevation around construction of 2.3 metres GSC datum, and no building within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. Whether a specific Beach Grove lot falls inside SD3 is a parcel-level check — UNCONFIRMED for any individual lot here. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **"Seniors congregate housing" in Delta's zoning:** the Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone added to Bylaw No. 7600 lists seniors congregate housing as a principal use alongside apartment buildings, in six-storey development under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation — the mechanism by which a future Beach Grove-area seniors building would most likely be zoned, since the current pocket has none. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing:** Delta's SSMUH rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, and the City supports accessory dwelling units such as coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites, subject to the bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Foreshore tenure:** the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; private moorage is held by lease or licence via FrontCounter BC under General Permission or Specific Permission — a Boundary Bay dock is tenure, not ownership. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **Property taxes and 55+ relief:** Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. The provincial regular property tax deferment loan program covers homeowners 55 and older, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) covers seniors 65+. No FHSA or Home Buyers' Plan dollar limit is stated in this file — UNVERIFIED, not read off a live CRA page in this research. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Subsidized seniors rental applications** for Delta run through BC Housing's Housing Registry (Zone 3 — Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025). https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo or townhome I can buy in Beach Grove?** None verified. The Pillars (1700 56 St) expressly has no age restrictions in its building source, and no source states a 55+ bylaw for Heron Cove (1706 56 St) or Tsawwassen Mews (5622 16 Ave). The verified buyable 55+ buildings in Tsawwassen are in Central: The Lexington and Tsawwassen Greene. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/pillars-1700-56-street and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — does that matter for a Beach Grove purchase?** Not in the buildings checked here, because none carries an age bylaw. Where a 55+ bylaw does exist (Central's buildings), provincial law exempts a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident, plus defined caregiver and child cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent instead?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Beach Grove condo long-term?** Strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, so a long-term rental ban cannot be enforced — even where an older building summary for Heron Cove still says rentals are not allowed. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals, and the building's other bylaws still apply — read the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/heron-cove-1704-56-street
- **Q: What flood rules apply to a Beach Grove waterfront home?** Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 sets a 2.9 m GSC datum minimum habitable floor level, a 2.3 m minimum land elevation around construction, and a 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of a tidal area; the shore also sits behind the Boundary Bay dike, which the City of Delta maintains as diking authority. Whether a specific lot is inside SD3 needs a lot-level (DeltaMap) check. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Is the dock or moorage in front of a waterfront home part of the purchase?** The foreshore is provincial Crown land; private moorage is held under General Permission or Specific Permission (or a lease/licence) via FrontCounter BC, not owned with the upland lot. Verify the tenure paperwork, not just the physical dock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **Q: What is The Wexford, and can I buy a suite there?** No. The Wexford, 1737 56 St at 18 Avenue, is a Bria Communities rental retirement residence with 65 all-inclusive independent living suites; you rent the suite and the services, you do not buy strata title. https://briacommunities.ca/communities/wexford/
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd in Ladner (Fraser Health), is the nearest hospital; Fraser Health lists a South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 1826–4949 Canoe Pass Way in Tsawwassen. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **Q: What bus serves Beach Grove and Boundary Bay?** TransLink route 601 runs Boundary Bay trips via 12 Avenue, Boundary Bay Road, 3 Avenue and 67 Street to 1A Avenue, continuing via South Delta Exchange and Ladner Exchange to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line). https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- **Q: Does a 55+ buyer get any tax break on a Beach Grove house?** The provincial tools are age-based, not area-based: property tax deferment as a low-interest loan from age 55, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in this region) from age 65, both for a principal residence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Beach Grove's waterfront zoning is a named zone, not standard urban zoning.** RD2 exists, in the bylaw's own words, for waterfront lots in Beach Grove, with a 4,000 m² subdivision minimum — the reason the pocket stayed detached houses while the 56 Street corridor got the condo buildings. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf
- **There is a specific Beach Grove setback rule in the zoning bylaw.** Section 6.2.3 of Bylaw No. 7600 addresses lots fronting Boundary Bay along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue and lets a seawall encroach into the minimum front setback; the bylaw states no Beach Grove flood construction level, which is why the SD3 permit-area numbers (2.9 m / 2.3 m GSC datum) are the ones to check instead. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **The two "Heron" buildings are different buildings in different sub-areas, and neither is 55+.** Heron Cove (1706 56 St, Beach Grove) has no verified age bylaw; Heron Place (1153 54A St, Central) has an express "no age restrictions" statement in its building source — and it is Heron Place, not Heron Cove, that sits across from the 55+ Lexington. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/heron-place-1153-54-a-street
- **An elementary school sits in the pocket, but that is not a catchment promise.** Beach Grove Elementary is at 5955 17a Ave, yet SD37 catchments are address-based and this program's only south-Tsawwassen locator sample (570 Boundary Bay Rd) returned South Park Elementary, not Beach Grove Elementary. Run the exact address. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **No published market statistics cover this pocket.** SnapStats publishes no South Delta figures — its Delta coverage is North Delta only — so no Beach Grove median, inventory or sales figure can be stated from the sources used here, and North Delta figures must never be presented as Beach Grove figures. (Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, as recorded in the Beach Grove area research file.)
- **Delta's flood system is city-scale:** over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure maintained by the City — the dike in front of Beach Grove is one piece of that system, and lot-level SD3 status still has to be confirmed per parcel. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
