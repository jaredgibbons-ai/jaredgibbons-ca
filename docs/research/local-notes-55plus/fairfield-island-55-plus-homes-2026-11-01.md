## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. This is why the 40+ and 45+ numbers on the park list in Section 2 cannot be read as strata-style age bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **On Fairfield Island the rules that actually bind a 55+ buyer are the farm and house rules, not strata rules** — the purchase is freehold land. Properties in the Agricultural Land Reserve are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALR regulations, ALC approval may be required before the City can approve related development applications, and an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, for placing fill or removing soil, for non-farm uses, and for subdivision or a boundary adjustment; the City directs owners to check ALR status on the City Webmap. Whether any specific Fairfield Island parcel is in the ALR is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED for any individual property. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Farm Home Plate:** the OCP includes Development Permit Area 4 – Farm Home Plate, to reduce the long-term impact of residential uses on agricultural land, and a Development Permit is required when the Farm Home Plate regulations in the Zoning Bylaw are not met. Which Schedule D permit areas apply to a given Fairfield Island parcel is a per-parcel check: UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000.** Its suite and ADU figures are confirmed in this program's rules verification: a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling, one accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot, garden suites are capped at 100 m², and the bylaw's parking rules sit at s. 5.04. Application to any specific parcel remains a per-parcel check. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 amended Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 and was adopted June 18, 2024 — the City's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **City plan: Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500**, including Schedule B2, the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan, and the Schedule D development permit areas (D-1 Drinking Water Supply, D-2 Hillside Environment, D-3 Riparian Area, D-4 Farm Home Plate, D-5 Hillside Development, D-6 Downtown, D-7 Mixed-Use Areas, D-8 Residential, D-9 Commercial, D-10 Industrial). https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Flood:** flood facts for Fairfield Island are UNVERIFIED in the research file — the City flood sources that would settle dike, flood construction level and setback positions could not be opened in that pass, so no flood claim is relied on here; a buyer must confirm the parcel's flood position directly with the City. The geography that makes the check necessary is sourced: the neighbourhood sits north of the Hope Slough and is bounded by ALR lands, per the Schedule B-2 plan. https://jaredgibbons.ca/fairfield-island.html and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=5211
- **Property taxes:** City of Chilliwack property tax notices are mailed annually at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% late payment penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing in Fairfield Island itself?** No — none was verified in any source. The neighbourhood is detached houses and ALR-edge acreages; Chilliwack's five verified 55+ strata complexes and its one verified 55+ park are all in the urban core or on the Sardis side. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Where is the closest 55+ home I can actually buy?** Across the Hope Slough in the urban core: the Spadina Avenue pair downtown — Ivy Green (45775 Spadina Ave, 24 condos, built 1994, no pets) and Spadina Regency (45765 Spadina Ave, built 1989, rentals and pets permitted per listings) — carry a board label of "Chilliwack Downtown," so a search set to Fairfield Island will not surface them. https://www.admiraal.ca/buildings/view/27410 and https://www.robbiejohal.com/listing/r3101299-45765-spadina-avenue-501-chilliwack-bc-v2p-1t5/brochure
- **Q: I'm 55+ and buying a house on Fairfield Island. Can a caregiver or family member live in a second dwelling?** That is an ALR and zoning question, not a strata exemption question, because the purchase is freehold. Chilliwack's Zoning Bylaw permits one accessory dwelling unit per lot where the zone permits it (secondary suite capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling; garden suite capped at 100 m²), and on ALR land an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite — check the parcel's ALR status on the City Webmap first. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Does that matter if we buy into a Chilliwack 55+ strata instead?** In a strata, no — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. On a freehold Fairfield Island house there is no age bylaw at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata in Chilliwack?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. That is the current-law reading behind Spadina Regency's listing line "rentals and pets are permitted," and it overrides any older "rentals not allowed" directory line. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Why are there no market numbers for Fairfield Island?** Chilliwack is a CADREB market that SnapStats does not cover, and CADREB publishes statistics board-wide only with no sub-area tables — so any Fairfield Island statistic is UNVERIFIED here rather than borrowed from a neighbouring board. https://www.cadreb.com/market/
- **Q: Is Fairfield Island flood-safe?** UNVERIFIED in this research — the City flood sources could not be opened in the research pass, so no dike or flood construction level claim is made. The Schedule B-2 plan does establish the setting: the neighbourhood is separated from Chilliwack Proper by the Hope Slough and reached by bridges at Menzies and Young Roads. Confirm the specific parcel with the City before writing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=5211 and https://jaredgibbons.ca/fairfield-island.html
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and what if I need care later?** Chilliwack General Hospital is at 45600 Menholm Rd in the urban core. The in-city care ladder sits there too — the Chartwell residences on Patten Avenue, Hodgins Avenue and Corbould Street and The Cascades on McIntosh Drive are all registered assisted-living / retirement addresses in the sibling files; none is on Fairfield Island. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital
- **Q: When are Chilliwack property taxes due, and what is the penalty?** Notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1 each year; for 2026, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Q: Which schools serve Fairfield Island?** The live page names Strathcona Elementary (46375 Strathcona Rd), Little Mountain Elementary (9900 Carleton St), A D Rundle Middle School (45660 Hocking Ave) and Chilliwack Secondary (46363 Yale Rd), with names and addresses confirmed against the BC Ministry K-12 file. Catchments are address-based and no catchment for a Fairfield Island address is confirmed in this file — UNVERIFIED. https://jaredgibbons.ca/fairfield-island.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The island is a slough, not the Fraser River, that makes it an island in daily life.** The Schedule B-2 plan states Fairfield Island is separated from the rest of Chilliwack Proper by the Hope Slough, is currently accessible only via road bridges at Menzies and Young Roads, and will add a planned pedestrian and cyclist bridge at Williams Street — every trip to the hospital, the care cluster and the downtown 55+ buildings crosses one of those two bridges. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=5211
- **The plan expects infill, not a seniors project.** The Schedule B-2 framework addresses infill development opportunities — redevelopment of existing large lots into smaller-lot single detached, duplex or row housing, some convenience commercial, and new attached housing along Hope River Road — and the plan notes there are limited commercial services within the neighbourhood at present. No 55+ or seniors project for Fairfield Island appears in any source checked. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=5211 and https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Chilliwack's downtown 55+ street is three buildings with three different rule sets.** On Spadina Avenue: Ivy Green (45775) is 55+ with no pets; Spadina Regency (45765) is 55+ with rentals and pets permitted; Chilliwack Gardens (45749) states "no age restrictions" in its own listing. A Fairfield buyer crossing the slough to buy there has to check the civic number and the bylaws, not the street. https://www.admiraal.ca/buildings/view/27410 and https://www.robbiejohal.com/listing/r3101299-45765-spadina-avenue-501-chilliwack-bc-v2p-1t5/brochure and https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3075638-45749-spadina-avenue-205-chilliwack-chilliwack-downtown-homes-for-sale-217900/brochure
- **Two near-identical building names are two different buildings, and only one is in the standing roster.** Mountainview Estates (46966 Yale Rd, Proper East) is one of the city's five verified 55+ ownership complexes; Mountview Apartments (46374 Margaret Ave) is a different building whose listings also claim 55+, with conflicting build years in its sources (1977 / 1974) — it is flagged as a roster conflict in Section 2, not counted as a sixth complex in this series. https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/46966-yale-road/104 and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/mountview-apartments-46374-margaret-ave
- **The city's one 55+ park is on leased First Nations land with a stated horizon.** Cottonwood Village's listing information states a lease expiry in 2076 with an option to extend — the tenure detail a buyer in any Chilliwack park should ask about on day one, and the reason Fairfield Island's freehold ground and the city's park product are not interchangeable. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/listing/r3069101-50-7610-evans-road-chilliwack-bc-v2r-2y8
- **Fairfield Island's housing era is stated in the City's own plan.** The Schedule B-2 plan characterises the current building stock as predominantly one- and two-storey 1970s–1980s detached homes on 600 m² lots, with limited connectivity via bridges shaping the built form — a downsizer buying here is buying a house of that era, with a house inspection to match, not a strata purchase with a depreciation report. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=5211
- **Chilliwack's tax penalty is two steps.** A 5% penalty lands after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 4, 2026 — unlike a flat single-step penalty, the second step is a separate date a Fairfield buyer closing in summer has to diary. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
