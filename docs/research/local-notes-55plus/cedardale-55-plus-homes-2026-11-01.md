# Cedardale — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Cedardale is on the District of West Vancouver's east edge, below the British Properties and west of the Capilano River, around Burley Drive, Kings Avenue and Mathers Avenue. The District uses the Cedardale name on its neighbourhood and municipal maps but publishes NO street-by-street boundaries for it, and Cedardale is not an OCP plan area — treat any precise boundary line drawn by a listing or a map site as UNVERIFIED. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- École Cedardale (K–7), 595 Burley Dr, is inside the area — but it is a single-track French Immersion school and an IB World School (authorized 2016), with admission by lottery each early December; it is NOT the English catchment school for the surrounding houses. The English catchment school for a Cedardale address is UNVERIFIED — the SD45 locator was not run. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- Parks inside the area per the District's Parks Master Plan: Cedardale Park (zoned RS3) and Burley Drive Park (zoned RS5). (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- Transit runs on Cedardale's own streets: TransLink route 251 serves Kings Avenue, Burley Drive and Mathers Avenue and connects to Park Royal Exchange, which is the terminus of the R2 RapidBus — since Sep 7, 2026 the R2 runs one-seat from Park Royal to Metrotown. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- Park Royal (2002 Park Royal South) sits beside the area and is designated the regional shopping centre in the District's OCP — Cedardale's services are effectively Park Royal's. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- Where the 55+ housing actually is: not here. Cedardale's stock is detached houses on RS lots, and no seniors product of any kind carries a Cedardale address in sources checked (see Section 2). The nearest seniors cluster is the Park Royal / Ambleside group to the south and south-east.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- Provincial law (relevant to any strata purchase a Cedardale buyer makes elsewhere in West Vancouver): under the Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, only 55+ age-restriction bylaws are valid in BC; all lower age bylaws (19+/40+/45+) have been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it. No strata stock in Cedardale itself was verified for these rules to attach to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01: legacy residents who lived in the unit when the bylaw passed, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse/partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the resident cared for before age 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- All residential rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022; stratas may still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- District zoning (Bylaw 4662): Cedardale's houses sit on RS lots (the District's Parks Master Plan zones Cedardale Park RS3 and Burley Drive Park RS5); Bylaw 4662 s. 200 RS minimum lot sizes include RS3 975 m² and RS5 488 m². District-wide, secondary suites and detached secondary suites are permitted, and ground-oriented rules allow up to 4 dwellings, or 6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop. (Area research files, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- **Provincial density directive — the live uncertainty over Cedardale:** on Jul 21, 2025 the Province directed added density in areas adjacent to Ambleside/Dundarave and to Park Royal–Taylor Way. Whether any specific Cedardale street falls inside the affected geography is per-property UNVERIFIED. The District missed the Dec 31, 2025 deadline connected to this file, a provincial order for the Ambleside Local Area Plan took effect Apr 7, 2026, and quarterly provincial progress reports run through 2026–2027. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- West Vancouver taxes 2026: due Jul 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after Jul 2 and a further 5% after Sep 2; water and sewer are metered and billed quarterly. Provincial additional school tax for 2026: 0.2% on residential value between $3M and $4M and 0.4% above $4M, rising to 0.3% / 0.6% on Jan 1, 2027. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md — District-wide rules)
