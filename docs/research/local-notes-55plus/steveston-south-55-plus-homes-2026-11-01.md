# Steveston South — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Steveston South is the south-west corner of the City of Richmond, on the South Arm of the Fraser River. "Steveston South" is the Greater Vancouver REALTORS® (GVR) board sub-area name; the City does not use it as a planning name — the district sits inside the Steveston planning area governed by the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the City Official Community Plan). The board's exact boundary is UNVERIFIED; in listing practice the north/south split of Steveston is generally drawn at Steveston Highway, with the village core, Garry Point and the residential streets south of the highway in Steveston South. The historic village core inside this district has its own notes file (Steveston Village). https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- The Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area sits at the district's south-west waterfront, and properties inside it are subject to Heritage Alteration Permit requirements under the Steveston Area Plan. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- Parks and waterfront: Garry Point Park, a 39-acre waterfront park at the mouth of the Fraser River; Imperial Landing Waterfront Park on the historic BC Packers cannery site; the West Dyke Trail along the west dike; Steveston Community Park, 4011 Moncton Street, a 30-acre park with the Steveston Community Centre, pool and library in the park complex; Richmond Public Library's Steveston Branch (Easthope Hub) is at 4320 Moncton Street. The Gulf of Georgia Cannery at 12138 4th Avenue is a national historic site. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails-cycling/trails-map/steveston.htm and https://parks.canada.ca/culture/designation/lieu-site/gulf-of-georgia
- Schools physically in the district: Lord Byng Elementary School, 3711 Georgia Street; Steves Elementary School, 10111 Fourth Avenue; Westwind Elementary School, 11371 Kingfisher Drive; R.A. McMath Secondary School, 4251 Garry Street, one of Richmond's two French Immersion secondary schools. School District 38 catchments are address-based and the district has no middle school tier. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- Transit: buses serve Steveston Exchange on Chatham Street, including TransLink route 401 via No. 1 Road to Richmond–Brighouse Station for Canada Line connections. Main roads: Moncton Street through the village, No. 1 Road north to Richmond centre, Steveston Highway east to Highway 99, Railway Avenue north–south at the east side of Steveston, and No. 2 Road north from the village. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt401.pdf
- The district fronts the South Arm of the Fraser River behind the City dike system, and the City's Steveston Island Dike project addresses the Steveston waterfront and harbour. Nearest hospital: Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: **Garry Street and the village-edge streets south of Steveston Highway.** Wisteria Place (4388 Garry Street), Lions Park (4200–4206 Garry Street) and River's End Housing Co-op (4080 Garry Street) are all on Garry Street within blocks of McMath Secondary and Steveston Community Park; the Chatham Street seniors buildings (The Maple Residences, Bill Rigby) sit at the village core's east edge and are listed in the Steveston Village file. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Heritage rule that applies in this district and nowhere else in Richmond:** properties inside the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area are subject to Heritage Alteration Permit requirements under the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the Official Community Plan) — exterior changes to a house or strata building in the conservation area need a permit, which affects renovation plans and timelines for any buyer, senior or not. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **City zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500.** Houses sit in the Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m², with a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones; village-core strata sits in Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU32) – Steveston Village and Steveston Commercial (CS3) zones. Secondary suite rules are in Section 5.4 (suite at least 33.0 m², at most the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m²). https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Suites and added units:** the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Flood:** the district fronts the South Arm of the Fraser River behind the City dike system, designed for a 1:500 flood event with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres; the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction levels, and the City's Steveston Island Dike project is the named long-term flood project for this waterfront. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.letstalkrichmond.ca/StevestonIslandDike
- **Property taxes (city-wide Richmond rule):** property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 3: Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025); the co-ops in Section 2 take applications directly. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Steveston South?** None with an age restriction — no 55+ strata was verified in the district. The buyable stock is detached houses and general-market strata (Imperial Landing, The Village on Moncton/Bayview, London Landing), none of it age-restricted. The seniors-specific stock here is rental (Wisteria Place), subsidized (Lions Park, Steveston Residence) and co-op (Garry Point, River's End, Richmond Project) — none of it purchasable strata. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Wisteria Place something I buy into?** No. Wisteria Place at 4388 Garry Street is a rental independent-living residence operated by Optima Living — 107 bachelor, one- and two-bedroom suites on three floors, on land retained by the Steveston Buddhist Temple, built by the Wisteria Senior Health and Housing Society to provide affordable housing for low and moderate income seniors. Whether it enforces a specific minimum age is UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked. https://wshhs.org/ and https://optimaliving.ca/2021/10/wisteria-place-opens-its-doors-to-new-residents-and-the-richmond-community/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. Note Minato West's directory note "C" (couples under 55 accepted) is a subsidized-housing directory code, not a strata rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How do the Garry Street co-ops work — can I buy a unit there?** Not on the open market. Garry Point Housing Co-op (11631 7th Avenue), River's End Housing Co-op (4080 Garry Street) and the Richmond Project (11820 No. 1 Road) are listed in BC Housing's Zone 3 seniors directory as apply-directly co-ops/non-profits — a home there is a co-op membership, with share purchase and participation requirements in the directory notes, not a strata purchase. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: Is Minato West a seniors building?** The sources conflict and this file does not smooth it: BC Housing's seniors directory lists Minato West (4151 Regent Street) as a Richmond — South seniors listing, while Metro Vancouver, its operator, describes a general affordable complex of 110 suites built in 1977. Verify the current tenancy mix with the operator before treating it as seniors housing. https://metrovancouver.org/services/housing/minato-west
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Does buying near the village mean heritage rules on my house?** Inside the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area, yes: exterior alterations need a Heritage Alteration Permit under the Steveston Area Plan. Outside the conservation area boundary, the standard Bylaw 8500 rules apply — check the specific lot against the Area Plan map before pricing a renovation. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Q: What happens if I need assisted living or care later?** The Steveston ladder is: independent rental at Wisteria Place or The Maple Residences (village file), affordable assisted living at Austin Harris Residence on Moncton Street (village file, through Vancouver Coastal Health), then long-term care through VCH — no long-term care home has a Steveston South address. Sources: Section 2 above and the Steveston Village file.
- **Q: Is Steveston South in a floodplain?** Yes — it fronts the South Arm of the Fraser River in the Richmond floodplain, behind the City perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations; Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction levels, and the City is raising dikes from 3.5 m to 4.7 m. The Steveston Island Dike project is the City's named long-term project for this waterfront. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Garry Street is a seniors-housing street because of a temple land deal.** Wisteria Place exists because the Steveston Buddhist Temple retained ownership of its land and spearheaded the project through a separate non-profit, the Wisteria Senior Health and Housing Society, with Optima Living as operator — which is why the district's newest seniors building is non-profit rental beside the temple, not a strata project a buyer can purchase into. https://optimaliving.ca/2021/10/wisteria-place-opens-its-doors-to-new-residents-and-the-richmond-community/
- **The City sells a 55+ facility pass for this district's community centre.** Richmond's Steveston Community Centre seniors pass material states a Seniors Facility Pass is available for individuals 55+ years old, and to spouses less than 55 years old, for one year — a City recreation rule, not a housing rule, and the one place "55+" is a hard number in Steveston daily life. https://www.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Steveston_Seniors_Pass_Schedule_Summer_202679122.pdf
- **The flood project has a navigation gate in it.** The City's Steveston Island Dike project proposes a dike around Steveston Island from London Farm to Garry Point Park with a navigation gate for Steveston Harbour — flood protection here has to keep a working fishing harbour open, which is why the project is specific to this waterfront. https://www.letstalkrichmond.ca/StevestonIslandDike
- **The district's school question has no locator shortcut.** SD38 catchments are address-based, and for the village-core sample address the locator returned no result in the village research pass — a buyer counting on Lord Byng, Steves, Westwind or McMath for grandchildren must run the exact address, street by street. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Two different "Villages" sit in this district's listings.** "The Village" (4280 Moncton Street, a 2005 general-market strata) and the "Steveston Village" building (12040 2nd Avenue, general-market strata with a retail unit) both sound like seniors or heritage products and are neither — both were checked, and neither carries an age restriction in its sources. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/the-village-4280-moncton-street and https://goodmanreport.com/off-market/1131-steveston-village-12040-2nd-ave-richmond/
