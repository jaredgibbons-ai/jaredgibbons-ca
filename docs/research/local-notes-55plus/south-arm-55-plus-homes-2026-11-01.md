# South Arm — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- South Arm is the south-central residential district of the City of Richmond, along Williams Road, between the Steveston side of the city and No. 4 Road, fronting the South Arm of the Fraser River along the city's south edge. "South Arm" is the Greater Vancouver REALTORS® (GVR) board sub-area name; the City also uses "South Arm" officially as a recreation area name — the City's park listing for South Arm Community Park gives its recreation area as "South Arm". The board's exact boundary for the sub-area is not published on an official source and is UNVERIFIED. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=50
- South Arm Community Park, 8680 Williams Road, is a 57.3-acre community park at Williams Road and Garden City Road, adjacent to South Arm Community Centre (8880 Williams Road) and to Hugh McRoberts Secondary and James Whiteside Elementary schools; the park hosts 10 sports fields, 9 baseball diamonds, 4 basketball courts, 8 tennis courts and 3 pickleball courts. South Arm Outdoor Pool is at 10100 South Arm Place and South Arm Community Hall is at 9020 Williams Road. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=50 and https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/city-departments/contact/departments/prcs/recreation.htm
- At the west end of the Williams Road corridor, Steveston-London Secondary School, 6600 Williams Road, is a Grades 8–12 school of 1,250 students, formed from the merger of Steveston Secondary and Charles E. London Secondary. School District 38 (Richmond) catchments are address-based and the district has no middle school tier. https://slss.sd38.bc.ca/school-information/about-us and https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- Main roads: Williams Road east–west through the district, with Garden City Road, No. 3 Road and No. 4 Road running north to the city centre, and Steveston Highway connecting west Richmond to Highway 99. There is no SkyTrain station in the district — Canada Line access is by bus to Richmond–Brighouse Station. Transit route numbers serving Williams Road: UNVERIFIED. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=50
- The district is on Lulu Island in the Fraser River floodplain along the South Arm channel, behind the City perimeter dike system. Nearest hospital: Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/about-bc-s-health-care-system/heath-care-partners/hospitallist.pdf
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: it does not concentrate in South Arm. The district's documented housing form is single-detached houses, and the only verified seniors building with a South Arm address is a long-term care home at the east end of Williams Road (Fraserview, Section 2). There is no verified 55+ ownership stock anywhere in the district. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500.** Houses in South Arm sit in the Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m² and a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Secondary suites and added units:** a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in the RS1/RS2 zones; Section 5.4 sets a suite minimum of 33.0 m² and a maximum of the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m². The City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Plan:** no separate South Arm area plan is confirmed; the district is covered by the City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000, framework rather than a Schedule 2 area plan of its own. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **Flood:** the district is in the Fraser River floodplain along the South Arm channel, behind the City perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations, designed for a 1:500 flood event, with dikes being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres. The Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Property taxes (city-wide Richmond rule):** property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 3: Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025). https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in South Arm?** None with an age restriction — no 55+ strata was verified anywhere in the district. What you can buy is a detached house (often with suite potential under Bylaw 8500 Section 5.4) or a general-market townhouse such as Mandalay on Hemlock Drive, with no age bylaw attached. Richmond's verified 55+ ownership stock is in Terra Nova (Quilchena Green) and at Covenant Court on No. 2 Road. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Richmond?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Is Fraserview on Williams Road somewhere I can rent or buy into later?** No — Fraserview Retirement Community at 9580 Williams Road is a long-term care home providing 24-hour nursing care, rebuilt in 2021 with 197 subsidized beds per Vancouver Coastal Health. Admission runs through the health authority. A commercial directory that prices it like independent living conflicts with VCH's own listing — the VCH description governs. https://travelclinic.vch.ca/en/location/fraserview-retirement-community
- **Q: Is there subsidized seniors rental in South Arm?** Only at the flagged west edge: Rosewood Village, 8280 No. 2 Road, is in BC Housing's Zone 3 seniors directory (May 2025) under its "Richmond — West" grouping, applied for through the Housing Registry. No subsidized seniors building with an address in the district's Williams Road core appears in that directory. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Note the conflict at Covenant Court, where older listing copy states no rentals are allowed — that statement cannot survive the 2022 law change for long-term rentals, and the current Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can a South Arm house have a suite for a caregiver or family member?** The RS1/RS2 zones permit a secondary suite as a secondary use (minimum 33.0 m²; maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling or 110 m² under Section 5.4), and the City states at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit is allowed per lot, with Bill 44 minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. A live-in caregiver is also one of the built-in exemptions to a 55+ strata bylaw, but that exemption matters in strata buildings, not detached houses. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway — the city's hospital, on Lulu Island north of the district. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/about-bc-s-health-care-system/heath-care-partners/hospitallist.pdf
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** There is no SkyTrain station in the district; Canada Line access is by bus to Richmond–Brighouse Station. The specific bus route numbers serving Williams Road are UNVERIFIED — no official timetable was confirmed for the district's streets in this pass. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=50
- **Q: Is South Arm in a floodplain?** Yes — like all of Richmond it is in the Fraser River floodplain, protected by the perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations, designed for a 1:500 flood event with dikes being raised from 3.5 m to 4.7 m; Bylaw 8204 sets flood construction levels for new building, and dike setbacks matter for lots near the south dike. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The park cluster is the district's documented anchor, and it is newer than it looks.** The revitalized South Arm Community Park playground was officially opened by Richmond City Council in July 2023 with funding support from the South Arm Community Association, and James Whiteside Elementary beside the park had a full seismic upgrade project documented by the Province with occupancy from fall 2023 — the school building area remaining 3,107 m². https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/news/2023/southarmplaygroundopen26jul2023.htm and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/education-training/k-12/administration/capital/major-capital-projects/james-whiteside-elementary
- **The care home at the end of Williams Road is one of Richmond's oldest.** Fraserview's operator history traces the home to Fraserview Intermediate Care Lodge, founded over forty years ago and described by its operator as the first care home in the community; the current building is the 2021 rebuild with 197 subsidized beds per VCH. https://travelclinic.vch.ca/en/location/fraserview-retirement-community
- **"South Arm" is a recreation name before it is a real estate name.** The City's own park listing assigns South Arm Community Park the recreation area "South Arm" — the board sub-area boundary a buyer sees in listings is not published by the City or the board on an official source, which is why edge addresses like 8280 No. 2 Road cannot be assigned to the sub-area with certainty. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=50
- **There is no South Arm area plan.** Unlike Steveston, which has the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the OCP), South Arm is governed by the city-wide 2041 OCP, Bylaw 9000, framework — no neighbourhood-specific plan document exists to check before a purchase. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **The suite math is in the bylaw, not the listing.** A legal suite in a South Arm detached house must fit Section 5.4 of Bylaw 8500 (at least 33.0 m², at most the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m²) — a suite described in a listing that exceeds those limits is a due-diligence item, not a feature. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
