# Hastings (Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

**IDENTITY POINT — read first:** the slug "hastings" is ambiguous. The City of Vancouver's official local area is **Hastings-Sunrise** — there is no City local area called simply "Hastings" (the City's areas page lists "Hastings East" and "Hastings Crossing" as locality names). The Paragon name "Hastings" follows the Hastings Street corridor. The City of Burnaby has its own Hastings area east across Boundary Road — a different municipality, NOT this file. Following the research file's determination, these notes treat the Paragon "Hastings" sub-area as anchored on Hastings-Sunrise at the east end of the corridor; corridor facts west of Hastings-Sunrise are flagged as corridor facts, and Burnaby facts are excluded entirely.

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **How the City creates new 55+ rental:** Vancouver's Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy (approved July 23, 2024; last amended May 19, 2026) treats "Social Housing (where occupancy is restricted to older adults 55+)" as social housing secured by a housing agreement restricting occupancy to residents aged 55 or older, owned and operated by a non-profit or government, and secures seniors supportive or independent living housing as rental — no stratification, no separate sales covenants — for 60 years or the life of the building, whichever is greater. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-rezoning-seniors-housing.pdf
- **House-street zoning — R1-1 (the zoning an older Hastings-Sunrise owner actually holds):** the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 sets minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m) and 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units, and a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house. Corridor commercial and mixed-use sites on Hastings Street sit under other district schedules, including C-3A on parts of the street; the zone for a specific lot: UNCONFIRMED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plans:** the western edge of Hastings-Sunrise is included in the Grandview-Woodland Community Plan area, and the citywide Vancouver Plan also applies; a separate community plan named only for Hastings-Sunrise: UNCONFIRMED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/hastings-sunrise.aspx
- **Tax calendar:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after each due date, plus the annual Empty Homes Tax property status declaration (2025 payment due April 16, 2026). https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Hastings-Sunrise?** None that any source verifies. No age-restricted strata building was found in the area — an older buyer here buys what the area actually is: a freehold house, duplex or multiplex home on the house streets, or a general-market corridor strata unit with no age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is "Hastings" in Burnaby or Vancouver?** Both cities have one, and they are different areas in different municipalities. This file is Vancouver's Hastings-Sunrise, west of Boundary Road. Always write "Hastings-Sunrise, Vancouver" in copy — a bare "Hastings" search pulls in Burnaby's Hastings area and the whole downtown-bound corridor. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **Q: What seniors housing does exist in Hastings-Sunrise?** Subsidized rental only: the BC Housing Zone 6 directory's Hastings and Sunrise buildings — Beechwood at Beulah Gardens (2075 Cassiar St), Rupert Street (2050 Rupert St), Skeena Terrace (2108 Cassiar St) and The Bentall Residence (3350 East 5th Ave) — plus Sunrise Village (2924 Venables St, 146 mixed affordable rental homes including seniors, anticipated to open in 2027). Nothing to buy, and no care home was verified in the area. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2024HOUS0145-001283.pdf
- **Q: How do I get into one of the subsidized buildings, or into Sunrise Village?** The directory buildings are non-profit rentals listed in BC Housing's Zone 6 seniors directory (May 2025) — apply through BC Housing's Housing Registry or directly with the operator; the City points seniors to the Seniors Services Society, which maintains the Seniors Housing Directory of BC. Sunrise Village will be owned and managed by Brightside Community Homes Foundation; its opening is anticipated in 2027 on the Province's statement, so it is not current stock. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/find-supportive-housing-for-seniors.aspx
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a BC 55+ strata elsewhere?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can an older owner add a suite or a laneway house here instead of moving?** On the house streets, the R1-1 schedule allows a single detached house to include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and allows multiplexes of up to 6 units (up to 8 rental units) subject to the minimum site areas (306 m² to 557 m² by unit count) — the in-place downsizing and family-compound options this area actually offers, since it has no 55+ strata to move into. The zone and dimensions of a specific lot: UNCONFIRMED — confirm parcel by parcel. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: How does the area connect to downtown and SFU without a SkyTrain station?** By the R5 Hastings St RapidBus, which runs from Burrard Station to SFU Exchange with stops at Nanaimo, Renfrew and Kootenay on Hastings Street in the area; routes 14, 16 and 20 also serve Hastings Street, and Kootenay Loop sits at Boundary Road. For an older buyer the honest transit fact is: frequent bus on the corridor, no station in the area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/ttR5.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** No hospital inside Hastings-Sunrise was verified on a health-authority page; Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, is in Fairview. The in-area health-relevant anchors in the sources read are the community centres and the Hastings Branch library, not a care facility. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital
- **Q: Is a north-slope home in the floodplain?** The City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain, and the Burrard Inlet shoreline at the area's north side, including New Brighton Park, is the part to check against that dataset; land in the floodplain is subject to Vancouver Building By-law setbacks and flood construction levels. Whether a specific parcel is in it: UNCONFIRMED. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The area's history is a townsite that never happened:** the City states Hastings-Sunrise sits on the northern half of a block of land the provincial government set aside in 1863 for a town expected to develop along the shore of Burrard Inlet; Gastown farther west became the port city instead, and a small resort, New Brighton, developed on the shoreline roughly where New Brighton Park is today. That is why the area reads as a later, mainly-residential house neighbourhood rather than an old commercial core. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/hastings-sunrise.aspx
- **Sunrise Village is the area's one big seniors-relevant build, and it replaces seniors stock rather than adding a 55+ building:** the Province states its two six-storey buildings at 2924 Venables Street replace a demolished 64-unit low-rise, for a net 82 additional affordable rental units (per the project account), with former tenants offered right of first refusal — and it is deliberately multi-generational (families, independent-living seniors, people with disabilities), built to Passive House standards. Filing it as "55+ condos coming" would be wrong on tenure, on age rule and on timing (anticipated opening 2027). https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2024HOUS0145-001283.pdf and https://canada.constructconnect.com/joc/news/projects/2024/08/new-sunrise-village-affordable-homes-being-built-in-vancouver
- **The Capri and Cove purchase shows how this area's affordable seniors-adjacent stock is actually preserved:** not by an age bylaw, but by a non-profit (New Chelsea Society) buying ordinary walk-up rental buildings with Rental Protection Fund money and holding rents at about half of market for 20 years — with tenants who, on the society's account, live there for more than 20 years on average. Long tenure is not an age restriction. https://www.vancitylookout.com/p/below-market-housing-maintained-in-hastings-sunrise
- **BC Housing's directory files a Hastings Street building under Grandview:** Constance Court, 2518 Hastings Street East, sits in the directory's Grandview section, while the Hastings and Sunrise section's buildings are on Cassiar, Rupert and East 5th — a reminder that around this corridor, the directory section, the City local area and the street name are three different geographies. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **The PNE is the area's seasonal fact:** Hastings Park is the home of the Pacific National Exhibition (PNE) and Playland, and the City calls the PNE the thing Hastings-Sunrise is best known for — an amenity and an event-traffic pattern a buyer on the streets around Templeton Drive and Renfrew Street should price with open eyes. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/hastings-sunrise.aspx
