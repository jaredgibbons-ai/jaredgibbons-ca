## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** Since Nov 24, 2022 no strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw; stratas may still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Vancouver Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575.** Kitsilano's RT areas sit under the RT-7 and RT-8 District Schedules — RT-8 states a minimum site area of 306 m² for a duplex, and the RT-7/RT-8 intent is to encourage retention and renovation of buildings consistent with the historical character of the area; house lots elsewhere sit under R1-1 (minimum site area 306 m² for 3–4-unit multiple dwellings, 464 m² for 5 units, 557 m² for 6 or more units, with secondary suites and laneway houses in the forms the schedules state). Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/rt-8.pdf
- **Plan: the Broadway Plan** covers Vine Street to Clark Drive and 1st Avenue to 16th Avenue, including parts of Kitsilano, and is the framework tied to the Broadway Subway; a Kitsilano-specific community plan is UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Character-retention rules are the seniors-housing constraint here:** the RT schedules' stated heritage-character intent is why the area's seniors supply arrived as small non-profit infill inside a neighbourhood house (15 + 30 units) rather than as a purpose-built strata — confirm the exact district schedule and any character-house rules before pricing any Kitsilano lot on redevelopment value. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/rt-8.pdf
- **Flood / shoreline:** the City's Coastal Flood Risk Assessment covers a Point Grey–Kitsilano study area — the shoreline from Point Grey to Kitsilano Point faces the Strait of Georgia, is exposed to waves from the northwest, flood protection is limited and riprap west of Kitsilano Beach is discontinuous; the Province advises planning for 0.5 m of sea level rise by 2050 and 1 m by 2100. http://vancouver.ca/files/cov/CFRA-Phase-1-Final_Report.pdf
- **Vancouver tax calendar:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; owners must also file the annual Empty Homes Tax property status declaration (the 2025 tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% late penalty) — directly relevant to Kitsilano's second-home and investor stock. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Kitsilano?** None that any source verifies. No age-restricted strata ownership building was found in Kitsilano — its named strata buildings (Solo, Kits West, Star of Kitsilano, Avanti, Kits Palisades) carry no 55+ bylaw in any source. A buyer set on Kitsilano buys a general-market house, duplex or condo; a buyer set on 55+ ownership buys in a different neighbourhood. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/rt-8.pdf
- **Q: What seniors housing does exist in Kitsilano?** Three verified buildings, all rental-form: Seasons Arbutus (2799 Yew St), a private independent-living retirement residence also registered for assisted living; Red Oak Place (2305 West 7th Ave), 15 affordable seniors' rental units at Kits House; and Linden Tree Place (2304 West 8th Ave), 30 affordable rental apartments for seniors and persons with disabilities. https://seasonsretirement.com/locations/seasons-arbutus/ and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2015MNGD0030-000711 and https://sci-bc.ca/new-accessible-housing-development-seeks-tenants/
- **Q: Is Seasons Arbutus in Kitsilano or Arbutus Ridge?** Kitsilano, on its own sources: the operator markets it as being in Vancouver's Kitsilano neighbourhood and the Assisted Living Registry address is 2799 Yew Street, V6K 4W2 — a Kitsilano postal code, on Yew Street south of West 12th Avenue. Do not confuse it with Amica Arbutus Manor (2125 Eddington Drive) or Tapestry at Arbutus Walk, which are Arbutus Ridge. https://seasonsretirement.com/locations/seasons-arbutus/ and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f[0]=city:Burnaby&f[1]=city:Qualicum Beach&f[2]=city:Vancouver&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=asc&page=1
- **Q: How do I get into Red Oak Place or Linden Tree Place?** They are non-profit affordable rentals run by Kitsilano Neighbourhood House, not market rentals: Linden Tree Place applications run through BC Housing (building code 177 in SCI BC's application guidance), and its housing model expects residents able to live independently who volunteer four to five hours a month in Kits House programs. Expect a waitlist process, not a viewing. https://sci-bc.ca/new-accessible-housing-development-seeks-tenants/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a BC 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can a Kitsilano house lot hold a suite or laneway house for a caregiver or family?** In the forms the district schedules state: R1-1 allows a secondary suite and a laneway house with a single detached house, and the RT-7/RT-8 areas are built around retention and conversion of existing character houses — the practical aging-in-place route in a neighbourhood with no 55+ strata to buy. Confirm the parcel's schedule first. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/rt-8.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** No hospital sits inside Kitsilano; Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, is east of the area in Fairview. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital
- **Q: If I need more support later, can I stay in Kitsilano?** The in-area ladder is short: independent house/condo living, then Seasons Arbutus (independent living with flexible wellness services, and a registered assisted-living component). No long-term care home with a Kitsilano address was verified. https://seasonsretirement.com/locations/seasons-arbutus/ and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f[0]=city:Burnaby&f[1]=city:Qualicum Beach&f[2]=city:Vancouver&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=asc&page=1

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Kitsilano's subsidized seniors housing is literally inside the neighbourhood house:** Red Oak Place's 15 units sit in the upper levels of Kits House at 2305 West 7th Ave — residents pass through the neighbourhood house to reach their homes, and the project was built on land the non-profit itself donated, valued at $6.4 million in the Province's release. A research proposal on the model records that Red Oak residents volunteer a minimum of five hours a month in Kits House programs. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2015MNGD0030-000711 and http://neighbourhoodchange.ca/documents/2016/04/proposal-21-lauer-ageing-in-changing-places-vancouver-july-2015.pdf
- **The two Kits House buildings are one block and one program apart:** Red Oak Place (15 units, West 7th Ave) and Linden Tree Place (30 units, West 8th Ave at Vine, with a public seniors resource centre on its main floor per a 2022 Council submission) are both operated by Kits House — together they are 45 of the very few affordable seniors units on Vancouver's west side. https://council.vancouver.ca/20220628/documents/phea3opposed20220628_dist1_Redacted.pdf and https://sci-bc.ca/new-accessible-housing-development-seeks-tenants/
- **"Arbutus" is the Kitsilano search trap:** three different seniors products carry the word — Seasons Arbutus (2799 Yew St, actually in Kitsilano), Amica Arbutus Manor (2125 Eddington Dr, Arbutus Ridge) and Tapestry at Arbutus Walk (Arbutus Ridge). Only the first belongs in Kitsilano copy; the name alone tells you nothing. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3AMerritt&f%5B1%5D=city%3AVancouver&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc
- **Linden Tree Place is named for a tree, and part of its rent is volunteering:** SCI BC's opening story records the building was named after an old sprawling linden tree on the property, has ten fully accessible units of its 30, and was designed for older adults willing to volunteer at least four to five hours a month for Kits House. https://sci-bc.ca/new-accessible-housing-development-seeks-tenants/
- **The beach blocks carry a shoreline file the Broadway blocks do not:** the City's coastal assessment describes the Point Grey–Kitsilano shoreline as exposed to northwest waves with limited, discontinuous flood protection west of Kitsilano Beach — get the parcel-level flood and insurance picture early on any beach-side purchase, and note the Province's planning figures of 0.5 m sea level rise by 2050 and 1 m by 2100. http://vancouver.ca/files/cov/CFRA-Phase-1-Final_Report.pdf
- **The Broadway Plan splits the neighbourhood's south edge from its beach blocks:** the Plan (1st to 16th Avenue, Vine Street to Clark Drive) is tied to the Broadway Subway and covers parts of Kitsilano — south-edge lots carry plan expectations, and future station-area density, that the RT character blocks north of 4th Avenue do not. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **The Empty Homes Tax declaration is an annual chore every Vancouver owner carries:** a Kitsilano owner holding a property as a second home must still file the yearly property status declaration — the 2025 payment deadline was April 16, 2026 with a 5% late penalty — a standing cost of the second-home stock this neighbourhood is known for. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
