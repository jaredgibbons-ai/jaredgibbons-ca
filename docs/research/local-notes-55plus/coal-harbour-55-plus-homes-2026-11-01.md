# Coal Harbour (Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Coal Harbour is the waterfront neighbourhood on the north side of the downtown peninsula, on Burrard Inlet between the downtown core and Stanley Park. It is NOT one of the City of Vancouver's 22 official local areas: the City lists "Downtown" among its 22 areas and treats Coal Harbour as a neighbourhood inside Downtown — and, in a quirk a buyer will meet in City links, the Coal Harbour locality link on the City's areas page resolves to the West End page, even though the West End is a separate official local area and Coal Harbour sits inside Downtown. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/downtown.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- "Coal Harbour" is the Paragon sub-area name. A board search on Coal Harbour is narrower than the City's Downtown local area and does not include Yaletown, Gastown or the West End. Stand-alone boundary roads for Coal Harbour from a City text source: UNCONFIRMED. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- The area has its own council-adopted plan, the Coal Harbour Official Development Plan (By-law 6754), which states Coal Harbour should be an integral part of downtown Vancouver — so while the City local area is Downtown, the planning identity of Coal Harbour is distinct. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/ODP/odp-coal-harbour.pdf
- Main streets: West Georgia Street, West Hastings Street, West Cordova Street, Burrard Street and Thurlow Street serve the area; the Official Development Plan lists public street-end view corridors at Burrard, Thurlow, Bute, Jervis, Broughton, Nicola and Cardero Streets to the water. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/ODP/odp-coal-harbour.pdf
- Civic and recreation anchors: Coal Harbour Community Centre, 480 Broughton Street (multi-purpose rooms and a gymnasium); Harbour Green Park on the waterfront; Devonian Harbour Park, 1929 West Georgia Street at Denman Street, described by the Park Board as a gateway between Stanley Park and Coal Harbour. https://Vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/community-and-cultural-centres.aspx and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/ODP/odp-coal-harbour.pdf
- Schools: Vancouver School Board (SD39) catchments are address-based. The VSB states it added new elementary school capacity in downtown Vancouver with a new elementary school in Coal Harbour (named on the VSB site as the Seaside school in Coal Harbour); its street address: UNCONFIRMED in this pass, and no public secondary school located inside Coal Harbour was confirmed. The Downtown elementaries in the VSB directory — Elsie Roy Elementary, 150 Drake Street, and Crosstown šxʷwəq̓ʷəθət Elementary, 55 Expo Boulevard — sit in Yaletown and Crosstown, not in Coal Harbour, and must not be presented as Coal Harbour schools. SD39 has no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12). https://www.vsb.bc.ca/capital-planning and https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
- Library and health: a Vancouver Public Library branch located inside Coal Harbour: UNCONFIRMED (the Central Library, 350 West Georgia Street, is in the containing Downtown local area). A hospital or clinic located inside Coal Harbour: UNCONFIRMED. Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, is in Fairview, across False Creek. https://www.vpl.ca/ and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital
- Transit: a SkyTrain station located inside Coal Harbour itself: UNCONFIRMED — the containing Downtown local area is the hub where the Expo Line, Millennium Line and Canada Line meet, and the R5 Hastings St RapidBus terminates at Burrard Station in the downtown core. Bus route numbers serving Coal Harbour on a TransLink source: UNCONFIRMED in this pass. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/ttR5.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw — in Coal Harbour towers, the short-term rental bylaw and the City's licensing rules, not an age bylaw, are what a buyer will actually meet in the strata documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **How the City creates new 55+ rental:** Vancouver's Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy (approved July 23, 2024; last amended May 19, 2026) treats "Social Housing (where occupancy is restricted to older adults 55+)" as social housing secured by a housing agreement restricting occupancy to residents aged 55 or older, owned and operated by a non-profit or government, and secures seniors supportive or independent living housing as rental — no stratification, no separate sales covenants — for 60 years or the life of the building, whichever is greater. That is the only route by which new age-restricted product is being created in Vancouver, and it produces rental, never strata for sale. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-rezoning-seniors-housing.pdf
- **Coal Harbour zoning and plan:** parcels sit under the Downtown District (DD) Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 together with the Coal Harbour Official Development Plan (By-law 6754). The DD schedule permits commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses in conformity with an Official Development Plan; a minimum lot size figure for DD parcels: UNCONFIRMED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf
- **Tax calendar and the extra Coal Harbour taxes:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after each due date; the Empty Homes Tax requires an annual property status declaration, with the 2025 payment due April 16, 2026. At Coal Harbour price points the Province's additional school tax can also apply: 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Coal Harbour?** None that any source verifies. No age-restricted strata building was found in this sub-area — a buyer who wants an age-restricted community buys in another neighbourhood, and a buyer set on Coal Harbour buys a general-market strata unit in a waterfront tower whose bylaws to check are its short-term rental and pet bylaws, not an age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: The towers look quiet and full of downsizers — doesn't that make them 55+?** No. A building's resident mix is not a bylaw. The named Coal Harbour towers (Avila, Cascina, Denia, Escala, the Harbour Green buildings) carry no age restriction in any source checked; only a registered 55+ bylaw under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 makes a building 55+, and none was verified here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What seniors housing does exist near Coal Harbour?** None inside the sub-area. The downtown peninsula's verified seniors supply is outside Coal Harbour: Yaletown House long-term care (1099 Cambie Street, Yaletown) and the BC Housing Zone 6 directory's Downtown South / Yaletown non-profit buildings on Seymour, Davie, Granville, Smithe, Richards Streets and Pacific Boulevard — all rental or care, nothing to buy. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://www.yaletown.org/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a BC 55+ strata elsewhere?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Coal Harbour unit, or run it as a short-term rental?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Short-term rentals are the exception that survives: a strata can still restrict them by bylaw, which is the bylaw that matters most in waterfront towers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Does Coal Harbour work without a car for an older buyer?** On the sourced facts, the daily-life anchors inside the sub-area are the Coal Harbour Community Centre at 480 Broughton Street, Harbour Green Park and the continuous waterfront walkway the Official Development Plan requires; the SkyTrain hub is in the containing Downtown local area, and a station inside Coal Harbour itself: UNCONFIRMED. The practical question is the specific tower's walk to Burrard Station and to the community centre, not the area's service level. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/ODP/odp-coal-harbour.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** No hospital inside Coal Harbour was verified. Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, is across False Creek in Fairview; St. Paul's Hospital, 1081 Burrard Street, is in the Downtown core to the south-east (covered in the Downtown VW notes). https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital
- **Q: Is a waterfront Coal Harbour unit in the floodplain?** The City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain, and the Burrard Inlet shoreline is the part of this area to check against that dataset; land in the floodplain is subject to Vancouver Building By-law setbacks and flood construction levels. Whether a specific parcel is in it: UNCONFIRMED — check the parcel in the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Coal Harbour?** Not at a verified assisted-living, subsidized or long-term care step inside the sub-area — those steps require leaving Coal Harbour (Yaletown House in Yaletown for long-term care; the subsidized band in Downtown South). Independent strata living in a general-market tower, with services brought in, is the only in-sub-area step verified here. https://www.yaletown.org/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Coal Harbour's form is plan-made, and the plan protects the view a buyer is paying for:** the Official Development Plan (By-law 6754) requires the waterfront walkway system to be continuous along the Coal Harbour waterfront and protects street-end view corridors at Burrard, Thurlow, Bute, Jervis, Broughton, Nicola and Cardero Streets — so a unit's view-corridor status is a documentable selling point, not a promise. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/ODP/odp-coal-harbour.pdf
- **Even the City's own area links blur this geography:** on the City's areas page the Coal Harbour locality link resolves to the West End page, even though Coal Harbour sits inside Downtown — the same page pattern that sends Yaletown to the Downtown page. Name the street and the tower in any listing copy, not just "Coal Harbour waterfront." https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **There is a new elementary school in Coal Harbour, but do not promise a catchment from it:** the VSB states the new Coal Harbour elementary school added capacity in downtown Vancouver, but its street address is UNCONFIRMED in this pass and SD39 catchments are address-based — run every address in the VSB locator, and never present Yaletown's Elsie Roy or Crosstown elementaries as Coal Harbour schools. https://www.vsb.bc.ca/capital-planning and https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
- **The Empty Homes Tax declaration is the standing annual chore Coal Harbour owners actually face:** every Vancouver owner must file the yearly property status declaration, and waterfront investor-held stock makes the seller's declaration status a standard due-diligence check on any purchase here — the 2025 payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **New 55+ product, if it ever comes to Coal Harbour, will be rental by law of the City's own policy:** the Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy secures new seniors housing as rental with no stratification for 60 years or the life of the building — a future "55+" Coal Harbour project under that policy is something to rent, not to buy. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-rezoning-seniors-housing.pdf
