## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Who the host at an open house works for.** In BC, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. The host at an advertised open house is ordinarily the seller's side. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **The open-house disclosure exception.** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report, provides that a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information about a party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **The live page's own "Before you walk in" frame (site copy):** the listing agent works for the seller; ask for the Form B, depreciation report and minutes at a strata open house; and BC's 3-business-day rescission period at 0.25% of the price applies with leasehold, auction and court-ordered exceptions. Carried as the page's copy, consistent with the BCFSA frame above. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-open-houses.html
- **How open-house dates and times are set and entered for publication, and any visitor sign-in requirement: UNVERIFIED** — no BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states either in the research pass. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-open-houses.md
- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now** (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; sub-55 bylaws invalid since Nov 24, 2022) — so at a strata open house, "55+" is the only age restriction that can lawfully be in the registered bylaws; a host describing a 40+ or 45+ building is describing a bylaw that no longer has effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it,** with Regulation 7.01 exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** in strata since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Municipal split on this feed:** Surrey listings sit under Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 and Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900; White Rock listings sit under White Rock's Zoning Bylaw 2024 No. 2506 and OCP Bylaw 2017 No. 2220. The feed mixes both on one page. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents
- **Data provenance on the site's listing pages:** listing data comes in part from the MLS® Reciprocity program of the Greater Vancouver REALTORS®, the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board or the Chilliwack and District Real Estate Board, which assume no responsibility for its accuracy — the notice carried on this page, and the reason a card time is not authoritative on its own. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-open-houses.html

The community context (September 2026, Source: SnapStats, via the research file) is 600 Detached listings and 593 Condos & Townhomes listings community-wide. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-open-houses.md

- **Q: Who does the agent hosting the open house represent?** The seller, unless you have your own client relationship with a licensee — BCFSA states a professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction. Use the open house for facts and save your motivation, budget and needs for your own agent. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: Do I have to disclose anything just by walking in?** No — under Rule 5-10(3), disclosure of representation is not required while a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or giving factual answers; the position changes once you discuss motivation, finances or needs with the seller's licensee. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download

- **Q: What days and times do open houses usually run?** The page's FAQ (site copy) says most occur on Saturdays and Sundays from 1–4 PM, with some weekday twilight showings. No official source publishes open-house scheduling statistics — confirm each home's own time with the listing brokerage, especially given the card-time defect above. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-open-houses.html
- **Q: How do I tour a verified 55+ complex if none shows an open house this weekend?** The page offers a private showing route for every home on it ("Book a showing" or text 604-928-1361, site copy) — for the Section 2 complexes, a private showing through your own agent is also the setting where the Form B, bylaws and minutes can be reviewed properly. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-open-houses.html
