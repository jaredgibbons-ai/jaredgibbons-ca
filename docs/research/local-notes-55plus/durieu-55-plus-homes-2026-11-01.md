# Durieu (FVRD Electoral Area F, Hatzic Valley) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**GEOGRAPHY FLAG, stated up front and prominently: Durieu is NOT in the City of Mission. Durieu is an unincorporated farming community in Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area F, in the Hatzic Valley north of Hatzic Prairie — even though every listing carries a "Mission" mailing address and SnapStats groups the "Durieu" sub-area under the board community Mission for reporting only. The FVRD's Electoral Areas Housing Needs Report lists "Electoral Area F: McConnell Creek, Hatzic Prairie" and states the Hatzic Valley includes the historic settlement areas of Hatzic Prairie, Durieu, Miracle Valley and McConnell Creek. The FVRD, not the City of Mission, is the local government; City of Mission bylaws, zoning and tax rules do NOT apply here — in particular, do NOT apply Mission's flat 10% tax penalty to a Durieu property. Rural taxes here are paid to the Province under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act (5% after July 2, further 5% after October 31). This file is titled and framed accordingly. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. There is no strata in Durieu for this to attach to today — it is the rule a buyer would meet in the City of Mission's two 55+ strata complexes named in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; exemptions attach to a "specified resident." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. These are strata exemptions — they do not create a second-dwelling right on a Durieu acreage. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022 (strata rule); SHORT-TERM rental bylaws are still allowed in stratas. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Land use runs through the FVRD, not a city hall:** FVRD official community plan and zoning bylaws govern Electoral Area F (the Hatzic Valley OCP covers the inhabited valley), but the specific OCP/zoning bylaw numbers and zone names applying to Durieu lots are UNVERIFIED in this research — no FVRD bylaw document for Area F was opened, and feed zone labels are listing text only. Confirm with the FVRD for the PID. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf
- **Secondary suites in Area F have a hard shape limit.** The FVRD records significant limits on development options in Electoral Area F with the majority of lands in the ALR, and that secondary suites are freely permitted with no restrictions related to farm use, but must be located within the principal dwelling and could not be a separate detached structure, such as a garden suite, or a multi-unit structure for farm workers. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf
- **ALR is the second gate on most of this ground.** On ALR parcels the ALC housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), and landowners who wish to pursue other uses or to subdivide ALR property must apply under the Agricultural Land Commission Act and secure ALC approval. Whether a specific Durieu parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level — first-page feed text itself puts one Durieu Road parcel in the ALR and states roughly 18 acres of a Seux Road parcel are NOT in the ALR, both as listing claims to verify, not facts. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://jaredgibbons.ca/durieu.html
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law** (relevant if a buyer looks at valley parks outside Durieu): pad tenancies fall under BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act province-wide — the resident owns the home and rents the pad; pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. Park rules, approval on assignment, and any age policy are park documents. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Property taxes are rural-area taxes, paid to the Province:** a property not in a city, town, district or village is in a rural area; its owner receives the tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province, not the FVRD and not the City of Mission. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on any portion unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on any portion unpaid on October 31. (This is the rural regime — do NOT apply Mission's flat 10% city penalty here.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01

Confirm both the FVRD zoning and the parcel's ALR status for the specific PID before relying on a second-dwelling plan. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — does that matter here?** Not under any age bylaw, because no Durieu complex checked has one. The provincial spouse/partner exemption matters only inside a 55+ strata (such as the Mission complexes above), where a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is exempt by regulation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

A feed card's "zoned for a secondary dwelling" line clears neither gate by itself. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
