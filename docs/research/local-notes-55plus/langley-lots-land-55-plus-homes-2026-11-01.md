- Land types in the feed: urban infill building lots in the City and the Township's urban communities, suburban residential parcels under the SR zones, and large rural/ALR parcels in Salmon River, Campbell Valley, County Line Glen Valley and Otter District. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-lots-land.html

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Vacant land has no 55+ product as such. A bare lot carries no strata corporation, no age bylaw and no park rule — there is nothing to be "55+" about until a home is built or a home is placed, and even then the age rule lives in the strata bylaws or the park rules, not in the land. Langley therefore has ZERO verified 55+ ownership complexes on the lots-and-land page itself. What a 55+ buyer CAN buy in Langley today is (a) a manufactured home in a verified 55+ park on leased or co-operative land — you own the home, not the land — verified by name below, and (b) a lot to build on, under the municipal rules in Section 3.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. This is the rule a 55+ buyer who builds and later strata-titles, or who buys into a bare-land strata, will live under. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. Note: these are STRATA exemptions — in a manufactured-home park, the park's own rules govern occupancy, not Regulation 7.01. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. The "Rentals Not Allowed" lines in manufactured-home park listing data are park-tenancy rules, not strata bylaws, and are not governed by this rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Manufactured-home parks run on a different statute:** pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not the Strata Property Act. A home owner must provide a purchaser with the written tenancy agreement and the written park rules, and a sale is an assignment of the pad tenancy that requires the park owner's consent; the assignment rules expressly recognise a park where every pad is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or 2+ tenants, at least one of whom has reached 55), as set out in s. 10(2)(b)(i) of the Human Rights Code. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Township zoning — confirmed from tol.ca: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987, No. 2500** "applies to the entirety of the Township." Its s. 110.1 minimum lot areas (as reproduced in a Township council report) include SR-1 at 3,716 m², SR-2 at 8,094 m², RU-1 at 1.7 ha, RU-2 and RU-3 at 8.0 ha, and urban R-1B/R-1C at 464.5 m². https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **City zoning — confirmed from langleycity.ca: City of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300.** The City's public hearing notice records Bylaw No. 3300 as the new Zoning Bylaw presented for adoption to replace Zoning Bylaw No. 2100 (originally adopted in 1996), and the bylaw text states it "applies to the entire area within the boundaries of the City of Langley" and repeals Bylaw 2100. NOTE: an earlier brief for this program named a "Zoning Bylaw 2023, No. 3200" for Langley City — no City source checked confirms that number; the confirmed current bylaw is No. 3300 (2025). As printed in Bylaw 3300: R1 Suburban Residential minimum lot size 557 m² / 16 m frontage; R2 Townhouse Residential 1,000 m²; R3 Low Rise Residential 1,000 m² / 26.0 m frontage. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/public-hearing-noticepdf and https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw
- **What a 55+ buyer CAN do with a Township lot — suites and added units:** Township added-unit housing eligibility requires a property to "be connected to both municipal water and sewer," and in areas without existing water or sanitary system capacity the required upgrades are determined under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019, No. 5382. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **ALR housing rules govern most rural Langley lots:** a principal residence built without an Agricultural Land Commission application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; and one additional detached residence is permitted up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or smaller or 186 m² on larger parcels, where Township bylaws also permit it. Subdivision of ALR land requires ALC permission apart from limited cases in the Agricultural Land Reserve General Regulation. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Subdivision route — Township:** "All subdivisions in the Township must be approved by the Approving Officer, as required under the Land Title Act," through application, review, decision letter, conditions, final approval and registration in the Land Title Office, then servicing completion and building permit application. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **Development cost charges differ by municipality:** Township of Langley Development Cost Charges Bylaw No. 5897; City of Langley Development Cost Charges Bylaw, 2024, No. 3256 — a land purchase's build budget must carry the applicable municipality's charges. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Building-Bylaws/Development-Cost-Charges-Bylaw-(No.-5897).pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a "55+ lot" in Langley?** No such product exists as land. Vacant land carries no age restriction — the 55+ rule, where one exists, lives in a strata's registered bylaws (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1/123.2) or in a manufactured-home park's rules. A lot marketed for downsizers is still a general-market lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What 55+ homes CAN I buy in Langley right now?** Built product, not land: manufactured homes in the verified 55+ parks — Cedar Creek Estates (3031 200 St) and Pineridge (2270 196 St) and Fernridge Park (20071 24 Ave), all in Brookswood, and the Pioneer Park co-operative (27111 0 Ave, Aldergrove) — plus strata rancher townhomes at Creekside Villa (27435 29A Ave, Aldergrove, LMS361). In the parks you own the home and not the land. https://www.estateblock.com/langley/5-3031-200-st-1968212 and https://realestatecoalharbour.com/fraser-valley-real-estate/listing.r3165233-23-2270-196-street-langley-v2z-1n6.109859015/ and https://fraservalleyhomesold.ca/mylistings.html/listing.r3112002-33-27435-29a-avenue-langley-v4w-3m4.108533948
- **Q: If I buy land and build a rancher, can I make my own home "55+"?** A single freehold house has no strata corporation, so there is no age-bylaw mechanism for it. If the build is later strata-titled (for example a bare-land strata), only a 55+ bylaw is legal in BC, and it would restrict who may live there, not who may own. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can the park refuse my purchase because of age?** In a 55+ park, in effect yes: assignment of a pad tenancy needs the park owner's consent, and the provincial assignment rules recognise refusal where no purchaser meets the park's 55+ age requirement (Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i) framework). The seller must provide the written tenancy agreement and park rules — get them before writing an offer. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Do the strata exemptions help us in a park?** No — the Regulation 7.01 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregiver, legacy resident) are strata rules. In a park, the assignment test in the provincial rules is that at least one of 2+ tenants has reached 55, and the park's own rules govern. Read the park rules, not the strata page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Q: Which Langley am I buying land in, and why does it matter?** It decides the bylaw: Township land sits under Zoning Bylaw 1987, No. 2500, and City land under Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300, with different minimum lot sizes, subdivision routes and development cost charges (Township DCC Bylaw No. 5897; City DCC Bylaw 2024, No. 3256). Establish the municipality before anything else. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx and https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/public-hearing-noticepdf
- **Q: Can I build a second home or a suite on a rural Langley lot for a caregiver or family?** On ALR land, one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and Township bylaws must also permit it; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence, which is capped at 500 m². In the Township's added-unit program, eligibility requires connection to both municipal water and sewer. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: How small a rural lot can I create?** It depends on the zone under Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 110.1: RU-1 requires 1.7 ha for a new lot, RU-2 and RU-3 require 8.0 ha, SR-2 requires 8,094 m² and SR-1 requires 3,716 m² — and ALR land also needs Agricultural Land Commission permission to subdivide. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **Q: What servicing costs should I price into a rural lot?** A lot not connected to municipal water and sewer carries well, septic and servicing-bylaw costs: the Township's development servicing forms include Septic Sewer Certification and Well Testing, Fraser Health requires a home not connected to a community sewer to use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person, and the provincial GWELLS registry holds recorded wells (older wells may not appear). https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-servicing.aspx and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: Are all Langley manufactured-home parks 55+?** No. Langley Grove Estates (24330 Fraser Hwy) states it has no age restrictions, and Aldergrove Mobile Home Park (26892 Fraser Hwy) states "NO AGe restrictions" in its listing text — check each park's rules by name, as was done for the four verified parks in Section 2. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/24330-fraser-highway/52 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3104543-21-26892-fraser-highway-langley-british-columbia-v4w3t5-299000/brochure

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two Langleys, two bylaw numbers — and one wrong number in circulation.** The Township's bylaw is 1987, No. 2500; the City's current bylaw is 2025, No. 3300, which repeals the 1996 Bylaw No. 2100. A "Langley City Zoning Bylaw 2023, No. 3200" appears in an earlier program brief but is not confirmed on any City source checked — the City's own hearing notice and bylaw text both say No. 3300. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/public-hearing-noticepdf and https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw
- **The co-op park is a third tenure, different from both strata and pad rental.** At Pioneer Park (27111 0 Ave) the buyer purchases co-op shares and must be accepted by the co-op; at Cedar Creek Estates and Pineridge the MLS tenure is "Leasehold not prepaid-NonStrata." Three Langley 55+ park products, three different purchase mechanics — none of them is freehold land ownership. https://www.estateblock.com/langley/134-27111-ave-1991100 and https://www.estateblock.com/langley/5-3031-200-st-1968212
- **Meeting the minimum lot size does not guarantee a subdivision.** On the City side, Bylaw 3300 states the bylaw does not bind the Approving Officer to approve a subdivision that meets the minimum standards; on the Township side, every subdivision needs the Approving Officer's approval under the Land Title Act in any case. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw and https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **The ALR is the default, not the exception, for Township land.** With 235 km² of the Township in the Agricultural Land Reserve, the ALC's 500 m² principal-residence cap and its 90 m² / 186 m² additional-residence limits sit underneath most rural lot purchases — run the ALC parcel check before pricing any rural lot. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Streamside and tree rules can cut a lot's usable area before zoning does.** The Township's Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Areas cover watercourses outside the ALR (Schedule 3 of the Township Official Community Plan), and a permit is required to cut or remove a protected tree under the Tree Protection Bylaw 2019, No. 5478. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-activity.aspx
- **There are no land-specific market statistics in the SnapStats reports used in this program.** SnapStats publishes no vacant-land category; the Langley figures elsewhere in this research program are residential context only, and land sale counts, price per acre and land days-on-market are UNVERIFIED.
