# Abbotsford Lots & Land — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Scope note: this is a topic page for the City of Abbotsford — a feed of vacant lots and land across all nine board sub-areas — so these notes use city-wide Abbotsford framing with a lots/land angle: how 55+ housing sits on land. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2: a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; every younger age bylaw has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). That bylaw power — including in a bare land strata like Downes Road Estates — is the only legal route to an age rule on owned land in Abbotsford. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts residing, not owning.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted, and a tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home pads are tenancy law, not strata law and not land ownership.** Under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act the buyer owns the home and rents the site; pad rent can be increased only once every 12 months, on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for increases taking effect in 2026) plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. Resale in a park also runs through the park's approval — Maplewood Estates' listings state the buyer must be approved by the park owner. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **ALR housing frame (the rural side of the land feed):** a principal residence on ALR land is capped at 500 m² total floor area with no more than 1,000 m² of fill; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional residence is permitted up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; building beyond those limits requires ALC approval, and local bylaws may be more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Agricultural subdivision scale:** the City's AgRefresh parcel-size work records a minimum subdivision lot area of 8.0 ha in the Agricultural One Zone (A1) and 16.0 ha in the Agricultural Two Zone (A2) — a senior cannot carve a family lot off an A1/A2 acreage below those minimums. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **Suites and added units on non-ALR urban lots:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings (within the principal unit, registered with the City, under 50% of its size); the SSMUH amendment (Bylaw No. 3560-2024, adopted June 25, 2024) counts accessory units toward up to 4 units on one lot — 3 units on lots 280 m² or less, 4 on larger lots; coach houses and garden suites may occupy up to 15% of the lot to a maximum of 100 m². https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites and https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/57884/
- **The City's seniors use class:** the Zoning Bylaw framework defines "Congregate Housing" as an independent living setting with common dining and amenity areas (added by Bylaw 3249-2022). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-abbotsford-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/7217/8514/3396/d9117e5011287c79b090adc71e32c234_Appendix_B_-_Draft_ZB_Amendments.pdf
- **The governing plan:** Abbotsforward 2050, the Official Community Plan (Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated); zoning, future land use and development permit areas for a specific lot are checked through the City's property information search or WebMap. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/development-planning

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Abbotsford that is "55+"?** Not as a fee-simple lot — no source records an age restriction attaching to a vacant lot itself. The age rule lives in a strata's bylaws (as at Downes Road Estates, a bare land strata) or in a manufactured-home park's rules (Paradise Park, Georgian Park, Maplewood Estates). Outside those two structures, a lot carries no 55+ rule. Sources: Sections 2 and 3.
- **Q: At Downes Road Estates, what do I own?** A bare land strata lot (plan LMS2380) and the rancher home on it, inside a gated 52-home strata corporation whose listings record "Age Restricted 55+", pets allowed with restrictions and rentals allowed. Strata bylaws, fees and common property apply — it is not a fee-simple subdivision. https://www.estateblock.com/abbotsford/16-32250-downes-rd-518712
- **Q: In a manufactured-home park, what do I actually own — and what happens to the pad rent?** The home only; the pad is rented under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. Pad rent can rise only once a year, on at least three full months' notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus the proportional levy/utility amount. One Georgian Park listing quoted $782/month pad rent for a specific home — listing-stated, confirm in the tenancy documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/3300-horn-street/55
- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on my own acreage instead of a park?** That is a zoning, ALR and servicing question, not a 55+ question: on ALR land the residence frame (500 m² principal-residence cap, suite only inside it, 90 m² / 186 m² additional-residence tiers) and City zoning both apply, and servicing (well, septic, health authority approval) is per-parcel — UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel until the City/ALC check is run. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I subdivide my Abbotsford acreage for my kids?** On agricultural zones, the City's parcel-size work sets subdivision minimums of 8.0 ha (A1) and 16.0 ha (A2), and ALR subdivision also engages the ALC. Urban residential zone minimums are UNVERIFIED from the bylaw text in this research — confirm the specific zone's minimum with the City for the parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **Q: If I build on a lot and age in place, can I add a suite or coach house?** On a non-ALR urban lot, yes within the frame: one secondary suite inside the principal dwelling (registered, under 50% of its size), and coach/garden suites up to 15% of the lot to a maximum of 100 m², under the SSMUH amendment's 3-to-4-unit counts. On ALR land the suite must sit inside the principal residence. https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — does that block us at Downes Road Estates or in a park?** For the strata, no: Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident. For a manufactured-home park, the age rule is the park's own tenancy rule, not the Strata Property Act — the exemption structure above does not automatically apply, so read the park's rules for the specific park: UNCONFIRMED at park level in the sources checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How do I check a lot's floodplain, zoning and plan designation?** Through the City's property information search or a property search in WebMap — the route the City's Development Planning page itself publishes for current zoning, future land uses and development permit areas. No feed parcel's status is verified in this file. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/development-planning
- **Q: What down payment and GST should I budget for a lot?** Neither is stated here: the live page gives two different down-payment passages and a GST summary, and all are UNVERIFIED in this research — no CRA or lender source was read. Have a lender and a lawyer confirm both for the specific purchase. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-lots-land.html
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale market in a bare land strata or park?** In the strata, mechanically yes — the next resident must meet the bylaw or an exemption. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Abbotsford's only land-owning 55+ form is one bare land strata.** Of 26 verified 55+ strata complexes, Downes Road Estates (LMS2380, 52 rancher homes) is the single bare land strata; the other three land-based options are parks where the land is never owned at all. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The "land" feed spans two unrelated products.** The underlying research records the visible feed running from small serviced urban lots of roughly 4,400–4,810 sq ft to rural parcels of 8.5 to 19.49 acres — listing-card snapshots, not statistics — with subdivision scale on the agricultural side set by the A1 (8.0 ha) and A2 (16.0 ha) minimums. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-lots-land.html and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **There is no published land market to price against.** SnapStats has no vacant-land category, so the Abbotsford residential medians sometimes quoted beside land listings ($949,000 detached; $497,500 attached, September 2026) are housing-market context only — a lot is priced on its zone, services and comparable land sales. (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report)

Sources: Section 2 above.
