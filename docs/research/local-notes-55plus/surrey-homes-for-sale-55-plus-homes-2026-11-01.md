## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. (Glenwood Village's older "45+" description is exactly this kind of superseded wording.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. Several Surrey listings state the local form of this as "one occupant must be 55+" — the registered bylaw for the specific complex governs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. Directory lines for Surrey complexes that say "rentals not allowed" (Manor Ridge, Semiahmoo Estates) predate or ignore this — confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **OCP — STANDING CORRECTION:** the current plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900, finally adopted June 15, 2026. Bylaw No. 18020 (2013) is superseded and is never the current OCP. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Zoning and suites (Zoning By-law No. 12000):** one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. There is NO current 90 m² suite cap in the consolidated bylaw text — never quote one as current. A coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), with one extra parking space required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **SSMUH:** Surrey's zoning allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the July 8, 2024 SSMUH changes renamed the former RF-12 / RF-12C zones to the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone and RF-SD to R6 Semi-Detached. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey only — White Rock has its own rules):** a short-term rental is under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required first, under Zoning By-law No. 12000, Part 4, s. B.7(d) and Business License By-law No. 13680, s. 60.1. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Manufactured-home pad rules (the three roster parks, and NOT Sea Ridge Bays):** under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act framework, a pad rent can be increased once every 12 months on three full months' notice, by at most the annual limit — for 2026, 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. Sea Ridge Bays is a bare-land strata, so its owners pay strata fees under the Strata Property Act instead of pad rent — do not apply pad rules to it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Membership tenure is its own rule set:** Sullivan Mews is not a strata — there is no strata lot, and its listings state conventional mortgages are not possible. Financing, transfer and occupancy rules come from the membership documents, not the Strata Property Act. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many 55+ complexes are there in Surrey?** Answer it with the flag, never as a bare number: the consolidated roster is 15 strata complexes + Sullivan Mews (membership) + 3 manufactured-home parks, PLUS two verified complexes outside the roster (Camberley Green in Whalley and The Greens at Douglas in Pacific Douglas) PLUS Sea Ridge Bays as its own bare-land strata category — "15 + flagged additions pending re-consolidation." Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a Surrey 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Several Surrey complexes state it as "1 occupant must be 55+" (Manor Ridge, Lakewood Heights) — the specific complex's registered bylaw governs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy for a parent, or to hold?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside, not who may own. A 55+ parent can be the resident of a unit owned by a younger buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ Surrey building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals; in Surrey a short-term rental also needs the owner-occupier business licence in Section 3. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What is the difference between the parks, Sea Ridge Bays, and a strata townhouse?** In Westwood Court, Westwood Estates and Newton Mobile Home Park you own the home and rent the pad — pad increases are capped (2026: 2.3% plus the proportional levy/utility amount) and the home sits on land you do not own. At Sea Ridge Bays you own the bare-land strata lot itself and pay a strata fee instead of pad rent. In a strata townhouse you own the strata lot and a share of the common property. Three different rule books — confirm which one a listing is actually selling. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and https://realtylink.org/en/print/property/263120958
- **Q: Can I get a mortgage on Sullivan Mews?** Its own listings say no: "This is not Strata. It is a membership therefore not mortgages possible." Treat financing as a membership-document question and confirm it with the society and a lender before writing an offer — do not assume a strata-condo mortgage works. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** It runs the full range inside Surrey's verified stock: Carriage House listings say no pets permitted; Semiahmoo Estates' directory records pets not allowed; Charleston Green and Westwood Court record pets welcome / allowed; Manor Ridge allows 1 pet with restrictions in the listing checked. There is no Surrey-wide answer — read the specific complex's bylaws. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is there any 55+ stock near City Centre / SkyTrain?** On the verified roster, no: City Centre / Whalley tower stock checked in this series is general-market, and Bear Creek Green Timbers and Guildford have zero verified 55+ ownership complexes. The one flagged addition in the north is Camberley Green (10172 141 St, Whalley filing) — a low-rise gated rancher complex, not a tower. The nearest roster complexes to the centre are in Fleetwood and Newton. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Where are the hospitals?** Surrey Memorial Hospital (13750 96 Ave) is the city's regional hospital with 24/7 emergency; the Surrey-Newton UPCC is at 6830 King George Blvd; Peace Arch Hospital (15521 Russell Ave, White Rock) serves South Surrey. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Surrey/surrey-memorial-hospital
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or a buyer who will not live in the unit. Surrey's stock is mostly small 1980s–1990s complexes, so the pool effect is felt complex by complex, not city-wide. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Surrey's roster is in transition, and the transition is the story.** Two complexes verified in later passes (Camberley Green, The Greens at Douglas) never made it into the consolidated count of 15, and a third (Sea Ridge Bays) does not fit either the strata-townhouse or the pad-park box. Any page, post or pitch that states a single clean Surrey 55+ total is ahead of the evidence — the honest form is "15 + flagged additions pending re-consolidation." Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The newest verified 55+ strata in Surrey are at opposite ends of the city and a generation apart in form:** Fleetwood Gardens (condo building, built 2005, Fleetwood) on the roster, and The Greens at Douglas (townhouses, built 2012–2013, Pacific Douglas) outside it. Everything else verified is 2003 or older, and most of it is 1986–1996 gated townhouse / rancher stock. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Boundary Park in Newton holds three separate verified 55+ complexes on two adjoining drives** — Lakeview Estates (6090 W Boundary Dr, filed West Newton), Lakewood Heights (6211 W Boundary Dr) and Lakewood Place (6061 W Boundary Dr, both filed Panorama Ridge). Similar names, separate stratas, separate bylaws and fees — a search on "Lakewood" alone mixes two of them, and a search on the drive alone mixes all three. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Two of Surrey's care residences carry another city's name or a misleading one.** "Amica White Rock" is at 15333 16 Ave, Surrey; Whitecliff's operator address (15501 16 Ave, V4A 9M5) is also on the Surrey side. Both sit in South Surrey, a short distance from Peace Arch Hospital in White Rock — the name describes the market, not the municipality, and the municipality decides which bylaws and tax calendar apply. https://www.amica.ca/white-rock and https://www.cogirseniorliving.ca/retirement-home-south-surrey-british-columbia/whitecliff
- **The Crescent's tenure is contradicted inside its own listings** — "Freehold NonStrata" in one, Freehold Strata LMS4241 in the building record and other listings. On a 57-home complex, that is not a footnote: it changes the documents, the financing conversation and which Act governs. Settle it from the strata plan and Form B before relying on either version. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/15-3500-144-st-69975
- **Westwood Estates' park address is stated two ways in current listings (8224 and 8254 134 Street)**, and its pad rents differ by listing ($822/mo and $935/mo in the listings checked, different units and dates). Neither figure is "the" park rate — the park office and the tenancy agreement for the specific pad are the sources. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3121759-103-8224-134-street-surrey-british-columbia-v3w4v1-249800/brochure
- **Surrey has no verified 55+ market-rental tower.** A senior who wants to rent with an age rule in Surrey is looking at care residences, society housing (Bethshan Gardens, Guru Nanak Niwas) or a park pad — the age-restricted ownership stock is where the supply actually is. Sources: Section 2 above.
