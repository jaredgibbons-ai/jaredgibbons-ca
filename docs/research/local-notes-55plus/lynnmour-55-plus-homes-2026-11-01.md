# Lynnmour, District of North Vancouver — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Geography note (read first):** the research file's verified geography places Lynnmour in the **District of North Vancouver** — Lynnmour North and Lynnmour South, also known in the community as Inter River and Seylynn, within the wider Lynn Creek area. It is not in the City of North Vancouver. This file is titled accordingly. https://www.sd44.ca/media/nvsd/nvsd/content-assets/documents/board-of-education/Letter-to-Minister-Bowinn-Ma---Lynnmour-Replacement---Nov-2025.pdf

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Lynnmour sits on the flat-to-gently-sloped bench between Lynn Creek (west) and the Seymour River (east), from the mountain slopes down to Highway 1 and the Second Narrows crossing at its south end — per the area research file.
- The District itself identifies the area as Lynnmour North and Lynnmour South, also known as the Inter River and Seylynn neighbourhoods, within the wider Lynn Creek area. https://www.sd44.ca/media/nvsd/nvsd/content-assets/documents/board-of-education/Letter-to-Minister-Bowinn-Ma---Lynnmour-Replacement---Nov-2025.pdf
- Main streets buyers search are Lillooet Road, Mountain Highway and Oxford Street. Capilano University's main campus is in the neighbourhood at 2055 Purcell Way. https://www.capilanou.ca/
- Lynnmour's south end forms part of the Lynn Creek Town Centre, one of the centres where the District's OCP directs growth; the Lynn Creek Community Recreation Centre at 1491 Hunter Street (at Mountain Highway) opened February 19, 2025, fronting Seylynn Park. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf and https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/community-recreation-centres/lynn-creek-community-recreation
- Inter River Park, at 1301 Lillooet Road west of Capilano University, serves the north end with sports fields, an outdoor lacrosse box, a BMX bike park, off-leash dog areas and forest trails; the Lower Seymour Conservation Reserve trails begin at the end of Lillooet Road. https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=3383857 and https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/Pages/Lower-Seymour-Conservation-Reserve.aspx
- Nearby areas: Maplewood (east, across the Seymour River), Lynn Valley (north), Lower Lynn / Lynn Creek Town Centre (southwest), and the City of North Vancouver (west, across Lynn Creek) — per the area research file. Note that Lynn Valley, where the North Shore's Kiwanis seniors buildings sit, is a different neighbourhood north of Lynnmour.
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: **nowhere in Lynnmour, on any source checked.** No source places any 55+ strata, 55+ rental, retirement residence, assisted-living residence or subsidized seniors building inside Lynnmour North or South. The seniors buildings that appear in "Lynnmour" searches are in Lynn Valley (Whiteley Court) — see Section 2's checked list.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This matters in Lynnmour, where much of the stock is investor- and Capilano University-oriented: a Lynnmour strata cannot ban long-term rentals, whatever its older bylaws say. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **District zoning: Zoning Bylaw 3210** is the District of North Vancouver's zoning bylaw; as amended by Bylaw 8698 (June 2024) it allows a main house, secondary suite and coach house on most single-family lots. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/new-provincial-housing-legislation-fact-sheet.pdf
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw 7900** framework — Lynnmour's south end is part of the Lynn Creek Town Centre, one of the four centres where the District's OCP directs 75–90% of residential growth; Phibbs Exchange and the Capilano University area were designated Transit-Oriented Areas under Bylaw 8679 (2024). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/new-provincial-housing-legislation-fact-sheet.pdf
- **Secondary suites and coach houses:** the District's fact sheet states Zoning Bylaw 3210 (as amended by Bylaw 8698, June 2024) allows a main house, secondary suite and coach house on most single-family lots, and December 2025 Ground-Oriented Housing rules permit up to four homes on more than 4,000 eligible District properties — the realistic route to a caregiver suite on an Inter River detached lot, since there is no 55+ building to buy into. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/new-provincial-housing-legislation-fact-sheet.pdf
- **Flood/creek hazard checking is essential on the bench between the creeks:** the District designates Natural Hazard Development Permit Areas and publishes property-level hazard reports through its GeoWeb viewer; the low ground between Lynn Creek and the Seymour River is where flood and creek-hazard mapping matters most. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/bgclandslideriskassessment2026updatereportfinal.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ home I can buy in Lynnmour?** No verified one. No public source checked states a 55+ age-restriction bylaw for any Lynnmour strata — not the Seylynn Village towers, not Hunter at Lynn Creek, not the older Mountain Highway / Oxford Street low-rises named in the research file. A buyer who needs a legally age-restricted building should look at verified 55+ buildings in other North Shore neighbourhoods, or buy general-market in Lynnmour without the age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I saw Kiwanis seniors housing in "North Vancouver" — is it in Lynnmour?** No. Kiwanis Lynn Manor (2555 Whiteley Court, 65+) and Kiwanis Lynn Woods (2551 Whiteley Court, 60+, market and subsidized) are in Lynn Valley, per the Kiwanis North Shore Housing Society's own pages — north of Lynnmour, a separate neighbourhood. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/lynn-manor/ and https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/lynn-woods/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata (elsewhere on the North Shore)?** Yes in the provincial exemption cases — a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt, as are live-in caregivers, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children the resident cared for before 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Lynnmour condo — to Capilano University students, for example?** On the rental-ban question, yes: every strata rental-restriction bylaw in BC became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, so a Lynnmour strata cannot prohibit long-term rentals (short-term rental bylaws are still allowed). Building-specific rules, parking and the university's September intake cycle still need checking per building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.capilanou.ca/
- **Q: Which schools serve Lynnmour?** Lynnmour Elementary is the only school serving the Lynn Creek area (Lynnmour North and South); its current building dates to 1973, enrolment reached 256 students in September 2025, and SD44 is seeking a full replacement on seismic and condition grounds. For the sample address 1491 Hunter Street, the SD44 locator returns Lynnmour Elementary (K–7) and **Windsor Secondary (8–12)** — not Sutherland Secondary, which an earlier research draft named without locator confirmation. Confirm any other address with SD44. https://www.sd44.ca/media/nvsd/nvsd/content-assets/documents/board-of-education/Letter-to-Minister-Bowinn-Ma---Lynnmour-Replacement---Nov-2025.pdf
- **Q: Where is the recreation centre and library service?** The Lynn Creek Community Recreation Centre, 1491 Hunter Street at Mountain Highway, opened February 19, 2025 — an NVRC-run hub fronting Seylynn Park with licensed childcare operated by Parkgate Society inside, a public plaza, and an NVDPL library kiosk inside the centre. https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/community-recreation-centres/lynn-creek-community-recreation and https://www.nvdpl.ca/lynn-creek-kiosk
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day?** Phibbs Exchange, immediately south across Highway 1, is the North Shore's eastern transit hub — the R2 RapidBus runs from Phibbs to Park Royal and, since September 7, 2026, through to Metrotown Station, every 6–7 minutes at peak; local routes include the 245 Phibbs Exchange–Capilano University and the 227 Lynn Valley–Phibbs Exchange along Mountain Highway. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- **Q: Is the low bench between the creeks a flood concern?** It is exactly where the District's creek/flood hazard mapping applies — pull the property's GeoWeb hazard report before subject removal on any low-lying lot between Lynn Creek and the Seymour River. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/bgclandslideriskassessment2026updatereportfinal.pdf
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to an Inter River house for a caregiver or adult child?** The District's rules allow a main house, secondary suite and coach house on most single-family lots under Zoning Bylaw 3210 as amended by Bylaw 8698 (June 2024), with December 2025 Ground-Oriented Housing rules permitting up to four homes on more than 4,000 eligible District properties — confirm the specific lot, and its hazard permit areas, with the District. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/new-provincial-housing-legislation-fact-sheet.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Lynnmour seniors housing" in search results is almost always Lynn Valley.** The Whiteley Court Kiwanis campus (Lynn Manor, operating since 1974 per the Province's 2019 release, plus the 106-unit Lynn Woods beside it) is the North Shore's big subsidized seniors cluster — and it is in Lynn Valley, not Lynnmour. The two neighbourhoods share the "Lynn" name and little else for housing purposes. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2017-2021/2019MAH0181-002254.pdf
- **Lynnmour Elementary is an end-of-life building with an active replacement campaign.** SD44's November 2025 letter to the Minister states the current school dates to 1973, enrolment reached 256 in September 2025, and the district's capital plan seeks a full replacement on seismic and condition grounds — a material fact for families, and for anyone pricing an Inter River home off the school. https://www.sd44.ca/media/nvsd/nvsd/content-assets/documents/board-of-education/Letter-to-Minister-Bowinn-Ma---Lynnmour-Replacement---Nov-2025.pdf
- **The secondary catchment correction matters for resale copy.** An earlier research draft named Sutherland Secondary as Lynnmour's catchment secondary; the SD44 locator returns Windsor Secondary for the in-area sample address (1491 Hunter Street). Listing copy that promises "Sutherland catchment" for a Lynnmour home would be wrong on the only locator evidence in hand. https://www.sd44.ca/media/nvsd/nvsd/content-assets/documents/board-of-education/Letter-to-Minister-Bowinn-Ma---Lynnmour-Replacement---Nov-2025.pdf
- **Supply is about to reset at the south end.** District council approved the Seylynn Centre project on October 20, 2025 — two towers adding nearly 700 homes at the Lynn Creek Town Centre — on top of the Hunter at Lynn Creek and Seylynn Village towers already standing. None of it is age-restricted; it is general-market supply that will set the pricing context for every older Lynnmour low-rise. https://www.sd44.ca/media/nvsd/nvsd/content-assets/documents/board-of-education/Letter-to-Minister-Bowinn-Ma---Lynnmour-Replacement---Nov-2025.pdf
- **The recreation centre is brand new and was a developer-delivered amenity.** The Lynn Creek Community Recreation Centre opened February 19, 2025, delivered as one component of Intergulf's Hunter at Lynn Creek development along the southern edge of Seylynn Park — so the neighbourhood's main indoor hub is newer than most of its condo stock. https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/community-recreation-centres/lynn-creek-community-recreation and https://www.nvrc.ca:443/sites/default/files/2022-09/lynn_creek_cc_-_call_to_artists_final_1.pdf
- **District taxes for budgeting:** District of North Vancouver council approved a 3.53% municipal tax increase for 2026; the published 2026 Class 1 (residential) total rate is $3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value, taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **The reserve at the end of Lillooet Road is a water-supply landscape, not a normal park.** The Lower Seymour Conservation Reserve is 5,668 hectares managed by Metro Vancouver primarily for water supply, with more than 100 km of trails and the stocked Rice Lake fishing lake, drawing over 900,000 visitors a year — the trail network Lynnmour residents use daily starts just past Capilano University. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/Pages/Lower-Seymour-Conservation-Reserve.aspx
