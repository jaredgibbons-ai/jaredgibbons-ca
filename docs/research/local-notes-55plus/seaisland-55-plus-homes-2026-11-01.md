# Sea Island (Richmond) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Sea Island is a separate island in the Fraser River, between Lulu Island (the main part of Richmond) to the south and east and the City of Vancouver across the North Arm to the north. "Sea Island" is both the GVR board sub-area name and a City of Richmond planning area — planning area 12 on the City Official Community Plan Key Map. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/CNCL_062209_Bylaw_840323090.pdf
- The island is dominated by Vancouver International Airport (YVR) on federal land; the only residential pocket is Burkeville, in the south-east part of the island near Miller Road and Russ Baker Way. The Vancouver Airport Authority states the airport occupies federal property within the City of Richmond. https://www.yvr.ca/en/about-yvr/community/engagement and https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/CNCL_062209_Bylaw_840323090.pdf
- Nearby areas: Bridgeport (Richmond, across the Moray Channel / Dinsmore Bridge), Terra Nova (Richmond, across the Middle Arm), and Vancouver (across the North Arm via the Arthur Laing Bridge). https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/CNCL_062209_Bylaw_840323090.pdf
- Roads and bridges: Grant McConachie Way serves the airport terminals, Russ Baker Way runs south to the Dinsmore Bridge and Richmond, and Miller Road runs through Burkeville; bridges off the island are the Arthur Laing Bridge (to Vancouver), the Moray Channel Bridge, the Dinsmore Bridge and the No. 2 Road Bridge. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/CNCL_062209_Bylaw_840323090.pdf
- Transit: the Canada Line airport branch serves Sea Island at Templeton, Sea Island Centre and YVR-Airport stations, with a change at Bridgeport Station for Richmond–Brighouse branch trains. Local bus route numbers for Burkeville streets: UNVERIFIED. https://www.translink.ca/rider-guide/visiting-vancouver
- Schools: there is no operating public school on Sea Island — Sea Island Elementary School on Wellington Crescent has closed. School District 38 catchments are address-based with no middle-school tier; for the sample address 7140 Miller Road (Sea Island Community Centre, Burkeville) the SD38 locator gives Brighouse Elementary and Richmond Secondary. https://www.richmondmuseum.ca/locations/sea-island-school/ and https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- Parks and recreation: Burkeville Park and the Sea Island Community Centre are at 7140 Miller Road; Larry Berg Flight Path Park is on Russ Baker Way; McDonald Beach Park is at the end of McDonald Road; Iona Beach Regional Park is at 900 Ferguson Road at the north-west end of the island. https://www.richmondmuseum.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Sea-Island_Self-Guided-Historical-Tour-Brochure-1.pdf
- Library and hospital: there is no public library branch on Sea Island in Richmond Public Library's branch list, and the nearest hospital is Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway, on Lulu Island. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/about-bc-s-health-care-system/heath-care-partners/hospitallist.pdf
- Where the seniors housing actually is: **nowhere on the island.** Sea Island's entire housing stock is the Burkeville pocket of single-detached houses; no seniors building of any tenure — strata, rental, retirement, assisted living, care or subsidized — has an address on Sea Island in any source checked. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. On Sea Island this is a filter rule only: no strata stock was verified on the island for such a bylaw to attach to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Who zones what on Sea Island:** most of the island is federal airport land governed by the YVR land use plan and federal Airport Zoning Regulations (administered through Transport Canada, including maximum building heights, so aircraft can operate safely) rather than Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500. The exact Richmond zoning bylaw zone and minimum lot size applying to Burkeville lots: UNVERIFIED. https://news.yvr.ca/statement-regarding-airport-zoning-regulations/ and https://news.yvr.ca/yvrs-new-master-plan-has-arrived/
- **Aircraft noise is a standing planning file, not background colour:** the City of Richmond's Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development (ANSD) Policy Area framework (City Bulletin DEVAPPS-05, "Aircraft and Land Use Noise") applies to noise-sensitive development in the airport's vicinity, and YVR manages noise through its 2025–2029 Noise Management Plan and publishes annual noise reports with monitoring data. Burkeville is the closest residential pocket to the runways — check a specific lot against the published noise material. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/devapps0534378.pdf and https://www.yvr.ca/en/about-yvr/noise-management/reports
- **Plan:** Sea Island is planning area 12 in the City Official Community Plan framework for the Burkeville pocket, while airport-side changes follow the federal / YVR process under the YVR 2037 Master Plan, the Airport Authority's plan for land use decision-making and facility development at YVR, approved by the federal Minister of Transport. https://news.yvr.ca/yvrs-new-master-plan-has-arrived/
- **Flood — a dike-protected island inside a dike-protected city:** Richmond is made up of low-lying islands in the Fraser River floodplain, protected by a perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations — 49 km of dikes and 39 pump stations city-wide, designed for a 1:500 flood event, with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres under way for sea level rise. The Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets flood construction level requirements; confirm the lot's requirements with the City before planning a rebuild. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **Property taxes:** City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026; the city-wide rule applies to Burkeville homes. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy on Sea Island?** None with an age restriction — there is no 55+ strata, and no strata complex of any kind was verified on the island. What you can buy is a Burkeville detached house: general-market, no age bylaw, in the island's only residential pocket. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is there any seniors rental, assisted living or care on the island if I need it later?** None verified. BC's Assisted Living Registry lists Richmond residences only on Lulu Island (Moncton Street, Garripie Avenue, Moffatt Road), and BC Housing's Zone 3 directory lists no Sea Island seniors development — the care and subsidized steps both mean crossing a bridge to Lulu Island. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=city%3ARichmond&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc and https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: How loud is Burkeville, honestly?** No source in this pass gives a Burkeville-specific noise figure — treat any number as UNCONFIRMED. What is documented: YVR's 2025–2029 Noise Management Plan covers land use planning near the airport, YVR publishes annual noise reports with monitoring data, and the City's ANSD policy framework (Bulletin DEVAPPS-05) governs noise-sensitive development near the airport. Read the lot against those published sources, and note the federal Airport Zoning Regulations also cap building heights around the airport, which affects additions and rebuilds. https://www.yvr.ca/en/about-yvr/noise-management/reports and https://news.yvr.ca/statement-regarding-airport-zoning-regulations/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If we buy into a 55+ strata on Lulu Island, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I add a suite in Burkeville for a caregiver or family member?** UNCONFIRMED for Burkeville specifically: the exact Richmond zoning zone and minimum lot size for Burkeville lots were not confirmed in this pass (most of the island is federal airport land under the YVR plan and federal airport zoning), so suite potential is a lot-by-lot City confirmation, not an assumption. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- **Q: What transit and services does a Burkeville senior actually use?** The Canada Line airport branch (Templeton, Sea Island Centre, YVR-Airport stations) is on the island; everything else — hospital (Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway), library branches, grocery (a grocery store on Sea Island: UNVERIFIED — no official source confirms one) — is on Lulu Island. https://www.translink.ca/rider-guide/visiting-vancouver and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/about-bc-s-health-care-system/heath-care-partners/hospitallist.pdf
- **Q: Is Sea Island in a floodplain?** Richmond's islands sit in the Fraser River floodplain behind the City dike system (49 km of dikes, 39 pump stations, designed for a 1:500 event, dike raising from 3.5 m to 4.7 m under way), and Bylaw 8204 flood construction levels apply to new construction — confirm the lot's level before planning a rebuild. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **Q: Which schools would grandchildren use?** There is no operating school on the island (Sea Island Elementary on Wellington Crescent has closed); SD38 catchments are address-based, and the locator result for 7140 Miller Road is Brighouse Elementary and Richmond Secondary — both off-island, checked per exact address. https://www.richmondmuseum.ca/locations/sea-island-school/ and https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Burkeville was built in three designs for aviation workers** — the City Archives/museum material describes a pocket of single-detached houses in three designs, and states Burkeville is the only remaining population centre on Sea Island; the island was once mostly farm land with small communities including Eburne and Burkeville, before the Vancouver Airport opened on it. The "three designs" fact is the real story of the housing stock — there is no second housing era to shop in. https://www.richmondmuseum.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Sea-Island_Self-Guided-Historical-Tour-Brochure-1.pdf and http://richmondarchives.ca/tag/planning/
- **Two governments plan this island, and only one of them answers to City Hall.** The Burkeville pocket sits inside the City's OCP framework (planning area 12), but senior governments are not regulated by the City OCP — airport-side change follows the federal/YVR process under the YVR 2037 Master Plan. A buyer asking "what is the City planning next door?" may be asking about land the City does not plan. https://news.yvr.ca/yvrs-new-master-plan-has-arrived/
- **The island's recreation anchor is a single community centre:** the Sea Island Community Centre at 7140 Miller Road, with Burkeville Park — plus the flight-path and beach parks (Larry Berg Flight Path Park on Russ Baker Way, McDonald Beach Park, Iona Beach Regional Park at 900 Ferguson Road). There is no seniors centre, library or clinic building on the island in any source checked. https://www.richmondmuseum.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Sea-Island_Self-Guided-Historical-Tour-Brochure-1.pdf
- **Height limits here come from Ottawa, not Richmond:** federal Airport Zoning Regulations administered through Transport Canada set zoning in areas surrounding the airport, including maximum building heights — on a Burkeville lot that can bind additions, rebuilds and even tree height, a due-diligence item most Richmond buyers never meet. https://news.yvr.ca/statement-regarding-airport-zoning-regulations/
- **The dike is part of the utility bill:** Richmond's 2026 typical single-family metered utility total of $2,251 per year includes a $342 flood protection line — Burkeville homes pay annually for the dike system like every Richmond home. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/finance/utility-fees/utilityrates.htm
