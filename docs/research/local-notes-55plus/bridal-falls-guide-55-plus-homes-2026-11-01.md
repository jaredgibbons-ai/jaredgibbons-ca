# Bridal Falls (FVRD Electoral Area D) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

> **⚠️ GEOGRAPHY FLAG — READ FIRST: Bridal Falls is NOT in the City of Chilliwack.** It is a small unincorporated community in Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area D (Popkum–Bridal Falls), east of Chilliwack near Rosedale and Popkum. The FVRD — not the City — is its local government for planning and zoning, so City of Chilliwack zoning, Chilliwack tax penalties and the Chilliwack 55+ roster do **not** apply here. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **There is no age bylaw on a freehold rural home, and none was found on the one strata product here.** Strata age law only bites inside a strata corporation with a registered 55+ bylaw; no source checked states such a bylaw for the resort at 53480 Bridal Falls Road, and its registered rules are UNVERIFIED (Section 2). A senior buying in Bridal Falls faces FVRD zoning, servicing and — where it applies — Agricultural Land Reserve rules, not a 55+ residency bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **If the same buyer instead buys 55+ strata elsewhere in the region, the provincial frame is this:** under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it — people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01), wherever a 55+ bylaw does apply:** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide in strata.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This matters at the Bridal Falls resort precisely because its listings market rental use: the registered bylaws, not the listing text, settle what rental use is actually permitted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Local government: the FVRD, not the City.** Bridal Falls is in FVRD Electoral Area D (Popkum–Bridal Falls); the FVRD provides planning and development approvals, building inspection and bylaw enforcement among its electoral-area services, while member municipalities govern themselves — City of Chilliwack zoning does not apply in Bridal Falls. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bridal-falls-guide.html
- **Community plan:** the guide page states the Popkum-Bridal Falls (Area D) Official Community Plan, FVRD Bylaw No. 1501, was adopted by the FVRD Board in January 2021, after a public hearing on December 15, 2020. The bylaw text itself was not opened on an FVRD source in the research pass, so the bylaw number and adoption details are UNVERIFIED against the FVRD record — confirm with the FVRD before relying on them. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bridal-falls-guide.html
- **Zoning:** the FVRD sets zoning for Electoral Area D, but the specific FVRD zoning bylaw and the zone of any Bridal Falls parcel were not confirmed on an FVRD source in the research pass: UNVERIFIED. One listing at the resort states CD-1 zoning allows a cottage home or newer recreational vehicle on its lot — listing text for one lot, not a zoning confirmation for the community. Confirm any parcel's zone with the FVRD. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bridal-falls-guide.html and https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3103511-53480-bridal-falls-road-86-chilliwack-bc-v0x-1x1/brochure
- **ALR housing frame, for any parcel in the Agricultural Land Reserve:** the principal residence maximum is a total floor area of 500 m²; a secondary suite is permitted only when it is entirely contained within the principal residence and local bylaws permit it; and one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application at up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, subject to local bylaws. Parcel-level ALR status for any specific Bridal Falls property: UNVERIFIED — confirm on the ALC's ALR maps. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Flood and hazard:** the research file states the published records it read do not list an FVRD or BC document stating a flood or debris-flow hazard for the Bridal Falls area. Parcel-level flood or hazard mapping for any Bridal Falls property: UNVERIFIED — check site-specific hazard mapping with the FVRD before building or buying. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bridal-falls-guide.html
- **Property taxes — the RURAL regime applies here, not any city's.** Because Bridal Falls is unincorporated FVRD ground, its taxes are levied under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s. 11: taxes are due July 2, a 5% penalty is added to taxes unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% penalty is added to taxes still unpaid on October 31. Do not apply the City of Chilliwack penalty dates (5% after July 2, 2026 + 5% after August 4, 2026) across the municipal line — they do not govern here. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **GST on a brand-new cottage or home:** the resort's stock includes newly built cottages, and new homes carry GST where resale homes do not. Two federal rebates are verified from the CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 (April 2026): (1) the GST/HST new housing rebate — generally 36% of the GST or federal part of the HST paid, to a maximum of $6,300, gradually reduced for homes valued between $350,000 and $450,000 and unavailable at $450,000 or more; and (2) the first-time home buyers' (FTHB) GST/HST rebate under Bill C-4, which received royal assent on March 13, 2026 — together with the new housing rebate it rebates 100% of the GST on a newly constructed or substantially renovated home valued up to $1 million, gradually reduced between $1 million and $1.5 million and unavailable at $1.5 million or more, for purchase agreements in the March 20, 2025–2031 window. Whether either rebate applies to a bare-land strata resort lot or a cottage on one is a tax-advice question for the specific purchase: UNVERIFIED for this product type in this file. Home Buyers' Plan and First Home Savings Account figures: UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Almost the whole visible market at this exit is one address.** Four of the five listing cards read on the guide page sit at 53480 Bridal Falls Road — the resort — and the fifth is a house in Rosedale. A "Bridal Falls market" conversation is, on the feed evidence, mostly a conversation about one bare-land strata resort's lots and cottages, plus rural homes in the neighbouring communities. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bridal-falls-guide.html
- **The falls themselves are a hazard site, not just a postcard.** As stated on the guide page citing BC Parks, the base of Bridal Veil Falls is extremely hazardous, with slippery footing and falling rock and ice, and the falls are prone to freezing in colder winters, forming an unstable wall of ice; the park became a Provincial Park in 1965 and the falls generated electricity for the Bridal Falls Chalet in the early 1900s. Direct verification on BC Parks is UNVERIFIED (the host returned a 429 in the research pass). https://jaredgibbons.ca/bridal-falls-guide.html
- **The nearest regional park bans pets outright.** Cheam Lake Wetlands Regional Park, the 107-hectare FVRD park in Electoral Area D with 184 recorded bird species, does not permit pets or other domestic animals at all, under FVRD Bylaw No. 1500, 2018 — a real adjustment for a dog-owning buyer who pictures daily walks there. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/cheam-lake-wetlands.html?media=standard
- **Two different "resorts" share the Bridal Falls name and exit.** The buyable bare-land strata community at 53480 Bridal Falls Road and Camperland RV Resort at 53730 Bridal Falls Rd are different operations — one is strata ownership, the other is an RV campground. Search results mix them freely. https://www.tripadvisor.ie/Hotel_Review-g2578537-d8530426-Reviews-Camperland_RV_Resort_Bridal_Falls-Rosedale_Chilliwack_British_Columbia.html
- **"Mostly senior occupied" is doing quiet work in the nearest park's marketing.** The Mt Cheam park listing in Rosedale pairs "Mostly Senior Occupied" with "Families are ALLOWED" in the same paragraph — the park is not age-restricted, and a buyer who stops reading at the first phrase will misread the product. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3089923-52604-yale-road-10-rosedale-bc-v0x-1x1/brochure
- **The guide page's own framing does not match its feed.** The guide's "What to look at" cards divide the stock into rural homes and a "Townhomes and condos — strata living" card, but no townhome or condo card appears among the five feed cards read — the strata product the feed actually shows is bare-land strata resort lots. A 55+ buyer landing on the guide expecting condo stock will not find it here. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bridal-falls-guide.html
- **There are no published statistics to price against here.** SnapStats publishes no Chilliwack or FVRD figures and CADREB publishes no area stats held in this program, so Bridal Falls pricing evidence is the community's own recent sales and live competition — not a median borrowed from Chilliwack or the Fraser Valley. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bridal-falls-guide.html
