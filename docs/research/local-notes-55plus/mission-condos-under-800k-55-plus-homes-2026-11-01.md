## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. This is the rule that decides the Briskham Manor "35+" advertisement flagged in Section 2: whatever that building's bylaw once said, only a 55+ restriction can be enforced today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. Cherry Lane's listing wording "55+ Community with Family Exemptions" points at this same framework — confirm the complex's registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the "rentals not allowed" lines still showing in directories for Cherry Lane, Cedarbrook Estates and The Courtlands cannot be relied on for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law.** Pad tenancies at Mission Mobile Home Park and Oasis Country Estates fall under BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act: the resident owns the home and rents the pad, pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, and the raise is capped at the provincial limit plus a proportional share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. The current-year cap figure is stated in the city-wide Mission file; confirm the figure for the year of purchase on the Province's page. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Life lease is its own legal animal.** At Cedar Valley Manor the operator states the resident does not own the unit or the building and instead purchases an interest (the right to occupy), with the original entrance fee returned at termination less a set percentage for operation costs — no strata Form B, no title transfer. Read the life lease agreement and the society's disclosure before treating it like a purchase. https://www.missionseniors.ca/our-communities/cedar-valley-manor/
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020** is Mission's zoning bylaw. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning
- **Current plan: Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026** replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025, keeping the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction with a Regional Context Statement added; the OCP carries Urban Residential, Suburban Residential and Urban Residential Townhouse designations. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/notice-public-hearing-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan
- **Property taxes:** Mission's 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 (Mission has no staged 5% + 5% scheme — that staged form is the separate rural/FVRD regime and does not apply to the City of Mission); the City's tax rates page also states additional taxes are levied for diking on top of the municipal, school, regional district and BC Assessment levies. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- **Property transfer tax and the first-time buyer position in this band:** general PTT is 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value and 2% on the portion above $200,000 and up to $2,000,000. Under the first time home buyers' program, the full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024) and exempts the tax on the first $500,000 of the purchase price, with a partial exemption only above $835,000 and below $860,000, subject to the program's other conditions. Because this band caps at $800,000, a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in the band sits under the full-exemption value ceiling. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **First-home programs:** the First Home Savings Account and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file — no live CRA page stating the current limits was read in this pass, so no figure is published here.
- **Flood:** the Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's south edge and low areas such as Hatzic Prairie — where Mission Mobile Home Park sits — are protected by the Dewdney Dyke system; flood status is parcel-specific. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf

- **Q: Is Briskham Manor really 55+, or was it 35+?** Current listings uniformly describe it as 55+, and since Nov 24, 2022 only a 55+ age bylaw is legally enforceable in a BC strata in any case — the "35+" wording in a January 2023 advertisement cannot describe an enforceable bylaw today. The settling document is the registered bylaw set read with the Form B for LMS1049; get it before writing. https://www.jagsidhu.com/listing/r3093660-7554-briskham-street-309-mission-bc-v2v-3l9/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ Mission condo?** Yes in a strata — provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Cherry Lane's own listing text advertises "Family Exemptions." In a manufactured-home park the park's own rules govern instead — read them before writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in Briskham Manor as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident, and a long-term tenant must meet the bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, whatever a directory still prints. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Briskham Manor?** Listings record up to 2 pets, cats and dogs, with restrictions — but the weight limit differs between listings checked (24 lbs in one, under 25 lbs in another). There is no Mission-wide pet rule: Cherry Lane records 1 cat or 1 small dog, Luther Place records pets welcome with restrictions, and St. Andrew's Place co-op has a strict no-pets policy. Confirm the specific rule in the registered bylaws for the building you are buying in. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85456/FullPublic.pdf?i=1506184905 and https://www.kentbraaten.com/listing-crea/r3052611-106-7554-briskham-street-mission-homes-for-sale-380000/brochure
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and what care is in town if I need it later?** Mission Memorial Hospital is at 7324 Hurd St, with the Mission Community Health Centre at 7298 Hurd St and the Mission Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 304-32555 London Ave (open 7 days, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m.). The in-town ladder runs: independent strata (Briskham Manor, Cherry Lane, Luther Place) → life lease (Cedar Valley Manor) → independent/assisted rental (Chartwell Cedarbrooke, Chartwell Carrington House, The Cedars' assisted and supported units) → long-term care (The Residence in Mission, 200 beds per the 2020 inventory). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Mission/mission-memorial-hospital and https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Homelessness/2020%20Mission%20Housing%20Inventory.pdf
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, a caregiver, or a legacy case — or buy without living in the unit. Mission's attached market is also thin and volatile (community Condos & Townhomes median days on market hit 61 in September 2026 on 10 sales, SnapStats FVR September 2026), so price off the specific complex, not the monthly median. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **Mission's 55+ condo supply is one building, and the city-wide file in this series missed it.** Briskham Manor (7554 Briskham St, LMS1049, built 1993) is described as 55+ by four separate current listings, yet the mission-homes-for-sale file verifies only Cherry Lane and Luther Place — both townhouses. - **One building in Mission has been marketed at two different ages.** A January 2023 newspaper ad called Briskham Manor "35+!"; every current listing calls it 55+.
