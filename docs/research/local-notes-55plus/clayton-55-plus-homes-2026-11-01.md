## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the Manor Ridge directory line "rentals not allowed" cannot be read at face value. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000** governs Clayton, including the small-scale multi-unit housing framework: up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. The former RF-12 / RF-12C zones were replaced by the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone and RF-SD by R6 Semi-Detached in the July 8, 2024 zoning changes — zone names on older Clayton listings may be pre-2024 names. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites:** only one secondary suite is permitted per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite, and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. Coach houses and garden suites run 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), with lane access required for a coach house on smaller lots and one extra parking space. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Short-term rentals in Surrey:** a short-term rental is under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required before operating — on top of any strata short-term rental bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **OCP: "Surrey 2050" Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900** is the current OCP — finally adopted June 15, 2026, replacing the 2013 OCP Bylaw No. 18020. The City records that the East Clayton and West Clayton NCP land use designations have been superseded by that OCP update — confirm a lot's current OCP designation rather than relying on an NCP label alone. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton
- **ALR edge:** Clayton Heights runs to the ALR in the north and west; the ALC housing frame for an ALR parcel is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **First-home programs:** the First Home Savings Account and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file — no live CRA page stating the current limits was read in this pass, so no figure is published here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Clayton?** None verified inside Clayton. The verified 55+ ownership supply for the wider Cloverdale community is two strata complexes outside Clayton: Carriage House (17695 58 Ave, a 1987 condo building in downtown Cloverdale) and The Estates at Manor Ridge (6140 192 St, a 1989 townhouse complex). No 55+ manufactured-home park or membership complex was verified inside Clayton either. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: Clayton has lots of townhouses — aren't some of them 55+?** Not in any source checked. Clayton's attached stock was the larger half of the Cloverdale community's attached inventory in September 2026 (96 of 194 Condos & Townhomes listings, SnapStats FVR September 2026), but that stock is general-market in the sources checked — a Clayton listing that reads "downsizer-friendly" is not an age bylaw (Clayton area research file, Sections 2 and 5).
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. At Manor Ridge, listings state 1 occupant must be 55+ in terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ complex as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, including any older "rentals not allowed" record for Manor Ridge. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I have a pet in the nearest 55+ complexes?** The two are opposites in the sources checked: Carriage House listings state "No pets permitted," while Manor Ridge listings allow 1 pet with restrictions. There is no Cloverdale-wide pet rule — read the individual complex's bylaws. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: Is Clayton Heights Care Community a place I can move into independently?** No. It is a Park Place Seniors Living long-term care community at 18788 71 Ave delivering specialized long-term care programs — care housing accessed through the care system, not independent housing you buy or rent on the open market. https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/find-a-location/british-columbia/lower-mainland/clayton-heights/
- **Q: Where is the hospital for a Clayton downsizer?** There is no hospital inside Clayton or Cloverdale today; the planned new Cloverdale hospital and BC Cancer Centre are part of why the Town Centre Plan is being updated, and their address, opening date and services are UNVERIFIED on a Fraser Health page opened in this program. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
- **Q: Will the SkyTrain create 55+ stock in Clayton?** No source checked states that. The project names Hillcrest-184 Street and Clayton (190 Street) stations with an anticipated in-service date of 2029, and West Clayton's plan centres on a mixed-use urban centre at 184 Street and Fraser Highway — neither source describes any age-restricted housing as part of that build-out: UNCONFIRMED as a 55+ supply claim. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/ and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/west-clayton-ncp
- **Q: Does a 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool (at the nearest complexes)?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Clayton looks like classic downsizer territory and has zero age-restricted ownership stock.** A post-1999 planned community of townhouses and condos built through the East Clayton (2003) and West Clayton (2015) NCPs — and every complex name in its feed (Yale Bloc, Harlo, The EDGE, Clayton Rise, Sun @ 72) is general-market in the sources checked. A buyer filtering Clayton listings for "55+" is filtering an empty set (Clayton area research file, Sections 4–5).
- **The only seniors facility with a Clayton address is a care home, not a residence you choose like housing.** Clayton Heights Care Community (18788 71 Ave) is a long-term care community — it belongs in a care conversation, not a downsizing shortlist. https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/find-a-location/british-columbia/lower-mainland/clayton-heights/
- **The Cloverdale community's two verified 55+ complexes sit at opposite edges of Clayton, not in it.** Carriage House is in the downtown Cloverdale core on 58 Avenue; Manor Ridge is on 192 Street — a Clayton buyer who widens the search to "Cloverdale" gains exactly two complexes, in two different forms (a no-pets condo building and a pet-allowing townhouse complex). https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Two near-identical Clayton Heights streets can sit in different municipalities.** Clayton's eastern edge is the Surrey–Township of Langley boundary, with different bylaws, taxes and school districts (SD36 vs SD35) on each side — confirm which side of the boundary a listing sits on before comparing it to a cross-border comp. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton
- **The plans a Clayton seller quotes may already be superseded.** The City records that the East Clayton and West Clayton NCP designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update (Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026) — the current OCP designation is the live one. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/west-clayton-ncp
- **Old zone names still circulate on Clayton listings.** Surrey replaced RF-12 / RF-12C with R4 and RF-SD with R6 on July 8, 2024 — a listing quoting RF-12 coach-house potential is quoting a repealed zone name; the lot's current zone has to be checked in Zoning Bylaw 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
