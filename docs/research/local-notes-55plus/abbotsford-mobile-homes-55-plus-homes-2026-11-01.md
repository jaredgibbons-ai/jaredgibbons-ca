## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The two tenures run on different law.** In a manufactured home park, the tenant owns the home and rents the site under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. Where the home sits on land the owner also owns, the land transfer runs through the Land Title Office instead. In both cases, in B.C. the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if the transaction is registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, which maintains the central register of manufactured home ownership. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Pad-rent increases are capped by law (MHPTA / RTB):** a park landlord can increase rent only once every 12 months, with 3 full months' notice, and the increase cannot exceed the yearly limit — for manufactured home site tenancies the 2026 limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees, and the 2027 limit is 2.2% plus the same proportional amount. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale needs the park's consent (MHPTA s.28):** a tenant may assign a site tenancy agreement, or sublet the site, only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed obtained under the Regulation), an order of the director, or authorization in the tenancy agreement itself; consent may be withheld only in prescribed circumstances, and under s.28(3) the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting. The request runs on form RTB-10. Source: Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, s.28, as verified against the current Act on BC Laws in this program's batch-12 verification pass.
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s.42):** where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or a residential use other than a manufactured home park, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice covering a full year, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; the tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, may dispute the notice within 15 days, and may in defined circumstances (home cannot be moved, no site within a reasonable distance, no tax owing on the home) apply for additional compensation of the home's assessed value minus the $20,000; if the landlord does not take steps toward the stated conversion within a reasonable period, the landlord must pay the greater of $5,000 or 12 months' rent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **The CSA label is a sale blocker if it is missing:** mobile homes are built under CSA Z-240 standards and modular homes under CSA A-277; the manufacturer attaches a CSA label with a unique identifier, usually near the front door or on the electrical panel, and the home may not be sold unless the original legible CSA decal is attached. If the label is removed or lost, the home must be re-certified — Technical Safety BC has a licensed electrical contractor inspect the wiring and an electrical approval label is prepared, which can be used in lieu of a CSA label to list the home for sale (the official route behind the "silver label" shorthand). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **A park's 55+ rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw.** The Strata Property Act age machinery (below) does not govern a pad-rental park: occupancy, pets, rentals of the home and buyer approval are set by the park's own rules and the tenancy agreement — Georgian Park's listings, for example, bar rentals of the homes outright, which no strata could do today. Get the specific park's current rules in writing. Sources: the park entries in Section 2.
- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now** (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; younger age bylaws invalid since Nov 24, 2022, Bill 44) — relevant to the strata route off this topic, not to the parks. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The strata bylaw restricts residing, not owning,** and Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. These exemptions are strata law; they do not answer an occupancy question inside a manufactured-home park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022 — in strata. A park's no-rentals rule (Georgian Park) is a tenancy/park rule and is not touched by that change. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City frame:** Abbotsford's Zoning Bylaw is Bylaw No. 2400 (2014), and the City's SSMUH amendment is Bylaw No. 3560-2024 (June 25, 2024). No manufactured-home-park zone section of Bylaw No. 2400 was extracted for this file, so no City park-zone figure, park standard or park count is stated — UNVERIFIED. Source: batch-15 research file for this topic.
- **Property taxes:** City of Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. How a specific park home is assessed and taxed depends on its tenure and registration — a per-property check. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which Abbotsford parks are actually verified as 55+?** Three, on their own listing evidence: Paradise Park (31313 Livingstone Ave, Abbotsford West), Georgian Park (3300 Horn St, Central Abbotsford) and Maplewood Estates (2035 Martens St, Poplar). The live page's broader claim that most Central Abbotsford and Clearbrook parks are 55+ is UNVERIFIED — no source publishes an Abbotsford park count or age split. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What am I buying in a park, and what do I pay monthly?** You buy the manufactured home itself (Registry transfer) and hold a site tenancy under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, paying monthly pad rent to the park. The only pad figure in the verified sources is listing-stated: $782/month quoted for Georgian Park homes in two listings a year apart — confirm the current figure, what it includes, and its increase history for the exact site before offering. https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/3300-horn-street/55
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up each year?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, and no more than the yearly limit: 2.3% for 2026 and 2.2% for 2027, each plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park have to approve me, and can it charge for that?** Approval is part of the structure: under MHPTA s.28 the site tenancy can be assigned only with the landlord's prior written consent (or a director's order, or authorization in the agreement), consent can be withheld only in prescribed circumstances, and the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting (s.28(3)). Maplewood Estates' listings state the buyer must be approved by the park owner before purchase. Source: Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, s.28; park entry in Section 2.
- **Q: Why does the CSA label matter?** A manufactured home may not be sold unless the original legible CSA decal is attached (Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular). If it is lost, the home must be re-certified through Technical Safety BC's electrical inspection and approval-label route before it can be listed — the "silver label" fix. Check the label (usually near the front door or on the electrical panel) before anything else. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: What happens if the park is sold for redevelopment?** MHPTA s.42 requires the landlord to have all permits and approvals in place first, then give 12 months' notice and pay $20,000 compensation per tenant, with defined routes to additional compensation where the home cannot be moved or no site is available within a reasonable distance. The risk is real but it is priced and procedural, not a sudden eviction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can I rent out my park home?** In Georgian Park, no — its listings state rentals are not allowed, and that is a park rule, not a strata bylaw, so the province-wide voiding of strata rental bans does not change it. Paradise Park and Maplewood Estates: their listings checked do not state a rental rule — UNCONFIRMED; get the park's current rules in writing. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the 55+ parks?** Park by park, from listings: Paradise Park — one small pet with park approval; Georgian Park — two smaller pets (one listing specifies dogs up to 16" at the shoulder) with park manager approval; Maplewood Estates — one small pet allowed. Confirm against the park's current rules before relying on any of it. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is there an official price picture for Abbotsford manufactured homes?** No. SnapStats publishes no manufactured-home category, so no median, sales ratio or inventory figure for Abbotsford manufactured homes exists in the sources used, and none is stated in this file. The live page's own price range for the stock is its unsourced editorial — UNVERIFIED. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-mobile-homes.html
- **Q: Do I pay Property Transfer Tax on a pad-rental purchase?** UNVERIFIED in this file: the live page states PTT does not apply to a home-only purchase on a rented pad, but no Province PTT passage stating that treatment was opened in this program. A lawyer or notary should confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure before subjects come off. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-mobile-homes.html
