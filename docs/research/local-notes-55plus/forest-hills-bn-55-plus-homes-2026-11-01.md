# Forest Hills (Burnaby) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Forest Hills is the residential pocket on the lower south-west slopes of Burnaby Mountain, built around Forest Grove Drive and Beaverbrook Crescent, below Simon Fraser University (SFU) and above Lougheed Highway. The City publishes no street-by-street boundary for the MLS sub-area — exact boundary streets are UNVERIFIED. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- "Forest Hills" is the GVR board sub-area name (listing copy shortens it to "Forest Hills BN" for Burnaby North). City of Burnaby use of "Forest Hills" as an official neighbourhood name was not confirmed — the City-facing name for the pocket is "Forest Grove", after Forest Grove Drive, Forest Grove Elementary and Forest Grove Park. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- Schools: Burnaby is School District 41 — 41 elementary and 8 secondary schools, no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12), with address-based catchments. Two SD41 elementaries sit inside the sub-area: Forest Grove Elementary (K–7), 8525 Forest Grove Dr, and Stoney Creek Community Elementary (K–7), 2740 Beaverbrook Cres; the nearest secondary is Burnaby Mountain Secondary (8–12), 8800 Eastlake Dr. The SD41 locator result for a pocket address was not run in this research — UNVERIFIED per address. https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/ and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- Transit: Production Way–University Station (Millennium Line, with the Expo Line Lougheed branch) is south-west of the sub-area and is the SkyTrain transfer point for SFU bus service up Burnaby Mountain; no SkyTrain station sits inside Forest Hills. https://translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/september-2026/burnaby-and-new-westminster-map.pdf
- Recreation: Forest Grove Park, 8505 Forest Grove Dr, is the in-area City park, with outdoor tennis and basketball courts at the same address; the Burnaby Mountain Conservation Area trail network is in and above the sub-area, and Stoney Creek runs through the area toward Burnaby Lake. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Outdoor-Facilities/Outdoor-Courts.html
- Where the seniors housing actually is: there is no 55+ building of any kind verified in Forest Hills — no age-restricted strata, no retirement residence, no assisted living and no care home. The only seniors-directory presence in the pocket is two housing co-operatives on Forest Grove Drive and Ash Grove Crescent that BC Housing lists in its seniors-and-disabilities directory without stating any age restriction (Section 2) — co-op membership housing, not homes anyone can buy.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning bylaw — note the name change.** Research briefs for Burnaby sometimes still cite the "Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742)". The City states the new **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026** was adopted June 9, 2026, replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, and took effect July 1, 2026. Use the 2026 bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **R1 SSMUH zoning:** Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district in June 2024 — 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites, and multi-unit lots may be stratified. On the pocket's detached streets, SSMUH infill is the only mechanism adding smaller ground-oriented homes; none of it is age-restricted. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP: the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan maps. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Co-op housing is not strata:** the Hillside Place and Pine Ridge co-operatives run on membership and a share purchase under their own rules and the BC Housing directory's application route (apply directly to the co-op) — the Strata Property Act age-bylaw framework does not apply to them. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076
- **Property taxes:** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; the City's 2026–2030 Financial Plan approved a 2.9% tax increase (about $61 on the average home). The detached single-family water utility charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Forest Hills?** None that any source verifies. The pocket's strata complexes — Rossmoor, Wembley Estates, Henley Estates, Ashbrook Place — are family-marketed townhome complexes with no age restriction stated in any source checked. A 55+ buyer here is buying a regular house or townhome with no age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Hillside Place Co-op on Forest Grove Drive a 55+ building?** Not proven. BC Housing's Zone 5 seniors-and-disabilities directory (January 2021) lists the co-op, but its row states no age restriction — and the same directory does print an age where one applies ("55 & older", "50 & older") on other Burnaby rows. Treat any "55+" claim for Hillside Place as UNCONFIRMED until the co-op itself confirms it; and it is membership housing with a share purchase, not a strata purchase. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Burnaby?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a second dwelling for a parent or caregiver?** That is the realistic ageing-in-place route on the detached streets: Burnaby's R1 SSMUH zoning allows 3–4 units on most house lots (up to 6 near frequent transit), with suites among the permitted forms. Stoney Creek runs through the area, so creek setbacks and slope conditions are lot-by-lot checks. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** There is no hospital in the sub-area; the city's hospital is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St (Fraser Health), in central Burnaby south-west of Forest Hills. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day?** Production Way–University Station (Millennium Line) is south-west of the sub-area and is the transfer point for the SFU bus loop up Burnaby Mountain. TransLink is also developing the business case for a Burnaby Mountain Gondola from that station straight to SFU — proposed, not approved or funded. https://translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/september-2026/burnaby-and-new-westminster-map.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/news/2023/november/translink%20seeking%20feedback%20on%20burnaby%20mountain%20gondola
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Forest Hills?** No assisted-living or long-term care home was verified inside the sub-area. The nearest registered assisted living is outside it — for example the Swedish Assisted Living Residence at 7230 Halifax St in Montecito, whose subsidized suites are allocated through Fraser Health. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc
- **Q: Is Forest Hills the same as Forest Grove?** Substantially, yes — "Forest Hills" is the MLS sub-area name and "Forest Grove" is the local/City-facing pocket name. When checking City plans, permits or zoning history for a Forest Hills property, search both names. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The proposed gondola would pass over this neighbourhood.** TransLink's endorsed Route 1 for the Burnaby Mountain Gondola runs straight from Production Way–University Station to SFU with cabins passing above the Forest Grove neighbourhood; Burnaby Council endorsed the project and route in 2022 and over 85% of 2020 engagement participants supported the straight route — but the project is not yet approved or funded, so no buyer should pay today for a station that does not exist. https://www.translink.ca/news/2023/november/translink%20seeking%20feedback%20on%20burnaby%20mountain%20gondola
- **This tiny sub-area has two elementary schools inside it** — Forest Grove Elementary (8525 Forest Grove Dr) and Stoney Creek Community Elementary (2740 Beaverbrook Cres) — one reason its townhome complexes are family-marketed rather than adult-oriented. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **The attached side carries this market.** In September 2026 Forest Hills recorded just 1 detached listing against 6 condos & townhomes listings, and the sub-area's only sale was attached (1 sold of 6, 17%) — unusually attached-heavy for a house pocket, and far too thin to price from a single month. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **A seniors directory listing is not an age bylaw.** Hillside Place Co-op (8400 Forest Grove Dr) and Pine Ridge Co-op (8763 Ash Grove Cres) appear in BC Housing's Zone 5 seniors-and-disabilities directory with no age stated in their rows — the directory also lists Burnaby buildings expressly marked "19 & older", so directory presence alone never proves a building is 55+. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076
- **Burnaby replaced its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (adopted June 9, 2026, in force July 1, 2026) repealed and replaced the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 — documents or summaries that still cite the 1965 bylaw are out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Burnaby taxes have two penalty steps.** 2026 property taxes were due July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date, and the detached water charge ($652.33) carries a 5% early-payment discount. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
