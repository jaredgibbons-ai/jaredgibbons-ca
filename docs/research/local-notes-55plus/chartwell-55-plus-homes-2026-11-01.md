# Chartwell (West Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Framing note:** "Chartwell" here is a topic slug for the Chartwell retirement-residence brand, not a neighbourhood — so this file is framed city-wide for the District of West Vancouver and centred on the Chartwell residences a West Vancouver searcher is looking for. (West Vancouver also has a British Properties neighbourhood called Chartwell, around Chartwell Drive and Chartwell Elementary; that is a detached-house area with no seniors housing, and it is not what this file covers.) The headline finding is in Section 2: **Chartwell operates no residence in West Vancouver.** Its only North Shore residence is in North Vancouver.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. Retirement residences like Chartwell's are rental businesses, not stratas — this bylaw framework does not apply to them at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. (Building-data pages for some West Vancouver buildings still show "rentals not allowed" — stale data, not law.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Retirement residences are regulated differently from strata:** assisted-living suites (including Chartwell Churchill House's) are registered with BC's Assisted Living Registry, and publicly funded long-term care (Inglewood's 230 beds) is allocated through Vancouver Coastal Health after an assessment — neither is bought, and neither uses a strata age bylaw. Eligibility is the operator's/health authority's, residence by residence. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=may5&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc&f%5B0%5D=city%3AWest%20Vancouver
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010.** Houses sit in the RS single-family zones (Section 200 minimum site areas from 488 m² in RS5 to 8,094 m² in RS1, with secondary suites and detached secondary suites permitted); the apartment and care stock concentrates in the Ambleside town centre under the OCP. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018**, plus the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan, implemented by provincial order effective April 7, 2026 after the District missed the December 31, 2025 deadline. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0038-000377
- **Property taxes:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026; water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Additional school tax:** 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027 — a routine line item at West Vancouver values. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a Chartwell retirement residence in West Vancouver?** No. Chartwell has no West Vancouver residence. Its only North Shore residence is Chartwell Churchill House, 150 West 29th St in North Vancouver (independent + assisted living, rental). Anyone advertising a "Chartwell in West Vancouver" is confusing the brand with the Chartwell neighbourhood in the British Properties. https://chartwell.com/bc/north-vancouver/churchill-house and https://chartwell.com/find-a-residence/british-columbia
- **Q: What are the Chartwell-style options actually in West Vancouver?** The same product type — rental retirement living with care on site — is offered by Amica West Vancouver (659 Clyde Ave), Amica Lions Gate (701 Keith Rd) and Hollyburn House (2095 Marine Dr), plus independent-living rental at Westerleigh PARC (725 22nd St). All are rental; none is Chartwell, and none is strata ownership. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What 55+ homes can I buy in West Vancouver?** The verified age-restricted strata set is three Ambleside buildings: Westview Terrace (1390 Duchess Ave), Tudor Gardens (843 22nd St) and Lincoln Gardens (2110 Marine Dr). Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. (This is strata law; a rental residence sets its own occupancy terms.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Does Chartwell Churchill House let a resident age in place?** That is its stated model: Chartwell markets it as a continuum of care — independent living and assisted living in one residence, so support can be added without moving. It is the North Shore's only Chartwell and, by Chartwell's own description, the North Shore's only residence offering both lifestyles. https://chartwell.com/bc/north-vancouver/churchill-house/care-and-support/assisted-living
- **Q: Is there below-market seniors rental in West Vancouver?** Yes — Kiwanis Manor, 959 21st St in Ambleside, run by the non-profit Kiwanis North Shore Housing Society as 55+ independent living at below-market rents for most of its communities, applications year-round. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-manor/
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, serves the whole North Shore; Chartwell Churchill House markets itself as five minutes from it. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://chartwell.com/bc/north-vancouver/churchill-house
- **Q: If I need long-term care, what exists in West Vancouver itself?** Inglewood Care Centre (725 Inglewood Ave), 230 publicly funded beds operated by Baptist Housing Society, allocated through Vancouver Coastal Health — and its approved redevelopment (District approval September 21, 2026) rebuilds it at 364 beds in Phase 1, then adds 200 independent living units and 161 seniors rental units in Phase 2. https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/75989/ and https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/development-applications/inglewood-campus-care

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The brand and the neighbourhood share a name and nothing else.** "Chartwell" in West Vancouver search results is Chartwell Drive and its two schools in the British Properties. Chartwell Retirement Residences has never appeared in this research with a West Vancouver address; its BC residences are organized by city on its own site and West Vancouver is not among them. Any page implying a Chartwell residence in West Vancouver is built on the name collision. https://chartwell.com/find-a-residence/british-columbia and https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/chartwell/
- **Operator brands churn faster than registries.** Hollyburn House (2095 Marine Dr) is currently marketed as "Venvi Hollyburn House" by Cogir Senior Living; job postings from the transition period brand it Revera; the Assisted Living Registry's legal operator is "WC Operating (British Columbia - 1) GP Inc." The registry entry, not the brand, is the stable fact to cite. https://cogirseniorliving.ca/retirement-home-west-vancouver-british-columbia/venvi-hollyburn-house and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=may5&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc&f%5B0%5D=city%3AWest%20Vancouver
- **One Ambleside address is claimed by two operators.** At 959 21st St, the Kiwanis society describes its own 55+ independent living while the provincial registry lists a registered assisted-living residence under Dundarave Assisted Living Residence Partnership, with Park Place Seniors Living publishing that residence's page. Treat "Kiwanis Manor" as two products at one address until the operators confirm the split. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-manor/ and https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/find-a-location/british-columbia/lower-mainland/kiwanis-manor/
- **The District's biggest seniors-housing event is a rebuild, not a new brand.** The Inglewood Campus of Care approval (September 21, 2026) replaces a 230-bed 1979 care home with 364 long-term care beds, then adds 200 independent living and 161 seniors rental units on the same Ambleside site — more new seniors homes than every private residence opening in this file combined, and wait lists run through Baptist Housing, not the District. https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/development-applications/inglewood-campus-care
- **All of the District's care capacity is on the Ambleside flat.** The four Assisted Living Registry entries, the one long-term care home and all three verified 55+ strata buildings sit within a few blocks of Marine Drive between Clyde Avenue and 22nd Street. The hillside and the western villages have no registered seniors housing at all — a fact a "West Vancouver seniors" page should state rather than paper over. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=may5&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc&f%5B0%5D=city%3AWest%20Vancouver
- **Chartwell's North Shore foothold is one boutique residence.** Churchill House's marketing point — the North Shore's only independent-plus-assisted-living residence under the Chartwell brand — also means a West Vancouver senior who specifically wants Chartwell has exactly one option, and it requires crossing into North Vancouver. https://chartwell.com/bc/north-vancouver/churchill-house
