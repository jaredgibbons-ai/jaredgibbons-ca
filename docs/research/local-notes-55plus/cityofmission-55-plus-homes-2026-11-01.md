# City of Mission — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Scope note: this is the CITY-WIDE file for the City of Mission. The slug behind it (cityofmission) is a land-use plans page, not a listing feed — it links to the OCP and the Cedar Valley, Downtown, Silverdale and Stave Heights plans. ## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The City of Mission sits on the north side of the Fraser River in the Fraser Valley Regional District, bounded by the Fraser River to the south, the District of Maple Ridge to the west, and FVRD electoral areas to the north and east. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan and https://careers.fraserhealth.ca/pages/mission-community-profile
- The board sub-areas are Dewdney Deroche, Durieu, Hatzic, Hemlock, Lake Errock, Mission, Mission West, Stave Falls and Steelhead — none of the page's four plan areas (Cedar Valley, Downtown Mission, Silverdale, Stave Heights) is a board sub-area, so no board figure describes a plan area. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/cityofmission.md
- Where the 55+ stock actually sits: almost all of it is in the flat central bench around 7th Avenue and Cedar Street — Cherry Lane, Luther Place and Briskham Manor (strata), Chartwell Cedarbrooke and Chartwell Carrington House (retirement residences), Cedar Valley Manor (life lease) and St. Andrew's Place (co-op) are all within a few blocks of each other — plus the Hurd Street care cluster (The Cedars, Pleasant View) near Mission Memorial Hospital at 7324 Hurd St, and Welton Towers downtown at 33214 2nd Ave. The only rural 55+ product is manufactured-home parks on Lougheed Highway (Mission Mobile Home Park in Hatzic; the adult side of Oasis Country Estates in Dewdney Deroche). Sources: Section 2 below, and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Mission/mission-memorial-hospital

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. This is the rule that decides the Briskham Manor "35+" advertisement flagged in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. Cherry Lane's listing wording "55+ Community with Family Exemptions" points at this same framework — confirm the complex's registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law.** Pad tenancies at Mission Mobile Home Park and Oasis Country Estates fall under BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act: the resident owns the home and rents the pad, pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, and the raise is capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Life lease is its own legal animal.** At Cedar Valley Manor the operator states the resident does not own the unit or the building and instead purchases an interest (the right to occupy), with the original entrance fee returned at termination less a set percentage for operation costs — no strata Form B, no title transfer. https://www.missionseniors.ca/our-communities/cedar-valley-manor/
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020** is Mission's zoning bylaw as carried in this program; rural zones such as RU16 carry a 1.6 ha minimum lot size in City notices — relevant to the rural sub-areas, where there is no strata stock at all. The batch-15 research file itself did not open the parent zoning bylaw text, so SSMUH zones, unit counts and secondary-suite specifics for Mission are UNVERIFIED in that file. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/cityofmission.md
- **Current plan: Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026.** The City's April 7, 2026 public-hearing notice names OCP Bylaw 6405-2026, proposed to replace and repeal OCP Bylaw 6350-2025 while leaving the vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction unchanged and formally incorporating a Regional Context Statement; the earlier citation in this program (5948-2020) is superseded. Whether 6405-2026 was adopted after that hearing was UNVERIFIED in the batch-15 research pass — confirm the number the City currently cites before putting one in an offer. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/notice-public-hearing-april-7-2026
- **Silverdale layering:** the Silverdale Comprehensive Planning Area covers 1,392 ha (3,440 ac) inside the urban growth boundary, bordered by Silverdale Creek to the east, Silvermere Lake to the west and the Fraser River and ALR lands to the south, and significant portions of its 2018 Master Infrastructure Strategy have been superseded by the Central Neighbourhood Plan and the Utilities Master Plan. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/lan64c-silverdale-comprehensive-planning-area-neighbourhood-planning-terms-referencepdf and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/silverdale-master-infrastructure-strategy-mis
- **Property taxes — City of Mission only:** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding (including unclaimed home owner grants) on July 3, 2026; the City states it has no authority to waive or modify the penalty, and it does not accept credit card payments. This flat 10% is the INSIDE-city regime only. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/payment-methods
- **Outside the city, a different regime:** FVRD electoral areas C, F and G follow the rural Taxation (Rural Area) Act pattern of 5% + 5% — that regime does not apply inside the City of Mission, and the city regime does not apply in the electoral areas. Source: standing program position for Mission / FVRD.
- **Flood:** the Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's south edge and low areas such as Hatzic Prairie — where Mission Mobile Home Park sits — are protected by the Dewdney Dyke system; flood status is parcel-specific. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually BUY in Mission?** Three verified age-restricted strata complexes on the evidence carried here: Cherry Lane (32165 7th Ave, 20 townhouses, 1989, NWS2971), Luther Place (7525 Martin Place, NWS2729, 1988) and Briskham Manor (7554 Briskham St, LMS1049, counted on listing evidence with its conflict flagged in Section 2). Beyond strata, the buy-in products are a life lease at Cedar Valley Manor (8358 Cedar St — an occupancy interest, not ownership) and manufactured homes on rented pads at Mission Mobile Home Park (34519 Lougheed Hwy). Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Cedar Valley Manor a condo I own?** No. The operator states it directly: as a life lease, "you do not actually own the unit or the building" — you purchase an interest that gives you the right to live there, and at termination the original entrance fee is returned less a set percentage for operation costs. https://www.missionseniors.ca/our-communities/cedar-valley-manor/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Cherry Lane or Luther Place?** Yes in a strata — provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Cherry Lane's own listing text advertises "Family Exemptions." In a manufactured-home park the park's own rules govern instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm 52. Can I buy in a 55+ strata now and move in at 55?** You can buy now — the age bylaw restricts residing, not owning. You could not live in the unit until you meet the bylaw or fit an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Directories say Cherry Lane doesn't allow rentals. Can I rent my unit out?** For a long-term tenancy, the directory line is stale law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void across BC since Nov 24, 2022. Your tenant still has to meet the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and bylaws can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Oasis Country Estates a 55+ park?** Not as a whole. Listings split it into an "adult side" and a "family section," and no source checked states the adult side's age number — treat "55+" for this park as UNCONFIRMED and get the park rules in writing. Mission Mobile Home Park on the same highway is the one whose listings state 55+ outright. https://www.zolo.ca/mission-real-estate/41168-lougheed-highway/13
- **Q: What pets are allowed?** Building by building: Cherry Lane records 1 cat or 1 small dog (15 in. at the shoulder); Luther Place records pets welcome with restrictions; Briskham Manor records up to 2 cats or dogs with a listing-conflicted weight limit; St. Andrew's Place co-op has a strict no-pets policy. Confirm against the registered bylaws or park rules for the specific home. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and what care is in town if I need it later?** Mission Memorial Hospital is at 7324 Hurd St, with the Mission Community Health Centre at 7298 Hurd St and the Mission Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 304-32555 London Ave (open 7 days, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m.). The in-town ladder runs: independent strata (Cherry Lane, Luther Place, Briskham Manor) → life lease (Cedar Valley Manor) → independent/assisted rental (Chartwell Cedarbrooke, Chartwell Carrington House, The Cedars) → long-term care (The Residence in Mission, 200 beds per the 2020 inventory). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Mission/mission-memorial-hospital and https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Homelessness/2020%20Mission%20Housing%20Inventory.pdf
- **Q: How is transit for someone giving up driving?** The West Coast Express from Mission City Station (33200 North Railway Ave) is a weekday rush-hour commuter service to Downtown Vancouver — it is not all-day transit. Local movement is BC Transit's Central Fraser Valley system, including Route 31 to Abbotsford. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express and https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, a caregiver, or a legacy case — or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
