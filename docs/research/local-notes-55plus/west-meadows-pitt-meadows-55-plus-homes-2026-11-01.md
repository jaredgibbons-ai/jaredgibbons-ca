# West Meadows (Pitt Meadows) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "West Meadows" is the Paragon/MLS sub-area name for the west side of Pitt Meadows, west of Harris Road. In City terms it is mostly farm land plus the Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK): the City's 2020 Business Plan states 78% of the city's land base is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, and West Meadows holds the largest share of it. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- The airport is the sub-area's anchor: YPK, 1028-18799 Airport Way, with three paved runways and a water runway on the river side. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport
- The only real residential pocket is the Fraser Way / Wildwood Crescent area in the south-east corner, near the Maple Ridge border — the rest is farm parcels on roads like Ford Road, Harris Road's west end, and the dike roads. The September 2026 market snapshot shows how thin the housing is: 1 detached listing, 0 sales, and 0 condos & townhomes listed in the sub-area (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).
- There is no school, recreation centre, library or grocery store inside the sub-area. For the sample address 19452 Fraser Way, the SD42 School Locator returns Davie Jones Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary — both east of Harris Road — and the city's library, rec centre and seniors centre are all in Central Meadows. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/ and https://fvrl.bc.ca/locations/pitt-meadows-public-library
- Transit: the route 722 bus loop runs the Ford Road / Fraser Way segment — the only scheduled service through the sub-area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt722.pdf
- Health: no hospital in Pitt Meadows. The nearest is Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge, a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency care; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 121-11900 Haney Place, Maple Ridge. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/F/C/urgent-primary-care-centre---ridge-meadows
- Flood setting: the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of Pitt Meadows is in the floodplain, behind roughly 60 km of dikes, and West Meadows' farm parcels sit inside the City's drainage areas (Area 3 is the Kennedy area system). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**West Meadows has NO verified 55+ housing of any tenure — and almost no housing stock of any kind. There is no strata complex in the sub-area at all in the September 2026 data, no retirement residence, no assisted-living registration and no seniors building.** The one non-market complex on its edge, the Ford Road Housing Co-operative, is not a seniors building (see Checked, below). Do not market anything in this sub-area as 55+.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None — there is no strata stock in the sub-area to be age-restricted (0 condos & townhomes listed, as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- None. No 55+ rental building with a West Meadows address appears in any source checked, including BC Housing's Zone 4 seniors directory. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

### Retirement residences and care (rental/care — NOT strata ownership)

- None in the sub-area. BC's Assisted Living Registry shows one registered seniors assisted-living residence for the whole City of Pitt Meadows — The Wesbrooke, 12000 190A Street — in the Central Meadows Town Centre pocket, across the city from West Meadows. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&address=&f%5B0%5D=ha%3AFraser%20Health&order=alr_residence_type&sort=desc&page=4

### Subsidized seniors rental (non-profit, income-based — not ownership)

- None verified in the sub-area. The nearest non-market stock is the Ford Road co-op below — mixed-tenure family and adult housing, not seniors housing — and BC Housing's Zone 4 directory lists no West Meadows seniors building. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

### Checked and NOT 55+ (do not put these on a 55+ page)

- **Ford Road Housing Co-operative — 19041 Ford Road.** The sub-area's one named complex, and the file's big caution: it appears in BC Housing's Zone 4 directory of housing for seniors and adults with disabilities (Section 2, apply directly) — but it is NOT a seniors co-op. Its own application package describes an "Adult building" and a "Family building" of apartments plus 3–4 bedroom townhouses, and the Co-operative Housing Federation of BC's description mentions a playground and a small spray park. A directory listing is not a 55+ designation; this is mixed-tenure co-op housing for adults and families. (The market housing charges printed in its application package are version-dated — the copy checked covers Aug 1, 2025–July 31, 2026 — so do not republish them as current.) Placement caveat: Ford Road runs through both board readings of the city — MLS sources class Ford Road addresses near 122 Avenue as "Central Meadows" — so which sub-area the board assigns to 19041 Ford Road is UNCONFIRMED from public sources. It is filed here, where the road's farm-side reach sits, and cross-noted in the Central Meadows file. Either way, it is not 55+. https://www.fordroadcoop.ca/_files/ugd/a2fd0d_1869d1cc1d7b4dcd8f7a2488a1d79fb6.pdf and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/ and https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **The Fraser Way / Wildwood pocket houses.** The sub-area's only conventional homes — freehold detached houses on RS / R-3 / R-4 / R-6 zoning near the Maple Ridge border. No complex, no strata, no age restriction of any kind. (Research statement: sources checked Nov 1, 2026.)
- **Farm parcels.** The balance of the sub-area is A-1 / A-2 zoned farm land in the ALR — freehold farm properties, not strata, and not age-restricted product in any sense. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 - Consolidated Version_0.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. In West Meadows this is academic — there is no strata stock here — but it governs any 55+ purchase elsewhere in the city. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Farm zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011.** Most of West Meadows is A-1 General Agriculture — one farm house per parcel, maximum 500 m², subdivision minimum 8 ha, and a 15.0 m siting setback from watercourses and ditches — and A-2, with a 16 ha subdivision minimum. The Fraser Way pocket carries the RS / R-3 / R-4 / R-6 residential zones instead. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 - Consolidated Version_0.pdf
- **ALR housing rules:** on Agricultural Land Reserve land, the Agricultural Land Commission permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its rules are met — up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties more than 40 ha — subject to City bylaws, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. That is the legal ceiling on "a second home for family" on a West Meadows farm. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Plan: the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Bylaw No. 2864, 2020** (adopted September 27, 2022) designates most of this sub-area for agriculture. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- **Suites (residential pocket):** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 allows one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit, 33 to 90 m², OR one garden suite per lot in the listed zones — never both on one lot. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Farm taxes are a different world:** the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 sets the Farm class rate at $48.6824 per $1,000 of assessed value plus a farm drainage levy of $1.8524 per $1,000 — compare the residential drainage levy of $0.1082 per $1,000. 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- **Flood and water:** the City states over 95% of Pitt Meadows is in the floodplain behind roughly 60 km of dikes; farm properties here also commonly rely on private wells — the Province's well records and registration information is the starting point for a well check on any parcel. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a 55+ home in West Meadows?** No. There is no 55+ product here of any tenure — in fact there is no strata complex in the sub-area at all (0 condos & townhomes listed, as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report). The city's one verified 55+ purchase is Meadowland Village, 18960 Advent Road, in Central Meadows.
- **Q: The Ford Road Co-op is in BC Housing's seniors directory. Is it a seniors co-op?** No. Its own application package describes an adult building, a family building and 3–4 bedroom townhouses, and the CHF BC description mentions a playground and spray park. It sits in the Zone 4 directory for seniors and adults with disabilities, but the co-op itself is mixed-tenure — a directory listing is not an age designation. https://www.fordroadcoop.ca/_files/ugd/a2fd0d_1869d1cc1d7b4dcd8f7a2488a1d79fb6.pdf and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: Which sub-area is the Ford Road Co-op actually in?** UNCONFIRMED at board level. Ford Road runs from the Town Centre edge into the farm side; MLS sources class Ford Road addresses near 122 Avenue as Central Meadows, while the road's south-west reach is West Meadows geography. The co-op is filed in this file with the caveat stated, and cross-noted in the Central Meadows file. https://www.fordroadcoop.ca/_files/ugd/a2fd0d_1869d1cc1d7b4dcd8f7a2488a1d79fb6.pdf
- **Q: Can I build a second house on a farm here for my parents or adult kids?** Only within the ALR rules: the Agricultural Land Commission permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its conditions are met — up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, up to 186 m² above 40 ha — subject to City zoning (A-1/A-2) and building rules, and it cannot be stratified or sold separately. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Why do farm properties here carry such different taxes?** Because they are taxed as Farm class: Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 sets $48.6824 per $1,000 plus a $1.8524 per $1,000 drainage levy, where a residential property pays a $0.1082 per $1,000 drainage levy. Run the actual parcel's assessment class before comparing two properties' tax bills. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- **Q: Does the airport affect living in the Fraser Way pocket?** YPK is a working regional airport — three paved runways and a water runway — at 1028-18799 Airport Way, on the sub-area's north side. How much any given Fraser Way or Wildwood home hears it is a visit-and-listen question; no public source quantifies noise for a specific address, so treat any specific noise claim as UNCONFIRMED. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport
- **Q: What services are actually out here?** None in the sub-area itself: no school, library, rec centre, grocery or seniors centre. The SD42 locator sends a Fraser Way sample address to Davie Jones Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary, and everything else — library, rec centre, the West Coast Express — is in Central Meadows. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/ and https://fvrl.bc.ca/locations/pitt-meadows-public-library
- **Q: If I buy a farmhouse here, can I rent out a suite?** In the residential zones, one secondary suite (33–90 m²) or one garden suite per lot is allowed, never both; on ALR land the additional-residence rules above apply instead, and an ALR residence cannot be stratified. Which regime applies depends on the parcel's zoning — check the specific lot in Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Where would I go for seniors programs or care from West Meadows?** The Pitt Meadows Seniors Activity Centre (19065 119B Ave) and The Wesbrooke retirement/assisted-living residence (12000 190A St) are both in Central Meadows — a drive across the city, not a walk. https://bc.211.ca/agency-details/ridge-meadows-seniors-society-46654954/
- **Q: Could someone build a 55+ development in West Meadows in future?** Nothing in any source checked proposes one, and the land base works against it: most of the sub-area is ALR farm land under A-1/A-2 zoning and the City plan's agriculture designation. Treat any future 55+ supply here as UNCONFIRMED speculation. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **A "sub-area" that is really an airport and a farm belt:** West Meadows had exactly one detached listing and zero condo/townhome inventory in September 2026 — listing alerts set for "West Meadows 55+" will simply never fire, because there is no strata stock here at all (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).
- **The seniors-directory trap:** the Ford Road Housing Co-operative is the clearest local example of why directory listings must be checked against the operator's own documents — BC Housing's seniors directory includes it, its own application package describes family townhouses and a spray park. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://www.fordroadcoop.ca/_files/ugd/a2fd0d_1869d1cc1d7b4dcd8f7a2488a1d79fb6.pdf
- **Ford Road is two sub-areas at once:** the same road is classed Central Meadows at its north end (near 122 Ave) in MLS data and reads as West Meadows farm country at its south-west end — an address on Ford Road needs its board classification checked in Paragon, not assumed. (Research statement: sources checked Nov 1, 2026.)
- **The farm drainage levy is about 17 times the residential one:** $1.8524 vs $0.1082 per $1,000 of assessed value under Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 — a line item that surprises buyers comparing a farm parcel's tax notice with a town house's. (Computed from the two rates in the bylaw.) https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- **A water runway, not just pavement:** YPK's water runway on the river side makes this one of the few BC sub-areas where floatplane traffic is part of the soundscape — worth a site visit at a busy hour before buying in the Fraser Way pocket. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport
- **78% of the whole city is ALR, and most of it is this side:** West Meadows is where Pitt Meadows' farm identity physically sits — which is also why its "housing market" is a handful of farm sales and one small residential pocket, and why no seniors product has ever been built here. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
