# Hope — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Hope is a district municipality in the Fraser Valley Regional District at the confluence of the Fraser and Coquihalla rivers, at the eastern end of the Fraser Valley where the Fraser Canyon begins; it is a CADREB (Chilliwack and District) board community with its own MLS area name, "Hope & Area." https://hope.ca/official-community-plan-and-zoning-bylaw
- The townsite grid is the seniors-housing centre: the Park Street cluster (Park Street Manor at 555 Park St and Stalo Seniors Housing at 541 Park St), the 7th Avenue campus (Fraser Hope Lodge and Fraser Canyon Hospital, both at 1275 7th Ave), and Old Hope Princeton Way (Riverside Manor at 765, the Riverstone at 755). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Wallace Street is the downtown spine — the District's Downtown Action Plan defines the downtown core along Wallace from 3rd/4th Avenue south to Water Avenue, with "Uptown" running north along Wallace to 6th Avenue; District Hall is at 325 Wallace St. http://hope.ca/uploads/dm/151652/Hope_Downtown_Action_Plan
- The town's other named areas are Silver Creek (west, with its own elementary school), Kawkawa Lake (east), and Flood/Laidlaw (west fringe, where most of the manufactured-home parks line Flood-Hope Road). https://hopebc.ca/neighborhoods-of-hope-bc/
- Fraser Canyon Hospital, 1275 7th Ave, is a 10-bed Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency stabilization and care, transferring complex cases to Abbotsford Regional Hospital or Royal Columbian Hospital. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Hope/fraser-canyon-hospital
- The Hope & Area Recreation Centre, 1005 6th Ave (arena, Dan Sharrers Aquatic Centre, fitness facilities), is managed by the FVRD. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2021/2-million-grant-for-new-heating-and-cooling-system-at-hope-area-recreation-centre.html
- Transit is thin: BC Transit Route 72 connects Hope and Agassiz via Highway 7, with onward connections to Chilliwack via Route 71; the FVRD administers the routes and service is a few trips daily. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html
- Schools are SD78 (Fraser-Cascade): Coquihalla Elementary (K–6, 455 6th Ave), Silver Creek Elementary (K–7), and Hope Secondary (Gr. 7–12, 444 Stuart St) — one secondary school for the whole area. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/078
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: two clusters — Park Street in the townsite (Stalo, 55+, and Park Street Manor, 65+) and Old Hope Princeton Way (Riverside Manor; the Riverstone affordable homes next door) — plus seniors manufactured-home parks on Flood-Hope Road in Flood. There is no 55+ strata ownership stock in any source checked (Section 2).
- Hope's population context: 6,686 people (2021 Census, +8.2% from 2016), median age 54.8 — an older-than-average town, which is why the rental/care cluster is as large as it is relative to the town's size. https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/as-sa/fogs-spg/page.cfm?topic=10&dguid=2021A00055909009&lang=E

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Hope has ZERO verified 55+ strata (ownership) stock — no age-restricted strata building was found in any source checked. What Hope has instead is an unusually complete rental and care ladder for a town of 6,686: a 55+ seniors rental, a 65+ independent-living manor, a mixed independent/assisted manor, a long-term care lodge, and seniors manufactured-home parks. Do not present any of it as something to buy into as strata.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks are a different legal world from strata.** A park's 55+ rule is a park rule under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act framework, not a strata bylaw: the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003) lets a park owner refuse consent to an assignment or sublet where no proposed purchaser or subtenant meets the age requirement in a park where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or 2+ tenants at least one of whom has), as set out in s. 10(2)(b)(i) of the BC Human Rights Code. That is why a park's exact age must come from its own written rules — and why Lismore's and Windmill's unstated ages stay UNCONFIRMED here. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Zoning: District of Hope Zoning Bylaw No. 1324, 2012** (consolidated to March 20, 2025) is the governing zoning bylaw; its amendment list includes Bylaw 1580, 2024 (Small Scale Multi-Unit Housing, RS-1 zone, adopted June 10, 2024). https://hope.ca/uploads/dm/86919/Zoning_Bylaw_No._1324_2012_Consolidated_March_20_2025
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw 1617, 2025, adopted February 9, 2026** is the current plan, updated together with the Zoning Bylaw to meet current provincial housing requirements. https://hope.ca/official-community-plan-and-zoning-bylaw
- **Suite / SSMUH rules:** the District's Interim Housing Needs Report (Nov 2024) records that the 2024 SSMUH amendments allow 3 units on parcels under 280 m² and 4 on larger parcels in former 1–2-unit zones, and that permits from 2021 to Oct 2024 included 22 suites alongside 70 single-family dwellings. https://hope.ca/Home/DownloadDocument?docId=e153cf66-dc40-4da9-be75-4cc2789bafe3
- **Short-term rentals:** Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 1603, 2025 (adopted September 8, 2025) made short-term rentals a permitted use in designated zones in Hope, and Council voted to opt in to the provincial registry and require a District business licence as of January 1, 2026 — a secondary (non-primary) summary of the District's bylaws and minutes; confirm the current District guide before relying on fee figures. https://www.bnbcalc.com/blog/short-term-rental-regulation/Hope-Canada-Guide
- **Flood rules are a building rule here, not background:** construction in hazard areas needs a Flood and Erosion Hazards Development Permit — Council (Jan 13, 2025) approved one for an addition at 62180 Delair Road conditional on a satisfactory qualified-professional report; the District administers the Coquihalla River dikes (River Parade Flood Protection, 0.93 km; Glenhalla Subdivision, 1.01 km). http://hopebc.web.catalisgov.ca/vertical/sites/%7B7725007B-138F-4C56-A51E-2B444ECE829A%7D/uploads/RegularMeetingofCouncil_Minutes_20250113.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/air-land-water/water/integrated-flood-hazard-mgmt/dikes_listed_by_riverwatercoursepdf.pdf
- **Taxes:** District of Hope municipal taxes plus FVRD levies (recreation is an FVRD service here); the 2025 Annual Tax Rate Bylaw is No. 1601, 2025, and BC Assessment notices go out each January with rates set before May 15. The property-tax due date and penalty could NOT be confirmed on a District of Hope page in the area research — due date/penalty UNCONFIRMED from a District source (do not import Mission's flat 10% or any other town's scheme). Utility fees (water/sewer/solid waste) ride on the tax notice with 5% + 5% late penalties under Utility Bylaw No. 1153, per the research file. http://hope.ca/p/tax-rates
- **ALR:** some agricultural land exists on the District's fringes (e.g., the Flood/Laidlaw side); the ALR share of the District is UNVERIFIED — no District OCP or ALC source found states an ALR area or percentage for Hope, so no figure is substituted. (Area research file, 2026-11-01.)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a 55+ condo or townhouse in Hope?** No verified one exists. Hope's named strata complexes (Timberlane, Riverwynd Estates, Dogwood Place) carry no age restriction in any source found, and every purpose-built seniors option in town — Stalo (55+), Park Street Manor (65+), Riverside Manor — is rental or care, not strata ownership. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Homelessness/2020%20Hope%20Housing%20Inventory.pdf
- **Q: What is the actual 55+ rental option in Hope?** Stalo Seniors Housing, 541 Park St: 16 units run by the Coquihalla Intercare Society, recorded by the FVRD's 2020 inventory as "for seniors 55 years of age and older." Current availability, rents and subsidy status are UNCONFIRMED — contact the operator directly. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Homelessness/2020%20Hope%20Housing%20Inventory.pdf
- **Q: I'm 62. Can I get into Park Street Manor?** Not yet on the inventory's wording: Park Street Manor's 21 one-bedroom suites are for independent seniors **65 and over** — it is a 65+ building, and its fees include meals, laundry and room cleaning. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Homelessness/2020%20Hope%20Housing%20Inventory.pdf and https://hopebc.ca/live/retire-in-hope/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 (or under the building's age). Can we live together in a 55+ strata?** In strata law, yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt. Note these are STRATA exemptions (Regulation 7.01); a non-profit rental like Stalo sets its own admission rules — ask the operator. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How do the seniors manufactured-home parks work — do I own anything?** You own the home and rent the pad: the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation lets a 55+ park refuse an assignment or sublet where no incoming purchaser/subtenant meets the 55 requirement (Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i)). Lismore (87 pads) is called a "Senior's mobile home park" and Windmill (13 pads) "for seniors" in the FVRD inventory, but neither source states the exact age — get the park rules in writing before buying a home in either. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Homelessness/2020%20Hope%20Housing%20Inventory.pdf
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Hope?** The in-town ladder is unusually complete for a small town: independent non-profit rental with services (Park Street Manor), assisted living (Riverside Manor's 12 publicly subsidized assisted living suites, plus its 18 independent suites), then long-term care (Fraser Hope Lodge, 49 complex care beds + 1 respite) and hospice on the same 7th Avenue campus — all within about a kilometre of each other. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/2/F/assisted-living-at-riverside-manor and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/A/9/fraser-hope-lodge
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the seniors buildings?** Building by building: Fraser Health states small resident pets are permitted at Riverside Manor with management approval; pet policies for Stalo and Park Street Manor are UNCONFIRMED in the sources checked. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/2/F/assisted-living-at-riverside-manor
- **Q: Is Hope in a floodplain?** Parts of it are, and Hope is literally the reference point: the Fraser River's design flood profile is anchored to the Hope gauging station (the 1894 flood, treated as roughly a 500-year event), and new provincial floodplain maps for the Coquihalla and Fraser rivers from Yale to Mission were released in June 2026. Check the parcel against the maps and expect a Flood and Erosion Hazards Development Permit, with a qualified-professional report, for construction in hazard areas. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026WLRS0029-000715 and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/drought-flooding-dikes-dams/integrated-flood-hazard-management/governance/dike-management/flood-profiles-scenarios
- **Q: How would I get to specialist care in Abbotsford or Chilliwack without a car?** Slowly: BC Transit Route 72 runs Hope–Agassiz via Highway 7 with a connection to Route 71 for Chilliwack, a few trips daily; Riverside Manor also provides a courtesy taxi three times a week and bus trips for its residents. Hope is a driving community — price that in. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/2/F/assisted-living-at-riverside-manor
- **Q: Can I rent out a Hope home I buy, or run it short-term?** Long-term: no strata in BC can ban rentals anymore (rental-restriction bylaws are void since Nov 24, 2022), and a tenant in a 55+ strata would still need to meet the age bylaw or an exemption. Short-term in Hope specifically: Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 1603, 2025 permits STRs in designated zones, with a provincial registration and a District business licence required from January 1, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bnbcalc.com/blog/short-term-rental-regulation/Hope-Canada-Guide

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Park Street Manor turned 50 in 2025.** The Hope Standard's July 18, 2025 report marks the manor's 50th anniversary (founded 1975, a Society/provincial/federal partnership) — the town's seniors-housing backbone predates most of its housing stock discussions, and its manager had been in place since 2019 at the anniversary. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/HSL/250718/HSLN250718_A09.pdf
- **The seniors buildings cluster because the hospital campus anchors them.** Fraser Hope Lodge, the Hope Hospice Residence and Fraser Canyon Hospital all sit at 1275 7th Ave, and the Park Street buildings are a few blocks away — Hope's care geography is one walkable townsite node, not scattered sites. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Homelessness/2020%20Hope%20Housing%20Inventory.pdf
- **Hope got a brand-new OCP in 2026.** Official Community Plan Bylaw 1617, 2025 was adopted February 9, 2026, with a companion zoning update — and the District's housing-needs math behind it calculates a 20-year need of 2,123 units, mostly from anticipated growth, in a town that built 70 single-family homes and 22 suites in 2021–Oct 2024. https://hope.ca/official-community-plan-and-zoning-bylaw and https://hope.ca/Home/DownloadDocument?docId=e153cf66-dc40-4da9-be75-4cc2789bafe3
- **The Province has proposed 52 supportive homes at 660/670 Old Hope Princeton Way** with the Hope and Area Transition Society (BC Housing) — on the same street as Riverside Manor and the Riverstone, which would deepen the Old Hope Princeton Way seniors/supportive cluster if built; status is a proposal in the source, not a completed project. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/province-takes-next-step-to-bring-supportive-housing-to-hope
- **The 2020 inventory is the last full count — treat its numbers as dated.** The FVRD's District of Hope inventory (October 2020) is the source behind most unit counts in Section 2 (Stalo 16, Park Street Manor 21, Riverside 30, Fraser Hope Lodge 50 beds); the Riverstone was still a "proposed" project in it and opened in 2021, which shows how fast a 2020 snapshot ages. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Homelessness/2020%20Hope%20Housing%20Inventory.pdf and https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-homes-open-for-indigenous-peoples-in-hope
- **Hope's median age explains the market (2021 Census):** 54.8 years, in a population of 6,686 — yet all of its purpose-built seniors supply is rental or care, so an ownership-minded downsizer is competing for ordinary ranchers and townhomes, not a 55+ product. https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/as-sa/fogs-spg/page.cfm?topic=10&dguid=2021A00055909009&lang=E
