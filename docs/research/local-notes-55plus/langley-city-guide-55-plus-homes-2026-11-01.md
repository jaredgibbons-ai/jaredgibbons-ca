## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This overrides the "rentals not allowed" wording still displayed for Parkway Estates and Parkway II in building-data sources. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw, 2025, No. 3300** is the City of Langley's current zoning bylaw; it repeals Zoning Bylaw 1996 No. 2100. Its exact adoption date is UNVERIFIED in this program — sources conflict on the date, so no adoption date is stated here. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/public-hearing-noticepdf and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **City OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 3200**, adopted November 22, 2021 — the City's plan frame, distinct from both the Township's plans and Surrey's. For OCP/zoning-map questions, use the langleycity land-plans research (batch-13), not this guide. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/langley-city-guide.md
- **SSMUH / plex homes:** the City amended its Zoning Bylaw to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on RS1 and RS2 lots by Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 — RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. This is the rule behind the guide feed's "fourplex potential" listing language — a claim to verify lot by lot. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 3300):** a suite must not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the gross floor area of the building; the registered owner must reside on the lot; at least one extra off-street parking space is required; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Garden suites and carriage homes (Bylaw No. 3300, s. 2.4(d)):** permitted in the R1 zone; maximum gross ground floor area 100 m² for a carriage home or garden suite in R1, maximum total floor area of accessory buildings on an R1 lot 60 m², and the registered owner must reside on the lot. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Short-term rentals in Langley City: UNVERIFIED** — no City STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule could be read on an official City page in this program. Confirm with the City before any Airbnb/Vrbo claim is written. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Floodplain near the Nicomekl:** the guide page itself warns buyers to ask the City about low-lying lots near the Nicomekl floodplain — confirm the exact lot with the City before pricing in a build. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-city-guide.html
- **Property taxes:** City of Langley payments received after the July 2 due date incur a penalty; the penalty percentage for 2026 is UNVERIFIED — the City payment page read states that a penalty applies but does not state the rate. The same page lists City Hall at 20399 Douglas Crescent and a 2.2% processing fee on credit-card payments. https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes
- **Tax relief at 55+:** the Province's regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **GST rebates on a NEW home (context for the City's newer condo stock, not for a resale in the four complexes):** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST rebate under Bill C-4 (royal assent Mar 13, 2026) rebates 100% of the GST on a newly constructed or substantially renovated home valued up to $1 million, phasing out between $1 million and $1.5 million for a maximum of $50,000, for agreements in the Mar 20, 2025–2031 window; the older GST/HST new housing rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. Home Buyers' Plan and First Home Savings Account dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a Parkway or Glenmont unit?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases, and live-in caregivers are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: The building records say "rentals not allowed" at Parkway Estates. Can I rent my unit out?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, whatever an older bylaw set or a building-data site still displays. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Langley City's own STR rules are UNVERIFIED in this program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** It is building-by-building, and the 205/206 cluster is restrictive: building sources record pets not allowed at Parkway Estates and Parkway II, and a current Glenmont Park listing describes it as a no-pet complex. - **Q: What do strata fees cover in these buildings?** More than in most condos, per current listings: Parkway II listings state heat, hot water and electricity are all covered by the strata fee, and a Glenmont Park listing states the fee includes electricity/gas/hot water. The Riverglen listing at 20600 53A Avenue makes the same inclusion claim (listing-stated). Confirm the current fee and inclusions on the Form B for the specific unit. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/5363-206-street/210 and https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/20600-53a-avenue/205
- **Q: Is Harrison Landing a condo I can buy? It has full kitchens and balconies.** No. Harrison Landing (20899 Douglas Crescent) is a rental retirement residence — 67 suites, marketed to independent seniors aged 55+ — and it is registered as an assisted-living residence. A City buyer is minutes from it, but it is not a City building. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/langley/langley-memorial-hospital
