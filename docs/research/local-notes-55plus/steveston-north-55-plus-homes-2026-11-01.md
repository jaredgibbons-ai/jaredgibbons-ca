# Steveston North — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Steveston North is the residential district in Richmond's south-west, north of the historic Steveston village and south of Blundell Road. "Steveston North" is the Greater Vancouver REALTORS® (GVR) board sub-area name; the City does not use it as a planning name — the district spans the northern part of the Steveston planning area (Steveston Area Plan, Schedule 2.4 of the Official Community Plan) and edges into the City's Seafair planning area to the north. The board's exact boundary is UNVERIFIED; in listing practice the split from Steveston South is generally drawn at Steveston Highway. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- Hugh Boyd Community Park, 9200 No. 1 Road, has a pitch and putt course, artificial turf fields, turf sports fields, basketball courts, tennis courts, a playground and paved walking paths; West Richmond Community Centre sits at the park, with a fitness centre, gymnasium, squash court and badminton courts. The West Dyke Trail runs along the western dike at the district's west edge. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=8 and https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=131

Dixon Elementary, 9331 Diamond Road, at the district's north-west edge. For the sample address 9200 No. 1 Road, the SD38 School Locator gives Diefenbaker Elementary and Boyd Secondary; School District 38 catchments are address-based and the district has no middle school tier. https://boyd.sd38.bc.ca/school-information/about-us and https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments

Main roads: No. 1 Road north–south through the district, Francis Road and Williams Road east–west, Blundell Road at the north edge, Steveston Highway at the south edge running east to Highway 99. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt401.pdf

Nearest full-service library: Richmond Public Library Brighouse (Main) Branch, 100-7700 Minoru Gate. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://rpl.yourlibrary.ca/about/hours-locations

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500.** Houses in Steveston North sit in the Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m² and a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones; Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones (RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L, RSM/XL) have been applied under Bill 44. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Secondary suites and added units:** a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in the RS1/RS2 zones, and the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. Suite size rules are in Section 5.4. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Plan:** the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the Official Community Plan) guides the Steveston planning area, including this district's southern streets, within the City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000, framework. https://Richmond.Ca/city-hall/strategies/sale.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Flood:** the district is on Lulu Island in the Fraser River floodplain, behind the City perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations, designed for a 1:500 flood event, with dikes being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. The Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Property taxes (city-wide Richmond rule):** property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 3: Richmond and West Delta directory, May 2025). https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Steveston North?** None with an age restriction — no 55+ strata was verified in the district, and its documented form is detached houses. A buyer who needs an age-restricted purchase in Richmond is looking at the city-wide verified stock (Quilchena Green in Terra Nova; Covenant Court on No. 2 Road) or at general-market Steveston North houses and townhouses with no age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is there subsidized seniors rental in Steveston North?** At the edges, with flags: Ukrainian Village, 8720 Railway Avenue (east edge) is in BC Housing's Zone 3 seniors directory under its "Richmond — West" grouping, and Maple Vine Court, 7500 Francis Road, is in the same directory under "Richmond — South" and also appears in the family directory. Both are applied for through the Housing Registry or the operator as the directory directs; neither is ownership. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Steveston?** The Steveston-side care step is south of Steveston Highway: Austin Harris Residence on Moncton Street (affordable assisted living, listed in the Steveston Village file). At this district's north edge, Rosewood Manor on Blundell Road provides 24-hour nursing care, but its sub-area assignment is UNCONFIRMED (see Section 2). Long-term care elsewhere in Richmond is accessed through Vancouver Coastal Health. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Can a Steveston North house have a suite or an added unit?** Yes — the RS1/RS2 zone lists a secondary suite as a secondary use, and the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 to 4 units depending on lot size. Verify the lot's subzone minimums in Bylaw 8500 Section 8.1.8 before pricing redevelopment value. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: Which schools serve a Steveston North address?** For the sample address 9200 No. 1 Road, the SD38 locator gives Diefenbaker Elementary and Boyd Secondary — Hugh Boyd Secondary School itself is at 9200 No. 1 Road and R.M. Grauer Elementary is at 4440 Blundell Road; catchments are address-based, so check the exact address. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Q: How do buses connect this area to the Canada Line?** Route 401 runs on No. 1 Road between Steveston Exchange and Richmond–Brighouse Station, stopping beside Hugh Boyd Park and Hugh Boyd Secondary School. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt401.pdf
- **Q: Is Steveston North in a floodplain?** Yes — it is on Lulu Island in the Fraser River floodplain, behind the City perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations; the City is raising dikes from 3.5 m to 4.7 m for sea level rise, and Bylaw 8204 sets flood construction levels. The West Dyke Trail runs on the dike at the district's west edge. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The north/south line is listing practice, not law.** Steveston Highway is the generally drawn split between Steveston North and Steveston South, but the board's exact boundary is not published on an official source — a complex on Steveston Highway itself (like Heron at 5651 Steveston Highway) can be marketed under either name, which is why this file checks the address and the bylaws rather than the marketing label. (Per the area research file, sources read 2026-11-01.)
- **The district's newest townhouse stock is general-market.** Heron (2022, Steveston Highway) and Innova30 (2023, No. 1 Road) are the named new strata complexes tied to the district in listing and strata sources, and both record pet/rental restrictions only — no age bylaw. There is no new 55+ strata project in the district in any source checked. https://www.estateblock.com/richmond/6-5651-steveston-hwy-1972993 and https://www.estateblock.com/richmond/15-7580-no-1-rd-122259
- **A provincial school investment lands at the district's north-west edge.** The Province's February 2026 announcement lists seismic upgrades at Alfred B. Dixon Elementary (9331 Diamond Road) among Richmond projects opening in 2026. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026INF0008-000184
- **BC Housing groups Richmond differently from the real estate board.** The Zone 3 directory's "Richmond — South" and "Richmond — West" groupings cut across the board's Steveston North/South/Village sub-areas — Ukrainian Village is a "Richmond — West" listing on a Steveston street — so a subsidized-housing search by board sub-area name will miss buildings a Steveston senior can actually apply for. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **The recreation anchor is a combined park, school and community centre site.** Hugh Boyd Community Park, Hugh Boyd Secondary School and West Richmond Community Centre share the 9200 No. 1 Road site — pitch and putt, turf fields, tennis courts, a fitness centre, gymnasium and squash court on one block, with the route 401 bus stopping beside it. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=8 and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt401.pdf
