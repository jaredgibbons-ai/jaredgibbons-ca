# Cape Horn (Coquitlam) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Cape Horn is the established residential slope in south Coquitlam above the Cape Horn interchange, where Highway 1 (Trans-Canada Highway), Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and the Mary Hill Bypass (Highway 7B) meet at the Port Mann Bridge approach; the City tracks the area as planning areas 15 and 16 in its development statistics. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/291/Cape-Horn-C15-C16-PDF?bidId=
- No City neighbourhood-plan boundary for Cape Horn is stated on a City page in the sources read: boundaries are UNVERIFIED. The working description from the area research is the slope above the interchange, below Austin Avenue / Mariner Way, with Cape Horn Avenue and Mariner Way as the residential roads connecting the slope to the interchange. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquitlam
- United Boulevard, below the slope, is the light-industrial and commercial road in the City's planning geography — it is also the address corridor for the new Fraser Mills waterfront lands at United Boulevard & King Edward Street (see Section 2). https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/291/Cape-Horn-C15-C16-PDF?bidId= and https://www.stanleylamprec.com/buildings/view/27576/chapter/coquitlam/cape-horn/58-kong-st
- Nearby areas: Maillardville and Coquitlam West to the west and north-west, Central Coquitlam up Mariner Way to the north, Port Coquitlam across the Coquitlam River / Mary Hill Bypass to the east, and Surrey across the Port Mann Bridge to the south. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- Schools: School District 43 catchments are address-based, and no Cape Horn address-level catchment is verified — the only locator run in the research used 575 Poirier Street (the library), which sits outside Cape Horn. Run every Cape Horn address in the SD43 locator individually. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- Library: Coquitlam Public Library Poirier branch, 575 Poirier Street, is the nearest branch; the City Centre branch is at 1169 Pinetree Way. https://www.coqlibrary.ca/
- Health care: Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody, is the Fraser Health hospital on the Port Moody / Coquitlam border and the nearest hospital for this area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- Transit: the nearest SkyTrain stations are Braid, Lougheed Town Centre and Burquitlam — none is inside Cape Horn; bus routes serving the slope's interior streets: UNVERIFIED. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- Where 55+ housing actually sits: nowhere inside this sub-area that any source verifies. The slope itself is detached houses (the City's 2022 permit record for planning areas 15–16 is entirely single-family / secondary-suite / carriage-house form, with no townhouse or apartment units created that year), and the strata product that does carry the Cape Horn board label is the new Fraser Mills waterfront product below the slope — none of it age-restricted. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/291/Cape-Horn-C15-C16-PDF?bidId=

Do not market any Cape Horn home as part of a 55+ community.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. No Cape Horn strata checked has used this power — the absence of a 55+ bylaw is the verified fact here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996** is Coquitlam's zoning bylaw. The slope's small-scale residential zones are R-1, R-2 and R-3 (subdivision minimums 650 m² / 18.5 m width for R-1 and R-3; 465 m² / 12.5 m for R-2). https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) in Coquitlam:** the City's adopted rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the rules — the route by which a Cape Horn house lot, not a strata, becomes a multi-generational or suite property. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Watercourse setbacks:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam; Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and s.523 covers riparian areas — a live diligence item on slope-edge and creek-edge lots. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Property tax dates that apply citywide:** Coquitlam property tax payment was due July 2, 2026 for the Jan 1–Dec 31, 2026 year, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Cape Horn?** None that any source verifies. The sub-area's September 2026 listings were 18 detached and 13 condos & townhomes, and every named complex checked — the Fraser Mills buildings below the slope and Crescentview Townhomes on Cape Horn Avenue — is general-market, with no age restriction in any source. A senior who wants a legal 55+ purchase in Coquitlam is looking at the Town Centre buildings or Hideaway Park in other sub-areas (see the Central Coquitlam file). (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file; building checks Nov 1, 2026.)
- **Q: Is Wildwood Park, the mobile home park near here, a 55+ park?** No. Wildwood Park (201 Cayer St, Maillardville) listings describe a family-oriented park with a playground, an indoor pool, tennis courts and a clubhouse — the opposite designation. The 55+ park in Coquitlam is Hideaway Park on Dewdney Trunk Road, in Ranch Park. https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3091187-201-cayer-street-212-coquitlam-maillardville-homes-for-sale-288880/brochure and https://vancouvernashdom.com/coquitlam-mobile-homes-floating-homes-recreat/SearchResults.form/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Coquitlam?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can a Cape Horn house work as a multi-generational home instead?** That is the realistic in-area route: Coquitlam's SSMUH rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes, where the lot is connected to municipal water and sewer. Confirm the zone (R-1 / R-2 / R-3) and services for the specific lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Q: Will the new Fraser Mills buildings include seniors housing?** No source says so. The master plan is marketed as housing "for every lifestyle" — market condominiums and stacked townhomes plus market and non-market rental apartments — and developer-marketing sources conflict on even the rental counts, so any seniors component is UNCONFIRMED. Do not buy a presale here on a seniors-housing promise. https://www.stanleylamprec.com/buildings/view/27576/chapter/coquitlam/cape-horn/58-kong-st and https://www.jahangirhedayati.ca/blogs/presale-projects/fraser-mills-chapter
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody — the Fraser Health hospital on the Port Moody / Coquitlam border, reached from Cape Horn up Mariner Way and across the city. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** There is no SkyTrain station in Cape Horn; the nearest stations are Braid, Lougheed Town Centre and Burquitlam, so a car-free senior here is bus-dependent — and the bus routes serving the slope's interior streets are UNVERIFIED in the sources checked. Check the exact address's bus service before buying on a no-car plan. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- **Q: Is the slope itself a diligence issue?** Yes, in a specific documented way: the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area applies within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse, and Zoning Bylaw ss.519 / 523 set flood-protection and riparian setbacks — on a slope above a highway cut, lot-level geotechnical and setback checks belong in subjects, not after. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Cape Horn?** Not in a seniors building — there is none in the sub-area. The in-city ladder sits elsewhere in Coquitlam and the Tri-Cities: 55+ strata in the Town Centre pocket, retirement and care on Dufferin Street and in Port Coquitlam, and two new S.U.C.C.E.S.S. 55+ affordable rental buildings under construction at 290 Laval Square and 443 Clayton Street. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026HMA0070-000764.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The board label and the ground do not match at the bottom of the slope (researched Nov 1, 2026):** the newest strata stock carrying the "Cape Horn" sub-area label is not on the slope at all — it is the Fraser Mills waterfront product at Ghuman Place and Kong Court, on the old sawmill lands below United Boulevard, marketed by Beedie as Coquitlam's first waterfront community on a 95-acre site to be developed over the next 25 years. A "Cape Horn condo" and a "Cape Horn house" are two different neighbourhoods in practice. https://www.stanleylamprec.com/buildings/view/27576/chapter/coquitlam/cape-horn/58-kong-st and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3174963-808-1251-ghuman-place-coquitlam-bc-v0v-0v0
- **A four-kilometre water main is coming through the slope's corridor (Metro Vancouver project update, September 2026):** the Cape Horn Section of the Coquitlam Water Main is approximately four kilometres long and 2.2 metres in diameter, running from Westwood Street and Dewdney Trunk Road to Hickey Drive and Mariner Way, with a tunnel boring machine crossing under Lougheed Highway; Metro Vancouver's September 2026 update anticipates Cape Horn Section construction beginning in spring 2027 and continuing into the early 2030s, with exact shaft locations still to be determined. https://metrovancouver.org:443/services/water/coquitlam-water-main-cape-horn-section and https://metrovancouver.org/services/water/ConstructionDocuments/2026-september-coquitlam-water-main-project-full-alignment-project-update.pdf
- **The permit record says this is still a house neighbourhood (City development statistics, 2022):** Cape Horn's planning areas 15 and 16 recorded 18 net residential units created in 2022 (36 created, 18 demolished), all in single-family / secondary-suite / carriage-house form, with no townhouse or apartment units — which is why there is no strata 55+ stock to find on the slope itself. https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/291/Cape-Horn-C15-C16-PDF?bidId=
- **The two parks in every search are a trap (checked Nov 1, 2026):** Wildwood Park reads like a seniors park (clubhouse, indoor pool, quiet treed setting) but its own listings call it family-oriented and list a playground; Hideaway Park really is 55+, but it sits on Dewdney Trunk Road in Ranch Park. Neither gives Cape Horn a 55+ entry, and putting either on a Cape Horn page would be wrong on both geography and, for Wildwood, designation. https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3091187-201-cayer-street-212-coquitlam-maillardville-homes-for-sale-288880/brochure and https://vancouvernashdom.com/coquitlam-mobile-homes-floating-homes-recreat/SearchResults.form/
- **Developer numbers for Fraser Mills do not agree with each other (checked Nov 1, 2026):** marketing sources put the finished community at 4,700 households in 16 towers in one place and about 5,500 homes in 20 towers in another, and the rental split at 118 market / 117 non-market versus 260 market / 200 non-market. Treat every Fraser Mills total as UNCONFIRMED until a City or Beedie primary document settles it. https://www.stanleylamprec.com/buildings/view/27576/chapter/coquitlam/cape-horn/58-kong-st and https://www.jahangirhedayati.ca/blogs/presale-projects/fraser-mills-chapter
- **Strata sold nothing here in the snapshot month (September 2026):** Cape Horn's condos & townhomes recorded 13 listings and 0 solds in September 2026, while detached recorded 18 listings and 2 solds (11%) — a strata seller on the slope has no in-area strata sold to price from in that month (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file).
