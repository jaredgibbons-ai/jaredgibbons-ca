# Cariboo — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Cariboo is the residential area in north-east / east Burnaby around Cariboo Road and Cariboo Hill, between Lougheed Highway and the Edmonds side, anchored by Cariboo Hill Secondary (8580 16th Ave) and Armstrong Elementary (8757 Armstrong Ave). The City's heritage record for the secondary school places it in the "Cariboo-Armstrong" planning study area and the East Burnaby historic neighbourhood. The exact MLS sub-area boundary streets are UNVERIFIED — no City source checked publishes them. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark837 and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- Main roads: Cariboo Road and Lougheed Highway, with Armstrong Avenue, 16th Avenue and Government Road linking the area west and south, and North Road forming the Coquitlam border to the east. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt110.pdf
- Schools: School District 41 (Burnaby) — 41 elementary and 8 secondary schools city-wide, no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12), address-based catchments. In-area schools on the Province's list are Armstrong Elementary (K–7), 8757 Armstrong Ave, and Cariboo Hill Secondary (8–12), 8580 16th Ave, with Lyndhurst Elementary (K–7), 9847 Lyndhurst St, on the north side toward Lougheed. https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/ and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- Green space: the City's park dedication bylaw dedicates the Cariboo and Brunette River Conservation Lands and the Stoney Creek Park and Trail System on the area's north side — creek and conservation land, not manicured park; the City's outdoor courts list gives Stoney Creek Park 2 tennis courts and 2 pickleball courts. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html and https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/outdoor-courts
- Transit: Lougheed Station (Millennium and Evergreen lines) and Production Way–University Station are on the area's north side across Lougheed Highway; route 110 runs between Lougheed Station and Metrotown Station via North Road, Cameron, Beaverbrook and Eastlake on the north side. A bus route running on Cariboo Road itself is UNVERIFIED in the sources checked. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt110.pdf
- Health: the Edmonds Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #201-7315 Edmonds St, south-west of Cariboo in the Edmonds town centre; the city's hospital is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St, west across the city. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/burnaby/edmonds-urgent-and-primary-care-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- Where the seniors housing actually is: nowhere verified inside this sub-area. Cariboo's market, on the September 2026 data, is attached strata plus detached houses that did not turn over that month (23 condos and townhomes listed, 1 sold; 0 detached listed or sold — SnapStats GVR September 2026 report), and none of that attached stock is verified as age-restricted. The real 55+ buildings that searches surface for this corner of Burnaby — Salishan Court, Derby Manor, the 115 Place co-op towers — all sit at addresses whose placement in THIS sub-area is unconfirmed or outside it (see Section 2).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **A co-op's age note is not a strata bylaw.** The one age-marked housing form found near this sub-area — the 115 Place co-op towers, listed "50 & older" in BC Housing's directory — runs on co-op membership rules with a share purchase, not on a Strata Property Act age bylaw, and its 50+ threshold is below the only age a BC strata may now set (55+). Do not describe a co-op as a "55+ strata." https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning bylaw — note the name change.** Research briefs for Burnaby sometimes still cite the "Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965". The City states the new **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026** was adopted June 9, 2026, replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, and took effect July 1, 2026. Use the 2026 bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **R1 SSMUH zoning:** Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district in June 2024 — 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; multi-unit lots may be stratified. On Cariboo's detached streets, that is the rule set behind new duplex and multiplex forms — the realistic new ground-oriented supply here, none of it age-restricted. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP: the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan maps; the Lochdale Urban Village Community Plan, for the Lougheed-side urban village north-west of Cariboo, was adopted July 25, 2022. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/official-community-plan and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- **Property taxes:** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; the City's 2026–2030 Financial Plan approved a 2.9% tax increase (about $61 on the average home). The detached single-family water utility charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Cariboo?** None that any source verifies. No age-restricted strata building with an address confirmed inside the Cariboo sub-area was found in any source checked — the area's attached stock (23 listings in September 2026, SnapStats GVR September 2026 report) is general-market condos and townhomes, and its houses are freehold detached. A 55+ buyer here is buying an ordinary home with no age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Derby Manor, the 55+ building on 16th Ave, in Cariboo?** UNCONFIRMED. Derby Manor (8601 16th Ave) is a real 55+ non-profit rental — 122 units for individuals 55+, built 2017, run by the George Derby Care Society — and it stands beside Cariboo Hill Secondary, a Cariboo anchor. But a listing aggregation places nearby 16th Ave and Armstrong Ave addresses in "The Crest" sub-area, and no source checked assigns the address to the Cariboo MLS sub-area, so it is documented in the East Burnaby notes instead of being counted here. https://derbymanor.ca/ and https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/the-crest-real-estate/
- **Q: What about the Salishan building near Lougheed Mall?** Real, but not Cariboo: Salishan Court (9210 Salish Court) is a 188-suite non-profit seniors 55+ independent-living rental for low-income individuals and couples, north of Lougheed Highway in the Lougheed Mall area — on the far side of the highway from the Cariboo-Armstrong streets. Rental only, by application — nothing to buy. https://www.seniorcareaccess.com/bc/Burnaby-lang-English and https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076
- **Q: The co-op towers on Cardston Court say "50 & older" — is that a 55+ option I can buy?** It is a different product on two counts: BC Housing's directory lists the 115 Place co-op towers (9380 and 9390 Cardston Court) as "50 & older," not 55+, and a housing co-op is membership housing with a share purchase — you do not buy a strata title. Its placement inside the Cariboo sub-area is also UNCONFIRMED (see Section 2). https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Burnaby?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: Where do seniors in Cariboo go for programs?** The Temporary Cameron Community Centre and Library, inside The City of Lougheed Shopping Centre (110-9855 Austin Ave), operates during the Cameron rebuild with a 55+ seniors lounge and programs, and more fitness and children's programming at the nearby Charles Rummel Centre — recreation, not housing, and the civic hub for north-east Burnaby. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/cameron-recreation-complex
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** The SkyTrain stations (Lougheed, Production Way–University) and the route 110 bus corridor are on the Lougheed Highway side, north of the Cariboo-Armstrong streets — the interior streets are a walk or a short local trip from that corridor, and a bus route on Cariboo Road itself is UNVERIFIED in the sources checked. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt110.pdf
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Cariboo?** Not inside the sub-area — there is no assisted-living residence or care home at a Cariboo address in BC's Assisted Living Registry. The nearest ladder is south and west: assisted living at Liberty Place (7899 17th Ave, Edmonds side) and the George Derby campus on the east side's Cumberland St / 16th Ave edge (long-term care at George Derby Centre; 55+ independent rental at Derby Manor) — all accessed by application, not purchase, and all documented in the Edmonds and East Burnaby notes. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc and https://derbymanor.ca/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Cariboo had a month with no detached market at all.** In September 2026 the sub-area's detached row was 0 inventory and 0 solds, while its attached row was 23 listings and 1 sale (4% sales ratio) (SnapStats GVR September 2026 report). Anyone quoting a "Cariboo detached price" from that month is quoting nothing — and none of the 23 attached listings is verified 55+ stock.
- **The neighbourhood's anchor school dates the growth era.** The City's heritage record dates Cariboo Hill Secondary to 1961 — built because Armstrong, Seaforth and Lyndhurst elementaries had grown and their students otherwise travelled to Edmonds Junior High; it opened with 410 students, peaked at over 1,100 in 1974, and had additions in 1969, 1971 and 1974. The housing around it is of that era, which is why there is no purpose-built seniors stock: the area was built for young families. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark837
- **The area's big civic project is a rebuild, with a mall as the temporary home.** Council approved the construction contract for the Cameron Community Centre and Library replacement — a new library, aquatic facility, two-court gymnasium, weight room and cardio facilities, indoor running track and fitness studios, at an estimated total construction cost of $267 million; the old complex closed August 18, 2024, and the temporary centre and library (with the 55+ seniors lounge) operate inside The City of Lougheed Shopping Centre in the meantime. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2024-07-23/council-approves-award-contract-advance-construction-replacement-cameron and https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/cameron-recreation-complex
- **Three different sub-area names meet at Cariboo's north-east corner.** Listing sources place 9521 Cardston Court in Government Road, 2205-9603 Manchester Drive in Cariboo, and 8701 16th Ave in The Crest — three adjacent streets, three MLS sub-area labels. That is exactly why the 55+ buildings on those streets (the 115 Place co-ops, Derby Manor) are flagged UNCONFIRMED for placement here rather than claimed: on a boundary street, the sub-area name on a search portal is not evidence. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3138063-507-9521-cardston-court-burnaby-bc-v3n-4r8 and https://www.strawhomes.com/2205-9603-manchester-drive-burnaby-bc-v3n-4y7/ and https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/the-crest-real-estate/
- **The green space here is creek land, with setbacks that travel with it.** The Cariboo and Brunette River Conservation Lands and the Stoney Creek Park and Trail System are dedicated park by bylaw along the area's north side; for creek-side lots, watercourse-setback checks come early, because the setback rules follow the creek, not the lot line. A floodplain designation for Cariboo's residential streets was not confirmed in the sources checked. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- **Burnaby replaced its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (adopted June 9, 2026, in force July 1, 2026) repealed and replaced the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 — documents or summaries that still cite the 1965 bylaw are out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Burnaby taxes have two penalty steps.** 2026 property taxes were due July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date, and the detached water charge ($652.33) carries a 5% early-payment discount. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
