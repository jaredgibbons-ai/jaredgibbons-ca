# Kerrisdale, Vancouver — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Kerrisdale is a City of Vancouver neighbourhood centred on the West 41st Avenue village between roughly Larch Street and Blenheim Street, extending north toward West 33rd and south toward West 49th. The Kerrisdale Business Association promotes more than 250 businesses in the village district, concentrated on West 41st Avenue between Maple and Larch streets and along West and East Boulevards. http://www.kerrisdalevillage.com/
- The Arbutus Greenway runs along Kerrisdale's eastern edge; the City confirms the temporary path runs along the east side of Arbutus Street and West Boulevard for 8.5 km between Fir Street and Milton Street, crossing West 41st Avenue at the village's east end. https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/explore-the-arbutus-greenway.aspx?mgid=123893
- The Kerrisdale Community Centre, 5851 West Boulevard, offers an indoor pool, an ice rink and a fitness centre, and has been operated by the Kerrisdale Community Centre Society jointly with the Vancouver Park Board since 1943; the complex includes a public library branch and Kerrisdale Centennial Park, and the Kerrisdale Cyclone Taylor Arena is at 5670 East Boulevard. https://kerrisdalecc.com/contact-us/ and https://Vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/community-and-cultural-centres.aspx
- Transit is bus-based — there is no SkyTrain station in Kerrisdale; the City lists the nearby frequent transit corridors as the #41 West 41st, #49 West 49th and #14 Arbutus/West Boulevard routes (alongside the #99 and #25 further north). https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/explore-the-arbutus-greenway.aspx?mgid=123893
- Where seniors housing actually concentrates: not on the 41st Avenue shopping strip itself. The verified seniors residences sit just off it — Crofton Manor on West 41st Avenue at its western end (2803 W 41st Ave, on a 5.65-acre site) and Granville Gardens at West 49th Avenue and Granville Street (1550 W 49th Ave), at Kerrisdale's south edge. No source checked places any 55+ age-restricted strata in Kerrisdale. Sources: Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575** is the City of Vancouver's zoning bylaw — the City states the Zoning and Development By-law 3575 establishes regulations for the development of land in Vancouver, under authority granted by the Vancouver Charter. There is no separate municipal-style "zoning bylaw number" beyond No. 3575. http://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/zoning-and-development-bylaw.aspx?_ga=2.254851912.1179624217.1620063955-1581307344.1585854167
- **No Kerrisdale area plan; citywide plan instead.** Kerrisdale has no separate adopted community plan; citywide land-use direction now comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. (The Crofton Manor site sits within the older Arbutus Ridge/Kerrisdale/Shaughnessy (ARKS) Community Vision area.) https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/crofton-manor.aspx
- **Multiplex / R1-1 rule for Kerrisdale house lots:** By-law No. 13817 amended the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 to replace the old RS districts with the R1-1 district, allowing up to 6 strata units (or up to 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots — the redevelopment lens for a Kerrisdale house a downsizer is selling, or a lot bought for a family compound. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **Secondary suites (Vancouver):** the City states a secondary suite is an approved dwelling unit within a larger one-family or two-family dwelling, that only one secondary suite is allowed per single-family home under the Zoning and Development By-law, that a suite cannot be strata titled, and that a third suite is not allowed. That is the caregiver/family suite route on a Kerrisdale house lot. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/creating-a-secondary-suite.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ condo I can buy in Kerrisdale?** No verified one. No public source checked states a 55+ age-restriction bylaw for any Kerrisdale strata — the village condos (Sterling, McKinnon and the other West Boulevard / West 41st buildings) are general-market on every source checked. A buyer set on Kerrisdale ownership is buying a general-market condo or house, or confirming a bylaw in a specific building's strata documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What seniors options exist in Kerrisdale itself?** Rental/care, not ownership: Crofton Manor at 2803 West 41st Avenue (independent living and care suites on a 5.65-acre site) and, at the south edge, Granville Gardens at 1550 West 49th Avenue (independent living, assisted living, respite; Assisted Living Registry registered). https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/crofton-manor.aspx and https://www.verveseniorliving.com/granville-gardens/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes in the provincial exemption cases — a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt, as are live-in caregivers, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children the resident cared for before 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022 — but the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which schools serve Kerrisdale?** Public schools in the area include Kerrisdale Elementary (5555 Carnarvon Street), Kerrisdale Annex (3250 West 43rd Avenue), Maple Grove Elementary (1924 West 45th Avenue) and McKechnie Elementary (7455 Maple Street); secondary catchments split between Point Grey Secondary and Magee Secondary (6360 Maple Street, in Kerrisdale) by address — VSB locator samples give Kerrisdale Elementary / Point Grey Secondary for 5555 Carnarvon Street and McKechnie Elementary / Magee Secondary for 7455 Maple Street. Confirm any specific address with the VSB. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/039 and https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods
- **Q: Is there a seniors centre / recreation hub in the village?** The Kerrisdale Community Centre at 5851 West Boulevard — indoor pool, ice rink and fitness centre, operating with the Park Board since 1943, with a library branch in the complex — is the neighbourhood's recreation anchor, a short walk from the West 41st shops. https://kerrisdalecc.com/contact-us/
- **Q: What transit can I use without a car?** Buses only — no SkyTrain in Kerrisdale. The City lists the #41 West 41st, #49 West 49th and #14 Arbutus/West Boulevard among the nearby frequent corridors, and the Arbutus Greenway's temporary path crosses West 41st at the village's east end for walking and cycling. https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/explore-the-arbutus-greenway.aspx?mgid=123893
- **Q: What extra Vancouver taxes should I budget for?** City of Vancouver advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after the due date; the City also charges an Empty Homes Tax of 3% of assessed taxable value on homes declared or deemed vacant (2025 reference year, payment due April 16, 2026) — relevant if a Kerrisdale house will sit empty between sale and a move. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Q: If I sell my Kerrisdale house, does its lot have multiplex value?** Generally, the zoning now allows it: By-law No. 13817 rezoned Vancouver's RS districts to R1-1, allowing up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units per lot subject to the district schedule — site-specific confirmation with the City is still required, and heritage status should be checked first. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Crofton Manor's site is a live planning file, not a settled building.** The City states the 5.65-acre Crofton Manor site's seniors facility "is in need of a renewal", that retention and replacement of the existing facility with minimum disruption to residents is a minimum requirement of any redevelopment, and that the ARKS vision calls for additional planning study and community consultation before a rezoning can take place for seniors housing on the site. Anyone considering Crofton Manor should ask the operator about the renewal timeline directly. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/crofton-manor.aspx
- **A second large Kerrisdale seniors project was proposed, not built, in the sources checked.** A rezoning application by Amica and Intracorp for seven single-family lots on Granville Street at West 47th proposed a six-storey seniors housing project of 226 units (independent living, assisted living, memory care), considered under the ARKS Community Vision. Its current status is UNCONFIRMED in this file — do not present it as an existing Kerrisdale option. https://www.urbanyvr.com/seniors-home-granville-street/
- **Kerrisdale's demographics are the reason developers keep proposing seniors housing here.** The Granville Street application materials noted that in 2016, 41% of residents of the west side and Kerrisdale were 50 or older, projected to rise to 49% by 2026 — an applicant-stated figure from that proposal, not a current census citation. https://www.urbanyvr.com/seniors-home-granville-street/
- **The community centre predates most of the village stock around it.** The Kerrisdale Community Centre complex has operated jointly with the Vancouver Park Board since 1943 — one of Vancouver's first community centres — and its facilities include the Cyclone Taylor Arena (5670 East Boulevard) and the Maple Grove outdoor pool (6875 Yew Street), with the Kerrisdale Play Palace running inside the arena in summer. https://kerrisdalecc.com/contact-us/ and https://kerrisdalecc.com/facilities-rentals/
- **The Greenway through Kerrisdale is still in temporary form.** The City confirms the Arbutus Greenway path is a temporary path along Arbutus Street and West Boulevard, crossing West 41st Avenue — homes backing onto it should check the City's current greenway plans before closing, rather than assuming the present layout is final. https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/explore-the-arbutus-greenway.aspx?mgid=123893
