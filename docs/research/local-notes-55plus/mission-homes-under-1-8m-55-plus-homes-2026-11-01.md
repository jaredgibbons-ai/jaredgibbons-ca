## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. Cherry Lane's listing wording "55+ Community with Family Exemptions" points at this same framework — confirm the complex's registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm and https://www.zolo.ca/mission-real-estate/32165-7-avenue/5
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the "rentals not allowed" lines still showing in directories for Cherry Lane and Cedarbrook Estates cannot be relied on for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law.** Pad tenancies at Mission Mobile Home Park and Oasis Country Estates fall under BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act: the resident owns the home and rents the pad, pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, and the raise is capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Life lease is its own legal animal.** At Cedar Valley Manor the operator states the resident does not own the unit or the building and instead purchases an interest (the right to occupy), with the original entrance fee returned at termination less a set percentage for operation costs — no strata Form B, no title transfer. Read the life lease agreement and the society's disclosure before treating it like a purchase. https://www.missionseniors.ca/our-communities/cedar-valley-manor/
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020** is Mission's zoning bylaw; rural zones such as RU16 carry a 1.6 ha minimum lot size in City notices — relevant to the rural sub-areas, where there is no strata stock at all. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning
- **Current plan: Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026** replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025, keeping the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction with a Regional Context Statement added; the OCP carries Urban Residential, Suburban Residential and Urban Residential Townhouse designations. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing:** the City's SSMUH page states zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted, and City planning pages set out how secondary suites and detached accessory units are handled by zone — relevant to a senior selling a central detached house inside this band. https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **Property taxes:** Mission's 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 (Mission has no staged 5% + 5% scheme); the City's tax rates page also states additional taxes are levied for diking on top of the municipal, school, regional district and BC Assessment levies. The Province's regular property tax deferment program is for people 55 and older. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Flood:** the Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's south edge and low areas such as Hatzic Prairie — where Mission Mobile Home Park sits — are protected by the Dewdney Dyke system; flood status is parcel-specific. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which of Mission's 55+ options can I actually buy?** Three forms: strata townhouses (Cherry Lane, 32165 7th Ave; Luther Place, 7525 Martin Place), a life lease (Cedar Valley Manor, 8358 Cedar St — a purchased right to occupy, not title), and manufactured homes on rented pads (Mission Mobile Home Park, Hatzic — listings state 55+; Oasis Country Estates' "adult side" age number is UNCONFIRMED). The co-op (St. Andrew's Place) is membership, and everything else on the list is rental or care. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Cherry Lane's own listing wording advertises "Family Exemptions." Note the co-op, life lease and parks set their own occupancy terms — the strata exemptions do not automatically apply there. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/mission-real-estate/32165-7-avenue/5
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC strata — every rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, whatever a directory still shows for Cherry Lane. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What is the difference between the life lease and a condo at the same price point?** At Cedar Valley Manor you purchase an interest — the right to live there — and at termination you receive back the original entrance fee less a set percentage for operation costs. There is no title, no strata, and no market resale; read the life lease agreement and the society's disclosure before treating it like a purchase. https://www.missionseniors.ca/our-communities/cedar-valley-manor/
- **Q: What does a pad cost in the Hatzic park, and how fast can it rise?** Listing-stated pad rents at Mission Mobile Home Park are $650 and $671.29 on two current-era listings — UNCONFIRMED park-wide. Under the MHPTA, pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional share of levy/utility changes. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85367/FullPublic.pdf?i=979581669 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the 55+ complexes?** Building by building: Cherry Lane's listings record 1 cat or 1 small dog (15 in. at the shoulder); Luther Place records pets welcome with restrictions; St. Andrew's Place co-op has a strict no-pets policy. Confirm in the bylaws / park rules / co-op documents for the specific purchase. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Mission Memorial Hospital — Fraser Health's community hospital for Mission, in the urban core. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Mission/mission-memorial-hospital
- **Q: Is the Heritage Park area a seniors area? It looks like one.** It reads like one and is not: listings for Heritage Park Place (33751 7th Ave) and the Heritage Park condos (33755 7th Ave) state plainly "no age restrictions." The same trap sits one block over at Cedarbrook Estates (32339 7th Ave) beside Chartwell Cedarbrooke. https://vancouvernashdom.com/mission-mission-bc-townhouses/SearchResults.form/?_pg=5 and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/cedarbrook-estates-32339-7-ave
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+ in Mission?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). Mission's own penalty structure is a flat 10% after July 2, 2026 if taxes are unpaid — deferment has to be arranged before that date bites. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Q: Does the under-$1.8M band actually cover Mission's 55+ stock?** The band cuts the whole city feed and the city's medians sit far below the ceiling, so the mainstream of the market — including the forms in Section 2 when they list — falls inside it; but the exact share of listings inside the band cannot be stated from the permitted sources, and 55+ resales are episodic rather than a standing feed. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/mission-homes-under-1-8m.md

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Mission's OCP number changed in 2026.** OCP Bylaw 6405-2026 replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025, keeping the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction with a Regional Context Statement added — citations to the older number are superseded. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026
- **Two of the city's official-adjacent sources disagree with themselves about Mission seniors buildings.** Chartwell Carrington House carries two different civic addresses (Chartwell's site vs the Assisted Living Registry), Welton Towers carries two unit counts inside a single inventory document, and Pleasant View is a "seniors' complex" in the inventory but class MH in the Registry — all three conflicts are flagged in Section 2 rather than resolved by picking one. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The city's seniors housing is run substantially by one society.** MASH (Mission Association for Seniors Housing) stands behind Cedar Valley Manor (life lease), The Cedars and Welton Towers, and the co-op and Legion-style models cluster on the same Cedar Street / 7th Avenue bench — Mission's 55+ market is a small, society-led system, not a developer market. https://www.missionseniors.ca/our-communities/cedar-valley-manor/ and https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Homelessness/2020%20Mission%20Housing%20Inventory.pdf
- **The Rural Mission Strategy is finalized.** The City's rural plan — directly relevant to the rural half of this band — is now finalized and posted, building on the OCP; the urban/rural split in the feed (Mission / Mission West vs the seven rural sub-areas) follows the same line. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/rural-mission-strategy-now-live
- **Mission's tax penalty is flat 10%, unlike the staged penalties around it** — due July 2, 2026, with the full penalty on July 3 and diking taxes levied on top. A 55+ buyer comparing Mission with Abbotsford or Chilliwack should not assume the same penalty shape. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
