## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. No Yaletown strata verified in Section 2 carries a 55+ bylaw at all — the rule matters here as the test a claim must pass, not as a description of any Yaletown building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw — the live rental question in Yaletown towers, where the building-data records above state "rentals allowed" for long-term rentals and say nothing about short-term rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Heritage frame:** Yaletown is one of the City of Vancouver's protected heritage areas (with Chinatown, Gastown and Shaughnessy); the City has created regulations and companion design guidelines to preserve its historic fabric, and the Historic Area District Schedule for Yaletown (HA-3) encourages the conversion and renovation of its buildings and compatible new buildings, to produce a mix of commercial, industrial and residential uses. Confirm a renovation plan against the HA-3 district schedule before promising it. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **Tax calendar (City of Vancouver, applies to every Yaletown condo):** advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances after the main-tax deadline. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Empty Homes Tax:** every Vancouver residential owner must file an annual Empty Homes Tax property status declaration by the 2nd business day in February under Vacancy Tax By-law No. 11674; a vacancy tax unpaid as of the 10th business day of April bears a 5% penalty. The current Empty Homes Tax rate: UNVERIFIED in this pass. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/11674c.pdf
- **How the City creates new 55+ rental:** Vancouver's Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy (approved July 23, 2024; last amended May 19, 2026) secures new 55+ social housing as rental — no stratification, no separate sales — for 60 years or the life of the building. New City-policy seniors supply in or near Yaletown would add rental stock, not ownership stock. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-rezoning-seniors-housing.pdf
- **Land tenure:** Yaletown condo land tenure varies by building, and this pass opened no official source stating which Yaletown buildings are leasehold and which are freehold — building-by-building tenure is UNVERIFIED here and must be read off title/strata documents for any specific unit (see the one listing-copy example flagged in Section 2).
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** (Zone 6: Vancouver directory, May 2025) for Bridgeview Place and Quayside; long-term care at Yaletown House runs through Vancouver Coastal Health assessment instead. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://www.yaletown.org/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ condos can I actually buy in Yaletown?** None that any source verifies. Yaletown has no verified 55+ strata ownership building — the checked towers (Yaletown Park 1/2/3, Yaletown Ltd) record pets-and-rentals restrictions only, and the remaining feed buildings carry no age restriction on any source checked. Vancouver's one verified 55+ ownership building is Terraces on 7th in Fairview — see the Vancouver city-wide file in this batch. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: A listing calls a Yaletown tower "adult-oriented" or good for seniors. Is it 55+?** Not on any evidence in this pass. Only a registered strata bylaw makes a building 55+, and since Nov 24, 2022 only a 55+ bylaw can be valid at all. Amenities (pool, concierge, recreation centre — Yaletown Ltd has all three) and seawall-adjacent marketing are not an age bylaw. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/yaletown-ltd-1018-cambie-street and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Are Yaletown condos freehold or leasehold?** It varies by building, and no official source opened in this research states the tenure building by building — treat any tenure claim, including listing text describing a prepaid Crown lease, as unverified until it is read off the unit's title and strata documents. Price a prepaid-lease building against its lease term, not against freehold comparables. https://jaredgibbons.ca/yaletown-condos-for-sale.html
- **Q: Can I rent out a Yaletown condo?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, and the checked Yaletown towers state "rentals allowed" in any case. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals, which is the operative question for a Yaletown investor — read the building's short-term bylaw in the strata documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/yaletown-park-3-977-mainland-street
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Does that affect a Yaletown purchase?** No — no Yaletown building carries an age bylaw to be exempt from. The provincial spouse/partner exemption matters only inside a true 55+ strata elsewhere in the city: provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption, with defined exemptions for children and live-in caregivers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How do I get into Bridgeview Place or Quayside?** Both are non-profit seniors rentals in BC Housing's Zone 6 directory (May 2025) — apply through BC Housing's Housing Registry or directly with the operator, and expect a waitlist. Neither building sells units. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Yaletown?** The in-neighbourhood rung is Yaletown House, 1099 Cambie Street — 127 subsidized long-term care beds in a non-profit care home, accessed through the long-term care system after assessment, not by purchase or lease. There is no verified assisted-living or retirement-residence rung inside Yaletown itself in this pass: UNCONFIRMED. https://travelclinic.vch.ca/en/location/yaletown-house and https://www.yaletown.org/
- **Q: Which elementary school is in Yaletown?** Elsie Roy Elementary, 150 Drake St — confirmed by name and address against the BC Ministry K-12 file in SD39, and Drake Street is one of Yaletown's City-defined boundary streets; catchment assignment for a specific condo address is UNVERIFIED, so check the exact address with SD39. (Ministry K-12 file, read 2026-11-01.) https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **Q: What Vancouver owner costs catch Yaletown buyers out?** Two: main taxes each July (advance taxes each February, 5% penalty after the main-tax deadline) and the annual Empty Homes Tax declaration every owner must file by the 2nd business day in February — an investor-buyer needs both explained before closing, not after. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/11674c.pdf
- **Q: Can I renovate a heritage loft freely?** The HA-3 schedule encourages conversion and renovation of Yaletown's heavy-timber, brick and concrete buildings, but the heritage-area regulations govern what can change — confirm a renovation plan against the HA-3 district schedule before promising it. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
