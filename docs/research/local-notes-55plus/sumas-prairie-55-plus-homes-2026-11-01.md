# Sumas Prairie, Abbotsford — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

> **Geography note — read first:** Sumas Prairie is the low agricultural prairie in **southeast Abbotsford, in the City of Abbotsford**, between Abbotsford and Chilliwack in the Sumas River watershed — rural floodplain farmland, not an urban neighbourhood. The board sub-area also surfaces an upland fringe in its feed (Majuba Hill / Old Yale Road cards labelled Sumas Prairie). Flood is the defining fact of this sub-area, and it shapes every seniors-housing answer below: there is no urban 55+ stock here at all. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/supporting-flood-mitigation-planning-sumas-prairie and https://jaredgibbons.ca/sumas-prairie.html

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **There is no age bylaw on a freehold farm or rural house.** Strata age law only bites inside a strata corporation, and this sub-area has no verified strata stock (Section 2). A senior buying on the prairie faces flood, zoning and Agricultural Land Reserve rules — not a 55+ residency bylaw, and not a restricted resale pool. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **If the same buyer instead buys 55+ strata in urban Abbotsford, the provincial frame is this:** the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1/123.2, has allowed only 55-and-over residency bylaws since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residing, not owning; and Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — since May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19. Strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since the same date, though short-term rental bylaws still apply and a tenant must still meet the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks, if a park purchase elsewhere in Abbotsford is involved:** tenancies fall under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — the resident owns the home and rents the pad, pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. A park's age rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw. No park is verified in this sub-area (Section 2). http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Flood is an active planning file, not a settled fix.** The Sumas River Watershed Flood Mitigation Planning Initiative was signed in April 2023 in response to widespread flooding on Sumas Prairie from heavy rainfall in November 2021; the Province is providing $3.3 million for critical design and assessment work, and has provided approximately $220 million in recovery, preparedness and mitigation funding for Abbotsford and Sumas Prairie since 2021, including $76.6 million for upgrades to the Barrowtown Pump Station to better withstand floodwaters from the Sumas and Nooksack rivers. The same City-hosted release records that December flooding in the Sumas region again demonstrated the corridor is highly prone to water surges during heavy rainfall, and that the initiative's hydraulic model, risk assessment, ecological assessment and pump-station studies are at various stages of completion. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/supporting-flood-mitigation-planning-sumas-prairie
- **⚠️ LIVE-PAGE LAND CLAIMS, flagged and not carried as verified:** the live page states Sumas Prairie "lies on the former Sumas Lake bed (drained in 1924)" and is "almost entirely within the Agricultural Land Reserve (A2 zone) and the City of Abbotsford Floodplain Development Permit Area, protected by the Barrowtown Pump Station and Sumas Dike." The Barrowtown Pump Station's flood role is corroborated by the City source above, but the drained-in-1924 date, the "almost entirely" ALR share, the A2-zone framing for the whole prairie, and the Floodplain Development Permit Area claim were not confirmed on an official source opened in this program — UNVERIFIED as stated. https://jaredgibbons.ca/sumas-prairie.html
- **Parcel-level flood status is its own check.** Elevation, Flood Construction Level position and dyke/pump position for any specific parcel are UNVERIFIED in this file — no parcel-level flood document was opened in this program. The City's flood notices have named properties south of the Sumas River and along Whatcom Road in evacuation order and alert areas (see the Abbotsford East file in this set). Confirm with the City for the specific property before subjects come off.
- **ALR housing rules govern added homes on farm parcels.** Under Agricultural Land Commission housing rules, land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless the owner qualifies for an additional residence; the principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less; a secondary suite is permitted in the ALR only within the principal residence; and an additional detached residence may be up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. ALR status for any specific prairie parcel is UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Zoning frame: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 applies city-wide, including its agricultural zones; there is no Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw 3100.** The City's AgRefresh parcel-size work records a minimum subdivision lot area of 8.0 ha in the Agricultural One Zone (A1) and 16.0 ha in the Agricultural Two Zone (A2). The zone of any specific prairie parcel is a per-parcel check: UNVERIFIED for any individual parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **Suites and coach/garden suites in Abbotsford:** under Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, a secondary suite must be less than 50% of the size of the principal unit, and garden suites and coach houses may occupy up to 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m² — subject on ALR land to the ALC frame above. The small-scale multi-unit housing amendment is Bylaw No. 3560-2024, adopted June 25, 2024; how it applies on ALR prairie parcels is a per-parcel question and is not stated here. The Official Community Plan is Bylaw 2600-2016. Sources: the research program's rules-gaps pass, as recorded in the batch-12 research file for this area.
- **Servicing is per-property.** Feed cards describe septic systems and wells on individual acreage listings (listing text only); servicing for any individual Sumas Prairie property is UNVERIFIED in this file — pull the well record and septic filings on the specific property. https://jaredgibbons.ca/sumas-prairie.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there really no 55+ condo, townhouse or park to buy on Sumas Prairie?** Correct, on every source checked: no age-restricted strata, freehold complex or manufactured-home park is verified in this sub-area, and its September 2026 attached board row is 0 listings / 0 solds. Abbotsford's buyable 55+ stock — 26 verified strata complexes, three parks and two life lease residences — is all in the urban city (Abbotsford West, Central Abbotsford, Poplar and the Delair Road edge of Abbotsford East). See those files in this set. (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report)
- **Q: So how does a senior actually live on the prairie?** In a regular freehold farm or rural-residential house, on the sources checked. There is no age bylaw to qualify under, and no restricted buyer pool when the home is resold. The seniors-specific work is practical, not legal: single-level living in the existing house, a suite for a caregiver or family member where the ALR and zoning frames allow one, and the flood file on the specific parcel. Sources: Sections 2 and 3 above.
- **Q: What is being done about flooding, and should it decide my purchase?** It should be the first subject, not the last. The watershed initiative signed in April 2023 followed the November 2021 floods; the Province has provided approximately $220 million for Abbotsford and Sumas Prairie since 2021, including $76.6 million for Barrowtown Pump Station upgrades, plus $3.3 million for the initiative's design and assessment work — and the initiative's studies are still at various stages of completion, with December flooding again demonstrating the corridor's exposure. Get the parcel's elevation, Flood Construction Level position and insurance position in writing before subjects come off. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/supporting-flood-mitigation-planning-sumas-prairie
- **Q: Can a second dwelling be built on a prairie farm parcel for family or a caregiver?** Where the parcel is in the ALR, the ALC frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² of floor area, a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional detached residence of 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (parcels over 40 ha); the City's suite cap (less than 50% of the principal unit) and coach/garden cap (15% of the lot to 100 m²) sit alongside it. Confirm the parcel's ALR status and zone first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Which public schools are on the prairie itself if grandchildren are part of the plan?** Two SD34 schools, confirmed by name and address against the BC Ministry K-12 file: Semá:th Elementary School at 36321 Vye Rd and Barrowtown Elementary at 5137 Tolmie Rd. The middle and secondary schools the live page names sit outside the prairie in the city. Catchments are address-based and no locator run for a prairie address is on record in this program, so confirm the exact address with the district. https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments and the BC Ministry K-12 file, as recorded in the batch-12 research file for this area.
- **Q: I saw "Upper Sumas Elementary" on a listing page. Is that the Vye Road school?** The name does not check out. The BC Ministry K-12 file lists the school at 36321 Vye Rd as Semá:th Elementary School, and no "Upper Sumas Elementary" appears at that address in the Ministry file. Search and register under the Semá:th name. Source: BC Ministry K-12 file, as recorded in the batch-12 research file for this area; https://jaredgibbons.ca/sumas-prairie.html
- **Q: How far is the hospital from the prairie?** Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre is at 32900 Marshall Rd, in the urban city northwest of the prairie, and the Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 2692 Clearbrook Rd #100. No official source publishes drive times from prairie addresses — any minute figure is UNVERIFIED. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre
- **Q: If I sell a prairie farm and buy 55+ in the city, can my 53-year-old spouse live with me?** Yes — Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption; the same regulation covers live-in caregivers and the defined children cases. That exemption matters only in the city purchase — a prairie house itself has no age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I subdivide a prairie parcel to create a smaller homesite for retirement?** Generally no, on the published minimums: A1 subdivision minimums are 8.0 ha and A2 minimums are 16.0 ha in the City's parcel-size work, so most prairie parcels cannot be split further — and the parcel's actual zone must be confirmed first. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
