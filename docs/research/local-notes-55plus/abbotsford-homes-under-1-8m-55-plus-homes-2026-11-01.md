## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw, and any tenant in a 55+ building must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Property transfer tax across this band:** the Province's general rates are 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion of value above $3,000,000 — a purchase under $1,800,000 meets only the first two tiers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED** — no live CRA page stating those limits was read for this file, so no FHSA or HBP figure is stated here.
- **Suites and added units in a detached house:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings; a secondary suite must be within the principal unit, registered with the City, and less than 50% of the principal unit's size; the SSMUH amendment (Bylaw No. 3560-2024, adopted June 25, 2024) counts accessory units toward up to 4 units on one lot in SSMUH areas — 3 units on lots 280 m² or less, 4 on larger lots. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140 and https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites
- **The City's zoning vocabulary for seniors projects:** the Zoning Bylaw framework defines "Congregate Housing" as an independent living setting with common dining and amenity areas (added by Bylaw 3249-2022) — the use category under which supportive seniors projects are processed. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-abbotsford-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/7217/8514/3396/d9117e5011287c79b090adc71e32c234_Appendix_B_-_Draft_ZB_Amendments.pdf
- **The governing plan:** Abbotsforward 2050 is the City's Official Community Plan (Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated). https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2016b2600schedulee
- **Manufactured-home pads follow tenancy law, not strata law.** Under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act the buyer owns the home and rents the site (as at Paradise Park, Georgian Park and Maplewood Estates). Pad rent can be increased only once every 12 months, with at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for increases taking effect in 2026) plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **ALR edge of the band:** on Agricultural Land Reserve land a principal residence is capped at 500 m² total floor area with a 1,000 m² fill limit, a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence, and one additional residence is permitted up to 90 m² (properties of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property taxes:** Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many Abbotsford homes under $1.8M are 55+ right now?** That count cannot be stated from the sources used here. The roster's house-form option is Downes Road Estates — rancher-style homes in a bare land strata (plan LMS2380), where you own the strata lot and share the common property under strata law. No age-restricted fee-simple detached subdivision is verified anywhere in the city. https://www.estateblock.com/abbotsford/16-32250-downes-rd-518712
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in one of these complexes?** Yes, in law: Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption), and some complexes state the rule themselves — Royal Court's listing data says only one owner must be at least 55, Regency Park's says "One owner must be 55+", and The Wedgewood's says only one resident needs to be over 55. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/31930-old-yale-road/215
- **Q: I'm under 55 — can I buy for a parent, or as an investment?** Ownership is not age-restricted; the bylaw restricts residing. The Province states under-55s can own in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in the unit, so a 55+ parent can be the resident of a unit you own. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: A listing says "no rentals allowed." Can I rent my unit out?** Yes for long-term tenancies: every strata rental-restriction bylaw in BC has been void since Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be banned by bylaw. The parks are different — Georgian Park's listings state rentals of the homes are not allowed under park rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I keep a pet?** Building-by-building, and Abbotsford's 55+ buildings lean strict: Evergreen Village, Elmwood Manor, Heather Ridge, Silverwood Manor, Fernwood Manor, Centennial Court and Fircrest Estates all record no pets; Cloud Croft Manor allows one lap-sized dog or two cats; Crown Point records cats/dogs allowed, limit one, with restrictions; Parkside Place allows one cat and no dogs; Downes Road Estates allows pets with restrictions. Summerset Place is unresolved — treat pets there as UNCONFIRMED until the registered bylaws are read. Sources: the building entries in Section 2.
- **Q: What PTT do I pay under $1.8M?** Only the first two general tiers apply below $2,000,000: 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value and 2% on the portion above $200,000. Exemptions are program- and buyer-specific — confirm eligibility with your legal professional. FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan limits are UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Can a detached house in this band carry a suite for a caregiver or family?** The zoning route exists city-wide: one secondary suite is permitted in all single detached dwellings (Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8), the suite must sit inside the principal unit, be registered with the City and stay under 50% of the principal unit's size; on ALR land the suite must also sit inside the principal residence under the ALC frame. https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What's the difference between Garden Park Tower and a strata at Evergreen Village?** Tenure. Evergreen Village is freehold strata — you own the unit and can sell it on the open market. Garden Park Tower is life lease: an up-front capital payment (typically the BC Assessment value, per the Society) buys lifetime occupancy plus monthly maintenance charges, and on departure the Society redeems the lease under its terms — there is no strata lot to list. https://gardenparktower.ca/life-lease and http://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/evergreen-village-31955-old-yale-road
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale market?** Mechanically yes: the next buyer must be able to live there — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case — or buy without residing. That is the built-in trade-off of every BC 55+ strata since the 2022 changes confined age bylaws to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
