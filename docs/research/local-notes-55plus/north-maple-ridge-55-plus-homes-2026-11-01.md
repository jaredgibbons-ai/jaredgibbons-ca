## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019.** Urban-fringe houses sit under RS-1 (Single Detached Residential), s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40%; the rural and agricultural zones covering the acreage side carry much larger minimums in the same bylaw's schedule — A-1 2.0 ha, A-2 4.0 ha, A-3 8.0 ha, RS-2 0.4 ha, RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha depending on the zone text. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **City plan: OCP Bylaw No. 7060-2014.** No plan is written for "North Maple Ridge" as a whole; the Silver Valley Area Plan update adopted November 25, 2025 covers the north part of this scope, and Council adopted OCP and area-plan updates (Town Centre, Hammond, Silver Valley, Albion) in fall 2025. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan
- **Suites and added units:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Floodplain:** the City publishes North and South Alouette River and Fraser River floodplain maps; the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), no habitable floor space may sit below that level on affected lots, and rental accommodation (suites) is prohibited on floodplain properties under Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h). Which lots in this scope are affected is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **ALR:** the City describes Yennadon as an active agricultural production area, but ALR status is per-parcel — check the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping for any specific lot before valuing subdivision or second-home potential. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025). https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ complex in North Maple Ridge itself?** No — on the working scope (Yennadon / Silver Valley / north of Dewdney Trunk Road), no age-restricted strata, 55+ park, seniors rental or care residence was verified in any source checked. The attached product that does exist there (Brighton, Archstone Rockridge, Windfall) is family townhome product. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: I saw "North" listed with zero listings — does that mean nothing sells here?** No. The board row named "North" published 0 detached and 0 attached listings in September 2026, but the ground this scope covers overlaps the "Northwest" and "Silver Valley" rows, which carried 99 listings between them the same month. The zero row is a mapping problem, not a market fact — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Brighton or Archstone Rockridge in Silver Valley — are they 55+?** No source says so. Brighton's own complex marketing describes families and kids in the playground, and Archstone Rockridge listings market fenced family yards; neither building source records an age restriction. https://bohomarketinggroup.com/45-13819-232nd-st-silver-valley-maple-ridge/ and https://www.zolo.ca/maple-ridge-real-estate/13260-236-street/1
- **Q: What about Haney Pioneer Village and Fraserwood on 230 Street / 121 Avenue — aren't they in this area?** Their addresses fall inside this scope's rough rectangle, but MLS and directory sources place that pocket in East Central, and both are documented in the East Central file: Haney Pioneer is an 84-unit seniors co-op with a stated 60+ bar (not 55+), and Fraserwood is non-profit income-based housing managed by Metro Vancouver Housing. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/ and https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: Where is the nearest actual 55+ ownership stock?** South in East Central Maple Ridge — the four Fraserview Village strata buildings around 116 Avenue and 225–227 Streets, plus the Alouette Retirement Mobile Home Park (12868 229 St, own the home, rent the pad). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata (in the city complexes)?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I age in place on a Yennadon or Silver Valley acreage and add a suite later?** The standard route is one secondary suite per lot inside the house (maximum 40% of gross floor area), with SSMUH options on eligible urban lots — but the Alouette River floodplain crosses this scope and suites are prohibited on floodplain properties, and ALR parcels in Yennadon carry provincial rules on top of the City's. Pull the floodplain map and ALR status for the exact lot first. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, is the community hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows with 24/7 emergency care, in the Town Centre to the south; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 121 – 11900 Haney Place, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://www.healthlinkbc.ca/clinic/ridge-meadows-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- **Q: If I need care later, is there anything in this area?** No care or assisted-living residence in this scope appears in BC's Assisted Living Registry; the registered Maple Ridge residences are all at downtown addresses, so the care ladder means moving south to the Town Centre cluster. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=carouselnext&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&page=0&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **There is no official "North Maple Ridge" — and that has a practical consequence:** because the name is a page/MLS label with no City boundary and an unsettled board mapping, any statistic quoted for "North" (including its empty September 2026 shelf) may describe different ground than the streets a buyer is actually looking at. Price from the overlapping Northwest / Silver Valley evidence and Maple Ridge community figures until the mapping is settled — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
- **The Silver Valley Area Plan update was adopted November 25, 2025** as part of the fall 2025 OCP and area-plan update round — it sets the designations for the ground north of the Alouette River, so a lot's plan designation, not just its current house, drives its long-term value. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan
- **The two feed listings the live page showed for this scope were both acreages** — a renovated rancher on 2.62 acres and a house on 8 acres, both at the north/west edge — which matches the zoning reality: minimum lot areas run from RS-2 at 0.4 ha to A-3 at 8.0 ha on the rural side of this scope. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Silver Valley's townhome stock is recent family product, not downsizer product:** Brighton (2011, 145 units), Archstone Rockridge (2017/2018) and Windfall (2023) are all three-storey family townhomes — a downsizer buying here is buying stairs, in a general-market complex, with no age bylaw. https://www.estateblock.com/maple-ridge/110-13819-232-st-89301 and https://www.estateblock.com/maple-ridge/52-23183-136-ave-1911733
- **The bus claim for Silver Valley does not survive checking:** the live page's route 741 statement cites a TransLink diagram that is actually the route 748 (Haney Place / Thornhill) diagram and does not serve the Silver Valley corridor — treat bus access from a Silver Valley or Yennadon address as UNVERIFIED until the exact stop is confirmed on TransLink.

- **Property taxes:** Maple Ridge property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
