## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it,** with Regulation 7.01 exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** in strata since Nov 24, 2022; stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **The documents that decide a townhome purchase:** the Form B (Information Certificate), the bylaws and rules, recent minutes, the budget and the depreciation report; the live page states the strata must provide the Form B within one week. The Form B — not the band page — is where a complex's age bylaw, fees and levies are proved. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.html
- **No band statistic exists to price against.** SnapStats publishes no price-band data and no townhome-only cut; its attached category combines condos and townhomes at community level only, and per-sub-area medians are not published. The complex's own recent sales are the pricing evidence; the $700,000 combined median (September 2026) is context only. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.md
- **Property transfer tax, general rates:** 1% up to and including $200,000, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **The page's exemption thresholds are site copy only, UNVERIFIED on an official source in the research pass:** a first-time buyer exemption framed around $835,000 / $860,000, a newly built home exemption around $1.1M / $1.15M, and a federal GST rebate framed to $50,000 on new homes up to $1M phasing out to $1.5M. Verify each against the Province and the Government of Canada before budgeting a band purchase. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.html
- **Surrey-wide rules on this ground:** SSMUH allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; a secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit (a "90 m²" cap is NOT the current Surrey rule). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Municipal split inside the band:** check which municipality the unit is in before applying any rule — White Rock units sit under White Rock's bylaws; Surrey units under Surrey's. The band crosses the boundary without marking it. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.html

- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ townhome complex?** Yes, under the standard framework: a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is exempt, as are legacy residents, live-in caregivers, children under 19 in the resident's care and certain adult children. Confirm the registered bylaw wording on the Form B for the specific complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a townhome in a 55+ complex?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC strata — rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. The tenant must still satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What should I check in the strata documents before writing?** The Form B, the bylaws and rules (pets, parking, rentals, the age bylaw itself), recent minutes, the budget and the depreciation report — the live page's own checklist. In an older complex, the depreciation report and levy history matter more than the list price. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.html
