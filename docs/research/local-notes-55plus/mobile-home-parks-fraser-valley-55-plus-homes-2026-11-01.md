# Mobile Home Parks Fraser Valley — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

This file is a TOPIC page (slug `mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley`), not a neighbourhood. It is framed region-wide around manufactured-home parks across the Fraser Valley and the neighbouring communities the live directory page lists, and around which of those parks are verified 55+. Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named parks and complexes themselves.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT THIS HOME TYPE

- **Governing law (MHPTA):** manufactured homes are built off-site and transported to a final location for installation, and BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act covers most manufactured home tenancies. Where a tenant owns the home and rents the site, a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement applies; where both the home and the site are rented to the same tenant, a Residential Tenancy Agreement applies instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md
- **Pad-rent increases:** a park landlord may increase rent only once every 12 months, only with 3 full months' written notice, and only up to the yearly limit; the landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion of a prior year's limit. The 2026 limit for manufactured home site tenancies is 2.3% plus a proportional amount, and the 2027 limit is 2.2% plus a proportional amount; the proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale (MHPTA s.28):** the site tenancy is assigned to the buyer — the buyer does not get a new tenancy. Assignment requires the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations, or an order of the director, or authorization in the tenancy agreement); the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting to an assignment; and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Park closure / change of use (MHPTA s.42):** a landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to another use must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date of the notice; a tenant may end the tenancy early on at least 10 days' written notice; the dispute window for a 12-month notice is 15 days; and additional compensation may be ordered where the tenant could not obtain the permits needed to move the home or could not move it to another site within a reasonable distance, with a further $5,000 or 12 months' pad rent, whichever is greater, payable if the landlord does not proceed with the conversion purpose within a reasonable period. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **The 55+ rule in a park is a park designation, not a strata bylaw.** The Strata Property Act's 55+ regime (ss. 123.1/123.2, Regulation 7.01 — only 55+ age bylaws valid since Nov 24, 2022, with exemptions for legacy residents, caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses and defined children) applies to strata corporations; a park's age rule lives in its own tenancy agreement and park rules, which are the documents to read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/how-to-buy-a-mobile-home-in-bc-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Registration of the home itself:** a sale or transfer of a manufactured home is only effective once registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, filed through a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: If I buy a home in a park, do I own the land?** No — in a pad-rental park you own the home and rent the site under a pad tenancy agreement governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. The exceptions prove the rule: in a bare-land strata park such as Sea Ridge Bays you own the lot, and in a co-operative such as Pioneer Park the land is held through the co-op. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes and Section 2 above.
- **Q: Which Fraser Valley parks are actually verified 55+?** Twelve pad-rental parks in the sibling files: Paradise Park, Georgian Park and Maplewood Estates (Abbotsford); Westwood Court, Westwood Estates and Newton Mobile Home Park (Surrey); Cedar Creek Estates, Fernridge Park and Pineridge (Langley); Cottonwood Village (Chilliwack); and Alouette and Val Maria (Maple Ridge). Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: The directory lists a park in my town. Can I rely on its address and assume it is 55+?** No on both counts: the directory is site copy with no official registry behind it, it duplicates two Chilliwack parks (one with two different postal codes), and it states no age rule for any park — it lists all-ages parks such as Crispen Bays and Crestway Bays alongside verified 55+ parks. Confirm the park's legal name, address and age rule from the park itself before writing an offer. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.html and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md
- **Q: How much is pad rent?** No park tariff is stated here: no official source publishes a pad-rent figure, average or range, and the live directory page states none. The figure that matters is the specific site's current rent in its written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up each year?** Once every 12 months, on 3 full months' written notice, up to 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027 — no rounding up, no carry-forward of an unused portion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can the park owner refuse my purchase?** The sale needs the park owner's prior written consent to assign the tenancy, but the owner cannot charge a fee for considering or giving consent, and on assignment your pad rent and the date of the next increase carry over unchanged. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: What happens if the park is sold or redeveloped?** A sale of the park alone does not end your tenancy. A conversion of all or a significant part of the park requires permits and approvals first, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation paid on or before the notice's effective date, with a 15-day dispute window and possible additional compensation through dispute resolution. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Is a park's 55+ rule the same law as a strata's 55+ bylaw?** No — the strata regime (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, Regulation 7.01) governs strata corporations only. A park's age rule is a park designation in its tenancy documents; do not apply strata exemption rules to a pad tenancy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/how-to-buy-a-mobile-home-in-bc-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: What paperwork should the seller give me before I offer?** The written tenancy agreement and the written park rules — the assignment framework requires the seller to give the proposed purchaser both before the park owner is asked to consent — plus the current pad rent and the date of the last increase, so the capped formula can be checked. ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/how-to-buy-a-mobile-home-in-bc-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Do I pay property tax on a home on a rented pad?** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land; some parks include the land portion of taxes inside the pad rent — check what the specific park's rent includes. ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/how-to-buy-a-mobile-home-in-bc-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
