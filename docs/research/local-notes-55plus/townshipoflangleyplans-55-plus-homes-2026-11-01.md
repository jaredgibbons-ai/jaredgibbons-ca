## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Plans do not create age restrictions; strata bylaws do.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now: under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older, and any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. No OCP, community plan or neighbourhood plan on this page grants, confirms or removes that status for any complex — the registered bylaws and Form B do. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it;** Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19. Co-operative and park rules (Pioneer Park, the Brookswood parks) are set by the co-op or park, not by strata law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022 — so the "rentals not allowed" lines recorded for Township complexes such as Willow Park Estates are legally dead for long-term rentals; short-term rental bylaws remain possible, and a tenant must still meet a valid 55+ bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Read a Township parcel in layers:** OCP designation (2025 OCP Update, Bylaw No. 6200, adopted December 1, 2025), then the community plan, then any neighbourhood plan, then current zoning under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 — the plan shows where the Township wants growth; the zoning is what can be built as-of-right today. Per-parcel designation, plan, zoning and Development Permit Area status are per-parcel checks with the Township: UNVERIFIED for any individual parcel in this file. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/community-planning.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/willoughby-community-plan-update.aspx
- **Murrayville plan (verified at bylaw level):** the Murrayville Community Plan is Bylaw 1988 No. 2661, adopted April 17, 1989 as an amendment to the Langley Official Community Plan Bylaw 1979 No. 1842, as amended including by the 2025 OCP Update (Bylaw No. 6200) — old but live; read the consolidated version. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Community-Plan-Bylaws/Murrayville-Community-Plan-(Bylaw-No.-2661).pdf
- **Brookswood-Fernridge / Rinn (verified at bylaw level):** the Rinn Neighbourhood Plan is Bylaw No. 6010 within the Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan (Bylaw 2017 No. 5300), adopted June 10, 2024, with a Rinn Update 2026 (Bylaw No. 6219) recorded May 25, 2026 — the plan layer that sits over the Brookswood parks' ground, alongside the parks' own Manufactured Home Park designations. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Neighbourhood-Plan-Bylaws/Rinn-Neighbourhood-Plan-(No.-6010).pdf
- **SSMUH layer:** Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 Amendment (SSMUH) Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024, permits 3 units at 280 m² or less and 4 units at 280–4,050 m² on eligible lots (Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area; lots over 4,050 m² not eligible; no 6-unit tier), and on April 27, 2026 Council adopted Schedule 4 of the OCP implementing a Development Permit Area with form and character guidelines for SSMUH. This is the layer that lets a Township family lot add a suite or coach house for a parent — priced separately from the plan layer. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Manufactured-home parks:** tenancies in the Brookswood parks fall under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — own the home, rent the pad. A park's age rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw and not a plan policy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Township taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies at midnight July 2, 2026, and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. Langley City addresses (the four City strata, The Maple) sit under the City's separate tax notice. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I buy inside the Township of Langley?** Seven verified strata complexes — Willow Park Estates (Willoughby), Churchill Park, Spencer Green, Chartwell Green and Chelsea Green (Walnut Grove), Creekside Villa (Aldergrove) and Waterfront at Bedford Landing (Fort Langley) — plus homes in three verified Brookswood parks (Cedar Creek Estates, Fernridge Park, Pineridge) and the Pioneer Park co-operative in Aldergrove. Four more verified strata (Huntsfield Green, Parkway Estates, Parkway II, Glenmont Park) are Langley's but sit in the City of Langley, outside this page's plans. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Does being inside a community plan area make a complex 55+, or protect its age restriction?** No. Plans set land-use policy; the age restriction exists only in the strata's registered bylaws under SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, valid only at 55+ since Nov 24, 2022. A plan update — including the December 1, 2025 OCP Update — does not change any complex's age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/willoughby-community-plan-update.aspx
- **Q: Have the Willoughby plans over Willow Park Estates been repealed?** UNVERIFIED as stated on the live page: its tiles state June 15, 2026 repeals replaced by Bylaw Nos. 6202/6203, but the Township's Willoughby update page records only first and second reading of Bylaw Nos. 6201, 6202 and 6203 on May 25, 2026. Confirm the current plan for the parcel with the Township before relying on either version — and note the question affects future density around the complex, not its 55+ bylaw. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/willoughby-community-plan-update.aspx
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a Township 55+ strata?** Yes — Strata Property Regulation 7.01 requires an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); caregivers and the defined children cases are also exempt. At Pioneer Park the co-op's own occupancy wording in one listing is stricter ("both occupants must be 55") — a co-op sets its own rules, so ask the co-op, not the strata regulation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a Township 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, including the "rentals not allowed" lines in older Township building records. The tenant must still satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Instead of buying 55+, can I add a suite or coach house to a Township lot for a parent?** Potentially, under SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020: 3 units at 280 m² or less, 4 units at 280–4,050 m², if the lot is inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated and not in a Transit-Oriented Area — plus a Development Permit under the April 27, 2026 SSMUH DPA unless an exemption applies. Read the plan layer first, then test eligibility. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: Which plan governs the Brookswood parks?** The Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan (Bylaw 2017 No. 5300) at community level — it documents Cedar Creek Manufactured Home Park at 3031 200 Street with a Manufactured Home Park designation — with neighbourhood plans such as Rinn (Bylaw No. 6010, adopted June 10, 2024) refining parts of the area lot-by-lot. The parks' age rules themselves are park rules under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act framework, not plan policies. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/brookswoodfernridge-community-plan-adopted.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Neighbourhood-Plan-Bylaws/Rinn-Neighbourhood-Plan-(No.-6010).pdf
- **Q: Is there subsidized seniors housing inside the Township?** None verified in this program. Langley's subsidized seniors buildings — The Maple (101 homes, 55+ prioritized) and Evergreen Timbers — are in Langley City on the Langley Lions campus, under the City's jurisdiction. Township seniors apply through the same BC Housing Housing Registry; the buildings themselves are across the municipal line. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/opening-more-seniors-housing-in-langley
- **Q: What were Langley market figures in September 2026?** As community context only — no plan-area figures are published: the Langley community detached median was $1,345,000 on 449 listings and 64 sales, and the Condos & Townhomes median was $611,000 on 699 listings and 128 sales (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report).
- **Q: When are Township property taxes due?** Thursday, July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties at midnight July 2, 2026 and again at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Langley" 55+ stock splits at a municipal line this page never draws.** Eleven verified strata sound like one market; seven sit under the Township's plans and four under the City of Langley's — different OCPs, different zoning bylaws, different tax notices. A buyer comparing Creekside Villa (Township, Aldergrove) with Huntsfield Green (City) is comparing two municipalities, not two complexes. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Five tiles on this page state repeals the Township has not recorded as adopted.** The June 15, 2026 repeal wording for the Carvolth, Gordon Estate, Jericho, Latimer and Routley plans outruns the Township's own record (first and second reading only, May 25, 2026) — the single most consequential currency error for anyone pricing Willoughby land, including land around the verified 55+ Willow Park Estates, off this page. https://jaredgibbons.ca/townshipoflangleyplans.html and https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/willoughby-community-plan-update.aspx
- **Only one 55+ complex in the Township is named inside a plan document itself.** Cedar Creek Estates appears in the Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan as Cedar Creek Manufactured Home Park, 3031 200 Street, designated Manufactured Home Park — for every other complex, the plan is silent on seniors housing and the age rule lives entirely in strata or park documents. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/brookswoodfernridge-community-plan-adopted.aspx
- **The plan stack over a Brookswood park lot is still moving.** The Rinn Neighbourhood Plan (adopted June 10, 2024) already has a Rinn Update 2026 (Bylaw No. 6219, recorded May 25, 2026) — Brookswood-Fernridge is being refined lot-by-lot around the parks, which is context a park-home buyer will not get from the park's own documents. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Neighbourhood-Plan-Bylaws/Rinn-Neighbourhood-Plan-(No.-6010).pdf
- **The Township's densest seniors cluster has no plan tile of its own on this page.** Walnut Grove — four verified 55+ strata plus Chartwell Langley Gardens care campus — is one tile among twelve, while Willoughby's neighbourhood plans take most of the page. The page's emphasis reflects growth politics, not where Langley's seniors actually live. Sources: Section 2 above and https://jaredgibbons.ca/townshipoflangleyplans.html
- **The oldest plan on the page still governs a hospital neighbourhood.** Murrayville's community plan dates to April 17, 1989 (Bylaw No. 2661) and, as amended by the December 1, 2025 OCP Update, still frames the ground around Langley Memorial Hospital and Sunridge Gardens — the Township's care anchor sits under its oldest community plan. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Community-Plan-Bylaws/Murrayville-Community-Plan-(Bylaw-No.-2661).pdf
