## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996** is Coquitlam's zoning bylaw. The small-scale residential zones are R-1, R-2 and R-3 (subdivision minimums 650 m² / 18.5 m width for R-1 and R-3; 465 m² / 12.5 m width for R-2). https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) in Coquitlam:** the City's adopted rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the rules. In a detached-only sub-area like Chineside, this — not a 55+ strata — is the practical downsizing and multi-generational mechanism. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Watercourse setbacks:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam; Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and s.523 covers riparian areas — a live diligence item on ravine-edge lots above the Como Lake slope. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Property tax dates that apply citywide:** Coquitlam property tax payment was due July 2, 2026 for the Jan 1–Dec 31, 2026 year, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes

- **Q: Is L.J. Christmas Manor the Chineside seniors building?** No. L.J. Christmas Manor is a real non-profit seniors building, but its address — 560 Austin Avenue, V3K 3M9 — is the west end of Austin Avenue in the Austin Heights / Burquitlam orbit (its development permit was issued to the Burquitlam Senior Housing Society). - **Q: Is Chineside in Coquitlam or Port Coquitlam?** Coquitlam — SnapStats carries Chineside only under the Coquitlam community, and City of Coquitlam sources place it in central Coquitlam around Austin Avenue and Como Lake Avenue. File the municipality, taxes (Coquitlam, due July 2, 2026) and school district (SD43) accordingly. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquitlam and https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Coquitlam?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can a Chineside house work as a multi-generational home instead?** That is the realistic in-area route: Coquitlam's SSMUH rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes, where the lot is connected to municipal water and sewer. Current listings already market the point — a Mohawk Avenue listing advertises "future redevelopment potential for up to 4 dwelling units, subject to applicable zoning and City requirements." Confirm the zone (R-1 / R-2 / R-3) and services for the specific lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId= and https://vancouvernashdom.com/coquitlam-chineside-houses/SearchResults.form/
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody — the Fraser Health hospital on the Port Moody / Coquitlam border. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q: What transit and seniors recreation can I actually use day to day?** The nearest SkyTrain station is Burquitlam (Millennium Line) — there is no station in Chineside and the interior bus routes are UNVERIFIED, so check the exact address before buying on a no-car plan. For programs, the City's Dogwood Pavilion at 640 Poirier Street is the multi-purpose centre for adults over 50 serving this side of Coquitlam. https://vcn.bc.ca/srsinfo/seniors-olderadults.html
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Chineside?** Not in a seniors building — there is none in the sub-area. The ladder a Chineside senior actually uses is: independent non-profit seniors rental at the west end of the same road (L.J.
