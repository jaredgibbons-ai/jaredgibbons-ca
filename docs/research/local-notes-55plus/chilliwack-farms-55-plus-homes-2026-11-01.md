# Chilliwack Farms — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Plain finding up front: the 55+ angle on Chilliwack farm and ALR land is thin, and this file says so with sources. No 55+ strata, manufactured-home park, co-operative, rental or care complex was verified on Chilliwack farm land or in the farm districts (Greendale, Yarrow, East Chilliwack, Rosedale, Fairfield Island). Chilliwack's verified 55+ stock is urban — four strata complexes and one 55+ manufactured-home park, all in the city core / Sardis side — listed in Section 2 for contrast. What a 55+ farm buyer actually faces here is ALR housing law, plus the OCP's Farm Home Plate development permit area.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The strata age-bylaw rules, for the record — with no local strata to apply them to.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; age bylaws below 55 invalid since Nov 24, 2022, under Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term restrictions remain allowed. A farm buyer gets none of this machinery, because a farm purchase is freehold land, not a strata lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR housing caps are the rules that actually bind a 55+ farm buyer.** Land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The second-dwelling rule is the ageing-in-place rule on a farm.** The ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, with a total floor area of 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and Chilliwack's bylaws must also permit it. Anything larger needs an ALC application. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Chilliwack adds a Farm Home Plate permit area on top of the ALR rules.** The Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 5500, includes in its Schedule D the permit areas D-1 Drinking Water Supply, D-2 Hillside Environment, D-3 Riparian Area, D-4 Farm Home Plate and D-5 Hillside Development — the Farm Home Plate DPA is the City's own siting control over where the farm residence sits on the parcel. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **City plan: Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 5500** presents the long-term vision and growth strategy for the whole city, including long-term growth strategies for residential (urban, rural and hillside), commercial, industrial and institutional uses and the protection of the Agricultural Land Reserve; the Agricultural Area Plan is a schedule of the OCP. https://chilliwack.com/ocp and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** — Section 6 sets the agricultural zones (AL, AU, AS, AR, AC, AFP). SSMUH amendments are in Bylaw No. 5395, adopted June 18, 2024; the Province later granted Chilliwack an extension for part of the Proper neighbourhood and surrounding Hazel Park area, with a new compliance date of Dec. 31, 2030 for that area — SSMUH timing is not uniform across the city, and the farm zones are not where it lands in any case. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **Farm classification is a continuing obligation, not a one-time label.** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income requirements at farm gate prices are $2,500 if the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha. A downsizing buyer who stops producing can lose the classification and its tax treatment. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Manufactured-home parks run on a different statute** (relevant to the Rosedale park in Section 2): pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not the Strata Property Act, and the assignment rules expressly recognise a park where every pad is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55, per s. 10(2)(b)(i) of the Human Rights Code — Mt Cheam's listing states families are allowed, so it is not such a park. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol20/consol20/02077_pit
- **Water and septic are diligence items with legal teeth:** under the Water Sustainability Act, domestic groundwater use does not need a licence, but non-domestic use including irrigation requires a water licence, and well records are searched in the provincial GWELLS registry; a home not on community sewer must use an onsite system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, with only an Authorized Person designing, installing or inspecting it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Property taxes (2026, as published by the City):** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% penalty applies for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% for taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. The Province's regular property tax deferment program is for people 55 and older. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ farm community or seniors farm complex in Chilliwack?** No — none was verified on any source. Farm parcels are freehold, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. Chilliwack's verified 55+ complexes (Ivy Green, Knight Lodge, Mountainview Estates, Cottage Grove, Cottonwood Village park) are urban. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: I'm 55+ and buying a farm to downsize onto. What limits the house I can build?** On ALR land, a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. In Chilliwack the OCP's Farm Home Plate development permit area (Schedule D-4) also controls where on the parcel the residence sits. Confirm the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps before designing anything. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://chilliwack.com/ocp and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can my adult child or a caregiver live in a second dwelling on the farm?** The ALC permits one additional residence without an application where its size rules are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels, detached — and City bylaws must also permit it. There is no strata-style caregiver exemption here because there is no strata; the land rules are the whole answer. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: If I stop farming, do I keep farm-class taxes?** Not automatically: BC Assessment requires a producing farm to keep meeting minimum gross farm income thresholds that scale with the operation's size ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha). Plan the production, or lease the land to someone who produces, before assuming the classification survives. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Is the Rosedale park a 55+ park? It's mostly seniors, isn't it?** Mostly senior-occupied, yes — but Mt Cheam Mobile Home Park's own listing states families are allowed, so there is no age restriction to rely on. A park is only 55+ in law when its rules reserve the pads by age; this one's do not. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3089923-52604-yale-road-10-rosedale-bc-v0x-1x1/brochure
- **Q: Where is the hospital for a farm-district address?** Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Rd — a Fraser Health community hospital with emergency and acute care. The Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #104 - 7955 Evans Road. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/Chilliwack-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+ on a farm property?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. Farm classification is a separate BC Assessment matter. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Q: Does the farm belt flood?** The valley-floor districts (Greendale, Fairfield Island, Rosedale side) are low-lying Fraser River / Vedder River ground; the OCP's Schedule D includes riparian and drinking-water permit areas (D-3, D-1), and flood status has to be checked parcel by parcel — it is not uniform across the belt. (Parcel-level flood designation for any specific farm: confirm with the City.) https://chilliwack.com/ocp

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Agriculture is the city's land base, not a fringe use.** The City states agriculture accounts for 67% of Chilliwack's total land base and 29% of economic activity — which is why the "seniors community on farmland" product does not exist here: the belt is working farm ground under an Agricultural Area Plan. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Chilliwack's plan is new: the Chilliwack 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 5500,** carries the Agricultural Area Plan as a schedule and adds the Farm Home Plate development permit area (D-4) — a siting control most farm buyers have never heard of until their building permit hits it. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **The city's SSMUH timing is split.** Bylaw No. 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024) brought in the small-scale multi-unit amendments, but the Province granted an extension for part of the Proper neighbourhood and the Hazel Park area, with a new compliance date of Dec. 31, 2030 — added-unit rules are not uniform across the city. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **Chilliwack is a CADREB market.** Listing data, park lists and building records for the city run through the Chilliwack and District Real Estate Board, not the Fraser Valley or Greater Vancouver boards — one reason Chilliwack's 55+ complexes are thinner in the usual Metro Vancouver building directories. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/chilliwack-farms.md
