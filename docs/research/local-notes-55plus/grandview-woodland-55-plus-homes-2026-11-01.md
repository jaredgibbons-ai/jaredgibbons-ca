# Grandview-Woodland (Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Grandview-Woodland, with a hyphen, is the City of Vancouver's official local area name, one of its 22 distinct areas, built around Commercial Drive and East Hastings Street. The Grandview-Woodland Community Plan study area runs from Clark Drive to the west, Kamloops Street to the east, East 12th Avenue and Grandview Highway segments to the south, and Burrard Inlet to the north; a parcel at the edge should be checked against the City's local-area map. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/grandview-woodland-community-profile-2014.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- Commercial Drive and Hastings Street are the area's main commercial shopping streets; Commercial Drive, Victoria Drive, Nanaimo Street and East Broadway are the other main streets named in City sources. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/grandview-woodland-community-profile-2014.pdf
- Schools located in the area (VSB, SD39; catchments are address-based — check the VSB locator for any specific home): Lord Nelson Elementary, 1355 Garden Drive; χpey̓ Elementary, 1950 East Hastings Street; Britannia Elementary, 1110 Cotton Drive; and Britannia Secondary, 1001 Cotton Drive. SD39 has no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12). https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods and https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
- The recreation core is the Britannia Community Services Centre site — Britannia Branch of Vancouver Public Library, an elementary school, a skating rink, a fitness facility and a swimming pool — which the City and its partners are renewing through the Britannia Renewal project. Trout Lake sits at the area's southern edge. http://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/grandview.aspx
- Health: no hospital is located inside Grandview-Woodland — a hospital inside the area is UNVERIFIED in the sources checked; the hospitals named on health-authority pages for Vancouver are Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, and Mount Saint Joseph Hospital, 3080 Prince Edward Street. No long-term care home inside the area was verified in this pass either (Section 2). https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital
- Transit: the R5 Hastings St RapidBus runs from Burrard Station to SFU Exchange with stops at Hastings at Commercial, Nanaimo and Renfrew, and routes 14, 16 and 20 also serve Hastings Street; route 20 runs on Commercial Drive and Victoria Drive to downtown; Commercial-Broadway Station, at the area's south-west edge, provides Expo Line and Millennium Line service. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/ttR5.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt020.pdf
- Where the seniors housing actually is: two pockets, neither on Commercial Drive itself — the East 2nd / East 3rd Avenue towers at the area's west edge (Grandview Towers, by Clark Drive) with Adanac Towers just north on Adanac Street, and the East 12th Avenue site at the area's south edge, where Timbre and Harmony replaced two older Brightside seniors buildings. All of it is non-profit rental; there is no 55+ ownership building (Section 2). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**No verified 55+ strata ownership complex was found in Grandview-Woodland.** The area's verified seniors housing is three non-profit rental operations — Grandview Towers, Adanac Towers and the new Timbre and Harmony — plus one more directory-listed seniors building. All rental, nothing to buy.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None verified in this area. The area's housing stock is older houses, duplexes and multiplexes under R1-1 and the RT districts, with strata product concentrated on and near the arterials; no age restriction is stated for any strata complex inside Grandview-Woodland in the sources checked.

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- No stand-alone market-rental 55+ apartment building was verified in Grandview-Woodland — the rental seniors housing that does exist here is the non-profit stock below, including the area's one explicitly 55+ building, Timbre and Harmony.

### Retirement residences and care (rental/care — NOT strata ownership)

- None verified inside Grandview-Woodland. No private retirement residence and no long-term care home with a Grandview-Woodland address appears in the sources checked in this pass; a care home inside the area is UNVERIFIED. The care ladder for a Grandview-Woodland senior runs outside the local area (the nearest hospitals named on health-authority pages are Vancouver General Hospital and Mount Saint Joseph Hospital). https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital

### Subsidized seniors rental (non-profit, income-based — not ownership)

- **Timbre and Harmony — 1425 and 1451 East 12th Avenue.** The area's explicitly 55+ building, and its newest: the operator, Brightside Community Homes Foundation, lists "Timbre & Harmony, 1425 & 1451 East 12th Avenue – NOW OPEN," describing two buildings that "replace 57 units originally constructed in 1962 and 1971 with 157 units of affordable rental homes for seniors," built to Passive House Certified standard, highly energy efficient and climate resilient, with fully accessible homes available. The project's architect records the design brief the same way: designed for people 55 and older and people with disabilities, 157 secure homes along East 12th Avenue in Grandview-Woodland, 20 per cent of the units fully accessible and the remainder readily adaptable, enabling residents to age in place. Non-market rental — nothing to buy. https://brightsidehomes.ca/residents-and-properties/new-projects/ and https://canadianarchitect.com/timbre-and-harmony-opens-providing-passive-house-non-market-housing-to-vancouver-seniors/

### Checked and NOT 55+ (do not put these on a 55+ page)

- **800 Commercial Drive — the approved 93-unit social housing building is NOT a 55+ building, and is not built.** The City's July 26, 2024 news release records Council's approval (public hearing July 25, 2024) of a future 15-storey, mixed-use residential building at 800 Commercial Drive, operated by Entre Nous Femmes Housing Society, with 26 studio, 29 one-bedroom, 26 two-bedroom and 12 three-bedroom units, whose vision is intergenerational housing prioritising individuals and families facing barriers — seniors are named among the groups the operator serves in the community, but the project is family-and-individual social housing with a minimum of 30 per cent of homes at or below Housing Income Limits, not age-restricted housing. https://Vancouver.ca/news-calendar/new-social-housing-grandview-woodland-neighbourhood-july-2024.aspx

- **Britannia Community Services Centre is services, not housing.** The Britannia site (library, schools, rink, fitness facility, pool) anchors the area's daily life and its Renewal is the area's major civic project, but it contains no seniors homes to buy or rent. http://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/grandview.aspx

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. Note the limit of this rule in Grandview-Woodland: Timbre and Harmony's 55+ rule is not a strata bylaw at all — it is a non-profit rental operator's admission rule under the City's seniors housing framework below, so the strata exemptions do not apply to it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **How the City creates new 55+ rental — Timbre and Harmony's framework:** Vancouver's Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy (approved July 23, 2024; last amended May 19, 2026) treats "Social Housing (where occupancy is restricted to older adults 55+)" as social housing that meets the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 definition, secured by a housing agreement or other security restricting occupancy to residents aged 55 or older, owned and operated by a non-profit or government — exactly the form Timbre and Harmony takes (Brightside, non-profit, 55-and-older by design brief). Legal agreements under the policy run for 60 years or the life of the building, whichever is greater, and for supportive/independent seniors housing they secure the units as rental with no stratification and no separate sales. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-rezoning-seniors-housing.pdf
- **House-lot zoning: R1-1 District Schedule of By-law No. 3575** — minimum site area 306 m² for a multiple dwelling with 3 or 4 units, a duplex or a single detached house, 464 m² for 5 units, and 557 m² for 6, 7 or 8 units; single detached houses may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, duplexes may include secondary suites and lock-off units, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. RT districts also apply on parts of the area's older house stock. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **The area plan:** the Grandview-Woodland Community Plan is the area-specific plan, with the citywide Vancouver Plan over it — read the plan before pricing land, since its policies, not just the base zoning, govern the Commercial Drive and station-area sites near the seniors pockets. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/grandview-woodland-community-profile-2014.pdf
- **How the City routes a senior looking for this housing:** Council actively encourages supportive, assisted living and affordable rental housing for seniors, but City staff do not manage these facilities or their rental processes; the City directs seniors to the Seniors Services Society, which maintains the Seniors Housing Directory of BC. https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/find-supportive-housing-for-seniors.aspx
- **Tax calendar:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after each due date; the Empty Homes Tax requires an annual property status declaration, with the 2025 payment due April 16, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Grandview-Woodland?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata building in the area; the verified seniors addresses (Timbre and Harmony, Grandview Towers, Adanac Towers, Grandview Terrace) are non-profit rental. A 55+ buyer here buys a regular house, duplex, multiplex unit or condo with no age bylaw — in an area whose character stock is its older house streets. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Timbre and Harmony something I can buy into, or a co-op?** Neither — it is non-market rental housing run by Brightside Community Homes Foundation: 157 affordable rental homes for seniors at 1425 and 1451 East 12th Avenue, designed for people 55 and older and people with disabilities, in two Passive House Certified six-storey buildings. You apply to rent; there is no share to purchase and no strata lot. https://brightsidehomes.ca/residents-and-properties/new-projects/ and https://canadianarchitect.com/timbre-and-harmony-opens-providing-passive-house-non-market-housing-to-vancouver-seniors/
- **Q: How do I get into Timbre and Harmony, Grandview Towers or Adanac Towers?** Through the operators and the directory routes, not a listing: Timbre and Harmony is Brightside's building (apply with Brightside); Grandview Towers is applied for directly with the Baptist Housing Society of BC, per BC Housing's Zone 6 directory (May 2025); Adanac Towers is applied for directly with the V.E.L. Housing Foundation at the building. Expect waitlists. The City's own guidance is that staff do not manage these facilities or their rental processes. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/find-supportive-housing-for-seniors.aspx
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a BC 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. Note this is the strata rule; a non-profit rental like Timbre and Harmony sets its household rules through its operator and housing agreement, so ask Brightside directly for a mixed-age household. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. There is simply no such building to buy in this area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: Can a Grandview-Woodland house hold a suite, laneway or lock-off for a caregiver or family?** In the forms the R1-1 schedule states: single detached houses may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, duplexes may include secondary suites and lock-off units, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units — the practical aging-in-place route in an area with no 55+ strata to buy. Confirm the parcel's schedule (R1-1 or an RT district) first. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Is the new building at 800 Commercial Drive seniors housing?** No. The City's July 2024 approval is a 93-unit intergenerational social housing building (studios to three-bedrooms, at least 30 per cent at or below Housing Income Limits) operated by Entre Nous Femmes Housing Society for individuals and families facing barriers — approved, not yet built, and not age-restricted. https://Vancouver.ca/news-calendar/new-social-housing-grandview-woodland-neighbourhood-july-2024.aspx
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** The R5 RapidBus on Hastings Street (stop at Hastings and Commercial), route 20 on Commercial Drive itself to downtown, and Commercial-Broadway Station at the area's south-west edge for Expo and Millennium Line service — Timbre and Harmony at East 12th Avenue sits closest to that station end of the area, while Grandview Towers and Adanac Towers rely on the Clark Drive / East 1st Avenue bus network. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/ttR5.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt020.pdf
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Grandview-Woodland?** On verified sources, not inside the area: no long-term care home or retirement residence with a Grandview-Woodland address was verified. The in-area ladder stops at independent non-profit rental with supportive suites — Grandview Towers includes 30 suites with supportive housing services alongside its affordable seniors rental apartments — and anything beyond that runs through the Vancouver Coastal Health system outside the local area. https://www.baptisthousing.org/communities/lower-mainland/grandview-towers

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Timbre and Harmony nearly tripled its site — and the two names are the two buildings:** Brightside records the project replaces 57 units originally constructed in 1962 and 1971 with 157 units; the architect records two cleanly proportioned, six-storey, L-shaped volumes on either side of a central right-of-way, a 56 per cent reduction in energy use and carbon emissions against its baseline, and a heating demand of 12 kWh/m² per year under its Passive House design — the heating-bill profile of these suites is unlike anything else in the area's seniors stock. https://brightsidehomes.ca/residents-and-properties/new-projects/ and https://canadianarchitect.com/timbre-and-harmony-opens-providing-passive-house-non-market-housing-to-vancouver-seniors/
- **Grandview Towers is three towers with three addresses and one operator:** BC Housing's directory lists Grandview Towers #1 at 1455 East 3rd Avenue, #2 at 1425 East 3rd Avenue and #3 at 1420 East 2nd Avenue, all applied for through the Baptist Housing Society of BC — searches that use only one address miss two-thirds of the complex. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Even Grandview Towers' opening year is stated two ways by its own operator's materials:** the Baptist Housing corporate brochure gives "Year Opened: 1971" and locates the towers "on the top of East Vancouver's Grandview slope" with park-like grounds, while a Baptist Housing 50th-anniversary article says Grandview Towers "came on stream in 1973." A two-year discrepancy in the primary operator record — quote either year with its source. https://www.baptisthousing.org/communities/lower-mainland/grandview-towers and https://churchforvancouver.ca/baptist-housing-celebrates-50-years-in-16-seniors-communities-around-bc/
- **Adanac Towers is the area's quiet non-conforming tower:** a Council-file letter notes it as a 12-storey building in a neighbourhood of low-level structures, among the "existing nonconforming apartment buildings located in parts of Grandview on sites that are currently zoned for detached housing," a legacy of earlier zoning — which is why a seniors tower stands on Adanac Street among houses, and why nothing like it can be rebuilt under the current schedules. https://council.vancouver.ca/20210211/documents/phea1oppose2021-02-11_Redacted_000.pdf
- **The area's seniors stock was rebuilt before it was expanded:** Timbre and Harmony is a replacement project first — 57 Brightside seniors units from 1962 and 1971 came down so 157 could go up on the same East 12th Avenue land, with 20 per cent fully accessible and the rest readily adaptable. The net gain (+100 units, per the federal funding announcement coverage) is the largest single addition to Grandview-Woodland's seniors supply in the sources checked. https://brightsidehomes.ca/residents-and-properties/new-projects/ and http://globalawards.news/government-provides-funds-to-underpin-social-housing-project/
- **The Britannia Renewal is the amenity story beside the seniors pockets:** the City is working with partners on the renewal of the Britannia Community Services Centre site and the larger Britannia lands — library, elementary school, rink, fitness facility and pool a short walk from the Adanac and Grandview Towers pockets — the civic investment most likely to change daily life for the area's existing seniors renters. http://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/grandview.aspx
