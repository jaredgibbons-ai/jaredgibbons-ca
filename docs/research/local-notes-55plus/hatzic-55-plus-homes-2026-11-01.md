## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. There is no strata in Hatzic for this to attach to today — and note it does NOT govern manufactured-home parks: Mission Mobile Home Park's 55+ rule is a park rule under tenancy law, not a strata bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; exemptions attach to a "specified resident." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. These are strata exemptions — they do not automatically apply inside Mission Mobile Home Park; read the park rules instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022 (strata rule); SHORT-TERM rental bylaws are still allowed in stratas. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law.** Pad tenancies at Mission Mobile Home Park fall under BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act province-wide, whichever side of the City/FVRD line the park sits on: the resident owns the home and rents the pad; pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. Park rules, approval on assignment, and the park's age policy are park documents — read them before writing. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Secondary suites, two frames on one page.** Inside the City (Bench only): all secondary suites must be registered with the City, a suite is a self-contained living space in a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity, and suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes. In FVRD Electoral Area F (Prairie): secondary suites are freely permitted with no restrictions related to farm use, but they must be located within the principal dwelling and could not be a separate detached structure such as a garden suite. Which frame applies depends on which side of the City/FVRD line the parcel sits on. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program and https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf
- **ALR:** farmland parcels on the Prairie/Island side that sit in the Agricultural Land Reserve follow the ALC housing frame of one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). Whether a specific parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level — check the ALC maps for the PID. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **City frame (Bench only):** Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 is Mission's zoning bylaw, and Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026 is the current City plan. Neither governs the FVRD parts of this page. FVRD official community plan and zoning bylaws govern Electoral Areas F and G — the specific bylaw numbers and zone names for a given Prairie/Island lot: confirm with the FVRD for the PID. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026
- **Property taxes, two systems on one page — confirm the PID first.** City of Mission properties (Bench): 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026, and additional taxes levied for diking on top of the municipal, school, regional district and BC Assessment levies. Rural (FVRD Prairie/Island) properties: the owner receives the tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province, not the FVRD or the City; under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Flood:** the Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's south edge and low areas such as Hatzic Prairie — where Mission Mobile Home Park sits, per the city-wide file — are protected by the Dewdney Dyke system; flood status is parcel-specific. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf
