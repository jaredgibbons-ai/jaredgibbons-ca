## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019.** The area lies outside the Urban Area Boundary and is dominated by RS-2 (minimum lot area 0.4 ha) and RS-3 (0.8 ha or 2.0 ha, per the zone text) with Agricultural Land Reserve parcels; the same bylaw's schedule also lists A-1 at 2.0 ha, A-2 at 4.0 ha and A-3 at 8.0 ha. Confirm any parcel's zone per property. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **City plan: OCP Bylaw No. 7060-2014.** The North 256 Street Industrial Lands Area Plan — Council approved the OCP amendments creating a plan area on both sides of 256 Street north of 128 Avenue (about 500 acres, per the City release) — touches this area's northwest side; the plan establishes future designations and does not itself approve construction. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-approves-new-500-acre-256-street-industrial-area-plan-support-jobs-and-economic
- **Suites and added units:** one secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, contained within the single detached building, to a maximum of 40% of gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area. On ALR parcels, the provincial ALR Use Regulation also governs: it permits one secondary suite in the principal residence (s. 31) and an additional residence only if the s. 32 conditions are met. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **ALR, wells and septic:** ALR status is per-parcel — check the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder. Rural properties may be on wells and septic: wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database, and onsite sewerage must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation, which sets setbacks including 30 m from a well unless professional advice is obtained. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004
- **Flood and hazards:** the City's floodplain framework addresses flood risks from the Fraser River, the Alouette River system, steep creek watercourses and groundwater-related flooding; which Websters Corners lots are affected is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-climate-action-flood-resilience-environmental-protection-and-mckenney-creek
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025). https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ complex in Websters Corners itself?** No. No age-restricted strata, 55+ park, seniors rental or care residence with a Websters Corners address was verified in any source checked — and no condo or townhome complex of any kind in the sub-area was confirmed on an official, developer or strata source. The sub-area recorded 0 attached listings in September 2026 — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: What about the manufactured-home parks east of here — Ruskin Place, or the "55+ Ruskin Park" I saw mentioned?** Both are in Ruskin, a separate sub-area east of Websters Corners, not in this area. Ruskin Place is described as family-friendly with no age restrictions; the "55+ Ruskin Park" reference is a 2015 classified advertisement, with no current source checked verifying it in 2026. https://www.rodrhearyan.com/blog/affordable-manufactured-homes-ruskin-deroche-oasis-country-triple-creek-nicoman/ and https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/151125/MRNN151125_A39.pdf
- **Q: Where is the nearest actual 55+ ownership stock?** West in East Central Maple Ridge — the four Fraserview Village strata buildings around 116 Avenue and 225–227 Streets, plus the Alouette Retirement Mobile Home Park (12868 229 St, own the home, rent the pad) and the Haney Pioneer Village Co-op (11830 230 St, 60+). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata (in the city complexes)?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I age in place on a Websters Corners acreage and add a suite or second dwelling?** One secondary suite per lot is permitted in the A-1, A-2 and A-3 zones if it sits inside the house, is at most 40% of gross floor area and is not in the Floodplain Area; on ALR land the ALR Use Regulation also applies, and an additional separate residence is allowed only under its s. 32 conditions. Well and septic capacity is the practical limit — both are per-lot checks. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **Q: Is there bus service if I stop driving?** Route 701 runs along Dewdney Trunk Road, but most eastbound trips end at 248 Street and only some weekday trips continue toward Mission City Station; there is no West Coast Express station in the sub-area. Day-to-day life on a Websters Corners acreage effectively requires a car. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/194/tt701.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, is the community hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows with 24/7 emergency care, in the Town Centre to the west; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 121 – 11900 Haney Place, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. Mission's hospital services are across the eastern municipal boundary, but no source checked assigns Websters Corners addresses to a Mission facility — treat Ridge Meadows as the documented in-city route. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://www.healthlinkbc.ca/clinic/ridge-meadows-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- **Q: If I need care later, is there anything in Websters Corners?** No care or assisted-living residence in Websters Corners appears in BC's Assisted Living Registry; the registered Maple Ridge residences are all at downtown addresses, so ageing out of an acreage here means moving west to the Town Centre cluster. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=carouselnext&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&page=0&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc
