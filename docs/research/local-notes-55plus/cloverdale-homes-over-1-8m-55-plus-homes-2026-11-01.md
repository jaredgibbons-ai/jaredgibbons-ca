## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why the Manor Ridge directory line "rentals not allowed" cannot be read at face value. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Property transfer tax — the general tiers a downsizer budgets on BOTH ends of the move:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000; 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000; 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000. There is no PTT on selling — PTT is a buyer's tax, paid on the purchase the downsizer makes into the 55+ strata. Every purchase in this slug's band reaches the 3% tier on its top portion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Further tax over $3,000,000:** if a property has residential property worth over $3,000,000, a further 2% tax applies to the residential property value greater than $3,000,000; on a mixed-class property the further 2% applies only to the residential portion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Neither BC purchase exemption reaches this band — and neither helps a repeat buyer downsizing into 55+:** the first time home buyers' program full exemption caps at a fair market value of $835,000 and the newly built home exemption's full threshold is $1,100,000 with elimination at $1,150,000 — both far below this page's floor, and a downsizer has owned before in any case, so budget full PTT. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Home owner grant phase-out, directly relevant at this band:** the Province states the grant threshold for 2026 is $2,075,000 — a full regular grant at or below that assessed (or partitioned) value, reduced by $5 for each $1,000 of assessed value over $2,075,000, and $0 for the regular grant if the value is over $2,189,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **ALR housing frame (relevant to the band's acreage cards):** for parcels in the Agricultural Land Reserve, the ALC housing rules are one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), with any secondary suite inside the principal residence. ALR status is parcel-specific. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000** governs Cloverdale, including the small-scale multi-unit housing framework: up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. **OCP: "Surrey 2050" Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900**, finally adopted June 15, 2026, replacing the 2013 OCP Bylaw No. 18020. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **First-home programs:** the First Home Savings Account and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file — no live CRA page stating the current limits was read in this pass, so no figure is published here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: I am selling a Cloverdale home over $1.8M and downsizing. Is there 55+ product at anywhere near that price point in Cloverdale?** No verified 55+ ownership product in Cloverdale is marketed as upper-end or luxury product in any source checked — the verified stock is a 1987 downtown condo building (Carriage House) and a 1989 townhouse complex (Manor Ridge). No price is stated for any complex in this file; the point is the product type, not a figure: the $1.8M band is detached houses and acreage parcels, and the 55+ supply is strata form. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Cloverdale?** Two strata complexes verified in this research: Carriage House (17695 58 Ave) and The Estates at Manor Ridge (6140 192 St). No 55+ manufactured-home park, membership complex or subsidized ownership product was verified inside the Cloverdale community. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: Do I pay property transfer tax when I SELL the $1.8M+ home?** No — PTT is paid by the buyer when a property is registered. Your tax event is on the purchase side: the 55+ home you buy is taxed at the general tiers (1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000), with the further 2% only on residential value over $3,000,000 — a tier a typical 55+ strata purchase will not reach. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Can I use the first-time buyer exemption on the 55+ purchase? I have not bought in years.** No on the band's facts: the program's full exemption caps at a fair market value of $835,000, and a seller downsizing out of a home they owned does not meet the program's first-time conditions as published — budget full PTT on the 55+ purchase at the general tiers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If we sell the house and buy into a 55+ strata, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Manor Ridge's own listings soften it further in their own words: 1 occupant must be 55+. Confirm the complex's bylaw in its strata documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: Could I buy the 55+ unit first, rent it out while my house sells, then move in?** In a strata, long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches: the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed when we downsize?** The two Cloverdale complexes are opposites in the sources checked: Carriage House listings state "No pets permitted," while Manor Ridge listings allow 1 pet with restrictions. A pet from the detached house is a real filter — check it complex by complex before shortlisting. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6140-192-street/8
- **Q: Does the home owner grant change when I move?** The grant is claimed on the principal residence you occupy, and at this slug's band the phase-out is the live issue: for 2026 the threshold is $2,075,000, the regular grant reduces by $5 per $1,000 over that value, and it is $0 over $2,189,000. Leaving a home assessed in that range and claiming on a lower-assessed 55+ strata is a different grant calculation — confirm the assessed value of the specific unit, not the old house. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Q: Is Bethshan Gardens something I can buy into instead?** No. Bethshan Gardens (17528 59 Ave) is an independent-living rental residence owned by the Cloverdale Senior Citizen's Housing Society — you rent there; you do not buy a unit. Its numeric age eligibility is UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked, so ask the society directly. http://bethshangardens.org/
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool when I eventually sell the strata unit?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. That trade-off is built into every BC 55+ strata since the 2022 law change confined age bylaws to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **Cloverdale's whole verified 55+ ownership supply is two complexes, and they are different products.** A 1987 downtown condo building with a no-pets rule (Carriage House) and a 1989 townhouse complex on 192 Street that allows a pet (Manor Ridge). - **The city-wide Surrey 55+ notes understate Cloverdale.** The Surrey city-wide file in this series lists Manor Ridge as Cloverdale's only verified complex; Carriage House was verified separately in this series from three independent listings (R3164723, R3080712, R3089060) that all state 55+ in terms. A Cloverdale-only search run off the city-wide file alone misses half the community's verified stock.
- **Cloverdale's seniors rental sits one block from its seniors condo.** Bethshan Gardens (17528 59 Ave, society-owned rental) and Carriage House (17695 58 Ave, strata ownership) are both in the downtown core — the local ladder from owned condo to rental-with-community exists inside two blocks, but the rental side is a society residence, not a strata you can buy later. http://bethshangardens.org/
