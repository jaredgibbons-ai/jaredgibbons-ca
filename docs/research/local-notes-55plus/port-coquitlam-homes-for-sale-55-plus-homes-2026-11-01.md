## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Port Coquitlam splits into a north side and a south side. The six north neighbourhoods are Birchland Manor, Glenwood, Lincoln Park, Oxford Heights, Riverwood and Woodland Acres; the four south neighbourhoods are Central Port Coquitlam (the downtown), Citadel, Lower Mary Hill and Mary Hill. (Neighbourhood list credited to the City of Port Coquitlam: https://www.fnw-recruitment.com/port-coquitlam)
- The Pitt River runs along the city's east side; the City's dike and drainage system along it protects low-lying neighbourhoods, with drainage flowing through Hyde Creek and DeBoville Slough into the Pitt River. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects/cedar-drainage-pump-station-0
- Shaughnessy Street is the main north–south street through the downtown core; Wilson Avenue is the downtown's east–west civic street — City Hall area, the Port Coquitlam Community Centre at 2150 Wilson Ave, and the seniors housing cluster all sit within a few blocks of it. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/new-community-centre-brings-amenities-excitement-port-coquitlam
- The West Coast Express station is at 2125 Kingsway Avenue, just south of the downtown core. https://www.translink.ca/parkandride?utm_source=direct&utm_medium=shorturl&utm_campaign=parkandride
- Coast Meridian Road is the main route from downtown up to the north side, carried over the CP rail yard by the Coast Meridian Overpass (a $134-million project, the city's largest to date). https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/terry-fox-tributes-unveiled-port-coquitlam-community-centre
- Dominion Avenue anchors the east side of the city (Archbishop Carney Regional Secondary is at 1335 Dominion Ave); the OCP names the "Dominion Riverfront" as one of the city's new residential neighbourhoods. http://www.acrss.org/ and https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11285
- School District 43 (Coquitlam) serves the city; its two public secondary schools in Port Coquitlam are Riverside Secondary (2215 Reeve St) and Terry Fox Secondary (1260 Riverwood Gate). https://www.sd43.bc.ca/terryfox and http://www.sd43.bc.ca/riverside/
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: almost all of it is in the downtown / Central Port Coquitlam flat, within a few blocks of each other — Atkins Avenue (Mayfair Terrace), Kelly Avenue (Aspira Astoria and Aspira Mayfair), Mary Hill Road (Kew Lifestyles at Montrose) and Hawthorne Avenue (Hawthorne campus, Mary Hill edge). The one exception is the Dogwood seniors buildings on Seymour Street in Lincoln Park, on the north side. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Prairie Avenue and the Oxford Heights / Coast Meridian corridor: no source found for any 55+ complex there — UNCONFIRMED as a 55+ concentration.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Port Coquitlam's verified 55+ ownership stock is exactly one strata building — Mayfair Terrace (46 units).** Everything else verified in the city is 55+ rental, retirement/care, or subsidized rental, each labelled separately below. Listing-stated figures — confirm against the strata documents and the current service agreement before relying on them. https://www.zolo.ca/port-coquitlam-real-estate/2266-atkins-avenue/311

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw, 2008, No. 3630** is Port Coquitlam's zoning bylaw; Mayfair Terrace sits in a site-specific CD-19 comprehensive development zone created for that project. http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf and https://rennie.com/listings/atkins-2266-avenue-mayfair-terrace-108-port-coquitlam
- **Secondary suites:** the City permits secondary suites only in the RS-1, RS-2, RS-3 and RS-4 zones, and a building permit is required. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/building-permits/secondary-suites
- **Suite utility-charge exemptions that matter to seniors:** the City waives 50% of the extra suite utility levy when the suite is occupied by a parent or grandparent of the owner, and 100% when a paid caregiver (minimum $150/month) lives in the suite to care for a permanently disabled owner or family member; applications are annual, due by Dec 31 (2026 amounts: $964 full / $482 half). https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills/secondary-suite-exemptions
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2013, No. 3838** carries a "Non-Market and Special Needs Housing" policy section in §7.2 (Housing and Neighbourhoods), and its 2024 amendment confirms secondary suites are permitted in single-detached houses, duplexes and triplexes, with accessory dwelling units accessory to single-detached houses. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11285
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4: Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Maple Ridge, Pitt Meadows directory, May 2025), which is how the Dogwood buildings fill units. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **FHSA / Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits:** UNVERIFIED — no live CRA page was read for this file, so no limit figure is stated.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Besides strata fees, is there a second mandatory monthly charge at Mayfair Terrace?** Yes. Listing information for the building states a required base service plan on top of the strata fee — $715/mo for the first resident and $400 for a second (MLS R3179064, Oct 2026) — covering daily breakfasts plus 10 meals, weekly housekeeping, a 24-hour emergency response system, activities and transportation. Treat the figures as listing-stated and confirm the current amounts in the strata documents and service agreement. https://www.zolo.ca/port-coquitlam-real-estate/2266-atkins-avenue/311
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, and listings for Mayfair Terrace state rentals are allowed. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.zolo.ca/port-coquitlam-real-estate/2266-atkins-avenue/311
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — i.e., be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. That is the trade-off built into every BC 55+ strata since the 2022 law change confined age bylaws to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** It is building-by-building. Sources for Mayfair Terrace record pets allowed with restrictions, and current listings specify lap pets. Aspira Astoria, the rental residence on Kelly Ave, describes itself as pet-friendly. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/mayfair-terrace-2266-atkins-ave and https://www.aspiralife.ca/our-locations/british-columbia/port-coquitlam/aspira-astoria-retirement-living/
- **Q: What about parking and storage?** At Mayfair Terrace, listings state secure underground parking and a storage locker are included with units; the downtown buildings are low-rise (4 storeys), so parking is not the tower-style scramble buyers may expect. Confirm the specific unit's stall and locker on the Form B. https://rennie.com/listings/atkins-2266-avenue-mayfair-terrace-108-port-coquitlam
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Port Coquitlam has no hospital of its own. The Tri-Cities hospital is Eagle Ridge Hospital at 475 Guildford Way in Port Moody — a Fraser Health community hospital with a 24/7 emergency department, diagnostics, inpatient and outpatient care, rehabilitation and surgery specialties including urology, plastics and orthopedics. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital#.XSqzNyUTGEc
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** The West Coast Express stops at Port Coquitlam Station (2125 Kingsway Ave), but it is a commuter service only: five trains in each direction on weekdays (mornings toward Vancouver, evenings toward Mission), with no weekend or holiday service, per TransLink's timetable effective Jan 5, 2026. SkyTrain does not reach Port Coquitlam — the nearest Millennium Line stations are in Coquitlam — so local buses plus the station bus loop are the everyday option. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- **Q: Is downtown PoCo in a floodplain?** Parts of low-lying Port Coquitlam sit behind the Pitt River dike system, and the City actively maintains it — the Cedar Creek Pump Station, which sits within the dike system and protects a large upstream area of homes, businesses and schools, is being rebuilt with construction scheduled summer 2026 to summer 2027. Flood status has to be checked lot by lot; it is not uniform across the downtown. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects/cedar-drainage-pump-station-0
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in PoCo?** The in-city ladder is: independent strata living with a service plan (Mayfair Terrace), independent/assisted rental (Aspira Astoria, Hawthorne Tower's 70 assisted-living suites), then long-term care (Hawthorne Lodge, 131 beds; Shaughnessy Seniors Community on Wilson Ave). Hawthorne's programs are publicly funded through Fraser Health. https://hawthornecare.com/ and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3APort%20Coquitlam&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASupportive%20Recovery&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **There is no SkyTrain coming on any funded timeline.** TransLink's Transport 2050: 10-Year Priorities (approved June 2022) commits only to an "exploratory business case" for extending the Millennium Line to Port Coquitlam, with a fast Port Coquitlam Express bus as the interim measure. As of the Mayors' Council's Feb 19, 2026 work plan, that exploratory business case is still simply "underway" — no route, no stations, no construction funding and no dates have been approved. https://roar-assets-auto.rbl.ms/files/53822/attachment-2-transport-2050-10-year-priorities-for-translink.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/about-translink/governance-and-board/council-minutes-and-reports/2026/february/report--2026-mc-budget--feb-2026.pdf
- **The downtown already had its big rebuild.** The $132-million Port Coquitlam Community Centre (205,000 sq ft, 2150 Wilson Ave) fully opened Oct 2, 2021 after four years of phased construction — three arenas, leisure pool, fitness centre, Terry Fox Library and 425 underground parking stalls; its south plaza is Terry Fox Hometown Square. It sits within walking distance of every downtown 55+ building in Section 2. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/new-community-centre-brings-amenities-excitement-port-coquitlam and https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/terry-fox-tributes-unveiled-port-coquitlam-community-centre
- **The newest 55+ stock in the city is rental, not strata.** Kew Lifestyles at Montrose (56 units, 2430 Mary Hill Rd) completed in 2023 beside the Community Centre; no new age-restricted strata project appears in any source checked — the strata 55+ supply is still the single 46-unit Mayfair Terrace, built in 2000. https://www.soarchitects.com/portfolio/kew-lifestyles-at-montrose-condominiums/ and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/mayfair-terrace-2266-atkins-ave
- **Provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules were folded into PoCo's bylaws in 2024.** The City's May 28, 2024 council report recommended amending Zoning Bylaw No. 3630 and OCP Bylaw No. 3838 to comply with Bill 44 (the provincial SSMUH law), allowing four to six units on most single-residential lots (three or fewer on small lots, six near prescribed bus stops) — which is why older RS-1 to RS-4 streets around the downtown are slowly adding suites, coach houses and multiplexes. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/ssmuh-staff-report-may-28-2024
- **Transit-Oriented Areas are now in the OCP.** OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 4373 (2024) added a Transit-Oriented Areas policy section — provincially required density designations around prescribed transit stations — alongside the housing-policy rewrite that confirmed suites in houses, duplexes and triplexes. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11285
- **Flood infrastructure is mid-upgrade right now.** The City's Cedar Drainage Pump Station project (Cedar Drive near Chelsea Ave, in the dike system at the northeast end of the city) is in tendering with construction set for summer 2026 to summer 2027 and completion expected late summer 2027, funded partly by the Government of Canada; the 1970s-era station is getting fish-friendly pumps, a backup generator and seismic upgrades. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects/cedar-drainage-pump-station-0
- **The seniors-housing cluster is older than the current buildings suggest.** The Port Coquitlam Senior Citizens' Housing Society has operated since 1958 and runs both the Hawthorne campus (2111 Hawthorne Ave) and the Dogwood buildings on Seymour St — the non-profit backbone of seniors housing in the city, alongside the Legion's RJ Kent Manor. https://hawthornecare.com/ and https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/social-housing-services-resource-guide-2020v4
- **One building, two frontages, two products.** Mayfair Terrace the strata (2266 Atkins Ave) and Aspira Mayfair the registered assisted-living service (2267 Kelly Ave) are the same complex seen from two streets — a buyer reads the strata documents for the purchase and the service agreement for the monthly plan; neither document substitutes for the other. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3APort%20Coquitlam&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASupportive%20Recovery&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc
