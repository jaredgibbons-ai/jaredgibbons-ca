## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- For this area, "South Granville" means the Granville Street corridor on Vancouver's west side from the Granville Bridge to West 16th Avenue — not the downtown Granville entertainment corridor. The corridor runs through the Fairview local area, with Shaughnessy at its south end; the research file frames it as the Granville Street corridor roughly between West 5th and West 16th Avenues, spanning the Fairview and Shaughnessy local areas. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/fairview.aspx and (area research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-granville.md, Section 1.)
- The South Granville Business Improvement Association district runs along Granville Street from the Granville Bridge to 16th Avenue, branded "Gallery Row" for its concentration of art galleries (the BIA names Bau-Xi Gallery and Uno Langmann Gallery among them), alongside fashion and home-décor shops and the Meinhardt Fine Foods grocery; the residential side streets run east toward Oak Street and west toward Burrard Street. http://www.southgranville.org/
- The corridor's landmark is the Stanley Theatre (the Arts Club's Stanley stage), 2750 Granville Street at West 12th Avenue — a 650-seat venue in an art deco building that opened in 1931 as a cinema and vaudeville house. https://artsclub.com/about/venue-rentals/stanley-industrial-alliance-stage
- Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, operated by Vancouver Coastal Health, sits a few blocks east of the corridor and is BC's referral centre for many provincial programs as well as a teaching hospital. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital
- South Granville sits inside the Broadway Plan area (Clark Drive to Vine Street, 1st to 16th Avenues; approved June 22, 2022), which provides for a South Granville subway station area with buildings up to 30–40 storeys near the station; the Broadway Subway is scheduled to enter service in fall 2027, and transit today is buses including the 99 B-Line along West Broadway. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025TT0110-001048
- Schools: L'École Bilingue Elementary, 1166 West 14th Avenue, is a Kindergarten-to-Grade 7 single-track French Immersion school in Fairview; the sample address run in the research file (1166 West 14th Ave) returns Emily Carr Elementary / Eric Hamber Secondary as its regular-program catchment. Catchments for other corridor addresses: UNCONFIRMED. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods
- Where the seniors housing actually is (per the sibling file): one block west of Granville Street, on West 12th and West 14th Avenues — the supportive-housing building at 1450 West 12th Avenue and the long-term care home at 1645 West 14th Avenue, with the South Granville Seniors Centre (a centre, not housing) at 1420 West 12th Avenue between them. See granville file, Sections 1–2. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **The Broadway Plan governs this corridor's future form.** The plan covers Clark Drive to Vine Street and 1st to 16th Avenues, was approved by Council on June 22, 2022 and updated in December 2024, and provides for a South Granville Station area with buildings up to 30–40 storeys near the station. The plan is a policy framework — most higher-density forms still require rezoning, so plan heights are not as-of-right zoning. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx and https://syc.vancouver.ca/projects/broadway-plan/broadway-plan-summary-highlight-document.pdf
- **City of Vancouver Seniors Housing Strategy (approved July 23, 2024):** the City's first seniors housing strategy, with a Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy (last amended May 19, 2026) to enable more seniors housing across all neighbourhoods and a Resident Relocation Policy adding protections for seniors in community care and assisted living residences in the event of a redevelopment. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/seniors-housing-strategy-address-needs-of-older-adults-july-2024.aspx and https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-rezoning-seniors-housing.pdf
- **City zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575.** For detached-house lots off the corridor, By-law No. 13817 replaced the old RS districts with the R1-1 district, allowing up to 6 strata units (or 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots, subject to the district schedule. Citywide land-use direction now comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf
- **Tax calendar:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. The Empty Homes Tax matters more here than in most areas because of the corridor's second-home and investor stock: an annual declaration is required, and the tax is 3% of assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in South Granville?** On the corridor itself, none — the sibling granville file verified no age-restricted strata building on Granville Street or its West 12th/West 14th side streets. The one verified 55+ ownership building for the wider area is NEW in this file: Terraces on 7th (1570 West 7th Ave), a strata retirement community with a 55+ age restriction — but note its MLS sub-area is Fairview VW and it sits west of Granville at 7th Avenue, so search under Fairview as well as South Granville. Sources: Section 2 above and the granville file, Section 2.
- **Q: Besides strata fees, is there a second mandatory charge at Terraces on 7th?** Yes, on the listing evidence: a mandatory service agreement that includes 10 meals per month, on top of which the listing records weekly housekeeping, 24-hour concierge, fitness centre, lounge, library, rooftop deck and theatre. The current dollar amount of the service agreement is not stated in the sources checked — UNCONFIRMED — confirm it in the strata documents and the operator's current agreement before an offer. https://www.robbiejohal.com/listing/r3117996-1570-w-7th-avenue-1001-vancouver-bc-v6j-5m1/brochure
- **Q: Is Shaughnessy Lodge on West 10th a seniors building? The name suggests it.** No source says so. It is a 69-unit general-market rental apartment building (built in 1912) at West 10th Avenue and Birch Street, managed as ordinary rental apartments with studio and one-bedroom plans. Neither "Shaughnessy" nor "Lodge" in its name is an age designation. https://www.seinecliffe.com/copy-of-queen-anne and https://www.apartments.com/shaughnessy-lodge-vancouver-bc/4hpmtyh/
- **Q: Can I still move into Chalmers Lodge on West 12th?** No — see granville file: Chalmers Lodge closed in 2022, its residents were relocated to the Rideau Residence in Burnaby, and the building now operates as Granville Gates supportive housing under Connective.
- **Q: Can I buy a suite at South Granville Park Lodge?** No — see granville file: it is a long-term care home at 1645 West 14th Avenue; admission is a care decision and there is nothing to buy.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live at Terraces on 7th?** Provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) — that is the framework a 55+ strata bylaw operates under. Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. Confirm Terraces' own bylaw wording in its strata documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit I buy at Terraces on 7th, or elsewhere on the corridor?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022 — and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. In a 55+ building the tenant still has to satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption. Purpose-built rental buildings on the corridor also carry tenant-relocation implications under the Broadway Plan's renter protections, which an investor should verify before purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Q: When does the subway actually reach South Granville?** On the Province's schedule, fall 2027: the Broadway Subway is a 5.7 km Millennium Line extension with six new underground stations including South Granville Station. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025TT0110-001048
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue — a few blocks east of Granville Street, operated by Vancouver Coastal Health and BC's referral centre for many provincial programs. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital
- **Q: The sources disagree on when Terraces on 7th was built. Which is right?** Unresolved in this file: the MLS listing states 2002 and the building source states 2003. The one-year difference does not change the building's 55+ status, but a buyer should take the year from the strata plan / City records at the offer stage rather than from either secondary source. https://www.robbiejohal.com/listing/r3117996-1570-w-7th-avenue-1001-vancouver-bc-v6j-5m1/brochure and http://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/terraces-on-7-1570-west-ave

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The area's one 55+ ownership building hides under a different sub-area name.** Terraces on 7th is filed by the board as Fairview VW, described by its operator side as being in "Fairview and South Granville," and marketed on its steps-to-South-Granville-shops location. A buyer (or an agent) who searches only "South Granville 55+" will find the care home and the supportive housing from the granville file and miss the only strata building in the wider area that they can actually buy. http://www.terracesonseventh.com/ and https://www.robbiejohal.com/listing/r3117996-1570-w-7th-avenue-1001-vancouver-bc-v6j-5m1/brochure
- **This file and the granville file must not be added together.** The corridor's care and supportive-housing buildings (South Granville Park Lodge, Granville Gates) are verified once, in the granville file, and only cross-referenced here. The combined verified 55+ OWNERSHIP count for South Granville across both files is one — Terraces on 7th, added in this file — not one per file.
- **The corridor's seniors story is still a closure, not a building boom** — see granville file, Section 5, for the Chalmers Lodge history (decades of non-profit seniors housing at 1450 West 12th Ave, residents moved to Burnaby in 2022, the building reopened as supportive housing) and for the unresolved one-unit disagreement in Granville Gates' count (operator 114 vs BC Housing 115). That detail is recorded once, in the sibling, and is not repeated here.
- **There is a seniors centre on the West 12th block — but it is not housing** — see granville file, Section 5: the South Granville Seniors Centre at 1420 West 12th Avenue works with adults aged 55+ on programs and has no homes, which is why the block reads as a seniors district.
- **A second name trap sits two blocks off Granville.** Shaughnessy Lodge (1298 West 10th Ave, NEW checked finding in this file) combines a prestige neighbourhood name with a lodge suffix and is neither in Shaughnessy nor a seniors building — it is a 1912 general-market rental apartment building. Between it, Chalmers Lodge (closed) and South Granville Park Lodge (care), three of the corridor's four "lodge" names mislead in three different ways. https://www.seinecliffe.com/copy-of-queen-anne
- **Plan policy is not zoning on Granville Street** — see granville file, Section 5: the Broadway Plan contemplates 30–40 storeys near the future South Granville Station, but the plan is a policy framework (approved 2022, updated December 2024) and most higher-density forms still require a rezoning.
