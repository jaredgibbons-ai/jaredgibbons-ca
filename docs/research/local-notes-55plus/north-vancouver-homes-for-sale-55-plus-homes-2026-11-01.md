# North Vancouver — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Municipality warning that runs through this whole file:** "North Vancouver" is two municipalities — the City of North Vancouver and the District of North Vancouver — with separate councils, OCPs, zoning bylaws and tax bills. Every complex below is labelled City or District where a source settles it, and UNCONFIRMED where it does not. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The District wraps around three sides of the City: the District runs from Deep Cove and Lynn Valley to Capilano and the Lions Gate area, while the City is the compact waterfront municipality along the Lonsdale corridor. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- The City's spine is Lonsdale Avenue — Lower, Central and Upper Lonsdale — and Lower and Central Lonsdale together are recognized by Metro Vancouver as a Regional City Centre. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Economic-Development/business-opportunities
- Where the verified 55+ strata stock sits: Central Lonsdale (Ridge Park Gardens and Sundale, both on Chesterfield Avenue, City), Upper Lonsdale (Vista 29 and Somerset Green, both on West 29th Street — see the municipality flag in Section 2), Delbrook (Queensbrook, District side), Edgemont Village (Edgemont Villa and Highland House, District), Lynn Valley (Balmoral House, District) and Cedar Village / Kirkstone (Kirkstone Gardens, District). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Where the Kiwanis seniors rental stock sits: Lower Lonsdale (Kiwanis Towers, 170 West 2nd St, City), Central Lonsdale (St. Andrews Place, 1480 St. Andrews Ave, City) and Lynn Valley (Lynn Manor and Lynn Woods, both on Whiteley Court, District). https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-tower/ and https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/lynn-manor/
- School District 44 serves both municipalities, with no middle tier: elementary is Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary is Grade 8 to Grade 12; catchments are address-based and must be confirmed with the district for the exact address. https://queenmary.sd44.ca/
- The SeaBus sails from Lonsdale Quay (City, foot of Lonsdale Avenue) to Waterfront Station in Vancouver; the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus runs Park Royal to Metrotown via Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange; route 230 runs Lonsdale Quay to Upper Lonsdale via Lonsdale Avenue and route 229 runs Lonsdale Quay to Lynn Valley. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt998.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/news/2026/september/r2%20rapidbus%20launches%20between%20phibbs%20exchange%20and%20metrotown%20three%20months%20early%20and%20under%20budget
- Health care for the whole North Shore: Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street (City), operated by Vancouver Coastal Health; the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 221 West Esplanade in Lower Lonsdale. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- Recreation anchors near the 55+ clusters: the City's John Braithwaite Community Centre at 145 West 1st Street and North Vancouver City Library at 120 West 14th Street (Central Lonsdale); in the District, Lynn Valley Town Centre is the District's Municipal Town Centre and Edgemont Village is the commercial centre of the Upper Capilano area. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Economic-Development/business-opportunities and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why older directory entries that say "rentals not allowed" (Ridge Park Gardens, Section 2) cannot be read at face value. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Two zoning bylaws, and they do not cross over.** The City is governed by City of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw, 1995, No. 6700 and its Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2014, No. 8400; the District is governed by District OCP Bylaw 7900 and the District's own zoning bylaw. District rules do not apply in the City and City rules do not apply in the District. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **City small-scale multi-unit housing (GO Zone):** in the City's Ground Oriented Zone, a lot of 280 m² or less allows up to 3 units; a lot over 280 m² allows up to 4 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 designation and up to 6 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations. This affects the detached streets around the Lonsdale 55+ buildings, not the strata lots inside them. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **Kiwanis eligibility is not strata law.** The Kiwanis buildings' 55+, 60+ and 65+ ages are operator eligibility rules for below-market rental housing, with income and independent-living requirements set by the society — they are not strata age bylaws, and a unit cannot be bought. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-tower/
- **Streamside and waterfront permit areas (City):** the City's Shipyards and Harbourside Waterfront precincts are Development Permit Areas, and Streamside Protection rules apply within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse or 10 metres of a ravine bank — relevant to renovation and rebuild plans on the side streets near the Lonsdale corridor, not to a strata unit purchase itself. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits
- **Taxes:** City and District properties are taxed by their own municipality (plus shared school and regional levies); the two municipalities set separate rates and due dates. Current-year City and District due dates and penalty dates: UNVERIFIED in this file.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is my building in the City or the District — and why does it matter?** It decides your zoning bylaw, your OCP, your council and your tax bill: the City runs on Zoning Bylaw No. 6700 and OCP Bylaw, 2014, No. 8400, the District on OCP Bylaw 7900 and its own zoning. West 29th Street buildings (Vista 29, Somerset Green) sit at the Upper Lonsdale edge — confirm the municipality on the tax notice before repeating a listing's wording. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in North Vancouver?** Nine strata complexes were verified in this research: Somerset Green and Vista 29 (Upper Lonsdale), Ridge Park Gardens and Sundale (Central Lonsdale), Queensbrook (Delbrook), Edgemont Villa and Highland House (Edgemont Village), Balmoral House (Lynn Valley) and Kirkstone Gardens (Cedar Village). Three of them — Sundale, Highland House and Kirkstone Gardens — rest on a single brokerage 55+ list in this pass, so confirm their bylaws in the strata documents. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-buildings-north-vancouver/
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55?** No. At Vista 29 and Ridge Park Gardens, listings state one owner / one occupant must be 55+. Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt in any case — confirm the complex's own bylaw wording in the Form B. https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/188-west-29-street/208 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Older building directories that still print "rentals not allowed" are recording the old bylaw, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are the Kiwanis buildings something I can buy into?** No. Kiwanis Towers (55+), St. Andrews Place (55+), Lynn Woods (age by unit tier — see the conflict flagged in Section 2) and Lynn Manor (65+) are below-market rental housing run by the Kiwanis North Shore Housing Society, with eligibility requirements and applications accepted year-round. Nothing in them is for sale. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-tower/ and https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/lynn-manor/
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Building by building. Listings record Somerset Green as a pet-friendly 55+ community, Vista 29 as pet friendly, and Ridge Park Gardens as allowing pets with restrictions (one listing specifies 2 cats or 1 dog and 1 cat). Kiwanis states pets are not permitted in its seniors housing communities — the opposite rule, in the rental stock. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3118271-419-121-w-29th-street-north-vancouver-british-columbia-v7n4l6-635000/brochure and https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/lynn-woods/
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** Lions Gate Hospital is at 231 East 15th Street in the City — walking distance from the Central Lonsdale 55+ buildings — and the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 221 West Esplanade in Lower Lonsdale, near the SeaBus. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- **Q: Can I get downtown without a car from these buildings?** From the Lonsdale buildings, yes: the SeaBus runs from Lonsdale Quay to Waterfront Station, route 230 runs up Lonsdale Avenue to Upper Lonsdale, route 229 runs to Lynn Valley, and the R2 RapidBus crosses the south side east–west. From Edgemont Village and Lynn Valley, the trip is bus-first to the Quay or Phibbs Exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt998.pdf
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **North Vancouver's 55+ map is really two maps.** The strata ownership stock clusters on the Lonsdale corridor and in the District's village centres (Edgemont, Lynn Valley), while the below-market rental stock is one operator — Kiwanis — in Lower/Central Lonsdale and Lynn Valley. A buyer who searches only one system misses the other entirely. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-buildings-north-vancouver/ and https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/
- **The same Whiteley Court address pair runs two different age rules.** Lynn Manor (2555 Whiteley Ct) is 65+ and Lynn Woods (2551 Whiteley Ct), directly beside it, is tiered 55+/60+ after a council-approved lowering from an original 65+ rezoning condition — "the Kiwanis building in Lynn Valley" is not one rule. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/lynn-manor/ and http://globalawards.news/charity-cannot-fill-affordable-seniors-housing-new-build/
- **Kiwanis is older than the buildings suggest.** BC Housing records that the society's commitment to below-market seniors housing on the North Shore began in 1948, and that it has operated Lynn Manor since 1974 — the non-profit backbone of North Shore seniors rental, across both municipalities. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/new-affordable-rental-homes-for-seniors-coming-to-north-vancouver
- **Two of the nine strata buildings are small enough that listings are rare events.** Queensbrook is a 22-unit building and Ridge Park Gardens is 42 units in their directory/listing records — in buildings this size, a 55+ search can sit empty for months and then turn over in a single listing. https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/678-west-queens-road/206 and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/ridge-park-gardens-2059-chesterfield-ave
- **The Edgemont name covers both an ownership building and a care residence, a few blocks apart.** Edgemont Villa (3151 Connaught Cres) is a strata you can buy; Amica Edgemont Village (3225 Highland Blvd) is a rental care residence registered as class SN. Listings and directories shorten both to "Edgemont" — check which product a search result actually is. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3102670-3151-connaught-crescent-206-north-vancouver-bc-v7r-4x6/brochure and https://www.amica.ca/edgemont-village
- **The newest seniors supply on the Lonsdale corridor is care and mixed rental, not 55+ strata.** The Lonsdale Square redevelopment pairs a 99-unit Sunrise assisted living / dementia care building with general condos and rentals, and the Neighbourhood House Hub added 89 mixed family/senior affordable rentals — no new age-restricted strata project appears in any source checked, so the strata 55+ supply remains the older Lonsdale, Edgemont and Lynn Valley buildings in Section 2. https://renx.ca/darwin-build-seniors-living-office-vancouver-lonsdale-square and https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/opening-89-new-affordable-homes-in-north-vancouver
