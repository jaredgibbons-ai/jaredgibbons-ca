## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. That is why a "45+" label, like Country Park Village's or Promontory Lake Estates', cannot be a valid strata age bylaw in BC today — at non-strata (leasehold / manufactured-home) communities the restriction, if any, has to live in the lease or park rules instead, and its exact legal form at those two communities is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City short-term rentals are separately regulated** under the City of Chilliwack's Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** is Chilliwack's zoning bylaw. Its small-scale multi-unit (SSMUH) amendment, Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024), allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Suites and accessory units:** under Bylaw 5000 only 1 accessory dwelling unit may sit on a lot; a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the habitable floor space of the building, and a garden suite is capped at 100 m². https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Hillside permit areas apply on the Promontory side:** the OCP's Schedule D separates D-2 Hillside Environment from D-5 Hillside Development; which permit areas apply to a specific parcel, and that parcel's Flood Construction Level, are per-parcel City checks and are UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel in these notes. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Property taxes:** City of Chilliwack notices are mailed at the end of May, taxes are due the first working day after July 1, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and a further 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ strata building I can buy in Sardis East Vedder?** No — none was verified in this pass. The verified Sardis-side 55+ strata buildings are all west of Vedder Road: Knight Lodge (45660 Knight Rd), Cotton Ridge (45645 Knight Rd), The Sapphire (7685 Amber Dr) and 7651 Amber Dr. See the Sardis West Vedder file in this series.
- **Q: Country Park Village shows up under "Sardis East Vedder" — is it a 55+ community?** No. Board data labels 45918 Knight Road "Sardis East Vedder," but the community markets a "45+ lifestyle," its homes are manufactured homes, and its title is recorded as First Nations Lease — it is a leasehold adult community, not a 55+ strata. https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3102457-45918-knight-road-6-chilliwack-bc-v2r-3x4/brochure
- **Q: What about Promontory Lake Estates, up on Promontory Road?** Also 45+, not 55+ — listings describe a gated 45+ rancher community, and board data files the address under "Sardis South" even though Promontory Road is on this side of Vedder Road. Listing text shows both a maintenance fee and a lease fee, so read the tenure documents before treating it as ordinary freehold strata. https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/6001-promontory-road/91 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3078460-231-6001-promontory-roadsardis-south-chilliwack-british-columbia-v2r3e3-615000/brochure
- **Q: If a complex says 45+, can it legally keep out a 50-year-old — or a 30-year-old?** In a BC strata, no: since Nov 24, 2022 the only valid age bylaw is 55+, so a strata "45+" bylaw is invalid. Country Park Village and Promontory Lake Estates are not ordinary freehold stratas on the sources opened, so any age rule there would sit in lease or park documents instead — that form is UNCONFIRMED for both, and a buyer under 55 should get the actual documents before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If we buy in a true 55+ strata nearby, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals (and the City regulates short-term rentals under Bylaw 5141). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at the adult communities here?** Country Park Village's listing states 3 pets welcome with no size restrictions. Promontory Lake Estates' listing text records 2 pets with an 18-inch withers limit (listing-stated). Both are listing statements, not the communities' own documents — confirm against the current rules before relying on them. https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3102457-45918-knight-road-6-chilliwack-bc-v2r-3x4/brochure and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3078460-231-6001-promontory-roadsardis-south-chilliwack-british-columbia-v2r3e3-615000/brochure
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Road — a Fraser Health community hospital providing primary, core secondary and some specialty services, on the Chilliwack Proper side north of Highway 1. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital
- **Q: If I need rental or care housing later, is any of it on this side of Sardis?** None was verified in Sardis East Vedder in this pass. The Sardis-side retirement residence found is Chilliwack Lifestyles (6980 Vedder Road, independent-living rental studios) on the Sardis South side; Chilliwack's registered assisted-living residences in the sources opened are on the Chilliwack Proper / downtown side. https://chilliwacklifestyles.ca/about and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f[0]=city:Castlegar&f[1]=city:Chilliwack&f[2]=city:Nanaimo&f[3]=residence_type:Seniors and Persons with Disabilities&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction in the buildings across Vedder Road shrink resale there?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. That trade-off is part of why the east side's general-market stock trades to a wider pool than the west side's 55+ buildings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **There are no market statistics to quote for this sub-area — from anyone.** CADREB publishes board-wide figures only and SnapStats does not cover Chilliwack, so any Sardis East Vedder "median price" a seller or a website offers is not coming from a published board or SnapStats source. - **The school list on a guide page is not a catchment.** Sardis Elementary, Sardis Secondary, Vedder Middle School and Stitó:s Lá:lém Totí:lt are the schools the guide page names for this side, but SD33 catchments are set by address and no locator result for a Sardis East Vedder address is on record in this program — run the exact address in the SD33 School Locator before relying on any school. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
