# Matsqui — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Matsqui" is the board (Paragon) sub-area name; the City of Abbotsford's planning profile name for the same ground is "Matsqui Prairie," with Matsqui Village as a historic rural centre surrounded by agricultural land. BC Geographical Names lists "Matsqui" as an official urban community in the City of Abbotsford. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2014%20Matsqui%20Prairie%20Demographic%20Profile_spreads.pdf and https://apps.gov.bc.ca/pub/bcgnws/names/25776.html
- The prairie sits north of Abbotsford Centre along the Fraser River, with the river on its north side; Mission is across the river via Highway 11. Precise east and west boundary roads are not stated in the City profile text — UNVERIFIED. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2014%20Matsqui%20Prairie%20Demographic%20Profile_spreads.pdf
- This is a farm sub-area, full stop: single-detached houses on agricultural and rural parcels are the dominant form, and the board's own numbers show it — in September 2026 Matsqui had 6 detached listings and **zero** attached (condo/townhome) listings and zero attached sales (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report). There is no attached housing stock here for an age-restricted complex to exist in.
- Services are all outside the sub-area: the nearest City recreation complex is the Matsqui Recreation Centre at 3106 Clearbrook Road (in Clearbrook, despite the name), the nearest library is Clearbrook Library at 32320 George Ferguson Way, and no shopping centre or grocery store inside Matsqui Prairie was verified on an official source. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/ and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2014%20Matsqui%20Prairie%20Demographic%20Profile_spreads.pdf
- Health: Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre, 32900 Marshall Road (24-hour emergency, surgery, maternity), and — often closer in practice — Mission Memorial Hospital across the Fraser River in Mission, which Fraser Health describes as providing 24/7 emergency stabilization and care. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Abbotsford/abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/your-community/mission/locations-in-mission
- Transit: BC Transit Route 31 (Abbotsford/Mission) serves the sub-area — 31A trips stop at Matsqui Village, running between Bourquin Exchange in Abbotsford and Mission City Station via Junction Mall. There is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express in Abbotsford. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=31
- One school is physically in the sub-area: Máthxwi Elementary, 33661 Elizabeth Avenue (renamed from Matsqui Elementary); an SD34 locator run for that address returns Máthxwí Elementary → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary (SD34 School Locator, run 2026-11-01). https://mathxwi.abbyschools.ca/

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**There is no verified 55+ housing of any kind in the Matsqui sub-area** — no 55+ strata or freehold complex, no 55+ manufactured-home park, no 55+ rental building, no retirement residence, no care home and no subsidized seniors building appears in any source checked (building directories, MLS listing data, BC's Assisted Living Registry, the Abbotsford & Mission Seniors Directory, or the base area guide). That is consistent with the housing stock: the sub-area recorded zero attached listings in September 2026, and its homes are freehold farm and rural-residential properties (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report).

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None. Abbotsford's verified 55+ strata stock is in Abbotsford West (15 complexes), Central Abbotsford (9) and Poplar (2) — see those files.

### Verified 55+ rental / retirement residences and care / subsidized seniors rental / manufactured-home parks

- None verified in this sub-area, in any of those categories. A Matsqui senior who wants age-restricted or care housing is choosing between staying on the farm property (Section 3 sets out the suite and second-dwelling rules that make that work) or moving into urban Abbotsford.

### Checked and NOT 55+ in Matsqui (do not put these on a 55+ page)

- **Pioneer Park** is a genuine 55+ co-operative modular-home park, but it is not in this sub-area and not in Abbotsford: it sits on Matsqui First Nation land at 272 Street and 0 Avenue in Aldergrove (Township of Langley). The shared "Matsqui" name is the trap — the First Nation and the Abbotsford sub-area are different places. https://pioneerparkco-op.org
- **Matsqui Recreation Centre (3106 Clearbrook Road)** carries the Matsqui name but is located in Clearbrook, across the city from the prairie; it is a recreation complex, not seniors housing. (Base guide, Section 4.)
- No manufactured-home park of any age profile inside the Matsqui sub-area was verified from a public source; any claim of an age-restricted park on the prairie is UNCONFIRMED.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The 55+ rules that matter here are farm-housing rules, not strata rules.** Matsqui land is governed by Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014's rural zones and, for most parcels, the Agricultural Land Reserve. In the A1 Agricultural Zone (s.210), parcels created through subdivision must be at least 8.0 ha; in the RR Rural Residential Zone (s.310), the minimum lot size is 4.0 ha — which is why no strata product exists here and none is coming under current zoning. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400310
- **Bringing family or a caregiver onto the farm — the second-dwelling rules:** the A1 zone permits one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot only in accordance with the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the Agricultural Land Commission, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, capped at 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha; the A1 zone also permits one secondary suite per lot, to a maximum of 100 m². https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **The ALC's own housing limits sit on top of the zoning:** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless the owner qualifies for an additional residence; the principal residence is capped at 500 m² of floor area; a secondary suite is permitted in the ALR only within the principal residence; and an additional detached residence is capped at 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **RR-zone coach houses:** in the Rural Residential Zone, a coach house is permitted only on a lot greater than 0.4 ha, and a coach house or secondary suite is limited to one per lot and to the lesser of 90 m² or 40% of the net floor area of the single detached dwelling. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400310
- **If a Matsqui owner does buy a 55+ strata in town, provincial strata law runs it:** only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, since Nov 24, 2022); the bylaw restricts who may live in the unit, not who may own it; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19 (expanded May 1, 2023); and strata rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide (short-term rental bylaws still allowed). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **The dyking levy:** properties in the Matsqui Prairie Dyking District pay a separate dyking district levy on top of the general municipal rates, per the City's 2026 Tax Rates Summary (taxes are set under Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026; due July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties on July 3 and September 16, 2026). https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Summary%20May%2022.pdf and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Flood protection is infrastructure, not a promise:** the City states the Matsqui Dykes are designed to prevent Fraser River water from entering Matsqui Prairie; parcel-level flood status still has to be confirmed with the City for any specific property. http://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/2022-spring-freshet-update-2
- **Wells and septic are the servicing reality:** rural properties rely on wells (recorded in the provincial GWELLS database) and onsite sewage systems, for which Fraser Health requires an authorized person's filing before construction, alteration or repair and a letter of certification after. Servicing for any individual property: UNVERIFIED until pulled. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons
- **Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act:** not applicable inside this sub-area — no manufactured-home park is verified here. The Act's rules are set out in the Abbotsford West, Central Abbotsford and Poplar files, where the verified 55+ parks are. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing in Matsqui at all?** No — nothing verified in any category: no 55+ strata, park, rental, care or subsidized building in the sub-area, and the board recorded zero attached listings here in September 2026. The honest answer for a 55+ buyer who wants to stay near the prairie is a farm property with a suite or second dwelling (below), or a move into urban Abbotsford's stock. Sources: Section 2; SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: I heard there's a 55+ park called Pioneer Park in Matsqui. Is that here?** No. Pioneer Park is a 55+ co-operative modular-home park on Matsqui First Nation land at 272 Street and 0 Avenue in Aldergrove, in the Township of Langley — a different municipality from the City of Abbotsford's Matsqui Prairie sub-area, despite the shared name. https://pioneerparkco-op.org
- **Q: Can my son and his family live on my farm so someone's nearby as I age?** Possibly, within tight caps: the A1 zone allows one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot via the ALR Use Regulation (B.C. Reg. 30/2019) or ALC approval, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, up to 90 m² (lots ≤ 40 ha) or 186 m² (lots > 40 ha), and one secondary suite per lot up to 100 m². The ALC separately requires that an ALR suite sit within the principal residence. Confirm the specific parcel's ALR status and approvals before designing around it. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What about a coach house on a rural residential lot?** The RR zone permits a coach house only on a lot over 0.4 ha, one per lot, at the lesser of 90 m² or 40% of the main house's net floor area; the same one-per-lot and size limits apply to an RR secondary suite. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400310
- **Q: Can I subdivide the farm so my kids each get a piece?** Almost certainly not at family scale: A1 subdivision minimums are 8.0 ha per new parcel and RR minimums are 4.0 ha, and ALR parcels carry Agricultural Land Commission controls on top. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400310
- **Q: What's the extra line on the tax bill?** The Matsqui Prairie Dyking District levy — a separate charge on properties in the district, on top of general municipal rates, shown in the City's 2026 Tax Rates Summary. It funds the dyke system the City says is designed to keep Fraser River water out of the prairie. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Summary%20May%2022.pdf and http://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/2022-spring-freshet-update-2
- **Q: If I sell the farm and buy a 55+ condo in Clearbrook, what rules follow me?** The strata's 55+ bylaw governs who may live in the unit (owning is not restricted), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions for a younger spouse or partner, live-in caregivers and legacy residents; rental-restriction bylaws are void across BC, though a tenant must still meet the age bylaw. The verified buildings are listed in the Abbotsford West and Central Abbotsford files. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Which hospital serves the prairie?** Two, in practice: Abbotsford Regional Hospital and Cancer Centre (32900 Marshall Road, 24-hour emergency) south in the city, and Mission Memorial Hospital across the Fraser River, which Fraser Health describes as providing 24/7 emergency stabilization and care. Published drive times: UNVERIFIED. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/your-community/mission/locations-in-mission
- **Q: Is there a bus if I stop driving?** Route 31 (Abbotsford/Mission) is the sub-area's service, with 31A trips stopping at Matsqui Village between Bourquin Exchange and Mission City Station. It is a rural route, not a frequent urban service — a senior planning to age in place on the prairie should read the current timetable before counting on it. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=31

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Matsqui" names three different things.** The board sub-area (this file) is the City of Abbotsford's Matsqui Prairie; Matsqui Village is the historic rural centre inside it; and Matsqui First Nation is a separate nation whose land at Aldergrove hosts the 55+ Pioneer Park co-op. Searches blend all three, and only the first two are in Abbotsford. https://apps.gov.bc.ca/pub/bcgnws/names/25776.html and https://pioneerparkco-op.org
- **The recreation centre named for this area is not in this area.** Matsqui Recreation Centre and the MRC Sports Complex are at 3106 Clearbrook Road in Clearbrook — the naming regularly misleads newcomers about where Matsqui Prairie's services are (there are effectively none in the sub-area beyond Fire Hall No. 4 at 5775 Wallace Street in Matsqui Village and Matsqui Village Park at 6074 Riverside Road). https://www.abbotsford.ca/public-safety/fire-rescue-service/fire-safety-events and https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds
- **The prairie's flood protection shows up on the tax bill.** The Matsqui Prairie Dyking District levy is a separate line in the City's 2026 Tax Rates Summary — buyers from urban Abbotsford sub-areas will not have seen a dyking levy before, and it recurs annually. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Summary%20May%2022.pdf
- **The dyke is also the recreation.** Matsqui Trail Regional Park runs walking and cycling trails along the Fraser River dyke — a Fraser Valley Regional District regional park maintained and operated by the City of Abbotsford — so the flood infrastructure doubles as the sub-area's main park amenity. http://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/matsqui-trail-regional-park-grade-railway-crossing-closure
- **The village is policy-protected as a rural centre.** The Abbotsforward 2050 OCP's rural centres policy recognizes Abbotsford's seven historical rural centres, Matsqui Village among them, and the City's AgRefresh review (OCP Amendment Bylaw 3247-2022; Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 3246-2022) tightened the agricultural policy framework around it — the rural character a senior buys into here is deliberate and bylaw-backed, not a development lag. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/abbotsforward and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city_hall/plan_for_200K/plan_for_200K_-_agrefresh.htm
- **The elementary school carries the area's Indigenous name now.** Máthxwi Elementary (33661 Elizabeth Avenue) was renamed from Matsqui Elementary; SD34 records that Mathxwí refers to an area that can easily be portaged with one's canoe, and BC Geographical Names traces "Matsqui" itself to the Halq'eméylem language and a tribe of the Halkomaylem division. https://indigenous.abbyschools.ca/blog/renaming-and-intertwining-history and https://apps.gov.bc.ca/pub/bcgnws/names/25776.html
