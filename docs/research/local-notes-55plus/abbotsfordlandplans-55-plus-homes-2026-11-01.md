## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **OCP versus zoning — the distinction this page exists to explain:** the Zoning Bylaw (2014, No. 2400) regulates what is legally permitted on a parcel today without rezoning; the Official Community Plan (Abbotsforward 2050, Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated — see the bylaw-number conflict in Section 1) sets the long-term future land use designation and rezoning policy. The City directs both checks, plus development permit areas, to its property information search or a property search in WebMap. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/development-planning and https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsfordlandplans.html
- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2: a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; every younger age bylaw has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). A plan designation never creates an age restriction — only a strata bylaw does. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts residing, not owning,** and Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted, and a tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **SSMUH — the plan-era rule that changes what a house lot can hold:** the SSMUH amendment is Bylaw No. 3560-2024, adopted June 25, 2024, with accessory units counting toward up to 4 units on one lot in SSMUH areas — 3 units on lots 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots bigger than 280 m². A secondary suite must be within the principal unit, registered with the City, and less than 50% of the principal unit's size; coach houses and garden suites may occupy up to 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m². Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/57884/ and https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **The City's seniors use class:** the Zoning Bylaw framework defines "Congregate Housing" as an independent living setting with common dining and amenity areas (added by Bylaw 3249-2022) — the use category under which new supportive seniors projects are processed in any plan area. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-abbotsford-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/7217/8514/3396/d9117e5011287c79b090adc71e32c234_Appendix_B_-_Draft_ZB_Amendments.pdf
- **McKee's permit layer:** the McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023, OCP Schedule E) sits with a Steep Slope Development Permit Area, and Clayburn Village at the foot of Sumas Mountain carries Heritage Conservation Area protection in the OCP — plan designation alone does not settle what can be built on a McKee parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- **ALR ground cannot be planned around:** removing land from the Agricultural Land Reserve requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; inside the ALR the residence frame (principal residence 500 m² or less with a 1,000 m² fill limit, suite only inside it, additional residence 90 m² at 40 ha or less / 186 m² over 40 ha) and the A1/A2 subdivision minimums (8.0 ha / 16.0 ha) govern regardless of any corridor plan nearby. The live page's claim that about 72% of Abbotsford's land base is in the ALR is UNVERIFIED on any City or ALC source checked. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf and https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsfordlandplans.html
- **Live-page plan details carried as UNVERIFIED:** the City Centre and UDistrict density figures as stated on the page (including "6-to-20+ storey" City Centre density), the full OCP designation set as listed there (Urban 1 Mid-Rise, Urban 2 Ground-Oriented, Urban 3 Infill, Suburban Residential), and the current Abbotsforward 2050 population horizon behind the page's "Plan for 200K" framing — none was read in full on a City source checked for this file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsfordlandplans.html
- **Manufactured-home pads are tenancy law:** under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, pad rent can be increased only once every 12 months, on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes

- **FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED** — no live CRA page stating those limits was read for this file.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is the difference between Abbotsford's OCP and its Zoning Bylaw for a home I'm buying?** The Zoning Bylaw (No. 2400-2014) is what the parcel can be built as today without rezoning; the OCP (Abbotsforward 2050) is the long-term designation the City will judge a rezoning against. For an existing 55+ strata unit the plan layer matters mainly for what gets built around you — the strata's own bylaws, not the plan, carry the age rule. Check both layers through the City's property information search or WebMap. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/development-planning
- **Q: Which Abbotsford plans are actually adopted neighbourhood plans?** UDistrict, City Centre (Bylaw 2913-2019), Historic Downtown (Bylaw 2920-2019) and McKee (Bylaw 3400-2023, OCP Schedule E), as carried in this program's verified research. The live page's fifth topic, a Marshall Road Corridor Plan, is UNVERIFIED as an adopted City plan on any City source checked. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans and https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsfordlandplans.html
- **Q: The plans page cites the OCP as Bylaw 2500-2016. Is that right?** It conflicts with this program's verified research, which carries Abbotsforward 2050 as Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated — and the current citation is UNVERIFIED from the bylaw text in this research. Confirm the citation with the City before relying on either number in a document. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsfordlandplans.html
- **Q: Does a plan designation make a building 55+?** No. No plan, zone or OCP designation creates an age restriction in BC — only a strata corporation's 55+ bylaw under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 does, or a manufactured-home park's own rules. A new tower in a designated growth node (Mahogany at Mill Lake is the local example) is general market unless its strata registers a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ complex in a plan area?** Yes, in law: Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident — Royal Court's listing data states the same position as "only one owner must be at least 55." The plan area the building sits in is irrelevant to the exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/31930-old-yale-road/215
- **Q: Can I add a suite or extra units to a house near a 55+ complex, to age in place instead?** The SSMUH frame applies city-wide in its areas, not by plan topic: up to 4 units on one lot counting accessory units (3 on lots 280 m² or less), a registered secondary suite inside the principal unit under 50% of its size, and coach/garden suites to 15% of the lot, maximum 100 m². Development Permit Area overlays can still apply per parcel — check WebMap. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/57884/ and https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites
- **Q: How did the plan areas sell last month?** No plan-area figures exist in the permitted source.
