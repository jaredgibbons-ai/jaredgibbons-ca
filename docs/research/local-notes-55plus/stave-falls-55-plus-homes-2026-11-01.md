## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. There is no strata in Stave Falls for this to attach to today — it is the rule a buyer would meet in the city's two 55+ strata complexes in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; exemptions attach to a "specified resident." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; SHORT-TERM rental bylaws are still allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law.** Pad tenancies in the Wilson Street parks fall under BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act: the resident owns the home and rents the pad; pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026) plus a proportional share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. One Stave Falls feed card states a pad rental of $808/month in listing text — listing-stated for that home only, confirm in the park documents. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and https://jaredgibbons.ca/stave-falls.html
- **City frame, not FVRD frame:** Stave Falls is inside the City of Mission, so the City's Rural Mission Strategy / Official Community Plan process and City zoning govern. **Current plan: Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026** replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025 with the same vision, framework and mapping plus a Regional Context Statement. Stave Falls' specific OCP designation, zone names and the draft strategy's Stave Falls recommendations: UNVERIFIED in the research file (mission.ca returned a bot-check page on direct fetch). https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026
- **Secondary suites and second dwellings (City rules):** a Mission secondary suite is a self-contained space inside a single family home; suites are not coach homes or garden cottages (detached secondary dwellings, which on rural lots generally require the secondary-dwelling "s" variant of the zone); suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; all suites must be registered with the City, new suites go through building permit, and an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus utility fees applies. Rural zones in the City's Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 include RU80, RU36 and RU16 (RU16 carries a 1.6 ha minimum lot size in City notices) — a given parcel's zone is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/notice-rezoning-bylaws-june-1-2026
- **Wells, septic, ALR and reservoir shoreline are the working rules on this ground:** wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database; onsite sewerage must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation, including the 30 m setback from a well unless professional advice is obtained; ALR status is per-parcel (ALR parcels follow the ALC frame of one principal residence to a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha)); and the Stave/Hayward shoreline is a working BC Hydro reservoir system, so dock/moorage claims need title and BC Hydro confirmation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.BChydro.com/news/conservation/2023/visit-stave-falls.html?fixZoomMobile=true
- **Property taxes:** Stave Falls properties are inside the City of Mission and pay the City. City of Mission 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding (including unclaimed home owner grants) on July 3, 2026 — a single flat step, NOT the rural 5% + 5% regime, which does not apply inside the City. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
