# MacKenzie Heights (Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- MacKenzie Heights is a west-side residential pocket on the slope above the Fraser River, inside the Kerrisdale local area, centred on the West 37th to West 49th Avenue blocks west of Granville Street, between Shaughnessy and Kerrisdale. The City lists "MacKenzie Heights" as a recognised smaller area, but it is not one of the City's 22 distinct local areas. The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for it; exact board boundary roads are UNVERIFIED. Spelling note: the City and the board use "MacKenzie Heights" with a capital K. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/kerrisdale.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- West 41st Avenue is the pocket's east–west spine, with Granville Street at its east edge and West Boulevard / Arbutus Street carrying traffic north–south; the streets slope down toward the Fraser River to the south. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/kerrisdale.aspx
- Shopping is Kerrisdale Village on West 41st Avenue: the City calls it the main shopping area of the surrounding local area, and the Kerrisdale Business Association promotes more than 250 businesses and services concentrated on West 41st Avenue between Maple and Larch Streets and along West and East Boulevards. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/kerrisdale.aspx
- Schools (SD39; catchments are address-based): Maple Grove Elementary, 1924 West 45th Avenue, and McKechnie Elementary, 7455 Maple Street, are the elementaries listed for Kerrisdale; Magee Secondary, 6360 Maple Street, is physically in Kerrisdale, and the pocket's secondary catchment splits between Magee and Point Grey Secondary by address — run every address in the VSB locator. Crofton House School, 3200 West 41st Avenue, is an independent JK-to-Grade-12 girls' day school founded in 1898 on a 10-acre campus at Kerrisdale's western edge. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods and https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries and https://www.croftonhouse.ca/apply/how-to-apply
- Recreation: Kerrisdale Community Centre, 5851 West Boulevard (indoor pool, ice rink, fitness centre; operated jointly with the Vancouver Park Board since 1943); the Kerrisdale Cyclone Taylor Arena is at 5670 East Boulevard and the Maple Grove outdoor pool at 6875 Yew Street. The Arbutus Greenway's temporary path runs along the pocket's eastern side for 8.5 km between Fir Street and Milton Street, crossing West 41st Avenue at the village's east end. https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/kerrisdale-community-centre.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/explore-the-arbutus-greenway.aspx
- Transit: no SkyTrain station sits inside MacKenzie Heights. The frequent corridors near the pocket are the #41 West 41st, #49 West 49th, #14 Arbutus / West Boulevard, #25 King Edward and #99 Broadway routes, with the R4 41st Ave RapidBus serving the West 41st Avenue corridor to the east. https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/explore-the-arbutus-greenway.aspx
- Where the seniors housing actually is: NOT on the pocket's detached streets. The one operating seniors residence at the pocket's edge is Granville Gardens Retirement Residence at 1550 West 49th Avenue — the Granville Street / West 49th Avenue corner at the pocket's south-east edge, which its operator markets as Kerrisdale. The other Kerrisdale-named seniors residence, Crofton Manor, sits well west of the pocket (Section 2). A second, much larger seniors project exists only as an approved rezoning on the pocket's east edge at Granville and West 47th (Section 2).

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**No verified 55+ strata ownership complex was found in this sub-area.** No market 55+ rental apartment building and no subsidized independent seniors rental were verified inside the pocket either. Its one operating seniors residence is a private rental retirement residence at the south-east corner; everything else named for the area is outside the pocket or not yet built.

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- None verified in this sub-area. The pocket is almost entirely freehold detached houses; the strata condos and townhouses sit toward the West 41st Avenue village edges, and no source checked states an age restriction for any of them.

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- None verified as a rental apartment building in this sub-area. (Granville Gardens below is a retirement residence with services, not a rental apartment building.)

### Retirement residences and care (rental/care — NOT strata ownership)

- **Granville Gardens Retirement Residence — 1550 West 49th Avenue.** A Verve Senior Living rental retirement residence at the junction of Granville Street and West 49th Avenue. The operator offers four levels on one site — Independent Living, Independent Supportive Living, Assisted Living and Respite Stays — with studio and one-bedroom suites in both the independent and assisted programs, pet-friendly suites, weekly housekeeping, a 24/7 emergency response system, three chef-prepared meals daily, and amenities including a restaurant, bar and bistro, fitness centre and library. A retirement-homes directory profile gives its opening year as 2017. BOUNDARY NOTE: the residence sits at the pocket's south-east corner as the pocket is framed in the area research (West 37th–49th blocks west of Granville); Verve itself markets the location as Kerrisdale, and the exact board sub-area boundary is UNVERIFIED — confirm the sub-area on the specific listing before putting this building on a MacKenzie Heights page as in-area stock. Rental only — nothing to buy. https://www.verveseniorliving.com/granville-gardens/ and https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/granville-gardens-vancouver/1311

### Subsidized seniors rental (non-profit, income-based — not ownership)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **House-street zoning: R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575** — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units, 557 m² for 6 to 8 units; a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. SOURCE CONFLICT to check parcel by parcel: the City's 2022 referral report for the Granville-edge lots describes their existing district as the legacy **RS-3 (Residential) District**, not R1-1 — district names differ by street and era, so confirm the specific parcel's zone rather than assuming one district covers the pocket. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf and https://council.vancouver.ca/documents/rr8_000.pdf
- **Seniors care gets built here only by site-specific rezoning.** The pocket has no seniors-housing zoning as-of-right in the sources read: the one large seniors project (Granville/West 47th) needed a CD-1 rezoning from house zoning (RS-3) to permit a Community Care Facility – Class B, considered under the Arbutus Ridge/Kerrisdale/Shaughnessy (ARKS) Community Vision. The City's seniors-housing pages note Council encourages supportive, assisted and affordable rental seniors housing citywide, but the City does not manage facilities or their rental processes. https://council.vancouver.ca/documents/rr8_000.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/find-supportive-housing-for-seniors.aspx
- **Plans:** MacKenzie Heights has no separate adopted community plan; citywide direction comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/kerrisdale.aspx
- **Tax calendar:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026 (5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date); the Empty Homes Tax is 3% of assessed taxable value for homes vacant for the 2025 reference year, payment due April 16, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in MacKenzie Heights?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata building in the pocket; a 55+ buyer here is buying a regular detached house (or a general-market condo at the village edges) with no age bylaw. The seniors-specific product attached to this area is rental: Granville Gardens at West 49th and Granville. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Can I buy into Granville Gardens?** No. It is a Verve Senior Living rental retirement residence — independent living, independent supportive living, assisted living and respite stays in studio and one-bedroom suites at 1550 West 49th Avenue. You rent a suite with services; there is no strata lot to purchase. https://www.verveseniorliving.com/granville-gardens/
- **Q: Is the big Amica seniors home at Granville and 47th open yet?** No operating residence was verified there. What exists in the public record is a CD-1 rezoning for a six-storey, 226-unit Community Care Facility – Class B referred to public hearing in January 2023, trade-press reporting of a March 2023 approval, and a later report that the assembled site was listed for sale. Its current status is UNCONFIRMED — treat any "new seniors residence at Granville" marketing as a rezoning story, not housing you can move into. https://council.vancouver.ca/documents/rr8_000.pdf and https://storeys.com/intracorp-homes-6137-granville-street-rental-project-listing/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Vancouver?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a second dwelling for a parent or caregiver?** That is the realistic ageing-in-place route in this pocket: under the R1-1 schedule a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and qualifying lots can hold a multiplex of up to 6 units (or up to 8 rental units). What a specific lot can hold depends on its site area and frontage — check the lot against the schedule. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Granville Gardens?** The operator lists the residence as pet-friendly and states second occupants and pets may join a resident (additional fees apply to second occupants). Confirm the current pet policy for the specific suite program before relying on it. https://www.verveseniorliving.com/granville-gardens/
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day?** The #41 and R4 on West 41st Avenue, the #49 on West 49th, and the #14 along Arbutus / West Boulevard — plus the Arbutus Greenway for walking and cycling along the pocket's east side. There is no SkyTrain pitch here. https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/explore-the-arbutus-greenway.aspx
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and what if I need care later?** No hospital is located inside MacKenzie Heights, and the nearest hospital confirmed on a health-authority page for this pocket is UNVERIFIED in the sources checked — do not quote one without checking. The care step that does exist at the pocket's edge is Granville Gardens (independent living through assisted living and respite); there is no long-term care home verified inside the pocket, so the step beyond assisted living means leaving MacKenzie Heights. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/kerrisdale.aspx and https://www.verveseniorliving.com/granville-gardens/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The pocket's seniors project is a rezoning, not a building.** The Granville/West 47th Amica assembly took seven single-family lots, a zoning change from RS-3 to CD-1, 2.8 FSR and up to 30.3 m of height to permit 226 seniors care units — and the site was subsequently reported listed for sale with the development permit still not issued (as of the 2023 trade report). In this neighbourhood, even approved seniors housing is not the same as seniors housing. https://council.vancouver.ca/documents/rr8_000.pdf and https://storeys.com/intracorp-homes-6137-granville-street-rental-project-listing/
- **"Kerrisdale" seniors addresses scatter across three different areas.** Granville Gardens (West 49th at Granville) markets itself as Kerrisdale from the pocket's south-east corner; Crofton Manor (2803 West 41st) is Kerrisdale proper, west of the pocket; the Leo Wertman Residence (611 West 41st) is east of Granville in Oakridge. A "Kerrisdale seniors" search result tells you nothing until the address is mapped against the pocket. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The name trap runs the other way too:** Sir Alexander Mackenzie Elementary School (960 East 39th Avenue) is in Kensington-Cedar Cottage on the east side — it has nothing to do with this west-side pocket, and the two are routinely confused in search. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods
- **The newest seniors residence at the pocket's edge is also its only one.** Granville Gardens opened in 2017 (per its directory profile) — before that, the pocket's detached streets had no seniors residence at their edge at all, and none has been verified since. https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/granville-gardens-vancouver/1311
- **The demand case was made in the rezoning record:** the applicant's material (reported in 2022) noted 41% of west side and Kerrisdale residents were 50 or older in 2016, projected to reach 49% by 2026 — in a pocket whose housing stock remains almost entirely detached houses. Projection figures are the applicant's, quoted from its application coverage. https://www.urbanyvr.com/seniors-home-granville-street/
- **Kerrisdale's civic anchor predates the pocket's build-out:** the Kerrisdale Community Centre has been operated jointly with the Vancouver Park Board since 1943, and its complex on West Boulevard also houses the Kerrisdale library branch and the seniors programming for the wider Kerrisdale area. https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/kerrisdale-community-centre.aspx
