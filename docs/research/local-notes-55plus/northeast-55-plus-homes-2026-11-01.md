## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. There is no verified strata stock in this sub-area for this law to bite on — it matters here when a buyer compares against East Central's Fraserview Village buildings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019** (verified on the City's planning pages Nov 1, 2026 — not 7600-2020). Its schedule minimum lot areas for this sub-area's zones include A-1 2.0 ha; A-2 4.0 ha; A-3 8.0 ha; A-4 1.8 ha; A-5 8.0 ha; RS-2 0.4 ha; RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha (refer to zone text); RS-1d 2,000 m²; RS-1c 1,200 m². https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/land-development/planning-application-forms-procedures and http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **City plan: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 7060-2014** is the OCP bylaw cited on the City's own OCP chapter documents (not 7601-2020); its Thornhill Urban Reserve designation identifies lands for long-term future urban services and housing. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/01ocp-chapter-1201402201612431396 and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/official-community-plan-chapter-2
- **Secondary suites:** one secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, contained within the single detached building, to a maximum of 40% of gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area. On ALR parcels, the provincial ALR Use Regulation also governs: it permits one secondary suite in the principal residence (s.31) and an additional residence only if the s.32 conditions are met. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **ALR, wells and septic — the rural due-diligence trio:** ALR status is per-parcel — check the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder. Wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database, and onsite sewerage must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation, which sets setbacks including 30 m from a well for a sewerage system unless professional advice is obtained. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004
- **Flood / hazards:** the City's floodplain framework addresses flood risks from the Fraser River, the Alouette River system, steep creek watercourses and groundwater-related flooding; low land along the Fraser River at the sub-area's south edge is the area to check on City flood maps, property by property. A City-documented wildfire interface map for specific Northeast streets: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/city-advances-climate-action-flood-resilience-environmental-protection-and-mckenney-creek
- **FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED** — no live CRA page was read for this file, so no FHSA or HBP limit is stated here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ home I can actually buy in Northeast Maple Ridge?** No verified one. No 55+ strata, co-op, life lease or manufactured-home park in Ruskin, Thornhill, Webster's Corners, Whonnock or Yennadon was verified in any source checked, and the sub-area recorded zero attached listings in September 2026. The nearest verified 55+ purchases are the Fraserview Village strata buildings in East Central and St. George's Village co-op on Dewdney Trunk Road. Sources: Section 2 above (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).
- **Q: Is Ruskin Place a 55+ park?** Not on current evidence. A current park guide describes Ruskin Place as family-friendly with no age restrictions; a 2015 classified line calling a Ruskin park "55+" is a decade old and conflicts with that description, so the park's current age rule is UNCONFIRMED — get the park rules in writing from the park before writing an offer on that basis. https://www.rodrhearyan.com/blog/affordable-manufactured-homes-ruskin-deroche-oasis-country-triple-creek-nicoman/ and https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/151125/MRNN151125_A39.pdf
- **Q: Can I just buy an acreage here and age in place — what changes versus a 55+ complex?** Everything about the rulebook: a freehold acreage carries no age bylaw (so no resale-pool restriction and no exemption questions), but it carries the rural checks a complex handles for you — ALR status by parcel, well records in GWELLS, septic compliance under the Sewerage System Regulation (including the 30 m well setback), and flood mapping on the south-edge low land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Does that matter for a home here?** Not for the freehold acreage stock — there is no age bylaw to exempt you from. It matters only if you buy into a strata 55+ complex elsewhere in the city, where provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can an acreage home here add a suite or a second dwelling for a caregiver or family?** One secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3 if it sits inside the single detached building, is at most 40% of gross floor area and is not in the Floodplain Area; on ALR land the ALR Use Regulation also applies, and an additional separate residence is allowed only under its s.32 conditions. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and how far is daily life?** Ridge Meadows Hospital (11666 Laity Street) and the Urgent and Primary Care Centre at Haney Place Mall are both in Town Centre, west of the sub-area; there is no City recreation centre, library branch or shopping centre inside the sub-area on any City source checked — daily life here is drive-to-town by design. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre
- **Q: What transit can I actually use if I stop driving?** Very little, and that is the honest answer for an aging-in-place plan here: route 701 on Dewdney Trunk Road (most east trips end at 248 Street) and the 748 Thornhill loop at wide intervals on weekdays and Saturdays only. There is no West Coast Express station in the sub-area. A buyer planning to give up driving should price that in before buying rural. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/194/tt701.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt748.pdf
- **Q: What sold here recently — can I price off the sub-area?** Not off September 2026: the sub-area recorded 0 sales from 2 detached listings and had no attached listings at all, so price from the Maple Ridge community evidence instead (detached median $1,131,000, 97% sale-to-list, 29 days on market) plus acreage comparables from surrounding sub-areas (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).
- **Q: Does the 256 Street industrial plan affect a home I buy north of 128 Avenue?** The North 256 Street Industrial Lands Area Plan — Council-approved OCP amendments covering both sides of 256 Street north of 128 Avenue (about 500 acres) — changes the long-term designation around those parcels without approving any construction; disclose it as a designation change, not a development approval. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-approves-new-500-acre-256-street-industrial-area-plan-support-jobs-and-economic
