## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Open-house conduct rules are provincial, not local:** BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party; dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018. How open houses are scheduled inside the MLS/board systems (TouchBase, ShowingTime) is not documented on official public sources — UNVERIFIED. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/tsawwassen-open-houses.md
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** is Delta's zoning bylaw; seniors housing is typically delivered through site-specific comprehensive development zones (The Waterford's site is CD Zone No. 276). The OCP is Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future"). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/71542/ and https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing:** Delta's SSMUH rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Property taxes:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026; the Province's regular property tax deferment program is for people 55 and older. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: The card time and the listing text give different open-house times. Which do I trust?** Neither can be confirmed from an official source — the research file records a systematic offset between the feed card's time block and the time inside the same listing's own text (for example a card headed 12:00 PM–02:00 PM whose listing text says 11:00AM–1:00PM). Confirm the time with the listing agent before travelling, especially on a 55+ trip across town. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/tsawwassen-open-houses.md
- **Q: Which Tsawwassen open houses are actually 55+?** Only ones at The Lexington (1150 54A St) or Tsawwassen Greene (1140 55 St) — the community's two verified 55+ strata buildings. An open house at Heron Place across the street, or at any other Tsawwassen condo, is general market. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Can an agent at an open house act for me and the seller?** No. BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party; dual agency has been prohibited in BC since 2018. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); The Lexington's listing formulation is "one occupant must be 55+." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/1150-54a-street/201
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at the 55+ buildings I'd be viewing?** The Lexington: one pet allowed, no dogs. Tsawwassen Greene: one dog or cat to 12 inches at the shoulder (listing-stated). KinVillage rental buildings: no pets. Confirm in the strata bylaws before relying on a listing line. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street and https://www.kinvillage.org/independent-living
- **Q: Where is the hospital, if I'm planning a day of viewings around care access?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner — Fraser Health's community hospital for Delta with a 24/7 emergency department. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or a buyer who purchases without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Is Tsawwassen flood-protected?** The City of Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure — lot-level flood construction requirements still have to be checked parcel by parcel. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
