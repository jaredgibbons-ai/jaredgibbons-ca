## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. This is the exact legal point the Cedar Crest flag turns on: a pre-2022 "45+" description, if it reflects the registered bylaw, means there is no enforceable age bylaw today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Community plan: the Murrayville Community Plan is Bylaw 1988 No. 2661** to the Langley Official Community Plan Bylaw 1979 No. 1842, adopted April 17, 1989, as amended including by the 2025 OCP Update (Bylaw No. 6200, December 1, 2025). Read the current consolidated version, not the 1989 text alone, before valuing a lot's potential. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Community-Plan-Bylaws/Murrayville-Community-Plan-(Bylaw-No.-2661).pdf
- **SSMUH (Township): Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 Amendment (SSMUH) Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024** — eligible lots (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on both municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units if 280 m² or smaller, or 4 units if between 280 m² and 4,050 m²; lots larger than 4,050 m² are not eligible, and there is no 6-unit transit tier in the Township. That is the rule that lets a Murrayville family lot add a suite or coach house for a parent or caregiver where the lot qualifies. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Township taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies at midnight July 2, 2026, and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **Subsidized seniors housing for Langley runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry and the Langley Lions campus in Langley City** — there is no subsidized seniors building with a Murrayville address in the sources checked (Section 2). https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/opening-more-seniors-housing-in-langley

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Murrayville?** None that are verified. No Murrayville complex is in Langley's verified roster of 11 strata + 3 parks + 1 co-operative, and the complex marketed as 55+ here — Cedar Crest Estates, 21848 50 Ave — has conflicting 55+ / 45+ listing evidence with no bylaws checked, so it is flagged, not counted. The nearest verified strata are in Langley City, Walnut Grove, Willoughby, Aldergrove and Fort Langley. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: The guide page itself shows a Cedar Crest card calling it 55+ — why won't you call it verified?** Because that wording is the listing's own text on a feed card, and the wider evidence conflicts on the one fact that matters: current listings say "55+," an earlier listing for the same complex said "45+," and the MLS data sheet records "Age Restrictions" without a number. Under current BC law a 55+ bylaw is enforceable and a 45+ bylaw is void — those are opposite outcomes, and only the registered bylaws and Form B settle which applies. https://jaredgibbons.ca/murrayville-guide.html and https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/21848-50-avenue/61 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: If Cedar Crest's bylaw does check out as 55+, can my under-55 spouse live with me?** Yes — Strata Property Regulation 7.01 requires an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); live-in caregivers and the defined children cases are also exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I buy in Murrayville and add a suite or coach house for a parent instead?** Potentially, under the Township's SSMUH rules: an eligible lot (Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area, 4,050 m² or smaller) may hold 3 units at 280 m² or less or 4 units at 280–4,050 m² under Bylaw No. 6020. Eligibility is per-lot — confirm with the Township, and check the Murrayville Community Plan designation and any heritage layer for the exact lot first. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: Is Sunridge Gardens a condo I can buy?** No. Sunridge Gardens (22301 Fraser Hwy) is a Bria Communities independent-living rental residence — suites are rented, with a licensed care centre on the same model. There is nothing to purchase. https://briacommunities.ca/pie/ and https://centurygroup.ca/seniors-living/
- **Q: How did Murrayville sell in September 2026?** Its own SnapStats row shows Detached 30 listings / 5 sold / 17% and Condos & Townhomes 35 / 8 / 23% — the highest published attached ratio in the Langley community that month. No Murrayville-specific median is published; the $1,345,000 / $611,000 medians are whole-Langley community figures (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report).
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and what recreation is in the neighbourhood?** Langley Memorial Hospital is at 22051 Fraser Highway, with 24/7 emergency service; W.C. Blair Recreation Centre (pool and gym) is at 22200 Fraser Hwy. Both are inside Murrayville's hospital/civic area on the Fraser Highway corridor. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital and https://www.tol.ca/en/parks-recreation/wc-blair-recreation-centre.aspx
- **Q: If I buy a regular Murrayville house, is my resale pool restricted?** No. A general-market Murrayville home carries no age bylaw, so any buyer can purchase and reside — the restricted-pool trade-off only exists inside a strata that actually has a valid 55+ bylaw, which no Murrayville complex is verified to have. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Are there any 55+ parks or a co-op in Murrayville?** None verified. Langley's verified parks (Cedar Creek Estates, Fernridge Park, Pineridge) are in Brookswood and its co-operative (Pioneer Park) is in Aldergrove; Cedar Lane Park and Deer Creek Estates are held UNCONFIRMED in this program. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: When are Township property taxes due?** Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Murrayville's 55+ question is a documents question, not a marketing question.** The neighbourhood's one age-marketed complex, Cedar Crest Estates, is described as 55+ in current listings and in the guide page's own feed card, and as 45+ in an earlier listing, on a data sheet that records age restrictions without a number — and since Nov 24, 2022 those two numbers have opposite legal effects. The Form B and registered bylaws are the whole answer here. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The hospital and the seniors residence are a pair.** Sunridge Gardens was built in historic Murrayville a short walk from Langley Memorial Hospital (22051 Fraser Hwy, 24/7 emergency) — the rental/care option in this neighbourhood is anchored to the hospital, while the ownership option is simply the general housing stock around it. https://briacommunities.ca/pie/ and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital
- **Murrayville's attached market ran hot in the last published month while its 55+ shelf stayed empty.** Its Condos & Townhomes sales ratio of 23% in September 2026 was the highest published attached ratio in the Langley community — demand for Murrayville attached homes is not the constraint; verified age-restricted supply is (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report).
- **The community plan is from 1989 and still governs — as amended.** The Murrayville Community Plan (Bylaw No. 2661, adopted April 17, 1989) has been carried forward through amendments including the December 1, 2025 OCP Update (Bylaw No. 6200); a buyer reading only the original 1989 text will miss the current designations. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Community-Plan-Bylaws/Murrayville-Community-Plan-(Bylaw-No.-2661).pdf
- **The guide page and the area page are different pages with different card sets.** The guide carries the neighbourhood editorial and a smaller appended card set; the separate homes-for-sale page carries the larger mixed feed. A card appearing on the guide does not make the guide a 55+ page, and the guide's exact feed filter criteria are UNVERIFIED in the research file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/murrayville-guide.html
- **Murrayville lots are where Langley's SSMUH math actually bites for seniors.** With no verified 55+ complex to buy, the practical downsizing route in this neighbourhood is a family lot adding a suite or coach house — and the Township's eligibility test (containment boundary, water AND sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit-Oriented Area, 4,050 m² cap, no 6-unit tier) is stricter than the provincial headline suggests. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Two of the guide page's local colour claims are unverified at source.** The heritage conservation area / Traveller's Hotel (1887) framing and the Aquifer 51 flowing-wells framing both come from the guide page's own copy; neither was confirmed on a Township or provincial document in the research pass, so neither should be quoted as fact in an offer or a listing. https://jaredgibbons.ca/murrayville-guide.html
