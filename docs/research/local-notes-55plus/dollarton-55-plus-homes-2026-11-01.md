# Dollarton (District of North Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Dollarton is the District of North Vancouver area along Dollarton Highway on the north shore of Burrard Inlet, between Maplewood at the west end and Deep Cove to the east; the port authority's shoreline report places Maplewood Flats at the west end, the Tsleil-Waututh Nation reserve lands (Burrard Inlet IR #3) along the shore backed by Dollarton Highway, and Whey-ah-Wichen (Cates Park), 4141 Dollarton Highway, farther east. A single District boundary line for the whole sub-area beyond that shoreline description is UNVERIFIED. (Area research file, sources read 2026-11-01: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md)
- Dollarton Highway is the only through road: west via Main Street to Phibbs Exchange and Highway 1 (Ironworkers Memorial Second Narrows Crossing), east to Deep Cove. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt211.pdf
- No public school inside Dollarton itself is confirmed on SD44's school pages. The schools beside the area are Cove Cliff Elementary, 1818 Banbury Road, and Seycove Secondary, 1204 Caledonia Avenue, both in Deep Cove to the east, and Seymour Heights Elementary, 2640 Carnation Street, to the north-west. Catchments are address-based — check each address with SD44. https://covecliff.sd44.ca/
- Transit: routes 211, 212 and 215 all run along Dollarton Highway to Phibbs Exchange, and some 211 trips continue to Burrard Station; the R2 RapidBus at Phibbs has run one-seat to Metrotown since September 7, 2026. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt211.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/206/tt212.pdf and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- Services sit at the ends, not the middle: the District OCP's Maplewood Village Centre plans retail with apartments and live/work units above along Old Dollarton Road at the west end; Parkgate Community Centre (3625 Banff Court) and the Parkgate library branch (3675 Banff Court) are north of the area. A grocery store by name on Dollarton Highway is UNVERIFIED on an official source. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf and https://www.nvdpl.ca/locations
- Where the 55+ housing actually sits: nowhere on the Dollarton shore. The area's one named strata complex, Cates Landing beside Cates Park, is a general-market waterfront development with no age restriction in any source checked (Section 2). The nearest seniors rental is Cedar Springs PARC at Parkgate, north-west of the area.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **District zoning (Bylaw 3210, s. 525):** R1 minimum lot 4,000 m² / 30 m / 34 m; R2 minimum lot 660 m² / 18 m / 34 m, with modifiers R2(S1) 335 m², R2(S2) 1,100 m² and R2(S3) 4,000 m²; the R1/R2 setback table sets a minimum setback to the Ocean Natural Boundary Line of 7.6 m for a principal building — directly relevant on Dollarton's waterfront lots. Since June 2024, a principal house plus a secondary suite plus a coach house (3 units) is permitted on single-family lots inside the Urban Containment Boundary. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Shoreline and foreshore rules are the Dollarton-specific layer:** the port authority states the Whey-ah-Wichen (Cates Park) shoreline is eroding and likely to worsen with sea level rise and climate change, and Burrard Inlet's waters and intertidal area are federal lands and waters managed by the Vancouver Fraser Port Authority — foreshore work is not a District-only approval. District OCP Bylaw 7900 Schedule B also sets streamside, creek, slope and wildfire permit areas, and the District publishes modelled flood construction levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. https://www.portvancouver.com/permit/shoreline-adaptation-and-restoration-whey-ah-wichen-cates-park and https://www.portvancouver.com/about and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **District taxes and utilities 2026:** taxes due Jul 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after Jul 2 and a further 5% after Sep 1; municipal rate 1.83419 and total residential rate 3.28195 per $1,000. Single-family utility charges total $2,571.20 (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

**Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Dollarton?**
A: None were verified. There is no age-restricted strata, manufactured-home park or seniors product of any kind on the Dollarton shore in any source checked — the buyable stock is freehold detached houses plus the general-market Cates Landing strata. A buyer who needs a true 55+ bylaw has to leave the area (the District's verified example is Queensbrook in Delbrook, 678 West Queens Rd). https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/real-estate-FAQS/55-age-restricted-stratas-in-north-west-vancouver/

**Q: Is Cates Landing a 55+ building? It looks like a downsizer product.**
A: No. Building data for Cates Landing (built 2017) records only pet restrictions, and current listings call it "pet & rental friendly" waterfront housing beside Cates Park with no age restriction stated anywhere. Downsizer-friendly is not age-restricted — confirm any building's status in its registered bylaws and Form B, not from its look or marketing. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/cates-landing-3873-way and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3093442-104-3825-cates-landing-way-north-vancouver-british-columbia-v7g0a6-1300000/brochure

**Q: Is there seniors rental or care on the shore if I want to stay local?**
A: No — none verified in Dollarton. The nearest independent-living rental is Cedar Springs PARC, 3633 Mt Seymour Pkwy at Parkgate (outside the area); the registry's assisted-living residences are all on the District's west side (Cedar Garden, Amica Edgemont Village, Chartwell Churchill House). https://parcliving.ca/cedarsprings and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc

**Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to a Dollarton house for family or a caregiver?**
A: Since June 2024 the District permits a principal house plus a secondary suite plus a coach house (3 units) on single-family lots inside the Urban Containment Boundary; on waterfront lots the 7.6 m Ocean Natural Boundary setback and the hazard permit areas can limit where anything goes. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf

**Q: My spouse is under 55 — how do the exemptions work in a BC 55+ strata?**
A: Strata Property Regulation 7.01 (expanded May 1, 2023) exempts a younger spouse/partner of the specified 55+ resident, children under 19 in that resident's care, adult children the resident cared for before age 19, live-in caregivers, and legacy residents who lived in the unit when the bylaw passed. The bylaw restricts who may live in the unit, not who may own it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm

**Q: What shoreline risks come with a Dollarton waterfront home?**
A: The port authority states the Cates Park shoreline is eroding and likely to worsen with sea level rise; the District's flood construction levels are modelled to a 2100 horizon plus 0.6 m, waterfront principal buildings need a 7.6 m setback from the Ocean Natural Boundary Line, and dock or seawall work also engages the port authority's federal foreshore jurisdiction. Get an insurance quote and the GeoWeb report before subjects come off. https://www.portvancouver.com/permit/shoreline-adaptation-and-restoration-whey-ah-wichen-cates-park and https://www.portvancouver.com/about

**Q: Where is the hospital?**
A: Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver — reached from Dollarton via Dollarton Highway, Main Street and Highway 1. The North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 2nd floor, 221 West Esplanade. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre

**Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?**
A: Buses only: routes 211, 212 and 215 on Dollarton Highway to Phibbs Exchange, some 211 trips through to Burrard Station, and the R2 RapidBus from Phibbs to Metrotown (one-seat since September 7, 2026). There is no SkyTrain on the North Shore and no second road off the shore. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt211.pdf and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/

**Q: Which schools serve a Dollarton address?**
A: No public school inside Dollarton is confirmed. The schools beside the area are Cove Cliff Elementary and Seycove Secondary in Deep Cove and Seymour Heights Elementary to the north-west; Dorothy Lynas Elementary (4000 Inlet Crescent) is a French Immersion choice program entered by registration, not a catchment school. Run the exact address with SD44. https://covecliff.sd44.ca/ and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044

**Q: What do District taxes and utilities run?**
A: 2026 total residential tax rate 3.28195 per $1,000 (municipal portion 1.83419), due Jul 2, 2026, with 5% penalties after Jul 2 and after Sep 1; single-family utilities total $2,571.20 (water $932.80, sewer/drainage $1,255.60). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The shoreline itself is an active federal file.** A shoreline adaptation and restoration project at Whey-ah-Wichen (Cates Park) is proposed by the District and the Tsleil-Waututh Nation and is under review by the Vancouver Fraser Port Authority, because the port authority states the shoreline is eroding and the risk worsens with sea level rise — the park's condition is not just a parks-department matter. https://www.portvancouver.com/permit/shoreline-adaptation-and-restoration-whey-ah-wichen-cates-park
- **The park's name carries the area's history.** The port authority's shoreline report states Burrard Inlet and the surrounding lands have been occupied, governed and protected by the Tsleil-Waututh Nation for thousands of years, and that Whey-ah-Wichen (Cates Park) holds important cultural significance for the Tsleil-Waututh people; the reserve shoreline (Burrard Inlet IR #3) runs along the middle of the Dollarton shore. https://www.portvancouver.com/sites/default/files/2025-04/02%20A4%20Overview%20Effects%20Assessment.pdf
- **MLS calls the east end "Roche Point," not Dollarton.** Cates Landing and Raven Woods listings are filed under the Roche Point sub-area name — a buyer searching only "Dollarton" listings will miss the shore's only strata stock, and a 55+ page that borrows Roche Point buildings still has no age-restricted product to show, because none of them is 55+. https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/3825-cates-landing-way/207 and https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/650-roche-point-drive/49
- **One road in, one road out.** Every Dollarton trip — hospital, groceries at Parkgate or Maplewood, the Phibbs buses — funnels onto Dollarton Highway and Main Street to Highway 1. For a senior buyer, that single-corridor fact matters more day to day than the view. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt211.pdf
- **Maplewood's planned village is at the wrong end for most of the shore.** The OCP's Maplewood Village Centre (retail with apartments and live/work above on Old Dollarton Road) is a west-end plan; the Cates Park and Deep Cove end of Dollarton has no equivalent centre in any source read, and a grocery store by name on the highway is UNVERIFIED. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **(June 2024)** The District's three-unit rule (house + suite + coach house) inside the Urban Containment Boundary is the only "age in place" mechanism Dollarton offers: there is no 55+ product to buy, so staying on the shore past downsizing age means adapting a house, not moving into a seniors building. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
