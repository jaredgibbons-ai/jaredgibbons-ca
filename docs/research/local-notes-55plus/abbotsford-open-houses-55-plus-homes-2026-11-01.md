# Abbotsford Open Houses — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**What this file is: a city-wide Abbotsford framing for the open-houses topic page, with the city's full verified 55+ roster assembled from the Central Abbotsford, Abbotsford East and Abbotsford West files in this series, plus the Poplar file (the three named files themselves state Poplar holds 2 more strata complexes). One candidate, Carriage Lane, is flagged as a conflict at the end of Section 2 and is NOT counted in the 26.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw, and a tenant in a 55+ building must still meet the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act covers the three parks in Section 2: the resident owns the home and rents the pad; pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026; 2.2% for 2027) plus a proportional share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Open-house law (the topic of this page):** in BC, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); a licensed professional must work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party — the hosting agent at an open house represents the seller. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **The open-house disclosure exception:** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report, provides that a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **How open-house dates/times are entered for publication, and any visitor sign-in requirement:** UNVERIFIED — no BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states either. On this site's Abbotsford page the open-house time appears only inside the listing's description text (the cards carry no time field), and the page's own copy states typical hours two different ways (12:00 PM–4:00 PM in its route guide; 1:00 PM–4:00 PM or 2:00 PM–4:00 PM in its FAQ) — both are site copy; confirm the specific time with the listing brokerage. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-open-houses.html
- **Suites:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, s.140, allows 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots over 280 m² in the stated conditions, and s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings; a suite must be less than 50% of the size of the principal unit. Whether a specific open-house home's suite is permitted or registered: UNVERIFIED at property level. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **ALR:** any Abbotsford parcel in the Agricultural Land Reserve follows the ALC housing frame — one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), checked parcel by parcel on the ALC's maps. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Property taxes:** Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026; penalties are legislated and not waivable. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

- **Q: Will the 55+ buildings hold open houses I can just walk into?** UNVERIFIED building by building. The Abbotsford open-houses page states every listing it displays is an active property with a scheduled public open house, and its cards carry an "OPEN" badge — but the badge shows no date or time, the time sits in the listing description text, and no source states a no-appointment rule or a sign-in requirement for visitors. Confirm the specific home's time and access with the listing brokerage before driving. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-open-houses.html
- **Q: Who does the agent hosting the open house work for?** The seller. BCFSA states a licensed professional must work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party; the Rule 5-10(3) disclosure exception covers only hosting and factual answers — once you discuss your motivation, finances or needs, you are sharing with the seller's side. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in one of these buildings?** Yes in a strata — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption), and some buildings state a one-owner rule themselves: Regency Park's listing data says "One owner must be 55+," Royal Court's says only one owner must be at least 55, and The Wedgewood's says only one resident needs to be over 55. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent, or to move into later?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: A listing says "no rentals allowed." Can I rent my unit out?** For a long-term tenancy, yes: every strata rental-restriction bylaw in BC has been void since Nov 24, 2022, whatever an older bylaws summary (like Central Heights Manor's) says. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be banned. The manufactured-home parks are different — Georgian Park's listings state rentals of the homes are not allowed under park rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I keep a pet?** Building by building, and Abbotsford's 55+ stock leans strict: Evergreen Village, Elmwood Manor, Heather Ridge, Silverwood Manor, Fernwood Manor, Centennial Court and Fircrest Estates record no pets; Cloud Croft Manor allows one lap-sized dog or two cats; Parkside Place allows one cat, no dogs; Trethewey Tower's current MLS data allows one cat; Crown Point allows one cat or dog with restrictions; Downes Road Estates allows pets with restrictions. Summerset Place is UNCONFIRMED — its listing sources conflict. Confirm against the registered bylaws. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What's the difference between Garden Park Tower, a condo at Evergreen Village, and a home in Paradise Park?** Tenure. Evergreen Village is freehold strata — you own the unit and can sell it on the open market. Garden Park Tower is life lease — an up-front capital payment plus monthly maintenance buys lifetime occupancy, redeemed under the lease terms on departure. Paradise Park is a manufactured-home park — you own the home and rent the pad under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, with pad increases capped (2.3% for 2026; 2.2% for 2027) plus the proportional levy/utility share. https://gardenparktower.ca/life-lease and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Is there any 55+ stock on the east side or in the rural areas?** No strata or freehold ownership stock is verified in Abbotsford East, Aberdeen, Bradner, Matsqui, Sumas Mountain or Sumas Prairie. The east side's only verified 55+ housing is Cascade Court, a life lease residence on Delair Road at the sub-area's west edge. Sources: Section 2 above and the sub-area files in this series.
- **Q: If I buy 55+ now and need care later, what's in the city?** The ladder is in the urban core: independent strata or life lease → independent rental (Tabor Manor, 38 apartments; The Mayfair) → assisted living (Menno Assisted Living, Terrace East, Tabor Court, Bevan Village, the Hallmark residences) → long-term care (Menno Home, Menno Hospital, Tabor Home, MSA Manor, Maplewood House, plus the new 200-bed community rising at 1919 Jackson St, expected 2027). Sources: Section 2 above.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **This page's open-house cards cannot show the two-times problem found on sibling feeds.** A live visit showed the Abbotsford cards carry no dedicated date/time field at all — the only time is inside the listing description text (one card's text began "OPEN HOUSE- SAT 2:00- 3:30, SUN 12:30- 2:00") — so there is no second stated time for it to disagree with. The catch is the reverse: a buyer who reads only the card sees no time at all. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-open-houses.html
- **The page states typical open-house hours two different ways** — 12:00 PM–4:00 PM in its route guide, 1:00 PM–4:00 PM (or 2:00 PM–4:00 PM) in its FAQ. Both are site copy; no official source states a typical Abbotsford open-house window, so typical hours are UNVERIFIED as a fact. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-open-houses.html
- **Abbotsford's 55+ map is a west-side map.** Fifteen of the 26 verified strata complexes sit in Abbotsford West, most built 1979–1996 along Old Yale Road, Tims Avenue and Gladwin Road; the rural sub-areas recorded almost no attached stock at all in September 2026 (Bradner, Matsqui, Sumas Mountain and Sumas Prairie attached rows were 0 or near 0), which is why no age-restricted product exists there (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report).
- **Sub-area labels in listing data are unreliable, and this roster had to be de-duplicated.** Central Heights Manor is routinely labelled "Central Abbotsford" but belongs to Poplar; Parkside Place is filed Central by the building directory while some feeds say West; Fircrest Estates' feeds split between West and Central; Downes Road Estates is filed West only because the MLS says so. Each is counted once in Section 2, on the directory/plan evidence stated there.
- **Three "Maplewoods," three tenures, two sub-areas.** Maplewood Manor (32040 Tims Ave, Abbotsford West) is a buyable 55+ strata; Maplewood House (1919 Jackson St, Poplar) is a Fraser Health-funded care home with 78 beds; Maplewood Estates (2035 Martens St, Poplar) is a 55+ manufactured-home park. Search results blend all three. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Two life lease buildings bookend the city, run by charities, and neither shows up in strata searches.** Garden Park Tower (Clearbrook Golden Age Society, 111 suites, completed 1997) in the west and Cascade Court (Salvation Army) in the east are Abbotsford's two verified life lease seniors residences — a 55+ buyer who searches only MLS strata listings never sees them. https://gardenparktower.ca/history and https://rennie.com/listings/delair-35230-road-cascade-court-118-abbotsford
- **The subsidized care beds beside the hospital are scheduled to move.** Fraser Health's new long-term care community at 1919 Jackson St (Poplar) will replace the 109 beds at Cottage and Worthington Pavilion and add 91 more, with completion expected in 2027 — the city's care map changes on that date. https://thebeat.fraserhealth.ca/news/2023/Jun/new-long-term-care-community-coming-to-abbotsford
- **Old directories still call Trethewey Tower 45+.** Sub-55 age bylaws have been unenforceable since Nov 24, 2022, and current MLS data for 32440 Simon Ave records "Restricted Age: 55+" — price and qualify against the current registered bylaws, not a directory page. https://geraldlucas.ca/mylistings.html/listing.r3052595-1602-32440-simon-avenue-abbotsford-v2t-5r3.106964680
