## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on a different statute.** Pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA), not the Strata Property Act. A home owner must provide a purchaser with the written tenancy agreement and the written park rules, and a sale is an assignment of the pad tenancy that requires the park owner's consent; the assignment rules expressly recognise a park where every pad is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or 2+ tenants, at least one of whom has reached 55), as set out in s. 10(2)(b)(i) of the Human Rights Code — the park owner can refuse an assignment where no purchaser meets that age requirement. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol20/consol20/02077_pit
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** sets Chilliwack's urban residential zones, including the R1-A (Urban Residential) Zone with a minimum lot area of 500 m2 on community water and sewer and a minimum lot width of 15 m on the same services, and the R1-C (Urban Infill) Zone with a minimum lot area of 300 m2 on an interior lot and 360 m2 on a corner lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH:** Chilliwack's small-scale multi-unit housing amendments are in Bylaw No. 5395, adopted June 18, 2024 (per the assignment brief, from chilliwack.com). The Province later granted Chilliwack an extension for part of the Proper neighbourhood and surrounding Hazel Park area, with a new compliance date of Dec. 31, 2030 for that area — so SSMUH timing is not uniform across the city. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **City plan: Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 5500,** presents the long-term vision and growth strategy for the whole city, including long-term growth strategies for residential (urban, rural and hillside), commercial, industrial and institutional uses and the protection of the Agricultural Land Reserve; its Schedule D includes D-1 Drinking Water Supply, D-2 Hillside Environment, D-3 Riparian Area, D-4 Farm Home Plate and D-5 Hillside Development permit areas. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Open-house conduct rules apply in Chilliwack exactly as elsewhere in BC:** BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA) states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party; dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Property taxes (2026, as published by the City):** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% penalty applies for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% for taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026; on a sale, the buyer's and seller's lawyer or notary allocates the bill and advises which party claims the Home Owner Grant on the Statement of Adjustments. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where in Chilliwack should a 55+ buyer actually look first?** Downtown and Chilliwack Proper for strata condos — Ivy Green (45775 Spadina Ave), Knight Lodge (45660 Knight Rd) and Mountainview Estates (46966 Yale Rd) — then Cottage Grove (8500 Young Rd) for a gated rancher form, and Cottonwood Village (7610 Evans Rd) if a manufactured home on a rented pad is acceptable. No 55+ product was verified on Chilliwack Mountain or in Yarrow in this pass. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: I saw an open house for a home in a "55+" park that says 45+ or 40+ — is that the same thing?** No. Country Park Village is recorded as 45+ and Rainbow Estates as 40+, not 55+. In a strata, any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022; in a park, the age rule is a park rule under the MHPTA assignment framework, which is written around age 55 — read the park's own rules in writing. https://bentruscottrealestate.com/manufactured-homes.html?utm_medium=paid&utm_source=fb&utm_id=52609658579346&utm_content=52609658626346&utm_term=52609658585146&utm_campaign=52609658579346 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: At Cottonwood Village, what am I actually buying?** The manufactured home only. You rent the pad under the MHPTA, the park is on First Nations leased land, and listings state a lease expiry in 2076 with an option to extend — the lease term, the pad tenancy agreement and the park rules are the due-diligence documents, not a strata Form B. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/listing/r3069101-50-7610-evans-road-chilliwack-bc-v2r-2y8
- **Q: Can the park refuse my purchase because of age?** In a 55+ park, in effect yes: assignment of a pad tenancy needs the park owner's consent, and the provincial assignment rules recognise refusal where no purchaser meets the park's 55+ age requirement. The seller must provide the written tenancy agreement and park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. In a park, the test in the assignment rules is that at least one of 2+ tenants has reached 55. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent, or as an investment?** In a strata, ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside; the Province states under-55s can own but may not be able to live in the unit, and a 55+ parent can be the resident. In a park, the equivalent answer comes from the park rules and the assignment consent, not strata law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC strata — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, so a directory line saying "rentals not allowed" at Mountainview Estates does not survive that law. The tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Do I need my own agent at a Chilliwack open house, and do I have to sign in?** No official source requires your own agent to attend. BCFSA requires a licensee to inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who represents the other party, and dual agency is prohibited — so factual chat with the host does not make you their client, but sharing your motivation, budget or needs is sharing with the seller's side. An identification or sign-in requirement for open-house visitors is NOT stated in any BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source found — UNVERIFIED as a rule; an individual host may still ask on the seller's instructions. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: Are pets allowed in Chilliwack's 55+ buildings?** Building by building, and the verified examples point opposite ways: Ivy Green records pets not allowed; Mountainview Estates records no pets in its listings; Knight Lodge records pets allowed with restrictions; Cottonwood Village's park list records 1 pet allowed. There is no city-wide pet rule. https://www.admiraal.ca/buildings/view/27410 and https://www.estateblock.com/chilliwack/203-45660-knight-rd-49464
- **Q: Where are the hospital, recreation and library for a city-wide open-house run?** Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Rd; the Chilliwack Landing Leisure Centre, Unit #1, 9145 Corbould St, and the Cheam Leisure Centre, 45501 Market Way; and the Chilliwack Library at 45860 First Avenue. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=46

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Chilliwack has no published area stats to check a price against in the way Fraser Valley buyers are used to.** Chilliwack is CADREB territory and is not in SnapStats coverage, so this file states no inventory, sold-count, median-price or days-on-market figures for the city at all — a seller quoting a "Chilliwack median" at an open house is not quoting a SnapStats or CADREB published area figure from any source found here. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-open-houses.html
- **Two addresses a few doors apart on Evans Road are opposite products.** 7610 Evans Rd (Cottonwood Village) is 55+ leasehold park housing; 7694 Evans Rd (Creekside Estates), beside Evans Elementary, is a general-market strata whose listings state "no age restrictions." A corridor search that blurs the two misleads a 55+ buyer in both directions. https://bentruscottrealestate.com/manufactured-homes.html?utm_medium=paid&utm_source=fb&utm_id=52609658579346&utm_content=52609658626346&utm_term=52609658585146&utm_campaign=52609658579346 and https://rennie.com/listings/evans-7694-road-creekside-estates-314-chilliwack
- **The name trap at city scale: "Mountainview" is not on the mountain.** Mountainview Estates (46966 Yale Rd) is a genuine 55+ building in Chilliwack Proper East, while Chilliwack Mountain itself — Reflections at Cedar Sky, Eagles Perch, Osprey Ridge — has no verified 55+ product and Reflections expressly states no age restrictions. https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/46966-yale-road/104 and https://kw.com/property/43680-Chilliwack-Mountain-Road-23-Chilliwack-BC-V2R-6A6/2042669732905526
- **The city's 55+ strata supply is small, old and central.** The verified strata buildings in Section 2 are 1989–1994 stock (Mountainview Estates and Cottage Grove 1989, Ivy Green 1994) in the downtown/Proper core — no new age-restricted strata project anywhere in Chilliwack appears in any source checked in this pass, so the ownership supply turns over listing by listing, which is why the open-house feed is where it surfaces. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **SSMUH timing has a Chilliwack-specific exception.** While the city's SSMUH bylaw (No. 5395) was adopted June 18, 2024, the Province granted an extension to Dec. 31, 2030 for part of the Proper neighbourhood and surrounding Hazel Park — a buyer counting on suite or multiplex potential on a Proper-area lot needs the lot-specific answer, not the city-wide headline. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **The Sardis Neighbourhood Plan (Schedule B5 of the 2050 OCP) expects the fastest growth in the seniors cohort city-wide** — residents aged 75+ growing at 2.4% to 4.5% per year between 2016 and 2041 — the City's own context for demand on the south side, where the verified ownership supply is thinnest. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- **Chilliwack taxes have two penalty steps and a dyking add-on:** a 5% penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026, plus separate drainage and dyking levies on top of the municipal rates — relevant to any completion straddling early July. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **The City does not run any of the housing in its own inventory:** the Chilliwack Housing Inventory 2026 states the City does not own, operate or manage any listed housing option and that inclusion does not imply endorsement or guaranteed availability — eligibility and availability always come from the operator. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8558
