## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. On Serpentine's ground this matters only if a parcel is ever developed as a strata — the land itself has no age rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR frame — the rule book that actually governs most of this feed's ground:** on Agricultural Land Reserve parcels, one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m², a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); subdivision of ALR land requires Agricultural Land Commission permission outside limited regulation exceptions. Which feed parcels are in the ALR: UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel on ALC mapping; listing text is not an ALR determination. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Surrey SSMUH (urban lots only — not ALR parcels):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey, non-ALR):** only one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, not within a coach house or garden suite, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Coach houses and garden suites (Surrey, non-ALR):** allowed in all residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes; 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access; one extra parking space required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **City zoning and OCP:** Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000 governs the ground, parcel by parcel in COSMOS; the current OCP is Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900 — adopted June 15, 2026, replacing Bylaw No. 18020, which must never be cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Short-term rentals in Surrey:** under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

Can I buy in a 55+ complex as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside; the Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them. - **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a nearby 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022; the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Only where a 55+ bylaw exists — in the neighbouring complexes, mechanically yes (the next buyer must be 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit).
