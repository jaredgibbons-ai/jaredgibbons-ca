# Fraser VE (Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Fraser VE is the Fraser Street corridor in Vancouver East, running south from the East Broadway / Kingsway area to SE Marine Drive. "Fraser" is not one of the City's 22 distinct local areas — the corridor runs through four official local areas: Kensington-Cedar Cottage, Riley Park, Sunset and Victoria-Fraserview. A block-by-block board mapping of Fraser VE to those four areas is UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/kensington-cedar-cottage.aspx
- The City's Villages program names Fraser Street and East 33rd Avenue as a village area, with retail expansion planned through the Villages Plan. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/share-your-thoughts-on-vancouver-villages-nov-2025.aspx
- Cross streets: East Broadway, Kingsway, East King Edward Avenue, East 33rd Avenue, East 41st Avenue, East 49th Avenue and SE Marine Drive. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/ttN8.pdf
- Schools on or beside the corridor: John Oliver Secondary, 530 East 41st Avenue, sits on Fraser Street itself; elementaries in the local areas the corridor runs through include Sir Richard McBride (1300 East 29th Ave), Sir Alexander Mackenzie (960 East 39th Ave), Henderson (451 East 53rd Ave), Moberly (1000 East 59th Ave) and Trudeau (449 East 62nd Ave). Catchments are address-based — for the sample address 530 East 41st Avenue, the SD39 locator result is Sir Alexander Mackenzie Elementary and John Oliver Secondary. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods and https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
- Library: South Hill Branch, Vancouver Public Library, 6076 Fraser Street. https://www.vpl.ca/location/south-hill-branch
- Parks: Memorial South Park, 5955 Ross Street, beside the corridor in Sunset. https://covapp.vancouver.ca/parkfinder/parkdetail.aspx?inparkid=186
- Health: Mount Saint Joseph Hospital is beside the corridor in Kensington-Cedar Cottage (Prince Edward Street entrance); Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, is west of the corridor. https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/visitor-guides/mount-saint-joseph-hospital-patient-visitor-guide/when-you-arrive
- Transit: Route 8 Fraser runs Waterfront Station to SE Marine Drive via Fraser Street, ending beside Marine Drive Station (Canada Line); the N8 is the night service. Cross-corridor routes include the 49 at East 49th Ave, the R4 41st Ave RapidBus and the 41 at East 41st Ave, and the 9 at East Broadway. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/stop/50849/schedule
- Where 55+ housing actually sits: one address — the corner of Fraser Street and East 19th Avenue, where the North Arm Housing Co-operative (58 homes, 55+) shares its building with the 411 Seniors Centre (Section 2). No 55+ ownership building was verified anywhere on the corridor.

The one verified 55+ community on the corridor is a non-profit housing co-operative — affordable rental in co-op form, not a purchase — below.

### Subsidized seniors rental (non-profit, income-based — not ownership)

- **North Arm Housing Co-operative — 710 East 19th Avenue (Fraser Street at East 19th Ave; the City's project site address is 3510 Fraser Street).** A 58-home co-operative for people aged 55+: studio and one-bedroom homes in a six-storey building (the co-op's own site says seven storeys — the storey count conflicts between sources, UNCONFIRMED; the 58-home count and the 55+ rule agree across all sources). BC Housing records it as 58 new affordable rental homes for seniors with low or moderate incomes on the Fraser Street corridor, operated as a co-operative managed by the Community Land Trust of British Columbia (CLTBC), with BC Housing grant funding of approximately $10.8 million and the City leasing the land at a nominal rate. The Co-operative Housing Federation of BC lists it as a 55+ community in Kensington-Cedar Cottage, and CHF BC tour coverage records it opened in 2023. The ground floor is the permanent home of the 411 Seniors Centre Society, with a commercial kitchen, multipurpose space and offices. Co-op membership is not ownership of a strata lot — you cannot buy a unit here. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/nearly-60-affordable-homes-for-seniors-open-in-vancouver and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/ and https://northarmcoop.ca/ and https://council.vancouver.ca/20180918/documents/a12.pdf
- The City of Vancouver's Seniors Housing Strategy (July 2024) uses North Arm as its case study: "Located at Fraser Street and 19th Ave, this non-profit co-op houses 58 new studio and one-bedroom homes for older adults aged 55+," with five fully accessible one-bedroom homes and the 411 Seniors Centre's 8,000 sq ft ground-floor space. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/seniors-housing-strategy.pdf
- Corridor-edge entries whose Fraser VE placement is UNCONFIRMED: BC Housing's Zone 6 directory (May 2025) groups **Culloden Court, 6265 Knight St** and **Wilson Heights Manor, 1602 East 41st Ave E** under "Vancouver - Victoria, Fraserview and Sunset." Both sit east of Fraser Street toward Knight Street / Victoria Drive; no source places either inside the Fraser VE board sub-area, so neither is counted as Fraser VE stock here. https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). Note the North Arm co-op's 55+ rule is a co-op membership rule, not a strata bylaw — the strata sections do not govern it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** Since Nov 24, 2022 no strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw; stratas may still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Vancouver Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575.** The residential side streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m), 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. The district schedule for the Fraser Street commercial frontage itself: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plans:** the Kensington-Cedar Cottage Vision and the Sunset and Victoria-Fraserview / Killarney Visions apply to the local areas the corridor runs through, alongside the citywide Vancouver Plan; the Villages Planning Program names Fraser Street and East 33rd Avenue among its village areas. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/share-your-thoughts-on-vancouver-villages-nov-2025.aspx
- **City seniors policy:** the City of Vancouver Seniors Housing Strategy (July 2024) is the City's framework for older-adult housing and uses the corridor's North Arm co-op as a case study for the campus-of-care / NORC (naturally occurring retirement community) model. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/seniors-housing-strategy.pdf
- **Flood:** the corridor is inland from the Fraser River; the City's designated floodplain covers the Fraser River coastal floodplain at the south end of the city, and land in the floodplain is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for setbacks and flood construction levels. Whether a specific corridor parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED — check the City dataset parcel by parcel. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Vancouver tax calendar:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; owners must also file the annual Empty Homes Tax property status declaration (the 2025 tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% late penalty). https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Fraser VE?** None that any source verifies. No age-restricted strata ownership building was found on the corridor — its named strata buildings (Fraser Commons, Mondella, The Sheridan, Fraserview Court, Century, Bold on Fraser, Magnolia) carry no 55+ bylaw in any source. What a buyer can purchase here is a house on the R1-1 side streets or a general-market corridor condo. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: What 55+ housing does exist on Fraser Street?** One community: the North Arm Housing Co-operative, 710 East 19th Ave — 58 studio and one-bedroom homes for people 55+, for low- or moderate-income seniors, run as a co-op by the Community Land Trust of BC with the 411 Seniors Centre on the ground floor. It is membership housing, not a purchase. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/nearly-60-affordable-homes-for-seniors-open-in-vancouver and https://northarmcoop.ca/
- **Q: How is a housing co-op different from buying a 55+ condo?** At North Arm you hold co-op membership and pay monthly housing charges — coverage of the arrangement in CHF BC's magazine records charges set at not more than 30% of gross income for its first member — rather than owning a strata lot you can sell. There is no equity to buy or resell; admission is through the co-op / Community Land Trust process. https://healthyagingcore.ca/news/news-vancouver-senior-gets-affordable-housing-at-north-arm-co-op-1
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a BC 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in the defined cases. (For the North Arm co-op, ask the co-op how its own 55+ membership rule treats a younger spouse — its published sources do not state it: UNCONFIRMED.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What does the side-street zoning let a senior do with a Fraser-area house?** Under R1-1, a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house — the corridor's practical aging-in-place route: a caregiver or family member in the suite or laneway house, on lots from a 306 m² minimum site area. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Mount Saint Joseph Hospital sits beside the corridor in Kensington-Cedar Cottage; Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, is west of the corridor. https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/visitor-guides/mount-saint-joseph-hospital-patient-visitor-guide/when-you-arrive
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** Route 8 runs the full corridor from Waterfront Station to SE Marine Drive and the Canada Line at Marine Drive Station, with the N8 at night; the R4 RapidBus and route 41 cross at East 41st Ave and route 49 at East 49th Ave. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/stop/50849/schedule
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay on the corridor?** No assisted-living residence or care home with a Fraser Street address was verified — the Registry's Vancouver list places the nearest registered residences off-corridor (e.g. Legacy Senior Living, 611 West 41 Ave; Icelandic Harbour, 2020 Harrison Drive). The corridor's seniors infrastructure is the 411 Seniors Centre's programs inside the North Arm building, not care beds. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc&f%5B0%5D=city%3AVancouver&page=1

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The corridor's one 55+ building has two addresses:** BC Housing and the co-op use 710 East 19th Avenue; the City's 2018 project report for the same building uses the site address 3510 Fraser Street (the southeast corner of Fraser and 19th). Same building, same 58 homes — a search under only one address misses half the record. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/nearly-60-affordable-homes-for-seniors-open-in-vancouver and https://council.vancouver.ca/20180918/documents/a12.pdf
- **Its storey count conflicts in its own sources:** BC Housing's release calls North Arm "a six-storey building"; the co-op's own website describes "the seven-story building." The 58-home count and the 55+ rule agree everywhere; the storeys do not — flagged here rather than resolved. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/nearly-60-affordable-homes-for-seniors-open-in-vancouver and https://northarmcoop.ca/
- **The name is a history lesson:** the co-op's site explains Fraser Street was historically known as North Arm Road, the route between the Indigenous foot trails now known as Kingsway and SE Marine Drive — the co-op is named for the street's old name, not for a developer brand. https://northarmcoop.ca/
- **"Fraser" is four neighbourhoods wearing one street name:** because the corridor crosses Kensington-Cedar Cottage, Riley Park, Sunset and Victoria-Fraserview, City services, visions and even BC Housing directory groupings file corridor addresses under different neighbourhood names — Culloden Court and Wilson Heights Manor sit in the same Zone 6 "Victoria, Fraserview and Sunset" list as corridor stock while lying east of Fraser Street. Verify the sub-area by address, not by which list a building appears in. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx and https://www.bchousing.org/publications/Zone-6-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **The City chose this corner on purpose:** the City's 2018 report for 3510 Fraser Street states the site sits "at the intersection of three Vancouver neighbourhoods that are currently underserved by senior's services and represent a large number of low income seniors and seniors living alone," and ties the project to the City's Age Friendly Action Plan — the co-op exists because the corridor's seniors were already there without seniors housing. https://council.vancouver.ca/20180918/documents/a12.pdf
- **The Empty Homes Tax declaration is an annual chore every Vancouver owner carries:** a Fraser VE owner who leaves a property vacant between homes, or holds it as a second property, must still file the yearly property status declaration — the 2025 payment deadline was April 16, 2026 with a 5% late penalty. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
