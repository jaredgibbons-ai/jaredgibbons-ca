## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. Northwest has no verified 55+ strata, so this matters here mainly when comparing against East Central's Fraserview Village buildings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019.** RS-1 subdivision minimums are 668.0 m² lot area, 18.0 m width and 27.0 m depth, maximum coverage 40%; the rural and agricultural zones carry much larger minimums — RS-2 at 0.4 ha, RS-3 at 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha, A-1 at 2.0 ha, A-2 at 4.0 ha, A-3 at 8.0 ha. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Suites and added units:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws — the practical age-in-place route in a sub-area with no 55+ complex. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Floodplain:** the City publishes North and South Alouette River and Fraser River floodplain maps; the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), no habitable floor space may sit below it on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. Which Northwest lots are affected is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **ALR:** the sub-area's north side includes farm land per the live page's framing, but ALR status is per-parcel — check the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping for any specific lot. On ALR parcels: one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025) — with no building in this sub-area, applications are for the downtown buildings listed in that directory. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ complex in Northwest Maple Ridge itself?** No. No age-restricted strata, 55+ park, seniors rental or care residence with a Northwest address was verified in any source checked, and the sub-area's shelf is detached-led (30 detached listings vs 5 attached in September 2026) — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Is the "Westbrooke" condo in the feed a 55+ building?** No source says so — the name appears in one feed card's listing text only, with no developer or strata source confirming the building and no age restriction stated. Do not treat a feed name as a verified complex. https://jaredgibbons.ca/northwest-maple-ridge.html
- **Q: Where is the nearest actual 55+ ownership stock?** East, in East Central and Cottonwood MR: the four Fraserview Village strata buildings around 116 Avenue and 225–227 Streets, St. George's Village Co-op on Dewdney Trunk Road (55+, share purchase), plus the Alouette 55+ manufactured-home park (12868 229 St) and the Haney Pioneer Village Co-op (11830 230 St, 60+). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata (in the city complexes)?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I age in place in a Northwest house and add a suite or second dwelling?** On a standard lot: one secondary suite inside the house (maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the floodplain area), with SSMUH paths to three, four or six units on eligible lots. On an ALR parcel the provincial frame applies instead — one principal residence ≤500 m² plus at most one additional residence at 90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size — and floodplain lots cannot have suites at all under s. 402.8(h). Confirm zone, ALR status and floodplain status for the exact parcel first. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is there bus or train service if I stop driving?** The transit anchors are Maple Meadows Station (West Coast Express, weekdays) on the city's west side and Haney Place Exchange with the R3 RapidBus to Coquitlam Central Station (SkyTrain). Which local bus routes serve a specific Northwest street was not verified route by route in this pass: UNVERIFIED — check the exact address on TransLink. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, with 24/7 emergency care, east of this sub-area toward the Town Centre; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 121 – 11900 Haney Place, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- **Q: If I need care later, is there anything in Northwest?** No care or assisted-living residence in Northwest Maple Ridge appears in BC's Assisted Living Registry; the registered Maple Ridge residences are all at downtown addresses, and the in-city ladder (Fraserview Village strata, 224 Street retirement residences, Zone 4 subsidized rentals) is an East Central / Town Centre ladder. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=carouselnext&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&page=0&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc
