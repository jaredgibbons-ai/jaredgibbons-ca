# Central Park BS (Burnaby) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Central Park BS is the residential area around Central Park in south-west Burnaby, on the Vancouver border at Kingsway, directly west of Metrotown — between Boundary Road (the Vancouver border) and Metrotown, along Kingsway and Imperial Street. The "BS" in the site slug is this site's Burnaby South filing tag, not part of the area's name. The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for the MLS sub-area — exact boundary streets are UNVERIFIED. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01) and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- The MLS name: "Central Park" is the GVR board sub-area name as it appears in the SnapStats GVR September 2026 report under community "Burnaby," matching Central Park itself, the City park the area surrounds. It is a different sub-area from "Central" (the Canada Way / Deer Lake civic area) — do not confuse the two. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report)
- The park itself is bounded by Boundary Road on the west, Kingsway on the north, Patterson Avenue on the east and Imperial Street on the south, with its main entrance off Kingsway near Patterson Avenue. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/central-park
- The market is a genuine mix weighted to strata: September 2026 recorded 11 detached listings with 2 solds (18% sales ratio, a very small sample) against 22 condos & townhomes listings with 6 solds (27%). (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report)
- Schools are School District 41 (Burnaby): 41 elementary and 8 secondary schools city-wide, no middle-school tier — elementary is K–7 and secondary is 8–12. SD41 schools in or beside this sub-area on the Province's list are Maywood Community Elementary (K–7), 4567 Imperial St, Chaffey-Burke Elementary (K–7), 4404 Sardis St, north of the park, and École Inman Elementary (K–7), 3963 Brandon St, with Moscrop Secondary (8–12), 4433 Moscrop St, and Burnaby South Secondary (8–12), 5455 Rumble St, the nearest secondaries. École Inman is a French Immersion program school — an application-based program of choice, not the default catchment school. Catchments are address-based through the SD41 locator; the locator result for a Central Park sample address is UNVERIFIED. https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/ and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- Transit: Patterson Station (Expo Line) is at the park's north-east corner and Metrotown Station (Expo Line) is east of the park — two SkyTrain stations frame the sub-area. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Recreation: Bonsor Recreation Complex is in the Metrotown area east of the park and serves this side of the city. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities
- Library: the branch serving this area is the Bob Prittie Metrotown Branch, 6100 Willingdon Ave, east of the park in Metrotown. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/city-departments/burnaby-public-library
- Health care: the Metrotown Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 102-4555 Kingsway, east of the park; the city's hospital is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St (Fraser Health), in central Burnaby. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/burnaby/metrotown-urgent-and-primary-care-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- Where the seniors housing actually is: one building — Kingsway Court on Inman Avenue, on the park's west side, a non-profit subsidized seniors rental run by the Central Park Citizen Society. Everything else a "Central Park 55+" search returns is either a general-market tower that borrows the park's name or a care building on a boundary street assigned to a neighbouring sub-area in these notes. See Section 2.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Kingsway Court's age test is not a strata bylaw:** it is a non-profit subsidized seniors development in BC Housing's Zone 5 directory, operated by the Central Park Citizen Society — eligibility and applications run through the BC Housing / operator route, not through a purchase or the Strata Property Act. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076
- **Zoning bylaw — note the name change.** Research briefs for Burnaby sometimes still cite the "Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965". The City states the new **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026** was adopted June 9, 2026, replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, and took effect July 1, 2026. Use the 2026 bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **R1 SSMUH zoning:** Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district in June 2024 — 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and minimum lot area of 281 m², lots with two or more primary units may be stratified, and secondary suites count toward the unit total and cannot be stratified. With two Expo Line stations framing this sub-area, the 400 m frequent-transit test matters on more house lots here than in most Burnaby sub-areas. The Kingsway/Imperial apartment parcels follow their own districts, not R1. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP: the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict; Metrotown, directly east of the park, is Burnaby's regional city centre and downtown under it. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- **Property taxes:** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; the City's 2026–2030 Financial Plan approved a 2.9% tax increase (about $61 on the average home). The detached single-family water utility charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy around Central Park?** None that any source verifies as an age-restricted strata. The sub-area's one verified seniors building, Kingsway Court (5560 Inman Ave), is subsidized non-profit rental. A buyer here is purchasing a regular condo, townhome or house with no age bylaw — including in the towers that carry the park's name. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Central Park House a seniors building? It sounds like one.** No. Central Park House (5987 Wilson Ave) is a general-market Bosa condo tower — 356 condominiums and 6 townhomes in the project summary, marketed on its Patterson Station and park position, with no age restriction stated. The name refers to the park across the street, not to an age group. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/wilson-bluesky-metrotown-burnaby-presale-condos/
- **Q: How do I get into Kingsway Court?** By application, not purchase: it is listed in BC Housing's Zone 5 seniors directory as a non-profit development of 106 bachelor and 10 one-bedroom units run by the Central Park Citizen Society, so the route in is the BC Housing housing-registry/application process and the society. Note the directory edition located is January 2021 — re-confirm current details with BC Housing. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076
- **Q: Is this the same area as "Central" in Burnaby listings?** No. Central Park is the park-side sub-area on the Vancouver border, west of Metrotown; "Central" is the Canada Way / Deer Lake civic area to the north-east. Listings, searches and price comparisons that mix the two are mixing two different markets. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report)
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Burnaby?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** Two Expo Line stations — Patterson at the park's north-east corner and Metrotown east of the park — plus the BC Parkway trail running through the park under the SkyTrain line. For a senior household this is one of the best-served sub-areas in Burnaby, even though its housing is general-market. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres and https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- **Q: Where is urgent care and the hospital?** The Metrotown Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 102-4555 Kingsway, east of the park; the nearest hospital is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/burnaby/metrotown-urgent-and-primary-care-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay around Central Park?** The in-area step is rental, not care: Kingsway Court covers independent subsidized living, and the nearest registered assisted-living residence is Courtyard Terrace on Burke St at the park's south side — counted in the Garden Village notes, with its sub-area assignment flagged UNCONFIRMED in Section 2. No long-term care home was verified inside this sub-area. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASupportive%20Recovery&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The operator's name is the tell.** The one real seniors building here is run by the Central Park Citizen Society — a non-profit citizens' society, not a developer. When a "Central Park" building's operator is a housing society and its entry sits in BC Housing's seniors directory, it is application-based rental; when the operator is a condo developer, it is a market tower, whatever the name suggests. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076 and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/wilson-bluesky-metrotown-burnaby-presale-condos/
- **The subsidized-seniors paper trail is old.** The newest BC Housing Zone 5 seniors directory located in this research is dated January 2021 — Kingsway Court's unit counts (106 bachelor, 10 one-bedroom) come from that edition and should be re-confirmed with BC Housing before being quoted to an applicant. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076
- **Attached outsold detached here in September 2026.** Six attached solds on 22 listings (27%) against two detached solds on 11 listings (18%, a very small sample) — condo sellers could price with some confidence that month while house sellers needed a longer window. Sub-area median prices by property type: UNVERIFIED — SnapStats publishes medians at community level only. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report)
- **The French Immersion school is not a catchment promise.** École Inman Elementary (3963 Brandon St) sits in or beside this sub-area, but French Immersion in SD41 is an application-based program of choice — a listing that presents Inman as the neighbourhood school for a park-side address is overstating it. https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/
- **Burnaby replaced its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (adopted June 9, 2026, in force July 1, 2026) repealed and replaced the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 — documents or summaries that still cite the 1965 bylaw are out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Burnaby taxes have two penalty steps.** 2026 property taxes were due July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date, and the detached water charge ($652.33) carries a 5% early-payment discount. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
