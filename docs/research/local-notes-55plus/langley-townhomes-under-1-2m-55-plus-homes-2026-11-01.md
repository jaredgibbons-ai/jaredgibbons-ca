## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. Read Willow Park Estates' "at least one owner must be 55" and Chelsea Green's "only one occupant to be 55+" listing wordings against these exemptions: a listing summary is not the bylaw, and the bylaw cannot remove the regulation's exemptions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. Willow Park Estates still carries "rentals not allowed" in its building record; for a long-term rental that line cannot be relied on. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Township townhouse suites — a live source conflict, flagged not resolved.** Under Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102 (as amended July 2025), a secondary suite may be located within a townhouse, rowhouse or manorhome, is capped at 120 m² maximum gross floor area, and each principal dwelling unit may have up to one; the Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states the opposite — suites are NOT permitted in townhouses/rowhouses. The bylaw definition is the later source; where a listing in this band markets a lower-level suite or mortgage helper, confirm the specific unit's permits and strata bylaws before valuing the income. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, cited in the research file.
- **Langley City suites (for the feed's City townhomes):** under Langley City Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300, a secondary suite may not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, and the registered owner must reside on the lot (s. 2.4). The City and Township suite rules are different — apply the one for the municipality the complex is actually in. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, cited in the research file.
- **SSMUH, by municipality:** Township — Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024: 3 units on lots ≤280 m², 4 on lots to <4,050 m², larger lots ineligible, no 6-unit tier; strata townhome lots are not the SSMUH shape, and no SSMUH claim is made for any complex in this file. City — SSMUH Bylaw 3284, adopted June 17, 2024, permits RS1/RS2 lots "4; 6 if within 400 m of frequent bus stop." Sources: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, cited in the research file.
- **Willoughby's compact-lot zone (Township Bylaw 2500, s. 403.7, R-CL):** minimum lot area 220 m², maximum lot area 464.5 m², frontages 11 m / 8.2 m. A "220–355 m²" range that circulated in earlier program research is wrong — no 355 figure exists in s. 403. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, cited in the research file.
- **Manufactured-home parks run on park rules, not strata law:** pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), which recognises a park reserved for tenants who have reached 55 and allows a landlord to refuse an assignment where the purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement; pad rent increases are capped at 2.3% for 2026 plus a proportional levy/utility amount, once per 12 months on 3 full months' notice. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Property taxes — two calendars:** Township taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026, and utility fees billed separately; the City also sets a July 2 due date, with the City's penalty rate UNCONFIRMED on the page cited in the roster file. Six of the seven verified 55+ townhouse complexes are in the Township; Huntsfield Green is in the City. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx and https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes

- **Q: Which verified 55+ townhouses could a buyer in this band look at?** Seven complexes: Huntsfield Green in Langley City; Willow Park Estates in Willoughby; Churchill Park, Spencer Green, Chartwell Green and Chelsea Green in Walnut Grove; and Creekside Villa in Aldergrove. No price for any of them is stated here — whether a specific unit is listed under $1.2M is a live-listing question. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ townhouse?** For a strata townhouse, yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a 55+ townhouse for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a 55+ townhouse?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC strata — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, whatever Willow Park Estates' building record still shows. The tenant must still satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can a Langley townhome have a secondary suite?** The Township's current bylaw definition says yes, with a conflict to know about: Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102 (as amended July 2025) permits a secondary suite within a townhouse, capped at 120 m², one per principal dwelling unit — while the Township's January 2025 guide states suites are not permitted in townhouses. In Langley City, Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 caps a suite at the lesser of 100 m² and 40% with owner-occupancy required. Strata bylaws and permits for the specific unit still govern in practice. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, cited in the research file.
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the 55+ townhouse complexes?** Complex by complex: Creekside Villa records pets allowed with restrictions (2 maximum, shoulder-height limit in listings); Chartwell Green listings state 2 pets allowed with a 16-inch shoulder-height limit for dogs; Willow Park Estates sources conflict — one listing records cats and dogs OK to a limit of two, another a one-pet weight limit — SOURCE CONFLICT between listings, flagged in the roster file. The registered bylaw for the specific complex governs. Sources: Section 2 above and the roster file named there.
- **Q: Is Chartwell Green the same as Chartwell Langley Gardens?** No. Chartwell Green (8737 212 St) is a verified 55+ townhouse complex you can buy into; Chartwell Langley Gardens (8888 202 St) is a Chartwell rental continuum-of-care residence you cannot buy. Both are in Walnut Grove, which is why the names collide in searches. https://www.tmbrealestate.com/walnut-grove-townhomes/churchill-park and https://chartwell.com/en/bc/langley/langley-gardens
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
