Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The strata age-bylaw rules, for the record — with no local strata to apply them to.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; age bylaws below 55 invalid since Nov 24, 2022, under Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term restrictions remain allowed. A Rosedale buyer gets none of this machinery, because the purchase is freehold land, not a strata lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **The governing frame is the FVRD's, not the City of Chilliwack's.** Rosedale sits in FVRD Electoral Area D; the FVRD is responsible for governance, administration and services for its electoral areas. The live page's claim that a "Rosedale Neighbourhood Plan and Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan" govern Rosedale is UNVERIFIED as the governing frame — confirm planning, permits and servicing with the FVRD for the specific parcel. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/about-the-fvrd/electoral-areas.html and https://jaredgibbons.ca/rosedale-homes-for-sale.html
- **ALR housing caps are the rules that actually bind a 55+ buyer here.** Land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; local government bylaws may further regulate or restrict residences. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The second-dwelling rule is the ageing-in-place rule on rural land.** The ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and FVRD bylaws must also permit it. Anything larger needs an ALC application. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Additional manufactured home on a farm parcel: family occupancy only.** Under the ALR Use Regulation's additional-residence provisions (s. 34(3) as summarised in ALC Policy L-25), an additional manufactured-home residence that is not a pre-existing structure is permitted only if it is 9 m or less in width and is used only by the owner or specified relatives of the owner. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_-_policy_l-25_-_manufactured_homes_in_the_alr.pdf
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law** (relevant to the Mt Cheam park in Section 2): pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — the resident owns the home and rents the pad; pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% for 2026; 2.2% for 2027) plus a proportional share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees. The assignment rules expressly recognise a park where every pad is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55, per s. 10(2)(b)(i) of the Human Rights Code — Mt Cheam's listing states families are allowed, so it is not such a park. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Farm classification is a continuing obligation.** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income requirements at farm gate prices are $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha (over 4 ha), and $10,000 (under 0.8 ha); applications are due by October 31 for the next tax year. A downsizing buyer who stops producing can lose the classification. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property taxes — the RURAL regime applies here, not the City of Chilliwack's.** Rosedale is unincorporated, so taxes are paid to the Province under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11: rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on any portion unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on any portion unpaid on October 31. City of Chilliwack dates (due the first working day after July 1; second 5% after August 4, 2026) do NOT apply to a Rosedale parcel. The Province's regular property tax deferment program is for people 55 and older. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Floodplain and servicing are parcel-level checks.** The live page states Fraser River floodplain bylaws and flood-construction requirements apply in Rosedale; no FVRD floodplain bylaw or flood map was opened in the research pass, so any floodplain designation, flood construction level or hazard overlay for a specific parcel is UNVERIFIED. Community water/sewer vs well/septic for Rosedale lots is likewise UNVERIFIED at parcel level — the live page's "sewered village boundary" wording is site copy only. https://jaredgibbons.ca/rosedale-homes-for-sale.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Rosedale part of the City of Chilliwack?** No — Rosedale is an unincorporated community in FVRD Electoral Area D, east of Chilliwack; the FVRD places its Rosedale-addressed Cheam Lake Wetlands Regional Park in Electoral Area D and is the local government for its electoral areas, so FVRD — not City of Chilliwack — planning is the frame to confirm for a parcel. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/cheam-lake-wetlands.html?media=standard and https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/about-the-fvrd/electoral-areas.html
- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing in Rosedale itself?** No — none was verified in any category: no age-restricted strata, 55+ park, rental or care complex with a Rosedale address. Chilliwack's verified 55+ complexes and its one 55+ park are all in the urban city. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: The Rosedale park looks like a seniors park. Is it 55+?** No. Mt Cheam Mobile Home Park's own listing describes it as mostly senior-occupied but states families are allowed — there is no age restriction in its rules as listed. A park is only 55+ in law when its rules reserve the pads by age; this one's do not. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3089923-52604-yale-road-10-rosedale-bc-v0x-1x1/brochure
- **Q: I'm 55+ and buying a Rosedale acreage. What limits the house I can build?** On ALR land, a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. Confirm the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps first — parcel-level ALR status is UNVERIFIED until checked. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can a caregiver or my adult child live in a second dwelling on the parcel?** The ALC permits one additional residence without an application where its size rules are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels, detached — and FVRD bylaws must also permit it. There is no strata-style caregiver exemption because there is no strata. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Which school is in Rosedale itself?** Rosedale Traditional Community, 50850 Yale Rd Rr 1, Rosedale, V0X 1X2 — an SD33 Chilliwack public school listed as Elementary in the BC Ministry K-12 file. Catchment assignment for a specific home address is UNVERIFIED, so check the exact address with the district. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q: When are property taxes due on a Rosedale home?** To the Province, under the rural regime: due July 2, with a 5% penalty on any portion unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on any portion unpaid on October 31. The City of Chilliwack's schedule does not apply to unincorporated Rosedale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Q: Is there a bus if I stop driving?** BC Transit Route 71 Agassiz–Harrison runs along Yale Road through Rosedale, with timepoints at Yale Road at McGrath Road and Highway 9 at Yale Road, on Monday-to-Friday and Saturday timetables (Sunday service in the July 1 to Labour Day period), per the Rider's Guide effective June 28, 2026. It is a rural inter-community route, not a frequent urban service — read the current timetable before counting on it. https://www.bctransit.com/agassiz-harrison/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2026/06/chw_rg_june2026.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Rd, Chilliwack — a Fraser Health community hospital providing primary, core secondary and some specialty services, west of Rosedale in the urban city. Published drive times: UNVERIFIED. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The mailing shorthand misleads the whole purchase.** Rosedale addresses read as "Chilliwack" country to most buyers, but the parcel sits in FVRD Electoral Area D — so the planning counter, the servicing answers and the tax bill all belong to a different government than the live page's Chilliwack OCP framing suggests. Start with the jurisdiction, not the postal address. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/about-the-fvrd/electoral-areas.html
- **The tax calendar changes at the city line.** A City of Chilliwack home takes its second 5% penalty after August 4, 2026; a Rosedale home under the rural regime takes its further 5% on any portion unpaid on October 31. Same corridor, two different penalty clocks. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **The park is the amenity and the landmark.** Cheam Lake Wetlands Regional Park — 107 hectares, about 2 km of trails, a floating boardwalk, open 7 a.m. to sunset, no pets permitted — is the FVRD park that anchors Rosedale's identity, and it is also the source that settles the Electoral Area D question in Section 1. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/cheam-lake-wetlands.html?media=standard
