# Burnaby Hospital (Burnaby) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The Burnaby Hospital sub-area is the residential area around the Burnaby Hospital campus, south of Canada Way and west of Deer Lake. The exact MLS sub-area boundary streets are UNVERIFIED — no City or board source checked states them. Sources: the area research file and the hospital entry below.
- The anchor institution is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St, a Fraser Health hospital. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- Schools: Douglas Road Elementary, 4861 Canada Way; Cascade Heights Elementary, 4343 Smith Ave (south side); Burnaby Central Secondary, 6011 Deer Lake Parkway. The SD41 locator for the sample address 4861 Canada Way returns Douglas Road Elementary and Burnaby Central Secondary. Burnaby is School District 41 — 41 elementary and 8 secondary schools, no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12), and catchments are address-based through the district locator. https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/ and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- Transit: there is no SkyTrain station in this sub-area; local buses run on Kincaid St past the hospital. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- Planning: the Cascade Heights Community Plan, covering the south side of the sub-area, was adopted in March 2025. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- Where the seniors housing actually is: the verified seniors stock in and around this sub-area is the Dania campus of care on Norland Ave, between Canada Way and Deer Lake — all of it rental, assisted living, long-term care or subsidized non-profit housing. No age-restricted strata ownership building was verified here (see Section 2). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning bylaw — note the name change.** Research briefs for Burnaby sometimes still cite the "Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742)". The City states the new **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026** was adopted June 9, 2026, replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, and took effect July 1, 2026. Use the 2026 bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **R1 SSMUH zoning:** Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district in June 2024 — 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; multi-unit lots may be stratified. That is the rule set behind the multiplex and coach-house infill appearing on this sub-area's detached streets. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP: the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan maps. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Property taxes:** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; the City's 2026–2030 Financial Plan approved a 2.9% tax increase (about $61 on the average home). The detached single-family water utility charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Subsidized and assisted-living seniors housing here runs through applications, not listings:** Dania's subsidized suites are BC Housing / Fraser Health program housing, and its assisted-living suites are registered with BC's Assisted Living Registry — the registry and BC Housing are the routes in, not the MLS. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ABurnaby&f%5B1%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASupportive%20Recovery&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy near Burnaby Hospital?** None that any source verifies as an age-restricted strata. Searches for a 55+ ownership building with an address in this sub-area came up empty; the verified seniors stock here — the Dania campus on Norland Ave — is rental, assisted living, long-term care and subsidized housing. A buyer who wants to own near the hospital is buying a regular house, condo or townhome with no age bylaw, or looking at other Burnaby sub-areas. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Can I buy into the Dania campus on Norland Ave?** No. Dania Gardens (4277 Norland Ave) is an affordable RENTAL building — 109 subsidized suites for low-income seniors and 46 market-rate suites — run by a non-profit society with BC Housing; Dania Manor is assisted living and Dania Home is long-term care. None of it is strata ownership. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/affordable-homes-open-for-seniors-in-burnaby and https://www.dania.bc.ca/about-us/about-dania/
- **Q: Who qualifies as "55+" at Dania?** The City's housing agreement for the site defines a "Senior" as a person aged 55 or older (Housing Agreement Bylaw 14477, 4279 Norland Ave), and the society describes its independent-living suites as housing for individuals 55 and over. Subsidized suites are also income-tested through the BC Housing program. https://bylaws.burnaby.ca/media/14000/14477.pdf and https://www.dania.bc.ca/residences/independent-living/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we live in a 55+ strata if one comes up here?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How close is the hospital, really?** The sub-area wraps around the Burnaby Hospital campus at 3935 Kincaid St itself — the seniors campus on Norland Ave sits between Canada Way and Deer Lake, in the same pocket. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital and https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/construction-begins-on-housing-for-seniors-in-burnaby
- **Q: Is the hospital being rebuilt?** Yes, in phases. Phase 1 — a six-storey pavilion with 83 beds — opened in March 2026, and on September 21, 2026 the Province announced a renewed redevelopment plan with $210 million for an expanded emergency department and surgical services, with a cancer care centre and an acute-care tower to follow. Expect construction activity around the campus for years. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026INF0056-001108.pdf and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2026/Sep/Moving-forward-on-renewed-plan-to-redevelop-Burnaby-Hospital
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day?** There is no SkyTrain station in this sub-area; buses on Kincaid St serve the hospital, and the sub-area's practical transit is bus-based. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in this area?** The local ladder sits on one campus: independent subsidized/market rental at Dania Gardens, assisted living at Dania Manor (40 publicly funded plus 10 private-pay suites), and long-term care at Dania Home (102 beds) — all on Norland Ave, all accessed by application through the society, BC Housing or Fraser Health rather than by purchase. https://www.dania.bc.ca/about-us/about-dania/ and https://www.dania.bc.ca/residences/residential-care/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The "seniors buildings near the hospital" are one non-profit campus, not a market.** The Dania Society has operated on its Norland Ave site since 1938; the campus mixes subsidized rental, market affordable rental, assisted living and long-term care on one site between Canada Way and Deer Lake. Search results that make the hospital area look rich in "55+ homes" are describing this campus — and none of it can be bought. https://www.dania.bc.ca/about-us/about-dania/
- **The campus size is stated two ways.** The society describes a campus of about 7 acres; BC Housing's release describes the site as 2.8 hectares. The two figures do not reconcile exactly — treat campus size as approximately 7 acres / 2.8 ha and do not quote a precise figure. https://www.dania.bc.ca/about-us/about-dania/ and https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/construction-begins-on-housing-for-seniors-in-burnaby
- **The 55+ test at Dania is a housing agreement, not a strata bylaw.** Because the campus is non-profit rental/care housing, its age rule lives in the City housing agreement (Bylaw 14477, defining "Senior" as 55+) — a buyer reading about "55+ at Dania" is reading about rental eligibility, not an ownership product. https://bylaws.burnaby.ca/media/14000/14477.pdf
- **Burnaby replaced its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (adopted June 9, 2026, in force July 1, 2026) repealed and replaced the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 — documents, listings or summaries that still cite the 1965 bylaw are out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **The hospital next door is a construction zone on a long timeline.** Phase 1 of the Burnaby Hospital redevelopment opened in March 2026 (six storeys, 83 beds), and the Province's September 21, 2026 announcement adds a $210-million expanded emergency and surgical phase, with cancer care and an acute-care tower still to come — proximity to the hospital comes with years of phased construction around Kincaid St. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026INF0056-001108.pdf
- **Part of the Deer Lake boardwalk is closed.** A section of the Deer Lake Park boardwalk closed on September 21, 2026, with replacement expected by winter 2027 — the park is at this sub-area's east edge, so the lakeside walking loop is interrupted in the meantime. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- **The south side has a brand-new community plan.** The Cascade Heights Community Plan was adopted in March 2025, covering the sub-area's south side — newer than the Burnaby 2050 OCP process that followed it, and the document that sets expectations for the streets around Cascade Heights Elementary. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- **Burnaby taxes have two penalty steps.** 2026 property taxes were due July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date, and the detached water charge ($652.33) carries a 5% early-payment discount. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
