## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The regulator context for any testimonial a consumer acts on:** real estate trading services in BC are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), which states a licensed professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party, disclose at the outset whether they can represent the consumer and how to complain to BCFSA, and work for only the buyer or the seller in a transaction (dual agency prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances) — the frame a consumer reading reviews is actually hiring under. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Any rule, board standard or platform policy governing how a REALTOR® may display client testimonials on his own website** — attribution, consent, or whether a selection may be shown without the full set: UNVERIFIED — no BCFSA, board or platform policy page on testimonial display was opened for the research file or for this file, and none is described here from industry habit. https://jaredgibbons.ca/reviews.html
- **Whether the full review record behind this page** (the Google profile the site's other pages cite, and the Rate-My-Agent profile the footer links to) **supports any particular rating or count:** UNVERIFIED — neither profile was opened, and the footer's rate-my-agent.com link is recorded as a footer link only. No rating or count is stated in this file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/reviews.html
- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption (Strata Property Regulation 7.01: legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and since May 1, 2023 a younger spouse/partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Manufactured-home pads follow tenancy law, not strata law,** wherever a 55+ park from Section 2 is involved: the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act covers most manufactured home tenancies in BC, pad rent can be increased only once every 12 months with at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026; 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027), and assignment under s. 28 requires the landlord's prior written consent or an order of the director, with the pad rent unchanged on assignment. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
