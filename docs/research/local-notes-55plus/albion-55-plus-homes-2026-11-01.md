## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019.** Houses sit under the compact urban zones of that bylaw, with RS-1 (Single Detached Residential), s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40% — as the standard urban house zone; the Albion Area Plan adds RM townhome sites around the Albion Commercial Corner and Agricultural Land Reserve parcels in South Albion. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **City plan: OCP Bylaw No. 7060-2014, Albion Area Plan (Chapter 10.2).** Council adopted updates to the Albion Area Plan on October 28, 2025 — enhancing walking and cycling connections, supporting growth of local commercial nodes, and introducing buffers where urban development meets farmland; the plan's residential designations include Low Density Residential, Ground-Oriented Residential, Cluster Residential and Townhouse, plus an Albion Village Commercial node. No age-restricted or seniors project in the plan area was verified on any source checked. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId= and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/albion-area-plan-schedule-1pdf-1
- **Suites and added units:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-family and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Floodplain (south edge only):** along the Fraser River and Kanaka Creek the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. Which Albion lots are affected is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Manufactured home parks run on different law** (relevant because Maple Ridge's one 55+ park is in East Central, not here): pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), not the Strata Property Act, and the Regulation expressly contemplates parks where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025). https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ complex in Albion itself?** No. No age-restricted strata, 55+ park, seniors rental or care residence with an Albion, Kanaka Creek or 240 Street–corridor address was verified in any source checked, and Albion's attached shelf (18 listings in September 2026) sits in general-market family complexes — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Albion Station, Ridgemont, Spencer Brook, The Warwick — are any of them 55+?** No source says so. Albion Station is marketed to first-time buyers, investors and growing families as pet- and rental-friendly; The Warwick is marketed as "family-friendly Albion"; Ridgemont and Spencer Brook listings and building data show family townhomes with no age restriction recorded. Family marketing is the opposite signal from a 55+ bylaw — check the Form B if a listing ever claims otherwise. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3105045-115-10151-240-street-maple-ridge-british-columbia-v2w0g9-665000/brochure and https://www.zolo.ca/maple-ridge-real-estate/10392-240a-street/4 and https://www.estateblock.com/maple-ridge/37-23986-104-ave-92785
- **Q: Where is the nearest actual 55+ ownership stock?** West along Lougheed Highway / Dewdney Trunk Road in East Central Maple Ridge — the four Fraserview Village strata buildings around 116 Avenue and 225–227 Streets, plus the Alouette Retirement Mobile Home Park (12868 229 St, own the home, rent the pad) and the Haney Pioneer Village Co-op (11830 230 St, 60+). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata (in the city complexes)?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I age in place in an Albion house and add a suite or coach house later?** The standard route is one secondary suite per lot inside the house (maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the floodplain area), with up to three, four or six units on eligible lots under the City's SSMUH bylaws; on the Fraser River / Kanaka Creek south edge, floodplain lots cannot have suites at all under Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h). https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q: Is there bus service if I stop driving?** The transit anchors are west of Albion: Haney Place Exchange and the R3 RapidBus to Coquitlam Central Station (SkyTrain), plus weekday West Coast Express trains from Port Haney and Maple Meadows stations. Which local routes run on Albion streets was not verified route by route in this pass: UNVERIFIED — check the exact address on TransLink before relying on a stop. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects and https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- **Q: Where is the hospital and urgent care?** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, is the community hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows with 24/7 emergency care, west of Albion in the Town Centre side of the city; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 121 – 11900 Haney Place, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://www.healthlinkbc.ca/clinic/ridge-meadows-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- **Q: If I need care later, is there anything in Albion?** No care or assisted-living residence in Albion appears in BC's Assisted Living Registry; the registered Maple Ridge residences are all at downtown addresses, and the in-city ladder (independent 55+ strata at Fraserview Village, retirement residences on 224 Street, subsidized rentals through BC Housing's Zone 4 directory) is an East Central / Town Centre ladder, not an Albion one. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=carouselnext&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&page=0&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The Albion Area Plan was updated on October 28, 2025, and it plans family growth, not seniors housing:** the update enhances walking and cycling connections, supports growth of local commercial nodes, adds buffers where urban development meets farmland, and contemplates added density including townhouses along 248 Street in the North East Albion review — no age-restricted or seniors component is described in the plan material checked. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId=
- **The newest attached stock in Albion is explicitly family product:** The Warwick (10392 240a St, built 2026) sells "family-friendly Albion" three-bedroom townhomes, and Rydge at 248 Street names the two local schools as its selling point for residents' children — the growth pipeline is aimed at young families, which is why no 55+ supply is coming from it. https://www.zolo.ca/maple-ridge-real-estate/10392-240a-street/4 and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/rydge-by-raicon-developments-albion-presale-maple-ridge-townhomes/
- **Two of the four school addresses on the live area page are wrong:** it gives c̓əsqənelə Elementary as "10445 240B Street" (10445 is Samuel Robertson Technical's street number) and Kanaka Creek Elementary as "11180 234A Street"; the BC Ministry K-12 file records 24093 104 Avenue and 11120 234a Street. Quote school addresses from the district or Ministry file, and run catchments by exact address in the SD42 locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Albion's attached market is one of Maple Ridge's most active, which cuts both ways for a downsizer buying a general-market townhome here:** 18 active listings, 4 sold and a 22% sales ratio in September 2026 — the city's second-highest attached ratio — so resale competition among near-identical family townhomes is real — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
- **The floodplain bites only at the south edge, but it bites hard there:** Fraser River / Kanaka Creek–side lots carry the 6.7 m geodetic flood construction level, a floodplain covenant on title, no habitable floor space below that level, and a ban on suites on floodplain properties — check title before valuing a lower-level suite in South Albion. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **A Langley–Haney Place bus rapid transit line is in detailed planning** — 22 km between Willowbrook and Haney Place with peak service every 10 minutes; designs, costs and timelines are not yet determined, so it is a planning fact, not a service a buyer can use today. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/bus-rapid-transit
- **Property taxes:** Maple Ridge property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
