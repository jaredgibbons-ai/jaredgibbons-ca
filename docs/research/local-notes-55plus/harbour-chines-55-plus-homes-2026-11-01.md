## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City of Coquitlam zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996** — small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3; subdivision minimum lot size 650 m² with 18.5 m width for R-1 and R-3, and 465 m² with 12.5 m width for R-2. The provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules as adopted in Coquitlam allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId= and https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquitlam
- **Watercourse permits:** the City's Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam; Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and s.523 covers riparian areas — a live check on the Chines slope's ravine and creek-edge lots. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **City plan:** the City-wide Official Community Plan applies; a Harbour Chines neighbourhood plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquitlam
- **Coquitlam taxes 2026:** payment due July 2, 2026, covering January 1 through December 31, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants). https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025) or by applying directly to the non-profit societies listed there. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

**Q: Is Harbour Chines in Coquitlam or Port Coquitlam?**
A: Coquitlam — full stop. SnapStats carries Harbour Chines only under the Coquitlam community, City of Coquitlam sources place it in central Coquitlam around Como Lake Avenue, and there is no Harbour Chines sub-area in Port Coquitlam (or North Vancouver). The name carries no waterfront. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquitlam

**Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Harbour Chines?**
A: None were verified — no strata complex trades under this sub-area name (0 condos & townhomes listings in September 2026) and no age-restricted product of any kind was confirmed inside it. The nearest verified 55+ strata are Princess Gate (2985 Princess Crescent) and Marlborough House (3098 Guildford Way) in Town Centre Coquitlam, and the nearest 55+ manufactured homes are at Hideaway Park, 4200 Dewdney Trunk Rd in Ranch Park — all outside the sub-area. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.) https://bridgewellgroup.ca/property-search/?_pg=84

**Q: How is a manufactured home in a 55+ park different from a 55+ strata?**
A: At Hideaway Park the listing model is: you buy the manufactured home and rent the pad (a current listing states approximately $750/month, listing-stated — confirm with the park). There is no strata corporation and no Strata Property Act age bylaw; the park's own rules and the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act govern. That is a different legal product from a 55+ strata condo like Princess Gate. https://vancouvernashdom.com/coquitlam-mobile-homes-floating-homes-recreat/SearchResults.form/

**Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata like Princess Gate?**
A: Yes — Strata Property Regulation 7.01 (expanded May 1, 2023) exempts a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care, adult children the resident cared for before 19, and live-in caregivers are also exempt. The bylaw restricts residency, not ownership. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

**Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?**
A: Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. The tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

**Q: Can a Harbour Chines house lot add units — a suite or carriage home for family?**
A: Yes, under the City's SSMUH rules: up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes, where zoning (R-1/R-2/R-3 under Bylaw No. 3000) and municipal services allow. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquitlam

**Q: Is there subsidized seniors rental in the area?**
A: None inside Harbour Chines. The BC Housing Zone 4 directory's Coquitlam Housing Registry buildings are all in north Coquitlam (Johnson St, Pipeline Rd, Ozada Ave), and its apply-directly list includes the Earl Haig Retirement Residence at 1800 Austin Ave — all outside this sub-area. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

**Q: Where is the hospital, and where would care be?**
A: Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way in Port Moody, is the Fraser Health hospital on the Port Moody / Coquitlam border and the nearest hospital for this area. The nearest care cluster is on Dufferin Street in Central Coquitlam (Parkwood Manor retirement residence, 1142 Dufferin St; Dufferin Care Centre long-term care, 1131 Dufferin St) — outside the sub-area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital and https://www.retirementconcepts.com/locations/dufferin-care-centre/

**Q: Do creek or ravine rules affect Chines-slope lots?**
A: They can — a Watercourse Protection Development Permit can be required within 30 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam, and Zoning Bylaw ss.519/523 set flood and riparian setbacks. Check the specific lot early. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits

**Q: When were Coquitlam property taxes due in 2026?**
A: July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
