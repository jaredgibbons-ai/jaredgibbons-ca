# South Meadows (Pitt Meadows) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- South Meadows is the Paragon sub-area name for the south end of the City of Pitt Meadows. It is not an official City neighbourhood name — the City describes this part of the city as "South Bonson", the Bonson Road corridor south of Hammond Road and the CPKC rail tracks to the Fraser River, including the South Bonson Community Centre at Osprey Village (10932 Barnston View Road). No official boundary is published for the MLS name; rough working limits are Hammond Road and the rail line to the north, the Fraser River to the south, the Bonson Road corridor in the middle and the Golden Ears Business Park lands toward Harris Road to the west — exact east and west limits are UNVERIFIED. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/council-supports-business-park-land-use and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- Main streets: Bonson Road, Hammond Road, Harris Road, Fraser Way and Barnston View Road. Osprey Village, at Fraser Way and Barnston View Road, is the area's commercial pocket, with the South Bonson Community Centre and the Pitt Meadows Art Gallery at 10932 Barnston View Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- Parks: Bonson Park (11546 Bonson Road, disc golf), Pitt Meadows Athletic Park (11431 Bonson Road), the Pitt Meadows Arena complex, Shoreline Park (51 Bonson Road) on the Fraser River, and dyke-top trails along the Fraser River. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- Schools: School District 42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows) has no middle-school tier — elementary is K–7 and secondary is 8–12, with address-based catchments. No public school building sits inside this sub-area; for the sample address 10932 Barnston View Road (South Bonson Community Centre), the SD42 School Locator result is Davie Jones Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary (19438 116B Avenue, the city's only public secondary school). https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/ and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042
- Transit: routes 719 and 722 run as a combined circular service through Bonson Road and Fraser Way (719: Meadowtown to Fraser Way via Maple Meadows Station, Hammond Road and Bonson Road, continuing as 722); route 701 runs along Hammond Road at the north edge to Coquitlam Central Station and Haney Place; the West Coast Express runs weekday trains from Pitt Meadows Station, north of the sub-area — no station is inside it. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt719.pdf
- Where the seniors housing actually is: not in South Meadows. Pitt Meadows' one registered seniors residence, The Wesbrooke, is at 12000 190A Street in the city's north end by Lougheed Highway, and the city's BC Housing directory entries are north-side co-operatives — nothing of any seniors type was verified south of the rail tracks (Section 2).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — this covers the Osprey Village / Nature's Walk townhome stratas as much as any condo. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011.** Houses in the sub-area sit under RS Large Lot Residential, R-3, R-4 and R-6 Urban Residential zones — RS minimum lot area 0.2 ha, with residential buildings sited at least 15.0 m from watercourses and key or constructed ditches; R-3 allows up to 3 units per lot, R-4 up to 4, and R-6 up to five (six where at least one unit meets the BC Building Code adaptable housing requirements on lots close to frequent transit). RM-1 (townhouse) minimum lot area 0.4 ha; RM-2 (townhouse and low-density apartment) 3,035 sq m. The 1,215 sq m figure in the bylaw is a subdivision minimum only and does not apply to development on existing lots, per the City's SSMUH implementation update. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 - Consolidated Version_0.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **Suites:** one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit, 33 to 90 sq m, inside the same building, where the zone permits it; garden suites are one per lot at 33 to 90 sq m in the zones listed in the City's garden suite guide, including RS — and a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **SSMUH:** under the provincial rules applied citywide (SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986, adopted June 25, 2024; OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3034, 2025), lots 280 sq m or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 sq m up to 4 units, and lots over 280 sq m within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units. None of this infill is age-restricted. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **OCP: "I See Pitt Meadows 2040", Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020** (adopted September 27, 2022) covers the whole city including South Bonson; it repealed the former OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- **Flood / dikes:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the floodplain for the Fraser River and, to a smaller degree, the Pitt River — this current figure governs (an older City release, from the superseded OCP era, stated approximately 86%: SOURCE CONFLICT, resolved in favour of the 2020 Business Plan). The city is almost entirely protected by 60 km of dikes, plus ditches, pump stations and flood boxes; this sub-area sits behind the Fraser River dyke in drainage Area 3 (Kennedy, including McKechnie) of the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Property taxes and the drainage levy:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 (adopted May 5, 2026), with a 5% penalty added after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026. The same bylaw sets a separate residential (Class 1) drainage levy of $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value, alongside the general municipal rate of $2.8438 per $1,000. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing's Housing Registry** for this area (Zone 4 directory, May 2025). https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in South Meadows?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata, no 55+ rental and no seniors residence south of the rail tracks — the housing is detached houses and family townhomes/duplexes around Osprey Village. A 55+ buyer here is buying a regular home with no age bylaw. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is The Wesbrooke in South Meadows?** No. The Wesbrooke Seniors Living Community is at 12000 190A Street in the north end of Pitt Meadows — it is the city's registered assisted-living residence and an all-inclusive rental, so it is neither in this sub-area nor something that can be bought. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=pique%20newsmagazine&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc&page=1 and http://www.thewesbrooke.com/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a townhome in a strata here?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals, and in a true 55+ building (none verified here) the tenant would still have to satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I add a suite or garden suite for a parent or caregiver?** That is the realistic ageing-in-place route here: one secondary suite (33–90 sq m, inside the same building) or one garden suite per lot where the zone permits — never both on the same lot — and SSMUH unit counts of 3, 4 or 6 turn on the 280 sq m lot-size threshold and the 400 m frequent-transit test. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Pitt Meadows has no hospital. Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge, is a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency care serving Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 121-11900 Haney Place, Maple Ridge (Haney Place Mall). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/F/C/urgent-primary-care-centre---ridge-meadows
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** The 719/722 circular bus service loops through Bonson Road and Fraser Way to Meadowtown, Maple Meadows Station and Pitt Meadows Station; route 701 runs along Hammond Road at the north edge. The West Coast Express from Pitt Meadows Station is a weekday commuter service — there is no SkyTrain and no station inside the sub-area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt719.pdf
- **Q: Is South Meadows in a floodplain?** Effectively the whole city is: the City's current figure is that over 95% of Pitt Meadows lies within the Fraser/Pitt River floodplain, protected by 60 km of dikes. This sub-area sits behind the Fraser River dyke in drainage Area 3, and Shoreline Park and the dyke-top trails run on the dike itself — confirm floodplain, dike and drainage details, including the 15.0 m RS siting setback from watercourses and ditches, for the specific lot with the City. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Pitt Meadows?** Only at one step, and outside this sub-area: The Wesbrooke (12000 190A St, north end) covers independent rental suites plus a registered assisted-living component. There is no long-term care home in Pitt Meadows — that step means Maple Ridge or further. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=pique%20newsmagazine&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&name=&address=&order=alr_title&sort=desc&page=1

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The sub-area has two names and the City only uses one of them.** "South Meadows" is a Paragon/MLS name; every City document — plans, permits, the community centre — calls the area South Bonson. A City-side search under only "South Meadows" will miss the material. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/council-supports-business-park-land-use
- **The 45% detached sales ratio is small numbers, not a signal.** South Meadows detached posted a 45% sales ratio in September 2026 on 11 listings and 5 solds — one or two sales swing it. Price from comparable sales and the Pitt Meadows community median, not the ratio. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **There is a drainage levy on top of the tax rate.** Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 charges the 2026 residential drainage levy at $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value as its own line, alongside the $2.8438 general municipal rate — and Pitt Meadows' second 5% tax penalty lands August 1, 2026, a month earlier than most neighbouring cities' second step. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- **The flood figure was corrected upward — use the new one.** Older City material (superseded OCP era) put about 86% of the city in the floodplain; the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95%. Anything quoting 86% is quoting the old document. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **The dike is the area's front yard.** Shoreline Park (51 Bonson Road) and the dyke-top trails sit on the Fraser River dike itself, and the Harris Landing / Pitt River Regional Greenway's first phase runs 10.2 km along the Fraser and Pitt River dikes west of the sub-area — the flood infrastructure is also the recreation network. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/harris-landing-pitt-river-greenway
- **The 1,215 sq m number is not a building rule.** It appears in Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 as a subdivision minimum for R-3/R-4/R-6 lots; the City's SSMUH implementation update clarifies it does not apply to development on existing lots — sellers quoting it as a limit on suites or extra units are misreading the bylaw. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **Most of the city is farmland; this pocket is the exception.** The City's 2020 Business Plan states 78% of Pitt Meadows land falls within the Agricultural Land Reserve — the urban South Bonson pocket is developed residential/village land, and ALR status at its edges is a per-address check on Agricultural Land Commission maps. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
