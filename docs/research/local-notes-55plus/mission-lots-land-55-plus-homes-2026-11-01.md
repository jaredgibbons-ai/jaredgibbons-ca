## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. There is no strata on a vacant lot for this to attach to — it is the rule a buyer meets in Cherry Lane and Luther Place, and the only age rule a future strata on a Mission lot could lawfully adopt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Current plan (City parcels): Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026.** It replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025, keeping the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction with a Regional Context Statement added. Live pages stating a different adoption date for this bylaw are not followed here. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan
- **Zoning (City parcels): Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020** is Mission's zoning bylaw; rural zones such as RU16 carry a 1.6 ha minimum lot size in City notices. The specific zone, subdivision requirements, servicing standards and approval steps for any individual parcel: UNVERIFIED in the research file — confirm with the approving authority for the PID. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/notice-rezoning-bylaws-june-1-2026
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (City parcels):** the City's SSMUH page states zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted — SSMUH Bylaw 6299-2024-5949(163), adopted July 17, 2024 — and City planning pages set out how secondary suites and detached accessory units are handled by zone. https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **Secondary suites (City parcels):** a Mission secondary suite is a self-contained space inside a single family home; suites are not coach homes or garden cottages, are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes, must be registered with the City, new suites go through building permit, and an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus utility fees applies. Feed claims such as a UC372 suite claim or an RR7s coach/garden-home claim are listing text only: UNVERIFIED against the bylaw. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program and https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-lots-land.html
- **ALR gates a rural lot twice:** subdivision of Agricultural Land Reserve land requires an application under the Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALC approval, and a lease of part of an ALR parcel for more than 3 years is itself a subdivision requiring the Commission's permission; the housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), with a secondary suite only if entirely contained within the principal residence and local bylaws permit suites. Whether a specific parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level — check the ALC maps for the PID. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Lougheed Highway Corridor Plan: in preparation, NOT adopted.** Corridor parcels on Lougheed Highway — including the Hatzic park ground in Section 2 — are not governed by an adopted corridor plan; do not treat corridor-plan language in listing or draft material as in-force policy. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies
- **Property taxes — two regimes on one feed.** City of Mission parcels: 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026; Mission has NO alternative-scheme bylaw under Community Charter s.235, and the flat 10% rests on Community Charter ss.233–234 and Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s.3. The City also levies additional taxes for diking. FVRD parcels on this feed (Lake Errock, Dewdney Deroche, Durieu): rural taxes are paid to the Province through the Surveyor of Taxes Office, due July 2, with 5% on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31 under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11 — that rural 5% + 5% form does NOT apply inside the city. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **No land statistic exists in the permitted source.** SnapStats splits product only as Detached vs Condos & Townhomes, publishes medians at community level only, and publishes no land-only split — Mission community context for September 2026 was Detached 231 active / 28 sold and Condos & Townhomes 122 active / 10 sold. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a 55+ lot in Mission?** No product by that name was verified. Vacant land carries no age restriction in any source checked; 55+ is a residency rule that exists only inside a built strata (Cherry Lane, Luther Place), a life lease (Cedar Valley Manor), a co-op (St. Andrew's Place) or a park rule (Mission Mobile Home Park). A buyer who wants to build an age-restricted project on a lot would be creating a new strata — its only lawful age bylaw would be 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually BUY in Mission instead of building?** Two verified age-restricted strata complexes: Cherry Lane (32165 7th Ave, 20 townhouses, 1989, NWS2971) and Luther Place (7525 Martin Place, NWS2729, 1988). Beyond strata, the buy-in products are a life lease at Cedar Valley Manor (8358 Cedar St — an occupancy interest, not ownership) and manufactured homes on rented pads at Mission Mobile Home Park (34519 Lougheed Hwy). Sources: the building entries in Section 2.
- **Q: Are all the lots on this page inside the City of Mission?** No. The first page alone spans City sub-areas and FVRD ground — Lake Errock (Area C), Dewdney Deroche (Area G) and Durieu (Area F) — which follow FVRD plans and rural taxes paid to the Province, alongside City lots that follow the City OCP and pay City taxes. Establish which government approves the build before anything else. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-lots-land.html and https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf
- **Q: Is there a median price for Mission lots?** Not in the permitted source. SnapStats publishes no land-only split and no lot median; its Mission figures cover dwellings only. Price a lot against comparable lot sales for its own sub-area and servicing state, not against a dwelling median. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Mission-area acreage for family lots?** It depends on the governing authority and on ALR status: City parcels follow the City's process, FVRD parcels the regional district's, and ALR subdivision needs ALC approval — with even a lease of part of an ALR parcel for more than 3 years counting as a subdivision. "Subdividable" in listing text clears neither gate. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: What can I build on ALR land, including a home for a caregiver or family member?** One principal residence at most 500 m², a secondary suite only if entirely inside that principal residence and local bylaws allow suites, and at most one additional detached residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). Check the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps by PID first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can I put a suite or coach house on the home I build on a city lot?** An in-house secondary suite can be registered with the City (building permit for a new suite; $63 annual suite fee plus utility fees; suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes). A detached coach home or garden cottage is a separate detached accessory dwelling handled by zone, and the City's SSMUH framework (Bylaw 6299-2024-5949(163), adopted July 17, 2024) governs small-scale multi-unit forms on former single-family/duplex zones. A feed card's zoning claim for a specific lot is UNVERIFIED until checked against the bylaw. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program and https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **Q: Who do I pay tax to on a vacant lot, and what is the penalty if I am late?** The government the lot sits in. City lots: pay the City — due July 2, 2026, flat 10% penalty on outstanding current balances on July 3, 2026, plus diking levies where they apply. FVRD lots: pay the Province — due July 2, 5% on amounts unpaid July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid October 31. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Q: Is a "lot" on this feed always fee-simple vacant land?** No. The first page includes a resort use-area at Hatzic Lake listed as Land and a Bergen Road parcel with a large shop already on it. Confirm the tenure line and exactly what transfers before valuing a card as vacant land. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-lots-land.html
- **Q: How do I price the servicing gap between lots?** Feed cards range from fully serviced city lots to raw acreage described with a drilled well and approved septic — all listing text for that parcel only. Parcel-level servicing status, connection costs and development cost charges: UNVERIFIED in the research file. Confirm water, septic approval, connection costs and DCCs for the specific PID in writing before comparing a raw parcel to a serviced lot. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-lots-land.html

- **One feed, two tax penalties.** A City lot missed by one day after July 2, 2026 attracts the full flat 10%; an FVRD lot on the same feed attracts 5% after July 2 and a further 5% after October 31. The address's municipality — not its "Mission" mailing label — decides which. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
