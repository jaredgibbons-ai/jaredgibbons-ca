## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Township SSMUH — the settled rule this page must be read against: Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024)** amended Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 to allow small-scale multi-unit housing on eligible lots (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area, lot not larger than 4,050 m²) — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger eligible lots, lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and NO 6-unit tier currently applies in the Township. The development guide's 6-plex-for-transit-adjacent-lots framing does not match the bylaw. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Community plan:** the Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan covers Brookswood-Fernridge, replacing an earlier community plan, with Booth, Fernridge and Rinn Neighbourhood Plans under it; the Township states the plan allows previously undeveloped areas to grow while protecting trees and watercourses. The development guide's further claim that the Township designated Brookswood-Fernridge as its "next major area of growth after Willoughby" is UNVERIFIED — no Township source opened in this program states that designation. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/brookswoodfernridge-community-plan-adopted.aspx and https://jaredgibbons.ca/brookswood-real-estate-development-guide.html
- **Plan-area claims made only on the live page (site copy only):** that 27% of Brookswood-Fernridge is now designated environmentally protected — UNVERIFIED; that Booth requires a 12-acre school-and-park site dedication before broader development proceeds — UNVERIFIED; that Fernridge includes areas designated at 10,000 sq ft lots and that CAC rates were reduced 10–15% in the 2024 revisions — UNVERIFIED; and that a Township Engineering Capacity Study for water and sewer is underway — UNVERIFIED. https://jaredgibbons.ca/brookswood-real-estate-development-guide.html
- **Township zoning: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500** governs Brookswood. Its Section 110.1 zone ladder, as read out in Township council reports, includes SR-1 (minimum lot area 3,716 m², frontage 40 m), SR-2 (8,094 m², 45 m) and SR-3 (1,765 m², 27.45 m); RU-2 minimum lot area and frontage are UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section; the Brookswood-Fernridge plan designates Cedar Creek's site as Manufactured Home Park. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf and https://www.tol.ca/en/news/brookswoodfernridge-community-plan-adopted.aspx
- **A park's 55+ rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw — and SSMUH does not rewrite it.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003) expressly recognises a park where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (per Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i)) and allows a park landlord to refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement. Nothing in Bylaw No. 6020 changes a park's age rule, pad tenancy or Manufactured Home Park plan designation — get the park's rules in writing. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **The governing law for a rented pad is provincial.** Manufactured home site tenancies are governed by BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) wherever the park sits; the tenant owns the manufactured home and rents the site under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **The 2026 pad-rent increase limit: 2.3% plus a proportional amount** (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of sites in the park); the 2027 limit is 2.2% plus the proportional amount. Increases require 3 full months' written notice and can occur only once every 12 months. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Buying a park home does not reset the pad rent (MHPTA s. 28 assignment), and park closure follows MHPTA s. 42:** on assignment the pad rent stays the same with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given) and no consent fee; on conversion to another use the landlord must have all permits in place, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date. Development pressure around a park does not shorten either protection. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **For comparison — the strata rules, which do NOT govern Brookswood's parks:** under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, only a 55+ age bylaw is valid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and since May 1, 2023 also a younger spouse/partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and strata rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **ALR / second dwellings:** the Agricultural Land Commission's housing rules permit one principal residence and, subject to conditions in the ALR Use Regulation, one additional residence, limited to 90 m² or less on parcels 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha. Township zoning for a second dwelling or coach house on non-reserve Brookswood acreage is UNVERIFIED. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Wells and septic (acreage lots):** the Province's GWELLS application is the registry for recorded wells and aquifers; older wells may not appear in it. Fraser Health states authorized persons must file a record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair and a letter of certification after construction — servicing decides what a lot can actually support, whatever a development guide says about unit counts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons
- **Property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026, with a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026, and utility fees are now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **GST rebates on a NEW home (the only place the development frame meets a tax saving):** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST rebate under Bill C-4 (royal assent Mar 13, 2026) rebates 100% of the GST on a newly constructed or substantially renovated home valued up to $1 million, phasing out between $1 million and $1.5 million for a maximum of $50,000, for agreements in the Mar 20, 2025–2031 window; the older GST/HST new housing rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. Home Buyers' Plan and First Home Savings Account dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Brookswood?** Manufactured homes in three verified 55+ parks: Cedar Creek Estates (3031 200 St), Fernridge Park (20071 24 Ave) and Pineridge (2270 196 St). You buy the home and rent the pad under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement — there is no verified 55+ strata complex in Brookswood, and this development page names no 55+ project. https://www.estateblock.com/langley/5-3031-200-st-1968212 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3109616-33-20071-24-avenue-langley-british-columbia-v2z2a1-99000/brochure
- **Q: Can I build a fourplex — or a sixplex near transit — on a Brookswood lot, as the development guide suggests?** A fourplex, possibly: under Bylaw No. 6020 an eligible lot (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area, 4,050 m² or smaller) permits 4 units if it is over 280 m² and 3 units at 280 m² or smaller. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **This development page has no feed — its claims are the product, so they carry the risk.** The page frames every Brookswood property as having "three values" — as a home, as a building lot, and as part of an assembly — and states the three do not always rank in that order, with creek greenway or SPEA setbacks able to make a property worth more as a home than as a lot (site copy). That framing is the page's own content, not a verified valuation method. https://jaredgibbons.ca/brookswood-real-estate-development-guide.html
- **The page promises a tier the bylaw does not contain.** Its repeated 6-plex-for-transit-adjacent-lots framing conflicts with Bylaw No. 6020 in two ways at once: there is no 6-unit tier in the Township, and Transit Oriented Area lots are excluded from SSMUH eligibility. A buyer who pays a 6-plex premium on a Brookswood lot has paid for a right that does not exist in the bylaw as settled. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx and https://jaredgibbons.ca/brookswood-real-estate-development-guide.html
- **Every verified 55+ home in Brookswood is a manufactured home on a rented pad — development value does not accrue to the pad tenant.** Brookswood holds all three of Langley's verified 55+ pad-rental parks (Cedar Creek Estates, Fernridge Park, Pineridge) and no 55+ strata at all; the fourth Langley 55+ manufactured-home community, Pioneer Park in Aldergrove, is a co-operative. If Brookswood land values rise on rezoning, the uplift belongs to landowners — a park home's owner holds the home and a tenancy, not the lot. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The page's two named projects are leads to verify with the Township, not approvals to price against.** Neither the 60-unit SSMUH project (December 2025, $1.17M CAC, per the page) nor the 37-lot subdivision on the 19800-block of 32 Avenue (February 2026, per the page) is confirmed on a Township or developer source in this program — both are UNVERIFIED, and neither is a seniors project in the page's own copy. https://jaredgibbons.ca/brookswood-real-estate-development-guide.html
- **A park's age rule can lawfully stop a sale, even in a growth area.** Under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation, a landlord in a 55+ park may refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the age requirement — a refusal power with no equivalent in a strata sale. Build the park's written consent into the timeline. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **The CSA label is a hard gate, not a detail.** A manufactured home without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 mobile, A-277 modular) cannot be sold until Technical Safety BC re-certification — label status is a price and timeline fact on every Brookswood park purchase, and no rezoning changes it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **The verified board shape behind the page's "buyer's market" framing is narrower than the page states.** September 2026 Brookswood: 98 detached listings, 7 sales, 7% sales ratio (the lowest published detached ratio in Langley), against 2 attached listings and no attached sales — a detached, slow-moving shelf, with no published Brookswood median to support the page's $2.35M figure (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report, via ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/brookswood-real-estate-development-guide.md).
