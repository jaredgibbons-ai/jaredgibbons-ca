# East Delta — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

The City plans Delta as three communities — Ladner, Tsawwassen (together South Delta) and North Delta — and publishes no boundary for "East Delta." The name is a Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name for the rural farm belt and the East Ladner urban edge east of the Ladner village core. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219618/

- Fraser Health describes this ground only as farmland, noting the Good Samaritan Delta View Care Centre is located in the farmlands of East Delta. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/your-community/delta/locations-in-delta
- Highway 17A, Ladner Trunk Road and Highway 99 through the George Massey Tunnel are the main road links; TransLink route 601 runs via Ladner Exchange, Ladner Trunk Road, Highway 17A and Highway 99, and route 620 runs via Ladner Exchange, Highway 17A and Highway 17. A bus route serving the interior farm roads: UNCONFIRMED. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- The Boundary Bay Dyke Trail runs from 17A Avenue to Mud Bay in Boundary Bay Regional Park, on the south side of the Delta farm area. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park

No sample East Delta address was run in the base research, so no catchment result is stated — UNVERIFIED for any specific farm address. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, is Fraser Health's community hospital for Delta, next to Ladner Exchange. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- Ladner Pioneer Library, 4683 51 Street (Fraser Valley Regional Library), serves the area; no City park, recreation centre or shopping centre inside East Delta itself was confirmed on an official source. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). In East Delta this currently has nothing to attach to — no strata here carries a verified age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents in place when a 55+ bylaw passed; live-in caregivers; a younger spouse or partner; children under 19 in the specified resident's care; and adult children cared for before 19 (expanded May 1, 2023). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rental bylaws are still allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600.** In the A1 Agriculture zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision requires OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, and the minimum lot size created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception. The A1 zone provides one farm house per lot (maximum 500 m²), a farm home plate maximum of 3,600 m², one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit of maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on larger lots. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **ALR housing caps (Agricultural Land Commission):** generally no more than one residence per ALR parcel; a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m²; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence if local bylaws allow it; one additional detached residence is permitted up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); anything beyond needs ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future"** — Delta's current consolidated OCP; its Agriculture policies (s. 2.6) apply to this farm belt, and the former East Ladner Area Plan (Schedule B.1, Bylaw No. 3950) was consolidated away in 2024. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Secondary suites in Delta:** Delta legalized secondary suites in single-family dwellings in 2010, and Bylaw No. 8242 (2023) amended Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 to set a secondary suite's floor area at less than 50% of the gross floor area of the single detached dwelling (excluding an attached garage). On A1 farm land the suite sits inside the farm house (see the A1 rule above). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/218413/
- **Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (BC):** if a manufactured-home park tenancy ever arises in Delta, site tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, SBC 2002, c. 77 — the tenant typically owns the home and rents the site, and park rules are set under s. 32 of that Act. No Delta park was verified in this research, so this is the governing statute only, not evidence of a local park. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/02077_01
- **Wells and septic:** a property's well is searched in the provincial GWELLS registry (wells built before the Water Sustainability Act may not appear); Fraser Health requires a building with indoor plumbing not connected to a community sewer to use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Property taxes:** Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026; BC's regular property tax deferment program is open to homeowners 55 or older on a principal residence. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ home I can buy in East Delta?** No verified stock. No strata, townhome complex or manufactured-home park in this sub-area has a source stating a 55+ bylaw or age rule. Ladner's verified 55+ purchases are Delta Green, Laburnum Park Place and South Pointe, all west of the farm belt. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Delta View a place I can buy into or rent as a senior?** No. Good Samaritan Delta View is a 292-bed long-term care campus (212-bed Life Enrichment Centre plus 80-bed Habilitation Centre) on Burns Drive — publicly subsidized care beds accessed through Fraser Health, not strata ownership and not market rental. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/2/A/good-samaritan-delta-view-care-centre---residential-care
- **Q: Can a farm parcel here have a second dwelling for family or a caregiver?** Within tight caps: the ALC allows one principal residence (500 m² cap without an application) plus, in some cases, one additional detached residence up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (larger parcels), and Delta's A1 zone mirrors those accessory farm dwelling limits; a secondary suite is allowed inside the farm house. Anything beyond needs ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I subdivide a farm lot for a retirement lot?** Generally no: a new A1 lot created by subdivision needs a minimum of 8 ha, OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, with only a home site severance exception under ALC policy. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If we buy in a Ladner 55+ building, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); caregivers, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt in defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How do older owners age in place on an East Delta farm?** Through the farm house itself: a secondary suite inside the farm house and one accessory farm dwelling unit within the A1/ALC size caps are the local routes for a caregiver or family member — subject to the home plate maximum of 3,600 m². https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **Q: What about water, septic and flood on a farm purchase?** Search the well in GWELLS (older wells may be unrecorded) and ask for the Authorized Person's sewerage filings; Delta's low farm land sits behind dikes — the City is raising sections of the Boundary Bay dike between 72 Street and 88 Street — and flood requirements are lot-specific. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems and https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-receives-provincial-grant-boundary-bay-dike
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, beside Ladner Exchange — Fraser Health's community hospital for Delta with 24/7 emergency care. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **Q: Can I defer property taxes at 55+ on an East Delta farm home?** Yes, if it is your principal residence — BC's regular deferment program opens at 55 or older during the current year. Delta's 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The sub-area name itself is the first trap.** "East Delta" exists only as a Paragon sub-area label; the City has no East Delta neighbourhood, plan or boundary — older documents citing the "East Ladner Area Plan" are citing Schedule B.1 of the repealed OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, consolidated away by Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). Price and market any property here as what it legally is: an ALR farm parcel or an urban East Ladner lot. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219618/
- **The only seniors-named institution in the farmlands is a care campus, and it is large.** Delta View's 292 beds (212 Life Enrichment + 80 Habilitation, 212 publicly subsidized) make it one of Delta's biggest care sites — and it offers nothing to buy or rent on the open market. Buyers who find "Delta View" in a seniors search are finding a Fraser Health care address, not housing stock. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/2/A/good-samaritan-delta-view-care-centre---residential-care
- **North Delta statistics must never be used for this area.** SnapStats covers North Delta only; South Delta (Ladner / East Delta) has no published sub-area or community figures in the reports held for this program, so every market number for East Delta is UNVERIFIED. Source: the area research file, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/east-delta.md, Section 2.
- **The home plate, not the house, is the farm constraint buyers miss.** Delta's A1 farm home plate maximum is 3,600 m² — it caps the whole residential footprint (house plus accessory buildings), which is what limits a "big retirement build plus suite plus shop" plan on a farm lot. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **The urban edge and the farm belt obey different rulebooks on the same street.** Parcels on the East Ladner edge take urban RS/RD zones parcel by parcel under Bylaw No. 7600, while the A1 farm parcels beside them carry the 8 ha subdivision minimum and ALC housing caps — two adjoining listings can have opposite development potential. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **No bus serves the interior farm roads on any verified source.** TransLink's Ladner routes (601, 620) run the Ladner Trunk Road / Highway 17A spine; a route with stops on the interior farm roads is UNVERIFIED — a car-free 55+ buyer should treat East Delta as car-dependent until a specific address proves otherwise. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
