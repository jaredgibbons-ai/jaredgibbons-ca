# Fort Langley (Township of Langley) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Fort Langley is a village in the Township of Langley, on the south bank of the Fraser River; the Township describes it as known as the birthplace of BC and publishes a Fort Langley Community Plan for it (Bylaw No. 2527, adopted October 26, 1987, as an amendment to the Township Official Community Plan). https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/fort-langley-community-projects.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- The commercial core is on Glover Road; River Road, 88 Avenue, 96 Avenue, 240 Street and the Salmon River / Bedford Channel side frame the wider village area. Exact board sub-area boundary roads are UNVERIFIED. https://www.pc.gc.ca/apps/dfhd/page_nhs_eng.aspx?id=66
- The 55+ stock sits in one pocket: Bedford Landing, on Billy Brown Road on the Bedford Channel / Fraser River side of the village, north of the Glover Road core — the Waterfront building at 23215 Billy Brown Rd is the 55+ building; its immediate neighbour, the Village building at 23255/23285 Billy Brown Rd, is not (see Section 2). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- The village's care home is also in the core: Fort Langley Seniors Community, 8838 Glover Road, on the main street south of the historic site. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/B/0/fort-langley-care-centre
- Schools physically in the village: Fort Langley Elementary, 8877 Bartlett Street, and Langley Fine Arts School, 9096 Trattle Street (a district K–12 fine arts choice school). For the sample address 8877 Bartlett Street, the SD35 School Locator returns Fort Langley Elementary, no middle school shown, and D.W. Poppy Secondary. https://fortlangley.sd35.bc.ca/ and https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- Library and museum are both in salishan Place by the River, 23430 Mavis Avenue — the Township's arts, culture and heritage facility, home to the Fort Langley Community Library and the museum. https://www.tol.ca/en/centre-information.aspx
- The village grocery is Lee's Market, 23320 Mavis Avenue; Glover Road holds the village shops. There is no shopping centre in Fort Langley. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/garbage-cart.aspx
- Transit: TransLink route 562 runs from Langley Centre via Glover Road, 96 Avenue, Trattle Street, 88 Avenue, Walnut Grove Drive, 212 Street, 204 Street and 202 Street to Carvolth Exchange, serving Glover Road in the village. There is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in Fort Langley. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf
- Health: no hospital in the village; Langley Memorial Hospital, 22051 Fraser Highway, Langley, is the Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency, south of the village, and the Langley UPCC is at 202 - 20434 64th Avenue, Langley. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- **Waterfront at Bedford Landing — 23215 Billy Brown Road (Bedford Landing).** The one verified age-restricted strata ownership building in Fort Langley. Freehold strata, plan BCS3620, 4 storeys, 78 units, built 2009, zoning CD-55. Listings for the building state "Ages 55+" / "55+ age restriction," and one listing states the form of the rule as "one occupant needing to be 55+ in age." Building sources record age restrictions, pets allowed with restrictions (cats and dogs OK, number limit two), and rentals recorded inconsistently between sources — one building source records "rentals not allowed" while an MLS data sheet records "Rentals Allwd w/Restrctns" — confirm the current rental position in the strata bylaws and Form B, not from a listing. Amenities recorded in listings: bike room, clubhouse/amenity room, exercise centre, secure underground parking (listings commonly include 2 stalls) and a storage locker. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/buildings/langley/fort-langley and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/waterfront-at-bedford-landing-23215-billy-brown-road and https://www.rew.ca/properties/416-23215-billy-brown-road-langley-bc and https://rennie.com/listings/billy-brown-23215-road-waterfront-at-bedford-landing-407-langley and https://www.cotala.com/tours/85164/FullPublic.pdf?i=603115479

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- None in Fort Langley — no purpose-built 55+ rental building with a Fort Langley address appears in any source checked, including BC Housing's seniors directories. The Township's own Canada Community Building Fund reporting notes the Township's affordable stock is shared with the City of Langley and that close to 700 of those units serve low-income or frail seniors — those units are in Langley City / the urban Langley core, not in the Fort Langley village. https://www.ubcm.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Langley%20Township.pdf

Care only — you cannot buy in. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/B/0/fort-langley-care-centre and https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/85092/ and https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/find-a-location/british-columbia/lower-mainland/fort-langley/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. (This is why the conflicting "rentals not allowed / rentals allowed with restrictions" lines in Waterfront at Bedford Landing listing data must be resolved from the registered bylaws: a long-term rental ban cannot stand, but the building's exact current position is a documents question.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Township zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 2500** governs the village; the Waterfront at Bedford Landing building sits in a site-specific CD-55 comprehensive development zone (the zone number is a coincidence — it is not an age designation). https://www.cotala.com/tours/85164/FullPublic.pdf?i=603115479
- **Village plan: the Fort Langley Community Plan, Bylaw No. 2527 (1987),** is the governing plan; its consolidated text (updated by Bylaw 6200, the 2025 OCP Update, adopted December 1, 2025) states the existing single-family residential area will continue to develop at the permitted minimum lot size of 930 m² where vacant and subdividable land is available, and that densities should not increase — the plan context behind why the village has exactly one purpose-built 55+ building. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Community-Plan-Bylaws/Fort-Langley-Community-Plan-(No.-2527).pdf
- **Heritage Conservation Area:** the Community Plan designates a Heritage Conservation Area under the Local Government Act covering the downtown core (added by Bylaw 3710, October 27, 1997); new development and alterations must complement and enhance the area's heritage character, and work that is not exempt requires approval under the heritage rules — exemptions include minor additions under 50 m² that do not front a road (other than a lane) and conform to the guidelines. The village is also covered by a Development Permit Area (Bylaw 3204, 1993) and the Fort Langley Design Guidelines (Bylaw 5063, 2014). https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **Township housing policy context:** municipal housing policy now has to plan for the seven classes of housing needs in the Housing Needs Report, which include seniors' housing specifically; the Township exempts certain development fees for new construction of non-market rental units, but it does not provide a rent supplement or other assistance program. No Fort Langley-specific seniors-housing project was verified under these policies. https://www.ubcm.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Langley%20Township.pdf
- **Flood:** Fort Langley is a low-lying Fraser River / Salmon River community — the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station provides flood protection for the Fort Langley community, and its flood box gates close when Fraser River levels rise. Parcel-level floodplain status: check with the Township for the specific lot. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **ALR edges:** farm land on the village edges is in the ALR in places; on ALR land the ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its rules are met — up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties more than 40 ha — subject to Township bylaws, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Fort Langley?** One building: Waterfront at Bedford Landing, 23215 Billy Brown Rd — a 78-unit, 4-storey freehold strata condo building (plan BCS3620), built 2009, with a 55+ age restriction. Everything else strata in the village (the Village at Bedford Landing next door, Lily Terrace, Leah townhomes) carries no verified age restriction. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/waterfront-at-bedford-landing-23215-billy-brown-road and https://www.tmbrealestate.com/310-23285-billy-brown-rd
- **Q: Is the whole Bedford Landing development 55+?** No — only the Waterfront building (23215 Billy Brown Rd). The Village at Bedford Landing (23255/23285 Billy Brown Rd, plan EPS998) states "No age restrictions" in its bylaws summary, with rentals allowed and 2 pets allowed with restrictions. The two buildings share a street and a name; check the civic address, not the marketing name. https://www.tmbrealestate.com/310-23285-billy-brown-rd
- **Q: How is the 55+ rule worded at Waterfront?** Listing data records "Restricted Age: 55+" and one listing states it as "one occupant needing to be 55+ in age" — which fits the provincial model, where the bylaw governs who may reside and a younger spouse/partner is exempt by regulation. Confirm the registered bylaw wording in the strata documents before relying on the listing phrasing. https://vancouvernashdom.com/langley-fort-langley-apartments/listing.r2965145-312-23215-billy-brown-road-langley-v1m-0b7.104197382/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a unit there as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit at Waterfront?** The listing record conflicts with itself — one building source says "rentals not allowed," an MLS data sheet says "Rentals Allwd w/Restrctns." The law underneath it is clear: long-term strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. Get the answer from the current bylaws and Form B, not a listing. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/waterfront-at-bedford-landing-23215-billy-brown-road and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Waterfront?** Building and MLS sources record pets allowed with restrictions — cats and dogs OK, number limit two. Confirm the size/other limits in the bylaws. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85164/FullPublic.pdf?i=603115479
- **Q: What parking and storage come with a unit?** Listings for the building commonly include 2 side-by-side underground parking stalls and a storage locker (in several listings, a locker on the same floor as the unit), plus a bike room, amenity/clubhouse room and exercise centre in the building. Confirm the specific unit's stalls and locker on the Form B. https://rennie.com/listings/billy-brown-23215-road-waterfront-at-bedford-landing-407-langley and https://www.jagsidhu.com/listing/r3092466-23215-billy-brown-road-309-langley-bc-v1m-0b7/brochure
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in the village?** Partly: the village has a long-term care home — Fort Langley Seniors Community, 8838 Glover Rd (~96 beds, mostly publicly funded, accessed through Fraser Health) — but no verified assisted-living or independent-living retirement residence in the village; the registered assisted-living residences are elsewhere in Langley (e.g. Langley Seniors Village, 20363 65th Ave). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/B/0/fort-langley-care-centre and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=42&name=&address=&f%5B0%5D=ha%3AFraser%20Health&order=alr_residence_type&sort=desc&page=4
- **Q: Does the heritage area affect a Bedford Landing purchase?** The Heritage Conservation Area covers the downtown core, and the Community Plan's purpose for it is to protect the downtown's heritage character — a strata condo purchase inside Bedford Landing is governed by the strata's documents, but any buyer also looking at a village house should check whether that property is inside the HCA and what approvals exterior work needs. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The two Bedford Landing buildings are a trap for search filters:** "Bedford Landing" returns both the 55+ Waterfront building (23215 Billy Brown Rd, BCS3620, 2009) and the all-ages Village building (23255/23285 Billy Brown Rd, EPS998, 2011/2012). A listing that says only "Bedford Landing" has not told you which legal building — or which age rule — you are looking at. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/buildings/langley/fort-langley and https://www.tmbrealestate.com/310-23285-billy-brown-rd
- **The village's seniors care home and its seniors hall are two different Glover/88 Ave institutions:** Fort Langley Seniors Community (8838 Glover Rd) is a ~96-bed long-term care home opened in 2008, operated by Park Place Seniors Living; the Fort Langley Senior's Hall (23022 88 Ave) is a Lions community hall. Only one of them is housing, and it is care, not a purchase. https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/85092/ and https://fortlangleylionsseniorshall.com/
- **The Community Plan has carried a "Langley Lions Seniors Housing" line since 1998** (Bylaw 3826 map amendment in the Fort Langley Community Plan's amendment table) — but the operating Langley Lions seniors complex is in Langley City at 5464 203rd St. A plan-history mention is not a Fort Langley building. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Community-Plan-Bylaws/Fort-Langley-Community-Plan-(No.-2527).pdf and https://sci-bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Housing-Package-Lower-Mainland-2025-Spinal-Cord-Injury-BC-1.pdf
- **The village plan caps the supply side:** the consolidated Fort Langley Community Plan states the single-family area will continue at a 930 m² minimum lot size and that "densities should not increase," and its population estimate is 3,435 as of 2021 with growth considerably slower than the rest of the Township — the structural reason the 55+ supply is one 78-unit building from 2009 and nothing since. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Community-Plan-Bylaws/Fort-Langley-Community-Plan-(No.-2527).pdf
- **The newest village housing project is three townhomes, and it is not 55+:** The 1912 (Lanstone Homes) is a presale of three heritage-inspired townhomes plus a restored 1912 house, with pre-sales coming Winter 2027 and move-in Fall 2027 — heritage-form product at village scale, with no age restriction stated. https://www.the1912.ca/
- **Flood protection is mechanical here:** the Township's Salmon River Pump Station provides flood protection for the Fort Langley community and its flood box gates close when Fraser River levels rise; the station is mid-upgrade, with Council having authorized detailed design and tendering. Ask for the parcel's flood information from the Township before purchase. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Township taxes have two penalty steps:** Township of Langley property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026, with a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026, and utility fees are now billed separately from the tax notice. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
