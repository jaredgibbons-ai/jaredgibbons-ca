# Maple Ridge Farms — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Plain finding up front: the 55+ angle on Maple Ridge farm and ALR land is thin, and this file says so with sources. No 55+ strata, manufactured-home park, co-operative, rental or care complex was verified on Maple Ridge farm land or in its rural districts (Websters Corners, Whonnock, Thornhill, the northeast and northwest lowlands). Maple Ridge's verified 55+ stock is all in East Central, listed in Section 2 for contrast. What a 55+ farm buyer actually faces here is ALR housing law — house-size caps and a tightly limited second dwelling — not an age-restricted community.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **The strata age-bylaw rules, for the record — with no local strata to apply them to.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; age bylaws below 55 invalid since Nov 24, 2022, under Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term restrictions remain allowed. A farm buyer gets none of this machinery, because a farm purchase is freehold land, not a strata lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **ALR housing caps are the rules that actually bind a 55+ farm buyer.** Land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The second-dwelling rule is the ageing-in-place rule on a farm.** The ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, with a total floor area of 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and Maple Ridge's bylaws must also permit it. That is the legal route to a caregiver's or family member's dwelling on a Maple Ridge farm; anything larger needs an ALC application. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019** applies to all lands in the City (replacing Bylaw No. 3510-1985); the OCP is Bylaw No. 7060-2014. Urban houses sit under RS-1 (s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m²); the rural districts carry the bylaw's agricultural and rural residential zones — a specific rural zone's minimum parcel size from direct bylaw text was not confirmed in this pass: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/Zoning%20bylaw%20report.pdf and http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Farm classification is a continuing obligation, not a one-time label.** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income requirements at farm gate prices are $2,500 if the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha. A downsizing buyer who stops producing can lose the classification and its tax treatment. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Floodplain rules bite on the lowland belt.** The City's floodplain framework sets a flood construction level of 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard) along the river flats, with no habitable floor space below the flood construction level on affected lots and a prohibition on rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties (Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h)) — directly relevant to a lowland farm purchase and to any suite plan on it. Floodplain status is per-lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Water and septic are diligence items with legal teeth:** under the Water Sustainability Act, domestic groundwater use does not need a licence, but non-domestic use including irrigation requires a water licence, and well records are searched in the provincial GWELLS registry; a home not on community sewer must use an onsite system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, with only an Authorized Person designing, installing or inspecting it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Property taxes / deferment:** the Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities), and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in Metro Vancouver / Fraser Valley) is for seniors 65+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ farm community or seniors farm complex in Maple Ridge?** No — none was verified on any source. Farm parcels are freehold, strata farm product is not a Maple Ridge pattern, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. The city's 55+ complexes are urban strata and parks in East Central and Southwest Maple Ridge. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: I'm 55+ and buying a farm to downsize onto. What limits the house I can build?** On ALR land, a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. Confirm the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps before designing anything. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can my adult child or a caregiver live in a second dwelling on the farm?** The ALC permits one additional residence without an application where its size rules are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels, detached — and City bylaws must also permit it. There is no strata-style caregiver exemption here because there is no strata; the land rules are the whole answer. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: If I stop farming, do I keep farm-class taxes?** Not automatically: BC Assessment requires a producing farm to keep meeting minimum gross farm income thresholds that scale with the operation's size ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha). Plan the production, or lease the land to someone who produces, before assuming the classification survives. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Can I put a suite in the farmhouse for rental income?** Two separate gates: on ALR land a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence under the ALC rules; and the City's floodplain rule (Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h)) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties — a live issue on the northwest lowland belt. Check both before assuming a suite is legal on a specific parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q: Where is the hospital for a rural-district address?** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, in the urban core — a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency care serving Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- **Q: Can I defer my property taxes at 55+ on a farm property?** The Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). Farm classification is a separate BC Assessment matter and is not affected by deferment. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
- **Q: How much of Maple Ridge is actually farm-reserve land?** The BC Ministry of Agriculture's Agricultural Land Use Inventory records 3,787 hectares of ALR land in Maple Ridge — 15% of the city's total land area of 24,881 hectares. That inventory is a 2011 survey published in 2013; the current ALR total is UNVERIFIED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/farming-natural-resources-and-industry/agriculture-and-seafood/agricultural-land-and-environment/strengthening-farming/land-use-inventories/mapleridge2011_aluireport_complete.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The city's zoning bylaw is newer than most agents' references.** Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 replaced a zoning bylaw (No. 3510-1985) that had governed for over three decades, and it applies to all lands in the City — rural parcels included. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/Zoning%20bylaw%20report.pdf
- **The floodplain suite ban is the hidden trap in the lowland belt.** Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties, and the flood construction level along the river flats is 6.7 m geodetic including freeboard — a "suiteable farmhouse" claim on a lowland parcel needs both the ALC answer and the floodplain answer. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Maple Ridge's 55+ market and its farm market never touch.** The entire verified 55+ inventory — four strata buildings, two parks, a 60+ co-op, two life leases, five care/rental residences and three subsidized buildings — sits in the urban core (East Central, plus one park in Southwest). A buyer who wants both "farm" and "55+" is really choosing between two different purchases. Sources: Section 2 above and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/east-central-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
