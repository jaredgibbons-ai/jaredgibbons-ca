## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; exemptions attach to a "specified resident." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, all strata residential rental-restriction bylaws are invalid; stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals. Chilliwack regulates short-term rentals under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** sets what can be built as-of-right today. Its s. 2 caps a secondary suite at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling; s. 4.06(1) permits only 1 accessory dwelling unit per lot, garden suites cap at 100 m² gross floor area, and ADU parking is at s. 5.04. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH: Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395**, adopted June 18, 2024, amending Bylaw No. 5000 — the City's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing, per the consolidated bylaw record. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **OCP: Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500.** The live page's stated adoption date of December 16, 2025 is UNVERIFIED — the City's OCP page read states no adoption date. The live page's schedule labels are WRONG against the current OCP: Sardis is Schedule B5 (not "Schedule D"), Eastern Hillsides is Schedule B4 (not "Schedule C" — Schedule C is Mountain View Heritage Conservation Area), and there is no "DPA2 Hillside & Upland" — Schedule D lists D-2 Hillside Environment and D-5 Hillside Development separately. Do not copy the live page's labels into an offer. https://chilliwack.com/ocp and https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwacklanduseplans.html
- **ALR housing frame:** one principal residence at a maximum 500 m² total floor area, a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; subdivision and non-farm use inside the ALR require Agricultural Land Commission approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Floodplain:** the live page cites "Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519" and Flood Construction Level mechanics — UNVERIFIED in the research file, because no City floodplain bylaw or FCL table was opened in that pass. Confirm the current bylaw and the parcel's FCL with the City; separately, Bylaw No. 5000 provides no new suite is permitted below the flood construction level. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwacklanduseplans.html and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Per-parcel check route:** the City's OCP page points readers to the City Webmap alongside the OCP Maps — zoning, OCP designation, Schedule D permit areas and floodplain layers for a specific parcel are per-parcel Webmap checks: UNVERIFIED for any individual parcel in this file. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Property taxes:** notices are mailed at the end of May, due the first working day after July 1; a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law:** Cottonwood Village pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), which recognises a park reserved for tenants who have reached 55 and allows refusal of an assignment where the purchaser does not meet the age requirement. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Chilliwack?** Five verified strata — Ivy Green and Spadina Regency on Spadina Avenue, Mountainview Estates in Proper East, Knight Lodge and Cottage Grove on the Sardis side — plus homes in Cottonwood Village park, where you buy the home and rent the pad on First Nations leased land. Mountview Apartments is a flagged conflict, not a sixth. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Why does a land-use-plans page matter to a 55+ buyer?** Because the second-home options around a 55+ purchase — a suite for a caregiver, a coach/garden suite for family, or infill on a house lot instead of a complex — are governed by the plan stack: current zoning under Bylaw No. 5000 (as-of-right), the OCP designation under Bylaw No. 5500 (what the City supports on rezoning), the Schedule B neighbourhood plan, and any Schedule D permit areas on the parcel. Check both layers on the City Webmap before valuing potential. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Q: The live page calls Sardis "Schedule D" and Eastern Hillsides "Schedule C" — can I rely on that?** No. In the current OCP, Sardis is Schedule B5, Eastern Hillsides is Schedule B4, Schedule C is the Mountain View Heritage Conservation Area, and Schedule D is the Development Permit Areas schedule (D-1 to D-10). The live page's labels look like previous-OCP numbering carried forward — flagged for the rebuild. https://chilliwack.com/ocp and https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwacklanduseplans.html
- **Q: Can a Chilliwack house lot take a suite or coach house for a caregiver?** One accessory dwelling unit per lot where the zone permits it under Bylaw No. 5000: a secondary suite capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40%, a coach house, or a garden suite capped at 100 m²; a suite must be built with a permit meeting BC Building/Fire Code and flood requirements, and no new suite is permitted below the flood construction level. SSMUH Bylaw No. 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024) is the 3-to-4-unit frame for eligible urban lots. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a Chilliwack 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children and caregivers are exempt in defined cases. In Cottonwood Village park, spouse treatment runs through the park's own rules, not the strata exemption — get them in writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022; the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, a strata can still restrict short-term rentals, and the City regulates STRs under Bylaw No. 5141. Spadina Regency listings already state rentals and pets are permitted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Building by building: Ivy Green and Mountainview Estates record no pets; Spadina Regency listings state pets permitted; Knight Lodge records pets allowed with restrictions; Cottonwood Village's park list records 1 pet allowed. Confirm in the registered bylaws or park rules. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Can ALR land be subdivided or built on for family housing?** Not through the City alone: ALR subdivision and non-farm use need ALC approval, and the residence frame is 500 m² principal, suite only inside it, plus one additional residence up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is my lot in a floodplain, and does that affect a suite or a purchase?** Check the parcel on the City Webmap and confirm its Flood Construction Level with the City. The live page's Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519 citation is UNVERIFIED in this program, and Bylaw No. 5000 bars new suites below the flood construction level — valley-floor diligence is not optional in Chilliwack. https://chilliwack.com/ocp and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Chilliwack?** The city ladder runs: independent 55+ strata (Section 2) → subsidized independent rental (Jubilee Place, The Paramount) → independent/assisted retirement rental at the hospital corner (the three Chartwell residences) → registered assisted living (Birchwood, The Cascades, Waverly on the Sardis side) — all inside the city, most within blocks of Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Road. Sources: Section 2 above.

- **Three strata share Spadina Avenue and are three different products.** Ivy Green (45775) is 55+ with no pets; Spadina Regency (45765) is 55+ with rentals and pets permitted; Chilliwack Gardens (45749) states no age restrictions. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Two near-identical names must never be merged.** Mountainview Estates (46966 Yale Rd) is verified 55+ in Proper East; Mountview Apartments (46374 Margaret Ave) stays a flagged conflict with a build-year conflict (1977 vs 1974) and no bylaws/Form B check. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **One operator runs the whole hospital-corner care cluster.** Chartwell operates Birchwood, Hampton House and Lynnwood, each described as on the same corner as the other two — comparing them is comparing suite types and service packages inside one operator, not three competitors. https://chartwell.com/bc/chilliwack/hampton-house
- **Cottonwood Village's land has a clock.** Listing information states the park sits on First Nations leased land with a lease expiry in 2076 plus an option to extend — the head-lease term and extension mechanics are the due-diligence items a lender and a future buyer will ask about, and they have no equivalent in a freehold strata purchase at Cottage Grove. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/listing/r3069101-50-7610-evans-road-chilliwack-bc-v2r-2y8
- **There are no Chilliwack market numbers to borrow.** No SnapStats coverage and CADREB board-wide-only publishing mean any "Chilliwack median" on a land-use or 55+ page would be invented or imported from another board. This file states none, deliberately. https://www.cadreb.com/market/
- **The OCP adoption date on the live page could not be verified.** The page states December 16, 2025; the City's OCP page states no adoption date for Bylaw No. 5500. The bylaw number and plan structure are verified; the date is not. https://chilliwack.com/ocp and https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwacklanduseplans.html
