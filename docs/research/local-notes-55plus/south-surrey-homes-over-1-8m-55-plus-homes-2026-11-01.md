Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Scope note:** this file covers South Surrey for the over-$1.8M price-band slug. The band is a price filter over the whole S Surrey White Rock board community, not a neighbourhood. **The honest core of this file: South Surrey's verified 55+ stock sits at the opposite end of the market from the $1.8M band.** The community's overall detached median was $1,628,000 in September 2026 — below this page's $1.8M threshold — and SnapStats publishes no price-band cut, so no count, share or median for homes over $1.8M is stated anywhere here (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report, via the slug's research file). ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **The price band itself has no legal meaning.** "Over $1.8M" is a listing-feed filter, not a City, board or planning designation, and SnapStats publishes no price-band cut — there is no published count, share or median for S Surrey White Rock homes over $1,800,000 (slug's research file, Sections 1 and 2).
- **Property transfer tax at and above this band:** the Province's general rates are 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion of value above $3,000,000. Neither purchase exemption reaches this band for a downsizer: the first time home buyers' program full exemption caps at a fair market value of $835,000 and requires the buyer to have never owned a registered interest in a principal residence; the newly built home exemption's full threshold is $1,100,000 with elimination at $1,150,000. A downsizer selling a $1.8M+ home has owned before by definition — budget full PTT on the 55+ purchase at the general tiers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Plans:** land use across South Surrey is guided by the **Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026)** — never cite the replaced Bylaw No. 18020 as current — with older neighbourhood concept plans rescinded or superseded into it. White Rock-side properties sit under the City of White Rock's own plans and Zoning Bylaw No. 2506 (2024). https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents
- **ALR:** eastern South Surrey carries extensive Agricultural Land Reserve land; the ALC housing frame for ALR parcels is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), and ALR status is confirmed with the ALC, not from a note on title. Whether a specific parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Secondary suites and coach houses (Surrey):** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area under Zoning By-law No. 12000 (the old 90 m² cap is NOT current); a coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA/R2/R2-O/R3 lots. Band listings marketing legal suites and detached shops are claims to verify against the bylaw and City records, not the card. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Short-term rentals in Surrey:** permitted — owner-only hosting from the principal residence, City business licence $358/yr. White Rock's STR rule: UNVERIFIED in this pass. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md

No City floodplain page was opened in the research pass, so those figures are UNVERIFIED beyond site copy — do not republish them without a City source. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m.html

- **Q: How many South Surrey homes are listed over $1.8M?** UNVERIFIED as a statistic — SnapStats publishes no price-band cut for S Surrey White Rock, so no band count, share or median exists in the source data. - **Q: Do both of us have to be 55?** Peninsula Village listings state the local form expressly — "One person 55+." Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is exempt in any case, as are live-in caregivers and legacy residents. Each complex's own bylaw wording must still be confirmed in its Form B. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3098715-2500-152-street-67-surrey-bc-v4p-1m8/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ complex as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Could I buy the 55+ unit first, rent it out while my house sells, then move in?** In a strata, long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches: the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. At Sea Ridge Bays the same strata frame applies (it is a bare-land strata, not a park tenancy). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: At Sea Ridge Bays, do I rent a pad like in a mobile-home park?** No — listings state "NO PAD FEES" and "YOU OWN THE LOT": it is a bare-land strata, so you pay a strata fee ($210/mo including water in the listings checked — listing-stated, confirm current in the Form B) and strata law governs, not the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. https://realtylink.org/en/print/property/263120958 and https://www.jagsidhu.com/listing/r3099331-15875-20-avenue-50-surrey-bc-v4a-2b1/brochure
- **Q: What extra checks matter on a band purchase itself?** The ones the page's own guide copy names — zoning, ALR status, wells and septic, and floodplain rules. ALR status is confirmed with the Agricultural Land Commission (its housing frame allows one principal residence up to 500 m² plus one additional residence of 90 m² or 186 m² depending on parcel size), not from a note on title; parcel-level servicing for any specific listing is UNVERIFIED until confirmed with the City. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m.html
- **Q: Are pets allowed when we downsize?** Complex by complex, and the sources conflict in tone on this corridor: a directory records Semiahmoo Estates as pets not allowed; Peninsula Village listings state pets ok; The Crescent listing checked allows two pets with restrictions; Sea Ridge Bays listings allow a dog owner two dogs with no size restrictions.
