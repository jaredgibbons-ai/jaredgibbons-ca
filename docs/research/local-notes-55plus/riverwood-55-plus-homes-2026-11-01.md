- Riverwood is an established residential neighbourhood on Port Coquitlam's north-east side, by the Pitt River; the City's Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 3838) names Riverwood and Citadel Heights as established residential neighbourhoods, and the board (GVR) uses "Riverwood" as the sub-area name in the SnapStats GVR September 2026 report. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

- The neighbourhood centres on Riverwood Gate and Coast Meridian Road; Terry Fox Secondary School is at 1260 Riverwood Gate, and a School District 43 locator run for that address returned Blakeburn Elementary, Minnekhada Middle and Terry Fox Secondary. Boundary roads for Riverwood: UNVERIFIED — no City neighbourhood map states them. https://www.sd43.bc.ca/terryfox and https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- Coast Meridian Road connects the area south to Lougheed Highway and the Pitt River Bridge corridor toward Pitt Meadows. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf

All of Port Coquitlam's verified 55+ and seniors stock is in the downtown / Central Port Coquitlam flat and in Lincoln Park, on the other side of the city — see Section 2. Riverwood's own housing is single-detached homes under RS1–RS4 zones plus townhomes; no condo buildings inside Riverwood were confirmed from an official source. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2025-08/3630 - Zoning Bylaw 2008.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — since May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw, 2008, No. 3630.** Riverwood's detached homes sit in RS1–RS4 zones (minimum lot widths from at least 9.5 m in RS4 to at least 30 m in RS3); townhouse zones RTh1, RTh2 and RTh3 exist in the bylaw. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2025-08/3630 - Zoning Bylaw 2008.pdf
- **Secondary suites:** the City permits secondary suites only in the RS-1, RS-2, RS-3 and RS-4 zones, and a building permit is required — the route a Riverwood owner uses to house a parent or caregiver in the existing house. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/building-permits/secondary-suites
- **Suite utility-charge exemptions that matter to seniors:** the City waives 50% of the extra suite utility levy when the suite is occupied by a parent or grandparent of the owner, and 100% when a paid caregiver (minimum $150/month) lives in the suite to care for a permanently disabled owner or family member; applications are annual, due by Dec 31. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills/secondary-suite-exemptions
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing:** the City permits 3 to 6 SSMUH units on RS-zoned lots without rezoning, depending on lot size, location and transit proximity — relevant to Riverwood's larger RS3 lots. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2013, No. 3838** carries the "Non-Market and Special Needs Housing" policy section (§7.2); no Riverwood-specific seniors housing project is named in it — UNCONFIRMED as planned for Riverwood. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11285

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ place to buy right in Riverwood?** No. No age-restricted strata, co-op, rental or care building was verified inside Riverwood. The city's one verified 55+ strata ownership building is Mayfair Terrace at 2266 Atkins Ave downtown, and its newest 55+ building (Kew Lifestyles at Montrose, 2023) is rental. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata like Mayfair Terrace?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt categories. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for my parent?** Yes — ownership is not age-restricted; the bylaw governs who may reside, and a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, but the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Instead of moving, can I add a suite in my Riverwood house for a parent or caregiver?** Secondary suites are permitted in RS-1 to RS-4 zones with a building permit, and the City halves the extra suite utility levy if the suite is occupied by your parent or grandparent — and waives it entirely if a paid caregiver lives there to care for a permanently disabled owner or family member (annual application, due Dec 31). https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/building-permits/secondary-suites and https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/utility-bills/secondary-suite-exemptions
- **Q: Where is the hospital for Riverwood residents?** Port Coquitlam has no hospital; the nearest is Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody (Fraser Health, 24/7 emergency). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital
- **Q: What transit can I actually use from Riverwood?** Local TransLink buses connect to Port Coquitlam Station (West Coast Express — a weekday commuter service only, five trains each direction, no weekend service per TransLink's timetable effective Jan 5, 2026) and to Coquitlam Central Station (Millennium Line). SkyTrain does not reach Port Coquitlam. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- **Q: Is Riverwood in a floodplain?** Land toward the Pitt River is low-lying and much of the city sits in the Fraser/Pitt floodplain behind dykes; the City actively maintains the dike system (the Cedar Drainage Pump Station rebuild runs summer 2026 to summer 2027). Check the specific lot with the City — status is not uniform. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects/cedar-drainage-pump-station-0
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink the resale pool at a building like Mayfair Terrace?** Yes, mechanically: the next buyer must be 55+, an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. That is the built-in trade-off of every BC 55+ strata since 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Riverwood's attached market is its fast lane, but none of it is age-restricted.** Riverwood condos & townhomes posted a 38% sales ratio in September 2026 (21 listed, 8 sold) — the strongest attached pace of any Port Coquitlam sub-area that month — in general-market townhome complexes like Mountain View Estates and Dominion. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.
- **The neighbourhood and a complex share one name.** "Riverwood" is both the neighbourhood and a 1995 townhouse complex at 3075 Skeena Street; search results blend the two, and neither the complex nor the neighbourhood carries a verified 55+ product. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/riverwood-3075-skeena-street?prompt=login
- **The city's 55+ supply is a single 46-unit strata built in 2000, plus a 2023 rental.** Citywide, the ownership option has not grown since Mayfair Terrace (2000); the only newer age-restricted stock, Kew Lifestyles at Montrose (56 units, 2023), is rental only. A Riverwood seller downsizing in-city is competing for a very small pool. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/mayfair-terrace-2266-atkins-ave and https://www.soarchitects.com/portfolio/kew-lifestyles-at-montrose-condominiums/
- **No SkyTrain is funded for Port Coquitlam.** TransLink's Transport 2050 10-Year Priorities commits only to an "exploratory business case" for a Millennium Line extension to PoCo, still simply "underway" in the Mayors' Council's Feb 19, 2026 work plan — no route, stations or construction funding approved. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/about-translink/governance-and-board/council-minutes-and-reports/2026/february/report--2026-mc-budget--feb-2026.pdf
- **Riverwood lots carry multi-unit potential a downsizer may be selling without knowing it.** RS-zoned lots can hold 3 to 6 small-scale multi-unit housing units without rezoning depending on size, location and transit proximity, and RS3 lots (minimum 30 m width) are the neighbourhood's largest. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Property taxes:** Port Coquitlam's 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026 (5% penalty after July 2, a further 5% after September 15, 2026); the City states it has the lowest average property taxes and utility fees in Metro Vancouver, and utility bills are flat annual water and sewer fees due March 31, 2026, billed separately. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes
