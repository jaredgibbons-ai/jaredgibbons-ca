# Tilbury — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Tilbury is the industrial district on the south arm of the Fraser River in the City of Delta, between Ladner and North Delta. "Tilbury" is the Paragon (FVREB MLS) sub-area name as given; it is not one of the residential neighbourhoods named in the former North Delta Area Plan (Schedule C.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950) — the City treats it as an industrial area, the Tilbury Industrial Park / Tilbury Island lands. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- The industrial streets are River Road / River Way, Tilbury Road, Hopcott Road, Progress Way, Vantage Way and 72 Street / 80 Street, in the Highway 17 (South Fraser Perimeter Road) corridor, with Tilbury Island in the Fraser River; boundary detail from a City industrial map: UNVERIFIED. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/47276/
- The one City recreation facility inside the industrial area is Tilbury Ice, the City arena at 7187 Vantage Way (at 72 Street); a City lease posting for its restaurant space describes the surrounding area as mainly industrial warehouse space, off River Road at 72 Street and Vantage Way, with Highway 17 proximity, between Highway 91 / Alex Fraser Bridge and Highway 99 / Massey Tunnel. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-09/Tilbury%20Ice%20-%20For%20Lease%20Brochure.pdf
- No public school, City park, Fraser Valley Regional Library branch, shopping centre or grocery store inside the Tilbury industrial lands was confirmed in official sources. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/ and https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search
- Transit: a TransLink route serving inside the Tilbury industrial lands was not confirmed from a timetable; the nearest exchanges are Ladner Exchange and Scottsdale Exchange. https://www.translink.ca/
- The nearest hospital is Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner; the North Delta Public Health Unit is at 11245 84 Avenue. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- Flood context: Tilbury Island and the River Road lands are Fraser River riverfront industrial lands inside Delta's dike system; the City is the diking authority and operates over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure citywide. Parcel-level Flood Construction Level detail: UNVERIFIED. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates in Tilbury: nowhere. There is no residential housing market inside the industrial lands (see Section 2).

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**No 55+ housing of any kind in Tilbury — and no residential housing market at all.** Tilbury is designated industrial land in the OCP, and no residential housing mix — no houses, condos or townhomes as a market — was confirmed inside the Tilbury industrial lands in City of Delta sources. A "small residential pocket" for Tilbury was NOT CONFIRMED from City sources in this research; the dwellings in the wider Tilbury postal area sit on the surrounding Ladner / East Delta farm and riverfront parcels, outside the industrial park lands. What trades instead is industrial strata units (small-bay and mid-bay warehouse/office strata complexes on streets like Progress Way and Vantage Way) and freehold industrial lots. Do not put Tilbury on a 55+ homes page as a place to live. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

- None. There is no residential strata inside the industrial lands.

### Verified 55+ rental (not ownership)

- None. There is no rental housing inside the industrial lands.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

These rules are stated here because buyers reach this page looking for 55+ housing in Delta; inside Tilbury itself there is no housing for them to apply to.

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** governs Tilbury as industrial land; City material references the I2 and I7 industrial zone codes for Tilbury-area properties, and the full industrial zone names and minimum lot standards were not confirmed from a City zone sheet — confirm the zone and standards for any parcel on the City zoning maps. Permitted-use, outdoor-storage and parking rules differ by zone, so a use that works in one bay can be prohibited two streets over. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")** covers Tilbury as industrial land; the OCP states Delta's industries are primarily located along River Road East, in Tilbury Industrial Park, Annacis Island and Roberts Bank, with pockets along River Road West and at Boundary Bay Airport. There is no residential designation for the Tilbury industrial lands in the plan. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Seniors congregate housing is a named use in Delta's apartment zones — elsewhere.** The City's Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone, added to Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 in 2024, lists "seniors congregate housing" as a permitted principal use in the apartment zones tied to the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation. Tilbury's industrial zones are not apartment zones; no seniors housing permission for the industrial lands was confirmed. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **ALR and lease status on edge parcels:** City material shows farm use leases on some Tilbury-area parcels, and ALR status is parcel-specific through the Agricultural Land Commission — an edge parcel can be farm or leased land rather than clean industrial freehold. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property tax deferment at 55+** exists for Delta homeowners generally — BC's regular Property Tax Deferment Program lets a homeowner who is 55 or older during the current year defer annual property taxes on a principal residence — but it has no application to Tilbury industrial parcels, which are not principal residences. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a 55+ home in Tilbury?** No. Tilbury is designated industrial land in the OCP, and no residential housing mix was confirmed inside the industrial lands in City sources — there are no homes to buy at any age. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Is there any rental, seniors or subsidized housing in Tilbury?** No. The City of Delta Affordable Housing Guide (April 2026) lists Delta's non-market seniors buildings in Ladner, Tsawwassen and North Delta (Scottsdale) — none in Tilbury. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **Q: I see houses with Tilbury postal addresses. Are they in Tilbury?** The dwellings in the wider Tilbury postal area sit on the surrounding Ladner / East Delta farm and riverfront parcels, outside the industrial park lands — a postal address is not the sub-area. Any such house is a Ladner/East Delta purchase, with its own ALR, flood and zoning checks, not a Tilbury industrial-lands purchase. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Could I live in a caretaker suite in an industrial unit I buy?** No source checked states any residential, caretaker-suite or live-work permission in Tilbury's industrial zones (City material references I2 and I7 codes; the full zone standards were not confirmed from a City zone sheet) — UNCONFIRMED. Do not price or market a Tilbury unit as livable without the parcel's zone sheet and a City answer in hand. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/
- **Q: Where is the closest 55+ housing to Tilbury?** The verified options are in the neighbouring sub-areas: in Scottsdale, the subsidized 55+ rentals Scott Road Lodge, Strawberry Hill Manor and White Birch Manor; in Nordel, the Sunstone 55+ Apartments market rental; in Ladner, the Evergreen Lane non-market cluster and Ladner's verified 55+ ownership buildings (see those files). No verified 55+ ownership strata was found in Tilbury or anywhere in North Delta in this research. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf and https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/sunstone-apartments/1370
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Delta?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain where a valid 55+ bylaw exists: the tenant still has to satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is there transit or a seniors bus inside Tilbury?** No TransLink route serving inside the industrial lands was confirmed from a timetable; the nearest exchanges are Ladner Exchange and Scottsdale Exchange. The City's free Delta Seniors Bus is for Delta residents aged 65+ at residential addresses inside Delta — it is not an industrial-area service. https://www.translink.ca/ and http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/seniors-services/seniors-bus

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Tilbury" in a housing search is the industrial park's name spilling into postal addresses.** The Paragon sub-area name, the Tilbury Industrial Park name and the FortisBC Tilbury LNG facility all use the same word; none of them is a residential neighbourhood, and the former North Delta Area Plan did not list Tilbury among its residential neighbourhoods. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- **Delta's industrial land base is regionally significant, and Tilbury is a named pillar of it.** The OCP cites Metro Vancouver's 2020 Regional Industrial Lands Inventory: Delta's industrially zoned and designated land totaled 1,527 hectares, approximately 13% of the region's overall inventory — the industrial designation here is the plan, not a placeholder awaiting housing. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **The area's best-known industrial operation sets the neighbour profile.** The FortisBC Tilbury LNG facility is the area's best-known industrial operation; expansion approval detail from an official project page was not confirmed in the sources read — UNCONFIRMED. Any buyer of a nearby farm or riverfront home is buying beside an active LNG and warehouse district, which belongs in the due-diligence conversation, not the marketing copy. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **The Highway 17 alignment runs through the industrial area.** City application material shows Tilbury Industrial area properties affected by the South Fraser Perimeter Road alignment, and the City's own Tilbury Ice posting markets the position between the Alex Fraser Bridge and the Massey Tunnel — access is the area's value case, for industrial buyers. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-09/Tilbury%20Ice%20-%20For%20Lease%20Brochure.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/47276/
- **Flood homework is the first subject on any riverfront parcel, home or not.** Tilbury Island and the River Road lands are Fraser River riverfront lands inside the City's dike system; confirm the parcel's Flood Construction Level, dike setbacks and any Section 219 flood covenants on title before subject removal. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
