# Uptown NW (Uptown, New Westminster) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Uptown is New Westminster's uphill commercial and civic core, centred on Sixth Street and Sixth Avenue above the Downtown waterfront; the City identifies Uptown in its planning material as a Frequent Transit Development Area centred on that Sixth Street / Sixth Avenue core. The working bounds used in the area research are roughly between Royal Avenue / Sixth Avenue, Eighth Street and Tenth Street, taking in City Hall, the main public library, Moody Park's east edge and the Sixth Street shopping corridor — exact board sub-area boundary roads are UNVERIFIED. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- "Uptown" is the board (GVR) sub-area name as used in the SnapStats GVR September 2026 report; the missing-areas list shortens it to "Uptown NW". Nearby areas: Downtown and the Quay below the hill to the south, Queen's Park to the east, Brow of the Hill to the west, Moody Park and Glenbrooke North to the north. (Sources: the area research file, sources read 2026-11-01.)
- Civic anchors inside the area: New Westminster City Hall is at 511 Royal Avenue at the south edge of the Uptown plateau, and the New Westminster Public Library Main Branch is at 716 6th Avenue. Royal City Centre, the enclosed mall at 610 Sixth Street, is the area's main shopping centre, described by its operator as New Westminster's premier shopping destination with over 40 shops and services; the Sixth Street and Sixth Avenue street-front corridors carry the rest of Uptown's shops, restaurants and services. https://www.nwpl.ca/ and http://www.royalcitycentre.ca/
- Recreation: Moody Park, at the area's northwest edge, has an outdoor pool, spray park and sports fields, and Moody Park Arena is at 701 8th Avenue; the təməsew̓txʷ Aquatic and Community Centre at 65 East Sixth Avenue, just east of Uptown, fully opened in May 2024. https://www.newwestcity.ca/facilities and http://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/community-parks
- Schools: School District 40 (New Westminster) catchments are address-based, and every New Westminster student attends the district's single secondary school. A School Locator run for the sample address 835 Eighth Street returned Lord Kelvin Elementary, Fraser River Middle and New Westminster Secondary; a run for 1714 Eighth Avenue returned École Lord Tweedsmuir Elementary, Fraser River Middle and New Westminster Secondary. The replacement New Westminster Secondary School at 820 Sixth Street opened to students on January 11, 2021 and is reported above capacity. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/ and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2021EDUC0003-000034.htm
- Transit: there is no SkyTrain station inside Uptown; the nearest stations are New Westminster Station and Columbia Station (Expo Line) at the foot of the hill downtown. TransLink route 105 (Uptown / New Westminster Station) and route 106 (Edmonds Station / New Westminster Station) run through the Uptown corridors, and routes 103 and 123 also serve the Sixth Street / Sixth Avenue area. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf
- Health: Royal Columbian Hospital, 330 East Columbia Street, is in Sapperton, east of Uptown — out of area, but it is the city's Fraser Health regional hospital and the hospital an Uptown buyer will use. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/New-Westminster/royal-columbian-hospital
- Market shape (for context): strata is the bulk of the market here — in September 2026 the sub-area listed 108 condos & townhomes with 13 sold (12% sales ratio) against 14 detached listings with no detached sales (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file).
- Where the seniors housing actually is: Uptown holds two of the city's subsidized seniors buildings — Legion Manor on Sixth Avenue and Hillside Place on Queens Avenue (Section 2) — and the city's best-known retirement residence, Thornebridge Gardens, sits at its Moody Park edge and is filed in the Moody Park notes, not counted here. No 55+ strata ownership was verified anywhere in the sub-area.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. No Uptown strata checked has a verified 55+ bylaw — the absence of one is the verified fact here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. This is why The Woodward's database line "rentals not allowed" has to be read against the current registered bylaws, not taken at face value. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001** (consolidated as of June 8, 2026 on the City's current consolidation) governs the city. In Uptown, apartment buildings are concentrated along the Sixth Street / Sixth Avenue core under the multiple-unit residential and commercial high-rise districts, while the side streets sit in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series), where RS-1 (s.310) sets a minimum site area of 557.40 sq. m. (6,000 sq. ft.) and a floor space ratio of 0.5; the RS districts allow secondary suites, and laneway / carriage houses are regulated under the same bylaw. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/zoning-bylaw/sb_expander_articles/1618.php
- **Plan: Our City 2041, the Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 7925, 2017)**, governs the city, and Uptown is identified in the OCP as a Frequent Transit Development Area — one of the areas where the City directs higher-density, transit-supportive growth. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **Adaptable housing rule (the City's actual seniors-housing lever):** since a Zoning Bylaw amendment adopted June 28, 2011 (Bylaw No. 7464, 2011), a minimum of 40% of all single-storey dwelling units in multiple-unit residential buildings city-wide must be built as adaptable dwelling units under the BC Building Code — the City states the policy is a proactive means of planning for its aging population, enabling seniors to remain in their community. Adaptable is not age-restricted: any buyer can purchase these units. http://newwestcity.ca/housing/adaptable-housing
- **Flood:** the City's floodplain study zones only the city's low-lying edges — Zone 1 Queensborough, Zone 2 west-end Fraser River industrial land, Zone 3 the Quay waterfront and Zone 4 the Brunette River industrial area; no floodplain zone for the Uptown plateau was confirmed on a City source, and lot-level status should be checked with the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf and http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through two different doors in this area:** Hillside Place fills through BC Housing's Housing Registry, while Legion Manor takes applications directly from its non-profit operator, the Royal City Legion Housing Society — applying to the wrong one does not put an applicant on the other building's list. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076
- **Property taxes and utilities:** City of New Westminster 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining unpaid on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. New Westminster operates its own electrical utility alongside City water / sewer services. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Uptown?** None that any source verifies. No Uptown strata — not Viceroy, The Woodward, Amara Terrace, Queens Terrace or the Ash Street low-rises — carries a verified 55+ bylaw; all are general-market buildings. A 55+ buyer in Uptown is buying a regular condo in the city's most walkable core, with no age bylaw attached; the sub-area's seniors-specific stock is subsidized rental (Legion Manor, Hillside Place), not ownership. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What is Legion Manor, and how does someone get in?** A non-profit seniors rental building at 505 Sixth Avenue, classed "seniors specific" in the City's non-market inventory (123 units in that 2009 inventory; current count UNCONFIRMED) and operated by the Royal City Legion Housing Society. BC Housing's Zone 5 directory puts it in the apply-directly section — applications go to the society itself, not to the Housing Registry. It is not sold and not market rental. https://newwestcity.ca/database/rte/Housing%20web%20update%20-%20Non-Market%20details.pdf and https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076
- **Q: Is the Hillside Place on Queens Avenue the same building as the Hillside Place listings downtown?** No — there are two. The subsidized seniors building is at 1025 Queens Avenue in Uptown (16 homes in BC Housing's directory, Housing Registry applications). The strata called Hillside Place at 509 Carnarvon Street is a general-market Downtown building marketed to families and investors. And the strata tower at 1026 Queens Avenue, across the street from the seniors building, is Amara Terrace — also general-market. Three buildings, one name cluster, only one of them seniors housing. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076 and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/hillside-place-509-carnarvon-street
- **Q: Can I buy at Thornebridge Gardens?** No. Thornebridge Gardens (649 8th Avenue) is a Verve Senior Living retirement RESIDENCE — independent living through assisted living and respite, rented with services, and registered as an assisted-living residence with the Province. It sits at the Moody Park / Uptown edge, is filed in the Moody Park notes, and nothing there is strata ownership. https://www.verveseniorliving.com/thornebridge-gardens/
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in BC?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. Hillside Place's directory row likewise shows couples under 55 accepted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. (In Uptown this is academic for now: there is no 55+ strata here to buy into.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out an Uptown condo?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, whatever a building database still prints — The Woodward's record still says "rentals not allowed." Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Confirm the building's current registered bylaws and Form B before counting on rental income. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/the-woodward-612-sixth-street
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Royal Columbian Hospital, 330 East Columbia Street in Sapperton (Fraser Health) — east of Uptown, at Sapperton Station on the Expo Line. It is the regional hospital for the city; there is no hospital inside Uptown. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/New-Westminster/royal-columbian-hospital
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** No SkyTrain station is in Uptown — New Westminster and Columbia Stations are downtown at the foot of the hill. The everyday link is the bus: routes 105 and 106 run through the Sixth Street / Sixth Avenue corridors to the stations, and routes 103 and 123 also serve the area. A car-free senior should test the hill walk from the specific building to its bus stop before buying. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf
- **Q: Is Uptown in the floodplain, and what about the hill?** The City's floodplain study assigns its four zones to the low-lying edges of the city — Queensborough, the west-end industrial lands, the Quay waterfront and the Brunette River industrial area — and no zone for the Uptown plateau was confirmed; confirm lot-level status with the City. The practical Uptown issue is slope, not water: the plateau sits above Downtown, and the streets down to the SkyTrain stations and the Quay are the daily climb. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Uptown?** Partly. The subsidized independent step is in-area (Legion Manor, Hillside Place — both application-based rental, not a move a homeowner can schedule). The assisted-living step at the area's edge is Thornebridge Gardens (649 8th Ave, filed under Moody Park), and long-term care and the hospital are outside the sub-area, in Sapperton and at the city's other care sites. https://www.verveseniorliving.com/thornebridge-gardens/ and https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **One street, three buildings, one trap:** on Queens Avenue at Tenth Street, the subsidized seniors building (Hillside Place, 1025) faces a general-market strata tower (Amara Terrace, 1026) across the street, and a second general-market complex (Queens Terrace, 1032) stands on the same block. A page or a search built on the name "Hillside" or the street alone will mix rental seniors housing with ordinary condos for sale. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076 and https://www.zolo.ca/new-westminster-real-estate/1026-queens-avenue/506
