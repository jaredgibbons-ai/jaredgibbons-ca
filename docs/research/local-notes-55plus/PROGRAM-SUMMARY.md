## 4. Standing corrections the program established (bylaw currency and settled facts)

These supersede anything in earlier drafts, older research files, or the live site where they conflict. Later batches applied them throughout; earlier-batch files may still carry superseded citations (a sweep item, §5).

- **Surrey OCP:** Bylaw 18020 is replaced — the current OCP is the **Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)**.
- **Burnaby zoning:** the Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) is superseded — the **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (adopted June 9, 2026, in force July 1, 2026)** is current.
- **Port Moody OCP:** **Bylaw 2026, No. 3522**.
- **Mission OCP:** **Bylaw 6405-2026** (5948-2020 superseded), adopted April 21, 2026. **Mission's tax penalty is a flat 10%** on unpaid taxes after July 2 (Community Charter ss. 233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s. 3) — the 5% + 5% form belongs to the separate **rural regime** (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11: 5% after July 2 + 5% after October 31) that governs the FVRD electoral areas.
- **Maple Ridge:** Zoning Bylaw **7600-2019**. The OCP number is **contested** (§5).
- **Langley City:** Zoning Bylaw **2025 No. 3300** (its adoption date is itself conflicted in sources — UNVERIFIED).
- **White Rock:** Zoning Bylaw **2024 No. 2506**; OCP Bylaw **2017 No. 2220** — White Rock ground is never governed by Surrey bylaws.
- **West Vancouver (established Batch 21):** Zoning Bylaw **No. 4662 (2010)**; OCP Bylaw **No. 4985 (2018)**, per the District's own site. The 2506/2220 pair is White Rock's (above) — it does **not** verify for West Vancouver, and any file citing it for West Vancouver ground is wrong. Because the same pair failed in one city, the White Rock attribution itself is queued for re-verification in the White Rock pass (§5, item 2).
- **Delta OCP:** consolidated **Bylaw 8400 (2024)** — it contains **no** Tsawwassen Area Plan schedule; farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". (The "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" attribution swept to zero across the program.)
- **SSMUH bylaws:** Chilliwack Bylaw **5395** (adopted June 18, 2024); Abbotsford Bylaw **3560-2024** (adopted June 25, 2024); Township of Langley Bylaw **6020** (adopted Nov 18, 2024).
- **BC strata age law (the frame every file uses):** only **55+** age-restriction bylaws are valid, since **November 24, 2022** (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2; Strata Property Regulation 7.01), with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded **May 1, 2023** — younger spouses/partners and defined children. All residential **rental-restriction bylaws are void** from the same 2022 date; any directory or listing line saying "rentals not allowed" is dead law and is flagged wherever the files quote it.
- **CRA figures — VERIFIED in the program's later batches (this closes the long-standing UNVERIFIED hold):** Home Buyers' Plan limit **$60,000**; FHSA **$40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year**; first-time buyer GST rebate terms per **Bill C-4**; CMHC premium range **0.6%–4.5%** (the live mortgage-calculator page's 2.80%–4.00% range is wrong).
- **Geography corrections settled by the program:** County Line Glen Valley is Township of Langley, not Abbotsford; Durieu is FVRD Electoral Area F, Dewdney-Deroche Area G, Lake Errock and Hemlock Valley Area C (all rural-regime, not City of Mission); Stave Falls **is** inside the City of Mission; Norgate is **District** of North Vancouver (District OCP Bylaw 7900), not City; "North Arm" is a New Westminster board sub-area, not Richmond; "Lower Landing" is not a Richmond name — the official name is London Landing (Steveston); Vancouver Heights' secondary catchment is **Alpha Secondary** (SD41), not Burnaby North; Upper Lonsdale straddles the City/District line at 29th Street; University VW is the unincorporated UEL/UBC, not the City of Vancouver; Harbour Chines is Coquitlam, Brunette is New Westminster, Calverhall is City of North Vancouver.
