## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law.** Pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), not the Strata Property Act — so the strata age-bylaw machinery (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, Regulation 7.01 exemptions) does not apply inside a park. A home owner must provide a purchaser with the written tenancy agreement and the written park rules, and a sale is an assignment of the pad tenancy that requires the park owner's consent. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **A park's 55+ rule has its own legal footing.** The MHPTA assignment rules expressly recognise a park where every pad is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or 2+ tenants, at least one of whom has reached 55), as set out in s. 10(2)(b)(i) of the Human Rights Code — the park owner can refuse an assignment where no purchaser meets that age requirement. That is how Westwood Court's, Westwood Estates' and Newton Mobile Home Park's 55+ rules operate: through park rules and assignment consent, not through a strata bylaw. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol20/consol20/02077_pit
- **Assignment consent has a clock.** Under MHPTA s. 28 a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an arbitrator's order, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it; the park owner must respond within 10 days of the written request (RTB Form 10), silence counts as consent given, and no fee can be charged for consent. Listings phrase this as "subject to park approval." Before asking, the seller must give the proposed buyer written copies of the park rules and the relevant parts of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **What transfers on assignment, and how pad rent rises:** on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. Otherwise, park landlords can only increase rent with 3 full months' notice, can increase rent every 12 months, and the increase cannot exceed the yearly rent increase limit; a park landlord may also add a proportional amount — the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park — and may apply to the Residential Tenancy Branch for an additional increase for expenses or capital expenditures. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Registration is what makes a sale effective, and the CSA label is a sale gate.** In BC, the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if the transaction is registered with the Manufactured Home Registry, through a lawyer or notary filing the Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership forms. A home may not be sold unless it has the original legible CSA decal attached (Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular); if the label is lost, the home must be re-certified — Technical Safety BC has a licensed electrical contractor inspect the wiring, and the electrical approval label prepared can be used in place of a CSA label to list the home for sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Taxes on a home in a park:** the Province states a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner — which is why Westwood Estates listings can fold a "land portion of property taxes" into the pad rent. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/8254-134-street/48
- **For contrast — the strata rule that applies at Sea Ridge Bays, not in the parks:** only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; sub-55 bylaws invalid since Nov 24, 2022), the bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit rather than who may own it, Regulation 7.01 exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19; and strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. A park can still set its own occupancy and rental rules in its park rules — do not import strata assumptions into a park purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000** carries a dedicated zone for this housing form — Part 19, Zone RM-M, Manufactured Home Residential Zone — and every Surrey property's zone is confirmed parcel by parcel in the City's COSMOS mapping and the consolidated bylaw text. The City's regulatory-bylaw list also carries a separate Bylaw No. 6142, "Mobile Homes & Trailer." A specific park's zone as shown in COSMOS: confirm parcel by parcel before relying on it. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/regulatory-bylaws
- **Surrey parks carry a City policy layer on top of the provincial Act:** the City's Manufactured Home Park Policy brochure tells park residents the property owner must give 12 months' notice of a final moving date, can only give that notice after receiving all permits and approvals, and must pay $20,000 on or before the move-out date. Surrey's Tenant Protection Bylaw, 2025, No. 21788 does NOT apply to property governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — park residents rely on the provincial Act and the City policy, not that bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21788.pdf
- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900** is the current OCP — adopted June 15, 2026, replacing the 2013 OCP Bylaw No. 18020. A seller or park quoting an old plan designation may be quoting a document no longer in effect. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Property taxes / deferment:** the Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). Note a park listing can separately fold a land-tax portion into pad rent — who pays what on a pad is set by the tenancy agreement, not by strata practice. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/8254-134-street/48
- **First-home programs:** the First Home Savings Account and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file — no live CRA page stating the current limits was read in this pass, so no figure is published here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which Surrey parks are actually 55+?** Three pad-rent parks verify: Westwood Court (8670 156 St, Fleetwood), Westwood Estates (134 St, Newton — listings use both 8224 and 8254) and Newton Mobile Home Park (6571 King George Blvd, West Newton, 34 sites) — each on current listing or directory text that names the park and states the 55+ age restriction. A fourth manufactured-home option, Sea Ridge Bays (15875 20 Ave, South Surrey), is 55+ but is a bare-land strata, not a park. Any other Surrey park claimed as 55+ is UNCONFIRMED in this research — and two King George Boulevard parks that sound like candidates, Crispen Bays and Crestway Bays, are listed as all-ages. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What do I own when I buy in a 55+ park?** The manufactured home — not the land. You rent the pad under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement, and your purchase is an assignment of that tenancy, which requires the park owner's prior written consent under MHPTA s. 28; the purchase is only effective once registered with the Manufactured Home Registry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Can the park refuse me because of age — or refuse my buyer later?** In a park whose pads are reserved by age, yes: the MHPTA assignment rules expressly recognise parks reserved for tenants who have reached 55 (Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i)), and the park owner can refuse an assignment where no purchaser meets the age requirement. The same rule that admits you at 55+ narrows your resale pool the same way a strata bylaw does. Consent itself cannot be stalled indefinitely: the owner must respond within 10 days of a written request, silence counts as consent, and no consent fee can be charged. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ park?** Park age rules operate through the park's own rules and the Human Rights Code framing (a pad reserved for a tenant who has reached 55, or 2+ tenants at least one of whom has reached 55) — the strata spouse exemption does not apply inside a park. Read the specific park's rules for how a younger spouse is treated. At Sea Ridge Bays, which is a strata, the provincial younger-spouse exemption does apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What are the carrying costs on top of the purchase?** Pad rent is the big one, and it is park-specific and listing-stated only in this research: Westwood Court's listings state the pad fee includes water, garbage and management; Westwood Estates' listings state pad rent including the land portion of property taxes, garbage pickup, sewer and water — but the amounts stated across its listings conflict ($822, $912.52, $915 and $935 a month for different units and dates), and Newton Mobile Home Park's current pad rent is UNCONFIRMED. Confirm the current figure for the specific pad in the tenancy agreement — pad rent is not a strata fee and is not set by the seller. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Can the park raise my pad rent when I buy?** No — on assignment of the site tenancy the pad rental for the new tenant stays the same, and the scheduled date for a subsequent rent increase does not change; increases otherwise require 3 full months' notice, at most once every 12 months, within the yearly limit plus the proportional levy and utility amount. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How does Sea Ridge Bays differ from the three parks?** You own the lot. Sea Ridge Bays is a bare-land strata — title to land is recorded as Freehold Strata, listings state "NO PAD FEES" in the series' earlier pass and a monthly strata fee ($210 in the listings checked, including water) instead of pad rent, and strata law (including the 55+ bylaw and its exemptions) governs instead of the MHPTA. Its listings also state "Council Approval Required," a strata-side wording a park buyer will not meet in a pad-rent park, where the equivalent step is the park owner's assignment consent. https://www.jagsidhu.com/listing/r3099331-15875-20-avenue-50-surrey-bc-v4a-2b1/brochure and https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3058972-15875-20-avenue-24-surrey-king-george-corridor-homes-for-sale-599000/brochure
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Park by park, and all three verified parks allow some: Westwood Court's listings state pets welcome; Westwood Estates allows large dogs with park approval, no vicious breeds; Newton Mobile Home Park listings state one pet allowed, subject to park rules; Sea Ridge Bays listings state two dogs with no size restrictions. Confirm in the park rules or strata bylaws for the specific community before writing. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What if the home has lost its CSA label?** A manufactured home may not be sold unless it has the original and legible CSA decal attached (Z-240 for a mobile home, A-277 for a modular). If the label is removed or lost, the home must be re-certified: Technical Safety BC arranges a licensed electrical contractor's inspection, and the electrical approval label prepared can be used in place of a CSA label — a current Westwood Estates listing advertises exactly that history ("Electrical re-certification, silver label"). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/8254-134-street/48
- **Q: What happens if a park is redeveloped?** Surrey adds a City layer to the provincial Act: under the City's Manufactured Home Park Policy the property owner must give 12 months' notice of a final moving date, can only give it after receiving all permits and approvals, and must pay $20,000 on or before the move-out date. Surrey's 2025 Tenant Protection Bylaw does not cover park residents — it expressly does not apply to property governed by the MHPTA. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21788.pdf
