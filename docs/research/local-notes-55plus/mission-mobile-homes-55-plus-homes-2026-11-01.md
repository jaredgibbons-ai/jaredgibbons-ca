## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law — this is the governing frame for this whole topic.** Pad tenancies fall under BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA): the resident owns the home and rents the pad. A park's age rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw — which is why the Strata Property Act's 55+ framework does not settle Oasis's "adult side," and why Mission Mobile Home Park's 55+ status rests on its park documents and listing record, not on a strata Form B. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Pad rent increases are capped and paced.** A pad rent increase may happen only once in 12 months, on at least three full months' written notice, and cannot exceed the provincial limit — 2.3% for 2026 and 2.2% for 2027 for manufactured home site tenancies — plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees (the change in levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of sites in the park). A landlord may apply to the Residential Tenancy Branch for an additional increase above the limit for increased operating or financing costs or for substantial and essential repairs or renovations. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Selling a home that stays in the park is an assignment, and it needs consent.** Under s.28 of the MHPTA, a tenant may assign the site tenancy agreement to the buyer only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed to have been obtained under the Regulation), with an order of the director authorizing the assignment, or where the tenancy agreement itself authorizes it; the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting (s.28(3)); before requesting consent, the seller must provide the proposed buyer with written copies of the park rules and the relevant parts of the tenancy agreement; on assignment the pad rent and the scheduled date for a subsequent increase stay the same; and a tenant who believes consent is being unreasonably refused can apply for RTB dispute resolution. Note on sourcing in the research file: this information sheet was seen via search-result text, not as a full read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Park rules must be given in writing before signing.** Landlords are required to give tenants a written copy of the manufactured home park rules (if there are any) before signing the tenancy agreement — pet limits, age provisions and approval requirements for any specific park on this feed are park documents, not listing text: feed claims such as "no age restrictions" or "Park Approval Is Needed" are UNVERIFIED until the park's own rule package is in hand. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf and https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-mobile-homes.html
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s.42).** Where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or a residential use other than a manufactured home park, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice covering a full year, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; the tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, may dispute the notice within 15 days, may in defined circumstances (home cannot be moved, no site within a reasonable distance, no tax owing on the home) apply for additional compensation of the home's assessed value minus the $20,000, and if the landlord does not take steps toward the stated conversion within a reasonable period the landlord must pay the greater of $5,000 or 12 months' rent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now — relevant only to the strata alternatives in Section 2.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it, and Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. These strata exemptions do NOT automatically apply inside a manufactured-home park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022 in stratas; SHORT-TERM rental bylaws are still allowed in stratas. This is a strata rule — it does not govern a park tenancy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Local zoning split.** Inside the City of Mission, manufactured-home parks sit under City zoning; the Dewdney Deroche and Hatzic Island parks on this feed sit under FVRD electoral-area zoning instead. The City's or FVRD's park-specific zoning, redevelopment or closure-notice rules for any named park: UNVERIFIED in the research file — no park bylaw document was opened. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf
- **Current plan (City parks): Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026,** replacing and repealing OCP Bylaw 6350-2025 with the same vision, framework and mapping plus a Regional Context Statement. The **Lougheed Highway Corridor Plan is in preparation, NOT adopted** — corridor parks on Lougheed Highway are not governed by an adopted corridor plan. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies
- **Property taxes — two regimes behind one feed.** Park homes inside the City of Mission: 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty on current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 (Community Charter ss.233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s.3; Mission has NO alternative-scheme bylaw). Parks in FVRD electoral areas: rural taxes paid to the Province through the Surveyor of Taxes Office, due July 2, with 5% on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31 under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11. How tax is split between a park owner and individual pad tenants in any specific park: UNVERIFIED — it is set by the park's tenancy agreements. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which Mission-area park is actually 55+?** Mission Mobile Home Park, 34519 Lougheed Hwy in Hatzic — its listings state "This has been 55+ mobile park" and record the park as "Adult Oriented." It is the only park in this market verified 55+ on the sources checked. Oasis Country Estates has an adult side with no age number stated (UNCONFIRMED as 55+), and Ruskin Place states no age restrictions outright. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85367/FullPublic.pdf?i=979581669 and https://www.zolo.ca/mission-real-estate/41168-lougheed-highway/13 and https://www.estateblock.com/mission/19-9960-wilson-st-126341
- **Q: What am I actually buying with a manufactured home in a park?** The home itself — and a site tenancy for the pad it sits on, under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement governed by the MHPTA. That is why pad rent, park rules and the assignment process matter as much as the home's price. One feed card claims a home on its own lot instead — confirm tenure on title for that home. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf and https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-mobile-homes.html
- **Q: How much can my pad rent go up, and how fast?** Once every 12 months at most, on three full months' written notice, by no more than the provincial limit — 2.3% for 2026, 2.2% for 2027 — plus the proportional levy/utility amount divided across the park's sites. Anything above that requires an RTB order. Feed pad rents ($440–$808/month across cards checked, and $650 / $671.29 at Mission Mobile Home Park) are listing text only — get the current pad rent and last increase date for the specific pad in writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-mobile-homes.html
- **Q: Can the park refuse my buyer when I sell?** The sale works by assigning your site tenancy, which under MHPTA s.28 requires the landlord's prior written consent (or deemed consent, or an order of the director, or authorization in the agreement itself). The landlord cannot charge a fee for consent, you must give the proposed buyer the park rules and written tenancy terms before requesting consent, the pad rent carries over unchanged on assignment, and an unreasonable refusal can be taken to RTB dispute resolution. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in the 55+ park?** The strata spouse exemption does not answer this — a park is not a strata. The park's own rules and the tenancy agreement govern who may occupy, so get Mission Mobile Home Park's rule package in writing before writing an offer. In a 55+ strata (Cherry Lane, Luther Place) the answer would be yes by provincial exemption; do not carry that answer into a park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Q: Is Oasis Country Estates a 55+ park?** Not as a whole, and not verifiably at 55+ at all: listings split it into an "adult side" and a "family section" and state no age number for the adult side. Treat any "55+" label for this park as UNCONFIRMED and get the park rules in writing. https://www.zolo.ca/mission-real-estate/41168-lougheed-highway/13
- **Q: What happens if the park is sold for redevelopment?** Under MHPTA s.42 the landlord needs all permits in place first, then 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation per tenancy, with defined routes to additional compensation where the home cannot be moved or no site is available within a reasonable distance — and a further payment (the greater of $5,000 or 12 months' rent) if the landlord does not proceed with the conversion within a reasonable period. Confirm the park's ownership and any known redevelopment signals as part of due diligence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Are all the parks on this page in the City of Mission?** No — the feed includes parks in Dewdney Deroche and on Hatzic Island, which are FVRD Electoral Area G ground with rural taxes paid to the Province, alongside City parks. The MHPTA applies in both, but zoning and the tax penalty regime differ: City flat 10% after July 2, 2026, versus rural 5% after July 2 plus a further 5% after October 31. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-mobile-homes.html and https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Q: What is the median price of a manufactured home in Mission?** SnapStats publishes no manufactured-home median — its Mission splits are Detached and Condos & Townhomes combined, covering all dwellings. No park-home median is stated here; price off comparable park-home sales and the specific park's pad rent and rules. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: What pets are allowed in the parks?** Park by park, from listing text only: Ruskin Place allows small pets with approval and states no age restrictions; Triple Creek Estates lists "No Pet Restrictions"; Nicomen Village listings state 2 pets allowed; Mission Mobile Home Park listings in the city-wide file record cats and dogs OK with a two-pet limit. None of that replaces the park rules — confirm in the rule package for the specific park. https://www.estateblock.com/mission/19-9960-wilson-st-126341 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3053612-10221-wilson-street-34-mission-bc-v4s-1e2/brochure and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3047274-43201-lougheed-highway-111-mission-bc-v0m-1g0/brochure

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The feed's own parks pull in opposite age directions.** On one feed: a park whose listings state 55+ outright (Mission Mobile Home Park, Hatzic), a park whose listing states "Family Friendly, no age restrictions" (Ruskin Place), a park with a playground and no age rule stated (Triple Creek Estates), a park under two names with no age rule stated (Nicomen Park / Nicomen Village), and a gated park split into an adult side and a family section with no age number (Oasis Country Estates). "Mission mobile homes" is five different rule books, not one seniors market. Sources: the park entries in Section 2.
- **Pad rent figures on this feed do not agree with each other, because they are per-home listing text.** Mission Mobile Home Park shows $650 and $671.29 on two listings; the wider feed shows $440–$808/month across parks. There is no park-wide verified pad rent for any park in the research file — the tenancy agreement for the specific pad is the only settling document. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85367/FullPublic.pdf?i=979581669 and https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-mobile-homes.html
- **Assignment consent is the hidden closing step in every park sale.** A house sale closes on title; a park-home sale also needs the landlord's consent to assign the site tenancy (MHPTA s.28), with the park rules handed to the buyer before consent is even requested. Build that timeline into the contract, or the home is bought and the pad is not yet the buyer's. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **The Hatzic 55+ park sits on floodplain-adjacent ground.** Mission Mobile Home Park is in Hatzic, on the Hatzic Prairie side of the city behind the Dewdney Dyke system — the trade for the park's price point is dyke-protected lowland and car dependence, so confirm the parcel's flood status and the pad tenancy terms together. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf and https://www.cotala.com/tours/85367/FullPublic.pdf?i=979581669
- **The corridor plan buyers may hear about does not exist as law yet.** The Lougheed Highway Corridor Plan — the highway every park in Section 2 except the Wilson Street parks fronts or sits near — is in preparation, NOT adopted. Park redevelopment speculation priced on an adopted corridor plan is priced on a plan that has not been adopted. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies
- **Mission's newest seniors growth is not in parks at all.** The Bellevue (32998 1st Ave) is a brand-new downtown seniors rental building, and the strata 55+ supply is still the two late-1980s complexes, Cherry Lane (1989) and Luther Place (1988) — the park route (Mission Mobile Home Park) is the rural-priced alternative, with tenancy law instead of strata law as its rule book. https://thebellevue.ca/ and https://www.estateblock.com/mission/15-32165-seventh-ave-49679
