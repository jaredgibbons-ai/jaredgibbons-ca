## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. For a presale buyer this bites at the disclosure statement: a future 55+ community must carry a valid 55+ bylaw in its filed documents — presentation-centre language is not a bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it;** exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rental bylaws remain possible. A presale's marketing claim about "investor-friendly" or "owner-occupier only" product has to be read against that law and the disclosure statement, not the brochure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Seven-day rescission (REDMA):** under the Real Estate Development Marketing Act, a purchaser may rescind by written notice to the developer within seven days after the later of the date the purchase agreement was made and the date the developer obtained the purchaser's receipt for the Disclosure Statement (s.21); the developer must give each purchaser a copy of the filed Disclosure Statement, and any filed amendment, before entering into the agreement (s.15). https://www.bcfsa.ca/industry-resources/real-estate-developer-resources/real-estate-development-marketing-act-faqs
- **Property transfer tax — Newly Built Home Exemption:** effective April 1, 2024, the full-exemption threshold is a fair market value of $1,100,000, with a partial exemption phasing out over the $50,000 range above it and complete elimination at $1,150,000, for a qualifying principal-residence purchase; the buyer does not need to be a first-time buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Federal first-time buyers' GST rebate:** under Bill C-4 (royal assent March 13, 2026, per CRA guidance), an eligible first-time buyer receives a rebate of 100% of the GST (or federal part of HST) on a newly constructed home valued up to $1 million, gradually reduced between $1 million and $1.5 million, with no rebate at $1.5 million or more. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Deposit structures are project-specific and UNVERIFIED here.** The live page's range ("most Langley projects ask for 10% to 20% in total, paid in two to four instalments") and its worked-cost table are the page's own presentation; no developer source for any named project was opened in the research pass, so neither is adopted as fact. Confirm any project's deposit schedule in its disclosure statement. https://jaredgibbons.ca/new-construction-presales-in-langley-bc.html
- **Warranty: UNVERIFIED as stated.** The live page's statement that new homes in BC carry 2-5-10 warranty coverage was not opened on a BC Housing or other official source in the research pass, so it is not carried as verified here. https://jaredgibbons.ca/new-construction-presales-in-langley-bc.html
- **FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED in this file.** No live CRA page stating the current limits was read in this pass, so no limit figure is stated — check the current CRA pages before running a first-time buyer's numbers.
- **Township SSMUH / Transit-Oriented Area frame for small-lot new product:** eligible Township lots may hold 3 units at 280 m² or less or 4 units at 280–4,050 m² under Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024), and the Willowbrook Transit-Oriented Area on 196 Street carries provincial minimum allowable densities of 8–20 storeys within 800 m — the planning frame behind station-area presale density, not a project fact. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
