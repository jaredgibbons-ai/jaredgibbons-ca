# Shaughnessy — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Shaughnessy is the City of Vancouver's original prestige residential district, on the ridge between roughly West 16th Avenue (north) and West 37th/41st Avenues (south), Oak Street (east) and Arbutus/East Boulevard (west). The GVR board sub-area name is simply "Shaughnessy." (Area research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md, Section 1.)
- First Shaughnessy — the section north of King Edward Avenue — is a designated Heritage Conservation Area. The City defines its boundaries as West 16th, Marpole, Wolfe and Richelieu Avenues to the north, Arbutus Street to the west, West King Edward Avenue to the south, and the west lane of Oak Street to the east. Council adopted the HCA Official Development Plan (By-law No. 11349) on September 29, 2015. South of King Edward are Second and Third Shaughnessy, without HCA status. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- There is no commercial strip inside Shaughnessy — the First Shaughnessy District Schedule's stated intent is to protect the district's estate residential character. The nearest village shopping is Kerrisdale (West 41st Avenue) and South Granville (Granville Street); the nearest Park Board community centre is Kerrisdale Community Centre, 5851 West Boulevard. http://former.vancouver.ca/commsvcs/BYLAWS/zoning/fsd.pdf and https://Vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/community-and-cultural-centres.aspx
- Transit: Shaughnessy has no rapid transit station and limited in-neighbourhood bus service; the frequent corridors around it are King Edward Avenue (#25), West 41st Avenue (#41), West 49th Avenue (#49) and Arbutus Street/West Boulevard (#14), and the Arbutus Greenway runs along the neighbourhood's western edge. https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/explore-the-arbutus-greenway.aspx?mgid=123893
- Schools: Shaughnessy Elementary (4250 Marguerite St) is the in-area public elementary school and Prince of Wales Secondary (2250 Eddington Dr) the public secondary; the one catchment actually run was 4250 Marguerite Street → Shaughnessy Elementary / Prince of Wales Secondary. Independent schools in the area include York House School (4176 Alexandra St) and Little Flower Academy (4195 Alexandra St). Catchments for other addresses: UNCONFIRMED. https://vancouver.ca/election/2026/list-of-all-voting-places.aspx and https://www.yorkhouse.ca/about
- VanDusen Botanical Garden (5251 Oak St at West 37th Ave, 55 acres) sits on the sub-area's southeast edge — the recreation anchor beside the district's only strata pocket, Shaughnessy Place on Cartier Street. https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/vandusen-botanical-garden.aspx
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates: nowhere inside the boundary. No verified 55+ ownership, rental, assisted-living or long-term care building with an address inside Shaughnessy was found in any source checked. Every seniors facility that carries a Shaughnessy association sits outside the boundary — the care campus at West 41st and Oak is on the district's south boundary line (see Section 2, checked list), and the retirement residence that markets to Shaughnessy residents is in Arbutus Ridge. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. This matters in Shaughnessy specifically because older building sources for its few strata complexes (Shaughnessy Place II, The Shaughnessy) still print "rentals not allowed" — that wording is stale under current law. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575**, and First Shaughnessy has its own district schedule under it: the schedule's intent is protecting the district's estate character and conserving protected heritage property while allowing multiple conversion dwellings, infill and secondary suites; multiple dwellings are permitted only on sites without protected heritage property, and the maximum floor space ratio is 0.45 where a protected heritage property is conserved, otherwise 0.25 plus 139 m² to a maximum floor area of 911 m². http://former.vancouver.ca/commsvcs/BYLAWS/zoning/fsd.pdf
- **First Shaughnessy Heritage Conservation Area (By-law No. 11349, adopted September 29, 2015):** protected (pre-1940) heritage properties can only be removed from the protected list through a Council process following a heritage character and value assessment; the HCA framework gives owners of protected homes additional development options — coach house, multiple conversion dwelling and infill buildings — as incentives to conserve the heritage house. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx and http://council.vancouver.ca/20150721/documents/phea-QandA.pdf
- **Subdivision minimums in First Shaughnessy:** the City's Subdivision By-law, Schedule A, sets a minimum parcel width of 100 ft (30.48 m) and minimum parcel area of 13,000 sq ft (1,207.7 m²) — which is why estate lots here cannot simply be split to create smaller seniors product. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/scheda.pdf
- **Outside the HCA, the R1-1 multiplex rules apply:** By-law No. 13817 replaced the RS districts with R1-1, allowing up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units — but the interaction between R1-1 and the First Shaughnessy District Schedule must be confirmed property by property. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **City of Vancouver Seniors Housing Strategy (approved July 23, 2024):** the City's first seniors housing strategy, with a Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy (last amended May 19, 2026) to enable more seniors housing across all neighbourhoods and a Resident Relocation Policy adding protections for seniors in community care and assisted living residences in the event of a redevelopment. No Shaughnessy seniors project under this policy was found in the sources checked: UNCONFIRMED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/seniors-housing-strategy-address-needs-of-older-adults-july-2024.aspx and https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-rezoning-seniors-housing.pdf
- **Additional school tax is a live carrying cost at Shaughnessy price points:** BC charges 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% effective January 1, 2027 — on top of City taxes (2026 advance taxes due February 3, 2026; main taxes July 3, 2026; 5% penalty on unpaid balances). https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Shaughnessy?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata building inside the sub-area; the housing stock is almost entirely freehold detached estate homes, and its one well-known strata pocket (Shaughnessy Place, 4900 Cartier St) has no age restriction in any source checked. A 55+ buyer in Shaughnessy is buying a regular house or a regular strata unit. Sources: Section 2 above, and the area research file, Section 5.
- **Q: Is Shaughnessy Place a seniors complex? The gate and the pool make it look like one.** No source says so. Listings describe a gated community with 24-hour guarded entry, a saltwater outdoor pool, gym and meeting room, and pets welcome — amenities, not an age bylaw. Building sources for Place II record pet and (stale) rental restrictions only. Confirm on the Form B and the strata's bylaws before assuming any age rule. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3118452-311-4900-cartier-street-vancouver-british-columbia-v6m4h2-1399000/brochure
- **Q: Where do Shaughnessy residents actually go for retirement living?** On the evidence of the operators' own pages, outside the boundary: Amica Arbutus Manor (2125 Eddington Dr) says some of its residents join from Shaughnessy, and the Louis Brier / Weinberg campus at West 41st and Oak provides long-term care and assisted living immediately south of the district. Both are rental/care, not purchases. https://www.amica.ca/arbutus-manor and https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/75923/
- **Q: Can a Shaughnessy estate home be converted into seniors housing or a care home?** Not as of right in First Shaughnessy: the district schedule's intent is estate residential character, multiple dwellings are permitted only on sites without protected heritage property, and the City's Seniors Housing Rezoning Policy is a rezoning route, not a permission. Any such conversion is a rezoning question for the specific property: UNCONFIRMED for any given lot without a City answer. http://former.vancouver.ca/commsvcs/BYLAWS/zoning/fsd.pdf and https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-rezoning-seniors-housing.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Could we both live in a 55+ strata elsewhere in Vancouver?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Older listings for Shaughnessy strata say "rentals not allowed." Can I rent out a unit I buy there now?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, whether or not the strata amended its bylaws. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals. The "rentals not allowed" lines in older building sources for this area are stale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I tear down a pre-1940 Shaughnessy house and build a smaller, accessible home?** Not as of right if the home is on the HCA's protected list: removal from the protected list requires a heritage character and value assessment and Council approval at a public hearing. The HCA instead rewards conservation with coach house, multiple conversion and infill options. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/bulletins/bulletin-first-shaughnessy-heritage-conservation-area-procedure-for-review-of-protected-heritage.pdf and http://council.vancouver.ca/20150721/documents/phea-QandA.pdf
- **Q: What will a live-in caregiver arrangement look like in a 55+ building, if I buy elsewhere and bring help into a Shaughnessy house instead?** In a strata 55+ building, a live-in caregiver is one of the built-in exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 — the caregiver may reside with the specified resident. In a Shaughnessy detached house there is no strata at all, so no age bylaw applies; the relevant rules are the district schedule's suite/infill permissions, not strata law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How do I get around without driving, living in Shaughnessy?** Plan on buses from the edges: the frequent corridors are King Edward Avenue (#25), West 41st Avenue (#41), West 49th Avenue (#49) and Arbutus/West Boulevard (#14). There is no rapid transit station in or beside the district, and limited in-neighbourhood bus service — a material point for a senior buyer giving up a car. https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/explore-the-arbutus-greenway.aspx?mgid=123893
- **Q: What extra tax should I model on a Shaughnessy purchase?** BC's additional school tax: 0.2% on the residential assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% from January 1, 2027 — a five-figure annual line on a high-value assessment, on top of City property taxes. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The district's boundary does real work in seniors searches.** Three different facilities trade on the Shaughnessy name or a Shaughnessy-looking address — Amica Arbutus Manor on Eddington Drive (Arbutus Ridge, per its operator), the Louis Brier campus at West 41st and Oak (Oakridge, per its operator), and Shaughnessy Seniors Community (Port Coquitlam) — and none of them is inside Shaughnessy. A 55+ page for this area that lists any of them as in-area stock would be wrong. Sources: Section 2 checked list.
- **Shaughnessy is one of the few Vancouver sub-areas with literally no strata seniors product to inherit.** The research file records strata as minimal and edge-only, and the First Shaughnessy District Schedule caps floor space at 0.45 FSR with a conserved protected heritage property (otherwise 0.25 plus 139 m², to 911 m² maximum) — the zoning framework that preserves the estates is the same framework that keeps purpose-built seniors buildings out. http://former.vancouver.ca/commsvcs/BYLAWS/zoning/fsd.pdf
- **The one strata pocket sits beside a 55-acre garden, not a shopping street.** Shaughnessy Place (4900 Cartier St) is described in listings as set on the edge of VanDusen Botanical Garden (5251 Oak St, 55 acres, jointly operated by the Vancouver Park Board and the Vancouver Botanical Gardens Association) — parkside and gated, but a general-market complex with no age rule in any source. https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/vandusen-botanical-garden.aspx and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3118452-311-4900-cartier-street-vancouver-british-columbia-v6m4h2-1399000/brochure
- **"First Shaughnessy" is a legal boundary, not a prestige label.** Only the area north of King Edward Avenue inside the City-defined HCA boundaries carries the heritage protections and the district schedule; a listing south of King Edward marketed as "First Shaughnessy" is under different rules. Verify the boundary before pricing heritage restrictions — or heritage incentives — into a purchase. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **Aging in place here usually means adapting an estate home, not moving within the area.** Because there is no in-area retirement residence, assisted living or long-term care, a Shaughnessy senior's local ladder is: stay in the house (the HCA permits coach house, multiple conversion and infill options on protected lots, which can house family or a caregiver), or leave the district for the facilities in the checked list. That is the practical consequence of the zero-stock finding in Section 2. http://council.vancouver.ca/20150721/documents/phea-QandA.pdf
- **Parcel-level flood status was not verified in the research pass.** The research file notes Shaughnessy sits on the Point Grey ridge away from the shorelines, but flood status must be confirmed per property with the City of Vancouver — do not treat the ridge location as a flood clearance. (Area research file, Section 4 and UNVERIFIED list.)
