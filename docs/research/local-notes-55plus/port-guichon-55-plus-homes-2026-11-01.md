# Port Guichon — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Port Guichon is a Paragon (MLS) sub-area name for the historic harbour neighbourhood in west Ladner, around River Road West and 46A Street; the name is the name of the School District 37 elementary school in the area and of the historic Port Guichon neighbourhood named in the Canadian Register of Historic Places entry for the Victoria Terminal Railway and Ferry Company Station. https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=6344
- Port Guichon Elementary is physically in the sub-area at 4381 46A Street; the school's own history says it was built as Fairview Elementary and later renamed for the Guichon family. http://pg.deltasd.bc.ca/
- The sub-area sits under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024); Delta Manor and the Ladner village core are to the east. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- Ladner Harbour Park, 4905 McNeelys Way, is the City-listed park on this shore of Ladner. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/ladner-harbour-park
- Ladner Leisure Centre (4600 Clarence Taylor Crescent), Ladner Pioneer Library (4683 51st Street) and McKee Seniors Recreation Centre (5155 47 Avenue) serve the sub-area from central Ladner; Delta Hospital (5800 Mountain View Blvd, 24/7 emergency) sits beside Ladner Exchange. http://delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/ladner-leisure-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- Transit: TransLink's Ladner Exchange bay map lists route 618 (the Ladner loop) calling at 46A Street at River Road — the Port Guichon stop; route 601 runs from Ladner Exchange to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line). http://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/exchange_maps/ladner.pdf
- Flood context — the defining fact of this sub-area: Port Guichon is low land on the Fraser River side of Ladner, behind the City's dike system (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations, ~538 km of drainage). In nearby Ladner approvals on Chisholm Street the City required Section 219 flood-proofing covenants and statutory rights-of-way so the dike can be raised to 5.5 metres. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/104061/
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates in this sub-area: it does not. There is no verified 55+ ownership, rental, care or subsidized stock inside Port Guichon (Section 2). Ladner's verified 55+ ownership stock is Delta Green (Ladner Elementary sub-area) and South Pointe (Neilsen Grove sub-area); its subsidized 55+ rentals are on Evergreen Lane and 48 Avenue in central Ladner.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. In Port Guichon this matters mainly as a checking rule: with no verified 55+ strata in the sub-area, any "55+" claim for a local building has to be proven from the registered bylaws, not a listing aggregator. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. Note the practical consequence at River West: listing data records rentals allowed, which is consistent with the province-wide rule and says nothing about age. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** assigns zoning parcel by parcel; zones confirmed in west Ladner include RS1, RS5, RS6 and RS7 in the urban area and A1 Agriculture toward the west edge, where the A1 zone sets an 8-hectare minimum lot area for parcels created by subdivision. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Seniors congregate housing in Delta's zoning:** the Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone added to Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 in 2024 lists "seniors congregate housing" as a permitted principal use for six-storey apartment development under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation. No RA6 or seniors congregate project in Port Guichon appears in any source checked. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")**, adopted July 8, 2024, is Delta's consolidated OCP covering Ladner. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/ and http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Flood covenants are a title item here, not fine print.** In Ladner approvals on Chisholm Street the City required Section 219 flood-proofing covenants and statutory rights-of-way allowing future dike raising to 5.5 metres; the City directs owners to DeltaMap for streamside areas, diked channels and parcel-level flood information. Any Port Guichon purchase — at any age — should include a title search for flood covenants and a City check of flood construction requirements. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/104061/ and https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection
- **Suites and SSMUH:** the RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use to a single detached dwelling, and Delta's SSMUH amendments allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — the realistic "age in place with a caregiver or family suite" route in a sub-area with no 55+ buildings. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Property tax deferment at 55+:** BC's regular deferment program lets a homeowner 55 or older during the current year defer annual property taxes on a principal residence as a provincial loan; Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026 (5% penalty after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026), and the City tax page links the deferment and Home Owner Grant programs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing I can buy in Port Guichon?** No verified stock. The sub-area's only condo building, River West, failed age-restriction verification (Section 2), and no other strata, rental, care or subsidized 55+ complex inside Port Guichon was verified from any public source. Ladner's verified 55+ purchases are Delta Green (51 Street, Ladner Elementary sub-area) and South Pointe (Neilsen Grove). https://www.estateblock.com/ladner/202-4743-w-river-rd-52494
- **Q: A website says River West is 55+. Why don't you list it?** Because the only source for the claim is one aggregator page with a blank age field, while the building's current listing data records pet and rental bylaws and no age bylaw. Under BC law a 55+ restriction lives in the strata's registered bylaws — if a seller produces those bylaws showing a 55+ bylaw, the position changes; until then it is UNCONFIRMED and treated as general-market. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If we buy in a Ladner 55+ building elsewhere, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: If there's no 55+ stock, how do older buyers age in place in Port Guichon?** Through the detached stock itself: a secondary suite is an accessory use in the RS1 zone, and Delta's SSMUH amendments add duplex, coach house and garden suite options where the zoning applies — the suite-for-a-caregiver or family arrangement is the local substitute for a 55+ building. Confirm the parcel's zone on the Bylaw No. 7600 zoning maps first. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q: What about flood risk and flood covenants on title?** This is the river-side edge of Ladner. The City has required Section 219 flood-proofing covenants and rights-of-way for future dike raising (to 5.5 m) in Ladner approvals, and directs owners to DeltaMap for parcel-level flood information. Search title and ask the City about flood construction requirements before subjects come off — on any purchase here, not just a strata one. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/104061/ and https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection
- **Q: Which bus serves Port Guichon?** TransLink's Ladner Exchange bay map lists route 618 calling at 46A Street at River Road; from Ladner Exchange, route 601 runs to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line), and the free Delta Seniors Bus (home-based at McKee Seniors Recreation Centre) serves seniors across Delta. http://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/exchange_maps/ladner.pdf and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2021-06/SocialServicesInventory%20CS.pdf
- **Q: Where is care if I need it later?** In Ladner, Augustine House (3820 Arthur Dr) offers independent and assisted living plus care, including a Fraser Health assisted living program at 55+; Delta Hospital (5800 Mountain View Blvd) is the community hospital with 24/7 emergency care. Both are outside this sub-area, in Ladner proper. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/augustine-house and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **Q: Can I defer property taxes at 55+ on a Port Guichon house?** Yes, if it is your principal residence — BC's regular deferment program opens at 55 or older during the current year (also surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The sub-area's name is a ferry-landing name, not a marketing name.** The Canadian Register of Historic Places places the Victoria Terminal Railway and Ferry Company Station in the historic Port Guichon neighbourhood, and the elementary school (4381 46A St) was built as Fairview Elementary and renamed for the Guichon family — the "Port Guichon" identity predates the MLS sub-area by a century. https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=6344 and http://pg.deltasd.bc.ca/
- **River West had a major building repair early in its life.** A listing write-up for the building records a building enclosure rehabilitation completed in 1999 — nine years after its 1990 construction. For a 55+ buyer comparing River West against newer Ladner stock, the depreciation report and that repair history are the documents that matter. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3058563-304-4753-w-river-road-ladner-bc-v4k-1r9
- **The same building is filed under two different sub-areas by listing sources.** River West units appear as "Port Guichon" in some listings and "Ladner Elementary" in others. A buyer searching only one sub-area name will miss units; a seller's agent should check both. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3058563-304-4753-w-river-road-ladner-bc-v4k-1r9
- **The west edge changes tenure entirely.** Zoning at the west edge of the community runs to A1 Agriculture with an 8-hectare minimum lot area for parcels created by subdivision — the lots west of the harbour grid are farm parcels, not subdividable downsizer lots, whatever a listing implies. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Streamside rules cover the ditches, not just the river.** Any Delta property within 30 m of a stream — including sloughs and drainage ditches, which cross this sub-area — is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, which can limit additions and coach houses. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Catchments are address-based even here.** For the sample address 4381 46A Street (the school's own address), the SD37 School Locator returns Port Guichon Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12); every other address must be checked in the locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Ladner's seniors services are a bus ride east, and the bus is free.** The Delta Seniors Bus offers a free transportation service for seniors in Delta, home-based at McKee Seniors Recreation Centre (5155 47 Ave) — the practical link between a Port Guichon house and the McKee / Ladner Leisure Centre / library cluster in central Ladner. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2021-06/SocialServicesInventory%20CS.pdf
