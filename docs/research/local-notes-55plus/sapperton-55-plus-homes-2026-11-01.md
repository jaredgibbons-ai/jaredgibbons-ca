# Sapperton — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Sapperton is the historic neighbourhood at New Westminster's northeast end, on the slope above the Fraser River between McBride Boulevard and the Coquitlam boundary at Brunette Creek, taking in Royal Columbian Hospital, Sapperton and Braid SkyTrain stations, Hume Park and the Brewery District; the City also uses "McBride-Sapperton" as a neighbourhood name, and "Sapperton" is the board (GVR) sub-area name in the SnapStats GVR September 2026 report. Exact board boundary roads are UNVERIFIED. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php
- The name is from the Columbia Detachment of Royal Engineers' 1858 camp — "Sappers' Town," after the Sapper rank — which stayed after the corps disbanded. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Sapperton_Plaza_History.pdf
- East Columbia Street is the main street and commercial strip (Sapperton Plaza at East Columbia and Knox Street dates to the 1977–78 civic revitalization plan); Brunette Avenue, McBride Boulevard and North Road are the other arterials, with Highway 1 access at the Brunette Interchange. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Sapperton_Plaza_History.pdf
- Two Expo Line stations serve the area — Sapperton Station, across from the hospital, and Braid Station at the east end — and TransLink route 109 (New Westminster Station / Lougheed Station) runs the East Columbia corridor. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf
- Royal Columbian Hospital, 330 East Columbia Street, is in Sapperton beside Sapperton Station; its Jim Pattison Acute Care Tower (10 storeys, 388 beds) opened to patients on May 31, 2026. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2026/May/Opening-the-new-Jim-Pattison-Acute-Care-Tower-at-Royal-Columbian-Hospital
- Schools are School District 40, address-based: a locator run for 331 Richmond Street returned Skwo:wech Elementary (the 2022 school at that address), École Glenbrook Middle and New Westminster Secondary. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- Where 55+ and seniors housing concentrates: there is no 55+ strata cluster. The seniors-specific buildings sit at the area's edges — Hospital Street beside the hospital (Cedar Manor, mixed housing), Ross Drive on the former Woodlands grounds at the west edge (Victoria Heights assisted living), and Colborne Street at the McBride-Sapperton north edge (Dunwood Place, flagged below). The Brewery District condo stock across from Sapperton Station is general-market. Sources: Section 2 below.

The Brewery District towers and Sapperton townhomes are general-market strata, and the co-op on Ward Street is a general housing co-op. Everything verified below is rental, assisted living or non-profit housing — none of it buyable ownership.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — since May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City OCP: Our City 2041, Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 7925, 2017.** The Sapperton and Braid station areas are one of the City's Frequent Transit Development Areas, directing transit-supportive density around both stations; the 38.35-acre site at 97 Braid Street is designated the Sapperton Green Transit-Oriented Mixed-Use Community. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001.** Houses sit in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series, s.310 — RS-1 minimum site 557.40 sq. m. / 6,000 sq. ft., FSR 0.5), with secondary suites and laneway/carriage houses regulated in the same bylaw; townhouse form is regulated under districts RT-A and RT-B where designated. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Secondary suites:** most single detached dwellings and some duplexes in New Westminster are zoned to permit a secondary suite, which must meet Zoning Bylaw s.190.29 and the Building Code (one suite per house; a property may have both a suite and a laneway/carriage house) — the in-place option for housing a parent or caregiver. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Secondary_Suite_Guide_August_2025.pdf
- **Townhouse program update (2025–26):** OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 8547, 2025 and Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 8524, 2025 create RT-A and RT-B townhouse districts, permit secondary suites in townhouse units, and pre-zone 570 properties designated Townhouse (RT) — future ground-oriented supply around Sapperton, none of it age-restricted in the sources. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/2026_Jan_26_Bylaw_8547_and_Bylaw_8524_Public_Hearing.pdf
- **Flood:** the City's floodplain study places the industrial land along the Brunette River in Floodplain Zone 4, below the Flood Construction Level — the risk pocket is the low Braid-side land, not the residential slope; check the exact lot with the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo or townhouse I can buy in Sapperton?** No verified one. The Brewery District towers (The Columbia, The Sapperton) and Sapperton Living townhomes are general-market strata with no age restriction in any source, and the Ward Street co-op is a general housing co-op. Sapperton's seniors stock is assisted living and non-profit rental. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children cared for before 19 are also exempt categories. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Yes — ownership is not age-restricted; the bylaw governs who may reside, and a 55+ parent can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Brewery District condo?** Long-term rental bans are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals by bylaw — check the specific strata's bylaws on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What care is actually in Sapperton if I need help later?** One registered assisted-living residence at the area's west edge — Victoria Heights, 230 Ross Drive (Good Samaritan Canada, class SN) — plus Royal Columbian Hospital across from Sapperton Station. There is no long-term care home verified inside Sapperton itself; the city's long-term care sits elsewhere in New Westminster — UNCONFIRMED for any Sapperton address. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?f%5B0%5D=city%3ANew%20Westminster&f%5B1%5D=city%3ANorth%20Vancouver&f%5B2%5D=residence_type%3ASeniors%20and%20Persons%20with%20Disabilities&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=asc
- **Q: Is the co-op on Ward Street a seniors co-op?** No source says so. Sapperton Terrace Housing Co-operative (318 Ward St, 51 units) is listed by the City as a co-op with mostly family units and by CHF BC with general 1- and 2-bedroom homes and a share purchase — no age rule. Co-op membership is also not strata ownership: members hold membership in the co-operative, not title to a unit. https://newwestcity.ca/database/rte/Housing%20web%20update%20-%20Non-Market%20details.pdf
- **Q: Sapperton Green — will it bring 55+ homes?** UNCONFIRMED. The Council-endorsed master plan concept for 97 Braid Street proposes roughly 4,700 homes including at least 1,122,000 sq. ft. of secured market rental and a minimum 255,000 sq. ft. of affordable housing, plus a community centre and 7.9 acres of open space — no age-restricted component is stated, and construction timing is UNVERIFIED. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWDP_AGENDA_2020_12_08(1).pdf
- **Q: Is Sapperton in a floodplain?** The residential slope sits above the Fraser; the City's study puts only the low Brunette River / Braid-side industrial land in Floodplain Zone 4, below the Flood Construction Level. Price the pocket, not the neighbourhood, and check the exact lot. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Q: What transit do I get?** Two Expo Line stations (Sapperton and Braid) plus route 109 on East Columbia Street — the strongest transit of any neighbourhood in this batch, and the reason the station areas are a Frequent Transit Development Area in the OCP. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The hospital just rebuilt itself next door.** The Jim Pattison Acute Care Tower at Royal Columbian Hospital opened May 31, 2026 — 388 beds, new emergency department and main entrance — as Phase 2 of a redevelopment whose Phases 2 and 3 together are estimated at nearly $2 billion. That supports demand near Sapperton Station and also means hospital traffic and parkade effects have to be checked street by street. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2026/May/Opening-the-new-Jim-Pattison-Acute-Care-Tower-at-Royal-Columbian-Hospital
- **Sapperton's strata market is small and slow, hospital or not.** In September 2026 the sub-area listed 33 condos & townhomes and sold 2 (6% sales ratio), and listed 10 detached homes with 1 sale (10%). Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.
- **The Brewery District is built on a brewery.** Wesgroup's master plan sits on the former Labatt brewery site across from Sapperton Station: 1.4 million sq. ft. of buildable area across eight buildings, seven complete, including the TransLink and Transit Police head office at 290 East Columbia Street. The old brewery's wrought iron gates now mark Sapperton Plaza. https://wesgroup.ca/brewery-district/ and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Sapperton_Plaza_History.pdf
- **Sapperton Green would also bring a utility bill most buyers have never seen.** The 97 Braid Street plan is to connect to the City's Sapperton District Energy System — a City utility cost future owners there should ask about before pricing the plan's amenities. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Council_Feb_24_2020___Sapperton_Green_Update.pdf
- **The elementary school is new and its name is a word, not a person.** Skwo:wech Elementary at 331 Richmond Street opened June 6, 2022, replacing the seismically unsafe Richard McBride Elementary; the $34.98 million school serves 400+ K–5 students with an attached child care centre, and its name is the halq'eméylem word for sturgeon. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2022ECC0041-000900
- **Taxes:** New Westminster 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 3, 2026. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php
- **New Westminster is dense and getting denser at the stations.** The 2021 Census counted 78,916 people in the city at 5,052 people per sq. km — the second-densest municipality in Canada — and the OCP directs the next density to the Sapperton and Braid station areas, which is the context for Sapperton Green's 4,700 proposed homes. https://pub-newwestcity.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=15356
