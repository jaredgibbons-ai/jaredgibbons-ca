# Canterbury (West Vancouver) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Canterbury is a residential enclave on the British Properties side of the District of West Vancouver, on the hillside above Ambleside and the Upper Levels Highway. It is a board/MLS name, not a District planning name: "Canterbury" does not appear as a named neighbourhood in the District's Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 4985, 2018), whose commercial structure is the Ambleside Municipal Town Centre, Park Royal, and the village centres of Dundarave, Caulfeild Village and Horseshoe Bay. Street-by-street boundaries: UNVERIFIED — no District source read publishes them. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01), and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- The schools in Canterbury's orbit are Chartwell Elementary (K–7), 1300 Chartwell Drive, described by SD45 as situated in the British Properties overlooking Vancouver and Burrard Inlet, and Sentinel Secondary (Grades 8–12), 1250 Chartwell Drive, beside it; Westcot Elementary (K–7), 760 Westcot Road, sits at the foot of the British Properties to the west. School District 45 catchments are address-based — check each address in the SD45 locator. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/chartwell/ and https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/
- Everything a senior uses day to day is downhill: Park Royal (Marine Drive and Taylor Way) is the OCP-recognized regional shopping centre, Ambleside is the Municipal Town Centre, the West Vancouver Community Centre and Aquatic Centre is at 2121 Marine Drive, and West Vancouver Memorial Library is at 1950 Marine Drive. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/facilities/community-centre and https://westvancouver.ca/arts-culture/west-vancouver-memorial-library
- Transit: TransLink's system map shows the British Properties served by West Vancouver Blue Bus route 254 (British Properties) from Park Royal Exchange, with route 250 on Marine Drive below the hillside running between Horseshoe Bay, Dundarave, Park Royal and downtown Vancouver via the Lions Gate Bridge. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/september-2026/north-and-west-vancouver-map.pdf
- Where the seniors housing actually is: **not in Canterbury.** No seniors housing of any type — no 55+ strata, no retirement residence, no assisted living, no subsidized seniors building — was verified inside Canterbury. All of West Vancouver's verified seniors housing is downhill in and around Ambleside (Duchess Avenue, Marine Drive, 21st Street, Inglewood Avenue), roughly a 5–10 minute drive below the hillside. Sources: Section 2 below and the Assisted Living Registry's West Vancouver list. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=may5&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&name=&address=&order=alr_legal_operator_name&sort=desc&f%5B0%5D=city%3AWest%20Vancouver

A 55+ buyer in Canterbury is buying a regular detached house with no age bylaw, and ageing in place here means a house, a suite or a coach house — the care and rental products are all downhill in Ambleside.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 took effect. In Canterbury this is mostly academic — there is no strata stock in the sub-area for a bylaw to attach to — but it governs the Ambleside buildings a Canterbury downsizer would buy into. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. The caregiver exemption is the one that matters on a Canterbury house lot too, in planning terms: it is how a live-in caregiver arrangement is contemplated in BC's age-restriction framework. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010, Section 200 (RS zones).** Minimum site areas run from 488 m² (RS5) to 8,094 m² (RS1), with the larger RS1/RS2 minimums associated with the British Properties benches; secondary suites and a detached secondary suite are permitted uses in the single-family zones — the suite/coach house is Canterbury's real ageing-in-place mechanism, for a parent, a caregiver or rental income. The zone for a specific lot is shown on the District's Zoning Map. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **British Properties covenants:** whether a Canterbury lot carries building-scheme or covenant restrictions beyond District zoning is UNVERIFIED — it sits on the individual title, not in the bylaw. Pull the title before marketing a rebuild or a coach house. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- **Hazard permits:** the District requires a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area (within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface) and an Environmental Development Permit for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank — both can apply on Canterbury's hillside and creek lots, including to a new coach house. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Provincial density directive:** on July 21, 2025 the Province directed West Vancouver to amend its OCP to provide increased density in the single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave by December 31, 2025. Canterbury sits above Ambleside; whether its lowest streets fall inside the "adjacent" areas is UNVERIFIED and must be checked per property. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698
- **Property taxes:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes
- **Additional school tax:** at British Properties price points this will commonly apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What 55+ homes can I actually buy in Canterbury?** None that any source verifies. There is no age-restricted strata, no retirement residence and no seniors rental inside Canterbury — it is a detached-house hillside. A 55+ buyer here buys a regular house; the District's actual 55+ purchase options are the Ambleside strata buildings downhill (Westview Terrace, Tudor Gardens, Lincoln Gardens). Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is there a Chartwell retirement residence near Canterbury?** No. Despite the neighbouring Chartwell neighbourhood and Chartwell Elementary sharing the name, Chartwell the operator has no West Vancouver residence; its only North Shore residence is Chartwell Churchill House at 150 West 29th St in North Vancouver. https://chartwell.com/bc/north-vancouver/churchill-house
- **Q: How would a senior age in place in Canterbury?** In the house itself: the RS zones permit a secondary suite and a detached secondary suite (coach house), which is the standard local arrangement for a caregiver or family member on the same lot. What a specific lot can hold depends on its zone and size, and a British Properties covenant, if any, sits on the title on top of zoning. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. If we downsize into a 55+ strata in Ambleside, can we both live there?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ building for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a BC age bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ strata?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What transit can a senior actually use from Canterbury?** Route 254 (British Properties) runs from Park Royal Exchange through the properties' road network; route 250 on Marine Drive below connects Dundarave, Park Royal and downtown Vancouver via the Lions Gate Bridge. Realistically this is a drive-to-the-bus neighbourhood — the services themselves are downhill. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/september-2026/north-and-west-vancouver-map.pdf
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver, is the Vancouver Coastal Health hospital serving the North Shore, including West Vancouver; the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at Suite 200, 221 West Esplanade, North Vancouver. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- **Q: If I need care later, can I stay in Canterbury?** Not in a care building — there is none. The West Vancouver ladder is downhill in Ambleside: independent and assisted-living rental residences (Hollyburn House, Amica West Vancouver, Westerleigh PARC, Kiwanis Manor) and long-term care at Inglewood Care Centre (725 Inglewood Ave, 230 publicly funded beds, allocated through Vancouver Coastal Health). https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/75989/
- **Q: Is Canterbury an official District neighbourhood?** No — it is a board/market name for part of the British Properties hillside. It does not appear as a named neighbourhood in the District's OCP, so City-side searches (permits, zoning history, plans) should be run under "British Properties" and the street address as well. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The name is market language, not municipal language.** Canterbury exists in MLS/board use and on real-estate pages; District documents file the same hillside under the British Properties. A buyer (or a page) that treats "Canterbury" as an official neighbourhood will miss plan and permit material filed the other way. Sources: the area research file (sources read 2026-11-01).
- **Canterbury's seniors story is a drive, not a walk.** The nearest verified seniors housing — Kiwanis Manor's below-market 55+ suites at 959 21st St and the rest of the Ambleside cluster — sits on the flat below the highway. Nothing in the sub-area itself softens that: no in-area park is confirmed on a District source, and no shopping or grocery inside Canterbury is confirmed either. https://kiwanisnorthshorehousing.org/seniors-housing/kiwanis-manor/
- **The coach house is the product.** Because the RS zones list a detached secondary suite among permitted uses, Canterbury lots routinely carry the one form of "55+ housing" the hillside actually produces — a self-contained second dwelling for a parent, caregiver or tenant. Its feasibility is lot-by-lot (zone, size, hazard permits, title covenants), never assumable from the street. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **The Inglewood expansion changes the downhill math.** The District's September 21, 2026 approval of the Inglewood Campus of Care redevelopment (725 Inglewood Ave, Ambleside) adds, in Phase 2, 200 independent living units and 161 seniors rental housing units on top of a new 364-bed long-term care facility in Phase 1 — the largest addition to West Vancouver seniors housing in this research, and the practical "next step" address for a Canterbury owner planning ahead. https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/development-applications/inglewood-campus-care
- **Two hazard permits shadow hillside projects.** Work within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface needs a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit, and work within 15 metres of a watercourse bank needs an Environmental Development Permit — both live issues on Canterbury's forested, creek-cut lots, and both add cost and time to the suite/coach-house route. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **The additional school tax is a standing line item here.** At Canterbury assessed values the Province's additional school tax (0.2% between $3M and $4M, 0.4% above $4M in 2026, rising Jan 1, 2027) belongs in the buyer's closing math from the first offer, not as an afterthought. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
