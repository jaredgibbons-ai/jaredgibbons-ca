## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 (Bill 44). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** People under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** legacy residents; live-in caregivers; and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rental bylaws are still allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Parks run on tenancy law, not strata law.** The three pad-rent parks in Section 2 are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — a sale is an assignment of the pad tenancy requiring the park owner's consent, and the strata exemptions above do not apply inside a park. Sea Ridge Bays is the exception: as a bare-land strata, strata law governs it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/surrey-mobile-homes-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **City zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000.** Secondary suites: one per dwelling unit, capped at 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite. Coach houses / garden suites: 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR). SSMUH: 3 units on lots under 280 m², 4 units over 280 m², up to 6 near a frequent bus stop. Zone names changed July 8, 2024 — former RF-12 / RF-12C became R4 and RF-SD became R6; older material using the old names is out of date. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900** (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — never cite the replaced Bylaw No. 18020 as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Financing tiers inside this band (CMHC):** homes $500,000 or less require a minimum 5% down payment; homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999 require 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% above it; homes at $1,500,000 or more require a minimum 20% down payment with mortgage loan insurance not available. This band straddles all three tiers. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Property transfer tax:** general rates are 1% of fair market value up to $200,000, 2% above $200,000 up to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000 — a purchase under $1,800,000 meets only the first two tiers. First-time buyer full exemption requires a value of $835,000 or less (partial to $860,000); newly built full exemption threshold is $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Short-term rentals:** owner-only hosting, owner must live at the property as primary residence, business licence $358 per year. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Property taxes:** due July 2, 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **First-home programs:** the First Home Savings Account and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits are UNVERIFIED in this file — no live CRA page stating the current limits was read in this pass, so no figure is published here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many Surrey homes under $1.8M are 55+?** No sourced number exists — SnapStats publishes no price-band cut, and no feed count for the 55+ subset is published. What is verified is the roster: 15 strata complexes, one membership complex, three pad-rent parks and one bare-land strata (Section 2). Whether any specific complex has a unit listed today, and at what price, changes with the feed. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/surrey-homes-under-1-8m.md
- **Q: Is this page really detached homes only?** No — despite the heading, the visible feed includes a condo, two half-duplexes and a townhome alongside detached houses. Check the property type on every card; strata product carries Form B, fees and bylaws a detached purchase does not. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-homes-under-1-8m.html
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — the provincial younger-spouse/partner exemption applies, along with the under-19 child, adult-child and caregiver exemptions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in a 55+ complex?** Ownership is not age-restricted; residency is. An under-55 buyer can own and can rent the unit to a 55+ tenant, or hold it for a 55+ parent to live in. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Yes for long-term rentals — rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022. The tenant must still meet the age bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. In a pad-rent park, the park's own rules govern instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Why does Sullivan Mews say no mortgages are possible?** Because it is not a strata — the purchase is a membership, so there is no strata title for a lender to register against, and its listings state a purchase must be cash. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure
- **Q: What down payment does a home in this band need?** It depends on the tier: 5% flat at $500,000 or less; 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% above it between $500,000 and $1,499,999; 20% minimum at $1,500,000 or more, where mortgage insurance is not available. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale pool?** Yes, mechanically — the next buyer must be 55+, exempt, or buying without living in the unit. Across a city-wide search that matters most in the small complexes (Lakeview Estates has 20 homes); Chelsea Gardens' scale is the counterweight. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Are the two Lakewood complexes the same place?** No. Lakewood Heights (6211 W Boundary Dr) and Lakewood Place (6061 W Boundary Dr) are separate complexes on the same street at Boundary Park — confirm which one a listing belongs to before comparing fees or bylaws. ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/surrey-condos-under-800k-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: Where do I check a school for a specific listing?** SD36 catchments are address-based; schools named in listing text are confirmed by name and address against the BC Ministry K-12 file, but no locator result for a feed address is on record — run the exact address in the district locator. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/surrey-homes-under-1-8m.md
