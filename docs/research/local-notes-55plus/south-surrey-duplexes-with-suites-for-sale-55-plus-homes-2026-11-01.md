## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT THIS HOME TYPE

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. Most homes in this feed are non-strata ("NO STRATA FEES" in card after card), and a non-strata half duplex has no strata age bylaw at all — the Act's framework only bites if the specific property is a strata lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-duplexes-with-suites-for-sale.html
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it** — where a strata 55+ bylaw does exist, the Province states people under 55 can own the lot but may not be able to reside in it, with Regulation 7.01 exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide** in strata since Nov 24, 2022; stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals. For the non-strata duplexes that dominate this feed, there is no strata rental bylaw to void — the rental questions are tenancy-law and suite-legality questions instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **What Surrey counts as a secondary suite (the rule this whole page turns on):** a dwelling unit that is an accessory use, with habitable floor area not exceeding 40% of the principal dwelling unit, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex residential building, and not a separate strata lot, under Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000. The current cap is the 40% rule; a "90 m²" cap is NOT current and is not stated as a rule anywhere in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Suite conditions (City of Surrey):** only one secondary suite is permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a secondary suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; one suite may be permitted in dwellings within small-scale multi-unit housing zones, including Comprehensive Development zones; and each application is reviewed for legal restrictions such as Land Use Contracts and Building Schemes. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **How a suite becomes legal (City of Surrey):** the owner must meet the conditions of zoning, hold the necessary building permits, meet the BC Building Code, and pay the secondary suite utility and service fees. The live page's own editorial (site copy) adds that suites must be registered with a Secondary Suite Declaration Form and states a Secondary Suite Service Fee of $893 per suite and a $1,000 fee for each unregistered suite the City becomes aware of — those dollar figures are site copy, UNVERIFIED against a City fee page in this research; the City's registration-check tool is its Secondary Suite Fee Web Inquiry. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-duplexes-with-suites-for-sale.html
- **SSMUH does not legalize an existing suite retroactively.** Surrey adopted its small-scale multi-unit housing zoning changes on July 8, 2024 — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — and the City states the new zoning does not retroactively legalize suites built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **A suite cannot be strata-titled or otherwise legally separated from the rest of the home** — the live page states this from the Province's Home Suite Home guide (site copy); if the half duplex is itself a strata lot, the strata's bylaws apply as well, and Surrey's suite pages do not address strata permission. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-duplexes-with-suites-for-sale.html
- **OCP — STANDING CORRECTION:** Surrey's current plan is the **Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900**, finally adopted June 15, 2026. Bylaw No. 18020 (2013) is superseded and is never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Property taxes (city-wide):** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **No duplex-only market figure exists.** SnapStats splits are Detached and Condos & Townhomes only; a half duplex is counted inside the board's ordinary splits depending on its tenure, so no figure in any program source is a duplex figure. September 2026 community context only: Detached median $1,628,000; Condos & Townhomes median $700,000 (S Surrey White Rock). ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ half duplex with a suite I can buy in South Surrey?** None verified. This page's feed is general-market product with no age bylaw stated for any card, and South Surrey's verified 55+ complexes (Peninsula Village, The Greens at Douglas, Bishop's Green, St. Martin's Lane, The Crescent) are condo/townhouse complexes — the duplex-style ones among them are strata townhouses with no suite claim in any source checked. A buyer who needs both "55+" and "suite" has to verify both facts separately, on documents, for the specific property. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: The listing says the suite is "legal." Can I rely on that?** No — the page itself warns that a listing noting a suite is not proof the suite has permits. Pull the City registration status (Secondary Suite Fee Web Inquiry) and the permit history; Surrey's SSMUH zoning does not legalize an unpermitted suite retroactively. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-duplexes-with-suites-for-sale.html and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: How big can the suite in a Surrey duplex be?** Its habitable floor area cannot exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit, it must sit within the duplex building, and it cannot be a separate strata lot. A suite over the 40% cap falls outside Surrey's definition whatever the listing text says. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Q: Can a half duplex have two suites — one for family, one to rent?** No. Only one secondary suite is permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, and a suite is not permitted inside a coach house or garden suite. A card advertising both a legal suite and a second guest suite needs the second unit's status explained in writing before subjects come off. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Q: Can I rent out the suite if I buy in a 55+ complex instead?** Two different rule books. In a non-strata duplex there is no strata bylaw at all. In a strata 55+ complex, long-term rental bans have been void since Nov 24, 2022 — but the tenant must still satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, short-term rentals can still be restricted, and whether the strata permits a suite inside a unit at all is UNCONFIRMED for every South Surrey 55+ complex checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — does that rule out the 55+ townhouse complexes?** No. Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident is exempt, as are legacy residents, live-in caregivers, children under 19 in the resident's care and certain adult children. Peninsula Village listings state the common local form expressly — "One person 55+." Confirm the registered bylaw wording on the Form B for the specific complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3098715-2500-152-street-67-surrey-bc-v4p-1m8/brochure
- **Q: Is there a "duplex median" to price against?** No. SnapStats publishes Detached and Condos & Townhomes splits only, and no duplex-only figure exists in program sources. Price the property from its own half-duplex comparables; the community medians are board context only. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, as recorded in the area research file.)
- **Q: What does "NO STRATA FEES" on these cards actually mean for a 55+ buyer?** That the typical card in this feed is not a strata lot — so there is no strata age bylaw, no Form B, and no strata rental rule; the buyer is purchasing an undivided-interest / fee-simple style half duplex (stated from card text, not from title searches) and carries the suite's legality risk personally. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-duplexes-with-suites-for-sale.html
- **Q: Where is the hospital for South Surrey?** Peace Arch Hospital, 15521 Russell Avenue, White Rock — Fraser Health community hospital, 24-hour emergency, surgery and maternity care. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/White-Rock/peace-arch-hospital
- **Q: Does a 55+ buyer get any tax break on a South Surrey duplex?** The provincial tools are age-based, not property-type-based: property tax deferment as a low-interest loan from age 55, and the additional home owner grant (total $845 in this region) from age 65, both for a principal residence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits
