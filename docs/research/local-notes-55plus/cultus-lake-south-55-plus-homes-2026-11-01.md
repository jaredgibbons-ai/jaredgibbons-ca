# Cultus Lake South — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

Jurisdiction note: the south end of Cultus Lake, including Lindell Beach on the south shore, is in FVRD Electoral Area H, south of the City of Chilliwack, and is OUTSIDE the Cultus Lake Park Board lease lands — it is not part of the City of Chilliwack, per the area research file. Sibling files: Cultus Lake North (Park lease lots) and Cultus Lake East (provincial park shore plus the lake's one age-restricted manufactured-home park) cover the same lake — complexes are filed in one sub-area file only and cross-referenced in the others, never double-counted.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. This is the governing frame for the south end's bare-land stratas (Creekside Mills, The Cottages, Aquadel Crossing): a 55+ page must not describe any of them as age-restricted, because no source states that any of them holds a 55+ bylaw — and a 45+ label, of the kind still seen on some Chilliwack-area communities, could not be a valid strata bylaw in any case. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The strata bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in strata exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide in strata.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw — and at Lindell Beach a second, local gate also applies (next item). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Short-term rentals are actively regulated in Area H.** The FVRD Board specifically enabled Electoral Area H (Lindell Beach) to proactively address short-term vacation rentals and RV use for accommodation; FVRD Zoning Bylaw No. 1638 excludes "Temporary Tourist Accommodation" from Residential use unless the zone expressly permits it, and a provincial short-term rental registration number has been required since May 1, 2025. A buyer counting on vacation-rental income from a south-end cottage has three gates: the strata bylaws, the FVRD zoning, and the provincial registry. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Strategic~Plans/2020%203rd%20Quarter%20Update.pdf and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0032-000389
- **Tenure contrast (the south end's defining rule):** unlike the north and east Park shores, south-end homes are outside the Cultus Lake Park leasehold system — tenure here is freehold lots plus bare land strata, so title and strata documents, not a Park lease, drive the deal. PlanCultus (Bylaw No. 1080, 2016) applies only inside Cultus Lake Park at the north end. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/CLP-Plan-Bylaw-1080-hig-res.pdf
- **OCP: FVRD Official Community Plan for Electoral Areas E and H, Bylaw No. 1115** (titled "Columbia Valley, Lindell Beach and Chilliwack River Valley," as amended by Bylaw No. 1533, 2019) covers the south end; under its designations the only residential use permitted is single family residential use, with housing needs met mainly through infill in existing hamlets, and its resort-residential policies (§5.9) calculate permitted sites/strata lots/units on "useable land." https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws/planning-land-use.html and https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA Housing Needs Report.pdf and https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11249
- **Zoning: FVRD Consolidated Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021** is the zoning bylaw for the south end; the zone names and the minimum parcel area and frontage figures applying to Lindell Beach parcels are UNVERIFIED and must be checked per address. https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/zoning
- **Sewer, water and their parcel taxes:** the FVRD operates the Cultus Lake sewer and water systems in Electoral Area H under service-area bylaws, and lots outside a sewer service area use private septic systems regulated by the Fraser Health Authority. The South Cultus Lake sewer service area was established by Bylaw No. 1416, 2017, with a parcel tax under Bylaw No. 1497, 2018; Amendment Bylaw No. 1779, 2025 set the per-parcel amount as the annual requisition divided equally by parcels on the roll. The FVRD's Area H residential rates list Water – Cultus Lake and Sewer – Cultus Lake South as "No Utility Billing / Property Taxes: Yes" (tax-funded) — confirm the specific lot is a participant. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/sewer-septic.html?media=standard and https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com:443/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=30839 and https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area H - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- **Rural property taxes:** Electoral Area H properties pay FVRD and provincial rural taxes plus service-area levies. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, rural taxes are due and payable on July 2 of the year they are levied, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on October 31 — the FVRD rural regime; the City of Chilliwack's own penalty dates differ and do not apply here. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area
- **ALR:** much of the non-mountainous part of Electoral Area H west of Lindell Beach is in the Agricultural Land Reserve; the ALR status of individual south-shore lots is UNVERIFIED — check per parcel before assuming a build or subdivision. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA Housing Needs Report.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ home I can buy at the south end of Cultus Lake?** Nothing verified. No source states a 55+ age restriction for Creekside Mills, The Cottages at Cultus Lake, Aquadel Crossing, the Lindell Beach Holiday Resort or any other south-end property. The lake's only age-restricted product, the Cultus Lake Village manufactured-home park, is board-classified in Cultus Lake East — and its own threshold is UNCONFIRMED because its listings conflict between 45+ and 55+. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=15206 and https://www.zolo.ca/cultus-lake-real-estate/3942-columbia-valley-road/28
- **Q: Is Cultus Lake South part of the Cultus Lake Park lease lands?** No. The south end is in FVRD Electoral Area H outside the Park Board boundary, and PlanCultus applies only inside Cultus Lake Park at the north end — south-end tenure is freehold or bare land strata. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/CLP-Plan-Bylaw-1080-hig-res.pdf
- **Q: Creekside Mills or The Cottages — what am I actually buying?** In both cases a bare land strata lot plus the home on it, inside a gated community with shared amenities: Creekside Mills is strata plan EPS3777, a 129-home community (FVRD Board report), and The Cottages is a freehold bare land strata cottage community at 1777 Columbia Valley Rd (developer source). Neither carries a 55+ bylaw in any source checked — read each strata's own bylaws for pet, rental and occupancy rules, which are UNCONFIRMED here. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=15206 and http://www.cultuslakecottages.com/
- **Q: Can I run my cottage as a vacation rental?** Only through three gates: strata short-term rental bylaws are still allowed in BC even though long-term rental bans are void; the FVRD has specifically enabled Area H to regulate short-term vacation rentals and its Zoning Bylaw No. 1638 excludes Temporary Tourist Accommodation from Residential use unless the zone expressly permits it; and a provincial STR registration number has been required since May 1, 2025. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0032-000389
- **Q: Is the south end on sewer and water, or septic and well?** Both systems exist: FVRD service-area bylaws cover the Cultus Lake water supply and the South Cultus Lake sewage treatment system, with parcel taxes for participating properties, and lots outside a service area are on private septic regulated by Fraser Health. South-shore water and sewer are tax-funded (no separate utility bill in the FVRD's Area H rates), and the South Cultus sewer parcel tax is split equally across parcels on the roll. Confirm connection status lot by lot. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area H - Residential Rates 2026.pdf and https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com:443/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=30839
- **Q: Does the bus reach Lindell Beach?** No. Route 56 Cultus Lake ends at the Cultus Lake Marina at the north end, and service is seasonal in summer only — year-round living at the south end means driving, on the one road in and out. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=56 and https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/FVRD-Area-H-Update-with-Emergency-and-Evacuation-Plans-May-14-2025.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a south-end address?** No public school is located at the south end, and no sample address was run for this sub-area, so the address-level catchment is UNVERIFIED. At area level, SD33 Administrative Procedure 611 groups the "Columbia Valley/Cultus Lake/Chilliwack Lake Road area" as Cultus Lake Elementary School → Mount Slesse Middle School → G.W. Graham Secondary School — an area pattern, not an address-level locator result. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/school/03333009 and https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2023-03/611%20AP%20Transportation%20-%20Operating%20Instructions.pdf
- **Q: What are the property taxes at Lindell Beach?** FVRD and provincial rural taxes plus service-area levies (sewer/water where the lot participates), under the rural regime: due July 2, 5% penalty on amounts unpaid July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid October 31. Per-lot amounts are UNVERIFIED — they turn on the lot's service-area participation. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and where do I shop?** Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Rd, Chilliwack, with the Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre at #104 - 7955 Evans Road. No shopping centre or grocery store at the south end is confirmed; the nearest grocery identified in the verified research is Save-on-Foods Sardis, 6014 Vedder Road, Sardis, and the nearest library is Sardis Library, 5819 Tyson Road, Chilliwack. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1893 and https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/
- **Q: What catches buyers out here?** Tenure and services, per the research: importing the Park lease assumption (the south end is not leasehold), assuming a lot is on sewer when it is on septic, the single access road for emergencies and summer traffic, and — for a 55+ buyer specifically — assuming the gated cottage communities are age-restricted when no source says they are. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/CLP-Plan-Bylaw-1080-hig-res.pdf and https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/sewer-septic.html?media=standard

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The south end's sewer tax is shared equally, not metered.** Amendment Bylaw No. 1779, 2025 set the South Cultus Lake sewer parcel tax as the annual requisition divided equally by the parcels on the roll — every participating parcel pays the same amount regardless of use, and the FVRD's Area H rates show the south sewer and the Cultus Lake water service as tax-funded with no separate utility bill (the north sewer, by contrast, carries a $1,369.00 flat rate for 2026 plus taxes). https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com:443/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=30839 and https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area H - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- **The local hazards are creek fans, not the Fraser River.** The governing local hazards at Lindell Beach are lake-level and creek (Frosst Creek) debris/flood events on the Cultus Lake alluvial fans; the FVRD funds sediment-basin cleanouts at Frosst Creek and Elbow Creek in this area, and its Area H guide lists flooding, landslide and slope stability, interface fire, debris slides and flash flooding. Property-specific hazard reports are UNVERIFIED. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Strategic~Plans/2020%203rd%20Quarter%20Update.pdf and https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/FVRD-Area-H-Update-with-Emergency-and-Evacuation-Plans-May-14-2025.pdf
- **There are no SnapStats numbers for anywhere at Cultus Lake.** The SnapStats FVR September 2026 report contains no Chilliwack section at all, and neither does the GVR report — any inventory, sales-ratio or median figure quoted for this sub-area has no source in the named stats program and should be treated as UNVERIFIED. Lindell Beach is a CADREB (Chilliwack and District) board sub-area under the Cultus Lake MLS area, not an FVREB reporting row. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/cultus-lake-south.md and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/lindell-beach.md
- **Soowahlie First Nation (IR 14) is at the south end of the lake.** The PlanCultus plan map shows Soowahlie First Nation (IR 14) at the south end — a neighbouring jurisdiction a south-end buyer will see on maps and in addressing, distinct from both the Park and the FVRD lots. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/about-the-fvrd/electoral-areas/electoral-area-h.html
- **The Holiday Resort's tenure is documented by a court, not a brochure.** The only hard source on the Lindell Beach Holiday Resort's model is the BC Court of Appeal's 2009 record (owned pre-fabricated trailer on a leased pad; 130 sites, 100 occupied at that time) — the resort itself publishes no current lease terms, so any pad-rent or term figure quoted today is UNVERIFIED until the resort states it. https://www.bccourts.ca/jdb-txt/CA/09/00/2009BCCA0005.htm
- **Population context is Area H-wide only.** Statistics Canada's 2021 Census counted 2,459 people in Fraser Valley H (up 33.1% from 1,847 in 2016); no Lindell Beach-only figure is published — UNVERIFIED for the community itself. https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/as-sa/fogs-spg/Page.cfm?lang=e&topic=9&dguid=2021S0503930
- **Fire protection is an FVRD Area H service with two halls in the frame.** The FVRD financial plan lists two Area H fire services — Cultus Lake (213) and Columbia Valley (215); the first-due hall for a specific Lindell Beach address is UNVERIFIED, and there is no FVRD garbage/recycling service in Area H. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=29616
