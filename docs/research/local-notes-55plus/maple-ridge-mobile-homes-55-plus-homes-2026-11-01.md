## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Manufactured-home parks run on tenancy law, not strata law.** Pad tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and its Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), not the Strata Property Act — so the strata age-bylaw machinery (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, Regulation 7.01 exemptions) does not apply inside a park. A home owner must provide a purchaser with the written tenancy agreement and the written park rules, and a sale is an assignment of the pad tenancy that requires the park owner's consent. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **A park's 55+ rule has its own legal footing.** The MHPTA assignment rules expressly recognise a park where every pad is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or 2+ tenants, at least one of whom has reached 55), as set out in s. 10(2)(b)(i) of the Human Rights Code — the park owner can refuse an assignment where no purchaser meets that age requirement. That is how Alouette's and Val Maria's 55+ rules operate: through park rules and assignment consent, not through a strata bylaw. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol20/consol20/02077_pit
- **For contrast — the strata rule that does NOT apply here:** only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; sub-55 bylaws invalid since Nov 24, 2022), and strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since the same date. A park can still set its own occupancy and rental rules in its park rules — do not import strata assumptions into a park purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019, s. 626, Zone RMH (Manufactured Home Park Residential)** is the dedicated zone for this housing form in Maple Ridge. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/Zoning%20bylaw%20report.pdf
- **Tenure paperwork is the diligence core of a park purchase.** The Province's tenancy materials distinguish the Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement (you own the home, rent the site) from a Residential Tenancy Agreement (both home and site rented) — which agreement a listing actually carries changes the buyer's rights, and the research file flags this split as the topic's defining issue. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **The registry covers both manufactured and modular homes.** The Province's manufactured-home registry covers both forms, which is why this page in practice covers manufactured and modular product together. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md
- **Floodplain:** the City's floodplain framework (flood construction level 6.7 m geodetic including freeboard along the river flats; Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibiting rental accommodation/suites on floodplain properties) is per-lot diligence for any park or owned-land manufactured home at the city's south/west lowland edges. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Property taxes / deferment:** the Province's Benefits Connector states the regular property tax deferment loan program is for people 55 and older (plus surviving spouses and persons with disabilities). Note the Alouette listing separately itemises "city taxes" on top of pad rent — who pays what on a pad is set by the tenancy agreement, not by strata practice. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/benefits and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3067037-12868-229th-street-18-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-6r1/brochure

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which Maple Ridge parks are actually 55+?** Two verify: Alouette Retirement Mobile Home Park (12868 229 St) and Val Maria (21163 Lougheed Hwy) — both on current listing text that names the park and states the 55+ age restriction. Any other Maple Ridge park claimed as 55+ is UNCONFIRMED in this research. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What do I own when I buy in a 55+ park?** The manufactured home — not the land. You rent the pad under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement, and your purchase is an assignment of that tenancy, which requires the park owner's consent. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: Can the park refuse me because of age — or refuse my buyer later?** In a park whose pads are reserved by age, yes: the MHPTA assignment rules expressly recognise parks reserved for tenants who have reached 55 (Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i)), and the park owner can refuse an assignment where no purchaser meets the age requirement. The same rule that admits you at 55+ narrows your resale pool the same way a strata bylaw does. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Q: What are the carrying costs on top of the purchase?** Pad rent is the big one, and it is park-specific: listing-stated figures are $713.31/month at Alouette (plus city taxes of $457.37 and quarterly metered water, per the same listing) and $850/month at Val Maria. Confirm the current figure for the specific pad in the tenancy agreement — pad rent is not a strata fee and is not set by the seller. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3067037-12868-229th-street-18-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-6r1/brochure and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3089789-18-21163-lougheed-highway-maple-ridge-british-columbia-v2x2r4-259000/brochure
- **Q: Can I get a mortgage on a park home?** It depends on the park — and at Alouette the listing states plainly "no financing permitted in this community." Read the park rules before assuming any lender product applies; a park purchase can be a cash purchase by rule, not just by price. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3067037-12868-229th-street-18-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-6r1/brochure
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Park by park, and opposite at the two verified parks: Alouette's listing states no dogs, indoor cat only, 2 residents per unit; Val Maria's listing states 2 small pets allowed. Confirm in the park rules for the specific park. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ park?** Park age rules operate through the park's own rules and the Human Rights Code framing (a pad reserved for a tenant who has reached 55, or 2+ tenants at least one of whom has reached 55) — the strata spouse exemption does not apply inside a park. Read the specific park's rules for how a younger spouse is treated. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street — a Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency care serving Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows. Both verified parks are in the urban core's orbit (229 Street east; Lougheed Highway west). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- **Q: Is a manufactured home on its own lot the same thing as a park home?** No. A manufactured or modular home on owned land (the feed carried one on Calvin Crescent) is a freehold purchase — no pad rent, no park rules, no age restriction. The registry covers both forms, which is why they appear on the same page, but the tenure is entirely different. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md
