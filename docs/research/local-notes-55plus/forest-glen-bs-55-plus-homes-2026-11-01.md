# Forest Glen (Burnaby) — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Forest Glen is the Burnaby South sub-area between Metrotown and Edmonds, around Kingsway, Willingdon Avenue and the Bond Street / Nelson Avenue streets, named for Forest Glen Park at 4855 Bond St. It mixes the Kingsway condo corridor with detached-house streets south and east of Metrotown. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- The MLS name: SnapStats' GVR report row is "Forest Glen," and listings in the sub-area are labelled "Forest Glen BS" — the "BS" is a Burnaby South area tag, not part of the neighbourhood's name. Listing data for the area's named tower records the subdivision exactly that way. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report) and https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/6055-nelson-avenue/705
- Boundaries: UNVERIFIED — no City source read publishes street-by-street boundaries for the MLS Forest Glen sub-area. The confirmed anchors are Forest Glen Park on Bond Street, Kingsway along the north side, Metrotown and Patterson Station to the west, and the Wesburn / Clinton school streets to the south and east. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- The market is two-sided: in September 2026 the sub-area row showed 18 detached listings and 1 detached sale (6% sales ratio) against 64 condos and townhomes listed and 6 sold (9%) — the attached stock on and off Kingsway outweighs the house streets in listings. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report)
- Schools are School District 41 (Burnaby): 41 elementary and 8 secondary schools city-wide, no middle-school tier — elementary is K–7 and secondary is 8–12. https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/
- SD41 schools in or beside the area: Clinton Elementary (K–7), 5858 Clinton St; École Inman Elementary (K–7), 3963 Brandon St; Nelson Elementary (K–7), 4850 Irmin St; École Marlborough Elementary (K–7), 6060 Marlborough Ave; and Burnaby South Secondary (8–12), 5455 Rumble St, to the south-east. Catchments are address-based through the SD41 locator, and the locator result for a Forest Glen sample address is UNVERIFIED — check every address before marketing school appeal. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- Recreation: Wesburn Community Centre, 4781 Parkwood Ave, at the area's south side in Wesburn Park, has a 700 sq. m meeting/activity room accommodating 100 people; the centre has no regular office hours or staff onsite and visits are by arrangement. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/wesburn-community-centre

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **City zoning: the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026** is the current bylaw — adopted June 9, 2026 and in effect July 1, 2026. (The old 1965 zoning bylaw is not current; do not cite it as such.) https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **Small-scale multi-unit housing (R1 SSMUH District, in place since June 2024):** R1 house lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. The Kingsway corridor towers are not R1 — confirm the zone parcel by parcel. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Community plan:** the Cascade Heights Community Plan, for the neighbourhood north-east of Forest Glen toward Burnaby Hospital, was officially adopted by the City in March 2025; the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/cascade-heights-community-plan
- **Property taxes:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan, about $61 for the average home. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **Utilities:** Burnaby's 2026 residential utility notice covers annual water, annual sewer use, a cross-connection device charge where applicable, and a residential garbage toter fee; the detached single-family dwelling water charge is $652.33, rates carry a 5% discount if paid by the due date, and for single-family homes sewer is billed as a sewer parcel tax on the property tax notice. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Subsidized seniors housing runs through BC Housing.** The Housing Registry and the Zone 5 (Burnaby and New Westminster) seniors directory are the application route for the non-profit seniors buildings elsewhere in the city; the directory edition located for this research is dated January 2021. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo I can buy in Forest Glen?** No verified one. No strata in the sub-area carries a verified 55+ age-restriction bylaw — the named tower, La Mirage (6055 Nelson Ave, Bosa, 1988), is general-market with no age restriction stated in any source checked. A buyer who needs the 55+ bylaw itself has to look outside this sub-area. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/6055-nelson-avenue/705
- **Q: If I find a 55+ strata nearby, what does the bylaw actually restrict?** Who may live in the unit, not who may own it. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a resident who is 55+ can be the "specified resident." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata?** Yes — provincial rules exempt a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St — immediately north-east of Forest Glen, with the hospital lands adjoining the area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
- **Q: Where do I go for urgent care that is not an emergency?** The Edmonds Urgent and Primary Care Centre, #201-7315 Edmonds St, east of the sub-area in the Edmonds town centre (Fraser Health). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/burnaby/edmonds-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- **Q: Is there a seniors centre in Forest Glen?** No 55+ centre inside the sub-area. The City's Bonsor 55+ Centre is at 6533 Nelson Ave, far south on the Metrotown side — a 55+ single-centre membership is $22.60/yr and a multi-centre membership is $56.45/yr, with the Bonsor 55+ Society running clubs. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/bonsor-55-plus-centre
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day without a car?** Route 110 runs on Bond and Nelson streets themselves, linking Metrotown Station to Sperling and Lougheed stations, and Metrotown and Patterson Expo Line stations are at the area's west side. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt110.pdf and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- **Q: Can a Forest Glen house lot add units?** Under Burnaby's R1 SSMUH District, a house lot may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and multi-unit lots may be stratified. Kingsway corridor parcels follow their own zones. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: How did the two sides of this market do in September 2026?** Attached carried the volume: 64 condos and townhomes listed and 6 sold (9% sales ratio), against 18 detached listings and 1 detached sale (6%). Sub-area median prices by property type: UNVERIFIED — SnapStats publishes medians at community level only. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The zoning bylaw itself is new.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026, replacing the old bylaw framework; the R1 small-scale multi-unit rules (3/4/6 units) that govern Forest Glen's house streets sit inside it. Any document still citing the 1965 bylaw as current is out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **The city plan above it is new too.** The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan was adopted December 9, 2025 and replaces the 1998 OCP; the Cascade Heights Community Plan for the hospital-side neighbourhood north-east of Forest Glen was adopted in March 2025. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/cascade-heights-community-plan
- **2026 tax dates have already passed.** Burnaby's 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; the 2026–2030 Financial Plan's increase was 2.9%, about $61 for the average home. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **The one named tower is in its capital-projects era.** La Mirage was built in 1988; an October 2026 listing describes CRF-funded re-piping, boilers, parkade waterproofing, fob security and EV chargers already done, with an elevator upgrade underway — for a downsizer, the strata's depreciation report and contingency fund matter more here than the amenity list. Listing-stated; confirm in the strata documents. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/6055-nelson-avenue/501
- **The Grange Street pocket is a boundary trap.** Two seniors facilities sit on Grange Street by the Willingdon corridor — Willingdon Care Centre (4435 Grange St, Fraser Health long-term care) and Chaffey Lane (4389 Grange St, 37 non-profit seniors units in BC Housing's Zone 5 directory) — but listings label the surrounding Willingdon addresses Metrotown, and no source verified either address inside the Forest Glen MLS sub-area. They are real and close; they are not counted in this file. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/willingdon-care-centre and https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076
- **The subsidized-seniors paper trail is old.** The newest BC Housing Zone 5 seniors directory located in this research is dated January 2021 — unit counts and application details for the non-profit seniors buildings around Burnaby should be re-confirmed with BC Housing before relying on them. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076
- **The "55+" on Nelson Avenue is a recreation centre, not a residence.** Bonsor 55+ Centre (6533 Nelson Ave) is one of the addresses a "55+ Forest Glen" search surfaces first; it is a City recreation centre with a seniors society, at the south end of Nelson by Metrotown — there is no housing attached to it. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/bonsor-55-plus-centre
