# Chilliwack Mountain, Chilliwack — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**The plain answer for this area: Chilliwack Mountain has NO verified 55+ product of any kind — no age-restricted strata, no 55+ manufactured-home park, no seniors rental, no care residence on the mountain.** The mountain is hillside freehold lots and general-market townhouse complexes. Every 55+ option named below sits elsewhere in Chilliwack, and this file says so at each entry.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Chilliwack Mountain is a residential hillside neighbourhood in the City of Chilliwack, on the named mountain on the south side of the Fraser River, just west of Chilliwack Creek and west of the downtown core. The City treats Chilliwack Mountain as a neighbourhood in its own reporting but publishes no street-by-street boundary for the board sub-area name. https://apps.gov.bc.ca/pub/bcgnws/names/5861.html and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3237
- The community sits above the Industrial Way / Progress Way industrial area on the valley floor; the City's Schweyey Road forcemain provides sewage service along Schweyey Road for Chilliwack Mountain and portions of Industrial Way and Progress Way — service for a specific parcel is not confirmed by that page. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3237
- Nearby areas: Chilliwack Downtown and Chilliwack Proper West to the east, Little Mountain to the southeast, Fairfield Island across the river to the north, and the Lickman Road / Industrial Way corridor below the mountain to the west. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- Housing form: hillside residential lots carrying single-detached houses, plus a small number of newer townhouse complexes on Chilliwack Mountain Road (Section 2). The form is governed by the OCP's hillside Development Permit Areas D-2 and D-5; there is no Chilliwack Mountain neighbourhood plan in Schedule B of the Chilliwack 2050 OCP. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- No SD33 public school is located on Chilliwack Mountain itself; catchments are address-based in SD33's School Locator, and no mountain address was run in the underlying research, so catchment schools for mountain addresses are UNCONFIRMED. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/chilliwack and https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- Transit: no BC Transit route in the Chilliwack list runs onto the mountain's residential streets; the closest listed service is route 59 Industrial – Downtown Chilliwack to Cottonwood Mall via the Lickman Park & Ride, with peak-period trips. Highway 1 access is via the Lickman Road interchange south of the mountain. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/ and https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=59
- Hillkeep Regional Park (FVRD) is a nature reserve at the top of the mountain on Hillkeep Place, reached by Grandview Drive, with hiking trails and a viewing platform — the area's documented amenity. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/hillkeep.html
- Hospital and urgent care are both on the valley floor, not the mountain: Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Rd, and the Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre at #104 – 7955 Evans Road. The nearest leisure centre is Chilliwack Landing Leisure Centre, 9145 Corbould St, and the nearest library is Chilliwack Library, 45860 First Avenue. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/Chilliwack-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre and https://www.ymcabc.ca/locations/chilliwack-landing-leisure-centre and https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/chilliwack-library
- Market numbers: Chilliwack is not covered by either SnapStats September 2026 report (the FVR report covers Surrey, South Surrey/White Rock, North Delta, Cloverdale, Langley, Abbotsford and Mission only), so every market figure for Chilliwack Mountain is UNVERIFIED.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. On the mountain this matters mostly in the negative: the townhouse complexes are strata, but no source states any of them has a 55+ bylaw, and Reflections at Cedar Sky's listings state the opposite. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Most mountain homes are freehold, where strata age law does not apply at all.** The confirmed housing form on the mountain is freehold hillside lots; a freehold detached house carries no strata bylaw, so there is no age-restriction mechanism of the strata kind — any "55+" claim for a mountain detached listing would need a source, and none was found. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Hillside zoning (Zoning Bylaw No. 5000):** the HR (Hillside Residential) Zone sets a minimum lot area of 0.2 ha where served by a community water system and 0.4 ha for all other uses, minimum lot width 25 m; the RH (Rural Hillside) Zone sets 8 ha minimum lot area and 25 m width; the SHR (Suburban Hillside Residential) Zone sets minimum lot area by slope band, from 360 m² in the lowest band to 990 m² in the highest; the SR (Suburban Residential) Zone sets 650 m² minimum on community water and sewer. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Hillside development permits:** Development Permit Area D-2 (Hillside Environment) applies to Chilliwack Mountain, and a permit with a geotechnical hazard and slope assessment is required before subdivision, construction, land clearing or earthworks — the rule that shapes any plan to add a suite, rebuild, or age-in-place retrofit on a mountain lot. DPA D-5 (Hillside Development) also covers the mountain. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf and https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **ALR status is parcel-specific** and was not confirmed for mountain parcels; the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping is the check for any individual lot. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Property taxes:** City of Chilliwack property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% late penalty after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026; additional taxes are levied for drainage and dyking on top of the municipal rate. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **Flood:** no mountain-specific floodplain statement was found in City material; the City's flood pages address the Fraser River dyke system on the valley floor below the mountain. A specific lot's flood status is UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked — check it parcel by parcel. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing on Chilliwack Mountain itself?** No verified product of any type — no 55+ strata, park, rental or care residence with a mountain address appears in any source checked. The mountain's named townhouse complex, Reflections at Cedar Sky (43680 Chilliwack Mountain Rd), states "No age restrictions" in its listings. https://kw.com/property/43680-Chilliwack-Mountain-Road-23-Chilliwack-BC-V2R-6A6/2042669732905526
- **Q: I saw "Mountainview Estates" listed as 55+ — is that on the mountain?** No. Mountainview Estates is at 46966 Yale Rd and listing data files it in Chilliwack Proper East. It is a genuine 55+ building (built 1989), but it is a valley-floor Proper East address, not Chilliwack Mountain — the name is the trap. https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/46966-yale-road/104
- **Q: Where are Chilliwack's actual 55+ strata buildings?** Downtown and the central city: Ivy Green (45775 Spadina Ave — 55+, 24 condos, built 1994, LMS1336, no pets), Knight Lodge (45660 Knight Rd — 55+ per listing restriction data), Mountainview Estates (46966 Yale Rd, Proper East), and the gated 55+ rancher community Cottage Grove (8500 Young Rd, Proper South). The city's 55+ manufactured-home park product is Cottonwood Village on Evans Road (valley floor). https://www.admiraal.ca/buildings/view/27410 and https://www.estateblock.com/chilliwack/203-45660-knight-rd-49464 and https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/8500-young-road/21
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a ground-floor primary to a mountain house for aging in place?** The governing constraint is the hillside permit regime, not age law: the whole mountain is in Development Permit Area D-2, and a permit with a geotechnical hazard and slope assessment is required before construction or earthworks; lot potential in the SHR zone is slope-banded (360 m² to 990 m² minimums). Budget the report and permit timing into any retrofit plan, and confirm the specific lot's zone in Zoning Bylaw No. 5000. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: Is there a bus I can use if I stop driving?** Not on the mountain. No BC Transit route serves the mountain's residential streets; the closest service is route 59 at the Lickman Park & Ride below the mountain, a peak-period service to Downtown Chilliwack and Cottonwood Mall. A buyer planning to age in place here is planning around a car or rides from family. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- **Q: Where are the hospital and urgent care?** Down the hill: Chilliwack General Hospital at 45600 Menholm Rd (emergency care, Fraser Health), and the Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre at #104 – 7955 Evans Road. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/Chilliwack-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre
- **Q: Is the mountain in a floodplain?** No mountain-specific floodplain statement was found in City material — the City's flood work addresses the Fraser River dyke system on the valley floor. Treat any individual lot's status as UNCONFIRMED until it is checked against City flood mapping for that parcel. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- **Q: Are the mountain homes on City sewer and water?** Partly confirmed only: the City states the Schweyey Road forcemain provides sewage services for Chilliwack Mountain and portions of Industrial Way and Progress Way, but it does not confirm service for every parcel — and the HR zone's two lot minimums (0.2 ha on community water, 0.4 ha otherwise) tell you unserviced lots exist in the zoning. Verify water and sewer connection status for the specific lot before writing an offer. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3237 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: If I buy on the mountain now and need care later, what is in Chilliwack?** The registered assisted-living residences are on the east/downtown side (e.g. Waverly Seniors Village, 8445 Young Rd; The Cascades, 45586 McIntosh Dr), the subsidized seniors buildings are downtown/central (e.g. the Paramount, 46187 Yale Rd), and the hospital is on the valley floor — the care ladder exists in the city, just not on the mountain. https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence?bcgovtm=pique%20newsmagazine&field_residence_name_value=&field_residence_type_tid=41&f%5B0%5D=ha%3AFraser%20Health&page=1&name=&address=&order=alr_residence_type&sort=asc and https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-7-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: Does buying a general-market mountain home carry any resale age issue?** No age bylaw applies to the mountain's freehold lots, and none of its townhouse complexes is verified as age-restricted, so the buyer pool is the general market — the opposite trade-off from a true 55+ strata, where the next buyer must be able to reside under the bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The mountain's townhouse stock now has names — the older research did not.** City council records from 2017 tie a townhouse complex at 43660 and 43710 Chilliwack Mountain Road to Development Permit DP000817 (Cedarsky Developments Ltd.) without naming it; current listings name the built product on that stretch of road as Reflections at Cedar Sky (43680), with Eagles Perch (43610, built 2022) and Osprey Ridge (43998, 34 homes) further along Chilliwack Mountain Road. All three are general-market; none states an age restriction, and Reflections states the opposite. https://www.chilliwack.com/apps/council/meetings/agendapackage/reg/2017.01.24%20-%20Regular%20Council%20Agenda%20Package.pdf and https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/43610-chilliwack-mountain-road/3 and https://www.estateblock.com/chilliwack/25-43998-chilliwack-mountain-rd-125893
- **There is no neighbourhood plan coming to change this.** No Chilliwack Mountain plan appears in Schedule B (B1 to B7) of the Chilliwack 2050 OCP — the mountain is governed by the city-wide OCP and its hillside Development Permit Areas D-2 and D-5, so no plan-driven seniors project is signalled for the mountain in the sources checked. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **The slope, not the floorplan, is the due-diligence item.** In the SHR zone the minimum lot area rises from 360 m² in the lowest slope band to 990 m² in the highest, and DPA D-2 requires a geotechnical hazard and slope assessment before subdivision, construction, clearing or earthworks — a lot's rebuild or suite potential depends on its measured slope, not just its size or its view. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- **The amenity at the top is a park, not a plaza.** Hillkeep Regional Park, the FVRD nature reserve at the top of the mountain (Hillkeep Place, via Grandview Drive), offers trails and a viewing platform — and there is no shopping centre, grocery store, leisure centre or library on the mountain in any source checked; all of those are valley-floor trips. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/hillkeep.html and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1893
- **Chilliwack taxes have two penalty steps and a dyking add-on:** a 5% penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026, plus separate drainage and dyking levies on top of the municipal rates — the dyking levy applies city-wide even though the mountain sits above the dyke system. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
