## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **No age bylaw can attach to farmland itself.** An age restriction in BC is created by a strata corporation's bylaws or by a manufactured-home park's rules. A Campbell Valley farm or acreage parcel is freehold land: nothing in its title, zoning or ALR status makes it "55+," and a buyer acquires no age-restricted community with it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **ALR housing caps are the rules that actually bind a 55+ acreage buyer.** On ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted, up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may be more restrictive and may not be more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Stratification on ALR land needs ALC permission:** depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR — ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6 ("Strata Plans Cause Subdivision"). Feed cards that raise stratification or subdivision ideas in their own text are, on an ALR parcel, under this rule; on a non-ALR parcel they are a Township question. Either way, listing text is not permission. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **The second-dwelling rule is the ageing-in-place rule on an acreage.** The ALC's additional-residence tier (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size, above) is the legal route to a caregiver's or family member's dwelling on an ALR Campbell Valley parcel, and Township bylaws must also permit it. There is no strata-style caregiver exemption here because there is no strata. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Township frame:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 applies across the Township, and s.102 is the cited source for the Township's coach-house definition (an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage). What a specific Campbell Valley parcel may build — dwellings, barns, a second residence — is UNVERIFIED at parcel level in this file; feed cards use RU-3 labels in their own listing text only. Source: batch-15 research file for this topic.
- **Do not apply SSMUH math to these acreages.** The Township adopted Bylaw 6020 on November 18, 2024 — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and no 6-unit tier. Campbell Valley acreage parcels in this feed are measured in acres, well over the 4,050 m² ceiling, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them; parcel-level eligibility is UNVERIFIED. Source: batch-15 research file for this topic.
- **Farm classification is a continuing obligation, not a one-time label.** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income thresholds are $2,500 if the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha; applications must be submitted by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A downsizing buyer who stops producing can lose the classification and its tax treatment. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **The strata age-bylaw rules, for the record — with no local strata to apply them to.** Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2; age bylaws below 55 invalid since Nov 24, 2022, under Bill 44); the bylaw restricts residency, not ownership; Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children cared for before 19; and residential rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, while short-term restrictions remain allowed. A Campbell Valley acreage buyer gets none of this machinery, because the purchase is freehold land, not a strata lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Property transfer tax (general tiers):** 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% above $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. How PTT and GST apportion on a specific Campbell Valley farm/acreage purchase is UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Quota and supply management: UNVERIFIED in every particular** — no marketing-board or CFIA source was opened for this topic, so no quota position for any operation in this feed is stated. Source: batch-15 research file for this topic.
- **FHSA and Home Buyers' Plan dollar limits: UNVERIFIED** — no live CRA page stating those limits was read for this file.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ acreage or seniors farm community in Campbell Valley?** No — none was verified on any source. There is no 55+ strata, park or co-op in the sub-area, and an ALR parcel cannot be stratified without ALC permission. Langley's 15 verified 55+ ownership complexes are all in the urban communities (Section 2). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: I'm 55+ and buying an acreage to downsize onto. What limits the house I can build?** If the parcel is in the ALR, a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. Confirm the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps before designing anything — cards in this one feed state both "in the ALR" and "outside the ALR." https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can my adult child or a caregiver live in a second dwelling on the acreage?** On ALR land, the ALC permits one additional residence without an application where its size rules are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and Township bylaws must also permit it. Off the ALR, it is a Township zoning question for the specific parcel. Either way there is no strata-style caregiver exemption, because there is no strata. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: A card mentions a mobile home pad and another mentions stratification potential. Can I rely on those?** No — treat both as listing-text claims, not rights. A mobile home pad on a farm parcel is not a manufactured-home park and carries no park-tenancy or 55+ status; stratification of an ALR parcel needs ALC permission under Information Bulletin 09 s.6. Verify both against Township and ALC records before subjects come off. https://jaredgibbons.ca/campbell-valley-acreages-farms-for-sale.html
- **Q: If I stop farming, do I keep farm-class taxes?** Not automatically: BC Assessment requires a producing farm to keep meeting minimum gross farm income thresholds that scale with the operation's size ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha), and a buyer continuing to farm must file the General Application by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Does provincial small-scale multi-unit housing law let me add units to a Campbell Valley acreage?** Generally no, on size alone: Township Bylaw 6020 makes lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and this feed's parcels are measured in acres. Parcel-level eligibility is UNVERIFIED in this file. Source: batch-15 research file for this topic.
- **Q: How active is this market, for timing a purchase?** Thin in the published data: Campbell Valley recorded 16 detached listings and 0 detached sales in September 2026, and 0 Condos & Townhomes listings — so there is no sub-area median or pace figure to anchor on, and none is stated. Those zero combinations are not unique to Campbell Valley: Otter District is also Condos & Townhomes 0 / 0, and County Line Glen Valley is Detached 0 inventory with 1 sale (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, as recorded in the batch-15 research file).
- **Q: The feed shows 47 results. Is that how many Campbell Valley farms are for sale?** No. "1-12 / 47" is a live-feed snapshot read on Nov 1, 2026, not a market statistic, and it measures something different from the sub-area's September 2026 SnapStats detached inventory of 16 — neither count corrects the other, and 47 must not be presented as a sub-area farm inventory. https://jaredgibbons.ca/campbell-valley-acreages-farms-for-sale.html
- **Q: What about wells and septic on these acreages?** Parcel-level position is UNVERIFIED in this file: feed cards refer in their own text to drilled wells, filtration systems and septic fields, which are listing copy for those cards only. Verify the specific parcel's well, septic and any second residence with professionals and the Township. https://jaredgibbons.ca/campbell-valley-acreages-farms-for-sale.html
- **Q: Where would I actually go if I need care while living in Campbell Valley?** To a community care home, reached through Fraser Health — the care stock is in Langley's communities (for example Jackman Manor in Aldergrove), and the hospital is Langley Memorial at 22051 Fraser Highway. There is no care option in Campbell Valley itself. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/jackman-manor and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital
