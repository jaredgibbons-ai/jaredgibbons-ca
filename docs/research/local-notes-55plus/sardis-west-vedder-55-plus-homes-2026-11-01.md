## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Sardis West Vedder" is an MLS® sub-area name in the City of Chilliwack for the part of Sardis west of Vedder Road; the City publishes no boundary for it. The guide page's own rough guide is the part of Sardis west of Vedder Road, from Highway 1 in the north toward the Vedder neighbourhood in the south, around the Evans Road and Unsworth Road corridors — an orientation from school addresses, not a City boundary. https://jaredgibbons.ca/sardis-west-vedder-guide.html
- Vedder Road is the divider: Sardis East Vedder and Promontory sit east of it, Sardis West Vedder west of it, with Chilliwack Proper north of Highway 1 and Vedder Crossing / Garrison Crossing to the south. https://jaredgibbons.ca/sardis-west-vedder-guide.html
- The 55+ stock in this sub-area concentrates in two pockets: the Knight Road pair just west of Vedder Road (Knight Lodge at 45660 Knight Road and Cotton Ridge at 45645 Knight Road, near the Luckakuck Way shopping area), and the Amber Drive pair further west (The Sapphire at 7685 Amber Drive and the building at 7651 Amber Drive), with the Cottonwood Village manufactured-home park on Evans Road. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Sardis as a whole sits inside the Schedule B5 Sardis Neighbourhood Plan area of the City's 2050 Official Community Plan (Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500); Schedule D of that OCP is the Development Permit Areas schedule, not the Sardis plan. Live pages that call the Sardis plan "OCP Schedule D, through Bylaw 2020, No. 5024" are using stale previous-OCP numbering — do not copy it. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- Schools on this side are SD33: Evans Elementary (7600 Evans Rd) and Unsworth Elementary (5685 Unsworth Rd), with Vedder Middle School, Mount Slesse Middle School, Sardis Secondary and GW Graham Secondary named for the wider Sardis side; catchments are address-based, and the one recorded SD33 locator run in this program (7600 Evans Road) returned Evans Elementary, Vedder Middle School and Sardis Secondary for that address only. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- BC Transit Route 52 Evans runs between Cottonwood Mall and downtown Chilliwack via Evans Road and Yale Road, and Route 66 Fraser Valley Express serves Chilliwack, Abbotsford, Langley and Burnaby with daily service. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=52 and https://www.bctransit.com/plan-a-trip/interregional-routes/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://choa.bc.ca/resources/strata-property-act/legislative-updates/
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2023HOUS0038-000601.htm
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **City short-term rentals are separately regulated** under the City of Chilliwack's Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141 — a strata's permission does not replace the City's rules. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000** is Chilliwack's zoning bylaw. Its small-scale multi-unit (SSMUH) amendment, Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024), allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Suites and accessory units:** under Bylaw 5000 only 1 accessory dwelling unit may sit on a lot; a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the habitable floor space of the building, and a garden suite is capped at 100 m². https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Manufactured-home parks are not strata.** Cottonwood Village homes sit on leased pads (listing data: "Leasehold not prepaid — NonStrata"), so the SPA age-bylaw frame above does not govern the park the way it governs Knight Lodge, Cotton Ridge or The Sapphire; the park's age rule, pad rent and lease term live in the park's own lease and tenancy documents, which must be read directly. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3102243-7610-evans-road-49-chilliwack-bc-v2r-2y8/brochure
- **Property taxes:** City of Chilliwack notices are mailed at the end of May, taxes are due the first working day after July 1, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and a further 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many 55+ buildings can I actually BUY in Sardis West Vedder?** Four verified age-restricted strata ownership buildings: Knight Lodge (45660 Knight Rd, built 1989), Cotton Ridge (45645 Knight Rd, built 2006), The Sapphire (7685 Amber Dr, 49 units per the building directory) and the building at 7651 Amber Dr (built 1992, name UNCONFIRMED). The Cottonwood Village park on Evans Road is a fifth place to buy a home, but it is leasehold manufactured housing, not strata ownership. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Why do earlier Chilliwack guides name only Knight Lodge on this side of Sardis?** Because the city-wide roster settled in earlier research carried five strata complexes for all of Chilliwack and did not include Cotton Ridge, The Sapphire or 7651 Amber Drive. This pass verified all three from listing and building-directory sources; treat the older five-complex city-wide count as superseded for Sardis. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a 55+ strata here?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy in one of these buildings as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, and listings for Cotton Ridge and The Sapphire both state rentals allowed. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals (and the City regulates short-term rentals under Bylaw 5141). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Building by building, and the verified examples point in different directions: Knight Lodge records pets allowed with restrictions; Cotton Ridge listings state 2 pets allowed; The Sapphire's directory records pets not allowed and a current listing states no pets. There is no area-wide pet rule. https://www.estateblock.com/chilliwack/203-45660-knight-rd-49464 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3021740-45645-knight-road-108-chilliwack-bc-v2r-0b1/brochure and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/the-sapphire-7685-amber-drive
- **Q: How is Cottonwood Village different from the Knight Road buildings?** Tenure. At Knight Lodge, Cotton Ridge and The Sapphire you buy a strata lot and own an interest in the land. At Cottonwood Village you buy the manufactured home only, on a leased pad — listing data records "Leasehold not prepaid — NonStrata," with a lease expiry of 2076 with an option to extend stated in one listing — so the pad rent, lease terms and park rules are the documents that matter, not a strata Form B. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3102243-7610-evans-road-49-chilliwack-bc-v2r-2y8/brochure and https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/7610-evans-road/50
- **Q: Does the 55+ restriction shrink my resale buyer pool?** Mechanically, yes: the next buyer must also be able to live there under the bylaw — be 55+, be an exempt spouse/partner, caregiver or legacy case, or buy without living in the unit. That is the trade-off built into every BC 55+ strata since the 2022 law change confined age bylaws to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Where is the hospital?** Chilliwack General Hospital, 45600 Menholm Road — a Fraser Health community hospital providing primary, core secondary and some specialty services. It is on the Chilliwack Proper side, north of Highway 1 from Sardis. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** Route 52 Evans runs between Cottonwood Mall and downtown Chilliwack via Evans Road and Yale Road — the route that serves the Evans Road corridor beside Cottonwood Village — and Route 66 Fraser Valley Express runs interregionally to Abbotsford, Langley and Burnaby with daily service. Route-level detail for the other Sardis routes named on guide pages is timetable-UNVERIFIED in this program. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=52 and https://www.bctransit.com/plan-a-trip/interregional-routes/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The Knight Road pair is really a pair.** Knight Lodge (45660 Knight Rd, built 1989) and Cotton Ridge (45645 Knight Rd, built 2006) are neighbouring civic addresses on the same street — two separate 55+ strata buildings a short walk apart, 17 years apart in age, both marketing guest suites. A buyer searching only the older, better-known name will miss the newer building entirely. https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/45660-knight-road/203 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3021740-45645-knight-road-108-chilliwack-bc-v2r-0b1/brochure
- **Amber Drive works the same way.** The Sapphire (7685 Amber Dr) and the unnamed 55+ building at 7651 Amber Dr sit on the same short street west of Vedder Road, both built in the early 1990s, both marketing a gym/workshop/guest-suite amenity set. Whether they share a strata plan is UNCONFIRMED — price and compare them as separate buildings until the strata documents say otherwise. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/the-sapphire-7685-amber-drive and https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3091259-7651-amber-drive-210-chilliwack-sardis-west-vedder-homes-for-sale-305000/brochure
- **The board's own labels contradict each other on Knight Road.** Listings at 45660 and 45645 Knight Road are labelled "Sardis West Vedder," while a listing at 45918 Knight Road (Country Park Village) is labelled "Sardis East Vedder" — the same street filed on both sides of the Vedder Road divider. A buyer who filters by sub-area label alone will be shown stock that belongs to the other Sardis page and miss stock on this one. https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/45660-knight-road/203 and https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3102457-45918-knight-road-6-chilliwack-bc-v2r-3x4/brochure
- **The live guide page's plan label is stale.** Pages for this area still describe the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan as "OCP Schedule D, through Bylaw 2020, No. 5024" (adopted January 5, 2021). In the current OCP the Sardis plan is Schedule B5 of Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, and Schedule D is the Development Permit Areas schedule. Do not copy the old label into an offer or a listing. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **This side of Sardis sits on the Vedder alluvial fan.** The guide research records the area sloping down toward the Vedder River, over the Sardis–Vedder Aquifer, under the City's floodplain framework — but the floodplain bylaw specifics and the Flood Construction Level for any specific lot are per-parcel City checks and are UNVERIFIED in these notes. Confirm a lot's flood construction level and setbacks with the City before removing subjects, especially toward the Vedder River, the Vedder Canal or a creek. Source: the Sardis West Vedder area research file, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/sardis-west-vedder-guide.md
- **There are no market statistics to quote for this sub-area — from anyone.** CADREB publishes board-wide figures only and SnapStats does not cover Chilliwack, so any Sardis West Vedder "median price" a seller or a website offers is not coming from a published board or SnapStats source. Price from the property's own CADREB comparables. https://www.cadreb.com/market/
- **The newest 55+ strata building on this side is from 2006.** Cotton Ridge is the youngest of the four verified strata buildings (1989, 1992, early-1990s and 2006); no newer age-restricted strata project in Sardis West Vedder appears in any source checked. Sources: Section 2 above.
