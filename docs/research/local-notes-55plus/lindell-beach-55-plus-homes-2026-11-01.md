# Lindell Beach — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**GEOGRAPHY FLAG — read first:** Lindell Beach is NOT inside the City of Chilliwack, despite "Chilliwack, BC" mailing addresses. It is an unincorporated community at the south end of Cultus Lake in Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area H. Permits, zoning and services run through the FVRD, not Chilliwack City Hall. It is a CADREB board sub-area under the MLS area Cultus Lake. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Strategic~Plans/2020%203rd%20Quarter%20Update.pdf

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Lindell Beach sits at the south end of Cultus Lake; Columbia Valley Road is the single access road, running north past the community to Chilliwack and the Highway 1 Vedder Road interchange, and south toward Columbia Valley and the U.S. border. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Strategic~Plans/2020%203rd%20Quarter%20Update.pdf
- The community's named addresses line Columbia Valley Road: Lindell Beach Holiday Resort at 1920, The Cottages at Cultus Lake at 1777, Aquadel Crossing at 1885, and Creekside Mills in the same south-lake area. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- The community is built around its residents' beach and docks on Cultus Lake's south shore; Cultus Lake Park (the separate, park-board north-shore community) and its Main Beach are a short drive north, and BC Parks' Delta Grove Campground sits at the lake's southeast end. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/cultus-lake-beaches/ and https://bcparks.ca/cultus-lake-park/
- There is no commercial centre in Lindell Beach and no public transit serving it — the nearest BC Transit service is in Chilliwack (Route 56 reaches only the north-end Marina, summer only), and daily shopping is in Sardis/Garrison Crossing in Chilliwack. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html and https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=56
- Schools are School District 33 (Chilliwack): Cultus Lake Community School, 71 Sunnyside Blvd in Cultus Lake Park, is the lake's elementary school; SD33's AP 611 groups the Columbia Valley/Cultus Lake area as Cultus Lake Elementary → Mount Slesse Middle → G.W. Graham Secondary. https://cultuslake.sd33.bc.ca/ and https://www.sd33.bc.ca/sites/sd33.bc.ca/files/2023-03/611%20AP%20Transportation%20-%20Operating%20Instructions.pdf
- Services are FVRD service areas, not city utilities: the Cultus Lake Integrated Water Supply and Distribution system and the South Cultus Lake Sewage Treatment system, with parcel taxes on participating properties; Area H also has two fire services (Cultus Lake 213 and Columbia Valley 215) and no FVRD garbage/recycling service. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Strategic~Plans/2020%203rd%20Quarter%20Update.pdf
- Population context: Statistics Canada counted 2,459 people in all of FVRD Electoral Area H in 2021 (+33.1% from 2016); no Lindell Beach-only figure is published — UNVERIFIED. https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/as-sa/fogs-spg/Page.cfm?lang=e&topic=9&dguid=2021S0503930
- Where 55+ housing concentrates: **nowhere in Lindell Beach itself — no source found places any verified 55+ complex, seniors building, retirement residence or care facility in the community.** The named complexes are general-market cottage/strata communities that attract retiree buyers without any age bylaw (Section 2). The nearest concentration of verified 55+ strata is in the City of Chilliwack (see "Checked and NOT in Lindell Beach" below).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. This is the rule that kills every "45+" label a listing still carries. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Manufactured-home parks run on park rules, not strata bylaws.** Under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003), a park owner may refuse consent to an assignment or sublet where no incoming purchaser/subtenant meets the age requirement in a park where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or 2+ tenants at least one of whom has), per s. 10(2)(b)(i) of the BC Human Rights Code. A park's age therefore has to be verified from the park itself — a listing's "55+" or "45+" adjective is not verification, which is exactly the Cultus Lake Village problem above. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Plan: FVRD OCP Bylaw No. 1115, 2011 for Electoral Areas E and H** — the FVRD titles it "Columbia Valley, Lindell Beach and Chilliwack River Valley," so Lindell Beach is covered by name — as amended by Bylaw No. 1533, 2019. Under its designations the only residential use permitted is single-family; its resort-residential policies (§5.9) calculate permitted sites/strata lots/units on "useable land." https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws/planning-land-use.html and https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11249
- **Zoning: FVRD Consolidated Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021** (approved Oct 27, 2022, consolidating nine earlier bylaws) governs land outside Cultus Lake Park; Bylaw No. 1375, 2016 applies only inside the Park. One Lindell-area zone is confirmed from a primary/developer source: PRD-1, Private Resort Residential Development (Creekside Mills). The zone and minimum parcel size for the older beach-strip lots are UNVERIFIED — check per address in Bylaw 1638 / the FVRD webmap. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2022/consolidated-zoning-bylaw-approved.html?media=contrast and https://creeksidemills.com/faq
- **Short-term rentals are an FVRD matter here, and an active one:** the FVRD Board specifically enabled Electoral Area H to proactively address short-term vacation rentals and RV use for accommodation. The mechanism is zoning — Bylaw 1638 excludes "Temporary Tourist Accommodation" from Residential use except where a zone expressly permits it — and provincially, hosts have needed a registration number since May 1, 2025; electoral areas are exempt from the provincial principal-residence rule unless the board opts in (no opt-in found for Area H — UNVERIFIED). https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Strategic~Plans/2020%203rd%20Quarter%20Update.pdf and https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=22845 and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0032-000389
- **Flood/hazard layer:** FVRD Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 1669, 2022 applies region-wide except municipalities and Park lands — so it applies here — and requires alluvial-fan development to meet the flood construction level/setback set by a site-specific geohazard assessment by a Professional Engineer/Geoscientist. The FVRD's Frosst Creek works (two debris basins and two dikes) protect the community on the Frosst Creek alluvial fan at the lake's south end. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Floodplain%20Management%20Bylaw%20No.%201669.pdf and https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/flood-protection.html?media=contrast
- **Rural taxes and the FVRD/rural penalty regime:** Lindell Beach properties pay rural (provincial) property taxes plus FVRD service parcel taxes. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, rural taxes are due and payable July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on October 31. South-shore water and sewer are tax-funded (no separate utility bill in the FVRD's Area H 2026 rates: Water – Cultus Lake (485) and Sewer – Cultus Lake South (448) both show "No Utility Billing / Property Taxes: Yes"), and the South Cultus Lake sewer parcel tax (service area Bylaw No. 1416, 2017; parcel tax Bylaw No. 1497, 2018, as amended by Bylaw No. 1779, 2025) is the annual requisition divided equally among parcels on the roll. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 and https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area H - Residential Rates 2026.pdf and https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com:443/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=30839
- **Tenure warning that shapes everything at this lake:** Cultus Lake Park homes on the north shore sit on leased park land under the Park's leaseholder system; Lindell Beach is a separate community with predominantly freehold tenure — but resort properties are the exception (the Holiday Resort's documented model is an owned trailer on a leased pad), so title must be verified on every individual property. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/new-resident-information/ and https://www.bccourts.ca/jdb-txt/CA/09/00/2009BCCA0005.htm

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a 55+ community in Lindell Beach I can buy into?** No verified one. Creekside Mills (129 homes, bareland strata), The Cottages at Cultus Lake (bare land strata) and Aquadel Crossing (gated single-family) are all general-market — no source states an age restriction for any of them — and the Holiday Resort is leased pads, not ownership of land. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=15206 and https://www.aquadelcrossing.com/phase-3
- **Q: A search showed a "55+ park" at Cultus Lake. Is that in Lindell Beach?** The park behind those results is Cultus Lake Village, 3942 Columbia Valley Road, in Cultus Lake East by the golf course — not Lindell Beach — and its own listings disagree on the age (55+ in one, 45+ in others), so its exact age rule is UNCONFIRMED until the park states it in writing. https://www.zolo.ca/cultus-lake-real-estate/3942-columbia-valley-road/65 and https://www.zolo.ca/cultus-lake-real-estate/3942-columbia-valley-road/28
- **Q: Does a "45+" label mean anything legally in BC now?** For a strata, no: age bylaws below 55 have been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 — only 55+ bylaws survive, with the Regulation 7.01 exemptions (legacy residents, live-in caregivers, younger spouse/partner, children under 19 in the resident's care, adult children cared for before 19). For a manufactured-home park, the age rule is a park rule under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy framework tied to the Human Rights Code's 55 threshold — again, verify the park's own written rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q: Where is the nearest real 55+ strata stock?** In the City of Chilliwack: Cottage Grove (8500 Young Road, gated 55+ ranchers), Mountainview Estates (46966 Yale Road, 55+, pets not allowed per its strata records), and the Spadina corridor buildings (Ivy Green, 45775 Spadina Ave; Spadina Regency, 45765 Spadina Ave). All are Chilliwack properties — none is in Lindell Beach. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058912-8500-young-road-31-chilliwack-bc-v2p-4p1/brochure and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/mountain-view-estates-46966-yale-road-chilliwack-east
- **Q: If I buy a cottage at Lindell Beach, can I rent it out short-term?** Not by default: Bylaw 1638 excludes Temporary Tourist Accommodation from Residential use unless the zone expressly permits it, the FVRD has specifically enabled STR enforcement in Electoral Area H, and a provincial STR registration number has been required since May 1, 2025. A seller's Airbnb history is not proof a buyer can continue it. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=22845 and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0032-000389
- **Q: Do I pay Chilliwack taxes or a city utility bill?** Neither: rural provincial property tax (due July 2; 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after October 31) plus FVRD parcel taxes — and on the south shore, water and sewer are tax-funded with no separate utility bill. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 and https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area H - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- **Q: Is Lindell Beach leasehold like Cultus Lake Park?** Mostly no — Lindell Beach is predominantly freehold, while north-shore Park homes are leasehold — but the Holiday Resort is the documented exception (owned trailer on a leased pad, per the 2009 BC Court of Appeal record), so tenure is a per-property title check, never an assumption. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/new-resident-information/ and https://www.bccourts.ca/jdb-txt/CA/09/00/2009BCCA0005.htm
- **Q: What happens when I can no longer drive?** There is no public transit serving Lindell Beach (Route 56 reaches only the lake's north-end Marina, in summer), no care facility in the community in any source checked, and Columbia Valley Road is the only way in or out — aging in place here is entirely car- and family-dependent. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html and https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=56
- **Q: Are there flood or debris risks for a south-shore lot?** The governing hazards are lake-level and creek debris/flood events on the Cultus Lake alluvial fans — Frosst Creek's fan sits at the lake's south end — and Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 1669, 2022 requires a site-specific geohazard assessment to set the flood construction level and setback for fan development. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Floodplain%20Management%20Bylaw%20No.%201669.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The north side of the lake got its sewage upgrade first (2025).** The FVRD's North Cultus wastewater plant was commissioned in April 2025 (construction July 2023, substantial completion December 2024) under the Liquid Waste Management Plan (approved 2019), producing Class A effluent with phosphorus removal — while the south shore (Lindell Beach) remains on the older South Cultus Lake Sewage Treatment service, funded by its equal-share parcel tax. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2025/north-cultus-wastewater-treatment-plant-comes-online.html and https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/wwtp
- **Lindell Beach is named in its own OCP.** OCP Bylaw No. 1115, 2011 is titled "Columbia Valley, Lindell Beach and Chilliwack River Valley" — the community is small, but it is a named planning community of Electoral Area H, not a Chilliwack neighbourhood that drifted south. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws/planning-land-use.html
- **The Holiday Resort's tenure was settled in court, not in a brochure.** The leading public record for Lindell Beach's biggest complex is a 2009 BC Court of Appeal decision (2009 BCCA 5) describing leased pads and owner-placed trailers (130 sites, 100 occupied at that time) — a reminder that resort "ownership" at the lake can mean owning a trailer and renting the ground under it. https://www.bccourts.ca/jdb-txt/CA/09/00/2009BCCA0005.htm
- **Area H is growing fast — on paper, at least (2021 Census).** Electoral Area H grew 33.1% between 2016 and 2021 (1,847 → 2,459 people), the context behind the new strata cottage communities at the lake's south end — none of which adopted an age restriction in any source checked. https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/as-sa/fogs-spg/Page.cfm?lang=e&topic=9&dguid=2021S0503930
- **"Chilliwack" on the envelope, FVRD at the counter.** Building permits, zoning enforcement and services for a Lindell Beach address all run through the FVRD; Chilliwack City Hall has no jurisdiction, even though the mailing address, the school district (SD33) and the MLS board (CADREB) all say Chilliwack. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Strategic~Plans/2020%203rd%20Quarter%20Update.pdf
