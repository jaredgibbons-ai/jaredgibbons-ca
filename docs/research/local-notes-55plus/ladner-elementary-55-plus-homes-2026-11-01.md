# Ladner Elementary — 55+ Homes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Ladner Elementary" is a Paragon (MLS) sub-area name, not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name — it follows Ladner Elementary School, a School District 37 school at 5016 44 Avenue, in central Ladner under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://le.deltasd.bc.ca/ and http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- The working area is central Ladner around the school, Memorial Park (5010 47 Ave) and Arthur Drive, including the Ladner Village core west of Chilukthan Slough. https://le.deltasd.bc.ca/ and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf
- Delta Secondary School (4615 51 St) is the secondary school for this part of Ladner; School District 37 catchments are address-based and checked in the SD37 School Locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Ladner Leisure Centre is at 4600 Clarence Taylor Crescent; Ladner Pioneer Library at 4683 51st Street; McKee Seniors Recreation Centre at 5155 47 Avenue. http://delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/ladner-leisure-centre and http://www.delta.ca/city-hall/working-delta/volunteering/mckee-seniors-recreation-centre
- Delta Hospital, Fraser Health's community hospital for Delta with 24/7 emergency care, is at 5800 Mountain View Blvd, beside the Ladner Exchange bus loop. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- Transit: route 609 runs South Delta Exchange to Ladner Exchange via Arthur Drive and Ladner Trunk Road, serving the Ladner Elementary / Delta Secondary area; route 601 runs South Delta to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line) via Ladner Exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt609.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Flood context: Ladner is low land protected by the City of Delta's flood protection system — dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, over 63 kilometres of infrastructure, plus 31 drainage pump stations maintained by the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Where 55+ housing actually concentrates in this sub-area: one small strata building — Delta Green at 4686 51 Street, across from Memorial Park and the library — plus the subsidized Marina Park apartments on 48 Avenue in the village core. Ladner's care residence, Augustine House, is on Arthur Drive in south Ladner (see Section 2). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

### Verified 55+ strata (ownership)

- **Delta Green — 4686 51 Street (Ladner Village core).** The one verified age-restricted strata ownership building in this sub-area, and one of only two verified in all of Ladner. Freehold strata, plan EPS8064, built 2021, 24 units. Listing data for the building records "Restricted Age: 55+" and describes "Premier 55+ living in the heart of Ladner Village directly across from Memorial Park & the library," with 9 ft ceilings, radiant in-floor heating and air conditioning; amenities in listings include a rooftop deck, meeting room, fitness gym and secure bike room. The project's City of Delta public hearing record (2018, for 4674 & 4684 51 Street) likewise describes the proposal as 24 private-market strata ownership units including "the 55+ age category." Pets: listing data for a current unit records cats and dogs OK, limited to one. Rental position: confirm in the strata bylaws / Form B — no building source in this pass states it. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3091870-107-4686-51-street-delta-bc-v4k-2v7 and https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/4686-51-street/106 and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/179807/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT 55+ HOMES

- **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal in BC strata now.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw set below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when Bill 44 (Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022) took effect. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaw restricts who may LIVE in the unit, not who may own it.** The Province states people under 55 can own a strata lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to reside in it; the exemptions attach to a "specified resident" who meets the age requirement or holds a legacy exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Built-in exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01):** residents who were lawfully living in the unit when a 55+ bylaw passed (legacy residents); live-in caregivers; and — expanded effective May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed for that to apply. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **How Delta Green's 55+ status was secured at approval:** the City's 2018 public hearing material for the 51 Street site discusses the project's "55+ age category" and a restrictive covenant on "adaptable-ready" design under the BC Building Code, so residents can age in place with future unit modifications. A strata age bylaw and a covenant are different instruments — a buyer should confirm which one(s) are registered on title and in the bylaws via the Form B and strata documents. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/179807/
- **City zoning: Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017** assigns zoning parcel by parcel; Delta Green sits in the Ladner Village core, where the OCP's Ladner Village (LV1) and Ladner Village Core (LVC) development permit areas also apply and address the Village's historic buildings. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf
- **Seniors congregate housing in Delta's zoning:** the Apartment Residential 6 (RA6) zone added to Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 in 2024 lists "seniors congregate housing" as a permitted principal use for six-storey apartment development under the OCP's Neighbourhood Centres and Corridors designation. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/
- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 ("Housing Our Future")**, adopted July 8, 2024, is Delta's consolidated OCP covering Ladner, including the Ladner Village development permit areas. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/230647/ and http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Suites and SSMUH:** the RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use to a single detached dwelling, and Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing amendments add coach house and garden suite options where the zoning applies — relevant to a 55+ buyer weighing a house with a suite for a caregiver or family member. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Property tax deferment at 55+:** BC's regular deferment program lets a homeowner 55 or older during the current year defer annual property taxes on a principal residence as a provincial loan; Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026 (5% penalty after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026), and the City tax page links the deferment and Home Owner Grant programs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/property-tax-deferment-program/eligibility and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Subsidized assisted living runs through Fraser Health and BC Housing.** The City's Affordable Housing Guide (April 2026) covers subsidized assisted living in Delta — in Ladner, the Augustine House program — and the SAFER rent supplement for elderly renters. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What exactly is Delta Green?** A 24-unit freehold strata at 4686 51 Street, built 2021, strata plan EPS8064. Listing data records the building as "Restricted Age: 55+" and markets it as premier 55+ living across from Memorial Park and the Ladner Pioneer Library; the City's approval record describes the same 55+ category at the application stage. It is the only verified 55+ strata in this sub-area. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3091870-107-4686-51-street-delta-bc-v4k-2v7 and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/179807/
- **Q: With only 24 units, how often does anything sell there?** Mechanically, rarely — a 24-unit building generates few listings in any year, and the 55+ bylaw further narrows both the seller's and buyer's pool to residents who meet the age requirement or an exemption. No source publishes a turnover rate — UNCONFIRMED; the practical point is to be ready when a unit lists. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live at Delta Green?** Yes — provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption). Children can also be exempt in defined cases: a child under 19 in the resident's care, and an adult child the resident cared for before that child turned 19. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I'm under 55. Can I buy a Delta Green unit as an investment or for a parent?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. The Province states under-55s can own strata lots in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in them; a parent who is 55+ can be the resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit in a 55+ building?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist in BC — every strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two catches remain: the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. Delta Green's own rental position must be confirmed in its bylaws — no building source in this research states it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Delta Green?** Listing data for a current unit records pets allowed — cats OK, dogs OK, with a number limit of one. Confirm the size/breed rules in the registered bylaws before relying on it. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/4686-51-street/106
- **Q: Is River West 55+? It keeps coming up in searches.** Not verified. One aggregator page shows an age-restriction field with no value in it; current listings for the building record pet and rental bylaws only, with no age bylaw. Treat River West as a general-market riverfront building unless the strata's registered bylaws, produced on a purchase, say otherwise. https://www.estateblock.com/ladner/202-4743-w-river-rd-52494
- **Q: Where is the hospital, and what if I need care later?** Delta Hospital (5800 Mountain View Blvd) is Fraser Health's Delta community hospital with 24/7 emergency care, beside Ladner Exchange. For care housing in Ladner itself, Augustine House (3820 Arthur Dr) offers independent and assisted living plus care, including a 20-unit Fraser Health assisted living program at 55+ accessed through a case manager. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital and http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **Q: What is Marina Park, and can I buy there?** No — Marina Park (4843 48 Ave) is subsidized non-market rental: 50 one-bedroom units at 30% of monthly gross income, age requirement 55+, applied for through BC Housing's Housing Registry. It is a rental option for income-qualifying seniors, not strata stock. https://www.seniorsservicessociety.ca/location/marina-park/
- **Q: Is this part of Ladner behind dikes?** Ladner is low land protected by the City's dike system (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 pump stations). Lots within 30 m of a stream — including Chilukthan Slough, which borders the village core — are also in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. Check the specific lot on DeltaMap. https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Delta Green was purpose-built for aging in place, not just age-labelled.** The City's hearing record for the site pairs the 55+ category with a covenant for "adaptable-ready" units under the BC Building Code — wider clearances and roughed-in features so a unit can be modified later — and the developer page describes all 24 units as adaptable living units. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/179807/ and https://en.youlive.ca/delta-building/13200-delta-green
- **The seniors services are clustered within a few blocks of Delta Green.** The developer page for the building lists its neighbours as the Ladner Lawn Bowling Club, the Pioneer Library, the McKee Seniors Centre and the Ladner pool; McKee Seniors Recreation Centre (5155 47 Ave) runs programs for adults 50+, with annual membership for 50+ at $30 per its society newsletter. https://en.youlive.ca/delta-building/13200-delta-green and https://www.mckeesociety.com/s/McKee-February-2026-Newsletter.pdf
- **The free Delta Seniors Bus is home-based at McKee.** The City's service directory states the Delta Seniors Bus offers a free transportation service for seniors in Delta, home-based at McKee Seniors Recreation Centre in Ladner — a practical car-free option alongside TransLink routes 601 and 609. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2021-06/SocialServicesInventory%20CS.pdf
- **Augustine House predates the current seniors-housing wave.** The society behind it was created in November 1992 by members of the Knights of Columbus Council 7916, and the residence opened in August 2003 on Arthur Drive — it has been Ladner's care address for over two decades, which is why locals name it first when care comes up. https://safecarebc.ca/partner/augustine-house/
- **The village core has a heritage overlay that affects renovations.** The OCP's Ladner Village Core development permit area addresses the Village's historic buildings, so exterior work on older village-core homes — including a downsizer purchase being adapted — can need a development permit on top of a building permit. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf
- **The sub-area name is a school name, not a catchment promise.** SD37 catchments are address-based; the locator result for the school's own address (5016 44 Ave) is Ladner Elementary with Delta Secondary, but every other address must be checked individually — it matters for grandparents who expect grandchildren to attend the named school. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **New general-market supply is arriving in the village, but none of it is 55+.** Bridge & Elliott's 131 homes (studios to three bedrooms) are under construction in Ladner Village per the developer, with no age restriction in any source — a buyer who wants a legal 55+ bylaw in this sub-area has exactly one building: Delta Green. https://headwaterprojects.com/development/bridge-and-elliott
